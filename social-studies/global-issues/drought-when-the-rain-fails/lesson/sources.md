Angola's own way (Grade 6): a resources-and-environment year — every world topic (air, water, soils, forests) is closed with the Angolan case: Angola's fresh-water use, Angola's farm land, Angola's forest areas. That way is stocked here as the way Angola teaches this thing.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 3) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 3 transport opens with the relationship between population and transport systems, then road accidents, pedestrian and passenger safety; global issues are drought, afforestation and reforestation. Contents numbering jumps from Unit 23 to Unit 25 (no Unit 24 listed). That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Social Studies): one integrated Social Studies book per grade weaving History, Geography, Civics and Culture strands; Geography is taught through Eritrea itself first (Eritrea's location and size, Eritrean settlement, migration and disasters) with very practical, activity-led lessons.

Eritrea's own way (ER G7 Social Studies): Grade 7 turns fully to skills and Eritrea's land — map scale, area and population arithmetic first, then Eritrea's natural heritage (Red Sea coast, wildlife) and Eritrea's own disaster set (drought, locusts, earthquakes, volcanism) as the environmental core.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 10 Geografija 1 (gimnazija)): the gimnazija first year is systematic physical geography first (Earth's interior, rocks, landforms, weather and climate, soils, vegetation, waters), then human geography (population, settlements, farming, industry and energy, transport, tourism), closing on sustainable development and spatial planning — with Slovenian cases (earthquake risk in Slovenia, the Port of Koper) set inside the world topics.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 6 geo-6af-m): The final primary book turns outward: five of its six units place Mauritania inside its organisations — the Arab Maghreb Union, the Senegal River organisation (OMVS), the Arab League, the African Union, the Sahel drought committee (CILSS), the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation — and inside the industrial world (Europe, North America, the industrial East), before a closing world unit on continents, oceans, seas, rivers and straits.

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 10 CCSS Geografía 10): A distance-learning self-study module ("Geografía Dinámica") built entirely around Nicaragua itself — every physical topic (relief, climate, watersheds, coasts) is taught through Nicaraguan regions, and risk/vulnerability (hurricanes, Casita landslide, SINAPRED) closes the book. Non-Geography parts: Unit IV population-policy content (Hambre Cero, Usura Cero, housing projects) is Civics/social policy — named here, not placed beyond its population-geography topics.
