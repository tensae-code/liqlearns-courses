# Drought — when the rain fails

## Do it first

A desert is always dry — that is aridity, its permanent state. Drought is different: dryness that comes to a place that expects rain.

## Sub lesson 1 — Drought is temporary; aridity is permanent

Drought is a temporary reduction in water or moisture availability — distinct from aridity, the permanent rainfall deficiency of deserts. Drought-prone regions are those dry on a regular or seasonal basis where the probability of a drought year is high; they are identified by dryness ratios and mapped against world aridity and annual rainfall, with Ethiopia's drylands among them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The chain, again

Drought, desertification and famine are explicitly linked as a chain: failed rain stresses land and livelihoods; stressed land degrades; degraded land and failed harvests, where poverty stands, become famine. That is why this lesson sits between desertification and famine in the unit — drought is the chain's moving link, the one that arrives as weather and stays as hunger.
