# Drought — when the rain fails — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Separate drought from aridity.
**Answer:** Drought is temporary; aridity is permanent rainfall deficiency.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes a region drought-prone?
**Answer:** It is dry regularly/seasonally with a high probability of a drought year.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does drought sit between desertification and famine in the unit?
**Answer:** It is the moving link of their chain.
