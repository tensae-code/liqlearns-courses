# Drought — when the rain fails — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Drought is a… reduction in water availability
a) permanent  b) temporary  c) imaginary
**Answer: b) temporary**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Aridity is…
a) temporary dryness  b) the permanent rainfall deficiency of deserts  c) a flood
**Answer: b) the permanent rainfall deficiency of deserts**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Drought-prone regions have a high probability of…
a) a drought year  b) snow  c) earthquakes
**Answer: a) a drought year**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Drought-prone areas are identified by…
a) dryness ratios  b) population  c) language
**Answer: a) dryness ratios**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Drought is the chain's link that arrives as…
a) weather  b) law  c) trade
**Answer: a) weather**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The chain runs drought → land stress → degradation →…
a) famine  b) wealth  c) forest
**Answer: a) famine**
