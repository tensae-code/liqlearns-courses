# Drought — when the rain fails — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A desert in its normal state is in drought.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's drylands are among the drought-prone areas mapped.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Drought, desertification and famine are linked as a chain.
**Answer: True**
