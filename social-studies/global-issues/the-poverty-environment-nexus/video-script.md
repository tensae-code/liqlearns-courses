# Video script — The poverty–environment nexus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Classic market failures degrade the environment, and poverty locks the damage in: the poor are both agents and victims. Necessity or ignorance makes them destroy the resources they live from — the firewood cutter, the farmer on marginal land.

The resulting degradation cuts productivity and raises health costs, deepening the poverty that caused it. Poverty, tree-cutting, eroded land, deeper poverty: the trap is a loop, and single-point fixes leak around it.

The book's paradox for national accountants: unsustainable production can raise current GNP while quietly destroying the long-run product it is measured against. The measure applauds the cutting of the ground it stands on.
