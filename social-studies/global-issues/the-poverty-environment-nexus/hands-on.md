# Hands-on — The poverty–environment nexus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Turn the circle
Draw the circular arrow — poverty, tree-cutting, eroded land, deeper poverty — and mark two points where a policy could cut the loop, naming the policy at each point.

## GNP trap
A region doubles charcoal output and its GNP rises while its forests vanish. Write the national accountant's honest footnote to the GNP figure, in three lines.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
