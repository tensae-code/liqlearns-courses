# The poverty–environment nexus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Agents and victims at once"?
a) The poor are both agents and victims of environmental degradation  b) Degradation cuts productivity and raises health costs, deepening poverty — a loop  c) Unsustainable production can raise current GNP while destroying the long-run product
**Answer: a) The poor are both agents and victims of environmental degradation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The circle closes"?
a) Degradation cuts productivity and raises health costs, deepening poverty — a loop  b) Unsustainable production can raise current GNP while destroying the long-run product  c) The poor are both agents and victims of environmental degradation
**Answer: a) Degradation cuts productivity and raises health costs, deepening poverty — a loop**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The accountant's paradox"?
a) Unsustainable production can raise current GNP while destroying the long-run product  b) The poor are both agents and victims of environmental degradation  c) Degradation cuts productivity and raises health costs, deepening poverty — a loop
**Answer: a) Unsustainable production can raise current GNP while destroying the long-run product**
