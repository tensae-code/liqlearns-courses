# The poverty–environment nexus — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The poor are both agents and victims of environmental degradation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Degradation cuts productivity and raises health costs, deepening poverty — a loop.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Unsustainable production can raise current GNP while destroying the long-run product.
**Answer: True**
