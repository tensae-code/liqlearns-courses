# The poverty–environment nexus — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Agents and victims at once" in your own words.
**Answer:** The poor are both agents and victims of environmental degradation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The circle closes" in your own words.
**Answer:** Degradation cuts productivity and raises health costs, deepening poverty — a loop.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The accountant's paradox" in your own words.
**Answer:** Unsustainable production can raise current GNP while destroying the long-run product.
