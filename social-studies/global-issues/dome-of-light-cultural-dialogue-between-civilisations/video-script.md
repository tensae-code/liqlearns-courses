# Video script — Dome of Light Cultural Dialogue Between Civilisations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G9 teacher guide, volume 2, printed p. 117, teaches: the faces of cultural cooperation between the UAE and France; civilisational contact between nations and peoples across history and the foundations on which relations between human civilisations rest; the message of the Louvre Abu Dhabi to the world; and valuing the government's work in guarding and protecting cultural heritage

United Arab Emirates' own way: the volume closes the year with identity — a whole unit on Zayed as "an identity and witnessed stances", in which the Emirati woman's changing place in society and the citizen's social responsibility are taught as parts of that identity.
