# Video script — Choices That Help the Climate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The discussion cards of Exercise E ask the pupil to talk, not only to sort: why is cycling instead of driving better for the climate? The choice is small, everyday and the pupil's own — that is why Sweden's book teaches climate through it.

The second card asks why buying Swedish apples beats apples from South America. Food that travels a shorter way is the lesson's point: the climate choice hides inside an ordinary shopping choice.
