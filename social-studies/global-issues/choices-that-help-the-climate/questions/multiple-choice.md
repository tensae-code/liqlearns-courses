# Choices That Help the Climate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Cycling instead of driving  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Cycling instead of driving**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) two everyday choices that help the climate — cycling instead of driving, and buying Swedish apples instead of apples from South America — from the discussion cards of Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise E  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) two everyday choices that help the climate — cycling instead of driving, and buying Swedish apples instead of apples from South America — from the discussion cards of Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise E**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Swedish apples instead of apples from South America  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) Swedish apples instead of apples from South America**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Sweden's discussion cards ask why cycling instead of driving is better for the climate.  b) Sweden's discussion cards teach that driving everywhere is the best choice for the climate.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about Sweden
