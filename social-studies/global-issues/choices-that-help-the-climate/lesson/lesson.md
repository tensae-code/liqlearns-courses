# Choices That Help the Climate

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: two everyday choices that help the climate — cycling instead of driving, and buying Swedish apples instead of apples from South America — from the discussion cards of Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise E. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cycling instead of driving

The discussion cards of Exercise E ask the pupil to talk, not only to sort: why is cycling instead of driving better for the climate? The choice is small, everyday and the pupil's own — that is why Sweden's book teaches climate through it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Swedish apples instead of apples from South America

The second card asks why buying Swedish apples beats apples from South America. Food that travels a shorter way is the lesson's point: the climate choice hides inside an ordinary shopping choice.

Sweden's own way: the textbook's Geography is fixed by doing — colouring Sweden on the globe, sorting picture cards of Swedish houses, nature types, plants and animals under the right heading, and sorting photographs under warm climate / cold climate.
