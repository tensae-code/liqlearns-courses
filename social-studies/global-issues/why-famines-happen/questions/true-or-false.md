# Why famines happen — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Either master cause alone can produce famine.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Unseasonable frost is a natural precipitant.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Famine is impossible where food exists in markets.
**Answer: False**
