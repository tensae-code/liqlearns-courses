# Why famines happen — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two master causes are availability decline and…
a) entitlement failure  b) overpopulation  c) imports
**Answer: a) entitlement failure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Locusts, army worms and quelea birds are…
a) fertilisers  b) pest invasions  c) grains
**Answer: b) pest invasions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Conflict causes famine by…
a) stopping planting, weeding, harvesting and selling  b) raising prices only  c) building roads
**Answer: a) stopping planting, weeding, harvesting and selling**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Forced state procurement can push availability…
a) above need  b) below need  c) nowhere
**Answer: b) below need**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Entitlement failure means people cannot…
a) grow food  b) acquire food that exists  c) cook
**Answer: b) acquire food that exists**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Coping mechanisms include…
a) selling assets and migrating  b) waiting for rain only  c) celebrating
**Answer: a) selling assets and migrating**
