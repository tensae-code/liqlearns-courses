# Why famines happen — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three natural precipitants.
**Answer:** Any three of: agricultural drought, floods, high winds, unseasonable cold/frost, crop disease, pest invasions.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two human precipitants.
**Answer:** Any two of: conflict stopping farm work/selling, external price/earnings shocks, pricing mismanagement/overtaxed exports, forced procurement/over-export.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What price do coping steps charge later?
**Answer:** They buy time at a price paid later — assets sold and strength spent must be rebuilt.
