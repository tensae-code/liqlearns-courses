# Hands-on — Cities and the environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Haze map
Draw a crowded informal settlement under a haze, a clean-technology factory at its edge. Draw arrows for the three emission sources (vehicles, industry, stoves) landing on the settlement.

## Capacity list
List the five strained services (water, sanitation, housing, power, transport) for one fast-growing city you know of. For each, write the environmental harm when it fails.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
