# Cities and the environment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The urban population will grow 1.5 times to 6 billion by 2045.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The harm concentrates on the urban poor in slums and informal settlements.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Clean technologies pollute less and use resources more efficiently by design.
**Answer: True**
