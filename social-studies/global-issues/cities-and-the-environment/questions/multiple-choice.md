# Cities and the environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The urban majority grows"?
a) The urban population will grow 1.5 times to 6 billion by 2045  b) The harm concentrates on the urban poor in slums and informal settlements  c) Clean technologies pollute less and use resources more efficiently by design
**Answer: a) The urban population will grow 1.5 times to 6 billion by 2045**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Strain, concentrated on the poor"?

**Answer: a) The harm concentrates on the urban poor in slums and informal settlements**
