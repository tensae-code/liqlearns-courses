# Cities and the environment — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The urban majority grows" in your own words.
**Answer:** The urban population will grow 1.5 times to 6 billion by 2045.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Strain, concentrated on the poor" in your own words.
**Answer:** The harm concentrates on the urban poor in slums and informal settlements.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The offered direction — clean technology" in your own words.
**Answer:** Clean technologies pollute less and use resources more efficiently by design.
