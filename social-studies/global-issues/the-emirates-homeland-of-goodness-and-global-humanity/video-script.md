# Video script — The Emirates Homeland of Goodness and Global Humanity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G6 Term 1, printed p. 72, teaches: giving as the state's founding character — Sheikh Khalifa's initiatives, the Year of Giving 2017 (عام الخير), the concept of true goodness, and the Emirates' humanitarian support in the COVID pandemic: medical aid and support to more than 100 countries, the Emirates Humanitarian City in Abu Dhabi, and care for the families of those who died of the virus whatever their nationality

United Arab Emirates' own way: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries.
