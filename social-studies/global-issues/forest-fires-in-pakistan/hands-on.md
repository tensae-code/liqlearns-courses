# Hands-on — Forest Fires In Pakistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Forest fires in Pakistan: forests far below the 25% ideal cover, how fires start (lightning, carelessness), and their damage to ecosystems, wildlife and catchment areas. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
