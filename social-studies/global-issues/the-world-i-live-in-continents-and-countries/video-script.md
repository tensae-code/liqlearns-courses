Oman Grade 1 Identity & Citizenship TG T2, printed pp. 43–50, teaches: The world's continents defined as the land areas of the earth, seven in number, each named — Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Europe, Australia, Antarctica — discovered through the animals that live on each.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 1, Term 2): term 2 completes the widening circles — after self, family and neighbourhood come the homeland (its flag raised every morning, its Sultan named with a prayer, its Renaissance told as one man's building) and then the whole world, where the Omani child is taught that difference itself is what makes a good citizen.
