# Hands-on — The World I Live in Continents and Countries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The World I Live in Continents and Countries
Work through this part with your hands: The world's continents defined as the land areas of the earth, seven in number, each named — Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Europe, Australia, Antarctica — discovered through the animals that live on each. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
