# Climate conventions — how the world organised — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the timeline in order: 1979, 1988, 1990, 1992.
**Answer:** First World Climate Conference; Toronto Conference/IPCC created; IPCC First Assessment; Framework Convention produced.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does COP stand for, in the book's usage?
**Answer:** The Conference of the Parties — the treaty's meeting of its member countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two pieces of the 2001 machinery.
**Answer:** Any two of: emissions trading, Clean Development Mechanism, sink accounting, compliance.
