# Climate conventions — how the world organised — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The First World Climate Conference was in…
a) 1979  b) 1992  c) 2001
**Answer: a) 1979**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The IPCC was created by…
a) WMO and UNEP  b) the World Bank  c) one country
**Answer: a) WMO and UNEP**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Resolution 43/53 called climate change…
a) a national problem  b) a common concern of mankind  c) a hoax
**Answer: b) a common concern of mankind**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Framework Convention came from negotiations in…
a) 1979–80  b) 1991–92  c) 2003–4
**Answer: b) 1991–92**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Marrakesh Accords (2001) set up…
a) emissions trading and the Clean Development Mechanism  b) the first weather stations  c) the IPCC
**Answer: a) emissions trading and the Clean Development Mechanism**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** COP 10, Buenos Aires 2004, added…
a) the adaptation programme  b) the first assessment  c) the Toronto call
**Answer: a) the adaptation programme**
