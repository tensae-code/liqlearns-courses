# Hands-on — When Warming Feeds Disaster

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Impacts of global warming: more floods (Pakistan 2022), glacier melt and tsunami risk, severer cyclones, drying wetlands and droughts, heat waves (Karachi) and forest fires. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
