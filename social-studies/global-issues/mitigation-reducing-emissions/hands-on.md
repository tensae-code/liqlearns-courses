# Hands-on — Mitigation — reducing emissions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Valve and forest
Draw a smokestack with a valve being closed, a forest being replanted beside it. Label one instrument on the valve and one mechanism on the forest.

## Burden brief
Write the two halves of the burden argument as two speeches of three lines each — the historic-responsibility speech and the shared-future speech — both fair to the book's plain statement.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
