# Mitigation — reducing emissions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Cutting the cause, cheaply" in your own words.
**Answer:** Mitigation cuts emissions using carbon markets, carbon taxes and technology subsidies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Forests and finance mechanisms" in your own words.
**Answer:** REDD and REDD+ pay for avoided deforestation; the CDM channels reduction finance to developing countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Who cuts first" in your own words.
**Answer:** Developed countries hold historic responsibility and must cut first; the trail runs Kyoto, Paris and Kigali.
