# Mitigation — reducing emissions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Cutting the cause, cheaply"?
a) Mitigation cuts emissions using carbon markets, carbon taxes and technology subsidies  b) REDD and REDD+ pay for avoided deforestation; the CDM channels reduction finance to developing countries  c) Developed countries hold historic responsibility and must cut first; the trail runs Kyoto, Paris and Kigali
**Answer: a) Mitigation cuts emissions using carbon markets, carbon taxes and technology subsidies**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Forests and finance mechanisms"?
a) REDD and REDD+ pay for avoided deforestation; the CDM channels reduction finance to developing countries  b) Developed countries hold historic responsibility and must cut first; the trail runs Kyoto, Paris and Kigali  c) Mitigation cuts emissions using carbon markets, carbon taxes and technology subsidies
**Answer: a) REDD and REDD+ pay for avoided deforestation; the CDM channels reduction finance to developing countries**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Who cuts first"?
a) Developed countries hold historic responsibility and must cut first; the trail runs Kyoto, Paris and Kigali  b) Mitigation cuts emissions using carbon markets, carbon taxes and technology subsidies  c) REDD and REDD+ pay for avoided deforestation; the CDM channels reduction finance to developing countries
**Answer: a) Developed countries hold historic responsibility and must cut first; the trail runs Kyoto, Paris and Kigali**
