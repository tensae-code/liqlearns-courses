# Mitigation — reducing emissions — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mitigation cuts emissions using carbon markets, carbon taxes and technology subsidies.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** REDD and REDD+ pay for avoided deforestation; the CDM channels reduction finance to developing countries.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Developed countries hold historic responsibility and must cut first; the trail runs Kyoto, Paris and Kigali.
**Answer: True**
