# Hands-on — Ecological Footprint and Biocapacity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Footprint of a day
List everything you ate, used and threw away today, and say what land or water each thing needed, from the lesson.

## One Earth or more?
Say whether the way of living in the lesson's country examples fits inside one Earth, and what would have to change if it does not.
