# Impacts of climate change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Beyond 2°C: heat waves, flooding, displaced coastal populations and lost ecosystems.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fisheries, mangroves and coral reefs degrade under warming.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Over 140,000 excess deaths a year by 2004 from diarrhoea, malaria and malnutrition attributable to warming.
**Answer: True**
