# Impacts of climate change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Beyond two degrees"?
a) Beyond 2°C: heat waves, flooding, displaced coastal populations and lost ecosystems  b) Fisheries, mangroves and coral reefs degrade under warming  c) Over 140,000 excess deaths a year by 2004 from diarrhoea, malaria and malnutrition attributable to warming
**Answer: a) Beyond 2°C: heat waves, flooding, displaced coastal populations and lost ecosystems**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Fisheries, reefs and coasts"?
a) Fisheries, mangroves and coral reefs degrade under warming  b) Over 140,000 excess deaths a year by 2004 from diarrhoea, malaria and malnutrition attributable to warming  c) Beyond 2°C: heat waves, flooding, displaced coastal populations and lost ecosystems
**Answer: a) Fisheries, mangroves and coral reefs degrade under warming**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The health ledger"?
a) Over 140,000 excess deaths a year by 2004 from diarrhoea, malaria and malnutrition attributable to warming  b) Beyond 2°C: heat waves, flooding, displaced coastal populations and lost ecosystems  c) Fisheries, mangroves and coral reefs degrade under warming
**Answer: a) Over 140,000 excess deaths a year by 2004 from diarrhoea, malaria and malnutrition attributable to warming**
