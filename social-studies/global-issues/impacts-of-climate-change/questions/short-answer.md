# Impacts of climate change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Beyond two degrees" in your own words.
**Answer:** Beyond 2°C: heat waves, flooding, displaced coastal populations and lost ecosystems.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Fisheries, reefs and coasts" in your own words.
**Answer:** Fisheries, mangroves and coral reefs degrade under warming.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The health ledger" in your own words.
**Answer:** Over 140,000 excess deaths a year by 2004 from diarrhoea, malaria and malnutrition attributable to warming.
