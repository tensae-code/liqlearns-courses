# Hands-on — Impacts of climate change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Split landscape
Draw the split landscape — drought-cracked field on one side, flooded homes on the other — and write across the divide the one sentence that joins them from the lesson.

## Worst-of-it audit
Take four impact items and for each name who takes the worst of it and through what dependence (rain-fed farming, exposed housing, coastal fishing...). The pattern is the lesson.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
