# Impacts of climate change

## Do it first

Pass two degrees of warming and the impacts stop adding — they cascade. Sketch one landscape of flood and one of drought, and guess who, in both pictures, takes the worst of it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Beyond two degrees

Beyond 2°C of warming the impacts cascade: heat waves, hurricanes, flooding and moisture stress; displaced coastal populations; lost grasslands, farmland and marine ecosystems. In semi-arid regions shorter growing seasons and lower yields deepen food insecurity and malnutrition.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fisheries, reefs and coasts

Fisheries, mangroves and coral reefs degrade — the sea's nurseries failing with its temperatures and acidity. Each lost ecosystem is also a lost livelihood for the people who harvested it sustainably for generations.

## Sub lesson 3 — The health ledger

WHO estimated over 140,000 excess deaths a year by 2004, from diarrhoea, malaria and malnutrition attributable to warming since the 1970s — with malaria set to climb to cities like Nairobi and Harare. The poor, dependent on rain-fed agriculture and housed in the most exposed places, take the worst of nearly every item on the list.
