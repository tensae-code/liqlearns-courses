## Sub lesson 1 — Living in Safety and Peace

Oman Grade 3 Identity & Citizenship TG T2, printed pp. 45–52, teaches: Safety as a shared right and a shared duty — living بأمان (in safety) is something citizens owe one another across the world, not only at home. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: at Grade 3 the subject turns from feelings to law-shaped habits — rights first, then duties, then the homeland held as a trust (أمانة); and the same rights-and-duties pair is then taught again at world scale, where equality, safety and being heard are the child's global rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
