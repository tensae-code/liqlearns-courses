# Video script — Protecting Wildlife

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 10 Geography (Dari)* (printed p. 125), teaches: Ch. 4 Lesson 34: protecting wildlife. Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 11 Geography (Dari)* (printed p. 19), teaches: Afghanistan Ch. 1 Lesson 7: wild animals (same NEW lesson as the Grade 10 book's wildlife placement).

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 10 Geography (Dari)): the upper-secondary pattern changes from continent tours to themes studied in depth through Afghanistan itself: a full map-skills chapter (map types, scale, colour, projection, satellite photos), Afghanistan's climate and each of its river basins one by one (Amu, Kabul, Helmand–Sistan, Harirud, the closed basins), an environment-protection chapter aimed at Kabul's air and streets, a natural-hazards chapter, then earth-and-space and atmosphere chapters.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 11 Geography (Dari)): an economic-geography year in two halves — first Afghanistan (farming and livestock, energy and mines, industries and trade), then the same themes for the world (water and farming, energy, mines and industries, trade and tourism), each Afghanistan chapter closing with a "review of the situation" lesson on the country's own state.
