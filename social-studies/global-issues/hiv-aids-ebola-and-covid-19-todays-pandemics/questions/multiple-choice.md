# HIV/AIDS, Ebola and COVID-19 — today's pandemics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** HIV/AIDS was first identified in…
a) 1981  b) 2013  c) 1918
**Answer: a) 1981**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** HIV weakens immunity by destroying…
a) red cells  b) t-cells  c) bones
**Answer: b) t-cells**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Deaths from HIV/AIDS since its discovery number about…
a) 3.5 million  b) 35 million  c) 350 million
**Answer: b) 35 million**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ebola's index case was…
a) an 18-month-old boy in a Guinean village, Dec 2013  b) a traveller in 1981  c) unknown entirely
**Answer: a) an 18-month-old boy in a Guinean village, Dec 2013**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Ebola index case was believed infected by…
a) water  b) bats  c) mosquitoes
**Answer: b) bats**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit's closing point: health crises are…
a) purely medical  b) geographic events with socioeconomic consequences  c) unpredictable in every way
**Answer: b) geographic events with socioeconomic consequences**
