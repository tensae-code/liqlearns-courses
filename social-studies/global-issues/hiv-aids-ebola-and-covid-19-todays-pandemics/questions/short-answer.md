# HIV/AIDS, Ebola and COVID-19 — today's pandemics — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does HIV attack the body?
**Answer:** It weakens the immune system by destroying t-cells.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell Ebola's index case in one sentence.
**Answer:** An 18-month-old boy in a Guinean village in December 2013, believed infected by bats.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** State the lesson's closing geographic point.
**Answer:** Health crises are geographic events with socioeconomic consequences, not only medical ones.
