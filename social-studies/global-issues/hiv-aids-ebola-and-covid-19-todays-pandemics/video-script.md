# Video script — HIV/AIDS, Ebola and COVID-19 — today's pandemics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Today's three crises — HIV/AIDS since 1981, Ebola from a Guinean village in 2013, and COVID-19 — teach that pandemics are geographic events with social and economic consequences, not medical events alone. First, hiv/aids and ebola: HIV/AIDS was first identified in 1981. Then, covid-19 — and the geographic lesson: COVID-19 is the most recent of the three crises — the pandemic every living learner has lived through. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
