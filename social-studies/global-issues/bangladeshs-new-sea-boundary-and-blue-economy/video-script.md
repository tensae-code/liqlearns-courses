The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bangladesh's Grade 7 student book, the *History and Social Science, Class Seven* (printed pp. 142-144), teaches: green and blue economy; Bangladesh's new maritime boundary and sea rights.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 7): Geography thins into economic history and a long Sustainable Development inquiry: pupils build a delta in a sand tray, trace the rivers that enter Bangladesh from other countries, map the country's new maritime boundary and its minerals, and audit groundwater and fossil fuels — the delta and the sea are the classroom.
