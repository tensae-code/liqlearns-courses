# Hands-on — Human Rights Institutions That Protect Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 1 "Instituciones de Derechos Humanos"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 31–33, teaches: human rights are inherent and irrenunciable, and states must recognise, spread, protect and guarantee them; the institutions that watch over them — Guatemala's Procurador de los Derechos Humanos, designated by Congress, and the United Nations, founded in 1945 to keep peace and security. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unit 4 "Instituciones de derechos humanos y justicia"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 6 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 190 (same lesson first needed by Book 7), teaches: public, autonomous institutions watch over compliance with the conventions, treaties, laws and agreements on justice and people's rights — the Procurador de los Derechos Humanos and the Archbishop's Human Rights Office for rights; the Supreme Court of Justice and the Constitutional Court for justice. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 8) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the sixth-grade book widens the lens — democracy is traced from Athens to Guatemala's own authoritarian past, and citizenship ends at the world: Guatemala's Belize claim put to its own citizens in the 2018 popular consultation on the International Court of Justice.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
