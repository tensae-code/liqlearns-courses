# Hands-on — Japan rises again; China turns communist

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two timelines
Draw two parallel timelines, 1945–1960, one for Japan and one for China, from the lesson's facts. Read across the years and say how the two countries' positions in the Cold War differ by 1950.

## Fix the swap
A card says: 'Chiang Kai-shek founded the Communist Party and won in 1949.' Rewrite the card correctly, then explain to a partner what was swapped.
