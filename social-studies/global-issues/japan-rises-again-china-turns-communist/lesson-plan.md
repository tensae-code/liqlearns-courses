# Lesson plan — Japan rises again; China turns communist

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Japan rises again; China turns communist
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues — the Cold War in Asia thread; the Grade 10 book's first Cold War Asia lesson (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** post-war Asia split the Cold War's map: Japan, ruined in 1945, recovered to stand as Asia's first post-war recovery case — while China, Asia's largest state, turned communist under Mao Zedong in 1949.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Cold War begins lesson — the two superpowers' rivalry is the frame both stories hang in. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Japan — ruin and recovery** — Asia's most industrialised nation loses its factories and possessions, yet becomes the first Asian case of post-war recovery.
2. **China — 1949, the largest turn of all** — Mao Zedong's communists defeat Chiang Kai-shek's nationalists; Asia's largest state joins the communist side.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Cold War lessons in Modern World (the superpowers and the beginning of the Cold War). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Japan's arc: most industrialised, ruined, recovered.
2. Name China's winner and loser in 1949.
3. Say why China's turn changed the Cold War's balance.

## What you need
