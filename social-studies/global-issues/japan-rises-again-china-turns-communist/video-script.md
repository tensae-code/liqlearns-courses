# Video script — Japan rises again; China turns communist

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Asia, 1945. Japan's cities are grey rubble; its empire's colour drains away to the home islands.
Then the rebuilding: cranes, factories, ships — the grey turning to light, fastest in Asia.
The camera moves to the mainland. Red banners advance city by city; in 1949 a vast country changes colour at a proclamation in its capital.
Pull back: the Cold War's map now has giants on both sides of the Pacific.
