# Video script — Natural Hazards and Managing Their Risk

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC teaches natural hazards in Chapter 13: hurricanes, volcanoes and landslides — natural events that can harm people and the places they live.

The chapter turns from the hazards to managing them — managing natural hazards in general, and managing the risk of hurricanes in particular — with Haiti and Grenada as case studies.
