# Famine and food security — availability is not enough — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things can famine's process reduce?
**Answer:** Food availability, or people's food entitlements.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Separate chronic from transitory insecurity.
**Answer:** Chronic = persistent inability; transitory = temporary decline from prices, production failure or income loss.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is availability not enough?
**Answer:** People must also be able to acquire the food that exists.
