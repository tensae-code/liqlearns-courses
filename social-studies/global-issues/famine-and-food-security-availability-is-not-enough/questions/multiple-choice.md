# Famine and food security — availability is not enough — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Famine is best defined as…
a) a single bad harvest  b) a process reducing availability or entitlements until deaths rise  c) any hunger
**Answer: b) a process reducing availability or entitlements until deaths rise**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Feeding-camp images mislead because famine is…
a) rare  b) a process, not the camp at its end  c) staged
**Answer: b) a process, not the camp at its end**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Chronic hunger affects as much as… of the world's people
a) a third  b) a tenth  c) half
**Answer: a) a third**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Food security (World Bank, 1986) is access by all people, at all times, to…
a) cheap food  b) enough food for an active, healthy life  c) imported food
**Answer: b) enough food for an active, healthy life**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Its two pillars are availability and…
a) exports  b) the ability to acquire food  c) storage
**Answer: b) the ability to acquire food**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Famine is which kind of insecurity at its worst?
a) chronic  b) transitory  c) imaginary
**Answer: b) transitory**
