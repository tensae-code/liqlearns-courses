# Video script — Bandung to Belgrade — the third camp refuses both blocs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens divided: two colours, two camps, arrows of alliance crossing the oceans.
In the mountains of Indonesia, 29 flags rise over one hall — colours belonging to neither camp. Bandung, April 1955.
1961: the camera moves to Belgrade. Five leaders stand on one platform — Yugoslav, Egyptian, Indian, Ghanaian, Indonesian.
The map redraws itself: between the two blocs, a third colour spreads across Asia and Africa — the camp that refused to be a camp.
