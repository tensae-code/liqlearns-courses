# Bandung to Belgrade — the third camp refuses both blocs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Bandung's states agreed to join the Western bloc together.
**Answer: False** — they refused both blocs — that refusal is the movement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Non-alignment meant indifference to world questions.
**Answer: False** — it meant judging each question on its merits, not on a bloc's orders.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Bandung's 29 were heads of state and government of the first post-colonial generation.
**Answer: True** — leaders of newly won or winning independence.
