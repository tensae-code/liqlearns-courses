# Hands-on — Bandung to Belgrade — the third camp refuses both blocs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The third chair
Draw a table with two chairs labelled with the two blocs — then draw the third chair Bandung added. Write under it the conference's two practical stands.

## Five leaders, five flags
Make five name cards — Tito, Nasser, Nehru, Nkrumah, Sukarno — and match each to its country card (Yugoslavia, Egypt, India, Ghana, Indonesia). Shuffle and re-match against the clock.
