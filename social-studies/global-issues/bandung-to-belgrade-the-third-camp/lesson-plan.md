# Lesson plan — Bandung to Belgrade — the third camp refuses both blocs

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Bandung to Belgrade — the third camp refuses both blocs
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues — the post-1945 thread; the Grade 10 book's Non-Aligned Movement lesson (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the newly free states of Asia and Africa refused to enlist in either Cold War bloc: the Bandung Conference of April 1955 gathered 29 states, and the first Non-Aligned conference followed at Belgrade in 1961 under Tito, Nasser, Nehru, Nkrumah and Sukarno.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Cold War lessons — you must know the two blocs to understand the refusal of both. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bandung, April 1955 — twenty-nine states say neither** — the Asia-Africa conference at Bandung, Indonesia, 18–24 April 1955: a UN development fund urged, apartheid and colonialism condemned.
2. **Belgrade 1961 — the movement founded** — the first NAM conference at Belgrade under Tito, Nasser, Nehru, Nkrumah and Sukarno; the purpose later enumerated in Havana, 1979.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Japan rises again; China turns communist. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give Bandung: place, dates, the 29 states, and two of its stands.
2. Name the five leaders of Belgrade 1961.
3. State NAM's purpose as the Havana Declaration of 1979 enumerates it.
