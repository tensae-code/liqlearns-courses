The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bangladesh's Grade 8 student book, the *History and Social Science, Class Eight* (printed pp. 155-159), teaches: bangladesh's main disaster-prone areas (map): lightning, city waterlogging, salinity, nor'westers; disaster management and preparedness.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): the year's Geography is one long chapter, "Protect the Bond between Nature and People": pupils model the world's biomes, compare world forest cover in 2000 and 2020, map Bangladesh's own forests and disaster-prone areas, and read a long inquiry-reader on biomes, sea resources and the Sundarbans mangrove.
