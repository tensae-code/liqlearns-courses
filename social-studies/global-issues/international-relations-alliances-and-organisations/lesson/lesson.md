## Sub lesson 1 — The system of international relations (Система международных отношений) — Grade 11

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 198), teaches: International relations as the whole of political, economic, legal, military and cultural ties between states, peoples and organisations, regulated by international law; their kinds (by sphere, by participants, by the balance of forces — multipolar, bipolar, unipolar — and by tension, from peaceful coexistence to war); and the international organisations, above all the United Nations (founded 24 October 1945) — its aims (peace and security, friendly relations, cooperation), its principles (sovereign equality, peaceful settlement, no threat of force, non-interference) and its six main organs. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
