**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The system of international relations (Система международных отношений) — Grade 11 — international relations as the whole of political, economic, legal, military and cultural ties between states, peoples and organisations, regulated by international law; their kinds (by sphere, by participants, by the balance of forces — multipolar, bipolar, unipolar — and by tension, from peaceful coexistence to war); and the international organisations, above all the United Nations (founded 24 October 1945) — its aims (peace and security, friendly relations, cooperation), its principles (sovereign equality, peaceful settlement, no threat of force, non-interference) and its six main organs (any close wording).**
