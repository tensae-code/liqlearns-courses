# Hands-on — International Relations Alliances and Organisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: International relations as the whole of political, economic, legal, military and cultural ties between states, peoples and organisations, regulated by international law; their kinds (by sphere, by participants, by the balance of forces — multipolar, bipolar, unipolar — and by tension, from peaceful coexistence to war); and the international organisations, above all the United Nations (founded 24 October 1945) — its aims (peace and security, friendly relations, cooperation), its principles (sovereign equality, peaceful settlement, no threat of force, non-interference) and its six main organs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
