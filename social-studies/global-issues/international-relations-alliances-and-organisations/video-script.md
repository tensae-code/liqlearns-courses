# Video script — International Relations Alliances and Organisations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 198), teaches: International relations as the whole of political, economic, legal, military and cultural ties between states, peoples and organisations, regulated by international law; their kinds (by sphere, by participants, by the balance of forces — multipolar, bipolar, unipolar — and by tension, from peaceful coexistence to war); and the international organisations, above all the United Nations (founded 24 October 1945) — its aims (peace and security, friendly relations, cooperation), its principles (sovereign equality, peaceful settlement, no threat of force, non-interference) and its six main organs.
Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics..
