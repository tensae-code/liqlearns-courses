# Video script — Ethiopia's Green Economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This script is written in full — the placements name a full script need for this lesson.

---

This is a case study — one country's answer, told in summary.

Ethiopia's emissions: 150 million tonnes, in the year 2002 Ethiopian calendar.

Left alone, 400 million by 2022 Ethiopian calendar.

So the country chose a green economy that the climate cannot shake.

Cut the gases. Build resilience. Grow green.

Four pillars hold it: farms that yield with fewer emissions.

Clean electricity, for home and for neighbours.

Energy saved in transport, industry, homes and offices.

And forests — protected, restored, pulling carbon from the air.

Middle income by 2017 Ethiopian calendar: that was the aim.
