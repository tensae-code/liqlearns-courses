# Lesson plan — Ethiopia's Green Economy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Ethiopia's Green Economy
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 14 — Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — the fourteenth lesson of the title, after Climate Change — Effects and Responses (TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's answer, as the book's case study tells it: emissions of 150 million tonnes in 2002 E.C. heading for 400 million by 2022 E.C. on business-as-usual — met by a climate-resilient green economy strategy with three great aims and four pillars, aiming at middle-income standing by 2017 E.C. All dates are Ethiopian calendar dates, labelled E.C.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on both climate lessons. This is the Grade 8 book's case study (ውስን ጥናት, a limited study), taught as summary only, as the placement directs. The dates are Ethiopian calendar dates as printed and stay labelled E.C. throughout.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the case's two emission figures with their E.C. dates and the 150% rise.
2. Name the strategy and its middle-income aim by 2017 E.C.
3. State the three great aims.
4. Name the four pillars in order.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads 2002 and 2022 as European years → every date in this case is an Ethiopian calendar date as printed; the lesson labels each one E.C. and so must the learner.
- Swaps pillars 2 and 3 → pillar 2 GENERATES clean electricity (renewable sources, also for neighbours); pillar 3 SAVES energy (efficient technologies in transport, industry, homes, offices).
