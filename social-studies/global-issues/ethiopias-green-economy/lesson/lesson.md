# Ethiopia's Green Economy

## Do it first

If a country's smoke could grow from 150 to 400 in twenty years, what would you build so the growth of the country did not depend on the growth of the smoke? Write your design in three lines before you read Ethiopia's.

## Sub lesson 1 — The numbers and the strategy

The case study's warning: had Ethiopia continued as usual, the **150 million metric tonnes of greenhouse-gas emissions of 2002 E.C.** could have grown to **400 million tonnes of carbon dioxide by 2022 E.C.** — a **150% rise**. (These are **Ethiopian calendar dates, labelled E.C.** — not European years.)

To meet the danger sustainably, Ethiopia designed a **climate-resilient green economy strategy**, aiming to build it and join the **middle-income countries by 2017 E.C.**

[Picture 11415 — the four pillars holding one green roof, 150 to 400 E.C.]

## Sub lesson 2 — Three aims, four pillars

The strategy's **three great aims**: bring **green economic growth and development that the climate cannot shake**; **cut, avoid and reduce greenhouse-gas emissions**; and **strengthen resilience** — the capacity to withstand and check the changes climate change brings, so safety and growth are not stalled.

Its **four pillars**: **(1)** raise crop and livestock productivity while emissions fall, securing food supply and farmers' income; **(2)** generate and expand electricity from renewable sources, for home use and for neighbouring countries; **(3)** spread energy-saving technologies in transport, industry, homes and office buildings, to stop energy waste; **(4)** protect and restore forests so ecosystem services multiply, including drawing carbon out of the air.

## Say it to your companion

Say the two figures with their E.C. dates. Your companion names the four pillars in order; swap, and end by saying 'E.C.' after every date — the habit this lesson builds.
