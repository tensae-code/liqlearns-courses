# Hands-on — Ethiopia's Green Economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pillar mapping
Take one pillar and find its local face: a farm raising yield, a renewable electricity source, an energy-saving practice, a protected forest — within your own woreda if you can. Write the pillar's job and its local example side by side, marking the example as yours.

## E.C. date drill
Write five dates from this lesson and the title as a set, each with E.C. after it, then explain aloud to a partner why the label matters — what mistake it prevents.
