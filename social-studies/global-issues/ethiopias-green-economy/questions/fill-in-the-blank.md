# Ethiopia's Green Economy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The strategy is a climate-resilient ______ economy strategy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pillar 1 raises crop and ______ productivity while emissions fall.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Resilience is the capacity to ______ and check the changes climate change brings.
