# Ethiopia's Green Economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Emissions in 2002 E.C. were —
a) 400 million tonnes  b) 150 million tonnes  c) 15 million tonnes
**Answer: b) 150 million tonnes** — 150 million metric tonnes in 2002 E.C.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On business-as-usual they could reach, by 2022 E.C. —
a) 200 million tonnes  b) 300 million tonnes  c) 400 million tonnes
**Answer: c) 400 million tonnes** — 400 million tonnes — a 150% rise.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The middle-income aim was set for —
a) 2017 E.C.  b) 2030 E.C.  c) 2002 E.C.
**Answer: a) 2017 E.C.** — by 2017 E.C., as printed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pillar 2 is —
a) raising crop productivity  b) generating electricity from renewable sources  c) energy-saving technologies
**Answer: b) generating electricity from renewable sources** — renewable electricity, for home use and for neighbouring countries.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Protecting and restoring forests is pillar —
a) 1  b) 3  c) 4
**Answer: c) 4** — pillar 4.
