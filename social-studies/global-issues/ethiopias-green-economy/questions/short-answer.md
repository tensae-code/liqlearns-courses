# Ethiopia's Green Economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four pillars in order, one line each.
**Answer:** (1) Raise crop and livestock productivity while emissions fall; (2) generate and expand renewable electricity for home and neighbours; (3) spread energy-saving technologies in transport, industry, homes and offices; (4) protect and restore forests.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does this lesson print 'E.C.' after every date?
**Answer:** Because the case study's dates are Ethiopian calendar dates as printed in the book; the label stops them being misread as European years.
