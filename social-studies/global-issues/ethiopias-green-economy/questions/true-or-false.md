# Ethiopia's Green Economy — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The dates 2002 and 2022 in this case are European calendar years.
**Answer: False** — they are Ethiopian calendar dates, labelled E.C. as printed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Strengthening resilience is one of the strategy's three aims.
**Answer: True** — with green growth and cutting emissions.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pillar 3 spreads energy-saving technologies in transport, industry, homes and offices.
**Answer: True** — that is pillar 3's job.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The rise from 150 to 400 million tonnes is a 150% rise.
**Answer: True** — as the book prints it.
