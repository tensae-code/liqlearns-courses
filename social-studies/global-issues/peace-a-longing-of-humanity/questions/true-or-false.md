# Peace a Longing of Humanity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Peace, a longing of humanity — without justice there is no peace: social justice means defending everyone's right to food, a hygienic house, health, education, paid work and respect for their dignity, and peoples' self-determination makes peaceful coexistence possible; defending human rights everywhere is an individual and collective responsibility, and rights begin in small places close to home — the neighbourhood, the school, the workplace (Eleanor Roosevelt); Venezuela's constitution carries the UN's pacifist principles in its own preamble: peaceful coexistence among nations and respect for peoples' self-determination.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Peace spokespersons in the secondary school — summon your school's student spokespersons and constitute the peace spokespersonships (vocerías de paz), to learn to build the culture of peace needed to strengthen citizen coexistence in educational spaces.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Venezuela's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
