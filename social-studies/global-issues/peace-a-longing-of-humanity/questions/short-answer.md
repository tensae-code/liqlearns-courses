# Peace a Longing of Humanity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: La paz, un anhelo de la humanidad — Peace, a longing of humanity — without justice there is no peace: social justice means defending everyone's right to food, a hygienic house, health, education, paid work and respect for their dignity, and peoples' self-determination makes peaceful coexistence possible; defending human rights everywhere is an individual and collective responsibility, and rights begin in small places close to home — the neighbourhood, the school, the workplace (Eleanor Roosevelt); Venezuela's constitution carries the UN's pacifist principles in its own preamble: peaceful coexistence among nations and respect for peoples' self-determination (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vocerías de paz en el liceo — Peace spokespersons in the secondary school — summon your school's student spokespersons and constitute the peace spokespersonships (vocerías de paz), to learn to build the culture of peace needed to strengthen citizen coexistence in educational spaces (any close wording).**
