# Hands-on — Peace a Longing of Humanity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Peace, a longing of humanity — without justice there is no peace: social justice means defending everyone's right to food, a hygienic house, health, education, paid work and respect for their dignity, and peoples' self-determination makes peaceful coexistence possible; defending human rights everywhere is an individual and collective responsibility, and rights begin in small places close to home — the neighbourhood, the school, the workplace (Eleanor Roosevelt); Venezuela's constitution carries the UN's pacifist principles in its own preamble: peaceful coexistence among nations and respect for peoples' self-determination. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Peace spokespersons in the secondary school — summon your school's student spokespersons and constitute the peace spokespersonships (vocerías de paz), to learn to build the culture of peace needed to strengthen citizen coexistence in educational spaces. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
