## Sub lesson 2 — Syria's way — the book's own teaching way

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 8 Geography G8): Grade 8 leaves the Arab world and tours the "new" continents in a fixed six-lesson pattern (location and surface, natural environments, settlement/migration, population and cities, resources and economy, one big country study — USA, Brazil, Australia), gives Antarctica a whole unit as a land of ice and science, and closes with a unit of contemporary global challenges (water, food, pollution, warming, population, desertification). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
