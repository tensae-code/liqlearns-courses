# Video script — Living Things Depend on Each Other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The natural environment is not a pile of separate things: it is ecosystems, where living things depend on each other. The reader's bush ecosystem makes it plain — fruit bats live from the trees that feed them, and the trees' life is bound up with the bats; change one part and every part feels it.

This part stands on the teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. Its notes for use of Book 3 set out the ecological, socio-cultural and economic pillars of living sustainably with the land, and build activities on the bat ecosystem — interdependence is taught by doing, around the same bats and trees.
