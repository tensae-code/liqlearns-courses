# Hands-on — Political Regions And The State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 9 The meaning and concept of a political region (ناحیه سیاسی): organising and managing space (the school as the worked example); political regions at four levels — sub-national, national, transnational (regional, several countries under one regional management) and special; every political region has three pillars: territory and geographical space, the human structure, and the management system, with its core; political geography the discipline that studies them; its pioneers in ancient Greece — Plato, Aristotle, Herodotus and Strabo. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
