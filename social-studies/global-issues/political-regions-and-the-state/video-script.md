Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 116), teaches: Lesson 9 The meaning and concept of a political region (ناحیه سیاسی): organising and managing space (the school as the worked example); political regions at four levels — sub-national, national, transnational (regional, several countries under one regional management) and special; every political region has three pillars: territory and geographical space, the human structure, and the management system, with its core; political geography the discipline that studies them; its pioneers in ancient Greece — Plato, Aristotle, Herodotus and Strabo.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).
