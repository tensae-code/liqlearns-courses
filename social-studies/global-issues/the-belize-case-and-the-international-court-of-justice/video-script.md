Guatemala Grade 6 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 198–200, teaches: Guatemala's territorial, insular and maritime differendum with Belize is to be resolved by an international court of experts from other countries, deciding under international law; in April 2018 the government called a popular consultation in which Guatemalan citizens voted on whether to submit the case to the International Court of Justice.

Guatemala's own way: the sixth-grade book widens the lens — democracy is traced from Athens to Guatemala's own authoritarian past, and citizenship ends at the world: Guatemala's Belize claim put to its own citizens in the 2018 popular consultation on the International Court of Justice.
