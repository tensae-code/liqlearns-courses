# Hands-on — The Belize Case and the International Court of Justice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 4, the territorial-differendum lessons
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 6 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 198–200, teaches: Guatemala's territorial, insular and maritime differendum with Belize is to be resolved by an international court of experts from other countries, deciding under international law; in April 2018 the government called a popular consultation in which Guatemalan citizens voted on whether to submit the case to the International Court of Justice. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 8) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the sixth-grade book widens the lens — democracy is traced from Athens to Guatemala's own authoritarian past, and citizenship ends at the world: Guatemala's Belize claim put to its own citizens in the 2018 popular consultation on the International Court of Justice.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
