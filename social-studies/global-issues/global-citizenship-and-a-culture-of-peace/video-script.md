Niue's Years 5–8 ESD Teacher Guide (printed pp. 32–33), teaches: Niue's curriculum aims: peace, rights and global citizenship — teaches: TEACHER — Niue's Department of Education works toward SDG 4.7: all learners acquiring the knowledge and skills for sustainable lifestyles, human rights, gender equality, a culture of peace and non-violence, global citizenship and appreciation of cultural diversity; in the 2020 primary curriculum plan the ESD themes sit under 'Respect for all creation' and widen term by term — respect for our village and self, our nation, our Pacific neighbours, our world.

Niue's own way: The guide's Niue notes define sustainability the Niuean way — a person is part of the community, practising reciprocity, sharing and caring; knowledge passes father-to-son and mother-to-daughter inside the family; and the school's respect themes widen in rings from village and self, to nation, to Pacific neighbours, to the world.
