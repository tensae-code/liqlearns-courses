Niue's own way (teacher guide — evidence): The guide's Niue notes define sustainability the Niuean way — a person is part of the community, practising reciprocity, sharing and caring; knowledge passes father-to-son and mother-to-daughter inside the family; and the school's respect themes widen in rings from village and self, to nation, to Pacific neighbours, to the world.
