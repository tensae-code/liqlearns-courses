# Why the book ends on peace — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'peace — the curriculum's deliberate closing choice' is ______.
**Answer: last word**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'government by the people's choice, the coda's second peace-grown good' is ______.
**Answer: democratisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'a conclusion the evidence supports, as the coda is' is ______.
**Answer: judgement**
