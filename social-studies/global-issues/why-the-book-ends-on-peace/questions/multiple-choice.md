# Why the book ends on peace — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is coda?
a) a closing passage that states a work's final meaning
b) last word
c) moral
**Answer: a) a closing passage that states a work's final meaning**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is precondition?
a) democratisation
b) industrialisation
c) what must exist first — peace, before industry and democracy grow
**Answer: c) what must exist first — peace, before industry and democracy grow**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is enlightened despot (teacher guide — evidence)?
a) the guide's verdict on Napoleon — reforming yet ruling without consent
