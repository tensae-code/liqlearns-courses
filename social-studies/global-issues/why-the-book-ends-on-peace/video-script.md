# Video script — Why the book ends on peace

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — A book of wars ends on peace. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 book is full of wars — invasions, revolutions, civil wars. Watch the names appear one by one: coda, moral, last word. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Peace as a precondition. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The coda's argument is practical, not soft: peace is the precondition of industrialisation and democratisation — factories, schools and parliaments grow where fields are not burned and years are not stolen by war.

Every unit of this course proves it backwards: count what war cost Aksum's trade, the highlands under Gragn, Africa under the slave trades' gun cycle.. Watch the names appear one by one: precondition, industrialisation, democratisation. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Carrying the coda forward. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The teacher guide's last word on Napoleon matches: even the age's greatest conqueror left France weakened where his reforms, in peace, might have strengthened her. Watch the names appear one by one: enlightened despot (teacher guide — evidence), arithmetic, judgement. This is the part of the story to hold.
