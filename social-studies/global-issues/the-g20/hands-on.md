# Hands-on — The G20

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: after the financial crises of the 1990s the Group of Eight decided in 1999 to found a new forum, the G20, adding eleven states — Saudi Arabia, Indonesia, Turkey, South Africa, China, South Korea, Argentina, Brazil, India, Mexico and Australia — plus the European Union, its finance ministers meeting that year to study the situation and reach common decisions and policies for world economic balance and financial reform; Saudi Arabia's choice among the twenty as proof of its economic weight; the rotating presidency, chosen from different regions, which runs a temporary secretariat to coordinate the meetings and prepare reports; the group's more than 90% of world gross product and 80% of world trade; and the Kingdom's hosting of the 1442H/2020 Riyadh summit. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
