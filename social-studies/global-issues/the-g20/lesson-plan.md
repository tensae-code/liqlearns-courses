# Lesson plan — The G20

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** The G20
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Saudi Arabia, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** after the financial crises of the 1990s the Group of Eight decided in 1999 to found a new forum, the G20, adding eleven states — Saudi Arabia, Indonesia, Turkey, South Africa, China, South Korea, Argentina, Brazil, India, Mexico and Australia — plus the European Union, its finance ministers meeting that year to study the situation and reach common decisions and policies for world economic balance and financial reform; Saudi Arabia's choice among the twenty as proof of its economic weight; the rotating presidency, chosen from different regions, which runs a temporary secretariat to coordinate the meetings and prepare reports; the group's more than 90% of world gross product and 80% of world trade; and the Kingdom's hosting of the 1442H/2020 Riyadh summit — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
