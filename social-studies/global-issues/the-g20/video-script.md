Saudi Arabia Grade 10 Social Studies (Secondary Tracks, First Common Year) (printed pp. 118–121), teaches: After the financial crises of the 1990s the Group of Eight decided in 1999 to found a new forum, the G20, adding eleven states — Saudi Arabia, Indonesia, Turkey, South Africa, China, South Korea, Argentina, Brazil, India, Mexico and Australia — plus the European Union, its finance ministers meeting that year to study the situation and reach common decisions and policies for world economic balance and financial reform; Saudi Arabia's choice among the twenty as proof of its economic weight; the rotating presidency, chosen from different regions, which runs a temporary secretariat to coordinate the meetings and prepare reports; the group's more than 90% of world gross product and 80% of world trade; and the Kingdom's hosting of the 1442H/2020 Riyadh summit.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20.
