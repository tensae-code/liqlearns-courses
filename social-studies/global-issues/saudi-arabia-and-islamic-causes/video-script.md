Saudi Arabia Grade 10 Social Studies (Secondary Tracks, First Common Year) (printed pp. 42–43), teaches: The Kingdom undertakes its Islamic responsibility — gathering the Muslims' word, solving their causes, strengthening their standing and supporting them economically, politically and culturally; the cause of Afghanistan (the Soviet invasion of 1400H/1979, the Afghan people's resistance, and the Kingdom's support, King Salman receiving the Afghan delegation at the Jeddah conference of Muslim scholars in 1438H and affirming Afghanistan's unity, territorial safety and its people's right to live in security and peace) and the cause of Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Balkan Muslim land Islam entered in the eighth Hijri century.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20.
