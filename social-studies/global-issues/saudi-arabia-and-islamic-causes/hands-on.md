# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia and Islamic Causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Kingdom undertakes its Islamic responsibility — gathering the Muslims' word, solving their causes, strengthening their standing and supporting them economically, politically and culturally; the cause of Afghanistan (the Soviet invasion of 1400H/1979, the Afghan people's resistance, and the Kingdom's support, King Salman receiving the Afghan delegation at the Jeddah conference of Muslim scholars in 1438H and affirming Afghanistan's unity, territorial safety and its people's right to live in security and peace) and the cause of Bosnia and Herzegovina, the Balkan Muslim land Islam entered in the eighth Hijri century. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
