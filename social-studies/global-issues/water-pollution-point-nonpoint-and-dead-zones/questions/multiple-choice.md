# Water pollution — point, nonpoint and dead zones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Point-source pollution comes from…
a) one identifiable source  b) everywhere at once  c) the rain only
**Answer: a) one identifiable source**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nonpoint-source pollution is…
a) from a single pipe  b) diffuse, spread over an area  c) impossible
**Answer: b) diffuse, spread over an area**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Heavy metals arrive in…
a) industrial and municipal wastewater  b) clean springs  c) snow only
**Answer: a) industrial and municipal wastewater**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dead zones are areas of…
a) too much oxygen  b) very low oxygen  c) no water
**Answer: b) very low oxygen**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's dead-zone map uses oxygen at or below…
a) 2 mg/l  b) 20 mg/l  c) 200 mg/l
**Answer: a) 2 mg/l**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Dead zones form where pollution meets…
a) ice  b) nutrient overload and warming  c) deserts
**Answer: b) nutrient overload and warming**
