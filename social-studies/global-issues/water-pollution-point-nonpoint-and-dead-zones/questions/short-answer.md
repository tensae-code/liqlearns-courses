# Water pollution — point, nonpoint and dead zones — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Separate point from nonpoint in one line.
**Answer:** Point = one identifiable source; nonpoint = diffuse sources over an area.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three ingredients make a dead zone?
**Answer:** Pollution, nutrient overload and warming.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two pollutant loads named in the lesson.
**Answer:** Industrial/municipal wastewater chemicals and heavy metals (or microorganisms).
