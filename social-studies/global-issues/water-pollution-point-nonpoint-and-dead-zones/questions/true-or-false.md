# Water pollution — point, nonpoint and dead zones — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nonpoint pollution can be managed by inspecting one pipe.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dead-zone water cannot support the life it should.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Microorganisms can be water pollutants.
**Answer: True**
