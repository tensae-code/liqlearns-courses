# Lesson plan — Conflict and Cooperation in International Society

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Conflict and Cooperation in International Society
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (South Korea, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** VI.2 국제 사회의 모습과 갈등·협력, VIII.2-01 국제 갈등과 협력 및 국제 사회의 행위 주체, 세계화와 평화 2 국제 사회의 모습과 평화를 위한 노력, Ⅵ 국제 사회와 국제 정치 (국제 사회의 특성과 주체), Ⅵ 국제 사회와 국제 정치 — teaches: the face of international society — conflict and cooperation between states and other actors; international conflict and cooperation, and the actors of international society; the face of international society and the efforts made for peace in it; international society and its politics — the unit wrap-up names the logic of power, multinational firms and cooperation, under the principle of sovereign equality; international society and international politics — as South Korea's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — the face of international society — conflict and cooperation between states and other actors.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
