# Video script — Population pressure on resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

World population was about 7.7 billion in 2019, projected at 8.1 billion in 2025 and 9.6 billion by 2050, over three-quarters in the developing world. Food production must rise at least 50% in three decades — from land already overexploited.

Population pressure shrinks holdings below viability — the falling land–man ratio; disguised unemployment grows; wages fall; saving, investment and improved technique all stall; food imports strain the balance of payments; semi-arid land is over-cultivated into desertification; and migrants carry the pressure into polluted cities.

Slower population growth eases every link. And environmental policy in countries like Ethiopia succeeds only if it first addresses poverty, landlessness and insecure tenure, credit and information — the pressures underneath the population numbers.
