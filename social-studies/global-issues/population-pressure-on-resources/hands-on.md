# Hands-on — Population pressure on resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three panels
Draw one plot divided among one, then three, then nine heirs across three panels. Under the last panel, write the holding's viability verdict and the first chain-link it triggers.

## Ease a link
Pick one link of the chain (wages, saving, food imports, desertification, migration) and write the policy that eases it, then name the link upstream of yours that makes your policy hard.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
