# Population pressure on resources — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The numbers" in your own words.
**Answer:** World population: 7.7 billion in 2019, projected 9.6 billion by 2050; food production must rise at least 50%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The chain for farm economies" in your own words.
**Answer:** Shrinking holdings bring disguised unemployment, falling wages, stalled investment, import strain and desertification.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Where policy must start" in your own words.
**Answer:** Environmental policy succeeds only if it first addresses poverty, landlessness, insecure tenure, credit and information.
