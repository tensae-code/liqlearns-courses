# Population pressure on resources — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** World population: 7.7 billion in 2019, projected 9.6 billion by 2050; food production must rise at least 50%.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Shrinking holdings bring disguised unemployment, falling wages, stalled investment, import strain and desertification.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Environmental policy succeeds only if it first addresses poverty, landlessness, insecure tenure, credit and information.
**Answer: True**
