# Hands-on — Refugees Causes Problems and Solutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: why people are displaced (war, persecution, disaster), the problems refugees face, and solutions including peaceful resolution of conflict. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
