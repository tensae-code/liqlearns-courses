# Hands-on — Commonwealth Week and the Commonwealth Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Commonwealth Week each March — countries once ruled by England, the Queen as Head of the Commonwealth, and school exhibitions of the foods, clothing and books of Commonwealth countries. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
