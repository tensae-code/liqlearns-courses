# Video script — Symbols of the East African Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Uganda Excel P6 SST (printed p. 17), teaches: The eac flag, anthem, constitution, motto and emblem; the anthem sung at assemblies, official ceremonies and parliamentary sessions, promoting regional unity.

Uganda Shine P6 SST (printed p. 20), teaches: The eac flag (six colours, blue for lake victoria, the rest taken from member states' flags) and where it is raised; the motto “one people one destiny”; and the eac anthem, a hybrid song from the three founding countries' composers.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Excel P6 SST): P6 looks outward and inward at once — the East African Community's own organs and symbols beside the member states' flags, then the citizen: what citizenship is, the child's rights, what violating rights looks like (mob justice named plainly), and how elections actually run.
Uganda's own way (Uganda Shine P6 SST): a second publisher teaches the same P6 civics — the EAC's organs (Secretariat at Arusha, Court of Justice) and symbols, then each member state's flag and arms and why symbols matter. PARTIAL copy, as filed: only the held span (printed pp. 1–84) is read.
