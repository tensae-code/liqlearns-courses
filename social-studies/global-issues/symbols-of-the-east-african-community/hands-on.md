# Hands-on — Symbols of the East African Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the EAC flag, anthem, constitution, motto and emblem; the anthem sung at assemblies, official ceremonies and parliamentary sessions, promoting regional unity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Uganda's way
Say how Uganda's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
