# Hands-on — Are Humans Rational — and the Environment?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Test the claim
Write one action by humans toward nature that looks rational and one that does not, as Folha 14 asks: Os seres humanos são racionais. Será?

## Argue the Folha's question
Write a short answer, in your own words, to whether human beings are rational in how they treat nature, giving your evidence.
