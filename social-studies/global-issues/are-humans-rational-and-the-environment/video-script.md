# Video script — Are Humans Rational — and the Environment?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Folha 14 states Os seres humanos são racionais. Será? / Are human beings rational. Really? The statement is given and then doubted in the same breath — the learner must test the claim, not repeat it.

The Folha's subject is humans and nature: the claim of rationality is tested against how humans actually treat nature. That test — what rational beings would do, set against what is done to nature — is this lesson's one thing.
