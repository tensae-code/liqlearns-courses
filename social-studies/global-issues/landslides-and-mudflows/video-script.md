# Video script — Landslides And Mudflows

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 94), teaches: Lesson 5: Landslides (زمین‌لغزش) on fairly steep slopes — huge volumes of rock, sand and mud moving down; volcanic eruptions laying thick ash on slopes, then rain saturating it into very fluid mudflows; the slopes most prone to sliding (steep, covered in loose eroded sediment); Iran's many landslide-dammed lakes in mountain valleys (Lake Valasht; the Alamut slide in Qazvin).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
