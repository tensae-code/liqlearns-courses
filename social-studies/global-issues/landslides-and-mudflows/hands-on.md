# Hands-on — Landslides And Mudflows

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 5: Landslides (زمین‌لغزش) on fairly steep slopes — huge volumes of rock, sand and mud moving down; volcanic eruptions laying thick ash on slopes, then rain saturating it into very fluid mudflows; the slopes most prone to sliding (steep, covered in loose eroded sediment); Iran's many landslide-dammed lakes in mountain valleys (Lake Valasht; the Alamut slide in Qazvin). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
