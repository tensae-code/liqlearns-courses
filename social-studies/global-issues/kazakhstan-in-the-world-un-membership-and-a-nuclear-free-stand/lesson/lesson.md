## Sub lesson 1 — Kazakhstan in the World UN Membership and a Nuclear Free Stand

Kazakhstan Grade 4 Poznanie mira (110/88) (printed p. 114), teaches: foreign policy follows from a country's geographic position and surroundings, and Kazakhstan in the centre of Eurasia builds relations first with its nearest neighbours — the priority circles run Russia; Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan and the CIS; China, Turkey, India and Asia; then the USA and Europe (Germany, France, England); the country is an active member of 70 international organisations and joined the United Nations on 2 March 1992; it is valued for striving for a world without nuclear weapons — the Semipalatinsk test site closed by decree on 29 August 1991, the 2016 Manifesto "The World. The 21st Century", 29 August made the International Day against Nuclear Tests — and hosted EXPO-2017 on the energy of the future. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the books' own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 5): Grade 4 is where the primary course turns civic in earnest — the republic's own map of 14 oblasts and three cities of republican significance, an elected school council drawn as a working model of the state, the Constitution's rights quoted article by article, and the country's face to the world built from the UN seat, the closed Semipalatinsk test site and EXPO-2017. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
