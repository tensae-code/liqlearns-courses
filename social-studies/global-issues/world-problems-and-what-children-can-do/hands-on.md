# Hands-on — World Problems and What Children Can Do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: TEACHER: the world's shared problems opened to ten-year-olds — disease, poverty, destruction of the earth, and people harming others — each taken in turn in group discussion with the question "what can we do?", as the doorway into the United Nations lessons that follow. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
