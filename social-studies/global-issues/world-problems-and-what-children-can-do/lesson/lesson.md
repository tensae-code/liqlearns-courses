## Sub lesson 1 — World Problems and What Children Can Do

Bhutan Class V Social Studies Teachers' Manual 2019, printed p. 74, teaches: TEACHER: the world's shared problems opened to ten-year-olds — disease, poverty, destruction of the earth, and people harming others — each taken in turn in group discussion with the question "what can we do?", as the doorway into the United Nations lessons that follow. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bhutan's way — the book's own teaching way

Bhutan's own way: the world is brought to the village school through the agencies the child can already see — the UN is introduced not from a chart of organs but from UNICEF's materials in the child's own school, the WFP food the school serves and the WHO's work in the village health unit, and only then by name and function. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
