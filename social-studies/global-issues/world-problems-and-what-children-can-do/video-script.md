# Video script — World Problems and What Children Can Do

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bhutan Class V Social Studies Teachers' Manual 2019, printed p. 74, teaches: TEACHER: the world's shared problems opened to ten-year-olds — disease, poverty, destruction of the earth, and people harming others — each taken in turn in group discussion with the question "what can we do?", as the doorway into the United Nations lessons that follow.

Bhutan's own way: the world is brought to the village school through the agencies the child can already see — the UN is introduced not from a chart of organs but from UNICEF's materials in the child's own school, the WFP food the school serves and the WHO's work in the village health unit, and only then by name and function.
