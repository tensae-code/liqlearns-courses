# Video script — Arab Integration and Its Organisations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 7 Term 2 (printed pp. 81–82), teaches: Arab integration — organised cooperation among the Arab states in all fields for shared interests and balanced development — why it matters (economic strength, a stronger political position, a rooted cultural identity), its fields, and its organisations: the Arab League (Cairo), the Arab Fund for Economic and Social Development (Kuwait), ALECSO (Tunis), the Arab Organization for Agricultural Development (Khartoum) — and the joint Kuwait–Oman Duqm refinery as integration at work.

Kuwait's own way: Grade 7 Term 2 teaches the pupil to read their region's troubles whole — political (occupation, separatism, nuclear risk), social (addiction, rumours, bullying) — and then to answer them with plans: a national vision with traits and shared Arab goals, Kuwait's own 2035 vision, and Arab integration through named organisations; the values unit grounds it at home — the grandmother who runs the family's learning, and the neighbour who feeds the absent diver's family.
