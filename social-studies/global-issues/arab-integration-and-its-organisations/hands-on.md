# Hands-on — Arab Integration and Its Organisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Arab integration — organised cooperation among the Arab states in all fields for shared interests and balanced development — why it matters (economic strength, a stronger political position, a rooted cultural identity), its fields, and its organisations: the Arab League (Cairo), the Arab Fund for Economic and Social Development (Kuwait), ALECSO (Tunis), the Arab Organization for Agricultural Development (Khartoum) — and the joint Kuwait–Oman Duqm refinery as integration at work. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
