# Honduras Foreign Relations and International Law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: public international law studies and regulates the behaviour of states and other international subjects to guarantee peace and international cooperation; Honduras's foreign policy is directed from the Secretariat of Foreign Relations (Cancillería); its borders were fixed by international awards — King Alfonso XIII's 1906 award for the Nicaragua line and the Hughes tribunal award for Guatemala — and the country has ratified treaties and conventions with international organisms to protect its citizens.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: honduras's own foreign-relations history — the Federation's official dissolution in 1839 made each province an individual State, and the 1839 Constitution (Art. 51) ordered the creation of the Ministry of Relations for all political affairs abroad and at home; the first official diplomatic relations were with Great Britain in the 19th century, through Consul Frederick Chatfield; the 1969 war with El Salvador did not end the border dispute — the International Court of Justice at The Hague, seized of it from 1986, ruled in Honduras's favour, granting two-thirds of the six disputed territories and fixing a border of some 420 km², and the two States disputed Isla Conejo in the Gulf of Fonseca again in 2014.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: honduras's presence on the international political stage is tied to Central America's — the isthmus, a natural bridge between North and South America, has drawn the great powers' attention and intervention through history; Honduras today holds diplomatic relations with some 112 countries (the sequence lists them, from Canada and Germany to the Holy See and Kosovo), maintained through its embassies to solidify relations that benefit the country through economy, industry, education, health, security and job generation — the rest of the sequence is Economics (sectors, world economy), placed by the Economics strand.
**Answer: True**
