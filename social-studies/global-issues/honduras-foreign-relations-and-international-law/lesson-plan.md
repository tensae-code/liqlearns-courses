# Lesson plan — Honduras Foreign Relations and International Law

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Global Issues · **Lesson:** Honduras Foreign Relations and International Law
**Place in the curriculum:** Global Issues (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Honduras, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Honduras Foreign Relations and International Law — public international law studies and regulates the behaviour of states and other international subjects to guarantee peace and international cooperation; Honduras's foreign policy is directed from the Secretariat of Foreign Relations (Cancillería); its borders were fixed by international awards — King Alfonso XIII's 1906 award for the Nicaragua line and the Hughes tribunal award for Guatemala — and the country has ratified treaties and conventions with international organisms to protect its citizens — as Honduras's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Honduras Foreign Relations and International Law — public international law studies and regulates the behaviour of states and other international subjects to guarantee peace and international cooperation; Honduras's foreign policy is directed from the Secretariat of Foreign Relations (Cancillería); its borders were fixed by international awards — King Alfonso XIII's 1906 award for the Nicaragua line and the Hughes tribunal award for Guatemala — and the country has ratified treaties and conventions with international organisms to protect its citizens.
