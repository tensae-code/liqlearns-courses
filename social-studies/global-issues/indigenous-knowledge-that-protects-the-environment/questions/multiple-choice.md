# Indigenous knowledge that protects the environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is…
a) book knowledge  b) locally held, experience-tested, transmitted across generations  c) imported technique
**Answer: b) locally held, experience-tested, transmitted across generations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It is best described as…
a) a museum piece  b) a working environmental technology  c) a superstition
**Answer: b) a working environmental technology**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its examination is…
a) a written test  b) generations of yearly use  c) a laboratory
**Answer: b) generations of yearly use**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's evidence includes…
a) terraced hillsides and local protective rules  b) only treaties  c) only laboratories
**Answer: a) terraced hillsides and local protective rules**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson for the unit is that some solutions are…
a) inherited, not only invented  b) useless  c) secret
**Answer: a) inherited, not only invented**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Conservation in Ethiopia began with…
a) modern environmentalism  b) long-standing local practice  c) the United Nations
**Answer: b) long-standing local practice**
