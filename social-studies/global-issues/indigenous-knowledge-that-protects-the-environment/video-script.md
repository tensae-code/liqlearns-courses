# Video script — Indigenous knowledge that protects the environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Indigenous knowledge is locally held, experience-tested knowledge transmitted across generations — and it is a working environmental technology, not a museum piece. The section sets out indigenous knowledge for environmental issues generally: reading land, water, seasons and species through long local experience, and keeping the practices that experience proved. The section's home evidence is Ethiopia's own environmentally friendly practices — the terraced hillsides, closures, rotations and local rules by which communities protected land and water for generations. The lesson they teach the whole unit: solutions to sustainability problems are not only invented in laboratories and treaties; some are inherited, and the wise course keeps and learns from them.
