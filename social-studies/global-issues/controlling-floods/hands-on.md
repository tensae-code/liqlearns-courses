# Hands-on — Controlling Floods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Measures of flood control in Pakistan: flood control department, cemented river banks, protective bridges, relief camps, better forecasting, dams, and forests planted along rivers and roads. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
