Saudi Arabia Grade 10 Social Studies (Secondary Tracks, First Common Year) (printed pp. 38–41), teaches: The Kingdom's distinguished place among the Arab states by its religious, political and economic weight, and its founding membership of the League of Arab States and of the Gulf Cooperation Council; its early work for Arab unity under King Abdulaziz (the Arab brotherhood treaties, and the Saudi delegation's memorandum to the Alexandria Protocol committee carrying the King's proposals as guarantees of a unity free of personal projects and division); and King Khalid's role in founding the GCC — calling the Gulf states to close ranks against the surrounding dangers at the Taif summit, and joining the Council's first meeting in Abu Dhabi on 21 Rajab 1401H/1981, where it was decided that Riyadh host the Council's General Secretariat for the Kingdom's strategic position among the Gulf states and the Islamic world.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20.
