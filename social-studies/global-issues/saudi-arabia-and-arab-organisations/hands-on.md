# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia and Arab Organisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Kingdom's distinguished place among the Arab states by its religious, political and economic weight, and its founding membership of the League of Arab States and of the Gulf Cooperation Council; its early work for Arab unity under King Abdulaziz (the Arab brotherhood treaties, and the Saudi delegation's memorandum to the Alexandria Protocol committee carrying the King's proposals as guarantees of a unity free of personal projects and division); and King Khalid's role in founding the GCC — calling the Gulf states to close ranks against the surrounding dangers at the Taif summit, and joining the Council's first meeting in Abu Dhabi on 21 Rajab 1401H/1981, where it was decided that Riyadh host the Council's General Secretariat for the Kingdom's strategic position among the Gulf states and the Islamic world. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
