# Video script — Current Events and Their Impact

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal Grade 7 Social Studies and Human Value Education (printed p. 178), teaches: What is happening in the world now.

Nepal Grade 9 Social Studies (printed p. 232), teaches: Today's events and what they do to us.

Nepal Grade 10 Social Studies (printed p. 277), teaches: Reading the events of one's own time as a citizen.

Nepal's own way: Grade 7 completes the state — elements of the state, democratic government, our constitution, human and women's rights, the provincial government's functions and the security agencies — then turns outward: the United Nations, the proper use of social media, and the world's current events close the book. Grade 9 gives civic awareness its fullest form yet — constitution from first introduction through the constituent assembly, fundamental rights, citizen identity and duties, good governance and democratic conduct — and ends the values unit with a culture of peace. the Grade 10 book is organised as the citizen's widening world — society and identity, social values, social problems, then a full civic-awareness unit on Nepal's own federal legislature, executive and judiciary, closing with Nepal's foreign policy and the UN.
