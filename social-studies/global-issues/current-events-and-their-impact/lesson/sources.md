**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Nepal).**

Nepal's own way (Book 9): Grade 7 completes the state — elements of the state, democratic government, our constitution, human and women's rights, the provincial government's functions and the security agencies — then turns outward: the United Nations, the proper use of social media, and the world's current events close the book.

Nepal's own way (Book 13): Grade 9 gives civic awareness its fullest form yet — constitution from first introduction through the constituent assembly, fundamental rights, citizen identity and duties, good governance and democratic conduct — and ends the values unit with a culture of peace.

Nepal's own way (Book 15): the Grade 10 book is organised as the citizen's widening world — society and identity, social values, social problems, then a full civic-awareness unit on Nepal's own federal legislature, executive and judiciary, closing with Nepal's foreign policy and the UN.
