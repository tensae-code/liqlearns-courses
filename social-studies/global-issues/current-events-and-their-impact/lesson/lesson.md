## Sub lesson 4 — Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way

Nepal's own way: Grade 7 completes the state — elements of the state, democratic government, our constitution, human and women's rights, the provincial government's functions and the security agencies — then turns outward: the United Nations, the proper use of social media, and the world's current events close the book. Grade 9 gives civic awareness its fullest form yet — constitution from first introduction through the constituent assembly, fundamental rights, citizen identity and duties, good governance and democratic conduct — and ends the values unit with a culture of peace. the Grade 10 book is organised as the citizen's widening world — society and identity, social values, social problems, then a full civic-awareness unit on Nepal's own federal legislature, executive and judiciary, closing with Nepal's foreign policy and the UN. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
