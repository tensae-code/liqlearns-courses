## Sub lesson 2 — Geography of Belarus's foreign trade; Belarusian-Russian union programmes

Theme IX §§56–57 follows with the geography of Belarus's foreign trade — what it trades and with whom — and the Belarusian-Russian union programmes that join the two countries' work.
