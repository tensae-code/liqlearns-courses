# Hands-on — What Holds A Country Back

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Problems of underdeveloped countries: economic problems (foreign loans and interest, low income, dependence on farming), social problems (illiteracy, population growth, poor health) and political problems; the geographical factors (natural and human resources) behind development. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
