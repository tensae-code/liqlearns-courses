## Sub lesson 1 — What Holds A Country Back

Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 8 Geography 8* (printed pp. 73–75), teaches: Problems of underdeveloped countries: economic problems (foreign loans and interest, low income, dependence on farming), social problems (illiteracy, population growth, poor health) and political problems; the geographical factors (natural and human resources) behind development. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pakistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 8 Geography 8): the Urdu-medium twin of the English Geography course — deep map-and-graph craft first (dot and choropleth maps of Pakistan itself), then landform agents, oceans, a full Pakistan-anchored disaster chapter, the world's natural regions, Pakistan's own climate, its four neighbouring regions and strategic place, underdevelopment with Pakistan's own causes, and modern techniques down to Pak Sat-1.
