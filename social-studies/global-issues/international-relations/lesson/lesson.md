Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what international relations are — with globalisation as its key term — their forms, and why they matter in a constantly changing world — as Kuwait's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The importance of international relations and their role in the contemporary world

Kuwait Grade 9 Term 2 (printed pp. 32–34), teaches: What international relations are — with globalisation as its key term — their forms, and why they matter in a constantly changing world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Social and cultural international relations

Kuwait Grade 9 Term 2 (printed pp. 39–41), teaches: What cultural relations between states are and how they show themselves — with the State of Kuwait as the model of international social and cultural cooperation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way

Kuwait's own way: the last Kuwaiti schoolbook widens citizenship to the planet — a Kuwaiti pupil is taught global citizenship with Kuwaiti evidence (women's rights under Kuwait's own practice, Kuwait as the model of cultural cooperation, a WHO prize named for its late Amir), then the machinery of world order — international relations, world organisations, the UN — and finally the future's challenges, where identity and bullying sit beside poverty as things a citizen must face. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
