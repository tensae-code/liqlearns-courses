# Video script — International Relations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 9 Term 2 (printed pp. 32–34), teaches: What international relations are — with globalisation as its key term — their forms, and why they matter in a constantly changing world.

Kuwait Grade 9 Term 2 (printed pp. 39–41), teaches: What cultural relations between states are and how they show themselves — with the State of Kuwait as the model of international social and cultural cooperation.

Kuwait's own way: the last Kuwaiti schoolbook widens citizenship to the planet — a Kuwaiti pupil is taught global citizenship with Kuwaiti evidence (women's rights under Kuwait's own practice, Kuwait as the model of cultural cooperation, a WHO prize named for its late Amir), then the machinery of world order — international relations, world organisations, the UN — and finally the future's challenges, where identity and bullying sit beside poverty as things a citizen must face.
