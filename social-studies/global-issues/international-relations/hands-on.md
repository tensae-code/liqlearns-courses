# Hands-on — International Relations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What international relations are — with globalisation as its key term — their forms, and why they matter in a constantly changing world. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What cultural relations between states are and how they show themselves — with the State of Kuwait as the model of international social and cultural cooperation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
