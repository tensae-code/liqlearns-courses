# Video script — Major Risks Around Us — Mapping Local Hazards

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti's Grade 7 risk sequence opens in the pupils' own locality: a field survey of the hazards around them, sorted into natural risks and technological risks, and drawn into a local risk map — the risks are found outside first, then mapped.

This is a teacher sequence, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Haiti's Projet Nectar sequence risk is taught as geography first: before any definition is given, the class surveys and maps the hazards of its own environment, so the map of risk begins with the ground the pupils walk every day.
