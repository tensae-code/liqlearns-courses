# Hands-on — Major Risks Around Us — Mapping Local Hazards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Survey your corner
Walk the ground around your school or home in your mind and list every hazard you pass. Sort each one: natural or technological.

## Draw the risk map
Draw a simple map of that same ground and mark each hazard from your list on it with a symbol. Add a legend so someone else can read it.
