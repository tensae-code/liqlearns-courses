# Hands-on — Factors Of National Power

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 11: The factors and sources of national power (عوامل و سرچشمه‌های قدرت ملی) in nine factors by importance — geographical factors written first, the most important makers of national power; among them the shape of countries (compact, square- or circle-like, versus elongated) and the country's energy and mineral resources (oil and gas; metal ores — copper, zinc, iron — used in industry and military affairs). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
