## Sub lesson 1 — Factors Of National Power

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 149), teaches: Lesson 11: The factors and sources of national power (عوامل و سرچشمه‌های قدرت ملی) in nine factors by importance — geographical factors written first, the most important makers of national power; among them the shape of countries (compact, square- or circle-like, versus elongated) and the country's energy and mineral resources (oil and gas; metal ores — copper, zinc, iron — used in industry and military affairs). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
