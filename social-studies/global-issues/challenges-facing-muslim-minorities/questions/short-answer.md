**Answer: The challenges facing Muslim minorities — the situation of Muslim minorities in non-Muslim states — the pressures and discrimination they face and the Islamic world's concern for them (any close wording).**

**Answer: Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 8 Term 2 frames the whole Islamic world as one body of challenges and answers — wars, contested borders and besieged minorities on one side; on the other, an organisation with principles (self-determination, human rights, rule of law) and Kuwait itself cast as the helper-state — its Fund, its charities, its Red Crescent — while the values unit keeps the same virtues at home scale: unity, altruism, rushing to help, the mother's home clinic (any close wording).**
