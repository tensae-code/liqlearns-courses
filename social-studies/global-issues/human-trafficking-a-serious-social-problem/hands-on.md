# Hands-on — Human Trafficking a Serious Social Problem

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tema 25 Trata de seres humanos
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 7 CS_Grado07, printed pp. 225–228, teaches: human trafficking as modern slavery and discrimination — illegal immigrants hired for lethal-risk work with no right to claim, "trata de blancas" and false modelling-scholarship offers that end in forced prostitution, children kidnapped for begging or prostitution, child labour in inhuman conditions and forced recruitment by illegal armed groups; campaigns answer by backing institutions that support abandoned children. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: discrimination is opened from a Colombian school's own cases — pupils mocked for obesity, for being the course's only Afro-descendant girl, for being the best student, for being homosexual — and the student council is called in to plan the solution before the concept is defined. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
