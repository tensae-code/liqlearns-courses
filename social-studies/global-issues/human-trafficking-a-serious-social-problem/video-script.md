# Video script — Human Trafficking a Serious Social Problem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 7 CS_Grado07, printed pp. 225–228, teaches: human trafficking as modern slavery and discrimination — illegal immigrants hired for lethal-risk work with no right to claim, "trata de blancas" and false modelling-scholarship offers that end in forced prostitution, children kidnapped for begging or prostitution, child labour in inhuman conditions and forced recruitment by illegal armed groups; campaigns answer by backing institutions that support abandoned children.

Colombia's own way: discrimination is opened from a Colombian school's own cases — pupils mocked for obesity, for being the course's only Afro-descendant girl, for being the best student, for being homosexual — and the student council is called in to plan the solution before the concept is defined.
