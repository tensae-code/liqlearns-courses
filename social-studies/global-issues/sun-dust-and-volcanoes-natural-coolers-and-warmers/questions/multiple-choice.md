# Sun, dust and volcanoes — natural coolers and warmers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Solar output varies by about…
a) 10%  b) 1%  c) 0.1%
**Answer: c) 0.1%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sunspot cycle lasts roughly…
a) 11 years  b) 100 years  c) 1 year
**Answer: a) 11 years**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** During spotty times the sun is slightly…
a) dimmer  b) brighter  c) unchanged
**Answer: b) brighter**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Aerosols in the air mostly…
a) warm the surface  b) cool the surface by blocking sunlight  c) make rain acid
**Answer: b) cool the surface by blocking sunlight**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The strongest natural cooler is a…
a) dust storm  b) wildfire  c) sulfur-rich volcanic eruption
**Answer: c) sulfur-rich volcanic eruption**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The volcanic haze forms in the…
a) troposphere  b) stratosphere  c) ocean
**Answer: b) stratosphere**
