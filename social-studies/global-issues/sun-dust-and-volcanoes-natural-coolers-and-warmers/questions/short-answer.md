# Sun, dust and volcanoes — natural coolers and warmers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a spotty sun brighter overall?
**Answer:** The bright areas around the spots outweigh the dark spots.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the volcanic haze do to sunlight?
**Answer:** It reflects it, shading and cooling the surface.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two other aerosol sources besides volcanoes.
**Answer:** Wildfires and dust storms.
