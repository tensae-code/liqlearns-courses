# Sun, dust and volcanoes — natural coolers and warmers — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sunspots themselves are bright patches.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A sulfuric-acid haze can persist for years.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wildfire aerosols mostly warm the surface.
**Answer: False**
