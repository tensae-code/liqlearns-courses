# Hands-on — Power Sharing and Why It Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activities 5–6 Power sharing and its methods
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: power sharing is the distribution of power among the legislature, executive and judiciary; it reduces conflict between social groups, ensures stability and participation, gives minorities a fair share and provides checks and balances — in South Sudan through an all-inclusive cabinet of different tribes and genders; its methods are autonomy (symmetrical and asymmetrical federalism) and the group building-block (consociational) approach with devolution to ethnic groups. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
