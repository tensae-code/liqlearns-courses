# Environmental awareness and the Kuznets curve

## Do it first

A smog that killed thousands in 1952 London; a book about silent springs in 1962. When did the world start worrying — and does getting richer first dirty, then clean? Hold both questions.

## Sub lesson 1 — How the world woke up

Modern environmental concern dates from the 1950s: London's Great Smog (1952), Rachel Carson's Silent Spring (1962), The Limits to Growth (1972), then the Brundtland Report (1987), the Rio Earth Summit (1992), the Kyoto Protocol (1997), the IPCC, the MDGs (2000), Rio+20 (2012) and the SDGs (2015).

## Sub lesson 2 — The environmental Kuznets curve

One hopeful hypothesis threads through: the environmental Kuznets curve — degradation first rises with income, then falls, as societies can afford and demand protection. An inverted-U, paralleling Kuznets's inequality curve from the poverty unit.
