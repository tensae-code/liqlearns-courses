# Hands-on — Environmental awareness and the Kuznets curve

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Timeline over curve
Draw the dated milestone timeline above an inverted-U Kuznets curve. Mark roughly where a poor, a middle-income and a rich economy sit on the curve, and defend each placement.

## Hope audit
Write two lines for the Kuznets hope and two lines of caution (what if the fall never comes for some harms — carbon, extinction?). Use one milestone from the timeline as evidence on either side.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
