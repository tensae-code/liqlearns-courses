# Environmental awareness and the Kuznets curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "How the world woke up"?
a) The milestones run from London's Great Smog (1952) and Silent Spring (1962) through Brundtland, Rio and Kyoto to the SDGs (2015)  b) The environmental Kuznets curve is an inverted-U: degradation rises, then falls, as incomes grow
**Answer: a) The milestones run from London's Great Smog (1952) and Silent Spring (1962) through Brundtland, Rio and Kyoto to the SDGs (2015)**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The environmental Kuznets curve"?
a) The environmental Kuznets curve is an inverted-U: degradation rises, then falls, as incomes grow  b) The milestones run from London's Great Smog (1952) and Silent Spring (1962) through Brundtland, Rio and Kyoto to the SDGs (2015)
**Answer: a) The environmental Kuznets curve is an inverted-U: degradation rises, then falls, as incomes grow**
