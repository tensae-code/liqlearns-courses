# Environmental awareness and the Kuznets curve — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "How the world woke up" in your own words.
**Answer:** The milestones run from London's Great Smog (1952) and Silent Spring (1962) through Brundtland, Rio and Kyoto to the SDGs (2015).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The environmental Kuznets curve" in your own words.
**Answer:** The environmental Kuznets curve is an inverted-U: degradation rises, then falls, as incomes grow.
