# Hands-on — Recycling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 13 Recycling (بازیافت): every day you and your family throw away fruit skins and leftover food; not all rubbish is to be thrown away — some rubbish can be recycled; recycling means making new things from materials already used. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
