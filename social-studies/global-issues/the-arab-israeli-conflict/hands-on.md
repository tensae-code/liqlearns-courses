# Hands-on — The Arab-Israeli conflict — a war that never quite ends

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One land, two claims
On one sheet, draw the territory. On the left write the claim as Israel's founders stated it; on the right, as the Palestinian Arabs stated it. The exercise is accuracy, not verdict: each side must be stated as its holders state it.

## The three questions wall
Write land, statehood, refugees on three cards. Under each, pin one fact from the lesson that keeps the question open.

## Grade 12 depth: Suez 1956 and Iran 1979 — the Middle East widens

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the new part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
