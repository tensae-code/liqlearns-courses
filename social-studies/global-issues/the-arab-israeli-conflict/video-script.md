# Video script — The Arab-Israeli conflict — a war that never quite ends

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a narrow strip of land between sea and desert — small on the world map, enormous in the century's news.
Ships arrive; a new state's flag rises in 1948; the neighbouring flags turn hostile. The lines on the map jump, war by war.
Behind the region, two greater flags shadow the map — the superpowers, drawn in by oil and position.
The camera rests on three words written over the land: land — statehood — refugees. The questions are still open as the map fades.
