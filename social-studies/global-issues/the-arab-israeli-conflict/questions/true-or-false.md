# The Arab-Israeli conflict — a war that never quite ends — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Israel's creation was accepted at once by all its neighbours.
**Answer: False** — its Arab neighbours rejected the settlement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Successive wars redrew the territory's lines again and again.
**Answer: True** — each war created new grievances.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** The land in question was empty of inhabitants before Israel's creation.
