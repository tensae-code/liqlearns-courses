# Video script — The Solar System — How It Formed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

First: hot gas, spinning, loose and wide.

Gravity pulls it in. It flattens to a disc.

The disc splits into rings.

The middle ring becomes the Sun. The outer rings become the planets.

4.6 billion years ago. That is the guide's story of our beginning.
