# The Solar System — How It Formed

## Do it first

This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only — the student book opens with the universe as it is, and the guide adds how it came to be. Judge the story as a story of steps.

## From spinning gas to rings to worlds

Step one: long ago there was a large filament — a thread — of hot spinning gases: loosely packed hydrogen, helium and dust. Gravity pulled it together, and as it gathered it flattened into a thin disc, still spinning.

Step two: the disc split into rings, and the rings kept spinning. The central ring formed the Sun. The outer rings formed the Sun's family — the planets, Earth among them.

Step three: within the rings, dust particles collided again and again, sticking and growing, until collisions had built planets. All of this happened some 4.6 billion years ago — the same deep date the four-eras lesson gives for the start of Earth's story.
