# The Solar System — How It Formed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The solar system formed about —
a) 4.6 million years ago  b) 4.6 billion years ago  c) 600 million years ago
**Answer: b) 4.6 billion years ago**

**2.** The starting material was —
a) solid rock  b) a filament of hot spinning gases  c) ocean water
**Answer: b) a filament of hot spinning gases**

**3.** The gases were mostly —
a) oxygen and nitrogen  b) hydrogen, helium and dust  c) iron and nickel
**Answer: b) hydrogen, helium and dust**

**4.** Gravity drew the filament together into —
a) a ball of rock  b) a thin disc  c) a cube
**Answer: b) a thin disc**

**5.** The central ring formed —
a) the Earth  b) the Sun  c) the Moon
**Answer: b) the Sun**

**6.** In the story, Earth formed from —
a) the central ring  b) an outer ring  c) outside the disc
**Answer: b) an outer ring**
