# Hands-on — The Solar System — How It Formed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spin and flatten
Sprinkle flour on a plate and spin the plate gently: the flour spreads outward into a flat disc. That flattening — spin making a disc — is the shape of step one.

## Collision building
Roll a small ball of sticky clay across scattered crumbs: it picks them up and grows. Again and again, collisions built planets out of dust — your clay ball is the model.
