## Sub lesson 2 — India's way — the book's own teaching way

India's own way (Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat): The first Geography year goes planet → globe → motions → maps → Earth's domains → landforms, and only then turns home: the last two chapters are India itself (location, neighbours, physical divisions, monsoon climate, vegetation and wildlife), so world basics are immediately applied to the home country. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
