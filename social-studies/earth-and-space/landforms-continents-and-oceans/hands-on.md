# Hands-on — Landforms, Continents and Oceans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Local landform hunt
From a high point or on a walk, find and sketch one example of each shape you can see: plateau, plain, mountain, rift valley. If one is missing near you, say so — that is an answer too.

## Ranking cards
Write the seven continents and five oceans on twelve cards with their areas on the back. Shuffle, then lay each set in order largest to smallest without looking at the backs. Check yourself.
