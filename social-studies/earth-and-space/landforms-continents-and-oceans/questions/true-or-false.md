# Landforms, Continents and Oceans — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A rift valley is one of the great shapes found on the continents.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Pacific Ocean is larger than the continent of Asia.
**Answer: True — 165,384,000 km² against 44,810,880 km².**
