# Landforms, Continents and Oceans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Folding, faulting and volcanism are —
a) external forces  b) internal forces  c) weather
**Answer: b) internal forces**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Erosion, weathering and deposition are —
a) internal forces  b) external forces  c) volcanism
**Answer: b) external forces**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The largest continent is —
a) Africa  b) Asia  c) North America
**Answer: b) Asia**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Africa ranks ______ among the continents by area.
a) first  b) second  c) third
**Answer: b) second**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The smallest continent in the table is —
a) Europe  b) Antarctica  c) Australia
**Answer: c) Australia**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The largest ocean is —
a) Atlantic  b) Indian  c) Pacific
**Answer: c) Pacific**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The smallest ocean is —
a) Arctic  b) Southern  c) Indian
**Answer: a) Arctic**
