# Inside the Earth — Crust, Mantle, Core — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Earth's three layers, outside to centre, are —
a) core, mantle, crust  b) crust, mantle, core  c) mantle, crust, core
**Answer: b) crust, mantle, core**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the oceans, the crust is about —
a) 5–7 km thick  b) 30–65 km thick  c) 2,885 km thick
**Answer: a) 5–7 km thick**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The layer holding 82–84% of Earth's volume is —
a) the crust  b) the mantle  c) the core
**Answer: b) the mantle**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The boundary between crust and mantle is called —
a) the Moho discontinuity  b) the date line  c) the equator
**Answer: a) the Moho discontinuity**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a DIRECT source of knowledge about the inside?
a) earthquake waves  b) mining  c) guessing from maps
**Answer: b) mining**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Earthquake waves and meteors are —
a) direct sources  b) indirect sources  c) not used
**Answer: b) indirect sources**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** The inner core is solid because —
a) it is cold  b) gravity's pressure compresses the iron-nickel into a solid  c) it is made of rock
**Answer: b) gravity's pressure compresses the iron-nickel into a solid**

**8. (Sub lesson 1)** Core temperatures are given up to about —
a) 3,700 °C  b) 6,000 °C  c) 600 °C
**Answer: b) 6,000 °C**
