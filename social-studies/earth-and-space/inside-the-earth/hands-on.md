# Hands-on — Inside the Earth — Crust, Mantle, Core

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Egg dissection
Cut a boiled egg in half. Point to the shell (crust), the white (mantle) and the yolk (core). Say each layer's Earth-depth from the lesson as you point.

## Wave detective
Tap one end of a long table hard while a partner presses an ear to the far end. The tap arrives through the wood — that is the same trick earthquake waves play through the Earth: the inside reports itself by vibration.
