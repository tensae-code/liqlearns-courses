## Sub lesson 1 — The polar regions

Chapter 10 teaches the polar regions, closing the whole volume — after the continents, the nearby countries, the other regions and the Americas, the book ends at the ends of the Earth.

## Sub lesson 2 — China's way — the book's own teaching way

China learns this inside its own books' way: world regional geography taught continent-by-continent and neighbour-by-neighbour — Asia first and in most detail (China's own continent), then the nearby countries (Japan, Southeast Asia, India, Russia), then the other eastern-hemisphere regions, the Americas, and the polar regions last. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
