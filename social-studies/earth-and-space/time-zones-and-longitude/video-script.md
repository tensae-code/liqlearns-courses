Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 73), teaches: Geographical techniques and skills 2: Time differences over the Earth — places on the same meridian see sunrise and noon together, places east of it earlier; the time zones east of the Greenwich meridian +1, +2, +3 ahead (the San Francisco–New York–London–Mumbai–Hong Kong line); worked example: Tehran and Tabriz differ by about 20 minutes — from Tehran's longitude 51°25′ find Tabriz's; activity: should Kerman-to-Najaf flight passengers put their watches forward or back?; GPS and the rival systems being designed so countries need not depend on one; the road-management centre's traffic applications.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
