# Hands-on — Eras Of Earths History

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 5: The eons and eras of Earth's history — the Hadean eon (4,600–4,000 million years), and the Phanerozoic eon from 540 million years with its three eras: Palaeozoic periods (Ordovician graptolites; Carboniferous coal forests), the Mesozoic era (252–66 million years; Triassic, chalk-rich Cretaceous), and the Cenozoic era to the Quaternary, the short age that matters to man. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
