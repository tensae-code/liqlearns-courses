Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 11 Earth Science* (printed p. 114), teaches: Ch. 5: The eons and eras of Earth's history — the Hadean eon (4,600–4,000 million years), and the Phanerozoic eon from 540 million years with its three eras: Palaeozoic periods (Ordovician graptolites; Carboniferous coal forests), the Mesozoic era (252–66 million years; Triassic, chalk-rich Cretaceous), and the Cenozoic era to the Quaternary, the short age that matters to man.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 11 Earth Science): the scientific track's earth-science course — atmosphere, earthquakes and volcanoes, weathering and soil, environment and pollution, and fossils and geological time — bilingual with English technical terms throughout, and Iraqi touches kept (the air masses over Iraq, the yellow dust storms, Iraq's Natural History Museum).
