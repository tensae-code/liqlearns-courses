# Hands-on — Day And Night Sun And Moon

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: La Luna le dijo al Sol; La Luna adorna las noches / Dormimos y nos despertamos en nuestra casa; El Sol sale todos los días. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
