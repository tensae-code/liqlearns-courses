# Video script — The Universe and Space

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look up on a clear night.

Everything you see is a tiny sample of the universe.

The universe holds all matter, and the empty space between it.

Stars gather in galaxies. Ours is the Milky Way.

And the nearest star's light left it 4.3 years ago.

Looking far is looking back in time.
