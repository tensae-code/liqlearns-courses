# Hands-on — The Universe and Space

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Night-sky list
On a cloudless night, list every kind of body you can see. Beside each, write what you think it is. Bring the list to your next study time and check it against Sub lesson 1.

## Branching tree on paper
Draw the universe tree from Sub lesson 2 from memory: universe at the top, down to the solar system at the bottom. Then check your drawing against the picture and fix what you missed.
