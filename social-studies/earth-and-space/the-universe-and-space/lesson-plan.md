# Lesson plan — The Universe and Space

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** The Universe and Space
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 2 — Earth and Space (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the universe is the vast, boundless space holding all matter — and our galaxy, our star-neighbours and our place inside it can all be pictured.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the opening lesson (What Social Studies Is) and on the learner's own night sky.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The universe: boundless space holding all matter**
2. **How the universe is organised**
3. **Stars and galaxies: the Milky Way**
4. **The nearest star: seeing the past**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the lessons named in this lesson's own level line above. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer, from the lesson alone: The universe is — — and the rest of this lesson's question set.
2. Answer, from the lesson alone: We can see the whole universe in one photograph. — and the rest of this lesson's question set.
3. Answer, from the lesson alone: Name the three states of matter the universe contains. — and the rest of this lesson's question set.
