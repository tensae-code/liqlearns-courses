# The Universe and Space — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** We can see the whole universe in one photograph.
**Answer: False — a photograph shows only a sample, a part of it.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Stars make their own light and heat.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** When you look at Proxima Centauri tonight, you see it as it is tonight.
**Answer: False — you see it as it was 4.3 years ago.**
