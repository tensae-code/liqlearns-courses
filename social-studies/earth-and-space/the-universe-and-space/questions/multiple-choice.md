# The Universe and Space — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The universe is —
a) only the stars we can see  b) the vast, boundless space containing all matter  c) the solar system only
**Answer: b) the vast, boundless space containing all matter**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the branching diagram, the solar system sits inside —
a) a comet  b) the Milky Way  c) the Moon
**Answer: b) the Milky Way**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "Space" inside the universe means —
a) the empty, matter-free void  b) a kind of star  c) dust grains
**Answer: a) the empty, matter-free void**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A galaxy is —
a) one single star  b) an ordered group of stars, gases and dust  c) a telescope
**Answer: b) an ordered group of stars, gases and dust**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Our solar system sits in the Milky Way —
a) at its centre  b) towards its edge  c) outside it
**Answer: b) towards its edge**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The nearest star after the Sun is —
a) the Moon  b) Proxima Centauri  c) Venus
**Answer: b) Proxima Centauri**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Proxima Centauri is how far away?
a) 4.3 kilometres  b) 4.3 light-years  c) 43 light-years
**Answer: b) 4.3 light-years**
