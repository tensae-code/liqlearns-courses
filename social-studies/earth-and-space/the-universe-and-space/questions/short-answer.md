# The Universe and Space — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three states of matter the universe contains.
**Answer: Solid, liquid and gas.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List four things found inside the solar system, from the diagram.
**Answer: Any four of: Sun, planets, asteroids, moons, meteors, comets.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the science of space called, and what is one of its instruments?
**Answer: Astronomy; the space telescope.**
