# The Solar System — the Sun and the Planets — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1,390,176 km is the Sun's diameter.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two planets can share the same orbit.
**Answer: False — each planet follows its own orbit.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The moon counts in the book's planet table are the counts known at the book's printing time.
**Answer: True — they are period figures, flagged as dated.**
