# The Solar System — the Sun and the Planets — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The solar system is —
a) the Sun plus all that circles it  b) only the eight planets  c) the Milky Way
**Answer: a) the Sun plus all that circles it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sun is mostly made of —
a) iron and rock  b) hydrogen (75%) and helium (25%) gas  c) water
**Answer: b) hydrogen (75%) and helium (25%) gas**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sun's surface temperature is —
a) 600 °C  b) 6,000 °C  c) 15,000,000 °C
**Answer: b) 6,000 °C — the 15,000,000 °C figure is its interior.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Planets shine because they —
a) make their own light  b) reflect sunlight  c) burn like stars
**Answer: b) reflect sunlight**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The rocky planets are —
a) Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune  b) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars  c) Venus, Earth, Jupiter, Saturn
**Answer: b) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Venus is hotter than Mercury because —
a) it is nearer the Sun  b) its dense atmosphere traps heat  c) it spins faster
**Answer: b) its dense atmosphere traps heat**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Pluto was removed from the planet list in —
a) 1996  b) 2006  c) 2016
**Answer: b) 2006**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** A planet table can tell you all of these EXCEPT —
a) its diameter  b) its moon count (as printed)  c) the names of its moons' discoverers
**Answer: c) the names of its moons' discoverers**
