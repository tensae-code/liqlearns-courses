# Hands-on — The Solar System — the Sun and the Planets

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two sets from memory
Write the eight planets in order on paper. Draw a line after Mars. Label the left side ROCKY and the right side GAS GIANTS. Check against Picture 11009.

## Blanket test for Venus
Put one thermometer (or the back of your hand) in open shade and another under a thick blanket in the same shade. After ten minutes, compare. That trapped warmth is why Venus, under its dense atmosphere, is hotter than Mercury.
