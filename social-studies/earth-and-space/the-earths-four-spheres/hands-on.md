# Hands-on — The Earth's Four Spheres

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sphere sort on a walk
Walk outside with paper divided into four boxes: LAND, WATER, LIVING, AIR. Write or draw ten things you meet, one per box where each belongs. Star any thing you argued about — those are the interesting ones.

## Interaction chain
Start with "rain falls". With a partner, build a chain of at least five links, naming the sphere at each link: rain (air) fills the river (water), the river waters the field (land), the field feeds the goat (living)...
