# The Earth's Four Spheres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The lithosphere is the sphere of —
a) water  b) land and rock  c) air
**Answer: b) land and rock**

**2.** The hydrosphere is the sphere of —
a) living things  b) water  c) rock
**Answer: b) water**

**3.** A goat belongs to —
a) the biosphere  b) the lithosphere  c) the atmosphere
**Answer: a) the biosphere**

**4.** The atmosphere is the sphere of —
a) soil  b) air  c) oceans
**Answer: b) air**

**5.** "Everything in Earth's system is placed in —"
a) one sphere only, never interacting  b) one of the four spheres, and the spheres interact  c) no sphere at all
**Answer: b) one of the four spheres, and the spheres interact**
