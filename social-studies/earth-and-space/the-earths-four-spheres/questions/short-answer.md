# The Earth's Four Spheres — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name all four spheres and what each holds.
**Answer: Lithosphere — land/rock; hydrosphere — water; biosphere — living things; atmosphere — air.**

**2.** Give one example of two spheres interacting.
**Answer: Any true example, e.g. rain from the atmosphere filling rivers in the hydrosphere; rivers eroding the lithosphere; plants of the biosphere drinking water.**

**3.** If a forest (biosphere) is cut, name one other sphere that feels it and say how.
**Answer: E.g. lithosphere — soil erodes without roots; hydrosphere — rivers carry more soil; atmosphere — less clean air (any one, sensibly explained).**
