# Rocks in Ethiopia — Distribution and Value — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Addis Ababa stands in the region of which rock family?
a) sedimentary  b) igneous  c) metamorphic
**Answer: b) igneous**

**2.** Sedimentary rocks mainly cover —
a) central Ethiopia  b) eastern Ethiopia  c) the northern edge
**Answer: b) eastern Ethiopia**

**3.** Metamorphic rocks lie —
a) in the centre only  b) along the northern, western and southern edges  c) under the oceans
**Answer: b) along the northern, western and southern edges**

**4.** Igneous rocks cover large areas in —
a) the east  b) the centre of the country  c) the southern edge
**Answer: b) the centre of the country**

**5.** Food nutrients reach us from rocks through —
a) rivers only  b) soil that plants draw from  c) the wind
**Answer: b) soil that plants draw from**
