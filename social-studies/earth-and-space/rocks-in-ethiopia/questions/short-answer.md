# Rocks in Ethiopia — Distribution and Value — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the four economic values of rocks given in the lesson.
**Answer: Source of energy/fuel; building and construction; formation of soil; source of food nutrients.**

**2.** Why can past conditions of the Earth be learned from sedimentary rocks?
**Answer: Because they contain fossils.**

**3.** A builder in eastern Ethiopia digs layered rock full of shells. Which family is it most likely to be, and what else might it hold?
**Answer: Sedimentary — and it might hold fossils.**
