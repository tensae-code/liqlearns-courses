The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Centre of Ethiopia: igneous rock. Addis Ababa stands on it.

East: sedimentary rock.

Edges, north, west and south: metamorphic rock.

Rocks give fuel, buildings, soil, and food nutrients.

And sedimentary rocks keep the fossils that tell the past.
