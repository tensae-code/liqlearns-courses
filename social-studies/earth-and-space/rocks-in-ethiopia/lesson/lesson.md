## Where each family lies, and what rocks are for

Igneous rocks cover large areas in the centre of the country — including Addis Ababa itself: the capital stands on fire-born rock. Sedimentary rocks mainly cover eastern Ethiopia. Metamorphic rocks lie along the edges: the northern, western and southern margins of the country.

Why should anyone care where rocks lie? Because rocks have economic value, in four named ways: they are a source of energy and fuel; they serve building and construction; they break down into soil; and they are a source of food nutrients — the minerals plants draw from the ground end up in what we eat.

One more value, from a keyed discussion: the past can be read from rocks. Sedimentary rocks hold fossils, so past conditions of the Earth can be learned from them — the same fossil evidence that divided Earth's story into four eras.
