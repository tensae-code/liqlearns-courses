# Hands-on — Rocks in Ethiopia — Distribution and Value

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Home rock audit
Look at your home and street: a stone wall, a grinding stone, gravel, a clay pot's grit. List every use of rock you find and match it to the four values: fuel, building, soil, food nutrients.

Draw Ethiopia's outline from memory and shade the three rock regions: centre, east, edges. Check against Picture 11040 and fix your shading.
