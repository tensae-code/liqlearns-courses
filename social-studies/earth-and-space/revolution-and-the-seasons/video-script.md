# Video script — Revolution and the Seasons

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch the lean. The axis always points the same way.

Here, the north leans to the Sun. Long days in the north.

The Sun stands overhead at Cancer.

Half a year on: the south leans in. The Sun stands over Capricorn.

Between them, twice, it stands over the equator.

That sliding — Cancer, equator, Capricorn, equator — is the seasons.
