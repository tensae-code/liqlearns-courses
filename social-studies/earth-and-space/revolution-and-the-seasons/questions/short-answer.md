# Revolution and the Seasons — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the three results of revolution.
**Answer: The Sun appears to travel between the tropics; day length varies through the year; the seasons alternate.**

**2.** Why does day length change through the year?
**Answer: Because the tilted axis leans each hemisphere towards the Sun for part of the year and away for the other part.**

**3.** Which two facts about the axis make the seasons happen?
**Answer: It leans 23½°, and it keeps pointing the same way (at the North Star) all through the revolution.**
