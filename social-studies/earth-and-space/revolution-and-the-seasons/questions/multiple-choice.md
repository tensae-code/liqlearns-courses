# Revolution and the Seasons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The seasons are caused by —
a) Earth being nearer the Sun in summer  b) revolution with the tilted axis  c) rotation alone
**Answer: b) revolution with the tilted axis**

**2.** The Sun stands directly overhead at the tropic of Capricorn at —
a) the same time as at Cancer  b) the opposite extreme of the year from Cancer  c) never
**Answer: b) the opposite extreme of the year from Cancer**

**3.** How often in a year does the Sun stand overhead at the equator?
a) once  b) twice  c) four times
**Answer: b) twice**

**4.** The northern tropic, where the overhead Sun reaches its northern limit, is —
a) Capricorn  b) Cancer  c) the equator
**Answer: b) Cancer**

**5.** A hemisphere leaning towards the Sun has —
a) shorter days  b) longer days  c) no change in day length
**Answer: b) longer days**
