# Hands-on — Revolution and the Seasons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lamp and leaning ball
Put a lamp on a table (the Sun). Carry a tilted ball round it, keeping the tilt pointing at the same wall (the North Star). At four stops, see which half of the ball catches the strongest light. That half has its warm season.

## Day-length diary
For one week, note sunrise and sunset times from a calendar or by watching. Is your day growing longer or shorter? Which way is your hemisphere leaning?
