## Sub lesson 1 — The biosphere and the environment

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide teaches the biosphere and the environment in its environmental geography strand (§3.5): the biosphere — the living sphere of the earth — is met through its place in the environment, after the same strand has taught the lithosphere, the hydrosphere and the atmosphere and the environment.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — sphere by sphere

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. Myanmar's environmental strand takes the earth's spheres one by one — lithosphere (§3.2), hydrosphere (§3.3), atmosphere (§3.4), then the biosphere (§3.5) — and closes with the interaction between the earth's four spheres (§3.6).

Myanmar's own way: the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week — physical geography, social geography, environmental geography, neighbouring countries and cartography — each lesson plan built student-centred (Bloom steps, key questions, classroom assessment) around Myanmar and its five neighbours. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
