# Video script — Measuring Space — the Light-Year

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A kilometre is too small for space.

Light runs 300,000 kilometres in one second.

Times 60, times 60, times 24, times 365.25.

One light-year: 9,467,280,000,000 kilometres.

That is the ruler of space.
