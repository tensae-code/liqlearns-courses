# Hands-on — Measuring Space — the Light-Year

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Climb the staircase yourself
With a calculator or on paper, multiply it out yourself: 300,000 x 60 x 60 x 24 x 365.25. Check that you land on 9,467,280,000,000 km.

## Zeros count
Write the distance to Proxima Centauri two ways: in kilometres (about 40,000,000,000,000 km) and in light-years (4.3). Which one would you rather say in a sentence? Say why.
