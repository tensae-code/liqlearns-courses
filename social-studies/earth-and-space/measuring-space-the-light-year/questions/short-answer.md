# Measuring Space — the Light-Year — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why do astronomers use light-years instead of kilometres?
**Answer: Space distances are so great that kilometre numbers become too large to use; the light-year is a unit big enough for them.**

**2.** What are the four multiplication steps from one second to one year?
**Answer: x60 (minute), x60 (hour), x24 (day), x365.25 (year).**

**3.** A star is 2 light-years away. What does that say about its light?
**Answer: Its light takes 2 years to reach us; we see it as it was 2 years ago.**
