# Measuring Space — the Light-Year — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Light travels in one second —
a) 300 km  b) 300,000 km  c) 3,000,000 km
**Answer: b) 300,000 km**

**2.** One light-year is the distance light travels in —
a) one day  b) one year  c) one hour
**Answer: b) one year**

**3.** One light-year equals about —
a) 9,467,280 km  b) 9,467,280,000 km  c) 9,467,280,000,000 km
**Answer: c) 9,467,280,000,000 km**

**4.** "4.3 light-years" tells you —
a) how bright the star is  b) how far away it is  c) how hot it is
**Answer: b) how far away it is**
