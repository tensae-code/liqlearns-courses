# Video script — Solar and Lunar Eclipses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide teaches solar and lunar eclipse in its physical geography strand (§1.5): an eclipse — the sun's eclipse and the moon's eclipse — is its own topic, closing the strand's run through the earth's size and shape, the seasons, the zones of the earth and the moon's features.

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Myanmar the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week, and eclipses sit in the physical geography strand, taught student-centred — Bloom steps, key questions and classroom assessment — around Myanmar and its five neighbours.
