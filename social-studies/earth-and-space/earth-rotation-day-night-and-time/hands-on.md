# Hands-on — Earth Rotation Day Night And Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 12 The movements of the Earth (حرکت‌های زمین): the Earth, like the other planets of the solar system, circles the Sun; all places on Earth continually come in front of the Sun's light and then pass away from it and fall into darkness — though we do not feel the Earth's turning on itself; and the revolution movement (حرکت انتقالی), the Earth's circling of the Sun, by which the seasons come to be; activity: how many times have you circled the Sun so far, and how do you know?; Lesson 2 The movements of the Earth (حرکات زمین): the turning on its axis — look at the clocks and say the time; the people of India see the sunrise earlier than the people of Iran, and noon comes to Iran before Arabia; in wide countries travellers east–west must put their watches back or forward (the Bering Strait pictured). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
