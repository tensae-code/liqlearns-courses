# Video script — Features of the Moon

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide teaches the characteristics and features of the moon in its physical geography strand (§1.4): the moon is studied for what it is like — its own features, named and described as a topic of its own, after the zones of the earth.

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Myanmar the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week, and the moon's features sit in the physical geography strand, taught student-centred — Bloom steps, key questions and classroom assessment — just before solar and lunar eclipses.
