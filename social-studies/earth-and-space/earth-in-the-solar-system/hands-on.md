# Hands-on — Earth In The Solar System

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 1 Earth, the beautiful cradle of mankind (زمین، مهد زیبای انسان‌ها): our Earth a small part of the solar system — the Sun the star at its centre and the heavenly bodies circling it in their orbits; the Sun itself one star of the Milky Way galaxy, and the Milky Way one of billions of galaxies. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
