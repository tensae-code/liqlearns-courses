## Sub lesson 1 — The zones of the earth

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide teaches the zones of the earth in its physical geography strand (§1.3): the earth is divided into zones — the bands of the earth that run around it, each zone met as its own part of the planet to name and place.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — one strand of five

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Myanmar the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week, and the zones of the earth sit in the physical geography strand, taught student-centred — Bloom steps, key questions and classroom assessment — straight after the size and shape of the earth and the formation of seasons.

Myanmar's own way: the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week — physical geography, social geography, environmental geography, neighbouring countries and cartography — each lesson plan built student-centred (Bloom steps, key questions, classroom assessment) around Myanmar and its five neighbours. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
