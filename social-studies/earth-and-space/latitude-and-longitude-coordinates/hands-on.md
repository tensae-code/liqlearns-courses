# Hands-on — Latitude And Longitude Coordinates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 12: The other imaginary lines drawn at equal distances over the globe (the North Pole and 90 degrees marked) — the equator (خطّ استوا) and the prime meridian (نصف‌النهار مبدأ); activity: bring geographical globes to class and, with the teacher's guidance, find these lines on them; Lesson 1: The circles drawn parallel to the equator in both hemispheres (the parallels, مدارها) and the meridians; latitude and longitude — the geographical coordinates (مختصات جغرافیایی) by which every point on the globe stands on one parallel and one meridian; the degrees are angles made inside the sphere. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
