# Time Zones and the Date Line — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Local time alone is enough for a whole country's dealings.
**Answer: False — every place has its own local time; national and world dealings need standard zones.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's western and eastern tips are about 15° apart — one full hour of solar time inside one zone.
**Answer: True (33°E to 48°E).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The date line changes the hour of the day.
**Answer: False — the clocks agree either side; it is the date that flips.**
