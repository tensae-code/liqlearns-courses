# Time Zones and the Date Line — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Show where 15° per hour comes from.
**Answer: Earth turns 360° in 24 hours; 360 ÷ 24 = 15° per hour.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the three steps for working a time problem.
**Answer: 1. Find the degree difference (add across hemispheres, subtract within one). 2. Divide by 15 to get hours. 3. Add eastward, subtract westward.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's telling, what happens to Sunday when you cross the line east to west?
**Answer: Sunday becomes Saturday — a day is dropped.**
