# Time Zones and the Date Line — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The world is divided into ______ standard time zones.
a) 12  b) 15  c) 24
**Answer: c) 24**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each zone spans —
a) 15°  b) 24°  c) 360°
**Answer: a) 15°**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Crossing a zone boundary eastward, you —
a) add an hour  b) subtract an hour  c) change the date
**Answer: a) add an hour**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** It is 3:00 at 60°W. The time at 60°E is —
a) 5:00  b) 11:00  c) 19:00
**Answer: b) 11:00**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In Step 1, for two places in opposite hemispheres you —
a) subtract the degrees  b) add the degrees  c) divide by 15 first
**Answer: b) add the degrees**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia keeps which time offset?
a) GMT+1  b) GMT+3  c) GMT+8
**Answer: b) GMT+3**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The International Date Line stands at —
a) 0°  b) 90°E  c) 180°
**Answer: c) 180°**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Crossing the date line from east to west, you —
a) gain a day  b) drop a day  c) gain an hour
**Answer: b) drop a day**
