# Hands-on — Time Zones and the Date Line

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Zone walk
Chalk a line of 24 boxes on the ground — the 24 zones. Start in the middle box at 12:00. A partner calls "three zones east!" or "two zones west!"; you step and shout the new time.

## Home problems
Work these with the three steps, showing each step: (1) It is 9:00 at Greenwich; what time is it at 90°E? (2) It is 6:00 at 45°E; what time is it at 30°W? Answers: (1) 15:00. (2) 1:00.
