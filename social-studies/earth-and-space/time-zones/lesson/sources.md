**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Ethiopia, Barbados, Indonesia, Dominican Republic, India, Rwanda, Vietnam). Haiti is named here as teacher-sequence evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Indonesia's own way (Grade 11): the whole book is organised around Indonesia itself — its strategic position first, then its own biodiversity (Wallace/Wallacea), its own environment-and-population problems, and its own disaster map; every concept is taught through Indonesian examples, maps and infographics, with a chapter-end knowledge assessment (Asesmen) per chapter. That way is stocked here as the way Indonesia teaches this thing.

Haiti's own way (teacher sequence — evidence): Geography opens with representing the Earth itself — globe-to-flat projection problems, orientation practised on the ground with compass/GPS/Google Maps, and time-zone puzzles worked on Haiti's own links (midday shared with Québec, 18h in Paris, Port-au-Prince–Paris flights); every séance ends in a croquis, a glossary and field observation. That evidence is stocked here as the way Haiti teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project. That way is stocked here as the way Barbados teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 10): This is the physical-geography year: the universe and Earth's layers, continental drift with volcanoes and earthquakes — including earthquake-prevention measures as a group task — water and winds across the continents, and a closing unit on political and economic geography that treats borders, geopolitical order and international climate agreements as geography, with hurricane-season citizen responsibility as the water unit's group activity.

India's own way (Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat): The first Geography year goes planet → globe → motions → maps → Earth's domains → landforms, and only then turns home: the last two chapters are India itself (location, neighbours, physical divisions, monsoon climate, vegetation and wildlife), so world basics are immediately applied to the home country.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 6 Social Studies SB): the P6 pupil's book is the fullest Geography book of the set — a whole unit on direction and location works Rwanda onto the East African and African maps by latitude and longitude and time zones, then physical features and climate, and a climate-change sequence (types, change, effects, overcoming) sits inside it; regional co-operation is taught bloc by bloc (COMESA, CEPGL, ECCAS, AU, NEPAD).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Địa lí 10): a single Grade 10 course that runs the whole discipline — map craft, the Earth's spheres, then economic geography sector by sector — with Vietnam itself as the standing worked example (its power-plant and rice maps, the Red River's regime at Hanoi, Hanoi's climate chart, Vietnam's GDP structure, its internet and tourism figures) inside world-scale treatment.
