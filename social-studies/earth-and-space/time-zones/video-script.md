It is 3:00 at 60 degrees west. What time is it at 60 degrees east?

Step one: degree difference. Across hemispheres, add. 60 + 60 = 120.

Step two: divide by 15. That is 8 hours.

Step three: eastward, add. 3:00 plus 8 is 11:00.

Three steps. Every time problem. The same three steps.
