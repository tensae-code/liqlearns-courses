# Hands-on — The Earth's Movements — Rotation and Revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spindle spin
Push a stick through a ball of clay and spin it: the clay ball rotating on its stick-axis is rotation. Now walk slowly round a chair while still spinning it: you are doing rotation and revolution together, like Earth.

## Who is awake?
When it is noon where you live, name one place where it is midnight and one where it is morning, using a globe or Picture 11021. Explain to a partner why, using the words "west to east" and "15 degrees".
