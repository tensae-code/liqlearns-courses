# The Earth's Movements — Rotation and Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many movements of the Earth do the books list?
a) two  b) three  c) four
**Answer: c) four**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two movements that shape human life most are —
a) moving with the galaxy, and revolving with the solar system  b) rotation and revolution  c) only rotation
**Answer: b) rotation and revolution**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Earth rotates from —
a) east to west  b) west to east  c) north to south
**Answer: b) west to east**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Earth turns ______ every hour.
a) 1°  b) 15°  c) 24°
**Answer: b) 15°**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT an effect of rotation named in the lesson?
a) day and night alternating  b) the Sun's apparent movement  c) the seasons
**Answer: c) the seasons — they belong to revolution and the tilt.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Perihelion is —
a) the farthest point, 152,600,000 km  b) the nearest point, 147,100,000 km  c) the tilt of the axis
**Answer: b) the nearest point, 147,100,000 km**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Earth's axis tilts ______ from upright.
a) 66½°  b) 23½°  c) 15°
**Answer: b) 23½°**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Aphelion falls at about —
a) Tahsas 25  b) Sene 28  c) Meskerem 1
**Answer: b) Sene 28**
