# The Earth's Movements — Rotation and Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are pupils in America asleep while pupils in Japan are at school?
**Answer: Because Earth's rotation puts America on the night side while Japan is on the day side at that moment.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What is revolution?
**Answer: Earth's year-long journey around the Sun.**
