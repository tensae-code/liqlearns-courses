# The Moon and Its Phases

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the Moon's revolution around the Earth (دوران القمر حول الأرض) and the phases that come with it, as Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 book teaches it in Unit 5. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Moon goes around the Earth

Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 Social Studies book teaches the Moon in Unit 5, Lesson 20: دوران القمر حول الأرض — the Moon's revolution around the Earth — printed p. 126. The lesson comes straight after the Earth's rotation lesson in the same unit.

## Sub lesson 2 — The phases we see

As the Moon goes around the Earth, the lit part of the Moon that we can see changes from night to night. Those changing shapes we see are the Moon's phases — they come with the Moon's revolution around the Earth, the movement Lesson 20 teaches.
