# The Moon and Its Phases — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which lesson teaches the Moon in Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 book, and on what printed page?
**Answer: Unit 5 Lesson 20 (دوران القمر حول الأرض), printed p. 126 (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do we see phases of the Moon, in this lesson?
**Answer: Because as the Moon revolves around the Earth, the lit part we can see changes from night to night (any close wording).**
