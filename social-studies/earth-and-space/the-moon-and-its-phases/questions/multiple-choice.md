# The Moon and Its Phases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 Lesson 20 teaches —
a) the Moon's revolution around the Earth  b) only the Earth's rotation  c) only maps
**Answer: a) the Moon's revolution around the Earth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Moon revolves around —
a) the Earth  b) a river  c) a continent
**Answer: a) the Earth**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** As the Moon goes around the Earth, the lit part we see —
a) changes  b) never changes  c) disappears for ever
**Answer: a) changes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The changing shapes of the lit Moon we see are called its —
a) phases  b) themes  c) emirates
**Answer: a) phases**
