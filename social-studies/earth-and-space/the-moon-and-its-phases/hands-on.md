# Hands-on — The Moon and Its Phases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow the Moon around
Draw the Earth in the middle of a page and the Moon in four places around it, showing the Moon's revolution around the Earth.

## Watch the lit part change
On your drawing, shade the part of the Moon facing away from the Sun's light in each place, and write that the part we see lit changes — the phases.
