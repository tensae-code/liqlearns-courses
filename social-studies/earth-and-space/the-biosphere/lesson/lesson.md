## Sub lesson 2 — Yemen's way — the book's own teaching way

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 7 G7 Principles of Physical Geography): from Grade 7 the subject splits — this book is pure physical geography organised by the Earth's spheres (atmosphere, lithosphere, hydrosphere, biosphere), opened by map skills and Earth-and-space.

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 10 G10 Geography of Man and the Environment): secondary geography is thematic, not regional — man and space, man representing his geographic field (remote sensing, projections, maps), man organising it (rural/urban), climate and water, the dynamic Earth, and the great environmental systems. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
