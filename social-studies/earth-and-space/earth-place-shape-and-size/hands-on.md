# Hands-on — The Earth — Place, Shape and Size

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Horizon climb
Stand at the lowest ground you can find and note the farthest thing you can see. Then climb a hill, a roof platform or a high building (safely, with an adult) and look again. The horizon has widened — write one sentence on what changed.

## Ball and bulge
Roll a clay ball. Press it gently from top and bottom until it flattens a little. That slightly squashed ball is an oblate spheroid — your model of the Earth's true shape.
