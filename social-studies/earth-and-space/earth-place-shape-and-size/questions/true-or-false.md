# The Earth — Place, Shape and Size — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Earth's orbit is a perfect circle.
**Answer: False — it is an ellipse, a stretched circle.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The higher you climb, the farther you can see.
**Answer: True — the visible horizon grows with height.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Most of the Earth's surface is land.
**Answer: False — 71% is water, 29% land.**
