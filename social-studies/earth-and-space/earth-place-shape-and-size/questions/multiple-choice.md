# The Earth — Place, Shape and Size — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Earth is the ______ planet from the Sun.
a) second  b) third  c) fourth
**Answer: b) third**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Earth's neighbours are —
a) Mercury and Mars  b) Venus and Mars  c) Venus and Jupiter
**Answer: b) Venus and Mars**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Magellan's expedition (1519–1522) proved the Earth is round because —
a) it found the edge  b) the crew returned to Spain from the other side  c) it photographed the Earth
**Answer: b) the crew returned to Spain from the other side**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Earth's true shape is called —
a) a perfect ball  b) an oblate spheroid  c) a cube
**Answer: b) an oblate spheroid — flattened at the poles, bulging at the equator**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How much of Earth's surface is water?
a) 29%  b) 50%  c) 71%
**Answer: c) 71%**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The equatorial diameter is —
a) 12,714 km  b) 12,756 km  c) 40,075 km
**Answer: b) 12,756 km**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The polar diameter is ______ than the equatorial diameter.
a) longer  b) shorter  c) equal
**Answer: b) shorter — 12,714 km against 12,756 km**
