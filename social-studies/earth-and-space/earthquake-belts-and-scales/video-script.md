Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 84), teaches: Lesson 5: Earthquakes — vertical faults and the mid-ocean ridge; comparing earthquake intensity and magnitude on the Mercalli and Richter scales, and the seismograph (لرزه‌نگار) recording an earthquake on its graduated drum, sensitive to the faintest ground movements; the world's earthquake and volcano belts — Iran in the middle of the Alpine–Himalayan earthquake belt, the Arabian and Indian plates converging on Eurasia; the table of Iran's great deadly earthquakes of the last fifty years.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
