# Hands-on — Earthquake Belts And Scales

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 5: Earthquakes — vertical faults and the mid-ocean ridge; comparing earthquake intensity and magnitude on the Mercalli and Richter scales, and the seismograph (لرزه‌نگار) recording an earthquake on its graduated drum, sensitive to the faintest ground movements; the world's earthquake and volcano belts — Iran in the middle of the Alpine–Himalayan earthquake belt, the Arabian and Indian plates converging on Eurasia; the table of Iran's great deadly earthquakes of the last fifty years. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
