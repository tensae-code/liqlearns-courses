## Sub lesson 2 — Bhutan's way — the book's own teaching way

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 7 Geography): From Class VII Geography becomes a standalone subject organised in three strands (Time and Space; Physical Environment; People and Environment), every world topic closed with its Bhutan case — climate of Bhutan, vegetation types of Bhutan, minerals of Bhutan, and Bhutan's Himalayan disaster set (earthquake, flood, windstorm, fire, GLOF).

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 10 Geography): Class X pairs deep physical theory (Big Bang, plate tectonics, the making of the Himalayas) with an applied Bhutan block — climate and vegetation zones of Bhutan, Bhutanese agriculture's challenges and advancement, industries and their distribution, and a Bhutan disaster chapter that adds Education in Emergency. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
