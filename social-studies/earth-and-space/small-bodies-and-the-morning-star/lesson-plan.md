# Lesson plan — Small Bodies and the Morning Star

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Earth and Space · **Lesson:** Small Bodies and the Morning Star
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 2 — Earth and Space (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the solar system also holds comets with shining tails, meteors that burn up as shooting stars, and asteroids between Mars and Jupiter — and the brilliant "morning star" is not a star at all.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Solar System — the Sun and the Planets.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
