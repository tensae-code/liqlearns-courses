# Small Bodies and the Morning Star — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What trails behind a comet?
**Answer: A shining tail of gas.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who corrected the father's answer in the story, and what did he say?
**Answer: A teacher travelling with them; he said the morning star is not a true star but the planet Venus.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How is a meteor different from a meteorite?
**Answer: A meteor burns up in the air; a meteorite is the piece that reaches the ground.**
