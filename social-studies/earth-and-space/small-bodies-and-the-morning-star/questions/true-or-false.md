# Small Bodies and the Morning Star — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A shooting star is really a star falling.
**Answer: False — it is a meteor, a small body burning up in the air.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Venus shines with its own light.
**Answer: False — like every planet, it reflects sunlight.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Asteroids are also called planetoids.
**Answer: True**
