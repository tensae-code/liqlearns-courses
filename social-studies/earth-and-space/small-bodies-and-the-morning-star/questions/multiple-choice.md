# Small Bodies and the Morning Star — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Halley's comet returns every —
a) 7 years  b) 76 years  c) 760 years
**Answer: b) 76 years**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A meteor that reaches the ground is called —
a) a comet  b) a meteorite  c) an asteroid
**Answer: b) a meteorite**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Most asteroids lie between the orbits of —
a) Earth and Mars  b) Mars and Jupiter  c) Jupiter and Saturn
**Answer: b) Mars and Jupiter**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The "morning star" in the story is really —
a) a true star  b) the planet Venus  c) the Moon
**Answer: b) the planet Venus**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Kirubel was travelling from —
a) Addis Ababa to Gondar  b) Gondar to Addis Ababa  c) Harar to Addis Ababa
**Answer: b) Gondar to Addis Ababa**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Venus is —
a) the planet nearest Earth  b) the farthest planet  c) a star near the Sun
**Answer: a) the planet nearest Earth**
