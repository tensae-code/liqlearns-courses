# Hands-on — Small Bodies and the Morning Star

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dawn watch
On a clear morning, look east before sunrise. If one brilliant "star" outshines everything, you are most likely looking at Venus. Note the time and where it stood.

## Three-body sort
Make three cards: COMET, METEOR, ASTEROID. A partner reads a clue — "has a shining gas tail", "burns up in the air", "lies between Mars and Jupiter" — and you hold up the right card. Ten clues, then swap.
