# Video script — Small Bodies and the Morning Star

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before dawn, a bus waits in the dark.

A boy looks up. One body shines, bright and steady.

"Father, what is that star?"

A teacher smiles. "It is not a star at all."

"It is Venus, a planet, shining with the Sun's light."

The morning star is not a star. Remember that.
