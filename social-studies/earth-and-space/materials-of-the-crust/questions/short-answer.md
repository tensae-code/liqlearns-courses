# Materials of the Crust — Elements, Minerals and Rocks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the eight crust elements in order.
**Answer: Oxygen, silicon, aluminium, iron, calcium, sodium, potassium, magnesium.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A rock deep underground cools slowly from a melt and never reaches the surface. Which kind is it?
**Answer: Intrusive (plutonic) igneous rock.**
