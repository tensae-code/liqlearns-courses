# Materials of the Crust — Elements, Minerals and Rocks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many elements make over 98% of the crust?
a) eight  b) ninety  c) six
**Answer: a) eight**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The most abundant element in the crust is —
a) iron  b) oxygen  c) silicon
**Answer: b) oxygen**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A mineral is —
a) any stone you find  b) an inorganic solid with a specific internal structure and definite chemical composition  c) a kind of rock
**Answer: b) an inorganic solid with a specific internal structure and definite chemical composition**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the six major rock-forming mineral groups?
a) plastics  b) silicates  c) petrol
**Answer: b) silicates**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Igneous rocks form when —
a) sediments settle  b) molten rock cools  c) old rocks are squeezed
**Answer: b) molten rock cools**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Lava cooling on the surface makes —
a) intrusive igneous rock  b) extrusive (volcanic) igneous rock  c) metamorphic rock
**Answer: b) extrusive (volcanic) igneous rock**

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** Sedimentary rocks form by —
a) melting  b) transport, deposition and settling of sediments  c) heat and pressure on old rocks
**Answer: b) transport, deposition and settling of sediments**

**8. (Sub lesson 2)** Metamorphic means —
a) fire rocks  b) changed rocks  c) settled rocks
**Answer: b) changed rocks**
