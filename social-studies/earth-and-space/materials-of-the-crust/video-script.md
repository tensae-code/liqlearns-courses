Eight elements build almost all the crust.

Oxygen first. Then silicon. Then aluminium.

Elements join into minerals. Minerals build rocks.

Rock from fire: igneous. Rock from settling: sedimentary.

Rock changed by heat and pressure: metamorphic.

Three families. Pick up a stone: which family is it?
