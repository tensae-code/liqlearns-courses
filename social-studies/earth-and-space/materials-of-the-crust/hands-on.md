# Hands-on — Materials of the Crust — Elements, Minerals and Rocks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compound collection
Collect six stones around your school or home. For each, write where you found it and what people use it for. Sort them into a best-guess family and say what clue you used.

## Settling jar
Shake soil, sand and small stones in a jar of water, then let it stand. The layers that settle — heavy first, light last — are the sedimentary story in miniature.
