## Sub lesson 2 — Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 6 G6 English): geography starts from the child's own school and home — location in the Grama Niladhari → Divisional Secretariat → District → Province hierarchy, a plan of the school garden, home-made weather instruments, and care of the home surroundings — before widening to Sri Lanka's location in the world in the final chapter.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 6 G6 Tamil 2007): dialogue-driven lessons (teacher–pupil conversations) that open with Sri Lanka's location and a comparison with the world's other islands, then work through the home and its surroundings, caring for that environment, and finish with map-making and scale. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
