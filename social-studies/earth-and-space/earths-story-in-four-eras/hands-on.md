# Hands-on — The Earth's Story in Four Eras

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Era strip
Take a long strip of paper. Mark 90% of it for the Pre-Cambrian — see how little room the other three eras get. Write each era's big event in its part.

## Pangaea puzzle
Draw today's continents roughly on card, cut them out, and push Africa and South America together. Their coasts fit like puzzle pieces — that fit is part of the drift evidence.
