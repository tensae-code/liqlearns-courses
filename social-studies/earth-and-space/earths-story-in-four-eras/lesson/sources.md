Bolivia's own way (Grade 7, Secundaria 1): The Bolivian Texto de Aprendizaje runs every unit through the same four moments — Práctica, Teoría, Valoración, Producción — and frames geography inside the plurinational, Madre Tierra worldview: continental drift and geological time sit beside the Laws of Mother Earth (071 and 300), the human right to water, and a closing general geography of Bolivia itself (regions, orography, political map, salt flats and lithium).

Bolivia's own way (teacher guide — evidence): The teacher compendium teaches the same four-moment lesson shape (Práctica, Teoría, Valoración, Producción) with QR-linked videos and local openings — a La Paz landslide video opens the geography unit — and it adds two geography units the sampled student books do not carry at those grades: the scientific development of geographic science with cartography and coordinates (year 3), and water as a human right with Andean water management (year 4). That evidence is stocked here as the way Bolivia teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

North Macedonia's own way (Grade 10 mk-g10): the gymnasium course restarts Geography as a systems science — Theme 1 works through the four spheres (lithosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, biosphere) lesson by lesson, then natural disasters by sphere; Theme 2 turns to people in geographical space (world urbanisation, energy, farming, industry, transport and trade, tourism, environmental impact); Theme 3 treats the world as one global system (globalisation, transport corridors and sea straits/canals, global networks and navigation systems, international organisations, global challenges and climate-change solutions). 43 numbered lessons.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 3): after world/economic geography in Modules 1–2, Module 3 turns to Mozambique itself as a complete regional study — cities, then the country's location, coast, geology, minerals, soils, relief, climate, plant and animal geography, hydrography — and closes with Southern Africa and Mozambique's SADC integration.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 7 Geography student): opens school geography with the geographic map itself (plan, scale, azimuth, projections) before any Earth science, and runs every theme to a summary lesson plus practical work; world economy is taught branch by branch with Armenia (ՀՀ) used as the worked example in the closing questions.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 7 Geografía Física G7): Physical geography is taught sphere by sphere (lithosphere → atmosphere → hydrosphere → biosphere) but every sphere chapter closes on rational use and sustainability — wind energy, rational water use, soil protection — and Cuban evidence runs through it (Cuba's forest cover fell from ~95 % at the Spanish arrival to 16 % by 1959 and was recovered by policy).

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 6 Geografía de Cuba G6): The child's first geography book is the home country itself — one chapter of tools (coordinates, orientation, scale), then Cuba whole (situation, relief, coasts, climate, population, economy), then its two great regions landscape by landscape; the political-administrative division taught is the pre-2011 one (Ciudad de La Habana appears as a province), and History episodes woven into the regional chapters (the Santa Clara battle, the Granma landing) are named here but not placed — they belong to the History course.
