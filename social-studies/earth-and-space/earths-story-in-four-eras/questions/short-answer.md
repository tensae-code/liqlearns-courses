# The Earth's Story in Four Eras — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What is Gondwanaland?
**Answer: The great southern landmass that drifted south in the Palaeozoic.**

**2.** Name two things that happened in the Cenozoic.
**Answer: Any two of: continents reached today's places; Greenland and North America parted; Africa moved north away from Antarctica; the Americas joined at Panama.**

**3.** In which era did Pangaea split fully apart?
**Answer: The Mesozoic.**
