# The Earth's Story in Four Eras — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Earth's history is divided by fossil evidence into —
a) two eras  b) four eras  c) seven eras
**Answer: b) four eras**

**2.** The era that covers about 90% of Earth's story is —
a) Cenozoic  b) Mesozoic  c) Pre-Cambrian
**Answer: c) Pre-Cambrian**

**3.** Pangaea was —
a) an ocean  b) one supercontinent  c) a fossil
**Answer: b) one supercontinent**

**4.** The Atlantic and Indian oceans opened in the —
a) Palaeozoic  b) Mesozoic  c) Cenozoic
**Answer: b) Mesozoic**

**5.** The Pre-Cambrian ran from 4.6 billion years ago to —
a) 600 million years ago  b) 225 million years ago  c) 70 million years ago
**Answer: a) 600 million years ago**
