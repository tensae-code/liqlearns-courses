# Video script — The Earth's Story in Four Eras

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four eras. One long story.

First, almost everything: the solar system forms.

Then the continents join into one: Pangaea.

Then Pangaea splits, and oceans open.

Last, the continents drift to today's places.

The ground under you is still moving.
