# The Fascist invasion — preparation and the Walwal pretext — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy's fundamental motive in 1935 was —
a) Trade  b) To avenge the shame of Adwa and fulfil the colonial dream  c) To help the League  d) Border security
**Answer: b) To avenge the shame of Adwa and fulfil the colonial dream** — Revenge for Adwa; the colonial dream fulfilled.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The consulates at Adwa, Gondar, Debre Markos and Dessie were opened to —
a) Teach Italian  b) Gather information  c) Sell goods  d) Register births
**Answer: b) Gather information** — To gather information — listening posts.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Eritrea's governor who worked to split Ethiopia was —
a) Graziani  b) Corrado Zoli  c) Badoglio  d) De Bono
**Answer: b) Corrado Zoli** — Corrado Zoli.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy stockpiled troops and arms in —
a) Djibouti and Kenya  b) Eritrea and Somaliland  c) Sudan  d) Libya only
**Answer: b) Eritrea and Somaliland** — Eritrea and Somaliland.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Walwal clash happened on —
a) December 5, 1934  b) October 3, 1935  c) May 2, 1889  d) March 31, 1936
**Answer: a) December 5, 1934** — December 5, 1934.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the 1908 boundary agreement Walwal lay —
a) Inside Italian Somaliland  b) Inside Ethiopia  c) In Sudan  d) On the sea
**Answer: b) Inside Ethiopia** — Inside Ethiopia.
