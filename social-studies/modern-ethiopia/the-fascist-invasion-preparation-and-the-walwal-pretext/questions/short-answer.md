# The Fascist invasion — preparation and the Walwal pretext — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Italy prepare the invasion from inside Ethiopia?
**Answer:** Consulates as listening posts in four towns, Zoli working to split the country, and lords of Tigray, Wollo, Begemdir and Gojjam bribed and incited against the emperor.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Ethiopia do after Walwal?
**Answer:** Refused Italy's demand for a public apology and indemnity, and took the case to the League of Nations for peaceful settlement.
