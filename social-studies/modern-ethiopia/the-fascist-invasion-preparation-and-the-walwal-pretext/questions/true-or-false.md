# The Fascist invasion — preparation and the Walwal pretext — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ras Hailu, Aba Jifar and Haile Selassie Gugsa became Italian collaborators.
**Answer: True** — Named in the book, aggrieved at the central administration.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia accepted Italy's demand for an apology and indemnity.
**Answer: False** — Ethiopia refused and took the case to the League of Nations.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Italy attacked the border police at Walwal first.
**Answer: True** — December 5, 1934 — then Italy accused Ethiopia.
