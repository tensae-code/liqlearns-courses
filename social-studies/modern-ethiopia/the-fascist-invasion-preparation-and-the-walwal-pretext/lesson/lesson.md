# The Fascist invasion — preparation and the Walwal pretext

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Italy prepared revenge for Adwa for decades — consulates as listening posts, lords bribed — and found its pretext at Walwal, December 5, 1934. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Revenge prepared

After Adwa Italy prepared long and invaded again in **1935**: the fundamental motive was to **avenge the shame of Adwa** and fulfil the colonial dream. Hiding the aim, Fascist Italy opened **consulates at Adwa, Gondar, Debre Markos and Dessie** to gather information, worked through Eritrea's governor **Corrado Zoli** to split Ethiopia internally, and **bribed and incited the lords** of Tigray, Wollo, Begemdir and Gojjam against the emperor — **Ras Hailu of Gojjam, Aba Jifar of Jimma and Dejazmach Haile Selassie Gugsa of Tigray** among those who became Italian collaborators, aggrieved at Haile Selassie's central administration. Italy stockpiled troops and arms in **Eritrea and Somaliland**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Walwal — the manufactured pretext

The pretext: under the **1908 boundary agreement Walwal lay inside Ethiopia**, but on **December 5, 1934** Italians attacked Ethiopian border police there; Italy then accused Ethiopia, demanding a **public apology and war indemnity**. Ethiopia refused and took the case to the **League of Nations** for a peaceful settlement.
