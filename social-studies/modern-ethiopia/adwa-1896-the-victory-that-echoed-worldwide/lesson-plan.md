# Lesson plan — Adwa 1896 — the victory that echoed worldwide

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Adwa 1896 — the victory that echoed worldwide
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Social, Economic and Political Developments in Ethiopia, Mid-19th C. to 1941 (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Italy turned a treaty's two texts into a claim: the **Treaty of Wuchale's Article XVII** differed between its Amharic and Italian versions — Italy read a **protectorate** into it. The claim was settled at the **Battle of Adwa (1896)**: the **first decisive victory of Black people over a powerful white European power**, which brought down Crispi's government and echoed to freedom fighters worldwide.
**This lesson's own level:** it crowns Unit 3's Ethiopia story — the aggressions lesson leads straight to this field. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Wuchale — one treaty, two texts** — Explain the Treaty of Wuchale and Article XVII's difference between the Amharic and Italian versions — the clause Italy read as a protectorate.
2. **Adwa — the morning that changed the story** — Tell Adwa (1896) as the first decisive victory of Black people over a powerful white European power; a source of pride and motivation for freedom fighters worldwide; and the fall of Premier Francesco Crispi's government in Italy.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

3. Name the Italian political consequence: Crispi's government fell.
