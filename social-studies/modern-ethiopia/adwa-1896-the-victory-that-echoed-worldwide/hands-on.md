# Hands-on — Adwa 1896 — the victory that echoed worldwide

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two texts
Write Article XVII twice, as the lesson describes: the Amharic version (Ethiopia may use Italy's good offices) and the Italian claim (Ethiopia must). Underline the single word-family where the freedom of a country hides: may versus must.

## Echo map
On a world map, draw arrows out of Adwa labelled with the book's echo: 'pride and motivation for freedom fighters worldwide'. Add one arrow into Italy labelled 'Crispi's government falls'.
