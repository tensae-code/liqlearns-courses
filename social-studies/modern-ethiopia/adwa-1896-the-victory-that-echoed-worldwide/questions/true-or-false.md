# Adwa 1896 — the victory that echoed worldwide — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Amharic and Italian versions of Wuchale's Article XVII said the same thing.
**Answer: False** — They differed — that difference is the lesson's first task.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Adwa discouraged freedom fighters in the colonised world.
**Answer: False** — It was a source of pride and motivation for freedom fighters worldwide.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia emerged from Adwa still an Italian protectorate.
**Answer: False** — Adwa destroyed the protectorate claim; Ethiopia kept its sovereignty.
