# Adwa 1896 — the victory that echoed worldwide — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The disputed treaty was the Treaty of —
a) Hewett  b) Wuchale  c) Frankfurt  d) Berlin
**Answer: b) Wuchale** — The Treaty of Wuchale carried the disputed clause.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The differing clause was —
a) Article I  b) Article XVII  c) Article 34  d) The preamble
**Answer: b) Article XVII** — Article XVII differed between the versions.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy read the clause as making Ethiopia —
a) An ally of France  b) A protectorate — bound to use Italy in its foreign affairs  c) A member of the Zollverein  d) A free-trade area only
**Answer: b) A protectorate — bound to use Italy in its foreign affairs** — Italy read a protectorate into the Italian version.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Battle of Adwa was fought in —
a) 1889  b) 1896  c) 1906  d) 1936
**Answer: b) 1896** — Adwa, 1896.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Adwa is described as —
a) A minor border clash  b) The first decisive victory of Black people over a powerful white European power  c) Italy's greatest victory  d) A naval battle
**Answer: b) The first decisive victory of Black people over a powerful white European power** — The book's own description of Adwa.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy, Adwa brought down the government of —
a) Cavour  b) Premier Francesco Crispi  c) Mussolini  d) King William
**Answer: b) Premier Francesco Crispi** — Crispi's government fell after Adwa.
