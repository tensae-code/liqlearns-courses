# Adwa 1896 — the victory that echoed worldwide — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elaborate the difference between the two versions of Article XVII, as the book sets pupils to do.
**Answer:** The Italian version bound Ethiopia to conduct its foreign relations through Italy — read by Italy as a protectorate; the Amharic version carried no such obligation. The protectorate claim rested on that gap between the texts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give Adwa's three consequences as the book states them.
**Answer:** It settled Italy's colonising attempt by force — Ethiopia's sovereignty preserved; it brought down Premier Francesco Crispi's government in Italy; and it became a source of pride and motivation for freedom fighters worldwide, as the first decisive victory of Black people over a powerful white European power.
**3. (Unit 3 review — Grade 12)** Unit 3 review: Name the three rulers who consolidated central government 1855–1913 and one act of each.
**Answer: Tewodros II (reunification by force), Yohannes IV (held the north against Egypt, Mahdists and Italy), Menilek II (built the modern central state and expanded it).**

**4. (Unit 3 review — Grade 12)** Unit 3 review: Why does Adwa echo beyond Ethiopia?
**Answer: It destroyed a European invasion in open battle and became a world symbol of anti-colonial resistance, sealed by the Treaty of Addis Ababa.**
