# Video script — Adwa 1896 — the victory that echoed worldwide


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on northern Ethiopia — hills, and two armies moving toward one small town: Adwa.

But first, a document. Two pages side by side — Amharic, Italian. Zoom to one clause: Article Seventeen.

In one language, a favour that may be asked. In the other, a chain that must be worn. A protectorate, claimed by a sentence.

The emperor reads. The treaty is denounced. The columns march.

Dawn, 1896. From every hill path, Ethiopia's armies converge — tens of thousands, one nation.

By afternoon the Italian army is broken. In Rome, a government falls.

And the news crosses oceans: a Black nation has defeated a European power, decisively. Freedom fighters everywhere lift their heads.
