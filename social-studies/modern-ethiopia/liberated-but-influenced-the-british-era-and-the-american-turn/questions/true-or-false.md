# Liberated but influenced — the British era and the American turn — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Official recognition of sovereignty ended all foreign influence in Ethiopia at once.
**Answer: False** — British influence continued in fact through the treaties.

**Answer: True** — the written form of the influence.
