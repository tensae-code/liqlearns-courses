# Hands-on — Liberated but influenced — the British era and the American turn

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Evaluate the treaty
Make two columns — Ethiopia gained / Ethiopia conceded — for the 1942 and 1944 treaties, from the lesson. Then write the verdict the lesson invites: was the price fair for 1942? Defend it in three sentences.

## The changing anchor
Draw Ethiopia as a harbour and the great powers as ships at anchor: Britain 1941, America by the 1950s. Label each ship with what it wanted and what it gave.
