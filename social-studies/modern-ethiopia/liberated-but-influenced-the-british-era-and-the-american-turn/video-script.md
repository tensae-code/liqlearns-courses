# Video script — Liberated but influenced — the British era and the American turn

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Addis Ababa, 5 May 1941. The Emperor's car enters; flags rise; the occupation's colour drains from the map.
But one flag stays planted beside the palace — Britain's influence, in treaty ink: 1942, 1944.
The years turn. Across the Atlantic another flag brightens; the anchor chain shifts, link by link, from London to Washington.
The empire is free, restored — and moored, now, to a new great power.
