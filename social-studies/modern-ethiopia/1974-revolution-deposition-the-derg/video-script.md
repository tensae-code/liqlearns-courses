# Video script — 1974 — revolution, deposition, the Derg

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Addis Ababa, February 1974. A taxi queue strikes; a teachers' march; a barracks gate opens from inside.
Streams of people converge on the palace quarter — peasants' petitions, students' slogans, soldiers' demands, all arriving in the same weeks.
A committee room: junior officers around a table, a radio microphone, a list of demands becoming a list of ministers.
An old man is led from the palace. The imperial flag comes down. The monarchy — 1930 to 1974 in one reign — ends.
