# Hands-on — 1974 — revolution, deposition, the Derg

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The streams meet
Draw four streams — countryside, campus, barracks, city — each labelled with its grievance from the earlier lessons, joining into one river marked February 1974.

## Who authored it?
Debate in pairs: 'The revolution was made by the crowds' against 'The revolution was taken by the committee.' Each side must use the lesson's sequence as evidence.
