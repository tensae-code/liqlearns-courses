# Lesson plan — 1974 — revolution, deposition, the Derg

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** 1974 — revolution, deposition, the Derg
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the opposition unit's climax; Grade 10 Unit 6 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in February 1974, amid worsening conditions and protests from every section of society, the movement that toppled the empire began: the spontaneous rising enabled the organised military to assume power — the Emperor was deposed, the Derg emerged, and the monarchy ended.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on all the opposition lessons — peasants, coup, students are the three streams that meet in 1974. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **February 1974 — every section protests** — worsening social, economic and political conditions; protests from every section of society open the revolution.
2. **The military assumes power — deposition and the Derg** — the spontaneous movement enables the organised military to take power; the Emperor is deposed; the Derg emerges and the monarchy ends.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The student movement — the empire's children turn critics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Date the revolution's opening — February 1974.
2. Name the sections of society in protest.
3. Tell the sequence: spontaneous movement, military assumes power, deposition, Derg.

## What you need
