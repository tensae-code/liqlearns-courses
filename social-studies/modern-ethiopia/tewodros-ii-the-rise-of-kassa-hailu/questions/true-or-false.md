# Tewodros II — the rise of Kassa Hailu — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kassa gained church education and traditional military training under Dejazmach Kinfu.
**Answer: True** — Raised by Kinfu after his parents' death.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ras Ali of Yejju was defeated at Guramba in 1852.
**Answer: False** — Goshu of Gojjam fell at Guramba (1852); Ras Ali fell at Ayshal (1853).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Abune Selama crowned Kassa at Derasge in 1855.
**Answer: True** — Crowned Tewodros II, King of Kings, at Derasge Maryam church.
