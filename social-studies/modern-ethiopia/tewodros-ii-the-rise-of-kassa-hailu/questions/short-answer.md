# Tewodros II — the rise of Kassa Hailu — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Kassa learn during his shifta years?
**Answer:** How armies are organised and how lands are administered.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Kassa's four victories in order, with years.
**Answer:** Guramba 1852 (Goshu), Gorgora Bichen 1853 (Birru Aligaz), Ayshal 1853 (Ras Ali), Derasge 1855 (Wube).
