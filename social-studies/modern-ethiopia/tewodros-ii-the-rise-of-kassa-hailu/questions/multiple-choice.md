# Tewodros II — the rise of Kassa Hailu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kassa Hailu was born about 1818 in —
a) Gondar  b) Qwara  c) Shewa  d) Harar
**Answer: b) Qwara** — Born about 1818 in Qwara.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After Dejazmach Kinfu died in 1839, Kassa —
a) Became a monk  b) Took to shifta life  c) Left Ethiopia  d) Was crowned
**Answer: b) Took to shifta life** — He took to shifta life and learned army organisation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At Debarki in 1848 Kassa was —
a) Crowned  b) Defeated by the Egyptians  c) Made Ras  d) Given modern guns
**Answer: b) Defeated by the Egyptians** — The Egyptians defeated him at Debarki.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Debarki defeat shaped his drive for —
a) Sea trade  b) Modern weapons and a disciplined standing army  c) A navy  d) Church land
**Answer: b) Modern weapons and a disciplined standing army** — Modern weapons and a disciplined standing army.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Kassa was crowned Tewodros II in 1855 at —
a) Aksum  b) Derasge Maryam church  c) Gondar castle  d) Debre Tabor
**Answer: b) Derasge Maryam church** — Crowned by Abune Selama at Derasge Maryam church.
