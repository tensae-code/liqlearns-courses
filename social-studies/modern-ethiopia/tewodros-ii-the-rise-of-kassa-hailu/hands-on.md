# Hands-on — Tewodros II — the rise of Kassa Hailu

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the route
On a hand-drawn map mark Qwara, Guramba, Ayshal and Derasge. Walk a finger along the route, saying the year at each stop.

## One defeat, one lesson
Write 'Debarki 1848' on a stone or card. Under it write what it taught him: modern weapons, a disciplined standing army.
