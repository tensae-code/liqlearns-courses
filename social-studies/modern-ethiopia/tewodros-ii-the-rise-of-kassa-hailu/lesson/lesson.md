# Tewodros II — the rise of Kassa Hailu

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Kassa Hailu, beaten by Egypt at Debarki in 1848, won battle after battle and was crowned Tewodros II at Derasge in 1855. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — From Qwara to shifta life

**Kassa Hailu** was born about **1818 in Qwara**, son of **Dejazmach Hailu Welde Giorgis** and **Woizero Ategeba**. Orphaned, he was raised by **Dejazmach Kinfu**, gaining a church education and traditional military training. When Kinfu died in **1839**, Kassa took to **shifta life** — life outside the law in the hills — and there learned how armies are organised and lands administered.

## Sub lesson 2 — Defeat at Debarki, then victory after victory

In **1848** the Egyptians defeated him at **Debarki** — a defeat that shaped his drive for **modern weapons and a disciplined standing army**. Then the victories came in sequence: over Gojjam's **Goshu at Guramba (1852)**; over **Birru Aligaz** and three other dejazmachs at **Gorgora Bichen (1853)**; over the last Yejju lord **Ras Ali at Ayshal (1853)**; and over **Wube at Derasge (1855)**, where **Abune Selama crowned him Tewodros II, King of Kings, at Derasge Maryam church**.
