# Ethiopia in transition, 1991–1995 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The government after 1991 was the —
a) Imperial council  b) EPRDF-led Transitional Government  c) Derg restored  d) A UN administration
**Answer: b) b) EPRDF-led Transitional Government**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The transition's founding law was the —
a) 1955 constitution  b) Transitional charter  c) Wuchale treaty  d) 1987 constitution
**Answer: b) b) Transitional charter**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Eritrea became independent in —
a) 1991  b) 1993  c) 1995  d) 1962
**Answer: b) b) 1993**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Eritrea's independence followed —
a) A thirty-year insurgency and war  b) A foreign invasion  c) A royal decree  d) A drought
**Answer: a) a) A thirty-year insurgency and war**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The transition reordered the state on —
a) The old provinces  b) Ethnic-federal lines  c) Colonial borders  d) River basins only
**Answer: b) b) Ethnic-federal lines**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The reordering prepared the way for —
a) The empire's return  b) The 1995 constitution  c) The Derg's party  d) A unitary state
**Answer: b) b) The 1995 constitution**
