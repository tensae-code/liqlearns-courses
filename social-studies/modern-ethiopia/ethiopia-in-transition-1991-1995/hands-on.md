# Hands-on — Ethiopia in transition, 1991–1995

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Charter in five lines
Write a five-line charter for a classroom republic: who governs in-between, what must be decided, what right every group keeps. Compare its jobs with the real charter's.

## Two maps
Sketch Ethiopia's map before 1991 and after 1993. Mark what changed at the coast, and say in one line what 'landlocked' costs and means.
