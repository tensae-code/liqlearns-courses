# Hands-on — Ethiopia's UNESCO heritages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Register card
Make a museum card for one registered place or practice: name, type (cultural/natural/intangible), one fact, one duty. Write it as the register would.

## Guard duty roster
List three threats to one heritage near you (neglect, building over, the doing lapsing). Against each, write the guard action a pupil can actually take.
