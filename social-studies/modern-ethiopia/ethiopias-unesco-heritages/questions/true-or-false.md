# Ethiopia's UNESCO heritages — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gada system is inscribed as intangible heritage.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Intangible heritage can be preserved by putting it in a museum case.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 12 course ends on heritage and preservation, not on a government.
**Answer: True**
**4. (Unit 9 review — Grade 12)** True or false: Irreecha is among Ethiopia's intangible heritages discussed in the Unit 9 review.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Unit 9 review — Grade 12)** True or false: Timket and Meskel are inscribed Ethiopian intangible heritages.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Unit 9 review — Grade 12)** True or false: The Unit 9 review treats preservation duties as somebody else's problem, not the pupils'.
**Answer: False — The review's items put preservation duties on the pupils' own generation.**

**7. (Unit 9 review — Grade 12)** True or false: The Gada system and Fiche Chembelela stand among the intangible inscriptions the unit discusses.
**Answer: True**
