# Ethiopia's UNESCO heritages — true or false

**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Intangible heritage can be preserved by putting it in a museum case.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 12 course ends on heritage and preservation, not on a government.
