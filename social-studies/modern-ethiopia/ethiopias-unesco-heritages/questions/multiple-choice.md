# Ethiopia's UNESCO heritages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lalibela's churches were —
a) Built upward in brick  b) Cut downward into living rock  c) Imported stone by stone  d) Painted caves only
**Answer: b) b) Cut downward into living rock**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Harar Jegol is —
a) A terraced hillside  b) A walled city of mosques and lanes  c) A stele field  d) A castle group
**Answer: b) b) A walled city of mosques and lanes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is intangible heritage? —
a) Aksum's obelisks  b) Timket  c) Gondar's castles  d) Tiya's stones
**Answer: b) b) Timket**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Fiche Chembelela is —
a) A harvest tax  b) The Sidama new year  c) A Gondar festival  d) A Harari gate
**Answer: b) b) The Sidama new year**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The book says Ethiopia's unregistered heritage is —
a) Worthless  b) A wealth no list has reached yet  c) Forbidden to visit  d) All destroyed
**Answer: b) b) A wealth no list has reached yet**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Heritage survives, in the book's closing argument, only if —
a) Tourists pay  b) Each generation guards it  c) It is fenced forever  d) The UN guards it alone
**Answer: b) b) Each generation guards it**
