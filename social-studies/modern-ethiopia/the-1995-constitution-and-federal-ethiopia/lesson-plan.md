# Lesson plan — The 1995 Constitution and federal Ethiopia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** The 1995 Constitution and federal Ethiopia
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A federal republic** — Say what the 1995 FDRE constitution established: a federal democratic republic of regional states.
2. **Nations, nationalities, and Article 39** — State the constitution's rights for nations and nationalities, including Article 39's right of secession, exactly as the book presents it.
3. **Elections and the party landscape since 1995** — Say that elections and a multi-party landscape have operated since 1995 under the constitution's frame.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

2. State the nations' and nationalities' rights, including Article 39.
3. Say how the country has been governed since 1995 in the constitution's frame.
