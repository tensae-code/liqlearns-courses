# Hands-on — The 1995 Constitution and federal Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Divide the power
List six jobs of governing (army, schools, police, language of instruction, taxes, courts). For each, argue federal or regional — then check your instinct against the lesson's idea of a federation.

## Article 39 debate
Hold the guide's own method: a short formal debate, two speakers a side, on whether a constitution should carry a secession clause. Every argument must use the lesson's facts.
