# The 1995 Constitution and federal Ethiopia — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The FDRE constitution was adopted in 1995.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Article 39 removes all rights from nations and nationalities.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The constitution guarantees nations' rights to language, culture and self-government.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The party landscape since 1995 has stayed completely unchanged.
**Answer: False**
