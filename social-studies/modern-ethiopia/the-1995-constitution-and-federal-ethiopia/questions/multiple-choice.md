# The 1995 Constitution and federal Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1995 constitution made Ethiopia —
a) A unitary empire  b) A federal democratic republic  c) A military republic  d) A colony
**Answer: b) b) A federal democratic republic**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In a federation, power is —
a) Held by one office  b) Divided between federal government and regional states  c) Given to the army  d) Abolished
**Answer: b) b) Divided between federal government and regional states**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sovereignty under the constitution rests with —
a) The army  b) The nations, nationalities and peoples of Ethiopia  c) Foreign guarantors  d) The capital city
**Answer: b) b) The nations, nationalities and peoples of Ethiopia**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Article 39 concerns —
a) Tax rates  b) The right of nations and nationalities, including secession by the constitution's procedures  c) The army's size  d) The flag
**Answer: b) b) The right of nations and nationalities, including secession by the constitution's procedures**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Since 1995 the country has been governed through —
a) Hereditary rule  b) Elections under the constitution  c) Military decree only  d) Foreign administration
**Answer: b) b) Elections under the constitution**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Elections under the 1995 constitution are held for —
a) The emperor  b) Federal and regional governments  c) The army command  d) Foreign observers
**Answer: b) Federal and regional governments**
