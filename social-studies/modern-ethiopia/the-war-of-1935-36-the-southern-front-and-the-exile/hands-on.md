# Hands-on — The war of 1935–36 — the southern front and the exile

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The desert march
Draw the Dolo road across a bare page. Mark 'no provisions', 'epidemic', 'Genale Doria — three days' along it.

## A speech unanswered
Stand and read one sentence as the emperor to a silent room: 'We ask the League to judge.' Then sit in the silence — that was the answer.
