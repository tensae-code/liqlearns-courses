# The war of 1935–36 — the southern front and the exile — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three Ethiopian commanders in the south.
**Answer:** Any three of: Dejazmach Nasibu, Ras Desta Damtew, Dejazmach Balcha Safo, Dejazmach Endalkachew Makonnen (and Grazmach Afework at Qorahe).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did Haile Selassie go into exile?
**Answer:** With both fronts collapsed, he went through Djibouti to Britain to present Italy's invasion in person to the League of Nations.
