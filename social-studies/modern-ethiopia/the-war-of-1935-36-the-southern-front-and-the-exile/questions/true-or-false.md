# The war of 1935–36 — the southern front and the exile — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Qorahe's fall opened Jijiga and Harar to Graziani.
**Answer: True** — The book's sequence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After Genale Doria, Ras Desta surrendered to Graziani.
**Answer: False** — He retreated inland and began the first guerrilla resistance.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The League gave the exiled emperor a satisfactory answer.
**Answer: False** — The Europeans gave no satisfactory answer.
