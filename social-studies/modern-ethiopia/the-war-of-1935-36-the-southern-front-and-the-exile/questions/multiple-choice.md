# The war of 1935–36 — the southern front and the exile — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Graziani's first strategic target in the south was —
a) Qorahe  b) Harar itself  c) Addis Ababa  d) Dolo Ado
**Answer: a) Qorahe** — Qorahe, on the road to Jijiga and Harar.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The strong defence at Qorahe was mounted by —
a) Grazmach Afework  b) Ras Desta  c) Balcha Safo  d) Nasibu
**Answer: a) Grazmach Afework** — Grazmach Afework.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Graziani entered Ethiopia through —
a) Eritrea  b) The Ogaden  c) Kenya  d) Sudan
**Answer: b) The Ogaden** — Through the Ogaden.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ras Desta's army lost many men to —
a) A flood  b) Epidemic on the desert march to Dolo Ado  c) A mutiny  d) Gas at Qorahe
**Answer: b) Epidemic on the desert march to Dolo Ado** — Epidemic, crossing the desert without adequate provisions.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** At Genale Doria the weakened army fought —
a) Thirteen hours  b) Three days  c) Six days  d) One hour
**Answer: b) Three days** — Three days, and was defeated.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Haile Selassie left into exile through —
a) Mitsiwa  b) Djibouti  c) Khartoum  d) Zeila
**Answer: b) Djibouti** — Through Djibouti, to Britain.
