# Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Menilek's expansion was ____ — greater than any before it.
**Answer: unprecedented**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The third method, beside subjugation and submission, was boundary-____ treaties.
**Answer: delimitation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Menilek's reforms formed the founding ____ of the modern state.
**Answer: order**
