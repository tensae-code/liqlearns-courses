# Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Menilek's expansion was achieved by peaceful means alone.
**Answer: False** — It combined forceful subjugation, peaceful submission and treaties.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's present shape is credited to Menilek II.
**Answer: True** — The book credits him with creating the present shape of Ethiopia.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Menilek neglected administration while expanding.
**Answer: False** — He combined expansion with centralisation and modernisation — administrative and religious reforms.
