# Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Menilek's three methods of expansion with one line each.
**Answer:** Forceful subjugation — rule imposed by armies; peaceful submission — rulers accepting his overlordship without war; boundary-delimitation treaties — the state's edges fixed by diplomacy against surrounding powers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the book join expansion and centralisation in one lesson?
**Answer:** Because territory alone is not a state: Menilek's administrative and religious reforms drew the enlarged lands under one government's order — the founding order of the modern state.
