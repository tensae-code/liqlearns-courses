# Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Menilek II is credited with —
a) Ending the Zemene-Mesafint  b) Unprecedented territorial expansion and the creation of Ethiopia's present shape  c) Founding Gondar  d) The 1931 constitution
**Answer: b) Unprecedented territorial expansion and the creation of Ethiopia's present shape** — Unprecedented expansion and the present shape of Ethiopia.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** His expansion combined —
a) Only war  b) Forceful subjugation, peaceful submission and boundary-delimitation treaties  c) Only treaties  d) Trade alone
**Answer: b) Forceful subjugation, peaceful submission and boundary-delimitation treaties** — All three methods combined.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Peaceful submission meant —
a) Rulers accepting Menilek's overlordship without war  b) Surrender after siege  c) Colonial partition  d) Exile of all rulers
**Answer: a) Rulers accepting Menilek's overlordship without war** — Submission without war — overlordship accepted.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Boundary-delimitation treaties fixed —
a) Tax rates  b) The new state's edges against the powers around it  c) Church doctrines  d) Trade prices
**Answer: b) The new state's edges against the powers around it** — They delimited the state's boundaries.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Menilek paired expansion with —
a) Decentralisation only  b) Centralisation and modernisation attempts  c) Isolation  d) Abolishing the army
**Answer: b) Centralisation and modernisation attempts** — Administrative and religious reforms under one centre.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The making-of-the-state lesson runs from —
a) 1270 to 1500  b) 1855 (Tewodros) to Menilek's reign — the founding order of the modern state  c) 1930 to 1974  d) 1991 to today
**Answer: b) 1855 (Tewodros) to Menilek's reign — the founding order of the modern state** — From Kassa's crowning in 1855 through Menilek — the founding order.
