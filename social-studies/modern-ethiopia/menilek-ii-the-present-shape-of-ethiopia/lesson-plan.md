# Lesson plan — Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Social, Economic and Political Developments in Ethiopia, Mid-19th C. to 1941 (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** **Menilek II** is credited with **unprecedented territorial expansion** and the **creation of the present shape of Ethiopia** — by **forceful subjugation, peaceful submission and boundary-delimitation treaties** combined — together with the **centralisation and modernisation** that founded the modern state's order.
**This lesson's own level:** it completes Unit 3's three state-makers: Tewodros began, Yohannes held, Menilek shaped. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Expansion by three methods** — Explain Menilek's expansion by its three combined methods: forceful subjugation, peaceful submission, and boundary-delimitation treaties — producing Ethiopia's present shape.
2. **Centralisation and modernisation — the founding order** — State that Menilek combined expansion with centralisation and modernisation attempts — administrative and religious reforms, the founding order of the modern state — closing the 1855–1913 making-of-the-state lesson.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Menilek's three methods of expansion.
2. Say what he is credited with creating: Ethiopia's present shape.
3. Describe the centralisation and modernisation that accompanied expansion.
