# Hands-on — Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-method map
On an outline map of today's Ethiopia, mark three regions with three different symbols — sword (subjugation), handshake (submission), pen (treaty) — as a class discussion assigns them. The mixed map is the lesson.

## Boundary walk
Walk your schoolyard's boundary slowly, as a treaty commission would. At each corner, say what a boundary-delimitation treaty must settle: whose land, whose people, what happens to those the line divides.
