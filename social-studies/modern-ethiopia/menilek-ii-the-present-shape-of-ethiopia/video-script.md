# Video script — Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Shewa — and begins to breathe outward.

South, east, west: the colour spreads. Some lands bow and join in peace.

Others are taken by war. The sword draws part of this map — the book does not hide it.

Now men with pens: treaty tables at the edges, lines agreed against the empires pressing in.

The boundary crawls — and closes. Look at the shape. You know it. It is Ethiopia today.

And inside the line, one government reaches every province: clerks, taxes, reforms.

Menilek's work stands: the present shape, and the modern state inside it.
