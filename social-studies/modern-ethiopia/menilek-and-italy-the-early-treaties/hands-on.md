# Hands-on — Menilek and Italy — the early treaties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two aims, one table
Draw a table with two chairs. On one side write Italy's aim, on the other Menilek's. Put the Let Marefia card in the middle.

## Arms road
On a map draw Assab → Ankober and mark Let Marefia — the road Menilek's firearms would travel.
