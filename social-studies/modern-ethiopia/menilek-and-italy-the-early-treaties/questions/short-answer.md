# Menilek and Italy — the early treaties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did each side want from the early Italy tie?
**Answer:** Italy wanted to study Ethiopia for colonial expansion; Menilek wanted firearms to supply his bid for the crown.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did Italy need the neutrality treaty after Dogali?
**Answer:** Beaten by Alula, Italy badly needed Menilek not to join Yohannes — so it bought his neutrality by treaty.
