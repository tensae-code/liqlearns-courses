# Menilek and Italy — the early treaties — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy promised Menilek a place at Assab for his arms-buying agent.
**Answer: True** — Part of the early bargain.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Antonelli's mission began in 1882.
**Answer: True** — Pietro Antonelli from 1882 deepened the tie.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Menilek entered the neutrality treaty eagerly.
**Answer: False** — Italy persuaded the unwilling Menilek.
