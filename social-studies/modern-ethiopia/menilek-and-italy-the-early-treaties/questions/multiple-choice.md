# Menilek and Italy — the early treaties — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Italian Geographical Society reached Shewa in 1876 under —
a) Antonelli  b) Marquis Orazio Antinori  c) Baratieri  d) Hewett
**Answer: b) Marquis Orazio Antinori** — Marquis Orazio Antinori.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The mission's scientific cover served —
a) Mapping rivers only  b) Studying Ethiopia for colonial expansion  c) Translating books  d) Building schools
**Answer: b) Studying Ethiopia for colonial expansion** — Studying Ethiopia for colonial expansion.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Menilek wanted Italy chiefly for —
a) Ships  b) Firearms for his bid for the crown  c) Doctors  d) Railways
**Answer: b) Firearms for his bid for the crown** — Firearms.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The station Menilek granted Italy was —
a) Let Marefia, near Ankober  b) Fort Marefia at Harar  c) Assab itself  d) A post at Mitsiwa
**Answer: a) Let Marefia, near Ankober** — Let Marefia near Ankober.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1883 treaty provided for —
a) Exchange of consuls, free trade and movement, free preaching of religion  b) A military alliance  c) Sale of Eritrea  d) A protectorate
**Answer: a) Exchange of consuls, free trade and movement, free preaching of religion** — Consuls, free trade and movement, free preaching.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** By the 1887 neutrality treaty Menilek agreed to —
a) Attack Yohannes  b) Stay neutral in Italy's war with Yohannes  c) Pay tribute to Italy  d) Cede Ankober
**Answer: b) Stay neutral in Italy's war with Yohannes** — Stay neutral — though unwilling.
