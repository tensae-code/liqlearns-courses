# Menilek and Italy — the early treaties

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Menilek's Italy tie began in 1876 with the Antinori mission and the Let Marefia station — Italy wanted a colony; Menilek wanted firearms. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — A scientific mission with a colonial purpose

Menilek's formal contact with Italy began in **1876**, when the **Italian Geographical Society** reached Shewa under **Marquis Orazio Antinori** — a mission whose scientific cover served **studying Ethiopia for colonial expansion**. Menilek's own aim was plain: he wanted Italy for the **firearms** it could supply his bid for the crown. He granted the Italians the **Let Marefia station near Ankober**, and Italy promised him a place at **Assab** for his arms-buying agent.

## Sub lesson 2 — Friendship, commerce — and neutrality

**Pietro Antonelli's** mission from **1882** deepened the tie: a **treaty of friendship and commerce in 1883** — exchange of consuls, free trade and movement of citizens, free preaching of religion — and after Dogali, when Italy badly needed Menilek's help, the **1887 'neutrality treaty'** by which Italy persuaded the unwilling Menilek to **stay neutral in her war with Yohannes**.
