# Hands-on — Heritage hunt: your own town

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The hunt itself
This lesson's hands-on IS the guide's own task: in a small group, walk (or, where walking is not possible, recall and map) your own town and list its tangible and intangible heritage sites and practices. For each find write: what it is, where it is, who keeps it, and one line on why it matters. Bring the list back for the group naming in Sub lesson 3, and let the teacher help with the identifications you are unsure of.

## The keeper interview
Choose one intangible find — a craft, a festival, a ceremony — and ask the person who keeps it two questions: how did you learn it, and who are you teaching it to? Write the answers beside the find. Heritage that is being taught is heritage that is surviving.
