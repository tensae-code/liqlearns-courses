# Heritage hunt: your own town — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tangible heritage is —
a) Heritage that lives in doing  b) Heritage you can stand in front of — sites and things  c) Heritage from abroad  d) Written heritage only
**Answer: b) Heritage you can stand in front of — sites and things**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A festival carried on by doing it each year is —
a) Tangible heritage  b) Not heritage  c) Intangible heritage  d) Natural heritage only
**Answer: c) Intangible heritage**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The hunt widens in this order —
a) Region, zone, town  b) Town, zone, region  c) Capital, town, village  d) Region only
**Answer: b) Town, zone, region**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A proper find record has four parts. Which set is right? —
a) Name, price, owner, buyer  b) What it is, where it is, who keeps it, why it matters  c) Date, ruler, war, treaty  d) Photo, map, song, story
**Answer: b) What it is, where it is, who keeps it, why it matters**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the group naming, the teacher's role is to —
a) Write the whole list alone  b) Help the identification and settle hard cases  c) Mark the list and keep it  d) Choose the town's heritage by decree
**Answer: b) Help the identification and settle hard cases**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The agreed group list matters first because it is —
a) The first step of preserving the heritage  b) A competition entry  c) A tax record  d) A tourist advertisement
**Answer: a) The first step of preserving the heritage**
