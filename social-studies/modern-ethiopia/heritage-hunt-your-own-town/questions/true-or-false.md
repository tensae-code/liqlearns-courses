# Heritage hunt: your own town — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If you can only photograph people in the act, the heritage is intangible.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A town's heritage hunt should stop exactly at the town's edge.
**Answer: False** — The hunt widens to the zone and the region.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Arguing in the group about whether a find truly counts is a failure of the method.
**Answer: False** — Deciding together what counts is the method itself.
