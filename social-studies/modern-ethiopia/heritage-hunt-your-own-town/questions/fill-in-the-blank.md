# Heritage hunt: your own town — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heritage you can touch and stand in front of is ___ heritage.
**Answer: tangible**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Record every find with its ___ — the person or people who keep it alive.
**Answer: keeper**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The teacher helps the group with the ___ of hard finds.
**Answer: identification**
