# Heritage hunt: your own town — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two kinds to hunt — the touchable and the living
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sort finds into tangible heritage (sites and things) and intangible heritage (practices), using the touch test.

## Sub lesson 2 — Walking your own ground — town, zone, region
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Describe a hunt in widening circles — town, then zone, then region — recording each find with its place and its keeper.

## Sub lesson 3 — Naming it together — the group list
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Explain the group identification, the teacher's helping role, and why the agreed list matters for keeping the heritage alive.
