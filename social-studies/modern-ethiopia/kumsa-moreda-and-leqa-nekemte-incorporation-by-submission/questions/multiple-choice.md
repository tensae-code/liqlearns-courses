# Kumsa Moreda and Leqa Nekemte — incorporation by submission — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kumsa Moreda was —
a) A general in Menelik's army  b) The hereditary ruler of Leqa Nekemte  c) A ruler of Harar  d) An Italian envoy
**Answer: b) The hereditary ruler of Leqa Nekemte**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Leqa Nekemte lay in the land we now call —
a) Tigray  b) Wollega  c) Gojjam  d) Sidamo
**Answer: b) Wollega**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Leqa Nekemte joined Menelik's Ethiopia by —
a) Winning a war against it  b) Submitting peacefully  c) Being sold to it  d) Fleeing from it
**Answer: b) Submitting peacefully**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "Incorporation by submission" means the state was —
a) Destroyed and emptied  b) Taken inside the empire by agreement, without a battle  c) Left completely alone  d) Given to another empire
**Answer: b) Taken inside the empire by agreement, without a battle**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** After baptism, the rulers of Leqa Nekemte —
a) Were exiled  b) Lost all rank  c) Kept their power  d) Became soldiers
**Answer: c) Kept their power**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A traditional title the rulers held, as the guide names it, was —
a) Ras Bitweded  b) Dejazmach  c) Negus of Shewa  d) Bahr Negash
**Answer: b) Dejazmach**
