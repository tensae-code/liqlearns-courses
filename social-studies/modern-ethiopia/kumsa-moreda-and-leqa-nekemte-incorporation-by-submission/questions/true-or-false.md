# Kumsa Moreda and Leqa Nekemte — incorporation by submission — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Leqa Nekemte was an Oromo state with hereditary rulers.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Leqa Nekemte was conquered after a long war with Menelik's forces.
**Answer: False** — It submitted peacefully; there was no war in this case.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Baptism ended the ruling house's power in Leqa Nekemte.
**Answer: False** — The rulers kept their power after baptism.
