# Kumsa Moreda and Leqa Nekemte — incorporation by submission — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ruler of Leqa Nekemte was ___ Moreda.
**Answer: Kumsa**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The state submitted ___ to Menelik's forces — without a war.
**Answer: peacefully**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The rulers held traditional titles such as ___.
**Answer: Dejazmach**
