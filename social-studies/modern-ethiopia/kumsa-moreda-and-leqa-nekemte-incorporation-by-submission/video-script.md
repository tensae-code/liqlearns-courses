# Video script — Kumsa Moreda and Leqa Nekemte — incorporation by submission

The story voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Told as story, in the animated-map spirit: the map and the timeline move as the story moves. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

[Animated map: western Ethiopia glows. One state lights up — Leqa Nekemte, in the land we now call Wollega.]

In the west of Ethiopia stood a state of its own: Leqa Nekemte, ruled in line, father to son, by its own hereditary rulers. Its ruler was Kumsa Moreda.

[The map widens. From the centre, the power of Emperor Menelik is spreading outward, state by state.]

Menelik's forces were remaking the map of Ethiopia. Many states met them in war. Leqa Nekemte did not.

[The marker travels to Nekemte. No clash, no fire — a banner rises, and the two sides meet standing.]

Kumsa Moreda's state submitted — peacefully. It laid its independence down without a battle, and joined.

[Close on the ruler. Water of baptism. A title written beneath his name: Dejazmach. His seat stays his.]

And here is what makes the story worth keeping: the rulers of Leqa Nekemte kept their power. After baptism they ruled on, holding traditional titles — Dejazmach among them.

[The map settles: one Ethiopia, with Nekemte inside it — joined by submission, not by the sword.]

Not every road into the empire was a road of war. Remember the name of the road that was not: Kumsa Moreda, and Leqa Nekemte.
