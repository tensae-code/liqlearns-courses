# Lesson plan — Kumsa Moreda and Leqa Nekemte — incorporation by submission

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Kumsa Moreda and Leqa Nekemte — incorporation by submission
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Tell how Leqa Nekemte joined Menelik's Ethiopia without a war — its ruler submitted peacefully, was baptised, kept his power and took a traditional title, Dejazmach.
**This lesson's own level:** the Grade 12 depth of this title — it stands on the title's Menelik lessons and needs nothing else; a learner may jump straight in, as every name is explained where it first appears. This lesson stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. History is taught as story: the video script is a story script in the animated-map spirit.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Leqa Nekemte — a hereditary Oromo state** — Meet Kumsa Moreda, hereditary ruler of the Leqa Nekemte Oromo state in present-day Wollega.
2. **The submission — joining without a war** — Tell how the state submitted peacefully to Menelik's forces instead of fighting them.
3. **Baptism and the title kept — Dejazmach** — Tell how the rulers kept their power after baptism, holding traditional titles such as Dejazmach.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: "Menilek II — the present shape of Ethiopia" in this title — the expansion this lesson adds one peaceful case to. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Kumsa Moreda and the state he ruled, and say where it lay.
2. Explain what "incorporation by submission" means in this case.
3. Say what the rulers kept after baptism, and name the title the guide gives.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A map of Ethiopia helps. Nothing else.

## If the learner is stuck
- Learner thinks every state joined the empire by conquest → this is the guide's own counter-case: Leqa Nekemte submitted peacefully, and its rulers stayed in power.
- Learner mixes up the title → Dejazmach is the traditional title the guide names; the point is that the title, and the power, were kept — not taken away.
