# Hands-on — Kumsa Moreda and Leqa Nekemte — incorporation by submission

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two roads in
Draw Ethiopia's expansion as two roads on one page: a road of war and a road of submission. Put Leqa Nekemte on the submission road, and write under it the three things its rulers kept — power, place, and title.

## The research task the book itself sets
Do the guide's own Activity 9.2 in miniature: take one name from this lesson — Kumsa Moreda — and find out one more true thing about him from a library book or a trusted older person's telling. Write it as one sentence, and write beside it where it came from. A fact without its source is only a rumour.
