# Tewodros II — ending the Era of Warlords — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tewodros II reigned from 1855 to 1868.
**Answer: True** — His reign dates are 1855–1868.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tewodros tried to keep the regional armies of the Zemene-Mesafint as they were.
**Answer: False** — He tried to replace them with a salaried national army (teacher guide — evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tewodros tried to separate church and state, reducing church land and priests.
**Answer: True** — The guide lists this among his reforms (teacher guide — evidence).
