# Tewodros II — ending the Era of Warlords — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is Tewodros called the first emperor of modern Ethiopia?
**Answer:** Because he ended the Zemene-Mesafint order and was the first to attempt systematically to unify and modernise the whole country — the programme the rest of the unit's state-making follows.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the military reform's three parts from the teacher guide.
**Answer:** Decimal command titles (Yasr, Yamsa, Yeshi Aleqa); replacing regional armies with a salaried national army; and manufacturing firearms at Gafat — about 35 cannons including the Sebastopol mortar.
