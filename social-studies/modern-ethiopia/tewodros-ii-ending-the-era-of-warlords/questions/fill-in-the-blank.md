# Tewodros II — ending the Era of Warlords — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kassa Hailu came from ____, in the west.
**Answer: Quara**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The era Tewodros ended is called the Zemene-____.
**Answer: Mesafint**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The commander of a thousand in Tewodros's decimal titles was the ____ Aleqa.
**Answer: Yeshi**
