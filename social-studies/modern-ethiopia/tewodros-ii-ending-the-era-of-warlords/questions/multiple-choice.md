# Tewodros II — ending the Era of Warlords — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tewodros II was born —
a) Menilek of Shewa  b) Kassa Hailu of Quara  c) Kassa Mircha of Tigray  d) Wagshum Gobeze of Lasta
**Answer: b) Kassa Hailu of Quara** — Kassa Hailu of Quara took the throne as Tewodros II.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kassa rose serving his half-brother —
a) Dejjazmach Kenfu of Dembya  b) Ras Ali  c) Dejjazmach Wube  d) Sahle Selassie
**Answer: a) Dejjazmach Kenfu of Dembya** — He rose serving Dejjazmach Kenfu of Dembya.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Tewodros's crowning ended the order called —
a) The Solomonic restoration  b) The Zemene-Mesafint  c) The Gondar period  d) The Zagwe dynasty
**Answer: b) The Zemene-Mesafint** — His rise ended the Zemene-Mesafint — the Era of the Princes.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The twin processes of making modern Ethiopia were —
a) Trade and mission  b) Unification of the north and centre, and expansion into the south  c) Colonisation and resistance  d) Federation and secession
**Answer: b) Unification of the north and centre, and expansion into the south** — Unification of the northern and north-central regions, and territorial expansion into the southern half.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Yasr Aleqa means commander of —
a) Ten  b) A hundred  c) A thousand  d) The whole army
**Answer: a) Ten** — Yasr Aleqa — ten; Yamsa Aleqa — hundred; Yeshi Aleqa — thousand (teacher guide — evidence).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** At Gafat, Tewodros's works produced —
a) About 35 cannons, including the mortar 'Sebastopol'  b) Printing presses  c) Steam locomotives  d) Rifles only
**Answer: a) About 35 cannons, including the mortar 'Sebastopol'** — About 35 cannons including Sebastopol, cast with European missionaries and artisans (teacher guide — evidence).
