# Video script — Tewodros II — ending the Era of Warlords


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Ethiopia in pieces — the Era of the Princes, every lord his own army.

From Quara, in the west, a soldier rises: Kassa Hailu, serving his half-brother Kenfu, outfighting them all.

1855. The crown comes down on his head: Tewodros the Second. The era of warlords ends.

Now watch him build. Titles by tens and hundreds and thousands. Soldiers on salary, not on plunder.

At Gafat the furnaces glow — Ethiopian smiths and European artisans cast cannon after cannon. The great mortar takes its name: Sebastopol!

One army. One paymaster. One state. The first modern unifier has begun.
