# Hands-on — Tewodros II — ending the Era of Warlords

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Decimal command
Line up ten classmates as a squad: appoint a Yasr Aleqa over them; group five squads under a Yamsa Aleqa on paper; then ten of those under a Yeshi Aleqa. The ladder you built is Tewodros's army reform.

## Foundry visit in words
Write the Gafat foundry's day in five lines: the fire, the mould, the smiths and artisans, the barrel cooling, the mortar named. End with the count: about 35 cannons.
