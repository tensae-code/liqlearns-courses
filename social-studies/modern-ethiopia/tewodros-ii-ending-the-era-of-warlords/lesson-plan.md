# Lesson plan — Tewodros II — ending the Era of Warlords

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Tewodros II — ending the Era of Warlords
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Social, Economic and Political Developments in Ethiopia, Mid-19th C. to 1941 (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** **Kassa Hailu of Quara** rose from serving his half-brother Dejjazmach Kenfu to take the throne as **Tewodros II (1855–1868)** — ending the **Zemene-Mesafint** order and attempting to **unify and modernise** Ethiopia: the first emperor of modern Ethiopia, whose military reforms the teacher guide details down to the **Sebastopol mortar**.
**This lesson's own level:** it opens the state-making story of Unit 3 — the learner has met the Zemene-Mesafint in this title's earlier lessons; now the man who ended it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Kassa of Quara takes the throne** — Tell Kassa Hailu's rise: of Quara, serving his half-brother Dejjazmach Kenfu of Dembya, taking the throne as Tewodros II (1855–1868), ending the Zemene-Mesafint order.
2. **The reformer — a salaried army and the Sebastopol mortar (teacher guide — evidence)** — From the guide's feedback: decimal command titles (Yasr, Yamsa, Yeshi Aleqa); the attempt to replace regional armies with a salaried national army; firearms manufactured at Gafat (about 35 cannons, including the mortar Sebastopol) with European missionaries and artisans; church and state separated, church land and priest numbers reduced.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title, in the title's order — most nearest, the lesson just before this one. Recommended, never forced.
- A learner may jump straight in here: the lesson stands alone, and names are explained where they first appear.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Kassa Hailu's rise and date Tewodros II's reign.
2. Say what order his crowning ended, and what he attempted.
3. Recite the guide's reform list: decimal titles, salaried army, Gafat's cannons and Sebastopol, church and state.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.
