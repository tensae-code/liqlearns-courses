# Foreign invasions — Mitsiwa lost and Dogali won

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: In 1885 Italy took Mitsiwa as Egypt left it — and in 1887 Ras Alula destroyed 500 Italian soldiers at Dogali. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mitsiwa handed to Italy

Under the treaty **Bogos returned to Ethiopia**, but **Mitsiwa was given to the Italians**: in **1885**, at **Britain's invitation** — Britain wanted a weak Italy, no rival, to block French expansion on the Red Sea — **Italy took Mitsiwa** as the Egyptians left it, violating the treaty. Italy then **blocked arms entering Ethiopia, taxed heavily and expanded** into the Mereb Mellash.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dogali answers

**Ras Alula** protested, then advanced with about **20,000 men** against the Italian positions at **Sahati and Wi'a**; the entrenched Italians could not be shifted, but at **Dogali in 1887** Alula's force **destroyed 500 Italian soldiers** — the Dogali victory, a shock to Italy. Britain's envoy **Gerald Portal's** peace terms — Yohannes to apologise publicly for Dogali; Italy to keep Sahati, Wi'a, Keren and other Red Sea shores — failed, and the Italians **retook Sahati during the talks**, ringing Yohannes's government in.
