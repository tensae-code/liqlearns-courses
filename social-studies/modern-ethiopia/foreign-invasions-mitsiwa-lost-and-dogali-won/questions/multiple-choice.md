# Foreign invasions — Mitsiwa lost and Dogali won — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy took Mitsiwa in —
a) 1884  b) 1885  c) 1887  d) 1889
**Answer: b) 1885** — 1885, as the Egyptians left it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain invited Italy in order to —
a) Help Ethiopia  b) Block French expansion on the Red Sea with a weak, non-rival Italy  c) Fight Egypt  d) Open trade
**Answer: b) Block French expansion on the Red Sea with a weak, non-rival Italy** — A weak Italy to block France — no rival to Britain.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the treaty, which land did return to Ethiopia?
a) Mitsiwa  b) Bogos  c) Keren  d) Sahati
**Answer: b) Bogos** — Bogos returned; Mitsiwa went to Italy.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At Dogali in 1887 Alula's force destroyed —
a) 500 Italian soldiers  b) 20,000 Egyptians  c) A Mahdist army  d) The Sahati fort itself
**Answer: a) 500 Italian soldiers** — 500 Italian soldiers.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Portal's terms demanded Yohannes —
a) Pay for Mitsiwa  b) Apologise publicly for Dogali while Italy kept Sahati, Wi'a and Keren  c) Cede Gondar  d) Disband his army
**Answer: b) Apologise publicly for Dogali while Italy kept Sahati, Wi'a and Keren** — A public apology, with Italy keeping the Red Sea positions.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** During the peace talks the Italians —
a) Left Sahati  b) Retook Sahati  c) Freed prisoners  d) Signed Liche
**Answer: b) Retook Sahati** — They retook Sahati during the talks.
