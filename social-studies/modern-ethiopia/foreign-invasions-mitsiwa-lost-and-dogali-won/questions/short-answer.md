# Foreign invasions — Mitsiwa lost and Dogali won — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Italy's taking of Mitsiwa violate the Hewett Treaty?
**Answer:** The treaty had promised Ethiopia free use of Mitsiwa and the territories back; instead the port was handed to Italy as Egypt evacuated it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why was Dogali a shock to Italy?
**Answer:** A colonial force of 500 was destroyed outright by Alula — proof Ethiopia could beat a European army in the field.
