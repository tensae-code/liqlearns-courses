# Foreign invasions — Mitsiwa lost and Dogali won — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy taxed heavily and blocked arms entering Ethiopia after 1885.
**Answer: True** — The book lists both.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Alula's 20,000 men stormed and took the Sahati trenches.
**Answer: False** — The entrenched Italians could not be shifted at Sahati; the victory was at Dogali.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Portal's peace terms were accepted by Yohannes.
**Answer: False** — The terms failed.
