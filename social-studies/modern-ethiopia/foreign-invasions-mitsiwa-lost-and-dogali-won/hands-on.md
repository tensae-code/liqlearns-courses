# Hands-on — Foreign invasions — Mitsiwa lost and Dogali won

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Flags over a port
Draw Mitsiwa port three times: Egypt's flag, then Italy's (1885), with a card between them reading 'the treaty broken'.

## Entrenched
Pile books as Italian trenches at Sahati. Show why Alula could not shift them there — and why Dogali, on the move, was different.
