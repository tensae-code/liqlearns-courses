# Autocracy and the rising opposition — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the restored empire's later years, power —
a) Was shared equally among regional rulers  b) Centralised in the emperor  c) Passed to parliament  d) Moved to the provinces
**Answer: b) b) Centralised in the emperor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ministers and governors held office —
a) For life by inheritance  b) By election  c) At the emperor's pleasure  d) By lot
**Answer: c) c) At the emperor's pleasure**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The December 1960 coup attempt was made while the emperor was —
a) In exile  b) Abroad  c) At the front  d) Crowned that week
**Answer: b) b) Abroad**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1960 coup attempt ended with —
a) A new constitution  b) Its collapse and its leaders destroyed  c) The emperor's abdication  d) Foreign mediation
**Answer: b) b) Its collapse and its leaders destroyed**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The student movement's famous demand was —
a) Lower fees only  b) Land to the tiller  c) A new capital  d) Free transport
**Answer: b) b) Land to the tiller**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Armed insurgency began in —
a) Bale  b) Gojjam  c) Eritrea  d) Harar
**Answer: c) c) Eritrea**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Peasant revolts named in the lesson rose in —
a) Bale and Gojjam  b) Kafa and Jimma  c) Gondar and Aksum  d) Harar and Dire Dawa
**Answer: a) a) Bale and Gojjam**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The famine that discredited the throne struck mainly —
a) The coast  b) Wollo and Tigray  c) The south-west  d) Addis Ababa only
**Answer: b) b) Wollo and Tigray**
