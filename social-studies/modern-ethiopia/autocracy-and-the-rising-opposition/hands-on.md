# Hands-on — Autocracy and the rising opposition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Timeline of cracks
Draw a line from 1960 to 1974. Mark the coup attempt, the start of the Eritrean insurgency, one provincial revolt, and the famine. Under each, write in one line what thread of the empire it pulled.

## Land to the tiller
As a student of the time, write a four-line placard text demanding land reform. Keep it to what the lesson teaches: who holds the land, who works it, what should change.
