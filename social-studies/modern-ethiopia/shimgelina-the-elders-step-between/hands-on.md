# Hands-on — Shimgelina — the elders step between

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Step between
In groups of five — two parties with a grievance, two elders, one witness — enact a Shimgelina mediation of a schoolyard dispute. The witness reports: at what moment did the parties begin to move?

## My locality's way
Interview two adults: 'When two families quarrel here, who settles it, and how?' Write the account and compare it, point by point, with Shimgelina.
