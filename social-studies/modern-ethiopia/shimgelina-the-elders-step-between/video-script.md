# Video script — Shimgelina — the elders step between

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a village path where two men argue, voices rising. From three houses, old men come walking — slowly, unarmed.
They seat themselves between the parties. The shouting stops, not from fear but from the custom's weight. Heads bow; the hearing begins.
Hands are shaken before the whole village. The elders rise and walk home by different paths.
The camera pulls back over Ethiopia: under a hundred local names, the same figures step between — the country's oldest peace machinery, still at work.
