# Shimgelina — the elders step between — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Shimgelina settlements are enforced by a police force.
**Answer: False** — the community's acceptance is the only enforcement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Every community in Ethiopia runs some version of this mediation machinery, named or not.
**Answer: True** — the lesson's closing claim, to be tested by the learner's own inquiry.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Elders in Shimgelina are armed officials of the state.
**Answer: False** — unarmed elders; their authority is reputation, not office.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Comparing one's own locality's method with Shimgelina is optional decoration in the book.
**Answer: False** — it is the book's set pair-work task.
