# Hands-on — Ethiopia on the World Heritage List

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The registered map
On a blank Ethiopia map, mark the five walked sites and the two review sites, each with its year. Present the map as the class's World Heritage wall chart.

## Nominate one more
Choose one unlisted Ethiopian heritage — from this unit's lessons — and write its nomination in five lines: what it is, its kind, its value, its threat, its keeping plan.
