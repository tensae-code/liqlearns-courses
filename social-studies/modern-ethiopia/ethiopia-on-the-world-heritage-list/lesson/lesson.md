# Ethiopia on the World Heritage List

## Do it first

In 1978 the world began keeping a list of the places humanity must not lose. One of the very first names written on it — anywhere on earth — was an Ethiopian town of churches carved down into rock. This lesson walks Ethiopia's entries on that list, site by site.

## Sub lesson 1 — 1978 — Lalibela, among the first on earth

The World Heritage List opened in 1978, and Ethiopia stood on its first page: the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela were one of the first sites ever listed. The honour fits the wonder — a town of churches carved downward into living rock in the Zagwe age, still in worship today: tangible heritage that remains a living practice, the two families of the earlier lesson fused in one site.

Hold Lalibela's date as the list's anchor: when the world started counting its treasures, Ethiopia was counted first. The sites that follow were inscribed two years later, in a single Ethiopian cluster.

## Sub lesson 2 — 1980 — Aksum, Awash, Omo, Tiya

The Stelae of Aksum, inscribed 1980, are the symbol of the Aksumite kingdom's wealth and importance — the stone towers of the Red Sea empire the ancient lessons built. The Lower Valley of the Awash, also 1980, lies 300 km north-east of Addis Ababa in the Afar country: a palaeo-anthropological research landscape whose fossils speak to human origins. Its twin, the Lower Valley of the Omo, 1980 likewise, holds human and animal fossils and stone tools fundamental to studying human evolution — Ethiopia registered, twice over, as humanity's own archive.

Tiya completes the set, 1980: an archaeological field of 36 monuments including 32 carved stelae, 90 km south of Addis Ababa — grave-stones of a culture whose name we do not know, kept because their carving still speaks. And the review adds two more to the pupil's map of Ethiopia's registered heritage: Harar Jugol, the walled city of the earlier lessons, and Fiche-Chambalala. The list is a curriculum in itself: kingdoms, churches, fossils and gravestones — the whole span of this course, registered by the world.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the list by years: 1978 and its distinction; the 1980 four with what each preserves; the review's two additions.
