# Video script — Ethiopia on the World Heritage List

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens in 1978. A single point lights in the northern highlands — LALIBELA — among the first lights on the whole world map.
1980: the lights multiply. Aksum's stelae in the north; two river valleys, Awash and Omo, glowing with the oldest human story; Tiya's carved stones south of the capital.
Two more lights join from the review's list: Harar's walls in the east, Fiche-Chambalala in the centre-south.
The camera pulls back: one small country holding an outsized share of humanity's registered memory.
