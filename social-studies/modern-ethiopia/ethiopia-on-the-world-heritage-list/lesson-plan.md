# Lesson plan — Ethiopia on the World Heritage List

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Ethiopia on the World Heritage List
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the heritage thread; Grade 10 Unit 9 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's UNESCO-registered sites walked one by one: Lalibela (1978, among the first ever listed), Aksum and the Lower Awash and Lower Omo valleys and Tiya (all 1980) — with Harar Jugol and Fiche-Chambalala completing the pupil's map in the review.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Three kinds of heritage — the list is that sorting applied to Ethiopia. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1978 — Lalibela, among the first on earth** — the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, inscribed 1978 as one of the first sites ever listed — symbol and substance of the Zagwe achievement.
2. **1980 — Aksum, Awash, Omo, Tiya** — the Stelae of Aksum (1980), symbol of the kingdom's wealth and importance; the Lower Awash (1980), palaeo-anthropological research 300 km north-east of Addis Ababa; the Lower Omo (1980), fossils and tools fundamental to studying human evolution; Tiya (1980), 36 monuments including 32 carved stelae, 90 km south of Addis Ababa.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Three kinds of heritage — nature, practice, thing. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give Lalibela's year and its first-list distinction.
2. Match Aksum, Awash, Omo and Tiya to their years and what each preserves.
3. Name the two review-added sites: Harar Jugol, Fiche-Chambalala.
