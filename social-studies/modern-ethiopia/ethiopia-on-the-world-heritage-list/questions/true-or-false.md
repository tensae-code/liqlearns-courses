# Ethiopia on the World Heritage List — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** All of Ethiopia's walked sites were inscribed in 1980.
**Answer: False** — Lalibela stands apart — 1978.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Awash valley site lies in the Afar country, 300 km north-east of Addis Ababa.
**Answer: True** — palaeo-anthropological research landscape.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Aksum, Awash, Omo and Tiya were all inscribed in 1980.
**Answer: True** — the Ethiopian cluster two years after Lalibela.
