# The occupation — why Ethiopia lost; the six divisions

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: The book's reckoning: aircraft, gas and modern arms against Adwa-era rifles, no radios, no organised supply — then Italy carved Ethiopia into six divisions. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why Ethiopia lost — the book's reckoning

Italy held **superiority in military technology and numbers** — modern rifles, machine guns, artillery, tanks and above all **aircraft dropping bombs and poison gas** — against Ethiopian soldiers carrying **rifles left from the Adwa campaign** and short of ammunition. Add: **no organised supply** of provisions and equipment; **no radio** for coordinated attack; **rivalry among commanders**; lords aggrieved at the emperor not fighting wholeheartedly; **want of experienced military leadership**; Haile Selassie's **mistaken trust in the League**; and a country **in transition**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Six divisions of a conquered country

Italy then organised the conquered country into **six administrative divisions**: **Eritrea** (Eritrea and Tigray joined, capital Asmara), **Gondar** (Begemdir, Gojjam, Wollo), **Shewa** (Addis Ababa), **Jimma** (all the south-west and south), **Harar** (with part of Bale), and **Somalia** (Italian Somaliland with the Ogaden, capital Mogadishu) — Addis Ababa made seat of the **Viceroy of Italian East Africa**.
