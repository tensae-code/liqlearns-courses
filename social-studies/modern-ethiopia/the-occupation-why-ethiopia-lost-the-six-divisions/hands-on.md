# Hands-on — The occupation — why Ethiopia lost; the six divisions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two equipment tables
Set two tables: Italy's — machine guns, tanks, aircraft, gas; Ethiopia's — Adwa-era rifles, little ammunition. The reckoning is visible.

## Six pieces of a map
Cut a map outline into six pieces and label them Eritrea, Gondar, Shewa, Jimma, Harar, Somalia — Italy's carving, soon to be undone.
