# The occupation — why Ethiopia lost; the six divisions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy's greatest superiority was —
a) Cavalry  b) Aircraft dropping bombs and poison gas, with modern arms  c) Numbers of horses  d) Naval guns
**Answer: b) Aircraft dropping bombs and poison gas, with modern arms** — Above all aircraft with bombs and gas.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Many Ethiopian soldiers carried —
a) New machine guns  b) Rifles left from the Adwa campaign, short of ammunition  c) Italian rifles  d) No weapons
**Answer: b) Rifles left from the Adwa campaign, short of ammunition** — Adwa-campaign rifles, short of ammunition.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia lacked radios, so it could not —
a) Hear the news  b) Coordinate its attacks  c) Call aircraft  d) Pay its army
**Answer: b) Coordinate its attacks** — No radio for coordinated attack.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The division joining Eritrea and Tigray had its capital at —
a) Asmara  b) Mogadishu  c) Addis Ababa  d) Gondar
**Answer: a) Asmara** — Asmara.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Somalia division joined Italian Somaliland with —
a) Wollo  b) The Ogaden, capital Mogadishu  c) Bale  d) Harar
**Answer: b) The Ogaden, capital Mogadishu** — The Ogaden, capital Mogadishu.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Addis Ababa was seat of —
a) The emperor in exile  b) The Viceroy of Italian East Africa  c) The League  d) The Gideon Force
**Answer: b) The Viceroy of Italian East Africa** — The Viceroy of Italian East Africa.
