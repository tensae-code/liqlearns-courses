# The occupation — why Ethiopia lost; the six divisions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give four reasons the book gives for Ethiopia's defeat.
**Answer:** Any four: Italy's technology/numbers and aircraft with gas; Adwa-era rifles short of ammunition; no organised supply; no radio; rivalry among commanders; aggrieved lords; want of experienced leadership; mistaken trust in the League; a country in transition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the six divisions.
**Answer:** Eritrea, Gondar, Shewa, Jimma, Harar, Somalia.
