# Hands-on — The caravan trade — routes and goods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Salt money
Cut a bar of soap or a block of salt dough to amole size. Use it to 'pay' for three goods in a class market, bartering the rest.

## Walk the route
Chalk the route Bonga → Seqa → Sodo → Harar → Zeila on the ground. Walk it carrying a light load, stopping where the negadras would rest the caravan.
