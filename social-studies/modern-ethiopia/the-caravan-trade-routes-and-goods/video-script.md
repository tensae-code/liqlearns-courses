# Video script — The caravan trade — routes and goods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Say caravans traded by barter with amole salt bars as money, led by a negadras who coordinated, chose rest places and organised defence.
Trace both route families from Bonga: north-west to Gondar and Metema (and to Mitsiwa via Adwa), and east to Harar, Zeila and Berbera.

And now the wider map, as the older book draws it. Two great routes light up from Bonga, capital of Kafa. Along them walk the Afkala and the Jabarti — the traders on whom the whole system turned. Their purses hold salt bars and silver thalers. And wherever the caravans pass, peoples who were strangers become partners: trade itself is the great connector.
