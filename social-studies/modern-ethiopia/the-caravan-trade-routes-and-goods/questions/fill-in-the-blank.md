# The caravan trade — routes and goods — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Salt bars used as money are called ____.
**Answer: amole**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The caravan hub the book's map starts from is ____.
**Answer: Bonga**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Beside amole, the coin-money of the trade was the Maria Theresa ____.
**Answer: thaler**
