# The caravan trade — routes and goods — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three jobs did the negadras do?
**Answer:** Coordinated the journey, chose the resting places, and organised the caravan's defence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did caravans travel in groups?
**Answer:** For defence on the long road — the group, organised by the negadras, was the caravan's protection.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What are the four contents of the Grade 10 book's long-distance trade lesson?
**Answer:** Routes, major market centres, trade items, and people's interaction — with trade itself named the most important catalyst in relations between peoples and polities.
