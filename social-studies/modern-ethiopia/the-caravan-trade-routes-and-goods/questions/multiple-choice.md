# The caravan trade — routes and goods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Caravan trade was conducted mainly by —
a) Bank notes  b) Barter  c) Cheques  d) Credit cards
**Answer: b) Barter** — Mainly by barter.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Amole salt bars served as —
a) Food only  b) Money  c) Building blocks  d) Medicine
**Answer: b) Money** — Amole and the like served as money.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A caravan was led by a —
a) Ras  b) Negadras  c) Patriarch  d) Negus
**Answer: b) Negadras** — The negadras coordinated the journey.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The negadras also —
a) Printed money  b) Chose resting places and organised defence  c) Collected church tax  d) Built roads
**Answer: b) Chose resting places and organised defence** — Chose resting places and organised defence.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** From Bonga, one route family ran north-west to —
a) Harar  b) Gondar and on to Metema  c) Zeila  d) Jimma only
**Answer: b) Gondar and on to Metema** — Through Asendabo, Baso, Darita and Yifag to Gondar, on to Metema.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Harar, Zeila and Berbera were reached from Seqa through —
a) Adwa  b) Sodo, Aliyu Amba and Abduresul  c) Gondar  d) Mitsiwa
**Answer: b) Sodo, Aliyu Amba and Abduresul** — Sodo–Aliyu Amba–Abduresul to Harar, Zeila and Berbera.
