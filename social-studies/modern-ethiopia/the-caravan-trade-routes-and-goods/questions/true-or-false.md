# The caravan trade — routes and goods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For Ethiopia and the Horn, trade was the main meeting ground and economic base.
**Answer: True** — The book's own words on trade's place.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Caravans usually travelled alone for speed.
**Answer: False** — They travelled in groups under a negadras.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The route map in the book follows Bahru Zewde (2002).
**Answer: True** — The map is credited 'after Bahru Zewde, 2002'.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 10 book calls trade the most important catalyst in relations between peoples and polities.
**Answer: True** — Trade itself is named the most important catalyst.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Afkala and Jabarti traders played only a minor role in the long-distance trade.
**Answer: False** — They played the pivotal role.
