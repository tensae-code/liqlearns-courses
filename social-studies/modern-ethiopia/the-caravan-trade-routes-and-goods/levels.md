# The caravan trade — routes and goods — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Barter, salt money, and the negadras
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Say caravans traded by barter with amole salt bars as money, led by a negadras who coordinated, chose rest places and organised defence.

## Sub lesson 2 — The routes from Bonga
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Trace both route families from Bonga: north-west to Gondar and Metema (and to Mitsiwa via Adwa), and east to Harar, Zeila and Berbera.
