# Hands-on — December 1960 — the coup that lasted two days

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The proclamation
Draft the coup's radio proclamation in five lines from the lesson's facts: who speaks, what is abolished, what is proclaimed, who holds the three posts, what the public is asked to do.

## Why it fell
List everything the coup held on day one (palace, radio, proclamation) and everything it lacked (army mass, church, public). Under each lack, write the lesson it teaches a future coup-maker.
