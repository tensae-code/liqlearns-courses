# Video script — December 1960 — the coup that lasted two days

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Addis Ababa, December 1960. The Emperor's plane is far away. At dawn, the palace guard changes sides.
A radio tower lights up: a new government is proclaimed — a salaried king, a prime minister, a chief of staff. Three names over the city.
Two days of flashes in the streets. The Emperor's plane returns. The city's lights go over, quarter by quarter, to the loyalists.
The proclamation's paper fades. One sentence remains on the screen: lack of support.
