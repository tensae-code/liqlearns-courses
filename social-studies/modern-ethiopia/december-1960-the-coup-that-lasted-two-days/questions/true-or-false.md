# December 1960 — the coup that lasted two days — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Mengistu Neway escaped abroad after the coup.
**Answer: False** — he was captured and sentenced to death.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Germame Neway and Workneh Gebeyehu committed suicide.
**Answer: True** — rather than be taken.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** General Tsige Dibu, the police chief, survived the fighting.
**Answer: False** — he was killed in the two days.
