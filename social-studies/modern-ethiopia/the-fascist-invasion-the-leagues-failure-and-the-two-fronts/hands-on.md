# Hands-on — The Fascist invasion — the League's failure and the two fronts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two supply lines
Draw two arrows into the war: Italy's, thick with fuel and arms from Germany and America; Ethiopia's, crossed by the embargo. Say which side the embargo really disarmed.

## Two arrows close
On a map draw the northern arrow from Eritrea and the southern from Somaliland, both pointing at Addis Ababa.
