# The Fascist invasion — the League's failure and the two fronts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain and France delayed the Walwal agenda because they —
a) Doubted the facts  b) Hoped to keep Italy from joining Germany and chose to sacrifice Ethiopia  c) Feared Ethiopia  d) Were at war
**Answer: b) Hoped to keep Italy from joining Germany and chose to sacrifice Ethiopia** — They sacrificed Ethiopia to court Italy against Germany.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The League's arms embargo fell on —
a) Italy only  b) Both countries  c) Ethiopia only  d) Neither
**Answer: b) Both countries** — Both countries — on paper.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The embargo did not hurt Italy because Italy —
a) Made all its own arms  b) Kept importing fuel and arms from Germany and neutral America  c) Had no army  d) Left the League
**Answer: b) Kept importing fuel and arms from Germany and neutral America** — Imports continued from Germany and neutral America.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The northern invasion began on —
a) October 3, 1935  b) December 5, 1934  c) March 31, 1936  d) May 5, 1941
**Answer: a) October 3, 1935** — October 3, 1935.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The northern command passed from De Bono to —
a) Graziani  b) Pietro Badoglio  c) Zoli  d) Mussolini in person
**Answer: b) Pietro Badoglio** — Badoglio, under whom the cruel phase ran.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The southern front was commanded by —
a) De Bono  b) General Rodolfo Graziani  c) Badoglio  d) Wingate
**Answer: b) General Rodolfo Graziani** — Graziani.

**7. (Sub lesson 3) ** In the Grade 10 framing, Italy's desire for Ethiopia —
a) Began in 1935  b) Never ended despite Adwa  c) Was Britain's idea  d) Concerned trade only
**Answer: b) Never ended despite Adwa** — Mussolini's invasion fulfilled a forty-year ambition.

**8. (Sub lesson 3) ** The League of Nations met the Emperor's plea with —
a) An army  b) Deaf ears  c) Immediate sanctions that worked  d) A new capital for Ethiopia
**Answer: b) Deaf ears** — the Grade 10 book's own phrase.

**9. (Sub lesson 3) ** The invasion proceeded on —
a) One front  b) Two mapped fronts of Italian advance  c) No fronts — by sea only  d) A single road
**Answer: b) Two mapped fronts of Italian advance** — the book maps the advances.
