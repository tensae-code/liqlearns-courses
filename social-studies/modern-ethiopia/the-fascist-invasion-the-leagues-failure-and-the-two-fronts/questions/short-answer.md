# The Fascist invasion — the League's failure and the two fronts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why was the arms embargo unjust in effect?
**Answer:** Italy kept importing fuel and arms from Germany and neutral America, so only Ethiopia — which had no such supply — was truly disarmed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two fronts and their commanders.
**Answer:** North from Eritrea: De Bono first, then Badoglio. South from Italian Somaliland: Graziani.
