# The Fascist invasion — the League's failure and the two fronts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The League imposed its embargo a year before Walwal.
**Answer: False** — Walwal was December 1934; the invasion came a year after it, the embargo in between.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Poison gas used in Tigray was internationally banned.
**Answer: True** — The book calls it internationally banned.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The southern invasion began on October 3, 1935 with the northern.
**Answer: False** — The north began October 3, 1935; Graziani advanced later.
