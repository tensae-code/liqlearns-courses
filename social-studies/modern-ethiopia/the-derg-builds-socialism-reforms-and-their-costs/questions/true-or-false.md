# The Derg builds socialism — reforms and their costs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The Derg kept the imperial land order unchanged.
**Answer: False** — the land reform abolished tenancy by proclamation.

**2. (Sub lesson 3) ** The book asks pupils to weigh the Derg's human and democratic rights record beside its reforms.
**Answer: True** — both ledgers are the lesson.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The literacy programme travelled with the December 1974 campaign.
**Answer: True** — the zemecha carried teaching into the villages — guide evidence.
