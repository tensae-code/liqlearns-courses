# Video script — The Derg builds socialism — reforms and their costs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on 1974's cleared ground. A red flag rises; the words appear: Ethiopia Tikdem.
December: columns of students and teachers walk out of the cities into the villages, books in hand — the campaign arrives under the trees.
March 1975: a proclamation is read in a village square. The gult seal shatters in the drawing; the field's arrows of tribute stop.
Then the map darkens: drought cracks the soil, 1984; villages are picked up and set down elsewhere by decree. The two ledgers lie side by side as the map fades.
