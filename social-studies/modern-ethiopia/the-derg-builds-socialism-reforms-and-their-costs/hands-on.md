# Hands-on — The Derg builds socialism — reforms and their costs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The proclamation read aloud
Stage the March 1975 reading: one learner reads a three-line summary of the proclamation to a 'village meeting'; the meeting's members (class) each say one thing that changes for their household the next morning.

## Both ledgers
Rule a page in two: Reforms / Costs. Enter every item from the lesson. Then write the one sentence a fair judge would pronounce over both columns.
