# Lesson plan — The Derg builds socialism — reforms and their costs

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** The Derg builds socialism — reforms and their costs
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the Derg unit; Grade 10 Unit 6, with Grade 10 teacher-guide additions stocked as labelled evidence (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Derg adopted Marxism-Leninism and built a socialist state: the land reform at the head of its reforms — Ethiopia Tikdem, the December 1974 campaign, the March 1975 land proclamation — beside a harder ledger of rights, the 1984/85 famine, and resettlement and villagization judged ineffective and poorly coordinated.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on 1974 — revolution, deposition, the Derg. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A socialist state — ideology and reforms** — Marxism-Leninism adopted as official ideology; socio-economic and political reforms with the land reform at their head.
2. **Ethiopia Tikdem, the campaign, the March 1975 land reform (teacher guide — evidence)** — the Grade 10 guide's specifics: the philosophy Ethiopia Tikdem, the Development through Co-operation Campaign of December 1974 with its literacy programme, and the March 1975 Land Reform Proclamation ending feudal relations.
3. **The costs — rights, famine, villagization** — the record on human and democratic rights, the 1984/85 famine after drought and crop failure, and resettlement and villagization judged ineffective and poorly coordinated.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: 1974 — revolution, deposition, the Derg. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the ideology the Derg adopted and the state it set out to build.
2. Give the guide's dated specifics: Ethiopia Tikdem, December 1974's campaign, March 1975's land reform.
3. Weigh the costs the book lists: rights, famine, resettlement and villagization.
