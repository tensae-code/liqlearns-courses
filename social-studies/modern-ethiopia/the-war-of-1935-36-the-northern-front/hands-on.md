# Hands-on — The war of 1935–36 — the northern front

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three wing cards
Pin three cards — EAST Aradom/Mulugeta, CENTRE Tembien/Kassa+Seyoum, WEST Shire/Imru. Retell each wing's fate.

## Thirteen hours
Mark thirteen tallies on paper, one per hour at Maychew. Say what the Bodyguard faced: entrenchments, aircraft, gas.
