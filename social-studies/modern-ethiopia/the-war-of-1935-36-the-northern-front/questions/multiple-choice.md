# The war of 1935–36 — the northern front — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The northern army's overall commander was —
a) Ras Kassa Hailu  b) Ras Imru  c) Graziani  d) Ras Desta
**Answer: a) Ras Kassa Hailu** — Ras Kassa Hailu.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At Amba Aradom the eastern wing was led by —
a) Ras Seyoum  b) The war minister Ras Mulugeta Yigezu  c) Ras Imru  d) Dejazmach Nasibu
**Answer: b) The war minister Ras Mulugeta Yigezu** — Ras Mulugeta Yigezu, who was killed there.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The First Tembien battle was —
a) An Italian victory  b) Won by the Ethiopian counter-attacks  c) Never fought  d) A naval battle
**Answer: b) Won by the Ethiopian counter-attacks** — The first counter-attacks won First Tembien.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Second Tembien, Badoglio —
a) Withdrew  b) Used poison gas on a wide scale  c) Asked for peace  d) Lost his army
**Answer: b) Used poison gas on a wide scale** — Poison gas on a wide scale.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ras Imru won at Shire on —
a) April 2, 1936  b) March 31, 1936  c) October 3, 1935  d) May 5, 1941
**Answer: a) April 2, 1936** — April 2, 1936 — a victory that did not last.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Maychew, the north's last decisive battle, was fought on —
a) March 31, 1936  b) May 31, 1936 as correctly printed  c) March 1, 1896  d) June 1936
**Answer: a) March 31, 1936** — March 31, 1936 — the printed May 31 is the book's error.
