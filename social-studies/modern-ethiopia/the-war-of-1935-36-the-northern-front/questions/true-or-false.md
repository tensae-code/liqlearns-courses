# The war of 1935–36 — the northern front — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ras Mulugeta was killed at Amba Aradom after six days of bombardment.
**Answer: True** — Air and artillery for six days; very many killed, Mulugeta among them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At Maychew the emperor led 6,000 Imperial Bodyguard among his force.
**Answer: True** — The book gives the figure.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The fighting at Maychew lasted thirteen hours.
**Answer: True** — Heroically fought for thirteen hours against entrenched, air-supported Italians.
