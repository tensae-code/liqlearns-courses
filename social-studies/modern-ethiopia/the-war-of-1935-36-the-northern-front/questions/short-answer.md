# The war of 1935–36 — the northern front — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did the two Tembien battles differ?
**Answer:** The first was won by Ethiopian counter-attacks; in the second, Badoglio's wide use of poison gas put the outnumbered Ethiopians out of action, Kassa and Seyoum escaping to Korem.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why could Maychew not be won?
**Answer:** The Italians were entrenched and supported from the air; thirteen hours of heroic fighting could not beat aircraft and gas, and the retreat was bombed and gassed.
