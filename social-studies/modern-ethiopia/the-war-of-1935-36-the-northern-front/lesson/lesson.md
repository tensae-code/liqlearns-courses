# The war of 1935–36 — the northern front

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Three wings — Aradom, Tembien, Shire — broke under gas and bombs; the north's last battle was Maychew, March 31, 1936, where the emperor himself led. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three wings and the first battles

The northern army was commanded by **Ras Kassa Hailu** and met Italy on three wings: the war minister **Ras Mulugeta Yigezu** in the east at **Amba Aradom**; **Ras Kassa and Ras Seyoum Mangasha** in the centre, the **Tembien front**; **Ras Imru Haile Selassie** in the west, the **Shire front**. The first Ethiopian counter-attacks **won the First Tembien battle**; at **Amba Aradom** the Fascists bombarded by air and artillery for **six days** and killed very many, **Ras Mulugeta among them**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gas, Shire, and Maychew

Mussolini, hurrying to finish before the rains, made **Badoglio** commander; Badoglio used **poison gas on a wide scale**, and in the **Second Tembien battle** the outnumbered Ethiopians were put out of action, Kassa and Seyoum escaping the encirclement to join the emperor at **Korem**. In the west **Ras Imru won at Enda Baguna** and, despite part of his force deserting home, **won at Shire on April 2, 1936** — a victory that did not last. The north's last decisive battle was **Maychew, on March 31, 1936**, where **Haile Selassie himself** led the remaining force, **6,000 Imperial Bodyguard** among them; the entrenched Italians were fought heroically for **thirteen hours**, but air-supported Italy could not be beaten, and the retreating Ethiopians were bombed and gassed.
