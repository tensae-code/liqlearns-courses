# Video script — The imperial ledger — trade, schools, roads, clinics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on 1960s Addis Ababa: a bank, a school, a bus, a clinic in one street — the ledger's best page, drawn in one frame.
The camera travels outward: coffee trucks on new roads, a train, telephone wires — the threads thinning as the countryside widens.
In a village the threads end. A tax assessor arrives; the school and clinic do not. The ledger's gap stands drawn.
Back in the capital, students are reading — in the schools the ledger built. What they read there opens the next lessons.
