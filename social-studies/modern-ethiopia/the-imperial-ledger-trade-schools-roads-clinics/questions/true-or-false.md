# The imperial ledger — trade, schools, roads, clinics — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The economy's backbone remained agriculture throughout the period.
**Answer: True** — the sectors modernised the top, not the base.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Health and education reached the rural majority as fully as the capital.
**Answer: False** — reach stopped far short where most Ethiopians lived.
