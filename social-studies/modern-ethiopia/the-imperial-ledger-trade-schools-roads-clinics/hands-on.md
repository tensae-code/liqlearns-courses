# Hands-on — The imperial ledger — trade, schools, roads, clinics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ledger page
Rule a page into six rows, one per sector. In each row write: what was built / how far it reached. Present the page as the opposition's evidence at a mock hearing.

## Two maps
Draw two outline maps of Ethiopia: shade where good roads run, then where clinics and secondary schools reach. Compare the two shadings and say the lesson's point in one sentence.
