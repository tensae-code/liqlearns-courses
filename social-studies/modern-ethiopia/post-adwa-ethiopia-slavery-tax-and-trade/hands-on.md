# Hands-on — Post-Adwa Ethiopia — slavery, tax and trade

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three date cards
Lay out 1923 / proclamation / 1935 in order. Under each write what changed.

## Tax in money
Count out 30 counters as birr for one gasha of land. Compare with paying in grain — say one gain and one burden of money tax.
