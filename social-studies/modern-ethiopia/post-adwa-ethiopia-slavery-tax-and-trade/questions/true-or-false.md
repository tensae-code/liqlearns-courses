# Post-Adwa Ethiopia — slavery, tax and trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The slave trade invited foreign interference in Ethiopia.
**Answer: True** — One of the book's listed harms.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Slavery was abolished before Ethiopia joined the League.
**Answer: False** — The proclamation came a year after joining, in the wake of 1923.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tax in money replaced older ways of paying tax.
**Answer: True** — Tax was made payable in money.
