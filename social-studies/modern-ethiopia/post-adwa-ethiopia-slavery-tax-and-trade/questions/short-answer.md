# Post-Adwa Ethiopia — slavery, tax and trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three harms of the slave trade from the lesson.
**Answer:** Any three: its chief victims were the youth; it destroyed security of life and property where slaves were raided; it killed productive initiative; it invited foreign interference.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things revived trade after Adwa?
**Answer:** The peace that followed centralisation and Adwa, and Ethiopia's wide diplomacy giving better trade links than before Adwa.
