# Post-Adwa Ethiopia — slavery, tax and trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The slave trade's chief victims were —
a) The elderly  b) The youth  c) Merchants  d) Soldiers
**Answer: b) The youth** — The youth.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia joined the League of Nations in —
a) 1923  b) 1935  c) 1896  d) 1905
**Answer: a) 1923** — 1923.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A year after joining, Ethiopia proclaimed —
a) A new tax  b) Slaves freed and slave buying and selling illegal  c) War on Italy  d) A republic
**Answer: b) Slaves freed and slave buying and selling illegal** — Slaves freed; buying and selling illegal.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The law banning forced labour came in —
a) 1924  b) 1935  c) 1908  d) 1917
**Answer: b) 1935** — 1935.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Tax was made payable —
a) In grain only  b) In money — 30 birr per gasha as printed  c) In salt bars  d) In labour days
**Answer: b) In money — 30 birr per gasha as printed** — In money, 30 birr per gasha as printed.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade revived because of —
a) Peace after centralisation and Adwa, and wider diplomacy  b) Italian investment  c) The railway alone  d) Egyptian demand
**Answer: a) Peace after centralisation and Adwa, and wider diplomacy** — Peace and better trade links through wide diplomacy.
