# Yohannes IV — besieging Sahati — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Yohannes's army ran short of provisions during the Sahati siege.
**Answer: True** — A month besieging with 80,000 men exhausted supplies.

**2.** Egypt's attack was one of Yohannes's troubles in 1889.
**Answer: False** — The assessment stock names Egypt's attack as the one that was NOT among his 1889 troubles — Italy, the Mahdists and internal rivals were.

**3.** The three pressures came all at once, 1887–1889.
**Answer: True** — Italians, Mahdists and internal friction together.
