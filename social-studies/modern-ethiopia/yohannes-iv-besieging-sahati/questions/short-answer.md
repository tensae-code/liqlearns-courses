# Yohannes IV — besieging Sahati — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the three pressures at once, 1887–1889.
**Answer:** Italians to the north; Mahdists to the north-west; internal friction with King Menilek and King Tekle Haymanot.

**2.** Why did the Sahati siege end without victory?
**Answer:** The Italians refused to leave their trenches for a decisive battle, his army ran short of provisions, and the Mahdist burning of Gondar forced him back to the interior.
