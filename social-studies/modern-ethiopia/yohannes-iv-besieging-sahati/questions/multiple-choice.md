# Yohannes IV — besieging Sahati — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** After Hewett, Yohannes faced Italians to the —
a) South  b) North  c) East  d) West only
**Answer: b) North** — Italians to the north.

**2.** The Mahdists pressed from the —
a) North-west  b) South-east  c) Coast  d) Lake Tana
**Answer: a) North-west** — The north-west.

**3.** Inside the country he had friction with —
a) King Menilek and King Tekle Haymanot  b) The patriarch only  c) Harar's emir  d) Aba Jifar
**Answer: a) King Menilek and King Tekle Haymanot** — Menilek and Tekle Haymanot.

**4.** At Sahati in 1888 Yohannes besieged the Italians with —
a) 20,000 men  b) 80,000 men  c) 500 men  d) 100,000 men
**Answer: b) 80,000 men** — 80,000 men for a month.

**5.** The siege failed to force a decisive battle because —
a) His army mutinied  b) The Italians would not leave their trenches  c) Rain flooded the camp  d) Egypt attacked
**Answer: b) The Italians would not leave their trenches** — The Italians stayed in their trenches.

**6.** He broke off and returned inland when he heard —
a) Menilek had crowned himself  b) The Mahdists had burned Gondar  c) Italy offered peace  d) The rains came
**Answer: b) The Mahdists had burned Gondar** — The Mahdists had burned Gondar.
