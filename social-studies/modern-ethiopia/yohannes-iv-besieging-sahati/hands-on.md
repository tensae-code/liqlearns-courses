# Hands-on — Yohannes IV — besieging Sahati

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ring
Draw a circle on paper. Write 'the throne' in the middle. Around it write the three pressures with arrows pointing in.

## A month of provisions
Put 30 stones in a bowl — one per day of the siege. Take one out per 'day' as you retell the siege; watch the bowl empty as the army's food did.
