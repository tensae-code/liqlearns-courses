# Yohannes IV — besieging Sahati

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: In 1888 Yohannes besieged the Italians at Sahati with 80,000 men for a month — ringed himself by Italy, the Mahdists and rivals at home. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## The story

After the Hewett Treaty, Yohannes faced troubles all around at once, **1887–1889**: **Italians to the north, Mahdists to the north-west**, and internal friction with **King Menilek and King Tekle Haymanot**. In **1888** he marched on **Sahati with 80,000 men** and besieged the Italians for a **month** — but they would not leave their trenches for a decisive battle, his army ran short of provisions, and news that the **Mahdists had burned Gondar** forced him back to the interior.
