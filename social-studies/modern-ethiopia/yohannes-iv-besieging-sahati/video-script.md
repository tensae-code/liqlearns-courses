# Video script — Yohannes IV — besieging Sahati

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Name the three pressures on Yohannes after Hewett: Italians north, Mahdists north-west, Menilek and Tekle Haymanot inside.
Tell the Sahati siege: 80,000 men, a month, no decisive battle, provisions short.
Say what forced him back: news that the Mahdists had burned Gondar.
