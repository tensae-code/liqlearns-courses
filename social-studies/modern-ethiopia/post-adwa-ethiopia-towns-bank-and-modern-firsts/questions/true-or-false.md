# Post-Adwa Ethiopia — towns, bank and modern firsts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gore and Goba grew from Menilek's army centres.
**Answer: True** — The book pairs army-centre towns with railway towns.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Until mid-century Ethiopia's foreign trade ran through Gambela alone.
**Answer: False** — It ran through Djibouti.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first school is dated 1908 and the first hospital 1910.
**Answer: True** — School 1908, hospital 1910, press 1912.
