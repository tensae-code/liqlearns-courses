# Post-Adwa Ethiopia — towns, bank and modern firsts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who founded the Bank of Abyssinia, and when?
**Answer:** The emperor and empress with great lords, in 1905 — Ethiopia's first bank.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the modern firsts in date order.
**Answer:** Motor car 1904; school 1908; hospital 1910; printing press 1912; aeroplane 1929.
