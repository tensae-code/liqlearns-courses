# Post-Adwa Ethiopia — towns, bank and modern firsts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first bank, founded in 1905, was —
a) Bank of Ethiopia  b) Bank of Abyssinia  c) Taytu Bank  d) Gambela Bank
**Answer: b) Bank of Abyssinia** — The Bank of Abyssinia, 1905.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Addis Ababa's first hotel (1907) was opened by —
a) Ras Hailu  b) Empress Taytu, in her own name  c) A French company  d) The Bank
**Answer: b) Empress Taytu, in her own name** — Empress Taytu.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The British trade station at Gambela (1904) served above all —
a) The coffee export trade  b) Arms imports  c) The salt trade  d) Pilgrims
**Answer: a) The coffee export trade** — The coffee export trade.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Dire Dawa, Nazret and Mojo grew from —
a) Army centres  b) The railway reaching Addis Ababa in 1917  c) Mines  d) Ports
**Answer: b) The railway reaching Addis Ababa in 1917** — The railway towns.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The first motor car came in —
a) 1904  b) 1908  c) 1912  d) 1929
**Answer: a) 1904** — 1904.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The aeroplane, the last of the firsts, came in —
a) 1912  b) 1917  c) 1929  d) 1935
**Answer: c) 1929** — 1929.
