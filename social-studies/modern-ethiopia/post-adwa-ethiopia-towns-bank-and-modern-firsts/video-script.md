# Video script — Post-Adwa Ethiopia — towns, bank and modern firsts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Give bank 1905, hotel 1907, Gambela 1904, and the railway towns Dire Dawa, Nazret and Mojo.
Recite the firsts in order: car 1904, school 1908, hospital 1910, press 1912, aeroplane 1929.
