# Hands-on — Post-Adwa Ethiopia — towns, bank and modern firsts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Firsts staircase
Write the five firsts on five steps drawn on paper, 1904 at the bottom, 1929 at the top. Climb them aloud.

## Railway towns
Draw the line Djibouti → Dire Dawa → Mojo → Nazret → Addis Ababa. Mark 1917 at the Addis end.
