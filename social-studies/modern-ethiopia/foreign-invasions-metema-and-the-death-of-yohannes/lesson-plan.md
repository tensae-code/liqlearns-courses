# Lesson plan — Foreign invasions — Metema and the death of Yohannes

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Foreign invasions — Metema and the death of Yohannes
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** At **Metema on March 9, 1889** Yohannes was gravely wounded fighting the **Mahdists**; he died the next day — and **Menilek proclaimed himself King of Kings**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has followed modern Ethiopia's story in this title's order from 1855. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The north-west burns** — Tell the sequence: Tekle Haymanot takes Metema (Jan 1887), then Sar Weha, Dembiya, the monastery destroyed and Gondar sacked (1888).
2. **Metema, March 9, 1889** — Give Metema's date, March 9, 1889, the death the next day, and Menilek's proclamation.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title, in the title's order — most nearest, the lesson just before this one. Recommended, never forced.
- A learner may jump straight in here: the lesson stands alone, and names are explained where they first appear.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say who the Ansar were and where they invaded.
2. Tell the 1887–1888 fighting in order (Metema taken, Sar Weha, Gondar sacked).
3. Give Yohannes's wounding and death dates and what Menilek did next.

## If the learner is stuck
- Learner confuses Metema 1889 with Maqdala 1868 → both emperors fell in battle season, but Tewodros died by his own hand at Maqdala; Yohannes died of wounds taken fighting the Mahdists at Metema.
