# Foreign invasions — Metema and the death of Yohannes

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: At Metema on March 9, 1889 Yohannes was gravely wounded fighting the Mahdists; he died the next day — and Menilek proclaimed himself King of Kings. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — The north-west burns

The **Mahdists** — their soldiers called **Ansar** — invaded the north-west. **King Tekle Haymanot** fought them in **January 1887** and took **Metema**, but a Dervish army struck his force at **Sar Weha in 1888**, invaded **Dembiya**, destroyed the **Mahbere Silase monastery** and **sacked Gondar**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Metema, March 9, 1889

**Yohannes**, leading an army put at **100,000**, was **gravely wounded at Metema on March 9, 1889**; his army scattered, **he died the next day**, and the Ansar **carried his head to their leader at Omdurman**. **Menilek then proclaimed himself King of Kings** — the throne passed south, to Shewa.
