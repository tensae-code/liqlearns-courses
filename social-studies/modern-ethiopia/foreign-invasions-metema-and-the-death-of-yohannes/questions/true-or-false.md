# Foreign invasions — Metema and the death of Yohannes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ansar carried Yohannes's head to their leader at Omdurman.
**Answer: True** — The book records it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Yohannes died on the battlefield the same hour he was wounded.
**Answer: False** — He died the next day.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Yohannes's army scattered after his wounding.
**Answer: True** — His army scattered; he died the next day.
