# Foreign invasions — Metema and the death of Yohannes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mahdist soldiers were called —
a) Askaris  b) Ansar  c) Banda  d) Shiftas
**Answer: b) Ansar** — Ansar — the book's glossary word.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In January 1887 King Tekle Haymanot —
a) Lost Gondar  b) Took Metema  c) Signed Liche  d) Fled to Shewa
**Answer: b) Took Metema** — He fought the Mahdists and took Metema.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In 1888 the Dervish army struck at —
a) Sar Weha, then invaded Dembiya and sacked Gondar  b) Dogali  c) Kufit  d) Ayshal
**Answer: a) Sar Weha, then invaded Dembiya and sacked Gondar** — Sar Weha, Dembiya, the Mahbere Silase monastery destroyed, Gondar sacked.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Yohannes was gravely wounded at Metema on —
a) March 9, 1889  b) April 13, 1868  c) March 1, 1896  d) May 2, 1889
**Answer: a) March 9, 1889** — March 9, 1889.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** His army at Metema is put at —
a) 20,000  b) 100,000  c) 500  d) 6,000
**Answer: b) 100,000** — 100,000 as the book gives it.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** After Yohannes died, Menilek —
a) Went into exile  b) Proclaimed himself King of Kings  c) Ceded Mitsiwa  d) Became a monk
**Answer: b) Proclaimed himself King of Kings** — Menilek proclaimed himself King of Kings.
