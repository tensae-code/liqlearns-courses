# Foreign invasions — Metema and the death of Yohannes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the Dervish army do in 1888?
**Answer:** Struck Tekle Haymanot's force at Sar Weha, invaded Dembiya, destroyed the Mahbere Silase monastery and sacked Gondar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did Yohannes IV's reign end?
**Answer:** Gravely wounded leading his army at Metema on March 9, 1889, he died the next day; the Ansar took his head to Omdurman, and Menilek proclaimed himself King of Kings.
