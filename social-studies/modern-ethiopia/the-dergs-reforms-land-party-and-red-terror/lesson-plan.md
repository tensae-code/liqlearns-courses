# Lesson plan — The Derg's reforms — land, party and Red Terror

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** The Derg's reforms — land, party and Red Terror
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Say what the 1975 land reform did.
**This lesson's own level:** the Grade 12 depth of this title — it stands on the title's earlier lessons (the empire to 1941, the restoration) and needs nothing else; a learner may jump straight in, as every name is explained where it first appears.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. History is taught as story: the video script is a story script in the animated-map spirit.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Land reform, 1975 — the revolution's great promise** — Say what the 1975 land reform did and why peasants had demanded it for a generation.
2. **Nationalisations, villagisation and resettlement** — Name the regime's other great interventions: nationalisations, villagisation, and resettlement — and that they were imposed from above.
3. **The Workers' Party, the 1987 constitution — and the Red Terror** — Say how the regime organised itself politically (the Workers' Party, the 1987 PDRE constitution) and name the Red Terror as the book names it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. Say what the 1975 land reform did.
2. Name the nationalisations, villagisation and resettlement, and that they were imposed from above.
3. Name the Workers' Party and the 1987 PDRE constitution.
4. Name the Red Terror plainly, as the book does.
