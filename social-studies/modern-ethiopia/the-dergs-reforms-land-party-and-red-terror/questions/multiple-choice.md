# The Derg's reforms — land, party and Red Terror — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1975 land reform —
a) Gave land to foreign companies  b) Took land from landlords and made it public property for those who tilled it  c) Changed nothing  d) Sold land to the highest bidder
**Answer: b) b) Took land from landlords and made it public property for those who tilled it**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The students' old demand that the reform answered was —
a) Free cities  b) Land to the tiller  c) Lower taxes only  d) A new flag
**Answer: b) b) Land to the tiller**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Nationalisation placed banks and factories —
a) Under private owners  b) Under government command  c) Under village elders  d) Under foreign rule
**Answer: b) b) Under government command**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Villagisation meant —
a) Building cities  b) Gathering scattered farms into planned villages, by order  c) Voluntary town picnics  d) Planting trees only
**Answer: b) b) Gathering scattered farms into planned villages, by order**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The regime's party was the —
a) Workers' Party of Ethiopia  b) Imperial Party  c) Students' League  d) Peasants' Union only
**Answer: a) a) Workers' Party of Ethiopia**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1987 constitution renamed the country the —
a) Empire of Ethiopia  b) People's Democratic Republic of Ethiopia  c) Federal Republic  d) Kingdom of Ethiopia
**Answer: b) b) People's Democratic Republic of Ethiopia**

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The Red Terror was —
a) A drought  b) A campaign of arrests and killings against opponents  c) A harvest festival  d) A war in Eritrea
**Answer: b) b) A campaign of arrests and killings against opponents**
