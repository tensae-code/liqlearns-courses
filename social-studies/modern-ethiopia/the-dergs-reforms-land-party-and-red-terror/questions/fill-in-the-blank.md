# The Derg's reforms — land, party and Red Terror — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The great land reform came in ___.
**Answer: 1975**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Moving people to new land by order was called ___.
**Answer: resettlement**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1987 constitution created the ___ (initials PDRE).
**Answer: People's Democratic Republic of Ethiopia**
