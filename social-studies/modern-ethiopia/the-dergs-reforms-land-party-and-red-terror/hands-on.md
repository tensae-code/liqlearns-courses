# Hands-on — The Derg's reforms — land, party and Red Terror

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Promise and cost
Draw two columns, Promise and Cost. Fill each with two items from the lesson. Read both columns aloud — the lesson's rule is that a true telling fills both.

## One proclamation
Write the 1975 land reform as a five-line proclamation in plain words: what changes, for whom, from when.
