# Lesson plan — 1991 — how the Derg fell

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** 1991 — how the Derg fell
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the Derg-era unit's close; Grade 10 Unit 6 summary content (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** beset by the EPLF, TPLF, OLF and other armed fronts, and stripped of its Soviet support as the USSR itself changed, the Derg fell when the EPRDF coalition entered Addis Ababa on 28 May 1991.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Derg lessons and the Ogaden and Eritrea lessons — the fronts named here were raised in them. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The fronts close in; the patron departs** — armed opposition from EPLF, TPLF, OLF and others amid impoverishing civil war; change in the Soviet Union ends Ethiopia's eastern-bloc military support and aid.
2. **28 May 1991 — the EPRDF enters Addis Ababa** — the EPRDF front — TPLF, EPDM, OPDO and EDORM — controls the capital, ending the Derg regime and opening the post-1991 unit.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Derg builds socialism — reforms and their costs. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the armed fronts the Derg faced.
2. Say what the Soviet change removed from the Derg.
3. Give the date and the coalition that took the capital.

## What you need
