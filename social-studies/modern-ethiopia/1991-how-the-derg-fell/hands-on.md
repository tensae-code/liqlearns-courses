# Hands-on — 1991 — how the Derg fell

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The pillars
Draw the Derg's state as a roof on three pillars — army, ideology, Soviet support. Cross out what the lesson removes from each pillar, in order, and say when the roof must fall.

## Two falls compared
Make a two-column sheet: 1974 / 1991. Rows: who fell, who entered the capital, what the entrants called themselves, what they promised first. Read the columns against each other.
