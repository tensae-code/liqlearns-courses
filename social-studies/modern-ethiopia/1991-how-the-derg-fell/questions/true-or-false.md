# 1991 — how the Derg fell — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The civil war enriched Ethiopia's countryside.
**Answer: False** — it impoverished the country year by year.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Derg regime lasted seventeen years, 1974–1991.
**Answer: True** — from deposition of the Emperor to the EPRDF's entry.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** The Derg's fronts were born from grievances this unit traces — the undone federation among them.
**Answer: True** — EPLF, TPLF, OLF and others.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The EPRDF was a single movement with no components.
**Answer: False** — a coalition — TPLF, EPDM, OPDO and EDORM.
