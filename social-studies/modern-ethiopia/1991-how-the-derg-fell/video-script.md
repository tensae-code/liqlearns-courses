# Video script — 1991 — how the Derg fell

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Ethiopia ringed with fronts: north, north-west, west — small flags growing, year by year, into armies.
Far away in Moscow, the red flag itself is changing. The supply line drawn across the map from the USSR thins, and snaps.
The fronts merge their colours into one coalition's flag. The arrows converge on the capital.
28 May 1991: the flag over the palace changes. A seventeen-year state ends; the camera holds on the city's ordinary morning — shops opening under a new power.
