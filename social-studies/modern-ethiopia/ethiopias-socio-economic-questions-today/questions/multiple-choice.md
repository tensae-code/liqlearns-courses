# Ethiopia's socio-economic questions today — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Post-1991 Ethiopia's record includes —
a) No change  b) Years of fast economic growth, with roads, dams and universities  c) Only decline  d) The end of farming
**Answer: b) b) Years of fast economic growth, with roads, dams and universities**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book teaches growth together with —
a) Nothing else  b) Its discontents — citizens experiencing it as prices and joblessness  c) Foreign praise only  d) A census
**Answer: b) b) Its discontents — citizens experiencing it as prices and joblessness**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The countryside's central question remains —
a) The flag  b) Land  c) Railways only  d) The calendar
**Answer: b) b) Land**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Inflation does what, in the lesson's words? —
a) Raises savings  b) Eats wages and savings  c) Ends trade  d) Builds dams
**Answer: b) b) Eats wages and savings**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** ADLI stands for —
a) Agricultural Development Led Industrialisation  b) African Development League International  c) Army Development Logistics Institute  d) Addis Development Land Initiative
**Answer: a) a) Agricultural Development Led Industrialisation**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The book presents development policy as —
a) Settled forever  b) An open question for the pupils' generation  c) A secret  d) A foreign matter
**Answer: b) b) An open question for the pupils' generation**
