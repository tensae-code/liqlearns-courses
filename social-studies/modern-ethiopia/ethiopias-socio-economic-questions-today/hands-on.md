# Hands-on — Ethiopia's socio-economic questions today

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four questions, four households
Write one line for each open question as a household would feel it: a farmer (land), a graduate (unemployment), a shopper (inflation), a grandmother (poverty).

## ADLI on a whiteboard
Draw ADLI as the lesson teaches it: farms first, incomes up, industry growing on agriculture's back. Label each arrow with the reason it points that way.
