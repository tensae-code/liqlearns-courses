# Hands-on — Peasants against the empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The refusal ladder
Draw Gojjam's sequence as a ladder of four rungs — refuse entry, petition, defy, armed answer. At each rung write what the peasants hoped and what the state did.

## A united front
List Woyane's three components — semi-pastoralists, peasants, local lords. For each, write one reason the restored regime disappointed them.
