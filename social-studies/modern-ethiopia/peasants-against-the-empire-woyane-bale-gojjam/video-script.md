# Video script — Peasants against the empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Tigray, 1943. Herders, farmers and lords light the same signal fire — Woyane.
South to Bale: another fire, another refusal, years long. A general's name is written, then rewritten — as governor.
1968, Gojjam: a tax assessor stops at a village boundary. The gate does not open. Petitions travel to the capital and come back unanswered.
Soldiers' boots on the roads of Mota, Qolla Dega Damot, Bichena. The countryside has spoken; the empire has answered — and neither has finished.
