# Lesson plan — Peasants against the empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Peasants against the empire — Woyane, Bale, Gojjam
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the opposition unit; Grade 10 Unit 6 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the earliest opposition to the restored empire was rural: the Woyane revolt in Tigray (1943), the Bale revolt, and the Gojjam revolts that flared finally in 1968 over the agricultural income tax.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Land, rist and gult — the tenancy grievances are these revolts' fuel. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Woyane 1943 and Bale — the first refusals** — Tigray's united front under Bilata Haile Mariam Reda against the returned regime; the Bale revolt in the south.
2. **Gojjam 1968 — the tax refused** — the 1967 agricultural income tax triggers refusal: assessors turned away, petitions, defiance — and the state's armed answer in Mota, Qolla Dega Damot and Bichena.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Land, rist and gult — who ate from whose field. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Woyane 1943: who rose, under whom, against what.
2. Place Bale and its later governor, Jagema Kello.
3. Tell Gojjam 1968: the tax, the refusal, the state's answer.
