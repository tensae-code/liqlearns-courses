# Tewodros II — unifying and modernising Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tewodros campaigned through Wollo, Gojjam, Shewa and Tigray.
**Answer: True** — The four named campaign regions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** He decreed two priests and three deacons enough for one church.
**Answer: True** — The decree as the book gives it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The clergy welcomed the tax on church land.
**Answer: False** — They resisted fiercely.
