# Tewodros II — unifying and modernising Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tewodros replaced local lords with —
a) Foreign officers  b) His own loyal governors  c) Priests  d) Merchants
**Answer: b) His own loyal governors** — His own loyal governors in Wollo, Gojjam, Shewa and Tigray.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which harmful practices did he try to abolish?
a) Farming and trade  b) Slavery and polygamy  c) Church building  d) Caravan trade
**Answer: b) Slavery and polygamy** — Slavery, polygamy and other harmful practices.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** He fought the plundering of peasants by —
a) Shiftas and soldiers  b) Tax collectors only  c) Egyptians  d) Caravans
**Answer: a) Shiftas and soldiers** — He fought plundering by shiftas and soldiers.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** His standing army was —
a) Unpaid volunteers  b) Salaried  c) Made of foreign troops  d) Only cavalry
**Answer: b) Salaried** — A salaried standing army.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** To raise revenue he tried to tax —
a) Caravans only  b) Previously exempt church land  c) The poor only  d) Foreign embassies
**Answer: b) Previously exempt church land** — Previously exempt church land.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The clergy answered his measures by —
a) Paying at once  b) Resisting fiercely and defaming him  c) Leaving Ethiopia  d) Crowning him again
**Answer: b) Resisting fiercely and defaming him** — Fierce resistance and a defamation campaign.
