# Tewodros II — unifying and modernising Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Tewodros want a united Ethiopia?
**Answer:** To be strong enough to resist foreign invasion, learning Europe's technology as he went.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did a salaried army force the church-land question?
**Answer:** A paid army and home-made arms needed revenue, and church land had been exempt from tax.
