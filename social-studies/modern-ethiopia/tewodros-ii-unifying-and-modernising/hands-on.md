# Hands-on — Tewodros II — unifying and modernising Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The revenue chain
Lay out four cards in a chain: standing army → needs pay → needs revenue → church land taxed. Say each link aloud.

## Lords out, governors in
On a map of the four regions — Wollo, Gojjam, Shewa, Tigray — cover each old lord's name with a card reading 'the king's governor'.
