# Lesson plan — Tewodros II — unifying and modernising Ethiopia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Tewodros II — unifying and modernising Ethiopia
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Tewodros replaced local lords with **his own loyal governors**, built a **salaried standing army**, and tried to tax **church land** — and the clergy resisted fiercely.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has followed modern Ethiopia's story in this title's order from 1855. It needs nothing else.

Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **One country, one rule** — Name the regions he campaigned through and say he replaced local lords with his own loyal governors.
2. **A paid army needs revenue — the church-land fight** — Say the army was salaried, the arms home-made, and the revenue plan was taxing church land — resisted by the clergy.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title, in the title's order — most nearest, the lesson just before this one. Recommended, never forced.
- A learner may jump straight in here: the lesson stands alone, and names are explained where they first appear.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how Tewodros made unity real: campaigns, loyal governors, one faith.
2. Name the harmful practices he tried to abolish.
3. Explain why he taxed church land and how the clergy answered.
