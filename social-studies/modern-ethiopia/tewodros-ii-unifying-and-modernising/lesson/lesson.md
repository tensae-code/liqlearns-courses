# Tewodros II — unifying and modernising Ethiopia

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Tewodros replaced local lords with his own loyal governors, built a salaried standing army, and tried to tax church land — and the clergy resisted fiercely. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — One country, one rule

To make political unity real, Tewodros worked with the patriarch for **one Orthodox faith**, campaigned through **Wollo, Gojjam, Shewa and Tigray** replacing local lords with **his own loyal governors**, fought division inside the church and the **plundering of peasants** by shiftas and soldiers, and tried to abolish **slavery, polygamy** and other harmful practices — all to build a united Ethiopia strong enough to resist foreign invasion, learning Europe's technology as he went.

## Sub lesson 2 — A paid army needs revenue — the church-land fight

His instruments were a **salaried standing army** and **home-made arms** — and both needed revenue. So he tried to **tax previously exempt church land**, and decreed that **two priests and three deacons were enough for one church**. The clergy resisted fiercely and ran a defamation campaign against him.
