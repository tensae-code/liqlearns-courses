# 1974 — the Ethiopian revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One pressure of early 1974 was —
a) A fall in world oil prices  b) Rising oil prices pushing up living costs  c) A bumper harvest  d) A new constitution
**Answer: b) b) Rising oil prices pushing up living costs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The soldiers' anger concerned —
a) Uniforms  b) Pay and conditions  c) Leave only  d) Parade duties
**Answer: b) b) Pay and conditions**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Derg was —
a) A political party  b) A committee of soldiers from the armed forces  c) A student union  d) A council of elders
**Answer: b) b) A committee of soldiers from the armed forces**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Derg gathered power —
a) In a single night  b) Piece by piece through the summer of 1974  c) Only after 1980  d) By election
**Answer: b) b) Piece by piece through the summer of 1974**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In September 1974 the emperor was —
a) Crowned again  b) Sent to open parliament  c) Deposed, and the monarchy abolished  d) Made head of the army
**Answer: c) c) Deposed, and the monarchy abolished**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The monarchy claimed descent from —
a) The Zagwe  b) Solomon  c) The Adal sultans  d) Aksum's Ezana only
**Answer: b) b) Solomon**
