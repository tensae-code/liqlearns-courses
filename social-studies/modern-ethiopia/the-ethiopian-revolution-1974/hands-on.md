# Hands-on — 1974 — the Ethiopian revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The cascade
Make a five-link chain — famine's shame, oil prices, mutiny, strikes, the Derg — each link an arrow into the next. Say the chain aloud to a partner without looking.

## The small car
Write the September 1974 scene as a six-line story: the palace, the soldiers, the emperor, the car. Story only — the facts are the lesson's.
