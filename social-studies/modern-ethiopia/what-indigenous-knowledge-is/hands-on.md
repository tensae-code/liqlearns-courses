# Hands-on — What indigenous knowledge is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Interview the library
Ask an elder at home for one piece of tested local knowledge — a weather sign, a soil rule, a remedy. Write it with its four marks: where it is local, how it is carried, how it was tested, what else it connects to.

## Terrace engineer
Build a hillside of books or cushions. Run water (or dried beans as rain) down it bare, then behind paper terrace steps. Say what the Konso builders knew, in engineering words.
