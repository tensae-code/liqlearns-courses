# What indigenous knowledge is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is —
a) Imported textbook knowledge  b) Communities' own tested knowledge, built over generations  c) Random superstition  d) Knowledge from one inventor
**Answer: b) b) Communities' own tested knowledge, built over generations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It is kept because —
a) It is old only  b) It worked — it survived being tried  c) It is written in law  d) Nobody questioned it
**Answer: b) b) It worked — it survived being tried**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the four marks in the lesson? —
a) Local  b) Oral  c) Printed and standardised  d) Holistic
**Answer: c) c) Printed and standardised**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Holistic' means —
a) It covers one subject only  b) Farming, healing, calendar and council are one cloth  c) It is taught in holes  d) It is secret
**Answer: b) b) Farming, healing, calendar and council are one cloth**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The gadaa system is an example of indigenous —
a) Medicine  b) Governance  c) Terracing  d) Calendar-making
**Answer: b) b) Governance**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Konso's terraces are best described as —
a) Decoration  b) Civil engineering holding hillsides and water  c) Natural rock  d) Modern concrete work
**Answer: b) b) Civil engineering holding hillsides and water**
