# What indigenous knowledge is — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is carried largely ___ — in memory and practice, not print.
**Answer: orally**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Oromo governance system named is ___.
**Answer: gadaa**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Stone ___ at Konso hold the hillsides.
**Answer: terraces**
