# What indigenous knowledge is — true or false

Say true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book treats indigenous knowledge as folklore, not knowledge.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Indigenous knowledge is fitted to one place's soil, rain and people.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia's calendar is one of the book's examples.
**Answer: True**
