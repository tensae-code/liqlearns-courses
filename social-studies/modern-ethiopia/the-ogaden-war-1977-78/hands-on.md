# Hands-on — The Ogaden war 1977–78

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## 700 kilometres
Measure 700 km on a map's scale with string — the depth of the invasion. Lay the string from the border into Ethiopia.

## The motherland call
300,000 militia in a short time: tap the table 30 times saying 'ten thousand' — feel the scale of the call.
