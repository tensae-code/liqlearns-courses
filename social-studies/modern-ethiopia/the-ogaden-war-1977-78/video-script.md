# Video script — The Ogaden war 1977–78

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Give the handovers 1948/1954, the first war 1964, and the 1977 invasion's 700 km advance under Siad Barre.
Give the answer: 300,000 militia, Soviet arms, Cuban and South Yemeni soldiers, Karamara March 1978.

A five-pointed star is drawn over the Horn; one point lies inside Ethiopia's border. The map itself is the casus belli.
