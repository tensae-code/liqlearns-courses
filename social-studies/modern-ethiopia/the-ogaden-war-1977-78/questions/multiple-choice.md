# The Ogaden war 1977–78 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain handed most of the Ogaden to Ethiopia in —
a) 1948, and the whole in 1954  b) 1960  c) 1964  d) 1977
**Answer: a) 1948, and the whole in 1954** — Most in 1948, the whole in 1954.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two governments first fought over the Ogaden in —
a) 1964, Ethiopia winning  b) 1960  c) 1954  d) 1978
**Answer: a) 1964, Ethiopia winning** — 1964 — Ethiopia won.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Greater Somalia aimed to gather under one flag —
a) Only the Ogaden  b) All Somali-inhabited lands, including the Ogaden and northern Kenya  c) Eritrea  d) The Sudan borderlands
**Answer: b) All Somali-inhabited lands, including the Ogaden and northern Kenya** — All Somali-inhabited lands: the three Somalilands, the Ogaden and northern Kenya.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In 1977 the Somali army advanced —
a) 70 km  b) 700 km  c) 7 km  d) To Addis Ababa
**Answer: b) 700 km** — 700 km into eastern Ethiopia.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia trained, in a short time, —
a) 30,000 militia  b) 300,000 militia  c) 3,000 militia  d) 6,000 Bodyguard
**Answer: b) 300,000 militia** — 300,000 militia under the motherland call.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Somali army was smashed at —
a) Karamara, March 1978  b) Maychew  c) Keren  d) Genale Doria
**Answer: a) Karamara, March 1978** — Karamara, March 1978; then it left Ethiopian territory altogether.

**7. (Sub lesson 3) ** The five points of Somalia's star stand for —
a) Five rivers  b) The five Somali-speaking territories to be united  c) Five parties  d) Five ports
**Answer: b) The five Somali-speaking territories to be united** — including the Ogaden and Kenya's Northern Frontier District.

**8. (Sub lesson 3) ** The border war of January 1964 ended —
a) With Somali victory  b) After OAU peacemaking, with no change of boundaries  c) With the Ogaden's transfer  d) In 1977
**Answer: b) After OAU peacemaking, with no change of boundaries** — the contest resumed in 1977.

**9. (Sub lesson 3) ** The teacher guide assigns the 1977 war's initiative to —
a) Ethiopia  b) President Siad Barre of Somalia, pursuing a Greater Somalia  c) The OAU  d) Cuba
**Answer: b) President Siad Barre of Somalia, pursuing a Greater Somalia** — guide evidence, printed p. 111.
