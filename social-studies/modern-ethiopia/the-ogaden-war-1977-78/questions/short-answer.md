# The Ogaden war 1977–78 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Somalia resent the Ogaden handovers?
**Answer:** Its Greater Somalia aim claimed all Somali-inhabited lands under one flag; Britain's handing the Ogaden to Ethiopia in 1948 and 1954 blocked that aim.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did Ethiopia reverse the invasion by March 1978?
**Answer:** 300,000 militia were trained under the motherland call; Soviet arms came after the Soviets broke with Somalia; Cuban and South Yemeni soldiers fought alongside; the air force and militia led the counter-attack that smashed the Somali army at Karamara.
