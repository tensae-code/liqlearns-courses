# The Ogaden war 1977–78 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Siad Barre claimed the land 'as far as the Awash is ours'.
**Answer: True** — The book quotes the claim.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cuban and South Yemeni soldiers fought beside Ethiopia in 1978.
**Answer: True** — Alongside Soviet arms after the Soviets broke with Somalia.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The air force played only a small role in the reversal.
**Answer: False** — The air force and the militia played a great role.
