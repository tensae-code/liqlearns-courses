# Towards Adwa — denouncing Wuchale and the great levy

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Menilek denounced Wuchale in February 1893, Tigray's lords swore loyalty, and on September 17, 1895 he proclaimed the kitet — the whole country rose. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Diplomacy first, divide-and-rule resisted

**Umberto of Italy** refused Menilek's letter asking that Article 17 be corrected. In **April 1891** Menilek opened his own diplomatic campaign, writing to **all the European powers** that he was not obliged to accept Italy's protectorate claim. Italy answered with persuasion — **Antonelli** pressing acceptance, wholly rejected when Menilek **publicly denounced the treaty in February 1893** — and with **divide-and-rule in Tigray**: by the **1891 Mereb agreement** **Ras Mangasha Yohannes's** Tigrayan lords promised to work with Italy, until Mangasha saw through the plot, rejected it wholly and **swore loyalty to Menilek** — in **June 1894** Mangasha and Alula came to Addis Ababa to confirm it, and in **1895** they crossed the Mereb and fought Italy at **Qoatit and Senafe**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The kitet — and Taytu's preparation

With war certain, **Empress Taytu, Fitawrari Gebeyehu** and others pressed for military action; Menilek sought **French and Russian friendship and arms**, and on **September 17, 1895** proclaimed the **kitet: the whole country rose**. Taytu had prepared early — women gathered at the palace preparing **food, drink, cooking gear, traditional medicines and wound dressings**; she ordered the northern lords to feed the army as it passed, and **herself led more than 6,000 soldiers**; women served not only in morale but **fighting, nursing, provisioning and guarding the camp**.
