# Hands-on — Towards Adwa — denouncing Wuchale and the great levy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Letters to the powers
Write Menilek's April 1891 message in one sentence on five cards — one for each power. Post them around the room.

## The palace prepares
Pack a real bundle as Taytu's women did: food, a water cup, cooking gear, a cloth for dressings. Carry it as the army's supply.
