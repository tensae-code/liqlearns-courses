# Towards Adwa — denouncing Wuchale and the great levy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Italy try divide-and-rule in Tigray, and how did it end?
**Answer:** By the 1891 Mereb agreement Italy won promises from Mangasha's lords; Mangasha saw through the plot, rejected it, swore loyalty to Menilek and fought Italy at Qoatit and Senafe.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Taytu do before the army marched?
**Answer:** Gathered women at the palace to prepare food, drink, cooking gear, medicines and wound dressings; ordered the northern lords to feed the passing army; and led more than 6,000 soldiers herself.
