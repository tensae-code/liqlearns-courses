# Towards Adwa — denouncing Wuchale and the great levy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Menilek's diplomatic campaign to the European powers opened in —
a) April 1891  b) February 1893  c) June 1894  d) September 1895
**Answer: a) April 1891** — April 1891.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Menilek publicly denounced the Treaty of Wuchale in —
a) 1891  b) February 1893  c) 1894  d) 1896
**Answer: b) February 1893** — February 1893.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy's divide-and-rule plot worked through —
a) Harar's merchants  b) Ras Mangasha Yohannes's Tigrayan lords (the 1891 Mereb agreement)  c) The patriarch  d) Shewa's governors
**Answer: b) Ras Mangasha Yohannes's Tigrayan lords (the 1891 Mereb agreement)** — The Tigrayan lords under the 1891 Mereb agreement.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Mangasha finally —
a) Joined Italy for good  b) Saw through the plot, rejected it and swore loyalty to Menilek  c) Fled to Sudan  d) Crowned himself
**Answer: b) Saw through the plot, rejected it and swore loyalty to Menilek** — He rejected the plot and swore loyalty.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The kitet was proclaimed on —
a) September 17, 1895  b) March 1, 1896  c) May 2, 1889  d) January 7, 1896
**Answer: a) September 17, 1895** — September 17, 1895 — the whole country rose.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Empress Taytu herself led —
a) 600 soldiers  b) More than 6,000 soldiers  c) The navy  d) Only the palace guard
**Answer: b) More than 6,000 soldiers** — More than 6,000 soldiers.
