# Towards Adwa — denouncing Wuchale and the great levy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Umberto agreed at once to correct Article 17.
**Answer: False** — He refused Menilek's letter.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In 1895 Mangasha and Alula crossed the Mereb and fought Italy at Qoatit and Senafe.
**Answer: True** — After confirming loyalty at Addis Ababa in June 1894.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Women's part in the kitet was morale only.
**Answer: False** — Women fought, nursed, provisioned and guarded the camp, besides preparing supplies.
