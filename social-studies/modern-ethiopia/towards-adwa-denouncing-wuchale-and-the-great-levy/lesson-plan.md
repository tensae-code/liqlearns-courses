# Lesson plan — Towards Adwa — denouncing Wuchale and the great levy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Towards Adwa — denouncing Wuchale and the great levy
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Menilek **denounced Wuchale in February 1893**, Tigray's lords **swore loyalty**, and on **September 17, 1895** he proclaimed the **kitet** — the whole country rose.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has followed modern Ethiopia's story in this title's order from 1855. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Diplomacy first, divide-and-rule resisted** — Give the sequence: April 1891 letters, February 1893 denunciation, Mangasha's loyalty sworn and confirmed June 1894.
2. **The kitet — and Taytu's preparation** — Give the kitet date, September 17, 1895, and Taytu's preparations and her 6,000 soldiers.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell the diplomatic road: 1891 letters, 1893 denunciation.
2. Explain Italy's divide-and-rule in Tigray and how Mangasha answered it.
3. Describe the kitet of September 17, 1895 and Taytu's part in the preparation.
