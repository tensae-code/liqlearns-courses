# Hands-on — Haile Selassie consolidates — the 1931 constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Strings to one hand
Draw five strings from five provincial houses to the throne in 1916; draw the same map in 1935 with the strings cut or held by the throne. Label what changed.

## First constitution
Draft three articles you would expect in a first constitution for a centralising monarchy of 1931. Then mark which of your articles serve the centre and which, if any, limit it.
