# Video script — Haile Selassie consolidates — the 1931 constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the palace: two thrones side by side — the diarchy. One light goes out; one remains.
1930: the coronation. Envoys' flags fill Addis Ababa; a crown descends; the world's cameras flash.
The map redraws: province by province, the old houses' colours fade into the centre's single colour.
A document is laid over the map — 1931. The autocracy now has a legal frame, written down.
