# Video script — ADLI and after — developing a poor, proud, agrarian nation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the paradox drawn plainly: coffee's birthplace, an uncolonised flag, Africa's largest herd — and a poverty line under the whole map.
1992: a policy document changes hands; the command economy's colour drains from the map, market colours spread town by town.
The camera goes to a farm. A better harvest; a surplus sack; a coin changing hands at market; and at the horizon, a factory chimney rising because the farmer could buy.
The last shot is a ballot box beside the factory — two arcs, development and democratisation, running forward together, unfinished.
