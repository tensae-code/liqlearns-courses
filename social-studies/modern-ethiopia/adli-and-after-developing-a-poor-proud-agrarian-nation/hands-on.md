# Hands-on — ADLI and after — developing a poor, proud, agrarian nation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The chain in objects
Lay four objects in a row — a seed sack (productivity), a grain pile (surplus), a coin (demand), a tool (industry). Walk the row aloud, saying each link's 'because'.

## Judge the bet
Debate: 'In a country where most people farm, development must begin with the farm' against 'Industry first — farms follow factories.' Both sides must use the lesson's facts.
