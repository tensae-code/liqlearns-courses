# Lesson plan — ADLI and after — developing a poor, proud, agrarian nation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** ADLI and after — developing a poor, proud, agrarian nation
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the post-1991 unit's close; Grade 10 Unit 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** post-1991 Ethiopia — among the world's poorest countries despite its history and resources — adopted a free-market model in 1992 and the ADLI strategy: raise agricultural productivity to make the surplus that funds industrialisation, while democratisation faces its challenges.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole post-1991 unit. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1992 — the free-market turn** — the TGE adopts a free-market economic model, a clear departure from the previous regime's socialism; the starting fact of deep poverty.
2. **ADLI — industry out of agriculture** — Agricultural Development-Led Industrialization: agricultural productivity generates the surplus that stimulates demand and founds industrialisation of the agrarian economy; democratisation's challenges close the arc.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Who owns the Nile? — Abay hydro-politics. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the starting fact: poverty amid history and resources.
2. Give 1992's model change and what it departed from.
3. Explain ADLI's chain: farm productivity → surplus → demand → industry.
