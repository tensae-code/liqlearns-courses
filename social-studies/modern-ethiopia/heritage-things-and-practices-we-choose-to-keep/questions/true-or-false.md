# Heritage — things and practices we choose to keep — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Heritage survives automatically, without any generation choosing to keep it.
**Answer: False** — heritage is a decision renewed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** A practice can be heritage exactly as a building can.
**Answer: True** — the lesson's central insight.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Language counts as heritage in the practice family.
**Answer: True** — language, culture, song, literature, dress.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Understanding heritage means watching who bestows value on it, and why.
**Answer: True** — heritage values are bestowed, not intrinsic prices.
