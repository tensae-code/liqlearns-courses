# Hands-on — Heritage — things and practices we choose to keep

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The unsellable object
Choose one object in your home that would not be sold at any reasonable price. Write its heritage account: what it is, who kept it before you, what value is bestowed on it, who should receive it next.

## A practice's pedigree
Take one song, greeting or dress practice of your community. Trace its handing-down across three generations by interviewing the oldest bearer you can reach.
