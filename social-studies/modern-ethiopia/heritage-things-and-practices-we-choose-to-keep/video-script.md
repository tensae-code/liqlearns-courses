# Video script — Heritage — things and practices we choose to keep

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a house at evening: an old coffee pot is lifted down with two hands, carefully, and set before guests.
The camera widens: a stela field, a rock church, a manuscript chest — the things a country keeps.
Then sound rises: a song being taught, line by line, to children — nothing in their hands, everything in their memory.
The two streams — thing and practice — flow together into one word over the map: HERITAGE, the glue of who we are.
