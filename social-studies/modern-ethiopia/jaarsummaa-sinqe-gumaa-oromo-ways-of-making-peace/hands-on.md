# Hands-on — Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo ways of making peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The stick's authority
Discuss and write: why does a society give its formally least powerful members an unstoppable instrument? Find the design logic — who can safely say 'stop' to armed men, and why does the custom protect exactly them?

## Cut the chain
Draw a feud as a chain of five links, killing answering killing. Mark where Gumaa's settlement cuts it, and write beside the cut what each side gives and receives.
