# Lesson plan — Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo ways of making peace

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo ways of making peace
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the indigenous knowledge unit; Grade 10 Unit 9 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three Oromo peace systems: Jaarsummaa, reconciliation mediated by elders; Sinqe, the institution of the woman's ritual stick, through which women's collective voice intervenes against injustice; and Gumaa, the blood-compensation settlement that closes a killing's feud.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Mada'a and Dagu — the Afar systems are the comparison set. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Jaarsummaa — the elders reconcile** — elder-mediated reconciliation, figured in the book as a process: the community's senior men hearing, weighing and joining the parties again.
2. **Sinqe — the women's voice; Gumaa — closing the feud** — the Sinqe institution centred on the Oromo woman's ritual stick, women's collective intervention against injustice; Gumaa, blood-compensation settling a killing.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Mada'a and Dagu — Afar law and the human news network. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define Jaarsummaa: elder-mediated reconciliation.
2. Explain Sinqe: the stick, whose voice it carries, what it can stop.
3. Define Gumaa: what it settles and how it ends a feud.
