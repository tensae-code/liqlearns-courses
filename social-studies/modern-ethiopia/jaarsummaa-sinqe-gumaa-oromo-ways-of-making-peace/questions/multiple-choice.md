**5. (Sub lesson 1) ** Set beside the Afar systems, the Oromo systems share the architecture of —

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In Sinqe, the women's institution, women could intervene in a dispute by —
a) Issuing written fines  b) Raising their ritual voice together so the fighters must stop and listen  c) Electing the judges  d) Closing the market
**Answer: b) Raising their ritual voice together so the fighters must stop and listen** — Sinqe gives women a protected way to halt violence.

**7. (Sub lesson 2)** A Gumaa settlement is complete when —

**Answer: b) Compensation is paid and the two families are reconciled, so the feud does not restart** — payment plus reconciliation is what closes a feud.
