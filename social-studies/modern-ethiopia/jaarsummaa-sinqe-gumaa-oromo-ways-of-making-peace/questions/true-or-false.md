# Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo ways of making peace — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Jaarsummaa rests on the elders' moral authority rather than force.
**Answer: True** — a settlement spoken before witnesses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Sinqe is an instrument of the society's military rulers.
**Answer: False** — it arms the formally least powerful — women — with an unstoppable voice.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Jaarsummaa settlements are spoken before witnesses.
**Answer: True** — publicity is part of the settlement's force.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Gumaa lets a killing's feud run until one side is destroyed.
**Answer: False** — it cuts the chain at its first link.
