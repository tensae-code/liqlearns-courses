# Video script — Jaarsummaa, Sinqe, Gumaa — Oromo ways of making peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens under an Oromo sycamore. Elders take their seats in a half-circle; two parties are joined, hand to hand — Jaarsummaa.
A procession of women enters the frame, the siinqee raised. Armed men lower their weapons as the stick passes. The intervention holds.
Night: two families at a boundary, a feud's first link glowing hot. Elders step between; compensation changes hands; the link cools and is cut.
Three instruments, one word over the map as it fades: peace — engineered, not wished for.
