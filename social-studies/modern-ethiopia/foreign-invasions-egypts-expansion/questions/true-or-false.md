# Foreign invasions — Egypt's expansion — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia and Egypt had long ties of culture, trade and religion.
**Answer: True** — The book's opening for this section.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt taxed the border people lightly and protected their crops.
**Answer: False** — It taxed heavily, enslaved, and burned houses and crops.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ismail sought European support for his greater-Egypt plan.
**Answer: True** — He sought it under his cover story.
