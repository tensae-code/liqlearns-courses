# Foreign invasions — Egypt's expansion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Egypt do in the north-west borderlands?
**Answer:** Taxed the people heavily, enslaved, and burned houses and crops.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did control of the Abay's source matter so much to Egypt?
**Answer:** The Abay feeds the Nile, Egypt's lifeline — holding its source meant a hand on Egypt's water and power in North Africa.
