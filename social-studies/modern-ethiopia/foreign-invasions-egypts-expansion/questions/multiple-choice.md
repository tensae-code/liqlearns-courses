# Foreign invasions — Egypt's expansion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Muhammad Ali's government took Sudan in —
a) 1821  b) 1872  c) 1863  d) 1884
**Answer: a) 1821** — Sudan in 1821.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt struck Ethiopia's —
a) South  b) North-west — Simien, Dembiya, the Asosa sheikhdoms  c) East — Harar  d) Centre — Shewa
**Answer: b) North-west — Simien, Dembiya, the Asosa sheikhdoms** — The north-west: Simien, Dembiya, Asosa.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In 1872 Egyptian forces took —
a) Gondar  b) Mitsiwa and the Bogos country  c) Adwa  d) Jimma
**Answer: b) Mitsiwa and the Bogos country** — Mitsiwa, its dependencies and Bogos.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Khedive Ismail Pasha ruled —
a) 1821–1848  b) 1863–79  c) 1879–1882  d) 1889–1913
**Answer: b) 1863–79** — 1863–79.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ismail planned to control —
a) The caravan routes  b) The Abay's source  c) Lake Tana's fisheries only  d) The port of Zeila
**Answer: b) The Abay's source** — The source of the Abay.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** His cover story to Europe was —
a) Freeing the slaves of Egypt  b) 'Spreading Western civilisation and stopping the slave trade'  c) Building railways  d) Converting Ethiopia
**Answer: b) 'Spreading Western civilisation and stopping the slave trade'** — The quoted cover: civilisation and stopping the slave trade.
