# Hands-on — Foreign invasions — Egypt's expansion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow the river
Draw the Abay flowing from Ethiopia's highlands north to Egypt. Mark who held its source and who wanted it — the map explains the invasion.

## Cover and aim
Write Ismail's cover words on one card and his real aim on another. Hold them up in order and say which Europe heard.
