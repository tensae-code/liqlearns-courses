# Hands-on — Who owns the Nile? — Abay hydro-politics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the flow
On the lesson's basin picture, trace the Abay from Lake Tana to the sea with your finger, naming each country the water passes and one thing each country wants from it.

## A fair division draft
In groups, draft three articles for a new Nile agreement that all riparians could sign: one on rights, one on use, one on disputes. Read them against the lesson's two key words — equitable, harmonious.
