# Video script — Who owns the Nile? — Abay hydro-politics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens in Ethiopia's highlands in the rains. Streams gather into the Abay; the Tekkeze joins the story; the rivers pour north.
A number is written at the source: 86 percent. The camera follows the water through Sudan to Egypt's green Delta and the sea.
Two documents fall across the river like dams — 1929, 1959 — each stamped with two names only. Upstream, the giver's name is missing.
New flags rise along the upper river. A third document is being drafted on screen, its heading two words: equitable — harmonious. The river flows on beneath all the paper.
