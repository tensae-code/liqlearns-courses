# Video script — Reading Ethiopia in stone and parchment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a monastery chest: a Ge'ez manuscript is lifted into light, its parchment pages turning.
North to Yeha: a stone temple stands against the dawn, older than Rome, its shadow long.
The camera travels the tour in one flight — mosque domes at Harar, a wooden palace at Jimma, a river vanishing into Sof Omar's dark, a wall walking a Dawuro ridge.
Six kinds, one country. The tour ends where the unit ends: heritage is what a people chooses, again and again, to keep.
