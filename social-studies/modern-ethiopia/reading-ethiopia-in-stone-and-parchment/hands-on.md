# Hands-on — Reading Ethiopia in stone and parchment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The county tour
Design a one-day heritage tour of your own locality in the book's manner — by kinds: one written, one built, one natural-adopted heritage. Write its stops with one line each on what each stop teaches.

## Stone or parchment?
Take two heritage items — one stone, one parchment/practice. Argue which is harder to keep and why, using the three kinds' keeping-verbs from the earlier lesson.
