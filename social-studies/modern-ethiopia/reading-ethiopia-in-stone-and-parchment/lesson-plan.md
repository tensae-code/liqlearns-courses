# Lesson plan — Reading Ethiopia in stone and parchment

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Reading Ethiopia in stone and parchment
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the heritage thread's closing tour; Grade 10 Unit 9 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** beyond the UNESCO list, Ethiopia's tangible heritage toured by kind: Ge'ez manuscripts, the Temple of Yeha (c.700 BC), mosques, palaces such as Jimma's Abba Jifar II palace, the Sof Omar caves, and the Halala Keela wall of Dawuro.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ethiopia on the World Heritage List — this tour walks the unlisted wealth beside the listed. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Parchment and temple — manuscripts, Yeha, mosques** — Ge'ez manuscripts figured; the Temple of Yeha linked to South Arabia around 700 BC; Ethiopia's historic mosques.
2. **Palaces, caves, walls — Jimma, Sof Omar, Halala Keela** — palaces (the review names Jimma's Abba Jifar II palace), houses and caves — the Sof Omar caves figured; the Halala Keela (Halala Kab) wall of Dawuro figured.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ethiopia on the World Heritage List. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the tour's kinds: manuscripts, temples, mosques, palaces, caves, walls.
2. Give Yeha's date and South Arabia link; Sof Omar; Halala Keela's place.
3. Name Jimma's palace and its builder as the review gives it.
