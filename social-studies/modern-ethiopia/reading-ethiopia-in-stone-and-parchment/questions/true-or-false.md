# Reading Ethiopia in stone and parchment — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The tour teaches only UNESCO-listed sites.
**Answer: False** — it tours the unlisted wealth beside the listed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The tour walks both Christian and Islamic built heritage.
**Answer: True** — manuscripts and churches' world, and mosques alike.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Ethiopia's manuscript culture is carried in churches and monasteries.
**Answer: True** — the written twin of oral transmission.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Halala Keela wall was raised by a people's own labour as a defensive work.
**Answer: True** — Dawuro's wall, figured in the book.
