# Video script — The Battle of Adwa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

March 1, 1896. The feast of St George.
A double agent whispers: they will not fight today.
Baratieri believes him. Attack at dawn.
Fewer Italians — but better guns, modern training.
What they do not know: Ethiopia's size, Ethiopia's tactics.
By afternoon, the stunning victory.
The shattered army abandons Tigray.
The dream of colonising Ethiopia has failed.
That is Adwa.
