# The Battle of Adwa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Baratieri choose dawn on March 1?
**Answer:** On Basha Awalom's false report that the Ethiopians would not fight on a feast day, he expected to catch them unprepared on St George's feast.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What was the battle's outcome by afternoon?
**Answer:** A stunning Ethiopian victory; Baratieri ordered his shattered army to abandon Tigray, and Italy's colonising dream failed.
