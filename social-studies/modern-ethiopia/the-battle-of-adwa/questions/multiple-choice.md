# The Battle of Adwa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Italian army at Adwa was —
a) Larger and better armed  b) Fewer but better armed, with modern training  c) Untrained  d) Without officers
**Answer: b) Fewer but better armed, with modern training** — Fewer but better armed.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the Italians lack?
a) Guns  b) True knowledge of the Ethiopian army's size and tactics  c) Courage  d) Ammunition entirely
**Answer: b) True knowledge of the Ethiopian army's size and tactics** — Knowledge of Ethiopian size and tactics.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Basha Awalom's report claimed the Ethiopians —
a) Had no guns  b) Would not fight on a feast day  c) Had retreated  d) Were divided
**Answer: b) Would not fight on a feast day** — Would not fight on a feast day — false assurance.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The battle was fought on —
a) Sunday, March 1, 1896 — the feast of St George  b) March 31, 1936  c) May 5, 1941  d) January 21, 1896
**Answer: a) Sunday, March 1, 1896 — the feast of St George** — Sunday, March 1, 1896.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** By the afternoon —
a) The Italians held Adwa  b) The Ethiopians had won a stunning victory  c) Rain stopped the battle  d) Both sides retreated
**Answer: b) The Ethiopians had won a stunning victory** — A stunning Ethiopian victory by the afternoon.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** After the battle Baratieri ordered his army to —
a) Hold Tigray  b) Abandon Tigray altogether  c) March on Addis Ababa  d) Besiege Mekelle again
**Answer: b) Abandon Tigray altogether** — Abandon Tigray altogether.
