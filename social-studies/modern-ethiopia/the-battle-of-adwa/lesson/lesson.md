# The Battle of Adwa

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: On Sunday, March 1, 1896 — St George's feast — Italy attacked at dawn on a false report, and by afternoon Ethiopia had won Adwa. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — The false assurance

The Italians were **fewer but better armed**, with modern training and seasoned officers; what they lacked was true knowledge of the Ethiopian army's size and tactics. **Baratieri** sent detachments to provoke the Ethiopians out of their positions into artillery range; local people fed Ethiopian intelligence — among it the **double agent Basha Awalom's report that the Ethiopians would not fight on a feast day** — and on that false assurance Baratieri ordered the attack for **dawn on Sunday, March 1, 1896, the feast of St George**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dawn attack, afternoon victory

The Italians attacked at dawn; after heavy fighting the Ethiopians, **fighting with great heroism, won a stunning victory by the afternoon**, and Baratieri ordered his shattered army to **abandon Tigray altogether**. **Italy's dream of colonising Ethiopia had failed** — on the field where one word in a treaty, may against must, had been defended with arms.
