# Hands-on — The Battle of Adwa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dawn to afternoon
Draw the day's arc on paper: dawn attack on the left, afternoon victory on the right. Mark where the false report sits — before the arc begins.

## The double agent's card
Write Awalom's report on a card: 'they will not fight on a feast day'. Turn it over: 'false'. Say who believed it and what it cost him.
