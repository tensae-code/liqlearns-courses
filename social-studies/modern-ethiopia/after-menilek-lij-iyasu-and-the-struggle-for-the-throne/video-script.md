# Video script — After Menilek — Lij Iyasu and the struggle for the throne

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Addis Ababa, 1906. In the palace, a sickroom. Around it, the great houses' lights come on, one by one, calculating.
A document is sealed: the succession arranged, a young name written — Lij Iyasu.
The young ruler rides out — new alliances, new reforms — and the old lights around the palace turn from gold to red.
The camera pulls back. The throne stands between two eras; the struggle for it will run for thirty years.
