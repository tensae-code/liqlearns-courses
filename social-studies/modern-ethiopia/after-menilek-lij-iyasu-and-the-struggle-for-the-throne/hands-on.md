# Hands-on — After Menilek — Lij Iyasu and the struggle for the throne

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The counting court
Draw the palace at the centre and the great houses around it. Against each house write what it stands to gain or lose at a succession. Read the drawing as the lesson's first scene.

## The heir's dilemma
Write two short counsels to the young Iyasu: one from an old courtier urging caution, one from a reformer urging speed. Read both; say which counsel history rewarded.
