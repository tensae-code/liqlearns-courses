# Hands-on — After Adwa — recognition and borders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Embassy flags in order
Make five small flags — Italy 1896, Britain 1897, France 1897, USA 1903, Germany 1905. Stand them in a row in year order.

## Trace today's shape
Trace Ethiopia's outline on paper. Along each edge write the treaty and year that fixed it.
