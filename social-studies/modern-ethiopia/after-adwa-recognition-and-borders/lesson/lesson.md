# After Adwa — recognition and borders

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Adwa won world recognition: embassies opened in Addis Ababa from 1896, borders were drawn 1897–1908, and the Treaty of Addis Ababa cancelled Wuchale. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Embassies in Addis Ababa

Adwa made Ethiopia the **only African country to defend its freedom in war** and won her **world recognition as a sovereign state**, shown first in embassies and consulates opened in Addis Ababa: **Italy 1896, Britain and France 1897, the USA 1903, Germany 1905**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Borders drawn, Wuchale cancelled

Second, Ethiopia's **borders** with the neighbouring colonial powers were drawn between **1897 and 1908** — Djibouti with France 1897; British Somaliland 1897; Eritrea with Italy 1900, 1902, 1908; Sudan 1902; Kenya 1907; Italian Somaliland 1908 — the victory giving **today's Ethiopia its shape**. In **October 1896** Italy's envoys signed the **Treaty of Addis Ababa**: **Wuchale cancelled, Ethiopia's independence recognised**, and Ethiopia agreeing to release the prisoners of war.
