# After Adwa — recognition and borders — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The USA opened its embassy in 1903 and Germany in 1905.
**Answer: True** — The book's order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The victory gave today's Ethiopia its shape.
**Answer: True** — The borders drawn 1897–1908 fixed the modern outline.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the Treaty of Addis Ababa Ethiopia kept the Italian prisoners.
**Answer: False** — Ethiopia agreed to release the prisoners of war.
