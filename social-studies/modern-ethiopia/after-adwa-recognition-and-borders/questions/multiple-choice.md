# After Adwa — recognition and borders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Adwa made Ethiopia —
a) A protectorate  b) The only African country to defend its freedom in war  c) A League member at once  d) An Italian ally
**Answer: b) The only African country to defend its freedom in war** — The only African country to defend its freedom in war.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first embassy opened after Adwa was —
a) Britain, 1897  b) Italy, 1896  c) USA, 1903  d) Germany, 1905
**Answer: b) Italy, 1896** — Italy 1896.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain and France opened embassies in —
a) 1896  b) 1897  c) 1903  d) 1905
**Answer: b) 1897** — 1897.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's borders were drawn between —
a) 1889 and 1896  b) 1897 and 1908  c) 1908 and 1917  d) 1935 and 1941
**Answer: b) 1897 and 1908** — 1897–1908.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Kenya border treaty was in —
a) 1897  b) 1902  c) 1907  d) 1908
**Answer: c) 1907** — Kenya 1907.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Treaty of Addis Ababa —
a) Restored Wuchale  b) Cancelled Wuchale and recognised Ethiopia's independence  c) Ceded Eritrea anew  d) Made Italy protector
**Answer: b) Cancelled Wuchale and recognised Ethiopia's independence** — Wuchale cancelled; independence recognised; prisoners released.
