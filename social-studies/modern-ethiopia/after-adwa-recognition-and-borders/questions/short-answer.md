# After Adwa — recognition and borders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the embassies in the order they opened.
**Answer:** Italy 1896; Britain and France 1897; the USA 1903; Germany 1905.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do the border treaties count as recognition?
**Answer:** Colonial powers fixed Ethiopia's borders with her by treaty, 1897–1908 — treating her as the sovereign state Adwa had proved her to be.
