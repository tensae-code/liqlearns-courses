# The Patriots — the first phase, 1936–37

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Italy held the towns; the countryside resisted — the Patriots' first phase peaked in the 1936 rains attack on Addis Ababa, and Abune Petros was shot rather than submit. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — The countryside refuses

Italian rule was **confined to the towns**; the national liberation struggle ran in the **countryside**, strongest in **Shewa, Gondar and Gojjam**, in two phases. The first phase continued the main war to **early 1937**, led by the great lords who had fought in it: **Ras Imru** retreated to **Gore** and, gathering **Holeta academy cadets** and educated Ethiopians, founded the **Black Lion organisation** to continue the fight.

## Sub lesson 2 — The attempt on Addis Ababa; Abune Petros

The first phase's great effort was the **attempt to free Addis Ababa in the rains of 1936** — a coordinated attack from several directions to raise a popular rising — led by Ras Kassa's sons **Dejazmach Abera Kassa and Asfawosen Kassa**, the Adwa hero **Dejazmach Balcha**, **Balambaras (later Ras) Abebe Aregay** and **Dejazmach Fiqre Mariam Yinadu**; uncoordinated, each group fought the Fascists separately. **Abune Petros**, asked to make the people submit to Italy, **refused and was shot dead**.
