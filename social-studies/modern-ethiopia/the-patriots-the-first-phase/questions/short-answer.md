# The Patriots — the first phase, 1936–37 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who gathered into the Black Lion organisation?
**Answer:** Holeta academy cadets and educated Ethiopians, gathered by Ras Imru at Gore.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three leaders of the 1936 attempt on Addis Ababa.
**Answer:** Any three of: Abera Kassa, Asfawosen Kassa, Dejazmach Balcha, Abebe Aregay, Fiqre Mariam Yinadu.
