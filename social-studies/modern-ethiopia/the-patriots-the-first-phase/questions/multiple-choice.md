# The Patriots — the first phase, 1936–37 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italian rule was confined to —
a) The countryside  b) The towns  c) The north only  d) The coast
**Answer: b) The towns** — The towns; the struggle ran in the countryside.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The liberation struggle was strongest in —
a) Harar and Jimma  b) Shewa, Gondar and Gojjam  c) Tigray and Wollo  d) The Ogaden
**Answer: b) Shewa, Gondar and Gojjam** — Shewa, Gondar and Gojjam.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Black Lion organisation was founded by —
a) Ras Imru, at Gore  b) Abebe Aregay  c) Ras Desta  d) Balcha
**Answer: a) Ras Imru, at Gore** — Ras Imru, gathering Holeta cadets and educated Ethiopians at Gore.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The first phase's great effort was —
a) The Ogaden war  b) The attempt to free Addis Ababa in the rains of 1936  c) The march to Keren  d) A naval raid
**Answer: b) The attempt to free Addis Ababa in the rains of 1936** — The coordinated 1936 rains attack on Addis Ababa.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The attack failed in part because —
a) It rained  b) Uncoordinated, each group fought separately  c) The emperor forbade it  d) Graziani surrendered
**Answer: b) Uncoordinated, each group fought separately** — Each group fought the Fascists separately.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Abune Petros was shot because he —
a) Led an army  b) Refused to make the people submit to Italy  c) Spied for Italy  d) Blessed the invasion
**Answer: b) Refused to make the people submit to Italy** — He refused the demand to preach submission.
