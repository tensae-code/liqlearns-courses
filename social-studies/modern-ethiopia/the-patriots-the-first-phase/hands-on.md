# Hands-on — The Patriots — the first phase, 1936–37

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Towns and countryside
On a map dot the towns in one colour and shade the countryside in another. Say which colour Italy held.

## The bishop's answer
Write the demand 'make the people submit' on a card and Petros's answer beneath: 'I refuse.' Read both aloud.
