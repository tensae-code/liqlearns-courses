# Lesson plan — The Patriots — the first phase, 1936–37

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** The Patriots — the first phase, 1936–37
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Italy held the **towns**; the countryside resisted — the **Patriots'** first phase peaked in the **1936 rains attack on Addis Ababa**, and **Abune Petros** was shot rather than submit.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has followed modern Ethiopia's story in this title's order from 1855. It needs nothing else.

The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The countryside refuses** — Say Italian rule held the towns only, the struggle ran in Shewa, Gondar and Gojjam, and Imru founded the Black Lion at Gore.
2. **The attempt on Addis Ababa; Abune Petros** — Name the 1936 attack's leaders and Abune Petros's refusal and death.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say where Italian rule reached and where the struggle ran.
2. Tell Ras Imru's Black Lion founding at Gore.
3. Name the leaders of the 1936 Addis Ababa attempt and Abune Petros's stand.
