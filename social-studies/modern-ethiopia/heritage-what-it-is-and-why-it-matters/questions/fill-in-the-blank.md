# Heritage — what it is and why it matters — fill in the blank

Fill in the blank with the lesson's own word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A festival carried in doing is ___ heritage.
**Answer: intangible**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The World Heritage Convention dates to ___.
**Answer: 1972**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What people built is ___ heritage; what the earth made is natural.
**Answer: cultural**
