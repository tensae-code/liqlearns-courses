# Heritage — what it is and why it matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Intangible heritage is —
a) Stone monuments  b) What lives in doing — festivals, systems, ceremonies  c) Buried treasure  d) Museum stores
**Answer: b) b) What lives in doing — festivals, systems, ceremonies**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A terraced hillside is —
a) Intangible  b) Tangible heritage  c) Not heritage  d) Natural only
**Answer: b) b) Tangible heritage**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of heritage's values in the book? —
a) Commercial rent  b) National-symbolic — identity a country points to  c) Military use  d) Secrecy
**Answer: b) b) National-symbolic — identity a country points to**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** As historical value, heritage is —
a) A story only  b) Evidence — the past you can stand in front of  c) A tax source  d) A border marker
**Answer: b) b) Evidence — the past you can stand in front of**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The World Heritage Convention was agreed in —
a) 1945  b) 1963  c) 1972  d) 1995
**Answer: c) c) 1972**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The Convention's two great types are —
a) Old and new  b) Cultural and natural  c) Urban and rural  d) Royal and common
**Answer: b) b) Cultural and natural**
