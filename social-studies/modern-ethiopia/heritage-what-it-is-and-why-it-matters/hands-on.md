# Hands-on — Heritage — what it is and why it matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your town's two columns
Do the book's own closing task in miniature: list your town or neighbourhood's heritage in two columns, tangible and intangible. Star the one you would register first, and say why.

## Value detective
Take one famous Ethiopian site. Write its three values in three lines — evidence, identity, binding — each with a fact from this course.
