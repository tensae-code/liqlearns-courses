# Lesson plan — Heritage — what it is and why it matters

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Heritage — what it is and why it matters
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Define heritage, tangible and intangible, with one example each.
**This lesson's own level:** the Grade 12 depth of this title — it stands on the title's earlier lessons (the empire to 1941, the restoration) and needs nothing else; a learner may jump straight in, as every name is explained where it first appears.

History is taught as story: the video script is a story script in the animated-map spirit.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **What heritage is: the tangible and the intangible** — Define heritage as inherited physical artefacts and intangible attributes of a group or society.
2. **Why it matters: historical, national-symbolic, socio-political values** — Name heritage's values as the book gives them: historical, national-symbolic, and socio-political.
3. **The 1972 World Heritage Convention: cultural and natural** — Say that the 1972 World Heritage Convention sorts protected heritage into cultural and natural types.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
