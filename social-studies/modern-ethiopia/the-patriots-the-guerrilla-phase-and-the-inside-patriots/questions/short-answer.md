# The Patriots — the guerrilla phase and the inside patriots — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happened in Yekatit 1937?
**Answer:** Deboch and Asgedom's bomb wounded Graziani; the Fascists answered with three days of mass killing aimed chiefly at educated Ethiopians — 30,000 killed in Addis Ababa alone, as printed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the guerrilla Patriots solve their supply problem?
**Answer:** They sheltered in farmers' houses, captured provisions and arms from the enemy, and received medicine and intelligence from inside patriots.
