# The Patriots — the guerrilla phase and the inside patriots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The bomb thrown at Graziani was thrown by —
a) Abriham Deboch and Moges Asgedom  b) Two Italian officers  c) Abebe Aregay  d) Shewareged Gedle
**Answer: a) Abriham Deboch and Moges Asgedom** — Abriham Deboch and Moges Asgedom.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Fascist answer was —
a) Withdrawal  b) Three days of mass killing in Addis Ababa and surroundings  c) A trial  d) Freeing prisoners
**Answer: b) Three days of mass killing in Addis Ababa and surroundings** — Three days of mass killing, aimed chiefly at the educated.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The book prints the Addis Ababa toll as —
a) 3,000  b) 30,000  c) 300,000  d) 300
**Answer: b) 30,000** — 30,000 Ethiopians killed in Addis Ababa alone, as printed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Guerrilla tactics included —
a) Set battles only  b) Sudden attacks and cutting communication lines  c) Naval blockades  d) Trench sieges
**Answer: b) Sudden attacks and cutting communication lines** — Sudden attacks, cut lines, striking when conditions favoured.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The most famous named inside patriot is —
a) Kebedech Seyoum  b) Woizero Shewareged Gedle  c) Taytu  d) Liqieylesh Beyan
**Answer: b) Woizero Shewareged Gedle** — Woizero Shewareged Gedle.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Patriots got provisions and arms by —
a) Italian supply depots' gifts  b) Sheltering in farmers' houses and capturing them from the enemy  c) Importing by air  d) Taxing towns
**Answer: b) Sheltering in farmers' houses and capturing them from the enemy** — Farmers' shelter and capture from the enemy.
