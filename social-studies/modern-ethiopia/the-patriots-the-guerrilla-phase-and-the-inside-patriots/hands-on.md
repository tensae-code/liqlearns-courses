# Hands-on — The Patriots — the guerrilla phase and the inside patriots

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cut the line
Stretch a string 'telegraph line' between two chairs. A Patriot team must cut it (untie it) silently while a 'patrol' counts to twenty with eyes closed.

## The note passed
Write a one-line message — 'convoy at dawn' — and pass it hand to hand across the room hidden in a book, as inside patriots passed intelligence.
