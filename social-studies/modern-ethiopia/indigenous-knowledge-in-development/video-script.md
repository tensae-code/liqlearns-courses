# Video script — Indigenous knowledge in development

The story voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Told as story, in the animated-map spirit: the map and the timeline move as the story moves. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Development that arrives ignoring the local library usually fails against it. The book's argument runs the other way: join them. In agriculture, local seed knowledge and soil practice stand beside new agronomy. In health, tested traditional remedies are studied, not sneered at. In environmental care, customary rules that protected forests and grazing for centuries can carry modern conservation. And in conflict resolution, elders' councils and customary mediation settle what courts reach slowly.

The library burns a little every year — and its books are people. When an elder dies unheard, a weather system, a seed line, a mediation craft dies with her. Knowledge carried orally has no second copy. That is the risk the book sets beside the promise: join the old knowledge to the new in time, record it with respect, and let its carriers teach — or watch whole shelves of tested answers vanish within one generation.
