# Indigenous knowledge in development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's argument about indigenous knowledge and development is to —
a) Replace the old with the new  b) Join them  c) Ignore development  d) Ban modern methods
**Answer: b) b) Join them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Customary rules protecting forests can carry —
a) Nothing  b) Modern conservation  c) Only festivals  d) Tax policy
**Answer: b) b) Modern conservation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Indigenous knowledge is at risk because —
a) It is printed too widely  b) It is carried orally by people, with no second copy  c) It is banned  d) It changes too fast
**Answer: b) b) It is carried orally by people, with no second copy**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Elders' councils contribute to development through —
a) Tax collection  b) Conflict resolution and mediation  c) Road building  d) Printing
**Answer: b) b) Conflict resolution and mediation**
