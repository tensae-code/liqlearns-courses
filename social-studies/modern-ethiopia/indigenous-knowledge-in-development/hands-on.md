# Hands-on — Indigenous knowledge in development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Join, don't replace
Pick one local practice from home or neighbourhood. Write two columns: what it already solves, and what a modern method could add to it — not replace.

## Record one shelf
With permission, record (in writing) one elder's tested rule and its story of being tried. File it as the previous lesson's four marks require.
