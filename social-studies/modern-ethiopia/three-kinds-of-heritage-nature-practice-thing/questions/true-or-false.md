# Three kinds of heritage — nature, practice, thing — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** A festival uncelebrated for a generation is safely stored heritage.
**Answer: False** — uncelebrated, it is lost — this kind lives in people.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Simien mountains exemplify natural heritage in this lesson.
**Answer: True** — the picture's first panel.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Intangible heritage includes its associated instruments and artefacts.
**Answer: True** — the stick, the instrument, the costume travel with the practice.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Natural heritage must be kept by practising it yearly.
**Answer: False** — it is kept by protection — a habitat cannot be stored or performed.
