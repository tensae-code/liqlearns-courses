# Hands-on — Three kinds of heritage — nature, practice, thing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the shelf
Write twelve heritage items (from home, town and the lessons) on cards. Sort them into the three kinds; where a card resists — a historic garden? a manuscript's text vs its pages? — argue the sort aloud.

## The keeping plan
For one item of each kind in your locality, write its keeping plan: protect / practise / preserve — who must do what, this year, for it to survive.
