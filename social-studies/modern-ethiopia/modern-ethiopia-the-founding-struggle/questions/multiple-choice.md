# Modern Ethiopia — the founding struggle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Zemene Mesafint ran —
a) 1769–1855  b) 1855–1889  c) 1500–1769  d) 1896–1935
**Answer: a) 1769–1855** — The Era of the Princes, 1769–1855.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** During the Zemene Mesafint, Ethiopia's central government was —
a) At its strongest  b) Weakened by internal war  c) Ruled from Egypt  d) A republic
**Answer: b) Weakened by internal war** — Weakened by internal war among princes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Era of the Princes ended with —
a) The Italian invasion  b) Tewodros's rise  c) The founding of Addis Ababa  d) The OAU
**Answer: b) Tewodros's rise** — Tewodros rose and set out to rebuild a central government.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tewodros set out to —
a) Divide Ethiopia into kingdoms  b) Rebuild a central government and bring modern change  c) Make peace with Egypt forever  d) Abolish the crown
**Answer: b) Rebuild a central government and bring modern change** — Rebuild a central government and bring modern change.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these did Ethiopia later defend itself against in this chapter's story?
a) Only Italy  b) Egypt, the Mahdists, Italy and Somalia  c) Only Egypt  d) France and Britain
**Answer: b) Egypt, the Mahdists, Italy and Somalia** — Egypt, the Mahdists, Italy and Somalia are the chapter's named enemies.
