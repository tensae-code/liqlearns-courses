# Modern Ethiopia — the founding struggle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What weakened Ethiopia's central government in the Zemene Mesafint?
**Answer:** Internal war among rival princes and lords.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things this chapter follows after the founding struggles.
**Answer:** Any two of: the caravan trade; the defence against Egypt, the Mahdists, Italy and Somalia; the post-Adwa changes; the Fascist occupation and liberation.
