# Hands-on — Modern Ethiopia — the founding struggle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The hinge year
Draw a line on paper. Write 1769 at one end and today at the other. Mark 1855 near the start and say aloud what changed there.

## Pieces and one whole
Tear a paper into pieces and lay them apart — that is the Era of the Princes. Slide the pieces together into one sheet's shape — that is what Tewodros set out to do.
