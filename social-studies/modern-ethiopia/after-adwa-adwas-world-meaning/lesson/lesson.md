# After Adwa — Adwa's world meaning

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Adwa shook Italy (Crispi fell), filled Europe's front pages, and raised anti-colonial consciousness across Africa and the Black world — Ethiopianism among its fruits. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Shock in Italy, pride at home

Adwa drew the **world press** to Ethiopia — European papers put **Menilek's and Taytu's pictures on their front pages** — and foreign travellers and merchants followed. At home it became the **symbol of national pride**, fixing later generations' resolve to defend the country's freedom. In Italy the defeat brought shock and crisis: **crowds demonstrated** demanding Baratieri be court-martialled, the prisoners freed and Italy leave Africa; Prime Minister **Francesco Crispi was forced from office** and Baratieri resigned and was imprisoned.

## Sub lesson 2 — A spark for the colonised world

Across Africa and the world Adwa **raised anti-colonial consciousness among Black peoples** — a source of pride for **Black Americans** and for **Black South Africans** living under white supremacy. Ethiopia's preserved freedom headed the agenda of the **first Pan-African conferences**, and the victory helped start Black religious movements breaking from white churches, among them **Ethiopianism** — movements religious in form but **fighting white supremacy in substance**.
