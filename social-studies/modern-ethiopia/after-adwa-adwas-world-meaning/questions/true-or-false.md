# After Adwa — Adwa's world meaning — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Baratieri resigned and was imprisoned after Adwa.
**Answer: True** — The book records both.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Adwa weakened anti-colonial consciousness in Africa.
**Answer: False** — It raised it — among Black peoples across Africa and the world.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At home Adwa became the symbol of national pride.
**Answer: True** — Fixing later generations' resolve to defend freedom.
