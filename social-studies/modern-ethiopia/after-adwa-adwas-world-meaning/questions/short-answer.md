# After Adwa — Adwa's world meaning — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happened in Italy after the defeat?
**Answer:** Shock and crisis: demonstrations demanding Baratieri's court-martial and Italy's exit from Africa; Crispi forced from office; Baratieri resigned and was imprisoned.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did Adwa matter to Black South Africans and Black Americans?
**Answer:** A Black African state had beaten a European power and kept its freedom — a source of pride and proof against white supremacy.
