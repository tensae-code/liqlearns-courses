# After Adwa — Adwa's world meaning — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** European papers put on their front pages the pictures of —
a) Baratieri and Crispi  b) Menilek and Taytu  c) Hewett and Portal  d) Awalom
**Answer: b) Menilek and Taytu** — Menilek and Taytu.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy, Prime Minister Crispi —
a) Was re-elected  b) Was forced from office  c) Declared war again at once  d) Visited Addis Ababa
**Answer: b) Was forced from office** — Forced from office.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Italian crowds demanded —
a) More colonies  b) Baratieri be court-martialled, prisoners freed, Italy leave Africa  c) A new king  d) Free trade
**Answer: b) Baratieri be court-martialled, prisoners freed, Italy leave Africa** — Court-martial, prisoners freed, Italy out of Africa.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopianism was —
a) An Italian party  b) A Black religious movement breaking from white churches, fighting white supremacy in substance  c) A trade union  d) A newspaper
**Answer: b) A Black religious movement breaking from white churches, fighting white supremacy in substance** — Religious in form, anti-supremacy in substance.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's preserved freedom headed the agenda of —
a) The League of Nations  b) The first Pan-African conferences  c) The OAU  d) The Berlin conference
**Answer: b) The first Pan-African conferences** — The first Pan-African conferences.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which was NOT an outcome of Adwa?
a) Pan-Africanism weakening  b) National pride at home  c) Crispi's fall  d) Anti-colonial consciousness rising
**Answer: a) Pan-Africanism weakening** — Pan-Africanism was strengthened, not weakened — the chapter's own 'not' question.
