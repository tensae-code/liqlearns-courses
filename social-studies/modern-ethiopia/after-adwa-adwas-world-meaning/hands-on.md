# Hands-on — After Adwa — Adwa's world meaning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Front page
Design a newspaper front page for March 1896: Menilek and Taytu's pictures, one headline. That is what Europe woke to.

## Three echoes
Write the three echoes on three cards — Italy's crisis, national pride, world anti-colonial pride. Arrange them near-to-far and retell.
