# Video script — The student movement — the empire's children turn critics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the university: lecture halls filling with the empire's best-educated generation.
Notebooks close. A journal is printed in secret; a slogan is chalked; a crowd of students fills the street, books still under their arms.
One voice rises over the crowd — Tilahun Gizaw. The camera holds on the listeners: these are the empire's own children.
The words travel outward from the campus gates — land, reform, revolution — toward a year the calendar already holds: 1974.
