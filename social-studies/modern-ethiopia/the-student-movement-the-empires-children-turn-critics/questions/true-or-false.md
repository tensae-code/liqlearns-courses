# The student movement — the empire's children turn critics — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The students were educated outside the regime's school system.
**Answer: False** — they were the empire's own schools' product — the lesson's irony.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** A speech by a participant is treated in this lesson as evidence.
**Answer: True** — a movement is its arguments.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** The students compared the regime's promises with its record in a way no village could publish.
**Answer: True** — literate, organised, and placed to publish.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Tilahun Gizaw was a leader of the student movement.
**Answer: True** — the speech the book builds the lesson around is his.
