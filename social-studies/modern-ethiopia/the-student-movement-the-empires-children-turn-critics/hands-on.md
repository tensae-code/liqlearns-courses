# Hands-on — The student movement — the empire's children turn critics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read the ledger as a student
Take the imperial ledger lesson's six sectors. As a student of 1970, write a four-line leaflet choosing the sector that most condemns the regime — and defend your choice to the class.

## Evidence, not summary
List three things a participant's speech can teach that a textbook summary cannot. Then say what a speech cannot prove, and what you would check beside it.
