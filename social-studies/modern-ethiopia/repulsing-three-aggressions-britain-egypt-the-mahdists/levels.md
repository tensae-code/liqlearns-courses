# Repulsing three aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Napier — the expedition Tewodros's modernisation provoked
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Explain that the British expedition under Sir Robert Napier arose from Tewodros's modernisation drive and his detention of envoys, and ended with Tewodros's death.

## Sub lesson 2 — Egypt at Gundat and Gura — and the Hewett Treaty
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tell Egypt's attempt to dominate the Nile basin and Ethiopia, repulsed decisively at Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876); name the Hewett Treaty and its place after the victories.

## Sub lesson 3 — Italy creeps north — and the Mahdists take an emperor
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tell the Italian encroachment in the north, repulsed through the 1880s; and the Mahdist invasion, which consumed Emperor Yohannes IV's life in 1889; and state the lesson's theme: unity in defence of sovereignty.
