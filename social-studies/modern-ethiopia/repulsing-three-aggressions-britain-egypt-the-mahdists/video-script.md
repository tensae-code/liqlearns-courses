# Video script — Repulsing three aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Ethiopia, ringed by hungry neighbours.

From the sea, a red column climbs the mountain roads — Napier's engineers, bridge by bridge, to Maqdala. A emperor falls. The column marches home.

Now the north-east flares: Egypt reaches for the Nile. Gundat! Gura! The invasions break and roll back.

Pens follow swords: the Hewett Treaty signs the north quiet — for a while.

West now: the Mahdist banners. At the frontier an emperor gives his life for the line — 1889.

Three storms in thirty years. One country still standing. Unity is its wall.
