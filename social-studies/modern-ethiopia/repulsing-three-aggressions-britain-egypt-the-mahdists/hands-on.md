# Hands-on — Repulsing three aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sequence strip
Make a strip 1868–1889 with the three aggressions placed: Napier, Gundat/Gura, Metemma. Under each, one line: who came, who answered, what it cost.

## Template hunt
Write the template the lesson names — invasion, decisive repulse, treaty — and check it against Gundat/Gura and Hewett. Where does the Napier story break the template? (Britain withdrew without colonising.)
