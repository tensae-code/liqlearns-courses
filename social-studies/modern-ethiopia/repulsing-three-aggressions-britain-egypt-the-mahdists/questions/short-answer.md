# Repulsing three aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the sequence the lesson teaches for the Egyptian phase.
**Answer:** Egypt invaded, seeking dominion over the Nile basin and Ethiopia; it was repulsed decisively at Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876); the Hewett Treaty and its results followed.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How did each aggression test a different emperor, and what single theme joins them?
**Answer:** Napier's expedition ended Tewodros's reign and life; Egypt's invasions were broken under Yohannes IV, who later fell against the Mahdists in 1889. The joining theme is unity in defence of sovereignty.
