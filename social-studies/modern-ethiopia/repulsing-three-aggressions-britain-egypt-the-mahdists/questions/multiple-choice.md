# Repulsing three aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Napier's expedition arose from —
a) A trade dispute over coffee  b) Tewodros's modernisation drive and his detention of envoys  c) Egyptian invitation  d) The Berlin Conference
**Answer: b) Tewodros's modernisation drive and his detention of envoys** — His modernisation demands and detention of envoys provoked it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Napier expedition ended with —
a) British colonisation of Ethiopia  b) Tewodros's death and the expedition's withdrawal  c) Yohannes's coronation  d) The Hewett Treaty
**Answer: b) Tewodros's death and the expedition's withdrawal** — Tewodros died; Britain withdrew without colonising.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt's invasions were repulsed decisively at —
a) Adwa and Maqdala  b) Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876)  c) Dogali and Sahati  d) Metemma and Gallabat
**Answer: b) Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876)** — Gundat 1875 and Gura 1876.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt sought dominion over —
a) The Nile basin and Ethiopia  b) The Red Sea islands only  c) Shewa's trade  d) Lake Tana's fisheries
**Answer: a) The Nile basin and Ethiopia** — Egypt attempted to dominate the Nile basin and the country.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The treaty that followed this phase in the north was —
a) Wuchale  b) The Hewett Treaty  c) Frankfurt  d) Versailles
**Answer: b) The Hewett Treaty** — The Hewett Treaty and its results follow the victories.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Yohannes IV lost his life in 1889 against —
a) The Egyptians  b) The Italians  c) The Mahdist invasion  d) The British
**Answer: c) The Mahdist invasion** — The Mahdist invasion consumed his life in 1889.
