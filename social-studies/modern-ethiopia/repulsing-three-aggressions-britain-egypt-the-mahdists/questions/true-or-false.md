# Repulsing three aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain annexed Ethiopia after Napier's expedition.
**Answer: False** — The expedition withdrew; it had come over the detained envoys, not for a colony.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt won at Gundat and Gura.
**Answer: False** — Egypt was repulsed decisively at both, in 1875 and 1876.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Italian encroachment in the north was repulsed through the 1880s.
**Answer: True** — The book records it repulsed through the decade.
