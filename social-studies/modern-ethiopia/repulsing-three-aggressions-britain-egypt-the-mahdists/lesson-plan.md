# Lesson plan — Repulsing three aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Repulsing three aggressions — Britain, Egypt, the Mahdists
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Social, Economic and Political Developments in Ethiopia, Mid-19th C. to 1941 (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's survival was tested in sequence: **Napier's British expedition**, which ended with Tewodros's death; **Egypt's bid for the Nile**, repulsed at **Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876)**; the **Hewett Treaty**; Italian encroachment repulsed through the 1880s; and the **Mahdist invasion** that cost **Yohannes IV his life in 1889** — unity in defence of sovereignty is the theme.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands inside Unit 3's external-aggressions lesson, between the state-makers and Adwa. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Napier — the expedition Tewodros's modernisation provoked** — Explain that the British expedition under Sir Robert Napier arose from Tewodros's modernisation drive and his detention of envoys, and ended with Tewodros's death.
2. **Egypt at Gundat and Gura — and the Hewett Treaty** — Tell Egypt's attempt to dominate the Nile basin and Ethiopia, repulsed decisively at Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876); name the Hewett Treaty and its place after the victories.
3. **Italy creeps north — and the Mahdists take an emperor** — Tell the Italian encroachment in the north, repulsed through the 1880s; and the Mahdist invasion, which consumed Emperor Yohannes IV's life in 1889; and state the lesson's theme: unity in defence of sovereignty.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain Napier's expedition: its cause and its end.
2. Give the Gundat and Gura victories, their dates, and the Hewett Treaty's place.
3. Tell how Yohannes IV died, and state the lesson's theme.
