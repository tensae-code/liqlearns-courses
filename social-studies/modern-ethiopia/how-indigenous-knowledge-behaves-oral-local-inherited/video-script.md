# Video script — How indigenous knowledge behaves — oral, local, inherited

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three symbols appear over a village scene: an ear and a mouth — oral; a map pin — local; a seed passing hand to hand — inherited.
A printed manual lands on the same scene, its pages showing another continent's rains. It fails the three symbols one by one; they dim.
The elder's lesson resumes by the fire. The three symbols brighten again. The marks hold.
