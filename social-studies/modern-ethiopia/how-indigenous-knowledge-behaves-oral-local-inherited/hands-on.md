# Hands-on — How indigenous knowledge behaves — oral, local, inherited

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mark the knowledge
Collect five knowledge items from home — a recipe, a planting rule, a proverb, a remedy, a song. Score each against the three marks and present the table.

## The broken chain
Tell a three-step instruction (a knot, a recipe step) down a line of five learners, whispered once each. Compare the end with the start — and discuss why indigenous transmission pairs voice with supervised practice.
