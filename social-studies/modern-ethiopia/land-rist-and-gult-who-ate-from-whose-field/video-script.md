# Video script — Land, rist and gult — who ate from whose field

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens over farmland — teff fields, coffee groves, and herds moving like slow rivers. Africa's largest herd; coffee going out to the world.
The camera settles on one field. In the soil, a word is written: rist — the lineage's own claim.
From the palace a seal descends on the field: gult. Arrows of grain begin to rise from the field toward the holder of the right.
Year by year the arrows grow heavier in the drawing. In the distance, smoke — the first peasant revolts are already on the horizon.
