# Hands-on — Land, rist and gult — who ate from whose field

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One field, two claims
Draw one field. In the soil write the rist claim and its holders; above it draw the gult right descending from the throne, with the gibir arrows rising. Label every arrow.

## The tribute sack
Act the delivery: a farming family divides its harvest into sacks — seed, food, tribute. Say aloud what decides the tribute sack's size, and what happens in a bad year.
