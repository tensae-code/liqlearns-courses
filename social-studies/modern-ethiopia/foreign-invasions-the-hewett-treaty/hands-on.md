# Hands-on — Foreign invasions — the Hewett Treaty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three article cards
Write the three articles on three cards. Lay them in order, then turn a fourth card face up: 'the cost — the Mahdists' enmity'.

## The evacuation road
On a map, draw the road the besieged Egyptian troops would travel: border garrisons → through Ethiopia → Mitsiwa → Egypt.
