# Foreign invasions — the Hewett Treaty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why could Britain and Egypt not lift the sieges themselves?
**Answer:** The Mahdist rising had shattered Egypt's army, and Britain, holding Egypt, needed Ethiopia's army to reach and relieve the besieged garrisons.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the treaty cost Ethiopia?
**Answer:** It made Ethiopia the Mahdists' enemy — attacks on the north-west followed — and it opened the door for Italy to take Mitsiwa.
