# Foreign invasions — the Hewett Treaty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain held Egypt from —
a) 1876  b) 1882  c) 1884  d) 1889
**Answer: b) 1882** — From 1882.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mahdist rising had —
a) Taken Cairo  b) Shattered Egypt's army and besieged its border garrisons  c) Allied with Yohannes  d) Bought Mitsiwa
**Answer: b) Shattered Egypt's army and besieged its border garrisons** — It besieged Egypt's garrisons on the Ethio-Sudan border.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The treaty was signed for Britain by —
a) Napier  b) Rear-Admiral Sir William Hewett  c) Portal  d) Wingate
**Answer: b) Rear-Admiral Sir William Hewett** — Rear-Admiral Sir William Hewett.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Article 1 gave Ethiopia —
a) Ownership of Mitsiwa  b) Free use of Mitsiwa port  c) A railway  d) Egyptian arms
**Answer: b) Free use of Mitsiwa port** — Free use of Mitsiwa port.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Article 3 promised Ethiopia would —
a) Attack Sudan  b) Evacuate the besieged Egyptian troops through her territory  c) Pay indemnity  d) Cede Bogos
**Answer: b) Evacuate the besieged Egyptian troops through her territory** — Evacuate the besieged troops to Mitsiwa and on to Egypt.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Ras Alula fought the Mahdists under the treaty at —
a) Gura  b) Kufit, 1885  c) Dogali  d) Metema
**Answer: b) Kufit, 1885** — Kufit in 1885.
