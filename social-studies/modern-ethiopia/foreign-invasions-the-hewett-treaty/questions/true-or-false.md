# Foreign invasions — the Hewett Treaty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The treaty is also called the Adwa Treaty.
**Answer: True** — Hewett (Adwa) Treaty, 1884.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The treaty returned the occupied territories under Article 2.
**Answer: True** — Article 2: return of the occupied territories.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The treaty kept Italy out of Mitsiwa for good.
**Answer: False** — It opened the door for Italy to take Mitsiwa.
