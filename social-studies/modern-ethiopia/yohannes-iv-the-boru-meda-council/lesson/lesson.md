# Yohannes IV — the Boru Meda council

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: At Boru Meda in May 1878 Yohannes proclaimed Orthodox Tewahedo the only faith — flexible in politics, he was unyielding on religion. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## The story

Flexible in politics, **Yohannes IV** was unyielding on religion. At the **Boru Meda council**, which he called in **May 1878**, the different faiths were invited to **settle their differences by discussion** — after which **Orthodox Tewahedo was proclaimed the only faith**, and Muslims and other believers were ordered to accept it. The forced conversion fell mainly on **Wollo**, because he believed **religious unity was essential to hold the country together**. Those who refused were acted against, and **Wollo Muslims, stripped of property, migrated to Jimma, Bale, Gurage** and elsewhere.
