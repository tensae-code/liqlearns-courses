# Hands-on — Yohannes IV — the Boru Meda council

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A council on the plain
Set chairs in a circle outdoors or in the room. Each chair carries a faith's name card. Read the proclamation aloud as the council's decision.

## Roads of migration
On a map, draw arrows from Wollo to Jimma, Bale and Gurage — the roads of those who refused and lost their property.
