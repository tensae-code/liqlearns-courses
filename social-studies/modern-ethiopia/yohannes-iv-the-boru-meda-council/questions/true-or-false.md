# Yohannes IV — the Boru Meda council — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Yohannes was flexible in politics but unyielding on religion.
**Answer: True** — The book's own pairing of his traits.

**2.** Everyone at Boru Meda accepted the proclamation willingly.
**Answer: False** — Those who refused were acted against.

**3.** The Boru Meda council met the same month as the Liche agreement.
**Answer: True** — Both May 1878 — Liche on May 20.
