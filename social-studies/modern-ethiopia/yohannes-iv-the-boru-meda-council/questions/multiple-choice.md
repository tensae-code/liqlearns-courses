# Yohannes IV — the Boru Meda council — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Boru Meda council met in —
a) May 1878  b) January 1872  c) March 1889  d) May 1889
**Answer: a) May 1878** — May 1878.

**2.** At the council the different faiths were first invited to —
a) Fight a duel  b) Settle their differences by discussion  c) Leave Ethiopia  d) Pay tribute
**Answer: b) Settle their differences by discussion** — Settle their differences by discussion.

**3.** The council proclaimed —
a) Freedom for all faiths equally  b) Orthodox Tewahedo the only faith  c) Islam the state faith  d) No state faith
**Answer: b) Orthodox Tewahedo the only faith** — Orthodox Tewahedo was proclaimed the only faith.

**4.** The forced conversion fell mainly on —
a) Shewa  b) Wollo  c) Gojjam  d) Harar
**Answer: b) Wollo** — Mainly on Wollo.

**5.** Yohannes acted because he believed —
a) Religious unity was essential to hold the country together  b) Trade needed one faith  c) Egypt demanded it  d) The clergy paid him
**Answer: a) Religious unity was essential to hold the country together** — He believed religious unity was essential to national unity.

**6.** Wollo Muslims stripped of property migrated to —
a) Egypt and Sudan  b) Jimma, Bale, Gurage and elsewhere  c) Italy  d) Somalia
**Answer: b) Jimma, Bale, Gurage and elsewhere** — Jimma, Bale, Gurage and elsewhere.
