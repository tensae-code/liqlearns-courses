# Yohannes IV — the Boru Meda council — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What happened to believers who refused the Boru Meda proclamation?
**Answer:** They were acted against; Wollo Muslims were stripped of property and many migrated to Jimma, Bale, Gurage and elsewhere.

**2.** How does Boru Meda sit beside the Liche agreement in Yohannes's character?
**Answer:** Liche shows him flexible in politics — settling with Menilek by bargain; Boru Meda shows him unyielding on religion, imposing one faith for unity.
