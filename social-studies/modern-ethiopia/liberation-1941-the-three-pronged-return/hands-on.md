# Hands-on — Liberation 1941 — the three-pronged return

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three arrows return
Reuse your invasion map. Draw the three 1941 arrows in a new colour — Keren, Gojjam, Harar — all converging on Addis Ababa.

## Five years counted
Count five years on your fingers, 1936–1941, naming one Patriot fact per year from the two Patriots lessons.
