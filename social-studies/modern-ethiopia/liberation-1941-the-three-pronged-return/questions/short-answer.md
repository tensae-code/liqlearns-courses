# Liberation 1941 — the three-pronged return — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Britain bring the emperor to Khartoum?
**Answer:** It saw his key role in coordinating the Patriots inside and outside Ethiopia once the war went world-wide in 1940.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three prongs of the 1941 attack.
**Answer:** Platt breaking Keren in Eritrea; the Gideon Force entering with the emperor through Gojjam, clearing Gondar and Gojjam; Cunningham from Kenya through Italian Somaliland toward Harar.
