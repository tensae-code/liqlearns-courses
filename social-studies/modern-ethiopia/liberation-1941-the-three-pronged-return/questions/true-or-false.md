# Liberation 1941 — the three-pronged return — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Patriots were called to the Sudan border for arms and equipment.
**Answer: True** — The book records the call.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cunningham's force came from Kenya and advanced on Harar.
**Answer: True** — It quickly took Italian Somaliland and forced surrender at Harar.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The occupation lasted ten years.
**Answer: False** — Five years of struggle, 1936–1941, ended on May 5, 1941.
