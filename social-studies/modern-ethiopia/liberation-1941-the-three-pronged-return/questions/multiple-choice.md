# Liberation 1941 — the three-pronged return — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In 1940 Mussolini joined —
a) The Allies  b) Nazi Germany against the Allies  c) The League  d) Ethiopia
**Answer: b) Nazi Germany against the Allies** — Nazi Germany, hoping to share the victory and keep his conquests.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain brought Haile Selassie to —
a) Khartoum, Sudan  b) Cairo  c) Nairobi  d) Djibouti
**Answer: a) Khartoum, Sudan** — Khartoum, on June 25, 1940, as printed.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain needed the emperor because of his key role in —
a) Trade  b) Coordinating the Patriots inside and outside  c) Training pilots  d) Printing money
**Answer: b) Coordinating the Patriots inside and outside** — Coordinating the Patriots.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Keren position was broken by General Platt's force after —
a) 53 days of fighting  b) Three days  c) Thirteen hours  d) Six days
**Answer: a) 53 days of fighting** — 53 days of fighting.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The force that entered with the emperor through Gojjam was the —
a) Black Lion  b) Gideon Force  c) Desert Rats  d) Ansar
**Answer: b) Gideon Force** — The Gideon Force, under Sandford and Wingate.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Haile Selassie re-entered Addis Ababa on —
a) May 5, 1941  b) April 1936  c) March 31, 1936  d) June 25, 1940
**Answer: a) May 5, 1941** — May 5, 1941.
