# Liberation 1941 — the three-pronged return

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: In 1941 Allied forces and the Patriots struck from three directions — Keren, Gojjam with the Gideon Force, and Harar — and on May 5, 1941 the emperor re-entered Addis Ababa. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — The war goes world-wide

The Patriots' decisive phase came when the war went world-wide: in **1940 Mussolini joined Nazi Germany** against the Allies (Britain and France). Britain, seeing the emperor's key role in coordinating the Patriots, brought **Haile Selassie to Khartoum, Sudan, on June 25, 1940 (as printed)**, and Ethiopian patriots were called to the Sudan border for arms and equipment.

## Sub lesson 2 — Three prongs; May 5, 1941

The Allied attack came from **three directions**: **General Platt's** force broke Italy's strong **Keren** position in Eritrea after **53 days** of fighting; a second force entered **with the emperor through Gojjam** under **Colonel Sandford and Major Charles Wingate** — the **'Gideon Force'** — which with the Patriots cleared the Fascists from **Gondar and Gojjam**; and **General Cunningham's** force from Kenya quickly took Italian Somaliland and advanced on **Harar**, forcing surrender. Cunningham's force and the Patriots drove the Fascists from **Addis Ababa in April 1941**, and on **May 5, 1941** Haile Selassie **re-entered his capital**: after five years of struggle Ethiopia had expelled the invader.
