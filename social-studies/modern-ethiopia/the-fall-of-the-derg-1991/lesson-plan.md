# Lesson plan — The fall of the Derg, 1991

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** The fall of the Derg, 1991
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Say how the Eritrea and Tigray wars drained the regime.
**This lesson's own level:** the Grade 12 depth of this title — it stands on the title's earlier lessons (the empire to 1941, the restoration) and needs nothing else; a learner may jump straight in, as every name is explained where it first appears.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. History is taught as story: the video script is a story script in the animated-map spirit.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Wars that would not end** — Say how the wars in Eritrea and Tigray drained the regime year after year.
2. **An economy failing, a patron falling** — Name the regime's two collapsing supports: the failing economy and the Soviet bloc's collapse.
3. **May 1991 — Mengistu flees, the EPRDF advances** — Tell May 1991: Mengistu's flight and the EPRDF's advance into Addis Ababa.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
