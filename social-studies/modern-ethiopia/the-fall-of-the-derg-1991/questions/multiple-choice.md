# The fall of the Derg, 1991 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The regime's long wars were fought in —
a) Kafa and Jimma  b) Eritrea and Tigray  c) Harar and the Ogaden only  d) Gondar only
**Answer: b) b) Eritrea and Tigray**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The wars ate —
a) Nothing  b) The budget and generations of young men sent to the fronts  c) Only foreign aid  d) Port revenues
**Answer: b) b) The budget and generations of young men sent to the fronts**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Derg's great foreign patron was —
a) Britain  b) The Soviet Union and its bloc  c) Italy  d) The United States throughout
**Answer: b) b) The Soviet Union and its bloc**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** By the end of the 1980s the Soviet bloc was —
a) Expanding  b) Collapsing, and its aid thinned and stopped  c) Invading Ethiopia  d) Buying Ethiopian land
**Answer: b) b) Collapsing, and its aid thinned and stopped**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In May 1991 Mengistu —
a) Won an election  b) Fled the country  c) Crowned a successor  d) Signed a trade deal
**Answer: b) b) Fled the country**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The forces that entered Addis Ababa were the —
a) Imperial Guard  b) EPRDF  c) A UN army  d) The Eritrean navy
**Answer: b) b) EPRDF**
**7. (Unit 6 review — Grade 12)** Unit 6 review: The Derg took power in —
a) 1960  b) 1974  c) 1987  d) 1991
**Answer: b) 1974 — Revolution, deposition, the Derg.**

**8. (Unit 6 review — Grade 12)** Unit 6 review: The land reform at the head of the Derg's programme came in —
a) 1974  b) 1975  c) 1987  d) 1990
**Answer: b) 1975 — Land to the tiller.**

**9. (Unit 6 review — Grade 12)** Unit 6 review: Mengistu fled and the Derg fell in —
a) 1987  b) 1989  c) May 1991  d) 1995
**Answer: c) May 1991 — The EPRDF advance closed the regime.**
