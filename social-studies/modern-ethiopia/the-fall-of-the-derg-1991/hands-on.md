# Hands-on — The fall of the Derg, 1991

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three supports kicked away
Draw the regime as a stool with three legs — quiet fronts, a working economy, a strong patron. Cross out each leg as the lesson removes it, and say what happens to a stool.

## May 1991 diary
Write a six-line diary entry for a resident of Addis Ababa in late May 1991, using only the lesson's facts.
