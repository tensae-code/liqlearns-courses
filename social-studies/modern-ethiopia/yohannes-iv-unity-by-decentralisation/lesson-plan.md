# Lesson plan — Yohannes IV — unity by decentralisation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Yohannes IV — unity by decentralisation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Social, Economic and Political Developments in Ethiopia, Mid-19th C. to 1941 (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After Tewodros fell, three contenders came to the throne in turn — and **Yohannes IV (Kassa Mircha of Tigray)** held Ethiopia together the opposite way from his predecessor: by a **decentralised administration** that accommodated regional power, and by **ending the religious controversy** inside the Ethiopian Orthodox Church.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands between the Tewodros and Menilek lessons — the second of Unit 3's three state-makers. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Three contenders, in turn** — Name the three contenders after Tewodros: Wagshum Gobeze of Lasta (short, uneventful reign), Kassa Mircha of Tigray, and Menilek of Shewa — coming to the throne in turn.
2. **Rule by accommodating — and a church settled** — Explain Yohannes IV's renown: a decentralised system of administration accommodating regional power rather than crushing it, and terminating the religious controversy within the Ethiopian Orthodox Church.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

2. Explain decentralisation as Yohannes's method of unity.
3. Say what Yohannes settled inside the Orthodox Church.
