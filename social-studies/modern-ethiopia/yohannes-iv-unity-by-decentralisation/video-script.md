# Video script — Yohannes IV — unity by decentralisation


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Ethiopia after Maqdala — the centre empty again.

A Lasta lord takes the throne — briefly, quietly, gone.

Then from Tigray in the north rides Kassa Mircha: Yohannes the Fourth.

Watch his method — he does not smash the regional thrones. He visits them. He confirms them. He binds them with loose, strong cords.

And in the church, the old quarrel of doctrines is called to council — and ended.

One empire, many thrones, one faith settled. Unity, this time, by agreement.
