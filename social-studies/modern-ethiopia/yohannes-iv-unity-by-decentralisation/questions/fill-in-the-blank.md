# Yohannes IV — unity by decentralisation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yohannes IV's birth name was Kassa ____.
**Answer: Mircha**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Yohannes ruled by a ____ system of administration.
**Answer: decentralised**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** He terminated the religious ____ within the Orthodox Church.
**Answer: controversy**
