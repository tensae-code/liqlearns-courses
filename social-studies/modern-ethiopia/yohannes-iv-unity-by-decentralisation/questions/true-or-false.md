# Yohannes IV — unity by decentralisation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Menilek of Shewa took the throne immediately after Tewodros.
**Answer: False** — Gobeze and then Kassa Mircha (Yohannes IV) came in turn; Menilek's turn follows.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Yohannes's decentralisation meant the empire broke into independent states.
**Answer: False** — Regions kept their place inside one empire — decentralised administration, not dissolution.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The religious controversy in the church outlived Yohannes unsettled.
**Answer: False** — He terminated it.
