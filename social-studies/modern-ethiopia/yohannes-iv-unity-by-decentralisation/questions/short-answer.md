# Yohannes IV — unity by decentralisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the three contenders after Tewodros, in the book's order.
**Answer:** Wagshum Gobeze of Lasta; Kassa Mircha of Tigray (Yohannes IV); and Menilek of Shewa — coming to the throne in turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contrast Yohannes's unity with Tewodros's in two sentences.
**Answer:** Tewodros unified by crushing regional power under a centralised, salaried state. Yohannes unified by decentralisation — accommodating regional rulers inside one empire — and by settling the church's controversy, binding the country by agreement rather than force.
