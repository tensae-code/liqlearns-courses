# Yohannes IV — unity by decentralisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Tewodros, the first contender's reign was —
a) Long and glorious  b) Wagshum Gobeze of Lasta's — too short and uneventful  c) Menilek's  d) Iyasu's
**Answer: b) Wagshum Gobeze of Lasta's — too short and uneventful** — Wagshum Gobeze of Lasta reigned briefly and uneventfully.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Yohannes IV was born —
a) Menilek of Shewa  b) Kassa Mircha of Tigray  c) Kassa Hailu of Quara  d) Gobeze of Lasta
**Answer: b) Kassa Mircha of Tigray** — Kassa Mircha of Tigray became Yohannes IV.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The third contender, waiting in Shewa, was —
a) Gobeze  b) Menilek  c) Kenfu  d) Wube
**Answer: b) Menilek** — Menilek of Shewa was the third contender.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Yohannes IV is renowned for attempting —
a) Total centralisation  b) A decentralised system of administration  c) Abolishing the regions  d) A republic
**Answer: b) A decentralised system of administration** — He attempted decentralised administration.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** His method toward regional power was to —
a) Crush it  b) Accommodate it rather than crush it  c) Ignore it  d) Sell it
**Answer: b) Accommodate it rather than crush it** — He ruled by accommodating regional power.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Inside the church, Yohannes —
a) Started a new controversy  b) Terminated the religious controversy in the Orthodox Church  c) Separated all regions' churches  d) Banned councils
**Answer: b) Terminated the religious controversy in the Orthodox Church** — He terminated the religious controversy within the Ethiopian Orthodox Church.
