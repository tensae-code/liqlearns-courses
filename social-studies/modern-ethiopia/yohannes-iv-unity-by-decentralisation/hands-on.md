# Hands-on — Yohannes IV — unity by decentralisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two methods chart
Draw two columns — Tewodros, Yohannes — and one row: 'How are regional rulers handled?' Fill from the two lessons. Then one sentence: which method kept unity longer, and at what price?

## The web
Tie four strings (regions) to one centre knot (the emperor) with loose knots, not tight ones. The looseness is the design: say aloud what holds the web together if not force.
