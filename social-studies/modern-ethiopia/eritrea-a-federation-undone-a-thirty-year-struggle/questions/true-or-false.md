# Eritrea — a federation undone, a thirty-year struggle — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The federation gave Eritrea no self-governing powers at all.
**Answer: False** — it held domestic affairs through its own institutions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The armed struggle outlasted both the empire and the Derg.
**Answer: True** — thirty years, through both regimes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The federation's Eritrean half was absorbed step by step into the imperial administration.
**Answer: True** — until the federation existed mainly on paper.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The federation's Eritrean half was absorbed step by step into the imperial administration.
**Answer: True** — until the federation existed mainly on paper.
