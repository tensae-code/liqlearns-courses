# Video script — Eritrea — a federation undone, a thirty-year struggle

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the Red Sea coast, 1941. Italy's colour drains from Eritrea; Britain's administration settles over it.
New York, 1950: a gavel falls. On the map, Eritrea and Ethiopia are joined by a drawn seam labelled FEDERATION — two flags, one sovereignty.
The seam's threads are cut, one a year, in the drawing: assembly, courts, language. The Eritrean flag lowers; the imperial one flies alone.
In the hills, small fires: the armed struggle begins. The camera pulls back across thirty years of them, to a ballot box — the referendum.
