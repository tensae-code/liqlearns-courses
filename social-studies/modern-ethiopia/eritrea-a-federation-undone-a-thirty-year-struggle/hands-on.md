# Hands-on — Eritrea — a federation undone, a thirty-year struggle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two lists
On one sheet draw the federation as a house with two floors. Label the lower floor with Eritrea's powers and the upper with the federal powers, from the lesson. Then mark, with a second colour, what 'measures contrary to the Act' would have to remove to hollow the house.

## Paper and practice
Write two short paragraphs: 'The federation as written' and 'The federation as practised.' Read them back to back and say which one the armed struggle answered.
