# Tewodros II — Maqdala and his legacy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** By 1868 Tewodros's army was —
a) At its largest  b) Much reduced after repeated campaigns and the 1867 decrees  c) Fully foreign  d) Disbanded by the church
**Answer: b) Much reduced after repeated campaigns and the 1867 decrees** — Much reduced by 1867.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The British expedition of 1868 was led by —
a) General Robert Napier  b) Colonel Arendrup  c) Admiral Hewett  d) General Baratieri
**Answer: a) General Robert Napier** — General Robert Napier.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Kassa Mircha aided the British and was rewarded with —
a) A crown  b) Many arms and experts, including Kirkham  c) Mitsiwa port  d) A title in London
**Answer: b) Many arms and experts, including Kirkham** — Arms and experts, among them the adviser Kirkham.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The great battle below Maqdala was fought —
a) At Derasge, 1855  b) On the Arogi plain, April 10, 1868  c) At Gundet, 1875  d) At Adwa, 1896
**Answer: b) On the Arogi plain, April 10, 1868** — Arogi plain, April 10, 1868.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Tewodros died on —
a) April 13, 1868, by his own hand rather than surrender  b) May 2, 1889  c) March 9, 1889  d) January 1872
**Answer: a) April 13, 1868, by his own hand rather than surrender** — He shot himself on April 13, 1868, refusing unconditional surrender.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The struggle after his death was —
a) A return to the Zemene Mesafint  b) For the King of Kings' title — his dream was inherited  c) Won by Egypt  d) Settled by Italy
**Answer: b) For the King of Kings' title — his dream was inherited** — For the one crown; the dream of unity was inherited and later fulfilled by Menilek II.
