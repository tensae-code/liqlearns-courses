# Tewodros II — Maqdala and his legacy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Kassa Mircha help bring Tewodros's government down?
**Answer:** He guided and aided Napier's British expedition, and was rewarded with arms and experts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why was the succession struggle not a new Zemene Mesafint?
**Answer:** The rivals — Gobezé, Kassa Mircha, Menilek — fought for the single King of Kings title, not to keep Ethiopia divided; the unity dream was inherited.
