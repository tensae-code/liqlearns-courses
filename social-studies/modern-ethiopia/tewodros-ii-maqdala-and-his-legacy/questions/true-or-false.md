# Tewodros II — Maqdala and his legacy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The British looted heavily at Maqdala, including the king's crown.
**Answer: True** — The book names the crown among the loot.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fitawrari Gebriye survived the Arogi battle.
**Answer: False** — His force was mauled and its leader killed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Menilek II later fulfilled Tewodros's dream of a united Ethiopia.
**Answer: True** — The book's own closing verdict on Tewodros.
