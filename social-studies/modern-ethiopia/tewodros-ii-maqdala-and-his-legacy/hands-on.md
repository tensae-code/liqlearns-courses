# Hands-on — Tewodros II — Maqdala and his legacy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Amba and plain
Build Maqdala as a flat-topped hill of books or boxes, with a plain below it. Place a marker on the plain: Arogi, April 10, 1868.

## What outlives a king
Write one sentence Tewodros might have left behind: 'a united Ethiopia'. Pass it hand to hand around your group — inherited, as the book says.
