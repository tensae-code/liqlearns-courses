# Hands-on — 1991 — the Charter and the transitional government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Charter in five lines
Summarise the Charter in five lines: who wrote it, what it governed as, what it founded (regions), on what principle, and what it pointed toward (federation).

## Three architectures
Draw Ethiopia three times — empire (one throne), unitary state (one centre), charter order (14 regions). Label the source of authority in each drawing.
