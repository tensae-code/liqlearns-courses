# Lesson plan — 1991 — the Charter and the transitional government

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** 1991 — the Charter and the transitional government
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the post-1991 unit's opening lesson; Grade 10 Unit 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** after the civil war the EPRDF-led Transitional Government, through an 87-member Council of Representatives, adopted the National Charter as a transitional constitution — founding 14 self-governing regions of nations, nationalities and peoples, and laying the foundation of the federal order.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on 1991 — how the Derg fell. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **London, May 1991 — the transition negotiated** — the US-sponsored London Conference: the Derg government under Tesfaye Dinka, the EPLF under Isaias Afwerki among the parties, preceding the transition.
2. **The Charter — 87 councillors, 14 regions, a federal seed** — the 87-member Council of Representatives drafts the National Charter as transitional constitution, establishing 14 self-governing regions and laying federalism's foundation.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: 1991 — how the Derg fell. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the coalition that dominated the TGE and its senior partner.
2. Tell the London Conference of May 1991 and who sat in it.
3. Give the Charter's machinery: 87-member Council, 14 regions, the federal foundation.
