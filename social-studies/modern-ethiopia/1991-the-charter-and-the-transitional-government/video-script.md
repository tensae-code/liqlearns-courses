# Video script — 1991 — the Charter and the transitional government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on London, May 1991: flags around a table — a falling government, a victorious front — and an American chair.
Addis Ababa, July: a conference hall fills with the country's nations and movements. Eighty-seven councillors take their seats.
A charter is raised. On the map behind the hall, Ethiopia divides itself — not by provinces of a throne, but into 14 self-governing regions.
The camera rests on the new map's caption: the foundation of a federation.
