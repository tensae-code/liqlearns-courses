# Video script — Mada'a and Dagu — Afar law and the human news network

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the Afar lowlands: wells as bright points in a vast dry country, caravan lines between them.
At one well, two elders from far clans take their seats between two disputing parties. The word Mada'a appears; a transfer of goods is drawn; hands are shaken.
At another well, two travellers meet. A point of light — news — passes between them, then splits at the next wells, rippling outward person to person.
The whole country glows softly with exchanged news. No wires. No offices. The network is the people themselves.
