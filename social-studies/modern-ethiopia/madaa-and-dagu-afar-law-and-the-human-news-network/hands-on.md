# Hands-on — Mada'a and Dagu — Afar law and the human news network

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Judge from outside
Stage a small dispute (a borrowed goat, a damaged fence). Choose judges by the Mada'a rule — from 'clans' (groups) not involved — and let them settle it by the three values. Compare the settlement with a winner-takes-all verdict.

## Run a Dagu relay
Send one true, checkable piece of news ('the library opens at nine tomorrow') person to person across the class by the Dagu rule: each meeting must ask, hear and carry on. Count the meetings until everyone knows; check the news arrived unchanged.
