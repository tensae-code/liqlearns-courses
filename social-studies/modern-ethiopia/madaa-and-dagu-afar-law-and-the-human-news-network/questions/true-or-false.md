# Mada'a and Dagu — Afar law and the human news network — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Mada'a aims to produce a winner and a humiliated loser.
**Answer: False** — it aims at a settlement — compensation and forgiveness.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Dagu needs telephones to work.
**Answer: False** — it is a person-to-person custom — greeting, asking, telling.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Dagu's protocol is greeting, asking and telling, run by custom.
**Answer: True** — no office, no fee, no editor.
