# Tewodros II — the Gafat foundry — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Tewodros asked Britain repeatedly for skilled help before turning to Gafat.
**Answer: True** — He requested it above all from Britain; it was not supplied.

**2.** Sebastopol was bought from a European factory.
**Answer: False** — It was cast at Gafat at home.

**3.** Ethiopian craftsmen worked at the foundry alongside the missionaries.
**Answer: True** — The book pairs missionaries with Ethiopian craftsmen.
