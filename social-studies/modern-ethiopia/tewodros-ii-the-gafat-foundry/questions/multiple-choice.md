# Tewodros II — the Gafat foundry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Europe, above all Britain, would not supply Tewodros with —
a) Grain  b) Skilled help for making arms  c) Horses  d) Books
**Answer: b) Skilled help for making arms** — The skilled help he repeatedly requested.

**2.** The foundry was at —
a) Gafat, near Debre Tabor  b) Harar  c) Aksum  d) Addis Ababa
**Answer: a) Gafat, near Debre Tabor** — Gafat near Debre Tabor.

**3.** Who did the casting work?
a) Only foreign soldiers  b) European missionaries with Ethiopian craftsmen  c) Egyptian engineers  d) Portuguese sailors
**Answer: b) European missionaries with Ethiopian craftsmen** — Missionaries in the country, with Ethiopian craftsmen.

**4.** The foundry's famous product was —
a) A printing press  b) The great mortar named Sebastopol  c) A steam engine  d) A cannon called Arogi
**Answer: b) The great mortar named Sebastopol** — The great mortar named Sebastopol.

**5.** Gafat cast —
a) Church bells only  b) Guns and cannon  c) Coins  d) Ploughs only
**Answer: b) Guns and cannon** — Guns and cannon.
