# Tewodros II — the Gafat foundry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why did Tewodros build the Gafat foundry?
**Answer:** Europe would not supply the skilled help he asked for, so he made guns and cannon at home with the missionaries and Ethiopian craftsmen in the country.

**2.** What does Gafat show about Tewodros's modernising drive?
**Answer:** He would not wait for Europe: refused help became home production — the same drive as his standing army and unity campaigns.
