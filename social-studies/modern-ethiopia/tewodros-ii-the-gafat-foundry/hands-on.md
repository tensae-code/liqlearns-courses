# Hands-on — Tewodros II — the Gafat foundry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make it at home
List three things your home or village once bought from far away and now makes itself. Say what changed — as Gafat changed arms-making.

## The foundry in clay
Model a mortar's wide mouth and short barrel in clay or mud. Label it Sebastopol and say where the real one was cast.
