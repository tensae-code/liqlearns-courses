# Tewodros II — the Gafat foundry

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: When Europe would not send skilled help, Tewodros set missionaries and Ethiopian craftsmen to casting guns at Gafat — among them the great mortar Sebastopol. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## The story

Europe, above all Britain, would not supply the **skilled help** Tewodros repeatedly requested. So he gathered the **European missionaries** in the country and set them, **with Ethiopian craftsmen**, to casting **guns and cannon** at his foundry at **Gafat near Debre Tabor** — among their products the great mortar named **Sebastopol**. Gafat is the standing-army drive turned into iron: what Europe would not give, Ethiopia would make.
