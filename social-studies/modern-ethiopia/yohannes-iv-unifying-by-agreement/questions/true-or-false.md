# Yohannes IV — unifying by agreement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yohannes continued Tewodros's centralising work.
**Answer: True** — Campaigns in Gondar, Gojjam, Yejju and Hamasen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Liche agreement was signed after a great battle between Yohannes and Menilek.
**Answer: False** — Their difference was settled by agreement, May 20, 1878.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Under Liche, both sides took a common stand against common enemies.
**Answer: True** — Part of the agreement's terms.
