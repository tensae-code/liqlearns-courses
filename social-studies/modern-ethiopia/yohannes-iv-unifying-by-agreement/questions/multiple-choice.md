# Yohannes IV — unifying by agreement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before Yohannes, Wagshum Gobezé reigned as —
a) Tekle Giyorgis, 1868–1871  b) Tewodros II  c) Ras Ali  d) Menilek II
**Answer: a) Tekle Giyorgis, 1868–1871** — Tekle Giyorgis, 1868–1871.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kassa Mircha defeated Tekle Giyorgis at —
a) Ayshal  b) Assem, July 1871  c) Derasge  d) Liche
**Answer: b) Assem, July 1871** — Assem, July 1871.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Yohannes IV was crowned in —
a) January 1872  b) May 1878  c) January 1889  d) July 1871
**Answer: a) January 1872** — January 1872.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the Liche agreement Menilek would —
a) Keep the King of Kings title  b) Pay tribute and drop the King of Kings title for King of Shewa  c) Rule Tigray  d) Cede Shewa
**Answer: b) Pay tribute and drop the King of Kings title for King of Shewa** — Tribute, the lesser title, and no separate foreign agreements.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In return Menilek received —
a) Gojjam  b) Wollo to administer besides Shewa  c) Mitsiwa  d) The crown
**Answer: b) Wollo to administer besides Shewa** — Wollo to administer besides Shewa.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In 1881 Yohannes crowned Tekle Haymanot king of —
a) Shewa  b) Gojjam and Kefa  c) Wollo  d) Harar
**Answer: b) Gojjam and Kefa** — King of Gojjam and Kefa.
