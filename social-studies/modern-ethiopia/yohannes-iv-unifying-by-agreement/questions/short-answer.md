# Yohannes IV — unifying by agreement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Yohannes deal with Gojjam's Tekle Haymanot?
**Answer:** He made peace with him and in 1881 crowned him king of Gojjam and Kefa.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Yohannes gain from the Liche agreement?
**Answer:** Menilek's tribute, his dropping of the King of Kings title and of separate foreign dealings, and a common stand against common enemies.
