# Yohannes IV — unifying by agreement

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Kassa Mircha, crowned Yohannes IV in January 1872, continued the centralising work — and settled with Menilek by the Liche agreement of May 20, 1878. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — From Assem to the crown

After Maqdala, **Wagshum Gobezé** reigned as **Tekle Giyorgis (1868–1871)** until **Kassa Mircha defeated and captured him at Assem in July 1871**; Kassa was crowned **Yohannes IV in January 1872**. He continued the centralising work — campaigning in **Gondar, Gojjam, Yejju and Hamasen**, making peace with Gojjam's **Tekle Haymanot** and in **1881 crowning him king of Gojjam and Kefa**.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Liche agreement with Menilek

Shewa's **Menilek**, who styled himself King of Kings, paid no tribute and dealt separately with foreign powers, expanding into Wollo. The difference was settled **without a deciding war**, by the **Liche agreement of May 20, 1878**: Menilek would **pay tribute, drop the King of Kings title for King of Shewa, and end separate foreign agreements** — and in return received **Wollo to administer besides Shewa**, both sides taking a common stand against common enemies.
