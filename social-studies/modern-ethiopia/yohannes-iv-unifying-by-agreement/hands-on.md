# Hands-on — Yohannes IV — unifying by agreement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two columns of a bargain
Write Liche as two columns: 'Menilek gives' and 'Menilek receives'. Fill each from the lesson and read the bargain aloud.

## Crown the ally
Make a paper crown for Tekle Haymanot, king of Gojjam and Kefa, 1881 — Yohannes binding a rival by honouring him.
