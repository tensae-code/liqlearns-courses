# Menilek and Italy — Wuchale and Article 17

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: At Wuchale, May 2, 1889, one word split two texts: Amharic said Ethiopia may use Italy's help; Italian said it must — a protectorate trap in Article 17. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Article 3 — the boundary

The friendship peaked, and ended, with **Wuchale, signed May 2, 1889 at Wuchale in Wollo**: twenty articles, the fateful ones the third and the seventeenth. **Article 3** drew the boundary: Italy was recognised as holding **most of the lowlands and some highlands across the Mereb** — and, colonial appetite unsatisfied, she kept crossing it inward.

## Sub lesson 2 — Article 17 — may against must

**Article 17** governed Ethiopia's foreign relations, and Italy **twisted the Italian text**, launching a diplomatic campaign that Ethiopia was an **Italian protectorate**. The **Amharic text** said Ethiopia **may** use Italy's help in dealings with other European countries; the **Italian text** said Ethiopia **must** conduct all foreign relations **only through Italy** — an obligatory wording that **struck at Ethiopia's sovereignty**. One word — may against must — carried a country's freedom.
