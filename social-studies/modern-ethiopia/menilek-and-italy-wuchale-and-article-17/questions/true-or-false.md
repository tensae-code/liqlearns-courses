# Menilek and Italy — Wuchale and Article 17 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy kept crossing the Article 3 boundary inward after Wuchale.
**Answer: True** — Colonial appetite unsatisfied, she kept crossing it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Both language texts of Article 17 said the same thing.
**Answer: False** — May in Amharic, must in Italian — the trap of the treaty.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The obligatory wording struck at Ethiopia's sovereignty.
**Answer: True** — The book's own judgement.
