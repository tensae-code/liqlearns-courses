# Menilek and Italy — Wuchale and Article 17 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Article 3 give Italy?
**Answer:** Recognition that Italy held most of the lowlands and some highlands across the Mereb — a boundary Italy still kept crossing inward.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did one word threaten Ethiopia's sovereignty?
**Answer:** 'May' left Ethiopia free to deal with Europe itself; 'must — only through Italy' made her foreign relations Italy's to run, the mark of a protectorate.
