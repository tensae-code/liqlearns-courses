# Menilek and Italy — Wuchale and Article 17 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Treaty of Wuchale was signed —
a) May 2, 1889, at Wuchale in Wollo  b) May 20, 1878, at Liche  c) June 1884, at Adwa  d) October 1896, at Addis Ababa
**Answer: a) May 2, 1889, at Wuchale in Wollo** — May 2, 1889, at Wuchale in Wollo.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The treaty had —
a) Three articles  b) Twenty articles  c) Seventeen articles  d) Fifty articles
**Answer: b) Twenty articles** — Twenty articles.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Article 3 concerned —
a) Trade  b) The boundary — Italy holding land across the Mereb  c) Religion  d) Prisoners
**Answer: b) The boundary — Italy holding land across the Mereb** — The boundary across the Mereb.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Amharic text of Article 17 said Ethiopia —
a) Must use Italy always  b) May use Italy's help with other European countries  c) Must pay Italy  d) Could not trade
**Answer: b) May use Italy's help with other European countries** — May — a choice, not a duty.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Italian text said Ethiopia —
a) May ignore Italy  b) Must conduct all foreign relations only through Italy  c) Must crown Umberto  d) May use any helper
**Answer: b) Must conduct all foreign relations only through Italy** — Must — only through Italy.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Italy's campaign claimed Ethiopia was —
a) An Italian protectorate  b) A republic  c) A British colony  d) At war with France
**Answer: a) An Italian protectorate** — An Italian protectorate.
