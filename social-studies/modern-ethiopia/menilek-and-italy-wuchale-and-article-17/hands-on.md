# Hands-on — Menilek and Italy — Wuchale and Article 17

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One word ringed
Write the two sentences on paper, Amharic meaning and Italian meaning. Ring MAY and MUST in red. Read both aloud.

## Boundary creep
Draw the Mereb line on a map. Step one counter across it, again and again — Italy 'kept crossing it inward'.
