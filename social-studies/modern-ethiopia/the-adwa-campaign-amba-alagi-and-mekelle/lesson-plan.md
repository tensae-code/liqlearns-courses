# Lesson plan — The Adwa campaign — Amba Alagi and Mekelle

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** The Adwa campaign — Amba Alagi and Mekelle
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Before Adwa came **Amba Alagi (1895)**, where Toselli fell, and the **siege of Mekelle in January 1896**, won by seizing the fort's **spring**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has followed modern Ethiopia's story in this title's order from 1855. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Amba Alagi — the first blow** — Say Amba Alagi 1895 destroyed Toselli's force and drove the survivors into Mekelle fort.
2. **Mekelle — the spring decides** — Give Mekelle's corrected dates, January 7–21, 1896, the spring stratagem as Taytu's advice, and Baratieri's four brigades plus Ellena's reserve.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title, in the title's order — most nearest, the lesson just before this one. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Amba Alagi: Gebeyehu, Toselli killed, survivors to Mekelle.
2. Explain how the Mekelle siege was won (the spring) and its correct dates.
3. Name Baratieri's brigade commanders and the reserve commander.
