# Hands-on — The Adwa campaign — Amba Alagi and Mekelle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Thirst as a weapon
Put a cup of water outside a 'fort' of chairs. Show the garrison's problem when the spring outside the walls is held by the besiegers.

## Four brigades
Set four rows of stones for Albertoni, Arimondi, Dabormida and the fourth brigade, with a fifth small row behind: Ellena's reserve.
