# The Adwa campaign — Amba Alagi and Mekelle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ras Makonnen commanded the combined force besieging Mekelle.
**Answer: True** — The book names Ras Makonnen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Menilek massacred the Mekelle garrison when it surrendered.
**Answer: False** — He let them leave in peace.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Baratieri's army stood in four brigades.
**Answer: True** — Albertoni, Arimondi, Dabormida and a fourth, with Ellena's reserve.
