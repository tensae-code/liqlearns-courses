# The Adwa campaign — Amba Alagi and Mekelle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happened to Toselli's force at Amba Alagi?
**Answer:** It was destroyed by the Ethiopian force under Fitawrari Gebeyehu and others; Toselli was killed and the survivors fled to the fort at Mekelle.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How was Mekelle won without storming its walls?
**Answer:** On Taytu's advice the besiegers seized the spring that served the garrison; thirst forced the Italians to terms, and Menilek let them leave in peace.
