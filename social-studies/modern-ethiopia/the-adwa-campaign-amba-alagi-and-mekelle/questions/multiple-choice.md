# The Adwa campaign — Amba Alagi and Mekelle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At Amba Alagi the Ethiopian force was led by —
a) Fitawrari Gebeyehu and others  b) Ras Makonnen alone  c) Alula  d) Menilek himself
**Answer: a) Fitawrari Gebeyehu and others** — Fitawrari Gebeyehu and others.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Major Toselli —
a) Escaped to Eritrea  b) Was among the killed at Amba Alagi  c) Surrendered at Mekelle  d) Won the battle
**Answer: b) Was among the killed at Amba Alagi** — Toselli was among the killed.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Amba Alagi survivors fled to —
a) Adwa town  b) Italy's strong fort at Mekelle  c) Asmara  d) Massawa
**Answer: b) Italy's strong fort at Mekelle** — The strong fort at Mekelle.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Mekelle siege ran —
a) January 7–21, 1896  b) January 7–21, 1895 as correctly printed  c) March 1, 1896  d) December 1895
**Answer: a) January 7–21, 1896** — January 7–21, 1896 — the printed 1895 is the book's error.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The siege turned on seizing —
a) The fort's gate  b) The small spring beside the fort, on Taytu's advice  c) Baratieri's guns  d) A supply convoy
**Answer: b) The small spring beside the fort, on Taytu's advice** — The spring, on Taytu's advice; the garrison suffered from thirst.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Baratieri's reserve at Adwa was commanded by —
a) Albertoni  b) Ellena  c) Arimondi  d) Dabormida
**Answer: b) Ellena** — Brigadier Ellena commanded the reserve.
