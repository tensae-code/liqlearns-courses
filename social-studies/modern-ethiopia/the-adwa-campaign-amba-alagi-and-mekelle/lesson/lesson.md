# The Adwa campaign — Amba Alagi and Mekelle

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Before Adwa came Amba Alagi (1895), where Toselli fell, and the siege of Mekelle in January 1896, won by seizing the fort's spring. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Amba Alagi — the first blow

Before the main battle the sides met twice. At **Amba Alagi in southern Tigray (1895)** the Ethiopian force under **Fitawrari Gebeyehu** and others **destroyed Major Toselli's Italian force** — Toselli among the killed, the survivors fleeing to Italy's **strong fort at Mekelle**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mekelle — the spring decides

A combined force under **Ras Makonnen** besieged Mekelle; on **Taytu's advice** the Ethiopians seized the **small spring beside the fort** that served the garrison, and in the siege of **January 7–21, 1896** the Italians suffered badly from thirst until **Menilek let them leave in peace**. Meanwhile General **Oreste Baratieri's** army stood in **four brigades under Albertoni, Arimondi and Dabormida**, with Brigadier **Ellena** commanding the reserve — the army that would march to Adwa.
