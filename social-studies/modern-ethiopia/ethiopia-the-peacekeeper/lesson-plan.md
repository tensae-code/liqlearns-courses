# Lesson plan — Ethiopia the peacekeeper

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Ethiopia the peacekeeper
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 11 — Modern Ethiopia (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Say what role Ethiopia chose internationally after 1991.
**This lesson's own level:** the Grade 12 depth of this title — it stands on the title's earlier lessons (the empire to 1941, the restoration) and needs nothing else; a learner may jump straight in, as every name is explained where it first appears.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. History is taught as story: the video script is a story script in the animated-map spirit.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **A chosen international role** — Say that peacekeeping is presented as Ethiopia's chosen international role, and that Ethiopia is a leading troop contributor.
2. **The missions the book names** — Name the missions the book names: Liberia, Rwanda, Sudan and Somalia.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
