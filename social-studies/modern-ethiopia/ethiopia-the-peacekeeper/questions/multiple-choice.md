# Ethiopia the peacekeeper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After 1991 Ethiopia's chosen international role was —
a) Isolation  b) Peacekeeping — a leading troop contributor  c) Colonisation  d) Neutrality with no missions
**Answer: b) b) Peacekeeping — a leading troop contributor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopian peacekeepers serve under the flags of —
a) NATO only  b) The United Nations and the African Union  c) Single nations only  d) No flag
**Answer: b) b) The United Nations and the African Union**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a mission the book names? —
a) Liberia  b) Vietnam  c) Korea in the 1950s as this lesson's case  d) The Balkans only
**Answer: a) a) Liberia**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The mission closest to home was in —
a) Liberia  b) Rwanda  c) Somalia  d) Sudan
**Answer: c) c) Somalia**
**5. (Unit 8 review — Grade 12)** Unit 8 review: Eritrea's independence followed the transition in —
a) 1991  b) 1993  c) 1995  d) 2002
**Answer: b) 1993 — After the transitional charter period began.**

**6. (Unit 8 review — Grade 12)** Unit 8 review: The 1995 Constitution established —
a) A unitary empire  b) The FDRE's ethnic federalism  c) A military state  d) A monarchy
**Answer: b) The FDRE's ethnic federalism — Nations' and nationalities' rights, Article 39 among them.**

**7. (Unit 8 review — Grade 12)** Unit 8 review: Ethiopia's chosen international role, as the unit closes, is —
a) Isolation  b) Peacekeeping — a leading troop contributor  c) Colonial expansion  d) Neutrality by law
**Answer: b) Peacekeeping — a leading troop contributor — Liberia, Rwanda, Sudan and Somalia missions named.**
