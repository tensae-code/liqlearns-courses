# Ethiopia the peacekeeper

## Do it first

Before any explanation, tell this story to yourself in one breath, from the title alone: Ethiopia the peacekeeper. What do you already think happened, and what would you need to check? Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — A chosen international role

A country that spent decades as the scene of wars chose, after 1991, a different part to play abroad: the peacekeeper. Ethiopia became one of the world's leading contributors of troops to peacekeeping missions — its soldiers serving under the United Nations and African Union flags in other people's war zones, keeping ceasefires and guarding civilians.

## Sub lesson 2 — The missions the book names

The book names the fields where Ethiopian peacekeepers served: Liberia, in West Africa; Rwanda, after its catastrophe; Sudan, in its long north–south agony; and Somalia, Ethiopia's own neighbour, where the mission was hardest and closest to home. Four names, four different kinds of broken peace — and in each, Ethiopians in blue helmets or AU green, standing between armed men and the people behind them.
