# Hands-on — Ethiopia the peacekeeper

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four flags
On an Africa outline, mark Liberia, Rwanda, Sudan and Somalia. Under each, write one line on what 'keeping a ceasefire' would mean there.

## The peacekeeper's kit
List what a peacekeeper carries that a conquering soldier does not aim to use the same way — mandate, rules of engagement, the flag of the UN or AU — and say why each matters.
