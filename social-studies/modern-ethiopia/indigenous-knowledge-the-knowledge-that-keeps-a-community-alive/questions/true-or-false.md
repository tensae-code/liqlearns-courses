# Indigenous knowledge — the knowledge that keeps a community alive — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Indigenous knowledge is written-first and universal.
**Answer: False** — it is local, practised and inherited — the next lesson's marks.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Indigenous knowledge guides a community's social, economic, political and spiritual survival.
**Answer: True** — it shapes worldviews and survival alike.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Indigenous knowledge is a museum of dead customs, unused by living communities.
**Answer: False** — it is the operating knowledge of living communities.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Rituals and local classifications count among indigenous knowledge's forms.
**Answer: True** — laws, rituals, classifications, management, decisions, conflict practices, rights.
