# Video script — Indigenous knowledge — the knowledge that keeps a community alive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens not on a capital but on a village at dawn: an elder selecting seed, grain by grain, teaching a grandchild's hands.
The camera travels: a herder reading grassland; elders gathering under a tree; a law being spoken, not written, and obeyed.
Each scene is labelled with a form — law, classification, management, decision, peace-making. The labels accumulate into one phrase: indigenous knowledge.
The last shot returns to the seed selection. The knowledge passes hand to hand, as it has for a hundred generations.
