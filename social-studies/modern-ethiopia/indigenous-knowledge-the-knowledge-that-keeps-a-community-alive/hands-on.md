# Hands-on — Indigenous knowledge — the knowledge that keeps a community alive

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Interview an elder
Ask an elder or grandparent for one piece of knowledge they were taught orally — a weather sign, a remedy, a rule of the house. Record who taught them, and teach it to the class in their words.

## Classify your street
Indigenous knowledge classifies the local world. Make your community's classification of one thing — rains, winds, soils or market days — as the elders name them, in a chart.
