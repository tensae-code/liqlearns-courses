# Hands-on — The FDRE — unity in diversity, and its open questions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The founding map
On a blank Ethiopia map, label the nine states from the guide's list and the two city administrations. Keep it as the class's FDRE wall map for the rest of the unit.

## Objective against challenges
Write 'unity in diversity' at the top of a page. Under it, pair each challenge from the book's list with the article-principle of the constitution meant to answer it (self-determination, equality, supremacy of the constitution). Say where the pairing is weakest.
