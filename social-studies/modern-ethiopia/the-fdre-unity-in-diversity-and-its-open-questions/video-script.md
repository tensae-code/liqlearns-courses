# Video script — The FDRE — unity in diversity, and its open questions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on 1991's fourteen regions. The lines settle, merge and clarify — 1995: nine states, two cities, one federation.
A document unrolls over the map: 106 articles. Its first line names its authors — the Nations, Nationalities and Peoples.
Nine flags rise around one flag. Between them, four words are written: unity in diversity.
Then the questions rise beside the flags, one by one — secession, imbalance, language, the capital. The camera does not answer them. It hands them forward.
