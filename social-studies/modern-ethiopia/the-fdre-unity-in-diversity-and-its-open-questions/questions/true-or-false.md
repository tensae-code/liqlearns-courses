# The FDRE — unity in diversity, and its open questions — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Sovereignty in the 1995 constitution rests with the Nations, Nationalities and Peoples.
**Answer: True** — they are named its authors and owners.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The nine-state list is the federation's founding list of 1995.
**Answer: True** — held as the guide gives it — a founding fact.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** The book presents the federal design as having no open challenges.
**Answer: False** — it lists six posed challenges honestly.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Harari and Gambella are among the nine founding regional states named for the FDRE.
**Answer: True** — both stand in the guide's nine-state founding list.
