# Video script — The 1955 constitution — an empire re-founded on paper

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the restored empire, its provinces in many hands. One by one the hands change: imperial appointees, one system, one centre.
1955: a new document is proclaimed. Beside it, the old document of 1931 rises for comparison — two papers, two eras.
The camera travels down both texts and stops at the same drawing underneath: a throne, and lines of power running up to it from every province.
Re-founded on paper — the empire of the next twenty years stands framed.
