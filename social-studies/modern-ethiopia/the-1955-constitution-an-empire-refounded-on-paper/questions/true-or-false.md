# The 1955 constitution — an empire re-founded on paper — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Administrative reform weakened the centre's hold on the provinces.
**Answer: False** — it drew the provinces into one system answerable to the throne.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The 1955 constitution replaced the 1931 constitution.
**Answer: True** — a quarter-century and an invasion stand between them.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** British officials and wartime arrangements formed part of the administration the restored emperor inherited.
**Answer: True** — consolidation rebuilt one imperial system over them.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** A quarter-century, an invasion and a liberation stand between the 1931 and 1955 documents.
**Answer: True** — yet both frame a centralised monarchy.
