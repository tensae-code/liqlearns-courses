# Hands-on — The 1955 constitution — an empire re-founded on paper

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sketch the units
Draw a simple map of imperial Ethiopia divided into administrative units answerable to one centre. Label the arrows: who appoints, who reports, who collects.

## The side-by-side
Make a two-column sheet: 1931 / 1955. Fill the rows — occasion, language, structure of power — from the two lessons. Read down the structure row last.
