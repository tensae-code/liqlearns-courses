# Hands-on — Foreign invasions — Gundet and Gura

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two battle cards
Make two cards — GUNDET 16 Nov 1875 / GURA 7–9 Mar 1876. Under each write the Egyptian commander beaten there.

## The levy
A kitet raised about 20,000 men. Count out 20 stones as hundreds — no: as thousands — and feel how a levy gathers a countryside.
