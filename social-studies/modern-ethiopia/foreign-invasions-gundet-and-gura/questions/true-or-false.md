# Foreign invasions — Gundet and Gura — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A kitet is a levy — a call that raises an army.
**Answer: True** — The book glosses kitet as levy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After Gundet, Egypt evacuated all the territories it held.
**Answer: False** — Egypt did not evacuate them — hence 'neither peace nor war'.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The defeats helped bring Ismail down in 1879.
**Answer: True** — And Egypt passed under Britain in 1882.
