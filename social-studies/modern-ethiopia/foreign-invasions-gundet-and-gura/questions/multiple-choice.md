# Foreign invasions — Gundet and Gura — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Werner Munzinger's 500 were destroyed by —
a) The Mahdists  b) The Afar, before reaching Shewa  c) Yohannes at Gundet  d) Ras Alula
**Answer: b) The Afar, before reaching Shewa** — Destroyed by the Afar on the way inland from Tajura.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Muhammad Raouf Pasha's force took —
a) Harar  b) Mitsiwa  c) Gondar  d) Metema
**Answer: a) Harar** — Harar.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The force that reached the Mereb was led by the Danish colonel —
a) Loring  b) Arendrup  c) Hewett  d) Munzinger
**Answer: b) Arendrup** — Colonel Arendrup.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At Gundet, November 16, 1875, Yohannes's kitet numbered about —
a) 2,000  b) 20,000  c) 80,000  d) 500
**Answer: b) 20,000** — About 20,000 men raised by the levy.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The second Egyptian army, beaten at Gura, was led by —
a) Arendrup  b) Muhammad Ratib Pasha and the American general Loring  c) Raouf Pasha  d) Ismail himself
**Answer: b) Muhammad Ratib Pasha and the American general Loring** — Ratib Pasha with General Loring.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Gura was fought on —
a) March 7–9, 1876  b) November 16, 1875  c) May 20, 1878  d) March 1, 1896
**Answer: a) March 7–9, 1876** — March 7–9, 1876, near Gundet.
