# Foreign invasions — Gundet and Gura — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What were the three Egyptian thrusts of 1875?
**Answer:** Raouf Pasha took Harar; Munzinger's 500 were destroyed by the Afar; Arendrup's force reached the Mereb river.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did the Gundet victory not end the war?
**Answer:** Egypt kept the territories it occupied and came back for revenge in 1876; even after Gura its demands left things neither peace nor war.
