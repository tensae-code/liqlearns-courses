# Foreign invasions — Gundet and Gura

## Do it first

Before any dates, walk the story once in your head: Yohannes's levy destroyed Egypt's invasions twice: at Gundet, November 16, 1875, and at Gura, March 7–9, 1876. Now read it slowly, part by part, and let the story carry the facts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three thrusts of 1875

Egypt struck three ways in **1875**: **Muhammad Raouf Pasha's** force took **Harar**; **Werner Munzinger's 500**, moving inland from Tajura, were **destroyed by the Afar** before reaching Shewa; and the well-armed force under the Danish colonel **Arendrup** crossed into the Mereb Mellash and reached the **Mereb river**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gundet and Gura

Yohannes's peace effort failing, he proclaimed a **kitet (levy)** that raised about **20,000 men**, crossed the Mereb and **won at Gundet on November 16, 1875** — Arendrup and many Egyptians killed, arms captured — though Egypt did not evacuate the territories it held. Ismail prepared revenge: a well-armed army under **Muhammad Ratib Pasha** and the American general **Loring** invaded again and was **defeated a second time at Gura, near Gundet, March 7–9, 1876**. The defeat helped bring Ismail down in **1879** and Egypt under Britain in **1882**; Egypt's demands made the state of things **neither peace nor war**.
