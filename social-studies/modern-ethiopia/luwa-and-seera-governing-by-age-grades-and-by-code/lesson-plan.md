# Lesson plan — Luwa and Seera — governing by age grades and by code

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern Ethiopia · **Lesson:** Luwa and Seera — governing by age grades and by code
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern Ethiopia — the indigenous knowledge unit; Grade 10 Unit 9 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Sidama Luwa system rotates all males through five age grades — Darara, Fullassa, Hirobora, Wawassa, Mogissa — changing every eight years, training defenders and growing elders; in Kambata and Hadiya, Seera is the elders-administered code of behaviour governing life from marriage to the treatment of the environment.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the peace-systems lessons — Luwa and Seera widen the unit from settling disputes to governing a whole society. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Luwa — the five grades turning** — Sidama's age-grade institution: five rotating grades changing every eight years, with ritual, cultural and political roles; defence and elder-succession as twin objectives.
2. **Seera — a whole life under one code** — Kambata and Hadiya's code of behaviour: governing love, marriage and family, peer groups, work parties, games, hunting bands, treatment of the environment and of elders — aiming at peace, correction and reintegration, administered by village elders.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List Luwa's five grades and its eight-year rotation.
2. State Luwa's twin objectives.
3. Describe Seera's range and its administrators.
