# Hands-on — Luwa and Seera — governing by age grades and by code

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The wheel
Draw the Luwa wheel: five segments named, an eight-year arrow, the elders' council at the hub. Place a marker for a boy of 10, a man of 34 and an elder of 66, and say what each is doing in the system.

## One code, many rooms
List Seera's domains as rooms of a house. In each room write one rule the code might hold (from the lesson's spirit, marked as your example, not the book's text).
