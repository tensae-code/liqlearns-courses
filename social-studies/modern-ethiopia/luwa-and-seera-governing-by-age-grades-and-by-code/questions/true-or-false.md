# Luwa and Seera — governing by age grades and by code — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** In Luwa, a man may rule as an elder without passing through the grades.
**Answer: False** — all males progress cyclically — government as apprenticeship.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Seera leaves private life — love, marriage, games — outside its range.
**Answer: False** — its range is the whole of life.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Luwa's grades carry ritual, cultural and political roles, not only military ones.
**Answer: True** — an age-related institution of the whole society.
