# Video script — Luwa and Seera — governing by age grades and by code

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Sidama country. A great wheel is drawn over it — five segments, five names — and it begins to turn, eight years per station.
Figures climb the wheel as it turns: youths with spears at the rim, councillors midway, white-bearded Cimeeyye gathering at the hub.
The camera moves to Kambata: a village day unfolds — a wedding, a work party, a hunt, a farmer sparing a tree — one word over every scene: Seera.
An elder corrects a young offender and seats him back among his peers. The wheel and the code fade together: government, as a way of life.
