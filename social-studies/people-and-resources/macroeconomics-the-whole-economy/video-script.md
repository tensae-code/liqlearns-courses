# Video script — Macroeconomics — the Whole Economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 10 book opens its Economics chapter with the foundations of macroeconomics: the study of the economy as a whole — the whole country's production, prices, jobs and trade taken together — as distinct from the choices of one household or one firm. This topic lays the foundation the chapter's problem topics (inflation, unemployment, financial markets) then build on.

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the senior cycle drops the “Individual and…” frame for straight disciplines — Society, Culture, Law, Economics, Politics, International Relations — with Mongolia's own culture, macroeconomy and foreign relations as the worked examples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
