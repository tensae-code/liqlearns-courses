# Hands-on — The Sea Gods Blessing Food Tourism And Care

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 18 The sea, a divine blessing (دریا، نعمت خداوندی): the sea and food resources — part of the caught fish goes to the canning factories (pupils visit a cannery in Bandar Abbas and talk with its experts and workers); the sea and tourism — great numbers of our people travel to Iran's northern coasts for their summer holidays, and boating at Chabahar; and let us protect the sea — the waters of the Persian Gulf and the Sea of Oman among the greatest habitats of sea creatures (ornamental fish, corals), with the oil tanker and spilt oil pictured. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
