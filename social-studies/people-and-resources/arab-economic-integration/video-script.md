# Video script — Arab Economic Integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Yemen's book, the *Yemen Grade 6 G6 Geography of the Arab World* (printed p. 22), teaches: Unit 1 Lesson 4 Arab Economic Integration. Yemen's book, the *Yemen Grade 11 G11 Geography of Yemen and the Arab World* (printed p. 188), teaches: Unit 6 Lesson 6 Arab Economic Integration.

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 6 G6 Geography of the Arab World): Grade 6 widens from Yemen to the whole Arab world, taught region by region (Arabian Peninsula, Fertile Crescent, Horn of Africa and Comoros, Nile Valley, Arab Maghreb) with a fixed three-lesson pattern per region: location and borders, natural features, population and economic activity.

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 11 G11 Geography of Yemen and the Arab World): Grade 11 does Yemen in depth first (physical base, population and social development, economic resources), then the Arab world's physical geography, its people and resources, and finally its contemporary problems — water, food, poverty, illiteracy, integration.
