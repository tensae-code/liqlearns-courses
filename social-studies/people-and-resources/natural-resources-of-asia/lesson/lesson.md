## Sub lesson 2 — Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 8 np-g8): this is the old kingdom-era standalone Geography volume — Nepal is taught through its 14 zones (anchal), with full regional accounts of Bagmati, Narayani, Mechi and Mahakali zones (position, environment, resources, development, chief towns, each with maps); Asia is the one continent studied in depth; world economic geography is built on "environment and man" (geographical control). Chapters marked ** in the contents (Earth's surface/interior; the population chapter) were prescribed only for certain school types. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
