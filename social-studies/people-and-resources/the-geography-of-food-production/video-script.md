# Video script — The Geography of Food Production

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Costa Rica's Grade 7 CONED anthology maps food itself. In Unit III Tema II A–B it follows the spatial distribution of food production — which parts of the world grow, raise and catch what, and why there — and then takes a spatial view of food consumption: who eats what, where the food travels, and how the producing map and the eating map do not match. Costa Rica's own farming sits inside the world picture as the home case.

Costa Rica learns this inside its own book's way: a distance-education anthology organised around "the human footprint on geographic space" — every unit opens with a generating question and objectives, and Costa Rica is used as the home case inside world topics (tropical forest conservation, food production problems). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
