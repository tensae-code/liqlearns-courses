# Video script — Energy Resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Tuvalu the coconut gives energy as well as food. Its sap — toddy — serves as a sugar substitute, and coconut bioethanol is a biofuel that could replace about 31% of the nation's petrol: an island's own tree standing in for imported fuel.

Nauru meets energy resources inside the coconut case study: energy is not a separate chapter of faraway fuels, but one more gift of the tree of life the class has just studied — sap and biofuel counted beside food, drink, oil, fibre, timber and thatch.
