# The Multidimensional Poverty Index — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Deprivations arrive together" in your own words.
**Answer:** The MPI (UNDP, 2010) scores health, education and standard of living.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Ten indicators" in your own words.
**Answer:** Ten indicators across health (2), education (2) and living standards (6).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The dual cut-off" in your own words.
**Answer:** Multidimensional poverty uses a dual cut-off: deprived in an indicator, and in enough indicators overall.
