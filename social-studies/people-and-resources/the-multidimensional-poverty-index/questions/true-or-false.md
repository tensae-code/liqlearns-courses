# The Multidimensional Poverty Index — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The MPI (UNDP, 2010) scores health, education and standard of living.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ten indicators across health (2), education (2) and living standards (6).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Multidimensional poverty uses a dual cut-off: deprived in an indicator, and in enough indicators overall.
**Answer: True**
