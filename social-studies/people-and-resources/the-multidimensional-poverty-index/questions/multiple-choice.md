# The Multidimensional Poverty Index — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Deprivations arrive together"?
a) The MPI (UNDP, 2010) scores health, education and standard of living  b) Ten indicators across health (2), education (2) and living standards (6)  c) Multidimensional poverty uses a dual cut-off: deprived in an indicator, and in enough indicators overall
**Answer: a) The MPI (UNDP, 2010) scores health, education and standard of living**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Ten indicators"?
a) Ten indicators across health (2), education (2) and living standards (6)  b) Multidimensional poverty uses a dual cut-off: deprived in an indicator, and in enough indicators overall  c) The MPI (UNDP, 2010) scores health, education and standard of living
**Answer: a) Ten indicators across health (2), education (2) and living standards (6)**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The dual cut-off"?
a) Multidimensional poverty uses a dual cut-off: deprived in an indicator, and in enough indicators overall  b) The MPI (UNDP, 2010) scores health, education and standard of living  c) Ten indicators across health (2), education (2) and living standards (6)
**Answer: a) Multidimensional poverty uses a dual cut-off: deprived in an indicator, and in enough indicators overall**
