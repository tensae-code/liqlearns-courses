# Video script — The Multidimensional Poverty Index

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Income alone misses deprivations that arrive together. The Multidimensional Poverty Index, the MPI (UNDP, 2010), scores households on three dimensions: health, education and standard of living.

The ten indicators: health — child death and malnutrition; education — no member with five years of schooling, and a school-age child out of school; living standards — electricity, drinking water, sanitation, flooring, cooking fuel and assets. Indicators are equally weighted within dimensions, at one-sixth each.

A person is counted multidimensionally poor by a dual cut-off: deprived in an indicator, and deprived in enough indicators overall. The method is the Alkire–Foster extension of the FGT method from the last lesson — depth logic applied to many dimensions at once.
