# Hands-on — The Multidimensional Poverty Index

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Score a house
Draw a house scored on ten small icons, some lit, some dark — one per MPI indicator. Decide a cut-off, rule whether the household counts as MPI-poor, and defend the hardest icon.

## Income versus MPI
Invent a household that is income-non-poor but MPI-poor, and one the reverse. Write what each measure sees and misses in the two homes.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
