# Hands-on — Resource Depletion in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The elder interview
Interview an elder: what resources were richer here in their youth — trees, fish, grass, spring water, soil depth? For each loss they name, ask what changed: more people? More fields? More cutting? Sort their answers under the four pressures.

## The locality sketch map
Draw a sketch map of your locality locating its natural resources — the activity the guide itself sets. Mark fields, forest, grazing land, water points and quarries. Colour the depleting places red and the cared-for places green.

## One remedy, started
With family, school or community, start one remedy in miniature: plant trees with a crop row (agroforestry), plant a community woodlot corner (social forestry), or mark a bare slope for protection. Photograph or draw it on day one — your baseline for watching depletion run backwards.
