# Resource Depletion in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three of Ethiopia's six named troubles of depletion.
**Answer:** Any three of: farmland cropped without rest (with backward land care); grazing land stripped of grass; forests felled; lake fish catches falling; soil eroding; water supply shrinking.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A classmate says: "Famine is simple — it is drought." Answer them using the paradox.
**Answer:** Egypt and Israel are desert lands and Norway and Finland snowbound, yet they feed themselves and export; Ethiopia is resource-rich yet has suffered repeated famine. So drought alone cannot be the whole cause — forest-felling, erosion and land care decide what a land can feed its people.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Walk the phosphorus chain from the start to the end.
**Answer:** No phosphorus → plants cannot grow → no oxygen production → human and other life ceases.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two kinds of damage that resource destruction causes.
**Answer:** Any two of: ecological disturbance; destruction of flora and fauna; soil and rock instability; landscape degradation; desertification; global warming.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** A fast-growing city is eating the farmland and forest around it. Which remedy answers it directly, and what does it mean?
**Answer:** Growing urban centres upward — building the city taller instead of spreading it wider over land and forest.
