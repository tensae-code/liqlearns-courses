# Resource Depletion in Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cutting one tree is, by itself, depletion.
**Answer: False** — depletion is the race: using a resource faster than it can replace itself, year after year.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson teaches that drought alone fully explains famine.
**Answer: False** — the book poses that as the debate question, and its paradox (dry and snowbound lands feeding themselves) shows land care decides alongside climate.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If petroleum oil were depleted, transport, manufacture, building, planting, mining and harvesting would all be hit, because they depend on its liquid fuels.
**Answer: True.**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Resettlement, as this lesson teaches it, means moving a group of people to a new settlement inside the country.
**Answer: True** — that is the section's own internal sense.
