# Video script — Resource Depletion in Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A tree grows slowly. An axe is fast.
Cut faster than the growing — that is **depletion**.

Now look at a whole country. Our country.
Fields cropped with no rest. Grazing land stripped bare.
Forests felled. Lake fish — fewer every year.
Soil washing away. Springs shrinking.
Six troubles. One race lost six ways.

But wait. Egypt is desert — and Egypt exports food.
Norway is snow — and Norway eats well.
Ethiopia is rich in land and water — and Ethiopia has starved.
How?

Not drought alone. Say it plainly:
a land that loses its trees loses its water.
A land that loses its soil loses its food.
**What people do to the land decides what the land can do for them.**

And the chains run on. No oil — nothing moves, nothing is made.
No phosphorus in the soil — no plants, no oxygen, no life.

The remedies are in our hands too:
trees planted with the farming; forests kept by the community;
cities growing **up**, not out.

Now you. Name the six troubles.
Then name one remedy — and where you would start it.
