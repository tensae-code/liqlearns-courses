Pairing and error honesty. (1) Book 2 is an Addis Ababa bureau guide (2022, in English) and Book 1 is an Amhara bureau student book (© 2015 E.C., in Amharic) — NOT edition-matched companions; pupil content comes from the student book, and everything sourced to Book 2 is labelled teacher evidence. (2) The guide's §3.4 answer key defines resettlement in refugee-protection terms ("a tool to provide international protection … for refugees") — that keyed answer is WRONG for the section, which is about internal resettlement (moving a group of people to a new settlement inside the country); this lesson teaches resettlement in the section's own internal sense and does not teach from the refugee-terms key. (3) The tree-line GIF in Sub lesson 4 draws the demographic-pressure mechanism (a growing population depending on wood for fuel, house construction and tools cuts trees in great quantity) that the guide's §3.4 frame and its Unit 7 demographic-overload placement state; it is filed here because the four-pressures sub lesson is where this build teaches that pressure. (4) The student book's desert-types list (§7.1, numbered 1, 2, 2, 3) belongs to a different chapter and is not taught in this lesson; it is not corrected here because this lesson never presents that list.
