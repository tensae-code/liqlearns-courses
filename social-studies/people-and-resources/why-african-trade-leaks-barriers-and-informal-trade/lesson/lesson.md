## Sub lesson 1 — Barriers worse than tariffs

Non-tariff barriers — paperwork, delays, roadblocks of procedure — bite harder than tariffs themselves. Both push trade out of the formal channel into smuggling and informality.

## Sub lesson 2 — The black-market birr

An East African case study shows informal traders converting prices at black-market rates: the birr worth far less than officially against the Kenyan shilling and the Djibouti franc. Ethiopia's informal terms of trade are left unfavourable — its goods cheapened at the border.

## Sub lesson 3 — Perishables cannot wait

Administrative delays are unworkable for perishables without cold chains, which is why fruit and vegetables trade informally. The remedy offered: investment in roads, transport and market information along priority corridors and agri-food value chains.
