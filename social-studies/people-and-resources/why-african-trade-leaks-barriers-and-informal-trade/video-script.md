# Video script — Why African trade leaks — barriers and informal trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Non-tariff barriers — paperwork, delays, roadblocks of procedure — bite harder than tariffs themselves.

An East African case study shows informal traders converting prices at black-market rates: the birr worth far less than officially against the Kenyan shilling and the Djibouti franc.

Administrative delays are unworkable for perishables without cold chains, which is why fruit and vegetables trade informally.
