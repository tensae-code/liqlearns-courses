# Why African trade leaks — barriers and informal trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which barriers bite harder?
a) non-tariff barriers  b) tariffs  c) weather
**Answer: a) non-tariff barriers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Barriers push trade into —
a) smuggling and informality  b) formal channels  c) storage
**Answer: a) smuggling and informality**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At black-market rates the birr is worth —
a) far less than officially  b) far more  c) exactly the official rate
**Answer: a) far less than officially**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Fruit and vegetables trade informally because —
a) delays ruin perishables without cold chains  b) they are banned formally  c) traders prefer it
**Answer: a) delays ruin perishables without cold chains**
