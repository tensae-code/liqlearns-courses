# Why African trade leaks — barriers and informal trade — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are non-tariff barriers?
**Answer:** Paperwork, delays and procedures that hinder trade without being taxes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Against which two currencies is the birr's black-market fall measured?
**Answer:** The Kenyan shilling and the Djibouti franc.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the remedy.
**Answer:** Investment in roads, transport and market information along priority corridors and value chains.
