# Lesson plan — Renewable, non-renewable — and using sustainably

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** People and Resources · **Lesson:** Renewable, non-renewable — and using sustainably
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Natural Resource Base of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a resource is what people extract from an environment they did not make; renewable resources replenish as fast as used while non-renewable ones exist in fixed amounts; and sustainable use means taking no faster than renewal.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the natural-resources lessons of the earlier grades, reframed at the world scale the Grade 10 unit opens with.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **What a resource is** — Resources as what people extract and use from an environment they did not make.
2. **Renewable and non-renewable** — The two kinds, told apart by whether the amount renews or is fixed.
3. **Ecosystem services and sustainable use** — What ecosystems do for people, and what sustainable use means.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What counts as a natural resource. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define a natural resource in the book's words.
2. Tell renewable from non-renewable with the book's examples.
3. State the rule of sustainable use.

## If the learner is stuck
The definition first: taken from an environment people did not make. Then the one test — renews, or fixed? Sunlight and wind renew; petroleum and coal are fixed.
