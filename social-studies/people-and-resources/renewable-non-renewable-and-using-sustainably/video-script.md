# Video script — Renewable, non-renewable — and using sustainably

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A resource is taken from a world we did not make.
Some resources renew — sunlight, wind.
Some are fixed — coal, oil.
Use no faster than nature replaces.
