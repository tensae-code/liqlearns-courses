# Hands-on — Renewable, non-renewable — and using sustainably

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the shelf
Collect or list ten things from home and the street. Sort them into two piles — came from a renewable resource, came from a non-renewable one — and defend each sort aloud.

## The renewal test
Pick one renewable resource your family uses. Work out, in words, what 'using it sustainably' would mean this year, and what over-using it would look like.
