# Renewable, non-renewable — and using sustainably — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book stress that people did not make the environment resources come from?
**Answer:** Because a resource is defined by the taking: people extract and use what nature provides — they did not make the sunlight, wind, soil or minerals themselves.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What are ecosystem services, in the book's framing?
**Answer:** The work living systems do that people depend on — introduced in these pages alongside the rule of sustainable use.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sort these and say why: wind, coal, petroleum, sunlight.
**Answer:** Renewable: wind and sunlight (they replenish as fast as used). Non-renewable: coal and petroleum (fixed amounts).
