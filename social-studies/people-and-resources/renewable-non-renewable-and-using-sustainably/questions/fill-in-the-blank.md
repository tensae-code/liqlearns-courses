# Renewable, non-renewable — and using sustainably — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Resources that replenish as fast as they are used are called ______.
**Answer:** renewable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Petroleum exists in a ______ amount.
**Answer:** fixed.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Using a resource no faster than it renews itself is ______ use.
**Answer:** sustainable.
