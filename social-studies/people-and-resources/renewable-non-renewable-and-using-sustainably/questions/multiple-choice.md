# Renewable, non-renewable — and using sustainably — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A natural resource is —
a) anything people make in factories  b) what people extract and use from an environment they did not make  c) only gold and oil  d) only farmland
**Answer: b) what people extract and use from an environment they did not make** — the book's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pair is renewable?
a) coal and oil  b) sunlight and wind  c) gold and coal  d) oil and gas
**Answer: b) sunlight and wind** — they replenish as fast as used.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Petroleum and coal are non-renewable because —
a) they are expensive  b) they exist in fixed amounts  c) they are underground  d) they are black
**Answer: b) they exist in fixed amounts** — once used, gone.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Sustainable use means —
a) using nothing  b) taking no faster than a resource can renew itself  c) using everything quickly  d) importing resources
**Answer: b) taking no faster than a resource can renew itself**.
