# Video script — What Mongolia Buys and Sells — Foreign Trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 10 book closes its International Relations chapter with Mongolia's foreign economic relations: the trade and economic ties that join Mongolia to the world — what it sells abroad, what it buys in, and the partners those flows run through. The topic teaches foreign trade from Mongolia's own case.

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the senior cycle drops the “Individual and…” frame for straight disciplines — Society, Culture, Law, Economics, Politics, International Relations — with Mongolia's own culture, macroeconomy and foreign relations as the worked examples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
