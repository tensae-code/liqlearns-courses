# Tourism — selling the landscape — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A natural attraction named is —
a) a factory  b) a port  c) the Simien heights
**Answer: c) the Simien heights**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A cultural attraction named is —
a) an airport  b) the rock-hewn churches  c) a shopping mall
**Answer: b) the rock-hewn churches**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tourism contributes —
a) earnings, jobs and protection of attractions  b) only earnings  c) nothing yet
**Answer: a) earnings, jobs and protection of attractions**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Growth is constrained by —
a) lack of attractions  b) too many visitors  c) infrastructure, services and promotion
**Answer: c) infrastructure, services and promotion**
