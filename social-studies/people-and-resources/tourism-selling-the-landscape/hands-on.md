# Hands-on — Tourism — selling the landscape

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sell your area
Write a one-page tourism pitch for your own area using only attractions the course has taught you to name honestly — no invented wonders.

## Brake audit
List what a visitor to your area would need that is missing — road, lodging, guiding, signs — and sort the list into the lesson’s constraint kinds.
