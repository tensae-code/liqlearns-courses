## Sub lesson 1 — Economic integration: the Mercosur case

In Argentina, the teacher notebook Cuadernos para el aula: Ciencias Sociales gives its Geography axis (Eje 1) a worked teaching proposal: La integración económica en América Latina: el caso del Mercosur — economic integration in Latin America, taught through the case of Mercosur. Integration is not taught as an abstract word: it is taught as something real countries in Latin America actually built together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Learning integration from a real case

Mercosur is the case the proposal works with: neighbouring countries joining their economies together. Argentina's own way here is case-first — take the real bloc the country belongs to, and learn what economic integration means by looking at what it does. The notebook's wider Geography axis studies that integration through maps, and through a real gas-pipeline case in its later proposals.
