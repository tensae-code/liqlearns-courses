# Hands-on — Mercosur — Economic Integration in Latin America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the bloc
Draw a simple map of South America from an atlas. Mark Argentina and its neighbours, and draw lines joining the countries of one bloc together — the way integration joins economies across borders.

## List what neighbours can share
Write a list of things neighbouring countries can do together when their economies are integrated — trade, travel, shared projects. Keep the list; the gas-pipeline lesson builds on it.
