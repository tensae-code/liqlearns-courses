Eritrea's own way (Eritrea Grade 4 Social Studies 4 (Tigrinya)): the first Economics a Tigrinya-medium pupil meets is inside the community unit — needs first (what a person must have to live), then work as the way needs are met, split into making goods and giving services.

**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Nepal, Uganda, Eritrea).**

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 4 np-g4b): The newer (2078 BS) trial book reorganises the same ground by units ending in "Hamro Prithvi" (Our Earth): Earth as our common home, our needs from the Earth, our local level, and drawing a map / making a map of our village; economic life is taught through production, income-generating work and the haat bazaar (periodic market).

Uganda's own way (Uganda Primary 4 Home-Study Social Studies): Economics enters through the district — the work people around the child do, how a child adds to family income, and the services the government brings to the district; this placement keeps that way: a child's own work — crops, goats, chickens, selling in shops — counted as a real contribution to family income.
