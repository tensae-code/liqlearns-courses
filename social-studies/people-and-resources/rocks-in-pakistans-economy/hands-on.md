# Hands-on — Rocks In Pakistans Economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Role of rocks in the economy (fossil fuels, gemstones, building material, minerals, soil) and rocks found in Pakistan: Khewra rock salt, limestone, Thar coal, Murree sandstone, marble (Mardan, Swat, Hazara, Gilgit, Attock). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
