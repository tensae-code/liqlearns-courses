# Cottage industries and cooperatives against poverty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cottage industries are small, home-based, low-technology units like weaving, leather work, pottery, basketry and blacksmithing.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cooperatives like coffee unions carry smallholders into better markets.
**Answer: True**
