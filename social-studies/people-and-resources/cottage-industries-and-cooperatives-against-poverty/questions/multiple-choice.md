# Cottage industries and cooperatives against poverty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Cottage industries — production at home"?
a) Cottage industries are small, home-based, low-technology units like weaving, leather work, pottery, basketry and blacksmithing  b) Cooperatives like coffee unions carry smallholders into better markets
**Answer: a) Cottage industries are small, home-based, low-technology units like weaving, leather work, pottery, basketry and blacksmithing**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Cooperatives — the same role at scale"?
a) Cooperatives like coffee unions carry smallholders into better markets  b) Cottage industries are small, home-based, low-technology units like weaving, leather work, pottery, basketry and blacksmithing
**Answer: a) Cooperatives like coffee unions carry smallholders into better markets**
