# Cottage industries and cooperatives against poverty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Cottage industries — production at home" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cottage industries are small, home-based, low-technology units like weaving, leather work, pottery, basketry and blacksmithing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Cooperatives — the same role at scale" in your own words.
**Answer:** Cooperatives like coffee unions carry smallholders into better markets.
