# Hands-on — Cottage industries and cooperatives against poverty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Loom and warehouse
Draw a weaver at a home loom and a coffee-union warehouse sharing one frame. Label what each route needs (investment, members, market) and whom each employs.

## Exit map
For one village you know or invent, map two poverty exits — one cottage, one cooperative — naming the product, the workers reached, and the market each sells into.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
