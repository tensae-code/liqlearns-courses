# Moroccan Agriculture and Livestock — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's Geography Lesson 6 teaches —
a) agriculture and plant and animal wealth  b) only mining  c) only transport
**Answer: a) agriculture and plant and animal wealth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Production in the lesson is of two kinds —
a) crop and animal  b) sea and air  c) old and new
**Answer: a) crop and animal**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The vegetation cover is to be —
a) protected  b) burned  c) ignored
**Answer: a) protected**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Farming assets belong to the homeland's —
a) wealth  b) history lessons only  c) sea fronts
**Answer: a) wealth**
