## Sub lesson 1 — Farming assets — crop and animal production

The sixth Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Features of my homeland — agriculture and plant and animal wealth. The learner meets the homeland's farming assets and its production in both kinds: crop production from the plants, and animal production from the livestock.

## Sub lesson 2 — Protecting the vegetation cover

The lesson adds the care of what grows: protecting the vegetation cover. The plant cover of the homeland is part of its wealth, and the guide teaches the learner that it is to be protected, not used up.
