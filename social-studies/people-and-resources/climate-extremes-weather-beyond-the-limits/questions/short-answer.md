# Climate extremes — weather beyond the limits — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a climate extreme.
**Answer:** Temperature or rainfall far beyond normal thresholds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the warming figures.
**Answer:** About 1.0 °C by 2017, heading for 1.5 °C around 2040.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three photographed impacts.
**Answer:** Dead livestock in Somali Region, failed sorghum, a flooded Philippine town.
