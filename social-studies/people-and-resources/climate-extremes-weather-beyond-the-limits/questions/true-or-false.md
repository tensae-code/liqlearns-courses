# Climate extremes — weather beyond the limits — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Landslides and plagues count among climate extremes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rainfall rises everywhere as the world warms.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Dead livestock in the Somali Region illustrate an extreme's impact.
**Answer: True**
