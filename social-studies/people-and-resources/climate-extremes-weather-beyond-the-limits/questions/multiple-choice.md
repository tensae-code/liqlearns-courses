# Climate extremes — weather beyond the limits — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An extreme is a value —
a) far beyond normal thresholds  b) slightly unusual  c) exactly average
**Answer: a) far beyond normal thresholds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Warming by 2017 stood at about —
a) 1.0 °C above pre-industrial  b) 0.1 °C  c) 3 °C
**Answer: a) 1.0 °C above pre-industrial**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1.5-degree mark is projected around —
a) 2040  b) 2100  c) 2020
**Answer: a) 2040**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Rainfall falls, on these figures, in —
a) the tropics and subtropics  b) mid latitudes  c) high latitudes
**Answer: a) the tropics and subtropics**
