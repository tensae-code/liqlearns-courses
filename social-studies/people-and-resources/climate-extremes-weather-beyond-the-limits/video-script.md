# Video script — Climate extremes — weather beyond the limits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Climate extremes are temperature and rainfall values far beyond normal thresholds: heat waves and cold waves, floods, droughts, landslides and plagues.

Measured warming stood at about 1.0 degree above pre-industrial levels by 2017, heading for 1.5 degrees around 2040 on the book's IPCC figures.

The book's photo set shows Ethiopian and world impacts: dead livestock in the Somali Region, failed sorghum, a flooded Philippine town.
