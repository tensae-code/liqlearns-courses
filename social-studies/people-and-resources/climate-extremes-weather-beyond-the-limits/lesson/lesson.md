# Climate extremes — weather beyond the limits

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: when does weather stop being weather and become an extreme? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What an extreme is

Climate extremes are temperature and rainfall values far beyond normal thresholds: heat waves and cold waves, floods, droughts, landslides and plagues. The threshold matters — an extreme is judged against what a place normally bears.

## Sub lesson 2 — A warming world

Measured warming stood at about 1.0 degree above pre-industrial levels by 2017, heading for 1.5 degrees around 2040 on the book's IPCC figures. Rainfall rises in mid and high latitudes but falls in the tropics and subtropics — the burden lands unevenly.

## Sub lesson 3 — Seen in photographs

The book's photo set shows Ethiopian and world impacts: dead livestock in the Somali Region, failed sorghum, a flooded Philippine town. Extremes are not abstractions; they have addresses.
