Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 91), teaches: Lesson 7: Specialised single-crop farming — the plantation (پالنتیشن): bananas, coffee, pineapple and cacao grown far away and brought to us; paintings of 18th–19th-century cotton and sugar plantations with white masters and slave workers; today's plantations mostly owned by big companies; activity on the excerpt from García Márquez's 'One Hundred Years of Solitude'.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).
