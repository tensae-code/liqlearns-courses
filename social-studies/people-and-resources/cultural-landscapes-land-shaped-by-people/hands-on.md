# Hands-on — Cultural landscapes — land shaped by people

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a landscape
Take one view you know — real or remembered — and read it as a document: list what nature gave and what culture wrote on it, line by line.

## Archive entry
Write the archive entry for one Ethiopian cultural landscape: name, makers, centuries, what it still does for a living, what threatens it.
