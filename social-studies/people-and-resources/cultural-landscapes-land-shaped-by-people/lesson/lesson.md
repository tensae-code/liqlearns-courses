# Cultural landscapes — land shaped by people

## Do it first

A hillside of stone terraces is not nature and not merely farming: it is a landscape a culture built over centuries. Read the cultural landscape.

## Sub lesson 1 — The meaning

A cultural landscape is a geographic area shaped by the combined work of nature and cultural heritage — land whose present form carries a people’s history, farming, building and belief. It is read like a document: the land is the page, the culture the writing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia’s examples

Ethiopia’s examples: the terraced highlands, where generations walled the slopes; the rock-hewn and historic sites; and the coffee landscapes of the south-west, where the forest and the crop grew up together. Each is at once scenery, livelihood and archive.
