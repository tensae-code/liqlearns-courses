**Acceptance count: taught in 6 countries (Ethiopia, Angola, Samoa, Colombia, Cambodia, Slovenia). Haiti is named here as teacher-sequence evidence only, counted nowhere. Papua New Guinea is named here as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Haiti's own way (teacher sequence — evidence): Geography begins as a daily practice, not a chapter: pupils draw the landscape around their school, the plan of their quarter and Haiti with their school placed on it, read landscape forms and functions from their own croquis, then trace one everyday object (a coffee cup, a phone) from raw material to shop shelf to see the world from the local. That evidence is stocked here as the way Haiti teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Searchable tags: cultural landscape, terraces, konso, rock-hewn, coffee landscape, social studies, Haiti, Faire de la géographie, teacher sequence evidence

Angola's own way (Grade 5): Geography opens as "geographic space" — the natural space and the human-built space (Luanda port, Benguela railway, Cambambe dam, Huambo housing) — before any physical topic; Angola's own position, borders, population figures (INE) and farm/fishery tables carry the world topics. That way is stocked here as the way Angola teaches this thing.

Angola's own way (Grade 6): a resources-and-environment year — every world topic (air, water, soils, forests) is closed with the Angolan case: Angola's fresh-water use, Angola's farm land, Angola's forest areas. That way is stocked here as the way Angola teaches this thing.

Samoa's own way (Year 9, Book 2): Place is taught through Samoan lived places — ordinary, sacred (tapu), natural and political places such as Mulinu'u — and migration is taught through Samoa's own movement story and its New Zealand diaspora, closing with a full mapping-skills unit.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado2_01): Escuela Nueva self-paced guides (parts A–D: explore, deepen, practise, apply) set in rural Colombia — veredas, resguardos, coffee regions; geography starts from the walk home-to-school, the classroom plan and the home municipality (Aracataca/Ciénaga worked example).

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado05_02): Grade 5's second cartilla turns from history to landscape and economy: how people transform natural into cultural landscape, the region's environmental problems, then work and money — geography taught through the child's own region (mountain/valley/savanna/near-the-sea location exercise).

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado6 Postprimaria): the rural Postprimaria Grade 6 guide is built entirely around indigenous peoples — where they live, what they do in their territories, their ancestral struggles for land, and their population and resource use — geography taught as peoples-and-territory.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 7 Social Studies): from Grade 7 the combined Social Studies book splits into four named subject sections; its Geography section teaches people-in-environments — hot zones, deserts and oases, polar peoples, climate zones — through world case studies (Kenya farmers, Amazon, Inuit, Sahara oil) before turning to world population.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 10 Geografija 1 (gimnazija)): the gimnazija first year is systematic physical geography first (Earth's interior, rocks, landforms, weather and climate, soils, vegetation, waters), then human geography (population, settlements, farming, industry and energy, transport, tourism), closing on sustainable development and spatial planning — with Slovenian cases (earthquake risk in Slovenia, the Port of Koper) set inside the world topics.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): at secondary level Social Science splits into five strands — Geography, History, Political Science, Economics, Environment. The Geography strand is a weather-and-climate skills course (instruments, data, graphs — with Port Moresby and Kokopo data) moving to world climate/vegetation, migration, and land use; the Environment strand carries Earth systems, resources and sustainability. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 10 Geography is a population course — population pyramids in depth, world physical geography's effects on settlement, density and economic activity, then PNG-only migration (types, internal migration, routes and trends) and PNG's land-mobilisation questions (undeveloped land, Special Agricultural Business Leases, land grabbing). That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
