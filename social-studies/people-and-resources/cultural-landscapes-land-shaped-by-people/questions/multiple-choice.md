# Cultural landscapes — land shaped by people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cultural landscape is shaped by —
a) nature alone  b) culture alone  c) nature and cultural heritage combined
**Answer: c) nature and cultural heritage combined**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The land in the metaphor is —
a) the reader  b) the page; culture is the writing  c) the writer
**Answer: b) the page; culture is the writing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An Ethiopian example is —
a) rock-hewn and historic sites  b) the Danakil salt flats alone  c) ocean beaches
**Answer: a) rock-hewn and historic sites**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Coffee landscapes lie in —
a) Afar  b) the Ogaden  c) the south-west
**Answer: c) the south-west**
