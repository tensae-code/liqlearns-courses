# Video script — Who trades what inside Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Africa dominates, with about a third of formal intra-African agricultural exports.

Ethiopia's own trade is concentrated: its top sources — Morocco, South Africa and Egypt — and its top destinations — Somalia, Djibouti and Kenya — account for nearly all of it.

Of the top 20 intra-African products, only six are key foods.
