# Who trades what inside Africa — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who dominates, and with what share?
**Answer:** South Africa, about a third.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Ethiopia's top sources and destinations.
**Answer:** Sources: Morocco, South Africa, Egypt. Destinations: Somalia, Djibouti, Kenya.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What fills the top-20 list besides the six key foods?
**Answer:** Sweeteners, fats, beverages, processed foods and traditional exports like tea, coffee and cotton.
