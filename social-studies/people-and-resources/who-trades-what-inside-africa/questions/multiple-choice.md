# Who trades what inside Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** South Africa's share of formal intra-African agricultural exports is about —
a) a third  b) a tenth  c) two thirds
**Answer: a) a third**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's export share by 2016–2018 fell to —
a) 0.5%  b) 5%  c) 15%
**Answer: a) 0.5%**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's top destinations include —
a) Somalia, Djibouti and Kenya  b) Morocco and Egypt  c) Brazil and China
**Answer: a) Somalia, Djibouti and Kenya**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Of the top 20 products, key foods number —
a) six  b) sixteen  c) twenty
**Answer: a) six**
