# Who trades what inside Africa

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The top traders

South Africa dominates, with about a third of formal intra-African agricultural exports. Egypt, Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania rise behind it — while Ethiopia's export share fell to 0.5 percent by 2016–2018.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's concentrated trade

Ethiopia's own trade is concentrated: its top sources — Morocco, South Africa and Egypt — and its top destinations — Somalia, Djibouti and Kenya — account for nearly all of it. Few partners means high exposure when one route fails.

## Sub lesson 3 — What actually crosses borders

Of the top 20 intra-African products, only six are key foods. The rest are sweeteners, fats, beverages, processed foods and traditional exports like tea, coffee and cotton — the border trade of a continent is not mainly a food trade.
