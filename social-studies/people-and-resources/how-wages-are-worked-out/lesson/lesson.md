# How Wages Are Worked Out

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: how wages are calculated — how a worker's pay is worked out, as Mongolia's Grade 9 *Нийгэм судлал IX (Social Studies IX)* teaches it in Chapter 3 Individual and Economy: 3.2 How are wages calculated? on printed p. 46. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — How a wage is calculated

After the labour-market topic, Mongolia's Grade 9 book asks the practical question directly: how are wages calculated? The topic takes the learner from the idea of a wage to the working-out of one — pay is not a guess but a calculation, and a worker who understands the calculation can check their own pay.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the book's own teaching way

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: Social Studies is a five-chapter spiral (Individual & Society, Individual & Culture, Individual & Economy, Human Rights & Law, Public Administration) continued from Grade 8; there is no physical-geography chapter — Mongolia's geography is a separate subject (Газарзүй) — so this book's Geography-wave content is its culture and economics teaching, grounded in Mongolian surveys, wage tables and heritage inventories. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
