# Video script — Oil Economy Reshapes Space

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 5 G5 CS Venezuela y Su Gente* (printed p. 28), teaches: Cuando la economía petrolera cambió costumbres y hasta la estructura espacial. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela* (printed p. 152), teaches: Petróleos de Venezuela S.A.: una industria comprometida con el pueblo.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 5 G5 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): the Geography block is a journey ("Viajemos por Venezuela") through work and wealth — farmland and its natural supports, climate, biodiversity, relief, oil basins and minerals — always tied to how the oil economy rearranged the country's population and space.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela): a final-year economic geography of Venezuela alone — population past/present/future, human development in numbers, then resource by resource (minerals, land, fisheries, forests, oil, water/hydroelectricity) each read through sovereignty, collective participation and environmental conscience, closing on Latin American union.
