# Video script — Products of Ecuador's Provinces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 5 ties land to livelihood: los productos de cada provincia — each province of Ecuador is known by what it produces, and the book matches provinces to their products (the placements name Guayas, Esmeraldas, El Oro, Manabí, Tungurahua and Loja as the worked matches). A province's products follow from its region, climate and soils, so this lesson is the territorial division learned a second way — by what each part of the country grows and makes.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the 2024 Grade 5 book is History-led (five of eight sections are national history, from the roots of society to the Republic); Geography is concentrated at the end, where the country is re-read spatially — Andean relief and rivers, natural regions and climatic floors, population diversity and migration, territorial division, provincial products, and the protected-area system taught together with climate-change action. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
