# Video script — Arab World Agricultural Production

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 8 LY G8 Arab World* (printed p. 62), teaches: Ch.6: الإنتاج الزراعي — Agricultural production in the Arab World and the factors affecting it. Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World* (printed p. 104), teaches: Ch.6: الزراعة في الوطن العربي — Agriculture in the Arab World: concept and characteristics, and the natural and human requirements of Arab farm production. Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World* (printed p. 108), teaches: Ch.6: الصعوبات التي تواجه الإنتاج الزراعي العربي ومعالجتها — The difficulties facing Arab agricultural production and how they are treated.

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 8 LY G8 Arab World): The scale widens from Libya (Grade 7) to the whole Arab homeland: one regional geography — location and strategic importance, relief, climate, vegetation, population, economic resources and transport — closed by a full unit on the Arab food problem and food security.

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World): The Grade 8 Arab World survey returns at secondary depth in two parts — physical (relief treated separately for the African and Asian sides, climate factors and regions, soils, vegetation, water with its problems) and human (population structure and problems, agriculture and its difficulties, crop-by-crop chapters, livestock and sea wealth, minerals metal by metal, oil and gas, alternative energy, industry, transport).
