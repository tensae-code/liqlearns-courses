# Video script — Laos — a Neighbour Study

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide studies the Lao People's Democratic Republic in its neighbouring-countries strand (§4.5): Laos is one of Myanmar's five neighbours, and the guide gives it a study of its own — a whole lesson on the neighbour country, taught from Myanmar.

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. Myanmar's neighbouring-countries strand first takes the neighbouring countries together (§4.1, with ASEAN, BIMSTEC and GMS membership), then studies each neighbour one by one — Bangladesh, India, China, Laos and Thailand — and Laos's study is its place in that run.
