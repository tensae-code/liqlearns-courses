# The faces of poverty — absolute, relative, persistent — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distinguish absolute from relative poverty.
**Answer:** Absolute: below the income securing basic needs, fixed by need. Relative: about 50% below your society's average income, lacking what it counts as normal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes poverty 'persistent'?
**Answer:** The relative shortfall present in two of three years.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two wider dimensions beyond income.
**Answer:** Any two of: health care and status; education; income security; opportunity; empowerment.
