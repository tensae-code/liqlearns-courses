# The faces of poverty — absolute, relative, persistent — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Absolute poverty is fixed by basic needs, not by national averages.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Persistent poverty does less damage than a single poor year.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The relative poverty line shifts as the economy shifts.
**Answer: True**
