# The faces of poverty — absolute, relative, persistent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Absolute poverty is income below the level securing…
a) luxuries  b) food, drinking water, education and healthcare  c) average income
**Answer: b) food, drinking water, education and healthcare**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The absolute line…
a) rises with national growth  b) is unchanged by national growth  c) is set by neighbours
**Answer: b) is unchanged by national growth**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Relative poverty is about…
a) 50% less than average household income  b) no income at all  c) rural life
**Answer: a) 50% less than average household income**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Persistent poverty is the relative shortfall in…
a) one month  b) two of three years  c) ten years
**Answer: b) two of three years**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Relative poverty includes lacking what society counts as normal, such as…
a) internet, clean clothes, a safe healthy home  b) a car factory  c) foreign travel
**Answer: a) internet, clean clothes, a safe healthy home**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Wider dimensions of poverty include…
a) opportunity and empowerment  b) rainfall  c) altitude
**Answer: a) opportunity and empowerment**
