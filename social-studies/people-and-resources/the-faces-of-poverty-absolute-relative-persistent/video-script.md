# Video script — The faces of poverty — absolute, relative, persistent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Poverty is measured three ways: absolute — below the line of basic needs; relative — far below your own society's average; persistent — relative poverty lasting two years in three. First, absolute and relative: Poverty is deficiency in basic needs, and it is measured several ways. Then, persistent — and the wider dimensions: Persistent poverty is the relative shortfall lasting: that same gap in two of three years. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
