# Video script — Crude Density

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One square of land. Count the people on it.

Many people on the square? **High density** — over a hundred.
Some? **Medium** — twenty-one to a hundred.
Few? **Low** — five to twenty.
Almost none — under five? We call it **uninhabited**.

Now the measure. All the people, divided by all the land.
Ten million people. Two thousand square kilometres.
Ten million divided by two thousand — **five thousand** on every square.
High. Very high.

But careful. The measure counts ALL the land —
even rock, even desert, where nobody can farm.
It can hide a crowded farm behind empty land.

That is why it is called **crude** — rough.
Next lesson, we measure only the land that feeds people.
