# Crude Density — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — a short, single-skill lesson
with no sub lessons (his ruling 2026-11-09). Its whole-lesson pass
mark: the learner can state the four density bands (over 100 high,
21–100 medium, 5–20 low, under 5 uninhabited), work a crude density
from a population and an area, place the answer in its band, and
state why crude density can mislead about food supply.

Practice deepens inside the one skill: first, band-reading from
given densities; then, working crude densities from raw population
and area figures; then, harder corners — judging whether a given
crude figure is hiding crowded farmland behind empty unfarmable
land, which is the doorway into the next lesson.
