# Crude Density — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, showing your working where numbers are asked.

**1.** A territory holds 600,000 people on 100,000 km². Work its crude density and name its band.
**Answer:** 600,000 ÷ 100,000 = 6 people per km² — the low band (5–20).

**2.** Why is the simple measure called "crude" density?
**Answer:** Because it divides the people by the total area, counting unfarmable land (rock, desert, water) as if people were spread over it too.

**3.** Two woredas both show a crude density of 50 per km². In one, nearly all the land is farmable; in the other, only a quarter is. Which one's farmers are under more pressure, and why?
**Answer:** The second one's. Its people really stand on a quarter of the land, so its farmland carries about four times the crude figure — the crude measure hides the crowding.
