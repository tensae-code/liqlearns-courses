# Crude Density — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Population density measures —
a) how fast people are growing  b) how many people live on each square kilometre  c) how many children each family has
**Answer: b) how many people live on each square kilometre.**

**2.** A density of 150 people per km² is which band?
a) Medium  b) Low  c) High
**Answer: c) High** — high is over 100 per km².

**3.** A density of 3 people per km² is counted as —
a) Low  b) Uninhabited  c) Medium
**Answer: b) Uninhabited** — under 5 per km².

**4.** The crude density of 10,000,000 people on 2,000 km² is —
a) 500 people per km²  b) 5,000 people per km²  c) 50 people per km²
**Answer: b) 5,000 people per km²** — 10,000,000 ÷ 2,000 = 5,000.
