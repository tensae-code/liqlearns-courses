# Crude Density — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1.** The bands run: over 100 high, 21–100 medium, 5–20 low, under 5 uninhabited.
**Answer: True.**

**2.** Crude density counts only the land that can be farmed.
**Answer: False** — crude density counts ALL the land, including unfarmable land; that is why it is called crude.

**3.** A crude density can mislead about a place's food supply.
**Answer: True** — it spreads the people over land that feeds nobody.
