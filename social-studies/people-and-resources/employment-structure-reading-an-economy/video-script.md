# Video script — Employment structure — reading an economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The classic three sectors: primary work extracts and farms — taking from land, water and mine.

A fourth sector is newly emerging: the quaternary — knowledge work in information technology, research and development, and finance.

A country's employment split across the sectors is its employment structure — and geographers read it as a development signal.
