# Employment structure — reading an economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Primary work is —
a) extraction and farming  b) manufacturing  c) services
**Answer: a) extraction and farming**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The quaternary sector is —
a) mining  b) transport  c) knowledge work — ICT, research and development, finance
**Answer: c) knowledge work — ICT, research and development, finance**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Employment structure means —
a) the size of a factory  b) a country's employment split across the sectors  c) one worker's timetable
**Answer: b) a country's employment split across the sectors**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Quaternary work is noted as —
a) not yet counted in standard assessments  b) the largest sector everywhere  c) already counted everywhere
**Answer: a) not yet counted in standard assessments**
