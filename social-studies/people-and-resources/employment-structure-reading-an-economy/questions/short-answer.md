# Employment structure — reading an economy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four sectors in order.
**Answer:** Primary, secondary, tertiary and quaternary.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What work does the quaternary sector hold?
**Answer:** Knowledge work — information technology, research and development, and finance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is employment structure a signal?
**Answer:** Because the split across sectors differs between less and more developed economies, and moves as development proceeds.
