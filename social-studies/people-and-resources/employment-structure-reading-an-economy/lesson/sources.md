Dominican Republic's own way (Dominican Republic Grade 12 Ciencias Sociales 6° Secundaria): the school-leaving book ends with the national economy measured — GDP, openness, the balance of payments and per-capita income are all taught on Central Bank tables for 2010–2022, and the unit's own conclusion is critical: GDP per capita doubled while the average worker's income stagnated, so other sectors appropriated the increase.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 8): the Grade 8 year is the LOCAL year — every chapter is re-anchored to the child's own орон нутаг (local area/homeland): its environment and problems, its heritage, its economy and market, its governance. The book's Law/Civics parts (Chapters IV–V, printed pp. 63–94) are named in the placements, not placed. That way is stocked here as the way Mongolia teaches this thing.

No book error is flagged for this lesson's pages in the placements. The teacher guide was first published in 2022 against the student book's 2023; the student book is the learner text of record, and the guide is cited as evidence only.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 9 Social Studies): Grade 9 Geography turns homeward and outward — Cambodia's own minerals, natural tourist sites and economy first, then full country profiles of the wider Asian neighbours (China, India, Malaysia, Brunei, Timor-Leste).

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.

Moldova's own way (Moldova Grade 11 G11 Geography): a human geography of the world year — political map and state typology, natural resources by sphere, population and settlements, then the world economy branch by branch (agriculture, each industry, services, transport, tourism), closing with economic-geographic characterisations of 14 states on every continent (Egypt among them) written to one template, plus case studies including labour migration at local/regional/global scale and using Google Maps/Google Earth as geo-information portals.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 5): population geography taught as a developed-world/developing-world contrast throughout — distribution factors, growth, Malthus and the transition, then the two evolutions (developed countries; the "Terceiro Mundo" set) with their opposite population policies, closing on structures, demographic problems (incl. HIV cutting life expectancy below 50) and population–economy/environment relations.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 11 Geography student): the senior Geography course is two big themes only — the world economy in depth (structure, division of labour, every branch, transport systems, trade) and then the environment (ecological balance, pollution of air and water, land degradation, biodiversity, ecosystem services), i.e. economy first, its ecological bill second.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 8 OM Geografía Económica y Social G8) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide's distinctive Cuban layer is territorial identity — learners characterise their own municipality's resources and the environmental problems of its economic sectors, and the world material (political map, population, resources, production) is always closed by comparison with Cuba. That evidence is stocked here as the way Cuba teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
