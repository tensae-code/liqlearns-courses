# The Lorenz curve — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Cumulative shares on a square" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Lorenz curve plots cumulative population share against cumulative income share, poorest first.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The equality diagonal" in your own words.
**Answer:** Perfect equality is the 45-degree diagonal, and real curves bow below it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The picture behind the Gini" in your own words.
**Answer:** Max Lorenz devised the curve in 1905; the Gini coefficient is computed from it.
