# The Lorenz curve — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Cumulative shares on a square"?
a) The Lorenz curve plots cumulative population share against cumulative income share, poorest first  b) Perfect equality is the 45-degree diagonal, and real curves bow below it  c) Max Lorenz devised the curve in 1905; the Gini coefficient is computed from it
**Answer: a) The Lorenz curve plots cumulative population share against cumulative income share, poorest first**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The equality diagonal"?

**Answer: a) Perfect equality is the 45-degree diagonal, and real curves bow below it**
