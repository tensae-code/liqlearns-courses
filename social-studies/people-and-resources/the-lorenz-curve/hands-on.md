# Hands-on — The Lorenz curve

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plot three points
On squared paper, plot (20, 5), (40, 14) and (100, 100), join them as a bowed curve under the diagonal, and label both lines. Your curve is a Lorenz curve built from real shares.

## Bow further
Draw a second, more unequal country on the same axes — its bottom 40% holding just 8%. Say, in the diagram's language, what 'more bowed' means for the households inside it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
