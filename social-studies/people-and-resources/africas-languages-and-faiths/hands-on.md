# Hands-on — Africa's languages and faiths

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Language-families map talk
On a blank Africa outline, mark regions where languages you have heard of are spoken — from your own knowledge, marked as your knowledge. Read the lesson picture's language-families map and compare what the book shows.

## Read a landscape
Walk a short route and list every cultural sign you can see — worship places, dress, scripts on signs, market goods. Write which language or faith each sign belongs to. That list is a cultural landscape, read.
