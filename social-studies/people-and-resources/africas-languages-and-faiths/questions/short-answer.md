# Africa's languages and faiths — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are folk religions, as the book presents them?
**Answer:** The indigenous belief traditions of African peoples, rooted in their own lands and ancestors — a real part of the continent's religious composition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four visible signs of a cultural landscape.
**Answer:** Any four of: places of worship, festivals, dress, markets, schools (also scripts/signs).
