# Africa's languages and faiths — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Language is a working part of economic life — trade and teaching happen through it.
**Answer: True** — the unit's deliberate placement.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's religious composition has only one strand.
**Answer: False** — it is many-stranded, world faiths and folk religions together.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Culture has a distribution and can be mapped, like climate.
**Answer: True** — the lesson's closing geographic claim.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Folk religions are a footnote the book leaves aside.
**Answer: False** — the book names them as a real part of the map.
