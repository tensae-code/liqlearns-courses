# Africa's languages and faiths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's linguistic diversity is —
a) among the smallest on Earth  b) among the greatest on Earth  c) one language per country  d) only written languages
**Answer: b) among the greatest on Earth** — many languages in every region.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In daily African life, multilingual living is —
a) rare  b) normal for millions  c) forbidden  d) only for traders
**Answer: b) normal for millions** — home, market and school languages differ.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Folk religions in the book's account are —
a) ignored  b) a real part of Africa's religious map  c) found outside Africa  d) a single faith
**Answer: b) a real part of Africa's religious map** — indigenous traditions named in full.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A cultural landscape is —
a) a painted picture  b) the visible pattern languages and faiths form on the land  c) a national park  d) a weather map
**Answer: b) the visible pattern languages and faiths form on the land** — read from worship places, dress, markets, schools.
