# Video script — Africa's languages and faiths

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Africa speaks in many languages.
And worships in many faiths — folk religions among them.
Together they paint cultural landscapes on the land.
Culture has a map. Learn to read it.
