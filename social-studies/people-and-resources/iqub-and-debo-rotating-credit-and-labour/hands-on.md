# Hands-on — Iqub and Debo — rotating credit and labour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rotate the pot
Draw a circle of contributors with the pot moving from member to member across ten rounds. Beside it, draw the Debo crew harvesting one member's field, the field rotating next round.

## Bank or Iqub
A family needs a lump sum for a small business. Write the bank route and the Iqub route side by side: requirements, timing, cost, and what happens on a missed payment in each.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
