# Iqub and Debo — rotating credit and labour — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iqub — the rotating pot — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Idir and Iqub — one family of institutions — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debo — rotating labour — b

Statements:
a) Idir is long-term and Iqub can be temporary; both are informal, bottom-up and participatory
b) Debo is a work group labouring on each other's farms in rotation — pooling hands instead of money
c) Iqub rotates a common pot: members contribute regularly and take the whole pot in turn

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
