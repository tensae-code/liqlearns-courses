# Iqub and Debo — rotating credit and labour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Iqub — the rotating pot" in your own words.
**Answer:** Iqub rotates a common pot: members contribute regularly and take the whole pot in turn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Idir and Iqub — one family of institutions" in your own words.
**Answer:** Idir is long-term and Iqub can be temporary; both are informal, bottom-up and participatory.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Debo — rotating labour" in your own words.
**Answer:** Debo is a work group labouring on each other's farms in rotation — pooling hands instead of money.
