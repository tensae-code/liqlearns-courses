# Iqub and Debo — rotating credit and labour — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iqub rotates a common pot: members contribute regularly and take the whole pot in turn.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Idir is long-term and Iqub can be temporary; both are informal, bottom-up and participatory.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debo is a work group labouring on each other's farms in rotation — pooling hands instead of money.
**Answer: True**
