# Iqub and Debo — rotating credit and labour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Iqub — the rotating pot"?
a) Iqub rotates a common pot: members contribute regularly and take the whole pot in turn  b) Idir is long-term and Iqub can be temporary; both are informal, bottom-up and participatory  c) Debo is a work group labouring on each other's farms in rotation — pooling hands instead of money
**Answer: a) Iqub rotates a common pot: members contribute regularly and take the whole pot in turn**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Idir and Iqub — one family of institutions"?
a) Idir is long-term and Iqub can be temporary; both are informal, bottom-up and participatory  b) Debo is a work group labouring on each other's farms in rotation — pooling hands instead of money  c) Iqub rotates a common pot: members contribute regularly and take the whole pot in turn
**Answer: a) Idir is long-term and Iqub can be temporary; both are informal, bottom-up and participatory**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Debo — rotating labour"?
a) Debo is a work group labouring on each other's farms in rotation — pooling hands instead of money  b) Iqub rotates a common pot: members contribute regularly and take the whole pot in turn  c) Idir is long-term and Iqub can be temporary; both are informal, bottom-up and participatory
**Answer: a) Debo is a work group labouring on each other's farms in rotation — pooling hands instead of money**
