# Iqub and Debo — rotating credit and labour — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or figure from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Members of an Iqub take the whole ____ in turn.
**Answer: pot**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The shared motto: Idir, Iqub, for mutual ____, to lessen societal burden.
**Answer: benefits**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Debo pools ____ instead of money.
**Answer: labour (hands)**
