# Video script — Iqub and Debo — rotating credit and labour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iqub (Ikub) is a rotating savings and credit association: members contribute to a common pool weekly or monthly and take the whole pot in turn. It gives a poor family lump-sum access to funds for a wedding, a house or a small business — more flexible and less paper-bound than a bank, with social ties discouraging default.

Idir is long-term and Iqub can be temporary, but both are informal, bottom-up and participatory. Their shared motto says it: 'Idir, Iqub, for mutual benefits, to lessen societal burden.'

Debo completes the set: a work group whose members labour on each other's farms in rotation, pooling hands instead of money. Where cash is scarcest, labour itself becomes the currency of cooperation.
