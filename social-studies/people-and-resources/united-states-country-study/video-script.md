Yemen's book, the *Yemen Grade 8 G8 Geography of the Continents* (printed p. 80), teaches: Unit 5 Lesson 2 The United States of America (country study). Yemen's book, the *Yemen Grade 12 G12 Geography of the Contemporary World* (printed p. 41), teaches: Unit 2 Lesson 3 The United States of America.

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 8 G8 Geography of the Continents): Grade 8 tours the continents one by one — natural features, population, economic activities — with one country studied in depth per continent (Indonesia, Eritrea, Germany, the USA, Brazil), and closes on contemporary world issues.

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 12 G12 Geography of the Contemporary World): the final year is political and economic geography of the whole contemporary world — how it is classified and developed, its blocs in the developed and developing worlds, transport and communications, world trade and tourism, and its shared problems (energy, pollution, unemployment).
