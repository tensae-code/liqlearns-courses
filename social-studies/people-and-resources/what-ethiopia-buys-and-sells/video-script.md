# Video script — What Ethiopia buys and sells

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What leaves Ethiopia: coffee first, then the farm’s other gifts.
What arrives: fuel, machinery, factory goods.
Year after year, the arriving stack is worth more.
That gap has a name: trade deficit.
