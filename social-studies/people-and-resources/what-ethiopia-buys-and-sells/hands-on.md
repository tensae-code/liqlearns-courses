# Hands-on — What Ethiopia buys and sells

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two stacks
Draw the two cargo stacks, imports taller than exports, and label five items on each from the lesson.

## Bill tracing
With a partner, trace how one imported machine is paid for: how many coffee sacks must leave to balance it? Argue from the lesson’s compositions, not invented prices.
