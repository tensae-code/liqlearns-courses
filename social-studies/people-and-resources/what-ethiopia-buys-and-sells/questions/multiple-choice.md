# What Ethiopia buys and sells — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia’s exports are led by —
a) machinery  b) fuel  c) farm goods, coffee above all
**Answer: c) farm goods, coffee above all**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Imports are led by —
a) flowers  b) fuel, machinery and manufactures  c) coffee and pulses
**Answer: b) fuel, machinery and manufactures**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A trade deficit means —
a) imports are worth more than exports  b) exports exceed imports  c) no trade at all
**Answer: a) imports are worth more than exports**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia’s deficit is —
a) a one-year accident  b) a surplus in disguise  c) persistent — year after year
**Answer: c) persistent — year after year**
