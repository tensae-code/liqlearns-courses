# Von Thünen's rings — farming around a city — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the rings in order.
**Answer:** Dairying/vegetables, forest, grain, ranching, wilderness.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do vegetables hug the city?
**Answer:** They are perishable and costly to transport.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the model's three assumptions.
**Answer:** An isolated state, a uniform plain, equally rational farmers.
