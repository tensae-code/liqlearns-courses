# Von Thünen's rings — farming around a city — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Von Thünen's model dates from —
a) 1826  b) 1909  c) 1933
**Answer: a) 1826**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The plain around the city is assumed —
a) uniform  b) mountainous  c) coastal
**Answer: a) uniform**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Nearest the city sit —
a) dairying and vegetables  b) ranches  c) wilderness
**Answer: a) dairying and vegetables**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Locational rent falls as —
a) distance and transport cost rise  b) distance falls  c) the city shrinks
**Answer: a) distance and transport cost rise**
