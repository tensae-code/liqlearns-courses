**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Ukraine, Fiji).**

Fiji's own way (Fiji Grade 7 Year 7 Social Science Pupil's Book): Economics widens from Fiji to the Pacific — resources are owned by tribes and clans as well as individuals, Fiji's story is told through sugar, tourism, fish, gold and bottled water exports, and work is taught through migration and remittances.

Ukraine's own way: A "Geography: Regions and Countries" course — every region is done in the same order (position, nature/resources, population, primary/secondary/tertiary sectors) then typological country studies with rubrics (country visiting-card, capital, pride of the country, unique experience), ending with Ukraine's own place in the world; research tasks (Дослідження) embedded in chapters. That way is stocked here as the way Ukraine teaches this thing. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
