## Sub lesson 2 — Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way

Kuwait's own way (Kuwait Grade 7 Social Studies G7 T2): Term 2 turns from the Arab homeland's nature (Term 1) to its people, its wealth, its problems and its futures, each unit fronted by a guide character — the Architect/Engineer leads Unit 2, the Crisis Observer Unit 3, the Future Pioneer Unit 4. Unit 1 (Geography) covers population in Kuwait and the Arab world; Unit 2 (Geography) resources and wealth; Unit 3 contemporary challenges — natural, political, social and economic problems, and Kuwait's own; Unit 4 visions and ambitions — national visions, Kuwait Vision 2035, future projects, the digital future and Arab integration; the values unit "Stations with Good Kuwait" closes the book. Non-Geography parts (named, not placed): History/Civics — Unit 3 Lesson 2's treatment of the Palestine question (from 1948) and of nuclear risks (weapons, radiation leaks, nuclear waste), printed pp. 54–56 — the geographic parts (Ceuta and Melilla's strategic position; the Sudan split) are placed below. Health/values — Unit 3 Lesson 3, social problems: addiction (drugs, smoking, digital) and unaware digital interaction, rumours and blind imitation, pp. 57–59. Values — Unit 3 Lesson 5's bullying section, p. 62. Civics/planning — Unit 4 Lesson 1, the concept of a national vision and the traits of a successful one, pp. 72–73; Lesson 2, Kuwait Vision 2035 (turning Kuwait into a financial and trade hub; its seven pillars) and its digital projects, pp. 74–76 — the development projects' Geography is placed below. ICT — Unit 4 Lesson 4, the digital future: digital transformation, smart government services and artificial intelligence in the Arab world (Saudi SDAIA, Jordan's Smart Desert), pp. 79–80. Civics/institutions — Unit 4 Lesson 5, Arab integration: its definition, importance and fields, and its organisations — the Arab League (Cairo), the Arab Fund for Economic and Social Development (Kuwait), ALECSO (Tunis), the Arab Organization for Agricultural Development (Khartoum) — and the joint Kuwait–Oman Duqm refinery, pp. 81–82. Values — the values unit "Stations with Good Kuwait": a meeting of old friends in Souq Al-Mubarakiya, pp. 88–89; Nabati poetry as Kuwaiti cultural heritage (the Diwaniya of Nabati Poets), p. 90; the Kuwaiti grandmother, the beating heart of the family, pp. 92–93; the neighbour's duty — a story set in the pearl-diving season, pp. 94–96 (the heritage part is placed below). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
