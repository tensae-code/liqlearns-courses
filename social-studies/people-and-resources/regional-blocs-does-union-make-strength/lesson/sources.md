**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Brazil, Egypt).**

Brazil's own way: No chapters in the old style — 17 "Folhas" (sheets), each opening with a provocative question ("Could Brazil be different?", "Do you take poison?"), grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place. This lesson teaches the placement's thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Searchable tags: regional blocs, European Union, union, Folha 3, A união faz a, SEED-PR, Brazil Grade 10, social studies, people and resources

Egypt's own way (Grade 12): a full political-geography course to close school geography — the state first (definition, unitary/federal/confederal types, capitals chosen for stated reasons, the natural then the human components of a state's power, electoral geography), then borders taught with the border/frontier distinction and the Great Wall of China as the historical case, then blocs and alliances, ending with international relations and the new world order; activities, exam models and a maps appendix fill the back half of the book.
