# Regional Blocs — Does Union Make Strength?

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Folha 3: A união faz a...? / Does union make…? — regional blocs, with the European Union as the example, as Brazil's Grade 10 Geografia (SEED-PR) teaches it in one of its 17 Folhas, each opening with a provocative question. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Regional blocs — countries joining together

Folha 3 opens with A união faz a...? / Does union make…? It teaches regional blocs: groups of countries that join together in a bloc, so that what each country does is no longer done alone.

## Sub lesson 2 — The European Union — does union make strength?

The Folha's example is the European Union — a regional bloc of European countries. The unfinished opening question is the lesson's test: the learner weighs whether union makes strength, using the bloc and the European Union as the case in front of them.

Brazil's book has no chapters in the old style: it is 17 "Folhas" (sheets) like this one, each opening with a provocative question, grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
