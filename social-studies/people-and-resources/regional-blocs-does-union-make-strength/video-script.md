# Video script — Regional Blocs — Does Union Make Strength?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Folha 3 opens with A união faz a...? / Does union make…? It teaches regional blocs: groups of countries that join together in a bloc, so that what each country does is no longer done alone.

The Folha's example is the European Union — a regional bloc of European countries. The unfinished opening question is the lesson's test: the learner weighs whether union makes strength, using the bloc and the European Union as the case in front of them.
