## Sub lesson 1 — People's Republic of Bangladesh — Myanmar's neighbour

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide studies the People's Republic of Bangladesh in its neighbouring-countries strand (§4.2): Bangladesh is one of Myanmar's five neighbours, and the guide gives it a study of its own — a whole lesson on the neighbour country, taught from Myanmar.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — one neighbour at a time

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. Myanmar's neighbouring-countries strand first takes the neighbouring countries together (§4.1, with ASEAN, BIMSTEC and GMS membership), then studies each neighbour one by one — Bangladesh, India, China, Laos and Thailand — and Bangladesh's study is its place in that run.

Myanmar's own way: the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week — physical geography, social geography, environmental geography, neighbouring countries and cartography — each lesson plan built student-centred (Bloom steps, key questions, classroom assessment) around Myanmar and its five neighbours. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
