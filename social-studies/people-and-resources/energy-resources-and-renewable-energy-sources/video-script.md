# Video script — Energy Resources And Renewable Energy Sources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Cuba's book, the *Cuba Grade 8 OM Geografía Económica y Social G8* (printed p. 55), teaches: Unidad 4 Recursos energéticos: minerales energéticos y fuentes renovables de energía (con el potencial renovable del municipio). (teacher guide — evidence) Cuba's book, the *Cuba Grade 8 Geografía 2 G8* (printed p. 22), teaches: Capítulo 2 Recursos energéticos: el petróleo, las reservas mundiales, la OPEP y las transnacionales. Cuba's book, the *Cuba Grade 6 Geografía de Cuba G6* (printed p. 88), teaches: Capítulo 3 El aprovechamiento de las aguas y el ahorro de energía en la región: embalses e hidroeléctricas, petróleo, calentadores solares y biogás. Cuba's book, the *Cuba Grade 9 Geografía de Cuba G9 (2025)* (printed p. 133), teaches: Capítulo 4.1 Principales recursos naturales de Cuba y utilización de la energía renovable.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 8 OM Geografía Económica y Social G8) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide's distinctive Cuban layer is territorial identity — learners characterise their own municipality's resources and the environmental problems of its economic sectors, and the world material (political map, population, resources, production) is always closed by comparison with Cuba. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Cuba is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 8 Geografía 2 G8): This older edition teaches world economic geography as a story — first the political map's dynamics through history (colonial empires, independences, 1914, post-1945, the 1960s, the 1990s), then resources, then production, and it closes with full regional studies; Latin America is studied first and in most depth, and every world topic is measured against Cuba's own sugar, tobacco, nickel and trade.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 6 Geografía de Cuba G6): The child's first geography book is the home country itself — one chapter of tools (coordinates, orientation, scale), then Cuba whole (situation, relief, coasts, climate, population, economy), then its two great regions landscape by landscape; the political-administrative division taught is the pre-2011 one (Ciudad de La Habana appears as a province), and History episodes woven into the regional chapters (the Santa Clara battle, the Granma landing) are named here but not placed — they belong to the History course.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 9 Geografía de Cuba G9 (2025)): The 2025 rewrite keeps the home-country arc but rebuilds it for the Tercer Perfeccionamiento: Chapter 2 now profiles every province inside the political-administrative division itself, the climate chapter adds the Tarea Vida state plan by name, the economy chapter is organised by primary/secondary/tertiary sectors with self-employment (trabajo por cuenta propia) and the Mariel development zone, and the social chapter faces an ageing, shrinking population (life expectancy 73.7 years, a "Vivir 120" programme) and closes with Cuba's Human Development Index. It is a different book from the 2012 edition (Book 15), not a parallel copy, and is placed separately.
