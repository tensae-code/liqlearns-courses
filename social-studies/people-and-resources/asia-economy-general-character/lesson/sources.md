**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Turkmenistan, Nicaragua).**

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 6 Estudios Sociales 6): the world is read continent by continent through a three-sector table first built for América, then each continent's farming, industry, trade and tourism in turn.

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 9 Estudios Sociales 9): "How much we produce and how much we consume" — the world economy continent by continent, framed from the start by dependence: advanced technology and cheap raw materials bought from dependent countries, an often unjust trade, before globalisation is debated in three positions.

Turkmenistan's own way (Grade 10): A classic post-Soviet economic-and-social geography of the whole world in one book: Part I general world (political map, resources, population, world economy by sector), Part II the same template region by region and country by country, Part III global problems — with Turkmenistan's own memberships and ecological policy woven into the questions.
