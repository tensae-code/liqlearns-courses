# Hands-on — Africa's minerals in the world league

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## League-table poster
Make a poster table with four rows — chromium, manganese/platinum, tantalum, bauxite/iron ore — columns: mineral, African producer named in the lesson, one everyday use. Present it and read the headline aloud.

## Mineral hunt at home
Find five metal objects at home. For each, guess its metal and whether Africa leads its world league from this lesson. Mark guesses honestly as guesses, then check two with a partner.
