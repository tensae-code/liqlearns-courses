# Africa's minerals in the world league — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** South Africa leads the table across several minerals at once.
**Answer: True** — chromium, manganese and platinum.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The league table shows Africa holds no significant world mineral shares.
**Answer: False** — it shows commanding shares — that is its point.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Iron ore appears in the book's mineral table.
**Answer: True** — named among the wider cast.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia appears nowhere in the mineral table.
**Answer: False** — Ethiopia is named among tantalum producers.
