# Africa's minerals in the world league — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three minerals of the South Africa headline.
**Answer:** Chromium, manganese and platinum.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does a world-leading production share matter for a country?
**Answer:** It is real weight: the world needs that mineral, so the leading producer holds a strong card in every negotiation about it.
