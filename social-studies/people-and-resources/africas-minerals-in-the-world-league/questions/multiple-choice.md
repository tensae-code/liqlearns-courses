# Africa's minerals in the world league — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** South Africa's three headline minerals in the table are —
a) gold, oil, copper  b) chromium, manganese and platinum  c) tantalum, tin, zinc  d) bauxite, iron, coal
**Answer: b) chromium, manganese and platinum** — the table's strongest showing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A production league table measures —
a) prices only  b) each producer's weight — share — in world production  c) population  d) rainfall
**Answer: b) each producer's weight — share — in world production** — weight, country by country.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tantalum production in the table includes —
a) only Europe  b) Ethiopia among its producers  c) no African country  d) only South Africa
**Answer: b) Ethiopia among its producers** — a home-country entry in a world league.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Bauxite is —
a) a gemstone  b) the ore of aluminium  c) a fuel  d) a fertiliser
**Answer: b) the ore of aluminium** — named in the table.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The problems of mineral utilisation are about —
a) finding new minerals  b) the gap between having mineral wealth and benefiting from it  c) naming minerals  d) drawing maps
**Answer: b) the gap between having mineral wealth and benefiting from it** — the book's closing question for the section.

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** The Grade 10 minimum competencies ask learners to describe locations of major world resources including —
a) only coal  b) water, wildlife, oil and gas, gold, iron ore  c) only sand  d) no resources
**Answer: b) water, wildlife, oil and gas, gold, iron ore** — the MLC's own list (teacher guide — evidence).
