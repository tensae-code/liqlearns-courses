Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 7) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 7 turns analytical — factors behind transport-mode choice, up-to-date maps/charts (routes, contours, scale-and-distance work) for domestic, regional and international travel, transport hazards from floods/fog/earthquakes, shelter shortages and siting factors, and desertification with land rehabilitation (afforestation, gully filling). That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 6 Social Studies SB): the P6 pupil's book is the fullest Geography book of the set — a whole unit on direction and location works Rwanda onto the East African and African maps by latitude and longitude and time zones, then physical features and climate, and a climate-change sequence (types, change, effects, overcoming) sits inside it; regional co-operation is taught bloc by bloc (COMESA, CEPGL, ECCAS, AU, NEPAD).

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 12 SD Geography 12): The final-year book is organised as applied geography: it opens with a full revision exercise on a Port Sudan contour map (contours, scale, cross-sections, watershed lines), then remote sensing and photo interpretation, then world economic geography, and closes on human–environment global problems, repeatedly pulling Sudanese examples (Gezira scheme, gum arabic, Sudanese oil, farming systems of Sudan) into the world treatment.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 6 geo-6af-m): The final primary book turns outward: five of its six units place Mauritania inside its organisations — the Arab Maghreb Union, the Senegal River organisation (OMVS), the Arab League, the African Union, the Sahel drought committee (CILSS), the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation — and inside the industrial world (Europe, North America, the industrial East), before a closing world unit on continents, oceans, seas, rivers and straits.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 9 geo-3as-m): A home-and-region year in three units: the Mauritanian geographic space (the country set astronomically between 15°–27° N and 5°–17° W, area about 1,030,700 km²), Mauritania's economic activities, then the two spaces Mauritania belongs to — the Maghreb and West Africa — ending on sub-regional economic integration.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 10 geo-4as-m): This book re-teaches the Grade 9 programme lesson-for-lesson (its contents list is identical to Book 8's) at greater depth and with fuller map work — its Mauritania unit works through three location maps (in the Arab world, geographic and astronomical location) where the Grade 9 book works from text. Filed as its own book because it is a distinct, longer edition (116 pp vs 104 pp), not a duplicate file.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 8 TG np-g8tg) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 8 economics is monetary — monetary transactions, Nepal's financial institutions, tourism, foreign trade and foreign employment; the Earth unit pairs Earth's motions with weather/climate elements, climate change, Nepal's bordering neighbours and SAARC, disaster management (with the 2072 earthquake as case material) and a map-making practical (draw Nepal, fill in geographical facts). That evidence is stocked here as the way Nepal teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 3): after world/economic geography in Modules 1–2, Module 3 turns to Mozambique itself as a complete regional study — cities, then the country's location, coast, geology, minerals, soils, relief, climate, plant and animal geography, hydrography — and closes with Southern Africa and Mozambique's SADC integration.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 7): industry taught as a long history — from Palaeolithic tool-making through manufacture and mercantilism to the Industrial Revolution and its consequences — before location, types and industrial landscapes; commerce likewise from barter and the invention of coin to trade blocs and unequal world flows.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 8 Geografía 2 G8): This older edition teaches world economic geography as a story — first the political map's dynamics through history (colonial empires, independences, 1914, post-1945, the 1960s, the 1990s), then resources, then production, and it closes with full regional studies; Latin America is studied first and in most depth, and every world topic is measured against Cuba's own sugar, tobacco, nickel and trade.
