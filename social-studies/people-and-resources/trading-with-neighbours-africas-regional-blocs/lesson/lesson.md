# Trading with neighbours — Africa's regional blocs

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: how much of Africa's trade stays inside Africa? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Africa's trade shape

The shape is stated plainly: processed food is imported, unprocessed products are exported. Africa sells the raw and buys back the finished — the pattern integration is meant to change.

[Picture 27060 — an intra-African agricultural exports chart by region, 2003–2018]

## Sub lesson 2 — Growth through the blocs

Intra-African food and agricultural trade has grown since 2000 through the Regional Economic Communities: AMU, COMESA, ECOWAS and SADC. Exports rose to a 2013 peak, dipped, and rose again from 2016.

## Sub lesson 3 — Who keeps trade at home

SADC keeps 84 percent of its intra-African exports inside its own region, and COMESA 66 percent. ECCAS keeps the least inside — the blocs differ in how real their integration is.
