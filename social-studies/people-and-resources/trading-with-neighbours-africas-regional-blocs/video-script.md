# Video script — Trading with neighbours — Africa's regional blocs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The shape is stated plainly: processed food is imported, unprocessed products are exported.

Intra-African food and agricultural trade has grown since 2000 through the Regional Economic Communities: AMU, COMESA, ECOWAS and SADC.

SADC keeps 84 percent of its intra-African exports inside its own region, and COMESA 66 percent.
