# Trading with neighbours — Africa's regional blocs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's pattern is: processed food imported, unprocessed products —
a) exported  b) banned  c) ignored
**Answer: a) exported**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Intra-African trade has grown since —
a) 2000  b) 2013  c) 1990
**Answer: a) 2000**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Intra-African exports peaked first in —
a) 2013  b) 2016  c) 2003
**Answer: a) 2013**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** SADC keeps what share of its intra-African exports in-region?
a) 84%  b) 66%  c) 34%
**Answer: a) 84%**
