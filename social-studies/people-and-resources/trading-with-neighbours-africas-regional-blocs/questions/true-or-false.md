# Trading with neighbours — Africa's regional blocs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** COMESA keeps 66% of its intra-African exports inside its region.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ECCAS keeps the most trade inside its region.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade dipped after 2013, then rose again from 2016.
