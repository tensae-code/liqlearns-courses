# Hands-on — Gulf Water Resources Groundwater Desalination And Treated Wastewater

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 1 Lesson 5, Water Resources in the Arab Gulf States: natural sources — scarce rain, the wadis (Al-Dawasir, Al-Rumah, Al-Batin, some 1,200 km), and groundwater (fresh at Al-Rawdatain, brackish at Al-Sulaibiya in Kuwait); made sources — seawater desalination (Saudi Arabia and the UAE lead; Kuwait has eight plants, the first at Shuwaikh in 1953) and treated wastewater used for industry, roadside planting and land reclamation; Unit 4 Lesson 3, Water Scarcity: the Gulf states face acute water scarcity from their desert nature, the high salinity of their groundwater, low rainfall and rapid population growth; the two options are importing water or desalinating it, and desalination is the fitting solution now; Kuwait is a pioneer — its East Doha power and water-distillation station was founded in 1977 near Doha port; the United Nations marks 22 March as World Water Day — this extends Book 1's NEW Gulf-water lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
