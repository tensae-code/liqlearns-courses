# Hands-on — United States of America — Country Study

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a visiting-card for the United States of America
Make a visiting-card for the United States of America from the lesson: name, capital, position and one fact each for nature, population and economy.

## Find the pride of the country
Say what the lesson names as the pride of the United States of America and one unique experience it offers, and why.
