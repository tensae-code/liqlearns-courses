# Hands-on — Fishing Grounds of South Sudan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Location of fishing industries (grounds) in South Sudan, linked to physical features (map work); Guide: draw the map and locate fishing grounds; types of fishing grounds. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Sudan's way
Say how South Sudan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
