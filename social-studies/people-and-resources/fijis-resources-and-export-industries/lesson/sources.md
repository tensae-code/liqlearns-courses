**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Fiji).**

Fiji's own way (Fiji Grade 10 Year 10 Social Science): Year 10 economics is economic geography of Fiji itself — village, semi-subsistence and commercial farming, the sugar industry's markets and problems, then industry as primary, secondary and tertiary activity with location factors and trade.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Grade 11 Economics Year 11 Part 1): A full direct-approach course built on the circular flow, with Fiji examples throughout — ginger supply curves, Fiji import tables, quarry and land-rent income, the Attu family budget, Fiji credit facilities, sugar-industry production stages and Reserve Bank of Fiji policy tools.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 7 Social Science): Year 7 widens the lens to the whole South Pacific — island types, "islandness" and vulnerability, Pacific hazards, and resources owned by tribes and clans; Fiji is taught as the hub of the South West Pacific.
