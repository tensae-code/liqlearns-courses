# Hands-on — Morocco's Two Sea Fronts and Ports

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the two fronts
On a map of Morocco, trace the Atlantic coast in one colour and the Mediterranean coast in another, and mark the main ports, as the guide's lesson locates them.

## One model port
Write three lines on the Tangier-Med port as the lesson's model: where it sits, and what a port like it does for the country's development.
