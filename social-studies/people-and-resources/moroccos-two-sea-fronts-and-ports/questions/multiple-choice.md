# Morocco's Two Sea Fronts and Ports — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco has two sea fronts —
a) the Atlantic and the Mediterranean  b) two rivers  c) two deserts
**Answer: a) the Atlantic and the Mediterranean**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Along the coasts, the lesson locates —
a) the main ports  b) only farms  c) only mountains
**Answer: a) the main ports**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The coasts are taught for their role in —
a) development  b) ancient history  c) class rules
**Answer: a) development**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The model port of the lesson is —
a) Tangier-Med  b) a river dam  c) an airport
**Answer: a) Tangier-Med**
