# Video script — Morocco's Two Sea Fronts and Ports

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The seventh Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Features of my homeland — the two sea fronts. Morocco faces the sea twice: the learner describes its Atlantic coast and its Mediterranean coast, and locates the main ports along them.

The lesson then asks what the two sea fronts do for the country: the coasts' role in development. One port is held up as the model — the Tangier-Med port — an example of what a port on Morocco's coasts can be for the homeland's development.
