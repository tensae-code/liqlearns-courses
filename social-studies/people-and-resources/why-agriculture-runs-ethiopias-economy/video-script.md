# Video script — Why agriculture runs Ethiopia’s economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Three counts decide an economy: jobs, exports, food.
Agriculture leads all three in Ethiopia.
The guide’s figure: 80% of the people work the land.
That is a backbone — everything else stands on it.
