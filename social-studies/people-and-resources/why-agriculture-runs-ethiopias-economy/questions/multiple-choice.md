# Why agriculture runs Ethiopia’s economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agriculture employs —
a) a small minority  b) only highlanders  c) the majority of Ethiopians
**Answer: c) the majority of Ethiopians**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Agriculture supplies most of Ethiopia’s —
a) fuel  b) exports  c) imports
**Answer: b) exports**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide’s figure for agriculture’s employment share is —
a) 80%  b) 30%  c) 55%
**Answer: a) 80%**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture is called the economy’s —
a) brake  b) roof  c) backbone
**Answer: c) backbone**
