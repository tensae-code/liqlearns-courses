# Why agriculture runs Ethiopia’s economy

## Do it first

Count the jobs, the exports and the dinners of Ethiopia. One activity leads all three counts. Read the evidence.

## Sub lesson 1 — The evidence

Agriculture employs the majority of Ethiopians, supplies most of the country’s exports and the raw materials of its industries, and produces most of its food. The teacher guide’s figure puts the employment share at 80% of the population (teacher guide — evidence). No other activity comes close on any of the three counts.

[Picture 21043 — three bars — jobs, exports, food — all led by agriculture]

## Sub lesson 2 — The backbone

That is why agriculture is called the backbone of the economy: when farming does well, the country eats, exports and employs; when drought or erosion strikes farming, the whole economy feels it. Every other sector — factories needing raw material, towns needing food — stands on the farm.
