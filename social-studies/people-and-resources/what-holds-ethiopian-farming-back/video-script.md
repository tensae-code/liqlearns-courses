# Video script — What holds Ethiopian farming back

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The backbone has cracks.
Plots small and split. Soil washing away.
Rain that may fail. A sickle where a machine should be.
Pests. Bad roads. That is what holds farming back.
