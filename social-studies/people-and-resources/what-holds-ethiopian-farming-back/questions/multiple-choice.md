# What holds Ethiopian farming back — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Holdings in Ethiopian farming are —
a) large and joined  b) mechanised estates  c) small and fragmented
**Answer: c) small and fragmented**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil problems named are —
a) waterlogging only  b) erosion and fertility loss  c) salinity only
**Answer: b) erosion and fertility loss**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The tool the book instances is —
a) the sickle  b) the tractor  c) the combine harvester
**Answer: a) the sickle**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Poor rural infrastructure keeps —
a) rain from falling  b) pests away  c) harvests from markets and help from farms
**Answer: c) harvests from markets and help from farms**
