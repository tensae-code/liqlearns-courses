# Hands-on — What holds Ethiopian farming back

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Problem chain
Pick one problem and draw its chain: erosion → thinner soil → smaller harvest → less food and money. Do the same for poor roads.

## Sickle vs machine
With a partner, estimate: how long to sickle-harvest a field you know, against a machine’s day? Present the gap as the tools problem in numbers.
