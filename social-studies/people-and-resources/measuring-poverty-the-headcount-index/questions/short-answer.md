# Measuring poverty — the headcount index — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Why measure at all" in your own words.
**Answer:** Poverty is measured to keep the poor on the agenda, target help, monitor interventions and judge institutions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The three steps" in your own words.
**Answer:** Choose a welfare indicator, set the poverty line, summarise with a statistic.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The headcount index — H/N" in your own words.
**Answer:** The headcount index is H/N, the share of the population below the line.
