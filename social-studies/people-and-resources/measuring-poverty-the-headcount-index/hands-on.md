# Hands-on — Measuring poverty — the headcount index

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the heads
Draw a line across a crowd of twenty figures and count the heads below it. Compute H/N, then argue what the number cannot see — leading into the next lesson.

## Change a step
Recompute your count after moving the poverty line up, then after switching the indicator from consumption to income. Write one sentence on why reports must name their steps.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
