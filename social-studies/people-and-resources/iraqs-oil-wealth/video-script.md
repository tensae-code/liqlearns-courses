# Video script — Iraq's Oil Wealth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies* (printed p. 39), teaches: Unit 1 Ch. 2: Oil wealth (الثروة النفطية) — Iraq's oil fields, refineries, pipelines and export ports. Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies* (printed p. 61), teaches: Unit 1 Ch. 2: Mineral wealth and the oil fields in three geographical regions (north, centre — East Baghdad, south — Rumaila), oil transport by tanker, and natural gas.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies): the whole first unit is Iraq's own geography — location first (world, region, locality, then the astronomical location), then the land itself region by region off the relief map, then people and economic activity; history and national education follow as separate units.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies): Grade 9 returns to Iraq for the fullest treatment yet — the same national geography as Grade 5 but measured: the plain is 24% and about 600 km long, the desert plateau about 55%, the desert plant belt about 70%, two-thirds of the people live on the central and southern plain; every topic is pinned to numbered maps of Iraq.
