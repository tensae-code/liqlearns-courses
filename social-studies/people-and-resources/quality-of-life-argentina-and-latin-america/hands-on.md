# Hands-on — Quality of Life — Argentina and Latin America Compared

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compare two places you know
Pick two places you know — two streets, two villages, two towns. For each, note what daily life is like: water, school, work, health, travel. Set the two lists side by side, the comparative way Argentina's notebook teaches.

## Say where life is harder, and why
From your two lists, write three sentences: where life is harder, where it is easier, and one reason for each. That is a quality-of-life comparison in your own words.
