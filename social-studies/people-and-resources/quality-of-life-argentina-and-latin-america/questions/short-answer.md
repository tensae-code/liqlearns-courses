# Quality of Life — Argentina and Latin America Compared — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Argentina's quality of life get set against in this proposal?
**Answer: The quality of life of other Latin American countries — the proposal works with a comparative perspective (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why teach quality of life by comparison instead of for one country alone?
