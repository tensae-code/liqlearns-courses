# Quality of Life — Argentina and Latin America Compared — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The proposal at printed p. 40 studies Argentina's quality of life —
a) alone, with no other country  b) compared with other Latin American countries  c) compared with the Moon
**Answer: b) compared with other Latin American countries**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The perspective the proposal works with is called —
a) a comparative perspective  b) a backwards perspective  c) a secret perspective
**Answer: a) a comparative perspective**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A comparison across countries shows what a single-country picture —
a) shows better  b) cannot show  c) hides forever
**Answer: b) cannot show**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The countries Argentina is compared with are in —
a) Latin America  b) East Asia  c) Europe only
**Answer: a) Latin America**
