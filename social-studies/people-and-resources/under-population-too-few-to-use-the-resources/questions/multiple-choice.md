# Under-population — too few to use the resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under-population is population —
a) above the optimum  b) at the optimum  c) below the optimum
**Answer: c) below the optimum**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In under-population, resources are —
a) exported entirely  b) under-used  c) exhausted
**Answer: b) under-used**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The chief cost is —
a) labour shortage  b) overcrowding  c) soil erosion
**Answer: a) labour shortage**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Development under under-population —
a) accelerates automatically  b) is unaffected  c) slows despite abundant resources
**Answer: c) slows despite abundant resources**
