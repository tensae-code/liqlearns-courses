# Under-population — too few to use the resources — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define under-population.
**Answer: Too few people to use the resources fully — population below the optimum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does development slow?
**Answer: Labour shortages slow the resource industries that development needs.**


**5. (Sub lesson 1)** List the three indications of an optimum population.
**Answer:** Full employment; high average living standards; balanced development (Grade 9 student book, printed pp. 162–164).
