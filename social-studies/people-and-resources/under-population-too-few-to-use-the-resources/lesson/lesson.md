# Under-population — too few to use the resources

## Do it first

The other tip of the scale: rich land, empty hands. Mines unworked, fields unfarmed — not for lack of wealth, but for lack of people. Read under-population.

## Sub lesson 1 — Below the optimum

Under-population is population below the optimum: too few people to use the resources fully. Land and minerals lie abundant while the hands to work them are missing — wealth in the ground, poverty in the using of it.

[Picture 21108 — a balance scale tipping to the resources side, an idle mine and farm]

## Sub lesson 2 — Its costs

The cost is labour shortage: resource industries cannot staff themselves, and development slows despite abundant land and resources. Under-population is the quiet mirror of over-population — the same scale, tipped the other way.
