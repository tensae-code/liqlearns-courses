# Hands-on — Under-population — too few to use the resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mirror drawing
Draw the balance scale three times — over, optimum, under — using the three population lessons. Label what each pan holds in each drawing.

## Empty-hands case
Invent (and label as invented) a district with rich land and few people: list three things that would not get done, and what the district might do — invite settlement, mechanise, or wait.
