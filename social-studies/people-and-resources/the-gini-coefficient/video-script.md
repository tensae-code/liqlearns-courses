# Video script — The Gini coefficient

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Gini coefficient is the area between the Lorenz curve and the equality diagonal, divided by the whole area under the diagonal. A ratio of areas — pure geometry turned into an inequality measure.

Zero means perfect equality — the curve lies on the diagonal and the lens vanishes. One means one person holds everything — the curve hugs the floor and the lens fills the triangle. Every real country sits between.

The book's comparison fixes the reading: country A at 0.28 against country B at 0.60 — higher Gini, more unequal. (Corrado Gini, 1912.) A single number to rank the bows of all the Lorenz curves in the world.
