# Hands-on — The Gini coefficient

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shade the lens
On your Lorenz drawing from the last lesson, shade the lens between curve and diagonal and label it 'the Gini's numerator'. Under it, write the 0 and 1 meanings in your own words.

## Rank four countries
Rank four invented Gini values (0.25, 0.41, 0.33, 0.58) from most equal to least, then attach A and B from the book in their right places in the ranking.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
