# The Gini coefficient — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Area over area"?
a) The Gini is the area between curve and diagonal divided by the whole area under the diagonal  b) Gini 0 is perfect equality and Gini 1 is one person holding everything  c) Country A at 0.28 is more equal than country B at 0.60; higher Gini means more unequal
**Answer: a) The Gini is the area between curve and diagonal divided by the whole area under the diagonal**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Zero to one"?
a) Gini 0 is perfect equality and Gini 1 is one person holding everything  b) Country A at 0.28 is more equal than country B at 0.60; higher Gini means more unequal  c) The Gini is the area between curve and diagonal divided by the whole area under the diagonal
**Answer: a) Gini 0 is perfect equality and Gini 1 is one person holding everything**
