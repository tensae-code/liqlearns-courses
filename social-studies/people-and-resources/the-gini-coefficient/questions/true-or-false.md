# The Gini coefficient — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gini is the area between curve and diagonal divided by the whole area under the diagonal.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gini 0 is perfect equality and Gini 1 is one person holding everything.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Country A at 0.28 is more equal than country B at 0.60; higher Gini means more unequal.
**Answer: True**
