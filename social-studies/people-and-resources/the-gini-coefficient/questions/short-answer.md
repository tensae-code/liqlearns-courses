# The Gini coefficient — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Area over area" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Gini is the area between curve and diagonal divided by the whole area under the diagonal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Zero to one" in your own words.
**Answer:** Gini 0 is perfect equality and Gini 1 is one person holding everything.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Reading the direction" in your own words.
**Answer:** Country A at 0.28 is more equal than country B at 0.60; higher Gini means more unequal.
