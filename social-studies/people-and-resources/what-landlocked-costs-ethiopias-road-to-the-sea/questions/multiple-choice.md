# What landlocked costs — Ethiopia's road to the sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Doubling transport costs cuts trade volume by about —
a) 80%  b) 8%  c) 20%
**Answer: a) 80%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tourism demand can fall up to —
a) eightfold  b) twofold  c) not at all
**Answer: a) eightfold**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Border delays normally run —
a) 24–48 hours  b) 24 minutes  c) 48 days
**Answer: a) 24–48 hours**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia lost its ports of Assab and Massawa after —
a) 1997  b) 1977  c) 2007
**Answer: a) 1997**
