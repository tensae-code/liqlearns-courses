Theme 1 re-teaches its world theme on France itself: des milieux métropolitains et ultramarins entre valorisation et protection — France's home and overseas environments, between being used and being protected.

The worked tension is the gold mine in Guyane (French Guiana), set against the protection of spaces and the national parks.
