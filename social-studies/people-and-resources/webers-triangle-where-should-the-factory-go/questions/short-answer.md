# Weber's triangle — where should the factory go? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three costs does Weber weigh?
**Answer:** Transport first, then labour and agglomeration.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the material index.
**Answer:** Weight of inputs divided by weight of outputs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give one example of each locating rule.
**Answer:** Weight-losing at the material: ore refining, sugar milling. Weight-gaining at the market: bottling, brewing.
