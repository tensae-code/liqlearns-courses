# Weber's triangle — where should the factory go? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weber's model dates from —
a) 1909  b) 1826  c) 1933
**Answer: a) 1909**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Firms adjust transport cost for labour and —
a) agglomeration  b) rainfall  c) latitude
**Answer: a) agglomeration**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The material index is input weight divided by —
a) output weight  b) labour cost  c) distance
**Answer: a) output weight**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Sugar milling, a weight-losing industry, locates at —
a) the raw material  b) the market  c) the port
**Answer: a) the raw material**
