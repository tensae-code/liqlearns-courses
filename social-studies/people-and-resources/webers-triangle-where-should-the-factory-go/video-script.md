# Video script — Weber's triangle — where should the factory go?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Industrial location gets its model in 1909: firms minimise total cost, transport first, then adjust for labour costs and agglomeration — the savings of clustering with other firms.

The deciding tool is the material index: the weight of inputs divided by the weight of outputs.

Below 1, the process gains weight — locate at the market, like bottling and brewing.
