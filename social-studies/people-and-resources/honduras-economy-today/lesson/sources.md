**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Honduras).**

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 9 Student Book): widening-circles design — the world continent by continent — hazards and geographic defence first (Hurricane Mitch), territory versus population, then geographic tours of Europe, Asia, Africa and Oceania, closing on a warming world; Bloque 4 adds world population, the world and Honduran economies and migration. Every lesson runs the same 'Sembrar y cosechar' activity cycle.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 9 Teacher Guide) (teacher guide — evidence): widening-circles design — the world continent by continent — hazards and geographic defence first (Hurricane Mitch), territory versus population, then geographic tours of Europe, Asia, Africa and Oceania, closing on a warming world; Bloque 4 adds world population, the world and Honduran economies and migration — the teacher guide mirrors the student book lesson-for-lesson (same 38 lessons and blocks) and adds didactics, theory summaries and answer keys; its geography lessons are placed here at the guide's own printed pages. Every lesson runs the same 'Sembrar y cosechar' activity cycle. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Honduras is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 7 G7 Student CCSS): Grade 7 carries the set's first real Economics block — it starts from the community economy and the Banco Central's own activity classification, then builds Honduras's national story: sectors, formal/informal work (70.8% informal per INE), indicators, poverty, globalisation and the foreign lenders behind structural adjustment.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 3 G3 Student CCSS): Grade 3 scales up from family to country — occupations and production for the whole nation, social classes by income, the production-distribution-exchange-consumption chain, and exports/imports through customs with the arancel explained.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 6 G6 Student CCSS): Grade 6 goes global — world tourism, the maldistribution of world wealth, and integration blocs with the common market explained, while Honduras's own trade (US as main market) anchors it home.
