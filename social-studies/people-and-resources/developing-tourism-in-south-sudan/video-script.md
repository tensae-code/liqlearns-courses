# Video script — Developing Tourism in South Sudan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 11 S3 Geography SB* (printed p. 91), teaches: A tourism development plan for South Sudan; East African tourism marketing. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 11 S3 Geography TG* (printed p. 56), teaches: Guide: what the South Sudan government should improve to sustain tourism; tax measures. (teacher guide — evidence) South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 6 P6 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 39), teaches: Location of tourist attraction sites: national parks and nature reserves (map). South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 6 P6 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 47), teaches: Challenges facing tourism: political instability and disease outbreaks. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 75), teaches: EAC joint tourism promotion: marketing East Africa as a single tourist destination.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 11 S3 Geography SB): A world-geography book (trade routes, megacities, Southeast Asia) anchored back to South Sudan throughout — humanitarian airlift airfields, Thuraya satellite phones, a tourism plan for South Sudan, Juba as the urbanisation example.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 11 S3 Geography TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Mirrors the student's book's 8 units with content maps and expected answers; repeatedly localises world topics (domestic trade with South Sudan examples, what the South Sudan government should improve to sustain tourism). That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 6 P6 Social Studies SB): Tourism is taught as national homework — map the parks, weigh the landlocked disadvantage against South Africa, set up the anti-poaching unit — and the fishing unit ties grounds back to Unit 2's physical features and forward to roads that must be improved so fish can reach market.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies SB): Everything is costed against home ground — Mount Imatong both blocks movement and feeds rivers and the Imatong forest industry; mining is debated head-on ("Mining is the backbone to the economy of South Sudan") with a field trip to a real mining sector.
