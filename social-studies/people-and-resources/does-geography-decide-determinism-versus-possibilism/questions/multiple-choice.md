# Does geography decide? Determinism versus possibilism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Determinism says development is dictated by —
a) location, climate and landforms  b) technology alone  c) chance
**Answer: a) location, climate and landforms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Huntington belongs to the —
a) determinist line  b) possibilist line  c) neither
**Answer: a) determinist line**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Nature is never more than an adviser' is the voice of —
a) possibilism  b) determinism  c) the HDI
**Answer: a) possibilism**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In the modern balance, location shapes transport costs and —
a) market access  b) nothing at all  c) the weather only
**Answer: a) market access**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In the Grade 10 statement, possibilism is aided by —
a) climate alone  b) chance  c) technology and modernisation
**Answer: c) technology and modernisation**
