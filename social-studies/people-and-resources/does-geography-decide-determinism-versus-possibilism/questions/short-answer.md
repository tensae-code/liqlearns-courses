# Does geography decide? Determinism versus possibilism — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State determinism in one sentence.
**Answer:** Development is dictated by location, climate and landforms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State possibilism in one sentence.
**Answer:** The environment offers possibilities, not necessities; people decide.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things location still shapes.
**Answer:** Population distribution, transport costs, market access (any two).
