# Video script — Does geography decide? Determinism versus possibilism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Determinism holds that development is dictated by location, climate and landforms.

Possibilism answers that the environment offers possibilities, not necessities; human creativity and technology decide which are taken.

The balance the book states: location still shapes population distribution, transport costs and market access — but it does not decide alone.
