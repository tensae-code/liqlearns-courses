# Video script — Poverty and conflict — which causes which?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Conflict certainly causes poverty; poverty causing conflict is contentious — at most a permissive factor among others; and the two reinforce each other in cycles that peace work and development work now fight together. First, three hypotheses: Three hypotheses organise the research. Then, the cycle — and the converged response: Third: the two reinforce each other in cycles. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
