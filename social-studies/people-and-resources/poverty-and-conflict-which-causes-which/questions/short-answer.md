# Poverty and conflict — which causes which? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the three hypotheses.
**Answer:** Conflict causes poverty (consensus); poverty causes conflict (contentious — at most permissive); the two reinforce each other in cycles.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does 'permissive, not deterministic' mean here?
**Answer:** Poverty alone does not trigger conflict; it only makes conflict more possible in combination with other factors at particular times and places.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why have peace work and development work converged?
**Answer:** Because poverty and conflict form a cycle in conflict-affected states, so each must be treated with the other.
