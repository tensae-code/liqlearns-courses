# Poverty and conflict — which causes which? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The hypothesis with consensus behind it is…
a) poverty causes conflict  b) conflict causes poverty  c) neither affects the other
**Answer: b) conflict causes poverty**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Conflict causes poverty through…
a) destruction, displacement, collapsed states  b) trade  c) rainfall failure only
**Answer: a) destruction, displacement, collapsed states**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its costs include…
a) intergenerational exclusion  b) higher savings  c) better services
**Answer: a) intergenerational exclusion**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Poverty causes conflict' is…
a) proven fully  b) contentious — poverty is at most permissive  c) disproven entirely
**Answer: b) contentious — poverty is at most permissive**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A permissive factor means poverty…
a) triggers conflict alone  b) interacts with others at particular times and places  c) prevents conflict
**Answer: b) interacts with others at particular times and places**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Development and peace work have converged on…
a) chronic poverty in conflict-affected states  b) cities only  c) trade policy
**Answer: a) chronic poverty in conflict-affected states**
