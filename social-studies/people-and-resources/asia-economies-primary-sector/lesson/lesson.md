## Sub lesson 1 — Asia's primary sector

Ukraine's Grade 10 book studies the economies of Asia in §18 through the primary sector — the work that takes directly from nature. Learners see how farming, mining and other primary work are spread across the region and what they produce.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ukraine's way — regions and countries in one order

Ukraine learns this inside its "Geography: Regions and Countries" course: A "Geography: Regions and Countries" course — every region is done in the same order (position, nature/resources, population, primary/secondary/tertiary sectors) then typological country studies with rubrics (country visiting-card, capital, pride of the country, unique experience), ending with Ukraine's own place in the world; research tasks (Дослідження) embedded in chapters.
