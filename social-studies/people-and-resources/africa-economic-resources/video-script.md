The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 9 LY G9 World Geography* (printed p. 47), teaches: Ch.1 Africa: موارد الثروة الاقتصادية — Africa's economic resources (agricultural resources, livestock, minerals and energy sources, with production tables).

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 9 LY G9 World Geography): A continent-by-continent world survey built on one repeated pattern — every continent gets location, surface features, climate (with summer/winter maps), climate-and-vegetation regions, population (with density map) and economic resources (agriculture, livestock, minerals and energy tables) — starting with Africa because, as the preface says, Libya's own continent links three continents and controls world trade routes.
