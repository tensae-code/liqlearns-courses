The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Joining together is not one single thing. Integration has types.

Argentina's teacher notebook teaches the different types of integration in its third axis.

And the types are read through a real bloc — Mercosur, the one Argentina belongs to.

What countries share, and what they keep apart: that is how you tell the types apart.
