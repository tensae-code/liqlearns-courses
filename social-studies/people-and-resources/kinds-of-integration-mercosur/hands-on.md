# Hands-on — Kinds of Integration — the Mercosur Case

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the joinings
Write down four ways groups you know join together — a team, a market, two villages sharing a road, countries in a bloc. Sort them: what does each share, and what does each keep apart? You are sorting types of joining.

## Find the type in the bloc
From a map of Mercosur, pick one thing its countries share and one thing each keeps for itself. Write both down under the heading: types of integration, read in a real bloc.
