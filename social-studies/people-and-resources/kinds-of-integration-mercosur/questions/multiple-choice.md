# Kinds of Integration — the Mercosur Case — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The proposal at printed p. 108 teaches —
a) the different types of integration  b) the types of clouds  c) the parts of a plant
**Answer: a) the different types of integration**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Countries that join together —
a) all join in exactly the same way  b) do not all join in the same way  c) never join at all
**Answer: b) do not all join in the same way**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The types of integration are read through the case of —
a) Mercosur  b) a single village  c) the oceans
**Answer: a) Mercosur**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** For each type, the learner asks what member countries share and what they —
a) still keep apart  b) throw away  c) hide
**Answer: a) still keep apart**
