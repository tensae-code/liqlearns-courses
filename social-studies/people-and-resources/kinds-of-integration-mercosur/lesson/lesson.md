## Sub lesson 1 — The different types of integration

In Argentina, the teacher notebook Cuadernos para el aula: Ciencias Sociales gives a worked teaching proposal in its third axis (Eje 3): Los distintos tipos de integración — the different types of integration. Countries that join together do not all join in the same way: integration has types, and telling them apart is the knowledge at stake in this proposal.

## Sub lesson 2 — Types, read through Mercosur

The types are not learned as a bare list. Argentina's notebook works its integration teaching through Mercosur — the bloc studied across its proposals, from economic integration to the gas-pipeline database. The learner meets a type, then asks where it shows in the real bloc: what the member countries share, and what they still keep apart.
