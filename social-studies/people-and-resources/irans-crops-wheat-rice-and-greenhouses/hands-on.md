# Hands-on — Irans Crops Wheat Rice And Greenhouses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 6 Agricultural products, from production to consumption (محصولات کشاورزی، از تولید تا مصرف): farming (زراعت) and gardening (باغداری) the two main agricultural activities; the chart of the country's wheat production in different years — in which year was it most?; rice, a one-year plant got in wet and fairly temperate regions — over half of our country's rice produced in the northern provinces, though some other provinces grow it too; and greenhouse cultivation (کشت گلخانه‌ای), a new method making an artificial environment for growing plants.
