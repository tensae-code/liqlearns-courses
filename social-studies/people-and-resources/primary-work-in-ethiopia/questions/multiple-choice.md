# Primary work in Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Primary activities —
a) make factory goods  b) provide services  c) take things directly from nature
**Answer: c) take things directly from nature**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fishing is —
a) a tertiary activity  b) a primary activity  c) a secondary activity
**Answer: b) a primary activity**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia’s workforce is concentrated in —
a) the primary sector  b) factories  c) offices
**Answer: a) the primary sector**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Primary work is the economy’s —
a) roof  b) last step  c) ground floor
**Answer: c) ground floor**
