# Primary work in Ethiopia

## Do it first

A farmer, a fisher, a woodcutter, a miner: four workers who take their living straight from nature. Read what makes their work primary.

## Sub lesson 1 — Taking from nature

Primary activities extract or grow things directly from the environment. In Ethiopia they are agriculture — the giant of the sector; fishing, on the lakes and rivers; forestry, taking fuel, timber and forest products; and mining, digging the minerals the geology lessons named.

## Sub lesson 2 — The primary scale

Primary work is where Ethiopia’s workforce is concentrated: most working Ethiopians do primary work, and agriculture alone dwarfs the rest. The primary sector is the economy’s ground floor — everything made or served later starts with what primary workers take from nature.
