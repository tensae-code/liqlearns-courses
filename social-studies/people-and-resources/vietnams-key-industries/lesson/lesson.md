## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnams Key Industries

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 74–82), teaches: Bài 17: Some of Vietnam's industries — coal (45.3 million tons of clean coal in 2024, centred on Quang Ninh), oil and gas (the Bach Ho, Rang Dong and Hong Ngoc fields; the Dung Quat refinery of 2009), electricity (293.1 billion kWh in 2024 from hydro, thermal and fast-growing renewables; the Son La plant at 2,400 MW), electronics and computers, food and drink production and processing, textiles and garments (5,929.8 million garments in 2024) and footwear. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
