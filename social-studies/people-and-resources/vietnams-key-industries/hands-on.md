# Hands-on — Vietnams Key Industries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 17: Some of Vietnam's industries — coal (45.3 million tons of clean coal in 2024, centred on Quang Ninh), oil and gas (the Bach Ho, Rang Dong and Hong Ngoc fields; the Dung Quat refinery of 2009), electricity (293.1 billion kWh in 2024 from hydro, thermal and fast-growing renewables; the Son La plant at 2,400 MW), electronics and computers, food and drink production and processing, textiles and garments (5,929.8 million garments in 2024) and footwear. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
