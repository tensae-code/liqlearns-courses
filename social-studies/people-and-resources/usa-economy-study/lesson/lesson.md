## Sub lesson 2 — Turkmenistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Turkmenistan's own way (Grade 10): A classic post-Soviet economic-and-social geography of the whole world in one book: Part I general world (political map, resources, population, world economy by sector), Part II the same template region by region and country by country, Part III global problems — with Turkmenistan's own memberships and ecological policy woven into the questions. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
