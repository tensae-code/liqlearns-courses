# Water, Air, Forest, Wildlife, Soil — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons follow the book's own order
through the five resources, grouped where two share one trouble.

## Sub lesson 1 — Water and air
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say why life is impossible without water and without air, and name pollution as the main trouble of both.

## Sub lesson 2 — Plants and forests
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the forest's three gifts (food, fuel, building wood) and its trouble — heavy depletion in Ethiopia and the world.

## Sub lesson 3 — Wildlife
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name wildlife's three values (scientific, economic, beauty), say its numbers are falling, and name the walia ibex and mountain nyala as Ethiopia's endangered treasures.

## Sub lesson 4 — Soil and erosion
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what soil is (the living/non-living mixture of the crust's top) and explain erosion — rain washing soil away, worst on bare land — with topsoil as the layer plants feed on.
