# Water, Air, Forest, Wildlife, Soil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the main trouble of water?
a) Erosion  b) Pollution  c) Falling numbers
**Answer: b) Pollution.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the main trouble of the air?
a) Pollution  b) Depletion  c) Erosion
**Answer: a) Pollution** — smoke and fumes mixed into the air.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the forest's three gifts?
a) Food  b) Fuel  c) Rain-making machines
**Answer: c)** — the gifts are food, fuel and building wood.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The walia ibex and the mountain nyala are —
a) farm animals found everywhere  b) Ethiopian wildlife whose numbers are endangered  c) kinds of trees
**Answer: b)** — Ethiopia's own treasures, and endangered.

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** What is erosion?
a) Rain washing the soil away  b) Waste poured into a river  c) Smoke rising into the air
**Answer: a) Rain washing the soil away.**

**6. (Sub lesson 4)** Which land loses the most soil in a hard storm?
a) Land covered with grass and trees  b) Bare land  c) Land under a roof
**Answer: b) Bare land** — cover holds the soil; bare soil washes.
