# Water, Air, Forest, Wildlife, Soil — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For the water nearest your home: name one cause of its pollution and one remedy people there could carry out.
**Answer:** Any true local pair — e.g. cause: waste thrown into the river; remedy: keep waste away from the bank and protect the spring or river edge. (Cause and remedy must fit the locality named.)

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three gifts of plants and forests.
**Answer:** Food, fuel, and wood for building.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three kinds of value wildlife carries.
**Answer:** Scientific value, economic value, and the value of its beauty.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is topsoil, and why does losing it hurt a farm?
**Answer:** Topsoil is the dark, rich top layer of the soil — the layer plants feed on. Losing it to erosion leaves the field thin and poor, so crops grow badly.
