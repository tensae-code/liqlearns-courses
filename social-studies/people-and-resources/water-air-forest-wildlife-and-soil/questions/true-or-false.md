# Water, Air, Forest, Wildlife, Soil — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Life is possible without water, as long as there is air.
**Answer: False** — life is impossible without water, and impossible without air.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Forests are being heavily depleted in Ethiopia and in the world.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Wildlife has only beauty value and no other.
**Answer: False** — wildlife carries scientific, economic and beauty value.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Soil is purely non-living material.
**Answer: False** — soil is a mixture of living and non-living things, at the top of the crust.
