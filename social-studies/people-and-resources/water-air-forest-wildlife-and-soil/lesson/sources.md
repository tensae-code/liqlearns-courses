Angola's own way (Grade 6): a resources-and-environment year — every world topic (air, water, soils, forests) is closed with the Angolan case: Angola's fresh-water use, Angola's farm land, Angola's forest areas. That way is stocked here as the way Angola teaches this thing.

Turkmenistan's own way (Grade 10): A classic post-Soviet economic-and-social geography of the whole world in one book: Part I general world (political map, resources, population, world economy by sector), Part II the same template region by region and country by country, Part III global problems — with Turkmenistan's own memberships and ecological policy woven into the questions.

Samoa's own way (Year 9, Book 1): Geography is taught through Samoa's own resources first — environment, land, rainforest, water and marine resources of Samoa itself, then how resources are shared out in Samoan community life.

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Social Studies): one integrated Social Studies book per grade weaving History, Geography, Civics and Culture strands; Geography is taught through Eritrea itself first (Eritrea's location and size, Eritrean settlement, migration and disasters) with very practical, activity-led lessons.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 3 Amasomo Mbonezamubano SB): P3 moves the home ground up one rung of Rwanda's administrative ladder, from the cell to the SECTOR (Umurenge) — and it is here, at age 8–9, that formal map work begins: the sector map of Gikomero, the district map of Gasabo, symbols, scale, and the sectors that share a border with ours.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies SB): the pupil's book teaches the province through named Rwandan things — the five provinces with Kigali City as the case study, Rwanda's four seasons by their Kinyarwanda names (Umuhindo, Urugaryi, Itumba, Icyi), and the country's two international airports (Kigali and Kamembe) in the transport-places lesson.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 2 G2 CS Viva Venezuela): population education (UNESCO population-education guides reworked) woven into story form — Tío Conejo tales, grandparents' memories and the child's own school and street carry population, housing, landscape and weather-sign content.

Moldova's own way (Moldova Grade 11 G11 Geography): a human geography of the world year — political map and state typology, natural resources by sphere, population and settlements, then the world economy branch by branch (agriculture, each industry, services, transport, tourism), closing with economic-geographic characterisations of 14 states on every continent (Egypt among them) written to one template, plus case studies including labour migration at local/regional/global scale and using Google Maps/Google Earth as geo-information portals.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 6 np-g6): Grade 6 has a full "Hamro Prithvi" (Our Earth) unit — Earth's introduction, latitude/longitude, outer-layer structure, then Nepal itself: geographical description, climate, rivers/glaciers/lakes, natural vegetation, natural resources and map study, closing with disaster management; a population unit follows (demography, the census). Economic activities are organised by region and resource: agriculture and industry, services and trade, tourism, and economic resources (land and water; forest, minerals and people).

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 np-g7): Grade 7 Earth science is motion-first — Earth's daily and annual motion, surface form and the factors that change it, surface form's relation to human life — then Nepal's climate, the seven provinces of federal Nepal, climate change, disasters and map work; population is taught as structure, change and management.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 TG np-g7tg) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide teaches the same Grade 7 course as np-g7 with globe-and-ball demonstrations of rotation/revolution, chart work on Nepal's demographic structure (age, sex, religion, caste, language distributions), and discussion-led lessons; its geography content mirrors the student book lesson for lesson, so placements repeat with guide page references. That evidence is stocked here as the way Nepal teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Nepal is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).
