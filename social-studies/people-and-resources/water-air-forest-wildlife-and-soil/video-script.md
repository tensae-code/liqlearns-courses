# Video script — Water, Air, Forest, Wildlife, Soil

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five gifts. Five troubles. Walk with me.

**Water.** No life without it. Its trouble: **pollution**.
**Air.** You breathe it now. Its trouble: **pollution** too.

**The forest.** Food, fuel, wood to build.
Its trouble: it is being **cut down** — heavily, here and everywhere.

**Wild animals.** They teach us. They bring travellers.
They are beautiful. Their trouble: their **numbers are falling**.
The walia ibex. The mountain nyala. Ours — and in danger.

**Soil.** The top of the Earth, alive and not-alive mixed.
Its trouble: **erosion**. Rain comes — and washes it away.
One storm can carry off the best layer, the topsoil.

Now you. Five gifts. Name the five troubles.
