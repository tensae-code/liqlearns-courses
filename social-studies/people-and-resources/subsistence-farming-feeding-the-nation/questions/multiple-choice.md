# Subsistence farming — feeding the nation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsistence farming is worked by —
a) estate companies  b) urban workers  c) smallholder families
**Answer: c) smallholder families**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rain-fed means —
a) flood farming  b) dependent on the seasons’ rains  c) fully irrigated
**Answer: b) dependent on the seasons’ rains**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Subsistence harvests feed —
a) the households and the country  b) only the family  c) only export markets
**Answer: a) the households and the country**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The backbone is —
a) a few large estates  b) imported food  c) millions of subsistence smallholders
**Answer: c) millions of subsistence smallholders**
