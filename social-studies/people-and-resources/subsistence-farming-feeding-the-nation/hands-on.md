# Hands-on — Subsistence farming — feeding the nation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Holding portrait
Draw one subsistence holding from the lesson: plots, oxen, enset or coffee corner, the house. Label the three character marks where they show in your drawing.

## Rain-fed risk
With a partner, walk one failed-rain year through a subsistence household month by month. Mark where mixed livestock saves the family — and where it cannot.
