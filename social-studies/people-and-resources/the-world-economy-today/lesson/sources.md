**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Honduras, Dominican Republic).**

Dominican Republic's own way (Dominican Republic Grade 8 Ciencias Sociales 2° Secundaria): development is taught as a world divided in two — the industrialised rich North against the previously colonised South — and Latin America's underdevelopment is explained from inside the South, with external debt, corruption, the technology gap and missing regional integration named as its marks.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 9 Student Book): widening-circles design — the world continent by continent — hazards and geographic defence first (Hurricane Mitch), territory versus population, then geographic tours of Europe, Asia, Africa and Oceania, closing on a warming world; Bloque 4 adds world population, the world and Honduran economies and migration. Every lesson runs the same 'Sembrar y cosechar' activity cycle.
