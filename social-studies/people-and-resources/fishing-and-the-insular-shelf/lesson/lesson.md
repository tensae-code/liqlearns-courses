## Sub lesson 1 — Fishing And The Insular Shelf

Cuba's book, the *Cuba Grade 6 Geografía de Cuba G6* (printed p. 30), teaches: Capítulo 2 La plataforma insular: su importancia económica y la pesca (combinados pesqueros, especies de plataforma). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 6 Geografía de Cuba G6): The child's first geography book is the home country itself — one chapter of tools (coordinates, orientation, scale), then Cuba whole (situation, relief, coasts, climate, population, economy), then its two great regions landscape by landscape; the political-administrative division taught is the pre-2011 one (Ciudad de La Habana appears as a province), and History episodes woven into the regional chapters (the Santa Clara battle, the Granma landing) are named here but not placed — they belong to the History course.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 6 Cuaderno de actividades G6): The workbook is the practice twin of the G6 student book and the Atlas Escolar — nearly every activity is done ON a map (tracing coordinates, shading the insular shelf, locating features on outline maps) — and it is where the programme's fifth unit, "El paisaje de la localidad", actually lives: the student book has no locality chapter, but the workbook closes with sketching, describing and defending the child's own locality. Section 2 is pure tools: outline maps of the world and of Cuba plus graph paper. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
