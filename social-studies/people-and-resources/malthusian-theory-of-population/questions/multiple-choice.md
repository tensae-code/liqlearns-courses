# Malthusian theory of population — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About malthus's theory — population outruns food — the lesson teaches that —
a) Malthus taught that population tends to grow faster than the food supply  b) the theory's points are population growth, slower subsistence growth, and the checks  c) the theory links to diminishing returns — more labour on fixed land adds less output
