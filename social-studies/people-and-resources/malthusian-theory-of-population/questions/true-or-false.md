# Malthusian theory of population — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Malthus taught that food always grows faster than population.
**Answer: False** — his theory is the opposite — population outruns food.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The theory has no checks in it.
**Answer: False** — the checks are one of its main points.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Diminishing returns means each added worker adds more output.
**Answer: False** — each added worker adds less — that is diminishing.
