# Malthusian theory of population — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Malthus teach about population and food?
**Answer:** Population tends to grow faster than the food supply, pressing against the means of feeding people.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the main points of Malthus's theory as the book sets them out?
**Answer:** Population growth, the slower growth of subsistence, and the checks that press population back.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does Malthus's theory link to diminishing returns?
**Answer:** On fixed land, each added worker adds less output — the farm-side form of the pressure Malthus described.
