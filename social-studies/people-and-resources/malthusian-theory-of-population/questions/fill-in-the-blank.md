# Malthusian theory of population — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Malthus's theory, population tends to grow faster than the _____ supply.
**Answer: food**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When population outruns food, the theory names _____ that press it back.
**Answer: checks**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** More labour on fixed land adding less output is the law of _____ returns.
**Answer: diminishing**
