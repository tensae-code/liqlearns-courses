## Sub lesson 1 — Malthus's theory — population outruns food

In the Class 11 production chapter, the book teaches **Malthus's theory of population**: population tends to grow faster than the food supply, so unless it is checked, people press against the means of feeding them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The main points of the theory

The book sets out the theory's main points in order: the growth of population, the slower growth of subsistence, and the checks that press population back when it outruns food.

## Sub lesson 3 — Its link to diminishing returns

The book places the theory inside the production chapter because of its link to the **law of diminishing returns**: as more labour is applied to fixed land, each added worker adds less output — the same pressure Malthus described, seen from the farm's side.
