# Hands-on — Irans Water Resources Rivers And Springs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 6 Iran's water resources (منابع آب ایران): activity — imagine there is no water in your living place from morning to night: what problems?; explain the stages of a river's forming from the picture; sometimes in some regions the ground waters find their way to the surface by themselves and make the springs (چشمه‌ها); most of Iran's rivers rise in the high ranges of the Alborz and Zagros; activities: what do villagers use river water for, and what do city people use it for?; and what things pollute the water (the factory pictures), with proposals for using it properly. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
