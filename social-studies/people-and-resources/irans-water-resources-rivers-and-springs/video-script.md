The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 5 Social Studies* (printed p. 29), teaches: Lesson 6 Iran's water resources (منابع آب ایران): activity — imagine there is no water in your living place from morning to night: what problems?; explain the stages of a river's forming from the picture; sometimes in some regions the ground waters find their way to the surface by themselves and make the springs (چشمه‌ها); most of Iran's rivers rise in the high ranges of the Alborz and Zagros; activities: what do villagers use river water for, and what do city people use it for?; and what things pollute the water (the factory pictures), with proposals for using it properly.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 5 Social Studies): a primary Social Studies book of 21 lessons in 5 chapters.
