# Hands-on — Clothes From Production To Consumption

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 16 Clothes, from production to consumption (لباس از تولید تا مصرف): clothes production in past and present — mass production (تولید انبوه): a sewing workshop needs cloth, cloth is made of yarn, yarn may be natural or synthetic and goes to the spinning and textile (نساجی) factories; in the workshop all work together, each part doing a share of the production until the good is ready; and buying clothes — how to choose and buy the right clothes, and that sometimes there is no need to buy new ones. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
