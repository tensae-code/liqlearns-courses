# What counts as a natural resource — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Natural resources are —
a) only minerals  b) factory products  c) useful materials of the environment
**Answer: c) useful materials of the environment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A renewable resource —
a) is always abiotic  b) replaces itself if used with care  c) never runs out whatever we do
**Answer: b) replaces itself if used with care**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Biotic resources come from —
a) living things  b) rocks  c) water only
**Answer: a) living things**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Abiotic resources come from —
a) animals  b) plants  c) the non-living world
**Answer: c) the non-living world**
