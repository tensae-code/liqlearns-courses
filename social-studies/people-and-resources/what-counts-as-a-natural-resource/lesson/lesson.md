# What counts as a natural resource

## Do it first

Water, soil, forest, gold: four riches of one country. What makes them one family — resources? Read the meaning and the two classifications.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meaning and first classification

Natural resources are the useful materials of the environment — things people take and use from nature. They class first by renewal: renewable resources replace themselves if used with care (water, forests, soils under good husbandry); non-renewable ones (minerals, fossil fuels) do not replace within human time.

## Sub lesson 2 — Second classification, and the pressure

They class second by origin: biotic resources come from living things; abiotic from the non-living world. The guide’s keys add biotic-resource uses such as raw materials and energy uses (teacher guide — evidence). Ethiopia presses heavily on its resources, and the resulting degradation frames the whole unit.
