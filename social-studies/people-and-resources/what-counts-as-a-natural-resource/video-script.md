# Video script — What counts as a natural resource

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A resource is nature made useful.
Some renew — water, forest, careful soil. Some do not — minerals, fuels.
Some are living-born, biotic. Some are not, abiotic.
And Ethiopia presses hard on all of them.
