# Hands-on — What counts as a natural resource

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Resource wall sort
Make a wall chart of twelve Ethiopian resources and pin each under two labels — renewable or not, biotic or abiotic. Defend the hard cases (soil, water) to a partner.

## Pressure diary
For one day, list every resource your household uses and where its pressure shows — the woodpile, the water queue, the eroding plot.
