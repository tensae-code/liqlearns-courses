# Video script — Rural Problems And Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 31), teaches: Lesson 2: The problems of villages (economic problems first, on the UN's reports) and rural development — the measures tried in the world, land reform and dividing land among them; rural development in Iran: about 62,000 villages in the 1390 (2011) statistics; after the Islamic Revolution, programmes to remove deprivation; 56,000 km of asphalted rural roads by 1385 (2006) and still growing.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
