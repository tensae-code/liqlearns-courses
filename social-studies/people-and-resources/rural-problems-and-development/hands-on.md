# Hands-on — Rural Problems And Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 2: The problems of villages (economic problems first, on the UN's reports) and rural development — the measures tried in the world, land reform and dividing land among them; rural development in Iran: about 62,000 villages in the 1390 (2011) statistics; after the Islamic Revolution, programmes to remove deprivation; 56,000 km of asphalted rural roads by 1385 (2006) and still growing. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
