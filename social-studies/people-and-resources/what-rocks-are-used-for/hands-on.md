# Hands-on — What Rocks Are Used For

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Uses of rocks: cement industry (gypsum, limestone), salt in daily life, chalk in schools, marble decoration and utensils, coal in industry, jewellery and coins. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
