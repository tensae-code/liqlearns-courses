# Case study: living with the Baro River floods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Itang stands on which river?
a) the Baro  b) the Blue Nile  c) the Awash
**Answer: a) the Baro**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Major floods came almost yearly in —
a) 2006–2012  b) 1990–1995  c) 2015–2020
**Answer: a) 2006–2012**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In 2007, households losing crops severely were —
a) 94%  b) 51%  c) 9.4%
**Answer: a) 94%**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A long-term defence is —
a) raised floors  b) early harvest  c) selling stock
**Answer: a) raised floors**
