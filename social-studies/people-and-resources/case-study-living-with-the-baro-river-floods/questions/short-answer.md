# Case study: living with the Baro River floods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where is Itang, and on what river?
**Answer:** In Gambella, on the Baro River.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the 2007 loss figures.
**Answer:** 94% lost crops severely; 51% lost livestock; dairy output halved.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two short-term and two long-term defences.
**Answer:** Short: moving property/animals to relatives, early harvest, selling stock. Long: ditches, raised floors, boundary walls (two of each).
