# Video script — Case study: living with the Baro River floods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Agropastoralist households on the Baro River in Itang, Gambella, faced a major flood almost every year from 2006 to 2012.

In the 2007 flood, 94 percent of surveyed households lost crops severely and 51 percent lost livestock, with dairy output halved.

Short-term defences: moving property and animals to relatives, early harvest, selling stock.
