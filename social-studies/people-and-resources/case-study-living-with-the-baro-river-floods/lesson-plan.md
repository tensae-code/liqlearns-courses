# Lesson plan — Case study: living with the Baro River floods

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** People and Resources · **Lesson:** Case study: living with the Baro River floods
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 11 Geography, Unit 5 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In Itang, Gambella, Baro River floods hit agropastoralist households almost yearly from 2006 to 2012; in 2007, 94% lost crops severely and 51% lost livestock.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in People and Resources, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
