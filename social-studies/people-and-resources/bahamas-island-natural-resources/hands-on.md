# Hands-on — Bahamas Island Natural Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Natural Resources: the resources each Bahamian island is known for — clear waters, coral reefs and pine forests; Abaco for farming; Andros for crabs; Grand Bahama for citrus fruit, onions, corn, sponge, cement and medicine; Food & Natural Resources: the resources pictured and named for The Bahamas — cascarilla, aragonite and salt; Marine Resources: The Bahamas is an archipelago whose surrounding water provides marine resources, coral reefs, beaches and food that support its economy; aragonite, a fine calcium carbonate, is mined off the shores of Bimini. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahamas' way
Say how the Bahamas' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
