# Bahamas Island Natural Resources — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Natural Resources: the resources each Bahamian island is known for — clear waters, coral reefs and pine forests; Abaco for farming; Andros for crabs; Grand Bahama for citrus fruit, onions, corn, sponge, cement and medicine; Food & Natural Resources: the resources pictured and named for The Bahamas — cascarilla, aragonite and salt; Marine Resources: The Bahamas is an archipelago whose surrounding water provides marine resources, coral reefs, beaches and food that support its economy; aragonite, a fine calcium carbonate, is mined off the shores of Bimini.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on the Bahamas' student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
