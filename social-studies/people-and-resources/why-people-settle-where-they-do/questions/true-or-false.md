# Why People Settle Where They Do — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population distribution is uneven because settlement is tied directly to resources.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The equator's dense forest is the most thickly settled land on Earth.
**Answer: False** — the book sets this as its puzzle: the densest forest is thinly settled; vegetation alone does not settle a place.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A kind climate holds settlement medium to dense.
**Answer: True.**
