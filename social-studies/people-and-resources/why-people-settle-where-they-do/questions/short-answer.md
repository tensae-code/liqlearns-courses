# Why People Settle Where They Do — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did the ancient civilisations settle on great rivers?
**Answer:** Because water serves power, farming and drinking — a river valley could feed and sustain a settled people.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain the equator puzzle: the densest forest on Earth, yet thinly settled.
**Answer:** Vegetation alone does not settle a place. Dense forest is hard to clear and farm, hard to move through, and the other controllers (soil, water's usability, climate's pressures) do not all pull the same way — so settlement stays thin despite the green cover. (Any reasoned answer using the controllers together earns the mark.)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A dry hill with poor soil and fierce heat stands empty, while the river plain below it is full of villages. Explain with two controllers.
**Answer:** The plain has water and fertile soil (answers may also use vegetation/climate); the hill lacks water and its soil is poor, and its climate is harsh — so settlement follows the resources to the plain.
