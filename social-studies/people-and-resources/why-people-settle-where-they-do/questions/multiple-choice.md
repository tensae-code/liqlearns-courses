# Why People Settle Where They Do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ancient civilisations settled on which rivers?
a) Nile, Euphrates, Tigris and Indus  b) Amazon, Congo, Niger and Volta  c) Thames, Rhine, Danube and Seine
**Answer: a) Nile, Euphrates, Tigris and Indus.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Water serves a settlement in three ways. Which set is right?
a) Power, farming and drinking  b) Fishing, swimming and skating  c) Trade, war and travel only
**Answer: a) Power, farming and drinking.**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A place with fertile soil is —
a) avoided by settlers  b) chosen by settlers  c) always left for animals
**Answer: b) chosen by settlers** — fertile soil can feed a settlement.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A harsh climate does what to settlement?
a) Makes it dense  b) Thins it out  c) Has no effect
**Answer: b) Thins it out** — kind climates hold settlement medium to dense; harsh ones thin it.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is the right list of the four controllers of settlement?
a) Roads, markets, schools, hospitals  b) Water, soil, vegetation, climate  c) Kings, armies, traders, priests
**Answer: b) Water, soil, vegetation, climate.**
