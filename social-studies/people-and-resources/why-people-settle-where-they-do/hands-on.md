# Hands-on — Why People Settle Where They Do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Interview the oldest person you can
Ask an elder of your family or neighbourhood: why was this settlement founded here? What water, what soil, what grazing or forest, what weather did the founders find? Write the answer down and mark which of the four controllers each reason belongs to.

## The empty-place survey
Walk or look over one thinly settled place near you — a dry hill, a stony patch, a flooded corner. Score it on the four controllers. Which controller fails it most? Could any remedy change its score (a well, a terrace, a planted wood)? Write the verdict in two sentences.

## Settlement map of your walk to school
Draw your walk to school as a simple map. Mark every house cluster and every empty stretch. Under each empty stretch, write the controller that keeps it empty; under each cluster, the controller that gathered it.
