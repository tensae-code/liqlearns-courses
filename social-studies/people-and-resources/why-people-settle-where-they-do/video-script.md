# Video script — Why People Settle Where They Do

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why is this valley full of villages?
Why is that plain empty?

People settle where the resources are.
Four things decide. Count them with me.

**Water.** The first towns grew on rivers —
the Nile, the Euphrates, the Tigris, the Indus.
Water to drink. Water for fields. Water for power.

**Soil.** Rich soil feeds a town. Poor soil starves it.

**Plants.** Wood, grazing, shade — green land calls people.

**Climate.** Harsh land — few people.
Kind land — people stay, and grow.

Four controllers. One map of people.

Now you. Take your own town.
Which of the four made it stand there?
