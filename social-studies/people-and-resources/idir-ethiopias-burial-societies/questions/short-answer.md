# Idir — Ethiopia's burial societies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Indigenous knowledge at work" in your own words.
**Answer:** Indigenous knowledge is locally developed know-how a community uses to solve its own problems; Idir is Ethiopia's flagship example.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "How Idir works" in your own words.
**Answer:** Idir's small regular contributions guarantee a member's family full support at a death — group life insurance.
