# Hands-on — Idir — Ethiopia's burial societies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Run an Idir of ten
Simulate an Idir of ten members contributing equally each month. One member's family faces a funeral: show the fund paying out, then compute the monthly contribution that rebuilds it.

## Insurance or Idir
Set Idir beside a commercial life-insurance policy: what each collects, what each pays, and what Idir adds that no policy sells — the crowd, the attendance, the obligation.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
