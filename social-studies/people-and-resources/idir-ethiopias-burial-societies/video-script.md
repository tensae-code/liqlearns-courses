# Video script — Idir — Ethiopia's burial societies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Indigenous knowledge is the accumulated, locally developed know-how a community uses to solve its own problems. Ethiopia's flagship example is Idir — proof that the solutions to poverty are not all imported.

Idir is an association of neighbours, workmates or friends whose small weekly or monthly contributions guarantee a member's family complete financial and social support at a death. In effect it is group life insurance, with obligatory funeral attendance, found in villages, workplaces and kin groups alike.
