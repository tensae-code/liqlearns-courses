## Sub lesson 2 — Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 5 np-g5): Grade 5 "Our Earth" is a first full geography of Nepal — physical features, climate and lifestyle, natural vegetation, soils, map-drawing with symbols, globe and maps, and the wider South Asian neighbours; economic geography is district-based (Sankhuwasabha's occupations mapped place by place, from yak and sheep rearing and herb collecting in the high Himalaya to paddy in the low valleys).

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 5 TG np-g5tg) (teacher guide — evidence): Guide to the new-curriculum Grade 5 book (companion to the trial-edition line, not to the older English np-g5): its "Hamro Prithvi" unit teaches the solar system and Earth's structure, then Nepal's natural form, climate, vegetation, population and social life, local disasters, and map-making — learners draw their own province's map showing their district and local level and study the Nepal map. Economic unit works from local economic activities and locally produced goods (and the markets that sell them) up to the country's major economic activities across Himalayan, Hilly and Terai regions. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
