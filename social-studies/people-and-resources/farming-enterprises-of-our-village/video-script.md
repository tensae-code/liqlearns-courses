# Video script — Farming Enterprises of Our Village

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar Social Studies Grade 3 (printed p. 24), teaches: The village's farming work as its living — what is grown, where, and how the work is organised.

Myanmar's own way (Book 6): Grade 3 studies one real town (Nyaungdon, on the Pan Hlaing river) and one real village (Zee Hpyu Kyun) from big picture maps, then interviews a real home-industry owner — the town's banana-chip maker, with her 35 workers, their wages and their problems-and-solutions poster.
