Bhutan's book, the *Bhutan Grade 6 Social Studies* (printed p. 38), teaches: 7.1 Hydropower. Bhutan's book, the *Bhutan Grade 12 Geography* (printed p. 115), teaches: Ch.11 11.2 Hydropower in Bhutan.

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 6 Social Studies): Grade 6 turns to Bhutan's own climate zones, population and signature environment topics — hydropower, urbanisation and glacial lake outburst floods (GLOF) — a hazard set specific to a Himalayan kingdom.

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 12 Geography): Class XII closes the Advanced course with specialist topics (surveying, geospatial technologies, glaciers and the "Third Pole", Köppen in full, fluvial processes, Davis's cycle of erosion) and Bhutan data chapters — fertility and mortality trends in Bhutan, migration trends, hydropower in Bhutan, and creative industries including Bhutanese film.
