## Sub lesson 1 — Could Brazil be different? — the opening question

Brazil's Grade 10 Geography book opens with a provocative question — Folha 1: O Brasil podia ser diferente? / Could Brazil be different? It does not start from the map as a fixed fact; it asks whether the Brazil on the map had to be this way.

## Sub lesson 2 — How Brazilian territory was formed

The Folha teaches the formation of Brazilian territory: the country's territory was formed over time, so its present shape and extent are a result, not a given. That is why the opening question can be asked at all — a territory that was formed could have been formed differently.

Brazil's book has no chapters in the old style: it is 17 "Folhas" (sheets) like this one, each opening with a provocative question, grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
