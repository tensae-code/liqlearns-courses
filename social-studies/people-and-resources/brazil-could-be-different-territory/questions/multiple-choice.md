# Brazil Could Be Different — Territory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Folha 1 of Brazil's Grade 10 book opens with —
a) O Brasil podia ser diferente? / Could Brazil be different?  b) a chapter number and no question  c) an exam timetable
**Answer: a) O Brasil podia ser diferente? / Could Brazil be different?**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) the formation of Brazilian territory  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the formation of Brazilian territory**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second sub lesson is —
a) How Brazilian territory was formed  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) How Brazilian territory was formed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Brazil's Grade 10 Geography book is organised as —
a) 17 Folhas (sheets), each opening with a provocative question  b) old-style chapters only  c) a dictionary
**Answer: a) 17 Folhas (sheets), each opening with a provocative question**
