# Hands-on — Brazil Could Be Different — Territory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ask the opening question
Look at a map of Brazil and write, in your own words, one way its territory could have been different, as Folha 1 asks: O Brasil podia ser diferente?

## Trace the formation
Write a short account of how Brazilian territory was formed, in the order the lesson gives: formed over time, not given all at once.
