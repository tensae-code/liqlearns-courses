# Growth is not development — the HDI — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the growth/development difference in one sentence.
**Answer:** Growth is more output regardless of welfare; development is sustained improvement in well-being.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the HDI's three dimensions.
**Answer:** Longevity, knowledge, standard of living.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Where does Ethiopia stand?
**Answer:** 0.485, rank 173 of 189.
