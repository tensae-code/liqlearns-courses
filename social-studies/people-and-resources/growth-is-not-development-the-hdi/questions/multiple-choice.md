# Growth is not development — the HDI — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic growth means —
a) more output, with no regard to welfare  b) better health for all  c) longer lives
**Answer: a) more output, with no regard to welfare**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Development is measured in well-being: education, health, housing, sanitation and —
a) work  b) exports  c) armies
**Answer: a) work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The HDI's three dimensions are longevity, knowledge and —
a) standard of living  b) latitude  c) rainfall
**Answer: a) standard of living**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia's HDI in the table is —
a) 0.485, rank 173 of 189  b) 0.985, rank 3  c) 0.185, rank 189
**Answer: a) 0.485, rank 173 of 189**
