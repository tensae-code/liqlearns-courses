**Acceptance count: taught in 9 countries (Ethiopia, Brazil, France, Barbados, Costa Rica, Mongolia, Indonesia, Dominican Republic, Samoa). Argentina is named here as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere. Haiti is named here as teacher-sequence evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Indonesia's own way (Grade 12): Grade 12 is applied human geography built entirely on Indonesian cases — regional development and spatial planning (tata ruang) measured against a Happiness Index, Industry 4.0 / Society 5.0, and Indonesia's own geopolitics and ASEAN-era cooperation as pillars of national resilience. That way is stocked here as the way Indonesia teaches this thing.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 11): the final year is the state-and-world year — cultural globalization and freedom, monetary policy, foreign trade and investment, and Mongolia's signature “third neighbour” and soft-power policies taught as named topics. The book's Sociology part (Chapter I Topic 1.2, printed p. 29) and Law/Civics parts (Chapter III, printed pp. 71–103; Chapter V, printed pp. 139–165; Chapter VI except the placed topics, printed pp. 167–176) are named in the placements, not placed. That way is stocked here as the way Mongolia teaches this thing.

Haiti's own way (teacher sequence — evidence): World inequality is questioned through the words used for it (Nord/Sud, développé/sous-développé) and through Haiti's own case — a rich-country/poor-country comparison (Canada or USA vs Haiti), the independence debt, food insecurity and Covid vaccination in Haiti — with every claim built from authenticated statistics, graphs and maps crossed from local to world scale. That evidence is stocked here as the way Haiti teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project. That way is stocked here as the way Barbados teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Argentina's own way (teacher guide — evidence): quality of life is taught through statistics and graphs — the Argentine population's quality of life is read from its numbers and charts, in a teacher's notebook whose worked proposals are built on statistics, graphs, thematic maps and newspaper articles. That evidence is stocked here as the way Argentina teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

No book error is flagged for this lesson's pages in the placements. The student book's body numbering for Unit Two drifts from its own contents (regions printed as 2.4, factors as 2.5, local classification as 2.6, where the contents and the teacher guide's contents agree on 2.3, 2.4 and 2.5); this lesson follows the contents numbering and teaches no misprint.

Costa Rica's own way: the CONED 11 anthology opens with a Geography unit on globalisation read "from the geographic viewpoint" (spatial flows of production and consumption, global cities, space-time compression), then turns to Costa Rica's own 20th-century history; its commercial workbook twin, Porras y Gamboa's *Estudios Sociales 11*, teaches the same ministry programme as a fixed cycle (problem question, basic concepts, my previous ideas, widening my knowledge, I apply my knowledge, problem-based learning, exam preparation) with heavy map-work and Costa Rica-specific data: ICE internet-coverage map, services-export league table, Costa Rica's geo-strategic bridge position between North/South America and the Caribbean. That way is stocked here as the way Costa Rica teaches this thing.

Samoa's own way (Year 9, Book 3): Culture and development are taught comparatively — Samoan cultural treasures and symbols first, then development measured and lived through India and Samoa side by side, with map-profile work on both countries.
