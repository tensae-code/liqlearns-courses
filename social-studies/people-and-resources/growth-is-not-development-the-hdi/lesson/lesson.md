# Growth is not development — the HDI

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Growth versus development

Economic growth is more output — a bigger GDP — with no regard to welfare. Development is sustained improvement in material well-being: education, health, housing, sanitation and work. A country can grow without developing.

## Sub lesson 2 — The HDI — development's ruler

The Human Development Index scores three dimensions, each scaled 0 to 1: longevity, knowledge and standard of living. One number, three human questions: how long do people live, how much do they learn, how well do they live?
