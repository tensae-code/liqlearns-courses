Economic growth is more output — a bigger GDP — with no regard to welfare.

The Human Development Index scores three dimensions, each scaled 0 to 1: longevity, knowledge and standard of living.

The 2019 table's top is Norway, Ireland and Switzerland; its bottom is Niger, the Central African Republic and Chad.
