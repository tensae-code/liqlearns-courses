# Hands-on — Mineral Rich Areas And Rare Minerals Of Pakistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Areas of Pakistan rich in mineral resources (Reko Diq copper-gold, Tharparkar, Potwar, Shahrag, Quetta, Sibi) and rare minerals: ruby, topaz and emerald gemstones, polished for export. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
