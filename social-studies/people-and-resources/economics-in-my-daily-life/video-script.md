# Video script — Economics in My Daily Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 7 book opens its economy chapter at the scale of the child's own life: economics in my life. The topic shows that economics is not only banks and markets — it is the choices a person makes every day about what to use, what to save and what to give up, because no one can have everything at once.

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the Grade 7 year works at the scale of the child's own life — my groups, my culture, my family's economy and budget, our school — before the local-area year in Grade 8. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
