**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Mongolia, Yemen, Nepal).**

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 3 Social Studies & Creative Arts G3): economics enters through the child's own shopping and village — a Bhailo gift of Rs. 100 spent from a written needs list with a priced bill and change kept, and village industries (Dhaka cloth, tea) taught through the chain: use local products, more people get employment, income rises, the village becomes rich.

Yemen's own way (Grade 3 Al-Tarbiya al-Ijtima'iya): economics begins as the jobs on your own street — a profession is the work a person does every day, and each profession exists because it meets a need of the neighbours.

Mongolia's own way: the Grade 7 year works at the scale of the child's own life — my groups, my culture, my family's economy and budget, our school — before the local-area year in Grade 8.
