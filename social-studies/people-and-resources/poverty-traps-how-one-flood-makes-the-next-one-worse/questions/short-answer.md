# Poverty traps — how one flood makes the next one worse — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things that build vulnerability.
**Answer:** Weak governance, remoteness, insecure tenure, missing infrastructure and markets (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two short-term and two long-term impacts.
**Answer:** Short: lost income/assets, schooling abandoned. Long: lost land, food prices, debt, stunting, reduced resilience (two of each).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain the trap in one sentence.
**Answer:** Assets sold to survive each shock leave the household unable to recover before the next one.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Walk the trap circle once, station by station.
**Answer:** Low income → low saving and investment → low productivity → low income again.
