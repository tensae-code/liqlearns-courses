# Hands-on — Physiological and Agricultural Density

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shade the farmland
Draw a rectangle for a territory of 5,000 km². Shade exactly 20% of it (divide it into five equal strips and shade one) and label the shaded part "farmland — 1,000 km²". Write 20,000,000 dots' worth of people over the whole rectangle in words, then cross out the unshaded part: the people must live off the shaded strip. Write the physiological density beside it.

## Count a real field
With a farmer's help if you can, pace out one cultivated field near you and estimate its area. How many people work it, and how many mouths does its harvest feed? Work the field's own agricultural density in words: farming people on this cultivated land.

## The three-number report
For your kebele (ask a teacher, a parent or the kebele office for the figures you need): total people, total area, farmable share, rural share, cultivated share. Work all three densities and write the food-supply judgement in two sentences — what the crude number claims, and what the sharper two say.
