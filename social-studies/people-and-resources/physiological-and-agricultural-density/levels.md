# Physiological and Agricultural Density — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons run in the book's order: the
farmland measure, the cultivated-land measure, then the closing
problem that reads all three densities together.

## Sub lesson 1 — Physiological density
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the formula (total population ÷ farmland area), find a farmland area from a percentage of a total area, work the division, and judge the food pressure it shows.

## Sub lesson 2 — Agricultural density
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: state the formula (rural population ÷ cultivated land), and say what the measure is for — how crowded the farming people are on the land they actually work — against physiological density's food question.

## Sub lesson 3 — Judging food supply: the three densities together
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: work all three densities for the book's closing territory (crude 6, physiological 10, agricultural 12 per km²) and give the food-supply judgement the three numbers support together.
