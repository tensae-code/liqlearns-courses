# Physiological and Agricultural Density — ordering

Put each set in the right order.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the steps for working a physiological density in order:
a) Divide the total population by the farmland area
b) Find the farmland area (e.g. take the given percentage of the total area)
c) Judge what the answer says about food pressure

**Answer: b, a, c** — farmland first, then the division, then the judgement.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Put the closing territory's three densities in order from smallest to largest:
a) Agricultural density (12 per km²)
b) Crude density (6 per km²)
c) Physiological density (10 per km²)

**Answer: b, c, a** — crude 6, physiological 10, agricultural 12.
