# Physiological and Agricultural Density — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Physiological density = —
a) total population ÷ total area  b) total population ÷ farmland area  c) rural population ÷ cultivated land
**Answer: b) total population ÷ farmland area.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A zone of 20,000,000 people has 1,000 km² of farmland. Its physiological density is —
a) 2,000 per km²  b) 20,000 per km²  c) 200 per km²
**Answer: b) 20,000 per km²** — 20,000,000 ÷ 1,000 = 20,000.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Agricultural density = —
a) rural population ÷ cultivated land  b) total population ÷ farmland area  c) urban population ÷ total area
**Answer: a) rural population ÷ cultivated land.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What does agricultural density measure?
a) How much rain falls on the fields  b) How crowded the farming people are on the land they actually cultivate  c) How many towns a territory has
**Answer: b).**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** For the closing territory (600,000 people, 100,000 km², 60% farmable), the physiological density is —
a) 6 per km²  b) 10 per km²  c) 12 per km²
**Answer: b) 10 per km²** — farmland 60,000 km²; 600,000 ÷ 60,000 = 10.
