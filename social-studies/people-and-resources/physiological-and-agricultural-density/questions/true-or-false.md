# Physiological and Agricultural Density — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Physiological density divides the people by the total area of the territory.
**Answer: False** — it divides by the farmland area only.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Agricultural density counts all the people, town and country together.
**Answer: False** — it counts only the rural population, on the cultivated land.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A low crude density always means a territory's food supply is comfortable.
**Answer: False** — the closing territory's crude density is 6 (low), yet its physiological and agricultural densities show real pressure; crude density counts unfarmable land too.
