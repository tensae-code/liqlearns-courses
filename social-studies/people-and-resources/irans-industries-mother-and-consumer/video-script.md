Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 5 Social Studies* (printed p. 34), teaches: Lesson 7 Iran's important industrial regions (نواحی صنعتی مهم ایران): in the third-grade book you read that all the goods we use come from natural resources (منابع طبیعی), which are of different kinds, such as oil; in the distant past most people made the things they needed themselves, at home or in small workshops — sewing clothes for the family or weaving carpets; our country has different industries, and factory industries can be divided into two groups: mother industries (صنایع مادر), which make goods people do not use directly — machine-building factories making machinery and tools, steam boilers, cranes and the like for other factories — and consumer industries (صنایع مصرفی); the mother industries get the raw materials they need from the mines (معدن‌ها).
