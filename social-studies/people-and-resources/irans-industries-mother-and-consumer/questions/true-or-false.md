# Irans Industries Mother And Consumer — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Lesson 7 Iran's important industrial regions (نواحی صنعتی مهم ایران): in the third-grade book you read that all the goods we use come from natural resources (منابع طبیعی), which are of different kinds, such as oil; in the distant past most people made the things they needed themselves, at home or in small workshops — sewing clothes for the family or weaving carpets; our country has different industries, and factory industries can be divided into two groups: mother industries (صنایع مادر), which make goods people do not use directly — machine-building factories making machinery and tools, steam boilers, cranes and the like for other factories — and consumer industries (صنایع مصرفی); the mother industries get the raw materials they need from the mines (معدن‌ها).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iran's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
