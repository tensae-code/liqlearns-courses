# Quintiles, deciles and the Kuznets ratio — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The bottom 20% receive 5%, the second quintile 9%, the bottom 40% hold 14% and the top 20% hold 51%.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Kuznets ratio is 51/14 ≈ 3.64; the inequality ratio is 14/51 ≈ 0.27.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The bottom 10% receive 1.8%, the top 10% 28.5%, and the richest household alone 15%.
**Answer: True**
