# Quintiles, deciles and the Kuznets ratio — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Quintiles — the table read in fifths" in your own words.
**Answer:** The bottom 20% receive 5%, the second quintile 9%, the bottom 40% hold 14% and the top 20% hold 51%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The Kuznets ratio and its inverse" in your own words.
**Answer:** The Kuznets ratio is 51/14 ≈ 3.64; the inequality ratio is 14/51 ≈ 0.27.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Deciles sharpen it" in your own words.
**Answer:** The bottom 10% receive 1.8%, the top 10% 28.5%, and the richest household alone 15%.
