# Quintiles, deciles and the Kuznets ratio — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Quintiles — the table read in fifths"?
a) The bottom 20% receive 5%, the second quintile 9%, the bottom 40% hold 14% and the top 20% hold 51%  b) The Kuznets ratio is 51/14 ≈ 3.64; the inequality ratio is 14/51 ≈ 0.27  c) The bottom 10% receive 1.8%, the top 10% 28.5%, and the richest household alone 15%
**Answer: a) The bottom 20% receive 5%, the second quintile 9%, the bottom 40% hold 14% and the top 20% hold 51%**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The Kuznets ratio and its inverse"?
a) The Kuznets ratio is 51/14 ≈ 3.64; the inequality ratio is 14/51 ≈ 0.27  b) The bottom 10% receive 1.8%, the top 10% 28.5%, and the richest household alone 15%  c) The bottom 20% receive 5%, the second quintile 9%, the bottom 40% hold 14% and the top 20% hold 51%
**Answer: a) The Kuznets ratio is 51/14 ≈ 3.64; the inequality ratio is 14/51 ≈ 0.27**
