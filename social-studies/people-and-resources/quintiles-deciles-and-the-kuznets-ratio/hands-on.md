# Hands-on — Quintiles, deciles and the Kuznets ratio

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five bars
Draw five bars for the five quintile shares you know (5, 9, and 51 for the top, with the middle two to estimate between). Mark the bottom-40 total (14) and the top bar towering at 51.

## Ratio drill
Compute the Kuznets ratio and the inequality ratio from 51 and 14 with your own division, then explain in one sentence why both are reported: what each direction makes a reader feel.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
