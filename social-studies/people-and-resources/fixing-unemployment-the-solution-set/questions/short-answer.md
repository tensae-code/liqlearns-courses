# Fixing unemployment — the solution set — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three legs of the solution set.
**Answer:** Job creation; employability and skills; employment tied to sustainable development.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do skills and job creation belong together?
**Answer:** Skills without jobs strand people, and jobs without skills import workers — each leg fails without the other.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What makes employment sustainable in this lesson?
**Answer:** It does not consume the resource base it stands on, so today's jobs do not become tomorrow's unemployment.
