# Hands-on — Making things — craft and factory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Craft or factory?
List ten made things in your home. For each, decide: craft-made, factory-made at home, or imported? Present your count as a picture of the lesson’s sector.

## Cotton to cloth
Trace a cotton shirt backwards: factory sewing, weaving, spinning, cotton farming. Mark which steps are secondary and which step was primary.
