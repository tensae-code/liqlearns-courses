# Making things — craft and factory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Secondary activities —
a) grow crops  b) carry passengers  c) manufacture goods from materials
**Answer: c) manufacture goods from materials**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Weaving is —
a) a service  b) a traditional craft  c) a primary activity
**Answer: b) a traditional craft**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Factory branches named include —
a) food, textile, leather and cement  b) only food  c) mining and fishing
**Answer: a) food, textile, leather and cement**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The modern factory sector is —
a) the largest employer  b) evenly spread over the country  c) small and concentrated near the main towns
**Answer: c) small and concentrated near the main towns**
