# Making things — craft and factory

## Do it first

A weaver at a loom and a textile factory line make the same family of thing, centuries apart in method. Read Ethiopia’s making.

## Sub lesson 1 — Craft and factory

Secondary activities manufacture — they change materials into goods. Ethiopia has both kinds: traditional crafts, like weaving, practised in homes and workshops for centuries; and modern manufacturing in factories. The guide’s examples name steel from minerals, textiles from cotton and furniture from logging (teacher guide — evidence).

## Sub lesson 2 — A small, concentrated sector

Modern factories — food, textile, leather, cement and others — remain a small sector, concentrated in and around the main towns. The making economy is real but narrow: most Ethiopians still meet manufactured goods made by craft, or imported, rather than by a large home factory sector.
