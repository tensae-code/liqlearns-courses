# Hands-on — Water Sources On Earth And In Pakistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Major sources of water on Earth and in Pakistan: oceans (97% of water; desalination), rivers, lakes, glaciers and ice caps, ponds, springs, aquifers and rainwater. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
