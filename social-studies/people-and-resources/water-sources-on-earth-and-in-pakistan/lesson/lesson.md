## Sub lesson 1 — Water Sources On Earth And In Pakistan

## Sub lesson 2 — Pakistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 7 Geography 7): a dedicated English-medium Geography book built entirely on Pakistan's own physical and economic geography — the Indus plain and delta, the canal system, Rabi/Kharif crops, Sui gas, Thar coal, Khewra salt and Tarbela power — with world concepts (river courses, settlement hierarchy, greenhouse effect, mining types) taught through Pakistani examples first. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
