## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: China studied as a whole country — its visiting-card, capital and place, nature, population and economy — as Ukraine's Grade 10 *Geografiya* (Gilberg) teaches it in §21 on printed p. 125. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — China — the visiting-card study

Ukraine's Grade 10 book gives China a full country study in §21. Learners build the country's visiting-card — its capital and position — then work through its nature, its population and its primary, secondary and tertiary sectors in the book's fixed regional order, closing with what the book names as the pride of the country and its unique experience.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ukraine's way — regions and countries in one order

Ukraine learns this inside its "Geography: Regions and Countries" course: A "Geography: Regions and Countries" course — every region is done in the same order (position, nature/resources, population, primary/secondary/tertiary sectors) then typological country studies with rubrics (country visiting-card, capital, pride of the country, unique experience), ending with Ukraine's own place in the world; research tasks (Дослідження) embedded in chapters. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
