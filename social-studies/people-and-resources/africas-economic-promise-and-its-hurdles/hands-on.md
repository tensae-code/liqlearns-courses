# Hands-on — Africa's economic promise and its hurdles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The balance poster
Draw the balance from the picture: promise on one side, hurdles on the other. Under each hurdle write one sentence on what it costs an economy.

## Local promise, local hurdle
Name one resource and one working-age strength of your own locality — its small promise — and one hurdle it faces. Compare with a partner: is your hurdle on the book's list of four?
