# Africa's economic promise and its hurdles — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book presents Africa's resources as a promise of inclusive growth. True or false?
**Answer: True** — people and resources are the promise's substance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book names poor policy among the hurdles. True or false?
**Answer: True** — slave trade, colonialism, conflict and poor policy.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The hurdles mean the promise is false. True or false?
**Answer: False** — both stand; the hurdles are named, specific burdens.
