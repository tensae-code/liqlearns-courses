# Africa's economic promise and its hurdles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three parts of Africa's economic promise as the book frames it.
**Answer:** About 1.3 billion people; a large working-age population; rich human and natural resources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the four hurdles in the book's order.
**Answer:** The slave trade, colonialism, conflict, and poor policy.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can a continent be rich and poor at once, on this lesson's account?
**Answer:** Because endowment is not outcome: rich resources and many people promise growth, but the named historical burdens — slave trade, colonialism, conflict, poor policy — hold economies back.
