# Africa's economic promise and its hurdles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's population, as the book states it, is about —
a) 130 million  b) 1.3 billion  c) 13 billion  d) 300 million
**Answer: b) 1.3 billion** — with a large working-age share.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The promise side of the balance holds —
a) only oil  b) rich human and natural resources and a large working-age population  c) only gold  d) no resources
**Answer: b) rich human and natural resources and a large working-age population**.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the hurdles the book names?
a) the slave trade  b) colonialism  c) earthquakes  d) poor policy
**Answer: c) earthquakes** — the named hurdles are the slave trade, colonialism, conflict and poor policy.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Conflict is a hurdle because it —
a) builds schools  b) destroys what is built and holds economies back  c) raises savings  d) trains workers
**Answer: b) destroys what is built and holds economies back**.
