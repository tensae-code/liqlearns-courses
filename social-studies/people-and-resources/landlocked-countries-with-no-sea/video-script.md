A landlocked state has no seacoast: every one of its borders is a land border.

They arose at three moments: after the First World War; at decolonisation, when Africa's inherited colonial borders enclosed whole states; and at the Soviet Union's collapse..

Forty-nine countries — a fifth of the world — are landlocked, and they sit mostly in continental interiors, far from the sea their trade depends on..
