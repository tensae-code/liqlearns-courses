# Landlocked — countries with no sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A landlocked country has —
a) no seacoast  b) no rivers  c) one seaport
**Answer: a) no seacoast**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Landlocked countries number —
a) 49  b) 19  c) 149
**Answer: a) 49**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Africa's landlocked states arose largely at —
a) decolonisation  b) the Berlin Conference ball  c) the Soviet collapse
**Answer: a) decolonisation**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Landlocked states make up about —
a) a fifth of the world's countries  b) half the world  c) a fiftieth
**Answer: a) a fifth of the world's countries**
