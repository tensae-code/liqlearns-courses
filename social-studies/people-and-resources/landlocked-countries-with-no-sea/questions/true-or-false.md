# Landlocked — countries with no sea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Landlocked states sit mostly in continental interiors.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every border of a landlocked state is a sea border.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Soviet collapse created new landlocked states.
**Answer: True**
