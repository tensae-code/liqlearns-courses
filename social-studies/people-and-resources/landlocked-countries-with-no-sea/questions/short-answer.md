# Landlocked — countries with no sea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a landlocked state.
**Answer:** A country with no seacoast; every border a land border.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three moments they arose.
**Answer:** After the First World War, at decolonisation, at the Soviet Union's collapse.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How many are there, and what share of the world?
**Answer:** 49 countries, about a fifth.
