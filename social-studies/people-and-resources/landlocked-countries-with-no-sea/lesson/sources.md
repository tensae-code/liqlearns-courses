Bolivia's own way (Grade 10, Secundaria 4): Secundaria 4 is the nineteenth-century history year, and its geography is the geography of what Bolivia lost and what it lives from: the Atacama Litoral and the sea it no longer has, Potosí's silver and the tin north, the Amazon rubber boom, and a closing resources unit that runs from natural-resource classification to Vivir Bien and sustainable development.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 5) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 5 transport is taught as a landlocked country's system — goods by rail/road/air/sea, animal transport (riding, leading, herding), and the roadports, seaports and airports Zimbabwe depends on; shelter links heritage sites (Matobo Hills) and map-drawing of Zimbabwe to building safely in disaster-prone areas. That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 5 UG Excel P5 SST): A full-year commercial companion to the NCDC syllabus, far more detailed than the home-study booklet: Uganda's districts/regions are listed sub-region by sub-region, and each Uganda topic (physical features, climate, vegetation, resources, population) gets its own full treatment. Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Topic 6 The People of Pre-colonial Uganda — origins, ethnic groups, political/social/economic organisation (History, printed pp. 89–117, except its migration and settlement sections, placed below); Topic 7 Foreign Influence — traders, explorers, missionaries, colonial administrators (History, pp. 118–142); Topic 8 How Uganda Became a Nation (History, pp. 143–148); Topic 9 The Road to Independence (History, pp. 149–158); Topic 10 Uganda as an Independent Nation — presidents, national symbols, democracy, elections (Civics, pp. 159–173); Topic 11 The Government of Uganda — levels, organs, constitution, revenue, budgeting, citizens' rights (Civics, pp. 174–193).

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 9 Geography of Armenia student): the whole grade-9 year is the home country — first the Armenian Highlands as a physical whole, then the Republic section by section (nature, population, economy), finishing marz by marz (all 10 provinces) so every learner ends by studying their own province.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Geography TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Mirrors the student's book's 8 units with content maps, expected answers and group-presentation case studies (agriculture solutions, fishing solutions, fold-mountain impacts), plus a field-observation exercise on the environment near the school. That evidence is stocked here as the way South Sudan teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Egorina): the 2025 Atamura course is organised by numbered paragraphs (§1–§68) with coded learning goals (e.g. 7.3.2.4) and a «Негізгі ұғымдар» key-concepts box per paragraph; it gives Kazakhstani science its own paragraph (§3, research in the war and post-war years), a whole practical strand on building geographic databases (§§10, 18, 49, 54–55), and a country-studies block that classifies states by position — continental, archipelago, island and peninsular — with Kazakhstan's own economic-geographic position as the worked example.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Egorina 2017): the 2017 first edition of the Atamura course (the 2025 Book 2 is its revision): same six-section architecture but twelve numbered practical works, four of them database-building exercises on Kazakhstan itself — a geographic database for Kazakhstan's regions (§51) and for Kazakhstan's transport system (§52) — plus a practical on the natural complex of the pupil's own locality (§37).
