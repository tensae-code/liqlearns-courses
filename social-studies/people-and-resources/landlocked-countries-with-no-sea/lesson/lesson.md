# Landlocked — countries with no sea

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: what does a country lose when every border is land? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What landlocked means

A landlocked state has no seacoast: every one of its borders is a land border. Everything it trades with the wider world must cross somebody else's country first.

[Picture 27058 — a world distribution map of landlocked countries]

## Sub lesson 2 — How they arose

They arose at three moments: after the First World War; at decolonisation, when Africa's inherited colonial borders enclosed whole states; and at the Soviet Union's collapse.

## Sub lesson 3 — The count

Forty-nine countries — a fifth of the world — are landlocked, and they sit mostly in continental interiors, far from the sea their trade depends on.
