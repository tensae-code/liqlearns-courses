## Sub lesson 2 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies SB): Weather is learned by building the instruments — thermometer, rain gauge, wind vane and windsock made from local materials, then a school weather station — and map reading is taught on one worked map ("Upendo Area") with scale, symbol key and area measured by counting grid squares.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide grounds every weather idea in farm and river work — wind for threshing and winnowing, rough water making fishing risky — and teaches that forests help in the formation of rain. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB): Landforms are made, not just named — learners push a sheet of paper from both ends to make a fold mountain, and the home examples are the Ironstone Plateau and a farming landscape being changed by the farming itself. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
