## Sub lesson 1 — How Farming Changes the Land

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 70), teaches: Effects of types of agriculture on physical features. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 38), teaches: Effects of the development of agriculture on the environment: desertification, water and air pollution, agrochemicals. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB): Landforms are made, not just named — learners push a sheet of paper from both ends to make a fold mountain, and the home examples are the Ironstone Plateau and a farming landscape being changed by the farming itself.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies SB): Everything is costed against home ground — Mount Imatong both blocks movement and feeds rivers and the Imatong forest industry; mining is debated head-on ("Mining is the backbone to the economy of South Sudan") with a field trip to a real mining sector. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
