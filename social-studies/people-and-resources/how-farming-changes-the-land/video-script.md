# Video script — How Farming Changes the Land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 70), teaches: Effects of types of agriculture on physical features. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 38), teaches: Effects of the development of agriculture on the environment: desertification, water and air pollution, agrochemicals.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB): Landforms are made, not just named — learners push a sheet of paper from both ends to make a fold mountain, and the home examples are the Ironstone Plateau and a farming landscape being changed by the farming itself.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 7 P7 Social Studies SB): Everything is costed against home ground — Mount Imatong both blocks movement and feeds rivers and the Imatong forest industry; mining is debated head-on ("Mining is the backbone to the economy of South Sudan") with a field trip to a real mining sector.
