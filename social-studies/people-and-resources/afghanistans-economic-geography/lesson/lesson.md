## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 7 Geography (Dari)): the Dari MoE pattern — the world topic is taught first in the abstract (sphere, equator, latitude), then re-taught as Afghanistan's own geography (its position, relief, six climate regions, river basins, lakes, minerals, fuels), then widened to the continent (Asia). Every lesson ends with in-class group activity, questions and an out-of-class task naming the pupil's own locality.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 11 Geography (Dari)): an economic-geography year in two halves — first Afghanistan (farming and livestock, energy and mines, industries and trade), then the same themes for the world (water and farming, energy, mines and industries, trade and tourism), each Afghanistan chapter closing with a "review of the situation" lesson on the country's own state. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
