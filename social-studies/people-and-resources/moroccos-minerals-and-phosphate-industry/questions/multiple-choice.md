# Morocco's Minerals and the Phosphate Industry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's Geography Lesson 10 teaches —

**Answer: a) minerals and the metal industry**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson begins with the kinds of minerals and their —

**Answer: a) distribution**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The model sector of the lesson is —
a) the phosphate sector  b) the tourist sector  c) the railway sector
**Answer: a) the phosphate sector**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Minerals are joined in the lesson to —
a) industry  b) weather  c) prehistory
