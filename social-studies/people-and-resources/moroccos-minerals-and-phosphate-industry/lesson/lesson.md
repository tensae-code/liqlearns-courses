## Sub lesson 1 — Kinds and distribution of minerals

The tenth Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Features of my homeland — minerals and the metal industry, the phosphate sector as a model. It begins underground: the kinds of minerals Morocco has, and their distribution — where each kind is found in the homeland.

## Sub lesson 2 — Minerals and industry — phosphate as the model

The lesson then joins minerals to industry: what is dug from the land feeds what is made. One sector is held up as the model for the whole lesson — the phosphate sector — Morocco's own example of minerals at work in industry.
