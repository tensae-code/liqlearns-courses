Morocco's own way: Primary Social Studies is one integrated subject (History + Geography + Civics) organised around "my homeland" (وطني): at Year 5 the Geography component is «تعرف الوطن ومؤهلاته الطبيعية» — knowing my country and its natural assets — taught as 12 two-session lessons, each with a scientific summary, a didactic lesson card and unit evaluation activities; the History component runs Morocco from prehistory to the Alaouite state and Civics covers organising work, rights and civic behaviour.
