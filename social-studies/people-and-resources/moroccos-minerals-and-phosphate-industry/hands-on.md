# Hands-on — Morocco's Minerals and the Phosphate Industry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the minerals
On a map of Morocco, mark where minerals are found, as the lesson teaches their distribution. You are mapping kinds and places, the lesson's first half.

## From rock to industry
Write the chain in three steps for the phosphate sector as the lesson's model: the mineral in the ground, what industry does with it, and why it matters to the homeland.
