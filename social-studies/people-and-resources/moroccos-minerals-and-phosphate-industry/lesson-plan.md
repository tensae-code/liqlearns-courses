# Lesson plan — Morocco's Minerals and the Phosphate Industry

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** People and Resources · **Lesson:** Morocco's Minerals and the Phosphate Industry
**Place in the curriculum:** People and Resources (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Morocco, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Morocco's Grade 5 teacher guide teaches the homeland's minerals — their kinds and distribution, and minerals and industry, with the phosphate sector held up as the model.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Morocco's minerals and its phosphate industry, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
