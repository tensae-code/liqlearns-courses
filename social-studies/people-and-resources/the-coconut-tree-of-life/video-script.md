The reader's second case study is the amazing coconut tree: a sea-voyaging seed, carried by the ocean and adapted to the tropics, that gives a Pacific family food, drink, oil, fibre, timber and thatch from a single tree — which is why it is called the tree of life.

The case study follows the coconut island by island. In Niue and Tonga it becomes fans, baskets and canoe lashings, and feeds the coconut crab; Tonga even names the tree's life in fourteen coconut growth stages — a whole culture counted out on one tree.
