# Four kinds of work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Primary activities —
a) manufacture  b) provide services  c) extract or grow
**Answer: c) extract or grow**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade and transport are —
a) quaternary  b) tertiary  c) primary
**Answer: b) tertiary**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia’s workforce concentrates in —
a) the primary sector  b) quaternary work  c) manufacturing
**Answer: a) the primary sector**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The service and knowledge sectors in Ethiopia are —
a) the largest  b) empty  c) smaller than the primary sector
**Answer: c) smaller than the primary sector**
