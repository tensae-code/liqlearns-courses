# Four kinds of work

## Do it first

A farmer, a weaver, a shopkeeper, a researcher. Four floors of one building — every job in Ethiopia stands on one of them. Read the classification.

## Sub lesson 1 — The four classes

Economic activities group into four classes in the student book. Primary activities extract or grow — farming, fishing, forestry, mining. Secondary activities manufacture. Tertiary activities provide services, trade and transport. Quaternary activities work with knowledge and information.

[Picture 21097 — a four-step chain from farm to factory to shop to office]

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia’s shape of work

Ethiopia’s workforce is concentrated in the primary sector: most workers extract and grow, few manufacture, and the service and knowledge sectors are smaller still. The country’s work-shape is the ground floor of every economic lesson that follows.
