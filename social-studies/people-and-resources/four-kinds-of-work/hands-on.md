# Hands-on — Four kinds of work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Street census of work
Count twenty workers on a street or market you know and class each job. Present Ethiopia-in-miniature: does your street match the country’s primary-heavy shape?

## Chain building
Build one chain of four: cotton (primary) → cloth (secondary) → shop (tertiary) → fashion research (quaternary). Then build a second chain from coffee.
