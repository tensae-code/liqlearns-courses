# Ethiopia’s minerals — what is where

## Do it first

Gold in the west, potash in the Danakil heat, salt in Afar. Ethiopia’s minerals are scattered like a treasure map. Read what is where.

## Sub lesson 1 — The metal and fuel minerals

Ethiopia’s known mineral wealth includes gold, potash (in Afar), tantalum, platinum, iron and coal, with prospects of petroleum and natural gas. Each is located on the distribution map — minerals are where geology put them, not where people would choose.

## Sub lesson 2 — Industrial minerals

Beside them stand the industrial minerals: limestone, soda ash, salt and gemstones among them. The guide’s evidence adds the scale of the sector for now: mining contributes only about 1.5% of GDP (teacher guide — evidence) — the known wealth is large, the worked share still small.
