# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s minerals — what is where

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mineral map
On an outline map, place symbols: Au where gold is known, a potash mark in Afar, a salt mark in the Danakil. Write the rule under your map: minerals are where geology put them.

## Known vs worked
With a partner, explain the gap the lesson names — large known wealth, small worked share — and give two reasons a known mineral might stay in the ground.
