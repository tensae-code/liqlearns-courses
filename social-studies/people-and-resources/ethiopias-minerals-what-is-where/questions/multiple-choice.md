# Ethiopia’s minerals — what is where — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Potash is found in —
a) the Bale Mountains  b) Lake Tana  c) Afar
**Answer: c) Afar**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is an Ethiopian mineral named in the lesson?
a) uranium  b) tantalum  c) bauxite only
**Answer: b) tantalum**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An industrial mineral named is —
a) soda ash  b) coffee  c) teff
**Answer: a) soda ash**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mining’s share of GDP in the guide’s figure is about —
a) 50%  b) 25%  c) 1.5%
**Answer: c) 1.5%**
