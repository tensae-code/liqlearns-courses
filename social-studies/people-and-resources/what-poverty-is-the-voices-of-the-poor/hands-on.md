# Hands-on — What poverty is — the voices of the poor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Voice first
Read the three voices aloud in turn. Under each, list what that speaker names — then count how many of the six dimensions your own do-first sentences caught.

## Asset, not income
A poor household manages assets (a goat, seed, stored grain) more than income. Write its week as asset management decisions, and say what a cash-only definition would miss.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
