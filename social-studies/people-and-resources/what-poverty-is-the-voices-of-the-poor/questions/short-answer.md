# What poverty is — the voices of the poor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The poor describe it themselves" in your own words.
**Answer:** The poor describe poverty as hunger, voicelessness and no progress — far more than low income.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Six dimensions" in your own words.
**Answer:** The six dimensions include hunger, psychological wounds, missing infrastructure, schooling, ill-health and asset management.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The World Bank definition" in your own words.
**Answer:** The World Bank defines poverty as pronounced deprivation in wellbeing.
