# What poverty is — the voices of the poor — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The poor describe poverty as hunger, voicelessness and no progress — far more than low income.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The six dimensions include hunger, psychological wounds, missing infrastructure, schooling, ill-health and asset management.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The World Bank defines poverty as pronounced deprivation in wellbeing.
**Answer: True**
