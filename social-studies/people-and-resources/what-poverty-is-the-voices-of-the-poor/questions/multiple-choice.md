# What poverty is — the voices of the poor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The poor describe it themselves"?
a) The poor describe poverty as hunger, voicelessness and no progress — far more than low income  b) The six dimensions include hunger, psychological wounds, missing infrastructure, schooling, ill-health and asset management  c) The World Bank defines poverty as pronounced deprivation in wellbeing
**Answer: a) The poor describe poverty as hunger, voicelessness and no progress — far more than low income**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Six dimensions"?
a) The six dimensions include hunger, psychological wounds, missing infrastructure, schooling, ill-health and asset management  b) The World Bank defines poverty as pronounced deprivation in wellbeing  c) The poor describe poverty as hunger, voicelessness and no progress — far more than low income
**Answer: a) The six dimensions include hunger, psychological wounds, missing infrastructure, schooling, ill-health and asset management**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The World Bank definition"?
a) The World Bank defines poverty as pronounced deprivation in wellbeing  b) The poor describe poverty as hunger, voicelessness and no progress — far more than low income  c) The six dimensions include hunger, psychological wounds, missing infrastructure, schooling, ill-health and asset management
**Answer: a) The World Bank defines poverty as pronounced deprivation in wellbeing**
