# Hands-on — Free Trade Zones

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 8: Free trade zones (مناطق آزاد تجاری) — not all have reached their goals (in some countries they became import bases); they usually stand beside great ports; and most of countries' economic exchange moves by sea — bulk carriers (grain, coal, iron ore, cement), container ships, oil tankers and gas carriers. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
