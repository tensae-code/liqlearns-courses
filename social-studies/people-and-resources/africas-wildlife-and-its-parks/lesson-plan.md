# Lesson plan — Africa's wildlife and its parks

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** People and Resources · **Lesson:** Africa's wildlife and its parks
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Natural Resource Base of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Africa's wildlife — its great mammals, its endemic species found nowhere else, and its marine life — is protected through a system of parks and sanctuaries, and where wildlife lives is governed by the same land and climate that govern its vegetation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Africa's vegetation belts — wildlife distribution follows the plant cover it feeds and shelters in.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Mammals, endemic species and marine life** — The cast of Africa's wildlife.
2. **Parks and sanctuaries — the protection system** — Guarded ground for wild life.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Africa's vegetation belts. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define endemic species.
2. Name the wildlife cast: mammals, endemic species, marine life.
3. Explain parks and sanctuaries as habitat protection.
4. Link wildlife distribution to the land and climate controls of the vegetation map.

## If the learner is stuck
