# Hands-on — Africa's wildlife and its parks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Habitat web
Draw one savanna animal in the centre of a page. Around it draw and label everything its life runs on — grass, water, trees, other animals. Draw a boundary around the whole web and label it: 'what a park protects'.

## Endemic detective
Choose one African animal you know. Find out (from a book, an elder, or the lesson) whether it lives anywhere else naturally. Present the verdict: endemic or not, and what that means for its protection.
