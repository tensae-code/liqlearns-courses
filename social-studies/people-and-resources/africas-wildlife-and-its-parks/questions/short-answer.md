# Africa's wildlife and its parks — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is losing an endemic species final in a way losing a widespread species is not?
**Answer:** Because its whole natural population lives in the one place — if it is lost there, it is lost from the Earth entirely.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the vegetation map help predict where wildlife lives?
**Answer:** Wildlife follows the plant cover it feeds and shelters in — herds on savanna grass, forest species in forest — and vegetation is governed by land and climate.
