# Africa's wildlife and its parks — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If an endemic species is lost in Africa, it survives naturally elsewhere.
**Answer: False** — by definition it lives nowhere else.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's marine life belongs to its wildlife account.
**Answer: True** — the cast reaches the coasts and seas.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A sanctuary and a park both give wildlife guarded ground.
**Answer: True** — the protected-area system.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Protecting vegetation does nothing for wildlife.
**Answer: False** — wildlife lives on its habitat — protect the cover and you protect the animals.
