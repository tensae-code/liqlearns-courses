# Africa's wildlife and its parks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An endemic species is one that —
a) lives everywhere  b) is found naturally nowhere else on Earth  c) lives only in zoos  d) migrates yearly
**Answer: b) is found naturally nowhere else on Earth** — lose it here and it is lost everywhere.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The wildlife account includes which three parts?
a) Only mammals  b) Mammals, endemic species and marine life  c) Only birds  d) Fish only
**Answer: b) Mammals, endemic species and marine life** — the full cast of the section.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A park protects —
a) animals only, in cages  b) the whole habitat — grass, water, cover  c) a single famous animal  d) the park office
**Answer: b) the whole habitat — grass, water, cover** — animals live on their habitat.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wildlife distribution is governed by —
a) town boundaries  b) factors of land and climate — the vegetation controls  c) railway lines  d) languages
**Answer: b) factors of land and climate — the vegetation controls** — herds stand where the grass feeds them.
