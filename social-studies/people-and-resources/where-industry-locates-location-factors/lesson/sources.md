**Acceptance count: taught in 8 countries (Kazakhstan, Palestine, Venezuela, Egypt, Yemen, Iraq, India, Nigeria). South Sudan is named here as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Nigeria's own way (Nigeria Grade 11 New Economics SS2): SS2 pairs every theory block with its Nigerian application in the same theme — wage policy and trade unions, agricultural policy and marketing, revenue allocation in the federation, the Nigerian Stock Exchange and inflation in Nigeria.

India's own way (India Grade 8 Resources and Development): Economic geography is taught through Indian anchors — a 1.5-hectare Uttar Pradesh farm set against a 250-hectare Iowa farm, Jamshedpur steel, Ahmedabad as the "Manchester of India", and cooperative dairies (Amul/AMUL pattern, Sudha) as an ownership form.

India's own way (India Grade 10 Contemporary India-II): The Class X geography book doubles as economic geography of India — land reform and Bhoodan, manufacturing's stuck 17% GDP share, and trade read as the country's "economic barometer".

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 8 Social Studies): Grade 8 teaches the Arab world as one economic space — its crops, animals, oil and minerals, the conditions that make industry succeed, and its trade, foreign and domestic — after a first term spent on the same resources governorate by governorate at home.

Yemen's own way (Grade 4 Al-Tarbiya al-Ijtima'iya): the economy is taught as the homeland's wealth — crops, herds, fish, workshops and ports — with the state, the agricultural associations and the export harbour all named as actors a nine-year-old can point to.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 6 Venezuela G6 Social Sciences SB Spanish): G6 names the system outright — capitalism, dependent capitalism and the informal economy are defined in the pupil's own book, and development is redefined as gains in people's standard of living with the least environmental damage. This lesson teaches the placements' content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 8 Abilmazhinova): the Grade 8 Mektep course deepens every Grade 7 line — relief classification and rock ages, global circulation and the climatic belts, natural zones of both hemispheres, demographic calculation and policy design (Practical №7 has pupils propose their own demographic-policy model), resource assessment, industry location factors, and the political map — closing with Kazakhstan's own place in political-integration processes (§54).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 8 Karatabanov): the Almatykitap Grade 8 course runs the same six-section architecture as the Mektep book (Book 7) but in Karatabanov's question-led style, closing Part 2 with §68 «Мой географический год» (My geographic year) — a pupil's own review of a year of geographic work.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 9 Sakhova NIS): the NIS trial Grade 9 course is organised by problems rather than by the standard sections — forecasting lithospheric cataclysms and safety measures (3.1.2–3.1.3), the causes of water scarcity (3.3.4), the noosphere and its development in Kazakhstan (3.4.1–3.4.2), and a research lesson on the very concept of sustainable development (5.1.4).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 8 Sakhova NIS): the NIS Grade 8 course phrases its lessons as questions and tasks — «Әр жерде неліктен уақыт әркелкі?» (Why is time different in different places?, 2.2), «Тау жыныстары қалай пайда болады?» (How do rocks form?, 3.1.5), «Өзеннің суы қалай өзгереді?» (How does a river's water change?, 3.3.4) — and adds a lesson on the culture of showing geographic names on the map (2.3).

Egypt's own way (Grade 9 combined edition, egypt-placements.md): Economics is taught as world economic geography — each activity (farming, grazing, mining, industry) is mapped worldwide with production bar charts, and every foreign country model ends with a section on that country's economic relations with Egypt.
