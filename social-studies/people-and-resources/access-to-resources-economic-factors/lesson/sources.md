**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Samoa).**

Samoa's own way (Year 9 Social Studies Book 3): Development is taught by direct comparison — Samoa's own indicators (GNI per capita US$1430, life expectancy 69) are set beside India's and the USA's in one table pupils must graph and rank.

Samoa's own way (Year 10 Social Studies Book 3): Access is the organising idea — whether a Samoan can get goods depends on income type, tax, government policy, land tenure and social position, each taught with Samoan data (formal employment 2000, a pupil's tax essay, farming/fishing tax exemptions).

Samoa's own way (Year 10, Book 3): Access to resources is split into economic and social factors with Pacific examples, and development is taught as change that can bring conflict in places — ending in consensus-building and appropriate technology for small island states.
