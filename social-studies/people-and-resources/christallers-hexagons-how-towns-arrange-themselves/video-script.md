# Video script — Christaller's hexagons — how towns arrange themselves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Settlement location completes the trio of theories (1933, southern Germany).

K = 3 is the marketing principle: each higher centre serves itself plus a third of each of its six neighbours.

K = 7 is the administrative principle: lower centres lie wholly inside one higher centre's area, so control is clean and no town answers to two masters.
