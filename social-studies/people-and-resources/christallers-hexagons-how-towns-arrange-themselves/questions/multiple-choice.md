# Christaller's hexagons — how towns arrange themselves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Christaller's theory dates from —
a) 1933  b) 1826  c) 1909
**Answer: a) 1933**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hexagons beat circles as market areas because they —
a) tile without gaps or overlaps  b) are prettier  c) are larger
**Answer: a) tile without gaps or overlaps**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** K = 3 is the —
a) marketing principle  b) transport principle  c) administrative principle
**Answer: a) marketing principle**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** K = 7 arranges lower centres —
a) wholly inside one higher centre's area  b) at road midpoints  c) on coasts
**Answer: a) wholly inside one higher centre's area**
