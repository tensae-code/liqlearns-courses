# Christaller's hexagons — how towns arrange themselves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why hexagons?
**Answer:** They tile a plain without gaps or overlaps, unlike circles.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish K = 3 from K = 4.
**Answer:** K = 3 marketing: higher centre serves itself plus thirds of six neighbours. K = 4 transport: lower centres at road midpoints.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What problem does K = 7 solve?
**Answer:** Clean administration — each lower centre wholly inside one higher area.
