# The poverty gap — depth and severity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The total poverty gap"?
a) The total poverty gap sums how far below the line every poor person falls  b) The average poverty gap divides the total gap by the whole population  c) The FGT measures can weight the poorest most heavily, capturing severity
**Answer: a) The total poverty gap sums how far below the line every poor person falls**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The average gap"?
a) The average poverty gap divides the total gap by the whole population  b) The FGT measures can weight the poorest most heavily, capturing severity  c) The total poverty gap sums how far below the line every poor person falls
**Answer: a) The average poverty gap divides the total gap by the whole population**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "The FGT family — weighting the poorest"?
a) The FGT measures can weight the poorest most heavily, capturing severity  b) The total poverty gap sums how far below the line every poor person falls  c) The average poverty gap divides the total gap by the whole population
**Answer: a) The FGT measures can weight the poorest most heavily, capturing severity**
