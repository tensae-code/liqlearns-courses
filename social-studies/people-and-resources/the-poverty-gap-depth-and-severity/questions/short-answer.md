# The poverty gap — depth and severity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The total poverty gap" in your own words.
**Answer:** The total poverty gap sums how far below the line every poor person falls.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The average gap" in your own words.
**Answer:** The average poverty gap divides the total gap by the whole population.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The FGT family — weighting the poorest" in your own words.
**Answer:** The FGT measures can weight the poorest most heavily, capturing severity.
