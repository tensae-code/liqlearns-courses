# The poverty gap — depth and severity — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The total poverty gap sums how far below the line every poor person falls.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The average poverty gap divides the total gap by the whole population.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The FGT measures can weight the poorest most heavily, capturing severity.
**Answer: True**
