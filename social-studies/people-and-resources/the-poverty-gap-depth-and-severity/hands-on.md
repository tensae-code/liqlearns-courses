# Hands-on — The poverty gap — depth and severity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fill the pit
Draw people standing in a pit of different depths under a line, the fill needed for each marked. Sum the fill (your TPG), then divide by everyone for the average gap.

## Same headcount, different gap
Build two five-person towns with identical headcounts but very different gaps. Compute both gaps and write the policy difference the gap reveals.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
