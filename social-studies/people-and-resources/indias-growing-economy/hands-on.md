# Hands-on — India's Growing Economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the road
Draw a road in two stretches. Label the first Gandhi's economic self-reliance, and the second the 1990s liberalisation. You are drawing the road India's economy travelled.

## Money and workers
Write the three GDP shares — services, industry, agriculture — then circle the one that employs most people. Write one line on what the gap between the money and the workers shows.
