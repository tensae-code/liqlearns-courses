# India's Growing Economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gandhi's economic road for India was —
a) economic self-reliance  b) liberalisation in the 1990s  c) joining Iceland's economy
**Answer: a) economic self-reliance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The liberalisation came in —
a) the 1990s  b) 1947  c) the 1800s
**Answer: a) the 1990s**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Services make up what share of India's GDP, as the guide teaches? —
a) 54%  b) 16%  c) 27%
**Answer: a) 54%**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture is 16% of GDP, but it —
a) employs most people  b) employs nobody  c) is India's biggest GDP share
**Answer: a) employs most people**
