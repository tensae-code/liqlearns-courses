# India's Growing Economy

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: India's economy changed its road in living memory: from trying to rely on itself, to opening up — and today most of its money and most of its workers are in different places. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — From self-reliance to liberalisation

Chapter 3 of Iceland's India guide, Þróun hagkerfisins — the development of the economy — tells the road in two stretches. First, Gandhi's road: economic self-reliance, India providing for itself. Then the turn: the liberalisation of the 1990s, when that road was opened up.

## Sub lesson 2 — Services, industry, agriculture

The chapter then reads today's economy in three shares of GDP: services 54%, industry 27%, agriculture 16%. But the shares of money are not the shares of people: agriculture, the smallest share of GDP, is the part that employs most people. That gap — where the money is, and where the workers are — is the chapter's point.
