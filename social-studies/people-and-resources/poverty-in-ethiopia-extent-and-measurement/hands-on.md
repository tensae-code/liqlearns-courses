# Hands-on — Poverty in Ethiopia — extent and measurement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the dashboard
Draw a dashboard of Ethiopia's figures — 38% to 24%, $883, HDI 0.485 — over a map split rural and urban. Under it, write the one figure that most qualifies the good news, and why.

## Audit a claim
A speech claims 'poverty is solved: growth is 9.8%'. Using three figures from the lesson, write the honest reply — what growth did, what it has not yet done, and who is still waiting.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
