# Poverty in Ethiopia — extent and measurement

## Do it first

Growth averaged nearly 10% a year for a decade in Ethiopia, while poverty fell from 38% to 24%. Headline or half-story? Hold your verdict until the dashboard is full.

## Sub lesson 1 — The headline numbers

Population about 118 million (2021). Growth averaged 9.8% a year from 2008/09 to 2018/19 while the share below the national poverty line fell from 38% to 24%. Per-capita income was estimated at $883 (MoFEC, 2019), and the official headcount fell from 59% in 1992 to 29.5% in 2011.

## Sub lesson 2 — The depth behind the headline

Ethiopia's 2019 HDI was 0.485, ranked 173 of 189 — low human development. 44% lived on under a dollar a day and 89% under $2; IFAD estimated over 12 million chronically or periodically food insecure, most of them smallholder farmers subsisting under $0.50 per person a day, with rural poverty falling more slowly than urban.

## Sub lesson 3 — The measurement machinery

The machinery is Ethiopian: the Central Statistical Agency's HICES and Welfare Monitoring Surveys, running since 1996, feed the Ministry's Poverty Analysis Reports, under the PRSP process. The numbers in this lesson exist because that machinery measures, year after year.
