# Poverty in Ethiopia — extent and measurement — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The headline numbers" in your own words.
**Answer:** Growth averaged 9.8% a year 2008/09–2018/19 and poverty fell from 38% to 24%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The depth behind the headline" in your own words.
**Answer:** HDI 0.485 (173 of 189); over 12 million food insecure, mostly smallholders under $0.50 a day.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The measurement machinery" in your own words.
**Answer:** The CSA's HICES and Welfare Monitoring Surveys, since 1996, feed the Poverty Analysis Reports.
