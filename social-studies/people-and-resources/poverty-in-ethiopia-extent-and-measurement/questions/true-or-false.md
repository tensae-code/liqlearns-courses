# Poverty in Ethiopia — extent and measurement — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Growth averaged 9.8% a year 2008/09–2018/19 and poverty fell from 38% to 24%.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** HDI 0.485 (173 of 189); over 12 million food insecure, mostly smallholders under $0.50 a day.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The CSA's HICES and Welfare Monitoring Surveys, since 1996, feed the Poverty Analysis Reports.
**Answer: True**
