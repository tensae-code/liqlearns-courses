# Hands-on — Over-population — too many for the resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Symptom chain
Draw the chain from falling resources-per-person to each of the four symptoms, one arrow each, with the middle step written in (smaller plots → landlessness, and so on).

## History instance
The book instances old European history for begging as a symptom. With a partner, discuss what that teaches: over-population is a relation between people and resources, not a property of any one nation or age.
