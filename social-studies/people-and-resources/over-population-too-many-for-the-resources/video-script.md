# Video script — Over-population — too many for the resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Past the optimum, the scale tips.
Resources per person fall below a decent life.
Then the symptoms: hunger, long unemployment, landlessness, begging.
Over-population is read in lives, not only in ratios.
