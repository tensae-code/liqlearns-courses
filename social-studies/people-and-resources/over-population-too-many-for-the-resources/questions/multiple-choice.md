# Over-population — too many for the resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Over-population is population —
a) at the optimum  b) below the optimum  c) beyond the optimum
**Answer: c) beyond the optimum**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its measure is —
a) city size  b) resources per person falling  c) total population alone
**Answer: b) resources per person falling**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A symptom named is —
a) prolonged unemployment  b) full employment  c) rising wages
**Answer: a) prolonged unemployment**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book instances begging’s history from —
a) Ethiopia only  b) modern cities  c) old European countries
**Answer: c) old European countries**


**5. (Sub lesson 1)** The optimum population's indications include —
a) full employment, high average living standards, balanced development  b) famine and begging  c) empty land and idle machines  d) falling living standards
**Answer: a) full employment, high average living standards, balanced development** — the book's signs of the best fit (printed pp. 162–164).
