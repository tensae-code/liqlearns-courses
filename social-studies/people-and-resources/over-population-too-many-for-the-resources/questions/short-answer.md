# Over-population — too many for the resources — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define over-population.
**Answer: More people than resources and technology can support at good living standards.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the four symptoms.
**Answer: Famine and hunger, prolonged unemployment, landlessness and begging.**


**5. (Sub lesson 1)** List the three indications of an optimum population.
**Answer:** Full employment; high average living standards; balanced development (Grade 9 student book, printed pp. 162–164).
