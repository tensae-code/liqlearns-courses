# Video script — Why are people poor — systems or choices?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two voices explain poverty. The first voice points at the system: wages too low to live on, schools and clinics never funded, insurance that never arrives — and behind them, patriarchy, capitalism, racial privilege. Poverty, says this voice, is manufactured. The second voice points at the household: a culture of poverty, choices that defeat themselves, handed down the generations. Poverty, says this voice, is perpetuated. Between them stands the asset lens: count what a family actually holds — labour, skills, health, schooling — and ask what policy does to those assets. Judge the two voices against that count.
