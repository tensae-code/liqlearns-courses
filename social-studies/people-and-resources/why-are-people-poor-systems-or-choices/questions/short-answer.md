# Why are people poor — systems or choices? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the structural explanation in one sentence.
**Answer:** Poverty is produced by systems — labour-market failure and low wages, underinvestment in public services, and deeper structures such as patriarchy, capitalism and racial privilege.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the behavioural explanation in one sentence.
**Answer:** A culture of poverty: bad, self-defeating decisions perpetuate deprivation across generations.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the asset lens add?
**Answer:** It mediates: poverty's determinants run through human assets, demography, education and policy — where systems and choices meet.
