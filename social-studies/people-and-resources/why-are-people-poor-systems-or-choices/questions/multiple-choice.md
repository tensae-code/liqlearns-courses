# Why are people poor — systems or choices? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The structural account blames…
a) systems — labour-market failure, low wages, underinvestment  b) individual choices  c) the weather
**Answer: a) systems — labour-market failure, low wages, underinvestment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Deeper structures named include…
a) patriarchy, capitalism, racial privilege  b) rivers and roads  c) languages
**Answer: a) patriarchy, capitalism, racial privilege**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Underinvestment harms through…
a) education, health care, social insurance gaps  b) too many schools  c) exports
**Answer: a) education, health care, social insurance gaps**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The 'culture of poverty' account says…
a) systems produce poverty  b) self-defeating decisions perpetuate deprivation across generations  c) poverty is random
**Answer: b) self-defeating decisions perpetuate deprivation across generations**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The behavioural account is favoured, the book notes, by…
a) conservative and libertarian writers  b) geographers  c) doctors
**Answer: a) conservative and libertarian writers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The asset lens runs determinants through…
a) human assets, demography, education, policy  b) luck  c) ancestry
**Answer: a) human assets, demography, education, policy**
