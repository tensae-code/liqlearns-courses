# Hands-on — What forests do for the economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Forest ledger
Draw the forest’s ledger: products on one side, protections on the other. Under each entry write who benefits — household, farmer downstream, the whole country.

## Cut or keep?
Debate one standing forest with a partner: felled, it pays once in products; standing, it pays yearly in protection. The lesson must supply both sides’ facts.
