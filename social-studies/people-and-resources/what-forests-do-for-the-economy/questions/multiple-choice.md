# What forests do for the economy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A forest product is —
a) coffee cherries  b) potash  c) fuelwood
**Answer: c) fuelwood**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Construction forests supply —
a) no building material  b) timber and poles  c) only firewood
**Answer: b) timber and poles**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Forests protect —
a) watersheds and soils  b) only wildlife  c) roads
**Answer: a) watersheds and soils**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Forestry’s direct foreign-exchange role is —
a) the largest of all sectors  b) equal to coffee  c) small
**Answer: c) small**
