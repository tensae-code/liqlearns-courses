# Video script — How the State Steers the Economy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 11 book opens its Economics chapter by asking by what policy the state regulates the economy. The state does not stand outside economic life: through its policies it steers production, prices, work and trade, and this topic teaches the means of that steering before the monetary-policy topic that follows it.

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the final year is the state-and-world year — cultural globalization and freedom, monetary policy, foreign trade and investment, and Mongolia's signature “third neighbour” and soft-power policies taught as named topics. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
