# Kinds of unemployment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Employment means —
a) willing work at the prevailing wage  b) any work at any wage  c) work only in offices
**Answer: a) willing work at the prevailing wage**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's example of voluntary unemployment is —
a) being dismissed in a closure  b) retiring at pension age  c) quitting a job to start a small business
**Answer: c) quitting a job to start a small business**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Employability means —
a) living in a city  b) having the skills and fitness work requires  c) owning a business
**Answer: b) having the skills and fitness work requires**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Involuntary unemployment is —
a) willing to work at the prevailing wage, yet without work  b) choosing not to work  c) studying full-time
**Answer: a) willing to work at the prevailing wage, yet without work**
