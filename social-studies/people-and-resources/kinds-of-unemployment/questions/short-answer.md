# Kinds of unemployment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define employment as the book does.
**Answer:** Willing work at the prevailing wage.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes unemployment voluntary?
**Answer:** It is joblessness by choice — the person could take work but has chosen not to, as in quitting to start a small business.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two working words that stand around the involuntary line.
**Answer:** Employability and active job-seeking.
