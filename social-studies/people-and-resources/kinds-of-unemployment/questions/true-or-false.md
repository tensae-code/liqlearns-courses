# Kinds of unemployment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Not every person without a job stands in the same relation to work.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Voluntary unemployment is a defeat forced on the worker.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Active job-seeking shows that willingness to work is real.
**Answer: True**
