# Video script — Kinds of unemployment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Employment is defined first: willing work at the prevailing wage — being ready and able to work for the wage the market offers.

Voluntary unemployment is joblessness by choice: the book's example is a person quitting a job to start a small business of their own.

Involuntary unemployment is the other kind: willing to work at the prevailing wage, yet without work.
