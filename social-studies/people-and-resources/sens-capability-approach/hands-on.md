# Hands-on — Sen's capability approach

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Same bicycle
Draw two people given the same bicycle — one riding it to market, one unable — and label the conversion factors that differ between them (health, road, custom, skill).

## Convert one good
Take one good (a radio, a plot, a smartphone). Write three different functionings it becomes for three different people, naming the conversion factor behind each difference.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
