# Sen's capability approach — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Functionings and capabilities"?
a) Functionings are what a person actually is and does; capabilities are the real freedom to achieve them  b) Conversion factors — personal, social, environmental — decide what a good becomes for a person  c) Poverty is the inability to pursue wellbeing, not merely low income
**Answer: a) Functionings are what a person actually is and does; capabilities are the real freedom to achieve them**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Conversion factors"?
a) Conversion factors — personal, social, environmental — decide what a good becomes for a person  b) Poverty is the inability to pursue wellbeing, not merely low income  c) Functionings are what a person actually is and does; capabilities are the real freedom to achieve them
**Answer: a) Conversion factors — personal, social, environmental — decide what a good becomes for a person**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Poverty as unfreedom"?
a) Poverty is the inability to pursue wellbeing, not merely low income  b) Functionings are what a person actually is and does; capabilities are the real freedom to achieve them  c) Conversion factors — personal, social, environmental — decide what a good becomes for a person
**Answer: a) Poverty is the inability to pursue wellbeing, not merely low income**
