# Sen's capability approach — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Functionings are what a person actually is and does; capabilities are the real freedom to achieve them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Conversion factors — personal, social, environmental — decide what a good becomes for a person.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Poverty is the inability to pursue wellbeing, not merely low income.
**Answer: True**
