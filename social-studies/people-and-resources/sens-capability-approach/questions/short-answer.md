# Sen's capability approach — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Functionings and capabilities" in your own words.
**Answer:** Functionings are what a person actually is and does; capabilities are the real freedom to achieve them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Conversion factors" in your own words.
**Answer:** Conversion factors — personal, social, environmental — decide what a good becomes for a person.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Poverty as unfreedom" in your own words.
**Answer:** Poverty is the inability to pursue wellbeing, not merely low income.
