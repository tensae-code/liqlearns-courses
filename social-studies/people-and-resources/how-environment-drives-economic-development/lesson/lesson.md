Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 8 np-g8): this is the old kingdom-era standalone Geography volume — Nepal is taught through its 14 zones (anchal), with full regional accounts of Bagmati, Narayani, Mechi and Mahakali zones (position, environment, resources, development, chief towns, each with maps); Asia is the one continent studied in depth; world economic geography is built on "environment and man" (geographical control).
