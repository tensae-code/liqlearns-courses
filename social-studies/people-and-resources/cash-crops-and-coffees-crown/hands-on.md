# Hands-on — Cash crops and coffee’s crown

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Crown chart
Draw an export chart with coffee’s bar first and longest, the others behind it in the lesson’s order. Title it with the lesson’s warning: still first, share declining.

## Home and abroad
With a partner, list coffee’s two lives — export earner abroad, daily drink at home — and say which parts of the lesson each life touches.
