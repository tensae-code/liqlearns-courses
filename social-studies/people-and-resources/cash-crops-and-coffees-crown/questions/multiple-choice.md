# Cash crops and coffee’s crown — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The leading export earner is —
a) khat  b) flowers  c) coffee
**Answer: c) coffee**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Since 2018/19, coffee’s export share has —
a) vanished  b) declined, though it stays first  c) doubled
**Answer: b) declined, though it stays first**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Other cash crops named include —
a) oilseeds, pulses, khat and flowers  b) teff and barley only  c) cotton only
**Answer: a) oilseeds, pulses, khat and flowers**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cash crops earn —
a) only local money  b) nothing until processed  c) foreign currency
**Answer: c) foreign currency**
