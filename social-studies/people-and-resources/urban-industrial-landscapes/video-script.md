# Video script — Urban Industrial Landscapes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente* (printed p. 38), teaches: Paisajes urbano-industriales en un país con economía dependiente.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): the whole Geography block is one argument — "Nuestro espacio geográfico en tiempos del petróleo": how oil remade population (2011 census), cities (metropolisation), industry location, internal migration and landscapes; learners start by learning how to *look* at a piece of oil-era Venezuela.
