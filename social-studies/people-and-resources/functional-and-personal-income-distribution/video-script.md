# Video script — Functional and personal income distribution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Economic inequality is the uneven distribution of national income, studied two ways. The functional, or factor-share, distribution asks what share goes to each factor of production: labour's wages against land's rent and capital's interest and profit.

The personal, or size, distribution ignores the source and simply ranks persons or households by the total income each receives. It is the distribution the measurement tools that follow — quintiles, Lorenz, Gini — all use.
