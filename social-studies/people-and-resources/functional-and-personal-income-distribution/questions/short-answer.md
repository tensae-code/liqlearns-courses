# Functional and personal income distribution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "The functional distribution" in your own words.
**Answer:** The functional distribution asks what share goes to each factor of production.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The personal distribution" in your own words.
**Answer:** The personal distribution ranks households by total income received, ignoring source.
