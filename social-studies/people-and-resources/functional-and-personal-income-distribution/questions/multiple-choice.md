# Functional and personal income distribution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "The functional distribution"?
a) The functional distribution asks what share goes to each factor of production  b) The personal distribution ranks households by total income received, ignoring source
**Answer: a) The functional distribution asks what share goes to each factor of production**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The personal distribution"?
a) The personal distribution ranks households by total income received, ignoring source  b) The functional distribution asks what share goes to each factor of production
**Answer: a) The personal distribution ranks households by total income received, ignoring source**
