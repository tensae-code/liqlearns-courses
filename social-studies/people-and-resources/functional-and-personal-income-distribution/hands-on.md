# Hands-on — Functional and personal income distribution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Slice it twice
Draw one national-income pie sliced two ways — by factor, then by household rank. Under each drawing write the question it answers, in the lesson's own words.

## Which slicing
A minister asks 'are workers getting their share?' and 'are the poorest households closing the gap?' Match each question to its distribution and say why the other slicing cannot answer it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
