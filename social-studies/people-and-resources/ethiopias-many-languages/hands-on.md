# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s many languages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Language census of home
List every language spoken by people you know personally. Count them, and mark which is your mother tongue and which you use as a bridge with others.

## Bridge thinking
With a partner, act a market meeting between two speakers with no shared mother tongue. Then do it again through a shared working language, and say what changed.
