# Video script — Ethiopia’s many languages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

More than eighty languages live in Ethiopia.
Each is a mother tongue, a community, a world.
Over them all, one bridge: Amharic, the federal working language.
Diversity is the fact. The bridge is the answer.
