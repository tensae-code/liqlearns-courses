# Ethiopia’s many languages

## Do it first

Stand in a market and count the languages you hear. In Ethiopia the count runs past eighty. Read the language map of the country.

## Sub lesson 1 — More than 80 languages

Ethiopia is home to more than 80 languages — one of the most language-rich countries anywhere. Census data names the largest language groups; around and between them live dozens of smaller languages, each the mother tongue of its own community.

[Picture 21048 — a language map of Ethiopia in many colours]

## Sub lesson 2 — Amharic’s role

Among the many, Amharic holds a special working role: it is the federal working language, the language through which the federal government works across all the language communities. Diversity is the fact; a shared working language is the bridge built over it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion how many languages Ethiopia holds and what Amharic’s special role is.

## Do It Yourself

1. Give the language count from memory.
2. Say which body’s data names the largest groups.
