# Video script — Myanmar's Neighbouring Countries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide opens its neighbouring-countries strand (§4.1) with the neighbouring countries of Myanmar as a whole: Myanmar has five neighbours — Bangladesh, India, China, Laos and Thailand — each then studied one by one in the lessons that follow.

The same opening lesson also places Myanmar in its groupings: the guide names Myanmar's membership of ASEAN, BIMSTEC and GMS as part of the neighbouring-countries topic. This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil — Myanmar's own way is to teach its neighbours and its regional memberships together, before the one-by-one country studies.
