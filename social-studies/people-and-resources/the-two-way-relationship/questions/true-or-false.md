# The Two-Way Relationship — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farming, building and road-cutting change the environment.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The environment never changes how people live; only people change the environment.
**Answer: False** — climate changes how people dress and eat; the relationship is two-way.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Salting keeps food because the salt draws moisture out and kills bacteria.
**Answer: True.**
