# The Two-Way Relationship — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a way people change the environment?
a) The rain deciding the planting day  b) Building houses, roads and factories  c) The climate making people wear light clothes
**Answer: b) Building houses, roads and factories** — that is people acting on the land.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A village throws its waste into the river it drinks from. This is —
a) a help from people to the environment  b) a harm from people to the environment  c) the environment shaping people
**Answer: b) a harm from people to the environment.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the relationship between people and the environment called two-way?
a) Because there are two kinds of people  b) Because people change the environment and the environment changes people's lives back  c) Because there are two seasons
**Answer: b)** — the change runs in both directions.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The worms and weeds around a crop are which kind of factor?
a) Abiotic  b) Biotic  c) Neither
**Answer: b) Biotic** — they are living things.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does drying keep food from spoiling?
a) It makes the food heavier  b) It takes the water out, so bacteria and yeasts cannot grow  c) It paints the food a dark colour
**Answer: b)** — no water, no growth for the spoiling things.
