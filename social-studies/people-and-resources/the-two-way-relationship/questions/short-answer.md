# The Two-Way Relationship — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one help and one harm that people bring to a river near a village.
**Answer:** Help: building a well or a protected spring point / keeping its banks planted (any sensible help). Harm: dirtying it with waste, or cutting all the trees on its banks (any sensible harm).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a hot climate shape the way people dress and eat?
**Answer:** People wear light clothes against the heat, and their foods and eating ways fit what the hot land grows and how fast food spoils there.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A family in a hot place has meat and no refrigerator. Name the two old ways they can keep it, and say how each works.
**Answer:** Drying — sun and air remove the water so bacteria and yeasts cannot grow. Salting — salt draws out moisture and kills bacteria.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two modern ways of keeping food the lesson gives.
**Answer:** Freezing, and canning (food sealed in tins, sometimes with added chemicals).
