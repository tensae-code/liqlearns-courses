# Hands-on — The Two-Way Relationship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-way walk
Walk around your home or school grounds with a partner. Stop at each natural thing you find — a tree, a spring, a patch of soil, a stream. At each stop say two sentences: "People change this by …" and "This changes people's lives by …". Five stops minimum.

## Dry it yourself
Take two small pieces of the same food (a slice of fruit or a piece of meat set aside for the lesson). Salt one piece well and leave both in the sun on a clean cloth. Look at them at the end of the day and again the next day. Which one kept better? Say why, using the words *water* and *bacteria*.

## The locality report
On one page, list your locality's resources — river, pond, spring, soil, forest, wildlife, and any others. Beside each one write one positive effect and one negative effect of how people use it today. This is the report the lesson's first activity asks for — bring it back to class or to your companion.
