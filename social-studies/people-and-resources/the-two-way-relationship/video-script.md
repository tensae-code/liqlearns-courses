# Video script — The Two-Way Relationship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a village. Here is its river.

The people farm the land. They build houses.
They cut roads. The land changes.
**People change the environment.**

Now look the other way.
The sun is hot. The people wear light clothes.
The rains come — or they do not. The people plant.
**The environment changes the people.**

Two arrows. Two ways. Both true at the same time.

Hot sun spoils meat fast.
So the people dry it. They salt it.
No water in the food — the spoiling things cannot grow.
The climate decided. The people answered.

Now you. Name one thing your place gives you.
Name one thing you do to your place.
Two ways.
