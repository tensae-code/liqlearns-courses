# The Two-Way Relationship — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons run in the book's own order:
first what people do to the environment, then what the environment
does back, then the food-preservation worked example that shows both
directions on one table.

## Sub lesson 1 — People change the environment
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the ways people change the environment (farming, building houses, roads, factories) and give a help and a harm from the learner's own locality.

## Sub lesson 2 — The environment shapes people
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say how climate changes the way people dress and eat, call the whole relationship two-way, and sort environment factors into biotic (living) and abiotic (non-living).

## Sub lesson 3 — Keeping food: how the climate decides
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: explain drying and salting (water removed, bacteria and yeasts cannot grow) against freezing and canning, and say why a hot climate forces the choice.
