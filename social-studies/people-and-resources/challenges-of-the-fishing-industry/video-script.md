The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 6 P6 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 81), teaches: Improving roads near fishing grounds so fish reach market; fishing industry challenges and solutions compared with other industries.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 6 P6 Social Studies SB): Tourism is taught as national homework — map the parks, weigh the landlocked disadvantage against South Africa, set up the anti-poaching unit — and the fishing unit ties grounds back to Unit 2's physical features and forward to roads that must be improved so fish can reach market.
