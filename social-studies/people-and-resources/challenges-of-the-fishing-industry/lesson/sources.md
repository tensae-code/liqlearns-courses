South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Primary 4): the economy is taught sector by sector through the child's own country — crop farming, forestry and White Nile fishing, each with its process, its problems (bad roads, pests, drought, floods, pollution) and what it gives the country — before "industry" is defined as turning raw materials into finished products, traditional industries first.

**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (South Sudan).** South Sudan was already counted in this lesson (Primary 6 pupil's book above); the Economics-expansion rows are stocked as depth for the same country, so the count does not rise.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 6 P6 Social Studies SB): Tourism is taught as national homework — map the parks, weigh the landlocked disadvantage against South Africa, set up the anti-poaching unit — and the fishing unit ties grounds back to Unit 2's physical features and forward to roads that must be improved so fish can reach market.
