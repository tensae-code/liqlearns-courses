# Hands-on — Alternative Energy In Pakistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Alternate sources of energy to generate power in Pakistan: solar (Quaid-e-Azam Solar Park, Bahawalpur), wind (Jhimpir, Thatta) and hydro (Tarbela on the Indus, Mangla on the Jhelum, Ghazi Barotha, Warsak). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
