Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 59–65), teaches: Bài 13: Vietnam's forestry and fisheries — forestry (14,874.3 thousand ha of forest in 2024, 42.0% cover, the regional table; some 24.0 million m³ of roundwood; planting, tending and protecting the forest) and fisheries (over 2,000 fish species and four great fishing grounds; the catch-and-farming table 2010–2024 — 9,656.3 thousand tons in all in 2024, farmed fish now ahead of the wild catch).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
