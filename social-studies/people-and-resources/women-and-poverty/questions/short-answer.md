# Women and poverty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "A substantial majority of the poor" in your own words.
**Answer:** Women form a substantial majority of the world's poor, and female-headed households are over-represented among the poorest.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Narrower doors" in your own words.
**Answer:** Women's access to education, formal employment, social security and programmes is narrower.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Inside the household, and the policy lesson" in your own words.
**Answer:** Resources inside households can be biased against females, and nutrition programmes work better targeted at women.
