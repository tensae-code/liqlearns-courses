# Women and poverty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "A substantial majority of the poor"?
a) Women form a substantial majority of the world's poor, and female-headed households are over-represented among the poorest  b) Women's access to education, formal employment, social security and programmes is narrower  c) Resources inside households can be biased against females, and nutrition programmes work better targeted at women
**Answer: a) Women form a substantial majority of the world's poor, and female-headed households are over-represented among the poorest**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Narrower doors"?
a) Women's access to education, formal employment, social security and programmes is narrower  b) Resources inside households can be biased against females, and nutrition programmes work better targeted at women  c) Women form a substantial majority of the world's poor, and female-headed households are over-represented among the poorest
**Answer: a) Women's access to education, formal employment, social security and programmes is narrower**
