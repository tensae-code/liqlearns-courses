# Women and poverty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Women form a substantial majority of the world's poor, and female-headed households are over-represented among the poorest.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Women's access to education, formal employment, social security and programmes is narrower.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Resources inside households can be biased against females, and nutrition programmes work better targeted at women.
**Answer: True**
