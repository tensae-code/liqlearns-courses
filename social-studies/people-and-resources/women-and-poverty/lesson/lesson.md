# Women and poverty

## Do it first

If poverty had a face in the statistics, whose would it be? Guess the pattern the data shows about women — then check it against the lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — A substantial majority of the poor

Women form a substantial majority of the world's poor: female-headed households are over-represented among the poorest, and women's earning capacity and control over household income are lower than men's.

## Sub lesson 2 — Narrower doors

Women's access to education, formal employment, social security and government programmes is narrower, and property customs and laws restrict rural women further. Each closed door is an income, a protection or an asset denied.

## Sub lesson 3 — Inside the household, and the policy lesson

Inside the household, resources are not shared equally — nutrition, medical care, education and inheritance can be biased against females. Since women spend a higher share of income on food and family health, nutrition programmes work better when targeted at women. Poverty policy that works only through men deepens the inequality it claims to fight.
