# Hands-on — Women and poverty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow one birr
Trace 100 birr given to a household through a man and through a woman, using the lesson's spending pattern. Show the food and health outcomes the book predicts for each route.

## Open one door
Choose one narrowed door (education, formal employment, property, programme access) for rural women. Write the custom or law that narrows it and one change that would widen it.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
