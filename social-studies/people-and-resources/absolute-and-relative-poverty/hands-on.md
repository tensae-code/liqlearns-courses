# Hands-on — Absolute and relative poverty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two neighbours
Draw the two neighbours from the do-first task, labelled absolute and relative poverty, with the test that separates them written under each.

## Line drawing
Set two poverty lines for one invented town: a subsistence line and a relative line at half the median income. Place six households and count the poor under each line, explaining the difference.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
