# Absolute and relative poverty — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Absolute poverty is inability to meet subsistence essentials, against lines like $1.25 or $2 a day.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Relative poverty is standing far below others in one's society while meeting basic needs.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The poor are disproportionately rural (about 80% in Africa and Asia), agricultural, female and children.
**Answer: True**
