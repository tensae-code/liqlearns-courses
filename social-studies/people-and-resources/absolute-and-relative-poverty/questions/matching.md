# Absolute and relative poverty — matching

Match each part to its right statement.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Absolute poverty — below subsistence — c
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Relative poverty — below your society — a
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Who the poor are — b

Statements:
a) Relative poverty is standing far below others in one's society while meeting basic needs
b) The poor are disproportionately rural (about 80% in Africa and Asia), agricultural, female and children
c) Absolute poverty is inability to meet subsistence essentials, against lines like $1.25 or $2 a day

**Answer key:** 1 — c; 2 — a; 3 — b
