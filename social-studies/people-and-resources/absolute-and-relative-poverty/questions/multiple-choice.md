# Absolute and relative poverty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Absolute poverty — below subsistence"?
a) Absolute poverty is inability to meet subsistence essentials, against lines like $1.25 or $2 a day  b) Relative poverty is standing far below others in one's society while meeting basic needs  c) The poor are disproportionately rural (about 80% in Africa and Asia), agricultural, female and children
**Answer: a) Absolute poverty is inability to meet subsistence essentials, against lines like $1.25 or $2 a day**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "Relative poverty — below your society"?
a) Relative poverty is standing far below others in one's society while meeting basic needs  b) The poor are disproportionately rural (about 80% in Africa and Asia), agricultural, female and children  c) Absolute poverty is inability to meet subsistence essentials, against lines like $1.25 or $2 a day
**Answer: a) Relative poverty is standing far below others in one's society while meeting basic needs**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which statement best matches "Who the poor are"?
a) The poor are disproportionately rural (about 80% in Africa and Asia), agricultural, female and children  b) Absolute poverty is inability to meet subsistence essentials, against lines like $1.25 or $2 a day  c) Relative poverty is standing far below others in one's society while meeting basic needs
**Answer: a) The poor are disproportionately rural (about 80% in Africa and Asia), agricultural, female and children**
