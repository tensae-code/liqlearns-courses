# Absolute and relative poverty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Absolute poverty — below subsistence" in your own words.
**Answer:** Absolute poverty is inability to meet subsistence essentials, against lines like $1.25 or $2 a day.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "Relative poverty — below your society" in your own words.
**Answer:** Relative poverty is standing far below others in one's society while meeting basic needs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "Who the poor are" in your own words.
**Answer:** The poor are disproportionately rural (about 80% in Africa and Asia), agricultural, female and children.
