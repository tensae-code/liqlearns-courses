# Belarus Mineral Resources

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the mineral resources of Belarus, as Belarus's Grade 10 book teaches them in its theme on the country's nature. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The mineral resources of Belarus — what the book places there

In its nature theme, after geological structure, Belarus's Grade 10 book gives mineral resources their own paragraph (Theme II §10): the useful minerals found in the country are part of its natural wealth, studied between the making of the land and its relief.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mineral resources as part of Belarus's natural wealth

Mineral resources are the part of nature people dig and drill for: the book treats them as one component of the country's nature, in the same sequence as relief, climate, waters, soils and living things.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
