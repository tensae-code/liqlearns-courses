# Video script — Belarus Mineral Resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In its nature theme, after geological structure, Belarus's Grade 10 book gives mineral resources their own paragraph (Theme II §10): the useful minerals found in the country are part of its natural wealth, studied between the making of the land and its relief.

Mineral resources are the part of nature people dig and drill for: the book treats them as one component of the country's nature, in the same sequence as relief, climate, waters, soils and living things.
