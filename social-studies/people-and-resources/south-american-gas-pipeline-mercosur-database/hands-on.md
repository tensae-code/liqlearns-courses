# Hands-on — The South American Gas Pipeline — a Mercosur Database

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Start a database of your own
Take one subject — your school, your market, your river. Make three folders or three pages: maps, pictures, numbers. Put two entries in each. You have organised a database, the notebook's way.

## Trace the pipeline
On a map of South America, draw a line for a pipeline carrying gas from one country across a border into another. Under the map, write two numbers a database would hold about it — for example its length and the countries it joins.
