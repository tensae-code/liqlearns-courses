## Sub lesson 1 — Building a database about Mercosur

In Argentina, the teacher notebook Cuadernos para el aula: Ciencias Sociales gives a worked teaching proposal in its third axis (Eje 3): La organización de una base de datos sobre el Mercosur — organising a database about Mercosur. The learners do not only read about the bloc: they gather its information and organise it, so the bloc can be looked up, compared and questioned from what they have collected.

## Sub lesson 2 — The gas pipeline case — maps, images and statistics

The database is built through one real case: el caso del gasoducto sudamericano — the South American gas pipeline. A pipeline that carries gas across borders is physical integration you can trace: the database holds it in maps, images and statistics, the three kinds of material the proposal names. The case gives the database its content; the database gives the case its order.
