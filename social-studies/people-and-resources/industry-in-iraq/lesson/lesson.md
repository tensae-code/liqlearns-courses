## Sub lesson 1 — Industry In Iraq

Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography* (printed p. 98), teaches: Ch. 4 applied study: The geography of industry in Iraq — the country's energy, water and mineral resources; the problems of the oil wells; electricity (the Ma'aqil thermal station for the south); pharmaceuticals; cement; and brickworks spread over 12 governorates, Baghdad first. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography): a human-geography course in six chapters — population, cities and countryside, agriculture, industry and mining, transport and trade, political geography and tourism — and every chapter closes with an applied study of Iraq itself, so the world pattern is always brought home. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
