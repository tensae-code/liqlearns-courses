# Hands-on — The fifth sector — quinary activities (teacher-guide extension)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fifth step
Draw the activity chain as five steps, the fifth drawn in a different colour and labelled guide-only. Under it, write the pairing rule in your own words.

## Spot the quinary
List five real jobs and place each in a sector; for any quinary one — a top decision-maker — say what decisions it makes for the sectors below.
