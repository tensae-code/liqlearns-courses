# The fifth sector — quinary activities (teacher-guide extension) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The fifth class is called —
a) quaternary  b) quintary major  c) quinary
**Answer: c) quinary**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Quinary work is —
a) factory work  b) the highest decision-making and knowledge services  c) farm work
**Answer: b) the highest decision-making and knowledge services**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The student book teaches —
a) four classes  b) five classes  c) three classes
**Answer: a) four classes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The pairing rule says the fifth sector is —
a) to replace the four  b) to be hidden from learners  c) placed beside the four as a guide extension, never silently merged
**Answer: c) placed beside the four as a guide extension, never silently merged**
