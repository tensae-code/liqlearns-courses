# The fifth sector — quinary activities (teacher-guide extension)

## Do it first

Four kinds of work fill the student book. The teacher’s book quietly adds a fifth. Read what the fifth sector is — and why it is labelled guide-only.

## Sub lesson 1 — Five, not four (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide classifies economic activities into five: primary, secondary, tertiary, quaternary and quinary. Quinary activities are the top layer of services — the highest-level decision-making and knowledge work: the people and bodies whose decisions direct the other sectors. This fifth class is the guide’s extension; the student book teaches four.

## Sub lesson 2 — Holding both honestly (teacher guide — evidence)

The pairing rule of this course: the student book’s four-class lesson stands as the pupil teaching, and the quinary sector is placed beside it as a guide extension, never silently merged. A learner who meets five sectors in a marking key will know exactly where the fifth came from.
