# Video script — Jamaica's Mineral Wealth — Bauxite and Limestone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jamaica's Grades 7–9 Social Studies guide (Grade 7 Term 2 Unit 1, printed p. 26) teaches Jamaica's mineral deposits, their extraction and the nearby communities, with map work — the lesson's own title names the two minerals it headlines: bauxite and limestone. Where the minerals lie, how they are taken out, and the communities that live beside the work are taught together, on the map. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence.

Jamaica's own way (Grades 7–9 Social Studies, curriculum guide — evidence): one guide carries all three secondary grades, each year mixing Civics, heritage and a strong resources-and-environment Geography spine — natural and human resources, movements of the Earth, Caribbean hazards, climate change, green technology and tourism — taught through Jamaican case material (bauxite communities, Caribbean disasters). That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
