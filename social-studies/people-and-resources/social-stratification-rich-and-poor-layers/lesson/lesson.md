Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Social stratification — society in layers

Mongolia's Grade 11 book opens with social stratification: society is not one flat crowd but layers — strata — that differ in wealth, occupation, standing and power. The topic teaches what the layers are, what puts people in them, and why the layering matters for how a society works.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the book's own teaching way

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the final year is the state-and-world year — cultural globalization and freedom, monetary policy, foreign trade and investment, and Mongolia's signature “third neighbour” and soft-power policies taught as named topics. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
