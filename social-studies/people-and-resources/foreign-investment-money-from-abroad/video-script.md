# Video script — Foreign Investment — Money from Abroad

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 11 book gives foreign investment its own topic: money from abroad put into a country's enterprises and projects. The topic teaches what foreign investment is, why countries welcome it, and what questions it raises — investment brings capital, work and skills, and it also takes a return out of the country.

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the final year is the state-and-world year — cultural globalization and freedom, monetary policy, foreign trade and investment, and Mongolia's signature “third neighbour” and soft-power policies taught as named topics. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
