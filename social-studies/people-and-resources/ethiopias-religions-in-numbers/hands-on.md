# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s religions in numbers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pie drawing
Draw the census pie with five slices sized by feel from the lesson (largest first), and label each. Say why exact sizes need the census table, not a guess.

## Portrait talk
Explain to a partner the difference between a composition and a ranking, using the religion pie and a race with five runners as your two pictures.
