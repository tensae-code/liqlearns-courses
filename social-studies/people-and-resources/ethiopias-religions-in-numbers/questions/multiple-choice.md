# Ethiopia’s religions in numbers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The religion shares come from the —
a) 1994 school survey  b) 2020 estimate in the guide  c) 2007 census
**Answer: c) 2007 census**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Groups in the census pie include —
a) five foreign religions  b) Orthodox, Muslim, Protestant, Catholic, traditional/other  c) only Orthodox and Muslim
**Answer: b) Orthodox, Muslim, Protestant, Catholic, traditional/other**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A composition is —
a) a portrait of the whole, not a ranking  b) a league table  c) a prediction
**Answer: a) a portrait of the whole, not a ranking**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shares change —
a) every week  b) never  c) slowly, as populations grow at different rates
**Answer: c) slowly, as populations grow at different rates**
