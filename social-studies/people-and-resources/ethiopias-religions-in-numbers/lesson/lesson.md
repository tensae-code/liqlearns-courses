# Ethiopia’s religions in numbers

## Do it first

A pie sliced by faith: that is how a census draws a country’s religions. Read the slices of Ethiopia.

## Sub lesson 1 — The census shares

The 2007 census states Ethiopia’s religious composition as shares of the whole population: Orthodox Christians, Muslims, Protestants, Catholics, and traditional and other beliefs. Each share is a slice of one pie — the slices must add to the whole country.

[Picture 21049 — a pie chart of the census religion shares]

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading a composition

Shares describe the whole at census time: they show the weight of each community in the nation, and they change slowly as populations grow at different rates. A composition is a portrait, not a ranking — each named community is part of the one country the census counted.
