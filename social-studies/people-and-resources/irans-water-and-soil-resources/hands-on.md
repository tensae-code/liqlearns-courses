# Hands-on — Irans Water And Soil Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 14 Water and soil resources (منابع آب و خاک) — the lesson's real title in the book (the contents page names it 'population movement and migration'): the people of a region need resources to secure their food; Iran has limited water resources, and the pupil is asked to say why, from the climate chapters; the map of Iran's important rivers, the chief water source of city and village people; the qanat (قنات / کاریز), an invention of the ancient Iranians over two thousand years ago; water consumption, and a school's visit to the city's water-treatment plant; our dependence on soil, and what destroys soil (over-use of food materials and repeated cropping among the examples). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
