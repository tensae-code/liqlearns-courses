## Sub lesson 1 — How American economies work

Ukraine's Grade 10 book studies the features of the economies of American countries in §28. Learners compare economies across the region — what they take from nature, what they make and what they sell to the world — in the book's sector order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ukraine's way — regions and countries in one order

Ukraine learns this inside its "Geography: Regions and Countries" course: A "Geography: Regions and Countries" course — every region is done in the same order (position, nature/resources, population, primary/secondary/tertiary sectors) then typological country studies with rubrics (country visiting-card, capital, pride of the country, unique experience), ending with Ukraine's own place in the world; research tasks (Дослідження) embedded in chapters. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
