# Hands-on — Dependency across Africa's regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Household count
Count your household (or class): how many young, how many working-age, how many old? Compute dependents per 100 working-age. Compare with a partner's count and discuss which household carries more — and why.

## Regional bars
Draw the lesson's bar chart yourself: two bars for the regional loads, with the median-age figures — Africa 19.7, Europe 42.5 — written beneath as the explanation.
