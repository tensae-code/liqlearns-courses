# Dependency across Africa's regions — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define age dependency.
**Answer:** Age dependency counts how many dependents (young plus old) stand behind each hundred working-age people — the load each working group carries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is Sub-Saharan Africa's load the heaviest?
**Answer:** Because its age structure is the youngest — a large share of its population is children, filling the dependent column while the working share is smaller.
