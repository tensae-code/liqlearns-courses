# Dependency across Africa's regions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three groups of the dependency count are —
a) rich, middle, poor  b) young, working-age, old  c) urban, rural, migrant  d) students, teachers, farmers
**Answer: b) young, working-age, old** — dependents = young + old.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's median age as printed (mid-2020) is —
a) 42.5 years  b) 19.7 years  c) 30.0 years  d) 12.0 years
**Answer: b) 19.7 years** — against Europe's 42.5 — half of Africans under twenty.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The heaviest regional dependency load is in —
a) Northern Africa  b) Sub-Saharan Africa  c) the islands only  d) Europe
**Answer: b) Sub-Saharan Africa** — its population is the youngest.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A heavy dependency load means each school, clinic and job is shared from —
a) a larger working base  b) a smaller working base  c) no base  d) foreign aid only
**Answer: b) a smaller working base** — fewer workers carrying more dependents.
