# Dependency across Africa's regions — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dependents are the young and old together.
**Answer: True** — those the working group supports.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Europe's median age (42.5) is lower than Africa's (19.7).
**Answer: False** — 42.5 is higher — Europe's population is older.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Northern Africa's dependency figures are heavier than Sub-Saharan Africa's.
**Answer: False** — Northern figures are markedly lighter; Sub-Saharan carries the heavy load.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A young population also means a large working generation is coming.
**Answer: True** — the lesson's closing balance — if educated and employed.
