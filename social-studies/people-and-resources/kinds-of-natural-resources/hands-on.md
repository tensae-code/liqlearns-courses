# Hands-on — Kinds of Natural Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The resource hunt
Walk your compound, street or school grounds with a notebook. Write down every natural resource you can see or touch — at least ten. Do not write made things (a chair is made; the wood it came from is the resource).

## Four boxes on the ground
With chalk, string or four cloths, lay the four boxes on the ground: living / non-living / can run out / renews itself. Carry small samples or name cards of the resources you found and place each one twice — once for the living sort, once for the coming-back sort. A sample you cannot place is a question to bring back.

## The too-fast check
Choose one renewable resource near your home — trees, grass, river water. Watch how people use it for a day. Are they taking it faster than it replaces itself? Write two sentences of evidence.
