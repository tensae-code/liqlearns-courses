# Kinds of Natural Resources — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil is a biotic resource.
**Answer: False** — soil is abiotic (non-living); minerals, water and soil are the book's abiotic examples.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Plants and animals are biotic resources.
**Answer: True.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Natural gas is a renewable resource.
**Answer: False** — natural gas is exhaustible/non-renewable, like minerals, oil and coal.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A renewable resource can never be depleted.
**Answer: False** — used faster than it replaces itself, even a renewable resource can be depleted.
