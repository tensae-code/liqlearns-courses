# Kinds of Natural Resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list is all biotic resources?
a) Minerals, water, soil  b) Plants and animals  c) Oil, coal, sunlight
**Answer: b) Plants and animals** — biotic means living.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Water belongs to which sort?
a) Biotic  b) Abiotic  c) Neither sort
**Answer: b) Abiotic** — water is non-living, even though nothing lives without it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which resource is exhaustible?
a) Sunlight  b) Coal  c) Air
**Answer: b) Coal** — once dug and burned, it does not renew itself.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A forest that is cut faster than it regrows is —
a) safe, because forests are renewable  b) being depleted, even though it is renewable  c) exhaustible by definition
**Answer: b)** — a renewable resource used faster than it replaces itself can still be used up.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which resource is renewable AND abiotic?
a) A tree  b) Water  c) Coal
**Answer: b) Water** — non-living, and it renews itself.
