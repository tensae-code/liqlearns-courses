# Kinds of Natural Resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Place a tree and a lump of coal in BOTH sorts (living/non-living, and used-up/renews-itself).
**Answer:** A tree is biotic (living) and renewable. Coal is abiotic (non-living) and exhaustible.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A village cuts ten trees for every one that grows back. The forest is renewable — so is the village safe? Explain.
**Answer:** No. Even a renewable resource can be depleted when it is used faster than it replaces itself; at that rate the forest will finish.
