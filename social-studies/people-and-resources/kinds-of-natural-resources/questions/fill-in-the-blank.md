# Kinds of Natural Resources — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Living resources such as plants and animals are called ______ resources.
**Answer: biotic.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Oil, coal and natural gas are ______ resources, because they can be used up and do not renew themselves.
**Answer: exhaustible (non-renewable).**
