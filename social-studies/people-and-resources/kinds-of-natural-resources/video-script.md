# Video script — Kinds of Natural Resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pick up a stone. Pick a leaf.

The leaf was alive. The stone was not.
**Living** things in one box — plants, animals.
**Non-living** in the other — minerals, water, soil.
That is sort one.

Now burn coal. It is gone. It does not come back.
Feel the sun. It comes back every morning.
**Can be used up** in one box — oil, coal, gas, minerals.
**Renews itself** in the other — air, water, plants, sunlight.
That is sort two.

But listen. A forest renews itself.
Cut it faster than it grows — and one day, no forest.
Even a renewing resource can be used up.

Now you. Take three things around you.
Alive — or not? Comes back — or not?
