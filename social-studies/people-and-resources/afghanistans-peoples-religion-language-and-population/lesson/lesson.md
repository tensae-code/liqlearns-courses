## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)): primary social studies starts from the child's own world — family, village, city — and only then opens the map: directions, Afghanistan's boundaries and mountains and rivers, the continents and oceans, and finally the Earth's shape, equator, latitude/longitude and movements.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 12 Geography (Dari)): the final year is pure human geography — Afghanistan's borders side by side (north, north-east, east/south, west), its population and ethnic make-up, religion and language, transport and communications, then the same themes for the world: population, races, religions, languages, global communications, and global environmental problems to close the whole twelve-year course. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
