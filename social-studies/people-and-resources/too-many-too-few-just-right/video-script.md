# Video script — Too Many, Too Few, Just Right

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A scale. People on one side. Resources on the other.

The people side crashes down. **Too many.**
Little money each. No jobs. Thin food. People leaving.
That is **over-population**.

Now the other side sits heavy. **Too few.**
Rich land, no hands to work it. Workers wanted. People arriving.
That is **under-population**.

Now — level. Quiet. Balanced.
People enough. Resources enough. **Just right.**
That is **optimum population** — the balanced relationship.

And countries push the scale.
Too many? Slow the births — **anti-natalist**.
Too few? Encourage births — **pro-natalist**.

Now you. I read the signs — you name the state.
Low income each… no work… people leaving…
Which pan is down?
