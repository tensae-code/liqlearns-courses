# Too Many, Too Few, Just Right — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Over-population means —
a) a country with a large area  b) an excess of population against available resources  c) any country with cities
**Answer: b) an excess of population against available resources.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a sign of over-population?
a) High per-capita income  b) Inward migration  c) Malnutrition and famine
**Answer: c) Malnutrition and famine** — with low per-capita income, high unemployment and outward migration.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Under-population means —
a) too few people for the full use of resources  b) a country with no government  c) people living only in villages
**Answer: a) too few people for the full use of resources.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a sign of under-population?
a) Outward migration  b) Low unemployment and inward migration  c) Malnutrition
**Answer: b) Low unemployment and inward migration.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Optimum population is —
a) exactly one million people  b) the balanced population–resource relationship  c) the largest population a country can force onto its land
**Answer: b) the balanced population–resource relationship.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** An anti-natalist policy aims to —
a) increase the birth rate  b) reduce the birth rate and slow population growth  c) move people between regions
**Answer: b) reduce the birth rate and slow population growth.**
