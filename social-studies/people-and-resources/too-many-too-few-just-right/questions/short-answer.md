# Too Many, Too Few, Just Right — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three signs by which you can read over-population in a place.
**Answer:** Any three of: low per-capita income; high unemployment/underemployment; malnutrition and famine; low living standards; outward migration.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A territory has rich unworked land, employers begging for workers, and newcomers arriving every month. Name its state and the evidence.
**Answer:** Under-population — too few people for full use of its resources; low unemployment and inward migration are its signs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A country's population is balanced with its resources: people enough to use them fully, resources enough to carry the people well. Name the state, and say whether it needs a natalist policy.
**Answer:** Optimum population — the balanced relationship. It needs neither policy pushing births up nor down; policies exist to repair imbalance.
