# Too Many, Too Few, Just Right — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In an over-populated place, people tend to migrate outward, looking for a life elsewhere.
**Answer: True.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An under-populated country suffers high unemployment because there are too many workers.
**Answer: False** — under-population shows LOW unemployment; workers are wanted, and people migrate inward.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A pro-natalist policy is the answer a country chooses when it is pressing toward over-population.
**Answer: False** — pro-natalist policy increases the birth rate; it answers under-population. Over-population is answered by anti-natalist policy.
