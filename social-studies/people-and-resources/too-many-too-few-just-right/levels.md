# Too Many, Too Few, Just Right — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its SUB
LESSONS (2026-11-08 restructure — README, "Units, Lessons and Sub
Lessons"). Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as
before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the
by-sub-lesson view. The sub lessons are the three states of the
population–resource balance, in the guide's order: too many, too
few, just right — with the two policy answers closing the third.

## Sub lesson 1 — Too many: over-population
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define over-population (excess of population against resources) and name its signs — low per-capita income, high unemployment/underemployment, malnutrition and famine, low living standards, outward migration.

## Sub lesson 2 — Too few: under-population
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define under-population (too few people for full use of resources) and name its signs — high per-capita income, low unemployment, inward migration.

## Sub lesson 3 — Just right: optimum population, and the two policy answers
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: define optimum population as the balanced population–resource relationship, and match anti-natalist (reduce birth rate, slow growth) and pro-natalist (increase birth rate and growth) policies to the imbalances they answer.
