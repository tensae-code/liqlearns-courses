# Hands-on — Global poverty and SDG 1

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shrink the map
Draw a world map outline with the extreme-poverty share shrinking everywhere except a stubborn sub-Saharan band. Annotate the two dates (36% in 1990, 10% in 2015) as the map's legend.

## Poverty down, inequality up
Write four lines explaining how extreme poverty can fall while global inequality rises. Use China, sub-Saharan Africa, and one mechanism (globalisation or technology) in the explanation.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
