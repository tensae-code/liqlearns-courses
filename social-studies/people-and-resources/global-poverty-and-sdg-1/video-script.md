# Video script — Global poverty and SDG 1

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Extreme poverty fell from 36% of the world in 1990 to 10% in 2015, with China lifting millions out. It is one of the fastest improvements in human welfare ever recorded.

Yet in 2010 over 1.2 billion people lived under $1.25 a day and 2.4 billion under $2, and more than 700 million remain in extreme poverty today — most of those under $1.90 a day in sub-Saharan Africa, where rates barely moved from 1950 to 2000.

SDG 1 — end poverty in all its forms — is the world's stated answer, and the World Bank-backed Poverty Reduction Strategy Paper process, which Ethiopia follows, is its working machinery. Meanwhile globalisation, technology and policy have pushed global inequality up even as poverty fell.
