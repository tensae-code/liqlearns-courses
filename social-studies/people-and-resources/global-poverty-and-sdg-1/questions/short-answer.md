# Global poverty and SDG 1 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain "Progress — 36% to 10%" in your own words.
**Answer:** Extreme poverty fell from 36% of the world in 1990 to 10% in 2015.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain "The remainder — billions under $2" in your own words.
**Answer:** More than 700 million remain in extreme poverty, most in sub-Saharan Africa.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain "The institutional answers" in your own words.
**Answer:** SDG 1 aims to end poverty in all its forms; Ethiopia follows the PRSP process.
