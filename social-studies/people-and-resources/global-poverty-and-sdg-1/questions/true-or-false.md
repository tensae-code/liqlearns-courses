# Global poverty and SDG 1 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Extreme poverty fell from 36% of the world in 1990 to 10% in 2015.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** More than 700 million remain in extreme poverty, most in sub-Saharan Africa.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** SDG 1 aims to end poverty in all its forms; Ethiopia follows the PRSP process.
**Answer: True**
