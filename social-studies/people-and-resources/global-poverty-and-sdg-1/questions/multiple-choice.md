# Global poverty and SDG 1 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which statement best matches "Progress — 36% to 10%"?
a) Extreme poverty fell from 36% of the world in 1990 to 10% in 2015  b) More than 700 million remain in extreme poverty, most in sub-Saharan Africa  c) SDG 1 aims to end poverty in all its forms; Ethiopia follows the PRSP process
**Answer: a) Extreme poverty fell from 36% of the world in 1990 to 10% in 2015**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which statement best matches "The remainder — billions under $2"?
a) More than 700 million remain in extreme poverty, most in sub-Saharan Africa  b) SDG 1 aims to end poverty in all its forms; Ethiopia follows the PRSP process  c) Extreme poverty fell from 36% of the world in 1990 to 10% in 2015
**Answer: a) More than 700 million remain in extreme poverty, most in sub-Saharan Africa**
