Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 7 Geography (Dari)* (printed p. 57), teaches: Ch. 2 Lesson 20: livestock farming, pastures and forests of Afghanistan. Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 7 Geography (Dari)): the Dari MoE pattern — the world topic is taught first in the abstract (sphere, equator, latitude), then re-taught as Afghanistan's own geography (its position, relief, six climate regions, river basins, lakes, minerals, fuels), then widened to the continent (Asia). Every lesson ends with in-class group activity, questions and an out-of-class task naming the pupil's own locality.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 11 Geography (Dari)): an economic-geography year in two halves — first Afghanistan (farming and livestock, energy and mines, industries and trade), then the same themes for the world (water and farming, energy, mines and industries, trade and tourism), each Afghanistan chapter closing with a "review of the situation" lesson on the country's own state.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)): primary social studies starts from the child's own world — family, village, city — and only then opens the map: directions, Afghanistan's boundaries and mountains and rivers, the continents and oceans, and finally the Earth's shape, equator, latitude/longitude and movements.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari)): Grade 5 social studies is an economic tour of Afghanistan for children — public services first (water, electricity, telecom, post, transport), then farming crop by crop, livestock animal by animal, industries craft by craft and factory by factory, trade and the country's (dry) ports, ending with environment, climate and mines.
