# The Atlantic slave trade — why Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did America's own peoples fail as the labour supply, in the book's words?
**Answer:** Under European torment and new diseases, they could not supply the labour the farms and mines needed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the book's three reasons Europe turned to Africa.
**Answer:** A tropical climate like the New World's; cost; and African chiefs' readiness to sell war captives and others.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three American destinations and what captives worked on in each.
**Answer:** Brazil — coffee and sugar; the Caribbean — sugar; southern North America — cotton and tobacco.
