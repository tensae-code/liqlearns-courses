# The Atlantic slave trade — why Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Europeans needed labour in America above all for —
a) Ships and maps  b) Farms and mines  c) Churches  d) Schools
**Answer: b) Farms and mines** — America's farm and mining wealth needed much labour.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Enslaving America's own peoples could not supply the labour because they —
a) Refused all work  b) Suffered under European torment and new diseases  c) Lived too far away  d) Had no farms
**Answer: b) Suffered under European torment and new diseases** — Under torment and new diseases they could not supply it.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The book calls America's own peoples —
a) The Red Indians  b) The Slavs  c) The Fante  d) The colons
**Answer: a) The Red Indians** — The book's own term, kept as printed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** One reason Europe turned to Africa was —
a) Africans lived in a tropical climate like the New World's  b) Africa was nearest to Europe  c) Africans spoke European languages  d) Africa had steam ships
**Answer: a) Africans lived in a tropical climate like the New World's** — Climate fit is the first named reason.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** African chiefs encouraged the trade by —
a) Building ports  b) Being ready to sell war captives and others  c) Refusing all trade  d) Freeing captives
**Answer: b) Being ready to sell war captives and others** — Their readiness to sell captives encouraged the trade.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Most captives were taken to work in —
a) European factories  b) Brazil, the Caribbean and southern North America  c) Asia's mines  d) North Africa's farms
**Answer: b) Brazil, the Caribbean and southern North America** — Brazil's coffee and sugar, the Caribbean's sugar, southern North America's cotton and tobacco.

**7. (Sub lesson 3) ** The guide says Henry's voyages turned to slaves because —
a) Gold was illegal  b) The voyages had to pay for themselves; gold yielded only dust, while the slave trade proved profitable  c) Portugal lacked ships  d) Africa demanded it
**Answer: b) The voyages had to pay for themselves; gold yielded only dust, while the slave trade proved profitable** — economics first — guide evidence.

**8. (Sub lesson 3) ** The guide's answer on African participation is that suppliers received —
a) Nothing  b) Guns and goods from Europeans, making the trade lucrative for both sides  c) Land in Europe  d) Ships
**Answer: b) Guns and goods from Europeans, making the trade lucrative for both sides** — the gun cycle's fuel — guide evidence.
