# The Atlantic slave trade — why Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Europeans first tried enslaving America's own peoples before turning to Africa.
**Answer: True** — That is the book's order.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cost was one of the reasons Europe turned to Africa.
**Answer: True** — Climate, cost, and chiefs ready to sell.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Most captives went to Europe itself.
**Answer: False** — Most went to the Americas.
