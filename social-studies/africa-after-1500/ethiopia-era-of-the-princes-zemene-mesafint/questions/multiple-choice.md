# Ethiopia — Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Era of the Princes, emperors became —
a) Puppets  b) Stronger than ever  c) Priests only
**Answer: a) Puppets** — emperors become puppets, in the book's word.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who really ruled Ethiopia in pieces?
a) Foreign sultans  b) Regional lords  c) Merchant companies
**Answer: b) Regional lords** — regional lords rule Ethiopia in pieces.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Whom did the people wait for, by prophecy?
a) A foreign king  b) No one  c) A Theodore who would reunite them
**Answer: c) A Theodore who would reunite them** — the prophesied Theodore.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Era of the Princes runs up to about —
a) 1855  b) 1500  c) 1900
**Answer: a) 1855** — society and government to 1855.
