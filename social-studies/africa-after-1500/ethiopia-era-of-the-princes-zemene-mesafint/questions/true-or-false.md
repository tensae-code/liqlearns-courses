# Ethiopia — Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint) — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Regional lords ruled Ethiopia in pieces during the Era of the Princes.
**Answer: True** — that is the era's defining fact.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The emperors of this era held firm, united power.
**Answer: False** — they became puppets.
