# Ethiopia — Era of the Princes (Zemene Mesafint)

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In Ethiopia's Era of the Princes, emperors become puppets while regional lords rule the country in pieces — and the people wait for a prophesied Theodore to reunite them. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Emperors become puppets

Ethiopia's society, economy and government to 1855 slide into the Era of the Princes: emperors become puppets while regional lords rule in pieces.

In your mind's eye: a puppet emperor on a throne while regional lords rule their own districts on a map behind him. The student explains who really ruled and whom the people waited for.

## Sub lesson 2 — Waiting for a prophesied Theodore

Divided and ruled by lords, the people wait for a prophesied Theodore to reunite them — the hope that closes this part of the story.
