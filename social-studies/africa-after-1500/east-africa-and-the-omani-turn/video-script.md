# Video script — East Africa and the Omani turn

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — The Swahili coast under Portuguese pressure. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
After 1500 the Portuguese seized the Swahili coast's trade by force — bombarding cities, building forts like Fort Jesus at Mombasa, and taxing the monsoon commerce that had enriched the coast for centuries.

The city-states resisted, endured and adapted; Portuguese power on the coast was real but thin, resting on ships and strongpoints.. Watch the names appear one by one: Fort Jesus, monsoon commerce, strongpoint. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — The Omani turn. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
In time the coast's balance swung to Oman: Omani Arabs expelled the Portuguese from Mombasa and built their own maritime power, until an Omani sultan ruled from Zanzibar over the coast's trade.

The turn matters because it was not European: an Arab-African power, rooted at Zanzibar, ran the western Indian Ocean in the age before the Scramble.. Watch the names appear one by one: Omani turn, Zanzibar, western Indian Ocean. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Inland peoples and their states. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Behind the coast, inland peoples built and rebuilt their own states and trade networks, feeding ivory and other goods to whichever power held the ports.

Coast and interior were one economy with two stages — never read the forts without the caravans behind them.. Watch the names appear one by one: ivory, caravans, one economy. This is the part of the story to hold.
