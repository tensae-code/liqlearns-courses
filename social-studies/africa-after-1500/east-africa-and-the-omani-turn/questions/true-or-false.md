# East Africa and the Omani turn — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The seasonal-wind trade the Portuguese tried to tax — this is monsoon commerce.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Fort Jesus means a fort holding a coast it could not otherwise rule.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The island capital of Omani power on the coast — this is Zanzibar.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, Omani turn means the sea-world the Omani sultanate came to run.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The inland convoys linking interior states to the coast — this is caravans.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, ivory means coast and interior as a single trading system.
**Answer: False**
