# East Africa and the Omani turn — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Fort Jesus?
a) monsoon commerce
b) strongpoint
c) the Portuguese fortress at Mombasa
**Answer: c) the Portuguese fortress at Mombasa**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Omani turn?
a) Zanzibar
b) western Indian Ocean
c) the swing of coastal power from Portugal to Oman
**Answer: c) the swing of coastal power from Portugal to Oman**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is ivory?
a) caravans
b) a chief inland product carried to the coastal ports
c) one economy
**Answer: b) a chief inland product carried to the coastal ports**
