# East Africa and the Omani turn — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'a fort holding a coast it could not otherwise rule' is ______.
**Answer: strongpoint**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the sea-world the Omani sultanate came to run' is ______.
**Answer: western Indian Ocean**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'coast and interior as a single trading system' is ______.
**Answer: one economy**
