# Video script — Spain and the Knights in Tripoli

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Libya's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After the Almohads fall in 1269 the Maghreb fragments and Spain strikes the coast: 120 ships bombard Tripoli in July 1510, two thousand are killed and the mosques looted — an occupation that lasts until the Ottomans come in 1551.
