# Hands-on — Spain and the Knights in Tripoli

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Spain and the Knights in Tripoli — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Spanish and the Knights of St John, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
