# From treaties to military conquest — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: From treaties to military conquest.
**Answer: African chiefs sign what they think are trade treaties, then watch European troops move in — the paper road from treaty to military conquest (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
