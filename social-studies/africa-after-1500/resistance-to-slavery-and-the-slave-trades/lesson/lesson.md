# Resistance to slavery and the slave trades

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The enslaved resist at every step — and slavery is named for what it is: a crime against humanity. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Resistance at every step

the enslaved resist at every step of slavery and the slave trades (UA4 Les résistances à l'esclavage et aux traites)

In your mind's eye: enslaved Africans resisting — refusing, escaping and fighting back along the trade routes. Name one form of resistance.

## Sub lesson 2 — A crime against humanity

the lesson names slavery a crime against humanity (UA4 L'esclavage, crime contre l'humanité)
