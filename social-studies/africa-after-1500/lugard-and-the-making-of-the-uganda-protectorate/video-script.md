# Video script — Lugard and the making of the Uganda protectorate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Uganda is ruled 1888–1894 by a company, IBEACO, personified by Captain Frederick Lugard (in Uganda Dec 1890–May 1892); commissioners follow, Coryndon forms LEGCO in 1921 and Archer opens Makerere.
