# Hands-on — The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a continent partitioned on paper

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The empty row
Draw the Berlin table: fourteen flags on one side, and opposite them an empty row of chairs. Under the drawing, write whose chairs they should have been and why they stood empty.

## Thirty-year ruler
Make a timeline strip 1884–1914. Mark the baseline at its start ('about 10% ruled') and the outcome at its end ('nearly all ruled'). The strip's shortness is the Scramble's lesson.
