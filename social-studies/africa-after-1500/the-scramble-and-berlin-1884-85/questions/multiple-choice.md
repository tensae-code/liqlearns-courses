# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a continent partitioned on paper — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Scramble for Africa ran between —
a) 1800 and 1830  b) 1884 and 1914  c) 1914 and 1945  d) 1960 and 1990
**Answer: b) 1884 and 1914** — Between 1884 and 1914 Europe took control of the continent.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The partitioned interior was divided into —
a) Kingdoms and republics  b) Protectorates, colonies and 'free-trade areas'  c) Provinces of one empire  d) Free African states
**Answer: b) Protectorates, colonies and 'free-trade areas'** — Protectorates, colonies and free-trade areas.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Berlin Conference was called to —
a) Free Africa  b) Regulate colonial rivalries before they became wars among Europeans  c) Crown an African emperor  d) End the slave trade only
**Answer: b) Regulate colonial rivalries before they became wars among Europeans** — It regulated rivalries among Europeans.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Berlin Conference was also known as —
a) The Vienna Congress  b) The 'Congo Conference'  c) The Versailles Conference  d) The Bandung Conference
**Answer: b) The 'Congo Conference'** — The guide names it the Congo Conference (teacher guide — evidence).

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The conference was proposed by —
a) Germany  b) Britain  c) Portugal  d) Belgium
**Answer: c) Portugal** — Portugal proposed it (teacher guide — evidence).

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** How many European countries met at Berlin, and who was not invited?
a) Seven; the Americans  b) Fourteen; Africans were not invited nor involved  c) Twenty; the Russians  d) Fourteen; the Portuguese
**Answer: b) Fourteen; Africans were not invited nor involved** — Fourteen European countries; Africans were neither invited nor involved (teacher guide — evidence).
