# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a continent partitioned on paper — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Scramble partitioned Africa slowly over two centuries.
**Answer: False** — It took the continent between 1884 and 1914 — about thirty years.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** African rulers sat at the Berlin Conference as partners.
**Answer: False** — Africans were not invited nor involved, though the conference was about Africa.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** France, Germany, Great Britain and Portugal were the major players at Berlin.
**Answer: True** — The guide names those four as the major players.
