# The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a continent partitioned on paper — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the Berlin Conference actually do, and what did it not do?
**Answer:** It regulated European colonial rivalries so the partition could proceed without wars among Europeans; it did not consult or include Africans, whose continent was its subject.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four major players at Berlin, from the teacher guide.
**Answer:** France, Germany, Great Britain and Portugal.
**3. (Unit 2 review — Grade 12)** Unit 2 review: Compare indirect and direct rule in two sentences.
**Answer: British indirect rule governed through existing chiefs and institutions; French direct rule governed through European officials, with assimilation and later association.**

**4. (Unit 2 review — Grade 12)** Unit 2 review: How did 'legitimate trade' prepare colonisation?
**Answer: Legal commerce in produce deepened European footholds on the coasts until the Scramble turned footholds into colonies.**
