# Video script — The Scramble and Berlin 1884–85 — a continent partitioned on paper


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens in 1880 — Africa, mostly its own colours, as you saw.

Then the colours begin to run. 1885. 1890. Coast to interior, the foreign shades spread.

Protectorates. Colonies. 'Free-trade areas.' Lines drawn in far-off capitals.

Cut to a Berlin room, winter 1884. A long table. Fourteen flags.

Portugal called them. France, Germany, Britain, Portugal lead the talk — about Africa.

Now pan along the far side of the table: chair after empty chair. No Africans were invited.

1914. The map is covered. The partition was agreed on paper — and imposed on the ground.
