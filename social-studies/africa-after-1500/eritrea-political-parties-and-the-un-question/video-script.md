# Video script — Eritrea — political parties and the UN question

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After Britain replaces Italy in 1941, Eritrea's first political leaders — Ibrahim Sultan, Woldeab Woldemariam, Ras Tesemma Asberom — organise parties to demand the country's own future.

Woldeab Woldemariam, Ibrahim Sultan and Ras Tesemma Asberom lead the independence parties of the 1940s against the unionists, as the Four Powers fail and the UN takes up Eritrea's case.

Mahber Fiqri Hager (1941) seeds Eritrea's first parties — the Muslim League and the Liberal Progressive Party demand independence while Ethiopia builds the Unionist Party — and the Four Powers, deadlocked, hand Eritrea's future to the United Nations.
