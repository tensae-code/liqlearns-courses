# Eritrea — political parties and the UN question — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eritrea's first parties grew from —
a) Mahber Fiqri Hager, 1941  b) the United Nations  c) the Italian government
**Answer: a) Mahber Fiqri Hager, 1941** — Mahber Fiqri Hager (1941) seeded the first parties.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Muslim League and the Liberal Progressive Party demanded —
a) union with Ethiopia  b) independence  c) Italian rule again
**Answer: b) independence** — they demanded independence; the Unionist Party was Ethiopia's answer.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When the Four Powers deadlocked, Eritrea's future went to —
a) Italy  b) the United Nations  c) Britain alone
**Answer: b) the United Nations** — the UN took up Eritrea's case.
