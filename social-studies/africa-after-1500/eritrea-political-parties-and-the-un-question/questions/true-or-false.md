# Eritrea — political parties and the UN question — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ibrahim Sultan and Woldeab Woldemariam were leaders of the independence parties.
**Answer: True** — all three books name them among the first party leaders.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every Eritrean party of the 1940s wanted union with Ethiopia.
**Answer: False** — the independence parties stood against the unionists.
