# Eritrea — political parties and the UN question — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two leaders of Eritrea's first political parties.
**Answer: Ibrahim Sultan and Woldeab Woldemariam (also accepted: Ras Tesemma Asberom).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What divided the parties of the 1940s?
**Answer: The independence parties demanded Eritrea's own future; the Unionist Party, built by Ethiopia, wanted union with Ethiopia (any close wording).**
