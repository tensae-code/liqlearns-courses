# Hands-on — Eritrea — political parties and the UN question

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Make two party posters in words: one for an independence party, one for the Unionist Party — each poster must say what future it wants for Eritrea.

## Task 2
Hold a class vote of the 1940s: argue the independence case and the union case in one minute each, then vote and count.
