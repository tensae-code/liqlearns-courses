## Sub lesson 2 — The party leaders

Woldeab Woldemariam, Ibrahim Sultan and Ras Tesemma Asberom lead the independence parties of the 1940s against the unionists, as the Four Powers fail and the UN takes up Eritrea's case.

## Sub lesson 3 — Political movements during the British period

Mahber Fiqri Hager (1941) seeds Eritrea's first parties — the Muslim League and the Liberal Progressive Party demand independence while Ethiopia builds the Unionist Party — and the Four Powers, deadlocked, hand Eritrea's future to the United Nations.
