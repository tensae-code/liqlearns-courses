# Video script — Explorers and missionaries map the interior

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Europe turns inland. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Between 1770 and 1870, European explorers pushed into Africa's interior — tracing the Niger, crossing to the lakes, following the Zambezi. Watch the names appear one by one: explorer, Livingstone, interior. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Missionaries beside explorers. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Missionaries came to preach and stayed to translate, teach and heal — building schools and clinics, learning languages, and writing the first books in many of them.

Africans received, resisted and reshaped missions for their own purposes; conversion was never simply done to passive hearers.. Watch the names appear one by one: missionary, translate, conversion. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Knowledge that prepared partition. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The explorers' and missionaries' reports did double work: they informed Europe — and they prepared its ambitions. Watch the names appear one by one: Scramble, partition, report. This is the part of the story to hold.
