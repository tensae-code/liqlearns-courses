# Explorers and missionaries map the interior — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The missionary-explorer named in the book — this is Livingstone.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, explorer means the inland regions Europeans now entered and mapped.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: Rendering scripture and books into African languages — this is translate.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, missionary means adopting a new faith — chosen and reshaped by converts themselves.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The division of the interior among empires — this is partition.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Scramble means the explorer's account that informed — and armed — Europe.
**Answer: False**
