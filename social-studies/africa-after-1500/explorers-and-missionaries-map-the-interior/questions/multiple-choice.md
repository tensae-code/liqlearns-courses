# Explorers and missionaries map the interior — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is explorer?
a) interior
b) a traveller mapping unknown country for the outside world
c) Livingstone
**Answer: b) a traveller mapping unknown country for the outside world**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is missionary?
a) translate
b) one sent to preach, who often also taught and healed
c) conversion
**Answer: b) one sent to preach, who often also taught and healed**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Scramble?
a) partition
b) the European partition of Africa that followed the mapping
c) report
**Answer: b) the European partition of Africa that followed the mapping**
