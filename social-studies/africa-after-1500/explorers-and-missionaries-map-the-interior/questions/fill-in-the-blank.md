# Explorers and missionaries map the interior — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the inland regions Europeans now entered and mapped' is ______.
**Answer: interior**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'adopting a new faith — chosen and reshaped by converts themselves' is ______.
**Answer: conversion**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the explorer's account that informed — and armed — Europe' is ______.
**Answer: report**
