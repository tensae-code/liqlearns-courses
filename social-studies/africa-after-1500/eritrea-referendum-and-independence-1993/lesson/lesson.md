# Eritrea — referendum and independence, 1993

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: in April 1993 Eritreans voted under international observation and 99.8 percent chose independence, declared on 24 May 1993 — two years after liberation, the vote made Eritrea a sovereign country in law as well as in fact. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Referendum and independence

In April 1993 Eritreans vote 99.8 percent for independence, and the flag of a sovereign Eritrea rises on 24 May 1993.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Referendum, 23–25 April 1993

Two years after actual liberation on 24 May 1991, Eritreans vote under UN, OAU and Arab League observation, and over 99.8 percent choose independence, formally declared on 24 May 1993.
