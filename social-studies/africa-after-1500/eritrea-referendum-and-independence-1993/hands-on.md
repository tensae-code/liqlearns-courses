# Hands-on — Eritrea — referendum and independence, 1993

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Run a class referendum: make ballot slips, vote on a class question, count openly, and announce the percentage — then compare your count with 99.8 percent.

## Task 2
Draw the flag-raising moment of 24 May 1993 as the Grade 5 book describes it, and caption it with the vote result.
