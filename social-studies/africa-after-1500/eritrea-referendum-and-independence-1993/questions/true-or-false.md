# Eritrea — referendum and independence, 1993 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Observers from the UN, the OAU and the Arab League watched the vote.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 8 book names all three.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The referendum happened before the liberation of Asmara.
**Answer: False** — liberation came first, on 24 May 1991; the vote followed in 1993.
