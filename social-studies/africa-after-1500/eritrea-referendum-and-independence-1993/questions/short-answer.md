# Eritrea — referendum and independence, 1993 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Eritrea need a referendum after it was already liberated?
**Answer: Liberation in 1991 freed the country in fact; the 1993 vote, watched by the world, made independence the people's own recorded choice and the basis of formal declaration (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the two May dates of this story and what each one is.
**Answer: 24 May 1991 — liberation; 24 May 1993 — independence formally declared (any close wording).**
