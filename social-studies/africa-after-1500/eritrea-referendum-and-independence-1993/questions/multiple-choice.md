# Eritrea — referendum and independence, 1993 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The referendum was held in —
a) April 1993  b) May 1991  c) November 1962
**Answer: a) April 1993** — 23–25 April 1993, says the Grade 8 book.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The share that chose independence was —
a) about half  b) over 99.8 percent  c) 62 percent
**Answer: b) over 99.8 percent** — over 99.8 percent chose independence.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Independence was formally declared on —
a) 24 May 1993  b) 1 September 1961  c) 2 December 1950
**Answer: a) 24 May 1993** — 24 May 1993 — two years after liberation day.
