# Video script — Uganda after independence — leaders and symbols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The presidents file past — Obote twice, Amin, Lule's 68 days — Amin brings Muteesa's body home to Kasubi in 1972; the expelled Asians are allowed back, UPE and USE are introduced, and the flag, anthem and crested-crane coat of arms teach the new nation's meanings.
The flag's red is brotherhood, the coat of arms — designed by Cecil Todd — joins drum, spear and shield to coffee and cotton, and the child draws the crested crane and asks why it was chosen.
Muteesa II, Kabaka from 1939, dies in 1969; Amin expels the Asians in 1972 and rules by decree until exiles of the UNLF and Tanzania overthrow him in 1979; the leaders' list runs to Museveni, 1986 to date.
