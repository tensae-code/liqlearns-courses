# Uganda after independence — leaders and symbols — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Uganda as an Independent Nation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the presidents file past — Obote twice, Amin, Lule's 68 days — Amin brings Muteesa's body home to Kasubi in 1972; the expelled Asians are allowed back, UPE and USE are introduced, and the flag, anthem and crested-crane coat of arms teach the new nation's meanings

## Sub lesson 2 — Uganda as an Independent Nation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the flag's red is brotherhood, the coat of arms — designed by Cecil Todd — joins drum, spear and shield to coffee and cotton, and the child draws the crested crane and asks why it was chosen

## Sub lesson 3 — Post-independence Uganda
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Muteesa II, Kabaka from 1939, dies in 1969; Amin expels the Asians in 1972 and rules by decree until exiles of the UNLF and Tanzania overthrow him in 1979; the leaders' list runs to Museveni, 1986 to date
