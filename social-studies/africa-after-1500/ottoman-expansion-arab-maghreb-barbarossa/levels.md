## Sub lesson 1 — Ottoman expansion in the Arab Maghreb
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — after Granada falls in 1492, Spain and Portugal fall on a Maghreb split among Hafsids, Zayyanids, Wattasids and Saadians and seize Tripoli, Tunis, Oran and Marrakesh ports; the sea-captains Aruj and Khayr al-Din Barbarossa fight back and call in the Ottomans, and the empire answers — Algeria in 1532 becomes the base, Tunis is taken, lost to a Venetian-papal fleet in 1571 and retaken by Sinan Pasha in 1574, Libya is cleared of the Knights of St John by 1551 — while Saadian Morocco, strong enough to expel the Europeans itself, never joins and is never annexed
