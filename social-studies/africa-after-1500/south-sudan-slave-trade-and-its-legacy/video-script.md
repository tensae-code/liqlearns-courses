# Video script — South Sudan — the slave trade and its legacy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as South Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From the 652 AD Baqt, under which Christian Nubia traded 360 slaves a year, through Funj slave armies and Muhammad Ali's raids described by Samuel Baker, to Simon Deng, abducted at nine, carried up the Nile, who escapes, regains his Shilluk scars and becomes an abolitionist.
