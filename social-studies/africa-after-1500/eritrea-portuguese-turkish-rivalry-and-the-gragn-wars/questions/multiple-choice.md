# Eritrea — Portuguese-Turkish rivalry and the Gragn wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Portuguese and Turks fought over —
a) the Red Sea trade  b) the Dahlak pearl beds only  c) highland grain
**Answer: a) the Red Sea trade** — control of the Red Sea trade.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gragn's wars tore through —
a) the islands  b) the highlands  c) the Sudan
**Answer: b) the highlands** — the highlands, weakening the Bahri Negestat's order.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The ruler whose order was weakened was —
a) the Bahri Negassi  b) the Naib of Massawa  c) the Khedive
**Answer: a) the Bahri Negassi** — the Bahri Negestat's old order.
