# Eritrea — Portuguese-Turkish rivalry and the Gragn wars — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rivalry was between two sea powers reaching for the same trade.
**Answer: True** — Portugal and Ottoman Turkey both sought the Red Sea trade.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gragn's wars strengthened the Bahri Negestat.
**Answer: False** — they weakened the old order it held together.
