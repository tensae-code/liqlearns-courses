# The Legitimate Trade in palm oil and produce — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — After abolition — a new cargo

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Legitimate Trade** (the post-abolition commerce in produce, named against the slave trade).

## Sub lesson 2 — African producers respond

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **brokers** (the middlemen who organised trade between producers and ships).

## Sub lesson 3 — New pressures of its own

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **firms** (the European companies that pressed for control of the trade).
