# The Legitimate Trade in palm oil and produce — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Legitimate Trade?
a) groundnuts
b) the post-abolition commerce in produce, named against the slave trade
c) palm oil
**Answer: b) the post-abolition commerce in produce, named against the slave trade**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is brokers?
a) produce
b) middlemen
c) the middlemen who organised trade between producers and ships
**Answer: c) the middlemen who organised trade between producers and ships**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is firms?
a) advance
b) internal slavery
c) the European companies that pressed for control of the trade
**Answer: c) the European companies that pressed for control of the trade**
