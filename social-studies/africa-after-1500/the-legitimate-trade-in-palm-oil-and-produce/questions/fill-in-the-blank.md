# The Legitimate Trade in palm oil and produce — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'a produce export that grew alongside palm oil' is ______.
**Answer: groundnuts**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'goods grown and gathered for export — oil, nuts, ivory' is ______.
**Answer: produce**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the Legitimate Trade's real moral gain over the trade in people' is ______.
**Answer: advance**
