# The Legitimate Trade in palm oil and produce — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The forest product that became the coasts' chief new export — this is palm oil.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Legitimate Trade means a produce export that grew alongside palm oil.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: Coastal traders who grew powerful in the produce trade — this is middlemen.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, brokers means goods grown and gathered for export — oil, nuts, ivory.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Enslaved labour used inside Africa, sometimes in produce-making — this is internal slavery.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, firms means the Legitimate Trade's real moral gain over the trade in people.
**Answer: False**
