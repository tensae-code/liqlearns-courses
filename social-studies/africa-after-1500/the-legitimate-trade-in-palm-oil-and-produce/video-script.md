# Video script — The Legitimate Trade in palm oil and produce

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — After abolition — a new cargo. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
When the Atlantic slave trade was abolished, the coasts needed a new export. Watch the names appear one by one: Legitimate Trade, palm oil, groundnuts. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — African producers respond. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
African farmers, brokers and rulers organised the new trade with the same skill they had shown before: palm groves were worked, prices bargained, and coastal middlemen grew rich and powerful.

The response proves the point abolitionists hoped for — the coasts' energy was commercial, not slaving by nature.. Watch the names appear one by one: brokers, middlemen, produce. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — New pressures of its own. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Legitimate commerce was not innocent of power: European firms pressed for control, prices were set far away, and palm oil itself was sometimes produced by enslaved labour inside Africa.

The lesson's balance: a real moral advance over the slave trade — and a doorway through which new dominations walked.. Watch the names appear one by one: firms, internal slavery, advance. This is the part of the story to hold.
