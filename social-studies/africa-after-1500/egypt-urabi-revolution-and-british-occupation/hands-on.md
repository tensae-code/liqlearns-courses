# Hands-on — Egypt — the Urabi Revolution and the British occupation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Egypt — the Urabi Revolution and the British occupation — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Urabi Revolution and the start of the British occupation, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
