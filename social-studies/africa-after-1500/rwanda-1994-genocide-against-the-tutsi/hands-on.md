# Hands-on — Rwanda — the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The story on a line
Draw a line. Mark this lesson's parts on it in order: Meaning and causes — classes made ethnic, hatred built; Planning and execution — and how it was stopped; Ideology, denial, prevention — and the consequences counted. Under each mark, write in your own words what happened and what changed.

## Tell it walking
Walk the line slowly. At each mark, stop and say that part of the story out loud, as if you were telling it to a younger learner who has never heard it.

## One hard question
Write the hardest question you can ask about this lesson. Swap with a companion: answer theirs, and let them answer yours — from the lesson.
