# Rwanda — the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi

## Do it first

Colonial rule turns social classes into ethnic groups with identity cards; divisionism, impunity, the Akazu, Hutu Power, Kangura and RTLM build the hatred that explodes in April 1994 — planned, executed by militia, stopped by the RPF, and answered with unity, healing and Abunzi reconciliation. Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think happened — hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Meaning and causes — classes made ethnic, hatred built

Colonial rule turns social classes into ethnic groups with identity cards. Divisionism, poor leadership, impunity, the Akazu, Hutu Power, Kangura and RTLM then build the hatred that explodes in April 1994.

## Sub lesson 2 — Planning and execution — and how it was stopped

The Akazu plan a 'final solution': lists of 'traitors', imported machetes, and the Interahamwe and Impuzamugambi militia. Prime Minister Uwilingiyimana is murdered; the RPF defeats the perpetrators and forms a Government of National Unity on 19 July 1994.

## Sub lesson 3 — Ideology, denial, prevention — and the consequences counted

Genocide ideology and denial are crimes. Prevention means unity, healing and Abunzi reconciliation, while the country counts widows, orphans, the displaced and a ruined economy — the cost the lesson refuses to hide.
