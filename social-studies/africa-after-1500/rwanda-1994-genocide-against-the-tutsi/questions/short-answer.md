# Rwanda — the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three causes the book names for the genocide.
**Answer: Any three of: classes made ethnic with identity cards, divisionism, poor leadership, impunity, the Akazu, Hutu Power, Kangura, RTLM (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How was the genocide stopped, and what government followed?
**Answer: The RPF defeated the perpetrators and formed a Government of National Unity on 19 July 1994 (any close wording).**
