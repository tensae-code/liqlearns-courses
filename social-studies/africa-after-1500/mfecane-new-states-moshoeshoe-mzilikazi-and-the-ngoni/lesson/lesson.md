# Mfecane — new states: Moshoeshoe, Mzilikazi and the Ngoni

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Shaka's wars — the Mfecane — scatter peoples who build new kingdoms far away: Moshoeshoe's Lesotho, Mzilikazi's Ndebele, Soshangane's Gaza and the Ngoni — while emptied land invites the Boers in. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Mfecane scatters peoples

Shaka's wars scatter peoples across Southern Africa. This scattering is the Mfecane, and its impact reaches far beyond Zululand.

In your mind's eye: peoples scattering from Zululand in many directions, founding new kingdoms marked Lesotho, Ndebele, Gaza and Ngoni. The student traces each new state to its builder.
