# Video script — Mfecane — new states: Moshoeshoe, Mzilikazi and the Ngoni

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Nigeria book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Shaka's wars scatter peoples across Southern Africa. This scattering is the Mfecane, and its impact reaches far beyond Zululand.

Scattered peoples build new kingdoms: Moshoeshoe's Lesotho, Mzilikazi's Ndebele, Soshangane's Gaza and the Ngoni. Emptied land invites the Boers in.

Hold that story: Shaka's wars — the Mfecane — scatter peoples who build new kingdoms far away: Moshoeshoe's Lesotho, Mzilikazi's Ndebele, Soshangane's Gaza and the Ngoni — while emptied land invites the Boers in.
