# Mfecane — new states: Moshoeshoe, Mzilikazi and the Ngoni — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mfecane was —
a) The scattering caused by Shaka's wars  b) A single battle  c) A trade route
**Answer: a) The scattering caused by Shaka's wars** — Shaka's wars scatter peoples.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Moshoeshoe built which kingdom?
a) Gaza  b) Lesotho  c) The Ndebele kingdom
**Answer: b) Lesotho** — Moshoeshoe's Lesotho.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mzilikazi built which people into a kingdom?
a) The Ngoni  b) The Sotho  c) The Ndebele
**Answer: c) The Ndebele** — Mzilikazi's Ndebele.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Emptied land left by the Mfecane invited in —
a) The Boers  b) The Portuguese  c) The Arabs
**Answer: a) The Boers** — emptied land invites the Boers in.
