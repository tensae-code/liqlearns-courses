# Mfecane — new states: Moshoeshoe, Mzilikazi and the Ngoni — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Peoples scattered by the Mfecane built new kingdoms far away.
**Answer: True** — Lesotho, the Ndebele, Gaza and the Ngoni.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Mfecane left the land fuller and kept the Boers out.
**Answer: False** — emptied land invites the Boers in.
