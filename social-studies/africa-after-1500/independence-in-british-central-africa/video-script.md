# Video script — Independence in British Central Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nyasaland and the two Rhodesias travel Britain's road to independence, each with local particularities that bend the route.
Southern Rhodesia's settlers refuse London's neocolonial proposal and entrench their own regime against the nationalist tide.
