# Indians and the Uganda Railway — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Allidina Visram opens Kampala's first shop, Indian coolies build the Kenya–Uganda railway, rupees become currency and Banyan moneylenders finance the new economy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Contributions of the Indians

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 5 — First Edition* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Contributions of the Indians

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The testing exercises ask why Indian coolies were brought to Uganda and which Indians became the money lenders — labour first, then the dukas.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Indians in East Africa

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 6 — First Edition* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Indians in East Africa

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Besides the Europeans, the Asians take an interest in East Africa — the chapter plants them inside the colonisation story, not after it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Asians in East Africa

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *History and Political Education Textbook — Senior One (Lower Secondary Curriculum, PROTOTYPE)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Asians in East Africa

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Britain wants Uganda as the Nile's source and for its cotton, richer than Kenya's; the line planned from 1892 kills those who refuse it passage — the Nandi leader Koitale among them — and grows towns on European and Indian businesses.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Uganda Railway
