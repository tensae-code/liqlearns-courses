# Cabo Verde — the two Republics

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of islands with no people on them at all — then people arriving by ship, building towns, rising in revolt, and at last proclaiming their own country. Which part of that long story do you think this lesson tells? Hold your guess; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The First Republic

The First Republic rules one-party under the PAIGC/PAICV with its first leaders and symbols.

In your mind's eye: two ballot scenes: one with a single party's banner, and one in 1991 with many parties' banners over a polling station. Say which Republic each scene belongs to.
