# Lesson plan — Cabo Verde — the two Republics

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Cabo Verde — the two Republics
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Cabo Verde, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the First Republic rules one-party under the PAIGC/PAICV with its first leaders and symbols; in 1991 multiparty democracy installs the Second Republic, with new symbols and a changed country.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: one party, then many: the First Republic under one party, the Second Republic from 1991 under multiparty democracy. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
