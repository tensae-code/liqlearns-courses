# Cabo Verde — the two Republics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The First Republic ruled —
a) With no parties  b) One-party, under the PAIGC/PAICV  c) Under a king
**Answer: b) One-party, under the PAIGC/PAICV** — one-party under the PAIGC/PAICV.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Second Republic began in —
a) 1975  b) 1991  c) 2010
**Answer: b) 1991** — 1991, with multiparty democracy.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Second Republic brought —
a) One-party rule  b) Multiparty democracy, new symbols and a changed country  c) A return to colonial rule
**Answer: b) Multiparty democracy, new symbols and a changed country** — multiparty democracy installs the Second Republic.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The workbook teaches the two Republics inside the story of —
a) The slave trade  b) The formation of the Cabo Verdean state  c) The discovery of the islands
**Answer: b) The formation of the Cabo Verdean state** — Tema 2 §11: formation of the Cabo Verdean state.
