# Cabo Verde — the two Republics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do the two Republics differ, as the workbook teaches them?
**Answer: The First Republic ruled one-party under the PAIGC/PAICV with its first leaders and symbols; in 1991 multiparty democracy installed the Second Republic, with new symbols and a changed country (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In which year did the Second Republic begin, and by what change?
**Answer: 1991, by the coming of multiparty democracy (any close wording).**
