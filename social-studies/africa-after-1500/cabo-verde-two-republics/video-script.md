# Video script — Cabo Verde — the two Republics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Cabo Verde book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The First Republic rules one-party under the PAIGC/PAICV with its first leaders and symbols.

In 1991 multiparty democracy installs the Second Republic, with new symbols and a changed country.

Hold that story: one party, then many: the First Republic under one party, the Second Republic from 1991 under multiparty democracy.
