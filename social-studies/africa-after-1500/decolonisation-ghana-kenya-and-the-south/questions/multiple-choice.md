# Decolonisation — Ghana, Kenya and the south — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ghana became independent in —
a) 1951  b) 1957  c) 1960  d) 1963
**Answer: b) 1957** — 1957 — first sub-Saharan African country independent.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nkrumah founded the Convention People's Party in —
a) 1947  b) 1948 (as printed)  c) 1951  d) 1957
**Answer: b) 1948 (as printed)** — 1948, as printed.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The CPP's methods included —
a) Armed invasion  b) Strikes, demonstrations and boycotts of British goods  c) Silence  d) Petitions only to Berlin
**Answer: b) Strikes, demonstrations and boycotts of British goods** — Strikes, demonstrations and boycotts — despite imprisonment.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kenya's nationalism was sparked by —
a) A tax on salt  b) Land seizure for white settlers under a 1901 law  c) A closed port  d) A new language law
**Answer: b) Land seizure for white settlers under a 1901 law** — The 1901 law let Kenyan land be taken for settlers.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Kenya won independence in —
a) 1957  b) 1960  c) 1963  d) 1965
**Answer: c) 1963** — 1963.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** South Africa's colonial rule ended in —
a) 1965  b) 1976  c) 1994  d) 1957
**Answer: c) 1994** — 1994 — Mandela first Black president.
