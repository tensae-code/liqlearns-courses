# Decolonisation — Ghana, Kenya and the south — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is Ghana called 'first' in this story?
**Answer:** In 1957 Ghana became the first sub-Saharan African country independent, under Nkrumah.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain Kenya's spark and its hardest-hit people.
**Answer:** A 1901 British law let Kenyan land be taken for white settlers; the Kikuyu, hardest hit, organised the Young Kikuyu Association under Harry Thuku (as printed), demanding protest and reform.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List the followers with their years: Nigeria, Sierra Leone, Gambia.
**Answer:** Nigeria 1960, Sierra Leone 1961, Gambia 1965.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How long separates Ghana's independence from South Africa's ending of colonial rule?
**Answer:** From 1957 to 1994 — thirty-seven years.
