# Decolonisation — Ghana, Kenya and the south — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ghana won self-government in 1951, before full independence.
**Answer: True** — Self-government 1951; independence 1957.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nigeria became independent in 1960.
**Answer: True** — Nigeria 1960; Sierra Leone 1961; Gambia 1965.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Nelson Mandela was South Africa's first Black president.
**Answer: True** — In 1994, when colonial rule there ended.
