# Zimbabwe — work and leisure, past and present

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The same needs, two worlds: Zimbabwe's Grade 5 lesson sets the work and play of grandparents against the work and play of their grandchildren, to show what changed — and what did not. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Work and leisure in the past and present (Grade 5)

The same needs, two worlds. The work and play of grandparents are set against the work and play of their grandchildren — hoe beside phone, story-fire beside screen — and the class must sort what changed from what did not.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: work and leisure in the past and present. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
