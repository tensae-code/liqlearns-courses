# Video script — Zimbabwe — work and leisure, past and present

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Zimbabwe's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The same needs, two worlds. The work and play of grandparents are set against the work and play of their grandchildren — hoe beside phone, story-fire beside screen — and the class must sort what changed from what did not.
