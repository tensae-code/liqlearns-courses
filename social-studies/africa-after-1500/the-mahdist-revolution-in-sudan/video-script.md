# Video script — The Mahdist revolution in Sudan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

a holy man with no army declares the empire's rule finished, and Sudan believes him.

ten thousand government soldiers march into the forest of Shaykan and do not march out.

port by port the east rises, and then the empire's most famous general dies on the palace steps.
