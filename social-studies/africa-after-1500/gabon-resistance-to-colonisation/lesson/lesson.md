# Gabon — resistance to colonisation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: From the first treaties, Gabonese peoples refuse French domination — around King Denis's court and beyond, and in the risings of Emane Ntole, Nyonda Makita (Mavurulu), Mbombè and the Binzima movement. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
