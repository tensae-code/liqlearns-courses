# Gabon — resistance to colonisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From the first treaties, people around King Denis's court and beyond refuse French domination
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 41 La résistance à la colonisation).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Emane Ntole, Nyonda Makita (Mavurulu), Mbombè and the Binzima movement rise against French rule
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 31 La résistance à la colonisation au Gabon).
