# Gabon — resistance to colonisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Resistance from the first treaties  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Resistance from the first treaties** — from the first treaties, people around King Denis's court and beyond refuse French domination.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide this part is —
a) Leçon 41 La résistance à la colonisation  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Leçon 41 La résistance à la colonisation** — the guide's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Gabon's resisters rise against French rule  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Gabon's resisters rise against French rule** — Emane Ntole, Nyonda Makita (Mavurulu), Mbombè and the Binzima movement rise against French rule.
