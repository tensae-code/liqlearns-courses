# Lesson plan — Cabo Verde — discovery and settlement

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Cabo Verde — discovery and settlement
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Cabo Verde, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Portuguese sailors find the uninhabited islands of Cabo Verde in the age of European expansion, and Europeans and Africans — many enslaved — settle Santiago and Fogo first, then the remaining islands, farming cotton and maize, raising cattle, fishing and trading.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: empty islands, hard to settle: no one lived there, the land was dry, and peopling it took Europeans and Africans together, island by island, Santiago and Fogo first. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Discovery of the islands** — In the age of European expansion, Portuguese sailors find the uninhabited islands; the book asks who really "discovered" land no one lived on.
2. **Occupation and peopling of the islands** — Settling empty, dry islands is hard; Europeans and Africans (many enslaved) people Santiago and Fogo first, then the remaining islands, farming cotton and maize, raising cattle, fishing and trading.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that the islands were uninhabited when Portuguese sailors found them, and why the book questions the word "discovered".
2. Name Santiago and Fogo as the first islands peopled, and name cotton, maize, cattle, fishing and trading.
