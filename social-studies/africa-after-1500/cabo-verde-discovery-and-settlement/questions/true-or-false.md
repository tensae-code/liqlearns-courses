# Cabo Verde — discovery and settlement — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The islands were already inhabited when Portuguese sailors arrived.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches they were uninhabited.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Europeans and Africans together peopled the islands.
**Answer: True** — both peoples people Santiago and Fogo first, then the rest.
