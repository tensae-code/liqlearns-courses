# Cabo Verde — discovery and settlement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The islands of Cabo Verde, when Portuguese sailors found them, were —
a) Inhabited by a large kingdom  b) Uninhabited — no one lived on them  c) Part of the African mainland
**Answer: b) Uninhabited — no one lived on them** — the book's starting point: an uninhabited archipelago.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first islands to be peopled were —
a) São Vicente and Santo Antão  b) Santiago and Fogo  c) All ten islands at once
**Answer: b) Santiago and Fogo** — Santiago and Fogo first, then the remaining islands.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** People on the first settled islands lived by —
a) Mining gold only  b) Farming cotton and maize, raising cattle, fishing and trading  c) Building railways
**Answer: b) Farming cotton and maize, raising cattle, fishing and trading** — the work the book names for the settlement.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Many of the Africans who peopled the islands came —
a) As free tourists  b) As enslaved people  c) As Portuguese officials only
**Answer: b) As enslaved people** — the book is plain: many enslaved.
