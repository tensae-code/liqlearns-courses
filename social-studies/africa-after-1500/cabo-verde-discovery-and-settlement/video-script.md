# Video script — Cabo Verde — discovery and settlement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Cabo Verde book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the age of European expansion, Portuguese sailors find the uninhabited islands; the book asks who really "discovered" land no one lived on.

Settling empty, dry islands is hard; Europeans and Africans (many enslaved) people Santiago and Fogo first, then the remaining islands, farming cotton and maize, raising cattle, fishing and trading.

Hold that story: empty islands, hard to settle: no one lived there, the land was dry, and peopling it took Europeans and Africans together, island by island, Santiago and Fogo first.
