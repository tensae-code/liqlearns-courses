# Video script — Social conflict and anti-colonial resistance in Mozambique

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oppression breeds contestation — strikes, revolts and other struggles by students, peasants and workers; after WWII nationalism speaks through associations, press and poetry.
After the war, nationalist organisations multiply — the MJDM youth movement and the NESAM secondary students' nucleus among them.
