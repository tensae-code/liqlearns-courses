# Hands-on — Eritrea — Samhar, Saho, Afar and the Naibs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Hold a council election: choose five elders by vote, give them one grazing dispute to settle, and record their judgement.

## Task 2
Draw the two governments side by side — council circle and Naib's house — with one line under each saying where power comes from.
