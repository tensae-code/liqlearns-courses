# Eritrea — Samhar, Saho, Afar and the Naibs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Saho and Afar governed themselves through —
a) elected councils of elders  b) hereditary kings  c) foreign governors
**Answer: a) elected councils of elders** — elected councils of elders.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Samhar later passed under —
a) the Naib family of Belau origin  b) the Diglal  c) the Portuguese
**Answer: a) the Naib family of Belau origin** — the Naib family, of Belau origin.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Between Saho and Afar people there were —
a) master-serf relations  b) no master-serf relations  c) slave markets in every village
**Answer: b) no master-serf relations** — the book stresses there were none.
