# Eritrea — Samhar, Saho, Afar and the Naibs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The councils of elders were elected, not inherited.
**Answer: True** — the book says elected councils.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Samhar was always ruled by the Naibs.
**Answer: False** — it passed under them later; before that the tribes governed themselves.
