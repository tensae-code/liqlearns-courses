# Eritrea — Samhar, Saho, Afar and the Naibs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is rule by an elected council different from rule by a Naib family?

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which two peoples does this lesson follow?
