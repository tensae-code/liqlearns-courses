# Eritrea — the armed struggle: the ELF

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: denied self-determination, Eritreans moved from protest to arms: the ELM organised in secret from 1958, workers struck, the ELF was founded in Cairo in 1960, and Hamid Idris Awate fired the first shot at Mount Adal on 1 September 1961 — though a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands bred the defections of 1967. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The political struggle 1958–1961

The ELM organises in secret from 1958, workers strike, and in 1960 the ELF is founded in Cairo — the road from peaceful protest to arms.
