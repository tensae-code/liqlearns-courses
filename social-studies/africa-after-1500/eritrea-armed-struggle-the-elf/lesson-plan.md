# Lesson plan — Eritrea — the armed struggle: the ELF

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — the armed struggle: the ELF
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Denied self-determination, Eritreans moved from protest to arms: the ELM organised in secret from 1958, workers struck, the ELF was founded in Cairo in 1960, and Hamid Idris Awate fired the first shot at Mount Adal on 1 September 1961 — though a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands bred the defections of 1967.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The political struggle 1958–1961** — the ELM organises in secret from 1958, workers strike, and in 1960 the ELF is founded in Cairo — the road from peaceful protest to arms (The political struggle 1958–1961)
2. **The Eritrean Liberation Front** — denied self-determination, Eritreans move from the ELM's coup hopes to the ELF in 1960, and Hamid Idris Awate fires the first shot at Mount Adal on 1 September 1961 — but a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands breed the defections of 1967 (The Eritrean Liberation Front)
