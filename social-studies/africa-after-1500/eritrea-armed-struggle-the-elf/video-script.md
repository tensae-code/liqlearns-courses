# Video script — Eritrea — the armed struggle: the ELF

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The ELM organises in secret from 1958, workers strike, and in 1960 the ELF is founded in Cairo — the road from peaceful protest to arms.

Denied self-determination, Eritreans move from the ELM's coup hopes to the ELF in 1960, and Hamid Idris Awate fires the first shot at Mount Adal on 1 September 1961 — but a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands breed the defections of 1967.
