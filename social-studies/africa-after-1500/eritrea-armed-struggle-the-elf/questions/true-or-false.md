# Eritrea — the armed struggle: the ELF — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ELM organised in secret from 1958, before the ELF existed.
**Answer: True** — the ELM came first, from 1958.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The armed struggle began with a vote at the United Nations.
**Answer: False** — it began with Awate's first shot at Mount Adal in 1961.
