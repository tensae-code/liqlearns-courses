# Eritrea — the armed struggle: the ELF — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ELF was founded in —
a) Cairo, 1960  b) Asmara, 1941  c) Cairo, 1993
**Answer: a) Cairo, 1960** — founded in Cairo in 1960.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first shot was fired —
a) at Mount Adal, 1 September 1961  b) at Keren, March 1941  c) at Massawa, 1990
**Answer: a) at Mount Adal, 1 September 1961** — Mount Adal, 1 September 1961, by Hamid Idris Awate.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The defections of 1967 grew from —
a) a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands  b) a lost referendum  c) the Racial Laws
**Answer: a) a remote Supreme Council and ethnic zonal commands** — the Grade 8 book blames the remote council and zonal commands.
