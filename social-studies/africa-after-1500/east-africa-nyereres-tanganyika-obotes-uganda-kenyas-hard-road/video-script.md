# Video script — East Africa — Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda, Kenya's hard road

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on East Africa. Tanganyika lights up first — 1961 — a teacher's portrait beside the flag: Nyerere.
Uganda, 1962: two banners — a kingdom's and a congress's — braided into one flag-raising. Zanzibar's island flag follows, 1963; then the two merge: TANZANIA, 1964.
The camera crosses to Kenya. The highlands are fenced in another colour; the map's mood changes — here the road runs through conflict.
Rwanda and Burundi's small flags close the region's account as the camera pulls back.
