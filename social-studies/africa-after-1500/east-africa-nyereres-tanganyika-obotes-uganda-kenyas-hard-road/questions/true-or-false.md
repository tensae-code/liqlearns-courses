# East Africa — Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda, Kenya's hard road — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Zanzibar became independent before Tanganyika.
**Answer: False** — Tanganyika 1961; Zanzibar 1963.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Settler colonies negotiated their dispossession away as readily as other colonies.
**Answer: False** — the settler wall made Kenya's road one of conflict.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Burundi remained a kingdom until November 1966.
**Answer: True** — Rwanda, by contrast, was a republic at independence.
