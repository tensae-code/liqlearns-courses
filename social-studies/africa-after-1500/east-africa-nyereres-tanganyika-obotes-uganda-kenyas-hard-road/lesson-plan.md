# Lesson plan — East Africa — Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda, Kenya's hard road

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** East Africa — Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda, Kenya's hard road
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — the liberation unit; Grade 10 Unit 7 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** East and Central Africa's contrasting roads: Tanganyika free in 1961 under Nyerere's TANU, Uganda in 1962 under Obote's coalition, Zanzibar 1963 and Tanzania in 1964 — against Kenya's hard road, where dispossessed peasants made liberation the region's most bitter struggle.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on West Africa's negotiated pattern — East Africa tests it against a settler colony. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tanganyika and Uganda — the negotiated roads** — Tanganyika independent 1961 under Julius Nyerere's TANU; Uganda 1962 as one entity under Milton Obote's UPC in coalition with the royalist Kabaka Yekka; Zanzibar 1963; the 1964 federation as Tanzania.
2. **Kenya — the hard road** — Kenya's peasants dispossessed by settler land-taking; its struggle the region's hardest, against the settler exception.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: West Africa — independence by organisation and negotiation. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give Tanganyika 1961, Uganda 1962, the 1964 union as Tanzania.
2. Name TANU, the UPC and Kabaka Yekka, with their men.
3. Say why Kenya's road was the region's hardest.
