# Hands-on — East Africa — Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda, Kenya's hard road

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four flags timeline
Draw a timeline 1961–1964 and pin the four events — Tanganyika, Uganda, Zanzibar, Tanzania — with party and leader under each.

## The dispossession map
Sketch Kenya's highlands: settler farms on the best land, reserves at the edges. Under the sketch write what a liberation movement in such a country must demand besides a flag.
