# Zimbabwe — land reform and the return of the land

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The war's central grievance outlived the war: land stayed in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller, Britain's funding promises failed, and in 2000 fast-track reform under Robert Mugabe redistributed the land to black Zimbabweans — taught in Zimbabwe's Grade 7 guide with benefits of empowerment, food production and cultural restoration as its settled verdict. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The land reform programme (Grade 7)

The war's central grievance outlives the war. After independence the land stays in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller; Britain's funding promises fail — the guide says Britain backtracked. Then, in 2000, fast-track reform under Robert Mugabe redistributes the land to black Zimbabweans. The guide's settled verdict names the benefits: empowerment, food production and cultural restoration.
