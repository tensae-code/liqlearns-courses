# Zimbabwe — land reform and the return of the land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After independence, land at first stayed in settler hands under —
a) willing-buyer-willing-seller  b) the Rudd Concession  c) the Pioneer Column
**Answer: a) willing-buyer-willing-seller** — land stayed in settler hands under willing-buyer-willing-seller

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fast-track land reform began in —
a) 2000  b) 1980  c) 1966
**Answer: a) 2000** — fast-track reform comes in 2000

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The fast-track reform was led under —
a) Robert Mugabe  b) Lobengula  c) Mzilikazi
**Answer: a) Robert Mugabe** — it is under Robert Mugabe
