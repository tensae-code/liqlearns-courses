# The Atlantic slave trade — how it ended — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Europeans concluded in the end that the trade no longer paid.
**Answer: True** — Falling profits ended the economic case.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Britain set an anti-slavery naval force to patrol the West African coast.
**Answer: True** — After the 1807 law, the patrol followed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Captives in America and the Caribbean never resisted.
**Answer: False** — They never stopped rebelling — Haiti 1791 is the book's example.
**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The organised campaigns that turned opinion against the trade — this is abolitionist movements.
**Answer: True**
