# The Atlantic slave trade — how it ended — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the book's economic chain that ended the trade, in order.
**Answer:** Expanded sugar production glutted the market and cheapened sugar; Industrial Revolution machines flooded the market further; West African traders and chiefs raised slave prices; planters' profits fell until Europeans concluded the trade no longer paid.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Britain do from the 1800s against the trade?
**Answer:** Britain outlawed slavery and the slave trade in 1807 and set an anti-slavery naval force to patrol the West African coast.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two ways Africans themselves helped end the trade.
**Answer:** Equiano and Cugoano exposed its horror in their writings; and captives in America and the Caribbean kept rebelling — in Haiti (Saint-Domingue) the slaves rose in 1791.
