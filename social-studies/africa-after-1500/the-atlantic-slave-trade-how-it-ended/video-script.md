# Video script — The Atlantic slave trade — how it ended

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Now: The trade stops paying.
Now: Britain acts — and Africa resists.
The trade ended when it stopped paying — cheap sugar, machines, rising prices — and when Britain outlawed it in 1807 and Africans resisted, in writing and in revolt.
Tell it back — that is the lesson.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Abolition in depth — how the trade was ended. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Abolition came country by country, and the Grade 11 depth names the drivers together: Denmark first, Britain in 1807, the United States after — abolitionist movements organising opinion, enslaved people's own resistance raising the trade's cost and shame, and economics shifting under it. The names to hold: 1807, abolitionist movements.
