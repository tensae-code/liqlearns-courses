# Cape Colony and the frontier wars — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dutch settlement and the frontier wars
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station grows into a settler colony. Its trekboers fight a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa over land and cattle.

## Sub lesson 2 — Britain takes the Cape — the reforms to 1835
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Britain takes the Cape twice, lands 5,000 settlers in 1820, anglicises law and language and emancipates the slaves — reforms the Boers read as an attack on their whole way of life.
