# Cape Colony and the frontier wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Van Riebeeck's station at the Cape, founded in 1652, was a —
a) Refreshment station  b) Gold mine  c) Mission school
**Answer: a) Refreshment station** — a refreshment station, in the book's words.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The trekboers fought a century of frontier wars with whom, over land and cattle?
a) The Zulu  b) The Xhosa  c) The San only
**Answer: b) The Xhosa** — frontier wars with the Xhosa.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many British settlers landed in 1820?
a) 500  b) 50,000  c) 5,000
**Answer: c) 5,000** — 5,000 settlers in 1820.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the Boers read Britain's reforms?
a) As an attack on their whole way of life  b) As a gift  c) They ignored them
**Answer: a) As an attack on their whole way of life** — an attack on their whole way of life.
