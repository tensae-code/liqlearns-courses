# Cape Colony and the frontier wars — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cape settlement began as a refreshment station in 1652.
**Answer: True** — Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Britain emancipated the slaves at the Cape as part of its reforms.
**Answer: True** — emancipation is one of the reforms named.
