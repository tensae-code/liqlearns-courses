# Lesson plan — Cape Colony and the frontier wars

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Cape Colony and the frontier wars
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Nigeria, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station at the Cape grows into a settler colony whose trekboers fight a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa — then Britain takes the Cape, lands 5,000 settlers in 1820 and reforms the Boers read as an attack on their whole way of life.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Dutch settlement and the frontier wars** — Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station grows into a settler colony. Its trekboers fight a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa over land and cattle.
2. **Britain takes the Cape — the reforms to 1835** — Britain takes the Cape twice, lands 5,000 settlers in 1820, anglicises law and language and emancipates the slaves — reforms the Boers read as an attack on their whole way of life.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
