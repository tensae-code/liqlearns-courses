# Cape Colony and the frontier wars

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station at the Cape grows into a settler colony whose trekboers fight a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa — then Britain takes the Cape, lands 5,000 settlers in 1820 and reforms the Boers read as an attack on their whole way of life. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dutch settlement and the frontier wars

Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station grows into a settler colony. Its trekboers fight a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa over land and cattle.

In your mind's eye: the Cape refreshment station in 1652 growing into farms, with Xhosa frontier country beyond and British settler ships arriving in 1820. The student tells the Dutch and British parts in order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Britain takes the Cape — the reforms to 1835

Britain takes the Cape twice, lands 5,000 settlers in 1820, anglicises law and language and emancipates the slaves — reforms the Boers read as an attack on their whole way of life.
