# Video script — Cape Colony and the frontier wars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Nigeria book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station grows into a settler colony. Its trekboers fight a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa over land and cattle.

Britain takes the Cape twice, lands 5,000 settlers in 1820, anglicises law and language and emancipates the slaves — reforms the Boers read as an attack on their whole way of life.

Hold that story: Van Riebeeck's 1652 refreshment station at the Cape grows into a settler colony whose trekboers fight a century of frontier wars with the Xhosa — then Britain takes the Cape, lands 5,000 settlers in 1820 and reforms the Boers read as an attack on their whole way of life.
