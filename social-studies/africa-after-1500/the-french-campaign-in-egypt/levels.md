## Sub lesson 1 — The French Campaign on Egypt, 1798–1801
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Napoleon lands and Egyptians resist in Cairo's two revolts; the Levant campaign ends in the Jaffa massacre and failure before Acre; Kléber is assassinated by the Azhar student Sulayman al-Halabi in 1800, Menou dreams of a colony, and Britain and the Ottomans force the evacuation of September 1801 — but the campaign's scholars found the Scientific Institute, write the Description de l'Égypte and discover the Rosetta Stone in 1799, and the invasion wakes Egypt's national spirit

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 1: The French Campaign on Egypt and the Levant, 1798–1801
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — France dreams of Egypt for a century first — Louis XIV in 1672 and Louis XV in 1769 both reach for her to wound Dutch and British trade; in 1798 the causes ripen — cut the Cape route, strike England's Indian empire, replace the lost American colonies with an eastern empire; and the Egypt it strikes is an Ottoman province of tax-farmers and decayed crafts, two societies — Turkish-Mamluk rulers and Egyptian ruled — governed by wali, diwan and beys
