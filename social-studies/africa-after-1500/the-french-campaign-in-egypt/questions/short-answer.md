# The French Campaign in Egypt, 1798–1801 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The French Campaign on Egypt, 1798–1801.
**Answer: Napoleon lands and Egyptians resist in Cairo's two revolts; the Levant campaign ends in the Jaffa massacre and failure before Acre; Kléber is assassinated by the Azhar student Sulayman al-Halabi in 1800, Menou dreams of a colony, and Britain and the Ottomans force the evacuation of September 1801 — but the campaign's scholars found the Scientific Institute, write the Description de l'Égypte and discover the Rosetta Stone in 1799, and the invasion wakes Egypt's national spirit. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Egypt's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Prep9 full-year "Geography of the World and Modern & Contemporary History of Egypt"* (Grade 9 (Third Preparatory), both terms in one volume), The French Campaign on Egypt, 1798–1801, printed p. 47.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Chapter 1: The French Campaign on Egypt and the Levant, 1798–1801.
**Answer: France dreams of Egypt for a century first — Louis XIV in 1672 and Louis XV in 1769 both reach for her to wound Dutch and British trade; in 1798 the causes ripen — cut the Cape route, strike England's Indian empire, replace the lost American colonies with an eastern empire; and the Egypt it strikes is an Ottoman province of tax-farmers and decayed crafts, two societies — Turkish-Mamluk rulers and Egyptian ruled — governed by wali, diwan and beys. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Egypt's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Sec12 "History of Egypt and the Arabs, Modern and Contemporary"* (Grade 12 (Third Secondary, Literary section)), Chapter 1: The French Campaign on Egypt and the Levant, 1798–1801, printed p. 1.**
