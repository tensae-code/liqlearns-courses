# Video script — The French Campaign in Egypt, 1798–1801

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Napoleon's invasion of 1798 meets Egyptians resisting in Cairo's two revolts, fails before Acre, and ends in evacuation in 1801 — but its scholars write the Description de l'Égypte and find the Rosetta Stone, and the invasion wakes Egypt's national spirit.
