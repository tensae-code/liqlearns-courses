# Uganda's road to independence, 1921–1962

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: LEGCO is formed in 1921 under Governor Coryndon at European businessmen's prompting; parties follow — DP in 1954, UPU in 1959 — the world wars shake the empire, the 1961 London conference promises elections and a referendum on Bunyoro's lost counties, and self-government comes in 1962. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
