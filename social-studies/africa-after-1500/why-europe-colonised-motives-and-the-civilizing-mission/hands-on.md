# Hands-on — Why Europe colonised — motives and the civilizing-mission claim

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Weigh the scales
Draw balance scales. Put motive-cards on the pans — raw materials, markets, strategy, missionaries, civilizing ideas — until the scales match the lesson's weighing: economics heaviest. Defend your arrangement in three sentences.

## Document detective
Write two columns about Kipling's poem: 'What it claims about non-Europeans' and 'What it actually proves about Europeans'. Fill each with two entries.
