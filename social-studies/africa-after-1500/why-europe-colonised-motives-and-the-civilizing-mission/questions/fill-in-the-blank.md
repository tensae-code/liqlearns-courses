# Why Europe colonised — motives and the civilizing-mission claim — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The driving motives of colonialism were ____: raw materials and markets.
**Answer: economic**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The colonisers' self-justification was called the ____ mission.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The poet of 'Take up the White Man's Burden' was Rudyard ____.
