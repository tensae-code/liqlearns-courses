# Why Europe colonised — motives and the civilizing-mission claim — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weigh the motives: which drove colonisation, and which assisted?
**Answer:** Economic motives — raw materials and markets — were the driving factors; strategic motives (positions and routes against rivals) and ideological motives (including the civilizing-mission claim) assisted and justified.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the civilizing-mission claim exactly as an assertion, not a fact.
**Answer:** It asserted that European practices were civilised and universal while non-European societies were uncivilised and inferior — a structure of ideas used to justify colonisation.
