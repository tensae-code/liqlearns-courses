# Why Europe colonised — motives and the civilizing-mission claim — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Strategic motives played no part in colonisation.
**Answer: False** — Strategic motives are weighed beside the economic and ideological.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The civilizing mission was a neutral description of the world.
**Answer: False** — It was an asserted structure of ideas — a self-justification for conquest.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kipling's poem is treated in the book as a historical document of its age.
**Answer: True** — It is printed as the period document of the ideology.
