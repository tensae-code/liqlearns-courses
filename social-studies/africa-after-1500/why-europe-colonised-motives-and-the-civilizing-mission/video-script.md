# Video script — Why Europe colonised — motives and the civilizing-mission claim


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on the trading Africa of the last lesson — dots of foreign rule on a self-ruled continent.

Now the camera pulls to Europe: chimneys smoking, mills hungry, warehouses waiting.

Raw materials in. Finished goods out. That hunger is the engine — watch the scales tip: economics heaviest.

Flags follow the trade routes — strategy plants them where rivals must not stand.

And over it all, a voice recites a poem: a 'burden', a 'mission' — conquest dressed as duty.

Motives weighed, excuse exposed. Next: the Scramble itself.
