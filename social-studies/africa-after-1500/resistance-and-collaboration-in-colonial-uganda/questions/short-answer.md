# Resistance and collaboration in colonial Uganda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Road to Independence — Resistance and Collaborators.
**Answer: Kabaka Mwanga, Omukama Kabalega and Chief Awich resist while Ankole's premier Nuwa Mbaguta extends British rule and builds roads and schools; in 1907 the Banyoro rise in the Nyangire ("I have refused") rebellion against Baganda chiefs ruling them for the British (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Uganda's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 5 — First Edition* (Uganda), The Road to Independence — Resistance and Collaborators, printed pp. 152–154.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Semei Kakungulu.
**Answer: Kakungulu plants Baganda agents as chiefs across the east and north, helps Colonel Colville capture Mwanga and Kabalega in Lango in 1899, and tries to push British rule into Karamoja (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Uganda's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 6 — First Edition* (Uganda), Semei Kakungulu, printed p. 149.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Reactions to colonial rule in East Africa.
**Answer: the class sorts the names into two columns — collaborators Kaggwa, Kakungulu, Mbaguta, Mumia; resisters Mwanga, Kabalega, Chief Awich (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Uganda's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 6 — First Edition* (Uganda), Reactions to colonial rule in East Africa, printed p. 160.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: The Road to Independence.
**Answer: Africans react to foreign rule in two directions, and the newly schooled generation finds the natural resources suddenly hard to exploit without factories (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Uganda's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Home-Study Learning — Primary 6 — Social Studies* (Uganda), The Road to Independence, printed p. 50.**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Retell this part in your own words: Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule.
**Answer: collaborators hope for guns and rewards; resisters rise over land, imposed culture and treaties they were cheated in translation — and lose, the book says, to weak economies, internal wars, missionary teaching, and betrayal like the Jumbe Magoya's (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** In Uganda's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Home-Study Learning — Senior 1 — History (cover title; the subject of record is History and Political Education)* (Uganda), Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule, printed p. 49.**

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** Retell this part in your own words: Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule.
**Answer: Muteesa I collaborates for firearms against his western enemies, Nabongo Mumia and Laibon Lenana calculate likewise, while Mwanga resists at once; Kakungulu then extends British rule over the rest of Uganda for them (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** In Uganda's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *History and Political Education Textbook — Senior One (Lower Secondary Curriculum, PROTOTYPE)* (Uganda), Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule, printed p. 91.**

**13. (Sub lesson 7)** Retell this part in your own words: Results of the response.
**Answer: resistance ends in destroyed villages and food stores, white brutality in Bunyoro and Hehe land, and a lost confidence in the traditional leaders and medicine men who "falsely led the masses" (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**14. (Sub lesson 7)** In Uganda's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *History and Political Education Textbook — Senior One (Lower Secondary Curriculum, PROTOTYPE)* (Uganda), Results of the response, printed p. 97.**

**15. (Sub lesson 8)** Retell this part in your own words: Why neighbours chose differently.
**Answer: Buganda collaborates because Bunyoro has resisted; Toro collaborates because Ankole has; and Kakungulu exports the whole Kiganda system of administration into eastern Uganda (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**16. (Sub lesson 8)** In Uganda's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *History and Political Education Teacher's Guide — Senior One (Lower Secondary Curriculum, PROTOTYPE)* (Uganda), Why neighbours chose differently, printed p. 110.**

**17. (Sub lesson 9)** Retell this part in your own words: African Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule.
**Answer: Toro and Buganda collaborate to win British guns against hostile neighbours; others reject and fight — the lesson makes the learner argue both columns (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
