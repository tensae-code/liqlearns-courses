## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Road to Independence — Resistance and Collaborators** — Kabaka Mwanga, Omukama Kabalega and Chief Awich resist while Ankole's premier Nuwa Mbaguta extends British rule and builds roads and schools; in 1907 the Banyoro rise in the Nyangire ("I have refused") rebellion against Baganda chiefs ruling them for the British
2. **Semei Kakungulu** — Kakungulu plants Baganda agents as chiefs across the east and north, helps Colonel Colville capture Mwanga and Kabalega in Lango in 1899, and tries to push British rule into Karamoja
3. **Reactions to colonial rule in East Africa** — the class sorts the names into two columns — collaborators Kaggwa, Kakungulu, Mbaguta, Mumia; resisters Mwanga, Kabalega, Chief Awich
4. **The Road to Independence** — Africans react to foreign rule in two directions, and the newly schooled generation finds the natural resources suddenly hard to exploit without factories
5. **Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule** — collaborators hope for guns and rewards; resisters rise over land, imposed culture and treaties they were cheated in translation — and lose, the book says, to weak economies, internal wars, missionary teaching, and betrayal like the Jumbe Magoya's
6. **Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule** — Muteesa I collaborates for firearms against his western enemies, Nabongo Mumia and Laibon Lenana calculate likewise, while Mwanga resists at once; Kakungulu then extends British rule over the rest of Uganda for them
7. **Results of the response** — resistance ends in destroyed villages and food stores, white brutality in Bunyoro and Hehe land, and a lost confidence in the traditional leaders and medicine men who "falsely led the masses"
8. **Why neighbours chose differently** — Buganda collaborates because Bunyoro has resisted; Toro collaborates because Ankole has; and Kakungulu exports the whole Kiganda system of administration into eastern Uganda
9. **African Response to the Establishment of Colonial Rule** — Toro and Buganda collaborate to win British guns against hostile neighbours; others reject and fight — the lesson makes the learner argue both columns
