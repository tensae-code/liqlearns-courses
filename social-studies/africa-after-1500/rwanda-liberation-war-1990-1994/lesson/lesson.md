# Rwanda — the Liberation War, 1990–1994

## Do it first

The RPF attacks from Uganda on 1 October 1990 to end exile and discrimination; Rwigema falls, Kagame takes command, the Arusha Accords are signed in 1993, and the RPF stops the genocide and liberates Kigali on 4 July 1994. Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think happened — hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — 1 October 1990 — the RPF attacks from Uganda

The RPF attacks from Uganda on 1 October 1990 to end exile and discrimination. In Rwanda's book the war's cause is stated first: people kept out of their own country fight their way home.

[Picture slot — a picture of the world of this lesson as the book shows it: point to the picture and say what changed in this part of the story.]

## Sub lesson 2 — Rwigema falls; Kagame takes command; Arusha, 1993

Rwigema falls early in the war, and Kagame takes command. Fighting and talking run together until the Arusha Accords are signed in 1993 — a peace that does not hold.

## Sub lesson 3 — 4 July 1994 — the genocide stopped, Kigali liberated

The RPF stops the genocide and liberates Kigali on 4 July 1994. That date, Liberation Day, is where the war story and the genocide story meet.
