# Rwanda — the Liberation War, 1990–1994 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Liberation War began on —
a) 1 October 1990  b) 1 July 1962  c) 4 July 1994
**Answer: a) 1 October 1990** — The RPF attacks from Uganda on 1 October 1990.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The RPF attacked to end —
a) Trade  b) Exile and discrimination  c) The monarchy
**Answer: b) Exile and discrimination** — To end exile and discrimination.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After Rwigema fell, command passed to —
a) Habyarimana  b) Kagame  c) Kayibanda
**Answer: b) Kagame** — Kagame takes command.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Kigali was liberated on —
a) 4 July 1994  b) 1 October 1990  c) 19 July 1994
**Answer: a) 4 July 1994** — Liberation is 4 July 1994.
