# Video script — Rwanda — the Liberation War, 1990–1994

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The RPF attacks from Uganda on 1 October 1990 to end exile and discrimination; Rwigema falls, Kagame takes command, the Arusha Accords are signed in 1993, and the RPF stops the genocide and liberates Kigali on 4 July 1994.

The RPF attacks from Uganda on 1 October 1990 to end exile and discrimination. In Rwanda's book the war's cause is stated first: people kept out of their own country fight their way home.

Rwigema falls early in the war, and Kagame takes command. Fighting and talking run together until the Arusha Accords are signed in 1993 — a peace that does not hold.

The RPF stops the genocide and liberates Kigali on 4 July 1994. That date, Liberation Day, is where the war story and the genocide story meet.
