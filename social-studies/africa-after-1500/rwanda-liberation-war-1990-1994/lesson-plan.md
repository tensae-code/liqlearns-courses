# Lesson plan — Rwanda — the Liberation War, 1990–1994

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Rwanda — the Liberation War, 1990–1994
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Rwanda, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The RPF attacks from Uganda on 1 October 1990 to end exile and discrimination; Rwigema falls, Kagame takes command, the Arusha Accords are signed in 1993, and the RPF stops the genocide and liberates Kigali on 4 July 1994.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1 October 1990 — the RPF attacks from Uganda** — The RPF attacks from Uganda on 1 October 1990 to end exile and discrimination.
2. **Rwigema falls; Kagame takes command; Arusha, 1993** — Rwigema falls early in the war, and Kagame takes command.
3. **4 July 1994 — the genocide stopped, Kigali liberated** — The RPF stops the genocide and liberates Kigali on 4 July 1994.
