# Lesson plan — The emirates of Mauritania

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** The emirates of Mauritania
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Mauritania, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Dossier 11: The emirates in Mauritania (1)** — contact between the Maqil Hassaniya tribes and the peoples south of the Sahara strengthens as Arab emirates take shape
2. **Dossier 12: The emirates in Mauritania (2)** — the Arab Hassaniya tribes expand and secure their rule as the Almoravid-era order ends — Trarza, Brakna, Adrar and Tagant govern the country
3. **Lesson 9: The emirates in Mauritania** — Trarza, Brakna, Adrar and Tagant — the emirates that ruled Mauritania when Europe arrived
4. **Lesson 2: The Mauritanian emirates** — the emirates that grew from the Hassaniya settlement rule Mauritania for centuries before France
5. **Lesson 8: The Mauritanian emirates** — at secondary depth, the emirates are the state-form France found in Mauritania
