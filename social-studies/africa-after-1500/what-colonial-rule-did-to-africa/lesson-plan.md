# Lesson plan — What colonial rule did to Africa — political, economic, social

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** What colonial rule did to Africa — political, economic, social
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Africa and the Colonial Experience 1880s–1960s (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Colonial rule's impacts fall in three registers: **political** — artificial states and boundaries, destroyed indigenous authorities; **economic** — policies that **extracted rather than developed**, weakening Africa as its resources and labour served the coloniser; **social and cultural** — indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed.
**This lesson's own level:** it closes Unit 2 — the verdict lesson, standing on everything the unit has shown. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Political — lines on the map, thrones emptied** — State the political impacts: artificial states and boundaries, and destroyed indigenous authorities.
2. **Economic — extraction, not development** — State the economic verdict: colonial policies extracted rather than developed; Africa was weakened economically, its resources and labour serving the coloniser; the summary's verdict — colonial economic policies destroyed rather than helped the colonies.
3. **Social and cultural — a way of life broken** — State the social/cultural impacts: indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed, Africa weakened culturally.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
