# Video script — What colonial rule did to Africa — political, economic, social


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on today's Africa — and the camera lingers on its borders. Too many straight lines.

Those lines are the first wound: states drawn on paper, peoples cut in two, old thrones emptied.

Descend to a mine, a plantation port. Carts roll one way only — out. Wealth leaves; the land stays poor.

Enter a classroom of the old days: a child punished for her own language. A culture pushed to the margins.

Political. Economic. Cultural. Three registers — one verdict.

The flags will come down in Unit 7. The lines, and the wounds, will remain.
