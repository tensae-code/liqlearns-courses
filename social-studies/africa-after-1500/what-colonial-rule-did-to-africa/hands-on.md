# Hands-on — What colonial rule did to Africa — political, economic, social

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three registers sort
Write twelve impact cards of your own from the lesson and sort them into three piles: political, economic, social/cultural. Any card that fits two piles gets a star — impacts overlap in life.

## The cut people
Choose one real African people divided by a colonial border (your teacher or map can help). Write three sentences: the line, the two states, one problem the line still causes.
