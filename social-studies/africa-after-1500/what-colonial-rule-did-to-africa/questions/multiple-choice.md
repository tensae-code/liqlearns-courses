# What colonial rule did to Africa — political, economic, social — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The political impacts of colonial rule include —
a) Artificial states and boundaries, destroyed indigenous authorities  b) Stronger African kings  c) Continental unity  d) Free elections everywhere
**Answer: a) Artificial states and boundaries, destroyed indigenous authorities** — Artificial boundaries and destroyed authorities are the political register.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonial boundaries typically —
a) Followed peoples' homelands  b) Cut through peoples' territories with straight, artificial lines  c) Were drawn by African congresses  d) Matched language maps
**Answer: b) Cut through peoples' territories with straight, artificial lines** — The partition's lines ignored the peoples inside them.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Colonial economic policies are judged to have —
a) Developed the colonies first  b) Extracted rather than developed — resources and labour serving the coloniser  c) Been neutral  d) Industrialised Africa fully
**Answer: b) Extracted rather than developed — resources and labour serving the coloniser** — Extraction, not development, is the book's verdict.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit summary's verdict on colonial economic policies is that they —
a) Helped rather than destroyed  b) Destroyed rather than helped the colonies  c) Were never applied  d) Ended poverty
**Answer: b) Destroyed rather than helped the colonies** — 'Destroyed rather than helped' is the summary's blunt verdict.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The social and cultural impacts include —
a) Indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed  b) African languages made official everywhere  c) Customs strengthened by law  d) No change at all
**Answer: a) Indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed** — Indigenous lifestyles and culture were destroyed; Africa was weakened culturally.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The three registers of colonial impact are —
a) Military, naval, aerial  b) Political, economic, social/cultural  c) Legal, medical, postal  d) Urban, rural, coastal
**Answer: b) Political, economic, social/cultural** — Political, economic, and social/cultural.
