# What colonial rule did to Africa — political, economic, social — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's economic verdict in full.
**Answer:** Colonial economic policies extracted rather than developed: Africa was weakened economically, its resources and labour serving the coloniser — in the summary's words, the policies destroyed rather than helped the colonies.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three registers of impact with one example each.
**Answer:** Political — artificial boundaries and destroyed indigenous authorities; economic — extraction of resources and labour for the coloniser; social/cultural — indigenous lifestyles and culture destroyed.
