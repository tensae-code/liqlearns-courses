# What colonial rule did to Africa — political, economic, social — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonial boundaries were drawn carefully around each people's homeland.
**Answer: False** — They were artificial lines that cut through peoples' territories.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Africa grew economically stronger because its resources served its own development under colonialism.
**Answer: False** — Its resources and labour served the coloniser; Africa was weakened economically.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Colonial rule left indigenous cultures untouched.
**Answer: False** — Indigenous lifestyles and culture were destroyed.
