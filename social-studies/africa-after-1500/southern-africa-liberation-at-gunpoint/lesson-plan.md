# Lesson plan — Southern Africa — liberation at gunpoint, 1960s–1980

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Southern Africa — liberation at gunpoint, 1960s–1980
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — the liberation unit; Grade 10 Unit 7 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in the settler belt — Rhodesia, Namibia held by South Africa, Portugal's Angola and Mozambique — liberation's dominant theme was armed struggle: ZANU and ZAPU's long war ended white minority rule in Zimbabwe in April 1980, SWAPO fought for Namibia, and the Portuguese colonies waited until the mid-1970s.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the negotiated-road lessons — this is the settler exception at full length. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The settler belt — why the table stood empty** — British Southern and Northern Rhodesia, Nyasaland, Bechuanaland, Basutoland; South Africa holding South West Africa/Namibia under mandate; Portugal's Angola and Mozambique.
2. **Zimbabwe 1980, Namibia's long war, Portugal's late fall** — ZANU under Mugabe and ZAPU under Nkomo end white minority rule — majority rule April 1980, Rhodesia renamed Zimbabwe; SWAPO's war for Namibia; Angola and Mozambique freed only in the mid-1970s.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: East Africa — Nyerere's Tanganyika, Obote's Uganda, Kenya's hard road. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the settler belt's territories as the book lists them.
2. Tell Zimbabwe's war: ZANU, ZAPU, their leaders, April 1980.
3. Name SWAPO's enemy and the Portuguese colonies' delay.
