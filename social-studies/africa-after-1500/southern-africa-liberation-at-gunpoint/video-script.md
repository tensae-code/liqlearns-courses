# Video script — Southern Africa — liberation at gunpoint, 1960s–1980

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the southern third of Africa, coloured in settler and colonial flags — the belt drawn in one hard colour.
The northern countries light up and change, 1960s — while the belt's colour does not move. A conference table is drawn, empty.
Bush country: guerrilla columns move at night; the Rhodesia label cracks. April 1980 — the flag changes; the name changes: ZIMBABWE.
Westward, SWAPO's long war burns on against the strongest flag of all. Eastward, Lisbon falls — and Angola and Mozambique light up at last, mid-1970s.
