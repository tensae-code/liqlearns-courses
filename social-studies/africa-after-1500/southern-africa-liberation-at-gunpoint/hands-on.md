# Hands-on — Southern Africa — liberation at gunpoint, 1960s–1980

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The belt on the map
Shade the settler belt on a blank map from the lesson's list, one colour for settler-ruled, another for the rest. Under the map write the unit's theme sentence.

## Two roads compared
Write two short dispatches dated 1961 and 1979: the first from Accra's negotiated independence, the second from the Zimbabwe bush war. Each must state its road's reason in one line.
