# Southern Africa — liberation at gunpoint, 1960s–1980 — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** White minorities in the settler belt negotiated majority rule readily.
**Answer: False** — they declined — the rifle replaced the table.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Zimbabwe's name replaced Rhodesia's at majority rule in 1980.
**Answer: True** — the ancient stone city's name for the new state.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Northern Rhodesia, Nyasaland, Bechuanaland and Basutoland complete the belt's British-ruled list in the lesson.
**Answer: True** — alongside Southern Rhodesia.
