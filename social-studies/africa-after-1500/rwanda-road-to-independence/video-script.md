# Video script — Rwanda — the road to independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Berlin hands Rwanda to Germany, which rules through the king; King Rudahigwa reforms, demands independence in 1954 and dies in 1959; five parties fight over the future, and on 1 July 1962 Rwanda becomes independent under Grégoire Kayibanda.

The Berlin Conference of 1884–85 hands Rwanda to Germany, which rules through the king. Rwanda's road to independence therefore starts from indirect rule: the king stays, but under a foreign power.

King Rudahigwa abolishes Ubuhake and Uburetwa, opposes tribal identity cards and in 1954 demands independence. He dies in Bujumbura in 1959, on the eve of the change he demanded.

MSM, APROSOMA, UNAR, RADER and MDR-PARMEHUTU fight over the future. Tutsi flee after the November 1959 chaos, and on 1 July 1962 Rwanda becomes independent with Grégoire Kayibanda as first President.
