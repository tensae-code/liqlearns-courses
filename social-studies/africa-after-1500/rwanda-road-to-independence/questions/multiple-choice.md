# Rwanda — the road to independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Berlin 1884–85 handed Rwanda to —
a) Belgium  b) Germany  c) France
**Answer: b) Germany** — Berlin hands Rwanda to Germany.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rudahigwa demanded independence in —
a) 1931  b) 1954  c) 1962
**Answer: b) 1954** — He demands independence in 1954.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rwanda became independent on —
a) 4 July 1994  b) 1 July 1962  c) 5 July 1973
**Answer: b) 1 July 1962** — Independence is 1 July 1962.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Rwanda's first President was —
a) Habyarimana  b) Grégoire Kayibanda  c) Rudahigwa
**Answer: b) Grégoire Kayibanda** — Grégoire Kayibanda is first President.
