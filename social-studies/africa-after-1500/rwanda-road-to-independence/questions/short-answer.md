# Rwanda — the road to independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Rudahigwa do that put Rwanda on the road to independence?
**Answer: He abolished Ubuhake and Uburetwa, opposed tribal identity cards and demanded independence in 1954 (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two of the five parties and the date independence came.
**Answer: Any two of MSM, APROSOMA, UNAR, RADER, MDR-PARMEHUTU; independence 1 July 1962 (any close wording).**
