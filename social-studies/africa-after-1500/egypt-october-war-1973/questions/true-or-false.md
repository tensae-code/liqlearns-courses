# Egypt — the October 1973 War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Abdel Nasser's death in 1970, Sadat prepares with Syria in secret; the opening air strike, the Canal crossing and the Bar Lev Line falls in six hours while Syria storms the Golan; Arab states send troops and money and the oil weapon answers America's airlift; Security Council Resolution 338 stops the fighting — and the myth of the invincible army is broken.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The October 1973 War (Term 2)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Prep9 full-year "Geography of the World and Modern & Contemporary History of Egypt"* is about Egypt and the Arab-Israeli conflict (Term 2).
**Answer: False** — it is about The October 1973 War (Term 2)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From the War of Attrition and the Rogers initiative to the Madrid conference of 1991 and the Oslo agreement of 1993, Egypt backs the Palestinian Authority — and the book argues that peace frees the region's wealth from war for development.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Egypt and the Arab-Israeli conflict (Term 2)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Prep9 full-year "Geography of the World and Modern & Contemporary History of Egypt"* is about The October 1973 War (Term 2).
**Answer: False** — it is about Egypt and the Arab-Israeli conflict (Term 2)
