# Europeans on the coast — trade on African terms — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — First goods — gold, ivory, pepper

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **trading post** (a fortified coastal station for commerce, held by African permission).

## Sub lesson 2 — African rulers set the terms

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **customs** (payments rulers charged traders for permission to trade).

## Sub lesson 3 — The darkening trade

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Atlantic trade** (the ocean trade that came to centre on enslaved people).
