# Video script — Europeans on the coast — trade on African terms

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — First goods — gold, ivory, pepper. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Europeans came first to trade, not to rule: Portuguese, then Dutch, English and French ships sought gold, ivory and pepper along the western and central coasts.

They built fortified trading posts — castles like Elmina — but the forts stood on rented ground, by African permission.. Watch the names appear one by one: trading post, Elmina, pepper. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — African rulers set the terms. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
For centuries the terms were Africa's: rulers charged rents and customs, controlled who traded and at what prices, and played European rivals against each other.

This is the fact the colonial centuries later hide: on the coast, before the Scramble, Europeans were tenants and customers, not masters.. Watch the names appear one by one: customs, rivals, tenants. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — The darkening trade. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Over time one cargo grew until it overshadowed the rest: enslaved people. Watch the names appear one by one: Atlantic trade, depot, cargo. This is the part of the story to hold.
