# Europeans on the coast — trade on African terms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: A famous coastal castle on the Gold Coast — this is Elmina.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, trading post means one of the first goods Europeans sought on the coast.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The competing European nations African rulers played against each other — this is rivals.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, customs means Europeans' true standing on the coast before the Scramble.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: A holding station, as forts became for the slave trade — this is depot.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Atlantic trade means the goods — and then people — carried by the ships.
**Answer: False**
