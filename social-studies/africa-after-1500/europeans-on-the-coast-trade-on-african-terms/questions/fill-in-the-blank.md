# Europeans on the coast — trade on African terms — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'one of the first goods Europeans sought on the coast' is ______.
**Answer: pepper**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'Europeans' true standing on the coast before the Scramble' is ______.
**Answer: tenants**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the goods — and then people — carried by the ships' is ______.
**Answer: cargo**
