# Europeans on the coast — trade on African terms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is trading post?
a) Elmina
b) a fortified coastal station for commerce, held by African permission
c) pepper
**Answer: b) a fortified coastal station for commerce, held by African permission**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is customs?
a) tenants
b) rivals
c) payments rulers charged traders for permission to trade
**Answer: c) payments rulers charged traders for permission to trade**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Atlantic trade?
a) cargo
b) depot
c) the ocean trade that came to centre on enslaved people
**Answer: c) the ocean trade that came to centre on enslaved people**
