# African unity — the founding of the OAU — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Pan-African congresses before 1958 all met —
a) In Africa  b) Outside Africa  c) In Addis Ababa  d) In Accra
**Answer: b) Outside Africa** — 1919–1921, 1927 and 1945 — all outside Africa.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first congress on African soil was at Accra in —
a) 1945  b) 1958  c) 1963  d) 1957
**Answer: b) 1958** — Accra, 1958.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Casablanca group included —
a) Nigeria and Liberia  b) Ghana, Mali, Egypt, Algeria, Morocco  c) Only Ethiopia  d) Britain and France
**Answer: b) Ghana, Mali, Egypt, Algeria, Morocco** — Ghana, Mali, Egypt, Algeria, Morocco.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The decisive reconcilers were —
a) Zaghlul, Bourguiba, Ben Bella  b) Haile Selassie, Nasser and Nkrumah  c) Lugard, Sarraut, Kitchener  d) Mandela, Thuku, Leopold
**Answer: b) Haile Selassie, Nasser and Nkrumah** — Haile Selassie, Gamal Abdel Nasser and Kwame Nkrumah.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The OAU charter was signed on —
a) June 25, 1963 (the book's misprint)  b) May 25, 1963  c) May 25, 1958  d) January 1, 1960
**Answer: b) May 25, 1963** — May 25, 1963 — taught corrected; the book prints June.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** How many independent countries approved the OAU charter? —
a) 8  b) 14  c) 31  d) 54
**Answer: c) 31** — 31 independent countries.

**7. (Sub lesson 4) ** Pan-Africanism originated —
a) In Addis Ababa in 1963  b) In the New World in the 18th–19th centuries, within the Black liberation struggle  c) In Berlin in 1884  d) In Manchester in 1945
**Answer: b) In the New World in the 18th–19th centuries, within the Black liberation struggle** — Manchester was its turning point, not its birth.

**8. (Sub lesson 4) ** The Fifth Pan-African Congress (Manchester, October 1945) is marked by —
a) European delegates dominating  b) African delegates dominating for the first time, the focus turning to liberating colonised Africa  c) The OAU's founding  d) A trade agreement
**Answer: b) African delegates dominating for the first time, the focus turning to liberating colonised Africa** — the congress that aimed the movement at the colonies.

**9. (Sub lesson 4) ** The OAU was in time replaced by —
a) The League  b) The African Union  c) NAM  d) The UN
**Answer: b) The African Union** — the succession the founding lesson traces.
