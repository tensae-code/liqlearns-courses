# African unity — the founding of the OAU — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the eight states in the Accra 1958 room.
**Answer:** Ghana, Egypt, Sudan, Tunisia, Morocco, Libya, Liberia and Ethiopia — eight, though the book's text says "6" before listing them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the Monrovia and Casablanca groups disagree on, and what did they do about it in 1963?
**Answer:** They differed over political union and relations with the former colonisers; in 1963 they set the political-union question aside for the time, so the OAU could be founded.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List the OAU's four aims as the book gives them.
**Answer:** Strengthening understanding and unity among Africa's peoples; speeding Africa's freedom from imperialism and colonialism; gathering world opinion on violations of Africans' political and basic human rights; building the feeling that could one day found a United States of Africa.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the founding facts: place, corrected date, number of states, seat.
**Answer:** Addis Ababa; charter signed May 25, 1963 (the book prints June 25 — corrected); 31 independent countries approved the charter; Addis Ababa chosen as the seat.
**5. (Unit 7 review — Grade 12)** Unit 7 review: Give the three beats of the unit's politics pattern.
**Answer: One-party states; military coups; the democratic openings of the 1990s.**

**6. (Unit 7 review — Grade 12)** Unit 7 review: How did the Cold War fight inside Africa? Give the Horn example.
**Answer: The superpowers armed rival states; in 1977–78 Somalia and Ethiopia exchanged patrons — the Ogaden war's alliance flip.**
