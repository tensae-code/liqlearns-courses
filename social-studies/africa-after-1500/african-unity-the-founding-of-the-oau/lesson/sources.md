(3) The book (§5.3.1) dates the OAU's founding "June 25, 1963" and the Addis Ababa conference to June 1963 — the OAU charter was in fact signed May 25, 1963; this lesson teaches May 25, 1963. (4) The book (§5.3.1) says the 1958 Accra conference gathered "6" previously independent states, then lists eight (Ghana, Egypt, Sudan, Tunisia, Morocco, Libya, plus Liberia and Ethiopia) — the count and the list disagree as printed; this lesson teaches the list of eight and names the disagreement.
