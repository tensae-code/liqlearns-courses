# African unity — the founding of the OAU — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Accra 1958 — and the two camps
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Pan-African congresses met outside Africa until Accra 1958; union then foundered, and Africa split into the Monrovia and Casablanca groups.

## Sub lesson 2 — The reconciliation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Haile Selassie, Nasser and Nkrumah reconciled the groups; the political-union question was set aside for the time.

## Sub lesson 3 — Addis Ababa 1963 — the founding and its aims
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: 31 states approved the charter; Addis Ababa became the seat; the aims — unity, freedom from colonialism, world opinion, and one day a United States of Africa.

## Sub lesson 4 — The Grade 10 depth — Manchester 1945 and the road to the OAU
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: tell Pan-Africanism's origin, Manchester's distinction, and the OAU-to-AU succession.
