# Video script — The Mahdist revolution — El Obeid and Shaykan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
