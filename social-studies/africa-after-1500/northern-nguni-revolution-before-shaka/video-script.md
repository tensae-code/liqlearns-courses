# Video script — Northern Nguni revolution before Shaka

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Nigeria book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dingiswayo of the Mthethwa and Zwide of the Ndwandwe build confederations and age-regiment armies in the northern Nguni area, c. 1790–1818.

Zwide kills Dingiswayo in 1817. With the old order broken, the stage is set for Shaka.

Hold that story: Before Shaka, Dingiswayo of the Mthethwa and Zwide of the Ndwandwe build confederations and age-regiment armies among the northern Nguni — until Zwide kills Dingiswayo in 1817 and the stage is set for Shaka.
