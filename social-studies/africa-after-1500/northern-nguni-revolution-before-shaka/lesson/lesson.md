# Northern Nguni revolution before Shaka

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Before Shaka, Dingiswayo of the Mthethwa and Zwide of the Ndwandwe build confederations and age-regiment armies among the northern Nguni — until Zwide kills Dingiswayo in 1817 and the stage is set for Shaka. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

Dingiswayo of the Mthethwa and Zwide of the Ndwandwe build confederations and age-regiment armies in the northern Nguni area, c. 1790–1818.
