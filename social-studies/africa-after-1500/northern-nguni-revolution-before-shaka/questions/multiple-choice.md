# Northern Nguni revolution before Shaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dingiswayo led which people?
a) The Mthethwa  b) The Ndwandwe  c) The Zulu
**Answer: a) The Mthethwa** — Dingiswayo of the Mthethwa.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Zwide led which people?
a) The Mthethwa  b) The Ndwandwe  c) The Swazi
**Answer: b) The Ndwandwe** — Zwide of the Ndwandwe.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What new kind of army did these builders raise?
a) Mercenary armies  b) Naval fleets  c) Age-regiment armies
**Answer: c) Age-regiment armies** — age-regiment armies.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In what year did Zwide kill Dingiswayo?
a) 1817  b) 1790  c) 1828
**Answer: a) 1817** — Zwide kills Dingiswayo in 1817.
