# Northern Nguni revolution before Shaka — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The northern Nguni revolution happened before Shaka's rise.
**Answer: True** — c. 1790–1818, before Shaka.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dingiswayo killed Zwide in 1817.
**Answer: False** — Zwide kills Dingiswayo.
