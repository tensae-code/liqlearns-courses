# The founding of FRELIMO — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Three exile movements merge into one front — FRELIMO — so a united Mozambican people can fight Portuguese colonialism together.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The founding of FRELIMO

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *História — Módulo 3 (PESD I, Programa do Ensino Secundário à Distância – 1º Ciclo)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The founding of FRELIMO

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Beyond the PIDE's reach, Mozambican workers in neighbouring countries build the first exile movements — UDENAMO (1960), UNAMI (1961) and MANU (1961) — whose seed becomes FRELIMO.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The exile movements — UDENAMO, UNAMI and MANU

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Módulo 8 de História — O Colonialismo Português a partir de 1930 (PESD II, 2º Ciclo)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The exile movements — UDENAMO, UNAMI and MANU

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The nationalist peak is unification — the diverse forces of the early 1960s merge into a single movement, FRELIMO.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The creation of FRELIMO
