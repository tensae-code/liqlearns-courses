# Lesson plan — Zimbabwe — my country and its peoples

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Zimbabwe — my country and its peoples
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Zimbabwe, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Zimbabwe is learned as a gathering of distinct peoples — Harare its capital, ten provinces, and each people known by where it lives, the tongue it speaks, its dances and its customs: the Ndau of Chipinge, the Tonga of Binga, the Chewa of Hwange and many more.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **My country and its ethnic groups (Grade 1)** — Zimbabwe is named as home, with Harare its capital and ten provinces. (Topic 2, Unit 6 My country and ethnic groups)
2. **Ethnic groups and their languages (Grade 2)** — The peoples lesson returns, now with languages attached. (Topic 2, Unit 7 Ethnic groups and languages)
3. **Customs of different cultural groups (Grade 4)** — Each cultural group in Zimbabwe keeps its own customs. (Topic 3, Unit 11 Customs of different cultural groups)
4. **The different languages of Zimbabwe (Grade 5)** — Now the country's many tongues are counted and mapped. (Topic 1, Unit 6 Different languages in Zimbabwe)
5. **Ethnic groups and their cultures (Grade 6)** — At the top of the cycle the peoples of Zimbabwe are paraded group by group, each with its culture. (Topic 1, Unit 3 Different ethnic groups of Zimbabwe and their cultures)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
