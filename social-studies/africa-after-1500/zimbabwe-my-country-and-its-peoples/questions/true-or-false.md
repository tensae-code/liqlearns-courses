# Zimbabwe — my country and its peoples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ndau are introduced dancing muchongoyo at Chipinge.
**Answer: True** — Ndau dancing muchongoyo at Chipinge is the Grade 1 introduction

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A people's language is part of how the lesson identifies it.
**Answer: True** — each group is known by where it lives and the tongue it speaks

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Zimbabwe's peoples all share exactly the same customs.
**Answer: False** — each group keeps its own customs

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Zimbabwe is a one-language country.
**Answer: False** — the lesson counts its many tongues

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Learning Zimbabwe means learning its peoples one by one.
**Answer: True** — the peoples are paraded group by group with their cultures
