# Zimbabwe — my country and its peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zimbabwe's capital is —
a) Harare  b) Binga  c) Chipinge
**Answer: a) Harare** — Harare is named as the capital

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tonga of Binga are introduced —
a) fishing  b) dancing muchongoyo  c) mining gold
**Answer: a) fishing** — the Tonga are shown fishing at Binga

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At Grade 2, each ethnic group is known by —
a) where it lives and the tongue it speaks  b) its football team  c) the colour of its houses
**Answer: a) where it lives and the tongue it speaks** — each group is known by place and language

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The new thing added to the peoples lesson at Grade 2 is —
a) languages  b) national exams  c) the national anthem
**Answer: a) languages** — languages are attached to the groups at Grade 2

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Lining the groups' customs side by side shows —
a) difference itself as the national picture  b) that one custom must win  c) that customs should be banned
**Answer: a) difference itself as the national picture** — difference itself becomes the national picture

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Each cultural group in Zimbabwe —
a) keeps its own customs  b) has no customs  c) copies Harare in everything
**Answer: a) keeps its own customs** — each group keeps its own customs

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Counting Zimbabwe's languages works as —
a) a census of its peoples  b) a spelling test  c) a weather report
**Answer: a) a census of its peoples** — language becomes the census of Zimbabwe's peoples

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** The languages lesson asks learners to —
a) count and map the country's tongues  b) ban all but one language  c) memorise one dictionary
**Answer: a) count and map the country's tongues** — the tongues are counted and mapped

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** At Grade 6, the nation is learned as —
a) a gathering of distinct peoples  b) one single culture  c) a list of towns only
**Answer: a) a gathering of distinct peoples** — the nation is a gathering of distinct peoples

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** Each group is presented with —
a) its own culture  b) a number only  c) the same custom as all others
**Answer: a) its own culture** — each group is paraded with its culture
