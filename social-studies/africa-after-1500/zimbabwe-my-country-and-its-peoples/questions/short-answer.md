**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many provinces does Zimbabwe have in this lesson?
**Answer: Ten (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two facts name a people in this lesson?
**Answer: Where it lives and the language it speaks (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a custom, in this lesson's sense?
**Answer: A group's own kept way of doing things, handed down (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Why does counting languages tell you about peoples?
**Answer: Because each people is known by the tongue it speaks (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** How are the peoples presented at Grade 6?
