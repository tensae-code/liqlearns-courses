# Maroon resistance and Queen Nanny — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Nanny de los Cimarrones.
**Answer: Nanny, an escaped slave of African royal descent, frees others, founds mountain-top Nanny Town in British Jamaica, camouflages her Maroons as trees to ambush soldiers, and holds out until hunger — not British guns — tests her people (any close wording).**
