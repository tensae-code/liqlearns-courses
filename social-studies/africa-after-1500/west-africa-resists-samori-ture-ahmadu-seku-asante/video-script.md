# Video script — West Africa resists — Samori Ture, Ahmadu Seku, Asante


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on West Africa — and this time, it fights back.

In the north, a horseman-statesman builds an empire between the Niger and the Volta: Samori Ture.

Year after year the French columns come. Year after year he answers — 1882, 1890, 1898. Sixteen years of war.

Beside him, Ahmadu Seku tries the pen of diplomacy first — until violations make him take the sword.

South, the golden stool of Asante gleams; British guns answer. By 1900 the Gold Coast is a colony.

And in Nigeria, the toolkit completes its work: divide, outgun, recruit. By 1910, conquest.

But hear the presses printing and the unions forming in the towns. Resistance is changing its weapons — not ending.
