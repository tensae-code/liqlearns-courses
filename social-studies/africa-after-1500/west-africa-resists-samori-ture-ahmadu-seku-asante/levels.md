# West Africa resists — Samori Ture, Ahmadu Seku, Asante — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Many kinds of answer
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: List the range of African responses: armed resistance, and nonviolent forms — indigenous press, trade unionism, organised religion, associations, literature and art, mass action.

## Sub lesson 2 — Samori Ture and Ahmadu Seku
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tell Samori Ture's war: Mandinka Muslim leader, united the Niger sources to Upper Volta basin, protracted war with France 1882–1898; and Ahmadu Seku: diplomacy first, then clashes after French violations, defeat, exile to Sokoto, the French ruling through his brother.

## Sub lesson 3 — Asante and Nigeria
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tell the Asante story: the kingdom under Asantehene Prempeh, defeated after bloody war as Britain completed the Gold Coast colony by 1900; and Nigeria's conquest by 1910 through divide-and-rule, superior weapons and African-recruited troops.
