# Hands-on — West Africa resists — Samori Ture, Ahmadu Seku, Asante

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sixteen-year line
Draw a timeline 1882–1898 for Samori's war and compare its length with your own school life. Write one sentence on what sixteen years of resistance required: unity, supplies, and a state behind the army.

## The conqueror's toolkit
Make three cards — divide-and-rule, superior weapons, African-recruited troops — and for Nigeria's conquest, write one line on each card explaining how that tool worked.
