# West Africa resists — Samori Ture, Ahmadu Seku, Asante — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list gives nonviolent forms of African response?
a) Press, trade unionism, associations, mass action  b) Only cavalry charges  c) Surrender and exile  d) Tax strikes by Europeans
**Answer: a) Press, trade unionism, associations, mass action** — Indigenous press, trade unionism, organised religion, associations, literature and art, mass action.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Samori Ture united the region between —
a) The Nile and the Red Sea  b) The Niger sources and the Upper Volta basin  c) The Gold Coast and Lagos  d) Senegal and Mali's coast
**Answer: b) The Niger sources and the Upper Volta basin** — He united the Niger sources to the Upper Volta basin.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Samori Ture's war against the French lasted —
a) 1882–1898  b) 1896 only  c) 1905–1907  d) 1879–1881
**Answer: a) 1882–1898** — Protracted war from 1882 to 1898 — sixteen years.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ahmadu Seku first preferred —
a) Immediate war  b) Diplomacy, until French violations forced clashes  c) Exile  d) Alliance with Britain
**Answer: b) Diplomacy, until French violations forced clashes** — He preferred diplomacy until French violations forced clashes; defeated, he was exiled to Sokoto.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Asante kingdom was ruled by the —
a) Asantehene Prempeh  b) Samori Ture  c) Ahmadu Seku  d) Kinjikitle Ngwale
**Answer: a) Asantehene Prempeh** — Asantehene Prempeh led Asante.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Nigeria was conquered by 1910 through —
a) One treaty  b) Divide-and-rule, superior weapons and African-recruited troops  c) A referendum  d) Missionary schools
**Answer: b) Divide-and-rule, superior weapons and African-recruited troops** — The classic toolkit: divide-and-rule, superior weapons, African-recruited troops.
