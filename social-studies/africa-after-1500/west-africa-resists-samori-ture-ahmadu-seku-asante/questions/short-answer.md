# West Africa resists — Samori Ture, Ahmadu Seku, Asante — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is Samori Ture the book's exemplar of organised resistance?
**Answer:** He first built a united empire between the Niger sources and the Upper Volta basin, then sustained a protracted war against France for sixteen years (1882–1898) — a state, not a single battle, resisting.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three tools of conquest the book lists for Nigeria.
**Answer:** Divide-and-rule, superior weapons, and African-recruited troops.
