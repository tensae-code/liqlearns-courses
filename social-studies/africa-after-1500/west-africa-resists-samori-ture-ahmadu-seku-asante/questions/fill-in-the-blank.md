# West Africa resists — Samori Ture, Ahmadu Seku, Asante — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mandinka Muslim leader who fought France for sixteen years was Samori ____.
**Answer: Ture**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ahmadu Seku was exiled to ____ after his defeat.
**Answer: Sokoto**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Asante king's title was the ____.
**Answer: Asantehene**
