# West Africa resists — Samori Ture, Ahmadu Seku, Asante — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All African response to conquest was armed.
**Answer: False** — Responses included press, unions, religion, associations, literature, art and mass action.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Samori Ture's resistance lasted about sixteen years.
**Answer: True** — 1882 to 1898.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Britain completed the Gold Coast colony by 1900 after war with Asante.
**Answer: True** — Asante was defeated after bloody war; the colony was completed by 1900.
