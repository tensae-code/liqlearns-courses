# Zimbabwe colonised — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lobengula understood the concession exactly as Rhodes's men did.
**Answer: False** — he was misled into it

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonisation here began with a signature before soldiers marched.
**Answer: True** — the concession of 1888 precedes the Column of 1890
