# Zimbabwe colonised — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Rudd Concession gave away —
a) the kingdom's minerals, for 1,000 guns and £100 a month  b) a single farm  c) fishing rights on the Zambezi
**Answer: a) the kingdom's minerals, for 1,000 guns and £100 a month** — minerals were signed away for 1,000 guns and £100 a month

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Pioneer Column marched in —
a) 1890  b) 1888  c) 1980
**Answer: a) 1890** — the Column marches in 1890

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Union Jack was raised at —
a) Fort Salisbury  b) Bulawayo's market  c) Great Zimbabwe
**Answer: a) Fort Salisbury** — the flag is raised at Fort Salisbury
