# Zimbabwe colonised — the Rudd Concession and the Pioneer Column

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Zimbabwe was colonised by paper before it was colonised by soldiers: Lobengula, misled, signed the Rudd Concession in 1888 — 1,000 guns and £100 a month for his kingdom's minerals — and in 1890 the Pioneer Column marched in and raised the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — How colonisation was done — concession and column (Grade 6)

Lobengula signs friendship with Moffat in 1888 — then is misled into the Rudd Concession: 1,000 guns and £100 a month, for his kingdom's minerals. Two years later, in 1890, the Pioneer Column marches in on the strength of that paper and raises the Union Jack at Fort Salisbury. The kingdom is taken first by signature, then by marching feet.
