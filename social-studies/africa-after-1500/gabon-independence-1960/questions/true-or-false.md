# Gabon — independence, 1960 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After years inside French rule, Gabon raises its own flag in 1960
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 32 L'indépendance du Gabon).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Step by step the colony unwinds toward the independence of 1960
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 45 La décolonisation).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Aubame is elected, Mba becomes prime minister, and at 23:58 on 16 August 1960 independence is proclaimed
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 47 La marche du Gabon vers l'indépendance).
