# Gabon — independence, 1960

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: After years inside French rule, Gabon unwinds the colony step by step and proclaims independence on 16 August 1960, with Léon Mba as prime minister. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
