# Cabo Verde — decline and Porto Grande

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of islands with no people on them at all — then people arriving by ship, building towns, rising in revolt, and at last proclaiming their own country. Which part of that long story do you think this lesson tells? Hold your guess; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The abandonment and decline of the islands

As the old routes fade, Ribeira Grande decays and the islands are neglected.

[Picture slot — a picture of two harbours side by side: an old quiet harbour with empty warehouses, and a busy steamship taking on coal — the student says which is Ribeira Grande and which is Porto Grande.]


## Sub lesson 2 — In the time of Porto Grande

Steam shipping makes Mindelo's Porto Grande a great coaling port and São Vicente takes Santiago's place.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the lesson as its beats: The abandonment and decline of the islands — As the old routes fade, Ribeira Grande decays and the islands are neglected.; In the time of Porto Grande — Steam shipping makes Mindelo's Porto Grande a great coaling port and São Vicente takes Santiago's place.. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write, from memory, the places and dates this lesson names.
2. Write who acted in this lesson, and what changed because of them.
3. Write two sentences explaining this lesson's contrast in your own words: one port fades, another rises: Ribeira Grande decays with the old sailing routes, then steam ships make Porto Grande at Mindelo great.
