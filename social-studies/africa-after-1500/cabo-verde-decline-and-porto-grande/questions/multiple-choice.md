# Cabo Verde — decline and Porto Grande — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When the old routes faded, Ribeira Grande —
a) Grew larger  b) Decayed, and the islands were neglected  c) Became the capital of Portugal
**Answer: b) Decayed, and the islands were neglected** — decline and neglect, as the book teaches.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Porto Grande is the port of —
a) Praia  b) Mindelo  c) Ribeira Grande
**Answer: b) Mindelo** — Mindelo's Porto Grande.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Porto Grande became great because steam ships needed —
a) Gold  b) Coal — it was a great coaling port  c) Fresh water only
**Answer: b) Coal — it was a great coaling port** — a coaling port for steam shipping.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Porto Grande's rise meant São Vicente took the place of —
a) Fogo  b) Santiago  c) Santo Antão
**Answer: b) Santiago** — São Vicente takes Santiago's place.
