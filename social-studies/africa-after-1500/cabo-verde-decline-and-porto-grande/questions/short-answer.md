# Cabo Verde — decline and Porto Grande — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the change in one story: what faded, and what rose?
**Answer: The old routes faded, Ribeira Grande decayed and the islands were neglected; then steam shipping made Mindelo's Porto Grande a great coaling port and São Vicente took Santiago's place (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did steam change which island mattered most?
**Answer: Steam ships needed coal stops, and Porto Grande at Mindelo served them — so São Vicente rose over Santiago (any close wording).**
