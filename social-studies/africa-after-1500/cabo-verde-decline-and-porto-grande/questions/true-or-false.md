# Cabo Verde — decline and Porto Grande — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Porto Grande was a great port in the age of sailing ships only.
**Answer: False** — it rose in the time of steam shipping, as a coaling port.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ribeira Grande decayed as the old routes faded.
**Answer: True** — the book's decline story.
