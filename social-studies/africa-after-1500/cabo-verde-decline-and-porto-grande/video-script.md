# Video script — Cabo Verde — decline and Porto Grande

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Cabo Verde book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

As the old routes fade, Ribeira Grande decays and the islands are neglected.

Steam shipping makes Mindelo's Porto Grande a great coaling port and São Vicente takes Santiago's place.

Hold that story: one port fades, another rises: Ribeira Grande decays with the old sailing routes, then steam ships make Porto Grande at Mindelo great.
