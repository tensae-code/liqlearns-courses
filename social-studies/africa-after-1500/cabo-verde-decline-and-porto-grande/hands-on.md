# Hands-on — Cabo Verde — decline and Porto Grande

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tell it on a line
Draw a timeline or a row of boxes, one box for each sub lesson of this lesson. In each box write the place, the people and what changed. Walk the line with your finger and tell the whole story without looking at the lesson.

## Draw the story
Draw two panels: the islands before this lesson's change, and the islands after it. Under each panel write one sentence saying what is different.

## Ask at home
Ask an older person what they know of this part of Cabo Verde's story. Write down one thing they said, and check it against the lesson — what matches, and what is new?
