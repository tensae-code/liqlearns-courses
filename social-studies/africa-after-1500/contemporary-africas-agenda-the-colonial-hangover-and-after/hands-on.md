# Hands-on — Contemporary Africa's agenda — the colonial hangover and after

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One homeland, two flags
Draw one ethnic group's homeland, then draw a state border cutting it. List three practical problems the cut creates for the people living there — school language, markets, family visits.

## The agenda wall
Write each agenda item on a card and pin them in a row. Under each card, the class adds one line: a step a government, a school or a family can take on that item. The wall is the unit's closing exhibit.

## Grade 12 depth: One-party states, coups and the 1990s openings — the pattern

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the new part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
