# Video script — Contemporary Africa's agenda — the colonial hangover and after

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on independence's flags — then the camera tilts, and under each flag the old trade routes still glow, running to the old capitals. The hangover, drawn.
Along one border, a single people's colour is cut in two by a straight colonial line.
Cards rise over the map one by one: corruption — poverty — population — climate. The agenda assembles itself.
The last card is blank. The camera leaves it on the map, for the generation now learning.
