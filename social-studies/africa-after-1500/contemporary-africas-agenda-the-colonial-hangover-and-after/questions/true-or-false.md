# Contemporary Africa's agenda — the colonial hangover and after — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The newly independent states are described as products of colonialism.
**Answer: True** — their borders, economies and administrations were drawn by the departed powers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Ethnic conflicts are listed among contemporary Africa's major issues.
**Answer: True** — ethnicity and ethnic conflicts stand on the agenda.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** The unit ends by declaring Africa's problems solved.
**Answer: False** — it ends with an agenda, not a verdict.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Neo-colonial ties can persist through trade, currency, military and cultural links under new flags.
**Answer: True** — the hangover's structure, not a conspiracy of persons.

**5. (Sub lesson 2) ** Population pressure falls on land, schools and jobs alike.
**Answer: True** — one of the agenda's material pressures.
**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: In the pattern, military coups were a rare exception in independent Africa.
**Answer: False — Coups were a standing possibility in state after state for decades.**
