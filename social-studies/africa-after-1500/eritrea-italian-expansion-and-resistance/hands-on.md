# Hands-on — Eritrea — Italian expansion and resistance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw the road in: Assab 1869, Massawa 1885, Dogali 1887, the colony named 1890 — four stops on one line, with a drawing at each stop.

## Task 2
Write the speech Bahta Hagos might have made in 1894, in your own words: what was taken, and why he rose.
