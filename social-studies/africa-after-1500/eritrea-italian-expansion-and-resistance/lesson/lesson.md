## Sub lesson 2 — Italian rule 1882–1941

Italy lands at Assab and Massawa, proclaims the colony of Eritrea in 1890, and meets resisters like Bahta Hagos — while Eritrean askari are shipped to Italy's other wars.

## Sub lesson 3 — Italian expansion and Eritrean resistance

Sapeto buys Assab in 1869, Italy takes Massawa in 1885, is bloodied by Ras Alula at Dogali in 1887, then rides famine and Yohannes's death to the Treaty of Wuchale and proclaims the colony of Eritrea on 1 January 1890.
