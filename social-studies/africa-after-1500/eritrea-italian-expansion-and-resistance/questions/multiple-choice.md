# Eritrea — Italian expansion and resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy first bought its foothold at —
a) Massawa  b) Assab  c) Asmara
**Answer: b) Assab** — Sapeto bought Assab in 1869.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The colony of Eritrea was proclaimed in —
a) 1869  b) 1887  c) 1890
**Answer: c) 1890** — on 1 January 1890, says the Grade 8 book.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bahta Hagos rose against —
a) the port taxes  b) the land seizures  c) the Naibs
**Answer: b) the land seizures** — he rose in 1894 against Italy's seizure of Eritrean land.
