# Hands-on — Why foreigners came to Uganda — and what they changed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Why foreigners came to Uganda — and what they changed — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Their influence and contributions, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
