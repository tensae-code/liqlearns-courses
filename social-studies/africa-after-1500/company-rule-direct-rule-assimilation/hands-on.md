# Hands-on — Company rule, direct rule, assimilation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three offices role-play
In threes, play the three offices: a company clerk collecting tax for profit, a French resident issuing an order from Paris, a teacher in an assimilation classroom. Each speaks two lines in role; the others name the system.

## Compare chart
Draw a three-row chart — company rule, direct rule, assimilation — with columns: Who governs? By what right? What happens to local culture? Fill it from the lesson.
