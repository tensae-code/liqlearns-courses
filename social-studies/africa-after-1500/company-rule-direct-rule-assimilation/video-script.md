# Video script — Company rule, direct rule, assimilation


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on partitioned Africa — new colours everywhere. Now zoom into one colony's capital.

First office: a company seal on the door. Ledgers and tax lists. A firm is governing.

Second office: a French resident at a great desk. Every order travels down from Paris, through European hands.

Third room: a classroom. Children recite in French. Their own language waits outside the door.

Company, official, classroom — three machines of rule. The next lesson shows Britain's cheaper design.
