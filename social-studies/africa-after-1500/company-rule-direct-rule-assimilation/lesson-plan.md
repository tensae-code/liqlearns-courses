# Lesson plan — Company rule, direct rule, assimilation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Company rule, direct rule, assimilation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Africa and the Colonial Experience 1880s–1960s (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Three systems ruled the partitioned continent: **company rule**, where chartered companies governed as the first administration; **direct rule**, the French-style government through European officials; and **assimilation**, its aim — to make colonised people into French citizens in culture.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Scramble lesson — the continent is partitioned; now how the pieces were governed. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Company rule — a firm governs first** — Explain company rule: chartered companies governed colonies as the first administration.
2. **Direct rule — officials from Europe** — Explain direct rule as the French-style system of ruling through European officials.
3. **Assimilation — the aim behind the rule** — Explain assimilation as the French policy aiming to make colonised people into French citizens in culture.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

1. Explain company rule and why a firm could govern a colony.
2. Describe direct rule's chain of command.
3. Define assimilation and its cultural aim.
