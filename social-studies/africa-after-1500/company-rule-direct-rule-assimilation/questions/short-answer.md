# Company rule, direct rule, assimilation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** How do direct rule and assimilation fit together?
**Answer:** Direct rule was the machinery — government through European officials; assimilation was the aim that machinery served — to make colonised people into French citizens in culture.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the classroom the right third panel for assimilation?
**Answer:** Because assimilation worked by remaking culture — French language and schooling were its instruments, turning colonised children toward French identity.
