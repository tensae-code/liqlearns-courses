# Company rule, direct rule, assimilation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under company rule, a colony was governed first by —
a) African kings  b) Chartered companies  c) The United Nations  d) Missionary societies only
**Answer: b) Chartered companies** — Chartered companies governed as the first administration.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A chartered company's rule joined government to —
a) Profit — rule and revenue in one office  b) Charity  c) African parliaments  d) The church alone
**Answer: a) Profit — rule and revenue in one office** — Companies taxed, judged and profited — rule and revenue together.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Direct rule governed through —
a) Existing African chiefs  b) European officials  c) Elected assemblies  d) Company clerks
**Answer: b) European officials** — Direct rule is the French-style government through European officials.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In direct rule, African authorities were —
a) Made supreme  b) Set aside or subordinated  c) Sent to Paris as ministers  d) Unchanged
**Answer: b) Set aside or subordinated** — The chain of command was European at every link that mattered.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Assimilation aimed to make colonised people into —
a) Soldiers only  b) French citizens in culture  c) Company shareholders  d) Independent farmers
**Answer: b) French citizens in culture** — Assimilation aimed at French citizens in culture — language, schooling, law, manners.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Assimilation is best described as —
a) The civilizing mission turned into an administrative system  b) A trade treaty  c) Britain's policy  d) A form of resistance
**Answer: a) The civilizing mission turned into an administrative system** — It is the civilizing mission as administration.
