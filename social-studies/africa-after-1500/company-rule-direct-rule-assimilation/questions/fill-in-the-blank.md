# Company rule, direct rule, assimilation — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Colonies first governed by firms were under ____ rule.
**Answer: company**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The French-style system of ruling through European officials is ____ rule.
**Answer: direct**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The policy of making colonised people French in culture is ____.
**Answer: assimilation**
