# Company rule, direct rule, assimilation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Company rule means rule by a committee of African elders.
**Answer: False** — It means government by chartered companies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Direct rule kept African authorities at the top of the chain of command.
**Answer: False** — European officials held the chain; African authorities were set aside or subordinated.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Assimilation sought to preserve colonised cultures untouched.
**Answer: False** — It aimed to remake the colonised into French citizens in culture.
