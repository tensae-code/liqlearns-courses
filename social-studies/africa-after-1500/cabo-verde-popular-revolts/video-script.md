# Video script — Cabo Verde — popular revolts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Cabo Verde book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The poor of the islands rise again and again — Ribeira Grande 1811, the Engenhos 1822, Monte Agarro 1835, Achada Falcão 1841, Casa Martins 1847 — against taxes, hunger and abuse.

Ribeirão Manuel 1910 — the rising the book carries into the 1900s, still against taxes, hunger and abuse.

Hold that story: again and again, for a hundred years: the poor rise against taxes, hunger and abuse — 1811, 1822, 1835, 1841, 1847, 1910.
