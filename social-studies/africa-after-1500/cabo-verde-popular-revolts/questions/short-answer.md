# Cabo Verde — popular revolts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the six revolts with their years, as the book gives them.
**Answer: Ribeira Grande 1811, the Engenhos 1822, Monte Agarro 1835, Achada Falcão 1841, Casa Martins 1847, Ribeirão Manuel 1910 (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three things did the revolts rise against?
**Answer: Taxes, hunger and abuse (any close wording).**
