# Cabo Verde — popular revolts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The popular revolts were risings of —
a) Foreign merchants  b) The poor of the islands  c) Portuguese kings
**Answer: b) The poor of the islands** — the poor of the islands, again and again.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The revolts rose against —
a) Bad weather only  b) Taxes, hunger and abuse  c) Each other
**Answer: b) Taxes, hunger and abuse** — taxes, hunger and abuse.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which rising came in 1910?
a) Casa Martins  b) Ribeirão Manuel  c) Monte Agarro
**Answer: b) Ribeirão Manuel** — Ribeirão Manuel, 1910.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The first rising the book names is —
a) Ribeira Grande, 1811  b) Achada Falcão, 1841  c) The Engenhos, 1822
**Answer: a) Ribeira Grande, 1811** — Ribeira Grande 1811 heads the list.
