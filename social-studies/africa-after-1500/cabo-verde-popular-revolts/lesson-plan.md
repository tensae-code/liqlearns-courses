# Lesson plan — Cabo Verde — popular revolts

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Cabo Verde — popular revolts
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Cabo Verde, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the poor of the islands rise again and again — Ribeira Grande 1811, the Engenhos 1822, Monte Agarro 1835, Achada Falcão 1841, Casa Martins 1847, Ribeirão Manuel 1910 — against taxes, hunger and abuse.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: again and again, for a hundred years: the poor rise against taxes, hunger and abuse — 1811, 1822, 1835, 1841, 1847, 1910. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
