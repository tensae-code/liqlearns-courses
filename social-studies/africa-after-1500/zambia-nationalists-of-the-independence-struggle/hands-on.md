# Hands-on — Zambia — nationalists of the independence struggle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A wall of names
Write each nationalist and freedom fighter in this lesson on a separate card. Pin or lay them in two rows — leading nationalists above, other freedom fighters below — and say one fact about each as you place it.

## The man on the statue
Draw a figure breaking chains, as the Freedom Statue shows Mutembo. Under it, write his full name and the two places his figure can be seen.

## Honouring by name
Choose one of the women organisers. Write three lines, in your own words, on why Zambia's module teaches learners to honour her by name.
