# Zambia — nationalists of the independence struggle — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kenneth Kaunda carried the struggle to the presidency at independence in 1964.
**Answer: True** — with Simon Mwansa Kapwepwe beside him among the leading nationalists.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The module teaches that independence was the work of leaders alone.
**Answer: False** — it names the other freedom fighters and even the Mposa Mabwe stone throwers of Kitwe alongside the leaders.
