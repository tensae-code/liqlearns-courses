# Zambia — nationalists of the independence struggle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mama Chikamoneka's full name is —
a) Chibesa Kankasa  b) Julia Mulenga Nsofwa  c) Nalumino Mundia
**Answer: b) Julia Mulenga Nsofwa** — Chibesa Kankasa is Mama Kankasa.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The one demand the leading nationalists carried to 1964 was —
a) Majority rule  b) The Federation  c) Lower taxes only
**Answer: a) Majority rule** — from the welfare societies to Kaunda's presidency.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** John Zanco Mpundu Mutembo is the man —
a) Who signed the Federation  b) Breaking his chains on the Freedom Statue, and on the currency  c) Who led the Ngoni in 1897
**Answer: b) Breaking his chains on the Freedom Statue, and on the currency** — the fighter every Zambian has seen.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Mposa Mabwe were —
a) Stone throwers of Kitwe  b) A Copperbelt union  c) London delegates
**Answer: a) Stone throwers of Kitwe** — boys whose arrests and courage sped the British toward the exit.
