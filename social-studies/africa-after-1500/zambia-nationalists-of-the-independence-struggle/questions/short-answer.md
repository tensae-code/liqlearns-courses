# Zambia — nationalists of the independence struggle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two women organisers in this lesson, with their well-known names.
**Answer: Julia Mulenga Nsofwa (Mama Chikamoneka) and Chibesa Kankasa (Mama Kankasa) (both, with either name form).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name any three of the other freedom fighters besides Mutembo.
**Answer: Any three of Moses Chinyama Luneta, Lewis Changufu, Nalumino Mundia, Reuben Kamanga, Sikota Wina, Justin Chimba, Grey Zulu and Aaron Milner.**
