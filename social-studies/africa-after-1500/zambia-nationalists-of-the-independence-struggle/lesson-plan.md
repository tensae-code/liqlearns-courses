# Lesson plan — Zambia — nationalists of the independence struggle

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Zambia — nationalists of the independence struggle
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Zambia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Zambia's independence was carried by named people: the leading nationalists — from Dauti Yamba and Godwin Mbikusita Lewanika through Nkumbula, Kaunda and Kapwepwe, and the women organisers Mama Chikamoneka and Mama Kankasa — and behind them the other freedom fighters, from John Zanco Mpundu Mutembo, whose chained figure stands on the Freedom Statue, to the Mposa Mabwe stone throwers of Kitwe.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Zambia's own two-stage telling of resistance and on the names, places and dates the Zambian module itself names. It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The leading nationalists** — Dauti Yamba, Godwin Mbikusita Lewanika, Harry Mwaanga Nkumbula, Kenneth Kaunda, Simon Mwansa Kapwepwe, Julia Mulenga Nsofwa (Mama Chikamoneka), Chibesa Kankasa (Mama Kankasa) and Mainza Chona carry the demand for majority rule from the welfare societies to the presidency in 1964
2. **The other freedom fighters** — John Zanco Mpundu Mutembo — the man on the Freedom Statue and the currency, breaking his chains — with Moses Chinyama Luneta, Lewis Changufu, Nalumino Mundia, Reuben Kamanga, Sikota Wina, Justin Chimba, Grey Zulu, Aaron Milner and the Mposa Mabwe stone throwers of Kitwe

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Colonisation — direct and indirect rule. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the leading nationalists, including the two women organisers by their well-known names.
2. Say what single demand they carried, and where it ended in 1964.
3. Name Mutembo and say where his figure can be seen.
4. Name the Mposa Mabwe stone throwers and their town.
