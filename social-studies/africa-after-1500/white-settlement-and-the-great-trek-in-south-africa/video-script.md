# Video script — White settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Settlement at the Cape. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Dutch settlers came to the Cape in the 1600s to farm and supply ships; Britain later took the colony. Watch the names appear one by one: the Cape, settler, frontier. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — The Great Trek. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
In the 1830s many Boer farmers — Dutch-descended settlers — loaded wagons and trekked inland, leaving British rule to found their own republics beyond the colony's reach.

The Trek is remembered by its descendants as a founding exodus. Watch the names appear one by one: Great Trek, Boers, republics. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Conflict over land. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Trekker republics and African kingdoms — Zulu, Ndebele, Sotho and others — fought and negotiated over the interior for decades. Watch the names appear one by one: landholding, Ndebele, roots. This is the part of the story to hold.
