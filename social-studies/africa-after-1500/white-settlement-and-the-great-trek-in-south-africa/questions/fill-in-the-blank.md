# White settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the moving edge of settlement and conflict over land' is ______.
**Answer: frontier**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the independent states the trekkers founded' is ______.
**Answer: republics**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the 19th-century origins of later land law' is ______.
**Answer: roots**
