# White settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Cape?
a) settler
b) frontier
c) the southern colony where Dutch, then British, settlement began
**Answer: c) the southern colony where Dutch, then British, settlement began**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Great Trek?
a) Boers
b) the Boer wagon migration inland in the 1830s
c) republics
**Answer: b) the Boer wagon migration inland in the 1830s**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is landholding?
a) roots
b) who holds the land — the map drawn in these conflicts
