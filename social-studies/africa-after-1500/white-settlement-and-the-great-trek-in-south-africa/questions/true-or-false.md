# White settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: A European who came to stay and farm African land — this is settler.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, the Cape means the moving edge of settlement and conflict over land.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The Dutch-descended settler farmers — this is Boers.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, Great Trek means the independent states the trekkers founded.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: An African kingdom in the trekkers' path, itself born of the Mfecane — this is Ndebele.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, landholding means the 19th-century origins of later land law.
**Answer: False**
