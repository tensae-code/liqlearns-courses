# White settlement and the Great Trek in South Africa — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Settlement at the Cape

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **the Cape** (the southern colony where Dutch, then British, settlement began).

## Sub lesson 2 — The Great Trek

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Great Trek** (the Boer wagon migration inland in the 1830s).

## Sub lesson 3 — Conflict over land

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **landholding** (who holds the land — the map drawn in these conflicts).
