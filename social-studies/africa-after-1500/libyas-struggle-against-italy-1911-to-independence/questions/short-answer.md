**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Why Italy invaded, and how it prepared.
**Answer: Cheated of Tunisia in 1881, Italy wins Europe's consent, then creeps in peacefully — Banco di Roma branches in Tripoli and Benghazi, schools, clinics, doctors — before an ultimatum over the Edirne ship affair gives Istanbul 24 hours; war is declared on 29 September 1911 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: The invasion and the Treaty of Ouchy.
**Answer: The fleet shells Tripoli on 3 October 1911 against barely 7,000 Ottoman troops, and the garrison refuses Istanbul's surrender order; at Ouchy on 18 October 1912 the Sultan withdraws — but his firman grants Libyans self-rule, so Libyans reject the treaty and fight on: Derna's Friday battle in May 1913, Janduba, and Mahruqa in Fezzan (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: At Misrata on 16 November 1918 the leaders — Baruni, al-Murayyid, al-Suwayhili, Bilkhayr — proclaim the Tripolitanian Republic; the 1919 Suani Ben Yadam peace wins a Fundamental Law of rights, and at Gharyan in 1920 the leaders resolve that only unity under one elected leader can save the country (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
