# Eritrea — British military administration

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: britain defeated Italy at Keren in March 1941 and ruled Eritrea through a Military Administration for eleven years — banning politics, protecting Italian property, stripping factories, and working to keep Eritrea weak and divided while Eritreans organised for their own future.
