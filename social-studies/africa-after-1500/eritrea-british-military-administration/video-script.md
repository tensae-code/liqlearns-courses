# Video script — Eritrea — British military administration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Britain defeats Italy in 1941 and rules Eritrea as an occupied territory for eleven years while Eritreans organise for their own future.

Britain defeats Italy at Keren in March 1941 and rules through a Military Administration that bans politics, protects Italian property, strips factories, and works to keep Eritrea weak and divided so the west can be joined to Sudan.
