# Eritrea — British military administration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** British rule lasted eleven years, 1941–1952.
**Answer: True** — both books give the eleven years.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Britain built up Eritrea's factories during the occupation.
**Answer: False** — it stripped factories and kept Eritrea weak.
