# Eritrea — British military administration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things the Military Administration did to keep Eritrea weak.
**Answer: Any two of: banned politics, protected Italian property, stripped factories, worked to divide the country (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What were Eritreans doing during the British years?
**Answer: Organising for their own future — the parties of the 1940s grew in these years (any close wording).**
