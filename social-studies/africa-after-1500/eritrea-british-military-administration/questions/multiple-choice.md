# Eritrea — British military administration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain defeated Italy in Eritrea at —
a) Keren, March 1941  b) Dogali, 1887  c) Afabet, 1988
**Answer: a) Keren, March 1941** — the battle of Keren, March 1941.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The British Military Administration —
a) encouraged parties  b) banned politics  c) gave Eritrea independence at once
**Answer: b) banned politics** — it banned politics and protected Italian property.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Britain wanted Eritrea's west joined to —
a) Ethiopia  b) Sudan  c) Italy
**Answer: b) Sudan** — so the west could be joined to Sudan, says the Grade 8 book.
