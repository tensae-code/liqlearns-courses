# Lesson plan — Eritrea — British military administration

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — British military administration
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain defeated Italy at Keren in March 1941 and ruled Eritrea through a Military Administration for eleven years — banning politics, protecting Italian property, stripping factories, and working to keep Eritrea weak and divided while Eritreans organised for their own future.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **British rule 1941–1952** — Britain defeats Italy in 1941 and rules Eritrea as an occupied territory for eleven years while Eritreans organise for their own future (British rule 1941–1952)
2. **British Colonial Administration (Occupation)** — Britain defeats Italy at Keren in March 1941 and rules through a Military Administration that bans politics, protects Italian property, strips factories, and works to keep Eritrea weak and divided so the west can be joined to Sudan (British Colonial Administration (Occupation))

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer, in their own words: name two things the Military Administration did to keep Eritrea weak.
2. Answer, in their own words: what were Eritreans doing during the British years?
