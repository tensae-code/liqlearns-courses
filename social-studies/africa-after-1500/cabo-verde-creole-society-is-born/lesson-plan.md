# Lesson plan — Cabo Verde — a creole society is born

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Cabo Verde — a creole society is born
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Cabo Verde, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** peoples and cultures from Europe and Africa meet on the islands and a new society is born — creole in language, food, music and custom — and the Grade 6 workbook presents that creole culture, in Amílcar Cabral's words, as the people's own wealth to defend and develop.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: a new people, not a copy: neither Europe nor Africa alone, but a creole society born from their meeting — in language, food, music, custom and housing. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Origin and evolution of Cabo Verdean society and culture** — Peoples and cultures from Europe and Africa meet on the islands and a new society is born — creole in language, food, music and custom, ordered in a strict social structure.
2. **Cabo Verdean culture** — The creole culture born from the meeting of peoples — language, oral traditions, typical dishes, religious festivals, music and dance, literature, crafts and housing — is presented, in Cabral's words, as the people's own wealth to defend and develop.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that the new society was creole — born from Europe and Africa meeting — and name language, food, music and custom.
2. List the parts of creole culture the workbook names, and say whose words call it the people's wealth — Amílcar Cabral's.
