# Cabo Verde — a creole society is born — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cabo Verdean culture is simply Portuguese culture copied onto islands.
**Answer: False** — it is a new creole culture born from Europe and Africa meeting.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first Cabo Verdean society was ordered in a strict social structure.
**Answer: True** — as the Grade 5 book teaches.
