# Cabo Verde — a creole society is born — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cabo Verdean society was born from the meeting of peoples and cultures from —
a) Asia and America  b) Europe and Africa  c) Two African kingdoms only
**Answer: b) Europe and Africa** — Europe and Africa meet on the islands.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The new society is called creole in —
a) Language, food, music and custom  b) Only its flag  c) Only its money
**Answer: a) Language, food, music and custom** — creole in language, food, music and custom.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 6 workbook presents creole culture, in whose words, as the people's own wealth?
a) A Portuguese governor's  b) Amílcar Cabral's  c) A visiting sailor's
**Answer: b) Amílcar Cabral's** — Cabral's words, as the workbook presents them.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these does the workbook name as part of Cabo Verdean culture?
a) Oral traditions, typical dishes, festivals, music and dance, literature, crafts and housing  b) Only written law  c) Only farming tools
**Answer: a) Oral traditions, typical dishes, festivals, music and dance, literature, crafts and housing** — the workbook's own list.
