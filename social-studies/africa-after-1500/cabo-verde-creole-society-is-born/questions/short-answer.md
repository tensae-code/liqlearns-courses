# Cabo Verde — a creole society is born — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does "creole" mean for Cabo Verdean society, as the books teach it?
**Answer: A new society and culture born from the meeting of European and African peoples on the islands — in language, food, music and custom (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four parts of Cabo Verdean culture from the Grade 6 workbook.
**Answer: Any four of: language, oral traditions, typical dishes, religious festivals, music and dance, literature, crafts, housing (any close wording).**
