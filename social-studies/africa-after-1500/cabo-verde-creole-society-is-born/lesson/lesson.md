# Cabo Verde — a creole society is born

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of islands with no people on them at all — then people arriving by ship, building towns, rising in revolt, and at last proclaiming their own country. Which part of that long story do you think this lesson tells? Hold your guess; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Origin and evolution of Cabo Verdean society and culture

Peoples and cultures from Europe and Africa meet on the islands and a new society is born — creole in language, food, music and custom, ordered in a strict social structure.

In your mind's eye: an island market where European and African foods, instruments and cloth are laid out side by side becoming one stall. Name three things that became creole.


## Sub lesson 2 — Cabo Verdean culture

The creole culture born from the meeting of peoples — language, oral traditions, typical dishes, religious festivals, music and dance, literature, crafts and housing — is presented, in Cabral's words, as the people's own wealth to defend and develop.
