**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Cabo Verde).**

Cabo Verde's own way (Grade 5): The child's first History subject is the past of her own islands only — an uninhabited archipelago found by Portuguese ships, peopled by Europeans and enslaved Africans, becoming a new creole society — taught theme by theme with documents, a mini-dictionary of concepts and "Vou resolver" activities. Cabo Verde's own way (Grade 6): The 6th-year workbook continues the islands' story where Grade 5 stopped — from creole culture to Cabral's liberation struggle — and teaches it through source documents (including Amílcar Cabral's own PAIGC texts) that pupils interrogate themselves. The book's stories are filed here as plot summaries in the course's own words only — never transcribed.
