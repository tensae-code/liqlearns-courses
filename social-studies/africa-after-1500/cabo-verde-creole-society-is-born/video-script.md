# Video script — Cabo Verde — a creole society is born

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Cabo Verde book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Peoples and cultures from Europe and Africa meet on the islands and a new society is born — creole in language, food, music and custom, ordered in a strict social structure.

The creole culture born from the meeting of peoples — language, oral traditions, typical dishes, religious festivals, music and dance, literature, crafts and housing — is presented, in Cabral's words, as the people's own wealth to defend and develop.

Hold that story: a new people, not a copy: neither Europe nor Africa alone, but a creole society born from their meeting — in language, food, music, custom and housing.
