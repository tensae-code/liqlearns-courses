# Video script — Kongo and the South-Central kingdoms

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

[Scene 1 — Kongo's Christian kings. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The kingdom of Kongo adopted Christianity early and voluntarily: its kings corresponded with Portugal as fellow monarchs, built churches, and sent embassies to Europe.

For generations Kongo dealt with Portugal as a partner. Watch the names appear one by one: Kongo, embassy, slave trade. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Angola's kingdoms. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
South of Kongo, the kingdoms of Angola — Ndongo among them, famous for Queen Nzinga's resistance and diplomacy — faced Portuguese pressure from the coastal colony at Luanda.

Their wars and negotiations with the Portuguese run through the whole period: African statecraft under mounting external strain.. Watch the names appear one by one: Ndongo, Nzinga, Luanda. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Luba and Lunda successor states. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
In the savanna interior, the Luba and Lunda worlds generated successor states — kingdoms carrying their titles, regalia and trade connections outward across South-Central Africa.

A political technology travelling: kingship itself, exported from people to people.. Watch the names appear one by one: Luba, Lunda, successor states. This is the part of the story to hold.
