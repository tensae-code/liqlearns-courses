# Kongo and the South-Central kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Kongo?
a) slave trade
b) the kingdom whose kings adopted Christianity and dealt with Portugal as partners
c) embassy
**Answer: b) the kingdom whose kings adopted Christianity and dealt with Portugal as partners**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Ndongo?
a) Luanda
b) an Angolan kingdom, famed for Queen Nzinga
c) Nzinga
**Answer: b) an Angolan kingdom, famed for Queen Nzinga**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Luba?
a) Lunda
b) an interior people whose kingship model spread widely
c) successor states
**Answer: b) an interior people whose kingship model spread widely**
