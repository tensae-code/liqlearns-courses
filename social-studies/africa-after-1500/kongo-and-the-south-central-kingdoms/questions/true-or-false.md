# Kongo and the South-Central kingdoms — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: A royal mission sent abroad, as Kongo sent to Europe — this is embassy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Kongo means the drain that corroded Kongo's kings' authority.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The queen whose resistance and diplomacy faced the Portuguese — this is Nzinga.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, Ndongo means the Portuguese coastal base in Angola.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The second great interior kingship tradition — this is Lunda.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Luba means kingdoms founded carrying Luba and Lunda titles and regalia outward.
**Answer: False**
