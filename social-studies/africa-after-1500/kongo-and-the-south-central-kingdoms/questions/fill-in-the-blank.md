# Kongo and the South-Central kingdoms — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the drain that corroded Kongo's kings' authority' is ______.
**Answer: slave trade**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the Portuguese coastal base in Angola' is ______.
**Answer: Luanda**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'kingdoms founded carrying Luba and Lunda titles and regalia outward' is ______.
**Answer: successor states**
