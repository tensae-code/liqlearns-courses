# Hands-on — Colonisation — the Berlin Conference

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story map
On a big sheet, draw this lesson as a map or timeline — places, arrows and dates from lesson.md only. Walk along it with a finger and retell the story at each stop.

## Act the turning point
With a partner, act the lesson's turning point in six lines of speech — one of you for each side. Then swap sides and act it again: the other side's reasons must be spoken fairly.

## Teach it home
Tell the lesson's story at home in five sentences. Note the one question you are asked that you cannot yet answer, and find its answer in lesson.md tomorrow.
