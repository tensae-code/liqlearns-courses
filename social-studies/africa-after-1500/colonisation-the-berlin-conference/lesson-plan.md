# Lesson plan — Colonisation — the Berlin Conference

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Colonisation — the Berlin Conference
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 12 — Africa after 1500 (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** At **Berlin in 1884–85**, **fourteen European countries** under **Bismarck** signed the **General Act** — Africa's colonial ordering settled on paper, by **Article 34's** notification rule.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has met Africa after 1500 in this title's order. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say why the Berlin Conference was called, who chaired it, and how many countries met.
2. Give the date the General Act was signed, as printed.
3. State Article 34's rule as the book gives it.
