# Colonisation — the Berlin Conference — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why was the Berlin Conference called?
**Answer:** The Scramble's race — sped by Leopold of Belgium and Germany entering the rivalry — bred conflict among Europeans; Berlin was called to settle the colonial ordering of Africa peacefully.

**2.** State Article 34's rule as the book gives it.
**Answer:** Any European state taking a colony in Africa had to notify the other signatories, who could approve, reject or counter-claim the holding.

**3.** Who chaired the conference, and when did it meet?
**Answer:** Otto von Bismarck chaired it; it met in 1884–85.
