# Colonisation — the Berlin Conference — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Berlin Conference's main aim was to scramble for Africa without war among Europeans.
**Answer: True** — The chapter's own assessment: Berlin's aim was an orderly, war-free scramble.

**2.** African rulers sat at the Berlin table as parties.
**Answer: False** — No Africans were at the table — Africa was the subject, not a party.

**3.** King Leopold of Belgium and Germany entering the rivalry sped the Scramble's race.
**Answer: True** — Their entry sped the race that Berlin was called to order.
