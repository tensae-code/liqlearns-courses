# Colonisation — the Berlin Conference — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Berlin Conference was called to —
a) Free the slaves  b) Settle the Europeans' own colonial rivalry peacefully  c) Divide Asia  d) Elect a king of Africa
**Answer: b) Settle the Europeans' own colonial rivalry peacefully** — It settled conflict among Europeans over the colonial ordering of Africa.

**2.** The conference was chaired under —
a) King Leopold  b) Otto von Bismarck  c) Lord Kitchener  d) Frederick Lugard
**Answer: b) Otto von Bismarck** — Bismarck chaired it.

**3.** How many European countries met at Berlin? —
a) 7  b) 10  c) 14  d) 31
**Answer: c) 14** — Fourteen European countries.

**4.** Article 34 required a state taking a colony to —
a) Free its people  b) Notify the other signatories  c) Pay Africa  d) Ask the OAU
**Answer: b) Notify the other signatories** — Notify — the others could approve, reject or counter-claim.
