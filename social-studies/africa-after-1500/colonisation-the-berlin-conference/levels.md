# Colonisation — the Berlin Conference — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Say why the Berlin Conference was called, who chaired it, and how many countries met.; Give the date the General Act was signed, as printed.; State Article 34's rule as the book gives it. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
