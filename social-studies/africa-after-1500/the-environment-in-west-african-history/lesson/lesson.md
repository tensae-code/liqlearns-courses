# The environment in West African history

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Climate, disease and landscape shape states and trade in West Africa as surely as kings do — the environment is an actor in the region's history, not just its setting. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Climate shapes history

Climate shapes West African history. Rain and dryness set what can grow, where people can live, and how states rise and struggle.

## Sub lesson 2 — Disease shapes history

Disease shapes history too. Sickness limits where people and animals can go, and bends the paths of settlement and power.

## Sub lesson 3 — Landscape shapes states and trade

Landscape shapes states and trade as surely as kings do. Rivers, savanna and forest decide where towns grow and which routes trade can take — the land itself helps write the story.
