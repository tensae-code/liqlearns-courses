# The environment in West African history — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The environment is only a setting in West African history, never an actor.
**Answer: False — climate, disease and landscape shape states and trade as surely as kings do.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Disease shaped West African history.
**Answer: True — disease is one of the three environmental forces the book names.**
