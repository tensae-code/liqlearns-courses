# The environment in West African history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate in West African history —
a) Shapes what can grow and where people can live  b) Has no effect on history  c) Is decided by kings
**Answer: a) Shapes what can grow and where people can live**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Disease in West African history —
a) Shapes settlement and power  b) Never affected anyone  c) Only matters today
**Answer: a) Shapes settlement and power**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Landscape shapes —
a) States and trade  b) Nothing at all  c) Only stories
**Answer: a) States and trade**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The book teaches the environment shapes West Africa —
a) As surely as kings do  b) Much less than any other place  c) Only in prehistory
**Answer: a) As surely as kings do**
