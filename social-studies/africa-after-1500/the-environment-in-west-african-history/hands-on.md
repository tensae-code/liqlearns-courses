# Hands-on — The environment in West African history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three actors, no crowns
Draw three figures labelled Climate, Disease and Landscape — no crowns on any of them. Under each, write one way it shapes states or trade in West Africa.

## The land writes the route
Draw a simple map with a river, a dry land and a forest. Mark where you think a trade town would grow, and write one sentence saying why the landscape put it there.
