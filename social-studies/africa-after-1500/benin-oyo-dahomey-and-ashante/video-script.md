# Video script — Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Benin — bronzes and walls. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The kingdom of Benin, in the forest of today's Nigeria, cast bronze plaques and heads of a quality Europe admired, and ringed its capital with enormous earthwork walls.

Benin traded with the Portuguese from the 1480s — pepper, ivory, cloth — as a sovereign power setting its own terms for centuries.. Watch the names appear one by one: Benin, bronze plaques, earthwork walls. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Oyo and Dahomey — cavalry and palace. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
North of the forest, Oyo built a cavalry empire on the savanna edge, its horsemen projecting power where horses could live. Watch the names appear one by one: Oyo, Dahomey, cavalry empire. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Ashante and the Golden Stool. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Ashante union was bound together by the Golden Stool — not a throne to sit on but the sacred soul of the nation, descended, the tradition says, from the sky for the first Asantehene.

A symbol doing constitutional work: whoever understands the Stool understands why Ashante held together — and why it fought so hard when outsiders threatened it.. Watch the names appear one by one: Golden Stool, Asantehene, union. This is the part of the story to hold.
