# Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the vast ramparts ringing Benin's capital' is ______.
**Answer: earthwork walls**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'a state whose power rode on horsemen, as Oyo's did' is ______.
**Answer: cavalry empire**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the binding of Ashante states into one nation around the Stool' is ______.
**Answer: union**
