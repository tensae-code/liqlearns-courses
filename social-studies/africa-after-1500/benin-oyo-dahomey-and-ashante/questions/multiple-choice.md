# Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Benin?
a) earthwork walls
b) the forest kingdom famed for bronze casting and great earthwork walls
c) bronze plaques
**Answer: b) the forest kingdom famed for bronze casting and great earthwork walls**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Oyo?
a) cavalry empire
b) the cavalry empire on the savanna edge
c) Dahomey
**Answer: b) the cavalry empire on the savanna edge**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Golden Stool?
a) union
b) Asantehene
c) the sacred soul-symbol of the Ashante nation
**Answer: c) the sacred soul-symbol of the Ashante nation**
