# Benin, Oyo, Dahomey and Ashante — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: Benin's cast metal artworks recording court life — this is bronze plaques.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Benin means the vast ramparts ringing Benin's capital.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The tight palace state with its famous army — this is Dahomey.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, Oyo means a state whose power rode on horsemen, as Oyo's did.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The Ashante king — this is Asantehene.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Golden Stool means the binding of Ashante states into one nation around the Stool.
**Answer: False**
