# Video script — Missionaries and the Uganda Martyrs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

CMS Protestants come first, Verona Fathers reach the north in 1910, Apollo Kivebulaya becomes "apostle to the pygmies" — and at Namugongo on 3 June 1886 Kizito and his fellow pages are burned, the Uganda Martyrs whose shrine still marks the walk to death.
