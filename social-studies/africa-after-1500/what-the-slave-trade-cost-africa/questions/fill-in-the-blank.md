# What the slave trade cost Africa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the seizure of people that fed the trades' is ______.
**Answer: raiding**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the armed slaver the cycle rewarded' is ______.
**Answer: raider**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'what historians agree on, even while totals are argued' is ______.
**Answer: direction**
