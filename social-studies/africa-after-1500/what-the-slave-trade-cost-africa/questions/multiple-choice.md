# What the slave trade cost Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is population loss?
a) raiding
b) the draining of working-age people from raided regions
c) distortion
**Answer: b) the draining of working-age people from raided regions**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is gun cycle?
a) guns paid for captives, captives taken to buy guns
b) fragmentation
c) raider
**Answer: a) guns paid for captives, captives taken to buy guns**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is underdevelopment?
a) direction
b) labour
c) the long economic distortion the trades left behind
**Answer: c) the long economic distortion the trades left behind**
