# What the slave trade cost Africa — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — People — the first cost

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **population loss** (the draining of working-age people from raided regions).

## Sub lesson 2 — War and the gun cycle

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **gun cycle** (guns paid for captives, captives taken to buy guns).

## Sub lesson 3 — The long bill

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **underdevelopment** (the long economic distortion the trades left behind).
