# Video script — What the slave trade cost Africa

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — People — the first cost. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The trades took people in their working and child-bearing years, and took more men than women to the Atlantic. Watch the names appear one by one: population loss, distortion, raiding. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — War and the gun cycle. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Guns came in payment for captives; captives were taken to buy guns. Watch the names appear one by one: gun cycle, fragmentation, raider. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — The long bill. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The economic bill ran longest: labour lost, trades skewed toward human exports, and development distorted for centuries — the underdevelopment later colonialism inherited and deepened.

Historians still argue the totals. Watch the names appear one by one: underdevelopment, labour, direction. This is the part of the story to hold.
