# Ken Saro-Wiwa and the Ogoni struggle — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** writer Ken Saro-Wiwa champions the Ogoni, whose oil-rich Niger Delta land is polluted while they stay poor; after Abacha's 1993 seizure of power he is jailed repeatedly and hanged on 10 November 1995 after an unfair trial.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2, Case Study 3 "Fair And Equal Treatment For All"

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Studies, Year 10 Book One* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 2, Case Study 3 "Fair And Equal Treatment For All"
