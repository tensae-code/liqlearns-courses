# Video script — Zimbabwe — culture, language and respect for elders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Zimbabwe's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A small child wants something from an elder. She learns to swap a blunt demand for polite words, and in that swap she learns something big: language itself is the way respect is handed down — and respect is culture, carried in the words we choose.
