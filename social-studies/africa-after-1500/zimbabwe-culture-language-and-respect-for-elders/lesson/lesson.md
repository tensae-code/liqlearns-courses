# Zimbabwe — culture, language and respect for elders

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In Zimbabwe's first lessons, culture is handed down through language itself: a child who swaps a blunt demand for polite words to an elder is learning how respect — and culture — passes from one generation to the next. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Culture in relation to language (ECD A)

A small child wants something from an elder. She learns to swap a blunt demand for polite words, and in that swap she learns something big: language itself is the way respect is handed down — and respect is culture, carried in the words we choose.
