# Zimbabwe — culture, language and respect for elders — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture can only be handed down in books.
**Answer: False** — here culture is handed down in spoken language

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Respect for elders is part of Zimbabwe's heritage.
**Answer: True** — respect, carried in language, is the heritage being handed down
