# Zimbabwe — culture, language and respect for elders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, culture is handed down through —
a) language — the words we choose  b) locked cupboards  c) written exams
**Answer: a) language — the words we choose** — language itself becomes the way respect and culture are handed down

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A child shows the lesson has landed when she —
a) swaps a blunt demand for polite words to an elder  b) shouts louder than before  c) avoids all elders
**Answer: a) swaps a blunt demand for polite words to an elder** — the child swaps a blunt demand for polite words

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The polite words are owed especially to —
a) elders  b) the youngest baby  c) visitors from other countries only
**Answer: a) elders** — polite words are for an elder
