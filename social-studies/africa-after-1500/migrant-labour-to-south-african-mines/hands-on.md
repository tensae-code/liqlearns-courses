# Hands-on — Migrant labour to the South African mines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Migrant labour to the South African mines — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The south and migrant labour, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
