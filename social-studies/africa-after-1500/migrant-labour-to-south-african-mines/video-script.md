# Video script — Migrant labour to the South African mines

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the south it is South African mining capital that rules; southern Mozambique's economy is bound to the mines, and its men migrate to work them.
Salazar preaches ending dependence on foreign capital, yet the Estado Novo keeps exporting Mozambican labour — the contradiction at the regime's heart.
