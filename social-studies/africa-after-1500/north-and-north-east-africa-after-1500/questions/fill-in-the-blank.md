# North and North-East Africa after 1500 — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'of the river banks — the Nile valley's settled world' is ______.
**Answer: riverain**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the pattern of neighbouring states around Ethiopia' is ______.
**Answer: board**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the Ottoman capital to which North Africa was tied' is ______.
**Answer: Istanbul**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The term for 'the Mediterranean shore the Ottomans held' is ______.
**Answer: Maghreb coast**
