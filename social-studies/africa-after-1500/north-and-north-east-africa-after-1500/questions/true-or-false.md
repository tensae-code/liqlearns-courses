# North and North-East Africa after 1500 — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The Funj capital on the Nile — this is Sennar.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Funj sultanate means of the river banks — the Nile valley's settled world.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The sea that became heavily Ottoman after 1500 — this is Red Sea.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, regional powers means the pattern of neighbouring states around Ethiopia.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The local rulers through whom Istanbul governed — this is deputies.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Ottoman North Africa means the Ottoman capital to which North Africa was tied.
**Answer: False**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** True or false: The Mediterranean shore the Ottomans held — this is Maghreb coast.
**Answer: True**
