# Hands-on — North and North-East Africa after 1500

One line of framing: this lesson is learned by doing — paper, talk and the learner's own ground.

## Map the story
On a blank page, draw the lesson's map from memory: its places in their true rough positions, its routes and movements as arrows. Then open the lesson and correct your map in a second colour — the corrections are the learning.

## Terms in the hand
Write each key term (Funj sultanate / Sennar / riverain, regional powers / Red Sea / board, Ottoman North Africa / deputies / Istanbul) on its own slip. Shuffle, deal, and for each slip tell one true sentence of the story that uses it.

## Teach it onward
Teach the story to someone at home in five minutes, in order, without the book. Where you stall is where you re-read.

## Grade 11 depth — Ottoman North Africa in depth

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
