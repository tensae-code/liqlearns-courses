# North and North-East Africa after 1500 — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Nile valley after 1500

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Funj sultanate** (the sultanate of Sennar ruling the middle Nile after 1500).

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's neighbours in a new age

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **regional powers** (the African and Ottoman powers contesting the valley, before later colonialism).

## Sub lesson 3 — Ottoman North Africa

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Ottoman North Africa** (Egypt and the Maghreb coast under Ottoman rule for three centuries).

## Sub lesson 4 — Grade 11 depth: Ottoman North Africa in depth

Pass mark: the learner can retell the Grade 11 depth part in the story's order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key terms are **Ottoman rule** (North Africa's three centuries inside the Ottoman system) and **Maghreb coast** (the Mediterranean shore the Ottomans held).
