# Rwanda — the First Republic, 1962–1973 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the First Republic, Tutsi were driven from schools.
**Answer: True** — The book says the republic drives Tutsi from schools.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The First Republic ended with a free election.
**Answer: False** — It ends when Habyarimana overthrows Kayibanda in 1973.
