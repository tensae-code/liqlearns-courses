# Rwanda — the First Republic, 1962–1973 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The monarchy was abolished by a referendum in —
a) 1959  b) 1961  c) 1973
**Answer: b) 1961** — The 1961 referendum abolishes the monarchy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The First Republic's ruling party was —
a) MRND  b) PARMEHUTU  c) UNAR
**Answer: b) PARMEHUTU** — Kayibanda's PARMEHUTU rules one-party.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Tutsi quota imposed was —
a) 9%  b) 50%  c) 1%
**Answer: a) 9%** — A 9% Tutsi quota is imposed.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Kayibanda was overthrown on 5 July 1973 by —
a) Kagame  b) Habyarimana  c) Rwigema
**Answer: b) Habyarimana** — Habyarimana overthrows him.
