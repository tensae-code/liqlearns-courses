# Rwanda — the First Republic, 1962–1973

## Do it first

After the 1959 violence and the 1961 referendum abolishing the monarchy, Kayibanda's PARMEHUTU republic rules one-party, imposes a 9% Tutsi quota and drives Tutsi from schools, until Habyarimana overthrows him on 5 July 1973. Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think happened — hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — 1959 violence and the 1961 referendum

After the 1959 violence, a 1961 referendum abolishes the monarchy. The First Republic is therefore born from a vote that ends kingship — and from violence that already divides the new country.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kayibanda's PARMEHUTU republic — one party, a quota, schools closed to Tutsi

Kayibanda's PARMEHUTU republic rules one-party, imposes a 9% Tutsi quota and drives Tutsi from schools. Independence, in this telling, does not bring equal citizenship with it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Overthrown — 5 July 1973

Habyarimana overthrows Kayibanda on 5 July 1973. The First Republic ends not at an election but by a takeover, opening the Second Republic.
