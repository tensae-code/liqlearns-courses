# Zimbabwe — transport and communication through time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The story of transport in Zimbabwe begins with —
a) footpaths and runners  b) aeroplanes  c) motorways
**Answer: a) footpaths and runners** — it begins with footpaths and runners

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rail, road and air each —
a) shrank the country, speeding goods and messages  b) made travel slower  c) ended all walking
**Answer: a) shrank the country, speeding goods and messages** — each new way shrank the country

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson is told as —
