# Video script — Zimbabwe — transport and communication through time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Zimbabwe's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

It begins on foot: footpaths through the land and runners carrying messages. Then come rail, road and air, each one shrinking the country. Zimbabwe's way of moving goods and news is told as one long story of change over time.
