# Zimbabwe — transport and communication through time

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: From footpaths and runners to rail, road and air: Zimbabwe's Grade 5 lesson tells how the country learned to move goods and messages, as a story of change over time. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Development of transport and communication (Grade 5)

It begins on foot: footpaths through the land and runners carrying messages. Then come rail, road and air, each one shrinking the country. Zimbabwe's way of moving goods and news is told as one long story of change over time.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
