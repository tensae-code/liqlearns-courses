# Video script — West Africa — independence by organisation and negotiation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on West Africa's ports and capitals. Party offices light up; a newspaper press runs; a harbour falls silent as the cranes stop — a strike.
Conference rooms: African delegations on one side, colonial officials on the other, documents changing hands.
Flag by flag, the region's colours change at midnight ceremonies — no battle smoke, but crowds beyond counting.
The camera drifts south, where the map's colour is different: settler country. The next lessons' harder road begins there.
