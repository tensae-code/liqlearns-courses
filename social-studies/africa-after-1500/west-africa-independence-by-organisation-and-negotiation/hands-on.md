# Hands-on — West Africa — independence by organisation and negotiation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Instruments wall
Write the movements' instruments — party, union, press, boycott, vote — on cards. Under each, write what it could compel a colonial government to do.

## Rule and exception
Draw the continent and shade two zones: negotiated roads and the settler belt. Say the summary's sentence aloud as you shade.
