# West Africa — independence by organisation and negotiation — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** West Africa's road to independence was mainly an armed one.
**Answer: False** — mainly organisation and negotiation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Ghana's road set the pattern for the region's independences.
**Answer: True** — the first of the region's sub-Saharan independences in this telling.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Negotiated independence means power was transferred to governments the movements themselves formed.
**Answer: True** — inherit the state, in the lesson's phrase.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** West Africa had the continent's densest political organisation in the post-war decades.
**Answer: True** — parties, unions, press in strength.
