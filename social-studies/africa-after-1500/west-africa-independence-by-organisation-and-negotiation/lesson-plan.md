# Lesson plan — West Africa — independence by organisation and negotiation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** West Africa — independence by organisation and negotiation
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — the liberation unit; Grade 10 Unit 7 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** West Africa's nationalist movements won independence mainly by organisation and negotiation — the pattern the unit generalises for most of Africa: non-violent passive resistance, hardest only where settlers were many.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the colonial-experience lessons — the movements answer the rule described there. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Movements that organised a region free** — West Africa's nationalist movements and negotiated independences as the unit's regional survey.
2. **The pattern — and its settler exception** — the unit summary's generalisation: most African countries won independence through non-violent passive resistance; the road was hardest where settlers were many.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: What colonial rule did to Africa — political, economic, social. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe West Africa's road: movements, organisation, negotiated independence.
2. State the unit's general pattern and its exception.
