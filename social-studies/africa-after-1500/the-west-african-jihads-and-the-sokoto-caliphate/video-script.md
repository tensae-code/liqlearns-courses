# Video script — The West African jihads and the Sokoto Caliphate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Uthman dan Fodio, Fulani preacher and scholar, leads the first jihad in Hausa land against unfair courts and impure practice; his successor Mohammed Bello builds the Sokoto Caliphate's Quranic schools and mosques.
