# Hands-on — Zimbabwe — the Second Chimurenga and independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Build a timeline of the Second Chimurenga: Chinhoyi 1966, Lancaster House 1979, independence 18 April 1980 — one line on each.

## Task 2
Write the definition of independence in your own words, using the Grade 7 lesson's three tests: choosing leaders, controlling resources, keeping identity.
