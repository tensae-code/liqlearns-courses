# Zimbabwe — the Second Chimurenga and independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things the thirteen years of war were fought over.
**Answer: Any two of land, taxes and unequal schooling.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On what date did independence come?
**Answer: 18 April 1980 (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the three tests of independence in this lesson.
**Answer: Choosing their leaders, controlling their resources, keeping their own identity (any close wording).**
