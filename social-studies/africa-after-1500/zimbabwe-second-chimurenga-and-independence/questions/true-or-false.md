# Zimbabwe — the Second Chimurenga and independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Second Chimurenga lasted thirteen years.
**Answer: True** — 1966 to 1979 is thirteen years of war

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Independence came before the Lancaster House talks.
**Answer: False** — Lancaster House 1979 comes first; independence follows on 18 April 1980

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Self-governance is part of what independence means.
**Answer: True** — independence is re-read as self-governance
