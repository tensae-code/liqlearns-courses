# Zimbabwe — the Second Chimurenga and independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Second Chimurenga opened fire at —
a) Chinhoyi, 1966  b) Fort Salisbury, 1890  c) Lancaster House, 1979
**Answer: a) Chinhoyi, 1966** — the war opens at Chinhoyi in 1966

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** ZANLA and ZIPRA were the armies of —
a) ZANU and ZAPU  b) the British South Africa Company  c) the Pioneer Column
**Answer: a) ZANU and ZAPU** — ZANLA and ZIPRA are ZANU and ZAPU's armies

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The war ended at —
a) Lancaster House, 1979  b) the Heroes Acre  c) Great Zimbabwe
**Answer: a) Lancaster House, 1979** — it ends at Lancaster House in 1979

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Independence, in the Grade 7 definition, means a people —
a) choose their leaders, control their resources and keep their identity  b) pay no taxes ever  c) leave their country
**Answer: a) choose their leaders, control their resources and keep their identity** — the definition is choosing leaders, controlling resources, keeping identity

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Under colonial rule, the people had —
a) no say  b) full self-governance  c) their own parliament
**Answer: a) no say** — they had no say under colonial rule
