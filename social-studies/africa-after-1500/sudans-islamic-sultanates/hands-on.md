# Hands-on — Sudan's Islamic sultanates — the Funj and the Fur

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sudan's Islamic sultanates — the Funj and the Fur — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Islamic Sultanates in Sudan (1), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
