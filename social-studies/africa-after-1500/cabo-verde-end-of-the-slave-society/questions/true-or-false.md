# Cabo Verde — end of the slave society — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Slavery's abolition changed nothing in Cabo Verde.
**Answer: False** — society, culture and economy were remade without it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The remaking happened in the second half of the 1800s.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches.
