# Cabo Verde — end of the slave society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is about the end of —
a) The Portuguese language  b) The slave-owning society — slavery abolished  c) Fishing
**Answer: b) The slave-owning society — slavery abolished** — slavery is abolished; the slave society ends.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After abolition, society, culture and economy were remade in —
a) The second half of the 1800s  b) The 1400s  c) The 2000s
**Answer: a) The second half of the 1800s** — the second half of the 1800s.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The society before abolition is described by the book as —
a) A slave-owning society  b) A factory society  c) A nomad society
**Answer: a) A slave-owning society** — the slave-owning society.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Abolition meant Cabo Verdean life had to be —
a) Left unchanged  b) Remade without slavery  c) Moved to Portugal
**Answer: b) Remade without slavery** — remade without it.
