# Cabo Verde — end of the slave society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What ended, and when were its effects worked through?
**Answer: Slavery was abolished, ending the slave-owning society; in the second half of the 1800s society, culture and economy were remade without it (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three parts of life the book says were remade.
**Answer: Society, culture and economy (any close wording).**
