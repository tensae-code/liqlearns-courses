# Lesson plan — Cabo Verde — end of the slave society

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Cabo Verde — end of the slave society
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Cabo Verde, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** slavery is abolished and, in the second half of the 1800s, Cabo Verdean society, culture and economy are remade without it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: a society remade: slavery ends, and in the second half of the 1800s work, culture and daily life have to be built again without it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Slavery is abolished** — Slavery is abolished — the slave-owning society ends.
2. **Society remade without slavery** — In the second half of the 1800s, Cabo Verdean society, culture and economy are remade without slavery.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what ended — slavery, and with it the slave-owning society.
2. Name the second half of the 1800s, and the three things remade — society, culture and economy.
