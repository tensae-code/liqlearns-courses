# Eritrea — the Beni Amir federation and the Funj — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ali Abu Gassim was an agent of —
a) the Funj Kingdom of Sennar  b) Portugal  c) Egypt
**Answer: a) the Funj Kingdom of Sennar** — the Funj kingdom sent him.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The federation united about —
a) 21 tribes  b) 5 kingdoms  c) 9 provinces
**Answer: a) 21 tribes** — some 21 tribes under the Diglal.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The three castes under the Diglal were —
a) nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves  b) priests, warriors and merchants  c) elders, mothers and children
**Answer: a) nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves** — nabtab, serfs and slaves — three castes.
