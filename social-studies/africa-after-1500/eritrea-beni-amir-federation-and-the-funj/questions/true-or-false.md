# Eritrea — the Beni Amir federation and the Funj — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ali Abu Gassim's son was born after his father's death.
**Answer: True** — the book calls him a posthumous son who returned with an army.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Beni Amir federation abolished all castes.
**Answer: False** — it was ordered in three castes — nabtab, serfs and slaves.
