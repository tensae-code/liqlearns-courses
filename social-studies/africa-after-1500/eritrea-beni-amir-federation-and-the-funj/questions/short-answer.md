# Eritrea — the Beni Amir federation and the Funj — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the story's turn: how did the son succeed where the father failed?
**Answer: The father married into the Belaw line and was killed by them; his posthumous son returned with an army, defeated the Belaw and united the tribes (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What was the Diglal?
**Answer: The ruler at the head of the Beni Amir federation of some 21 tribes (any close wording).**
