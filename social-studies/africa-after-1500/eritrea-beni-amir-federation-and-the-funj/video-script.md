# Video script — Eritrea — the Beni Amir federation and the Funj

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Funj agent Ali Abu Gassim marries into the Belaw royal line and is killed by them; his posthumous son returns with an army, defeats the Belaw and unites some 21 tribes under the Diglal, with nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves in three castes.
