# Eritrea — the Beni Amir federation and the Funj

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: the Funj Kingdom of Sennar reached into Eritrea: its agent Ali Abu Gassim married into the Belaw royal line and was killed by them; his posthumous son returned with an army, defeated the Belaw and united some 21 tribes under the Diglal, with nabtab rulers, serfs and slaves in three castes. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.
