# Hands-on — Eritrea — the Beni Amir federation and the Funj

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw the federation as 21 small tents under one big tent — the Diglal's — and label the three castes.

## Task 2
Act the return: the son arrives with an army; the Belaw must choose — speak both sides in one line each.
