# Video script — The Mahdist revolution — why it rose

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Muhammad Ahmad, born 1843 on Labab Island and formed by the Sufi orders, proclaims the Mahdiyya from Aba Island; heavy taxes beaten out of people, conquest violence and favouritism make Sudan ready, and the revolt runs in two acts — revolution 1881–1885, state 1885–1898.
