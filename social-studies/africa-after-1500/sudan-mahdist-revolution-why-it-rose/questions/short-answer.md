# The Mahdist revolution — why it rose — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Why the Mahdist revolution rose and won.
**Answer: Muhammad Ahmad, born 1843 on Labab Island and formed by the Sufi orders, proclaims the Mahdiyya from Aba Island; heavy taxes beaten out of people, conquest violence and favouritism make Sudan ready, and the revolt runs in two acts — revolution 1881–1885, state 1885–1898 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Sudan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ — الصف الثالث (المرحلة المتوسطة) (History, Third Year of the Intermediate Stage = Grade 9)* (Sudan), Why the Mahdist revolution rose and won, printed p. 8.**
