# South Sudan — the Nuer and Dinka, peoples without kings

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A decentralised people of tribes and lineages whose leopard-skin religious chiefs settle disputes; cattle are wealth and prestige, raiding the Dinka is a duty blessed by women's rituals, and Kwoth is honoured through spirits, ghost marriages and marriages completed only at the third child. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
