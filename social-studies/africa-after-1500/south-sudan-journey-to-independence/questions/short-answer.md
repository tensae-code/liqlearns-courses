# South Sudan — the journey to independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Change in our country.
**Answer: Marginalised and exploited by the north, the south needs change, and the biggest change is secession — South Sudan breaks from Sudan and becomes independent on 9 July 2011 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In South Sudan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Primary Social Studies 6 Pupil's Book* (South Sudan), Change in our country, printed p. 1.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Journey to independence for South Sudan.
**Answer: 1947 Juba Conference demands separation and is rejected; 1955 Torit Mutiny opens the Anya-nya 1 war under Joseph Lagu; 1972 Addis Ababa gives semi-autonomy; 1983 Nimeiri tears it up and John Garang's SPLM/A fights to the 2005 Naivasha peace; a 99% referendum vote leads to independence in 2011 with Salva Kiir as first president (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In South Sudan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Primary Social Studies 6 Pupil's Book* (South Sudan), Journey to independence for South Sudan, printed p. 6.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Comparison between change in South Sudan and change in South Africa.
**Answer: Both countries won change through armed struggle and bloodshed, and both through a long chain of meetings and signed agreements lasting many years (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In South Sudan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Primary Social Studies 6 Pupil's Book* (South Sudan), Comparison between change in South Sudan and change in South Africa, printed p. 10.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: South Sudan in the 20th century: separation policy.
**Answer: Britain's Milner Report of 1920 proposes dividing Sudan, and the Closed District Ordinance of 1922 seals the south off — northern staff and traders excluded, southerners barred from northern work and education, Arab dress and names prohibited (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In South Sudan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Secondary History 2 Student's Book* (South Sudan), South Sudan in the 20th century: separation policy, printed p. 65.**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Retell this part in your own words: The Juba Conference of 1947.
**Answer: Southern chiefs and officials, asked their opinion for the first time under the condominium, refuse Khartoum's Legislative Assembly on day one and accept it on day two after Judge Shingeiti's persuasion — Clement Mboro's reversal shows their unpreparedness; unity is postponed, but southern nationalism is born (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** In South Sudan's book, where is this part taught?

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** Retell this part in your own words: Anya-Nya Movement (1955-1972).
**Answer: Ex-Torit soldiers attack at Pamoju with knives and sticks and capture their first firearms; Joseph Lagu joins in 1963 and gives the movement weight; Abboud expels all missionaries in 1964; factions split along ethnic lines (ALF, SSPG) until Lagu unites the South Sudan Liberation Front in 1970 and forces talks (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** In South Sudan's book, where is this part taught?

**13. (Sub lesson 7)** Retell this part in your own words: Addis-Ababa Agreement.
**Answer: The 1972 agreement makes the south one autonomous region with its own assembly and High Executive Council, promises Abyei a referendum and integrates Anya-Nya fighters, but keeps oil and economic planning in Khartoum — welcomed by a war-weary south, denounced in the north, and plagued by rivalry from its first moment (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**14. (Sub lesson 7)** In South Sudan's book, where is this part taught?

**15. (Sub lesson 8)** Retell this part in your own words: The SPLM/A struggle for independence (1983-2005).
**Answer: Battalion 105 at Bor refuses transfer north in 1983 and is attacked; 2,500 soldiers gather at Bilpham; Akuot Atem's secession manifesto is rejected by Ethiopia, so John Garang writes the manifesto for a socialist, united, secular Sudan that wins Ethiopian support; the struggle runs to the CPA, whose challenges, achievements and effects on South Sudanese pupils assess (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
