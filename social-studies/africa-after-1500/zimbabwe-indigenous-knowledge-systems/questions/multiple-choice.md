# Zimbabwe — indigenous knowledge systems — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** IKS stands for —
a) Indigenous Knowledge Systems  b) International Knowledge School  c) Imported Knowledge Stores
**Answer: a) Indigenous Knowledge Systems** — IKS is Indigenous Knowledge Systems

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The knowledge in IKS was learned —
a) without books, handed down in the community  b) only from the internet  c) in colonial schools only
**Answer: a) without books, handed down in the community** — it is knowledge learned without books

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The old ways of preserving food named in the lesson are —
a) drying, smoking and storing  b) freezing only  c) throwing food away
**Answer: a) drying, smoking and storing** — drying, smoking and storing are the old food-keeping ways

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The old methods are judged —
a) on their own merits, beside today's methods  b) as worthless because they are old  c) only by visitors
**Answer: a) on their own merits, beside today's methods** — they are set beside today's methods and judged on their merits
