# Video script — Early attempts at East African federation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Lugard tries to merge Kenya and Uganda in 1896, Johnston in 1900, Eliot in 1905 — all fail; the Baganda, fearing for their privileged position under the 1900 Agreement, oppose under Muteesa, and Tanganyika's governor opposes too.
