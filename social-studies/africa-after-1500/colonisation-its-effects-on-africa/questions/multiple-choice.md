# Colonisation — its effects on Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The book's first harm of colonial rule: it —
a) Built Africa's industries  b) Weakened Africa's economy and culture  c) United all peoples  d) Ended all conflict
**Answer: b) Weakened Africa's economy and culture** — Weakened economy and culture.

**2.** Colonial rule exposed Africa's wealth to —
a) Protection  b) Plunder  c) Equal sharing  d) Storage
**Answer: b) Plunder** — Her wealth was exposed to plunder.

**3.** The fourth harm, drawn on modern maps, is —
a) Wrongly drawn borders causing conflict between states  b) Too many ports  c) Mountain ranges  d) River changes
**Answer: a) Wrongly drawn borders causing conflict between states** — Wrongly drawn borders became a cause of conflict between states.

**4.** Colonial rule forced on Africans —
a) Their own culture only  b) Division and the acceptance of an alien culture  c) Free elections  d) Industrial wages
**Answer: b) Division and the acceptance of an alien culture** — Forced division and an alien culture.
