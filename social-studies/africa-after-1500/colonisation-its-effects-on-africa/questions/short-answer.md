# Colonisation — its effects on Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** List the book's four harms of colonial rule.
**Answer:** It weakened Africa's economy and culture; it exposed her wealth to plunder; it forced division and the acceptance of an alien culture; its wrongly drawn borders became a cause of conflict between states.

**2.** Why do wrongly drawn borders still matter after independence?
**Answer:** The lines stayed when the flags changed: borders cut through peoples and joined strangers, so states inherited quarrels drawn for them in Europe.

**3.** What two things did colonial rule force on Africans, in the book's third harm?
**Answer:** Division, and the acceptance of an alien culture.
