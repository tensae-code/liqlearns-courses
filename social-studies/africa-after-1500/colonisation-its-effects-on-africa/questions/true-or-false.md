# Colonisation — its effects on Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The borders drawn by the colonisers disappeared at independence.
**Answer: False** — The lines stayed — states inherited them.

**2.** The book judges colonial rule in four harms.
**Answer: True** — Four harms, as listed.

**3.** Colonial rule strengthened Africa's economy and culture, in the book's verdict.
**Answer: False** — It weakened both.
