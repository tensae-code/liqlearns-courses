# Hands-on — Zambia — liberation struggle sites

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the map of freedom
Draw a simple map with Kabwe, Lusaka and Choma. Mark every liberation place in this lesson on it, with its number where the lesson gives one.

## A guide's card
Choose one place and write a guide's card for it: its name, where it is, and the meeting or memory it holds, in your own words.

## Your own place
Think of a place in your own town where people gather to decide things. Write two lines on why meeting places matter to a movement — from what this lesson's places show.
