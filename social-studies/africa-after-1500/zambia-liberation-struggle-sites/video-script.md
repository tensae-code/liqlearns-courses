# Video script — Zambia — liberation struggle sites

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: a rock, a tree and ordinary township houses become the map of a nation's freedom, as Zambia's Grade 8 History Teaching Module tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A nation's freedom does not live only in books. In Zambia, it lives in places you can stand in.

At Kabwe stands the Mulungushi Rock of Authority, where the nationalist movement gathered to speak to the people. Nearby is the Mukuyu Tree, another meeting place of the struggle years.

In Lusaka's townships, the places are ordinary houses. Chilenje House No. 394 was Kenneth Kaunda's home from 1960 to 1962. Freedom House and Matero House No. 3144B held the movement's work. And at Bwacha House No. E1376, on 8 March 1958, Kaunda was elected president of ZANC.

Then there are the memorials: the Freedom Statue, and Isa ya Mwaamba at Choma. Each place holds a meeting that moved Zambia toward 1964 — a map of freedom written in rock, wood, brick and bronze.
