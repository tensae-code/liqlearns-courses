# Zambia — liberation struggle sites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mulungushi Rock of Authority is at —
a) Lusaka  b) Kabwe  c) Choma
**Answer: b) Kabwe** — with the Mukuyu Tree, it was a gathering place of the movement.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mukuyu at Kabwe is a —
a) Tree  b) House  c) Statue
**Answer: a) Tree** — an open-air meeting place of the struggle years.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Chilenje House No. 394 was —
a) A colonial office  b) Kaunda's home, 1960–62  c) A prison
**Answer: b) Kaunda's home, 1960–62** — an ordinary township house remembered by its number.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At Bwacha House No. E1376 on 8 March 1958, Kaunda was —
a) Arrested  b) Elected president of ZANC  c) Made president of Zambia
**Answer: b) Elected president of ZANC** — six years before independence in 1964.
