# Zambia — liberation struggle sites — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Isa ya Mwaamba, named among the liberation places, is at Choma.
**Answer: True** — with the Freedom Statue, it carries the struggle's memory in public.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mulungushi Rock of Authority was a colonial government building.
**Answer: False** — it is a rock at Kabwe where the nationalist movement held its gatherings.
