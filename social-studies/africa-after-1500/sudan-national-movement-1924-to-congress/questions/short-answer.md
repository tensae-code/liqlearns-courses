# Sudan's national movement — 1924 to the Graduates' Congress — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 1918–1953.
**Answer: the Sudanese Union (1920) works in secret leaflets, Ali Abd al-Latif's White Flag League leads the 1924 revolution — demonstrations in Khartoum, the cadets' stand and Abd al-Fadil al-Maz at the Blue Nile bridge — and the Graduates' Congress of 1938 turns protest into a national programme, demanding self-determination in 1942 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
