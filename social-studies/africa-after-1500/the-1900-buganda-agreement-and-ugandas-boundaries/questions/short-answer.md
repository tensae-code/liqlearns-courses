# The 1900 Buganda Agreement and Uganda's boundaries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: How Uganda Became a Nation.
**Answer: the 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: How Uganda became a nation.
**Answer: before the treaties, the British form the Imperial British East Africa Company; then agreements are signed with the kings of Buganda, Ankole, Bunyoro and Toro, one kingdom at a time (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: The agreements.
**Answer: the 1900 Buganda Agreement is signed by Johnston and Apollo Kaggwa while Kabaka Mwanga sits exiled in the Seychelles; Bunyoro's follows in 1933, Ankole's dates to 1901 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: Methods of colonisation.
**Answer: where it is peaceful, the treaty does the work — Buganda 1900, Toro 1900, Ankole 1901 — signed with promises of protection from Bunyoro (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: Johnston signs with the three regents of the child Kabaka Daudi Chwa II — Kagwa, Mugwanya, Kisingiri; Buganda expects British guns against Bunyoro and Egypt, and the Kabaka keeps his throne as "His Highness" — under close British supervision (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: the agreement exists in two versions, English and Luganda — and where they conflict, the English one rules; it is also signed to settle Buganda's boundary quarrel with Bunyoro after Kabalega's rebellion (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
