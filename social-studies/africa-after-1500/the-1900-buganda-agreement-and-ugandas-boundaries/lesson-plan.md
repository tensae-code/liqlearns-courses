## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **How Uganda Became a Nation** — the 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910
2. **How Uganda became a nation** — before the treaties, the British form the Imperial British East Africa Company; then agreements are signed with the kings of Buganda, Ankole, Bunyoro and Toro, one kingdom at a time
3. **The agreements** — the 1900 Buganda Agreement is signed by Johnston and Apollo Kaggwa while Kabaka Mwanga sits exiled in the Seychelles; Bunyoro's follows in 1933, Ankole's dates to 1901
4. **Methods of colonisation** — where it is peaceful, the treaty does the work — Buganda 1900, Toro 1900, Ankole 1901 — signed with promises of protection from Bunyoro
5. **The 1900 Buganda Agreement, case study of the peaceful method** — Johnston signs with the three regents of the child Kabaka Daudi Chwa II — Kagwa, Mugwanya, Kisingiri; Buganda expects British guns against Bunyoro and Egypt, and the Kabaka keeps his throne as "His Highness" — under close British supervision
6. **The Buganda Agreement** — the agreement exists in two versions, English and Luganda — and where they conflict, the English one rules; it is also signed to settle Buganda's boundary quarrel with Bunyoro after Kabalega's rebellion
