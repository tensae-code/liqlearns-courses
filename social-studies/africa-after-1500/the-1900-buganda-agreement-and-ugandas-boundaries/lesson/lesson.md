# The 1900 Buganda Agreement and Uganda's boundaries

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 4 — Methods of colonisation

## Sub lesson 5 — The 1900 Buganda Agreement, case study of the peaceful method

Johnston signs with the three regents of the child Kabaka Daudi Chwa II — Kagwa, Mugwanya, Kisingiri; Buganda expects British guns against Bunyoro and Egypt, and the Kabaka keeps his throne as "His Highness" — under close British supervision
