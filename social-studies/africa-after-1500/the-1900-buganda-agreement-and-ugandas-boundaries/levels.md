# The 1900 Buganda Agreement and Uganda's boundaries — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — How Uganda Became a Nation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the 1900 Buganda Agreement settles land, governance and gun and hut taxes; the 1901 Ankole Agreement enlarges Ankole, and Uganda's borders are trimmed piece by piece — the north-east to Kenya in 1914, the Lake Albert shore to Belgian Congo in 1910

## Sub lesson 2 — How Uganda became a nation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — before the treaties, the British form the Imperial British East Africa Company; then agreements are signed with the kings of Buganda, Ankole, Bunyoro and Toro, one kingdom at a time

## Sub lesson 3 — The agreements
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the 1900 Buganda Agreement is signed by Johnston and Apollo Kaggwa while Kabaka Mwanga sits exiled in the Seychelles; Bunyoro's follows in 1933, Ankole's dates to 1901

## Sub lesson 4 — Methods of colonisation
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — where it is peaceful, the treaty does the work — Buganda 1900, Toro 1900, Ankole 1901 — signed with promises of protection from Bunyoro

## Sub lesson 5 — The 1900 Buganda Agreement, case study of the peaceful method
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Johnston signs with the three regents of the child Kabaka Daudi Chwa II — Kagwa, Mugwanya, Kisingiri; Buganda expects British guns against Bunyoro and Egypt, and the Kabaka keeps his throne as "His Highness" — under close British supervision

## Sub lesson 6 — The Buganda Agreement
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the agreement exists in two versions, English and Luganda — and where they conflict, the English one rules; it is also signed to settle Buganda's boundary quarrel with Bunyoro after Kabalega's rebellion
