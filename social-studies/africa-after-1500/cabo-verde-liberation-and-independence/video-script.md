# Video script — Cabo Verde — liberation and independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Cabo Verde book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After remote roots of resistance, the PAIGC under Amílcar Cabral first tries negotiation with Portugal, then, refused, launches the armed struggle in Guinea.

Portugal's Carnation Revolution of 25 April 1974 ends the war, and on 5 July 1975 Cabo Verde's independence is proclaimed, in unity with Guinea-Bissau.

Hold that story: negotiation refused, struggle, then freedom: Cabral's PAIGC talks first, fights when refused, and independence comes on 5 July 1975.
