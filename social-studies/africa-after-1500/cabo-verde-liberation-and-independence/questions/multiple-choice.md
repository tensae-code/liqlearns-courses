# Cabo Verde — liberation and independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The liberation struggle was led by —
a) The PAIGC under Amílcar Cabral  b) A Portuguese party  c) The United Nations army
**Answer: a) The PAIGC under Amílcar Cabral** — the PAIGC under Cabral.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cabral's PAIGC first tried —
a) Immediate war  b) Negotiation with Portugal  c) Leaving the islands
**Answer: b) Negotiation with Portugal** — negotiation first; refused, it launched the armed struggle.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The armed struggle was launched in —
a) Portugal  b) Guinea  c) Brazil
**Answer: b) Guinea** — the armed struggle in Guinea.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cabo Verde's independence was proclaimed on —
a) 25 April 1974  b) 5 July 1975  c) 1 January 2000
**Answer: b) 5 July 1975** — 5 July 1975; 25 April 1974 was Portugal's Carnation Revolution.
