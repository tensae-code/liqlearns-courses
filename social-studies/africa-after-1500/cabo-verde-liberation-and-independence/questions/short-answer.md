# Cabo Verde — liberation and independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the road to independence in order.
**Answer: Remote roots of resistance; the PAIGC under Amílcar Cabral tries negotiation with Portugal; refused, it launches the armed struggle in Guinea; Portugal's Carnation Revolution of 25 April 1974 ends the war; independence is proclaimed on 5 July 1975, in unity with Guinea-Bissau (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happened on 25 April 1974?
**Answer: Portugal's Carnation Revolution, which ended the war (any close wording).**
