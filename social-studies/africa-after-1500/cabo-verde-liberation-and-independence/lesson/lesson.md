# Cabo Verde — liberation and independence

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of islands with no people on them at all — then people arriving by ship, building towns, rising in revolt, and at last proclaiming their own country. Which part of that long story do you think this lesson tells? Hold your guess; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The National Liberation Struggle

After remote roots of resistance, the PAIGC under Amílcar Cabral first tries negotiation with Portugal, then, refused, launches the armed struggle in Guinea.

[Picture slot — a picture of a flag being raised on 5 July 1975 while a crowd watches — the student says whose struggle brought this day and in unity with which country it was proclaimed.]


## Sub lesson 2 — The Proclamation of Independence

Portugal's Carnation Revolution of 25 April 1974 ends the war, and on 5 July 1975 Cabo Verde's independence is proclaimed, in unity with Guinea-Bissau.
