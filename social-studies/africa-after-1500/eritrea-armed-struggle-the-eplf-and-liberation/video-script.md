# Video script — Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The ELF begins the armed struggle under Hamid Idris Awate in 1961, the EPLF carries it through thirty years of war, and Eritrea is liberated on 24 May 1991.

Hamid Idris Awate opens the armed struggle in 1961; the fronts survive civil war, the 1977–78 offensives, the strategic retreat and the Red Star campaign, win at Afabet in 1988 and Massawa in 1990, and liberate Asmara on 24 May 1991.

Three splinter groups unite as the EPLF by 1973, survive a civil war with the ELF, adopt the National Democratic Program, take the big towns in 1977, endure the Soviet-backed strategic retreat to Sahil, and fight through to liberation.
