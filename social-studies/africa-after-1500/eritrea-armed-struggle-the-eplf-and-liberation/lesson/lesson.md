# Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: the ELF began the armed struggle under Hamid Idris Awate in 1961; the EPLF carried it through thirty years of war — offensives, a strategic retreat, Afabet and Massawa — until Eritrea was liberated on 24 May 1991. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Armed Struggle 1961–1991

Hamid Idris Awate opens the armed struggle in 1961; the fronts survive civil war, the 1977–78 offensives, the strategic retreat and the Red Star campaign, win at Afabet in 1988 and Massawa in 1990, and liberate Asmara on 24 May 1991.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Eritrean People's Liberation Front

Three splinter groups unite as the EPLF by 1973, survive a civil war with the ELF, adopt the National Democratic Program, take the big towns in 1977, endure the Soviet-backed strategic retreat to Sahil, and fight through to liberation.
