# Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The armed struggle opened in 1961 under —
a) Hamid Idris Awate  b) Tedla Bairu  c) Bahri Negassi Yeshaq
**Answer: a) Hamid Idris Awate** — Hamid Idris Awate opened it in 1961.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The EPLF's great victories of the last years were —
a) Dogali and Wuchale  b) Afabet 1988 and Massawa 1990  c) Gundet and Gura
**Answer: b) Afabet 1988 and Massawa 1990** — Afabet in 1988 and Massawa in 1990, then Asmara.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Asmara was liberated on —
a) 24 May 1991  b) 14 November 1962  c) 23 April 1993
**Answer: a) 24 May 1991** — 24 May 1991 — liberation day.
