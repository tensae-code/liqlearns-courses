# Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The struggle lasted thirty years, from 1961 to 1991.
**Answer: True** — 1961–1991 is the span all three books give.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The EPLF and ELF never fought each other.
**Answer: False** — the books tell of a civil war between the fronts, which both survived.
