# Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What was the strategic retreat, as the books tell it?
**Answer: After taking the big towns in 1977, the EPLF fell back before a Soviet-backed offensive to Sahil and held on there (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the last three beats of the struggle in order.
**Answer: Afabet 1988, Massawa 1990, and the liberation of Asmara on 24 May 1991 (any close wording).**
