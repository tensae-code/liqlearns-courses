# Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The National Struggle
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the ELF begins the armed struggle under Hamid Idris Awate in 1961, the EPLF carries it through thirty years of war, and Eritrea is liberated on 24 May 1991.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Armed Struggle 1961–1991
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Hamid Idris Awate opens the armed struggle in 1961; the fronts survive civil war, the 1977–78 offensives, the strategic retreat and the Red Star campaign, win at Afabet in 1988 and Massawa in 1990, and liberate Asmara on 24 May 1991.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Eritrean People's Liberation Front
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — three splinter groups unite as the EPLF by 1973, survive a civil war with the ELF, adopt the National Democratic Program, take the big towns in 1977, endure the Soviet-backed strategic retreat to Sahil, and fight through to liberation.
