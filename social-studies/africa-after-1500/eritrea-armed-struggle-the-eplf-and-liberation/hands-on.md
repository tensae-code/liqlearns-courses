# Hands-on — Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw the thirty-year line: 1961 first shot, 1973 EPLF united, 1977 towns taken, retreat to Sahil, 1988 Afabet, 1990 Massawa, 24 May 1991 liberation — one drawing per beat.

## Task 2
Tell the struggle as a relay race: each runner (ELF, then EPLF) carries the same baton — act who passes it, and where.
