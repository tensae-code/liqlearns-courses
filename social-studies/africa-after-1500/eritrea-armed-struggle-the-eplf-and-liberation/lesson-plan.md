# Lesson plan — Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — the armed struggle: the EPLF and liberation
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The ELF began the armed struggle under Hamid Idris Awate in 1961; the EPLF carried it through thirty years of war — offensives, a strategic retreat, Afabet and Massawa — until Eritrea was liberated on 24 May 1991.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The National Struggle** — the ELF begins the armed struggle under Hamid Idris Awate in 1961, the EPLF carries it through thirty years of war, and Eritrea is liberated on 24 May 1991 (The National Struggle)
2. **The Armed Struggle 1961–1991** — Hamid Idris Awate opens the armed struggle in 1961; the fronts survive civil war, the 1977–78 offensives, the strategic retreat and the Red Star campaign, win at Afabet in 1988 and Massawa in 1990, and liberate Asmara on 24 May 1991 (The Armed Struggle 1961–1991)
3. **The Eritrean People's Liberation Front** — three splinter groups unite as the EPLF by 1973, survive a civil war with the ELF, adopt the National Democratic Program, take the big towns in 1977, endure the Soviet-backed strategic retreat to Sahil, and fight through to liberation (The Eritrean People's Liberation Front)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
