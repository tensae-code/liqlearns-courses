# Egypt — from the protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution — short answer

**Answer: The 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul, the declaration of 28 February 1922 and the constitutions of 1923 and 1930, with Egyptian women marching in 1919; the 1936 treaty turns occupation into alliance; British tanks surround Abdeen Palace on 4 February 1942 to impose a ministry; Nahhas abrogates the treaty in 1951, the Ismailia police battle of 25 January 1952 and the Cairo Fire follow — and the monarchy falls to the July Revolution.
