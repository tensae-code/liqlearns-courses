# Egypt — from the protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul, the declaration of 28 February 1922 and the constitutions of 1923 and 1930, with Egyptian women marching in 1919; the 1936 treaty turns occupation into alliance; British tanks surround Abdeen Palace on 4 February 1942 to impose a ministry; Nahhas abrogates the treaty in 1951, the Ismailia police battle of 25 January 1952 and the Cairo Fire follow — and the monarchy falls to the July Revolution.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Egypt from the British protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Prep9 full-year "Geography of the World and Modern & Contemporary History of Egypt"* is about Chapter 4: Egypt after the First World War.
**Answer: False** — it is about Egypt from the British protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From the 1919 revolution through the treaty age to the eve of 1952 — Egypt's interwar struggle for full independence.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 4: Egypt after the First World War

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sec12 "History of Egypt and the Arabs, Modern and Contemporary"* is about Egypt from the British protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution.
