# Egypt — from the protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: From the 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul through the 1936 treaty and the tanks at Abdeen in 1942, Egypt's struggle for full independence ends with the monarchy's fall to the July 1952 Revolution. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt from the British protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution

The 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul, the declaration of 28 February 1922 and the constitutions of 1923 and 1930, with Egyptian women marching in 1919; the 1936 treaty turns occupation into alliance; British tanks surround Abdeen Palace on 4 February 1942 to impose a ministry; Nahhas abrogates the treaty in 1951, the Ismailia police battle of 25 January 1952 and the Cairo Fire follow — and the monarchy falls to the July Revolution.
