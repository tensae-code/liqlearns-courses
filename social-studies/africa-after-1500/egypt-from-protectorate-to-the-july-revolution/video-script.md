# Video script — Egypt — from the protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From the 1919 revolution and Saad Zaghloul through the 1936 treaty and the tanks at Abdeen in 1942, Egypt's struggle for full independence ends with the monarchy's fall to the July 1952 Revolution.
