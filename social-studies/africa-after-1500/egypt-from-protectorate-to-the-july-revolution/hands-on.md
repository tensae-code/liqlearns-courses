# Hands-on — Egypt — from the protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Egypt — from the protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Egypt from the British protectorate to the July 1952 Revolution, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
