# Egypt's flag — from ancient standards to today

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Egyptian kings once carried standards into battle to mark their districts; in modern times the flag changes shape with each great political turn until it settles as today's tricolour with the Eagle of Saladin. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 3 Lesson 1: the flag of our homeland Egypt

Ancient Egyptian kings carry standards into battle to mark their districts, and in modern times Egypt's flag changes shape with each great political turn until it settles as today's tricolour with the Eagle of Saladin.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Unit 3 Lesson 1: the flag of our homeland Egypt. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
