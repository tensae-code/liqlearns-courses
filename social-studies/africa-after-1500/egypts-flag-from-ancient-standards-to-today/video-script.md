# Video script — Egypt's flag — from ancient standards to today

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Egyptian kings once carried standards into battle to mark their districts; in modern times the flag changes shape with each great political turn until it settles as today's tricolour with the Eagle of Saladin.
