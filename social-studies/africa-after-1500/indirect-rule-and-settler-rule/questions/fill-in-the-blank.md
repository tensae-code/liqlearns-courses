# Indirect rule and settler rule — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Governing through existing chiefs is called ____ rule.
**Answer: indirect**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where settlers held land and power directly, the system was ____ rule.
**Answer: settler**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The officer supervising a chief was the ____ officer.
**Answer: district**
