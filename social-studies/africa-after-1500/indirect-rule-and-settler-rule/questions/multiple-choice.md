# Indirect rule and settler rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indirect rule governed through —
a) European officials only  b) Existing African chiefs and institutions  c) Chartered companies  d) Settler parliaments
**Answer: b) Existing African chiefs and institutions** — Britain governed through existing chiefs and institutions.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Indirect rule is associated above all with —
a) France  b) Britain  c) Portugal  d) Belgium
**Answer: b) Britain** — Indirect rule is the British system, contrasted with French direct rule.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Above the chief in indirect rule stood —
a) No one  b) The British district officer — the chief ruled on the coloniser's licence  c) The company clerk  d) An elected assembly
**Answer: b) The British district officer — the chief ruled on the coloniser's licence** — The district officer supervised; the chief's authority ran on licence.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Under settler rule, land and power were held directly by —
a) African chiefs  b) White settler minorities  c) Absent companies  d) Missionaries
**Answer: b) White settler minorities** — Settler minorities held land and power directly.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book sets pupils to sketch the colonial map in order to —
a) Locate the white settlement areas  b) Plan railways  c) Find gold  d) Draw mission stations
**Answer: a) Locate the white settlement areas** — Locating the white settlement areas is the set task.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Settler geography explains where —
a) Trade was richest  b) Later liberation would be hardest  c) Rain fell most  d) Languages mixed
**Answer: b) Later liberation would be hardest** — Where settlers held the land, liberation was later fought hardest.
