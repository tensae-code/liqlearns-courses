# Indirect rule and settler rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contrast indirect rule with direct rule in two sentences.
**Answer:** Direct rule governed through European officials and set African authorities aside; indirect rule governed through existing African chiefs and institutions, supervised by British officers and held on the coloniser's licence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the book make pupils locate the white settlement areas on the map?
**Answer:** Because settler rule's geography — where minorities held land and power directly — explains where later liberation struggles would be hardest and longest.
