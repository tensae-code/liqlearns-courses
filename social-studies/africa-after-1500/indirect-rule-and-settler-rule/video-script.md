# Video script — Indirect rule and settler rule


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens again on the partitioned continent — same colours, different machinery.

Zoom to a village court. A chief judges, as his fathers did.

Now pan up: behind his chair stands a British officer, arms folded. The chief rules — on licence.

Fly south. Great farms stretch to the horizon, fenced and guarded. Settler rule: the land itself is held.

Mark those farms on your map in red. Where the red lies thickest, freedom will cost the most.

Two systems, one lesson: who holds the land holds the future.
