# Hands-on — Indirect rule and settler rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sketch the settler map
Draw a rough Africa and shade the white-settlement areas as the book directs — the south and east highlands belt. Under the map write: 'Liberation will be hardest here.' Keep the map for Unit 7.

## Two chairs
Set two chairs: a chief's stool and an officer's chair behind it. Sit in each and speak one order as that ruler. Which orders could the chief refuse? That answer is indirect rule.
