# Video script — Why some Africans collaborated

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Not every African resisted: some peoples, weakened by disease and conflict or hoping for goods, protection or missionary favour, chose to work with the Europeans — Rwanda's P5 book names the Maasai and the Banyankole.

The Maasai and the Banyankole, weakened by disease, rinderpest and conflict, choose to work with the Europeans. Collaboration, in this book, often begins from weakness, not from agreement.

Others hope for goods and protection. Working with the newcomers looks, from inside a hard moment, like a way to gain things and to be kept safe.

Others again seek missionary favour. The missions hold schools, medicine and influence, and standing well with them is one more reason a community works with the Europeans.
