# Why some Africans collaborated — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three reasons the book gives for collaboration.
**Answer: Weakness after disease, rinderpest and conflict; hope of goods; hope of protection; hope of missionary favour (any three, close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is 'they agreed with colonisation' a poor explanation, on this book's evidence?
**Answer: Because the reasons given are weakness and hopes for goods, protection or favour in a hard moment — not agreement with being ruled (any close wording).**
