# Why some Africans collaborated — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every African community resisted colonisation in the same way.
**Answer: False** — This lesson is about those who collaborated, and why.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Collaboration could begin from weakness after disease and conflict.
**Answer: True** — That is the book's first reason: peoples weakened by disease, rinderpest and conflict.
