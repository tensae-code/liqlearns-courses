# Why some Africans collaborated — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two peoples Rwanda's book names as collaborating are —
a) Maasai and Banyankole  b) Zulu and Ashante  c) Kongo and Mali
**Answer: a) Maasai and Banyankole** — The Maasai and Banyankole are named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Maasai and Banyankole had been weakened by —
a) Gold and trade  b) Disease, rinderpest and conflict  c) Too much rain
**Answer: b) Disease, rinderpest and conflict** — Disease, rinderpest and conflict weakened them.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Some collaborated hoping for —
a) Goods and protection  b) Exile  c) Higher taxes
**Answer: a) Goods and protection** — Goods and protection are named hopes.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Missionary favour meant standing well with —
a) The missions, with their schools and influence  b) The slave traders  c) The Berlin Conference
**Answer: a) The missions, with their schools and influence** — Missions bring schools, medicine and influence.
