# Colonisation — direct and indirect rule — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain indirect rule and why Britain used it.
**Answer:** Ruling through Africa's traditional rulers so it looked as if they still led their people; Lugard's system, first in Northern Nigeria, used because it cut cost and the shortage of trained military and civil manpower.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Explain direct rule as the book presents it.
**Answer:** A strongly centralised French administration: the colonies' good kept by teaching French, ruling by French law and granting political and human rights — extending French citizens' rights to the colonial peoples.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In which two decades did the colonisers mainly build these administrations?
**Answer:** The first two decades of the 1900s.
