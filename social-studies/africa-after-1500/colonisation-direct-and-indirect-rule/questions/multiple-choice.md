# Colonisation — direct and indirect rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indirect rule meant ruling —
a) Directly from Paris  b) Through Africa's traditional rulers  c) Through elected parliaments  d) Through armies only
**Answer: b) Through Africa's traditional rulers** — Through traditional rulers, so it looked as if they still led.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The architect of indirect rule was —
a) Albert Sarraut  b) Frederick Lugard  c) Lord Kitchener  d) Otto von Bismarck
**Answer: b) Frederick Lugard** — Frederick Lugard.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Indirect rule was first applied in —
a) Senegal  b) Northern Nigeria  c) Algeria  d) Tanganyika
**Answer: b) Northern Nigeria** — Northern Nigeria.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Direct rule was the system of —
a) Britain  b) France  c) Germany  d) Belgium
**Answer: b) France** — Direct rule was French.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** France held the colonies' good required —
a) Chiefs left alone  b) Teaching French, ruling by French law, granting rights  c) Immediate independence  d) No schools
**Answer: b) Teaching French, ruling by French law, granting rights** — French language, French law, citizens' rights — as the book presents it.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Chiefs under indirect rule kept power and freedom —
a) Forever  b) Only while loyal  c) Never at all  d) By election
**Answer: b) Only while loyal** — Only while loyal.
