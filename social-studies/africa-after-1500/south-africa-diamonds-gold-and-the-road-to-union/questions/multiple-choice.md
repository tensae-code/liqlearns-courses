# South Africa — diamonds, gold and the road to union — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Diamonds were found at —
a) Kimberley  b) The Witwatersrand  c) Natal
**Answer: a) Kimberley** — Kimberley diamonds.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gold was found at —
a) Kimberley  b) The Witwatersrand  c) The Cape
**Answer: b) The Witwatersrand** — Witwatersrand gold.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In what year did the Boers surrender independence?
a) 1835  b) 1880  c) 1902
**Answer: c) 1902** — surrender in 1902.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After 1902, South Africa —
a) United under Britain  b) Became fully independent  c) Split into more republics
**Answer: a) United under Britain** — South Africa unites under Britain.
