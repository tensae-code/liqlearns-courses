# South Africa — diamonds, gold and the road to union — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kimberley is remembered for diamonds and the Witwatersrand for gold.
**Answer: True** — the book pairs them that way.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Boers kept their independence after the Anglo-Boer War.
**Answer: False** — they surrender independence in 1902.
