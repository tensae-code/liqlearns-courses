# South Africa — diamonds, gold and the road to union

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Kimberley diamonds and Witwatersrand gold pull Gujarati traders and Gandhi into South Africa and bring two Boer wars — until the Boers surrender independence in 1902 and South Africa unites under Britain. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Diamonds and gold transform South Africa

Kimberley diamonds and Witwatersrand gold pull in Gujarati traders and Gandhi. Mineral wealth transforms the country the trekkers founded.

In your mind's eye: diamond diggings at Kimberley and gold mines on the Witwatersrand, with a road running on to a united South Africa in 1902. Name each mineral, its place and the road's end.

## Sub lesson 2 — Two Boer wars — surrender and union

Gold and diamonds bring two Boer wars. The Boers surrender independence in 1902, and South Africa unites under Britain.
