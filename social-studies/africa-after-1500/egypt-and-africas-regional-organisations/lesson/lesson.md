# Egypt and Africa's regional organisations

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Egypt stands among the founders of the Organisation of African Unity in 1963 and of the African Union of 2002, and works through Africa's regional blocs for the continent's peace and development. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt and the regional organisations in Africa

Egypt stands among the founders of the Organisation of African Unity in 1963 and of its successor, the African Union of 2002 seated in Addis Ababa, and works through Africa's regional blocs, COMESA among them, for the continent's peace, security and development.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Egypt and the regional organisations in Africa. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
