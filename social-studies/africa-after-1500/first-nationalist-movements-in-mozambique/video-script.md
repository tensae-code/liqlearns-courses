# Video script — First nationalist movements in Mozambique

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Africans resist colonisation in defence of sovereignty; cultural associations become the seedbed of Mozambique's first nationalist manifestations.
Out of the colonial order's own contradictions, the first nationalist formulations appear in Mozambique — the idea of the nation takes shape.
