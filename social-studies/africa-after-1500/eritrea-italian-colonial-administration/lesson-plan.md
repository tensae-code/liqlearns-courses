# Lesson plan — Eritrea — Italian colonial administration

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — Italian colonial administration
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Italy ran Eritrea for the coloniser: commissariati and municipalities, separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, forced labour on roads and the great Massawa–Asmara cableway — and from 1937 Racial Laws that barred Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and schooling beyond grade 4.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Life under Italy** — separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, Fascist propaganda posters, forced labour on roads and the great Massawa–Asmara cableway — a colony run for the coloniser (Life under Italy)
2. **Italian colonial administration and the Racial Laws** — commissariati and municipalities administer a colony whose aims shift from settler farms to markets to a springboard against Ethiopia, and the 1937 Racial Laws bar Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and schooling beyond grade 4 (Italian colonial administration and the Racial Laws)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends

2. Answer, in their own words: what were the commissariati?
