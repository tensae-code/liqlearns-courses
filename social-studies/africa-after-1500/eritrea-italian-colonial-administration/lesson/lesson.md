# Eritrea — Italian colonial administration

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: italy ran Eritrea for the coloniser: commissariati and municipalities, separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, forced labour on roads and the great Massawa–Asmara cableway — and from 1937 Racial Laws that barred Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and schooling beyond grade 4. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

Separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, Fascist propaganda posters, forced labour on roads and the great Massawa–Asmara cableway — a colony run for the coloniser.

## Sub lesson 2 — Italian colonial administration and the Racial Laws

Commissariati and municipalities administer a colony whose aims shift from settler farms to markets to a springboard against Ethiopia, and the 1937 Racial Laws bar Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and schooling beyond grade 4.
