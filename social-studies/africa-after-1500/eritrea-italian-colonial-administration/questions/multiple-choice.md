# Eritrea — Italian colonial administration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The great engineering work built with forced labour was —
a) the Massawa–Asmara cableway  b) the Suez Canal  c) the Dahlak lighthouse
**Answer: a) the Massawa–Asmara cableway** — the Massawa–Asmara cableway.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Racial Laws came in —
a) 1890  b) 1937  c) 1952
**Answer: b) 1937** — 1937 — barring Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and higher schooling.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the Racial Laws, Eritrean schooling was capped at —
a) grade 4  b) grade 8  c) university
**Answer: a) grade 4** — schooling beyond grade 4 was barred.
