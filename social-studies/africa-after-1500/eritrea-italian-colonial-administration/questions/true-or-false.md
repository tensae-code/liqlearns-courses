# Eritrea — Italian colonial administration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eritrean and Italian children were taught in separate schools.
**Answer: True** — both books tell of separate schools.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Italy's aims in Eritrea never changed.
**Answer: False** — they shifted — settler farms, then markets, then a springboard against Ethiopia.
