# Eritrea — Italian colonial administration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two ways the colony was 'run for the coloniser'.
**Answer: Any two of: separate schools, forced labour on roads and the cableway, propaganda, the 1937 Racial Laws capping schooling at grade 4 (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What were the commissariati?
**Answer: The Italian administrative districts that, with the municipalities, ran the colony (any close wording).**
