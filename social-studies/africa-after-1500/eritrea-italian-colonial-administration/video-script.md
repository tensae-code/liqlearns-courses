# Video script — Eritrea — Italian colonial administration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Separate schools for Eritrean and Italian children, Fascist propaganda posters, forced labour on roads and the great Massawa–Asmara cableway — a colony run for the coloniser.

Commissariati and municipalities administer a colony whose aims shift from settler farms to markets to a springboard against Ethiopia, and the 1937 Racial Laws bar Eritreans from Italian streets, bars and schooling beyond grade 4.
