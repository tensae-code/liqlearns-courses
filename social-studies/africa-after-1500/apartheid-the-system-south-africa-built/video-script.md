# Video script — Apartheid — the system South Africa built

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a South African city. Signs divide the frame: benches, counters, platforms, each with its permitted colour of person.
A hand presents a pass book at a checkpoint. The queue behind it stretches out of the frame.
The camera pulls back to the continent: conference halls in Bandung and Addis Ababa, resolutions naming one word — apartheid.
Arrows from the frontline states converge on the map's south. The system stands drawn, entire — and entirely surrounded.
