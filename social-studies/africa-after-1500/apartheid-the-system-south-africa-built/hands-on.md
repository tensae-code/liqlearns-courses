# Hands-on — Apartheid — the system South Africa built

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A day under the pass laws
Write a day's itinerary for a black worker in apartheid South Africa — home, travel, work, shopping, return — and mark every point where a pass or a sign controls the day. Read it aloud without comment; the itinerary is the comment.

## The geometry of the unit
Draw the unit's map: the frontline states as arrows, all converging on one state at the bottom. Label the state with the system it built.
