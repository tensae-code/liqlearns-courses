# Lesson plan — Apartheid — the system South Africa built

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Apartheid — the system South Africa built
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — the liberation unit; Grade 10 Unit 7 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** apartheid was South Africa's system of institutionalised racial rule — separation enforced by law over every part of life — the internal regime at which the region's liberation struggles, and the OAU's liberation mission, were ultimately aimed.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Southern Africa — liberation at gunpoint; apartheid is the system that struggle confronted inside South Africa itself. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Rule by race, written into law** — the system's character: institutionalised racial separation governing where people live, move, work and learn, enforced by the pass and the police.
2. **Why it stands in the liberation unit** — the region's struggles and the OAU's liberation mission were ultimately aimed at this internal regime; apartheid as the unit's target.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Southern Africa — liberation at gunpoint, 1960s–1980. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define apartheid as institutionalised racial rule.
2. Say how it ordered daily life — passes, separation, reserved land and labour.
3. State why this lesson stands inside the liberation unit.
