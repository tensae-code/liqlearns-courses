# Apartheid — the system South Africa built — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Apartheid was only a matter of private prejudice, not law.
**Answer: False** — it was institutionalised — enforced by the state.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** Apartheid classified rights and places by race.
**Answer: True** — residence, movement, work, schooling, the vote.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** Apartheid answered questions of residence, work, schooling and the vote from race classification first.
**Answer: True** — the law answered by race before anything else.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** Apartheid was condemned at Bandung as early as 1955.
**Answer: True** — a generation before its fall.
