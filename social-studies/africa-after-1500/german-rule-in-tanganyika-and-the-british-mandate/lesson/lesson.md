# German rule in Tanganyika and the British mandate

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: German rule is hated for cruelty and forced plantation labour; after the war the British expel German settlers, replace Jumbes and Akidas with traditional chiefs, and rename the country Tanganyika. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
