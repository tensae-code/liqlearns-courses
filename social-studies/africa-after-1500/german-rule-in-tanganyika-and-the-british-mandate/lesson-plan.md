# Lesson plan — German rule in Tanganyika and the British mandate

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** German rule in Tanganyika and the British mandate
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Uganda, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** German rule is hated for cruelty and forced plantation labour; after the war the British expel German settlers, replace Jumbes and Akidas with traditional chiefs, and rename the country Tanganyika
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tanganyika changes hands** — German rule is hated for cruelty and forced plantation labour; after the war the British expel German settlers, replace Jumbes and Akidas with traditional chiefs, and rename the country Tanganyika
2. **Colonial Administration in Tanganyika (Direct Rule)** — the Germans rule directly — by force, an 1891 railway to the coast, and missionaries whose "beautiful" preaching softens hearts — because, having tasted Tanganyikan resistance, they dare not rule indirectly

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
