# Hands-on — Zambia — secondary resistance and the road to independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Widening circles
Draw four widening circles. Label them, from the middle out: welfare associations; Watchtower and the Copperbelt strikes; the Congress and the ANC; Cha Cha Cha. Write one date inside each circle from the lesson.

## A timeline to 1964
Draw a line from 1920 to 1964. Mark 1935, 1940, 1948, 1951, 1953 and 1961, and write beside each mark what happened.

## The Federation fight
Write, in your own words, a short speech a member of the 1952 London delegation might have given against the 1953 Federation. Keep it to what the lesson teaches: Africans had not consented to be joined into it.
