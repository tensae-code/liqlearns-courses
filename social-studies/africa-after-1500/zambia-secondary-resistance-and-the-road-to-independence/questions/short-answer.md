# Zambia — secondary resistance and the road to independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How was secondary resistance different from primary resistance?
**Answer: It was organised and nationwide — welfare associations, movements, strikes and parties aiming at the colonial system itself, not single local refusals (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Africans do against the 1953 Federation?
**Answer: A delegation went to London in 1952 to oppose it, and at home people boycotted elections and burned their passes (any close wording).**
