# Zambia — secondary resistance and the road to independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Zambia won independence in 1964.
**Answer: True** — after UNIP's Cha Cha Cha campaign of 1961, the colonial government conceded.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The welfare associations began in the 1960s.
**Answer: False** — they grew from the 1920s, before the strikes and parties.
