# Zambia — secondary resistance and the road to independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Watchtower movement was banned in —
a) 1920  b) 1935  c) 1953
**Answer: b) 1935** — the same year as the first Copperbelt strike in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Copperbelt strikes in this lesson happened in —
a) 1935 and 1940  b) 1948 and 1951  c) 1961 and 1964
**Answer: a) 1935 and 1940** — the miners' strikes shook the whole colony.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Northern Rhodesia African Congress of 1948 became, in 1951 —
a) UNIP  b) The Federation  c) The ANC
**Answer: c) The ANC** — the party line continues to UNIP and Cha Cha Cha.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** UNIP's Cha Cha Cha campaign of 1961 helped push the country to —
a) The 1953 Federation  b) Independence in 1964  c) The 1935 strike
**Answer: b) Independence in 1964** — Northern Rhodesia became Zambia.
