# Colonisation — the road to the Scramble — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** European colonisation of Africa was opened by —
a) The French  b) The Portuguese, with 15th-century coastal voyages  c) The Germans  d) The Belgians
**Answer: b) The Portuguese, with 15th-century coastal voyages** — The Portuguese opened it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After the Portuguese came —
a) Dutch, English, French and Spanish  b) Only the English  c) Americans and Russians  d) No one for 300 years
**Answer: a) Dutch, English, French and Spanish** — Dutch, English, French and Spanish followed.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the 19th century Europe wanted Africa as —
a) A holiday land  b) A source of raw materials, a market, and cheap labour  c) A prison colony only  d) A neutral zone
**Answer: b) A source of raw materials, a market, and cheap labour** — Raw materials, markets, cheap labour.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Britain defeated Ashanti, with Fante help, in —
a) 1814  b) 1860  c) 1874  d) 1885
**Answer: c) 1874** — 1874.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Dutch settlers trekked inland after Britain took —
a) Cape Town in 1814  b) Senegal in 1860  c) Fashoda in 1898  d) Egypt in 1882
**Answer: a) Cape Town in 1814** — Cape Town, 1814 — the trek escaped British control.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The decisive stage, the Scramble for Africa, came in the —
a) 1750s  b) 1820s  c) 1880s  d) 1950s
**Answer: c) 1880s** — The 1880s.
