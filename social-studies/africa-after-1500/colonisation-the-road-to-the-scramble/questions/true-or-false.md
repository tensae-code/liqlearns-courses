# Colonisation — the road to the Scramble — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Portuguese voyages of the 15th century were coastal.
**Answer: True** — Colonisation opened at the coast.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sugar-cane plantation expansion drove demand for slave labour in the earlier age.
**Answer: True** — The chapter's assessment stock confirms the plantation driver of slave-labour demand.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Explorers mapped Africa's wealth, rivers and market centres before traders pushed inland.
**Answer: True** — Mapping came with exploring; traders then pushed from coastal stations.
