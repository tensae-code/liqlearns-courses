# Colonisation — the road to the Scramble — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who opened colonisation, and who followed?
**Answer:** The Portuguese opened it with 15th-century coastal voyages; the Dutch, English, French and Spanish followed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the book's three stepping-stones on the road to the Scramble.
**Answer:** France's small Senegal colony of the 1860s; Britain's defeat of Ashanti with Fante help in 1874; the Dutch settlers' inland trek after Britain took Cape Town in 1814.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What three things did 19th-century Europe want from Africa?
**Answer:** Industrial raw materials, a market for its goods, and a reservoir of cheap labour.
