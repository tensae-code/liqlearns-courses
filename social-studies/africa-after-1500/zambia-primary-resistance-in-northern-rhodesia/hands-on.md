# Hands-on — Zambia — primary resistance in Northern Rhodesia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A map of refusals
Draw a simple map of Northern Rhodesia. Mark Mwinilunga, the Gwembe valley and the Luapula Valley. Beside each place, write in your own words what refusal happened there and in what year, where the lesson gives one.

## Two kinds of no
Make two columns. In the first, list the quiet refusals in this lesson — refusing the tax, refusing forced labour. In the second, list the armed resistances. Say out loud what both kinds were defending.

## What was being defended?
Write four short lines, one each for land, the chiefs' authority, chitemene farming and independent churches, saying why each one mattered to the people defending it.
