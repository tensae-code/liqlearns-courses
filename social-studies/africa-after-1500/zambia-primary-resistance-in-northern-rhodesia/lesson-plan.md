# Lesson plan — Zambia — primary resistance in Northern Rhodesia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Zambia — primary resistance in Northern Rhodesia
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Zambia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the first, "primary" stage of Zambian resistance, chiefs, farmers and whole villages said no to colonial rule in Northern Rhodesia — refusing the tax, refusing forced labour, fighting with arms, and defending their land, their chiefs, their chitemene farming and their independent churches.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Zambia's own two-stage telling of resistance and on the names, places and dates the Zambian module itself names. It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Saying no to the tax and the labour draft** — the Lunda under Kasanza at Mwinilunga refuse the tax in 1907, the Gwembe Tonga refuse in 1909, and the Namwanga and Bisa refuse forced labour
2. **Armed resistance, land and the chiefs** — the Ngoni under Mpezeni I resist with arms in 1897, Mwata Kazembe resists in the Luapula Valley, and communities defend their land, their chiefs' authority, their chitemene farming and their independent churches

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Colonisation — direct and indirect rule. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Zambia's module means by primary resistance.
2. Name the 1907 and 1909 tax refusals and who made them.
3. Name the Ngoni and Luapula armed resistances and their leaders.
4. Say what people defended besides land — chiefs' authority, chitemene farming and independent churches.
