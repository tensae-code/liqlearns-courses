# Zambia — primary resistance in Northern Rhodesia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Zambia's module mean by primary resistance?
**Answer: The first, local, chief-led stage of resistance — villages and chiefs refusing the tax and forced labour and resisting with arms, before organised parties existed (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one armed resistance in this lesson and its leader.
**Answer: The Ngoni under Mpezeni I in 1897, or Mwata Kazembe in the Luapula Valley (either, with its leader).**
