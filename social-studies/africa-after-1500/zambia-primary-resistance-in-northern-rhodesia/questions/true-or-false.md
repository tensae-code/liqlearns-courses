# Zambia — primary resistance in Northern Rhodesia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gwembe Tonga refused the colonial tax in 1909.
**Answer: True** — two years after the Lunda refusal at Mwinilunga in 1907.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Primary resistance was led by national political parties.
**Answer: False** — it was local and chief-led; organised parties belong to the later, secondary stage.
