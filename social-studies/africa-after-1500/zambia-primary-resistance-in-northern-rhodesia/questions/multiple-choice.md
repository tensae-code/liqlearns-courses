# Zambia — primary resistance in Northern Rhodesia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Lunda under Kasanza refused the colonial tax at Mwinilunga in —
a) 1907  b) 1897  c) 1961
**Answer: a) 1907** — the Gwembe Tonga refused in 1909.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Namwanga and the Bisa refused —
a) To farm  b) Forced labour  c) To pay their chiefs
**Answer: b) Forced labour** — they would not be drafted away from their homes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Ngoni resisted with arms in 1897 under —
a) Mwata Kazembe  b) Kenneth Kaunda  c) Mpezeni I
**Answer: c) Mpezeni I** — Mwata Kazembe resisted in the Luapula Valley.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Besides land, the primary resistance defended —
a) Chiefs' authority, chitemene farming and independent churches  b) Colonial taxes  c) The Federation
**Answer: a) Chiefs' authority, chitemene farming and independent churches** — their rulers, their farming and their own worship.
