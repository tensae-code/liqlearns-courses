# Gabon — daily life changes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The traditional house of planks and beaten earth gives way, in town, to villas and apartment blocks
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 39 L'évolution des modes de vie (1): l'habitat).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** From barter between villages to money and markets, Gabonese trade keeps changing shape
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 42 L'évolution des échanges et du commerce).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Canoes, feet and bush paths give way to roads, vehicles and new journeys
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 45 L'évolution des modes de vie: les transports).
