# Lesson plan — Gabon — daily life changes

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Gabon — daily life changes
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Gabon, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In Gabon, houses, trade and travel keep changing shape — from planks and beaten earth, barter and canoes, to villas, money markets, roads and vehicles.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Houses change — habitat** — the traditional house of planks and beaten earth gives way, in town, to villas and apartment blocks (Leçon 39 L'évolution des modes de vie (1): l'habitat)
2. **Trade changes — from barter to money and markets** — from barter between villages to money and markets, Gabonese trade keeps changing shape (Leçon 42 L'évolution des échanges et du commerce)
3. **Travel changes — transports** — canoes, feet and bush paths give way to roads, vehicles and new journeys (Leçon 45 L'évolution des modes de vie: les transports)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
