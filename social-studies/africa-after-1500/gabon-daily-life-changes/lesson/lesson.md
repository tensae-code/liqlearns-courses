# Gabon — daily life changes

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In Gabon, houses, trade and travel keep changing shape — from planks and beaten earth, barter and canoes, to villas, money markets, roads and vehicles. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Houses change — habitat

The traditional house of planks and beaten earth gives way, in town, to villas and apartment blocks (Leçon 39 L'évolution des modes de vie (1): l'habitat)

In your mind's eye: a traditional Gabonese house of planks and beaten earth beside a modern town villa, with a canoe and a road vehicle. Say one thing that changed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Trade changes — from barter to money and markets

From barter between villages to money and markets, Gabonese trade keeps changing shape (Leçon 42 L'évolution des échanges et du commerce)

## Sub lesson 3 — Travel changes — transports

Canoes, feet and bush paths give way to roads, vehicles and new journeys (Leçon 45 L'évolution des modes de vie: les transports)
