# Africa in the Second World War

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ruled by the belligerents, African states are made to join the war; thousands of African men are recruited to the fronts for Europe's quarrel. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
