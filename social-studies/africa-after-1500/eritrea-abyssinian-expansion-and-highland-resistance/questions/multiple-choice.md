# Eritrea — Abyssinian expansion and highland resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The raiders came for —
a) tribute, plunder and coastal arms  b) silk and spices  c) students for schools
**Answer: a) tribute, plunder and coastal arms** — tribute, plunder and a road to coastal arms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ras Michael Sehul deposed a Bahri Negassi in —
a) 1770  b) 1876  c) 1991
**Answer: a) 1770** — 1770, says the Grade 8 book.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The famine years remembered after the exactions were —
a) 1888–1892  b) 1557–1560  c) 1941–1952
**Answer: a) 1888–1892** — the famine years of 1888–1892.
