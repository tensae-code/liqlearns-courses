# Eritrea — Abyssinian expansion and highland resistance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Saho beat Ras Alula's men at Rub-Rubia.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 8 book tells it as highland resistance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The highlands never resisted the raids.
**Answer: False** — Woldemichael Solomon of Hazzega led the resistance.
