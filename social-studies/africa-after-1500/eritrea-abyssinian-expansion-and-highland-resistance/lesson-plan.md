# Lesson plan — Eritrea — Abyssinian expansion and highland resistance

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — Abyssinian expansion and highland resistance
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the 19th century Abyssinian and Tigrean rulers raided Medri Bahri for tribute, plunder and coastal arms — Ras Michael Sehul deposed a Bahri Negassi in 1770 — while the Saho beat Ras Alula's men at Rub-Rubia and Woldemichael Solomon of Hazzega led the highland resistance, and the famine years of 1888–1892 followed the exactions.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Abyssinian raids** — Abyssinian rulers raid and tax the highlands in the 19th century, and Eritreans remember both the exactions and the famine years of 1888–1892 that followed (Abyssinian raids)
2. **Abyssinian Expansion and Eritrean Resistance** — Tigrean and Abyssinian rulers raid Medri Bahri for tribute, plunder and coastal arms — Ras Michael Sehul deposes a Bahri Negassi in 1770 — while the Saho beat Ras Alula's men at Rub-Rubia and Woldemichael Solomon of Hazzega leads highland resistance (Abyssinian Expansion and Eritrean Resistance)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

2. Answer, in their own words: what did the raids take, and what followed for ordinary people?
