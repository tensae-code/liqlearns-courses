# Hands-on — Eritrea — Abyssinian expansion and highland resistance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw the tribute road: a village, a tax load, and the raiders' path — then mark where the resistance stood.

## Task 2
Write two memory lines as an Eritrean elder might keep them: one for the exactions, one for the famine years.
