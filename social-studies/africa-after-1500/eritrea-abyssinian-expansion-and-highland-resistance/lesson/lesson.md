# Eritrea — Abyssinian expansion and highland resistance

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: in the 19th century Abyssinian and Tigrean rulers raided Medri Bahri for tribute, plunder and coastal arms — Ras Michael Sehul deposed a Bahri Negassi in 1770 — while the Saho beat Ras Alula's men at Rub-Rubia and Woldemichael Solomon of Hazzega led the highland resistance, and the famine years of 1888–1892 followed the exactions. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Abyssinian raids

Abyssinian rulers raid and tax the highlands in the 19th century, and Eritreans remember both the exactions and the famine years of 1888–1892 that followed.

In your mind's eye: the key place or moment of Eritrea — Abyssinian expansion and highland resistance as the book shows it: point to it and say what it shows.
