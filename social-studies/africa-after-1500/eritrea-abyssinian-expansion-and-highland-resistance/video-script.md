# Video script — Eritrea — Abyssinian expansion and highland resistance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Abyssinian rulers raid and tax the highlands in the 19th century, and Eritreans remember both the exactions and the famine years of 1888–1892 that followed.

Tigrean and Abyssinian rulers raid Medri Bahri for tribute, plunder and coastal arms — Ras Michael Sehul deposes a Bahri Negassi in 1770 — while the Saho beat Ras Alula's men at Rub-Rubia and Woldemichael Solomon of Hazzega leads highland resistance.
