# The Boer republics — Transvaal, Orange Free State and Natal

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Dingane falls to his half-brother Mpande, backed by Pretorius; the Sand River Convention (1852) frees the Transvaal, Bloemfontein (1854) the Orange Free State — but Natal, annexed by Britain in 1843, is the republic that does not last. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
