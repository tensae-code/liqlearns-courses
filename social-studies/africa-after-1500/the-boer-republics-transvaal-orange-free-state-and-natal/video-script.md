# Video script — The Boer republics — Transvaal, Orange Free State and Natal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Dingane falls to his half-brother Mpande, backed by Pretorius; the Sand River Convention (1852) frees the Transvaal, Bloemfontein (1854) the Orange Free State — but Natal, annexed by Britain in 1843, is the republic that does not last.
