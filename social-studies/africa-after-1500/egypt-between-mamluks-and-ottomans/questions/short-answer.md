# Egypt between the Mamluks and the Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Egypt between the Mamluks and the Ottomans.
**Answer: Three powers collide over the Islamic world — a Mamluk state ruined by the Cape of Good Hope route of 1498, the fleet's destruction at Diu in 1509 and its own infighting; Safavid Persia; and Selim's Ottomans, fresh from Chaldiran in 1514; at Marj Dabiq in 1516 and al-Raydaniyya in 1517 the Mamluks fall, Egypt becomes an Ottoman province and the caliphate's seat moves to the Ottoman state — four stagnant centuries follow, broken only by Ali Bey al-Kabir's bid for independence in 1768. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Prep9 full-year "Geography of the World and Modern & Contemporary History of Egypt"* (Grade 9 (Third Preparatory), both terms in one volume), Egypt between the Mamluks and the Ottomans, printed p. 41.**
