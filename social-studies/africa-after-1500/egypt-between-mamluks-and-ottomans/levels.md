## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt between the Mamluks and the Ottomans
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — three powers collide over the Islamic world — a Mamluk state ruined by the Cape of Good Hope route of 1498, the fleet's destruction at Diu in 1509 and its own infighting; Safavid Persia; and Selim's Ottomans, fresh from Chaldiran in 1514; at Marj Dabiq in 1516 and al-Raydaniyya in 1517 the Mamluks fall, Egypt becomes an Ottoman province and the caliphate's seat moves to the Ottoman state — four stagnant centuries follow, broken only by Ali Bey al-Kabir's bid for independence in 1768
