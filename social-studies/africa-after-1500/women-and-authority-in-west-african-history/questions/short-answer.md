# Women and authority in West African history — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two kinds of authority did women hold in precolonial West Africa, and before what did this come?
**Answer: Spiritual and political authority, long before colonial patriarchy (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Who was Ahebi Ugbabe, and what makes her unique?
**Answer: A woman in colonial Nigeria who rose to warrant chief and king — the only female king in British Africa (any close wording).**
