# Women and authority in West African history — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Women in precolonial West Africa held authority at times over men.
**Answer: True — the book teaches women held spiritual and political authority, at times over men.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Ahebi Ugbabe was one of many female kings in British Africa.
**Answer: False — she was the only female king in British Africa.**
