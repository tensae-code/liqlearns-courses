# Women and authority in West African history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In precolonial West Africa, women held —
a) Spiritual and political authority  b) No authority of any kind  c) Only household chores
**Answer: a) Spiritual and political authority**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Women's political authority was at times —
a) Over men  b) Never recognised  c) Only in stories
**Answer: a) Over men**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ahebi Ugbabe became —
a) Warrant chief and king  b) A colonial governor  c) A missionary
**Answer: a) Warrant chief and king**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ahebi Ugbabe was —
a) The only female king in British Africa  b) One of many female kings in British Africa  c) Never a king
**Answer: a) The only female king in British Africa**
