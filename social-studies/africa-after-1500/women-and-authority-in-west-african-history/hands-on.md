# Hands-on — Women and authority in West African history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two kinds of authority
Draw two columns headed Spiritual authority and Political authority. In each, write what the book says women held in precolonial West Africa, and one sentence on why it matters that this came before colonial patriarchy.

## The female king
Draw a crown over the name Ahebi Ugbabe. Around it, write the two titles she rose to — warrant chief and king — and the one fact that makes her unique in British Africa.
