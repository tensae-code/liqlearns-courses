# Video script — Women and authority in West African history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Gambia's Grade 10 WASSCE History tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before colonial rule remade West Africa, women held power there — spiritual power, and political power, at times over men.



That is the first half of this story: female authority is old in West Africa.



And then comes Ahebi Ugbabe of Nigeria — warrant chief, and king — the only female king in British Africa.
