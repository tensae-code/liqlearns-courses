# Video script — Challenges of post-independence East Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The new states inherit illiteracy, poor transport, civil wars, coups and human-rights violations — the independence generation's unfinished work.
After independence the region's economies stall — little to export, low income, poverty and poor living standards are the inheritance the lessons name.
