# Rwanda — key dates: independence, genocide and liberation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rwanda gained independence in —
a) 1959  b) 1962  c) 1994
**Answer: b) 1962** — 1962 is the independence year on the timeline.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Liberation Day, when the genocide was stopped, is —
a) 1 July 1962  b) 4 July 1994  c) 1 February
**Answer: b) 4 July 1994** — Liberation is 4 July 1994.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In 2016 Rwanda hosted —
a) The Berlin Conference  b) The African Union summit  c) The first Olympics
**Answer: b) The African Union summit** — Rwanda hosts the African Union summit in 2016.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** On a timeline, these three dates go in the order —
a) 1994, 1962, 2016  b) 1962, 1994, 2016  c) 2016, 1994, 1962
**Answer: b) 1962, 1994, 2016** — Earliest first: 1962, then 1994, then 2016.
