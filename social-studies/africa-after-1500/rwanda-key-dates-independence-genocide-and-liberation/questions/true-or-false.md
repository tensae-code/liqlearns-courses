# Rwanda — key dates: independence, genocide and liberation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The Genocide against the Tutsi happened in 1994.
**Answer: True** — The P3 timeline marks 1994 for the genocide and for Liberation on 4 July.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Rwanda hosted the African Union summit before it became independent.
**Answer: False** — The summit is 2016, long after independence in 1962.
