# Rwanda — key dates: independence, genocide and liberation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Rwanda's three marked timeline years and what each one means.
**Answer: 1962 independence; 1994 the Genocide against the Tutsi and Liberation on 4 July; 2016 Rwanda hosts the African Union summit (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the same year, 1994, carry both the genocide and Liberation?
**Answer: Because the genocide happened in 1994 and was stopped that same year — Liberation on 4 July 1994 (any close wording).**
