# Egypt — the 23 July 1952 Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The 23 July 1952 Revolution (Term 2).
**Answer: The revolution abolishes feudalism — agrarian reform from September 1952 caps ownership at fifty feddans — makes education free at every stage, builds the High Dam and Helwan's steelworks and gives women the vote in 1956; abroad it backs the independence of Sudan and Algeria and the rights of Palestine, breaks the arms monopoly with the Czech deal, co-founds the Non-Aligned Movement at Bandung in 1955, and unites with Syria in 1958.
