# Lesson plan — Eritrea — federation with Ethiopia and its end

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — federation with Ethiopia and its end
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In 1952 Eritrea was federated with Ethiopia under a UN resolution, with its own government — but Ethiopia dismantled the federation piece by piece until, on 14 November 1962, it annexed Eritrea outright.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The federation** — Eritrea is federated with Ethiopia in 1952 with its own government, but Ethiopia dismantles the federation piece by piece until nothing is left (The federation)
2. **The federation and its end** — UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950 federates Eritrea with Ethiopia; Chief Executive Tedla Bairu heads Eritrea's own government, but Ethiopia strips the federation away and annexes Eritrea outright on 14 November 1962 (The federation and its end)
3. **The UN federation and its dismantling** — UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950 federates Eritrea with Ethiopia, giving Eritrea its own government and taxes — but Ethiopia weakens Eritrea's economy and strips the federation away piece by piece (The UN federation and its dismantling)
