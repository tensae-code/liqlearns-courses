# Eritrea — federation with Ethiopia and its end

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: in 1952 Eritrea was federated with Ethiopia under a UN resolution, with its own government — but Ethiopia dismantled the federation piece by piece until, on 14 November 1962, it annexed Eritrea outright. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The federation

Eritrea is federated with Ethiopia in 1952 with its own government, but Ethiopia dismantles the federation piece by piece until nothing is left.

## Sub lesson 2 — The federation and its end

UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950 federates Eritrea with Ethiopia; Chief Executive Tedla Bairu heads Eritrea's own government, but Ethiopia strips the federation away and annexes Eritrea outright on 14 November 1962.

## Sub lesson 3 — The UN federation and its dismantling

UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950 federates Eritrea with Ethiopia, giving Eritrea its own government and taxes — but Ethiopia weakens Eritrea's economy and strips the federation away piece by piece.
