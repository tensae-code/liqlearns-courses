# Eritrea — federation with Ethiopia and its end — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the federation Eritrea kept its own government and taxes.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 8 book stresses its own government and taxes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The federation ended because Eritrea voted to dissolve it.
**Answer: False** — Ethiopia stripped it away piece by piece and annexed Eritrea.
