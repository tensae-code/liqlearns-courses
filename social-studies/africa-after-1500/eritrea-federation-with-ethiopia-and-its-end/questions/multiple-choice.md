# Eritrea — federation with Ethiopia and its end — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The federation was set up by —
a) UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950  b) the Treaty of Wuchale  c) Italy
**Answer: a) UN Resolution 390A(V) of 2 December 1950** — Resolution 390A(V), 2 December 1950.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Eritrea's first Chief Executive was —
a) Tedla Bairu  b) Bahta Hagos  c) Hamid Idris Awate
**Answer: a) Tedla Bairu** — Tedla Bairu headed Eritrea's own government.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ethiopia annexed Eritrea outright on —
a) 24 May 1991  b) 14 November 1962  c) 1 January 1890
**Answer: b) 14 November 1962** — 14 November 1962, says the Grade 5 book.
