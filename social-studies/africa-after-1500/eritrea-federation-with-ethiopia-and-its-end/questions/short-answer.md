# Eritrea — federation with Ethiopia and its end — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the federation give Eritrea at the start?
**Answer: Its own government, headed by Chief Executive Tedla Bairu, and its own taxes (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the federation end?
**Answer: Ethiopia dismantled it piece by piece and annexed Eritrea outright on 14 November 1962 (any close wording).**
