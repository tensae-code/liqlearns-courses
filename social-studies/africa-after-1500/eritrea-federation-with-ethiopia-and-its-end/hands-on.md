# Hands-on — Eritrea — federation with Ethiopia and its end

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Build the federation as a paper bridge: two ends labelled Eritrea and Ethiopia, the UN resolution as its arch — then remove one piece at a time and say what was lost each time.

## Task 2
Write the two dates on a line — 1952 and 14 November 1962 — and one sentence under each.
