# Video script — Uganda becomes a British protectorate, 1894

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Portal goes home, recommends the protectorate and dies of typhoid at 36; on 18 June 1894 Uganda is declared a British Protectorate, and governors from Jackson of the First World War to Crawford of the first general elections administer it.
