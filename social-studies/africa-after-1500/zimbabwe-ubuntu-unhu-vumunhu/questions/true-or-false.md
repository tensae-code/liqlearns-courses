# Zimbabwe — Ubuntu, Unhu, Vumunhu: a person through other people — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Unhu is only about greeting visitors.
**Answer: False** — it is the value system — respect, sharing and care — that carries the heritage

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Unhu stays one unexplained word through primary school.
**Answer: False** — at Grade 2 it is unpacked into its attributes
