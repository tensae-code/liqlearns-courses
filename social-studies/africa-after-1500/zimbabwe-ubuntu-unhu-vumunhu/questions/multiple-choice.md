# Zimbabwe — Ubuntu, Unhu, Vumunhu: a person through other people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ubuntu/Unhu/Vumunhu teaches that a person is a person —
a) through other people  b) through money alone  c) through being alone
**Answer: a) through other people** — a person is a person through other people

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The value turns respect, sharing and care for elders into —
a) the rules the community lives by  b) optional extras  c) things for festivals only
**Answer: a) the rules the community lives by** — they become the rules the community lives by

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At Grade 2, unhu becomes —
a) a checklist of how a good person treats others  b) a song only  c) a rule for teachers only
**Answer: a) a checklist of how a good person treats others** — unhu is unpacked into its attributes — a checklist

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An attribute of unhu is something a child can —
a) practise day by day  b) only read about  c) buy at the market
**Answer: a) practise day by day** — the attributes are how a good person treats others, day by day
