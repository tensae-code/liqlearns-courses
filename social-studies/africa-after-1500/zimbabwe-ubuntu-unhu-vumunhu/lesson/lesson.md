# Zimbabwe — Ubuntu, Unhu, Vumunhu: a person through other people

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ubuntu — Unhu in Shona, Vumunhu — is the value that carries Zimbabwe's heritage: a person is a person through other people, so respect, sharing and care for elders become the rules the community lives by. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Naming the value — Ubuntu/Unhu/Vumunhu (Grade 1)

The heritage topics close by naming the value that carries them. A person is a person through other people — so respect, sharing and care for elders stop being extras and become the rules the community lives by.
