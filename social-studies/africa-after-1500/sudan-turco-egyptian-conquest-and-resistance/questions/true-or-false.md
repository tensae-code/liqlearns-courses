# The Turco-Egyptian conquest of Sudan — and the resistance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** an empire marches a thousand miles up the Nile in one season, and spends sixty years being resisted.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan and Its Resistance, and Sudanese Resistance to Turco-Egyptian Rule

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ — التعليم الثانوي — الصف الثاني (History, Secondary Education, Second Year = Grade 11)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan and Its Resistance, and Sudanese Resistance to Turco-Egyptian Rule

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a divided land of sultanates meets a modern army, and in one campaigning season Sudan has a foreign master.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف السادس — المرحلة الابتدائية (History, Grade 6, Primary Stage)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** sixty years of refusal, in arms, in flight and in mutiny, until refusal becomes a revolution.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Turco-Egyptian Rule in Sudan (3)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف الثاني — المرحلة المتوسطة (History, Second Intermediate Year = Grade 8)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Turco-Egyptian Rule in Sudan (3)
