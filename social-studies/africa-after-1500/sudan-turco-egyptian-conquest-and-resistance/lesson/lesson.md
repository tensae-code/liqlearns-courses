# The Turco-Egyptian conquest of Sudan — and the resistance

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: an empire marches a thousand miles up the Nile in one season, and spends sixty years being resisted. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
