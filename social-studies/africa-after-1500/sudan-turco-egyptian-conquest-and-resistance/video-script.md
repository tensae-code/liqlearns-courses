# Video script — The Turco-Egyptian conquest of Sudan — and the resistance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

an empire marches a thousand miles up the Nile in one season, and spends sixty years being resisted.

a divided land of sultanates meets a modern army, and in one campaigning season Sudan has a foreign master.

sixty years of refusal, in arms, in flight and in mutiny, until refusal becomes a revolution.
