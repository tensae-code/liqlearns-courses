# Hands-on — The Turco-Egyptian conquest of Sudan — and the resistance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Turco-Egyptian conquest of Sudan — and the resistance — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Turco-Egyptian Invasion of Sudan and Its Resistance, and Sudanese Resistance to Turco-Egyptian Rule, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
