# Africa after 1500 — slavery before the Atlantic trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Slavery, in the book's opening, is tied to —
a) Race alone  b) Africa alone  c) No single race, colour or continent — it was wide and old  d) Europe alone
**Answer: c) No single race, colour or continent — it was wide and old** — The book opens: slavery is not tied to race, skin colour or Africa alone.

**2.** In Europe, slaves were often —
a) War captives of different peoples  b) Only kings  c) Only merchants  d) Only farmers
**Answer: a) War captives of different peoples** — War captives of different peoples were sold as slaves in Europe.

**3.** The word "slave" derives from —
a) Africa  b) Slav  c) Mediterranean  d) Atlantic
**Answer: b) Slav** — So many Slavs were enslaved that "slave" derives from "Slav".

**4.** Africa's slave trade toward the Mediterranean reached its height —
a) In ancient times  b) From the 9th century  c) Only after the Age of Exploration began  d) In the 20th century
**Answer: c) Only after the Age of Exploration began** — It grew from the 9th century but reached its height only after the Age of Exploration began.
