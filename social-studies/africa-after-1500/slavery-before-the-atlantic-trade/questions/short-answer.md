# Africa after 1500 — slavery before the Atlantic trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Give the European example that shows slavery was not Africa's alone.
**Answer:** Slavs from eastern and central Europe were sent as slaves to the Middle East and North Africa (and European war captives were sold).

**2.** When did Africa's Mediterranean slave trade grow, and when did it reach its height?
**Answer:** It grew from the 9th century; it reached its height only after the Age of Exploration began.

**3.** Why does the origin of the word "slave" matter to this lesson?
**Answer:** It proves from language itself that slavery was wide and old — a people's name (Slav) became the word for the condition, far from Africa.
