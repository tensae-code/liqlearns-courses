# Africa after 1500 — slavery before the Atlantic trade — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Slavs from eastern and central Europe were sent as slaves to the Middle East and North Africa.
**Answer: True** — For long ages, exactly so.

**2.** Africa sent no slaves anywhere before the Atlantic trade.
**Answer: False** — From ancient times sub-Saharan Africa sent slaves toward the Mediterranean.

**3.** The Atlantic trade was the beginning of slavery in the world.
**Answer: False** — Slavery is far older and wider than the Atlantic trade.
