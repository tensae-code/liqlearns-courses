# Lesson plan — Africa after 1500 — slavery before the Atlantic trade

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Africa after 1500 — slavery before the Atlantic trade
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 12 — Africa after 1500 (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Slavery is **older and wider than Africa**: war captives were sold in Europe too, and the word **"slave" itself derives from "Slav"**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has met Africa after 1500 in this title's order. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that slavery is not tied to race, skin colour or Africa alone, and give the European example.
2. Explain where the word "slave" comes from.
3. Say when Africa's trade toward the Mediterranean grew, and when it reached its height.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks slavery began with the Atlantic trade → the trade is far older and wider: Slav captives in Europe, and Africa's Mediterranean trade growing from the 9th century; the Atlantic height came only after the Age of Exploration began.
