# Africa after 1500 — slavery before the Atlantic trade — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Say that slavery is not tied to race, skin colour or Africa alone, and give the European example.; Explain where the word "slave" comes from.; Say when Africa's trade toward the Mediterranean grew, and when it reached its height. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
