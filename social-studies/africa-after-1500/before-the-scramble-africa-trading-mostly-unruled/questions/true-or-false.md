# Before the Scramble — Africa trading, mostly unruled — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's trade with Europe before the 1880s was limited to a single good.
**Answer: False** — Exports broadened across oils, nuts, gum, ivory, gold, hides, rubber, cotton, copper and cash crops.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Most of Africa was under foreign rule before the 1880s.
**Answer: False** — Only about 10% was ruled by foreign powers.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Portuguese foothold named in the book is Mozambique.
**Answer: True** — Portuguese in Mozambique — one of the six footholds.
