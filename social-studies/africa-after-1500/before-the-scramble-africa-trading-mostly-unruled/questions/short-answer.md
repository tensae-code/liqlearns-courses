# Before the Scramble — Africa trading, mostly unruled — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name six goods in Africa's broadened export trade.
**Answer:** Any six of: vegetable and palm oils, groundnuts, gum, ivory, gold, hides and skins, rubber, cotton, copper, cocoa, coffee, tea, tobacco.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the foreign footholds in Africa before the 1880s.
**Answer:** Nominal Turkish rule in much of North Africa; Omani rule in Zanzibar; the British in Sierra Leone; the French in Algeria; Boers and British in South Africa; the Portuguese in Mozambique.
