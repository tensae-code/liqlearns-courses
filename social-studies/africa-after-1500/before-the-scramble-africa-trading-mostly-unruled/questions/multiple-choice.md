# Before the Scramble — Africa trading, mostly unruled — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's exports broadened in this period because of —
a) Industrialising Europe's rising demand for industrial inputs  b) A ban on African farming  c) The Berlin Conference  d) African rulers' isolation
**Answer: a) Industrialising Europe's rising demand for industrial inputs** — Europe's demand for industrial inputs drove the transition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which goods are in the book's export list?
a) Palm oils, groundnuts, ivory, gold, rubber  b) Cars, radios, aeroplanes  c) Only slaves  d) Wheat and wine only
**Answer: a) Palm oils, groundnuts, ivory, gold, rubber** — Oils, groundnuts, gum, ivory, gold, hides and skins, rubber, cotton, copper and cash crops.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Cocoa, coffee, tea and tobacco appear in the lesson as —
a) Imports from Asia  b) Cash crops among Africa's exports  c) Banned goods  d) Church products
**Answer: b) Cash crops among Africa's exports** — They are the cash crops in the broadened export list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Before the 1880s, the share of Africa ruled by foreign powers was about —
a) 90%  b) 50%  c) 10%  d) 1%
**Answer: c) 10%** — About 10% — the baseline the Scramble overturned.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Omani rule before the Scramble held —
a) Sierra Leone  b) Zanzibar  c) Algeria  d) Mozambique
**Answer: b) Zanzibar** — Omani rule in Zanzibar; Britain held Sierra Leone, France Algeria, Portugal Mozambique.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Nominal rule in much of North Africa before the 1880s was —
a) French  b) British  c) Turkish  d) German
**Answer: c) Turkish** — Nominal Turkish rule in much of North Africa.
