# Hands-on — Before the Scramble — Africa trading, mostly unruled

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cargo list
Write a cargo manifest for an 1880 trading port: choose eight goods from the lesson's export list and write one line each on what industrial Europe made from it.

## Dot the map
On a blank Africa outline, dot the six footholds the book names. Then shade the rest and label it: 'ruled by Africans — about 90%'.
