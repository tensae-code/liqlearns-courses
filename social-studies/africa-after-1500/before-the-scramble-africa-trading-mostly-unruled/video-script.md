# Video script — Before the Scramble — Africa trading, mostly unruled


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Africa, about 1880 — and it is busy, not empty.

Ships load palm oil and groundnuts. Caravans carry ivory and gold.

Europe's factories are hungry, and African merchants feed the trade.

Now look for foreign flags. A dot here — Sierra Leone. A dot there — Algeria, Mozambique, Zanzibar.

Count them: about ten parts in a hundred. The rest of the continent rules itself.

Hold this picture. In the next lessons, watch how fast it is overturned.

Two mirrors face each other across the sea. In one, Europe admires itself; in the other, an invented Africa glares back — drawn by the same hand.
