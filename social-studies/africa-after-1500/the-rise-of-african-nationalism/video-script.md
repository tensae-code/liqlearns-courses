# Video script — The rise of African nationalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The ex-servicemen of the Second World War come home having seen that "even the white man cries and can die"; Ethiopia's defeat of Italy in 1935–41 inspires the continent, and the OAU backs the last liberations.
