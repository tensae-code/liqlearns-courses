# Hands-on — Gordon in Sudan — and the road to the Mahdiyya

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Gordon in Sudan — and the road to the Mahdiyya — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Gordon's Hukmdariyya in Sudan (1877–1879), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
