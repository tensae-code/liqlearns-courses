# Video script — Egypt — parliamentary and party life, and women's national work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From Muhammad Ali's advisory councils and Ismail's Shura Council of 1866 to the party age of the 1923 constitution and beyond, Egypt's parliamentary life grows — and women's road runs from al-Tahtawi's call for girls' education toward full political rights.
