# Video script — The OAU judged — liberator, but not peacekeeper

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Addis Ababa, 1963's founding hall reimagined a generation on: the flags are all independent now.
The credit column writes itself in flags: colony after colony liberated, the last settler flag lowering.
The debit column writes itself in smoke: civil wars the organisation watched, economies sagging, summits passing resolutions.
The hall empties; the nameplate is taken down. A new one rises — African Union. The judgement stays carved over the door: liberator, but not peacekeeper.
