# The OAU judged — liberator, but not peacekeeper — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** By the 1990s the colonial map the OAU was founded to erase had been erased.
**Answer: True** — and apartheid, its last target, had fallen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The OAU intervened freely in members' civil wars.
**Answer: False** — non-interference tied its hands.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** The book's verdict on the OAU is entirely negative.
**Answer: False** — it is double — liberator first, failed peacekeeper second.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** The African Union was designed with wider powers, including peace and security machinery.
**Answer: True** — answering precisely the OAU's listed failures.

**5. (Sub lesson 1) ** Apartheid, the OAU mission's last target, had fallen by the 1990s.
**Answer: True** — the founding mission completed.
