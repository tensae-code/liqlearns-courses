# The OAU judged — liberator, but not peacekeeper

## Do it first

Every institution deserves two questions: what did you do that no one else could have done — and what did you fail to do that you existed to do? This lesson puts both questions to the OAU, and uses the book's own double-edged answers.

## Sub lesson 1 — The credit side — liberator

On the credit side the verdict is unqualified: Pan-Africanism and the OAU played an irreplaceable role in liberating Africa from European domination and white minority rule in particular. The organisation gave the liberation movements a continental platform, a diplomatic army at the United Nations, material support and moral legitimacy — the frontline struggles of this unit were fought with the OAU at their back, its liberation committee coordinating what no single state could have sustained alone.

Measured against its founding mission — the total liberation of the continent — the OAU succeeded. By the 1990s the colonial map it was founded to erase had been erased, and apartheid itself, the mission's last target, had fallen. An institution may fairly be judged first by the task it set itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — The debit side — and the successor

But the book's verdict is double, and the second edge is blunt: the OAU failed to bring peace, prosperity, security and stability to the continent it liberated. Civil wars burned inside member states while the organisation's doctrine of non-interference tied its hands; economies stagnated under the debt and drought of the next lesson; the borders it sanctified held, but the peace inside them often did not.

For this reason and others, the book concludes, the OAU was dissolved at the beginning of the 21st century and replaced by the African Union — a successor designed with wider powers, including a peace and security machinery meant to answer precisely the failures this lesson lists. Liberator, but not peacekeeper: the title is the judgement, and both halves are the OAU's history.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion both columns without softening either: irreplaceable liberator; failed peacekeeper; dissolved; the AU succeeds.
