# Hands-on — The OAU judged — liberator, but not peacekeeper

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two-column verdict
Rule the page: Credit / Debit. Enter the lesson's items. Then write the title's judgement — 'liberator, but not peacekeeper' — across the top, and defend the comma: why does the verdict need both halves?

## Design the successor
List the OAU's three greatest weaknesses from this lesson. Beside each, write the power or organ its successor, the AU, would need to answer it.
