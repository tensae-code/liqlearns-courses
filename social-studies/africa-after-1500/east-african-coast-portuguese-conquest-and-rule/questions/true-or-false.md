# East African coast — Portuguese conquest and rule — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Portuguese arrived on the coast mainly to hold ports for the India route.
**Answer: True** — that is the book's stated purpose.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Portuguese ruled the coast for ever, with no one driving them out.
**Answer: False** — Oman drives them out.
