# East African coast — Portuguese conquest and rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did the Portuguese come to the East African coast?
a) To hold ports on the sea route to India  b) To farm cloves  c) To find the Nile's source
**Answer: a) To hold ports on the sea route to India** — the book's reason: India-route ports.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which places does the book name as plundered?
a) Kilwa only  b) Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba  c) Sofala only
**Answer: b) Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba** — the three places the story beats name.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the Portuguese hold the coast?
a) As free city-states  b) They never ruled it  c) As one province
**Answer: c) As one province** — the coast held as one province.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Who finally drove the Portuguese out?
a) Oman  b) Britain  c) Germany
**Answer: a) Oman** — Oman drives them out, in the book's telling.
