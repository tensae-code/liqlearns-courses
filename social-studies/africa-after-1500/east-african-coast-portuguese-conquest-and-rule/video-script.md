# Video script — East African coast — Portuguese conquest and rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Nigeria book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Portuguese come to the East African coast looking for ports on the sea route to India. They demand tribute from the coastal cities and plunder Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba.

The Portuguese hold the coast as one province. Their rule lasts until Oman drives them out — the end the Nigerian book gives this conquest story.

Hold that story: The Portuguese conquer the East African coast to hold ports on the sea route to India, demand tribute, plunder Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba, and rule the coast as one province — until Oman drives them out.
