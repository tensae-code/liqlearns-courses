# East African coast — Portuguese conquest and rule

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Portuguese conquer the East African coast to hold ports on the sea route to India, demand tribute, plunder Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba, and rule the coast as one province — until Oman drives them out. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Portuguese arrive for the India route

The Portuguese come to the East African coast looking for ports on the sea route to India. They demand tribute from the coastal cities and plunder Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba.

[Picture slot — a picture of Portuguese sailing ships anchored off a Swahili coastal city, with tribute being demanded on the shore — the student says why the Portuguese came and which cities were plundered.]

## Sub lesson 2 — One province — until Oman drives them out

The Portuguese hold the coast as one province. Their rule lasts until Oman drives them out — the end the Nigerian book gives this conquest story.
