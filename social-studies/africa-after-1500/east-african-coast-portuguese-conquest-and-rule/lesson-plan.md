# Lesson plan — East African coast — Portuguese conquest and rule

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** East African coast — Portuguese conquest and rule
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Nigeria, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Portuguese conquer the East African coast to hold ports on the sea route to India, demand tribute, plunder Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba, and rule the coast as one province — until Oman drives them out.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Portuguese arrive for the India route** — The Portuguese come to the East African coast looking for ports on the sea route to India. They demand tribute from the coastal cities and plunder Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba.
2. **One province — until Oman drives them out** — The Portuguese hold the coast as one province. Their rule lasts until Oman drives them out — the end the Nigerian book gives this conquest story.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
