# East African coast — Portuguese conquest and rule — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Portuguese arrive for the India route
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Portuguese come to the East African coast looking for ports on the sea route to India. They demand tribute from the coastal cities and plunder Mombasa, Zanzibar and Pemba.

## Sub lesson 2 — One province — until Oman drives them out
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The Portuguese hold the coast as one province. Their rule lasts until Oman drives them out — the end the Nigerian book gives this conquest story.
