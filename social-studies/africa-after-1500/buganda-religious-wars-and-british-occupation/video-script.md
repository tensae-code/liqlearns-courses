# Video script — Buganda — religious wars and British occupation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Nigeria book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mwanga kills Bishop Hannington and tries to balance Muslims and Christians. The parties fight three civil wars, and Lugard's company army settles who rules.

Protestant chief Apolo Kaggwa backs Britain for 37 years. Baganda agents like Kakungulu conquer and administer the east, and Buganda rises above all its neighbours.

Hold that story: Kabaka Mwanga's Buganda splits into Muslim, Protestant and Catholic parties that fight three civil wars — and Lugard's company army, backed by Baganda agents like Apolo Kaggwa, settles British occupation over Uganda.
