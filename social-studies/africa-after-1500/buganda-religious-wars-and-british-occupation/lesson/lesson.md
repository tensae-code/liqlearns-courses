# Buganda — religious wars and British occupation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Kabaka Mwanga's Buganda splits into Muslim, Protestant and Catholic parties that fight three civil wars — and Lugard's company army, backed by Baganda agents like Apolo Kaggwa, settles British occupation over Uganda. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mwanga and the three civil wars

Mwanga kills Bishop Hannington and tries to balance Muslims and Christians. The parties fight three civil wars, and Lugard's company army settles who rules.

In your mind's eye: buganda's court divided into rival parties, with a company officer arriving. Name the parties, the wars and Lugard's role.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Baganda as agents of occupation

Protestant chief Apolo Kaggwa backs Britain for 37 years. Baganda agents like Kakungulu conquer and administer the east, and Buganda rises above all its neighbours.
