## Sub lesson 1 — Mwanga and the three civil wars
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Mwanga kills Bishop Hannington and tries to balance Muslims and Christians. The parties fight three civil wars, and Lugard's company army settles who rules.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Baganda as agents of occupation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Protestant chief Apolo Kaggwa backs Britain for 37 years. Baganda agents like Kakungulu conquer and administer the east, and Buganda rises above all its neighbours.
