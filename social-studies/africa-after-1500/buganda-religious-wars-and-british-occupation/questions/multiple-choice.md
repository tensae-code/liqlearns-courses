# Buganda — religious wars and British occupation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which bishop did Mwanga have killed?
a) Bishop Hannington  b) Bishop Tucker  c) No bishop was killed
**Answer: a) Bishop Hannington** — Mwanga kills Bishop Hannington.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many civil wars did Buganda's religious parties fight?
a) One  b) Three  c) Seven
**Answer: b) Three** — the parties fight three civil wars, 1888–1890.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which Protestant chief backed Britain for 37 years?
a) Kakungulu  b) Mwanga  c) Apolo Kaggwa
**Answer: c) Apolo Kaggwa** — Apolo Kaggwa backs Britain for 37 years.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Baganda agents like Kakungulu did what in the east?
a) Conquered and administered it for the occupation  b) Refused all contact with Britain  c) Left Uganda
**Answer: a) Conquered and administered it for the occupation** — Kakungulu conquers and administers the east.
