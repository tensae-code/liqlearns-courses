# Buganda — religious wars and British occupation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buganda's court split into rival Muslim, Protestant and Catholic parties.
**Answer: True** — the parties are the heart of the chapter.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Baganda played no part in the British occupation of Uganda.
**Answer: False** — Baganda agents helped conquer and administer the east.
