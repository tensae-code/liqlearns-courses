# Mozambique's slave-trade cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The slave cycle.
**Answer: after three centuries of trading Mozambique's products, from the mid-18th century the merchants traffic the producer himself — Mozambicans captured, sold and stripped of all rights (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Mozambique's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Módulo 6 de História — Os Estados em Moçambique e a Penetração Mercantil Estrangeira (PESD II, 2º Ciclo)* (Mozambique), The slave cycle, printed p. 63.**
