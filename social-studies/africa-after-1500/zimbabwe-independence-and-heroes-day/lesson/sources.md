Zimbabwe's own way (PlusOne Social Science Grade 2 Teacher's Guide): Grade 2 adds the nation's calendar to the symbols — Independence Day and Heroes Day with named heroes and heroines (Josiah Tongogara, Herbert Chitepo, John Nkomo, Sally Mugabe, Ruth Chinamano) buried at the National Heroes Acre, visited on an educational tour. That way is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing.

Zimbabwe's own way (PlusOne Social Science Grade 3 Teacher's Guide): Grade 3 turns identity into method — children draw a family tree to “find their origins” and capture stories for the next generation — and turns heroes local, from district heroes up to Mbuya Nehanda, Joshua Nkomo and Josiah Tongogara; heritage sites are learned by drawing, model-building and story-writing. That way is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing.

Zimbabwe's own way (PlusOne Social Science Grade 6 Teacher's Guide): Grade 6 Topic 2 is a full national narrative in four acts — colonisation by treaty trickery and the Pioneer Column, the First Chimurenga of 1896–97, the Second Chimurenga of 1966–79, and the symbols-and-holidays settlement that follows independence — with spirit mediums given a named role in both wars. That way is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing.
