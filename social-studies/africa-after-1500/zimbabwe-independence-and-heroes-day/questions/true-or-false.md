# Zimbabwe — Independence Day and Heroes Day — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heroes Day names those who died for Zimbabwe's freedom.
**Answer: True** — Heroes Day names those who died for that freedom

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** National celebrations happen only in the capital.
**Answer: False** — at Grade 3 the district's own remembrance days join the calendar

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Defence Forces Day is one of the five holidays.
**Answer: True** — the five are Independence Day, Heroes Day, Defence Forces Day, Unity Day and Workers Day
