# Zimbabwe — Independence Day and Heroes Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Independence Day, 18 April, marks —
a) the 1980 break from colonial rule  b) the start of the rains  c) the founding of Harare's market
**Answer: a) the 1980 break from colonial rule** — 18 April marks the 1980 break from colonial rule

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The named heroes and heroines are buried at —
a) the National Heroes Acre  b) Great Zimbabwe  c) Victoria Falls
**Answer: a) the National Heroes Acre** — they are laid at the National Heroes Acre

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At Grade 3, the national calendar gains —
a) the district — the town's own days of remembrance  b) a new month  c) school fees
**Answer: a) the district — the town's own days of remembrance** — the district is added to the national calendar

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The country's milestones become, at Grade 3 —
a) the town's own days of remembrance too  b) only capital-city events  c) history homework
**Answer: a) the town's own days of remembrance too** — the milestones are also the town's own days of remembrance

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Unity Day falls on —
a) 22 December, remembering 1987  b) 18 April  c) 1 May
**Answer: a) 22 December, remembering 1987** — Unity Day is 22 December 1987

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The five holidays together work as —
a) chapters of the national story retold annually  b) days with no meaning  c) a sports calendar
**Answer: a) chapters of the national story retold annually** — each holiday is a chapter of the national story retold annually
