# Hands-on — Zimbabwe — Independence Day and Heroes Day

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Make a one-page national calendar: mark Independence Day, Heroes Day, Defence Forces Day, Unity Day and Workers Day, with one line on what each remembers.

## Task 2
Write a short tribute for one named hero or heroine of the Heroes Acre, saying what they are remembered for.
