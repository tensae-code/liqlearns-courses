# Hands-on — Eritrea — the Ottoman coast and the Naibs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw two zones on one page: the coast under the Naib and the free highlands, and write one sentence under each saying who ruled there.

## Task 2
Act the tax day: one learner is the Naib, one the Bahri Negestat's messenger — share out ten stones as port taxes between you and explain the sharing.
