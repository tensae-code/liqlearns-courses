Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — from 1557 the Turks hold the Red Sea coast for some 300 years, ruling through the Naib family while the highlands stay out of their grip.

## Sub lesson 2 — Modern history: the Turks
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Turks seize the coast in 1557 and hold it some 300 years through the Naibs, never mastering the highlands.

## Sub lesson 3 — Turkish rule through the Naibs
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Turks never take the highlands, so they rule the coast through the Naib of the Belau family, who judges disputes, settles grazing rights and shares the port taxes with the Bahri Negestat.
