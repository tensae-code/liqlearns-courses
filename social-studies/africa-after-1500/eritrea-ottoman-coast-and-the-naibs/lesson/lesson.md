# Eritrea — the Ottoman coast and the Naibs

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: from 1557 the Turks held Eritrea's Red Sea coast for some 300 years, ruling through the Naib family of the coast while the highlands stayed out of their grip. ## Sub lesson 2 — Modern history: the Turks

The Turks seize the coast in 1557 and hold it some 300 years through the Naibs, never mastering the highlands.

## Sub lesson 3 — Turkish rule through the Naibs

The Turks never take the highlands, so they rule the coast through the Naib of the Belau family, who judges disputes, settles grazing rights and shares the port taxes with the Bahri Negestat.
