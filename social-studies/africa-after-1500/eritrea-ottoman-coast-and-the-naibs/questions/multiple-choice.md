# Eritrea — the Ottoman coast and the Naibs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Turks seized Eritrea's coast in —
a) 1557  b) 1869  c) 1941
**Answer: a) 1557** — 1557 is the date all three books give.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Naib was —
a) a Turkish general sent from Istanbul  b) the local ruler of the Belau family who governed the coast for the Turks  c) a highland king
**Answer: b) the local ruler of the Belau family who governed the coast for the Turks** — the Naib of the Belau family judged disputes and shared the port taxes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Turks never mastered —
a) the coast  b) the ports  c) the highlands
**Answer: c) the highlands** — the highlands stayed out of their grip for all 300 years.
