# Eritrea — the Ottoman coast and the Naibs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What work did the Naib do?
**Answer: He judged disputes, settled grazing rights and shared the port taxes with the Bahri Negestat (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did the Turks rule through Naibs instead of directly?
**Answer: Because they never took the highlands and held only the coast, they governed it through the local Belau family (any close wording).**
