# Eritrea — the Ottoman coast and the Naibs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Turkish rule on the coast lasted some 300 years.
**Answer: True** — from 1557 the Turks held the coast for about three centuries.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Naibs ruled the highlands for the Turks.
**Answer: False** — the Naibs ruled the coast; the highlands were never taken.
