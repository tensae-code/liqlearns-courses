# Video script — Eritrea — the Ottoman coast and the Naibs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From 1557 the Turks hold the Red Sea coast for some 300 years, ruling through the Naib family while the highlands stay out of their grip.

The Turks seize the coast in 1557 and hold it some 300 years through the Naibs, never mastering the highlands.

The Turks never take the highlands, so they rule the coast through the Naib of the Belau family, who judges disputes, settles grazing rights and shares the port taxes with the Bahri Negestat.
