# The Mozambique Company — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Mozambique Company.
**Answer: a company with sovereign rights runs Manica and Sofala for Portugal and international capital from 1892 to 1942 — the clearest face of the alliance (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Mozambique's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Módulo 7 de História — O Colonialismo Português em Moçambique de 1890 a 1930 (PESD II, 2º Ciclo)* (Mozambique), The Mozambique Company, printed p. 71.**
