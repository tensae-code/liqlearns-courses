# Buganda — kingdom, clans, Kabaka and trade

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Buganda, the strongest state on Lake Victoria, is built from some forty totemic clans under one Kabaka — and from 1845 Zanzibari Arab traders bring guns, cloth and Islam into the Kabaka's court. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Forty clans under one Kabaka

Forty totemic clans under one Kabaka build the strongest state on Lake Victoria. No one may marry inside their own or their mother's clan — except the king.

[Picture slot — a picture of the Kabaka's court on Lake Victoria, with clan elders gathered and Arab traders arriving with cloth and guns — the student names the clans, the Kabaka and what the traders brought.]

## Sub lesson 2 — The Zanzibari Arabs reach Buganda

Arab traders reach Buganda by 1845 with guns and cloth. Islam follows them, and the Kabaka's court starts to change from the inside.
