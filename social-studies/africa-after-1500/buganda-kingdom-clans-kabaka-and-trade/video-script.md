# Video script — Buganda — kingdom, clans, Kabaka and trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Nigeria book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Forty totemic clans under one Kabaka build the strongest state on Lake Victoria. No one may marry inside their own or their mother's clan — except the king.

Arab traders reach Buganda by 1845 with guns and cloth. Islam follows them, and the Kabaka's court starts to change from the inside.

Hold that story: Buganda, the strongest state on Lake Victoria, is built from some forty totemic clans under one Kabaka — and from 1845 Zanzibari Arab traders bring guns, cloth and Islam into the Kabaka's court.
