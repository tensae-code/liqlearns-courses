# Buganda — kingdom, clans, Kabaka and trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What marriage rule bound everyone except the king?
**Answer: No one may marry inside their own or their mother's clan — except the king (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the Kabaka's court begin to change after 1845?
**Answer: Arab traders brought guns and cloth, Islam followed them, and the court started to change from the inside (any close wording).**
