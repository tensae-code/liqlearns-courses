# Buganda — kingdom, clans, Kabaka and trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buganda's people were organised in about how many totemic clans?
a) About forty  b) About four  c) About four hundred
**Answer: a) About forty** — forty totemic clans, in the book's count.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who stood over all the clans?
a) A council of traders  b) The Kabaka  c) The Arab merchants
**Answer: b) The Kabaka** — one Kabaka over the clans.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When did Arab traders reach Buganda, in the book's telling?
a) By 1700  b) By 1900  c) By 1845
**Answer: c) By 1845** — Arab traders reach Buganda by 1845.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the Arab traders bring?
a) Guns and cloth — and Islam followed  b) Only gold  c) Only books
**Answer: a) Guns and cloth — and Islam followed** — guns and cloth, with Islam following them.
