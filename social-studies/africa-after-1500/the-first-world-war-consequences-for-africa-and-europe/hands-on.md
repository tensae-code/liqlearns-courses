# Hands-on — The First World War — consequences for Africa and Europe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The First World War — consequences for Africa and Europe — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Consequences of the war for Africa and Europe, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
