# Ethiopia — Tewodros II the unifier — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tewodros II was the prophesied Theodore who reunited Ethiopia.
**Answer: True** — he ends the Era of the Princes and reunites the country.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tewodros died peacefully in his palace in Gondar.
**Answer: False** — he dies at Maqdala facing a British expedition.
