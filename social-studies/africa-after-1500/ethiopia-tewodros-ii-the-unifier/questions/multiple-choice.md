# Ethiopia — Tewodros II the unifier — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before he was emperor, Kassa was —
a) An outlawed shifta  b) A Gondar priest  c) An Egyptian officer
**Answer: a) An outlawed shifta** — the outlawed shifta of the story beats.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Kassa's widowed mother do in Gondar?
a) Sold cloth  b) Hawked medicine  c) Taught school
**Answer: b) Hawked medicine** — she hawked medicine in Gondar.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two things did Tewodros reform?
a) Trade and taxes only  b) Roads and schools only  c) Church and army
**Answer: c) Church and army** — he reforms church and army.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where did Tewodros die, facing a British expedition?
a) Maqdala  b) Gondar  c) Aksum
**Answer: a) Maqdala** — he dies at Maqdala.
