# Ethiopia — Tewodros II the unifier

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Kassa, the outlawed shifta whose widowed mother hawked medicine in Gondar, fights his way to the throne as Tewodros II, ends the Era of the Princes, reforms church and army — and dies at Maqdala facing a British expedition. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kassa fights his way to the throne

Kassa, the outlawed shifta whose widowed mother hawked medicine in Gondar, fights his way to the throne and ends the Era of the Princes.

In your mind's eye: kassa rising from outlaw to emperor, crowned over a reunited Ethiopia, with Maqdala's mountain fortress in the distance. The student tells his rise, his reforms and his death.
