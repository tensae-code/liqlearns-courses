# Ethiopia — Yohannes IV against Egypt and Italy

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Yohannes IV wins Menelik's submission by accord not war, crushes Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura, watches Italy take Eritrea with British blessing — and dies fighting the Mahdists. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Accord with Menelik, victory over Egypt

Yohannes wins Menelik's submission by accord not war. He crushes Khedive Ismail's Egyptian invasions at Gundet and Gura.

In your mind's eye: emperor Yohannes IV receiving Menelik's submission by agreement, with the battlefields of Gundet and Gura marked on a map. Name the accord, the battles and his death.
