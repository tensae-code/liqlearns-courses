# Ethiopia — Yohannes IV against Egypt and Italy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yohannes forced Menelik's submission by accord rather than war.
**Answer: True** — accord not war.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Britain opposed Italy's taking of Eritrea.
**Answer: False** — Italy took Eritrea with British blessing.
