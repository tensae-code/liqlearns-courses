# Ethiopia — Yohannes IV against Egypt and Italy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Yohannes win Menelik's submission?
a) By accord, not war  b) By a long siege  c) He never won it
**Answer: a) By accord, not war** — by accord not war, in the book's phrase.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose Egyptian invasions did Yohannes crush at Gundet and Gura?
a) The Mahdi's  b) Khedive Ismail's  c) Italy's
**Answer: b) Khedive Ismail's** — Khedive Ismail's invasions.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which land did Italy take, with British blessing?
a) Somalia  b) Sudan  c) Eritrea
**Answer: c) Eritrea** — Italy takes Eritrea.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Yohannes died fighting —
a) The Mahdists  b) The Egyptians at Gura  c) The Italians at Dogali
**Answer: a) The Mahdists** — he dies fighting the Mahdists.
