# Rwanda — resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga

## Do it first

King Yuhi V Musinga refuses Belgian direct rule and the loss of his power; the Belgians and the Catholic Church exile him in 1931, and he dies in exile in 1944. Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think happened — hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Musinga refuses direct rule

King Yuhi V Musinga refuses Belgian direct rule and the loss of his power. Where other rulers were made to serve the coloniser, Rwanda's king says no — and that refusal is the resistance this lesson remembers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Exile — Kamembe, then Moba, 1931

The Belgians and the Catholic Church exile Musinga to Kamembe, then to Moba, in 1931. A king removed from his own country is the price Rwanda's book puts on resistance.

## Sub lesson 3 — Death in exile, 1944 — and what the resistance cost

Musinga dies in exile in 1944, never restored. The P5 guide's resistance lessons add the wider cost: the king's power lowered and Rwandans divided by the Belgians — resistance is taught with its consequences, not as a victory parade.
