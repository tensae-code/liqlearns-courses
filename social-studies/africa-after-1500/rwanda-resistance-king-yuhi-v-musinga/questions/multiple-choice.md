# Rwanda — resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Rwandan king who resisted Belgian direct rule was —
a) Rudahigwa  b) Yuhi V Musinga  c) Kayibanda
**Answer: b) Yuhi V Musinga** — King Yuhi V Musinga refuses direct rule.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Musinga was exiled in —
a) 1900  b) 1931  c) 1962
**Answer: b) 1931** — He is exiled to Kamembe then Moba in 1931.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Musinga died —
a) On his throne in Rwanda  b) In exile, in 1944  c) In battle in 1931
**Answer: b) In exile, in 1944** — He dies in exile in 1944.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Musinga resisted because Belgium —
a) Offered him more power  b) Took direct rule and lowered the king's power  c) Left Rwanda alone
**Answer: b) Took direct rule and lowered the king's power** — Direct rule stripped the king's power.
