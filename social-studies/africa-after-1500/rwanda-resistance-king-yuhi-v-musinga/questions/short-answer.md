# Rwanda — resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell Musinga's story in order: refusal, exile, death.
**Answer: He refuses Belgian direct rule; the Belgians and the Catholic Church exile him to Kamembe then Moba in 1931; he dies in exile in 1944 (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What did resistance cost, in the guide's lessons?
**Answer: The king's power was lowered, Rwandans were divided by the Belgians, and the king himself was exiled (any close wording).**
