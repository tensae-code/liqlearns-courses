# Rwanda — resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Musinga welcomed Belgian direct rule.
**Answer: False** — He refuses direct rule and the loss of his power.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Catholic Church played a part in Musinga's exile, in Rwanda's book.
**Answer: True** — The book says the Belgians and the Catholic Church exile him in 1931.
