# Lesson plan — Rwanda — resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Rwanda — resistance: King Yuhi V Musinga
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Rwanda, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** King Yuhi V Musinga refuses Belgian direct rule and the loss of his power; the Belgians and the Catholic Church exile him in 1931, and he dies in exile in 1944.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Musinga refuses direct rule** — King Yuhi V Musinga refuses Belgian direct rule and the loss of his power.
2. **Exile — Kamembe, then Moba, 1931** — The Belgians and the Catholic Church exile Musinga to Kamembe, then to Moba, in 1931.
3. **Death in exile, 1944 — and what the resistance cost** — Musinga dies in exile in 1944, never restored.
