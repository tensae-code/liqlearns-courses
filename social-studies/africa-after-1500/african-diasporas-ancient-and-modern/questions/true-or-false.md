# African diasporas, ancient and modern — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africans scattered by slavery, migration and exile form diasporas — ancient, modern and contemporary
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (UA9 Diasporas anciennes, modernes et contemporaines).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The diasporas include the brain drain — fuite des cerveaux — and the contributions diasporas make to africa and the world
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (UA9 Fuite des cerveaux; contributions des diasporas).
