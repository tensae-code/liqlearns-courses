# African diasporas, ancient and modern

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Africans scattered by slavery, migration and exile keep building the modern world and stay tied to home. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Diasporas ancient, modern and contemporary

Africans scattered by slavery, migration and exile form diasporas — ancient, modern and contemporary (UA9 Diasporas anciennes, modernes et contemporaines)

In your mind's eye: the African diaspora across the world — communities abroad still tied to Africa. Name one contribution of the diasporas.
