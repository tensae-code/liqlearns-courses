# Hands-on — Sudan under Turco-Egyptian rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sudan under Turco-Egyptian rule — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Administration under Muhammad Ali Pasha; Sudan under Abbas and Muhammad Said; The Era of Khedive Ismail, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
