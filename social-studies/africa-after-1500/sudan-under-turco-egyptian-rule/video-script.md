# Video script — Sudan under Turco-Egyptian rule

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

occupation doubles as state-building; the Sudan that exists today is drawn on the map in these sixty years.

the occupier's schools are also the seedbed of the men who will end the occupation.

before the Mahdi can be understood, the sixty years that made him necessary are retold.

occupation as state-building — the map of modern Sudan is drawn by the men its people learned to hate.

reform and reaction alternate, and every change in Cairo is felt on the Nile within the year.

an empire that claims to fight slavery grows by the slave-traders' own roads, until it rules from Dongola to the lakes.

the machine of the Turkiyya examined part by part, as the sultanates were before it.

a balance sheet of empire, in which everything Sudan has — including its sons — is priced and taken.
