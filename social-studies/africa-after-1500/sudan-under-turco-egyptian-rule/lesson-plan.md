# Lesson plan — Sudan under Turco-Egyptian rule

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Sudan under Turco-Egyptian rule
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Sudan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** occupation doubles as state-building; the Sudan that exists today is drawn on the map in these sixty years
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Administration under Muhammad Ali Pasha; Sudan under Abbas and Muhammad Said; The Era of Khedive Ismail** — occupation doubles as state-building; the Sudan that exists today is drawn on the map in these sixty years
2. **Education in the Turco-Egyptian Era** — the occupier's schools are also the seedbed of the men who will end the occupation
3. **Review Lesson, Sudan under Turco-Egyptian Rule 1821–1885** — before the Mahdi can be understood, the sixty years that made him necessary are retold
4. **Rule and Administration in the Turco-Egyptian Era (Muhammad Ali's era, 1821–1848)** — occupation as state-building — the map of modern Sudan is drawn by the men its people learned to hate
5. **Sudan under Muhammad Ali's Successors Abbas and Said (1848–1863)** — reform and reaction alternate, and every change in Cairo is felt on the Nile within the year
6. **The Era of Khedive Ismail in Sudan (1863–1879)** — an empire that claims to fight slavery grows by the slave-traders' own roads, until it rules from Dongola to the lakes
7. **Turco-Egyptian Rule in Sudan (1) (1821–1885)** — the machine of the Turkiyya examined part by part, as the sultanates were before it
8. **Turco-Egyptian Rule in Sudan (2)** — a balance sheet of empire, in which everything Sudan has — including its sons — is priced and taken

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
