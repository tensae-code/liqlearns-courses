# Video script — Egypt — the revolutions of 2011 and 2013

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rigged elections, the emergency law and rising prices ignite the revolution of 25 January 2011 in Tahrir Square; two years later millions fill the squares again on 30 June 2013 and the roadmap removes President Morsi.
