# Egypt — the revolutions of 2011 and 2013 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Our two revolutions: 25 January 2011 and 30 June 2013 (Term 2).
**Answer: The rigged elections of 2010 that gave the ruling party ninety-seven per cent, the emergency law in force since 1981, rising prices and jobless graduates ignite a revolution organised through social media; for eighteen days Tahrir Square holds, popular committees guard the streets, the army protects the installations, and on 11 February 2011 President Mubarak steps down. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Egypt's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Prep9 full-year "Geography of the World and Modern & Contemporary History of Egypt"* (Grade 9 (Third Preparatory), both terms in one volume), Our two revolutions: 25 January 2011 and 30 June 2013 (Term 2), Term 2 printed p. 71.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Chapter 8: The revolutions of 25 January 2011 and 30 June 2013.
**Answer: The army council dissolves parliament in February 2011, the Freedom and Justice Party sweeps the elections, and Muhammad Morsi wins the presidency with fifty-one per cent; his November 2012 declaration seizes wider powers, his party fills the state's posts, debt and prices climb — until the call goes out for early elections by 30 June 2013, General al-Sisi gives one week for reconciliation, millions fill the squares on 30 June, a forty-eight-hour ultimatum runs out, and on 3 July the roadmap removes Morsi; the Rabia sit-in is dispersed in August, a new constitution passes in January 2014, and al-Sisi is elected president. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Egypt's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Sec12 "History of Egypt and the Arabs, Modern and Contemporary"* (Grade 12 (Third Secondary, Literary section)), Chapter 8: The revolutions of 25 January 2011 and 30 June 2013, printed p. 140.**
