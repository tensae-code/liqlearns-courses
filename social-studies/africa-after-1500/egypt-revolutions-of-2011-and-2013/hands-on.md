# Hands-on — Egypt — the revolutions of 2011 and 2013

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Egypt — the revolutions of 2011 and 2013 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Our two revolutions: 25 January 2011 and 30 June 2013 (Term 2), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
