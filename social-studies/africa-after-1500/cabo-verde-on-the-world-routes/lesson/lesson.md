# Cabo Verde — on the world routes

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of islands with no people on them at all — then people arriving by ship, building towns, rising in revolt, and at last proclaiming their own country. Which part of that long story do you think this lesson tells? Hold your guess; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The archipelago on the international routes

The islands become a strategic Atlantic stopping point.

In your mind's eye: sailing ships anchored below the town of Ribeira Grande on Santiago, with goods being loaded for the West African coast. Say why ships stopped here.


## Sub lesson 2 — Ribeira Grande rises

Ribeira Grande on Santiago rises fast on the trade with the nearby West African coast, with the Church at its centre.

Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
