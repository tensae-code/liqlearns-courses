# Cabo Verde — on the world routes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The islands became important because they were —
a) A strategic Atlantic stopping point  b) The largest islands in the world  c) Rich in gold mines
**Answer: a) A strategic Atlantic stopping point** — a strategic stopping point on the international routes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ribeira Grande is on which island?
a) São Vicente  b) Santiago  c) Fogo
**Answer: b) Santiago** — Ribeira Grande on Santiago.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ribeira Grande rose fast on trade with —
a) China  b) The nearby West African coast  c) Northern Europe only
**Answer: b) The nearby West African coast** — trade with the nearby West African coast.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At the centre of Ribeira Grande's rise, the book places —
a) The railway  b) The Church  c) A university
**Answer: b) The Church** — with the Church at its centre.
