# Cabo Verde — on the world routes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Ribeira Grande rise fast?
**Answer: It stood on Santiago where Atlantic ships stopped, and it traded with the nearby West African coast, with the Church at its centre (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What made the archipelago strategic?
**Answer: Its place on the international Atlantic routes — ships used the islands as a stopping point (any close wording).**
