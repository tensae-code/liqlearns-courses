# Video script — Cabo Verde — on the world routes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Cabo Verde book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The islands become a strategic Atlantic stopping point.

Ribeira Grande on Santiago rises fast on the trade with the nearby West African coast, with the Church at its centre.

Hold that story: a stopping point the world needed: ships crossing the Atlantic stopped at the islands, and Ribeira Grande grew rich and fast on that trade.
