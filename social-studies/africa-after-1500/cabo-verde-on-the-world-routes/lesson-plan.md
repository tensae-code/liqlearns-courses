# Lesson plan — Cabo Verde — on the world routes

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Cabo Verde — on the world routes
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Cabo Verde, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the islands become a strategic Atlantic stopping point, and Ribeira Grande on Santiago rises fast on the trade with the nearby West African coast, with the Church at its centre.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: a stopping point the world needed: ships crossing the Atlantic stopped at the islands, and Ribeira Grande grew rich and fast on that trade. It needs nothing else.
