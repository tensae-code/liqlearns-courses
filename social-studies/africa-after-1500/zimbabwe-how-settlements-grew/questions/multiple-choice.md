# Zimbabwe — how settlements grew — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Settlements in Zimbabwe grow in the order —
a) village, town, city  b) city, village, town  c) town, village, city
**Answer: a) village, town, city** — they grow from village to town to city

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Today's settlement pattern is —
a) the deposit of everything that came before  b) pure accident  c) planned in one year
**Answer: a) the deposit of everything that came before** — the pattern today is the deposit of everything before

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson treats where people live as —
a) a history in itself  b) geography with no past  c) a list of names to memorise
**Answer: a) a history in itself** — where people live is itself a history
