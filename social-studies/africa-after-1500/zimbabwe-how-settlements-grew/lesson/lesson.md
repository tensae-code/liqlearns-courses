# Zimbabwe — how settlements grew

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Where people live is itself a history: Zimbabwe's settlements grow from village to town to city, and their pattern today is the deposit of everything that came before. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Development of settlements in Zimbabwe (Grade 6)

Where people live is itself a history. Settlements grow — village to town to city — each stage laid over the last. The pattern on today's map is not an accident; it is the deposit of everything that came before, and reading it is reading Zimbabwe's past.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
