# Video script — Zimbabwe — how settlements grew

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Zimbabwe's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where people live is itself a history. Settlements grow — village to town to city — each stage laid over the last. The pattern on today's map is not an accident; it is the deposit of everything that came before, and reading it is reading Zimbabwe's past.
