# Video script — Abolition and the founding of Sierra Leone and Liberia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Britain declares the trade illegal first; Sierra Leone and Liberia are founded as homes for freed slaves from England, Jamaica and America, whose Creole settlers publish the Sierra Leone Weekly News and raise Bishop Ajayi Crowther.
