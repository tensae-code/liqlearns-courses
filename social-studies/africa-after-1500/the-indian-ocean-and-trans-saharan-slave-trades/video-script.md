# Video script — The Indian Ocean and trans-Saharan slave trades

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — The older trades east and north. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Long before the Atlantic trade peaked, enslaved Africans were carried east and north: across the Sahara to North Africa, across the Red Sea to Arabia, and across the Indian Ocean to Persia and beyond.

These trades ran for many centuries. Watch the names appear one by one: eastern trades, Arabia, Persia. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Routes and destinations. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Caravans crossed the desert in stages between wells; dhows rode the monsoon to the Gulf and India. Watch the names appear one by one: dhow, monsoon, property. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Why this lesson stands before the Atlantic. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Placing the eastern trades first corrects two errors: that slavery in Africa began with Europeans, and that the Atlantic was the only ocean of enslavement.

It also sharpens the Atlantic's true distinction — not that it existed, but its industrial scale, its racial plantation system, and its speed.. Watch the names appear one by one: distinction, plantation system, scale. This is the part of the story to hold.
