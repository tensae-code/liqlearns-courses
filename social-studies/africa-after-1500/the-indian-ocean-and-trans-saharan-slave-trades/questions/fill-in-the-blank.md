# The Indian Ocean and trans-Saharan slave trades — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'what enslaved people were made into by the trades' is ______.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the Atlantic peak's unmatched yearly numbers' is ______.
