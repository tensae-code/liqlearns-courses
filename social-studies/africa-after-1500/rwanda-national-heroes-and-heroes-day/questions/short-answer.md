# Rwanda — national heroes and Heroes Day — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things Rwanda's heroes are said to have done.
**Answer: Ended the genocide; freed the country from bad rule; promoted unity (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Match two heroes to their deeds.
**Answer: Rudahigwa — independence; Rwagasana — against divisionism; Sister Félicité — refused to abandon Tutsi families (any two, close wording).**
