# Rwanda — national heroes and Heroes Day — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rwanda's heroes are remembered every —
a) 1 July  b) 1 February  c) 4 July
**Answer: b) 1 February** — Heroes are remembered every 1 February (see also the Heritage lesson).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A Rwandan hero, in this book, is measured by —
a) Wealth  b) Ending the genocide, freeing the country and promoting unity  c) Winning elections
**Answer: b) Ending the genocide, freeing the country and promoting unity** — Those contributions define the heroes here.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rwagasana is remembered against —
a) Divisionism  b) Independence  c) The monarchy
**Answer: a) Divisionism** — Rwagasana against divisionism.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Sister Félicité Niyitegeka refused to —
a) Leave Rwanda  b) Abandon Tutsi families  c) Teach children
**Answer: b) Abandon Tutsi families** — She refused to abandon Tutsi families.
