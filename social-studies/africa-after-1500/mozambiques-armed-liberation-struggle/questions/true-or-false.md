# Mozambique's armed liberation struggle — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The armed liberation struggle

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *História — Módulo 3 (PESD I, Programa do Ensino Secundário à Distância – 1º Ciclo)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Faced with Lisbon's refusal to negotiate, the united front launches the armed liberation struggle.
