# Mozambique's armed liberation struggle

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The armed liberation struggle

Refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — The launch of the armed struggle

Faced with Lisbon's refusal to negotiate, the united front launches the armed liberation struggle

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: The armed liberation struggle — refused a peaceful path, Mozambicans take up arms on 25 September 1964; the liberation war begins; The launch of the armed struggle — faced with Lisbon's refusal to negotiate, the united front launches the armed liberation struggle. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
