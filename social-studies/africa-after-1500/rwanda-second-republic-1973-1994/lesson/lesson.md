# Rwanda — the Second Republic, 1973–1994

## Do it first

Habyarimana suspends the constitution, bans parties and rules through the MRND one-party state; under pressure he allows parties in 1991, and three transitional governments try to hold the country before 1994. Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think happened — hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Habyarimana and the MRND one-party state

Habyarimana suspends the constitution, bans parties and rules through the MRND one-party state. The Second Republic begins, like the first ended, with power gathered into one pair of hands.

In your mind's eye: the world of this lesson as the book shows it. Say what changed in this part of the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pressure — parties allowed in 1991

Under pressure, parties are allowed in 1991. The one-party wall cracks, but late, and into a country already tense.

## Sub lesson 3 — Three transitional governments

Three transitional governments follow, under Nsanzimana, Nsengiyaremye and Uwilingiyimana. Their names matter in Rwanda's book because the last of them, Prime Minister Uwilingiyimana, is murdered as the genocide begins.
