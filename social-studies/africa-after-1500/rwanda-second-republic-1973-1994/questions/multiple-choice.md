# Rwanda — the Second Republic, 1973–1994 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Second Republic's one party was —
a) PARMEHUTU  b) MRND  c) RPF
**Answer: b) MRND** — Habyarimana rules through the MRND.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At its start Habyarimana —
a) Held free elections  b) Suspended the constitution and banned parties  c) Restored the king
**Answer: b) Suspended the constitution and banned parties** — He suspends the constitution and bans parties.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Parties were allowed again in —
a) 1973  b) 1991  c) 1962
**Answer: b) 1991** — Parties are allowed in 1991.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The last transitional prime minister named is —
a) Nsanzimana  b) Uwilingiyimana  c) Kayibanda
**Answer: b) Uwilingiyimana** — Uwilingiyimana is the third named.
