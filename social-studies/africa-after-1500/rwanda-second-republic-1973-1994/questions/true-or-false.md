# Rwanda — the Second Republic, 1973–1994 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Second Republic was a multiparty democracy from 1973.
**Answer: False** — It is an MRND one-party state until parties are allowed in 1991.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** There were three transitional governments before 1994.
**Answer: True** — Under Nsanzimana, Nsengiyaremye and Uwilingiyimana.
