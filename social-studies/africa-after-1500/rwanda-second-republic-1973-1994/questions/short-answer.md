# Rwanda — the Second Republic, 1973–1994 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Habyarimana rule in the Second Republic?
**Answer: He suspended the constitution, banned parties and ruled through the MRND one-party state (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three transitional government leaders.
**Answer: Nsanzimana, Nsengiyaremye and Uwilingiyimana (any close wording).**
