# Zimbabwe — the First Chimurenga, 1896–97

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In 1896–97 Shona and Ndebele, old rivals, rose together against the British South Africa Company — land and cattle taken, labour forced, taxes imposed, drought and cattle disease on top — and were beaten by superior guns: the First Chimurenga, Zimbabwe's first liberation war. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The First Chimurenga/Umvukela, 1896–97 (Grade 6)

The grievances pile up: land and cattle taken, labour forced, taxes imposed — and then drought and cattle disease on top. In 1896–97 the Shona and the Ndebele, old rivals, rise together against the British South Africa Company. They are beaten by superior guns — but the rising has a name that never dies: the First Chimurenga.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: the first chimurenga/umvukela, 1896–97. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
