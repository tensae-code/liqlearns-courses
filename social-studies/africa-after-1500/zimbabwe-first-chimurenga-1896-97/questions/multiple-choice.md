# Zimbabwe — the First Chimurenga, 1896–97 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The First Chimurenga rose against —
a) the British South Africa Company  b) the Mutapa state  c) the Portuguese in 1500
**Answer: a) the British South Africa Company** — the rising is against the British South Africa Company

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two peoples who rose together were —
a) the Shona and the Ndebele  b) the Tonga and the Chewa  c) the Venda and the Ndau
**Answer: a) the Shona and the Ndebele** — Shona and Ndebele, old rivals, rise together

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The rising was beaten by —
a) superior guns  b) drought alone  c) a signed treaty
**Answer: a) superior guns** — they are beaten by superior guns
