# Zimbabwe — the First Chimurenga, 1896–97 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Shona and Ndebele fought each other during 1896–97 instead of the Company.
**Answer: False** — old rivals rose together against the Company

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Forced labour and taxes were among the rising's causes.
**Answer: True** — labour forced and taxes imposed are named grievances
