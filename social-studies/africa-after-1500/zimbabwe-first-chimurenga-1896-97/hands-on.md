# Hands-on — Zimbabwe — the First Chimurenga, 1896–97

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the grievances that lit the rising — land, cattle, labour, taxes, drought, disease — and rank them as a fighter of 1896 might.

## Task 2
Write a two-voice account: a Shona fighter and an Ndebele fighter explain why old rivals now stand together.
