# Eritrea — Egyptian expansion and resistance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Munzinger led Egypt's advance into the Barka and Keren.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 8 book names Munzinger.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt conquered the Eritrean highlands in 1875–76.
**Answer: False** — its highland drive was broken at Gundet and Gura.
