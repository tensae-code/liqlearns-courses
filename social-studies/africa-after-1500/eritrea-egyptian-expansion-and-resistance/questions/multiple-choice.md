# Eritrea — Egyptian expansion and resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's advance reached Keren in —
a) 1840  b) 1874  c) 1890
**Answer: b) 1874** — Kassala 1840, Massawa 1846, Keren 1874.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The highland invasion broke at —
a) Dogali and Adwa  b) Gundet and Gura  c) Afabet and Massawa
**Answer: b) Gundet and Gura** — Gundet and Gura, 1875–76.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Woldemichael Solomon captured —
a) 700 rifles  b) the port of Massawa  c) the Khedive
**Answer: a) 700 rifles** — 700 rifles, following the Egyptian retreat.
