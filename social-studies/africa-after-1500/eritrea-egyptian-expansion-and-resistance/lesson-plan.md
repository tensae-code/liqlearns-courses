# Lesson plan — Eritrea — Egyptian expansion and resistance

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — Egyptian expansion and resistance
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Egypt pushed from the Sudan — Kassala 1840, Massawa 1846, Keren 1874 — until Khedive Ismail's highland invasion broke at Gundet and Gura in 1875–76, and Woldemichael Solomon followed the retreat and captured 700 rifles.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Egyptian advance** — Egypt pushes from the Sudan — Kassala 1840, Massawa 1846, Keren 1874 — until its highland invasion breaks in 1875–76 (The Egyptian advance)
2. **Egyptian Expansion and Eritrean Resistance** — Muhammad Ali's Egypt swallows the Sudan, Kassala, the Barka and Keren under Munzinger, until Khedive Ismail's highland drive is broken at Gundet and Gura in 1875–76, where Woldemichael Solomon follows the retreat and captures 700 rifles (Egyptian Expansion and Eritrean Resistance)
