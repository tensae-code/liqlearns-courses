The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Egypt pushes from the Sudan — Kassala 1840, Massawa 1846, Keren 1874 — until its highland invasion breaks in 1875–76.

Muhammad Ali's Egypt swallows the Sudan, Kassala, the Barka and Keren under Munzinger, until Khedive Ismail's highland drive is broken at Gundet and Gura in 1875–76, where Woldemichael Solomon follows the retreat and captures 700 rifles.
