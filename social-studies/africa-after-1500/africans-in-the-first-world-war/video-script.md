# Video script — Africans in the First World War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A European war reaches into Africa — Africans are drawn in as soldiers, carriers and victims on their own soil.
The First World War worsens living conditions in Mozambique, and the economic and social crisis of the 1920s follows the war home.
