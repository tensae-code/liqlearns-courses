# Africans in the First World War

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A European war reaches into Africa — Africans are drawn in as soldiers, carriers and victims on their own soil. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Africans in the First World War — a European war reaches into Africa — Africans are drawn in as soldiers, carriers and victims on their own soil; The First World War and the crisis of the 1920s in Mozambique — the First World War worsens living conditions in Mozambique, and the economic and social crisis of the 1920s follows the war home. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
