# Lesson plan — Eritrea — Turkish expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — Turkish expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ozdemir Pasha took Massawa in 1557 and pushed for the highlands; Bahri Negassi Yeshaq first barred him, then allied with the Turks against Abyssinia's King Minas, and was killed fighting King Sertse-Dengel in 1578 — after which Turkish rule shrank back to the coast and the Naibs.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
