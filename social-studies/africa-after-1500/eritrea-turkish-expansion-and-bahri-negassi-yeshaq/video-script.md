# Video script — Eritrea — Turkish expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ozdemir Pasha takes Massawa in 1557 and pushes for the highlands; Bahri Negassi Yeshaq first bars him, then allies with the Turks against Abyssinia's King Minas, and is killed fighting King Sertse-Dengel in 1578 — after which Turkish rule shrinks back to the coast and the Naibs.
