# Eritrea — Turkish expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yeshaq first barred the Turks, then allied with them against King Minas.
**Answer: True** — the book tells both turns of his story.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Turks conquered the highlands after 1578.
**Answer: False** — their rule shrank back to the coast.
