# Eritrea — Turkish expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ozdemir Pasha took Massawa in —
a) 1557  b) 1578  c) 1840
**Answer: a) 1557** — 1557.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bahri Negassi Yeshaq died fighting —
a) King Sertse-Dengel, in 1578  b) the Egyptians at Gura  c) Ras Alula
**Answer: a) King Sertse-Dengel, in 1578** — fighting King Sertse-Dengel in 1578.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After Yeshaq's death, Turkish rule —
a) took the highlands  b) shrank back to the coast and the Naibs  c) ended at once
**Answer: b) shrank back to the coast and the Naibs** — it shrank back to the coast, ruled through the Naibs.
