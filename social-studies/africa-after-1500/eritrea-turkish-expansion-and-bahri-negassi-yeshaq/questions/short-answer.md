# Eritrea — Turkish expansion and Bahri Negassi Yeshaq — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Yeshaq change sides, as the book tells it?
**Answer: He first barred Ozdemir Pasha, then made common cause with the Turks against Abyssinia's King Minas (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What followed Yeshaq's death in 1578?
**Answer: Turkish rule shrank back to the coast and the rule of the Naibs began (any close wording).**
