# Video script — Shaka and the Zulu Kingdom

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Shaka's military revolution. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
In southern Africa, Shaka transformed Nguni warfare: close-order fighters with short stabbing spears and great shields, drilled regiments, and tactics that closed with the enemy instead of skirmishing at a distance.

With that army he built the Zulu kingdom out of many small chiefdoms — by conquest, absorption and fear — in little more than a decade.. Watch the names appear one by one: Shaka, stabbing spear, regiments. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Building a kingdom. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Zulu kingdom was more than an army: conquered peoples were incorporated, regiments doubled as labour and loyalty schools, and the king's homesteads anchored a new political geography.

State-building at speed — the same vocabulary of territory, tribute and legitimacy this course uses everywhere.. Watch the names appear one by one: incorporation, homestead, legitimacy. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — The Mfecane — a subcontinent reshaped. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Shaka's wars set peoples moving across southern Africa — fleeing, conquering, founding new states far away. Watch the names appear one by one: Mfecane, subcontinent, new states. This is the part of the story to hold.
