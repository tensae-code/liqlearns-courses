# Shaka and the Zulu Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Shaka?
a) stabbing spear
b) the builder of the Zulu kingdom through military revolution
c) regiments
**Answer: b) the builder of the Zulu kingdom through military revolution**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is incorporation?
a) absorbing conquered peoples into the kingdom
b) homestead
c) legitimacy
**Answer: a) absorbing conquered peoples into the kingdom**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Mfecane?
a) the upheaval of movements and state-foundings set off by the Zulu wars
b) subcontinent
c) new states
**Answer: a) the upheaval of movements and state-foundings set off by the Zulu wars**
