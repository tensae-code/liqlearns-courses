# Shaka and the Zulu Kingdom — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the drilled age-based formations of the Zulu army' is ______.
**Answer: regiments**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the accepted right to rule, which Shaka built as well as seized' is ______.
**Answer: legitimacy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the kingdoms founded far away by peoples set moving' is ______.
**Answer: new states**
