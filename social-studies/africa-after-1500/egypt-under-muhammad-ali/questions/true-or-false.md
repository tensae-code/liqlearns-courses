# Egypt under Muhammad Ali — building the modern state — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crushed by Khurshid Pasha's taxes and looting soldiers, Egypt's sheikhs and people choose Muhammad Ali as wali in 1805 — "we accept only you, on our conditions"; he survives the attempt to transfer him to Salonica in 1806, defeats Fraser's British campaign with the people's resistance at Rosetta in 1807, exiles the popular leader Omar Makram in 1809, and destroys the Mamluks in the Citadel massacre of 1811.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Muhammad Ali takes power in Egypt

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Prep9 T1 "Egypt and the Continents of the New World"* is about Muhammad Ali builds the modern state.
**Answer: False** — it is about Muhammad Ali takes power in Egypt

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Seven directorates under Cairo and advisory councils; a new army — when his Albanians mutiny and the Sudanese experiment fails in Egypt's climate, he conscripts Egyptians and has French officers train them; schools of medicine, engineering and languages, missions to Europe and the Bulaq press; the iltizam tax-farms are abolished, a state monopoly imposed, long-staple cotton planted and the Delta Barrage begun.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Muhammad Ali builds the modern state

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Prep9 T1 "Egypt and the Continents of the New World"* is about Egypt under Muhammad Ali's successors.
**Answer: False** — it is about Muhammad Ali builds the modern state

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Abbas I closes most schools and factories; Said gives the fellah the right to bequeath his land, cuts military service to one year and opens promotion to Egyptian officers — but grants De Lesseps the Suez concessions of 1854 and 1856 and takes Egypt's first foreign loan in 1862; Ismail doubles the education budget, digs the Ibrahimiyya and Ismailia canals and rebuilds Cairo with Abdeen Palace and the Opera House — on borrowed money.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Egypt under Muhammad Ali's successors

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Prep9 T1 "Egypt and the Continents of the New World"* is about The Egyptian people's revolution, Muhammad Ali's accession, and building modern Egypt.
**Answer: False** — it is about Egypt under Muhammad Ali's successors

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The people and their leaders raise Muhammad Ali to power in 1805 and he builds the modern state — the same story told in this older book's own words, with the Fraser campaign, Omar Makram and the Citadel at its centre.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Egyptian people's revolution, Muhammad Ali's accession, and building modern Egypt

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Prep9 full-year "Geography of the World and Modern & Contemporary History of Egypt"* is about Muhammad Ali's successors and the growth of foreign influence.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Egyptian people's revolution, Muhammad Ali's accession, and building modern Egypt

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Said grants De Lesseps the Suez concessions of 1854 and 1856 — ninety-nine years, four-fifths Egyptian labour, fifteen per cent of profits — and takes the first foreign loan in 1862; digging begins at Port Said in 1859; Ismail's renaissance — the Ibrahimiyya canal, sugar factories, the Saniyya girls' school of 1873, the Shura Council of Deputies of 1866 — is paid for with loans, the canal's opening celebrations of 17 November 1869 deepen the crisis, consular courts and capitulations tighten the foreign grip, and Egyptian expeditions reach the equator and the whole Red Sea coast by 1875.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Muhammad Ali's successors and the growth of foreign influence

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Prep9 full-year "Geography of the World and Modern & Contemporary History of Egypt"* is about Chapter 2: Building the modern state in Egypt.
**Answer: False** — it is about Muhammad Ali's successors and the growth of foreign influence

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** Out of the campaign's wreckage Muhammad Ali and his house build the modern Egyptian state — the nineteenth-century transformation the book's introduction promises: its economy, army, schools and society remade.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 2: Building the modern state in Egypt

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** This part of *Sec12 "History of Egypt and the Arabs, Modern and Contemporary"* is about Muhammad Ali takes power in Egypt.
**Answer: False** — it is about Chapter 2: Building the modern state in Egypt
