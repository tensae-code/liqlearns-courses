# Video script — Egypt under Muhammad Ali — building the modern state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Chosen by Egypt's sheikhs and people in 1805, Muhammad Ali survives his enemies, destroys the Mamluks in 1811, and builds the modern state — army, schools, press, canals and cotton — and his successors trade its future for loans and the Suez Canal.
