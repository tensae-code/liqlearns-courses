# Lesson plan — After the flag — debt, drought and the integration answer

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** After the flag — debt, drought and the integration answer
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — the post-liberation unit; Grade 10 Unit 7 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** political independence did not bring economic independence: growth from 1965 to 1974 gave way to the debt-and-drought decades — the 1980s the "lost decade" — and African leaders' counter-move was regional economic integration.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the liberation lessons — this is what independence had to govern. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Growth, then the lost decade** — substantial growth 1965–1974, then downward trend, GDP barely keeping pace with population; debt and drought the principal adverse factors of the 1980s–90s.
2. **The integration answer** — regional economic integration: the regional economic organisations African leaders formed to integrate their economies — the counter-move to small, exposed national markets.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the growth arc: 1965–74 up, then down, GDP barely pacing population.
2. Name the two principal adverse factors of the 1980s–90s.
3. State the counter-move: regional economic integration and its instruments.
