# Hands-on — After the flag — debt, drought and the integration answer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The sagging line
Draw the GDP-versus-population race as two lines, 1965–1995: totals rising, per-person share flattening. Mark 1974, the lost decade, and the later-1990s recovery.

## One market or many?
List three goods your town imports and one it exports. Now redraw the map with your country inside a regional bloc: what changes for each good? Say the integration argument in your own example.
