# After the flag — debt, drought and the integration answer

## Do it first

The flags went up, the anthems were sung — and the economies the new states inherited kept facing outward, priced in other people's currencies, hostage to rain and to lenders. This lesson asks the hardest post-independence question: what is a flag worth, economically — and what did Africa's leaders build to make it worth more?

## Sub lesson 1 — Growth, then the lost decade

The book's economic verdict begins hopefully: political independence did not bring economic independence, but the first full decade held real growth — Africa grew substantially from 1965 to 1974. Then the trend turned downward: GDP barely kept pace with population growth, meaning the average African's share stood still or shrank even where the totals rose.

Two principal adverse factors drove the hard decades: international debt — borrowed development money returning as an obligation that consumed budgets — and drought, the rains failing across the continent's drylands. The 1980s earned their name in consequence: the "lost decade", with only modest recovery arriving in the later 1990s. The liberation generation's children inherited states whose economies the world market and the weather jointly disciplined.

## Sub lesson 2 — The integration answer

The counter-move taught here is regional economic integration. The new states were mostly small markets, each exporting raw materials in competition with its neighbours and importing manufactures at prices set elsewhere. African leaders formed regional economic organisations to integrate their economies — pooling markets, coordinating infrastructure and bargaining together — so that the continent's fragmentation, itself a colonial inheritance, would stop taxing every African transaction.

Integration is the economic form of the unit's political lesson: as Pan-Africanism united the liberation struggle, regional integration was to unite the peace that followed it. Whether the organisations matched the ambition is the pupil's judgement to practise — the mechanism, and the reason for it, are the lesson's content.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the arc with dates, the two adverse factors, the lost decade's name, and the integration answer with its reason.
