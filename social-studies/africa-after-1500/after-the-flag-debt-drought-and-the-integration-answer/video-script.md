# Video script — After the flag — debt, drought and the integration answer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the independence decade. A growth line climbs across the flags, 1965 to 1974.
The line sags. Two shadows fall across the map — a debt ledger growing heavier, and rain clouds that do not come. The 1980s pass in a dimmer colour.
Then the map's internal borders begin to glow and link: regional blocs drawing their member states into single markets.
The growth line, joined now across regions, steadies — modestly, in the later 1990s. The lesson's question hangs over it: can unity do in peace what it did in liberation?
