# Zimbabwe's heroes — from Nehanda to the liberation war

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Zimbabwe's heroes run from the district's own remembered names up to three great faces — Mbuya Nehanda, the spirit medium who mobilised the First Chimurenga and was executed for it, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara — with Sekuru Kaguvi and Mukwati beside Nehanda in the war of 1896–97. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Local heroes and heroines (Grade 3)

Heroes shrink from the national acre to the district — the brave people of the child's own place — then grow back through three faces: Mbuya Nehanda, the brave woman who fought for freedom, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara. Children draw them proud and brave for the classroom wall.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Spirit mediums and heroes of the First Chimurenga (Grade 6)

The war of 1896–97 is mobilised by the spirits. Mbuya Nehanda, Sekuru Kaguvi and Mukwati predict battles, protect fighters and urge the people on — and Nehanda and Kaguvi are executed for it. In Zimbabwe's telling, the mediums are not background to the war; they are among its leaders.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: local heroes and heroines; spirit mediums and heroes of the first chimurenga. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
