# Zimbabwe's heroes — from Nehanda to the liberation war — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The heroes lesson at Grade 3 begins with —
a) the district's own local heroes and heroines  b) foreign kings  c) sports stars
**Answer: a) the district's own local heroes and heroines** — heroes shrink from the national acre to the district

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mbuya Nehanda is remembered as —
a) the brave woman who fought for freedom  b) a queen of Egypt  c) a Portuguese trader
**Answer: a) the brave woman who fought for freedom** — Nehanda is the brave woman who fought for freedom

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the First Chimurenga, the spirit mediums —
a) predicted battles, protected fighters and urged the people on  b) signed the Rudd Concession  c) built the railways
**Answer: a) predicted battles, protected fighters and urged the people on** — the mediums mobilised the war — predicting, protecting, urging on

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Nehanda and Kaguvi were —
a) executed for their part in the war  b) made governors  c) sent to London as guests
**Answer: a) executed for their part in the war** — Nehanda and Kaguvi are executed for it
