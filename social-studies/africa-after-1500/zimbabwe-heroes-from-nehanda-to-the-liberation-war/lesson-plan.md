# Lesson plan — Zimbabwe's heroes — from Nehanda to the liberation war

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Zimbabwe's heroes — from Nehanda to the liberation war
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Zimbabwe, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Zimbabwe's heroes run from the district's own remembered names up to three great faces — Mbuya Nehanda, the spirit medium who mobilised the First Chimurenga and was executed for it, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara — with Sekuru Kaguvi and Mukwati beside Nehanda in the war of 1896–97.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Local heroes and heroines (Grade 3)** — Heroes shrink from the national acre to the district — the brave people of the child's own place — then grow back through three faces: Mbuya Nehanda, the brave woman who fought for freedom, Joshua Nkomo, and Josiah Tongogara. (Topic 2, Unit 8 Local heroes and heroines)
2. **Spirit mediums and heroes of the First Chimurenga (Grade 6)** — The war of 1896–97 is mobilised by the spirits. (Topic 2, Unit 8 The liberation history of Zimbabwe — spirit mediums and heroes of the First Chimurenga)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
