# Hands-on — Zimbabwe's heroes — from Nehanda to the liberation war

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw one hero or heroine “proud and brave” for the classroom wall, and write two lines on what they did.

## Task 2
Make a hero timeline: place Nehanda and Kaguvi in 1896–97 and Tongogara and Nkomo in the liberation war, with one line for each.
