# Video script — The mineral revolution in South Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Diamonds at Kimberley in 1867, two years before Suez, reverse the interior's fortunes — Africans had been doing better than the white interior communities; now Kimberley and Johannesburg rise, slums with them, and the newcomers are named Uitlanders.
