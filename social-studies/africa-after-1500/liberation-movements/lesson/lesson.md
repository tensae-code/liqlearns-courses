# Liberation movements

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Resistance grows into organised liberation movements that aim at independence. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Resistance becomes organisation

resistance grows into organised liberation movements (UA6 Les mouvements de libération)

In your mind's eye: an organised liberation movement — people meeting, organising and marching toward independence. Say what the movement aims at.

## Sub lesson 2 — Aiming at independence

the liberation movements aim at independence for their peoples (UA6 Les mouvements de libération)

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the lesson as its beats: Resistance becomes organisation — resistance grows into organised liberation movements (UA6 Les mouvements de libération); Aiming at independence — the liberation movements aim at independence for their peoples (UA6 Les mouvements de libération). Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
