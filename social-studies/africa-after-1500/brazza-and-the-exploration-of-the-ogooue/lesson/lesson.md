# Brazza and the exploration of the Ogooué

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Brazza ascends the Ogooué river and signs the treaties that open Gabon and Congo to France. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Brazza ascends the Ogooué

Brazza ascends the Ogooué and explores the river into Gabon's interior (Leçon 24 De Brazza et l'exploration de l'Ogooué)

In your mind's eye: brazza travelling up the Ogooué river by canoe through the forest. Say which river Brazza explored.
