# Lesson plan — Gabon in French Congo and the AÉF

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Gabon in French Congo and the AÉF
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Gabon, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Gabon is folded into French Congo and then regrouped as one territory of four in French Equatorial Africa (AÉF), ruled from outside its own borders.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Gabon in French Congo** — Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders (Leçon 36 Le Gabon dans le Congo français)
2. **From French Congo to French Equatorial Africa** — the colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa, with Gabon one territory among four (Leçon 37 Du Congo français à l'Afrique équatoriale française)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
