# Gabon in French Congo and the AÉF — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Gabon in French Congo  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Gabon in French Congo** — Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide this part is —
a) Leçon 36 Le Gabon dans le Congo français  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Leçon 36 Le Gabon dans le Congo français** — the guide's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) From French Congo to French Equatorial Africa  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) From French Congo to French Equatorial Africa** — the colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa, with Gabon one territory among four.
