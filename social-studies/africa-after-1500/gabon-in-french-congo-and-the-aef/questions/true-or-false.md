# Gabon in French Congo and the AÉF — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 36 Le Gabon dans le Congo français).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa, with Gabon one territory among four
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 37 Du Congo français à l'Afrique équatoriale française).
