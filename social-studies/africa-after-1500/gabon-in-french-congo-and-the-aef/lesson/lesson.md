# Gabon in French Congo and the AÉF

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Gabon is folded into French Congo and then regrouped as one territory of four in French Equatorial Africa (AÉF), ruled from outside its own borders. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gabon in French Congo

Gabon is folded into a larger French colony and ruled from outside its own borders (Leçon 36 Le Gabon dans le Congo français)

In your mind's eye: a map of French Equatorial Africa showing Gabon as one territory among four. Say how many territories the AÉF had.

## Sub lesson 2 — From French Congo to French Equatorial Africa

The colonies are regrouped as French Equatorial Africa, with Gabon one territory among four (Leçon 37 Du Congo français à l'Afrique équatoriale française)
