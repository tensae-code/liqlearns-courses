# Hands-on — Problems of the newly independent states

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Problems of the newly independent states — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Lesson 12: The problems of the states newly come to independence, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
