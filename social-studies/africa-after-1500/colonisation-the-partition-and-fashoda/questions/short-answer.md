# Colonisation — the partition and Fashoda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which northern lands were already in European hands when Berlin closed?
**Answer:** Egypt (Britain, 1882), and Algeria and Tunisia (France).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell the Fashoda story in three steps.
**Answer:** Kitchener's joint army defeated the Mahdists at Omdurman in 1898; a week later Marchand's French force was reported at Fashoda; the crisis between Britain and France was settled peacefully by diplomacy.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Germany gain in its fast expansion?
**Answer:** South-West Africa (today's Namibia) and Tanganyika.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which three powers does the book name competing after Berlin, besides France?
**Answer:** Britain, Germany and Belgium competed likewise.
