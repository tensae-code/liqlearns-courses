# Colonisation — the partition and Fashoda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When Berlin closed, Egypt was already held by —
a) France  b) Britain (from 1882)  c) Germany  d) Belgium
**Answer: b) Britain (from 1882)** — Britain took Egypt in 1882.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After Berlin, France consolidated West Africa by taking —
a) Dahomey, Ivory Coast and Guinea  b) Only Senegal  c) Nigeria and Ghana  d) Tanganyika
**Answer: a) Dahomey, Ivory Coast and Guinea** — Dahomey, Ivory Coast and Guinea.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At Omdurman in 1898 Kitchener defeated —
a) The French  b) The Mahdists  c) The Ashanti  d) The Germans
**Answer: b) The Mahdists** — The joint British-Egyptian army defeated the Mahdists.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Marchand's French force had reached —
a) Fashoda  b) Omdurman  c) Cape Town  d) Accra
**Answer: a) Fashoda** — Marchand reached Fashoda, a week after Omdurman.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Fashoda crisis was settled —
a) By a great battle  b) Peacefully, by diplomacy  c) By the Berlin Act  d) It never was
**Answer: b) Peacefully, by diplomacy** — Settled peacefully by diplomacy.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Germany's two fast gains were —
a) Egypt and Sudan  b) South-West Africa (Namibia) and Tanganyika  c) Senegal and Guinea  d) Libya and Algeria
**Answer: b) South-West Africa (Namibia) and Tanganyika** — Namibia and Tanganyika.
