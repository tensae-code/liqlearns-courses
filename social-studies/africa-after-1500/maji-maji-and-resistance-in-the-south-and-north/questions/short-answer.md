# Maji-Maji — and resistance in the south and north — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did the maji water solve the resistance's hardest problem?
**Answer:** The problem was unity — separate peoples with separate chiefs. Ngwale's maji gave them one shared belief and one shared movement, uniting peoples across the south against the common grievances of taxation and forced labour.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the unit's two-stage pattern of African resistance.
**Answer:** First, primary resistance — armed risings against conquest, like Samori's war and Maji-Maji; then, in the colonial period proper, organised movements — the associations, unions, press and parties that grew toward liberation.
