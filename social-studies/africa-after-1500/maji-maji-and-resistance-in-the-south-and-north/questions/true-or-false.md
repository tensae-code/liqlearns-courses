# Maji-Maji — and resistance in the south and north — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forced labour in Tanganyika included compulsory cotton-growing and road-building.
**Answer: True** — Both are named in the book.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Maji-Maji was a minor protest of a single village.
**Answer: False** — It was the most significant African challenge to German colonial rule, uniting peoples across the south.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Germany held no colonies in Africa besides Tanganyika.
**Answer: False** — Germany held four: Tanganyika, Togo, Cameroon and Namibia.
