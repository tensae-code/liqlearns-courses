# Maji-Maji — and resistance in the south and north — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Maji-Maji uprising rose in —
a) British Kenya  b) German Tanganyika  c) French Senegal  d) Portuguese Mozambique
**Answer: b) German Tanganyika** — German Tanganyika, 1905–1907.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its causes included —
a) High taxation and forced labour  b) A drought only  c) Missionary schools  d) Railway building by choice
**Answer: a) High taxation and forced labour** — High taxation and forced labour — compulsory cotton-growing and road-building.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The prophet who united the movement was —
a) Prempeh  b) Kinjikitle Ngwale  c) Samori Ture  d) Ahmadu Seku
**Answer: b) Kinjikitle Ngwale** — Kinjikitle Ngwale led the movement.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Maji-Maji uprising ran —
a) 1882–1898  b) 1896–1900  c) 1905–1907  d) 1914–1918
**Answer: c) 1905–1907** — 1905 to 1907.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Germany's four African colonies were —
a) Tanganyika, Togo, Cameroon, Namibia  b) Kenya, Uganda, Ghana, Nigeria  c) Algeria, Tunisia, Libya, Egypt  d) Angola, Mozambique, Guinea, Cape Verde
**Answer: a) Tanganyika, Togo, Cameroon, Namibia** — Tanganyika, Togo, Cameroon and Namibia.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The unit's pattern of resistance runs —
a) Organised movements first, then primary resistance  b) Primary resistance first, then the organised movements of the colonial period  c) Only religious movements  d) Only trade unions
**Answer: b) Primary resistance first, then the organised movements of the colonial period** — Primary resistance first, then organised movements.
