# Video script — Maji-Maji — and resistance in the south and north


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on East Africa — Tanganyika under the German flag.

Tax collectors count hut taxes. Labour gangs bend over cotton rows they did not choose, and roads they will not own.

Then a prophet walks the southern villages with a calabash of water — maji. Drink, and bullets will turn to water.

Village joins village, people joins people — one rising, 1905!

For two years the south burns with it, the greatest challenge Germany faced in Africa.

The rising is crushed. But the pattern is set: resist first with arms — then organise. The movements are coming.
