# Lesson plan — Maji-Maji — and resistance in the south and north

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa After 1500 · **Lesson:** Maji-Maji — and resistance in the south and north
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 2 — Africa and the Colonial Experience 1880s–1960s (Grade 10 History).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** East Africa's exemplar is the **Maji-Maji uprising (1905–1907)** in German Tanganyika — against **high taxation and forced labour**, the prophet **Kinjikitle Ngwale** united peoples across the south with the maji water; the unit completes the pattern with southern and northern resistance: primary resistance first, then the organised movements of the colonial period.
**This lesson's own level:** it follows the West Africa lesson — the resistance survey crosses the continent. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Taxes, forced labour — and a prophet** — State Maji-Maji's causes: high taxation and forced labour (compulsory cotton-growing and road-building) in German Tanganyika; and name Kinjikitle Ngwale, whose movement united peoples across the south.
2. **The rising of 1905–1907 — and the pattern of a continent** — Tell the rising as the most significant African challenge to German colonial rule (1905–1907); name Germany's four African colonies; and state the unit's pattern: primary resistance first, then the organised movements of the colonial period proper.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State Maji-Maji's causes and its dates.
2. Name Kinjikitle Ngwale and what the maji water did for the movement.
3. Name Germany's four African colonies and state the unit's resistance pattern.
