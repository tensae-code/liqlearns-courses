# Video script — Salazar's Estado Novo and Mozambique

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it.
In the first phase (1890–1930) international companies and South African mining capital, not Portugal, ran Mozambique's economy; 1930–1937 lays the bases of Salazar's economic nationalism.
