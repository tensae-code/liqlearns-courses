## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A 1926 coup brings Salazar's Estado Novo; he restructures the colonies and nationalises the economy for Portugal's bourgeoisie, and Mozambique feels it. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
