# Decolonisation — North Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Libya was placed by the UN under British trusteeship to 1949.
**Answer: True** — Trusteeship to 1949; independence 1952 (as printed).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Morocco was split between France and Spain.
**Answer: True** — France signed independence in 1956 and Spain freed its zone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Algeria's provisional government in exile sat in Cairo.
**Answer: True** — The FLN's government in exile was in Cairo.
