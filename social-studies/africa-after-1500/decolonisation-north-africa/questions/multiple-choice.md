# Decolonisation — North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's Wafd Party was led by —
a) Habib Bourguiba  b) Saad Zaghlul  c) Ahmed Ben Bella  d) Muhammad V
**Answer: b) Saad Zaghlul** — Saad Zaghlul's Wafd Party.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sudan's independence was declared in —
a) 1952  b) January 1956  c) 1962  d) 1945
**Answer: b) January 1956** — January 1956, after the 1953 transition.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Morocco's first king of the united country was —
a) Bourguiba  b) Sultan Muhammad V  c) Ben Bella  d) Nasser
**Answer: b) Sultan Muhammad V** — Sultan Muhammad V became first king.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tunisia's first president was —
a) Habib Bourguiba  b) Muhammad Inafa  c) Saad Zaghlul  d) Marchand
**Answer: a) Habib Bourguiba** — Bourguiba, at independence in 1956.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Algeria's struggle was led from 1954 by —
a) The Wafd  b) The National Liberation Front (FLN)  c) Neo-Destour  d) The UGCC
**Answer: b) The National Liberation Front (FLN)** — The FLN fought from 1954 to 1962.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Algeria's first head of state in 1962 was —
a) Bourguiba  b) Ben Bella  c) Zaghlul  d) Muhammad V
**Answer: b) Ben Bella** — Ahmed Ben Bella.
