# Decolonisation — North Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give Sudan's steps from 1953 to independence.
**Answer:** A three-year transition in February 1953; a parliament elected that year; the last British troops left in 1955; independence declared in January 1956.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Libya reach independence, as printed?
**Answer:** Freed of Italy by the war, placed by the UN under British trusteeship to 1949, and independent in 1952 (as printed).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Morocco's two leading movements and Tunisia's two leading parties.
**Answer:** Morocco: the Moroccan National Front (1947) and the Istiqlal Party (1928). Tunisia: Bourguiba's Neo-Destour and Muhammad Inafa's Communist Party.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell Algeria's path: settlers, FLN, referendum, first leader.
**Answer:** Against the white settler colons, Algerians founded the FLN and fought a bitter struggle 1954–1962 with a provisional government in exile in Cairo; France agreed to a referendum in 1961, the people chose independence, and Ben Bella became first head of state in 1962.
