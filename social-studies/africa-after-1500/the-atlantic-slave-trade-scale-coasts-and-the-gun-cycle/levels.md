# The Atlantic slave trade — scale, coasts and the gun cycle — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first crossings — and the great rise
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The first captives crossed in 1532; numbers stayed small for a century, then rose steeply after the 1630s.

## Sub lesson 2 — The coasts that supplied captives
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Senegal in the 16th century, Angola from the 16th to the 19th, the Slave Coast, and at the peak the whole coast Senegal to Angola.

## Sub lesson 3 — The guns-for-slaves cycle
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Europeans supplied firearms to rival chiefs to make war; whatever a war's cause, its result was people delivered into slavery.

## Sub lesson 4 — Grade 11 depth: The trans-Atlantic trade in depth — the system's scale

Pass mark: the learner can retell the Grade 11 depth part in the story's order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key terms are **Middle Passage** (the Atlantic crossing of the enslaved, at the system's heart) and **triangular trade** (the three-legged Atlantic system of goods, captives and produce).
