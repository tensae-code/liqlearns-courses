# The Atlantic slave trade — scale, coasts and the gun cycle — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first Atlantic crossing of captives was in ____.
**Answer: 1532**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The stretch named for the trade itself was the ____ Coast.
**Answer: Slave**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Portuguese worked the conflicts of the ____ chiefs as the book's example.
**Answer: Kongo–Ndongo**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The term for 'the three-legged Atlantic system of goods, captives and produce' is ______.
**Answer: triangular trade**
