# The Atlantic slave trade — scale, coasts and the gun cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The figure of 10 million includes the 2 million who died on the voyage.
**Answer: False** — It EXCLUDES them — 10 million sold, 2 million more dead.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At the 18th-century peak, the whole coast from Senegal to Angola supplied captives.
**Answer: True** — The peak drew on the whole Atlantic coast.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whatever a war's cause, its result in the gun cycle was people delivered into slavery.
**Answer: True** — That is the cycle's cruel engine, as the book states it.
