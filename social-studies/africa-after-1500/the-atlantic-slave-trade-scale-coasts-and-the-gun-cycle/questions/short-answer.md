# The Atlantic slave trade — scale, coasts and the gun cycle — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the trade's full scale in one sentence, with both numbers.
**Answer:** Across 300 years more than 10 million Africans were sold into slavery, and 2 million more died on the voyage (not counted in the 10 million).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three supplying coasts and each one's time.
**Answer:** Senegal — the 16th century's main source; the Slave Coast; Angola — from the 16th to the 19th century; at the 18th-century peak the whole coast Senegal to Angola.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain the guns-for-slaves cycle.
**Answer:** Europeans, weakened by disease and beaten in direct raids, supplied firearms to rival African chiefs to make war; the wars' captives were exchanged for the guns — so every war, whatever its cause, ended in people delivered into slavery.
