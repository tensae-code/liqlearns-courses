# Lesson plan — Zimbabwe — my country, its anthem and its flag

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa after 1500 · **Lesson:** Zimbabwe — my country, its anthem and its flag
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa after 1500 — built in the History expansion (Zimbabwe, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Zimbabwe's children learn their country through its anthem, its flag and its pledge — every colour of the flag carries a piece of the freedom story: green for the land, gold for minerals, red for the blood heroes lost, black for the people, white for peace.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **My country, its anthem and its flag (ECD A)** — A small child learns that her country is Zimbabwe. (Topic 2 National history, sovereignty and governance — My country, National anthem, National flag)
2. **The flag's five colours, the pledge and the anthem (Grade 1)** — A year on, the flag's five colours are sung as a rhyme — red for the blood of those who fought for freedom, white for peace — and the morning pledge turns that story into a daily promise: hard work, honesty and unity, spoken by the child herself.. (Topic 2, Units 7–9 National flag, National school pledge, National anthem)
3. **National colours (ECD B)** — The anthem and the flag return, and now the colours step out of the flag to be learned on their own. (Topic 2 National history, sovereignty and governance — National anthem, National flag, National colours)
4. **Anthem, symbols and pledge — one kit of belonging (Grade 2)** — Anthem, symbols and pledge are gathered as one kit of belonging. (Topic 2, Units 3–5 National anthem, National symbols, National school pledge)
5. **National symbols explained (Grade 3)** — Now the symbols are revisited as a set to be explained, not just recognised. (Topic 2, Unit 7 National symbols)
6. **Why the national school pledge matters (Grade 4)** — The pledge is now studied for why it matters. (Topic 2, Unit 10 Importance of the national school pledge)
7. **Symbols of the settlement — flag, bird and coat of arms (Grade 6)** — After the war, the settlement is symbolic. (Topic 2, Unit 10 National symbols and celebrations)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
