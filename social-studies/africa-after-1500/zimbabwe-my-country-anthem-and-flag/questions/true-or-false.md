# Zimbabwe — my country, its anthem and its flag — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The black of Zimbabwe's flag stands for its people.
**Answer: True** — the flag's colours are decoded as land, minerals, heroes' blood and the people

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The school pledge is said once a year only.
**Answer: False** — it is a morning, daily promise

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The anthem and flag disappear after the first school year.
**Answer: False** — they return at ECD B, with the colours learned on their own

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The pledge and anthem are learned by reciting and singing them repeatedly.
**Answer: True** — they are recited and sung until the story is carried in the child's voice

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Recognising a symbol is enough at Grade 3.
**Answer: False** — the symbols are revisited to be explained

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** By Grade 4 the pledge is only recited, never studied.
**Answer: False** — at Grade 4 the pledge is studied for why it matters

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** The Zimbabwe bird is one of the national symbols.
**Answer: True** — flag, Zimbabwe bird and coat of arms are the settlement's symbols
