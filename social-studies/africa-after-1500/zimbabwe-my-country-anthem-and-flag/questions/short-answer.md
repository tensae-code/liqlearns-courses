# Zimbabwe — my country, its anthem and its flag — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the green of Zimbabwe's flag stand for?
**Answer: The land (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do Grade 1 children learn the flag's colours?
**Answer: They sing them as a rhyme (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are the national colours learned separately from the flag?
**Answer: So the child learns each colour's meaning as her first national vocabulary (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Name the three parts of Grade 2's kit of belonging.
**Answer: The national anthem, the national symbols and the national school pledge (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** How does Grade 3 treat the symbols differently from the first years?
**Answer: The child must explain what each symbol means, not only recognise it (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the daily pledge bind together?
**Answer: The schoolchild and the country's story (any close wording).**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Name two places the Zimbabwe bird or coat of arms is seen every day.
**Answer: On money and on official documents (any close wording).**
