# Video script — South Sudan — missionaries and churches

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as South Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Comboni Missionaries founded by Daniel Comboni at Verona, the Anglican Church Missionary Society and others enter South Sudan to convert, teach and heal, face disease and resistance, and leave schools and churches whose problems and positive impact pupils weigh.
