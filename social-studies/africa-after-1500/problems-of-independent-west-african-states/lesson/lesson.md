# Problems of independent West African states

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Independence in West Africa opens with schools, roads and clinics — then coups, debt and one-party rule shadow the promise the new states began with. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The promise — schools, roads and clinics

Independence opens with building: schools, roads and clinics. The new states set out to give their people the things colonial rule had not.

## Sub lesson 2 — Coups and one-party rule

Then the promise is shadowed. Coups overthrow governments, and one-party rule narrows who gets to decide — the freedom won at independence is squeezed from inside.

## Sub lesson 3 — Debt shadows the promise

Debt shadows the promise too. The new states owe, and owing limits what they can build — the schools, roads and clinics of the first hope are harder to pay for.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Problems of independent West African states in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
