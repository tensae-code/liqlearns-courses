# Problems of independent West African states — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Independence opened with schools, roads and clinics in West Africa.
**Answer: True — that building is how the book says independence opened.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Coups and one-party rule left the promise of independence untouched.
**Answer: False — coups, debt and one-party rule shadowed the promise.**
