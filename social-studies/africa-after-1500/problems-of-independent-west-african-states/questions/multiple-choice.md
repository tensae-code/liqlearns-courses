# Problems of independent West African states — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Independence in West Africa opened with building —
a) Schools, roads and clinics  b) Only armies  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Schools, roads and clinics**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Governments in the new states were overthrown by —
a) Coups  b) Elections only  c) Foreign tourists
**Answer: a) Coups**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One-party rule —
a) Narrows who gets to decide  b) Widens everyone's say  c) Ends all government
**Answer: a) Narrows who gets to decide**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Debt did what to the new states' promise?
a) Shadowed it, limiting what they could build  b) Made building free  c) Had no effect
**Answer: a) Shadowed it, limiting what they could build**
