# Hands-on — Problems of independent West African states

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Promise and shadow
Draw two panels. In the first, draw a new school, road or clinic at independence. In the second, write the three shadows the book names — coups, debt and one-party rule — and one sentence on each.

## What owing does
Write a short list of things a new state wants to build. Beside it, write what debt does to that list, in your own words.
