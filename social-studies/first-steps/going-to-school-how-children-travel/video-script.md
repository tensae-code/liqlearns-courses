India Grade 4 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Looking Around (EVS) (printed p. 1), teaches: children reach school in astonishingly different ways across India — bamboo bridges, trolleys over rivers, camel carts, boats and snow paths — the journey depends on the place.

India's own way: families change for named reasons — a baby arrives, a father's job transfers the family, a wedding joins two homes — and the law itself enters the child's world: the panchayat sends married girls back to school.
