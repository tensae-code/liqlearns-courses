# Going to School How Children Travel — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: children reach school in astonishingly different ways across India — bamboo bridges, trolleys over rivers, camel carts, boats and snow paths — the journey depends on the place.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on India's student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
