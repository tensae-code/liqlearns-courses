# Video script — Archaeology, palaeontology, anthropology — who studies prehistory

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a bare hillside. No writing. No books. Silence.
But look closer. A trench. A brush. A clay pot rises out of the soil. That is archaeology.
Beside it, a bone in stone. A fossil. Palaeontology reads the story of life itself.
And in the village beyond, someone listens — how do people live, marry, decide? Anthropology.
Three sisters. One question. What was life like before anyone could write?
