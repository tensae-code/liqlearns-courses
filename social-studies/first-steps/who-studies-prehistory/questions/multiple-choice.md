# Archaeology, palaeontology, anthropology — who studies prehistory — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Archaeology studies the past mainly by —
a) reading chronicles  b) digging up and studying artefacts and fossils  c) interviewing kings
**Answer: b) digging up and studying artefacts and fossils** — The dig is archaeology's tool.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Palaeontology is the study of —
a) fossils  b) languages  c) trade routes
**Answer: a) fossils** — Fossils of living things.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Anthropology studies —
a) only stones and bones  b) human society — how humans developed and related  c) the stars
**Answer: b) human society — how humans developed and related** — Society and human development.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** History differs from story because history —
a) is always longer  b) deals with real people and events  c) has no characters
**Answer: b) deals with real people and events** — A story may be imagined; history may not.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's timescale, Homo sapiens is dated to about —
a) 4.4 million years ago  b) 100,000 years ago  c) 5,500 years ago
**Answer: b) 100,000 years ago** — The guide's working figure.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Hominids and Homo sapiens first appeared, in the guide's account, in —
a) Asia  b) Europe  c) Africa
**Answer: c) Africa** — Africa first.
