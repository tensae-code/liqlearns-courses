# Archaeology, palaeontology, anthropology — who studies prehistory — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An artefact is a thing people made.
**Answer: True** — Pots, tools, ornaments — made things.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A story and a history are the same thing because both tell narratives.
**Answer: False** — History deals with real people and events; a story may be imagined.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern human beings are dated to about 40,000 years ago in the guide's frame.
**Answer: True** — The guide's second figure.
