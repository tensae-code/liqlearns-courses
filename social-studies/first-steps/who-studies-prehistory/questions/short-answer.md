# Archaeology, palaeontology, anthropology — who studies prehistory — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which discipline would study a fossil bone found in the Afar, and which a clay pot dug from a ruined village?
**Answer:** The fossil bone — palaeontology (and archaeology reads it in its site); the clay pot — archaeology, which digs and studies artefacts.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does an anthropologist studying a living village help us understand prehistory?
**Answer:** Because how people live, organise and relate today gives clues for reading how people may have lived before writing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Say the difference between history and story in one sentence.
**Answer:** Both are narratives, but history deals with real people and events while a story may be imagined.
