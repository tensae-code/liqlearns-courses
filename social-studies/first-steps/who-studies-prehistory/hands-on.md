# Hands-on — Archaeology, palaeontology, anthropology — who studies prehistory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The future dig
Bury nothing — instead, lay out five objects from one room on a cloth. A partner, playing archaeologist, may only look and touch: they must say what each object was for and what it proves about the people who used it.

## Three hats
Make three paper hats labelled Archaeology, Palaeontology, Anthropology. Read out clues — a coin, a dinosaur bone, a wedding custom, a spear point — and the learner wearing the right hat stands up for their clue.
