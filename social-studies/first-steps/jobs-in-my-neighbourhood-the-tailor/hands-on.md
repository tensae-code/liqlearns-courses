# Hands-on — Jobs in My Neighbourhood the Tailor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the class visits the neighbourhood's tailoring workshop (کارگاه خیاطی) to know the tailor's job up close — coloured cloths ordered on the shelves, the tailors cutting and sewing; the jobs of the neighbourhood are learned by visiting the people who do them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
