# Video script — Jobs in My Neighbourhood the Tailor

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 44, teaches: the class visits the neighbourhood's tailoring workshop (کارگاه خیاطی) to know the tailor's job up close — coloured cloths ordered on the shelves, the tailors cutting and sewing; the jobs of the neighbourhood are learned by visiting the people who do them.

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces.
