## Sub lesson 1 — Jobs in My Neighbourhood the Tailor

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 44, teaches: the class visits the neighbourhood's tailoring workshop (کارگاه خیاطی) to know the tailor's job up close — coloured cloths ordered on the shelves, the tailors cutting and sewing; the jobs of the neighbourhood are learned by visiting the people who do them. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
