## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the last primary year turns from places to the inner life — friendship, decisions and free time — and every moral rule is anchored in a saying of Imam Ali or a classroom dilemma (a friend who urges cheating, a found purse with no name in it).
