# Video script — Free Time and How to Use It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 6 (student book, Persian), printed p. 83, teaches: the kinds of free-time activity (sport, study and reading, art and crafts, social participation such as the Red Crescent youth organisation, religious gatherings), planning free time alone and with family, and the public places built for it — libraries, parks, museums, cultural centres, sports halls.

Iran's own way: the last primary year turns from places to the inner life — friendship, decisions and free time — and every moral rule is anchored in a saying of Imam Ali or a classroom dilemma (a friend who urges cheating, a found purse with no name in it).
