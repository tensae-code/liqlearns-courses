# Hands-on — Free Time and How to Use It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the kinds of free-time activity (sport, study and reading, art and crafts, social participation such as the Red Crescent youth organisation, religious gatherings), planning free time alone and with family, and the public places built for it — libraries, parks, museums, cultural centres, sports halls. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
