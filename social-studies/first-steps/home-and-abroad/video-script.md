# Video script — Home and Abroad

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India Grade 4 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Looking Around (EVS) (printed p. 191), teaches: some family members work far away — a father working in Dubai sends money and gifts home, and the family lives between two places.

India's own way: families change for named reasons — a baby arrives, a father's job transfers the family, a wedding joins two homes — and the law itself enters the child's world: the panchayat sends married girls back to school.
