# Video script — Jordan My Homeland Map And Capital

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan's book, the *Jordan G1 SS T1* (printed p. 42), teaches: Unit 2 Lesson 1: Jordan Is My Homeland (الأردن وطني) — colouring Jordan's map, the capital Amman, the 12 governorates.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G1 SS T1): Social Studies opens at Grade 1 with the child's own circles — self, family, neighbourhood — then the homeland (Jordan's map, capital, flag, king), all taught through colouring, matching and picture talk rather than text.
