# Hands-on — Rules of Our Classroom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The class designs its own fair set of duties and rules — every child helps, with different tasks matched to different abilities, and the class votes on the set it prefers. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Class Rights and Responsibilities
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every right carries a matching responsibility (the right to be safe ↔ the duty not to harm); responsibilities agreed by consensus become the class rules and hold longer than rules imposed by others. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vanuatu's way
Say how Vanuatu's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
