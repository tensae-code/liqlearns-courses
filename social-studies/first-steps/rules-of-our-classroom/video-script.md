# Video script — Rules of Our Classroom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vanuatu Citoyenneté Sociale Guide Years 1–6 (printed p. 18), teaches: The class designs its own fair set of duties and rules — every child helps, with different tasks matched to different abilities, and the class votes on the set it prefers.

Vanuatu Citoyenneté Sociale Guide Years 1–6 (printed p. 27), teaches: Every right carries a matching responsibility (the right to be safe ↔ the duty not to harm); responsibilities agreed by consensus become the class rules and hold longer than rules imposed by others.

Vanuatu's own way: social citizenship is taught as roles before rules — the Year 1 child maps who they are in family and village (chief, pastor, nurse, police, women's and youth group leaders), learns that roles change and are not fixed by gender, and only in Years 5–6 reaches rights, and how a community's rules are made and by whom.
