# Rules of Our Classroom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Rules of Our Classroom — The class designs its own fair set of duties and rules — every child helps, with different tasks matched to different abilities, and the class votes on the set it prefers (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Class Rights and Responsibilities — Every right carries a matching responsibility (the right to be safe ↔ the duty not to harm); responsibilities agreed by consensus become the class rules and hold longer than rules imposed by others (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vanuatu's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
