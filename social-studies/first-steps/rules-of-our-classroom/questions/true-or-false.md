# Rules of Our Classroom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The class designs its own fair set of duties and rules — every child helps, with different tasks matched to different abilities, and the class votes on the set it prefers.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Every right carries a matching responsibility (the right to be safe ↔ the duty not to harm); responsibilities agreed by consensus become the class rules and hold longer than rules imposed by others.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Vanuatu's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
