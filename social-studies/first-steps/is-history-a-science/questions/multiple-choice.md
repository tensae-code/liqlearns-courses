# Is history a science? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is controlled experiment?
a) value judgement
b) reliable source
c) a test the natural sciences can repeat at will — history cannot
**Answer: c) a test the natural sciences can repeat at will — history cannot**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is social sciences?
a) the past
b) the studies of human society, among which history sits
c) comparison
**Answer: b) the studies of human society, among which history sits**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Leopold von Ranke?
a) German Historical School
b) founder figure of modern academic history, of the German Historical School
c) as it actually was
**Answer: b) founder figure of modern academic history, of the German Historical School**
