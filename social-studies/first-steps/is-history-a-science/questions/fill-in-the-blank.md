# Is history a science? — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'evidence that survives criticism and can be checked by others' is ______.
**Answer: reliable source**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'history's own ground, where the social sciences mostly study the present' is ______.
**Answer: the past**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'Ranke's demand — the past shown from the documents, not invented' is ______.
**Answer: as it actually was**
