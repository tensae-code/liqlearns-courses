# Video script — Is history a science?

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — History beside the natural sciences. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
A chemist can repeat an experiment; a historian cannot re-run the fall of an empire. Watch the names appear one by one: controlled experiment, value judgement, reliable source. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Among the social sciences. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
History sits among the social sciences — with the studies of societies, economies and peoples — because its subject is human beings in groups, across time. Watch the names appear one by one: social sciences, comparison, the past. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Ranke and the modern school. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Modern academic history is traced to Leopold von Ranke and the German Historical School. Watch the names appear one by one: Leopold von Ranke, German Historical School, as it actually was. This is the part of the story to hold.
