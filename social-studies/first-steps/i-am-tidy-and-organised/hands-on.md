# Hands-on — I Am Tidy and Organised

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Cleanliness is the basis of tidiness and organisation, and tidiness and organisation begin with me myself first: each pupil arranges his books and notebooks in his bag and cleans his desk, and when everything around us is organised, reaching our things becomes easy and organisation makes work and life easy; organisation is not for things only — when you organise your time your life becomes easy and beautiful, for time is like gold, if it goes it does not return; and the class sets its class rules and the family its home rules to commit to. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
