## Sub lesson 1 — I Am Tidy and Organised

Iraq Grade 1 Moral Education (printed pp. 12–19), teaches: Cleanliness is the basis of tidiness and organisation, and tidiness and organisation begin with me myself first: each pupil arranges his books and notebooks in his bag and cleans his desk, and when everything around us is organised, reaching our things becomes easy and organisation makes work and life easy; organisation is not for things only — when you organise your time your life becomes easy and beautiful, for time is like gold, if it goes it does not return; and the class sets its class rules and the family its home rules to commit to. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: moral education is a separate subject from Grade 1, taught entirely as stories about five named children (Muhammad, Yusuf, Zahraa, Lana, Sara) whose small school and family incidents carry each value; every lesson ends in the child's own voice — "What I learned" — and the second unit's family tree is drawn with the father's help, branch by branch, until the big family shades them all. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
