# Video script — Protecting Our Water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 76–77), teaches: Water belongs to everyone — use it sensibly and thriftily and never pollute it; who pollutes (factories, households, farms, dumps) and the protection measures: no waste in water, rational use, guarding drinking-water sources, treating waste water.

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
