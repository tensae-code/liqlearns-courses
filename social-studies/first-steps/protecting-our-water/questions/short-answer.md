# Protecting Our Water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Значај и заштита воде (Importance and protection of water) — water belongs to everyone — use it sensibly and thriftily and never pollute it; who pollutes (factories, households, farms, dumps) and the protection measures: no waste in water, rational use, guarding drinking-water sources, treating waste water (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way — water belongs to everyone — use it sensibly and thriftily and never pollute it; who pollutes (factories, households, farms, dumps) and the protection measures: no waste in water, rational use, guarding drinking-water sources, treating waste water (any close wording).**
