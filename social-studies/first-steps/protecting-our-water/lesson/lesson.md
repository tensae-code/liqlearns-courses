# Protecting Our Water

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: water belongs to everyone — use it sensibly and thriftily and never pollute it; who pollutes (factories, households, farms, dumps) and the protection measures: no waste in water, rational use, guarding drinking-water sources, treating waste water — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Значај и заштита воде (Importance and protection of water)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 76–77), teaches: water belongs to everyone — use it sensibly and thriftily and never pollute it; who pollutes (factories, households, farms, dumps) and the protection measures: no waste in water, rational use, guarding drinking-water sources, treating waste water. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
