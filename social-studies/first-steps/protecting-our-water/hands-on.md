# Hands-on — Protecting Our Water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Значај и заштита воде (Importance and protection of water)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: water belongs to everyone — use it sensibly and thriftily and never pollute it; who pollutes (factories, households, farms, dumps) and the protection measures: no waste in water, rational use, guarding drinking-water sources, treating waste water. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
