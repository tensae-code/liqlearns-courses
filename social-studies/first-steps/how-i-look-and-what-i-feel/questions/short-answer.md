# How I Look and What I Feel — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: How do I look, what do I feel? — our physical features (hair and eye colour, height, weight — some change as we grow) and our basic feelings (happiness, sadness, excitement, joy, worry) shown in words and faces; different people can feel differently about the same event, and we name our features and feelings when we introduce ourselves (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
