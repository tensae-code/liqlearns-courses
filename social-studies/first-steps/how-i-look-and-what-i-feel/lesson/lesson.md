# How I Look and What I Feel

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: our physical features (hair and eye colour, height, weight — some change as we grow) and our basic feelings (happiness, sadness, excitement, joy, worry) shown in words and faces; different people can feel differently about the same event, and we name our features and feelings when we introduce ourselves — as Turkey's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: the Grade 1 year opens school itself as the first society: a six-year-old first learns to introduce herself and listen, then the school's rooms and workers, then rules the class itself votes on, and only then the family as the second society — nuclear and extended family, the values that hold it together, and jobs shared like a team.
