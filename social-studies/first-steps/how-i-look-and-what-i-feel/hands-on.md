# Hands-on — How I Look and What I Feel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: our physical features (hair and eye colour, height, weight — some change as we grow) and our basic feelings (happiness, sadness, excitement, joy, worry) shown in words and faces; different people can feel differently about the same event, and we name our features and feelings when we introduce ourselves Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
