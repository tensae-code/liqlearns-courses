# Hands-on — What My Homeland Has Built

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Achievements of my homeland
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What my homeland has achieved for its people — education, health care, infrastructure and electronic services, known through pictures of today's schools, hospitals, roads and services. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Jordan's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Jordan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
