# Hands-on — My Family Cooperates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Cooperation with the family members in finishing the housework: when Yusuf's mother cannot join the family's outing because of her many tasks, the whole family divides the work — washing the dishes, tidying the bedrooms, cleaning the window glass, mowing and watering the garden, the kitchen work — and in a short time the cooperative family finishes, the house becomes clean and tidy, and they go out together; cooperation makes the family cohesive, saves time and effort, and increases joy and intimacy among its members — with cooperation our house becomes more beautiful, and with cooperation our life becomes better. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
