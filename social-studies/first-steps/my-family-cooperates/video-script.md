# Video script — My Family Cooperates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 1 Moral Education (printed pp. 55–60), teaches: Cooperation with the family members in finishing the housework: when Yusuf's mother cannot join the family's outing because of her many tasks, the whole family divides the work — washing the dishes, tidying the bedrooms, cleaning the window glass, mowing and watering the garden, the kitchen work — and in a short time the cooperative family finishes, the house becomes clean and tidy, and they go out together; cooperation makes the family cohesive, saves time and effort, and increases joy and intimacy among its members — with cooperation our house becomes more beautiful, and with cooperation our life becomes better.

Iraq's own way: moral education is a separate subject from Grade 1, taught entirely as stories about five named children (Muhammad, Yusuf, Zahraa, Lana, Sara) whose small school and family incidents carry each value; every lesson ends in the child's own voice — "What I learned" — and the second unit's family tree is drawn with the father's help, branch by branch, until the big family shades them all.
