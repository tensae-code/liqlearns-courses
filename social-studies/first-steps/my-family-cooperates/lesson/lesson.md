# My Family Cooperates

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: cooperation with the family members in finishing the housework: when Yusuf's mother cannot join the family's outing because of her many tasks, the whole family divides the work — washing the dishes, tidying the bedrooms, cleaning the window glass, mowing and watering the garden, the kitchen work — and in a short time the cooperative family finishes, the house becomes clean and tidy, and they go out together; cooperation makes the family cohesive, saves time and effort, and increases joy and intimacy among its members — with cooperation our house becomes more beautiful, and with cooperation our life becomes better — as Iraq's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — My Family Cooperates

Iraq Grade 1 Moral Education (printed pp. 55–60), teaches: Cooperation with the family members in finishing the housework: when Yusuf's mother cannot join the family's outing because of her many tasks, the whole family divides the work — washing the dishes, tidying the bedrooms, cleaning the window glass, mowing and watering the garden, the kitchen work — and in a short time the cooperative family finishes, the house becomes clean and tidy, and they go out together; cooperation makes the family cohesive, saves time and effort, and increases joy and intimacy among its members — with cooperation our house becomes more beautiful, and with cooperation our life becomes better. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: moral education is a separate subject from Grade 1, taught entirely as stories about five named children (Muhammad, Yusuf, Zahraa, Lana, Sara) whose small school and family incidents carry each value; every lesson ends in the child's own voice — "What I learned" — and the second unit's family tree is drawn with the father's help, branch by branch, until the big family shades them all. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
