# Hands-on — Families Show Care for Each Other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Mother cooks every day and takes the children to school; naming other ways your mother cares for the family. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Caring for a pet (Ron bathes his black-and-white dog); naming your pet and how you care for it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
