## Sub lesson 1 — Families show care

Guyana Grade 2 Social Studies For Our Children Book 2 (printed p. 4), teaches: Mother cooks every day and takes the children to school; naming other ways your mother cares for the family. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — We care for our pets

Guyana Grade 2 Social Studies For Our Children Book 2 (printed p. 5), teaches: Caring for a pet (Ron bathes his black-and-white dog); naming your pet and how you care for it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: Book 2 widens Book 1's world — the same Ron and Sue now meet family types, class rules, neighbourhood shops and, for the first time, the country's leaders (President, Prime Minister, Ministers) and its national days (Republic Day, May Day, Enmore Martyrs' Day, Freedom Day). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
