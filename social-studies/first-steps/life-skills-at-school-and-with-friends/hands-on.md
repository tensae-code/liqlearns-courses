# Hands-on — Life Skills at School and with Friends

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: we spend part of our lives at school — how many months a year, how many hours a day?; at school we became literate and met the different subjects — and besides lessons, school taught us good behaviours: the class thinks together about what else it has learned at school and which good behaviours it picked up there. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
