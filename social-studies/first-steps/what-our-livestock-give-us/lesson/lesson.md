## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 8 "The benefits of livestock" (Малын ашиг шим)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 26, teaches: The five snouts give milk, meat, wool, cashmere and hides for food and everything a household uses; each animal is named by age and sex; and from milk come the white foods (цагаан идээ) — өрөм, зөөхий, аарц, ааруул, бяслаг, ээзгий, айраг, хоормог, тараг and the yellow and white fats — milk being the most honoured food, offered first to a respected guest. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
