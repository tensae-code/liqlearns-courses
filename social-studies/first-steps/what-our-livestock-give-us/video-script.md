Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 26, teaches: The five snouts give milk, meat, wool, cashmere and hides for food and everything a household uses; each animal is named by age and sex; and from milk come the white foods (цагаан идээ) — өрөм, зөөхий, аарц, ааруул, бяслаг, ээзгий, айраг, хоормог, тараг and the yellow and white fats — milk being the most honoured food, offered first to a respected guest.

Mongolia's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact.
