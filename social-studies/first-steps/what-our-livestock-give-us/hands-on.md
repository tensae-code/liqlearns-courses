# Hands-on — What Our Livestock Give Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 8 "The benefits of livestock" (Малын ашиг шим)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The five snouts give milk, meat, wool, cashmere and hides for food and everything a household uses; each animal is named by age and sex; and from milk come the white foods (цагаан идээ) — өрөм, зөөхий, аарц, ааруул, бяслаг, ээзгий, айраг, хоормог, тараг and the yellow and white fats — milk being the most honoured food, offered first to a respected guest. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
