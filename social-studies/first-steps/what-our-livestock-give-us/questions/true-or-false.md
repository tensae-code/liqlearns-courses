# What Our Livestock Give Us — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the five snouts give milk, meat, wool, cashmere and hides for food and everything a household uses; each animal is named by age and sex; and from milk come the white foods (цагаан идээ) — өрөм, зөөхий, аарц, ааруул, бяслаг, ээзгий, айраг, хоормог, тараг and the yellow and white fats — milk being the most honoured food, offered first to a respected guest.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
