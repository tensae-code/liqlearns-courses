# What Our Livestock Give Us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 8 "The benefits of livestock" (Малын ашиг шим) — The five snouts give milk, meat, wool, cashmere and hides for food and everything a household uses; each animal is named by age and sex; and from milk come the white foods (цагаан идээ) — өрөм, зөөхий, аарц, ааруул, бяслаг, ээзгий, айраг, хоормог, тараг and the yellow and white fats — milk being the most honoured food, offered first to a respected guest (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact (any close wording).**
