# Hands-on — The Road and Its Parts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Road and Its Parts
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A road is a strip of land for the movement of cars and other wheeled vehicles, with parts set aside for pedestrians; roads join population centres — cities, villages and their quarters; a road's components are the carriageway (the paved part for all vehicles), the pavement (the raised part at the sides for pedestrians), the pedestrian crossing, and the traffic signs that organise movement and reduce accidents — guidance signs, warning signs, information signs and traffic lights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
