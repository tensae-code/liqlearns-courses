# The Road and Its Parts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Road and Its Parts — a road is a strip of land for the movement of cars and other wheeled vehicles, with parts set aside for pedestrians; roads join population centres — cities, villages and their quarters; a road's components are the carriageway (the paved part for all vehicles), the pavement (the raised part at the sides for pedestrians), the pedestrian crossing, and the traffic signs that organise movement and reduce accidents — guidance signs, warning signs, information signs and traffic lights (any close wording).**
