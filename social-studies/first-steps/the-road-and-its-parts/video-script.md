Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2, printed p. 78, teaches: a road is a strip of land for the movement of cars and other wheeled vehicles, with parts set aside for pedestrians; roads join population centres — cities, villages and their quarters; a road's components are the carriageway (the paved part for all vehicles), the pavement (the raised part at the sides for pedestrians), the pedestrian crossing, and the traffic signs that organise movement and reduce accidents — guidance signs, warning signs, information signs and traffic lights.

Palestine's own way: civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom.
