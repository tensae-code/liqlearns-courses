# My Classroom My Second Home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: My classroom — greeting the teacher at the door; the classroom's own things and how they are arranged (learning corner, creativity corner, the class-rules tree); the teacher and the classmates and each one's task; lending a friend your pen; the class's activities in lessons and outside them — the classroom is a second home where the children learn and play (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way — the local community is introduced through two big picture scenes — the home village (health station, post office, market, school) against the city (hospital, supermarket, cinema, traffic) — and every job, from farmer to traffic policeman, is summed up in one sentence: any work that benefits society is precious. (any close wording).**
