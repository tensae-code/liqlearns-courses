## Sub lesson 1 — My classroom

Vietnam Grade 1 Tự nhiên và Xã hội 1, printed pp. 24–29, teaches: Greeting the teacher at the door; the classroom's own things and how they are arranged (learning corner, creativity corner, the class-rules tree); the teacher and the classmates and each one's task; lending a friend your pen; the class's activities in lessons and outside them — the classroom is a second home where the children learn and play. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: the local community is introduced through two big picture scenes — the home village (health station, post office, market, school) against the city (hospital, supermarket, cinema, traffic) — and every job, from farmer to traffic policeman, is summed up in one sentence: any work that benefits society is precious. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
