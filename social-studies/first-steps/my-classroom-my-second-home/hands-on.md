# Hands-on — My Classroom My Second Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: greeting the teacher at the door; the classroom's own things and how they are arranged (learning corner, creativity corner, the class-rules tree); the teacher and the classmates and each one's task; lending a friend your pen; the class's activities in lessons and outside them — the classroom is a second home where the children learn and play. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
