# Video script — Keeping My Things Tidy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 1 Đạo đức 1, printed pp. 38–39, teaches: Books, clothes and toys each in their place so they are easy to find.

Vietnam's own way: Đạo đức (Ethics) is Vietnam's standalone primary moral-education subject; every lesson runs the same four moves — a song to open, picture stories to explore, practice pictures to judge, a role-play to apply — and closes with a two-line rhyme that states the duty; the family is taught three-generational (ông bà – cha mẹ – anh chị em) and school rules are taught as the child's first shared rules.
