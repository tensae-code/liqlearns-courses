## Sub lesson 1 — Social Life Living Together in Society

Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 1, teaches: humans are social beings; social life means people living together in one place sharing religion, history and customs, cooperating and meeting each other's needs — no one can live alone. ## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: the child's world widens in rings — social life, family, mosque, school, village, city, homeland, world, Earth — and every ring carries its duties with it: respect for elders at home, duties and order at school, reverence for the flag, obedience to traffic rules.
