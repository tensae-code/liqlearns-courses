# Video script — Social Life Living Together in Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 1, teaches: humans are social beings; social life means people living together in one place sharing religion, history and customs, cooperating and meeting each other's needs — no one can live alone.

Afghanistan's own way: the child's world widens in rings — social life, family, mosque, school, village, city, homeland, world, Earth — and every ring carries its duties with it: respect for elders at home, duties and order at school, reverence for the flag, obedience to traffic rules.
