# Hands-on — Social Life Living Together in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Humans are social beings; social life means people living together in one place sharing religion, history and customs, cooperating and meeting each other's needs — no one can live alone. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
