# Hands-on — My Role in My School and Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 6 A. Peran dan Tugasku di Lingkungan Sekolah dan Masyarakat, Bab 6 B. Peraturan dan Tanggung Jawabku, Bab 6 C. Interaksi Sosial di Lingkungan Sekolah dan Masyarakat, Bab VI: Peranku di Lingkungan Sekolah dan Masyarakat — teaches: my roles and duties in the school and community environments — observing and tabulating everyone's role, and why roles differ by place; rules and my responsibilities in school and community; social interaction in school and community — how people meet, speak and cooperate across roles; essential material and guidance for roles and duties, rules and responsibilities, and social interaction in school and community. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
