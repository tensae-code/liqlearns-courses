# Hands-on — Our Country Iran and Its Provinces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Iran is our big home; as a school is divided into classes, each with its pupils and a teacher who runs it, our country is divided for better running into big parts called provinces (استان), each with its own cities and villages. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
