Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Our Country Iran and Its Provinces

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 76, teaches: Iran is our big home; as a school is divided into classes, each with its pupils and a teacher who runs it, our country is divided for better running into big parts called provinces (استان), each with its own cities and villages. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
