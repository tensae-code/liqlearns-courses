# Hands-on — My Settlement and Its People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## У насељу (In the settlement)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the people who live in one settlement are its residents and fellow citizens, each with defined rights and duties; much is shared and belongs to all — street, park, river, school, bus — and residents meet, agree and organise the work their settlement needs, from a village feast's invitations to the House of Culture. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
