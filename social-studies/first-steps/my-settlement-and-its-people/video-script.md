# Video script — My Settlement and Its People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed pp. 12–13), teaches: The people who live in one settlement are its residents and fellow citizens, each with defined rights and duties; much is shared and belongs to all — street, park, river, school, bus — and residents meet, agree and organise the work their settlement needs, from a village feast's invitations to the House of Culture.

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays.
