# Good Deeds Become Habits — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: how one good deed returns — the boy who feeds a lost puppy is repaid when the puppy wakes the family and saves them from a night fire; good deeds are traced as a chain from a generous heart to the good the deed finally does.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
