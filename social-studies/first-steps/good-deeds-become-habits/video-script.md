# Video script — Good Deeds Become Habits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 18, teaches: How one good deed returns — the boy who feeds a lost puppy is repaid when the puppy wakes the family and saves them from a night fire; good deeds are traced as a chain from a generous heart to the good the deed finally does.

Mongolia's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact.
