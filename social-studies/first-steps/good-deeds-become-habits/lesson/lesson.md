# Good Deeds Become Habits

Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 5 "Let's make good deeds our habit" (Сайн үйлд хэвшье)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 18, teaches: How one good deed returns — the boy who feeds a lost puppy is repaid when the puppy wakes the family and saves them from a night fire; good deeds are traced as a chain from a generous heart to the good the deed finally does. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
