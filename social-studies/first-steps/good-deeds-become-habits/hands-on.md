# Hands-on — Good Deeds Become Habits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 5 "Let's make good deeds our habit" (Сайн үйлд хэвшье)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How one good deed returns — the boy who feeds a lost puppy is repaid when the puppy wakes the family and saves them from a night fire; good deeds are traced as a chain from a generous heart to the good the deed finally does. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
