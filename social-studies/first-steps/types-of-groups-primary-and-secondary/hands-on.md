# Hands-on — Types of Groups Primary and Secondary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Primary groups (close ties, face-to-face — family, peer group) versus secondary groups (larger, purpose-based, written rules, leaders — school, youth group, choir). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
