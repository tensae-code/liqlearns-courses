# Video script — Types of Groups Primary and Secondary

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 9 Book 1 (printed p. 60), teaches: Primary groups (close ties, face-to-face — family, peer group) versus secondary groups (larger, purpose-based, written rules, leaders — school, youth group, choir).

Samoa's own way: Social organisation is taught from the inside out — the groups a child belongs to first, then the aiga and its matai, then the village's own circle of groups (matai at the centre, aumaga as the matai's right hand), and only then a neighbour kingdom's political system as the worked example of government.
