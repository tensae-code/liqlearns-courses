# Types of Families — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Types of Families — a family is a group of people related to each other and the basic unit of society; Tanzania's family types — nuclear, extended, single-parent, orphan, childless, adoptive and grandparent families (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way — Tanzania's own way (Standard Three, Social Studies Standard Three Pupil's Book): culture is taught through the body — each community's greeting is a different act (Zaramo shake hands, Hehe kiss hands, Kinga rub shoulders, Sukuma women kneel for men) and greeting itself is taught as manners and respect; the family chapter names seven Tanzanian family types, including orphan and grandparent families. (any close wording).**
