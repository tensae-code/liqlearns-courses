## Sub lesson 1 — Types of Families

## Sub lesson 2 — Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way

Tanzania's own way (Standard Three, Social Studies Standard Three Pupil's Book): culture is taught through the body — each community's greeting is a different act (Zaramo shake hands, Hehe kiss hands, Kinga rub shoulders, Sukuma women kneel for men) and greeting itself is taught as manners and respect; the family chapter names seven Tanzanian family types, including orphan and grandparent families. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
