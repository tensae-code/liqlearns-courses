# Hands-on — Types of Families

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Types of Families
Work through this part with your hands: a family is a group of people related to each other and the basic unit of society; Tanzania's family types — nuclear, extended, single-parent, orphan, childless, adoptive and grandparent families. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
