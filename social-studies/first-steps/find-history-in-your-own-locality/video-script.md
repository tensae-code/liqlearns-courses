# Video script — Find history in your own locality

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Every locality holds primary evidence. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
History does not live only in capitals. Watch the names appear one by one: locality, primary evidence, elder. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — How to hunt it. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Hunt in order: ask what stands oldest around you; ask who remembers and record them carefully; photograph, copy and date what you find; then test it — whose is it, how old, who says so, what agrees?

Local does not mean loose. Watch the names appear one by one: record, test, criticism. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Bringing it back. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The hunt ends in an account: what you found, what it proves, what it cannot prove, and what you still do not know. Watch the names appear one by one: account, vary, ground up. This is the part of the story to hold.
