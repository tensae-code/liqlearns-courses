# Find history in your own locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is locality?
a) elder
b) primary evidence
c) your own place — town, village and neighbourhood
**Answer: c) your own place — town, village and neighbourhood**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is record?
a) criticism
b) test
c) to write down or capture testimony before it is lost
**Answer: c) to write down or capture testimony before it is lost**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is account?
a) vary
b) ground up
c) the written result of the hunt — finds, proof and gaps
**Answer: c) the written result of the hunt — finds, proof and gaps**
