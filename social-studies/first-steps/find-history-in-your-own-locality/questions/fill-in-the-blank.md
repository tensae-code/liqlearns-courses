# Find history in your own locality — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'a living carrier of local memory' is ______.
**Answer: elder**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the historian's testing of sources, applied to local finds too' is ______.
**Answer: criticism**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'history built from local evidence toward the national story' is ______.
**Answer: ground up**
