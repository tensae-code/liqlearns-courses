# Find history in your own locality — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: Traces from the time itself, found locally as elsewhere — this is primary evidence.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, locality means a living carrier of local memory.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: To check a source's age, maker and agreement with others — this is test.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, record means the historian's testing of sources, applied to local finds too.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: To differ from place to place, as local answers honestly do — this is vary.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, account means history built from local evidence toward the national story.
**Answer: False**
