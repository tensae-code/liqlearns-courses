**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Greece).**

Greece's own way (Book 3): the youngest pupils' integrated subject is built as widening circles — classroom, school, family, home, neighbourhood — introduced by two puppet-like guides (Leon and Milo), with Greek feasts and customs carrying the culture strand.

Greece's own way (Book 4): Grade 2 widens Grade 1's circles — the neighbourhood is now studied service by service (its church named as a landmark, its green discovered, its past compared with today), and the child's first rights appear as their own unit: the right to life and the right to peace.
