# Hands-on — The Plan Of A House

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 16 What does your house look like? (خانه‌ی شما چه شکلی است؟) — in every house there are different places, and in each place different work is done; 'this is the plan of a house (نقشه‌ی خانه)'; and the shape of houses changes with time — the picture of an old house and Mr Mahmoudi's memories of it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
