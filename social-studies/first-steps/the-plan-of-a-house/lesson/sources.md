Iran's own way (Iran Grade 3 Social Studies): a primary Social Studies book of 23 lessons in 7 chapters that works outward from the child — me, my family, my house, my school, from home to school. The Geography parts placed below are the resources lessons of Ch. 4 The family's needs (Lessons 11–13, printed pp. 37–55), the houses lessons of Ch. 5 Our house (Lessons 15–16, printed pp. 57–62), the school-map worksheet of Ch. 6 (printed p. 80) and the address and directions lessons of Ch. 7 From home to school (Lessons 20–21, printed pp. 81–85). The other parts are named here, not placed: Personal and family life (Civics) — Ch. 1 I am growing up (Lessons 1–3, printed pp. 1–15), Ch. 2 The family (Lessons 4–7, printed pp. 16–29), Ch. 3 Cooperation in the family (Lessons 8–9, printed pp. 30–37, with Lesson 10 of Ch. 4, How the family's needs are met, printed p. 38); Ch. 5 Lesson 14 I love my house and Lesson 17 Let us protect the house (printed pp. 56, 63); Ch. 6 Our school (Lessons 18–19, printed pp. 71–79); Ch. 7 Lesson 22 The post (printed p. 86) and Lesson 23 Safety in alley and street (printed p. 88).


Serbia's own way (Grade 1 Свет око нас): the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast.
