# Video script — Jobs People Do in Our Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Latvia Grade 1 Sociālās zinības teacher methodological book (printed p. 25), teaches: Theme 4 Kādas prasmes un īpašības vajadzīgas mācoties un strādājot? (What skills and qualities are needed for learning and working) — teaches: wants versus needs, and professions — interviewing people about their jobs.

Latvia's own way: social studies is taught integrated with the Latvian language itself (CLIL) for linguistically mixed classes — every topic is framed as a question ("What does it mean to be a pupil?") and the social content is carried by the vocabulary and courtesies of school life.
