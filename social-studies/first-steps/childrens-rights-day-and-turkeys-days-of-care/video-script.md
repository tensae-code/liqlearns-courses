The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Turkey Grade 1 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed pp. 14, 50–51, 84–85, teaches: the year's care-and-values days — World Children's Rights Day (20 November: children's rights to education and health), Red Crescent Week (helping people in need), World Disability Day (3 December: understanding people with special needs and respecting differences), Animal Protection Day (4 October: animals need food, shelter, love and care), Energy Saving Week (using limited water and electricity carefully), Teachers' Day and Primary Education Week

Turkey's own way: the Grade 1 year opens school itself as the first society: a six-year-old first learns to introduce herself and listen, then the school's rooms and workers, then rules the class itself votes on, and only then the family as the second society — nuclear and extended family, the values that hold it together, and jobs shared like a team.
