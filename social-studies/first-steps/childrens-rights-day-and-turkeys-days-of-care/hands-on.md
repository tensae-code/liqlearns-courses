# Hands-on — Childrens Rights Day and Turkeys Days of Care

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the year's care-and-values days — World Children's Rights Day (20 November: children's rights to education and health), Red Crescent Week (helping people in need), World Disability Day (3 December: understanding people with special needs and respecting differences), Animal Protection Day (4 October: animals need food, shelter, love and care), Energy Saving Week (using limited water and electricity carefully), Teachers' Day and Primary Education Week Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Turkey's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
