# Video script — My Roles in My Family and Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vanuatu Citoyenneté Sociale Guide Years 1–6 (printed p. 7), teaches: The child's roles and responsibilities in family, class and community; family relationships (parents, grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins); the community's role-holders from the chief down; roles are shared, are not fixed, and change as people age and circumstances change.

Vanuatu's own way: social citizenship is taught as roles before rules — the Year 1 child maps who they are in family and village (chief, pastor, nurse, police, women's and youth group leaders), learns that roles change and are not fixed by gender, and only in Years 5–6 reaches rights, and how a community's rules are made and by whom.
