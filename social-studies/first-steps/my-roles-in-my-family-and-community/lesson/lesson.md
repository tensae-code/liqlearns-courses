## Sub lesson 1 — My Roles in My Family and Community

Vanuatu Citoyenneté Sociale Guide Years 1–6 (printed p. 7), teaches: The child's roles and responsibilities in family, class and community; family relationships (parents, grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins); the community's role-holders from the chief down; roles are shared, are not fixed, and change as people age and circumstances change. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vanuatu's way — the book's own teaching way

Vanuatu's own way: social citizenship is taught as roles before rules — the Year 1 child maps who they are in family and village (chief, pastor, nurse, police, women's and youth group leaders), learns that roles change and are not fixed by gender, and only in Years 5–6 reaches rights, and how a community's rules are made and by whom. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
