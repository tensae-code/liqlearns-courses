# Hands-on — My Roles in My Family and Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The child's roles and responsibilities in family, class and community; family relationships (parents, grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins); the community's role-holders from the chief down; roles are shared, are not fixed, and change as people age and circumstances change. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vanuatu's way
Say how Vanuatu's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
