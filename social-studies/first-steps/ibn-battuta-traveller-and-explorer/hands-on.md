# Hands-on — Ibn Battuta — Traveller and Explorer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the journey
On a world map, draw one long journey line of your own in the way the Grade 1 book traces Ibn Battuta's: start at home, cross several countries, and mark with a wavy line the stretches that would be by sea and a plain line the stretches by land.

## Count the countries
Follow a traced journey on a map and count aloud every country the line passes through, the way the class counts Ibn Battuta's — more than 40 countries. Say at the end how many your own line crossed.
