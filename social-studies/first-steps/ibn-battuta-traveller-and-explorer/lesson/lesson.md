# Ibn Battuta — Traveller and Explorer

## Do it first

Think of the longest journey you have ever made, before you read on. Now think of a traveller who kept travelling until his journeys covered country after country.

## Sub lesson 1 — A traveller and explorer

The United Arab Emirates Grade 1 book gives its children a traveller-hero: Ibn Battuta, in Unit 6, Lesson 2 — A traveller and explorer. He is met the Grade 1 way: as a person to follow, picture by picture, not as dates to memorise.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tracing his journeys on a map

The book traces Ibn Battuta's journeys on a map. His travels reached more than 40 countries, and he travelled both by land and by sea. Following his line on the map is the lesson: a journey is a path across real places, some crossed on foot, by animal or by road, and some crossed over water.
