United Arab Emirates' own way: Geography at Grade 1 is homeland-first and picture-led: my environment and its pollution, my country's location on its own map with its landmarks, a traveller-hero (Ibn Battuta) traced on a world map, and the desert's own plants and animals — every lesson built from photos, colouring and tick-the-picture tasks. This lesson keeps that way: a traveller-hero followed on a map, his journeys counted in countries and split into land and sea stretches. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
