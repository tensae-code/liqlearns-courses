# Ibn Battuta — Traveller and Explorer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ibn Battuta is taught in the UAE Grade 1 book as —
a) a king  b) a traveller and explorer  c) a farmer
**Answer: b) a traveller and explorer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book shows Ibn Battuta's journeys —
a) in a table of dates  b) traced on a map  c) only in a song
**Answer: b) traced on a map**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ibn Battuta's journeys reached —
a) more than 40 countries  b) 4 countries  c) only his own country
**Answer: a) more than 40 countries**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** He travelled —
a) only by sea  b) only by land  c) by land and by sea
**Answer: c) by land and by sea**
