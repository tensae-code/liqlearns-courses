# Ibn Battuta — Traveller and Explorer — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the Grade 1 book teach Ibn Battuta's journeys?
**Answer: It traces them on a map, so the child follows his path across real places (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does tracing the line matter?
**Answer: Because a journey is a path across real places — some stretches by land and some by sea — and the map shows which is which (any close wording).**
