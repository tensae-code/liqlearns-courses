# Video script — Our Neighbourhood

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Meet Ron and Sue.

Ron does not live alone. Many families live near his home. They are his neighbours.

Their neighbourhood has a name: Green Valley. It has homes, a school, trees and roads. Name them with Ron.

And neighbours help each other. A carpenter builds and mends. A nurse cares for the sick.

Now look at your own street. Who are your neighbours?
