# Our Neighbourhood — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: who are your neighbours?
**Answer: The families who live in the homes near my own home (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three buildings or features you can find in a neighbourhood.
**Answer: Any three of: homes, the school, trees, roads, shops (or the learner's own neighbourhood's real buildings and features).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one worker in Ron and Sue's neighbourhood and say how that worker helps the neighbours.
**Answer: The carpenter, who builds and mends things made of wood; or the nurse, who cares for people who are sick.**
