# Our Neighbourhood — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Many families live in a neighbourhood, near each other.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A neighbourhood is only houses — it has no other buildings or features to name.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A nurse is a neighbourhood worker who cares for people who are sick.
**Answer: True**
