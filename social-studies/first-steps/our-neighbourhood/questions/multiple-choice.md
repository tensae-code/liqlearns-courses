# Our Neighbourhood — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The families who live near Ron's home are his —
a) classmates  b) neighbours  c) teachers
**Answer: b) neighbours**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A neighbourhood is —
a) one house on its own  b) the homes and families that live near each other around your home  c) a faraway country
**Answer: b) the homes and families that live near each other around your home**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The name of Ron and Sue's neighbourhood is —
a) Green Valley  b) Georgetown  c) Ron Street
**Answer: a) Green Valley**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Knowing your neighbourhood starts with —
a) counting its cars  b) naming its buildings and features  c) leaving it
**Answer: b) naming its buildings and features**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In Ron and Sue's neighbourhood, the father who builds and mends things made of wood is a —
a) nurse  b) driver  c) carpenter
**Answer: c) carpenter**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Neighbours help each other —
a) through their work  b) never  c) only on holidays
**Answer: a) through their work**
