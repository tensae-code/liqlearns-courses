# Hands-on — Our Neighbourhood

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The neighbourhood name-and-draw
Draw your own neighbourhood on paper: your home in the middle, the homes around it, and its buildings and features — the school, the trees, the roads, the shops. Name every part you draw, the way the book names Green Valley. Show your drawing to a partner and name each part out loud.

## The neighbours-who-help list
Write a list of the workers in your neighbourhood — like the carpenter and the nurse in Ron and Sue's neighbourhood. Next to each worker, write in five words or fewer how their work helps the neighbours. Compare lists with a partner.
