Guyana's own way: a story-led first social-studies book built around two children, Ron and Sue — every idea (school, neighbourhood, nation, weather) is met through their home, street and school day, with a draw/name/complete task on almost every page. This lesson keeps that way: the neighbourhood is met through Ron's home and street first, then named in the learner's own neighbourhood. The lesson teaches the placements' content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed. The book's non-Geography parts (Children, The Family, School Rules, Keeping Healthy, Our Nation, Celebrations) are named in the placements file and are not placed in this lesson.


Albania's own way (teacher guide — evidence): Citizenship in Grade 5 is taught as a ladder — family, group, community, then the state: democracy is first practised inside the family and in class elections, local self-government is compared with the class's own elections, and a whole chapter (Kreu 3, The Road) treats the child as pedestrian, cyclist and passenger with duties on the road.
