Guyana's own way: a story-led first social-studies book built around two children, Ron and Sue — every idea (school, neighbourhood, nation, weather) is met through their home, street and school day, with a draw/name/complete task on almost every page. This lesson keeps that way: the neighbourhood is met through Ron's home and street first, then named in the learner's own neighbourhood. The lesson teaches the placements' content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
