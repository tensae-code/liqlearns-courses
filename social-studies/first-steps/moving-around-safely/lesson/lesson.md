## Sub lesson 1 — I move around with safety

Greece Grade 2 Meleti Perivallontos B' Dimotikou (printed p. 116), teaches: Road safety as part of community life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — We move around with safety

Greece Grade 3 Meleti Perivallontos G' Dimotikou (printed p. 53), teaches: Safe movement in traffic as community behaviour. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Greece's way — the book's own teaching way

Greece's own way: Grade 2 widens Grade 1's circles — the neighbourhood is now studied service by service (its church named as a landmark, its green discovered, its past compared with today), and the child's first rights appear as their own unit: the right to life and the right to peace. Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
