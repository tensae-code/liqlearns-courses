# Hands-on — Communication in Our Province

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Communication
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The means of communication — television, telephone, radio, newspapers, letters, internet and signposts — and that communication lets people share knowledge, advertise their work, find where to trade and help one another. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Communication (Primary Five)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Traditional and modern means of communication (phones, internet, newspapers, post) and why good communication promotes peace, friendship, trade and development. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Rwanda's way
Say how Rwanda's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
