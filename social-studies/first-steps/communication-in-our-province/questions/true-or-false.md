# Communication in Our Province — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: the means of communication — television, telephone, radio, newspapers, letters, internet and signposts — and that communication lets people share knowledge, advertise their work, find where to trade and help one another.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: traditional and modern means of communication (phones, internet, newspapers, post) and why good communication promotes peace, friendship, trade and development.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Rwanda's student books, named in lesson/sources.md.**
