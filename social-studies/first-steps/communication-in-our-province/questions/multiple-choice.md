# Communication in Our Province — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Communication  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Communication**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) the means of communication — television, telephone, radio, newspapers, letters, internet and signposts — and that communication lets people share knowledge, advertise their work, find where to trade and help one another  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the means of communication — television, telephone, radio, newspapers, letters, internet and signposts — and that communication lets people share knowledge, advertise their work, find where to trade and help one another**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Communication (Primary Five)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Communication (Primary Five)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) traditional and modern means of communication (phones, internet, newspapers, post) and why good communication promotes peace, friendship, trade and development  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) traditional and modern means of communication (phones, internet, newspapers, post) and why good communication promotes peace, friendship, trade and development**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this lesson is —
a) Rwanda's way — the books' own teaching way  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Rwanda's way — the books' own teaching way**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches the means of communication — television, telephone, radio, newspapers, letters, internet and signposts — and that communication lets people share knowledge, advertise their work, find where to trade and help one another.  b) Rwanda's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) This lesson teaches the means of communication — television, telephone, radio, newspapers, letters, internet and signposts — and that communication lets people share knowledge, advertise their work, find where to trade and help one another.**
