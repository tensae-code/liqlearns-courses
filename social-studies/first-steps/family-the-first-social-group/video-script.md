Albania Grade 5 Qytetaria teacher guide (printed p. 19), teaches: The family as the first and most important group a person belongs to.

Albania Grade 5 Qytetaria teacher guide (printed p. 19), teaches: What a family does for its members.

Albania's own way: Citizenship in Grade 5 is taught as a ladder — family, group, community, then the state: democracy is first practised inside the family and in class elections, local self-government is compared with the class's own elections, and a whole chapter (Kreu 3, The Road) treats the child as pedestrian, cyclist and passenger with duties on the road.
