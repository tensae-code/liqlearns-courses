# Hands-on — My Teacher And Being a Good Pupil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 3 Багш бид хоёр (The teacher and I)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The good teacher's marks (compassionate, wise, diligent, tireless) and the good pupil's marks (honest, diligent, respectful, devoted to learning, thoughtful), with the pupil's three faults drawn as three teapots — the dirty, the tipped-over and the cracked. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
