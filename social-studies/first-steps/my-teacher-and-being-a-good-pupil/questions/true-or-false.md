# My Teacher And Being a Good Pupil — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the good teacher's marks (compassionate, wise, diligent, tireless) and the good pupil's marks (honest, diligent, respectful, devoted to learning, thoughtful), with the pupil's three faults drawn as three teapots — the dirty, the tipped-over and the cracked.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
