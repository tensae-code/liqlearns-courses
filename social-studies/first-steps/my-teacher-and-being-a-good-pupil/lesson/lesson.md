## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 3 Багш бид хоёр (The teacher and I)

Mongolia Grade 1 Civic Education, printed p. 7, teaches: The good teacher's marks (compassionate, wise, diligent, tireless) and the good pupil's marks (honest, diligent, respectful, devoted to learning, thoughtful), with the pupil's three faults drawn as three teapots — the dirty, the tipped-over and the cracked. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: civic education for six-year-olds is pure Mongolian custom — greeting elders the Mongolian way, the ger and the five snouts, colour symbolism, the biyelgee dance and the Tsagaan Sar greeting embrace — with traditional script verses running down every page beside the Cyrillic. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
