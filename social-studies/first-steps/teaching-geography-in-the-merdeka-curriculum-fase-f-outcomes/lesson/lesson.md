## Sub lesson 2 — Indonesia's way — the book's own teaching way

Indonesia's own way (Grade 11 teacher guide — evidence): a Kurikulum Merdeka teacher guide — a general part sets out the Fase F learning outcomes (Capaian Pembelajaran), Pancasila-student-profile targets per chapter, teaching strategies and assessment design; a special part then guides each student-book chapter activity by activity, with prerequisite concepts, pre-learning assessment, competence + character scoring rubrics for every activity, answer keys and reflection. Note: its Bab IV is retitled "Perubahan Iklim dan Kebencanaan" (climate change and disasters), widening the student book's disaster chapter. Indonesia's own way (Grade 12 teacher guide — evidence): Kurikulum Merdeka guide mirroring the Grade 12 student book: a general part (Fase F outcomes, how the student book works, general strategies) then a chapter guide for each student chapter, each with the teacher's role, alternative learning routes, named misconceptions to correct, handling of special-needs learners, parent interaction, assessment and answer keys. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
