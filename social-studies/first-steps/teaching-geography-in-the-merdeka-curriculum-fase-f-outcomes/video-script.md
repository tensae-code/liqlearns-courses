# Video script — Teaching Geography in the Merdeka Curriculum — Fase F Outcomes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Indonesia's book teaches: Panduan Umum: Pendahuluan, Capaian Pembelajaran Fase F, strategies and assessment design for Geography (Panduan Guru Geografi untuk SMA/MA Kelas XI (Edisi Revisi), Grade 11, printed p. 2); Bab 1 Panduan Umum: Pendahuluan, Capaian Pembelajaran Fase F, Penjelasan Buku Siswa, Strategi Umum Pembelajaran (Buku Panduan Guru Geografi untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII, Grade 12, printed p. 1).

Indonesia's own way (Grade 11 teacher guide — evidence): a Kurikulum Merdeka teacher guide — a general part sets out the Fase F learning outcomes (Capaian Pembelajaran), Pancasila-student-profile targets per chapter, teaching strategies and assessment design; a special part then guides each student-book chapter activity by activity, with prerequisite concepts, pre-learning assessment, competence + character scoring rubrics for every activity, answer keys and reflection. Note: its Bab IV is retitled "Perubahan Iklim dan Kebencanaan" (climate change and disasters), widening the student book's disaster chapter. Indonesia's own way (Grade 12 teacher guide — evidence): Kurikulum Merdeka guide mirroring the Grade 12 student book: a general part (Fase F outcomes, how the student book works, general strategies) then a chapter guide for each student chapter, each with the teacher's role, alternative learning routes, named misconceptions to correct, handling of special-needs learners, parent interaction, assessment and answer keys.
