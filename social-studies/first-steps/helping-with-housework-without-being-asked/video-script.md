# Video script — Helping with Housework Without Being Asked

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba Grade 3 El mundo en que vivimos (printed p. 236), teaches: Helping at home is an important task for your family — putting school materials and your uniform in their place when you arrive keeps the house clean and beautiful.

Cuba's own way: third-graders join Nature Protection Brigades — organised pioneer groups that clean and care for the classroom, school and community — and the book's closing civics chapter binds courtesy, the patriotic symbols and the traffic law into one code of “comportamiento social”.
