# Helping with Housework Without Being Asked

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: helping at home is an important task for your family — putting school materials and your uniform in their place when you arrive keeps the house clean and beautiful — as Cuba's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Helping with Housework Without Being Asked

Cuba Grade 3 El mundo en que vivimos (printed p. 236), teaches: Helping at home is an important task for your family — putting school materials and your uniform in their place when you arrive keeps the house clean and beautiful. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way

Cuba's own way: third-graders join Nature Protection Brigades — organised pioneer groups that clean and care for the classroom, school and community — and the book's closing civics chapter binds courtesy, the patriotic symbols and the traffic law into one code of “comportamiento social”. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
