# Hands-on — Relations Between Friends

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The relation between friends is social bonds linking the individual with other individuals close to him in age, study level or different life interests, and this relation rests on a set of foundations, among them the shared hobbies, the similarity of social behaviour, familiarity and sympathy, and how free time is spent; and the relation with peers and friends is among the most influential social relations in the individual's life, for it affects the individuals' upbringing and socialisation, so families are always keen to follow their children's friends and direct the children to choose the good and righteous friends, to protect their children from the errors and problems some bad friends may cause. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
