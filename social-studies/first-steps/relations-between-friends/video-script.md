The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies (printed p. 141), teaches: The relation between friends is social bonds linking the individual with other individuals close to him in age, study level or different life interests, and this relation rests on a set of foundations, among them the shared hobbies, the similarity of social behaviour, familiarity and sympathy, and how free time is spent; and the relation with peers and friends is among the most influential social relations in the individual's life, for it affects the individuals' upbringing and socialisation, so families are always keen to follow their children's friends and direct the children to choose the good and righteous friends, to protect their children from the errors and problems some bad friends may cause.

Iraq's own way: values are defined first as a system — standards that steer behaviour and spread peace "regardless of sex, religion, nationality, language or race" — and citizenship is cashed out in named rights (education, work, candidacy, the vote, freedom of opinion and belief), while the internet chapter warns in plain terms that the open network "is governed by no restrictions or conditions".
