# Video script — Ways of Communicating and Getting Informed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece Grade 2 Meleti Perivallontos B' Dimotikou (printed p. 118), teaches: How people communicate and learn the news.

Greece Grade 3 Meleti Perivallontos G' Dimotikou (printed p. 131), teaches: How people communicate with each other.

Greece's own way: Grade 2 widens Grade 1's circles — the neighbourhood is now studied service by service (its church named as a landmark, its green discovered, its past compared with today), and the child's first rights appear as their own unit: the right to life and the right to peace. Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos.
