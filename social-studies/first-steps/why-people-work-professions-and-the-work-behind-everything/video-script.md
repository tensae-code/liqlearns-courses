# Video script — Why People Work Professions and the Work Behind Everything

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kazakhstan Grade 2 Poznanie mira (970/980) (printed p. 18, printed p. 14, printed p. 54), teaches: why people work — everything we use is made or grown by people's work, labour is mental and physical, work is rewarded, each profession benefits others, and professions have their own holidays.

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 turns the Grade 1 circles outward — the child meets "society" itself, etiquette as society's rules and the golden rule of politeness, then rights and duties read straight out of the Constitution, and service to society taught through volunteers and four named Kazakh figures (Abai, Akhmet Baitursynov, Kanysh Satpaev, Bauyrzhan Momyshuly). Kazakhstan's own way (Book 4): Grade 3 gives the child the Constitution itself — its preamble read aloud ("We, the people of Kazakhstan…"), the citizen's rights drawn as a diagram and Constitution Day named — and roots the family in the Kazakh shezhire, the genealogy every Kazakh was expected to know to the seventh generation (Zheti ata). Kazakhstan's own way (Book 5): Grade 4 is where the primary course turns civic in earnest — the republic's own map of 14 oblasts and three cities of republican significance, an elected school council drawn as a working model of the state, the Constitution's rights quoted article by article, and the country's face to the world built from the UN seat, the closed Semipalatinsk test site and EXPO-2017.
