# Hands-on — Loving and Helping Family Members with Special Needs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Loving Family Members
Work through this part with your hands: what a family is (nuclear, single-parent and extended), every member's role and need to be loved, respected and heard, and how to help members with special needs — children, the elderly, people with disabilities, blindness, deafness, mental illness and albinism — without denying them their rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Loving Yourself and Others
Work through this part with your hands: love is caring for yourself and others; people with special needs — the aged, disabled, poor and orphans — need more love because of their social and physical conditions, and we must not hurt them by our acts. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
