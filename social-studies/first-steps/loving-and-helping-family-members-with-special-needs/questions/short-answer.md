# Loving and Helping Family Members with Special Needs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Loving Family Members — what a family is (nuclear, single-parent and extended), every member's role and need to be loved, respected and heard, and how to help members with special needs — children, the elderly, people with disabilities, blindness, deafness, mental illness and albinism — without denying them their rights (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Loving Yourself and Others — love is caring for yourself and others; people with special needs — the aged, disabled, poor and orphans — need more love because of their social and physical conditions, and we must not hurt them by our acts (any close wording).**
