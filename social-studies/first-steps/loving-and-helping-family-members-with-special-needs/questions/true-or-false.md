# Loving and Helping Family Members with Special Needs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part 1 teaches: What a family is (nuclear, single-parent and extended), every member's role and need to be loved, respected and heard, and how to help members with special needs — children, the elderly, people with disabilities, blindness, deafness, mental illness and albinism — without denying them their rights.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part 2 teaches: Love is caring for yourself and others; people with special needs — the aged, disabled, poor and orphans — need more love because of their social and physical conditions, and we must not hurt them by our acts.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on Tanzania's student books, counted in the acceptance count in lesson/sources.md.
**Answer: True**
