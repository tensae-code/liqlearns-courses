## Sub lesson 2 — Panama's way — the book's own teaching way

Panama's own way: Grade 1 social studies is a home workbook ("Conéctate en casa") worked through with family; rights and duties are taught as "normas de convivencia" — the rules of living together — sorted by the child into family, school and community. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
