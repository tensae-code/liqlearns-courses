# Video script — The Aiga and the Matai Samoas Family Order

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 9 Book 1 (printed p. 62), teaches: How families are organised (nuclear, extended, matriarchal, patriarchal) and Samoa's aiga as the most important unit of the fa'aSamoa, headed by the matai who holds pule (authority) over the aiga's traditional lands.

Samoa's own way: Social organisation is taught from the inside out — the groups a child belongs to first, then the aiga and its matai, then the village's own circle of groups (matai at the centre, aumaga as the matai's right hand), and only then a neighbour kingdom's political system as the worked example of government.
