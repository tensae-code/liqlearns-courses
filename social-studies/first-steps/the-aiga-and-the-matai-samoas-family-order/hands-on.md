# Hands-on — The Aiga and the Matai Samoas Family Order

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How families are organised (nuclear, extended, matriarchal, patriarchal) and Samoa's aiga as the most important unit of the fa'aSamoa, headed by the matai who holds pule (authority) over the aiga's traditional lands. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Samoa's way
Say how Samoa's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
