# Hands-on — Who Will Do This Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ch 16 Who Will Do This Work?
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: cleaning work falls on one community by birth — Gandhi made everyone at his ashram clean toilets, young Bhim (Ambedkar) was refused a haircut, and some schoolgirls are still made to clean: work must carry dignity, not caste. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
