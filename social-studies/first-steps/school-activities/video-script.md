# Video script — School Activities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 4 Social Studies (printed p. 51), teaches: The school carries out several educational activities alongside its teaching activity — scientific activities such as the science competitions between pupils, recreational trips, artistic activities (especially in the religious and national celebrations) and exhibitions, and social activities; those activities are nothing but the pupil's opportunity to express his ideas and talents, bringing joy into himself and making him and his classmates part of the school.

Iraq's own way: civics starts at the school desk — the first community the Grade 4 child is given duties toward is the school family — and the homeland is then built piece by piece (land, people, waters, sky), with every one of Iraq's communities named in the population list, before rights are matched one-for-one with duties.
