# Hands-on — School Activities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The school carries out several educational activities alongside its teaching activity — scientific activities such as the science competitions between pupils, recreational trips, artistic activities (especially in the religious and national celebrations) and exhibitions, and social activities; those activities are nothing but the pupil's opportunity to express his ideas and talents, bringing joy into himself and making him and his classmates part of the school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
