# Video script — The Education System and the School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece Grade 6 Koinoniki kai Politiki Agogi ST' Dimotikou (printed p. 17), teaches: How Greece's education system is built and where the pupil's school sits in it.

Greece's own way: two Aristophanes characters (Dikaiopolis and Eirini, "Justice-City" and "Peace") guide the pupil outward from the family to the state, the EU and the world; Greece's own municipalities, constitution and EU membership are the worked examples.
