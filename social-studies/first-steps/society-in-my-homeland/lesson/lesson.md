## Sub lesson 1 — Society in my homeland

Jordan Grade 2 SS G2 T1, printed p. 44, teaches: What a society is — the people who live together in the homeland's towns and villages and share one life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Society

Jordan Grade 3 SS G3 T1, printed p. 16, teaches: My duty to society — what society is and what the child owes the people around him. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Jordan's way — the book's own teaching way

Jordan's own way: Grade 2 civics is the family first, then the child's own rights — safety, health care, education and play, each as "my right" — and only then society around the child. duty is taught as four widening circles — family, school, society, homeland — and citizenship is two skills: saying your opinion and keeping the rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
