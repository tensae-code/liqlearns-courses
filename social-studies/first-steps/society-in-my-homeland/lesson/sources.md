**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Jordan).**

Jordan's own way (Book 4): Grade 2 civics is the family first, then the child's own rights — safety, health care, education and play, each as "my right" — and only then society around the child.

Jordan's own way (Book 6): duty is taught as four widening circles — family, school, society, homeland — and citizenship is two skills: saying your opinion and keeping the rules.
