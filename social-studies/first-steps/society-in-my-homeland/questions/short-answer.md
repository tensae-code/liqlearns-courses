# Society in My Homeland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Society in my homeland — What a society is — the people who live together in the homeland's towns and villages and share one life (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Society — My duty to society — what society is and what the child owes the people around him (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Jordan's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 2 civics is the family first, then the child's own rights — safety, health care, education and play, each as "my right" — and only then society around the child. duty is taught as four widening circles — family, school, society, homeland — and citizenship is two skills: saying your opinion and keeping the rules. (any close wording).**
