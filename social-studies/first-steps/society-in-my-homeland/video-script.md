# Video script — Society in My Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan Grade 2 SS G2 T1, printed p. 44, teaches: What a society is — the people who live together in the homeland's towns and villages and share one life.

Jordan Grade 3 SS G3 T1, printed p. 16, teaches: My duty to society — what society is and what the child owes the people around him.

Jordan's own way: Grade 2 civics is the family first, then the child's own rights — safety, health care, education and play, each as "my right" — and only then society around the child. duty is taught as four widening circles — family, school, society, homeland — and citizenship is two skills: saying your opinion and keeping the rules.
