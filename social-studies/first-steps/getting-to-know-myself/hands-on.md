# Hands-on — Getting to Know Myself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 1 A. Aku Mengenal Diriku, Bab 1 A. Aku, Keluarga, dan Temanku, Bab 1 D/F. Materi Esensial dan Panduan Pembelajaran: Aku dan Teman-Temanku — teaches: introducing yourself, naming who you are and your body/identity, through the five character friends; me, my family and my friends — individual identity read through family and classmates, with Indonesia's cultural diversity met in picture form; essential material and lesson-by-lesson guidance for self, friends and play rules, with parent/community interaction tasks. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indonesia's way
Say how Indonesia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
