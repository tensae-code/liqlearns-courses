**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Indonesia).** Indonesia's teacher-guide parts in this lesson are teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere — Indonesia counts through its student-book parts.

Indonesia's own way (Book 1): Pancasila values are taught from the child's own body, friends and family outward — five recurring child characters from Papua, Bali, Jakarta, Sulawesi and Kalimantan carry every chapter, so national unity is shown as named children from different islands.

Indonesia's own way (Book 2): Grade 3 is the first book to name the state the child lives in — your own street is taught as part of the NKRI (Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia), and rights are introduced paired, always, with duties.

Indonesia's own way (Book 3): the guide frames the whole subject through the Profil Pelajar Pancasila — six learner dimensions (faith and noble character, global diversity, gotong royong, independence, critical reasoning, creativity) that every chapter must build.
