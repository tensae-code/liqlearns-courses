The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Indonesia's Grade 1 PP-G1 student (printed p. 3); Grade 3 PP-G3 student (printed p. 3); Grade 1 PP-G1 teacher (printed p. 33), teaches: Bab 1 A. Aku Mengenal Diriku, Bab 1 A. Aku, Keluarga, dan Temanku, Bab 1 D/F. Materi Esensial dan Panduan Pembelajaran: Aku dan Teman-Temanku — teaches: introducing yourself, naming who you are and your body/identity, through the five character friends; me, my family and my friends — individual identity read through family and classmates, with Indonesia's cultural diversity met in picture form; essential material and lesson-by-lesson guidance for self, friends and play rules, with parent/community interaction tasks.

Indonesia's own way: Pancasila values are taught from the child's own body, friends and family outward — five recurring child characters from Papua, Bali, Jakarta, Sulawesi and Kalimantan carry every chapter, so national unity is shown as named children from different islands. Grade 3 is the first book to name the state the child lives in — your own street is taught as part of the NKRI (Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia), and rights are introduced paired, always, with duties. the guide frames the whole subject through the Profil Pelajar Pancasila — six learner dimensions (faith and noble character, global diversity, gotong royong, independence, critical reasoning, creativity) that every chapter must build.
