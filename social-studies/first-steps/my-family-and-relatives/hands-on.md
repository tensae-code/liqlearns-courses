# Hands-on — My Family and Relatives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Naming family relations and how we show respect to different people — grandparents, elders, neighbours. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vanuatu's way
Say how Vanuatu's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
