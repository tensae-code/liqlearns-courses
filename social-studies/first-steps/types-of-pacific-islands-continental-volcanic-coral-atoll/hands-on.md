# Hands-on — Types of Pacific Islands — Continental, Volcanic, Coral, Atoll

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Strand 3 Place and Environment, 3.1 Features of Places: the South Pacific and its four island types (continental, volcanic, coral limestone, atoll) and their size, soils and life; Geography of Fiji: types of islands and their characteristics (volcanic high islands, coral islands/islets such as Nukulau and Wailagilala, reefs); Chapter 8: Oceania's three island groups (continental islands, volcanic high islands, coral raised limestone and atoll islands) and how each is formed. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
