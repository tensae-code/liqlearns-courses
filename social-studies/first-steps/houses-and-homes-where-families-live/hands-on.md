# Hands-on — Houses And Homes Where Families Live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: La casita de una ronda y nuestras casas; La casa de Mateo es así; Nuestra vivienda familiar / A cada familia su casa; La vivienda indígena; Un paseo por la casa; Tu casa y sus alrededores: kinds of housing, rural and urban dwellings. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
