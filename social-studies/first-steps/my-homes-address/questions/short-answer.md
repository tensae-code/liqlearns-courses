# My Homes Address — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: My Homes Address — when the family grows, a bigger house is needed; every house has an address (نشانی) that tells where it is — the square's name, the street, the alley, the house number (پلاک) and the floor; mother writes the new address on a paper in the pupil's bag, and the pupil reads it to the school-bus driver: Ferdowsi Square, Mehr Street, Omid Alley, No. 15 (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Iran's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
