# My Homes Address — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) My Homes Address  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) My Homes Address**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) when the family grows, a bigger house is needed; every house has an address (نشانی) that tells where it is — the square's name, the street, the alley, the house number (پلاک) and the floor; mother writes the new address on a paper in the pupil's bag, and the pupil reads it to the school-bus driver: Ferdowsi Square, Mehr Street, Omid Alley, No. 15  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Iran's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) Iran's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches when the family grows, a bigger house is needed.  b) Iran's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
