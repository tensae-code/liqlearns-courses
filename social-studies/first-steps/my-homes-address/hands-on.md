# Hands-on — My Homes Address

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: when the family grows, a bigger house is needed; every house has an address (نشانی) that tells where it is — the square's name, the street, the alley, the house number (پلاک) and the floor; mother writes the new address on a paper in the pupil's bag, and the pupil reads it to the school-bus driver: Ferdowsi Square, Mehr Street, Omid Alley, No. 15. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
