# Video script — My Homes Address

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 22, teaches: when the family grows, a bigger house is needed; every house has an address (نشانی) that tells where it is — the square's name, the street, the alley, the house number (پلاک) and the floor; mother writes the new address on a paper in the pupil's bag, and the pupil reads it to the school-bus driver: Ferdowsi Square, Mehr Street, Omid Alley, No. 15.

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces.
