# Historiography — history of history-writing — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does historiography study?
**Answer:** How knowledge of the past is attained and transmitted — how history itself has been written through the ages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Ethiopia's two shelves of history-writing and the date of each turn.
**Answer:** The indigenous chronicle tradition (centuries old, changed in the early 20th century) and academic history (from the 1960s).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is Ranke's demand — build history from documents — a turning point?
**Answer:** Because it made evidence, tested in archives, the foundation of the discipline rather than tradition or royal praise.
