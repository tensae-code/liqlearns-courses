# Historiography — history of history-writing — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Leopold von Ranke is named father of modern historiography.
**Answer: True** — The man of the archives.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia had no written history tradition before the 1960s.
**Answer: False** — Its indigenous chronicle tradition is centuries old; the 1960s mark its academic form.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Herodotus and Thucydides were Chinese record-keepers.
**Answer: False** — Both were Greek; China's records are older still.
**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The Greek inquirer named the father of history — this is Herodotus.
**Answer: True**
