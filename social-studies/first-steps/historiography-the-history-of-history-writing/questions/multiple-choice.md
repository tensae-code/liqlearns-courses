# Historiography — history of history-writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Historiography is the study of —
a) old coins  b) how knowledge of the past is attained and transmitted  c) map-making
**Answer: b) how knowledge of the past is attained and transmitted** — History of history-writing itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The oldest written history is placed in —
a) Greece  b) China  c) Egypt
**Answer: b) China** — Records before 1000 BC.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The father of history is —
a) Thucydides  b) Ranke  c) Herodotus
**Answer: c) Herodotus** — Herodotus, the great collector.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Thucydides is named the first — writer.
a) romantic  b) critical  c) royal
**Answer: b) critical** — He weighed and questioned accounts.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** History became an academic discipline in —
a) the later 19th century  b) 1000 BC  c) the 1960s everywhere
**Answer: a) the later 19th century** — The 1960s date is Ethiopia's academic start.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopian academic history dates from —
a) 1270  b) the 1960s  c) the early 20th century
**Answer: b) the 1960s** — The chronicle tradition changed in the early 20th century; the academic discipline dates from the 1960s.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What is historiography?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) the Greek inquirer named the father of history
c) the study of how history has been written
**Answer: c) the study of how history has been written**
