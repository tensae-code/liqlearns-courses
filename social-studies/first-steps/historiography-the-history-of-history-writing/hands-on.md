# Hands-on — Historiography — history of history-writing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the shelf
On five cards write: Chinese records, Herodotus, Thucydides, Ranke, Ethiopian chronicle. Arrange them in order on a table 'shelf', then defend the order to a partner using dates.

## Chronicler for a day
Write a six-line royal-chronicle entry for an ordinary school day, in the old style — then rewrite the same day as a critical modern paragraph. Read both aloud; the class names what each style adds and hides.

## Grade 11 depth — How history-writing itself changed

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
