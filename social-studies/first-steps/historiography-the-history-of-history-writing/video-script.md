# Video script — Historiography — history of history-writing

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The camera walks a long shelf. First, scrolls — China, before 1000 BC. The oldest written history.
Then Greece. Herodotus gathers. Thucydides questions. Father of history — and its first critic.
The shelf jumps centuries. An archive door opens. Ranke: documents, or it did not happen.
And here — a shelf of Ethiopia's own. Royal chronicles, written at court for ages.
History has a history. Now you know its name: historiography.
