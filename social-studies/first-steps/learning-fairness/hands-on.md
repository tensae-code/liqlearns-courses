# Hands-on — Learning Fairness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 19 "Let's learn fairness" (Шударга ёсонд суралцъя)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Fairness (шударга ёс) means one rule for all — everyone under the same rules, the same responsibility and the same punishment for breaking them, no one treated better or worse than another; good deeds are encouraged and bad ones never supported; worked through cases — is it fair that only the girls wash up after the picnic, is the teacher's punishment of the boy who drew on the desk fair, should only the best players vote for the team leader?. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
