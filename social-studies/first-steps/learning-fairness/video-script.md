# Video script — Learning Fairness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 72, teaches: Fairness (шударга ёс) means one rule for all — everyone under the same rules, the same responsibility and the same punishment for breaking them, no one treated better or worse than another; good deeds are encouraged and bad ones never supported; worked through cases — is it fair that only the girls wash up after the picnic, is the teacher's punishment of the boy who drew on the desk fair, should only the best players vote for the team leader?.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
