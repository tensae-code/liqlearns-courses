# Learning Fairness — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 19 "Let's learn fairness" (Шударга ёсонд суралцъя) — Fairness (шударга ёс) means one rule for all — everyone under the same rules, the same responsibility and the same punishment for breaking them, no one treated better or worse than another; good deeds are encouraged and bad ones never supported; worked through cases — is it fair that only the girls wash up after the picnic, is the teacher's punishment of the boy who drew on the desk fair, should only the best players vote for the team leader? (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone (any close wording).**
