# Learning Fairness — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: fairness (шударга ёс) means one rule for all — everyone under the same rules, the same responsibility and the same punishment for breaking them, no one treated better or worse than another; good deeds are encouraged and bad ones never supported; worked through cases — is it fair that only the girls wash up after the picnic, is the teacher's punishment of the boy who drew on the desk fair, should only the best players vote for the team leader?.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
