# Hands-on — My Address Where My House Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 20 Where is your house? (خانه‌ی شما کجاست؟) — has it ever happened that you got lost in the alley or street? Ali's house is on Hafez street: knowing where your house is and its address. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
