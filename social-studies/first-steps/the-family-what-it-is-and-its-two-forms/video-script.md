# Video script — The Family What It is and Its Two Forms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 1, printed p. 74, teaches: a family is a group of people bound by strong kinship ties (parents, children, grandparents, uncles, aunts); Palestinian society holds two forms — the small nuclear family (father, mother, children) and the large extended family, the hamula (adding uncles and grandparents).

Palestine's own way: the first civics unit opens with the child's own right to live inside a family and a state; the Palestinian family is taught in its two real forms — the small nuclear family and the large extended hamula — and the state lesson is taught under occupation, asking openly what prevents Palestine's independence while naming Jerusalem as its capital.
