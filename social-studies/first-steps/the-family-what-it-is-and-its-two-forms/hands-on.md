# Hands-on — The Family What It is and Its Two Forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Family What It is and Its Two Forms
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A family is a group of people bound by strong kinship ties (parents, children, grandparents, uncles, aunts); Palestinian society holds two forms — the small nuclear family (father, mother, children) and the large extended family, the hamula (adding uncles and grandparents). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Palestine's way
Say how Palestine's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
