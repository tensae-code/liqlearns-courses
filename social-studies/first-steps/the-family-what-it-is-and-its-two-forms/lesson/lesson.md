## Sub lesson 1 — The Family What It is and Its Two Forms

Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 1 (printed p. 74), teaches: a family is a group of people bound by strong kinship ties (parents, children, grandparents, uncles, aunts); Palestinian society holds two forms — the small nuclear family (father, mother, children) and the large extended family, the hamula (adding uncles and grandparents). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way

Palestine's own way (Grade 5 Social Studies Part 1): the first civics unit opens with the child's own right to live inside a family and a state; the Palestinian family is taught in its two real forms — the small nuclear family and the large extended hamula — and the state lesson is taught under occupation, asking openly what prevents Palestine's independence while naming Jerusalem as its capital. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
