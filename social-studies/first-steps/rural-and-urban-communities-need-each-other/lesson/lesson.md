## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: Escuela Nueva self-paced guides (parts A–D) set in rural Colombia; rights are introduced through Colombia's own documents (Political Constitution, Declaration of the Rights of the Child, Universal Declaration of Human Rights), and local government through the real Colombian ladder department → municipality → corregimiento → vereda, with resguardos inside municipalities. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
