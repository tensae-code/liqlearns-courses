# Video script — Rural and Urban Communities Need Each Other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 2 CS_Grado2_01, printed pp. 78–85, teaches: rural communities (veredas, farm work, teachers, doctors, community leaders) and urban communities (companies, public services, market squares) need each other and exchange products and services.

Colombia's own way: Escuela Nueva self-paced guides (parts A–D) set in rural Colombia; rights are introduced through Colombia's own documents (Political Constitution, Declaration of the Rights of the Child, Universal Declaration of Human Rights), and local government through the real Colombian ladder department → municipality → corregimiento → vereda, with resguardos inside municipalities.
