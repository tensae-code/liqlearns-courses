# Hands-on — I Am Able What I Can Do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every child can do something that marks them out (gymnastics, solving the cube fast), abilities differ from child to child and grow as you grow. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
