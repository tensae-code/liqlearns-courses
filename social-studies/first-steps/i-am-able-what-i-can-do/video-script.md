# Video script — I Am Able What I Can Do

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Syria Grade 1 Social Studies (Syria Grade 1 Social Studies G1, printed p. 13), teaches: Every child can do something that marks them out (gymnastics, solving the cube fast), abilities differ from child to child and grow as you grow

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 1): the whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free).
