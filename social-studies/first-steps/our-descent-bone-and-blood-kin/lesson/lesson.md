# Our Descent Bone And Blood Kin

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: father's kin are "bone kin" (яс төрөл) and mother's kin "blood kin" (цусан төрөл); the generations above me are named up the tree, and the genealogy chart is drawn with its signs; Chinggis Khan knew his own descent for twenty-two generations, and the ancestors kept the genealogy book so the line stays pure — "if the source is muddy, the stream is muddy" — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 1 "Our descent" (Манай удам)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 5, teaches: Father's kin are "bone kin" (яс төрөл) and mother's kin "blood kin" (цусан төрөл); the generations above me are named up the tree, and the genealogy chart is drawn with its signs; Chinggis Khan knew his own descent for twenty-two generations, and the ancestors kept the genealogy book so the line stays pure — "if the source is muddy, the stream is muddy". This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
