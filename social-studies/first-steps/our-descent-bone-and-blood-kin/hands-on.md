# Hands-on — Our Descent Bone And Blood Kin

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 1 "Our descent" (Манай удам)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Father's kin are "bone kin" (яс төрөл) and mother's kin "blood kin" (цусан төрөл); the generations above me are named up the tree, and the genealogy chart is drawn with its signs; Chinggis Khan knew his own descent for twenty-two generations, and the ancestors kept the genealogy book so the line stays pure — "if the source is muddy, the stream is muddy". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
