The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 5, teaches: Father's kin are "bone kin" (яс төрөл) and mother's kin "blood kin" (цусан төрөл); the generations above me are named up the tree, and the genealogy chart is drawn with its signs; Chinggis Khan knew his own descent for twenty-two generations, and the ancestors kept the genealogy book so the line stays pure — "if the source is muddy, the stream is muddy".

Mongolia's own way (Link book L1): Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for.
