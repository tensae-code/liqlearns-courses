# Our Descent Bone And Blood Kin — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: father's kin are "bone kin" (яс төрөл) and mother's kin "blood kin" (цусан төрөл); the generations above me are named up the tree, and the genealogy chart is drawn with its signs; Chinggis Khan knew his own descent for twenty-two generations, and the ancestors kept the genealogy book so the line stays pure — "if the source is muddy, the stream is muddy".
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
