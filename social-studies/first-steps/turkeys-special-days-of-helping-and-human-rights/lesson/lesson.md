## Sub lesson 3 — Turkey's own way (Hayat Bilgisi 2. Sınıf, 1. Kitap) — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: Hayat Bilgisi (Life Studies) is Turkey's integrated primary subject, rebuilt under the Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli as six learning areas; this first book runs Me-and-My-School → My Health and Safety → My Family and Society, every topic in the same rhythm (remember, explore, knowledge journey, practise, what we learned), with the year's special days carried as poems at the head of each area and the Student Oath (Andımız) printed at the front. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Turkey's own way (Hayat Bilgisi 3. Sınıf, 1. Kitap) — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: Grade 3 is where Hayat Bilgisi turns explicitly civic: an eight-year-old is taught that rights and responsibilities are a pair, and then reads the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child itself — in a child-worded card set (definition of the child, no discrimination, best interests, life and development) — two years before any civics course exists. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
