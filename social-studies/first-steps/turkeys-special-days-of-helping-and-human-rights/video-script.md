# Video script — Turkeys Special Days of Helping and Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed pp. 46, 80, teaches: the year's helping-and-values days as poems — Red Crescent Week (Kızılay Haftası, 29 October–4 November), World Disability Day (3 December), Human Rights and Democracy Week, and the Thrift, Investment and Turkish Goods Week (Tutum, Yatırım ve Türk Malları Haftası) — the special-days poems the History wave named but did not place

Turkey Grade 3 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed pp. 44, 76, teaches: Red Crescent Week, World Children's Rights Day, World Disability Day and the Human Rights and Democracy Week poem of the Grade 3 year (the Domestic Goods Week poem in the same spread is a celebration poem, named not placed — the Economics wave's verdict on its Grade 2 twin stands)

Turkey's own way: Hayat Bilgisi (Life Studies) is Turkey's integrated primary subject, rebuilt under the Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli as six learning areas; this first book runs Me-and-My-School → My Health and Safety → My Family and Society, every topic in the same rhythm (remember, explore, knowledge journey, practise, what we learned), with the year's special days carried as poems at the head of each area and the Student Oath (Andımız) printed at the front.

Turkey's own way: Grade 3 is where Hayat Bilgisi turns explicitly civic: an eight-year-old is taught that rights and responsibilities are a pair, and then reads the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child itself — in a child-worded card set (definition of the child, no discrimination, best interests, life and development) — two years before any civics course exists.
