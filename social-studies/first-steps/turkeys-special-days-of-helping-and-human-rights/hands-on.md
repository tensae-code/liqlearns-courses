# Hands-on — Turkeys Special Days of Helping and Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the year's helping-and-values days as poems — Red Crescent Week (Kızılay Haftası, 29 October–4 November), World Disability Day (3 December), Human Rights and Democracy Week, and the Thrift, Investment and Turkish Goods Week (Tutum, Yatırım ve Türk Malları Haftası) — the special-days poems the History wave named but did not place Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Belirli Gün ve Haftalar spreads of Areas 2–3
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Red Crescent Week, World Children's Rights Day, World Disability Day and the Human Rights and Democracy Week poem of the Grade 3 year (the Domestic Goods Week poem in the same spread is a celebration poem, named not placed — the Economics wave's verdict on its Grade 2 twin stands) Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
