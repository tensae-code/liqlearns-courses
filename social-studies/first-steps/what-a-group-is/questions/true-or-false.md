# What a Group is — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: A group is made up of two or more persons who share common characteristics; groups at school — the class, the football club in its uniform.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: A group can be known by its uniform, its name and its emblem or badge — the Cub Scouts and their promise.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
