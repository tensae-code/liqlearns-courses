# Hands-on — Public Property and Private Property

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Public property (schools, hospitals, parks, streets, government buildings) belongs to everyone and must be preserved, unlike private property (house, car), through a father-and-son dialogue and picture sorting. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahrain's way
Say how Bahrain's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
