# Hands-on — Coping with Changes in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: family changes (death, moving house, divorce, new baby, parent working away) can make children sad or confused; cope by talking to trusted adults, playing, singing, drawing, helping at home and seeing a counsellor if very sad. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
