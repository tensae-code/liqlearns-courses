# Video script — Children Are Special

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 1 Social Studies For Our Children Book 1 (printed p. 1), teaches: Every boy and girl is a child, and children are special because their parents love and care for them.

Guyana's own way: a story-led first book built around two children, Ron and Sue — family, school, rules and nation are all met through their home, street and school day, with a draw/name/complete task on almost every page.
