# Video script — My Neighbourhood And Its Places

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan's book, the *Jordan G1 SS T1* (printed p. 29), teaches: Unit 1 Lesson 5: The Neighbourhood (الحي) — homes, school, grocery, health centre and civil defence in my neighbourhood.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G1 SS T1): Social Studies opens at Grade 1 with the child's own circles — self, family, neighbourhood — then the homeland (Jordan's map, capital, flag, king), all taught through colouring, matching and picture talk rather than text.
