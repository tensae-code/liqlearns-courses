## Sub lesson 2 — Zimbabwe's way — the book's own teaching way

Zimbabwe's own way (ECD A): Heritage-Based Curriculum organised by cross-cutting topics repeated at every level (Identity, National history, Heritage, Work and leisure, Transport and communication, Shelter, Global issues…); teaching is play-, song- and role-play-based with workbook colouring/cutting tasks; local examples throughout (totems, pada hopscotch, veld fires, Zimbabwe outline map). Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 1): From Grade 1 the guide is unit-numbered (41 units across the same 14 HBC topics) with suggested answers to the Learner's Book; geography grows outward from home/school — my country and its ethnic groups and neighbours (South Africa, Zambia, Botswana), then transport, shelter, and Zimbabwe-specific hazards (veldfires, water disasters). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
