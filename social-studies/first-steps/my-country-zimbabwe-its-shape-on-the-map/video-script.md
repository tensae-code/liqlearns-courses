# Video script — My Country Zimbabwe — Its Shape on the Map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe's ECD A teacher guide, the *PlusOne Social Science ECD A Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 7), teaches: My country (colouring the outline map of Zimbabwe). Zimbabwe's Grade 1 teacher guide, the *PlusOne Social Science Grade 1 Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 11), teaches: Unit 6: My country and ethnic groups (neighbouring countries named).

Zimbabwe's own way (ECD A): Heritage-Based Curriculum organised by cross-cutting topics repeated at every level (Identity, National history, Heritage, Work and leisure, Transport and communication, Shelter, Global issues…); teaching is play-, song- and role-play-based with workbook colouring/cutting tasks; local examples throughout (totems, pada hopscotch, veld fires, Zimbabwe outline map). Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 1): From Grade 1 the guide is unit-numbered (41 units across the same 14 HBC topics) with suggested answers to the Learner's Book; geography grows outward from home/school — my country and its ethnic groups and neighbours (South Africa, Zambia, Botswana), then transport, shelter, and Zimbabwe-specific hazards (veldfires, water disasters).
