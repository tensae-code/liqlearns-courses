# Hands-on — Teaching Geography to Beginning Readers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a pupil checklist
Write or draw a short checklist of the Geography things in this lesson, the way the Swedish book gives its pupils a checklist, and tick each one as you learn it.

## Teach a concept with a picture
Pick one Geography word from the lesson, draw a big clear picture of it, and use the picture to teach the word to a companion — concept training the Swedish way.
