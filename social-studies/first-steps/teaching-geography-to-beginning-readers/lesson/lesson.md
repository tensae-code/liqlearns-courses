## Sub lesson 1 — A Geography book for beginning readers

Sweden's SPSM series Människan och världen is a Geography textbook made for beginning readers — pupils who read and write only a little. Its teacher guide opens by setting out the series' purpose: the subject's core content, structured so a beginning reader can meet it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rich pictures, concept training and a pupil checklist

The book teaches through rich pictures and concept training, and the pupil works with a checklist of what they have learned. The chapters the guide frames run from the child's own surroundings outward: Sweden's place on the globe, the home town, Swedish nature and its animals, and sorting the household's own waste.

Sweden's own way: Geography starts from the child's own surroundings — Sweden's place on the globe, the home town, Swedish nature and its animals, and sorting the household's own waste — taught with big pictures and sorting games for pupils who read and write only a little.
