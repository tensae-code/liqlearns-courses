**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Colombia).**

Colombia's own way (Book 1): Grade 1 worksheet "fichas" built around Alejo and Mariana; social studies starts from the child's own body, name and family, widening to neighbours and region; care is taught as a right and as men's and boys' work too, and no family type is ranked above another.

Colombia's own way (Book 11): culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own.
