# Video script — My Feelings and How I Express Them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 1 CS_Fichas, printed p. 7, teaches: feelings (joy, sadness, anger, love) are felt by everyone but expressed differently, and children name how they want to be most of the time.

Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 30–42, teaches: emotions are innate ways of reacting (fear, anger, joy), grouped in types (anger/resentment, pain/sadness, joy), shown in faces and behaviour; it is healthy to express them — telling someone how you feel and how their acts affect you — and families handle emotions together (worked through Little Red Riding Hood's and the wolf's feelings).

Colombia's own way: Grade 1 worksheet "fichas" built around Alejo and Mariana; social studies starts from the child's own body, name and family, widening to neighbours and region; care is taught as a right and as men's and boys' work too, and no family type is ranked above another.

Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own.
