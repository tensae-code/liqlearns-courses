# Hands-on — What Social Studies Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The three-lens walk
Walk around your home or school. Find one thing for each lens: one land thing (a slope, a river, a field), one past thing (an old building, an old tree people remember), one today thing (a new road, a shop, a phone mast). Tell a partner which lens each one belongs to and why.

## The belongs sort
On paper, write ten things people study. Sort them into two piles: belongs in Social Studies / does not belong. Under each one, write your reason in five words or fewer. Compare piles with a partner.
