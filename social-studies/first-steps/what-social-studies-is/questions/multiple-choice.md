# What Social Studies Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the first lens of Social Studies in this course?
a) history  b) geography  c) today's world
**Answer: b) geography**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The three lenses, in the course's order, are —
a) past, land, today  b) land, past, today  c) today, land, past
**Answer: b) land, past, today**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Social Studies draws its content from —
a) only one science  b) the social sciences, fitted to the learner's age  c) laboratory experiments only
**Answer: b) the social sciences, fitted to the learner's age**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs in Social Studies?
a) researching a new drug, on its own  b) how people interact with the natural things around them  c) testing a chemical in a laboratory
**Answer: b) how people interact with the natural things around them**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In this course, when do you meet the plain definition of an idea?
a) before you try anything  b) after you have sorted, observed or debated it first  c) never
**Answer: b) after you have sorted, observed or debated it first**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Dates in this course are given in —
a) the Ethiopian calendar only  b) the European calendar  c) no calendar
**Answer: b) the European calendar**
