# What Social Studies Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three lenses of Social Studies, in order.
**Answer: Geography (land), history (the past), today's world.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: what does Social Studies study?
**Answer: How people interact with each other and with the natural and human-made things around them (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two of the five review-question shapes used in this course.
**Answer: Any two of: true/false, matching, multiple choice, short answer, challenging questions.**
