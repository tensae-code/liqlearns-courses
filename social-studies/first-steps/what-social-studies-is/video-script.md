# Video script — What Social Studies Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One subject. Three lenses.

First lens: the land. The Earth under your feet.

Second lens: the past. The people who came before you.

Third lens: today. The world you live in now.

Geography. History. Today's world.

And in every lesson: you do it first. Then we name it.

That is Social Studies. Let us start.
