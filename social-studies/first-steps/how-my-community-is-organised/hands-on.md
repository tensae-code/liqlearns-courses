# Hands-on — How My Community is Organised

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Proyecto 1 Conozco nuestra organización social
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How the community is organised — who organises it, the function of its institutions, the rules that hold it together — learned by mapping the community and its important places. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Dominican Republic's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
