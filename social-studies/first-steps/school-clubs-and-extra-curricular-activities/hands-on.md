# Hands-on — School Clubs and Extra Curricular Activities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Extra-curricular activities are the programmes not usually done during normal school hours which help to support or improve the main programme of the school, conducted by clubs, societies and other groups — the Literary and Debating Society, the Pioneer Corps, Bible Club, Hindu and Muslim Societies, Scouts and Guides; membership is open to all and voluntary, and students meet and socialise with different age groups, accept positions of leadership and responsibility, and develop socially. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
