Guyana Grade 7 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 1 (printed p. 27), teaches: Extra-curricular activities are the programmes not usually done during normal school hours which help to support or improve the main programme of the school, conducted by clubs, societies and other groups — the Literary and Debating Society, the Pioneer Corps, Bible Club, Hindu and Muslim Societies, Scouts and Guides; membership is open to all and voluntary, and students meet and socialise with different age groups, accept positions of leadership and responsibility, and develop socially.

Guyana's own way: Book 1 opens secondary Social Studies where the primary books end — the child as citizen of nested communities (family, school, village or town, country), national symbols learned part by part with their meanings, and local government taught through the rates-and-taxes bargain: services exist because citizens pay for them and care for what they buy.
