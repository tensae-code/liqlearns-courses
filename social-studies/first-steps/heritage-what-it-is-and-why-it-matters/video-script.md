# Video script — Heritage — what it is and why it matters

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map flies south. Stone walls rise on a hill — no mortar, course on course of stone.
Great Zimbabwe. The Shona built this. Gold walked from here to the ocean ships.
Visitors once said: not Africans. Impossible. The stones kept their own counsel.
That is heritage. Not decoration — evidence. A people's memory, standing where all can read it.
