# Hands-on — Heritage — what it is and why it matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Heritage audit walk
Walk your neighbourhood or school grounds and list everything that could count as heritage in a hundred years — and what would be lost if each vanished. Present the list as a one-page audit.

## Defend the stones
In groups: one group plays writers claiming 'outsiders built this place'; the other defends the local builders using only evidence — materials, dates, trade goods, building style. The class judges which side used evidence and which used assumption.
