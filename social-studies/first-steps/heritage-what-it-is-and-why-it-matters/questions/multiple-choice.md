# Heritage — what it is and why it matters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heritage is best described as —
a) anything old  b) what a people inherits from its past and chooses to keep  c) only royal treasure
**Answer: b) what a people inherits from its past and chooses to keep** — Things and practices handed down and kept.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's pointed question asks why Europeans called Africans uncivilised when —
a) Africans had no languages  b) African heritages such as Great Zimbabwe show otherwise  c) Europeans had not visited Africa
**Answer: b) African heritages such as Great Zimbabwe show otherwise** — The ruins answer the slur.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Great Zimbabwe was built by —
a) the Portuguese  b) the Shona  c) the Romans
**Answer: b) the Shona** — A southern African people.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Its great walls and tower rose in —
a) the 13th–14th centuries  b) the 19th century  c) 500 BC
**Answer: a) the 13th–14th centuries** — From a city begun c.1000 AD.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Great Zimbabwe's trading empire rested on —
a) silk and spices  b) cattle, crops and gold traded to the coast  c) iron ships
**Answer: b) cattle, crops and gold traded to the coast** — Gold reached the Indian Ocean coast.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** After 1450 Great Zimbabwe's successor empire was —
a) Ghana  b) Mwene Mutapa  c) Songhai
**Answer: b) Mwene Mutapa** — The Rozwi clan kings' empire.
