# Heritage — what it is and why it matters — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is heritage fought over — what does a heritage site prove?
**Answer:** It proves, in material form, that a people built, governed and traded — evidence about who they were that later writers cannot talk away.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give Great Zimbabwe's dates: begun, walls and tower, empire's span.
**Answer:** Begun from about 1000 AD; great walls and tower in the 13th–14th centuries; the trading empire flourished 11th–15th centuries.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the empire that succeeded Great Zimbabwe after 1450.
**Answer:** Mwene Mutapa.
