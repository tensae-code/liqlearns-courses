# Heritage — what it is and why it matters — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A heritage site can serve as primary evidence about the people who built it.
**Answer: True** — It was made at the time, by them.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Great Zimbabwe's walls were built with mortar by foreign architects.
**Answer: False** — Shona-built, without mortar — the book's account gives a local trading empire.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Heritage includes practices and skills, not only buildings.
**Answer: True** — Things and practices we choose to keep.
