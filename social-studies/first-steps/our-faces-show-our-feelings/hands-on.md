# Hands-on — Our Faces Show Our Feelings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Наше очи причају и наше лице говори (Our eyes talk and our face speaks)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: feelings (joy, sadness, anger, fear, pride) are read from eyes, face and movements, acted out in a guessing game, and everyone has the right to express feelings in different ways. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
