# Our Faces Show Our Feelings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Наше очи причају и наше лице говори (Our eyes talk and our face speaks) — feelings (joy, sadness, anger, fear, pride) are read from eyes, face and movements, acted out in a guessing game, and everyone has the right to express feelings in different ways (any close wording).**
