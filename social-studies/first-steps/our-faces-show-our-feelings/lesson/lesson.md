# Our Faces Show Our Feelings

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: feelings (joy, sadness, anger, fear, pride) are read from eyes, face and movements, acted out in a guessing game, and everyone has the right to express feelings in different ways — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Наше очи причају и наше лице говори (Our eyes talk and our face speaks)

Serbia Grade 1 Свет око нас 1 (printed pp. 6–7), teaches: feelings (joy, sadness, anger, fear, pride) are read from eyes, face and movements, acted out in a guessing game, and everyone has the right to express feelings in different ways. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
