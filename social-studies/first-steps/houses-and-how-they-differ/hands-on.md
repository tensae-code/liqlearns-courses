# Hands-on — Houses And How They Differ

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 15 Houses differ from each other (خانه‌ها با هم تفاوت دارند): why do the shapes of houses differ? In our country Iran people live in different places; in village houses there are usually places for storing crops or farm tools and a place for keeping animals. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
