Iran's own way (Iran Grade 3 Social Studies): a primary Social Studies book of 23 lessons in 7 chapters that works outward from the child — me, my family, my house, my school, from home to school. The Geography parts placed below are the resources lessons of Ch. 4 The family's needs (Lessons 11–13, printed pp. 37–55), the houses lessons of Ch. 5 Our house (Lessons 15–16, printed pp. 57–62), the school-map worksheet of Ch. 6 (printed p. 80) and the address and directions lessons of Ch. 7 From home to school (Lessons 20–21, printed pp. 81–85).
