# Video script — Communicating with Others

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 10 (special needs, Kardanesh) (student book, Persian), printed p. 37, teaches: no one lives wholly alone; to meet our own needs and learn of others' needs we must communicate (ارتباط); communication is of two kinds — verbal (کلامی), through speaking and conversation, and non-verbal (غیرکلامی), without words, which we use many times a day without noticing; it begins at birth — the baby's cry tells its mother what it needs — and widens as the child grows: the family first, then school, with classmates, the teacher and the school's officials; we also communicate by signs and by the movements of the body's members, as the pictures show; and successful communication has its rules — keep politeness and respect, attend to the other person's age and character, consider the time and place (a mosque, a school, a cinema, a celebration, a mourning gathering), speak of what interests the listener, and avoid too much talk — with the closing inquiry: how do we communicate with someone who cannot hear, cannot see, or cannot move?.

Iran's own way: the special-needs secondary book is a citizen's primer in the plainest form — needs first (things, then love and company), then the circles that meet them: family, groups, institutions — and at the top, government and the Majlis, explained through a ship and its captain and a school and its rules.
