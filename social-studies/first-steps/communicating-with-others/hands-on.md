# Hands-on — Communicating with Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: no one lives wholly alone; to meet our own needs and learn of others' needs we must communicate (ارتباط); communication is of two kinds — verbal (کلامی), through speaking and conversation, and non-verbal (غیرکلامی), without words, which we use many times a day without noticing; it begins at birth — the baby's cry tells its mother what it needs — and widens as the child grows: the family first, then school, with classmates, the teacher and the school's officials; we also communicate by signs and by the movements of the body's members, as the pictures show; and successful communication has its rules — keep politeness and respect, attend to the other person's age and character, consider the time and place (a mosque, a school, a cinema, a celebration, a mourning gathering), speak of what interests the listener, and avoid too much talk — with the closing inquiry: how do we communicate with someone who cannot hear, cannot see, or cannot move?. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
