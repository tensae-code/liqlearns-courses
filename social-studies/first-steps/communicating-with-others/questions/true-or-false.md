# Communicating with Others — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: no one lives wholly alone; to meet our own needs and learn of others' needs we must communicate (ارتباط); communication is of two kinds — verbal (کلامی), through speaking and conversation, and non-verbal (غیرکلامی), without words, which we use many times a day without noticing; it begins at birth — the baby's cry tells its mother what it needs — and widens as the child grows: the family first, then school, with classmates, the teacher and the school's officials; we also communicate by signs and by the movements of the body's members, as the pictures show; and successful communication has its rules — keep politeness and respect, attend to the other person's age and character, consider the time and place (a mosque, a school, a cinema, a celebration, a mourning gathering), speak of what interests the listener, and avoid too much talk — with the closing inquiry: how do we communicate with someone who cannot hear, cannot see, or cannot move?.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iran's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
