# Hands-on — Rules and Helping at School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: our school rules — come on time and stand in line, listen well while the teacher teaches, ask the teacher before leaving the class, keep classroom and school clean; and cooperation at school — helping the teacher prepare the class things, keeping our turn at the shop, helping friends; every member of the school has a duty — pupil, teacher, supervisor (ناظم), principal. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
