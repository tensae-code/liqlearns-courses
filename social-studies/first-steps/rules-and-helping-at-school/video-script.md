# Video script — Rules and Helping at School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 54, teaches: our school rules — come on time and stand in line, listen well while the teacher teaches, ask the teacher before leaving the class, keep classroom and school clean; and cooperation at school — helping the teacher prepare the class things, keeping our turn at the shop, helping friends; every member of the school has a duty — pupil, teacher, supervisor (ناظم), principal.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
