# Two timelines side by side: Ethiopia and the world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'holding both timelines in view at the same date' is ______.
**Answer: side by side**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the false picture of Ethiopia waiting outside world history' is ______.
**Answer: offstage**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the dual timeline itself, made for the pairing work' is ______.
**Answer: tool**
