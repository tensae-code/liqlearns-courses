# Video script — Two timelines side by side: Ethiopia and the world

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — One book, two clocks. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 book runs a single long story — human origins, ancient civilisations, Ethiopia to the 13th century, the medieval world, Africa, and revolutions — but its Ethiopian and world strands run on two clocks that must be held side by side.

While Gondar rose, what was Europe doing? While Europe industrialised, where was Ethiopia? The braid only teaches if both strands stay in view.. Watch the names appear one by one: spine, strand, side by side. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Where the clocks overlap. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Units 7 and 8 — Ethiopia 1270 to 1855 — overlap the world's medieval, early-modern and revolutionary centuries. Watch the names appear one by one: overlap, Zemene Mesafint, offstage. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Holding both — a skill. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Build the habit: for any date in this course, name one Ethiopian event and one world event. Watch the names appear one by one: paired vision, habit, tool. This is the part of the story to hold.
