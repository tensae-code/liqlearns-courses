# Video script — How Our Family and Community Meet Our Needs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 1 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 67–69), teaches: Family members work together to meet the family's needs — parents work to buy food, clothing and books, children help with chores, families grow crops and rear animals — and when the family needs help, community groups provide the services: doctors and nurses, farmers and butchers, pharmacists and businesses.

Guyana's own way: the series opens at Grade 1 inside the home — the family that cooks and gardens together, the chores shared out fairly, the groups a small child already belongs to, and rules taught as the way a home and a classroom stay in harmony.
