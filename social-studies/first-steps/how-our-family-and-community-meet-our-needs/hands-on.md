# Hands-on — How Our Family and Community Meet Our Needs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Family members work together to meet the family's needs — parents work to buy food, clothing and books, children help with chores, families grow crops and rear animals — and when the family needs help, community groups provide the services: doctors and nurses, farmers and butchers, pharmacists and businesses. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
