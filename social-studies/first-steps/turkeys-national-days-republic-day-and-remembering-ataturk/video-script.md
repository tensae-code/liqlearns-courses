# Video script — Turkey's national days — Republic Day and remembering Atatürk

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 2 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Turkey, a child of seven meets the nation's history first as poems. The Grade 2 book carries them in its Special Days and Weeks spreads.

On 29 October, Republic Day, the poem says the calendars showed the day the homeland became a republic — and the whole nation decks itself in flags.

In Atatürk Week, 10 to 16 November, the poem remembers five past nine in the morning — the moment of grief when Atatürk died — and the crowds running to Anıtkabir, his tomb. It praises him as patriotic, honest, determined and hardworking.

And on 15 July, the poem retells the night the nation and the army fought for democracy and won.

Three poems, three days: a birthday, a goodbye, and a night of unity. That is how Turkey's youngest pupils first learn their country's story.
