# Turkey's national days — Republic Day and remembering Atatürk — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Student Oath (Andımız) is printed at the front of the Grade 2 book.
**Answer: True** — the daily oath stands at the book's front.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Republic Day poem says the season was spring.
**Answer: False** — the poem says the season was autumn, and the calendars showed 29 October.
