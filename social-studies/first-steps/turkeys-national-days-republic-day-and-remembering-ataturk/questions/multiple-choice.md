# Turkey's national days — Republic Day and remembering Atatürk — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Republic Day in Turkey falls on —
a) 29 October  b) 15 July  c) 10 November
**Answer: a) 29 October** — the day the homeland's form of government became a republic.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Atatürk Week remembers the moment of Atatürk's death at —
a) Midnight  b) 09:05  c) Noon
**Answer: b) 09:05** — the 09:05 moment of grief the poem names.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Anıtkabir is —
a) A harbour in Istanbul  b) A school in Ankara  c) Atatürk's tomb in Ankara, where the crowds run
**Answer: c) Atatürk's tomb in Ankara, where the crowds run** — the place the Atatürk Week poem names.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** 15 July is kept as —
a) Democracy and National Unity Day  b) Flag Day  c) Children's Day
**Answer: a) Democracy and National Unity Day** — the night the nation-army fought for democracy and won.
