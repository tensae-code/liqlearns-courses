# Hands-on — Turkey's national days — Republic Day and remembering Atatürk

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A flag for 29 October

Draw the Turkish flag — red, with the white crescent and star — and under it write what happened on 29 October. Hang your drawing where your family can see it, the way the poem says the nation decks itself in flags.

## Five past nine

Draw a clock showing 09:05. Under it, write whose death that moment remembers, and one word the poem uses for him — patriotic, honest, determined or hardworking — and why you chose it.

## Three days on a line

Draw a year line with three marks: 29 October, 10 November and 15 July. Under each mark, write in your own words what the day remembers. Say the three beats aloud to someone at home.
