# Hands-on — Greetings for Each Season

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 1 "Seasonal greetings" (Улирлын мэнд)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Mongolians greet each season differently, because the greeting speaks of the season's work and wishes it well — in spring, "Are the lambs and kids thriving?"; in autumn at a felt-making household, a blessing answer about being white as snow and strong as felt; the reply to a seasonal greeting is itself a blessing (ерөөл), tied to that household's labour. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
