# Video script — Greetings for Each Season

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 5, teaches: Mongolians greet each season differently, because the greeting speaks of the season's work and wishes it well — in spring, "Are the lambs and kids thriving?"; in autumn at a felt-making household, a blessing answer about being white as snow and strong as felt; the reply to a seasonal greeting is itself a blessing (ерөөл), tied to that household's labour.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
