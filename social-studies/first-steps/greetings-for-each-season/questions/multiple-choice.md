# Greetings for Each Season — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 1 "Seasonal greetings" (Улирлын мэнд)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 1 "Seasonal greetings" (Улирлын мэнд)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Mongolians greet each season differently, because the greeting speaks of the season's work and wishes it well — in spring, "Are the lambs and kids thriving?"; in autumn at a felt-making household, a blessing answer about being white as snow and strong as felt; the reply to a seasonal greeting is itself a blessing (ерөөл), tied to that household's labour  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Mongolians greet each season differently, because the greeting speaks of the season's work and wishes it well — in spring, "Are the lambs and kids thriving?"; in autumn at a felt-making household, a blessing answer about being white as snow and strong as felt; the reply to a seasonal greeting is itself a blessing (ерөөл), tied to that household's labour**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Mongolia's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.  b) Mongolia's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
