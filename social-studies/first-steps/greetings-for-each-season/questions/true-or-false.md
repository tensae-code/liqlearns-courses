# Greetings for Each Season — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Mongolians greet each season differently, because the greeting speaks of the season's work and wishes it well — in spring, "Are the lambs and kids thriving?"; in autumn at a felt-making household, a blessing answer about being white as snow and strong as felt; the reply to a seasonal greeting is itself a blessing (ерөөл), tied to that household's labour.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
