# School Traditions That Bring Us Together — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: All schools have traditions of which they are proud — traditions are beliefs and practices handed down from one group to another, differing from school to school and making each school special and unique: Inter-house Competitions, Speech Days, Open Days for parents and visitors, Market Day, Annual Fairs, special assemblies and prize-giving ceremonies, which mould students into the kind and quality of citizens of which we can all be justifiably proud; groups within the school — the Debating Society, House System, Bible Club and Parent-Teacher Association — have goals and rules which govern behaviour, clearly defined status, shared values, and common symbols such as a badge, pledge, uniform or prayer, and they promote social interaction and co-operation among members.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
