# Hands-on — School Traditions That Bring Us Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: All schools have traditions of which they are proud — traditions are beliefs and practices handed down from one group to another, differing from school to school and making each school special and unique: Inter-house Competitions, Speech Days, Open Days for parents and visitors, Market Day, Annual Fairs, special assemblies and prize-giving ceremonies, which mould students into the kind and quality of citizens of which we can all be justifiably proud; groups within the school — the Debating Society, House System, Bible Club and Parent-Teacher Association — have goals and rules which govern behaviour, clearly defined status, shared values, and common symbols such as a badge, pledge, uniform or prayer, and they promote social interaction and co-operation among members. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
