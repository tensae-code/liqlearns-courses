# Hands-on — I Talk with Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: we human beings live with others and communicate with them; the class studies pictures of people in conversation — who is each person talking with, and what might they be saying? — and acts the scenes out, standing in the speakers' place. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
