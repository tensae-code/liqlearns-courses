# Hands-on — Our Pride

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 14 "Our pride" (Бидний бахархал)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: As every person has their pride, so does a country: Mongolians are proud of their independence (тусгаар тогтнол), their democracy (ардчилал) and their freedom (эрх чөлөө) — a land where people speak their minds freely, travel freely and own their property; and a country's pride is built by its people's diligent work — the young chess champion D. Nomin-Erdene, world youth champion at ten, honoured by the President — alongside the great names of the past: Chinggis Khan, S. Zorig, N. Norovbanzad. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
