# Our Pride — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 14 "Our pride" (Бидний бахархал) — As every person has their pride, so does a country: Mongolians are proud of their independence (тусгаар тогтнол), their democracy (ардчилал) and their freedom (эрх чөлөө) — a land where people speak their minds freely, travel freely and own their property; and a country's pride is built by its people's diligent work — the young chess champion D. Nomin-Erdene, world youth champion at ten, honoured by the President — alongside the great names of the past: Chinggis Khan, S. Zorig, N. Norovbanzad (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for (any close wording).**
