## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 14 "Our pride" (Бидний бахархал)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 51, teaches: As every person has their pride, so does a country: Mongolians are proud of their independence (тусгаар тогтнол), their democracy (ардчилал) and their freedom (эрх чөлөө) — a land where people speak their minds freely, travel freely and own their property; and a country's pride is built by its people's diligent work — the young chess champion D. Nomin-Erdene, world youth champion at ten, honoured by the President — alongside the great names of the past: Chinggis Khan, S. Zorig, N. Norovbanzad. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
