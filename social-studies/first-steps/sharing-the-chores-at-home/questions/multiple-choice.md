# Sharing the Chores at Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) We are a good team  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) We are a good team**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) in a family everyone has duties and responsibilities; the family first decides and shares out the jobs (tidying toys while parents clean, setting the table, caring for pets, helping elders in the garden), and doing our share keeps family life orderly, grows unity and leaves more time together  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) in a family everyone has duties and responsibilities; the family first decides and shares out the jobs (tidying toys while parents clean, setting the table, caring for pets, helping elders in the garden), and doing our share keeps family life orderly, grows unity and leaves more time together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches sharing the Chores at Home as Turkey's books teach it, in Turkey's own way.  b) Turkey's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) This lesson teaches sharing the Chores at Home as Turkey's books teach it, in Turkey's own way.**
