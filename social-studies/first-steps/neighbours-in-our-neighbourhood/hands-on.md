# Hands-on — Neighbours in Our Neighbourhood

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the people who live beside one another in a neighbourhood are neighbours (همسایه); neighbours must be kind to one another, respect one another and help one another in their work; good neighbours keep informed of each other's state and keep the building and the neighbourhood clean. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
