# Neighbours in Our Neighbourhood

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the people who live beside one another in a neighbourhood are neighbours (همسایه); neighbours must be kind to one another, respect one another and help one another in their work; good neighbours keep informed of each other's state and keep the building and the neighbourhood clean — as Iran's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Neighbours in Our Neighbourhood

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 40, teaches: the people who live beside one another in a neighbourhood are neighbours (همسایه); neighbours must be kind to one another, respect one another and help one another in their work; good neighbours keep informed of each other's state and keep the building and the neighbourhood clean. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
