# Video script — My Region and Its Landscape

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia's CS_Fichas G1 book, the *Ciencias Sociales — Fichas, Escuela Nueva (Grado 1)* (printed p. 16), teaches: ¿Cómo es mi región? landscape, animals, plants, fruits and climate of my region.

Colombia's own way (CS_Fichas G1): a Grade 1 "fichas" (worksheet) book built around the characters Alejo and Mariana; geography enters through the child's own region — its landscape, nature's gifts, typical foods, festivals and dress — before any map work.
