## Sub lesson 1 — My Region and Its Landscape

Colombia's CS_Fichas G1 book, the *Ciencias Sociales — Fichas, Escuela Nueva (Grado 1)* (printed p. 16), teaches: ¿Cómo es mi región? landscape, animals, plants, fruits and climate of my region. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way (CS_Fichas G1): a Grade 1 "fichas" (worksheet) book built around the characters Alejo and Mariana; geography enters through the child's own region — its landscape, nature's gifts, typical foods, festivals and dress — before any map work. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
