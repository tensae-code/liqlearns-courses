The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Palestine Grade 6 Social Studies Part 2, printed p. 87, teaches: among the causes of traffic accidents — which bring loss of life, injuries and material losses — are excessive speed, the vehicle's unfitness, not keeping the instructions, obstacles on the road, and the inattention of driver and pedestrians; traffic safety is achieved by drivers' and pedestrians' commitment to the traffic laws and signs, care for the fitness of the means of transport and of the streets they run on, and holding traffic-awareness workshops and programmes for school pupils.

Palestine's own way: citizenship is defined by a contrast the pupils must judge — one group of sixth-graders, faced with repeated traffic accidents at their school, forms a committee, organises the crossing and teaches the others (the active citizens), while the second group only blames and criticises; the book's verdict is that citizenship is measured by what you do.
