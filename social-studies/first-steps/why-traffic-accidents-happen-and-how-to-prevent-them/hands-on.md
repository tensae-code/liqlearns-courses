# Hands-on — Why Traffic Accidents Happen and How to Prevent Them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Why Traffic Accidents Happen and How to Prevent Them
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Among the causes of traffic accidents — which bring loss of life, injuries and material losses — are excessive speed, the vehicle's unfitness, not keeping the instructions, obstacles on the road, and the inattention of driver and pedestrians; traffic safety is achieved by drivers' and pedestrians' commitment to the traffic laws and signs, care for the fitness of the means of transport and of the streets they run on, and holding traffic-awareness workshops and programmes for school pupils. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
