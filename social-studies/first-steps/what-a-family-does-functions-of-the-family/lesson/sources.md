**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Yemen, Libya).**

Yemen's own way (G6 National Education): Grade 6 National Education builds citizenship in widening circles — family, then tribe, then constitution — and teaches the tribe honestly as a real social component with its own customs and a role in keeping social peace, before the constitution unit names the citizen's rights, duties and the qualities of the good citizen (love of homeland shown in deeds, guarding public property, constructive criticism).

Searchable tags: what a family does functions of the family, Yemen, social studies, first-steps, Libya

Libya's own way (National Education G4): the whole book is written in the first person — every lesson is "I and …" (I and my family, I and my school, I and the neighbourhood) — citizenship starts as the child's own relationships, and even the environment unit is framed as the child's own behaviour: what I throw away, what I plant, which beach I clean.
