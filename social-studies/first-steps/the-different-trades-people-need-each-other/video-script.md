# Video script — The Different Trades People Need Each Other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 35), teaches: nobody lives alone — a person needs food, water, a house, clothes, shoes, medicine, learning, peace and tools, and no one can make them all; the farmer grows, the herder milks, the fisherman catches, the tailor sews, the metalworker makes the knives and farm tools, the teacher teaches (without knowledge a person is blind), the water, firewood and charcoal workers, office, trade and cleaning workers, the military, the police and the Guulwade keep the peace — every trade is great work for the nation, each person needs the other, and all deserve honour and love.

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag.
