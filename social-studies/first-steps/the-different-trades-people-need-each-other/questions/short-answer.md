# The Different Trades People Need Each Other — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Xirfadaha kala geddisan (The Different Trades) — nobody lives alone — a person needs food, water, a house, clothes, shoes, medicine, learning, peace and tools, and no one can make them all; the farmer grows, the herder milks, the fisherman catches, the tailor sews, the metalworker makes the knives and farm tools, the teacher teaches (without knowledge a person is blind), the water, firewood and charcoal workers, office, trade and cleaning workers, the military, the police and the Guulwade keep the peace — every trade is great work for the nation, each person needs the other, and all deserve honour and love (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag. (any close wording).**
