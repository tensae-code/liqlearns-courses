# The Different Trades People Need Each Other — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Xirfadaha kala geddisan (The Different Trades)" teaches: nobody lives alone — a person needs food, water, a house, clothes, shoes, medicine, learning, peace and tools, and no one can make them all; the farmer grows, the herder milks, the fisherman catches, the tailor sews, the metalworker makes the knives and farm tools, the teacher teaches (without knowledge a person is blind), the water, firewood and charcoal workers, office, trade and cleaning workers, the military, the police and the Guulwade keep the peace — every trade is great work for the nation, each person needs the other, and all deserve honour and love.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag..
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
