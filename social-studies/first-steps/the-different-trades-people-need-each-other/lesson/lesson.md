# The Different Trades People Need Each Other

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: nobody lives alone — a person needs food, water, a house, clothes, shoes, medicine, learning, peace and tools, and no one can make them all; the farmer grows, the herder milks, the fisherman catches, the tailor sews, the metalworker makes the knives and farm tools, the teacher teaches (without knowledge a person is blind), the water, firewood and charcoal workers, office, trade and cleaning workers, the military, the police and the Guulwade keep the peace — every trade is great work for the nation, each person needs the other, and all deserve honour and love — as Somalia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Xirfadaha kala geddisan (The Different Trades)

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 35), teaches: nobody lives alone — a person needs food, water, a house, clothes, shoes, medicine, learning, peace and tools, and no one can make them all; the farmer grows, the herder milks, the fisherman catches, the tailor sews, the metalworker makes the knives and farm tools, the teacher teaches (without knowledge a person is blind), the water, firewood and charcoal workers, office, trade and cleaning workers, the military, the police and the Guulwade keep the peace — every trade is great work for the nation, each person needs the other, and all deserve honour and love. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
