# Hands-on — The Different Trades People Need Each Other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Xirfadaha kala geddisan (The Different Trades)
Work through this part with your hands: nobody lives alone — a person needs food, water, a house, clothes, shoes, medicine, learning, peace and tools, and no one can make them all; the farmer grows, the herder milks, the fisherman catches, the tailor sews, the metalworker makes the knives and farm tools, the teacher teaches (without knowledge a person is blind), the water, firewood and charcoal workers, office, trade and cleaning workers, the military, the police and the Guulwade keep the peace — every trade is great work for the nation, each person needs the other, and all deserve honour and love. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
