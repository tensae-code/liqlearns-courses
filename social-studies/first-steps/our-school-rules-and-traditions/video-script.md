# Video script — Our School Rules and Traditions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sri Lanka Grade 6 Civic Education (printed p. 24), teaches: Why a school needs rules (a good learning environment, discipline, safeguarding everyone's rights and the school's identity), the pupil's own rules, and the traditions each school hands down.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 6 Civic Education): civics starts inside the child's own school — its history, its people and their duties, its rules, symbols and the pupil's own rights — then widens to the region (the Grama Niladhari ladder up to the province, the officers and councils that serve it) and closes with the good citizen's virtues: polite speech, listening, discipline, telling good from bad, using resources frugally, guarding public property and preserving the local identity with pride.
