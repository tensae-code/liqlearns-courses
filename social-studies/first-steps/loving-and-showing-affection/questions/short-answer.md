# Loving and Showing Affection — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guía 9 ¿A quién quieres? — Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 43–51, teaches: listing the people, animals and objects I love and who loves me; how I show affection and like it shown; maternal love and the baby's first bonds, and family love as a source of wellbeing that varies with closeness in each family. (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way — Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own. (any close wording).**
