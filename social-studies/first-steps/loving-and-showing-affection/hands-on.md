# Hands-on — Loving and Showing Affection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 9 ¿A quién quieres?
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 43–51, teaches: listing the people, animals and objects I love and who loves me; how I show affection and like it shown; maternal love and the baby's first bonds, and family love as a source of wellbeing that varies with closeness in each family. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
