# Loving and Showing Affection

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: listing the people, animals and objects I love and who loves me; how I show affection and like it shown; maternal love and the baby's first bonds, and family love as a source of wellbeing that varies with closeness in each family — as Colombia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 9 ¿A quién quieres?

Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 43–51, teaches: listing the people, animals and objects I love and who loves me; how I show affection and like it shown; maternal love and the baby's first bonds, and family love as a source of wellbeing that varies with closeness in each family. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
