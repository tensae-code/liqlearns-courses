# Hands-on — Our Feelings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: we have different feelings in different situations — we may be happy or sad, angry at something or frightened of something; the lesson reads the feelings from children's faces and names them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
