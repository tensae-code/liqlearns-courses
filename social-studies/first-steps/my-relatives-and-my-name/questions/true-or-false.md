# My Relatives And My Name — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: relatives on the father's side are авга and on the mother's side нагац, drawn on the family tree; my birth certificate carries my clan name (овог), my father's name and the name my parents gave me; and the Mongolian saying that a name breaks before a bone does — a person's name carries the honour of the whole clan.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
