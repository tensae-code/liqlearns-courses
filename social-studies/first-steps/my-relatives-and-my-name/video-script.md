The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 5, teaches: Relatives on the father's side are авга and on the mother's side нагац, drawn on the family tree; my birth certificate carries my clan name (овог), my father's name and the name my parents gave me; and the Mongolian saying that a name breaks before a bone does — a person's name carries the honour of the whole clan.

Mongolia's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact.
