# Hands-on — My Relatives And My Name

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 1 "My kin" (Миний ураг төрөл)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Relatives on the father's side are авга and on the mother's side нагац, drawn on the family tree; my birth certificate carries my clan name (овог), my father's name and the name my parents gave me; and the Mongolian saying that a name breaks before a bone does — a person's name carries the honour of the whole clan. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
