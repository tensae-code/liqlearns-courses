# Me and My Friends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) About Maung Maung and his friends  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) About Maung Maung and his friends**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Children introduce themselves and their friends through pictures of what they like, how they play and what they dream of becoming (doctor, teacher, footballer)  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Children introduce themselves and their friends through pictures of what they like, how they play and what they dream of becoming (doctor, teacher, footballer)**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Myanmar's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.  b) Myanmar's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) Myanmar's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.**
