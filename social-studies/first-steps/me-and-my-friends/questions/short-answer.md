# Me and My Friends — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: About Maung Maung and his friends — Children introduce themselves and their friends through pictures of what they like, how they play and what they dream of becoming (doctor, teacher, footballer) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole primary course is carried by one child, Maung Maung, and his family — self, friends and family are introduced through full-page picture scenes with almost no text, and the family tree, the members' jobs and the house itself are all drawn from his own household. (any close wording).**
