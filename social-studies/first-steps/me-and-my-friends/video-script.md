# Video script — Me and My Friends

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar Social Studies, Grade 1 (printed p. 1), teaches: Children introduce themselves and their friends through pictures of what they like, how they play and what they dream of becoming (doctor, teacher, footballer).

Myanmar's own way (Book 1): the whole primary course is carried by one child, Maung Maung, and his family — self, friends and family are introduced through full-page picture scenes with almost no text, and the family tree, the members' jobs and the house itself are all drawn from his own household.
