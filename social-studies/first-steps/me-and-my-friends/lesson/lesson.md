# Me and My Friends

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: children introduce themselves and their friends through pictures of what they like, how they play and what they dream of becoming (doctor, teacher, footballer) — as Myanmar's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — About Maung Maung and his friends

Myanmar Social Studies, Grade 1 (printed p. 1), teaches: Children introduce themselves and their friends through pictures of what they like, how they play and what they dream of becoming (doctor, teacher, footballer). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way

Myanmar's own way: the whole primary course is carried by one child, Maung Maung, and his family — self, friends and family are introduced through full-page picture scenes with almost no text, and the family tree, the members' jobs and the house itself are all drawn from his own household. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
