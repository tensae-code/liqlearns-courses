# Hands-on — Me and My Friends

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## About Maung Maung and his friends
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Children introduce themselves and their friends through pictures of what they like, how they play and what they dream of becoming (doctor, teacher, footballer). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
