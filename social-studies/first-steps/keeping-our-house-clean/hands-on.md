# Hands-on — Keeping Our House Clean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: keeping the dwelling clean is loving your house — wiping the tables, scrubbing the bathroom, sweeping the yard, washing the dishes with mother, rubbish in the bin, the steps of sweeping a room, and making a storage box from used material so things have their place. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
