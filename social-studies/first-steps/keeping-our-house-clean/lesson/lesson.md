# Keeping Our House Clean

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: keeping the dwelling clean is loving your house — wiping the tables, scrubbing the bathroom, sweeping the yard, washing the dishes with mother, rubbish in the bin, the steps of sweeping a room, and making a storage box from used material so things have their place — as Vietnam's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Keep the house clean

Vietnam Grade 2 Tự nhiên và Xã hội 2, printed pp. 18–21, teaches: Keeping the dwelling clean is loving your house — wiping the tables, scrubbing the bathroom, sweeping the yard, washing the dishes with mother, rubbish in the bin, the steps of sweeping a room, and making a storage box from used material so things have their place. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: work is met first inside the family — the adults' occupations, paid and unpaid, with Hồ Chí Minh's saying "labour is glory" — and traffic is taught as a system of kinds (road, rail, air, water) whose signs fall into three colour-coded families every road user must obey. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
