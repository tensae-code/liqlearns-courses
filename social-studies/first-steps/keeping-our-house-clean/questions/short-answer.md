# Keeping Our House Clean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Keep the house clean — keeping the dwelling clean is loving your house — wiping the tables, scrubbing the bathroom, sweeping the yard, washing the dishes with mother, rubbish in the bin, the steps of sweeping a room, and making a storage box from used material so things have their place (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way — work is met first inside the family — the adults' occupations, paid and unpaid, with Hồ Chí Minh's saying "labour is glory" — and traffic is taught as a system of kinds (road, rail, air, water) whose signs fall into three colour-coded families every road user must obey. (any close wording).**
