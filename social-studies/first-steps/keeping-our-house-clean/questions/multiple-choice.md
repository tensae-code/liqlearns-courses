# Keeping Our House Clean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The this part of this lesson is —
a) Keep the house clean  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Keep the house clean**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Keeping the dwelling clean is loving your house — wiping the tables, scrubbing the bathroom, sweeping the yard, washing the dishes with mother, rubbish in the bin, the steps of sweeping a room, and making a storage box from used material so things have their place  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Keeping the dwelling clean is loving your house — wiping the tables, scrubbing the bathroom, sweeping the yard, washing the dishes with mother, rubbish in the bin, the steps of sweeping a room, and making a storage box from used material so things have their place**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The last part of this lesson is —
a) Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Vietnam's own way: work is met first inside the family — the adults' occupations, paid and unpaid, with Hồ Chí Minh's saying "labour is glory" — and traffic is taught as a system of kinds (road, rail, air, water) whose signs fall into three colour-coded families every road user must obey.  b) Vietnam's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) Vietnam's own way: work is met first inside the family — the adults' occupations, paid and unpaid, with Hồ Chí Minh's saying "labour is glory" — and traffic is taught as a system of kinds (road, rail, air, water) whose signs fall into three colour-coded families every road user must obey.**
