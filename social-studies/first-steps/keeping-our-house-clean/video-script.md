# Video script — Keeping Our House Clean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 2 Tự nhiên và Xã hội 2, printed pp. 18–21, teaches: Keeping the dwelling clean is loving your house — wiping the tables, scrubbing the bathroom, sweeping the yard, washing the dishes with mother, rubbish in the bin, the steps of sweeping a room, and making a storage box from used material so things have their place.

Vietnam's own way: work is met first inside the family — the adults' occupations, paid and unpaid, with Hồ Chí Minh's saying "labour is glory" — and traffic is taught as a system of kinds (road, rail, air, water) whose signs fall into three colour-coded families every road user must obey.
