# Hands-on — Where I Live Places In My Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 9 Where do I live? (من کجا زندگی می‌کنم؟) — pupils from different parts of Iran describe their own living places; the knowledge of geography helps us know the natural and human features of places and understand the relations between them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
