# Hands-on — Rules and Helping at Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: our house rules — shoes off outside and into the shoe rack, no noise while family members rest or study, ask father and mother before going out, sleep on time; and cooperation at home — I help father with the shopping, my sister helps me with schoolwork, father helps mother lay the table, my brother helps mother tidy the house. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
