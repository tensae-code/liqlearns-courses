# Video script — Rules and Helping at Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 36, teaches: our house rules — shoes off outside and into the shoe rack, no noise while family members rest or study, ask father and mother before going out, sleep on time; and cooperation at home — I help father with the shopping, my sister helps me with schoolwork, father helps mother lay the table, my brother helps mother tidy the house.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
