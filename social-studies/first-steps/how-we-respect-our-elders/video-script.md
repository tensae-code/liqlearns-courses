# Video script — How We Respect Our Elders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 7, teaches: Respect for elders through the folk tale of the lion and the old mouse, then the taboos of respect — never call an elder by name, never cross in front of one, never touch an elder's headgear, never eat or drink before an elder, give the upper seat when an elder enters the ger — and the class inviting an elder who knows the traditions as a guest teacher.

Mongolia's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact.
