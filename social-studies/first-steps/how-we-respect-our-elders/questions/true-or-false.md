# How We Respect Our Elders — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: respect for elders through the folk tale of the lion and the old mouse, then the taboos of respect — never call an elder by name, never cross in front of one, never touch an elder's headgear, never eat or drink before an elder, give the upper seat when an elder enters the ger — and the class inviting an elder who knows the traditions as a guest teacher.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
