# Hands-on — How We Respect Our Elders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 2 "The custom of respect" (Хүндлэх ёс)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Respect for elders through the folk tale of the lion and the old mouse, then the taboos of respect — never call an elder by name, never cross in front of one, never touch an elder's headgear, never eat or drink before an elder, give the upper seat when an elder enters the ger — and the class inviting an elder who knows the traditions as a guest teacher. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
