## Sub lesson 3 — Greece's way — the book's own teaching way

Greece's own way: Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos. Grade 4 turns from the neighbourhood to the whole country and its culture — Greek culture is set beside other peoples' cultures, and work is taught as the web of professions that together make one product. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
