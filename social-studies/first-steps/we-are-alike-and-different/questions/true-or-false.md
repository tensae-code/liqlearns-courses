# We Are Alike and Different — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Classmates resemble and differ in skin, eye and hair colour and in height, and the class gathers them all the same — difference is normal and no bar to playing and learning together.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: How five friends at a Damascus art centre resemble and differ in body (face colour, eyes, hair) and in character (cooperative, calm, merry, patient) — and the trait that marks me out, written in my own notebook (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: Writing your personal traits (shy, happy, confident, stubborn, optimistic, self-reliant) on the petals of your flower and your classmates' traits in your notebook — we are known by body and by character alike (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part teaches: The whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free)..
**Answer: True**
