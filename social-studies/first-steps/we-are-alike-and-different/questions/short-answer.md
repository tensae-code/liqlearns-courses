# We Are Alike and Different — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: We Are Alike and Different 1 — classmates resemble and differ in skin, eye and hair colour and in height, and the class gathers them all the same — difference is normal and no bar to playing and learning together (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: We Are Alike and Different 2 — how five friends at a Damascus art centre resemble and differ in body (face colour, eyes, hair) and in character (cooperative, calm, merry, patient) — and the trait that marks me out, written in my own notebook (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1) (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: We Are Alike and Different 3 — writing your personal traits (shy, happy, confident, stubborn, optimistic, self-reliant) on the petals of your flower and your classmates' traits in your notebook — we are known by body and by character alike (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1) (any close wording).**
