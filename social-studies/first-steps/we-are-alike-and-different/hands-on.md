# Hands-on — We Are Alike and Different

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: classmates resemble and differ in skin, eye and hair colour and in height, and the class gathers them all the same — difference is normal and no bar to playing and learning together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
