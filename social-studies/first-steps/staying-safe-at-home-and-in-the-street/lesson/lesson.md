## Sub lesson 1 — Staying Safe at Home and in the Street

Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 104, teaches: Danger at home and in the street — Cucarachita Martínez's story teaches kitchen safety (cover the pot, close the kitchen door), and the street pages teach the traffic signs and behaviours that avoid danger: seatbelts are obligatory, drivers must respect speed limits and not drink, pedestrians must obey the crossing signals. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: coexistence is the spine of the primary book — respect, rules, family and community are each defined in plain words, then practised: the class votes in assembly, community leaders must learn to agree, and solidarity is taught from Lincoln's collection for an enslaved sailor to Caracas's free children's heart hospital serving the whole continent. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
