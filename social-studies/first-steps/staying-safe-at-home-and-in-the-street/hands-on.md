# Hands-on — Staying Safe at Home and in the Street

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Danger at home and in the street — Cucarachita Martínez's story teaches kitchen safety (cover the pot, close the kitchen door), and the street pages teach the traffic signs and behaviours that avoid danger: seatbelts are obligatory, drivers must respect speed limits and not drink, pedestrians must obey the crossing signals. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
