# Transport in My Country — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Transport in my country — the means of transport sorted by where they travel — land transport and how people move from place to place; air transport (aeroplane, spacecraft, balloon); sea transport classified (the ship that carries people and goods, the sailing boat on the waves, the fast jet ski) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — at six years old the course already runs on two rails — the working country (how we travel, the jobs our grandfathers did, the job I will do) and the inherited country (Zayed the father, the days we love, our heritage) — and every lesson plan is required to name the citizenship concepts it builds: belonging, citizenship, social responsibility, cooperation, national identity.
