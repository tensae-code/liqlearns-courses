# Transport in My Country — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the means of transport sorted by where they travel — land transport and how people move from place to place; air transport (aeroplane, spacecraft, balloon); sea transport classified (the ship that carries people and goods, the sailing boat on the waves, the fast jet ski)
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: at six years old the course already runs on two rails — the working country (how we travel, the jobs our grandfathers did, the job I will do) and the inherited country (Zayed the father, the days we love, our heritage) — and every lesson plan is required to name the citizenship concepts it builds: belonging, citizenship, social responsibility, cooperation, national identity.
**Answer: True**
