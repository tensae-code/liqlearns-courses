## Sub lesson 1 — Transport in my country

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: at six years old the course already runs on two rails — the working country (how we travel, the jobs our grandfathers did, the job I will do) and the inherited country (Zayed the father, the days we love, our heritage) — and every lesson plan is required to name the citizenship concepts it builds: belonging, citizenship, social responsibility, cooperation, national identity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
