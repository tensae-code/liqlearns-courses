# Hands-on — Transport in My Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Transport in my country
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the means of transport sorted by where they travel — land transport and how people move from place to place; air transport (aeroplane, spacecraft, balloon); sea transport classified (the ship that carries people and goods, the sailing boat on the waves, the fast jet ski) Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
