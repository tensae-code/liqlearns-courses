# Hands-on — How the Community Supports Families

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a community is a group of people living together in the same area sharing common values; it supports families with emotional support (mentorship, after-school programmes, counselling), financial support (crowdfunding, donations, emergency assistance), childcare, health and nutrition programmes, safety and protection, and practical help at funerals, weddings and birthdays, including nhimbe/ilima communal work parties. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
