# Video script — How the Community Supports Families

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe Grade 6 zw-g6 PlusOne Social Science Grade 6 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 1), teaches: A community is a group of people living together in the same area sharing common values; it supports families with emotional support (mentorship, after-school programmes, counselling), financial support (crowdfunding, donations, emergency assistance), childcare, health and nutrition programmes, safety and protection, and practical help at funerals, weddings and birthdays, including nhimbe/ilima communal work parties.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 8) (teacher guide — evidence): the three arms of government are taught through school comparisons (executive = school head, legislature = student council, judiciary = disciplinary committee) and the full bill-to-Act path; protection law is explicit — the age of consent is 18, sex with a minor is statutory rape, and child pledging is named as a harmful past practice; community support to families still runs through nhimbe/ilima communal work parties.
