# Me Myself — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Me Myself  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Me Myself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) I am a boy or a girl, my name, my class, the colour of my hair; the things I like and can do, and the feelings they bring — happiness, sadness, anger — matched to their pictures; and how I have grown: the day I was born, the day I walked, the day I talked, the day I went to school  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) I am a boy or a girl, my name, my class, the colour of my hair; the things I like and can do, and the feelings they bring — happiness, sadness, anger — matched to their pictures; and how I have grown: the day I was born, the day I walked, the day I talked, the day I went to school**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Iran's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) Iran's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches I am a boy or a girl, my name, my class, the colour of my hair.  b) Iran's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) This lesson teaches I am a boy or a girl, my name, my class, the colour of my hair.**
