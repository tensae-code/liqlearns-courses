# Me Myself — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Me Myself — I am a boy or a girl, my name, my class, the colour of my hair; the things I like and can do, and the feelings they bring — happiness, sadness, anger — matched to their pictures; and how I have grown: the day I was born, the day I walked, the day I talked, the day I went to school (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
