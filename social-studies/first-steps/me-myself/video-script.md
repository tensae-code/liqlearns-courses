# Video script — Me Myself

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 10, teaches: I am a boy or a girl, my name, my class, the colour of my hair; the things I like and can do, and the feelings they bring — happiness, sadness, anger — matched to their pictures; and how I have grown: the day I was born, the day I walked, the day I talked, the day I went to school.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
