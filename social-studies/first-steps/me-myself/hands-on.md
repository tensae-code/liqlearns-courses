# Hands-on — Me Myself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: I am a boy or a girl, my name, my class, the colour of my hair; the things I like and can do, and the feelings they bring — happiness, sadness, anger — matched to their pictures; and how I have grown: the day I was born, the day I walked, the day I talked, the day I went to school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
