# Hands-on — My Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Recording who is in my family, what makes it special, its history and its celebrations, and the affirmative actions families take for each other. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
