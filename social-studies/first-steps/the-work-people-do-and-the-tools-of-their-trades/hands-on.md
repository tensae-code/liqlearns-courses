# Hands-on — The Work People Do and the Tools of Their Trades

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the work people do in the community — the carpenter and his wooden boxes, the tailor, the baker, the photographer — and the tools each trade uses (the tools' change over time was placed by the History wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
