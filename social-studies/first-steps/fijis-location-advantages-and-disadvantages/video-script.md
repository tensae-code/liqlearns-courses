The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Fiji's book, the *Fiji Year 7 Social Science* (printed p. 48), teaches: 3.1 Being an island country: Fiji's position among Pacific neighbours, hub of the South West Pacific, advantages (tropical climate, gateway flights) and disadvantages (reefs, remoteness, small size, cyclones) of its location.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 7 Social Science): Year 7 widens the lens to the whole South Pacific — island types, "islandness" and vulnerability, Pacific hazards, and resources owned by tribes and clans; Fiji is taught as the hub of the South West Pacific.
