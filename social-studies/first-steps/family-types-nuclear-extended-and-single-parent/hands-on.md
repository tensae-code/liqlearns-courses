# Hands-on — Family Types Nuclear Extended and Single Parent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: family types (nuclear, extended, single-parent) with their advantages and disadvantages, and the functions of the family — care, education, teaching acceptable behaviour and values, affection and security. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
