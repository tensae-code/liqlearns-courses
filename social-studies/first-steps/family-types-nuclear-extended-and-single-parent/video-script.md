# Video script — Family Types Nuclear Extended and Single Parent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sierra Leone's SL SS BECE JSS3 teacher guide (printed p. 54), teaches: family types (nuclear, extended, single-parent) with their advantages and disadvantages, and the functions of the family — care, education, teaching acceptable behaviour and values, affection and security.

Sierra Leone's own way: the whole syllabus is taught through Sierra Leone's own peoples and institutions — culture is taught ethnic group by ethnic group (Mende, Temne, Limba, Krio…: dress, music, dance, craft, secret societies), and global bodies are taught through what they have done for Sierra Leone itself (UN Special Court, Sierra Leone as the UN's 100th member in 1961).
