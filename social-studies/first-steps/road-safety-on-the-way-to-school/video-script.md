# Video script — Road Safety on the Way to School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 1 Свет око нас 1 (printed pp. 38–39), teaches: The street's parts named — pavement for walkers, roadway for vehicles, zebra crossing for crossing — and where there is no pavement the walker keeps to the left edge, facing the traffic so drivers see him.

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed p. 30), teaches: Where a road has no pavement, walkers may use the roadway itself but must keep as close as possible to its left edge, facing the traffic, inside and outside the settlement.

Serbia's own way: the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast. the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays.
