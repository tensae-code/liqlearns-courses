# Hands-on — Road Safety on the Way to School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## На путу од куће до школе / На улици без тротоара (On the way from home to school / On a street without a pavement)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the street's parts named — pavement for walkers, roadway for vehicles, zebra crossing for crossing — and where there is no pavement the walker keeps to the left edge, facing the traffic so drivers see him. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## На улици и путу без тротоара (On a street and road without a pavement)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: where a road has no pavement, walkers may use the roadway itself but must keep as close as possible to its left edge, facing the traffic, inside and outside the settlement. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
