# Road Safety on the Way to School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: На путу од куће до школе / На улици без тротоара (On the way from home to school / On a street without a pavement) — the street's parts named — pavement for walkers, roadway for vehicles, zebra crossing for crossing — and where there is no pavement the walker keeps to the left edge, facing the traffic so drivers see him (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: На улици и путу без тротоара (On a street and road without a pavement) — where a road has no pavement, walkers may use the roadway itself but must keep as close as possible to its left edge, facing the traffic, inside and outside the settlement (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way — the street's parts named — pavement for walkers, roadway for vehicles, zebra crossing for crossing — and where there is no pavement the walker keeps to the left edge, facing the traffic so drivers see him (any close wording).**
