# Road Safety on the Way to School — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called “На путу од куће до школе / На улици без тротоара (On the way from home to school / On a street without a pavement)” is —
a) На путу од куће до школе / На улици без тротоара (On the way from home to school / On a street without a pavement)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) На путу од куће до школе / На улици без тротоара (On the way from home to school / On a street without a pavement)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part is mainly about —
a) the street's parts named — pavement for walkers, roadway for vehicles, zebra crossing for crossing — and where there is no pavement the walker keeps to the left edge, facing the traffic so drivers see him  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the street's parts named — pavement for walkers, roadway for vehicles, zebra crossing for crossing — and where there is no pavement the walker keeps to the left edge, facing the traffic so drivers see him**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The part of this lesson called “На улици и путу без тротоара (On a street and road without a pavement)” is —

**Answer: a) На улици и путу без тротоара (On a street and road without a pavement)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part is mainly about —

**Answer: a) where a road has no pavement, walkers may use the roadway itself but must keep as close as possible to its left edge, facing the traffic, inside and outside the settlement**
