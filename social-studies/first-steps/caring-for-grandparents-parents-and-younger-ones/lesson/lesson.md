## Sub lesson 1 — Caring for Grandparents Parents and Younger Ones

Eritrea Grade 6 ER G6 Social Studies (printed p. 113), teaches: older children as companions who cooperate, share and teach good behaviour, and their duty to keep younger children safe from home dangers — Amal pulling Abdu away from the burning kettle. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Eritrea's way — the book's own teaching way

Eritrea's own way: the Social Studies book closes not with an exam unit but with the family — an older Eritrean child's real job of minding younger brothers and sisters, taught through Amal, who gained a stepmother and a half-brother and pulls him from the stove.
