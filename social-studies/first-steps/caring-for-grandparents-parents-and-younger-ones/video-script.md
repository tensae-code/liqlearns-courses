The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Eritrea Grade 6 ER G6 Social Studies (printed p. 113), teaches: Older children as companions who cooperate, share and teach good behaviour, and their duty to keep younger children safe from home dangers — Amal pulling Abdu away from the burning kettle.

Eritrea's own way: the Social Studies book closes not with an exam unit but with the family — an older Eritrean child's real job of minding younger brothers and sisters, taught through Amal, who gained a stepmother and a half-brother and pulls him from the stove.
