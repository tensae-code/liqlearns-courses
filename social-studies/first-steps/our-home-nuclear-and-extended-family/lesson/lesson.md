## Sub lesson 1 — Our home

Turkey Grade 1 Hayat Bilgisi 1. ## Sub lesson 2 — Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: the Grade 1 year opens school itself as the first society: a six-year-old first learns to introduce herself and listen, then the school's rooms and workers, then rules the class itself votes on, and only then the family as the second society — nuclear and extended family, the values that hold it together, and jobs shared like a team. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
