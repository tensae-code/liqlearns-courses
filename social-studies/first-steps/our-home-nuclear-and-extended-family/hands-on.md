# Hands-on — Our Home Nuclear and Extended Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a nuclear family is mother, father and children; an extended family adds other relatives such as grandparents; in the family we feel safe and strong, and the values that hold a family together are love, compassion, self-sacrifice, understanding, unity and togetherness — through Elif's diary of rehearsing a poem with her whole family Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Turkey's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
