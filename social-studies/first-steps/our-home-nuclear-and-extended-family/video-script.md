The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 1 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed p. 86, teaches: a nuclear family is mother, father and children; an extended family adds other relatives such as grandparents; in the family we feel safe and strong, and the values that hold a family together are love, compassion, self-sacrifice, understanding, unity and togetherness — through Elif's diary of rehearsing a poem with her whole family

Turkey's own way: the Grade 1 year opens school itself as the first society: a six-year-old first learns to introduce herself and listen, then the school's rooms and workers, then rules the class itself votes on, and only then the family as the second society — nuclear and extended family, the values that hold it together, and jobs shared like a team.
