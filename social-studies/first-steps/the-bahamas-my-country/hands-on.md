# Hands-on — The Bahamas My Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The child's own country — the Commonwealth of The Bahamas as a group of islands, independence on 10 July 1973, and the national symbols (flag, anthem, pledge, coat of arms, flamingo, blue marlin, lignum vitae, yellow elder). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahamas' way
Say how Bahamas' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
