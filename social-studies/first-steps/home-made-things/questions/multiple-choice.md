# Home Made Things — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Home made things  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Home made things**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) the things made at home by hand  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) the things made at home by hand**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches The things made at home by hand.  b) Nepal's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
