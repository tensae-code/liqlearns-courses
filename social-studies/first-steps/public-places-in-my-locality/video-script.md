# Video script — Public Places In My Locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Mauritania's book, the *Mauritania Grade 3 geo-3af-m* (printed p. 31), teaches: Axis 4 Landmarks: public places. Mauritania's book, the *Mauritania Grade 4 geo-4af-m* (printed p. 28), teaches: Public places.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 3 geo-3af-m): The youngest geography book in the set is built on five axes worked through observation pictures (I observe / I discuss / I conclude): the map key, position, location, landmarks, and a "time landmark" axis. Geography scope placed below; the time-landmark axis is local HISTORY and is NOT placed: History of my village (printed p. 37), History of the founding of the mosque (p. 39), History of the founding of the mahadra (p. 41), History of the founding of the school (p. 43), History of the founding of the dispensary (p. 45).

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 4 geo-4af-m): A one-topic-per-spread observation book that moves from the child's own wilaya outward: the wilaya map first, then the landforms of home (watercourses, mountains, plains, plateaus, sand dunes), the weather elements and the two measuring instruments (thermometer, rain gauge), then the lived place — public places, archaeological sites, street names, my village, the capital of my wilaya — closing with integration exercises on the relief and climate of the wilaya capital.
