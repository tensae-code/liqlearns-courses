# Video script — My Job When I Grow Up

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G1 teacher guide, volume 2, printed p. 28, teaches: the child looks forward from the grandfathers' jobs to his own — the jobs of today's country and the one he hopes to do when he grows up, work as something every person in the community takes part in

United Arab Emirates' own way: at six years old the course already runs on two rails — the working country (how we travel, the jobs our grandfathers did, the job I will do) and the inherited country (Zayed the father, the days we love, our heritage) — and every lesson plan is required to name the citizenship concepts it builds: belonging, citizenship, social responsibility, cooperation, national identity.
