# Connecting with Our Community

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the deeds by which a school joins hands with the community around it — painting and cleaning the neighbourhood wall with the residents, collecting for the flood areas ("giving and sending love"), the fair that swaps rubbish for gifts and green plants with parents and neighbours joining in, campaigning for traffic safety, and planting trees — each deed matched to its meaning: spreading love, sharing difficulties, protecting the environment — as Vietnam's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Activities connecting with the community

Vietnam Grade 3 Tự nhiên và Xã hội 3, printed pp. 22–25, teaches: The deeds by which a school joins hands with the community around it — painting and cleaning the neighbourhood wall with the residents, collecting for the flood areas ("giving and sending love"), the fair that swaps rubbish for gifts and green plants with parents and neighbours joining in, campaigning for traffic safety, and planting trees — each deed matched to its meaning: spreading love, sharing difficulties, protecting the environment. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: kinship is mapped on both sides of the family with the exact word of address for every relative (and a note that regions address them differently), and the school's link to its community is made of named joint deeds — flood relief, a rubbish-for-plants fair, traffic-safety campaigning and tree planting. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
