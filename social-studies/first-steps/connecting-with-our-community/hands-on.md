# Hands-on — Connecting with Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the deeds by which a school joins hands with the community around it — painting and cleaning the neighbourhood wall with the residents, collecting for the flood areas ("giving and sending love"), the fair that swaps rubbish for gifts and green plants with parents and neighbours joining in, campaigning for traffic safety, and planting trees — each deed matched to its meaning: spreading love, sharing difficulties, protecting the environment. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
