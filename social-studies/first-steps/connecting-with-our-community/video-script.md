# Video script — Connecting with Our Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 3 Tự nhiên và Xã hội 3, printed pp. 22–25, teaches: The deeds by which a school joins hands with the community around it — painting and cleaning the neighbourhood wall with the residents, collecting for the flood areas ("giving and sending love"), the fair that swaps rubbish for gifts and green plants with parents and neighbours joining in, campaigning for traffic safety, and planting trees — each deed matched to its meaning: spreading love, sharing difficulties, protecting the environment.

Vietnam's own way: kinship is mapped on both sides of the family with the exact word of address for every relative (and a note that regions address them differently), and the school's link to its community is made of named joint deeds — flood relief, a rubbish-for-plants fair, traffic-safety campaigning and tree planting.
