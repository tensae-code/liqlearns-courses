# Hands-on — Duties of Family Members Everyone Helps at Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every member has duties and cooperation is necessary; the father provides and shares in raising children and housework, mothers run the home and also work outside as teachers and doctors, and sons and daughters help as they are able — a happy family is one whose members are close and cooperate. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Afghanistan's way
Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
