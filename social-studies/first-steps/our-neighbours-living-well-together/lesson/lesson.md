## Sub lesson 1 — Our Neighbours Living Well Together

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (student book, Persian), printed p. 2, teaches: a neighbourhood (محله) is the part of a city or village where a group of people live side by side; good neighbours know each other's state, help each other and are kind. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the neighbourhood is taught as a moral community before it is a map — the Prophet's saying on treating neighbours well opens the book, and the nation itself is introduced as "one big family in a big house called Iran" whose shared rules are written in the Constitution. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
