# Hands-on — Our Neighbours Living Well Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a neighbourhood (محله) is the part of a city or village where a group of people live side by side; good neighbours know each other's state, help each other and are kind. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
