# Video script — What history is

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — History — inquiry into the human past. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The word history comes from the Greek istoria, which means inquiry. Watch the names appear one by one: istoria, pre-history, cause and effect. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Change, continuity, cause and effect. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Some things change: empires rise, tools improve, cities grow. Watch the names appear one by one: continuity, change, evidence. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Why the past needs a method. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Because the past cannot be visited, it must be reconstructed — and reconstruction without rules becomes invention. Watch the names appear one by one: method, reconstruction, proof. This is the part of the story to hold.
