# What history is — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — History — inquiry into the human past

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **istoria** (the Greek word for inquiry, from which 'history' comes).

## Sub lesson 2 — Change, continuity, cause and effect

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **continuity** (what stays the same across long stretches of the past).

## Sub lesson 3 — Why the past needs a method

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **method** (the shared rules by which historians test claims from sources).
