# What history is — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the historian's question of what made things happen and what followed' is ______.
**Answer: cause and effect**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the surviving traces — writings, objects, memories — on which history rests' is ______.
**Answer: evidence**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'a claim that can be shown from reliable sources' is ______.
**Answer: proof**
