# What history is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The human past before writing, before about 4000 BC — this is pre-history.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, istoria means the historian's question of what made things happen and what followed.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: What alters — states, tools, beliefs and ways of life — this is change.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, continuity means the surviving traces — writings, objects, memories — on which history rests.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Rebuilding the past from surviving evidence — this is reconstruction.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, method means a claim that can be shown from reliable sources.
**Answer: False**
