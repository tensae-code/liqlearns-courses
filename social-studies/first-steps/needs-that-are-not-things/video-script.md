# Video script — Needs That Are Not Things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 10 (special needs, Kardanesh) (student book, Persian), printed p. 15, teaches: are our needs only food, clothing and housing? — no: we also have non-material needs — to have friends and be loved, to talk with others and receive their help; no one can meet these needs alone — even the nursing baby, who cannot drink milk or be bathed and dressed by itself, needs its mother's embrace and love; people need one another, as the pictures show — the boy in the wheelchair, the volleyball team, the gift given with a smile.

Iran's own way: the special-needs secondary book is a citizen's primer in the plainest form — needs first (things, then love and company), then the circles that meet them: family, groups, institutions — and at the top, government and the Majlis, explained through a ship and its captain and a school and its rules.
