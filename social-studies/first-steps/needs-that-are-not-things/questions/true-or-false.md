# Needs That Are Not Things — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: are our needs only food, clothing and housing? — no: we also have non-material needs — to have friends and be loved, to talk with others and receive their help; no one can meet these needs alone — even the nursing baby, who cannot drink milk or be bathed and dressed by itself, needs its mother's embrace and love; people need one another, as the pictures show — the boy in the wheelchair, the volleyball team, the gift given with a smile.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iran's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
