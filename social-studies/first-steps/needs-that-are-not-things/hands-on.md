# Hands-on — Needs That Are Not Things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: are our needs only food, clothing and housing? — no: we also have non-material needs — to have friends and be loved, to talk with others and receive their help; no one can meet these needs alone — even the nursing baby, who cannot drink milk or be bathed and dressed by itself, needs its mother's embrace and love; people need one another, as the pictures show — the boy in the wheelchair, the volleyball team, the gift given with a smile. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
