# Hands-on — My House, My Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The my-house portfolio page
Take a blank page, the way the Ik-boek does. Draw your own house on it — the whole dwelling as you see it from outside — and write about it underneath: who lives there with you, and what your house is like. Keep the page; it is the first page of your own place-portfolio.

## A dwelling for Botje
The workbook asks: "This is Botje. Where would he live?" Draw a dwelling place for Botje — a place where he could live, with what he would need in it. Show your drawing to a partner and say in one sentence why that place works as his dwelling.
