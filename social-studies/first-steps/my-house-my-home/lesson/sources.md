**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Suriname, Yemen).**

Suriname's own way: OJW (Oriëntatie op Jezelf en de Wereld) starts Geography from the child outward — a blank-page personal portfolio the pupil fills in: self, family, house, then town/district/village, widening place by place; the companion workbook works one worksheet per OJW lesson — draw, colour, match — and its Geography enters as drawing a dwelling. This lesson keeps that way: the house is met as a blank page the learner fills with their own dwelling first, then as a dwelling drawn for Botje. The lesson teaches the placements' content in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed. The books' non-Geography parts (Who am I, My family, What am I good at, and the workbook's senses, health, plant and animal lessons) are named in the placements file and are not placed in this lesson.

Yemen's own way (G3 Social Studies): the combined Social Education book starts from the child's own family, mosque and school, then widens to neighbours and the neighbourhood/village — the remaining strands are taught as lived duties (kindness to parents, care of the home, good neighbourliness, school order) before any geography appears. That evidence is stocked here as the way Yemen teaches this thing; it is counted in the acceptance count above (student book).
