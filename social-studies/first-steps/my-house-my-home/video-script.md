# Video script — My House, My Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is my house.

In Suriname's Ik-boek, the page for my house starts blank. I fill it in myself: I draw my house, and I write about it.

A house is a dwelling place — the place where someone lives.

And this is Botje. Where would he live? Draw a dwelling place for him too.

Now draw your own house. That is where you live.
