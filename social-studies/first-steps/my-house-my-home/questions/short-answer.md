# My House, My Home — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What do you do on the Ik-boek's My house page, and why does the page start blank?
**Answer: You draw your own house and write about it; the page starts blank because the Ik-boek is a personal portfolio the pupil fills in themselves (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does Botje need a dwelling place drawn for him?
**Answer: Because every living thing needs a place where it lives — a dwelling place of its own (any close wording).**
