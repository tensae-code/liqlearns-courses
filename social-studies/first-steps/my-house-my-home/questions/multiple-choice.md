# My House, My Home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Suriname's Ik-boek, the page headed Mijn huis (My house) starts —
a) full of printed text to copy  b) blank, for the pupil to fill in  c) with a map of the world
**Answer: b) blank, for the pupil to fill in**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Your house is your —
a) dwelling place, the place where you live  b) school  c) market
**Answer: a) dwelling place, the place where you live**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The workbook's first lesson asks you to draw —
a) a flag  b) a dwelling place for Botje  c) a river
**Answer: b) a dwelling place for Botje**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The workbook page asks about Botje: —
a) "What does he eat?"  b) "Where does he go to school?"  c) "Where would he live?"
**Answer: c) "Where would he live?"**
