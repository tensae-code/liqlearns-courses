# Video script — My Neighbours and Friendship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nicaragua Grade 2 Conociendo mi Mundo G2 (printed p. 12), teaches: Friendship with neighbours — how they live, work, play and practise solidarity.

Nicaragua's own way: the first combined book builds the social world in rings — family, neighbours, school, homeland, municipality — and even its taxes lesson is told through the family's shopping receipts and the works taxes build in the child's own community.
