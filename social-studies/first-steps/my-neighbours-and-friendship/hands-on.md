# Hands-on — My Neighbours and Friendship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: friendship with neighbours — how they live, work, play and practise solidarity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Nicaragua's way
Say how Nicaragua's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
