# The Street is Not for Playing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called “Улица није за игру / Безбедни смо (The street is not for playing / We are safe)” is —
a) Улица није за игру / Безбедни смо (The street is not for playing / We are safe)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Улица није за игру / Безбедни смо (The street is not for playing / We are safe)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part is mainly about —
a) never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you**

**Answer: a) Безбедност у саобраћају (Safety in traffic)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part is mainly about —
