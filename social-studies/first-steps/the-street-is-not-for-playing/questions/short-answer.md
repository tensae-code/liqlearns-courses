# The Street is Not for Playing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Улица није за игру / Безбедни смо (The street is not for playing / We are safe) — never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Безбедност у саобраћају (Safety in traffic) — everyone in traffic — drivers, pedestrians, cyclists, passengers — must know and keep the traffic rules for their own safety and others'; the street is not a place for play, and the roadway is dangerous for standing, lingering and talking (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way — never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you (any close wording).**
