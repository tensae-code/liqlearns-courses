# The Street is Not for Playing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Everyone in traffic — drivers, pedestrians, cyclists, passengers — must know and keep the traffic rules for their own safety and others'; the street is not a place for play, and the roadway is dangerous for standing, lingering and talking.
**Answer: True**
