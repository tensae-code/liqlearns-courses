# Hands-on — The Street is Not for Playing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Улица није за игру / Безбедни смо (The street is not for playing / We are safe)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Безбедност у саобраћају (Safety in traffic)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: everyone in traffic — drivers, pedestrians, cyclists, passengers — must know and keep the traffic rules for their own safety and others'; the street is not a place for play, and the roadway is dangerous for standing, lingering and talking. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
