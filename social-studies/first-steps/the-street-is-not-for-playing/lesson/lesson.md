# The Street is Not for Playing

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Улица није за игру / Безбедни смо (The street is not for playing / We are safe)

Serbia Grade 1 Свет око нас 1 (printed pp. 42–43), teaches: never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Безбедност у саобраћају (Safety in traffic)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed p. 36), teaches: everyone in traffic — drivers, pedestrians, cyclists, passengers — must know and keep the traffic rules for their own safety and others'; the street is not a place for play, and the roadway is dangerous for standing, lingering and talking. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast. the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
