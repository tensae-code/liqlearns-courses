# Video script — The Street is Not for Playing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 1 Свет око нас 1 (printed pp. 42–43), teaches: Never play or run on the roadway, pavement or car park or chase a ball into the street; safe play places are yards, playgrounds, parks and meadows away from traffic, and in a car sit belted in the back seat and wear bright, reflective clothes so drivers notice you.

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed p. 36), teaches: Everyone in traffic — drivers, pedestrians, cyclists, passengers — must know and keep the traffic rules for their own safety and others'; the street is not a place for play, and the roadway is dangerous for standing, lingering and talking.

Serbia's own way: the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast. the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
