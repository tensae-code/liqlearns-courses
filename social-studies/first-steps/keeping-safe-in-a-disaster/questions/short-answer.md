**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 21 "How do I protect myself in natural disasters and emergencies?" (Байгалийн гамшиг, онц байдлын үед өөрийгөө хэрхэн хамгаалах вэ?) — A natural disaster strikes without warning — "a disaster does not come announced" — so everyone stays prepared: what to do before, during and after earthquake, fire, flood and storm (do not panic, get under a strong table, stay away from windows; check yourself for injury and signal others afterwards); and an emergency (онц байдал) is when the state itself acts to protect the citizens — in earthquake, storm, drought, zud, flood, fire or epidemic — with what a family must keep ready (any close wording).**
