# Video script — Keeping Safe in a Disaster

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 61, teaches: A natural disaster strikes without warning — "a disaster does not come announced" — so everyone stays prepared: what to do before, during and after earthquake, fire, flood and storm (do not panic, get under a strong table, stay away from windows; check yourself for injury and signal others afterwards); and an emergency (онц байдал) is when the state itself acts to protect the citizens — in earthquake, storm, drought, zud, flood, fire or epidemic — with what a family must keep ready.

Mongolia's own way (Book 4): the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other.
