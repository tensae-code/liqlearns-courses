# Hands-on — Keeping Safe in a Disaster

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 21 "How do I protect myself in natural disasters and emergencies?" (Байгалийн гамшиг, онц байдлын үед өөрийгөө хэрхэн хамгаалах вэ?)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A natural disaster strikes without warning — "a disaster does not come announced" — so everyone stays prepared: what to do before, during and after earthquake, fire, flood and storm (do not panic, get under a strong table, stay away from windows; check yourself for injury and signal others afterwards); and an emergency (онц байдал) is when the state itself acts to protect the citizens — in earthquake, storm, drought, zud, flood, fire or epidemic — with what a family must keep ready. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
