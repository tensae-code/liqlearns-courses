The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Fiji's book, the *Fiji Year 5 Social Studies* (printed p. 27), teaches: Unit 3 Place and Environment, 5.3.1 Features of Places: where Fiji is in the South Pacific, its 322 islands, Pacific neighbours and capitals table.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 5 Social Studies): Geography is Fiji-first — the child locates Fiji among Pacific neighbours, works on the map of Fiji's islands and divisions, and learns land care through cane farming, river-farm flooding and village land use.
