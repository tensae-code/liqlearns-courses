# Hands-on — Mine And Other Peoples Property

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 16 "Mine and others' property" (Миний болон бусдын өмч)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Everyone has their own things and their own creations — your notebook, book and pencil are yours alone, and a family's fence, house, livestock and car are the family's private property; a scholar's book or invention is intellectual property; you may not take what others made by their labour without permission, and what you find you return to its owner — worked through Bat and Bold's quarrel over a pen taken without asking. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
