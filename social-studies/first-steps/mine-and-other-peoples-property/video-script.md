The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 47, teaches: Everyone has their own things and their own creations — your notebook, book and pencil are yours alone, and a family's fence, house, livestock and car are the family's private property; a scholar's book or invention is intellectual property; you may not take what others made by their labour without permission, and what you find you return to its owner — worked through Bat and Bold's quarrel over a pen taken without asking.

Mongolia's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact.
