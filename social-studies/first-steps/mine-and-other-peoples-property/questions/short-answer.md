# Mine And Other Peoples Property — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 16 "Mine and others' property" (Миний болон бусдын өмч) — Everyone has their own things and their own creations — your notebook, book and pencil are yours alone, and a family's fence, house, livestock and car are the family's private property; a scholar's book or invention is intellectual property; you may not take what others made by their labour without permission, and what you find you return to its owner — worked through Bat and Bold's quarrel over a pen taken without asking (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact (any close wording).**
