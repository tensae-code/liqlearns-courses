# Mine And Other Peoples Property — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 16 "Mine and others' property" (Миний болон бусдын өмч)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 16 "Mine and others' property" (Миний болон бусдын өмч)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Everyone has their own things and their own creations — your notebook, book and pencil are yours alone, and a family's fence, house, livestock and car are the family's private property; a scholar's book or invention is intellectual property; you may not take what others made by their labour without permission, and what you find you return to its owner — worked through Bat and Bold's quarrel over a pen taken without asking  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Everyone has their own things and their own creations — your notebook, book and pencil are yours alone, and a family's fence, house, livestock and car are the family's private property; a scholar's book or invention is intellectual property; you may not take what others made by their labour without permission, and what you find you return to its owner — worked through Bat and Bold's quarrel over a pen taken without asking**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
