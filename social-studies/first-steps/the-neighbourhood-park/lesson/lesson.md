## Sub lesson 1 — The Neighbourhood Park

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 74, teaches: in the park we take turns on the swings, ask the teacher before drinking water, and after eating fruit together we put our rubbish in the bin so the park stays clean and beautiful; the park's signs — drinking water, non-drinking water, keep off the grass. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
