# Video script — The Neighbourhood Park

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 74, teaches: in the park we take turns on the swings, ask the teacher before drinking water, and after eating fruit together we put our rubbish in the bin so the park stays clean and beautiful; the park's signs — drinking water, non-drinking water, keep off the grass.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
