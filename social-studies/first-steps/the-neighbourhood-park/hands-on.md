# Hands-on — The Neighbourhood Park

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: in the park we take turns on the swings, ask the teacher before drinking water, and after eating fruit together we put our rubbish in the bin so the park stays clean and beautiful; the park's signs — drinking water, non-drinking water, keep off the grass. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
