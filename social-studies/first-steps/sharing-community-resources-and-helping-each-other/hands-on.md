# Hands-on — Sharing Community Resources and Helping Each Other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: shared resources the whole community uses together (borehole, dip tank, community hall, playgrounds, parks) and how people help each other at events and in need; sharing builds teamwork. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
