# Video script — Sharing Community Resources and Helping Each Other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe Grade 1 zw-g1 PlusOne Social Science Grade 1 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 83), teaches: Shared resources the whole community uses together (borehole, dip tank, community hall, playgrounds, parks) and how people help each other at events and in need; sharing builds teamwork.
Zimbabwe ECD B zw-ecdb PlusOne Social Science ECD B Teacher's Guide (printed p. 85), teaches: Sharing and helping practised in the wider community, the third ring of the ECD B helping spiral.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 3) (teacher guide — evidence): Heritage-Based Curriculum infant guide teaches civics through the child's own duties — rights arrive paired with responsibilities (homework, cleaning, respecting elders) and protection is taught as naming who to tell (grandmother, teacher, police), with community taught as shared things (borehole, dip tank, hall) people take turns to use.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 2) (teacher guide — evidence): ECD B widens ECD A's family civics one ring — rights gain the right to identity (your name, clan and totem) and to clean water, responsibilities are framed as being a "model citizen" of the school, and sharing-and-helping is taught in three widening places: school, community, family.
