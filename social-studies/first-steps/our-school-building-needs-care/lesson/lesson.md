## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: the family is taught through Venezuela's own Family Protection Law and through how families actually form and grow (courtship, weddings, births, learning from babyhood); equality is taught straight from the 1999 Constitution — equal rights whatever your skin, religion, sex or disability, in a mestizo population.
