# Video script — Our School Building Needs Care

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela Grade 2 G2 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 14, teaches: The school building itself may need something — leaking pipes, roofs and dirty walls in old school buildings, or rooms to reorganise in converted houses — and pupils learn to diagnose the school's problems, split them into what organised pupil teams can fix with teachers and families and what must be reported with reasons to the authorities, then elect a spokesperson to meet the school directors.

Venezuela's own way: the family is taught through Venezuela's own Family Protection Law and through how families actually form and grow (courtship, weddings, births, learning from babyhood); equality is taught straight from the 1999 Constitution — equal rights whatever your skin, religion, sex or disability, in a mestizo population.
