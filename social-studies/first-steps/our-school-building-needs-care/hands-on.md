# Hands-on — Our School Building Needs Care

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The school building itself may need something — leaking pipes, roofs and dirty walls in old school buildings, or rooms to reorganise in converted houses — and pupils learn to diagnose the school's problems, split them into what organised pupil teams can fix with teachers and families and what must be reported with reasons to the authorities, then elect a spokesperson to meet the school directors. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
