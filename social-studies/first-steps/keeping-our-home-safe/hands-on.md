# Hands-on — Keeping Our Home Safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: home safety rules — gas smells and what to do, never touching stove valves without an adult, fire and fireworks dangers, faulty heaters and electric wires, never leaving the door open or opening it to strangers when alone. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
