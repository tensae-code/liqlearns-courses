# Hands-on — Staying Safe at Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: sorting the things around the house by colour code — safe to use, use with care, dangerous (scissors, sockets, the stove, sharp garden tools). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
