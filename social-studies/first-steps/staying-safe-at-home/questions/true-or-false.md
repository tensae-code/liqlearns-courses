# Staying Safe at Home — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Sorting the things around the house by colour code — safe to use, use with care, dangerous (scissors, sockets, the stove, sharp garden tools).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Protecting yourself in high and closed places — Karam on the castle wall's edge, the ladder and the open window, the heater in Saeed's shut room (fresh air is needed when the heater burns), the sick friend playing too close; the child writes their own safety rules for high places and for closed places (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: Being careful with tools and materials — Ghayth helps his father mend the bicycle; the dangerous things in the tool cupboard are marked, the pliers are carried by the handle with the point down, and the harms are named: fires, poisoning, skin burns, wounds (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part teaches: The whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free)..
**Answer: True**
