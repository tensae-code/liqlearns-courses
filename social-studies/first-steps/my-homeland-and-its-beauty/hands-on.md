# Hands-on — My Homeland and Its Beauty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: saying your homeland's address (commune/ward, district, province) and presenting its beauty — Lũng Cú's rocky plateau, the wide sea, quiet rivers, endless fields, city towers, the Trường Sa islands — and its kind, hard-working people. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
