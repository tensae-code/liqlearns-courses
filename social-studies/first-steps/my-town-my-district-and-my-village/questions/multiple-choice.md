# My Town, My District and My Village — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the page for your house, the Ik-boek asks you to record —
a) only faraway countries  b) your town, your district and your village  c) the names of rivers only
**Answer: b) your town, your district and your village**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The place your house stands in is your —
a) town or village  b) district office  c) house roof
**Answer: a) town or village**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Your district is —
a) a room in your house  b) the wider place around your town or village  c) another country
**Answer: b) the wider place around your town or village**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In widening order, the places go —
a) district, town, house  b) house, district, town  c) house, town or village, district
**Answer: c) house, town or village, district**
