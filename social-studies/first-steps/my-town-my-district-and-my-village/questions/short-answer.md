# My Town, My District and My Village — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: what is the difference between where your house stands and the house itself?
**Answer: The house is my own dwelling; the town or village is the wider place the house stands in, where my neighbours live too (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name your own places in widening order, and say how the district holds the others.
**Answer: My house, then my town or village, then my district; the district is the wider place around the town or village, which in turn holds the house (with the learner's own real place names; any close wording).**
