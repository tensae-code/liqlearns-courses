# Video script — My Town, My District and My Village

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Start at my house.

My house stands in a place. That place is my town — or my village.

And around my town or my village is a wider place: my district.

In Suriname's Ik-boek, you write these places down yourself, one page at a time, widening out from your own house.

Now say yours: my house, my town or village, my district.
