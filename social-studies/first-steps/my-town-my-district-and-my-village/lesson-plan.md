# Lesson plan — My Town, My District and My Village

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** My Town, My District and My Village
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 1 — First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Suriname, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Your house stands inside a wider place — your town or village, inside your district — and you know those places by recording them yourself, widening outward from your own home.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner's own house (My House, My Home) and widens one step: the town or village around the house, and the district around that.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
