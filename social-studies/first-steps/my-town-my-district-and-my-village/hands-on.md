# Hands-on — My Town, My District and My Village

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The widening-circles page
Draw three circles, one inside the next, the way the Ik-boek widens place by place. In the smallest circle write and draw your house. In the next circle write the name of your town or village and draw one thing you know there. In the widest circle write the name of your district. Show the page to a partner and read the circles outward, from your house to your district.

## My town, district and village portfolio pages
On blank pages of your own, record your town, your district and your village the way the Ik-boek's pages ask: the name of each place, and what you know of it — drawn or written by you. Keep the pages with your my-house page; together they are your record of the places you live in.
