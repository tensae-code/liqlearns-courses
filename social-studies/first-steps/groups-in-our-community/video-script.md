# Video script — Groups in Our Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 1 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 20–21), teaches: The community's groups — religious groups who come together to pray, volunteer groups who willingly give their services without pay, and music groups and bands who bring entertainment.

Guyana's own way: the series opens at Grade 1 inside the home — the family that cooks and gardens together, the chores shared out fairly, the groups a small child already belongs to, and rules taught as the way a home and a classroom stay in harmony.
