# Hands-on — Groups in Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The community's groups — religious groups who come together to pray, volunteer groups who willingly give their services without pay, and music groups and bands who bring entertainment. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
