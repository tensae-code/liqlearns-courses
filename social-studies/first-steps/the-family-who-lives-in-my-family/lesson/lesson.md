## Sub lesson 1 — The Family Who Lives in My Family

Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 3, teaches: the family is mother, father and children — in some homes grandparents, uncles and aunts too — the first place a child is raised and learns greeting and respect for elders; good people make good families and good families a good society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: the child's world widens in rings — social life, family, mosque, school, village, city, homeland, world, Earth — and every ring carries its duties with it: respect for elders at home, duties and order at school, reverence for the flag, obedience to traffic rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
