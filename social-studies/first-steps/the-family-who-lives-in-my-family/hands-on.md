# Hands-on — The Family Who Lives in My Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The family is mother, father and children — in some homes grandparents, uncles and aunts too — the first place a child is raised and learns greeting and respect for elders; good people make good families and good families a good society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Afghanistan's way
Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
