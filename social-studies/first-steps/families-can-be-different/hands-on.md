# Hands-on — Families Can Be Different

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Families are special — the family types (single-parent, nuclear, extended, blended, foster), how families meet needs and care for each other, and families as part of a community. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahamas' way
Say how Bahamas' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
