# Hands-on — Knowing Who to Ask for Help

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: which troubles at home need an adult (a stomach ache, a tap that will not close, a smell of gas — ask neighbour Uncle An; a stranger's unwanted touching — tell mother at once) and which you can solve yourself. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
