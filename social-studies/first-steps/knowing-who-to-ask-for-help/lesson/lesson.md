# Knowing Who to Ask for Help

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: which troubles at home need an adult (a stomach ache, a tap that will not close, a smell of gas — ask neighbour Uncle An; a stranger's unwanted touching — tell mother at once) and which you can solve yourself — as Vietnam's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Seek support at home

Vietnam Grade 2 Đạo đức 2, printed pp. 51–54, teaches: Which troubles at home need an adult (a stomach ache, a tap that will not close, a smell of gas — ask neighbour Uncle An; a stranger's unwanted touching — tell mother at once) and which you can solve yourself. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Seek support at school

Vietnam Grade 2 Đạo đức 2, printed pp. 55–58, teaches: Telling the teacher when you are ill, bullied or have lost something; asking the teacher's guidance on work you cannot do; and carrying a personal card with relatives' names, phone numbers and address. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Seek support in public places

Vietnam Grade 2 Đạo đức 2, printed pp. 59–61, teaches: Staying calm when lost at the zoo and asking the security guard; knowing Vietnam's emergency numbers by heart — 111 child protection, 112 rescue, 113 police, 114 fire, 115 ambulance. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: Grade 2 widens the circle from family to homeland and public space: love of homeland is proven in small deeds (a donation box for neighbours in difficulty, flowers planted along the village road, a class visit to a Heroic Vietnamese Mother), and the civics core is rules in public places, taught place by place — school, hospital, bus stop, library, cinema, zoo, historic site. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
