# Video script — Knowing Who to Ask for Help

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 2 Đạo đức 2, printed pp. 51–54, teaches: Which troubles at home need an adult (a stomach ache, a tap that will not close, a smell of gas — ask neighbour Uncle An; a stranger's unwanted touching — tell mother at once) and which you can solve yourself.

Vietnam Grade 2 Đạo đức 2, printed pp. 55–58, teaches: Telling the teacher when you are ill, bullied or have lost something; asking the teacher's guidance on work you cannot do; and carrying a personal card with relatives' names, phone numbers and address.

Vietnam Grade 2 Đạo đức 2, printed pp. 59–61, teaches: Staying calm when lost at the zoo and asking the security guard; knowing Vietnam's emergency numbers by heart — 111 child protection, 112 rescue, 113 police, 114 fire, 115 ambulance.

Vietnam's own way: Grade 2 widens the circle from family to homeland and public space: love of homeland is proven in small deeds (a donation box for neighbours in difficulty, flowers planted along the village road, a class visit to a Heroic Vietnamese Mother), and the civics core is rules in public places, taught place by place — school, hospital, bus stop, library, cinema, zoo, historic site.
