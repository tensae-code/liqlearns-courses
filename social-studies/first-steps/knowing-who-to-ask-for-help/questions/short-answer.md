# Knowing Who to Ask for Help — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Seek support at home — which troubles at home need an adult (a stomach ache, a tap that will not close, a smell of gas — ask neighbour Uncle An; a stranger's unwanted touching — tell mother at once) and which you can solve yourself (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Seek support at school — telling the teacher when you are ill, bullied or have lost something; asking the teacher's guidance on work you cannot do; and carrying a personal card with relatives' names, phone numbers and address (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Seek support in public places — staying calm when lost at the zoo and asking the security guard; knowing Vietnam's emergency numbers by heart — 111 child protection, 112 rescue, 113 police, 114 fire, 115 ambulance (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 2 widens the circle from family to homeland and public space: love of homeland is proven in small deeds (a donation box for neighbours in difficulty, flowers planted along the village road, a class visit to a Heroic Vietnamese Mother), and the civics core is rules in public places, taught place by place — school, hospital, bus stop, library, cinema, zoo, historic site. (any close wording).**
