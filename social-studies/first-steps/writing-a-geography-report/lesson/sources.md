**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Vietnam).**

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Địa lí 10): a single Grade 10 course that runs the whole discipline — map craft, the Earth's spheres, then economic geography sector by sector — with Vietnam itself as the standing worked example (its power-plant and rice maps, the Red River's regime at Hanoi, Hanoi's climate chart, Vietnam's GDP structure, its internet and tourism figures) inside world-scale treatment.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Chuyên đề 10): the elective companion goes deep on just three things — climate change, urbanisation, and how to write a geography report — and keeps pulling the world material back to Vietnam (a metre of sea-level rise would flood 10–12% of Vietnam's area; Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City as the home examples of developing-world urbanisation).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11): after Grade 10's world systems, Grade 11 walks the world region by region — Latin America, the EU, Southeast Asia and ASEAN, Southwest Asia, the USA, Russia, Japan, China, Australia and South Africa — always with the same lens (position, nature, people, economy) and always measuring Vietnam's own place among them (its HDI row in the opening table, EU–Vietnam trade, Vietnam inside ASEAN, Japan's FDI into Vietnam).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12): the final-year electives turn homeward — the disasters Vietnam actually faces and how to live with them, how Vietnam itself is divided into regions for development, and the craft villages that carry its rural economy and culture — even printing the country's new 34-province table in force from 1 July 2025.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
