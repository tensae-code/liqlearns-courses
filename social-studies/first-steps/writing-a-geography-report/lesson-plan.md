# Lesson plan — Writing A Geography Report

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Writing A Geography Report
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bài 32 (Practical): Writing a report on an industry topic — choosing the topic, gathering sources (World Bank, OECD, WTO, Vietnamese ministries), and structuring the report; Bài 38 (Practical): Writing a report on a service industry (the lesson slug was proposed as NEW in Book 1 of this file; counted here as an existing target); Topic 10.3: How to write a geography report — what a report is, its structure (significance, feasibility, current state, solutions), the five steps from choosing the topic to presenting it, and practice topics (the lesson slug was proposed as NEW in Book 1 of this file; counted here as an existing target); Bài 3 (Practical): Collecting material and reporting on the opportunities and challenges of globalisation and regionalisation for developing countries (the lesson slug was proposed as NEW in Book 1 of this file; counted here as an existing target); Bài 5 (Practical): Writing a report on the features and expressions of the knowledge economy; Bài 8 (Practical): Writing a report on Brazil's economic development and the social problems it must solve (the richest tenth holding over 40% of GDP against 1% for the poorest tenth); Bài 10 (Practical): Writing a report on the industrial development of the Federal Republic of Germany (industry about 30% of GDP; machinery output worth 260 billion euros; electronics about 3% of GDP); Bài 14 (Practical): Finding out about Southeast Asia's foreign economic activities (tourism arrivals and revenue 2005–2019; the region's export–import chart); Bài 17 (Practical): Writing a report on Southwest Asia's oil and gas (113.2 billion tons of oil reserves; extraction and export tables; OPEC, founded 1960); Bài 25 (Practical): Writing a report on Japan's foreign economic activities (FDI and ODA tables; Japan's 62.9 billion USD invested in Vietnam; technology transfer); Bài 28 (Practical): Writing a report on the changing economy of China's coastal region (11 provinces making 53.6% of national GDP in 2021); Bài 29 (Practical): Studying Australia's economy from its economic map (GDP and structure, 2000–2020); Topic 12.2 (Practical): Choosing one of Vietnam's socio-economic regions and writing a report on its development resources, present state and directions (the lesson slug was proposed as NEW in Book 1 of this file; counted here as an existing target); Topic 12.3 (Practical): Finding out about a craft village in your own locality and writing the report; Bài 4 (Practical): Writing a report on how one component of Vietnam's nature (landforms, climate or living things) is differentiated, north–south, east–west or by altitude (the lesson slug was proposed as NEW in Book 1 of this file; counted here as an existing target); Bài 6 (Practical): Writing a short advocacy piece calling on the local community to use natural resources rationally or to protect the local environment; Bài 10 (Practical): Writing a report on one population theme in Vietnam — the population strategy, labour and new kinds of jobs, or green and smart urbanisation; Bài 23 (Practical): Finding out about and presenting a distinctive local service product — a traditional market or shopping centre, or a local tourism product; Bài 26 (Practical): Analysing the issue of developing the marine and island economy in the Red River Delta; Bài 29 (Practical): Writing a report analysing what the region's socio-economic development means for national defence and security; Bài 32 (Practical): Writing a report on climate change in the Mekong Delta — its effects on nature, economic activities and people's lives, and the mitigation and adaptation answers; Bài 34 (Practical): Writing an advocacy report on protecting Vietnam's sea and island sovereignty (sovereignty over Hoang Sa and Truong Sa; the 2012 Law of the Sea of Vietnam); Bài 35 (Practical): Studying the geography of your home province — writing a report on one theme: its position and territory, its nature, its population and society, or its economy — as Vietnam's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
