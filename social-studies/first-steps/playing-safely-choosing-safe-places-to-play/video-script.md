# Video script — Playing Safely Choosing Safe Places to Play

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Syria Grade 1 Social Studies (Syria Grade 1 Social Studies G1, printed p. 29), teaches: Telling safe play places from dangerous ones — the fenced park and the pitch are safe; the street, the balcony edge and the room full of hazards are not

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 1): the whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free).
