# Hands-on — Playing Safely Choosing Safe Places to Play

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: telling safe play places from dangerous ones — the fenced park and the pitch are safe; the street, the balcony edge and the room full of hazards are not. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
