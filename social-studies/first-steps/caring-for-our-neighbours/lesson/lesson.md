# Caring for Our Neighbours

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the families of the barrio — neighbours are treated as family, with respect and cooperation between households and care for the shared surroundings; friendship as honesty, sincerity and solidarity, told apart from false friendship — as Cuba's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Caring for Our Neighbours

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 5–11), teaches: The families of the barrio — neighbours are treated as family, with respect and cooperation between households and care for the shared surroundings; friendship as honesty, sincerity and solidarity, told apart from false friendship. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
