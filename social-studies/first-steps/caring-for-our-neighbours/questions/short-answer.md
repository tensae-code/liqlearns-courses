# Caring for Our Neighbours — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Caring for Our Neighbours — the families of the barrio — neighbours are treated as family, with respect and cooperation between households and care for the shared surroundings; friendship as honesty, sincerity and solidarity, told apart from false friendship (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors) (any close wording).**
