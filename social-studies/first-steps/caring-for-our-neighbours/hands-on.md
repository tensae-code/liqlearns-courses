# Hands-on — Caring for Our Neighbours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the families of the barrio — neighbours are treated as family, with respect and cooperation between households and care for the shared surroundings; friendship as honesty, sincerity and solidarity, told apart from false friendship. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
