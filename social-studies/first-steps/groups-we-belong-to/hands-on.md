# Hands-on — Groups We Belong to

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The groups of our lives — family first, then neighbours, school, Scouts and Guides, sports teams and clubs; groups share interests, give members roles and responsibilities, and hold rules that guide behaviour. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Maldives's way
Say how Maldives's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
