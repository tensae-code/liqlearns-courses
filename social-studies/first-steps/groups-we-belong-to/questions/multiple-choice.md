# Groups We Belong to — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Groups we belong to  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Groups we belong to**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) the groups of our lives — family first, then neighbours, school, Scouts and Guides, sports teams and clubs; groups share interests, give members roles and responsibilities, and hold rules that guide behaviour  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the groups of our lives — family first, then neighbours, school, Scouts and Guides, sports teams and clubs; groups share interests, give members roles and responsibilities, and hold rules that guide behaviour**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Maldives's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) Maldives's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches the groups of our lives — family first, then neighbours, school, Scouts and Guides, sports teams and clubs; groups share interests, give members roles and responsibilities, and hold rules that guide behaviour.  b) Maldives's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
