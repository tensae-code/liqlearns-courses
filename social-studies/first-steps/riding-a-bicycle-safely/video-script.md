Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 38–39), teaches: Safe riding places are traffic-free — playgrounds, parks, cycle paths — with helmet and pads; the age rules (under 9 only in pedestrian zones under supervision, from 9 near school, from 12 on public roads), checking brakes and wheels, both hands on the handlebars, and signalling turns by hand.

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
