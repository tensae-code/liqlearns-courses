# Hands-on — Riding a Bicycle Safely

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Вожња ролера, тротинета и бицикла (Riding rollers, a scooter and a bicycle)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: safe riding places are traffic-free — playgrounds, parks, cycle paths — with helmet and pads; the age rules (under 9 only in pedestrian zones under supervision, from 9 near school, from 12 on public roads), checking brakes and wheels, both hands on the handlebars, and signalling turns by hand. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
