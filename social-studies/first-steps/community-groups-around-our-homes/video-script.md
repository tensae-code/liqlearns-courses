# Video script — Community Groups Around Our Homes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 2 P2 Social Studies TG, printed p. 36, teaches: a community group is a group of people who work for public benefit; community groups make people come together and give people jobs — churches, hospitals and learning institutions are the examples.

South Sudan's own way: The guide sharpens the pupil's book's definitions — a community shares the same language, tradition and laws, and its elders, teachers and parents are named as the people who maintain law and order and help the community choose well.
