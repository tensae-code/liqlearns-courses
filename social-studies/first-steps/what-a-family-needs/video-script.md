# Video script — What A Family Needs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Angola Estudo do Meio G2 (printed p. 29), teaches: What a family needs to live — a house, food, water, clothing, schooling and work.

Angola's own way: Grade 2 widens each Grade 1 topic by one step — the family gains kinship names and needs, the school gains its parts and the care of school materials, and the book closes with two short themes that are pure community life: work, and transport and communications.
