# Hands-on — Order at School Rules Respect and the Flag

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: order at school — treating my teacher with respect, keeping quiet in the library, respecting the school's rules and following instructions, saluting my country's flag with enthusiasm, the golden rule for riding the school bus in order, not being absent, and taking initiative in school activities Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
