# Video script — Order at School Rules Respect and the Flag

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education G1 Term 3, printed p. 17, teaches: order at school — treating my teacher with respect, keeping quiet in the library, respecting the school's rules and following instructions, saluting my country's flag with enthusiasm, the golden rule for riding the school bus in order, not being absent, and taking initiative in school activities

United Arab Emirates' own way: at Grade 1 "order" is taught as one widening circle — home, then school, then neighbourhood — each part closing with a "Zayed taught me" saying and a named national value (respect, safety), and the country itself is introduced as a homeland of happiness and tolerance with its own Minister of Happiness and a Year of Tolerance.
