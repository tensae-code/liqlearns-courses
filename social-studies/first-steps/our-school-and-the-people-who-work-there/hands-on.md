# Hands-on — Our School and the People Who Work There

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the school's places (classrooms, library, laboratory, health room, prayer room, shop, yard) and its people — the principal and staff each have a job, and a pupil learns who to go to for what (a medical note, the morning ceremony). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
