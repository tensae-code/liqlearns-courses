## Sub lesson 1 — Our School and the People Who Work There

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the whole primary course works outward from the child — me, my family, my house, my school, from home to school — and moral teaching (gratitude, cooperation, not mocking others) is carried by family stories, one of them built around a grandmother and the Qur'anic command to speak gently to aged parents. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
