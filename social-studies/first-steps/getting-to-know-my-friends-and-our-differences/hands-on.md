# Hands-on — Getting to Know My Friends and Our Differences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 1 B. Aku Mengenal Teman-Temanku, Bab 3 B. Berbeda Tetap Bersama, Panduan Khusus Bab 3: Aku dan Teman-temanku — teaches: knowing classmates and respecting differences between friends, including boys and girls; different yet together — friends differ in origin, looks and likes and still play and learn as one; guidance for hobbies, difference-together and religious-diversity symbols. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indonesia's way
Say how Indonesia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
