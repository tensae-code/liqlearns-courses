# Video script — Getting to Know My Friends and Our Differences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Indonesia's Grade 1 PP-G1 student (printed p. 13); Grade 2 PP-G2 student (printed p. 91); Grade 2 PP-G2 teacher (printed p. 111), teaches: Bab 1 B. Aku Mengenal Teman-Temanku, Bab 3 B. Berbeda Tetap Bersama, Panduan Khusus Bab 3: Aku dan Teman-temanku — teaches: knowing classmates and respecting differences between friends, including boys and girls; different yet together — friends differ in origin, looks and likes and still play and learn as one; guidance for hobbies, difference-together and religious-diversity symbols.

Indonesia's own way: Pancasila values are taught from the child's own body, friends and family outward — five recurring child characters from Papua, Bali, Jakarta, Sulawesi and Kalimantan carry every chapter, so national unity is shown as named children from different islands. Grade 2 turns Pancasila from a symbol to copy into behaviour at home — each principle (sila) is matched to a picture-symbol on the Garuda shield and then to a concrete act a 7-year-old can do in the family. the guide organises Grade 2 by Fase A (the Kurikulum Merdeka phase for Grades 1–2), so civics outcomes are stated as phase competences, not yearly facts.
