**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Indonesia).** Indonesia's teacher-guide parts in this lesson are teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere — Indonesia counts through its student-book parts.

Indonesia's own way (Book 1): Pancasila values are taught from the child's own body, friends and family outward — five recurring child characters from Papua, Bali, Jakarta, Sulawesi and Kalimantan carry every chapter, so national unity is shown as named children from different islands.

Indonesia's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 turns Pancasila from a symbol to copy into behaviour at home — each principle (sila) is matched to a picture-symbol on the Garuda shield and then to a concrete act a 7-year-old can do in the family.

Indonesia's own way (Book 3): the guide organises Grade 2 by Fase A (the Kurikulum Merdeka phase for Grades 1–2), so civics outcomes are stated as phase competences, not yearly facts.
