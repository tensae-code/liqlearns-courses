# Hands-on — My Neighbourhood

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 2 This is our neighbourhood (اینجا محله‌ی ماست): a walk through the neighbourhood (گردش در محله) — the neighbourhood mosque, one of the places where the people of the neighbourhood gather, for prayer and for meeting each other; and neighbourhoods change (محله‌ها تغییر می‌کنند) — with time, people move away to live elsewhere and others move in, and the shape and kind of buildings change. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
