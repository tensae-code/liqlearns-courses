# Hands-on — Helping Vulnerable Members of the Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: vulnerable members (orphans, the elderly, people with disabilities, the underprivileged) need extra support; help by visiting and talking, giving food/clothes/toys, helping across the road and showing kindness. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
