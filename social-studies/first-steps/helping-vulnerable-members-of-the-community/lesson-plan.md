# Lesson plan — Helping Vulnerable Members of the Community

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** Helping Vulnerable Members of the Community
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Zimbabwe, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** vulnerable members (orphans, the elderly, people with disabilities, the underprivileged) need extra support; help by visiting and talking, giving food/clothes/toys, helping across the road and showing kindness — as Zimbabwe's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — vulnerable members (orphans, the elderly, people with disabilities, the underprivileged) need extra support; help by visiting and talking, giving food/clothes/toys, helping across the road and showing kindness.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
