# Hands-on — How I Act in an Emergency Calling for Help

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: behaving in an emergency — Rama hears cries from the neighbours' burning house and calls the fire brigade instead of rushing in; Syria's emergency numbers (fire 113, ambulance 110, rescue/police 112) and keeping your home address and the numbers in your notebook. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
