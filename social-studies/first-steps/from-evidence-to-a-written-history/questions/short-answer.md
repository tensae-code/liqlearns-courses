# From evidence to a written history — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** In the car-accident case study, name one primary and one secondary voice, and say why.
**Answer:** Primary: the police report made at the scene (or a witness speaking of what they saw). Secondary: a later retelling or report built from those accounts — it derives from the primaries.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What four verbs describe what a historian does with sources after selecting a problem?
**Answer:** Describe, analyse, evaluate and explain.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** History aims at objective truth, yet bias is hard to avoid. What two habits protect the reader?
**Answer:** Testing every source before use, and declaring selections — so the reader can check the road from evidence to page.
