# Hands-on — From evidence to a written history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Coin detective
If a coin (any coin) is available, examine it like a primary source: whose head or name, what date, what metal, what does it prove — and what can it never tell you? No coin: draw the Endubis coin from the picture and list its evidence the same way.

## The bending story
Whisper a two-sentence story down a line of five people. Compare the last version with the first, list every bend — then say why oral data is cross-checked against documents.
