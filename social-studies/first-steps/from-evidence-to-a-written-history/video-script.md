# Video script — From evidence to a written history

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open on a table. A silver coin spins and settles. Endubis of Aksum — struck in his own reign. Primary.
The camera pulls back. Ruins. A chronicle. A letter. All speaking from inside their events.
Now the second shelf. Textbooks. Biographies. A grandfather telling. Secondary — worked from the first shelf.
A historian lifts each piece, tests it — real or forged? reliable or bent? — and only then, writes.
That is the road. Evidence first. Writing last.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Primary and secondary — and testing them. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 depth sorts the evidence shelf with new care. The names to hold: external criticism, internal criticism.

[Grade 11 depth scene — From evidence to a written history — interpretation and objectivity. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
After criticism comes the historian's real work, in order: collect the evidence, criticise it, interpret it, and write — objectively, letting the proof carry the judgement. The names to hold: objectivity, abuse of history.
