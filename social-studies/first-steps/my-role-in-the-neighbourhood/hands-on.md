# Hands-on — My Role in the Neighbourhood

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your role in the neighbourhood
Work through this part with your hands: The child's own part — helping others, being polite and responsible, taking part, following rules, and keeping the neighbourhood clean and tidy. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Fiji's way
Say how Fiji's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
