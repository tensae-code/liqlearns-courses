# Video script — My Role in the Neighbourhood

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 4 Social Studies Year 4 (printed p. 16), teaches: The child's own part — helping others, being polite and responsible, taking part, following rules, and keeping the neighbourhood clean and tidy.

Fiji's own way (Grade 4): the neighbourhood is taught through Fiji's own community types — farming, iTaukei village and urban/town communities — and the child's duties inside it.
