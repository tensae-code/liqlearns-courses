# Video script — The Needs of People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece Grade 1 Meleti Perivallontos A' Dimotikou (printed p. 75), teaches: The basic needs every person has.

Greece Grade 3 Meleti Perivallontos G' Dimotikou (printed p. 91), teaches: What people need in order to live.

Greece's own way: the youngest pupils' integrated subject is built as widening circles — classroom, school, family, home, neighbourhood — introduced by two puppet-like guides (Leon and Milo), with Greek feasts and customs carrying the culture strand. Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos.
