**Answer: Information in a thousand ways — the many channels that bring us information (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: We get informed in many ways — comparing the many ways of being informed (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Greece's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos. Grade 4 turns from the neighbourhood to the whole country and its culture — Greek culture is set beside other peoples' cultures, and work is taught as the web of professions that together make one product. (any close wording).**
