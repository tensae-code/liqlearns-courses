# Video script — Getting Informed in a Thousand Ways

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece Grade 3 Meleti Perivallontos G' Dimotikou (printed p. 139), teaches: The many channels that bring us information.

Greece Grade 4 Meleti Perivallontos D' Dimotikou (printed p. 144), teaches: Comparing the many ways of being informed.

Greece's own way: Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos. Grade 4 turns from the neighbourhood to the whole country and its culture — Greek culture is set beside other peoples' cultures, and work is taught as the web of professions that together make one product.
