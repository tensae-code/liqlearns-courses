# The Neighbourhood Mosque — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the mosque of our neighbourhood celebrates the birthday of Imam Zaman on Nimeh Sha'ban; the children help sweep the mosque yard; after the congregational prayer the prayer leader explains that in the mosque we pray and worship God, learn how one another is, and can help those who need help — and the mosque holds a library and Qur'an classes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iran's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
