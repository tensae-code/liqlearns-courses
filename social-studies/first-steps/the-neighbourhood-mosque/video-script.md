# Video script — The Neighbourhood Mosque

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 78, teaches: the mosque of our neighbourhood celebrates the birthday of Imam Zaman on Nimeh Sha'ban; the children help sweep the mosque yard; after the congregational prayer the prayer leader explains that in the mosque we pray and worship God, learn how one another is, and can help those who need help — and the mosque holds a library and Qur'an classes.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
