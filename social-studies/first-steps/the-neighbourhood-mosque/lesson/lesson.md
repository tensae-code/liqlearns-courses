## Sub lesson 1 — The Neighbourhood Mosque

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 78, teaches: the mosque of our neighbourhood celebrates the birthday of Imam Zaman on Nimeh Sha'ban; the children help sweep the mosque yard; after the congregational prayer the prayer leader explains that in the mosque we pray and worship God, learn how one another is, and can help those who need help — and the mosque holds a library and Qur'an classes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
