# Hands-on — The Neighbourhood Mosque

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the mosque of our neighbourhood celebrates the birthday of Imam Zaman on Nimeh Sha'ban; the children help sweep the mosque yard; after the congregational prayer the prayer leader explains that in the mosque we pray and worship God, learn how one another is, and can help those who need help — and the mosque holds a library and Qur'an classes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
