# Video script — Dating the past — calendars, timelines and periods

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on a line. A long line. Time.
Watch. An event slides in. The Battle of Adwa. 1 March 1896.
Another slides in, far to the right. A great dam begins. 2 April 2011.
Same line. Same country. One hundred and fifteen years between them.
But wait — who counts the years? Rome counts from Christ. Mecca counts from the Hijra, 622.
And Ethiopia counts its own way — seven, eight years behind.
Three calendars. One past. Put the events in order, and the past stands up straight.
