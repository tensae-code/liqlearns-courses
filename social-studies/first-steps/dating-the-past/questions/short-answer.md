# Dating the past — calendars, timelines and periods — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many years make a decade, a century and a millennium?
**Answer:** 10, 100 and 1,000 years.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The same event can carry different year-numbers in different calendars. Why?
**Answer:** Because each calendar starts counting from a different event — the birth of Christ, or the Hijra — and the Ethiopian count runs seven to eight years behind the Gregorian.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three period labels historians use for long stretches of the past.
**Answer:** Ancient, medieval and modern.
