# Dating the past — calendars, timelines and periods — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A century is ten years.
**Answer: False** — A century is 100 years; a decade is ten.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A timeline helps you see the order of events at a glance.
**Answer: True** — That is what a timeline is for.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Renaissance Dam's start is dated 2 April 2011 in the book.
**Answer: True** — The second worked example.
