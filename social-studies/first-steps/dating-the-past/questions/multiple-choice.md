# Dating the past — calendars, timelines and periods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Islamic calendar counts its years from —
a) the birth of Christ  b) the Hijra of 622 AD  c) the founding of Rome
**Answer: b) the Hijra of 622 AD** — BH/AH years run from the Hijra.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A millennium is —
a) 10 years  b) 100 years  c) 1,000 years
**Answer: c) 1,000 years** — Decade 10, century 100, millennium 1,000.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ethiopian calendar runs — behind the Gregorian.
a) seven to eight years  b) seventy years  c) seven to eight months
**Answer: a) seven to eight years** — The book's own figure.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Chronology means —
a) drawing maps of old places  b) putting events in their true order by date  c) telling stories about kings
**Answer: b) putting events in their true order by date** — Chronology orders events by calendar.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Battle of Adwa is dated —
a) 1 March 1896  b) 2 April 2011  c) 622 AD
**Answer: a) 1 March 1896** — The book's worked example.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Periodisation labels stretches of the past as —
a) BC and AD  b) ancient, medieval, modern  c) BH and AH
**Answer: b) ancient, medieval, modern** — The three period labels.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What is periodization?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) a named stretch of the past — ancient, medieval, modern
c) dividing the past into periods as a historian's tool
**Answer: c) dividing the past into periods as a historian's tool**
