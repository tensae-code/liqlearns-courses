# Hands-on — Dating the past — calendars, timelines and periods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-calendar hunt
Find one dated thing at home — a certificate, a book's title page, an old coin or a photo with a date. Work out its year in the other two calendars as far as you can, and write all three numbers side by side.

## The classroom timeline
On a long strip of paper, mark a line from 1800 to today. Each learner adds one event card — Adwa 1896, the Dam's start 2011, and events from their own family's memory — pinned in its true place.
