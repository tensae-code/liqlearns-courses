# Hands-on — Farming and the Farmers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beerista iyo Beeraleyda (Farming and the Farmers)
Work through this part with your hands: the farming community as working life — what farmers grow (sorghum, maize, rice, cotton, beans, sugarcane, sesame, vegetables and fruit), the seasons of planting, the Crash Programme born of the Revolution to raise food production and employ youth on some 8,000 hectares, the agricultural agency that buys the crop at a set price and sells it on cheaply, the tractor agency that hires out machines and seed, and the farmers' cooperatives. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
