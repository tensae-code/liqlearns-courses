# Farming and the Farmers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Beerista iyo Beeraleyda (Farming and the Farmers)" teaches: the farming community as working life — what farmers grow (sorghum, maize, rice, cotton, beans, sugarcane, sesame, vegetables and fruit), the seasons of planting, the Crash Programme born of the Revolution to raise food production and employ youth on some 8,000 hectares, the agricultural agency that buys the crop at a set price and sells it on cheaply, the tractor agency that hires out machines and seed, and the farmers' cooperatives.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic..
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
