# Hands-on — Caring for Our School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: behaving well at school — dialogue, respect, cooperation, equality, tolerance and keeping the school's property and cleanliness. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Libya's way
Say how Libya's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
