**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Libya, Angola).**

Angola's own way (Book 2): Estudo do Meio teaches the child's world through one named child at a time (Vihemba and her extended family, Mário and his school) — family includes grandparents, uncles and cousins who come to live in the house, and the home is located by bairro, município, street and house number so the child never gets lost.

Angola's own way (Book 3): Grade 2 widens each Grade 1 topic by one step — the family gains kinship names and needs, the school gains its parts and the care of school materials, and the book closes with two short themes that are pure community life: work, and transport and communications.

Angola's own way (Book 4): a moral-and-civic book built on the child's own reflection — every unit opens with the pupil's own life (my habits, my family, my school) and only then names the rule; equality is taught straight from Angola's own Constitution (art. 23: all are equal before the Constitution and the law), and the book ends not on the state but on national reconciliation — building a better Angola, then a better world.
