# Video script — Belarus Place-Names — Toponymy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus's Grade 10 book gives geographical names their own paragraph (Theme I §6): the names of Belarus's places are studied as part of the geography of the country itself, at the end of the opening theme on position and research.

Place-names belong to the land they name: a country's names are part of how its geography is known and remembered, which is why the book places them alongside the history of geographical research and before the study of nature begins.
