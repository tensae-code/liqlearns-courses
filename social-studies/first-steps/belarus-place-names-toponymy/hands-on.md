# Hands-on — Belarus Place-Names — Toponymy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Collect place-names
Write down ten Belarusian place-names you can find — from this lesson, a map or an atlas — and say what kind of place each one names (a town, a river, a region).

## Ask about a name
Choose one name from your list and find out, or reason out, what it might tell about the place or its history; write your idea in two sentences.
