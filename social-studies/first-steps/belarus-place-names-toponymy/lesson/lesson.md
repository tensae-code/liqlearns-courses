# Belarus Place-Names — Toponymy

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the geographical names of Belarus — toponymy: how a country's own place-names are studied as part of its geography, as Belarus's Grade 10 book teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Geographical names of Belarus — the book's toponymy paragraph

Belarus's Grade 10 book gives geographical names their own paragraph (Theme I §6): the names of Belarus's places are studied as part of the geography of the country itself, at the end of the opening theme on position and research.

## Sub lesson 2 — Place-names as part of studying one's own country

Place-names belong to the land they name: a country's names are part of how its geography is known and remembered, which is why the book places them alongside the history of geographical research and before the study of nature begins.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
