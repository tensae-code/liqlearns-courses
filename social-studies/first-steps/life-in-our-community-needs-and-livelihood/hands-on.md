# Hands-on — Life in Our Community Needs and Livelihood

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: life in the community rests on its environment and livelihoods; a family's basic needs (food, clothing, shelter), what a budget is, and a simple one-day budget from a child's allowance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Philippines's way
Say how Philippines's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
