# Video script — Life in Our Community Needs and Livelihood

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Philippines Grade 2 AP2 Tagalog Unit 3 (printed p. 169), teaches: Life in the community rests on its environment and livelihoods; a family's basic needs (food, clothing, shelter), what a budget is, and a simple one-day budget from a child's allowance.

Philippines's own way: Grade 2 social studies is organised by community ("Buhay Komunidad"): work, leadership and service are taught inside the child's own barangay-scale community, with leaders drawn from every part of it (barangay captain, school principal, priest/pastor/imam, doctor, parents).
