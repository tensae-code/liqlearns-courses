# Kinds of Transport

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: transport means carry people and goods by road, rail, water and air — car, bus, tram, trolleybus, train, ship, boat, plane, helicopter — and which of them one's own settlement uses — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Врсте саобраћаја (Kinds of transport)

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed pp. 28–29), teaches: transport means carry people and goods by road, rail, water and air — car, bus, tram, trolleybus, train, ship, boat, plane, helicopter — and which of them one's own settlement uses. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Саобраћај у мом крају (Transport in my region)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 34–35), teaches: transport carries people, goods and information — passenger, freight and information transport, by land, water and air — and public transport (bus, tram, trolleybus, taxi) as part of everyday life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays. the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
