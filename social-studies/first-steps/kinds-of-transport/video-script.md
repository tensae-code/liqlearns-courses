# Video script — Kinds of Transport

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed pp. 28–29), teaches: Transport means carry people and goods by road, rail, water and air — car, bus, tram, trolleybus, train, ship, boat, plane, helicopter — and which of them one's own settlement uses.

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 34–35), teaches: Transport carries people, goods and information — passenger, freight and information transport, by land, water and air — and public transport (bus, tram, trolleybus, taxi) as part of everyday life.

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays. the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
