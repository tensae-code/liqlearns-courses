# Kinds of Transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called “Врсте саобраћаја (Kinds of transport)” is —
a) Врсте саобраћаја (Kinds of transport)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Врсте саобраћаја (Kinds of transport)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part is mainly about —
a) transport means carry people and goods by road, rail, water and air — car, bus, tram, trolleybus, train, ship, boat, plane, helicopter — and which of them one's own settlement uses  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
