# Hands-on — Kinds of Transport

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Врсте саобраћаја (Kinds of transport)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: transport means carry people and goods by road, rail, water and air — car, bus, tram, trolleybus, train, ship, boat, plane, helicopter — and which of them one's own settlement uses. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Саобраћај у мом крају (Transport in my region)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: transport carries people, goods and information — passenger, freight and information transport, by land, water and air — and public transport (bus, tram, trolleybus, taxi) as part of everyday life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
