# Hands-on — Types of Communities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what a community is and its types — village, town, city and national communities, and the smaller family, school, religious and workplace communities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
