# Hands-on — Village Trades and Handicrafts the Work People Do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Beside farming, herding and gardening, villagers practise blacksmithing, carpentry, pottery, coppersmithing, goldsmithing, tinwork, mat, carpet, kilim and shawl weaving to meet the village's needs, selling the surplus in near and far markets. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
