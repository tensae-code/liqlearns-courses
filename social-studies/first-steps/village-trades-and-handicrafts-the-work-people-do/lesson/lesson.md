## Sub lesson 1 — Village Trades and Handicrafts the Work People Do

Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 21, teaches: beside farming, herding and gardening, villagers practise blacksmithing, carpentry, pottery, coppersmithing, goldsmithing, tinwork, mat, carpet, kilim and shawl weaving to meet the village's needs, selling the surplus in near and far markets. ## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: the child's world widens in rings — social life, family, mosque, school, village, city, homeland, world, Earth — and every ring carries its duties with it: respect for elders at home, duties and order at school, reverence for the flag, obedience to traffic rules.
