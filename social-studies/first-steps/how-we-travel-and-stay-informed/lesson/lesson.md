## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way: the youngest Libro Abierto volume builds citizenship from the child's own ground — my community and its public spaces, my family and its tree, then rights and duties told through stories (José, the boy with nowhere to live) and a class "poetry of values".
