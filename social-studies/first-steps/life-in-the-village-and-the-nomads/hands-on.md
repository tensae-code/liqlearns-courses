# Hands-on — Life in the Village and the Nomads

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the village is small, its air always clean, and its people mostly know one another and are kin; from early morning they are busy with farming, gardening and animal husbandry; and a few people move from place to place in the seasons to graze their animals — these are the nomads (عشایر), who live in tents, are kin to one another, and produce meat, dairy, kilims and jajims. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
