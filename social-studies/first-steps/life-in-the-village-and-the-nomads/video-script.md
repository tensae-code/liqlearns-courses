# Video script — Life in the Village and the Nomads

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 56, teaches: the village is small, its air always clean, and its people mostly know one another and are kin; from early morning they are busy with farming, gardening and animal husbandry; and a few people move from place to place in the seasons to graze their animals — these are the nomads (عشایر), who live in tents, are kin to one another, and produce meat, dairy, kilims and jajims.

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces.
