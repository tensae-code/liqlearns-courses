# Life in the Village and the Nomads — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the village is small, its air always clean, and its people mostly know one another and are kin; from early morning they are busy with farming, gardening and animal husbandry; and a few people move from place to place in the seasons to graze their animals — these are the nomads (عشایر), who live in tents, are kin to one another, and produce meat, dairy, kilims and jajims.
**Answer: True**
