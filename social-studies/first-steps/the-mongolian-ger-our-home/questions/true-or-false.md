# The Mongolian Ger Our Home — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the ger, Mongolia's home since ancient times — its parts (тооно roof ring, унь poles, хана lattice wall, хаалга door, багана pillars) and sizes, how a ger is raised, and how its inside is ordered by direction around the hearth (голомт).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
