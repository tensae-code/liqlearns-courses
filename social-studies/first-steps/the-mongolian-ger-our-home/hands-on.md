# Hands-on — The Mongolian Ger Our Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 8 Монгол гэр (The Mongolian ger)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The ger, Mongolia's home since ancient times — its parts (тооно roof ring, унь poles, хана lattice wall, хаалга door, багана pillars) and sizes, how a ger is raised, and how its inside is ordered by direction around the hearth (голомт). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
