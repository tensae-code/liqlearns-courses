## Sub lesson 1 — Look after school and class property

Vietnam Grade 1 Đạo đức 1, printed pp. 34–35, teaches: Shared property (desks, taps, lights, the flower garden) belongs to everyone, so everyone guards it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: Đạo đức (Ethics) is Vietnam's standalone primary moral-education subject; every lesson runs the same four moves — a song to open, picture stories to explore, practice pictures to judge, a role-play to apply — and closes with a two-line rhyme that states the duty; the family is taught three-generational (ông bà – cha mẹ – anh chị em) and school rules are taught as the child's first shared rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
