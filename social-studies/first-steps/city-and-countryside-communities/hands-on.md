# Hands-on — City and Countryside Communities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unidad 2 La vida en mi comunidad
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The urban community (the city, where Lía lives) and the rural community (fields and farm zones) — how each looks and how people live in them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unidad 3 ¡Venimos del campo y la ciudad!
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The ways of life of rural and urban dwellers — work, institutions, streets, transport and services in the city against life in the campo, compared in a pupil model. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Dominican Republic's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
