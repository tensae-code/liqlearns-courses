# Video script — City and Countryside Communities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dominican Republic — Ciencias Sociales 2° Primaria (printed p. 30), teaches: The urban community (the city, where Lía lives) and the rural community (fields and farm zones) — how each looks and how people live in them.

Dominican Republic — Ciencias Sociales 3° Primaria (printed p. 48), teaches: The ways of life of rural and urban dwellers — work, institutions, streets, transport and services in the city against life in the campo, compared in a pupil model.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 7): the youngest Libro Abierto volume builds citizenship from the child's own ground — my community and its public spaces, my family and its tree, then rights and duties told through stories (José, the boy with nowhere to live) and a class "poetry of values".

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 8): Grade 3 ends the book with a whole annex most countries never give 8-year-olds — the Constitución Dominicana Infantil, the country's constitution rewritten for children, grouped as civil, social and cultural rights; and its democracy unit starts from the school's own consejo de curso election.
