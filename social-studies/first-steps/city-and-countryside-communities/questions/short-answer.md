# City and Countryside Communities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Unidad 2 La vida en mi comunidad — The urban community (the city, where Lía lives) and the rural community (fields and farm zones) — how each looks and how people live in them (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Unidad 3 ¡Venimos del campo y la ciudad! — The ways of life of rural and urban dwellers — work, institutions, streets, transport and services in the city against life in the campo, compared in a pupil model (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way — the youngest Libro Abierto volume builds citizenship from the child's own ground — my community and its public spaces, my family and its tree, then rights and duties told through stories (José, the boy with nowhere to live) and a class "poetry of values". Grade 3 ends the book with a whole annex most countries never give 8-year-olds — the Constitución Dominicana Infantil, the country's constitution rewritten for children, grouped as civil, social and cultural rights; and its democracy unit starts from the school's own consejo de curso election. (any close wording).**
