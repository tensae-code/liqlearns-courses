# Video script — Adults Work and Children Learn

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

El Salvador Grade 1 Estudios Sociales 1 (student book, Spanish), printed p. 32, teaches: The social landscape is built by people's work; adults work to meet our needs, and work is an activity for adults, not for children, whose main activity is learning.

El Salvador Grade 2 Estudios Sociales 2 (student book, Spanish), printed p. 27, teaches: Adults' work builds the social landscape and meets our needs; work belongs to adults and learning to children (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).

El Salvador Grade 3 Estudios Sociales 3 (student book, Spanish), printed p. 32, teaches: Adults' work and the social landscape it builds (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).

El Salvador Grade 4 Estudios Sociales 4 (student book, Spanish), printed p. 92, teaches: Work is the main activity people do to earn a salary and meet basic needs; employment makes goods or services, unemployment is a social problem, and Article 37 of El Salvador's Constitution protects work (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).

El Salvador Grade 5 Estudios Sociales 5 (student book, Spanish), printed p. 78, teaches: The women and men who work in Central America and the work they do (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).

El Salvador Grade 6 Estudios Sociales 6 (student book, Spanish), printed p. 102, teaches: Work in the Americas (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).

El Salvador's own way (El Salvador Grade 1 Estudios Sociales 1): the Cipotas y Cipotes series teaches society from the child's own ground — adults' work in the local landscape, the family, the school and its institutions, then living together with rights and duties.

El Salvador's own way (El Salvador Grade 2 Estudios Sociales 2): Grade 2 widens Grade 1's frame to the whole society — "¡La sociedad somos todos!" — individuals, families, schools, public and private institutions and communities together form Salvadoran society, and its main institutions are named one by one.

El Salvador's own way (El Salvador Grade 3 Estudios Sociales 3): the same six-unit Cipotas y Cipotes frame deepens — society now "organises itself" (La sociedad se organiza) and the child's country closes the book (Mi país El Salvador).

El Salvador's own way (El Salvador Grade 4 Estudios Sociales 4): Grade 4 turns to the whole country — its media, its work, its rights — and closes with El Salvador's own internet story: first connection in December 1995 at ANTEL, first web pages at the UCA and Don Bosco universities.

El Salvador's own way (El Salvador Grade 5 Estudios Sociales 5): Grade 5 widens the frame from El Salvador to Central America — its workers, its families and its values are taught as a shared regional life.

El Salvador's own way (El Salvador Grade 6 Estudios Sociales 6): Grade 6 reaches the Americas, and its civics peak is El Salvador's own government-ethics law — public servants, their principles, and the 2006 Ley de Ética Gubernamental against corruption.
