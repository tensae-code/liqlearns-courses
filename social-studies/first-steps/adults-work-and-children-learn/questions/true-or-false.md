# Adults Work and Children Learn — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the social landscape is built by people's work; adults work to meet our needs, and work is an activity for adults, not for children, whose main activity is learning.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: adults' work builds the social landscape and meets our needs; work belongs to adults and learning to children (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: adults' work and the social landscape it builds (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: work is the main activity people do to earn a salary and meet basic needs; employment makes goods or services, unemployment is a social problem, and Article 37 of El Salvador's Constitution protects work (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's fifth part teaches: the women and men who work in Central America and the work they do (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson's sixth part teaches: work in the Americas (NEW lesson proposed in Book 1).
**Answer: True**
