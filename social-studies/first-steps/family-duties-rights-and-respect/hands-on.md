# Hands-on — Family Duties Rights and Respect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 2 ¿Cómo debe ser la familia?
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 1 CS_Fichas, printed p. 10, teaches: every family member has the right to be accepted, valued and respected in their differences, and duties/tasks that keep harmony at home; no physical or verbal abuse inside the family. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: Grade 1 worksheet "fichas" built around Alejo and Mariana; social studies starts from the child's own body, name and family, widening to neighbours and region; care is taught as a right and as men's and boys' work too, and no family type is ranked above another. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
