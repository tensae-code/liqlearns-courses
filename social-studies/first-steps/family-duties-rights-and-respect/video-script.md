# Video script — Family Duties Rights and Respect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 1 CS_Fichas, printed p. 10, teaches: every family member has the right to be accepted, valued and respected in their differences, and duties/tasks that keep harmony at home; no physical or verbal abuse inside the family.

Colombia's own way: Grade 1 worksheet "fichas" built around Alejo and Mariana; social studies starts from the child's own body, name and family, widening to neighbours and region; care is taught as a right and as men's and boys' work too, and no family type is ranked above another.
