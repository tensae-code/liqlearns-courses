## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 2 ¿Cómo debe ser la familia?

Colombia Grade 1 CS_Fichas, printed p. 10, teaches: every family member has the right to be accepted, valued and respected in their differences, and duties/tasks that keep harmony at home; no physical or verbal abuse inside the family. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: Grade 1 worksheet "fichas" built around Alejo and Mariana; social studies starts from the child's own body, name and family, widening to neighbours and region; care is taught as a right and as men's and boys' work too, and no family type is ranked above another. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
