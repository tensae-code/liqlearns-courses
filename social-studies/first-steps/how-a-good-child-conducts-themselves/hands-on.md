# Hands-on — How a Good Child Conducts Themselves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 3 "Judging a boy's and a girl's conduct" (Хөвгүүн, охины явдал мөрийг шинжихүй)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A person's worth is read from their conduct (явдал мөр) — their deeds, words and bearing; the good boy and good girl of the old teaching verses (heed the father's teaching, love learning, be compassionate) against their opposites, and the proverbs that sum a person up by what they do, not what they say. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
