# Video script — How a Good Child Conducts Themselves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 10, teaches: A person's worth is read from their conduct (явдал мөр) — their deeds, words and bearing; the good boy and good girl of the old teaching verses (heed the father's teaching, love learning, be compassionate) against their opposites, and the proverbs that sum a person up by what they do, not what they say.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
