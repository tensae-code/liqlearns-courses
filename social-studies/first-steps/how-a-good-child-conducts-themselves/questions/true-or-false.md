# How a Good Child Conducts Themselves — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: a person's worth is read from their conduct (явдал мөр) — their deeds, words and bearing; the good boy and good girl of the old teaching verses (heed the father's teaching, love learning, be compassionate) against their opposites, and the proverbs that sum a person up by what they do, not what they say.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
