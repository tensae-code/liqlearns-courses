# Hands-on — Traffic Signs and Road Safety for Pedestrians

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: keeping my body safe on the road — traffic signals and signs, crossing carefully, the seat belt, and the signs that protect pedestrians. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
