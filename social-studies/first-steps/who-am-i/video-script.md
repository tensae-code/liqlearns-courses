# Video script — Who Am I

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eritrea Grade 6 Citizenship Education (printed p. 1), teaches: Knowing yourself — personal timeline, likes, abilities and ambitions as the start of responsible citizenship.

Eritrea's own way: a standalone Citizenship Education subject built entirely from Eritrean stories, poems and dialogues — responsibility is taught through a shop-queue drama and a family Tandoor day, the state through the 1993 referendum story, and patriotism as practical service (keeping the school clean, planting trees at the new clinic) that even a child can do.
