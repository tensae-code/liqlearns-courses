## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the book's own teaching way

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 7 G7 CS Patria y Ciudadanía): a Civics book built on widening belonging — house, block (cuadra), community, homeland, planet — where the Geography enters as lived space: the Carta del Barrio, in which a community writes down its own geographic space (its social, cultural, historical, economic and environmental character), and a full postal address that nests house → block → parish → municipality → state → Venezuela → South America → Earth → Solar System → Milky Way.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
