# Hands-on — My Block My Community Lived Space

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Mi casa, nuestra cuadra, nuestra comunidad: lived space that widens from house to block to community; La localidad: un espacio para convivir y aprender. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
