# Hands-on — Relations with Neighbours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 3 Lesson 2 My Neighbours
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: who a neighbour is (the people whose homes are near mine), what I owe them — kindness, help, not disturbing them — and the reward of good neighbourliness. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Yemen's way
