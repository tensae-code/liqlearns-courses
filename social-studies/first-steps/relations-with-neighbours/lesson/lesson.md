## Sub lesson 2 — Yemen's way — the book's own teaching way

Yemen's own way: the combined Social Education book starts from the child's own family, mosque and school, then widens to neighbours and the neighbourhood/village — the remaining strands are taught as lived duties (kindness to parents, care of the home, good neighbourliness, school order) before any geography appears. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
