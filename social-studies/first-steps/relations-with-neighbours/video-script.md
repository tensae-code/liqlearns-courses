# Video script — Relations with Neighbours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yemen Grade 3 G3 Social Studies, printed p. 50, teaches: who a neighbour is (the people whose homes are near mine), what I owe them — kindness, help, not disturbing them — and the reward of good neighbourliness.

Yemen's own way: the combined Social Education book starts from the child's own family, mosque and school, then widens to neighbours and the neighbourhood/village — the remaining strands are taught as lived duties (kindness to parents, care of the home, good neighbourliness, school order) before any geography appears.
