## Sub lesson 1 — Groups and Our Duties in Them

Iran Social Studies Grade 10 (special needs, Kardanesh) (student book, Persian), printed p. 25, teaches: the groups around us, pictured and named — choir, theatre, sports, work, class and friendship groups, and outside school the local, helping, cultural and mountaineering groups; every group is formed to do a task; each member has a defined duty, and by doing the duties the group reaches its goal; the group's leader assigns the members' duties; and what being a member requires — follow the leader's instructions, do the work in order, consult the other members, do one's own work with interest and care, and solve problems with the others' help. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs secondary book is a citizen's primer in the plainest form — needs first (things, then love and company), then the circles that meet them: family, groups, institutions — and at the top, government and the Majlis, explained through a ship and its captain and a school and its rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
