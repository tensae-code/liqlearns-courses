# Hands-on — Groups and Our Duties in Them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the groups around us, pictured and named — choir, theatre, sports, work, class and friendship groups, and outside school the local, helping, cultural and mountaineering groups; every group is formed to do a task; each member has a defined duty, and by doing the duties the group reaches its goal; the group's leader assigns the members' duties; and what being a member requires — follow the leader's instructions, do the work in order, consult the other members, do one's own work with interest and care, and solve problems with the others' help. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
