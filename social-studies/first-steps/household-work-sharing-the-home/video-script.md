# Video script — Household Work Sharing the Home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal Grade 1 My Social Studies and Creative Arts (printed p. 16), teaches: The work of the household and everyone's share in it.

Nepal Grade 2 My Social Studies and Creative Arts (printed p. 4), teaches: The family's work and each member's part.

Nepal's own way: from Grade 1 the subject is values-first — the youngest child's social studies is seniors respected, juniors loved, the needy and disabled helped, and duties at home and school, all taught through pictures the teacher talks through. Grade 2's civic sense is property and patience — our common property, protecting public property, and waiting one's turn — beside neighbourliness taught through festivals and living styles.
