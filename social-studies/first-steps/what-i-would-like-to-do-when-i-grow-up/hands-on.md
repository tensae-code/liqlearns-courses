# Hands-on — What I Would Like to Do When I Grow Up

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 3 What I would like to do
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: each child names the job they want as an adult and says why, with songs about the jobs people do. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Sudan's way
Say how South Sudan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
