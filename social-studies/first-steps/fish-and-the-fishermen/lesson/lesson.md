## Sub lesson 1 — Kalluunka iyo Kalluumeysata (Fish and the Fishermen)

Somalia Grade 3 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 22), teaches: the fishing community — a coast over 3,000 km on the Indian Ocean and Red Sea, the Jubba and Shabelle rivers, the kinds of sea fish, how coastal people love fish while inland people, some of whom have never eaten it, are taught to — protein for the body and a wealth the nation under-uses. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 3): Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
