# Hands-on — Fish and the Fishermen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Kalluunka iyo Kalluumeysata (Fish and the Fishermen)
Work through this part with your hands: the fishing community — a coast over 3,000 km on the Indian Ocean and Red Sea, the Jubba and Shabelle rivers, the kinds of sea fish, how coastal people love fish while inland people, some of whom have never eaten it, are taught to — protein for the body and a wealth the nation under-uses. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
