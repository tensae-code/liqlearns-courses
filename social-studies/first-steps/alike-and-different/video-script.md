# Video script — Alike and Different

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan Grade 1 SS G1 T1, printed p. 18, teaches: Children are alike in some things and different in others, and difference is normal and respected.

Jordan's own way: citizenship starts at age six with the self, the family and the neighbours, then the homeland is introduced through its flag and its king, and rights are taught as the child's own rights to play and to learn.
