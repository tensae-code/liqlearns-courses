# Hands-on — Alike and Different

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Alike and different
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Children are alike in some things and different in others, and difference is normal and respected. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Jordan's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Jordan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
