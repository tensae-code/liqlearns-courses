# Hands-on — Cooperation in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a class breakfast and the family's housework show that work shared is work lightened — the family's task table divides cooking, cleaning, shopping and dishes among mother, father and children. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
