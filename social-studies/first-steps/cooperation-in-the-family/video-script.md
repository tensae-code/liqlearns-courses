# Video script — Cooperation in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (student book, Persian), printed p. 30, teaches: a class breakfast and the family's housework show that work shared is work lightened — the family's task table divides cooking, cleaning, shopping and dishes among mother, father and children.

Iran's own way: the whole primary course works outward from the child — me, my family, my house, my school, from home to school — and moral teaching (gratitude, cooperation, not mocking others) is carried by family stories, one of them built around a grandmother and the Qur'anic command to speak gently to aged parents.
