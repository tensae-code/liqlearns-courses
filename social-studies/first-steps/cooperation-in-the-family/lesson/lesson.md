## Sub lesson 1 — Cooperation in the Family

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (student book, Persian), printed p. 30, teaches: a class breakfast and the family's housework show that work shared is work lightened — the family's task table divides cooking, cleaning, shopping and dishes among mother, father and children. ## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the whole primary course works outward from the child — me, my family, my house, my school, from home to school — and moral teaching (gratitude, cooperation, not mocking others) is carried by family stories, one of them built around a grandmother and the Qur'anic command to speak gently to aged parents.
