# I Have a Role Everyone in the Family Has a Part — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Every member of the family has a role — mother prepares lunch, father repairs the bicycle, sister tidies the games, brother returns the stories to the library — and the role you dream of playing in building your homeland.
