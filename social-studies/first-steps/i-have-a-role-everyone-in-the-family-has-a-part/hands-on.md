# Hands-on — I Have a Role Everyone in the Family Has a Part

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every member of the family has a role — mother prepares lunch, father repairs the bicycle, sister tidies the games, brother returns the stories to the library — and the role you dream of playing in building your homeland. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
