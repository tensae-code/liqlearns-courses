# Comparing states: how kingdoms hold together — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'setting states side by side in one vocabulary' is ______.
**Answer: comparison**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the first sign where a state's weak part begins to fail' is ______.
**Answer: strain**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'to grant legitimacy — the people's part in every state' is ______.
**Answer: accept**
