# Lesson plan — My Role Model

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** First Steps · **Lesson:** My Role Model
**Place in the curriculum:** First Steps (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Iraq, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A role model (qudwa) is the one whose path I follow: be a good role model, and let your morals be what people talk about; in the park game "What do you want to be when you grow up?", Muhammad will finish his studies and become a soldier defending his homeland and his people, Lana an engineer building her homeland and making it beautiful, Yusuf a farmer planting his homeland's land and making it beautiful, and Sara a nurse like her mother, an angel of mercy staying up for the sick and spreading hope in their hearts; the professions are many — soldier, pilot, farmer, carpenter, writer, artist, engineer, dentist — they are human professions, and all of us build the homelands; and every profession has its importance — as Iraq's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
