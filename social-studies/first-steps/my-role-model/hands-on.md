# Hands-on — My Role Model

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A role model (qudwa) is the one whose path I follow: be a good role model, and let your morals be what people talk about; in the park game "What do you want to be when you grow up?", Muhammad will finish his studies and become a soldier defending his homeland and his people, Lana an engineer building her homeland and making it beautiful, Yusuf a farmer planting his homeland's land and making it beautiful, and Sara a nurse like her mother, an angel of mercy staying up for the sick and spreading hope in their hearts; the professions are many — soldier, pilot, farmer, carpenter, writer, artist, engineer, dentist — they are human professions, and all of us build the homelands; and every profession has its importance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
