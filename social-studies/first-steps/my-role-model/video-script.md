# Video script — My Role Model

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 1 Moral Education (printed pp. 66–71), teaches: A role model (qudwa) is the one whose path I follow: be a good role model, and let your morals be what people talk about; in the park game "What do you want to be when you grow up?", Muhammad will finish his studies and become a soldier defending his homeland and his people, Lana an engineer building her homeland and making it beautiful, Yusuf a farmer planting his homeland's land and making it beautiful, and Sara a nurse like her mother, an angel of mercy staying up for the sick and spreading hope in their hearts; the professions are many — soldier, pilot, farmer, carpenter, writer, artist, engineer, dentist — they are human professions, and all of us build the homelands; and every profession has its importance.

Iraq's own way: moral education is a separate subject from Grade 1, taught entirely as stories about five named children (Muhammad, Yusuf, Zahraa, Lana, Sara) whose small school and family incidents carry each value; every lesson ends in the child's own voice — "What I learned" — and the second unit's family tree is drawn with the father's help, branch by branch, until the big family shades them all.
