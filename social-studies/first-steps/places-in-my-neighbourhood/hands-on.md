# Hands-on — Places in My Neighbourhood

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: look carefully around you on the way home and write down your neighbourhood's places — Sara's house is on Danesh Street: a mosque at the start of the street, a beautiful park on its left, a big shop opposite her house and the neighbourhood bakery beside it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
