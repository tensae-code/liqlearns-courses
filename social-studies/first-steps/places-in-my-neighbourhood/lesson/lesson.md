## Sub lesson 1 — Places in My Neighbourhood

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 66, teaches: look carefully around you on the way home and write down your neighbourhood's places — Sara's house is on Danesh Street: a mosque at the start of the street, a beautiful park on its left, a big shop opposite her house and the neighbourhood bakery beside it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
