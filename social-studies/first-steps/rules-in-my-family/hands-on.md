# Hands-on — Rules in My Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every house has rules that say what must and must not be done — shoes off indoors, no ball games in the room, bed on time, quiet while others pray, rest or study, things put back in their place. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
