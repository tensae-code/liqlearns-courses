# Video script — What I Need and What I Want

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 18, teaches: before buying anything, think whether you need it — Saman's mother does not buy the beautiful bag because the bag at home is sound, and buys the coloured pencils he truly needs instead; and caring for things — covering notebooks and cleaning shoes are right, wasting fruit and over-sharpening pencils are wrong.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
