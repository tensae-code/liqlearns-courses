# Hands-on — What I Need and What I Want

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: before buying anything, think whether you need it — Saman's mother does not buy the beautiful bag because the bag at home is sound, and buys the coloured pencils he truly needs instead; and caring for things — covering notebooks and cleaning shoes are right, wasting fruit and over-sharpening pencils are wrong. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
