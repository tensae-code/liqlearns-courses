# Hands-on — Our Material Needs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what does a human being need to live? — the basic material needs (نیازهای مادی): food, clothing and housing; and where their raw materials come from — food from farming, gardening and animal husbandry; housing built from soil, stone and metal; clothing made from leather, wool and cotton; some materials must be worked before use — cotton first becomes thread, then cloth. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
