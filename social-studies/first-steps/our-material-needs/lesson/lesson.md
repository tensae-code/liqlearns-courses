# Our Material Needs

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what does a human being need to live? — the basic material needs (نیازهای مادی): food, clothing and housing; and where their raw materials come from — food from farming, gardening and animal husbandry; housing built from soil, stone and metal; clothing made from leather, wool and cotton; some materials must be worked before use — cotton first becomes thread, then cloth — as Iran's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Our Material Needs

Iran Social Studies Grade 10 (special needs, Kardanesh) (student book, Persian), printed p. 9, teaches: what does a human being need to live? — the basic material needs (نیازهای مادی): food, clothing and housing; and where their raw materials come from — food from farming, gardening and animal husbandry; housing built from soil, stone and metal; clothing made from leather, wool and cotton; some materials must be worked before use — cotton first becomes thread, then cloth. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs secondary book is a citizen's primer in the plainest form — needs first (things, then love and company), then the circles that meet them: family, groups, institutions — and at the top, government and the Majlis, explained through a ship and its captain and a school and its rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
