# Our School Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Members of the school family — the members of the school make up one family which we call the school family — the Head Teacher, other teachers, the P.T.A., ancillary staff, the Prefect Body, the Student Council and the students; our school is like a second home, and the process of education which begins in the home continues at school; like a family, each school has an organisational structure, with the Headteacher as head or leader of the school family, assisted by a Deputy Headteacher and senior teachers, all answerable for the smooth and proper running of the school (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The school — an extension of the family — we belong to our home as well as to our school, and in some ways our school is like our home: just as we learn to obey our parents at home, so we obey our teachers at school; the Headteacher plans activities and sets goals with the teachers, as parents set family goals at home; at school we are helped and trained by our teachers to be good members of the school family and to grow up to earn a decent livelihood, and every member of the school family has a special part to play so that success may be achieved (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
