# Our School Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Members of the school family  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Members of the school family**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) the members of the school make up one family which we call the school family — the Head Teacher, other teachers, the P.T.A., ancillary staff, the Prefect Body, the Student Council and the students; our school is like a second home, and the process of education which begins in the home continues at school; like a family, each school has an organisational structure, with the Headteacher as head or leader of the school family, assisted by a Deputy Headteacher and senior teachers, all answerable for the smooth and proper running of the school  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) the members of the school make up one family which we call the school family — the Head Teacher, other teachers, the P.T.A., ancillary staff, the Prefect Body, the Student Council and the students; our school is like a second home, and the process of education which begins in the home continues at school; like a family, each school has an organisational structure, with the Headteacher as head or leader of the school family, assisted by a Deputy Headteacher and senior teachers, all answerable for the smooth and proper running of the school**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) The school — an extension of the family  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) The school — an extension of the family**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson is mainly about —

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this lesson is —
a) Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
