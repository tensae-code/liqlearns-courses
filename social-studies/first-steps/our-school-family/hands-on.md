# Hands-on — Our School Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The members of the school make up one family which we call the school family — the Head Teacher, other teachers, the P.T.A., ancillary staff, the Prefect Body, the Student Council and the students; our school is like a second home, and the process of education which begins in the home continues at school; like a family, each school has an organisational structure, with the Headteacher as head or leader of the school family, assisted by a Deputy Headteacher and senior teachers, all answerable for the smooth and proper running of the school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: We belong to our home as well as to our school, and in some ways our school is like our home: just as we learn to obey our parents at home, so we obey our teachers at school; the Headteacher plans activities and sets goals with the teachers, as parents set family goals at home; at school we are helped and trained by our teachers to be good members of the school family and to grow up to earn a decent livelihood, and every member of the school family has a special part to play so that success may be achieved. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
