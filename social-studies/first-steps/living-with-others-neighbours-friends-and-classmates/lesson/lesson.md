## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 2 A convivência com outras pessoas

Angola Estudo do Meio G1 (printed p. 24), teaches: Living alongside others — neighbours, friends and classmates are respected, never insulted or called names, and other people's things are not touched without permission. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Angola's way — the book's own teaching way

Angola's own way: Estudo do Meio teaches the child's world through one named child at a time (Vihemba and her extended family, Mário and his school) — family includes grandparents, uncles and cousins who come to live in the house, and the home is located by bairro, município, street and house number so the child never gets lost. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
