# Video script — Living With Others Neighbours Friends And Classmates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Angola Estudo do Meio G1 (printed p. 24), teaches: Living alongside others — neighbours, friends and classmates are respected, never insulted or called names, and other people's things are not touched without permission.

Angola's own way: Estudo do Meio teaches the child's world through one named child at a time (Vihemba and her extended family, Mário and his school) — family includes grandparents, uncles and cousins who come to live in the house, and the home is located by bairro, município, street and house number so the child never gets lost.
