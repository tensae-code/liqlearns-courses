# Video script — Caring for Each Other in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 1 CS_Fichas, printed p. 12, teaches: care is a right and a need for everyone's development; men and boys also care and may show affection; children draw how they are cared for and how they care for themselves.

Colombia's own way: Grade 1 worksheet "fichas" built around Alejo and Mariana; social studies starts from the child's own body, name and family, widening to neighbours and region; care is taught as a right and as men's and boys' work too, and no family type is ranked above another.
