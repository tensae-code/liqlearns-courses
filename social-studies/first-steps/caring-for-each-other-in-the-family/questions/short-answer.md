# Caring for Each Other in the Family — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guía 2 ¿Qué es el cuidado? — Colombia Grade 1 CS_Fichas, printed p. 12, teaches: care is a right and a need for everyone's development; men and boys also care and may show affection; children draw how they are cared for and how they care for themselves. (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Colombia's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way — Colombia's own way: Grade 1 worksheet "fichas" built around Alejo and Mariana; social studies starts from the child's own body, name and family, widening to neighbours and region; care is taught as a right and as men's and boys' work too, and no family type is ranked above another. (any close wording).**
