# Hands-on — Caring for Each Other in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 2 ¿Qué es el cuidado?
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 1 CS_Fichas, printed p. 12, teaches: care is a right and a need for everyone's development; men and boys also care and may show affection; children draw how they are cared for and how they care for themselves. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: Grade 1 worksheet "fichas" built around Alejo and Mariana; social studies starts from the child's own body, name and family, widening to neighbours and region; care is taught as a right and as men's and boys' work too, and no family type is ranked above another. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
