# Me Who I Am — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Introducing yourself — name, age, governorate, where you live and what you love — and filling in your own identity card (name, gender, age).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Describing yourself and guessing classmates from descriptions — hair, eyes, skin and temperament — then filling your own personal card: name, age, gender, skin, eye and hair colour, and the personal traits that mark you out (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: The whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free)..
**Answer: True**
