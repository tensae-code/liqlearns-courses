# Me Who I Am — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Me Who I Am 1 — introducing yourself — name, age, governorate, where you live and what you love — and filling in your own identity card (name, gender, age) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Me Who I Am 2 — describing yourself and guessing classmates from descriptions — hair, eyes, skin and temperament — then filling your own personal card: name, age, gender, skin, eye and hair colour, and the personal traits that mark you out (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1) (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Syria's way — the books' own teaching way — the whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free). (any close wording).**
