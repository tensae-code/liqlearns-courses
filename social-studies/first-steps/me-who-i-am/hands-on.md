# Hands-on — Me Who I Am

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: introducing yourself — name, age, governorate, where you live and what you love — and filling in your own identity card (name, gender, age). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
