# Video script — What a Community is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eritrea Grade 4 ER G4 Social Studies (printed p. 85), teaches: A community is the people who live together in one place, and its shared needs — a school and playing fields for the children, services and cleanliness, peace and security, and care for the area's trees and plants — worked for together.

Eritrea's own way: the primary book builds the whole idea of community from the child's own neighbourhood — what a community is, how one forms around a factory or a railway, how Eritrea's nine peoples live together in one, what the local administration does for it, how village and town feed each other, and how a village changes — Dehab's village, ten kilometres from Asmara, is the running example.
