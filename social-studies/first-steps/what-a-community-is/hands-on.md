# Hands-on — What a Community is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a community is the people who live together in one place, and its shared needs — a school and playing fields for the children, services and cleanliness, peace and security, and care for the area's trees and plants — worked for together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
