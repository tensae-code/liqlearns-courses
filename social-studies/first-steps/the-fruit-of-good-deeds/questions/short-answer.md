# The Fruit of Good Deeds — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 3 "The benefit of good deeds" (Сайн үйлийн ач тус) — A good deed and its fruit — love (хайр) and a compassionate heart (нинжин сэтгэл); Bilguun, who told his tale honestly and admitted his own fault in it; the girl who warmed the freezing poor old woman and was herself warmed; the boy who saved the drowning dog; and the tale of the Three Gold Coins, in which the youngest brother spends his coins saving a pheasant, a golden fish and an otter — and is saved by them in return; good deeds are sorted into deeds of body, thought and speech (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for (any close wording).**
