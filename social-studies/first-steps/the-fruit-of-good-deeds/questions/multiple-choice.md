# The Fruit of Good Deeds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 3 "The benefit of good deeds" (Сайн үйлийн ач тус)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 3 "The benefit of good deeds" (Сайн үйлийн ач тус)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) A good deed and its fruit — love (хайр) and a compassionate heart (нинжин сэтгэл); Bilguun, who told his tale honestly and admitted his own fault in it; the girl who warmed the freezing poor old woman and was herself warmed; the boy who saved the drowning dog; and the tale of the Three Gold Coins, in which the youngest brother spends his coins saving a pheasant, a golden fish and an otter — and is saved by them in return; good deeds are sorted into deeds of body, thought and speech  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) A good deed and its fruit — love (хайр) and a compassionate heart (нинжин сэтгэл); Bilguun, who told his tale honestly and admitted his own fault in it; the girl who warmed the freezing poor old woman and was herself warmed; the boy who saved the drowning dog; and the tale of the Three Gold Coins, in which the youngest brother spends his coins saving a pheasant, a golden fish and an otter — and is saved by them in return; good deeds are sorted into deeds of body, thought and speech**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Mongolia's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.  b) Mongolia's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) Mongolia's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.**
