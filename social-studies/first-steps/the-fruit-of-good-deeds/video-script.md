# Video script — The Fruit of Good Deeds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 11, teaches: A good deed and its fruit — love (хайр) and a compassionate heart (нинжин сэтгэл); Bilguun, who told his tale honestly and admitted his own fault in it; the girl who warmed the freezing poor old woman and was herself warmed; the boy who saved the drowning dog; and the tale of the Three Gold Coins, in which the youngest brother spends his coins saving a pheasant, a golden fish and an otter — and is saved by them in return; good deeds are sorted into deeds of body, thought and speech.

Mongolia's own way (Link book L1): Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for.
