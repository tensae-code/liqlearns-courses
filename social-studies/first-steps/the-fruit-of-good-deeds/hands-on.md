# Hands-on — The Fruit of Good Deeds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 3 "The benefit of good deeds" (Сайн үйлийн ач тус)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A good deed and its fruit — love (хайр) and a compassionate heart (нинжин сэтгэл); Bilguun, who told his tale honestly and admitted his own fault in it; the girl who warmed the freezing poor old woman and was herself warmed; the boy who saved the drowning dog; and the tale of the Three Gold Coins, in which the youngest brother spends his coins saving a pheasant, a golden fish and an otter — and is saved by them in return; good deeds are sorted into deeds of body, thought and speech. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
