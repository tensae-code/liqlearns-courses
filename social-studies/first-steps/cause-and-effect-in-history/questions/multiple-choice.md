# Cause and effect in history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cause of an event is —
a) The event itself  b) What led to the event, before it happened  c) What followed the event, after it happened
**Answer: b) What led to the event, before it happened** — the way the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A result of an event is —
a) What followed from the event  b) What happened long before the event  c) The place where the event happened
**Answer: a) What followed from the event** — as the book teaches it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Saudi Arabia Grade 4 book, children practise cause and effect by —
a) Memorising dates only  b) Choosing examples and sorting them into causes and results  c) Copying a timeline without reading it
**Answer: b) Choosing examples and sorting them into causes and results** — the practice the book sets.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Nothing in the past — in this lesson's words —
a) Needs a cause  b) Can be explained without results  c) "Just happened"
**Answer: c) "Just happened"** — the lesson's story beat.
