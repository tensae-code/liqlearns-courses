# Cause and effect in history — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words, what is the difference between a cause and a result?
**Answer: A cause is what led to an event, before it happened; a result is what followed from the event, after it happened (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one event, one cause that led to it, and one result that followed from it.
**Answer: Any sensible chain, e.g. rain (cause) — the river rose (event) — the fields were watered (result) (any close wording).**
