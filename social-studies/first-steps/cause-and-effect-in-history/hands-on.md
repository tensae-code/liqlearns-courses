# Hands-on — Cause and effect in history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two piles
Take six cards. On three, write simple events — a plant grew tall, a game was won, a road was built. Leave the other three blank. For each event, write one cause on a blank card and lay it to the left of the event; say one result out loud. Then read each chain in order: cause, event, result.

## Push the domino
Stand three books on edge like dominoes, a finger's width apart. Push the first and watch the chain. Explain, using the books, which book is the cause, which is the event, and which is the result — then add a fourth book and say what new result your chain now has.

## Choose and sort, like the book
With your class or family, choose one event everyone knows. Each person names one thing that led to it and one thing that followed from it. Sort the answers into two piles — causes and results — and talk kindly about any answer that could belong in both.
