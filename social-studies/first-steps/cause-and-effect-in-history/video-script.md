# Video script — Cause and effect in history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Saudi Arabia Grade 4 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nothing in the past "just happened." That is the whole lesson, and it takes only two questions to learn it.

First question: what led to it? Every historical event has causes — things that came before it and pushed it into happening. Find the causes, and you know why the event happened at all.

Second question: what came of it? Every historical event has results — things that followed from it, that it pushed in turn.

Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 book teaches its children to practise exactly this: choose an example, and sort it — cause on one side, result on the other. Do that to any story in history, and the story becomes a chain you can follow.
