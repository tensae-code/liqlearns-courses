# Hands-on — Learning Together the Class as a Collective

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the class as a collective — a group joined by common work and goals — with the rules of pair and group work, class self-government and an elected school council, and a collective's strength in its unity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kazakhstan's way
Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
