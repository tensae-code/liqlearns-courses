# Hands-on — I Know My Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The family tree: the small family (father, mother, brothers and sisters) is drawn first, then the branches grow — the grandfathers and grandmothers, the uncles and aunts — for the family has branches and twigs that increase as its members increase, and the big family's tree is the more beautiful the more branches and leaves it has, and it shades us from storms and hardships; the grandfathers and grandmothers are of our family — they founded our big family — so we respect them, visit them regularly, and meet all their needs with love; and the number of a family's members differs from one family to another. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
