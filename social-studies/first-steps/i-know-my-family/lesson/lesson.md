# I Know My Family

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the family tree: the small family (father, mother, brothers and sisters) is drawn first, then the branches grow — the grandfathers and grandmothers, the uncles and aunts — for the family has branches and twigs that increase as its members increase, and the big family's tree is the more beautiful the more branches and leaves it has, and it shades us from storms and hardships; the grandfathers and grandmothers are of our family — they founded our big family — so we respect them, visit them regularly, and meet all their needs with love; and the number of a family's members differs from one family to another — as Iraq's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — I Know My Family

Iraq Grade 1 Moral Education (printed pp. 49–54), teaches: The family tree: the small family (father, mother, brothers and sisters) is drawn first, then the branches grow — the grandfathers and grandmothers, the uncles and aunts — for the family has branches and twigs that increase as its members increase, and the big family's tree is the more beautiful the more branches and leaves it has, and it shades us from storms and hardships; the grandfathers and grandmothers are of our family — they founded our big family — so we respect them, visit them regularly, and meet all their needs with love; and the number of a family's members differs from one family to another. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: moral education is a separate subject from Grade 1, taught entirely as stories about five named children (Muhammad, Yusuf, Zahraa, Lana, Sara) whose small school and family incidents carry each value; every lesson ends in the child's own voice — "What I learned" — and the second unit's family tree is drawn with the father's help, branch by branch, until the big family shades them all. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
