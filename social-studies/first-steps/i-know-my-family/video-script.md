# Video script — I Know My Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 1 Moral Education (printed pp. 49–54), teaches: The family tree: the small family (father, mother, brothers and sisters) is drawn first, then the branches grow — the grandfathers and grandmothers, the uncles and aunts — for the family has branches and twigs that increase as its members increase, and the big family's tree is the more beautiful the more branches and leaves it has, and it shades us from storms and hardships; the grandfathers and grandmothers are of our family — they founded our big family — so we respect them, visit them regularly, and meet all their needs with love; and the number of a family's members differs from one family to another.

Iraq's own way: moral education is a separate subject from Grade 1, taught entirely as stories about five named children (Muhammad, Yusuf, Zahraa, Lana, Sara) whose small school and family incidents carry each value; every lesson ends in the child's own voice — "What I learned" — and the second unit's family tree is drawn with the father's help, branch by branch, until the big family shades them all.
