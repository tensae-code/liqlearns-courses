# Video script — I Can Do It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 2 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 40–44, teaches: The self unit closes on capability — the child names what she can do, the confidence half of self-knowledge.

Oman Grade 4 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 29–44, teaches: Self-fulfilment as a skill cycle — discovering the activities one loves and does well, planning to develop them, and seeing their effect on oneself and others; the standard closes on valuing one's active role in one's society.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 2, Term 1): Grade 2 turns Grade 1's circles into verbs — accept, communicate, cooperate, consult, belong — and plants two purely Omani institutions in the child's civic world: the sablah, the village meeting hall where the community gathers, and the folk games of the neighbourhood.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 4, Term 1): Grade 4 turns skill into service in three widening steps — what I am good at, what my family can build together (a planned family project, with its effect on society traced), and then volunteering in the neighbourhood, named in the last lesson without metaphor: "my volunteering is life".
