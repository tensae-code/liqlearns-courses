## Sub lesson 2 — I Can Do It 2

Oman Grade 4 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 29–44, teaches: Self-fulfilment as a skill cycle — discovering the activities one loves and does well, planning to develop them, and seeing their effect on oneself and others; the standard closes on valuing one's active role in one's society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: Grade 2 turns Grade 1's circles into verbs — accept, communicate, cooperate, consult, belong — and plants two purely Omani institutions in the child's civic world: the sablah, the village meeting hall where the community gathers, and the folk games of the neighbourhood. Grade 4 turns skill into service in three widening steps — what I am good at, what my family can build together (a planned family project, with its effect on society traced), and then volunteering in the neighbourhood, named in the last lesson without metaphor: "my volunteering is life". This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
