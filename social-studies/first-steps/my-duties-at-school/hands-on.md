# Hands-on — My Duties at School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Everyone in school has their own work — the head links the school to parents and state offices and chairs the teachers' council, deputies arrange timetables, attendants keep the school clean, and pupils come daily, strive, listen, keep the school green and clean, respect all and follow its rules. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Afghanistan's way
Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
