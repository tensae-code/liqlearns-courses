## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: at Grade 1 "order" is taught as one widening circle — home, then school, then neighbourhood — each part closing with a "Zayed taught me" saying and a named national value (respect, safety), and the country itself is introduced as a homeland of happiness and tolerance with its own Minister of Happiness and a Year of Tolerance. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
