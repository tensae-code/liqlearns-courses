# Video script — Order at Home Respect and Helping

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education G1 Term 3, printed p. 13, teaches: order at home — obeying parents is a duty, respecting the family members I live with, helping my parents when they ask, cooperating with my brothers and sisters in housework, treating domestic workers well, and learning from sitting with my grandparents

United Arab Emirates' own way: at Grade 1 "order" is taught as one widening circle — home, then school, then neighbourhood — each part closing with a "Zayed taught me" saying and a named national value (respect, safety), and the country itself is introduced as a homeland of happiness and tolerance with its own Minister of Happiness and a Year of Tolerance.
