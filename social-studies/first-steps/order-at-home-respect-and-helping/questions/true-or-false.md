# Order at Home Respect and Helping — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: order at home — obeying parents is a duty, respecting the family members I live with, helping my parents when they ask, cooperating with my brothers and sisters in housework, treating domestic workers well, and learning from sitting with my grandparents
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: at Grade 1 "order" is taught as one widening circle — home, then school, then neighbourhood — each part closing with a "Zayed taught me" saying and a named national value (respect, safety), and the country itself is introduced as a homeland of happiness and tolerance with its own Minister of Happiness and a Year of Tolerance.
**Answer: True**
