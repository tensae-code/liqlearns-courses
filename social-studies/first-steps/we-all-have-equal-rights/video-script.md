Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 54, teaches: Every person is different, yet all have equal rights (тэгш эрх): a right (эрх) is the chance to do what one wishes and loves — but one person's right and freedom are limited by another's, which is called limiting a right (эрхийг хязгаарлах): I love loud music, but the law forbids noise after 23:00 because the neighbours have a right to peaceful rest; people differ in language, culture, custom, food, dress and ability — some children cannot join every game — yet by law all are equal, and no one may be mocked, bullied or left out for being different; the tale of the owl who, mocked by the fox and monkey for her weak eyes, saves them both in the dark.

Mongolia's own way (Link book L1): Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for.
