# Hands-on — We All Have Equal Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 15 "We have equal rights" (Бид тэгш эрхтэй)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every person is different, yet all have equal rights (тэгш эрх): a right (эрх) is the chance to do what one wishes and loves — but one person's right and freedom are limited by another's, which is called limiting a right (эрхийг хязгаарлах): I love loud music, but the law forbids noise after 23:00 because the neighbours have a right to peaceful rest; people differ in language, culture, custom, food, dress and ability — some children cannot join every game — yet by law all are equal, and no one may be mocked, bullied or left out for being different; the tale of the owl who, mocked by the fox and monkey for her weak eyes, saves them both in the dark. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
