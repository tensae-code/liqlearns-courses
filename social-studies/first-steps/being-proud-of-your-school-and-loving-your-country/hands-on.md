# Hands-on — Being Proud of Your School and Loving Your Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Being Proud of Your School and Loving Our Country
Work through this part with your hands: pride in school through its motto and song (the motto motivates pupils, teachers and community toward the school's vision), acts that bring honour to a school, and loving Tanzania by honouring its national treasures (Kiswahili, patriotism, unity, accountability, love, respect, humanism), national symbols and public holidays. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Being Proud of Our School and Country
Work through this part with your hands: pride in school and country is shown through the national symbols and festivals — the constitution as a symbol of the nation, the national festivals (Zanzibar Revolution Day 12 January, Union Day 26 April, Workers' Day 1 May, Farmers' Day 8 August, Nyerere Day 14 October, Independence Day 9 December) on which leaders report achievements and citizens raise their concerns. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
