# Being Proud of Your School and Loving Your Country — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part 1 teaches: Pride in school through its motto and song (the motto motivates pupils, teachers and community toward the school's vision), acts that bring honour to a school, and loving Tanzania by honouring its national treasures (Kiswahili, patriotism, unity, accountability, love, respect, humanism), national symbols and public holidays.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part 2 teaches: Pride in school and country is shown through the national symbols and festivals — the constitution as a symbol of the nation, the national festivals (Zanzibar Revolution Day 12 January, Union Day 26 April, Workers' Day 1 May, Farmers' Day 8 August, Nyerere Day 14 October, Independence Day 9 December) on which leaders report achievements and citizens raise their concerns.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on Tanzania's student books, counted in the acceptance count in lesson/sources.md.
**Answer: True**
