# Video script — Why School Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 4 Social Studies (printed p. 50), teaches: The school has great importance in our lives and in the life of the society we belong to, because it helps our family in raising us on good morals and good conduct, teaches us love of our homeland Iraq and applying the laws and respecting the regulations, and is the basic foundation for preparing the scholars, leaders, doctors, lawyers, engineers and all the workers to serve our society.

Iraq's own way: civics starts at the school desk — the first community the Grade 4 child is given duties toward is the school family — and the homeland is then built piece by piece (land, people, waters, sky), with every one of Iraq's communities named in the population list, before rights are matched one-for-one with duties.
