# Hands-on — Why School Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The school has great importance in our lives and in the life of the society we belong to, because it helps our family in raising us on good morals and good conduct, teaches us love of our homeland Iraq and applying the laws and respecting the regulations, and is the basic foundation for preparing the scholars, leaders, doctors, lawyers, engineers and all the workers to serve our society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
