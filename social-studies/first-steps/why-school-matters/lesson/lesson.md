## Sub lesson 1 — Why School Matters

Iraq Grade 4 Social Studies (printed p. 50), teaches: The school has great importance in our lives and in the life of the society we belong to, because it helps our family in raising us on good morals and good conduct, teaches us love of our homeland Iraq and applying the laws and respecting the regulations, and is the basic foundation for preparing the scholars, leaders, doctors, lawyers, engineers and all the workers to serve our society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: civics starts at the school desk — the first community the Grade 4 child is given duties toward is the school family — and the homeland is then built piece by piece (land, people, waters, sky), with every one of Iraq's communities named in the population list, before rights are matched one-for-one with duties. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
