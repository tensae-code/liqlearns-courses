# Creation and evolution — two accounts of human origin — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the creation account, each kind of living thing was made by a creator.
**Answer: True** — That is the account in its own terms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The evolution account says humans appeared suddenly, fully formed.
**Answer: False** — It says gradual succession and variation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book sets the two accounts side by side for debate.
**Answer: True** — Fair statement of both, then debate.
**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The scientific theory that species change across immense time — this is evolution.
**Answer: True**
