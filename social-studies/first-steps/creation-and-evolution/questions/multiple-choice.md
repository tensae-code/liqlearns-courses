# Creation and evolution — two accounts of human origin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The creationist view holds that living things were —
a) evolved from earlier forms  b) created by God or a supernatural being  c) formed by chance alone
**Answer: b) created by God or a supernatural being** — Created, not descended.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The evolutionist view was formulated by —
a) Herodotus  b) Charles Darwin  c) Ranke
**Answer: b) Charles Darwin** — Darwin, 1809–1882.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In Darwin's account, humans arose by —
a) sudden appearance  b) gradual succession and variation from earlier forms  c) migration from another planet
**Answer: b) gradual succession and variation from earlier forms** — Gradual change across ages.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which view dominates scientific thinking, in the book's account?
a) the creationist view  b) the evolutionist view  c) neither — science is undecided
**Answer: b) the evolutionist view** — The book's own summary.

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** Large numbers of people worldwide hold —
a) the creationist view  b) no view at all  c) only the evolutionist view
**Answer: a) the creationist view** — Both facts are stated together.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Darwin's dates are —
a) 551–479 BC  b) 1809–1882  c) 1312–1337
**Answer: b) 1809–1882** — The 19th-century naturalist.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What is natural selection?
a) Darwin's mechanism — the fittest variations survive and multiply
b) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
c) the scientific theory that species change across immense time
**Answer: a) Darwin's mechanism — the fittest variations survive and multiply**
