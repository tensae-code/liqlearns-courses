# Creation and evolution — two accounts of human origin — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the creationist account in one sentence.
**Answer:** Humans and all living things were created by God or a supernatural being.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the evolutionist account in one sentence, naming its formulator.
**Answer:** Formulated by Charles Darwin: living things, including humans, arose by gradual succession and variation from earlier forms.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things does the book say about how the views stand today?
**Answer:** The scientific (evolutionist) view dominates scientific thinking, and large numbers of people worldwide hold the creationist view.
