# Hands-on — Creation and evolution — two accounts of human origin

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The fair-summary game
In pairs: each partner must summarise the OTHER side's account to that partner's satisfaction before arguing their own. The listener scores the summary: fair, or a straw man?

## Two panels
Draw the lesson's picture yourself: two facing panels, creation and branching descent. Under each, write its one-sentence claim. No mockery in either panel — the rule of the lesson.
