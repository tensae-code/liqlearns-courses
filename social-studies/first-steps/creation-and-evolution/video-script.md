# Video script — Creation and evolution — two accounts of human origin

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A question hangs over the map. Where did we come from?
One voice answers: we were created — every living thing made by God.
Another voice, a man with a beard in 19th-century England: Darwin. Life changed, slowly, form from earlier form.
Science today walks Darwin's road. Multitudes still walk the first.
Two accounts. One question. Learn to state both fairly — then think.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Two accounts of human origins, side by side. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 book sets the two families of explanation side by side and lets them be compared fairly: the creation accounts, held by faith traditions, and the scientific theory of evolution, in which Darwin's natural selection explains how species change across immense time. The names to hold: natural selection, evolution.
