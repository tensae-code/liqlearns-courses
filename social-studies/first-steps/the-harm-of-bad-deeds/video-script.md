The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 18, teaches: A bad deed brings suffering to its doer and to others; the bad mind is greed (шунал), anger and pride (уур, омог), ignorance (мунхаг) and envy (атаа) — against them, speak gently, share others' joy, never envy another's goods or success, and keep a patient heart; the old woman of the tale undoes herself by halves — lying by halves, stealing by halves, slandering by halves — until her own life is halved.

Mongolia's own way (Link book L1): Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for.
