# The Harm of Bad Deeds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 4 "The harm of bad deeds" (Муу үйлийн хор уршиг)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 4 "The harm of bad deeds" (Муу үйлийн хор уршиг)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) A bad deed brings suffering to its doer and to others; the bad mind is greed (шунал), anger and pride (уур, омог), ignorance (мунхаг) and envy (атаа) — against them, speak gently, share others' joy, never envy another's goods or success, and keep a patient heart; the old woman of the tale undoes herself by halves — lying by halves, stealing by halves, slandering by halves — until her own life is halved  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) A bad deed brings suffering to its doer and to others; the bad mind is greed (шунал), anger and pride (уур, омог), ignorance (мунхаг) and envy (атаа) — against them, speak gently, share others' joy, never envy another's goods or success, and keep a patient heart; the old woman of the tale undoes herself by halves — lying by halves, stealing by halves, slandering by halves — until her own life is halved**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Mongolia's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.  b) Mongolia's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) Mongolia's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.**
