# The Harm of Bad Deeds — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: a bad deed brings suffering to its doer and to others; the bad mind is greed (шунал), anger and pride (уур, омог), ignorance (мунхаг) and envy (атаа) — against them, speak gently, share others' joy, never envy another's goods or success, and keep a patient heart; the old woman of the tale undoes herself by halves — lying by halves, stealing by halves, slandering by halves — until her own life is halved.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
