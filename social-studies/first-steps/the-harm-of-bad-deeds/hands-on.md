# Hands-on — The Harm of Bad Deeds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 4 "The harm of bad deeds" (Муу үйлийн хор уршиг)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A bad deed brings suffering to its doer and to others; the bad mind is greed (шунал), anger and pride (уур, омог), ignorance (мунхаг) and envy (атаа) — against them, speak gently, share others' joy, never envy another's goods or success, and keep a patient heart; the old woman of the tale undoes herself by halves — lying by halves, stealing by halves, slandering by halves — until her own life is halved. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
