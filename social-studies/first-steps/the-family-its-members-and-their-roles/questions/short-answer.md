# The Family Its Members and Their Roles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 30 Somos familia — A family descends from a couple, lives in harmony, and each member has responsibilities by age; parents protect and decide, grandparents guide; when each does their part everyone benefits (André's coffee-farming family in Teupasenti) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 1 La familia — The family is society's smallest unit and society works well if the family works well; its functions include material and emotional security and socialisation; Honduras's Family Code (Decree 76-84, 1984) guarantees each member's rights, and the lesson closes on rights and duties inside the family and on Garífuna community family life (children share fishing and yuca, rice and banana work; solidarity and respect for elders) (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 2 Tipos de familia — Family types by size, members and residence — the extensive family (parents, children, grandparents, uncles, cousins under one roof) against smaller forms; Honduran families have moved from mostly rural (to 2007) to mostly urban (UN Population Fund); maltreatment is named in its kinds — physical, psychological and sexual abuse, negligence and abandonment — and must be avoided (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the fourth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 1, teoría adicional — The family is a community of persons joined by kinship ties, with a name, a home and the duty of moral and material solidarity, above all between parents and children; in modern societies it has shrunk to the nuclear family, with the extended family, friends and schoolmates arriving later; the parents' own example — how they ask for things, yield a seat, share, renounce and defend others — is the core of values education, with effort, constancy, compassion, recognition of the other, justice and tolerance of frustration (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does the fifth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 2, teoría adicional — The International Convention on the Rights of the Child gives every child the right to a family, which links the child to a history and protects against violations and maltreatment — though sometimes the family itself is the aggressor; parents are the first who can act for their children: they decide their care and medical treatment (no practice without their consent except in emergency) and hold the essential duty of educating them, including moral, civic and religious education, so they can live in society with autonomy (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the sixth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — civics begins inside the child's own circles — feelings, family norms and tasks, classroom rules and school authorities, a rehearsal student-government election with a paper ballot, and only then the community's elected authorities (alcaldesa, regidores, patronato, junta de agua). secondary civics starts from the family as a legal institution — the Family Code (Decree 76-84) enters in Lesson 1 — and builds to the State itself: its elements (population, territory, power), Montesquieu's separation, and the full legislative process ending in publication in La Gaceta. (any close wording).**
