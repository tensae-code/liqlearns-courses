# Video script — Social Groups in Our Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 5 Year 5 Social Studies (printed p. 3), teaches: A community is people living together with something in common; the social groups we belong to (family first, then school and community groups), formal and informal groups, each with a leader, roles and responsibilities.

Fiji Grade 9 Social Science Year 9 (printed p. 20), teaches: A group is people interacting in an orderly way on shared expectations; primary (informal, first — family and friends, unwritten rules) and secondary (formal) groups.

Fiji's own way (Grade 5): community is taught as the social groups a child belongs to — family first, then school and community groups — and Fiji's multicultural mix is the civics lesson itself: all born in Fiji are Fijian, each keeping their own culture. Fiji's own way (Grade 9): society is analysed through Fiji's own structures — the iTaukei hierarchy of Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau and Tauvanua with its ascribed chiefly status — and conflict is settled the iTaukei way, by Bulubulu.
