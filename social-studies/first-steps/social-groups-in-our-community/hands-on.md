# Hands-on — Social Groups in Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Personal, social groups and processes
Work through this part with your hands: A community is people living together with something in common; the social groups we belong to (family first, then school and community groups), formal and informal groups, each with a leader, roles and responsibilities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Groups — primary and secondary
Work through this part with your hands: A group is people interacting in an orderly way on shared expectations; primary (informal, first — family and friends, unwritten rules) and secondary (formal) groups. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
