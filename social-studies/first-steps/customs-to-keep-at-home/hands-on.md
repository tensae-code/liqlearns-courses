# Hands-on — Customs to Keep at Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 6 "From the customs to keep at home" (Гэрт баримтлах ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The ger is honoured — a guest enters and moves to the right, the place of honour (хоймор) faces the door, and the taboos guard the house: never step on the threshold (it is said to bring poverty), never sit on the threshold or lean on the posts, close the door properly, sit by age — elders above, children below the posts — and on leaving, the eldest goes first. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
