Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 21, teaches: The ger is honoured — a guest enters and moves to the right, the place of honour (хоймор) faces the door, and the taboos guard the house: never step on the threshold (it is said to bring poverty), never sit on the threshold or lean on the posts, close the door properly, sit by age — elders above, children below the posts — and on leaving, the eldest goes first.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
