# Customs to Keep at Home — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the ger is honoured — a guest enters and moves to the right, the place of honour (хоймор) faces the door, and the taboos guard the house: never step on the threshold (it is said to bring poverty), never sit on the threshold or lean on the posts, close the door properly, sit by age — elders above, children below the posts — and on leaving, the eldest goes first.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
