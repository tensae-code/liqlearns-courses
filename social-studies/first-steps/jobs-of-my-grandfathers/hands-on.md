# Hands-on — Jobs of My Grandfathers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Jobs of my grandfathers
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the importance of work and its different forms; the jobs the grandfathers practised in the old days and how those jobs have developed in recent times; and the founding father's care for people's work in his state Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
