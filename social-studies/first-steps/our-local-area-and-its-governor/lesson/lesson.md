# Our Local Area And Its Governor

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: our country has 21 aimags, an aimag is made of soums and the soums of bags; the capital Ulaanbaatar has nine districts made of khoroos; a governor (Засаг дарга) — the government's representative — leads the soum, bag, district and khoroo; around the soum administration building stand the people who serve the citizens — police officer, family doctor, social worker, communications officer, nature ranger; my own address written in full, and the services the state gives citizens, such as a grandmother's pension — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 18 "Our local area" (Манай орон нутаг)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 52, teaches: Our country has 21 aimags, an aimag is made of soums and the soums of bags; the capital Ulaanbaatar has nine districts made of khoroos; a governor (Засаг дарга) — the government's representative — leads the soum, bag, district and khoroo; around the soum administration building stand the people who serve the citizens — police officer, family doctor, social worker, communications officer, nature ranger; my own address written in full, and the services the state gives citizens, such as a grandmother's pension. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
