# Hands-on — Our Local Area And Its Governor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 18 "Our local area" (Манай орон нутаг)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Our country has 21 aimags, an aimag is made of soums and the soums of bags; the capital Ulaanbaatar has nine districts made of khoroos; a governor (Засаг дарга) — the government's representative — leads the soum, bag, district and khoroo; around the soum administration building stand the people who serve the citizens — police officer, family doctor, social worker, communications officer, nature ranger; my own address written in full, and the services the state gives citizens, such as a grandmother's pension. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
