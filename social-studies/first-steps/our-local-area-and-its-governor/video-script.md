# Video script — Our Local Area And Its Governor

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 52, teaches: Our country has 21 aimags, an aimag is made of soums and the soums of bags; the capital Ulaanbaatar has nine districts made of khoroos; a governor (Засаг дарга) — the government's representative — leads the soum, bag, district and khoroo; around the soum administration building stand the people who serve the citizens — police officer, family doctor, social worker, communications officer, nature ranger; my own address written in full, and the services the state gives citizens, such as a grandmother's pension.

Mongolia's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact.
