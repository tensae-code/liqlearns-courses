# Our Local Area And Its Governor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 18 "Our local area" (Манай орон нутаг) — Our country has 21 aimags, an aimag is made of soums and the soums of bags; the capital Ulaanbaatar has nine districts made of khoroos; a governor (Засаг дарга) — the government's representative — leads the soum, bag, district and khoroo; around the soum administration building stand the people who serve the citizens — police officer, family doctor, social worker, communications officer, nature ranger; my own address written in full, and the services the state gives citizens, such as a grandmother's pension (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact (any close wording).**
