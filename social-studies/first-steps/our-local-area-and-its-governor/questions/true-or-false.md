# Our Local Area And Its Governor — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: our country has 21 aimags, an aimag is made of soums and the soums of bags; the capital Ulaanbaatar has nine districts made of khoroos; a governor (Засаг дарга) — the government's representative — leads the soum, bag, district and khoroo; around the soum administration building stand the people who serve the citizens — police officer, family doctor, social worker, communications officer, nature ranger; my own address written in full, and the services the state gives citizens, such as a grandmother's pension.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
