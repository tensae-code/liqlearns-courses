# Hands-on — My Dear Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what a nuclear family (çekirdek aile) is, the importance of the family, keeping family unity by listening to each other, love and respect at home, and visiting elders at bayrams, through Yağız's diary of a family Saturday Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Turkey's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
