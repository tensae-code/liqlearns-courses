# Video script — My Dear Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed p. 82, teaches: what a nuclear family (çekirdek aile) is, the importance of the family, keeping family unity by listening to each other, love and respect at home, and visiting elders at bayrams, through Yağız's diary of a family Saturday

Turkey's own way: Hayat Bilgisi (Life Studies) is Turkey's integrated primary subject, rebuilt under the Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli as six learning areas; this first book runs Me-and-My-School → My Health and Safety → My Family and Society, every topic in the same rhythm (remember, explore, knowledge journey, practise, what we learned), with the year's special days carried as poems at the head of each area and the Student Oath (Andımız) printed at the front.
