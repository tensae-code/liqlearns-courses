# Hands-on — Organising My Work at Home and School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## I organise my work and evaluate my performance
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 5 Dalil Moufid Ijtiamiat (Teacher Guide), printed p. 125, teaches: the rules of organised work — set goals, list tasks, rank priorities, plan time, choose the place, evaluate the result — applied to work at home and at school, with self-evaluation of one's own organisation and performance; school rules as part of it (respecting entry/exit times, saluting the national flag with discipline, respecting the school and class rules, accepting classmates with special needs, keeping school property clean). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: Moroccan primary Social Studies is one integrated subject (History + Geography + Civic Education) built around "my homeland"; its civics third is a full civic-education programme in its own right — civic behaviour defined as the citizen's relation to society and to the state through its institutions, civic values (freedom, dignity, solidarity, equality, tolerance, rejecting discrimination) learned by practice not memorisation, and the classroom itself run as a small democracy (a negotiated class charter and an elected class cooperative with ballot box and vote count). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
