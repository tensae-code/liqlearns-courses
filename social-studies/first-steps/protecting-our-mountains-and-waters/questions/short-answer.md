# Protecting Our Mountains And Waters — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 9 "Let's protect our mountains and waters" (Уул, усаа хамгаалья) — For thousands of years Mongolians have lived by their livestock, and mountain, water, plants and soil are the animals' pasture — so nature is worshipped and guarded: mountains and waters have their master-spirits (савдаг, лус), the first of the tea and food is offered to them, and the taboos forbid digging up the earth, felling trees, breaking branches, picking plants by the root, hunting beyond need, and playing with fire (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact (any close wording).**
