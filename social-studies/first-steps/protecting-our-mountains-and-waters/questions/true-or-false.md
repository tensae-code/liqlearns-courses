# Protecting Our Mountains And Waters — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: for thousands of years Mongolians have lived by their livestock, and mountain, water, plants and soil are the animals' pasture — so nature is worshipped and guarded: mountains and waters have their master-spirits (савдаг, лус), the first of the tea and food is offered to them, and the taboos forbid digging up the earth, felling trees, breaking branches, picking plants by the root, hunting beyond need, and playing with fire.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
