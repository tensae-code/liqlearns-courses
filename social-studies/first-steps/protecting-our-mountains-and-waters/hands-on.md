# Hands-on — Protecting Our Mountains And Waters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 9 "Let's protect our mountains and waters" (Уул, усаа хамгаалья)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: For thousands of years Mongolians have lived by their livestock, and mountain, water, plants and soil are the animals' pasture — so nature is worshipped and guarded: mountains and waters have their master-spirits (савдаг, лус), the first of the tea and food is offered to them, and the taboos forbid digging up the earth, felling trees, breaking branches, picking plants by the root, hunting beyond need, and playing with fire. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
