# Hands-on — Visiting a Fish Canning Factory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: in the waters of the Persian Gulf and the Sea of Oman live many kinds of fish; fish and shrimp are caught from these seas and sold in the country's markets — tuna, shrimp, halva, shir and shurideh are named and pictured — and the visit follows the catch into the cannery where it is preserved. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
