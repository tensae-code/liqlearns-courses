The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 8–9), teaches: How people live and work follows the land — flat regions farm with machines and grow wheat, corn and vegetables; hilly regions keep orchards, vineyards and livestock; mountain regions live from forests, sheep, mines and tourists.

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
