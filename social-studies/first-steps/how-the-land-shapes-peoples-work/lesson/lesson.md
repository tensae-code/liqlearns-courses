# How the Land Shapes Peoples Work

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: how people live and work follows the land — flat regions farm with machines and grow wheat, corn and vegetables; hilly regions keep orchards, vineyards and livestock; mountain regions live from forests, sheep, mines and tourists — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Живот и рад људи у мом крају (Life and work of people in my region)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 8–9), teaches: how people live and work follows the land — flat regions farm with machines and grow wheat, corn and vegetables; hilly regions keep orchards, vineyards and livestock; mountain regions live from forests, sheep, mines and tourists. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
