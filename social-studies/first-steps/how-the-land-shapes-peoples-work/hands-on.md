# Hands-on — How the Land Shapes Peoples Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Живот и рад људи у мом крају (Life and work of people in my region)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how people live and work follows the land — flat regions farm with machines and grow wheat, corn and vegetables; hilly regions keep orchards, vineyards and livestock; mountain regions live from forests, sheep, mines and tourists. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
