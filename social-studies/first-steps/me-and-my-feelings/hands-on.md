# Hands-on — Me and My Feelings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: All people pass through different feelings — joy, love, anger and sadness — and we must express our feelings and not keep them inside us, choosing the right way to express them and the suitable time for each matter: in the mother's feelings-cards game, Rami learns that we all live moments of anger, but you can control your anger and choose another way to express it besides crying and shouting — such as talking with your father or mother about what angers you — because crying and shouting do not change the thing that angered you; and Lana, sad because she pushed her friend Zahraa hard while playing, goes to her house and apologises to her. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
