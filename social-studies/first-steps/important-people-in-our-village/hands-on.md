# Hands-on — Important People in Our Village

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lesson 20 Important people in the village (ບຸກຄົນສໍາຄັນໃນບ້ານ)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The people the village depends on — its chief and elders and the workers in its services — and respecting each one's role. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Laos's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Laos's book teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
