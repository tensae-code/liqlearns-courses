## Sub lesson 1 — My Identity Booklet

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 14, teaches: my personal details (مشخصات فردی) — name, family name, father's name, national number — are written in my identity booklet (شناسنامه) and my national card; everyone's details are different; Sara reads hers aloud from the booklet's picture, and the pupil completes a registration form from their own. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
