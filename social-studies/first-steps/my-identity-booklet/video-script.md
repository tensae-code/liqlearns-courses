# Video script — My Identity Booklet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 14, teaches: my personal details (مشخصات فردی) — name, family name, father's name, national number — are written in my identity booklet (شناسنامه) and my national card; everyone's details are different; Sara reads hers aloud from the booklet's picture, and the pupil completes a registration form from their own.

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces.
