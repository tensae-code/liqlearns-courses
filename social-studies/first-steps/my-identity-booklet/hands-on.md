# Hands-on — My Identity Booklet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: my personal details (مشخصات فردی) — name, family name, father's name, national number — are written in my identity booklet (شناسنامه) and my national card; everyone's details are different; Sara reads hers aloud from the booklet's picture, and the pupil completes a registration form from their own. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
