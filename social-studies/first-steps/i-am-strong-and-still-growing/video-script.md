# Video script — I Am Strong and Still Growing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 3 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed p. 16, teaches: each of us has interests, strong sides and sides open to development; we discover them (the class-festival poem where each child offers what they do best — running, drawing, science experiments) and work on the sides we want to grow

Turkey's own way: Grade 3 is where Hayat Bilgisi turns explicitly civic: an eight-year-old is taught that rights and responsibilities are a pair, and then reads the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child itself — in a child-worded card set (definition of the child, no discrimination, best interests, life and development) — two years before any civics course exists.
