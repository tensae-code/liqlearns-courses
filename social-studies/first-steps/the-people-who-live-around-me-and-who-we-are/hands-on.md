# Hands-on — The People Who Live Around Me and Who We Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 1 A. Identitas Masyarakat di Lingkungan Tempat Tinggalku, Bab 1 B. Menghargai Perbedaan Identitas Masyarakat, Bab 5 C. Kehidupan Masyarakat Daerahku, Bab V: Ini Khas Daerahku!, Panduan Pembelajaran — teaches: the identity of the community where I live — who lives around me and what makes us a community; respecting differences of identity in the community; the life of my region's community — how the landscape shapes livelihoods (mata pencaharian): coastal Bima's fishers and salt farmers as the worked example; guidance for the community-life part of the chapter — livelihoods of my region's people. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indonesia's way
Say how Indonesia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
