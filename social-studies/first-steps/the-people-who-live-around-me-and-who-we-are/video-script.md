Indonesia's Grade 4 PP-G4 student (printed p. 4); Grade 4 PP-G4 student (printed p. 8); Grade 4 IPAS-G4 student (printed p. 111); Grade 4 IPAS-G4 teacher (printed p. 141), teaches: Bab 1 A. Identitas Masyarakat di Lingkungan Tempat Tinggalku, Bab 1 B. Menghargai Perbedaan Identitas Masyarakat, Bab 5 C. Kehidupan Masyarakat Daerahku, Bab V: Ini Khas Daerahku!, Panduan Pembelajaran — teaches: the identity of the community where I live — who lives around me and what makes us a community; respecting differences of identity in the community; the life of my region's community — how the landscape shapes livelihoods (mata pencaharian): coastal Bima's fishers and salt farmers as the worked example; guidance for the community-life part of the chapter — livelihoods of my region's people.

Indonesia's own way: Grade 4 introduces the child's first government — the perangkat desa/kelurahan, the village and urban-ward officials — inside a book about the child's own street, before any national institution is named. IPAS Grade 4 teaches community through kearifan lokal (local wisdom) — pupils interview classmates and teachers about habits handed down in their families (jamu drinking, speaking Batak at home) before any national culture is named. the guide's Bab VI essential material states roles, rules and responsibilities as one civic package across three environments — home first, then school, then masyarakat (society).
