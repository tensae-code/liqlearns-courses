## Sub lesson 2 — Indonesia's way — the books' own teaching way

Indonesia's own way: Grade 4 introduces the child's first government — the perangkat desa/kelurahan, the village and urban-ward officials — inside a book about the child's own street, before any national institution is named. IPAS Grade 4 teaches community through kearifan lokal (local wisdom) — pupils interview classmates and teachers about habits handed down in their families (jamu drinking, speaking Batak at home) before any national culture is named. the guide's Bab VI essential material states roles, rules and responsibilities as one civic package across three environments — home first, then school, then masyarakat (society).
