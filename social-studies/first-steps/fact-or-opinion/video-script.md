# Video script — Fact or opinion? Reading the past critically

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two sentences walk onto the screen. They look the same. They are not.
"He reigned for twenty years." Records nod. King lists nod. A fact — it can be proved.
"He was an evil despot." Silence. That is one writer judging. An opinion.
History is built of the first kind. Learn to spot the second, and no writer can smuggle his judgement past you.
