# Hands-on — Fact or opinion? Reading the past critically

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Prove it or park it
On cards, write eight sentences — four provable, four viewpoints. In pairs, sort them into two piles: PROVE IT (name the evidence) and PARK IT (opinion). Any card the pair cannot agree on goes to the class debate pile.

## The king's two sentences
Tell a two-minute story of any ruler or elder you know: first pass — facts only; second pass — add your opinions, and raise a hand each time one slips in, so the class hears the difference.
