The question over the whole set is in its title: care, or coercion? Switzerland's own way (the reader): Five representative fates stand for over 100,000 people hit by compulsory welfare measures; each story is told first, then continued by the pupil from photographed original files, and each ends on an open judgement question, not a verdict.
