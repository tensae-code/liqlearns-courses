# Fact or opinion? Reading the past critically — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes a statement a fact rather than an opinion?
**Answer:** It can be proved with supporting information — evidence can be produced to check it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Take the sentence "The emperor was a cruel man who reigned for thirty years." Split it into its fact and its opinion.
**Answer:** Fact: he reigned for thirty years. Opinion: he was a cruel man.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does history teaching train this skill?
**Answer:** Because telling fact from opinion — critical thinking — is one of the core skills history teaches, for reading the past and the present alike.
