# Fact or opinion? Reading the past critically — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** If evidence for a claim fell away, the fact built on it would fall too.
**Answer: True** — A fact stands on its evidence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Two honest writers can hold opposite opinions about the same king.
**Answer: True** — Opinions live in the judging.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Calling a ruler 'an evil despot' can be proved from a king list.
**Answer: False** — A king list proves reigns, not judgements.
