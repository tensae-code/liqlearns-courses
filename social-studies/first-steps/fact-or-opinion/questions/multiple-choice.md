# Fact or opinion? Reading the past critically — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fact is something that —
a) a writer feels strongly  b) can be proved with supporting information  c) appears in a novel
**Answer: b) can be proved with supporting information** — Proof is the mark of a fact.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** History, as a body of knowledge, is built on —
a) opinions  b) facts and evidence  c) imagination
**Answer: b) facts and evidence** — Not a novel, not an opinion.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** "He was an evil despot" is —
a) a fact  b) an opinion  c) a date
**Answer: b) an opinion** — The guide's worked example — a viewpoint.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An opinion cannot be —
a) written down  b) proved  c) read
**Answer: b) proved** — Unprovable is what makes it opinion.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a fact?
a) "She was the greatest queen ever"  b) "She reigned for twenty years"  c) "She was surely kind"
**Answer: b) "She reigned for twenty years"** — A reign's length can be checked in records.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Telling fact from opinion is —
a) not part of history  b) one of the core skills history teaches  c) only for lawyers
**Answer: b) one of the core skills history teaches** — Critical thinking is the skill.
