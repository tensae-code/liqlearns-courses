# Hands-on — Businesses and Services in Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The businesses and services pupils meet in everyday life (bakery and café, shop, pharmacy, hairdresser's and the rest), what each is for and when you need it, taught in easy swedish from discussion pictures and the pupils' own experience. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sweden's way
Say how Sweden's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
