# Video script — Businesses and Services in Our Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sweden's Grades 1–9 adapted school Så funkar samhället Teacher Guide (printed pp. 1–2), teaches: The businesses and services pupils meet in everyday life (bakery and café, shop, pharmacy, hairdresser's and the rest), what each is for and when you need it, taught in easy swedish from discussion pictures and the pupils' own experience.

Sweden's own way: the guide frames the whole of primary civics for the adapted school as the services and jobs of everyday life — part 1 the businesses a pupil meets (bakery, shop, pharmacy, hairdresser…), part 2 the people who work in them — opened through discussion pictures and real visits, with difficult words (kollektivtrafik/public transport, återvinningscentral/recycling centre) explained concretely. The pupil book is book 3 of a five-book "Så funkar samhället" series (traffic; culture and leisure; recycling; healthcare and blue-light services are the other parts). In the adapted school, civics is taught as "Så funkar samhället" (How society works) — the businesses and jobs a pupil actually meets, in very easy Swedish with symbols for every task type.
