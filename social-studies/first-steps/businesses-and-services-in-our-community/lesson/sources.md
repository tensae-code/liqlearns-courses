Sweden's own way (teacher guide — evidence): the guide frames the whole of primary civics for the adapted school as the services and jobs of everyday life — part 1 the businesses a pupil meets (bakery, shop, pharmacy, hairdresser…), part 2 the people who work in them — opened through discussion pictures and real visits, with difficult words (kollektivtrafik/public transport, återvinningscentral/recycling centre) explained concretely. The pupil book is book 3 of a five-book "Så funkar samhället" series (traffic; culture and leisure; recycling; healthcare and blue-light services are the other parts). In the adapted school, civics is taught as "Så funkar samhället" (How society works) — the businesses and jobs a pupil actually meets, in very easy Swedish with symbols for every task type.
