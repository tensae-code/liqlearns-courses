Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Businesses and Services in Our Community

Sweden's Grades 1–9 adapted school Så funkar samhället Teacher Guide (printed pp. 1–2), teaches: The businesses and services pupils meet in everyday life (bakery and café, shop, pharmacy, hairdresser's and the rest), what each is for and when you need it, taught in easy swedish from discussion pictures and the pupils' own experience. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sweden's way — the book's own teaching way

Sweden's own way: the guide frames the whole of primary civics for the adapted school as the services and jobs of everyday life — part 1 the businesses a pupil meets (bakery, shop, pharmacy, hairdresser…), part 2 the people who work in them — opened through discussion pictures and real visits, with difficult words (kollektivtrafik/public transport, återvinningscentral/recycling centre) explained concretely. The pupil book is book 3 of a five-book "Så funkar samhället" series (traffic; culture and leisure; recycling; healthcare and blue-light services are the other parts). In the adapted school, civics is taught as "Så funkar samhället" (How society works) — the businesses and jobs a pupil actually meets, in very easy Swedish with symbols for every task type. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
