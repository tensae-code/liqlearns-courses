# Hands-on — My Belongings Taking Care of Them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book’s topic
Work through this lesson’s topic with your hands: Responsibility for your school things and keeping your room tidy, matching belongings to where they belong. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mauritius’s way
Say how Mauritius’s book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
