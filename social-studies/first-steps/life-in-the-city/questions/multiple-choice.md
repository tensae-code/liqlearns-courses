Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Life in the City  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Life in the City**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) the city is bigger than the village and more people live in it; in city families most fathers and some mothers work in offices, factories, shops and other places; and running the city is the municipality's (شهرداری) charge  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the city is bigger than the village and more people live in it; in city families most fathers and some mothers work in offices, factories, shops and other places; and running the city is the municipality's (شهرداری) charge**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
