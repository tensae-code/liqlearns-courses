# Hands-on — Life in the City

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the city is bigger than the village and more people live in it; in city families most fathers and some mothers work in offices, factories, shops and other places; and running the city is the municipality's (شهرداری) charge. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
