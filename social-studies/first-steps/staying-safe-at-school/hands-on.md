# Hands-on — Staying Safe at School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: at school too we keep the safety points so we stay safe and well — come down the stairs slowly and hold the rail; do not run in the corridors and never push one another; if our food falls on the ground it is dirty and no longer eatable — it goes in the bin. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
