## Sub lesson 1 — Staying Safe at School

Iran Social Studies Grade 5 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 37, teaches: at school too we keep the safety points so we stay safe and well — come down the stairs slowly and hold the rail; do not run in the corridors and never push one another; if our food falls on the ground it is dirty and no longer eatable — it goes in the bin. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 5 special-needs book teaches social life through the Iranian year and the Iranian map — feelings and manners at the Yalda gathering and on the phone to the taxi office, safety as a daily habit in three places, and the country learned by travelling it at Nowruz, to Tabriz and to Shiraz. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
