# Video script — Staying Safe at School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 5 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 37, teaches: at school too we keep the safety points so we stay safe and well — come down the stairs slowly and hold the rail; do not run in the corridors and never push one another; if our food falls on the ground it is dirty and no longer eatable — it goes in the bin.

Iran's own way: the Grade 5 special-needs book teaches social life through the Iranian year and the Iranian map — feelings and manners at the Yalda gathering and on the phone to the taxi office, safety as a daily habit in three places, and the country learned by travelling it at Nowruz, to Tabriz and to Shiraz.
