## Sub lesson 1 — Family roles — everyone has a part

Fiji Grade 3 Social Studies Year 3 (printed p. 17), teaches: Everyone in the family has a role — a duty or job, like mother cooking and children setting the table — and each person must carry out their role well (Tomasi case study). ## Sub lesson 2 — Fiji's way — the books' own teaching way

Fiji's own way (Grade 3): the whole primary course starts from the child in the family and widens outward year by year; Year 3 is built as a UNESCO-backed Family Tree project, so family, culture and heritage are researched by the child from their own household. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
