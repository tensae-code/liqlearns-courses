# Video script — Family Relationships and Roles of Family Members

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 3 Social Studies Year 3 (printed p. 17), teaches: Everyone in the family has a role — a duty or job, like mother cooking and children setting the table — and each person must carry out their role well (Tomasi case study).

Fiji's own way (Grade 3): the whole primary course starts from the child in the family and widens outward year by year; Year 3 is built as a UNESCO-backed Family Tree project, so family, culture and heritage are researched by the child from their own household.
