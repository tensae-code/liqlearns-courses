# Hands-on — Family Relationships and Roles of Family Members

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Family roles — everyone has a part
Work through this part with your hands: Everyone in the family has a role — a duty or job, like mother cooking and children setting the table — and each person must carry out their role well (Tomasi case study). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
