# Hands-on — Being Good to Neighbours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Living peacefully with neighbours
Work through this part with your hands: Respecting one another and others' property, assisting those who need help, following rules and regulations, and being honest and polite. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Fiji's way
Say how Fiji's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
