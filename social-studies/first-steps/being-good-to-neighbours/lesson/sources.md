**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Fiji). Oman is named here as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Fiji's own way (Grade 4): The neighbourhood is taught through Fiji's own community types — farming, iTaukei village and urban/town communities — and the child's duties inside it.

Searchable tags: being good to neighbours, Fiji, social studies, first-steps, Oman

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 2, Term 1) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 2 turns Grade 1's circles into verbs — accept, communicate, cooperate, consult, belong — and plants two purely Omani institutions in the child's civic world: the sablah, the village meeting hall where the community gathers, and the folk games of the neighbourhood. That evidence is stocked here as the way Oman teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Country honesty note: Oman's student books were not held — its placements are based on teacher guides, and the G7 Term-2 content is a named GAP (the Grade 7 framework assigns the Basic Statute of the State and government institutions to Grade 7, no Term 1 unit teaches them, and the Term 2 book was not reached, as filed in the oman-placements.md record-only section); all Oman placements in this lesson are evidence only, outside acceptance counts.
