# Work and the Workers

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Shaqada iyo Shaqaalaha (Work and the Workers)

Somalia Grade 1 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (Teacher Guide) (printed p. 33), teaches: work and the people who do it — the kinds of work in the child's world and the dignity of the worker. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 1): this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
