# Hands-on — Work and the Workers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shaqada iyo Shaqaalaha (Work and the Workers)
Work through this part with your hands: work and the people who do it — the kinds of work in the child's world and the dignity of the worker. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
