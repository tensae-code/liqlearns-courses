# Work and the Workers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Shaqada iyo Shaqaalaha (Work and the Workers)" teaches: work and the people who do it — the kinds of work in the child's world and the dignity of the worker.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in..
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
