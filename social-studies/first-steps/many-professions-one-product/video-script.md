# Video script — Many Professions One Product

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece Grade 4 Meleti Perivallontos D' Dimotikou (printed p. 99), teaches: How many different jobs join to make a single product — people needing each other at work.

Greece's own way: Grade 4 turns from the neighbourhood to the whole country and its culture — Greek culture is set beside other peoples' cultures, and work is taught as the web of professions that together make one product.
