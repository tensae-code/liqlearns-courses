## Sub lesson 1 — Many professions, one product

Greece Grade 4 Meleti Perivallontos D' Dimotikou (printed p. 99), teaches: How many different jobs join to make a single product — people needing each other at work. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greece's way — the book's own teaching way

Greece's own way: Grade 4 turns from the neighbourhood to the whole country and its culture — Greek culture is set beside other peoples' cultures, and work is taught as the web of professions that together make one product. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
