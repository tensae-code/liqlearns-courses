# Many Professions One Product — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Many professions, one product  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —

**Answer: a) How many different jobs join to make a single product — people needing each other at work**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
