# Hands-on — A Day with Relatives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Friday is the holiday in our country; Sara plans her Friday — after her homework she helps her mother clean the house, then goes to her grandparents' house in their neighbourhood, for visiting elders is one of the customs of the kind people of Iran. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
