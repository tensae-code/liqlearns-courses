## Sub lesson 1 — A Day with Relatives

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 32, teaches: Friday is the holiday in our country; Sara plans her Friday — after her homework she helps her mother clean the house, then goes to her grandparents' house in their neighbourhood, for visiting elders is one of the customs of the kind people of Iran. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
