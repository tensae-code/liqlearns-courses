# Hands-on — How Communities Support Each Other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: no community provides everything itself — the farming village sends grain, vegetables and livestock to town and buys clothes, shoes, sugar and coffee back; the coastal fishing village sells its fish in Massawa — village and town complete each other. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
