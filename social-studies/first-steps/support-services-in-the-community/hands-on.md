# Hands-on — Support Services in the Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: support services are organisations and people who help individuals facing challenges — counsellors (emotional and psychological support), community care workers (daily help for families in need), BEAM (Basic Education Assistance Module, school-fees support), police officers (order and safety) and health workers (medical help and health education). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
