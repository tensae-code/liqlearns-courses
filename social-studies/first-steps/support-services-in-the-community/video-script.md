# Video script — Support Services in the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe Grade 3 zw-g3 PlusOne Social Science Grade 3 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 112), teaches: Support services are organisations and people who help individuals facing challenges — counsellors (emotional and psychological support), community care workers (daily help for families in need), BEAM (Basic Education Assistance Module, school-fees support), police officers (order and safety) and health workers (medical help and health education).
Zimbabwe Grade 4 zw-g4 PlusOne Social Science Grade 4 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 140), teaches: Local social service providers improve quality of life and reduce poverty through education (including Kapota School for the Blind and Henry Murray School for the Deaf), transport (ZUPCO buses), guidance and counselling (such as Danhiko Children's Home) and feeding schemes (school feeding and church schemes such as the Methodist Church Feeding Scheme).

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 5) (teacher guide — evidence): rights teaching is concrete and local — support services are named institutions a child can find (BEAM school-fees support, community care workers, police, health workers), government services are named ministries and ZUPCO buses, and the needy are helped by named Zimbabwean homes and trusts (Harare Children's Home, Capernaum Trust, Angel of Hope Foundation).

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 6) (teacher guide — evidence): child rights are sourced, not just listed — the Constitution of Zimbabwe Section 81, the African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child and the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child; cultural beliefs are tested against those rights (FGM, child marriage and corporal punishment named as violations), and local welfare includes the traditional Zunde Ramambo / Isiphala Senkosi (the chief's granary) alongside councils, BEAM and church feeding schemes.
