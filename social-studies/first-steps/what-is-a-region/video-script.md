Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12* (printed pp. 19–21), teaches: Topic 12.2 (part): What a region is — the concept of a region and of an economic region, why regions matter, and the bases on which they form: internal factors (position, natural conditions and resources, people and society, state policy) and external ones (links with other regions and countries, globalisation).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12): the final-year electives turn homeward — the disasters Vietnam actually faces and how to live with them, how Vietnam itself is divided into regions for development, and the craft villages that carry its rural economy and culture — even printing the country's new 34-province table in force from 1 July 2025.
