# Video script — History In Popular Culture Film Games And Street Art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as North Macedonia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Braveheart's kilts never existed, Gladiator invents its emperor, Assassin's Creed rebuilds cities stone-perfect and invents the story inside them; Maus tells the Holocaust in mice and cats, Budapest's iron Shoes on the Danube say it without words — pop culture is a fine doorway into history and a dangerous place to stop.
