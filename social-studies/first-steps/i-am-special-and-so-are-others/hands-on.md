# Hands-on — I Am Special and So Are Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## I Am Special and So Are Others 1
Work through this part with your hands: Each child is distinct — the class discovers similarities and differences between children and learns to accept them; the unit's named values are respect, acceptance, pride in oneself and in the homeland. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## I Am Special and So Are Others 2
Work through this part with your hands: The child expresses her traits creatively, practises activities that show self-acceptance, and — the standard's own words — proposes advice to others to accept themselves; accepting one's own traits and respecting them is taught as the root of accepting others. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
