## Sub lesson 1 — I Am Special and So Are Others 1

## Sub lesson 2 — I Am Special and So Are Others 2

Oman Grade 2 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 29–40, teaches: The child expresses her traits creatively, practises activities that show self-acceptance, and — the standard's own words — proposes advice to others to accept themselves; accepting one's own traits and respecting them is taught as the root of accepting others. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: citizenship is its own school subject from age six, built as widening circles — the child first names herself, her feelings and her family, and only then her neighbourhood; the state prints its map and anthem at the front of the teacher's guide, and the fourth unit-component is always participation, not obedience. Grade 2 turns Grade 1's circles into verbs — accept, communicate, cooperate, consult, belong — and plants two purely Omani institutions in the child's civic world: the sablah, the village meeting hall where the community gathers, and the folk games of the neighbourhood. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
