# I Am Special and So Are Others — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Each child is distinct — the class discovers similarities and differences between children and learns to accept them; the unit's named values are respect, acceptance, pride in oneself and in the homeland.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: The child expresses her traits creatively, practises activities that show self-acceptance, and — the standard's own words — proposes advice to others to accept themselves; accepting one's own traits and respecting them is taught as the root of accepting others.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: True — Oman's student books were not held; it stands on teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere, as named in lesson/sources.md.**
