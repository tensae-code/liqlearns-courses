# Video script — My Feelings Naming and Handling Them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 1 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 38–44, teaches: Telling feelings apart, expressing them in the right way, and dealing with them appropriately — the guide's assessment watches that children listen to each other and respect each other while doing it.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 1, Term 1): citizenship is its own school subject from age six, built as widening circles — the child first names herself, her feelings and her family, and only then her neighbourhood; the state prints its map and anthem at the front of the teacher's guide, and the fourth unit-component is always participation, not obedience.
