# Video script — Our Flag and the 22 Bahman Celebration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 58, teaches: the class makes flags from coloured paper and sings the country's anthem in the yard; the principal explains that 22 Bahman is the day the Islamic Revolution of Iran was victorious; and the colours of Iran's flag — green, white and red.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
