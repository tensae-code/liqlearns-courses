# Hands-on — Our Flag and the 22 Bahman Celebration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the class makes flags from coloured paper and sings the country's anthem in the yard; the principal explains that 22 Bahman is the day the Islamic Revolution of Iran was victorious; and the colours of Iran's flag — green, white and red. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
