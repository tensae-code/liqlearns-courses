# Video script — Caring for Shared Places and Obeying the Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education G1 Term 3, printed p. 21, teaches: my city's cleanliness is everyone's responsibility; committing to applying the laws; keeping the park tidy and beautiful, using the places made for pedestrians, putting waste in its bins, and protecting my country's property as a national duty

United Arab Emirates' own way: at Grade 1 "order" is taught as one widening circle — home, then school, then neighbourhood — each part closing with a "Zayed taught me" saying and a named national value (respect, safety), and the country itself is introduced as a homeland of happiness and tolerance with its own Minister of Happiness and a Year of Tolerance.
