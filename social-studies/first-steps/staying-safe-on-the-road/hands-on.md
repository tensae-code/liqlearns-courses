# Hands-on — Staying Safe on the Road

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Speed limits, seatbelts, helmets, stopping at zebra crossings, using the pavement and looking both ways; the Hulhumalé roundabout. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Maldives's way
Say how Maldives's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
