**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Cambodia).**

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 1 Science–Social G1): Grade 1 social studies is the child's own circle — family first, taught through the family tree and the sampeah greeting wheel (a different respectful greeting posture for monks, the King, elders, parents, teachers and friends), then house, then the day at home and school. That way is stocked here as the way Cambodia teaches this thing.
