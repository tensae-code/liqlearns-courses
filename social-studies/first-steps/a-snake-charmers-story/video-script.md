India Grade 5 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Looking Around (EVS) (printed p. 11), teaches: the snake charmers' community — their music, their knowledge of snakes and herbs, and their trouble: the law now protects snakes, so the old work is disappearing.

India's own way: the values teaching is carried by real people's lives — hereditary cleaning work challenged by Gandhi and Ambedkar, a dam-displaced family in a Mumbai slum, sugarcane migrants whose children miss school — the child meets the republic's promises through its hardest cases.
