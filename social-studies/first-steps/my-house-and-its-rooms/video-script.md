# Video script — My House and Its Rooms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 22, teaches: houses are of different kinds — Sara lives on the fourth floor of a five-storey building, Saman in a one-storey house with a garden; and knowing the places of my own house — when Sara comes in, the bathroom is on her right and the kitchen on her left.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
