# Hands-on — My House and Its Rooms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: houses are of different kinds — Sara lives on the fourth floor of a five-storey building, Saman in a one-storey house with a garden; and knowing the places of my own house — when Sara comes in, the bathroom is on her right and the kitchen on her left. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
