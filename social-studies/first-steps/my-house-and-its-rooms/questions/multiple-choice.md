# My House and Its Rooms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) My House and Its Rooms  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) My House and Its Rooms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) houses are of different kinds — Sara lives on the fourth floor of a five-storey building, Saman in a one-storey house with a garden; and knowing the places of my own house — when Sara comes in, the bathroom is on her right and the kitchen on her left  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) houses are of different kinds — Sara lives on the fourth floor of a five-storey building, Saman in a one-storey house with a garden; and knowing the places of my own house — when Sara comes in, the bathroom is on her right and the kitchen on her left**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Iran's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) Iran's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches houses are of different kinds — Sara lives on the fourth floor of a five-storey building, Saman in a one-storey house with a garden.  b) Iran's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) This lesson teaches houses are of different kinds — Sara lives on the fourth floor of a five-storey building, Saman in a one-storey house with a garden.**
