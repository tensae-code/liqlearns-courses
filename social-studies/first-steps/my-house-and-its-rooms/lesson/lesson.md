# My House and Its Rooms

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: houses are of different kinds — Sara lives on the fourth floor of a five-storey building, Saman in a one-storey house with a garden; and knowing the places of my own house — when Sara comes in, the bathroom is on her right and the kitchen on her left — as Iran's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — My House and Its Rooms

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 22, teaches: houses are of different kinds — Sara lives on the fourth floor of a five-storey building, Saman in a one-storey house with a garden; and knowing the places of my own house — when Sara comes in, the bathroom is on her right and the kitchen on her left. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
