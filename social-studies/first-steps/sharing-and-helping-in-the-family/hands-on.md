# Hands-on — Sharing and Helping in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: noticing who needs help right now (elderly person, crying child, teacher with heavy books) and helping with kind words — picking up dropped crayons, carrying the book, guiding Gogo to a chair. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
