## Sub lesson 3 — Zimbabwe's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 1) (teacher guide — evidence): at ECD the civics ladder starts inside the family — rights are taught as things every child must have (name, food, love, play) and protection as a spoken routine ("No, Stop, Tell someone") with named community helpers (teacher, parent, police officer, doctor/nurse); care is practised on the family's own vulnerable members (baby, Gogo, child on crutches). ## Sub lesson 4 — Zimbabwe's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 2) (teacher guide — evidence): ECD B widens ECD A's family civics one ring — rights gain the right to identity (your name, clan and totem) and to clean water, responsibilities are framed as being a "model citizen" of the school, and sharing-and-helping is taught in three widening places: school, community, family.
