# Video script — Caring for Social Service Centres

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study (printed p. 35), teaches: How learners help care for shared service centres — no littering, no trespassing, careful use.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study): the district is the child's civic unit — its leaders (LCV chairperson, CAO, RDC, DPC, DEO, DAO) are named by title and role, and children's rights are taught straight from Uganda's laws (a child is anyone below 18).
