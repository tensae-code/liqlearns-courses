# Hands-on — Getting to School on Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: punctuality as a school rule — pack the bag the night before, set the alarm, eat breakfast and leave on time; the late child misses the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
