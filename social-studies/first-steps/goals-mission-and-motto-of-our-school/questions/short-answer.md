# Goals Mission and Motto of Our School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Goals of education, mission, philosophy and motto — goals of education are broad statements spelling out what the education system hopes to achieve — our national goals — from which each school derives its educational goals; to achieve them, schools are guided by the Mission Statement of the Ministry of Education: "to provide equal access to all Guyanese children and young people to quality education"; each school also has a philosophy — a shared vision or belief — its own goals for learners (self-identity, skills, effective decision-making, working in cooperative and competitive circumstances), and a motto, a short phrase giving clear guidelines for achieving them, such as "Strive for Character, Wisdom, Morals and Skills" or "To Know, To Love, To Serve" (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
