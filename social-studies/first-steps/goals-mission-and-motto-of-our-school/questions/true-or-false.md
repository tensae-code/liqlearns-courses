# Goals Mission and Motto of Our School — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Goals of education are broad statements spelling out what the education system hopes to achieve — our national goals — from which each school derives its educational goals; to achieve them, schools are guided by the Mission Statement of the Ministry of Education: "to provide equal access to all Guyanese children and young people to quality education"; each school also has a philosophy — a shared vision or belief — its own goals for learners (self-identity, skills, effective decision-making, working in cooperative and competitive circumstances), and a motto, a short phrase giving clear guidelines for achieving them, such as "Strive for Character, Wisdom, Morals and Skills" or "To Know, To Love, To Serve".
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
