# Hands-on — Goals Mission and Motto of Our School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Goals of education are broad statements spelling out what the education system hopes to achieve — our national goals — from which each school derives its educational goals; to achieve them, schools are guided by the Mission Statement of the Ministry of Education: "to provide equal access to all Guyanese children and young people to quality education"; each school also has a philosophy — a shared vision or belief — its own goals for learners (self-identity, skills, effective decision-making, working in cooperative and competitive circumstances), and a motto, a short phrase giving clear guidelines for achieving them, such as "Strive for Character, Wisdom, Morals and Skills" or "To Know, To Love, To Serve". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
