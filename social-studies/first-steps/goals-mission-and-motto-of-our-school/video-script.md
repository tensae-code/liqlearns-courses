# Video script — Goals Mission and Motto of Our School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 7 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 1 (printed pp. 23–25), teaches: Goals of education are broad statements spelling out what the education system hopes to achieve — our national goals — from which each school derives its educational goals; to achieve them, schools are guided by the Mission Statement of the Ministry of Education: "to provide equal access to all Guyanese children and young people to quality education"; each school also has a philosophy — a shared vision or belief — its own goals for learners (self-identity, skills, effective decision-making, working in cooperative and competitive circumstances), and a motto, a short phrase giving clear guidelines for achieving them, such as "Strive for Character, Wisdom, Morals and Skills" or "To Know, To Love, To Serve".

Guyana's own way: Book 1 opens secondary Social Studies where the primary books end — the child as citizen of nested communities (family, school, village or town, country), national symbols learned part by part with their meanings, and local government taught through the rates-and-taxes bargain: services exist because citizens pay for them and care for what they buy.
