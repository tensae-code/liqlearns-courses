## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 3: Family gatherings and aspirations

Zimbabwe, *PlusOne Social Science Grade 4 Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 7), teaches: Families gather for weddings, funerals, birthdays and memorial services; family aspirations are the hopes and goals a family works toward together — educational (all children completing school), financial (saving to build a home, starting a family business) and social (strong relationships with extended family and community). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Zimbabwe's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 6) (teacher guide — evidence): child rights are sourced, not just listed — the Constitution of Zimbabwe Section 81, the African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child and the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child; cultural beliefs are tested against those rights (FGM, child marriage and corporal punishment named as violations), and local welfare includes the traditional Zunde Ramambo / Isiphala Senkosi (the chief's granary) alongside councils, BEAM and church feeding schemes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
