# Hands-on — Family Gatherings and Family Aspirations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: families gather for weddings, funerals, birthdays and memorial services; family aspirations are the hopes and goals a family works toward together — educational (all children completing school), financial (saving to build a home, starting a family business) and social (strong relationships with extended family and community). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
