# Hands-on — How Communities Form

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how communities come to be — long ago beside rivers and the sea for farming, fishing and trade; today around a new factory, new housing, a market or a railway line, as shops, schools and clinics follow the people. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
