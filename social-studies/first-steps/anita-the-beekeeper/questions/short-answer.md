# Anita the Beekeeper — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch 5 Anita and the Honeybees — Anita of Muzzafarpur keeps bees as her work — she learned beekeeping, keeps boxes of bees and sells the honey; a girl can run her own work (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: India's way — the book's own teaching way — families change for named reasons — a baby arrives, a father's job transfers the family, a wedding joins two homes — and the law itself enters the child's world: the panchayat sends married girls back to school. (any close wording).**
