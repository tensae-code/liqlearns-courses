## Sub lesson 1 — The School Community

Venezuela Grade 5 G5 CS Venezuela y Su Gente, printed p. 126, teaches: First of all, our school — everyone who studies and works in a school, with mothers, fathers, representatives and the community organisations linked to it, forms a school community whose great purpose is to teach and learn to live together in society; living together is learned by living together, helping each other as a team or an orchestra does; and by the Organic Education Law schools organise student councils so pupils learn to participate as the citizens they are — ciudadano: a person who holds legal duties and rights in a nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: Grade 5's citizenship block is a complete primary civics course in one closing movement — citizenship, the school as the first community where citizens are formed (with student councils by law), needs as human rights, the constitution as fundamental law, the five public powers including Bolívar's own Citizen and Electoral powers, and the municipal power as the one closest to daily life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
