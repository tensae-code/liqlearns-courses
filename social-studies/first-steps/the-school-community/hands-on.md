# Hands-on — The School Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: First of all, our school — everyone who studies and works in a school, with mothers, fathers, representatives and the community organisations linked to it, forms a school community whose great purpose is to teach and learn to live together in society; living together is learned by living together, helping each other as a team or an orchestra does; and by the Organic Education Law schools organise student councils so pupils learn to participate as the citizens they are — ciudadano: a person who holds legal duties and rights in a nation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
