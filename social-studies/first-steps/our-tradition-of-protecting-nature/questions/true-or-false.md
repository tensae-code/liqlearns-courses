# Our Tradition of Protecting Nature — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: protecting nature is a whole system of custom that grew from the deep bond between the person and the nature they live by — customs for guarding the earth's soil, for mountains and waters, and ways and traditions for cherishing plants and wild animals; the legend of the Delger Mörön valley tells how a treasured landscape came to be.
