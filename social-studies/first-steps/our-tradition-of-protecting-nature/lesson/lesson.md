# Our Tradition of Protecting Nature

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: protecting nature is a whole system of custom that grew from the deep bond between the person and the nature they live by — customs for guarding the earth's soil, for mountains and waters, and ways and traditions for cherishing plants and wild animals; the legend of the Delger Mörön valley tells how a treasured landscape came to be — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 13 "From the traditional custom of protecting nature" (Байгалиа хамгаалах уламжлалт ёсноос)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 41, teaches: Protecting nature is a whole system of custom that grew from the deep bond between the person and the nature they live by — customs for guarding the earth's soil, for mountains and waters, and ways and traditions for cherishing plants and wild animals; the legend of the Delger Mörön valley tells how a treasured landscape came to be. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
