# Hands-on — Our Tradition of Protecting Nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 13 "From the traditional custom of protecting nature" (Байгалиа хамгаалах уламжлалт ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Protecting nature is a whole system of custom that grew from the deep bond between the person and the nature they live by — customs for guarding the earth's soil, for mountains and waters, and ways and traditions for cherishing plants and wild animals; the legend of the Delger Mörön valley tells how a treasured landscape came to be. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
