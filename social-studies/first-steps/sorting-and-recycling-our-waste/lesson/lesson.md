# Sorting and Recycling Our Waste

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: recycling turns used material into new products — sort waste and never mix it: paper, glass, metal and plastic each into its own container, so less reaches the dump that poisons soil, water and air; protecting the environment is everyone's duty, and small habits help — both sides of the paper, a cloth bag for shopping, reusing jars and bags — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Рециклажа (Recycling)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 118–119), teaches: recycling turns used material into new products — sort waste and never mix it: paper, glass, metal and plastic each into its own container, so less reaches the dump that poisons soil, water and air; protecting the environment is everyone's duty, and small habits help — both sides of the paper, a cloth bag for shopping, reusing jars and bags. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
