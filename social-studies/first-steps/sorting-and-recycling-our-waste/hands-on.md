# Hands-on — Sorting and Recycling Our Waste

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Рециклажа (Recycling)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: recycling turns used material into new products — sort waste and never mix it: paper, glass, metal and plastic each into its own container, so less reaches the dump that poisons soil, water and air; protecting the environment is everyone's duty, and small habits help — both sides of the paper, a cloth bag for shopping, reusing jars and bags. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
