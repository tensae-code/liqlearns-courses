# Sorting and Recycling Our Waste — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Рециклажа (Recycling) — recycling turns used material into new products — sort waste and never mix it: paper, glass, metal and plastic each into its own container, so less reaches the dump that poisons soil, water and air; protecting the environment is everyone's duty, and small habits help — both sides of the paper, a cloth bag for shopping, reusing jars and bags (any close wording).**
