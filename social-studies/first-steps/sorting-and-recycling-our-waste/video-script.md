Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 118–119), teaches: Recycling turns used material into new products — sort waste and never mix it: paper, glass, metal and plastic each into its own container, so less reaches the dump that poisons soil, water and air; protecting the environment is everyone's duty, and small habits help — both sides of the paper, a cloth bag for shopping, reusing jars and bags.

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
