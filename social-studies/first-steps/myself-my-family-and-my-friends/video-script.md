# Video script — Myself My Family and My Friends

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe ECD A zw-ppp-ecda Best Approach to Family and Heritage Studies Teacher's Resource Book ECD 'A' (printed p. 1), teaches: Naming yourself and your gender, identifying family members, and talking about your friends — the child's first social map, taught through the naming game 'De de dzengera uyo ndiani?'.
Zimbabwe Grade 2 zw-ventures-g2 Ventures Primary Family and Heritage Studies Learner's Book Grade 2 (printed pp. 1–4), teaches: People live in a family to help one another, work together, share ideas and learn to care and love; relatives are the extended family (grandparents, aunts, uncles) shown on a family tree, who help in sickness and death and celebrate at weddings.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 10) (teacher guide — evidence): a second publisher's ECD A book for the same syllabus, laid out as weekly lesson grids (objective / skill / activities / media); its heritage unit teaches culture through languages, attire, food and truth-telling songs, and its rights unit is built almost entirely on recognising and dramatising abuse and being assured help exists.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 12): the learner's-book voice of the same syllabus — the community is "like a big family" that meets at the dare, shares the tap, sports grounds and clinics, and feeds its elderly, orphans and poor from the chief's own field (zunde ramambo/isiphal senkosi); children's roles are taught as changed by equality — in the past cooking was girls' work and herding boys', now both do the same roles.
