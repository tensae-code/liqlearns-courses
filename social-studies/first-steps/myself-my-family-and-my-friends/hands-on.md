# Hands-on — Myself My Family and My Friends

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: naming yourself and your gender, identifying family members, and talking about your friends — the child's first social map, taught through the naming game 'De de dzengera uyo ndiani?'. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
