# Hands-on — Caring for the Place Where I Live

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Caring for the Place Where I Live
Work through this part with your hands: responsibility as being an agent of change — citizen participation in decisions, and concrete care actions set out separately for home, school and community (saving water, sorting rubbish, compost, reusable containers, bus or bicycle, responsible consumption). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mexico's way
Say how Mexico's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
