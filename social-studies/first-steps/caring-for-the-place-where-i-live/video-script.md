# Video script — Caring for the Place Where I Live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mexico Grade 7 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 1 (secundaria) (printed pp. 132–134), teaches: Responsibility as being an agent of change — citizen participation in decisions, and concrete care actions set out separately for home, school and community (saving water, sorting rubbish, compost, reusable containers, bus or bicycle, responsible consumption).

---

Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution.
