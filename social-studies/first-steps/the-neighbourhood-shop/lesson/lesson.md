## Sub lesson 1 — The Neighbourhood Shop

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 70, teaches: at the shop we take a basket, choose the goods (کالا — a thing that meets our needs) we need, and pay at the till, keeping our place in the queue; the seller (فروشنده) serves us by selling the goods; and the purchases go into cloth bags, not plastic ones. ## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
