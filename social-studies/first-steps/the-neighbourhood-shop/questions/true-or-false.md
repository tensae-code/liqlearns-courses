# The Neighbourhood Shop — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: at the shop we take a basket, choose the goods (کالا — a thing that meets our needs) we need, and pay at the till, keeping our place in the queue; the seller (فروشنده) serves us by selling the goods; and the purchases go into cloth bags, not plastic ones.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iran's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
