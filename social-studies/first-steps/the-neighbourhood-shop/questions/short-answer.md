# The Neighbourhood Shop — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Neighbourhood Shop — at the shop we take a basket, choose the goods (کالا — a thing that meets our needs) we need, and pay at the till, keeping our place in the queue; the seller (فروشنده) serves us by selling the goods; and the purchases go into cloth bags, not plastic ones (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Iran's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
