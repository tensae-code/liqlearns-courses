# Video script — The Neighbourhood Shop

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 3 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 70, teaches: at the shop we take a basket, choose the goods (کالا — a thing that meets our needs) we need, and pay at the till, keeping our place in the queue; the seller (فروشنده) serves us by selling the goods; and the purchases go into cloth bags, not plastic ones.

Iran's own way: the special-needs track teaches the same social world as the regular book but rebuilt in one-sentence pages through two named children, Sara and Saman — self, family, school, neighbourhood — with every rule and every act of cooperation stated as a single pictured example.
