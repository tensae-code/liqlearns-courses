# Hands-on — The Neighbourhood Shop

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: at the shop we take a basket, choose the goods (کالا — a thing that meets our needs) we need, and pay at the till, keeping our place in the queue; the seller (فروشنده) serves us by selling the goods; and the purchases go into cloth bags, not plastic ones. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
