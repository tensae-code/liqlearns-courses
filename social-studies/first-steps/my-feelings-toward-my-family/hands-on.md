# Hands-on — My Feelings Toward My Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The most important feelings I should feel toward my family are love, happiness, joy and pride, and I express my feelings toward my family: Sara is proud of her mother the nurse, whose work — treating the sick and staying up for their comfort — is called the work of the angels of mercy, and proud of her father, who took his wife's place in caring for the children during her absence at the hospital; my presence inside the family is of great importance; every individual in the family has a profession he should be proud of; and responsibility inside the family is shared and exchanged — there are no works special to one member without the other. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
