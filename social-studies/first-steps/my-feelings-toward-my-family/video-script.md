# Video script — My Feelings Toward My Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 1 Moral Education (printed pp. 61–65), teaches: The most important feelings I should feel toward my family are love, happiness, joy and pride, and I express my feelings toward my family: Sara is proud of her mother the nurse, whose work — treating the sick and staying up for their comfort — is called the work of the angels of mercy, and proud of her father, who took his wife's place in caring for the children during her absence at the hospital; my presence inside the family is of great importance; every individual in the family has a profession he should be proud of; and responsibility inside the family is shared and exchanged — there are no works special to one member without the other.

Iraq's own way: moral education is a separate subject from Grade 1, taught entirely as stories about five named children (Muhammad, Yusuf, Zahraa, Lana, Sara) whose small school and family incidents carry each value; every lesson ends in the child's own voice — "What I learned" — and the second unit's family tree is drawn with the father's help, branch by branch, until the big family shades them all.
