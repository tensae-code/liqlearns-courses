# Hands-on — Life Skills at Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Hamid works in a confectionery and his cousin Reza in a food shop — why do they work and have jobs?; each of us will have a job in the future; the pupil names the jobs of family members and relatives and says which job they would like. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
