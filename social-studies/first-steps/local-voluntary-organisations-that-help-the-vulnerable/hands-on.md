# Hands-on — Local Voluntary Organisations That Help the Vulnerable

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a local voluntary organisation is a community group helping people in need without payment (food banks, homeless shelters, children's homes, disability support, women's empowerment); they give food, shelter, health care and clothing, teach life and job skills, counsel, and advocate for orphans, disabled people, widows, the homeless and the very poor. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
