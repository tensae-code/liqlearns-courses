# Video script — Local Voluntary Organisations That Help the Vulnerable

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe Grade 5 zw-g5 PlusOne Social Science Grade 5 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 141), teaches: A local voluntary organisation is a community group helping people in need without payment (food banks, homeless shelters, children's homes, disability support, women's empowerment); they give food, shelter, health care and clothing, teach life and job skills, counsel, and advocate for orphans, disabled people, widows, the homeless and the very poor.
Zimbabwe Grade 7 zw-g7 PlusOne Social Science Grade 7 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 122), teaches: Zimbabwe's named voluntary and humanitarian organisations and what each does — Red Cross (emergencies, First Aid, food), Jairos Jiri Association (disability education and inclusion), Childline (child-protection helpline), National AIDS Council, Disabled Persons Association, UNICEF Zimbabwe and World Food Programme.
Zimbabwe Grade 7 zw-ppp-g7 Best Approach to Heritage-Social Studies Teacher's Resource Book Grade 7 (printed p. 38), teaches: The organisations that offer social services in Zimbabwe, including the UN family (UNICEF, UNFPA, UNHCR named and expanded).

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 7) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 5 civics runs on two ladders at once — the traditional ladder (village head → headman → chief, with the councillor bridging into the state) and the citizen ladder (rights matched one-for-one with responsibilities: "every right has a matching responsibility"), with Zimbabwe-specific protectors named (Police Victim-Friendly Unit, village health workers, social workers).

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 9) (teacher guide — evidence): the primary cycle closes with adult law taught straight — inheritance under the Deceased Estates Succession Act with the Master of the High Court role-played in class, and gender equity taught through Zimbabwe's own institutions (Zimbabwe Gender Commission, Msasa Project, Women's Bank, community savings groups) with Constitution excerpts in the materials list.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 11) (teacher guide — evidence): the parallel Grade 7 book adds what the PlusOne guide does not teach — elections themselves (forms of elections, the electoral process, role-played class elections for class monitor) — and teaches international organisations as a table of UN agencies with their functions and years of formation.
