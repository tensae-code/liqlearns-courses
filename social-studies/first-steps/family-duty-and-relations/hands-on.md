# Hands-on — Family Duty and Relations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Xilka iyo xiriirka Qoyska (The Duty and Relations of the Family)
Work through this part with your hands: what the family owes and how its members relate — parents, children and kin bound by duty and care. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
