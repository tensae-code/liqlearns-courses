# Family Duty and Relations

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what the family owes and how its members relate — parents, children and kin bound by duty and care — as Somalia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Xilka iyo xiriirka Qoyska (The Duty and Relations of the Family)

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 52), teaches: what the family owes and how its members relate — parents, children and kin bound by duty and care. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
