# Video script — We Care About the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Greece Grade 3 Meleti Perivallontos G' Dimotikou (printed p. 17), teaches: Taking an interest in the community's problems and helping with them.

Greece's own way: Grade 3's opening unit is the primary civics core of the whole EVS series — living together needs rules, the community is cared for, and the child learns who decides by following municipal elections in their own dimos.
