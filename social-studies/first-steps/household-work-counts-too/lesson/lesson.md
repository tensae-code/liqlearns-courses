## Sub lesson 1 — Ch 12 Work We Do (Deepali part)

India Grade 3 EVS Looking Around (printed p. 73), teaches: household work is real work — Deepali left school to run her home, and unpaid work at home deserves the same respect as paid work outside. ## Sub lesson 2 — India's way — the book's own teaching way

India's own way: the child's own family is the first school — relations, pet names, family work and respect for elders are learned at home before any classroom; and every neighbourhood trade, from dhobi to traffic policeman, is shown as work of equal worth.
