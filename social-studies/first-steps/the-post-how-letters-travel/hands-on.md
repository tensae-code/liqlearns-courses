# Hands-on — The Post How Letters Travel

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the post office as a public service — letters, books and parcels are sorted by address and carried to faraway people; writing sender and receiver addresses and the postal code on an envelope; email as the newer way. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
