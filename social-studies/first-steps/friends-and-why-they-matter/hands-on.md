# Hands-on — Friends and Why They Matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Who is your friend and why friends matter
Work through this part with your hands: A friend is someone we play, share and go places with and look out for; people need friends beyond family, and good friends bring happiness. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
