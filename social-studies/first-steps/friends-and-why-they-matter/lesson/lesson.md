# Friends and Why They Matter

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a friend is someone we play, share and go places with and look out for; people need friends beyond family, and good friends bring happiness — as Fiji's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who is your friend and why friends matter

Fiji Grade 4 Social Studies Year 4 (printed p. 4), teaches: A friend is someone we play, share and go places with and look out for; people need friends beyond family, and good friends bring happiness. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
