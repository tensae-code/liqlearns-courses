# Hands-on — Learning to Live Together Convivencia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 10 Aprendamos a convivir
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 56–62, teaches: convivir is living with others; in family, school, neighbourhood and work, interactions bring tensions, which are normal, and living together is learned — the school is a space of socialisation where social norms, behaviour guidelines and values are explained. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
