# Video script — Learning to Live Together Convivencia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 56–62, teaches: convivir is living with others; in family, school, neighbourhood and work, interactions bring tensions, which are normal, and living together is learned — the school is a space of socialisation where social norms, behaviour guidelines and values are explained.

Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own.
