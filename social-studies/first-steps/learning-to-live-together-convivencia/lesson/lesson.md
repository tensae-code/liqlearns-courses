## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 10 Aprendamos a convivir

Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 56–62, teaches: convivir is living with others; in family, school, neighbourhood and work, interactions bring tensions, which are normal, and living together is learned — the school is a space of socialisation where social norms, behaviour guidelines and values are explained. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
