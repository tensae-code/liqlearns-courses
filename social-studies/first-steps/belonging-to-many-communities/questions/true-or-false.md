# Belonging to Many Communities — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: A child belongs to many communities at once — the family community, the school community, religious communities (churches, mandirs, mosques), sports communities and business communities.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: A worked story of one child, Hannah, who belongs at the same time to her family, her school and her church communities, and what she does in each.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: A person can belong to many groups at the same time — Adam is in the school choir, the cricket team and the Red Cross — and belonging brings help for one another, confidence, friends and learning to help people in need.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
