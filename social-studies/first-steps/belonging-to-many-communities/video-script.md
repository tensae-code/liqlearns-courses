# Video script — Belonging to Many Communities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 2 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 2–5), teaches: A child belongs to many communities at once — the family community, the school community, religious communities (churches, mandirs, mosques), sports communities and business communities.

Guyana Grade 2 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 11–13), teaches: A worked story of one child, Hannah, who belongs at the same time to her family, her school and her church communities, and what she does in each.

Guyana Grade 1 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 24–27), teaches: A person can belong to many groups at the same time — Adam is in the school choir, the cricket team and the Red Cross — and belonging brings help for one another, confidence, friends and learning to help people in need.

Guyana's own way: the Grade 2 book of the new series starts citizenship at the child's own doorstep — the many communities a child belongs to at once, the councils that serve them, the peoples and festivals that fill them, then decisions, conflict and rights, all before any map or market is taught. the series opens at Grade 1 inside the home — the family that cooks and gardens together, the chores shared out fairly, the groups a small child already belongs to, and rules taught as the way a home and a classroom stay in harmony.
