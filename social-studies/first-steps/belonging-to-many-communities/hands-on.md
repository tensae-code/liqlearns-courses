# Hands-on — Belonging to Many Communities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A child belongs to many communities at once — the family community, the school community, religious communities (churches, mandirs, mosques), sports communities and business communities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A worked story of one child, Hannah, who belongs at the same time to her family, her school and her church communities, and what she does in each. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A person can belong to many groups at the same time — Adam is in the school choir, the cricket team and the Red Cross — and belonging brings help for one another, confidence, friends and learning to help people in need. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
