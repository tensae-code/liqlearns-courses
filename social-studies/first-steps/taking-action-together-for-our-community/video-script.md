# Video script — Taking Action Together for Our Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Solomon Islands' Years 5–8 ESD Teacher Guide (printed pp. 3–4), teaches: The action-learning cycle — pupils identify a sustainability issue in their own community, explore the values and perspectives in it, engage the community, create a shared vision, decide together with a decision-making grid (options include a community poster, lobbying the government for a ban, a beach clean-up), plan, act and reflect — deciding and acting together, not being taken to do it.

Solomon Islands' own way: the guide prints country curriculum notes for Niue, Tonga and Tuvalu only — there is NO Solomon Islands notes section, so Solomon Islands' placement is the guide's shared Pacific teaching (the shared readers' teaching), honestly filed as such; the Solomon-specific grounding is left to the teacher and the Solomon Islands advisors named in the credits (Linda Pua and Grace Zimateko). The guide teaches through Pacific philosophy — head, heart and hands — with elders and the community as co-teachers.
