# Hands-on — Taking Action Together for Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The action-learning cycle — pupils identify a sustainability issue in their own community, explore the values and perspectives in it, engage the community, create a shared vision, decide together with a decision-making grid (options include a community poster, lobbying the government for a ban, a beach clean-up), plan, act and reflect — deciding and acting together, not being taken to do it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Solomon Islands' way
