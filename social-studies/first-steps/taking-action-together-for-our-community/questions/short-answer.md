# Taking Action Together for Our Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Taking Action Together for Our Community — The action-learning cycle — pupils identify a sustainability issue in their own community, explore the values and perspectives in it, engage the community, create a shared vision, decide together with a decision-making grid (options include a community poster, lobbying the government for a ban, a beach clean-up), plan, act and reflect — deciding and acting together, not being taken to do it (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Solomon Islands' way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
