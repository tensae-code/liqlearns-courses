# Hands-on — Long Journeys by Train

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ch 6 Omana's Journey
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a long train journey joins far places — Omana travels from Kerala towards Gujarat, watching stations, vendors and changing country roll past. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ch 8 Reaching Grandmother's House
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: reaching grandmother takes many vehicles in a row — bus, train, boat and walking — a journey is planned leg by leg. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
