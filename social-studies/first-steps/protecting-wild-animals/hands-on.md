# Hands-on — Protecting Wild Animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 15 "From the traditional custom of cherishing wild animals" (Ан амьтныг хайрлан хамгаалах уламжлалт ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The tradition is to let wild animals live and multiply and to take them only for real need; birds must not be frightened off their eggs ("a bird cannot fold its young away — do not make it abandon its nest"); the hunting bans: no hunting when animals are fattening or weak by season, no pregnant animal or one followed by young, no young male of the herd; birds and fish, heaven's and water's creatures, were not hunted at all, and game taken for medicine was asked permission of the mountain — set against the two hunters of Lodoidamba's story "The Marmot Hunter", the one who cherishes his rich khangai and the one who only shoots. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
