# Video script — Protecting Wild Animals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 44, teaches: The tradition is to let wild animals live and multiply and to take them only for real need; birds must not be frightened off their eggs ("a bird cannot fold its young away — do not make it abandon its nest"); the hunting bans: no hunting when animals are fattening or weak by season, no pregnant animal or one followed by young, no young male of the herd; birds and fish, heaven's and water's creatures, were not hunted at all, and game taken for medicine was asked permission of the mountain — set against the two hunters of Lodoidamba's story "The Marmot Hunter", the one who cherishes his rich khangai and the one who only shoots.

Mongolia's own way (Book 4): the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other.
