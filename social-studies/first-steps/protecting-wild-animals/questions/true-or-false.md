# Protecting Wild Animals — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the tradition is to let wild animals live and multiply and to take them only for real need; birds must not be frightened off their eggs ("a bird cannot fold its young away — do not make it abandon its nest"); the hunting bans: no hunting when animals are fattening or weak by season, no pregnant animal or one followed by young, no young male of the herd; birds and fish, heaven's and water's creatures, were not hunted at all, and game taken for medicine was asked permission of the mountain — set against the two hunters of Lodoidamba's story "The Marmot Hunter", the one who cherishes his rich khangai and the one who only shoots.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
