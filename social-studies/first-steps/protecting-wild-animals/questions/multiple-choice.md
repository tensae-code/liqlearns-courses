# Protecting Wild Animals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 15 "From the traditional custom of cherishing wild animals" (Ан амьтныг хайрлан хамгаалах уламжлалт ёсноос)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 15 "From the traditional custom of cherishing wild animals" (Ан амьтныг хайрлан хамгаалах уламжлалт ёсноос)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) The tradition is to let wild animals live and multiply and to take them only for real need; birds must not be frightened off their eggs ("a bird cannot fold its young away — do not make it abandon its nest"); the hunting bans: no hunting when animals are fattening or weak by season, no pregnant animal or one followed by young, no young male of the herd; birds and fish, heaven's and water's creatures, were not hunted at all, and game taken for medicine was asked permission of the mountain — set against the two hunters of Lodoidamba's story "The Marmot Hunter", the one who cherishes his rich khangai and the one who only shoots  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) The tradition is to let wild animals live and multiply and to take them only for real need; birds must not be frightened off their eggs ("a bird cannot fold its young away — do not make it abandon its nest"); the hunting bans: no hunting when animals are fattening or weak by season, no pregnant animal or one followed by young, no young male of the herd; birds and fish, heaven's and water's creatures, were not hunted at all, and game taken for medicine was asked permission of the mountain — set against the two hunters of Lodoidamba's story "The Marmot Hunter", the one who cherishes his rich khangai and the one who only shoots**
