Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 14, teaches: Right habits (зөв хэвшл) against wrong habits (буруу зуршил) — reading, helping and being at peace with friends against idling, wasting time, lying and screen addiction; the most dangerous harmful habit is drink — the old teaching measures it by age ("taste at forty, sip at fifty, drink at sixty"), and Chinggis Khan's law punished the drunk official, soldier and commoner by degrees.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
