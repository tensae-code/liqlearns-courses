# Hands-on — Right Habits And Harmful Habits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 4 "Let's avoid harmful habits" (Хорт зуршлыг тэвчье)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Right habits (зөв хэвшл) against wrong habits (буруу зуршил) — reading, helping and being at peace with friends against idling, wasting time, lying and screen addiction; the most dangerous harmful habit is drink — the old teaching measures it by age ("taste at forty, sip at fifty, drink at sixty"), and Chinggis Khan's law punished the drunk official, soldier and commoner by degrees. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
