# Hands-on — How a Family is Formed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 1 Lesson 1 The Family and How It Is Formed
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what a family is, the small and the extended Yemeni family, and how a family is formed through marriage, with the family's official documents (identity card, birth and death certificates). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Yemen's way
Say how Yemen's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
