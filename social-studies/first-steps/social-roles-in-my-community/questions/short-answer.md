# Social Roles in My Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guía 10 Los roles sociales — Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 63–87, teaches: everyone assumes social roles, each with expected behaviours, ideals and norms (child, parent, teacher, community workers); roles relate to each other and carry social responsibility — what happens if family members stop fulfilling theirs; women's roles have changed (owning property, voting, public office without social censure). **2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way — Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own.
