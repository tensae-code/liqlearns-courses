# Video script — Social Roles in My Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 63–87, teaches: everyone assumes social roles, each with expected behaviours, ideals and norms (child, parent, teacher, community workers); roles relate to each other and carry social responsibility — what happens if family members stop fulfilling theirs; women's roles have changed (owning property, voting, public office without social censure).

Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own.
