# Hands-on — Social Roles in My Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 10 Los roles sociales
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 63–87, teaches: everyone assumes social roles, each with expected behaviours, ideals and norms (child, parent, teacher, community workers); roles relate to each other and carry social responsibility — what happens if family members stop fulfilling theirs; women's roles have changed (owning property, voting, public office without social censure). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
