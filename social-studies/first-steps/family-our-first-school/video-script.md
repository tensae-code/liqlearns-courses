# Video script — Family Our First School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India Grade 3 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Looking Around (EVS) (printed p. 25), teaches: the family is our first school — who is in it, how members are related, what we learn from each of them, family practices and respect for elders.

India's own way: the child's own family is the first school — relations, pet names, family work and respect for elders are learned at home before any classroom; and every neighbourhood trade, from dhobi to traffic policeman, is shown as work of equal worth.
