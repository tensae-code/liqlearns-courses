# Hands-on — How Geographic Thought Grew

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lição 4: Evolução do Pensamento Geográfico (Greek antiquity; Aristotle's geocentrism and Earth's sphericity; Eratosthenes; Ptolemy; institutionalisation 1870); Lição 5: A Geografia na Idade Média (T-O world picture, three continents; Scandinavian expeditions, Marco Polo); Lição 6: A Geografia no Renascimento (cartography advances, Gutenberg press 1440, Columbus 1492 and the great voyages); Lição 7: As Missões Geográficas dos Séculos XVIII e XIX (Industrial Revolution and imperialism as drivers; Humboldt and Ritter; Paris Geographical Society 1821). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
