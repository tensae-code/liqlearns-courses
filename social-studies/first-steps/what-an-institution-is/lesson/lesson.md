## Sub lesson 1 — What an Institution is

Iran Social Studies Grade 10 (special needs, Kardanesh) (student book, Persian), printed p. 31, teaches: in past times most needs were met inside the family, but as families multiplied and cities and villages came into being, people met new needs and problems — so institutions (مؤسسه) arose to meet those needs and solve the problems of people's lives: once, fathers taught their own children their trade; today institutions carry the duty of teaching; for the sick we go to medical institutions — the clinic and the hospital; and because today's institutions are many and their services differ, we must know them, so that for each need we go to the right one — the bank, the fire service, the bus company, and the matching exercise: the post delivers letters and parcels, the traffic police enforce the traffic rules, rehabilitation services, electricity for homes, telephone calls, television programmes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs secondary book is a citizen's primer in the plainest form — needs first (things, then love and company), then the circles that meet them: family, groups, institutions — and at the top, government and the Majlis, explained through a ship and its captain and a school and its rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
