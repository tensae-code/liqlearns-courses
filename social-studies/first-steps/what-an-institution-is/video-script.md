# Video script — What an Institution is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 10 (special needs, Kardanesh) (student book, Persian), printed p. 31, teaches: in past times most needs were met inside the family, but as families multiplied and cities and villages came into being, people met new needs and problems — so institutions (مؤسسه) arose to meet those needs and solve the problems of people's lives: once, fathers taught their own children their trade; today institutions carry the duty of teaching; for the sick we go to medical institutions — the clinic and the hospital; and because today's institutions are many and their services differ, we must know them, so that for each need we go to the right one — the bank, the fire service, the bus company, and the matching exercise: the post delivers letters and parcels, the traffic police enforce the traffic rules, rehabilitation services, electricity for homes, telephone calls, television programmes.

Iran's own way: the special-needs secondary book is a citizen's primer in the plainest form — needs first (things, then love and company), then the circles that meet them: family, groups, institutions — and at the top, government and the Majlis, explained through a ship and its captain and a school and its rules.
