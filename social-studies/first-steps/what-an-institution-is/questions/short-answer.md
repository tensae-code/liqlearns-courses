# What an Institution is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: What an Institution is — in past times most needs were met inside the family, but as families multiplied and cities and villages came into being, people met new needs and problems — so institutions (مؤسسه) arose to meet those needs and solve the problems of people's lives: once, fathers taught their own children their trade; today institutions carry the duty of teaching; for the sick we go to medical institutions — the clinic and the hospital; and because today's institutions are many and their services differ, we must know them, so that for each need we go to the right one — the bank, the fire service, the bus company, and the matching exercise: the post delivers letters and parcels, the traffic police enforce the traffic rules, rehabilitation services, electricity for homes, telephone calls, television programmes (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Iran's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
