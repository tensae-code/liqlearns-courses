# Hands-on — What an Institution is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: in past times most needs were met inside the family, but as families multiplied and cities and villages came into being, people met new needs and problems — so institutions (مؤسسه) arose to meet those needs and solve the problems of people's lives: once, fathers taught their own children their trade; today institutions carry the duty of teaching; for the sick we go to medical institutions — the clinic and the hospital; and because today's institutions are many and their services differ, we must know them, so that for each need we go to the right one — the bank, the fire service, the bus company, and the matching exercise: the post delivers letters and parcels, the traffic police enforce the traffic rules, rehabilitation services, electricity for homes, telephone calls, television programmes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
