## Sub lesson 1 — How Social Relations Change

Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2 (printed p. 53), teaches: village relations were strong because people knew and needed each other, and relations thinned as population grew and work multiplied, in village and city alike; they are strengthened again by visiting, sharing social occasions, and using the development of transport, communications and social media. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way

Palestine's own way (Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2): civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
