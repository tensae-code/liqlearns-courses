# Hands-on — How Social Relations Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## How Social Relations Change
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Village relations were strong because people knew and needed each other, and relations thinned as population grew and work multiplied, in village and city alike; they are strengthened again by visiting, sharing social occasions, and using the development of transport, communications and social media. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
