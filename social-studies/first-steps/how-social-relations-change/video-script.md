# Video script — How Social Relations Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2, printed p. 53, teaches: village relations were strong because people knew and needed each other, and relations thinned as population grew and work multiplied, in village and city alike; they are strengthened again by visiting, sharing social occasions, and using the development of transport, communications and social media.

Palestine's own way: civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom.
