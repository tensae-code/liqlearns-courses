# Saying Without Speaking — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch 7 Saying without Speaking — people who cannot hear or speak still communicate — through signs, expressions and actions — and are part of the community like everyone else (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: India's way — the book's own teaching way — the child's own family is the first school — relations, pet names, family work and respect for elders are learned at home before any classroom; and every neighbourhood trade, from dhobi to traffic policeman, is shown as work of equal worth. (any close wording).**
