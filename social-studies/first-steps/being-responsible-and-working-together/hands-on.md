# Hands-on — Being Responsible And Working Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 25 "Why be responsible and work together" (Хариуцлагатай байж, хамтран ажиллахын учир)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: No one lives alone — we depend on each other as passengers depend on the driver and the bank's customers on its clerks, so each person consciously doing their own duty is how everyone lives in peace; failing a duty brings its fruits — fines, lost trust, others refusing to deal with you — while doing it brings reward and pride; and decisions are made together: in the team-leader's guide, know each member, hear everyone one by one, weigh every opinion, decide by discussion and let the decision be fair — with the story of class chair Khaliun, whose meeting went wrong when proposals were not heard. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
