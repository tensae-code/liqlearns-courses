# Being Responsible And Working Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 25 "Why be responsible and work together" (Хариуцлагатай байж, хамтран ажиллахын учир) — No one lives alone — we depend on each other as passengers depend on the driver and the bank's customers on its clerks, so each person consciously doing their own duty is how everyone lives in peace; failing a duty brings its fruits — fines, lost trust, others refusing to deal with you — while doing it brings reward and pride; and decisions are made together: in the team-leader's guide, know each member, hear everyone one by one, weigh every opinion, decide by discussion and let the decision be fair — with the story of class chair Khaliun, whose meeting went wrong when proposals were not heard (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other (any close wording).**
