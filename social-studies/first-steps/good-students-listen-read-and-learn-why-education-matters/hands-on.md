# Hands-on — Good Students Listen Read and Learn Why Education Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: listening to teachers and classroom rules, the good reader who reads many kinds of books, and why education matters — health, science, behaviour, success and wisdom, in pupils' own voices and a model school day. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
