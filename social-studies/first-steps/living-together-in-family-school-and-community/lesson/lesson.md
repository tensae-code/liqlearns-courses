## Sub lesson 1 — Experience 2, "Indagación familiar y comunitaria: ¿Cómo convivimos en la comunidad?" and "Un domingo familiar" dramatisation

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales Segundo ciclo TG, printed pp. 50–54, teaches: pupils interview family, school and community members on the four threads of coexistence (respect, gratitude, service, care of nature) and act out a family Sunday to see values in daily life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way

Guatemala's own way: coexistence is taught from the Maya cosmovision — its four threads are respect, gratitude, service and care for nature — and classroom rules are not imposed but built by the pupils as agreements.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
