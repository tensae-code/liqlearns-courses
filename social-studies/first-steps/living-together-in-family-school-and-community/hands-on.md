# Hands-on — Living Together in Family School and Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 2, "Indagación familiar y comunitaria: ¿Cómo convivimos en la comunidad?" and "Un domingo familiar" dramatisation
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales Segundo ciclo TG, printed pp. 50–54, teaches: pupils interview family, school and community members on the four threads of coexistence (respect, gratitude, service, care of nature) and act out a family Sunday to see values in daily life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: coexistence is taught from the Maya cosmovision — its four threads are respect, gratitude, service and care for nature — and classroom rules are not imposed but built by the pupils as agreements.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
