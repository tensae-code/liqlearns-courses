# Video script — Living Together in Family School and Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales Segundo ciclo TG, printed pp. 50–54, teaches: pupils interview family, school and community members on the four threads of coexistence (respect, gratitude, service, care of nature) and act out a family Sunday to see values in daily life.

Guatemala's own way: coexistence is taught from the Maya cosmovision — its four threads are respect, gratitude, service and care for nature — and classroom rules are not imposed but built by the pupils as agreements.
