# Video script — From stone tools to farming — the Neolithic Revolution

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Open on a band at dawn. Spears. The hunt. Palaeolithic — taking what the land gives.
Seasons turn. Camps last longer. Mesolithic — the long middle.
Then — hands in soil. Seeds. A field that stays. A clay pot by a settled hearth.
Teff rises green in Ethiopia, seven thousand years ago. The Neolithic Revolution has reached the highlands.
And where fields stay, villages stay — and history's next chapter, the state, is waiting.
