# From stone tools to farming — the Neolithic Revolution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The earliest tools were made of —
a) iron  b) stone  c) bronze
**Answer: b) stone** — Tool-making begins with stone.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Palaeolithic people lived by —
a) farming  b) hunting and gathering  c) trade
**Answer: b) hunting and gathering** — Taking what the land offered.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide adds that Palaeolithic people discovered — and began to use —.
a) pottery  b) fire, language  c) the plough
**Answer: b) fire, language** — Fire and language (teacher guide — evidence).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Neolithic Revolution is —
a) a war  b) the domestication of plants and animals and the start of settled farming  c) the invention of writing
**Answer: b) the domestication of plants and animals and the start of settled farming** — Producing instead of only taking.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's Neolithic is placed about —
a) 500 years ago  b) 7,000 years ago  c) 100,000 years ago
**Answer: b) 7,000 years ago** — The book's dating.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names Ethiopia's own domesticated plants?
a) wheat, rice, maize  b) teff, dagussa, nug, enset  c) olives, grapes, dates
**Answer: b) teff, dagussa, nug, enset** — Ethiopia's gifts to the world's fields.


**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In the guide's formulation, a civilisation's six characteristics are —
a) cities, government, religion, social structure, writing, art  b) gold, armies, ships, roads, coins, kings  c) farming, herding, fishing, hunting, pottery, weaving
**Answer: a) cities, government, religion, social structure, writing, art** — The guide's six marks (teacher guide — evidence).

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** In the guide's state-origin account, the earliest rulers were —
a) elected councils  b) priests — early states were theocratic  c) merchant guilds
**Answer: b) priests — early states were theocratic** — Village chiefs gradually replaced them (teacher guide — evidence).

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** The guide says the emergence of the state was led by — and accelerated by —.

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** What is Neolithic Revolution?
