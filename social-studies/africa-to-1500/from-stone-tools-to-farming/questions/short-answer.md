# From stone tools to farming — the Neolithic Revolution — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three Stone Ages in order and the way of life of the first.
**Answer:** Palaeolithic, Mesolithic, Neolithic; the first lived by hunting and gathering.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the farming turn called a revolution if it took generations?
**Answer:** Because it changed everything downstream — settlement, villages, surplus, and in time the need for states — even though it spread slowly.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Ethiopia's four domesticated plants and two Neolithic site areas.
**Answer:** Teff, dagussa, nug and enset; sites at Aksum and Lalibela (also near Chercher in Hararghe and at Metehara).


**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell the guide's road to the state in three steps.
**Answer:** Religion developed and priests became important, ruling early theocratic states; village chiefs gradually replaced priests; agriculture led to the emergence of the state and external trade accelerated it.
