# From stone tools to farming — the Neolithic Revolution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pottery began in the Palaeolithic, in this account.
**Answer: False** — Pottery begins with Ethiopia's Neolithic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Neolithic sites in Ethiopia are named at Aksum, Lalibela, Chercher and Metehara.
**Answer: True** — The book's four named sites.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Settled farming came before hunting and gathering in the human story.
**Answer: False** — Hunting and gathering ruled first, for most of the story.


**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Writing is one of the guide's six characteristics of a civilisation.
**Answer: True** — Cities, government, religion, social structure, writing, art.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the guide's account, village chiefs came before priests as rulers.
**Answer: False** — Priests ruled first; chiefs gradually replaced them.
