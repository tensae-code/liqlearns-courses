# Hands-on — From stone tools to farming — the Neolithic Revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stone, clay, seed
Lay out a stone, a clay pot (or shard) and a handful of grain or seeds. For each, say which age it belongs to and what way of life it proves.

## Settle or move?
Debate in pairs: a band must choose — keep following the game, or settle by the river and sow. Each side must use one fact from the lesson for every argument it makes.
