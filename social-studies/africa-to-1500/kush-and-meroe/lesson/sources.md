Pairing and error honesty. (1) Pairing note: pupil content comes from the Amhara student book; the teacher guide is an Addis Ababa bureau book (2022, in English) and the student book an Amhara bureau book (© 2015 E.C., in Amharic) — NOT edition-matched companions. Guide material, where used, is labelled (teacher guide — evidence) in the table above and counts nowhere as pupil-taught. The guide splits this material into two lessons (§5.2 Napata, §5.3 Meroe) where the student book combines it; the guide's dated spine (670 BC, Napata destroyed, Meroe's peak marks, end about 350 AD under Ezana) is folded into Sub lesson 2 and labelled (teacher guide — evidence) in the table. (2) Guide errors, taught corrected and named here: the guide's contents and syllabus spell the city 'Mereo' — this lesson teaches the corrected **Meroe**, as the student book prints it. The guide also tells the 670 BC blow two ways (the Nubians 'drove out by Assyrian in around 670 B.C.' after ruling Egypt, and 'the Assyrians destroyed Napata … in 670 B.C.' causing the flight to Meroe) — this lesson teaches the single coherent version both tellings share: Assyria drove the Kushite kings out about 670 BC, Napata was destroyed, and the rulers moved south to Meroe; the two-way telling is the guide's, flagged here, not taught as two events. (3) The guide's Activity 5.2 key answers 'Napata' to two different questions — no question in this lesson rests on that key. The picture the placement names (the Meroe pyramids with Napata and Meroe on a Nile map) is Picture 11304, a drawn render.

**Named correction — the Funj "Fuji" slip (filed here by Wave 4 History Band E, 2026-11-12).** The Grade 9 History student textbook's Unit 5 summary prints "Fuji sultanate" for the **Funj** sultanate of Sennar (flagged in the Grade 9 student placements' book-errors list, from *History, Student Textbook, Grade 9*, Unit 5 summary; the Funj content itself is at printed pp. 87–88, placement 48). The correct name is Funj, and any lesson teaching that state teaches it corrected as Funj. No standalone Funj / North Africa lesson stands in the tree at the time of this filing, so this correction is filed in this lesson — the standing Nile-valley / Sudan lesson — and flagged in Band E's build log for the coordinator to move it if a dedicated North Africa lesson lands later. This note adds no content to this lesson and changes no part of it.
