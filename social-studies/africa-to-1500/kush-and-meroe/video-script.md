# Video script — Kush and Meroe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South of Egypt, down the Nile — Kush.
Egypt wrote its name by 2000 BC.

Napata rose, strong and rich.
Then five Kushite kings — imagine it —
sat on Egypt's own throne.

Assyria struck. Napata fell.
The kings walked south and built Meroe —
new capital, old gods, its own writing,
iron in its hands, the Horn's trade in its grip.

Centuries passed. Farms failed, deserts crept,
and Aksum's Ezana came.
Kush's long day was done.
