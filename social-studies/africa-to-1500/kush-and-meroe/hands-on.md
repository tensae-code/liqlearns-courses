# Hands-on — Kush and Meroe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Capital move role-play
Two learners stand at a 'Napata' chair. A third, playing Assyria, orders them out. They must carry the kingdom's three treasures — a drawn pyramid, a gold-coloured stone, an iron tool (or its picture) — to a new 'Meroe' chair further south, and say on arrival what the new capital kept and what it invented.

## Two-arrow trade board
Lay out two arrows of cards on the floor: out of Kush — gold, ivory, ebony; into Kush — olive oil, incense, timber, bronze. A companion shuffles the cards; put every card back on its right arrow and say who received it.
