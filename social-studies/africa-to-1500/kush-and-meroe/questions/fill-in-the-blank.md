# Kush and Meroe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kush was centred first at ______.
**Answer: Napata**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Driven out by ______, the Kushite kings founded Meroe south of Khartoum.
**Answer: Assyria**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'Kush's southern capital of iron-working and pyramids' is ______.
**Answer: Meroe**
