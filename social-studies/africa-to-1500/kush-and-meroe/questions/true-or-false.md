# Kush and Meroe — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kush built over 200 pyramids.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kush traded gold, ivory and ebony to Rome and Arabia.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At Meroe, the people stopped using Egyptian language and gods at once.
**Answer: False** — Meroe stayed Egyptian in language (hieroglyphics) and gods, even as the guide adds its own later writing and gods at its peak.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Aksum's invasion was an internal cause of Meroe's decline.
**Answer: False** — it is the book's external cause; the internal causes are falling farm output, poorer charcoal/iron quality and spreading desert.
