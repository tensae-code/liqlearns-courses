# Kush and Meroe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Kush' is first found in Egyptian records about —
a) 750 BC  b) 2000 BC  c) 670 BC  d) 350 AD
**Answer: b) 2000 BC** — about 2000 BC, for today's Sudan.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kush's first centre, strong and rich to about 750 BC, was —
a) Meroe  b) Khartoum  c) Napata  d) Aksum
**Answer: c) Napata** — centred at Napata.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the 8th century BC, Kush —
a) was destroyed by Assyria  b) ruled Egypt itself under five kings in succession  c) founded Meroe  d) stopped all trade
**Answer: b) ruled Egypt itself under five kings in succession** — five Kushite kings in succession ruled Egypt.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Meroe was founded after Assyria drove the kings out, and stood —
a) north of Egypt  b) on the coast  c) south of Khartoum  d) in Arabia
**Answer: c) south of Khartoum** — Meroe lies south of Khartoum.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is an INTERNAL cause of Meroe's decline?
a) Aksum's invasion  b) spreading desert and falling farm output  c) Rome's trade  d) the Hyksos invasion
**Answer: b) spreading desert and falling farm output** — falling farm output, poorer charcoal/iron and spreading desert are the book's internal causes; Aksum's invasion is the external one.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide keys Meroe's end to about 350 AD, at the hands of —
a) Ahmose I  b) Menes  c) Ezana of Aksum  d) the Assyrians
**Answer: c) Ezana of Aksum** — King Ezana of Aksum (teacher guide — evidence).
