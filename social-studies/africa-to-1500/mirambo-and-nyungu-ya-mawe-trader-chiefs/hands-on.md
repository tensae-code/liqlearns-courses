# Hands-on — Mirambo and Nyungu ya Mawe — trader chiefs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Mirambo and Nyungu ya Mawe — trader chiefs — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Rise of Nyungu ya Mawe, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
