# Lesson plan — West African Empires — Songhai

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** West African Empires — Songhai
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Africa to 1500 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** as Mali weakened, the Songhai of the Niger bend rose — Sunni Ali (1464–1492) built the empire by conquest, Askia Muhammad gave it administration, Islam and learning — until Morocco's invasion of 1591 ended it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on West African Empires — Mali and Mansa Musa, the lesson just before it in this title — Songhai is the third empire on the same Niger ground. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Sunni Ali — the conqueror** — the Songhai of the Niger bend rising as Mali weakened; Sunni Ali (1464–1492) conquering — Tuaregs out of Timbuktu, Mossi off the bend — leaving an empire covering all Mali.
2. **Askia Muhammad — the builder** — founder of the Askia dynasty; a devout Muslim who expanded the state, built strong administration, used Islam to unify, posted Sharia judges empire-wide, subdued the Tuaregs, and raised learning centres in Timbuktu and Jenne.
3. **The fall** — late 16th century: succession struggles, new eastern rivals (Bornu, Hausa city-states, Tuareg sultanate), Europeans diverting the gold trade to the coast — and Morocco's invasion of 1591.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: West African Empires — Mali and Mansa Musa. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell how Sunni Ali made Songhai great (1464–1492) and what he left at his death.
2. List Askia Muhammad's works: expansion, administration, Islam as unity, Sharia judges, and the learning centres of Timbuktu and Jenne.
3. Name the late-16th-century pressures and the 1591 blow that ended Songhai.
