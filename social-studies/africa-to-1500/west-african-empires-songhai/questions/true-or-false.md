# West African Empires — Songhai — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sunni Ali drove the Tuaregs out of Timbuktu.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Askia Muhammad used Islam to unify his empire.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Europeans helped Songhai by bringing more gold traders to the bend.
**Answer: False** — they diverted the gold trade to the coast, draining the bend.
