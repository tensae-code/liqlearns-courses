# West African Empires — Songhai — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Sunni Ali conquer? Name the two drives the book gives.
**Answer:** He drove the Tuaregs out of Timbuktu and the Mossi off the Niger bend, leaving an empire covering all Mali.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List three of Askia Muhammad's building works.
**Answer:** Any three of: expanded the state; built strong administration; used Islam to unify; posted Sharia judges empire-wide to keep peace; subdued the Tuaregs; raised learning centres in Timbuktu and Jenne.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name Songhai's new eastern rivals.
**Answer:** Bornu, the Hausa city-states, and the Tuareg sultanate.
