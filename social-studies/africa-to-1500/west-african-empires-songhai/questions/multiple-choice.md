# West African Empires — Songhai — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sunni Ali ruled —
a) 1324–1325  b) 1464–1492  c) 1235–1260  d) 1591–1600
**Answer: b) 1464–1492** — 1464–1492.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At Sunni Ali's death, his empire —
a) covered Timbuktu only  b) covered all Mali  c) reached Egypt  d) held only Gao
**Answer: b) covered all Mali** — an empire covering all Mali.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Askia Muhammad posted ______ empire-wide to keep peace.
a) tax collectors  b) Sharia judges  c) governors from Morocco  d) priests
**Answer: b) Sharia judges** — Sharia judges.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Askia raised great learning centres in —
a) Gao and Niani  b) Timbuktu and Jenne  c) Koumbi Saleh and Gao  d) Jenne and Niani
**Answer: b) Timbuktu and Jenne** — Timbuktu and Jenne.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which was NOT one of Songhai's late pressures?
a) Bornu and the Hausa city-states rising in the east  b) Europeans diverting the gold trade to the coast  c) a Roman invasion  d) succession struggles
**Answer: c) a Roman invasion** — Rome belongs to Carthage's story, centuries earlier.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Songhai was ended by Morocco's invasion in —
a) 1492  b) 1235  c) 1591  d) 1640
**Answer: c) 1591** — 1591.
