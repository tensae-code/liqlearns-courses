# Video script — West African Empires — Songhai

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ghana taxed. Mali shone. Songhai conquered.

Sunni Ali took the bend — Timbuktu freed,
the Mossi pushed off — an empire by 1492
as wide as all Mali.

Then Askia Muhammad built what conquest cannot:
judges in every province, one faith to bind it,
schools rising in Timbuktu and Jenne.

But gold learned a new road — to the coast.
Rivals pressed from the east.
And in 1591 Morocco came over the desert,
and the bend's last great empire fell.
