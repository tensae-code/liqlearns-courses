# Hands-on — West African Empires — Songhai

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two kings, two toolboxes
Lay out two boxes. Into Sunni Ali's box put conquest cards (Timbuktu taken, Mossi pushed off the bend, empire covering Mali). Into Askia's box put building cards (administration, Sharia judges, learning centres). A companion deals the cards face down; sort them at speed and defend each placing.

## Gold road redrawn
On the bend map from the lesson, draw the old gold road north across the desert — then draw the new road the Europeans opened, south to the coast. Say in one sentence what the new road did to Songhai.
