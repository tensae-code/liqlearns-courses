# The Libyan kingdoms after Carthage — Numidia to Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The ancient Libyan kingdoms that succeeded Carthage.
**Answer: Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The Numidians.
**Answer: Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
