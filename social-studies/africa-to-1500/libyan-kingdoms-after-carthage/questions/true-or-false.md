# The Libyan kingdoms after Carthage — Numidia to Rome — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit (North Africa): the ancient Libyan kingdoms that succeeded Carthage

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G10 Ancient Arab World* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit (North Africa): the ancient Libyan kingdoms that succeeded Carthage

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Numidians

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G7 History of Libya and the Ancient World* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Numidians
