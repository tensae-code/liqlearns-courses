# The Libyan kingdoms after Carthage — Numidia to Rome

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The ancient Libyan kingdoms that succeeded Carthage

Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE

## Sub lesson 2 — The Numidians

Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
