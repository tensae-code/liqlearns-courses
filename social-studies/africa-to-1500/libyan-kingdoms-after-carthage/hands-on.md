# Hands-on — The Libyan kingdoms after Carthage — Numidia to Rome

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Libyan kingdoms after Carthage — Numidia to Rome — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The ancient Libyan kingdoms that succeeded Carthage, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
