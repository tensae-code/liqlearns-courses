**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The ancient Libyan kingdoms that succeeded Carthage** — Numidia under Massinissa unites the Libyan interior, the Three Cities — Leptis, Oea and Sabratha — pass under Numidian then Roman rule, and Rome finally destroys Carthage in 146 BCE
2. **The Numidians** — Massinissa unites Numidia from Cirta, backs Rome against Carthage and rules inland to the Gulf of Sirte; Jugurtha fights Rome six years before Caesar annexes the kingdom in 46 BCE; the Numidians write with letters adapted from Phoenician
