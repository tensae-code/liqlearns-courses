# Zimbabwe — totems, clans and family names

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In Zimbabwe's teaching, a totem is not a nickname: the totem system of clan names tells who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from — a map of belonging carried in a name. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Family names and totems — the totem system (Grade 3)

The totem a child was teased for not knowing in the first school years becomes, at Grade 3, a system. Clan names tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from — the totem is a whole social map, carried in a single name.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: family names and totems — the totem system. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
