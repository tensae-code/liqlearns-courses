# Video script — Zimbabwe — totems, clans and family names

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Zimbabwe's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The totem a child was teased for not knowing in the first school years becomes, at Grade 3, a system. Clan names tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from — the totem is a whole social map, carried in a single name.
