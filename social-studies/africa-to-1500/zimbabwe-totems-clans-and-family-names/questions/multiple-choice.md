# Zimbabwe — totems, clans and family names — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The totem system tells a family —
a) who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where it comes from  b) what the weather will be  c) which crops to plant only
**Answer: a) who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where it comes from** — clan names tell who may marry whom, who must be respected, and where a family comes from

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A totem is carried in —
a) a name  b) a uniform  c) a flag only
**Answer: a) a name** — the totem system works through clan and family names

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A child first meets her totem —
a) in the earliest school years, as her own identity  b) only at university  c) never at school
**Answer: a) in the earliest school years, as her own identity** — the totem is part of the child's identity from ECD
