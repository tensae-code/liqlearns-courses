# Zimbabwe — totems, clans and family names — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** People of the same totem are treated as kin by the system.
**Answer: True** — the totem marks clan kinship, which is why it governs marriage

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Totems have nothing to do with where a family comes from.
**Answer: False** — clan names tell where a family comes from
