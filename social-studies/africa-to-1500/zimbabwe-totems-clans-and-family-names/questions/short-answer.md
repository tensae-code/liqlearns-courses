# Zimbabwe — totems, clans and family names — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things the totem system decides or shows.
**Answer: Any two of: who may marry whom; who must be respected; where a family comes from.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the totem more than a nickname?
**Answer: Because it is a system — it carries marriage rules, respect and family origins (any close wording).**
