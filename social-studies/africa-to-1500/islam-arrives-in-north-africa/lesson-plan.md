## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Abu Bakr and Umar** — Abu Bakr holds Arabia together through the Ridda wars and sends the armies out; Umar organises the growing state with diwans, garrison cities and a calendar
2. **Uthman and Ali** — Uthman unifies the Quran's text and the fleets take to the sea; Ali's caliphate is torn by the first civil wars that split the community
3. **The Rashidun Caliphs** — Abu Bakr crushes the apostasy wars, kills the false prophet Musaylima and orders the Quran gathered into one book; Umar wins at Yarmouk, receives Jerusalem, breaks Persia at al-Qadisiyya in 635 and sends Amr ibn al-As to take Egypt and found Fustat
