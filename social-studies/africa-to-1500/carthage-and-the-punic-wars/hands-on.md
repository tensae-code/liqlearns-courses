# Hands-on — Carthage and the Punic Wars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wall measure
Outside, pace out 9 metres on the ground and mark it — that is the THICKNESS of Carthage's wall. Then look up at a wall or tree and judge 12 metres of height against it. The wall was a road in the sky.

## Council and strong men debate
Two companions argue the book's two halves: one defends 'the people's council ruled Carthage', the other 'a few strong men held the real power'. Each must use the book's own wording as evidence.
