# Lesson plan — Carthage and the Punic Wars

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Carthage and the Punic Wars
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Africa to 1500 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Carthage — founded 814 BC by Phoenicians near today's Tunis — grew rich on trade behind a mighty wall, until Rome's wish for Sicily brought the three Punic Wars (264–146 BC) and Carthage's destruction.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Kush lesson before it in this title — the learner has followed the Nile states; Carthage opens the Mediterranean story. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons

1. **The trading city** — founded 814 BC by Phoenicians near today's Tunis; rich on trade; walled 12 m high and 9 m thick; a people's council in form, a few strong men in fact.
2. **The Punic Wars** — Rome's wish for Sicily; three wars, 264–146 BC; the third destroyed Carthage into a Roman province.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Kush and Meroe. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say who founded Carthage, when, and where.
2. Describe Carthage's wall and its real rulers behind the people's council.
3. Tell the Punic Wars: their cause, their dates, and how the third one ended.

## If the learner is stuck
- Thinks the people's council truly ruled → in form yes; in fact, the book says, real power lay with a few strong men.
- Counts one long war → three separate Punic Wars spread over 264–146 BC; only the third destroyed the city.
