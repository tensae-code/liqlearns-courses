# Video script — Carthage and the Punic Wars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Phoenician sailors built a city on the sea:
Carthage, in 814 BC.
Rich with trade. Walled like a mountain —
twelve metres high, nine metres thick.

A council ruled it — so the papers said.
A few strong men ruled it — so the truth ran.

Across the water, Rome wanted Sicily.
Three wars followed. Twice Carthage stood.
The third time, Rome tore it down,
and Carthage became a Roman province.
