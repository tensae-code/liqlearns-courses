# Carthage and the Punic Wars — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who held power in Carthage — in form, and in fact?
**Answer:** In form, a people's council ruled; in fact, real power lay with a few strong men.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did Rome and Carthage go to war, and how did the wars end?
**Answer:** Rome wished for Sicily. After three Punic Wars (264–146 BC), the third destroyed Carthage and its land became a Roman province.
