# Carthage and the Punic Wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Carthage was founded in 814 BC by —
a) Romans  b) Phoenicians  c) Egyptians  d) Kushites
**Answer: b) Phoenicians** — the Phoenicians founded it near today's Tunis.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Carthage's wall was —
a) 9 m high, 12 m thick  b) 12 m high, 9 m thick  c) 12 m high, 12 m thick  d) 9 m high, 9 m thick
**Answer: b) 12 m high, 9 m thick** — 12 metres high and 9 metres thick.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Punic Wars began over Rome's wish for —
a) Tunis  b) Sicily  c) Egypt  d) Sardinia's gold
**Answer: b) Sicily** — Rome's wish for Sicily brought the wars.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The three Punic Wars ran —
a) 814–750 BC  b) 264–146 BC  c) 146–264 AD  d) 264–146 AD
**Answer: b) 264–146 BC** — 264–146 BC.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The third Punic War ended with Carthage —
a) victorious  b) allied to Rome  c) destroyed into a Roman province  d) moved to Sicily
**Answer: c) destroyed into a Roman province** — destroyed, and its land made a Roman province.
