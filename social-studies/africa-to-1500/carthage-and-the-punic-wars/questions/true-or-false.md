# Carthage and the Punic Wars — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In fact, real power in Carthage lay with a few strong men, though a people's council ruled in form.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Carthage stood near today's Tunis on the Mediterranean.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** There were three Punic Wars.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Carthage destroyed Rome in the third Punic War.
**Answer: False** — Rome destroyed Carthage; its land became a Roman province.
