# Origin stories — creation and evolution in East Africa — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Three origin stories sit side by side for judgement — the culture's own traditions, Adam fashioned from dust in Eden, and Darwin's evolution — and the learner must weigh their advantages and disadvantages in a table.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Traditional views, Adam and Eve, and Darwin

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History and Political Education Textbook — Senior One (Lower Secondary Curriculum, PROTOTYPE)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Traditional views, Adam and Eve, and Darwin

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide adds the scientists' universe — Newton's theory and the Big Bang — beside Adam and Eve, then names Darwin's the most reliable of the scientific theories and sets a classroom debate: creation versus evolution.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Origin of Man (guide)
