# Hands-on — Origin stories — creation and evolution in East Africa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Origin stories — creation and evolution in East Africa — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Traditional views, Adam and Eve, and Darwin, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
