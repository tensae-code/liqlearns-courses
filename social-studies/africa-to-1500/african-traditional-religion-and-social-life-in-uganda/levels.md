# African traditional religion and social life in Uganda — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Social Organisation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — before the foreign religions, each society names God its own way — Katonda for the Baganda, Rugaba for the Banyoro, Ruhanga for the Banyankole, Lokusuban for the Iteso — and clan ceremonies, taboos and traditional education hold the people together

## Sub lesson 2 — Culture and religion
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — one God, many names on the classroom table — Katonda for Buganda, Ruhanga for Ankole, Were for Bugishu, Ngai for the Kikuyu

## Sub lesson 3 — Culture and religions of East Africa
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the child is sent to the elders to name the religions and Christian sects of East Africa and to weigh how western culture is changing the old ways

## Sub lesson 4 — Culture and Ethnic Groupings in East Africa
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — culture is taught at family level — values, rituals, acceptable and unacceptable behaviour — and Buganda's Ekisaakaate, the Nabagereka's holiday camp, coaches the young in the old values

## Sub lesson 5 — Religions in East Africa
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — in traditional African religion the ancestors are present at every major event — wedding, birth, death — and at the small ones too, a job gained, a degree finished
