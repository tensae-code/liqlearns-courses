# African traditional religion and social life in Uganda

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Before the foreign religions, each society names God its own way — Katonda for the Baganda, Rugaba for the Banyoro, Ruhanga for the Banyankole, Lokusuban for the Iteso — and clan ceremonies, taboos and traditional education hold the people together. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
