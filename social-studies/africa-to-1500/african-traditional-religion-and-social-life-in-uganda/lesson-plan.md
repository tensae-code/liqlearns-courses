## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Social Organisation** — before the foreign religions, each society names God its own way — Katonda for the Baganda, Rugaba for the Banyoro, Ruhanga for the Banyankole, Lokusuban for the Iteso — and clan ceremonies, taboos and traditional education hold the people together
2. **Culture and religion** — one God, many names on the classroom table — Katonda for Buganda, Ruhanga for Ankole, Were for Bugishu, Ngai for the Kikuyu
3. **Culture and religions of East Africa** — the child is sent to the elders to name the religions and Christian sects of East Africa and to weigh how western culture is changing the old ways
4. **Culture and Ethnic Groupings in East Africa** — culture is taught at family level — values, rituals, acceptable and unacceptable behaviour — and Buganda's Ekisaakaate, the Nabagereka's holiday camp, coaches the young in the old values
5. **Religions in East Africa** — in traditional African religion the ancestors are present at every major event — wedding, birth, death — and at the small ones too, a job gained, a degree finished
6. **Traditional religions** — the chapter opens at the Nakayima tree in Mubende and a traditional shrine in Nyamwezi — living shrines, not museum pieces
