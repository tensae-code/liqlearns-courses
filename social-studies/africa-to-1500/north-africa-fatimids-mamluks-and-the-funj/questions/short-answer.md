# North Africa — Fatimids, Mamluk Egypt and the Funj — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: give the book's sequence of North Africa's great states in order.
**Answer: After the Arab conquest and the 11th-century revival: Fatimid Egypt, then Mamluk Egypt, with the Funj sultanate on the Nile to the south.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: who were the Mamluks and how long did they rule Egypt?
**Answer: Soldier-rulers of Turkish origin who ruled Egypt from 1250 to 1517.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: why did the Funj sultanate matter to Ethiopia?
**Answer: It fought Christian Ethiopia over the trade routes linking the Sudan, the highlands and the Red Sea, and lasted until Egypt conquered it in the 1820s.**
