# North Africa — Fatimids, Mamluk Egypt and the Funj — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: By the end of the 7th century the Arab conquest had run along the whole North African coast and into Spain.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: Ethio-Egyptian relations in this period were full of conflict.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The Funj sultanate was a tightly centralised empire like Egypt's.
**Answer: False — The book calls it loosely united.**
