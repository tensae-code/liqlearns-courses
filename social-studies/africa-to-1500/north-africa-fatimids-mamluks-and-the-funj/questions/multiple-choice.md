# North Africa — Fatimids, Mamluk Egypt and the Funj — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arab armies took Egypt in —
a) 622 AD  b) 642 AD  c) 750 AD  d) 1250 AD
**Answer: b) 642 AD — Egypt fell in 642; the coast to Morocco and into Spain followed by the century's end.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The 11th century brought North Africa —
a) A Christian revival  b) A revival of Islam with further Arab immigration  c) Roman rule  d) Persian rule
**Answer: b) A revival of Islam with further Arab immigration — The book pairs the revival with new immigration.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first of the period's great Egyptian states was —
a) The Mamluk sultanate  b) The Fatimid caliphate  c) The Funj  d) The Ottoman Empire
**Answer: b) The Fatimid caliphate — Fatimid Egypt precedes Mamluk Egypt in the book's sequence.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Mamluks, of Turkish origin, ruled Egypt —
a) 1250–1517  b) 642–750  c) 1000–1100  d) 1517–1820
**Answer: a) 1250–1517 — The book gives the Mamluk span as 1250–1517.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Funj sultanate fought Christian Ethiopia over —
a) Sea ports  b) Trade routes  c) The calendar  d) Fishing rights
**Answer: b) Trade routes — The roads between the Sudan, the highlands and the Red Sea.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The Funj sultanate lasted until —
a) 1517  b) Egypt conquered it in the 1820s  c) The Portuguese arrived  d) 642 AD
**Answer: b) Egypt conquered it in the 1820s — The book closes the Funj story with Egypt's conquest in the 1820s.**
