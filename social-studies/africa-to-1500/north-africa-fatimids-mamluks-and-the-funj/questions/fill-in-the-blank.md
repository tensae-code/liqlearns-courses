# North Africa — Fatimids, Mamluk Egypt and the Funj — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arab armies took Egypt in ____ AD.
**Answer: 642**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Turkish-origin rulers of Egypt 1250–1517 were the ____.
**Answer: Mamluks**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The loosely united Nile sultanate that fought Ethiopia over trade routes was the ____.
**Answer: Funj**
