# Kingdoms, empires and other African states — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** African peoples build kingdoms and empires, organise them politically, administratively, socially and economically, and hold them together
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (UA3 Royaumes, empires et autres formations: création, organisation politique, administrative, sociale et économique).

**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (UA3 Vie intellectuelle, artistique et culturelle des royaumes et empires).
