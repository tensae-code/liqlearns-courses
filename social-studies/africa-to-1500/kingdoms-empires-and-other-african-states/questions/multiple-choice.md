# Kingdoms, empires and other African states — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Building and organising states  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Building and organising states** — African peoples build kingdoms and empires, organise them politically, administratively, socially and economically, and hold them together.
