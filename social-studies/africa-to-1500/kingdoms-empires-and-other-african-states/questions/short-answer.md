# Kingdoms, empires and other African states — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Building and organising states.
**Answer: African peoples build kingdoms and empires, organise them politically, administratively, socially and economically, and hold them together (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Learning and art at court.
