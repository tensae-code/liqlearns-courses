# Hands-on — Islam in West Africa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three changes, one continuity
Draw three columns headed Law, Learning and Kingship. In each, write one sentence on how Islam reshaped it. Under all three, write the continuity: older ways were not erased.

## Old and new side by side
Draw a West African town with a new place of learning beside an older gathering place. Label each, and write one sentence on how both could stand in the same town.
