# Islam in West Africa — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Islam erased all older ways in West Africa.
**Answer: False — it reshaped law, learning and kingship without erasing older ways.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Islam reshaped learning in West Africa.
**Answer: True — learning is one of the three things the book says the new faith reshaped.**
