# Islam in West Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Islam in West Africa reshaped —
a) Law, learning and kingship  b) Only farming tools  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Law, learning and kingship**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Islam's growth do to older ways in West Africa?
a) It did not erase them  b) It erased them completely  c) It forbade all memory of them
**Answer: a) It did not erase them**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Learning in West African societies was —
a) Reshaped by the new faith  b) Ended by the new faith  c) Untouched in every way
**Answer: a) Reshaped by the new faith**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Kingship in West Africa was —
a) Reshaped, while older ways remained  b) Abolished everywhere at once  c) Never affected
**Answer: a) Reshaped, while older ways remained**
