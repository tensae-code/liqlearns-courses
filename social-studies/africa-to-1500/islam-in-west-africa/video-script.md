# Video script — Islam in West Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Gambia's Grade 10 WASSCE History tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A new faith comes to West Africa — and a society changes, without stopping being itself.



Law changes. Learning changes. Kingship changes.



And underneath the change, older ways remain — not erased, but living beside the new.
