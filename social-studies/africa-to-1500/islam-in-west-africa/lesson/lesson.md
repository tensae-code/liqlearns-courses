# Islam in West Africa

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Islam in West Africa reshapes law, learning and kingship without erasing older ways — the new faith changes West African societies, and older ways continue beside it. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Law reshaped

The new faith reshapes law in West African societies. How people judge, rule and settle questions of right and wrong changes as Islam takes root.

## Sub lesson 2 — Learning reshaped

The new faith reshapes learning too. West African scholarship and teaching are changed by Islam's arrival and growth.

## Sub lesson 3 — Kingship reshaped — and older ways remain

Kingship is reshaped by the new faith — but older ways are not erased. The book teaches the change and the continuity together: a society can take a new faith and still keep much of what it was.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Islam in West Africa in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
