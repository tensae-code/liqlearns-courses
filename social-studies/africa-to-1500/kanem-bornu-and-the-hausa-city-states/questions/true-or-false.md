# Kanem-Bornu and the Hausa city-states — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: Kanem-Bornu grew on trade protected by its army.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The Hausa states formed a single united empire.
**Answer: False — They were a family of separate city-states.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Kanem-Bornu grew stronger as trade moved to the Atlantic coast.
**Answer: False — It declined as the inland trade thinned.**
