# Kanem-Bornu and the Hausa city-states — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The grassland empire around Lake Chad under a Muslim dynasty was ____.
**Answer: Kanem-Bornu**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kano and Katsina were among the ____ city-states.
**Answer: Hausa**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Hausa city-states emerged between ____ and 1200 AD.
**Answer: 1000**
