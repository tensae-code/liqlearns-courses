# Kanem-Bornu and the Hausa city-states — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: what bargain made Kanem-Bornu rich?
**Answer: Its army kept the trade routes safe and the state taxed the traders who used them — protection sold for revenue.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: why did Kanem-Bornu decline?
**Answer: Trade shifted from the inland caravan routes to the Atlantic coast, so caravans and taxes thinned.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: how was the Hausa pattern different from Kanem-Bornu's?
**Answer: The Hausa formed separate walled city-states of herder-farmers, not one empire guarding one route system.**
