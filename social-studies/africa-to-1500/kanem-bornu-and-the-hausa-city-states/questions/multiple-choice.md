# Kanem-Bornu and the Hausa city-states — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kanem-Bornu's army earned the state revenue by —
a) Raiding caravans  b) Keeping the trade routes safe, so traders could be taxed  c) Minting coins  d) Building ships
**Answer: b) Keeping the trade routes safe, so traders could be taxed — Safety was the product; taxes were the price.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kanem-Bornu was ruled by —
a) A Christian dynasty  b) A Muslim dynasty  c) City councils  d) Portuguese governors
**Answer: b) A Muslim dynasty — The book places a Muslim dynasty over the grassland empire.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kanem-Bornu declined as —
a) The Sahara dried up  b) Trade shifted from inland routes to the Atlantic coast  c) Its army was disbanded  d) Lake Chad flooded
**Answer: b) Trade shifted from inland routes to the Atlantic coast — When the route moved seaward, the bargain stopped paying.**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Hausa city-states emerged about —
a) 500 BC  b) 1000–1200 AD  c) 1500–1600 AD  d) 1800 AD
**Answer: b) 1000–1200 AD — Kano, Katsina, Zazzau and the rest emerged in those centuries.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Hausa peoples were —
a) Only farmers  b) Only herders  c) Both pastoralists and farmers  d) Seafarers
**Answer: c) Both pastoralists and farmers — The double economy is the book's point.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Which list names Hausa city-states?
a) Kano, Katsina, Zazzau, Gobir, Kebbi, Rano, Zamfara  b) Ghana, Mali, Songhai  c) Aksum, Roha, Adulis  d) Cairo, Fez, Tunis
**Answer: a) Kano, Katsina, Zazzau, Gobir, Kebbi, Rano, Zamfara — The book names those seven.**
