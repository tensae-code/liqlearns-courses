# Hands-on — Kanem-Bornu and the Hausa city-states

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw this lesson as one picture with three panels — one for each sub lesson. Under each panel write its names and dates. Then tell the whole story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.

## Map it
On a blank outline map of the lesson's region, mark the places the lesson names, in the order the story reaches them. Draw the routes — roads, rivers, caravans — that join them.
