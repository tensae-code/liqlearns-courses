# Video script — Peoples, tribes and clans of Uganda

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A tribe shares one origin and language, a clan one forefather; drought, the search for water and for jobs drive migration, and fertile soil, rain, food and trade decide where people finally settle.
