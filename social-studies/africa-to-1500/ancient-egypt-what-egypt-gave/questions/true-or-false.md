# Ancient Egypt — What Egypt Gave — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's writing was used for crop and tax records, science, administration and diplomacy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Egyptian medicine included hospital inpatient care.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Great Pyramid named in the table stands at Karnak.
**Answer: False** — the Great Pyramid of Giza is named; Karnak is the named temple.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt's crafts included pottery, jewellery and religious carving in metal and wood.
**Answer: True**
