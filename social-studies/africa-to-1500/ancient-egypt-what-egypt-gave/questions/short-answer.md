# Ancient Egypt — What Egypt Gave — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six contributions in the book's table.
**Answer:** Writing, medicine, mathematics, crafts, architecture, and the calendar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did Egypt's medicine include? Give all three parts the book lists.
**Answer:** Hospital inpatient care; knowledge of the body's bones (the book prints 360); and the chemicals of mummy-making.
