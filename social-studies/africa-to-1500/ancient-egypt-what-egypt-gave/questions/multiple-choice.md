# Ancient Egypt — What Egypt Gave — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hieroglyphics were written on —
a) clay tablets  b) papyrus  c) stone only  d) wooden boards
**Answer: b) papyrus** — Egypt's writing material was papyrus.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's mathematics served mainly to —
a) count soldiers  b) build dams and canals  c) price gold  d) train scribes
**Answer: b) build dams and canals** — geometry and arithmetic were used for dams and canals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which temple does the book name under architecture?
a) Karnak  b) Lalibela  c) Aksum  d) Meroe
**Answer: a) Karnak** — temples such as Karnak.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The calendar Egypt gave the world has —
a) 10 months, 360 days  b) 12 months, 365 days  c) 13 months, 354 days  d) 12 months, 360 days
**Answer: b) 12 months, 365 days** — 12 months and 365 days.
