# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt — What Egypt Gave

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Papyrus page
On the roughest paper you have, invent ten picture-signs for ten words you use daily — water, bread, sun, house, cow — and write a three-line message with them. That is hieroglyphic thinking in your own hand.

## Calendar count
Write your own year's months in a row and add the days the way the Egyptian calendar frames them: 12 months, 365 days. Mark today's place in the count.
