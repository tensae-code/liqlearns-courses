# Video script — Ancient Egypt — What Egypt Gave

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six gifts from one river kingdom.

Writing — marks on papyrus,
for taxes, for science, for kings.
Medicine — healing houses, and the chemicals
that kept the dead unspoiled.
Mathematics — measuring dams and canals.
Crafts — pots, jewels, carved gods.
Building — pyramids, and Karnak's temple.
And the calendar — twelve months, 365 days.

Egypt fell long ago. Its gifts never did.
