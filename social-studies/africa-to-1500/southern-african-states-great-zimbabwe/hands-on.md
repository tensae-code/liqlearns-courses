# Hands-on — Southern African States — Great Zimbabwe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ten-metre wall
Measure 10 metres along a school or compound wall with a rope or pacing, and mark how high 10 metres reaches against a building or tree. Great Zimbabwe's builders raised stone that high without machines — stand under your mark and say the building dates aloud: 1200 to 1450.

## Stone enclosure model
With stones, bricks or mud, build a small oval enclosure wall on the ground, with toy cattle or stone 'cattle' inside it. Label it with a card: Zimbabwe = stone enclosure (Shona).
