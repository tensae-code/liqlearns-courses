# Hands-on — Southern African States — Great Zimbabwe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ten-metre wall
Measure 10 metres along a school or compound wall with a rope or pacing, and mark how high 10 metres reaches against a building or tree. Great Zimbabwe's builders raised stone that high without machines — stand under your mark and say the building dates aloud: 1200 to 1450.

## Stone enclosure model
With stones, bricks or mud, build a small oval enclosure wall on the ground, with toy cattle or stone 'cattle' inside it. Label it with a card: Zimbabwe = stone enclosure (Shona).

## Consolidation counters (Grade 9)
Start with twelve separate village counters. Each 'century' (round), merge two counters into a larger settlement and record what the merger gains (walls? trade? herds?). After five rounds, compare your map with the book's: villages consolidating from the 11th century.

## Grade 11 depth — Great Zimbabwe in depth — stone city, gold trade, Mutapa

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
