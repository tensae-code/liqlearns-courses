# Southern African States — Great Zimbabwe — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Local people built stone kraals for cattle at Great Zimbabwe.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The enclosure was the state's symbol of power and honour.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mwene was the name of Mutapa's capital city.
**Answer: False** — Mwene was the ruler's title.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Great Zimbabwe is still Zimbabwe's heritage emblem.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Mwene Mutapa was conquered by the Portuguese in the late 16th century.
**Answer: True** — The book's closing date for it.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Great Zimbabwe was founded by the Rozwi in 1450.
**Answer: False** — Great Zimbabwe begins c.1000; the Rozwi founded Mwene Mutapa after 1450.
