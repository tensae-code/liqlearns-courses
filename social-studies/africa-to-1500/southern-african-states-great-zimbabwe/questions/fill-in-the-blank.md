# Southern African States — Great Zimbabwe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Zimbabwe' means ______ in Shona.
**Answer: stone enclosure**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ was the ruler's title in Mwene Mutapa.
**Answer: Mwene**
**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The term for 'the coastal port of Zimbabwe's gold trade' is ______.
**Answer: Sofala**
