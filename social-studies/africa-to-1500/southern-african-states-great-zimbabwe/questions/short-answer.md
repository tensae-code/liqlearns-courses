# Southern African States — Great Zimbabwe — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did local people build in stone at Great Zimbabwe, besides the great walls?
**Answer:** Stone kraals for cattle and rich men's houses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell the succession after Great Zimbabwe, as the book gives it.
**Answer:** It was succeeded by Mwene Mutapa (Mwene being the ruler's title); a Mutapa city stood from the 11th–15th centuries, and the state fell under Portuguese administration in 1629 as the book states.
