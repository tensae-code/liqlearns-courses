# Southern African States — Great Zimbabwe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Shona, 'Zimbabwe' means —
a) gold field  b) stone enclosure  c) great river  d) king's house
**Answer: b) stone enclosure** — stone enclosure.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Great Enclosure's walls rose to —
a) 3 metres  b) 10 metres  c) 12 metres  d) 33 metres
**Answer: b) 10 metres** — up to 10 metres high.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The great walls were built between —
a) 1000 and 1100  b) 1200 and 1450  c) 1464 and 1492  d) 1500 and 1600
**Answer: b) 1200 and 1450** — 1200 and 1450.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Great Zimbabwe was succeeded by —
a) Songhai  b) Mwene Mutapa  c) Kongo  d) Mali
**Answer: b) Mwene Mutapa** — Mwene Mutapa — Mwene being the ruler's title.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** As the book states, Mutapa fell under Portuguese administration in —
a) 1500  b) 1580  c) 1629  d) 1450
**Answer: c) 1629** — 1629, as the book states.
