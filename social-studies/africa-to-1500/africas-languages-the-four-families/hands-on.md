# Hands-on — Africa's Languages — the Four Families

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Language walk
Walk your street or market and listen. Write down every language you hear spoken in one hour, and ask one speaker of each what other languages their language is kin to.

## Family tree poster
On a big sheet, draw a tree with four great branches — the four families — and Madagascar's small tree beside it. Hang the languages you know as leaves on the right branches.
