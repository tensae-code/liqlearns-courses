# Africa's Languages — the Four Families — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The island of ______ holds the Austronesian languages, apart from the mainland four families.
**Answer: Madagascar**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Afro-Asiatic holds five branches: Semitic, Cushitic, Berber, ______ and Omotic.
**Answer: Chadic**
**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The term for 'the click-language family, among the continent's oldest' is ______.
**Answer: Khoisan**
