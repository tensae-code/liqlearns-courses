# Africa's Languages — the Four Families — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four mother families of Africa's languages.
**Answer:** Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan, Niger-Congo and Khoisan.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's most-spoken ranking, in order.
**Answer:** Swahili first, Hausa second, Yoruba third.
