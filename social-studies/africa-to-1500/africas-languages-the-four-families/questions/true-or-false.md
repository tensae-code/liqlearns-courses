# Africa's Languages — the Four Families — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Khoisan is one of Africa's four mother language families.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Madagascar's languages belong to the Niger-Congo family.
**Answer: False** — they are Austronesian, standing apart from the mainland four.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Yoruba is the most-spoken language in the book's ranking.
**Answer: False** — Swahili is first, Hausa second, Yoruba third.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Amharic, Somali, Oromo and Tigrinya are Afro-Asiatic languages of the Horn.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Congo-Kordofanian is the book's name for the Niger-Congo super-family.
**Answer: True** — Given in that doubled form.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Every language spoken in Africa today originated in Africa.
**Answer: False** — The book names five arrivals.
