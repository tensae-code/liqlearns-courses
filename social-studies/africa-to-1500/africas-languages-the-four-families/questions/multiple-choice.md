# Africa's Languages — the Four Families — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's languages are grouped into how many mother families (before Madagascar is counted)?
a) Three  b) Four  c) Five  d) Seven
**Answer: b) Four** — Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan, Niger-Congo and Khoisan.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which family stands apart on Madagascar?
a) Khoisan  b) Niger-Congo  c) Austronesian  d) Nilo-Saharan
**Answer: c) Austronesian** — Madagascar's languages are Austronesian.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is one of the four mother families?
a) Austronesian  b) Khoisan  c) Chadic  d) Semitic
**Answer: b) Khoisan** — Chadic and Semitic are branches inside Afro-Asiatic, not families.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The most-spoken language in the book's ranking is —
a) Hausa  b) Yoruba  c) Swahili  d) Amharic
**Answer: c) Swahili** — Swahili first, Hausa second, Yoruba third.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Hausa belongs to which branch of Afro-Asiatic?
a) Semitic  b) Cushitic  c) Berber  d) Chadic
**Answer: d) Chadic** — Hausa sits in the Chadic branch — the placement says so.
