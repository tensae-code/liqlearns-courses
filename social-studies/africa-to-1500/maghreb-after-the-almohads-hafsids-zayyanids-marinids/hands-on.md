# Hands-on — The Maghreb after the Almohads — Hafsids, Zayyanids and Marinids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Maghreb after the Almohads — Hafsids, Zayyanids and Marinids — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The successor states of the Maghreb, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
