# The Maghreb after the Almohads — Hafsids, Zayyanids and Marinids

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: When the Almohads fade, Hafsids take Ifriqiya, Zayyanids Tlemcen and Marinids Morocco, and the Maghreb settles into three rival kingdoms. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
