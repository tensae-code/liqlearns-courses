# Bunyoro — rise, decline and Tooro

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Bunyoro, once the region's great power, declines because it is too big for one ruler, torn by internal conflict and pressed by rising neighbours — Tooro and Buganda — before the colonialists arrive. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
