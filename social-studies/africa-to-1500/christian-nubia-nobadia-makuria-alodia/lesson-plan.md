## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Christian Nubian kingdoms** — after Meroe falls the Nubians split into three Christian kingdoms — Nobadia at Faras, Makuria at Old Dongola, Alodia at Soba — building churches and living at peace with their neighbours until Islam arrives
2. **The Christian Nubian Kingdoms** — for nearly a thousand years the middle Nile is a land of churches, kings and painted saints
3. **The Entry of Christianity into Sudan** — a hunted faith climbs the Nile in refugee boats and returns as the religion of emperors and kings
4. **the Christian Nubian centuries** — between Meroe and the sultanates stand a thousand years of Nubian churches
