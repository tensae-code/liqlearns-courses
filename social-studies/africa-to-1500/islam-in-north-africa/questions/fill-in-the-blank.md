# Islam in North Africa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'a soldiers' post that could grow into a town' is ______.
**Answer: garrison**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'across the Sahara, the road linking the Maghreb to West African gold' is ______.
**Answer: trans-Saharan**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'building as one visible mark of the region's reshaping' is ______.
**Answer: architecture**
