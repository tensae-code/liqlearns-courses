# Islam in North Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is conquest?
a) Maghreb
b) the armed advance that first carried Islam across North Africa
c) garrison
**Answer: b) the armed advance that first carried Islam across North Africa**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is caravan?
a) scholars
b) the merchant convoy that carried goods — and Islam — across the desert
c) trans-Saharan
**Answer: b) the merchant convoy that carried goods — and Islam — across the desert**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Berber peoples?
a) architecture
b) Islamic world
c) the older peoples of North Africa, whose history runs through its Islamic centuries
**Answer: c) the older peoples of North Africa, whose history runs through its Islamic centuries**
