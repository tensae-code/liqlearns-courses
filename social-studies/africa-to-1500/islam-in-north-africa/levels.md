# Islam in North Africa — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conquest opens the door

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **conquest** (the armed advance that first carried Islam across North Africa).

## Sub lesson 2 — Trade and scholars do the deep work

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **caravan** (the merchant convoy that carried goods — and Islam — across the desert).

## Sub lesson 3 — A reshaped region

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Berber peoples** (the older peoples of North Africa, whose history runs through its Islamic centuries).
