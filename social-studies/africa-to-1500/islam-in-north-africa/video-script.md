# Video script — Islam in North Africa

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Conquest opens the door. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Islam first crossed North Africa by conquest, as Arab armies carried the new faith westward along the Mediterranean coast in the generation after the Prophet. Watch the names appear one by one: conquest, Maghreb, garrison. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Trade and scholars do the deep work. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The faith rooted itself by caravan and classroom: merchants carried Islam along the trade routes, and scholars and holy men taught it in the towns of the Maghreb and beyond.

By that road Islam crossed the Sahara too, linking North Africa to the gold lands of West Africa — the connection the next lessons trade on.. Watch the names appear one by one: caravan, scholars, trans-Saharan. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — A reshaped region. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Islam remade North Africa's law, learning, architecture and alliances, and bound the region into the wider Islamic world from Spain to Arabia.

Yet it arrived in a land with older peoples and tongues — the Berber peoples above all — whose history runs through the Islamic centuries, not under them.. Watch the names appear one by one: Berber peoples, Islamic world, architecture. This is the part of the story to hold.
