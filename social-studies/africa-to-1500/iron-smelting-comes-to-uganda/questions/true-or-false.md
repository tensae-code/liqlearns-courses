# Iron smelting comes to Uganda — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iron smelting begins with the Cushites at Meroe and is brought to Uganda by the Bachwezi; the blacksmith's pangas and hoes remake farming and war.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Iron smelting

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 5 — First Edition* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Iron smelting

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Iron smelting, begun (the book says) by the Cushites at Meroe, is brought into East Africa by the Bachwezi, and the blacksmith becomes the maker and repairer of the new tools.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Iron Age

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 6 — First Edition* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
