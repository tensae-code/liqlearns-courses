Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Portuguese sailors see a river mouth shaped like a hooded cloak — gabão — and a country gets its name (Leçon 26 L'origine du nom « Gabon »)

Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Migrations and mixtures over centuries make the peoples who are Gabonese today (Leçon 14 L'origine du peuple gabonais)
