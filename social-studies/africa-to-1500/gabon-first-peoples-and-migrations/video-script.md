# Video script — Gabon — first peoples and migrations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Gabon guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years; first peoples settle, then other populations arrive and mix

Portuguese sailors see a river mouth shaped like a hooded cloak — gabão — and a country gets its name

Migrations and mixtures over centuries make the peoples who are Gabonese today

Gabon's first peoples, called pygmées, hold the forest long before later arrivals

Hold that story: Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years — first peoples settle the forest, later peoples arrive and mix, and together they make the peoples of Gabon.
