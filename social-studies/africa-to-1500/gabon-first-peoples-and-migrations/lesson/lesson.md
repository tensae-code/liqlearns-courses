# Gabon — first peoples and migrations

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years — first peoples settle the forest, later peoples arrive and mix, and together they make the peoples of Gabon. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first peopling of Gabon

Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years; first peoples settle, then other populations arrive and mix (Leçon 19 Le peuplement du Gabon, les migrations)

[Picture slot — a picture of Gabon's first peoples settling in the forest, and later arrivals meeting and mixing with them — the student says who was in Gabon first]

## Sub lesson 2 — How Gabon got its name

Portuguese sailors see a river mouth shaped like a hooded cloak — gabão — and a country gets its name (Leçon 26 L'origine du nom « Gabon »)

## Sub lesson 3 — The origin of the Gabonese people

Migrations and mixtures over centuries make the peoples who are Gabonese today (Leçon 14 L'origine du peuple gabonais)

## Sub lesson 4 — Gabon's autochthonous peoples

Gabon's first peoples, called pygmées, hold the forest long before later arrivals (Leçon 11 Les peuples autochtones du Gabon)
