# Gabon — first peoples and migrations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The first peopling of Gabon  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The first peopling of Gabon** — Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years; first peoples settle, then other populations arrive and mix.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide this part is —
a) Leçon 19 Le peuplement du Gabon, les migrations  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Leçon 19 Le peuplement du Gabon, les migrations** — the guide's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) How Gabon got its name  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) How Gabon got its name** — Portuguese sailors see a river mouth shaped like a hooded cloak — gabão — and a country gets its name.
