# Gabon — first peoples and migrations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years; first peoples settle, then other populations arrive and mix
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 19 Le peuplement du Gabon, les migrations).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Portuguese sailors see a river mouth shaped like a hooded cloak — gabão — and a country gets its name
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 26 L'origine du nom « Gabon »).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Migrations and mixtures over centuries make the peoples who are Gabonese today
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 14 L'origine du peuple gabonais).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Gabon's first peoples, called pygmées, hold the forest long before later arrivals
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Leçon 11 Les peuples autochtones du Gabon).
