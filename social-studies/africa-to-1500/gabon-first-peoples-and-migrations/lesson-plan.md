# Lesson plan — Gabon — first peoples and migrations

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Gabon — first peoples and migrations
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa to 1500 — built in the History expansion (Gabon, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years — first peoples settle the forest, later peoples arrive and mix, and together they make the peoples of Gabon.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The first peopling of Gabon** — Gabon has been inhabited for thousands of years; first peoples settle, then other populations arrive and mix (Leçon 19 Le peuplement du Gabon, les migrations)
2. **How Gabon got its name** — Portuguese sailors see a river mouth shaped like a hooded cloak — gabão — and a country gets its name (Leçon 26 L'origine du nom « Gabon »)
3. **The origin of the Gabonese people** — migrations and mixtures over centuries make the peoples who are Gabonese today (Leçon 14 L'origine du peuple gabonais)
4. **Gabon's autochthonous peoples** — Gabon's first peoples, called pygmées, hold the forest long before later arrivals (Leçon 11 Les peuples autochtones du Gabon)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
