# Kingdoms of East Africa — centralised and decentralised — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Peoples ruled by kings live under centralised monarchy, those under chiefs under a decentralised system; every kingdom has one hereditary king, special regalia, classes, and royal tombs for its dead kings.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Kingdoms and chiefdoms

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Home-Study Learning — Primary 6 — Social Studies* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in State Formation in East Africa
