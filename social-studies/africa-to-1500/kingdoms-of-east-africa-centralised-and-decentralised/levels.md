This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kingdoms and chiefdoms
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — peoples ruled by kings live under centralised monarchy, those under chiefs under a decentralised system; every kingdom has one hereditary king, special regalia, classes, and royal tombs for its dead kings
