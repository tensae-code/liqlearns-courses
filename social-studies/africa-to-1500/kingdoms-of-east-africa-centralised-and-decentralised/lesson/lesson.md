# Kingdoms of East Africa — centralised and decentralised

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Peoples ruled by kings live under centralised monarchy, those under chiefs under a decentralised system; every kingdom has one hereditary king, special regalia, classes, and royal tombs for its dead kings. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
