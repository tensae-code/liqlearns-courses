No teacher-guide material is folded into this lesson: the Addis Ababa guide's Unit 5 covers Egypt and Nubia/Meroe only, so nothing here rests on teacher evidence. Care with the book's own printed words, exactly as the placement flags them: the market-centre rulers are taught as **wakilimi (as printed)** and the traditional beliefs as **Maklanjaru (as printed)** — the placement records both with that 'as printed' flag, so the lesson and its questions keep the flag and claim nothing beyond the book's printing. Azania's extent is taught as uncertain because the book itself leaves it uncertain.
