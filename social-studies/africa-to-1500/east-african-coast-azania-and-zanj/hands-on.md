# Hands-on — East African Coast — Azania and Zanj

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Coast in a line
Lay a long rope as the coast, Horn at one end, Mozambique at the other. Place market cards (town, port, market) along it for Azania, then king cards along it for Zanj, and walk the rope naming which name each card belongs to.

## Two-arrow coast trade
On paper, draw Azania's trade with South Arabia: ivory going out across the sea, iron goods and weapons coming in. Label the sailors: Himyarites of South Arabia.
