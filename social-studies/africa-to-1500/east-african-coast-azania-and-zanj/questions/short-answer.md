# East African Coast — Azania and Zanj — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Azania's trade carry, each way?
**Answer:** Ivory went out to the Himyarites of South Arabia; iron goods and weapons came in.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe Zanj's rulers and people as the book prints them.
**Answer:** Ruled by kings including market-centre rulers (wakilimi as printed); its people mostly Bantu-speaking of Arab and Persian descent as the book presents them, herding cattle, with traditional beliefs (Maklanjaru as printed) and planet, animal and mineral gods.
