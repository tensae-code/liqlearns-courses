# East African Coast — Azania and Zanj — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Azania's societies held —
a) stone enclosures  b) towns, ports and markets  c) pyramids  d) nomes
**Answer: b) towns, ports and markets** — towns, ports and markets.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Azania traded ivory with —
a) Rome  b) the Himyarites of South Arabia  c) Morocco  d) the Phoenicians
**Answer: b) the Himyarites of South Arabia** — the Himyarites, who brought iron goods and weapons.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Zanj stretched —
a) from Egypt to Napata  b) from the Horn to Mozambique  c) along the Niger  d) around Madagascar
**Answer: b) from the Horn to Mozambique** — the Horn to Mozambique.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Zanj's people, as the book presents them, were mostly —
a) Bantu-speaking, of Arab and Persian descent  b) Soninke farmers  c) Phoenician sailors  d) Khoisan speakers
**Answer: a) Bantu-speaking, of Arab and Persian descent** — mostly Bantu-speaking of Arab and Persian descent, herding cattle.
