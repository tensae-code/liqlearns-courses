# Video script — East African Coast — Azania and Zanj

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One coast, two old names.

Azania — towns, ports, markets.
Ivory sailed out to South Arabia.
Iron and weapons sailed in.

Zanj — the long coast,
from the Horn down to Mozambique.
Kings over market towns. Cattle on the shore.
Old beliefs, and gods of planet, animal and stone.

Remember these names.
The Swahili cities are about to rise on this coast.
