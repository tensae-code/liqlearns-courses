# Lesson plan — East African Coast — Azania and Zanj

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** East African Coast — Azania and Zanj
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Africa to 1500 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the East African coast under two old names — Azania, whose towns and ports traded ivory with South Arabia, and Zanj, the coast from the Horn to Mozambique with its kings, market towns and cattle-herding peoples.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Great Zimbabwe lesson before it in this title — the learner turns from inland stone to the trading coast. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Azania** — the East African coast, its exact extent uncertain; societies with towns, ports and markets; ivory traded with the Himyarites of South Arabia, who brought iron goods and weapons.
2. **Zanj** — the coast from the Horn to Mozambique; kings including market-centre rulers (wakilimi as printed); people mostly Bantu-speaking of Arab and Persian descent as the book presents them, herding cattle; traditional beliefs (Maklanjaru as printed) and planet, animal and mineral gods.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Southern African States — Great Zimbabwe. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Azania was, what its societies held, and what it traded with the Himyarites — each way.
2. Say what land Zanj covered, who ruled its market centres (as printed), and how its people lived.
3. Give Zanj's beliefs as the book prints them.

## If the learner is stuck
- Treats Azania and Zanj as two different coasts → both names are for the East African coast — Azania the older trading name, Zanj the long coast from the Horn to Mozambique.
- Drops the 'as printed' care → wakilimi and Maklanjaru are the book's own printed words for the rulers and the beliefs; the lesson keeps them exactly as printed.
