# Video script — Cultural leaders in pre-colonial Uganda

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before the state, cultural leaders settle disputes, protect the community, unite the people and keep the ceremonies of birth, marriage and circumcision.
