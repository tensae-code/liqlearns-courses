# Egypt and Africa in the Middle Ages

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Medieval Cairo shelters the caliphate, arms and recognises African kings, sends Ethiopia its bishop, houses African students at al-Azhar, and stands as middleman of the Sahara trade. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt's civilisational role in Africa

Cairo shelters the caliphate after Baghdad falls; African kings seek Mamluk recognition and Egyptian weapon-craft; Ethiopia's church receives its bishop from Alexandria; al-Azhar houses African students in their own riwaqs with stipends; and Sahara caravans make Egypt the middleman of the continent's trade.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
