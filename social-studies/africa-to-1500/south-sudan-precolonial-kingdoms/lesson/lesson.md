# South Sudan — its pre-colonial kingdoms

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Before Europeans, South Sudan's main kingdoms were Azande, Otuho, Chollo (Shilluk) and Anyuak, while Moru-Madi, Luo and Atker had no kings but chiefs, clan heads and family heads. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
