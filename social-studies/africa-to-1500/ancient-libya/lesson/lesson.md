# Ancient Libya

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A green land of elephants and giraffes dries into the Sahara; dinosaur bones at Nalut, giraffe carvings in the Acacus and pottery at Murzuq are the evidence its first peoples left. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
