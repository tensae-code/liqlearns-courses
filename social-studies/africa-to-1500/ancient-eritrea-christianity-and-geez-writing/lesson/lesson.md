# Ancient Eritrea — Christianity and Geez writing

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: in the 4th century the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius brought Christianity to the Eritrean highlands; the Sabean alphabet gave way to Geez, Aksumite coins changed their symbols, and by the 5th century the Bible was translated into Geez — while the first Muslims reached Dahlak in 610–614 CE. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Christianity and Islam arrive

Aksumite coins change their symbols as Christianity comes in the 4th century through Frumentius, and the first Muslims reach Dahlak in 610–614 CE.
