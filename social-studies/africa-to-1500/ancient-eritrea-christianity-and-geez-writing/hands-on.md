# Hands-on — Ancient Eritrea — Christianity and Geez writing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw two coins: one with an old symbol, one with a Christian symbol, and write what changed between them.

## Task 2
Write your name twice — once in the alphabet you know, once as a made-up 'old alphabet' — and say why a new faith might bring a new writing with it.
