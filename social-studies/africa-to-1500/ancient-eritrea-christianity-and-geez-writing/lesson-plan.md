# Lesson plan — Ancient Eritrea — Christianity and Geez writing

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Eritrea — Christianity and Geez writing
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa to 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the 4th century the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius brought Christianity to the Eritrean highlands; the Sabean alphabet gave way to Geez, Aksumite coins changed their symbols, and by the 5th century the Bible was translated into Geez — while the first Muslims reached Dahlak in 610–614 CE.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Christianity and Islam arrive** — Aksumite coins change their symbols as Christianity comes in the 4th century through Frumentius, and the first Muslims reach Dahlak in 610–614 CE (Christianity and Islam arrive)
2. **Religion, language and writing** — South Arabian and Greek gods give way in the mid-4th century when the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius bring Christianity, the Sabean alphabet yields to Geez, and by the 5th century the Bible is translated into Geez (Religion, language and writing)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Answer, in their own words: how do the coins show that Christianity had arrived?
2. Answer, in their own words: name the two monks and what each change they are tied to in this lesson.
