# Ancient Eritrea — Christianity and Geez writing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aksumite coins changed their symbols when Christianity came.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 5 book reads the coins as evidence of the change of faith.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Geez writing disappeared when Christianity arrived.
**Answer: False** — Geez rose with Christianity — the Bible was translated into it.
