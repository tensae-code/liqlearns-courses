# Ancient Eritrea — Christianity and Geez writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Christianity was brought by —
a) Frumentius and Aedesius  b) the Naibs  c) Portuguese sailors
**Answer: a) Frumentius and Aedesius** — the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius, in the mid-4th century.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The alphabet that gave way to Geez was —
a) Latin  b) Sabean  c) Arabic
**Answer: b) Sabean** — the Sabean alphabet yielded to Geez.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** By the 5th century, what had been translated into Geez?
a) the Bible  b) the Periplus  c) the tax lists
**Answer: a) the Bible** — the Bible was translated into Geez by the 5th century.
