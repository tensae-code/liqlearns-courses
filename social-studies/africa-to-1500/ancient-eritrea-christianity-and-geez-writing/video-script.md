# Video script — Ancient Eritrea — Christianity and Geez writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Aksumite coins change their symbols as Christianity comes in the 4th century through Frumentius, and the first Muslims reach Dahlak in 610–614 CE.

South Arabian and Greek gods give way in the mid-4th century when the Syrian monks Frumentius and Aedesius bring Christianity, the Sabean alphabet yields to Geez, and by the 5th century the Bible is translated into Geez.
