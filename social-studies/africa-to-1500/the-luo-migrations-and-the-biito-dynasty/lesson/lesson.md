# The Luo migrations and the Biito dynasty

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Luo speakers migrate from Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan to the lakes, bringing millet, sorghum and sweet potatoes, founding the Luo-Biito dynasty — and displacing those they find. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: The River-Lake Nilotes — Luo speakers migrate from Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan to the lakes, bringing millet, sorghum and sweet potatoes, founding the Luo-Biito dynasty — and displacing those they find; The River-Lake Nilotes — the Luo speakers leave Bahr-el-Ghazal in South Sudan and settle by the lakes and rivers that name them; The Luo — the River-Lake Nilotes come from Bahr el Gazel in Southern Sudan, pastoralists whose family fills the map — Dinka, Alur, Luo, Shilluk, Bor, Anuak, Acholi.
