# Zimbabwe's pre-colonial states — Mutapa, Rozvi and the Ndebele

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Before the colony, Zimbabwe's land held great states with three different answers to how a kingdom is made: Nyatsimba Mutota's Mutapa, built on gold and trade with the Portuguese; Changamire Dombo's Rozvi, holding power by the spear; and Mzilikazi's Ndebele state, built in ranks. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mutapa, Rozvi and the Ndebele state (Grade 5)

Three kingdoms, three answers. Nyatsimba Mutota founds Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese. Changamire Dombo's Rozvi hold power by the spear. Mzilikazi builds the Ndebele state in ranks. The class lays them side by side and asks: how is a kingdom made?
