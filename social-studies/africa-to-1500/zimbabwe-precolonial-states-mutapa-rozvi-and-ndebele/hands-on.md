# Hands-on — Zimbabwe's pre-colonial states — Mutapa, Rozvi and the Ndebele

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Make a comparison table of Mutapa, Rozvi and the Ndebele state: founder, source of power, and how each held together.

## Task 2
Write a trader's diary entry from Mutapa's gold country, naming what was traded and with whom.
