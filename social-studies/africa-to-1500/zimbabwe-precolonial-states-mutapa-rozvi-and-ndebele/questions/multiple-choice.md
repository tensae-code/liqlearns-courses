# Zimbabwe's pre-colonial states — Mutapa, Rozvi and the Ndebele — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mutapa was founded by —
a) Nyatsimba Mutota  b) Changamire Dombo  c) Mzilikazi
**Answer: a) Nyatsimba Mutota** — Nyatsimba Mutota founds Mutapa

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Rozvi held power —
a) by the spear  b) by the printing press  c) by the railway
**Answer: a) by the spear** — Changamire Dombo's Rozvi hold power by the spear

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Mzilikazi built the Ndebele state —
a) in ranks  b) on gold trade only  c) as a republic
**Answer: a) in ranks** — Mzilikazi builds the Ndebele state in ranks
