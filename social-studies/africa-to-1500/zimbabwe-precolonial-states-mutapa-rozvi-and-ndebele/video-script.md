# Video script — Zimbabwe's pre-colonial states — Mutapa, Rozvi and the Ndebele

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Zimbabwe's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three kingdoms, three answers. Nyatsimba Mutota founds Mutapa on gold and trade with the Portuguese. Changamire Dombo's Rozvi hold power by the spear. Mzilikazi builds the Ndebele state in ranks. The class lays them side by side and asks: how is a kingdom made?
