# Video script — Zimbabwe's early people — the Khoisan and the Bantu migration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Zimbabwe's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

About 2,000 years ago, newcomers walk into the Khoisan's land. The Bantu carry two world-changing things — iron-working and farming. They settle in permanent villages, keep cattle, and in time become the peoples of the land: Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others.
