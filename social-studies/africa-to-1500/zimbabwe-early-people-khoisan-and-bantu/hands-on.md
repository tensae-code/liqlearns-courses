# Hands-on — Zimbabwe's early people — the Khoisan and the Bantu migration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Make a two-column chart: what the Khoisan carried (bow, stone tools, movement) against what the Bantu newcomers carried (iron, farming, cattle, villages).

## Task 2
On a map of Zimbabwe, write the names of the peoples the Bantu settlers became — Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda.
