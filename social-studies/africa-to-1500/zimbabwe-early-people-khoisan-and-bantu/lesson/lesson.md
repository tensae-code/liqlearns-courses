# Zimbabwe's early people — the Khoisan and the Bantu migration

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: About 2,000 years ago Bantu newcomers walked into the Khoisan's land carrying iron-working and farming, settled in permanent villages with cattle, and became the Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others — the peoples of Zimbabwe. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Bantu migration into Zimbabwe (Grade 4)

About 2,000 years ago, newcomers walk into the Khoisan's land. The Bantu carry two world-changing things — iron-working and farming. They settle in permanent villages, keep cattle, and in time become the peoples of the land: Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: the bantu migration into zimbabwe. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
