# Zimbabwe's early people — the Khoisan and the Bantu migration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three peoples the Bantu settlers became in Zimbabwe.
**Answer: Any three of Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose land did the Bantu newcomers walk into?
**Answer: The Khoisan's land (any close wording).**
