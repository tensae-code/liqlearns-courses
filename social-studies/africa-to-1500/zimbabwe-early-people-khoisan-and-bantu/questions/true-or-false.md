# Zimbabwe's early people — the Khoisan and the Bantu migration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bantu migration brought iron-working to Zimbabwe.
**Answer: True** — iron-working and farming are what the newcomers carried

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bantu settlers became only one people.
**Answer: False** — they became many — Karanga, Zezuru, Manyika, Ndau, Ndebele, Tonga, Venda and others
