# Zimbabwe's early people — the Khoisan and the Bantu migration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bantu newcomers arrived in the Khoisan's land about —
a) 2,000 years ago  b) 200 years ago  c) 20,000 years ago
**Answer: a) 2,000 years ago** — the migration is dated to about 2,000 years ago

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two world-changing things the Bantu carried were —
a) iron-working and farming  b) guns and horses  c) paper and printing
**Answer: a) iron-working and farming** — they carried iron-working and farming

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Unlike the moving Khoisan, the Bantu settlers —
a) settled in permanent villages with cattle  b) lived only in caves  c) never kept animals
**Answer: a) settled in permanent villages with cattle** — they settle in permanent villages with cattle
