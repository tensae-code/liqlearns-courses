# Axum — trading kingdom of the Red Sea

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Axum, where the trade roads of the Red Sea meet, exports gold, ivory, perfumes and spices and is famed for its farming, pottery and glass. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The civilisation of Axum

Axum, in today's Eritrea and Ethiopia, sits where the trade roads meet, exporting gold, ivory, perfumes and spices, and is famed for its farming, pottery and glass.
