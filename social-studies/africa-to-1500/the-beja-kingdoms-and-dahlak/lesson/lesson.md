# The Beja kingdoms and Dahlak

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: across the Red Sea the faiths arrived early — Christianity about 350 CE, and Islam's first refuge at Dahlak in 615 CE — and the Beja raised five kingdoms in the western lowlands while Dahlak ruled its islands. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The faiths arrive by sea

Christianity reaches Eritrea across the Red Sea about 350 CE, and in 615 CE the first Muslims find refuge in Dahlak, planting Islam on the islands and coast.

In your mind's eye: the key place or moment of The Beja kingdoms and Dahlak as the book shows it. Point to it and say what it shows.

## Sub lesson 2 — Eritrea in the Medieval Age

The Dahlak kingdom rules the islands, the Beja hold the western lowlands, and mosques and inscriptions mark a coast turned toward the Islamic world.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Bejas and Islam

The Zenafij Beja push through the Barka Valley at the end of the 7th century and raise five kingdoms — Naqis, Baqlin, Bazin, Jarin, Qata — while Arab Muslims found the state of Dahlak and Islam spreads from the coast inland.
