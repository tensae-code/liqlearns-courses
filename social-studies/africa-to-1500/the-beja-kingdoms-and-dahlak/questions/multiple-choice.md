# The Beja kingdoms and Dahlak — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The five Beja kingdoms included —
a) Naqis, Baqlin and Bazin  b) Aksum and Adulis  c) Keren and Asmara
**Answer: a) Naqis, Baqlin and Bazin** — Naqis, Baqlin, Bazin, Jarin and Qata.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first Muslims found refuge at Dahlak in —
a) 615 CE  b) 1557 CE  c) 1890 CE
**Answer: a) 615 CE** — 615 CE, says the Grade 5 book.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Christianity reached Eritrea about —
a) 350 CE  b) 100 BCE  c) 1500 CE
**Answer: a) 350 CE** — across the Red Sea, about 350 CE.
