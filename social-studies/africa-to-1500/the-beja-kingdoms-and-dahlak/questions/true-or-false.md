# The Beja kingdoms and Dahlak — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Islam spread from the coast inland after Dahlak was founded.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 6 book tells of Islam spreading from the coast inland.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Beja kingdoms were on the Red Sea islands.
**Answer: False** — the Beja held the western lowlands; Dahlak ruled the islands.
