# The Beja kingdoms and Dahlak — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five Beja kingdoms.
**Answer: Naqis, Baqlin, Bazin, Jarin and Qata.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two faiths arrived by sea, and when?
**Answer: Christianity about 350 CE, and Islam — its first refugees reached Dahlak in 615 CE (any close wording).**
