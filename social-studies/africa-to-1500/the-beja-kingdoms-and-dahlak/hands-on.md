# Hands-on — The Beja kingdoms and Dahlak

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the five Beja kingdoms on five fingers, one per finger, until you can say them without looking.

## Task 2
Draw the two shores of the story: islands (Dahlak) and lowlands (the Beja), and write which faith and which rulers belong to each.
