# Video script — Ethiopia — kingdoms and faiths before 1800

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Nigeria book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Highland and lowland peoples fill Ethiopia's lands. Aksum's Christianity and the arrival of Islam shape the country's faiths over long centuries.

The Gondar emperors rule in the highlands. The Nigerian book surveys all this as the background to a country never colonised.

Hold that story: Ethiopia before Theodore is a land of highland and lowland peoples with a long history — Aksum's Christianity, the arrival of Islam and the Gondar emperors — the background to a country never colonised.
