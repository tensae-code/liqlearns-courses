# Ethiopia — kingdoms and faiths before 1800

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ethiopia before Theodore is a land of highland and lowland peoples with a long history — Aksum's Christianity, the arrival of Islam and the Gondar emperors — the background to a country never colonised. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Highlands, lowlands and Aksum's faith

Highland and lowland peoples fill Ethiopia's lands. Aksum's Christianity and the arrival of Islam shape the country's faiths over long centuries.

In your mind's eye: ethiopia's highlands with an old stone church and a mosque in a lowland town. Name Aksum's Christianity, Islam's arrival and the Gondar emperors.
