# Ethiopia — kingdoms and faiths before 1800 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which ancient kingdom's Christianity does the survey name?
a) Aksum  b) Kush  c) Ghana
**Answer: a) Aksum** — Aksum's Christianity is named in the survey.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which second faith's arrival does the survey name?
a) Buddhism  b) Islam  c) Hinduism
**Answer: b) Islam** — Islam's arrival is surveyed beside Christianity.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which emperors does the survey end with before Theodore?
a) The Solomonic restoration only  b) No emperors are named  c) The Gondar emperors
**Answer: c) The Gondar emperors** — the Gondar emperors close the survey.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia is presented in this book as a country that was —
a) Never colonised  b) A British colony  c) A Portuguese province
**Answer: a) Never colonised** — the background to a country never colonised.
