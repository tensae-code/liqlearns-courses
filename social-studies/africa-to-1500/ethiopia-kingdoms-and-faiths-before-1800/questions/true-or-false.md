# Ethiopia — kingdoms and faiths before 1800 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia's peoples lived in both highlands and lowlands.
**Answer: True** — highland and lowland peoples are surveyed.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia had been colonised by a European power before 1800.
**Answer: False** — the book presents a country never colonised.
