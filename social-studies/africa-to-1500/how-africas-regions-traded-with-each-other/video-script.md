# Video script — How Africa's regions traded with each other

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Four great exchange systems. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Close the Africa-to-1500 unit by drawing its map whole. Watch the names appear one by one: trans-Saharan trade, monsoon trade, exchange system. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — What moved, and why it mattered. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Salt went south where people sweated and lacked it; gold went north where kings coined and coveted it; ivory and slaves moved to the coasts; books and beliefs travelled with the merchants.

Trade did more than enrich: it built the Sudanic empires, the Swahili cities and Aksum's successor states, and it carried Islam and Christianity into new lands.. Watch the names appear one by one: salt, gold, Sudanic empires. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — The continent as one story. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
This is the unit's verdict: study Africa's regions together, because Africans did. Watch the names appear one by one: verdict, routes, outsiders. This is the part of the story to hold.
