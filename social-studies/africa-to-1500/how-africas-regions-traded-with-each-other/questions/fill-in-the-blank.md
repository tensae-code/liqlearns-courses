# How Africa's regions traded with each other — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'a whole web of routes moving goods, faiths and ideas together' is ______.
**Answer: exchange system**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'Ghana, Mali and Songhai — states built on the desert-edge trade' is ______.
**Answer: Sudanic empires**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the Europeans and others whose arrival the next unit tells' is ______.
**Answer: outsiders**
