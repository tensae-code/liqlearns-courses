# The Wolof and Fula states of Senegambia

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A stranger who divides the wood fairly is offered marriage and kingship and unites five Wolof states — Walo, Baol, Jolof, Cayor and one more; his successors can be removed by the nobles, and a king's death is kept secret to prevent succession wars. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
