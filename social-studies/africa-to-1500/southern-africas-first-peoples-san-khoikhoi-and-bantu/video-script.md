# Video script — Southern Africa's first peoples — San, Khoikhoi and Bantu

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Nigeria book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Click-speaking San hunter-gatherers and Khoikhoi herders hold the land for centuries — the earlier peoples of Southern Africa.

After the San and Khoikhoi come Bantu farming chiefdoms, spreading across Southern Africa and farming the land.
