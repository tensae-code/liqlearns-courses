# Lesson plan — Southern Africa's first peoples — San, Khoikhoi and Bantu

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Southern Africa's first peoples — San, Khoikhoi and Bantu
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa to 1500 — built in the History expansion (Nigeria, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Click-speaking San hunter-gatherers and Khoikhoi herders hold Southern Africa for centuries before Bantu farming chiefdoms spread across the land.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **San hunters and Khoikhoi herders** — Click-speaking San hunter-gatherers and Khoikhoi herders hold the land for centuries — the earlier peoples of Southern Africa.
2. **Bantu farming chiefdoms spread** — After the San and Khoikhoi come Bantu farming chiefdoms, spreading across Southern Africa and farming the land.
