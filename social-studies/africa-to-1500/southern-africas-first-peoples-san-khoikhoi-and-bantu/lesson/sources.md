Nigeria's own way: Nigeria's SSS history course deliberately teaches other regions' histories only for their effect on Nigeria and Africa — the preface frames the whole three-volume series as a decolonisation of African history, built by the curriculum's own authors (NERDC, Lagos 1985 / Kano 1986 workshops); Volume 2 makes SS2 pupils compare regions side by side (Dahomey with Buganda, Egypt with Zanzibar, the Great Trek as the road to apartheid in Volume 3). Each chapter closes with multiple-choice + essay Questions and a Further reading list drawn heavily on the UNESCO General History of Africa.
