# Southern Africa's first peoples — San, Khoikhoi and Bantu

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Click-speaking San hunter-gatherers and Khoikhoi herders hold Southern Africa for centuries before Bantu farming chiefdoms spread across the land. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — San hunters and Khoikhoi herders

Click-speaking San hunter-gatherers and Khoikhoi herders hold the land for centuries — the earlier peoples of Southern Africa.

In your mind's eye: san hunters and Khoikhoi herders on open land, with Bantu farming villages appearing in the distance. Name each people and how they lived.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bantu farming chiefdoms spread
