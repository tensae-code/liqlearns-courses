# Southern Africa's first peoples — San, Khoikhoi and Bantu — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Southern Africa's two earlier peoples and how each lived.
**Answer: The San (hunter-gatherers) and the Khoikhoi (herders) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who came after the San and Khoikhoi, in the book's order?
**Answer: Bantu farming chiefdoms spreading across the land (any close wording).**
