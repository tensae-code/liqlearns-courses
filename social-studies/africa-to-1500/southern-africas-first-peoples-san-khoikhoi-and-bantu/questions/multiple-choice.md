# Southern Africa's first peoples — San, Khoikhoi and Bantu — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The San lived by —
a) Hunting and gathering  b) Herding cattle only  c) Farming only
**Answer: a) Hunting and gathering** — San hunter-gatherers.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Khoikhoi lived by —
a) Mining  b) Herding  c) Sea trade
**Answer: b) Herding** — Khoikhoi herders.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Bantu peoples who spread across the land were —
a) Sea traders  b) Desert nomads only  c) Farming chiefdoms
**Answer: c) Farming chiefdoms** — Bantu farming chiefdoms.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The San are described in the book by their —
a) Click-speaking language  b) Iron spears  c) Stone cities
**Answer: a) Click-speaking language** — click-speaking San.
