# Southern Africa's first peoples — San, Khoikhoi and Bantu — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The San and Khoikhoi held Southern Africa for centuries before the Bantu chiefdoms.
**Answer: True** — for centuries, in the book's telling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Bantu were the first people ever to live in Southern Africa.
**Answer: False** — San and Khoikhoi held the land before them.
