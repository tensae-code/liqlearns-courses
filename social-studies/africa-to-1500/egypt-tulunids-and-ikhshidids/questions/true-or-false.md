# Egypt — the Tulunids and Ikhshidids — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ahmad ibn Tulun, the Turkish officer who came as a deputy in 868, gathers Egypt's reins, wins her people's love with justice and generosity, and stretches his state into the Levant as its shield; his son Khumarawayh defeats the caliphate's armies and wins a thirty-year grant of Egypt and the Levant — the first independent Egyptian state of the Islamic age, followed by Muhammad ibn Tughj al-Ikhshid's.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Egypt in the Tulunid and Ikhshidid ages

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Egypt in the Tulunid and Ikhshidid ages
