# Hands-on — Egypt — the Tulunids and Ikhshidids

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Egypt — the Tulunids and Ikhshidids — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Egypt in the Tulunid and Ikhshidid ages, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
