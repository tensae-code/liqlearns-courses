# Video script — Foreign merchants in Mozambique from the 9th century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From the 9th century, for almost ten centuries, Asian then European merchants penetrate Mozambique — a long, strictly commercial link to the world, with no political domination intended.
