# Hands-on — The Fatimids — from Tunisia to Cairo

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Fatimids — from Tunisia to Cairo — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Fatimid State (297–567 / 909–1171): the Ismaili da'wa wins the Kutama Berbers, Abu Abdallah al-Shi'i's army destroys the Aghla, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
