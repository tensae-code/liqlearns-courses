# Eritrea — early migrations and peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eritrea's peoples arrived —
a) all on one day  b) wave after wave, over thousands of years  c) from Europe
**Answer: b) wave after wave, over thousands of years** — the books tell of wave after wave of settlement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kushite herders reached Eritrea about —
a) 2000 BCE  b) 1000 CE  c) 1900 CE
**Answer: a) 2000 BCE** — the Grade 6 book dates the Kushite herders to about 2000 BCE.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Semites from Arabia brought —
a) trains and cars  b) camels, horses and new crops  c) steam ships
**Answer: b) camels, horses and new crops** — camels, horses and new crops came with them, about 1000 BCE.
