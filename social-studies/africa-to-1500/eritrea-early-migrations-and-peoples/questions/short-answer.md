# Eritrea — early migrations and peoples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three peoples who settled in Eritrea in the early waves.
**Answer: Kushites, Semites and Beja (also accepted: Nilotes, Bilen, Saho, Afar — any three named in the lesson).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the Semites from Arabia bring with them?
**Answer: Camels, horses and new crops (any close wording).**
