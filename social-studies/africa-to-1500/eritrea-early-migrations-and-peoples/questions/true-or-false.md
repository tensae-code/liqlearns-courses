# Eritrea — early migrations and peoples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eritrea was peopled by a single group arriving once.
**Answer: False** — many groups arrived in waves — Nilotes, Kushites, Semites, Beja, Afar and others.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Beja are among the later peoples who became part of Eritrea.
**Answer: True** — both books name the Beja among the later arrivals.
