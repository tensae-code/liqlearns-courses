# Eritrea — early migrations and peoples

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: eritrea was peopled wave after wave — Kushites, Semites and later Beja, Bilen, Saho and the rest move in and become the peoples of Eritrea. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The settlement of Eritrea's peoples

Kushites, Semites and later Beja, Bilen, Saho and the rest move in wave after wave and become the peoples of Eritrea.

In your mind's eye: the key place or moment of Eritrea — early migrations and peoples as the book shows it: point to it and say what it shows.

## Sub lesson 2 — The first peoples of Eritrea

Pygmoid first inhabitants are joined by Nilotes, then Kushite herders about 2000 BCE, then Semites from Arabia about 1000 BCE bringing camels, horses and new crops, and later Beja, Arab Muslims in Dahlak and Afar groups complete today's peoples.
