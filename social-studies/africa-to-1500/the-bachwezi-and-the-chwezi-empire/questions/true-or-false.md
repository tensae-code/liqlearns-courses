# The Bachwezi and the Chwezi empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Political Organisation — the Chwezi empire

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 5 — First Edition* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Political Organisation — the Chwezi empire

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Bachwezi's gifts are listed like a king's inventory — salt mining at Lake Katwe, iron smelting, long-horned cattle, coffee, bark cloth.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Political organisation of ethnic groups

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Excel Standard Social Studies, Pupil's Book 6 — First Edition* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Political organisation of ethnic groups

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Coffee, iron working, pottery and the salt mines of Lake Katwe — the Bachwezi are remembered as teachers of crafts.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Economic contributions of the Bachwezi

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Home-Study Learning — Primary 6 — Social Studies* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Economic contributions of the Bachwezi

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The Batembuzi, gods come from heaven, found Bunyoro; the Chwezi who follow prize long-horned cattle, grow coffee, beans and millet, make pottery, and play omweso in grass-thatched huts smeared with cow dung.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bachwezi Dynasty

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *History and Political Education Textbook — Senior One (Lower Secondary Curriculum, PROTOTYPE)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bachwezi Dynasty
