## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Political Organisation — the Chwezi empire** — the Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold
2. **Political organisation of ethnic groups** — the Bachwezi's gifts are listed like a king's inventory — salt mining at Lake Katwe, iron smelting, long-horned cattle, coffee, bark cloth
3. **Economic contributions of the Bachwezi** — coffee, iron working, pottery and the salt mines of Lake Katwe — the Bachwezi are remembered as teachers of crafts
4. **Bachwezi Dynasty** — the Batembuzi, gods come from heaven, found Bunyoro; the Chwezi who follow prize long-horned cattle, grow coffee, beans and millet, make pottery, and play omweso in grass-thatched huts smeared with cow dung
