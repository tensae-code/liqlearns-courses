## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Bachwezi build an empire on iron — strong tools to cultivate, strong weapons to expand — and lose it, the book says, because it grew too large to hold. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
