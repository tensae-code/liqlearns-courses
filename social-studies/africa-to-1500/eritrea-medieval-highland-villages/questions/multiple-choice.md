# Eritrea — medieval highland villages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The highland villages grew —
a) wheat, barley, teff and sorghum  b) coffee and cotton only  c) rice and maize
**Answer: a) wheat, barley, teff and sorghum** — wheat, barley, teff and sorghum.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Debre Bizen was —
a) a port  b) a monastery holding land  c) a market town
**Answer: b) a monastery holding land** — a monastery whose land was worked by tenants.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Self-sufficient' villages —
a) bought all their food  b) grew what they needed themselves  c) paid tax in rifles
**Answer: b) grew what they needed themselves** — they grew what they needed themselves.
