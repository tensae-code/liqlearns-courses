# Eritrea — medieval highland villages — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tenants worked land held by churches and monasteries.
**Answer: True** — Debre Bizen's land was worked by tenants.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The medieval highland villages depended on sea trade for food.
**Answer: False** — they were self-sufficient farming villages.
