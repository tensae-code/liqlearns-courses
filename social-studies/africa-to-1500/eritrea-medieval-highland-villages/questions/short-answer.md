# Eritrea — medieval highland villages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four crops of the highland villages.
**Answer: Wheat, barley, teff and sorghum.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who held land besides the village farmers, and who worked it?
**Answer: Churches and monasteries such as Debre Bizen held land, worked by tenants (any close wording).**
