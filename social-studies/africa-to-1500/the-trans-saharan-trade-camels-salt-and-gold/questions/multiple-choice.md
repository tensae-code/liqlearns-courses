# The Trans-Saharan trade — camels, salt and gold — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before the regular caravan trade, contact across the Sahara was —
a) Constant  b) Irregular  c) Impossible  d) Only by sea
**Answer: b) Irregular — Small, risky crossings long preceded the system.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The regular caravan trade was an expansion of —
a) Roman roads  b) Older intra- and inter-regional trade  c) Portuguese shipping  d) Nile trade only
**Answer: b) Older intra- and inter-regional trade — Short hops linked into a road.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The caravan trade became regular after —
a) The horse's introduction  b) The camel's introduction  c) Ships reached the coast  d) The wheel was invented
**Answer: b) The camel's introduction — The camel made the crossing a business.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The caravans ran regularly from —
a) The 1st century BC  b) The 4th century  c) The 11th century  d) The 16th century
**Answer: b) The 4th century — The book dates regular caravans from the 4th century.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The trade built, on both sides of the desert —
a) Only markets  b) Robust economic and political systems  c) Roman colonies  d) Churches
**Answer: b) Robust economic and political systems — Capitals, taxes and route-guarding armies on both shores.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** With Islam's spread, the trade strengthened relations between —
a) Europe and Asia  b) North Africa and tropical Africa  c) Egypt and Nubia only  d) Spain and France
**Answer: b) North Africa and tropical Africa — Faith travelled the same routes as the goods.**
