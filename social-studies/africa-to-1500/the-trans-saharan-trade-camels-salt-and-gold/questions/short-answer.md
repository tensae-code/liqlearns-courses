# The Trans-Saharan trade — camels, salt and gold — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: what changed when the camel was introduced?
**Answer: Crossings became regular and commercial from the 4th century — scheduled, taxed and guarded caravans instead of rare, risky journeys.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: name two systems the trade built on the desert's southern side.
**Answer: Answers drawn from the lesson: market towns and capitals, tax systems, and armies to guard the routes — the empires of Ghana, Mali and Songhai grew on them.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: why is the desert called a bridge in this lesson?
**Answer: Because trade and Islam crossed it together, linking North and tropical Africa in goods, faith, law and learning.**
