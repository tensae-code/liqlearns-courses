# The Trans-Saharan trade — camels, salt and gold — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: Salt travelled south and gold north in the caravan trade.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The Sahara trade began from nothing when the camel arrived.
**Answer: False — It expanded older regional trade.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The caravan trade weakened North–tropical Africa relations.
**Answer: False — With Islam's spread it strengthened them.**
