# The Trans-Saharan trade — camels, salt and gold — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The animal whose introduction made regular Sahara crossings possible was the ____.
**Answer: camel**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The caravans ran regularly from the ____ century.
**Answer: 4th**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The caravans carried ____ slabs south and gold north.
**Answer: salt**
