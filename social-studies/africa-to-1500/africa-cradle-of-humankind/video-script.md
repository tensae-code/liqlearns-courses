# Video script — Africa, cradle of humankind — Chad, Afar and Lucy

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map of Africa opens. Dark. Then — a light in the north. Chad. Seven million years.
The camera drops south, to Ethiopia's burning Afar. Aramis. 4.4 million years — Ardipithecus.
East a little. Hadar, 1974. Brushes move. A skeleton takes shape in the dust.
Lucy. Dinkinesh — you are wonderful. Three million years old, and she walked upright.
Every human story starts on this map. This is the cradle.
