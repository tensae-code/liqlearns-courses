# Africa, cradle of humankind — Chad, Afar and Lucy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa is presented as the centre of the human-origins evidence.
**Answer: True** — The unit's framing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Lucy was found complete with written records of her life.
**Answer: False** — She is a fossil, 3.18 million years old — long before writing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** East Africa's importance for human-origin study is a question the book sets pupils to argue.
**Answer: True** — One of the section's own tasks.
