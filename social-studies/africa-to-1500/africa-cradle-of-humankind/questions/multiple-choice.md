# Africa, cradle of humankind — Chad, Afar and Lucy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The oldest australopithecine was found in —
a) Hadar  b) northern Chad  c) Aramis
**Answer: b) northern Chad** — Found 2001; 7–6 million years old.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ardipithecus ramidus comes from —
a) Aramis in the Middle Awash, Afar  b) the Nile delta  c) South Africa
**Answer: a) Aramis in the Middle Awash, Afar** — Aged 4.4 million years, found 1994.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lucy was found at — in —.
a) Aramis, 1994  b) Hadar, 1974  c) Chad, 2001
**Answer: b) Hadar, 1974** — Johanson's 1974 Hadar find.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Lucy's age is about —
a) 40,000 years  b) 3.18 million years  c) 7 million years
**Answer: b) 3.18 million years** — Australopithecus afarensis, 3.18 million years.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Lucy's skeleton showed her species —
a) had big brains first  b) walked upright — bipedalism  c) lived in cities
**Answer: b) walked upright — bipedalism** — Upright walking before big brains.

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** Dinkinesh is —
a) a site in Chad  b) Lucy's Amharic name  c) a stone tool
**Answer: b) Lucy's Amharic name** — 'You are wonderful'.
