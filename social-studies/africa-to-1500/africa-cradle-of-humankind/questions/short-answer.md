# Africa, cradle of humankind — Chad, Afar and Lucy — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the three finds with place and date found: oldest australopithecine; Ardipithecus ramidus; Lucy.
**Answer:** Northern Chad, 2001 (7–6 million years old); Aramis, Middle Awash, 1994 (4.4 million years); Hadar, 1974 (3.18 million years).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does Lucy's upright walking matter so much?
**Answer:** Bipedalism is the hominid mark — her skeleton shows the human line walked upright millions of years before big brains.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two reasons East Africa matters for studying human origin, from this lesson.
**Answer:** Its Afar sites hold both Ardipithecus ramidus (4.4 million years) and Lucy (3.18 million years) — a long, dated fossil sequence in one region.
