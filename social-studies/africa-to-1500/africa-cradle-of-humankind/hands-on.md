# Hands-on — Africa, cradle of humankind — Chad, Afar and Lucy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plot the cradle
On a big outline map of Africa, pin the three find-cards (Chad 2001, Aramis 1994, Hadar 1974) in their places with their ages. The class then answers the book's question aloud: why does East Africa matter?

## Walk like Lucy
Watch a partner walk and list what upright walking frees the hands to do. Then list what Lucy's skeleton proves and what it cannot prove — no guessing beyond the bones.
