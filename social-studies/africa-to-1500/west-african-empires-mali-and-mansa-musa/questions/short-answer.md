# West African Empires — Mali and Mansa Musa — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things was Timbuktu famous for?
**Answer:** The gold trade and Islamic learning.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did Mali feed itself and earn, before its break-up?
**Answer:** Niani's rain grew millet, sorghum and rice; state farms worked by slaves grew grain; and gold was the other revenue.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three blows that broke Mali late in the 14th century.
**Answer:** Any three of: succession struggles; tributaries declaring independence; Mossi states striking its heart; Tuaregs taking Timbuktu.
