# West African Empires — Mali and Mansa Musa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mandinka leader who founded Mali in 1235 was ______ Keita.
**Answer: Sundiata**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mansa Musa's pilgrimage went through ______ to Mecca.
**Answer: Cairo**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ______ took Timbuktu as Mali broke up.
**Answer: Tuaregs**
**4. (Sub lesson 5)** The term for 'Mali's city of scholarship and trade' is ______.
**Answer: Timbuktu**
