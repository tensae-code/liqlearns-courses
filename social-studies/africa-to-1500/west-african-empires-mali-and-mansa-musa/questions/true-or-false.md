# West African Empires — Mali and Mansa Musa — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sundiata Keita was a Mandinka leader.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mansa Musa gave gold lavishly to the rulers of Egypt and Mecca on his pilgrimage.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Gold was Mali's only revenue.
**Answer: False** — gold was the OTHER revenue — state farms grew grain, and Niani's rains grew millet, sorghum and rice.
