# Hands-on — West African Empires — Mali and Mansa Musa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pilgrimage route walk
Lay a rope route across the room or yard: Niani, Cairo, Mecca, with Timbuktu beside the start. Walk it slowly carrying a small bag of gold-coloured stones, 'giving' one stone at Cairo and one at Mecca, and saying at each stop what the giving made people think.

## Two revenues table
Fill two baskets: one with grain names (millet, sorghum, rice — cards or real grains), one with gold-coloured stones. Mali's two revenues, in your hands — then explain to a companion which basket fed people and which basket bought fame.

## Timbuktu shelf (Grade 9)
Make a class 'Timbuktu shelf': each learner contributes one manuscript-card — a page copied or drawn on a subject worth learning (stars, law, poetry, medicine). Bind the cards into a book and state, in one line each, why a trading city would grow a library.
