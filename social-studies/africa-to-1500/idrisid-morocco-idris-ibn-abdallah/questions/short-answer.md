# The Islamic state in the age of the Idrisids in the Maghreb (173-375 AH) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Islamic state in the age of the Idrisids in the Maghreb (173-375 AH).
**Answer: after the Abbasid massacre of the Prophet's family at Fakhkh in 169 AH, Idris bin Abd Allah escapes hidden in Mecca, crosses Egypt with his servant Rashid and reaches Walili in the far Maghreb in 172 AH, where the Awraba Berbers pledge to him, his army takes Tlemcen in 173 AH, Harun al-Rashid has him poisoned in 177 AH, and his son Idris II builds Fez in 193 AH and the Qarawiyyin, and the state lasts three centuries (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* (Yemen), The Islamic state in the age of the Idrisids in the Maghreb (173-375 AH), printed p. 55.**
