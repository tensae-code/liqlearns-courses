# Video script — The Islamic state in the age of the Idrisids in the Maghreb (173-375 AH)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

after the Abbasid massacre of the Prophet's family at Fakhkh in 169 AH, Idris bin Abd Allah escapes hidden in Mecca, crosses Egypt with his servant Rashid and reaches Walili in the far Maghreb in 172 AH, where the Awraba Berbers pledge to him, his army takes Tlemcen in 173 AH, Harun al-Rashid has him poisoned in 177 AH, and his son Idris II builds Fez in 193 AH and the Qarawiyyin, and the state lasts three centuries.
