# Daily life in ancient Africa

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ancient Africans build homes and towns, live in families, eat from their land and wear their identity — daily life is where civilisation is lived. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Homes and towns

ancient Africans build homes and towns, and the town changes how people live together (UA2 Habitat et urbanisation dans l'Afrique ancienne)

In your mind's eye: an ancient African town — homes, a family at work, food being prepared and people in dress and ornaments. Name the four parts of daily life.

## Sub lesson 2 — The family

the family is the first community every child belongs to and learns from (UA2 La famille dans l'Afrique ancienne)

## Sub lesson 3 — Food

what ancient Africans grew, hunted and cooked decides how their societies thrive (UA2 L'alimentation dans l'Afrique ancienne)

## Sub lesson 4 — Dress, ornaments and art

dress, ornaments and art carry each civilisation's identity on the body (UA2 L'habillement, parures et arts dans l'Afrique ancienne)
