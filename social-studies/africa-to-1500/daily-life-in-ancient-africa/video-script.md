# Video script — Daily life in ancient Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Djibouti guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

ancient Africans build homes and towns, and the town changes how people live together

the family is the first community every child belongs to and learns from

what ancient Africans grew, hunted and cooked decides how their societies thrive

dress, ornaments and art carry each civilisation's identity on the body

Hold that story: Ancient Africans build homes and towns, live in families, eat from their land and wear their identity — daily life is where civilisation is lived.
