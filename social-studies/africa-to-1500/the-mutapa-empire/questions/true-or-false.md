# The Mutapa Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As Zimbabwe fades, the Mwene Mutapa empire rises in the Zambezi valley, ruling through gold trade and a sacred kingship until it too declines.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Mutapa Empire

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *História — Módulo 1 (PESD I, Programa do Ensino Secundário à Distância – 1º Ciclo)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Mutapa Empire

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** As Zimbabwe declines, the Mwenemutapa state rises north of its ruins in the mid-15th century, stretching from the Kalahari to the Indian Ocean on the Shona-Karanga gold trade.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Mwenemutapa state

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Módulo 6 de História — Os Estados em Moçambique e a Penetração Mercantil Estrangeira (PESD II, 2º Ciclo)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Mwenemutapa state
