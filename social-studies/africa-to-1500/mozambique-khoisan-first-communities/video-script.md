# Video script — Mozambique — Khoisan first communities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before the Bantu arrived, Khoisan hunter-gatherer communities lived across Mozambique, organised around kin, nature and spirit beliefs.
Mozambique's first people live by hunting and gathering, in small mobile communities long before farming or states.
