# Africa's Languages — the Families in Detail — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name five lands where Nilo-Saharan is spoken.
**Answer:** Any five of: Sudan, South Sudan, Chad, southern Egypt, northern Tanzania, Nigeria, Kenya, DR Congo, Ethiopia, Niger.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four Niger-Congo languages.
**Answer:** Any four of: Igbo, Fula, Shona, Zulu, Bantu, Yoruba.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is Khoisan famous among the families?
**Answer:** For its click sounds, made by drawing air in — and it is the oldest and smallest family, in Namibia and Botswana.
