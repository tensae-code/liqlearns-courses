# Africa's Languages — the Families in Detail — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list names branches of Nilo-Saharan?
a) Songhay, Koman, Chari-Nile  b) Semitic, Cushitic, Berber  c) Igbo, Fula, Shona  d) Khoikhoi, San, Bantu
**Answer: a) Songhay, Koman, Chari-Nile** — those are the family's named branches in the book.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which language belongs to Nilo-Saharan?
a) Yoruba  b) Gumuz  c) Zulu  d) Hausa
**Answer: b) Gumuz** — Gumuz is one of its named languages; Yoruba and Zulu are Niger-Congo, Hausa is Afro-Asiatic (Chadic).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Niger-Congo is, in the book's telling —
a) the oldest family  b) the smallest family  c) the world's largest by speakers  d) Madagascar's family
**Answer: c) the world's largest by speakers** — the book calls it the world's largest by speakers.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which language is Niger-Congo?
a) Kunama  b) Shona  c) Fur  d) Kadu
**Answer: b) Shona** — Shona is Niger-Congo; Kunama, Fur and Kadu are Nilo-Saharan.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Khoisan's click sounds are made by —
a) blowing air out  b) drawing air in  c) whistling  d) humming
**Answer: b) drawing air in** — the book says the clicks are made by drawing air in.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Khoisan is spoken mainly in —
a) Ethiopia and Kenya  b) Namibia and Botswana  c) Egypt and Sudan  d) Nigeria and Niger
**Answer: b) Namibia and Botswana** — the oldest and smallest family lives in Namibia and Botswana.
