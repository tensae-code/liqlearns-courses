# Africa's Languages — the Families in Detail — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Nilo-Saharan branches named in the book are Songhay, ______ and Chari-Nile.
**Answer: Koman**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ is a Niger-Congo language and the third most-spoken language in the book's ranking.
**Answer: Yoruba**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The name Khoisan joins two names: ______ and San.
**Answer: Khoikhoi**
