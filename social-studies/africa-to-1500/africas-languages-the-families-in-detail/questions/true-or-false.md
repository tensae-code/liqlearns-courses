# Africa's Languages — the Families in Detail — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kunama and Gumuz are Nilo-Saharan languages.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Niger-Congo covers west, central, east and southern Africa.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Khoisan is the largest of Africa's language families.
**Answer: False** — it is the oldest and smallest; Niger-Congo is the largest in the book's telling.
