# Video script — Africa's Languages — the Families in Detail

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three families, up close.

Nilo-Saharan — a wide belt of lands.
Songhay. Koman. Chari-Nile.
Berta, Fur, Kunama, Gumuz, Kadu.

Niger-Congo — the largest of all.
West, centre, east and south.
Igbo, Fula, Shona, Zulu, Yoruba.

Khoisan — the oldest. The smallest.
Khoikhoi plus San — that is its name.
And its words click — air drawn in.

Three families. Three stories.
