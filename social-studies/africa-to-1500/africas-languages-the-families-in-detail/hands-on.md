# Hands-on — Africa's Languages — the Families in Detail

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Click practice
With a companion, try the Khoisan way of making sound: a click made by drawing air in, like the 'tsk' of disappointment — but sharper, with the tongue. Practise three clicks in a row, then teach a younger child how it is done.

## Family lands map
On an outline map of Africa, shade the Nilo-Saharan belt, the great Niger-Congo lands, and the small Khoisan corner in the south-west. Label each with two of its languages.
