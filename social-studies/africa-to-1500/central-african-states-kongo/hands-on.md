# Hands-on — Central African States — Kongo

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Village to kingdom string
Lay out five small paper houses as villages along a drawn river. With one long string, circle them all into one kingdom and place a crown card at the centre house — Mbanza Kongo. Say the two dates aloud as you circle: spread by 1300, united by 1400.

## Title and place sort
Write ten cards: five place-names from this title's lessons (Mbanza Kongo, Koumbi Saleh, Timbuktu, Napata, Gao) and five titles or peoples (Manikongo, Bakongo, Soninke, Songhai, Kushite). Sort every card into PLACE or PEOPLE/TITLE and read the piles aloud.
