# Lesson plan — Central African States — Kongo

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Central African States — Kongo
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 6 — Africa to 1500 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** on the lower Zaire river, the Bakongo farming villages united about 1400 at Mbanza Kongo under a king titled Manikongo, in a fertile, craft-rich, trade-strong land.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the West African empires lessons before it in this title — the learner moves from the Niger bend to the Zaire river. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees
from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before
it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons
(ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **The Bakongo villages** — a fertile, craft-rich, trade-strong land on the lower Zaire; farming villages spread to the lower Zaire by about 1300; their faith a land spirit served by priests called manikabunga.
2. **Mbanza Kongo and the Manikongo** — the villages united about 1400 at Mbanza Kongo, political and cultural centre; the king titled Manikongo; by about 1580 the kingdom stretched from the Atlantic to the Kwango river.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: West African Empires — Songhai. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say where the Kongo kingdom stood and what its land gave its people.
2. Tell the Bakongo story: farming villages by about 1300, united about 1400 at Mbanza Kongo.
3. Name the king's title and the kingdom's stretch by about 1580.

## If the learner is stuck
- Reads Manikongo as a place → Manikongo is the KING's title; Mbanza Kongo is the place — keep the two names apart by their endings in the lesson.
- Dates the union at 1300 → about 1300 is when the villages had spread to the lower Zaire; the UNION at Mbanza Kongo is about 1400.
