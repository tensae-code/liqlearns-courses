# Video script — Central African States — Kongo

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Down the Zaire river, the land was rich —
rich soil, rich crafts, rich trade.

Bakongo villages farmed it.
Their priests, the manikabunga,
served the spirit of the land.

Then, about 1400, the villages became one.
Mbanza Kongo — the centre.
Manikongo — the king.

Ocean on one side, the Kwango on the other.
That was Kongo.
