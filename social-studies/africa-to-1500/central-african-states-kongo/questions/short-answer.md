# Central African States — Kongo — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What faith did the Bakongo villages hold, and who served it?
**Answer:** Faith in a land spirit, served by priests called manikabunga.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give Kongo's two key dates and what each marks.
**Answer:** About 1300 — the farming villages had spread to the lower Zaire. About 1400 — they united at Mbanza Kongo.
