# Central African States — Kongo — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bakongo land was fertile, craft-rich and trade-strong.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bakongo villages had spread to the lower Zaire by about 1300.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mbanza Kongo was only a market, not a political centre.
**Answer: False** — it was the kingdom's political and cultural centre.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** By about 1580 Kongo reached from the Atlantic to the Kwango river.
**Answer: True**
