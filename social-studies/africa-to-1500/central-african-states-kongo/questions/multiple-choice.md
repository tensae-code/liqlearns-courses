# Central African States — Kongo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Kongo kingdom stood on —
a) the Niger bend  b) the lower Zaire river  c) the upper Nile  d) the Senegal valley
**Answer: b) the lower Zaire river** — the lower Zaire.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bakongo villages' priests were called —
a) nomarchs  b) manikabunga  c) garads  d) qallu
**Answer: b) manikabunga** — manikabunga, serving the land spirit.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The villages united about 1400 at —
a) Gao  b) Mbanza Kongo  c) Niani  d) Koumbi Saleh
**Answer: b) Mbanza Kongo** — Mbanza Kongo, the political and cultural centre.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Kongo king's title was —
a) Askia  b) Manikongo  c) Pharaoh  d) Mansa
**Answer: b) Manikongo** — Manikongo.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** By about 1580 the kingdom stretched —
a) from the Nile to the Red Sea  b) from the Atlantic to the Kwango river  c) from Timbuktu to Jenne  d) along the Senegal only
**Answer: b) from the Atlantic to the Kwango river** — Atlantic to the Kwango river.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The Bantu movements began from — in the 1st millennium BC.
a) the Niger region  b) the Nile delta  c) the Cape
**Answer: a) the Niger region** — Slowly, from the Niger region.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The movers travelled as —
a) invading hordes  b) small communities  c) slave caravans
**Answer: b) small communities** — The book's own correction.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Kongo was founded on the lower — in the late 14th century.
a) Niger  b) Zaire (Congo) River  c) Zambezi
**Answer: b) Zaire (Congo) River** — By Bantu people.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** The Swahili coast is the book's discussion case for —
a) cultural diffusion  b) isolation  c) conquest
**Answer: a) cultural diffusion** — An African trading culture blended by Indian Ocean contact.
