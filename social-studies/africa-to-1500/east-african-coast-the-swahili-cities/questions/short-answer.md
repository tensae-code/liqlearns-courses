# East African Coast — the Swahili Cities — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five Swahili city-states.
**Answer:** Kilwa, Lamu, Malindi, Mombasa and Zanzibar.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What goods did the cities trade, and with which far lands?
**Answer:** Gold, ivory and slaves, traded with Persia, China and Indonesia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How did the city-states' world end?
**Answer:** The Portuguese seized the coast (1500–1600) and destroyed the trade routes and the civilisation.
