# East African Coast — the Swahili Cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Swahili' means —
a) sea traders  b) coast people  c) city builders  d) gold people
**Answer: b) coast people** — coast people.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Swahili language is today the national language of —
a) Kenya  b) Somalia  c) Tanzania  d) Mozambique
**Answer: c) Tanzania** — Tanzania — and East Africa's working language.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names the book's five city-states?
a) Kilwa, Lamu, Malindi, Mombasa, Zanzibar  b) Napata, Meroe, Aksum, Adulis, Zeila  c) Gao, Jenne, Timbuktu, Niani, Dakar  d) Dahlak, Ifat, Adal, Bale, Hadiya
**Answer: a) Kilwa, Lamu, Malindi, Mombasa, Zanzibar** — the five coastal city-states of 1200–1500.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The cities traded gold, ivory and slaves with —
a) Rome, Greece and Egypt  b) Persia, China and Indonesia  c) Morocco and Spain  d) India only
**Answer: b) Persia, China and Indonesia** — Persia, China and Indonesia.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Portuguese seized the coast in —
a) 1200–1300  b) 1400–1450  c) 1500–1600  d) 1629–1700
**Answer: c) 1500–1600** — 1500–1600, destroying the trade routes and the civilisation.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the Portuguese seizure destroy?
a) the Swahili language  b) the trade routes and the civilisation  c) the coast itself  d) Tanzania
**Answer: b) the trade routes and the civilisation** — the trade routes and the civilisation — the people and language remained.
