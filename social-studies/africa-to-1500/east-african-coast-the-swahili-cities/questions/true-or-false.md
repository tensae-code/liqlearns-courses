# East African Coast — the Swahili Cities — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Swahili are an Arab-Bantu mixed coastal people.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The city-states flourished from 1200 to 1500.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Portuguese seizure grew the cities' trade.
**Answer: False** — it destroyed the trade routes and the civilisation.
