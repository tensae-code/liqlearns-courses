# East African Coast — the Swahili Cities — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Swahili live along the coast from Somalia to ______.
**Answer: Mozambique**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** ______ is one of the five city-states, an island city off the coast.
**Answer: Zanzibar**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ______ seized the Swahili coast between 1500 and 1600.
**Answer: Portuguese**
