# Video script — East African Coast — the Swahili Cities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

They called themselves the coast people — Swahili.
Arab and Bantu together, from Somalia to Mozambique.
Their tongue became the working language of a whole coast —
and Tanzania's national language today.

Five cities rose: Kilwa. Lamu. Malindi.
Mombasa. Zanzibar.
Gold, ivory and slaves sailed out by dhow —
to Persia, to China, to Indonesia.

Then, after 1500, Portuguese sails came.
They seized the coast, broke the trade roads,
and the cities' world ended.
The coast people remained.
