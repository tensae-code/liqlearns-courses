# Hands-on — East African Coast — the Swahili Cities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five cities chain
Write the five city names on five cards and pin them in coast order on a wall string, north to south as best the lesson's map picture shows. Read the chain aloud three times — then remove one card and name the missing city.

## Dhow cargo load
Draw a dhow and load it with cargo cards: gold, ivory, slaves going out; mark its three destinations — Persia, China, Indonesia — as three harbours on the far side of the room. Walk the cargo to each harbour, naming the good and the land.
