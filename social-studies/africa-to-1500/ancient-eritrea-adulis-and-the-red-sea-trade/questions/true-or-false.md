# Ancient Eritrea — Adulis and the Red Sea trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Egyptian ships sailed to the Land of Punt long before Adulis was famous.
**Answer: True** — the books begin the trade story with Egypt's Punt expeditions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ancient Eritrea's towns lived by farming alone and never traded.
**Answer: False** — they were farming and craft towns grown rich on the Red Sea trade.
