# Ancient Eritrea — Adulis and the Red Sea trade — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Adulis was —
a) a highland fortress  b) a port on the Red Sea  c) a desert well
**Answer: b) a port on the Red Sea** — Adulis was Eritrea's great ancient port.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Goods carried OUT of Eritrea's ports included —
a) ivory, leopard skins and rhino horn  b) glass and wine only  c) books and paper
**Answer: a) ivory, leopard skins and rhino horn** — skins, ivory, rhino horn and cowries went out; cloth, glass and wine came in.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ruins that prove the ancient civilisation include —
a) stelae, temples and dams  b) railways and airports  c) castles of iron
**Answer: a) stelae, temples and dams** — stelae, temples and dams stand at Adulis, Kohaito and Metera.
