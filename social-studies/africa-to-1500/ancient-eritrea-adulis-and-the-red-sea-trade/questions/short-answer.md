# Ancient Eritrea — Adulis and the Red Sea trade — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two ancient Eritrean trading towns.
**Answer: Adulis and Kohaito (also accepted: Matara/Metera, Keskesse).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What goods left Eritrea's ports, and what came in?
**Answer: Out: leopard skins, ivory, rhino horn and cowries. In: goods from Arabia and India (any close wording).**
