# Hands-on — Ancient Eritrea — Adulis and the Red Sea trade

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw a trading ship and load it: draw three goods going out of Adulis and two goods coming in, and label each.

## Task 2
Find Adulis, Kohaito and Massawa on a map of the Red Sea coast and mark the sea route a Punt ship would sail.
