# Video script — Ancient Eritrea — Adulis and the Red Sea trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The ruins of Adulis, Kohaito and Metera — stelae, temples, dams — prove a trading civilisation on the Red Sea coast in ancient times.

Egyptian ships sail to the Land of Punt and Eritrea's ports grow rich on the sea trade — leopard skins, ivory, rhino horn and cowries out, goods from Arabia and India in.

Four thousand years of Red Sea history — Punt expeditions, the port of Adulis, the ruins of Kohaito, Matara and Keskesse, stelae, inscriptions and a carved sphinx.

Farming and craft towns grow along the Red Sea trade — Adulis, Kohaito, Matara, Keskesse — city-state communities that Queen Hatshepsut's Egypt already traded with as the Land of Punt.
