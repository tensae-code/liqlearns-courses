# Lesson plan — Ancient Eritrea — Adulis and the Red Sea trade

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Ancient Eritrea — Adulis and the Red Sea trade
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa to 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Long ago Eritrea's coast held rich trading towns — Adulis, Kohaito, Matara and Keskesse — where ships carried ivory, skins and horn out and goods from Arabia and India in, and the ruins still prove it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ancient Eritrea and its ruins** — the ruins of Adulis, Kohaito and Metera — stelae, temples, dams — prove a trading civilisation on the Red Sea coast in ancient times (Ancient Eritrea and its ruins)
2. **Ancient trade in the Red Sea** — Egyptian ships sail to the Land of Punt and Eritrea's ports grow rich on the sea trade — leopard skins, ivory, rhino horn and cowries out, goods from Arabia and India in (Ancient trade in the Red Sea)
3. **Four thousand years of Red Sea history** — four thousand years of Red Sea history — Punt expeditions, the port of Adulis, the ruins of Kohaito, Matara and Keskesse, stelae, inscriptions and a carved sphinx (Four thousand years of Red Sea history)
4. **Eritrean society in ancient times** — farming and craft towns grow along the Red Sea trade — Adulis, Kohaito, Matara, Keskesse — city-state communities that Queen Hatshepsut's Egypt already traded with as the Land of Punt (Eritrean society in ancient times)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
