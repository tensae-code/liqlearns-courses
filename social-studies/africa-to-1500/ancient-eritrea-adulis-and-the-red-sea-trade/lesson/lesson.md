# Ancient Eritrea — Adulis and the Red Sea trade

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: long ago Eritrea's coast held rich trading towns — Adulis, Kohaito, Matara and Keskesse — where ships carried ivory, skins and horn out and goods from Arabia and India in, and the ruins still prove it. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ancient Eritrea and its ruins

The ruins of Adulis, Kohaito and Metera — stelae, temples, dams — prove a trading civilisation on the Red Sea coast in ancient times.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ancient trade in the Red Sea

Egyptian ships sail to the Land of Punt and Eritrea's ports grow rich on the sea trade — leopard skins, ivory, rhino horn and cowries out, goods from Arabia and India in.

## Sub lesson 3 — Four thousand years of Red Sea history

Four thousand years of Red Sea history — Punt expeditions, the port of Adulis, the ruins of Kohaito, Matara and Keskesse, stelae, inscriptions and a carved sphinx.

## Sub lesson 4 — Eritrean society in ancient times

Farming and craft towns grow along the Red Sea trade — Adulis, Kohaito, Matara, Keskesse — city-state communities that Queen Hatshepsut's Egypt already traded with as the Land of Punt.
