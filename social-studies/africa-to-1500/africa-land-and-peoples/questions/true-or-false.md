# Africa — the land and its peoples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the lesson starts by telling African children that Africa is not the edge of the story but its beginning.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Africa and the Africans, and Africa's Location and Its Inhabitants

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف الخامس — المرحلة الابتدائية (History, Grade 5, Primary Stage)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Africa and the Africans, and Africa's Location and Its Inhabitants

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** one continent, hundreds of peoples, and every one of them with a past.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Inhabitants of the African Continent

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف الأول — المرحلة المتوسطة (History, First Intermediate Year = Grade 7)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Inhabitants of the African Continent

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Africa's past does not begin with Islam or with Europe; the chapter insists on the long before.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in An Introduction to the History of Africa before Islam

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف الأول — التعليم الثانوي (History, First Secondary Year = Grade 10)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about An Introduction to the History of Africa before Islam
