# Hands-on — Africa — the land and its peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Africa — the land and its peoples — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Africa and the Africans, and Africa's Location and Its Inhabitants, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
