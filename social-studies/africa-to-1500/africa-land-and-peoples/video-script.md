# Video script — Africa — the land and its peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the lesson starts by telling African children that Africa is not the edge of the story but its beginning.

one continent, hundreds of peoples, and every one of them with a past.

Africa's past does not begin with Islam or with Europe; the chapter insists on the long before.
