# Hands-on — Egypt from the Arab conquest to the independent states

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Egypt from the Arab conquest to the independent states — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Egypt from the Islamic conquest until the rise of the independent states, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
