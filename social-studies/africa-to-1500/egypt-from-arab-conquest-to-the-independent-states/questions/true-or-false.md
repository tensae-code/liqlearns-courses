# Egypt from the Arab conquest to the independent states — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For two centuries Egypt is a province governed from abroad — under the Umayyads Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, the best and justest of her governors, builds the Nilometer of the year 69 AH and makes Helwan his seat; under the Abbasids the governors change so fast, farmed out to Turkish amirs and their deputies, that Egypt learns to dream of ruling herself.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Egypt from the Islamic conquest until the rise of the independent states

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Egypt from the Islamic conquest until the rise of the independent states
