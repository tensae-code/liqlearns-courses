**Answer: For two centuries Egypt is a province governed from abroad — under the Umayyads Abd al-Aziz ibn Marwan, the best and justest of her governors, builds the Nilometer of the year 69 AH and makes Helwan his seat; under the Abbasids the governors change so fast, farmed out to Turkish amirs and their deputies, that Egypt learns to dream of ruling herself.
