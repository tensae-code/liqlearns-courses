# Video script — Peoples of East Africa — Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite migrations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

East Africa is settled side by side by peoples of different origins: Bantu farmers, Nilotes who came down the Nile, and Hamite and Nilo-Hamite pastoralists from Ethiopia — and people still move, for trade, work and safety.

Bantu farmers settle across East Africa, and Nilotes come down the Nile. Two great streams of people, with different languages and ways of life, arrive in the same region.

Hamites and Nilo-Hamite pastoralists come from Ethiopia, herding peoples alongside the farmers. East Africa's map of peoples is therefore a meeting, not a single origin.

People move for trade, work and safety, becoming immigrants in one country and emigrants from another, changing both places. The old migrations and today's movement are taught as one human habit.
