# Peoples of East Africa — Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite migrations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four peoples of East Africa in this lesson and where each came from, as the book tells it.
**Answer: Bantu farmers; Nilotes down the Nile; Hamites and Nilo-Hamite pastoralists from Ethiopia (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Give two reasons people move and say what changes.
**Answer: Trade, work or safety; the mover becomes immigrant in one place and emigrant from another, changing both (any close wording).**
