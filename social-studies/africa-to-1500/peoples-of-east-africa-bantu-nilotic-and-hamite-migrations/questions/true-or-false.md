# Peoples of East Africa — Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite migrations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** East Africa was settled by one people from one origin.
**Answer: False** — Bantu, Nilotes, Hamites and Nilo-Hamites settle side by side.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Moving changes both the place left and the place arrived in.
**Answer: True** — The book says movers change both places.
