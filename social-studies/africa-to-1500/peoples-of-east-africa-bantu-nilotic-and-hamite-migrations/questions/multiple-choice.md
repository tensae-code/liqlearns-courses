# Peoples of East Africa — Bantu, Nilotic and Hamite migrations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Nilotes, in this book, came —
a) Across the Atlantic  b) Down the Nile  c) From Ethiopia only
**Answer: b) Down the Nile** — Nilotes came down the Nile.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The pastoralist peoples named from Ethiopia are —
a) Hamites and Nilo-Hamites  b) Bantu only  c) Arabs only
**Answer: a) Hamites and Nilo-Hamites** — Hamites and Nilo-Hamite pastoralists from Ethiopia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** People move for —
a) Trade, work and safety  b) No reason  c) Only war
**Answer: a) Trade, work and safety** — Trade, work and safety are named.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A person leaving a country is an emigrant; arriving, they are an —
a) Immigrant  b) Emperor  c) Ancestor
**Answer: a) Immigrant** — Immigrant in the new country, emigrant from the old.
