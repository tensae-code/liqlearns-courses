# Zimbabwe — family life before the colony — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the pre-colonial family, motherhood and fatherhood were —
a) honoured roles  b) unimportant  c) invented by the colony
**Answer: a) honoured roles** — they carried their own honoured roles

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson presents the pre-colonial family as —
a) a world that worked by its own values  b) a world with no values  c) a copy of the colonial family
**Answer: a) a world that worked by its own values** — it restores the family as a world that worked by its own values

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson's subject is the family —
a) before the colony  b) after independence only  c) in modern cities only
**Answer: a) before the colony** — it is the pre-colonial family
