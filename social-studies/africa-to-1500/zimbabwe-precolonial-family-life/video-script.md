# Video script — Zimbabwe — family life before the colony

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Zimbabwe's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before the colony, motherhood and fatherhood carried their own honoured roles. The lesson restores the pre-colonial family as a world that worked — not a broken thing waiting to be fixed, but a family order with its own values, in which mother and father each held a place of honour.
