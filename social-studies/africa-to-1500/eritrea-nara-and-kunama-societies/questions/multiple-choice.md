# Eritrea — Nara and Kunama societies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Nara and Kunama live in —
a) the Gash-Setit lowlands  b) the Dahlak islands  c) the highlands of Debarwa
**Answer: a) the Gash-Setit lowlands** — the south-western Gash-Setit lowlands.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A Kunama person is known through —
a) the father line  b) the mother line  c) the village chief
**Answer: b) the mother line** — through the mother line.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Kunama creator is named —
a) Anna  b) Zeus  c) Amun
**Answer: a) Anna** — Anna, the creator.
