# Eritrea — Nara and Kunama societies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ancestor worship ordered life among the Nara and Kunama.
**Answer: True** — the book names kinship and ancestor worship as their order.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Kunama trace descent through the father only.
**Answer: False** — through the mother line.
