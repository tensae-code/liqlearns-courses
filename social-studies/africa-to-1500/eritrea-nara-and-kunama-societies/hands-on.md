# Hands-on — Eritrea — Nara and Kunama societies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

Draw a mother-line family tree: a grandmother at the top, her daughters, and their children — and say why the lines run as they do.

## Task 2
Map the Gash-Setit lowlands and mark where the Nara and Kunama live, beside the rivers.
