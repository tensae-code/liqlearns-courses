# Hands-on — The Baqt — the treaty between Nubia and the Muslims

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Baqt — the treaty between Nubia and the Muslims — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Treaties between the Muslim Arabs, Nubia and the Beja, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
