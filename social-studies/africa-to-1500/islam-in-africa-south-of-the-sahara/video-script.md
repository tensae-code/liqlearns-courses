# Video script — Islam in Africa south of the Sahara

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Islam crosses into Africa by Sinai, the Red Sea and the ocean roads — carried by preachers, honest traders, intermarriage and migration, until markets like Kano and Timbuktu become centres of the faith.
