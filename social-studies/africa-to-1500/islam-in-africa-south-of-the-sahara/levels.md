## Sub lesson 1 — The spread of Islam in Africa
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Islam crosses into Africa by Sinai, the Red Sea and the ocean roads — by preachers, by honest traders whose markets become centres like Kano and Timbuktu, by intermarriage and by migrations from the Companions' flight to Abyssinia onward; Amr ibn al-As takes Egypt, Barqa and Tripoli, Ibn Abi al-Sarh wins at Sbeitla, and Uqba and Musa complete the Maghreb
