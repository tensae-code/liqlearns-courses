# Hands-on — Islam in Africa south of the Sahara

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Islam in Africa south of the Sahara — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The spread of Islam in Africa, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
