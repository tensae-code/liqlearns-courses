# Islam in Africa south of the Sahara

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Islam crosses into Africa by Sinai, the Red Sea and the ocean roads — carried by preachers, honest traders, intermarriage and migration, until markets like Kano and Timbuktu become centres of the faith. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The spread of Islam in Africa

Islam crosses into Africa by Sinai, the Red Sea and the ocean roads — by preachers, by honest traders whose markets become centres like Kano and Timbuktu, by intermarriage and by migrations from the Companions' flight to Abyssinia onward; Amr ibn al-As takes Egypt, Barqa and Tripoli, Ibn Abi al-Sarh wins at Sbeitla, and Uqba and Musa complete the Maghreb.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
