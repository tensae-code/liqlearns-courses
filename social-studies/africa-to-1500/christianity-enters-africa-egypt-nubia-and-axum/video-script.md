# Video script — Christianity enters Africa — Egypt, Nubia and Axum

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

St Mark brings the faith to Egypt in the first century, trade carries it to Abyssinia where King Ezana makes it his state's religion, and Nubia's kingdoms receive it too.

a persecuted faith survives in the desert, then rides back down the Nile as the religion of kings.
