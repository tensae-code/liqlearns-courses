# Christianity enters Africa — Egypt, Nubia and Axum — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** St Mark brings the faith to Egypt in the first century, trade carries it to Abyssinia where King Ezana makes it his state's religion, and Nubia's kingdoms receive it too.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Christianity enters Africa

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ والتربية الوطنية — الصف الرابع (History and National Education, Grade 4)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Christianity enters Africa

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a persecuted faith survives in the desert, then rides back down the Nile as the religion of kings.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Entry of Christianity into Africa

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف الخامس — المرحلة الابتدائية (History, Grade 5, Primary Stage)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Entry of Christianity into Africa
