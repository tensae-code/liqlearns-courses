# Video script — The Wanga kingdom of western Kenya

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Around Lake Victoria in western Kenya stands the only kingdom Kenya had, named after its founder, Wanga.
When the British arrive in Kenya in 1883 they find one organised centralised state — the Wanga Kingdom of Mumia in Kakamega — and Kenya, unlike Uganda, barely recognises cultural leaders to this day.
