# Mamluk Egypt — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Mamluks?
a) cavalry
b) soldiers bought as slaves and trained into an elite that came to rule Egypt
c) sultan
**Answer: b) soldiers bought as slaves and trained into an elite that came to rule Egypt**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Cairo?
a) patronage
b) the Mamluk capital, rich on trade between two seas
c) hinge
**Answer: b) the Mamluk capital, rich on trade between two seas**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Mongols?
a) the invaders whose advance the Mamluks defeated
b) Crusaders
c) Levant
**Answer: a) the invaders whose advance the Mamluks defeated**
