# Mamluk Egypt — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The ruler of Mamluk Egypt and Syria — this is sultan.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Mamluks means the mounted elite arm in which Mamluks were trained.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: Rulers' funding of scholars, schools and building — this is patronage.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, Cairo means Egypt's standing role between the Indian Ocean and the Mediterranean.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The European invaders whose Levant presence the Mamluks ended — this is Crusaders.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Mongols means the eastern Mediterranean lands the Mamluks defended.
**Answer: False**
