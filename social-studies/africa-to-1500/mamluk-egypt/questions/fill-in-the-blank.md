# Mamluk Egypt — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the mounted elite arm in which Mamluks were trained' is ______.
**Answer: cavalry**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'Egypt's standing role between the Indian Ocean and the Mediterranean' is ______.
**Answer: hinge**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the eastern Mediterranean lands the Mamluks defended' is ______.
**Answer: Levant**
