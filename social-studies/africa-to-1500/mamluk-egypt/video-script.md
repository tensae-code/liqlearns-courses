# Video script — Mamluk Egypt

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Slave-soldiers who became sultans. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Mamluk Egypt was ruled by a remarkable caste: soldiers bought as slaves, trained as elite cavalry, freed into power — until the soldiers' own commanders took the throne itself. Watch the names appear one by one: Mamluks, sultan, cavalry. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Cairo — wealth and scholarship. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Under the Mamluks, Cairo grew rich on the trade between the Indian Ocean and the Mediterranean, and splendid with mosques, schools and hospitals. Watch the names appear one by one: Cairo, patronage, hinge. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Victors over Mongols and Crusaders. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Twice the Mamluks stopped invasions others could not: they defeated the Mongols, whose advance had seemed unstoppable, and they finished the Crusader presence in the Levant.

That double victory fixed their fame in the Islamic world — and kept Egypt independent while surrounding lands fell.. Watch the names appear one by one: Mongols, Crusaders, Levant. This is the part of the story to hold.
