# Mamluk Egypt — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Slave-soldiers who became sultans

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Mamluks** (soldiers bought as slaves and trained into an elite that came to rule Egypt).

## Sub lesson 2 — Cairo — wealth and scholarship

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Cairo** (the Mamluk capital, rich on trade between two seas).

## Sub lesson 3 — Victors over Mongols and Crusaders

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Mongols** (the invaders whose advance the Mamluks defeated).
