# Ancient Egypt — the Nile Kingdom — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A nome was —
a) a temple  b) an administrative province  c) a trading ship  d) a royal tomb
**Answer: b) an administrative province** — Egypt's Nile valley was divided into nomes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many nomes did Upper Egypt hold?
a) 20  b) 31  c) 22  d) 42
**Answer: c) 22** — 22 in Upper (southern) Egypt, 20 in Lower (northern).

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Menes united the nomes and founded the first dynasty about —
a) 1550 BC  b) 1640 BC  c) 750 BC  d) 3000 BC
**Answer: d) 3000 BC** — about 3000 BC.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Egypt had how many dynasties in all?
a) 3  b) 12  c) 31  d) 42
**Answer: c) 31** — 31 dynasties, some founded by invaders.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Hyksos invasion (about 1640 BC) brought Egypt —
a) the horse in war and bronze-working  b) the calendar  c) papyrus  d) the pyramid
**Answer: a) the horse in war and bronze-working** — the invasion broke the civilisation but brought the war-horse and bronze-working.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's skeleton, the Pharaoh's chief official — his prime minister — was the —
a) nomarch  b) vizier  c) scribe  d) Pharaoh
**Answer: b) vizier** — the vizier ran the government; the nomarch administered a nome (teacher guide — evidence).
