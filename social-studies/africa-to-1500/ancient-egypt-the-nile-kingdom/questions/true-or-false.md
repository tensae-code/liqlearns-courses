# Ancient Egypt — the Nile Kingdom — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Pharaoh was held divine, an earthly representative of the gods.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Most Egyptians were merchants and sailors.
**Answer: False** — society was mostly farmers, with scribes, merchants, craftsmen and slaves among them.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ahmose I expelled the Hyksos about 1550 BC.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide divides Egyptian history into three periods: Old, Middle and New Kingdoms.
**Answer: True** — teacher guide — evidence.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Civilian officials supported the pharaoh's rule.
**Answer: True** — Officials served the god-king.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 9 book dates Egypt's civilisation from the early 4th millennium BC.
**Answer: True** — Its opening dating.
**7. (Sub lesson 4)** True or false: Divine kingship administering the Nile valley — this is pharaonic government.
**Answer: True**
