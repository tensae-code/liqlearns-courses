# Ancient Egypt — the Nile Kingdom — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did most Egyptians do, and what did they grow and keep?
**Answer:** Most were farmers, growing wheat, barley, onions, flax and vegetables by Nile water, keeping cattle and goats, and fishing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is Egypt called 'the gift of the Nile'?
**Answer:** Because the Nile gave the land its life — irrigation water, and transport and communication (the irrigation point is the student book's farming picture; the 'gift' framing and the transport point are teacher guide — evidence).
