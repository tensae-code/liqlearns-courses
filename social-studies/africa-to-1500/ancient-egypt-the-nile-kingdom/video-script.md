# Video script — Ancient Egypt — the Nile Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One river. One long green land.
The Egyptians cut it into nomes —
twenty-two in the south, twenty in the north.

Then Menes came, about 3000 BC,
and joined them into one kingdom.

The Pharaoh stood at its head — a god on earth.
Commanders, tax men and priests served him.
And the people farmed the river's gift:
wheat and barley, cattle and fish.

Kingdoms rose — Old, Middle, New.
The Hyksos broke in — and Ahmose drove them out.
That is Egypt: the gift of the Nile.
