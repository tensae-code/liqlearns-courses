# Video script — Ancient Egypt — the Nile Kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One river. One long green land.
The Egyptians cut it into nomes —
twenty-two in the south, twenty in the north.

Then Menes came, about 3000 BC,
and joined them into one kingdom.

The Pharaoh stood at its head — a god on earth.
Commanders, tax men and priests served him.
And the people farmed the river's gift:
wheat and barley, cattle and fish.

Kingdoms rose — Old, Middle, New.
The Hyksos broke in — and Ahmose drove them out.
That is Egypt: the gift of the Nile.

---

Grade 9 story addition (Ethiopia Wave 4):

The map zooms to the Nile. Kingdoms stack like stones: Old, Middle, New.

A pharaoh-god on his throne; officials at their desks; a pyramid rising course by course.

And on papyrus, a scribe writes it all down — the writing that lets us read them still.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Egypt in depth — government, gods, writing and the calendar. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 telling deepens the Nile kingdom on every side: pharaonic government as divine kingship administering a river; religion woven through state and afterlife; hieroglyph writing carrying administration and eternity alike; pyramids as organised labour made eternal; and a calendar by which the flood, the sowing and the tax all kept time. The names to hold: hieroglyphs, pharaonic government.
