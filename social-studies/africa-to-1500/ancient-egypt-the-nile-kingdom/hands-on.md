# Hands-on — Ancient Egypt — the Nile Kingdom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Nome map
Draw the Nile on a long strip of paper. Cut the strip into nome pieces — 20 small pieces at the north end, 22 at the south — then join them back together and write Menes' name and date across the join.

## A farmer's day list
Write the day of an Egyptian farming family from the lesson's own list: which crops by the river, which animals, who fishes. Read it aloud in role to a companion, who must name the crop or animal at each step.
