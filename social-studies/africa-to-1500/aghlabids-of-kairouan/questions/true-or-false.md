# The Aghlabids of Kairouan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** an Abbasid governorship quietly becomes a kingdom, and its capital becomes one of Islam's great cities.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Aghlabid State (184–296 / 800–909): Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab holds Ifriqiya for Baghdad in name and rules it in fact from Kairoua

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف السادس — المرحلة الابتدائية (History, Grade 6, Primary Stage)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Aghlabid State (184–296 / 800–909): Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab holds Ifriqiya for Baghdad in name and rules it in fact from Kairoua
