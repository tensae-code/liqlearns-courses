# Hands-on — The Aghlabids of Kairouan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Aghlabids of Kairouan — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Aghlabid State (184–296 / 800–909): Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab holds Ifriqiya for Baghdad in name and rules it in fact from Kairoua, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
