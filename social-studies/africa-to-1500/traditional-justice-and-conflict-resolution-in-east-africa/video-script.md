# Video script — Traditional justice and conflict resolution in East Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two feuding clans cut a sheep and a goat in half and exchange the halves, then drink the bitter herb Oput together to "wash away bitterness" — justice as a shared drink, not a verdict.
