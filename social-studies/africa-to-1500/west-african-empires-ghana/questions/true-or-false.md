# West African Empires — Ghana — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ancient Ghana stood in today's south-east Mauritania and Mali.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ghana sat between the southern gold producers and the northern camel caravans.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ghana's rulers accepted Islam in the 11th century.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ghana fell because its gold ran out in a single year.
**Answer: False** — the book names three pressures: Mandinka pressure, resource degradation and Almoravid invasion.
