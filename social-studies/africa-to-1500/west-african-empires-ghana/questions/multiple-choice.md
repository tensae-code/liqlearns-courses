# West African Empires — Ghana — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ancient Ghana was founded by —
a) Mandinka chiefs  b) Soninke farmers  c) Phoenicians  d) Tuareg traders
**Answer: b) Soninke farmers** — the Soninke farmers founded it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ghana's kings grew rich by —
a) mining their own gold  b) taxing the gold trade at Koumbi Saleh  c) selling camels  d) farming the desert
**Answer: b) taxing the gold trade at Koumbi Saleh** — they taxed gold passing between south and north.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** By 1000 AD Ghana reached —
a) from the Nile to the coast  b) from the Senegal valley to the Niger's great bend  c) from Timbuktu to Gao only  d) across all of Africa
**Answer: b) from the Senegal valley to the Niger's great bend** — Senegal valley to the Niger's great bend.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** West Africa's first Muslims were —
a) the Almoravids  b) the Soninke traders  c) the Mandinka  d) Ghana's kings
**Answer: b) the Soninke traders** — the Soninke traders.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Koumbi Saleh was taken in 1235 by —
a) the Almoravids  b) Sundiata Keita  c) the Mandinka chief  d) Mansa Musa
**Answer: c) the Mandinka chief** — the Mandinka chief took the capital in 1235.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Ghana was founded by the —
a) Soninke  b) Songhai  c) Berbers
**Answer: a) Soninke** — The Soninke-founded empire.

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** At its height about 1000 AD, Ghana stood in modern —
a) Nigeria and Cameroon  b) Mauritania, Mali and Senegal  c) Morocco and Algeria
**Answer: b) Mauritania, Mali and Senegal** — The book's geography.

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Ghana grew great mainly by —
a) sea trade  b) controlling and taxing the Trans-Saharan trade  c) iron mining
**Answer: b) controlling and taxing the Trans-Saharan trade** — Trade, taxed — the main source of greatness.

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** A contributing factor named with the trade is —
a) the rise of Islam  b) Roman law  c) the camel's disappearance
**Answer: a) the rise of Islam** — Islam's rise contributed.
