# West African Empires — Ghana — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain Ghana's position in one sentence, and why it made the kings rich.
**Answer:** Ghana sat between the southern gold-producing peoples and the northern camel caravans, so all the gold trade passed through it and its kings could tax that trade from Koumbi Saleh.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three pressures that ended Ghana.
**Answer:** Mandinka pressure, resource degradation, and Almoravid invasion — ending with the Mandinka chief taking Koumbi Saleh in 1235.
