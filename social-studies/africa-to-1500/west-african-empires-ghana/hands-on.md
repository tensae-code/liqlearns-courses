# Hands-on — West African Empires — Ghana

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tax gate game
Set two chairs as a gate. One learner carries 'gold' stones from the south end of the room to the north; another carries 'salt' cloths back. The gate-keeper takes one stone in every five as the king's tax and keeps the tax pile visible — Ghana's wealth, made of passing trade.

## Three pressures mime
In threes, act Ghana's three ending pressures without words: a rival pressing (Mandinka), a land tiring (degradation), an army arriving (Almoravid). A watcher must name all three.
