# Video script — West African Empires — Ghana

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the
lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are
made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Between the forest and the desert stood Ghana.
Soninke farmers built it.
Gold came up from the south.
Camels came down from the north.
And at Koumbi Saleh, the king taxed it all.

By the year 1000, Ghana reached
from Senegal to the great bend of the Niger.

Then rivals pressed, the land tired,
invaders came — and in 1235
Koumbi Saleh fell. Ghana's day was done.
