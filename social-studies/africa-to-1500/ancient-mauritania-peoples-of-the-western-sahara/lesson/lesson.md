# Ancient Mauritania — peoples of the Western Sahara

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Farming and herding begin in the Neolithic (7000–2000 BC); as the Sahara dries, Garamantes, Bafour and Berbers migrate in — Sanhaja Berbers in the 3rd century AD settle the ponds and wadis, the Bafour hold Adrar from Azuggi behind trained dogs until the Almoravids come, Phoenician sailors trade down the Atlantic coast from 1500 BC, and Sudanic groups hold the south. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
