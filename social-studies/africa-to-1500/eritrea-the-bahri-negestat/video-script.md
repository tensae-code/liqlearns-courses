# Video script — Eritrea — the Bahri Negestat

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Eritrea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the medieval highlands the Bahri Negassi rules Medri Bahri from Debarwa while Gragn's wars of 1541 shake the whole region.

From the 1300s a 'Lord of the Sea' rises over the separate highland communities, ruling from Debarwa with a Council of Elders' approval while Abyssinian kings try to pull him under their control.

Districts grow out of groups of villages, but fighting among the local chiefs hollows out the Bahri Negassi's authority from inside.
