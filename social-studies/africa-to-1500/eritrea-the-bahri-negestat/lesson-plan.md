# Lesson plan — Eritrea — the Bahri Negestat

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Africa to 1500 · **Lesson:** Eritrea — the Bahri Negestat
**Place in the curriculum:** Africa to 1500 — built in the History expansion (Eritrea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From the 1300s a 'Lord of the Sea' — the Bahri Negassi — ruled the medieval Eritrean highlands from Debarwa with a Council of Elders' approval, while Abyssinian kings tried to pull him under their control — until fighting among the local chiefs hollowed his authority out from inside.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Bahri Negestat** — in the medieval highlands the Bahri Negassi rules Medri Bahri from Debarwa while Gragn's wars of 1541 shake the whole region (The Bahri Negestat)
2. **Rise of the Bahri Negestat** — from the 1300s a 'Lord of the Sea' rises over the separate highland communities, ruling from Debarwa with a Council of Elders' approval while Abyssinian kings try to pull him under their control (Rise of the Bahri Negestat)
3. **The Decline of the Bahri Negestat** — districts grow out of groups of villages, but fighting among the local chiefs hollows out the Bahri Negassi's authority from inside (The Decline of the Bahri Negestat)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
