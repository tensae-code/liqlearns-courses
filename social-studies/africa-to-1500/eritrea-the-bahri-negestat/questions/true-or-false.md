# Eritrea — the Bahri Negestat — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bahri Negassi needed the approval of a Council of Elders.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 6 book says he ruled with the council's approval.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Abyssinian kings never tried to control the Bahri Negassi.
**Answer: False** — they tried again and again to pull him under their control.
