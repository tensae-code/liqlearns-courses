# Eritrea — the Bahri Negestat — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bahri Negassi means —
a) Lord of the Sea  b) King of the Desert  c) Father of Rivers
**Answer: a) Lord of the Sea** — 'Lord of the Sea' — ruler of Medri Bahri, the land by the sea.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Bahri Negassi ruled from —
a) Massawa  b) Debarwa  c) Dahlak
**Answer: b) Debarwa** — from Debarwa, with a Council of Elders' approval.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Bahri Negestat declined because —
a) the sea dried up  b) local chiefs fought among themselves  c) Italy invaded
**Answer: b) local chiefs fought among themselves** — fighting among the local chiefs hollowed it out from inside.
