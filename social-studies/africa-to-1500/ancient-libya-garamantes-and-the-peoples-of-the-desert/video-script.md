# Video script — Ancient Libya — the Garamantes and the peoples of the desert

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Libya's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Libu and Rebu tribes face Egypt to the east, Greek settlers under Battus plant Cyrene, Barqa and Apollonia on the coast, and deep in Fezzan the Garamantes farm the Sahara and race four-horse chariots.
