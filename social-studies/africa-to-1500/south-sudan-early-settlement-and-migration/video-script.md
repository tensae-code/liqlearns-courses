# Video script — South Sudan — early settlement and migration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as South Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The peoples came from different places — Plain Nilotes from the Ethiopian Highlands, River-Lake Nilotes from the Kush kingdom in northern Sudan, Bantu and Sudanic groups from West Africa — and children name Dinka, Nuer, Shilluk, Acholi, Azande, Bari and many more.
