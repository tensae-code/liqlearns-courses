# Islam enters Sudan — Dongola and the Baqt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** once Egypt is opened, Arab Muslims reach Nubia; Old Dongola's church becomes a mosque as Islam settles among the Nubians by mixing and teaching.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Islam enters Sudan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ والتربية الوطنية — الصف الرابع (History and National Education, Grade 4)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Islam enters Sudan

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Islam comes to Sudan twice — first by treaty at the frontier, then slowly, family by family, with the migrating tribes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Islamic Conquest of the Land of Sudan and The Entry of the Arabs into Sudan

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف الخامس — المرحلة الابتدائية (History, Grade 5, Primary Stage)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Islamic Conquest of the Land of Sudan and The Entry of the Arabs into Sudan

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Sudan becomes Muslim not in a battle but over centuries of marriage, trade and teaching.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Islam in Sudan

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف الأول — التعليم الثانوي (History, First Secondary Year = Grade 10)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Islam in Sudan
