# Video script — Bantu expansion and settlement in Mozambique

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bantu farming peoples migrate from the Great Lakes region in the 2nd–3rd centuries and settle Mozambique, bringing crops, iron and new social organisation.
Farmers and herders arrive with the Bantu expansion and plant settled agricultural societies across Mozambique and southern Africa.
