# Bantu expansion and settlement in Mozambique

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Bantu farming peoples migrate from the Great Lakes region in the 2nd–3rd centuries and settle Mozambique, bringing crops, iron and new social organisation. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Bantu-speaking peoples settle Mozambique

Bantu farming peoples migrate from the Great Lakes region in the 2nd–3rd centuries and settle Mozambique, bringing crops, iron and new social organisation

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mozambican societies after the Bantu expansion

Farmers and herders arrive with the Bantu expansion and plant settled agricultural societies across Mozambique and southern Africa

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
