# Bantu expansion and settlement in Mozambique — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bantu farming peoples migrate from the Great Lakes region in the 2nd–3rd centuries and settle Mozambique, bringing crops, iron and new social organisation.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Bantu-speaking peoples settle Mozambique
