## Sub lesson 1 — Bulubulu — Fiji's traditional way of settling conflict

Fiji Grade 9 Social Science Year 9 (printed p. 52), teaches: Traditional societies settle conflict through the whole community, not just the parties — the iTaukei Bulubulu, presenting yaqona and food with a verbal apology whose acceptance ends hostilities (with Māori tradition, Vanuatu's Council of Chiefs and Kiribati's unimane as Pacific comparisons). ## Sub lesson 2 — Fiji's way — the books' own teaching way

Fiji's own way (Grade 9): society is analysed through Fiji's own structures — the iTaukei hierarchy of Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau and Tauvanua with its ascribed chiefly status — and conflict is settled the iTaukei way, by Bulubulu.
