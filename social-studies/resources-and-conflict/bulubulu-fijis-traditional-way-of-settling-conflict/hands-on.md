Work through this part with your hands: Traditional societies settle conflict through the whole community, not just the parties — the iTaukei Bulubulu, presenting yaqona and food with a verbal apology whose acceptance ends hostilities (with Māori tradition, Vanuatu's Council of Chiefs and Kiribati's unimane as Pacific comparisons).
