# Hands-on — Conflict and Resolution Around the World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Conflicts in international relations
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The conflicts that arise out of Papua New Guinea's bilateral and multilateral relationships and how they are investigated and understood. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Conflict between people of different democratic nations
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Cases of conflict between people of different democratic nations, evaluated for their purposes, impacts and outcomes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Conflicts and resolutions
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Comparing types of national and global conflict — their origins, nature and approaches to resolution, and their consequences for people, society and environment — studied through case studies including the Bougainville conflict. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
