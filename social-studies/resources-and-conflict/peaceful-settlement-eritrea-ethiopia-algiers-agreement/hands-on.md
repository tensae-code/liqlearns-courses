# Hands-on — Peaceful Settlement Eritrea Ethiopia Algiers Agreement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: settling a war between states by agreement and adjudication — the Algiers peace agreement of 12 December 2000, UN peacekeepers in the Temporary Security Zone, and a Boundary Commission whose final, binding decision both sides accepted in advance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
