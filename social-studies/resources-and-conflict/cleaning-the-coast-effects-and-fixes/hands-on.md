# Hands-on — Cleaning the coast — effects and fixes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beach clean-up plan (paper)
Plan a clean-up for a pictured or known beach: teams, bags, safety, where the collected waste goes — then add the harder paragraph: which SOURCE your clean-up does not stop, and what would stop it.

## Treatment-plant sketch
Draw a simple flow: town sewage → treatment plant → clean outflow to the sea, beside the 'before' flow straight to the bay. Label what the plant removes and why the guide calls unsatisfactory disposal the heart of the problem.
