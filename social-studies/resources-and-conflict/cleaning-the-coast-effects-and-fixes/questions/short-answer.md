# Cleaning the coast — effects and fixes — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three-step order for studying any polluted coast.
**Answer:** Sources first, then their effects on life and livelihoods, then fixes matched to the causes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why fix the biggest source first?
**Answer:** Because the most frequent cause — sewage and industrial discharge through unsatisfactory disposal — delivers the largest share of the pollution; fixing it removes the most harm for the effort.
