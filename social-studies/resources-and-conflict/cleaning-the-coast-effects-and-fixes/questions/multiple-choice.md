# Cleaning the coast — effects and fixes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pollution's effects fall FIRST on —
a) marine life, then the communities who live from the sea  b) only tourists  c) mountains  d) desert farms
**Answer: a) marine life, then the communities who live from the sea** — the section's order of harm.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's planet-scale reason ocean pollution matters: oceans are the source of —
a) all rivers  b) the earth's surface moisture and of fish and other marine life  c) all rainfall only inland  d) salt only
**Answer: b) the earth's surface moisture and of fish and other marine life** — Activity 7.3 key (teacher guide — evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The fix matched to the most frequent cause is —
a) more fishing  b) treatment and satisfactory disposal facilities before water reaches the coast  c) closing beaches  d) painting ships
**Answer: b) treatment and satisfactory disposal facilities before water reaches the coast** — unsatisfactory disposal is the named heart of the cause.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A fix that names no cause is —
a) the best fix  b) a wish, not a solution  c) a case study  d) a filter
**Answer: b) a wish, not a solution** — every fix must match a named cause.
