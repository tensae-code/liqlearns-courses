# Cleaning the coast — effects and fixes — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The case-study discipline is: sources, then effects, then fixes.
**Answer: True** — the section's working order.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ocean pollution harms nothing beyond the beach itself.
**Answer: False** — oceans supply the Earth's surface moisture and marine food.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Protecting mangroves is part of cleaning the coast.
**Answer: True** — they are the coast's natural filters.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A beach clean-up alone stops coastal pollution at its sources.
**Answer: False** — it removes results; sources need treatment, control and protection fixes.
