# Video script — Why resource conflicts start

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three drivers start conflicts over renewable resources: growing scarcity, poor governance with unclear rights, and transboundary pressure on shared resources. First, scarcity and governance: The first driver is growing scarcity and degradation: as renewable resources diminish, competition over what remains grows sharper. Then, transboundary pressure — and the hopeful record: The third driver crosses borders. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
