# Why resource conflicts start — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three drivers of conflict over renewable resources.
**Answer:** Growing scarcity/degradation and competition; poor governance of resources and environment; and transboundary dynamics.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two governance failures that start disputes.
**Answer:** Any two of: unclear rights and rules; groups with no recognised rights; overlapping institutional mandates.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the record since 1948 show about shared rivers?
**Answer:** Cooperative interactions outnumber conflictive ones more than two to one, with only 37 acute water conflicts.
