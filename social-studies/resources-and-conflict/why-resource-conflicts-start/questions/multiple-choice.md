# Why resource conflicts start — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first driver is…
a) growing scarcity and degradation of renewables  b) too much rain  c) falling population everywhere
**Answer: a) growing scarcity and degradation of renewables**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Poor governance includes…
a) clear rights for all  b) unclear rights and rules, unrecognised groups, overlapping mandates  c) strong courts
**Answer: b) unclear rights and rules, unrecognised groups, overlapping mandates**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Scarcity sharpens…
a) competition over diminishing resources  b) cooperation only  c) rainfall
**Answer: a) competition over diminishing resources**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The three dams pictured are…
a) GERD, Aswan, Sambangalou  b) Kariba, Hoover, Three Gorges  c) none are named
**Answer: a) GERD, Aswan, Sambangalou**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Since 1948, cooperative riparian interactions outnumber conflictive ones by…
a) less than one to one  b) more than two to one  c) ten to one
**Answer: b) more than two to one**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Acute water conflicts since 1948 number…
a) 37  b) 370  c) 3,700
**Answer: a) 37**
