# Video script — Peace Dialogues Ending Armed Conflict

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 8 CS_Grado8, printed pp. 178–183, teaches: peace dialogues as a society's negotiated answer to armed conflict — pupils frame questions and hypotheses about the political, economic and social facts behind Colombia's conflict and its peace processes.

Colombia's own way: the whole rights course is anchored in Colombia's own conflict and its own answer — peace dialogues and the 1991 Constitution, which pupils read as a deliberate act of inclusion of the ethnic groups, peoples and ideologies the country had excluded.
