# Hands-on — Peace Dialogues Ending Armed Conflict

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Módulo 5 Guía 14 Los diálogos de paz: una respuesta al conflicto
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 8 CS_Grado8, printed pp. 178–183, teaches: peace dialogues as a society's negotiated answer to armed conflict — pupils frame questions and hypotheses about the political, economic and social facts behind Colombia's conflict and its peace processes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 24) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: the whole rights course is anchored in Colombia's own conflict and its own answer — peace dialogues and the 1991 Constitution, which pupils read as a deliberate act of inclusion of the ethnic groups, peoples and ideologies the country had excluded. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
