# A Culture of Peace — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: A culture of peace — communication, empathy, tolerance, respect and peaceful conflict-solving practised in the family, at school (including facing bullying) and in the community — as the way discrimination and racism are prevented.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: Deciding against competition and hyper-individualism — the principles and values of the culture of peace, and decision-making through critical thinking and ethical judgement that weighs alternatives and their implications for the whole community.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: Peace as a value and a capacity — interpersonal, social and political conflicts distinguished, and participation strategies for environments built on a culture of peace (first placed from Grade 7 in this wave).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Mexico's student books, named in lesson/sources.md.**
