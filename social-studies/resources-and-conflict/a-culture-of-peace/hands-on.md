# Hands-on — A Culture of Peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A Culture of Peace in Family, School and Community (Grade 7)
Work through this part with your hands: a culture of peace — communication, empathy, tolerance, respect and peaceful conflict-solving practised in the family, at school (including facing bullying) and in the community — as the way discrimination and racism are prevented. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Deciding in a Culture of Peace (Grade 8)
Work through this part with your hands: deciding against competition and hyper-individualism — the principles and values of the culture of peace, and decision-making through critical thinking and ethical judgement that weighs alternatives and their implications for the whole community. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Conflict and the Culture of Peace (Grade 9)
Work through this part with your hands: peace as a value and a capacity — interpersonal, social and political conflicts distinguished, and participation strategies for environments built on a culture of peace (first placed from Grade 7 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
