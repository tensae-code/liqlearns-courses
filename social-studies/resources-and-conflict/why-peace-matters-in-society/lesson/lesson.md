# Why Peace Matters in Society

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: living in peace while playing and learning — not being selfish, playing without discrimination, sharing, forgiving each other — as Rwanda's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Culture of Peace

Rwanda P2 SB Kiny, printed p. 50, teaches: living in peace while playing and learning — not being selfish, playing without discrimination, sharing, forgiving each other. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Importance of peace in our society

Rwanda Grade 5 P5 SB, printed p. 21, teaches: peace as the condition that lets a community develop, stay secure and live in unity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rwanda's way — the books' own teaching way

Rwanda's own way (Primary Two — Rwanda P2 SB Kiny): P2 civics starts with who you belong to (small family, wide family, the exact Kinyarwanda name for every relation), then the two smallest rungs of the state — the village and the cell, their elected committees and what umuganda owes them — and the behaviour of peace: playing without discrimination, listening without shouting, and the rights of children and of people with disabilities

Rwanda's own way (Primary Five — Rwanda Grade 5 P5 SB): the province is the child's civic world — complementarity (everyone's work fitting together) is the organising value, and national symbols, the 2003/2015 constitution and province leadership are taught as Rwanda's own frame inside it This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
