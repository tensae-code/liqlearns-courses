# Natural Resources — First and Second Needs — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Unpolluted air with enough oxygen is a first-order need.**
**Answer: True** — it is listed among the first-order needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A settled daily climate is a second-order need.**
**Answer: False** — a settled daily climate is first order.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Second-order needs are luxuries that can be dropped without harm.**
**Answer: False** — they secure the first order at the wanted amount and quality.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The means of producing, changing and consuming goods is a second-order need.**
**Answer: True** — it is the book's third second-order item.
