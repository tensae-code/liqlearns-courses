# Natural Resources — First and Second Needs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which is a first-order need?**
a) Science-and-technology experience  b) Clean water with needed minerals  c) Minerals as a secured supply  d) The means of producing goods
**Answer: b) Clean water with needed minerals** — clean water is first order; the others are second order.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Resources underwrite, besides life's necessities, a society's —**
a) Entertainment only  b) Security capacity — self-defence, sovereignty, law enforcement, knowledge and invention  c) Foreign trade only  d) Sports
**Answer: b) Security capacity — self-defence, sovereignty, law enforcement, knowledge and invention** — the book names security capacity in those terms.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Second-order needs are the needs that —**
a) Are luxuries nobody requires  b) Secure the first at the wanted amount and quality  c) Come after food in time of day  d) Are met only in cities
**Answer: b) Secure the first at the wanted amount and quality** — securing the first at wanted amount and quality is their definition.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which list is all second-order needs?**
a) Food, water, air  b) Minerals and energy sources, science-and-technology experience, means of producing goods  c) Clothes, shelter, cooking fire  d) Climate, oxygen, disease-fighting food
**Answer: b) Minerals and energy sources, science-and-technology experience, means of producing goods** — that list is the book's second order.
