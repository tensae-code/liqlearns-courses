# Natural Resources — First and Second Needs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List four first-order needs.**
**Answer:** Any four of: energy-giving, building and disease-fighting foods; clean water with needed minerals; unpolluted air with enough oxygen; a settled daily climate; materials for clothes and shelter; energy for cooking and heating.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Trace one chain: a first-order need and the second-order need securing it.**
**Answer:** Example: clean water (first) secured by pipes, pumps and treatment knowledge — minerals, energy and science-and-technology experience (second). Any correct traced chain counts.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What security capacities do resources underwrite?**
**Answer:** Self-defence and sovereignty, law enforcement, and knowledge and invention.
