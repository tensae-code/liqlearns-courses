# Video script — Natural Resources — First and Second Needs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This morning you ate. You drank. You breathed. You dressed.
Food. Water. Air. Clothes. Shelter. Fire.
Those are first-order needs. Life needs them today.
Behind them stand other needs.
Minerals. Energy. Knowledge. Tools. Ways of making.
Second-order needs — they secure the first,
at the amount you want, at the quality you want.
No second order? The first order fails.
Now sort your own morning.
