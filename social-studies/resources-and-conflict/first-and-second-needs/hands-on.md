# Hands-on — Natural Resources — First and Second Needs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Morning audit
Tomorrow morning, list every need you meet before school — food, water, air, clothes, shelter, cooking energy — and mark each F (first) or S (the second-order thing that secured it).

## Water chain walk
Follow your drinking water backwards: cup, container, tap or spring, and every tool and piece of knowledge along the way. Each step back is a second-order need at work.
