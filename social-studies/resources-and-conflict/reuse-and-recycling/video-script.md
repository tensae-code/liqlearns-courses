# Video script — Reuse And Recycling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan's book, the *Jordan G2 SS T2* (printed p. 75), teaches: Unit 5 Lesson 4: Reuse and Recycling (إعادة الاستعمال والتدوير) — reusing bottles and paper. Jordan's book, the *Jordan G9 ES T2* (printed p. 41), teaches: Unit 4 Lesson 2: Disposing of Solid Waste (التخلص من النفايات الصلبة). Jordan's book, the *Jordan G9 ES T2 Activity Book* (printed p. 20), teaches: Unit 4 Enrichment: Paper Recycling experiment (تدوير الورق).

Jordan's own way (Jordan G2 SS T2): Term 2 teaches change over time (transport, housing, writing) and the child's economy (production, consumption, recycling, Jordanian-made goods), closing with the blue planet, Earth's surface shapes and a first map of Jordan.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G9 ES T2): Term 2 runs solar system, then solid waste — Jordan generates 2.7 million tons a year — then the atmosphere and its heating.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G9 ES T2 Activity Book): the activity book makes pupils model the solar system, take apart household waste, design a sanitary landfill and recycle paper.
