# Hands-on — The Makkah Charter 2019

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Makkah Charter (2019)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Charter studied as a document — its concept, aims, foundations and principles; the importance of civilisational dialogue, peaceful coexistence and shared living among the peoples of the world in the founding father's thought and the wise leadership's; and the link between good citizenship (المواطنة الصالحة) and holding to virtuous values and principles Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
