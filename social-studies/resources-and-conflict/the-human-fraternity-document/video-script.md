# Video script — The Human Fraternity Document

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G9 teacher guide, volume 1, printed p. 17, teaches: why the Emirates hosted the world Human Fraternity meeting in Abu Dhabi, in the founding father's words on tolerance, peaceful coexistence and rejecting violence and extremism; what the Document for Human Fraternity is and its main ideas; the values the state takes from it; and its likeness to the Medina Charter of the first year of the Hijra — a founding text of living together

United Arab Emirates' own way: the ninth-grade year opens with the state itself — its constitution first, then its place among peoples: Arab solidarity, and the Human Fraternity Document signed in its own capital, studied analytically and compared with the Prophet's Medina Charter.
