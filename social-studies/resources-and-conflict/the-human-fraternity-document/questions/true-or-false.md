# The Human Fraternity Document — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: why the Emirates hosted the world Human Fraternity meeting in Abu Dhabi, in the founding father's words on tolerance, peaceful coexistence and rejecting violence and extremism; what the Document for Human Fraternity is and its main ideas; the values the state takes from it; and its likeness to the Medina Charter of the first year of the Hijra — a founding text of living together
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: the ninth-grade year opens with the state itself — its constitution first, then its place among peoples: Arab solidarity, and the Human Fraternity Document signed in its own capital, studied analytically and compared with the Prophet's Medina Charter.
