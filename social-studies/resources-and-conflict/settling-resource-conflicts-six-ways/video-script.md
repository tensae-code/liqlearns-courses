# Video script — Settling resource conflicts — six ways

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A conflict shows itself from hidden grievance and distrust, through rule-breaking, to sabotage and violence.

Avoidance steps away; coercion forces an end; negotiation talks it out between the parties; mediation brings a third party who helps but cannot decide; arbitration brings a third party who decides; adjudication puts the decision to a judge..

The unit closes with a field project: study how your own area manages its land, water and forest resources, and how it resolves its disputes.
