# Hands-on — Settling resource conflicts — six ways

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Say it with things at home
Gather three real things from your home or street that show this lesson — for example something it names, measures or describes. Lay them out, and explain each one to a partner using the lesson's own words and figures. Your partner asks one hard question; answer from the lesson.

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main picture from memory — elders mediating under a tree beside a courtroom — the informal and formal ends of the ladder — and label every part. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare with the picture there, and fix every label you missed.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
