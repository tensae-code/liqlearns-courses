# Settling resource conflicts — six ways — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Order the six ways from least to most deciding power of outsiders.
**Answer:** Avoidance, coercion, negotiation, mediation, arbitration, adjudication.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do mediation and arbitration differ?
**Answer:** A mediator helps but cannot decide; an arbitrator decides.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must every fieldwork group carry, per the guide's safety code?
**Answer:** A first-aid kit (the rucksack also holds compass, whistle, torch and extra food).
