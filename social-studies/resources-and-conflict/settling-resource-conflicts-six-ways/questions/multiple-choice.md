# Settling resource conflicts — six ways — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A conflict's mildest visible sign in the lesson is —
a) hidden grievance and distrust  b) sabotage  c) violence
**Answer: a) hidden grievance and distrust**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Actors in resource conflicts run from households to —
a) multinationals and NGOs  b) rivers  c) mountains
**Answer: a) multinationals and NGOs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In mediation, the third party —
a) helps but cannot decide  b) decides the outcome  c) is a judge
**Answer: a) helps but cannot decide**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In adjudication, the decision is made by —
a) a judge  b) the stronger party  c) nobody
**Answer: a) a judge**
