# Ethiopia’s green-economy plan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The plan’s emissions target is —
a) a 50% increase allowed  b) emissions doubled by 2025  c) no net increase over 2010
**Answer: c) no net increase over 2010**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The deadline printed in the book is —
a) 2010  b) 2025  c) 2030
**Answer: b) 2025**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A carrying work of the plan is —
a) climate-smart agriculture  b) importing more fuel  c) clearing hillsides
**Answer: a) climate-smart agriculture**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The other carrying work named is —
a) road building only  b) mining expansion  c) the great planting movements
**Answer: c) the great planting movements**
