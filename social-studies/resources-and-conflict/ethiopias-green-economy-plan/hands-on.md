# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s green-economy plan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Target line
Draw the target line as a finish tape with the three target words on it. Under it, list the sectors that must each be measured against it.

## Plan audit
With a partner, audit one real local effort — a planting day, a farm change — against the plan: which target word does it serve, and how would you know it worked?
