# Ethiopia’s green-economy plan

## Do it first

A vision becomes a plan when it names its road. Read Ethiopia’s green-economy plan as a plan: target, deadline, and the work that carries it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Target and deadline

The plan as printed sets the target plainly: middle-income status, climate resilience, and no net increase in emissions over the 2010 level, by 2025. Every part of the plan — energy, farming, forestry, industry — is measured against that single target line.

[Picture 21058 — a climate-smart farm — trees, crops and livestock integrated]

## Sub lesson 2 — The carrying work

The plan is carried by named work: climate-smart agriculture on the farms, and the great planting movements on the hillsides and in the towns. The glossary of the book itself defines the plan’s key terms — the plan and its dictionary arrive together.
