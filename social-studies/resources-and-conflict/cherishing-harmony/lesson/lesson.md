## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for.
