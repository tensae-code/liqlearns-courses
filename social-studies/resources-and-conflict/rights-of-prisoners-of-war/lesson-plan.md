# Lesson plan — Rights of Prisoners of War

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Resources and Conflict · **Lesson:** Rights of Prisoners of War
**Place in the curriculum:** Resources and Conflict (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Palestine, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Fixed rules protect prisoners of war: they may not be pursued for taking part in the fighting, and their detention is not a punishment but only to stop their further part in the war, so they must be released and returned to their homelands as soon as the war ends; they may be tried only if they commit grave crimes; they must be treated humanely in all circumstances, protected from killing, torture and every act that degrades human dignity; they must be given a suitable place, food, clothing, cleanliness and medical care; civilian detainees must be protected and not held under house arrest or detained for security reasons, and released when the reason ends; and prisoners must be allowed to communicate with their families until their release — as Palestine's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
