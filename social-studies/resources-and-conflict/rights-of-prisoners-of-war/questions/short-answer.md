# Rights of Prisoners of War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Rights of Prisoners of War — fixed rules protect prisoners of war: they may not be pursued for taking part in the fighting, and their detention is not a punishment but only to stop their further part in the war, so they must be released and returned to their homelands as soon as the war ends; they may be tried only if they commit grave crimes; they must be treated humanely in all circumstances, protected from killing, torture and every act that degrades human dignity; they must be given a suitable place, food, clothing, cleanliness and medical care; civilian detainees must be protected and not held under house arrest or detained for security reasons, and released when the reason ends; and prisoners must be allowed to communicate with their families until their release (any close wording).**
