# Rights of Prisoners of War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: fixed rules protect prisoners of war: they may not be pursued for taking part in the fighting, and their detention is not a punishment but only to stop their further part in the war, so they must be released and returned to their homelands as soon as the war ends; they may be tried only if they commit grave crimes; they must be treated humanely in all circumstances, protected from killing, torture and every act that degrades human dignity; they must be given a suitable place, food, clothing, cleanliness and medical care; civilian detainees must be protected and not held under house arrest or detained for security reasons, and released when the reason ends; and prisoners must be allowed to communicate with their families until their release.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Palestine's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
