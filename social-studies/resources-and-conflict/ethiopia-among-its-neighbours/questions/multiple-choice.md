# Ethiopia among its neighbours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia’s neighbour to the south is —
a) Eritrea  b) Djibouti  c) Kenya
**Answer: c) Kenya**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** To the north-east lies —
a) Sudan  b) Djibouti  c) Somalia
**Answer: b) Djibouti**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** For a landlocked country, trade must —
a) cross neighbours to reach ports  b) stop entirely  c) go by air only
**Answer: a) cross neighbours to reach ports**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Standing at the Horn gives Ethiopia —
a) a long coastline  b) no neighbours to deal with  c) strategic weight near the Red Sea routes
**Answer: c) strategic weight near the Red Sea routes**
