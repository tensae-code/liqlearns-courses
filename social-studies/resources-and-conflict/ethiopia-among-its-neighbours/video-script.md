# Video script — Ethiopia among its neighbours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six neighbours ring Ethiopia.
Eritrea. Djibouti. Somalia. Kenya. South Sudan. Sudan.
No coast of its own — but the Red Sea near.
Its neighbours’ ports are its coastline by agreement.
