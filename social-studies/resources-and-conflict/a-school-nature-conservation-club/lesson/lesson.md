## Sub lesson 2 — Bangladesh's way — the book's own teaching way

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 6): an inquiry-based combined History and Social Science course built around a class teacher character and group investigations; Geography is taught through the landform of Bengal itself — rivers, silt and hills explaining settlement, ancient states, ports and even the 1971 war — plus hands-on map-making (home-to-school route maps, a world-tour board game) and a pupil-run nature conservation club. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
