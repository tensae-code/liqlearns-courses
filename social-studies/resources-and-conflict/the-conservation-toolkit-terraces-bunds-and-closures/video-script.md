# Video script — The conservation toolkit — terraces, bunds and closures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Each measure is defined: afforestation, reforestation, area closure, bench terracing, check dams, mulching, shelterbelts, diversion canals, trenches, drainage ditches, legume rotation, animal manure, intercropping, careful irrigation and green manure..

The hard structures slow water and hold soil: terraces step a slope, bunds follow its contour, check dams catch a gully's flow, trenches and ditches lead water away safely.

The book illustrates the toolkit with Tigray's stone bunds, China's loess terraces, and a restored gully at Awzet in Farta — proof that degraded land can be brought back..
