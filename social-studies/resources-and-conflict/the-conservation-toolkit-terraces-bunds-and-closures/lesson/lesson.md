# The conservation toolkit — terraces, bunds and closures

## Sub lesson 1 — The toolkit named

Each measure is defined: afforestation, reforestation, area closure, bench terracing, check dams, mulching, shelterbelts, diversion canals, trenches, drainage ditches, legume rotation, animal manure, intercropping, careful irrigation and green manure.

## Sub lesson 2 — What the tools do

The hard structures slow water and hold soil: terraces step a slope, bunds follow its contour, check dams catch a gully's flow, trenches and ditches lead water away safely. The living tools — mulching, rotation, intercropping, green manure — feed and cover the soil itself.

## Sub lesson 3 — Worked landscapes

The book illustrates the toolkit with Tigray's stone bunds, China's loess terraces, and a restored gully at Awzet in Farta — proof that degraded land can be brought back.
