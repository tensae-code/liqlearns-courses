# The conservation toolkit — terraces, bunds and closures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A check dam is built in —
a) a gully  b) a lake  c) a forest canopy
**Answer: a) a gully**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Area closure means —
a) resting land from grazing and cutting so it recovers  b) closing a market  c) fencing a town
**Answer: a) resting land from grazing and cutting so it recovers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bench terracing —
a) steps a slope so water slows and soil holds  b) raises dust  c) drains a swamp
**Answer: a) steps a slope so water slows and soil holds**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The restored gully example is at —
a) Awzet, Farta  b) Awasa  c) Gondar
**Answer: a) Awzet, Farta**
