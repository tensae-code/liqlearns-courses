# The conservation toolkit — terraces, bunds and closures — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four tools from the toolkit.
**Answer:** Any four of the named measures.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do terraces, bunds and check dams have in common?
**Answer:** They slow water and hold soil.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three illustrated landscapes.
**Answer:** Tigray stone bunds, Chinese loess terraces, the restored gully at Awzet, Farta.
