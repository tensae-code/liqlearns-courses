# Video script — Natural Resources — Renewable Resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some gifts never finish.
The sun. The wind. The rain. The Earth's deep heat.
Use them — their amount does not fall.
But listen: dirty the air, spoil the water —
the amount stays, the goodness goes.
Other gifts can finish.
Plants. Animals. Soil. They hold each other up.
Take more than they can give back — and they are gone.
Renewable means: it can come back.
It never means: it cannot be lost.
