# Hands-on — Natural Resources — Renewable Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wind and water watch
Stand outside for five minutes. Feel the wind and look at the sky. Write what the wind and sun are doing right now that people could turn to use — drying, turning, warming — the Adama farm's idea at your own scale.

## Capacity check
Choose one living renewable near home — a tree patch, grazing grass, a garden soil. Count or estimate what is taken from it in a week and what grows back. Is the taking inside its capacity, or beyond it? Write the evidence.
