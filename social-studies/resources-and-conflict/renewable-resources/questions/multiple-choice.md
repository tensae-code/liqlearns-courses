# Natural Resources — Renewable Resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which list is all renewables that cannot run out in quantity?**
a) Solar energy, wind energy, geothermal energy, the water cycle  b) Plants, animals, soil, forests  c) Coal, oil, copper, diamond  d) Soil, grass, cattle, fish
**Answer: a) Solar energy, wind energy, geothermal energy, the water cycle** — the book's table runs solar, wind, geothermal, the water cycle, the atmosphere and precipitation.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Use of the never-running-out stock cannot change its quantity, but it can —**
a) Make it grow  b) Damage its quality  c) Turn it into minerals  d) Stop the water cycle
**Answer: b) Damage its quality** — quantity is safe; quality can be damaged.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The Adama example pictured in the book is —**
a) A geothermal plant  b) A wind farm  c) A coal mine  d) A diamond field
**Answer: b) A wind farm** — the book pictures the Adama wind farm and a geothermal plant.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Plants, animals and soil, used beyond their capacity, can —**
a) Only grow faster  b) Be diminished, damaged or even disappear  c) Become non-renewable minerals  d) Never be harmed
**Answer: b) Be diminished, damaged or even disappear** — beyond capacity they can be diminished, damaged or disappear.
