# Natural Resources — Renewable Resources — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Solar energy drives the water cycle and warms the Earth for life.**
**Answer: True** — the book's table says exactly this.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) If air cannot run out, its quality cannot be damaged.**
**Answer: False** — use cannot change the quantity, but it can damage the quality.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Plants, animals and soil support each other.**
**Answer: True** — the book states the three support each other.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A renewable resource can never disappear.**
**Answer: False** — plants, animals and soil can disappear if used beyond capacity.
