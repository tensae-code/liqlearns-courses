# Natural Resources — Renewable Resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name four renewables from the book's never-running-out table.**
**Answer:** Any four of: solar energy, wind energy, geothermal energy, the water cycle, the atmosphere, precipitation (also the Earth's water, air, natural beauty, tides and waves).

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Give one example of a renewable being used beyond its capacity.**
**Answer:** A forest cut faster than it regrows, grass grazed beyond recovery, or soil cropped until it is tired — any case where taking exceeds replacing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What does 'renewable' promise, and what does it not promise?**
**Answer:** It promises the resource can replace and renew itself through natural processes; it does not promise it cannot be lost.
