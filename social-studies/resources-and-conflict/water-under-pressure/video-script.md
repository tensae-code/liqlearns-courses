# Video script — Water under pressure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rivers and lakes are polluted by domestic and industrial waste, plastics and water hyacinth, oil spills and chemical toxicity.

Pollution arrives from everywhere at once — homes, factories, farms and ships — while the people who suffer most from dirty water are rarely the ones who dirty it..

The remedies are practical: safe sewerage, laws on waste discharge, industry sited away from water, desalination, wastewater recycling, porous pavements, and watershed management..
