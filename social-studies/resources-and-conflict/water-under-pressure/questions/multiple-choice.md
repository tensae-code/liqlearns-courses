# Water under pressure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Demand is racing toward a projected water crisis at —
a) mid-century  b) the year 3000  c) no date at all
**Answer: a) mid-century**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Water hyacinth harms lakes as —
a) a polluting invader  b) a fish food  c) a water purifier
**Answer: a) a polluting invader**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The crisis projection comes as population nears —
a) 10 billion  b) 1 billion  c) 100 billion
**Answer: a) 10 billion**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A remedy that reuses water is —
a) wastewater recycling  b) oil spilling  c) plastic dumping
**Answer: a) wastewater recycling**
