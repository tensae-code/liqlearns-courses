# Water under pressure

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: why is a world covered in water heading for a water crisis? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The squeeze on freshwater

Rivers and lakes are polluted by domestic and industrial waste, plastics and water hyacinth, oil spills and chemical toxicity. At the same time demand races upward, toward a projected mid-century world water crisis as population nears 10 billion.

[Picture 27027 — a lake edge choked with water hyacinth and plastic, beside a clean protected spring]

## Sub lesson 2 — Why it is hard to fix

Pollution arrives from everywhere at once — homes, factories, farms and ships — while the people who suffer most from dirty water are rarely the ones who dirty it.

## Sub lesson 3 — The remedy list

The remedies are practical: safe sewerage, laws on waste discharge, industry sited away from water, desalination, wastewater recycling, porous pavements, and watershed management.
