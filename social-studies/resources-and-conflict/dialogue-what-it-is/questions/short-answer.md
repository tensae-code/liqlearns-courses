# Dialogue What It is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Culture of Dialogue — dialogue is a basic life skill and the peaceful way to settle disputes — the class elects a "dialogue ambassador"; absent dialogue breeds misunderstanding and violence; through dialogue the two boys' football quarrel ends in reconciliation, friendship and peace (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Libya's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 5 is a pure values book — eight lessons, each a single civic virtue (equality, justice, solidarity, tolerance, accepting the other, dialogue), each anchored in a real instrument a child can hold: the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, UNESCO's 1995 Declaration on Tolerance, and the Libyan Scouts' "A World of Peace for Children" charter, which the pupil signs. (any close wording).**
