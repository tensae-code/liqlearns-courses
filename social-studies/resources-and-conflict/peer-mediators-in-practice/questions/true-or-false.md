# Peer Mediators in Practice — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the mediator's toolkit — settlement (تسوية), a way of ending a dispute both sides accept though each gets less than he wanted; concession (تنازل), giving something up to end a dispute; and active listening (الإصغاء النشط), concentrating fully on what the other says — and the five styles of facing disputes, from forcing a win to working together for the fair outcome
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: mediation practised — the story of two classmates whose quarrel is ended by talking and listening to each other, then role-play scenes in which pupils mediate a dispute themselves, using settlements and concessions, and judge whether the characters reached one
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of the lesson teaches: in the Moral Education course, nine-year-olds are trained as peer mediators with a named toolkit — settlement, concession, active listening — and the five ways of facing a dispute are taught through animals (the shark forces, the turtle withdraws, the bear yields, the fox compromises, the owl solves); responsibility is taught by a lost wallet with 800 dirhams in it.
**Answer: True**
