# Video script — Peer Mediators in Practice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G4 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 41, teaches: the mediator's toolkit — settlement (تسوية), a way of ending a dispute both sides accept though each gets less than he wanted; concession (تنازل), giving something up to end a dispute; and active listening (الإصغاء النشط), concentrating fully on what the other says — and the five styles of facing disputes, from forcing a win to working together for the fair outcome

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G4 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 47, teaches: mediation practised — the story of two classmates whose quarrel is ended by talking and listening to each other, then role-play scenes in which pupils mediate a dispute themselves, using settlements and concessions, and judge whether the characters reached one

United Arab Emirates' own way: in the Moral Education course, nine-year-olds are trained as peer mediators with a named toolkit — settlement, concession, active listening — and the five ways of facing a dispute are taught through animals (the shark forces, the turtle withdraws, the bear yields, the fox compromises, the owl solves); responsibility is taught by a lost wallet with 800 dirhams in it.
