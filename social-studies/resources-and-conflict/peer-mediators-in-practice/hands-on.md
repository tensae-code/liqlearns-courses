# Hands-on — Peer Mediators in Practice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Peer mediators 1
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the mediator's toolkit — settlement (تسوية), a way of ending a dispute both sides accept though each gets less than he wanted; concession (تنازل), giving something up to end a dispute; and active listening (الإصغاء النشط), concentrating fully on what the other says — and the five styles of facing disputes, from forcing a win to working together for the fair outcome Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Peer mediators 2
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: mediation practised — the story of two classmates whose quarrel is ended by talking and listening to each other, then role-play scenes in which pupils mediate a dispute themselves, using settlements and concessions, and judge whether the characters reached one Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
