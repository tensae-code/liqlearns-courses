# Degradation in all its forms

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: degradation is not only soil washing away — how many forms are there? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The types

Degradation's types: soil degradation, water degradation, atmospheric degradation including ozone depletion — plus noise pollution and light pollution, the two forms you can neither see as damage nor hold in your hand.

[Picture 27072 — a four-panel set — eroded soil, a dumped river, a smoky sky, an overflowing landfill]

## Sub lesson 2 — The causes

Causes run from land disturbance, pollution, overpopulation and landfills to deforestation, illegal dumping — and natural events, which no policy can repeal.

## Sub lesson 3 — The effects

Effects: respiratory disease, biodiversity loss, ozone loss, disturbed natural cycles, scarcity of land, water, genetic resources and food crops — and the direct line from all of them to food shortage and famine.

## Say it to your companion

Explain degradation in all its forms to your companion in your own words: what it is, the one fact you would never want to forget from it, and one example from your own surroundings or from Ethiopia. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
