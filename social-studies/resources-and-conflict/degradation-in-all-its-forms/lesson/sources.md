Zambia's own way (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 4's Competence-Based Curriculum module teaches degradation by doing — naming the actions that destroy the environment (burning waste, deforestation, litter, charcoal burning, river pollution) inside a topic taught through observation walks, a community map, role-play and a school tree-planting and clean-up campaign, with Zambian examples (Zambezi and Kafue rivers, copper, maize soil, charcoal burning); Grade 8's Geography module is built around Zambia itself, opening its Environment topic with environmental degradation. That evidence is stocked here as the way Zambia teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
