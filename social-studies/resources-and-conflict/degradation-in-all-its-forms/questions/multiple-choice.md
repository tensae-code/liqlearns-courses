# Degradation in all its forms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ozone depletion belongs to —
a) atmospheric degradation  b) soil degradation  c) noise pollution
**Answer: a) atmospheric degradation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Noise and light are —
a) pollution types in the catalogue  b) remedies  c) natural events
**Answer: a) pollution types in the catalogue**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Illegal dumping is a —
a) cause  b) effect  c) type
**Answer: a) cause**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The effects' final line runs to —
a) food shortage and famine  b) higher yields  c) cleaner air
**Answer: a) food shortage and famine**
