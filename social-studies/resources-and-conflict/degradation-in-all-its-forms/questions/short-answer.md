# Degradation in all its forms — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the types.
**Answer:** Soil, water, atmospheric (incl. ozone), noise, light.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List three causes.
**Answer:** Land disturbance, pollution, overpopulation, landfills, deforestation, illegal dumping, natural events (any three).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List three effects.
**Answer:** Respiratory disease, biodiversity loss, ozone loss, disturbed cycles, scarcities, food shortage/famine (any three).
