# Video script — Degradation in all its forms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Degradation's types: soil degradation, water degradation, atmospheric degradation including ozone depletion — plus noise pollution and light pollution, the two forms you can neither see as damage nor hold in your hand..

Causes run from land disturbance, pollution, overpopulation and landfills to deforestation, illegal dumping — and natural events, which no policy can repeal..

Effects: respiratory disease, biodiversity loss, ozone loss, disturbed natural cycles, scarcity of land, water, genetic resources and food crops — and the direct line from all of them to food shortage and famine..
