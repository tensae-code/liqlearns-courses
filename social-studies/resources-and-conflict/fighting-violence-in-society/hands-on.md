# Hands-on — Fighting Violence in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fighting Violence in Society
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The book sends learners to research the mechanisms for fighting violence in Palestinian society, guided by strengthening the culture of dialogue, the role of the official and civic institutions, the role of the Ministry of Education in limiting school violence in particular, and the role of the media in raising awareness of violence's dangers. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Palestine's way
Say how Palestine's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
