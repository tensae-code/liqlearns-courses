# Rivers that cross borders

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: who owns a river that flows through three countries? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a shared watercourse is

A transboundary watercourse is a river, lake or aquifer that crosses borders; oceans are excluded from the definition. Sharing is the normal condition of the world's great waters.

[Picture 27031 — a world map of trans-state rivers, with the Danube and Nile picked out]

## Sub lesson 2 — The count

There are 263 international river and lake basins, plus some 300 transboundary aquifers. They carry about 60 percent of the world's freshwater flow, across 145 nations, for about 40 percent of humanity.

## Sub lesson 3 — The most-shared river

Africa and South America each have about 60 percent of their area inside international basins. The Danube, crossing 19 nations, is the most-shared river on Earth.
