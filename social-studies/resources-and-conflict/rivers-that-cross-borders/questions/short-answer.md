# Rivers that cross borders — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a shared watercourse.
**Answer:** A river, lake or aquifer crossing borders (oceans excluded).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How many nations share these basins?
**Answer:** 145 nations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which river is most shared, and by how many nations?
**Answer:** The Danube, 19 nations.
