# Rivers that cross borders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** International river and lake basins number —
a) 263  b) 63  c) 1,263
**Answer: a) 263**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shared basins carry about —
a) 60% of the world's freshwater flow  b) 6% of flow  c) all ocean water
**Answer: a) 60% of the world's freshwater flow**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Transboundary aquifers number about —
a) 300  b) 30  c) 3,000
**Answer: a) 300**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The most-shared river, crossing 19 nations, is —
a) the Danube  b) the Nile  c) the Congo
**Answer: a) the Danube**
