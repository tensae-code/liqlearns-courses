The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A transboundary watercourse is a river, lake or aquifer that crosses borders; oceans are excluded from the definition.

There are 263 international river and lake basins, plus some 300 transboundary aquifers.

Africa and South America each have about 60 percent of their area inside international basins.
