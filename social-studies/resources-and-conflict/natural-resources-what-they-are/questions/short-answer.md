# Natural Resources — What They Are — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the three soils in the book's table and say what pupils complete about plants.**
**Answer:** Clay soil, loam and sandy soil; pupils complete the plant kinds that grow and the uses of each plant.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) In one sentence, why does proper use matter more than possession?**
**Answer:** Because the book says the difference between development and prosperity comes from whether a country uses its resources properly or not.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Write the guide's three gains from natural resources.**
**Answer:** Fiscal revenue and income, job opportunities, and reduced poverty.
