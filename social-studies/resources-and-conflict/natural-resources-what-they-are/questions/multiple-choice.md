# Natural Resources — What They Are — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A natural resource is best defined as —**
a) Things people manufacture in factories  b) Material and non-material things in nature that people, using knowledge, can turn to use  c) Only minerals found underground  d) Money a government collects
**Answer: b) Material and non-material things in nature that people, using knowledge, can turn to use** — the book's definition covers material and non-material things in nature, turned to use by knowledge.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which of these is a non-material natural resource?**
a) Clay soil  b) A forest tree  c) Sunlight  d) River water
**Answer: c) Sunlight** — sunlight cannot be held, yet knowledge turns it to use.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) According to the book, the difference between development and prosperity comes from —**
a) How many resources a country has  b) Whether a country uses its resources properly or not  c) How big the country is  d) How many people it has
**Answer: b) Whether a country uses its resources properly or not** — proper use, not mere possession, decides prosperity.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Which three gains does the teacher guide name for resources used well?**
a) Revenue, jobs and reduced poverty  b) Armies, palaces and roads only  c) Imports, exports and loans  d) Rain, wind and sunlight
**Answer: a) Revenue, jobs and reduced poverty** — the guide frames the benefit as fiscal revenue and income, jobs, and reduced poverty.
