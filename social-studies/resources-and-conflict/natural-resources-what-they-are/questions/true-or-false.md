# Natural Resources — What They Are — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A mineral nobody knows how to use already gives a country its full benefit.**
**Answer: False** — knowledge must turn a thing in nature to use before it serves as a resource.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Every country meets its people's needs from natural resources.**
**Answer: True** — the book states this for every country.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) A country with fewer resources can never prosper.**
**Answer: False** — proper use, not amount, is the book's deciding difference.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Reduced poverty is one of the gains the guide names from resources.**
**Answer: True** — revenue, jobs and poverty reduction are the guide's three.
