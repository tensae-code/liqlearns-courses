Zambia's own way (teacher guide — evidence): Competence-Based Curriculum teaching modules written to bridge a textbook gap: each topic is taught by doing — observation walks, drawing a community map of local resources, role-play and a school tree-planting and clean-up campaign, with Zambian examples (Zambezi and Kafue rivers, copper, maize soil, charcoal burning). That evidence is stocked here as the way Zambia teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
