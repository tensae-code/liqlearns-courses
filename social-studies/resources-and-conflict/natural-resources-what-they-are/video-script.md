# Video script — Natural Resources — What They Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What is a natural resource?
Look. Soil. Water. Trees. Stone.
And look again. Sunlight. Wind. Beauty.
Things in nature — touched, and untouched.
They become resources when knowledge turns them to use.
Every country lives from its resources.
But having is not prospering.
Using properly — that is the difference.
Use well: revenue comes, jobs come, poverty falls.
Use badly: all three are lost.
Now you. Name one resource. How is it used — properly, or not?
