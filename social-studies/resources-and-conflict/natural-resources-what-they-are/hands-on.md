# Hands-on — Natural Resources — What They Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Resource walk
Walk your compound or street and write down every natural resource you meet — at least eight. Beside each, write whether it is material or non-material, and the use people make of it.

## The soil-and-plant table
Dig a small handful of soil in two different places. Rub each between your fingers: sticky clay, crumbly loam, or gritty sand? Write which plants grow in each soil, and one use of each plant — the book's table, filled from your own ground.
