# Governing natural resources fairly — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Good resource governance can serve both people and biodiversity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Forced removal of indigenous communities is allowed without consent.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Access to information is the first Aarhus pillar.
**Answer: True**
