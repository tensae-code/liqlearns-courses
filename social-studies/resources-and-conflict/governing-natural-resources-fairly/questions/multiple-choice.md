# Governing natural resources fairly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Resource governance decides…
a) how power and responsibility over resources are exercised  b) the weather  c) crop prices only
**Answer: a) how power and responsibility over resources are exercised**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Participation in governance includes…
a) officials only  b) women, men, indigenous peoples, local communities  c) companies only
**Answer: b) women, men, indigenous peoples, local communities**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous communities are hit hardest by…
a) exploitation — monoculture, industrial fishing, logging, mining, dams  b) tourism  c) rainfall
**Answer: a) exploitation — monoculture, industrial fishing, logging, mining, dams**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** UNDRIP Article 10 requires, for relocation…
a) nothing  b) free, prior, informed consent and fair compensation  c) a fence
**Answer: b) free, prior, informed consent and fair compensation**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The CBD recognises…
a) traditional knowledge  b) only laboratory science  c) mining rights
**Answer: a) traditional knowledge**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Aarhus pillars are information, participation and…
a) taxation  b) access to justice  c) silence
**Answer: b) access to justice**
