# Governing natural resources fairly — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define natural resource governance.
**Answer:** The norms, institutions and processes deciding how power and responsibility over resources are exercised, how decisions are taken, and how citizens participate in and benefit from management.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three Aarhus pillars.
**Answer:** Access to information; participation in decision-making; access to justice.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does UNDRIP Article 10 protect?
**Answer:** No forced removal: relocation only with free, prior, informed consent and fair compensation.
