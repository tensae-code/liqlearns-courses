Searchable tags: natural resource governance, UNDRIP, Aarhus Convention, indigenous rights, participation, social studies

Samoa's own way (Year 9, Book 1): Geography is taught through Samoa's own resources first — environment, land, rainforest, water and marine resources of Samoa itself, then how resources are shared out in Samoan community life.

Samoa's own way (Year 11, Book 1): The marine environment is the Geography core — Samoa's own reefs and fisheries, taught through perception, traditional and national management law (with SPREP input), and a student investigation.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado6 Postprimaria): the rural Postprimaria Grade 6 guide is built entirely around indigenous peoples — where they live, what they do in their territories, their ancestral struggles for land, and their population and resource use — geography taught as peoples-and-territory.
