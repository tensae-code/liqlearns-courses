# Video script — Governing natural resources fairly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Natural resource governance decides who holds power over resources and who benefits — and fairness means indigenous rights protected and every citizen able to know, to take part, and to seek justice. First, what governance is — and who pays: Natural resource governance is the norms, institutions and processes deciding how power and responsibility over resources are exercised, how decisions are taken, and how citizens — women, men, indigenous peoples, local communities — participate in and benefit from management. Then, rights and the three pillars: The protections have names. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
