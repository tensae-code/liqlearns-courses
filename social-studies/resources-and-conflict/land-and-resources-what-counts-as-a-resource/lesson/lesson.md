# Land and resources — what counts as a resource

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: is land just ground — or is it five things at once? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Land — five things at once

Land is introduced as a resource, as environment, as a legal object, as an economic asset and as cultural wealth — the foundation all life and wealth rest on. No other resource wears so many faces.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sorting resources five ways

Resources sort by regeneration (renewable and non-renewable), by origin (biotic and abiotic), by use and availability (stock and flow), by distribution (from ubiquitous to rare), and by stage (potential, actual and conditional).

## Sub lesson 3 — Renewable is not inexhaustible

Overuse depletes even renewable resources. Non-renewables like minerals and fossil fuels can only be husbanded, reused and recycled — what is burned or scattered is gone.
