# Land and resources — what counts as a resource — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Minerals and fossil fuels are non-renewable.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A renewable resource can never be depleted.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Non-renewables can be reused and recycled.
**Answer: True**
