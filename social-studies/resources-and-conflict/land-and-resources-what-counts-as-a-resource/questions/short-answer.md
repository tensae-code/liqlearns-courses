# Land and resources — what counts as a resource — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name land's five faces.
**Answer:** Resource, environment, legal object, economic asset, cultural wealth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two of the five sortings.
**Answer:** By regeneration, origin, use/availability, distribution or stage (any two).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How should non-renewables be treated?
**Answer:** Husbanded, reused and recycled.
