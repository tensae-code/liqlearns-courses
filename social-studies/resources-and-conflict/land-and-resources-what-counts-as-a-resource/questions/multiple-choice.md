# Land and resources — what counts as a resource — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land is at once a resource, environment, legal object, economic asset and —
a) cultural wealth  b) waste  c) climate
**Answer: a) cultural wealth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** By origin, resources sort into —
a) biotic and abiotic  b) hot and cold  c) local and foreign
**Answer: a) biotic and abiotic**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** By stage, a resource can be potential, actual or —
a) conditional  b) imaginary  c) free
**Answer: a) conditional**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Renewable resources —
a) can be depleted by overuse  b) can never run out  c) need no care
**Answer: a) can be depleted by overuse**
