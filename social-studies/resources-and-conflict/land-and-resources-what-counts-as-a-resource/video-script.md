# Video script — Land and resources — what counts as a resource

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Land is introduced as a resource, as environment, as a legal object, as an economic asset and as cultural wealth — the foundation all life and wealth rest on.

Resources sort by regeneration (renewable and non-renewable), by origin (biotic and abiotic), by use and availability (stock and flow), by distribution (from ubiquitous to rare), and by stage (potential, actual and conditional)..

Overuse depletes even renewable resources.
