# Conflict — Its Three Roots

## Do it first

Think of three quarrels you know: one over land or money, one over customs or beliefs, one over who gives orders.

Could they really be three different *kinds* of quarrel, growing from three different roots? The scholar Kart, as the book prints him, says yes. Check your three against his three.

## Sub lesson 1 — The economic root and the values root

Following the scholar **Kart** as printed:

**Economic conflict rises from resource limits** — each side's growing want to benefit more throws them into **rivalry, and then conflict**. This is the spring of the lesson before, given its root-name: where the resource is limited and wants grow against it, the quarrel is economic.

**Values conflict rises from ways of life, world-view choices and wants clashing** — when **one society's outlook does not fit another's**, or when **people are pressured to follow another's way of life by force**. Nobody here lacks a resource; what collides is what each side holds right and proper.

## Sub lesson 2 — The power root

**Power conflict rises when one group or person strains to dominate another.**

The dominated side does not rest: it **strives to win its balance of power back**. The struggle between straining-down and striving-back is the conflict itself.

And the book states its end plainly: the struggle **ends only when one side accepts defeat and the other's supremacy**. That hard ending is why power conflicts are the slowest to settle — the settlement methods of the next-but-one lesson must work differently on each root.

## Say it to your companion

Name Kart's three roots for your companion. Then take two quarrels your companion invents and sort each to its root, giving the mechanism as your reason.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write the three roots with a one-line mechanism for each, from memory, then check.
2. Sort three real disagreements you know to their roots and write the evidence for each sorting.
3. Write one quarrel that carries two roots at once, and say which root you judge stronger, and why.
