# Video script — Conflict — Its Three Roots

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every quarrel grows from a root. Kart names three.
First: economics. A limited resource. Growing wants.
Rivalry — then conflict.
Second: values. Ways of life collide.
One outlook does not fit another. Force makes it worse.
Third: power. One side strains to dominate.
The other strives to win its balance back.
It ends only when one accepts defeat — and the other's rule.
Name the root before you try to settle the quarrel.
