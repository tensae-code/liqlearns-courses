# Hands-on — Conflict — Its Three Roots

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Root hunt in stories
Take a story the class knows — a tale, a news event, a village case. In groups, argue its root: economic, values or power. Each group must quote the mechanism, not just the label.

## Panel drawing
Draw Kart's three panels yourself: a limited resource striven over; two customs colliding; one figure straining to dominate while the other strives back. Caption each with its root.
