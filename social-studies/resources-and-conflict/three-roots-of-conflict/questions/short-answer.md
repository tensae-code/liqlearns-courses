# Conflict — Its Three Roots — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Explain the economic root in one sentence.**
**Answer:** Resource limits set each side's growing want to benefit more against the other's, throwing them into rivalry and then conflict.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Give the two ways a values conflict can rise.**
**Answer:** When one society's outlook does not fit another's, and when people are pressured to follow another's way of life by force.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Trace a power conflict from opening to end.**
**Answer:** One group or person strains to dominate another; the dominated side strives to win its balance of power back; the struggle ends only when one side accepts defeat and the other's supremacy.
