# Conflict — Its Three Roots — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Kart's three roots are economic, values and power.**
**Answer: True** — those are the three as printed.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Values conflict is caused by a limited resource.**
**Answer: False** — values conflict is caused by clashing ways of life and world-views.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Power conflict begins when one group or person strains to dominate another.**
**Answer: True** — that straining is its opening.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A power conflict ends when resources are shared equally.**
**Answer: False** — as printed it ends when one side accepts defeat and the other's supremacy.
