# Conflict — Its Three Roots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Economic conflict rises from —**
a) Clashing customs  b) Resource limits — each side's growing want to benefit more  c) A wish to dominate  d) Misunderstanding only
**Answer: b) Resource limits — each side's growing want to benefit more** — limits plus growing want is the economic mechanism.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Values conflict rises when —**
a) A spring dries up  b) One society's outlook does not fit another's, or people are forced into another's way of life  c) One side wants more grazing  d) Prices rise
**Answer: b) One society's outlook does not fit another's, or people are forced into another's way of life** — clashing outlooks and forced ways of life define the values root.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In power conflict, the dominated side —**
a) Accepts at once  b) Strives to win its balance of power back  c) Leaves the country always  d) Asks for mediation first
**Answer: b) Strives to win its balance of power back** — striving back for balance is the dominated side's move.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A power struggle ends, as printed, when —**
a) Resources increase  b) One side accepts defeat and the other's supremacy  c) A mediator arrives  d) Both sides forget it
**Answer: b) One side accepts defeat and the other's supremacy** — accepted defeat and supremacy is the printed ending.
