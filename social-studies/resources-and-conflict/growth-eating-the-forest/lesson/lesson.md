# Growth eating the forest

## Do it first

The same numbers as the forest lesson — now read them as a mechanism: each added million of people takes its share of trees. Read growth eating the forest.

## Sub lesson 1 — The mechanism

Rapid growth multiplies every demand the forest meets: fuelwood for more kitchens, timber for more houses, land for more farms. Supply cannot multiply — a tree grows at a tree’s pace. Where multiplied demand meets unmultiplied supply, over-exploitation follows as arithmetic, not accident.

## Sub lesson 2 — The worked case

The worked Ethiopian case: forest cover fell from about 16% in the early 1950s to 3.6% in the early 1980s — and the pressure continued beyond. The case is the mechanism with dates and percentages attached: three decades of the arithmetic, drawn on the map of a country.
