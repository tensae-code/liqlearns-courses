# Hands-on — Growth eating the forest

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pace race
Model the race: one partner adds a demand token each round for new households; the other adds a tree token only every third round (a tree’s pace). Run ten rounds and count the forest.

## Beyond the eighties
The case ends in the early 1980s. Research-free reasoning: list what has happened to population and to protection since, and argue whether the cover line rose or fell — mark every claim as argument, not data.
