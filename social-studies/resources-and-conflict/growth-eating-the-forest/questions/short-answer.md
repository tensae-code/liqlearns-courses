# Growth eating the forest — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the mechanism.
**Answer: Multiplied demand meets supply that cannot multiply at the same pace, so exploitation exceeds renewal.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is this the worked case?
**Answer: It attaches dates and percentages to the mechanism — three decades of the arithmetic on one country’s map.**
