# Growth eating the forest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The demands multiplied by growth include —
a) only fuelwood  b) only land  c) fuelwood, timber and farm land
**Answer: c) fuelwood, timber and farm land**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Supply cannot multiply because —
a) demand falls  b) a tree grows at a tree’s pace  c) trees are banned
**Answer: b) a tree grows at a tree’s pace**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Forest cover in the early 1950s was about —
a) 16%  b) 3.6%  c) 40%
**Answer: a) 16%**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** By the early 1980s it stood at about —
a) 16%  b) 12%  c) 3.6%
**Answer: c) 3.6%**
