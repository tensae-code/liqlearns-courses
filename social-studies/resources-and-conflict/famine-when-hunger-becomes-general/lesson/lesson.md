# Famine — when hunger becomes general

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: drought dries a land — what turns hunger into famine? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What famine is

Famine is defined as persistent, widespread, acute hunger and starvation. Every word carries weight: not one season, not one village, not mild — persistent, widespread, acute.

[Picture 27082 — an aid distribution line beside a rebuilt village grain store]

## Sub lesson 2 — Causes, natural and human

Natural hazards cause it: drought, flood, landslide, locusts, epidemics. Human causes compound them: unfair resource sharing, inequality and war. Famine is rarely nature acting alone.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fighting it, in time order

The counter-strategies run in time order: humanitarian aid for immediate needs; community rebuilding and rehabilitation; strengthening local food-system resilience; and long-term economic diversification and employment.
