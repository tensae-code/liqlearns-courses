# Famine — when hunger becomes general — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Famine is hunger that is persistent, widespread and —
a) acute  b) mild  c) brief
**Answer: a) acute**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A natural cause of famine is —
a) locusts  b) inequality  c) war
**Answer: a) locusts**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An unfair-sharing cause is —
a) a human cause  b) a natural hazard  c) a remedy
**Answer: a) a human cause**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The first counter-strategy in time order is —
a) humanitarian aid  b) diversification  c) employment
**Answer: a) humanitarian aid**
