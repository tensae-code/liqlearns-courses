# Famine — when hunger becomes general — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define famine.
**Answer:** Persistent, widespread, acute hunger and starvation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two natural and two human causes.
**Answer:** Natural: drought, flood, landslide, locusts, epidemics. Human: unfair sharing, inequality, war (two of each).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List the counter-strategies in order.
**Answer:** Aid; rebuilding/rehabilitation; food-system resilience; diversification and employment.
