# Video script — Famine — when hunger becomes general

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Famine is defined as persistent, widespread, acute hunger and starvation.

Natural hazards cause it: drought, flood, landslide, locusts, epidemics.

The counter-strategies run in time order: humanitarian aid for immediate needs; community rebuilding and rehabilitation; strengthening local food-system resilience; and long-term economic diversification and employment..
