# Preventing conflict over resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Prevention aims to act…
a) after violence  b) before conflict escalates to violence  c) only in courts
**Answer: b) before conflict escalates to violence**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Prevention approaches avoid, minimise, resolve and…
a) contain  b) hide  c) fund
**Answer: a) contain**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Interventions must match…
a) one standard template  b) the mix of drivers, vulnerabilities and capacity  c) the donor's preference
**Answer: b) the mix of drivers, vulnerabilities and capacity**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The livelihoods framework reads…
a) assets, strategies, outcomes  b) births, deaths, migration  c) imports and exports
**Answer: a) assets, strategies, outcomes**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Resource conflicts are entangled with…
a) livelihoods and governance  b) nothing else  c) sport
**Answer: a) livelihoods and governance**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Livelihood and governance interventions together keep conflicts…
a) secret  b) discussable and preventable  c) permanent
**Answer: b) discussable and preventable**
