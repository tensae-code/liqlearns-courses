# Preventing conflict over resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What four verbs define prevention?
**Answer:** Avoid, minimise, resolve and contain — before escalation to violence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three parts of the sustainable livelihoods framework.
**Answer:** Assets, strategies and outcomes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must interventions match the mix?
**Answer:** Because resource conflicts are politicised and entangled with livelihoods and governance; the right response depends on the drivers, vulnerabilities and institutional capacity in each case.
