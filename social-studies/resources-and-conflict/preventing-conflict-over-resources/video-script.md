# Video script — Preventing conflict over resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Prevention works before violence: match the intervention to the mix of drivers, and plan through people's livelihoods — assets, strategies, outcomes — so conflicts stay discussable. First, what prevention means: Prevention means the approaches that avoid, minimise, resolve and contain conflict before it escalates to violence. Then, the sustainable livelihoods route: The book's planning tool is the sustainable livelihoods framework, which reads a community through its assets, its strategies, and its outcomes. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
