# Preventing conflict over resources

## Do it first

Think of a quarrel that was stopped early. What stopped it — a rule, a person, an alternative? Write it down; prevention in this lesson works the same way, at a larger scale.

## Sub lesson 1 — What prevention means

Prevention means the approaches that avoid, minimise, resolve and contain conflict before it escalates to violence. The work is done early, while the conflict can still be talked about.

Resource conflicts are politicised and entangled with livelihoods and governance, so no single fix fits all. Interventions must match the mix: the drivers at work, the vulnerabilities people carry, and the institutional capacity available to act.

## Sub lesson 2 — The sustainable livelihoods route

The book's planning tool is the sustainable livelihoods framework, which reads a community through its assets, its strategies, and its outcomes. Secure the livelihood, and the resource stops being worth a fight.

Sustainable-livelihood and governance interventions together keep conflicts discussable and preventable: people with working livelihoods and working institutions argue — they do not need to fight.
