# Saving Ethiopia’s forests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cause of forest degradation is —
a) planting seedlings  b) area closure  c) clearing for farms
**Answer: c) clearing for farms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An effect of degradation is —
a) rivers deepening usefully  b) soil washing away and streams silting  c) more rainfall everywhere
**Answer: b) soil washing away and streams silting**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The protection presented is —
a) community-forest protection with strict legal measures  b) foreign guards only  c) no protection at all
**Answer: a) community-forest protection with strict legal measures**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The second reversal measure is —
a) importing fuel  b) moving all farmers  c) moderating population growth
**Answer: c) moderating population growth**
