# Video script — Saving Ethiopia’s forests

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Forests fall to the axe, the plough, the herd.
Then the soil washes, the streams silt, the wildlife scatters.
The saving: communities guarding their own forest, law behind them.
And easing the pressure itself — slower growth of demand.
