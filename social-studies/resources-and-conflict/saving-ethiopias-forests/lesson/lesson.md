# Saving Ethiopia’s forests

## Do it first

A guarded community forest, green inside its boundary, bare outside it. Read how forests are saved where they still stand.

## Sub lesson 1 — Causes and effects

Forest degradation comes from clearing for farms, cutting for fuel and timber, and the grazing and burning that stop young trees returning. Its effects run downhill: soil washes away, streams silt and dry, wildlife loses its home, and the climate bargain of the forest — shade, rain-holding, carbon — is lost with it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The reversal

The reversal the book presents: community-forest protection — local people guarding their forest under strict legal measures — together with moderating population growth so the pressure itself eases. Protection works when the community owns it and the law backs it.
