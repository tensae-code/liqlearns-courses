# Hands-on — Saving Ethiopia’s forests

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Boundary walk in words
Describe a protected community forest you know or imagine: its boundary, its guards or rules, what is allowed in and what is not. Mark which elements come from the lesson.

## Cause-effect web
Draw forest clearing in the centre and web out its effects — soil, streams, wildlife, climate. Then web the protection answer onto the same page.
