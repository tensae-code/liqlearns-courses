# National Reconciliation Building A Better Angola

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: national reconciliation — Angolans building together an Angola that is better to live in — as Angola's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — A reconciliação nacional em Angola

Angola Educação Moral e Cívica G5 (printed p. 103), teaches: National reconciliation — Angolans building together an Angola that is better to live in. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Construir um Mundo melhor para se viver

Angola Educação Moral e Cívica G5 (printed p. 107), teaches: The same building widened — making a better world to live in. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Angola's way — the book's own teaching way

Angola's own way: a moral-and-civic book built on the child's own reflection — every unit opens with the pupil's own life (my habits, my family, my school) and only then names the rule; equality is taught straight from Angola's own Constitution (art. 23: all are equal before the Constitution and the law), and the book ends not on the state but on national reconciliation — building a better Angola, then a better world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
