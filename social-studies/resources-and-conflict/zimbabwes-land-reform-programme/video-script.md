# Video script — Zimbabwe's Land Reform Programme

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe's Grade 7 teacher guide, the *PlusOne Social Science Grade 7 Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 11), teaches: Unit 5: The land reform programme.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 7): Grade 7 turns analytical — factors behind transport-mode choice, up-to-date maps/charts (routes, contours, scale-and-distance work) for domestic, regional and international travel, transport hazards from floods/fog/earthquakes, shelter shortages and siting factors, and desertification with land rehabilitation (afforestation, gully filling).
