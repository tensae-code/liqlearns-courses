Bahamas' own way (Grade 1): One integrated "Social Science" book fusing Religious Studies, Science/Health and Social Studies in a single picture-led volume; Geography enters through the home island itself — The Bahamas as a group of islands, its national symbols, mailboat/jitney transport between islands, conch-and-crawfish fishing, national parks and bush-medicine plants.

Bahamas' own way (Grade 3): The integrated Social Science pattern continues, but Grade 3 adds a distinct Bahamas-geography block in the middle of the book: a full map study of the archipelago (700 islands and cays, Family Islands, three sections), inter-island travel by Bahamasair, fast ferry and mailboat, and an island-by-island inventory of natural resources (Andros crabs, Abaco farming, Grand Bahama citrus and sponge), closing with Hurricane Dorian (2019) as the national extreme-weather case.

Samoa's own way (Year 9, Book 1): Geography is taught through Samoa's own resources first — environment, land, rainforest, water and marine resources of Samoa itself, then how resources are shared out in Samoan community life.

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Social Studies): one integrated Social Studies book per grade weaving History, Geography, Civics and Culture strands; Geography is taught through Eritrea itself first (Eritrea's location and size, Eritrean settlement, migration and disasters) with very practical, activity-led lessons.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 6 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): P6 widens the frame from the province to the country, then to East Africa and Africa — Rwanda is located by latitude and longitude (1°S–3°S, 28°E–31°E), its landlocked position is taught as the reason for regional co-operation, and every unit is built around a problem statement with the pupil's book activities answered in the guide. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Rwanda is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 6 Social Studies SB): the P6 pupil's book is the fullest Geography book of the set — a whole unit on direction and location works Rwanda onto the East African and African maps by latitude and longitude and time zones, then physical features and climate, and a climate-change sequence (types, change, effects, overcoming) sits inside it; regional co-operation is taught bloc by bloc (COMESA, CEPGL, ECCAS, AU, NEPAD).

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 8 Social Science): Year 8 turns fully outward — "Exploring the World": world climate zones read from Fiji's tropical position, then global warming, resource management (including money and time) and globalisation from a Fiji/Pacific viewpoint.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): the five strands widen again — from district (Grade 4) to the physical environment of the province and nation; resources are taught through sustainable use (re-using water, reduce/reuse projects) and map work culminates in drawing sketch maps of the school and locality. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): the local-to-global ladder completes — after province (G5), nation (G7), Grade 8's Environment and Resources strand is global: global climate, global settlement and population, global farming, urbanization and logging impacts, global hazards and disaster resilience. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela): a final-year economic geography of Venezuela alone — population past/present/future, human development in numbers, then resource by resource (minerals, land, fisheries, forests, oil, water/hydroelectricity) each read through sovereignty, collective participation and environmental conscience, closing on Latin American union.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G2 SS T2): Term 2 teaches change over time (transport, housing, writing) and the child's economy (production, consumption, recycling, Jordanian-made goods), closing with the blue planet, Earth's surface shapes and a first map of Jordan.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G6 SS T2): Term 2's geography is climate done properly — elements, effects on human activity, Jordan's own climate regions — plus economic concepts and three more governorate journeys (Mafraq, Balqa, Aqaba).
