# Video script — Natural Resources — Using Resources Well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A resource in the ground is not yet in use.
What is missing? Money. A road. Skill. Tools. Policy.
Six conditions open the door.
Then use it properly.
Hold the population. Change the energy — sun, wind, Earth's heat.
Cover the bare land. Fence the protected place. Plant.
Use badly, and one bill arrives:
no water, no forest, minerals gone, species gone, land turning desert.
Use well, or pay the bill. Choose.
