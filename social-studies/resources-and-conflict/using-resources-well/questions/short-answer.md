# Natural Resources — Using Resources Well — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name any four of the six conditions.**
**Answer:** Any four of: capital capacity, location, trained manpower, state of technology, government policy, the resource's natural condition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name three methods of proper use and care from the student book.**
**Answer:** Any three of: controlling population growth, alternative energy (wind, geothermal, solar), increasing and protecting plant cover, covering exposed lands, modern land use (soil and water conservation, fencing protected places).

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Give four items from the guide's bill for unwise use.**
**Answer:** Any four of: water shortages, loss of forest cover, depletion of minerals, extinction of species, pollution, degradation of ecosystems, desertification, global warming, drought, ozone depletion, climate change.
