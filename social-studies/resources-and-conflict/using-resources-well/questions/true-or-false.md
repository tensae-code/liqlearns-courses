# Natural Resources — Using Resources Well — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Government policy is one of the six conditions.**
**Answer: True** — policy is condition five.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) A resource's natural condition does not affect whether it can be used.**
**Answer: False** — natural condition is the sixth condition.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Controlling population growth is one of the book's methods of proper use and care.**
**Answer: True** — it heads the book's methods list.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) The guide treats the consequences of unwise use as one connected bill.**
**Answer: True** — §3.3 and its keys consolidate them into a single list.
