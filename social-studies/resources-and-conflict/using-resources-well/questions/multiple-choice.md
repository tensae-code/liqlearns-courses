# Natural Resources — Using Resources Well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which list is the book's six conditions for using resources?**
a) Capital, location, trained manpower, technology, policy, natural condition  b) Rain, wind, sun, soil, rivers, forests  c) Imports, exports, taxes, trade, markets, prices  d) Terraces, fences, trees, dams, roads, wells
**Answer: a) Capital, location, trained manpower, technology, policy, natural condition** — those six are the enabling conditions on printed p. 83.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Which is one of the book's methods of proper use?**
a) Burning more forest  b) Fencing off protected places  c) Draining springs  d) Cropping soil until it tires
**Answer: b) Fencing off protected places** — fencing protected places is a named method.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which measure is on the guide's list but stated separately from the book's?**
a) Treat and recycle industrial sewage and waste  b) Use sunlight  c) Plant cover  d) Fence protected places
**Answer: a) Treat and recycle industrial sewage and waste** — the guide adds treating and recycling industrial sewage and waste, and crop rotation.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Which item is on the guide's bill for unwise use?**
a) More jobs  b) Extinction of species  c) Higher revenue  d) Better plant cover
**Answer: b) Extinction of species** — species extinction is one bill item.
