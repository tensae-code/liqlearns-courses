# Hands-on — Natural Resources — Using Resources Well

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Condition hunt
Pick an unused or under-used resource near home. Interview one elder or worker: what stops its use? Sort every answer they give into the book's six conditions.

## Fence and plant
With family or classmates, do one small care method from the lesson for real: fence a young tree from animals, plant one tree, or clear a spring's edge. Write what you did and which method it was.
