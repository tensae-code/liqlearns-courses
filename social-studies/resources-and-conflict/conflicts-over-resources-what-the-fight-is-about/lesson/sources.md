Searchable tags: resource conflicts, ownership, access, extractive resources, distribution, social studies

Samoa's own way (Year 10, Book 3): Access to resources is split into economic and social factors with Pacific examples, and development is taught as change that can bring conflict in places — ending in consensus-building and appropriate technology for small island states.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado6 Postprimaria): the rural Postprimaria Grade 6 guide is built entirely around indigenous peoples — where they live, what they do in their territories, their ancestral struggles for land, and their population and resource use — geography taught as peoples-and-territory.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado9 Postprimaria): the rural Postprimaria Grade 9 guide is organised as questions about campesino life — globalisation, why a resource-rich country has poverty, how Afro-descendant, indigenous and campesino communities face environmental change, and how rural communities administer and defend their territory.
