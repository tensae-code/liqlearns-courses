**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Ethiopia, Samoa, Colombia, Malaysia, Slovenia, Syria, Mexico).**

Mexico's own way (Grade 7 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution.

No book error in the placements for this lesson is taught here. Categories and contests are taught exactly as the placements report them.

Searchable tags: resource conflicts, ownership, access, extractive resources, distribution, social studies, Mexico

Samoa's own way (Year 10, Book 3): Access to resources is split into economic and social factors with Pacific examples, and development is taught as change that can bring conflict in places — ending in consensus-building and appropriate technology for small island states.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado6 Postprimaria): the rural Postprimaria Grade 6 guide is built entirely around indigenous peoples — where they live, what they do in their territories, their ancestral struggles for land, and their population and resource use — geography taught as peoples-and-territory.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado9 Postprimaria): the rural Postprimaria Grade 9 guide is organised as questions about campesino life — globalisation, why a resource-rich country has poverty, how Afro-descendant, indigenous and campesino communities face environmental change, and how rural communities administer and defend their territory.

Malaysia's own way (MY T3 Geografi Tingkatan 3): Form 3 turns from skills (tables, graphs, pie charts) to living Malaysia — its natural vegetation and wildlife and the human pressure on both — then outward to the world's biomes, resources and economic cooperation, closing with forest management and recycling.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 8 Geografija 8): Grade 8 is pure world regional geography, continent by continent (Africa, Australia/Oceania, the Americas and polar regions), each region worked through the same grid — position/relief/waters, climate/vegetation, population, economy — and the book closes with two world-problems units (rainforest loss, desert spread, water conflicts, the Aral Sea).

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 12 Geography G12-Literary): the final-year Literary-track book is a three-scale spiral — the Arab homeland as one integrated natural unit, then Syria itself at the same two levels (nature/resources, then human/economic life), then the world split into developing and developed with Japan, India and Senegal as the three case studies; every unit works from maps, statistical tables and graphs, and the water pair of lessons (scarcity, then "a looming crisis and future conflict") is the book's signature treatment.
