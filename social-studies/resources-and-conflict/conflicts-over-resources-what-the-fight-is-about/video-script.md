# Video script — Conflicts over resources — what the fight is about

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Resource conflicts are fights over ownership, access, decision-making and the sharing of benefits — and they turn destructive only when the ways of managing them break down. First, categories — what is at stake: Resource conflicts are categorised by the resource at stake. Then, the four contests — and when conflict turns bad: Underneath the categories, four contests drive resource conflicts, and they often overlap: over ownership — whose resource is it; over access — who may use it; over decision-making in management — who decides; and over distribution of revenues, benefits and burdens — who gains, who pays. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
