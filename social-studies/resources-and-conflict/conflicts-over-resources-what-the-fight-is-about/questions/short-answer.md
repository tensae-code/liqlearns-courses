# Conflicts over resources — what the fight is about — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four contests that drive resource conflicts.
**Answer:** Over ownership, over access, over decision-making in management, and over distribution of revenues, benefits and burdens.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the three resource categories.
**Answer:** Extractive resources, land, and water.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When does conflict turn destructive?
**Answer:** When the mechanisms for managing it break down.
