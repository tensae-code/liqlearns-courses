# Conflicts over resources — what the fight is about — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Extractive resources include…
a) hydrocarbons, minerals, gemstones, timber  b) rainfall  c) sunlight
**Answer: a) hydrocarbons, minerals, gemstones, timber**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The other two great resource stakes are…
a) land and water  b) roads and schools  c) air and fire
**Answer: a) land and water**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A conflict's dynamics are shaped by…
a) the resource's own nature  b) the weather only  c) nothing
**Answer: a) the resource's own nature**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the four contests?
a) over ownership  b) over access  c) over the weather
**Answer: c) over the weather**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The distribution contest is over…
a) revenues, benefits and burdens  b) place names  c) map colours
**Answer: a) revenues, benefits and burdens**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Conflict becomes destructive when…
a) it is discussed  b) the mechanisms for managing it break down  c) elders meet
**Answer: b) the mechanisms for managing it break down**
