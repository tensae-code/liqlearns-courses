# Hands-on — People and environment — a two-way street

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Object autopsy
Choose one everyday object and write its resource family tree to the environment — every material, back to soil, water, forest or rock.

## Two arrows
Draw a village and its land with arrows both ways: what the village takes, what the village does. Caption each arrow from your own area’s truth.
