# People and environment — a two-way street — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Humans depend on the environment for —
a) food only  b) nothing essential  c) all materials
**Answer: c) all materials**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The worked example traces —
a) soil types  b) the resources inside everyday objects  c) river lengths
**Answer: b) the resources inside everyday objects**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The relationship is —
a) two-way  b) one-way  c) imaginary
**Answer: a) two-way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Humans reshape the environment by —
a) only farming  b) wishing  c) farming, building and extracting
**Answer: c) farming, building and extracting**
