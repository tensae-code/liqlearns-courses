# People and environment — a two-way street — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the dependence claim.
**Answer: Humans depend on the environment for all materials.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do single-side accounts fail?
**Answer: People and environment continually act on each other; isolating either hides the relationship itself.**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two directions make the relationship two-way?
**Answer:** Dependence — humans draw on the environment — and modification — humans reshape it in return.
