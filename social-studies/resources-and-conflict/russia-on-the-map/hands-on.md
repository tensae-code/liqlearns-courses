# Hands-on — Russia on the Map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two sheets, one country
Draw one large country across two joined sheets of paper and mark where the join falls, as Russia's tactile map is split across two sheets.

## Make the keys
Write the keys your two-sheet map would need — the list of what the hand can find — as the three Braille keys serve Russia's map.
