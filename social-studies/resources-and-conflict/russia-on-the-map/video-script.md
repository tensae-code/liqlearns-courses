# Video script — Russia on the Map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sweden's SPSM pack teaches the Ukraine–Russia conflict of 2022 entirely by touch, for blind and visually impaired pupils. Russia is so large on the map that its tactile map takes two sheets.

The two sheets carry three Braille keys between them, naming what the hand finds as it reads the raised lines. The keys are part of the map — without them the raised lines cannot be read.
