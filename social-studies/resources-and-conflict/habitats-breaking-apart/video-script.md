# Video script — Habitats breaking apart

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A habitat is the physical conditions that let a species survive and reproduce.

Landscape change works five ways: perforation (holes punched in), dissection (cuts through), fragmentation (breaking into pieces), shrinkage (pieces getting smaller) and attrition (pieces vanishing).

Species can linger in ruined habitat without breeding, so the losses arrive late — an extinction debt owed to the future.
