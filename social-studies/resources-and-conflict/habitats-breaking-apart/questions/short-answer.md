# Habitats breaking apart — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define habitat.
**Answer:** The physical conditions letting a species survive and reproduce.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the five ways of breaking.
**Answer:** Perforation, dissection, fragmentation, shrinkage, attrition.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain extinction debt in one sentence.
**Answer:** Species linger without breeding in ruined habitat, so their loss arrives late.
