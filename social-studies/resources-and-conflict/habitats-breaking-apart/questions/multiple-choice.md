# Habitats breaking apart — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Habitat is the physical conditions letting a species —
a) survive and reproduce  b) migrate  c) hibernate
**Answer: a) survive and reproduce**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The chief modern destroyer of habitats is —
a) human landscape change  b) volcanoes  c) floods
**Answer: a) human landscape change**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cutting a road through a forest is —
a) dissection  b) attrition  c) perforation
**Answer: a) dissection**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Extinction debt means —
a) losses arriving late, after habitat is ruined  b) a fine for hunting  c) species borrowing range
**Answer: a) losses arriving late, after habitat is ruined**
