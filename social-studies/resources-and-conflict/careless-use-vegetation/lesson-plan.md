# Lesson plan — Careless Use — Vegetation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Resources and Conflict · **Lesson:** Careless Use — Vegetation
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Resources and Conflict (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md: natural resources, their care, and conflict over resources).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** vegetation is forests, grasses, swamp plants, desert plants and highland plants — and of these, people have hit forests and grasses hardest.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Water Pollution and Shortage, the first careless-use lesson. It is one whole lesson — a short, single-skill lesson.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Careless Use — Water Pollution and Shortage — the first careless-use trouble. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List the five kinds of vegetation in the book.
2. Name the two kinds people have hit hardest: forests and grasses.
3. Explain, from the earlier lessons, why hitting forests and grasses hardest sends harm into soil and water too.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Lists only trees → vegetation is five kinds wide: forests, grasses, swamp, desert and highland plants.
- Cannot say why forests matter beyond wood → recall the guide's forest list from the conservation lesson: soil, erosion, wildlife, climate, oxygen, water recycling.
- Forgets the second hardest hit → grasses, beside forests.
