# Video script — Careless Use — Vegetation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Plants wear five forms.
Forests. Grasses. Swamp plants. Desert plants. Highland plants.
Five kinds — one vegetation.
Which have people hit hardest?
Forests — and grasses.
Cut and grazed without care, their harm travels:
into soil, into water, into wildlife.
Name the five. Guard the two.
