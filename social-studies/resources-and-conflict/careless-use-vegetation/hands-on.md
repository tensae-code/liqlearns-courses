# Hands-on — Careless Use — Vegetation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five-kind walk
Walk a route with an adult and tick off every vegetation kind you pass — forest, grass, swamp, desert, highland. Photograph in words: write what you saw where.

## Hardest-hit evidence
Find one place where forest or grass has been used without care. Write three pieces of evidence and one thing that would let it recover.
