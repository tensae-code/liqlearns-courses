# Careless Use — Vegetation — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. Desert plants are part of vegetation in the book's list.**
**Answer: True** — desert plants are the fourth kind.

**2. People have hit swamp plants hardest of all.**
**Answer: False** — forests and grasses are the hardest hit.

**3. Highland plants are one of the five kinds.**
**Answer: True** — highland plants close the list.
