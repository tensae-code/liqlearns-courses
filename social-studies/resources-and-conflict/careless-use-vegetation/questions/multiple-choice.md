# Careless Use — Vegetation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. Which list is the book's vegetation, complete?**
a) Forests and grasses only  b) Forests, grasses, swamp plants, desert plants, highland plants  c) Trees, crops, flowers, weeds, reeds  d) Forests, rivers, grasses, soil, air
**Answer: b) Forests, grasses, swamp plants, desert plants, highland plants** — those five are the book's kinds.

**2. The two kinds people have hit hardest are —**
a) Swamp and desert plants  b) Forests and grasses  c) Highland plants and swamp plants  d) Desert plants and grasses
**Answer: b) Forests and grasses** — forests and grasses are named hardest hit.

**3. The harms of using vegetation without care are described as —**
a) None  b) Few  c) Many  d) Unknown
**Answer: c) Many** — the book says the harms are many.
