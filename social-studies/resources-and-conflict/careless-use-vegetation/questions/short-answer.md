# Careless Use — Vegetation — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. List the five kinds of vegetation.**
**Answer:** Forests, grasses, swamp plants, desert plants and highland plants.

**2. Which two kinds have people hit hardest, and why does the harm travel?**
**Answer:** Forests and grasses. The harm travels because forests hold soil, shelter wildlife, control climate and recycle water, and grasses cover ground and feed herds — harming them harms soil, water and wildlife too.

**3. What research step does the chapter set beside this lesson?**
**Answer:** To research what treating water means and how it is done.
