# Five principles of good resource governance — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lack of participation and transparency is one of the four failures.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Equitable access should be tied to livelihoods and poverty reduction.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Once an assessment is published, governance work is finished.
**Answer: False**
