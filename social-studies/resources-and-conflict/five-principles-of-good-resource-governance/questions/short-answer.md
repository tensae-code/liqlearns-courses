# Five principles of good resource governance — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four governance failures.
**Answer:** Unclear/overlapping/poorly enforced rights and laws; discriminatory policies; unequal distribution of benefits and burdens; lack of public participation and transparency.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State principles 1 and 3.
**Answer:** 1: Clarify rights, responsibilities, laws and mandates in law. 3: Require environmental and social impact assessments for all major projects, transparently done and published.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does principle 4 expand?
**Answer:** Public participation and collaborative, community-based management.
