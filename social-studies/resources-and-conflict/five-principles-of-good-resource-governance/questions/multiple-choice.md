# Five principles of good resource governance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is one of the four failures?
a) unclear, overlapping or poorly enforced rights  b) too many maps  c) low rainfall
**Answer: a) unclear, overlapping or poorly enforced rights**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Discriminatory policies…
a) marginalise specific groups  b) help everyone equally  c) are a principle
**Answer: a) marginalise specific groups**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Unequal distribution means…
a) benefits and burdens shared fairly  b) some gain while others bear the burdens  c) no development
**Answer: b) some gain while others bear the burdens**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Principle 1 is to…
a) clarify rights, responsibilities, laws and mandates in law  b) skip assessments  c) decide in secret
**Answer: a) clarify rights, responsibilities, laws and mandates in law**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Impact assessments for major projects must be…
a) optional  b) transparent and published  c) verbal only
**Answer: b) transparent and published**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Principle 5 requires…
a) monitoring for unexpected impacts even afterwards  b) stopping all checks at signing  c) no follow-up
**Answer: a) monitoring for unexpected impacts even afterwards**
