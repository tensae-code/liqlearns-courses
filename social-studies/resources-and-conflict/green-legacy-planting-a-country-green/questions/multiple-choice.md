# Green Legacy — planting a country green — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Green Legacy is a campaign of —
a) river damming  b) city building  c) mass seedling planting
**Answer: c) mass seedling planting**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Seedlings are planted on —
a) sea coasts  b) degraded hillsides, towns and farms  c) only school yards
**Answer: b) degraded hillsides, towns and farms**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An aim of the planting is —
a) resilience against climate shocks  b) ending rainfall  c) replacing all farms
**Answer: a) resilience against climate shocks**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Trees serve food security by —
a) blocking roads  b) shading markets only  c) feeding and protecting farms
**Answer: c) feeding and protecting farms**
