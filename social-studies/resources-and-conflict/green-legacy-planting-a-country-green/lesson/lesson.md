# Green Legacy — planting a country green

## Do it first

Millions of hands, millions of seedlings, one rainy season at a time. Read the planting movement itself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The campaign

The Green Legacy Initiative is a national campaign of mass seedling planting: seedlings raised in nurseries, carried to degraded hillsides, towns and farms, and planted by communities across the country. Pupils take part — planting is presented as every citizen’s work, including the learner reading the book.

## Sub lesson 2 — The aims

The aims are resilience against climate shocks, food security (trees that feed and protect farms), and reversing degradation — bringing back soil cover, water holding and forest. A seedling is small; the aim is the size of the country.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion how the Green Legacy plants, and what the planting is for.
