# Hands-on — Green Legacy — planting a country green

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plant one properly
Plant a real seedling if one is available — hole, roots spread, soil firm, watered — or walk through the steps on a potted plant. Each step done badly is a dead tree; do it as the campaign teaches.

## Aim mapping
Take one planted site you know. For each of the three aims, write one sentence on how that site serves it, or honestly say it does not yet.
