# Holding the soil — conservation toolkit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Afforestation is —
a) replanting cut forest  b) closing land  c) planting trees where forest was not
**Answer: c) planting trees where forest was not**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Area closure works by —
a) building walls  b) excluding grazing and cutting so vegetation returns  c) planting seedlings
**Answer: b) excluding grazing and cutting so vegetation returns**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Terracing —
a) cuts the hillside into soil-holding steps  b) ploughs downhill  c) clears slopes
**Answer: a) cuts the hillside into soil-holding steps**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Contour ploughing works —
a) straight downhill  b) only on flat land  c) along level lines, trapping rain
**Answer: c) along level lines, trapping rain**
