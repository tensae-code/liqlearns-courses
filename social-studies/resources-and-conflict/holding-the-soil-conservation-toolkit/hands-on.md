# Hands-on — Holding the soil — conservation toolkit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tool matching
Match each tool to a slope you know: which tool first, and why? Present the match with the slope’s problem named from the degradation lessons.

## Contour lines
On a slope, lay a rope along a true contour using a simple A-frame level or water hose. Mark how a furrow on that line would hold water a downhill furrow would spill.
