# Holding the soil — conservation toolkit

## Do it first

Six tools, one job: keep the soil where it belongs. Most cost labour, not money. Read the toolkit.

## Sub lesson 1 — Biological measures

The biological tools use living cover: afforestation — planting trees where forest was not; reforestation — replanting where it was cut; and area closure — fencing degraded land off from grazing and cutting so vegetation returns by itself. Living roots are the cheapest soil engineers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Physical measures

The physical tools reshape the slope: terracing cuts the hillside into steps that hold soil and water; contour ploughing works along the level lines instead of downhill, so furrows trap rather than channel the rain. Together the two families are presented as the cheapest-effective toolkit to discuss and apply.
