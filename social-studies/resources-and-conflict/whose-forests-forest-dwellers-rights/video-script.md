The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

India Grade 5 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Looking Around (EVS) (printed p. 182), teaches: the forests belong in law and life to the people who live in them — the Kuduk community of Jharkhand and their struggle to keep their forest home and way of life.

India's own way: the values teaching is carried by real people's lives — hereditary cleaning work challenged by Gandhi and Ambedkar, a dam-displaced family in a Mumbai slum, sugarcane migrants whose children miss school — the child meets the republic's promises through its hardest cases.
