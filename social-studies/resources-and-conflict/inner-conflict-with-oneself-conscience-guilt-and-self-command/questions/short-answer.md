# Inner Conflict with Oneself Conscience Guilt and Self Command — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: When difficulties with oneself (Калі цяжкасці з самім сабой) — Grade 9 — a person can be in conflict with himself — the intrapersonal conflict, a clash inside one's own "I" where conscience, guilt and fear work as inner regulators of behaviour, against the inter-role (micro-role) conflict, where the different roles a person must play at the same time (pupil–pupil, pupil–parents, pupil–school administration) pull against each other; an inner conflict is overcome not by suppressing one side but by a strong-willed effort of choice and by rethinking the situation, on the basis of self-respect — "to be a human being or not to be depends only on yourselves"; the practicum is a mini-course of self-support (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the civics track is the pupil's own state and law read from inside — "our state" is taught straight from the 1994 Constitution of the Republic of Belarus (unitary, democratic, social, law-governed; the people as the only source of power; the 1996 referendum; the 1999 Union treaty with Russia), rights are taught article by article from that Constitution (Arts. 1–3, 21–25, 30, 41–49, 53, 59–62) beside the Universal Declaration, and the course closes not with a test but with a discussion, "I — a citizen of the Republic of Belarus", on the citizen's own responsibility before the state. (any close wording).**
