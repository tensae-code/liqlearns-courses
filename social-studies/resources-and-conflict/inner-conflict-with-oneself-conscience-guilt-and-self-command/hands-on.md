# Hands-on — Inner Conflict with Oneself Conscience Guilt and Self Command

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A person can be in conflict with himself — the intrapersonal conflict, a clash inside one's own "I" where conscience, guilt and fear work as inner regulators of behaviour, against the inter-role (micro-role) conflict, where the different roles a person must play at the same time (pupil–pupil, pupil–parents, pupil–school administration) pull against each other; an inner conflict is overcome not by suppressing one side but by a strong-willed effort of choice and by rethinking the situation, on the basis of self-respect — "to be a human being or not to be depends only on yourselves"; the practicum is a mini-course of self-support. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
