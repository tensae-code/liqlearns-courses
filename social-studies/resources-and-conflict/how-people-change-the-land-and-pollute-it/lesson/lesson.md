# How people change the land — and pollute it

## Do it first

A hillside in two photographs: forest, then cleared and gullied, twenty years apart. The agent between the photographs is us. Read the modifications — and the pollution stream.

## Sub lesson 1 — Modification

Humans modify the environment through farming (clearing, ploughing, terracing), settlement (building, roads), industry (works, mines, energy) and resource extraction (cutting, digging, damming). Modification is not always damage — terraces modify to conserve — but every modification has effects the modifier must own.

## Sub lesson 2 — The waste stream

Modification leaves a waste stream: refuse, smoke, effluent — and, named plainly in the book, open defecation and poor sanitation, whose health and environmental effects fall on water, soil and neighbours. Pollution is modification that poisons its own maker’s environment.
