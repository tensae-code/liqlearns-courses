# Lesson plan — How people change the land — and pollute it

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Resources and Conflict · **Lesson:** How people change the land — and pollute it
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air climb of the Social Studies course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** humans modify the environment through farming, settlement, industry and resource extraction — and through the waste and pollution stream, including open defecation and poor sanitation.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on People and environment — a two-way street.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Modification** — four agents of change
2. **The waste stream** — pollution and sanitation

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
