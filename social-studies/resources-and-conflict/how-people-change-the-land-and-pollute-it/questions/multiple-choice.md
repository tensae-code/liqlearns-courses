# How people change the land — and pollute it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An agent of modification is —
a) rainfall  b) latitude  c) resource extraction
**Answer: c) resource extraction**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Terraces modify the land to —
a) abandon it  b) conserve it  c) pollute it
**Answer: b) conserve it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The waste stream includes —
a) refuse, smoke and effluent  b) only litter  c) only smoke
**Answer: a) refuse, smoke and effluent**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Poor sanitation’s effects fall on —
a) only the household  b) nothing  c) water, soil and neighbours’ health
**Answer: c) water, soil and neighbours’ health**
