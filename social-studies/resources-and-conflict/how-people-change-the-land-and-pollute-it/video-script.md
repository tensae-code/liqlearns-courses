# Video script — How people change the land — and pollute it

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

People change the land four ways: farm it, settle it, work it, extract from it.
Not all change is damage — terraces conserve.
But every change leaves a stream behind it.
Waste and poor sanitation poison the maker’s own water.
