# Hands-on — How people change the land — and pollute it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Before and after
Describe one real place in two states — ten years apart or as it is versus as it was told to you. Assign each change to its agent from the lesson.

## Sanitation map
Map your area’s waste stream honestly: where refuse goes, where water is drawn, where sanitation fails. Mark the point where the stream meets the drinking water — if it does.
