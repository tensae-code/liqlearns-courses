# Stop them early — the four phases of invasion — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four phases in order.
**Answer:** Introduction, naturalisation, colonisation, dispersal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When can an invader be eradicated?
**Answer:** Only while its populations are small and localised.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the lesson's moral in one sentence.
**Answer:** Stop them early: every later phase costs more and achieves less.
