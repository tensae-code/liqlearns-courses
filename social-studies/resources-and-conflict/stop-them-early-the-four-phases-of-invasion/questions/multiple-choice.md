# Stop them early — the four phases of invasion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first phase of invasion is —
a) introduction  b) dispersal  c) colonisation
**Answer: a) introduction**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Self-sustaining populations spreading describes —
a) naturalisation  b) introduction  c) prevention
**Answer: a) naturalisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Eradication is possible only while populations are —
a) small and localised  b) widespread  c) absent
**Answer: a) small and localised**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** For a widespread invader, the remaining option is —
a) suppression at great cost  b) prevention  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) suppression at great cost**
