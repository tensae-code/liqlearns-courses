# Video script — Conservation — the Konso Terraces Case Study

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Far south-west of Addis Ababa lies Konso.
Stone land. Dry land. Steep land.
A hardworking people farms it — behind stone terraces.
Step by step, the terraces hold the soil. The rain slows. Crops grow.
In 2011 the world wrote it down: Konso Cultural Landscape.
Three mora grounds gather the people.
Waka stones remember great deeds.
Olaahita poles pass power to the next generation.
Stone holds the soil. Custom holds the builders.
