# Conservation — the Konso Terraces Case Study — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Konso lies —**
a) 595 km south-west of Addis Ababa  b) Just outside Addis Ababa  c) On the coast  d) 595 km north of Addis Ababa
**Answer: a) 595 km south-west of Addis Ababa** — 595 km south-west is the study's placing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Konso's terraces keep —**
a) Rain from falling  b) Soil from washing away  c) Animals from grazing anywhere  d) Stones from rolling into rivers only
**Answer: b) Soil from washing away** — keeping soil from washing away is their work.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) The Konso Cultural Landscape was registered by UNESCO in —**
a) 2001  b) 2011  c) 1991  d) 2021
**Answer: b) 2011** — 2011 is the printed year.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The waka are —**
a) Generation poles  b) Tall stones raised for those who did great deeds  c) Gathering grounds  d) Terrace walls
**Answer: b) Tall stones raised for those who did great deeds** — waka honour great deeds; olaahita are the generation poles.
