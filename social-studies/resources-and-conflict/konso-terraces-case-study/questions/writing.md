# Conservation — the Konso Terraces Case Study — writing

Write as the prompt asks.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Write a short retelling of the Konso study for a younger pupil, in your own words.**
**A good answer includes:** Names Konso's place (595 km south-west of Addis Ababa), the hard land, the stone terraces and what they hold, and the 2011 UNESCO registration — summary only, in the writer's own words.
