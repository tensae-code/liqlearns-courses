# Conservation — the Konso Terraces Case Study — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How do stone terraces let a steep, stony slope be farmed?**
**Answer:** They turn the slope into stone steps: rain slows on each step instead of racing down, and soil stays where crops can use it instead of washing away.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name the three mora grounds and a use of each.**
**Answer:** Play and the traditional drink after work; worship and reconciliation oaths; the great yearly festival.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What recognition did Konso receive, and when?**
**Answer:** The Konso Cultural Landscape was registered by UNESCO in 2011.
