# Conservation — the Konso Terraces Case Study — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Most Konso people speak Afaan Konso.**
**Answer: True** — the study says most speak Afaan Konso, among nine peoples.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Konso's land is flat, wet and easy to farm.**
**Answer: False** — it is stony, dry, mountainous and rugged.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) One mora ground is for worship and reconciliation oaths.**
**Answer: True** — the second mora's use, as printed.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Olaahita stones honour great deeds.**
**Answer: False** — olaahita are generation poles; waka stones honour great deeds.
