# Hands-on — Conservation — the Konso Terraces Case Study

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Step a slope
On a small slope or a soil tray, build a miniature terrace from stones: one step, packed and level. Pour water above it and watch where the soil goes — then pour on an unterraced side and compare.

## Great-deed stones
As a class, agree on the waka idea in words: name one great deed for the community from the past year that deserves remembering, and write how your community does remember such deeds.
