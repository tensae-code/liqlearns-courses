# Hands-on — Peace and Order Programs of Government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the government's peace programmes and peace agencies (the Armed Forces of the Philippines and police) that keep order and safety so communities can live and earn in peace. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Philippines's way
Say how Philippines's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
