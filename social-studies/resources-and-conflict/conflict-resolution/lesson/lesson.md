## Sub lesson 2 — Albania's way — the book's own teaching way

Albania's own way: Citizenship in Grade 5 is taught as a ladder — family, group, community, then the state: democracy is first practised inside the family and in class elections, local self-government is compared with the class's own elections, and a whole chapter (Kreu 3, The Road) treats the child as pedestrian, cyclist and passenger with duties on the road. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
