# Hands-on — Why clean water runs short

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Source-to-cup audit
Trace your drinking water from source to cup. At each step mark which cause family threatens it, and which response protects it.

## Queue interview
Interview someone who queues for water: how long, how far, what fails most — source, cleanliness or delivery? Report with the lesson’s three families as your frame.
