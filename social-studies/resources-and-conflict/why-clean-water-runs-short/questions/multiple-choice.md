# Why clean water runs short — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cause family is —
a) rainfall increase  b) over-supply  c) catchment degradation
**Answer: c) catchment degradation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Distribution failure means —
a) too many pipes  b) clean water exists but does not reach people  c) no water exists anywhere
**Answer: b) clean water exists but does not reach people**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A response to degradation is —
a) protect and restore catchments  b) pollute less carefully  c) move the town
**Answer: a) protect and restore catchments**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Protected springs are a —
a) pollution cause  b) degradation cause  c) distribution response
**Answer: c) distribution response**
