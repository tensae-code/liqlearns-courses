# Why clean water runs short

## Do it first

A country of great rivers, with queues at the tap. The shortage is not of water but of clean water, delivered. Read the causes.

## Sub lesson 1 — The causes

Clean water runs short for three cause families: degradation — catchments stripped, springs drying, sources shrinking; pollution — what remains is dirtied (the pollution lesson’s stream); and distribution — clean sources exist but pipes, pumps and points do not reach the people. Town and country suffer the shortage differently, but both suffer it.

[Picture 21113 — a queue at a protected spring versus a drying unprotected source]

## Sub lesson 2 — The responses

The management responses answer cause by cause: protect and restore catchments; guard sources from pollution; and build and maintain distribution — protected springs, points and pipes that put clean water within everyone’s walk.
