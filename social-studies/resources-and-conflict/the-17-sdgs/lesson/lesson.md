# The 17 SDGs

## Do it first

Write down three changes you would wish for the whole world by 2030. After the lesson, find each wish in the 17 goals — most wishes are there.

## Sub lesson 1 — The goals, whole

The 2030 Agenda, adopted by the United Nations in 2015, sets 17 Sustainable Development Goals and 169 targets: 1 No Poverty; 2 Zero Hunger; 3 Good Health and Well-Being; 4 Quality Education; 5 Gender Equality; 6 Clean Water and Sanitation; 7 Affordable and Clean Energy; 8 Decent Work and Economic Growth; 9 Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure; 10 Reduced Inequalities.

The list completes: 11 Sustainable Cities and Communities; 12 Responsible Consumption and Production; 13 Climate Action; 14 Life below Water; 15 Life on Land; 16 Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions; 17 Partnerships for the Goals. The goals are integrated — none can be reached by breaking another.

## Sub lesson 2 — What they are for — and Ethiopia's adoption

The goals are designed to end poverty, hunger, HIV/AIDS and discrimination against women and girls — and to prioritise those furthest behind first.

Ethiopia has integrated the SDGs into its national development frameworks, treating the global agenda as the country's own poverty-eradication path. A world list, deliberately made local: that is the adoption this lesson records.
