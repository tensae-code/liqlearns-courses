# The 17 SDGs — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name goals 1 to 5 in order.
**Answer:** 1 No Poverty; 2 Zero Hunger; 3 Good Health and Well-Being; 4 Quality Education; 5 Gender Equality.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name goals 14 to 17 in order.
**Answer:** 14 Life below Water; 15 Life on Land; 16 Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions; 17 Partnerships for the Goals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How has Ethiopia adopted the SDGs?
**Answer:** By integrating them into its national development frameworks as its own poverty-eradication path.
