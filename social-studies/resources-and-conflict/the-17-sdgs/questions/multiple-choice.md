# The 17 SDGs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 2030 Agenda was adopted in…
a) 2000  b) 2015  c) 2023
**Answer: b) 2015**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** There are how many SDGs and targets?
a) 8 goals, 21 targets  b) 17 goals, 169 targets  c) 17 goals, 17 targets
**Answer: b) 17 goals, 169 targets**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Goal 1 is…
a) Zero Hunger  b) No Poverty  c) Climate Action
**Answer: b) No Poverty**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Goal 13 is…
a) Life on Land  b) Climate Action  c) Clean Water
**Answer: b) Climate Action**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The goals prioritise…
a) the richest first  b) those furthest behind  c) cities only
**Answer: b) those furthest behind**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia has…
a) rejected the SDGs  b) integrated them into its national development frameworks  c) adopted only Goal 4
**Answer: b) integrated them into its national development frameworks**
