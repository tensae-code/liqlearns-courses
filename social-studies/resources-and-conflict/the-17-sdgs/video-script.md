# Video script — The 17 SDGs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The 2030 Agenda sets 17 integrated goals and 169 targets — from No Poverty to Partnerships — prioritising those furthest behind, and Ethiopia has made them its own. First, the goals, whole: The 2030 Agenda, adopted by the United Nations in 2015, sets 17 Sustainable Development Goals and 169 targets: 1 No Poverty; 2 Zero Hunger; 3 Good Health and Well-Being; 4 Quality Education; 5 Gender Equality; 6 Clean Water and Sanitation; 7 Affordable and Clean Energy; 8 Decent Work and Economic Growth; 9 Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure; 10 Reduced Inequalities. Then, what they are for — and ethiopia's adoption: The goals are designed to end poverty, hunger, HIV/AIDS and discrimination against women and girls — and to prioritise those furthest behind first. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
