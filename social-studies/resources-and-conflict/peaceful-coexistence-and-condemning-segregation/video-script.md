# Video script — Peaceful Coexistence and Condemning Segregation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Tanzania Standard Five tz-civic5-en (printed p. 154), teaches: Peaceful coexistence is living together peacefully regardless of differences in religion, political ideology, sex or socio-economic status; Tanzania's peace comes from solidarity, mutual respect and brotherly love; segregation by race, religion, sex or tribe is immoral and must be strongly condemned — human beings are equal by God's design — and respecting cultural differences, right to life and human dignity keeps lasting peace.

Tanzania's own way (Standard Five, Civic and Moral Education Standard Five Pupil's Book): Standard Five civics teaches the state from the ward up — Ward Development Committee, district council and its heads of departments, regional commissioner — and the nation through its symbols in detail (the emblem's thirteen signs, the shilling, the Uhuru torch first lit 9 December 1961, national festivals from Zanzibar Revolution Day to Farmers' Day); democracy is taught with political parties' duties and political tolerance, and peace as peaceful coexistence that condemns segregation on any ground.
