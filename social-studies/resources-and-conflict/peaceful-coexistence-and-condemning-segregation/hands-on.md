# Hands-on — Peaceful Coexistence and Condemning Segregation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Peaceful Coexistence and Condemning Segregation
Work through this part with your hands: peaceful coexistence is living together peacefully regardless of differences in religion, political ideology, sex or socio-economic status; Tanzania's peace comes from solidarity, mutual respect and brotherly love; segregation by race, religion, sex or tribe is immoral and must be strongly condemned — human beings are equal by God's design — and respecting cultural differences, right to life and human dignity keeps lasting peace. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
