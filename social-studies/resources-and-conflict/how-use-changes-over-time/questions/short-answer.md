# Natural Resources — How Use Changes Over Time — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Name the four growers that multiply non-basic wants.**
**Answer:** Population growth, the deepening of civilisation, technology, and city growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What could happen if giant pressure on resources continues?**
**Answer:** Their giving could be interrupted — resources could stop yielding as before.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Name three consequences of high and changing consumption from the guide.**
**Answer:** Any three of: soil degradation, water shortages, biodiversity loss, damage to ecosystem functions, global warming.
