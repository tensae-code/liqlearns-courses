# Natural Resources — How Use Changes Over Time — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Today's basic needs, compared with the ancients', are —**
a) Completely different  b) Similar — it is non-basic wants that multiply  c) Smaller  d) Only about technology
**Answer: b) Similar — it is non-basic wants that multiply** — basics are similar; non-basic wants multiply.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Which is NOT one of the four growers of demand?**
a) Population growth  b) Deepening of civilisation  c) Technology  d) Rainfall increase
**Answer: d) Rainfall increase** — the growers are population, civilisation, technology and city growth.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The book describes present use as —**
a) Careful and sufficient  b) Hugely wasteful, putting giant pressure on resources  c) Too small to matter  d) Finished
**Answer: b) Hugely wasteful, putting giant pressure on resources** — hugely wasteful is the book's verdict.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Which is one of the guide's five pressure factors?**
a) Overconsumption  b) Terracing  c) Crop rotation  d) Fencing
**Answer: a) Overconsumption** — overconsumption is a pressure; the others are care methods.
