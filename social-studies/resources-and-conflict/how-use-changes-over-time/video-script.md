# Video script — Natural Resources — How Use Changes Over Time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Long ago, needs were few. Nature met them.
Today the basics are the same — but look at the list now.
More people. Deeper civilisation. New technology. Growing cities.
Wants multiply. Demand grows, widens, changes.
And present use? The book says: hugely wasteful.
Giant pressure — until the giving could stop.
Even empty places are precious: air, oceans, deserts, ice.
Hand them carefully to those who come after.
Care in use is now decisive.
