# Hands-on — Natural Resources — How Use Changes Over Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-generation interview
Interview an elder: what did a family need when they were young? What was never wasted? Write their list beside your family's list for this week and count both.

## Waste audit
For one day, note everything thrown away at home that came from a resource — food, water, wood, paper, fuel. Weigh or count it. That pile is 'hugely wasteful' made visible.
