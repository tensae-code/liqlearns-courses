One basin cannot be managed country by country: upstream acts land downstream.

The Nile Basin Initiative grew from TECCONILE in 1992 to the NBI of 1999, with Ethiopia a full member, aiming at equitable use and a shared legal framework.

The US–Canada International Joint Commission of 1909 is held up as the model: it has resolved every case referred to it.
