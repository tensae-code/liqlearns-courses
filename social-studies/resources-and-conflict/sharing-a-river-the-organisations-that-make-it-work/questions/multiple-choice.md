# Sharing a river — the organisations that make it work — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Nile Basin Initiative dates from —
a) 1999  b) 1909  c) 1992
**Answer: a) 1999**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** TECCONILE, the NBI's forerunner, dates from —
a) 1992  b) 1999  c) 2009
**Answer: a) 1992**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's place in the NBI is —
a) a full member  b) an observer  c) not a member
**Answer: a) a full member**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The model commission is between —
a) the US and Canada  b) Ethiopia and Egypt  c) Zambia and Zimbabwe
**Answer: a) the US and Canada**
