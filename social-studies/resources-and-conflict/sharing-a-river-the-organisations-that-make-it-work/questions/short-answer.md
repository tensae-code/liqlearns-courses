# Sharing a river — the organisations that make it work — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can't one country manage a basin alone?
**Answer:** Because upstream acts land downstream; the river is one system.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three river institutions from the lesson.
**Answer:** NBI, Zambezi River Authority, Kagera Basin Organization, Southern African Power Pool, SARCCUS, International Joint Commission (any three).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What record makes the 1909 commission a model?
**Answer:** It has resolved every case referred to it.
