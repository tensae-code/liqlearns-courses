# The world's great shared rivers — a table to map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The table covers how many major basins?
a) 15  b) 5  c) 50
**Answer: a) 15**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The table gives each basin's source, size, flow, destination, riparian count and —
a) length  b) colour  c) depth only
**Answer: a) length**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Okavango is a basin of —
a) Africa  b) Asia  c) South America
**Answer: a) Africa**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** GERD stands on which river system?
a) the Blue Nile  b) the Congo  c) the Mekong
**Answer: a) the Blue Nile**
