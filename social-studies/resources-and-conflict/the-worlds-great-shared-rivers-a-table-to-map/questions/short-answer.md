# The world's great shared rivers — a table to map — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four of the fifteen basins.
**Answer:** Nile, Congo, Zambezi, Okavango, Indus, Ganges, Mekong, La Plata (any four).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What six things does the table give per basin?
**Answer:** Source, basin size, flow volume, destination, number of riparian countries, length.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two photographed works on African rivers.
**Answer:** Kariba dam, GERD, Victoria Falls, Inga (any two).
