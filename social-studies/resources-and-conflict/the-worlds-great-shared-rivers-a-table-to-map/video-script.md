# Video script — The world's great shared rivers — a table to map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A data table of 15 major basins gives each river's source, basin size, flow volume, destination, number of riparian countries and length.

Among the fifteen: the Nile, Congo, Zambezi and Okavango in Africa; the Indus and Ganges in Asia; the Mekong in south-east Asia; and La Plata in South America..

Photographs show the economic life of African rivers: the Kariba and GERD dams, Victoria Falls, Inga, and Niger fishing.
