# Desertification in East Africa — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is cultivation in the 450–650 mm zone risky?
**Answer:** Its rain was never reliable enough for steady cropping, so cropping it accelerates degradation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three things does Kenya's programme pilot?
**Answer:** Research, training and demonstration.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What level do some national initiatives reach?
**Answer:** Presidential level.
