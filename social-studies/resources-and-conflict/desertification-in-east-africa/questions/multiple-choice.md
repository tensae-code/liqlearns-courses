# Desertification in East Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eastern Africa is described as a… for desertification
a) safe zone  b) frontline  c) bystander
**Answer: b) frontline**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The named country's drylands are…
a) Kenya's  b) Egypt's  c) Nigeria's
**Answer: a) Kenya's**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Risky cultivation happens in the rainfall zone of…
a) 450–650 mm  b) 1000–1500 mm  c) under 100 mm
**Answer: a) 450–650 mm**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Cultivation there accelerates…
a) the process of desertification  b) rainfall  c) forest growth
**Answer: a) the process of desertification**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Responses include initiatives at… level
a) village only  b) presidential  c) household only
**Answer: b) presidential**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Kenya's arid-lands programme has run since…
a) 1976  b) 2006  c) 1996
**Answer: a) 1976**
