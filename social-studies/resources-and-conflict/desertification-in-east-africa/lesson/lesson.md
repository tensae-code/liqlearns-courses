# Desertification in East Africa

## Do it first

East Africa's drylands are not a distant case study — they are home ground. The book maps the damage across the region.

## Sub lesson 1 — A frontline region

Eastern Africa is a frontline for desertification: the Sahelian belt and the semi-arid zones of the region show it already damaging livelihoods across the region, as the book's extent map shows. Kenya's rapid-population-growth drylands are named, where cultivation in the 450–650 mm rainfall zone — land whose rain was never reliable enough for steady cropping — accelerates the process.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fighting back

Against the damage stand national efforts to combat desertification, including presidential-level initiatives that put the fight at the top of government, and Kenya's arid-lands programme, running since 1976 as a pilot of research, training and demonstration — learning on the drylands themselves how to hold them, then teaching it. East Africa's answer, in the book's telling, is organised, researched and led.
