# Video script — Desertification in East Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eastern Africa is a frontline for desertification: the Sahelian belt and the semi-arid zones of the region show it already damaging livelihoods across the region, as the book's extent map shows. Kenya's rapid-population-growth drylands are named, where cultivation in the 450–650 mm rainfall zone — land whose rain was never reliable enough for steady cropping — accelerates the process. Against the damage stand national efforts to combat desertification, including presidential-level initiatives that put the fight at the top of government, and Kenya's arid-lands programme, running since 1976 as a pilot of research, training and demonstration — learning on the drylands themselves how to hold them, then teaching it. East Africa's answer, in the book's telling, is organised, researched and led.
