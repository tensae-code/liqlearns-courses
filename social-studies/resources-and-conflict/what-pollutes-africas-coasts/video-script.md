The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

What pollutes Africa's coasts? Count seven sources.
Sewage and factory waste — the most frequent of all.
Ships, cargo, oil, dumping, the worked seabed.
And the mangrove filters that clean the water are being destroyed.
