# What pollutes Africa's coasts — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the guide's seven sources of coastal pollution.
**Answer:** Sewage/industrial discharge from coastal channels; dumping wastes at sea; ships' sewage and rubbish; cargo handling; seabed exploration and exploitation; accidental oil pollution; pollutants arriving from land by air and other routes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does it matter that the guide's coastal material sits inside its migration lesson?
**Answer:** A teacher following only the guide could under-teach a full student-book section (§7.3); the material must be re-homed to the coastal lessons, as done here.
