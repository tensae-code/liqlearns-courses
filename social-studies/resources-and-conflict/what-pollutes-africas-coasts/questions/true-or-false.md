# What pollutes Africa's coasts — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oil spills are among the discharges polluting Africa's coasts.
**Answer: True** — named in the student book.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ships discharge nothing into the sea.
**Answer: False** — ships' sewage and rubbish are in the seven sources.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide teaches coastal pollution as its own §7.3 section.
**Answer: False** — the guide has no §7.3 — the material sits inside its §7.2 migration lesson.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Destruction of wetlands and mangroves has accelerated nutrient build-up in coastal waters.
**Answer: True** — the guide's warning (teacher guide — evidence).
