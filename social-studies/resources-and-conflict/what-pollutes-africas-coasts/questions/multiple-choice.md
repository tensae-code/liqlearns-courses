# What pollutes Africa's coasts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of the student book's named polluters?
a) Industrial waste discharge  b) Municipal sewage  c) Oil spills  d) Volcanic ash
**Answer: d) Volcanic ash** — the book names industry, sewage and oil/discharges.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Coastal pollution concentrates its harm where —
a) the open ocean only  b) the narrow coastal strip where people fish, swim, trade and live  c) the desert  d) the highlands
**Answer: b) the narrow coastal strip where people fish, swim, trade and live** — every inland flow ends at the coast.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The MOST FREQUENT cause, as the guide names it, is —
a) oil spills  b) discharge of municipal sewage and industrial wastes into coastal waters/estuaries  c) air-borne pollutants  d) cargo handling
**Answer: b) discharge of municipal sewage and industrial wastes into coastal waters/estuaries** — through unsatisfactory disposal facilities (teacher guide — evidence).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mangroves and wetlands matter to coastal water because they —
a) block ships  b) act as natural filters for sediment, excess nitrogen and wastes  c) make oil  d) attract pollution
**Answer: b) act as natural filters for sediment, excess nitrogen and wastes** — their destruction speeds nutrient build-up.
