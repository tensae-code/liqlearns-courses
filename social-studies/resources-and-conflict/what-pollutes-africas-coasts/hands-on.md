# Hands-on — What pollutes Africa's coasts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Seven arrows, one bay
Draw a bay and shoreline town. Draw seven arrows into the water, one per source, labelled. Colour the sewage/industrial arrow red as the most frequent cause. Add mangroves at the river mouth labelled 'natural filter'.

## Source detective
For a coast you know (or a pictured one), list which of the seven sources could operate there and the evidence you would look for. Present two sources with their evidence.
