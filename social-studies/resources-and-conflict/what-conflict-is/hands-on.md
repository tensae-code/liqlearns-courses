# Hands-on — Conflict over Resources — What Conflict Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Resource-limit survey
With an adult, list three resources your area shares with neighbours — water, grazing, wood, a road's edge. For each, write whether its distribution is even or uneven, and whether striving over it has ever caused a quarrel.

## Faces of a quarrel
Think of a small quarrel you have seen settled. Write which of Leslau's faces it showed at its worst, and which face it ended at.
