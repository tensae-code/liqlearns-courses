# Video script — Conflict over Resources — What Conflict Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One spring. Two villages. Not enough for both.
Resources are not everywhere the same.
High here. Low there. None there.
Each side strives to benefit more — and conflict is born.
What is conflict? One dictionary says: quarrel, strife.
Another lists its faces: difference, misunderstanding,
disagreement, enmity, struggle, fighting.
Six faces, one quarrel.
Now find a resource near you that two sides strive over.
