# Conflict over Resources — What Conflict Is — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) State the two ingredients that push groups into conflict over resources.**
**Answer:** The resource's distribution is uneven — high in one area, low or none in another — and each group or individual strives to benefit more than the other.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Write Leslau's six faces of conflict.**
**Answer:** Struggle, difference, disagreement, enmity, fighting, misunderstanding.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which dictionary gives ጥል/ጠብ, and what does it mean in English?**
**Answer:** The Ethiopian Languages Institute's Amharic dictionary; it means quarrel/strife.
