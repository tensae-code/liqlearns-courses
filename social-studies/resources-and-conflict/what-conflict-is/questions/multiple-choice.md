# Conflict over Resources — What Conflict Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Resources breed conflict because —**
a) They are everywhere equal  b) Their distribution is uneven and each side strives to benefit more than the other  c) People dislike resources  d) Springs are always small
**Answer: b) Their distribution is uneven and each side strives to benefit more than the other** — uneven distribution plus striving is the book's breeding ground.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) One area's distribution of a resource may be high while another's is —**
a) Always equal  b) Low or none  c) Secret  d) Measured in money
**Answer: b) Low or none** — high against low or none is the printed contrast.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The Ethiopian Languages Institute's dictionary gives conflict as —**
a) Struggle and fighting only  b) ጥል/ጠብ — quarrel/strife  c) Misunderstanding only  d) War between countries
**Answer: b) ጥል/ጠብ — quarrel/strife** — ጥል/ጠብ is the Institute's entry.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Which list is Leslau's (1973) set of faces?**
a) Quarrel, strife  b) Struggle, difference, disagreement, enmity, fighting, misunderstanding  c) Negotiation, mediation, arbitration  d) War, sanctions, treaties
**Answer: b) Struggle, difference, disagreement, enmity, fighting, misunderstanding** — those six are Leslau's faces.
