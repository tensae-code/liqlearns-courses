# Conflict over Resources — What Conflict Is — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A plentiful resource evenly shared breeds conflict at once.**
**Answer: False** — conflict breeds where a resource is limited/uneven and striven over.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Each side striving to benefit more than the other is one ingredient of resource conflict.**
**Answer: True** — striving is the second ingredient.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Misunderstanding is one of Leslau's faces of conflict.**
**Answer: True** — it stands in the six.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) The two dictionaries give exactly the same single word.**
**Answer: False** — the Institute gives ጥል/ጠብ; Leslau gives six faces.
