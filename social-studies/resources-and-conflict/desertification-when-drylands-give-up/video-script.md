# Video script — Desertification — when drylands give up

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Desertification is pinned down precisely: land degradation specifically in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid lands.

Its human causes: population pressure, dryland farm expansion, burning and poor management, with climate change intensifying them all.

The counter-measures are local: indigenous knowledge, water harvesting with pits and micro-basins, drought-resilient short-season crops, terracing and intercropping, livelihood diversification and early warning..
