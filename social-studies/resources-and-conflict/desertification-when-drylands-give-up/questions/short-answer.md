# Desertification — when drylands give up — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define desertification exactly.
**Answer:** Land degradation in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid lands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the Aridity Index's two end bands.
**Answer:** Hyper-arid below 0.05; humid above 0.65.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three counter-measures.
**Answer:** Indigenous knowledge, water harvesting, drought-resilient crops, terracing/intercropping, diversification, early warning (any three).
