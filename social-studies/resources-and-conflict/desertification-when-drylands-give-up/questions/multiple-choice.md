# Desertification — when drylands give up — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Desertification is degradation in —
a) arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid lands  b) all lands  c) wetlands
**Answer: a) arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid lands**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hyper-arid sits below an Aridity Index of —
a) 0.05  b) 0.65  c) 1.0
**Answer: a) 0.05**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A human cause of desertification is —
a) dryland farm expansion  b) water harvesting  c) early warning
**Answer: a) dryland farm expansion**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Water harvesting uses pits and —
a) micro-basins  b) dams only  c) wells only
**Answer: a) micro-basins**
