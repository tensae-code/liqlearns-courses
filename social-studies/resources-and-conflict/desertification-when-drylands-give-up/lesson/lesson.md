# Desertification — when drylands give up

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Degradation, in the drylands only

Desertification is pinned down precisely: land degradation specifically in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid lands. The book delimits the drylands by Aridity Index bands — hyper-arid below 0.05, up to humid above 0.65.

[Picture 27080 — a world drylands map beside an Afar desert landscape]

## Sub lesson 2 — Causes and impacts

Its human causes: population pressure, dryland farm expansion, burning and poor management, with climate change intensifying them all. Impacts: lost productivity and ecosystem services, lost biodiversity, invasive spread, salinisation, and more greenhouse gas — shown with Afar and Somali desert photographs.

## Sub lesson 3 — Local counter-measures

The counter-measures are local: indigenous knowledge, water harvesting with pits and micro-basins, drought-resilient short-season crops, terracing and intercropping, livelihood diversification and early warning.
