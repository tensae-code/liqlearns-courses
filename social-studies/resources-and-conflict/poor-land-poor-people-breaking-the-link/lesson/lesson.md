# Poor land, poor people — breaking the link

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: the poor degrade land — but is that the whole story? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Degradation defined again, deeper

Land degradation is persistent decline in land's productivity, biodiversity and services, from soil, biological, vegetation and water damage. This lesson ties it to poverty link by link.

## Sub lesson 2 — Both ways, and both classes

The poor degrade land, driven by need. And so do the rich, driven by scale — the book is careful to make the link two-way and two-class, not a blame laid on the poor alone.

## Sub lesson 3 — The management menu

Options organise in four families: soil (runoff, pH, salinisation, compaction), vegetation (grazing control, cropland care, tree cover), water (over-extraction, landslides, flooding), and integrated options (biodiversity, wetlands, wildlife corridors, pollution).
