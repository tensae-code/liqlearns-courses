# Video script — Poor land, poor people — breaking the link

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Land degradation is persistent decline in land's productivity, biodiversity and services, from soil, biological, vegetation and water damage.

The poor degrade land, driven by need.

Options organise in four families: soil (runoff, pH, salinisation, compaction), vegetation (grazing control, cropland care, tree cover), water (over-extraction, landslides, flooding), and integrated options (biodiversity, wetlands, wildlife corridors, pollution)..
