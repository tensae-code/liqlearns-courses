# Poor land, poor people — breaking the link — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land degradation is persistent decline in productivity, biodiversity and —
a) services  b) colour  c) ownership
**Answer: a) services**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The link between poverty and degradation runs —
a) both ways  b) one way only  c) not at all
**Answer: a) both ways**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who degrades land, per the book?
a) the poor and the rich  b) the poor only  c) the rich only
**Answer: a) the poor and the rich**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Wetlands and wildlife corridors belong to —
a) integrated options  b) soil options  c) water options
**Answer: a) integrated options**
