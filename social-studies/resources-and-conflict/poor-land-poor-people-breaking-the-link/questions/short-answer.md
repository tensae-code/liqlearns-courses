# Poor land, poor people — breaking the link — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define land degradation.
**Answer:** Persistent decline in land's productivity, biodiversity and services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the poverty link not simple blame?
**Answer:** Both the poor (from need) and the rich (from scale) degrade land.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four management families.
**Answer:** Soil, vegetation, water, integrated.
