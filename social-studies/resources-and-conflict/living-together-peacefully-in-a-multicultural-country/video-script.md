# Video script — Living Together Peacefully in a Multicultural Country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 5 Year 5 Social Studies (printed p. 13), teaches: Understanding and respecting each other's culture brings peace and harmony; shared national celebrations (Easter, Christmas, Diwali, Eid) and the Hibiscus festival unite the country.

Fiji's own way (Grade 5): community is taught as the social groups a child belongs to — family first, then school and community groups — and Fiji's multicultural mix is the civics lesson itself: all born in Fiji are Fijian, each keeping their own culture.
