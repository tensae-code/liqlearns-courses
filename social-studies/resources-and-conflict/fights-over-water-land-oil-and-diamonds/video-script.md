# Video script — Fights over water, land, oil and diamonds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Water and pasture fights include Darfur, the Nile riparians, and Somali–Afar pastoralists inside Ethiopia.

Land and border fights name Ethiopia–Eritrea, Ethiopia–Somalia and Kashmir.

Conflict minerals close the survey: diamonds funding UNITA and the RUF, gold in Indonesia, amber in Russia.
