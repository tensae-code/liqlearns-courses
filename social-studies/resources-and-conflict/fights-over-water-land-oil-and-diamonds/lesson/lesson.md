# Fights over water, land, oil and diamonds

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: which resources have started real fights in the real world? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Water, pasture and fish

Water and pasture fights include Darfur, the Nile riparians, and Somali–Afar pastoralists inside Ethiopia. Over fishing, the UK and Iceland fought the cod wars — a quarrel at sea over who may catch what, where.

## Sub lesson 2 — Land, timber and oil

Land and border fights name Ethiopia–Eritrea, Ethiopia–Somalia and Kashmir. Timber is contested in Burma, Cambodia, DR Congo and Liberia. Oil stands behind the Gulf War and the Cameroon–Nigeria dispute.

## Sub lesson 3 — Gems and the closing question

Conflict minerals close the survey: diamonds funding UNITA and the RUF, gold in Indonesia, amber in Russia. The class must then research the scholars' argument: does scarcity cause conflict — or abundance?
