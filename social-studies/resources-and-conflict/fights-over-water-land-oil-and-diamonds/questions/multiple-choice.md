# Fights over water, land, oil and diamonds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cod wars were fought between the UK and —
a) Iceland  b) Egypt  c) Liberia
**Answer: a) Iceland**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Darfur is the book's example of conflict over —
a) water and pasture  b) diamonds  c) timber
**Answer: a) water and pasture**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Timber conflict is named in —
a) DR Congo  b) Iceland  c) Kashmir
**Answer: a) DR Congo**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Diamonds funded —
a) UNITA and the RUF  b) the NBI  c) SARCCUS
**Answer: a) UNITA and the RUF**
