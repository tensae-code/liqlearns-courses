## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 10, Relaciones armoniosas

Dominican Republic — Ciencias Sociales 6° Primaria (printed p. 170), teaches: Harmonious relations in the world, starting at home ground — neighbourly relations between neighbours and communities as essential to building societies. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way: the last primary volume grounds the State in its thinkers and its text — Montesquieu's separation of powers, the Constitution as the backbone (columna vertebral) of the State, the Escudo defined by Article 32, and the municipality split into its two organs, the regidores who norm and oversee and the Alcaldía that executes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
