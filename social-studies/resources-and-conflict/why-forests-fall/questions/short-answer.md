# Why forests fall — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the underlying causes.
**Answer:** Climate change, population pressure, culture, flawed policies (tenure insecurity, weak enforcement).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four proximate acts.
**Answer:** Clearing/burning for farms, fuelwood and timber, settlement/infrastructure, mining, commercial logging, overgrazing (any four).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three consequences and two controls.
**Answer:** Consequences: carbon loss, biodiversity loss, broken water cycles, drought/famine, erosion/flooding, nutrient depletion, warming. Controls: afforestation, reforestation, area closure, controlled burning/logging, alternative energy.
