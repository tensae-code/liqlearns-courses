# Why forests fall — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tenure insecurity is an —
a) underlying cause  b) proximate act  c) consequence
**Answer: a) underlying cause**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Weak enforcement comes from —
a) flawed policies  b) overgrazing  c) burning
**Answer: a) flawed policies**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Commercial logging is a —
a) proximate act  b) underlying cause  c) control
**Answer: a) proximate act**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Alternative energy appears among —
a) the controls  b) the consequences  c) the causes
**Answer: a) the controls**
