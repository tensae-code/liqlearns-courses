Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project. That way is stocked here as the way Barbados teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Ordering slip carried as the placements flag it: in the printed student book, this section's consequences text (7.4.2) appears after the opening of section 7.5. The lesson teaches the consequences with the deforestation section, in the contents' order.

Costa Rica's own way: a distance-education anthology organised around "the human footprint on geographic space" — every unit opens with a generating question and objectives, and Costa Rica is used as the home case inside world topics (tropical forest conservation, food production problems). That way is stocked here as the way Costa Rica teaches this thing.

Malaysia's own way (MY T3 Geografi Tingkatan 3): Form 3 turns from skills (tables, graphs, pie charts) to living Malaysia — its natural vegetation and wildlife and the human pressure on both — then outward to the world's biomes, resources and economic cooperation, closing with forest management and recycling.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): the local-to-global ladder completes — after province (G5), nation (G7), Grade 8's Environment and Resources strand is global: global climate, global settlement and population, global farming, urbanization and logging impacts, global hazards and disaster resilience. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grades 11–12 Geography TG OBE) (teacher guide — evidence): an outcomes-based guide — it prescribes no lesson scripts; for each syllabus unit it lists suggested learning activities and assessment tasks built around the local environment (soil profiles, local hazard sites, a PNG mine and logging case study, a transect along latitude 6°S), with fieldwork and geographical inquiry as the core skills. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 4 SS4): one combined Social Studies book in Urdu where the Geography chapter (Ch.5, printed pp. 61–93) works entirely through Pakistan's own map, landforms, weather and census, with BOLTS as the map-elements mnemonic and "I have learnt!" / "Work for me to do" boxes closing every topic.
