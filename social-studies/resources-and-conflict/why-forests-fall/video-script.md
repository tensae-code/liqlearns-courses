# Video script — Why forests fall

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The driver analysis mirrors Unit Three's method.

The underlying causes work through proximate acts: clearing and burning for farms, fuelwood and timber taking, settlement and infrastructure, mining, commercial logging and overgrazing..

Consequences in full: carbon loss, biodiversity and habitat loss, broken water cycles, drought and famine, erosion and flooding, nutrient depletion, worsened warming.
