# Video script — What Peace is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 122–124, teaches: the word peace has many meanings — no war, a non-aggression pact, reconciliation, forgiveness, resolving a conflict; children define the peace that should exist at school and at home and test opinions about peace in a family survey.

Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city.
