# Hands-on — What Peace is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 15 (closing part)
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 122–124, teaches: the word peace has many meanings — no war, a non-aggression pact, reconciliation, forgiveness, resolving a conflict; children define the peace that should exist at school and at home and test opinions about peace in a family survey. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
