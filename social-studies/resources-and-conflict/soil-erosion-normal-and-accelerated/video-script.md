# Video script — Careless Use — Soil Erosion, Normal and Accelerated

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soil moves. That is erosion.
On a covered slope it moves slowly —
as fast as new soil forms beneath. In balance. Necessary.
That is normal erosion.
Now strip the cover. Fell. Overgraze. Plough badly.
Rain takes soil faster than it can form.
That is accelerated erosion — human-made.
It takes fertility. It starves plants. It cuts the harvest.
Cover is the difference. Keep the cover.
