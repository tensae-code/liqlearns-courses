# Careless Use — Soil Erosion, Normal and Accelerated — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Some erosion is natural and necessary.**
**Answer: True** — old soil must lift off the rock for new soil to form.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Normal erosion strips soil faster than it forms.**
**Answer: False** — faster than formation is accelerated erosion; normal keeps pace or stays below.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In practice, all accelerated erosion is human-made.**
**Answer: True** — the book states it in those terms.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Accelerated erosion improves a field's fertility.**
**Answer: False** — it takes fertility, starves plants and cuts output.
