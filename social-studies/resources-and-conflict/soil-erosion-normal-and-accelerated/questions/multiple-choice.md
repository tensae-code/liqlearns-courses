# Careless Use — Soil Erosion, Normal and Accelerated — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Erosion is —**
a) Soil forming on rock  b) Soil being moved from place to place by moving agents  c) Trees growing on soil  d) Rain falling on soil
**Answer: b) Soil being moved from place to place by moving agents** — moved by moving agents is the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Normal erosion keeps pace with, or stays below —**
a) Rainfall  b) Soil formation  c) Tree growth  d) River flow
**Answer: b) Soil formation** — at or below formation pace is normal.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which is a cause of accelerated erosion?**
a) Grass covering a slope  b) Repeatedly overgrazing grass  c) Terracing  d) Soil forming slowly
**Answer: b) Repeatedly overgrazing grass** — repeated overgrazing is a named human cause.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Accelerated erosion's cost includes —**
a) Richer soil  b) Cut farm output  c) More nutrients for plants  d) Deeper topsoil
**Answer: b) Cut farm output** — fertility taken, nutrients lost, output cut.
