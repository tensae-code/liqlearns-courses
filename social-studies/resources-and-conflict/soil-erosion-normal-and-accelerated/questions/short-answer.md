# Careless Use — Soil Erosion, Normal and Accelerated — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Why is some erosion necessary?**
**Answer:** Because old soil must lift off the rock for new soil to form, keeping formation and accumulation in balance.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name three human causes of accelerated erosion.**
**Answer:** Felling forests, repeatedly overgrazing grass, and backward ploughing (with climate change hitting plant cover).

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What does accelerated erosion do to a farm?**
**Answer:** It takes the soil's fertility, starves plants of nutrients and cuts farm output.
