# Hands-on — Careless Use — Soil Erosion, Normal and Accelerated

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-slope field check
Visit two slopes with an adult after rain if you can. Look for rills, gullies, exposed roots, soil fans at the foot. Score each slope: normal or accelerated, with evidence.

## Rain test in a tray
Fill two trays with soil — one bare, one covered with grass or leaves. Pour the same water over both from the same height. Compare the mud in the runoff. Plant cover is the difference, in your hands.
