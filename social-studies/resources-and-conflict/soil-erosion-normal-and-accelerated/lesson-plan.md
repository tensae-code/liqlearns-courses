# Lesson plan — Careless Use — Soil Erosion, Normal and Accelerated

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Resources and Conflict · **Lesson:** Careless Use — Soil Erosion, Normal and Accelerated
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Resources and Conflict (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md: natural resources, their care, and conflict over resources).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** erosion is soil moved by agents — normal (geological) erosion keeps pace with soil formation and is necessary, while accelerated erosion, in practice all human-made, strips made soil faster than it forms and takes fertility with it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Careless Use — Vegetation: the felling and overgrazing named there are two of this lesson's causes.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define erosion as soil moved from place to place by moving agents.
2. Distinguish normal erosion: at or below formation pace, natural and necessary.
3. Distinguish accelerated erosion: faster than formation, in practice all human-made.
4. Name accelerated erosion's causes and its cost: fertility taken, nutrients lost, farm output cut.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil.

## If the learner is stuck
- Calls all erosion bad → normal erosion is natural and necessary: old soil must lift for new soil to form, in balance.
- Blames rain alone for gullies → rain is the agent; the acceleration is human-made — felling, overgrazing, backward ploughing, cover lost.
- Cannot name the cost → fertility taken, plants starved of nutrients, farm output cut.
