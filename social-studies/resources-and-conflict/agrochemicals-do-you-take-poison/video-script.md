# Video script — Agrochemicals — Do You Take Poison?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Folha 17 asks Você toma veneno? / Do you take poison? The last Folha of Brazil's book puts the question to the learner's own plate: what is used in producing food can reach the person who eats it.

The Folha teaches agrochemicals in food production: chemicals used in growing food are this lesson's subject, and the opening question is their human end — the poison asked about is the agrochemical side of the food the learner eats.
