# Agrochemicals — Do You Take Poison?

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Folha 17: Você toma veneno? / Do you take poison? — agrochemicals in food production, as Brazil's Grade 10 Geografia (SEED-PR) teaches it in one of its 17 Folhas, each opening with a provocative question. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Do you take poison? — the opening question

Folha 17 asks Você toma veneno? / Do you take poison? The last Folha of Brazil's book puts the question to the learner's own plate: what is used in producing food can reach the person who eats it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Agrochemicals in food production

The Folha teaches agrochemicals in food production: chemicals used in growing food are this lesson's subject, and the opening question is their human end — the poison asked about is the agrochemical side of the food the learner eats.

Brazil's book has no chapters in the old style: it is 17 "Folhas" (sheets) like this one, each opening with a provocative question, grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
