# Hands-on — Agrochemicals — Do You Take Poison?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace one food
Pick one food you ate today and write what you know, and do not know, about chemicals used in producing it, as Folha 17 asks: Você toma veneno?

## Ask the producer's question
Write one question you would ask a farmer about agrochemicals in food production, and why it matters, in your own words.
