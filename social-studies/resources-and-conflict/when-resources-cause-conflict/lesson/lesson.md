# When resources cause conflict

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: when does a resource stop being wealth and start being a quarrel? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What resource conflict is

Conflict over resources means disputes over accessing, controlling and using natural materials. It arises when needs go unmet, or when some users are excluded from what they depend on.

## Sub lesson 2 — Four national-scale forms

The book distinguishes four forms at national scale: secessionist bids by resource-rich regions; disputes written into new laws and peace agreements; grievances over single projects like mines and dams; and the cumulative effect of many small clashes.

## Sub lesson 3 — What the fights are about

Whatever the form, the quarrel turns on one of four things: ownership, management power, revenue sharing, or damage from extraction.
