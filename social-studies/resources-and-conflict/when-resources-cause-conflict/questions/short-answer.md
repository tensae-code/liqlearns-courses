# When resources cause conflict — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define resource conflict.
**Answer:** Disputes over accessing, controlling and using natural materials.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four national-scale forms.
**Answer:** Secessionist bids; disputes in laws/peace agreements; grievances over single projects; cumulative small clashes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four things fights turn on.
**Answer:** Ownership, management power, revenue sharing, damage from extraction.
