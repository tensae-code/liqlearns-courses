# When resources cause conflict — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Resource conflict arises when needs go unmet or users are —
a) excluded  b) enriched  c) counted
**Answer: a) excluded**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A secessionist bid is made by —
a) a resource-rich region  b) a single farmer  c) a river commission
**Answer: a) a resource-rich region**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Grievances over a single mine or dam are —
a) one of the four forms  b) not resource conflict  c) always settled
**Answer: a) one of the four forms**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Fights turn on ownership, management power, revenue sharing or —
a) damage from extraction  b) weather  c) language
**Answer: a) damage from extraction**
