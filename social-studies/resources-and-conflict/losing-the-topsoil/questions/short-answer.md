# Losing the topsoil — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three losses.
**Answer: Root anchorage, nutrients and soil depth.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Walk the chain.
**Answer: Exposure by clearing/overgrazing/cropping; rain washes topsoil away; anchorage, nutrients, depth fall; yields fall.**
