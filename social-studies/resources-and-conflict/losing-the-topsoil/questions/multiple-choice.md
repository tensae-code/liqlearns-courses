# Losing the topsoil — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Topsoil loss reduces —
a) rainfall  b) sunshine  c) root anchorage
**Answer: c) root anchorage**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nutrients leave —
a) into the air  b) with the soil  c) by evaporation only
**Answer: b) with the soil**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The chain begins with —
a) clearing, overgrazing and cropping exposing the soil  b) yield failure  c) flooding
**Answer: a) clearing, overgrazing and cropping exposing the soil**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The final link is —
a) deeper soil  b) richer soil  c) falling yields
**Answer: c) falling yields**
