# Video script — Losing the topsoil

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The topsoil is thin, dark, and priceless.
Lose it, and plants lose anchorage, nutrients, depth.
Clearing exposes it. Rain carries it. Yields follow it down.
A chain — and every link can be broken.
