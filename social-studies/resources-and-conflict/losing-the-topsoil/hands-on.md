# Hands-on — Losing the topsoil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Layer jar return
Shake soil and water in a jar, let it settle, and identify the dark topsoil layer floating its richness downward in the settling. Compare jar layers with a real profile if one is open nearby.

## Chain intervention
Draw the degradation chain and mark every link where the conservation toolkit (next lesson) can cut it. This drawing is your revision map for both lessons.
