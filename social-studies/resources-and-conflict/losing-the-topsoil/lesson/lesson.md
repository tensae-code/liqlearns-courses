# Losing the topsoil

## Do it first

The harvest shrinks year by year on the same plot with the same work. The thief is invisible: the topsoil itself is leaving. Read the mechanism.

## Sub lesson 1 — What topsoil loss takes

Topsoil is the fertile surface layer. Losing it takes three things from the plant: root anchorage — plants hold worse and fall or wash out; nutrients — the food of crops leaves with the soil; and depth — roots run out of room above the hard subsoil.

## Sub lesson 2 — The mechanism

The mechanism joins the units: clearing, overgrazing and cropping expose the topsoil; rain washes it away; anchorage, nutrients and depth fall; yields fall with them. Soil degradation is not an event but a chain — and every link is a place to intervene.
