# Video script — Harmony and Disharmony in the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rwanda P4 SRS SB, printed p. 54, teaches: harmony is people living on good terms in the community; disharmony is misunderstanding between people — with disharmony among peers in the classroom, we cannot learn properly.

Rwanda Grade 5 P5 SB, printed p. 22, teaches: harmony vs disharmony and its causes (religious differences, bias/unfair judgement, low development) and effects.

Rwanda's own way (Primary Four — Rwanda P4 SRS SB): P4 states rights before duties of the state: rights belong to everybody whatever their place, age, sex or wealth; equality is then split from equity (same treatment vs a fair share of what each needs), and district government is taught as a council that collects local taxes and elects its own Mayor

Rwanda's own way (Primary Five — Rwanda Grade 5 P5 SB): the province is the child's civic world — complementarity (everyone's work fitting together) is the organising value, and national symbols, the 2003/2015 constitution and province leadership are taught as Rwanda's own frame inside it
