## Sub lesson 3 — Rwanda's way — the books' own teaching way

Rwanda's own way (Primary Four — Rwanda P4 SRS SB): P4 states rights before duties of the state: rights belong to everybody whatever their place, age, sex or wealth; equality is then split from equity (same treatment vs a fair share of what each needs), and district government is taught as a council that collects local taxes and elects its own Mayor

Rwanda's own way (Primary Five — Rwanda Grade 5 P5 SB): the province is the child's civic world — complementarity (everyone's work fitting together) is the organising value, and national symbols, the 2003/2015 constitution and province leadership are taught as Rwanda's own frame inside it This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
