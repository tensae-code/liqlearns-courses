# Video script — Three ways land breaks down

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Physical degradation compacts, crusts, seals and thins the soil — even its colour changes.

Chemical degradation drains nutrients, and tips water and soil chemistry out of balance: eutrophication, acidification and alkalinisation.

Biological degradation is loss of biological mass, through deforestation, overgrazing and harmful spraying.
