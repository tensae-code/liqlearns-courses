# Three ways land breaks down — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three forms.
**Answer:** Physical, chemical, biological.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two examples of each form.
**Answer:** Physical: compaction, crusting, sealing, thinning. Chemical: nutrient loss, eutrophication, acidification, alkalinisation. Biological: loss of biological mass (two per form as available).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one photographed place.
**Answer:** Pale degraded soil in Tigray; degraded land near Alaba Kulito / the Soddo–Shashemene road.
