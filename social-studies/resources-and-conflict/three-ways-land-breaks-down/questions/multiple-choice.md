# Three ways land breaks down — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Compaction and crusting are —
a) physical forms  b) chemical forms  c) biological forms
**Answer: a) physical forms**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil colour change signals —
a) physical degradation  b) recovery  c) fertility
**Answer: a) physical degradation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Eutrophication is a —
a) chemical form  b) physical form  c) remedy
**Answer: a) chemical form**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Loss of biological mass is —
a) biological degradation  b) sealing  c) thinning
**Answer: a) biological degradation**
