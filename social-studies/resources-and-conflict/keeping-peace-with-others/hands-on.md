# Hands-on — Keeping Peace with Others

## Keeping Peace with Others
Work through this part with your hands: the everyday building of peace — good treatment, collaboration and appreciation of persons; transformative actions that preserve life and dignity; school, community, national and world organisation for peaceful coexistence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
