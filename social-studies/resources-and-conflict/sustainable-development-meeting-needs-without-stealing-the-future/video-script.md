# Video script — Sustainable development — meeting needs without stealing the future

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sustainable development meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs — across three nested dimensions: economy inside society inside environment. First, the idea and its definition: The concept grew through world conferences: Stockholm in 1972, where the environment first took the world stage and UNEP was founded; Rio in 1992, whose Earth Summit produced Agenda 21 with its Principle 1 putting human beings at the centre; and Johannesburg in 2002, emphasising the social and economic sides. Then, the three dimensions, nested: Sustainable development stands on three dimensions. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
