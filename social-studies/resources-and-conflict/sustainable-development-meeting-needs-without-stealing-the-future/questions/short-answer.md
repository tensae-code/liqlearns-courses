# Sustainable development — meeting needs without stealing the future — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the Brundtland definition in full.
**Answer:** Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three dimensions and one requirement of each.
**Answer:** Economic (e.g. continuing production, manageable debt); environmental (e.g. stable resource base, no overexploitation of renewables); social (e.g. fair distribution, health, education, gender equity).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are the rings nested rather than side by side?
**Answer:** Because each inner dimension depends on the one holding it: economy inside society, society inside environment.
