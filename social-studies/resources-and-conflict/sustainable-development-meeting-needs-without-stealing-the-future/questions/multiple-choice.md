# Sustainable development — meeting needs without stealing the future — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Brundtland definition is from the report…
a) Agenda 21  b) Our Common Future (1987)  c) the Stockholm Declaration
**Answer: b) Our Common Future (1987)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Sustainable development means meeting present needs without…
a) any change  b) compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs  c) using resources at all
**Answer: b) compromising future generations' ability to meet theirs**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Rio 1992 produced…
a) Agenda 21  b) the Kyoto Protocol  c) UNEP
**Answer: a) Agenda 21**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The three dimensions are…
a) economic, environmental, social  b) local, national, global  c) past, present, future
**Answer: a) economic, environmental, social**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In the nested rings, the economy sits inside…
a) the environment directly  b) society  c) nothing
**Answer: b) society**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The environmental dimension requires…
a) no overexploitation of renewables; biodiversity maintained  b) maximum extraction  c) no farming
**Answer: a) no overexploitation of renewables; biodiversity maintained**
