# Hands-on — Conflict — the Abay Dispute Case Study

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Basin map by hand
Draw the Nile basin from the lesson's facts alone: Lake Tana and the Abay, Lake Victoria and the White Nile, the Tekeze joining at Khartoum, the Mediterranean at the end. Mark the shares 70 / 15 / 15 and the dam.

## Fair-shares debate
In two groups, debate the book's set question — Ethiopia's full right to use the Abay — one group for, one group testing it with the lower basin's case. Both groups must use the shares and the equitable-utilisation idea, not slogans.
