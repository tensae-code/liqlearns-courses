# Video script — Conflict — the Abay Dispute Case Study

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One river. Eleven countries. The longest crossing borders.
From Lake Victoria, the White Nile brings fifteen parts in a hundred.
From Lake Tana, the Abay brings seventy.
The Tekeze brings fifteen more.
Eighty-five in a hundred rise on Ethiopian ground.
Now every basin state reaches for the water:
fields, power, drinking, fish, visitors, food.
Ethiopia builds a great dam — for electricity.
One state that adds no water blocks the rest. Leaders talk of war.
Politics over shared water has a name: hydro-politics.
The direction named is fairness: equitable use.
Now argue the case — with the shares, not with slogans.
