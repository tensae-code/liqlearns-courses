# Conflict — the Abay Dispute Case Study — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the three tributaries with their shares and sources.**
**Answer:** White Nile from Lake Victoria — about 15%; the Abay from Lake Tana — about 70%; Tekeze/Atbara — about 15%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name four uses basin states now seek from the river.**
**Answer:** Any four of: irrigation, power, water supply, fisheries, tourism, food security.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) Define water conflict in the guide's words.**
**Answer:** A conflict between countries, states or groups over rights to access water resources.
