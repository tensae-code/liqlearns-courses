# Conflict — the Abay Dispute Case Study — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The Abay's share of the flow is about —**
a) 15%  b) 70%  c) 85% alone  d) 50%
**Answer: b) 70%** — the Abay from Lake Tana carries about 70%.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The Abay and Tekeze together carry about —**
a) 15%  b) 70%  c) 85%  d) 100%
**Answer: c) 85%** — 70 + 15 ≈ 85%.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) The Grand Renaissance Dam is built mainly for —**
a) Irrigation  b) Electricity  c) Tourism  d) Fisheries
**Answer: b) Electricity** — mainly for electricity, as printed.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) Hydro-politics is —**
a) River engineering  b) The study of inter-state politics regarding the management of shared water resources  c) A dam-building technique  d) Egyptian foreign policy only
**Answer: b) The study of inter-state politics regarding the management of shared water resources** — the guide's definition is about inter-state politics over shared water.
