# Conflict — the Abay Dispute Case Study — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) The White Nile rises from Lake Victoria.**
**Answer: True** — the White Nile comes from Lake Victoria at about 15%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Egypt contributes water to the Nile's flow, in the study's account.**
**Answer: False** — the study says Egypt contributes no water.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Basin states seek the river only for drinking water.**
**Answer: False** — they seek irrigation, power, water supply, fisheries, tourism and food security.

**4. (Sub lesson 3) The guide calls the river's upper/lower utilisation unbalanced and unfair.**
**Answer: True** — that is the guide's judgement, with movement towards equitable utilisation.
