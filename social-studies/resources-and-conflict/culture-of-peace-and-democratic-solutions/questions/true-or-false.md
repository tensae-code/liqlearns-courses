# Culture of Peace and Democratic Solutions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: culture of peace and peaceful solution of conflicts, democratic solutions, and recognising social, family and youth violence.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: culture of peace and conflict situations — harmonious relations in the world and the pupil as an ambassador of peace who resolves problems without violence.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: the culture of peace — an education oriented to peace that teaches people to value coexistence, with effective communication.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: culture of peace and conflictive situations — peace as a relation of harmony.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's fifth part teaches: the golden rule of peace as a universal principle for fostering peace.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson's sixth part teaches: conflict solution and culture of peace — respect and empathy as fundamental to solving conflicts, recognising and valuing others' opinions and perspectives.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** This lesson's seventh part teaches: building and sustaining a culture of peace is an educational challenge — a new way of seeing, understanding and living in society — closed by a group activity on human rights in relationships and a signed agreement of intentions.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** This lesson's eighth part teaches: a pupil panel "Together for a culture of peace", with questions directed to the panellists.
**Answer: True**

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
