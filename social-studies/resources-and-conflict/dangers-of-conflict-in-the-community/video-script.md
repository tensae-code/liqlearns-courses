# Video script — Dangers of Conflict in the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 3 P3 Social Studies SB, printed p. 47, teaches: conflicts are dangerous — they bring death, poverty and arrest, cause disunity and separate family members.

South Sudan Primary 4 P4 Social Studies SB, printed p. 70, teaches: conflict may lead to a fight, to hatred, to things destroyed and to people dying — we should always live in peace and love each other (the dangers-of-conflict lesson is filed NEW from Book 5 in this wave).

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 113, teaches: war's effects beyond deaths — refugees camping in foreign countries, poverty as houses, trading centres, schools and churches are destroyed, deserted homes, and poor infrastructure; conflicts are major causes of poverty, underdevelopment and ill health (the dangers lesson is filed NEW from Book 5 in this wave).

South Sudan's own way: Conflict is taught from the child's own ground — its named causes are theft, refusing other people's religions and corruption; its solvers are the payam chief and the village elder; and the flag itself carries the country's peace story, its two white meanings tied to the 1972 Addis Ababa and 2005 Naivasha agreements.
