# Hands-on — Dangers of Conflict in the Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 5 Dangers of conflict in the community
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: conflicts are dangerous — they bring death, poverty and arrest, cause disunity and separate family members. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unit 5: effects of conflict (poem)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: conflict may lead to a fight, to hatred, to things destroyed and to people dying — we should always live in peace and love each other (the dangers-of-conflict lesson is filed NEW from Book 5 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Activity 10 Effects of conflicts in South Sudan
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: war's effects beyond deaths — refugees camping in foreign countries, poverty as houses, trading centres, schools and churches are destroyed, deserted homes, and poor infrastructure; conflicts are major causes of poverty, underdevelopment and ill health (the dangers lesson is filed NEW from Book 5 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
