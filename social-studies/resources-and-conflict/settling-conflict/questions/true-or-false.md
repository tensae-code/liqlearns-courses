# Conflict — Negotiation, Mediation, Arbitration — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Negotiation breaks off if one side refuses.**
**Answer: True** — refusal ends it — willingness is its ground.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Any conflict can be settled by mediation.**
**Answer: False** — the chapter's own review trap: mediation needs both sides honouring the mediator and keeping their word.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Arbitration needs the parties' consent before it can take up a case.**
**Answer: False** — it does not need consent; it follows the law.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) An arbitrator hears both sides together.**
**Answer: True** — hearing both sides together is part of the printed method.
