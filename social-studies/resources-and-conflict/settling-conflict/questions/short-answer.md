# Conflict — Negotiation, Mediation, Arbitration — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Give the Amharic names of mediation and arbitration.**
**Answer:** Mediation — ሽምግልና; arbitration — ዳኝነት.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name three powers of the arbitrator that the mediator does not have.**
**Answer:** Any three of: legal power to decide the settlement, identifying the wrongdoer as a ruling, restoring the peaceful state by order, passing punishment, taking up the case without consent.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) What is a third party's role in negotiation, as printed?**
**Answer:** A third party often prepares the negotiation — but the parties themselves settle their difference by discussion.
