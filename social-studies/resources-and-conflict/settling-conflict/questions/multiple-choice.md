# Conflict — Negotiation, Mediation, Arbitration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Negotiation rests on —**
a) A court's power  b) Willingness — both parties wanting to settle by discussion  c) The mediator's honour  d) Punishment
**Answer: b) Willingness — both parties wanting to settle by discussion** — willingness is negotiation's ground, and refusal breaks it off.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In mediation, the final reconciliation decision belongs to —**
a) The mediator alone  b) The two parties, as their own joint agreement  c) A judge  d) The elder's family
**Answer: b) The two parties, as their own joint agreement** — the decision is the parties' own joint agreement.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Mediation works only while both sides —**
a) Pay the mediator  b) Honour the mediator and are ready to keep their word  c) Accept low standing  d) Live in one village
**Answer: b) Honour the mediator and are ready to keep their word** — honour plus word-keeping are its two conditions.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Arbitration differs because its third party —**
a) Only advises  b) Holds the legal power to decide, can identify the wrongdoer and pass punishment  c) Must be accepted by both sides to start  d) Never hears evidence
**Answer: b) Holds the legal power to decide, can identify the wrongdoer and pass punishment** — legal deciding power is arbitration's mark.
