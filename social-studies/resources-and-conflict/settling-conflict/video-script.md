# Video script — Conflict — Negotiation, Mediation, Arbitration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A quarrel can end three ways. Imboighe names them.
Negotiation: the two parties talk. Both willing.
One refuses — it breaks off.
Mediation — ሽምግልና. An elder both sides accept.
He reconciles. But the decision stays theirs.
Honour the mediator. Keep your word. Or it fails.
Arbitration — ዳኝነት. A third party with legal power.
He hears both. Names the wrongdoer. Can punish.
No consent needed. The law takes the case.
Ask of every settlement: who decided?
