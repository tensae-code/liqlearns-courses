# Hands-on — Conflict — Negotiation, Mediation, Arbitration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Elder interview
Interview an elder or community figure: when do people here negotiate alone, when do they call a mediator (ሽምግልና), and what goes to arbitration (ዳኝነት)? Write their answers as your community map's first draft.

## Role-play the three endings
In threes, act the same boundary quarrel three ways — negotiation, mediation, arbitration. After each, the class answers aloud: who decided? That answer is the method's name.
