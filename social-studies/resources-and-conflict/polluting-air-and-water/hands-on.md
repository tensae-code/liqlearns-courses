# Hands-on — Polluting air and water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Source census
Census your area’s pollution: list every air source and every water entry point you can verify, in two columns, with the effect each is already having.

## Downstream letter
Write a short letter from a downstream village to an upstream polluter, using only the lesson’s facts: what enters, what it does, what is asked.
