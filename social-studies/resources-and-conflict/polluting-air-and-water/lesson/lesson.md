# Polluting air and water

## Do it first

A river carrying waste past the washing place; smoke hanging over a town at cooking hour. Two pollutions, one maker. Read them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Air pollution

Air pollution in Ethiopia comes from human sources — smoke from fuel burning, dust, vehicle and industry emissions among the local examples. Its effects fall on health — lungs and eyes first — and on the air of towns and homes.

[Picture 21109 — a river carrying waste past a washing point]

## Sub lesson 2 — Water pollution

Water pollution enters rivers, lakes and wells from waste, effluent and poor sanitation. Its effects run downstream: sickness for those who drink and wash, harm to aquatic life and the ecosystems that depend on clean water.
