# Polluting air and water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An air-pollution source is —
a) river effluent  b) litter  c) smoke from fuel burning
**Answer: c) smoke from fuel burning**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Air pollution strikes first at —
a) hair  b) lungs and eyes  c) feet
**Answer: b) lungs and eyes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Water pollution enters from —
a) waste, effluent and poor sanitation  b) rain only  c) springs
**Answer: a) waste, effluent and poor sanitation**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Its effects run —
a) only at the entry point  b) uphill  c) downstream
**Answer: c) downstream**
