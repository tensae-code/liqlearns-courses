# Ethiopia’s wildlife and its endemics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Rift lakes are noted for —
a) penguins  b) ostriches only  c) flamingos
**Answer: c) flamingos**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Wildlife is protected in —
a) private farms only  b) national parks and sanctuaries  c) city zoos only
**Answer: b) national parks and sanctuaries**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An endemic species is —
a) found in one place and nowhere else  b) found on every continent  c) kept only in zoos
**Answer: a) found in one place and nowhere else**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The walia ibex lives in —
a) the Ogaden  b) Lake Tana  c) the Simien heights
**Answer: c) the Simien heights**
