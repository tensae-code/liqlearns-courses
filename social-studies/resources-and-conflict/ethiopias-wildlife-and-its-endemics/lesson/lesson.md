# Ethiopia’s wildlife and its endemics

## Do it first

Four animals exist in the whole world only in Ethiopia. Only here. Read the country’s wildlife — and meet the four.

## Sub lesson 1 — The wide wildlife

Ethiopia’s wildlife includes savanna mammals, forest species, rich birdlife — including the flamingos of the Rift lakes — and aquatic animals. National parks and sanctuaries protect the wildlife across these habitats.

## Sub lesson 2 — The endemics

An endemic species is found in one place and nowhere else. Ethiopia’s notable endemics include the walia ibex of the Simien heights, the Ethiopian wolf, the mountain nyala and the gelada. Lose them here, and they are lost to the world.
