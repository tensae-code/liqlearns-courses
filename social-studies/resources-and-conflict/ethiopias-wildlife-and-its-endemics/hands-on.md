# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s wildlife and its endemics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Endemic cards
Make a card for each endemic: its name, its home habitat from the course (Simien heights for the walia), and one line on why losing its home loses the species.

## Habitat match
Match each wildlife group — savanna mammals, forest species, Rift-lake birds, aquatic animals — to the vegetation belt it belongs to, using earlier lessons.
