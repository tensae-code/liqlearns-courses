## Sub lesson 2 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way: Gender equality is argued from South Sudanese lives — an elder who educates his daughters against tradition, and four named women leaders — and peace itself is put in women's hands: the book prints the view that gender equality is a tool that can end South Sudan's civil war. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
