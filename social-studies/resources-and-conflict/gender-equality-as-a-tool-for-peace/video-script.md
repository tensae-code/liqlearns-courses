# Video script — Gender Equality as a Tool for Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 98, teaches: the printed view that gender equality is a main tool for the country's development and can end South Sudan's civil war — women's voices must be heard to give peace a chance, and women given power to heal and reconcile over mistrust and pain.

South Sudan's own way: Gender equality is argued from South Sudanese lives — an elder who educates his daughters against tradition, and four named women leaders — and peace itself is put in women's hands: the book prints the view that gender equality is a tool that can end South Sudan's civil war.
