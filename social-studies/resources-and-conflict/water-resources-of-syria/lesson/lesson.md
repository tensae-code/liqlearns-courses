## Sub lesson 2 — Syria's way — the book's own teaching way

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 9 Geography G9): every unit is taught twice at two scales — the Arab homeland first, then Syria — with parallel lesson pairs (relief, climate, water, soil, agriculture, industry, transport, tourism); Unit 3's framing is pointed: "huge human resources… and weak investment", and the subsoil lessons are titled "hidden treasures".

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 4 Social Studies G4): the whole primary book is one-country geography of Syria — nature, then life (Badia, countryside, city), then work (agriculture, industry, trade, tourism) — with map tools (map symbols, four directions) and a tree-cutting/environment strand built into the opening unit's goals. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
