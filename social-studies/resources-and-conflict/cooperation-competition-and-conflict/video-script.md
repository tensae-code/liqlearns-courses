# Video script — Cooperation Competition and Conflict

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Armenia Grade 8 Social Science student, printed p. 76, teaches: all social interaction takes three forms — cooperation (first learned in the family, joining efforts for a common goal), competition and conflict — shown in daily life and in team sport, where rival teams cooperate inside themselves.

Armenia's own way: the democracy year opens from Armenia's own Constitution — Articles 1–2 ("the Republic of Armenia is a sovereign, democratic, social, rule-of-law state; power belongs to the people") — and every value is then tested against daily-life cases before rights are sorted by category and by holder.
