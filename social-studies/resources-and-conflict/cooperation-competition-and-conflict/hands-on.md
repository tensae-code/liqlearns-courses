# Hands-on — Cooperation Competition and Conflict

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: all social interaction takes three forms — cooperation (first learned in the family, joining efforts for a common goal), competition and conflict — shown in daily life and in team sport, where rival teams cooperate inside themselves. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
