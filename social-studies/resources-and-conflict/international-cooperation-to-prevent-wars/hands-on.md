# Hands-on — International Cooperation to Prevent Wars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## International Cooperation to Prevent Wars
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: States are pressed to sign international agreements preventing the spread of weapons, such as the chemical-weapons and landmine ban conventions; and the international agreements protect children during wars — the Convention on the Rights of the Child forbids bombing civilian areas and forbids recruiting and arming children in wars (its Article 38: no direct part in war under 18, no recruitment under 15, protection and care of children affected by armed conflict). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
