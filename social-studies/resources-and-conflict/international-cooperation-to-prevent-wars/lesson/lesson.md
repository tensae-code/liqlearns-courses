## Sub lesson 1 — International Cooperation to Prevent Wars

Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2 (printed p. 73), teaches: states are pressed to sign international agreements preventing the spread of weapons, such as the chemical-weapons and landmine ban conventions; and the international agreements protect children during wars — the Convention on the Rights of the Child forbids bombing civilian areas and forbids recruiting and arming children in wars (its Article 38: no direct part in war under 18, no recruitment under 15, protection and care of children affected by armed conflict). ## Sub lesson 2 — Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way

Palestine's own way (Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2): civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom.
