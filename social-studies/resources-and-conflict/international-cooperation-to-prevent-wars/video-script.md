The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2, printed p. 73, teaches: states are pressed to sign international agreements preventing the spread of weapons, such as the chemical-weapons and landmine ban conventions; and the international agreements protect children during wars — the Convention on the Rights of the Child forbids bombing civilian areas and forbids recruiting and arming children in wars (its Article 38: no direct part in war under 18, no recruitment under 15, protection and care of children affected by armed conflict).

Palestine's own way: civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom.
