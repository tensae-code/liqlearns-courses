# International Cooperation to Prevent Wars — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: International Cooperation to Prevent Wars — states are pressed to sign international agreements preventing the spread of weapons, such as the chemical-weapons and landmine ban conventions; and the international agreements protect children during wars — the Convention on the Rights of the Child forbids bombing civilian areas and forbids recruiting and arming children in wars (its Article 38: no direct part in war under 18, no recruitment under 15, protection and care of children affected by armed conflict) (any close wording).**
