Meteorological drought: rainfall shortfall.

Trends are regional: worsening in West Africa and the Mediterranean since the 1950s, easing in parts of the Americas and Australia — and sharpened everywhere by climate change..

Responses combine monitoring and early warning, water and crop management, awareness, watershed planning, soil-and-water conservation practices, and herd management.
