# Drought — five kinds of dry — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the five kinds.
**Answer:** Meteorological, hydrological, agricultural, environmental, socioeconomic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do the trends differ by region?
**Answer:** Worsening in West Africa and the Mediterranean; easing in parts of the Americas and Australia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three responses.
**Answer:** Monitoring/early warning, water and crop management, awareness, watershed planning, SWC practices, herd management (any three).
