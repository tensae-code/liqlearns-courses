# Drought — five kinds of dry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rainfall shortfall alone is —
a) meteorological drought  b) agricultural drought  c) socioeconomic drought
**Answer: a) meteorological drought**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil-moisture failure is —
a) agricultural drought  b) hydrological drought  c) environmental drought
**Answer: a) agricultural drought**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Drought has worsened since the 1950s in —
a) West Africa and the Mediterranean  b) the Americas everywhere  c) Australia everywhere
**Answer: a) West Africa and the Mediterranean**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Supply failing demand for fodder and food is —
a) socioeconomic drought  b) meteorological drought  c) hydrological drought
**Answer: a) socioeconomic drought**
