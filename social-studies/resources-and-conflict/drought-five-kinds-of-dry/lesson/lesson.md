# Drought — five kinds of dry

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: a drought for a meteorologist is not the same drought a farmer feels — how many kinds are there? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Five kinds, by profession

Meteorological drought: rainfall shortfall. Hydrological: low flows and levels. Agricultural: soil-moisture failure. Environmental: the three combined. Socioeconomic: supply failing demand for water, fodder, food and energy.

## Sub lesson 2 — Trends around the world

Trends are regional: worsening in West Africa and the Mediterranean since the 1950s, easing in parts of the Americas and Australia — and sharpened everywhere by climate change.

## Sub lesson 3 — The response kit

Responses combine monitoring and early warning, water and crop management, awareness, watershed planning, soil-and-water conservation practices, and herd management. Drought is survived by systems, not by single acts.
