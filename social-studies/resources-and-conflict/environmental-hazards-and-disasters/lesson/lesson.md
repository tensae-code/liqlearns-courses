# Environmental Hazards and Disasters

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Zambia's Grade 8 (Form 2) Geography module teaches environmental hazards and disasters as the third part of its Environment topic, after degradation and protection. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hazards — dangers in the environment

In Zambia, the Geography Teaching Module for Form 2 (Grade 8), Term 3, gives its Environment topic (Topic 2.3) a third sub-topic: Sub-topic 2.3.3, Environmental Hazards and Disasters, at printed p. 16. A hazard is a danger in the environment — something that can cause harm to people, to the land, or to both. Naming the hazards is the knowledge at stake in this sub-topic.

## Sub lesson 2 — Disasters — when a hazard strikes

A hazard is a danger that is there; a disaster is what happens when a hazard strikes and causes serious harm or damage. Zambia's module puts the two together in one sub-topic, and puts that sub-topic after environmental degradation (Sub-topic 2.3.1) and environmental protection (Sub-topic 2.3.2): first how the environment is damaged, then how it is protected, then the hazards that can strike it and the disasters they can become.
