# Environmental Hazards and Disasters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The sub-topic at printed p. 16 teaches —
a) environmental hazards and disasters  b) the parts of a plant  c) the kings of Zambia
**Answer: a) environmental hazards and disasters**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A hazard is —
a) a danger in the environment that can cause harm  b) a kind of tree  c) a school subject
**Answer: a) a danger in the environment that can cause harm**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A disaster is —
a) when a hazard strikes and causes serious harm or damage  b) a danger that has not struck  c) a map of Zambia
**Answer: a) when a hazard strikes and causes serious harm or damage**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Zambia's module, hazards and disasters come —
a) after degradation and protection, as the third sub-topic of the Environment topic  b) before the Environment topic begins  c) in a different book
**Answer: a) after degradation and protection, as the third sub-topic of the Environment topic**
