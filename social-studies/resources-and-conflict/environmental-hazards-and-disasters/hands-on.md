# Hands-on — Environmental Hazards and Disasters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the hazards near you
Walk around your home or school grounds in your mind and write down the dangers in the environment that could cause harm — anything that could hurt people or damage the land. You are naming hazards, the first half of Sub-topic 2.3.3.

## Hazard or disaster?
For each danger you named, write one line: is it still only a hazard, or has it already struck and become a disaster? Write what harm it did or could do. You are telling a hazard and a disaster apart.
