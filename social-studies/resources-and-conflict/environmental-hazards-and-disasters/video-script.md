# Video script — Environmental Hazards and Disasters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A hazard is a danger in the environment — something that can cause harm.

Zambia's Grade 8 Geography module teaches hazards and disasters in its Environment topic.

A disaster is what happens when a hazard strikes and causes serious harm.

First degradation, then protection, then hazards and disasters: that is the module's order.
