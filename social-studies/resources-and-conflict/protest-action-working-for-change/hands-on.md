# Hands-on — Protest Action Working for Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Protest as a way people work for rights and social justice — the right to protest and its responsibilities, and four types (physical, literary, visual, violent) with case studies (the 1981 PSA march, the Mau, letters to the editor, protests around the world). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Samoa's way
Say how Samoa's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
