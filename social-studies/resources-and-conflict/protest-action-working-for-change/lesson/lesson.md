## Sub lesson 2 — Samoa's way — the book's own teaching way

Samoa's own way: Government is taught as Samoa's own Westminster-style parliament first (matai seats, the Individual Voters Roll, its parties and its real 2001 election, problems included), then Japan's Peace-Constitution government as the comparison; rights are taught from the classroom's own rights list outward to the UN and Amnesty. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
