# Video script — Protest Action Working for Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 10 Book 1 (printed p. 48), teaches: Protest as a way people work for rights and social justice — the right to protest and its responsibilities, and four types (physical, literary, visual, violent) with case studies (the 1981 PSA march, the Mau, letters to the editor, protests around the world).

Samoa's own way: Government is taught as Samoa's own Westminster-style parliament first (matai seats, the Individual Voters Roll, its parties and its real 2001 election, problems included), then Japan's Peace-Constitution government as the comparison; rights are taught from the classroom's own rights list outward to the UN and Amnesty.
