# Hands-on — Social Cohesion and Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Promoting the Culture of Peace
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Peaceful talk (listening to others, not shouting, not joining conversations that are not ours) and peaceful school life (working together, helping, working with love, not discouraging others). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Social cohesion and conflict management in our province
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Social cohesion as people living and working together in unity, and managing the conflicts that arise inside it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unity and Co-operation
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unity as living together as one with love for each other; co-operation as doing things together in agreement. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The Importance of National Unity
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unity brings peace and stability; in peace people work hard and create wealth for themselves and their country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Rwanda's way
Say how Rwanda's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
