# Social Cohesion and Living Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Promoting the Culture of Peace — peaceful talk (listening to others, not shouting, not joining conversations that are not ours) and peaceful school life (working together, helping, working with love, not discouraging others) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Social cohesion and conflict management in our province — social cohesion as people living and working together in unity, and managing the conflicts that arise inside it (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Unity and Co-operation — unity as living together as one with love for each other; co-operation as doing things together in agreement (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Importance of National Unity — unity brings peace and stability; in peace people work hard and create wealth for themselves and their country (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Rwanda's way — the books' own teaching way (any close wording).**
