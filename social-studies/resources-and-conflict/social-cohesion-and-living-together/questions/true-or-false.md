# Social Cohesion and Living Together — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: peaceful talk (listening to others, not shouting, not joining conversations that are not ours) and peaceful school life (working together, helping, working with love, not discouraging others).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: social cohesion as people living and working together in unity, and managing the conflicts that arise inside it.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: unity as living together as one with love for each other; co-operation as doing things together in agreement.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part teaches: unity brings peace and stability; in peace people work hard and create wealth for themselves and their country.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Rwanda's student books, named in lesson/sources.md.**
