# Video script — Careless Use — Water Pollution and Shortage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pure water has no smell. No taste. No colour.
Pollution is unwanted things inside it —
a change that harms health, life, activity.
Left alone, that water cannot be used. It can kill.
Shortage is the other trouble.
Little water, used badly, never saved —
animals dry, plants die, people walk away searching.
Guard the purity. Save the little. Both are care.
