# Hands-on — Careless Use — Water Pollution and Shortage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stream inspection
Walk to the nearest stream, tap or well with an adult. List everything in or near the water that should not be there, and where each thing likely came from. That list is a pollution map.

## Saving measure
For one day, measure your household's water in buckets: how many drawn, how many thrown away still usable? Write one saving change the count suggests.
