# Lesson plan — Careless Use — Water Pollution and Shortage

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Resources and Conflict · **Lesson:** Careless Use — Water Pollution and Shortage
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 10 — Resources and Conflict (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md: natural resources, their care, and conflict over resources).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** water is spoiled two ways: pollution — unwanted things present that destroy its natural purity — and shortage, when the little water there is is used without saving until people and animals must migrate for it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What Conservation Means, and climbs from Water, Air, Forest, Wildlife and Soil (People and Resources) into the book's precise pollution definition.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define pollution in the book's words.
2. State water's natural character: without smell, taste or colour — pure.
3. Explain shortage: improper use without saving, until animals and plants die or dry up and people migrate for water.
4. Say what polluted water becomes without pollution-control methods: unusable, and able to kill.

## If the learner is stuck
- Defines pollution as 'dirt' only → the book's definition covers physical, chemical or biological change, in water, air, soil or food, harming health, life or activity.
- Thinks shortage is only drought → the book's shortage is improper use of the little water there is, without saving and with waste.
- Misses migration → shortage's stated end: people and animals migrate in search of water.
