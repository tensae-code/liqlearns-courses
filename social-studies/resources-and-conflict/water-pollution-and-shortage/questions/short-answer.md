# Careless Use — Water Pollution and Shortage — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Write the book's pollution definition in short form.**
**Answer:** An unwanted change in the physical, chemical or biological character of water, air, soil or food that harms the health, life or activity of people and other living things.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) What two failures make shortage worse where water is already little?**
**Answer:** Using it improperly — without saving, and with waste.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two water troubles of this lesson and one end of each.**
**Answer:** Pollution — water becomes unusable and can kill; shortage — animals and plants die or dry up and people and animals migrate for water.
