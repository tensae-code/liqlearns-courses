# Careless Use — Water Pollution and Shortage — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Water pollution is unwanted things present in water that spoil its purity.**
**Answer: True** — that is the book's water-pollution sentence.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Pollution harms only people's health, never other living things.**
**Answer: False** — it harms the health, life or activity of people and other living things.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) In shortage, people and animals may migrate in search of water.**
**Answer: True** — migration is shortage's stated end.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) A full stream always gives a village water it can use.**
**Answer: False** — a polluted stream can be full yet unusable.
