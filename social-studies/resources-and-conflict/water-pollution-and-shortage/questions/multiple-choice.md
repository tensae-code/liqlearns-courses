# Careless Use — Water Pollution and Shortage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Pollution, in the book's definition, is an unwanted change in the character of —**
a) Only water  b) Water, air, soil or food  c) Only factories  d) Rivers in the rainy season
**Answer: b) Water, air, soil or food** — the definition covers water, air, soil and food.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Pure water by nature is —**
a) Salty and blue  b) Without smell, taste or colour  c) Warm and sweet  d) Full of minerals you can see
**Answer: b) Without smell, taste or colour** — no smell, taste or colour is water's natural purity.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Without pollution-control methods, polluted water —**
a) Cleans itself at once  b) Becomes unusable and can kill  c) Becomes drinking water  d) Only smells bad
**Answer: b) Becomes unusable and can kill** — unusable and able to kill is the book's warning.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Shortage, in the book, comes from —**
a) Too much rain  b) Using the little water there is improperly, without saving and with waste  c) Rivers flowing to the sea  d) Cold weather
**Answer: b) Using the little water there is improperly, without saving and with waste** — improper use without saving is the book's shortage.
