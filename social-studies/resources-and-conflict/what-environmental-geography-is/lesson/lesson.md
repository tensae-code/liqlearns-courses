## Sub lesson 1 — The definition of environmental geography and its field of study

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide opens its environmental geography strand (§3.1) by defining the subject itself: environmental geography — what it is, and its field of study, the ground it covers. The strand is given its own definition before any of its topics begin.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — a strand of its own

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Myanmar the year is split into five parallel strands, and environmental geography is a whole strand of its own — after this definition it works through the lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere and biosphere and the environment, and how the earth's four spheres interact.

Myanmar's own way: the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week — physical geography, social geography, environmental geography, neighbouring countries and cartography — each lesson plan built student-centred (Bloom steps, key questions, classroom assessment) around Myanmar and its five neighbours. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
