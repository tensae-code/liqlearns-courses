# Hands-on — Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Destination banner
Make a banner poster: 'AGENDA 2063' across the top, the five destination words beneath, and one small drawing for each. Hang it and read it aloud to a partner, explaining one word each in turn.

## Flagship tracker
Start a notebook page: 'Flagships I meet'. Each time you hear of a continent-scale African project (a railway, a power pool, a trade area), log it with the destination word it serves.
