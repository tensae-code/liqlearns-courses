# Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agenda 2063 travels under Pan-Africanism and the African Renaissance.
**Answer: True** — its named guiding ideas.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Agenda 2063 is a one-year plan.
**Answer: False** — it is a fifty-year horizon, to 2063.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A strategic framework is the frame national plans are meant to fit into.
**Answer: True** — not a wish list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The OAU came after the African Union.
**Answer: False** — OAU → AU: the Union succeeded the Organisation.
