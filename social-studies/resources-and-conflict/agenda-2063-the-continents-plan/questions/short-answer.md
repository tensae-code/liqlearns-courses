# Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recite the plan's five destination words.
**Answer:** Unity, self-determination, freedom, progress and collective prosperity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a flagship programme, in one sentence?
**Answer:** A named, continent-scale project that carries Agenda 2063's goals into real delivery on the ground.
