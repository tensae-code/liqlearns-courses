# Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agenda 2063 is —
a) a UN plan for Asia  b) the African Union's master plan and strategic framework  c) a single country's budget  d) a trade tariff
**Answer: b) the African Union's master plan and strategic framework** — the continent's own framework.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The plan's transformation is described as —
a) inclusive and sustainable  b) secret and rapid  c) military only  d) urban only
**Answer: a) inclusive and sustainable** — its own framing words.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT among the five destination words?
a) Unity  b) Self-determination  c) Collective prosperity  d) Isolation
**Answer: d) Isolation** — unity, self-determination, freedom, progress, collective prosperity.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The lineage of the continental house runs —
a) AU → OAU  b) OAU → AU  c) ECOWAS → AU  d) UN → AU
**Answer: b) OAU → AU** — liberation's organisation became the Union.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Flagship programmes are —
a) flags for summits  b) named continent-scale projects that deliver the framework  c) school subjects  d) border posts
**Answer: b) named continent-scale projects that deliver the framework** — the vision's delivery arm.
