# Lesson plan — Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Resources and Conflict · **Lesson:** Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Major Economic and Cultural Activities of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Agenda 2063 is the African Union's master plan for the continent — a strategic framework for inclusive, sustainable transformation toward unity, self-determination, freedom, progress and collective prosperity under Pan-Africanism and the African Renaissance, delivered through flagship programmes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands in Unit 5 after Africa's economic hurdles — the plan is taught as the continent's answer to them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **What Agenda 2063 is** — The AU's master plan and strategic framework.
2. **Lineage and delivery — OAU to AU, flagship programmes** — Where the plan comes from and how it moves.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Dependency across Africa's regions — the development load the plan must lift. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what Agenda 2063 is: AU master plan, strategic framework, 2013–2063 horizon.
2. Recite its destination: unity, self-determination, freedom, progress, collective prosperity.
3. Name its guiding ideas: Pan-Africanism and the African Renaissance.
4. Trace OAU→AU and explain flagship programmes.
