# Hands-on — Sharing The East Sea Peacefully

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Topic 11.1 (part): Peaceful cooperation in exploiting the East Sea — fisheries agreements (the Gulf of Tonkin with China, Gulf of Thailand arrangements, anti-IUU rules), joint oil and gas development (Vietnam–Malaysia 1992; the 2005 joint seismic survey with China and the Philippines), renewable energy, marine tourism, protecting the sea's resources (the 2002 DOC and the COC under negotiation, UNCLOS 1982), sea transport and defence cooperation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
