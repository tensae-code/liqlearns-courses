Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11* (printed pp. 16–26), teaches: Topic 11.1 (part): Peaceful cooperation in exploiting the East Sea — fisheries agreements (the Gulf of Tonkin with China, Gulf of Thailand arrangements, anti-IUU rules), joint oil and gas development (Vietnam–Malaysia 1992; the 2005 joint seismic survey with China and the Philippines), renewable energy, marine tourism, protecting the sea's resources (the 2002 DOC and the COC under negotiation, UNCLOS 1982), sea transport and defence cooperation.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11): the Grade 11 electives take three deep dives that all circle home — sharing the Mekong and the East Sea with neighbours, the world's tourism and the jobs it offers Vietnamese school-leavers, and the Fourth Industrial Revolution remaking work.
