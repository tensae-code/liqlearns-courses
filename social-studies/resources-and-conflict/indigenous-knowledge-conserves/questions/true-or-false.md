# Indigenous knowledge conserves — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is gathered by observation over generations.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A used and valued medicine plant gives its forest no defenders.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Conservation that ignores indigenous knowledge wastes its built-in protection.
**Answer: True**
