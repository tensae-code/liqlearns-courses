# Indigenous knowledge conserves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous knowledge is —
a) deep accumulated knowledge of biodiversity and its uses  b) knowledge printed in new textbooks  c) knowledge held only by doctors
**Answer: a) deep accumulated knowledge of biodiversity and its uses**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Plant medicine is vital especially where —
a) cities are large  b) rainfall is high  c) clinics and health professionals are few
**Answer: c) clinics and health professionals are few**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Indigenous practices themselves —
a) have no effect on resources  b) conserve natural resources  c) always damage resources
**Answer: b) conserve natural resources**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Plant remedies are named as —
a) locally available, cheap and accepted  b) expensive and imported  c) rarely used
**Answer: a) locally available, cheap and accepted**
