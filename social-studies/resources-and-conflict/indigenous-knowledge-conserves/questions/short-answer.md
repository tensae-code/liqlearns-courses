# Indigenous knowledge conserves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is indigenous knowledge, in this lesson?
**Answer:** The deep, accumulated knowledge communities hold of biodiversity and its uses, gathered over generations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is plant medicine the lead case?
**Answer:** It is locally available, cheap and accepted — and vital where clinics and professionals are few; its use gives the resource a protecting value.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do indigenous practices conserve?
**Answer:** Communities that know what their biodiversity does for them guard it — the practices themselves protect the resources.
