# Video script — Indigenous knowledge conserves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Indigenous knowledge is the deep, accumulated knowledge African communities hold of biodiversity and its uses — which plants heal, which feed, which poison; which grounds rest and which bear.

The lead case is plant medicine.

The argument completes itself: indigenous practices themselves conserve natural resources.
