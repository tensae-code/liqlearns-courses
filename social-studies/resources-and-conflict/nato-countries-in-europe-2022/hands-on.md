# Hands-on — NATO Countries in Europe 2022

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the alliance on the map
On any map of Europe, mark the countries the lesson's map covers and say how a tactile version lets a blind pupil find the same thing by hand.

## Make the keys
Write the two keys a tactile alliance map needs — what the countries are and what the marks mean — as the pack's two Braille keys do.
