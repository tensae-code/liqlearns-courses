## Sub lesson 1 — A tactile map of the NATO countries in Europe 2022

Sweden's SPSM pack closes its Ukraine–Russia teaching with Europe's alliance map: a tactile map of the NATO countries in Europe in 2022, read by hand by blind and visually impaired pupils.

## Sub lesson 2 — Two Braille keys

The map carries two Braille keys, naming what the hand finds. Placed after the maps of Ukraine and Russia, it shows where the alliance's European countries lie in the same conflict the pack teaches.

Sweden's own way: current-events Geography for blind and visually impaired pupils — the Ukraine–Russia conflict taught entirely by touch: raised-line flags, emblems and maps with Braille keys, so the countries, their symbols and their place in Europe are learned by hand.
