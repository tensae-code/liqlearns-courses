# The Green Legacy movement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Green Legacy was launched in —
a) 2010  b) 2023  c) 2019
**Answer: c) 2019**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It was launched by —
a) a foreign agency  b) Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed  c) the Ministry alone unnamed
**Answer: b) Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A claimed wider benefit is —
a) fossil-fuel import dependency cut by about one-third  b) free fuel for all  c) an end to drought
**Answer: a) fossil-fuel import dependency cut by about one-third**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Among the aims is —
a) building more roads  b) expanding the Ogaden  c) preventing environment-related conflicts
**Answer: c) preventing environment-related conflicts**
