# The Green Legacy movement — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four components.
**Answer: Agroforestry; forest-sector development; greening/renewal of urban areas; integrated water and soil management.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the green-shoots project areas (any three).
**Answer: Any three of: pocket parks, tree planting, renewable energy, re-using and recycling resources, adaptation.**
