# The Green Legacy movement

## Do it first

The pupil book gives the planting. The teacher’s book gives the whole programme — its launch, its four components, its claimed benefits. Read the full account.

## Sub lesson 1 — Launch and components (teacher guide — evidence)

The guide states the fullest account: the Green Legacy is a government programme launched in 2019 by Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed to curb climate change and deforestation. Its components are agroforestry, forest-sector development, greening and renewal of urban areas, and integrated water and soil management.

[Picture 21056 — a Green Legacy component wheel — agroforestry, forests, urban greening, water and soil]

## Sub lesson 2 — Aims and claimed benefits (teacher guide — evidence)

Its aims: restore soil and forest cover, cut El Niño effects, reduce greenhouse-gas emissions while safeguarding growth, build climate resilience, intensify climate-smart agriculture, improve food security, prevent environment-related conflicts and ensure gender equality. Claimed wider benefits include a more secure electricity supply, and energy efficiency cutting fossil-fuel import dependency by about one-third, plus public-health and savings gains. Its "green shoots of recovery" project areas include pocket parks, tree planting, renewable energy, re-using and recycling resources, and adaptation.
