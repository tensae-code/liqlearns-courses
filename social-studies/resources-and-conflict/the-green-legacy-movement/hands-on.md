# Hands-on — The Green Legacy movement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Component wheel
Draw the four-component wheel — agroforestry, forest development, urban greening, water-soil management — with one real local example in each quarter, or an honest "none here yet".

## Claim check
Take one claimed benefit — fossil-fuel import dependency cut by about a third. With a partner, list what would have to happen for that claim to come true, step by step.
