## Sub lesson 1 — Tierra y territorio: the indigenous peoples' integral view of land, territory and the community of life

Bolivia's Grade 10 student book, the *Texto de Aprendizaje — Ciencias Sociales, Secundaria 4* (printed p. 89), teaches: tierra y territorio: the indigenous peoples' integral view of land, territory and the community of life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way

Bolivia's own way (Grade 10, Secundaria 4): Secundaria 4 is the nineteenth-century history year, and its geography is the geography of what Bolivia lost and what it lives from: the Atacama Litoral and the sea it no longer has, Potosí's silver and the tin north, the Amazon rubber boom, and a closing resources unit that runs from natural-resource classification to Vivir Bien and sustainable development. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
