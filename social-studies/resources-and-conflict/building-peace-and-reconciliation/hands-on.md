# Hands-on — Building Peace and Reconciliation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 3 Building peace and reconciliation in conflict areas
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the ten ways — dialogue, civic education, involving peacekeeping missions such as the UN and African Union, involving the judicial and court system, incorporating peace education in the curriculum, celebrating successes in peace and non-violence, a personal commitment to non-violence, speaking out against prejudice and discrimination, reporting offenders to teachers, and training in mental health and first aid. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
