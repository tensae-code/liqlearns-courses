# The vicious cycle — poverty and a damaged environment — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define the two terms the nexus joins.
**Answer:** Poverty: lack of capital assets for a livelihood. Environment: the renewable and non-renewable resource base.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trace one full turn of the cycle.
**Answer:** Poverty → overexploitation → degradation → deeper poverty.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must poverty policy be joined with?
**Answer:** Environmental care (mainstreaming).
