# The vicious cycle — poverty and a damaged environment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Poverty is defined as lack of —
a) capital assets for a livelihood  b) neighbours  c) markets only
**Answer: a) capital assets for a livelihood**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the cycle, poverty drives —
a) overexploitation of daily resources  b) saving  c) reforestation
**Answer: a) overexploitation of daily resources**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Degradation in the cycle does what to poverty?
a) deepens it  b) ends it  c) ignores it
**Answer: a) deepens it**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Poverty policy must be mainstreamed with —
a) environmental care  b) trade law  c) tourism
**Answer: a) environmental care**
