# Video script — The vicious cycle — poverty and a damaged environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Poverty is lack of the whole range of capital assets for a livelihood.

Poverty drives people to overexploit the resources they live on day to day — the only capital they can reach.

Because the two are locked, poverty policy must be 'mainstreamed' with environmental care: a programme that fights poverty while ignoring the resource base feeds the cycle it means to break..
