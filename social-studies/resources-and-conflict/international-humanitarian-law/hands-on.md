## International Humanitarian Law
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: International humanitarian law (the law of war, or of armed conflicts) is the set of basic principles and legal rules aiming to limit the effects armed conflicts cause to people who do not take part in the fighting (civilians — men, women, the elderly, children) or who can no longer take part (the wounded and prisoners); the protection extends to private property such as homes and cultural places and to public property that is not a military target — places of worship such as mosques and churches, museums and schools — and to the medical teams that give humanitarian aid; its aim is to guarantee respect for human rights and to lessen the woes, destruction and suffering of war. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
