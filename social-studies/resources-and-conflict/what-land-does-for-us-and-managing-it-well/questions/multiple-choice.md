# What land does for us — and managing it well — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One function of land is to act as a —
a) waste sink  b) river  c) market town
**Answer: a) waste sink**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Habitat and ecosystem services belong to land as —
a) environment  b) economic asset  c) legal object
**Answer: a) environment**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Land management balances exploitation against —
a) regeneration  b) imports  c) taxation
**Answer: a) regeneration**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Management builds on indigenous knowledge and —
a) local education  b) foreign models only  c) machines only
**Answer: a) local education**
