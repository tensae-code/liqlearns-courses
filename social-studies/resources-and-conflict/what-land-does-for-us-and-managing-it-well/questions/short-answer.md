# What land does for us — and managing it well — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three functions of land.
**Answer:** Production, waste sink, economic asset, cultural asset, environment (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Define land management.
**Answer:** Using and protecting land to balance exploitation against regeneration.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two management tools.
**Answer:** Farming practice, tree planting, controlled grazing, terracing, check dams, cleaner energy (any two).
