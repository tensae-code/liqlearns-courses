# What land does for us — and managing it well — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Terracing and check dams are land-management tools.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Good management means using land as fast as possible.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Controlled grazing is a management tool.
