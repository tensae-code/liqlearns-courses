# Video script — What land does for us — and managing it well

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Land produces: crops, grazing, timber.

Land management is using and protecting land so that exploitation stays balanced against regeneration — taking what the land can replace, and helping it replace it..

The tools are farming practice, tree planting, controlled grazing, terracing and check dams, and cleaner energy — all built on indigenous knowledge and local education, not imposed over them..
