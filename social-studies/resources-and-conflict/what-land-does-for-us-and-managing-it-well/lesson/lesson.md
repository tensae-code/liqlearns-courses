# What land does for us — and managing it well

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: what does a field do besides grow food? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The functions of land

Land produces: crops, grazing, timber. Land receives our waste as a sink. Land is an economic asset that can be held, rented and sold, and a cultural asset a people belongs to — and alongside all these, land-as-environment gives habitat and ecosystem services.

[Picture 27025 — a terraced, tree-planted hillside with a check dam in its gully]

## Sub lesson 2 — What management means

Land management is using and protecting land so that exploitation stays balanced against regeneration — taking what the land can replace, and helping it replace it.

## Sub lesson 3 — The manager's tools

The tools are farming practice, tree planting, controlled grazing, terracing and check dams, and cleaner energy — all built on indigenous knowledge and local education, not imposed over them.
