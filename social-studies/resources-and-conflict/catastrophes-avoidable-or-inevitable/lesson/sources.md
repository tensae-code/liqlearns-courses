Brazil's own way: No chapters in the old style — 17 "Folhas" (sheets), each opening with a provocative question ("Could Brazil be different?", "Do you take poison?"), grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
