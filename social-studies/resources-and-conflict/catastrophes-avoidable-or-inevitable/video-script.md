# Video script — Catastrophes — Avoidable or Inevitable?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Folha 16 asks Catástrofes são evitáveis ou inevitáveis? / Are catastrophes avoidable or inevitable? The question refuses a single easy answer: the learner must sort catastrophes, and parts of catastrophes, by whether they could have been avoided.

The avoidable half is the lesson's working half: a catastrophe is avoidable where human choices and preparation could have prevented it or lessened it, and inevitable only where they could not. Judging which is which, case by case, is this lesson's one thing.
