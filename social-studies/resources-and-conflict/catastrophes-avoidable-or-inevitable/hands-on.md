# Hands-on — Catastrophes — Avoidable or Inevitable?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort a catastrophe
Name one catastrophe you have heard of and write which parts of it were avoidable and which were inevitable, as Folha 16 asks.

## Prevent one
Choose the avoidable part from your first activity and write what could have been done beforehand to prevent or lessen it.
