# Video script — Protected Areas of Ecuador

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 5 closes its geography with care for the land: las áreas protegidas del Ecuador — the protected areas — national parks and reserves set aside in every region. The placements name the book's worked set: Cayambe Coca, El Ángel, Limoncocha, the Galápagos Marine Reserve, Arenillas and Machalilla. The lesson teaches where each protected area sits and what it guards, inside the book's framing of global warming and climate change and local strategies to reduce their effects.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the 2024 Grade 5 book is History-led (five of eight sections are national history, from the roots of society to the Republic); Geography is concentrated at the end, where the country is re-read spatially — Andean relief and rivers, natural regions and climatic floors, population diversity and migration, territorial division, provincial products, and the protected-area system taught together with climate-change action. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
