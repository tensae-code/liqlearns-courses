# Hands-on — Peace Equity and Justice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 8 La paz, un valor en permanente construcción
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Peace is a value under permanent construction; the UN Charter's Article 1 sets the purposes — maintain international peace and security, suppress aggression, settle by peaceful means; peace also means tranquillity, and it implies equity and justice — a people suffering inequality and injustice has its peace at risk; Central America's internal wars cost thousands of lives, and the region holds two Nobel Peace laureates (Óscar Arias 1987, Rigoberta Menchú); Gandhi: there is no way to peace, peace is the way. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Anexo: Decálogo para crecer en paz
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A ten-point decalogue for growing in peace, to be photocopied and posted in the classroom — love yourself, value your friends, find what unites you with others above what separates you, respect others' opinions to feed dialogue, learn to listen, finish your tasks well, fulfil your responsibilities because others need it, work in teams ("nobody knows more than everyone together"), share your things, and strive for peace inside yourself so peace exists around you. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 8, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Peace is a value born inside us, fed daily by family and church, and is the fruit of understanding and listening to others' needs before our own; a world of peace requires equal opportunities and respect for everyone's fundamental rights; at home there is peace when each lives in peace with the others — no shouting, complaints, criticism or lies — building family relations on sincerity, disinterested love, calm, affection and generosity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
