# Peace Equity and Justice — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: peace is a value under permanent construction; the UN Charter's Article 1 sets the purposes — maintain international peace and security, suppress aggression, settle by peaceful means; peace also means tranquillity, and it implies equity and justice — a people suffering inequality and injustice has its peace at risk; Central America's internal wars cost thousands of lives, and the region holds two Nobel Peace laureates (Óscar Arias 1987, Rigoberta Menchú); Gandhi: there is no way to peace, peace is the way.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: a ten-point decalogue for growing in peace, to be photocopied and posted in the classroom — love yourself, value your friends, find what unites you with others above what separates you, respect others' opinions to feed dialogue, learn to listen, finish your tasks well, fulfil your responsibilities because others need it, work in teams ("nobody knows more than everyone together"), share your things, and strive for peace inside yourself so peace exists around you.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: peace is a value born inside us, fed daily by family and church, and is the fruit of understanding and listening to others' needs before our own; a world of peace requires equal opportunities and respect for everyone's fundamental rights; at home there is peace when each lives in peace with the others — no shouting, complaints, criticism or lies — building family relations on sincerity, disinterested love, calm, affection and generosity.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it also stands on Honduras's teacher guide(s), named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
