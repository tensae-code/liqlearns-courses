# Adapting when you can't modify — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Autonomous adaptation is —
a) spontaneous — what people do on their own from experience  b) ordered by government  c) done only by engineers
**Answer: a) spontaneous — what people do on their own from experience**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Building on high ground against floods is an example of —
a) modifying the river  b) determinism  c) adapting when you can't modify
**Answer: c) adapting when you can't modify**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Adaptation is —
a) the same as modification  b) the second half of the human–environment relationship  c) surrender
**Answer: b) the second half of the human–environment relationship**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Using land slope for irrigation —
a) takes the environment's own shape as the tool  b) flattens the land first  c) needs a dam always
**Answer: a) takes the environment's own shape as the tool**
