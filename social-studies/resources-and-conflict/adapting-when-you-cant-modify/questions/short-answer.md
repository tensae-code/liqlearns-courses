# Adapting when you can't modify — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two kinds of adaptation.
**Answer:** Autonomous (spontaneous) adaptation and planned adaptation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's four worked adaptations.
**Answer:** Using land slope for irrigation; building on high ground against floods; thin clothes for heat; warm clothes for cold.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** When do humans adapt rather than modify?
**Answer:** Where the environment cannot be modified — they adjust the human side of the meeting instead.
