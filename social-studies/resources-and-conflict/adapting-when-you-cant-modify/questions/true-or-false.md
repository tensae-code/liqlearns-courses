# Adapting when you can't modify — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Planned adaptation is deliberate and organised in advance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Thin clothes for heat and warm clothes for cold are adaptations.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** If people adapt, they never modify anything.
**Answer: False**
