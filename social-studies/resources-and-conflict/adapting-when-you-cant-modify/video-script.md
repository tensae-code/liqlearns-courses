# Video script — Adapting when you can't modify

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Adaptation sorts into two kinds.

The book works its examples plainly: using land slope for irrigation — taking the environment's own shape as the tool; building on high ground against floods; thin clothes for heat; warm clothes for cold.

Adaptation is not surrender: it is the second half of the human–environment relationship.
