# Video script — The Need to Live Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 3 P3 Social Studies SB, printed p. 45, teaches: it is good to live peacefully with others — living together keeps a society safe from enemies and dangerous animals, and good relations with neighbours rest on respecting each other.

South Sudan's own way: Conflict is taught from the child's own ground — its named causes are theft, refusing other people's religions and corruption; its solvers are the payam chief and the village elder; and the flag itself carries the country's peace story, its two white meanings tied to the 1972 Addis Ababa and 2005 Naivasha agreements.
