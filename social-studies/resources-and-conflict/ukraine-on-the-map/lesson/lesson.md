## Sub lesson 1 — A tactile map of Ukraine 2022

Sweden's SPSM pack teaches the Ukraine–Russia conflict of 2022 entirely by touch, for blind and visually impaired pupils. Its map of Ukraine is a raised-line tactile map the pupil reads by hand.

## Sub lesson 2 — Three Braille keys — capital, cities, rivers, the Black Sea and neighbours

The map carries three Braille keys, which name what the hand finds: the capital, the cities, the rivers, the Black Sea and the neighbouring countries. The keys are part of the map — without them the raised lines cannot be read.

Sweden's own way: current-events Geography for blind and visually impaired pupils — the Ukraine–Russia conflict taught entirely by touch: raised-line flags, emblems and maps with Braille keys, so the countries, their symbols and their place in Europe are learned by hand.
