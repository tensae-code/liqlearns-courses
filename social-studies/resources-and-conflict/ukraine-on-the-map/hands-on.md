# Hands-on — Ukraine on the Map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the map by hand
With eyes closed or looking away, trace a raised-line or string outline map of a country and name what your hand finds, the way the tactile map is read.

## Make a key
Write a short key for your outline map — capital, cities, rivers, sea, neighbours — as the three Braille keys do for Ukraine's map.
