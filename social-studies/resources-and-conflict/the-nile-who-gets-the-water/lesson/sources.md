Book discrepancy carried exactly as the placements flag it: the student book's Unit Three summary gives Egypt's 1959 Nile share as 58.5 BCM, where its own treaty table in section 3.6 gives 55.5 BCM. This lesson teaches the table's figure, 55.5 BCM, and names the summary's 58.5 BCM as the book's internal inconsistency; the teacher guide does not resolve it.
