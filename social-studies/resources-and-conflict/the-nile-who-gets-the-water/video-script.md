# Video script — The Nile — who gets the water?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Per-capita water use stands at about 1,666 cubic metres in Ethiopia, 1,981 in Sudan and 830 in Egypt — below the 1,700 benchmark.

The 1929 exchange of notes recognised Egypt's 'historic rights'; proposals for Owen Falls and Aswan followed.

The story runs on to the Cooperative Framework Agreement, creating a permanent Nile Basin Commission — the attempt to replace division-by-exclusion with equitable sharing..
