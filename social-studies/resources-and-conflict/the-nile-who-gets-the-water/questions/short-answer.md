# The Nile — who gets the water? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which country uses the most water per person of the three?
**Answer:** Sudan, at about 1,981 cubic metres.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What shares did the 1959 treaty give?
**Answer:** 55.5 BCM to Egypt and 18.5 BCM to Sudan.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is Ethiopia's absence from 1959 the lesson's point?
**Answer:** The source of over 85% of the water was excluded from its division.
