# The Nile — who gets the water? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Over what share of the Nile's water flows from Ethiopia?
a) 85%  b) 55%  c) 18.5%
**Answer: a) 85%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Egypt's per-capita use in the lesson is about —
a) 830 m³  b) 1,981 m³  c) 1,666 m³
**Answer: a) 830 m³**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1959 treaty divided the water between —
a) Egypt and Sudan  b) Egypt and Ethiopia  c) Sudan and Ethiopia
**Answer: a) Egypt and Sudan**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Under the 1959 treaty, Egypt's share was —
a) 55.5 billion cubic metres  b) 18.5 billion cubic metres  c) 85 billion cubic metres
**Answer: a) 55.5 billion cubic metres**
