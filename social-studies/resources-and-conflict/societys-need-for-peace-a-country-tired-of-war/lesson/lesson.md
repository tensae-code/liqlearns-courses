## Sub lesson 1 — Societys Need for Peace a Country Tired of War

Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 7, teaches: peace is reconciliation, calm and life far from war; after years of war's damage to country and people, peace is a vital need and an Islamic and human duty of every citizen — with peace come better relations, schooling for children and youth studying in calm. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: society is taught as a ladder of belonging — family, school, village, district, city, country, and the wider Islamic umma — and the state appears as the servant of that society: ministries named one by one, each with its service, and peace named the first need of a country that has known decades of war. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
