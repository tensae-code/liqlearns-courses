# Video script — Societys Need for Peace a Country Tired of War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 7, teaches: peace is reconciliation, calm and life far from war; after years of war's damage to country and people, peace is a vital need and an Islamic and human duty of every citizen — with peace come better relations, schooling for children and youth studying in calm.

Afghanistan's own way: society is taught as a ladder of belonging — family, school, village, district, city, country, and the wider Islamic umma — and the state appears as the servant of that society: ministries named one by one, each with its service, and peace named the first need of a country that has known decades of war.
