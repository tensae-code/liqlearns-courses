# Hands-on — Conservation — What Conservation Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Photograph hunt (in words)
Find the two works the book photographs, in your own area: a check dam or gully work, and planting on damaged land. If one does not exist, write where it is needed most and why.

## Seven-technique hillside
Draw a hillside and place all seven soil techniques on it where each belongs — terraces on the slope, a shelter belt against the wind, a check dam in the gully. Label every one.
