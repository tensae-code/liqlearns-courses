# Conservation — What Conservation Means — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Conservation includes using resources properly, in the wanted amount, for a long time.**
**Answer: True** — that is working definition four.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Conservation means never using any resource at all.**
**Answer: False** — conservation is wise, protected use — including proper use for human comfort.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Illegal hunting is one of the factors harming wildlife in the guide's key.**
**Answer: True** — deforestation, illegal hunting, overgrazing, forest burning, pollution and climate change are the six.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Forests matter only for timber, in the guide's key.**
**Answer: False** — the guide keys soil productivity, erosion control, wildlife food and shelter, climate control, oxygen, CO₂ sink and water recycling.
