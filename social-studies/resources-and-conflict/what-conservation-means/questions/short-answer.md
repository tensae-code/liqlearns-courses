# Conservation — What Conservation Means — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Write two working definitions of conservation.**
**Answer:** Any two of: protecting and guarding from danger; defending from spoilage, damage, misuse, destruction and waste; keeping protected for future use; using properly in the wanted amount for a long time; keeping a good relation so resources yield without interruption or decline.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) List the seven soil conservation techniques.**
**Answer:** Terracing, afforestation, crop rotation, reforestation, shelter belt, check dams and strip cropping.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) What is water conservation, and what does its proper use minimise?**
**Answer:** The wise use, control and development of water bodies and prevention of pollution; proper use minimises drought and water shortage and reduces political conflict.
