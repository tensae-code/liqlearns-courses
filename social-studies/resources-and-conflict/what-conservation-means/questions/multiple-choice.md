# Conservation — What Conservation Means — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Which is one of the book's working definitions of conservation?**
a) Using resources as fast as possible  b) Defending resources from spoilage, damage, misuse, destruction and waste  c) Selling resources abroad  d) Fencing people out of all land forever
**Answer: b) Defending resources from spoilage, damage, misuse, destruction and waste** — defending from spoilage and waste is definition two.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) The book's two photographs compare —**
a) Two forests  b) A check-dam/gully work and vegetation planted on damaged land  c) Two rivers  d) A mine and a farm
**Answer: b) A check-dam/gully work and vegetation planted on damaged land** — those are the two pictured works.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which is NOT one of the seven soil techniques?**
a) Terracing  b) Shelter belt  c) Strip cropping  d) Overgrazing
**Answer: d) Overgrazing** — overgrazing harms soil and wildlife; it is no technique.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Sustainable use, by the guide's rate rule, is use that —**
a) Exceeds replacement slightly  b) Does not exceed the resource's capacity for replacement  c) Ignores replacement  d) Applies only to water
**Answer: b) Does not exceed the resource's capacity for replacement** — the rate rule is the guide's definition.
