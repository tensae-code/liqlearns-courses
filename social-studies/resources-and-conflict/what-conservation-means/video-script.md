# Video script — Conservation — What Conservation Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

What is conservation? Five voices, one idea.
Guard resources from danger.
Defend them from spoilage, misuse and waste.
Keep them protected for the future.
Use them properly — the wanted amount, for a long time.
Keep a good relation, so they yield without decline.
Soil has seven techniques. Count them on the hillside.
Water, forest, wildlife — each has its keeping.
And one rule for all: never take faster than it replaces.
