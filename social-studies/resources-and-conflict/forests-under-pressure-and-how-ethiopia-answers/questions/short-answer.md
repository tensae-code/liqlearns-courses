# Forests under pressure — and how Ethiopia answers — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the growing population need more of, that the forest paid for?
**Answer: Fuelwood, farm land and timber.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three answer fronts.
**Answer: Forest protection, the conservation toolkit, and mass planting (Green Legacy).**


**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three drivers of the world's forest loss and two remedies that mirror Ethiopia's practice.
**Answer:** Drivers (any three): plantation agriculture, ranching, biofuels, mining, urbanisation, trade. Remedies (any two): afforestation/reforestation like the Green Legacy, soil-and-water conservation, alternative energy, bylaws, participatory forest management, watershed protection.
