# Forests under pressure — and how Ethiopia answers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forest cover in the early 1950s was about —
a) 36%  b) 3.6%  c) 16%
**Answer: c) 16%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** By the early 1980s it was about —
a) 10%  b) 3.6%  c) 16%
**Answer: b) 3.6%**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One answer front is —
a) community forest protection with strict legal measures  b) importing all timber  c) banning farming
**Answer: a) community forest protection with strict legal measures**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mass planting in the answers is —
a) the sickle harvest  b) resettlement  c) the Green Legacy
**Answer: c) the Green Legacy**
