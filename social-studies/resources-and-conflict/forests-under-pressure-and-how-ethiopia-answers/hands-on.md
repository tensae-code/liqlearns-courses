# Hands-on — Forests under pressure — and how Ethiopia answers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shrink chart
Draw two Ethiopia outlines; shade 16% of the first green and 3.6% of the second. The tiny second shading is the lesson.

## Answer matching
Match each pressure — fuelwood cutting, farm clearing, timber taking — to the answer front that meets it, and defend the match to a partner.
