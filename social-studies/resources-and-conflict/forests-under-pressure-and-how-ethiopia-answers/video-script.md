# Video script — Forests under pressure — and how Ethiopia answers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

1950s: forests on about 16% of Ethiopia.
1980s: 3.6%. The green drained away.
More mouths, more fuel, more farms — the forest paid.
The answer: protect, conserve, plant.
