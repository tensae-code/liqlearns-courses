# Soil under pressure — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What makes a resource common-pool?
**Answer:** It is open to all, with nobody effectively restraining use.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three ways soil is depleted.
**Answer:** Nutrient loss, topsoil removal, crusting, compaction, pollution, salinity (any three).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two remedies.
**Answer:** Proper use, erosion-control structures, manure, natural fertilisers (any two).
