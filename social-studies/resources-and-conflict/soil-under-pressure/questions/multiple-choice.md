# Soil under pressure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil, water and forests are under greatest pressure as —
a) common-pool resources  b) rare resources  c) stock resources
**Answer: a) common-pool resources**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** About how much of the world's arable land has erosion destroyed?
a) a third  b) a tenth  c) almost all
**Answer: a) a third**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Crusting and compaction are —
a) physical forms of depletion  b) chemical forms  c) biological forms
**Answer: a) physical forms of depletion**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A remedy for soil depletion is —
a) manure and natural fertilisers  b) more ploughing downhill  c) burning stubble
**Answer: a) manure and natural fertilisers**
