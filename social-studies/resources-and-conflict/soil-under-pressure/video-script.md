# Video script — Soil under pressure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soil, water and forests are the renewable resources under greatest pressure, because they are common-pool resources — open to all, owned in effect by nobody, and so used by everybody without restraint..

Soil's depletion comes in physical, chemical and biological forms: nutrient loss, topsoil removal, crusting and compaction, pollution and salinity.

The remedies are proper use of the land, erosion-control structures, manure and natural fertilisers — feeding the soil while holding it in place..
