# Soil under pressure

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: how can soil — which looks endless — be running out? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — A resource open to all

Soil, water and forests are the renewable resources under greatest pressure, because they are common-pool resources — open to all, owned in effect by nobody, and so used by everybody without restraint.

## Sub lesson 2 — How soil is depleted

Soil's depletion comes in physical, chemical and biological forms: nutrient loss, topsoil removal, crusting and compaction, pollution and salinity. About a third of the world's arable land has already been destroyed by erosion.

## Sub lesson 3 — The remedies

The remedies are proper use of the land, erosion-control structures, manure and natural fertilisers — feeding the soil while holding it in place.
