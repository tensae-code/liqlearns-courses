Angola's own way (Grade 6): a resources-and-environment year — every world topic (air, water, soils, forests) is closed with the Angolan case: Angola's fresh-water use, Angola's farm land, Angola's forest areas. That way is stocked here as the way Angola teaches this thing.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 11 Geography): the standalone upper-secondary Geography book is a human-impact course — people reshaping the earth, people destroying natural resources, the sea, pollution of air/water/noise/waste, economic globalisation, city and countryside, and a world of uneven development.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 13 Geography): The final year integrates everything at advanced level (plate tectonics to urban models, largely from world texts such as Waugh) and then lands it in Chapter 8, South Pacific Islands Geography — Oceania's geology, island types, development prospects and regionalism.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 10 Geografija 1 (gimnazija)): the gimnazija first year is systematic physical geography first (Earth's interior, rocks, landforms, weather and climate, soils, vegetation, waters), then human geography (population, settlements, farming, industry and energy, transport, tourism), closing on sustainable development and spatial planning — with Slovenian cases (earthquake risk in Slovenia, the Port of Koper) set inside the world topics.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): at secondary level Social Science splits into five strands — Geography, History, Political Science, Economics, Environment. The Geography strand is a weather-and-climate skills course (instruments, data, graphs — with Port Moresby and Kokopo data) moving to world climate/vegetation, migration, and land use; the Environment strand carries Earth systems, resources and sustainability. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 6): farming taught historically first — the Neolithic revolution, the Iron civilisation, and what the Industrial Revolution gave agriculture — then agrarian space and its natural/human factors, farming systems by development level, and the tropical systems (dryland, oasis, monsoon rice) before modern farming and livestock.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies): Grade 9 returns to Iraq for the fullest treatment yet — the same national geography as Grade 5 but measured: the plain is 24% and about 600 km long, the desert plateau about 55%, the desert plant belt about 70%, two-thirds of the people live on the central and southern plain; every topic is pinned to numbered maps of Iraq.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Amangalieva NIS): the NIS (Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools) inquiry textbook: every lesson opens with a «Бұл сабақта» (In this lesson) goal box and closes with a «Сенің кезегің!» (Your turn!) task block; each section ends in a «Қорытынды» summary built round a concept map; the book closes with a trilingual Kazakh–Russian–English glossary. Country-studies section works through how a state improves its geographic position, with the UAE and Singapore as cases and Kazakhstan as the home example.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Egorina): the 2025 Atamura course is organised by numbered paragraphs (§1–§68) with coded learning goals (e.g. 7.3.2.4) and a «Негізгі ұғымдар» key-concepts box per paragraph; it gives Kazakhstani science its own paragraph (§3, research in the war and post-war years), a whole practical strand on building geographic databases (§§10, 18, 49, 54–55), and a country-studies block that classifies states by position — continental, archipelago, island and peninsular — with Kazakhstan's own economic-geographic position as the worked example.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Egorina 2017): the 2017 first edition of the Atamura course (the 2025 Book 2 is its revision): same six-section architecture but twelve numbered practical works, four of them database-building exercises on Kazakhstan itself — a geographic database for Kazakhstan's regions (§51) and for Kazakhstan's transport system (§52) — plus a practical on the natural complex of the pupil's own locality (§37).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Karatabanov): every paragraph is titled as a question («Как устроена Земля внутри?», «Все ли одинаково обеспечены водой?») and every section closes with a «Мои географические достижения» (My geographic achievements) self-check page; Part 2 asks what is known about "our country's soils" (§§37–38) and how to develop natural resources "without harm to nature and people" (§§55–57).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Tolybekova): the Mektep course teaches geographic nomenclature continent by continent (§§6–8: Africa/Australia/Antarctica, the Americas, Eurasia) with a practical on completing an outline map, then works through the natural complexes belt by belt (§§39–47: arctic, temperate forest/steppe, desert, tropical, subequatorial, equatorial); Kazakhstan anchors the applied ends — its regions' flora and fauna (§33), its soil types and soil resources (§§34–35), its population (§52, Practical №3) and its own economic-geographic position (§62).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Tolybekova 2017): the 2017 first edition of the Mektep course (Book 5 is its 2025 revision): the same continent-by-continent nomenclature and belt-by-belt natural-complexes architecture, with a lithosphere field-research practical, a §52 «Человек — часть биосферы» (The human being as part of the biosphere), and a closing §60 «Этот многоликий мир» (This many-faced world) survey of the world's diversity.
