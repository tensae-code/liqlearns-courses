# Video script — Forests and the poor — a two-way street

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Poverty causes deforestation — and so does wealth.

Deforestation can create assets for some poor people, a field where none was.

The forest strategy that balances both goals has three parts: strengthen poor and marginalised people's forest rights and participation (community forestry, agroforestry); integrate forests into development through governance reform and investment; and protect local and global values through protected areas and markets for biodiversity and carbon..
