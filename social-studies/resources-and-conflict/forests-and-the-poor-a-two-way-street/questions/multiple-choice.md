# Forests and the poor — a two-way street — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Deforestation is caused by —
a) poverty and wealth  b) poverty only  c) wealth only
**Answer: a) poverty and wealth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** More income alone —
a) may not stop the poor clearing  b) always stops clearing  c) never matters
**Answer: a) may not stop the poor clearing**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Deforestation destroys, for poor households, —
a) forest goods and services  b) nothing  c) only scenery
**Answer: a) forest goods and services**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Community forestry belongs to strategy part —
a) one — rights and participation  b) two — governance  c) three — protected areas
**Answer: a) one — rights and participation**
