# Forests and the poor — a two-way street — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the street two-way?
**Answer:** Poverty and wealth both drive clearing, and clearing both creates and destroys poor people's assets.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name goods a forest gives poor households.
**Answer:** Fuel, fodder, food, medicine, water regulation (any three).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List the strategy's three parts.
**Answer:** Rights and participation; integration into development; protecting values through protected areas and markets.
