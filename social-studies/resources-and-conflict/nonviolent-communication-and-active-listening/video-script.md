# Video script — Nonviolent Communication and Active Listening

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales Segundo ciclo TG, printed pp. 46–49, teaches: active listening and nonviolent communication — how the way we speak can inflame or defuse a conflict, practised on school and family cases.

Guatemala's own way: coexistence is taught from the Maya cosmovision — its four threads are respect, gratitude, service and care for nature — and classroom rules are not imposed but built by the pupils as agreements.
