# Hands-on — Nonviolent Communication and Active Listening

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 2, Activity "Aprendamos a dialogar" (case studies)
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales Segundo ciclo TG, printed pp. 46–49, teaches: active listening and nonviolent communication — how the way we speak can inflame or defuse a conflict, practised on school and family cases. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: coexistence is taught from the Maya cosmovision — its four threads are respect, gratitude, service and care for nature — and classroom rules are not imposed but built by the pupils as agreements.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
