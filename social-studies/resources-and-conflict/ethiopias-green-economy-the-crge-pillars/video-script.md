# Video script — Ethiopia’s green economy — the CRGE pillars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grow richer. Pollute no more. Stand firm as the climate shifts.
That is the CRGE vision — middle-income, climate-resilient, emissions held.
And on the farm it has a working name: climate-smart agriculture.
Trees, crops and livestock, integrated. That is the pillar at work.
