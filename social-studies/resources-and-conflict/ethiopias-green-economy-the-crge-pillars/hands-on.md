# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s green economy — the CRGE pillars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two legs drawing
Draw the plan as a figure standing on two legs — green growth, climate resilience — and label what happens to the figure if either leg is cut away.

## CSA holding
Design a one-hectare climate-smart holding on paper: where trees stand, where crops grow, where animals fit, and how each part protects the others.
