# Ethiopia’s green economy — the CRGE pillars

## Do it first

A plan with two legs: grow richer, pollute no more; and stand firmer as the climate shifts. Read the CRGE pillars.

## Sub lesson 1 — The vision

Ethiopia’s Climate Resilient Green Economy vision, as printed: to reach middle-income status while remaining climate-resilient, with no net increase in emissions over the 2010 level by 2025. Growth and restraint are set as one target, not two.

[Picture 21057 — the CRGE vision as two pillars under one roof — green growth and resilience]

## Sub lesson 2 — Climate-smart agriculture

On the farm the vision becomes climate-smart agriculture: farming that raises yields, withstands climate shocks and keeps emissions low together — trees, crops and livestock integrated on the same holding. Agriculture, the backbone sector, is where the green economy must first prove itself.
