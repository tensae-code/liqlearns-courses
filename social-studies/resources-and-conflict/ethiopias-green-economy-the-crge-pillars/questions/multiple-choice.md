# Ethiopia’s green economy — the CRGE pillars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** CRGE stands for —
a) Central Region Grain Exchange  b) Climate Research Group of Ethiopia  c) Climate Resilient Green Economy
**Answer: c) Climate Resilient Green Economy**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The vision holds emissions to —
a) zero farming  b) no net increase over the 2010 level  c) double the 2010 level
**Answer: b) no net increase over the 2010 level**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate-smart agriculture integrates —
a) trees, crops and livestock  b) only trees  c) only irrigation
**Answer: a) trees, crops and livestock**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** CSA must raise yields, withstand shocks and —
a) use more fuel  b) clear more forest  c) keep emissions low
**Answer: c) keep emissions low**
