# Conflict — Interpersonal, Intergroup, International — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Intergroup conflict is between groups of like outlook, culture and descent.**
**Answer: True** — that likeness defines the groups in Fisher's second kind.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) When an intergroup conflict cools, it is always solved for good.**
**Answer: False** — it can cool without a lasting solution.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Economic sanctions are a face of international conflict.**
**Answer: True** — sanctions stand beside war as a chief face.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Fisher's widest kind is the interpersonal.**
**Answer: False** — the widest is international, between countries.
