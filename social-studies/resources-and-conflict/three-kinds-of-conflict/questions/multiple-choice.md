# Conflict — Interpersonal, Intergroup, International — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Interpersonal conflict is between —**
a) Two countries  b) Two groups  c) Two people whose wants, aims, approach or communication fail to fit  d) A person and a resource
**Answer: c) Two people whose wants, aims, approach or communication fail to fit** — two people with unfitting wants/aims is Fisher's first kind.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) An interpersonal conflict eases if the two —**
a) Stop speaking  b) Narrow their difference and reconcile their aims  c) Call their groups in  d) Accept low standing
**Answer: b) Narrow their difference and reconcile their aims** — narrowing difference and reconciling aims is the easing.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Intergroup conflict flares when one group seeks —**
a) Trade  b) Dominance, exclusion and contempt  c) Mediation  d) Shared festivals
**Answer: b) Dominance, exclusion and contempt** — those three seekings flare it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) International conflict's chief faces are —**
a) Quarrels and misunderstandings  b) War opened by one country on another, and economic sanctions  c) Overgrazing and felling  d) Elections and taxes
**Answer: b) War opened by one country on another, and economic sanctions** — war and sanctions are the two chief faces.
