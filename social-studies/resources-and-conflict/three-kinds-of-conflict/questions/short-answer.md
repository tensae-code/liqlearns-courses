# Conflict — Interpersonal, Intergroup, International — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) How does an intergroup conflict cool without a lasting solution?**
**Answer:** When the weakened group accepts its low standing out of limited capacity or differing outlook — the quarrel cools, but nothing is solved lastingly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name Fisher's three kinds in widening order.**
**Answer:** Interpersonal, intergroup, international.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) Give the mechanism of interpersonal conflict.**
**Answer:** Two people's wants, aims, approach or communication fail to fit each other.
