# Video script — Conflict — Interpersonal, Intergroup, International

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fisher sorts conflict by who is inside it.
Two people first. Wants do not fit. Aims do not fit.
Narrow the difference — it eases.
Then groups. Like against like.
Dominance, exclusion, contempt — it flares.
It may cool — without ever being solved.
Then countries. Its faces: war — and sanctions.
Three circles. Widening.
Sort the quarrel by its circle before you judge it.
