# Hands-on — Conflict — Interpersonal, Intergroup, International

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Circle census
In groups, collect five quarrels from school and neighbourhood life. Sort each into Fisher's circles on a shared chart; quarrels the class cannot place get argued with mechanisms, not labels.

## Cooling or solving?
Take one cooled group quarrel the class knows. Write what made it cool — and whether, by the book's test, anything was actually solved.
