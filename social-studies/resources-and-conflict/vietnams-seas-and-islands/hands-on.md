# Hands-on — Vietnams Seas And Islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 33: Developing the marine economy and ensuring national defence in the East Sea and on the islands — the sea and Vietnam's sea-and-island territory (some 1 million km² of waters; 13 special island zones by 2025 including Hoang Sa and Truong Sa; a 3,260 km coast), the sea's resources (over 2,000 fish species; some 10 billion tons of oil equivalent under the shelf; titanium, sand and salt; Ha Long–Cat Ba; the region's best wind-energy coast), integrated exploitation (sea tourism; 34 ports; sea freight up from 61.6 to 129.5 million tons; offshore oil down from 14.8 to 8.1 million tons; fisheries and farming at sea), protecting the sea-island environment, and the strategic meaning (UNCLOS 1982, the DOC, the COC in negotiation). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
