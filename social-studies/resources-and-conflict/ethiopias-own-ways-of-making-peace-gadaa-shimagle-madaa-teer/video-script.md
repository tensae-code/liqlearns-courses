# Video script — Ethiopia's own ways of making peace — Gadaa, Shimagle, Mada'a, T'eer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethiopia makes peace in its own ways. Among the Amhara, elder mediators — the Shimagle — hear a dispute and settle it. Among the Oromo, the Gadaa system works under the Abbaa Gadaa. Among the Afar, the Mada'a, administered through figures like the Kedo Abba, has managed conflicts between clans and with neighbours for generations. And the Somali system is the T'eer. Four systems, one pattern: elders, customary law, legitimacy from inside the community — and restoration valued over punishment. These are living institutions, not museum pieces.
