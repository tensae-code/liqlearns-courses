# Ethiopia's own ways of making peace — Gadaa, Shimagle, Mada'a, T'eer — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Match each system to its people.
**Answer:** Shimagle — Amhara; Gadaa — Oromo; Mada'a — Afar; T'eer — Somali.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two values these systems carry.
**Answer:** Any two of: local legitimacy; restoration over punishment; knowledge transmitted across generations (in farming, fishing, forestry and water management).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who administers the Mada'a?
**Answer:** Figures such as the Kedo Abba.
