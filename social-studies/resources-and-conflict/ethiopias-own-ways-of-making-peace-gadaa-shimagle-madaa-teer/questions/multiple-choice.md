# Ethiopia's own ways of making peace — Gadaa, Shimagle, Mada'a, T'eer — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Shimagle are…
a) elder mediators among the Amhara  b) Oromo leaders  c) Somali elders
**Answer: a) elder mediators among the Amhara**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gadaa system works under the…
a) Kedo Abba  b) Abbaa Gadaa  c) Shimagle
**Answer: b) Abbaa Gadaa**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mada'a belongs to…
a) the Afar  b) the Amhara  c) the Oromo
**Answer: a) the Afar**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Somali system named is…
a) Gadaa  b) T'eer  c) Mada'a
**Answer: b) T'eer**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A shared value of these systems is…
a) punishment first  b) restoration over punishment  c) secrecy
**Answer: b) restoration over punishment**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Their legitimacy is…
a) local — decisions accepted from inside the community  b) imported  c) military
**Answer: a) local — decisions accepted from inside the community**
