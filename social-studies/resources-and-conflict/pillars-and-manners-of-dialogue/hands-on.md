# Hands-on — Pillars and Manners of Dialogue

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: dialogue's two pillars — the two parties and the topic — and its foundations: a clearly defined topic, the parties' agreement to dialogue, a set goal, and observing dialogue's manners, with suitable phrases ("What is your opinion?", "It seems to me…") and respect for the other party as the start of dialogue; the manners — knowledge of the issue and of the other party, gradualness beginning with the most important, honesty and evidence, clarity of expression, and the ethics of dialogue (good listening, humility in word and deed, a moderate voice, honesty in conveying ideas, no fanaticism for one's opinion) — and dialogue's styles: questioning, descriptive and inferential. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
