# Video script — Pillars and Manners of Dialogue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 242–247), teaches: Dialogue's two pillars — the two parties and the topic — and its foundations: a clearly defined topic, the parties' agreement to dialogue, a set goal, and observing dialogue's manners, with suitable phrases ("What is your opinion?", "It seems to me…") and respect for the other party as the start of dialogue; the manners — knowledge of the issue and of the other party, gradualness beginning with the most important, honesty and evidence, clarity of expression, and the ethics of dialogue (good listening, humility in word and deed, a moderate voice, honesty in conveying ideas, no fanaticism for one's opinion) — and dialogue's styles: questioning, descriptive and inferential.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue.
