# Hands-on — Costa Rica Without an Army Perpetual Neutrality and Peace Diplomacy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Costa Rica's peaceful way in the world — a country with no dictator in over 100 years signs the Proclama de Neutralidad Perpetua (never to take part in any war), and President Óscar Arias builds the Esquipulas peace plan with the other Central American presidents — ceasefires, free elections and respect between peoples — winning the 1987 Nobel Peace Prize. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
