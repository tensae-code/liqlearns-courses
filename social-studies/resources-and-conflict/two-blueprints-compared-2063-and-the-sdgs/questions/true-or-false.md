# Two blueprints compared — 2063 and the SDGs — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agenda 2063 and the SDGs are the same document. True or false?
**Answer: False** — one is the African Union's blueprint; the other is the UN's world goal set.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Goal 15 is Life on Land. True or false?
**Answer: True**.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Comparing indicator by indicator means checking where the two plans agree and where they differ. True or false?
**Answer: True** — that is the book's exercise.
