# Two blueprints compared — 2063 and the SDGs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Agenda 2063 is the blueprint of —
a) the United Nations  b) the African Union  c) one country  d) a single region
**Answer: b) the African Union** — Africa's own continental plan.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which theme is in Agenda 2063's goal list as the book gives it?
a) space exploration  b) independence from foreign powers  c) ocean mining only  d) none of these
**Answer: b) independence from foreign powers** — with development, integration, democracy and justice, peace and security, cultural identity and gender equality.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many Sustainable Development Goals are there?
a) 8  b) 10  c) 17  d) 27
**Answer: c) 17** — the Agenda 2030 set.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Goal 16 of the SDGs is —
a) Life on Land  b) Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions  c) Partnerships for the Goals  d) Zero Hunger
**Answer: b) Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions** — goal 15 is Life on Land; 17 is Partnerships.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The book compares the two blueprints —
a) by price  b) indicator by indicator  c) by colour  d) not at all
**Answer: b) indicator by indicator** — goal set against goal set, measure by measure.
