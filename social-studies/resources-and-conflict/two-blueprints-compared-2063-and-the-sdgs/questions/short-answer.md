# Two blueprints compared — 2063 and the SDGs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List Agenda 2063's goals as the book gives them.
**Answer:** Development, integration, democracy and justice, peace and security, cultural identity, gender equality, and independence from foreign powers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name SDG goals 15, 16 and 17.
**Answer:** 15 — Life on Land; 16 — Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions; 17 — Partnerships for the Goals.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does an indicator-by-indicator comparison show that reading either plan alone cannot?
**Answer:** Where the continent's plan and the world's plan agree, and where each asks for something the other does not.
