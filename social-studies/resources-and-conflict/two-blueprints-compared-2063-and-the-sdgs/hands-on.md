# Hands-on — Two blueprints compared — 2063 and the SDGs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-column comparison
Draw two columns — Agenda 2063 / the SDGs. Fill each side from the lesson, then draw lines joining the themes that match and circle the ones with no partner.

## Goal 15, 16, 17 cards
Make three cards for goals 15, 16 and 17: number, name, and one sentence on what the goal protects or builds.
