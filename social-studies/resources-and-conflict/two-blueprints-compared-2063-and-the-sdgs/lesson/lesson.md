# Two blueprints compared — 2063 and the SDGs

## Do it first

Two plans for the future stand in front of you: one written by Africa for Africa, one written by the whole world. Before you read, guess: would they want the same things, or different ones? Then read how the book compares them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Agenda 2063's goals

**Agenda 2063** is the African Union's blueprint for the continent. The goals the book lists for it: **development, integration, democracy and justice, peace and security, cultural identity, gender equality, and independence from foreign powers**. It is Africa's own statement of the future it intends — continental in scale, and owned by the continent.

## Sub lesson 2 — The 17 SDGs, goal by goal

Against it stands the United Nations' **17 Sustainable Development Goals** — Agenda 2030 — the world's goal set. The book walks the set **goal by goal**; the closing goals it works through include **Goal 15, Life on Land; Goal 16, Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions; and Goal 17, Partnerships for the Goals**. Seventeen goals, each with its own targets, covering the whole world's development — Africa's countries included, but not Africa alone.

## Sub lesson 3 — The comparison — indicator by indicator

The book's exercise is a **comparison indicator by indicator**: Agenda 2063's goals set against the SDGs' goals, measure by measure, to see where the continent's plan and the world's plan agree, and where each one asks for something the other does not. That is the skill this lesson teaches: two blueprints can share a direction and still differ — and the differences are found by comparing carefully, not by choosing a side first.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion Agenda 2063's goal list, the SDG count with its last three goals, and how the book compares the two.

## Do It Yourself

1. Write Agenda 2063's goals from memory.
2. Write the number of SDG goals and the names of goals 15, 16 and 17.
3. Pick one theme that appears in both blueprints and one that belongs to Agenda 2063's own list, and say which is which.
