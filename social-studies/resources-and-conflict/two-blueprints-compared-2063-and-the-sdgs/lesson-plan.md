# Lesson plan — Two blueprints compared — 2063 and the SDGs

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Resources and Conflict · **Lesson:** Two blueprints compared — 2063 and the SDGs
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Major Economic and Cultural Activities of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Agenda 2063's goals and the UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals are compared indicator by indicator — where the continent's blueprint and the world's agree, and where they differ.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Agenda 2063 lesson and the 17 SDGs lesson, and does the comparing neither of them does alone.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Agenda 2063's goals** — The African Union blueprint's goal set as the book lists it.
2. **The 17 SDGs, goal by goal** — The UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals walked as a set.
3. **The comparison — indicator by indicator** — How the book sets the two blueprints side by side.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. List Agenda 2063's goals as the book gives them.
2. State the SDG count and name goals 15, 16 and 17.
3. Explain what an indicator-by-indicator comparison does.
