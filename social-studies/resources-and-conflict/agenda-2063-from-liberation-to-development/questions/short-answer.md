# Agenda 2063 — from liberation to development — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four priorities of the refocused agenda.
**Answer:** Inclusive social and economic development; continental and regional integration; democratic governance; peace and security.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does the guide frame the plan as a 'refocus' rather than a new start?
**Answer:** Because the continental house and its energy continue — the OAU-era struggle for liberation was largely won, and Agenda 2063 turns the same agenda toward development, integration, governance and peace.
