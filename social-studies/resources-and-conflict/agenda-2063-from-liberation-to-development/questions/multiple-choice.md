# Agenda 2063 — from liberation to development — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The OAU-era agenda was the struggle —
a) for trade tariffs  b) against apartheid and for political independence  c) for a single currency  d) against urbanisation
**Answer: b) against apartheid and for political independence** — the guide's framing (teacher guide — evidence).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** That old agenda is described as —
a) never started  b) largely won — the states became formally free  c) abandoned  d) a failure
**Answer: b) largely won — the states became formally free** — freedom raised the next question: freedom for what?

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the four refocused priorities?
a) Inclusive social and economic development  b) Continental and regional integration  c) Democratic governance  d) Colonial expansion
**Answer: d) Colonial expansion** — development, integration, governance, peace and security.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The vision runs —
a) 2013–2063, fifty years  b) 2020–2030, ten years  c) 1963–2000  d) 2000–2063, sixty-three years
**Answer: a) 2013–2063, fifty years** — the span that gives the plan its name.
