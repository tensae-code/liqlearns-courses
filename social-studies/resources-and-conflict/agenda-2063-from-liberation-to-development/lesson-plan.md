# Lesson plan — Agenda 2063 — from liberation to development

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Resources and Conflict · **Lesson:** Agenda 2063 — from liberation to development
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 5 — Major Economic and Cultural Activities of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The teacher guide's fuller framing: Agenda 2063 refocuses Africa's agenda from the OAU era's struggle against apartheid and for political independence to four priorities — inclusive social and economic development, continental and regional integration, democratic governance, and peace and security — across a 50-year vision running 2013–2063.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan. This lesson is the guide's extension of it (teacher guide — evidence), taught as labelled depth on a pupil-taught lesson.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **The old agenda — liberation (teacher guide — evidence)** — What the OAU era was for.
2. **The four new priorities — the refocus (teacher guide — evidence)** — Development, integration, governance, peace.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Agenda 2063 — the continent's plan. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the OAU-era agenda: against apartheid, for political independence.
2. Name the four refocused priorities.
3. Give the vision's span: 2013–2063, fifty years.
4. Say why this lesson is labelled teacher-guide evidence, and what it extends.
