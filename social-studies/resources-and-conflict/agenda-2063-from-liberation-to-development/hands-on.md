# Hands-on — Agenda 2063 — from liberation to development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-agenda wall chart
Draw two columns: 'The old agenda' (apartheid struggle, independence) and 'The refocus' (the four priorities). Draw an arrow across the top labelled 'Agenda 2063 — the turn'. Present it, reading both columns.

## Priority sort
Write eight example projects on cards (your own ideas). Sort them under the four priority headings. Any card that fits two headings gets discussed: which priority leads, and why?
