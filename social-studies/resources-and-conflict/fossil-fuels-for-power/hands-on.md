# Hands-on — Fossil Fuels For Power

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Use of fossil fuels (coal, oil and gas) to generate power: Pakistan's thermal power units (Karachi, Lahore, Multan, Faisalabad, Guddu, Jam Shoro, Kot Addu and more). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
