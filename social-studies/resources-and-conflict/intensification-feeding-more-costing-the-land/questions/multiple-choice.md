# Intensification — feeding more, costing the land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Intensification raises output per unit of —
a) land, labour and input  b) time only  c) luck
**Answer: a) land, labour and input**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Green Revolution used improved seed, fertiliser, pesticide, machinery and —
a) irrigation  b) terracing only  c) fallowing
**Answer: a) irrigation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Human food now comes 90% from —
a) 15 crops and 8 livestock species  b) 150 crops  c) 8 crops only
**Answer: a) 15 crops and 8 livestock species**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Sustainable intensification includes —
a) agroforestry  b) monocropping  c) more pesticide
**Answer: a) agroforestry**
