# Intensification — feeding more, costing the land — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define intensification.
**Answer:** More output per unit of land, labour and input.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three harms.
**Answer:** Biodiversity loss, soil decline, pesticide persistence/bio-magnification, salinisation, pest build-up, compaction (any three).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three sustainable tools.
**Answer:** Agroforestry, integrated soil and pest management, efficient fertiliser and water use.
