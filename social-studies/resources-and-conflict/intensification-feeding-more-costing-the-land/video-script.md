# Video script — Intensification — feeding more, costing the land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Intensification means more output per unit of land, labour and input — Green Revolution style: improved seed, fertiliser, pesticide, machinery and irrigation, all pressed onto the same field..

It raised yields, but it opens natural ecosystems, drains soil nutrients and carbon, and its harms are listed one by one: biodiversity lost to monocropping (15 crops and 8 livestock species now supply 90 percent of human food), soil quality declining, pesticides persisting and bio-magnifying, irrigation salinising land, pests building up, and machinery compacting soil..

The unit's answer is sustainable intensification: agroforestry, integrated soil and pest management, and efficient fertiliser and water use — more from the field without eating the field..
