# Natural Resources — Non-Renewable Resources — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) List the book's minerals table with one use where the book gives one.**
**Answer:** Diamond (jewellery), copper (electrical goods), petroleum and natural gas, uranium and tin.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) Name the two fates of non-renewables and one example of each.**
**Answer:** Wholly consumed — petroleum (and non-metallic minerals); can serve again — metallic minerals such as copper, melted down and remade.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Why can non-renewables not renew on a human timescale?**
**Answer:** Because they form through geological, physical and chemical processes over hundreds of millions of years — far beyond any human timescale.
