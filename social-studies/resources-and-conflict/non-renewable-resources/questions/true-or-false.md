# Natural Resources — Non-Renewable Resources — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) Diamond in the book's table is used for jewellery.**
**Answer: True** — diamond — jewellery, as printed.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) Non-renewables replace themselves where they were, slowly but within a year.**
**Answer: False** — they cannot replace themselves where they were.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Recycling a metal makes the resource renewable.**
**Answer: False** — recycling stretches a limited amount; it does not renew it.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Non-renewables are made by geological, physical and chemical processes over hundreds of millions of years.**
**Answer: True** — that deep-time formation is why they cannot renew for us.
