# Natural Resources — Non-Renewable Resources — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) A non-renewable resource is one that —**
a) Renews itself every year  b) Exists in limited amount and cannot replace itself once used  c) Is always a fuel  d) Grows back when planted
**Answer: b) Exists in limited amount and cannot replace itself once used** — limited amount and no self-replacement is the definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) In the book's table, copper is used for —**
a) Jewellery  b) Electrical goods  c) Cooking fuel  d) Building stone only
**Answer: b) Electrical goods** — copper — electrical goods; diamond — jewellery.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) Which resource is wholly consumed when used?**
a) Copper  b) Tin  c) Petroleum  d) Uranium ore as metal
**Answer: c) Petroleum** — petroleum, burned, is the book's wholly consumed example.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) Metallic minerals can serve again when goods made from them are —**
a) Buried  b) Melted down and remade  c) Burned  d) Thrown in a river
**Answer: b) Melted down and remade** — melting and remaking is the recyclable path.
