# Hands-on — Natural Resources — Non-Renewable Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Metal hunt
Walk your home and list everything made of metal. For each, write the metal if you know it and whether, worn out, it could be melted and remade. Bring the list — the class sorts it together.

## Fuel diary
For one day, write down every use of a wholly consumed resource at home — cooking fuel, a match, transport fuel. At the day's end, mark what is simply gone. That mark is non-renewable in daily life.
