# Video script — Natural Resources — Non-Renewable Resources

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dig copper from a hill. The hill has no more.
Burn petrol. It is gone. No more comes.
These are the non-renewables. Their amount is limited.
But they split in two.
Petrol, burned, is finished forever.
Copper, melted, can serve again — a new wire from an old one.
Still limited. Recycling only stretches it.
Why no new supply? The Earth took hundreds of millions of years to make them.
Now you. Name one of each kind in your home.
