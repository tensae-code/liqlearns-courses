# Video script — Violence What it Is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 26–28, teaches: violence defined (Montoya; WHO) as the intentional use of force or power causing physical, psychological or emotional harm; its kinds and settings, the roots of aggression, and its effects on victims and communities.

Guatemala Grade 9 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana III TG, printed pp. 25–29, teaches: violence defined as immoderate force against another's will (humiliation, threat, rejection, harassment); the three explaining theories — personality traits, frustration-aggression, and social interaction — and the environmental risk factors in family and school that raise its likelihood, with prevention ideas.

Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.
Guatemala's own way: the oldest básico pupils study the newest faces of violence — the teacher's own authority, the phone in the pupil's pocket, and prejudice over identity — each answered from human-rights law and the peoples' own values.
