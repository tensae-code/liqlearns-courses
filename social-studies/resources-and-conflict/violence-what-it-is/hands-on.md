# Hands-on — Violence What it Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 1 content theme "La violencia"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 26–28, teaches: violence defined (Montoya; WHO) as the intentional use of force or power causing physical, psychological or emotional harm; its kinds and settings, the roots of aggression, and its effects on victims and communities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Experience 1 content theme "La violencia"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 9 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana III TG, printed pp. 25–29, teaches: violence defined as immoderate force against another's will (humiliation, threat, rejection, harassment); the three explaining theories — personality traits, frustration-aggression, and social interaction — and the environmental risk factors in family and school that raise its likelihood, with prevention ideas. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 4) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the oldest básico pupils study the newest faces of violence — the teacher's own authority, the phone in the pupil's pocket, and prejudice over identity — each answered from human-rights law and the peoples' own values.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
