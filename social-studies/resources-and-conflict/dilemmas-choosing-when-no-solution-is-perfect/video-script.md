The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 97–106, teaches: situations where a problem has no single solution are dilemmas (trato / dilema / conflicto / acuerdo word-work and a king-and-shepherd story); who can guide us to choose, and writing down disagreements when no agreement is reached.

Colombia Grade 5 CS_Grado05_02, printed pp. 96–103, teaches: our decisions in a dilemma affect other people, so they must be taken with respect for others; groups compare reasons and debate which is the best decision, and leaving a family dilemma undecided also has effects — not deciding is a way of deciding.

Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city.

Colombia's own way: dilemmas are decided as a group exercise in consequences — pupils must table how each solution will make each affected person feel before choosing, and "not deciding" is itself named a decision with effects.
