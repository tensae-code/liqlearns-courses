## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 13 ¿Cuáles son nuestros dilemas?

Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 97–106, teaches: situations where a problem has no single solution are dilemmas (trato / dilema / conflicto / acuerdo word-work and a king-and-shepherd story); who can guide us to choose, and writing down disagreements when no agreement is reached. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guía 15 Nuestros dilemas

Colombia Grade 5 CS_Grado05_02, printed pp. 96–103, teaches: our decisions in a dilemma affect other people, so they must be taken with respect for others; groups compare reasons and debate which is the best decision, and leaving a family dilemma undecided also has effects — not deciding is a way of deciding. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Colombia's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Colombia's way (Book 10) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: dilemmas are decided as a group exercise in consequences — pupils must table how each solution will make each affected person feel before choosing, and "not deciding" is itself named a decision with effects. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
