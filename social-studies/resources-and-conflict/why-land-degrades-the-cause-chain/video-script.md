# Video script — Why land degrades — the cause chain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Degradation is the overall loss of quality and quantity of land resources.

Underlying drivers: rapid population growth, climate change, rough topography, improper land use, poor policy and tenure insecurity.

The consequences run to lost biodiversity, desertification, low yields, hunger, poverty, food insecurity and migration.
