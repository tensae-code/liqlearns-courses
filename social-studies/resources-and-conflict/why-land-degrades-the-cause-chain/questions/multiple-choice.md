# Why land degrades — the cause chain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Degradation is the loss of land resources' —
a) quality and quantity  b) colour only  c) name
**Answer: a) quality and quantity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tenure insecurity is an —
a) underlying driver  b) proximate act  c) consequence
**Answer: a) underlying driver**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ploughing up-and-down slopes is a —
a) proximate act  b) underlying driver  c) remedy
**Answer: a) proximate act**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A consequence of degradation is —
a) desertification  b) higher yields  c) food security
**Answer: a) desertification**
