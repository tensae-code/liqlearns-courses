# Why land degrades — the cause chain — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define degradation.
**Answer:** The overall loss of quality and quantity of land resources.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one underlying driver and the proximate act it might drive.
**Answer:** E.g. population growth driving intensive tillage (any sensible pair).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three consequences.
**Answer:** Lost biodiversity, desertification, low yields, hunger, poverty, food insecurity, migration (any three).
