# Invaders — species out of place — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An invasive alien spreads beyond its —
a) historic range  b) garden  c) country of study
**Answer: a) historic range**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Invaders succeed as —
a) outstanding competitors  b) weak growers  c) natives
**Answer: a) outstanding competitors**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Disturbances that open the door include flood, fire and —
a) ploughing  b) terracing  c) closure
**Answer: a) ploughing**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Phenotypic plasticity means —
a) reshaping oneself to local conditions  b) being made of plastic  c) never changing
**Answer: a) reshaping oneself to local conditions**
