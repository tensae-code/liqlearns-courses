# Invaders — species out of place — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define an invasive alien species.
**Answer:** A plant, animal or microbe spreading beyond its historic range as an outstanding competitor.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the door-openers.
**Answer:** Climate change and disturbance — flood, fire, ploughing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the two plasticity examples.
**Answer:** The mayapple's two-year shoot decision; loosestrife's leaves reshaped to local light.
