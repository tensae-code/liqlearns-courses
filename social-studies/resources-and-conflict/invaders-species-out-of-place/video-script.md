# Video script — Invaders — species out of place

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Invasive alien species are plants, animals and microbes spreading beyond their historic range.

Climate change and disturbance open the door: flood, fire and ploughing clear ground and stress natives.

The terrestrial treatment rests on phenotypic plasticity: one genotype reshaping itself to local conditions.
