# Sustainable development — living without eating the future

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: how do you use a forest, a soil, a river — and hand them on whole? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — What sustainability means

Environmental sustainability means keeping life-support processes running: soil regeneration, nutrient recycling, water cleansing. It means preserving genetic diversity, and using species sustainably — taking the interest, not the capital.

## Sub lesson 2 — In practice

The practice list is plain: stop deforestation, regulate dumping, recycle, cut plastic, and spread environmental education. None of it needs new technology; all of it needs decision.

## Sub lesson 3 — Livelihoods fitted to variability

The unit closes with livelihoods adapted to variable environments: diverse crops and integrated crop–livestock systems, so one bad season cannot take everything a household has.
