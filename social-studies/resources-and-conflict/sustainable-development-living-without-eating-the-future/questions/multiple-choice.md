# Sustainable development — living without eating the future — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Life-support processes include soil regeneration, nutrient recycling and —
a) water cleansing  b) plastic making  c) dumping
**Answer: a) water cleansing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Using species sustainably means —
a) taking the interest, not the capital  b) taking all now  c) taking nothing ever
**Answer: a) taking the interest, not the capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cutting plastic is part of —
a) the practice list  b) the causes list  c) the effects list
**Answer: a) the practice list**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Livelihoods adapted to variability use —
a) diverse crops and integrated crop–livestock systems  b) one crop only  c) imports only
**Answer: a) diverse crops and integrated crop–livestock systems**
