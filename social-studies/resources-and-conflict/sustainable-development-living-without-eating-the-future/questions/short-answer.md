# Sustainable development — living without eating the future — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three life-support processes.
**Answer:** Soil regeneration, nutrient recycling, water cleansing.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three practices.
**Answer:** Stop deforestation, regulate dumping, recycle, cut plastic, environmental education (any three).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why diverse crops?
**Answer:** So one bad season cannot take everything a household has.
