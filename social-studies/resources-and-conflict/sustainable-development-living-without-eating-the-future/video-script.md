# Video script — Sustainable development — living without eating the future

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Environmental sustainability means keeping life-support processes running: soil regeneration, nutrient recycling, water cleansing.

The practice list is plain: stop deforestation, regulate dumping, recycle, cut plastic, and spread environmental education.

The unit closes with livelihoods adapted to variable environments: diverse crops and integrated crop–livestock systems, so one bad season cannot take everything a household has..
