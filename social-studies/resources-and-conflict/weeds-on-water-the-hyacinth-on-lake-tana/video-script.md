# Video script — Weeds on water — the hyacinth on Lake Tana

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Aquatic invasives sort by where they sit: floating weeds with free-hanging roots; emergent plants rooted in the shallows — mangroves breathing through roots raised above the mud; and fully submerged weeds under the surface..

Water hyacinth is a South American plant now on every continent but Antarctica.

On Lake Tana the hyacinth deoxygenates the water, kills fish, and cuts the income and food of lakeside people.
