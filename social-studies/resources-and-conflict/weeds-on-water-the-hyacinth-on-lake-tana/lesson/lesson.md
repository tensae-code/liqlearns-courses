## Sub lesson 1 — Three seats in the water

Aquatic invasives sort by where they sit: floating weeds with free-hanging roots; emergent plants rooted in the shallows — mangroves breathing through roots raised above the mud; and fully submerged weeds under the surface.

## Sub lesson 2 — Water hyacinth

Water hyacinth is a South American plant now on every continent but Antarctica. On the water it forms mats so thick that boats and nets cannot pass.

## Sub lesson 3 — Lake Tana pays

On Lake Tana the hyacinth deoxygenates the water, kills fish, and cuts the income and food of lakeside people. An invader's cost is counted in meals, not only in species.
