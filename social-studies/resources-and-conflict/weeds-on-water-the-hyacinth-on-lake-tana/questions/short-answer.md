# Weeds on water — the hyacinth on Lake Tana — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three seats of aquatic invaders.
**Answer:** Floating, emergent, submerged.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where is hyacinth from, and where is it absent?
**Answer:** From South America; absent only from Antarctica.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give three harms on Lake Tana.
**Answer:** Deoxygenated water, killed fish, cut income and food (the three named).
