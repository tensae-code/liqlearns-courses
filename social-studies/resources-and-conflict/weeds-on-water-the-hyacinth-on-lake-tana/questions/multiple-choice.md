# Weeds on water — the hyacinth on Lake Tana — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Floating weeds have —
a) free-hanging roots  b) deep taproots  c) no roots
**Answer: a) free-hanging roots**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mangroves breathe through roots —
a) raised above the mud  b) under the mud  c) in the canopy
**Answer: a) raised above the mud**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Water hyacinth comes from —
a) South America  b) Lake Tana  c) Asia
**Answer: a) South America**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The hyacinth harms the lake by —
a) deoxygenating the water  b) adding oxygen  c) feeding fish
**Answer: a) deoxygenating the water**
