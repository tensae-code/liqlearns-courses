# Social Studies course — CURRICULUM (the path)

Social Studies is one world course, organised by **levels, not
grades**: a learner climbs when they are ready, not when a
school calendar says so. The school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation. The path climbs from the learner's own
ground outward (see TITLES.md): **First Steps → Earth and Space
→ Population, Transport and Maps → People and Resources →
Ethiopia: Ancient and Medieval → Africa to 1500 → The Ancient
World → The Land and Air → Culture and Work → Resources and
Conflict → Modern Ethiopia → Africa after 1500 → The Modern
World → Global Issues**.

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this part from
Ethiopia's Social Studies books for Grades 7 and 8 — except the
**Tip boxes**: a tip in a box is from us, the people making
LiqLearns. Tips are ours; the lessons are the books'. See
ABOUT.md for the full book list, the pairing warning (Addis
Ababa guides against Amhara student books), the truncated
Grade 8 guide, and the acceptance reckoning.
