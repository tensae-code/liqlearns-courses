# Video script — Transport — kinds of transport

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch the road outside for one minute and list everything that carries something — feet, hooves, wheels, wings. This lesson sorts that living list into kinds, from the oldest carrier of all — your own feet — to the aeroplane.

This lesson teaches: the ways people and goods move — from walking and pack animals to land, water and air — and what each mode carries best. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
