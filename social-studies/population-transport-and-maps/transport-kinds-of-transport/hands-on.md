# Hands-on — Transport — kinds of transport

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Count the modes passing your home or school in ten minutes; sort them traditional / modern.

## Activity 2
Pick the best mode for: crude oil, fresh vegetables for export, grain to the next town. Say why each time.

## Activity 3
Walk a load the old way — carry something home on foot — and note what the cart or vehicle saves.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the transport arc — walking, animals, cart — then name the three modern modes and one load each is fit for.
