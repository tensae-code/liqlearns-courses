# Transport — kinds of transport — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first means of transport was —
a) The wooden cart  b) The train  c) The boat  d) Walking
**Answer: d) Walking**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After walking, people next used —
a) Aeroplanes  b) Trucks  c) Animals, and invented the wooden cart  d) Ships with engines
**Answer: c) Animals, and invented the wooden cart**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a traditional carrier in Ethiopia?
a) Aeroplane  b) Camel  c) Train  d) Ship
**Answer: b) Camel**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The cheapest mode for heavy goods like crude oil is —
a) Water  b) Air  c) Donkey  d) Rail
**Answer: a) Water**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Air transport fits costly and perishable goods because it is —
a) Cheapest  b) Slowest  c) Able to float  d) Fast
**Answer: d) Fast**
