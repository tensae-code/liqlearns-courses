# Morocco — Map, Borders and Cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's Grade 5 Geography component opens with —
a) the map, the borders and the cities of Morocco  b) the history of the Alaouites  c) class rules
**Answer: a) the map, the borders and the cities of Morocco**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first Geography lesson has the learner —
a) draw Morocco's outline  b) memorise a poem  c) build a dam
**Answer: a) draw Morocco's outline**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Relative position on the map is given with —
a) the compass rose  b) a thermometer  c) a clock
**Answer: a) the compass rose**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Onto the outline of Morocco the lesson locates —
a) its borders and major cities  b) only its rivers  c) only its deserts
**Answer: a) its borders and major cities**
