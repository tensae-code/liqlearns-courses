# Morocco — Map, Borders and Cities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Geography component of Morocco's Year 5 Social Studies called, and what is its first lesson about?
**Answer: «مميزات وطني» — Features of my homeland; its first lesson is the map, the borders and the cities of Morocco (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the guide teach a learner to give a place's relative position?
**Answer: With the compass rose — saying where a place sits in relation to another place on the map (any close wording).**
