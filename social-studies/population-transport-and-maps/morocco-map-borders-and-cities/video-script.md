# Video script — Morocco — Map, Borders and Cities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Morocco's Grade 5 Social Studies guide opens the Geography component — «مميزات وطني», Features of my homeland — with the map itself. The first Geography lesson has the learner draw Morocco's outline: the shape of the homeland, drawn by the learner's own hand, before anything is put inside it.

Onto the outline go the borders and the major cities, located on the map. Relative position is given with the compass rose: where a place sits in relation to another — that is how the guide teaches a child to say where things in the homeland are.
