# Hands-on — Morocco — Map, Borders and Cities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw your homeland's outline
Draw the outline of Morocco from memory of its map shape, the way the guide's first Geography lesson begins — the outline first, nothing inside it yet.

## Place the borders, cities and rose
On your outline, mark Morocco's borders and its major cities, and draw a compass rose. Use the rose to say where one city sits in relation to another.
