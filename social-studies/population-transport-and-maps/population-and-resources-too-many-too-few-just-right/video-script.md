# Video script — Population and resources — too many, too few, just right

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Imagine a boat. Too many people aboard and it sits low, slow, taking water. Too few and there are not enough hands to row or bail. Somewhere between is the loading at which the boat flies. People and a country's resources balance the same way — this lesson names all three states.

This lesson teaches: the balance between people and resources — over-population, under-population, optimum population — and the two policy answers, anti-natalist and pro-natalist. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
