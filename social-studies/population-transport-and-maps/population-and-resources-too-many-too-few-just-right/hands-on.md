# Hands-on — Population and resources — too many, too few, just right

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Write the three definitions from memory and check them against the lesson.

## Activity 2
Give your own area a verdict — pressing, balanced, or roomy — with two pieces of evidence.

## Activity 3
Argue both policies for one invented country each, in three sentences apiece.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the three states with one mark each, and which policy a crowded country and an empty country would each choose.
