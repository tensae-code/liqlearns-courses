# Population and resources — too many, too few, just right — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Over-population is —
a) Too few people for the resources  b) A balanced relationship  c) A kind of migration  d) An excess of population against available resources
**Answer: d) An excess of population against available resources**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a mark of over-population?
a) High per-capita income  b) Inward migration  c) Low per-capita income  d) Low unemployment
**Answer: c) Low per-capita income**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Under-population means —
a) Too many people  b) Too few people for the full use of resources  c) No resources at all  d) A balanced state
**Answer: b) Too few people for the full use of resources**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Optimum population is —
a) The balanced population–resource relationship  b) The largest possible population  c) The smallest possible population  d) Zero growth forever
**Answer: a) The balanced population–resource relationship**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** An anti-natalist policy works to —
a) Increase the birth rate  b) Ban migration  c) Build more roads  d) Reduce the birth rate and slow growth
**Answer: d) Reduce the birth rate and slow growth**
