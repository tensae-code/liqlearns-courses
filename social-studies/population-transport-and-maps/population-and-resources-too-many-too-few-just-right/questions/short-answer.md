# Population and resources — too many, too few, just right — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three marks of over-population.
**Answer:** Low per-capita income; high unemployment/underemployment; malnutrition/famine; low living standards; outward migration (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Which policy fits a country pressing against its resources, and why?
**Answer:** Anti-natalist — it reduces the birth rate and slows growth toward the balance.
