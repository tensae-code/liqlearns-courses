# Population and resources — too many, too few, just right — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Outward migration is a mark of over-population.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Under-population is marked by high unemployment.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A pro-natalist policy tries to increase the birth rate.
**Answer: True**
