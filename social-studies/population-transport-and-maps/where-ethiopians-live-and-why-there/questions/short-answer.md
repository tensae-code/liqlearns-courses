# Where Ethiopians live — and why there — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two densely peopled areas besides the highlands.
**Answer: SNNP and the cities Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa (any two).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the physical reasons.
**Answer: Altitude, rainfall, soil and disease.**
