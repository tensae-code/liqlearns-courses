# Where Ethiopians live — and why there — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population density is —
a) people per family  b) towns per region  c) people per square kilometre
**Answer: c) people per square kilometre**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The arid lowlands are —
a) empty of people entirely  b) sparsely peopled  c) the densest areas
**Answer: b) sparsely peopled**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A physical reason for the pattern is —
a) rainfall  b) infrastructure  c) history
**Answer: a) rainfall**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A human reason is —
a) altitude  b) disease  c) economy and infrastructure
**Answer: c) economy and infrastructure**
