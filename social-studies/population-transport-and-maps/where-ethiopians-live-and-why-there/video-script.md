# Video script — Where Ethiopians live — and why there

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

People are not spread evenly over Ethiopia.
Dense in the highlands. Dense in the southern region and the cities.
Sparse in the arid lowlands.
Rain, soil, health, history — that is why.
