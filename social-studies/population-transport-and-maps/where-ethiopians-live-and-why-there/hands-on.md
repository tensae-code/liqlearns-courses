# Hands-on — Where Ethiopians live — and why there

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Density reading
If your area holds about 500 people on 2 square kilometres, work out its density. Then say what would raise or lower it.

## Reason sort
Write eight reason cards — rainfall, soil, disease, altitude, history, economy, infrastructure, aridity — and sort them into physical and human piles.
