# Where Ethiopians live — and why there

## Do it first

Look at a population-density map of Ethiopia: dark patches and pale wastes in the same country. Read why people crowd in one place and thin out in another.

## Sub lesson 1 — The uneven map

Population is unevenly distributed. The highlands are dense; SNNP, Addis Ababa and Dire Dawa are also densely peopled. The arid lowlands are sparse. Density — people per square kilometre — is the measure that states the unevenness exactly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why there

The reasons are physical — altitude, rainfall, soil and disease — and human — history, economy and infrastructure. Good rain, workable soils, healthy altitudes and long-settled farming draw people in; aridity and disease push them away.
