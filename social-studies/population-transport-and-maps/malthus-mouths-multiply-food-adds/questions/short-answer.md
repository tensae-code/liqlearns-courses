# Malthus — mouths multiply, food adds — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Write the two growth sequences.
**Answer:** Population: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16… Food: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5…

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the positive checks.
**Answer:** Hunger, disease and war.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do neo-Malthusians differ from Malthus?
**Answer:** They keep the imbalance but make family planning the preventive check.
