# Malthus — mouths multiply, food adds — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Malthus wrote in —
a) 1798  b) 1965  c) 1998
**Answer: a) 1798**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Population, for Malthus, grows —
a) geometrically  b) arithmetically  c) not at all
**Answer: a) geometrically**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hunger, disease and war are —
a) positive checks  b) preventive checks  c) remedies
**Answer: a) positive checks**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Neo-Malthusians substitute, as the preventive check, —
a) family planning  b) war  c) migration
**Answer: a) family planning**
