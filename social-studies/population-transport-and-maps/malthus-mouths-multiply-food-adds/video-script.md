# Video script — Malthus — mouths multiply, food adds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Thomas Malthus, writing in 1798, set two staircases against each other.

When population outruns food, Malthus said it is cut back by 'positive checks' — hunger, disease and war.

Neo-Malthusians keep the imbalance but substitute a modern preventive check: family planning.
