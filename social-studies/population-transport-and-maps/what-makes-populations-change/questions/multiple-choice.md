# What makes populations change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population change is driven by —
a) births, deaths and migration  b) climate alone  c) censuses
**Answer: a) births, deaths and migration**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The modern growth turn came mainly from —
a) births rising sharply  b) migration stopping  c) deaths falling while births stayed high
**Answer: c) deaths falling while births stayed high**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is a historical shock named in the key-term frame?
a) the railway  b) the slave trade  c) the printing press
**Answer: b) the slave trade**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration affects the total by —
a) both adding and subtracting  b) only adding  c) neither
**Answer: a) both adding and subtracting**
