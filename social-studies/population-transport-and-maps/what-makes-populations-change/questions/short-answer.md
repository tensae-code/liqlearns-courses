# What makes populations change — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define population change.
**Answer:** The difference in a population's size over a period, driven by births, deaths and migration.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did medicine and living-standard gains change?
**Answer:** They cut infant, child and maternal mortality and raised life expectancy, powering growth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three historical shocks in the key-term frame.
**Answer:** Colonisation, the slave trade and deadly diseases.
