# Video script — What makes populations change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Population change is the difference in size over a period of time.

The modern turn came from the death flow: medicine and living-standard gains cut infant, child and maternal mortality and raised life expectancy, while births stayed high — so populations grew fast.

The key-term frame names the historical shocks: colonisation, the slave trade and deadly diseases all cut or moved populations by force.
