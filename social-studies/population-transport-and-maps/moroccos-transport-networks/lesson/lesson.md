# Morocco's Transport Networks

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Morocco's Grade 5 teacher guide teaches the homeland's transport — the kinds of transport and their roles, locating Morocco's ports, airports, railways and motorways. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kinds and roles of transport

The eleventh Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Features of my homeland — transport. It teaches the kinds of transport and the role of each: the different ways people and goods move around the homeland, and what each kind is for.

## Sub lesson 2 — Locating ports, airports, railways and motorways

Then the lesson goes to the map: the learner locates Morocco's ports, airports, railways and motorways. The networks are not a list to copy — they are placed on the homeland itself, where they actually run.
