# Hands-on — Morocco's Transport Networks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the kinds
List the kinds of transport you know and write the role of each in one line, the way the guide's lesson pairs kinds with roles.

## Put the networks on the map
On a map of Morocco, mark a port, an airport, a railway and a motorway where they belong, as the lesson locates them.
