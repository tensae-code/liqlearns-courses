# Morocco's Transport Networks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's Geography Lesson 11 teaches —
a) transport  b) only climate  c) only farming
**Answer: a) transport**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each kind of transport is taught with its —
a) role  b) ancient name  c) price in dirhams
**Answer: a) role**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On the map the learner locates ports, airports, railways and —
a) motorways  b) volcanoes  c) oases only
**Answer: a) motorways**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The transport networks are located —
a) in Morocco, on its map  b) in another continent  c) in no place at all
**Answer: a) in Morocco, on its map**
