# Ways governments slow population growth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Table 4.1's first-ranked method is…
a) family planning  b) the child tax  c) one-child policies
**Answer: b) the child tax**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The child tax limits exemptions usually to the first…
a) two children  b) five children  c) ten children
**Answer: a) two children**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** China's one-child programme began in…
a) the 1950s  b) the late 1970s / early 1980s  c) 2015
**Answer: b) the late 1970s / early 1980s**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** China's policy was announced in late 2015 to end in…
a) early 2016  b) 2020  c) never
**Answer: a) early 2016**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Family planning is framed as…
a) a rights, equality and poverty-reduction measure  b) a punishment  c) a tax
**Answer: a) a rights, equality and poverty-reduction measure**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** About how many governments subsidise family planning?
a) 13  b) 130  c) all of them
**Answer: b) 130**
