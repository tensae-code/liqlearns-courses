# Ways governments slow population growth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three ranked methods in order.
**Answer:** 1 The child tax; 2 one-child policies; 3 family planning.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the child tax work?
**Answer:** Tax exemptions are limited to a set number of children (usually the first two); parents are charged beyond it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is family planning's most popular instrument?
**Answer:** Contraception — temporary and irreversible methods.
