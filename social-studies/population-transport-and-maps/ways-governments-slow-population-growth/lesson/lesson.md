# Ways governments slow population growth

## Do it first

Rank these yourself before reading: a tax, a legal limit, free voluntary planning services. Which is fairest? Which works? Then compare with the book's Table 4.1.

## Sub lesson 1 — The ranked table — tax and limit

The book's Table 4.1 ranks the main population control methods. First, the child tax: tax exemptions limited to a set number of children — usually the first two — charging parents beyond that number.

Second, one-child policies: China's official programme, running from the late 1970s and early 1980s, restricting most families to one child. It was announced in late 2015 to end in early 2016 — the most drastic method, with a dated end.

## Sub lesson 2 — Family planning — the voluntary method

Third comes family planning: access to safe, voluntary means of planning family size, framed as a human-rights, gender-equality and poverty-reduction measure. Contraception — temporary and irreversible methods — is its most popular instrument worldwide.

The unit closes with control practice from various cultures, and two closing facts: over half of developing countries now have national population policies, and about 130 governments subsidise family planning. The voluntary method is the one the world settled on.
