# Video script — Ethiopia's population — growth, distribution, policy (guide evidence)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The 2010–11 guide's population series runs 11.8 million (1900) to 73.8 million (2007), making Ethiopia Africa's third most populous country in its framing — with urbanisation recent and a national population policy taught by its goals and strategies. (Teacher-guide evidence, dated.) First, the growth series (teacher guide — evidence): This lesson stands on the 2010–11 teacher's guide alone; every figure is dated and era-labelled. Then, distribution, urbanisation, policy (teacher guide — evidence): The guide presents urbanisation as a recent phenomenon, beginning in the late 19th century, and its review tasks ask learners to enumerate the three most and least urbanised regions and to discuss the consequences of Addis Ababa's high urbanisation rate. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
