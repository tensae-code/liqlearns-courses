# Ethiopia's population — growth, distribution, policy (guide evidence) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the guide's four-point series with years.
**Answer:** 11.8 million (1900); 23.5 million (1960); 42.6 million (1984); 73.8 million (2007).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does this lesson teach the series and not '63.8 million'?
**Answer:** Because the guide itself is internally layered: the series ends at the 2007 census figure of 73.8 million, while 63.8 million is an un-updated passage from an earlier round.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the guide's review tasks ask about urbanisation?
**Answer:** To enumerate the three most and least urbanised regions and discuss the consequences of Addis Ababa's high urbanisation rate.
