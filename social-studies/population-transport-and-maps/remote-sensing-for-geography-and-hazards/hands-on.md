# Hands-on — Remote Sensing For Geography And Hazards

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Geographical techniques and skills 3: Remote sensing (سنجش از دور) in geographical studies — gathering information about the phenomena of Earth's surface by indirect observation with sensors and processing it; digital satellite images recording phenomena the human eye cannot see, such as Earth's energy; the growing use of satellite images in studying and predicting natural hazards; activity: enter the Iranian Space Agency's site (isa.ir) and open its chosen satellite images; the Google Earth toolbar keys explained. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
