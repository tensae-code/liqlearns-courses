## Sub lesson 1 — Remote Sensing For Geography And Hazards

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 111), teaches: Geographical techniques and skills 3: Remote sensing (سنجش از دور) in geographical studies — gathering information about the phenomena of Earth's surface by indirect observation with sensors and processing it; digital satellite images recording phenomena the human eye cannot see, such as Earth's energy; the growing use of satellite images in studying and predicting natural hazards; activity: enter the Iranian Space Agency's site (isa.ir) and open its chosen satellite images; the Google Earth toolbar keys explained. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
