# Hands-on — Irans Country Divisions Provinces And Counties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Iran's country divisions: province (ostan), county (shahrestan), district and rural district, from past to present, and reading them on the map. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
