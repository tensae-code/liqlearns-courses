# Lesson plan — Africa urbanising — fastest and slowest

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Africa urbanising — fastest and slowest
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 4 — Population of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Africa is urbanising unevenly: by the book's 2016 table the urban share runs from Southern Africa's 62% down to Eastern Africa's 26%, with Gabon near 90% urban at the top and Burundi at 12% the least urbanised — and the urban share, about 41%, will pass the rural share in about two decades.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the population lessons of Unit 4 — distribution and density are its neighbours in this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **The five regions — the league table** — Urban shares from 62% down to 26%.
2. **Fastest and slowest — Gabon and Burundi** — The country extremes.
3. **What the movement costs and brings** — Strain — and the rural pair.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Where Africans live — density map. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Give the five regions' 2016 urban shares in order.
2. Name Gabon (~90%) and Burundi (12%) as the extremes.
3. State the continent's ~41% share and its projected crossing of the rural share.
4. Explain rural–urban migration as the engine and name the strain it brings.
