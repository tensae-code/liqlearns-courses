# Africa urbanising — fastest and slowest

## Do it first

Is your own country mostly town or mostly countryside? Guess Africa's split: what share of Africans live in towns today — a quarter, two-fifths, two-thirds? And which region is most urban? Guess, then check the table in this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The five regions — the league table

The book's 2016 table ranks Africa's five regions by **urban share** — the percentage of people living in urban places. **Southern Africa** leads at **62%**. **Northern Africa** follows at **51%**, **Central Africa** at **47%**, **Western Africa** at **45%** — and **Eastern Africa** is the **least urbanised region** at **26%**.

Read the table as a spread, not a single story: one region is nearly two-thirds urban while another is barely one-quarter. 'Africa is urbanising' is true everywhere, but at very different levels and speeds.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fastest and slowest — Gabon and Burundi

At country level the extremes are wider still. **Gabon**, near **90% urban**, heads the book's top-20 list of most urbanised countries. At the other end, **Burundi at 12%** is the **least urbanised** country in the table — nearly nine of every ten Burundians live rurally.

For the continent as a whole, the urban share stands at **about 41%** — and the book projects it will **pass the rural share in about two decades**: the moment when, for the first time, more Africans live in towns than in the countryside.

## Sub lesson 3 — What the movement costs and brings

The engine of urbanisation is **rural–urban migration** — people moving from countryside to town — and it brings **strain on services and infrastructure**: housing, water, transport and jobs must grow as fast as the crowds arriving. The town's promise and the town's pressure arrive together.

This lesson's pair is the countryside it leaves behind: rural settlement, taught in its own lesson in this title. Hold both: a rising urban share is always, at the same time, a changing rural share.

## Say it to your companion

Recite the league table to your companion in order, then the two country extremes. Your companion asks: which region is least urbanised, and what happens continent-wide in about two decades?
