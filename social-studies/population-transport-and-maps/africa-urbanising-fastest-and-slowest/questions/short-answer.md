# Africa urbanising — fastest and slowest — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the five regions with their urban shares in order.
**Answer:** Southern 62%, Northern 51%, Central 47%, Western 45%, Eastern 26%.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does Burundi's 12% mean in plain words?
**Answer:** Only about twelve of every hundred Burundians live in urban places — nearly nine in ten live rurally, making it the least urbanised country in the table.
