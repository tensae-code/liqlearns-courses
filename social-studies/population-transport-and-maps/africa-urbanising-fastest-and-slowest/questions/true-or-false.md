# Africa urbanising — fastest and slowest — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Northern Africa's urban share (51%) is higher than Central Africa's (47%).
**Answer: True** — 51 against 47 in the table.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Eastern Africa is Africa's most urbanised region.
**Answer: False** — at 26% it is the least urbanised.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The continent's urban share, about 41%, is projected to pass the rural share in about two decades.
**Answer: True** — the book's projection, printed pp. 113–115.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Urban growth brings no strain if people keep arriving.
**Answer: False** — it strains services and infrastructure — housing, water, transport, jobs.
