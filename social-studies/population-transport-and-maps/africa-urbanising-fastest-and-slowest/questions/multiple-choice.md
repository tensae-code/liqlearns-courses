# Africa urbanising — fastest and slowest — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The most urbanised region in the 2016 table is —
a) Northern Africa 51%  b) Southern Africa 62%  c) Western Africa 45%  d) Eastern Africa 26%
**Answer: b) Southern Africa 62%** — the top of the league table.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Eastern Africa's urban share is —
a) 62%  b) 47%  c) 26%  d) 12%
**Answer: c) 26%** — the least urbanised region.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The country near 90% urban, heading the list, is —
a) Burundi  b) Gabon  c) Ethiopia  d) Chad
**Answer: b) Gabon** — the top of the top-20 list.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Burundi's urban share, the least in the table, is —
a) 41%  b) 26%  c) 12%  d) 51%
**Answer: c) 12%** — nearly nine in ten live rurally.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The engine of urbanisation is —
a) international tourism  b) rural–urban migration  c) rural–rural migration  d) urban–rural migration only
**Answer: b) rural–urban migration** — people moving countryside to town.
