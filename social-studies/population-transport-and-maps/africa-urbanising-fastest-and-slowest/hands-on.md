# Hands-on — Africa urbanising — fastest and slowest

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## League-table bars
Draw five bars for the five regions at their true heights — 62, 51, 47, 45, 26. Add Gabon and Burundi as two thin flags at 90 and 12. Check the order against the lesson.

## Crossing lines
On graph paper draw two lines across twenty years: urban rising from 41, rural falling from 59. Mark where they cross and write what that crossing means in one sentence.
