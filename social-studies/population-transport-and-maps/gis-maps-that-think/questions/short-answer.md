# GIS — maps that think — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five components.
**Answer:** Data, software, hardware, people, methods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Separate primary from secondary data.
**Answer:** Primary = gathered directly (field survey, remote sensing); secondary = made by others (paper maps, existing datasets).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one nominal and one ratio example.
**Answer:** Nominal: a town's name. Ratio: its population (any correct pair).
