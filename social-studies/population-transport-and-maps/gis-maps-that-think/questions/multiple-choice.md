# GIS — maps that think — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GIS stands for…
a) Global Imaging Service  b) Geographical Information System  c) Geographic Internet System
**Answer: b) Geographical Information System**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It captures, stores, manipulates, analyses, manages and…
a) presents spatial data  b) prints posters  c) draws cartoons
**Answer: a) presents spatial data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its five components are data, software, hardware, people and…
a) methods  b) money  c) maps
**Answer: a) methods**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Primary data are…
a) made by others  b) gathered directly, e.g. field survey and remote sensing  c) always old
**Answer: b) gathered directly, e.g. field survey and remote sensing**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Paper maps used as data are…
a) primary  b) secondary  c) useless
**Answer: b) secondary**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Levels of measurement are nominal, ordinal, interval and…
a) ratio  b) verbal  c) visual
**Answer: a) ratio**
