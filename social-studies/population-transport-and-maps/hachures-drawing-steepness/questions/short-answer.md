# Hachures — drawing steepness — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are hachures?
**Answer:** Short disconnected lines drawn down the direction of slope.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do steep and gentle hachures differ?
**Answer:** Steep: thick, short, close. Gentle: thin, long, wide apart.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two limits of hachures.
**Answer:** No heights, no exact gradients, laborious, hard to read (any two).
