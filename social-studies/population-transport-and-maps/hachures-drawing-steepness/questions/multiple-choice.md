# Hachures — drawing steepness — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Hachures are drawn —
a) down the direction of slope  b) across the slope  c) around peaks only
**Answer: a) down the direction of slope**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Thick, short, close hachures mean —
a) steep slopes  b) gentle slopes  c) flat land
**Answer: a) steep slopes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Thin, long, wide-apart hachures mean —
a) gentle slopes  b) steep slopes  c) water
**Answer: a) gentle slopes**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Hachures do not give —
a) heights or exact gradients  b) slope direction  c) steepness feel
**Answer: a) heights or exact gradients**
