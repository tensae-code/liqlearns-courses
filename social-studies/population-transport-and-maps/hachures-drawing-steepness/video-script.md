# Video script — Hachures — drawing steepness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hachures are short disconnected lines drawn down the direction of slope or water flow.

The rule is tactile: thick, short and close together means steep slopes; thin, long and wide apart means gentle ones.

Hachures give no heights and no exact gradients; they are laborious to draw and hard to read precisely.
