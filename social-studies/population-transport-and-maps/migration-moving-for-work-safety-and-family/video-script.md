# Video script — Migration — moving for work, safety and family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Migration moves the balance B−D+I−E between places: one country's loss is another's gain, and the world's total does not change.

Today's moves include forced migration from war and disaster, and trafficking.

Labour-exporting senders include Bangladesh, Nepal and the Philippines; conflict-driven senders include Syria, Venezuela and Myanmar.
