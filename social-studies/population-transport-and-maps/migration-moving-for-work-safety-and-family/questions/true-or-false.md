# Migration — moving for work, safety and family — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forced migration comes from war and disaster.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Migration increases the world's total population.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Temporary labour migration runs mainly from poorer to richer economies.
**Answer: True**
