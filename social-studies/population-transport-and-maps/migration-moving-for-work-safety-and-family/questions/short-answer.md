# Migration — moving for work, safety and family — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does migration do, if not grow the total?
**Answer:** It redistributes people between places.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three push factors.
**Answer:** Weak jobs, low pay, large populations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two sender kinds in the lesson.
**Answer:** Labour-exporting countries and conflict-driven senders.
