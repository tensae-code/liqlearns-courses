# Migration — moving for work, safety and family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration's effect on world population is —
a) redistribution, not growth  b) growth  c) decline
**Answer: a) redistribution, not growth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the balance B−D+I−E, I and E stand for —
a) immigration and emigration  b) income and export  c) infants and elders
**Answer: a) immigration and emigration**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A push factor is —
a) weak jobs at home  b) high pay abroad alone  c) good weather
**Answer: a) weak jobs at home**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A labour-exporting sender country is —
a) Bangladesh  b) Japan  c) Canada
**Answer: a) Bangladesh**
