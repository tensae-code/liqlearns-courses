**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Suriname).**

Suriname's own way: the OJW workbook works one worksheet per lesson — draw, colour, match — moving from the child's body and senses out to the wider world; Geography enters as colouring Suriname's own outline map and flag. This lesson keeps that way: one workbook lesson (Les 5) is taught as its two colouring tasks — the outline map with its rivers, then the flag — and the learner colours both as the book shows them. No flag colours or river names are stated in this lesson, because the placement names none; the colouring follows the book's own page. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed. The workbook's non-Geography parts (senses, emotions, body, food, plants and animals, hygiene lessons) are named in the placements file and are not placed in this lesson.
