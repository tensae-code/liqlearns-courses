# Video script — Colouring Suriname's Map and Flag

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Here is a map with no colours yet.

It is the outline of Suriname — the shape of the whole country. Look: its rivers are drawn inside it.

Colour the map, the way the book shows it. Now you can see the country's shape and its rivers.

And here is the flag of Suriname. Colour it too, the way the book shows it. A flag is a country's own symbol.

Now hold up your page. That is your country: its map and its flag.
