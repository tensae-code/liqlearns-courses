# Hands-on — Colouring Suriname's Map and Flag

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Colour the outline map
Take the outline map of Suriname as the workbook gives it — the country's shape with its rivers drawn in. Trace the outline and each river with your finger first, saying what each line is. Then colour the map as the book shows it. When you finish, name out loud what your coloured map shows: the country's shape, and its rivers.

## Colour the flag
On the same workbook page, colour the Suriname flag as the book shows it. Lay your coloured flag next to your coloured map and tell a partner, in two sentences, what each one is: the map shows the country's shape and rivers; the flag is the country's own symbol.
