# Colouring Suriname's Map and Flag — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two things does the workbook's outline map of Suriname let you learn as you colour it?
**Answer: The shape of the country (its outline) and where its rivers run (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do the map and the flag on the workbook page belong together?
**Answer: The map shows the country's shape and its rivers — its land; the flag is the country's own symbol; together they put the whole country on one page (any close wording).**
