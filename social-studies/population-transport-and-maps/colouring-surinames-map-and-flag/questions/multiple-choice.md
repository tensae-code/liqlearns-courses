# Colouring Suriname's Map and Flag — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The workbook's outline map shows —
a) only one town  b) the shape of the whole country of Suriname, with its rivers  c) the whole world
**Answer: b) the shape of the whole country of Suriname, with its rivers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Les 5 Kleuren (Colours), the first thing you do with the outline map is —
a) cut it up  b) fold it away  c) colour it, as the book shows it
**Answer: c) colour it, as the book shows it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Besides the map, the same lesson asks you to colour —
a) the flag of Suriname  b) a house  c) a plant
**Answer: a) the flag of Suriname**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A flag is —
a) a river on the map  b) a country's own symbol  c) the outline of a town
**Answer: b) a country's own symbol**
