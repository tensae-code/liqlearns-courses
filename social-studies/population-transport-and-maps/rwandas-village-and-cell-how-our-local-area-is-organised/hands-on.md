# Hands-on — Rwanda's Village and Cell — How Our Local Area Is Organised

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ibiranga Umudugudu n'uburyo bwo kubyitaho: features of our village and how to care for them (Unit 2); Ibiranga Akagari n'uburyo bwo kubyitaho: features of our cell and how to care for them (Unit 2); Ibiranga Umudugudu n'ibiranga Akagari: features of our village and our cell, and how to care for them (Unit 2, Ishuri, Umudugudu n'Akagari). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Rwanda's way
Say how Rwanda's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
