# Rwanda's Village and Cell — How Our Local Area Is Organised — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Rwanda's Village and Cell — How Our Local Area Is Organised  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Ibiranga Umudugudu n'uburyo bwo kubyitaho: features of our village and how to care for them (Unit 2); Ibiranga Akagari n'uburyo bwo kubyitaho: features of our cell and how to care for them (Unit 2); Ibiranga Umudugudu n'ibiranga Akagari: features of our village and our cell, and how to care for them (Unit 2, Ishuri, Umudugudu n'Akagari)  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
