# Altitude decides settlement — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The favoured belt is about —
a) 0–500 m  b) 3,500–4,500 m  c) 1,800–2,600 m
**Answer: c) 1,800–2,600 m**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In that belt, the population share is —
a) exactly equal to the area share  b) far larger than the area share  c) smaller than the area share
**Answer: b) far larger than the area share**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Below the favoured belt, settlement is thinned by —
a) heat and disease  b) cold and steepness  c) lack of rivers everywhere
**Answer: a) heat and disease**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Above the favoured belt, settlement is thinned by —
a) malaria  b) flooding  c) cold and steepness
**Answer: c) cold and steepness**
