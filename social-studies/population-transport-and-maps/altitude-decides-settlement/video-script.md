# Video script — Altitude decides settlement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Lay the height bands side by side with the people bands. They do not match.
Between 1,800 and 2,600 metres, people crowd far beyond the land share.
Too hot and sickly below. Too cold and steep above.
Altitude decides settlement.
