# Hands-on — Altitude decides settlement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bar pairs
Draw paired bars for three bands — low, middle-high, highest — one bar for area share, one for population share, using the lesson’s pattern. Mark where population beats area.

## Bargain debate
Two partners argue: one defends the lowlands, one the highest tops. The middle partner judges, using the altitude bargain.
