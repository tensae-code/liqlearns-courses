## Sub lesson 1 — From the everyday journey

Theme 3 re-teaches mobility on France itself: Du quotidien aux vacances — from the everyday to holidays.

## Sub lesson 2 — To holidays — the diversity of French mobility

The lesson's thing is la diversité des mobilités des Français — the diversity of the ways French people move, daily trips and holiday trips held in one frame.

France's own way: every theme is built the same way — a world chapter taught through one deep case study (Los Angeles megafires, the Amazon, Japan, the Mediterranean), then the same theme re-taught on France itself, closing with map-sketch (croquis) and mind-map assessment; debate and role-play (nuclear debate, G7 simulation) are standard geography methods. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
