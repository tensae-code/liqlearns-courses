# Population policies — government's open and hidden hand

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — What policy is

A population policy is government's open or hidden hand on population size, growth, distribution and composition. Historically policies were pro-natalist — urging more births; now they are mostly anti-natalist and redistribution-minded.

## Sub lesson 2 — Direct and indirect measures

A table separates them. Direct: free family planning, child taxes, migration law, marriage age. Indirect: compulsory schooling, child-labour limits, female education, old-age security — measures that change family choices without naming population at all.

## Sub lesson 3 — The distribution toolkit

For where people live, the toolkit lists redistribution to smaller towns, slowing rural-to-urban flows, services for the urban poor, settling under-populated areas, and moving people out of fragile environments.
