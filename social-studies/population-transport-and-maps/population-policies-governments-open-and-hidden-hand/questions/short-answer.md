# Population policies — government's open and hidden hand — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define population policy.
**Answer:** Government's open or hidden hand on population size, growth, distribution and composition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two direct and two indirect measures.
**Answer:** Direct: family planning, child taxes, migration law, marriage age. Indirect: schooling, child-labour limits, female education, old-age security (two of each).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two distribution tools.
**Answer:** Redistribution to smaller towns; slowing rural-urban flows; services for the urban poor; settling under-populated areas; moving people from fragile environments (any two).
