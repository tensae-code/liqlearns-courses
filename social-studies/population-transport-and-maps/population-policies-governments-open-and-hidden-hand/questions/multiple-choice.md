# Population policies — government's open and hidden hand — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Historically, most policies were —
a) pro-natalist  b) anti-natalist  c) about migration only
**Answer: a) pro-natalist**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Policy acts on size, growth, distribution and —
a) composition  b) climate  c) soil
**Answer: a) composition**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Free family planning is a —
a) direct measure  b) indirect measure  c) distribution tool
**Answer: a) direct measure**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Female education works as an —
a) indirect measure  b) direct measure  c) migration law
**Answer: a) indirect measure**
