Uzbekistan's book, the *Uzbekistan Grade 6 Geografiya (Soatov & Abdulqosimov)* (printed p. 150), teaches: §65 Population of the globe. Uzbekistan's book, the *Uzbekistan Grade 9 Economic and Social Geography of the World (Qayumov, Safarov & Tillaboyeva)* (printed p. 33), teaches: §8 Population of the globe and its dynamics.

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 6 Geografiya): Geography in Grade 6 is the physical geography of continents and oceans in the Russian/Soviet textbook tradition — first the Earth's geographic envelope and the World Ocean, then every continent in a fixed order (position, geological structure and relief, climate, inland waters, natural zones, population), closing with the population of the Earth and the political map of the world; nearly every chapter ends in a practical work.

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 9 Economic and Social Geography of the World): Grade 9 turns from physical to economic and social geography of the whole world: Part I works through the political map, world natural resources, world population, the scientific-technical revolution and the branches of the world economy; Part II then reviews every region in a fixed country-profile pattern (territory and position, natural resources, population, economy, transport, foreign ties), with Central Asia and Uzbekistan's neighbours given the fullest treatment, and closes with the global problems of humanity.
