**Acceptance count: taught in 10 countries (Ethiopia, Ukraine, Bolivia, Fiji, Uganda, Sudan, Mauritania, Afghanistan, Iran, Honduras). Myanmar is named here as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Ukraine's own way: A "Geography: Regions and Countries" course — every region is done in the same order (position, nature/resources, population, primary/secondary/tertiary sectors) then typological country studies with rubrics (country visiting-card, capital, pride of the country, unique experience), ending with Ukraine's own place in the world; research tasks (Дослідження) embedded in chapters. That way is stocked here as the way Ukraine teaches this thing.

Myanmar's own way (teacher guide — evidence): the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week — physical geography, social geography, environmental geography, neighbouring countries and cartography — each lesson plan built student-centred (Bloom steps, key questions, classroom assessment) around Myanmar and its five neighbours. That evidence is stocked here as the way Myanmar teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Bolivia's own way (Grade 7, Secundaria 1): The Bolivian Texto de Aprendizaje runs every unit through the same four moments — Práctica, Teoría, Valoración, Producción — and frames geography inside the plurinational, Madre Tierra worldview: continental drift and geological time sit beside the Laws of Mother Earth (071 and 300), the human right to water, and a closing general geography of Bolivia itself (regions, orography, political map, salt flats and lithium).

Fiji's own way (Fiji Years 11–12 Geography): Senior geography splits into Human and Physical volumes; the human volume is organised as four issue-chapters (population/settlement, tourism, agriculture, marine resources), each run the same way — nature, problems, ways of minimising, contemporary issues, future — with Pacific/Fiji theme-country cases.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 8 UG S1 Geography Student): Competency-based lower-secondary course taught entirely through East Africa: every skill (maps, fieldwork, photographs) is practised on Ugandan/East African places (Tororo topographic map, Uganda lat/long map), and the physical half is East Africa's relief, landform formation, climate and vegetation rather than world coverage.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 11 SD Geography 11): The human-geography year: population first (races, religions, census, density, dynamics, structure), then a full practical unit on distribution maps and graphs, then regional geography — the world studied continent by continent as regions — and a closing full unit on Sudan itself, natural basis to environmental problems.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 8 geo-2as-m): A world-regional year: one unit of concepts (physical, human, economic geography), one unit walking the five inhabited continents by their natural and human features, and one unit of paired-country economic case studies (Egypt/South Africa, Brazil/Canada, India/Japan, Spain/Germany, Australia/New Zealand).

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 7 Geography (Dari)): the Dari MoE pattern — the world topic is taught first in the abstract (sphere, equator, latitude), then re-taught as Afghanistan's own geography (its position, relief, six climate regions, river basins, lakes, minerals, fuels), then widened to the continent (Asia). Every lesson ends with in-class group activity, questions and an out-of-class task naming the pupil's own locality.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 8 Geography (Dari)): the continent-by-continent march continues — after Asia in Grade 7, Grade 8 does Europe then the Americas, each continent in the same fixed order: position, mountains, rivers, lakes, seas, gulfs, climate, political geography, human geography (race, language, religion), economic geography, mines/industries, transport.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 9 Geography (Dari)): the continent march finishes with Africa, then Australia/Oceania and Antarctica — same fixed order per continent as Grade 8, with a data table of the independent countries of Africa appended to the Africa chapter.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran): the whole of Grade 10 geography is one national book — Iran's own natural and human geography for every branch; nearly every lesson closes with a "know your own living place" activity tied to the separate provincial-studies book, so national content is always re-applied to the learner's own province.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 5 Student Book): widening-circles design — the Americas — Honduras placed inside its continent: American population, resources, transport, trade and tourism. Every lesson runs the same 'Sembrar y cosechar' activity cycle.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 5 Teacher Guide) (teacher guide — evidence): widening-circles design — the Americas — Honduras placed inside its continent: American population, resources, transport, trade and tourism — the teacher guide mirrors the student book lesson-for-lesson (same 38 lessons and blocks) and adds didactics, theory summaries and answer keys; its geography lessons are placed here at the guide's own printed pages. Every lesson runs the same 'Sembrar y cosechar' activity cycle. That evidence is stocked here as the way Honduras teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Honduras is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).
