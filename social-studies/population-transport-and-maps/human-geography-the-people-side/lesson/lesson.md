# Human geography — the people side

## Do it first

Same country, other lens: not the land, but the people on it — where they live, how they work, how they move. Read the people side.

## Sub lesson 1 — The people side

Human geography studies the distribution and influence of human features and activities: population, settlement, economic activity, culture and transport. Units 4 and 5 of this course — population and economic/cultural activities — are its Ethiopian home ground.

## Sub lesson 2 — The counterpart

Human geography is the counterpart to physical geography: where the physical branch reads the land, the human branch reads what people do on the land — and geography’s full definition needs both, because the two continually change each other.
