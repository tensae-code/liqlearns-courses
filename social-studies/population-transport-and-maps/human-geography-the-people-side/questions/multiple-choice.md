# Human geography — the people side — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Human geography studies —
a) landforms  b) climate  c) human features and activities
**Answer: c) human features and activities**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A subject of human geography is —
a) ocean currents  b) transport  c) soil formation
**Answer: b) transport**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Human geography is the counterpart to —
a) physical geography  b) climatology  c) oceanography
**Answer: a) physical geography**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Its home-ground units in this course are —
a) Units 1 and 2  b) Unit 8 only  c) Units 4 and 5
**Answer: c) Units 4 and 5**
