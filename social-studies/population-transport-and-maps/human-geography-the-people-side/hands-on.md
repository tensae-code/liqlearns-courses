# Hands-on — Human geography — the people side

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Town audit
Take one town you know. List everything human geography would study in it — its people count, its settlement shape, its work, its culture, its transport — one example each.

## Counterpart pairs
Pair each physical fact with the human fact it shapes: steep gorge → isolated settlement; rich soil → dense farming. Make four pairs and defend them.
