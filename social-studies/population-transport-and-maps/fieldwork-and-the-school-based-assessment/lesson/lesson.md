## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: geographical fieldwork and project method that close the CSEC course, as Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC (2nd ed.) teaches it in School-Based Assessments. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Geographical fieldwork — learning outside the classroom

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC closes with the School-Based Assessments: geographical fieldwork — going out to study a real place with geographical questions, instead of only reading about places in a book.

## Sub lesson 2 — The project method — the course's closing project

The assessment is a project: the project method carries the fieldwork through — a question chosen, evidence gathered in the field, and the project written up — and it closes the whole CSEC course.

Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
