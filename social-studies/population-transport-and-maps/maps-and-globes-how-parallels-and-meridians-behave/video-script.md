# Video script — Maps and globes — how parallels and meridians behave

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Slice an orange with rings parallel to its middle, then slice it in wedges through both ends. Which rings are biggest? Are the wedges' edges equal? You have just done this lesson with your hands — now read its names.

This lesson teaches: how the two line families behave — parallels shrinking from the equator to nothing at the poles, meridians all equal through both poles. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
