# Hands-on — Maps and globes — how parallels and meridians behave

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Cut rings in paper of shrinking sizes and stack them equator-to-pole.

## Activity 2
Draw three meridians on a ball or orange and compare their lengths by string.

## Activity 3
Explain the shrinking to a partner using a ball and a belt moved up toward its top.

## With a partner
Tell your companion which parallel is longest, what a parallel measures at the pole, and why all meridians are equal.
