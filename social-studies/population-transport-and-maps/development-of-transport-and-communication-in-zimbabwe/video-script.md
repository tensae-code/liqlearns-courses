# Video script — Development of Transport and Communication in Zimbabwe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe's Grade 5 teacher guide, the *PlusOne Social Science Grade 5 Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 51), teaches: Unit 18: Development of transport and communication.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 5): Grade 5 transport is taught as a landlocked country's system — goods by rail/road/air/sea, animal transport (riding, leading, herding), and the roadports, seaports and airports Zimbabwe depends on; shelter links heritage sites (Matobo Hills) and map-drawing of Zimbabwe to building safely in disaster-prone areas.
