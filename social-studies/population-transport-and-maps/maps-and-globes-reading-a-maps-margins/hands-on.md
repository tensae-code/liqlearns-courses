# Hands-on — Maps and globes — reading a map's margins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Find a real map and tick off its seven margin items; note any that are missing.

## Activity 2
Work the scale for 1 cm = 1 km, using the formula. (Answer: 1:100,000.)

## Activity 3
Measure your classroom and draw it to a chosen scale with all seven margin items.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the seven margin items, the no-mark north rule, and walk them through the 1 cm = 5 km example to 1:500,000.
