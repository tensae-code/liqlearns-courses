# Maps and globes — reading a map's margins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many margin items does the book list?
a) Three  b) Five  c) Ten  d) Seven
**Answer: d) Seven**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A map with no direction mark has north —
a) At the bottom  b) On the left  c) At the top  d) Nowhere
**Answer: c) At the top**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The key/legend tells you —
a) The map's price  b) What the signs and colours mean  c) The weather  d) Who drew the borders
**Answer: b) What the signs and colours mean**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Scale = —
a) Map distance ÷ ground distance  b) Ground distance ÷ map distance  c) Map distance × ground distance  d) Title ÷ date
**Answer: a) Map distance ÷ ground distance**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 1 cm = 5 km gives the scale —
a) 1:5,000  b) 1:50  c) 5:1  d) 1:500,000
**Answer: d) 1:500,000**
