# Maps and globes — reading a map's margins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name any four of the seven margin items.
**Answer:** Scale, title, key/legend, north/direction, date, location lines, publisher (any four).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why must both distances be in the same unit before dividing?
**Answer:** So the division compares like with like — centimetres with centimetres.
