# Hands-on — Managing City Transport

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 4: Managing urban transport — air pollution and rising breathing diseases, headaches, tiredness, stress and blood pressure among the great cities' problems, with noise and visual pollution from cars and motorcycles; bus rapid transit (BRT) built in the world's cities since 1990, and Istanbul's street tram pictured; the old cycling culture of some Iranian cities (Isfahan, Yazd, Kashan, Bonab and Miandoab, where most families own bicycles); transport management in our country — Iran's very fitting geographical and transit position in the region. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
