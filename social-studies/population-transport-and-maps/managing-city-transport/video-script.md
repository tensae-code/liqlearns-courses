Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 67), teaches: Lesson 4: Managing urban transport — air pollution and rising breathing diseases, headaches, tiredness, stress and blood pressure among the great cities' problems, with noise and visual pollution from cars and motorcycles; bus rapid transit (BRT) built in the world's cities since 1990, and Istanbul's street tram pictured; the old cycling culture of some Iranian cities (Isfahan, Yazd, Kashan, Bonab and Miandoab, where most families own bicycles); transport management in our country — Iran's very fitting geographical and transit position in the region.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
