# Video script — Too many needs, too few services — DC population challenges

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The list is comprehensive: environmental change and biodiversity loss, slum growth, chronic poverty, food and water insecurity, poor health and education, rural–urban migration, housing shortage, inequality, climate hazards and transport shortage — in a world where 5 percent of people use 23 percent of energy, while 40 percent lack suitable hygiene..

No item on the list stands alone: slums grow where housing fails, disease spreads where hygiene fails, and migration answers rural poverty.

The remedy list deliberately answers the challenge list: life-course investment in human capital, healthy ageing, sustainable urban planning, special care for the vulnerable, international migration cooperation, rural–urban integration, and changing fertility through girls' education and reproductive health..
