# Too many needs, too few services — DC population challenges — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** People using 23% of the world's energy make up —
a) 5% of people  b) 40% of people  c) 23% of people
**Answer: a) 5% of people**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** People lacking suitable hygiene make up —
a) 40%  b) 5%  c) 23%
**Answer: a) 40%**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Slum growth belongs to —
a) the challenge list  b) the remedy list  c) neither
**Answer: a) the challenge list**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Changing fertility through girls' education is —
a) a remedy in the list  b) a challenge  c) a cause of slums
**Answer: a) a remedy in the list**
