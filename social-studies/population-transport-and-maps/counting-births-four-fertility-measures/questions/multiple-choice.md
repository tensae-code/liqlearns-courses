# Counting births — four fertility measures — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The crude birth rate is blind to —
a) age structure  b) the count of births  c) the year
**Answer: a) age structure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** TFR is built across how many five-year age groups?
a) seven  b) five  c) fifteen
**Answer: a) seven**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's TFR worked example resolves to —
a) 2 births per woman  b) 4.6  c) 1.0
**Answer: a) 2 births per woman**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The GFR counts births per 1,000 women aged —
a) 15–49  b) 25–64  c) 0–14
**Answer: a) 15–49**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Africa's crude birth rate in mid-2020, as printed, was —
a) 2 per woman  b) 149 per 100  c) 33 per 1,000
**Answer: c) 33 per 1,000**
