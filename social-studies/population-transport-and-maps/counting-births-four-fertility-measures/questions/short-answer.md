# Counting births — four fertility measures — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the CBR called crude?
**Answer:** It ignores the population's age structure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does GRR count, and what does 1.0 mean?
**Answer:** Daughters only; about 1.0 means replacement.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Whom does the GFR count births against?
**Answer:** Women aged 15 to 49, per 1,000.
