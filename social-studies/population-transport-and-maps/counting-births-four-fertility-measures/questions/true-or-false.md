# Counting births — four fertility measures — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The gross reproduction rate counts daughters only.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A GRR around 1.0 means each generation replaces itself.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The crude birth rate is the most refined measure.
**Answer: False**
