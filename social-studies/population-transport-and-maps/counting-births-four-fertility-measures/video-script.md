# Video script — Counting births — four fertility measures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The crude birth rate counts births per 1,000 people in a year.

The total fertility rate is built from age-specific rates across the seven five-year age groups; the book's worked example resolves to 2 births per woman.

The general fertility rate counts births per 1,000 women aged 15 to 49.
