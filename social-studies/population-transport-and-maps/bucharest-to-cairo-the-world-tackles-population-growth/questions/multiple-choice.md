# Bucharest to Cairo — the world tackles population growth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bucharest's conference was in…
a) 1974  b) 1994  c) 2015
**Answer: a) 1974**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many countries took part at Bucharest?
a) 36  b) 136  c) 196
**Answer: b) 136**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The World Population Plan of Action tied family planning to…
a) resource conservation and living standards  b) military spending  c) nothing
**Answer: a) resource conservation and living standards**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Cairo's conference was in…
a) 1974  b) 1984  c) 1994
**Answer: c) 1994**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Cairo said pressure comes jointly from growth in poor countries and…
a) high consumption in wealthy ones  b) cold climates  c) cities
**Answer: a) high consumption in wealthy ones**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Cairo's root attacks are…
a) women's education, health care, participation  b) taxes only  c) borders
**Answer: a) women's education, health care, participation**
