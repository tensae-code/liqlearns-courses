# Bucharest to Cairo — the world tackles population growth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Bucharest's Plan of Action do?
**Answer:** It tied family-planning programmes to resource conservation and living standards, agreed by 136 countries in 1974.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is 'the humane turn' at Cairo?
**Answer:** Reframing stabilisation to attack roots — women's education, health care and participation — instead of family planning alone, and counting rich-world consumption as part of the pressure.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Cairo's three root areas.
**Answer:** Women's education, health care, and economic and political participation.
