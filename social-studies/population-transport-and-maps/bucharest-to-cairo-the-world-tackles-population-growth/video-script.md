# Video script — Bucharest to Cairo — the world tackles population growth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Between Bucharest (1974) and Cairo (1994) the world's answer to population growth changed: from family-planning programmes alone to attacking the roots — women's education, health and participation. First, bucharest 1974 — the organised response begins: The organised world response starts at the 1974 Bucharest World Population Conference: 136 countries agreeing a World Population Plan of Action. Then, cairo 1994 — the humane turn: Twenty years later, the 1994 Cairo International Conference on Population and Development reframed the issue. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
