**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Syria, Iran, Colombia).**

Colombia's own way (Colombia Grade 6 Secundaria Activa G6): Secundaria Activa teaches the world economy comparatively — Asia's three agricultures, Japan's people, oil exporters — and names Colombia's own apertura económica debate.

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 7 Geography G7 عالم الجغرافية): a pure Geography book built entirely around activity lessons with story-style titles ("Mother of sciences", "Stones from the sky", "The origami art" for folding, "A tourist in my homeland"); technology first — aerial photos, GPS, remote sensing and GIS open the book before any physical geography — and each semester closes with student/teacher-designed lessons and an end-of-semester project.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 7 Social Studies): production and consumption are taught with Iranian anchors throughout — the historic bazaar as market and social centre, compulsory third-party car insurance, and the Silk Road crossing Iran as a bridge between East and West.
