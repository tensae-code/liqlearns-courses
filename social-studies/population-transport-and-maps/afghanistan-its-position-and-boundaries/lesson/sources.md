**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Afghanistan).**

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 7 Geography (Dari)): the Dari MoE pattern — the world topic is taught first in the abstract (sphere, equator, latitude), then re-taught as Afghanistan's own geography (its position, relief, six climate regions, river basins, lakes, minerals, fuels), then widened to the continent (Asia). Every lesson ends with in-class group activity, questions and an out-of-class task naming the pupil's own locality.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)): primary social studies starts from the child's own world — family, village, city — and only then opens the map: directions, Afghanistan's boundaries and mountains and rivers, the continents and oceans, and finally the Earth's shape, equator, latitude/longitude and movements.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 6 Social Studies (Dari)): Grade 6 social studies turns outward from the state to the neighbourhood — after the provinces of Afghanistan, the six neighbouring countries each get a lesson (Pakistan, Iran, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, China) before the book turns to civics and history.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 12 Geography (Dari)): the final year is pure human geography — Afghanistan's borders side by side (north, north-east, east/south, west), its population and ethnic make-up, religion and language, transport and communications, then the same themes for the world: population, races, religions, languages, global communications, and global environmental problems to close the whole twelve-year course.
