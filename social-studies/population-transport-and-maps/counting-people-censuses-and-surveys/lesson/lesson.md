# Counting people — censuses and surveys

## Do it first

How many people live in Ethiopia? Nobody counted them one by one yesterday. The number comes from instruments — read the four counters.

## Sub lesson 1 — What population means

Population is the number of people living in a defined area — a village, a region, a country. The definition’s key word is defined: every population figure belongs to a boundary and a date.

[Picture 21091 — a census enumerator at a doorway with a form]

## Sub lesson 2 — The four sources

Geographers draw on four sources: censuses — full counts at set dates; vital registration — the continuous record of births and deaths; surveys — counted samples; and estimates — figures worked forward between counts. Each source has limits of cost, coverage or accuracy that the reader must know.
