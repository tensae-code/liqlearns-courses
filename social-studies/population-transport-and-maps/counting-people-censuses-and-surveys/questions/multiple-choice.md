# Counting people — censuses and surveys — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population is —
a) the number of workers  b) a town’s area  c) the number of people in a defined area
**Answer: c) the number of people in a defined area**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Every population figure belongs to —
a) no date  b) a boundary and a date  c) a region only
**Answer: b) a boundary and a date**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A census is —
a) a full count at a set date  b) a sample  c) a guess
**Answer: a) a full count at a set date**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Vital registration records —
a) only births  b) land ownership  c) births and deaths continuously
**Answer: c) births and deaths continuously**
