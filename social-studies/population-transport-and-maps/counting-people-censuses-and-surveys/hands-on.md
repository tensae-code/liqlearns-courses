# Hands-on — Counting people — censuses and surveys

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Class census
Run a real mini-census of your class or household block: define the area, fix the moment, count everyone once. Then list what could go wrong — absence, double counting — as the census’s limits.

## Source match
Match four questions to their best source: "How many were born this year?" / "How many live in this region now?" — census, registration, survey or estimate, with reasons.
