# Video script — Counting people — censuses and surveys

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Population: people in a defined area, at a date.
Counted four ways — census, vital registration, survey, estimate.
A census counts everyone. A survey counts a sample.
Every source has limits. Know them, and the numbers behave.
