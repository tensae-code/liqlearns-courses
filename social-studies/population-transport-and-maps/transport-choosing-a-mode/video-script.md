# Video script — Transport — choosing a mode

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A farmer has grain, a trader has oil, a patient needs a hospital in the city. Each journey wants a different carrier. This lesson is the judgement table that chooses well — fill it in as you read.

This lesson teaches: judging modes against each other — carrying capacity, speed, cost and fitness for Ethiopia's terrain — and transport's role in farming, industry and trade. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
