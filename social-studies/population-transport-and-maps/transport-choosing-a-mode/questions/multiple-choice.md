# Transport — choosing a mode — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Which count is NOT one of the four the table judges?
a) Carrying capacity  b) Speed  c) Cost  d) The colour of the vehicle
**Answer: d) The colour of the vehicle**

**2.** The mode with the greatest carrying capacity is —
a) The donkey  b) The aeroplane  c) The ship  d) The car
**Answer: c) The ship**

**3.** The guide keys LAND transport's advantage as (teacher guide — evidence) —
a) Low speed  b) Door-to-door service  c) Floating loads  d) No roads needed
**Answer: b) Door-to-door service**

**4.** The guide keys AIR transport's disadvantage as (teacher guide — evidence) —
a) High cost  b) Low maintenance  c) Door-to-door cost  d) Slow travel
**Answer: a) High cost**
