# Hands-on — Transport — choosing a mode

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Draw the comparison table for donkey, car, train, ship and aeroplane from memory.

## Activity 2
Choose a mode for three loads from your area and defend each choice on the four counts.

## Activity 3
Name your area's dominant mode and the terrain reason for it.

## With a partner
Tell your companion which mode wins on capacity, which on speed, which on cost for heavy goods — and which modes dominate your own area, and why.
