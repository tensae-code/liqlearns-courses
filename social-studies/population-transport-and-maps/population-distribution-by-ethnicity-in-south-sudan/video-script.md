The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Geography SB* (printed p. 107), teaches: Population distribution according to ethnicity. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies TG* (printed p. 61), teaches: Guide: Unit 4 — where peoples settled: the Acholi and Bari in Equatoria; the Azande, Mundu, Baka and Avukaya also in the Equatorial region. (teacher guide — evidence)

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Geography SB): Each general topic is immediately re-taught through South Sudan — its named rivers (Sobat, Adar, Yabus, Daga), the Sudd swamplands, its oil, fishing and transport problems, and case-study research tasks set by a mock ministry brief.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide grounds every weather idea in farm and river work — wind for threshing and winnowing, rough water making fishing risky — and teaches that forests help in the formation of rain. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.
