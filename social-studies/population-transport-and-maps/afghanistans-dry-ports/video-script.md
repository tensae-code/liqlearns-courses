# Video script — Afghanistans Dry Ports

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari)* (printed p. 91), teaches: Lesson 46: the ports of our country (a landlocked country's dry ports and border crossings).

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari)): Grade 5 social studies is an economic tour of Afghanistan for children — public services first (water, electricity, telecom, post, transport), then farming crop by crop, livestock animal by animal, industries craft by craft and factory by factory, trade and the country's (dry) ports, ending with environment, climate and mines.
