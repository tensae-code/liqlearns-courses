## Sub lesson 2 — Bhutan's way — the book's own teaching way

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 11 Geography): The Advanced level (XI–XII) is full academic geography — map projections, remote sensing and GIS, insolation and pressure belts, climate classification — with Bhutan applied chapters on agriculture, manufacturing and tourism, the last taught on Bhutan's own sustainability principle ("high value, low volume" in the Bhutan Tourism Monitor data the book uses).

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 12 Geography): Class XII closes the Advanced course with specialist topics (surveying, geospatial technologies, glaciers and the "Third Pole", Köppen in full, fluvial processes, Davis's cycle of erosion) and Bhutan data chapters — fertility and mortality trends in Bhutan, migration trends, hydropower in Bhutan, and creative industries including Bhutanese film. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
