Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 11), teaches: Lesson 2 Man and the region (انسان و ناحیه): regions shape people's activities, and people with knowledge and technology reshape regions — the UAE building artificial islands in the Persian Gulf; regions interact and exchange with each other; political and administrative regions (province, district, county) rarely match natural and human regions, yet all regions are run by political or administrative bodies (councils, municipalities).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).
