# Video script — Population policies — encouraging or discouraging babies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Governments steer fertility in both directions: pro-natalist policies raise it with support for families; anti-natalist policies lower it, most famously China's one-child policy. First, direct and indirect influence: A population policy is a government's deliberate attempt to influence fertility and growth. Then, pro-natalist and anti-natalist: Pro-natalist policy seeks to raise fertility: incentives, parental leave, workplace equality measures, and — for ageing, low-fertility societies — facilitated immigration. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
