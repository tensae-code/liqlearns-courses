# Population policies — encouraging or discouraging babies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A population policy is…
a) an accident  b) a government's deliberate attempt to influence fertility and growth  c) a census
**Answer: b) a government's deliberate attempt to influence fertility and growth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Direct influence works through…
a) reproductive health and family planning access  b) weather  c) trade
**Answer: a) reproductive health and family planning access**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Indirect influence works through…
a) contexts shaping fertility choices  b) decrees only  c) borders
**Answer: a) contexts shaping fertility choices**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Pro-natalist policy seeks to…
a) lower fertility  b) raise fertility  c) freeze fertility
**Answer: b) raise fertility**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Pro-natalist tools include…
a) incentives, parental leave, workplace equality, facilitated immigration  b) child taxes  c) one-child rules
**Answer: a) incentives, parental leave, workplace equality, facilitated immigration**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The famous anti-natalist case is…
a) China's one-child policy  b) Sweden's parental leave  c) Ethiopia's policy
**Answer: a) China's one-child policy**
