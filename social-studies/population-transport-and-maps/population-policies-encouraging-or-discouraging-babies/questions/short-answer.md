# Population policies — encouraging or discouraging babies — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distinguish direct from indirect population policy.
**Answer:** Direct: access to reproductive health and family planning. Indirect: shaping the contexts in which fertility choices are made.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three pro-natalist measures.
**Answer:** Any three of: incentives; parental leave; workplace equality measures; facilitated immigration.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the most famous anti-natalist case?
**Answer:** China's one-child policy.
