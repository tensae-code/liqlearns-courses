# Population — Ethiopia's growth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Ethiopia's population is given as an estimated —
a) 15,000,000  b) 50,000,000  c) 200,000,000  d) 115,000,000
**Answer: d) 115,000,000**

**2.** Ethiopia's yearly growth rate is given as —
a) 0.54%  b) 5.24%  c) 2.54%  d) 10%
**Answer: c) 2.54%**

**3.** 2.54% a year means about how many added per 1,000 people?
a) 2  b) 25  c) 250  d) 1
**Answer: b) 25**

**4.** At that rate Ethiopia's population doubles in about —
a) 30 years  b) 3 years  c) 300 years  d) 100 years
**Answer: a) 30 years**
