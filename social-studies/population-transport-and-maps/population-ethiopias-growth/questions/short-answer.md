# Population — Ethiopia's growth — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name two things Ethiopia's growth must be judged against.
**Answer:** Resources/environment and social, economic or political conditions (any two named areas).

**2.** Why must services double when population doubles, just to stand still?
**Answer:** Because twice as many people need twice the schools, clinics, water and jobs for the same level of service.
