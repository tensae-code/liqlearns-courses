# Video script — Population — Ethiopia's growth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethiopia is one of the fastest-growing countries in the world. Before you read the numbers, guess: how many people live in Ethiopia today, and how many are added each year out of every 1,000? Then check your guess against the lesson.

This lesson teaches: Ethiopia's own numbers — about 115 million people growing at 2.54% a year, doubling in about 30 years — and what that means against resources. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
