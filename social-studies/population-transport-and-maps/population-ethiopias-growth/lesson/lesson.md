# Population — Ethiopia's growth

## Do it first

Ethiopia is one of the fastest-growing countries in the world. Before you read the numbers, guess: how many people live in Ethiopia today, and how many are added each year out of every 1,000? Then check your guess against the lesson.

## Ethiopia's numbers

Ethiopia's population is given as an estimated **115,000,000** (in 2013 E.C.), growing at **2.54% a year**.

[Picture 11082 — Ethiopia's growth curve with the doubling point marked]

What does 2.54% mean in real people? About **25 added per 1,000 people every year**. Keep that rate and the population **doubles in about 30 years** — one generation. That is among the fastest growth rates in the world.

## Growth against the real world

A growth rate is never just a number. Set it against the real things it presses on: **resources** — land, water, food; the **environment** — forests, soil; and the **social, economic and political conditions** — schools, jobs, health services, peace.

Look at Ethiopia's past, present and future growth together: the same curve that means more workers and more life also means every service must double just to stand still. Holding both truths at once is the skill of this lesson.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion Ethiopia's estimated population, its yearly growth rate, and the doubling time. Then name one resource the growth presses on.
