# Hands-on — Population — Ethiopia's growth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Write Ethiopia's numbers from memory: population, rate, added per 1,000, doubling time.

## Activity 2
Draw Ethiopia's growth curve and mark where it doubles.

## Activity 3
List two services in your town that must grow when population doubles.

## With a partner
Tell your companion Ethiopia's estimated population, its yearly growth rate, and the doubling time. Then name one resource the growth presses on.
