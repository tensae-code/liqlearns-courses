# Lesson plan — Population — Ethiopia's growth

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Population — Ethiopia's growth
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ethiopia's own numbers — about 115 million people growing at 2.54% a year, doubling in about 30 years — and what that means against resources
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the two population lessons before it in this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Population — why the world grew fast. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State Ethiopia's estimated population and growth rate from the book.
2. Say what 2.54% a year means in people per 1,000.
3. Say about how long doubling takes at that rate.
4. Judge Ethiopia's growth against resources, environment and social, economic and political conditions.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A globe or ball helps where the lesson names one. Nothing else.
