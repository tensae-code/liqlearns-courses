# Video script — The five parts of a GIS

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hardware splits into input (computers, GPS, scanners, digitisers) and output (printers, plotters).

Data is a component in its own right, the subject of the next lesson.

Methods are each organisation's designed plan and rules for using its GIS.

The Grade 10 book names the same five components — hardware, software, data, people and methods — and spells out the people: administrators, managers, technicians, application experts, end users and consumers.
