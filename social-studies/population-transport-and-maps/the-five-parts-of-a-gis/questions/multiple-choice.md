# The five parts of a GIS — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GIS has how many components?
a) five  b) three  c) seven
**Answer: a) five**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Scanners and digitisers are —
a) input hardware  b) output hardware  c) software
**Answer: a) input hardware**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** QGIS is —
a) free GIS software  b) a printer  c) a database
**Answer: a) free GIS software**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The component the book warns about is —
a) people — technology has limited value without them  b) hardware  c) methods
**Answer: a) people — technology has limited value without them**
