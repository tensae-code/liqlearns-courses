# The five parts of a GIS — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the five components.
**Answer:** Hardware, software, data, people, methods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the ESRI family and the free alternative.
**Answer:** ArcView, ArcInfo, ArcGIS; free QGIS.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the warning.
**Answer:** The technology has limited value without the people who manage it.
