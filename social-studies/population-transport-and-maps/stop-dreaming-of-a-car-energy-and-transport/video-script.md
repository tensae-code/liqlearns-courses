# Video script — Stop Dreaming of a Car — Energy and Transport

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Folha 15 commands Pare de sonhar com um carro! / Stop dreaming of a car! The dream of a car of one's own is challenged directly — the learner is asked to stop taking the private car as the natural goal of transport.

The Folha teaches energy, cars and transport together: a car is an energy choice as well as a transport choice, so dreaming of a car is dreaming of a way of spending energy. Transport choices are judged with their energy side in view.
