# Stop Dreaming of a Car — Energy and Transport

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Folha 15: Pare de sonhar com um carro! / Stop dreaming of a car! — energy, cars and transport, as Brazil's Grade 10 Geografia (SEED-PR) teaches it in one of its 17 Folhas, each opening with a provocative question. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stop dreaming of a car! — the opening challenge

Folha 15 commands Pare de sonhar com um carro! / Stop dreaming of a car! The dream of a car of one's own is challenged directly — the learner is asked to stop taking the private car as the natural goal of transport.

## Sub lesson 2 — Energy, cars and transport choices

The Folha teaches energy, cars and transport together: a car is an energy choice as well as a transport choice, so dreaming of a car is dreaming of a way of spending energy. Transport choices are judged with their energy side in view.

Brazil's book has no chapters in the old style: it is 17 "Folhas" (sheets) like this one, each opening with a provocative question, grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
