The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Colombia's CS_Grado3_01 book, the *Ciencias Sociales 3 — Primera Cartilla, Escuela Nueva* (printed p. 76), teaches: Unidad 3 Guía 5 Explorando mi país: Colombia and its departments, departmental capitals, borders (some along rivers) on the political map.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado3_01): Grade 3's first cartilla is the orientation cartilla — cardinal points and maps as guides, a different map for each kind of information, then Colombia's political map with its departments and capitals explored department by department.
