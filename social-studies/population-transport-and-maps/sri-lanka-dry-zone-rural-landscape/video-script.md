The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Sri Lanka's book, the *Sri Lanka Grade 8 G8 English* (printed p. 42), teaches: Unit 3 Landscape IV — Rural: Sri Lanka's Dry Zone tank-centred villages, paddy and chena cultivation, livestock, irrigation projects (Gal Oya, Udawalawa), Adivasi people, Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 8 G8 English): a four-unit arc — Solar System, Earth's uniqueness, then regional geography taught as six South Asian "landscapes" (mountain, valley, coastal, rural, industrial-urban, desert), closing with Sri Lanka's own 1:50,000 topographic-sheet system (92 sheets) that Grade 9 continues.
