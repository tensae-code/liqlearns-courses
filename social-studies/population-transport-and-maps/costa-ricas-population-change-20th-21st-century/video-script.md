# Video script — Costa Rica's Population Change — 20th and 21st Century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Costa Rica's Grade 10 CONED anthology gives its own country a full population lesson. In Unit 3 Tema I B it follows population change in Costa Rica across the 20th and 21st centuries — how the numbers grew and changed, and what drove the change — as the home case inside the book's closing Geography unit on population dynamics and spatial mobility, where every world pattern is paired with the Costa Rican case.

Costa Rica learns this inside its own book's way: two-thirds contemporary History/geopolitics, then a closing Geography unit on population dynamics and spatial mobility that always pairs the world pattern with the Costa Rican case (Costa Rica's own population change, internal and international mobility). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
