# Video script — Population — counting and graphing growth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before any world number: go to your own school's record office (or ask your teacher) and fetch the enrolment — how many pupils in each grade, how many boys and how many girls. Write the numbers down. You have just done what every population counter in the world does: count real people, in real groups, from real records.

This lesson teaches: how to count people where you are — starting with your own school — and show growth in bar and line graphs. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
