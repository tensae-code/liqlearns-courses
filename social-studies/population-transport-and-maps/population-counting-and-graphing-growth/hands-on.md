# Hands-on — Population — counting and graphing growth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Fetch your school's enrolment by grade and sex and write it as a table.

## Activity 2
Draw the last five or ten years as a bar graph, then as a line graph.

## Activity 3
Work out one year's percentage growth and say what the growth did to your classrooms.

## With a partner
Tell your companion your school's total enrolment, its biggest grade, and its ten-year growth. Then say one thing the growth changed at your school.
