# Population — counting and graphing growth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do you fetch your school's enrolment numbers?
a) A guess from the playground  b) The market  c) A newspaper from another town  d) The school record office
**Answer: d) The school record office**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A good enrolment count is split by —
a) Height and weight  b) Village and town only  c) Grade and sex  d) Day and night
**Answer: c) Grade and sex**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A line graph is best for —
a) Counting one pupil  b) Seeing how a number climbs over years  c) Naming the grades  d) Drawing the school
**Answer: b) Seeing how a number climbs over years**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Yearly percentage growth means —
a) Growth out of every hundred people  b) The total number of pupils  c) The number of classrooms  d) The size of the play space
**Answer: a) Growth out of every hundred people**
