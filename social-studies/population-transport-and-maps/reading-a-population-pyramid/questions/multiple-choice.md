# Reading a population pyramid — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a population pyramid, bars are stacked by —
a) income  b) region  c) age group
**Answer: c) age group**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Males and females are shown —
a) in separate pyramids only  b) on opposite sides  c) in one bar
**Answer: b) on opposite sides**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Comparing pyramids across censuses shows —
a) demographic change over time  b) nothing  c) only migration
**Answer: a) demographic change over time**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** If birth rates fall over time, the base —
a) widens  b) vanishes  c) narrows
**Answer: c) narrows**
