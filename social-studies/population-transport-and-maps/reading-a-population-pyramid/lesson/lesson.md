# Reading a population pyramid

## Do it first

A bar chart shaped like a pyramid: wide at the bottom, narrow at the top. That shape is a whole country’s story. Read Ethiopia’s pyramid.

## Sub lesson 1 — The pyramid’s shape

A population pyramid stacks horizontal bars by age group, males on one side and females on the other. Ethiopia’s pyramid is broad-based: the widest bars are the youngest ages, narrowing steadily upward — the portrait of a very young population. The guide’s marking form defines the pyramid as this age–sex diagram (teacher guide — evidence).

## Sub lesson 2 — Change over time

Compared across censuses, pyramids change: as birth rates and death rates shift, the base widens or narrows and the top thickens. Reading two pyramids of the same country is reading its demographic history in two portraits.
