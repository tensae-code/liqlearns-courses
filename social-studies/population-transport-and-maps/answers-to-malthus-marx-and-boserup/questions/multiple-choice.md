# Answers to Malthus — Marx and Boserup — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** For Marx, poverty is the product of —
a) capitalism  b) nature  c) climate
**Answer: a) capitalism**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The 'surplus population' is kept jobless to —
a) discipline wages  b) raise output  c) cut prices
**Answer: a) discipline wages**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Boserup's answer dates from —
a) 1965  b) 1798  c) 1909
**Answer: a) 1965**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Birth reduction alone delivers development —
a) never, without institutions, policy, jobs and equity  b) always  c) in one year
**Answer: a) never, without institutions, policy, jobs and equity**
