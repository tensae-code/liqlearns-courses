# Answers to Malthus — Marx and Boserup — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Boserup holds that population growth drives agricultural invention.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Marx blamed nature for poverty.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A demographic dividend opens when economic growth outruns population growth.
**Answer: True**
