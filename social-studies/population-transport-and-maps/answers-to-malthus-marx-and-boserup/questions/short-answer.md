# Answers to Malthus — Marx and Boserup — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State Marx's answer in one sentence.
**Answer:** Poverty is capitalism's product; the surplus population disciplines wages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Boserup's famous line, and what does it mean here?
**Answer:** 'Necessity is the mother of invention' — more mouths call forth more food.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must accompany birth reduction for development?
**Answer:** Institutions, policy, jobs and equity.
