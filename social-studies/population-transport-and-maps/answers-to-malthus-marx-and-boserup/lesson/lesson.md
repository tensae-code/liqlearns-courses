# Answers to Malthus — Marx and Boserup

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: two thinkers looked at Malthus's staircase and disagreed — how? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Marx — poverty is made, not natural

Marx's reply: poverty is capitalism's product, not nature's. The 'surplus population' is kept jobless to discipline wages — unemployment is a tool of the system, and socialism is the remedy.

[Picture 27077 — portraits of three thinkers in a row — Malthus, Marx, Boserup — with a one-line claim under each]

## Sub lesson 2 — Boserup — mouths call forth food

Boserup's 1965 answer runs the other way: population growth drives agricultural invention. 'Necessity is the mother of invention' — more mouths call forth more food, because farmers innovate when they must.

## Sub lesson 3 — The book's balance

If economic growth outruns population growth, per-capita income rises and a demographic dividend opens. But birth reduction alone never delivers development without institutions, policy, jobs and equity alongside it.
