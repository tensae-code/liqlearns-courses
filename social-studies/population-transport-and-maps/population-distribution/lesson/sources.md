Ukraine's own way: A "Geography: Regions and Countries" course — every region is done in the same order (position, nature/resources, population, primary/secondary/tertiary sectors) then typological country studies with rubrics (country visiting-card, capital, pride of the country, unique experience), ending with Ukraine's own place in the world; research tasks (Дослідження) embedded in chapters. That way is stocked here as the way Ukraine teaches this thing.

Haiti's own way (teacher sequence — evidence): Population geography is built entirely on Haiti's own data, handled critically: pupils confront the national statistics institute (IHSI) with international sources, note that the last census is 2003, build density maps and age pyramids themselves, then read the whole country through Port-au-Prince's primacy and the diaspora («les Haïtiens de l'extérieur»). That evidence is stocked here as the way Haiti teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project. That way is stocked here as the way Barbados teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Pairing warning (honesty): Book 2 is an Addis Ababa bureau guide in English; Book 1 is an Amhara bureau student book in Amharic. They are NOT edition-matched companions — the guide's unit order differs and whole student topics (maps, globes, GPS) have no guide counterpart, so no guide coverage is claimed for them. Guide material is used only as labelled teacher evidence.

Guatemala's own way: one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book per grade, organised in four units that alternate History of Guatemala/Central America with the physical geography of the isthmus — every map carries the note about the unresolved Belize territorial differendum, and Geography is always worked on maps of the seven Central American countries with Guatemala's own Sierra de las Minas, Motagua river and Maya Biosphere as the home examples. That way is stocked here as the way Guatemala teaches this thing.

Costa Rica's own way: two-thirds contemporary History/geopolitics, then a closing Geography unit on population dynamics and spatial mobility that always pairs the world pattern with the Costa Rican case (Costa Rica's own population change, internal and international mobility). That way is stocked here as the way Costa Rica teaches this thing.

Ecuador's own way (Grade 5 book): the 2024 Grade 5 book is History-led (five of eight sections are national history, from the roots of society to the Republic); Geography is concentrated at the end, where the country is re-read spatially — Andean relief and rivers, natural regions and climatic floors, population diversity and migration, territorial division, provincial products, and the protected-area system taught together with climate-change action. That way is stocked here as the way Ecuador teaches this thing.

Honesty note: the ministry's 2024 transición-curricular PDFs, as held, reuse one body per sublevel — the Grade 5 body also carries the Grade 6 and Grade 7 covers (Subnivel Media), and the Grade 8 body also carries the Grade 9 and Grade 10 covers (Subnivel Superior). That content is credited once, under its true Grade 5 / Grade 8 identity, exactly as the placements state; the genuine Ecuador Grade 6, 7, 9 and 10 Estudios Sociales books are NOT held (library debt).

China's own way: senior-high human geography built entirely around China's own questions — population opens the book (world distribution, push–pull migration, carrying capacity and the Hu Huanyong line as a self-study window), then villages and towns, then where each industry sits and how that changes, then transport and regional development in both directions, closing with the environment and China's own national strategies (main functional zones, the Yangtze Economic Belt, blue-economy space); every chapter ends with a problem inquiry drawn from Chinese everyday life (migrant workers, choosing where to live, physical shops, city traffic, low-carbon food), and two sections carry geographic-information-technology applications (GIS in city management inside Chapter 2 §2; GIS in delivery services inside Chapter 3 §3). That way is stocked here as the way China teaches this thing.

China's own way (teacher guide — evidence): the teacher guide mirrors the student book section by section, each with teaching aims, case material and problem-inquiry （问题研究） and knowledge-extension （知识拓展） apparatus — population and settlement are taught as China's own human-geography core before industry location is reached. That evidence is stocked here as the way China teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
