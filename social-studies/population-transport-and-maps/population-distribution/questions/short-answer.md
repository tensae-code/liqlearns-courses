# Population — distribution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the guide's three densely populated areas (teacher guide — evidence).
**Answer:** The Asiatic population belt, Europe and the American cluster.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one natural and one human factor that shape where people live.
**Answer:** Natural: water/soil/climate/relief. Human: farming/industry/trade/towns/history (any one of each).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why do people prefer cities, as the guide keys it?
**Answer:** Jobs, infrastructure, education, services and transport.
