# Population — distribution — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population is spread evenly over the Earth's land.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A hot-dry land with no water usually stays thinly settled.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rural settlements are keyed to planned building and office work.
**Answer: False**
