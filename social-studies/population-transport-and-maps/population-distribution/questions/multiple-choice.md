# Population — distribution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two-thirds of humanity crowds onto —
a) Half the land  b) All the land equally  c) The southern hemisphere  d) One-seventh of the land
**Answer: d) One-seventh of the land**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How much of humanity lives in the northern hemisphere?
a) 50%  b) 10%  c) 90%  d) 25%
**Answer: c) 90%**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a NATURAL factor shaping distribution?
a) A new factory  b) Fertile soil  c) A trade route  d) A town council
**Answer: b) Fertile soil**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Migration means —
a) A permanent change of residence  b) Any trip to market  c) Carrying goods one day  d) Visiting relatives
**Answer: a) A permanent change of residence**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** No work and drought at home are —
a) Pull factors  b) Settlements  c) Densities  d) Push factors
**Answer: d) Push factors**
