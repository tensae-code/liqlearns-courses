# Video script — Population — distribution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Picture the world at night from space: bright knots of light, and vast dark lands. People are not spread like butter on bread — they crowd into a few places and leave most land nearly empty. Your job in this lesson: find the pattern, then find its reasons.

This lesson teaches: where people live and why — an uneven world, the factors that shape it, and (from the teacher guide) migration, push and pull, and rural and urban settlement. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
