# Hands-on — Population — distribution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Draw a dot map of your kebele or neighbourhood: dots where people crowd, empty where they do not.

## Activity 2
Write the factor that explains your most crowded place and your emptiest place.

## Activity 3
Interview one person who moved to your area: what pushed, what pulled? Write both.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the two fractions that show how uneven the world is, one natural and one human factor, and one push and one pull factor from your own area's story.
