# Video script — Urban and Rural Parishes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador's smallest territorial division is the parish — parroquia. The books teach two kinds: urban parishes, inside a town or city, and rural parishes, in the countryside around it. Grade 3 learns it through the child's own ground — mi parroquia y mi cantón, my parish and my canton — so the learner first names the parish they live in, then says whether it is urban or rural and why.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: (Grade 2 book) Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. (Grade 3 book) the older SM edition is a classic skills-first book — a whole opening unit on orientation, cardinal points, the compass rose and plans before any content; every unit runs the same skeleton (content lessons, “Ser Estudiante” test, Cultura del Buen Vivir, scientific work, digital skills, unit evaluation), and the country is learned parish → canton → province → nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
