# Video script — Maps and globes — latitude and longitude

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Someone phones you from a ship in the middle of an ocean: 'Come and get us!' — but where ARE they? No town, no road, no landmark. The Earth needs an address system that works on empty water. This lesson is that system.

This lesson teaches: the line grid that gives every place its absolute location — parallels and meridians, the equator and the prime meridian, and the 23½° lines. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
