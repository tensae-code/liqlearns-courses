# Maps and globes — latitude and longitude — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Parallels run —
a) Top-to-bottom  b) Diagonally  c) Only on land  d) Side-to-side
**Answer: d) Side-to-side**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Together, parallels and meridians give a place's —
a) Population  b) Climate  c) Absolute location  d) Height
**Answer: c) Absolute location**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The prime meridian passes through —
a) Addis Ababa  b) Greenwich  c) The North Pole  d) Cancer
**Answer: b) Greenwich**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Latitude runs from the equator to —
a) 90°  b) 180°  c) 360°  d) 23½°
**Answer: a) 90°**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The 23½° line in the north is —
a) Capricorn  b) The equator  c) The prime meridian  d) Cancer
**Answer: d) Cancer**
