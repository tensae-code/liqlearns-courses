# Hands-on — Maps and globes — latitude and longitude

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
On a globe or ball, trace one parallel and one meridian with your finger.

## Activity 2
Find the equator and the prime meridian on a map and say where they cross.

## Activity 3
Say the absolute location idea in one sentence to a younger pupil, using a grid on paper.

## With a partner
Tell your companion which family runs side-to-side, which top-to-bottom, the two 0° lines' names, and the two degree limits.
