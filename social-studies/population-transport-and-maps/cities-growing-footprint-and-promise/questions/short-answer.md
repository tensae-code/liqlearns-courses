# Cities growing — footprint and promise — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define urbanisation as the book does.
**Answer:** The rural-to-urban shift of population, and society's adaptation to it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two marks make the city's footprint?
**Answer:** Land consumed at the edge — forests, wetlands, farmland cleared and fragmented — and pollution raised inside.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one development benefit of well-managed cities.
**Answer:** Economies of scale — services like water, power, schools and hospitals reaching many people cheaply.
