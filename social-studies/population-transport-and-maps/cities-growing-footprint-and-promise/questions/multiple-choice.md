# Cities growing — footprint and promise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Urbanisation is —
a) the rural-to-urban population shift and society's adaptation to it  b) the growth of farming  c) a census of cities
**Answer: a) the rural-to-urban population shift and society's adaptation to it**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At the city's edge, growth consumes —
a) only desert  b) nothing — cities grow upward only  c) forests, wetlands and farmland
**Answer: c) forests, wetlands and farmland**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Economies of scale mean —
a) rural life is cheaper  b) services reach many people cheaply because they live close  c) cities should always grow
**Answer: b) services reach many people cheaply because they live close**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Consumptive urban lifestyles raise —
a) air, water and soil pollution  b) rainfall  c) soil fertility
**Answer: a) air, water and soil pollution**
