Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 12 geo-6as-cd): A human-geography year in three units — world population (growth, distribution, migration, structure, with an applications lesson on the age pyramid and demographic projections), urbanisation and its problems, and economic activities from traditional farming to modern industry, transport, trade, craft industry and tourism.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 13 geo-7as-lm): The final-year Letters book is an exam-facing synthesis: Mauritania's natural and human features and its economic activities in full (including the structural problems of the Mauritanian economy and a lesson on how to write a geography essay), then the Arab world and Africa as economic powers (oil and minerals as the lenses), closing with the two great powers — the American and Chinese economies, their foundations, features and problems.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran): the whole of Grade 10 geography is one national book — Iran's own natural and human geography for every branch; nearly every lesson closes with a "know your own living place" activity tied to the separate provincial-studies book, so national content is always re-applied to the learner's own province.

Moldova's own way (Moldova Grade 11 G11 Geography): a human geography of the world year — political map and state typology, natural resources by sphere, population and settlements, then the world economy branch by branch (agriculture, each industry, services, transport, tourism), closing with economic-geographic characterisations of 14 states on every continent (Egypt among them) written to one template, plus case studies including labour migration at local/regional/global scale and using Google Maps/Google Earth as geo-information portals.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 12 np-g12): Grade 12 geography is federal and applied — each of Nepal's provinces studied four ways (administration, population, social-cultural life, economy) plus inter-provincial relations; disasters in Nepal with the national disaster-management system and plan-making; GIS/geo-information in social mapping; and a full urbanisation-and-migration unit (village–city relations, safe and dignified migration, migration effects).

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 3): after world/economic geography in Modules 1–2, Module 3 turns to Mozambique itself as a complete regional study — cities, then the country's location, coast, geology, minerals, soils, relief, climate, plant and animal geography, hydrography — and closes with Southern Africa and Mozambique's SADC integration.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 9): the cycle closes with a short urban module — the definitional problem of "city" (no universal definition; each country's criteria), city evolution from Jericho, city functions, city–countryside interdependence, and the problems of great urban concentrations.

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 11 laos-g11-geography-teacher): a world-population and world-countries year — Part I works through world population growth, changing characteristics, distribution, migration, the developed/developing contrast and urban growth; Part II then studies nine countries one by one (USA, Russia, France, Germany, UK, China, India, Japan, South Africa) plus development in Africa, with map plates (the book ends on a South Africa agriculture map, printed p. 140).

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 11 laos-g11-geography-student): the student twin of Book 5 — same 2 parts / 21 lessons (world population, then nine country studies plus development in Africa) with full illustrated text.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 10 Geography): the literary track opens geography as a discipline in its own right — its thought from the Sumerians to GIS, how a geographer researches, where geographical data come from and how they are drawn, and only then the fields of the science; Iraq supplies the worked examples throughout (Baghdad research problem, Iraqi topographic maps, Iraq's gas time series, the Baghdad–Basra distance exercise).

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 5 Estudios Sociales G5): The scale widens from Nicaragua to the whole Central American isthmus: Units I–IV give the planet and map skills, then Central America's position, relief, climate, hydrography, resources, population and economies, with Nicaragua's place in the isthmus as an explicit topic; the second half is Central American History. Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Unit V first settlers, Maya and Aztec history (printed pp. 70–90, History, except the two topics placed below); Unit VI European presence (printed pp. 92–111, History); Unit VII independence process (printed pp. 114–140, History); Unit VIII Central America in the 20th century (printed pp. 142–154, History).
