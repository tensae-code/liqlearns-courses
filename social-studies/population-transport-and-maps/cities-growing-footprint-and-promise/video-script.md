# Video script — Cities growing — footprint and promise

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Urbanisation is the shift of population from rural to urban life, and society's adaptation to that shift.

Growing cities expand outward into forests, wetlands and farmland.

The same concentration that burdens also serves: well-managed cities bring economies of scale — services, water, power, schools and hospitals reaching many people cheaply because they live close — and development benefits that scattered settlement cannot match.
