# A Day on the Border

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Argentina's teacher notebook teaches the border by living one day on it, through the testimonies of the people who are there. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Un día en la frontera — a day on the border

In Argentina, the teacher notebook Cuadernos para el aula: Ciencias Sociales gives a worked teaching proposal in its third axis (Eje 3): Un día en la frontera — a day on the border. A border here is not only a line on a map: it is a place where a day is lived — people crossing, working, trading and going home again, with another country on the other side.

## Sub lesson 2 — Border testimonies

The proposal works with border testimonies: the accounts of people who live and work at the border themselves. The learner hears the border from the people who are there, in their own words — what crosses, what waits, what a day on the line between two countries actually feels like.
