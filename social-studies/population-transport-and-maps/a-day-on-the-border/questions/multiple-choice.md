# A Day on the Border — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The proposal at printed p. 111 is called —
a) A day on the border  b) A night in the desert  c) A year at sea
**Answer: a) A day on the border**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In this proposal, a border is —
a) only a line on a map  b) a place where a day is lived  c) an empty space
**Answer: b) a place where a day is lived**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The proposal works with border testimonies, which are —
a) accounts of people who live and work at the border  b) weather reports  c) exam questions
**Answer: a) accounts of people who live and work at the border**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** From the testimonies, the learner hears the border —
a) from officials far away  b) from the people who are there, in their own words  c) from nobody
**Answer: b) from the people who are there, in their own words**
