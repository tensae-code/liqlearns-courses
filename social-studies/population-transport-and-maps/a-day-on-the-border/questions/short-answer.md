# A Day on the Border — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a border, in the way this proposal teaches it?
**Answer: Not only a line on a map but a place where a day is lived — people crossing, working, trading and going home, with another country on the other side (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are border testimonies?
**Answer: The accounts of people who live and work at the border themselves, telling in their own words what crosses, what waits and what the day feels like (any close wording).**
