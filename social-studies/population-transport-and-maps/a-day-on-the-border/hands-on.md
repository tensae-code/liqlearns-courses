# Hands-on — A Day on the Border

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Live a boundary day
Think of a boundary in your own life — the edge of your village, the gate of your school, the border of a farm. Write the story of one day on that boundary: who crosses, what they carry, when it is busy and when it is quiet.

## Take a testimony
Ask one person who crosses a boundary often — a trader, a traveller, a herder — to tell you about one crossing. Write their account down in their own words. That is a testimony, the notebook's way.
