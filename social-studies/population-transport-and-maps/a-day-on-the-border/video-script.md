# Video script — A Day on the Border

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A border is a line on a map. But it is also a place where people live a whole day.

Argentina's teacher notebook teaches it that way: Un día en la frontera — a day on the border.

And it works with testimonies — the people who live and work at the border, telling it themselves.

Crossing, working, trading, going home: hear the border from the people who are there.
