# Video script — Village And Town Two Kinds Of Settlement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 2 np-g2* (printed p. 30), teaches: Unit 5 Lesson 2: Our village and town. Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 12 np-g12* (printed p. 274), teaches: Unit 9 Lesson 3: Village–city relationship. Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 8 np-g8* (printed p. 108), teaches: Section Gha (Population): Villages and towns — village settlement patterns (linear, nucleated, ring, dispersed) and how towns arise and what work towns do (religious, industrial, administrative, trade, farming and education towns).

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 2 np-g2): "Our Earth" grows from living place to village/town and the village in the map; economic geography is comparative — things made in the village vs things made in the city.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 12 np-g12): Grade 12 geography is federal and applied — each of Nepal's provinces studied four ways (administration, population, social-cultural life, economy) plus inter-provincial relations; disasters in Nepal with the national disaster-management system and plan-making; GIS/geo-information in social mapping; and a full urbanisation-and-migration unit (village–city relations, safe and dignified migration, migration effects).

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 8 np-g8): this is the old kingdom-era standalone Geography volume — Nepal is taught through its 14 zones (anchal), with full regional accounts of Bagmati, Narayani, Mechi and Mahakali zones (position, environment, resources, development, chief towns, each with maps); Asia is the one continent studied in depth; world economic geography is built on "environment and man" (geographical control). Chapters marked ** in the contents (Earth's surface/interior; the population chapter) were prescribed only for certain school types.
