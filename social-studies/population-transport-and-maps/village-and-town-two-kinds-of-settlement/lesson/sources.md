**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Nepal, South Sudan).**

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 2 np-g2): "Our Earth" grows from living place to village/town and the village in the map; economic geography is comparative — things made in the village vs things made in the city.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 12 np-g12): Grade 12 geography is federal and applied — each of Nepal's provinces studied four ways (administration, population, social-cultural life, economy) plus inter-provincial relations; disasters in Nepal with the national disaster-management system and plan-making; GIS/geo-information in social mapping; and a full urbanisation-and-migration unit (village–city relations, safe and dignified migration, migration effects).

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 8 np-g8): this is the old kingdom-era standalone Geography volume — Nepal is taught through its 14 zones (anchal), with full regional accounts of Bagmati, Narayani, Mechi and Mahakali zones (position, environment, resources, development, chief towns, each with maps); Asia is the one continent studied in depth; world economic geography is built on "environment and man" (geographical control). Chapters marked ** in the contents (Earth's surface/interior; the population chapter) were prescribed only for certain school types.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 2 P2 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Features are located through the child's own payam and boma first, and the guide's answers stress what features give — hills, mountains and valleys make the land beautiful; a river or lake provides water. That evidence is stocked here as the way South Sudan teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — South Sudan is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 3 P3 Social Studies SB): The land unit is anchored on the child's own payam — Mount Kinyeti, the River Nile and the Sudd Swamp are the named examples — and features are taught as double-sided: benefits to us, and problems they can cause, like rivers that fill and flood.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 3 P3 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide adds the settlement half of migration — factors for migration and settlement together — and a practical question the pupil's book only implies: what can be done to minimise migration? That evidence is stocked here as the way South Sudan teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — South Sudan is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).
