## Sub lesson 2 — Rwanda's way — the book's own teaching way

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): from P5 the medium is English and the organising frame is the child's own PROVINCE — its location, services, environment, climate, population and infrastructure — before the frame widens to the whole country at P6. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Rwanda is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies SB): the pupil's book teaches the province through named Rwandan things — the five provinces with Kigali City as the case study, Rwanda's four seasons by their Kinyarwanda names (Umuhindo, Urugaryi, Itumba, Icyi), and the country's two international airports (Kigali and Kamembe) in the transport-places lesson. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
