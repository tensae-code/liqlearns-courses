# Video script — Our Province and Its Location in Rwanda

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rwanda's book, the *Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies TG* (printed p. xiv (content map)), teaches: Describe the location of our province; identify the neighbouring provinces (Unit 1). (teacher guide — evidence) Rwanda's book, the *Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 5), teaches: 1.1 Our provinces and their locations: Rwanda's five provinces on the administrative map (Unit 1, Our Province and its location in Rwanda). Rwanda's book, the *Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 7), teaches: 1.2 A case study of Kigali City Province (Unit 1).

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): from P5 the medium is English and the organising frame is the child's own PROVINCE — its location, services, environment, climate, population and infrastructure — before the frame widens to the whole country at P6. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Rwanda is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies SB): the pupil's book teaches the province through named Rwandan things — the five provinces with Kigali City as the case study, Rwanda's four seasons by their Kinyarwanda names (Umuhindo, Urugaryi, Itumba, Icyi), and the country's two international airports (Kigali and Kamembe) in the transport-places lesson.
