# Population — measuring change: rates, pyramids and structure — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three components of population change are —
a) Births, roads and trade  b) Farming, industry and towns  c) Climate, soil and water  d) Fertility, mortality and migration
**Answer: d) Fertility, mortality and migration**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which component is NON-biological?
a) Fertility  b) Mortality  c) Migration  d) None of them
**Answer: c) Migration**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A developing country's pyramid has —
a) A narrow base and wide apex  b) A wide base and a narrow apex  c) No base  d) Equal width with no bands
**Answer: b) A wide base and a narrow apex**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The working age group is —
a) 15–64  b) 0–14  c) 65+  d) 0–64
**Answer: a) 15–64**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The sex ratio counts —
a) Births per deaths  b) Children per school  c) Workers per town  d) Males per females (or the reverse)
**Answer: d) Males per females (or the reverse)**
