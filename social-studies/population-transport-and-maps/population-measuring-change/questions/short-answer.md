# Population — measuring change: rates, pyramids and structure — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you read a population pyramid?
**Answer:** Age bands stacked; males one side, females the other; shape shows past births and future needs.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the guide's three keyed fertility factors for developing countries.
**Answer:** Early marriage, low status of women, weak financial capacity.
