# Population — measuring change: rates, pyramids and structure — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fertility and mortality are the biological components.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Population structure is the distribution of males and females across age groups.
**Answer: True**

**Answer: True**
