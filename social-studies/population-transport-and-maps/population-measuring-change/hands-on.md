# Hands-on — Population — measuring change: rates, pyramids and structure

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Invent a small town's year: births, deaths, arrivals, departures — and work out its growth.

## Activity 2
Draw a wide-based pyramid and a column pyramid and label the three age groups on one.

## Activity 3
Ask what your own household's age groups are: how many young, working, old? Compute the dependent share.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the three components, which two are biological, the developing-country pyramid shape, the three age groups, and what the sex ratio counts.
