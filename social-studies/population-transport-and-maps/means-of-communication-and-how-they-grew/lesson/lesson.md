## Sub lesson 2 — Mozambique's way — the book's own teaching way

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 2): human/economic geography organised sector by sector — population first, then each economic activity taught world-then-Mozambique (agriculture, livestock, fishing, forestry, industry, commerce, tourism, transport), with Mozambique's own distribution tables, ports and exports as the local evidence.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 8): transport and communications taught as twin evolutions — transport from foot/animal traction to the Industrial Revolution's bulk cargo, communications from primitive signals to telematics (1970) and videoconference (1984) — with transport's environmental impact given its own lesson before tourism closes the module. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
