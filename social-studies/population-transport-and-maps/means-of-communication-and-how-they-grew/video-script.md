# Video script — Means Of Communication And How They Grew

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Mozambique's book, the *Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 2* (printed p. 145), teaches: Lição 31: Vias e meios de comunicação (evolution of means of communication from the sledge onward). Mozambique's book, the *Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 8* (printed p. 19), teaches: Lição 3: Evolução dos Meios de Comunicação (telecommunications stages; world diffusion; telematics 1970, videoconference 1984). Mozambique's book, the *Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 8* (printed p. 25), teaches: Lição 4: Sistemas de Comunicação (radio, television, telephone systems; the telegraph's decline; telecoms' decisive cultural/economic role).

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 2): human/economic geography organised sector by sector — population first, then each economic activity taught world-then-Mozambique (agriculture, livestock, fishing, forestry, industry, commerce, tourism, transport), with Mozambique's own distribution tables, ports and exports as the local evidence.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 8): transport and communications taught as twin evolutions — transport from foot/animal traction to the Industrial Revolution's bulk cargo, communications from primitive signals to telematics (1970) and videoconference (1984) — with transport's environmental impact given its own lesson before tourism closes the module.
