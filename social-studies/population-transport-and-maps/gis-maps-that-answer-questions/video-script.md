# Video script — GIS — maps that answer questions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A Geographic Information System is the computer-based system for capturing, storing, checking, retrieving, manipulating, analysing and displaying geographically referenced data — data tied to places on Earth..

Its power is the merge: census, infrastructure and base data stacked into one map, so decisions on land use, resources, transport and facilities rest on everything known, not on one survey..

The uses are argued plainly: tackling climate change, food production and disasters, and siting schools and hospitals where the merged map says they are needed.
