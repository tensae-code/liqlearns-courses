# GIS — maps that answer questions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define GIS.
**Answer:** A computer system for capturing, storing, checking, retrieving, manipulating, analysing and displaying geographically referenced data.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What three data kinds merge in the example?
**Answer:** Census, infrastructure and base data.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two decisions GIS supports.
**Answer:** Land use, resources, transport, facilities, siting schools/hospitals (any two).
