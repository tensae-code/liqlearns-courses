# GIS — maps that answer questions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GIS data is —
a) geographically referenced  b) unreferenced  c) imaginary
**Answer: a) geographically referenced**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** GIS handles data through capturing, storing, checking, retrieving, manipulating, analysing and —
a) displaying  b) burning  c) guessing
**Answer: a) displaying**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The merge combines census, infrastructure and —
a) base data  b) weather only  c) prices
**Answer: a) base data**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** GIS helps site —
a) schools and hospitals  b) only farms  c) only roads
**Answer: a) schools and hospitals**
