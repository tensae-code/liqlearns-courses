# Video script — Costa Rica's Geo-strategic Position and Internet Coverage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Costa Rica's Grade 11 Porras y Gamboa workbook turns the map on Costa Rica itself. In Unit 1 C (cont.) it teaches Costa Rica's geo-strategic position — a bridge between North and South America and the Caribbean — and its services economy, then compares internet and telecom access between Costa Rica and the world using the ICE internet-coverage map, closing with a map exercise on the Costa Rican areas with little or no connectivity.

Costa Rica learns this inside its own book's way: a commercial workbook twin of the CONED 11 anthology — same ministry programme, but every topic is a fixed cycle (problem question, basic concepts, my previous ideas, widening my knowledge, I apply my knowledge, problem-based learning, exam preparation) with heavy map-work and Costa Rica-specific data: ICE internet-coverage map, services-export league table, Costa Rica's geo-strategic bridge position between North/South America and the Caribbean. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
