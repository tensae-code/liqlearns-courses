Iceland's own way: Geography work cards (vinnukort) are single-topic map sheets: this one teaches Iceland's own administrative geography — the text explains the sheriff (sýslumaður) districts to 1992 and the 1989 separation-of-powers law, the second page lists all 27 administrative districts with every municipality in each, and the third page is the district-boundary map (taught in the companion lesson, Reading a District Boundary Map of Iceland). This lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
