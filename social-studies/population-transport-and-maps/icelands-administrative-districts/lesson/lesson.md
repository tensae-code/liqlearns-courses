# Iceland's Administrative Districts

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Iceland is one country, but for running its affairs it is divided into districts — and this lesson is Iceland's own map of those districts. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The sheriff districts

Iceland's geography work cards (vinnukort) are single-topic map sheets. This one, ÍSLAND — Umdæmisskipting, explains the country's administrative districts: the districts of the sýslumaður — the sheriff. Its text explains the sheriff districts as they stood to 1992, and the 1989 separation-of-powers law that changed them.

## Sub lesson 2 — 27 districts and their municipalities

The work card lists all 27 administrative districts, with every municipality in each one. Twenty-seven districts, each made of its own municipalities — that is how Iceland divides itself for administration, and the card gives the whole list for pupils to work with.
