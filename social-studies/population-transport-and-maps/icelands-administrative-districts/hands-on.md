# Hands-on — Iceland's Administrative Districts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## List a district's municipalities
Take the work card's list of the 27 districts. Choose one district and write out every municipality the card lists in it. You are reading Iceland's administrative division the way the card teaches it.

## Count the division
Write two lines: how many administrative districts the card lists for Iceland, and what each district is made of. Then say what the sýslumaður is.
