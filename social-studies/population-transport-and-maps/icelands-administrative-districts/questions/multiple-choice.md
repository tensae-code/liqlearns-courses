# Iceland's Administrative Districts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sýslumaður means —
a) the sheriff  b) the mayor of Reykjavík  c) a river
**Answer: a) the sheriff**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The work card's text explains the sheriff districts as they stood —
a) to 1992, and the 1989 separation-of-powers law  b) to the year 2000 only  c) in Viking times only
**Answer: a) to 1992, and the 1989 separation-of-powers law**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The card lists how many administrative districts? —
a) 27  b) 7  c) 100
**Answer: a) 27**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** For each district, the list gives —
a) every municipality in it  b) only its weather  c) only its rivers
**Answer: a) every municipality in it**
