# Iceland's Administrative Districts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a vinnukort, in Iceland's geography teaching?
**Answer: A single-topic map work card / sheet — one geography topic worked on one sheet (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is Iceland divided for administration, as the card teaches it?
**Answer: Into 27 administrative (sýslumaður / sheriff) districts, each made of its municipalities (any close wording).**
