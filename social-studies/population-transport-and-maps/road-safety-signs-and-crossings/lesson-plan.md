# Lesson plan — Road safety — signs and crossings

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Road safety — signs and crossings
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** reading the crossing — the traffic light's messages, the white zebra stripes, and the pedestrian lights: red standing figure, do not cross; green walking figure, cross quickly
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Road safety — staying alive on the road, next door in this title.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Road safety — staying alive on the road. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the traffic light's colours command.
2. Say what the zebra stripes mark.
3. Read the pedestrian lights: red standing figure and green walking figure.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A globe or ball helps where the lesson names one. Nothing else.

## The run of the lesson (in order)
1. **Do it first** — You are standing at a crossing. The light facing the cars is one colour; the little figure facing you is another. Which one is talking to YOU? Sort that out in your head before you read on — crossing on the wrong signal is the mistake this lesson exists to prevent. (lesson/lesson.md)
