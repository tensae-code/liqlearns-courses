# Road safety — signs and crossings — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The pedestrian figure talks to the walker; the traffic light commands the vehicles.
**Answer: True**

**2.** On the green walking figure you should linger in the middle of the road.
**Answer: False**

**3.** You cross on the zebra stripes, where drivers expect you.
**Answer: True**
