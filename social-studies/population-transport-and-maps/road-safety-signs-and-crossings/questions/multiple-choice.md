# Road safety — signs and crossings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The red standing figure means —
a) Cross quickly  b) Run across  c) The light is broken  d) Do not cross
**Answer: d) Do not cross**

**2.** The green walking figure means —
a) Stand still  b) Do not cross  c) Cross, quickly  d) Wait for a car
**Answer: c) Cross, quickly**

**3.** The white zebra stripes mark —
a) A parking place  b) The walker's crossing path  c) The bus stop  d) Where cars turn
**Answer: b) The walker's crossing path**
