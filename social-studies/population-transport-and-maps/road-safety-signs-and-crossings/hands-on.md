# Hands-on — Road safety — signs and crossings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
At a real crossing (with an adult if you are young), wait for the green walking figure before you cross — once, deliberately.

## Activity 2
Draw the two pedestrian figures from memory and write each one's command under it.

## Activity 3
Count the zebra crossings on your school route and note which have pedestrian lights.

## With a partner
Tell your companion what the red standing figure and the green walking figure each command, and what the zebra stripes are for.
