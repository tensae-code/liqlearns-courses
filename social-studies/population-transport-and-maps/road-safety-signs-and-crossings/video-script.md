# Video script — Road safety — signs and crossings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

You are standing at a crossing. The light facing the cars is one colour; the little figure facing you is another. Which one is talking to YOU? Sort that out in your head before you read on — crossing on the wrong signal is the mistake this lesson exists to prevent.

This lesson teaches: reading the crossing — the traffic light's messages, the white zebra stripes, and the pedestrian lights: red standing figure, do not cross; green walking figure, cross quickly. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
