## Sub lesson 2 — Rwanda's way — the book's own teaching way

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 6 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): P6 widens the frame from the province to the country, then to East Africa and Africa — Rwanda is located by latitude and longitude (1°S–3°S, 28°E–31°E), its landlocked position is taught as the reason for regional co-operation, and every unit is built around a problem statement with the pupil's book activities answered in the guide. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Rwanda is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 6 Social Studies SB): the P6 pupil's book is the fullest Geography book of the set — a whole unit on direction and location works Rwanda onto the East African and African maps by latitude and longitude and time zones, then physical features and climate, and a climate-change sequence (types, change, effects, overcoming) sits inside it; regional co-operation is taught bloc by bloc (COMESA, CEPGL, ECCAS, AU, NEPAD). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
