# Rural settlement patterns in Africa — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Settlement patterns are decoration with no link to livelihood.
**Answer: False** — homes gather or scatter as the livelihood rewards.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Scattered homesteads each stand on their own ground.
**Answer: True** — spread across the land, each beside its fields.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rural settlement is the starting point of rural–urban migration.
**Answer: True** — every urban move begins inside a rural pattern.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A 26% urban share means most people live in towns.
**Answer: False** — it means about 74% live rurally.
