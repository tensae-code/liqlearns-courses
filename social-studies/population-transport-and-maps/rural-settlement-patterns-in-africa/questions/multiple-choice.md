# Rural settlement patterns in Africa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A rural settlement pattern is —
a) a city's street plan  b) the way homes and villages are arranged across the countryside  c) a migration route  d) a farm crop rotation
**Answer: b) the way homes and villages are arranged across the countryside** — the pattern of homes on the land.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Homes gathered together into villages form a —
a) scattered pattern  b) clustered pattern  c) urban pattern  d) no pattern
**Answer: b) clustered pattern** — gathered, not spread.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A settlement's livelihood base is —
a) its town hall  b) the fields, grazing and water that feed it  c) its market day  d) its oldest house
**Answer: b) the fields, grazing and water that feed it** — above all farming.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In a region that is 26% urban, the share living in rural patterns is about —
a) 26%  b) 50%  c) 74%  d) 100%
**Answer: c) 74%** — 100 − 26: the rural remainder.
