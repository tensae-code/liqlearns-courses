# Rural settlement patterns in Africa — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do homes cluster in one place and scatter in another?
**Answer:** Homes gather where gathering helps — shared water, defence and work — and scatter where the livelihood rewards spreading out, each family beside its own fields.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two questions read any settlement pattern?
**Answer:** What is the pattern — clustered or scattered? And what is the livelihood base beneath it?
