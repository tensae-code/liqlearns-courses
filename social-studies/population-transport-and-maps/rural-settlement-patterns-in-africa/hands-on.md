# Hands-on — Rural settlement patterns in Africa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-pattern sketch
On one page draw the same farmland twice: once with homes clustered in a village at the water point, once with homesteads scattered by their fields. Under each, write one advantage the pattern gives its people.

## Settlement walk
Walk a rural area (or recall one well). Note: are homes clustered or scattered? Where is the water? Where are the fields? Write the pattern and its livelihood base in five lines.
