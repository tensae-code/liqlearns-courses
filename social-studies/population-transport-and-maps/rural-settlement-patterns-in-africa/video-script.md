# Video script — Rural settlement patterns in Africa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Before the towns, the countryside.
Homes gather into villages — or scatter over the fields.
The pattern follows the living: water, fields, work.
Read the homes, and you read the livelihood.
