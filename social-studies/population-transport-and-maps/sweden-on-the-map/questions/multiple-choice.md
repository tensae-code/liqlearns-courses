# Sweden on the Map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Sweden on the map — Stockholm, coast and lakes  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Sweden on the map — Stockholm, coast and lakes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Sweden on the map: the capital Stockholm, coast and lakes, and the islands Gotland and Öland, as Sweden's Grade 1 teacher guide notes the textbook's opening map pages  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Sweden on the map: the capital Stockholm, coast and lakes, and the islands Gotland and Öland, as Sweden's Grade 1 teacher guide notes the textbook's opening map pages**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) The islands Gotland and Öland  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) The islands Gotland and Öland**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Stockholm is the capital of Sweden, and Gotland and Öland are Swedish islands.  b) Gotland and Öland are the names of Sweden's two biggest lakes.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about Sweden
**Answer: a) Stockholm is the capital of Sweden, and Gotland and Öland are Swedish islands.**
