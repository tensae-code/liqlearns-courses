# Video script — Sweden on the Map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sweden's Grade 1 book puts Sweden itself on the map first: the capital Stockholm, the coast and the lakes. The teacher guide's notes for the textbook's pages 6–11 teach the child to find their own country and read what the map shows in it.

Off Sweden's coast lie its big islands, Gotland and Öland. The lesson names them as part of Sweden on the map, so the country's shape includes the islands as well as the mainland.
