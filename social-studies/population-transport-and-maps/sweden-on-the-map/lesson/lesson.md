# Sweden on the Map

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Sweden on the map: the capital Stockholm, coast and lakes, and the islands Gotland and Öland, as Sweden's Grade 1 teacher guide notes the textbook's opening map pages. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sweden on the map — Stockholm, coast and lakes

Sweden's Grade 1 book puts Sweden itself on the map first: the capital Stockholm, the coast and the lakes. The teacher guide's notes for the textbook's pages 6–11 teach the child to find their own country and read what the map shows in it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The islands Gotland and Öland

Off Sweden's coast lie its big islands, Gotland and Öland. The lesson names them as part of Sweden on the map, so the country's shape includes the islands as well as the mainland.

Sweden's own way: Geography starts from the child's own surroundings — Sweden's place on the globe, the home town, Swedish nature and its animals, and sorting the household's own waste — taught with big pictures and sorting games for pupils who read and write only a little.
