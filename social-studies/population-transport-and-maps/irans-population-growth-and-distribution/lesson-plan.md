# Lesson plan — Irans Population Growth And Distribution

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Irans Population Growth And Distribution
**Place in the curriculum:** Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Iran, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Lesson 5 Iran's population (جمعیت ایران): if those born outnumber those who die, population increases; our country's population is now over 80 million — compare the chart of Iran's population with the years past; the region of Tehran and around it is the most populous place in the country, Tehran being the capital with its opportunities; one cause of a region's population growing is migration (مهاجرت) — people going from one place to another to live or work — and some regions draw people by their good climate; Lesson 13 Iran's population (جمعیت ایران): population grows when those born in a time and place outnumber those who die; the Statistical Center of Iran (مرکز آمار ایران) holds the census (سرشماری); Iran's population density in 1400 (2021) was about 52 people per square kilometre — and how that density is worked out; the map of population distribution in Iran, and Tehran — the capital — with the towns around it the country's most populous and densest urban region — as Iran's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
