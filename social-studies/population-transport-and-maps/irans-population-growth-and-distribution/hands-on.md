# Hands-on — Irans Population Growth And Distribution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 5 Iran's population (جمعیت ایران): if those born outnumber those who die, population increases; our country's population is now over 80 million — compare the chart of Iran's population with the years past; the region of Tehran and around it is the most populous place in the country, Tehran being the capital with its opportunities; one cause of a region's population growing is migration (مهاجرت) — people going from one place to another to live or work — and some regions draw people by their good climate; Lesson 13 Iran's population (جمعیت ایران): population grows when those born in a time and place outnumber those who die; the Statistical Center of Iran (مرکز آمار ایران) holds the census (سرشماری); Iran's population density in 1400 (2021) was about 52 people per square kilometre — and how that density is worked out; the map of population distribution in Iran, and Tehran — the capital — with the towns around it the country's most populous and densest urban region. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
