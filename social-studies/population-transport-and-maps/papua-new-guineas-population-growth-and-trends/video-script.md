Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG* (printed p. 37), teaches: Strand 1 Unit 2: Population in Papua New Guinea and neighbouring countries; migration in Papua New Guinea, Lessons 14–16 (teacher guide — evidence). Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG* (printed p. 60), teaches: Strand 1 Unit 2, Topic 5: PNG's demographic features and other countries (teacher guide — evidence). Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 11 Geography TG OBE* (printed p. 35), teaches: Unit 11.4: census-based graphs of PNG population growth, birth and mortality rates, predicting PNG's future trends (teacher guide — evidence).

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Environment and Resources now turns national — formation of PNG's mountains, islands and rivers; PNG's population and migration; its mineral, marine and agricultural resources and how extraction impacts are managed; conservation areas located on the map of PNG; hazard safety and risk management. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 10 Geography is a population course — population pyramids in depth, world physical geography's effects on settlement, density and economic activity, then PNG-only migration (types, internal migration, routes and trends) and PNG's land-mobilisation questions (undeveloped land, Special Agricultural Business Leases, land grabbing). That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grades 11–12 Geography TG OBE) (teacher guide — evidence): an outcomes-based guide — it prescribes no lesson scripts; for each syllabus unit it lists suggested learning activities and assessment tasks built around the local environment (soil profiles, local hazard sites, a PNG mine and logging case study, a transect along latitude 6°S), with fieldwork and geographical inquiry as the core skills. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.
