# Hands-on — Inventing the Grid — History of the Prime Meridian

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the origin
Draw a circle for the Earth, draw the grid of parallels and meridians across it, and mark the origin meridian in a different colour. Label it from the lesson.

## Words for the grid
Say where the words latitude and longitude come from, in your own words, and tell your companion why the grid needed an agreed starting line.
