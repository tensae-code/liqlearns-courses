# Why population exploded — deaths fell first — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Growth accelerated above all because —
a) death rates fell  b) birth rates doubled  c) oceans shrank
**Answer: a) death rates fell**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Agriculture's surpluses allowed —
a) settled communities and specialisation  b) nomadism only  c) fewer people
**Answer: a) settled communities and specialisation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Medical knowledge and public health cut —
a) death rates  b) food output  c) marriage age
**Answer: a) death rates**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Modern contraception is called the —
a) reproductive revolution  b) industrial revolution  c) green revolution
**Answer: a) reproductive revolution**
