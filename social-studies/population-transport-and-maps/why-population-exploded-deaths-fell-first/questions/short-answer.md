# Why population exploded — deaths fell first — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the deep causes in order.
**Answer:** Agriculture's surpluses; the industrial and agricultural revolutions; falling death rates from medicine and public health.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What actually fell to cause the explosion?
**Answer:** Death rates.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three things fertility answers to.
**Answer:** Marriage age, contraception, breastfeeding's spacing.
