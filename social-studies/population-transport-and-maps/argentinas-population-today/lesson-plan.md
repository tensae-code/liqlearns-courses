# Lesson plan — Argentina's Population Today

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Argentina's Population Today
**Place in the curriculum:** Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Argentina, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Argentina's Grade 4 (Segundo Ciclo) teacher notebook makes the study of Argentina's own population today its first Geography proposal — who the country's people are now, studied as the knowledge at stake.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the proposal itself — Argentina's population today, as its own teacher guide frames the study.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
