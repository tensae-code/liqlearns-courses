## Sub lesson 1 — Studying Argentina's population today

In Argentina, the teacher notebook Cuadernos para el aula: Ciencias Sociales opens its Geography axis (Eje 1) with a worked teaching proposal: El estudio de la población argentina hoy — the study of Argentina's population today. The subject is the country's own people, as they are now: the population of Argentina today is the knowledge at stake, the thing the teaching is built to deliver.

## Sub lesson 2 — A teacher's notebook, not a pupil text

This book is written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. Each axis gives the knowledge at stake first, then worked teaching proposals built on statistics, graphs, thematic maps and newspaper articles. So Argentina's population is not met as a finished chapter to copy: the teacher is given a proposal for studying it, with real data and real sources as the working material.
