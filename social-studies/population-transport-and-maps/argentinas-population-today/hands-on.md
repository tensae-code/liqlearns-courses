# Hands-on — Argentina's Population Today

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the people around you
Take one place you know well — your street, your school, your village. Write down who lives or works there, group by group, the way a population study begins: with the real people of a real place, counted carefully.

## Make a population graph
From your count, draw one simple graph — bars for each group you counted. Argentina's notebook builds its population teaching on statistics and graphs; yours is a first one, from your own place.
