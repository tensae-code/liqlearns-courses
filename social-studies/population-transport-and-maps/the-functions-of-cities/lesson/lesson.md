## Sub lesson 2 — Mozambique's way — the book's own teaching way

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 3): after world/economic geography in Modules 1–2, Module 3 turns to Mozambique itself as a complete regional study — cities, then the country's location, coast, geology, minerals, soils, relief, climate, plant and animal geography, hydrography — and closes with Southern Africa and Mozambique's SADC integration.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 9): the cycle closes with a short urban module — the definitional problem of "city" (no universal definition; each country's criteria), city evolution from Jericho, city functions, city–countryside interdependence, and the problems of great urban concentrations. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
