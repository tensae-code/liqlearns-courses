# Push and pull — why Africans move — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a PUSH factor?
a) Better income abroad  b) Poverty at home  c) Good schools elsewhere  d) Health care in the city
**Answer: b) Poverty at home** — push factors are conditions at home that drive people out.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Unemployment as a push factor is linked in the book to —
a) population outgrowing resources and technology  b) too many schools  c) cold weather  d) river floods only
**Answer: a) population outgrowing resources and technology** — the book's own framing.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set is ALL pull factors?
a) Poverty, corruption, conflict  b) Income, security, education, health care  c) Low wages, unemployment, instability  d) Drought, famine, war
**Answer: b) Income, security, education, health care** — the destination's attractions.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A 'country of origin' is —
a) the destination country  b) the country a migrant comes from  c) a border town  d) a refugee camp
**Answer: b) the country a migrant comes from** — from the guide's migration glossary (teacher guide — evidence).

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The forced slave migrations of the 16th–19th centuries —
a) grew Africa's population  b) cut Africa's population  c) did not move people  d) were voluntary
**Answer: b) cut Africa's population** — the largest forced movement in the continent's history.
