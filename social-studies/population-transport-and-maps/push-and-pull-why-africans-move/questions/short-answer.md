# Push and pull — why Africans move — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sort these into push and pull: poverty, security, unemployment, health care.
**Answer:** Push: poverty, unemployment. Pull: security, health care.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is some of today's 'international' movement in Africa older than the border it crosses?
**Answer:** Because communities were settled across lands before the modern borders were drawn; the border later cut through the community's own land.
