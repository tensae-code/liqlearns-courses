# Hands-on — Push and pull — why Africans move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Push–pull wall chart
Draw a figure in the middle of a large sheet. On the left, arrows pushing it: label them with the seven push factors. On the right, arrows pulling: the four pull factors. Present the chart and read both lists aloud.

## Case sorting
Write six short move stories on cards (your own invention, one per card). With a partner, sort each story's reasons into push and pull columns and name each factor from the lesson's lists.
