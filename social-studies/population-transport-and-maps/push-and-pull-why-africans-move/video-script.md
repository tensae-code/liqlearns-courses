# Video script — Push and pull — why Africans move

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why do people move? Two lists answer.
Push: poverty. Low wages. No jobs. Conflict.
Pull: income. Security. School. Health care.
Pushed from behind. Pulled from ahead. That is migration.
