# Video script — Kinds of Houses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sweden's training material fixes the textbook's Geography by doing: in Exercise B the pupil sorts and names different houses. The sorting comes first — the names are learned by putting each house in its right group.

The Swedish words are the lesson's own content: flerfamiljshus (a many-family house) and enfamiljshus (a one-family house); höghus, a high-rise; lågt flerfamiljshus, a low many-family house; and villa, radhus (terraced house) and parhus (semi-detached house).
