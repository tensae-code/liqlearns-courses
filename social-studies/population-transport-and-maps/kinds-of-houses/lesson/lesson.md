# Kinds of Houses

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: different houses, sorted and named the Swedish way: flerfamiljshus and enfamiljshus, höghus (high-rise), lågt flerfamiljshus, villa, radhus and parhus, from Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise B. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Different houses — sorting and naming them

Sweden's training material fixes the textbook's Geography by doing: in Exercise B the pupil sorts and names different houses. The sorting comes first — the names are learned by putting each house in its right group.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Swedish house words — villa, radhus, parhus, höghus

The Swedish words are the lesson's own content: flerfamiljshus (a many-family house) and enfamiljshus (a one-family house); höghus, a high-rise; lågt flerfamiljshus, a low many-family house; and villa, radhus (terraced house) and parhus (semi-detached house).

Sweden's own way: the textbook's Geography is fixed by doing — colouring Sweden on the globe, sorting picture cards of Swedish houses, nature types, plants and animals under the right heading, and sorting photographs under warm climate / cold climate.
