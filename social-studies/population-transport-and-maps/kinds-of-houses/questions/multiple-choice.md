# Kinds of Houses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Different houses — sorting and naming them  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Different houses — sorting and naming them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) different houses, sorted and named the Swedish way: flerfamiljshus and enfamiljshus, höghus (high-rise), lågt flerfamiljshus, villa, radhus and parhus, from Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise B  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) different houses, sorted and named the Swedish way: flerfamiljshus and enfamiljshus, höghus (high-rise), lågt flerfamiljshus, villa, radhus and parhus, from Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise B**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) The Swedish house words — villa, radhus, parhus, höghus  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) The Swedish house words — villa, radhus, parhus, höghus**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) In Sweden's training material, höghus means a high-rise and a villa is a one-family house.  b) In the Swedish words, radhus means a high-rise tower.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about Sweden
**Answer: a) In Sweden's training material, höghus means a high-rise and a villa is a one-family house.**
