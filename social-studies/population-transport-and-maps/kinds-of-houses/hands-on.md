# Hands-on — Kinds of Houses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort house pictures
Draw or find six houses and sort them the Swedish way: one-family or many-family, high or low — then name each with the lesson's words.

## Name the houses on your street
Walk your street in your mind and name each house with the lesson's Swedish words where they fit: villa, radhus, parhus, höghus.
