# Video script — Making maps with GIS

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

GIS analysis works the layers — querying, combining and analysing spatial data to answer location questions. Which villages lie inside the flood zone and within reach of the clinic? No paper map of rivers, roads or clinics alone can say; the layers, questioned together, can. Analysis is where stored geography becomes decisions. The finished products: statistical diagrams built from generated data, and maps made and interpreted of the three working kinds — administrative maps (boundaries and seats of government), topographic maps (the land's shape and features) and watershed maps (where the water gathers and drains). A map made with GIS is not the end of the work; it is the argument the work makes, drawn.
