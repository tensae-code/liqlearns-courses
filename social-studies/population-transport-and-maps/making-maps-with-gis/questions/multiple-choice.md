# Making maps with GIS — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GIS analysis works by…
a) querying, combining and analysing layers  b) printing one layer  c) deleting data
**Answer: a) querying, combining and analysing layers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A location question GIS can answer combines…
a) one layer  b) several layers together  c) no data
**Answer: b) several layers together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Statistical diagrams are built from…
a) generated data  b) guesses  c) old maps only
**Answer: a) generated data**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The three map kinds are administrative, topographic and…
a) watershed  b) weather  c) road only
**Answer: a) watershed**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A topographic map shows…
a) boundaries only  b) the land's shape and features  c) trade
**Answer: b) the land's shape and features**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A watershed map shows…
a) where water gathers and drains  b) voting areas  c) soil only
**Answer: a) where water gathers and drains**
