# Making maps with GIS — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give a location question needing several layers.
**Answer:** E.g. which villages lie in the flood zone and within reach of a clinic (any multi-layer question).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three working map kinds.
**Answer:** Administrative, topographic and watershed maps.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What else besides maps does the unit produce?
**Answer:** Statistical diagrams built from generated data.
