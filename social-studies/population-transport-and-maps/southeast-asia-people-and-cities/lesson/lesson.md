## Sub lesson 1 — Southeast Asia People And Cities

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11* (printed pp. 49–52), teaches: Bài 11 (part): Southeast Asia's population (668.4 million in 2020; density 149/km², from Singapore's 8,019 down to Laos) and society — fast urbanisation (49.9%) and megacities (Manila, Jakarta, Bangkok), many peoples and the world's great religions side by side. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11): after Grade 10's world systems, Grade 11 walks the world region by region — Latin America, the EU, Southeast Asia and ASEAN, Southwest Asia, the USA, Russia, Japan, China, Australia and South Africa — always with the same lens (position, nature, people, economy) and always measuring Vietnam's own place among them (its HDI row in the opening table, EU–Vietnam trade, Vietnam inside ASEAN, Japan's FDI into Vietnam). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
