# Video script — Contours — lines of equal height

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Contours are imaginary lines joining points of equal elevation above mean sea level.

Every fifth contour is an index contour, drawn heavier and carrying the height value.

Contours never merge or cross, except at cliffs and waterfalls; they never branch; they are numbered uphill; and their spacing reads the slope — even spacing is a uniform slope, wide spacing gentle, close spacing steep.
