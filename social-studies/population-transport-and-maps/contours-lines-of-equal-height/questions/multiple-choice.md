# Contours — lines of equal height — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A contour joins points of equal —
a) elevation above mean sea level  b) temperature  c) rainfall
**Answer: a) elevation above mean sea level**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Contours are drawn in —
a) brown  b) blue  c) green
**Answer: a) brown**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Index contours occur every —
a) fifth line  b) tenth line  c) second line
**Answer: a) fifth line**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Close spacing means the slope is —
a) steep  b) gentle  c) uniform
**Answer: a) steep**
