# Contours — lines of equal height — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define a contour.
**Answer:** An imaginary line joining points of equal elevation above mean sea level.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the two sheet intervals.
**Answer:** 20 m on 1:50,000 sheets; 100 m on 1:250,000 sheets.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** State the four properties.
**Answer:** Never merge/cross (except cliffs, waterfalls); never branch; numbered uphill; spacing reads slope.
