# Contours — lines of equal height — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Contours can cross at cliffs and waterfalls.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Contours branch like rivers.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Contours are numbered uphill.
**Answer: True**
