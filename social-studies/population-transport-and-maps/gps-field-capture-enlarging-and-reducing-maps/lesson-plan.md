# Lesson plan — GPS field capture; enlarging and reducing maps

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** GPS field capture; enlarging and reducing maps
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 8 — Geospatial Information and Data Processing (syllabus), Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Two field skills the syllabus requires beyond the student book's sections: capturing spatial data in the field with GPS technology, and enlarging and reducing maps — including with GIS — so the same ground can be drawn at a new scale with its scale bar corrected.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the geospatial lessons of Unit 8 — data, sources and tools. This lesson rests on teacher-guide evidence only (the official syllabus), and its sources say so in full.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Representing spatial data using GPS technology (teacher guide — evidence)** — Field capture with GPS.
2. **Enlarging and reducing maps — including with GIS (teacher guide — evidence)** — The same ground at a new scale.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the Unit 8 geospatial lessons — what geospatial data is, where it comes from, and the geographer's toolkit. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe GPS field capture step by step.
2. Explain enlargement and reduction of maps, including with GIS.
3. State the scale-bar rule: rescale the bar with the map, or the map lies.
4. Name the evidence status of this lesson honestly.

## If the learner is stuck
Two skills, two one-line rules. GPS: walk, settle, record, note. Rescaling: the ground never changes — only the scale, and the bar must change with it. If you hold both rules, you hold the lesson.
