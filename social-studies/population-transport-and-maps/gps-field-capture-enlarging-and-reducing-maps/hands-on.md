# Hands-on — GPS field capture; enlarging and reducing maps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Boundary capture plan
Without any device, plan a GPS capture of your school or compound: list the corners and features in walking order, and design the notebook table you would fill — point number, what it is, coordinates space.

## Double-the-map
Draw a simple map of a room or compound with a 0–50 m scale bar. Redraw the whole sheet at twice the size on a larger page, with the scale bar correctly redrawn, and check three distances against the original.
