# GPS field capture; enlarging and reducing maps — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the GPS field-capture steps in order.
**Answer:** Walk to the feature, let the receiver settle, record the coordinates, and note what the point is.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the scale-bar rule for resized maps.
**Answer:** When a map is enlarged or reduced, its scale bar and scale statement must be redrawn to match the new size — otherwise the map lies about distances.
