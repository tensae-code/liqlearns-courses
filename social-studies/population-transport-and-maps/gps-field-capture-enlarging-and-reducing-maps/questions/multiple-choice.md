# GPS field capture; enlarging and reducing maps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In GPS field capture, after walking to a feature you —
a) guess its position  b) let the receiver settle and record its coordinates with a note  c) draw it from memory  d) ask a satellite
**Answer: b) let the receiver settle and record its coordinates with a note** — feature, settle, record, note.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide's Unit 8 framing names the two most important collection tools as —
a) compass and ruler  b) GPS and digitisers  c) camera and notebook  d) drone and printer
**Answer: b) GPS and digitisers** — (teacher guide — evidence).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** When a map is enlarged, what does NOT change?
a) The paper size  b) The ground the map shows  c) The scale  d) The scale bar
**Answer: b) The ground the map shows** — only the drawing and its scale change.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A map doubled in size without touching its scale bar —
a) stays true  b) now lies about distances  c) becomes a raster  d) cannot be read
**Answer: b) now lies about distances** — the bar must be recomputed with the map.
