# Counting deaths — death rate and infant mortality rate — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the CDR's definition exactly.
**Answer:** Deaths per 1,000 mid-year population.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the IMR's definition exactly.
**Answer:** Deaths under one year per 1,000 live births.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the determinants of mortality.
**Answer:** Living standards, nutrition, medical services, hygiene and sanitation.
