# Counting deaths — death rate and infant mortality rate — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nutrition is a determinant of mortality.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** IMR counts deaths per 1,000 mid-year population.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pupils calculate both measures for a fresh data set.
**Answer: True**
