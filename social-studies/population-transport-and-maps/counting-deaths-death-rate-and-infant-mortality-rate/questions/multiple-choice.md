# Counting deaths — death rate and infant mortality rate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The CDR counts deaths per —
a) 1,000 mid-year population  b) 1,000 live births  c) 100 people
**Answer: a) 1,000 mid-year population**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The IMR counts deaths —
a) under one year, per 1,000 live births  b) of elders only  c) in hospitals only
**Answer: a) under one year, per 1,000 live births**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A determinant of mortality is —
a) hygiene and sanitation  b) latitude  c) soil type
**Answer: a) hygiene and sanitation**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The most sensitive measure, per the lesson, is —
a) the infant mortality rate  b) the crude birth rate  c) the migration rate
**Answer: a) the infant mortality rate**
