The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Palestine's Grade 5 student book, the *Social Studies, Grade 5, Part Two* (printed p. 27), teaches: the Palestinian village.

Palestine's own way (Grade 5, PS G5P2): the settlement story is told as a ladder — farming, animal domestication, the agricultural village, the Palestinian village of today, then the city located on the map — and the year closes with the road itself: what a road is, its parts, and how to cross it safely.
