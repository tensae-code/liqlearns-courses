# Family size — education and income per child

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Family versus household

Family means kinship and marriage. A household means sharing food, shelter and essentials — kin or not. Household types are enumerated: single, couple-only, couple-with-children, single-parent, extended, non-relative, multi-generational and skipped-generation.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sizes around the world

Average household size runs under 3 in Europe and North America, and over 5 in Africa and the Middle East. The world's income gap frames it: mean North American incomes are about 16 times Sub-Saharan Africa's.

## Sub lesson 3 — What size does to a child

More children means a larger share of income spent per child's schooling in total, and less invested in each child's health and education. The other way: more schooling for girls delays childbirth and shrinks families — the book's chart sets education's share of income against total fertility.
