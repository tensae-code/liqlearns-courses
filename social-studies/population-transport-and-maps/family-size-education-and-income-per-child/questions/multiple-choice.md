# Family size — education and income per child — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A household is defined by sharing —
a) food, shelter and essentials  b) a surname  c) a village
**Answer: a) food, shelter and essentials**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A skipped-generation household is one of the —
a) household types  b) family laws  c) income bands
**Answer: a) household types**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Household size over 5 is typical of —
a) Africa and the Middle East  b) Europe  c) North America
**Answer: a) Africa and the Middle East**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** More schooling for girls —
a) delays childbirth and shrinks families  b) raises fertility  c) ends schooling
**Answer: a) delays childbirth and shrinks families**
