# Family size — education and income per child — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distinguish family from household.
**Answer:** Family: kinship and marriage. Household: sharing food, shelter and essentials.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four household types.
**Answer:** Single, couple-only, couple-with-children, single-parent, extended, non-relative, multi-generational, skipped-generation (any four).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does family size do to investment per child?
**Answer:** More children: less invested in each child's health and education.
