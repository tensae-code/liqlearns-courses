# Video script — Family size — education and income per child

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Family means kinship and marriage.

Average household size runs under 3 in Europe and North America, and over 5 in Africa and the Middle East.

More children means a larger share of income spent per child's schooling in total, and less invested in each child's health and education.
