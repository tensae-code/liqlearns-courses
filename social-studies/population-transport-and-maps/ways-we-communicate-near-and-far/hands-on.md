# Hands-on — Ways We Communicate Near and Far

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Amoko y'itumanaho, uko rikorwa n'akamaro karyo: kinds of communication, how they work and why they matter (Unit 8); Types and means of communication in our province, their importance, dangers and difficulties (Unit 9); Forms and types of communication in East Africa (Unit 10, Lesson 2); Itumanaho: television, telephone, letters, radio, newspapers, internet and signs, and the importance of communication (Unit 8); Itumanaho mu murenge: communication in our sector (Unit 8); 9.5 Communication (Unit 9); 10.2 Forms and types of communication in East Africa, incl. postal communication (Unit 10). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
