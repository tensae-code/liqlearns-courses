# Human evolution — fossils, archaeology and the first humans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** State the two theories of human origin, one sentence each.
**Answer:** Creationist: humans created by God. Evolution: humans developed from lower beings through gradual change.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name Ethiopia's four keyed fossil sites.
**Answer:** Omo basin, Melka Kunture, Konso-Gardula, Middle Awash.
