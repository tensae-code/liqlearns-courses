# Human evolution — fossils, archaeology and the first humans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Archaeology is the science that —
a) Studies the weather  b) Counts today's population  c) Builds roads  d) Digs out material objects and fossils of the past
**Answer: d) Digs out material objects and fossils of the past**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A fossil is —
a) A living animal  b) A new tool  c) Remains of a dead plant, human or animal from the past  d) A written record
**Answer: c) Remains of a dead plant, human or animal from the past**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Homo erectus is known for —
a) Making the first tools  b) Standing upright and walking  c) Knowledge as its mark  d) Building cities
**Answer: b) Standing upright and walking**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The distinctive feature of Homo sapiens is —
a) Knowledge  b) Upright walking  c) Tool-making  d) Long arms
**Answer: a) Knowledge**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is an Ethiopian fossil site?
a) Olduvai Gorge  b) Lake Turkana  c) Greenwich  d) Middle Awash
**Answer: d) Middle Awash**
**6. (Sub lesson 4)** What is Australopithecus?
a) the upright hominid who spread widely from Africa
b) the early hominid line of Lucy (Dinknesh)
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: b) the early hominid line of Lucy (Dinknesh)**
