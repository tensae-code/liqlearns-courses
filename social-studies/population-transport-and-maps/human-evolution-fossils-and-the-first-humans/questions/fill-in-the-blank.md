# Human evolution — fossils, archaeology and the first humans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The family of humans and human-like creatures is the ___.
**Answer: hominids**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Homo ___ was the tool-maker.
**Answer: habilis**
**3. (Sub lesson 4)** The term for 'the upright hominid who spread widely from Africa' is ______.
**Answer: Homo erectus**
