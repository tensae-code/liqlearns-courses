# Lesson plan — Human evolution — fossils, archaeology and the first humans

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Human evolution — fossils, archaeology and the first humans
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how we know about the first humans — archaeology and fossils, two theories of origin, the three Homo stages, and Ethiopia's fossil sites
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on teacher-guide evidence only — the learner needs nothing before it but curiosity about where people came from.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Digging the past — archaeology and fossils** — archaeology as the science of dug-up remains, fossils, hominids, and the study of prehistory.
2. **Two theories and three stages** — the creationist and evolution theories set side by side, and the Homo line: habilis, erectus, sapiens.
3. **Ethiopia's fossil ground** — the named Ethiopian and regional sites — Omo, Melka Kunture, Konso-Gardula, Middle Awash, Olduvai, Lake Turkana — on a sketch map.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: nothing — this lesson opens the people story. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Define archaeology, fossil and hominid.
2. State the two theories of human origin side by side, fairly.
3. Name the three Homo stages and what each is known for.
4. Mark Ethiopia's four named fossil sites and the two regional ones on a sketch map.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A globe or ball helps where the lesson names one. Nothing else.
