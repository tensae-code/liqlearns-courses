# Hands-on — Social Services and Important Places in Our Province

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Social services and important places in our province: locating them on a provincial map, their problems, and ways of preserving them; study tour to a social-service facility (Unit 6); Public places and assets in Rwanda (Unit 4, Lesson 1); Problems faced by public places and assets, incl. game parks (Unit 4, Lesson 2); Ibintu by'ingenzi biri mu murenge wacu: important things/places in our sector (Unit 2); Gufata neza umutungo rusange mu murenge: caring for public property in our sector (Unit 2); 6.1 Social services and important places in our province, incl. education, security, transport, medical, banking and market services (Unit 6); 6.8 Important places in our province (Unit 6); 4.1 Public places and assets in our country: public places, public assets and their importance (Unit 4); 4.2 Problems faced by public places and assets; 4.3 Preserving public places and assets (Unit 4). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
