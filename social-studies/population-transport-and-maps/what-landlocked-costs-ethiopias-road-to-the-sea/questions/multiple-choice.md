# What landlocked costs — Ethiopia’s road to the sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia is landlocked, meaning it has —
a) no neighbours  b) no rivers  c) no seacoast of its own
**Answer: c) no seacoast of its own**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia is bordered by —
a) eight countries  b) six countries  c) four countries
**Answer: b) six countries**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The lifeline corridor runs to —
a) Djibouti  b) Massawa  c) Mombasa
**Answer: a) Djibouti**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The corridor’s two carriers are —
a) road and air  b) rail and river  c) road and railway
**Answer: c) road and railway**
