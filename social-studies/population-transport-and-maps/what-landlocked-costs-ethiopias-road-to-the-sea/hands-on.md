# Hands-on — What landlocked costs — Ethiopia’s road to the sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Corridor drawing
Draw Ethiopia, its six neighbours in place, and the corridor from Addis Ababa to Djibouti port in red. Mark where the crossing happens — that border point is where the landlocked cost is paid.

## Cost list
With a partner, list everything being landlocked adds to a sack of exported coffee: distance, border time, port fees in another country, dependence on the corridor staying open.
