# Video script — What landlocked costs — Ethiopia’s road to the sea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Ethiopia has no coast. Every export crosses a neighbour.
Six neighbours ring it; the Red Sea glitters near, but not Ethiopia’s.
So one corridor matters above all: Addis to Djibouti.
Road and railway — the lifeline to the port.
