# Hands-on — Map Of Irans Seas And Ports

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Worksheets 21–22: The map of Iran's seas (نقشه‌ی دریاهای ایران) — write the names: the Persian Gulf, the Sea of Oman and the Caspian Sea; and The ports (بندرها) — write the names of the ports that are not written on the map. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
