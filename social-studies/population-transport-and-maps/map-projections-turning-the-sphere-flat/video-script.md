The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 10 Geography* (printed p. 91), teaches: Ch. 4: Map projections (مساقط الخرائط) — turning the spherical surface flat, the Greenwich meridian, and the kinds of projection (cylindrical, vertical, oblique).

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 10 Geography): the literary track opens geography as a discipline in its own right — its thought from the Sumerians to GIS, how a geographer researches, where geographical data come from and how they are drawn, and only then the fields of the science; Iraq supplies the worked examples throughout (Baghdad research problem, Iraqi topographic maps, Iraq's gas time series, the Baghdad–Basra distance exercise).
