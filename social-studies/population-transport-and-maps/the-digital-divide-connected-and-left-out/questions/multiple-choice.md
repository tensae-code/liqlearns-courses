# The digital divide — connected and left out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The divide is a gap in access to —
a) ICTs and the internet  b) land  c) water
**Answer: a) ICTs and the internet**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Access is measured by TVs, computers, phones and —
a) connections  b) radios only  c) roads
**Answer: a) connections**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A driver of the divide is —
a) connection cost and quality  b) latitude  c) soil
**Answer: a) connection cost and quality**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Banking for the unbanked is one of digitalisation's —
a) gifts  b) harms  c) drivers
**Answer: a) gifts**
