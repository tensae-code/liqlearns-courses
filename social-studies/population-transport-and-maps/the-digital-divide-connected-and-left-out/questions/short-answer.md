# The digital divide — connected and left out — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define the digital divide.
**Answer:** The gap in ICT and internet access between persons, households, firms and regions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four drivers.
**Answer:** Income, connection cost/quality, education/digital literacy, location.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two harms and two gifts.
**Answer:** Harms: lost jobs, widened inequality, cybercrime/hate speech/fake news. Gifts: telemedicine, e-learning, weather/market information, banking (two of each).
