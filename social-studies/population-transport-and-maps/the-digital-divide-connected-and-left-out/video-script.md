# Video script — The digital divide — connected and left out

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The divide is the gap between persons, households, firms and regions in access to ICTs and the internet — and so to economic and political opportunity.

Its drivers: income, connection cost and quality, education and digital literacy, and location.

Against the harms stand digitalisation's gifts: telemedicine, e-learning, weather and market information for farmers and fishers, and banking for the unbanked.
