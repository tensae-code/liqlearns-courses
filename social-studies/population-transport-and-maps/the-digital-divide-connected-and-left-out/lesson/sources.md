Guatemala's own way: one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book per grade, organised in four units that alternate History of Guatemala/Central America with the physical geography of the isthmus — every map carries the note about the unresolved Belize territorial differendum, and Geography is always worked on maps of the seven Central American countries with Guatemala's own Sierra de las Minas, Motagua river and Maya Biosphere as the home examples. That way is stocked here as the way Guatemala teaches this thing.

Costa Rica's own way: the CONED 11 anthology opens with a Geography unit on globalisation read "from the geographic viewpoint" (spatial flows of production and consumption, global cities, space-time compression), then turns to Costa Rica's own 20th-century history; its commercial workbook twin, Porras y Gamboa's *Estudios Sociales 11*, teaches the same ministry programme as a fixed cycle (problem question, basic concepts, my previous ideas, widening my knowledge, I apply my knowledge, problem-based learning, exam preparation) with heavy map-work and Costa Rica-specific data: ICE internet-coverage map, services-export league table, Costa Rica's geo-strategic bridge position between North/South America and the Caribbean. That way is stocked here as the way Costa Rica teaches this thing.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 6) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 6 shelter becomes settlement geography — development of settlements in Zimbabwe, siting of shelter in rural and urban areas, and disaster and risk management; transport adds road signs/signals/markings and Zimbabwe's transport and communication links with other countries, plus ICT development. That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Malaysia's own way (MY T2 Geografi Tingkatan 2): the year pairs map skills (scale, topographic maps) with weather and climate, then transport and telecommunications as the human-geography core, an Asia regional study, and global warming with green technology as the environmental close.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 10 Geografija 1 (gimnazija)): the gimnazija first year is systematic physical geography first (Earth's interior, rocks, landforms, weather and climate, soils, vegetation, waters), then human geography (population, settlements, farming, industry and energy, transport, tourism), closing on sustainable development and spatial planning — with Slovenian cases (earthquake risk in Slovenia, the Port of Koper) set inside the world topics.
