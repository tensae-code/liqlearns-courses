# Hands-on — Gulf Population Structure Imbalance And Its Effects

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 2 Lesson 2, Population Challenges in the Arab Gulf States: the imbalance in population structure — a large gap between groups of the population by nationality, age and sex; in 2022–2023 citizens were about 47% and non-citizens about 53% of the Gulf's people; its effects are security, social, economic (draining financial resources, unemployment among national workers), health and cultural (weakening of the Arabic language); the states' efforts — training citizens, linking education outputs to the labour market, drawing the private sector into employing national labour. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
