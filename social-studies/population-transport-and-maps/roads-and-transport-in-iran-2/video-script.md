Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 5 Social Studies* (printed p. 45), teaches: Lesson 9 Roads and transport (2) (راه‌ها و حمل و نقل ۲): the railway (راه‌آهن) — look at the map of Iran's nationwide railway: besides carrying passengers it moves goods, and carrying heavy loads by freight train is cheaper than by truck; the captain (ناخدا / کاپیتان) who steers the tugboat ship along the routes marked on the chart; and air transport (حمل و نقل هوایی) — the aeroplane is very suitable for passengers and light goods, for it is fast and covers long distances; look at the map of Iran's airports (نقشه‌ی فرودگاه‌های ایران).
