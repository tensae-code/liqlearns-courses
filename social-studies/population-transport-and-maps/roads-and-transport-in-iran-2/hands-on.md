# Hands-on — Roads And Transport In Iran 2

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 9 Roads and transport (2) (راه‌ها و حمل و نقل ۲): the railway (راه‌آهن) — look at the map of Iran's nationwide railway: besides carrying passengers it moves goods, and carrying heavy loads by freight train is cheaper than by truck; the captain (ناخدا / کاپیتان) who steers the tugboat ship along the routes marked on the chart; and air transport (حمل و نقل هوایی) — the aeroplane is very suitable for passengers and light goods, for it is fast and covers long distances; look at the map of Iran's airports (نقشه‌ی فرودگاه‌های ایران). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
