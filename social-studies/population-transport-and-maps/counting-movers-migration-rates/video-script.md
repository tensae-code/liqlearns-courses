# Video script — Counting movers — migration rates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The immigration rate counts arrivals per 1,000 population; the emigration rate counts departures per 1,000.

The net migration rate is the balance of the two: immigration minus emigration.

Set the net rate beside the map of countries where negative natural increase and net migration combine: deaths above births and leavers above arrivers together shrink a population from both sides..
