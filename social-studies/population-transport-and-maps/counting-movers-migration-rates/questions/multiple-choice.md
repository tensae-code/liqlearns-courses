# Counting movers — migration rates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration rates are counted per —
a) 1,000 population  b) 100 population  c) 1 person
**Answer: a) 1,000 population**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The net migration rate is —
a) immigration minus emigration  b) immigration plus emigration  c) births minus deaths
**Answer: a) immigration minus emigration**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's worked case resolves to a net rate of —
a) 2.6 per 1,000  b) 26 per 1,000  c) 0.26 per 1,000
**Answer: a) 2.6 per 1,000**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Population shrinks from both sides when negative natural increase combines with —
a) net out-migration  b) net in-migration  c) high fertility
**Answer: a) net out-migration**
