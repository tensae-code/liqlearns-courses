# Counting movers — migration rates — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define the net migration rate.
**Answer:** Immigration rate minus emigration rate, per 1,000 population.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What was the book's worked answer?
**Answer:** A net rate of 2.6 per 1,000.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things combine to shrink the mapped countries?
**Answer:** Negative natural increase and net out-migration.
