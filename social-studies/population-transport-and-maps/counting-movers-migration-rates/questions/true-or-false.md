# Counting movers — migration rates — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A positive net rate means more arrived than left.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The net rate adds immigration and emigration together.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Natural increase can be negative.
**Answer: True**
