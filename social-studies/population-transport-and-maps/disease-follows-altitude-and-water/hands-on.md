# Hands-on — Disease follows altitude and water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Risk map
Sketch your own area: mark water sources, low wet ground, and homes. Place a malaria-risk mark where the lesson’s conditions gather, and a clean-water mark where water is protected.

## Source audit
List the water sources homes around you actually use. For each, say what the lesson would warn about it.
