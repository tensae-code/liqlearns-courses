# Disease follows altitude and water

## Do it first

The mosquito does not climb high. Where you live by height and water shapes which diseases threaten you. Read the pattern.

## Sub lesson 1 — Malaria’s altitude

Disease environments differ by altitude. Malaria is concentrated in the lowlands and mid-altitudes, where warmth and standing water suit its mosquito. Other diseases are patterned by water, sanitation and climate. The guide’s evidence adds the clean-water side: rivers and lakes are the main rural water sources, and lack of access to clean water spreads disease.

## Sub lesson 2 — Health improving

Against the pattern stands progress: Ethiopia’s health investment and outcomes have been improving. The guide’s extension spells out the hard edge that remains — drought-linked disease, including cholera, strikes where water fails. Altitude, water and investment together write a place’s health.
