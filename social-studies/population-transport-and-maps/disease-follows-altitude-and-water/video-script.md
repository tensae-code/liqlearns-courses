# Video script — Disease follows altitude and water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Disease has an address: altitude and water.
Malaria keeps to the warm lowlands and mid-heights.
Dirty water spreads its own diseases.
And where drought dries the sources, cholera can follow.
