# Disease follows altitude and water — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Malaria is concentrated in —
a) the highest plateaus  b) the Wurch zone  c) the lowlands and mid-altitudes
**Answer: c) the lowlands and mid-altitudes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Malaria suits places with —
a) dry sand  b) warmth and standing water  c) cold and wind
**Answer: b) warmth and standing water**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The main rural water sources named in the evidence are —
a) rivers and lakes  b) piped taps in every home  c) rain tanks only
**Answer: a) rivers and lakes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A drought-linked disease spelled out is —
a) malaria  b) measles  c) cholera
**Answer: c) cholera**
