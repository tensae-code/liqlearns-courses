# Lesson plan — Iraq's Governorates And Administrative Divisions

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Iraq's Governorates And Administrative Divisions
**Place in the curriculum:** Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Iraq, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ch. 6 The homeland: The governorate (المحافظة) — Iraq's administrative divisions and how many governorates there are; Unit 1 Ch. 1: The administrative divisions (التقسيمات الإدارية) — Iraq's governorates on the map; Governorate of Dohuk (دهوك): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — far north, a Kurdistan Region governorate: mountain region, springs and eyes of water, food and construction industries, border crossings with Turkey and Syria, summer resorts; Governorate of Erbil (اربيل): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — north, Kurdistan Region: plains and plateaus of the south-west (Erbil plain), transport link between Iraq and its neighbours, food, chemical, textile and paper industries; Governorate of Sulaymaniyah (السليمانية): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — north-east, Kurdistan Region: Qara Dagh mountains, plateau and plains, Dokan Dam on the Little Zab storing water for irrigation and electricity, farming; Governorate of Nineveh (نينوى): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — north-west: relief in three parts (mountain region, undulating region, alluvial plain), Mosul Dam, population about 3,633,648 (2017 estimates), Badoush cement works; Governorate of Kirkuk (كركوك): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — transitional land between the undulating region and the alluvial plain, the Adhaim river and springs, oil country, farming and industry; Governorate of Salahuddin (صلاح الدين): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — many neighbouring governorates; the alluvial plain in its south, surface water resources, Baiji refinery; Governorate of Diyala (ديالى): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — centre-east: Hamrin hills, two climates (steppe in the north, desert in the south), Diyala river and other surface water, food and canning industries and mineral wealth; Governorate of Baghdad (بغداد): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — the capital and largest city by population (about 7,916,847 in 2017 estimates), administrative centre, electrical industries and Dora refinery; Governorate of Anbar (الانبار): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — largest governorate by area: Jazira plateau, alluvial plain and western plateau; oil export by pipeline along the Syrian, Jordanian and Saudi borders; Governorate of Wasit (واسط): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — centre-east of the alluvial plain, sub-tropical desert climate, Kut Barrage (سدة الكوت), farming and industry; Governorate of Babil (بابل): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — north of the Middle Euphrates region, desert climate of the central plain, Hindiya Barrage and the dams-and-irrigation-projects map exercise, textile and car-assembly plants; Governorate of Karbala (كربلاء المقدسة): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — centre-west on the edge of the western plateau, population about 1,187,245 (2017), religious tourism economy; route exercise naming every governorate passed from Karbala to Dohuk; Governorate of Al-Qadisiyyah / Diwaniyah (القادسية): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — south-centre of the alluvial plain, population about 1,257,689 (2017), farming-led economic activity; Governorate of Najaf (النجف الأشرف): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — west of Iraq, hot desert climate with dry hot summers and scant rain, religious tourism; Governorate of Maysan (ميسان): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — the Huwaiza marsh (هور الحويزة) and the marshes country, population about 1,083,937 (2017); Governorate of Dhi Qar (ذي قار): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — south of Iraq, population about 2,041,066 (2017), varied farming activity and the marshes; Governorate of Al-Muthanna (المثنى): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — south-west, hot dry desert climate with under 150 mm of rain, wheat and barley growing; Governorate of Basra (البصرة): geographical location, relief, climate, water, population and economic activity — far south-east, Iraq's sea outlet on the Gulf, desert climate with humid Gulf winds, the marshes (الأهوار), ports, oil and farming; Ch. 1 applied study: The population geography of Iraq — geographical distribution and its natural and human factors, natural growth (births about 34.9‰, deaths 5.7‰ in 1995–2000), and the sex ratio of 99 males per 100 females — as Iraq's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
