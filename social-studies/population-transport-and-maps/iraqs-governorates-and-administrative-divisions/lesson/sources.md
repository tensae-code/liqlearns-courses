**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Iraq).**

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 4 Social Studies): geography enters inside "Social Studies" through the child's own surroundings — phenomena are sorted from photographs first, then the local environment, then the map of Iraq's governorates, and only then the wider geographical environments (city, countryside); history and homeland/civics chapters follow in the same book.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies): the whole first unit is Iraq's own geography — location first (world, region, locality, then the astronomical location), then the land itself region by region off the relief map, then people and economic activity; history and national education follow as separate units.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies): a whole unit walks Iraq governorate by governorate — all eighteen in one fixed pattern (geographical location, relief, climate, water resources, population with 2017 estimates, economic activity, then a short historical note) — so the child learns the country as its provinces, each with its own map; a second unit on Iraqi society (Civics) follows.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography): a human-geography course in six chapters — population, cities and countryside, agriculture, industry and mining, transport and trade, political geography and tourism — and every chapter closes with an applied study of Iraq itself, so the world pattern is always brought home.
