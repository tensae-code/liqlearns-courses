# Hands-on — Arab Gulf States Location And Strategic Importance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 1 Lesson 1: the Arab Gulf is a water arm of the Arabian Sea running from the Gulf of Oman to Shatt al-Arab; the Gulf States lie in south-west Asia (relative location) at the centre of the world, commanding world trade routes, with shores fit for ports and huge oil reserves; Unit 1 Lesson 1, The Geographic Location of the Arab Gulf States and its Economic Importance: the region lies east of the Arab world and the Tropic of Cancer crosses some of its states (the UAE, Oman, Saudi Arabia); from old times the Gulf was an important water passage for trade from Europe to India and Africa (silk and spices), the region a world centre of the pearl trade, its location a trade intermediary between East and West; Unit 1 Lesson 5, Kuwait Shares the Arab Homeland's Natural Features: Kuwait's geographic location — in the Asian wing of the Arab homeland, at the north-east of the Arabian Peninsula — plays an important role for the Arab states: it is a point of connection between its parts and the world, and a sea outlet that helped trade flourish.
