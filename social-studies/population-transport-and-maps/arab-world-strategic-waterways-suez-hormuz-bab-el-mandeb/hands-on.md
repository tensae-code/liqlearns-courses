# Hands-on — Arab World Strategic Waterways Suez Hormuz Bab El Mandeb

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 2 Lesson 4: the Arab world's geographic location as a hidden resource — the strategic waterways of Gibraltar, Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb and the Suez Canal ease world trade and bring income to the states overlooking them; Unit 3 Lesson 2, Political Problems: Ceuta and Melilla are Moroccan cities still held by Spain because of their economic resources and their strategic position near the Strait of Gibraltar; Morocco seeks their return through diplomatic dialogue and international law; Unit 3 Lesson 1: the Islamic world overlooks the Pacific, Atlantic and Indian Oceans and the Mediterranean, Red Sea, Caspian Sea and Arab Gulf, and controls the waterways of Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb, Gibraltar and the Suez Canal. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
