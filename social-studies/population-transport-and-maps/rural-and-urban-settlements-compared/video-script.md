Eritrea's ER G6 Social Studies book, the *ER G6 Social Studies* (printed p. 41), teaches: Unit 6: differences between rural and urban settlements. Eritrea's ER G4 Social Studies book, the *ER G4 Social Studies* (printed p. 111), teaches: Chapter 7: 7.8 Rural and urban life.

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Social Studies): one integrated Social Studies book per grade weaving History, Geography, Civics and Culture strands; Geography is taught through Eritrea itself first (Eritrea's location and size, Eritrean settlement, migration and disasters) with very practical, activity-led lessons. Eritrea's own way (ER G4 Social Studies): the Tigrinya-medium primary book teaches Geography through the child's own surroundings first — our environment, protecting our soil, water, plants and animals, then maps and globes, community life, and the shapes of the land.
