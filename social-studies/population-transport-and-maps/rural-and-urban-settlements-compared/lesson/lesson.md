## Sub lesson 2 — Eritrea's way — the book's own teaching way

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Social Studies): one integrated Social Studies book per grade weaving History, Geography, Civics and Culture strands; Geography is taught through Eritrea itself first (Eritrea's location and size, Eritrean settlement, migration and disasters) with very practical, activity-led lessons. Eritrea's own way (ER G4 Social Studies): the Tigrinya-medium primary book teaches Geography through the child's own surroundings first — our environment, protecting our soil, water, plants and animals, then maps and globes, community life, and the shapes of the land. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
