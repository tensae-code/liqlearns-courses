# Hands-on — Road safety — what accidents cost

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Write the study's four key numbers from memory: the two age shares, the urban share, the student share.

## Activity 2
Say in one sentence why students are the largest victim group.

## Activity 3
List one duty each for government, public, students and drivers in your own area.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the world's yearly toll, the age group hit hardest in the Ethiopian study, and three harms from the list.
