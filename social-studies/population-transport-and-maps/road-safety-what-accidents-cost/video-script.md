# Video script — Road safety — what accidents cost

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An accident is over in a second. Its costs are not. Before you read the numbers, list everything one bad accident can take from one family. Then compare your list with the book's.

This lesson teaches: the toll of traffic accidents in the world and in Ethiopia — who is hit hardest, and the full list of harms an accident brings. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
