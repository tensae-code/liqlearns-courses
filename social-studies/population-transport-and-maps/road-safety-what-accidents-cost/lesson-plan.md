# Lesson plan — Road safety — what accidents cost

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Road safety — what accidents cost
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the toll of traffic accidents in the world and in Ethiopia — who is hit hardest, and the full list of harms an accident brings
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the two road-safety lessons before it in this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Road safety — signs and crossings. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the world's yearly toll: deaths and injuries.
2. Read the Ethiopian study: the age groups and groups hit hardest.
3. Explain why students are so exposed.
4. List the harms of an accident and name duties for government, public, students and drivers.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A globe or ball helps where the lesson names one. Nothing else.
