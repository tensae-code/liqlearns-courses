# Road safety — what accidents cost — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Worldwide, traffic accidents kill about how many people a year?
a) 13,000  b) 130,000  c) 13,000,000  d) 1,300,000
**Answer: d) 1,300,000**

**2.** In the Ethiopian study, the age group with the largest share of victims was —
a) 60–69  b) 0–9  c) 20–29  d) 50–59
**Answer: c) 20–29**

**3.** The largest occupational group among victims was —
a) Farmers  b) Students  c) Drivers  d) Teachers
**Answer: b) Students**

**4.** The study found victims were mostly —
a) Urban  b) Rural  c) At sea  d) Abroad
**Answer: a) Urban**
