# Road safety — what accidents cost — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name three harms an accident brings beyond the crash itself.
**Answer:** Death/disability/psychological crisis/property destruction/family breakup/lost income/medical costs/poverty (any three).

**2.** Why are students so exposed to traffic accidents?
**Answer:** They walk the most, at busy hours, on crowded urban roads — exposure.
