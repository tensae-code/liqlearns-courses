# Hands-on — The European Union

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 9: The European Union, a great regional economic union — its growth from 1957 to 27 members (17.8% of world GDP in 2021), the Maastricht aims and three pillars, its institutions, its place in the world economy (the Frankfurt, Paris and Amsterdam financial centres) and cooperation inside it (single market, the euro, Schengen, the European Green Deal). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
