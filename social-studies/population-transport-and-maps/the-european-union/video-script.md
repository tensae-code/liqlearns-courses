# Video script — The European Union

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11* (printed pp. 37–43), teaches: Bài 9: The European Union, a great regional economic union — its growth from 1957 to 27 members (17.8% of world GDP in 2021), the Maastricht aims and three pillars, its institutions, its place in the world economy (the Frankfurt, Paris and Amsterdam financial centres) and cooperation inside it (single market, the euro, Schengen, the European Green Deal).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11): after Grade 10's world systems, Grade 11 walks the world region by region — Latin America, the EU, Southeast Asia and ASEAN, Southwest Asia, the USA, Russia, Japan, China, Australia and South Africa — always with the same lens (position, nature, people, economy) and always measuring Vietnam's own place among them (its HDI row in the opening table, EU–Vietnam trade, Vietnam inside ASEAN, Japan's FDI into Vietnam).
