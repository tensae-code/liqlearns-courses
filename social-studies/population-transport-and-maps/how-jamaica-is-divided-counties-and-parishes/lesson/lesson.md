## Sub lesson 1 — How Jamaica is divided, and how divisions and places changed over time

Jamaica's Grade 4 Exploratory Core guide (Social Studies Term 2 Unit 2, printed p. 202) teaches how Jamaica is divided — the lesson's own title names the two levels the guide works with: counties and parishes — and how divisions and places changed over time. The divisions are learned as living things: they were made, and they have changed. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Jamaica's way — the book's own teaching way

Jamaica's own way (Grade 4 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): Social Studies is one of four subjects fused into a single Exploratory Core guide, organised by four attainment themes; Geography enters through Jamaica itself — Taino settlement maps, cardinal points, Jamaica's physical features, parishes, weather and the Earth in the Solar System — always with hands-on map and model work. That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
