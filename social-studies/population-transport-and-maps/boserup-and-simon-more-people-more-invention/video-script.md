# Video script — Boserup and Simon — more people, more invention?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Three voices argue about people and food. Malthus speaks first: population outgrows food, and crisis must follow. Boserup answers from the fields: no — pressure is the inventor; more people push farming to intensify, to terrace, to irrigate, to invent, and growth itself drives development. Her critics interrupt: shortages more often send people away than make them invent, and diets suffer quietly. Then Simon closes for the optimists: resources were never fixed — ingenuity keeps expanding what counts as a resource. Three positions, one question you must judge yourself: do more people mean more hunger, or more invention?
