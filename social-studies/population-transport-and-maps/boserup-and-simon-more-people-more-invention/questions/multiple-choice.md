# Boserup and Simon — more people, more invention? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Boserup's book appeared in…
a) 1798  b) 1965  c) 2001
**Answer: b) 1965**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For Boserup, population growth…
a) blocks all development  b) drives farming to intensify and innovate  c) does nothing
**Answer: b) drives farming to intensify and innovate**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Boserup argued from…
a) field evidence  b) pure theory  c) census tables only
**Answer: a) field evidence**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A critic's answer to Boserup: shortage more often triggers…
a) innovation  b) out-migration  c) celebration
**Answer: b) out-migration**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Simon argues resources are…
a) fixedly finite  b) not fixed — ingenuity expands supply  c) imaginary
**Answer: b) not fixed — ingenuity expands supply**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Boserup herself conceded overpopulation can drive…
a) unsuitable farming  b) nothing bad  c) emigration only
**Answer: a) unsuitable farming**
