# Boserup and Simon — more people, more invention? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does Boserup invert Malthus?
**Answer:** Malthus: population growth causes crisis. Boserup: population growth drives agricultural intensification and innovation, so growth leads to development.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give two criticisms of Boserup.
**Answer:** Any two of: thin economics; shortage triggers out-migration more often than innovation; diet quality ignored; adaptation can lag or turn damaging.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Simon's core claim?
**Answer:** Resources are not fixedly finite; human ingenuity expands supply.
