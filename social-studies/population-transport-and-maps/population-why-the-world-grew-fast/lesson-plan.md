# Lesson plan — Population — why the world grew fast

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Population — why the world grew fast
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** why world population stayed slow for ages and then grew fast from the Industrial Revolution — machines, medicines and modern transport
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the counting lesson next door — the learner can already read a growth graph.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Population — counting and graphing growth. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say when rapid world growth began and where.
2. Name the three great changes behind it: machines, medicines, modern transport.
3. Argue both sides of the debate: a large population hinders / helps development.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A globe or ball helps where the lesson names one. Nothing else.
