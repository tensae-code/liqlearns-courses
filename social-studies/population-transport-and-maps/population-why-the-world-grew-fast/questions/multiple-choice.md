# Population — why the world grew fast — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Rapid world population growth is dated especially from —
a) The first farming  b) The building of the pyramids  c) The first aeroplane  d) The Industrial Revolution
**Answer: d) The Industrial Revolution**

**2.** The Industrial Revolution began in —
a) Ethiopia, from 1950  b) China, from 1500  c) England, from 1750  d) America, from 1850
**Answer: c) England, from 1750**

**3.** Which change is NOT one of the three behind fast growth?
a) Machines replaced muscle  b) People stopped travelling  c) Medicines defeated killing diseases  d) Modern transport appeared
**Answer: b) People stopped travelling**

**4.** Medicines helped growth because —
a) People who would have died young lived  b) Babies were counted twice  c) Diseases travelled faster  d) Doctors built roads
**Answer: a) People who would have died young lived**
