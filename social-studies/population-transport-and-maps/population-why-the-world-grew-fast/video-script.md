# Video script — Population — why the world grew fast

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at a world population line in your mind: flat for thousands of years, then bending sharply upward. When did it bend — and what changed in the world at that moment? Hold your guess; the lesson answers it.

This lesson teaches: why world population stayed slow for ages and then grew fast from the Industrial Revolution — machines, medicines and modern transport. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
