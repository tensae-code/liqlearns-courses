# Hands-on — Population — why the world grew fast

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Draw the world growth line from memory: flat, then bending up. Mark 1750 and England on it.

## Activity 2
Write two reasons for each side of the debate.

## Activity 3
Ask an older person what has changed in your town in their lifetime — machines, medicine, transport — and note one answer.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the year and place where fast growth began, and the three changes behind it. Then give the strongest reason from the side of the debate you did NOT choose.
