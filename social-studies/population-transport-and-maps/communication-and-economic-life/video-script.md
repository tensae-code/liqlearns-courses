# Video script — Communication and Economic Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 11 S3 Geography SB* (printed p. 11), teaches: Communication: radio, telephone, mobile phone; its role in trade and economic uplift. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 8), teaches: Unit 1 Technology: how technology has changed the ways of communication in South Sudan. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies TG* (printed p. 13), teaches: Guide: Unit 1 — how people communicated in the past (picture study). (teacher guide — evidence)

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 11 S3 Geography SB): A world-geography book (trade routes, megacities, Southeast Asia) anchored back to South Sudan throughout — humanitarian airlift airfields, Thuraya satellite phones, a tourism plan for South Sudan, Juba as the urbanisation example.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies SB): Weather is learned by building the instruments — thermometer, rain gauge, wind vane and windsock made from local materials, then a school weather station — and map reading is taught on one worked map ("Upendo Area") with scale, symbol key and area measured by counting grid squares.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide grounds every weather idea in farm and river work — wind for threshing and winnowing, rough water making fishing risky — and teaches that forests help in the formation of rain. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.
