Greece's own way (Greece Grade 6 Geography Teacher Guide (old ed.)) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 6 turns outward from Greece to the whole Earth — Earth in space, the natural environment, Earth as people's living space (how humans live in deserts, polar lands, rainforests, temperate zones), then a continent-by-continent tour (Europe in depth first, then Asia, Africa, North and South America, Oceania). The guide works from children's own prior mental models (e.g. flat-Earth models, taught with oranges and globes) and gives per-chapter objectives, alternative pupil ideas, method, activities and workbook/assessment answers (printed pp. 100–103). That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Greece is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 6 Geography Workbook (old ed.)): a worksheet for every one of the 45 student chapters plus 10 assessment sheets — one per unit and then one per continent — so the world tour is practised continent by continent.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 6 Geography Student Book (old ed.)): the student twin of Book 4's 45 chapters, with one addition the guide only implies — every continent section opens with a one-page "identity" profile of the continent (area, extremes, population at a glance) before its position, environment, states and peoples chapters.
