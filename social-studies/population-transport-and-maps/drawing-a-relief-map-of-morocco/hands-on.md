# Hands-on — Drawing a Relief Map of Morocco

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw Morocco's relief
Draw a map of Morocco and put its relief on it with symbols you choose yourself, as the guide's lesson does.

## Finish it with title and key
Give your map a title that says what it shows, and add a key that explains every symbol you used. Swap maps with a companion: can they read yours from the key alone?
