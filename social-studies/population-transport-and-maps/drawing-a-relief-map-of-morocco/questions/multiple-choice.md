# Drawing a Relief Map of Morocco — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Geography Lesson 3 of Morocco's guide has the learner draw —
a) a relief map of Morocco  b) a picture of a ship  c) a family tree
**Answer: a) a relief map of Morocco**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On a drawn map, relief features are shown with —
a) chosen symbols  b) real stones  c) photographs only
**Answer: a) chosen symbols**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A finished map needs a title and —
a) a key  b) a song  c) a stamp
**Answer: a) a key**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The key of a map tells —
a) what each symbol means  b) the map's price  c) who drew it first
**Answer: a) what each symbol means**
