# Video script — Drawing a Relief Map of Morocco

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The third Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide turns the relief of the homeland into a map the learner draws: Relief features of my homeland — drawing a relief map of Morocco. A drawn map cannot show real mountains, so the learner chooses symbols to stand for the relief features.

A map with symbols needs two more things, and the lesson names them: a title, saying what the map shows, and a key, saying what each symbol means. With symbols chosen and the title and key in place, the relief map of Morocco is finished and readable by anyone.
