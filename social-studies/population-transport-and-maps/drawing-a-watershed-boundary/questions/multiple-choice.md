# Drawing a watershed boundary — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A confluence is —
a) where streams join  b) a stream's source  c) the basin outlet
**Answer: a) where streams join**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The watershed or divide is —
a) the high line separating basins  b) the lowest point  c) the river mouth
**Answer: a) the high line separating basins**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Step one of delineation is —
a) circle the outlet  b) draw flow arrows  c) connect high points
**Answer: a) circle the outlet**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** High points are marked with —
a) X's  b) circles  c) ticks
**Answer: a) X's**
