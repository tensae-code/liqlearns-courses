# Drawing a watershed boundary — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Flow arrows are drawn from the high points.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The boundary is traced by connecting high points around from the outlet.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A tributary is the basin's outlet.
**Answer: False**
