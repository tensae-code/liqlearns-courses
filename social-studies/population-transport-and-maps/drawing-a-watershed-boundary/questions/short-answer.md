# Drawing a watershed boundary — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define tributary, confluence and divide.
**Answer:** Tributary: feeder stream. Confluence: where streams join. Divide: the high line between basins.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the five steps in order.
**Answer:** Circle outlet; highlight tributaries and flow; mark high points X; draw flow arrows; connect high points around.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why start at the outlet?
**Answer:** Because the whole basin is defined by the point it drains through.
