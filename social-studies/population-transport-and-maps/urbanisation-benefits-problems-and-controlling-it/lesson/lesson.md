## Sub lesson 1 — Urbanisation — its benefits and problems

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC teaches urbanisation in Chapter 10: towns and cities growing as people move to them — a change that brings benefits and problems together.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kingston's urban problems, and controlling urbanisation in the Caribbean

The book reads the problems in one Caribbean city — Kingston, Jamaica — and then asks how urbanisation can be controlled in the Caribbean, so the lesson ends with answers, not only problems.

Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
