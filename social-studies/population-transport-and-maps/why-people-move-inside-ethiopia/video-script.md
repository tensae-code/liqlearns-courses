# Video script — Why people move inside Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

People move inside their own country.
Farm to farm — to the Awash valley schemes. Farm to town.
Some are resettled by programme.
Pushed by crowding. Pulled by work. Both ends feel it.
