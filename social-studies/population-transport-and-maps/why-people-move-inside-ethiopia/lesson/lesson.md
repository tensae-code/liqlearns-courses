# Why people move inside Ethiopia

## Do it first

A family leaves a crowded highland farm for a commercial farm in the Awash valley. Another leaves for the town. Both are this lesson. Read the movements.

## Sub lesson 1 — The movements

Internal migration in Ethiopia runs in three named directions: rural–rural movement — including to commercial-farm areas such as the Awash valley; rural–urban movement toward towns and cities; and resettlement, where people are moved and settled in new areas by programme.

[Picture 21039 — arrows from crowded highland farms to towns and farm schemes]

## Sub lesson 2 — Push and pull

Movements have push causes at the leaving end — crowding, land shortage, poor prospects — and pull causes at the arriving end — work, land, services. And they have effects at both ends: relief where people leave, pressure and new life where they arrive.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three movements, and explain any one of them by its push and its pull.
