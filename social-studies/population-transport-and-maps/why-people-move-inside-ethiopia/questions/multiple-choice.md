# Why people move inside Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Movement to commercial-farm areas like the Awash valley is —
a) rural–urban migration  b) resettlement  c) rural–rural migration
**Answer: c) rural–rural migration**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Resettlement is —
a) seasonal herding  b) movement and settling by programme  c) any move to a town
**Answer: b) movement and settling by programme**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A push cause is —
a) land shortage at the leaving end  b) jobs at the arriving end  c) city services
**Answer: a) land shortage at the leaving end**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A pull cause is —
a) crowding at home  b) a worn-out plot  c) work at the arriving end
**Answer: c) work at the arriving end**
