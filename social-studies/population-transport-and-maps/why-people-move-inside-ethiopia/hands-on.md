# Hands-on — Why people move inside Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arrow stories
Draw three arrows on an outline map — rural–rural, rural–urban, resettlement — and write a one-line story on each arrow.

## Push-pull sort
Make two piles from cause cards: land shortage, town jobs, crowded plot, farm-scheme work, poor prospects, city services. Label the piles PUSH and PULL.
