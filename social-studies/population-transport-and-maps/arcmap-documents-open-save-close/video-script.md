# Video script — ArcMap documents — open, save, close

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An ArcMap document, .mxd, holds the map, its display settings and pointers to the data.

Start ArcMap from its icon, or by double-clicking an .mxd file.

Close with File → Close.
