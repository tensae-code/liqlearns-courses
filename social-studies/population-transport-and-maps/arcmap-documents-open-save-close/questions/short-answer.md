# ArcMap documents — open, save, close — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does an .mxd hold, exactly?
**Answer:** The map, its display settings, and pointers to data.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you start ArcMap two ways?
**Answer:** From its icon, or by double-clicking an .mxd.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why save before opening a new map?
**Answer:** Because opening a new map closes the current one.
