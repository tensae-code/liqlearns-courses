# Hands-on — Asean Working Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 13: ASEAN — founded at Bangkok in 1967, its aims and 2008 Charter, the three Communities of 2015, how it works (summits and councils, consensus), its cooperation (AFTA, ATIGA, the AEC, the ASCC, RCEP, the SEA Games), its achievements and challenges — and Vietnam in ASEAN (a member since 1995, chair in 2010 and 2020). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
