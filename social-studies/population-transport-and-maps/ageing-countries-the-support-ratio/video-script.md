# Video script — Ageing countries — the support ratio

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Where LDCs strain under growth, more developed countries stagnate and age: working-age numbers fall while elder numbers rise, squeezing labour markets, pensions and health care..

The potential support ratio counts people aged 25 to 64 for each person over 65.

Japan's ratio, 1.8, is the world's lowest.
