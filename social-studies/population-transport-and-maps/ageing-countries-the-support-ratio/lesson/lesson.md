# Ageing countries — the support ratio

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: how many workers stand behind each elder in Japan? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The opposite problem

Where LDCs strain under growth, more developed countries stagnate and age: working-age numbers fall while elder numbers rise, squeezing labour markets, pensions and health care.

## Sub lesson 2 — The measuring tool

The potential support ratio counts people aged 25 to 64 for each person over 65. The higher the ratio, the more workers stand behind each elder.

## Sub lesson 3 — The numbers ahead

Japan's ratio, 1.8, is the world's lowest. Twenty-nine countries already sit below three, and by 2050, 48 countries may fall below two.
