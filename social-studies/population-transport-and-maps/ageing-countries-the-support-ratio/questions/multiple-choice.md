# Ageing countries — the support ratio — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The support ratio counts people aged —
a) 25–64 per person over 65  b) 0–14 per worker  c) 65+ per worker
**Answer: a) 25–64 per person over 65**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The world's lowest support ratio is —
a) Japan's, at 1.8  b) Ethiopia's, at 1.8  c) Japan's, at 18
**Answer: a) Japan's, at 1.8**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Countries already below a ratio of three number —
a) 29  b) 9  c) 48
**Answer: a) 29**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** By 2050, countries below two may number —
a) 48  b) 29  c) 12
**Answer: a) 48**
