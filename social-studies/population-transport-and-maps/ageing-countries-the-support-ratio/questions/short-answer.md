# Ageing countries — the support ratio — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define the potential support ratio.
**Answer:** People aged 25–64 per person over 65.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which country has the lowest, and what is it?
**Answer:** Japan, 1.8.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two things ageing squeezes.
**Answer:** Labour markets, pensions, health care (any two).
