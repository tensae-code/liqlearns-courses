# Video script — Central America — Its Countries and Limits

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Central America is an isthmus — a narrow strip of land joining North and South America. Seven countries share it, and the lesson locates each one on the map and names its limits: which sea, ocean or neighbouring country borders it on each side. Guatemala's own book works this on a map of the seven countries, the way every Geography topic in the book is worked.

Guatemala learns this inside one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book: one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book per grade, organised in four units that alternate History of Guatemala/Central America with the physical geography of the isthmus — every map carries the note about the unresolved Belize territorial differendum, and Geography is always worked on maps of the seven Central American countries with Guatemala's own Sierra de las Minas, Motagua river and Maya Biosphere as the home examples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
