# Hands-on — Transshipment and Container Ports

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Transshipment and Container Port: transshipment is unloading cargo from one vessel and loading it onto another to complete its journey; the Freeport container port in Grand Bahama provides many jobs and handles more than 10% of global container traffic; Transportation/Communication Worldwide: imports are inspected daily at the docks and entry ports; Prince George dock in Nassau is a port of entry where containers are offloaded and searched; surface mail, daily flights and instant electronic communication. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahamas' way
Say how the Bahamas' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
