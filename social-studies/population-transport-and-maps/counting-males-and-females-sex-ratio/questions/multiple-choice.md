# Counting males and females — sex ratio — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book frames the sex ratio as —
a) females per males  b) males per females  c) births per deaths
**Answer: a) females per males**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Regional sex ratios are compared across —
a) Europe's countries  b) Ethiopia's zones only  c) Africa's sub-regions
**Answer: c) Africa's sub-regions**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A tilted sex ratio can be moved by —
a) map scale  b) births, deaths or migration  c) the weather alone
**Answer: b) births, deaths or migration**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A measure means most when —
a) set beside its neighbours for comparison  b) read entirely alone  c) printed in a table
**Answer: a) set beside its neighbours for comparison**
