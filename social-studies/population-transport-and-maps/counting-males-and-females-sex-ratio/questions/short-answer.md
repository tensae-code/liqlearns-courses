# Counting males and females — sex ratio — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the book frame the sex ratio?
**Answer:** As females per males — the female count set against the male count.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why compare the ratio across sub-regions?
**Answer:** Because a measure means little alone and much beside its neighbours; where it tilts, you ask what moved it.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things that can tilt a population's sex ratio.
**Answer:** Births, deaths and migration.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Your count finds 48 females and 52 males. State the book's ratio in words.
**Answer:** Females per males: 48 females set against 52 males — a slightly male-tilted count, near balance.
