# Hands-on — Rural settlement patterns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pattern survey
From a hill, a map or memory, classify three real settlements near you by pattern. Sketch each in ten seconds — knot, scatter or ribbon — and defend the class.

## Why this pattern?
For one settlement, argue why its pattern fits its land: water point, road, defence in history, or each family on its own plot.
