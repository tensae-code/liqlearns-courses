# Rural settlement patterns

## Do it first

From the air, farm homes make shapes: a knot, a scatter, a ribbon along a road. Three patterns. Read them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The three patterns

Rural settlements are classified by pattern. Nucleated settlements cluster homes together in a knot, usually around a centre. Dispersed settlements scatter homes across the land, each on its own holding. Linear settlements string homes along a line — a road, a river, a valley.

## Sub lesson 2 — Permanence and the highland village

Settlements also classify by permanence — permanent homes against temporary ones. In Ethiopia’s highlands, the permanent farming village is the dominant rural form: nucleated or dispersed clusters of farming families on land they work year after year.
