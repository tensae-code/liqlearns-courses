# Rural settlement patterns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A nucleated settlement is —
a) scattered widely  b) strung along a road  c) clustered in a knot
**Answer: c) clustered in a knot**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A linear settlement follows —
a) a grid only  b) a road, river or valley  c) a circle
**Answer: b) a road, river or valley**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second classification is by —
a) permanence  b) size  c) wealth
**Answer: a) permanence**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The dominant highland rural form is —
a) the pastoral camp  b) the port town  c) the permanent farming village
**Answer: c) the permanent farming village**
