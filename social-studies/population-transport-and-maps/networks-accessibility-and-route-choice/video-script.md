The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 59), teaches: Lesson 4 Managing transport (مدیریت حمل‌ونقل): passenger and freight demand grown greatly in fifty years; route deviation and winding — the less the bends, the easier and cheaper to build the road (the A–B network diagrams); points with good access in a network mattering also in siting studies (placing a shop, a factory or a route); the kind of coast (indented or hazardous — tsunami, monsoon storms) affecting port building and management, and relief affecting the choice of transport mode and airport sites.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
