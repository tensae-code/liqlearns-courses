# Hands-on — Networks Accessibility And Route Choice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 4 Managing transport (مدیریت حمل‌ونقل): passenger and freight demand grown greatly in fifty years; route deviation and winding — the less the bends, the easier and cheaper to build the road (the A–B network diagrams); points with good access in a network mattering also in siting studies (placing a shop, a factory or a route); the kind of coast (indented or hazardous — tsunami, monsoon storms) affecting port building and management, and relief affecting the choice of transport mode and airport sites.
