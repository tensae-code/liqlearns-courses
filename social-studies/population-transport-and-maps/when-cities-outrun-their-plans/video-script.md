# Video script — When cities outrun their plans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Many African cities inherited a colonial street plan built for a small administered centre, not for the millions who came later.

Where growth outruns planning and housing, people house themselves: slums and squatter settlements rise on land the plan never serviced — Addis Ababa is the book's pictured case.

The unit's chain ends in strain: social instability, unrest and urban violence grow where work, housing and services fail the crowds the city attracted.
