# When cities outrun their plans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Urban primacy means —
a) one city towers over all others in its country  b) every town is the same size  c) the capital is the smallest town
**Answer: a) one city towers over all others in its country**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Slums and squatter settlements rise where —
a) land is most expensive  b) the plan is newest  c) growth outruns planning and housing
**Answer: c) growth outruns planning and housing**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The chain of unplanned growth ends in —
a) empty streets  b) social instability, unrest and urban violence  c) cheaper housing for all
**Answer: b) social instability, unrest and urban violence**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's pictured case of squatter settlement is —
a) Addis Ababa  b) Gore Town  c) Lake Tana
**Answer: a) Addis Ababa**
