# When cities outrun their plans — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the colonial legacy in the street plan?
**Answer:** Cities inherited plans built for a small administered centre, not for the millions who came later.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do squatter settlements form?
**Answer:** Because growth outruns planning and housing — people house themselves on land the plan never serviced.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must grow with the people, on this lesson's warning?
**Answer:** Plan, services and housing.
