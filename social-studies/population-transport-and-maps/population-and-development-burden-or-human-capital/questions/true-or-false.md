# Population and development — burden or human capital? — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Per-capita measurement divides development by the number of people.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Population size alone decides whether it is burden or asset.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Education and health investment builds human capital.
**Answer: True**
