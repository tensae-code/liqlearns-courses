# Population and development — burden or human capital? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Development's elements include…
a) income, employment, education, health, living standards  b) only income  c) only exports
**Answer: a) income, employment, education, health, living standards**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Development is measured…
a) per capita  b) per city  c) per factory
**Answer: a) per capita**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Population growth presses on…
a) jobs, housing, sanitation and services  b) nothing  c) only farms
**Answer: a) jobs, housing, sanitation and services**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The least developed group is marked by…
a) low mortality  b) high infant and maternal mortality, inadequate infrastructure  c) surplus housing
**Answer: b) high infant and maternal mortality, inadequate infrastructure**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Human capital is built by investment in…
a) education and health  b) roads only  c) armies
**Answer: a) education and health**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Human capital turns population into…
a) a burden  b) an asset  c) a statistic
**Answer: b) an asset**
