# Population and development — burden or human capital? — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name development's five elements.
**Answer:** Income, employment, education, health and living standards.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three things population growth presses on.
**Answer:** Any three of: jobs and employment; housing; sanitation; services.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is human capital and why is it the counterweight?
**Answer:** Capacity built by investment in education and health; it turns population into an asset that produces development instead of only consuming services.
