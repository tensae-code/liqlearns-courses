# Video script — Population and development — burden or human capital?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Population growth presses on every element of development — jobs, housing, services — but investment in education and health builds human capital, the counterweight that turns people into an asset. First, development unpacked: Development is unpacked through its elements — income, employment, education, health and living standards (the book's Figure 4.2) — and measured per capita, person by person. Then, human capital — the counterweight: Against the pressure stands one counterweight: human capital. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
