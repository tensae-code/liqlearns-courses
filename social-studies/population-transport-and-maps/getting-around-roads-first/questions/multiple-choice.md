# Getting around — roads first — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia’s dominant transport is —
a) rail  b) air  c) road
**Answer: c) road**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The road network’s pattern is —
a) a grid in the Ogaden  b) radial, out from Addis Ababa  c) a ring around the coast
**Answer: b) radial, out from Addis Ababa**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional carriage uses —
a) pack animals and porters  b) only camels  c) bicycles only
**Answer: a) pack animals and porters**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Traditional carriage still rules where —
a) the railway runs  b) airports stand  c) roads do not reach or loads are small
**Answer: c) roads do not reach or loads are small**
