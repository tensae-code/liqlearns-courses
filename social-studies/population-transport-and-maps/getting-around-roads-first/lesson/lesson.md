# Getting around — roads first

## Do it first

Count the ways goods leave your nearest town. Almost everything you count will go by road. Read why roads rule Ethiopia.

## Sub lesson 1 — Roads dominate

Road is Ethiopia’s dominant transport. The road classes are organised in a network whose pattern is radial: the main roads run out from Addis Ababa like spokes, joining the capital to the regions. Most passengers and most freight in the country move on these roads.

[Picture 21040 — a road map radiating from Addis Ababa, with a donkey cart inset]

## Sub lesson 2 — The older carriage

Beside the roads, traditional carriage persists: pack animals — donkeys, mules, camels — and porters still carry goods where roads do not reach or loads are small. Ethiopia’s transport is thus two systems at once: the modern road network and the ancient animal carriage beneath it.
