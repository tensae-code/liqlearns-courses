# Hands-on — Getting around — roads first

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Spoke map
Draw Addis Ababa as a hub and draw the main spokes to the regions you can name. Mark one place the spokes do not reach, and say what carries goods there.

## Load count
Watch a road or path near home for ten minutes and tally what passes: lorry, bus, car, donkey, person carrying. Present your tally as the lesson’s two systems.
