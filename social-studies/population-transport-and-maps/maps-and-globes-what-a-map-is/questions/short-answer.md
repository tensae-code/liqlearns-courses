# Maps and globes — what a map is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the definition of a map in your own words, keeping its four working parts.
**Answer:** A tool showing the Earth's surface (or part) from above, reduced from true size, systematically, on flat paper.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three of the map's seven services.
**Answer:** Wide area at once/positions, distances, areas, directions, spatial relationships, planning/research information, military strategy (any three).
