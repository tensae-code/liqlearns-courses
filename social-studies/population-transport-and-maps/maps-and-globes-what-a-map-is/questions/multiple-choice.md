# Maps and globes — what a map is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A map shows the Earth's surface —
a) From ground level at true size  b) Only in words  c) As a ball  d) From above, reduced, on flat paper
**Answer: d) From above, reduced, on flat paper**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can your classroom not be drawn on paper unshrunk?
a) Paper is white  b) Classrooms have no shape  c) The page is smaller than the room  d) Pencils are too short
**Answer: c) The page is smaller than the room**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A map can only show —
a) Everything at once  b) Selected information  c) Photographs  d) Sounds
**Answer: b) Selected information**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the map's seven services?
a) Showing distances  b) Cooking food  c) Telling tomorrow's rain exactly  d) Carrying goods
**Answer: a) Showing distances**
