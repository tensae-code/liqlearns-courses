# Video script — Maps and globes — what a map is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pace your classroom: count your steps along its length and its width. Now try to draw the room on one page of your exercise book at its true size. You cannot — the page is smaller than the room. That impossibility is where maps begin.

This lesson teaches: what a map is — the Earth's surface seen from above, shrunk systematically onto flat paper, showing selected information — and the seven things a map does for us. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
