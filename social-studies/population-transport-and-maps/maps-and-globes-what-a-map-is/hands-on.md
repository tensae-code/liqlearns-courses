# Hands-on — Maps and globes — what a map is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Measure your classroom by pacing and note its length and width in steps.

## Activity 2
Draw a sketch map of your home-to-school walk.

## Activity 3
Look at any real map and name two things it chose to show and one thing it left out.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the definition of a map word by word, and three of its seven services.
