# Malthus — population against food — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State Malthus's argument in one sentence.
**Answer:** Population's constant tendency to outgrow its food supply makes crisis and misery inevitable unless checked.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish preventive from positive checks.
**Answer:** Preventive checks limit births beforehand (above all moral restraint); positive checks raise mortality afterwards (famine, disease, war and every cause cutting life short).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who was Malthus?
**Answer:** Thomas Malthus (1776–1834), clergyman, demographer and economist, author of the 1798 Essay on the Principle of Population.
