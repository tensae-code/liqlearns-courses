# Malthus — population against food — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Malthus published his Essay in…
a) 1798  b) 1898  c) 1965
**Answer: a) 1798**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** His central claim: population tends to…
a) shrink  b) outgrow its food supply  c) match food exactly
**Answer: b) outgrow its food supply**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Malthusian crisis is…
a) a banking failure  b) misery following when population outgrows food  c) a drought type
**Answer: b) misery following when population outgrows food**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Preventive checks act…
a) after crisis  b) before it — e.g. moral restraint  c) never
**Answer: b) before it — e.g. moral restraint**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Positive checks include…
a) famine, disease and war  b) education  c) contraception
**Answer: a) famine, disease and war**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Moral restraint is a…
a) positive check  b) preventive check  c) tax
**Answer: b) preventive check**
