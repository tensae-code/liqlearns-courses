# Malthus — population against food — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Malthus lived 1776–1834.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Malthus's view, population growth has no tendency to outrun food.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Famine is a positive check in Malthus's scheme.
**Answer: True**
