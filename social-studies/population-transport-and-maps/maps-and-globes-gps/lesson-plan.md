# Lesson plan — Maps and globes — GPS

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Maps and globes — GPS
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Global Positioning System — satellites, receivers and software that show exactly where anything on Earth is — and what it does for flight and ships
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the whole maps-and-globes run before it in this title — the grid, the scale and the margins now carried by satellites.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons (a short, single-skill lesson; his ruling 2026-11-09).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Maps and globes — reading a map's margins. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what GPS is and its three parts.
2. State what GPS shows: exactly where anything on Earth is.
3. List GPS's services to flight.
4. Infer its service to ships.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A globe or ball helps where the lesson names one. Nothing else.
