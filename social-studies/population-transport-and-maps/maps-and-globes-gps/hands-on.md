# Hands-on — Maps and globes — GPS

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
If a phone is available, open its map and watch your position given as a point — the receiver at work.

## Activity 2
List GPS's four services to flight from memory.

## Activity 3
Write the inference for ships in two sentences of your own.

## With a partner
Tell your companion GPS's three parts, what it shows, and two of its services to flight.
