# Maps and globes — GPS — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** GPS stands for —
a) General Plane Service  b) Ground Position Satellites  c) Global Paper System  d) Global Positioning System
**Answer: d) Global Positioning System**

**2.** GPS is a system of —
a) Maps, globes and atlases  b) Pilots, ships and roads  c) Satellites, receivers and computer software  d) Stars, moons and planets
**Answer: c) Satellites, receivers and computer software**

**3.** GPS shows —
a) Only the weather  b) Exactly where anything on Earth is  c) Only town names  d) Yesterday's positions
**Answer: b) Exactly where anything on Earth is**

**4.** Which is a GPS service to flight?
a) Landing in fog  b) Cheaper tickets for students  c) Bigger aeroplanes  d) More airports
**Answer: a) Landing in fog**
