# Maps and globes — GPS — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** List two GPS services to flight.
**Answer:** Continuous exact position/weather information; flight safety; fewer delays and less fuel via direct routes; landing in fog (any two).

**2.** Infer GPS's service to ships, in your own words.
**Answer:** Exact position at sea, direct safe routes, safe arrival in fog — the same services as flight, over water.
