# Video script — Maps and globes — GPS

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The ship in the ocean from an earlier lesson phones again: 'Come and get us!' This time their phone knows where it is — to within a few steps — though no human looked at a chart. How? Three things working together, two of them in space. Read on.

This lesson teaches: the Global Positioning System — satellites, receivers and software that show exactly where anything on Earth is — and what it does for flight and ships. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
