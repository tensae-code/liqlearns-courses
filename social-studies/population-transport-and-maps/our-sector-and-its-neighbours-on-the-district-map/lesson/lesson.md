## Sub lesson 1 — Our Sector and Its Neighbours on the District Map

Rwanda's book, the *Rwanda Grade 3 Amasomo Mbonezamubano SB* (printed pp. 31–33), teaches: Imirenge ihana imbibi n'Umurenge wacu: the sectors that border ours, read from the district map of Gasabo (Kigali) (Unit 2). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rwanda's way — the book's own teaching way

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 3 Amasomo Mbonezamubano SB): P3 moves the home ground up one rung of Rwanda's administrative ladder, from the cell to the SECTOR (Umurenge) — and it is here, at age 8–9, that formal map work begins: the sector map of Gikomero, the district map of Gasabo, symbols, scale, and the sectors that share a border with ours. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
