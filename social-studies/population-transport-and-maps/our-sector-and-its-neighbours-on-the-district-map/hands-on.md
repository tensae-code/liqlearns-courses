# Hands-on — Our Sector and Its Neighbours on the District Map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Imirenge ihana imbibi n'Umurenge wacu: the sectors that border ours, read from the district map of Gasabo (Kigali) (Unit 2). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Rwanda's way
Say how Rwanda's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
