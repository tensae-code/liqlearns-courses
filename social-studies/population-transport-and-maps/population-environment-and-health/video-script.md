# Video script — Population, environment and health

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Environmental health defends people two ways — treating disease after it appears (clinical) or controlling its causes before illness (public health) — and the burden of a damaged environment falls hardest on the poor, the young, the elderly and women. First, the burden, counted: Environmental health covers the aspects of human health and disease determined by the environment. Then, two models of defence: Two models defend health (the book's Figure 4.4). Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
