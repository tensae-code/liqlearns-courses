# Population, environment and health — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Environmental health covers health and disease as determined by…
a) the environment  b) income only  c) age only
**Answer: a) the environment**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Unsafe water and poor sanitation drive what share of diarrhoeal disease in low/middle-income countries?
a) 5.8%  b) 58%  c) 98%
**Answer: b) 58%**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** SARS and Marburg are examples of…
a) zoonoses linking ecosystems to pandemic risk  b) water treatments  c) vaccines
**Answer: a) zoonoses linking ecosystems to pandemic risk**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The clinical model…
a) prevents hazards  b) treats disease after it appears  c) builds drains
**Answer: b) treats disease after it appears**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The public health model…
a) controls agents and hazards before illness  b) waits for disease  c) treats one patient at a time
**Answer: a) controls agents and hazards before illness**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Environmental harms fall hardest on…
a) the rich  b) the poor, young, elderly and women  c) nobody in particular
**Answer: b) the poor, young, elderly and women**
