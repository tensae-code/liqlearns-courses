# Population, environment and health — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clean environments cut mortality and raise productivity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The clinical and public health models are the same thing.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Zoonoses connect ecosystem damage to human pandemic risk.
**Answer: True**
