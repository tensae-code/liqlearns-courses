# Population, environment and health — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distinguish the two models of defence.
**Answer:** Clinical: treat disease after it appears. Public health: control disease-causing agents and environmental hazards before illness.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What share of diarrhoeal disease do unsafe water and sanitation drive, and where?
**Answer:** 58%, in low- and middle-income countries.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which groups bear environmental harms hardest?
**Answer:** The poor, the young, the elderly and women.
