# Hands-on — How Ethiopia’s population grew

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Curve drawing
Draw the growth curve from the lesson: a gentle start in 1900, steepening ever upward. Mark where doubling time is long and where it is short.

## Doubling game
Start with 10 stones. Double the pile, then double again, then again. Count how fast small doublings become big piles — that is doubling time at work.
