# Video script — How Ethiopia’s population grew

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In 1900: about 11.8 million people.
Then the curve steepens, decade by decade.
Growth speeds up. Doubling takes fewer and fewer years.
That is how Ethiopia became a country of well over a hundred million.
