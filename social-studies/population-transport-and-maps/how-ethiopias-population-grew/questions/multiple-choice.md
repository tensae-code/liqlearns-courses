# How Ethiopia’s population grew — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia’s population in 1900 was about —
a) 50 million  b) 2 million  c) 11.8 million
**Answer: c) 11.8 million**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Through the twentieth century, growth —
a) reversed  b) accelerated  c) stopped
**Answer: b) accelerated**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Doubling time is —
a) the years a population needs to double  b) the time between censuses  c) the average family size
**Answer: a) the years a population needs to double**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** As growth accelerated, doubling time —
a) lengthened  b) stayed fixed  c) shortened
**Answer: c) shortened**
