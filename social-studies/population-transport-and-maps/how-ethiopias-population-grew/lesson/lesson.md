# How Ethiopia’s population grew

## Do it first

In 1900 Ethiopia held fewer people than one of today’s regions. Watch the numbers climb — and speed up. Read the growth story.

## Sub lesson 1 — From 11.8 million

A table traces Ethiopia’s growth from about 11.8 million people in 1900. Through the twentieth century the growth rate accelerated: each generation added more people than the one before, and the population climbed decade by decade toward the large population of today.

## Sub lesson 2 — Doubling time shrinks

As growth accelerated, the doubling time — the years the population needs to double — shortened. A shortening doubling time is the signature of rapid growth: the same percentage increase acts on a bigger and bigger base, so the absolute numbers added each year keep rising.
