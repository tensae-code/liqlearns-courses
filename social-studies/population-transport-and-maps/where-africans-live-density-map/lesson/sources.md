Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 8 Geografija 8): Grade 8 is pure world regional geography, continent by continent (Africa, Australia/Oceania, the Americas and polar regions), each region worked through the same grid — position/relief/waters, climate/vegetation, population, economy — and the book closes with two world-problems units (rainforest loss, desert spread, water conflicts, the Aral Sea).

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 8 G8 Geography of the Continents): Grade 8 tours the continents one by one — natural features, population, economic activities — with one country studied in depth per continent (Indonesia, Eritrea, Germany, the USA, Brazil), and closes on contemporary world issues.

Moldova's own way (Moldova Grade 6 G6 Geography): a "Geography of the Continents" year — the southern continents (Africa, South America, Australia, Antarctica) each worked through with the identical template (physical position, relief and minerals, climate factors and zones, surface waters, natural zones, population, peoples and political map), and every continent closes with a "geographic risk processes" lesson; an annex gives a standard plan for characterising any continent.

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 7 laos-g07-geography-teacher): a continents year — general physical geography first, then continent by continent (Africa, Europe, the Americas, Oceania), each treated the same way: natural conditions, population, economy, then country case studies (Egypt, South Africa, Russia, France, USA, Brazil, Australia); closes with an additional-knowledge appendix (printed p. 69), not placed separately.

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 7 laos-g07-geography-student): the student twin of Book 3 — same 5 parts / 22 lessons (continents year) with full illustrated text and per-continent maps.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 8 Geography student): a pure regional-geography year — the world is divided into regions following the UN scheme, each region and chosen state is studied in the same fixed order (position, natural conditions and resources, population, economy), and Armenia's neighbours (Georgia, Azerbaijan, Turkey, Iran) get full country lessons of their own.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 12 S4 Geography SB): A global book (globalization, oceans, world energy, the development gap) that keeps returning to South Sudan — ARCISS signing photo in Juba for political globalization, South Sudan classroom progress-check scenarios, and a closing exercise asking learners, as citizens of South Sudan, how their nation can grow economically.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 3 P3 Social Studies SB): The land unit is anchored on the child's own payam — Mount Kinyeti, the River Nile and the Sudd Swamp are the named examples — and features are taught as double-sided: benefits to us, and problems they can cause, like rivers that fill and flood.
