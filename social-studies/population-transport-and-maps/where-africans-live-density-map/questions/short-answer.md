# Where Africans live — density map — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell distribution and density apart in one sentence each.
**Answer:** Distribution is how population is spread over an area — the pattern. Density is how many people live in each unit of area — the number.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do the dense pockets sit where they do?
**Answer:** Because they have water and workable land — rivers, coasts and well-watered highlands — while dry lands cannot support many people.
