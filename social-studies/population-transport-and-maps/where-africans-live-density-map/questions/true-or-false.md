# Where Africans live — density map — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's population is spread smoothly and evenly.
**Answer: False** — it is strikingly uneven — clusters and near-empty lands.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Density is the pattern and distribution is the number.
**Answer: False** — it is the other way: distribution is the pattern, density the number.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sahara is among Africa's most densely settled lands.
**Answer: False** — it is near-empty except at points like oases.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Highlands can be dense pockets where they are well-watered.
**Answer: True** — riverine, coastal and highland pockets are the clusters.
