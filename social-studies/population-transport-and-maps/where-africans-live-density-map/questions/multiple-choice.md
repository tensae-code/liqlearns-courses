# Where Africans live — density map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Population density measures —
a) the pattern of spread  b) how many people live in each unit of area  c) the birth rate  d) city sizes
**Answer: b) how many people live in each unit of area** — the crowding number of a place.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Distribution describes —
a) a number only  b) how population is spread — the pattern  c) the death rate  d) migration
**Answer: b) how population is spread — the pattern** — the pattern you see on the map.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dense pockets of Africa gather in all of these EXCEPT —
a) riverine lands  b) coastal strips  c) well-watered highlands  d) the hyper-arid desert interior
**Answer: d) the hyper-arid desert interior** — water scarcity keeps deserts near-empty.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The density map is, in disguise, a map of —
a) languages  b) water and workable land  c) railways  d) minerals
**Answer: b) water and workable land** — clusters follow water; emptiness follows dryness.
