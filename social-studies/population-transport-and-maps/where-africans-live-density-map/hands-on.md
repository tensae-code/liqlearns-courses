# Hands-on — Where Africans live — density map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shade the map
On a blank Africa outline, shade density: darkest along the Nile and the Guinea coast and the eastern highlands, palest over the Sahara and Kalahari. Add a three-step key: dense / medium / near-empty.

## Density of your street
Count the homes on your street or in your village block and estimate the people. Compare with a emptier place you know. Write which is denser and the water-or-land reason you think explains it.
