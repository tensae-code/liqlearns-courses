# Video script — Where Africans live — density map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Africa's people are not spread evenly.
They cluster — along rivers, on coasts, in highlands.
The deserts stay almost empty.
Distribution is the pattern. Density is the number.
