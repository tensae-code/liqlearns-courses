# Video script — The world's population explosion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

World population entered the twentieth century at 1.6 billion and left it at 6.1 billion.

The adding sped up: a billion was added in 1960–75 — fifteen years — and another billion in 1975–87, only twelve years.

Population geographers track the explosion with a small toolset: birth and death rates, fertility, mortality, migration — and doubling time, the years a population needs to become twice as large.
