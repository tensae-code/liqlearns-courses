Palestine's own way (Grade 9, PS G9P2): the course closes at world scale — the diversity of natural environments, peoples and economies, then the age's challenges (population explosion, famines, poverty) framed as matters for international cooperation.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 7 Social Studies): from Grade 7 the combined Social Studies book splits into four named subject sections; its Geography section teaches people-in-environments — hot zones, deserts and oases, polar peoples, climate zones — through world case studies (Kenya farmers, Amazon, Inuit, Sahara oil) before turning to world population.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 8 Geografija 8): Grade 8 is pure world regional geography, continent by continent (Africa, Australia/Oceania, the Americas and polar regions), each region worked through the same grid — position/relief/waters, climate/vegetation, population, economy — and the book closes with two world-problems units (rainforest loss, desert spread, water conflicts, the Aral Sea).

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 8 Geography G8): Grade 8 leaves the Arab world and tours the "new" continents in a fixed six-lesson pattern (location and surface, natural environments, settlement/migration, population and cities, resources and economy, one big country study — USA, Brazil, Australia), gives Antarctica a whole unit as a land of ice and science, and closes with a unit of contemporary global challenges (water, food, pollution, warming, population, desertification).

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 12 geo-6as-cd): A human-geography year in three units — world population (growth, distribution, migration, structure, with an applications lesson on the age pyramid and demographic projections), urbanisation and its problems, and economic activities from traditional farming to modern industry, transport, trade, craft industry and tourism.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 6 Student Book): widening-circles design — the whole world — continents and oceans, world population, migration and climate change, with Honduras located inside the world. Every lesson runs the same 'Sembrar y cosechar' activity cycle.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 6 Teacher Guide) (teacher guide — evidence): widening-circles design — the whole world — continents and oceans, world population, migration and climate change, with Honduras located inside the world — the teacher guide mirrors the student book lesson-for-lesson (same 38 lessons and blocks) and adds didactics, theory summaries and answer keys; its geography lessons are placed here at the guide's own printed pages.
