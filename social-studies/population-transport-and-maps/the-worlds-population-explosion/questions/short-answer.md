# The world's population explosion — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the century's start and end figures.
**Answer:** 1.6 billion in 1900; 6.1 billion by 2000.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did less-developed countries' growth accelerate after the war?
**Answer:** Death rates fell while births stayed high.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the working measures the book lists.
**Answer:** Birth and death rates, fertility, mortality, migration and doubling time.
