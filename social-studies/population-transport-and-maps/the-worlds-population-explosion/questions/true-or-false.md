# The world's population explosion — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The world left the twentieth century with about 6.1 billion people.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each added billion took longer than the one before.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Migration is one of the working measures of population change.
**Answer: True**
