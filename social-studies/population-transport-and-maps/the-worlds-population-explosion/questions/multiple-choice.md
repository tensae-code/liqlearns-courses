# The world's population explosion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** World population in 1900, as the book gives it, was —
a) 1.6 billion  b) 6.1 billion  c) 1 billion
**Answer: a) 1.6 billion**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The billion added in 1975–87 took —
a) fifteen years  b) fifty years  c) twelve years
**Answer: c) twelve years**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Doubling time is —
a) the average age of a population  b) the years a population needs to become twice as large  c) the time between two censuses
**Answer: b) the years a population needs to become twice as large**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Growth accelerated after the Second World War fastest in —
a) less-developed countries  b) Europe  c) North America
**Answer: a) less-developed countries**
