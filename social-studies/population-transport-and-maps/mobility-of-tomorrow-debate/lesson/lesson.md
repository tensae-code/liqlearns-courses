# Mobility of Tomorrow — a Debate

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what mobility for tomorrow? and ecological planning of transport — argued in debate, as France's Grade 10 book teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — What mobility for tomorrow?

Theme 3 closes by asking Quelles mobilités pour demain? — what mobility for tomorrow? — and teaches it as a debate.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecological planning of transport — argued in debate

The ground of the debate is the planification écologique des transports — planning transport ecologically.

France's own way: every theme is built the same way — a world chapter taught through one deep case study (Los Angeles megafires, the Amazon, Japan, the Mediterranean), then the same theme re-taught on France itself, closing with map-sketch (croquis) and mind-map assessment; debate and role-play (nuclear debate, G7 simulation) are standard geography methods. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
