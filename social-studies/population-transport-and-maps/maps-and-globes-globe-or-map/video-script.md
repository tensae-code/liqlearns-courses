# Video script — Maps and globes — globe or map?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two tools on the table: a ball and a sheet of paper. A sailor planning an ocean crossing, a pupil measuring a distance, a teacher showing all the continents at once — who should reach for which? Decide before the lesson rules on it.

This lesson teaches: choosing between the two tools — the 3-dimensional globe against the 2-dimensional map, what each does better. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
