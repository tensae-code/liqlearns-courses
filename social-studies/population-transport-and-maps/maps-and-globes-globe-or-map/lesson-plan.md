# Lesson plan — Maps and globes — globe or map?

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Maps and globes — globe or map?
**Place in the curriculum:** Title — Population, Transport and Maps (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** choosing between the two tools — the 3-dimensional globe against the 2-dimensional map, what each does better
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the globe and map lessons before it in this title.

## Sub lessons

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Maps and globes — what a map is. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State the globe's two limits: half visible, hard measuring.
2. State the map's three strengths: all at once, easy measuring, any part alone.
3. Choose the right tool for a given job and say why.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. A globe or ball helps where the lesson names one. Nothing else.
