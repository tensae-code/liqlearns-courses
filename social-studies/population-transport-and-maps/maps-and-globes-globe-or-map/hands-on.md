# Hands-on — Maps and globes — globe or map?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Try to measure a distance on a globe with a ruler, then with string. Note the difference.

## Activity 2
Show a partner all seven continents: first on a globe (turning), then on a map. Which was quicker?

## Activity 3
Choose the tool for: an ocean flight route; your kebele in detail; all oceans at once. Defend each.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the globe's two limits and the map's three strengths, then say which tool you would use to measure the distance between two towns, and why.
