# Maps and globes — globe or map? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The globe is —
a) 2-dimensional  b) Flat paper  c) A photograph  d) 3-dimensional
**Answer: d) 3-dimensional**

**2.** A globe's limits are —
a) Too light; too small  b) No oceans shown; no land shown  c) Only half visible at once; distances hard to measure  d) It tears; it fades
**Answer: c) Only half visible at once; distances hard to measure**

**3.** A map can show —
a) Only half the Earth  b) All continents and oceans at once  c) Nothing measurable  d) The Earth as a ball
**Answer: b) All continents and oceans at once**

**4.** A map of one chosen part alone —
a) Can be made; a globe must be made whole  b) Cannot be made  c) Needs a ball  d) Shows the whole Earth
**Answer: a) Can be made; a globe must be made whole**
