# Hands-on — Ways Of Showing Things On Maps

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 2: Methods of representing geographical objects on maps — symbols (Vietnam's power plants), movement arrows (wind and storm activity in Vietnam), chart maps (rice area and output by province), dot maps (Asia's population), the area method (rubber-growing areas), plus isolines, choropleth and locality diagrams. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
