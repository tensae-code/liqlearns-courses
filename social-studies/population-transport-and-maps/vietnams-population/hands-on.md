# Hands-on — Vietnams Population

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 7: Vietnam's population — 101.3 million in 2024 (third in Southeast Asia, 16th in the world; forecast to peak near 115.2 million around 2059), the 1979–2024 growth chart, the structures (an ageing age table 1999–2024, 54 ethnic groups with the Kinh about 85%, literacy 96.6%, a birth sex ratio of 111.4 boys per 100 girls), the uneven distribution (306/km² on average; 1,034/km² in the Red River Delta against 136 in the northern mountains; 61.5% still rural), its strengths and limits, and the Population Strategy to 2030. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
