Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 34–38), teaches: Bài 7: Vietnam's population — 101.3 million in 2024 (third in Southeast Asia, 16th in the world; forecast to peak near 115.2 million around 2059), the 1979–2024 growth chart, the structures (an ageing age table 1999–2024, 54 ethnic groups with the Kinh about 85%, literacy 96.6%, a birth sex ratio of 111.4 boys per 100 girls), the uneven distribution (306/km² on average; 1,034/km² in the Red River Delta against 136 in the northern mountains; 61.5% still rural), its strengths and limits, and the Population Strategy to 2030.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
