## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 1 Lesson 1, Defining the Arab Homeland: the Arab homeland is the group of states joined by shared traits — language, religion, customs and traditions, a shared history and unity of destiny — stretching from the Arab Gulf in the east to the Atlantic Ocean in the west across two continents, Asia and Africa; it has 22 states, 12 in the Asian wing and 10 in the African wing, and an area of about 13.5 million km², some 10% of the world's land — more than Europe, China or the United States.
