# Road safety — causes and prevention

## Do it first

The dominant transport is also the dangerous one. Before the countermeasures, the causes — in four groups. Read them, and check your own road habits against each.

## Sub lesson 1 — The scale and the four causes

Road traffic accidents kill and injure at scale: the WHO 2020 figures as printed give the toll, and Addis Ababa alone accounts for a large share of fatalities. Causes group into four factors: human (speeding, carelessness, drink), vehicle (faults and poor maintenance), road (design, surface, signs) and environmental (weather, darkness, animals).

## Sub lesson 2 — Countermeasures

Each cause group has its countermeasures: training, licensing and enforcement for humans; inspection for vehicles; design, maintenance and signs for roads; and care, lighting and timing for environmental risks. Road safety is the four groups answered one by one.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the four cause groups, and match a countermeasure to each.
