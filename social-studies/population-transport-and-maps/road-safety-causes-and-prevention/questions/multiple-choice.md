# Road safety — causes and prevention — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A human cause of accidents is —
a) a broken axle  b) a pothole  c) speeding
**Answer: c) speeding**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Addis Ababa accounts for —
a) only minor injuries  b) a large share of fatalities  c) no accidents
**Answer: b) a large share of fatalities**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A countermeasure for vehicles is —
a) inspection  b) more speed  c) fewer signs
**Answer: a) inspection**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Road-group countermeasures include —
a) driver training  b) vehicle inspection  c) design, maintenance and signs
**Answer: c) design, maintenance and signs**
