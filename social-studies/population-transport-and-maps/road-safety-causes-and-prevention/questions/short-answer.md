# Road safety — causes and prevention — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four cause groups.
**Answer: Human, vehicle, road and environmental factors.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one countermeasure per group.
**Answer: Human: training/licensing/enforcement; vehicle: inspection; road: design/maintenance/signs; environment: care, lighting, timing.**
