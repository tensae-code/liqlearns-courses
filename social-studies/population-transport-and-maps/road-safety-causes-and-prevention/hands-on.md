# Hands-on — Road safety — causes and prevention

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Junction audit
Audit one real junction or crossing near you against the four groups: list its human, vehicle, road and environmental dangers, then its missing countermeasures.

## Cause court
Stage a court for one invented accident: four witnesses argue the four cause groups. The class judges which groups were truly at fault and what would have prevented it.
