# Hands-on — Transport And Trade In Iraq

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 5 applied study: Transport, communications and trade in Iraq — cars per head (one car per 130 people in 1965), the Baghdad–Sharqat and Baghdad–Basra railways, 4,708 km of navigable rivers (Tigris branches, Shatt al-Gharaf, Shatt al-Basra), six airports (Baghdad, Mosul, Basra, Erbil, Sulaymaniyah, Najaf), and oil as the biggest part of Iraq's foreign trade. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
