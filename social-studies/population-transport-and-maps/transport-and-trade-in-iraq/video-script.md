Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography* (printed p. 130), teaches: Ch. 5 applied study: Transport, communications and trade in Iraq — cars per head (one car per 130 people in 1965), the Baghdad–Sharqat and Baghdad–Basra railways, 4,708 km of navigable rivers (Tigris branches, Shatt al-Gharaf, Shatt al-Basra), six airports (Baghdad, Mosul, Basra, Erbil, Sulaymaniyah, Najaf), and oil as the biggest part of Iraq's foreign trade.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography): a human-geography course in six chapters — population, cities and countryside, agriculture, industry and mining, transport and trade, political geography and tourism — and every chapter closes with an applied study of Iraq itself, so the world pattern is always brought home.
