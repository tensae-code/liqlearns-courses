# Temporary homes, and what makes a town

## Do it first

A camp that moves with the herds, and a town that never moves at all. What separates settlement from town? Read the definitions.

## Sub lesson 1 — Temporary settlements

Pastoralist settlements are temporary: camps that move as herds move, built lightly for a season’s grazing and water. They contrast with permanent settlements, whose buildings, fields and institutions assume the people stay.

## Sub lesson 2 — What makes a town

An urban settlement is defined by size and function and by non-agricultural work: its people mostly do not farm. Ethiopia’s urbanisation is still low — most Ethiopians are rural — but the urban share is growing as towns pull in migrants.
