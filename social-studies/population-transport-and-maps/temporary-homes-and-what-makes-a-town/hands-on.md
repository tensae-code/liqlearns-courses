# Hands-on — Temporary homes, and what makes a town

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Camp versus village
List everything a pastoral camp must do without (heavy buildings, fixed fields) and everything it gains (mobility). Then do the same, reversed, for a permanent village.

## Town test
Apply the three urban marks — size, function, non-agricultural work — to the largest settlement near you. Verdict: town or large village? Defend it.
