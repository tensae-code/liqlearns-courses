# Temporary homes, and what makes a town — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pastoralist settlements are —
a) permanent stone towns  b) urban  c) temporary, moving with the herds
**Answer: c) temporary, moving with the herds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Camps are built —
a) underground  b) lightly, for a season’s grazing and water  c) of concrete
**Answer: b) lightly, for a season’s grazing and water**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A town is defined by —
a) size, function and non-agricultural work  b) a river only  c) its age
**Answer: a) size, function and non-agricultural work**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia’s urbanisation is —
a) complete  b) falling  c) still low but growing
**Answer: c) still low but growing**
