# How many depend on each worker — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The dependency ratio counts —
a) workers against employers  b) towns against villages  c) dependents against working-age people
**Answer: c) dependents against working-age people**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia’s 2020 ratio was about —
a) 120 per 100  b) 76.8 per 100  c) 30 per 100
**Answer: b) 76.8 per 100**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The economically inactive are —
a) those not earning — dependents  b) all adults  c) only the retired
**Answer: a) those not earning — dependents**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A high ratio thins —
a) rainfall  b) land  c) savings
**Answer: c) savings**
