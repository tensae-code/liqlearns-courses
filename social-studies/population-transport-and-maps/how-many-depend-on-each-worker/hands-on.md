# Hands-on — How many depend on each worker

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Household ratio
Compute the dependency ratio of your own household: dependents over earners, times 100. Compare it with Ethiopia’s 76.8 and explain the difference.

## Burden tracing
Trace one wage through the three burdens: mouths fed, services demanded, savings left. Present the tracing as three arrows from one pay packet.
