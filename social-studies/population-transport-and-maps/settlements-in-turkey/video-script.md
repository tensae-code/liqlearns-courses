Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya builds its settlements unit step by step — settlement and how settlements develop, rural settlement, urban settlement — and closes it at home: II-C Ülkemizde Yerleşmeler, settlements in Turkey itself.

The Turkey case is where the unit's own tools are put to work: the settlement patterns and types taught earlier in the unit — rural settlement and urban settlement — are the very tools the book uses to read Turkey's settlements. That is Turkey's own way in this book — a Turkey case closing the unit.
