## Sub lesson 1 — Regions What They Are And How Geographers Draw Them

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 2), teaches: Lesson 1 The meaning and concept of a region (ناحیه): every place has its own features held together in coherence; geographers delimit regions by criteria — natural (the map of Canada's soils making soil regions that shape vegetation and farming) and human (population, ethnic groups, religion, language, culture, kind of economic activity; Switzerland's four official languages; Europe's industrial regions on the map). ## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).
