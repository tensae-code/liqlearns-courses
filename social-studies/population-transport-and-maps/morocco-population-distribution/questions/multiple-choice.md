# Morocco's Population Distribution — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's Geography Lesson 8 teaches population —
a) distribution and structure  b) in another country  c) only in cities abroad
**Answer: a) distribution and structure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Distribution is read from —
a) the density map of Morocco  b) a weather bulletin  c) a port list
**Answer: a) the density map of Morocco**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson's chart shows —
a) population growth  b) river levels  c) train times
**Answer: a) population growth**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A density map shows where people are —
a) many and where they are few  b) all exactly equal  c) only overseas
**Answer: a) many and where they are few**
