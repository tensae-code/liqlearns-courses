# Hands-on — Morocco's Population Distribution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a density map
Look at a density map of Morocco (or draw a simple shaded one): point to where people are most crowded and where they are fewest, reading only the shading, as the lesson teaches.

## Chart the growth
Draw a simple rising chart of population growth for a place you know, the way the guide's lesson charts Morocco's growth.
