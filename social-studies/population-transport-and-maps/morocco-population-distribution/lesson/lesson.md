## Sub lesson 1 — Reading the density map of Morocco

The eighth Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Population in my homeland — distribution and structure. Its map half teaches the learner to read the density map of Morocco: where the homeland's people are many and where they are few, read straight from the map's shading.

## Sub lesson 2 — Drawing a chart of population growth

The lesson's second half turns numbers into a picture: the learner draws a chart of population growth. Distribution on the map, growth on the chart — that is how Morocco's guide teaches its own population.
