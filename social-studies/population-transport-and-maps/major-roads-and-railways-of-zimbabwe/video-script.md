# Video script — Major Roads and Railways of Zimbabwe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe's Grade 4 teacher guide, the *PlusOne Social Science Grade 4 Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 64), teaches: Unit 20: Major roads and railways in Zimbabwe.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 4): Grade 4 heritage turns practical — Indigenous Knowledge Systems (aloe, mubvamaropa, moringa) and conservation of natural resources (gully filling, afforestation); transport names Zimbabwe's major roads and railways and their economic importance; shelter compares indigenous types (tsapi granary) with shelters outside Zimbabwe (igloo, pyramid) and the materials and climate factors behind them.
