# Hands-on — Roads And Transport In Iran 1

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 8 Roads and transport (1) (راه‌ها و حمل و نقل ۱): have you thought what role roads play in our lives — which of people's needs do they meet?; activity: ask what people used for transport before the car was invented, and how those means differ from today's; in the big cities one of the means that carries people from place to place is the urban train (مترو), passing mostly under the ground and very fast; today people everywhere need agricultural products and industrial goods and each other; activity: look at a good's packaging, see where its factory is, and mark on the map the route the good travelled to reach your city or village; the map of the country's main land roads (نقشه‌ی راه‌های اصلی زمینی کشور); and road safety for drivers and passengers. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
