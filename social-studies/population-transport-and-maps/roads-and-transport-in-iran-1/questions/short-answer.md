# Roads And Transport In Iran 1 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Roads And Transport In Iran 1 — Lesson 8 Roads and transport (1) (راه‌ها و حمل و نقل ۱): have you thought what role roads play in our lives — which of people's needs do they meet?; activity: ask what people used for transport before the car was invented, and how those means differ from today's; in the big cities one of the means that carries people from place to place is the urban train (مترو), passing mostly under the ground and very fast; today people everywhere need agricultural products and industrial goods and each other; activity: look at a good's packaging, see where its factory is, and mark on the map the route the good travelled to reach your city or village; the map of the country's main land roads (نقشه‌ی راه‌های اصلی زمینی کشور); and road safety for drivers and passengers (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
