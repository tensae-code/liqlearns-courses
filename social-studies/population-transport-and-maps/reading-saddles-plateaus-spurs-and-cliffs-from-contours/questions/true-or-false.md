# Reading saddles, plateaus, spurs and cliffs from contours — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A plateau shows broad, roughly rectangular closed contours.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A fill is drawn like a cut in reverse.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A gorge's contours spread wide apart.
**Answer: False**
