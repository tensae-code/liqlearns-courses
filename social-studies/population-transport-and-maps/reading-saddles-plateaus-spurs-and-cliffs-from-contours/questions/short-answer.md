# Reading saddles, plateaus, spurs and cliffs from contours — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is a depression marked?
**Answer:** Closed contours with inward ticks, values falling to the centre.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish spur from valley in one line.
**Answer:** Spur Vs point away from high ground; valley Vs point upstream toward it.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a carrying contour?
**Answer:** The single merged contour line of a cliff.
