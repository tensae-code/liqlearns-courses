# Reading saddles, plateaus, spurs and cliffs from contours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A saddle is —
a) the dip between two high areas  b) a closed depression  c) a cliff
**Answer: a) the dip between two high areas**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A depression's ticks point —
a) inward, values falling to the centre  b) outward  c) uphill
**Answer: a) inward, values falling to the centre**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A spur's contours point —
a) away from high ground  b) upstream  c) inward
**Answer: a) away from high ground**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A cliff shows contours —
a) merging into one carrying contour  b) branching  c) vanishing
**Answer: a) merging into one carrying contour**
