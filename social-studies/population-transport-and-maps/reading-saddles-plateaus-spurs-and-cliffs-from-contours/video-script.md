# Video script — Reading saddles, plateaus, spurs and cliffs from contours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A saddle is the dip between two high areas — the low pass on a ridge.

A plateau is broad, roughly rectangular closed contours — a table top in lines.

A cliff is contours merging into one carrying contour — the lines themselves fall together.
