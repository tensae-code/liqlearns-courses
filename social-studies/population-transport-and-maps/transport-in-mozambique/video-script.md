# Video script — Transport In Mozambique

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mozambique's book, the *Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 2* (printed p. 149), teaches: Lição 32: Transportes e comunicações em Moçambique (airports, the Zambezi/Rovuma/Maputo-Katembe bridges).

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 2): human/economic geography organised sector by sector — population first, then each economic activity taught world-then-Mozambique (agriculture, livestock, fishing, forestry, industry, commerce, tourism, transport), with Mozambique's own distribution tables, ports and exports as the local evidence.
