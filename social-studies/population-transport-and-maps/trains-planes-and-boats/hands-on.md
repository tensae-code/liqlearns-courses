# Hands-on — Trains, planes and boats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mode map
Draw Ethiopia’s modes map: roads as spokes, the railway in bold to Djibouti, air routes as arcs from Addis, and boat marks on the great lakes.

## Mode choice
Choose the mode for three loads: coffee for export, a sick person from a remote town, tourists to a Rift lake. Defend each choice from the lesson.
