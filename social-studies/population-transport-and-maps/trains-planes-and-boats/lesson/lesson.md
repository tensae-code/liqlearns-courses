# Trains, planes and boats

## Do it first

Roads rule, but three other modes matter: a railway to the sea, an airline that leads a continent, and boats on the lakes. Read them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rail and air

The Ethio-Djibouti railway — the Addis–Djibouti line — carries trade to the port. Ethiopian Airlines is Africa’s leading carrier: as printed, it serves more than 62 African and more than 127 international destinations — a landlocked country owning one of the world’s great airlines.

## Sub lesson 2 — Water transport

Inland water transport is limited: boats work the lakes and a few rivers, but Ethiopia’s steep, seasonal rivers forbid the great river-navigation that flatter countries enjoy. The modes together explain the transport map: road first, rail to the sea, air to the world, water at the margins.
