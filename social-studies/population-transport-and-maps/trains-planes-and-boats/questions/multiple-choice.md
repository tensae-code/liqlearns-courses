# Trains, planes and boats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The railway runs —
a) Addis to Gondar  b) Djibouti to Mekelle  c) Addis Ababa to Djibouti
**Answer: c) Addis Ababa to Djibouti**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopian Airlines is —
a) a cargo-only company  b) Africa’s leading carrier  c) a small local airline
**Answer: b) Africa’s leading carrier**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Inland water transport is —
a) limited  b) the dominant mode  c) nonexistent everywhere
**Answer: a) limited**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** River navigation is limited because rivers are —
a) too wide  b) frozen  c) steep and seasonal
**Answer: c) steep and seasonal**
