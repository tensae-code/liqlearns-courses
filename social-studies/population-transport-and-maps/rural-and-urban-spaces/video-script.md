# Video script — Rural and Urban Spaces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Angola's Grade 5 student book, the *Geografia 5ª Classe* (printed p. 28), teaches: rural spaces and urban spaces — field work in the countryside against industry, commerce and services in the city, and the problems that fast city growth brings.

Angola's own way (Grade 5): Geography opens as "geographic space" — the natural space and the human-built space (Luanda port, Benguela railway, Cambambe dam, Huambo housing) — before any physical topic; Angola's own position, borders, population figures (INE) and farm/fishery tables carry the world topics.
