# Video script — Plans and Their Symbols

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before any map of a country, Grade 3 teaches the plano — a plan: a drawing of a small place (a classroom, a school, a neighbourhood) seen from above. A plan speaks in symbols: small agreed drawings stand for real things, and the lesson el plano y sus símbolos teaches the child to read those symbols, so any plan can be read even without words.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the older SM edition is a classic skills-first book — a whole opening unit on orientation, cardinal points, the compass rose and plans before any content; every unit runs the same skeleton (content lessons, “Ser Estudiante” test, Cultura del Buen Vivir, scientific work, digital skills, unit evaluation), and the country is learned parish → canton → province → nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
