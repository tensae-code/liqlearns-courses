**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Uzbekistan, Armenia, Cuba).**

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 6 Geografiya): Geography in Grade 6 is the physical geography of continents and oceans in the Russian/Soviet textbook tradition — first the Earth's geographic envelope and the World Ocean, then every continent in a fixed order (position, geological structure and relief, climate, inland waters, natural zones, population), closing with the population of the Earth and the political map of the world; nearly every chapter ends in a practical work.

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 9 Economic and Social Geography of the World): Grade 9 turns from physical to economic and social geography of the whole world: Part I works through the political map, world natural resources, world population, the scientific-technical revolution and the branches of the world economy; Part II then reviews every region in a fixed country-profile pattern (territory and position, natural resources, population, economy, transport, foreign ties), with Central Asia and Uzbekistan's neighbours given the fullest treatment, and closes with the global problems of humanity.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 7 Geography student): opens school geography with the geographic map itself (plan, scale, azimuth, projections) before any Earth science, and runs every theme to a summary lesson plus practical work; world economy is taught branch by branch with Armenia (ՀՀ) used as the worked example in the closing questions.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 8 Geography student): a pure regional-geography year — the world is divided into regions following the UN scheme, each region and chosen state is studied in the same fixed order (position, natural conditions and resources, population, economy), and Armenia's neighbours (Georgia, Azerbaijan, Turkey, Iran) get full country lessons of their own.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 10 Geography student): grade-10 Geography is a methods-first course — it opens with the science's history, branches and GIS/GPS/aerospace methods, works through the lithosphere with a full natural-disasters lesson, and closes with resources measured by endowment; Armenia's own rivers fill the hydrology tables and Armenia's place in modern geopolitics closes the political-map theme.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 6 Geografía de Cuba G6): The child's first geography book is the home country itself — one chapter of tools (coordinates, orientation, scale), then Cuba whole (situation, relief, coasts, climate, population, economy), then its two great regions landscape by landscape; the political-administrative division taught is the pre-2011 one (Ciudad de La Habana appears as a province), and History episodes woven into the regional chapters (the Santa Clara battle, the Granma landing) are named here but not placed — they belong to the History course.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 6 Cuaderno de actividades G6): The workbook is the practice twin of the G6 student book and the Atlas Escolar — nearly every activity is done ON a map (tracing coordinates, shading the insular shelf, locating features on outline maps) — and it is where the programme's fifth unit, "El paisaje de la localidad", actually lives: the student book has no locality chapter, but the workbook closes with sketching, describing and defending the child's own locality. Section 2 is pure tools: outline maps of the world and of Cuba plus graph paper.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 9 Geografía de Cuba G9 (2012)): The 2012 edition follows the G9 programme chapter for chapter (archipiélago → naturaleza → panorama económico → desarrollo social → situación medioambiental → regiones) and is explicit about its own dating: data before 31 Dec 2010 use the 1975 provincial division, while Chapter 6 teaches the new 16-province organisation of 1 Jan 2011. NOTE — this is NOT the same book as the Biblioteca copy filed next (Book 16): that copy is the wholly rewritten 2025 Tercer Perfeccionamiento edition, so the two are placed separately.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 9 Geografía de Cuba G9 (2025)): The 2025 rewrite keeps the home-country arc but rebuilds it for the Tercer Perfeccionamiento: Chapter 2 now profiles every province inside the political-administrative division itself, the climate chapter adds the Tarea Vida state plan by name, the economy chapter is organised by primary/secondary/tertiary sectors with self-employment (trabajo por cuenta propia) and the Mariel development zone, and the social chapter faces an ageing, shrinking population (life expectancy 73.7 years, a "Vivir 120" programme) and closes with Cuba's Human Development Index. It is a different book from the 2012 edition (Book 15), not a parallel copy, and is placed separately.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grades 10-11 Cuaderno de mapas): The senior-cycle companion is pure map work — a full outline-map atlas the student fills in: world and continents first, then the "significant regions" exactly as the G11 Regional course carves them (Caribe, América Latina, América Anglosajona, África Subsahariana, Europa Occidental, Medio Oriente, Este y sureste de Asia), then one outline map per studied country, and it lands back home with the Cuban archipelago, the provinces and a street-level La Habana. It closes with a "Datos interesantes" page of world records.
