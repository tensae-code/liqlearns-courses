**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Uzbekistan, Armenia).**

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 6 Geografiya): Geography in Grade 6 is the physical geography of continents and oceans in the Russian/Soviet textbook tradition — first the Earth's geographic envelope and the World Ocean, then every continent in a fixed order (position, geological structure and relief, climate, inland waters, natural zones, population), closing with the population of the Earth and the political map of the world; nearly every chapter ends in a practical work.

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 9 Economic and Social Geography of the World): Grade 9 turns from physical to economic and social geography of the whole world: Part I works through the political map, world natural resources, world population, the scientific-technical revolution and the branches of the world economy; Part II then reviews every region in a fixed country-profile pattern (territory and position, natural resources, population, economy, transport, foreign ties), with Central Asia and Uzbekistan's neighbours given the fullest treatment, and closes with the global problems of humanity.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 7 Geography student): opens school geography with the geographic map itself (plan, scale, azimuth, projections) before any Earth science, and runs every theme to a summary lesson plus practical work; world economy is taught branch by branch with Armenia (ՀՀ) used as the worked example in the closing questions.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 8 Geography student): a pure regional-geography year — the world is divided into regions following the UN scheme, each region and chosen state is studied in the same fixed order (position, natural conditions and resources, population, economy), and Armenia's neighbours (Georgia, Azerbaijan, Turkey, Iran) get full country lessons of their own.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 10 Geography student): grade-10 Geography is a methods-first course — it opens with the science's history, branches and GIS/GPS/aerospace methods, works through the lithosphere with a full natural-disasters lesson, and closes with resources measured by endowment; Armenia's own rivers fill the hydrology tables and Armenia's place in modern geopolitics closes the political-map theme.
