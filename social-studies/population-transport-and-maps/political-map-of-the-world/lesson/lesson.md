## Sub lesson 2 — Uzbekistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 6 Geografiya): Geography in Grade 6 is the physical geography of continents and oceans in the Russian/Soviet textbook tradition — first the Earth's geographic envelope and the World Ocean, then every continent in a fixed order (position, geological structure and relief, climate, inland waters, natural zones, population), closing with the population of the Earth and the political map of the world; nearly every chapter ends in a practical work.

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 9 Economic and Social Geography of the World): Grade 9 turns from physical to economic and social geography of the whole world: Part I works through the political map, world natural resources, world population, the scientific-technical revolution and the branches of the world economy; Part II then reviews every region in a fixed country-profile pattern (territory and position, natural resources, population, economy, transport, foreign ties), with Central Asia and Uzbekistan's neighbours given the fullest treatment, and closes with the global problems of humanity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
