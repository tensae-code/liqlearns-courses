# Video script — Migration In Papua New Guinea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG* (printed p. 70), teaches: Strand 1 Unit 3 People and Resources, Topics 1–5: Types of migrants in PNG; internal migration in PNG; impact of and issues on migration in PNG; migration routes, patterns and trends in PNG (teacher guide — evidence).

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 10 Geography is a population course — population pyramids in depth, world physical geography's effects on settlement, density and economic activity, then PNG-only migration (types, internal migration, routes and trends) and PNG's land-mobilisation questions (undeveloped land, Special Agricultural Business Leases, land grabbing). That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.
