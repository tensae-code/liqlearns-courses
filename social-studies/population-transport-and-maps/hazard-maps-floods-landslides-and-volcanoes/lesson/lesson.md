# Hazard Maps — Floods, Landslides and Volcanoes

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: hazard maps — the flood and precipitation threat map, the landslide-zones map and the map of Ecuador's volcanoes — as Ecuador's Grade 2 *Estudios Sociales 2do EGB* teaches them on printed p. 69. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reading hazard maps

Ecuador's Grade 2 book teaches map-reading through danger: a mapa de amenaza de inundaciones shows where floods and heavy rain threaten, a map of landslide zones (zonas de deslizamientos) shows where slopes may give way, and a map of Ecuador's volcanoes shows where eruptions can reach. The child learns that a hazard map answers one question — where is it dangerous, and for what — before any building or travelling is planned.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
