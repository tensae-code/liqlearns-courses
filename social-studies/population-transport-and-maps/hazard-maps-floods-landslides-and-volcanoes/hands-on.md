# Hands-on — Hazard Maps — Floods, Landslides and Volcanoes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a hazard map
Look at a hazard map and point to one safe area and one threatened area; say which hazard threatens it.

## Hazards near me
Write which hazard map — floods, landslides or volcanoes — matters most where you live, and why.
