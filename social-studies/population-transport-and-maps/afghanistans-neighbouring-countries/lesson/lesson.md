## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 6 Social Studies (Dari)): Grade 6 social studies turns outward from the state to the neighbourhood — after the provinces of Afghanistan, the six neighbouring countries each get a lesson (Pakistan, Iran, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, China) before the book turns to civics and history. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
