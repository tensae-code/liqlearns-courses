# Hands-on — What Settlements Do Functions Of Places

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Functions of a settlement: market town, mining town (Pind Dadan Khan), industrial town (Faisalabad), port city (Karachi, Gwadar), service, cultural/religious, administrative (Islamabad) and tourist resort (Murree). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
