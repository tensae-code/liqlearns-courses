# Central America — Country Profiles

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the data of the countries of Central America — official name, capital, surface, language, currency and national symbols for each country, as Guatemala's Grade 4 *Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana* teaches it in Unit 1 on printed pp. 37–38. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — One profile for each country

Each Central American country gets a profile in the lesson: its official name, its capital, its surface, its language, its currency and its national symbols. Learners read the seven profiles side by side and compare the countries of their own isthmus, Guatemala among them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way — the isthmus learned from home

Guatemala learns this inside one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book: one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book per grade, organised in four units that alternate History of Guatemala/Central America with the physical geography of the isthmus — every map carries the note about the unresolved Belize territorial differendum, and Geography is always worked on maps of the seven Central American countries with Guatemala's own Sierra de las Minas, Motagua river and Maya Biosphere as the home examples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
