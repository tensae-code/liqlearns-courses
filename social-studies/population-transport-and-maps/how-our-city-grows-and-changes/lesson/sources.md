**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Egypt, Cambodia).**

Egypt's own way (Grade 4 Term 1): the very first Social Studies book starts from the child's own space — drawing a plan of the home and school, comparing walking routes by length and landmarks — before maps in general, then the child's own city and governorate, and only then Egypt as homeland; a robot mascot teaches alongside named child guides from different governorates.

Searchable tags: how our city grows and changes, Egypt, social studies, population-transport-and-maps

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 12 Geography): the final Geography year is economic geography — Cambodia's population, inequalities, farm/industry/energy/trade/tourism economy and urbanisation first, then the world's economic poles studied one by one: the USA, Japan and the European Union.
