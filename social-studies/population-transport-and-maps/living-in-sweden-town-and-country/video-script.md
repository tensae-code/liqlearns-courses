# Video script — Living in Sweden — Town and Country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sweden's Grade 1 book looks at the child's own home place: what buildings it has — a church, a school, shops. The teacher guide's notes for the textbook's pages 12–23 start settlement from the buildings a pupil already knows.

Homes differ: one-family houses and many-family houses. And places differ: town, suburb and countryside. The lesson sorts the pupil's own living place into those words.
