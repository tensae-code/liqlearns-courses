# Hands-on — Living in Sweden — Town and Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## List your home place's buildings
List the buildings of your own home place and check them against the lesson's list: church, school, shops — which does your place have?

## Sort homes and places
Name one home you know and say whether it is a one-family or many-family house, and whether it stands in a town, a suburb or the countryside.
