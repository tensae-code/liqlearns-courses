# Living in Sweden — Town and Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) What a home place has — church, school, shops  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) What a home place has — church, school, shops**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) living in Sweden: what buildings a home place has (church, school, shops), one-family and many-family houses, and town, suburb and countryside, as Sweden's Grade 1 teacher guide notes the textbook  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) living in Sweden: what buildings a home place has (church, school, shops), one-family and many-family houses, and town, suburb and countryside, as Sweden's Grade 1 teacher guide notes the textbook**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Town, suburb and countryside — one-family and many-family houses  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) Town, suburb and countryside — one-family and many-family houses**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Sweden's Grade 1 book sorts homes into one-family and many-family houses, and places into town, suburb and countryside.  b) In Sweden's Grade 1 book, a home place has no school or shops in it.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about Sweden
**Answer: a) Sweden's Grade 1 book sorts homes into one-family and many-family houses, and places into town, suburb and countryside.**
