# Hands-on — No Entry — Borders and Excluded Spaces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find a no-entry line
Name one border or boundary you know that says no entry to some people, and write who it bars and how, as Folha 2 teaches borders.

## Spot an excluded space
Name one closed or private space near you where entry is barred, and write how it compares with a ghetto as the lesson names ghettos and closed spaces.
