# Video script — No Entry — Borders and Excluded Spaces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Folha 2 opens with É proibida a entrada! / No entry allowed! It teaches borders as lines and rules that can forbid entry — a border is not only drawn on a map; it is enforced where people try to cross it.

The same Folha carries the idea inside places as well: ghettos and closed/private spaces are parts of a place where entry is barred to some people. Excluded spaces, at a country's edge or inside a city, are this lesson's one thing.
