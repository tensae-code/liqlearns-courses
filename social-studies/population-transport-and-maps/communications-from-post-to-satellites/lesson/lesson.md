## Sub lesson 2 — Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 8 Geografía 2 G8): This older edition teaches world economic geography as a story — first the political map's dynamics through history (colonial empires, independences, 1914, post-1945, the 1960s, the 1990s), then resources, then production, and it closes with full regional studies; Latin America is studied first and in most depth, and every world topic is measured against Cuba's own sugar, tobacco, nickel and trade.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 9 Geografía de Cuba G9 (2012)): The 2012 edition follows the G9 programme chapter for chapter (archipiélago → naturaleza → panorama económico → desarrollo social → situación medioambiental → regiones) and is explicit about its own dating: data before 31 Dec 2010 use the 1975 provincial division, while Chapter 6 teaches the new 16-province organisation of 1 Jan 2011. NOTE — this is NOT the same book as the Biblioteca copy filed next (Book 16): that copy is the wholly rewritten 2025 Tercer Perfeccionamiento edition, so the two are placed separately. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
