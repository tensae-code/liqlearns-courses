# Topographic maps — flat pictures of uneven land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Relief is the height difference between —
a) highest and lowest points  b) two towns  c) sea and river
**Answer: a) highest and lowest points**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A saddle is one of the —
a) landforms  b) map symbols  c) scales
**Answer: a) landforms**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A topographic map portrays —
a) natural and man-made features in detail  b) only rivers  c) only towns
**Answer: a) natural and man-made features in detail**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Map scale is the ratio of —
a) map distance to ground distance  b) height to width  c) north to south
**Answer: a) map distance to ground distance**
