# Topographic maps — flat pictures of uneven land — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define relief.
**Answer:** The height difference between a landscape's highest and lowest points.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name five landforms from the lesson.
**Answer:** Plateaus, escarpments, hills, mountains, volcanoes, saddles, cliffs, valleys, gorges, floodplains (any five).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is map scale?
**Answer:** The map-to-ground distance ratio.
