# Topographic maps — flat pictures of uneven land — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Landforms are three-dimensional features.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A topographic map shows only natural features.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Colours, symbols, letters and numbers build the portrait.
**Answer: True**
