# Video script — Topographic maps — flat pictures of uneven land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Relief is the height difference between the highest and lowest points of a landscape.

A topographic map is the detailed two-dimensional portrait of an area's natural and man-made features.

The portrait is built from colours, symbols, letters and numbers — and scale, the map-to-ground distance ratio that tells how much the portrait shrinks the land..
