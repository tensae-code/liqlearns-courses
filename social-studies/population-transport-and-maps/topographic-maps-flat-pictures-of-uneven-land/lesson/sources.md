Samoa's own way (Year 9, Book 2): Place is taught through Samoan lived places — ordinary, sacred (tapu), natural and political places such as Mulinu'u — and migration is taught through Samoa's own movement story and its New Zealand diaspora, closing with a full mapping-skills unit.

Malaysia's own way (MY T2 Geografi Tingkatan 2): the year pairs map skills (scale, topographic maps) with weather and climate, then transport and telecommunications as the human-geography core, an Asia regional study, and global warming with green technology as the environmental close.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 9 G9 TG English 2018) (teacher guide — evidence): competency-based guide — every unit pairs a syllabus table (competency level, content, learning outcomes, periods) with lesson-planning notes and a specimen group activity; geography is taught region-first (Asia, then Sri Lanka itself in depth), closing with hands-on 1:50,000 topographic-sheet work (Matara sheet No. 91). That evidence is stocked here as the way Sri Lanka teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Sri Lanka is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 8 G8 English): a four-unit arc — Solar System, Earth's uniqueness, then regional geography taught as six South Asian "landscapes" (mountain, valley, coastal, rural, industrial-urban, desert), closing with Sri Lanka's own 1:50,000 topographic-sheet system (92 sheets) that Grade 9 continues.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 9 G9 English): the student companion to Book 1 — five chapters moving from the whole Asian region down to Sri Lanka's own landscape, its district-by-district development contrasts, environmental balance, and a closing practical chapter reading the 1:50,000 topographic maps.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka G10 Tamil SB): the O/L year pairs every world topic with its Sri Lankan twin — world agriculture then Sri Lanka's land use, world industries then Sri Lanka's industries — and closes with map skills and an outline-map chapter; the syllabus (units 10.1–10.13) is printed at the back.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka G10 Tamil TG) (teacher guide — evidence): a 90-period competency syllabus (units 10.1–10.13, printed in full) with an expanded-content section and a timed activity set (செயற்பாடு) for every unit — quality inputs, process steps and assessment criteria — plus annexed readings (a poem on Earth and life, a 2004-tsunami reading) and term-test blueprints; the non-content parts (teaching-learning methods, pp. 18–23, and assessment, pp. 325–337) are pedagogy, named here but not placed. That evidence is stocked here as the way Sri Lanka teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Sri Lanka is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka G12 Tamil SB Part I): the old A/L course opens with a full astronomy block (universe, solar system, origin theories, telescopes, the space age) before turning to the Earth, then teaches geography as man-and-environment study in the Brunhes / Vidal de la Blache tradition (the bibliography names them), and closes with map interpretation on a Sri Lanka topographic sheet.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G12 ES T1 Workbook (10385)): the activities book opens the final year with the greenhouse effect modelled on the bench and acid rain simulated, then structures, plate tectonics through the Dead Sea transform, and geological exploration with Jordan-flavoured map work.

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 9 Geography): The Intermediate level (IX–X) treats each world topic at full textbook depth and keeps the Bhutan case inside it — Soils of Bhutan, agro-ecological zones and farming practices of Bhutan, and common disasters in Bhutan (including GLOF) alongside the world disaster survey.

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 10 Geography): Class X pairs deep physical theory (Big Bang, plate tectonics, the making of the Himalayas) with an applied Bhutan block — climate and vegetation zones of Bhutan, Bhutanese agriculture's challenges and advancement, industries and their distribution, and a Bhutan disaster chapter that adds Education in Emergency.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 10 Geography student): grade-10 Geography is a methods-first course — it opens with the science's history, branches and GIS/GPS/aerospace methods, works through the lithosphere with a full natural-disasters lesson, and closes with resources measured by endowment; Armenia's own rivers fill the hydrology tables and Armenia's place in modern geopolitics closes the political-map theme.
