# Hands-on — Vietnams Transport And Communications

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 20–21: The role of services and the factors behind them (42.4% of GDP in 2024), then Vietnam's transport and posts and telecommunications — freight by mode (roads carrying by far the most), the road, rail (the Thong Nhat line; city railways in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City), inland-waterway, sea (34 ports and 296 wharves; routes to North America and Europe) and air networks (23 airports, 12 of them international), pipelines, and posts and telecoms (121.3 million mobile subscriptions; 23.9 million fixed broadband). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
