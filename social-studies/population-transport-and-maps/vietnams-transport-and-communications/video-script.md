The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 87–95), teaches: Bài 20–21: The role of services and the factors behind them (42.4% of GDP in 2024), then Vietnam's transport and posts and telecommunications — freight by mode (roads carrying by far the most), the road, rail (the Thong Nhat line; city railways in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City), inland-waterway, sea (34 ports and 296 wharves; routes to North America and Europe) and air networks (23 airports, 12 of them international), pipelines, and posts and telecoms (121.3 million mobile subscriptions; 23.9 million fixed broadband).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
