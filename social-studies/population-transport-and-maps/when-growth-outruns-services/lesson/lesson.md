# When growth outruns services

## Do it first

A clinic built for a village of 2,000 now serves 6,000. Nothing about the clinic changed — everything about the number did. Read the pressure list.

## Sub lesson 1 — The pressure list

Rapid growth pressures six fronts at once: food supply, housing, education, health services, employment, and fuelwood supply. Each front is the same arithmetic: services and supplies grow slowly; mouths grow fast.

[Picture 21096 — queues at a clinic, a school and a shrinking fuelwood pile]

## Sub lesson 2 — The fuelwood example

The book works the fuelwood supply as its example: as population grows, demand for fuelwood strips the nearby trees, the walk for wood lengthens, prices rise, and households burn worse fuels or less. One collapsing supply shows the mechanism of all six fronts.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the six fronts, and tell the fuelwood story from first cut to last effect.

## Do It Yourself

1. List the six fronts from memory.
2. Say which front the book works as its example.
3. State the shared arithmetic in one sentence.
