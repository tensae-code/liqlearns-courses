# Hands-on — When growth outruns services

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Front audit
Audit your own area on the six fronts: for each, one sign of pressure you can point to, or an honest "not here". Present the audit as the lesson’s arithmetic at home.

## Wood walk
Measure or estimate how far fuelwood is gathered from a home you know, against what elders say it was. The lengthening walk is the worked example, measured.
