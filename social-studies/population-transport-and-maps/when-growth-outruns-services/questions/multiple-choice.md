# When growth outruns services — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pressure front named is —
a) television  b) tourism  c) fuelwood supply
**Answer: c) fuelwood supply**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Growth pressures education by —
a) closing schools  b) more pupils than school places grow  c) fewer children
**Answer: b) more pupils than school places grow**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the fuelwood chain, the first effect of growing demand is —
a) nearby trees are stripped  b) prices fall  c) forests expand
**Answer: a) nearby trees are stripped**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** As wood grows scarce, the walk for it —
a) shortens  b) stays fixed  c) lengthens
**Answer: c) lengthens**
