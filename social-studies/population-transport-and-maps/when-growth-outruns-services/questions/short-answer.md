# When growth outruns services — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the six fronts.
**Answer: Food, housing, education, health services, employment and fuelwood supply.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Walk the fuelwood chain.
**Answer: Demand strips nearby trees; the gathering walk lengthens; prices rise; households burn worse fuels or less.**
