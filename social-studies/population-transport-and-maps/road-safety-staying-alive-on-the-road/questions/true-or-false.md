# Road safety — staying alive on the road — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The faster a vehicle goes past 30 km/h, the better a struck person's chance of living.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Jumping over divider railings is one of the safe rules.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pedestrians should take extra care crossing at curves.
**Answer: True**
