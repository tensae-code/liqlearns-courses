# Road safety — staying alive on the road — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** List the five pedestrian rules.
**Answer:** Know your safety signals; extra care at curves; be careful while crossing; always use sidewalks; never jump divider railings.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book make you judge a street photograph?
**Answer:** To practise seeing safe and unsafe behaviour before meeting it on a real road.
