# Road safety — staying alive on the road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Road safety is —
a) A kind of vehicle  b) A road-building method  c) A driving licence  d) The discipline by which people on roads protect themselves from vehicles
**Answer: d) The discipline by which people on roads protect themselves from vehicles**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A struck person's chance of living falls as speed rises once a vehicle passes —
a) 100 km/h  b) 5 km/h  c) 30 km/h  d) 80 km/h
**Answer: c) 30 km/h**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** More than half of road deaths and injuries involve —
a) Train drivers  b) Vulnerable road users  c) Pilots  d) Ship crews
**Answer: b) Vulnerable road users**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the five pedestrian rules?
a) Always use sidewalks  b) Jump over divider railings  c) Cross without looking at curves  d) Walk in the middle of the road
**Answer: a) Always use sidewalks**
