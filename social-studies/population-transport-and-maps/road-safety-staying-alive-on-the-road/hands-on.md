# Hands-on — Road safety — staying alive on the road

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Judge one real street scene: list two safe and two unsafe things you see.

## Activity 2
Practise the five rules on one real walk to school or the shops.

## Activity 3
Teach the 30 km/h fact to one younger child in your own words.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the definition of road safety, the 30 km/h point, and the five pedestrian rules without looking.
