## Sub lesson 1 — Street Names And Finding Your Way

Mauritania's book, the *Mauritania Grade 4 geo-4af-m* (printed p. 32), teaches: Street names. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mauritania's way — the book's own teaching way

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 4 geo-4af-m): A one-topic-per-spread observation book that moves from the child's own wilaya outward: the wilaya map first, then the landforms of home (watercourses, mountains, plains, plateaus, sand dunes), the weather elements and the two measuring instruments (thermometer, rain gauge), then the lived place — public places, archaeological sites, street names, my village, the capital of my wilaya — closing with integration exercises on the relief and climate of the wilaya capital. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
