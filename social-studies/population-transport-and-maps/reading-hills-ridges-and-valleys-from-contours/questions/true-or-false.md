# Reading hills, ridges and valleys from contours — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mountains show elongated closed contours rising to the centre.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ridge and valley Vs point the same way.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A valley's pattern runs parallel to its stream before crossing it.
**Answer: True**
