# Reading hills, ridges and valleys from contours — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A hill's contours are —
a) concentric closed circles under 1,000 m  b) open lines  c) crossing lines
**Answer: a) concentric closed circles under 1,000 m**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The summit of a hill lies —
a) inside the smallest ring  b) outside the rings  c) on the index line only
**Answer: a) inside the smallest ring**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A ridge's V contours point —
a) away from the high ground  b) upstream  c) toward the summit
**Answer: a) away from the high ground**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A valley's V contours point —
a) upstream, toward high ground  b) downstream  c) away from the stream
**Answer: a) upstream, toward high ground**
