# Reading hills, ridges and valleys from contours — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do you recognise a hill?
**Answer:** Concentric closed circles under 1,000 m, summit in the smallest ring.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do ridge contours point?
**Answer:** U/V contours point away from the high ground.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do valley contours point, and why does it matter?
**Answer:** V points upstream toward high ground — the mirror of a ridge; mixing them up inverts the land.
