# Video script — Reading hills, ridges and valleys from contours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A hill is concentric closed circles under 1,000 metres high, its summit inside the smallest ring; concave and convex hillside spacing can be distinguished in the rings.

A ridge is an elliptical line of high ground.

A valley is the reverse: its V contours point upstream, toward high ground, and the pattern runs parallel to its stream before crossing it.
