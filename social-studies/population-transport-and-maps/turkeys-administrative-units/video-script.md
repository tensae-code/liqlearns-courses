# Video script — Turkey's Administrative Units

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey's Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi book closes with a map: Türkiye'nin Mülki İdare Bölümleri ile Kara ve Deniz Komşuları Haritası — the map of Turkey's administrative divisions together with its land and sea neighbours. A Grade 2 child meets the whole country at the end of the book: Turkey divided into its administrative units, and the neighbours, on land and at sea, that surround it.

Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya returns to the same thing at secondary depth: II-Ç Ülkemizde İdari Birimler — administrative units in Turkey — closing the Human Systems unit on settlements. What the Grade 2 child saw as one end-of-book map, the Grade 9 student studies as a topic in its own right.
