# Hands-on — Pakistan And South Asia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: South Asia: its countries (Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Afghanistan) and economic features; SAARC, founded 1985, and Pakistan's relations with its South Asian neighbours. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
