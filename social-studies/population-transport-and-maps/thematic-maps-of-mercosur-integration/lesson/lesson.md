# Thematic Maps of Mercosur Integration

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Argentina's teacher notebook teaches Mercosur's physical integration by analysing thematic cartography and newspaper articles together. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Thematic cartography for integration processes

In Argentina, the teacher notebook Cuadernos para el aula: Ciencias Sociales gives its Geography axis (Eje 1) a worked teaching proposal: El análisis de cartografía temática y de artículos periodísticos para la enseñanza de los procesos de integración física del Mercosur — analysing thematic cartography to teach the processes of Mercosur's physical integration. A thematic map is a map drawn for one theme: here, the theme is how the bloc's countries are physically joined.

## Sub lesson 2 — Newspaper articles beside the maps

The maps do not work alone: the proposal pairs them with artículos periodísticos — newspaper articles about the same integration. The learner reads the joining of countries two ways at once: what the thematic map shows in space, and what the press reports as it happens. Map and article check each other.
