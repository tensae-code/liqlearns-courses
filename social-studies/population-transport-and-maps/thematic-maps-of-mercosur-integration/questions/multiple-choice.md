# Thematic Maps of Mercosur Integration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A thematic map is a map drawn for —
a) one theme  b) decoration only  c) no subject at all
**Answer: a) one theme**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The theme of the maps in this proposal is —
a) Mercosur's physical integration  b) the weather in winter  c) cooking
**Answer: a) Mercosur's physical integration**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Beside the maps, the proposal also works with —
a) newspaper articles  b) songs  c) riddles
**Answer: a) newspaper articles**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Reading map and article together lets the two sources —
a) check each other  b) cancel each other  c) stay secret
**Answer: a) check each other**
