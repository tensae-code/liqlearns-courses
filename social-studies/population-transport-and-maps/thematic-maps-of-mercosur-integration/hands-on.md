# Hands-on — Thematic Maps of Mercosur Integration

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw a one-theme map
Take a plain outline map of your area. Choose ONE theme — the roads, the rivers, or where the shops are — and draw only that theme on it. You have made a thematic map.

## Pair your map with a report
Find or write a short news-style report about something on your thematic map — a new road, a bridge, a market day. Lay the report beside the map and note one thing each one tells you that the other does not.
