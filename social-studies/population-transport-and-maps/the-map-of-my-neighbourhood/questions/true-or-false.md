# The Map Of My Neighbourhood — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Lesson 4 The map of our neighbourhood (نقشه‌ی محله‌ی ما): look at the pictures — things shown from the front and from above; imagine how a bird looking down sees the ground; the classroom picture from the front, and the map a pupil drew of the same classroom as if looking from above; how is a map made? — from aerial photographs (عکس هوایی) taken by aeroplane over the land; and the map's key (راهنمای نقشه) — last year you met the maps of house and school: beside every map there is a key, and without it the different places on the map cannot be told apart.
