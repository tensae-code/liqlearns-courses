# Pyramid types — expansive to constrictive — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The pyramid's top shows the oldest age groups.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An expansive pyramid is the graphic of low fertility.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Intermediate shapes lie between expansive and constrictive.
**Answer: True**
