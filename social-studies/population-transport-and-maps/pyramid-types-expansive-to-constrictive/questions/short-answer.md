# Pyramid types — expansive to constrictive — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is a population pyramid built?
**Answer:** Stacked horizontal bars, one per age group, males on one side and females on the other.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the expansive type promise?
**Answer:** Rapid growth — many young people already born into the base, few elders.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a constrictive base tell you?
**Answer:** Fewer children than parents — fertility has fallen and the population is settling or shrinking.
