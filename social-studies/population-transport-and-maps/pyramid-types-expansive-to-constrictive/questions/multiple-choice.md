# Pyramid types — expansive to constrictive — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a pyramid, each bar stands for —
a) an age group's share, males one side, females the other  b) a region's income  c) a year of births
**Answer: a) an age group's share, males one side, females the other**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** An expansive pyramid has —
a) a narrow base and wide middle  b) equal bars throughout  c) a broad base narrowing steadily upward
**Answer: c) a broad base narrowing steadily upward**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A constrictive pyramid signals —
a) a young population  b) falling fertility and a population settling or shrinking  c) rapid growth
**Answer: b) falling fertility and a population settling or shrinking**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A wide base means —
a) many young children  b) few children  c) many elders
**Answer: a) many young children**
