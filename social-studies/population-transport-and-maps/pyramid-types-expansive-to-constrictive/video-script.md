# Video script — Pyramid types — expansive to constrictive

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A population pyramid stacks horizontal bars: each bar is an age group's share, males on one side, females on the other.

The expansive pyramid is a true triangle: a broad base of children narrowing steadily upward.

The constrictive pyramid narrows at the base: fewer children than parents, the graphic of falling fertility and a population settling or shrinking.
