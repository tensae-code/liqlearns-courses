# Kinds of migration — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration is —
a) a permanent change of residence  b) any journey  c) a holiday trip
**Answer: a) a permanent change of residence**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Forced migration is driven by —
a) better pay offers  b) school admissions  c) war, drought, famine, disaster or conflict
**Answer: c) war, drought, famine, disaster or conflict**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A move within one country is —
a) forced migration always  b) internal migration  c) international migration
**Answer: b) internal migration**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A family fleeing drought to another region of the same country is —
a) internal, forced migration  b) international, voluntary migration  c) internal, voluntary migration
**Answer: a) internal, forced migration**
