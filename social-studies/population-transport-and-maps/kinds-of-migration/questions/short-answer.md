# Kinds of migration — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two sortings of migration.
**Answer:** By distance: internal or international. By choice: voluntary or forced.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give three drivers of forced migration.
**Answer:** Any three of: war, drought, famine, disaster, conflict.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What decides whether a move is internal or international?
**Answer:** The border — whether the move crosses into another country or stays within one.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A student crosses a border to take a better job abroad. Place the move.
**Answer:** International and voluntary — across a border, and made for better opportunities.
