# Maps and globes — kinds of maps and scale — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A map showing borders is a —
a) Physical map  b) Statistical map  c) Sea chart  d) Political map
**Answer: d) Political map**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A time-zone map is a —
a) Physical map  b) Political map  c) Special-purpose map  d) Globe
**Answer: c) Special-purpose map**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Large-scale means —
a) 1:250,000 and smaller  b) 1:50,000 and larger  c) Exactly 1:1  d) No numbers
**Answer: b) 1:50,000 and larger**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A world map is —
a) Small-scale  b) Large-scale  c) Medium-scale only  d) Not a map
**Answer: a) Small-scale**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Coverage and detail —
a) Grow together  b) Never change  c) Are the same thing  d) Trade against each other
**Answer: d) Trade against each other**
