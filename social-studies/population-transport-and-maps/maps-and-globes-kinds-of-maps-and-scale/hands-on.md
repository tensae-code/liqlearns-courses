# Hands-on — Maps and globes — kinds of maps and scale

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Classify three real maps you can find by message and by scale.

## Activity 2
Draw your school compound at two scales: the whole compound, then one classroom block bigger.

## Activity 3
Say which scale a road-builder and a world traveller each needs, and why.

## With a partner
Tell your companion the three kinds by message, the three scale bands with numbers, and the coverage–detail rule.
