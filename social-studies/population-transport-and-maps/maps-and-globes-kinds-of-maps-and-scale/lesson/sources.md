Haiti's own way (teacher sequence — evidence): Geography opens with representing the Earth itself — globe-to-flat projection problems, orientation practised on the ground with compass/GPS/Google Maps, and time-zone puzzles worked on Haiti's own links (midday shared with Québec, 18h in Paris, Port-au-Prince–Paris flights); every séance ends in a croquis, a glossary and field observation. That evidence is stocked here as the way Haiti teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Myanmar's own way (teacher guide — evidence): the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week — physical geography, social geography, environmental geography, neighbouring countries and cartography — each lesson plan built student-centred (Bloom steps, key questions, classroom assessment) around Myanmar and its five neighbours. That evidence is stocked here as the way Myanmar teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Pairing warning (honesty): Book 2 is an Addis Ababa bureau guide in English; Book 1 is an Amhara bureau student book in Amharic. They are NOT edition-matched companions — the guide's unit order differs and whole student topics (maps, globes, GPS) have no guide counterpart, so no guide coverage is claimed for them. Guide material is used only as labelled teacher evidence.

Costa Rica's own way: a commercial workbook twin of the CONED 11 anthology — same ministry programme, but every topic is a fixed cycle (problem question, basic concepts, my previous ideas, widening my knowledge, I apply my knowledge, problem-based learning, exam preparation) with heavy map-work and Costa Rica-specific data: ICE internet-coverage map, services-export league table, Costa Rica's geo-strategic bridge position between North/South America and the Caribbean. That way is stocked here as the way Costa Rica teaches this thing.

Ecuador's own way (Grade 8 book): at Superior level the book turns thematic — history of work and social transformation, then Latin America and Ecuador today; Geography enters as a full cartography tema (map studies explicitly tied to Earth's formation, continents, oceans, climates, natural risks and contingency plans) and as the economic-sectors tema on Ecuador. That way is stocked here as the way Ecuador teaches this thing.

Honesty note: the ministry's 2024 transición-curricular PDFs, as held, reuse one body per sublevel — the Grade 5 body also carries the Grade 6 and Grade 7 covers (Subnivel Media), and the Grade 8 body also carries the Grade 9 and Grade 10 covers (Subnivel Superior). That content is credited once, under its true Grade 5 / Grade 8 identity, exactly as the placements state; the genuine Ecuador Grade 6, 7, 9 and 10 Estudios Sociales books are NOT held (library debt).

Palestine's own way (Grade 6, PS G6P1): Grade 6 opens with how we know — the map and its elements as the first source of geographical knowledge — then walks the Arab homeland region by region (peninsula, Levant, Palestine, Iraq, North Africa), each time fixing location first, then surface and climate, then the ancient civilisation the land produced.

Bahamas' own way (Grade 6): The Grade 6 book is civics-led (government, elections, courts, constitution, finance ministry) but opens the primary course's widest geography: the world's continents and oceans, political and physical maps, global warming and natural disasters, human resources and the job market, worldwide transport and communication through Nassau's ports of entry, and a closing cultural comparison of The Bahamas with Africa, Australia, Brazil and Japan.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 7) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 7 turns analytical — factors behind transport-mode choice, up-to-date maps/charts (routes, contours, scale-and-distance work) for domestic, regional and international travel, transport hazards from floods/fog/earthquakes, shelter shortages and siting factors, and desertification with land rehabilitation (afforestation, gully filling). That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado3_01): Grade 3's first cartilla is the orientation cartilla — cardinal points and maps as guides, a different map for each kind of information, then Colombia's political map with its departments and capitals explored department by department.
