# Video script — Maps and globes — kinds of maps and scale

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Lay out four maps in your mind: the whole world, Africa, your kebele, your school compound. One shows a flea's view, one an eagle's. Which shows the most land? Which shows the most detail? They cannot both win — that trade is this lesson's heart.

This lesson teaches: sorting maps two ways — by the message they carry (physical, political, special-purpose) and by scale (large, medium, small) — and the trade between coverage and detail. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
