# Video script — Raster and vector — two ways to store the world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Raster data is grids of cells, each cell holding a single value.

Vector data is geometry: points for wells, schools and airports; lines and polylines for rivers and roads; polygons for lakes, parks and boundaries.

Every feature carries two kinds of data.
