# Lesson plan — Raster and vector — two ways to store the world

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Raster and vector — two ways to store the world
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 11 Geography, Unit 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** GIS data — the most important component — splits into raster grids of cells and vector geometry of points, lines and polygons, with every feature carrying spatial and attribute data.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Population, Transport and Maps, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
