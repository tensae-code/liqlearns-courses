# Raster and vector — two ways to store the world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Raster data is —
a) grids of cells with single values  b) points and lines  c) paper maps
**Answer: a) grids of cells with single values**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Elevation and rainfall suit —
a) raster  b) vector points  c) attribute tables only
**Answer: a) raster**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A river in vector data is a —
a) line / polyline  b) point  c) polygon
**Answer: a) line / polyline**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A lake's name and depth are its —
a) attribute data  b) spatial data  c) raster cells
**Answer: a) attribute data**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** In vector data, a line is —
a) a single cell  b) a block of cells  c) a chain of vertices
**Answer: c) a chain of vertices**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** In raster data, an area is —
a) one coordinate pair  b) a block of contiguous cells  c) a closed polygon of vertices
**Answer: b) a block of contiguous cells**
