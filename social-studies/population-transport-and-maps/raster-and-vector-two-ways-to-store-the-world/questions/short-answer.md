# Raster and vector — two ways to store the world — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distinguish raster from vector.
**Answer:** Raster: grids of single-value cells. Vector: points, lines and polygons.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one vector example of each geometry kind.
**Answer:** Points: wells/schools/airports. Lines: rivers/roads. Polygons: lakes/parks/boundaries.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two data kinds does every feature carry?
**Answer:** Spatial (where) and attribute (what it is like).
