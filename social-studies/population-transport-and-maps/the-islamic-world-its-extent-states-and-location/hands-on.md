## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 3 Lesson 1, The Geographic Location of the Islamic World States: the Islamic world holds 58 states on four continents — 27 in Asia, 27 in Africa, 2 in Europe (Albania and Bosnia-Herzegovina) and 2 recently in South America (Suriname and Guyana) — covering 22.2% of the world's land; Kazakhstan is the largest Islamic state by area and the Maldives the smallest; its location's traits are the meeting of the old world's continents and civilizations, control of many important waterways, diverse natural and economic resources, and diverse climate regions.
