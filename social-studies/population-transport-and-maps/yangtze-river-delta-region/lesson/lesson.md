## Sub lesson 1 — The Yangtze River Delta region

## Sub lesson 2 — China's way — the book's own teaching way

China learns this inside its own books' way: after the world and national survey of Grade 7 and Grade 8 Volume 1, China is re-taught region by region — each of the four great regions gets the same treatment (natural features and agriculture first, then one emblematic sub-region or city in depth: the Northeast, the Loess Plateau, Beijing; the Yangtze Delta, Hong Kong and Macao, Taiwan; the Tarim Basin; the Three Rivers Source), closing with China's place in the world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
