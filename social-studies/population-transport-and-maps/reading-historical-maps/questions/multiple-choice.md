# Reading historical maps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is historical map?
a) argument
b) a map of the past — an argument in pictures, to be read critically
c) reference
**Answer: b) a map of the past — an argument in pictures, to be read critically**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is key?
a) scale
b) the box explaining a map's signs and shadings
c) claim
**Answer: b) the box explaining a map's signs and shadings**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is distribution map?
a) a map showing where something is spread
b) movement map
c) interrogate
**Answer: a) a map showing where something is spread**
