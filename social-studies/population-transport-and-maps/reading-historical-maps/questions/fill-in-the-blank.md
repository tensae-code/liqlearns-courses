# Reading historical maps — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'what a map asserts by its choices of shade, arrow and label' is ______.
**Answer: argument**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'what a drawn border asserts — to be questioned, not assumed' is ______.
**Answer: claim**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'to question a map until it gives up its meaning' is ______.
**Answer: interrogate**
