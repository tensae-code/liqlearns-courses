# Video script — Reading historical maps

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Maps carry the teaching. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Count the Grade 11 book's maps and you find its skeleton: evolution centres, civilisations, expansions, movements — twenty-one map references, in almost every unit. Watch the names appear one by one: historical map, reference, argument. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — The reader's checklist. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Read in order: title and date first — a map of 1500 must not be judged by 1900's borders; then the key, the scale, and the compass; then the patterns — where things cluster, spread, and stop.

Borders on old maps are claims, not fences. Watch the names appear one by one: key, scale, claim. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — From map to meaning. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The skill ends in sentences: this expansion followed that river; this trade hugged that coast; this movement stopped at that desert. Watch the names appear one by one: distribution map, movement map, interrogate. This is the part of the story to hold.
