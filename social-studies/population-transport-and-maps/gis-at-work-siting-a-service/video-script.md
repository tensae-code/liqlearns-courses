Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 38), teaches: Geographical techniques and skills 1: The geographical information system (سامانه اطلاعات جغرافیایی GIS) — a system of input, processing and output for managing geographical information better; worked example: siting a service centre (a school, clinic or sports hall) for a rural area.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
