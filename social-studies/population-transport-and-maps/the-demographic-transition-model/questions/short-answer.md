# The demographic transition model — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things frame population geography?
**Answer:** Fertility, mortality and migration.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does growth slow in stage three?
**Answer:** Births fall, with urbanisation and contraception.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do stages one and four compare?
**Answer:** Both balance births against deaths — stage one high, stage four low.
