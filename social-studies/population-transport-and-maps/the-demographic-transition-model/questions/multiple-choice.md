# The demographic transition model — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In stage one, birth and death rates are —
a) both high and in balance  b) both low  c) far apart
**Answer: a) both high and in balance**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The stage-two surge comes because —
a) death rates fall while births stay high  b) births rise sharply  c) migration doubles
**Answer: a) death rates fall while births stay high**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Births fall in stage three with urbanisation and —
a) contraception  b) famine  c) war
**Answer: a) contraception**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Developed and developing countries differ in the model by —
a) the centuries of their travel through it  b) the number of stages  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) the centuries of their travel through it**
