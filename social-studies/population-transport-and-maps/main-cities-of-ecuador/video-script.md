# Video script — Main Cities of Ecuador

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 2 places the country's life on the map through its principales ciudades — the main cities of Ecuador. The learner meets the great cities as the country's hubs: where the most people live, where roads meet, and where each region's goods and government gather, starting from the cities of their own province and widening to the whole country.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
