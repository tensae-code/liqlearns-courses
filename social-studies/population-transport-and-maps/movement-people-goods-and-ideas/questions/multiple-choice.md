# Movement — People, Goods and Ideas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 Geography unit, movement is —
a) one of the classic five themes, taught as its own lesson  b) only a road sign  c) only a river
**Answer: a) one of the classic five themes, taught as its own lesson**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Arabic title of the movement lesson is —
a) الحركة  b) المكان  c) الخريطة
**Answer: a) الحركة**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these does this lesson say moves between places? —
a) people, goods and ideas  b) only rocks  c) only rain
**Answer: a) people, goods and ideas**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Movement links —
a) one place to another  b) a place to nothing  c) only two pages of a book
**Answer: a) one place to another**
