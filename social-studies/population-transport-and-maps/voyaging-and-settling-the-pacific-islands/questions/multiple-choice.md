# Voyaging and Settling the Pacific Islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Settlement of the Pacific, in these books, is a story of —
a) voyaging and migration  b) only farming  c) only building roads
**Answer: a) voyaging and migration**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The readers teach Austronesian voyaging from —
a) Taiwan  b) Europe  c) Africa
**Answer: a) Taiwan**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Kapingamarangi reader names, with the second Austronesian wave —
a) the Lapita culture  b) a railway  c) a desert caravan
**Answer: a) the Lapita culture**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The voyages ended in —
a) the settlement of Micronesia's islands  b) no one settling anywhere  c) everyone returning at once
**Answer: a) the settlement of Micronesia's islands**
