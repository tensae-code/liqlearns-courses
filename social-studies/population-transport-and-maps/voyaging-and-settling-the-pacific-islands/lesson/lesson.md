# Voyaging and Settling the Pacific Islands

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Austronesian voyaging routes and the settlement of Micronesia's islands, as Micronesia's Grade 9 textbook and its Pohnpei Studies readers teach them. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Austronesian voyaging routes

Settlement in Micronesia's books is a voyaging story. The Grade 9 textbook's Chapter 2 teaches Austronesian voyaging routes, and the Pohnpei Studies readers open the same way: the first and second groups of people who came into the Pacific, with Austronesian voyaging from Taiwan. The Mwoakilloa reader teaches the first and second groups; the Nukuoro reader teaches the first people into the Pacific and the Austronesian settlement waves.

## Sub lesson 2 — Settling Micronesia's islands

Those voyages ended in settlement: people reached and settled Micronesia's islands. The Kapingamarangi reader names the second, Austronesian, wave and the Lapita culture as part of that story. So the lesson's order is: voyaging first, then settling — the islands were settled by people who voyaged across the Pacific.
