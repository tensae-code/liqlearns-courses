# Hands-on — Voyaging and Settling the Pacific Islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the voyage
Draw a simple route picture: people voyaging from Taiwan out into the Pacific, in waves. Label the first wave and the second, Austronesian, wave.

## Name the waves
Write the two waves the readers teach — the first group into the Pacific and the second, Austronesian, wave — and add the Lapita culture beside the second wave, as the Kapingamarangi reader does.
