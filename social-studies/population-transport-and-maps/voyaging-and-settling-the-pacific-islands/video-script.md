# Video script — Voyaging and Settling the Pacific Islands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

People settled the Pacific by voyaging.

The readers teach a first group and a second group coming into the Pacific.

The second wave is the Austronesian wave, voyaging from Taiwan.

And those voyages settled Micronesia's islands — the Kapingamarangi reader names the Lapita culture in that story.
