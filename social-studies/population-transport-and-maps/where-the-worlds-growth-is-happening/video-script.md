The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Sub-Saharan Africa adds about 1.1 billion people by 2050 — over half of all world growth — and keeps growing all century.

There were 62 million births to mothers aged 15 to 19 in 2015–2020, and 46 percent of them were in Sub-Saharan Africa.

Nine countries drive the growth of 2019–2050, and Ethiopia is one of them.
