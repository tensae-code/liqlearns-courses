# Where the world's growth is happening

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: the world grows by billions this century — but where, exactly? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Africa grows, others peak

Sub-Saharan Africa adds about 1.1 billion people by 2050 — over half of all world growth — and keeps growing all century. Eastern Asia, Europe and other regions peak and then decline.

[Picture 27040 — a regional-population chart with Sub-Saharan Africa's line climbing past all others]

## Sub lesson 2 — Adolescent fertility — a flagged cost

There were 62 million births to mothers aged 15 to 19 in 2015–2020, and 46 percent of them were in Sub-Saharan Africa. The book flags adolescent fertility for its health and social costs.

## Sub lesson 3 — The nine big growers

Nine countries drive the growth of 2019–2050, and Ethiopia is one of them. In the same years, around 2027, India passes China as the most populous country.
