# Where the world's growth is happening — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sub-Saharan Africa adds by 2050 about —
a) 1.1 billion  b) 0.1 billion  c) 11 billion
**Answer: a) 1.1 billion**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** That is what share of world growth?
a) over half  b) a tenth  c) almost none
**Answer: a) over half**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Births to mothers aged 15–19 in 2015–20 numbered —
a) 62 million  b) 6.2 million  c) 620 million
**Answer: a) 62 million**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** India passes China in population around —
a) 2027  b) 2050  c) 2100
**Answer: a) 2027**
