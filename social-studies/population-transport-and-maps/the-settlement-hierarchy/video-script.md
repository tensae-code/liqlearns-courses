# Video script — The Settlement Hierarchy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 7), teaches: Lesson 1: The settlement hierarchy (سلسله‌مراتب سکونتگاه‌ها) — settlements ranked like pupils by weight into classes, each rank richer in services (the European hierarchy picture), and each settlement's sphere of influence (حوزه نفوذ).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
