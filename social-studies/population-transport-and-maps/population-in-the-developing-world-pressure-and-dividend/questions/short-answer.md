# Population in the developing world — pressure and dividend — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What four things cut LDC death rates?
**Answer:** Medicine, piped water, housing and education.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two costs of fast growth.
**Answer:** Strained cities, slums, stretched services and housing (any two).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must governments do to collect the dividend?
**Answer:** Invest in young people's health and education.
