# Population in the developing world — pressure and dividend — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** LDC death rates fell after —
a) the Second World War  b) the First World War  c) the year 2000
**Answer: a) the Second World War**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** African LDCs grew at about —
a) 2.8% a year  b) 0.8% a year  c) 8.2% a year
**Answer: a) 2.8% a year**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The LDC population of 855 million in 2011 is projected toward —
a) 1.67 billion in 2050  b) 0.5 billion  c) 855 million still
**Answer: a) 1.67 billion in 2050**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The demographic dividend opens when —
a) the working-age share swells as fertility falls  b) deaths rise  c) cities empty
**Answer: a) the working-age share swells as fertility falls**
