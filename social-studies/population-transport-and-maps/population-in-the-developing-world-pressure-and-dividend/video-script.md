# Video script — Population in the developing world — pressure and dividend

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After the Second World War, medicine, piped water, housing and education cut death rates in less developed countries while births stayed high.

Fast growth strains cities, services and housing, and builds slums.

As fertility falls, the working-age share swells.
