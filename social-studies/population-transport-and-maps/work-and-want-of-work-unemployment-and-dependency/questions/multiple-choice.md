# Work and want of work — unemployment and dependency — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Minimum working age in Ethiopia is —
a) 18  b) 15  c) 16
**Answer: a) 18**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Internationally, minimum working age is —
a) 15  b) 18  c) 21
**Answer: a) 15**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's worked unemployment rate for 2022 is about —
a) 4%  b) 14%  c) 40%
**Answer: a) 4%**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The urban EDR of 149 means 100 employed people support —
a) 149 dependants  b) 149 workers  c) 49 dependants
**Answer: a) 149 dependants**
