## Sub lesson 1 — Three labour states

Employment, unemployment and underemployment are defined as the three labour states, with minimum working age 15 internationally and 18 in Ethiopia. Unemployment is read as an economic signal — a gauge of the whole economy, not only of the jobless.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia's unemployment sum

Worked in full from the book's Ethiopian figures, the unemployment rate comes to about 4 percent for 2022. The lesson is in the working: a rate is a division, and its meaning lives in what is divided by what.

## Sub lesson 3 — The dependency ratio

The Economic Dependency Ratio for urban Ethiopia in 2018 is 149: every 100 employed people support 149 dependants. The sex-disaggregated table shows women's ratio at 203 against men's 107 — and the unit closes on youth unemployment and the skills mismatch as Africa's policy challenge.
