# Video script — Work and want of work — unemployment and dependency

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Employment, unemployment and underemployment are defined as the three labour states, with minimum working age 15 internationally and 18 in Ethiopia.

Worked in full from the book's Ethiopian figures, the unemployment rate comes to about 4 percent for 2022.

The Economic Dependency Ratio for urban Ethiopia in 2018 is 149: every 100 employed people support 149 dependants.
