# Hands-on — Political Borders Land And Sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 2 Lesson 2, Political Borders: political borders are lines determined and drawn by people to separate one state's sovereignty from another's; they are of two kinds, land borders and sea borders; the Uqair Conference of 1922 settled the border question between Kuwait, Iraq and Najd (today's Saudi Arabia), setting clear, fixed international borders for the three states and a historical reference map. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
