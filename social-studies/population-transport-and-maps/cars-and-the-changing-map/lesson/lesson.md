## Sub lesson 1 — Cars And The Changing Map

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 46), teaches: Lesson 3: Road transport changing geographical space — the world's growing wish for a private car, some families owning more than one, and the fuel use and pollution that follow; the road-atlas map activity; the statistics of Iran's road transport to the end of 1401 (2023). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
