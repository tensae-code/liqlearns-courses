The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 46), teaches: Lesson 3: Road transport changing geographical space — the world's growing wish for a private car, some families owning more than one, and the fuel use and pollution that follow; the road-atlas map activity; the statistics of Iran's road transport to the end of 1401 (2023).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
