# Neo-Malthusians and their critics

## Do it first

Someone says a poor family's poverty is caused by its size alone. Write one thing this claim explains well and one thing it leaves out. The critics in this lesson start from your second answer.

## Sub lesson 1 — The neo-Malthusian update

Neo-Malthusians keep Malthus's diagnosis: population growth as the main cause of poverty, resting with the poor's reproduction. What they replace is the remedy: birth control instead of moral restraint.

As evidence they point to recurrent famine — Ethiopia is named — reading hunger as population's verdict on itself. It is Malthus modernised: same crisis, new instrument.

## Sub lesson 2 — The critics' six counts

The unit then gives the prosecution six counts. One: the view diverts attention from poverty's social and economic causes. Two: it gives the better-off an excuse not to improve the poor's living conditions. Three: world food production has in fact outpaced the predictions.

Four: many developing nations now challenge the framing. Five: fertility is better explained by development conditions than by reproduction alone. Six: the view treats population size in isolation from distribution and consumption — how much there is and who gets it matter, not only how many mouths.
