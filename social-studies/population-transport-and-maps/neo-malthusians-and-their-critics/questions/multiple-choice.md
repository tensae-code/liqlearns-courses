# Neo-Malthusians and their critics — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Neo-Malthusians keep Malthus's…
a) remedy  b) diagnosis — population growth as poverty's main cause  c) dates
**Answer: b) diagnosis — population growth as poverty's main cause**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Their remedy is…
a) moral restraint  b) birth control  c) emigration only
**Answer: b) birth control**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Their named famine evidence includes…
a) Ethiopia  b) Ireland only  c) no country
**Answer: a) Ethiopia**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Critic count one: the view diverts attention from…
a) weather  b) poverty's social and economic causes  c) maps
**Answer: b) poverty's social and economic causes**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Critic count three says world food production has…
a) collapsed  b) outpaced the predictions  c) stopped
**Answer: b) outpaced the predictions**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Count six faults treating population size apart from…
a) distribution and consumption  b) altitude  c) language
**Answer: a) distribution and consumption**
