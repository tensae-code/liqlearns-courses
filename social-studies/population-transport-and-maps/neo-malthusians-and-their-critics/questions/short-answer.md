# Neo-Malthusians and their critics — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do neo-Malthusians differ from Malthus himself?
**Answer:** Same diagnosis (population growth causes poverty), different remedy: birth control replaces moral restraint.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give any three of the six critic counts.
**Answer:** Any three of: diverts attention from social/economic causes; excuses the better-off; food production outpaced predictions; developing nations challenge the framing; fertility follows development conditions; size is treated apart from distribution and consumption.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What famine example do neo-Malthusians point to?
**Answer:** Recurrent famine, with Ethiopia named.
