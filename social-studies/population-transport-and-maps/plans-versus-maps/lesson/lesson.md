# Plans Versus Maps

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the difference between a plan and a map, as Ecuador's Grade 3 *Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016)* teaches it in Diferencia entre plano y mapa on printed p. 20. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — How a plan differs from a map

Grade 3 draws the line plainly: a plano (plan) shows a small place in detail, as if seen from just above it; a mapa (map) shows a large place — a province, a country, the world — shrunk far down, so detail must be chosen. The diferencia entre plano y mapa is a matter of size and detail: the bigger the ground, the smaller the drawing's scale, and the more the drawer must select what to show.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the older SM edition is a classic skills-first book — a whole opening unit on orientation, cardinal points, the compass rose and plans before any content; every unit runs the same skeleton (content lessons, “Ser Estudiante” test, Cultura del Buen Vivir, scientific work, digital skills, unit evaluation), and the country is learned parish → canton → province → nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
