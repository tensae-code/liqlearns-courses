# Video script — Maps and globes — the globe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Hold a ball in your hands — a football will do. The Earth is shaped like it, only flattened a breath at the poles. Every map you will ever use is this ball flattened somehow. Meet the ball first: this is the globe.

This lesson teaches: the globe as the Earth's true-shape model — what it shows truly, and its four uses. Watch, then do it yourself — the steps are in the lesson.
