# Hands-on — Maps and globes — the globe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Spin a globe or ball west to east and say which lands meet the light first.

## Activity 2
Shine a torch on a ball in a dark room: mark the day side and the night side.

## Activity 3
Find the air route between two far cities on a globe with a piece of string.

## With a partner
Tell your companion why a globe's shapes are true, and name its four uses.
