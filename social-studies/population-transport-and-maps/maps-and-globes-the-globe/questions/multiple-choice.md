# Maps and globes — the globe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** A globe shows land and water bodies —
a) Stretched flat  b) Upside down  c) Without oceans  d) In their true shapes and places
**Answer: d) In their true shapes and places**

**2.** Spinning a globe shows the Earth rotates —
a) East to west  b) North to south  c) West to east  d) It does not rotate
**Answer: c) West to east**

**3.** Which is NOT one of the globe's four uses?
a) Seeing day and night  b) Printing books  c) Telling climate zones  d) Finding air and sea routes
**Answer: b) Printing books**

**4.** A globe helps tell climate zones because it shows —
a) The bands around the Earth truly  b) Only one town  c) Tomorrow's weather  d) River names only
**Answer: a) The bands around the Earth truly**
