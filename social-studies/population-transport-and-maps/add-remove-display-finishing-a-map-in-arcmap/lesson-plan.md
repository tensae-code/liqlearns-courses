# Lesson plan — Add, remove, display — finishing a map in ArcMap

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Population, Transport and Maps · **Lesson:** Add, remove, display — finishing a map in ArcMap
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 11 Geography, Unit 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Data is added by dragging from ArcCatalog or by the Add Data button; layers order points above lines above polygons above rasters; data view explores, layout view finishes the page.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in Population, Transport and Maps, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
