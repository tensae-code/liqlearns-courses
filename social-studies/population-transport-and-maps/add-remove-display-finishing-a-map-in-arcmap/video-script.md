# Video script — Add, remove, display — finishing a map in ArcMap

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Add data two ways: drag it in from the ArcCatalog window, or use the Add Data button, with its Add Basemap and ArcGIS Online routes.

The Table of Contents orders layers by kind: points above lines, lines above polygons, polygons above rasters.

Data view works in real-world coordinates, for exploring and querying.
