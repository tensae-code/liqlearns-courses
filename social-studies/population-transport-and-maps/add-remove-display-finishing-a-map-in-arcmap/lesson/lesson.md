# Add, remove, display — finishing a map in ArcMap

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: a map is finished when its layers stack right and its page reads clean — how is that done? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Adding and removing data

Add data two ways: drag it in from the ArcCatalog window, or use the Add Data button, with its Add Basemap and ArcGIS Online routes. Remove a layer by right-clicking it and choosing Remove.

## Sub lesson 2 — The stacking order

The Table of Contents orders layers by kind: points above lines, lines above polygons, polygons above rasters. Drawn in that order, small things stay visible over big ones — the stacking is a readability rule, not a preference.

## Sub lesson 3 — Two views, and the project

Data view works in real-world coordinates, for exploring and querying. Layout view works in page space, for titles, north arrows and scale bars. The unit's project closes the course's map work: produce maps of Ethiopia and of a zone or district, and generate point, line and polygon features from GPS field data.
