# How many people will there be? Reading population projections

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The count so far

World population reached 1 billion in 1800 and 7.7 billion in 2019. The charts come from the UN's World Population Prospects 2019.

[Picture 27039 — the population-size and growth-rate chart, 1950 to 2100]

## Sub lesson 2 — The medium road to 2100

On the medium variant, the projection runs to 8.5 billion in 2030, 9.7 billion in 2050 and 10.7 billion in 2100. There is a stated 27 percent chance of a plateau or fall before 2100 — a projection is a reasoned path, not a promise.

## Sub lesson 3 — Fertility is the engine

Fertility falls from 2.5 births per woman in 2019 toward 1.9 in 2100. It is already below replacement in regions holding half of humanity — but still 4.6 in Sub-Saharan Africa.
