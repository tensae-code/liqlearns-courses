Colombia's own way (CS_Grado07 Secundaria Activa): Secundaria Activa organises Grade 7 as climate first (with climate change and hydrometeorological threats), then a paired physical-and-human geography of Europe and America — continents taught as physical environments plus population — before the history units.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela): a final-year economic geography of Venezuela alone — population past/present/future, human development in numbers, then resource by resource (minerals, land, fisheries, forests, oil, water/hydroelectricity) each read through sovereignty, collective participation and environmental conscience, closing on Latin American union.

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 10 Geography): Class X pairs deep physical theory (Big Bang, plate tectonics, the making of the Himalayas) with an applied Bhutan block — climate and vegetation zones of Bhutan, Bhutanese agriculture's challenges and advancement, industries and their distribution, and a Bhutan disaster chapter that adds Education in Emergency.
