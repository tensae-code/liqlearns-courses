# How many people will there be? Reading population projections — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three projected totals and their years.
**Answer:** 8.5 billion (2030), 9.7 billion (2050), 10.7 billion (2100).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Whose projections are these?
**Answer:** The UN's World Population Prospects 2019.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How does fertility change across the century?
**Answer:** It falls from 2.5 toward 1.9 births per woman.
