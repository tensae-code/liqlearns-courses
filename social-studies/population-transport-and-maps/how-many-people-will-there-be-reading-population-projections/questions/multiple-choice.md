# How many people will there be? Reading population projections — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** World population in 2019 was —
a) 7.7 billion  b) 1 billion  c) 9.7 billion
**Answer: a) 7.7 billion**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1-billion mark was reached in —
a) 1800  b) 1900  c) 2019
**Answer: a) 1800**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The medium variant projects 2050 at —
a) 9.7 billion  b) 8.5 billion  c) 10.7 billion
**Answer: a) 9.7 billion**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Fertility in Sub-Saharan Africa stands at —
a) 4.6 births per woman  b) 1.9  c) 2.5
**Answer: a) 4.6 births per woman**
