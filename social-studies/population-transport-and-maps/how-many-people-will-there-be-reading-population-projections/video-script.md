# Video script — How many people will there be? Reading population projections

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

World population reached 1 billion in 1800 and 7.7 billion in 2019.

On the medium variant, the projection runs to 8.5 billion in 2030, 9.7 billion in 2050 and 10.7 billion in 2100.

Fertility falls from 2.5 births per woman in 2019 toward 1.9 in 2100.
