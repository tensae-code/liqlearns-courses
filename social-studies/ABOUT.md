# About the Social Studies course

**The first page (what learners are told):** everything in this
course comes from government textbooks — this part is built from
Ethiopia's own Social Studies books for Grades 7 and 8, read in
full, page by page. The one exception is the **Tip boxes**: a tip
in a box is from us, the people making LiqLearns. Tips are ours;
the lessons are the books'.

## Where the course comes from

Social Studies is one world course: the Earth, its peoples and
their past are the same everywhere, and each country's way of
teaching them shows inside as examples. The first way stocked is
Ethiopia's: the books run geography → history → today's world,
activity first, and that way is kept as teaching throughout this
part of the course. Inside the course the path is organised by
**levels, not grades** — the school grades the books come from
appear only in each lesson's sources (provenance), never as the
course's organisation.

## The books (Ethiopia, Social Studies)

Book entries are named in full in each lesson's
`lesson/sources.md`. The set in hand for this build:

- *Social Studies Student Textbook, Grade 7* — Amhara National
  Regional State Education Bureau, © 2015 E.C. Complete, read in
  full (printed page = PDF page − 8).
- *Social Studies Student Textbook, Grade 8* — Amhara National
  Regional State Education Bureau, © 2015 E.C. Complete, read in
  full (printed page = PDF page − 9).
- *Social Studies Teacher's Guide, Grade 7* — Addis Ababa City
  Administration Education Bureau, first edition 2022 (2014
  E.C.). Complete, read in full. **Teacher evidence only.**
- *Social Studies Teacher's Guide, Grade 8* — Addis Ababa City
  Administration Education Bureau, 2015 E.C. **TRUNCATED: only
  printed pp. 1–47 are held — Units 1–3 complete; Units 4–7
  (printed pp. 48–109) are missing.** Teacher evidence only, and
  only for Units 1–3. A complete copy of this guide is still
  owed. Every Grade 8 lesson from Unit 4 on rests on the student
  book alone and is marked "no teacher evidence yet" in its
  sources; the truncated guide contributes nothing there and no
  source claims it does.

**Pairing warning, carried honestly:** the two teacher guides are
Addis Ababa bureau guides set against Amhara bureau student
books — they are NOT edition-matched. Unit order and coverage
diverge: whole student topics have no guide counterpart, and
whole guide topics have no student counterpart. Pupil content is
stocked from the student books; guide material appears only as
labelled teacher evidence, and guide-only topics stand as their
own lessons on teacher-guide evidence, counted nowhere.

**Book errors are taught corrected**, with the error noted in the
lesson sources: the Grade 7 book's Sun "circumference" figure
(that is its diameter), its Figure 8 caption date "2201" (the
body says 2001), its desert list numbered 1, 2, 2, 3, and its
period moon counts (flagged as dated); the Grade 8 book's siege
of Mekelle date (January 1896, printed 1895), Maychew date
(March 31, 1936, printed May 31), OAU charter date (May 25,
1963, printed June 25), its Accra 1958 count, and its CFC
wording. The Grade 8 Chapter 1 tables whose rows are
deliberately blank are kept as the library-completion tasks the
book sets — the blanks are the task, not data. Grade 8 Chapter 7
green-economy dates are Ethiopian calendar dates and stay
labelled E.C. The guides' own errors (including the Grade 7
guide's garbled Activity 1.2 rotation key and §7.2 "overload"
definition, and the Grade 8 guide's wrong mesosphere key) are
never taught from; where a guide key contradicts itself (the
Grade 8 guide's tax-history key) the tension is carried honestly
in the lesson sources.

**The syllabus as pacing reference (teacher evidence):** the
Grade 7 guide closes with the full Social Studies syllabus
(printed pp. 119–142): a content flowchart setting Grade 7
against Grade 8 for every thematic area, the minimum learning
competencies per unit for both grades, and the Grade 7 syllabus
proper — outcomes, competencies, contents, learning strategies
and assessment per unit, with the period budget per unit. It is
filed here as the course's pacing and Grade 8-progression
reference (teacher evidence): it confirms, for example, that
Grade 8 takes the forces shaping the Earth's surface, the
atmosphere and weather/climate, slavery and colonialism, and
modern Ethiopian history — the ground the later titles of this
course build on. Where the syllabus promises content the Amhara
student book lacks (crust materials, human evolution, optimum
population), the guide placements file it as guide-only content
and the lessons say so.

**Acceptance reckoning:** this part of the course is taught in
1 country (Ethiopia) from its own student books, except the
lessons whose sources state they stand on teacher-guide evidence
only — those are counted nowhere, and say so. The books'
reference shelves (Grade 7 placement 118, Grade 8 placement
149) are recorded here and in the lesson sources they touch;
they are provenance, not lessons.
