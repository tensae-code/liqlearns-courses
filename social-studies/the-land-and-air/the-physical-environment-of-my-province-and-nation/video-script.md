The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Papua New Guinea's book, the *Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG* (printed p. 56), teaches: Strand 4 Unit 1: The physical environment of the province (and of the province and nation) — features and map of the child's own province, Lessons 122–134 (teacher guide — evidence).

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): the five strands widen again — from district (Grade 4) to the physical environment of the province and nation; resources are taught through sustainable use (re-using water, reduce/reuse projects) and map work culminates in drawing sketch maps of the school and locality. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only, outside acceptance counts.
