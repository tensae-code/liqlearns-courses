# Video script — Volcano shapes — shield, strato, cinder cone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A volcano's shape is its lava's signature: fluid basalt spreads into broad shields, sticky lava and ash stack into steep strato cones, and loose cinders pile into small cones. First, shield and strato: Volcano form follows the lava and the eruption style. Then, cinder cones: Cinder cones are the smallest of the three: hills of loose, cinder-sized fragments thrown out and piled around the vent. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
