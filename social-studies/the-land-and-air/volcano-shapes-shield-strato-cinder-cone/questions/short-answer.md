# Volcano shapes — shield, strato, cinder cone — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are shield volcanoes so broad and low?
**Answer:** Their basalt lava is fluid and runs far before hardening, so each flow spreads thin and wide.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two materials alternate in a strato volcano?
**Answer:** Lava and pyroclastic material, layer on layer.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What sets a cinder cone's steepness?
**Answer:** The angle of repose of its loose cinder-sized fragments.
