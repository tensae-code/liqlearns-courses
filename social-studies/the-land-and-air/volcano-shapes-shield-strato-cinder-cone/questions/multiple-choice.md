# Volcano shapes — shield, strato, cinder cone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shield volcano sides slope at under…
a) 7 degrees  b) 45 degrees  c) 90 degrees
**Answer: a) 7 degrees**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Shield volcanoes form from…
a) viscous lava  b) fluid basalt  c) loose cinders only
**Answer: b) fluid basalt**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Hawaiian Islands rose how far from the sea floor?
a) 500 m  b) 5 km  c) 50 km
**Answer: b) 5 km**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The commonest volcano type is…
a) shield  b) cinder cone  c) strato
**Answer: c) strato**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Strato cones alternate layers of…
a) lava and pyroclastic material  b) sand and water  c) ice and rock
**Answer: a) lava and pyroclastic material**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A cinder cone is held at its…
a) boiling point  b) angle of repose  c) sea level
**Answer: b) angle of repose**
