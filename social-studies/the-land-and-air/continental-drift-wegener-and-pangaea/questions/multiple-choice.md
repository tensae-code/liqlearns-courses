# Continental drift — Wegener and Pangaea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pangaea was…
a) one supercontinent joining all land  b) a single great ocean  c) the first ocean
**Answer: a) one supercontinent joining all land**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pangaea stood centred near…
a) the North Pole  b) the South Pole  c) the equator
**Answer: b) the South Pole**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Pangaea split in the Triassic into…
a) Africa and Asia  b) Laurasia and Gondwanaland  c) seven continents at once
**Answer: b) Laurasia and Gondwanaland**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wegener proposed the drift theory in…
a) 1812  b) 1912  c) 1962
**Answer: b) 1912**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The drift theory says…
a) continents are fixed for ever  b) only oceans move  c) the continents themselves move
**Answer: c) the continents themselves move**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Laurasia was the…
a) northern land  b) southern land  c) ocean floor
**Answer: a) northern land**
