# Continental drift — Wegener and Pangaea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two lands Pangaea split into.
**Answer:** Laurasia in the north and Gondwanaland in the south.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** When did all land stand joined as Pangaea?
**Answer:** In the Carboniferous, about 350 million years ago.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who proposed continental drift, and when?
**Answer:** Alfred Wegener, in 1912.
