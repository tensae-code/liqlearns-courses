# Video script — Continental drift — Wegener and Pangaea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

About 350 million years ago, all the land on Earth stood joined as one supercontinent. Its name was Pangaea, and it sat centred near the South Pole. Then, in the Triassic, Pangaea split into two great lands: Laurasia in the north and Gondwanaland in the south. In 1912, Alfred Wegener looked at the map and said the bold thing out loud: the continents themselves move. That is the continental drift theory — and everything in this unit grows out of it.
