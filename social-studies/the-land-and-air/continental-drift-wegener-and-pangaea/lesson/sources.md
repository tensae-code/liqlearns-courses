Bolivia's own way (Grade 7, Secundaria 1): The Bolivian Texto de Aprendizaje runs every unit through the same four moments — Práctica, Teoría, Valoración, Producción — and frames geography inside the plurinational, Madre Tierra worldview: continental drift and geological time sit beside the Laws of Mother Earth (071 and 300), the human right to water, and a closing general geography of Bolivia itself (regions, orography, political map, salt flats and lithium).

Bolivia's own way (teacher guide — evidence): The teacher compendium teaches the same four-moment lesson shape (Práctica, Teoría, Valoración, Producción) with QR-linked videos and local openings — a La Paz landslide video opens the geography unit — and it adds two geography units the sampled student books do not carry at those grades: the scientific development of geographic science with cartography and coordinates (year 3), and water as a human right with Andean water management (year 4). That evidence is stocked here as the way Bolivia teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 10): This is the physical-geography year: the universe and Earth's layers, continental drift with volcanoes and earthquakes — including earthquake-prevention measures as a group task — water and winds across the continents, and a closing unit on political and economic geography that treats borders, geopolitical order and international climate agreements as geography, with hurricane-season citizen responsibility as the water unit's group activity.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 13 Geography): The final year integrates everything at advanced level (plate tectonics to urban models, largely from world texts such as Waugh) and then lands it in Chapter 8, South Pacific Islands Geography — Oceania's geology, island types, development prospects and regionalism.

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 6 Geografiya): Geography in Grade 6 is the physical geography of continents and oceans in the Russian/Soviet textbook tradition — first the Earth's geographic envelope and the World Ocean, then every continent in a fixed order (position, geological structure and relief, climate, inland waters, natural zones, population), closing with the population of the Earth and the political map of the world; nearly every chapter ends in a practical work.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 7 G7 Geography Humanidad): a Geohistoria (Ramón Tovar) approach — geographic space is always explained through the historical conditions that built it; every chapter opens with a literary epigraph, works through "Entérate / Entretejiendo saberes / Indagando" boxes, and keeps returning to the learner's own community and to Caracas as the worked example.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 9 Student Book): widening-circles design — the world continent by continent — hazards and geographic defence first (Hurricane Mitch), territory versus population, then geographic tours of Europe, Asia, Africa and Oceania, closing on a warming world; Bloque 4 adds world population, the world and Honduran economies and migration. Every lesson runs the same 'Sembrar y cosechar' activity cycle.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 9 Teacher Guide) (teacher guide — evidence): widening-circles design — the world continent by continent — hazards and geographic defence first (Hurricane Mitch), territory versus population, then geographic tours of Europe, Asia, Africa and Oceania, closing on a warming world; Bloque 4 adds world population, the world and Honduran economies and migration — the teacher guide mirrors the student book lesson-for-lesson (same 38 lessons and blocks) and adds didactics, theory summaries and answer keys; its geography lessons are placed here at the guide's own printed pages. Every lesson runs the same 'Sembrar y cosechar' activity cycle. That evidence is stocked here as the way Honduras teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Honduras is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 3): a systematic geomorphology course — rocks and Earth's interior first, then isostasy, continental drift and plate tectonics, then internal agents (folds, faults, earthquakes, volcanoes in four lessons), then external agents and the modelled landscapes of hot, temperate, cold and coastal environments, closing with the geological cycle.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G12 ES T1 Workbook (10385)): the activities book opens the final year with the greenhouse effect modelled on the bench and acid rain simulated, then structures, plate tectonics through the Dead Sea transform, and geological exploration with Jordan-flavoured map work.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G12 ES T1): the final year opens with fossil fuels and climate action, then structures and plate tectonics, closing with geological exploration — ending on uranium exploration in Jordan.

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 6 Estudios Sociales G6): The primary cycle closes at world scale: after the planet unit, Unit II treats the whole American continent and Unit III repeats the same template — position, political division, relief, resources, hydrography, climate, risk areas, economic activities — for Europe, Asia, Africa and Oceania; the remaining five units are world and Latin American History. Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Unit IV first human groups and the Neolithic (printed pp. 98–113, History); Unit V ancient civilisations to the medieval world (printed pp. 118–134, History); Unit VI the modern world (printed pp. 140–164, History); Unit VII transformations of the 18th–19th centuries (printed pp. 168–183, History); Unit VIII the world in the 20th century (printed pp. 188–204, History).

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 7 Geology-Geography Workbook): the Gymnasium workbook is a project book, not drills — pupils build a solar-system model, draw a relief profile from the Vouraikos topographic map, argue the Aral Sea case, retell ecosystems as comics, build an imaginary city, study the Ruhr, and "fly over" each continent from satellite images; it ends with blank outline maps to photocopy and enlarge.
