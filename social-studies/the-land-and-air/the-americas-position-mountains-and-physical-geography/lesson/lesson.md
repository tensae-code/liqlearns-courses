## Sub lesson 1 — The Americas Position Mountains And Physical Geography

This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 8 Geography (Dari)): the continent-by-continent march continues — after Asia in Grade 7, Grade 8 does Europe then the Americas, each continent in the same fixed order: position, mountains, rivers, lakes, seas, gulfs, climate, political geography, human geography (race, language, religion), economic geography, mines/industries, transport.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)): primary social studies starts from the child's own world — family, village, city — and only then opens the map: directions, Afghanistan's boundaries and mountains and rivers, the continents and oceans, and finally the Earth's shape, equator, latitude/longitude and movements. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
