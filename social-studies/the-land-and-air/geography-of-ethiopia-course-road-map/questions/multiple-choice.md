# Geography of Ethiopia — course road map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many units does the course have?
a) six  b) ten  c) eight
**Answer: c) eight**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Unit 1 studies —
a) trade  b) geological history and topography  c) population
**Answer: b) geological history and topography**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Each unit closes with —
a) a summary and a review exercise  b) a field trip  c) an exam from another country
**Answer: a) a summary and a review exercise**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A box at the start of each unit gives —
a) exam answers  b) a map of Africa  c) key terms
**Answer: c) key terms**
