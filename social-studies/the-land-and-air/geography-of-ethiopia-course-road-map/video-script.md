# Video script — Geography of Ethiopia — course road map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This course is one country, studied whole.
First, how its land was made. Then its climate. Then its riches — rivers, soils, forests.
Then its people, and their work. Then how people and land change each other.
Then today’s big questions, and the Green Legacy. Last, the map skills to read it all.
Eight units. One country. Ethiopia.
