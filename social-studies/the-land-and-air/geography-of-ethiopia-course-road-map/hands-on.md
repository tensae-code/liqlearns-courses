# Hands-on — Geography of Ethiopia — course road map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the road map
Draw a big outline of Ethiopia on paper. Write the eight unit names around it, each beside the part of the country it studies most — Unit 1 by the mountains, Unit 3 by a river, Unit 8 in the middle as the skill that reads all the rest.

## Find the rhythm
Take any school book you have. Find its opening recall, its outcomes or aims, and its closing summary or exercises. The pattern is the same one this course uses — point to each part and name it.
