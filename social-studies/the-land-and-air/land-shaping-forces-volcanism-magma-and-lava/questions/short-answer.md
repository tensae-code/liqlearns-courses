# Land-Shaping Forces — Volcanism, Magma and Lava — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name one thing that can ease the pressure on deep rock.
**Answer:** Any one of: folding, faulting, plate movement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happens to lava on the surface?
**Answer:** It cools back into solid rock.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the difference between intrusive and extrusive hardening?
**Answer:** Intrusive rock hardens inside the crust; extrusive rock hardens on the surface.
