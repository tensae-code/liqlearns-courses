# Land-Shaping Forces — Volcanism, Magma and Lava — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Volcanism is the process by which deep rock is —
a) frozen in place  b) forced into the crust or out onto the surface  c) washed to the sea
**Answer: b) forced into the crust or out onto the surface**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Melted rock underground is called —
a) lava  b) magma  c) ash
**Answer: b) magma**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Once melted rock reaches the surface it is —
a) magma  b) lava  c) a horst
**Answer: b) lava**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Volcanoes are classified as —
a) hot, warm, cold  b) active, dormant, extinct  c) tall, middle, short
**Answer: b) active, dormant, extinct**
