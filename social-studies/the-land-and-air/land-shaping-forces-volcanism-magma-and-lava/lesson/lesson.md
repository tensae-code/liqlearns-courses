## Sub lesson 1 — The whole process

Volcanism is the whole process by which molten, fluid or gassy deep rock is forced into the crust or out onto the surface. Deep rock stays partly solid under the crust's pressure; where folding, faulting or plate movement eases that pressure, the rock melts fully and rises through weak places.

## Sub lesson 2 — Magma below, lava above

Melted rock underground is magma. Once it reaches the surface it is lava — and there it cools back into solid rock. One rock melt, two names, and the boundary between them is the Earth's surface.
