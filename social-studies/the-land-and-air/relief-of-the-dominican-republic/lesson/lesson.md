## Sub lesson 1 — Relief of the Dominican Republic

Dominican Republic's Grade 3 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Tercer Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 30), teaches: The reliefs of my country: cordilleras, sierras and valleys. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 3): For third graders geography is the home island first: the planet and its components, then the Antilles and the relief of their own country, then countryside versus city, and finally where their own families came from — migration and a family census the children run themselves. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
