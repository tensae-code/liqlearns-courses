# Video script — Rain shadows and why seasons move

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Moist air meets a mountain. It must climb. It rains.
Over the top, it descends — warm and dry. A rain-shadow.
And the whole sky pattern moves with the year:
a tilted earth, circling the sun.
