# Rain shadows and why seasons move — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is a rain-shadow?
**Answer: The dry strip on the leeward side of a mountain barrier, where descended air has warmed and dried.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two earth facts.
**Answer: The earth’s revolution around the sun, and the tilt of its axis.**
