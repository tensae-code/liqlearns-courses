# Rain shadows and why seasons move — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The windward side is —
a) the sheltered side  b) the valley floor  c) the side the moist air climbs
**Answer: c) the side the moist air climbs**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Air descending on the leeward side —
a) freezes  b) warms and dries  c) cools and rains
**Answer: b) warms and dries**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The seasons are driven by —
a) revolution and axial tilt  b) ocean currents alone  c) volcanoes
**Answer: a) revolution and axial tilt**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** As the overhead sun migrates, —
a) the lapse rate changes  b) mountains move  c) pressure and wind belts shift with it
**Answer: c) pressure and wind belts shift with it**
