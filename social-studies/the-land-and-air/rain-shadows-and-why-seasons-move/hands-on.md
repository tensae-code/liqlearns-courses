# Hands-on — Rain shadows and why seasons move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sponge mountain
Blow damp breath (or use steam from a kettle, with care) against a cold slanted board: watch where the moisture gathers on the windward side. Sketch the rain-shadow on the dry far side.

## Tilt model
Tilt a ball on a stick toward a lamp and walk it around the lamp. Note which pole leans to the light in each quarter — that lean is the season maker.
