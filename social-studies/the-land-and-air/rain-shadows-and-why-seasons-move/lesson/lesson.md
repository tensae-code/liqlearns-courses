# Rain shadows and why seasons move

## Do it first

Stand on a ridge in the rains: one side walks in cloud, the other in dry wind. Same mountain, two climates. Read the rain shadow — and why the whole pattern moves with the seasons.

## Sub lesson 1 — Windward and leeward

A mountain barrier forces moist air to rise on its windward side; rising air cools and drops its rain there. Descending on the leeward side, the air warms and dries — leaving a rain-shadow, a dry strip in the mountain’s lee.

## Sub lesson 2 — Revolution, tilt, moving seasons

The earth’s revolution around the sun, with its axis tilted, drives the seasons: the overhead sun migrates, and the pressure and wind belts shift with it through the year. Ethiopia’s three seasons are this global movement read locally.
