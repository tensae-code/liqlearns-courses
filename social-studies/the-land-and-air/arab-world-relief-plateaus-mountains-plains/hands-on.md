# Hands-on — Arab World Relief Plateaus Mountains Plains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch.2: الهضاب — Plateaus of the Arab World; Ch.2: الجبال — Mountains of the Arab World; Ch.2: السهول — Plains of the Arab World; Ch.1: تضاريس الجانب الإفريقي — Relief of the African side: the Saharan plateau (raised edges, low basins, dry wadis, sand dunes, serir gravel plains, hamada); Ch.1: السلاسل الجبلية الإفريقية — The African mountain chains: Atlas (northern and southern), the Western Mountain and the Green Mountain of Libya, and the western Red Sea mountains; Ch.1: الهضاب والسهول الإفريقية — African-side plateaus (Moroccan Meseta, Chotts, Butnan) and plains (flood plains; coastal plains on the Atlantic, Mediterranean and Red Sea); Ch.1: تضاريس الجانب الآسيوي — Relief of the Asian side: the Arabian Peninsula plateau (Badiyat al-Sham, Najd, Hadramawt), faulted chains (Levant, Hijaz/Asir/Yemen), folded chains (Kurdistan–Zagros, Oman), and plains. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
