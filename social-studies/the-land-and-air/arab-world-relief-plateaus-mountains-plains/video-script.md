The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 8 LY G8 Arab World* (printed p. 21), teaches: Ch.2: الهضاب — Plateaus of the Arab World. Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 8 LY G8 Arab World* (printed p. 25), teaches: Ch.2: الجبال — Mountains of the Arab World. Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 8 LY G8 Arab World* (printed p. 28), teaches: Ch.2: السهول — Plains of the Arab World. Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World* (printed p. 24), teaches: Ch.1: تضاريس الجانب الإفريقي — Relief of the African side: the Saharan plateau (raised edges, low basins, dry wadis, sand dunes, serir gravel plains, hamada). Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World* (printed p. 28), teaches: Ch.1: السلاسل الجبلية الإفريقية — The African mountain chains: Atlas (northern and southern), the Western Mountain and the Green Mountain of Libya, and the western Red Sea mountains. Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World* (printed p. 31), teaches: Ch.1: الهضاب والسهول الإفريقية — African-side plateaus (Moroccan Meseta, Chotts, Butnan) and plains (flood plains; coastal plains on the Atlantic, Mediterranean and Red Sea). Libya's book, the *Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World* (printed p. 34), teaches: Ch.1: تضاريس الجانب الآسيوي — Relief of the Asian side: the Arabian Peninsula plateau (Badiyat al-Sham, Najd, Hadramawt), faulted chains (Levant, Hijaz/Asir/Yemen), folded chains (Kurdistan–Zagros, Oman), and plains.

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 8 LY G8 Arab World): The scale widens from Libya (Grade 7) to the whole Arab homeland: one regional geography — location and strategic importance, relief, climate, vegetation, population, economic resources and transport — closed by a full unit on the Arab food problem and food security.

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World): The Grade 8 Arab World survey returns at secondary depth in two parts — physical (relief treated separately for the African and Asian sides, climate factors and regions, soils, vegetation, water with its problems) and human (population structure and problems, agriculture and its difficulties, crop-by-crop chapters, livestock and sea wealth, minerals metal by metal, oil and gas, alternative energy, industry, transport).
