# The Ethiopian and Eritrean Highlands — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are the two highlands treated together?
**Answer: The same uplifted dome and flood-basalt lavas built both.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one thing the joint reading explains.
**Answer: The Tekeze drainage, the eastern escarpment, or how highland climates grade into the lowlands.**
