# The Ethiopian and Eritrean Highlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Eritrean highlands are —
a) built by a different uplift  b) younger volcanic islands  c) the northern continuation of the Ethiopian highland system
**Answer: c) the northern continuation of the Ethiopian highland system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Both highlands were built by —
a) wind erosion  b) uplift and flood-basalt lavas  c) river deposition
**Answer: b) uplift and flood-basalt lavas**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The river system named as draining across the region is —
a) the Tekeze system  b) the Genale system  c) the Baro system
**Answer: a) the Tekeze system**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia’s three-region frame is —
a) unrelated to Eritrea  b) a coastal frame  c) the south part of this bigger highland picture
**Answer: c) the south part of this bigger highland picture**
