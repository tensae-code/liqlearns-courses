# Hands-on — The Ethiopian and Eritrean Highlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Border-blind drawing
Draw the northern highlands without drawing any border. Mark the escarpment and the Tekeze. Then draw the border in — and say what the border changes and what it cannot change.

## Explain it
Explain to a partner, in three sentences, why a geography course treats the two highlands together.
