# Plate tectonics — the Earth's moving shell — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two layers make up the lithosphere?
**Answer:** The crust plus the upper mantle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How fast do plates move?
**Answer:** A few centimetres a year.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one reach of plate tectonics beyond landforms.
**Answer:** Any one of: it influences the composition of the atmosphere and oceans, long-term climate, or the environment in which life evolves.
