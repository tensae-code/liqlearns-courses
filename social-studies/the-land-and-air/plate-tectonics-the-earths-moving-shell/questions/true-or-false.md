# Plate tectonics — the Earth's moving shell — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** There are about a dozen large plates, plus smaller ones.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The asthenosphere is stronger and more rigid than the lithosphere.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Plate processes can influence long-term climate.
**Answer: True**
