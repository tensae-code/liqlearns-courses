# Plate tectonics — the Earth's moving shell — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lithosphere is…
a) crust plus upper mantle  b) the core only  c) the oceans
**Answer: a) crust plus upper mantle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Plates slide over the…
a) inner core  b) asthenosphere  c) atmosphere
**Answer: b) asthenosphere**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Plates move about…
a) a few metres a day  b) a few centimetres a year  c) not at all
**Answer: b) a few centimetres a year**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A plate may carry…
a) continent only  b) ocean floor only  c) continent, ocean floor, or both
**Answer: c) continent, ocean floor, or both**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Plate edges…
a) always match continent edges  b) do not coincide with continent edges  c) follow country borders
**Answer: b) do not coincide with continent edges**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The North American Plate includes…
a) half the North Atlantic floor  b) no ocean at all  c) the whole Pacific
**Answer: a) half the North Atlantic floor**
