# Video script — Plate tectonics — the Earth's moving shell

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Earth's outer shell is broken into rigid plates of lithosphere that slide slowly over the weaker asthenosphere beneath. First, lithosphere on asthenosphere: From the late 1960s, one theory has been accepted: plate tectonics. Then, what a plate can carry: A plate may carry continent, ocean floor, or both. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
