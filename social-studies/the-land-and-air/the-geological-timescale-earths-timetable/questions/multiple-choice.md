# The geological timescale — Earth's timetable — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The geological timescale was built by studying —
a) rocks and the fossils in them  b) clouds  c) ocean tides
**Answer: a) rocks and the fossils in them**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The largest divisions of the timescale are —
a) eons  b) epochs  c) periods
**Answer: a) eons**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Eras divide into —
a) periods  b) eons  c) oceans
**Answer: a) periods**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Periods divide into —
a) epochs  b) eons  c) eras
**Answer: a) epochs**
