# The geological timescale — Earth's timetable — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the geological timescale?
**Answer:** The timetable of Earth's history, built from rocks and fossils.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the divisions from largest to smallest.
**Answer:** Eons, eras, periods, epochs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why learn the timescale first?
**Answer:** Because every other deep-time topic takes its place inside it.
