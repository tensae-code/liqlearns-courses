## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the book's own teaching way

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 1 G1 CS Viva Venezuela): critical, question-first pedagogy ("pedagogía crítica") — spatial notions (up/down, north/south/east/west, near/far, yesterday/today) are built from the child's own home, classroom and community before any map appears.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 2 G2 CS Viva Venezuela): population education (UNESCO population-education guides reworked) woven into story form — Tío Conejo tales, grandparents' memories and the child's own school and street carry population, housing, landscape and weather-sign content.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela): the second half is a state-by-state tour of Venezuela (territorial organisation, Nueva Esparta, Sucre, the Capital, Vargas, Zulia, the Andean states, the Llanos, Guayana) taught through local stories and children of each region — regional geography as a journey around the child's own country.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 7 G7 CS Patria y Ciudadanía): a Civics book built on widening belonging — house, block (cuadra), community, homeland, planet — where the Geography enters as lived space: the Carta del Barrio, in which a community writes down its own geographic space (its social, cultural, historical, economic and environmental character), and a full postal address that nests house → block → parish → municipality → state → Venezuela → South America → Earth → Solar System → Milky Way. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
