# Video script — Venezuela Our Country And Its Landscapes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 1 G1 CS Viva Venezuela* (printed p. 84), teaches: Nuestra Venezuela es así. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 2 G2 CS Viva Venezuela* (printed p. 66), teaches: Paisajes de Venezuela. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela* (printed p. 68), teaches: Nuestros paisajes naturales. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 7 G7 CS Patria y Ciudadanía* (printed p. 76), teaches: La tierra donde nací: the Caribbean coast, the Atlantic and the Orinoco, the Guayana Massif, the cordilleras.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 1 G1 CS Viva Venezuela): critical, question-first pedagogy ("pedagogía crítica") — spatial notions (up/down, north/south/east/west, near/far, yesterday/today) are built from the child's own home, classroom and community before any map appears.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 2 G2 CS Viva Venezuela): population education (UNESCO population-education guides reworked) woven into story form — Tío Conejo tales, grandparents' memories and the child's own school and street carry population, housing, landscape and weather-sign content.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela): the second half is a state-by-state tour of Venezuela (territorial organisation, Nueva Esparta, Sucre, the Capital, Vargas, Zulia, the Andean states, the Llanos, Guayana) taught through local stories and children of each region — regional geography as a journey around the child's own country.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 7 G7 CS Patria y Ciudadanía): a Civics book built on widening belonging — house, block (cuadra), community, homeland, planet — where the Geography enters as lived space: the Carta del Barrio, in which a community writes down its own geographic space (its social, cultural, historical, economic and environmental character), and a full postal address that nests house → block → parish → municipality → state → Venezuela → South America → Earth → Solar System → Milky Way.
