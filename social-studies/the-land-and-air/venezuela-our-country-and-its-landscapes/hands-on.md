# Hands-on — Venezuela Our Country And Its Landscapes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Nuestra Venezuela es así; Paisajes de Venezuela; Nuestros paisajes naturales; La tierra donde nací: the Caribbean coast, the Atlantic and the Orinoco, the Guayana Massif, the cordilleras. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
