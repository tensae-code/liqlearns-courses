# Drawing a sketch map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sketch map is drawn —
a) from survey  b) by satellite  c) freehand from observation
**Answer: c) freehand from observation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its detail rule is —
a) include nothing small or large  b) maximise major features, minimise detail  c) include everything
**Answer: b) maximise major features, minimise detail**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book’s time limit is about —
a) an hour  b) a day  c) five minutes
**Answer: a) an hour**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The set practice is —
a) the whole region  b) a coastline  c) the school compound
**Answer: c) the school compound**
