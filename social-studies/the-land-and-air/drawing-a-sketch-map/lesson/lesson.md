# Drawing a sketch map

## Do it first

No instruments, no survey — just eyes, a pencil, and judgement about what matters. Read the sketch-map steps, then draw your school compound.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a sketch map is

A sketch map is drawn freehand from observation, not from survey: it maximises the major features and minimises detail. It is a map by the definition’s spirit — simplified by design — and it carries the honest components: title, north point, and an approximate sense of scale.

## Sub lesson 2 — Steps and the practice

The book gives the drawing steps and a time limit of about an hour, and sets the practice: sketch the school compound — its buildings, paths, trees and boundaries, major features only. The steps make the hour enough.
