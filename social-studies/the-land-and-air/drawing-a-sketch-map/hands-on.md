# Hands-on — Drawing a sketch map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Compound sketch
Draw the school-compound sketch by the book’s discipline: one hour at most, major features only, title and north point on. Then walk the compound against your sketch and correct it in another colour.

## Feature triage
List every feature you omitted and defend each omission by the maximising rule — or restore it, admitting it was major after all.
