## Sub lesson 2 — Mozambique's way — the book's own teaching way

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 1): distance-learning self-study module — every lesson follows the same rhythm (introduction, development with worked examples, exercises, answer key, lesson summary); the four Earth spheres are the organising spine of the whole module, each sphere closed by a "use, protection and conservation" lesson; local anchors throughout (Maputo temperature worked example, Zambezi delta, Moma/Namialo weather contrast).

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 2): a full physical-climatology course — atmosphere structure to magnetosphere, heat balance, pressure/wind circulation, air masses and fronts, humidity and precipitation, the water cycle, then Köppen/Thornthwaite classification worked through climate tables with African station graphs (Arkassa, Tamanrasset, Algiers), closing on protection of bioclimatic resources. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
