# Video script — Latitude and altitude — the two big controls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Latitude tilts the sun. Altitude cools the air.
Near the equator, strong sun. Toward the poles, slanting and weak.
But climb — and the thermometer falls, metre by metre.
In Ethiopia, the climb beats the map.
