# Hands-on — Latitude and altitude — the two big controls

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Torch angle
Shine a torch straight down on paper, then at a slant. The slant spot is bigger and dimmer — that is latitude’s control drawn in light.

## Lapse walk
If you climb or descend a hill nearby, note clothing and breathing at bottom and top. Write the lapse rate in your body’s words, then in the lesson’s.
