# Latitude and altitude — the two big controls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Latitude controls climate by setting —
a) the lapse rate  b) ocean currents  c) the sun’s angle
**Answer: c) the sun’s angle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Toward the poles, the sun’s rays are —
a) blocked  b) slanting, spreading heat thin  c) direct and strong
**Answer: b) slanting, spreading heat thin**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The lapse rate is —
a) the cooling of air with height  b) the warming of valleys  c) the speed of wind
**Answer: a) the cooling of air with height**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Ethiopia, the stronger control is —
a) latitude  b) longitude  c) altitude
**Answer: c) altitude**
