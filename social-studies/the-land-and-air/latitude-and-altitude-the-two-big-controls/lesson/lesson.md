# Latitude and altitude — the two big controls

## Do it first

Travel 500 kilometres toward the equator and you warm a little. Climb 2,000 metres and you freeze. In Ethiopia the climb wins. Read the two big controls.

## Sub lesson 1 — Latitude

Latitude sets the angle at which the sun’s rays strike: near the equator the sun stands high and heats strongly; toward the poles its slanting rays spread their heat thin. Latitude is the world’s first climate control.

## Sub lesson 2 — Altitude and the lapse rate

Altitude cools the air with height — the lapse rate. In Ethiopia this control outweighs latitude: the country is near the equator, yet its highlands are cool and its lowlands hot, because height changes over short distances what latitude would need whole continents to change.
