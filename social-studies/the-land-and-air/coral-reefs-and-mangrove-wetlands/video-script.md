# Video script — Coral Reefs and Mangrove Wetlands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC teaches coral reefs in Chapter 5: how a coral reef forms and the types of reef there are — living structures built in warm shallow sea water.

The same chapter turns to mangrove wetlands, with Belize as the case study: coasts where mangrove trees stand in the shallow water, a wetland world of its own beside the reefs.
