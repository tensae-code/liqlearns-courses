## Sub lesson 1 — How coral reefs form, and their types

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC teaches coral reefs in Chapter 5: how a coral reef forms and the types of reef there are — living structures built in warm shallow sea water.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mangrove wetlands — Belize

The same chapter turns to mangrove wetlands, with Belize as the case study: coasts where mangrove trees stand in the shallow water, a wetland world of its own beside the reefs.

Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project.
