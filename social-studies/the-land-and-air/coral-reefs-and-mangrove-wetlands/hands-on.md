# Hands-on — Coral Reefs and Mangrove Wetlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Reef or mangrove
Say one thing that makes a coral reef what it is, and one thing that makes a mangrove wetland what it is. Keep the two apart.

## Belize check
Tell your companion which case-study country the lesson uses for mangrove wetlands, and what the mangroves grow in.
