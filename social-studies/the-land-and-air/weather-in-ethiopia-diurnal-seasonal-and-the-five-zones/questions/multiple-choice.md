# Weather in Ethiopia — Diurnal and Seasonal Variation and the Five Agro-Climatic Zones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Bega skies are clear, so —
a) days and nights stay equal  b) days grow hotter and nights colder — a high daily range  c) rain falls all day
**Answer: b) days grow hotter and nights colder — a high daily range**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** (Teacher guide — evidence) In Kiremt, rain falls on —
a) no highlands  b) most highlands  c) only the west
**Answer: b) most highlands**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** (Teacher guide — evidence) How many traditional agro-climatic zones has Ethiopia?
a) three  b) five  c) seven
**Answer: b) five**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) A day with highest 28 and lowest 12 has range —
a) 40  b) 16  c) 20
**Answer: b) 16**
