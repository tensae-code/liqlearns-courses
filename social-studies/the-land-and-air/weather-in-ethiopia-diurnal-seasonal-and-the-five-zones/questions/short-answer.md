# Weather in Ethiopia — Diurnal and Seasonal Variation and the Five Agro-Climatic Zones — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Which part of Ethiopia receives rain all year?
**Answer:** The western part of the country.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** (Teacher guide — evidence) List the five zones, hottest to coldest.
**Answer:** Bereha, Kola, Woina Dega, Dega, Wurch.
