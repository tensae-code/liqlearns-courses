# Video script — Weather in Ethiopia — Diurnal and Seasonal Variation and the Five Agro-Climatic Zones

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Watch the sky over your own town for one full day.

Morning is cold. Midday is hot. Night is cold again.
That wide swing in one day is the daily — diurnal — range.

Highest 28. Lowest 12.
The range is 28 take away 12: 16.
The mean is 28 plus 12, divided by 2: 20.

Bega, the dry winter: clear skies, hot days, cold nights.
Kiremt, the summer rains: most highlands receive rain.
The west receives rain all year.

Five zones, hot to cold:
Bereha. Kola. Woina Dega. Dega. Wurch.
Each zone grows its own range of crops.

Grade 11 depth: Grade 11 depth, from the student book itself: Bereha is desert lowland below 500 metres, over 30 degrees, pastoralist country — the Afar and Somali lowlands. Grade 11 depth: Woina-Dega, 1,500 to 2,300 metres, is the temperate highland zone of teff, wheat and maize. Grade 11 depth: Wurch, above 3,300 metres, is afro-alpine country, where barley is the crop that still ripens.
