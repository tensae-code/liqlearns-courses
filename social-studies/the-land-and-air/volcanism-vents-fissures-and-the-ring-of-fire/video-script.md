# Video script — Volcanism — vents, fissures and the Ring of Fire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Volcanism forces magma to the surface through vents that build cones or fissures that flood plateaus — and almost 90% of active volcanoes ring the Pacific. First, vents and fissures: Volcanism forces magma, gas and solids to the surface. Then, where volcanoes live — and their three states: Of roughly 1,900 historically active volcanoes, almost 90% sit in the Pacific Ring of Fire, with others on oceanic ridges and along the Great Rift Valley of East Africa. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
