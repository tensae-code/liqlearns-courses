# Volcanism — vents, fissures and the Ring of Fire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pipe-like vent builds…
a) a lava plateau  b) a cone volcano  c) a delta
**Answer: b) a cone volcano**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A fissure eruption builds…
a) a lava plateau with little explosive activity  b) a single steep cone  c) a cave
**Answer: a) a lava plateau with little explosive activity**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Volcanism forces what to the surface?
a) magma, gas and solids  b) groundwater only  c) sand
**Answer: a) magma, gas and solids**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** There are roughly how many historically active volcanoes?
a) 190  b) 1,900  c) 19,000
**Answer: b) 1,900**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Almost 90% of them sit…
a) in the Pacific Ring of Fire  b) in Africa  c) under the oceans only
**Answer: a) in the Pacific Ring of Fire**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Erta Ale is classed as…
a) extinct  b) dormant  c) active
**Answer: c) active**
