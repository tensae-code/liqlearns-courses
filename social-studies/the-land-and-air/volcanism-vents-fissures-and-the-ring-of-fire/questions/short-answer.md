# Volcanism — vents, fissures and the Ring of Fire — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do vent and fissure eruptions differ in what they build?
**Answer:** A vent builds a cone volcano; a fissure floods lava out into a lava plateau.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one Ethiopian volcano of each state the book gives.
**Answer:** Extinct: Mount Zuqualla. Active: Erta Ale. (Dormant example given: Kilimanjaro, outside Ethiopia.)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where else, besides the Ring of Fire, do volcanoes sit?
**Answer:** On oceanic ridges and along the Great Rift Valley of East Africa.
