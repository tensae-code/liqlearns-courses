## Sub lesson 1 — How mountains are important to people's lives, and how human activities affect mountain environments

Jamaica's Grade 6 Exploratory Core guide (Social Studies Term 2 Unit 1, printed p. 183) teaches mountains both ways round: how mountains are important to people's lives, and how human activities affect mountain environments. The mountain gives to people, and people's activities act back on the mountain. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence.

## Sub lesson 2 — Jamaica's way — the book's own teaching way

Jamaica's own way (Grade 6 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): the Grade 6 Social Studies year is built as three paired terms — culture and independence first, then the physical world (mountains, landmasses and water bodies), then climate, Earth movements and the atmosphere, closing on Caribbean cooperation. That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
