# Video script — Mountains and People's Lives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jamaica's Grade 6 Exploratory Core guide (Social Studies Term 2 Unit 1, printed p. 183) teaches mountains both ways round: how mountains are important to people's lives, and how human activities affect mountain environments. The mountain gives to people, and people's activities act back on the mountain. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence.

Jamaica's own way (Grade 6 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): the Grade 6 Social Studies year is built as three paired terms — culture and independence first, then the physical world (mountains, landmasses and water bodies), then climate, Earth movements and the atmosphere, closing on Caribbean cooperation. That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
