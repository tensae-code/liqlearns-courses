Greece's own way (Greece Grade 5 Geography Teacher Guide (old ed.)) (teacher guide — evidence): the whole primary Geography course is one country studied whole — "Learning Greece": maps first as a tool, then Greece's natural environment (coasts, seas, islands, mountains, plains, climate, rivers, lakes, living things), then its human environment (population, cities, administration, farming, industry, services, transport), closing outward to the EU, Cyprus and the Greek diaspora. Each chapter is taught through a friendship/camp story frame, group activities and cross-curricular concepts; the guide gives per-chapter objectives, method, text structure, group activity, materials, extra scientific background, and worksheet answers (printed p. 116). That evidence is stocked here as the way Greece teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Greece is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 5 Geography Student Book (old ed.)): same 47-chapter "Learning Greece" course as the teacher guide, in 4 units (Maps; The natural environment of Greece; The human-made environment of Greece; Hellenism beyond the borders), each chapter built from story/dialogue, photos and maps, with activity symbols (individual work, discussion, geographical glossary, "add to your knowledge") and a per-chapter glossary.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 5 Geography Workbook (old ed.)): one activity worksheet per student-book chapter (draw your own park map, true/false, acrostics, map-reading tasks) plus 11 interspersed assessment sheets working whole maps of Greece — thematic, geomorphological (repeated at rising difficulty), national parks and political.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 7 Geology-Geography Student Book): Gymnasium Geography is organised by Earth's spheres — maps first, then the natural environment sphere by sphere (atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, biosphere), then the human environment (population, cities, resources), closing with one "snapshot" chapter per continent, Antarctica included. Lessons open with art and literature (a Little Prince extract introduces coordinates) and state their learning goals up front ("In this chapter you will learn…").

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 7 Geology-Geography Teacher Guide) (teacher guide — evidence): the guide opens with the philosophy of the whole package — against memorising lists of countries and numbers: geography as systems, change over time and cross-curricular links (the ΔΕΠΠΣ framework), with "mental maps" as the goal. Per lesson it gives objectives, visual material, teaching approach and worked answers (e.g. the coordinate games solved for Quito, Athens, Manila, Lusaka; Greece's own 1821–1999 birth/death-rate table under population). Its second half guides the workbook's project activities, and it closes with cross-curricular question sets and 32 suggested assessment topics. Europe gets no separate unit here — it is the whole of the Grade 8 course. That evidence is stocked here as the way Greece teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Greece is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Amangalieva NIS): the NIS (Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools) inquiry textbook: every lesson opens with a «Бұл сабақта» (In this lesson) goal box and closes with a «Сенің кезегің!» (Your turn!) task block; each section ends in a «Қорытынды» summary built round a concept map; the book closes with a trilingual Kazakh–Russian–English glossary. Country-studies section works through how a state improves its geographic position, with the UAE and Singapore as cases and Kazakhstan as the home example.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Tolybekova 2017): the 2017 first edition of the Mektep course (Book 5 is its 2025 revision): the same continent-by-continent nomenclature and belt-by-belt natural-complexes architecture, with a lithosphere field-research practical, a §52 «Человек — часть биосферы» (The human being as part of the biosphere), and a closing §60 «Этот многоликий мир» (This many-faced world) survey of the world's diversity.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 8 Karatabanov): the Almatykitap Grade 8 course runs the same six-section architecture as the Mektep book (Book 7) but in Karatabanov's question-led style, closing Part 2 with §68 «Мой географический год» (My geographic year) — a pupil's own review of a year of geographic work.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 8 Sakhova NIS): the NIS Grade 8 course phrases its lessons as questions and tasks — «Әр жерде неліктен уақыт әркелкі?» (Why is time different in different places?, 2.2), «Тау жыныстары қалай пайда болады?» (How do rocks form?, 3.1.5), «Өзеннің суы қалай өзгереді?» (How does a river's water change?, 3.3.4) — and adds a lesson on the culture of showing geographic names on the map (2.3).
