# Forces that shape the Earth — inside and outside — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two families of geological processes.
**Answer:** Endogenic forces from inside the Earth and exogenic forces from outside it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What lava comes from oceanic divergence?
**Answer:** Basaltic lava.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is Krakatoa the warning example?
**Answer:** Its 1883 eruption raised a tsunami that drowned over 36,000 people on Java and Sumatra.
