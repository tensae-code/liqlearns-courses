# Forces that shape the Earth — inside and outside — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Geological processes are studied to…
a) understand Earth's history, locate resources, predict disasters  b) change the weather  c) count countries
**Answer: a) understand Earth's history, locate resources, predict disasters**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Endogenic forces come from…
a) the wind  b) inside the Earth  c) the Moon
**Answer: b) inside the Earth**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** An example resource located through geology is…
a) metal ores  b) rainfall  c) sunlight
**Answer: a) metal ores**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Exogenic forces come from…
a) the Earth's core  b) outside, at the surface  c) the mantle only
**Answer: b) outside, at the surface**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Ocean-plate collisions erupt…
a) basalt and andesite  b) sand  c) ice
**Answer: a) basalt and andesite**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1883 Krakatoa tsunami drowned over…
a) 360 people  b) 3,600 people  c) 36,000 people
**Answer: c) 36,000 people**
