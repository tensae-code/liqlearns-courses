# Video script — Forces that shape the Earth — inside and outside

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Every landform is the score of a contest: endogenic forces from inside the Earth building up, exogenic forces from outside wearing down. First, two families of forces: Geological processes are the natural forces shaping the planet's physical makeup. Then, eruptions as the inside force at work: Eruption style shows the inside forces at work. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
