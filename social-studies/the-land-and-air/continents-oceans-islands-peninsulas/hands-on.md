# Hands-on — Continents, oceans, islands, peninsulas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three shapes on paper
Draw a coastline, then add an island offshore, a peninsula reaching out, and an isthmus joining two land blocks. Label each and write its definition under it.

## Ocean hunt
On any world map or globe at hand, find and name all five oceans aloud, then point to the largest.
