# Continents, oceans, islands, peninsulas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The oceans together cover about —
a) 29% of the surface  b) 50%  c) 71%  d) 90%
**Answer: c) 71%** — the book's figure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The largest and deepest ocean is —
a) the Atlantic  b) the Pacific  c) the Indian  d) the Arctic
**Answer: b) the Pacific** — covering about a third of the Earth.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many oceans are there?
a) three  b) four  c) five  d) seven
**Answer: c) five** — Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic, Southern.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** An isthmus is —
a) land fully surrounded by water  b) a narrow strip joining two larger lands  c) a deep ocean trench  d) a river mouth
**Answer: b) a narrow strip joining two larger lands** — with water on both sides.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Land washed by water on three sides and joined to the mainland is a —
a) island  b) isthmus  c) peninsula  d) delta
**Answer: c) peninsula**.
