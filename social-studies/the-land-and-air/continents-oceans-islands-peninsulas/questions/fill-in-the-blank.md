# Continents, oceans, islands, peninsulas — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The ______ Ocean is the largest and deepest, covering about a third of the Earth.
**Answer:** Pacific.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A narrow land strip joining two larger lands is an ______.
**Answer:** isthmus.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Together the oceans cover ______ percent of the Earth's surface.
**Answer:** 71.
