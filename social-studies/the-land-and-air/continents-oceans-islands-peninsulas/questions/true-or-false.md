# Continents, oceans, islands, peninsulas — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Land covers most of the Earth's surface. True or false?
**Answer: False** — oceans cover 71%; land is the smaller share.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Pacific covers about a third of the Earth. True or false?
**Answer: True** — as the book states.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** An island is joined to the mainland. True or false?
**Answer: False** — an island is completely surrounded by water.
