# Continents, oceans, islands, peninsulas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What share of the Earth's surface do the oceans cover, and what covers the rest?
**Answer:** The oceans cover 71% of the surface; the continents — the great land masses — cover the rest.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell a peninsula and an isthmus apart in one sentence each.
**Answer:** A peninsula is land reaching into water on three sides while joined to the mainland; an isthmus is a narrow strip of land joining two larger land areas.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the five oceans.
**Answer:** Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic and Southern.
