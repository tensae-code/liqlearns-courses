## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies): the whole first unit is Iraq's own geography — location first (world, region, locality, then the astronomical location), then the land itself region by region off the relief map, then people and economic activity; history and national education follow as separate units.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies): a whole unit walks Iraq governorate by governorate — all eighteen in one fixed pattern (geographical location, relief, climate, water resources, population with 2017 estimates, economic activity, then a short historical note) — so the child learns the country as its provinces, each with its own map; a second unit on Iraqi society (Civics) follows.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies): Grade 9 returns to Iraq for the fullest treatment yet — the same national geography as Grade 5 but measured: the plain is 24% and about 600 km long, the desert plateau about 55%, the desert plant belt about 70%, two-thirds of the people live on the central and southern plain; every topic is pinned to numbered maps of Iraq. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
