# Weather and Climate — Change Over Days, Seasons and Years — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weather change is —
a) a shift in the long average  b) the day-to-day and month-to-month swing over a small area  c) only seasonal
**Answer: b) the day-to-day and month-to-month swing over a small area**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate change is —
a) one hot day  b) a shift in the long-recorded average of the climate over many years  c) a single storm
**Answer: b) a shift in the long-recorded average of the climate over many years**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The harms of these changes slow —
a) only farming  b) people's safety and economic growth  c) nothing
**Answer: b) people's safety and economic growth**
