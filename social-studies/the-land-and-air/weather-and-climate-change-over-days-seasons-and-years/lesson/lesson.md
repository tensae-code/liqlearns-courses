## Sub lesson 1 — Weather change: the day-to-day swing

Weather change is the day-to-day and month-to-month swing of wind, rain, heat and cold over a small area. It is the change you dress for each morning.

[Picture 11476 — a one-week weather strip above a thirty-year climate line, the line itself shifting]

## Sub lesson 2 — Climate change: the long average shifts

Climate change is a shift in the long-recorded average of the climate — of humidity, cold, heat, wind direction, pressure and the like, over many years. When the long average itself moves, that is climate change. (The student book's §1.3.4 heading and definitions are tangled as printed; this lesson teaches the two changes as §1.3.2 states them — see sources.)

## Sub lesson 3 — How the two changes touch life

Climate change sets weather swinging, and the harms slow people's safety and economic growth. Your interview task: ask at home how daily and seasonal change, and the climate itself, shape dress, food, housing and farming where you live.
