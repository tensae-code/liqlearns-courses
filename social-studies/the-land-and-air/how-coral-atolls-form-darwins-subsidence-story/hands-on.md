# Hands-on — How Coral Atolls Form — Darwin's Subsidence Story

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the sinking story
Draw three pictures in order: a high island, the same island sinking, and a low coral atoll with a lagoon. Label each step.

## Say the order aloud
Tell a companion the order in the theory — high island, subsidence (sinking), low atoll with a lagoon — without looking at the lesson.
