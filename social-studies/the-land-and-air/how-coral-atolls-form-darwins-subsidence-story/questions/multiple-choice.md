# How Coral Atolls Form — Darwin's Subsidence Story — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsidence means —
a) sinking  b) rising  c) freezing
**Answer: a) sinking**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The subsidence theory in Chapter 1 is —
a) Darwin's theory  b) a trade theory  c) a weather forecast
**Answer: a) Darwin's theory**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the theory, a high island becomes —
a) a low coral atoll with a lagoon  b) a mountain range  c) a desert
**Answer: a) a low coral atoll with a lagoon**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The water inside the atoll ring is called —
a) the lagoon  b) a river  c) a lake on a mountain
**Answer: a) the lagoon**
