## Sub lesson 1 — Darwin's subsidence theory

Micronesia's Grade 9 textbook teaches Darwin's subsidence theory in Chapter 1. Subsidence means sinking: the theory says a high volcanic island slowly sinks, and that sinking is the key to how an atoll forms.

## Sub lesson 2 — From high island to low atoll

As the high island sinks, what is left at the surface is a low coral atoll with a lagoon — the ring of low coral land around shallow lagoon water. So the chapter's story runs in order: high island first, sinking next, low coral atoll with a lagoon last.
