The teacher guide widens the recipe: the atmosphere is a mixture of gases, floating or suspended solids, and liquids — including a variable amount of water vapour averaging around 1%, plus suspended dust particles and condensed moisture droplets, called aerosols (teacher guide — evidence).

Judged by how temperature changes with height, the atmosphere divides into four layers — the next lesson's work.
