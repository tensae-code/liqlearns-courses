# The Atmosphere — Its Gases and Layers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the student book's full recipe.
**Answer:** 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 0.9% argon, 0.03% carbon dioxide, and other gases.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** (Teacher guide — evidence) What are aerosols, in the guide's key?
**Answer:** Suspended dust particles and condensed moisture droplets.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) What is the second way of slicing the atmosphere?
**Answer:** By composition (the compositional structure), as well as by temperature.
