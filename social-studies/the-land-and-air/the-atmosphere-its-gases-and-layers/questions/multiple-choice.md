# The Atmosphere — Its Gases and Layers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The air is mostly —
a) oxygen, 21%  b) nitrogen, 78%  c) carbon dioxide, 60%
**Answer: b) nitrogen, 78%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Oxygen's share in the student book is —
a) 0.9%  b) 21%  c) 78%
**Answer: b) 21%**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Besides gases, the air holds —
a) nothing else  b) suspended solids and liquids, including water vapour and aerosols  c) only nitrogen
**Answer: b) suspended solids and liquids, including water vapour and aerosols**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The atmosphere is divided into layers by —
a) colour  b) how temperature changes with height  c) weight
**Answer: b) how temperature changes with height**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** How many layers does that division give?
a) two  b) four  c) seven
**Answer: b) four**
