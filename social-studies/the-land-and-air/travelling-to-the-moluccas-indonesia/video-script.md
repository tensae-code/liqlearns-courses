Greece's book, the *Greece Grade 7 Geology-Geography Teacher Guide* (printed p. 112), teaches: Workbook activity B3.3 Ταξίδι στις Μολούκες (a voyage to the Moluccas — Indonesia) — teacher guidance. (teacher guide — evidence)

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 7 Geology-Geography Teacher Guide) (teacher guide — evidence): the guide opens with the philosophy of the whole package — against memorising lists of countries and numbers: geography as systems, change over time and cross-curricular links (the ΔΕΠΠΣ framework), with "mental maps" as the goal. Per lesson it gives objectives, visual material, teaching approach and worked answers (e.g. the coordinate games solved for Quito, Athens, Manila, Lusaka; Greece's own 1821–1999 birth/death-rate table under population). Its second half guides the workbook's project activities, and it closes with cross-curricular question sets and 32 suggested assessment topics. Europe gets no separate unit here — it is the whole of the Grade 8 course. That evidence is stocked here as the way Greece teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
