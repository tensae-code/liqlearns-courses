# Water Bodies — Rivers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a river differ from a lake in one word?
**Answer:** It flows (moves) — a lake stands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does rain falling in the catchment end up?
**Answer:** In the river.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which continent is the Nile in, and how long is it in the table?
**Answer:** Africa; 6,671 km.
