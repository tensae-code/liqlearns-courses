# Water Bodies — Rivers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A river is —
a) standing water ringed by land  b) flowing water in a permanent or seasonal channel  c) a narrow sea opening
**Answer: b) flowing water in a permanent or seasonal channel**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A catchment area is —
a) the river's mouth  b) the land whose water drains into the river  c) a kind of dam
**Answer: b) the land whose water drains into the river**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The longest river in Table 5 is —
a) the Amazon, 6,437 km  b) the Nile, 6,671 km  c) the Bering
**Answer: b) the Nile, 6,671 km**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Rows 3–5 of Table 5 are —
a) a library completion-and-report task  b) printing errors to ignore  c) already filled
**Answer: a) a library completion-and-report task**
