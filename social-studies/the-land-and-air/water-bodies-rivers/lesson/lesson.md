## Sub lesson 1 — Flowing water in a channel

Begin with the rivers you know and what your community uses them for. A river is a body of flowing water moving in a permanent or seasonal channel. Flowing is the key word: a lake stands, a river moves.

## Sub lesson 2 — Every river has its catchment

Every river has its own catchment area — the land whose water drains into it. Rain that falls anywhere in the catchment finds its way to the river.
