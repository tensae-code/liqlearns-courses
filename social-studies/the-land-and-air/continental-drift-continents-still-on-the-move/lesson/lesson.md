# Continental drift — continents still on the move

## Sub lesson 1 — What drift is

Continental drift means the continents move apart from one another. The engine is sea-floor spreading: at mid-ocean ridges, new sea floor forms and pushes the plates on either side away from the ridge.

## Sub lesson 2 — Drift at work today

The book gives drift's present effects one by one: the Pacific Ocean narrows, the Atlantic Ocean widens, the Mediterranean Sea narrows, the Himalayas rise, and Australia drifts toward the equator.

## Sub lesson 3 — India's journey — the worked example

India once lay inside Gondwana. It travelled north, collided with Eurasia, and the collision pushed up the Himalayas — mountains still rising because the movement has not stopped.
