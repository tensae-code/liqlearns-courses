# Video script — Continental drift — continents still on the move

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Continental drift means the continents move apart from one another.

The book gives drift's present effects one by one: the Pacific Ocean narrows, the Atlantic Ocean widens, the Mediterranean Sea narrows, the Himalayas rise, and Australia drifts toward the equator..

India once lay inside Gondwana.
