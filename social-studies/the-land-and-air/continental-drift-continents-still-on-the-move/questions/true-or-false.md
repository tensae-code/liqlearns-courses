# Continental drift — continents still on the move — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sea-floor spreading pushes plates away from mid-ocean ridges.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Australia is drifting toward the equator.
