# Continental drift — continents still on the move — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Continental drift is driven by —
a) sea-floor spreading at mid-ocean ridges  b) river erosion  c) the Moon
**Answer: a) sea-floor spreading at mid-ocean ridges**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** New sea floor forms at —
a) mid-ocean ridges  b) river deltas  c) deserts
**Answer: a) mid-ocean ridges**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Today the Atlantic Ocean is —
a) widening  b) narrowing  c) dry
**Answer: a) widening**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Himalayas rose when India collided with —
a) Eurasia  b) Africa  c) Australia
**Answer: a) Eurasia**
