# Continental drift — continents still on the move — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is continental drift?
**Answer:** The continents moving apart from one another.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two present effects of drift.
**Answer:** The Pacific narrows; the Atlantic widens; the Mediterranean narrows; the Himalayas rise; Australia drifts toward the equator (any two).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which collision raised the Himalayas?
**Answer:** India's collision with Eurasia.
