# Belarus Climate and Seasons

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the climate of Belarus — its seasons and its climatic resources, as Belarus's Grade 10 book teaches them. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The climate of Belarus

Belarus's Grade 10 book gives the country's climate its own paragraph (Theme II §13): the climate of Belarus is studied as one component of its nature, after relief and before its waters.

## Sub lesson 2 — Seasons and climatic resources

Theme II §14 follows with the seasons and climatic resources: how the year divides in Belarus, and what the climate gives the country — the warmth and moisture its farming and life depend on.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
