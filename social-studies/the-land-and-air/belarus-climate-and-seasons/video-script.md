The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Belarus's Grade 10 book gives the country's climate its own paragraph (Theme II §13): the climate of Belarus is studied as one component of its nature, after relief and before its waters.

Theme II §14 follows with the seasons and climatic resources: how the year divides in Belarus, and what the climate gives the country — the warmth and moisture its farming and life depend on.
