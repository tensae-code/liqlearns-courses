# Asking people properly — field questionnaires and interviews

## Do it first

You cannot just walk up to a farmer with twenty questions. There is a proper way to ask people in the field. Read it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Before you ask (teacher guide — evidence)

Questionnaires and interview schedules must be reviewed — by the teacher, in school wording — before any surveying is done. Interviewers carry a letter of introduction, so the person asked knows who they are and why they are asking.

[Picture 21064 — a pupil with an introduction letter interviewing a farmer]

## Sub lesson 2 — While you ask (teacher guide — evidence)

In the field, interviewers minimise disturbance to the people and places they study. And as on all fieldwork, first-aid kits are required equipment — asking people properly includes being ready if anything goes wrong.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the proper way to ask people in the field, before and during.

## Do It Yourself

1. Say what must happen to a questionnaire before use.
2. Name what the interviewer carries.
3. State the disturbance rule and the required kit.
