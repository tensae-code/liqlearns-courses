Mongolia's own way (Grade 10): the senior cycle drops the “Individual and…” frame for straight disciplines — Society, Culture, Law, Economics, Politics, International Relations — with Mongolia's own culture, macroeconomy and foreign relations as the worked examples. The book's Sociology parts (Chapter 1, printed pp. 5–15) and Law/Civics parts (Chapter 3, printed pp. 49–72; Chapter 5, printed pp. 97–117; Chapter 6 except the placed topics, printed pp. 119–135) are named in the placements, not placed. That way is stocked here as the way Mongolia teaches this thing.

Guatemala's own way (teacher guide — evidence): Social Studies is taught through three long “learning experiences” rooted in the country's own peoples (Maya, Garífuna, Xinka, Ladino); Geography enters as lived territory — the Maya unity of peoples and territory, care for Mother Earth, and a linguistic map of Guatemala played as a lotería game. That evidence is stocked here as the way Guatemala teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): the year's Geography is one long chapter, "Protect the Bond between Nature and People": pupils model the world's biomes, compare world forest cover in 2000 and 2020, map Bangladesh's own forests and disaster-prone areas, and read a long inquiry-reader on biomes, sea resources and the Sundarbans mangrove.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 7 guide-geo-1as) (teacher guide — evidence): A competency-based guide (IPN 2025 reform) that fixes the whole Grade 7 course in three units — Earth and its main relief, Climate, Population and urbanisation — each unit ending in a class project, and every general topic anchored to Mauritania itself: the country's astronomical location, its relief map and Atlantic coast, rainfall differences between its wilayas, its age pyramid, and the growth of Nouakchott. Sample lesson plans (fiches) are given for one lesson per unit, plus model term exams with answers. That evidence is stocked here as the way Mauritania teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran): the whole of Grade 10 geography is one national book — Iran's own natural and human geography for every branch; nearly every lesson closes with a "know your own living place" activity tied to the separate provincial-studies book, so national content is always re-applied to the learner's own province.
