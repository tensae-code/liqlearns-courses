# Hands-on — Asking people properly — field questionnaires and interviews

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draft and review
Draft five interview questions for a local farmer or trader about the land. Exchange with a partner acting as reviewing teacher: cut rude, leading or pointless questions before keeping the set.

## Introduction letter
Write the letter of introduction for your survey: who you are, your school, what you are studying, what you will do with the answers.
