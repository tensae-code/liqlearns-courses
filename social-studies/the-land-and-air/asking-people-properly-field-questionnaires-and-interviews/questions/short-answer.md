# Asking people properly — field questionnaires and interviews — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why carry a letter of introduction?
**Answer: So the person asked knows who the interviewer is and why they are asking.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two conduct points does the lesson give for the asking itself?
**Answer: Minimise disturbance, and carry first-aid kits as required equipment.**
