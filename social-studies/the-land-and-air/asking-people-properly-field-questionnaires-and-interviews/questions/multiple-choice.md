# Asking people properly — field questionnaires and interviews — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before surveying, questionnaires must be —
a) printed in colour  b) signed by the farmer  c) reviewed by the teacher
**Answer: c) reviewed by the teacher**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Interviewers carry —
a) a camera only  b) a letter of introduction  c) a gift
**Answer: b) a letter of introduction**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the field, interviewers must —
a) minimise disturbance  b) ask as many people as possible at once  c) stay all day
**Answer: a) minimise disturbance**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Required equipment on fieldwork includes —
a) extra questionnaires only  b) whistles only  c) first-aid kits
**Answer: c) first-aid kits**
