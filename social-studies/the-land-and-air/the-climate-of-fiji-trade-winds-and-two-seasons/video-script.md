# Video script — The Climate of Fiji — Trade Winds and Two Seasons

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji's book, the *Fiji Year 6 Social Science* (printed p. 56), teaches: People and Care of Places: the climate of Fiji (two seasons, the trade winds); how climate change affects gardening and livestock. Fiji's book, the *Fiji Year 10 Social Science* (printed p. 57), teaches: Geography of Fiji: climate (tropical oceanic; windward wet / leeward dry; factors influencing climate; types of rainfall; the trade winds). Fiji's book, the *Fiji Years 11–12 Physical Geography* (printed p. 19), teaches: Chapter 2 Climate – Fiji: humid tropical maritime climate, south-east trade winds and topography controlling rainfall (1,500–4,000 mm), dry west suited to sugarcane vs wet humid east.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 6 Social Science): Year 6 turns outward to the whole Fiji map — map conventions, island groups, towns, rivers and roads — and to Fiji's climate and sea resources, with conservation taught through traditional taboos and harvest seasons.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 10 Social Science): Year 10 is the foundation for senior Geography and History — Fiji's own physical geography (tectonics, relief, rivers, climate, soils) taught in full, then its industries, population and urbanisation, all with Fiji data.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Years 11–12 Physical Geography): The physical volume is comparative — every theme (climate, relief, soil, vegetation, hazards) is taught twice, New Zealand first, then Fiji (Monsoon Asia for hazards), so Fiji's land is always read against a temperate and a monsoon case.
