# How soil is made — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A soil-forming factor is —
a) latitude of the town  b) market distance  c) parent rock
**Answer: c) parent rock**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Soil formation is —
a) instant on weathering  b) slow — the work of long periods  c) a one-season process
**Answer: b) slow — the work of long periods**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopian soils vary because —
a) the factors vary across the country  b) of one uniform climate  c) all parent rock is identical
**Answer: a) the factors vary across the country**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Climates across Ethiopia run from —
a) Kolla to Kolla  b) desert to desert  c) Bereha to Wurch
**Answer: c) Bereha to Wurch**
