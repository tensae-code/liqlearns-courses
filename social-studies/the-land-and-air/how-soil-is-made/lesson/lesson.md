# How soil is made

## Do it first

Five ingredients, one recipe, a thousand years of cooking. Read how soil is made.

## Sub lesson 1 — The five factors

Soil forms under five factors: the parent rock it weathers from, the climate that weathers it, the organisms that mix and enrich it, the relief it lies on, and time — soil is slow; a mature soil is the work of very long periods.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia’s varied soils

Ethiopian soils vary because the factors vary: different basement, sedimentary and volcanic parent rocks; climates from Bereha to Wurch; relief from plateau to gorge. Read the factors of a place, and its soil is half explained before you dig.
