# The Savanna grasslands — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Savanna trees close together into a dense forest.
**Answer: False** — they stand scattered in grass.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The savanna appears in both hemispheres, mirrored about the equator.
**Answer: True** — the belt order repeats south of the equator.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A bare savanna tree in the dry season is dead.
**Answer: False** — it is waiting — its water-saving is done.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dropping leaves increases a tree's water loss.
**Answer: False** — it cuts water loss to almost nothing.
