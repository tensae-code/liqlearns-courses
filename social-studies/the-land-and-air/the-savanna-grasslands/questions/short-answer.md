# The Savanna grasslands — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the belt order from the equator northward.
**Answer:** Rainforest, savanna, Sahel, Sahara.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does leaf-shedding help a savanna tree survive the dry season?
**Answer:** Most water leaves a tree through its leaves, so shedding them cuts water loss while the roots wait in dry soil until the rains return.
