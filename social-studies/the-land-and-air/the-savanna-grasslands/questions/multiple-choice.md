# The Savanna grasslands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The savanna lies between —
a) Sahara and Atlas  b) rainforest and Sahel  c) two oceans  d) highlands and coast
**Answer: b) rainforest and Sahel** — the wide middle belt in both hemispheres.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The savanna's true cover is —
a) closed forest  b) grass, with scattered trees and shrubs  c) bare rock  d) dunes
**Answer: b) grass, with scattered trees and shrubs** — the trees never close into forest.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Savanna trees shed their leaves in the dry season to —
a) attract rain  b) cut water loss  c) make shade  d) grow faster
**Answer: b) cut water loss** — a tree loses most water through its leaves.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What changes with the savanna's seasons?
a) Temperature — hot then cold  b) Rain — wet then dry, heat all year  c) Day length only  d) Nothing
**Answer: b) Rain — wet then dry, heat all year** — the heat is year-round.
