# Lesson plan — The Savanna grasslands

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** The Savanna grasslands
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Landforms of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The savanna is Africa's tropical grassland belt between rainforest and Sahel — grass with scattered drought-resistant trees that shed their leaves in the dry season to cut water loss under year-round heat.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on The Sahel and the rainforest lesson — the savanna is the belt between them.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **The grassland belt and its place** — Between rainforest and Sahel.
2. **How savanna trees survive the dry season** — Shedding leaves to save water.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Sahel — where the desert advances; Africa's rainforest and the Congo Basin. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Place the savanna between rainforest and Sahel, in both hemispheres.
2. Describe its cover: grass with scattered drought-resistant trees and shrubs.
3. Explain leaf-shedding as water saving in the dry season.
