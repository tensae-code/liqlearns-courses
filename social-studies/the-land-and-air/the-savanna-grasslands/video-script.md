# Video script — The Savanna grasslands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The savanna: grass to the horizon.
Scattered trees stand in the grass — never a forest.
Hot all year. The seasons are wet and dry.
In the dry season the trees drop their leaves — and save their water.
