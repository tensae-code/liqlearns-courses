# Hands-on — The Savanna grasslands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-season drawing
Draw the same savanna scene twice on one page: wet season (green grass, full leaves) and dry season (brown grass, bare branches). Label what changed and what did not.

## Belt strip
Make a paper strip of Africa's belts from equator to north edge: rainforest, savanna, Sahel, Sahara. Add the mirror order on the south half. Pin the savanna's place with a marker.
