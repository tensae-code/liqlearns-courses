# Hands-on — The western lowlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two Ethiopias
Make a two-column chart: Highlands / Western lowlands. Fill in height, temperature, farming and settlement from this lesson and the highland lessons.

## River tracing
On an outline map, draw the Baro, Akobo and Gilo flowing west and mark the border they flow toward.
