# The western lowlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The western lowlands lie along the border with —
a) Kenya and Somalia  b) Eritrea and Djibouti  c) Sudan and South Sudan
**Answer: c) Sudan and South Sudan**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lowlands are —
a) high and dry  b) hot and flat  c) cold and steep
**Answer: b) hot and flat**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Their rivers flow toward —
a) the Nile system  b) Lake Turkana  c) the Red Sea
**Answer: a) the Nile system**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Compared with the highlands, the lowlands have —
a) more towns  b) the same farming in every way  c) fewer people and different crops
**Answer: c) fewer people and different crops**
