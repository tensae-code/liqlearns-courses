# The western lowlands

## Do it first

Follow a western river downhill on a map until the land flattens at the border. Hot, flat, wet in the rains — read the western lowlands.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hot, flat plains

Along the border with Sudan and South Sudan, the Western Highlands drop to the western lowlands: the plains of the Baro, Akobo and Gilo rivers and their neighbours. They are hot and flat — the opposite face of the cool highlands a short distance east.

[Picture 21008 — a flat river plain scene labelled with its rivers]

## Sub lesson 2 — Drained westward

These plains are drained westward, by rivers flowing toward South Sudan and the Nile system beyond. Their heat, flatness and wet seasons give them a different pattern of settlement and farming from the highlands — fewer people, different crops, and movement shaped by water and season.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what the western lowlands are like — heat, flatness, rivers — and how they differ from the highlands.
