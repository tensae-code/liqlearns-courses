The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya opens its Regions and Countries unit with the idea itself: III-A Bölge Kavramı ve Türleri — the region concept and its kinds. Regions are sorted by their features: natural, human, economic and functional.

A region is only useful if you can say where it stops: III-B Bölge Sınırları teaches region boundaries — the lines that separate one region from the next.

The unit closes by setting regions beside countries: III-C Bölgeler ve Ülkeler — regions and countries. A country is one kind of division of the Earth's surface; a region is another, drawn by features rather than by government.
