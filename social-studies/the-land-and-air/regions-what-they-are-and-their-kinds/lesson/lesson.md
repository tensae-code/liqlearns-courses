# Regions — What They Are and Their Kinds

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: regions — the region concept and its kinds, region boundaries, and regions and countries, in Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya, as Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya teaches it in III-A–III-C. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The region concept and its kinds — Bölge Kavramı ve Türleri

Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya opens its Regions and Countries unit with the idea itself: III-A Bölge Kavramı ve Türleri — the region concept and its kinds. Regions are sorted by their features: natural, human, economic and functional.

## Sub lesson 2 — Region boundaries — Bölge Sınırları

A region is only useful if you can say where it stops: III-B Bölge Sınırları teaches region boundaries — the lines that separate one region from the next.

## Sub lesson 3 — Regions and countries — Bölgeler ve Ülkeler

The unit closes by setting regions beside countries: III-C Bölgeler ve Ülkeler — regions and countries. A country is one kind of division of the Earth's surface; a region is another, drawn by features rather than by government.

Turkey's own way: a systems course — Natural Systems first (nature–human interaction, coordinates, maps, Earth's motions, climate), then Human Systems (settlements), then Regions and Countries, then Environment and Society — with a Turkey case closing most chapters (Turkey's location, Turkey's climate, settlements in Turkey). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
