# Line graphs from raw data — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a line graph show that a bar graph does not?
**Answer:** Change over time — a trend, followed as one movement.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is plotted month by month in the example?
**Answer:** Temperature — twelve points, joined into a line.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three movements a line can make.
**Answer:** It can rise, fall, or run level.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why join the points in order?
**Answer:** Because the joined line shows the year's warming and cooling as one movement — the trend — instead of twelve separate numbers.
