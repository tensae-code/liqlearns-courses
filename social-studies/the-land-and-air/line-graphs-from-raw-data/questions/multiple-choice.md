# Line graphs from raw data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A line graph's special job is to show —
a) change over time  b) parts of a whole  c) places on a map
**Answer: a) change over time**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The worked example follows —
a) one day's rainfall  b) a river's length  c) a year's temperature
**Answer: c) a year's temperature**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The line's story is told by —
a) its thickness  b) its rises, falls and level stretches  c) its colour
**Answer: b) its rises, falls and level stretches**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A level stretch of the line means —
a) the value held steady over that time  b) the data ran out  c) the temperature was zero
**Answer: a) the value held steady over that time**
