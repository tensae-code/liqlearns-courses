# Line graphs from raw data

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — What a line graph does

Line graphs show change over time — trends. Where a bar graph compares separate categories, a line graph follows one thing as it moves: the line's rises, falls and level stretches are the story of the data.

## Sub lesson 2 — A year's temperature — points, then a line

The worked example builds a year's temperature graph from raw data: plot each month's point in its place, then join the points in order. The joined line lets the reader see the year's warming and cooling as one movement, not twelve numbers.
