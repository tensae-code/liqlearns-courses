# Hands-on — Shapes countries come in — and Ethiopia’s compact shape

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Shape hunt
On a map of Africa, find one country for each shape class you can. Draw each outline small in a row and label its class.

## The enclosing circle
Trace Ethiopia’s outline. Draw the smallest circle that holds it all. The land fills most of the circle — that is what compact means.
