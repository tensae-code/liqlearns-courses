# Shapes countries come in — and Ethiopia’s compact shape

## Do it first

Look at the shapes of five countries on a map. Which one is long and thin? Which is broken into pieces? Which is nearly round? Read on for the names of the shapes.

## Sub lesson 1 — Five shapes

Countries come in five shape classes. A compact country is nearly round — its centre is about as far from every border. An elongated country is long and thin. A fragmented country is broken into pieces, often islands. A prorupt country has a long arm sticking out. A perforated country wraps around another country inside it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia’s compact shape

Ethiopia is more or less compact — close to circular. Compactness is measured by a ratio: the area of the country divided by the area of the smallest circle that can enclose it. The closer that ratio is to one, the more compact the country is.
