# Video script — Shapes countries come in — and Ethiopia’s compact shape

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Countries have shapes, like fields have shapes.
Round — compact. Long — elongated. Broken — fragmented.
One with an arm — prorupt. One with a hole — perforated.
Ethiopia? Nearly round. Compact.
