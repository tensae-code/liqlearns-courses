# Shapes countries come in — and Ethiopia’s compact shape — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A nearly round country is —
a) elongated  b) fragmented  c) compact
**Answer: c) compact**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A country broken into pieces, often islands, is —
a) prorupt  b) fragmented  c) perforated
**Answer: b) fragmented**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Compactness is measured by —
a) the country’s area divided by the area of the smallest circle enclosing it  b) the length of its longest border  c) the number of its neighbours
**Answer: a) the country’s area divided by the area of the smallest circle enclosing it**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia’s shape is —
a) elongated  b) fragmented  c) more or less compact
**Answer: c) more or less compact**
