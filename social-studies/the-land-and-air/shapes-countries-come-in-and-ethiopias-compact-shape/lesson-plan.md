# Lesson plan — Shapes countries come in — and Ethiopia’s compact shape

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Shapes countries come in — and Ethiopia’s compact shape
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air climb of the Social Studies course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** countries are classed as compact, elongated, fragmented, prorupt and perforated — and Ethiopia is more or less compact.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ethiopia on the grid — its extremes and size.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the five country shapes.
2. Describe each shape.
3. Say how compactness is measured.
4. Give Ethiopia’s shape class.
