# Parts of a research project — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five elements in working order.
**Answer:** Problem identification/formulation, problem statement, objectives, research questions, literature review (then conclusion and recommendation).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do specific objectives prevent?
**Answer:** Unnecessary data collection.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do research questions do for the study?
**Answer:** They point the study in a direction.
