# Parts of a research project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The problem is formulated through discussion and…
a) guessing  b) reading conceptual and empirical literature  c) voting
**Answer: b) reading conceptual and empirical literature**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Problem statements can be sourced from…
a) professional experience and theory  b) dreams  c) headlines only
**Answer: a) professional experience and theory**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Specific objectives…
a) widen the study  b) focus the study and prevent unnecessary data collection  c) replace questions
**Answer: b) focus the study and prevent unnecessary data collection**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Objectives split into general and…
a) hidden  b) specific  c) personal
**Answer: b) specific**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Research questions are drawn from…
a) the objectives  b) the problem statement  c) the conclusion
**Answer: b) the problem statement**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The final parts are conclusion and…
a) recommendation  b) introduction  c) appendix
**Answer: a) recommendation**
