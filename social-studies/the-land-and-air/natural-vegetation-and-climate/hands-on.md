Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Natural vegetation vs agriculture (cultivated vegetation); vegetation types — forest, grassland, desert — set by rainfall (over 100 cm forest, 25–100 cm grassland, under 25 cm desert) and temperature. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
