# Hands-on — Sweden's Climate — a Long Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three homes, two seasons
Imagine a home in Sweden's south, middle and north. For each, say one thing about its summer and one about its winter, using only what the lesson teaches.

## Draw the long country
Draw a long thin country, mark south, middle and north, and write the season word that changes most at each mark.
