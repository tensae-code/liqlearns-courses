# Video script — Sweden's Climate — a Long Country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sweden is a long country, and its Grade 1 book teaches climate from that length: where you live — in the south, the middle or the north — changes your summer and winter. The teacher guide's notes are for the textbook's pages 62–66.

The same seasons are not the same along the country. The lesson holds the three parts — south, middle, north — side by side, so the pupil sees summer and winter change as you travel the length of Sweden.
