# Sweden's Climate — a Long Country

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: climate in a long country: living in the south, middle or north of Sweden changes summer and winter, as Sweden's Grade 1 teacher guide notes the textbook. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — A long country — south, middle and north

Sweden is a long country, and its Grade 1 book teaches climate from that length: where you live — in the south, the middle or the north — changes your summer and winter. The teacher guide's notes are for the textbook's pages 62–66.

## Sub lesson 2 — Summer and winter change along the country

The same seasons are not the same along the country. The lesson holds the three parts — south, middle, north — side by side, so the pupil sees summer and winter change as you travel the length of Sweden.

Sweden's own way: Geography starts from the child's own surroundings — Sweden's place on the globe, the home town, Swedish nature and its animals, and sorting the household's own waste — taught with big pictures and sorting games for pupils who read and write only a little.
