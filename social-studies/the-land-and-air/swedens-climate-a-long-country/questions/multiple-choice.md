# Sweden's Climate — a Long Country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) A long country — south, middle and north  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) A long country — south, middle and north**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) climate in a long country: living in the south, middle or north of Sweden changes summer and winter, as Sweden's Grade 1 teacher guide notes the textbook  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) climate in a long country: living in the south, middle or north of Sweden changes summer and winter, as Sweden's Grade 1 teacher guide notes the textbook**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Summer and winter change along the country  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) Summer and winter change along the country**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Sweden is a long country, and living in its south, middle or north changes summer and winter.  b) Sweden's climate is exactly the same in the south, the middle and the north.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about Sweden
**Answer: a) Sweden is a long country, and living in its south, middle or north changes summer and winter.**
