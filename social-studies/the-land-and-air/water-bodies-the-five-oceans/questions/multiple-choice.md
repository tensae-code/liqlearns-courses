# Water Bodies — The Five Oceans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oceans are the largest water bodies in —
a) area covered only  b) volume held only  c) area covered and volume held
**Answer: c) area covered and volume held**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The largest ocean is —
a) the Atlantic, 76.8 million km²  b) the Pacific, 155.1 million km²  c) the Indian, 68.1 million km²
**Answer: b) the Pacific, 155.1 million km²**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The smallest ocean in the table is —
a) the Southern, 20.3  b) the Arctic, 14.1 million km²  c) the Indian
**Answer: b) the Arctic, 14.1 million km²**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Red Sea belongs to which ocean's list?
a) the Pacific  b) the Atlantic  c) the Indian
**Answer: c) the Indian**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The smallest ocean is —
a) the Arctic  b) the Atlantic  c) the Indian
**Answer: a) the Arctic**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** The Puerto Rico Trench lies in —
a) the Atlantic  b) the Pacific  c) the Indian Ocean
**Answer: a) the Atlantic**

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** Challenger Deep is in —
a) the Mariana Trench  b) the Puerto Rico Trench  c) the Red Sea
**Answer: a) the Mariana Trench**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** The Southern Ocean was delimited in —
a) 2000  b) 1900  c) 2000 BC
**Answer: a) 2000**
