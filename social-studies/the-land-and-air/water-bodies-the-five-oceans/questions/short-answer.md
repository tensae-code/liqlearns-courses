# Water Bodies — The Five Oceans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are oceans called the largest water bodies?
**Answer:** They are largest in area covered and in volume of water held.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Rank the oceans smallest to largest.
**Answer:** Arctic, Southern, Indian, Atlantic, Pacific.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two seas of the Pacific from the table.
**Answer:** Any two of: Coral, Japan, China, Java seas.
