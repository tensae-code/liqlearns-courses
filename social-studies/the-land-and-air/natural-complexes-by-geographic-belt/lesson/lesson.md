## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the book's own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Tolybekova): the Mektep course teaches geographic nomenclature continent by continent (§§6–8: Africa/Australia/Antarctica, the Americas, Eurasia) with a practical on completing an outline map, then works through the natural complexes belt by belt (§§39–47: arctic, temperate forest/steppe, desert, tropical, subequatorial, equatorial); Kazakhstan anchors the applied ends — its regions' flora and fauna (§33), its soil types and soil resources (§§34–35), its population (§52, Practical №3) and its own economic-geographic position (§62).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Tolybekova 2017): the 2017 first edition of the Mektep course (Book 5 is its 2025 revision): the same continent-by-continent nomenclature and belt-by-belt natural-complexes architecture, with a lithosphere field-research practical, a §52 «Человек — часть биосферы» (The human being as part of the biosphere), and a closing §60 «Этот многоликий мир» (This many-faced world) survey of the world's diversity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
