# Video script — Jamaica's Physical Features — Mountains, Plains and Coast

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jamaica's Grade 4 Exploratory Core guide (Social Studies Term 2 Unit 1, printed p. 199) teaches the main physical features of Jamaica — the lesson's own title names its three headline kinds: mountains, plains and coast. Learners meet their own island's land first, as Jamaica itself, before wider landform work. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence.

Jamaica's own way (Grade 4 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): Social Studies is one of four subjects fused into a single Exploratory Core guide, organised by four attainment themes; Geography enters through Jamaica itself — Taino settlement maps, cardinal points, Jamaica's physical features, parishes, weather and the Earth in the Solar System — always with hands-on map and model work. That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
