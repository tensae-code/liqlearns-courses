# Hands-on — Mass Movement — Slope Failure and Landslides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Watch a slope
Find a slope near you — a bank, a cutting, a hillside. Say what loose material sits on it and what could start it moving downhill.

## Case-study check
Pick one case study from the lesson — Tobago, Hong Kong or Jamaica — and tell your companion what moved and where it happened.
