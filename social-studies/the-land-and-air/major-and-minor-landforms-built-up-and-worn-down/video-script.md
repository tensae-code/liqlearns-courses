# Video script — Major and minor landforms — built up and worn down

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The major landforms are the big shapes of the land: mountains, plateaus and plains.

Inside and between the majors sit the minor landforms: hills, gorges, valleys and basins.

Two families of processes make all of it.
