# Major and minor landforms — built up and worn down — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A basin is one of the major landforms.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Valleys and gorges are minor landforms.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** External processes push new land up from inside the Earth.
**Answer: False**
