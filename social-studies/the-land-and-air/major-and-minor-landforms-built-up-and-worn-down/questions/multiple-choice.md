# Major and minor landforms — built up and worn down — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three major landforms are —
a) mountains, plateaus and plains  b) hills, gorges and basins  c) rivers, lakes and seas
**Answer: a) mountains, plateaus and plains**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A gorge is —
a) a wide flat plain  b) a hollow that drains nowhere  c) a deep, narrow cut in the land
**Answer: c) a deep, narrow cut in the land**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Abbay Gorge was cut mainly by —
a) wind alone  b) river erosion  c) volcanic eruption
**Answer: b) river erosion**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Plate tectonics and vulcanism are —
a) internal processes that push land up  b) external processes that wear land down  c) names of minor landforms
**Answer: a) internal processes that push land up**
