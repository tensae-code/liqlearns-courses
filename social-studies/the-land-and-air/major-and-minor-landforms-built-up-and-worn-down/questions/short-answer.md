# Major and minor landforms — built up and worn down — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three major landforms.
**Answer:** Mountains, plateaus and plains.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a basin?
**Answer:** A hollow in the land that the surrounding ground drains into.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How deep is the Abbay Gorge, and between which two places?
**Answer:** About 1,000 metres deep, between Goha Tsiyon and Dejen.
