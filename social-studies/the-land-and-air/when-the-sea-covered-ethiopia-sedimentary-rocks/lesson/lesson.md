# When the sea covered Ethiopia — sedimentary rocks

## Do it first

Hold a piece of limestone thinking: this was once a sea floor, where Ethiopia now stands. Read how the sea came and went.

## Sub lesson 1 — The sea advances

In the Triassic and Jurassic periods, the sea advanced — transgressed — over the land from the south-east. Under that sea, sediments settled layer on layer: sandstones, limestones and shales. These are Ethiopia’s sedimentary sequences, still found under and around the highlands.

## Sub lesson 2 — The sea retreats

Later the sea withdrew — regression — and the former sea floor was exposed as land again. The sedimentary layers it left behind became the middle storey of Ethiopia’s geology: basement below, sediments in the middle, and (in the next lesson) volcanic rocks on top.
