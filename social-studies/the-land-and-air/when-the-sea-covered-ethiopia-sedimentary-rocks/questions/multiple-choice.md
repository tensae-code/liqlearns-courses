# When the sea covered Ethiopia — sedimentary rocks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The sea advanced over Ethiopia in —
a) the Precambrian  b) modern times  c) the Triassic and Jurassic
**Answer: c) the Triassic and Jurassic**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Transgression means —
a) a river flooding  b) the sea advancing over the land  c) the sea drying up
**Answer: b) the sea advancing over the land**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Regression means —
a) the sea withdrawing, exposing the sea floor  b) the sea advancing  c) lava erupting
**Answer: a) the sea withdrawing, exposing the sea floor**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the geological stack, the sedimentary layers sit —
a) below the basement  b) on top of everything  c) in the middle, above the basement
**Answer: c) in the middle, above the basement**
