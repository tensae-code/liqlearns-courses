# Hands-on — When the sea covered Ethiopia — sedimentary rocks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Layer jar
In a clear jar of water, drop sand, then fine mud, and let them settle. Watch layers form — that settling is how the sea laid Ethiopia’s sedimentary rocks.

## Three storeys
Draw the geological stack of Ethiopia: basement at the bottom, sedimentary layers in the middle, volcanic rocks on top. Label each storey with its story.
