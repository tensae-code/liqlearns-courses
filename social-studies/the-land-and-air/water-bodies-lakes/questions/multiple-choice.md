# Water Bodies — Lakes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A lake is —
a) water ringed on all sides by dry land  b) a narrow opening to an ocean  c) salt water
**Answer: a) water ringed on all sides by dry land**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first lake in Table 4 is —
a) Lake Tana  b) the Caspian, 371,000 km²  c) the Bering
**Answer: b) the Caspian, 371,000 km²**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rows 2–5 of Table 4 are blank because —
a) the printer failed  b) they are a library task for pupils to complete  c) there are no other lakes
**Answer: b) they are a library task for pupils to complete** — The blanks are deliberate — the book's own task.
