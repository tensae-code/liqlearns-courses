# Water Bodies — Lakes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is a lake different from a sea in its water?
**Answer:** Lake water is comparatively fresh/drinkable; sea water is mostly salty and undrinkable.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On which two continents' boundary does the Caspian lie?
**Answer:** Europe and Asia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What must you collect for rows 2–5?
**Answer:** The next great lakes, their areas and their continents — then rank them.
