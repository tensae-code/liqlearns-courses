# Africa's climate types — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which climate has hot, wet weather all year at the equator?
a) Semi-arid  b) Equatorial  c) Desert  d) Subtropical highland
**Answer: b) Equatorial** — the rainforest climate.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The savanna's climate is —
a) tropical wet-and-dry  b) equatorial  c) temperate  d) polar
**Answer: a) tropical wet-and-dry** — wet and dry seasons under year-round heat.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Temperate climates in Africa are —
a) the most common  b) rare  c) everywhere  d) only at the equator
**Answer: b) rare** — the continent sits mostly in and near the tropics.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Across Africa, what varies more?
a) Temperature  b) Rainfall  c) Day length  d) Altitude
**Answer: b) Rainfall** — climates are told apart by water, not heat.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The subtropical ridge makes deserts by —
a) bringing rain  b) subsiding, sinking dry air  c) cooling the sea  d) raising mountains
**Answer: b) subsiding, sinking dry air** — the sunniest, driest places on Earth.
