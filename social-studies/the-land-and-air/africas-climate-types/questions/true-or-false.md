# Africa's climate types — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Semi-arid climate is the Sahel's climate.
**Answer: True** — steppe and semi-desert.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's most widespread climate is temperate.
**Answer: False** — temperate climates are rare.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In most of Africa the temperature changes greatly between seasons.
**Answer: False** — temperature varies little; rainfall varies enormously.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Africa holds some of the hottest places ever measured.
**Answer: True** — the heat records stand in its deserts.
