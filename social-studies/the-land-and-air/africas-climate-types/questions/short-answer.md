# Africa's climate types — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the six climate types.
**Answer:** Equatorial, tropical wet-and-dry, tropical monsoon, semi-arid, desert, and subtropical highland.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why are Africa's climates told apart mainly by rainfall?
**Answer:** Because temperature stays strong and fairly even through the year in most places, while rainfall ranges from year-round rain to almost none, and swings between seasons.
