# Hands-on — Africa's climate types

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Six-climate key
Make a six-row key: climate type, its belt or place, its rain in three words. Test a partner: read the rain words, they name the type.

## Rain-not-heat diary
For one week, note each day: hot or not, rain or not. At week's end write which varied more — and compare with the lesson's claim for Africa.
