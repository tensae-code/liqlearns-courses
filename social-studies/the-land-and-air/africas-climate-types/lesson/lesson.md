# Africa's climate types

## Do it first

Africa is called the hot continent — but is it one climate? Guess how many climate types a single continent this size holds, from rainforest to snow-capped peak. Then read the list.

## Sub lesson 1 — The list — six African climate types

The continent's climates, as the book lists them: **equatorial** — hot and wet all year at the equator; **tropical wet-and-dry** — the savanna climate of wet and dry seasons; **tropical monsoon** — a seasonal wind climate with a heavy wet season; **semi-arid** — steppe and semi-desert, the Sahel's climate; **desert** — arid and hyper-arid, the Sahara and its southern mirrors; and **subtropical highland** — the mild climate of high ground in the subtropics.

**Temperate climates are rare** in Africa — the continent sits mostly between and near the tropics, so the cool middle-latitude climates of other continents appear only in small far-edge and highland pockets.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rainfall varies more than temperature

Across most of Africa, **temperature varies little** through the year — the sun is strong in every month — while **rainfall varies enormously**: between places, from rainforest to desert, and between seasons, from flood to drought in the same town. Africa's climates are therefore told apart more by **water** than by heat.

And the driest extreme: under the **subtropical ridge**, air subsides — sinks and dries — over the tropics' edges, making the great deserts the **sunniest, driest places on Earth**. This is also the continent of heat records: the hottest places ever measured stand in its deserts.
