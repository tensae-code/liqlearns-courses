## Sub lesson 2 — Bangladesh's way — the book's own teaching way

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 7): Geography thins into economic history and a long Sustainable Development inquiry: pupils build a delta in a sand tray, trace the rivers that enter Bangladesh from other countries, map the country's new maritime boundary and its minerals, and audit groundwater and fossil fuels — the delta and the sea are the classroom. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
