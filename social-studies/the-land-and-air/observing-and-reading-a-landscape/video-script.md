# Video script — Observing And Reading A Landscape

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente* (printed p. 8), teaches: ¡Venezuela a la vista! / ¿Cómo mirar un pedacito de nuestra Venezuela Petrolera?. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela* (printed p. 58), teaches: El espacio geográfico donde vivo y comparto: the walk through one's own geographic space.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): the whole Geography block is one argument — "Nuestro espacio geográfico en tiempos del petróleo": how oil remade population (2011 census), cities (metropolisation), industry location, internal migration and landscapes; learners start by learning how to *look* at a piece of oil-era Venezuela.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.
