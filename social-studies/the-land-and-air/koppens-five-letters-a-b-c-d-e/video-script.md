# Video script — Köppen's five letters — A B C D E

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Köppen built his system on vegetation, because plants answer to temperature and moisture together.

A is tropical: every month above 18 °C.

A, C and D climates grow trees; B and E generally do not.
