# Köppen's five letters — A B C D E

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: can five letters name every climate on Earth? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Built on vegetation

Köppen built his system on vegetation, because plants answer to temperature and moisture together. Where the plants change, the climate letter changes.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five letters

A is tropical: every month above 18 °C. B is dry: dryness, not cold, is the control. C is mesothermal, the mild climates. D is microthermal, the cold climates. E is polar: the warmest month stays below 10 °C. Highland H was added later, for mountains.

## Sub lesson 3 — Who grows trees

A, C and D climates grow trees; B and E generally do not. The book sets today's Köppen–Geiger world map beside the projected future one, so you can watch the letters themselves move as the world warms.
