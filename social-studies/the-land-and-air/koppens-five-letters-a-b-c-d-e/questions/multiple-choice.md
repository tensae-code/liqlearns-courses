# Köppen's five letters — A B C D E — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Köppen's system is built on —
a) vegetation responding to temperature and moisture  b) rivers  c) soil colour
**Answer: a) vegetation responding to temperature and moisture**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate A has every month above —
a) 18 °C  b) 10 °C  c) 0 °C
**Answer: a) 18 °C**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In climate B, the control is —
a) dryness, not cold  b) cold, not dryness  c) wind
**Answer: a) dryness, not cold**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The letter added later for mountains is —
a) H  b) F  c) W
**Answer: a) H**
