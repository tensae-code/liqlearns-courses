# Köppen's five letters — A B C D E — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** E climates have a warmest month below 10 °C.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** B climates generally grow forests.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A, C and D climates grow trees.
**Answer: True**
