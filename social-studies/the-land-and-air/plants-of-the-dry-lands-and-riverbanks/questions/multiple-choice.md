# Plants of the dry lands and riverbanks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Xerophytes are —
a) water plants  b) forest trees  c) drought-resisting plants
**Answer: c) drought-resisting plants**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The arid lowlands’ cover is —
a) grassland only  b) sparse thorn scrub  c) dense rainforest
**Answer: b) sparse thorn scrub**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Riverine vegetation is —
a) dense green strips along permanent rivers  b) plants in lakes  c) desert scrub
**Answer: a) dense green strips along permanent rivers**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Riverine strips are fed by —
a) rain alone  b) irrigation only  c) the river
**Answer: c) the river**
