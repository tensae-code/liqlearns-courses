# Plants of the dry lands and riverbanks

## Do it first

Two scenes from the same dry land: thorn scrub stretching to the horizon — and one green ribbon where a river runs. Read both.

## Sub lesson 1 — Semi-desert and desert plants

The arid lowlands carry semi-desert and desert vegetation: drought-resisting plants called xerophytes — thorn scrub and sparse, tough growth built to survive long dry months on little water.

[Picture 21090 — sparse thorn scrub beside a green river strip]

## Sub lesson 2 — Riverine vegetation

Through the dry country, the permanent rivers carry riverine vegetation: strips of dense green forest and thicket along the banks, fed by the river when all around is dry. On a vegetation map they draw the rivers in green.
