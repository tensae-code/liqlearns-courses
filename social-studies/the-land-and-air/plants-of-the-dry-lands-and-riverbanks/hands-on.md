# Hands-on — Plants of the dry lands and riverbanks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-scene drawing
Draw the lesson’s two scenes on one page: dry scrub to the horizon, one green river ribbon crossing it. Label the water source of each scene.

## Survival list
List what a xerophyte must survive — months without rain, fierce sun, grazing — and match each to a feature you can observe in a real thorn plant nearby.
