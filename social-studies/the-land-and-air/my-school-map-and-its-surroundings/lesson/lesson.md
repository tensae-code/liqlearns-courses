## Sub lesson 2 — Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 5 SD Geography 5): The first-ever Geography book in the post-2019 curriculum starts from the child's own classroom and school: directions are learned by standing facing the sunrise, map elements through a pupil's-house map, and only then does the book step out to Sudan, Africa and the world — a local-to-global ladder. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
