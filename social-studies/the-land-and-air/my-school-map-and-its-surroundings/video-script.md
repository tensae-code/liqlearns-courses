# Video script — My School Map And Its Surroundings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sudan's book, the *Sudan Grade 5 SD Geography 5* (printed p. 22), teaches: My school (1–4): school map, neighbouring buildings, routes and keeping the school environment clean.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 5 SD Geography 5): The first-ever Geography book in the post-2019 curriculum starts from the child's own classroom and school: directions are learned by standing facing the sunrise, map elements through a pupil's-house map, and only then does the book step out to Sudan, Africa and the world — a local-to-global ladder.
