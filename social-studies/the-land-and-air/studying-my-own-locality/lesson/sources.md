**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Mongolia, Cuba).**

Mongolia's own way: the Grade 8 year is the LOCAL year — every chapter is re-anchored to the child's own орон нутаг (local area/homeland): its environment and problems, its heritage, its economy and market, its governance. That way is stocked here as the way Mongolia teaches this thing. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Searchable tags: locality, орон нутаг, local environment, fieldwork, Mongolia, Нийгэм судлал VIII, social studies, the land and air

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 6 Cuaderno de actividades G6): The workbook is the practice twin of the G6 student book and the Atlas Escolar — nearly every activity is done ON a map (tracing coordinates, shading the insular shelf, locating features on outline maps) — and it is where the programme's fifth unit, "El paisaje de la localidad", actually lives: the student book has no locality chapter, but the workbook closes with sketching, describing and defending the child's own locality. Section 2 is pure tools: outline maps of the world and of Cuba plus graph paper.
