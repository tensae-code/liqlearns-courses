# Hands-on — Belarus Regions — Grodno, Mogilev and Minsk

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Region cards
Make a card for each of Grodno, Mogilev and Minsk regions: name, and two facts from the lesson for each.

## The whole country
Write the six regions of Belarus in the book's order — Brest, Vitebsk, Gomel, Grodno, Mogilev, Minsk — from memory.
