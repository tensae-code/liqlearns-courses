# Video script — Limestone and Tropical Karst Landscapes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC teaches limestone in Chapter 6: the characteristics of limestone, the rock that water works on and through to carve a karst landscape.

The chapter builds the features of tropical karst and then reads them in the Caribbean itself — the karst of the Bahamas, Jamaica and Puerto Rico as its case studies.
