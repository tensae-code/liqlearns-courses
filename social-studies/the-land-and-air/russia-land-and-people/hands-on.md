## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 20: The Russian Federation — position (about 17 million km² across two continents, plus Kaliningrad) and nature (the East European and West Siberian plains and the Urals; temperate-to-subarctic climates; the Volga, Ob, Yenisei and Lena and Lake Baikal; taiga — a fifth of the world's forests; minerals — 37.4 trillion m³ of gas; 37,000 km of coast), and population (145.9 million, declining and ageing, over 100 ethnic groups, three quarters west of the Urals, Moscow about 13 million).
