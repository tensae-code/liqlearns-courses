# Weathering — Chemical Weathering and Caves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Which rock does rainwater's weak acid dissolve, in the lesson?
**Answer:** Limestone.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three cave growths and where each stands.
**Answer:** Stalactites hang from the roof; stalagmites rise from the floor; pillars stand where the two meet.
