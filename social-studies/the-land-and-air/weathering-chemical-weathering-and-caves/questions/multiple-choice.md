# Weathering — Chemical Weathering and Caves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chemical weathering —
a) only breaks rock smaller  b) changes the rock's substance itself  c) never happens in hot places
**Answer: b) changes the rock's substance itself**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Chemical weathering is strongest where it is —
a) cold and dry  b) hot and wet  c) windy
**Answer: b) hot and wet**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Oxidation is —
a) oxygen joining minerals  b) ice splitting rock  c) wind lifting sand
**Answer: a) oxygen joining minerals**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Carbonation is —
a) weak acids in rainwater dissolving rocks like limestone  b) roots widening cracks  c) waves cutting a coast
**Answer: a) weak acids in rainwater dissolving rocks like limestone**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A stalactite —
a) rises from the floor  b) hangs from the roof  c) is a pillar
**Answer: b) hangs from the roof**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A pillar forms where —
a) a stalactite and a stalagmite meet  b) two rivers meet  c) ice meets rock
**Answer: a) a stalactite and a stalagmite meet**
