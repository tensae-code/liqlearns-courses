# Land-Shaping Forces — Earthquakes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What holds the plates before the slip?
**Answer:** Friction.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** On what scale is an earthquake's strength measured?
**Answer:** The Richter scale.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the epicentre, exactly?
**Answer:** The point on the surface directly above the focus.
