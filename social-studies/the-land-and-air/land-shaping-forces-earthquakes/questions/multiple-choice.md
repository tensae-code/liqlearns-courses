# Land-Shaping Forces — Earthquakes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An earthquake's shaking is released when —
a) rain falls  b) strained plates suddenly slip  c) the wind drops
**Answer: b) strained plates suddenly slip**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The focus is —
a) the point on the surface  b) the starting point underground  c) the measuring scale
**Answer: b) the starting point underground**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Shaking is strongest at the —
a) focus  b) epicentre  c) coast
**Answer: b) epicentre**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Most earthquakes occur along —
a) the Atlantic rim  b) the Pacific rim — the Ring of Fire  c) the equator
**Answer: b) the Pacific rim — the Ring of Fire**
