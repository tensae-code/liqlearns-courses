# Video script — Life in the Sahara

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India's Grade 7 Our Environment book, the *India Grade 7 Our Environment* (printed p. 65), teaches: Ch.9: the Sahara — world's largest hot desert, oases, oil, Bedouin and Tuareg life, changing cultural life.

India's own way (Grade 7 Our Environment): Grade 7 is organised around "environment" in widening rings — the concept itself, then inside/changing Earth, air and water, world vegetation belts, human settlement and transport, and finally three lived-in region studies (Amazon basin, Ganga-Brahmaputra basin, Sahara and Ladakh deserts) where all the strands meet in real places.
