# Hands-on — Four things maps let you do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four uses, one map
Take any map. Ask it the four questions about two places in turn and write the four answers — location, distance, area if it applies, direction.

## Air distance
Measure a straight air distance between two towns on a map and convert it by the scale. Say why the road distance must be longer.
