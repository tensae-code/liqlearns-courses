# Four things maps let you do

## Do it first

One sheet of paper answers four questions: where, how far, how big, which way. Read the four uses of maps.

## Sub lesson 1 — Location and distance

Maps give location — by site and situation, or by grid reference. And they give distance: through the scale, a ruler’s centimetres become the ground’s kilometres — the book works the Hawassa–Addis Ababa air distance as its example.

[Picture 21119 — one map used four ways, in four panels]

## Sub lesson 2 — Area and direction

Maps give area, by measurement worked through the scale; and direction and bearing, by the compass and the north point. Location, distance, area, direction: the four uses are the reasons maps are worth their definition’s care.
