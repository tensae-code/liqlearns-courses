# Four things maps let you do — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Location on a map is given by —
a) colour  b) title  c) site and situation, or grid
**Answer: c) site and situation, or grid**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Distance comes through —
a) the date  b) the scale  c) the legend
**Answer: b) the scale**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Area is found by —
a) measurement worked through the scale  b) the north point  c) the compass only
**Answer: a) measurement worked through the scale**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The fourth use is —
a) printing  b) colouring  c) direction and bearing
**Answer: c) direction and bearing**
