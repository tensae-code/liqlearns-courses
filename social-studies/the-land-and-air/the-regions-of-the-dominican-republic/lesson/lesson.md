## Sub lesson 1 — The Regions of the Dominican Republic

Dominican Republic's Grade 12 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Sexto Grado, Secundaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 172), teaches: The current regions of the Dominican Republic: Cibao, Este, Suroeste. Dominican Republic's Grade 3 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Tercer Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 34), teaches: I know my region: regions and provinces of my country. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 12): The final secondary year turns from national history to the country's present-day human geography, studied region by region: 150 years of population growth, accelerated urbanisation, the economy's branches and foreign trade, and the spatial distribution of the population across Cibao, Este and Suroeste, closing on migration and Santo Domingo as a fragmented metropolis. Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 3): For third graders geography is the home island first: the planet and its components, then the Antilles and the relief of their own country, then countryside versus city, and finally where their own families came from — migration and a family census the children run themselves. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
