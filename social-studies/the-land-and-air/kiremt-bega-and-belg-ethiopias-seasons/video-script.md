# Video script — Kiremt, Bega and Belg — Ethiopia’s seasons

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Three seasons turn Ethiopia’s year.
Kiremt — the main rains. Bega — the dry winter.
Belg — the small spring rains.
Above them all, the rain belt slides north, then south.
