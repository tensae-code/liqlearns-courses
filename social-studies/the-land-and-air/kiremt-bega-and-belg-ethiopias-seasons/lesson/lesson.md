# Kiremt, Bega and Belg — Ethiopia’s seasons

## Do it first

Think of your year in three parts, not four: the big rains, the dry, the small rains. Name them before you read.

## Sub lesson 1 — The three seasons

Ethiopia’s year has three seasons. Kiremt is the main rainy season, the summer rains on which the main harvest depends. Bega is the dry winter season. Belg is the small spring rainy season, vital where it is the main rain for local crops.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why the seasons move

The seasons follow the overhead sun and the movement of the Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone — the belt where winds meet and rain forms. As the ITCZ shifts north and south over the year, the rain belts shift with it, giving each part of Ethiopia its own season pattern.
