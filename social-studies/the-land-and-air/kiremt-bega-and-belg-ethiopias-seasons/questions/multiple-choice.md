# Kiremt, Bega and Belg — Ethiopia’s seasons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The main rainy season is —
a) Bega  b) Belg  c) Kiremt
**Answer: c) Kiremt**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The dry winter season is —
a) Belg  b) Bega  c) Kiremt
**Answer: b) Bega**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The seasons follow the overhead sun and —
a) the movement of the ITCZ  b) ocean tides  c) volcanic activity
**Answer: a) the movement of the ITCZ**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** ITCZ stands for —
a) Internal Temperature Control Zone  b) International Trade Centre Zone  c) Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone
**Answer: c) Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone**
