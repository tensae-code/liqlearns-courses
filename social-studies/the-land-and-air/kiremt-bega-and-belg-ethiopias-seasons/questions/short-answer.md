# Kiremt, Bega and Belg — Ethiopia’s seasons — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which season does the main harvest depend on?
**Answer: Kiremt, the main summer rains.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What forms in the ITCZ belt?
**Answer: Rain — it is the belt where winds meet and rain forms.**
