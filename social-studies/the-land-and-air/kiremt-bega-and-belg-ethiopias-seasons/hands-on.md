# Hands-on — Kiremt, Bega and Belg — Ethiopia’s seasons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Year wheel
Draw a circle for the year and divide it into the three seasons. Mark beside each season what a farming family is doing then, where you live.

## ITCZ tracking
Draw Ethiopia three times — north-shifted rain belt, overhead, south-shifted — and shade where the rain falls in each.
