## Sub lesson 1 — Caracas The Capital City

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela* (printed p. 134), teaches: La Capital de la República (the Caracas valley, Distrito Capital). Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente* (printed p. 22), teaches: "Caracas, allí está…": Caracas and the Waraira Repano. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the book's own teaching way

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela): the second half is a state-by-state tour of Venezuela (territorial organisation, Nueva Esparta, Sucre, the Capital, Vargas, Zulia, the Andean states, the Llanos, Guayana) taught through local stories and children of each region — regional geography as a journey around the child's own country.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): the whole Geography block is one argument — "Nuestro espacio geográfico en tiempos del petróleo": how oil remade population (2011 census), cities (metropolisation), industry location, internal migration and landscapes; learners start by learning how to *look* at a piece of oil-era Venezuela. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
