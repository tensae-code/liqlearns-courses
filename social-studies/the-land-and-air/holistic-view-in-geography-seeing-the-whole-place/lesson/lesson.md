## Sub lesson 1 — Holistic View In Geography Seeing The Whole Place

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran* (printed p. 4), teaches: Place, phenomenon, geographical environment and the holistic/combined view (کل‌نگری) in geography. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran): the whole of Grade 10 geography is one national book — Iran's own natural and human geography for every branch; nearly every lesson closes with a "know your own living place" activity tied to the separate provincial-studies book, so national content is always re-applied to the learner's own province. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
