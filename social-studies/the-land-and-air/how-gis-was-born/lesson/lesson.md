# How GIS was born

## Do it first

Before you read, think of the oldest map idea — a drawing scratched to show where water lies — and the newest — a map on a screen that answers questions. What had to be invented in between? Guess the steps, then read.

## Sub lesson 1 — From hand maps to data systems

Mapmaking developed over centuries: better surveys, printing, and standard symbols made maps accurate and common. But a paper map only shows; it cannot store layers, search them, or work on them. That limit is what the computer removed.

## Sub lesson 2 — The GIS idea

A Geographic Information System is the computer-based answer: a system to store geographic data, visualise it as maps, analyse it for patterns, and interpret what the patterns mean. GIS was born when mapmaking's drawings became data a machine could work with.
