# How GIS was born — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What limit of the paper map did the computer remove?
a) it could only show, not store, search or work on layers  b) it was too colourful  c) it showed too little land
**Answer: a) it could only show, not store, search or work on layers**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A GIS is built to —
a) replace all fieldwork  b) print paper maps only  c) store, visualise, analyse and interpret geographic data
**Answer: c) store, visualise, analyse and interpret geographic data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** GIS was born when —
a) paper was invented  b) mapmaking's drawings became data a machine could work with  c) the first satellite launched
**Answer: b) mapmaking's drawings became data a machine could work with**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** To interpret, in GIS, means —
a) to say what the patterns mean  b) to print the map  c) to file the data away
**Answer: a) to say what the patterns mean**
