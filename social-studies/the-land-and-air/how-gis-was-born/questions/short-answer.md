# How GIS was born — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four jobs a GIS does with geographic data.
**Answer:** Store, visualise, analyse and interpret.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What changed when maps became data?
**Answer:** They could be stored in layers, searched, and worked on by a computer — not only shown.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What does GIS stand for?
**Answer:** Geographic Information System.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four jobs again, in order, as a story: data goes in, and then?
**Answer:** It is stored, visualised as maps, analysed for patterns, and interpreted — what the patterns mean is drawn out.
