The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Mapmaking developed over centuries: better surveys, printing, and standard symbols made maps accurate and common.

A Geographic Information System is the computer-based answer: a system to store geographic data, visualise it as maps, analyse it for patterns, and interpret what the patterns mean.
