# How wide geography reaches — the five spheres — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The waters form the —
a) lithosphere  b) biosphere  c) hydrosphere
**Answer: c) hydrosphere**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The human world is the —
a) hydrosphere  b) anthroposphere  c) atmosphere
**Answer: b) anthroposphere**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Geography’s scope is —
a) wide and dynamic, but not limitless  b) limitless  c) only the land surface
**Answer: a) wide and dynamic, but not limitless**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide’s key names —
a) five spheres  b) three spheres  c) four spheres
**Answer: c) four spheres**
