# How wide geography reaches — the five spheres

## Do it first

Wrap the earth in five shells: rock, water, air, life, and humankind. Geography studies them all. Read the scope.

## Sub lesson 1 — The five spheres

Geography’s scope is wide and dynamic. It covers the hydrosphere (the waters), the atmosphere (the air), the lithosphere (the rock shell), the biosphere (the living world) and the anthroposphere (the human world) — the earth’s position and movements, its surface features and the forces behind them, weather and climate, earth materials and landforms, human–environment relations, and human economic activity and its impacts.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wide, not limitless (and the guide’s four)

Wide is not limitless: geography studies these things as they appear on and relate to the earth’s surface. The teacher guide’s answer key pins the scope question to four spheres — hydrosphere, biosphere, atmosphere, lithosphere (teacher guide — evidence, a marking reference) — while the student book teaches five, adding the anthroposphere. The pupil teaching is the five.
