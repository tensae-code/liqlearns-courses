# Video script — How wide geography reaches — the five spheres

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Five shells wrap the earth.
Rock — lithosphere. Water — hydrosphere. Air — atmosphere.
Life — biosphere. Humankind — anthroposphere.
Geography reaches across all five. Wide — but not limitless.
