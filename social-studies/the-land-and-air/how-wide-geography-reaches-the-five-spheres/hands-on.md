# Hands-on — How wide geography reaches — the five spheres

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sphere sorting
Sort twelve item cards — river, wind, basalt, lion, farm, rain, soil, town, glacier, market, forest, volcano — into the five spheres; some cards argue for two piles, and you must defend your choice.

## Scope boundary
With a partner, judge three questions: the moon’s craters; Ethiopia’s coffee trade; the inside of an atom. Which are geography’s business, by the lesson’s boundary rule?
