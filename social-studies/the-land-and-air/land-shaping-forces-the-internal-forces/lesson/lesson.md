## Sub lesson 1 — Forces that rise from inside

You have heard of earthquakes — this section explains where such power comes from. Internal forces rise from inside the Earth. There are two great kinds: diastrophism and volcanism.

[Picture 11458 — a cut-away Earth with heat arrows rising into a warping crust]

## Sub lesson 2 — Diastrophism and volcanism — together, tectonics

Diastrophism twists, cracks, warps and lifts the crust. Volcanism moves magma, which cools and hardens. Together the two are the tectonic processes. They are powered by heat circulation inside the Earth, by radioactivity, and by the expansion and contraction of lithosphere material.

## Sub lesson 3 — Their work: building relief

The main work of the internal forces is to build the Earth's relief — the ups and downs of the surface: mountains up, basins down. Everything the external forces later wear away was first built by these forces from inside.
