# Land-Shaping Forces — The Internal Forces — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two great kinds of internal force.
**Answer:** Diastrophism and volcanism.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one thing that powers the tectonic processes.
**Answer:** Any one of: heat circulation, radioactivity, expansion and contraction of lithosphere material.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does volcanism move, which then cools and hardens?
**Answer:** Magma.
