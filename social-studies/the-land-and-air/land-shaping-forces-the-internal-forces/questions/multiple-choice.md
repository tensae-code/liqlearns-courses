# Land-Shaping Forces — The Internal Forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Internal forces rise from —
a) the Sun  b) inside the Earth  c) the oceans
**Answer: b) inside the Earth**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Diastrophism and volcanism together are called —
a) the gradational forces  b) the tectonic processes  c) denudation
**Answer: b) the tectonic processes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Diastrophism does what to the crust?
a) dissolves it  b) twists, cracks, warps and lifts it  c) freezes it
**Answer: b) twists, cracks, warps and lifts it**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The main work of the internal forces is to —
a) level the relief  b) build the Earth's relief  c) make rain
**Answer: b) build the Earth's relief**
