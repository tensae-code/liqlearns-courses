# Video script — Evidence for continental drift

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four independent lines of evidence — fit, rocks, fossils and past climates — all point the same way: the continents were once joined. First, the fit and the matching rocks: The first evidence is the jigsaw fit of the coastlines: Africa fits against South America the way two torn pieces of paper fit. Then, fossils and past climates: The third evidence is fossils — above all Mesosaurus, an animal of about 250 million years ago, whose fossils are found on both sides of the Atlantic. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
