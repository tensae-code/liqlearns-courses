# Evidence for continental drift — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four evidences for drift.
**Answer:** The jigsaw fit of coastlines; matching rock types, structures and ages with mountain belts continuing across the ocean; fossil evidence such as Mesosaurus; and paleoclimatic evidence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does Mesosaurus matter so much?
**Answer:** Its fossils sit on both sides of the Atlantic, and a freshwater animal could not have crossed the ocean — so the land must once have been joined.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is paleoclimatic evidence?
**Answer:** Clues of past climates found in places where those climates do not belong today.
