# Evidence for continental drift — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The jigsaw evidence is…
a) matching fossils  b) the fit of coastlines, Africa against South America  c) the depth of the oceans
**Answer: b) the fit of coastlines, Africa against South America**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mountain belts across the ocean…
a) stop at every coast  b) continue on the matching coast  c) run north to south only
**Answer: b) continue on the matching coast**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Matching rock evidence includes…
a) types, structures and ages  b) colours only  c) today's weather
**Answer: a) types, structures and ages**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mesosaurus fossils are found…
a) only in Africa  b) only in South America  c) on both sides of the Atlantic
**Answer: c) on both sides of the Atlantic**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Mesosaurus lived about…
a) 250 million years ago  b) 25 million years ago  c) 2,500 years ago
**Answer: a) 250 million years ago**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Paleoclimatic evidence means…
a) today's weather records  b) clues of past climates in the wrong places  c) fossils of sea animals
**Answer: b) clues of past climates in the wrong places**
