# Evidence for continental drift — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The fit of Africa against South America is one evidence for drift.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Matching mountain belts break off at the coast and never continue.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mesosaurus could easily have swum across the Atlantic Ocean.
**Answer: False**
