# How climate helps people live — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four things climate underwrites.
**Answer:** Any four of: farming, water supply, health, settlement, clothing, transport, recreation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does climate shape settlement?
**Answer:** People gather where the climate allows farming and water together.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What debate does the book set at the end of the section?
**Answer:** A debate weighing climate's benefits against its disadvantages.
