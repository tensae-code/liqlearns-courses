# How climate helps people live — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate underwrites farming mainly through —
a) rainfall deciding what grows and when  b) the price of tools  c) the size of farms
**Answer: a) rainfall deciding what grows and when**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** People settle where —
a) the land is steepest  b) no rain ever falls  c) climate allows farming and water together
**Answer: c) climate allows farming and water together**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book closes this section with —
a) a list of capitals  b) a debate on benefits versus disadvantages  c) a map test
**Answer: b) a debate on benefits versus disadvantages**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Clothing is described as —
a) climate written on the body  b) unrelated to climate  c) the same everywhere in Africa
**Answer: a) climate written on the body**
