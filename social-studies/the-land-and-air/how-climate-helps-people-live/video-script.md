# Video script — How climate helps people live

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Climate underwrites farming first: the rains decide what grows and when, and the same climate fills the rivers, wells and stores people drink from.

Climate shapes health — which diseases thrive and which seasons are safe — and settlement: people gather where climate allows farming and water together.

Roads, rivers and air travel all run on climate's terms, and recreation follows the seasons too.
