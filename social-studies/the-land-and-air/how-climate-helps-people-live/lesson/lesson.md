# How climate helps people live

## Do it first

Before you read, list everything about your own day that the climate made possible or impossible: what you grew, wore, drank, and how you travelled. Keep the list — the lesson will sort it for you.

## Sub lesson 1 — Farming and water — the first gifts

Climate underwrites farming first: the rains decide what grows and when, and the same climate fills the rivers, wells and stores people drink from. No farming calendar and no water supply exists apart from the climate that feeds them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Health, settlement and clothing

Climate shapes health — which diseases thrive and which seasons are safe — and settlement: people gather where climate allows farming and water together. Even clothing is climate written on the body: thin clothes for heat, warm clothes for cold highlands.

## Sub lesson 3 — Transport, recreation — and the honest debate

Roads, rivers and air travel all run on climate's terms, and recreation follows the seasons too. The book closes the section with a set debate — benefits against disadvantages — because the same climate that feeds a farm can also fail it; the geographer's job is to weigh both.
