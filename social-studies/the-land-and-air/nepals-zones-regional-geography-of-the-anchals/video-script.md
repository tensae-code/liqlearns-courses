Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 8 np-g8* (printed p. 40), teaches: Section Kha: A short geographical account of the zones — the 14 zones table (area, population, headquarters) and the Bagmati Zone account (districts, environment, resources, development, Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur and other chief towns). Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 8 np-g8* (printed p. 46), teaches: Section Kha: Narayani Zone account (environment, economic resources and development; Birgunj, Hetauda, Bharatpur). Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 8 np-g8* (printed p. 49), teaches: Section Kha: Mechi Zone account (environment, economic resources and development; Bhadrapur, Ilam). Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 8 np-g8* (printed p. 51), teaches: Section Kha: Mahakali Zone account (environment, economic resources and development; Mahendranagar, Dadeldhura).

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 8 np-g8): this is the old kingdom-era standalone Geography volume — Nepal is taught through its 14 zones (anchal), with full regional accounts of Bagmati, Narayani, Mechi and Mahakali zones (position, environment, resources, development, chief towns, each with maps); Asia is the one continent studied in depth; world economic geography is built on "environment and man" (geographical control). Chapters marked ** in the contents (Earth's surface/interior; the population chapter) were prescribed only for certain school types.
