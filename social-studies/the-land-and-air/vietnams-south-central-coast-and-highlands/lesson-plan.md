# Lesson plan — Vietnams South Central Coast And Highlands

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Vietnams South Central Coast And Highlands
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bài 28: Socio-economic development in the South Central Coast and Central Highlands (five units including Da Nang; 15.9 million people; the Ly Son, Hoang Sa, Truong Sa and Phu Quy special zones) — sea fishing and aquaculture (35.9% of the national marine catch), sea transport (Da Nang the east–west gateway), tourism, bauxite (about 90% of the nation's reserves; the Tan Rai and Nhan Co alumina plants) and oil and gas (the Dung Quat refinery), hydropower (Ialy at 720 MW and the Se San, Srepok, Ba and Dong Nai cascades), perennial industrial crops (coffee on 678.6 thousand ha — 92.7% of the nation's coffee) and forestry (34.2% of national roundwood) — as Vietnam's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Bài 28: Socio-economic development in the South Central Coast and Central Highlands (five units including Da Nang; 15.9 million people; the Ly Son, Hoang Sa, Truong Sa and Phu Quy special zones) — sea fishing and aquaculture (35.9% of the national marine catch), sea transport (Da Nang the east–west gateway), tourism, bauxite (about 90% of the nation's reserves; the Tan Rai and Nhan Co alumina plants) and oil and gas (the Dung Quat refinery), hydropower (Ialy at 720 MW and the Se San, Srepok, Ba and Dong Nai cascades), perennial industrial crops (coffee on 678.6 thousand ha — 92.7% of the nation's coffee) and forestry (34.2% of national roundwood).
