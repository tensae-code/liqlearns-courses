# Hands-on — Vietnams South Central Coast And Highlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 28: Socio-economic development in the South Central Coast and Central Highlands (five units including Da Nang; 15.9 million people; the Ly Son, Hoang Sa, Truong Sa and Phu Quy special zones) — sea fishing and aquaculture (35.9% of the national marine catch), sea transport (Da Nang the east–west gateway), tourism, bauxite (about 90% of the nation's reserves; the Tan Rai and Nhan Co alumina plants) and oil and gas (the Dung Quat refinery), hydropower (Ialy at 720 MW and the Se San, Srepok, Ba and Dong Nai cascades), perennial industrial crops (coffee on 678.6 thousand ha — 92.7% of the nation's coffee) and forestry (34.2% of national roundwood). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
