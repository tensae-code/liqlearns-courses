Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 129–140), teaches: Bài 28: Socio-economic development in the South Central Coast and Central Highlands (five units including Da Nang; 15.9 million people; the Ly Son, Hoang Sa, Truong Sa and Phu Quy special zones) — sea fishing and aquaculture (35.9% of the national marine catch), sea transport (Da Nang the east–west gateway), tourism, bauxite (about 90% of the nation's reserves; the Tan Rai and Nhan Co alumina plants) and oil and gas (the Dung Quat refinery), hydropower (Ialy at 720 MW and the Se San, Srepok, Ba and Dong Nai cascades), perennial industrial crops (coffee on 678.6 thousand ha — 92.7% of the nation's coffee) and forestry (34.2% of national roundwood).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
