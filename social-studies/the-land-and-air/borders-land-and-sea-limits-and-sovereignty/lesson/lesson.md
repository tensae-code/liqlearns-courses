## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the book's own teaching way

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 4 G4 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): geography is the first block ("Conóceme, soy Venezuela") and is built as widening circles — the child's house drawn to a stated scale (1 cm on the plan = 1 m), then Tomás's town, then Venezuela's territory and borders, then Latin America and the unequal world.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): the whole Geography block is one argument — "Nuestro espacio geográfico en tiempos del petróleo": how oil remade population (2011 census), cities (metropolisation), industry location, internal migration and landscapes; learners start by learning how to *look* at a piece of oil-era Venezuela.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
