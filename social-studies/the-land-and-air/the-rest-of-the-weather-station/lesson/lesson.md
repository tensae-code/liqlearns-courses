# The rest of the weather station

## Do it first

Five more instruments complete the station. Each has one job. Read them one by one — this is a doing lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pressure, humidity, sunshine, cloud

Air pressure is read with a barometer. Humidity is read with a hygrometer. A sunshine recorder logs the hours of bright sun. Cloud cover is estimated by eye in oktas — eighths of the sky covered.

## Sub lesson 2 — Wind

Wind needs two measures: speed, read by an anemometer whose cups spin faster as the wind rises; and direction, shown by a wind vane pointing where the wind comes from. Together they complete the station’s seven elements.
