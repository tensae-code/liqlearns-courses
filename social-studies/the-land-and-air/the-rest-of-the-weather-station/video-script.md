# Video script — The rest of the weather station

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The rest of the station, one job each.
Barometer — pressure. Hygrometer — humidity. Sunshine recorder — bright hours.
Cloud by eye, in oktas — eighths of sky.
Wind: spinning cups for speed, a vane for direction.
