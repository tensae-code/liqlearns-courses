# The rest of the weather station — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Air pressure is read with a —
a) hygrometer  b) rain gauge  c) barometer
**Answer: c) barometer**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Humidity is read with a —
a) thermometer  b) hygrometer  c) barometer
**Answer: b) hygrometer**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wind speed is read by an —
a) anemometer  b) wind vane  c) barometer
**Answer: a) anemometer**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The wind vane shows —
a) speed  b) pressure  c) direction — where the wind comes from
**Answer: c) direction — where the wind comes from**
