# Hands-on — The rest of the weather station

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Okta practice
Go out and estimate the cloud cover in oktas, three times in a day. Compare with a partner’s estimates and argue any difference — estimation is a skill, and this is its practice.

## Vane building
Build a simple wind vane from card and a straw on a pin. Set it up, record the wind’s direction morning and evening, and say what a station would add to your record.
