# Map Scale — The Three Ways of Stating Scale — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does scale let a map do?
**Answer:** Fit a place's real features onto the map so distances can be measured.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the fraction scale called unit-free?
**Answer:** Both sides use the same unit, so no unit needs naming.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Work it: ground 100 km, map 10 cm — what is the scale?
**Answer:** 10 cm ÷ 100 km (10,000,000 cm) = 1:1,000,000.
