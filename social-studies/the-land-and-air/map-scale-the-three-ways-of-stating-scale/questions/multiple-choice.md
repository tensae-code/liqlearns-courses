# Map Scale — The Three Ways of Stating Scale — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Scale = —
a) ground distance ÷ map distance  b) map distance ÷ ground distance  c) area ÷ distance
**Answer: b) map distance ÷ ground distance**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 1:200,000 means —
a) 1 km stands for 200,000 cm  b) one unit on the map stands for 200,000 of the same unit on the ground  c) the map is 200,000 cm wide
**Answer: b) one unit on the map stands for 200,000 of the same unit on the ground**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** '1 cm to 1 km' is scale stated as —
a) a fraction  b) a statement in words  c) a drawn line
**Answer: b) a statement in words**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ground distances can be read off directly from —
a) the fraction  b) the statement  c) the drawn line (graphic) scale
**Answer: c) the drawn line (graphic) scale**
