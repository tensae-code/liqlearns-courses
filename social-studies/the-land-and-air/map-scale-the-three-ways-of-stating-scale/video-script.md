# Video script — Map Scale — The Three Ways of Stating Scale

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Scale fits the real world onto paper.

Scale is map distance divided by ground distance.

Say it three ways. A fraction: 1 to 200,000.
One unit on the map. 200,000 on the ground.

Words: 1 centimetre to 1 kilometre.

A drawn line: read the ground distance off the line.

Now work one. Ground: 100 kilometres.
Map: 10 centimetres.
100 kilometres is 10,000,000 centimetres.
10 divided by 10,000,000 is 1 to 1,000,000.
That is the scale.
