# Hands-on — Iraq's Natural Vegetation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 1 Ch. 2: Natural vegetation (النبات الطبيعي) of Iraq and how it follows the climate; Unit 1 Ch. 1: Natural vegetation (النبات الطبيعي) and its belts — the forest and mountain-herb belt of the high north-east; Unit 1 Ch. 1: The steppe belt (about 15% of Iraq) and the desert-plant belt, the widest at about 70%, whose plants are adapted to dryness.
