**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Egypt).**

Egypt's own way (Grade 5 Term 1): a home-country-first primary book — Egypt's own location (taught through longitude and latitude), surface, climate, plants and animals first, then its “treasures” (minerals, energy, water) and the state's national projects as the future vision; a robot mascot and child guides carry the explanations, with a value or skill practised in every lesson.

Egypt's own way (Grade 4 Term 1): the very first Social Studies book starts from the child's own space — drawing a plan of the home and school, comparing walking routes by length and landmarks — before maps in general, then the child's own city and governorate, and only then Egypt as homeland; a robot mascot teaches alongside named child guides from different governorates.
