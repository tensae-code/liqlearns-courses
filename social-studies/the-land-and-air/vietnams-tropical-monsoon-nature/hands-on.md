# Hands-on — Vietnams Tropical Monsoon Nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 2: Vietnam's tropical humid monsoon nature in every component — climate (mean temperatures from Lang Son's 21.3 °C to Ca Mau's 27.1 °C; 1,500–2,000 mm of rain; humidity over 80%; the north-east and south-west monsoons and the dry foehn), landforms (strong weathering, karst, erosion and the growing deltas), rivers (2,360 rivers and 839 billion m³ of water, with flood and dry seasons), ferralit soils and evergreen rainforest life — and its effects on farming and daily life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
