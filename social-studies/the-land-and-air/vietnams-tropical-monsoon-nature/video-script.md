Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 10–16), teaches: Bài 2: Vietnam's tropical humid monsoon nature in every component — climate (mean temperatures from Lang Son's 21.3 °C to Ca Mau's 27.1 °C; 1,500–2,000 mm of rain; humidity over 80%; the north-east and south-west monsoons and the dry foehn), landforms (strong weathering, karst, erosion and the growing deltas), rivers (2,360 rivers and 839 billion m³ of water, with flood and dry seasons), ferralit soils and evergreen rainforest life — and its effects on farming and daily life.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
