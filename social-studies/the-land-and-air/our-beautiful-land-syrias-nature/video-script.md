# Video script — Our Beautiful Land Syrias Nature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Syria's book, the *Syria Grade 2 Social Studies G2* (printed p. 62), teaches: Our beautiful land (Unit 5: My Environment). Syria's book, the *Syria Grade 1 Social Studies G1* (printed p. 58), teaches: Our beautiful nature.

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 2 Social Studies G2): the same widening-rings design as Grade 3 (Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland), with the Geography carried by the society/environment/homeland rings: Earth's inhabitants, building in the homeland, heritage, and an environment unit built on the land's beauty and riches, diverse nature, water drops and a windy day.

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 1 Social Studies G1): the Grade 1 book starts the same widening-rings series (Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland); Geography enters through street-crossing safety, then the environment unit — nature and the farm, the four friends (the seasons), beautiful nature, water "more precious than gold", a precious treasure, and a journey into space — and closes with the homeland's antiquities and pictures from across Syria.
