# Bar graphs from raw data — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does bar height stand for?
**Answer:** The value of that category — here, the month's rainfall.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe the worked example's journey in one line.
**Answer:** Gore Town's rainfall goes from raw data table to finished bar graph.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why draw the graph at all?
**Answer:** Because the eye compares a tall bar against a short one instantly — the comparison becomes readable at a glance.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What two axes does the builder set before drawing Gore Town's bars?
**Answer:** The months along one axis and the scaled rainfall values on the other.
