# Video script — Bar graphs from raw data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bar graphs compare independent data series — categories that stand apart, like months — by the height or length of their bars.

The book's worked example takes Gore Town's rainfall from raw data to final output: set the months along the axis, scale the rainfall axis, draw each month's bar to its height, and title and label the whole.
