## Sub lesson 1 — The regions of Belarus — Brest and Vitebsk

The closing theme of Belarus's Grade 10 book (Theme X §§58–59) takes the country's regions in turn, starting with Brest region and Vitebsk region — each region studied whole, as the administrative division set up at the book's opening is now filled in.

## Sub lesson 2 — The regions of Belarus — Gomel

Theme X §60 follows with Gomel region, the third of the six regions the book studies one by one.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
