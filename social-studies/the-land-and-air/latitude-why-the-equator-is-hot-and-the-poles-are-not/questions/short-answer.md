# Latitude — why the equator is hot and the poles are not — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is a slanting beam weaker?
**Answer:** It spreads over more ground, so each part gets less heat.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How long is an equatorial day?
**Answer:** About 12 hours, all year.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three Earth motions and features behind the seasons.
**Answer:** Revolution, rotation and axial tilt.
