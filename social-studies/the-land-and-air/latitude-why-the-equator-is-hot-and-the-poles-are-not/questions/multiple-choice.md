# Latitude — why the equator is hot and the poles are not — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Insolation is strongest where the Sun's angle is —
a) 90 degrees, overhead  b) 0 degrees  c) 45 degrees
**Answer: a) 90 degrees, overhead**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Toward the poles, the same sunbeam —
a) spreads over more ground  b) concentrates on less ground  c) stops
**Answer: a) spreads over more ground**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Equatorial days last about —
a) 12 hours all year  b) 24 hours  c) 6 hours
**Answer: a) 12 hours all year**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The seasonal march of light is driven by revolution, rotation and —
a) axial tilt  b) ocean currents  c) mountains
**Answer: a) axial tilt**
