**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Ethiopia, Turkey, Colombia, Venezuela, Mozambique, Jordan, Iraq).**

Turkey's own way: a systems course — Natural Systems first (nature–human interaction, coordinates, maps, Earth's motions, climate), then Human Systems (settlements), then Regions and Countries, then Environment and Society — with a Turkey case closing most chapters (Turkey's location, Turkey's climate, settlements in Turkey). That way is stocked here as the way Turkey teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

No book error is flagged for this lesson's pages in the placements. The student book's body numbering for Unit Two drifts from its own contents (regions printed as 2.4, factors as 2.5, local classification as 2.6, where the contents and the teacher guide's contents agree on 2.3, 2.4 and 2.5); this lesson follows the contents numbering and teaches no misprint.

Searchable tags: latitude, insolation, sun angle, seasons, axial tilt, ethiopia, social studies, the land and air

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado4_02): the second Grade 4 cartilla is almost pure physical geography of Colombia — relief first, then the tropical position and thermal floors, then a region-by-region tour of Colombia's six natural regions before the world's natural regions.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 2): a full physical-climatology course — atmosphere structure to magnetosphere, heat balance, pressure/wind circulation, air masses and fronts, humidity and precipitation, the water cycle, then Köppen/Thornthwaite classification worked through climate tables with African station graphs (Arkassa, Tamanrasset, Algiers), closing on protection of bioclimatic resources.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G4 SS T2): Term 2 ends with Jordan's region — the world around me (solar system, Earth's movement, the world's heat zones), geographic maps, then Bilad al-Sham and Iraq and the Arabian Peninsula as Jordan's neighbourhood.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 11 Physical Geography): a pure physical-geography course in six chapters — landforms, weather and climate, water, the biosphere, soil, and the environment — with Iraqi anchors kept throughout (flood visits to Iraq's rivers, the hills of northern Iraq, groundwater and the desert caravans, soil salinity in the alluvial plain).
