# Video script — Latitude — why the equator is hot and the poles are not

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Insolation — incoming solar radiation — is strongest where the Sun stands at 90 degrees, overhead at the equator.

The equator keeps 12-hour days and small seasons.

Because it sets both the Sun's angle and the day's length, latitude is the first control every other climate control must work within.
