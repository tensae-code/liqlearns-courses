# Video script — Forces from inside and outside the earth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Two families shape the land.
From inside: folding, faulting, vulcanism, uplift.
From outside: weathering and denudation, filing it all down.
Ethiopia is their argument, still running.
