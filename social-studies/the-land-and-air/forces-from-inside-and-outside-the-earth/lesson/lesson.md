# Forces from inside and outside the earth

## Do it first

One force lifts a plateau; another spends a million years filing it down. Every Ethiopian landform is their argument. Read the two force families.

## Sub lesson 1 — Endogenic forces

Endogenic forces work from inside the earth: folding (crust squeezed into bends), faulting (crust cracking and blocks moving), vulcanism (magma reaching the surface) and uplift or epeirogenesis (broad rising of the land). Ethiopia’s dome, traps and Rift are their work.

## Sub lesson 2 — Exogenic forces

Exogenic forces work from outside: weathering breaks rock down, and denudation — erosion and transport — carries the pieces away. The great Palaeozoic planing was exogenic work, and every gorge in the highlands is exogenic work still going on.
