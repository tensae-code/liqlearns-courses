# Forces from inside and outside the earth — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four endogenic forces.
**Answer: Folding, faulting, vulcanism and uplift (epeirogenesis).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two exogenic processes.
**Answer: Weathering (breaking rock down) and denudation (carrying the pieces away).**
