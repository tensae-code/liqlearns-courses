# Forces from inside and outside the earth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Endogenic forces work from —
a) outside the earth  b) the atmosphere  c) inside the earth
**Answer: c) inside the earth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Folding is —
a) sediment settling  b) the crust squeezed into bends  c) rock breaking down
**Answer: b) the crust squeezed into bends**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Denudation means —
a) erosion and transport carrying material away  b) magma rising  c) crust folding
**Answer: a) erosion and transport carrying material away**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Palaeozoic planing was —
a) endogenic work  b) volcanic work  c) exogenic work
**Answer: c) exogenic work**
