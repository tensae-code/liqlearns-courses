# Hands-on — Forces from inside and outside the earth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cloth and file
Crumple and crack a dried clay slab for the endogenic forces; then rub it with sandpaper for the exogenic ones. Photograph or sketch each stage and caption it with the force name.

## Force census
Take six Ethiopian features from earlier lessons — Rift, gorge, plateau top, planed surface, volcano, delta-less coast of a lake — and assign each its main maker: endo or exo.
