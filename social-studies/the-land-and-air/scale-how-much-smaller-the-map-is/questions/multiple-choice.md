# Scale — how much smaller the map is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Scale is —
a) the size of the paper  b) the number of symbols  c) the ratio of map to ground distance or area
**Answer: c) the ratio of map to ground distance or area**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 1:50,000 means one map unit stands for —
a) 500 ground units  b) 50,000 ground units  c) 50,000 map units
**Answer: b) 50,000 ground units**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A town plan is —
a) large-scale  b) small-scale  c) unscaled
**Answer: a) large-scale**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The larger scale of these two is —
a) 1:1,000,000  b) they are equal  c) 1:10,000
**Answer: c) 1:10,000**
