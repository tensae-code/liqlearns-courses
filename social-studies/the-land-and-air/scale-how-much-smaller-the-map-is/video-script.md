# Video script — Scale — how much smaller the map is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Scale is a ratio: map against ground.
1:50,000 — one unit here, fifty thousand out there.
Large scale: small area, great detail. Small scale: the continent as a dot-map.
Large names the fraction, not the paper.
