# Scale — how much smaller the map is

## Do it first

A town on one map is a street plan; on another, a dot. Both are true — at different scales. Read what scale is and how maps class by it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The ratio

Scale is the ratio between map and ground: the map distance (or area) compared with the ground distance (or area) it stands for. A scale of 1:50,000 says every map unit stands for 50,000 of the same units on the ground.

## Sub lesson 2 — Three scale classes

Maps class by scale into large-scale (great detail, small area — a town plan), medium-scale, and small-scale (little detail, great area — a continent). Large or small names the fraction, not the sheet: 1:10,000 is a larger scale than 1:1,000,000.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what scale is, and judge three scales by class.
