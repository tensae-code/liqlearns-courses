# Hands-on — Scale — how much smaller the map is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Same town, three scales
Draw one street block three times: large scale with every house, medium with streets only, small as a dot among towns. Label each class.

## Fraction judge
Order these scales: 1:1,000,000 / 1:10,000 / 1:250,000 from largest to smallest, and say which you would use for a school plan and for a world map.
