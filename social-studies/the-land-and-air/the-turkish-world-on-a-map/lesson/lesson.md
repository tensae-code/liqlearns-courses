# The Turkish World on a Map

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the Turkish world on a map — the end map Türk Dünyası Haritası (map of the Turkish world) in Turkey's Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi book, as Turkey's Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi book teaches it in its end map. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Türk Dünyası Haritası — the map of the Turkish world

The very last page of Turkey's Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi book is a map: Türk Dünyası Haritası — the map of the Turkish world. After a semester of self, school, health and family, the book ends by widening the child's map from Turkey itself to the wider Turkish world.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading the end map — from Turkey to the Turkish world

The map stands second of the book's two end maps: first Turkey's own administrative divisions and neighbours (printed p. 123), then this one (printed p. 124). Read in that order, the two maps carry the Grade 2 child outward — from their own country, divided and neighboured, to the Turkish world beyond it.

Turkey's own way: Hayat Bilgisi (Life Studies) carries almost no Geography in this semester book — self/school, health/safety and family/society — except road signs and the two end-of-book maps; the Geography parts only are placed. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
