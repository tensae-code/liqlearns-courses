# Hands-on — What geography is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Word building
Build three word cards — Geo, Graphos, Geography — and under each write its meaning. Then coin one imaginary science word of your own the same way and define it.

## Variation hunt
Pick one phenomenon — rainfall — and describe its variation across Ethiopia in place and across the year in time. That double description is geography at work.
