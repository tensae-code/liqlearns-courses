# What geography is

## Do it first

Break the word in two: Geo, Graphos. Earth, writing. Two Greek pieces that became the name of a science. Read what the science is.

## Sub lesson 1 — The word

The word geography joins two Greek words: Geo, the earth, and Graphos, writing or description. Eratosthenes coined it as the description of the earth. Later scholars widened the meaning far beyond description.

## Sub lesson 2 — The science

The book settles on this definition: geography is the scientific study of spatial and temporal variation in physical, biological and human phenomena — how they vary from place to place and time to time — and of their interrelationships on the earth’s surface.
