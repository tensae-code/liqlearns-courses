# What geography is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Geo means —
a) writing  b) water  c) earth
**Answer: c) earth**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The word was coined by —
a) Ptolemy  b) Eratosthenes  c) Herodotus
**Answer: b) Eratosthenes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Geography studies variation in —
a) space and time  b) space only  c) time only
**Answer: a) space and time**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The phenomena studied are —
a) physical only  b) human only  c) physical, biological and human
**Answer: c) physical, biological and human**
