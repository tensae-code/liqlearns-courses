# Where and when Ethiopia’s rain falls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The south-east of Ethiopia is the —
a) year-round region  b) summer-only region  c) autumn-and-spring-rainfall region
**Answer: c) autumn-and-spring-rainfall region**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Year-round rainfall is found in —
a) the Ogaden  b) the south-west  c) Afar
**Answer: b) the south-west**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The wettest part of the country is —
a) the south-western highlands  b) Afar  c) the Ogaden
**Answer: a) the south-western highlands**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The driest parts are —
a) the south-west  b) the central highlands  c) the north-east and south-east
**Answer: c) the north-east and south-east**
