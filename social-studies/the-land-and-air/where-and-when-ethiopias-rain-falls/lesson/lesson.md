# Where and when Ethiopia’s rain falls

## Do it first

Two towns can share a country and hardly share a climate of rain: one rained on all year, one waiting for a single season. Read the rain map of Ethiopia.

## Sub lesson 1 — Three timing regions

By timing and reliability, Ethiopia splits into three rainfall regions: a summer-rainfall region (the great Kiremt belt), an autumn-and-spring-rainfall region in the south-east, and a year-round-rainfall region in the south-west, where rain can fall in any month.

## Sub lesson 2 — The wet-to-dry slope

By amount, rainfall falls away from the wet south-west toward the arid north-east and south-east. The south-western highlands are the wettest part of the country; Afar and the Ogaden are the driest. Amount and timing together decide what can grow where.
