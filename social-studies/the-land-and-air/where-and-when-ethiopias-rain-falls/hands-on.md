# Hands-on — Where and when Ethiopia’s rain falls

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rain map
Shade an outline map in three colours for the three timing regions. Add arrows showing totals falling toward the north-east and south-east.

## Two towns
Pick a south-western town and an Afar town. Write each town’s rain story in three lines: when it rains, how much, what that means for farming.
