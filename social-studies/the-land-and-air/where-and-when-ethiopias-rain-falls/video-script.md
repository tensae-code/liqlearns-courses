# Video script — Where and when Ethiopia’s rain falls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rain does not visit all Ethiopia at the same time.
Summer rains in the great belt. Autumn and spring rains in the south-east.
Rain in any month in the south-west.
And the amounts fade toward the dry north-east.
