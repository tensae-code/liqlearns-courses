# Hands-on — What Ethiopian rivers are like

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two seasons
Draw the same river twice: in Kiremt flood and in Bega low water. Label what changes — width, speed, sediment colour, use.

## Potential hunt
With a partner, argue where on a river course a hydropower dam wants to be, and where irrigation wants the water instead. Use the lesson’s traits as your evidence.
