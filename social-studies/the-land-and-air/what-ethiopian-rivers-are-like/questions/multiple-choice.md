# What Ethiopian rivers are like — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopian rivers are —
a) the same all year  b) mostly underground  c) seasonal and fluctuating
**Answer: c) seasonal and fluctuating**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Falling from the highlands, rivers cut —
a) salt flats  b) deep gorges  c) wide deltas only
**Answer: b) deep gorges**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The rapids and falls mean large —
a) hydropower potential  b) gold deposits  c) salt reserves
**Answer: a) hydropower potential**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The rivers’ irrigation potential is —
a) fully used everywhere  b) zero  c) large but little used
**Answer: c) large but little used**
