# Video script — What Ethiopian rivers are like

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ethiopia’s rivers change with the season — floods, then a trickle.
They fall steeply, cutting gorges, carrying soil.
Rapids and waterfalls mark their courses.
Power and irrigation wait in them, little used.
