# What Ethiopian rivers are like

## Do it first

Watch a highland river in the rains and again in the dry season: two different rivers in one bed. Read the character of Ethiopian rivers.

## Sub lesson 1 — Seasonal and steep

Ethiopian rivers are seasonal and fluctuating — huge in the rainy season, small in the dry. Falling from highlands to lowlands they cut deep gorges, carry heavy loads of sediment, and break into rapids and waterfalls on their steep courses.

[Picture 21024 — a gorge river in flood and in dry season, side by side]

## Sub lesson 2 — Power waiting

Those same rapids and falls mean large hydropower potential, and the rivers’ water could irrigate wide areas — but both are little used. The character that makes the rivers difficult is the same character that makes them valuable.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the character of Ethiopian rivers — seasonal, steep, sediment-laden — and the treasure inside that character.
