# Six-figure grid references — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The extra figures come from —
a) a second square  b) the scale  c) tenths within the square
**Answer: c) tenths within the square**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tenths are —
a) printed on all maps  b) estimated by eye between grid lines  c) measured with a ruler only
**Answer: b) estimated by eye between grid lines**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The worked points are —
a) B, F and N  b) A, B and C  c) X, Y and Z
**Answer: a) B, F and N**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The worked square is —
a) square ZZ  b) square 10  c) square CA
**Answer: c) square CA**
