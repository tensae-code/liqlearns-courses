## Sub lesson 2 — Greece's way — the book's own teaching way

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 7 Geology-Geography Student Book): Gymnasium Geography is organised by Earth's spheres — maps first, then the natural environment sphere by sphere (atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, biosphere), then the human environment (population, cities, resources), closing with one "snapshot" chapter per continent, Antarctica included. Lessons open with art and literature (a Little Prince extract introduces coordinates) and state their learning goals up front ("In this chapter you will learn…").

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 7 Geology-Geography Teacher Guide) (teacher guide — evidence): the guide opens with the philosophy of the whole package — against memorising lists of countries and numbers: geography as systems, change over time and cross-curricular links (the ΔΕΠΠΣ framework), with "mental maps" as the goal. Per lesson it gives objectives, visual material, teaching approach and worked answers (e.g. the coordinate games solved for Quito, Athens, Manila, Lusaka; Greece's own 1821–1999 birth/death-rate table under population). Its second half guides the workbook's project activities, and it closes with cross-curricular question sets and 32 suggested assessment topics. Europe gets no separate unit here — it is the whole of the Grade 8 course. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Greece is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.
