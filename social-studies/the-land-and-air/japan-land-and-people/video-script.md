Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11* (printed pp. 114–120), teaches: Bài 23: Japan — position (an archipelago — Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu, Shikoku — on the Pacific Ring of Fire) and nature (mountains over four fifths of the land, Mt Fuji 3,776 m, the Kanto plain; climate differing north–south and east–west; short steep rivers and Lake Biwa; forests; few minerals but a 29,000 km coast where the Kuroshio meets the Oyashio), and population (126.2 million, shrinking at −0.3%, the world's oldest structure, 334/km², 91.8% urban).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11): after Grade 10's world systems, Grade 11 walks the world region by region — Latin America, the EU, Southeast Asia and ASEAN, Southwest Asia, the USA, Russia, Japan, China, Australia and South Africa — always with the same lens (position, nature, people, economy) and always measuring Vietnam's own place among them (its HDI row in the opening table, EU–Vietnam trade, Vietnam inside ASEAN, Japan's FDI into Vietnam).
