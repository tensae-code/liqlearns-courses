# Parts every map must have

## Do it first

A map without a title is a picture without a caption; without a scale, a guess. Read the checklist every honest map carries.

## Sub lesson 1 — The checklist

The basic components of a map: title; scale; direction or north point; grid reference; legend or key; date; place of publication; publisher; and magnetic declination shown with the compass directions. Each component answers a question the reader will certainly ask.

[Picture 21120 — a full map with all components labelled around its margins]

## Sub lesson 2 — Checking a map

The trained reader checks the components before the country: title for subject, scale for size, legend for symbols, date for freshness. A map missing components is a map to distrust until the missing pieces are supplied.
