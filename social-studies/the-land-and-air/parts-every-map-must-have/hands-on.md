# Hands-on — Parts every map must have

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Component hunt
Take any printed map or atlas page and hunt the nine components, ticking each found. Report any missing, in the lesson’s spirit of distrust.

## Make an honest map
Redraw a simple map of your school area and put every component on it — margins included. Exchange and audit a partner’s for completeness.
