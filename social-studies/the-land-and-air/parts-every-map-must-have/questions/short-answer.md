# Parts every map must have — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List five components.
**Answer: Any five of: title, scale, direction/north point, grid reference, legend/key, date, place of publication, publisher, magnetic declination/compass.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the checking order’s first items.
**Answer: Title for subject, scale for size, legend for symbols, date for freshness.**
