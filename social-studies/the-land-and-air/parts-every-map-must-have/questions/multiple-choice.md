# Parts every map must have — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A map component is —
a) a photograph  b) a story  c) the legend or key
**Answer: c) the legend or key**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The freshness of a map is told by its —
a) scale  b) date  c) title
**Answer: b) date**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The trained reader checks first —
a) the components, in the margins  b) the biggest town  c) the colours
**Answer: a) the components, in the margins**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A map missing components is —
a) always useless  b) illegal  c) to be distrusted until they are supplied
**Answer: c) to be distrusted until they are supplied**
