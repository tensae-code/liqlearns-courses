## Sub lesson 2 — China's way — the book's own teaching way

China learns this inside its own books' way: world regional geography taught continent-by-continent and neighbour-by-neighbour — Asia first and in most detail (China's own continent), then the nearby countries (Japan, Southeast Asia, India, Russia), then the other eastern-hemisphere regions, the Americas, and the polar regions last.
