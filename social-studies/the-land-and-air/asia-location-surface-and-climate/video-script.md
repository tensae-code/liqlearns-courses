The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine's Grade 7 student book, the *Social Studies, Grade 7, Part One* (printed pp. 4–12), teaches: asia's surface features and their effect on its climate; general characteristics of Asia.

Palestine's own way (Grade 7, PS G7P1): the world is taught continent by continent through one fixed lens — astronomical location (latitude and longitude) and geographical location first, then surface features and general characteristics — with the spread of the Islamic state across each continent as the historical thread.
