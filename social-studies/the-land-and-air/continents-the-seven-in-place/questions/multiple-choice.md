# Continents — The Seven in Place — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which ocean bounds Asia to the east?
a) the Atlantic  b) the Pacific  c) the Arctic
**Answer: b) the Pacific**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Asia is largest in —
a) area only  b) population only  c) area and population
**Answer: c) area and population**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Africa ranks ______ in area and population.
a) first  b) second  c) last
**Answer: b) second**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Antarctica is ringed by —
a) the Arctic Ocean  b) the Southern Ocean  c) the Indian Ocean
**Answer: b) the Southern Ocean**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** Africa is crossed by the equator and —
a) both tropics  b) the Arctic Circle  c) no lines
**Answer: a) both tropics**

**6. (Sub lesson 5)** Asia holds about —
a) a third of all land  b) a tenth of all land  c) half of all land
**Answer: a) a third of all land**

**7. (Sub lesson 5)** The smallest continent is —
a) Australia  b) Europe  c) Africa
**Answer: a) Australia**

**8. (Sub lesson 6)** South America holds the Amazon and —
a) Angel Falls and the Andes  b) the Sahara  c) the Dead Sea
**Answer: a) Angel Falls and the Andes**
