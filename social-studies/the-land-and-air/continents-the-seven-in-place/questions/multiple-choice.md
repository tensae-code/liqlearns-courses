# Continents — The Seven in Place — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which ocean bounds Asia to the east?
a) the Atlantic  b) the Pacific  c) the Arctic
**Answer: b) the Pacific**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Asia is largest in —
a) area only  b) population only  c) area and population
**Answer: c) area and population**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Africa ranks ______ in area and population.
a) first  b) second  c) last
**Answer: b) second**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Antarctica is ringed by —
a) the Arctic Ocean  b) the Southern Ocean  c) the Indian Ocean
**Answer: b) the Southern Ocean**
