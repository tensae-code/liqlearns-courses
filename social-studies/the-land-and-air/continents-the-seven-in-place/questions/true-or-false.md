# Continents — The Seven in Place — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The book calls Africa 'the continent where humankind began'.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Europe lies to the west of Asia.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Africa has 54 countries.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** Antarctica is ice-covered and uninhabited.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 6)** North and South America join at Panama.
**Answer: True**
