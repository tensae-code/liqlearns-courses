# Continents — The Seven in Place — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Asia's three sub-regions as printed.
**Answer:** East Asia, South Asia and South-East Asia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How many sub-regions does Africa have, and what are they?
**Answer:** Five: North, South, East, West and Central Africa.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which continent is ice-bound?
**Answer:** Antarctica.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Give Africa's portrait in one line.
**Answer:** Second largest; crossed by equator and both tropics; Sahara and Nile; 54 countries.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Which continent holds the Dead Sea?
**Answer:** Asia.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Name South America's three portrait features.
**Answer:** The Amazon river and rainforest, Angel Falls, the Andes.
