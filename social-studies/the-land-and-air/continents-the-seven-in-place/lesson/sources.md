Ecuador's own way (Grade 2 book): Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. That way is stocked here as the way Ecuador teaches this thing.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 6): The Libro Abierto primary books open with two full geography units before any history: geography as a science first (its concepts, principles and helper sciences), then a continent-by-continent tour that always pairs physical aspects with population and culture — and the year's history closes with the country's own lived geography, Hurricane Georges over the Dominican Republic.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 3): For third graders geography is the home island first: the planet and its components, then the Antilles and the relief of their own country, then countryside versus city, and finally where their own families came from — migration and a family census the children run themselves.

Eritrea's own way (ER G5 Social Studies): Grade 5 widens from the village to the world — Eritrea's regions (zobas) and neighbours, the continents, islands, oceans, seas, lakes and rivers of the Earth, then back home to the Red Sea, trade and transport, maps and weather.

India's own way (Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat): The first Geography year goes planet → globe → motions → maps → Earth's domains → landforms, and only then turns home: the last two chapters are India itself (location, neighbours, physical divisions, monsoon climate, vegetation and wildlife), so world basics are immediately applied to the home country.

North Macedonia's own way (Grade 7 mk-g7): regional geography taught continent by continent — Europe then Asia — each continent opened with its physical characteristics (position, coastline, relief, climate, hydrography), then its socio-geography (population, peoples and religions, science and culture, settlements), then region-by-region reviews with individual country lessons and a tourism-centres lesson per region; the home country is taught inside the Balkan/Southern Europe region alongside Albania.

North Macedonia's own way (Grade 9 mk-g9): Grade 9 finishes the regional-geography cycle with the non-European continents — Asia again in greater depth (climate factors and phenomena, monsoons and typhoons, air pollution), then Africa, the Americas, and Australia with Oceania and the polar regions — each continent in the same pattern: position and coastline, relief, climate, hydrography, natural wealth and economy, population, a regional review, one or two spotlight countries (China, India; South Africa; USA, Brazil) and one problem lesson (food and water shortage in Africa; air pollution in Asia; global warming in the polar regions).

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 8 Geografija 8): Grade 8 is pure world regional geography, continent by continent (Africa, Australia/Oceania, the Americas and polar regions), each region worked through the same grid — position/relief/waters, climate/vegetation, population, economy — and the book closes with two world-problems units (rainforest loss, desert spread, water conflicts, the Aral Sea).

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 7 SD Geography 7): A continents book: after a world opener (continents, oceans, relief, world environments), each continent — Africa, Asia, Europe — gets the same treatment in the same order (relief, drainage, climate, vegetation, population, economic activity, environmental problems), closing with a British Isles case study; Sudan and Africa anchor the examples throughout.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 1 G1 CS Viva Venezuela): critical, question-first pedagogy ("pedagogía crítica") — spatial notions (up/down, north/south/east/west, near/far, yesterday/today) are built from the child's own home, classroom and community before any map appears.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 6 geo-6af-m): The final primary book turns outward: five of its six units place Mauritania inside its organisations — the Arab Maghreb Union, the Senegal River organisation (OMVS), the Arab League, the African Union, the Sahel drought committee (CILSS), the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation — and inside the industrial world (Europe, North America, the industrial East), before a closing world unit on continents, oceans, seas, rivers and straits.

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 8 G8 Geography of the Continents): Grade 8 tours the continents one by one — natural features, population, economic activities — with one country studied in depth per continent (Indonesia, Eritrea, Germany, the USA, Brazil), and closes on contemporary world issues.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 4 SS4): one combined Social Studies book in Urdu where the Geography chapter (Ch.5, printed pp. 61–93) works entirely through Pakistan's own map, landforms, weather and census, with BOLTS as the map-elements mnemonic and "I have learnt!" / "Work for me to do" boxes closing every topic.
