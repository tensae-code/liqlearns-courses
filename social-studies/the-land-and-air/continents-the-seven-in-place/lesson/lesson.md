# Continents — The Seven in Place

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try the opening task: asia is bounded by the pacific ocean to the east, the indian ocean to the south, the arctic ocean to the north, and europe to the west, with the red sea and suez to the south-west — say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Asia — the largest

Asia is bounded by the Pacific Ocean to the east, the Indian Ocean to the south, the Arctic Ocean to the north, and Europe to the west, with the Red Sea and Suez to the south-west. It is the largest continent in both area and population. The book names three sub-regions as printed: East Asia, South Asia and South-East Asia.

[Picture 11451 — a world map with each continent's bounding oceans labelled]

## Sub lesson 2 — Africa — where humankind began

Africa is second in area and in population, and the book calls it 'the continent where humankind began'. It has five sub-regions: North, South, East, West and Central Africa.

## Sub lesson 3 — The other five

North America and South America are the two American continents. Antarctica is ice-bound and ringed by the Southern Ocean. Europe lies west of Asia. Australia is the smallest of the seven in the book's table.
