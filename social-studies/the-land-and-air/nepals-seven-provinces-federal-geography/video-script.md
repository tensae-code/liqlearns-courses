The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 7 np-g7* (printed p. 140), teaches: Unit 7 Lesson 7: The seven provinces of Nepal. Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 7 TG np-g7tg* (TG p. 133), teaches: Unit 7 Lesson 7: The seven provinces of Nepal. (teacher guide — evidence) Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 12 np-g12* (printed p. 102), teaches: Unit 5 Lesson 1: Administrative provinces of Nepal.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 np-g7): Grade 7 Earth science is motion-first — Earth's daily and annual motion, surface form and the factors that change it, surface form's relation to human life — then Nepal's climate, the seven provinces of federal Nepal, climate change, disasters and map work; population is taught as structure, change and management.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 TG np-g7tg) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide teaches the same Grade 7 course as np-g7 with globe-and-ball demonstrations of rotation/revolution, chart work on Nepal's demographic structure (age, sex, religion, caste, language distributions), and discussion-led lessons; its geography content mirrors the student book lesson for lesson, so placements repeat with guide page references. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 12 np-g12): Grade 12 geography is federal and applied — each of Nepal's provinces studied four ways (administration, population, social-cultural life, economy) plus inter-provincial relations; disasters in Nepal with the national disaster-management system and plan-making; GIS/geo-information in social mapping; and a full urbanisation-and-migration unit (village–city relations, safe and dignified migration, migration effects).
