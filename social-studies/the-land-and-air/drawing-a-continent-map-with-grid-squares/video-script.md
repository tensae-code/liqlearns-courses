# Video script — Drawing A Continent Map With Grid Squares

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sudan's book, the *Sudan Grade 5 SD Geography 5* (printed p. 35), teaches: Drawing the map of Africa square by square (grid-copy method).

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 5 SD Geography 5): The first-ever Geography book in the post-2019 curriculum starts from the child's own classroom and school: directions are learned by standing facing the sunrise, map elements through a pupil's-house map, and only then does the book step out to Sudan, Africa and the world — a local-to-global ladder.
