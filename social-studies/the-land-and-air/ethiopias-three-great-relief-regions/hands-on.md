# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s three great relief regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The cut-loaf model
Mould a ridge of clay or pile books for the western highlands, leave a gap for the Rift, and pile again for the southeastern highlands. Label the three regions on paper slips.

## Place the places
List five Ethiopian places you know. For each, say which of the three regions it belongs to — west, Rift, or southeast — and why you think so.
