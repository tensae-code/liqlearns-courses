The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Ethiopia’s land has a skeleton of three parts.
Highlands in the west. Highlands in the southeast.
And between them, the great Rift Valley.
West, Rift, southeast — place every place on that frame.
