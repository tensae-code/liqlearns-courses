# Ethiopia’s three great relief regions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many great relief regions does Ethiopia divide into?
a) two  b) five  c) three
**Answer: c) three**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The region between the two highland blocks is —
a) Lake Tana  b) the Rift Valley  c) the Ogaden plain
**Answer: b) the Rift Valley**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Simien Mountains belong to —
a) the Western Highlands  b) the Rift floor  c) the southeastern lowlands
**Answer: a) the Western Highlands**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Lake Ziway lies in —
a) the Western Highlands  b) the Ogaden  c) the Rift Valley
**Answer: c) the Rift Valley**
