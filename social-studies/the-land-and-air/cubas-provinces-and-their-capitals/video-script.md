# Video script — Cuba's Provinces And Their Capitals

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba's book, the *Cuba Grade 9 Geografía de Cuba G9 (2025)* (printed p. 19), teaches: Capítulo 2.2.1-2.2.2 Perfiles de las provincias cubanas (municipios, capital, gentilicio, límites) y La Habana, capital de la República. Cuba's book, the *Cuba Grades 10-11 Cuaderno de mapas* (printed p. 46), teaches: Cuba y sus provincias y La Habana, capital del país, con límites de provincia y de municipios.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 9 Geografía de Cuba G9 (2025)): The 2025 rewrite keeps the home-country arc but rebuilds it for the Tercer Perfeccionamiento: Chapter 2 now profiles every province inside the political-administrative division itself, the climate chapter adds the Tarea Vida state plan by name, the economy chapter is organised by primary/secondary/tertiary sectors with self-employment (trabajo por cuenta propia) and the Mariel development zone, and the social chapter faces an ageing, shrinking population (life expectancy 73.7 years, a "Vivir 120" programme) and closes with Cuba's Human Development Index. It is a different book from the 2012 edition (Book 15), not a parallel copy, and is placed separately.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grades 10-11 Cuaderno de mapas): The senior-cycle companion is pure map work — a full outline-map atlas the student fills in: world and continents first, then the "significant regions" exactly as the G11 Regional course carves them (Caribe, América Latina, América Anglosajona, África Subsahariana, Europa Occidental, Medio Oriente, Este y sureste de Asia), then one outline map per studied country, and it lands back home with the Cuban archipelago, the provinces and a street-level La Habana. It closes with a "Datos interesantes" page of world records.
