# Hands-on — Fieldwork safety — supervision and responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trip plan sheet
Draft a one-page plan for a real local field trip: leader, supervisor, meeting point, register, return time, and the accommodations one named classmate would need.

## Country Code poster
Write the Country Code as five poster rules for your class — protecting plants, animals, water, people’s property and other people’s work.
