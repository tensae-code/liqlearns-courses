# Fieldwork safety — supervision and responsibility — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A field trip extends —
a) the school day by an hour  b) the textbook  c) the classroom outdoors
**Answer: c) the classroom outdoors**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On site, supervision belongs to —
a) no one in particular  b) the Fieldwork Leader  c) the oldest pupil
**Answer: b) the Fieldwork Leader**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** For disability, the rule is —
a) no automatic exclusion; accommodations must be provided  b) exclusion is normal  c) the pupil stays behind
**Answer: a) no automatic exclusion; accommodations must be provided**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Country Code protects —
a) only school property  b) only animals  c) the natural and human environment
**Answer: c) the natural and human environment**
