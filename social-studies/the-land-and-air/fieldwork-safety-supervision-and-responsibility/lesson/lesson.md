# Fieldwork safety — supervision and responsibility

## Do it first

Before any class steps outside, someone must answer: who leads, who supervises, where do we meet, who is responsible for whom? Read the safety frame.

## Sub lesson 1 — Roles and responsibility (teacher guide — evidence)

A field trip extends the classroom outdoors and needs planning and paperwork that rise with its complexity. The Head of Discipline (in the guide’s imported wording, the head of the institution) is ultimately responsible for supervision; the Fieldwork Leader supervises on site; and individual pupils carry stated responsibilities and conduct standards of their own.

## Sub lesson 2 — For everyone — and the Country Code (teacher guide — evidence)

No pupil is automatically excluded from fieldwork for disability; needed accommodations must be provided. And all pupils follow the Country Code — protecting the natural and human environment they visit, leaving the field as they found it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion who is responsible on a field trip, and the two duties — inclusion and the Country Code.
