## Sub lesson 2 — Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 9 SD Geography 9): A single-country book: the whole of Sudan taught in three units — its physical geography, its human geography, then its environmental problems — with map-work activities on Sudan's own maps (states, roads, the Nile) and an infographic "resources of Sudan" spread; the preface warns its statistics change and are only indicators.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 11 SD Geography 11): The human-geography year: population first (races, religions, census, density, dynamics, structure), then a full practical unit on distribution maps and graphs, then regional geography — the world studied continent by continent as regions — and a closing full unit on Sudan itself, natural basis to environmental problems. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
