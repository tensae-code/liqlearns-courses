# Ethiopia and the Horn — land, wildlife and soils (guide evidence) — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Horn covers about…
a) 1.8 million km²  b) 180,000 km²  c) 18 million km²
**Answer: a) 1.8 million km²**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide gives Ethiopia about how many bird species?
a) 83  b) 832  c) 8,320
**Answer: b) 832**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Endemic bird species in the guide's count:…
a) 7  b) 25  c) 103
**Answer: b) 25**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Threats to wildlife named include…
a) habitat destruction, illegal hunting, grazing expansion, wildfire  b) tourism alone  c) rainfall
**Answer: a) habitat destruction, illegal hunting, grazing expansion, wildfire**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Nitosols are…
a) black basaltic soils  b) red basaltic soils  c) sands
**Answer: b) red basaltic soils**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Vertisols are…
a) red basaltic soils  b) black basaltic soils  c) peat
**Answer: b) black basaltic soils**
