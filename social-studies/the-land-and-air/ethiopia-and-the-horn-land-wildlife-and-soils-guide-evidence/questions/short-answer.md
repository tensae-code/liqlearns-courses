# Ethiopia and the Horn — land, wildlife and soils (guide evidence) — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State the guide's Horn figures.
**Answer:** About 1.8 million km² inhabited by more than 95 million people (2010–11 guide figures).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish Nitosols from Vertisols.
**Answer:** Nitosols are red basaltic soils; Vertisols are black basaltic soils.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three threats to wildlife the guide lists.
**Answer:** Any three of: habitat destruction; illegal hunting; grazing-land expansion; wildfire; migration to neighbouring countries.
