# Video script — Ethiopia and the Horn — land, wildlife and soils (guide evidence)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The 2010–11 teacher's guide states the Horn's scale, Ethiopia's wildlife wealth — 103 mammal and 832 bird species, with endemics — and its two great basaltic soils, Nitosols and Vertisols. (Teacher-guide evidence, era-labelled.) First, the horn and its wildlife (teacher guide — evidence): This lesson stands on the 2010–11 Grade 12 teacher's guide alone — evidence of what that course taught, era-labelled throughout. Then, threats — and the soils (teacher guide — evidence): The guide names the threats plainly: habitat destruction, illegal hunting, grazing-land expansion, wildfire, and migration to neighbouring countries; vegetation is likewise pressured by settlement expansion into forestland. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
