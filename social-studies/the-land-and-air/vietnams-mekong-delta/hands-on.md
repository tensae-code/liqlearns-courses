# Hands-on — Vietnams Mekong Delta

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 31: Using nature rationally to develop the Mekong Delta's economy (Can Tho and four provinces; 15.8 million people) — its strengths (three soil groups; a sub-equatorial climate; the Tien and Hau rivers; the U Minh forests and five national parks; the Ca Mau fishing ground) and limits (dry-season water shortage, salt intrusion, and the country's strongest climate-change exposure), the directions for rational use, its food production (rice on 3,319.6 thousand ha giving 21.3 million tons — 49.0% of the nation — at the country's highest yield; aquaculture on 71.8% of the national farmed area; 386.8 thousand ha of fruit) and its river-and-orchard tourism (the Cai Rang and Phung Hiep floating markets; Phu Quoc). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
