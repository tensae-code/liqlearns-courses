# Hands-on — Irans Mountains And Plains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Iran's relief: Alborz, Talesh, Zagros, the eastern and south-eastern mountains, and the flat lands — plains (dasht) and lowlands (jolge) and their economic value. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
