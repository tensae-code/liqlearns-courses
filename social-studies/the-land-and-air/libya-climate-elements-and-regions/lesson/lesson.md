## Sub lesson 2 — Libya's way — the book's own teaching way

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 7 LY G7 Geography of Libya): A whole book on Libya alone, in four units — physical (location, relief, climate, soil, water, vegetation), human (population and urban centres), economic (farming, herding, fishing, minerals and oil, industry, trade), then tourism and transport, with tourism taught through Libya's historical cities (Jarma of the Garamantes) and oases (Awjila). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
