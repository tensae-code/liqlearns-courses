**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (North Macedonia, South Sudan).**

North Macedonia's own way (Grade 10 mk-g10): the gymnasium course restarts Geography as a systems science — Theme 1 works through the four spheres (lithosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, biosphere) lesson by lesson, then natural disasters by sphere; Theme 2 turns to people in geographical space (world urbanisation, energy, farming, industry, transport and trade, tourism, environmental impact); Theme 3 treats the world as one global system (globalisation, transport corridors and sea straits/canals, global networks and navigation systems, international organisations, global challenges and climate-change solutions). 43 numbered lessons.

Searchable tags: weather and climate, North Macedonia, social studies, the-land-and-air

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 4 P4 Social Studies SB): Weather is learned by building the instruments — thermometer, rain gauge, wind vane and windsock made from local materials, then a school weather station — and map reading is taught on one worked map ("Upendo Area") with scale, symbol key and area measured by counting grid squares.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies SB): The climate unit is two-way and practical — how climate shapes people's activities (poor climate ruins roads) and how people adapt: use scarce water efficiently, build flood defences and raise dykes, grow drought-tolerant crops, and make biogas.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide keeps climate personal and economic — which climate would you choose to live in, and why — and ties the farming system that suits a local area directly to its climatic conditions. That evidence is stocked here as the way South Sudan teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — South Sudan is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).
