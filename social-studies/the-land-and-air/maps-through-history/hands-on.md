# Hands-on — Maps through history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Route sketch like the first makers
Draw a route sketch to water or food near home, primitive-style: no scale, only the way and its marks. Exchange with a partner and try to follow theirs.

## Then measure it
Walk the same route counting paces. Add the counts to your sketch. Say, in the lesson’s terms, which stage your improved sketch approaches.
