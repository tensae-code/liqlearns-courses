# Video script — Maps through history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

First maps were scratches: the way to the hunting ground.
Then came measurement — survey, scale, the whole world fixed.
Two stages: sketch from observation, map from measurement.
Both are maps. The method is what changed.
