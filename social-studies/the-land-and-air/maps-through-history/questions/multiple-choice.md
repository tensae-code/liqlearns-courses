# Maps through history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Primitive maps were —
a) satellite images  b) printed atlases  c) route sketches
**Answer: c) route sketches**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** They showed the way to —
a) mines  b) hunting and fishing grounds  c) airports
**Answer: b) hunting and fishing grounds**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern cartography fixes positions by —
a) survey and measurement  b) memory  c) guesswork
**Answer: a) survey and measurement**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The two stages are —
a) hand and print  b) old and coloured  c) observation-sketch and measured survey
**Answer: c) observation-sketch and measured survey**
