# Hands-on — Environmental Components — Natural and Man-Made

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Components of the environment; natural and man-made environments, found on a field tour around the school (Unit 7, Environment and climate in our province); Amoko y'ibidukikije: natural environment (hills, rivers, volcanoes, lakes, swamps, forests, soil, air…) and man-made environment (houses, factories, roads, crops, electricity…) in our sector, sorted in a table (Unit 7); 7.1 What is environment? (Unit 7, Environment and climate in our province); 7.3 Importance of man-made environments in our province (Unit 7). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
