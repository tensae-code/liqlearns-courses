# Video script — Environmental Components — Natural and Man-Made

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Rwanda's book, the *Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies TG* (printed p. xxii (content map)), teaches: Components of the environment; natural and man-made environments, found on a field tour around the school (Unit 7, Environment and climate in our province). (teacher guide — evidence) Rwanda's book, the *Rwanda Grade 3 Amasomo Mbonezamubano SB* (printed pp. 111–112), teaches: Amoko y'ibidukikije: natural environment (hills, rivers, volcanoes, lakes, swamps, forests, soil, air…) and man-made environment (houses, factories, roads, crops, electricity…) in our sector, sorted in a table (Unit 7). Rwanda's book, the *Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 72), teaches: 7.1 What is environment? (Unit 7, Environment and climate in our province). Rwanda's book, the *Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 78), teaches: 7.3 Importance of man-made environments in our province (Unit 7).

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): from P5 the medium is English and the organising frame is the child's own PROVINCE — its location, services, environment, climate, population and infrastructure — before the frame widens to the whole country at P6. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Rwanda is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 3 Amasomo Mbonezamubano SB): P3 moves the home ground up one rung of Rwanda's administrative ladder, from the cell to the SECTOR (Umurenge) — and it is here, at age 8–9, that formal map work begins: the sector map of Gikomero, the district map of Gasabo, symbols, scale, and the sectors that share a border with ours.

Rwanda's own way (Rwanda Grade 5 Social Studies SB): the pupil's book teaches the province through named Rwandan things — the five provinces with Kigali City as the case study, Rwanda's four seasons by their Kinyarwanda names (Umuhindo, Urugaryi, Itumba, Icyi), and the country's two international airports (Kigali and Kamembe) in the transport-places lesson.
