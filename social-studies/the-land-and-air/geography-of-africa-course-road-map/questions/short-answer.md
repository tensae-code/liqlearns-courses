# Geography of Africa — course road map — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name steps 4 and 5 of the course.
**Answer:** Step 4 is Population of Africa; step 5 is Major Economic and Cultural Activities of Africa.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two Ethiopian anchors the course uses.
**Answer:** Any two of: the Abbay Gorge, the Ethiopian Highlands' cool climate, Gore Town's rainfall, Addis Ababa's settlements, Ethiopian (Ethio-GIS) map data.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does a section's opening line 'At the end of this section you will be able to…' tell you?
**Answer:** It tells you what you will be able to do when that section is finished — the section's outcome.
