# Geography of Africa — course road map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which step comes FIRST in the course?
a) Climate of Africa  b) Landforms of Africa  c) Population of Africa  d) Geospatial data
**Answer: b) Landforms of Africa** — the land itself is step 1.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What is step 3 of the course?
a) The natural resource base  b) Geographic issues  c) Economic activities  d) Human–environment interactions
**Answer: a) The natural resource base** — rivers, soils, minerals, plants and animals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Each step opens with which view?
a) Ethiopia only  b) The world, then Africa  c) Africa only  d) One town only
**Answer: b) The world, then Africa** — the continent is measured against the whole Earth first.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's role inside the African treatment is —
a) never mentioned  b) the home anchor  c) the only example  d) a review topic
**Answer: b) the home anchor** — the course returns to Ethiopian ground in every step.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these belongs at the END of every step?
a) Key terms box  b) Unit outcomes  c) Review questions  d) Section openings
**Answer: c) Review questions** — each step closes with a summary, then review questions.
