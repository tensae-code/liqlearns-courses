# Video script — Geography of Africa — course road map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

This is the road map of Africa.
Eight steps. First, the land itself.
Second, the climate over the land.
Third, the riches of the land — rivers, soils, minerals.
Fourth, the people. Fifth, their work and cultures.
Sixth, people and land together. Seventh, today's problems.
Eighth, the maps and data that measure everything.
Every step starts with the world. Then Africa. And home is Ethiopia.
