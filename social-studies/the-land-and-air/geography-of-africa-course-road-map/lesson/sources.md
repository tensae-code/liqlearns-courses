**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Ethiopia, Colombia, Mauritania).**

Pairing: the teacher guide was first published in 2022 and the student textbook in 2023, with the same writers and the same eight units in the same order; the student book is the learner text of record, and teacher-guide rows above are evidence only.

The student book's figure captions carry encoding artefacts in the print file itself (captions render as symbol strings in the text layer); no caption text is taught in this lesson. The book's review headings repeatedly misprint 'Direction' as 'Dection'; the corrected spelling is used everywhere in this course.

Searchable tags: geography of Africa, course road map, eight units, Ethiopia Grade 10 geography, landforms climate resources population

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado08 Secundaria Activa): Grade 8 opens with a full geography unit — cartography first, then the paired physical-and-human treatment of Asia and Africa (spatial relations plus population development) — and only then turns to history.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 8 geo-2as-m): A world-regional year: one unit of concepts (physical, human, economic geography), one unit walking the five inhabited continents by their natural and human features, and one unit of paired-country economic case studies (Egypt/South Africa, Brazil/Canada, India/Japan, Spain/Germany, Australia/New Zealand).
