# Hands-on — Geography of Africa — course road map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The eight-step road
On a long strip of paper, draw a road with eight stops. Label each stop with one step of the course and one small drawing — a mountain, a sun, a river, a crowd, a market, two arrows, a city, a map. Hang it where you study.

## Anchor hunt
For each of the eight steps, name one Ethiopian place, river or town that could anchor it. Write the eight anchors on cards and sort them into step order with a partner.
