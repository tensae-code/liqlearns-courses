# Land-Shaping Forces — Faulting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Faulting happens when tectonic pressure —
a) melts the crust  b) pushes rock masses against, over or under each other  c) rains heavily
**Answer: b) pushes rock masses against, over or under each other**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A block forced up between two parallel faults is a —
a) rift valley  b) block mountain  c) horst only
**Answer: b) block mountain**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The block that sinks between faults forms a —
a) block mountain  b) rift valley  c) fold
**Answer: b) rift valley**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In order across a faulted landscape you meet —
a) valley, horst, valley  b) horst, rift valley, horst  c) fold, fold, fold
**Answer: b) horst, rift valley, horst**
