# Land-Shaping Forces — Faulting — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What pushes the rock masses in faulting?
**Answer:** Tectonic pressure.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a horst?
**Answer:** The unsunken ground on either side of a rift valley.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three landforms faulting draws at once.
**Answer:** Horst, rift valley, horst (block mountain where a block is forced up).
