# Video script — Landscapes of Ecuador's Provinces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

No two Ecuadorian provinces look the same: the book teaches the characteristics of each province's landscape — its relief, water, plants and settlements — so the child learns to read a paisaje: to say what a place looks like and why it looks that way. The coast, the highlands, the Amazon side and the islands each give their provinces a different face.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
