# The Chew Bahir Rift — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Chew Bahir Rift is —
a) a northern highland  b) a coastal plain  c) the southern arm of the Rift system
**Answer: c) the southern arm of the Rift system**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It runs toward —
a) the Red Sea  b) Lake Chew Bahir and the Omo  c) Lake Tana
**Answer: b) Lake Chew Bahir and the Omo**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The whole Rift system runs from —
a) Afar in the north-east to Chew Bahir in the south-west  b) Tigray to Bale only  c) the coast to Lake Tana
**Answer: a) Afar in the north-east to Chew Bahir in the south-west**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Rift separates —
a) two oceans  b) Ethiopia from Eritrea  c) Ethiopia’s two highland blocks
**Answer: c) Ethiopia’s two highland blocks**
