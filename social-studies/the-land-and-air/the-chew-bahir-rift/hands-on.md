# Hands-on — The Chew Bahir Rift

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Full trace
On an outline map, draw the complete Rift in one line: Afar, lakes section, Chew Bahir arm. Label the three parts.

## Same story?
Explain to a partner how the Chew Bahir arm is the same geological story as the Afar end — dropped floor, faults, splitting.
