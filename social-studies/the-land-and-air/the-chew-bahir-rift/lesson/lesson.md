# The Chew Bahir Rift

## Do it first

Follow the Rift to its southern end on a map, past the big lakes, toward Chew Bahir. Read the last arm of the system.

## Sub lesson 1 — The southern arm

The Rift system inside Ethiopia is completed in the south by the Chew Bahir arm — the southwestern Rift running toward Lake Chew Bahir and the Omo country. It is the same story as the north: a dropped floor between faults, continuing the great split beyond the main lakes section.

## Sub lesson 2 — The system whole

With the Chew Bahir arm in place, the whole system reads as one: from the Afar Depression in the north-east, down the lakes section, to Chew Bahir and the Omo in the south-west — a single geological structure crossing the entire country and separating its two highland blocks.
