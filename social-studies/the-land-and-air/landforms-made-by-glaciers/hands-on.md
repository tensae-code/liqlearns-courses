# Hands-on — Landforms Made By Glaciers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Landforms made by glaciers: valley and continental glaciers; erosional forms (cirque, tarn/cirque lake, U-shaped valley, hanging valley, fjord) and depositional forms (lateral, medial and terminal moraines, drumlins, marginal lakes and kames, eskers, outwash plain). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
