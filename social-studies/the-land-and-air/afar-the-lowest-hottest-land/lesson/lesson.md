# Afar — the lowest, hottest land

## Do it first

Find the Afar triangle in the north-east. Parts of it sink below sea level. Read about the lowest, hottest land in the country.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Danakil Depression

In the north, the Rift widens into the Afar lowland — the Danakil Depression. It is among the lowest land on earth, sinking below sea level, and among the hottest places anywhere. A raised block, the Afar Horst, stands within it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Salt plains and volcanoes

The Afar carries salt plains — salt is still cut and carried from them as it has been for centuries — and active volcanism, including the lava lake of Erta Ale. This is the Rift at its most extreme: the crust is thin, hot and still splitting here.
