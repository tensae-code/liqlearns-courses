# Hands-on — Afar — the lowest, hottest land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Below sea level
Draw a height line: mark sea level, then Ras Dashen high above it and the Danakil Depression below it. The lesson’s country holds both ends of the line.

## Salt journey
With a partner, trace the salt’s journey in words: cut in the depression, loaded, carried up to the highlands. What makes each step hard?
