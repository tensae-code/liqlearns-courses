# Afar — the lowest, hottest land — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the raised block inside the Afar lowland called?
**Answer: The Afar Horst.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is salt important in Afar?
**Answer: Salt is cut from the salt plains and carried to the highlands, as it has been for centuries.**
