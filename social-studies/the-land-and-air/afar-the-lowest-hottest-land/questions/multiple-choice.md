# Afar — the lowest, hottest land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Afar lowland is also called —
a) the Ogaden  b) the Chew Bahir Rift  c) the Danakil Depression
**Answer: c) the Danakil Depression**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Parts of the Danakil Depression lie —
a) under Lake Tana  b) below sea level  c) above 4,000 metres
**Answer: b) below sea level**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The volcano with a lava lake named in the lesson is —
a) Erta Ale  b) Fantale  c) Aluto
**Answer: a) Erta Ale**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The plains of Afar yield —
a) coffee  b) gold  c) salt
**Answer: c) salt**
