# True, magnetic and grid north — and bearings — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distinguish the three norths.
**Answer: True: to the geographic pole; magnetic: where the needle points; grid: along the map’s Eastings.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is grid north the convenient reference?
**Answer: Bearings can be taken from it with a protractor on the map itself, along the Eastings.**
