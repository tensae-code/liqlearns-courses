# True, magnetic and grid north — and bearings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The compass needle points to —
a) true north  b) grid north  c) magnetic north
**Answer: c) magnetic north**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Grid north runs along —
a) the equator  b) the Eastings  c) the parallels
**Answer: b) the Eastings**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bearings are measured —
a) clockwise from north  b) anticlockwise from south  c) from east
**Answer: a) clockwise from north**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The bearing of south is —
a) 090°  b) 270°  c) 180°
**Answer: c) 180°**
