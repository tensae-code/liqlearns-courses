# Video script — True, magnetic and grid north — and bearings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Three norths, three arrows, slightly apart.
True north: the pole. Magnetic north: the needle. Grid north: the map’s up.
A bearing runs clockwise from north.
East 090. South 180. West 270. Numbers for every direction.
