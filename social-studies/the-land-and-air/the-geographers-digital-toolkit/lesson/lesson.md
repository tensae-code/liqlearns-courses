# The geographer's digital toolkit

## Do it first

Before you read, lay out — in words — every tool you would need to map your walk to school: to fix positions, to see the whole area, to copy an old map, to measure on the ground. The lesson names the professional set.

## Sub lesson 1 — GPS — fixing where

The Global Positioning System fixes positions on the Earth's surface from satellite signals. It is the tool that answers 'exactly where?' — for a well, a boundary corner, or a field-survey point.

## Sub lesson 2 — Remote-sensing platforms — seeing widely

Remote-sensing platforms — satellites and their instruments — acquire data over whole regions. They are the acquisition tool for land cover, vegetation, water and change over time.

## Sub lesson 3 — Digitisers and field kit — copying and measuring

A digitiser turns an existing paper map into digital data by tracing it. Field-survey kit — tapes, compasses, gauges and notebooks — measures on the ground. Together the four tools acquire the data; maps, graphs and diagrams present it.
