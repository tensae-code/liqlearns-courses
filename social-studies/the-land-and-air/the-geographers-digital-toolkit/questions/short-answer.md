# The geographer's digital toolkit — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does GPS fix?
**Answer:** Positions on the Earth's surface, from satellite signals.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are remote-sensing platforms used to acquire?
**Answer:** Data over whole regions — land cover, vegetation, water and change over time.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the four tools of this lesson.
**Answer:** GPS, remote-sensing platforms, digitisers and field-survey kit.
