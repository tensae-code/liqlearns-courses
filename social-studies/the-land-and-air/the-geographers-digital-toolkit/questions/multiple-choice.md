# The geographer's digital toolkit — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GPS answers which question best?
a) exactly where a point is  b) what the land cover of a whole region is  c) what an old map says
**Answer: a) exactly where a point is**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Remote-sensing platforms acquire data —
a) one point at a time  b) only indoors  c) over whole regions at once
**Answer: c) over whole regions at once**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A digitiser turns —
a) a satellite into a GPS  b) an existing paper map into digital data  c) rain into numbers
**Answer: b) an existing paper map into digital data**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Field-survey kit is used —
a) to measure on the ground  b) to launch satellites  c) to print maps only
**Answer: a) to measure on the ground**
