Laos's own way (Laos Grade 9 laos-g09-geography-teacher): the whole year is the geography of Laos itself, in four parts — physical geography, the three regions (north/centre/south), population and administration, economic geography — taught through Lao data tables (1995/2008 provincial population, age-sex structure) and outline-map work on Laos's provinces; closes with an additional-knowledge data annex (electricity output tables, references), printed pp. 97–100, not placed separately.

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 12 laos-g12-geography-teacher): the advanced pass over Laos itself — after a 24-page general teaching introduction (printed pp. 1–24, pedagogy, not placed), the same Laos frame as Grade 9 is deepened with separate rural and urban geography, political geography, an economic-development-strategy lesson (2000–2020), a whole part on relations and cooperation with ASEAN, and a closing environmental-geography part; additional knowledge for teachers at printed p. 174, not placed separately.

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 9 laos-g09-geography-student): the student twin of Book 1 — the geography of Laos in 4 parts / 17 lessons with full illustrated text and an additional-knowledge section (printed p. 87), not placed separately.

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 12 laos-g12-geography-student): the student twin of Book 4 — the advanced Laos year (physical, social, economic geography of the Lao PDR, ASEAN cooperation, environmental geography) with full illustrated text and a closing map appendix (printed p. 196), not placed separately.
