The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India's Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat book, the *India Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat* (printed p. 47), teaches: Ch.7 Our Country – India: locational setting, latitudinal/longitudinal extent, standard meridian, seven land neighbours plus Sri Lanka and Maldives across the sea.

India's own way (Grade 6 The Earth Our Habitat): The first Geography year goes planet → globe → motions → maps → Earth's domains → landforms, and only then turns home: the last two chapters are India itself (location, neighbours, physical divisions, monsoon climate, vegetation and wildlife), so world basics are immediately applied to the home country.
