## Sub lesson 1 — The main relief units on the map

The second Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Features of my homeland — mountains, plateaus and plains. The learner meets Morocco's relief as its main units on the map: the mountain lands, the plateaus and the plains, each found and named on the homeland's own map.

## Sub lesson 2 — How relief affects water resources

The lesson does not stop at naming the relief: it asks how relief affects water resources. The shape of the land — high mountains, raised plateaus, low plains — changes what happens to the country's water, and the guide teaches the two together.
