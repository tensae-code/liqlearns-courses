# Hands-on — Morocco's Relief — Mountains, Plateaus and Plains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort Morocco's land into three
Draw a simple map of Morocco and shade three kinds of land differently: mountains, plateaus and plains — the main relief units of the guide's lesson.

## Follow the water
On your map, pick one mountain area and one plain. Write one sentence for each on what the relief does to the water there, as the lesson teaches relief and water together.
