# Ethiopia's five zones in Köppen letters — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia falls into how many modified-Köppen categories, per the guide?
a) fourteen  b) five  c) three
**Answer: a) fourteen**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** BWh in the guide's list means —
a) arid desert hot  b) cold semi-arid  c) monsoon
**Answer: a) arid desert hot**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's Köppen map of Ethiopia comes from —
a) Beck et al.'s Köppen–Geiger maps  b) the student book  c) a satellite photo
**Answer: a) Beck et al.'s Köppen–Geiger maps**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The second map set beside it shows —
a) Ethiopia's agroecological zones, Global 16-Class  b) Africa's rivers  c) world deserts
