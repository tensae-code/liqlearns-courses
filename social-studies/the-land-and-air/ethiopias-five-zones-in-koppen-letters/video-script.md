# Video script — Ethiopia's five zones in Köppen letters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The teacher guide's answer key states that, under the modified Köppen scheme, Ethiopia's climate falls into fourteen categories.

The guide supports the count with its own Köppen map of Ethiopia (its Figure 2.1, drawn from Beck et al.'s Köppen–Geiger maps).

The guide then bridges the two Ethiopian systems: it sets the Köppen map beside its own map of Ethiopia's agroecological zones on the Global 16-Class system (its Figure 2.2) and directs comparison of the two.
