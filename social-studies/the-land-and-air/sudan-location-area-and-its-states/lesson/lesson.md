This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 9 SD Geography 9): A single-country book: the whole of Sudan taught in three units — its physical geography, its human geography, then its environmental problems — with map-work activities on Sudan's own maps (states, roads, the Nile) and an infographic "resources of Sudan" spread; the preface warns its statistics change and are only indicators.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 5 SD Geography 5): The first-ever Geography book in the post-2019 curriculum starts from the child's own classroom and school: directions are learned by standing facing the sunrise, map elements through a pupil's-house map, and only then does the book step out to Sudan, Africa and the world — a local-to-global ladder.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 6 SD Geography 6): A whole-Sudan book in three units — its natural environments (desert to rich savanna, plus the Red Sea coast), its productive resources taught environment by environment (farming, herding with the transhumance year of the Kabbabish and Kawahla, industry, mining), then its human geography: population, public services and its important cities one by one. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
