The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Sudan's book, the *Sudan Grade 9 SD Geography 9* (printed p. 2), teaches: Sudan's location (astronomical and geographical), neighbours and 18 states, with map-work. Sudan's book, the *Sudan Grade 5 SD Geography 5* (printed p. 32), teaches: My homeland Sudan: village, town, country — where Sudan lies (Unit 3). Sudan's book, the *Sudan Grade 6 SD Geography 6* (printed p. 5), teaches: Sudan's location and its diversity (Lesson 3).

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 9 SD Geography 9): A single-country book: the whole of Sudan taught in three units — its physical geography, its human geography, then its environmental problems — with map-work activities on Sudan's own maps (states, roads, the Nile) and an infographic "resources of Sudan" spread; the preface warns its statistics change and are only indicators.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 5 SD Geography 5): The first-ever Geography book in the post-2019 curriculum starts from the child's own classroom and school: directions are learned by standing facing the sunrise, map elements through a pupil's-house map, and only then does the book step out to Sudan, Africa and the world — a local-to-global ladder.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 6 SD Geography 6): A whole-Sudan book in three units — its natural environments (desert to rich savanna, plus the Red Sea coast), its productive resources taught environment by environment (farming, herding with the transhumance year of the Kabbabish and Kawahla, industry, mining), then its human geography: population, public services and its important cities one by one.
