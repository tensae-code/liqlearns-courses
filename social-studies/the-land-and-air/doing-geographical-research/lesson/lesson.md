# Doing geographical research

## Do it first

Research is not 'looking something up'. It is an organised attack on a problem. Say what makes it more than curiosity.

## Sub lesson 1 — Research — the deepest possible look

Research is the organised, planned deployment of skills and knowledge to take the deepest possible look at a problem — a scientific search for solutions that costs time, money and skill, and whose value depends on reaching its intended audience. Developed countries' heavy research investment is given as the engine of new knowledge and innovation: nations that research, learn; nations that learn, invent.

## Sub lesson 2 — What makes research geographical

Geographic research is enquiry into places and people in location, with a 2,000-year history of absorbing new technologies and methods. Any project rests on three components: the researcher's geographic knowledge; the methods of collecting, compiling, analysing and displaying information (including sampling); and the theoretical perspective guiding them. Knowledge, methods, perspective — remove any leg and the project falls.
