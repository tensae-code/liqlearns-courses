# Video script — Doing geographical research

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Research is the organised, planned deployment of skills and knowledge to take the deepest possible look at a problem — a scientific search for solutions that costs time, money and skill, and whose value depends on reaching its intended audience. Developed countries' heavy research investment is given as the engine of new knowledge and innovation: nations that research, learn; nations that learn, invent. Geographic research is enquiry into places and people in location, with a 2,000-year history of absorbing new technologies and methods. Any project rests on three components: the researcher's geographic knowledge; the methods of collecting, compiling, analysing and displaying information (including sampling); and the theoretical perspective guiding them. Knowledge, methods, perspective — remove any leg and the project falls.
