# Doing geographical research — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three components of a research project.
**Answer:** The researcher's geographic knowledge; the methods of collecting/compiling/analysing/displaying information; the theoretical perspective.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What makes research geographical?
**Answer:** It enquires into places and people in location.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must research reach an audience?
**Answer:** Its value depends on it — findings unused solve nothing.
