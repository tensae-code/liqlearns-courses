# Doing geographical research — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Research is the organised deployment of skills to…
a) take the deepest possible look at a problem  b) win arguments  c) fill time
**Answer: a) take the deepest possible look at a problem**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Research costs…
a) nothing  b) time, money and skill  c) only money
**Answer: b) time, money and skill**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its value depends on…
a) secrecy  b) reaching its intended audience  c) length
**Answer: b) reaching its intended audience**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Geographic research enquires into…
a) places and people in location  b) numbers only  c) the past only
**Answer: a) places and people in location**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Its history of absorbing new methods runs about…
a) 200 years  b) 2,000 years  c) 50 years
**Answer: b) 2,000 years**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A project's three components are knowledge, methods and…
a) funding  b) theoretical perspective  c) luck
**Answer: b) theoretical perspective**
