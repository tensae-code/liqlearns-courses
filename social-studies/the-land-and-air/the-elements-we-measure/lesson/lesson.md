# The elements we measure

## Do it first

A weather station measures seven things. Count them on your fingers as you read — you will meet every one again in the instrument lessons.

## Sub lesson 1 — The seven elements

The elements of weather and climate are the measurable parts of the atmosphere: temperature, precipitation, humidity, air pressure, wind, sunshine and cloud. Each has its instrument and its unit — the measuring lessons later in the unit take them one by one.

[Picture 21075 — seven element icons around a weather station]

## Sub lesson 2 — Elements into climate

A place’s climate is its elements averaged: its usual temperature, its usual precipitation, its usual winds and sunshine. Change any element’s average, and the climate description changes with it.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the seven elements, and build your home’s climate from them.
