# Hands-on — The elements we measure

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Element walk
Go outside for five minutes and register every element you can sense: temperature on skin, wind, cloud cover, humidity in the air. Record which elements your body cannot sense — those need instruments.

## Climate sentence
Write your home’s climate in one sentence using at least four elements with their usual values or words (hot, dry, windy).
