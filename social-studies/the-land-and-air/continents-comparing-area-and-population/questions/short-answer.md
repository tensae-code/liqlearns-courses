# Continents — Comparing Area and Population — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Asia's area in the table?

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which continent is second in both area and population?

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rank these by area, largest first: Europe, Asia, Australia.
