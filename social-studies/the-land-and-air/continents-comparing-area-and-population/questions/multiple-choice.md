# Continents — Comparing Area and Population — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The largest continent by area is —
a) Africa  b) Asia  c) North America
**Answer: b) Asia**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Antarctica's population in the table is —
a) zero  b) about a million  c) the second largest
**Answer: a) zero**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Asia's share of world population in the table is —
a) 20.4%  b) 29.5%  c) 60%
**Answer: c) 60%**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The table's source is cited as —
a) English, 1995  b) the Amhara Bureau  c) NAVSTAR
**Answer: a) English, 1995**
