## Sub lesson 1 — The area ranking

Table 1 ranks the continents by area: Asia 43,820,000 km², Africa 30,370,000 km², North America 24,490,000 km², South America 17,840,000 km², Antarctica 13,720,000 km², Europe 10,180,000 km² and Australia 9,008,500 km². Asia alone holds 29.5% of the land and Africa 20.4%.

## Sub lesson 2 — The population ranking

Asia holds 4,164,252,000 people — 60% of the world's population in the book's table. Africa is second in population as well as in area. Antarctica has zero population: no one lives there permanently.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading a table like a geographer

The table's source is cited as 'English, 1995'. Your task is to rank the continents largest to smallest from the table — first by area, then by population — and notice that the two rankings do not fully agree: Europe is small in area but large in people.
