**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Greece).**

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 6 Geography Teacher Guide (old ed.)) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 6 turns outward from Greece to the whole Earth — Earth in space, the natural environment, Earth as people's living space (how humans live in deserts, polar lands, rainforests, temperate zones), then a continent-by-continent tour (Europe in depth first, then Asia, Africa, North and South America, Oceania). The guide works from children's own prior mental models (e.g. flat-Earth models, taught with oranges and globes) and gives per-chapter objectives, alternative pupil ideas, method, activities and workbook/assessment answers (printed pp. 100–103). That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Greece is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 6 Geography Workbook (old ed.)): a worksheet for every one of the 45 student chapters plus 10 assessment sheets — one per unit and then one per continent — so the world tour is practised continent by continent.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 8 Geology-Geography Teacher Guide) (teacher guide — evidence): the whole Grade 8 course is one continent — Europe — studied map-first (position, then natural environment, then inhabitants, then economic activities), with Greece re-entered inside every theme (Greece's geological history, mountains, climate, rivers, population, cities, sectors) and the Balkans treated as Greece's neighbourhood at the end of the people unit. The guide's general part sets out the cross-curricular framework (ΔΕΠΠΣ), aims, method and assessment before the 48 lesson plans. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Greece is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 6 Geography Student Book (old ed.)): the student twin of Book 4's 45 chapters, with one addition the guide only implies — every continent section opens with a one-page "identity" profile of the continent (area, extremes, population at a glance) before its position, environment, states and peoples chapters.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 8 Geology-Geography Workbook): one worksheet per lesson of the Europe course — tasks, mini-research, map and table work, newspaper commentary and supervised internet searches, often done as group work outside school.

Greece's own way (Greece Grade 8 Geology-Geography Student Book): the student book of the Europe course — 48 lessons in four units (Maps; Europe's natural environment; Europe's inhabitants; Europeans' economic activities), Greece re-entered in every theme, closing with a glossary and the flags and capitals of Europe's states. Its prologue quotes the UNESCO source book: no inhabitant of this planet is truly educated who has not learned geography.
