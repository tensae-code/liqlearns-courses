# The northern and western highlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia’s highest peak is —
a) Mount Batu  b) Tullu Wallel  c) Ras Dashen
**Answer: c) Ras Dashen**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tigray plateau and the North Central Massif are separated by —
a) Lake Tana  b) the Tekeze river  c) the Awash river
**Answer: b) the Tekeze river**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The plateaus are cut by —
a) deep river gorges  b) sand dunes  c) glacier valleys
**Answer: a) deep river gorges**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Gorges make —
a) the plateau higher  b) rain fall more  c) travel hard and isolate communities
**Answer: c) travel hard and isolate communities**
