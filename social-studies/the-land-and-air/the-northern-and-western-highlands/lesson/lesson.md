# The northern and western highlands

## Do it first

Find the Simien Mountains on a map. That sharp, high corner of the country holds its highest peak. Read how the northern and western highlands are built.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tigray plateau and North Central Massif

The north of the Western Highlands is built of two great blocks: the plateau of Tigray and the North Central Massif, separated by the Tekeze river. The Massif carries the country’s highest ground — Ras Dashen in the Simien Mountains, Ethiopia’s highest peak. These are high, cool plateaus, the roof of the country.

## Sub lesson 2 — Gorges cut the plateaus

The plateaus are not smooth tables. Rivers have cut deep gorges through them — the Tekeze and Abbay systems slice the highland into separated blocks. The gorges make travel hard, isolate communities, and carry the highlands’ soil and water away toward the lowlands.
