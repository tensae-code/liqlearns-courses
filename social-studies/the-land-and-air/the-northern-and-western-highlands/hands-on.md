# Hands-on — The northern and western highlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Roof of the country
Draw the north-west highlands as a side view: two plateau blocks, a deep gorge between them, and mark Ras Dashen on the higher block.

## Gorge thinking
With a partner, list two ways a deep gorge helps people (water, defence in history) and two ways it makes life hard (travel, carrying goods).
