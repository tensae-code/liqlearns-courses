# Ethiopia’s temperature pattern — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Temperature in Ethiopia varies mainly with —
a) season  b) longitude  c) altitude
**Answer: c) altitude**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The hottest area is —
a) the Sanetti Plateau  b) the Danakil Depression  c) the Simien Mountains
**Answer: b) the Danakil Depression**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The diurnal range is large in —
a) the dry season  b) the main rains only  c) no season
**Answer: a) the dry season**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Diurnal range means —
a) the yearly range  b) the range between towns  c) the swing between day and night temperatures
**Answer: c) the swing between day and night temperatures**
