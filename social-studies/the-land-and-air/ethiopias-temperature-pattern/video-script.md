# Video script — Ethiopia’s temperature pattern

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Ethiopia, height sets the temperature.
High plateaus — cool. The Danakil — the hottest land.
Seasons matter less than altitude.
And dry-season days swing: cold dawn, hot noon.
