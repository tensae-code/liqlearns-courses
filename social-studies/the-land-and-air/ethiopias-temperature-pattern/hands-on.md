# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s temperature pattern

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-thermometer day
If you have a thermometer, read it at dawn, noon and dusk and write the swing. If not, record how your clothing needs change through one dry-season day — that is the diurnal range on your skin.

## Altitude ladder
List five Ethiopian places from lowest to highest and predict their temperature order. Check your order against the lesson’s rule.
