# Ethiopia’s temperature pattern

## Do it first

It is the same month in Dallol and in Debre Berhan — one of the hottest places on earth, one cool highland town. What makes the difference? Read on.

## Sub lesson 1 — Altitude, not season

In Ethiopia, temperature varies mainly with altitude rather than with the season. Climb, and the air cools steadily; descend into the lowlands, and it heats. The Danakil Depression is the hottest area of the country; the high plateaus are the coolest.

## Sub lesson 2 — Daily swings

The daily (diurnal) range of temperature is large in the dry season: hot afternoons, cold nights, especially in the highlands and dry lands. A single Ethiopian day can ask for a jacket at dawn and short sleeves by noon.
