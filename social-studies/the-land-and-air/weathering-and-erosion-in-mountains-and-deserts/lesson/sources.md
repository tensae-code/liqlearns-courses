Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).

Searchable tags: weathering and erosion in mountains and deserts, Iran, social studies, the-land-and-air

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 10 LY G10 Principles): Secondary geography opens as a two-part systematic course — physical geography first (astronomy, surface-shaping factors, climate, vegetation and wild animals), then human geography (population and settlement, economic resources) — and closes with a whole book on spatial-data technology: remote sensing, GPS and GIS, then the history of maps from Babylon and Ibn Hawqal.
