# Hands-on — Weathering And Erosion In Mountains And Deserts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 4: Weathering and erosion at work — in mountains, physical and chemical weathering together (day–night temperature change, water freezing in cracks); V-shaped valleys (the Colca valley in Peru, Madagascar) and mountain glaciers; in deserts, wind the great eroder (soft soils, sparse vegetation), kaluts (کلوت) formed by wind and water in the dry lands of the USA, Egypt, China and Iran, and the kinds of sand dune (nebkha, seif, crescent barchan); on rocky coasts, sea stacks, caves and arches. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
