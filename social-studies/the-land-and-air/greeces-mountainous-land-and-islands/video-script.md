The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 7 Social Studies* (printed p. 109), teaches: Ch. 5 Greece, geographical setting: Greece's mountainous land (the Pindus range), its many islands and indented coasts, and how that land pushed the Greeks to the sea.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 7 Social Studies): at intermediate level Social Studies becomes the history of ancient civilisations, and every civilisation is opened through its land — relief, climate, soil and rivers are taught as the stage on which each civilisation arose, starting with Iraq's own four relief regions.
