# Inside the Rift — the lakes section

## Do it first

Imagine standing on the Rift floor: lake water on one side, fields, and a volcano cone on the horizon. Read the lakes section closely.

## Sub lesson 1 — The floor and its lakes

The middle part of the Ethiopian Rift — the lakes region — is where the chain of lakes crowds the floor. The floor here is lower than the highlands but watered and fertile: a corridor of lakes, wetlands and farm land running between the two escarpments.

[Picture 21013 — a Rift cross-section with lakes sitting on the valley floor]

## Sub lesson 2 — Farming and volcanoes

The lakes region supports sedentary farming — settled farmers working fertile soils near the water — and it is dotted with volcanic features left by the Rift’s splitting: cones, craters and hot ground that remind everyone the valley is still geologically alive.
