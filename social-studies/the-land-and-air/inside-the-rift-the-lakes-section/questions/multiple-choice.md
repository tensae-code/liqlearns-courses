# Inside the Rift — the lakes section — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lakes region is —
a) the Tigray plateau  b) the Ogaden plain  c) the middle part of the Rift where the lake chain crowds the floor
**Answer: c) the middle part of the Rift where the lake chain crowds the floor**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Rift floor in the lakes region is —
a) a salt desert  b) lower than the highlands, watered and fertile  c) higher than the highlands
**Answer: b) lower than the highlands, watered and fertile**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The farming of the Rift floor is —
a) sedentary — settled farmers on fertile soils  b) shifting only  c) impossible
**Answer: a) sedentary — settled farmers on fertile soils**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Volcanic features on the floor include —
a) sand dunes  b) glaciers  c) cones and craters
**Answer: c) cones and craters**
