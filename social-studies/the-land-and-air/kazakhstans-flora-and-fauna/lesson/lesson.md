## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the book's own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Egorina): the 2025 Atamura course is organised by numbered paragraphs (§1–§68) with coded learning goals (e.g. 7.3.2.4) and a «Негізгі ұғымдар» key-concepts box per paragraph; it gives Kazakhstani science its own paragraph (§3, research in the war and post-war years), a whole practical strand on building geographic databases (§§10, 18, 49, 54–55), and a country-studies block that classifies states by position — continental, archipelago, island and peninsular — with Kazakhstan's own economic-geographic position as the worked example.

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 7 Tolybekova): the Mektep course teaches geographic nomenclature continent by continent (§§6–8: Africa/Australia/Antarctica, the Americas, Eurasia) with a practical on completing an outline map, then works through the natural complexes belt by belt (§§39–47: arctic, temperate forest/steppe, desert, tropical, subequatorial, equatorial); Kazakhstan anchors the applied ends — its regions' flora and fauna (§33), its soil types and soil resources (§§34–35), its population (§52, Practical №3) and its own economic-geographic position (§62). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
