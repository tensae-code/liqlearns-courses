# Relative dating — reading the order of rock layers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Relative dating gives —
a) the order of events, without exact dates  b) exact dates in years  c) the temperature of rocks
**Answer: a) the order of events, without exact dates**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Relative dating reads mainly —
a) sedimentary rock layers  b) cloud layers  c) ocean waves
**Answer: a) sedimentary rock layers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** By superposition, the youngest layer in an undisturbed stack is —
a) the top layer  b) the bottom layer  c) the middle layer
**Answer: a) the top layer**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** An intrusion that cuts two layers is —
a) younger than both layers  b) older than both layers  c) the same age as both
**Answer: a) younger than both layers**
