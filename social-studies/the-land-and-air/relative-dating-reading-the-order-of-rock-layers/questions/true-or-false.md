# Relative dating — reading the order of rock layers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tilted layers were laid down tilted from the start.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Layers are laid down flat, by original horizontality.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A cutting intrusion is younger than what it cuts.
**Answer: True**
