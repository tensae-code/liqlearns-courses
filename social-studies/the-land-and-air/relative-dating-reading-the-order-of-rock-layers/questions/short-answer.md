# Relative dating — reading the order of rock layers — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does relative dating tell you?
**Answer:** The order of events, without exact dates.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the superposition rule.
**Answer:** In an undisturbed stack, the top layer is youngest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the cross-cutting rule in one sentence.
**Answer:** A layer or intrusion that cuts another is younger than what it cuts.
