# Relative dating — reading the order of rock layers

## Sub lesson 1 — Dating without numbers

Relative age tells the order of events — what came first, what came later — without giving an exact date in years. It works by reading sedimentary rock layers.

## Sub lesson 2 — Two layer rules

Original horizontality: layers are laid down flat, so tilted layers were disturbed later. Superposition: in an undisturbed stack, the top layer is the youngest and each layer below is older.

## Sub lesson 3 — The cutting rule

Cross-cutting: a layer or intrusion that cuts across another rock is younger than the rock it cuts. Whatever does the cutting must have arrived after the thing it cuts through.
