# Video script — Relative dating — reading the order of rock layers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Relative age tells the order of events — what came first, what came later — without giving an exact date in years.

Original horizontality: layers are laid down flat, so tilted layers were disturbed later.

Cross-cutting: a layer or intrusion that cuts across another rock is younger than the rock it cuts.
