# Flora and Fauna of Ecuador

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the flora and fauna of Ecuador, as Ecuador's Grade 2 *Estudios Sociales 2do EGB* teaches it in Flora y fauna del Ecuador on printed p. 87. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ecuador's plants and animals

Grade 2 closes its geography with the living country: the flora (plants) and fauna (animals) of Ecuador. Because the four natural regions hold such different lands — coast, highlands, Amazon and islands — the plants and animals change completely from region to region, and the book teaches the child to connect each living thing to the region that is its home.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
