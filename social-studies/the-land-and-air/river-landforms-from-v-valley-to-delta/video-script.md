# Video script — River landforms — from V-valley to delta

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Follow one river on its whole journey. It starts swift in the hills, cutting a V-shaped valley, dropping over waterfalls where hard rock resists, racing through rapids around interlocking spurs. In its middle course it slows and swings into meanders — and one bend, cut off, stays behind as an ox-bow lake. At last, in its lower course, it spreads its load across a flood plain, and at its mouth it builds a delta, the triangle named for a Greek letter. Cutting above, swinging in the middle, laying down below: that is the journey of a river.
