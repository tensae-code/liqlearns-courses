# River landforms — from V-valley to delta — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Flood plains are built of deposited sediment.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Deltas form at a river's source.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rapids occur on steep stretches of the bed.
**Answer: True**
