# River landforms — from V-valley to delta — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A waterfall forms where…
a) hard rock resists while soft rock below is cut away  b) the river slows  c) two rivers join
**Answer: a) hard rock resists while soft rock below is cut away**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Interlocking spurs belong to the…
a) lower course  b) upper course  c) delta
**Answer: b) upper course**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A gorge is…
a) a near-vertical deep valley  b) a wide plain  c) a lake
**Answer: a) a near-vertical deep valley**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Meanders belong to the…
a) upper course  b) middle course  c) lower course
**Answer: b) middle course**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** An ox-bow lake is…
a) a bend cut off and abandoned  b) a crater lake  c) a reservoir
**Answer: a) a bend cut off and abandoned**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A delta is named for…
a) a Greek letter  b) its discoverer  c) a fish
**Answer: a) a Greek letter**
