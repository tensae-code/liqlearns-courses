# River landforms — from V-valley to delta — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three upper-course landforms.
**Answer:** Any three of: V-shaped valley, gorge, waterfall, rapids, interlocking spurs.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does an ox-bow lake form?
**Answer:** A meander bend is cut off from the river and abandoned, leaving its crescent of water behind.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which course holds the flood plain and delta?
**Answer:** The lower course.
