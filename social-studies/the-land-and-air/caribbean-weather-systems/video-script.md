# Video script — Caribbean Weather Systems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC teaches Caribbean weather systems in Chapter 7: the systems that bring the Caribbean its weather, met as systems — patterns that move and develop over the islands.

The book reads those systems through case studies — St Lucia, Grenada and Barbados itself — so Barbados's own weather is studied as part of the Caribbean pattern, not apart from it.
