# Caribbean Weather Systems

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Caribbean weather systems (case studies St Lucia, Grenada, Barbados), as Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC (2nd ed.) teaches it in Ch. 7. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The weather systems of the Caribbean

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC teaches Caribbean weather systems in Chapter 7: the systems that bring the Caribbean its weather, met as systems — patterns that move and develop over the islands.

## Sub lesson 2 — Case studies — St Lucia, Grenada and Barbados

The book reads those systems through case studies — St Lucia, Grenada and Barbados itself — so Barbados's own weather is studied as part of the Caribbean pattern, not apart from it.

Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
