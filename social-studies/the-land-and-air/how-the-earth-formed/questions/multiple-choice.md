# How the Earth formed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The account of the solar system's formation most widely supported in the book is —
a) the Big Bang account  b) a flat-Earth account  c) a flood account
**Answer: a) the Big Bang account**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Big Bang account, the cosmic explosion happened about —
a) 10 to 20 billion years ago  b) 4.5 million years ago  c) 600 years ago
**Answer: a) 10 to 20 billion years ago**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Earth itself formed about —
a) 4.5 billion years ago  b) 250 million years ago  c) 10 billion years ago
**Answer: a) 4.5 billion years ago**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** After the explosion, what gathered gas and dust together?
a) gravity  b) ocean currents  c) wind
**Answer: a) gravity**
