# How the Earth formed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What gathered gas and dust together after the explosion?
**Answer:** Gravity gathered the gas and dust together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** About how old is the Earth?
**Answer:** About 4.5 billion years old.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name one thing that came after the planet formed.
**Answer:** Continents (also oceans, climates or life).
