# How the Earth formed

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: where did the Earth itself come from, and how old is it? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — A universe that exploded and gathered

Of the many theories of how the solar system formed, the account most widely supported is the Big Bang: a cosmic explosion some 10 to 20 billion years ago. After the explosion, matter collided, cooled, and gravity gathered gas and dust together.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Earth takes shape

About 4.5 billion years ago the gathered gas and dust formed the Earth. The young Earth cooled slowly, and solid ground appeared where there had been only hot, moving material.

## Sub lesson 3 — Why this story matters

Every later part of geography stands on this beginning: continents, oceans, climates and life all came after the planet itself formed. When you read the deep-time lessons that follow, keep this starting point in mind: a hot young planet, cooling, about 4.5 billion years ago.
