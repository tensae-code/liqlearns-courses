# Video script — How the Earth formed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Of the many theories of how the solar system formed, the account most widely supported is the Big Bang: a cosmic explosion some 10 to 20 billion years ago.

About 4.5 billion years ago the gathered gas and dust formed the Earth.

Every later part of geography stands on this beginning: continents, oceans, climates and life all came after the planet itself formed.
