# Video script — Mass wasting — when slopes give way

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Slopes fail when gravity wins: rain soaking the debris, a slope steeper than the material can hold, and people farming or grazing unstable ground are the three triggers. First, what holds a slope up: Mass wasting is the downslope movement of rock, regolith and soil under gravity. Then, angle and human activity: The second factor is the angle of slope itself: beyond its holding angle, no material is safe, however dry. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
