# Mass wasting — when slopes give way — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water makes slope material more likely to move.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Any slope angle is safe if the material is dry.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Overgrazing can help trigger mass wasting.
**Answer: True**
