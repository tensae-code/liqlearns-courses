# Mass wasting — when slopes give way — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three factors governing mass wasting.
**Answer:** Water, the angle of slope, and human activity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does water weaken a slope?
**Answer:** Rain soaking into the pores reduces the material's cohesion, so the grains no longer grip and the mass moves.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What slope range holds material firm?
**Answer:** About 25° to 40°, steeper for larger, angular particles.
