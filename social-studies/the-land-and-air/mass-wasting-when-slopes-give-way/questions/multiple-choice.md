# Mass wasting — when slopes give way — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mass wasting moves material…
a) upslope  b) downslope under gravity  c) sideways only
**Answer: b) downslope under gravity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Slope material holds firm between about…
a) 5° and 10°  b) 25° and 40°  c) 80° and 90°
**Answer: b) 25° and 40°**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Larger, angular particles hold at…
a) gentler angles  b) steeper angles  c) no angle
**Answer: b) steeper angles**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rain soaking into pores…
a) increases cohesion  b) reduces cohesion until material moves  c) freezes the slope
**Answer: b) reduces cohesion until material moves**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Human triggers named are…
a) farming and overgrazing steep unstable slopes  b) building dams  c) planting forests
**Answer: a) farming and overgrazing steep unstable slopes**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's home example of repeated destructive events is…
a) Iceland  b) Ethiopia  c) Japan
**Answer: b) Ethiopia**
