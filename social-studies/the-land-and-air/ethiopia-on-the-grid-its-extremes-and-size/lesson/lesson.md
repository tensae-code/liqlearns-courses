# Ethiopia on the grid — its extremes and size

## Do it first

On a map of Africa, find Ethiopia. Put one finger on its far north and one on its far south. How many degrees of latitude separate them? Read on and check.

## Sub lesson 1 — On the grid

Ethiopia lies between about 3 degrees north and 15 degrees north of the equator, and between about 33 degrees east and 48 degrees east of Greenwich. That box puts it in Eastern Africa, in the part called the Horn of Africa. Because all of it is north of the equator and east of Greenwich, every place in Ethiopia has a north latitude and an east longitude.

## Sub lesson 2 — Extreme points and size

The extreme points are named places: the northern tip in Tigray, Moyale in the south, and Akobo in the west. Ethiopia is a large country — its north–south and east–west extents run to many hundreds of kilometres, and that large size is one reason it holds so many climates, landscapes and peoples inside one border.
