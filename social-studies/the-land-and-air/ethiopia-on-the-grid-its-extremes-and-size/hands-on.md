# Hands-on — Ethiopia on the grid — its extremes and size

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the box
Draw a simple grid. Mark 3°N to 15°N up the side and 33°E to 48°E along the bottom. Shade the box — that is where Ethiopia sits on the grid.

## Tips of the country
On an outline map, mark the northern tip in Tigray, Moyale in the south and Akobo in the west. Join them and look at the shape the three points make.
