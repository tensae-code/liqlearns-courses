# Ethiopia on the grid — its extremes and size — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia lies between about —
a) 3°S and 15°S  b) 15°N and 30°N  c) 3°N and 15°N
**Answer: c) 3°N and 15°N**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its longitudes run from about —
a) 48°W to 33°W  b) 33°E to 48°E  c) 0° to 15°E
**Answer: b) 33°E to 48°E**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The southern extreme point named is —
a) Moyale  b) Akobo  c) Massawa
**Answer: a) Moyale**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The western extreme point named is —
a) Moyale  b) Juba  c) Akobo
**Answer: c) Akobo**
