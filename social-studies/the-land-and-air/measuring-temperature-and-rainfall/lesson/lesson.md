# Measuring temperature and rainfall

## Do it first

Two instruments do most of a school station’s work: the thermometer pair and the rain gauge. Read how each is used — this is a doing lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Thermometers

Temperature is read with thermometers. A maximum thermometer records the highest temperature reached; a minimum thermometer records the lowest. Together they give the day’s extremes, from which the diurnal range and the mean are worked out.

## Sub lesson 2 — The rain gauge

Rainfall is measured with a rain gauge, sited in the open, clear of buildings and trees, and read in the standard way at the standard time: the depth of water collected, in millimetres, is the rainfall. Site it wrong and the same rain gives a wrong number.
