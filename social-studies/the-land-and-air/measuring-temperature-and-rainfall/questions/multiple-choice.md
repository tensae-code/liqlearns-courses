# Measuring temperature and rainfall — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A maximum thermometer records —
a) the present temperature only  b) the average  c) the highest temperature reached
**Answer: c) the highest temperature reached**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The day’s diurnal range is worked from —
a) the wind  b) the maximum and minimum  c) the noon reading
**Answer: b) the maximum and minimum**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A rain gauge must be sited —
a) in the open, clear of buildings and trees  b) under a tree  c) against a wall
**Answer: a) in the open, clear of buildings and trees**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rainfall is measured in —
a) degrees  b) oktas  c) millimetres
**Answer: c) millimetres**
