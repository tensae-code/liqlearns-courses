# Video script — Measuring temperature and rainfall

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two instruments run the school station.
Thermometers — one keeps the day’s highest, one its lowest.
And the rain gauge, standing in the open, clear of roofs and trees.
Millimetres of water: that is the rainfall.
