# Hands-on — Measuring temperature and rainfall

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make a gauge
Make a rain gauge from a straight-sided bottle with a funnel top and a ruler scale in millimetres. Site it by the lesson’s rule and read it after the next rain.

## Extremes log
For three days, log the highest and lowest temperatures you can obtain (station, radio, phone). Work out each day’s diurnal range and mean.
