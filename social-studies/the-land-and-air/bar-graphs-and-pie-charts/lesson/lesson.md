# Bar graphs and pie charts

## Do it first

Bars for amounts across categories; a pie for shares of a whole. The book draws Kokosa’s rain in bars and Ethiopia’s export destinations in a pie. Read both constructions.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bar graphs

A bar graph draws amounts as bars of proportional height across categories. The book’s worked data is the monthly rainfall of Kokosa town, West Arsi, for 2011: twelve months, twelve bars — the year’s rain shape visible at a glance.

## Sub lesson 2 — Pie charts

A pie chart draws shares: the circle is the whole (100%), and each segment’s angle is its percentage share — the book draws export-destination shares this way. Bars compare amounts; pies divide a whole. Choosing between them is choosing the question.
