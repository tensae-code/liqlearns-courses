# Hands-on — Bar graphs and pie charts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw both
From one honest small dataset of your own (class transport modes, say), draw it as bars for counts and as a pie for shares. Say what each drawing shows better.

## Kokosa reasoning
Without the book open, reason what Kokosa’s bar shape must look like across a Kiremt year — then say which months you are least sure of, and why.
