# Bar graphs and pie charts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The bar-graph data is rainfall of —
a) Addis Ababa, 2020  b) Hawassa, 2000  c) Kokosa, 2011
**Answer: c) Kokosa, 2011**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kokosa is in —
a) Bale  b) West Arsi  c) Arsi
**Answer: b) West Arsi**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A pie segment’s angle shows —
a) its percentage share  b) its distance  c) its population
**Answer: a) its percentage share**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book’s pie draws —
a) rainfall  b) temperature  c) export-destination shares
**Answer: c) export-destination shares**
