# Video script — Mid- and high-latitude climate regions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mid-latitude deserts and steppes sit in continental interiors and in rain shadows.

Other mid-latitude interiors are winter-dry, the Cw and Dw patterns.

High latitudes hold polar tundra and polar ice cap, where snow's albedo reflects up to 90 percent of insolation back to space.
