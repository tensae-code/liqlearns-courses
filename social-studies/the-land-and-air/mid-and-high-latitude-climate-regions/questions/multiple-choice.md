# Mid- and high-latitude climate regions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mid-latitude deserts sit in interiors and —
a) rain shadows  b) river mouths  c) equatorial coasts
**Answer: a) rain shadows**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The wet mid-latitude region grows —
a) deciduous forest  b) rainforest  c) no trees
**Answer: a) deciduous forest**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mediterranean coasts grow —
a) chaparral  b) tundra moss  c) mangroves
**Answer: a) chaparral**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Snow albedo reflects up to —
a) 90% of insolation  b) 9% of insolation  c) all insolation
**Answer: a) 90% of insolation**
