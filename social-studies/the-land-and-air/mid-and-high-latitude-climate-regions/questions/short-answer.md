# Mid- and high-latitude climate regions — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where do mid-latitude deserts sit?
**Answer:** In continental interiors and rain shadows.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What grows on Mediterranean coasts?
**Answer:** Chaparral — hard shrubs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does snow's albedo do?
**Answer:** It reflects up to 90 percent of insolation.
