# Mid- and high-latitude climate regions

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Mid-latitude dry and wet

Mid-latitude deserts and steppes sit in continental interiors and in rain shadows. Against them stands the wet region of deciduous forest, where broad-leaved trees drop their leaves in winter.

## Sub lesson 2 — Winter-dry and summer-dry

Other mid-latitude interiors are winter-dry, the Cw and Dw patterns. The summer-dry Mediterranean Cs coasts grow chaparral — hard shrubs fitted to dry summers and wet winters.

## Sub lesson 3 — High latitudes and highlands

High latitudes hold polar tundra and polar ice cap, where snow's albedo reflects up to 90 percent of insolation back to space. Highlands close the survey: a climate of short distances and endless local variety.
