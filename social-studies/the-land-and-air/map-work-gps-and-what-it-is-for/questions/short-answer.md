# Map Work — GPS and What It Is For — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three parts is GPS made of?
**Answer:** Satellites, receivers and computer software.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two things Google Earth is shown finding.
**Answer:** Any two of: countries, cities, roads, famous places, distances, directions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name three of the eight uses.
**Answer:** Any three of: navigation with instant directions; low cost; crime and safety work; ease of use; employer tracking of drivers; parents tracking children; finding what is nearby; live traffic and severe-weather alerts.
