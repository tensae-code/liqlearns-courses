# Map Work — GPS and What It Is For — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** GPS shows —
a) only city positions  b) exactly where anything on Earth is, at any time, place and weather  c) tomorrow's weather
**Answer: b) exactly where anything on Earth is, at any time, place and weather**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first GPS, NAVSTAR, was built by —
a) a phone company  b) the US defence ministry, for military use  c) a map publisher
**Answer: b) the US defence ministry, for military use**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How many uses does the book list for GPS?
a) four  b) six  c) eight
**Answer: c) eight**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is one of the book's eight uses?
a) printing maps  b) parents tracking children's safety  c) making rain
**Answer: b) parents tracking children's safety**
