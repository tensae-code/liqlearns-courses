## Sub lesson 1 — Position from satellites, anywhere, any weather

The Global Positioning System — GPS — is the one system that shows exactly where anything on Earth is, at any time, in any place and in any weather. It is made of satellites, receivers and computer software, and it can fix even hard-to-reach places: mountains, deserts and oceans.

## Sub lesson 2 — NAVSTAR and Google Earth

The first GPS was NAVSTAR, built by the United States defence ministry for military use. ## Sub lesson 3 — Eight uses

The book lists eight uses: navigation with instant directions; low cost, since phone map apps are usually free; crime and safety work — tracking criminals and preventing theft; ease of use; employer tracking of drivers — fastest routes, no wasted fuel, speed limits; parents tracking children's safety; finding what is nearby; and live traffic and severe-weather alerts that shorten journeys and improve safety.
