## Sub lesson 1 — Plateaus of South Sudan

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 9 S1 Geography SB* (printed p. 75), teaches: Plateaus of South Sudan: Boma Plateau peoples and wild coffee. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 37), teaches: Plateau: the Ironstone Plateau in South Sudan. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 9 S1 Geography SB): Geography is built around South Sudan itself — its own physical features, resources, floods/drought experience and the Nile — with paper-folding and paper-tearing practical experiments for folding, faulting and sea-floor spreading, and group discussion of local examples in nearly every topic.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB): Landforms are made, not just named — learners push a sheet of paper from both ends to make a fold mountain, and the home examples are the Ironstone Plateau and a farming landscape being changed by the farming itself. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
