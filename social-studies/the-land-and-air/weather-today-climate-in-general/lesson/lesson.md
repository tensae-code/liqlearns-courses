# Weather today, climate in general

## Do it first

Today it rains. Does it rain here in this month, most years? The first question is weather; the second is climate. Read the difference.

## Sub lesson 1 — Weather

Weather is the short-term state of the atmosphere at a place and a moment: this morning’s rain, this afternoon’s heat, tonight’s wind. It changes in hours and days.

[Picture 21074 — one day’s storm beside a 30-year rainfall chart for the same town]

## Sub lesson 2 — Climate

Climate is the average pattern of the atmosphere over a long period — the kind of weather a place usually has, season by season, built from many years of weather added together. Every later section of the climate unit builds on this distinction.
