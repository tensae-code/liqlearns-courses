# Hands-on — Weather today, climate in general

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Weather diary, climate memory
Keep a three-day weather diary with times. Beside it, write from memory what this month is usually like — your climate knowledge. Label which column is which and why.

## Headline judge
Judge three headlines with a partner: "Rain flooded the market today" / "The south-west is Ethiopia’s wettest region" / "A cold night is coming". Weather or climate — defend each verdict.
