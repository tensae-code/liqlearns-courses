# Weather today, climate in general — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weather is —
a) the 30-year average  b) the climate of a region  c) the short-term state of the atmosphere
**Answer: c) the short-term state of the atmosphere**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Weather changes in —
a) no time at all  b) hours and days  c) centuries only
**Answer: b) hours and days**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate is built from —
a) many years of weather added together  b) a single storm  c) one hot day
**Answer: a) many years of weather added together**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Climate describes —
a) this moment’s sky  b) a forecast  c) the average pattern over a long period
**Answer: c) the average pattern over a long period**
