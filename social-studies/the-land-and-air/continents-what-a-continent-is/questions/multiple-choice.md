# Continents — What a Continent Is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the geographer's sense, a continent includes —
a) only the dry land  b) the dry land, its continental shelf and the islands on that shelf  c) only its islands
**Answer: b) the dry land, its continental shelf and the islands on that shelf** — The shelf and its islands belong to the continent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A continent is best described as —
a) a small island group  b) a vast, undivided landmass  c) a single country
**Answer: b) a vast, undivided landmass**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How many continents are there?
a) five  b) six  c) seven
**Answer: c) seven**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is a continent?
a) the Mediterranean  b) Australia  c) the Nile
**Answer: b) Australia**
