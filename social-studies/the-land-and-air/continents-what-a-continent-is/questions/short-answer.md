# Continents — What a Continent Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What separates one continent from another?
**Answer:** A width of water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the feature around the land that belongs to the continent.
**Answer:** The shallow-water continental shelf (and the islands on it).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List the seven continents.
**Answer:** Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia.
