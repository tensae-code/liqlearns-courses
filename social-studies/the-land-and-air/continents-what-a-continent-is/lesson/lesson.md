## Sub lesson 1 — A continent is more than dry land

In the geographer's sense, a continent is not only the dry land you can stand on. It also includes the shallow-water continental shelf around the land, and the islands that sit on that shelf. So the continent reaches out under the shallow sea to the edge of its shelf.

## Sub lesson 2 — A vast, undivided landmass

A continent is a vast, undivided landmass, separated from other continents by a width of water. That width of water is what makes each continent its own great piece of the Earth's land.

## Sub lesson 3 — The seven continents

There are seven continents: Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia. Your first task with them is simple and real: in pairs, label all seven on a world map.
