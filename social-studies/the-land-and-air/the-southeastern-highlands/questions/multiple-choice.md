# The southeastern highlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The heart of the Southeastern Highlands is —
a) the Simien Mountains  b) the Danakil Depression  c) the Arsi-Bale massif
**Answer: c) the Arsi-Bale massif**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The high plateau in the Bale area is —
a) the Shewa plateau  b) the Sanetti Plateau  c) the Tigray plateau
**Answer: b) the Sanetti Plateau**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The eastern highlands named are —
a) Hararge and Chercher  b) Wollega and Kefa  c) Gamo and Dawuro
**Answer: a) Hararge and Chercher**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where basalt is worn away, the rock exposed is —
a) granite  b) sandstone only  c) limestone
**Answer: c) limestone**
