# The southeastern highlands

## Do it first

Cross the Rift eastward on a map. The land climbs again — to Bale and Arsi, to Hararge. Read how this second highland block is built.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arsi-Bale massif

East of the Rift rises the Arsi-Bale massif, the heart of the Southeastern Highlands. In it stand the Bale Mountains and the high Sanetti Plateau. These are broad, high lands — among the widest high ground in the country — with afro-alpine tops.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hararge, Chercher and worn basalt

Further east run the Hararge and Chercher highlands. In places the basalt cap of the southeastern block has been worn away, exposing the limestones beneath — a reminder that the sedimentary rocks laid down when the sea covered Ethiopia still lie under the lava in the east.
