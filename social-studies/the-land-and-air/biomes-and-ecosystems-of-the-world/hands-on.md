# Hands-on — Biomes And Ecosystems Of The World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 5 The distribution of the world's biomes (پراکندگی زیست‌بوم‌های جهان): whether a biome exists in a region depends on climate, the form of the relief, the land's height and the kind of soil — climate the most important of them; map activity: write tundra, taiga, equatorial rainforest, savanna and desert on the map in their places; the taiga, among the widest of the biomes, south of the tundra across Asia, Europe and North America; the savanna, grassland with single scattered trees beside the equatorial forests, widest in Africa; and desert — dry lands whose yearly rain is under 50 millimetres; Lesson 5 Living regions (نواحی زیستی): every ecosystem (بوم‌سازگان) has a living and a non-living part — producers (plants), first-order consumers (herbivores), second-order consumers (carnivores) and decomposers; Whittaker's classification of biomes; and measuring the rate of plant organic production, which is uneven over the Earth. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
