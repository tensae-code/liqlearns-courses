# Video script — Seismic waves — P, S and surface waves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Focus, epicentre, and three families of waves. An earthquake starts at the focus, the rupture point underground. Straight above it on the surface is the epicentre. Through the Earth travel the body waves: P waves, squeezing and stretching the rock along their path, and S waves, vibrating at right angles to it. Then come the surface waves — Love and Rayleigh — slower, but bigger, and they do most of the destruction far away. And because each kind travels at its own speed, scientists compare their arrivals and find the quake, even thousands of miles away.
