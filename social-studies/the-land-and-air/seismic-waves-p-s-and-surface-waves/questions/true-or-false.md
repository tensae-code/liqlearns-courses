# Seismic waves — P, S and surface waves — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Body waves travel through the Earth.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Surface waves are the fastest waves of all.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** P waves arrive before S waves at a station.
**Answer: True**
