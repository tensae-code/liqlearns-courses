# Seismic waves — P, S and surface waves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The focus is…
a) the point on the surface above the rupture  b) the rupture point where waves start  c) the recording station
**Answer: b) the rupture point where waves start**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The epicentre is…
a) directly above the focus on the surface  b) deep in the core  c) the largest wave
**Answer: a) directly above the focus on the surface**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** P waves move rock by…
a) compressing and expanding it along the path  b) vibrating at right angles  c) rolling the surface
**Answer: a) compressing and expanding it along the path**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** S waves vibrate…
a) along their path  b) at right angles to their path  c) not at all
**Answer: b) at right angles to their path**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The waves doing most far-field destruction are…
a) P waves  b) S waves  c) surface waves (Love and Rayleigh)
**Answer: c) surface waves (Love and Rayleigh)**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Scientists locate distant quakes by…
a) comparing wave arrivals  b) watching the sea  c) asking residents
**Answer: a) comparing wave arrivals**
