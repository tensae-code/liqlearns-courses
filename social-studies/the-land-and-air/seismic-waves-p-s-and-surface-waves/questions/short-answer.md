# Seismic waves — P, S and surface waves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Distinguish focus from epicentre.
**Answer:** The focus is the rupture point where the waves start; the epicentre is the point directly above it on the surface.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two surface waves.
**Answer:** Love waves and Rayleigh waves.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why do surface waves destroy so much far away?
**Answer:** They travel with larger amplitude, rolling and swaying the ground and buildings far from the focus.
