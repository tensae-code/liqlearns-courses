# Africa's four landform regions — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The African Alpine System lies in the north-west of the continent.
**Answer: True** — young fold mountains along the Mediterranean margin.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Southern Africa Platform is in the east.
**Answer: False** — the platform is the south; the east is highlands and rift.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Massif is a young, freshly folded surface.
**Answer: False** — it is ancient and worn smooth.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Highlands and Rift System joins some of Africa's highest ground with a deep valley.
**Answer: True** — high plateaus are cut by the Rift.
