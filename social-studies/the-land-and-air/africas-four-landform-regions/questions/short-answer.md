# Africa's four landform regions — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the four landform regions of Africa.
**Answer:** The African Alpine System, the African Massif, the East African Highlands and Rift System, and the Southern Africa Platform.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which region would you place the Sahara in, and why?
**Answer:** The African Massif — the Sahara lies on the ancient northern block that the Massif names.
