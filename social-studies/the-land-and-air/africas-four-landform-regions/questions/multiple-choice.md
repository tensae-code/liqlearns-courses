# Africa's four landform regions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many great landform regions organise Africa in this unit?
a) Two  b) Three  c) Four  d) Eight
**Answer: c) Four** — Alpine System, Massif, Highlands and Rift System, Southern Platform.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The African Massif carries which great desert?
a) The Kalahari  b) The Namib  c) The Sahara  d) The Danakil
**Answer: c) The Sahara** — the ancient northern block holds the Sahara.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The East African Highlands belong to which region?
a) The Alpine System  b) The Southern Platform  c) The Highlands and Rift System  d) The Massif
**Answer: c) The Highlands and Rift System** — the east is the highlands-and-rift region.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Southern Africa Platform's edge toward the coast is —
a) a deep rift  b) a great escarpment  c) a river delta  d) a fold range
**Answer: b) a great escarpment** — the raised platform drops steeply at its escarpment.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The first question to ask about any African landform is —
a) How high is it?  b) Which region does it stand in?  c) Who lives there?  d) How old is its town?
**Answer: b) Which region does it stand in?** — the region gives the landform's family first.
