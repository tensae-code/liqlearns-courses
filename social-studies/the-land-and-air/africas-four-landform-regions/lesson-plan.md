# Lesson plan — Africa's four landform regions

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Africa's four landform regions
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Landforms of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Africa's land is organised into four great regions — the African Alpine System, the African Massif, the East African Highlands and Rift System, and the Southern Africa Platform — and every landform in this unit belongs to one of them.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the world landforms the course opens with — mountains, plateaus and plains — and on the learner's map of Africa.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **The four regions named** — The map that organises the whole unit.
2. **Reading the regions on a relief map** — What each region looks like from above.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Geography of Africa — course road map. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Africa's four landform regions.
2. Place each region correctly on a blank map of Africa.
3. Describe each region's relief character.
4. Use the region as the first question for any African landform.

## If the learner is stuck
If the regions mix up, use the compass: north-west folds (Alpine), north-and-centre ancient block (Massif), east high and split (Highlands and Rift), south raised table (Platform). Four corners, four families.
