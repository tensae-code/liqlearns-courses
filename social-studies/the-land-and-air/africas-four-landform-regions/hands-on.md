# Hands-on — Africa's four landform regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four-colour Africa
Draw Africa's outline on paper. Colour the four landform regions in four colours and write a key. Under each colour, add one word for its land: folded, ancient, rifted, platform.

## Region pointing game
With a partner at a wall map or your drawing: one names a place — Atlas, Sahara, Ethiopian Highlands, Kalahari edge — the other points and names its region. Ten rounds, then swap.
