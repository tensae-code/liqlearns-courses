# Africa's four landform regions

## Do it first

Look at a map of Africa in your mind. Point north-west, then the great northern desert, then the east, then the south. Guess: how many big land regions would you split the continent into, and where would the splits fall? Read on and compare.

## Sub lesson 1 — The four regions named

Four regions organise everything that follows in this unit. In the north-west stands the **African Alpine System** — young fold mountains along the Mediterranean margin. Across the north and centre lies the **African Massif** — the ancient block that carries the Sahara. In the east rise the **East African Highlands and Rift System** — high plateaus cut by the great Rift. And the south sits on the **Southern Africa Platform** — a raised platform edged by a great escarpment.

Hold the four as a map in your head: alpine north-west, massif north and centre, highlands and rift in the east, platform in the south. Every mountain, desert and plateau in the lessons after this one hangs on one of these four frames.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading the regions on a relief map

On a relief map the four regions read by height and age. The Alpine System is a narrow, high, folded rim. The Massif is a vast, old, worn surface — high in places, but ancient and smoothed, holding the world's greatest hot desert. The Highlands and Rift System shows the continent's sharpest contrasts: some of Africa's highest ground split by a deep, long valley. The Southern Platform is a broad raised table, dropping steeply at its escarpment edge toward the coasts.

The rule for reading any African landform from now on: first ask **which region** it stands in. The region tells you its family — folded, ancient, rifted or platform — before you ask anything else about it.

## Say it to your companion

Name the four regions to your companion and place each with a finger on an imaginary map. Your companion names a corner of the continent; you answer with its region and one sentence about its land.

## Do It Yourself

1. Draw a blank outline of Africa and colour the four regions in four colours, with a key.
2. Write one sentence per region: where it is, and what its land is like.
3. Name which region Ethiopia's highlands belong to, and say why.
