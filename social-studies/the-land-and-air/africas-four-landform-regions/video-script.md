# Video script — Africa's four landform regions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Africa's land comes in four great regions.
North-west: young folded mountains — the Alpine System.
North and centre: an ancient block — the African Massif.
East: highlands cut by a great rift.
South: a raised platform with a steep edge.
Four regions. Every landform belongs to one.
