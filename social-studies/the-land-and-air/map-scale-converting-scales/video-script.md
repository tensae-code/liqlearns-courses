To turn a fraction into a statement, divide the denominator by 100,000 cm — the centimetres in a kilometre.

To turn a statement into a fraction, multiply the ground figure by 100,000 cm and drop the units.

To turn a line scale into a statement, read the drawn length against the ground length written above it: if 2 cm of line stands for 4 km of ground, the statement is '1 cm to 2 km'.
