# Map Scale — Converting Scales — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why divide by 100,000?
**Answer:** Because 100,000 cm make 1 km — it turns the denominator into kilometres.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Statement to fraction: what two steps?
**Answer:** Multiply the ground figure by 100,000 cm, then drop the units.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** How do you read a line scale into a statement?
**Answer:** Read the drawn length against the ground length written above it.
