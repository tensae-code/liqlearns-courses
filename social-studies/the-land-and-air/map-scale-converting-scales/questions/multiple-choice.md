# Map Scale — Converting Scales — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 1:250,000 as a statement is —
a) 1 cm to 25 km  b) 1 cm to 2.5 km  c) 1 cm to 0.25 km
**Answer: b) 1 cm to 2.5 km**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** '1 cm to 2 km' as a fraction is —
a) 1:2,000  b) 1:20,000  c) 1:200,000
**Answer: c) 1:200,000**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A line scale shows 2 cm of line for 4 km of ground. Its statement is —
a) 1 cm to 4 km  b) 1 cm to 2 km  c) 1 cm to 8 km
**Answer: b) 1 cm to 2 km**
