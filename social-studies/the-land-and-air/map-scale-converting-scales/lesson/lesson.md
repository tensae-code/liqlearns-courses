# Map Scale — Converting Scales

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try the opening task: to turn a fraction into a statement, divide the denominator by 100,000 cm — the centimetres in a kilometre — say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fraction to statement

To turn a fraction into a statement, divide the denominator by 100,000 cm — the centimetres in a kilometre. So 1:250,000 becomes: 250,000 ÷ 100,000 = 2.5 — '1 cm to 2.5 km'.

## Sub lesson 2 — Statement to fraction

To turn a statement into a fraction, multiply the ground figure by 100,000 cm and drop the units. So '1 cm to 2 km' becomes 2 × 100,000 = 200,000 — 1:200,000.

## Sub lesson 3 — Line to statement — and practice

To turn a line scale into a statement, read the drawn length against the ground length written above it: if 2 cm of line stands for 4 km of ground, the statement is '1 cm to 2 km'. Now practise the book's set both ways — including writing conversions on the blackboard for the class.
