# Video script — Cracking the Köppen code — what the small letters mean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

For the tree climates: f means wet all year, m monsoon, s a dry summer, w a dry winter.

Third letters grade the summer: a hot, b warm, c mild, d very cold.

Put the letters together and the code speaks: Cfa is a mild climate, wet all year, with a hot summer.
