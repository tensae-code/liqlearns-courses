# Cracking the Köppen code — what the small letters mean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The second letter gives the —
a) rainfall pattern  b) summer heat  c) soil type
**Answer: a) rainfall pattern**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** For B climates, W means —
a) true desert  b) wet all year  c) tundra
**Answer: a) true desert**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Third letter a means the summer is —
a) hot  b) mild  c) very cold
**Answer: a) hot**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** BWk, the Gobi, is a —
a) cold desert  b) hot desert  c) steppe
**Answer: a) cold desert**
