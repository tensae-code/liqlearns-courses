# Cracking the Köppen code — what the small letters mean

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: what extra story do the small letters after the big one tell? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The second letter — rain

For the tree climates: f means wet all year, m monsoon, s a dry summer, w a dry winter. For B: W is true desert and S is steppe. For E: T is tundra and F is ice cap.

## Sub lesson 2 — The third letter — summer heat

Third letters grade the summer: a hot, b warm, c mild, d very cold. For deserts, h means hot and k means cold.

## Sub lesson 3 — Reading a whole code

Put the letters together and the code speaks: Cfa is a mild climate, wet all year, with a hot summer. BWh is the hot Sahara desert; BWk is the cold Gobi desert — same dryness, different heat.
