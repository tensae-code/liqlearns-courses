## Sub lesson 2 — Malaysia's way — the book's own teaching way

Malaysia's own way (MY T1 Geografi Tingkatan 1): a pure Geography textbook organised as skills first (direction, position, sketch maps), then physical geography, human geography, a regional study of Southeast Asia, environmental issues and a full fieldwork chapter — every chapter anchored to Malaysian places first, then the region. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
