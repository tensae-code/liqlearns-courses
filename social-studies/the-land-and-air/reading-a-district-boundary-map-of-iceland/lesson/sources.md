**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Iceland).**

Iceland's own way: Geography work cards (vinnukort) are single-topic map sheets: this one teaches Iceland's own administrative geography — the text explains the sheriff (sýslumaður) districts, the card lists all 27 districts (taught in the companion lesson, Iceland's Administrative Districts), and the third page is the district-boundary map itself, worked on directly. This lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
