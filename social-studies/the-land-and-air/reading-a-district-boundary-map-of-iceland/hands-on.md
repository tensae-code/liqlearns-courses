# Hands-on — Reading a District Boundary Map of Iceland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace a boundary
Take the district boundary map. Put your finger on one district and trace its whole boundary without lifting your finger. You are reading where that district runs.

## Find a sheriff's seat
On the same map, find the seat mark for one district's sheriff. Write the district's name and where its sheriff sits, as the map shows it.
