# Reading a District Boundary Map of Iceland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Umdæmismörk sýslumanna is —
a) the district boundary map of the sheriffs' districts  b) a list of rivers  c) a weather chart
**Answer: a) the district boundary map of the sheriffs' districts**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A boundary line on the map shows —
a) where one district ends and another begins  b) a road for cars  c) a river
**Answer: a) where one district ends and another begins**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The map also marks —
a) the seats of the sheriffs  b) every farm in Iceland  c) the fishing boats
**Answer: a) the seats of the sheriffs**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The boundary map is which page of the work card? —
a) the third page  b) the first page  c) there is no map
**Answer: a) the third page**
