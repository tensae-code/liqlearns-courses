# Reading a District Boundary Map of Iceland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two questions does the district boundary map answer at once?
**Answer: Where each district runs (its boundaries), and where its sheriff sits (the sheriffs' seats) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do you read one district on the boundary map?
**Answer: Follow its boundary lines — everything inside one set of lines is that one district (any close wording).**
