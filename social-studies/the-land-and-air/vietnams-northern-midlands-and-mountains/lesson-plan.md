# Lesson plan — Vietnams Northern Midlands And Mountains

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Vietnams Northern Midlands And Mountains
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bài 24: Using the strengths of the Northern Midlands and Mountains (nine provinces; 12.6 million people at 136/km², the country's lowest density and urbanisation) — its minerals (coal, Lao Cai apatite, Thai Nguyen iron and lead–zinc), its hydropower (the Da river holding some 30% of the nation's potential; Son La and Hoa Binh at 2,400 MW each), its industrial crops, fruit and vegetables (tea on 99.5 thousand ha, 81.6% of the nation's tea; Moc Chau's tea hills) and its large livestock (the country's biggest buffalo herd) — and why developing the region matters for national defence — as Vietnam's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Bài 24: Using the strengths of the Northern Midlands and Mountains (nine provinces; 12.6 million people at 136/km², the country's lowest density and urbanisation) — its minerals (coal, Lao Cai apatite, Thai Nguyen iron and lead–zinc), its hydropower (the Da river holding some 30% of the nation's potential; Son La and Hoa Binh at 2,400 MW each), its industrial crops, fruit and vegetables (tea on 99.5 thousand ha, 81.6% of the nation's tea; Moc Chau's tea hills) and its large livestock (the country's biggest buffalo herd) — and why developing the region matters for national defence.
