# Hands-on — Vietnams Northern Midlands And Mountains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 24: Using the strengths of the Northern Midlands and Mountains (nine provinces; 12.6 million people at 136/km², the country's lowest density and urbanisation) — its minerals (coal, Lao Cai apatite, Thai Nguyen iron and lead–zinc), its hydropower (the Da river holding some 30% of the nation's potential; Son La and Hoa Binh at 2,400 MW each), its industrial crops, fruit and vegetables (tea on 99.5 thousand ha, 81.6% of the nation's tea; Moc Chau's tea hills) and its large livestock (the country's biggest buffalo herd) — and why developing the region matters for national defence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
