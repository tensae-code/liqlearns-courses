Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 103–109), teaches: Bài 24: Using the strengths of the Northern Midlands and Mountains (nine provinces; 12.6 million people at 136/km², the country's lowest density and urbanisation) — its minerals (coal, Lao Cai apatite, Thai Nguyen iron and lead–zinc), its hydropower (the Da river holding some 30% of the nation's potential; Son La and Hoa Binh at 2,400 MW each), its industrial crops, fruit and vegetables (tea on 99.5 thousand ha, 81.6% of the nation's tea; Moc Chau's tea hills) and its large livestock (the country's biggest buffalo herd) — and why developing the region matters for national defence.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
