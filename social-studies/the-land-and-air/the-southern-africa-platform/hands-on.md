# Hands-on — The Southern Africa Platform

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Staircase profile
Draw a side profile on paper: a low coast, a steep escarpment slope, a high flat top. Label Highveld on top, Karoo at the inner foot, Kalahari in the centre, Namib at the west coast. Present it to a partner.

## Three dry lands sort
Write Karoo, Kalahari, Namib on three cards with one clue each (inner foot / centre / west coast). Shuffle and re-place them on your profile drawing from memory.
