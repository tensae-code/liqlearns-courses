# Lesson plan — The Southern Africa Platform

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** The Southern Africa Platform
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Landforms of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Southern Africa is a raised platform: the Great Escarpment drops from the high interior — the Highveld — through the Great Karoo toward the coasts, and the Kalahari and Namib deserts sit on and beside the platform.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Africa's four landform regions — this lesson is the Southern Africa Platform in detail.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **The Great Escarpment and the Highveld** — The platform's steep edge and high top.
2. **The Great Karoo, the Kalahari and the Namib** — The platform's dry lands.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Africa's four landform regions. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Describe the Southern Africa Platform as a raised block with a steep escarpment edge.
2. Place the Highveld on the platform's top.
3. Place the Great Karoo, Kalahari and Namib in their correct positions.

## If the learner is stuck
Walk the staircase in words: sea → escarpment step → Highveld floor. Then set the dry lands: Karoo at the step's inner foot, Kalahari in the middle, Namib on the west coast.
