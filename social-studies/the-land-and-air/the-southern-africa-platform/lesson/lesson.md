# The Southern Africa Platform

## Do it first

Imagine climbing from a sea beach up a long steep slope onto a high flat interior, then walking for days across dry land. Which part would be hardest? Where would water be scarcest? Hold those questions for this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Great Escarpment and the Highveld

The **Southern Africa Platform** is the raised southern block of the continent. Its edge is the **Great Escarpment** — the long, steep slope where the high interior drops toward the coastal lowlands. On top of the platform lies the **Highveld**: high, open grassland country, the platform's broad upper surface.

Read the land as a staircase: coast at the bottom, escarpment as the steep step, Highveld as the high floor at the top. Most journeys in southern Africa climb that one great step.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Great Karoo, the Kalahari and the Namib

Below and beside the high interior lie the dry lands. The **Great Karoo** is the dry belt at the escarpment's foot side of the interior. The **Kalahari** spreads across the platform's dry centre — a great sandy dryland, more thirstland than true dune desert. On the Atlantic edge lies the **Namib**, the coastal desert where the platform meets a cold ocean current's dry air.

Three dry lands, three places: Karoo at the escarpment's inner foot, Kalahari in the platform's centre, Namib on its western coast. Together with the escarpment and Highveld, they are the whole platform's portrait.

## Say it to your companion

Walk your companion up the platform in words, from the beach to the Highveld, naming each part. Then your companion names a dry land — Karoo, Kalahari or Namib — and you place it.
