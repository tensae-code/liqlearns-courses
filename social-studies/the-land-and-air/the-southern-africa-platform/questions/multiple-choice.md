# The Southern Africa Platform — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Great Escarpment is —
a) a river  b) the steep slope where the high interior drops to the coast  c) a desert  d) a lake
**Answer: b) the steep slope where the high interior drops to the coast** — it is the platform's great step.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Highveld lies —
a) under the sea  b) on top of the platform  c) inside the Rift  d) on the coast
**Answer: b) on top of the platform** — the high, open upper surface.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Namib is —
a) a highland lake  b) the coastal desert on the Atlantic edge  c) a grassland  d) a river basin
**Answer: b) the coastal desert on the Atlantic edge** — the platform's western coastal desert.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Kalahari spreads across —
a) the platform's dry centre  b) the Mediterranean coast  c) the Congo Basin  d) the Ethiopian Highlands
**Answer: a) the platform's dry centre** — a great sandy dryland at the centre.
