# The Southern Africa Platform — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the staircase order from the sea inland.
**Answer:** Coast, then the Great Escarpment (the steep step), then the Highveld on top of the platform.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the platform's three dry lands.
**Answer:** The Great Karoo, the Kalahari and the Namib.
