# The Southern Africa Platform — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Southern Africa is a raised platform.
**Answer: True** — the whole southern block is raised.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Highveld is a low coastal plain.
**Answer: False** — it is the high top of the platform.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Great Karoo lies at the escarpment's inner foot side.
**Answer: True** — the dry belt below the high interior.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Namib is on the Indian Ocean coast.
**Answer: False** — the Namib is on the Atlantic, western edge.
