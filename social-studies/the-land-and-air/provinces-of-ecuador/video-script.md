# Video script — Provinces of Ecuador

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 2 meets the whole country as a set of provinces: each provincia is one of the large divisions of Ecuador, with its own land, towns and landscapes. Learning the provinces — their names and where they sit on the map — gives the child the frame that every later topic hangs on: their own province first, then the provinces around it.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
