# Water underground

## Do it first

Rain does not all run away. Some sinks, travels in the dark, and rises in a spring a valley away. Read the hidden water.

## Sub lesson 1 — Aquifers, springs, wells

Groundwater is water stored underground in aquifers — rock layers that hold and carry water. It reaches the surface at springs, and people reach down to it through wells. Groundwater supplements the surface water of rivers and lakes, above all in the dry season.

## Sub lesson 2 — Large but uneven

Ethiopia’s total water endowment is large, but it is unevenly distributed — in space, with wet highlands and dry lowlands; and in season, with flood months and dry months. The geography of water is therefore a geography of managing unevenness.
