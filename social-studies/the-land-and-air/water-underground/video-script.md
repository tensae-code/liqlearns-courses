# Video script — Water underground

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Not all rain runs away. Some sinks.
It gathers in aquifers — rock that holds water in the dark.
It rises in springs. It is lifted from wells.
Ethiopia’s water is plentiful — and uneven.
