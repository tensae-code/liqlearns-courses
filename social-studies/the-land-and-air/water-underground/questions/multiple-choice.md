# Water underground — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An aquifer is —
a) a surface lake  b) a type of well  c) a rock layer that holds and carries water
**Answer: c) a rock layer that holds and carries water**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Groundwater reaches the surface naturally at —
a) deltas  b) springs  c) waterfalls
**Answer: b) springs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia’s water endowment is —
a) large but unevenly distributed  b) small and even  c) large and even
**Answer: a) large but unevenly distributed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The unevenness is in —
a) depth only  b) quality only  c) space and season
**Answer: c) space and season**
