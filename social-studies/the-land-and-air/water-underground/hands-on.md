# Hands-on — Water underground

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Well and spring map
Map the wells and springs your community uses. For each, say whether its water is groundwater, and what happens to it in the dry season.

## Unevenness chart
Chart the unevenness twice: a map for space (wet/dry areas) and a year-wheel for season (flood/dry months). Title it with the lesson’s verdict.
