## Sub lesson 1 — America — Location and Geographic Aspects

Dominican Republic's Grade 6 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Sexto Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 31), teaches: America: its location and geographic aspects. Dominican Republic's Grade 5 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Quinto Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 12), teaches: Location of the American continent and its spatial relation: extension and natural limits. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 6): The Libro Abierto primary books open with two full geography units before any history: geography as a science first (its concepts, principles and helper sciences), then a continent-by-continent tour that always pairs physical aspects with population and culture — and the year's history closes with the country's own lived geography, Hurricane Georges over the Dominican Republic. Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 5): Three geography units climb from the planet to the country: general geography and the American continent's location, then how climate varies across the continents — with climographs the children draw and read themselves — then the Dominican Republic's own climate, activities and population, ending with a “discovering the geography of my country” field activity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
