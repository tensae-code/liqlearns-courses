# Planning a geography field trip — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The checklist has how many steps?
a) 5  b) 11  c) 8
**Answer: b) 11**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Step 1 is…
a) decide where to go  b) write the report  c) choose the leader
**Answer: a) decide where to go**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The permission letter is step…
a) 3  b) 7  c) 10
**Answer: a) 3**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Transportation is arranged at step…
a) 2  b) 5  c) 9
**Answer: b) 5**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The team leader is selected at step…
a) 7  b) 4  c) 11
**Answer: a) 7**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The final step is…
a) execute the trip  b) produce a report  c) decide the food plan
**Answer: b) produce a report**
