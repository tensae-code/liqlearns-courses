# Planning a geography field trip — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Recite steps 1–6 in order.
**Answer:** Decide where to go; plan the schedule; prepare a permission letter; ask the school administrator; arrange transportation; decide the food plan.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Recite steps 7–11 in order.
**Answer:** Select a team leader; decide who is allowed to go; execute the trip; prepare a field-trip note; produce a report.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What separates a field trip from an outing?
**Answer:** The note and the report — the learning written down and kept.
