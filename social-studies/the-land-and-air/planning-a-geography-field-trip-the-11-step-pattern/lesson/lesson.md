# Planning a geography field trip

## Do it first

A field trip is won or lost before the bus leaves. The guide's checklist runs eleven steps; learn the first six as one motion. (Teacher guide — evidence.)

## Sub lesson 1 — Steps 1–6 — decide, schedule, ask, move, eat

The first six steps: (1) decide where to go; (2) plan the schedule; (3) prepare a permission letter; (4) ask the school administrator; (5) arrange transportation; (6) decide the food plan. Place, time, permission, authority, movement, food — a trip missing any one of them fails in a predictable way, which is exactly what a checklist is for. (This guide, unlike the Grade 9 guide, gives planning only, with no separate field-trip safety guide.)

## Sub lesson 2 — Steps 7–11 — lead, include, go, record, report

(7) Select a team leader; (8) decide who is allowed to go; (9) execute the trip; (10) prepare a field-trip note; (11) produce a report. The note and the report are what separate a school trip from a school outing: the learning is written down, checked and kept. Run the eleven in order and any geography field trip — a river, a market, a factory, a farm — is planned the professional way.

## Say it to your companion

Explain planning a geography field trip to your companion in your own words: what it is, the one fact you would never want to forget from it, and one example from your own surroundings or from Ethiopia. Your companion asks you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
