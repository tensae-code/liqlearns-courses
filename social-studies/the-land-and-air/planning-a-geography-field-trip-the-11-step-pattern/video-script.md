# Video script — Planning a geography field trip

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The first six steps: (1) decide where to go; (2) plan the schedule; (3) prepare a permission letter; (4) ask the school administrator; (5) arrange transportation; (6) decide the food plan. Place, time, permission, authority, movement, food — a trip missing any one of them fails in a predictable way, which is exactly what a checklist is for. (This guide, unlike the Grade 9 guide, gives planning only, with no separate field-trip safety guide.) (7) Select a team leader; (8) decide who is allowed to go; (9) execute the trip; (10) prepare a field-trip note; (11) produce a report. The note and the report are what separate a school trip from a school outing: the learning is written down, checked and kept. Run the eleven in order and any geography field trip — a river, a market, a factory, a farm — is planned the professional way.
