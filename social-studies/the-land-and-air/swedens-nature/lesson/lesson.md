# Sweden's Nature

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Sweden's nature: its natural environments — forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, and fells — and the animals that live there, as Sweden's Grade 1 book and its training material teach them. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sweden's nature — natural environments and their animals

Sweden's Grade 1 book teaches the country's own nature: its natural environments and their animals, learning the names of what lives there. The teacher guide's notes for the textbook's pages 24–49 frame the nature chapters.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sorting Sweden's nature — forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, fells

The training material fixes the learning by doing: the pupil sorts nature photographs under the right heading — forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, and fells. Sorting the photographs is how the environments are learned.

Sweden's own way: the textbook's Geography is fixed by doing — colouring Sweden on the globe, sorting picture cards of Swedish houses, nature types, plants and animals under the right heading, and sorting photographs under warm climate / cold climate.
