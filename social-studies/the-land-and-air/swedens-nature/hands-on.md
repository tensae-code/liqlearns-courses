# Hands-on — Sweden's Nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort nature photographs
Take six pictures of nature — your own drawings will do — and sort them under the lesson's headings: forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, fells.

## Name what lives there
For two of your sorted pictures, name one animal that lives in that environment, as Sweden's book teaches the names of what lives in each place.
