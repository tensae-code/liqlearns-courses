# Video script — Sweden's Nature

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sweden's Grade 1 book teaches the country's own nature: its natural environments and their animals, learning the names of what lives there. The teacher guide's notes for the textbook's pages 24–49 frame the nature chapters.

The training material fixes the learning by doing: the pupil sorts nature photographs under the right heading — forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, and fells. Sorting the photographs is how the environments are learned.
