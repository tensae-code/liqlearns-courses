# Sweden's Nature — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Sweden's nature — natural environments and their animals  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Sweden's nature — natural environments and their animals**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Sweden's nature: its natural environments — forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, and fells — and the animals that live there, as Sweden's Grade 1 book and its training material teach them  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Sweden's nature: its natural environments — forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, and fells — and the animals that live there, as Sweden's Grade 1 book and its training material teach them**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Sorting Sweden's nature — forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, fells  b) learning to swim  c) telling the time
**Answer: a) Sorting Sweden's nature — forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, fells**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Sweden's training material sorts nature photographs under forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, and fells.  b) Sweden's nature, as its Grade 1 book teaches it, is only desert.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about Sweden
**Answer: a) Sweden's training material sorts nature photographs under forest, farmed land, lakes, coast and sea, streams and rivers, and fells.**
