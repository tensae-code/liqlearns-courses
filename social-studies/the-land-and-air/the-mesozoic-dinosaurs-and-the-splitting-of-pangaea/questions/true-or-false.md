# The Mesozoic — dinosaurs and the splitting of Pangaea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Turtles, snakes and crocodiles lived alongside the dinosaurs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pangaea stayed whole through the whole Mesozoic.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The dinosaurs were extinct by the era's end.
**Answer: True**
