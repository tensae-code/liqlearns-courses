# The Mesozoic — dinosaurs and the splitting of Pangaea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mesozoic runs from —
a) 250 to 70 million years ago  b) 600 to 250 million years ago  c) 70 million years ago to now
**Answer: a) 250 to 70 million years ago**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The animals that dominated the Mesozoic land were —
a) dinosaurs  b) mammals  c) trilobites
**Answer: a) dinosaurs**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The era's buried swamps became —
a) coal deposits  b) ice caps  c) sand dunes
**Answer: a) coal deposits**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Pangaea split into —
a) Laurasia and Gondwanaland  b) Baltica and Siberia  c) Africa and Asia
**Answer: a) Laurasia and Gondwanaland**
