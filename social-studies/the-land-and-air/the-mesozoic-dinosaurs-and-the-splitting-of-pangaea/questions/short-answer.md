# The Mesozoic — dinosaurs and the splitting of Pangaea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What formed from the era's swamps?
**Answer:** Coal deposits.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two half-supercontinents.
**Answer:** Laurasia and Gondwanaland.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which animals were emerging as the era ended?
**Answer:** Land mammals.
