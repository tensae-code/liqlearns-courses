# The Mesozoic — dinosaurs and the splitting of Pangaea

## Sub lesson 1 — The age of dinosaurs

From 250 to 70 million years ago, dinosaurs dominated the land, alongside turtles, snakes and crocodiles. This is the era most people picture when they think of ancient life.

## Sub lesson 2 — Coal from swamps

The era's swamps were buried and became coal deposits — stored sunlight that people still dig up and burn today.

## Sub lesson 3 — The split, and the end

Mid-era, Pangaea split into two half-supercontinents: Laurasia in the north and Gondwanaland in the south. The era ends with land mammals emerging and the dinosaurs extinct.
