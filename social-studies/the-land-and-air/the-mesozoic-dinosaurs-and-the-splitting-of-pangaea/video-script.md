# Video script — The Mesozoic — dinosaurs and the splitting of Pangaea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From 250 to 70 million years ago, dinosaurs dominated the land, alongside turtles, snakes and crocodiles.

The era's swamps were buried and became coal deposits — stored sunlight that people still dig up and burn today..

Mid-era, Pangaea split into two half-supercontinents: Laurasia in the north and Gondwanaland in the south.
