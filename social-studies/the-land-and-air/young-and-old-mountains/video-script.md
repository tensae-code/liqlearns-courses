Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 9 Social Studies* (printed p. 17), teaches: Lesson 3: Relief (ناهمواری‌ها) — the continents recalled from Grade 8; plateaus holding very high mountains with sharp peaks: the very high mountains are young relief, their slopes steep and the valleys between them narrow and V-shaped; the box on how fold mountains form.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 9 Social Studies): one combined Social Studies book of 24 lessons in 12 chapters, and here the Geography comes first: Ch. 1 Our planet, the Earth (Lessons 1–2, printed pp. 1–14), Ch. 2 Lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere (Lessons 3–4, printed pp. 15–28), Ch. 3 Biosphere, an amazing diversity (Lessons 5–6, printed pp. 29–40) and Ch. 4 The inhabitants of planet Earth (Lessons 7–8, printed pp. 41–52).
