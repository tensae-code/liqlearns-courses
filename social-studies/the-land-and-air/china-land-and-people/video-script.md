Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11* (printed pp. 131–138), teaches: Bài 26: China — position (about 9.6 million km²) and a nature of two halves (the eastern plains and monsoon climate of the Yangtze and Yellow rivers; the western Himalaya, Tibetan plateau and Taklamakan desert), giant pandas and forests, some 150 kinds of minerals (coal over 13% of the world's reserves; rare earths first in the world), and population (1,439.3 million in 2020; the one-child policy of 1979 relaxed in 2016 and 2021; ageing and a 51.3% male share; 61% urban) and society (56 ethnic groups, Han over 90%).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11): after Grade 10's world systems, Grade 11 walks the world region by region — Latin America, the EU, Southeast Asia and ASEAN, Southwest Asia, the USA, Russia, Japan, China, Australia and South Africa — always with the same lens (position, nature, people, economy) and always measuring Vietnam's own place among them (its HDI row in the opening table, EU–Vietnam trade, Vietnam inside ASEAN, Japan's FDI into Vietnam).
