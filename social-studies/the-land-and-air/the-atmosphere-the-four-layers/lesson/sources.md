Pairing warning: the Grade 8 teacher guide is an Addis Ababa bureau book and the student book is an Amhara bureau book; they are NOT edition-matched, and the guide in hand is truncated (printed pp. 1–47 only, Units 1–3). Pupil content in this lesson comes from the Amhara student book; anything from the guide is labelled (teacher guide — evidence) and never presented as pupil-taught.

Book errors taught corrected: (1) The student book's §1.3.1 attributes ozone-layer damage to 'carbon emission (CFC)' — CFCs are chlorofluorocarbons, not carbon emissions; this lesson teaches the two apart and names CFCs as the ozone-thinning chemicals. (2) The Grade 8 teacher guide's Activity 1.2 key (guide printed p. 8) calls the mesosphere 'the layer of strong winds', while the guide's own §1.2 overview places jet streams — high-velocity winds — at the top boundary of the troposphere (guide printed p. 6). The wrong mesosphere key is never taught from; this lesson follows the overview: jet streams ride the tropopause.
