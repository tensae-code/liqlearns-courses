# The Atmosphere — The Four Layers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All weather happens in the —
a) stratosphere  b) troposphere  c) thermosphere
**Answer: b) troposphere**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ozone layer, absorbing ultraviolet rays, is in the —
a) troposphere  b) stratosphere  c) mesosphere
**Answer: b) stratosphere**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The coldest layer is the —
a) mesosphere  b) thermosphere  c) stratosphere
**Answer: a) mesosphere**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the thermosphere, temperature —
a) falls with height  b) rises with height  c) never changes
**Answer: b) rises with height**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) The ionosphere and exosphere are subdivisions of the —
a) troposphere  b) thermosphere  c) mesosphere
**Answer: b) thermosphere**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What is thinning the ozone layer?
a) chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)  b) carbon emissions  c) water vapour
**Answer: a) chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)** — CFCs are chlorofluorocarbons — taught corrected; see sources.
