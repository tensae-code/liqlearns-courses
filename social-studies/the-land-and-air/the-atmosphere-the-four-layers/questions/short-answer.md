# The Atmosphere — The Four Layers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the tropopause?
**Answer:** The thin top boundary of the troposphere.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the temperature pattern across the four layers, in order.
**Answer:** Falls (troposphere), rises (stratosphere), falls (mesosphere), rises (thermosphere).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Where do jet streams ride?
**Answer:** At the top boundary of the troposphere (the tropopause).
