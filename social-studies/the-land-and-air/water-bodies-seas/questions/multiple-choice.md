# Water Bodies — Seas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Compared with an ocean, a sea is —
a) bigger and deeper  b) smaller and holding less water  c) always fresh
**Answer: b) smaller and holding less water**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Mediterranean is ringed by —
a) Africa, Europe and Asia  b) Asia only  c) the Americas
**Answer: a) Africa, Europe and Asia**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The largest sea in Table 3 is —
a) the Caribbean  b) the South China Sea, 2,974,600 km²  c) the Arabian Sea
**Answer: b) the South China Sea, 2,974,600 km²**
