# Water Bodies — Seas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a sea join its ocean?
**Answer:** Through a narrow opening.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Through what does the Mediterranean join the Atlantic?
**Answer:** One narrow gap/opening.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rank Table 3's seas largest to smallest.
**Answer:** South China Sea, Caribbean, Bering, Gulf of Mexico, Arabian Sea.
