## Sub lesson 1 — Smaller than an ocean, mostly land-ringed

A sea is smaller than an ocean and holds less water. Most of a sea is ringed by land, and it joins an ocean only through a narrow opening.

[Picture 11455 — the Mediterranean with its one narrow Atlantic opening marked]

## Sub lesson 2 — The Mediterranean — the worked example

The Mediterranean is ringed by three continents — Africa, Europe and Asia — and joins the Atlantic through one narrow gap. Sea water is mostly salty and undrinkable, unlike lake water.
