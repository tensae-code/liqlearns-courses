**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (South Sudan).**

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 12 S4 Geography SB): A global book (globalization, oceans, world energy, the development gap) that keeps returning to South Sudan — ARCISS signing photo in Juba for political globalization, South Sudan classroom progress-check scenarios, and a closing exercise asking learners, as citizens of South Sudan, how their nation can grow economically.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 12 S4 Geography TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Mirrors the student's book's 9 units and adds South Sudan case studies the student book only hints at — Mining in South Sudan, corruption and development in South Sudan, and the importance of international trade to South Sudan in particular. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB): Landforms are made, not just named — learners push a sheet of paper from both ends to make a fold mountain, and the home examples are the Ironstone Plateau and a farming landscape being changed by the farming itself.
