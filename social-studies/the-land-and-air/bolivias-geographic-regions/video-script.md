The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bolivia's Grade 7 student book, the *Texto de Aprendizaje — Ciencias Sociales, Secundaria 1* (printed p. 65), teaches: general geography of Bolivia: its geographic regions (Geografía General de Bolivia, 1.1). Bolivia's teacher compendium, the *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros* (teacher guidance, printed p. 32), teaches: general geography of Bolivia (teacher guidance, Secundaria 1, third trimester).

Bolivia's own way (Grade 7, Secundaria 1): The Bolivian Texto de Aprendizaje runs every unit through the same four moments — Práctica, Teoría, Valoración, Producción — and frames geography inside the plurinational, Madre Tierra worldview: continental drift and geological time sit beside the Laws of Mother Earth (071 and 300), the human right to water, and a closing general geography of Bolivia itself (regions, orography, political map, salt flats and lithium).
