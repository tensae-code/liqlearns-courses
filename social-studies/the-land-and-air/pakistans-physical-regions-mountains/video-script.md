Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 5 SS5* (printed pp. 62–65), teaches: Physical Features of Pakistan I: the concept of a region; Northern ranges (Karakoram and K2 8,611 m; Great and Lesser Himalayas and Siwaliks, Nanga Parbat), North-Western range (Hindu Kush, Tirich Mir), Western ranges (Koh-e-Sufaid and Waziristan hills, Sulaiman and Kirthar, Ras Koh and Chaghai).

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 5 SS5): the Class 5 Geography chapter (Ch.5, printed pp. 51–85) deepens the Class 4 spiral — same BOLTS, landforms, weather and population topics, but now with scale types, latitude/longitude and time-zone arithmetic, worked population-density calculations, and graph-drawing exercises from real Pakistani city data.
