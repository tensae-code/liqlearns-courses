# Water Bodies — Oceans, Seas, Lakes, Rivers

## Do it first

Before any explanation, try the opening task: begin at home: where does your own community get its water? a spring, a river, a well, a lake? every answer names a water body — one of the great gatherings of water on the earth's surface. — say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where does your water come from?

Begin at home: where does your own community get its water? A spring, a river, a well, a lake? Every answer names a water body — one of the great gatherings of water on the Earth's surface.

## Sub lesson 2 — The family of water bodies

Water bodies are the great gatherings of water on the Earth's surface. The family the chapter names is: oceans, seas, lakes, ponds, gulfs, rivers and springs.

## Sub lesson 3 — The main kinds, one by one

The chapter then takes the main kinds one by one — oceans first, then seas, lakes and rivers — asking of each: how big is it, is its water salt or fresh, and what bounds it? Keep those three questions ready for the next four lessons.
