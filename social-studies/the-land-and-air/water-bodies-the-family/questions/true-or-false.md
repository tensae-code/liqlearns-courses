# Water Bodies — Oceans, Seas, Lakes, Rivers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A spring is a water body.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Oceans and rivers are both water bodies.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A lake is one of the main kinds taken one by one.
**Answer: True**
