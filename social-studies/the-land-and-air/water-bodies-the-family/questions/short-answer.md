# Water Bodies — Oceans, Seas, Lakes, Rivers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does your own community get its water? (Name the water body.)
**Answer:** Any true local answer — a spring, river, well or lake — named as a water body.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the water bodies the chapter names.
**Answer:** Oceans, seas, lakes, ponds, gulfs, rivers and springs.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What three questions does the chapter ask of each main kind?
**Answer:** How big is it, is its water salt or fresh, and what bounds it?
