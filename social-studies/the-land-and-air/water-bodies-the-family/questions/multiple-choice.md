# Water Bodies — Oceans, Seas, Lakes, Rivers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water bodies are —
a) gatherings of water on the Earth's surface  b) only underground water  c) clouds
**Answer: a) gatherings of water on the Earth's surface**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is NOT in the chapter's family list?
a) ponds  b) gulfs  c) glaciers
**Answer: c) glaciers**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The chapter takes the main kinds in which order?
a) rivers, lakes, seas, oceans  b) oceans, seas, lakes, rivers  c) seas first, oceans last
**Answer: b) oceans, seas, lakes, rivers**
