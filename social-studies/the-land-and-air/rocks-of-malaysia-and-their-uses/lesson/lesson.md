## Sub lesson 2 — Malaysia's way — the book's own teaching way

Malaysia's own way (MY T4 Geografi Tingkatan 4): upper-secondary Geography is a full topographic-map apprenticeship (bearings, area, cross-sections, landscape reading) followed by process geography — plates, rocks, weathering, mass movement, rivers and coasts — and a world-scale human geography of population, migration, settlements and urbanisation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
