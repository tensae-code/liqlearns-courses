# Video script — Iraq's Relief Mountains Plateaus And The Alluvial Plain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies* (printed p. 18), teaches: Unit 1 Ch. 2 Geography of Iraq, natural phenomena: Iraq's surface (relief) — the mountain region, the plateau and the alluvial plain, worked off the relief map. Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies* (printed p. 5), teaches: Unit 1 opening: Iraq's surface divisions and how relief shapes Iraq's climate and rainfall, with a blank-map (خريطة صماء) exercise. Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 7 Social Studies* (printed p. 19), teaches: Ch. 2 Mesopotamia, geographical setting: Iraq's relief regions — the mountainous region, the undulating region, the plain region and the plateau region. Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies* (printed p. 8), teaches: Unit 1 Ch. 1: The mountainous region — the high mountains and the low mountains (Bekhair, Aqra Dagh and others). Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies* (printed p. 10), teaches: Unit 1 Ch. 1: The semi-mountainous (undulating) region, the transition between the southern low plains and the high mountains of the far north. Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies* (printed p. 11), teaches: Unit 1 Ch. 1: The alluvial plain (السهل الرسوبي) — about 24% of Iraq with its marshes and lakes, a rectangle some 600 km long, and how it was formed. Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies* (printed p. 12), teaches: Unit 1 Ch. 1: The western desert plateau (الهضبة الصحراوية الغربية) — the largest relief region, about 55% of Iraq.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies): the whole first unit is Iraq's own geography — location first (world, region, locality, then the astronomical location), then the land itself region by region off the relief map, then people and economic activity; history and national education follow as separate units.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies): a whole unit walks Iraq governorate by governorate — all eighteen in one fixed pattern (geographical location, relief, climate, water resources, population with 2017 estimates, economic activity, then a short historical note) — so the child learns the country as its provinces, each with its own map; a second unit on Iraqi society (Civics) follows.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 7 Social Studies): at intermediate level Social Studies becomes the history of ancient civilisations, and every civilisation is opened through its land — relief, climate, soil and rivers are taught as the stage on which each civilisation arose, starting with Iraq's own four relief regions.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies): Grade 9 returns to Iraq for the fullest treatment yet — the same national geography as Grade 5 but measured: the plain is 24% and about 600 km long, the desert plateau about 55%, the desert plant belt about 70%, two-thirds of the people live on the central and southern plain; every topic is pinned to numbered maps of Iraq.
