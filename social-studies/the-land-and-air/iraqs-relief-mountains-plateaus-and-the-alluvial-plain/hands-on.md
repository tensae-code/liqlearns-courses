# Hands-on — Iraq's Relief Mountains Plateaus And The Alluvial Plain

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 1 Ch. 2 Geography of Iraq, natural phenomena: Iraq's surface (relief) — the mountain region, the plateau and the alluvial plain, worked off the relief map; Unit 1 opening: Iraq's surface divisions and how relief shapes Iraq's climate and rainfall, with a blank-map (خريطة صماء) exercise; Ch. 2 Mesopotamia, geographical setting: Iraq's relief regions — the mountainous region, the undulating region, the plain region and the plateau region; Unit 1 Ch. 1: The mountainous region — the high mountains and the low mountains (Bekhair, Aqra Dagh and others); Unit 1 Ch. 1: The semi-mountainous (undulating) region, the transition between the southern low plains and the high mountains of the far north; Unit 1 Ch. 1: The alluvial plain (السهل الرسوبي) — about 24% of Iraq with its marshes and lakes, a rectangle some 600 km long, and how it was formed; Unit 1 Ch. 1: The western desert plateau (الهضبة الصحراوية الغربية) — the largest relief region, about 55% of Iraq. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
