# Ethiopia’s lakes — highland and Rift — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia’s largest lake is —
a) Lake Shala  b) Lake Ziway  c) Lake Tana
**Answer: c) Lake Tana**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Lake Tana is the source of —
a) the Omo  b) the Abbay  c) the Awash
**Answer: b) the Abbay**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a Rift lake?
a) Hawassa  b) Tana  c) Chew Bahir only
**Answer: a) Hawassa**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Lakes give —
a) only drinking water  b) nothing of economic use  c) fish, water, transport and tourism
**Answer: c) fish, water, transport and tourism**
