# Ethiopia’s lakes — highland and Rift — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where does Lake Tana lie?
**Answer: In the highlands of the north-west.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four Rift lakes.
**Answer: Any four of: Abaya, Chamo, Ziway, Langano, Hawassa, Abijatta, Shala.**
