# Ethiopia’s lakes — highland and Rift

## Do it first

Count Ethiopia’s lakes on a map. One giant sits in the highlands; a chain of them threads the Rift. Read both families.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tana, the highland giant

Lake Tana is Ethiopia’s largest lake, a highland lake in the north-west — the source of the Abbay. A table of the major lakes gives each lake’s area, depth and type, and Tana stands at the head of it.

[Picture 21023 — a lakes map with Tana and the Rift chain highlighted]

## Sub lesson 2 — The Rift chain and lake uses

The Rift lakes — Abaya, Chamo, Ziway, Langano, Hawassa, Abijatta and Shala among them — with Turkana in the south, fill the valley floor. Lakes give fish, water for farms and towns, transport and tourism: their economic uses are part of why the lake country matters.

Tell your companion the two lake families — highland and Rift — with their names and uses.
