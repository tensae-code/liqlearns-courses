# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s lakes — highland and Rift

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lake map
On an outline map, draw Tana big in the north-west and the Rift chain as beads on the valley line. Label every lake you can.

## Lake ledger
Make a ledger of what a lake gives: fish, irrigation water, town water, transport, tourism. For one lake you know, fill in what is true of it.
