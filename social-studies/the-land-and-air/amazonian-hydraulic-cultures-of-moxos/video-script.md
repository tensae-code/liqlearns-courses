Bolivia's Grade 8 student book, the *Texto de Aprendizaje — Ciencias Sociales, Secundaria 2* (printed p. 6), teaches: the Amazonian hydraulic cultures of Moxos and Baures: farming and water control in the flooding lowlands. Bolivia's teacher compendium, the *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros* (teacher guidance, printed p. 47), teaches: amazonian hydraulic cultures: raised terraces and canals by Lake Titicaca and in Moxos (teacher guidance, Secundaria 2).

Bolivia's own way (Grade 8, Secundaria 2): Secundaria 2 is the colonial-history year, so geography enters through the land itself: the Tawantinsuyo's territorial division into suyos, the Amazonian hydraulic cultures who farmed a flooding landscape with raised beds and purified water, the ayllu-versus-hacienda struggle over communal land, and where Afro-Bolivian communities settled in the Yungas.
