# India — Landscape and Monsoon Climate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** India's climate is shaped by —
a) the Indian Ocean and the Himalayas  b) Iceland's weather  c) the Atlantic alone
**Answer: a) the Indian Ocean and the Himalayas**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Chapter 1 of the India guide is called —
a) Landslag og veðurfar — landscape and climate  b) Trúarbrögð — religions  c) Umhverfismál — environmental matters
**Answer: a) Landslag og veðurfar — landscape and climate**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** India's year, as the guide teaches it, has —
a) three seasons: dry, monsoon rain, cold  b) four seasons like Iceland's  c) one season all year
**Answer: a) three seasons: dry, monsoon rain, cold**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The monsoon is —
a) the great rain that names the rain season  b) a mountain  c) a city
**Answer: a) the great rain that names the rain season**
