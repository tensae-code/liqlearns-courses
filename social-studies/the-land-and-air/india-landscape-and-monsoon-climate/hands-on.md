# Hands-on — India — Landscape and Monsoon Climate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the land and its neighbours
Draw India's outline. Below it draw the ocean and label it the Indian Ocean; above it draw a mountain wall and label it the Himalayas. You are drawing the two neighbours that shape India's climate.

## Name the seasons in order
Write India's three seasons as the guide teaches them — dry, monsoon rain, cold — and under the rain season write what the monsoon is: the great rain that names it.
