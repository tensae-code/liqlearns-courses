# Lesson plan — India — Landscape and Monsoon Climate

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** India — Landscape and Monsoon Climate
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Iceland, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chapter 1 (Landslag og veðurfar) of Iceland's teacher guide for the India film teaches how India's landscape — the Indian Ocean and the Himalayas — shapes its climate, with three seasons: dry, monsoon rain, and cold.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the sub-topic itself — India's landscape and monsoon climate, Iceland's own way.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
