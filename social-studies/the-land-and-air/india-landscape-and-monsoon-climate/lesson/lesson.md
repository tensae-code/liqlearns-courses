# India — Landscape and Monsoon Climate

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: India is a giant land in the east, and its weather is not one weather: the land itself, the ocean and the great mountains shape it into seasons. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Landscape shapes the climate

Iceland teaches world geography partly through short national-study films with a printed teacher guide. Its India film — Indland, risinn í austri, India, the giant in the east — opens with Chapter 1, Landslag og veðurfar: landscape and climate. India's landscape does its climate work through two great neighbours: the Indian Ocean and the Himalayas, the mountain wall that shapes the country's climate.

## Sub lesson 2 — Three seasons

Because of that landscape, India's year is taught in three seasons, not four: a dry season, the monsoon rain season, and a cold season. The monsoon is the great rain that gives the rain season its name — the seasons follow from the land, the ocean and the mountains together.
