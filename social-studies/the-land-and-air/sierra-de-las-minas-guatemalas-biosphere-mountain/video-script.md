# Video script — Sierra de las Minas — Guatemala's Biosphere Mountain

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Sierra de las Minas is a mountain range in Guatemala that is protected as a biosphere reserve. The lesson locates it on the map of Central America and reads about it — Guatemala's own home example of a mountain range that people protect because of the life it holds.

Guatemala learns this inside one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book: one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book per grade, organised in four units that alternate History of Guatemala/Central America with the physical geography of the isthmus — every map carries the note about the unresolved Belize territorial differendum, and Geography is always worked on maps of the seven Central American countries with Guatemala's own Sierra de las Minas, Motagua river and Maya Biosphere as the home examples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
