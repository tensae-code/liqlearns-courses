The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 21), teaches: Major climatic zones and their characteristics in Africa.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies SB): The climate unit is two-way and practical — how climate shapes people's activities (poor climate ruins roads) and how people adapt: use scarce water efficiently, build flood defences and raise dykes, grow drought-tolerant crops, and make biogas.
