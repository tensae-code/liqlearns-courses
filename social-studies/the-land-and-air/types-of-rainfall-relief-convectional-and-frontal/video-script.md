# Video script — Types of Rainfall — Relief, Convectional and Frontal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jamaica's Grade 5 Exploratory Core guide (Social Studies Term 3 Unit 1, printed p. 343) teaches types of rainfall — the lesson's own title names the three types it works with: relief, convectional and frontal rainfall. Rain is not all made the same way: the types are told apart by how the air is lifted and cooled. This is a curriculum guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil: it is cited here as curriculum-guide evidence. The placements name the topic as types of rainfall; the three type-names come from this lesson's own title as placed.

Jamaica's own way (Grade 5 Exploratory Core, curriculum guide — evidence): Grade 5 Social Studies pairs a heritage year (Europeans and Africans, national heroes, Caribbean culture) with a physical-environment year — erosion, locating Jamaica and the Caribbean by compass and latitude/longitude, types of rainfall, the Solar System, plants and water pollution. That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.
