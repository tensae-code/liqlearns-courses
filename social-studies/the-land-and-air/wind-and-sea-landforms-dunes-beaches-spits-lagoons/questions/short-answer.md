# Wind and sea landforms — dunes, beaches, spits, lagoons — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two wind deposits does the lesson name?
**Answer:** Dunes (including the crescent barchan) and loess.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a lagoon form?
**Answer:** Loose sand banks cut salt water off from the open sea, leaving it sheltered behind them.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where is a spit attached?
**Answer:** To the land at one end; the other end reaches into the sea.
