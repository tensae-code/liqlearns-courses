# Wind and sea landforms — dunes, beaches, spits, lagoons — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A barchan is…
a) a crescent-shaped dune  b) a lagoon  c) a cave
**Answer: a) a crescent-shaped dune**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dunes form…
a) under the sea  b) where wind piles sand around obstacles  c) inside caves
**Answer: b) where wind piles sand around obstacles**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Loess is…
a) fertile wind-borne dust deposit  b) volcanic ash only  c) river gravel
**Answer: a) fertile wind-borne dust deposit**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A beach is…
a) mixed sediments along the coast  b) a deep-water trench  c) a cliff
**Answer: a) mixed sediments along the coast**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A spit is…
a) attached to land at both ends  b) attached at one end, reaching into the sea  c) fully detached
**Answer: b) attached at one end, reaching into the sea**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A lagoon is…
a) salt water cut off from the sea by sand banks  b) a freshwater lake  c) a dune hollow
**Answer: a) salt water cut off from the sea by sand banks**
