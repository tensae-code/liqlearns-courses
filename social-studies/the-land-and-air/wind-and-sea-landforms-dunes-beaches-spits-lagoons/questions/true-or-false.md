# Wind and sea landforms — dunes, beaches, spits, lagoons — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Large loess deposits lie in North China.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sea only erodes; it never deposits.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A spit is made of sand and silt.
**Answer: True**
