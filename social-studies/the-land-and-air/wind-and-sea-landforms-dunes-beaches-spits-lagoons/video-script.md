# Video script — Wind and sea landforms — dunes, beaches, spits, lagoons

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wind and sea are movers of loose material: wind builds dunes and lays down loess; the sea builds beaches, reaches out spits, and cuts off lagoons. First, wind at work: In deserts and semi-deserts, wind is the great mover. Then, the sea at work — eroding and depositing: The sea both erodes and deposits. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
