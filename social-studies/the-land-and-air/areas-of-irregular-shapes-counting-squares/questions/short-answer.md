# Areas of irregular shapes — counting squares — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe the counting rule.
**Answer: Count full squares; combine part squares into whole equivalents; add them for the area in grid squares.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How is the final area obtained?
**Answer: Square count × one square’s ground area (from the areal scale).**
