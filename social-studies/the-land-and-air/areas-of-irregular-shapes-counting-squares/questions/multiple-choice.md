# Areas of irregular shapes — counting squares — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Full squares inside the shape are —
a) ignored  b) halved  c) counted
**Answer: c) counted**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Part squares are —
a) counted double  b) combined into whole-square equivalents  c) thrown away
**Answer: b) combined into whole-square equivalents**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ground area is the count times —
a) one square’s ground area  b) the map scale directly  c) ten
**Answer: a) one square’s ground area**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The instrument alternative is a —
a) barometer  b) protractor  c) planimeter
**Answer: c) planimeter**
