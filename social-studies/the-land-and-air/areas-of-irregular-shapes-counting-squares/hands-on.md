# Hands-on — Areas of irregular shapes — counting squares

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lake area
Lay or draw a grid over a lake on a map. Count full and part squares by the rule, convert, and report the lake’s area with your method written under it.

## Estimate honesty
Count the same shape twice — once generously, once strictly — on part squares. Report both and the spread: that spread is your method’s honesty margin.
