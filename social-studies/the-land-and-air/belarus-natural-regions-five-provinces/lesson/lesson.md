## Do it first

Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Natural zoning of Belarus — dividing the country by nature

After studying Belarus's nature component by component, the Grade 10 book zones it (Theme IV §27): natural zoning divides the country into parts that share one nature.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five provinces — Paazerye, West-Belarusian, East-Belarusian, Peredpalesye and Palesye

Theme IV §§28–32 then takes the five provinces in turn: Paazerye, the West-Belarusian province, the East-Belarusian province, Peredpalesye and Palesye — each province is the whole of its nature in one region.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
