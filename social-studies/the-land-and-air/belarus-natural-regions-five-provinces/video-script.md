# Video script — Belarus Natural Regions — the Five Provinces

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

After studying Belarus's nature component by component, the Grade 10 book zones it (Theme IV §27): natural zoning divides the country into parts that share one nature.

Theme IV §§28–32 then takes the five provinces in turn: Paazerye, the West-Belarusian province, the East-Belarusian province, Peredpalesye and Palesye — each province is the whole of its nature in one region.
