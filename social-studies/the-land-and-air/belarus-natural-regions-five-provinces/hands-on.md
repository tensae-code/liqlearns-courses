# Hands-on — Belarus Natural Regions — the Five Provinces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the five
Write the five natural provinces of Belarus from memory, in the book's order: Paazerye, West-Belarusian, East-Belarusian, Peredpalesye, Palesye.

## One province whole
Choose one province and write three sentences pulling its nature together — its surface, waters and vegetation as the lesson presents them.
