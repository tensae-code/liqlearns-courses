# Hands-on — Colombia's Six Natural Regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Guía 10 Las regiones naturales de Colombia: Caribe, Andina, Pacífica, Orinoquía, Amazonía and Insular regions — relief, climate, vegetation, soils, resources and work in each; Questions 6, 13 and 16: benefits of Colombia being a tropical country; why Colombia is divided into natural regions; differences among the three branches (brazos) of the Cordillera de los Andes; Questions 6, 7, 8, 11 and 12: the cordillera under Antioquia and Santander; páramos above 3,000 m as water reserves; the Llanos Orientales' main economic activity; the Amazon rainforest region and Leticia (Amazonas). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
