Colombia's CS_Grado4_02 book, the *Ciencias Sociales 4 — Segunda Cartilla, Escuela Nueva* (printed p. 50), teaches: Guía 10 Las regiones naturales de Colombia: Caribe, Andina, Pacífica, Orinoquía, Amazonía and Insular regions — relief, climate, vegetation, soils, resources and work in each. ## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado4_02): the second Grade 4 cartilla is almost pure physical geography of Colombia — relief first, then the tropical position and thermal floors, then a region-by-region tour of Colombia's six natural regions before the world's natural regions. Colombia's own way (Insertos_sociales_04_Cartilla_02): the Grade 4 review game is built almost entirely from the cartilla's physical geography — relief, deserts, polar life, Colombia's tropical benefits, its natural regions and the three Andean branches. Colombia's own way (Insertos_sociales_05_Cartilla_02): the Grade 5 review game is a road trip across Colombia's own map — Sierra, coffee zone, Llanos capybara country, Amazon toucan country — drilling capitals, cordilleras, páramos, regional economies and biodiversity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
