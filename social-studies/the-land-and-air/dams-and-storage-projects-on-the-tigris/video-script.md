# Video script — Dams And Storage Projects On The Tigris

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 8 Social Studies* (printed p. 27), teaches: Part 1 Ch. 1: The Tigris and its dams and storage/irrigation projects in Iraq — Mosul Dam, Dokan, Darbandikhan, Samarra and Kut barrages. Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies* (printed p. 38), teaches: Unit 1 Ch. 1: Control and storage projects (مشاريع السيطرة والخزن) — Dokan Dam and lake on the Little Zab (6.8 billion m³), Darbandikhan on the Diyala, and the Euphrates projects; map activity.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 8 Social Studies): the intermediate sequence widens ring by ring — after Iraq itself (G5–G6) and the ancient world (G7), Grade 8 gives the whole Arab world as one geographical unit (its position between two continents, relief, climates, waters, people and economy), worked constantly off outline maps of the Arab world; the history of the Arab world follows as Part 2.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies): Grade 9 returns to Iraq for the fullest treatment yet — the same national geography as Grade 5 but measured: the plain is 24% and about 600 km long, the desert plateau about 55%, the desert plant belt about 70%, two-thirds of the people live on the central and southern plain; every topic is pinned to numbered maps of Iraq.
