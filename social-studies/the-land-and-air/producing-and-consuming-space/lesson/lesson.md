# Producing and Consuming Space

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Folha 5: Você produz ou consome o espaço? / Do you produce or consume space? — producing and consuming geographical space, as Brazil's Grade 10 Geografia (SEED-PR) teaches it in one of its 17 Folhas, each opening with a provocative question. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Producing space — people make the space they live in

Folha 5 asks Você produz ou consome o espaço? / Do you produce or consume space? Producing space means people make the space they live in — the places, routes and arrangements around them are produced, not simply found.

## Sub lesson 2 — Consuming space — using the space others made

Consuming space is the other half of the same question: people also use and consume space that others produced. The Folha puts both halves to the learner directly — you produce or consume space, and usually do both.

Brazil's book has no chapters in the old style: it is 17 "Folhas" (sheets) like this one, each opening with a provocative question, grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
