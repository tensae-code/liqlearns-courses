# Producing and Consuming Space — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What question does Folha 5 open with, and what is it about?
**Answer: Você produz ou consome o espaço? / Do you produce or consume space? — it is about producing and consuming geographical space (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second sub lesson teach?
**Answer: Consuming space — using the space others made (any close wording).**
