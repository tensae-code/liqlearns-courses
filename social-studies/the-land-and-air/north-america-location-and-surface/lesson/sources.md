**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Palestine, Syria, Libya).**

Palestine's own way (Grade 7, PS G7P1): the world is taught continent by continent through one fixed lens — astronomical location (latitude and longitude) and geographical location first, then surface features and general characteristics — with the spread of the Islamic state across each continent as the historical thread.

Searchable tags: north america — location and surface, Palestine, الدراسات الاجتماعية, social studies, the land and air

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 8 Geography G8): Grade 8 leaves the Arab world and tours the "new" continents in a fixed six-lesson pattern (location and surface, natural environments, settlement/migration, population and cities, resources and economy, one big country study — USA, Brazil, Australia), gives Antarctica a whole unit as a land of ice and science, and closes with a unit of contemporary global challenges (water, food, pollution, warming, population, desertification).

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 9 LY G9 World Geography): A continent-by-continent world survey built on one repeated pattern — every continent gets location, surface features, climate (with summer/winter maps), climate-and-vegetation regions, population (with density map) and economic resources (agriculture, livestock, minerals and energy tables) — starting with Africa because, as the preface says, Libya's own continent links three continents and controls world trade routes.
