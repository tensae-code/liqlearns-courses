# Video script — What each Köppen climate feels like

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dry climates have unreliable rain and huge daily temperature swings.

Polar E splits in two: ET tundra, with permafrost under the surface, and EF ice cap, where ice never leaves.

In highland climate, climbing 300 metres cools you like travelling 300 kilometres toward the pole.
