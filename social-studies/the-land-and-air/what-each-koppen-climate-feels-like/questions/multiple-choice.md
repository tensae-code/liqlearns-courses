# What each Köppen climate feels like — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dry climates are known for —
a) unreliable rain and huge daily temperature swings  b) steady rain all year  c) no temperature change
**Answer: a) unreliable rain and huge daily temperature swings**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cs, the Mediterranean pattern, is —
a) dry in summer  b) dry in winter  c) wet all year
**Answer: a) dry in summer**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ET tundra ground holds —
a) permafrost  b) lava  c) sand only
**Answer: a) permafrost**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Climbing 300 m cools like travelling —
a) 300 km poleward  b) 30 km poleward  c) 3,000 km poleward
**Answer: a) 300 km poleward**
