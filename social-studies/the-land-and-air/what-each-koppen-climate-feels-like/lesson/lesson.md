# What each Köppen climate feels like

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: what would each letter climate feel like on your skin? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dry, mild and cold

Dry climates have unreliable rain and huge daily temperature swings. Mesothermal C splits three ways: Cf, wet all year (Cfa humid subtropical, Cfb marine west coast); Cw, dry in winter; and Cs, the Mediterranean pattern, dry in summer. Continental D comes in Df, Dw and Ds forms.

## Sub lesson 2 — Polar — tundra and ice cap

Polar E splits in two: ET tundra, with permafrost under the surface, and EF ice cap, where ice never leaves. Tundra ground can grow low plants in its short summer; an ice cap grows nothing.

## Sub lesson 3 — Highland — climbing is travelling

In highland climate, climbing 300 metres cools you like travelling 300 kilometres toward the pole. Ethiopia's afro-alpine tops are the home examples: the Sanetti Plateau, the Simien mountains, Guna and Choke.
