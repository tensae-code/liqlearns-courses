# Video script — What earthquakes do — shaking, fire and tsunami

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An earthquake's damage does not stop at shaking: it moves water underground, starts fires that can burn longer than the quake, and can send a tsunami across an ocean. First, ground, works and fire: Beyond the shaking itself, earthquakes shift groundwater, trigger landslides and mudflows, and wreck the works people depend on — buildings, bridges, pipelines, railways and dams. Then, tsunami — the sea carries it: An underwater quake moves the sea floor, and the sea carries the energy away as a tsunami. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
