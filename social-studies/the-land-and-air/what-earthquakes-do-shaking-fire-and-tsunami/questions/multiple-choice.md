# What earthquakes do — shaking, fire and tsunami — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Earthquakes can shift…
a) groundwater  b) the seasons  c) the Moon's orbit
**Answer: a) groundwater**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Earthquake-triggered slope failures include…
a) landslides and mudflows  b) dunes  c) glaciers melting
**Answer: a) landslides and mudflows**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In San Francisco 1906, fire burned…
a) one street  b) 521 blocks for three days  c) nothing
**Answer: b) 521 blocks for three days**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Tokyo's great earthquake fire was in…
a) 1906  b) 1923  c) 2011
**Answer: b) 1923**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A tsunami in deep water travels at up to…
a) 80 km/h  b) 800 km/h  c) 8,000 km/h
**Answer: b) 800 km/h**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A tsunami can strike a coast up to…
a) 3 m high  b) 30 m high  c) 300 m high
**Answer: b) 30 m high**
