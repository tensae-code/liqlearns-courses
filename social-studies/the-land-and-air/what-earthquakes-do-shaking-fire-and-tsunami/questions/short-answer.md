# What earthquakes do — shaking, fire and tsunami — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three built works earthquakes wreck.
**Answer:** Any three of: buildings, bridges, pipelines, railways, dams.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does fire follow earthquakes so destructively?
**Answer:** The quake breaks pipes and blocks streets, so the shaken city cannot fight the fires the quake starts. (As the book's San Francisco 1906 and Tokyo 1923 cases show.)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a tsunami grow as it nears a coast?
**Answer:** As the water shallows, the fast, low deep-water wave slows and piles up, striking up to 30 m high.
