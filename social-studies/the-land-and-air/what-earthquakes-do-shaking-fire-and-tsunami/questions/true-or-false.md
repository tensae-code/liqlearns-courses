# What earthquakes do — shaking, fire and tsunami — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fire can cause more damage than the shaking itself.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tsunamis are raised by underwater earthquakes.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A tsunami is tallest in deep ocean water.
**Answer: False**
