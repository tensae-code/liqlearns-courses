# Video script — Eritrea's Strategic Red Sea Position

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eritrea's ER G8 Social Studies book, the *ER G8 Social Studies* (printed p. 58), teaches: Unit 4: 4.3 Importance of the Strategic Position of Eritrea. Eritrea's ER G5 Social Studies book, the *ER G5 Social Studies* (printed p. 53), teaches: Chapter 5: 5.3 The benefits and resources of the Red Sea.

Eritrea's own way (ER G8 Social Studies): Grade 8 is the Eritrea-physical-geography year — relief, hydrology and water shortage, people-environment adaptation and Eritrea's strategic Red Sea location are taught as four full Geography units before the History units begin. Eritrea's own way (ER G5 Social Studies): Grade 5 widens from the village to the world — Eritrea's regions (zobas) and neighbours, the continents, islands, oceans, seas, lakes and rivers of the Earth, then back home to the Red Sea, trade and transport, maps and weather.
