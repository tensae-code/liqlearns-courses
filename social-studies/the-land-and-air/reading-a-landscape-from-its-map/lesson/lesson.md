# Reading a landscape from its map

## Do it first

A good reader looks at lines and signs and sees hills, rivers, farms and towns standing up off the page. Read the landscape-reading skill.

## Sub lesson 1 — Contours into relief

Contours — lines joining equal heights — draw the land’s shape: close together, steep; far apart, gentle; rings, hills; V-shapes pointing uphill, valleys. Reading contours turns flat lines into relief the mind can walk over.

## Sub lesson 2 — The whole landscape

With symbols added, the reader describes the whole landscape of a mapped area: its relief, its drainage, its vegetation, its settlement and its land use — the book sets Ethiopia’s population-density map as the practice for settlement reading.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion how contours and symbols together give a whole landscape.
