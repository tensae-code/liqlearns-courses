# Reading a landscape from its map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Close contours mean —
a) gentle ground  b) flat sea  c) steep ground
**Answer: c) steep ground**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Contour Vs point —
a) to towns  b) uphill, marking valleys  c) downhill
**Answer: b) uphill, marking valleys**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The five descriptions are —
a) relief, drainage, vegetation, settlement, land use  b) roads only  c) weather
**Answer: a) relief, drainage, vegetation, settlement, land use**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The set practice map is —
a) a weather map  b) a soil map  c) Ethiopia’s population-density map
**Answer: c) Ethiopia’s population-density map**
