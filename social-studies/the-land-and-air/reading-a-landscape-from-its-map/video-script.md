# Video script — Reading a landscape from its map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Contours are hills, drawn flat.
Close lines: steep. Wide lines: gentle. V uphill: a valley.
Add the symbols, and the whole landscape stands up:
relief, drainage, vegetation, settlement, land use.
