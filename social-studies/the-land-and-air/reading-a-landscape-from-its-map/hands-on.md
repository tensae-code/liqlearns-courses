# Hands-on — Reading a landscape from its map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Contour walk
Trace one contour pattern on a map and describe the walk it implies — steep climb, gentle descent, valley crossing — in the lesson’s rules.

## Five-part description
Write the five-part description of one mapped area: a paragraph each for relief, drainage, vegetation, settlement and land use, every sentence traceable to the map.
