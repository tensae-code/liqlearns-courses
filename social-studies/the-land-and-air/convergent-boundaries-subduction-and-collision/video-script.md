# Video script — Convergent boundaries — subduction and collision

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where plates converge, the denser plate subducts beneath the other — building volcanoes, island arcs, and, continent against continent, the highest mountains on Earth. First, subduction — one plate dives: Plates are built of two kinds of crust: lighter granitic continental crust, or denser basaltic oceanic crust. Then, collision — continent against continent: When both plates carry continents, neither can easily dive. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
