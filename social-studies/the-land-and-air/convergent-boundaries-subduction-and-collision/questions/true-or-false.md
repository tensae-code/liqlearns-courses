# Convergent boundaries — subduction and collision — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oceanic crust is denser than continental crust.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In continent–continent collision, one continent easily dives to the core.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Andes are linked to the Nazca Plate subducting.
**Answer: True**
