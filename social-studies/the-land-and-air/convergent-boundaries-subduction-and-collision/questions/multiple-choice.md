# Convergent boundaries — subduction and collision — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Continental crust is mainly…
a) dense basalt  b) lighter granite  c) water
**Answer: b) lighter granite**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Subduction means…
a) one plate dives beneath another  b) plates slide sideways  c) plates move apart
**Answer: a) one plate dives beneath another**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Nazca Plate diving under South America raises…
a) the Himalayas  b) the Andes volcanoes  c) the Marianas only
**Answer: b) the Andes volcanoes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ocean–ocean convergence builds…
a) island arcs like the Marianas  b) deserts  c) rift valleys
**Answer: a) island arcs like the Marianas**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Himalayas rose where…
a) the Nazca Plate met Eurasia  b) the Indo-Australian Plate met Eurasia  c) two oceans met
**Answer: b) the Indo-Australian Plate met Eurasia**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Convergent boundaries are also called…
a) constructive  b) destructive  c) passive
**Answer: b) destructive**
