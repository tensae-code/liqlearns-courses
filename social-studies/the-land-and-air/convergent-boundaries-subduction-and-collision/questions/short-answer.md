# Convergent boundaries — subduction and collision — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two kinds of crust plates are built of?
**Answer:** Lighter granitic continental crust and denser basaltic oceanic crust.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the Himalayas form?
**Answer:** By continent–continent collision, where the Indo-Australian Plate met Eurasia and the land crumpled upward.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does ocean–ocean convergence build? Give the book's example.
**Answer:** Island arcs — the Marianas are the book's example.
