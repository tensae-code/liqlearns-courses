# Five African soils — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are Arenosols easily eroded?
**Answer:** They are sandy; their grains hold together weakly and hold little water.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where are Ferralsols widespread?
**Answer:** Across Central, East and Southern Africa.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What builds up in a Calcisol?
**Answer:** Calcium carbonate — lime.
