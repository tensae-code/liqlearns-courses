# Five African soils — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arenosols are —
a) sandy soils, easily eroded  b) soils rich in lime  c) deep, strongly weathered soils
**Answer: a) sandy soils, easily eroded**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ferralsols are —
a) shallow soils over hard rock  b) young soils with no profile  c) strongly weathered, widespread in Central, East and Southern Africa
**Answer: c) strongly weathered, widespread in Central, East and Southern Africa**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Calcisols are marked by —
a) permanent waterlogging  b) accumulation of calcium carbonate  c) deep sand
**Answer: b) accumulation of calcium carbonate**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Leptosols lie —
a) shallow over hard rock or gravel  b) deep over soft clay  c) only under rainforest
**Answer: a) shallow over hard rock or gravel**
