# Video script — Five African soils

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Arenosols are sandy soils, easily eroded because their grains hold together weakly and hold little water.

Cambisols are moderately developed soils — young, but with a real profile forming.

Calcisols are marked by accumulation of calcium carbonate — lime building up in the profile, the signature of dry country where evaporation pulls minerals upward.
