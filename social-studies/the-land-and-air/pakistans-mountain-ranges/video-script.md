Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6* (printed pp. 24–26), teaches: Mountains in Pakistan: the Northern Mountains (Himalayas — Siwalik, Lesser and Greater, Nanga Parbat 8,126 m; Karakoram — K2 8,611 m and the Siachin, Hispar, Baltoro, Biafo and Batura glaciers) and the North-Western and Western Mountains (Hindukush — Tirich Mir, Sufaid Koh, Waziristan Hills, Suleman and Kirthar), with the physical map of Pakistan.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6): world physical Geography taught through Pakistan's own land — its fault lines and 1935/2005 earthquakes, Khewra salt and Thar coal among the rocks, K2 and the Karakoram among the mountains, Pakistan's four climatic regions, its six forest types from mangrove to alpine, and the Indus flood as the country's defining disaster.
