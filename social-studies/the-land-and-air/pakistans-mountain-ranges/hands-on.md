# Hands-on — Pakistans Mountain Ranges

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Mountains in Pakistan: the Northern Mountains (Himalayas — Siwalik, Lesser and Greater, Nanga Parbat 8,126 m; Karakoram — K2 8,611 m and the Siachin, Hispar, Baltoro, Biafo and Batura glaciers) and the North-Western and Western Mountains (Hindukush — Tirich Mir, Sufaid Koh, Waziristan Hills, Suleman and Kirthar), with the physical map of Pakistan. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
