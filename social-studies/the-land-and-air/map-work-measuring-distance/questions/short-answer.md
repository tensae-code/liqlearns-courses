# Map Work — Measuring Distance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two steps of every map measurement.
**Answer:** Measure on the map, then convert through the scale.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you convert with, after measuring?
**Answer:** The scale.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the thread method in order.
**Answer:** Lay the thread along the curve, mark the end, straighten it, measure with a ruler, convert through the scale.
