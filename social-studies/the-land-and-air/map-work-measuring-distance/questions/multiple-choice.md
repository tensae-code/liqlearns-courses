# Map Work — Measuring Distance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is really wanted from a map measurement?
a) map distance  b) ground distance  c) the thread's length
**Answer: b) ground distance**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Map 5 cm at 1 cm to 2 km gives a ground distance of —
a) 2.5 km  b) 7 km  c) 10 km
**Answer: c) 10 km**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A curved line is measured by —
a) guessing  b) laying a thread along the curve, then measuring the thread  c) a ruler bent by hand
**Answer: b) laying a thread along the curve, then measuring the thread**
