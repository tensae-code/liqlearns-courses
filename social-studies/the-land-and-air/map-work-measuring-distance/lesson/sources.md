Malaysia's own way (MY T2 Geografi Tingkatan 2): the year pairs map skills (scale, topographic maps) with weather and climate, then transport and telecommunications as the human-geography core, an Asia regional study, and global warming with green technology as the environmental close.

Malaysia's own way (MY T4 Geografi Tingkatan 4): upper-secondary Geography is a full topographic-map apprenticeship (bearings, area, cross-sections, landscape reading) followed by process geography — plates, rocks, weathering, mass movement, rivers and coasts — and a world-scale human geography of population, migration, settlements and urbanisation.

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Social Studies): one integrated Social Studies book per grade weaving History, Geography, Civics and Culture strands; Geography is taught through Eritrea itself first (Eritrea's location and size, Eritrean settlement, migration and disasters) with very practical, activity-led lessons.

Eritrea's own way (ER G7 Social Studies): Grade 7 turns fully to skills and Eritrea's land — map scale, area and population arithmetic first, then Eritrea's natural heritage (Red Sea coast, wildlife) and Eritrea's own disaster set (drought, locusts, earthquakes, volcanism) as the environmental core.

Eritrea's own way (ER G4 Social Studies): the Tigrinya-medium primary book teaches Geography through the child's own surroundings first — our environment, protecting our soil, water, plants and animals, then maps and globes, community life, and the shapes of the land.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 4 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): the same five-strand structure as Grade 3, widening the child's world from the local area to the district — district features, renewable versus non-renewable resources, and map scale. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 7 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Environment and Resources now turns national — formation of PNG's mountains, islands and rivers; PNG's population and migration; its mineral, marine and agricultural resources and how extraction impacts are managed; conservation areas located on the map of PNG; hazard safety and risk management. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 8 UG S1 Geography Student): Competency-based lower-secondary course taught entirely through East Africa: every skill (maps, fieldwork, photographs) is practised on Ugandan/East African places (Tororo topographic map, Uganda lat/long map), and the physical half is East Africa's relief, landform formation, climate and vegetation rather than world coverage.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 5 UG P5 Merged Home-Study SST): The whole SST course is Uganda itself: locating Uganda and its districts/neighbours, Uganda's physical features, climate and vegetation, then Uganda's population. Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Mathematics section (PDF pp. 9–220); English section (PDF pp. 221–274); SST Term Two — The People of the Pre-Colonial Period, ethnic groups, explorers, colonial rule, Legislative Council and the independence struggle (History, SST printed pp. 36–54); SST Term Three — national symbols, democracy, The Government of Uganda, duties of citizens (Civics, SST printed pp. 56–70).

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 3 geo-3af-m): The youngest geography book in the set is built on five axes worked through observation pictures (I observe / I discuss / I conclude): the map key, position, location, landmarks, and a "time landmark" axis. Geography scope placed below; the time-landmark axis is local HISTORY and is NOT placed: History of my village (printed p. 37), History of the founding of the mosque (p. 39), History of the founding of the mahadra (p. 41), History of the founding of the school (p. 43), History of the founding of the dispensary (p. 45).
