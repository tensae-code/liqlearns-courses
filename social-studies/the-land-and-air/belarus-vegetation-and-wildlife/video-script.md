The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Belarus's Grade 10 book studies the country's vegetation in Theme II §19–20: first the forest, then the vegetation of meadows, bogs and waters — the plant cover sorted by where it grows.

Theme II §21 closes the nature theme's living part with the animal world of Belarus: the animals that live in those forests, meadows, bogs and waters.
