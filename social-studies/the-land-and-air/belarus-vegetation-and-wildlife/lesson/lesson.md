# Belarus Vegetation and Wildlife

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the vegetation of Belarus — forest; meadow, bog and water vegetation — and its animal world, as Belarus's Grade 10 book teaches them. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Types of vegetation — forest; meadow, bog and water vegetation

Belarus's Grade 10 book studies the country's vegetation in Theme II §19–20: first the forest, then the vegetation of meadows, bogs and waters — the plant cover sorted by where it grows.

## Sub lesson 2 — The animal world of Belarus

Theme II §21 closes the nature theme's living part with the animal world of Belarus: the animals that live in those forests, meadows, bogs and waters.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
