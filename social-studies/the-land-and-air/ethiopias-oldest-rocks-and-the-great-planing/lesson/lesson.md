# Ethiopia’s oldest rocks and the great planing

## Do it first

Scratch a rock outcrop in your mind down to its oldest layer. That bottom layer is older than almost everything in the country. Read its story.

## Sub lesson 1 — The basement complex

In the Precambrian — the oldest era — the basement complex formed: the hard, ancient foundation rocks of Ethiopia. They were folded by the earth movements of their time. Everything else in Ethiopian geology was later laid or erupted on top of this basement.

[Picture 21017 — a basement rock outcrop with a planed surface line]

## Sub lesson 2 — The great planing

The Palaeozoic era that followed was largely a long denudation interval: weathering and erosion planed the folded surface down toward a plain. That planed surface is the table on which the returning sea later spread its sediments.
