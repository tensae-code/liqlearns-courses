# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s oldest rocks and the great planing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Plane it
Crumple a sheet of paper into folds, then smooth it down flat with your palm, again and again. The smoothing is denudation; the flat sheet is the planed surface.

## Stack order
Write the order on three cards — basement, planed surface, sea sediments — and lay them in story order, explaining each to a partner.
