# Ethiopia’s oldest rocks and the great planing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The basement complex formed in —
a) the Jurassic  b) the Quaternary  c) the Precambrian
**Answer: c) the Precambrian**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The basement is —
a) a young lava flow  b) the hard, ancient foundation rock of Ethiopia  c) a type of soil
**Answer: b) the hard, ancient foundation rock of Ethiopia**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Palaeozoic was largely —
a) a long denudation interval that planed the surface down  b) a time of sea advance  c) a time of lava floods
**Answer: a) a long denudation interval that planed the surface down**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Denudation means —
a) mountain building  b) sediment settling  c) weathering and erosion levelling the surface
**Answer: c) weathering and erosion levelling the surface**
