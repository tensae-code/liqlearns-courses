## Sub lesson 1 — Continents as Human Environments

Colombia's CS_Grado06 Secundaria Activa book, the *Ciencias Sociales — Secundaria Activa, Grado 6 (module)* (printed pp. 101–131), teaches: Unidad 2 Cap. 4 Temas 12–15 América, tierra nativa y de mezcla de culturas; Europa, tierra de estaciones; Asia, la tierra de la ruta de la seda; África, la tierra de la Atenea Negra. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado06 Secundaria Activa): Grade 6 Secundaria Activa starts from the universe and the Earth as a "living machine" (rocks and wrinkles, water, air), then follows humans evolving, migrating from Africa to America and adapting to each continent's environment — geography as the stage for all the history that follows. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
