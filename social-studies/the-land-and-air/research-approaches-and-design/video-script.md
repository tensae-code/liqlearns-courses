# Video script — Research approaches and design

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The approach is the general way of treating the problem. Quantitative research uses designs and measures producing discrete, replicable numbers. Qualitative research uses methods without quantitative measurement — historical/narrative, ethnographic and action research among its types. Mixed research uses both, interrelated, under a flexible outlook. Method is narrower than approach: the techniques used — questionnaires, interviews and the rest. Methodology is the philosophy and logic of how research is done scientifically, starting from how the problem is identified. The research design is the blueprint decided after the problem is defined: the arrangement for data collection, measurement and analysis running from hypothesis to final analysis. At minimum, a design must state the problem clearly — a blueprint that cannot say what it is for will guide nothing.
