# Research approaches and design — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Separate method from methodology.
**Answer:** Method = the techniques used; methodology = the philosophy and logic of doing research scientifically.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What must a design state at minimum?
**Answer:** The problem, clearly.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** When is a mixed approach right?
**Answer:** When the problem needs both numbers and non-numerical methods, interrelated.
