# Research approaches and design — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Quantitative research produces…
a) stories  b) discrete, replicable numbers  c) opinions
**Answer: b) discrete, replicable numbers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethnographic research is a… approach
a) quantitative  b) qualitative  c) mixed
**Answer: b) qualitative**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Mixed research uses…
a) numbers only  b) both, interrelated  c) neither
**Answer: b) both, interrelated**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A questionnaire is a…
a) method (technique)  b) methodology  c) design
**Answer: a) method (technique)**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Methodology is…
a) a list of tools  b) the philosophy and logic of how research is done  c) a timetable
**Answer: b) the philosophy and logic of how research is done**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The research design is decided…
a) before the problem  b) after the problem is defined  c) at publication
**Answer: b) after the problem is defined**
