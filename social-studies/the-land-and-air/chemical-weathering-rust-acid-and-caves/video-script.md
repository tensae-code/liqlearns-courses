# Video script — Chemical weathering — rust, acid and caves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Chemical weathering changes the rock itself — rusting it, dissolving it with weak acid — and in limestone country it carves caves hung with stone icicles. First, rust, acid and clay: Chemical weathering changes the rock's internal structure — the pieces are not the same material as the rock they came from. Then, limestone country — caves and stone icicles: In limestone, solution carves whole cave systems. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
