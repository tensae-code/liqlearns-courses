# Chemical weathering — rust, acid and caves — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How is chemical weathering different from physical weathering?
**Answer:** Chemical weathering changes the rock's internal structure and material; physical weathering only breaks it into smaller pieces of the same chemistry.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name Ethiopia's two cave examples in the book.
**Answer:** The Weyb River caves and Sof Omar.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which grows up and which grows down in a cave?
**Answer:** Stalagmites grow up from the floor; stalactites grow down from the roof.
