# Chemical weathering — rust, acid and caves — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rust is a chemical weathering process.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Stalagmites hang from the cave roof.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A pillar forms where a stalactite and stalagmite join.
**Answer: True**
