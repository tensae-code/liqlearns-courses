# Chemical weathering — rust, acid and caves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chemical weathering changes the rock's…
a) size only  b) internal structure  c) location
**Answer: b) internal structure**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Carbonic acid forms from rainwater plus…
a) oxygen  b) dissolved carbon dioxide  c) sand
**Answer: b) dissolved carbon dioxide**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Solution/carbonation dissolves…
a) carbonate rocks like limestone  b) granite only  c) ice
**Answer: a) carbonate rocks like limestone**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Hydrolysis breaks rock down to…
a) lava  b) clay and soluble salts  c) cinders
**Answer: b) clay and soluble salts**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Stalactites grow…
a) up from the floor  b) down from the roof  c) sideways
**Answer: b) down from the roof**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Sof Omar is…
a) a limestone cave system in Ethiopia  b) a volcano  c) a rift lake
**Answer: a) a limestone cave system in Ethiopia**
