## Sub lesson 1 — Slovenias Location And Borders

Slovenia's book, the *Slovenia Grade 9 Geografija 9* (printed p. 1), teaches: Lega in meje Slovenije: Lega Slovenije / Narodnostna meja (Slovenia's location; the ethnic border). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Slovenia's way — the book's own teaching way

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 9 Geografija 9): Grade 9 turns home — the whole book is Slovenia itself: first the country as a system (position and borders, relief and rocks, climate/vegetation/waters, settlement, population, settlements, economy, transport, environment), then its five natural regions one by one (Alpine, Pre-Alpine with the Ljubljana basin, Dinaric-Karst, Mediterranean, Pannonian). Named, not placed: the EU unit's history of the European Union and Slovenia-in-EU pros/cons (printed p. 7, Civics/History) and the state symbols inside the opening unit (p. 1, Civics). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
