# Video script — Low-latitude climate regions — wet, savanna, desert

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Around the equator lies the tropical wet (Af) region — the Amazon, the Congo basin and the East Indies.

The tropical wet-and-dry savanna region answers to the ITCZ, the rain belt that migrates with the seasons.

The tropical desert and steppe belt (BWh and BSh) sits under subtropical-high subsidence — sinking air that brings no rain — on the western sides of continents..
