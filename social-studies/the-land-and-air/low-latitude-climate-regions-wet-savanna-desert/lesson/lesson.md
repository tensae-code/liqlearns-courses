## Sub lesson 1 — The tropical wet region

Around the equator lies the tropical wet (Af) region — the Amazon, the Congo basin and the East Indies. Daily convectional thunderstorms drop rain through the year, and rainforest grows under it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The savanna — ruled by a moving line

The tropical wet-and-dry savanna region answers to the ITCZ, the rain belt that migrates with the seasons. When the ITCZ comes, rain comes; when it leaves, drought rules. Grassland with scattered trees is its landscape.

## Sub lesson 3 — The tropical desert belt

The tropical desert and steppe belt (BWh and BSh) sits under subtropical-high subsidence — sinking air that brings no rain — on the western sides of continents.
