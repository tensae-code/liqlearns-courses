# Low-latitude climate regions — wet, savanna, desert — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The tropical wet region's code is —
a) Af  b) BWh  c) Cs
**Answer: a) Af**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Daily convectional thunderstorms belong to —
a) the tropical wet region  b) the desert belt  c) the tundra
**Answer: a) the tropical wet region**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The savanna's seasons are ruled by —
a) the ITCZ's migration  b) ocean tides  c) earthquakes
**Answer: a) the ITCZ's migration**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The desert belt sits under —
a) subtropical-high subsidence  b) rising wet air  c) the ITCZ all year
**Answer: a) subtropical-high subsidence**
