# Low-latitude climate regions — wet, savanna, desert — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Amazon and Congo are tropical wet regions.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The savanna has rain in every month.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Sinking air under the subtropical highs brings dryness.
**Answer: True**
