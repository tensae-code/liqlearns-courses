# Low-latitude climate regions — wet, savanna, desert — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does ITCZ stand for in the lesson's story?
**Answer:** The rain belt whose seasonal migration rules the savanna (the Intertropical Convergence Zone).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What landscape grows in the savanna?
**Answer:** Grassland with scattered trees.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why is the desert belt dry?
**Answer:** Sinking air under the subtropical highs brings no rain.
