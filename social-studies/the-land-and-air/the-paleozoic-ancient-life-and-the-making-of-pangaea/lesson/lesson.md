# The Paleozoic — ancient life and the making of Pangaea

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: when did life first fill the seas and step onto land? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Life takes off

From 600 to 250 million years ago, life multiplied. Trilobites and other shelled animals filled the seas, and the Devonian period stands as the age of fish.

[Picture 27007 — a trilobite, an early fish and a first amphibian — life of the Paleozoic]

## Sub lesson 2 — Onto the land

Plants came onto land first, then amphibians followed. The living world of the Paleozoic ends the long sea-only story of the Precambrian.

## Sub lesson 3 — Pangaea and the great dying

The era ends with all continents joined as Pangaea. Its extreme seasonal climates drove a mass extinction — the largest dying in the era's story.
