# The Paleozoic — ancient life and the making of Pangaea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Paleozoic runs from —
a) 600 to 250 million years ago  b) 70 million years ago to now  c) 4600 to 3900 million years ago
**Answer: a) 600 to 250 million years ago**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Trilobites are —
a) shelled sea animals of the era  b) dinosaurs  c) flowering plants
**Answer: a) shelled sea animals of the era**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Devonian is called the age of —
a) fish  b) mammals  c) birds
**Answer: a) fish**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Paleozoic ends with the continents joined as —
a) Pangaea  b) Rodinia  c) Laurasia only
**Answer: a) Pangaea**
