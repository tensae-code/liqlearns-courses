# The Paleozoic — ancient life and the making of Pangaea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which period is the age of fish?
**Answer:** The Devonian.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which animals stepped onto land after the plants?
**Answer:** Amphibians.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What landmass existed at the era's end?
**Answer:** Pangaea.
