# The Paleozoic — ancient life and the making of Pangaea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first land plants appeared in the Paleozoic.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dinosaurs dominated the Paleozoic.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** A mass extinction closed the Paleozoic.
**Answer: True**
