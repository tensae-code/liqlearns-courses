# Video script — What size and shape do to a country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Size gives variety — climates, resources, options.
Shape gives reach — how far the centre is from the edge.
Ethiopia: large, and compact.
Governing, defending, uniting — all made easier by the roundness.
