# Hands-on — What size and shape do to a country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-country test
Draw two countries of equal area — one compact, one elongated. Measure centre-to-farthest-edge on each with a ruler. Record the governing problem the longer distance creates.

## Variety count
From earlier lessons, count what Ethiopia’s size holds: climate zones, drainage systems, language count, mineral list. Present the count as the size effect in numbers.
