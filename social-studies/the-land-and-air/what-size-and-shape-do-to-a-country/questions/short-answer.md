# What size and shape do to a country — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two effects of large size.
**Answer: Climate variety and resource variety (also weight among neighbours).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does short centre-to-edge distance help?
**Answer: Governing, supplying and uniting the country is easier when no part is very far from the centre.**
