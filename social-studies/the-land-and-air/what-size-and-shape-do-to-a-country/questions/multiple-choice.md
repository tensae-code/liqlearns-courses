# What size and shape do to a country — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Large size brings —
a) only problems  b) a single climate  c) climate and resource variety
**Answer: c) climate and resource variety**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Size is described as —
a) irrelevant  b) a store of options  c) a burden only
**Answer: b) a store of options**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Compact shape affects —
a) administration, defence and national integration  b) rainfall totals  c) soil types
**Answer: a) administration, defence and national integration**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In a compact state, centre-to-periphery distance is —
a) the longest possible  b) meaningless  c) comparatively short
**Answer: c) comparatively short**
