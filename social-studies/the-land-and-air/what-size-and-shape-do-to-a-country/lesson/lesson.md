# What size and shape do to a country

## Do it first

Govern two countries in your mind: one round and huge, one long and thin. Which is easier to run, defend, unite? Read what size and shape do.

## Sub lesson 1 — Effects of size

Ethiopia’s large size brings climate variety — many zones inside one border — and resource variety, and gives the country weight among its neighbours. Size is a store of options: more land types, more minerals, more climates to farm in.

## Sub lesson 2 — Effects of compact shape

Its compact shape affects administration and defence and national integration: distance from the centre to the periphery stays comparatively short in a compact state, so governing, supplying and uniting the country is easier than in a long or broken shape of the same area.
