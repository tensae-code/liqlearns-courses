# Hands-on — Vietnams Floods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Topic 12.1 (part): Floods in Vietnam — river floods, flash floods and inundation; their causes; where they happen (the dyke-guarded Red River delta, the short steep central coastal plains, the Mekong delta's season of "living with the flood"); the damage record and the measures for each region. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
