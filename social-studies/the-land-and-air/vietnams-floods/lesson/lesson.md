## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnams Floods

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12* (printed pp. 10–13), teaches: Topic 12.1 (part): Floods in Vietnam — river floods, flash floods and inundation; their causes; where they happen (the dyke-guarded Red River delta, the short steep central coastal plains, the Mekong delta's season of "living with the flood"); the damage record and the measures for each region. ## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12): the final-year electives turn homeward — the disasters Vietnam actually faces and how to live with them, how Vietnam itself is divided into regions for development, and the craft villages that carry its rural economy and culture — even printing the country's new 34-province table in force from 1 July 2025.
