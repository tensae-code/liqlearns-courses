# Hands-on — Irans Plants And Animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 18 Vegetation cover and animal life in Iran (پوشش گیاهی و زندگی جانوری در ایران): in our country there are different climates, and in each climate region live that region's own plants and animals; the camel, one of the wonders of God's creation, made to bear its conditions; on the high mountain peaks, because of cold and freezing, plants cannot grow, but on the mountain slopes — the Zagros slopes — there are wide forests of oak and mountain pistachio; and in the temperate Caspian region the rain falls abundantly, and dense forests of beech and maple and beautiful green meadows have come to be — which animals live in these forests?. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
