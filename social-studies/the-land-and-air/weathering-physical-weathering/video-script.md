Denudation is the lowering and levelling of the surface, and it is made of two parts: weathering plus erosion.

Physical — mechanical — weathering breaks natural rock into small pieces without changing what the rock is.

Three workers do it: heat and cold crack the rock as it swells and shrinks; tree roots grow into cracks and widen them; and in cold climates water in cracks freezes to ice, swells, and splits the rock apart..
