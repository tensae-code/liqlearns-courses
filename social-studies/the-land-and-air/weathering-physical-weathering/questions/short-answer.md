# Weathering — Physical Weathering — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is weathering, in the lesson's words?
**Answer:** The breaking of rock where it stands.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What other name does the book give physical weathering?
**Answer:** Mechanical weathering.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three workers of physical weathering in the lesson.
**Answer:** Heat and cold, tree roots, and freezing water (ice).
