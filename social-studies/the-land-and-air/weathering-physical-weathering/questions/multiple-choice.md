# Weathering — Physical Weathering — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Denudation is made of —
a) folding plus faulting  b) weathering plus erosion  c) magma plus lava
**Answer: b) weathering plus erosion**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Physical weathering —
a) changes what the rock is  b) breaks rock into small pieces without changing what it is  c) dissolves rock in acid
**Answer: b) breaks rock into small pieces without changing what it is**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In cold climates, rock is split by —
a) tree roots only  b) water freezing to ice in cracks  c) ocean waves
**Answer: b) water freezing to ice in cracks**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Tree roots help physical weathering by —
a) dissolving rock  b) widening cracks as they grow  c) heating rock
**Answer: b) widening cracks as they grow**
