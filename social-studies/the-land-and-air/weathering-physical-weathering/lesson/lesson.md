## Sub lesson 1 — Denudation: weathering plus erosion

Denudation is the lowering and levelling of the surface, and it is made of two parts: weathering plus erosion. Weathering is the breaking of rock where it stands; erosion, the next lessons' subject, carries the pieces away.

[Picture 11467 — three panels — sun-cracked rock, a root prising a crack open, ice wedging a crack apart]

## Sub lesson 2 — Physical weathering breaks without changing

Physical — mechanical — weathering breaks natural rock into small pieces without changing what the rock is. Broken granite is still granite, only smaller. That 'without changing' is what makes it physical, not chemical.

[GIF 11483 — water freezing in a crack and the rock splitting]

## Sub lesson 3 — Heat, roots and ice

Three workers do it: heat and cold crack the rock as it swells and shrinks; tree roots grow into cracks and widen them; and in cold climates water in cracks freezes to ice, swells, and splits the rock apart.
