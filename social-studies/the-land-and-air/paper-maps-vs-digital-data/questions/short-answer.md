# Paper maps vs digital data — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List digital's five wins.
**Answer:** Device use; fast copying and transmission; dense storage in small space; resistance to deterioration; ease of transformation, processing and analysis.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What can a hardcopy map not do without redrawing by hand?
**Answer:** Be reworked — enlarged, layered, queried or re-scaled.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the analogue form of a map?
**Answer:** The hardcopy — the printed paper sheet.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why can the same digital map serve many jobs?
**Answer:** Because it can be enlarged, layered, queried and shared — transformed, processed and analysed in ways paper never allowed.
