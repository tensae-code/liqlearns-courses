# Paper maps vs digital data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Analogue representation means —
a) hardcopy, like paper maps  b) data on a computer  c) satellite images
**Answer: a) hardcopy, like paper maps**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of digital's five wins in the book?
a) it copies and transmits fast  b) it stores densely in small space  c) it never needs electricity
**Answer: c) it never needs electricity**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A paper map's weakness named in the lesson is —
a) it cannot show rivers  b) it deteriorates — fading and tearing  c) it is too heavy to read
**Answer: b) it deteriorates — fading and tearing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A digital map resists —
a) deterioration  b) sunlight  c) reading
**Answer: a) deterioration**
