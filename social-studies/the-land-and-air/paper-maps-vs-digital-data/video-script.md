# Video script — Paper maps vs digital data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Analogue representation is the hardcopy world: paper maps and printed sheets.

Digital representation wins five ways: it works on devices; it copies and transmits fast; it stores densely in small space; it resists deterioration; and it is easy to transform, process and analyse.
