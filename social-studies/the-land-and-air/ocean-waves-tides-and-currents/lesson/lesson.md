## Sub lesson 2 — Mozambique's way — the book's own teaching way

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 1): distance-learning self-study module — every lesson follows the same rhythm (introduction, development with worked examples, exercises, answer key, lesson summary); the four Earth spheres are the organising spine of the whole module, each sphere closed by a "use, protection and conservation" lesson; local anchors throughout (Maputo temperature worked example, Zambezi delta, Moma/Namialo weather contrast).

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 4): two half-courses bound together — pedogeography (soil composition, profile, properties, pedogenesis, Dokuchaev classification, soil conservation with terrace/sucalco practice) then hydrogeography (basins and river regimes, groundwater, water balance, lakes, oceans, tides and currents). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
