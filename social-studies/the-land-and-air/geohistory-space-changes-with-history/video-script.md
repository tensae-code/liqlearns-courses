The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 7 G7 Geography Humanidad* (printed p. 62), teaches: Los espacios geográficos bailan al ritmo de la historia: geohistory principles. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela* (printed p. 10), teaches: Reencuentro: el hoy y el ayer en el espacio geográfico; geohistorical nomenclature in the Constitution. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela* (printed p. 102), teaches: La Venezuela Agraria: spatial organisation under new historical conditions. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela* (printed p. 8), teaches: Nuestro espacio geográfico y su economía a través del tiempo.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 7 G7 Geography Humanidad): a Geohistoria (Ramón Tovar) approach — geographic space is always explained through the historical conditions that built it; every chapter opens with a literary epigraph, works through "Entérate / Entretejiendo saberes / Indagando" boxes, and keeps returning to the learner's own community and to Caracas as the worked example.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela): a final-year economic geography of Venezuela alone — population past/present/future, human development in numbers, then resource by resource (minerals, land, fisheries, forests, oil, water/hydroelectricity) each read through sovereignty, collective participation and environmental conscience, closing on Latin American union.
