# Video script — My Province and Its Tourist Attractions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Grade 2 book turns the map home: mi provincia — my province — with its own characteristics and its atractivos turísticos, the places visitors come to see. The child learns their province the way a guide would: what the land is like, what the province is known for, and which of its places they would show a visitor first.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
