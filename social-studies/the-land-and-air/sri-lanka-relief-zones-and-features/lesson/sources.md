That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Sri Lanka is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 9 G9 English): the student companion to Book 1 — five chapters moving from the whole Asian region down to Sri Lanka's own landscape, its district-by-district development contrasts, environmental balance, and a closing practical chapter reading the 1:50,000 topographic maps.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka G10 Tamil SB): the O/L year pairs every world topic with its Sri Lankan twin — world agriculture then Sri Lanka's land use, world industries then Sri Lanka's industries — and closes with map skills and an outline-map chapter; the syllabus (units 10.1–10.13) is printed at the back.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka G10 Tamil TG) (teacher guide — evidence): a 90-period competency syllabus (units 10.1–10.13, printed in full) with an expanded-content section and a timed activity set (செயற்பாடு) for every unit — quality inputs, process steps and assessment criteria — plus annexed readings (a poem on Earth and life, a 2004-tsunami reading) and term-test blueprints; the non-content parts (teaching-learning methods, pp. 18–23, and assessment, pp. 325–337) are pedagogy, named here but not placed.
