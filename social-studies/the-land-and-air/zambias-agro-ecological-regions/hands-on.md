# Hands-on — Zambia's Agro-Ecological Regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw Zambia's three regions
Draw a rough outline map of Zambia and divide it into three large parts. Label them Region 1, Region 2 and Region 3 under the heading: Zambia's agro-ecological regions. You are drawing the division Sub-topic 2.4.1 teaches.

## One country, three farming regions
Write one sentence for this heading: how can one country hold three different farming regions? Use the lesson's words — the parts of the country do not share the same farming conditions.
