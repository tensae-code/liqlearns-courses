## Sub lesson 1 — Zambia's agro-ecological regions — what they are

In Zambia, the Geography Teaching Module for Form 2 (Grade 8), Term 3, gives a whole topic to the country's own land: Topic 2.4, Agro-Ecological Regions in Zambia, opening with Sub-topic 2.4.1, Zambia's Agro-Ecological Regions, at printed p. 31. An agro-ecological region is a part of a country grouped with the land around it because they share farming conditions — the same kind of climate and land for growing and keeping animals.

## Sub lesson 2 — Three regions in one country

Zambia has three agro-ecological regions. The module is built around Zambia itself, so the learner does not study regions in the abstract: the country they live in is divided into its three regions, and each region is a farming region in its own right. The topics that follow in the module take up first the climate of the regions, then the agriculture practised in them.
