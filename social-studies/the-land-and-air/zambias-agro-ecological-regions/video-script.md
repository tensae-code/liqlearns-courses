# Video script — Zambia's Agro-Ecological Regions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zambia itself is divided into agro-ecological regions.

An agro-ecological region groups land that shares farming conditions.

Zambia has three of them — three farming regions in one country.

Zambia's Grade 8 module teaches its own country's regions, region by region.
