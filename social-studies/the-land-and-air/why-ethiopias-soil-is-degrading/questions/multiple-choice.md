# Why Ethiopia’s soil is degrading — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A human cause of degradation is —
a) terracing  b) area closure  c) deforestation
**Answer: c) deforestation**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Overgrazing strips —
a) the rivers  b) the grass cover  c) the bedrock
**Answer: b) the grass cover**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The leading natural factor is —
a) heavy rain on steep relief  b) earthquakes  c) frost
**Answer: a) heavy rain on steep relief**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Degradation means erosion —
a) stopped  b) reversed  c) accelerated far beyond its natural rate
**Answer: c) accelerated far beyond its natural rate**
