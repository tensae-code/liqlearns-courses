# Hands-on — Why Ethiopia’s soil is degrading

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Defence audit
Look at a slope you know (in person or in memory): list its defences — trees, grass, terraces — and mark which of the lesson’s four causes is at work on it.

## Cause chain
Draw four chains, one per cause, each ending in soil in the stream. Present the chain you judge most common near home, with your evidence.
