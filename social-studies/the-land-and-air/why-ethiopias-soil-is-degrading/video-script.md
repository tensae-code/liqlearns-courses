# Video script — Why Ethiopia’s soil is degrading

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Soil is lost when its defences are stripped.
Trees felled. Grass overgrazed. Ground over-cultivated. Steep slopes ploughed.
Then the rain does the rest — faster than nature ever meant.
That acceleration is degradation.
