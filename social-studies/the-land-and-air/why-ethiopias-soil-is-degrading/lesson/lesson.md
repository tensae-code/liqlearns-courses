# Why Ethiopia’s soil is degrading

## Do it first

A steep hillside planted to its very top, its soil bleeding into the stream after rain. Read the causes of Ethiopia’s soil degradation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Human causes

Human activities accelerate erosion: deforestation removes the tree cover that held the soil; overgrazing strips the grass; over-cultivation exhausts and loosens the ground; and farming steep slopes puts loosened soil where gravity and rain can take it. Each cause removes a protection the soil had.

[Picture 21061 — a steep farmed slope with rills becoming gullies]

## Sub lesson 2 — Natural factors and the joined effect

Natural factors — heavy rain on steep relief chief among them in Ethiopia — work on the same soil. Degradation is the joined effect: nature supplies the force, human practice removes the defences, and erosion accelerates far beyond its natural rate.
