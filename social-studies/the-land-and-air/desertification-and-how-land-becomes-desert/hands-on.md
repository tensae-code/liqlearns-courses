# Hands-on — Desertification And How Land Becomes Desert

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 3 Lesson 1, Natural Problems in the Arab World: desertification is the degradation of land and its turning into dry, barren desert; its factors are scarce rain, overgrazing and the cutting of trees; Unit 4 Lesson 2, Desertification: desertification here is the advance of sand over farmland and cities; its natural causes are falling rainfall, moving sand dunes and high evaporation, and its human causes are excessive tree removal, overgrazing and unorganised irrigation methods; the United Nations marks 17 June each year as the World Day to Combat Desertification and Drought — this extends Book 4's NEW lesson with the sand-encroachment form and the world day. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
