# Hands-on — Vietnams Typhoons And Tropical Storms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Topic 12.1 (part): Storms and tropical depressions in Vietnam — how they form over warm seas, where and when they strike (the season moving from north to south; 11–12 storms a year on the East Sea, 5–6 hitting the country directly), the damage record 2010–2024 (375 deaths and 87 trillion đồng in 2024) and what to do before, during and after. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
