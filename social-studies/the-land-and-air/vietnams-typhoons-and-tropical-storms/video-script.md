Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12* (printed pp. 7–10), teaches: Topic 12.1 (part): Storms and tropical depressions in Vietnam — how they form over warm seas, where and when they strike (the season moving from north to south; 11–12 storms a year on the East Sea, 5–6 hitting the country directly), the damage record 2010–2024 (375 deaths and 87 trillion đồng in 2024) and what to do before, during and after.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12): the final-year electives turn homeward — the disasters Vietnam actually faces and how to live with them, how Vietnam itself is divided into regions for development, and the craft villages that carry its rural economy and culture — even printing the country's new 34-province table in force from 1 July 2025.
