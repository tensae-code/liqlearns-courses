# Video script — What a map is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Shrink the earth onto a page — by rules.
Simplified. Diminished. Flat. From above. To scale, with symbols.
That is a map.
Every feature of the definition earns its place.
