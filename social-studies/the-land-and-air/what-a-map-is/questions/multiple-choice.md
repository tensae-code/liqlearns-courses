# What a map is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A map is seen from —
a) ground level  b) below  c) above — a bird’s-eye view
**Answer: c) above — a bird’s-eye view**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A map is drawn —
a) without symbols  b) to scale with accepted symbols  c) at full size
**Answer: b) to scale with accepted symbols**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Simplification makes a map —
a) readable  b) inaccurate by rule  c) illegal
**Answer: a) readable**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Accepted symbols let a map be read —
a) only by its maker  b) only locally  c) by anyone who knows the key
**Answer: c) by anyone who knows the key**
