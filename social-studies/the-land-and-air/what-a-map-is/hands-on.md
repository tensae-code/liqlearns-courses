# Hands-on — What a map is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bird’s-eye test
Stand on a chair (safely) or use a rooftop view of a yard: sketch only what the bird sees — roofs, paths, plot edges. Compare your sketch with the lesson’s five features, one by one.

## Definition dissection
Write the definition on a strip and cut it into its five features. Shuffle, reassemble, and under each feature write what would break if it were removed.
