# What a map is

## Do it first

Look down at your school compound in your mind, from directly above. Shrink it onto a page. That shrinking — done by rules — makes a map. Read the definition piece by piece.

## Sub lesson 1 — The definition

A map is a representation of all or part of the earth’s surface with five features: it is simplified (detail selected), diminished (shrunk), plane — flat, two-dimensional; it is a bird’s-eye view, seen from above; and it is drawn to scale with accepted symbols.

## Sub lesson 2 — Why each feature matters

Each feature does work: simplification makes the map readable; scale makes measurement possible; the bird’s-eye plane view makes shapes true; accepted symbols make the map readable by anyone, anywhere, who knows the key.
