## Sub lesson 2 — Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 4 np-g4): Grade 4 "Our Earth" moves to the district (Kanchanpur read on a map), the origin and structure of the Earth, and Nepal's two neighbours India and China; resources and traffic rules sit inside the Civic Awareness unit.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 4 np-g4b): The newer (2078 BS) trial book reorganises the same ground by units ending in "Hamro Prithvi" (Our Earth): Earth as our common home, our needs from the Earth, our local level, and drawing a map / making a map of our village; economic life is taught through production, income-generating work and the haat bazaar (periodic market). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
