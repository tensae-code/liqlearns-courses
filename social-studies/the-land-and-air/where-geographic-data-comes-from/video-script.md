# Video script — Where geographic data comes from

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The first route is direct collection: field survey on the ground, instruments that measure temperature, rainfall and humidity where they stand, GPS fixes, digitisers tracing existing maps, and base maps themselves.

The second route is remote sensing: satellites observe the land without touching it, returning images of whole regions at once.

Data also sorts by hand: primary sources are collected first-hand by the user; secondary sources come ready-made from someone else's work.
