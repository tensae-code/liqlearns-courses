# Where geographic data comes from — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is direct collection?
a) a field survey with measuring instruments  b) a satellite image bought ready-made  c) an old atlas
**Answer: a) a field survey with measuring instruments**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Remote sensing observes the land —
a) only on foot  b) only from ships  c) from satellites, without touching it
**Answer: c) from satellites, without touching it**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Primary sources are —
a) always older  b) collected first-hand by the user  c) always printed
**Answer: b) collected first-hand by the user**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A rain gauge is used to measure —
a) rainfall where it stands  b) wind direction at sea  c) soil depth
**Answer: a) rainfall where it stands**
