# Where geographic data comes from — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three direct ways geographic data is collected.
**Answer:** Any three of: field survey, measuring instruments (temperature, rainfall, humidity), GPS, digitiser, base maps.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a satellite give the geographer?
**Answer:** Remotely sensed images of whole regions at once, gathered without touching the land.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why must the geographer know a dataset's route?
**Answer:** Because trust depends on it — which route it came by and whether it is primary or secondary.
