# Africa across the equator — the mirrored continent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The equator divides Africa —
a) at its northern edge  b) almost equally  c) at its southern tip  d) not at all
**Answer: b) almost equally** — the straddle explains the mirror.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How many sovereign states does the book give Africa?
a) 30  b) 45  c) 54  d) 62
**Answer: c) 54** — as printed in the book's Unit 1.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Kalahari mirrors —
a) the Congo  b) the Sahara  c) the Maghreb  d) the Cape
**Answer: b) the Sahara** — dry lands at similar distances either side.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Cape matches —
a) the Sahara  b) the Mediterranean north  c) the Sahel  d) the Congo Basin
**Answer: b) the Mediterranean north** — the two mild far edges.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Karoo's mirror is —
a) the Maghreb  b) the Nile  c) the Rift  d) the Namib
**Answer: a) the Maghreb** — dry interior country behind each coastal rim.
