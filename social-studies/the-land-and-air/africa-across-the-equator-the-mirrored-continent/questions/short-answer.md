# Africa across the equator — the mirrored continent — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do Africa's belts repeat in mirror image?
**Answer:** Because the equator divides the continent almost equally and the same climate machinery works in both hemispheres — wet at the equator, drier toward each tropic, mild at each far edge.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the three mirror pairs.
**Answer:** Kalahari–Sahara, Karoo–Maghreb, Cape–Mediterranean north.
