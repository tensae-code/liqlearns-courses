# Africa across the equator — the mirrored continent — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa extends about as far south of the equator as north of it.
**Answer: True** — the straddle is near-equal.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's belts occur only in the northern hemisphere.
**Answer: False** — they repeat mirrored in the south.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mirror pairs sit at similar distances from the equator on opposite sides.
**Answer: True** — that is the rule behind the pairs.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Cape mirrors the Sahara.
**Answer: False** — the Cape matches the Mediterranean north; the Sahara's mirror is the Kalahari.
