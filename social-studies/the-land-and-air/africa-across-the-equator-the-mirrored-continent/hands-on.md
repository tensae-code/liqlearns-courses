# Hands-on — Africa across the equator — the mirrored continent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fold-the-map
Draw Africa on a sheet, draw the equator, and physically fold the sheet along it. Mark where Sahara, Maghreb and the Mediterranean edge land. Compare with the lesson's three pairs.

## Mirror pairs cards
Write the six place names on six cards. Lay them face down and play a memory matching game: flip two — a pair is a true mirror pair from the lesson.
