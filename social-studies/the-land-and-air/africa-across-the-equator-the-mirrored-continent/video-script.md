# Video script — Africa across the equator — the mirrored continent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The equator cuts Africa almost in half.
So its belts repeat — mirrored.
Kalahari mirrors Sahara. Karoo matches the Maghreb.
The Cape matches the Mediterranean north. One continent, folded.
