# Uplift, lava floods and the splitting Rift — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What poured over the uplifted dome?
**Answer: Flood basalts — runny lava flows, layer on layer.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What formed in the Quaternary in the north?
**Answer: The Afar structures, with active volcanoes such as Erta Ale.**
