# Uplift, lava floods and the splitting Rift — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tertiary uplift raised —
a) the Ogaden plain  b) Lake Tana  c) the Ethiopian dome
**Answer: c) the Ethiopian dome**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The flood basalts are called —
a) the sedimentary sequence  b) the trap series  c) the basement complex
**Answer: b) the trap series**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Rift opened by —
a) faulting — the floor dropping between great faults  b) a river cutting down  c) the sea flooding in
**Answer: a) faulting — the floor dropping between great faults**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An active Quaternary volcano named is —
a) Ras Dashen  b) Mount Batu  c) Erta Ale
**Answer: c) Erta Ale**
