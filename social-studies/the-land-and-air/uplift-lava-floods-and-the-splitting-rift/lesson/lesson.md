# Uplift, lava floods and the splitting Rift

## Do it first

Push your two fists up under a cloth, spread a sheet over them, then pull the cloth apart until it tears. You have just modelled this lesson. Read it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The dome rises and the lava floods

In the Tertiary, the Ethiopian dome was uplifted — a great broad rise of the land. Over it poured flood basalts: runny lava flows, layer on layer, called the trap series. The lavas capped the highlands and gave the plateaus their flat-topped form.

## Sub lesson 2 — The split

Then faulting split the dome: the floor between great faults dropped, opening the Ethiopian Rift Valley. In the Quaternary, the Afar structures formed and volcanoes such as Erta Ale became active — the splitting is not finished even today.
