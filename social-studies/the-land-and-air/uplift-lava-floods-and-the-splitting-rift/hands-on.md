# Hands-on — Uplift, lava floods and the splitting Rift

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cloth dome
Push up under a cloth to make a dome, pour a thin sheet of paper over it as lava, then pull gently until the paper tears in a line. Name each stage in the lesson’s words.

## Order the events
On cards: sea sediments, dome rises, lava floods, Rift splits, Erta Ale active. Put them in true time order and read the story aloud.
