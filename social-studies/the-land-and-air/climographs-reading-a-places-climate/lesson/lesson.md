## Sub lesson 1 — Climographs — Reading a Place's Climate

Dominican Republic's Grade 5 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Quinto Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 36), teaches: We make and read climographs (group activity). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 5): Three geography units climb from the planet to the country: general geography and the American continent's location, then how climate varies across the continents — with climographs the children draw and read themselves — then the Dominican Republic's own climate, activities and population, ending with a “discovering the geography of my country” field activity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
