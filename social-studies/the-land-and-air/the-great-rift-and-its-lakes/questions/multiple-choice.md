# The Great Rift and its lakes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ethiopian Rift runs —
a) east to west in a straight line  b) around Lake Tana  c) north-east to south-west
**Answer: c) north-east to south-west**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Rift floor was made by —
a) wind blowing sand away  b) the floor dropping between faults as the dome split  c) rivers filling a plain
**Answer: b) the floor dropping between faults as the dome split**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a Rift lake?
a) Ziway  b) Tana  c) Turkana only
**Answer: a) Ziway**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The land around the Rift lakes is —
a) too cold to farm  b) salt desert  c) fertile, used by sedentary farmers
**Answer: c) fertile, used by sedentary farmers**
