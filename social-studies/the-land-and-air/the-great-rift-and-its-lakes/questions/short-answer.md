# The Great Rift and its lakes — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How was the Rift floor formed?
**Answer: When the dome split, the floor between the faults dropped.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four Rift lakes.
**Answer: Any four of: Ziway, Langano, Abijatta, Shala, Hawassa, Abaya, Chamo.**
