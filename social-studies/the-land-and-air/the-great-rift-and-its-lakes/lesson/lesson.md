# The Great Rift and its lakes

## Do it first

Trace the Rift on a map with your finger, from Afar down to the south. Count the lakes sitting in it. Read how they came to be there.

## Sub lesson 1 — The valley between the highlands

The Ethiopian Rift Valley runs north-east–south-west across the country, between the Western Highlands and the Southeastern Highlands. When the dome split, the floor between the faults dropped — making a long, lower corridor through the middle of the highland country, with volcanic features along it.

## Sub lesson 2 — A chain of lakes

The dropped floor holds water: a chain of lakes runs down the Rift — among them Ziway, Langano, Abijatta, Shala, Hawassa, Abaya and Chamo. Around them lies fertile land used by sedentary farmers, and the lakes themselves give fish, water and transport.
