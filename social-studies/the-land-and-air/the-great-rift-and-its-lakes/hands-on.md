# Hands-on — The Great Rift and its lakes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chain drawing
Draw the Rift as a long corridor and place the lakes along it in a chain, north to south as best the map shows. Label the two highland walls.

## Lake uses
List everything a Rift lake gives the people around it — water, fish, farming land nearby, transport — and mark which come from the water and which from the floor.
