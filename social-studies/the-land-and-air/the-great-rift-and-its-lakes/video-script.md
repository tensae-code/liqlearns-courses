# Video script — The Great Rift and its lakes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Between the highlands, the land dropped.
A long valley runs north-east to south-west.
Water gathered in the dropped floor — lake after lake.
Ziway. Langano. Shala. Hawassa. Abaya. Chamo. The Rift lakes.
