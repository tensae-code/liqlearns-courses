# Craters, calderas, batholiths, sills and dikes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Intrusive forms are magma that solidified inside the crust.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A caldera is smaller than an ordinary crater.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A batholith can form the core of a mountain.
**Answer: True**
