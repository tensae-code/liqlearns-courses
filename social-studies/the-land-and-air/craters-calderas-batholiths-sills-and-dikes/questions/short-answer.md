# Craters, calderas, batholiths, sills and dikes — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a caldera differ from a crater in size and making?
**Answer:** A caldera is the large basin left when a drained magma chamber can no longer support the cone, which collapses inward — a collapse on a much bigger scale than an ordinary crater.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Distinguish sill from dike.
**Answer:** A sill is a near-horizontal sheet between rock layers; a dike is a near-vertical sheet filling a crack.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a batholith?
**Answer:** A huge dome-shaped mass of magma solidified inside the crust, sometimes the core of a mountain.
