# Craters, calderas, batholiths, sills and dikes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Craters form by…
a) collapse, explosion or erosion  b) rivers only  c) wind only
**Answer: a) collapse, explosion or erosion**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A caldera forms when…
a) lava builds a higher cone  b) a drained chamber cannot support the cone, which collapses inward  c) rain fills a crater
**Answer: b) a drained chamber cannot support the cone, which collapses inward**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's caldera-lake example is on…
a) Mount Rinjani, Indonesia  b) Kilimanjaro  c) Erta Ale
**Answer: a) Mount Rinjani, Indonesia**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A batholith is…
a) a huge dome-shaped intrusive mass  b) a small crack filling  c) a lava flow
**Answer: a) a huge dome-shaped intrusive mass**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A sill lies…
a) vertically in a crack  b) near-horizontally between layers  c) on the surface
**Answer: b) near-horizontally between layers**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** A dike fills…
a) a near-vertical crack  b) a lake  c) a cave
**Answer: a) a near-vertical crack**
