# Video script — Craters, calderas, batholiths, sills and dikes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Some volcanic forms are holes left by collapse — craters and great calderas — and some are magma that never erupted at all: batholiths, sills and dikes frozen inside the crust. First, craters and calderas: Craters form in three ways: by collapse, by violent explosion, or by later erosion wearing the top away. Then, the magma that never erupted: Not all magma reaches the surface. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
