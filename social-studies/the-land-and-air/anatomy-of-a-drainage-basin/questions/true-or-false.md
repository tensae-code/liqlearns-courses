# Anatomy of a drainage basin — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every basin has many outlets.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tributaries are the small streams feeding the main river.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rain falling outside the watershed rim still belongs to the basin inside it.
**Answer: False**
