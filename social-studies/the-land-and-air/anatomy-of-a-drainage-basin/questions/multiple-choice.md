# Anatomy of a drainage basin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A drainage basin sends all its surface water to —
a) one outlet  b) every direction at once  c) the sea directly
**Answer: a) one outlet**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A confluence is —
a) the highest point of a basin  b) a stream that dries up  c) where two streams meet
**Answer: c) where two streams meet**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The watershed is —
a) another name for the outlet  b) the high ground dividing one basin from the next  c) the deepest part of the river
**Answer: b) the high ground dividing one basin from the next**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Which pair are synonyms for drainage basin?
a) catchment and river basin  b) tributary and confluence  c) watershed and outlet
**Answer: a) catchment and river basin**
