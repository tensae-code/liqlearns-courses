# Anatomy of a drainage basin — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does a drainage basin converge to?
**Answer:** All its surface water converges to a single outlet.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is a tributary?
**Answer:** A small stream that feeds into the main river.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the line that divides two basins.
**Answer:** The watershed.
