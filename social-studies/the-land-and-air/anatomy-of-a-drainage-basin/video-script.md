# Video script — Anatomy of a drainage basin

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A drainage basin is the whole area whose surface water converges — gathers — to a single outlet.

The small streams that feed the main river are its tributaries.

Basins are divided from each other by a watershed: the high ground around the basin's rim.
