## Sub lesson 2 — Yemen's way — the book's own teaching way

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 9 G9 Geography of Yemen and the Arab World): Grade 9 returns to Yemen by environments (mountain, coastal-marine, desert), then Yemen's population and resources, then scales up to the Arab world's environment and water problems, population and industrial development.

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 11 G11 Geography of Yemen and the Arab World): Grade 11 does Yemen in depth first (physical base, population and social development, economic resources), then the Arab world's physical geography, its people and resources, and finally its contemporary problems — water, food, poverty, illiteracy, integration. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
