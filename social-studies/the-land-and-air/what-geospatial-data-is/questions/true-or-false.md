# What geospatial data is — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Data and information are exactly the same thing.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A school is a discrete object; elevation is continuous.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Qualitative variables are amounts in numbers.
**Answer: False**
