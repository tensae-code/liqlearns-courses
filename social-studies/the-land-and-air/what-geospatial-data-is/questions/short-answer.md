# What geospatial data is — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What turns data into information?
**Answer:** Organising the raw records so they tell you something you can decide from.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three parts every geographic record carries.
**Answer:** Location information, attribute information and temporal information.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is visualisation for?
**Answer:** Showing observations so they can be read — as maps, graphs and diagrams.
