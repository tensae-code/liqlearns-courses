# What geospatial data is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Raw records become information when they are —
a) organised so someone can decide from them  b) printed in colour  c) stored on paper
**Answer: a) organised so someone can decide from them**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The three parts of a geographic record are —
a) length, width and height  b) north, south and east  c) location, attribute and time
**Answer: c) location, attribute and time**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Elevation from sea level is the book's example of —
a) a qualitative variable  b) continuous data  c) a discrete object
**Answer: b) continuous data**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Attribute information tells you —
a) what a place is like  b) only where it is  c) only when it changed
**Answer: a) what a place is like**
