# A water world — how land and ocean are shared out — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What share of Earth's water do the oceans hold?
**Answer:** Over 97 percent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How much water is in ice, about?
**Answer:** About 2 percent.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Why are southern climates more moderate?
**Answer:** Because the south is mostly ocean, and water moderates climate.
