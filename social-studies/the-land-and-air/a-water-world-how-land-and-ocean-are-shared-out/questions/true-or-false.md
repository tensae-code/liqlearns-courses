# A water world — how land and ocean are shared out — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Groundwater is 36 times all lakes and rivers together.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The southern hemisphere has more land than the northern.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Southern climates are more moderate because of the great water share.
**Answer: True**
