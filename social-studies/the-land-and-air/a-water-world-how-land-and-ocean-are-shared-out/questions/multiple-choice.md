# A water world — how land and ocean are shared out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Earth's surface is about —
a) 510 million km²  b) 51 million km²  c) 5,100 million km²
**Answer: a) 510 million km²**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Oceans cover about —
a) over 71% of the surface  b) 29% of the surface  c) half the surface
**Answer: a) over 71% of the surface**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Oceans hold over —
a) 97% of all water  b) 2% of all water  c) 0.6% of all water
**Answer: a) 97% of all water**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The southern hemisphere's land-to-ocean ratio is about —
a) 1 to 4  b) 1 to 1.5  c) 4 to 1
**Answer: a) 1 to 4**
