# A water world — how land and ocean are shared out

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: how much of your planet is actually land? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The big numbers

Earth's surface measures about 510 million square kilometres. Oceans cover about 363 million of them — over 71 percent water, less than 29 percent land.

[Picture 27010 — a world map in two hemisphere halves, with the land and water ratios marked]

## Sub lesson 2 — Where the water sits

Oceans hold over 97 percent of all Earth's water. Ice holds about 2 percent, and groundwater about 0.6 percent — yet that groundwater is 36 times all the lakes and rivers together.

## Sub lesson 3 — The uneven split

Land-to-ocean is about 1 to 1.5 in the northern hemisphere but 1 to 4 in the southern. With so much more water, southern climates are more moderate than northern ones.
