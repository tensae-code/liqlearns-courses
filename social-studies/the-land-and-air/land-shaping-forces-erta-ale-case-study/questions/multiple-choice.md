# Land-Shaping Forces — Erta Ale Case Study — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Erta Ale is in which region?
a) Afar  b) Oromia  c) Amhara
**Answer: a) Afar**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At night, in Erta Ale's caldera you can see —
a) snow  b) red lava seething  c) a lake of fresh water
**Answer: b) red lava seething**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book presents Erta Ale as —
a) a danger to close  b) a rare natural wonder and tourist attraction  c) an extinct volcano
**Answer: b) a rare natural wonder and tourist attraction**
