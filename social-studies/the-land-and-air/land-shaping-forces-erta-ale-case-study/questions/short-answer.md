# Land-Shaping Forces — Erta Ale Case Study — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does 'Erta Ale' mean in Afar?
**Answer:** 'The smoking mountain'.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things show at the crater rim?
**Answer:** Rainbow-coloured lava and ash.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is a caldera, as the lesson uses it?
**Answer:** The great hollow at the top of the volcano.
