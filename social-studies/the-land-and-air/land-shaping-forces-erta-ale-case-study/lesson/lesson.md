## Sub lesson 1 — A limited study: the smoking mountain

This is a limited study — a case study. Erta Ale, in the Afar region, is an active volcano. Its name in the Afar language means 'the smoking mountain'.

[Picture 11463 — Erta Ale's crater at night with red lava in the caldera]

## Sub lesson 2 — What the crater shows

Its craters breathe strong-smelling fumes. Rainbow-coloured lava and ash show at the crater rim, and at night red lava can be seen seething — moving hot and liquid — in its caldera, the great hollow at the top.

## Sub lesson 3 — A rare natural wonder

The book presents Erta Ale as a rare natural wonder and a tourist attraction: a place where the internal forces of the last lessons can be seen at work with your own eyes, in Ethiopia itself.
