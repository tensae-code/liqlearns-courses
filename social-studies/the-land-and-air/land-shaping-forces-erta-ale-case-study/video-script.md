# Video script — Land-Shaping Forces — Erta Ale Case Study

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

High in the Afar land, a mountain smokes.

Its name is Erta Ale. In the Afar language,
it means: the smoking mountain.

Its craters breathe strong-smelling fumes.
At the rim: lava in rainbow colours, and ash.

At night, look into the caldera — the great hollow at the top.
Red lava seethes there. Hot. Liquid. Moving.

The inside of the Earth, seen with your own eyes,
in Ethiopia itself.
