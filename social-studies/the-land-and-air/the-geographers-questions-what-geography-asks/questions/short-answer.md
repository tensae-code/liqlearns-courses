# The geographer's questions — what geography asks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two sciences does geography bridge?
**Answer:** Natural science and social science.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two content concepts of geography.
**Answer:** Location/direction/distance; size and scale; physical and cultural attributes; interrelations between places; place similarity and regions (any two).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Ask three of the geographer's questions about your own town.
**Answer:** What is it? Where is it? How did it come to be? (any three of the guide's seven).
