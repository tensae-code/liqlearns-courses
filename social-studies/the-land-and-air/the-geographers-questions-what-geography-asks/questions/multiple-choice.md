# The geographer's questions — what geography asks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Geography is framed as the bridge between —
a) natural and social science  b) two oceans  c) past and future
**Answer: a) natural and social science**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Geography studies variability over —
a) space and time  b) sea level only  c) one village
**Answer: a) space and time**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Physical and cultural attributes are among geography's —
a) content concepts  b) map symbols  c) exam rules
**Answer: a) content concepts**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these is a geographer's key question?
a) How did it come to be?  b) Who won the match?  c) What is for lunch?
**Answer: a) How did it come to be?**
