# Measuring straight distances on a map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first step is —
a) convert  b) draw  c) measure with a ruler on the map
**Answer: c) measure with a ruler on the map**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Map distance is read in —
a) degrees  b) centimetres  c) kilometres
**Answer: b) centimetres**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At 1:50,000, 6 cm on the map is —
a) 3 km  b) 6 km  c) 0.3 km
**Answer: a) 3 km**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Conversion uses —
a) the legend  b) the title  c) the scale
**Answer: c) the scale**
