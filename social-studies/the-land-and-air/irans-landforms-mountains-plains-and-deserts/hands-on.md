# Hands-on — Irans Landforms Mountains Plains And Deserts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 10 Iran, our home (ایران، خانۀ ما): the landforms of Iran (اَشکال زمین در ایران) — not all of Iran is alike in height: the northern slopes of the Alborz facing the sea take heavy snow and rain and are covered in dense forest, the opposite slopes dry; the low and flat regions — plains (دشت‌ها) and lowlands (جلگه‌ها), and Iran's two vast deserts. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
