# The Cenozoic — mammals, modern continents and us — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which era do we live in?
**Answer:** The Cenozoic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happened to the continents in this era?
**Answer:** They reached their present positions.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name two forces that shaped modern landscapes.
**Answer:** Volcanism, folding and glaciation (any two).
