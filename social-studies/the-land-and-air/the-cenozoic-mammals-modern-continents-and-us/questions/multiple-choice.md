# The Cenozoic — mammals, modern continents and us — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cenozoic runs from —
a) 70 million years ago to now  b) 250 to 70 million years ago  c) 600 to 250 million years ago
**Answer: a) 70 million years ago to now**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cenozoic is the age of —
a) mammals  b) dinosaurs  c) trilobites
**Answer: a) mammals**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the Cenozoic, the continents —
a) reached their present positions  b) joined into Rodinia  c) sank under the sea
**Answer: a) reached their present positions**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Modern landscapes were shaped by volcanism, folding and —
a) glaciation  b) printing  c) farming only
**Answer: a) glaciation**
