# The Cenozoic — mammals, modern continents and us

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: which era do you live in? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The age of mammals

From 70 million years ago to the present, birds, mammals and flowering plants dominate the living world. This is the best-known era, because it is the one we live in.

[Picture 27009 — a mammal-and-bird landscape, with a small map of the continents today]

## Sub lesson 2 — The modern map appears

In the Cenozoic the continents reached their present positions. The world map you know is a Cenozoic product.

## Sub lesson 3 — Landscapes still being shaped

Volcanism, folding and glaciation shaped the modern landscapes in this era — and those same forces are still at work today.
