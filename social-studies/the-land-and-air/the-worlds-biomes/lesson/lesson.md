## Sub lesson 1 — Protect the Bond of Nature and People: investigating and modelling the different biomes of the world

Bangladesh's Grade 8 student book, the *History and Social Science, Class Eight* (printed pp. 128-131), teaches: protect the Bond of Nature and People: investigating and modelling the different biomes of the world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bangladesh's way — the book's own teaching way

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): the year's Geography is one long chapter, "Protect the Bond between Nature and People": pupils model the world's biomes, compare world forest cover in 2000 and 2020, map Bangladesh's own forests and disaster-prone areas, and read a long inquiry-reader on biomes, sea resources and the Sundarbans mangrove. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
