**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Bangladesh, Dominican Republic, Malaysia).**

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): the year's Geography is one long chapter, "Protect the Bond between Nature and People": pupils model the world's biomes, compare world forest cover in 2000 and 2020, map Bangladesh's own forests and disaster-prone areas, and read a long inquiry-reader on biomes, sea resources and the Sundarbans mangrove.

Searchable tags: biomes, modelling biomes, Protect the Bond between Nature and People, Bangladesh, History and Social Science, social studies, the land and air

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 10): This is the physical-geography year: the universe and Earth's layers, continental drift with volcanoes and earthquakes — including earthquake-prevention measures as a group task — water and winds across the continents, and a closing unit on political and economic geography that treats borders, geopolitical order and international climate agreements as geography, with hurricane-season citizen responsibility as the water unit's group activity.

Malaysia's own way (MY T3 Geografi Tingkatan 3): Form 3 turns from skills (tables, graphs, pie charts) to living Malaysia — its natural vegetation and wildlife and the human pressure on both — then outward to the world's biomes, resources and economic cooperation, closing with forest management and recycling.

Malaysia's own way (MY T5 Geografi Tingkatan 5): the final form is the world's systems book — advanced graphs and photo interpretation, then weather and climate at world scale, vegetation and wildlife by climate zone, energy, and the environmental bill for economic activity.
