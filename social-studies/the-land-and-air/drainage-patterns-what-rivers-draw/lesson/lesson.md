# Drainage patterns — what rivers draw

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: rivers draw four figures on the land — each one is a clue to the rock beneath? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dendritic and trellis

Dendritic drainage branches like a tree, on homogeneous horizontal rock that steers the water nowhere in particular. Trellis drainage runs in a rectangular grid on alternating hard and soft bands, steered by folding and faulting.

## Sub lesson 2 — Radial — outward

Radial drainage flows outward in all directions from a raised feature — a cone, a dome, a volcanic peak. The pattern points at the high ground in its middle.

## Sub lesson 3 — Centripetal — inward

Centripetal drainage is radial reversed: streams flow inward from surrounding high ground to a central basin. Four patterns, four geologies: read the drawing, name the rock.
