# Drainage patterns — what rivers draw — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dendritic drainage forms on —
a) homogeneous horizontal rock  b) folded bands  c) a volcano
**Answer: a) homogeneous horizontal rock**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dendritic means —
a) tree-like branching  b) a rectangular grid  c) spokes of a wheel
**Answer: a) tree-like branching**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Trellis drainage is steered by —
a) folding and faulting  b) uniform rock  c) a central basin
**Answer: a) folding and faulting**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Centripetal streams flow —
a) inward to a central basin  b) outward from a peak  c) to the sea only
**Answer: a) inward to a central basin**
