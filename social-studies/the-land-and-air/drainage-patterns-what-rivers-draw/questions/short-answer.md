# Drainage patterns — what rivers draw — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four patterns.
**Answer:** Dendritic, trellis, radial, centripetal.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which rock gives which of the first two?
**Answer:** Dendritic: homogeneous horizontal rock. Trellis: alternating hard/soft bands, folded/faulted.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Distinguish radial from centripetal.
**Answer:** Radial flows outward from raised ground; centripetal flows inward to a central basin.
