# Video script — Drainage patterns — what rivers draw

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dendritic drainage branches like a tree, on homogeneous horizontal rock that steers the water nowhere in particular.

Radial drainage flows outward in all directions from a raised feature — a cone, a dome, a volcanic peak.

Centripetal drainage is radial reversed: streams flow inward from surrounding high ground to a central basin.
