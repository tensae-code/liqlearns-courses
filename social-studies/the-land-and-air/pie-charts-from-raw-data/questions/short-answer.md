# Pie charts from raw data — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What question does a pie chart answer supremely well?
**Answer:** What share? — each part's percentage of the whole.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the construction steps in order.
**Answer:** Total the data; turn each part into a percentage; convert each percentage into its slice; draw the slices in order; label each part and share.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** If a part is 25 percent of the data, how much of the circle is its slice?
**Answer:** A quarter of the circle.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A class has 20 boys and 30 girls. What slice does each sex take?
**Answer:** Boys: 20 of 50 = 40 percent — two-fifths of the circle. Girls: 30 of 50 = 60 percent — three-fifths.
