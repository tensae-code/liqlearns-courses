# Pie charts from raw data — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A pie chart divides —
a) a whole into percentage segments  b) time into months  c) a map into squares
**Answer: a) a whole into percentage segments**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The first construction step is to —
a) draw the circle  b) colour the slices  c) total the data
**Answer: c) total the data**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A part that is half the data takes —
a) the whole circle  b) half the circle  c) a quarter of the circle
**Answer: b) half the circle**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Percentages of all parts must add to —
a) 100 percent  b) 50 percent  c) 360 percent only in number, not in share
**Answer: a) 100 percent**
