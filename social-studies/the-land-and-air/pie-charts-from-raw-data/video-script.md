# Video script — Pie charts from raw data

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A pie chart divides a whole into segments, each sized by its percentage of the whole.

The book gives the steps from raw data to final output: total the data, turn each part into its percentage of the whole, convert each percentage into its slice of the circle, draw the slices in order, and label each with its part and share.
