# Pie charts from raw data

## Do it first

Before you read, think of a whole day, or a whole farm, or a whole class. If you had to show its parts inside one circle, how big would each slice be? Guess the rule first — then read the construction.

## Sub lesson 1 — A whole in parts

A pie chart divides a whole into segments, each sized by its percentage of the whole. The circle is always 100 percent; a part that is half the data takes half the circle. That is why pie charts answer one question supremely well: what share?

[Picture 21955 — a circle being sliced into percentage segments, each labelled]

## Sub lesson 2 — The construction steps

The book gives the steps from raw data to final output: total the data, turn each part into its percentage of the whole, convert each percentage into its slice of the circle, draw the slices in order, and label each with its part and share. Followed in order, the steps never fail.
