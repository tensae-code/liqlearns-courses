Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6* (printed p. 40), teaches: Importance of climate in human life: lifestyles and house roofs, animals (the desert camel), and which crops grow where. Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 8 Geography 8* (printed pp. 59–60), teaches: Impact of climate on vegetation and human life in Pakistan: alpine forests above 4,000 m and riverine forests; farming, dress, housing and tourism shaped by climate (the lesson slug was proposed as NEW in Book 4 of this file; counted here as an existing target).

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6): world physical Geography taught through Pakistan's own land — its fault lines and 1935/2005 earthquakes, Khewra salt and Thar coal among the rocks, K2 and the Karakoram among the mountains, Pakistan's four climatic regions, its six forest types from mangrove to alpine, and the Indus flood as the country's defining disaster.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 8 Geography 8): the Urdu-medium twin of the English Geography course — deep map-and-graph craft first (dot and choropleth maps of Pakistan itself), then landform agents, oceans, a full Pakistan-anchored disaster chapter, the world's natural regions, Pakistan's own climate, its four neighbouring regions and strategic place, underdevelopment with Pakistan's own causes, and modern techniques down to Pak Sat-1.
