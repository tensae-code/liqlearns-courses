# Video script — Sorting the world's climates — three ways

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A planet of varying climates is too much to hold one place at a time.

The ancient Greeks divided the world by latitude into Frigid, Temperate and Torrid zones, judged by habitability.

Empirical schemes classify by observed data, with set boundaries drawn from measured temperature and rainfall.
