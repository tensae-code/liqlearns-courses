# Sorting the world's climates — three ways — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Classification simplifies climates into —
a) recognisable patterns  b) random lists  c) single places
**Answer: a) recognisable patterns**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Greek zones were judged by —
a) habitability  b) rainfall gauges  c) satellites
**Answer: a) habitability**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Genetic schemes classify by —
a) causes — air masses, circulation, fronts, radiation  b) observed data only  c) vegetation only
**Answer: a) causes — air masses, circulation, fronts, radiation**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Empirical schemes classify by —
a) observed data with set boundaries  b) latitude only  c) stories
**Answer: a) observed data with set boundaries**

**5. (Sub lesson 4)** The Grade 10 survey sets out, before Africa's climates —
a) only Ethiopia's zones  b) only rainfall maps  c) the world's climate types, regions and rival classification schemes
**Answer: c) the world's climate types, regions and rival classification schemes**
