# Sorting the world's climates — three ways — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why classify climates?
**Answer:** To simplify a varying planet into recognisable patterns.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name one cause a genetic scheme uses.
**Answer:** Air masses, circulation, fronts or solar radiation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two working empirical systems.
**Answer:** Köppen's and Trewartha's.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What is the Grade 10 section's stated outcome?
**Answer:** That pupils can name and weigh the rival schemes for classifying the world's climates.
