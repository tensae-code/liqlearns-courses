# Making an overlap diagram

## Do it first

Before you read, think of three things that each matter alone but matter most where they meet — like land, people and livelihood. Draw three overlapping circles on paper and guess what belongs in the middle. Then read.

## Sub lesson 1 — What a diagram is for

A diagram plans how separate parts work — and where they overlap. Unlike a graph of numbers, an overlap diagram shows belonging: what sits in one part only, what two parts share, and what all parts hold together in the middle.

## Sub lesson 2 — The worked example — sustainable development in Word

The book's worked example draws the components of sustainable development as overlapping circles in Microsoft Word, with text inserted in each circle. The middle — where all the circles cross — is the point of the whole diagram: sustainable development lives exactly where its components meet.
