# Making an overlap diagram — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An overlap diagram shows —
a) how separate parts work and where they overlap  b) change over time  c) bar heights
**Answer: a) how separate parts work and where they overlap**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The worked example draws —
a) a rainfall graph  b) a contour map  c) the components of sustainable development as overlapping circles
**Answer: c) the components of sustainable development as overlapping circles**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The middle of the diagram is where —
a) the title goes  b) all the parts cross — the shared heart  c) nothing belongs
**Answer: b) all the parts cross — the shared heart**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Text in the worked example is inserted —
a) in each circle  b) only in the middle  c) only in the title
**Answer: a) in each circle**
