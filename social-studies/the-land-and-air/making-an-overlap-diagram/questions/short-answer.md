# Making an overlap diagram — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does an overlap diagram show that a bar graph cannot?
**Answer:** Belonging — what sits in one part only, what parts share, and what all hold together.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In what program does the book build its example, and of what?
**Answer:** In Microsoft Word — the components of sustainable development as overlapping circles.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the middle zone the point of the diagram?
**Answer:** Because sustainable development lives where its components meet — the middle is what the diagram exists to show.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What belongs only in the middle zone of an overlap diagram?
**Answer:** What all the parts hold together — the shared heart that the diagram exists to show.
