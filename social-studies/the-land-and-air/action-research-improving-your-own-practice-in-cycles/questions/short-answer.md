# Action research — improving your own practice in cycles — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the cycle's four stages.
**Answer:** Plan, act, observe, reflect (then re-plan).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does action research differ from academic research?
**Answer:** It is practitioner-led and aims at improving the practitioner's own practice.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose evidence is this lesson?
**Answer:** The old-edition Grade 12 teacher guide's (2010–11) — teacher evidence, not the 2023 student book.
