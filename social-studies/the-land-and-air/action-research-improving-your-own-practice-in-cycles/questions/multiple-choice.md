# Action research — improving your own practice in cycles — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Action research is led by…
a) outside experts  b) the practitioner  c) the ministry
**Answer: b) the practitioner**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Its aim is improving…
a) one's own action/practice  b) general theory only  c) exam marks
**Answer: a) one's own action/practice**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Its shape is…
a) a straight line  b) cyclical  c) a pyramid
**Answer: b) cyclical**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The cycle runs plan, act, observe and…
a) publish  b) reflect  c) stop
**Answer: b) reflect**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** After reflecting, the researcher…
a) re-plans and turns the cycle again  b) ends forever  c) changes schools
**Answer: a) re-plans and turns the cycle again**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** It can be run on problems in…
a) one's own school and locality  b) other countries  c) laboratories only
**Answer: a) one's own school and locality**
