# Video script — Folding — anticlines, synclines and fold mountains

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Squeeze layered rock sideways and it bends before it breaks — up into anticlines, down into synclines, and, on the grand scale, into fold-mountain ranges. First, anticlines and synclines: Sideways compression bends rock layers. Then, fold mountains, young and old: Whole mountain ranges are folds written large. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
