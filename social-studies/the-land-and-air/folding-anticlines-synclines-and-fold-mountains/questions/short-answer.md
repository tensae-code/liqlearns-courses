# Folding — anticlines, synclines and fold mountains — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the difference between an anticline and a syncline?
**Answer:** An anticline is an upfold (an arch); a syncline is a downfold (a trough).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two young and two old fold-mountain ranges.
**Answer:** Young — any two of: Andes, Rockies, Alps, Himalayas, Atlas, Australian Alps. Old — any two of: Scandinavian Caledonides, Appalachians, Urals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How old are the old fold mountains?
**Answer:** About 250–300 million years old.
