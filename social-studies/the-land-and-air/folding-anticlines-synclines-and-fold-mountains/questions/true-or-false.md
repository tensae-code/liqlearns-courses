# Folding — anticlines, synclines and fold mountains — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Anticlines arch upward and synclines dip downward.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Appalachians are young fold mountains from the Alpine orogeny.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** There were three orogenies in the last 400 million years.
**Answer: True**
