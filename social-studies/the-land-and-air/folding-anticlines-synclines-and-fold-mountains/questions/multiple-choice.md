# Folding — anticlines, synclines and fold mountains — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An anticline is…
a) an upfold  b) a downfold  c) a crack
**Answer: a) an upfold**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A syncline is…
a) an upfold  b) a downfold  c) a volcano
**Answer: b) a downfold**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Folding is caused by…
a) sideways compression  b) rainfall  c) cooling
**Answer: a) sideways compression**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Continued pressure after simple folds produces…
a) deltas  b) asymmetrical, over- and over-thrust folds  c) caves
**Answer: b) asymmetrical, over- and over-thrust folds**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Which range is a YOUNG fold mountain range?
a) the Urals  b) the Appalachians  c) the Himalayas
**Answer: c) the Himalayas**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Old fold mountains are…
a) higher than young ones  b) lower and more weathered  c) still forming today
**Answer: b) lower and more weathered**
