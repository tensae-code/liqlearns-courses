# Ecuador in the Andean Space

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Ecuador as part of the Andean space — Andean relief, active volcanoes and the main rivers (Guayas, Pastaza, Napo, Chota, Machángara, Chone) — as Ecuador's Grade 5 *Estudios Sociales 5to EGB* teaches it in Ecuador como parte del espacio Andino on printed p. 112. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Relief, volcanoes and rivers of Andean Ecuador

Grade 5 re-reads the country spatially, starting with the Andes: Ecuador como parte del espacio Andino — Ecuador as part of the Andean space. The Andes give the country its relief, its chain of active volcanoes, and its great rivers — the Guayas, Pastaza, Napo, Chota, Machángara and Chone among them — which run down from the high spine to the coast and to the Amazon. To place Ecuador in the Andean space is to explain why its land, water and settlements lie where they do.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the 2024 Grade 5 book is History-led (five of eight sections are national history, from the roots of society to the Republic); Geography is concentrated at the end, where the country is re-read spatially — Andean relief and rivers, natural regions and climatic floors, population diversity and migration, territorial division, provincial products, and the protected-area system taught together with climate-change action. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
