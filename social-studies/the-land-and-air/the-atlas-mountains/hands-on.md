# Hands-on — The Atlas Mountains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cloth-fold mountains
Lay a cloth flat and push its two ends toward each other. Watch the folds rise. Those ridges are fold mountains in miniature — the Atlas was raised the same way, by sideways squeezing.

## Trace the ranges
On a printed or drawn outline of north-west Africa, trace the Atlas line along Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia and label the four ranges. Check your line against the lesson picture.
