# Lesson plan — The Atlas Mountains

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** The Atlas Mountains
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Landforms of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Atlas Mountains are Africa's alpine system: young fold mountains — High Atlas, Middle Atlas, Rif and Tell Atlas — running along the Mediterranean margin of Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Africa's four landform regions — the Atlas is the Alpine System in detail.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Young fold mountains on the Mediterranean margin** — What the Atlas is and where it runs.
2. **The four ranges — High, Middle, Rif and Tell** — The named parts of the system.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Africa's four landform regions. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the Atlas Mountains are and where they run.
2. Explain 'young fold mountains' in plain words.
3. Name the High Atlas, Middle Atlas, Rif and Tell Atlas and place each pair.

## What you need
