# The Atlas Mountains — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Atlas Mountains are —
a) ancient worn plains  b) young fold mountains  c) a rift valley  d) a raised platform
**Answer: b) young fold mountains** — layers squeezed until they bent and rose.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Atlas runs along which margin of Africa?
a) The Atlantic south  b) The Mediterranean margin  c) The Indian Ocean coast  d) The Red Sea only
**Answer: b) The Mediterranean margin** — through Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which pair of ranges stands in Morocco?
a) Rif and Tell  b) High and Middle Atlas  c) High and Rif  d) Tell and Middle
**Answer: b) High and Middle Atlas** — the Moroccan west holds High and Middle.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Tell Atlas is best described as —
a) the highest peak  b) the range nearest the Mediterranean shore in the east  c) a desert  d) a plateau lake
**Answer: b) the range nearest the Mediterranean shore in the east** — Rif and Tell run along the coast eastward.
