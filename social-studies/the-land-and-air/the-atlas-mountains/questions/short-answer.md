# The Atlas Mountains — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are Atlas mountains called 'young' beside the Massif?
**Answer:** Because they are recently folded and still stand high and sharp, while the Massif is ancient and worn smooth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four Atlas ranges.
**Answer:** High Atlas, Middle Atlas, Rif and Tell Atlas.
