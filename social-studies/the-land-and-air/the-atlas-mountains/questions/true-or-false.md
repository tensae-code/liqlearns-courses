# The Atlas Mountains — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Atlas is called Africa's Alpine System.
**Answer: True** — its folding matches the Alps' family.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Atlas lies south of the Sahara.
**Answer: False** — it is a rim between the Mediterranean and the Sahara — north of the desert.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The High Atlas is the highest part of the system.
**Answer: True** — the lesson names it so.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Rif is in the far south of the continent.
**Answer: False** — the Rif runs along the northern coast of the north-west.
