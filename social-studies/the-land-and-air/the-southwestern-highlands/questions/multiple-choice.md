# The southwestern highlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a southwestern highland block?
a) Bale  b) Hararge  c) Kefa
**Answer: c) Kefa**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Benishangul belongs to —
a) the Rift floor  b) the southwestern/western highlands  c) the southeastern highlands
**Answer: b) the southwestern/western highlands**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dissected means —
a) cut up by many valleys  b) covered in ice  c) completely flat
**Answer: a) cut up by many valleys**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** One of the named highest points is —
a) Ras Dashen  b) Mount Batu  c) Tullu Wallel
**Answer: c) Tullu Wallel**
