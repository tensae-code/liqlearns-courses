# The southwestern highlands — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List three of the six blocks.
**Answer: Any three of: Shewa, Wollega, Illubabor, Kefa, Gamo-Gofa, Benishangul.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two of the highest points given in the lesson.
**Answer: Any two of: Gamo-Konso, Maji-Korma, Dawuro-Konta, Tullu Wallel.**
