# Hands-on — The southwestern highlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Block map
Draw six boxes for the six blocks and arrange them as the map shows: Shewa central, Wollega and Benishangul west, Illubabor and Kefa south-west, Gamo-Gofa south.

## Word work
Explain the word dissected to a partner using a cake cut by a knife in many lines — then say it in the geography words of the lesson.
