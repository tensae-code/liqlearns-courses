# Video script — The southwestern highlands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

South-west, the highlands break into green blocks.
Shewa. Wollega. Illubabor. Kefa. Gamo-Gofa. Benishangul.
Cut by valleys. Covered in forest where the rain is heavy.
Tullu Wallel stands among their highest points.
