# The southwestern highlands

## Do it first

Look at the south-west of the highland map: green, rainy, cut by many rivers. Read the blocks that make it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The blocks of the south-west

The southwestern part of the Western Highlands is a set of blocks: Shewa, Wollega, Illubabor, Kefa, Gamo-Gofa and Benishangul. Each is highland in its own right, separated from its neighbours by the river valleys between them.

[Picture 21007 — a map highlighting each named southwestern highland block]

## Sub lesson 2 — Dissected and forested

These highlands are dissected — cut up by many valleys — and, in their wet parts, forested. Their highest points are named: among them Gamo-Konso, Maji-Korma, Dawuro-Konta and Tullu Wallel. This is the wettest corner of the highlands, the home of the moist forests.
