## Sub lesson 1 — South Africa Land And People

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11* (printed pp. 152–158), teaches: Bài 30: The Republic of South Africa — position (the land at Africa's southern tip, with three capitals and Lesotho inside it) and nature (the interior plateau and Drakensberg escarpment, the Kalahari; subtropical contrasts; the Orange and Limpopo; savanna and Kruger; minerals — 5,000 tons of gold, 9.3% of the world's reserves, diamonds, coal), population (59.3 million; 67.4% urban; Cape Town, Durban, Johannesburg) and society (HIV/AIDS and inequality among its challenges). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11): after Grade 10's world systems, Grade 11 walks the world region by region — Latin America, the EU, Southeast Asia and ASEAN, Southwest Asia, the USA, Russia, Japan, China, Australia and South Africa — always with the same lens (position, nature, people, economy) and always measuring Vietnam's own place among them (its HDI row in the opening table, EU–Vietnam trade, Vietnam inside ASEAN, Japan's FDI into Vietnam). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
