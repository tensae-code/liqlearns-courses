## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 30: The Republic of South Africa — position (the land at Africa's southern tip, with three capitals and Lesotho inside it) and nature (the interior plateau and Drakensberg escarpment, the Kalahari; subtropical contrasts; the Orange and Limpopo; savanna and Kruger; minerals — 5,000 tons of gold, 9.3% of the world's reserves, diamonds, coal), population (59.3 million; 67.4% urban; Cape Town, Durban, Johannesburg) and society (HIV/AIDS and inequality among its challenges). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
