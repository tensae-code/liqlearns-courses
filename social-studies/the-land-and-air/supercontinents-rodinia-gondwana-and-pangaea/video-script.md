# Video script — Supercontinents — Rodinia, Gondwana and Pangaea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The first solid rocks, formed between 4600 and 3900 million years ago, gathered into an early supercontinent called Rodinia.

Later, Gondwana formed around the South Pole.

Collisions closed the seas and built a pre-Pangaea.
