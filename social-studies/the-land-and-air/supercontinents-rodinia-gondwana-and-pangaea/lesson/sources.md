Pairing warning carried from the placements: the Grade 11 teacher guide's answer key (guide printed p.18) dates Rodinia to about 3800 Ma, against this student book's Figure 1.2 (about 750 Ma). The student book governs, and this lesson teaches the student book's figure; the guide's 3800 Ma date is NOT imported. The student book also misprints Panthalassa as “Panthalasu” in places; the lesson teaches the corrected name.
