# Supercontinents — Rodinia, Gondwana and Pangaea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The early supercontinent in the book's story is —
a) Rodinia  b) Pangaea  c) Baltica
**Answer: a) Rodinia**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first solid rocks formed about —
a) 4600 to 3900 million years ago  b) 200 years ago  c) 70 million years ago
**Answer: a) 4600 to 3900 million years ago**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gondwana formed around —
a) the South Pole  b) the equator only  c) the North Pole
**Answer: a) the South Pole**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** By about 255 to 210 million years ago, all land stood joined as —
a) Pangaea  b) Laurasia  c) Siberia
**Answer: a) Pangaea**
