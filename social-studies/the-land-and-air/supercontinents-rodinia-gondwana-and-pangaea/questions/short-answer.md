# Supercontinents — Rodinia, Gondwana and Pangaea — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which supercontinent came first in the book's story?
**Answer:** Rodinia.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two landmasses that stood apart from Gondwana.
**Answer:** Baltica, Siberia and Laurasia (any two).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What happened to Pangaea in the Triassic?
**Answer:** It began breaking apart.
