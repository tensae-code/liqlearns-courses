# Supercontinents — Rodinia, Gondwana and Pangaea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rodinia formed before Pangaea.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gondwana formed around the North Pole.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Pangaea began breaking apart in the Triassic.
**Answer: True**
