# Hands-on — How Lakes Are Formed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How lakes are formed: glaciation, caldera and oxbow lakes; Lakes formed naturally and lakes created by people (the Zambezi dam lake). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Sudan's way
Say how South Sudan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
