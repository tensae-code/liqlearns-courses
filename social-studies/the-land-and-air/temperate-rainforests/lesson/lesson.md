## Sub lesson 2 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Geography SB): Each general topic is immediately re-taught through South Sudan — its named rivers (Sobat, Adar, Yabus, Daga), the Sudd swamplands, its oil, fishing and transport problems, and case-study research tasks set by a mock ministry brief.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 11 S3 Geography SB): A world-geography book (trade routes, megacities, Southeast Asia) anchored back to South Sudan throughout — humanitarian airlift airfields, Thuraya satellite phones, a tourism plan for South Sudan, Juba as the urbanisation example.
