# Video script — Turkey's Geographic Location

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya teaches location in two ways: first absolute location, then 2B2 Göreceli Konum — relative location, where a place is in relation to the places, seas and routes around it. Relative location is the half of location that coordinates alone cannot give.

The book then turns the idea on Turkey itself: 2C Ülkemizin Coğrafi Konumu — our country's geographic location, and its consequences. It is the Turkey case that closes the location chapter: the general ideas of absolute and relative location are read on Turkey's own ground.
