Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Relative location — Göreceli Konum

Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya teaches location in two ways: first absolute location, then 2B2 Göreceli Konum — relative location, where a place is in relation to the places, seas and routes around it. Relative location is the half of location that coordinates alone cannot give.

## Sub lesson 2 — Turkey's geographic location and its consequences — Ülkemizin Coğrafi Konumu

The book then turns the idea on Turkey itself: 2C Ülkemizin Coğrafi Konumu — our country's geographic location, and its consequences. It is the Turkey case that closes the location chapter: the general ideas of absolute and relative location are read on Turkey's own ground.

Turkey's own way: a systems course — Natural Systems first (nature–human interaction, coordinates, maps, Earth's motions, climate), then Human Systems (settlements), then Regions and Countries, then Environment and Society — with a Turkey case closing most chapters (Turkey's location, Turkey's climate, settlements in Turkey). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
