# Hands-on — High Islands and Low Islands of Micronesia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Make the comparison table
Draw a table with two columns, High islands and Low islands, and four rows: soils, water, vegetation and settlement readiness. Fill each row from the lesson.

## Judge settlement readiness
Choose one kind of island and say, from the four comparison points, how ready it is for settlement. Give your reason from the lesson.
