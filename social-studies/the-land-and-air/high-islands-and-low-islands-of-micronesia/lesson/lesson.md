## Sub lesson 1 — High islands and low islands compared

Micronesia's Grade 9 textbook compares two kinds of island in Chapter 1: high islands, which rise high out of the sea, and low islands, which sit low and flat. The chapter puts the two side by side so learners compare them directly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Soils, water, vegetation and settlement

The comparison runs through four things: soils, water, vegetation and settlement readiness — how ready each kind of island is for people to settle on it. High and low islands differ across those four things, and that difference is the chapter's lesson.
