# High Islands and Low Islands of Micronesia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Chapter 1 compares —
a) high islands and low islands  b) only rivers  c) only cities
**Answer: a) high islands and low islands**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A low island is —
a) low and flat  b) the highest mountain  c) under the sea completely
**Answer: a) low and flat**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the four comparison points? —
a) soils  b) exam marks  c) money
**Answer: a) soils**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The four comparison points are —
a) soils, water, vegetation and settlement readiness  b) only weather  c) only names
**Answer: a) soils, water, vegetation and settlement readiness**
