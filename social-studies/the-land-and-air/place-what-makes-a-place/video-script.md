# Video script — Place — What Makes a Place

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 Social Studies book builds its Geography unit (Unit 3) on the classic five themes, and gives each theme its own lesson. After location (الموقع, Lesson 10) comes place: المكان, Lesson 11, printed p. 72.

Place asks what a place is like itself — the character of the place itself — not where it sits. Where a place sits is location, the lesson before this one; what the place itself is like is place, this lesson's one thing.
