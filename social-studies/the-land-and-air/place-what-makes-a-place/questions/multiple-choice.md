# Place — What Makes a Place — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 Geography unit, place is —
a) one of the classic five themes, taught as its own lesson  b) only a map symbol  c) only a kind of weather
**Answer: a) one of the classic five themes, taught as its own lesson**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Arabic title of the place lesson is —
a) المكان  b) الموقع  c) الحركة
**Answer: a) المكان**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Place asks —
a) what a place is like itself  b) only a place's latitude  c) only who rules a place
**Answer: a) what a place is like itself**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where a place sits is the theme called —
a) location  b) place  c) movement
**Answer: a) location**
