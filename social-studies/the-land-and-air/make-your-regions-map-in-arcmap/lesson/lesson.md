# Make your region's map in ArcMap

## Do it first

Before you read, imagine the finished thing: a clean map of one region of Ethiopia, with a legend, a scale bar and a north arrow, made by you. Hold that picture — every step in this lesson is one move toward it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Start ArcMap and load Ethiopia's shapefiles

Start ArcMap 10.x. Download the Ethiopia shapefiles — the book names the Ethiopia dataset on africaopendata.org — extract them, and connect ArcMap to the folder. Add the two layers: etbound01co, the country's boundary, and etregio01co, the regions. Set the regions layer to hollow symbology so the boundaries show without filling the map.

[Picture 21952 — an ArcMap screen with Oromia highlighted in the attribute table]

## Sub lesson 2 — Select Oromia and make it its own layer

Open the attribute table of the regions layer. Select Oromia's row. Export the selection as its own layer, rename it, and untick the rest so only your region stands on the map. Selection is the heart of the practical: the whole country goes in, one region comes out.

[GIF 21979 — a region being selected in the attribute table while all other layers fade off the map]

## Sub lesson 3 — Finish in layout view

Move to layout view and finish the map as a map: legend, scale bar, north arrow, coordinate information, gridlines, and the date of compilation. A map without these is a picture; with them, it is a document another geographer can trust.
