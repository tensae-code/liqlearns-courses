# Make your region's map in ArcMap — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two shapefile layers called?
**Answer:** etbound01co (country boundary) and etregio01co (regions).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do you do after selecting Oromia's row?
**Answer:** Export the selection as its own layer, rename it, and untick the rest.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** List the finishing items of layout view.
**Answer:** Legend, scale bar, north arrow, coordinate information, gridlines and date of compilation.
