# Make your region's map in ArcMap — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two layers added first are —
a) etbound01co and etregio01co  b) rivers and roads  c) rainfall and temperature
**Answer: a) etbound01co and etregio01co**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Oromia is selected from —
a) the layout view  b) the scale bar  c) the attribute table
**Answer: c) the attribute table**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which of these belongs in layout view's finish?
a) extracting the zip file  b) legend, scale bar and north arrow  c) the download of shapefiles
**Answer: b) legend, scale bar and north arrow**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The shapefiles come from —
a) the Ethiopia dataset on africaopendata.org, as the book names it  b) a printed atlas  c) the school notice board
**Answer: a) the Ethiopia dataset on africaopendata.org, as the book names it**
