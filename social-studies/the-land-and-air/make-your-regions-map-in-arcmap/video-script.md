# Video script — Make your region's map in ArcMap

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Start ArcMap 10.x.

Open the attribute table of the regions layer.

Move to layout view and finish the map as a map: legend, scale bar, north arrow, coordinate information, gridlines, and the date of compilation.
