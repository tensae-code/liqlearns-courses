# Lesson plan — Make your region's map in ArcMap

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Make your region's map in ArcMap
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 10 Geography, Unit 8 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The book's full doing-sequence: start ArcMap, load Ethiopia's shapefiles, select one region — Oromia — from the attribute table, export it as its own layer, and finish the map in layout view.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in The Land and Air, and on the earlier-grade lessons of this title.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

2. **Select Oromia and make it its own layer**
3. **Finish in layout view**

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
