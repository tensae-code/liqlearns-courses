# Desert Wildlife of the UAE — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a desert mammal in the UAE reader?
a) the camel  b) the scorpion  c) the dung beetle
**Answer: a) the camel**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The reader's mammals group also names —
a) the gazelle and the hare  b) the frog and the fish  c) the cow and the goat
**Answer: a) the gazelle and the hare**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the small desert creatures?
a) the camel spider  b) the camel  c) the gazelle
**Answer: a) the camel spider**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The small creatures in the reader are the scorpion, the camel spider and —
a) the hare  b) the dung beetle  c) the rodent
**Answer: b) the dung beetle**
