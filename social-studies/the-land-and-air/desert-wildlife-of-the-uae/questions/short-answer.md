# Desert Wildlife of the UAE — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two groups does the desert reader sort its wildlife into?
**Answer: Desert mammals (camel, gazelle, hare, and rodents) and small desert creatures (scorpion, camel spider and dung beetle) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the reader show about the desert?
**Answer: That the desert is not empty — mammals, rodents and small creatures all live there (any close wording).**
