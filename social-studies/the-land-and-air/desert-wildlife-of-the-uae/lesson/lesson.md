# Desert Wildlife of the UAE

## Do it first

Picture the desert before you read on: sand, heat, and very little water. Now ask in your mind: what animals can live in a place like that?

## Sub lesson 1 — Desert mammals — camel, gazelle, hare and rodents

At the back of the United Arab Emirates Grade 1 book is a reader — I love to learn — about wildlife in the desert. Its first group is the desert mammals: the camel, the gazelle and the hare, and the rodents of the desert. These are the bigger animals a child can spot and name, living in the UAE's own desert.

## Sub lesson 2 — Small desert creatures — scorpion, camel spider and dung beetle

The reader's second group is smaller: the small desert creatures — the scorpion, the camel spider and the dung beetle. They are easy to miss, but they are desert wildlife too. The desert is not empty: big mammals, rodents and tiny creatures all live there, each in its own way.
