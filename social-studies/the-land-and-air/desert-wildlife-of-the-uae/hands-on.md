# Hands-on — Desert Wildlife of the UAE

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the desert wildlife
Write or draw the desert animals from this lesson on cards — camel, gazelle, hare, a rodent, scorpion, camel spider, dung beetle. Sort your cards into the reader's two groups: desert mammals, and small desert creatures. Say each name aloud as you place it.

## Spot and tick
The Grade 1 book works with tick-the-picture tasks. Make your own: draw a desert scene with the animals of this lesson hidden in it, swap with a partner, and tick each animal as your partner finds and names it.
