The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Dominican Republic's Grade 5 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Quinto Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 30), teaches: Relevant physical-natural geographic features of the American continent. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 5): Three geography units climb from the planet to the country: general geography and the American continent's location, then how climate varies across the continents — with climographs the children draw and read themselves — then the Dominican Republic's own climate, activities and population, ending with a “discovering the geography of my country” field activity.
