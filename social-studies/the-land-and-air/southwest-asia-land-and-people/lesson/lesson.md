## Sub lesson 1 — Southwest Asia Land And People

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11* (printed pp. 67–73), teaches: Bài 15: Southwest Asia — position (some 20 countries at the junction of three continents; Suez) and nature (the Caucasus, Hindu Kush and Iranian and Arabian plateaus, the Mesopotamian plain, the Rub' al Khali and Syrian deserts, the Tigris and Euphrates, the Dead Sea — and over half the world's oil and about 40% of its gas), population (402.5 million; Arabs over half; 72% urban) and society (homeland of Judaism, Christianity and Islam; Petra, Shibam and Samarkand; oil-fuelled conflicts). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11): after Grade 10's world systems, Grade 11 walks the world region by region — Latin America, the EU, Southeast Asia and ASEAN, Southwest Asia, the USA, Russia, Japan, China, Australia and South Africa — always with the same lens (position, nature, people, economy) and always measuring Vietnam's own place among them (its HDI row in the opening table, EU–Vietnam trade, Vietnam inside ASEAN, Japan's FDI into Vietnam). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
