**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Egypt, Yemen, Libya).**

Egypt's own way (Grade 6 Term 1): primary school ends by widening from Egypt to the Arab homeland — the 22 Arab states in their Asian (12) and African (10) wings, their shared location, relief, climate and environmental challenges, then the region's economic development (agriculture with its natural and human problems and state solutions such as desalination, land reclamation, dams and modern irrigation; Egypt's New Delta and Toshka projects as worked examples).

Searchable tags: the arab world location and importance, Egypt, social studies, the-land-and-air

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 6 G6 Geography of the Arab World): Grade 6 widens from Yemen to the whole Arab world, taught region by region (Arabian Peninsula, Fertile Crescent, Horn of Africa and Comoros, Nile Valley, Arab Maghreb) with a fixed three-lesson pattern per region: location and borders, natural features, population and economic activity.

Yemen's own way (Yemen Grade 11 G11 Geography of Yemen and the Arab World): Grade 11 does Yemen in depth first (physical base, population and social development, economic resources), then the Arab world's physical geography, its people and resources, and finally its contemporary problems — water, food, poverty, illiteracy, integration.

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 8 LY G8 Arab World): The scale widens from Libya (Grade 7) to the whole Arab homeland: one regional geography — location and strategic importance, relief, climate, vegetation, population, economic resources and transport — closed by a full unit on the Arab food problem and food security.

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World): The Grade 8 Arab World survey returns at secondary depth in two parts — physical (relief treated separately for the African and Asian sides, climate factors and regions, soils, vegetation, water with its problems) and human (population structure and problems, agriculture and its difficulties, crop-by-crop chapters, livestock and sea wealth, minerals metal by metal, oil and gas, alternative energy, industry, transport).
