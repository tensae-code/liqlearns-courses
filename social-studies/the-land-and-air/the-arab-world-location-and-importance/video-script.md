Egypt's book teaches: Unit 1 Lesson 1: Our Arab homeland — its geographic and astronomical location, its 22 states in Asian and African wings, its borders, and its civilisational, economic and strategic importance (Social Studies, Grade 6, Term 1, Grade 6, printed p. 8).
