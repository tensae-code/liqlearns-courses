# Video script — The Pacific — a Sea of Islands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The reader opens the Pacific itself: not empty water between separate islands, but a sea of islands. Pacific voyagers crossed it guided by the stars, and the old community of Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue and Tuvalu exchanged goods and knowledge across it with no borders — the ocean connected people; it did not divide them.

This part stands on the teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. Its notes for use of Book 1 teach Pacific people's connectedness with the ocean, and set a reflection point on real coral-reef degradation at Funafuti, Tuvalu — the connected sea can be damaged, and the damage is studied where it is happening.
