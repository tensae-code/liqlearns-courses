Nauru's own way: Nauru uses the shared Pacific ESD reader suite (local advisors Fay Itaia & Sharon Kam for Nauru) — Geography is taught as ocean stewardship: the Pacific as a connected "sea of islands", its animals, and the traditional fishing knowledge of Tonga, Tuvalu and Niue, ending in a plastic-waste action plan. And the guide's way (teacher guide — evidence): the guide teaches the readers through Pacific philosophy (head, heart and hands), reflection points on real reef degradation (Funafuti, Tuvalu), elder interviews, and a take-action project cycle, with formal-curriculum links country by country. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
