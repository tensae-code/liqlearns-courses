## Sub lesson 1 — Land heats fast, water slowly

Continentality: land heats and cools quickly, water stores heat and releases it slowly. So interiors swing wider between summer and winter than coasts do — Asia, the greatest landmass, is the extreme case.

## Sub lesson 2 — Circulation — the great balancing

The atmosphere's circulation balances energy between latitudes, moving air from high pressure to low. The subtropical high-pressure belts and the ITCZ migrate with the seasons, carrying their rain and dryness with them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Forces that bend the wind

Three forces deflect or slow moving air: the Coriolis effect, centrifugal acceleration, and surface friction where wind drags over land and sea. Together they decide the paths the winds actually take.
