# Coasts, winds and pressure belts — the other climate controls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Water heats and cools —
a) slowly, storing heat  b) faster than land  c) not at all
**Answer: a) slowly, storing heat**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The extreme case of continentality is —
a) Asia  b) Iceland  c) the Congo
**Answer: a) Asia**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Circulation moves air from —
a) high pressure to low pressure  b) low to high only  c) pole to pole only
**Answer: a) high pressure to low pressure**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Winds are deflected or slowed by Coriolis, centrifugal acceleration and —
a) surface friction  b) gravity alone  c) magnetism
**Answer: a) surface friction**
