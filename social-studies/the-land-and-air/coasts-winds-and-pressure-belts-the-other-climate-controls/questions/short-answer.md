# Coasts, winds and pressure belts — the other climate controls — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is continentality?
**Answer:** Land heating and cooling fast, water slowly, so interiors swing wider than coasts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which belts migrate with the seasons?
**Answer:** The subtropical high-pressure belts and the ITCZ.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three forces on winds.
**Answer:** Coriolis effect, centrifugal acceleration, surface friction.
