Continentality: land heats and cools quickly, water stores heat and releases it slowly.

The atmosphere's circulation balances energy between latitudes, moving air from high pressure to low.

Three forces deflect or slow moving air: the Coriolis effect, centrifugal acceleration, and surface friction where wind drags over land and sea.
