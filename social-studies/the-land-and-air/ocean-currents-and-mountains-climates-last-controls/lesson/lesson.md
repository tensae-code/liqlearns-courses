# Ocean currents and mountains — climate's last controls

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Currents carry heat and cold

Ocean surface currents follow the semi-permanent pressure winds. They carry warmth toward cold coasts and cold toward warm ones, so two coasts at one latitude can live in different climates.

## Sub lesson 2 — Height — the lapse rate

Air cools as you climb, at the normal lapse rate of 6.4 degrees per kilometre. Work the book's sum for Ras Dejen: from 30 degrees at sea level, a climb of 4,620 metres subtracts about 29.6 degrees — leaving about 0.4 degrees at the summit.

## Sub lesson 3 — Slope and the rain shadow in brief

Which way a slope faces — its aspect — changes its sun and rain, and mountains force moist air up their windward side to rain, the first half of the rain-shadow story the courses before this one told.
