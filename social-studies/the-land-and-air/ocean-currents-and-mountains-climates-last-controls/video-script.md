# Video script — Ocean currents and mountains — climate's last controls

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ocean surface currents follow the semi-permanent pressure winds.

Air cools as you climb, at the normal lapse rate of 6.4 degrees per kilometre.

Which way a slope faces — its aspect — changes its sun and rain, and mountains force moist air up their windward side to rain, the first half of the rain-shadow story the courses before this one told..
