# Ocean currents and mountains — climate's last controls — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ocean surface currents follow —
a) the semi-permanent pressure winds  b) rivers  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the semi-permanent pressure winds**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A warm current along a cold coast makes its climate —
a) warmer  b) colder  c) drier always
**Answer: a) warmer**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The normal lapse rate is —
a) 6.4 °C per kilometre  b) 64 °C per kilometre  c) 0.64 °C per kilometre
**Answer: a) 6.4 °C per kilometre**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ras Dejen's summit temperature in the book's sum is about —
a) 0.4 °C  b) 29.6 °C  c) 30 °C
**Answer: a) 0.4 °C**
