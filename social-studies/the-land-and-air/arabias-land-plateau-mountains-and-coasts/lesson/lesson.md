## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 7 Social Studies): at intermediate level Social Studies becomes the history of ancient civilisations, and every civilisation is opened through its land — relief, climate, soil and rivers are taught as the stage on which each civilisation arose, starting with Iraq's own four relief regions.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 8 Social Studies): the intermediate sequence widens ring by ring — after Iraq itself (G5–G6) and the ancient world (G7), Grade 8 gives the whole Arab world as one geographical unit (its position between two continents, relief, climates, waters, people and economy), worked constantly off outline maps of the Arab world; the history of the Arab world follows as Part 2. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
