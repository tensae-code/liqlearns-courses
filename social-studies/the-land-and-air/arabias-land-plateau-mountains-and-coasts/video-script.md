The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 7 Social Studies* (printed p. 70), teaches: Ch. 4 Arabian Peninsula, geographical setting: Arabia's relief and climate — the great plateau, the Hijaz and Asir mountains, the Tihama and al-Ahsa plains and the south-eastern heights, under a mostly hot dry climate. Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 8 Social Studies* (printed p. 89), teaches: Part 2 Ch. 4 (History chapter, Geography part): Mecca — its location and its climate between Mediterranean and monsoon influences.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 7 Social Studies): at intermediate level Social Studies becomes the history of ancient civilisations, and every civilisation is opened through its land — relief, climate, soil and rivers are taught as the stage on which each civilisation arose, starting with Iraq's own four relief regions.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 8 Social Studies): the intermediate sequence widens ring by ring — after Iraq itself (G5–G6) and the ancient world (G7), Grade 8 gives the whole Arab world as one geographical unit (its position between two continents, relief, climates, waters, people and economy), worked constantly off outline maps of the Arab world; the history of the Arab world follows as Part 2.
