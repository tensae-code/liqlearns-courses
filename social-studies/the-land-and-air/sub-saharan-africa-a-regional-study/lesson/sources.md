**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Cuba).**

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 11 Geografía Regional G11): The capstone of Cuban school geography: five continents studied with one fixed plan (situation → physical character → socio-economic character → regions → political-map changes → countries-and-capitals → deep country monographs), then a full closing chapter on the Cuban archipelago longer than any continent chapter — the world tour exists to bring the learner home with the tools to characterise their own country.

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grades 10-11 Cuaderno de mapas): The senior-cycle companion is pure map work — a full outline-map atlas the student fills in: world and continents first, then the "significant regions" exactly as the G11 Regional course carves them (Caribe, América Latina, América Anglosajona, África Subsahariana, Europa Occidental, Medio Oriente, Este y sureste de Asia), then one outline map per studied country, and it lands back home with the Cuban archipelago, the provinces and a street-level La Habana. It closes with a "Datos interesantes" page of world records.
