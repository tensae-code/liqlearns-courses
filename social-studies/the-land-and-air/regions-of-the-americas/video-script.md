Honduras's book, the *Honduras Grade 8 Student Book* (printed p. 94), teaches: Lección 15: Regiones de América — regions of America. Honduras's book, the *Honduras Grade 8 Teacher Guide* (printed p. 63), teaches: Lección 15: Regiones de América — regions of America. (teacher guide — evidence)

That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Honduras is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.
