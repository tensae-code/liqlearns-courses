# What controls Africa's climate — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Latitude sets the strength of the sun a place receives.
**Answer: True** — first control of the lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The ITCZ stays fixed over the equator all year.
**Answer: False** — it moves north and south with the seasons.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Warm ocean currents bring milder, moister air to coasts.
**Answer: True** — the counterpart of cold currents like the Benguela.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A town 1,000 m up is about 6.4 °C cooler than the lowland, other things equal.
**Answer: True** — the lapse rate applied.
