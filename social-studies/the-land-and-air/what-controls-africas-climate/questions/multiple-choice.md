# What controls Africa's climate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A place's rainy season is largely —
a) the sea visiting  b) the ITCZ visiting as it moves with the seasons  c) the mountains growing  d) the wind stopping
**Answer: b) the ITCZ visiting as it moves with the seasons** — the rain belt drags the rains after it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsiding dry air at the tropics helps —
a) make rainforests  b) hold the great deserts in place  c) cool the highlands  d) fill the lakes
**Answer: b) hold the great deserts in place** — part of the pressure/wind-belt control.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Benguela current is —
a) warm and wetting  b) cold, cooling and drying its coast  c) a river  d) an inland wind
**Answer: b) cold, cooling and drying its coast** — it helps hold the Namib dry.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Continentality means deep-interior land —
a) stays mild and moist  b) heats and cools fast and dries out  c) never changes  d) is always coastal
**Answer: b) heats and cools fast and dries out** — against the sea's softening maritime influence.

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The lapse rate is —
a) 6.4 °C lost per 1,000 m climbed  b) 64 °C per metre  c) no change with height  d) 6.4 °C gained per 1,000 m
**Answer: a) 6.4 °C lost per 1,000 m climbed** — the altitude rule of the lesson.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Snow sits on Kilimanjaro although it stands on the equator because —
a) the equator is cold  b) its summit climbs into permanent cold by altitude  c) ocean currents freeze it  d) the ITCZ snows there
**Answer: b) its summit climbs into permanent cold by altitude** — altitude defeats latitude.
