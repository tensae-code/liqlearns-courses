# What controls Africa's climate — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six controls.
**Answer:** Latitude, the ITCZ's seasonal movement, continentality vs maritime influence, ocean currents, pressure and wind belts, and altitude.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** A lowland is 28 °C. About how warm is a highland town 2,000 m above it?
**Answer:** About 28 − (2 × 6.4) = about 15 °C (roughly 15.2 °C).
