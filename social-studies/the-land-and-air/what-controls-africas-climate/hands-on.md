# Hands-on — What controls Africa's climate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lapse-rate ladder
Draw a mountain side. Mark 0 m, 1,000 m, 2,000 m, 3,000 m up its slope and write the temperature at each level if the foot is 30 °C, dropping 6.4 °C per level. Put a snow cap where it falls below zero.

## Controls checklist field walk
Take the six controls as a checklist on a walk near home: for each, write one thing you can see that it explains — a cool hilltop, a dry inland wind, a rainy-season sky.
