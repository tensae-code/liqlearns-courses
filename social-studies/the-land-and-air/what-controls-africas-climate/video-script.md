# Video script — What controls Africa's climate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Six things control Africa's climate.
Latitude. The moving ITCZ. The wind belts.
The land against the sea. Cold and warm currents.
And height — climb a thousand metres, lose 6.4 degrees.
That is why highlands are cool — and Kilimanjaro wears snow on the equator.
