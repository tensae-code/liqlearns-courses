# Earthquake belts and Benioff zones

## Sub lesson 1 — The belts

Seismic energy concentrates in belts. The greatest is the Circum-Pacific belt — the 'Ring of Fire' — releasing about 80% of the world's seismic energy. Next comes the Mediterranean–Asian belt, with about 15%.

The remaining belts run along mid-ocean ridges and along the Great Rift Valley of East Africa — the same lines the plates draw. Where the plates meet, the energy lets go.

## Sub lesson 2 — Depths and Benioff zones

Focus depth runs from near the surface to about 700 km. Shallow foci — under about 60 km — release over 75% of the energy; intermediate foci (60–300 km) release about 12%, and deep foci about 3%.

The deep ones are the tell-tale: they lie in Benioff zones that dip down at an angle, tracing the subducting plate as it dives. The pattern of earthquake depths is a picture of the plate itself, sinking.
