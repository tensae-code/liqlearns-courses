# Earthquake belts and Benioff zones — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three belt families.
**Answer:** The Circum-Pacific Ring of Fire, the Mediterranean–Asian belt, and belts along mid-ocean ridges and the Great Rift Valley of East Africa.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What share of energy do intermediate foci (60–300 km) release?
**Answer:** About 12%.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What does a Benioff zone show?
**Answer:** The path of the subducting plate, traced by foci dipping deeper with distance.
