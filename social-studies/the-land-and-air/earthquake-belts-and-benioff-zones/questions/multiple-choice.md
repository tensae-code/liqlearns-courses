# Earthquake belts and Benioff zones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ring of Fire releases about…
a) 15% of seismic energy  b) 50%  c) 80%
**Answer: c) 80%**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mediterranean–Asian belt releases about…
a) 15%  b) 80%  c) 3%
**Answer: a) 15%**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Belts also run along…
a) mid-ocean ridges and the Great Rift Valley  b) every coastline equally  c) the equator only
**Answer: a) mid-ocean ridges and the Great Rift Valley**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shallow foci are under about…
a) 6 km  b) 60 km  c) 600 km
**Answer: b) 60 km**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Shallow foci release over…
a) 75% of the energy  b) 12%  c) 3%
**Answer: a) 75% of the energy**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Benioff zones are…
a) surface cracks  b) dipping zones of deep foci tracing the subducting plate  c) ocean currents
**Answer: b) dipping zones of deep foci tracing the subducting plate**
