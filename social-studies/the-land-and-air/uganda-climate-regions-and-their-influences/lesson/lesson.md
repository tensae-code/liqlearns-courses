## Sub lesson 2 — Uganda's way — the book's own teaching way

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 5 UG P5 Merged Home-Study SST): The whole SST course is Uganda itself: locating Uganda and its districts/neighbours, Uganda's physical features, climate and vegetation, then Uganda's population. Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Mathematics section (PDF pp. 9–220); English section (PDF pp. 221–274); SST Term Two — The People of the Pre-Colonial Period, ethnic groups, explorers, colonial rule, Legislative Council and the independence struggle (History, SST printed pp. 36–54); SST Term Three — national symbols, democracy, The Government of Uganda, duties of citizens (Civics, SST printed pp. 56–70).

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 5 UG Excel P5 SST): A full-year commercial companion to the NCDC syllabus, far more detailed than the home-study booklet: Uganda's districts/regions are listed sub-region by sub-region, and each Uganda topic (physical features, climate, vegetation, resources, population) gets its own full treatment. Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Topic 6 The People of Pre-colonial Uganda — origins, ethnic groups, political/social/economic organisation (History, printed pp. 89–117, except its migration and settlement sections, placed below); Topic 7 Foreign Influence — traders, explorers, missionaries, colonial administrators (History, pp. 118–142); Topic 8 How Uganda Became a Nation (History, pp. 143–148); Topic 9 The Road to Independence (History, pp. 149–158); Topic 10 Uganda as an Independent Nation — presidents, national symbols, democracy, elections (Civics, pp. 159–173); Topic 11 The Government of Uganda — levels, organs, constitution, revenue, budgeting, citizens' rights (Civics, pp. 174–193). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
