# Hands-on — Life In Pakistans Physical Regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Physical regions and lifestyle of the people: mountain life (handicrafts, farming, tourism and hotels) vs plain life (agriculture); the Potwar Plateau — rainfed farming, broken eroded land, the world's second largest salt mine, coal, gypsum, oil and gas. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
