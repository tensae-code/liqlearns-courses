The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela* (printed p. 152), teaches: Venezuela es un álbum de relieves: Costa-Montaña region, Sistema Coriano, Cordillera de la Costa, the Llanos and delta plain, ancient Guayana.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.
