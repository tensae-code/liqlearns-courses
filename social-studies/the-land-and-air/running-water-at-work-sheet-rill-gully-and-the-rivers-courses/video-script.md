# Video script — Running water at work — sheet, rill, gully and the river's courses

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Running water is the most powerful outside agent on land — washing in sheets, cutting rills, then gullies — and every river changes its work from its swift upper course to its slow lower one. First, sheet, rill, gully: Running water is the most powerful external agent shaping the land. Then, the river's three courses: Along its course, a river changes character. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
