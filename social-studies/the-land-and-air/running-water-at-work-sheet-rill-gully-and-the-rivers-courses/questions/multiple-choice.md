# Running water at work — sheet, rill, gully and the river's courses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Running water's force rises with its…
a) temperature  b) speed  c) colour
**Answer: b) speed**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Vegetation cover makes erosion…
a) stronger  b) weaker  c) impossible everywhere
**Answer: b) weaker**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Sheet erosion is…
a) deep flood cuts  b) even washing, easily missed  c) small channels
**Answer: b) even washing, easily missed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The deepest runoff erosion is…
a) sheet  b) rill  c) gully
**Answer: c) gully**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Tributaries join the main river at…
a) deltas  b) confluences  c) watersheds
**Answer: b) confluences**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The lower course mainly…
a) cuts down  b) deposits its load  c) flows fastest
**Answer: b) deposits its load**
