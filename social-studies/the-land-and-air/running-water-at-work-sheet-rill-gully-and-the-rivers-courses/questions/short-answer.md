# Running water at work — sheet, rill, gully and the river's courses — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put sheet, rill and gully in order of depth.
**Answer:** Sheet (even wash), then rill (small channels), then gully (deep cuts).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the river do in each of its three courses?
**Answer:** Upper: cuts down. Middle: widens its valley as tributaries join. Lower: deposits its load.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things set running water's force?
**Answer:** Its speed (force rises with speed) and vegetation cover (force falls where cover holds the soil).
