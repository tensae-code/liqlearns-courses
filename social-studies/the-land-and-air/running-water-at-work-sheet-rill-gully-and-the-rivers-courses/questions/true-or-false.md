# Running water at work — sheet, rill, gully and the river's courses — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gully erosion is more destructive than sheet erosion.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The upper course is where a river deposits most of its load.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A river widens its valley in its middle course.
**Answer: True**
