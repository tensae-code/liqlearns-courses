# Reading map symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Conventional signs are —
a) invented per map  b) local secrets  c) agreed internationally
**Answer: c) agreed internationally**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** They represent —
a) only towns  b) physical and human features  c) only rivers
**Answer: b) physical and human features**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Symbols must be —
a) uniform, simple and keyed  b) complex  c) different for each feature occurrence
**Answer: a) uniform, simple and keyed**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The reader checks first —
a) the largest symbol  b) the title only  c) the legend
**Answer: c) the legend**
