# Reading map symbols

## Do it first

A blue line is a river to every reader on earth who knows the signs. That agreement is a language. Read the conventional signs.

## Sub lesson 1 — Conventional signs

Standard conventional signs or symbols, agreed internationally, represent physical and human features on maps: rivers, roads, churches, forests, contours and the rest of the symbol sheet. Because they are agreed, a map printed anywhere can be read anywhere.

## Sub lesson 2 — Using symbols well

Symbol choice must be uniform — one feature, one sign throughout the map; simple — readable at map size; and keyed in the legend, which the reader checks first before reading the map itself. The legend is the map’s dictionary; symbols without it are guesses.
