# Video script — Why vegetation changes with the land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Plants obey three masters: height, rain, and soil.
Climb, and the belts change — forest, moorland, bare top.
Descend to the dry lowlands — thorn scrub, then desert.
Read the land, and you can read its plants.
