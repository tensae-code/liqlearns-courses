# Why vegetation changes with the land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vegetation varies with —
a) latitude only  b) river names  c) altitude, rainfall and soils
**Answer: c) altitude, rainfall and soils**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Temperature falls as —
a) soil deepens  b) you climb  c) rain increases
**Answer: b) you climb**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The topmost belt is —
a) afro-alpine  b) desert  c) rainforest
**Answer: a) afro-alpine**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The lowest, driest lands carry —
a) montane forest  b) afro-alpine plants  c) desert vegetation
**Answer: c) desert vegetation**
