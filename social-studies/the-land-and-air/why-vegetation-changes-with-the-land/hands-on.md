# Hands-on — Why vegetation changes with the land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Transect walk
On a long strip of paper draw a mountain-to-desert transect of Ethiopia and draw the plant belt that belongs at each height. Label the control that changes most at each boundary.

## Predict and check
Choose three Ethiopian places at very different heights. Predict each one’s vegetation from the controls, then check against the vegetation lessons that follow.
