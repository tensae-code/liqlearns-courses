# Hands-on — The Emirates, My Country — Location and the Emirates Map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw my country's map
Take a blank page and draw the outline map of the United Arab Emirates as the Grade 1 book shows it. Mark the Arabian Gulf beside it and write the country's name across it. Keep the page; it is your own location map.

## Colour the seven emirates
On your map, give each of the seven emirates its own colour, the way the Grade 1 book works with colouring tasks. Name Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah on your map, and count the emirates aloud as you colour: seven in all.
