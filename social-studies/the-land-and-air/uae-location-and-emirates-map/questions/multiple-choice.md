# The Emirates, My Country — Location and the Emirates Map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the Grade 1 map, the sea beside the UAE is —
a) the Arabian Gulf  b) the Red Sea  c) the Mediterranean Sea
**Answer: a) the Arabian Gulf**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Grade 1 children in the UAE first meet their country —
a) in a list of numbers  b) on its own map  c) only in stories
**Answer: b) on its own map**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many emirates make up the United Arab Emirates?
a) three  b) five  c) seven
**Answer: c) seven**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the emirates named in the Grade 1 book?
a) Abu Dhabi  b) Cairo  c) Muscat
**Answer: a) Abu Dhabi**
