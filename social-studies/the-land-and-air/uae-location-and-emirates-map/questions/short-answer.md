# The Emirates, My Country — Location and the Emirates Map — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the Grade 1 book teach the location of the UAE?
**Answer: It shows the country on its own map, with the Arabian Gulf beside it — the child sees the whole country and the sea next to it in one picture (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the map show the country is made of?
**Answer: Seven emirates joined in one country, Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah among them (any close wording).**
