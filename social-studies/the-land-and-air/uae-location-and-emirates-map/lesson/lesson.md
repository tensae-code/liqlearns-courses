# The Emirates, My Country — Location and the Emirates Map

## Do it first

Look at a map of the United Arab Emirates before you read on. Find the sea beside it, and say in your mind: this is where my country is.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where my country is — the UAE on its map

In the United Arab Emirates, Grade 1 children meet their country first on its own map. Unit 6, Lesson 1 — The Emirates, my country — starts with the location of my country: the UAE shown on its map, with the Arabian Gulf beside it. Location here is not numbers: it is seeing your whole country in one picture, and the sea that lies next to it.

## Sub lesson 2 — The seven emirates

The same map shows what the country is made of: seven emirates joined in one country. The Grade 1 book names Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah among them — seven emirates in all, each with its place on the map, together making the United Arab Emirates. When you look at the map, you are looking at all seven at once.
