United Arab Emirates' own way: Geography at Grade 1 is homeland-first and picture-led: my environment and its pollution, my country's location on its own map with its landmarks, a traveller-hero (Ibn Battuta) traced on a world map, and the desert's own plants and animals — every lesson built from photos, colouring and tick-the-picture tasks. This lesson keeps that way: the country is met on its own map first, the Gulf beside it, then the seven emirates it is made of. Only the emirates the placement names (Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah) are named here; the placement gives the count — seven — without listing all seven. The lesson teaches the placement's content in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
