Egypt's own way (Grade 8 Term 1): the Grade 7 Africa pattern repeated for the northern landmass — Asia and Europe taught together as “Eurasia” (one map, the straits that join and part them, each continent's strategic/economic/civilisational importance), then the same ecosystems sequence as Grade 7 (forests — here Mediterranean, tropical, monsoon, deciduous and coniferous systems each profiled by location, climate, plants, animals, soil — grasslands/deserts, aquatic), with a reforestation-and-sustainable-development reading.
