Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 Social Studies book teaches the Arabian Peninsula in Unit 7, Lesson 25: شبه الجزيرة العربية: الموقع والحضارة — the Arabian Peninsula: location and civilisation — printed p. 162. This lesson takes the location half only; the civilisation half is History.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading a peninsula's location

A peninsula is land with water around most of it, joined to bigger land on one side. To teach a peninsula's location is to say where that land sits and what lies around it — the work this lesson does for the Arabian Peninsula, in the Saudi book's own Unit 7 order, before its population lesson (Lesson 26).
