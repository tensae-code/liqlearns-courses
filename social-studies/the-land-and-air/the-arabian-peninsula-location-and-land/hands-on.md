# Hands-on — The Arabian Peninsula — Location and Land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the peninsula
On any map of the region, find the Arabian Peninsula and point to where it sits; write that Lesson 25 teaches its location first.

## Say what a peninsula is
Write in your own words what a peninsula is: land with water around most of it, joined to bigger land on one side.
