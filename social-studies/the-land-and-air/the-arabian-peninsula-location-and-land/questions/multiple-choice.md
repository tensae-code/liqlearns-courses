# The Arabian Peninsula — Location and Land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 Lesson 25 teaches the Arabian Peninsula's —
a) location (and, in its other half, its civilisation — History)  b) only its kings  c) only its weather today
**Answer: a) location (and, in its other half, its civilisation — History)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A peninsula is land —
a) with water around most of it, joined to bigger land on one side  b) completely under water  c) with no land at all
**Answer: a) with water around most of it, joined to bigger land on one side**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Geography half of Lesson 25 taken in this lesson is —
a) the location half  b) the civilisation half  c) neither half
**Answer: a) the location half**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson after the Arabian Peninsula location lesson (Lesson 26) teaches the peninsula's —
a) population and their conditions  b) moon phases  c) maps only
**Answer: a) population and their conditions**
