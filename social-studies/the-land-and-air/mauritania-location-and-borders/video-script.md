# Video script — Mauritania Location And Borders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mauritania's book, the *Mauritania Grade 5 geo-5af-m* (printed p. 7), teaches: Axis 1 File 1: location and borders (natural vs political borders).

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 5 geo-5af-m): The whole book is Mauritania itself, in four "I get to know" axes: my country's location and borders, my country's climate, my society, my country's wealth. Climate is taught through the country's own three wind types and the effect of rain on where people, animals and plants live; farming includes the flood-recession farming of the Chamama plain after the Senegal River falls.
