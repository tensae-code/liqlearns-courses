## Sub lesson 1 — Mauritania Location And Borders

Mauritania's book, the *Mauritania Grade 5 geo-5af-m* (printed p. 7), teaches: Axis 1 File 1: location and borders (natural vs political borders). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mauritania's way — the book's own teaching way

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 5 geo-5af-m): The whole book is Mauritania itself, in four "I get to know" axes: my country's location and borders, my country's climate, my society, my country's wealth. Climate is taught through the country's own three wind types and the effect of rain on where people, animals and plants live; farming includes the flood-recession farming of the Chamama plain after the Senegal River falls. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
