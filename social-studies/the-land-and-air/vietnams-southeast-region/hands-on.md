# Hands-on — Vietnams Southeast Region

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 30: Socio-economic development in the Southeast (Ho Chi Minh City, Dong Nai and Tay Ninh; some 21 million people, 62.8% urban — the most urbanised region) — its strengths (basalt and grey soils; the Sai Gon, Dong Nai and Vam Co rivers; offshore oil and gas giving about 83% of the nation's crude and over 73% of its gas in 2024), its leading industry (34.9% of national output value; the Phu My power complex over 4,000 MW), its services (28.9% of national retail; 33.3% of exports), its farming (59.1% of the nation's rubber, 61.9% of its cashew) and its marine economy around Ba Ria–Vung Tau and Con Dao, with environment protection. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
