# Video script — Europe Its Position And Physical Geography

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 8 Geography (Dari)* (printed p. 3), teaches: Ch. 1 Lessons 1–2: getting to know the continent of Europe, its position and boundaries; physical geography and the mountains of Europe. Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)* (printed p. 73), teaches: Lesson 37: the continent of Europe (same NEW lesson as the Grade 8 book's Europe placement).

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 8 Geography (Dari)): the continent-by-continent march continues — after Asia in Grade 7, Grade 8 does Europe then the Americas, each continent in the same fixed order: position, mountains, rivers, lakes, seas, gulfs, climate, political geography, human geography (race, language, religion), economic geography, mines/industries, transport.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)): primary social studies starts from the child's own world — family, village, city — and only then opens the map: directions, Afghanistan's boundaries and mountains and rivers, the continents and oceans, and finally the Earth's shape, equator, latitude/longitude and movements.
