# Hands-on — Morocco's Climate — Temperature and Rainfall

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read today's bulletin
Find or imagine today's weather bulletin for your place and describe the day's weather from it, the way the guide's lesson practises — weather is the day, not the climate.

## Chart the two measures
Draw two simple charts for a place you know: one for temperature, one for rainfall. Morocco's lesson represents its climate through exactly these two measures.
