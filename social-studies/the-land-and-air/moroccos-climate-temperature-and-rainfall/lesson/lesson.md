## Sub lesson 1 — Climate and weather — the day's bulletin

The fourth Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Features of my country's climate — temperature and rainfall and representing them. It begins by separating two ideas: climate and weather are not the same thing. The learner practises on the day itself, describing the day's weather from a bulletin.

## Sub lesson 2 — Charting temperature and rainfall

Climate is known through its measures, and the lesson's two measures are temperature and rainfall. The learner represents them — charting temperature and rainfall so the climate of the homeland can be seen, not only said.
