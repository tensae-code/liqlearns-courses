# Morocco's Climate — Temperature and Rainfall — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's Geography Lesson 4 teaches the climate through —
a) temperature and rainfall  b) imports and exports  c) kings and dates
**Answer: a) temperature and rainfall**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson separates climate from —
a) weather  b) geography  c) history
**Answer: a) weather**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The day's weather is described from —
a) a bulletin  b) a story book  c) a map key
**Answer: a) a bulletin**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Temperature and rainfall are represented by —
a) charting them  b) hiding them  c) naming them once
**Answer: a) charting them**
