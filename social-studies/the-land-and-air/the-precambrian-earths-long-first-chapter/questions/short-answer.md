# The Precambrian — Earth's long first chapter — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About what share of Earth's time is Precambrian?
**Answer:** About 85 percent.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What are the surviving Precambrian rock cores called?
**Answer:** Shields and cratons.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three Precambrian eons in order.
**Answer:** Hadean, Archean, Proterozoic.
