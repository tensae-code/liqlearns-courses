# The Precambrian — Earth's long first chapter — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Precambrian covers about —
a) 85% of Earth's time  b) 8% of Earth's time  c) half of Earth's time
**Answer: a) 85% of Earth's time**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Precambrian ends about —
a) 600 million years ago  b) 70 million years ago  c) last year
**Answer: a) 600 million years ago**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Precambrian basement rocks are rich in —
a) base metals  b) plastic  c) coal only
**Answer: a) base metals**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The three Precambrian eons in order are —
a) Hadean, Archean, Proterozoic  b) Proterozoic, Hadean, Archean  c) Archean, Proterozoic, Hadean
**Answer: a) Hadean, Archean, Proterozoic**
