# The Precambrian — Earth's long first chapter — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Precambrian is the longest part of Earth's story.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Precambrian rocks survive as shields and cratons.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Precambrian covers only a small share of Earth's time.
**Answer: False**
