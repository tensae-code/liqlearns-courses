# The Precambrian — Earth's long first chapter

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — The longest chapter

The Precambrian runs from Earth's formation to about 600 million years ago — roughly 85 percent of all Earth's time. Most of the planet's story happened in this one long chapter.

## Sub lesson 2 — A planet solidifies

In the Precambrian the Earth solidified. The oldest crystalline basement rocks formed, rich in base metals, and they survive today as the shields and cratons at the cores of continents.

## Sub lesson 3 — Its three eons

The Precambrian holds three eons: Hadean, Archean and Proterozoic. Learn them in order — they are the staircase up to the eras of visible life.
