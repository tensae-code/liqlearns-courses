The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The Precambrian runs from Earth's formation to about 600 million years ago — roughly 85 percent of all Earth's time.

In the Precambrian the Earth solidified.

The Precambrian holds three eons: Hadean, Archean and Proterozoic.
