# Ethiopia’s five climate zones — Bereha to Wurch — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five zones in climbing order.
**Answer: Bereha, Kolla, Woina-Dega, Dega, Wurch.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the zone system called a staircase?
**Answer: The zones climb in order with altitude, like steps.**
