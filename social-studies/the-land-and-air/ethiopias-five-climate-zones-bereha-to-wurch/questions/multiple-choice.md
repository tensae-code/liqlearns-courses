# Ethiopia’s five climate zones — Bereha to Wurch — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The hot arid lowland zone is —
a) Kolla  b) Dega  c) Bereha
**Answer: c) Bereha**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The cold afro-alpine top zone is —
a) Kolla  b) Wurch  c) Woina-Dega
**Answer: b) Wurch**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The cool highland zone is —
a) Dega  b) Bereha  c) Kolla
**Answer: a) Dega**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A place’s zone predicts —
a) its exact rainfall in millimetres  b) its population  c) its farming — crops and settlement
**Answer: c) its farming — crops and settlement**
