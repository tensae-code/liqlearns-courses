# Video script — Ethiopia’s five climate zones — Bereha to Wurch

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Climb the staircase of climate.
Bereha — hot and arid. Kolla — warm. Woina-Dega — cool and temperate.
Dega — the cool highland. Wurch — the cold top.
Five zones. Each with its own crops, its own life.
