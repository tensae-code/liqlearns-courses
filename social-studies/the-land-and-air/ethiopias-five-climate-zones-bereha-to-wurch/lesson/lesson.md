# Ethiopia’s five climate zones — Bereha to Wurch

## Do it first

Walk up a great Ethiopian mountain in your mind: hot plain, warm hills, cool fields, cold heights, icy top. Five zones. Read their names in order.

## Sub lesson 1 — The staircase

Ethiopia’s traditional agro-climatic zones climb like a staircase. Bereha is the hot arid lowland. Kolla is the warm semi-arid zone above it. Woina-Dega is the cool temperate highland fringe. Dega is the cool highland. Wurch is the cold afro-alpine top, above the farming limit.

## Sub lesson 2 — Zones at work

Each zone carries its own crops and its own pattern of settlement: the cereals and coffee of the middle zones, the hardy crops of Dega, grazing and special plants in Wurch. To name a place’s zone is to predict its farming before you ever visit.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the five zones in climbing order, with the temperature word of each.
