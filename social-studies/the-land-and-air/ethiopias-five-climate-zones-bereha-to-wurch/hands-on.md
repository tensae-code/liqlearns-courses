# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s five climate zones — Bereha to Wurch

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Staircase poster
Draw five steps and label each with its zone, its temperature word, and one thing that grows or lives there. Hang it where you study.

## Zone my home
Decide which zone your own home area belongs to, using altitude and temperature. Defend your choice to a partner with two pieces of evidence.
