# Erosion — Water, Wind, Ice and Waves — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four carriers of erosion in the lesson.
**Answer:** Running water, ice, wind and ocean waves.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** (Teacher guide — evidence) What is a barchan?
**Answer:** A sand hill with a crescent-moon shape, built by wind.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the glaciers leave behind them?
**Answer:** Thin-soiled land.
