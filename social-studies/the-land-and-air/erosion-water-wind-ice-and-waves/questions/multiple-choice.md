# Erosion — Water, Wind, Ice and Waves — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Erosion is —
a) rock breaking where it stands  b) worn rock being swept or washed from place to place  c) rock hardening
**Answer: b) worn rock being swept or washed from place to place**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Flood channels deepen and widen from —
a) rivers into gullies  b) gullies into great rivers  c) lakes into seas
**Answer: b) gullies into great rivers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Wind drops carried sand as —
a) loess, building new soil  b) lava  c) scree only
**Answer: a) loess, building new soil**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Ancient glaciers ploughed —
a) Ethiopia and Egypt  b) Canada, western Europe and Russia  c) Australia
**Answer: b) Canada, western Europe and Russia**
