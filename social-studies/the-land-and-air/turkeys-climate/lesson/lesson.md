# Turkey's Climate

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the climate in Turkey — Ülkemizde İklim, the closing Turkey case of the Grade 9 climate chapter, as Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya teaches it in 5D. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Climate in Turkey — Ülkemizde İklim

Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya builds its climate chapter step by step — atmosphere, weather and climate, temperature, pressure and winds, humidity and precipitation, then the world's climate types — and closes it at home: 5D Ülkemizde İklim, the climate in Turkey itself.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Turkey case — the chapter's ideas read at home

The Turkey case is where the chapter's own tools are put to work: the climate elements, the controls and the world climate types taught earlier in the chapter are the very tools the book uses to read Turkey's climate. That is Turkey's own way in this book — a Turkey case closing the chapter.

Turkey's own way: a systems course — Natural Systems first (nature–human interaction, coordinates, maps, Earth's motions, climate), then Human Systems (settlements), then Regions and Countries, then Environment and Society — with a Turkey case closing most chapters (Turkey's location, Turkey's climate, settlements in Turkey). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
