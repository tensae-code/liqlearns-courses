# Video script — Turkey's Climate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey's Grade 9 Coğrafya builds its climate chapter step by step — atmosphere, weather and climate, temperature, pressure and winds, humidity and precipitation, then the world's climate types — and closes it at home: 5D Ülkemizde İklim, the climate in Turkey itself.

The Turkey case is where the chapter's own tools are put to work: the climate elements, the controls and the world climate types taught earlier in the chapter are the very tools the book uses to read Turkey's climate. That is Turkey's own way in this book — a Turkey case closing the chapter.
