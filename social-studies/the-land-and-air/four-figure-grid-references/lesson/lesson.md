# Four-figure grid references

## Do it first

On a topographic map, every square has a four-figure name. The rule for reading it fits in one corridor walk. Read it, then do it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Eastings then Northings

Grid squares are named by their grid lines: Eastings are the vertical lines, numbered eastward; Northings are the horizontal lines, numbered northward. A four-figure reference takes the easting of the square’s left edge, then the northing of its bottom edge. Read Eastings first — "along the corridor" — then Northings upward.

## Sub lesson 2 — Naming the square

The four figures name the whole square, not a point: everything inside that square shares its reference. The book’s step table walks the reading in order — the method is the steps, done the same way every time.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the corridor rule, and name one square aloud step by step.

1. State the order of reading.
