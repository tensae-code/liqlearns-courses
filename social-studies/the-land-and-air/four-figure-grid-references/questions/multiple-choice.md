# Four-figure grid references — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eastings are —
a) the horizontal lines  b) the diagonal lines  c) the vertical grid lines
**Answer: c) the vertical grid lines**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Eastings are read —
a) last  b) first  c) second
**Answer: b) first**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A four-figure reference names —
a) a whole square  b) a single point  c) a line
**Answer: a) a whole square**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The northing figure comes from the square’s —
a) top edge  b) centre  c) bottom edge
**Answer: c) bottom edge**
