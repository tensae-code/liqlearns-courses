The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 4 Social Studies* (printed p. 20), teaches: Ch. 1: How to know the directions by day and by night: the sun, the pole star, and the compass.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 4 Social Studies): geography enters inside "Social Studies" through the child's own surroundings — phenomena are sorted from photographs first, then the local environment, then the map of Iraq's governorates, and only then the wider geographical environments (city, countryside); history and homeland/civics chapters follow in the same book.
