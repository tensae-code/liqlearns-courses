# Land-Shaping Forces — Folding — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Folding happens when internal force —
a) squeezes the outer crust  b) rains on the crust  c) freezes the crust
**Answer: a) squeezes the outer crust**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which range is named as fold-built in Africa?
a) the Atlas  b) the Alps  c) the Andes
**Answer: a) the Atlas**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Himalayas are in —
a) Europe  b) Asia  c) South America
**Answer: b) Asia**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The Andes are the fold-built range of —
a) Africa  b) North America  c) South America
**Answer: c) South America**
