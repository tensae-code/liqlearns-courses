# Hands-on — Island Countries and Landlocked Countries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Oceans and Continents of the World: Japan, Great Britain and The Bahamas are chains of islands; Switzerland, Bolivia, Botswana and Paraguay are landlocked countries; Australia is both a country and a continent. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahamas' way
Say how the Bahamas' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
