# Island Countries and Landlocked Countries

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Oceans and Continents of the World: Japan, Great Britain and The Bahamas are chains of islands; Switzerland, Bolivia, Botswana and Paraguay are landlocked countries; Australia is both a country and a continent — as the Bahamas' books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Island Countries and Landlocked Countries

Bahamas' Grade 6 student book, the *Bahamas Primary Social Studies — Digital Textbook, Grade 6* (PDF p. 8), teaches: Oceans and Continents of the World: Japan, Great Britain and The Bahamas are chains of islands; Switzerland, Bolivia, Botswana and Paraguay are landlocked countries; Australia is both a country and a continent. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bahamas' way — the book's own teaching way

Bahamas' own way (Grade 6): The Grade 6 book is civics-led (government, elections, courts, constitution, finance ministry) but opens the primary course's widest geography: the world's continents and oceans, political and physical maps, global warming and natural disasters, human resources and the job market, worldwide transport and communication through Nassau's ports of entry, and a closing cultural comparison of The Bahamas with Africa, Australia, Brazil and Japan. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
