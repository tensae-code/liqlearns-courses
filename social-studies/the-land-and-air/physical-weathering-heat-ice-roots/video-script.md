# Video script — Physical weathering — heat, ice, roots

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Physical weathering breaks rock into smaller pieces of the same chemistry — by heat peeling it, ice wedging it, and roots forcing it apart. First, heat and ice: Exogenic forces level the land through denudation — weathering plus erosion — and deposition. Then, plants and animals: The third process is living. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
