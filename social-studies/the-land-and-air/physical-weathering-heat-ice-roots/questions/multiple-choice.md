# Physical weathering — heat, ice, roots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Physical weathering changes the rock's…
a) chemistry  b) size only — same chemistry  c) colour only
**Answer: b) size only — same chemistry**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Exfoliation is…
a) layers peeling like an onion from heating and cooling  b) freezing water  c) root growth
**Answer: a) layers peeling like an onion from heating and cooling**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Frost action works because water…
a) shrinks when it freezes  b) expands when it freezes in cracks  c) dissolves rock
**Answer: b) expands when it freezes in cracks**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Scree is…
a) broken rock piled at a slope's foot  b) a kind of lava  c) river mud
**Answer: a) broken rock piled at a slope's foot**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Tree roots weather rock by…
a) cooling it  b) forcing cracks wider  c) covering it
**Answer: b) forcing cracks wider**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Denudation means…
a) weathering plus erosion  b) deposition only  c) folding
**Answer: a) weathering plus erosion**
