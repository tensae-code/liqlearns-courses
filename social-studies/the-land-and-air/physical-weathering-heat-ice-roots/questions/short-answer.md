# Physical weathering — heat, ice, roots — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three physical weathering processes.
**Answer:** Thermal expansion and contraction (exfoliation); frost action; and plant and animal action.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does frost split rock?
**Answer:** Water fills cracks, freezes and expands, wedging the rock apart.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do burrowing animals contribute?
**Answer:** Worms, rabbits and moles break and loosen the ground, opening it to further weathering.
