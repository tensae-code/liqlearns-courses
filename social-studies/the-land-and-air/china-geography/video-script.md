The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

North Macedonia's Grade 7 mk-g7 book, the *North Macedonia Grade 7 mk-g7* (printed p. 102), teaches: Тема 2: Кина. North Macedonia's Grade 9 mk-g9 book, the *North Macedonia Grade 9 mk-g9* (printed p. 38), teaches: Тема I: Кина — економски двигател на континентот.

North Macedonia's own way (Grade 7 mk-g7): regional geography taught continent by continent — Europe then Asia — each continent opened with its physical characteristics (position, coastline, relief, climate, hydrography), then its socio-geography (population, peoples and religions, science and culture, settlements), then region-by-region reviews with individual country lessons and a tourism-centres lesson per region; the home country is taught inside the Balkan/Southern Europe region alongside Albania. North Macedonia's own way (Grade 9 mk-g9): Grade 9 finishes the regional-geography cycle with the non-European continents — Asia again in greater depth (climate factors and phenomena, monsoons and typhoons, air pollution), then Africa, the Americas, and Australia with Oceania and the polar regions — each continent in the same pattern: position and coastline, relief, climate, hydrography, natural wealth and economy, population, a regional review, one or two spotlight countries (China, India; South Africa; USA, Brazil) and one problem lesson (food and water shortage in Africa; air pollution in Asia; global warming in the polar regions).
