# Hands-on — Belarus Soils and Land Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Soil and land
Write the difference, in your own words, between the soil cover of a country and its land resources, using this lesson.

## Use the land
List three uses of land named or implied by the lesson, and say for each whether it rests on the soil or on the land's position and size.
