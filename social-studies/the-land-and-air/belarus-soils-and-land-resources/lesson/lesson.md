# Belarus Soils and Land Resources

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the soil cover of Belarus and its land resources and their use, as Belarus's Grade 10 book teaches them. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The soil cover of Belarus

Belarus's Grade 10 book studies the country's soil cover in Theme II §17: the soils of Belarus are one component of its nature, following its waters.

## Sub lesson 2 — Land resources and their use

Theme II §18 follows with land resources and their use: the land itself counted as a resource, and how Belarus uses it — the ground under farming, forest and settlement.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
