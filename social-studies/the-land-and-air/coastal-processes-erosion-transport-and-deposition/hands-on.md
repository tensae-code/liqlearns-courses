# Hands-on — Coastal Processes — Erosion, Transport and Deposition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the processes
Name the three wave processes from the lesson — erosion, transportation and deposition — and say in your own words what each one does to coastal material.

## Read a coastline
Picture a stretch of coast. Point to where erosion is likely shaping it and where deposition is likely building it, and say why.
