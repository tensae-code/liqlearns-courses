# Coastal Processes — Erosion, Transport and Deposition

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the Caribbean coast; waves; wave processes of erosion, transportation and deposition; landforms of coastal erosion and of coastal deposition (case study Jamaica), as Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC (2nd ed.) teaches it in Ch. 4. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Waves at work on the coast

Barbados's Grade 10 Geography for CSEC teaches the coast in Chapter 4, starting from the Caribbean coast and its waves: waves erode the coast, transport the material they break off, and deposit it somewhere else along the shore.

## Sub lesson 2 — Landforms of erosion and deposition — Jamaica

The chapter builds the landforms those processes leave behind — the landforms of coastal erosion and the landforms of coastal deposition — with Jamaica as the case study, so each process is read in the shape it gives the coast.

Barbados's own way: this is the shared Caribbean CSEC examination textbook used in Barbados — every world process is re-taught through Caribbean case studies (the Caribbean plate, Montserrat's volcano, Jamaica's coasts and Kingston's urban problems, St Lucia's reefs, Bahamas/Jamaica/Puerto Rico karst, Guyana's rivers), map and field skills are folded into the chapters, and the course ends in a School-Based Assessment fieldwork project. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
