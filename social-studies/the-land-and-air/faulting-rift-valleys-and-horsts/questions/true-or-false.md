# Faulting — rift valleys and horsts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Graben and horst both form between parallel faults.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A horst is a sunken block.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Faulting breaks rock; folding bends it.
**Answer: True**
