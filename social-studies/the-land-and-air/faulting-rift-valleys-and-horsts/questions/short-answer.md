# Faulting — rift valleys and horsts — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What displaces rock along a fault?
**Answer:** Tension or compression.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How does a rift valley form?
**Answer:** The middle block sinks between two parallel faults, forming a graben.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name Ethiopia's horst example.
**Answer:** The Afar Horst.
