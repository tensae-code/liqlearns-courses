# Faulting — rift valleys and horsts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fault is…
a) a fold  b) a crack where rock is displaced up or down  c) a river channel
**Answer: b) a crack where rock is displaced up or down**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A graben is…
a) a block pushed up  b) a block sunk between parallel faults  c) a volcano crater
**Answer: b) a block sunk between parallel faults**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The East African Rift Valley is a…
a) horst  b) graben  c) delta
**Answer: b) graben**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A horst is…
a) a block pushed up into a block mountain  b) a sunken block  c) a crack with no movement
**Answer: a) a block pushed up into a block mountain**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's horst example in Ethiopia is…
a) the Afar Horst  b) Sof Omar Cave  c) Erta Ale
**Answer: a) the Afar Horst**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Faults are caused by…
a) rain and wind  b) tension or compression displacing rock  c) cooling lava
**Answer: b) tension or compression displacing rock**
