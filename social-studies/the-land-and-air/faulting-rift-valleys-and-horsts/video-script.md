# Video script — Faulting — rift valleys and horsts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

When rock cracks instead of bending, the blocks between faults move: down into rift valleys (graben) or up into block mountains (horst). First, faults and the sinking block: A fault is a crack in the rock where tension or compression displaces the rock — moves it up or down along the crack. Then, the rising block — horst: The opposite movement makes the opposite landform. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
