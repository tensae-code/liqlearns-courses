# Video script — Shebelle, Genale, Awash and Omo

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four rivers. Four endings.
The Wabe Shebelle — longest of all — fades into the Benadir coast.
The Genale reaches the sea by the Juba.
The Awash ends in Rift lakes. The Omo ends in Lake Turkana.
