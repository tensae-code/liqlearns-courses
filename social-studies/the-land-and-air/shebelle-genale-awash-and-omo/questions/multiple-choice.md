# Shebelle, Genale, Awash and Omo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The longest river in the country is —
a) the Abbay  b) the Awash  c) the Wabe Shebelle
**Answer: c) the Wabe Shebelle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Wabe Shebelle fades into —
a) the Red Sea  b) the Benadir coastlands of Somalia  c) Lake Turkana
**Answer: b) the Benadir coastlands of Somalia**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Awash ends in —
a) Rift lakes  b) the Indian Ocean  c) Lake Tana
**Answer: a) Rift lakes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Omo ends in —
a) Lake Hawassa  b) the Benadir coast  c) Lake Turkana
**Answer: c) Lake Turkana**
