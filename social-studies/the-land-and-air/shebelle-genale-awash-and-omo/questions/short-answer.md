# Shebelle, Genale, Awash and Omo — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which river system reaches the sea by the Juba?
**Answer: The Genale-Dawa system.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two lake-ending rivers of this lesson.
**Answer: The Awash (Rift lakes) and the Omo (Lake Turkana).**
