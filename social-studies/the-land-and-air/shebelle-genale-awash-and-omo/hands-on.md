# Hands-on — Shebelle, Genale, Awash and Omo

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Endings chart
Make a four-row chart: river / direction / ending. Fill it from the lesson, then close the lesson and refill it from memory.

## Longest river debate
The teacher guide’s fast-learner key also names the Wabe Shebelle as the longest river. With a partner, say what evidence a map gives for and against that claim.
