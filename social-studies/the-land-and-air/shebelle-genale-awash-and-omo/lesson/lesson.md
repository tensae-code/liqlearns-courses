# Shebelle, Genale, Awash and Omo

## Do it first

Four rivers, four different endings: sand, sea, lake, lake. Read which river ends where.

## Sub lesson 1 — Shebelle and Genale

The Wabe Shebelle is the longest river in the country; it flows south-east across the Ogaden and fades into the Benadir coastlands of Somalia. The Genale-Dawa system, further south, reaches the Indian Ocean by way of the Juba river.

## Sub lesson 2 — Awash and Omo

Two great rivers end in lakes. The Awash runs down the Rift and ends in the Rift lakes of the Afar side — the inward system. The Omo flows south from the highlands and ends in Lake Turkana. Lake, sand or sea: every Ethiopian river ends one of these ways.
