## Sub lesson 2 — India's way — the book's own teaching way

India's own way (Grade 7 Our Environment): Grade 7 is organised around "environment" in widening rings — the concept itself, then inside/changing Earth, air and water, world vegetation belts, human settlement and transport, and finally three lived-in region studies (Amazon basin, Ganga-Brahmaputra basin, Sahara and Ladakh deserts) where all the strands meet in real places. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
