**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (India, Vietnam, Iran, Iraq).**

India's own way (Grade 7 Our Environment): Grade 7 is organised around "environment" in widening rings — the concept itself, then inside/changing Earth, air and water, world vegetation belts, human settlement and transport, and finally three lived-in region studies (Amazon basin, Ganga-Brahmaputra basin, Sahara and Ladakh deserts) where all the strands meet in real places.

Searchable tags: waves tides and ocean currents, India, social studies, the-land-and-air

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Địa lí 10): a single Grade 10 course that runs the whole discipline — map craft, the Earth's spheres, then economic geography sector by sector — with Vietnam itself as the standing worked example (its power-plant and rice maps, the Red River's regime at Hanoi, Hanoi's climate chart, Vietnam's GDP structure, its internet and tourism figures) inside world-scale treatment.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 9 Social Studies): one combined Social Studies book of 24 lessons in 12 chapters, and here the Geography comes first: Ch. 1 Our planet, the Earth (Lessons 1–2, printed pp. 1–14), Ch. 2 Lithosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere (Lessons 3–4, printed pp. 15–28), Ch. 3 Biosphere, an amazing diversity (Lessons 5–6, printed pp. 29–40) and Ch. 4 The inhabitants of planet Earth (Lessons 7–8, printed pp. 41–52). The other parts are named here, not placed: History — Ch. 5 The age of unity and flourishing (Lessons 9–10, printed pp. 53–66), Ch. 6 Iran from Nader Shah to Naser al-Din Shah (Lessons 11–12, printed pp. 67–80), Ch. 7 Iran in the constitutional era (Lessons 13–14, printed pp. 81–94), Ch. 8 The fall of the monarchy and the forming of the Islamic Republic (Lessons 15–16, printed pp. 95–116); Civics and culture — Ch. 9 Culture and identity (Lessons 17–18, printed pp. 117–130), Ch. 10 Family and society (Lessons 19–20, printed pp. 131–142), Ch. 11 Government and people (Lessons 21–22, printed pp. 143–156); Economics — Ch. 12 Productivity (Lessons 23–24, printed pp. 157–175).

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 11 Physical Geography): a pure physical-geography course in six chapters — landforms, weather and climate, water, the biosphere, soil, and the environment — with Iraqi anchors kept throughout (flood visits to Iraq's rivers, the hills of northern Iraq, groundwater and the desert caravans, soil salinity in the alluvial plain).
