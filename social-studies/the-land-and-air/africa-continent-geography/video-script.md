Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 10 np-g10* (printed p. 159), teaches: Unit 6 Lesson 9: Continent of Africa. Nepal's book, the *Nepal Grade 10 TG np-g10tg* (TG p. 128), teaches: Earth Unit Lesson 9: Continent of Africa. (teacher guide — evidence)

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 10 np-g10): Grade 10 climate is taught by world climate types and the three heat zones (tropical, temperate, cold), then continent studies of North America, South America and Africa, earthquakes, map work and modern map technology (GIS); population closes the book — Nepal's size/growth, structure, distribution, family planning and the population–environment interrelation.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 10 TG np-g10tg) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide teaches the Grade 10 course of np-g10 lesson for lesson, adding map- and chart-based activities (world climate maps, heat-zone maps, continent maps comparing life and economic activity with Nepal) and earthquake-preparedness practice; placements mirror the student book with guide page references. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.
