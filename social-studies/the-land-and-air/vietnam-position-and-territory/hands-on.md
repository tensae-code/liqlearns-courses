# Hands-on — Vietnam Position And Territory

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 1: Vietnam's geographical position and territory — the extreme points (Lung Cu 23°23′N, Dat Mui 8°34′N, Sin Thau 102°09′E, Van Thanh 109°28′E), land and sea together (some 1 million km² of waters: internal waters, territorial sea, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf; the Hoang Sa and Truong Sa archipelagos), the 34 provinces and cities of 2025, and what the position means for nature, the economy and national defence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
