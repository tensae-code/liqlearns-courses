Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 5–9), teaches: Bài 1: Vietnam's geographical position and territory — the extreme points (Lung Cu 23°23′N, Dat Mui 8°34′N, Sin Thau 102°09′E, Van Thanh 109°28′E), land and sea together (some 1 million km² of waters: internal waters, territorial sea, exclusive economic zone and continental shelf; the Hoang Sa and Truong Sa archipelagos), the 34 provinces and cities of 2025, and what the position means for nature, the economy and national defence.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
