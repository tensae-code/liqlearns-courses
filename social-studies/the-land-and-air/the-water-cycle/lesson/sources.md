**Acceptance count: taught in 9 countries (India, Cambodia, Vietnam, Pakistan, Moldova, Mozambique, Laos, Iraq, Cuba).**

India's own way (Grade 7 Our Environment): Grade 7 is organised around "environment" in widening rings — the concept itself, then inside/changing Earth, air and water, world vegetation belts, human settlement and transport, and finally three lived-in region studies (Amazon basin, Ganga-Brahmaputra basin, Sahara and Ladakh deserts) where all the strands meet in real places.

Searchable tags: the water cycle, India, social studies, the-land-and-air

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 11 Earth Science): Earth Science is Cambodia's physical-geography strand — planet Earth and plate tectonics, water, a full atmosphere-and-climate course (ending with Cambodia's own weather and climate change), and the environment.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Địa lí 10): a single Grade 10 course that runs the whole discipline — map craft, the Earth's spheres, then economic geography sector by sector — with Vietnam itself as the standing worked example (its power-plant and rice maps, the Red River's regime at Hanoi, Hanoi's climate chart, Vietnam's GDP structure, its internet and tourism figures) inside world-scale treatment.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 7 Geography 7): a dedicated English-medium Geography book built entirely on Pakistan's own physical and economic geography — the Indus plain and delta, the canal system, Rabi/Kharif crops, Sui gas, Thar coal, Khewra salt and Tarbela power — with world concepts (river courses, settlement hierarchy, greenhouse effect, mining types) taught through Pakistani examples first.

Moldova's own way (Moldova Grade 5 G5 Geography): first Geography year built as a single Earth-systems arc — planet, envelopes (lithosphere/atmosphere/hydrosphere/biosphere), human society, then a closing "Protecting the Earth" chapter; every paragraph opens with "you will be able to" outcomes and chapters close with evaluation tests, plus two practical applications (map work; a week-long weather calendar).

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 1): distance-learning self-study module — every lesson follows the same rhythm (introduction, development with worked examples, exercises, answer key, lesson summary); the four Earth spheres are the organising spine of the whole module, each sphere closed by a "use, protection and conservation" lesson; local anchors throughout (Maputo temperature worked example, Zambezi delta, Moma/Namialo weather contrast).

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 2): a full physical-climatology course — atmosphere structure to magnetosphere, heat balance, pressure/wind circulation, air masses and fronts, humidity and precipitation, the water cycle, then Köppen/Thornthwaite classification worked through climate tables with African station graphs (Arkassa, Tamanrasset, Algiers), closing on protection of bioclimatic resources.

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 10 laos-g10-geography-teacher): a second world-geography pass at a higher level — Earth and map projection first, then life in the climate zones (hot, temperate, cold, mountain/coastal), population characteristics and problems, humans and environment, and a distinctive closing block on how geographic space is organised (agricultural areas, industrial areas, tourism areas, rural and urban geography).

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 10 laos-g10-geography-student): the student twin of Book 6 — same 6 parts / 20 lessons with full illustrated text, opening with a concept map of the whole year (Earth, life zones, population and quality of life, humans and environment, organisation of geographic space, rural and urban geography).

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 11 Physical Geography): a pure physical-geography course in six chapters — landforms, weather and climate, water, the biosphere, soil, and the environment — with Iraqi anchors kept throughout (flood visits to Iraq's rivers, the hills of northern Iraq, groundwater and the desert caravans, soil salinity in the alluvial plain).

Cuba's own way (Cuba Grade 7 Geografía Física G7): Physical geography is taught sphere by sphere (lithosphere → atmosphere → hydrosphere → biosphere) but every sphere chapter closes on rational use and sustainability — wind energy, rational water use, soil protection — and Cuban evidence runs through it (Cuba's forest cover fell from ~95 % at the Spanish arrival to 16 % by 1959 and was recovered by policy).
