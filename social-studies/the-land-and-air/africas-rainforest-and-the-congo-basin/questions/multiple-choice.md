# Africa's rainforest and the Congo Basin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's rainforest belt is centred on —
a) the Sahara  b) the Congo Basin  c) the Kalahari  d) the Highveld
**Answer: b) the Congo Basin** — the equatorial centre of the continent.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The rainforest belt sits on —
a) the Tropic of Cancer  b) the equator  c) the Arctic circle  d) the southern tip
**Answer: b) the equator** — rain and heat together all year.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Evergreen means the trees —
a) drop all leaves yearly  b) keep their leaves through the whole year  c) never grow leaves  d) grow only in winter
**Answer: b) keep their leaves through the whole year** — water comes in every month.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Beside the rainforest, the savanna tree differs by —
a) being taller  b) shedding leaves in its dry season  c) needing no water  d) growing in layers
**Answer: b) shedding leaves in its dry season** — seasonal drought writes its structure.
