# Africa's rainforest and the Congo Basin — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rainforest's growth is the densest on the continent.
**Answer: True** — rain and heat all year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The rainforest belt lies at Africa's southern tip.
**Answer: False** — it is equatorial, centred on the Congo Basin.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rainforest floors are bright and open under an open canopy.
**Answer: False** — the canopy is closed and the floor dim.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Rainforest and savanna structures are both written by water — one by year-round rain, one by seasonal drought.
**Answer: True** — the lesson's closing contrast.
