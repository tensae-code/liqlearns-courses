# Africa's rainforest and the Congo Basin — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do rainforest trees never need to shed all their leaves at once?
**Answer:** Because rain falls in every month — they are never short of water, so they stay evergreen.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which belts arrange themselves north and south of the rainforest core?
**Answer:** The savanna, then the Sahel, then the desert — mirrored about the equator.
