# Hands-on — Africa's rainforest and the Congo Basin

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Layered forest model
Build the forest's layers with paper: tall crown cut-outs at the back, mid trees, small shade plants in front. Label the layers and write under it why no layer ever stands bare.

## Belt neighbours
On a strip map, colour the rainforest at the equator and the savanna belts on both sides. Write one water sentence under each belt: 'rain all year' / 'wet and dry seasons'.
