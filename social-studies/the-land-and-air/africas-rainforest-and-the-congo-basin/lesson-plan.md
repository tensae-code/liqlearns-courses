# Lesson plan — Africa's rainforest and the Congo Basin

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Africa's rainforest and the Congo Basin
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 1 — Landforms of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Africa's equatorial rainforest belt is centred on the Congo Basin — a dense, evergreen forest that keeps its leaves and its green through the whole year under equatorial rain and heat.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the belt map of Africa — the rainforest is the equatorial belt the others arrange themselves around.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **The equatorial belt centred on the Congo Basin** — Where the rainforest stands.
2. **Dense and evergreen — the forest's structure** — Layers that never stand bare.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Savanna grasslands. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Place the rainforest belt on the equator, centred on the Congo Basin.
2. Explain why rainforest trees are evergreen.
3. Contrast rainforest structure with savanna trees in the dry season.
