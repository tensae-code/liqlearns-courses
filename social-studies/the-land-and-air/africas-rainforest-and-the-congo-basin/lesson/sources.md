**Acceptance count: taught in 5 countries (Ethiopia, Angola, Egypt, Slovenia, South Sudan).**

Pairing: the teacher guide was first published in 2022 and the student textbook in 2023, with the same writers and the same eight units in the same order; the student book is the learner text of record, and teacher-guide rows above are evidence only.

Searchable tags: rainforest, Congo Basin, evergreen, equatorial forest, Ethiopia Grade 10 geography

Angola's own way (Grade 6): a resources-and-environment year — every world topic (air, water, soils, forests) is closed with the Angolan case: Angola's fresh-water use, Angola's farm land, Angola's forest areas. That way is stocked here as the way Angola teaches this thing.

Egypt's own way (Grade 7 Term 1): Social Studies is rebuilt around “Africa and Egypt together” — the geography half teaches Africa first (position, surface, the Nile) then Africa's ecosystems system by system (forests, grasslands/deserts, aquatic), always tying Egypt into the continent; every lesson opens with outcome goals, map-work (“observe map 1 and share with your partner”) and a blue-economy / ICT-linked application section.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 8 Geografija 8): Grade 8 is pure world regional geography, continent by continent (Africa, Australia/Oceania, the Americas and polar regions), each region worked through the same grid — position/relief/waters, climate/vegetation, population, economy — and the book closes with two world-problems units (rainforest loss, desert spread, water conflicts, the Aral Sea).

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 11 S3 Geography SB): A world-geography book (trade routes, megacities, Southeast Asia) anchored back to South Sudan throughout — humanitarian airlift airfields, Thuraya satellite phones, a tourism plan for South Sudan, Juba as the urbanisation example.
