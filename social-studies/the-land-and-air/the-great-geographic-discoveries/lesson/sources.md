**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Egypt, Moldova).**

Egypt's own way (Grade 9 Term 1): preparatory school closes with the “New World” — the Americas and Australia taught continent by continent (position, then surface: Rockies and Appalachians, Andes and the Amazon plain), the ecosystem sequence once more, and for the first time a full lesson on climate change itself (greenhouse-gas shares from a pie chart, human and natural causes, effects on ecosystems, water, farming and transport) — with the Panama Canal read against Suez as the strategic-position comparison.

Searchable tags: the great geographic discoveries, Egypt, social studies, the-land-and-air

Moldova's own way (Moldova Grade 6 G6 Geography): a "Geography of the Continents" year — the southern continents (Africa, South America, Australia, Antarctica) each worked through with the identical template (physical position, relief and minerals, climate factors and zones, surface waters, natural zones, population, peoples and political map), and every continent closes with a "geographic risk processes" lesson; an annex gives a standard plan for characterising any continent.
