# Ethiopia’s main soils

## Do it first

Dig two pits in your mind: one in black cracking clay, one in red highland soil. Same country, different farming. Read the main soils.

## Sub lesson 1 — The main types

Ethiopia’s main soil types include Nitosols, Vertisols — the black cracking clays — Leptosols, Luvisols and Cambisols, together with the soils of the arid lowlands. Each type is described by its colour, its fertility and how easily it can be worked.

## Sub lesson 2 — Where they lie

The types are distributed with the land itself: deep soils where old surfaces and gentle slopes allowed them to form, thin Leptosols on steep and stony ground, black clays in flatter highland basins, dry soils in the arid lowlands. Soil maps of Ethiopia are relief and climate maps in disguise.
