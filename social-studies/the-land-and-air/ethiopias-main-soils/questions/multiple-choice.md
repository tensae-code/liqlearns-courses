# Ethiopia’s main soils — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The black cracking clays are —
a) Nitosols  b) Leptosols  c) Vertisols
**Answer: c) Vertisols**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Thin soils on steep, stony ground are —
a) Luvisols  b) Leptosols  c) Vertisols
**Answer: b) Leptosols**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Soil distribution follows —
a) relief and climate  b) town boundaries  c) language areas
**Answer: a) relief and climate**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Black clays are found in —
a) the steepest slopes  b) salt plains  c) flatter highland basins
**Answer: c) flatter highland basins**
