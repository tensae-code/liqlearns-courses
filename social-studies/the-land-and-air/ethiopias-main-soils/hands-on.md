# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s main soils

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Soil detective
Find two different soils near home — colour, stones, how they feel wet and dry. Match each to the lesson’s types as far as you honestly can, and say what evidence you used.

## Disguised maps
Explain to a partner why a soil map is a relief and climate map in disguise, using Leptosols and Vertisols as your two examples.
