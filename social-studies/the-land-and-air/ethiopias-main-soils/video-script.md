# Video script — Ethiopia’s main soils

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Ethiopia farms on many soils.
Red Nitosols. Black cracking Vertisols. Thin Leptosols on the steep land.
Luvisols. Cambisols. And the dry lowland soils.
Each soil: its own colour, its own farming.
