# Hands-on — Irans Seas Caspian Persian Gulf And Oman

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 17 The features of Iran's seas (ویژگی‌های دریاهای ایران): in past years you learned that Iran reaches the seas from north and south; the Caspian Sea (دریای خزر) — in truth a lake, yet called a sea: do you know why?; the rivers of the south-west on the map (Karun, Hendijan, Jarahi, and Iraq beside them); show our country's islands in the Persian Gulf and name the biggest island; the Persian Gulf and the Sea of Oman join our country to its southern neighbours. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
