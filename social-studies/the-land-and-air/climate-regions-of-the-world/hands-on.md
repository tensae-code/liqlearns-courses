# Hands-on — Climate Regions Of The World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 3 Climate regions (نواحی آب و هوایی): the air envelope's importance (nitrogen 78%, oxygen 21%), the sun's angle and its spread over the Earth, air pressure and the barometer, air masses, the pressure belts and the general circulation of the atmosphere, and the world's uneven rainfall — activity: why the northern slopes of the Alborz and the western slopes of the Zagros are rainy and the opposite slopes dry. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
