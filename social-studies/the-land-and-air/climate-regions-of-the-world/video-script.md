Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)* (printed p. 20), teaches: Lesson 3 Climate regions (نواحی آب و هوایی): the air envelope's importance (nitrogen 78%, oxygen 21%), the sun's angle and its spread over the Earth, air pressure and the barometer, air masses, the pressure belts and the general circulation of the atmosphere, and the world's uneven rainfall — activity: why the northern slopes of the Alborz and the western slopes of the Zagros are rainy and the opposite slopes dry.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 11 Geography (2)): the whole book is built on one organising idea — the region (ناحیه). Four chapters divide the world into regions four ways: what a region is, natural regions (climate, landforms, living, coasts, mountains — with Nepal as the highland case study), human regions (cultural, economic), and political regions (the state, borders, geopolitics — with the Persian Gulf and the European Union as case studies).
