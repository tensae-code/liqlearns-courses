# Sketching the moving map — Pangaea to today — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The single ocean around Pangaea was —
a) Panthalassa  b) the Atlantic  c) the Indian Ocean
**Answer: a) Panthalassa**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In stage one, the land stood as —
a) Pangaea  b) seven continents  c) islands only
**Answer: a) Pangaea**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the late Triassic, Laurasia moved —
a) north  b) south  c) under the sea
**Answer: a) north**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Near-modern positions were reached by —
a) the late Cretaceous  b) the Precambrian  c) last century
**Answer: a) the late Cretaceous**
