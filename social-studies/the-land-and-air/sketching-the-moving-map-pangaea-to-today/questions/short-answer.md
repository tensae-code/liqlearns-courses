# Sketching the moving map — Pangaea to today — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What was Panthalassa?
**Answer:** The single ocean that ringed Pangaea.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the oceans do as the land cracked?
**Answer:** They flooded into the rifts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three stages in order.
**Answer:** Triassic Pangaea; late-Triassic cracking; late-Cretaceous near-modern positions.
