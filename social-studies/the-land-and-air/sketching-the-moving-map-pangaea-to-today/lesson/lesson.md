## Sub lesson 1 — Stage one — Pangaea

In the Triassic, all land stood as Pangaea, ringed by the single great ocean Panthalassa. There was one land and one sea.

## Sub lesson 2 — Stage two — the cracking

In the late Triassic the land began to crack. Laurasia moved north, and the oceans flooded into the rifts between the separating blocks.

## Sub lesson 3 — Stage three — nearly today

By the late Cretaceous the continents stood near their modern positions. Your task, as the book sets it: draw sketch maps of each stage yourself, so the movement lives in your own hand.
