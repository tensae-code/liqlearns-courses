## Sub lesson 1 — From Local Space To The World

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 4 G4 CS Venezuela y Su Gente* (printed p. 16), teaches: De la casa de Tomás a la casa de todos: from home and town (Timotes) out to the nation and the world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the book's own teaching way

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 4 G4 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): geography is the first block ("Conóceme, soy Venezuela") and is built as widening circles — the child's house drawn to a stated scale (1 cm on the plan = 1 m), then Tomás's town, then Venezuela's territory and borders, then Latin America and the unequal world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
