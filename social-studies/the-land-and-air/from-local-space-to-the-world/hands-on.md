# Hands-on — From Local Space To The World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: De la casa de Tomás a la casa de todos: from home and town (Timotes) out to the nation and the world. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
