# Video script — From Local Space To The World

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 4 G4 CS Venezuela y Su Gente* (printed p. 16), teaches: De la casa de Tomás a la casa de todos: from home and town (Timotes) out to the nation and the world.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 4 G4 CS Venezuela y Su Gente): geography is the first block ("Conóceme, soy Venezuela") and is built as widening circles — the child's house drawn to a stated scale (1 cm on the plan = 1 m), then Tomás's town, then Venezuela's territory and borders, then Latin America and the unequal world.
