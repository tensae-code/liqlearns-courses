The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 9 Geography (Dari)* (printed p. 3), teaches: Ch. 1 Lessons 1–2: position and four boundaries of the continent of Africa; physical geography — the mountains. Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)* (printed p. 75), teaches: Lesson 38: the continent of Africa (same NEW lesson as the Grade 9 book's Africa placement).

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 9 Geography (Dari)): the continent march finishes with Africa, then Australia/Oceania and Antarctica — same fixed order per continent as Grade 8, with a data table of the independent countries of Africa appended to the Africa chapter.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)): primary social studies starts from the child's own world — family, village, city — and only then opens the map: directions, Afghanistan's boundaries and mountains and rivers, the continents and oceans, and finally the Earth's shape, equator, latitude/longitude and movements.
