## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 9 Geography (Dari)): the continent march finishes with Africa, then Australia/Oceania and Antarctica — same fixed order per continent as Grade 8, with a data table of the independent countries of Africa appended to the Africa chapter.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)): primary social studies starts from the child's own world — family, village, city — and only then opens the map: directions, Afghanistan's boundaries and mountains and rivers, the continents and oceans, and finally the Earth's shape, equator, latitude/longitude and movements. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
