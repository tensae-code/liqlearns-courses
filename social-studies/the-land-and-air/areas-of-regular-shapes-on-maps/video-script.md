# Video script — Areas of regular shapes on maps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Regular shapes surrender to formulae.
Rectangle: length by width. Triangle: half base by height. Circle: pi r squared.
Then square the scale — areas convert by the areal scale.
One square centimetre at 1:50,000 is a quarter of a square kilometre.
