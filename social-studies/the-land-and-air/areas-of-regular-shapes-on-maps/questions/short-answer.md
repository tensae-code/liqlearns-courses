# Areas of regular shapes on maps — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three formulae.
**Answer: Square/rectangle: length × width; triangle: ½ base × height; circle: πr².**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At 1:50,000, what is 4 cm² in ground area?
**Answer: 4 × 0.25 = 1 km².**
