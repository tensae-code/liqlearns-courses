# Areas of regular shapes on maps — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A rectangle’s area is —
a) length + width  b) length × height ÷ 4  c) length × width
**Answer: c) length × width**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A triangle’s area is —
a) base + height  b) half base × height  c) base × height
**Answer: b) half base × height**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Areas convert by —
a) the areal scale — the linear scale squared  b) the linear scale alone  c) the RF halved
**Answer: a) the areal scale — the linear scale squared**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** At 1:50,000, 1 cm² on the map is —
a) 0.5 km²  b) 1 km²  c) 0.25 km²
**Answer: c) 0.25 km²**
