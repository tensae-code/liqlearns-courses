# Video script — The Sahel — where the desert advances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South of the Sahara lies the Sahel.
Not desert. Not grassland. The edge between.
Its cover is thin. Its rains can fail.
When the cover goes, the desert advances. That is desertification.
