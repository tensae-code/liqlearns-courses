# Hands-on — The Sahel — where the desert advances

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Edge-line map
Draw the Sahara and shade it. Under it draw the Sahel strip in a lighter tone, then the savanna. Draw small arrows from the Sahara into the Sahel labelled 'desertification — the edge moves'.

## Protect the cover
On paper, list the Sahel's thin cover — grass, scattered trees, light soil. For each, write one way its loss makes the land more desert-like. Share and compare with a partner.
