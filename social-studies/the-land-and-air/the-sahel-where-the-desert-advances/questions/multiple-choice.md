# The Sahel — where the desert advances — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sahel lies —
a) north of the Sahara  b) south of the Sahara  c) inside the Congo Basin  d) on the coast
**Answer: b) south of the Sahara** — the belt between desert and savanna.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Semi-arid means —
a) no rain ever  b) drier than savanna, wetter than desert  c) rain all year  d) snow in winter
**Answer: b) drier than savanna, wetter than desert** — a transition climate.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Desertification is —
a) a desert by climate  b) land losing vegetation and soil until it behaves like desert  c) planting trees  d) a rainy season
**Answer: b) land losing vegetation and soil until it behaves like desert** — a change, not a fixed climate.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sahel is fragile mainly because —
a) its cover is thin and its rains unreliable  b) it is under the sea  c) it has no people  d) its soil is rock
**Answer: a) its cover is thin and its rains unreliable** — dry years or pressure can strip the cover.
