# The Sahel — where the desert advances — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sahel is a transition belt between the Sahara and the savanna.
**Answer: True** — desert north, grassland south.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sahel is wetter than the savanna.
**Answer: False** — it is drier than the savanna and wetter than the desert.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sahara is becoming desert through desertification.
**Answer: False** — the Sahara is desert by climate; the Sahel is the land that can tip.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What people do on Sahel land helps decide which way the desert edge moves.
**Answer: True** — pressure on thin cover is part of the process.
