# The Sahel — where the desert advances — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Define desertification in one sentence.
**Answer:** Desertification is the process by which land loses its vegetation and soil until it behaves like desert.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is a transition belt fragile?
**Answer:** Its vegetation cover is thin and its rainfall unreliable, so dry years or heavy pressure can strip the cover that holds the land together.
