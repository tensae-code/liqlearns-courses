# Hands-on — Rainforest, woodland and savanna

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three panels
Draw the three panels of the lesson — rainforest, acacia woodland, savanna — with the rainfall of each written beneath. Present them as one descent.

## Coffee’s home
Explain to a partner why wild coffee belongs to the moist forest: shade, moisture, and the forest floor, in the lesson’s terms.
