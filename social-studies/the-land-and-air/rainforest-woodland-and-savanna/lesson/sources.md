Costa Rica's own way: a distance-education anthology organised around "the human footprint on geographic space" — every unit opens with a generating question and objectives, and Costa Rica is used as the home case inside world topics (tropical forest conservation, food production problems). That way is stocked here as the way Costa Rica teaches this thing.

Searchable tags: moist montane forest, coffee origin, acacia woodland, savanna, awash, social studies, Costa Rica, CONED Antología, Estudios Sociales

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): the year's Geography is one long chapter, "Protect the Bond between Nature and People": pupils model the world's biomes, compare world forest cover in 2000 and 2020, map Bangladesh's own forests and disaster-prone areas, and read a long inquiry-reader on biomes, sea resources and the Sundarbans mangrove.

Colombia's own way (CS_Grado4_02): the second Grade 4 cartilla is almost pure physical geography of Colombia — relief first, then the tropical position and thermal floors, then a region-by-region tour of Colombia's six natural regions before the world's natural regions.

India's own way (Grade 7 Our Environment): Grade 7 is organised around "environment" in widening rings — the concept itself, then inside/changing Earth, air and water, world vegetation belts, human settlement and transport, and finally three lived-in region studies (Amazon basin, Ganga-Brahmaputra basin, Sahara and Ladakh deserts) where all the strands meet in real places.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 13 Geography): The final year integrates everything at advanced level (plate tectonics to urban models, largely from world texts such as Waugh) and then lands it in Chapter 8, South Pacific Islands Geography — Oceania's geology, island types, development prospects and regionalism.

Uzbekistan's own way (Uzbekistan Grade 6 Geografiya): Geography in Grade 6 is the physical geography of continents and oceans in the Russian/Soviet textbook tradition — first the Earth's geographic envelope and the World Ocean, then every continent in a fixed order (position, geological structure and relief, climate, inland waters, natural zones, population), closing with the population of the Earth and the political map of the world; nearly every chapter ends in a practical work.

Slovenia's own way (Slovenia Grade 8 Geografija 8): Grade 8 is pure world regional geography, continent by continent (Africa, Australia/Oceania, the Americas and polar regions), each region worked through the same grid — position/relief/waters, climate/vegetation, population, economy — and the book closes with two world-problems units (rainforest loss, desert spread, water conflicts, the Aral Sea).

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 3 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): Social Science is organised in five strands (Community and Organization; Trade; Culture and Society; Environment and Resources; History and Origins) taught through guided lessons that start outdoors in the school grounds — children observe, draw and label the features, resources and maps of their own local community first. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 8 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): the local-to-global ladder completes — after province (G5), nation (G7), Grade 8's Environment and Resources strand is global: global climate, global settlement and population, global farming, urbanization and logging impacts, global hazards and disaster resilience. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): at secondary level Social Science splits into five strands — Geography, History, Political Science, Economics, Environment. The Geography strand is a weather-and-climate skills course (instruments, data, graphs — with Port Moresby and Kokopo data) moving to world climate/vegetation, migration, and land use; the Environment strand carries Earth systems, resources and sustainability. That evidence is stocked here as the way Papua New Guinea teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 11 UG S4 Geography Home-Study): Africa-wide resource geography taught through single-country industry case studies (Gabon's forestry, Africa's fishing grounds with catch/export tables) that Ugandan learners compare with home.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 10 SD Geography 10): A second-edition foundations book (the preface says necessity forced this 2nd edition): physical geography first (universe to rivers), then climate and vegetation, then a long practical cartography unit, closing with GIS — geography taught as a skills subject, with an activity (نشاط) box on most spreads.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 7 SD Geography 7): A continents book: after a world opener (continents, oceans, relief, world environments), each continent — Africa, Asia, Europe — gets the same treatment in the same order (relief, drainage, climate, vegetation, population, economic activity, environmental problems), closing with a British Isles case study; Sudan and Africa anchor the examples throughout.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 9 G9 English): the student companion to Book 1 — five chapters moving from the whole Asian region down to Sri Lanka's own landscape, its district-by-district development contrasts, environmental balance, and a closing practical chapter reading the 1:50,000 topographic maps.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 8 Geography 8): the Urdu-medium twin of the English Geography course — deep map-and-graph craft first (dot and choropleth maps of Pakistan itself), then landform agents, oceans, a full Pakistan-anchored disaster chapter, the world's natural regions, Pakistan's own climate, its four neighbouring regions and strategic place, underdevelopment with Pakistan's own causes, and modern techniques down to Pak Sat-1.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 11 Physical Geography): a pure physical-geography course in six chapters — landforms, weather and climate, water, the biosphere, soil, and the environment — with Iraqi anchors kept throughout (flood visits to Iraq's rivers, the hills of northern Iraq, groundwater and the desert caravans, soil salinity in the alluvial plain).

Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 8 Geography): Class VIII deepens the same three strands with real map-work (scale conversion, grid references, contours and profiles) and Bhutan-specific hazard and resource content, including the Rubesa wind turbine and hydroelectric dams as national resource examples.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Geography SB): Each general topic is immediately re-taught through South Sudan — its named rivers (Sobat, Adar, Yabus, Daga), the Sudd swamplands, its oil, fishing and transport problems, and case-study research tasks set by a mock ministry brief.
