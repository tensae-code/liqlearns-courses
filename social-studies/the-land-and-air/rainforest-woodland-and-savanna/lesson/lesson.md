# Rainforest, woodland and savanna

## Do it first

From dripping rainforest to open savanna in one descent. Three plant worlds of the middle belts — read them in order.

## Sub lesson 1 — Moist montane forest

The south-west holds moist montane forest — true rainforest, fed by the year-round rains, and famous as the original home of coffee, which still grows wild under its canopy. It is Ethiopia’s richest, densest vegetation.

## Sub lesson 2 — Woodlands and savanna

The mid-altitude belts carry acacia woodlands, wooded grassland and savanna — as in Awash National Park: grass with scattered trees, the classic game country. Trees thin as rainfall falls, grading the forest down toward the dry lands.
