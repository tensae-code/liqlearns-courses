# Rainforest, woodland and savanna — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The moist montane rainforest lies in —
a) Afar  b) the Ogaden  c) the south-west
**Answer: c) the south-west**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The rainforest is the original home of —
a) cotton  b) coffee  c) teff
**Answer: b) coffee**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Savanna is —
a) grass with scattered trees  b) dense forest  c) treeless desert
**Answer: a) grass with scattered trees**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The park instanced for savanna is —
a) Simien National Park  b) Bale National Park  c) Awash National Park
**Answer: c) Awash National Park**
