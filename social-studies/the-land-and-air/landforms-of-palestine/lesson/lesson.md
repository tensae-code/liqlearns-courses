## Sub lesson 3 — Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way

Palestine's own way (Grade 5, PS G5P1): Social Studies opens with a full Earth-science unit (shape, movements, surface, life components, disasters) taught through Palestinian examples — seasons read off Palestinian crops, surface forms read off Palestine's own map, which pupils draw with its borders.
Palestine's own way (Grade 6, PS G6P1): Grade 6 opens with how we know — the map and its elements as the first source of geographical knowledge — then walks the Arab homeland region by region (peninsula, Levant, Palestine, Iraq, North Africa), each time fixing location first, then surface and climate, then the ancient civilisation the land produced. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
