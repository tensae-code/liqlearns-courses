# Video script — Landforms of Palestine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine's Grade 5 student book, the *Social Studies, Grade 5, Part One* (printed pp. 18–21), teaches: the surface forms (landforms) of Palestine.

Palestine's own way (Grade 5, PS G5P1): Social Studies opens with a full Earth-science unit (shape, movements, surface, life components, disasters) taught through Palestinian examples — seasons read off Palestinian crops, surface forms read off Palestine's own map, which pupils draw with its borders.
Palestine's own way (Grade 6, PS G6P1): Grade 6 opens with how we know — the map and its elements as the first source of geographical knowledge — then walks the Arab homeland region by region (peninsula, Levant, Palestine, Iraq, North Africa), each time fixing location first, then surface and climate, then the ancient civilisation the land produced.
