# Video script — Coastal Landforms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where Ecuador's land meets the Pacific, the edge is not a straight line: los accidentes costeros — the coastal features — shape it. Grade 3 teaches the child to name the features of a coast and to find them on Ecuador's own shoreline, as part of its Unit 2 on the irregularities of the Earth (Las irregularidades de la Tierra).

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the older SM edition is a classic skills-first book — a whole opening unit on orientation, cardinal points, the compass rose and plans before any content; every unit runs the same skeleton (content lessons, “Ser Estudiante” test, Cultura del Buen Vivir, scientific work, digital skills, unit evaluation), and the country is learned parish → canton → province → nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
