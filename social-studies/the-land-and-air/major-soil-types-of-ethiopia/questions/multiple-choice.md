# Major soil types of Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The pupil text’s black cracking clays are —
a) Regosols  b) Yermisols  c) Vertisols
**Answer: c) Vertisols**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide praises Vertisols as —
a) thin stony soils  b) excellent black basaltic soils  c) poor salty soils
**Answer: b) excellent black basaltic soils**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many soil types does the guide’s key name?
a) ten  b) four  c) six exactly
**Answer: a) ten**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Names in the guide’s list include —
a) only Vertisols  b) sand and clay  c) fluvisols and yermisols
**Answer: c) fluvisols and yermisols**
