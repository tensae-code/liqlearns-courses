# Video script — Major soil types of Ethiopia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Two books, two lists, one ground.

The teacher’s key names ten — with older spellings riding beside the new.
Good geography shows both, and says why they differ.
