# Hands-on — Major soil types of Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-list table
Build a two-column table: pupil names / guide names. Draw lines between names that clearly match (vertisols–Vertisols, cambisols–Cambisols) and leave the rest honestly unmatched.

## Name the difference
Explain to a partner, in plain words, why the same country’s soils carry two lists — editions and naming systems change; the ground does not.
