Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 17–26), teaches: Bài 3: The diversity of Vietnam's nature — the north–south difference at the Bach Ma range (a cold winter in the north, a near-equatorial south), the east–west difference (sea and shelf, deltas, mountains; the Hoang Lien Son against the Truong Son), the three altitude belts, and the three natural regions (the North and Northeast; the Northwest and North Central; the South Central and South), each with its own relief, climate, rivers, life and minerals.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
