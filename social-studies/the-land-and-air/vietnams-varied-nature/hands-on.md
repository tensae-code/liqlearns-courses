# Hands-on — Vietnams Varied Nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 3: The diversity of Vietnam's nature — the north–south difference at the Bach Ma range (a cold winter in the north, a near-equatorial south), the east–west difference (sea and shelf, deltas, mountains; the Hoang Lien Son against the Truong Son), the three altitude belts, and the three natural regions (the North and Northeast; the Northwest and North Central; the South Central and South), each with its own relief, climate, rivers, life and minerals. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
