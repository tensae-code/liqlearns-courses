Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 4 Social Studies (Dari)): primary social studies starts from the child's own world — family, village, city — and only then opens the map: directions, Afghanistan's boundaries and mountains and rivers, the continents and oceans, and finally the Earth's shape, equator, latitude/longitude and movements.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 8 Social Studies): one combined Social Studies book of 24 lessons in 12 chapters. The Geography parts placed below are the book's last four chapters — a tour of the continents: Ch. 9 Asia, the largest continent (Lessons 17–18, printed pp. 107–122), Ch. 10 The Southwest Asia region and Iran's place in it (Lessons 19–20, printed pp. 123–136), Ch. 11 Europe and Africa (Lessons 21–22, printed pp. 137–152) and Ch. 12 The continent of America and Oceania (Lessons 23–24, printed pp. 153–168), with illustrated maps of the continents at printed pp. 169–173. The other parts are named here, not placed: Civics — Ch. 1 Cooperation (Lessons 1–2, printed pp. 1–12), Ch. 2 The government's role in running the country (Lessons 3–4, printed pp. 13–22), Ch. 3 Adolescents and the law (Lessons 5–6, printed pp. 23–34), Ch. 4 The age of communications (Lessons 7–8, printed pp. 35–46); History — Ch. 5 From Hira to Nineveh (Lessons 9–10, printed pp. 47–62), Ch. 6 A new era in the history of Iran (Lessons 11–12, printed pp. 63–78), Ch. 7 From the Ghaznavids to the invasion of Chengiz (Lessons 13–14, printed pp. 79–92), Ch. 8 The Mongol and Timurid era (Lessons 15–16, printed pp. 93–106).
