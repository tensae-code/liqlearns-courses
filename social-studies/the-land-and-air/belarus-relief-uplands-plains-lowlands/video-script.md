# Video script — Belarus Relief — Uplands, Plains and Lowlands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus's Grade 10 book studies the country's relief in two paragraphs (Theme II §11–12): first the relief of Belarus as a whole — the character of its surface.

The second paragraph sorts that surface into its three kinds: uplands, plains and lowlands. Belarus is taught as a country of these three — raised land, flat land and low land — and the sorting is the lesson's work.
