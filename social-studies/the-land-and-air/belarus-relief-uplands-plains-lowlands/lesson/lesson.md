# Belarus Relief — Uplands, Plains and Lowlands

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the relief of Belarus — its uplands, plains and lowlands, as Belarus's Grade 10 book teaches them. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The relief of Belarus — its general character

Belarus's Grade 10 book studies the country's relief in two paragraphs (Theme II §11–12): first the relief of Belarus as a whole — the character of its surface.

## Sub lesson 2 — Uplands, plains and lowlands — the three kinds of surface

The second paragraph sorts that surface into its three kinds: uplands, plains and lowlands. Belarus is taught as a country of these three — raised land, flat land and low land — and the sorting is the lesson's work.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
