# Hands-on — Belarus Relief — Uplands, Plains and Lowlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the surface
Draw three columns — uplands, plains, lowlands — and sort what the lesson says about Belarus's surface into them.

## Profile sketch
Sketch a simple cross-section of a land that rises to an upland, flattens into a plain and sinks into a lowland, and label each part.
