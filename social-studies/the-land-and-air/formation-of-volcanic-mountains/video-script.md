# Video script — Formation of Volcanic Mountains

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Geography SB* (printed p. 4), teaches: Formation of volcanic mountains (Kilimanjaro, Mt Kenya, Jebel Marra, Nyamuragira). South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 32), teaches: Volcanic mountains: Mount Kenya; active and dormant volcanoes. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 6 P6 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 22), teaches: Formation of a volcanic mountain; three types of volcanoes (active, dormant, extinct).

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 10 S2 Geography SB): Each general topic is immediately re-taught through South Sudan — its named rivers (Sobat, Adar, Yabus, Daga), the Sudd swamplands, its oil, fishing and transport problems, and case-study research tasks set by a mock ministry brief.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB): Landforms are made, not just named — learners push a sheet of paper from both ends to make a fold mountain, and the home examples are the Ironstone Plateau and a farming landscape being changed by the farming itself.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 6 P6 Social Studies SB): Tourism is taught as national homework — map the parks, weigh the landlocked disadvantage against South Africa, set up the anti-poaching unit — and the fishing unit ties grounds back to Unit 2's physical features and forward to roads that must be improved so fish can reach market.
