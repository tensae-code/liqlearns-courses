# Video script — Irans Climate Types And Its Great Deserts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran* (printed p. 41), teaches: Types of Iran's climate and the desert lands (Dasht-e Kavir, how a place becomes desert).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 10 Geography of Iran): the whole of Grade 10 geography is one national book — Iran's own natural and human geography for every branch; nearly every lesson closes with a "know your own living place" activity tied to the separate provincial-studies book, so national content is always re-applied to the learner's own province.
