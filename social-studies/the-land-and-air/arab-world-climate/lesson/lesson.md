## Sub lesson 2 — Libya's way — the book's own teaching way

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 8 LY G8 Arab World): The scale widens from Libya (Grade 7) to the whole Arab homeland: one regional geography — location and strategic importance, relief, climate, vegetation, population, economic resources and transport — closed by a full unit on the Arab food problem and food security.

Libya's own way (Libya Grade 11 LY G11 Arab World): The Grade 8 Arab World survey returns at secondary depth in two parts — physical (relief treated separately for the African and Asian sides, climate factors and regions, soils, vegetation, water with its problems) and human (population structure and problems, agriculture and its difficulties, crop-by-crop chapters, livestock and sea wealth, minerals metal by metal, oil and gas, alternative energy, industry, transport). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
