# Weather and Climate — The Difference and the Elements — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Say the difference in one line.
**Answer:** Weather: narrow area, short time, changing. Climate: wide area, long time, comparatively steady.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the five elements.
**Answer:** Temperature, humidity/precipitation, water vapour, wind, and air pressure.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) What is air pressure?
**Answer:** The weight exerted by air on a unit area of the Earth's surface.
