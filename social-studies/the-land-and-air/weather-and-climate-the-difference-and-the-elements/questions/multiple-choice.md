# Weather and Climate — The Difference and the Elements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weather is the air's condition —
a) over a wide area for a long time  b) over a narrow area for a short time  c) only at night
**Answer: b) over a narrow area for a short time**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate is —
a) comparatively steady, over a wide area for a long time  b) changing every hour  c) only about rain
**Answer: a) comparatively steady, over a wide area for a long time**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How many elements make weather and climate?
a) three  b) five  c) seven
**Answer: b) five**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Sublimation is —
a) ice melting to water  b) ice changing directly into water vapour  c) rain freezing
**Answer: b) ice changing directly into water vapour**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Radiation is energy transferred by —
a) wind  b) electromagnetic waves  c) rivers
**Answer: b) electromagnetic waves**
