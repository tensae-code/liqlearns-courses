## Sub lesson 1 — Short and small against long and wide

Why do lowlanders wear white and thin clothes, highlanders dark and thick — and why does food differ between cold and hot places? Because of weather and climate. Daily weather is the air's condition over a narrow area for a short time, and it changes. Climate is the air's condition over a wide area for a long time, and it is comparatively steady.

## Sub lesson 2 — The elements that make both

The elements that make both weather and climate are the same five: temperature, humidity and precipitation, water vapour, wind, and air pressure. Weather varies and climates differ because these elements differ in amount and spread.

## Sub lesson 3 — Two transfer processes (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide's key defines two processes the elements work by: sublimation — ice changing directly into water vapour, with no liquid stage — and radiation — the transfer of energy from one body to another by electromagnetic waves; air pressure itself is the weight exerted by air on a unit area of the Earth's surface (teacher guide — evidence).
