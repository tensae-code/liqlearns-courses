Why do lowlanders wear white and thin clothes, highlanders dark and thick — and why does food differ between cold and hot places? Because of weather and climate.

The elements that make both weather and climate are the same five: temperature, humidity and precipitation, water vapour, wind, and air pressure.

The teacher guide's key defines two processes the elements work by: sublimation — ice changing directly into water vapour, with no liquid stage — and radiation — the transfer of energy from one body to another by electromagnetic waves; air pressure itself is the weight exerted by air on a unit area of the Earth's surface (teacher guide — evidence)..
