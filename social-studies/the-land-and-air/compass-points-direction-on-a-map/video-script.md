# Video script — Compass points — direction on a map

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Four cardinals: north, east, south, west.
Four intercardinals between them: north-east and its brothers.
Halve again — sixteen points on the rose.
Now any direction on the map has a name.
