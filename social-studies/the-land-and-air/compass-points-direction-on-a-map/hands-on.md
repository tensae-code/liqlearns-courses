# Hands-on — Compass points — direction on a map

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rose drawing
Draw the sixteen-point rose from memory in two passes — eight main points first, then the halving points. Check against the lesson and fix errors in another colour.

## Direction sentences
Write five direction sentences between real places on a local map, using progressively finer points: cardinal, intercardinal, secondary.
