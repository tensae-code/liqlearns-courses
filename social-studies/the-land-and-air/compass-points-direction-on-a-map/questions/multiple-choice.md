# Compass points — direction on a map — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cardinal point is —
a) north-east  b) north-north-east  c) north
**Answer: c) north**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** South-east is —
a) a secondary point  b) an intercardinal point  c) a cardinal point
**Answer: b) an intercardinal point**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The full rose has —
a) sixteen points  b) eight points  c) thirty-two points
**Answer: a) sixteen points**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Secondary points come from —
a) the legend  b) bearings  c) halving the eight again
**Answer: c) halving the eight again**
