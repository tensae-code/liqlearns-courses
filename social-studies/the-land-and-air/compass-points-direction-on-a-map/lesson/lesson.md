# Compass points — direction on a map

## Do it first

North, and the fifteen points that keep it company. Read the compass rose until any direction can be named at a glance.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cardinal and intercardinal

The four cardinal points are north, east, south and west. Between them stand the four intercardinal points: north-east, south-east, south-west and north-west. Eight points divide the circle into the directions everyday map-work uses.

## Sub lesson 2 — Secondary points

Halving again gives the secondary points — north-north-east and its fellows — completing the sixteen-point compass rose. Directions between places on a map are stated in these points: the town lies north-east of the river crossing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the compass points, and give one true direction sentence about your area.

## Do It Yourself

1. Name the four cardinals.
2. Name the four intercardinals.
3. Say how many points the full rose carries.
