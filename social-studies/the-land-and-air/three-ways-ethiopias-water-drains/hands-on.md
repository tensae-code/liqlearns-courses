# Hands-on — Three ways Ethiopia’s water drains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three arrows
On an outline map draw three fat arrows from the highlands: west, south-east, and inward to the Rift. Label each with where its water ends.

## Which way?
Name five Ethiopian rivers you know. For each, argue with a partner which of the three systems it belongs to — then check in the river lessons.
