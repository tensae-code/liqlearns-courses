# Three ways Ethiopia’s water drains — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three systems.
**Answer: Westward to the Nile; eastward/south-eastward to the Indian Ocean; inward to the Rift.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Where does the Awash end?
**Answer: In the Rift lakes of the Afar side.**
