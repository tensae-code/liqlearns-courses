# Three ways Ethiopia’s water drains — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many drainage systems does Ethiopia have?
a) two  b) five  c) three
**Answer: c) three**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The western system drains to —
a) the Rift lakes  b) the Nile and the Mediterranean  c) the Indian Ocean
**Answer: b) the Nile and the Mediterranean**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The inward system ends —
a) inside the country, in Rift lakes and closed basins  b) in the Red Sea  c) in Lake Victoria
**Answer: a) inside the country, in Rift lakes and closed basins**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The great example of the inward system is —
a) the Abbay  b) the Baro  c) the Awash
**Answer: c) the Awash**
