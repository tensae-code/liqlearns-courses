# Video script — Three ways Ethiopia’s water drains

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rain falls on the highlands. Where does it go?
West — to the Nile. East and south-east — to the Indian Ocean.
Or inward — ending in the Rift lakes, never reaching a sea.
Three ways. That is Ethiopia’s drainage.
