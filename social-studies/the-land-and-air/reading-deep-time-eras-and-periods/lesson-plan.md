# Lesson plan — Reading deep time — eras and periods

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Reading deep time — eras and periods
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air climb of the Social Studies course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** earth history divides into eras and periods — Precambrian, Palaeozoic, Mesozoic, and Cenozoic with Tertiary and Quaternary — and Ethiopia’s rocks are placed in that timescale.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on What geography is.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The timescale** — eras and periods
2. **Ethiopia in the timescale** — rocks in their eras; the guide’s event key (teacher guide — evidence)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
