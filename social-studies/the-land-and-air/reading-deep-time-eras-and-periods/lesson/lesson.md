# Reading deep time — eras and periods

## Do it first

A year is nothing in geology’s clock. Whole mountain ranges are single ticks. Read the timescale Ethiopia’s rocks are filed in.

## Sub lesson 1 — The timescale

Earth’s history is divided into eras, and eras into periods. The great eras are the Precambrian, the Palaeozoic, the Mesozoic and the Cenozoic — the last carrying the Tertiary and Quaternary periods, the Quaternary being the present age of the Rift’s active splitting.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethiopia in the timescale

Ethiopia’s geology files neatly into the scale: basement in the Precambrian, the planing in the Palaeozoic, the sea’s sediments in the Mesozoic, the uplift, lavas and Rift in the Cenozoic. The teacher guide’s activity key names the major geological events as frequent orogenic (mountain-building) movements, intensive volcanic activity and related events (teacher guide — evidence).
