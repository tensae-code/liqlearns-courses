# Reading deep time — eras and periods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The oldest era is —
a) the Mesozoic  b) the Cenozoic  c) the Precambrian
**Answer: c) the Precambrian**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tertiary and Quaternary are periods of —
a) the Precambrian  b) the Cenozoic  c) the Palaeozoic
**Answer: b) the Cenozoic**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia’s sea sediments belong to —
a) the Mesozoic  b) the Precambrian  c) the Palaeozoic
**Answer: a) the Mesozoic**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide’s key names as major events —
a) only erosion  b) only sedimentation  c) orogenic movements and intensive volcanic activity
**Answer: c) orogenic movements and intensive volcanic activity**
