# Köppen on trial — strengths and weaknesses

## Do it first

Before you read, think about this: if Köppen's system has faults, why does everyone still use it? Say what you think first, then read on and check yourself.

## Sub lesson 1 — The case against

Monthly means hide the extremes that real life feels. The system ignores sun and wind, and vegetation lags behind past climates, so the map can trail the weather. B is judged by a different rule than the rest, mountains and fog belts are missed, and the boundaries are too empirical — drawn by numbers, not by causes.

[Picture 27016 — a balance scale with the system's strengths on one side and weaknesses on the other]

## Sub lesson 2 — The case for

For the system: it is clear, quantifiable and simple enough to teach and map. Above all it ties climate to vegetation, which is what farmers, foresters and geographers actually need.

## Sub lesson 3 — The verdict

With faults named and strengths standing, Köppen's remains the system geographers use most. A good geographer uses it knowing exactly where it bends.
