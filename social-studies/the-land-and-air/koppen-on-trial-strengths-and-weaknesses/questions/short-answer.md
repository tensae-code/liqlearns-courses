# Köppen on trial — strengths and weaknesses — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two weaknesses.
**Answer:** Means hide extremes; it ignores sun and wind; vegetation lags; B judged by a different rule; mountains and fog belts missed; boundaries too empirical (any two).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two strengths.
**Answer:** Clear; quantifiable; simple; ties climate to vegetation (any two).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is the verdict of the trial?
**Answer:** Faults named, it is still the system geographers use most.
