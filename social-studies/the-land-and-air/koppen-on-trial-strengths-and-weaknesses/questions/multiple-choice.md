# Köppen on trial — strengths and weaknesses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One weakness is that means hide —
a) extremes  b) averages  c) maps
**Answer: a) extremes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The system ignores —
a) sun and wind  b) rainfall  c) temperature
**Answer: a) sun and wind**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** One strength is that the system is —
a) clear, quantifiable and simple  b) secret  c) unmeasurable
**Answer: a) clear, quantifiable and simple**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Despite its faults, the system —
a) is still the one geographers use most  b) was abandoned at once  c) is used by nobody
**Answer: a) is still the one geographers use most**
