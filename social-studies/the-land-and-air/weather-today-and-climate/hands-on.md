# Hands-on — Weather Today And Climate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 16 Weather (آب و هوا): how is the weather today — is it hot or cold, sunny or cloudy, does the wind blow or is the air calm?; air temperature and rainfall have the most important role in fixing a region's weather (climate); with what tools do we measure temperature and rainfall? — look at the thermometer: how many degrees is the air?; the tables of Yazd's average air temperature and its rainfall in the months of the year; worksheets: draw the temperature chart of Isfahan from its monthly table, and watch and record one week's weather in a table. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
