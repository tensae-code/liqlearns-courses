# Hands-on — Irans Mountains Plains And Lowlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 15 Beautiful mountains and plains (کوه‌ها و دشت‌های زیبا): Iran, a beautiful land with high mountains, wide plains (دشت‌ها) and green lowlands (جلگه‌ها) — is there a mountain or plain near your city or living place?; the highest place of a mountain is its peak (قله) — the peak of Damavand, 5,610 metres, the highest peak of Iran; the low land at the mountain's foot is the foothill (کوهپایه), and in most foothills cities and villages have come to be; label the picture (mountain range, peak, plain, foothill, river, lowland); the relief map of Iran (ناهمواری‌های ایران) showing the ups and downs in different colours; worksheet: colour the mountain ranges brown and the seas blue and write the seas' names. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
