# Points, lines and areas — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three shapes of the map model.
**Answer:** Point, line and area (polygon).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one example of each shape.
**Answer:** Point: a well (or school, airport). Line: a road (or river). Area: a lake (or park, region).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What two things define every feature?
**Answer:** Its location in a coordinate system, and its attributes.
