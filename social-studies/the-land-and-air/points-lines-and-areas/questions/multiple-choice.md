# Points, lines and areas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A well is modelled as —
a) a point  b) a line  c) an area
**Answer: a) a point**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A river is modelled as —
a) a point  b) an attribute  c) a line
**Answer: c) a line**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Every feature is defined by —
a) its name alone  b) its location in a coordinate system, plus its attributes  c) its colour alone
**Answer: b) its location in a coordinate system, plus its attributes**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A point is used when the feature's —
a) size does not matter at the map's scale  b) name is unknown  c) altitude is high
**Answer: a) size does not matter at the map's scale**
