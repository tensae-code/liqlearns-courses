# Points, lines and areas — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A lake is modelled as an area, or polygon.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Attributes fix a feature's location.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Points, lines and areas are the three shapes a map model uses.
**Answer: True**
