# Video script — Points, lines and areas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A point marks a feature whose size does not matter at the map's scale: a well, a school, an airport.

A line models a feature that runs from place to place: a road, a river, a boundary.

An area, or polygon, models a feature with extent: a lake, a park, a region.
