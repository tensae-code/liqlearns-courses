# Drawing line graphs — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Time goes on —
a) the side axis  b) the title  c) the bottom axis
**Answer: c) the bottom axis**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Points are —
a) never joined  b) plotted, then joined in order  c) joined randomly
**Answer: b) plotted, then joined in order**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The practice table is —
a) child mortality, 2000–2016  b) rainfall 2011  c) exports
**Answer: a) child mortality, 2000–2016**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The line reveals —
a) nothing  b) only errors  c) trends the table hides
**Answer: c) trends the table hides**
