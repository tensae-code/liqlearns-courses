# Hands-on — Drawing line graphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Graph the practice
Draw the line graph of any short time series you can source honestly (your school’s enrolment by year, or rainfall by month), following the steps and labelling both axes.

## Trend sentences
Write three trend sentences from your graph — direction, steepest stretch, any turning point — each provable from the line.
