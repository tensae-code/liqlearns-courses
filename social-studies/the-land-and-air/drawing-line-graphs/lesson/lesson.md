# Drawing line graphs

## Do it first

A table of years and numbers is hard to feel. A line climbing or falling is felt at once. Read the line-graph construction — a doing lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — Construction steps

To draw a line graph: choose scales for the two axes (time along the bottom, the measured value up the side); plot each year’s point above its year at its value; join the points in order. The book’s data kinds are time series: temperature, employment, and its practice table — child mortality, 2000–2016.

## Sub lesson 2 — Reading the line

The finished line speaks: rising, falling, steady, steep or gentle — trends the table hides. Statistical diagrams of this kind present large amounts of statistical data in a form the eye grasps whole (the guide’s additional-activity statement, teacher guide — evidence).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the construction steps, and read one trend from a line.
