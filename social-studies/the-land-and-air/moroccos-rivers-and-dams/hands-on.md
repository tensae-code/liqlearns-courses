# Hands-on — Morocco's Rivers and Dams

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Find the main rivers
On a map of Morocco, locate its main rivers, as the guide's lesson does. Mark where each one runs.

## River or wadi?
Write the two words — river and wadi — and under each, one line on what makes it that kind of watercourse, from the lesson. Then write one way you use water rationally at home.
