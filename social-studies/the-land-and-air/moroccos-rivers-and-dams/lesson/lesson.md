# Morocco's Rivers and Dams

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Morocco's Grade 5 teacher guide teaches the homeland's water on the land — telling a river from a wadi, locating the main rivers, what dams are for, and using water rationally. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rivers and wadis — the main rivers located

The fifth Geography lesson of Morocco's Grade 5 guide is Features of my homeland — rivers and dams. It first teaches the learner to tell a river from a wadi — two different kinds of watercourse in the homeland — and then to locate the main rivers of Morocco.

## Sub lesson 2 — What dams are for — using water rationally

The lesson goes on to the dams: what dams are for in Morocco. And it ends with the learner's own part — using water rationally, not wasting the water the rivers and dams provide.
