# Morocco's Rivers and Dams — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Morocco's Geography Lesson 5 teaches —
a) rivers and dams  b) only deserts  c) only ports
**Answer: a) rivers and dams**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson teaches the learner to tell a river from —
a) a wadi  b) a plateau  c) a forest
**Answer: a) a wadi**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The main rivers of Morocco are —
a) located on the map  b) left unnamed  c) in another country
**Answer: a) located on the map**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson ends by teaching the learner to use water —
a) rationally  b) wastefully  c) never
**Answer: a) rationally**
