Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 119–128), teaches: Bài 27: Socio-economic development in North Central Vietnam (Hue and five provinces; 11.3 million people) — farming, forestry and fisheries on its strengths (natural forest at 70% of its woods and cover above the national average; Phong Nha–Ke Bang and Pu Mat; rice in Thanh Hoa and Nghe An; the second-largest buffalo and third-largest cattle herds; 26.1% of the nation's roundwood; a growing fishery) and tourism (670 km of coast — Sam Son, Cua Lo, Thien Cam, Lang Co; Phong Nha's 300-plus caves; the Hue citadel).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
