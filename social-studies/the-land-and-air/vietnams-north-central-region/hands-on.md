## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 27: Socio-economic development in North Central Vietnam (Hue and five provinces; 11.3 million people) — farming, forestry and fisheries on its strengths (natural forest at 70% of its woods and cover above the national average; Phong Nha–Ke Bang and Pu Mat; rice in Thanh Hoa and Nghe An; the second-largest buffalo and third-largest cattle herds; 26.1% of the nation's roundwood; a growing fishery) and tourism (670 km of coast — Sam Son, Cua Lo, Thien Cam, Lang Co; Phong Nha's 300-plus caves; the Hue citadel).
