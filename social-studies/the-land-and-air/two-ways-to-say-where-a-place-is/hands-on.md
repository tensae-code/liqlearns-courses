# Hands-on — Two ways to say where a place is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Both ways
Pick your school. Write its location twice: once relatively, with three references; once absolutely, as nearly as a map or phone allows. Read both aloud — which would guide a stranger better, and why?

## Grid address game
With a partner, take turns: one gives a place’s relative location only; the other must find it on the map and then state its absolute location.
