**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Ethiopia, Guyana, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Ecuador, Malaysia, Fiji).**

Turkey's own way: a systems course — Natural Systems first (nature–human interaction, coordinates, maps, Earth's motions, climate), then Human Systems (settlements), then Regions and Countries, then Environment and Society — with a Turkey case closing most chapters (Turkey's location, Turkey's climate, settlements in Turkey). That way is stocked here as the way Turkey teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Ecuador's own way (Grade 3 book): the older SM edition is a classic skills-first book — a whole opening unit on orientation, cardinal points, the compass rose and plans before any content; every unit runs the same skeleton (content lessons, “Ser Estudiante” test, Cultura del Buen Vivir, scientific work, digital skills, unit evaluation), and the country is learned parish → canton → province → nation. That way is stocked here as the way Ecuador teaches this thing.

Searchable tags: absolute location, relative location, latitude longitude, social studies, Ecuador

Malaysia's own way (MY T1 Geografi Tingkatan 1): a pure Geography textbook organised as skills first (direction, position, sketch maps), then physical geography, human geography, a regional study of Southeast Asia, environmental issues and a full fieldwork chapter — every chapter anchored to Malaysian places first, then the region.

Fiji's own way (Fiji Year 5 Social Studies): Geography is Fiji-first — the child locates Fiji among Pacific neighbours, works on the map of Fiji's islands and divisions, and learns land care through cane farming, river-farm flooding and village land use.
