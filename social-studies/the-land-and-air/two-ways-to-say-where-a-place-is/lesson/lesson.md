# Two ways to say where a place is

## Do it first

"Ten degrees north, thirty-eight east" — or "just north of the river, next to the market". Both say where. Geography keeps both ways. Read them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Absolute location

Absolute location states a place by latitude and longitude — its fixed address on the earth’s grid. It never changes with viewpoint: a place’s coordinates are the same whoever asks, wherever they stand.

## Sub lesson 2 — Relative location

Relative location states a place by reference — to its neighbours, to land masses and water bodies: north of the river, between the highlands and the border, near the Red Sea. A geological-location variant uses large regional frames. Relative location is the way humans actually direct each other.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the two ways to say where, and give both ways for a place you both know.

## Do It Yourself

1. Define absolute location in one sentence.
2. Define relative location in one sentence.
3. Say which way never changes with viewpoint.
