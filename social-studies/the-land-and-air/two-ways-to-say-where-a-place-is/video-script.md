# Video script — Two ways to say where a place is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two ways to say where.
Absolutely: latitude and longitude — the grid address.
Relatively: north of the river, next to the market — the neighbour address.
Geography keeps both, because people need both.
