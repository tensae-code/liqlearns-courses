# Two ways to say where a place is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Absolute location is given by —
a) neighbouring towns  b) road distances  c) latitude and longitude
**Answer: c) latitude and longitude**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A place’s coordinates are —
a) only local knowledge  b) the same whoever asks  c) different from each viewpoint
**Answer: b) the same whoever asks**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Relative location refers to —
a) neighbours, land masses and water bodies  b) coordinates  c) time zones
**Answer: a) neighbours, land masses and water bodies**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** "North of the river, near the market" is —
a) absolute location  b) a grid reference  c) relative location
**Answer: c) relative location**
