Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): the year's Geography is one long chapter, "Protect the Bond between Nature and People": pupils model the world's biomes, compare world forest cover in 2000 and 2020, map Bangladesh's own forests and disaster-prone areas, and read a long inquiry-reader on biomes, sea resources and the Sundarbans mangrove.

Samoa's own way (Year 12 Geography): A full senior Geography course built on the Pacific itself — high and low island formation, island climates and ecosystems, then population of New Zealand, Asia and Samoa, and Samoan farming (bananas) beside Australian uranium mining.

Syria's own way (Syria Grade 7 Geography G7 عالم الجغرافية): a pure Geography book built entirely around activity lessons with story-style titles ("Mother of sciences", "Stones from the sky", "The origami art" for folding, "A tourist in my homeland"); technology first — aerial photos, GPS, remote sensing and GIS open the book before any physical geography — and each semester closes with student/teacher-designed lessons and an end-of-semester project.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 7 G7 CS Patria y Ciudadanía): a Civics book built on widening belonging — house, block (cuadra), community, homeland, planet — where the Geography enters as lived space: the Carta del Barrio, in which a community writes down its own geographic space (its social, cultural, historical, economic and environmental character), and a full postal address that nests house → block → parish → municipality → state → Venezuela → South America → Earth → Solar System → Milky Way.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.

Moldova's own way (Moldova Grade 8 G8 Geography): the home-country year — a full physical geography of the Republic of Moldova only (position and extreme points, structural units and minerals, relief units, climate and seasons, the Nistru/Prut river system, soils, steppe and forest-steppe zones), ending with its protected areas (national parks, scientific and landscape reserves, nature monuments) and Moldova in the context of sustainable development; hydrographic objects and protected areas are described "by algorithm" in set practicals.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 9 Geography of Armenia student): the whole grade-9 year is the home country — first the Armenian Highlands as a physical whole, then the Republic section by section (nature, population, economy), finishing marz by marz (all 10 provinces) so every learner ends by studying their own province.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 8 Geography teacher guide) (teacher guide — evidence): the guide plans the regions course around geographic problem-solving and project learning (including sample AI-assisted project plans), with assessment criteria and thematic/half-year written-work variants for every section. That evidence is stocked here as the way Armenia teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Armenia is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

That evidence is stocked here as the way Armenia teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Armenia is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

That evidence is stocked here as the way Armenia teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Armenia is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).
