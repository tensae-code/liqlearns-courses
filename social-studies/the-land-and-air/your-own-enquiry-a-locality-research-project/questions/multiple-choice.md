# Your own enquiry — a locality research project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Unit 8 is designed as a… unit
a) reading-only  b) doing  c) memorising
**Answer: b) doing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Your project studies…
a) another continent  b) your own locality  c) the capital only
**Answer: b) your own locality**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** It is done with…
a) basic tools  b) a research institute  c) satellites you own
**Answer: a) basic tools**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Within the project, GIS is used to…
a) locate your place and navigate routes  b) replace fieldwork  c) draw posters
**Answer: a) locate your place and navigate routes**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** A project's worth is measured by…
a) equipment price  b) honesty of problem, care of data, clarity of presentation  c) length
**Answer: b) honesty of problem, care of data, clarity of presentation**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The geographer's first laboratory is…
a) home ground  b) a university  c) abroad
**Answer: a) home ground**
