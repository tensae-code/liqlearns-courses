# Your own enquiry — a locality research project — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the project's working steps.
**Answer:** Choose a local problem; state it; set objectives and questions; gather data; present findings.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two GIS uses inside the project.
**Answer:** Locating your place; navigating routes to field sites.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why a locality?
**Answer:** It is the ground the learner can actually enquire into with basic tools — home ground as laboratory.
