This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
## Sub lesson 2 — Angola's way — the book's own teaching way

Angola's own way (Grade 5): Geography opens as "geographic space" — the natural space and the human-built space (Luanda port, Benguela railway, Cambambe dam, Huambo housing) — before any physical topic; Angola's own position, borders, population figures (INE) and farm/fishery tables carry the world topics.
