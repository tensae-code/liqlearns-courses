# Video script — Earthquakes — the slipping Earth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An earthquake is stored strain letting go: rock masses slip along a fault and the released energy shakes the ground as seismic waves. First, strain and sudden slip: An earthquake — a temblor — is the sudden shaking when strained rock masses slip along a fault. Then, measuring — the richter scale: The Richter scale reads from 0, no movement, to 9, extremely severe. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
