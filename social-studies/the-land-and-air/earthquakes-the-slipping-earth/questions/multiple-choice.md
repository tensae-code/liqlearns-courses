# Earthquakes — the slipping Earth — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An earthquake is…
a) the sudden shaking when strained rock slips along a fault  b) a volcanic eruption  c) a flood
**Answer: a) the sudden shaking when strained rock slips along a fault**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The released energy travels as…
a) ocean currents  b) seismic waves  c) wind
**Answer: b) seismic waves**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Most damaging quakes come from…
a) crustal ruptures on faults  b) rainfall  c) the Moon
**Answer: a) crustal ruptures on faults**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Richter scale runs from…
a) 1 to 5  b) 0 to 9  c) 0 to 100
**Answer: b) 0 to 9**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Logarithmic' means each step up…
a) adds one  b) multiplies the effect  c) halves the effect
**Answer: b) multiplies the effect**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Seismographs detect…
a) only the largest quakes  b) even tiny, near-continuous quakes  c) weather
**Answer: b) even tiny, near-continuous quakes**
