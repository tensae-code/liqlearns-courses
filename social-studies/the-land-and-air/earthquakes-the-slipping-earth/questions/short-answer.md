# Earthquakes — the slipping Earth — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happens at the moment of an earthquake?
**Answer:** Strained rock masses slip along a fault, releasing stored strain energy as seismic waves.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What instrument detects earthquakes?
**Answer:** The seismograph.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What do the Richter scale's 0 and 9 mean?
**Answer:** 0 means no movement; 9 means extremely severe.
