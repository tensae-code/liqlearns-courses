# Video script — Venezuelas Position Gateway To South America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela* (printed p. 82), teaches: Pase adelante, Venezuela es la puerta de entrada de Suramérica: Venezuela's geographic position in America and the world. Venezuela's book, the *Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela* (printed p. 22), teaches: Venezuela: país caribeño y atlántico.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 11 G11 CS Geografía de Venezuela): a final-year economic geography of Venezuela alone — population past/present/future, human development in numbers, then resource by resource (minerals, land, fisheries, forests, oil, water/hydroelectricity) each read through sovereignty, collective participation and environmental conscience, closing on Latin American union.
