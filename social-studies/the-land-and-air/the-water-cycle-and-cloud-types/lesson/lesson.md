## Sub lesson 1 — The Water Cycle and Cloud Types

Bahamas' Grade 3 student book, the *Social Science, Grade 3* (printed pp. 56–57), teaches: Clouds and the Water Cycle: how clouds form; cirrus, cumulus and stratus clouds; the water cycle — evaporation, condensation, precipitation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bahamas' way — the book's own teaching way

Bahamas' own way (Grade 3): The integrated Social Science pattern continues, but Grade 3 adds a distinct Bahamas-geography block in the middle of the book: a full map study of the archipelago (700 islands and cays, Family Islands, three sections), inter-island travel by Bahamasair, fast ferry and mailboat, and an island-by-island inventory of natural resources (Andros crabs, Abaco farming, Grand Bahama citrus and sponge), closing with Hurricane Dorian (2019) as the national extreme-weather case. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
