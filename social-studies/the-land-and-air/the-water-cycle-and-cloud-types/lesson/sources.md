**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Bahamas, Samoa, Cambodia).**

Bahamas' own way (Grade 3): The integrated Social Science pattern continues, but Grade 3 adds a distinct Bahamas-geography block in the middle of the book: a full map study of the archipelago (700 islands and cays, Family Islands, three sections), inter-island travel by Bahamasair, fast ferry and mailboat, and an island-by-island inventory of natural resources (Andros crabs, Abaco farming, Grand Bahama citrus and sponge), closing with Hurricane Dorian (2019) as the national extreme-weather case.

Searchable tags: the water cycle and cloud types, Bahamas, social studies, the-land-and-air

Samoa's own way (Year 13 Geography): The final-year course is hazard- and water-centred for an island nation — volcanic eruptions and tropical cyclones (including Cyclone Heta on Niue), the hydrological cycle and water management, then atmosphere and climate change with Pacific consequences.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 11 Earth Science): Earth Science is Cambodia's physical-geography strand — planet Earth and plate tectonics, water, a full atmosphere-and-climate course (ending with Cambodia's own weather and climate change), and the environment.
