# Saying scale three ways — and converting — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** "1 cm to 0.5 km" is a —
a) representative fraction  b) bar scale  c) statement scale
**Answer: c) statement scale**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 1:50,000 is a —
a) bar scale  b) representative fraction  c) statement scale
**Answer: b) representative fraction**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 50,000 cm equals —
a) 0.5 km  b) 5 km  c) 50 km
**Answer: a) 0.5 km**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 1:100,000 as a statement is —
a) 1 cm to 10 km  b) 1 cm to 0.1 km  c) 1 cm to 1 km
**Answer: c) 1 cm to 1 km**
