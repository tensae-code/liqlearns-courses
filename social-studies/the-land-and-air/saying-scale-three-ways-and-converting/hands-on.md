# Hands-on — Saying scale three ways — and converting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three costumes
Take one map scale and write it all three ways. Draw the bar carefully, with divided ground distances.

## Conversion drill
Convert with a partner, taking turns: 1:100,000 to a statement; "1 cm to 2 km" to an RF; check each other’s arithmetic aloud.
