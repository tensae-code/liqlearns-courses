# Video script — Saying scale three ways — and converting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Scale wears three costumes.
Words: 1 cm to 0.5 km. Fraction: 1:50,000. A drawn bar.
They are one scale — learn to change the costume.
50,000 centimetres is half a kilometre. That is the whole trick.
