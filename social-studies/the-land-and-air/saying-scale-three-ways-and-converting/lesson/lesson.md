# Saying scale three ways — and converting

## Do it first

Same scale, three costumes: words, a fraction, a bar. A map-reader must change costumes at will. Read the three ways and the conversions.

## Sub lesson 1 — The three ways

Scale is expressed three ways. The statement scale says it in words: "1 cm to 0.5 km". The representative fraction (RF) writes it as a ratio: 1:50,000. The graphic or bar scale draws it: a line divided into ground distances, against which measurements are read directly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Converting

The ways convert: an RF of 1:50,000 becomes the statement "1 cm to 0.5 km" by turning 50,000 cm into kilometres (50,000 cm = 0.5 km). The book sets RF-to-statement workout items — the conversion is a skill, practised until it is quick.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the three ways of saying scale, and convert one scale aloud.
