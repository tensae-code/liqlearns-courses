# Video script — Absolute dating — half-life clocks in rocks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Absolute age gives a number of years.

The rate is measured in half-lives.

The book tabulates the pairs scientists use: uranium-238 to lead-206, with a half-life of 4.5 billion years; potassium-40 to argon-40; and carbon-14 to nitrogen-14, with a half-life of 5,730 years — the short clock used for once-living things.
