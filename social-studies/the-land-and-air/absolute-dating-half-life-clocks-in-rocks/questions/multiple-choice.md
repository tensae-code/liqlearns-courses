# Absolute dating — half-life clocks in rocks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Absolute dating gives —
a) ages in numbers of years  b) only the order of events  c) rock colours
**Answer: a) ages in numbers of years**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In decay, the parent isotope changes into —
a) a daughter product  b) water  c) another parent
**Answer: a) a daughter product**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After two half-lives, the parent share left is —
a) a quarter  b) a half  c) nothing
**Answer: a) a quarter**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Carbon-14 decays to nitrogen-14 with a half-life of —
a) 5,730 years  b) 4.5 billion years  c) 70 years
**Answer: a) 5,730 years**
