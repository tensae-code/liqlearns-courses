# Absolute dating — half-life clocks in rocks — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After one half-life, half the parent remains.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Uranium-238 decays to argon-40.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The parent share halves at every half-life step.
**Answer: True**
