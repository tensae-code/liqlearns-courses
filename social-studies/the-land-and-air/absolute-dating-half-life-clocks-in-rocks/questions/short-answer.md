# Absolute dating — half-life clocks in rocks — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does absolute age give that relative age does not?
**Answer:** Ages in numbers of years.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After three half-lives, what share of the parent remains?
**Answer:** An eighth.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Which pair is used for once-living things?
**Answer:** Carbon-14 to nitrogen-14.
