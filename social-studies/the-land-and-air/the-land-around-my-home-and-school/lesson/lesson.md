## Sub lesson 2 — Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 1 np-g1): Geography begins as "Our Earth" at home scale — the land around the home and school, then a map of home, school and classroom; picture-led lessons ("look and talk about the pictures") with almost no text at Grade 1.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 2 np-g2): "Our Earth" grows from living place to village/town and the village in the map; economic geography is comparative — things made in the village vs things made in the city.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 3 np-g3): Grade 3 introduces Nepal's three ecological regions — Himalayan (cold, Gaurishankar), Hilly (Kathmandu valley) and Terai (flat, hot, Dhangadhi) — through three children's home places, with rivers (Tamakoshi, Narayani), landforms and climate tied to each child's village/town. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
