## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Climate differs from region to region

In Zambia, the Geography Teaching Module for Form 2 (Grade 8), Term 3, follows its first agro-ecological sub-topic with Sub-topic 2.4.2, Climate and Agro-Ecological Regions, at printed p. 40. The climate — the pattern of rain and heat over the years — is not the same across the whole country: it differs from region to region.

## Sub lesson 2 — Climate helps draw the regions

That difference is part of the division itself. An agro-ecological region is drawn where farming conditions are shared, and climate is one of those conditions: change the climate and the farming region changes with it. Zambia's module therefore ties climate to farming region by region — the learner meets a region, then asks what its climate is and how that climate marks it off from the regions beside it.
