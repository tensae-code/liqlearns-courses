# Hands-on — Climate and Agro-Ecological Regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Give each region its weather
Take the rough map of Zambia's three regions from the regions lesson, or draw a new one. Over each region draw the weather picture you think fits it — more rain, less rain, hotter, cooler — and write under the map: the climate differs from region to region.

## Ask the climate question
Pick two regions on your map. For each one write the climate question from the lesson — what is this region's climate, and how does it mark the region off from its neighbour? — and answer it in one line each from the lesson.
