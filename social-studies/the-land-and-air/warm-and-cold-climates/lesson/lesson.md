# Warm and Cold Climates

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: warm climate and cold climate, learned by sorting photographs — harvest in the heat, palm beach and lake swimming against penguins, reindeer in snow and snow play — from Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise E. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Warm climate — sorting the photographs

Exercise E of Sweden's training material teaches climate by sorting photographs under two headings: warm climate and cold climate. Warm climate takes the harvest in the heat, the palm beach and swimming in a lake.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cold climate — sorting the photographs

Cold climate takes the penguins, the reindeer in snow and playing in the snow. The pupil does not memorise a definition: the two climates are learned by deciding, picture by picture, which heading each photograph belongs under.

Sweden's own way: the textbook's Geography is fixed by doing — colouring Sweden on the globe, sorting picture cards of Swedish houses, nature types, plants and animals under the right heading, and sorting photographs under warm climate / cold climate.
