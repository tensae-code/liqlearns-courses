# Video script — Warm and Cold Climates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Exercise E of Sweden's training material teaches climate by sorting photographs under two headings: warm climate and cold climate. Warm climate takes the harvest in the heat, the palm beach and swimming in a lake.

Cold climate takes the penguins, the reindeer in snow and playing in the snow. The pupil does not memorise a definition: the two climates are learned by deciding, picture by picture, which heading each photograph belongs under.
