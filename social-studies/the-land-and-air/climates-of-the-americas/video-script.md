# Video script — Climates Of The Americas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Nicaragua's book, the *Nicaragua Grade 6 Estudios Sociales G6* (printed p. 48), teaches: El clima de América. Nicaragua's book, the *Nicaragua Grade 8 Estudios Sociales G8 full-year* (printed p. 32), teaches: Variedad climática de América; zonas climáticas de América. Nicaragua's book, the *Nicaragua Grade 8 CCSS Módulo Autoformativo G8* (printed p. 59), teaches: El clima de América.

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 6 Estudios Sociales G6): The primary cycle closes at world scale: after the planet unit, Unit II treats the whole American continent and Unit III repeats the same template — position, political division, relief, resources, hydrography, climate, risk areas, economic activities — for Europe, Asia, Africa and Oceania; the remaining five units are world and Latin American History. Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Unit IV first human groups and the Neolithic (printed pp. 98–113, History); Unit V ancient civilisations to the medieval world (printed pp. 118–134, History); Unit VI the modern world (printed pp. 140–164, History); Unit VII transformations of the 18th–19th centuries (printed pp. 168–183, History); Unit VIII the world in the 20th century (printed pp. 188–204, History).

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 8 Estudios Sociales G8 full-year): Grade 8 widens the lens to the whole American continent: five Geography units on America's territorial patrimony, physical landscape, ethnic-cultural diversity, space-and-economy and environmental protection (closing with Nicaragua's own SINAPRED disaster system), then five History units. Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Unit VI American origins (printed pp. 125–138, History); Unit VII arrival of the Europeans and colonial domination (printed pp. 145–165, History); Unit VIII emancipation processes (printed pp. 167–184, History); Unit IX changes in Latin American society (printed pp. 185–194, History); Unit X America in the 20th century (printed pp. 197–222, History).

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 8 CCSS Módulo Autoformativo G8): The distance-learning parallel of the Grade 8 full-year book: the same five Geography-of-America units rewritten for self-study, repeatedly narrowing from the continent to Centroamérica, El Caribe and Nicaragua itself (e.g. climate zones and water protection are treated region by region down to Nicaragua). Non-Geography parts named, not placed: Unit VI pre-Columbian origins (printed pp. 166–177, History); Unit VII arrival of the Europeans (printed pp. 182–201, History); Unit VIII emancipation of the indigenous peoples (printed pp. 206–220, History); Unit IX transformations after independence (printed pp. 224–246, History); Unit X America and the great changes of the 20th century (printed pp. 252–286, History).
