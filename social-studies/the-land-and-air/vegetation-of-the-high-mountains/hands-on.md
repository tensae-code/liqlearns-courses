# Hands-on — Vegetation of the high mountains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Belt ladder
Extend your zone staircase with the vegetation: write the belt that clothes each zone from Wurch down to Dega, using this and earlier lessons.

## Built for frost
List the afro-alpine’s hardships — frost, sun, thin air — and beside each, guess the plant feature that answers it; mark your guesses as guesses.
