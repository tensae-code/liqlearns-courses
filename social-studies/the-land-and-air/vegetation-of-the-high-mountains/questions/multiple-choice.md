# Vegetation of the high mountains — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The afro-alpine belt starts at about —
a) 1,800 m  b) 2,500 m  c) 3,300 m
**Answer: c) 3,300 m**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** An afro-alpine plant named is —
a) enset  b) giant lobelia  c) acacia
**Answer: b) giant lobelia**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Dry montane trees named include —
a) juniper, podocarpus and olive  b) teak and mahogany  c) palm and baobab
**Answer: a) juniper, podocarpus and olive**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Dry montane forest occupies —
a) the Rift floor  b) the Ogaden  c) the high plateaus
**Answer: c) the high plateaus**
