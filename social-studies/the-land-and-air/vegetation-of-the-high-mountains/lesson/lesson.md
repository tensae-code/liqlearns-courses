# Vegetation of the high mountains

## Do it first

Climb above the trees. The forest gives up; giant lobelias stand like green candles in the cold. Read the high-mountain belts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Afro-alpine and sub-afro-alpine

Above about 3,300 metres, the afro-alpine belt grows: giant lobelia and Erica heath, plants built for frost, fierce sun and thin air. Just below, the sub-afro-alpine grades into the forest line.

[Picture 21088 — giant lobelia moorland above a juniper forest edge]

## Sub lesson 2 — Dry montane forest and grassland

Below the alpine belts, dry montane forest and grassland complexes occupy the high plateaus: juniper, podocarpus and olive among their trees. This is the classic highland vegetation — the green of the Dega country.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the high-mountain belts, their heights and their plants.
