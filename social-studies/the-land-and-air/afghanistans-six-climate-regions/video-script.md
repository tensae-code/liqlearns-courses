The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 7 Geography (Dari)* (printed p. 29), teaches: Ch. 2 Lesson 12: the climate of Afghanistan and its six climatic regions. Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 10 Geography (Dari)* (printed p. 35), teaches: Ch. 2 Lessons 10–11: Afghanistan's climatic regions; the climate of the steppe region (same NEW lesson as the Grade 7 book's climate placement). Afghanistan's book, the *Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari)* (printed p. 97), teaches: Lesson 49: the climate of Afghanistan (same NEW lesson as the Grade 7 book's climate placement).

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 7 Geography (Dari)): the Dari MoE pattern — the world topic is taught first in the abstract (sphere, equator, latitude), then re-taught as Afghanistan's own geography (its position, relief, six climate regions, river basins, lakes, minerals, fuels), then widened to the continent (Asia). Every lesson ends with in-class group activity, questions and an out-of-class task naming the pupil's own locality.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 10 Geography (Dari)): the upper-secondary pattern changes from continent tours to themes studied in depth through Afghanistan itself: a full map-skills chapter (map types, scale, colour, projection, satellite photos), Afghanistan's climate and each of its river basins one by one (Amu, Kabul, Helmand–Sistan, Harirud, the closed basins), an environment-protection chapter aimed at Kabul's air and streets, a natural-hazards chapter, then earth-and-space and atmosphere chapters.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari)): Grade 5 social studies is an economic tour of Afghanistan for children — public services first (water, electricity, telecom, post, transport), then farming crop by crop, livestock animal by animal, industries craft by craft and factory by factory, trade and the country's (dry) ports, ending with environment, climate and mines.
