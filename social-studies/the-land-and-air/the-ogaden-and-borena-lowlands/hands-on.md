# Hands-on — The Ogaden and Borena lowlands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Slope drawing
Draw the land falling from the Bale highlands south-east into the Ogaden, with the two rivers drawn crossing the plain.

## Pastoral year
With a partner, describe a herder’s year in these lowlands: where water is found, when herds move, and why the rivers matter so much.
