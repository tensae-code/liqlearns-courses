# Video script — The Ogaden and Borena lowlands

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South-east, the highlands fall to the Ogaden.
A vast plain, sloping gently toward Somalia.
Two rivers cross it: the Wabe Shebelle and the Genale.
South lies Borena, reaching toward Kenya. Dry lands. Herding lands.
