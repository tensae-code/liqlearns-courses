# The Ogaden and Borena lowlands

## Do it first

Look south-east from the Bale highlands: the land falls away into a vast dry plain. Read the Ogaden and Borena lowlands.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Ogaden plain

South-east of the highlands lies the Ogaden plain, a vast lowland sloping gently toward Somalia. Two great river systems cross it: the Wabe Shebelle and the Genale. Away from the rivers it is dry country — pastoral land, where life follows water and grazing.

[Picture 21010 — an arid plain map with the Shebelle and Genale river systems traced]

## Sub lesson 2 — The Borena lowlands

South of the highlands, the Borena lowlands extend toward Kenya. Like the Ogaden, they are dry lowland plains where pastoralism — herding cattle across wide grazing lands — is the way of life the land itself allows.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion about the Ogaden — its slope, its two rivers — and about Borena in the south.
