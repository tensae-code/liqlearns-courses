# The Ogaden and Borena lowlands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ogaden plain slopes toward —
a) Sudan  b) Eritrea  c) Somalia
**Answer: c) Somalia**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The two river systems crossing the Ogaden are —
a) Awash and Omo  b) Wabe Shebelle and Genale  c) Abbay and Tekeze
**Answer: b) Wabe Shebelle and Genale**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Borena lowlands extend toward —
a) Kenya  b) Djibouti  c) South Sudan
**Answer: a) Kenya**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The way of life these dry lowlands support is —
a) factory work  b) fishing  c) pastoralism — herding across grazing lands
**Answer: c) pastoralism — herding across grazing lands**
