# Africa's vegetation belts — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vegetation means the plant cover — the flora — of a place.
**Answer: True** — the lesson's definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Climate has little effect on which plants grow in a place.
**Answer: False** — it is the master control.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Seeing a place's plants can tell you about its climate.
**Answer: True** — the two maps read both ways.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Biotic factors include grazing, burning and human use of the land.
**Answer: True** — living things shape the cover.
