# Africa's vegetation belts — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three factors that shape vegetation details under climate's control.
**Answer:** Landform, soils and biotic factors.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the vegetation belt order from the equator outward.
**Answer:** Rainforest, savanna grasslands, dry thorn-scrub/semi-desert belts, then sparse desert vegetation — with mountain vegetation changing by altitude on high ground.
