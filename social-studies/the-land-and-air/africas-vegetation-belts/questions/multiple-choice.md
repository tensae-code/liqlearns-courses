# Africa's vegetation belts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Africa's vegetation map closely matches —
a) its population map  b) its climate map  c) its roads map  d) its languages map
**Answer: b) its climate map** — climate is the master control.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The master control of vegetation distribution is —
a) soils  b) climate  c) landform  d) biotic factors
**Answer: b) climate** — the other three shape the details.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT one of the three detail-shaping factors?
a) Landform  b) Soils  c) Biotic factors  d) Ocean tides
**Answer: d) Ocean tides** — landform, soils and biotic factors shape the details.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Where climate gives rain all year, the vegetation is —
a) desert plants  b) rainforest  c) thorn scrub  d) bare ground
**Answer: b) rainforest** — dense and evergreen.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Mountain vegetation —
a) is the same at every height  b) changes with altitude, belt above belt  c) does not exist in Africa  d) is all desert
**Answer: b) changes with altitude, belt above belt** — landform shaping the climate pattern on one slope.
