# Hands-on — Africa's vegetation belts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-map match
Draw Africa twice. Colour one with the six climate types, the other with vegetation belts, using matching colours for matching belts. Draw lines joining each climate to its vegetation twin.

## Read the plants
Walk outside and list every plant type you can see in ten minutes. For each, write what it tells you about local water — plenty, seasonal, or scarce. Compare with a partner's list.
