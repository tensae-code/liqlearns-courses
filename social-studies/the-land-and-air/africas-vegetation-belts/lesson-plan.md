# Lesson plan — Africa's vegetation belts

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Africa's vegetation belts
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 3 — Natural Resource Base of Africa, Grade 10 (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Africa's vegetation is distributed in belts that closely match its climate map — climate is the master control, with landform, soils and living (biotic) factors shaping the details — from rainforest at the equator to desert plants at the dry edges.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Africa's climate types and the belt lessons — this lesson gathers them into the vegetation map.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
1. **Vegetation follows climate — the master control** — Two maps that match.
2. **The belts in order** — From rainforest to desert plants.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Africa's climate types; The Savanna grasslands; Africa's rainforest and the Congo Basin. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. State that Africa's vegetation map closely matches its climate map, with climate as master control.
2. Name landform, soils and biotic factors as the shaping details.
3. Give the vegetation belts in order and predict plants from climate and climate from plants.
