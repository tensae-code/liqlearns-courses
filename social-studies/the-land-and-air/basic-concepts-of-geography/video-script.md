Dominican Republic's Grade 6 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Sexto Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 14), teaches: The discipline of Geography: its basic concepts — territory, region, landscape. Dominican Republic's Grade 3 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Tercer Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 14), teaches: Geographic space: a space to enjoy. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 6): The Libro Abierto primary books open with two full geography units before any history: geography as a science first (its concepts, principles and helper sciences), then a continent-by-continent tour that always pairs physical aspects with population and culture — and the year's history closes with the country's own lived geography, Hurricane Georges over the Dominican Republic. Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 3): For third graders geography is the home island first: the planet and its components, then the Antilles and the relief of their own country, then countryside versus city, and finally where their own families came from — migration and a family census the children run themselves. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
