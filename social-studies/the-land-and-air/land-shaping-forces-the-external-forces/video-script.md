External forces come from outside the Earth and work mainly through climate.

They draw their energy from the Sun, and they work through moving agents: running water, wind, glaciers, ocean waves and groundwater.

Their main work is the opposite of the internal forces': levelling the relief the internal forces built — carrying worn material from high ground to low ground over millions of years.
