## Sub lesson 1 — Forces that come from outside

External forces come from outside the Earth and work mainly through climate. They wear rock away, carry it, and lay it down — together they are the gradational forces.

[Picture 11466 — high rough ground being worn and carried to a low flat side, the five agents labelled]

## Sub lesson 2 — Sun-powered, five agents

They draw their energy from the Sun, and they work through moving agents: running water, wind, glaciers, ocean waves and groundwater. If it moves on the surface, it is probably wearing, carrying or laying down rock.

## Sub lesson 3 — Levelling what the internal forces built

Their main work is the opposite of the internal forces': levelling the relief the internal forces built — carrying worn material from high ground to low ground over millions of years. The three processes are denudation, erosion and deposition, the subjects of the next lessons.
