# Land-Shaping Forces — The External Forces — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things do external forces do to rock?
**Answer:** Wear it away, carry it, and lay it down.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the five agents.
**Answer:** Running water, wind, glaciers, ocean waves and groundwater.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the three processes of the external forces.
**Answer:** Denudation, erosion and deposition.
