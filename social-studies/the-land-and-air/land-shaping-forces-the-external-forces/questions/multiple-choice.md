# Land-Shaping Forces — The External Forces — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** External forces come from —
a) inside the Earth  b) outside the Earth, working through climate  c) the Moon
**Answer: b) outside the Earth, working through climate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** External forces draw their energy from —
a) radioactivity  b) the Sun  c) the Earth's core
**Answer: b) the Sun**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is NOT one of the five agents?
a) glaciers  b) ocean waves  c) magma
**Answer: c) magma**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The main work of the external forces is —
a) building relief  b) levelling the relief  c) heating the crust
**Answer: b) levelling the relief**
