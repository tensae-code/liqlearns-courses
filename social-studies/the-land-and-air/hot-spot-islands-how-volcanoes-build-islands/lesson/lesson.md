## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Hot spots

Micronesia's Grade 9 textbook, Over Seas and Time, opens with the formation of the islands themselves. In Chapter 1 it teaches hot spots as part of how Micronesia's islands were made — a hot spot is the place the chapter uses to explain where the volcanic building of an island comes from.

## Sub lesson 2 — The volcanic high islands

The chapter names the volcanic high islands that hot spots built: Kosrae, Pohnpei and Chuuk. These are high islands — land that rises high out of the sea because volcanoes built it up — set against the low coral atolls the same chapter teaches next.
