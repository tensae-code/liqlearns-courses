# Hot-Spot Islands — How Volcanoes Build Islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Chapter 1, hot spots are part of explaining —
a) how Micronesia's islands were made  b) only the weather  c) only trade
**Answer: a) how Micronesia's islands were made**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A hot spot helps build —
a) a volcanic island  b) a river  c) a desert
**Answer: a) a volcanic island**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these does the chapter name as a volcanic high island? —
a) Kosrae  b) a low atoll with no name  c) a continent
**Answer: a) Kosrae**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The chapter's volcanic high islands are —
a) Kosrae, Pohnpei and Chuuk  b) only one island  c) no islands at all
**Answer: a) Kosrae, Pohnpei and Chuuk**
