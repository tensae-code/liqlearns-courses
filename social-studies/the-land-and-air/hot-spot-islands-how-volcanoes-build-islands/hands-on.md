# Hands-on — Hot-Spot Islands — How Volcanoes Build Islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Name the high islands
Write what a hot spot does in the chapter's story: in your own words, say how a hot spot leads to a volcanic high island, and name Kosrae, Pohnpei and Chuuk as the chapter's examples.

## Compare high and low
Draw two islands side by side: a high volcanic island and a low flat island. Label the high one with Kosrae, Pohnpei or Chuuk.
