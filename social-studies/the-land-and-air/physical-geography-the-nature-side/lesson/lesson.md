# Physical geography — the nature side

## Do it first

One landscape, seven sciences looking at it: one reads its shapes, one its climate, one its plants. Read the nature side of geography.

## Sub lesson 1 — The nature side

Physical geography studies the distribution, causes and interactions of natural features: climate, landforms, soil, vegetation, drainage, water and animals. This Grade 9 course’s first three units are mostly physical geography — Ethiopia’s land, climate and natural resources.

[Picture 21067 — a landscape split into labelled natural features, one per sub-branch]

## Sub lesson 2 — The sub-branches

It splits into sub-branches, each wedded to one feature family: geomorphology (landforms), climatology (climate), biogeography (living distribution), soil geography (soils), oceanography (oceans), phytogeography (plants) and zoogeography (animals).

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion what physical geography studies, and name its sub-branches with their features.
