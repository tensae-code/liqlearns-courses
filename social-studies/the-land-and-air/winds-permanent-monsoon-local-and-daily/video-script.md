Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 11 Physical Geography* (printed p. 44), teaches: Ch. 2: Winds — the permanent winds (trade winds), monsoon winds over South-East Asia, local winds, and daily winds (land and sea breezes, mountain and valley breezes).
