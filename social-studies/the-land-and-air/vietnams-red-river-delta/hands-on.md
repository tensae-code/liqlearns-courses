# Hands-on — Vietnams Red River Delta

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 25: Socio-economic development in the Red River Delta (Hanoi, Hai Phong and four provinces; 24.75 million people at 1,034/km², the densest region) — its strengths and limits (alluvial soils and a cold winter; Quang Ninh's coal, over 90% of national reserves; the sea and the Ha Long–Cat Ba World Heritage), its industry (36.5% of national industrial output value in 2024; 41.9 million tons of coal) and its services (44.7% of regional output; the North's transport and trade hub; Tràng An tourism). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
