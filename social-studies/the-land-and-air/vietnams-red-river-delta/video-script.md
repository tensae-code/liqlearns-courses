Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 110–117), teaches: Bài 25: Socio-economic development in the Red River Delta (Hanoi, Hai Phong and four provinces; 24.75 million people at 1,034/km², the densest region) — its strengths and limits (alluvial soils and a cold winter; Quang Ninh's coal, over 90% of national reserves; the sea and the Ha Long–Cat Ba World Heritage), its industry (36.5% of national industrial output value in 2024; 41.9 million tons of coal) and its services (44.7% of regional output; the North's transport and trade hub; Tràng An tourism).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
