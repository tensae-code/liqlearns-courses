# Hands-on — Asias Mountains Plateaus And Plains

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 17: Asia's relief — the high plateaus (Anatolia, Iran, Tibet and Mongolia) and high mountains stretched from west to east (activity: show them on the map); the deltas, good farming land holding dense populations yet always in danger from sea storms, river floods and inundation; and the volcanic archipelagos risen from the sea in east and south-east Asia on the Pacific shores (Japan, the Philippines, Indonesia). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
