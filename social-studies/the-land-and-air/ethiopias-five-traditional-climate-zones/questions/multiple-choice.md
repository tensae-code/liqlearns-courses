# Ethiopia’s five traditional climate zones — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The zone system is traditional because it was built by —
a) satellite maps  b) a single scientist  c) generations of farming experience
**Answer: c) generations of farming experience**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The zones sort land by —
a) soil colour only  b) altitude and temperature together  c) rainfall only
**Answer: b) altitude and temperature together**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A practical use of the zones is —
a) choosing which crops to trust on a plot  b) naming rivers  c) measuring earthquakes
**Answer: a) choosing which crops to trust on a plot**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Modern agro-climatic mapping —
a) replaced the names entirely  b) uses no zones  c) keeps the same five names
**Answer: c) keeps the same five names**
