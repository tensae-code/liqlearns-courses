# Hands-on — Ethiopia’s five traditional climate zones

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ask the tradition
Interview an elder or farmer: which zone do they call your area, and how do they know? Write their evidence in the lesson’s words.

## Crop trust list
For your home zone, list the crops families trust there and one crop they would never risk. Explain each choice by the zone’s temperature.
