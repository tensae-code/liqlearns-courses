# Video script — Ethiopia’s five traditional climate zones

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Long before weather stations, farmers read the land.
They named its climates: Bereha, Kolla, Woina-Dega, Dega, Wurch.
The names carry generations of planting and watching.
And science kept the names — because the farmers were right.
