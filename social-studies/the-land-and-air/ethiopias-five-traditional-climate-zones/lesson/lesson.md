# Ethiopia’s five traditional climate zones

## Do it first

Ask an older farmer what zone a piece of land is, and they answer without a thermometer. That knowledge is this lesson.

## Sub lesson 1 — A traditional system

The zone names — Bereha, Kolla, Woina-Dega, Dega, Wurch — are Ethiopia’s traditional way of reading climate: not measured first by instruments, but known through generations of farming the same slopes. The zones sort land by altitude and temperature together, in words every farmer shares.

## Sub lesson 2 — Using the zones

The tradition is practical: it tells a family which crops to trust on a plot, where settlement stays healthy, and where grazing replaces ploughing. Modern agro-climatic mapping keeps the same five names because the farming knowledge inside them proved right.
