# Lesson plan — Ethiopia’s five traditional climate zones

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Ethiopia’s five traditional climate zones
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air climb of the Social Studies course (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the five zones are a traditional Ethiopian knowledge system — altitude read through generations of farming experience, still used to plan crops and settlement.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Ethiopia’s five climate zones — Bereha to Wurch (the same teaching, deepened as tradition and practice).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

2. **Using the zones** — planning with the tradition

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what makes the zones a traditional system.
2. Name the five zones again from the tradition side.
3. Give three practical uses.
