# Sea’s moderation and ocean currents

## Do it first

A coastal town and an inland town at the same latitude live different years: one mild, one swinging hot-cold. The sea is the difference. Read its moderation.

## Sub lesson 1 — Moderation

Water heats and cools slowly. Coastal places are therefore moderated by the sea: milder winters, cooler summers, smaller swings. Continental interiors, far from that hand, heat and cool faster and are drier — their climates are extreme where the coast’s is gentle.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ocean currents

Ocean currents carry warm or cold water along coasts, warming or cooling the adjacent land. A coast’s climate is thus partly written by a river in the sea that the map of currents reveals.
