# Video script — Sea’s moderation and ocean currents

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The sea heats slowly, cools slowly — and lends its calm to the coast.
Inland, far from that hand, temperatures swing and rain thins.
And currents run like rivers in the sea,
warming one coast, cooling another.
