# Hands-on — Sea’s moderation and ocean currents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two tins
Put a tin of water and a tin of dry sand in the sun for an hour (with care), then in shade. Which changed temperature faster, both ways? That is moderation, measured.

## Current map
On a world map, draw one warm and one cold current as arrows along coasts, and write the climate gift each brings its coast.
