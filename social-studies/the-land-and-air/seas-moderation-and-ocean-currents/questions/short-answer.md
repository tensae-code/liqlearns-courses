# Sea’s moderation and ocean currents — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why are coasts milder?
**Answer: Water heats and cools slowly, so the sea moderates the nearby land’s temperatures.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What do warm and cold currents each do?
**Answer: Warm currents warm their coasts; cold currents cool theirs.**
