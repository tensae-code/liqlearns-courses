# Sea’s moderation and ocean currents — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The sea heats and cools —
a) instantly  b) never  c) slowly
**Answer: c) slowly**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Coastal climates have —
a) no rain  b) smaller temperature swings  c) the largest swings
**Answer: b) smaller temperature swings**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ocean currents —
a) warm or cool adjacent coasts  b) only affect ships  c) stop at the equator
**Answer: a) warm or cool adjacent coasts**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A coast’s climate is partly written by —
a) its longitude only  b) its soils  c) the currents passing it
**Answer: c) the currents passing it**
