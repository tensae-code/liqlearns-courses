# Video script — Divergent and transform boundaries — ridges, rifts and sideways slips

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Plates that pull apart build ridges and rift valleys; plates that slide sideways store strain and let go in jerks — and underneath it all, mantle convection drags the plates. First, divergent — apart, with new crust: Where plates diverge — move apart — magma rises into the gap and builds a ridge. Then, transform — sideways, in jerks: At a transform boundary, plates slide sideways past each other. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
