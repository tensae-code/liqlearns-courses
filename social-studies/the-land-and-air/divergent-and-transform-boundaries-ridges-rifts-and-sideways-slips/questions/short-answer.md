# Divergent and transform boundaries — ridges, rifts and sideways slips — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What forms where plates diverge under the ocean?
**Answer:** A mid-ocean ridge, with sea-floor spreading and shallow earthquakes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the book's rift-valley example.
**Answer:** The East African Rift.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does a transform boundary move?
**Answer:** Sideways, in stick-slip jerks — sticking while strain builds, then slipping suddenly.
