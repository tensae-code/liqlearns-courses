# Divergent and transform boundaries — ridges, rifts and sideways slips — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At a divergent boundary, plates…
a) move apart  b) collide  c) stay still
**Answer: a) move apart**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Magma rising between separating plates builds…
a) deltas  b) mid-ocean ridges  c) caves
**Answer: b) mid-ocean ridges**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The East African Rift is…
a) a rift valley opening inside a continent  b) a mid-ocean ridge  c) a transform fault
**Answer: a) a rift valley opening inside a continent**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Transform boundaries move…
a) apart  b) together  c) sideways in stick-slip jerks
**Answer: c) sideways in stick-slip jerks**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The San Andreas fault is a…
a) rift valley  b) transform boundary  c) ridge
**Answer: b) transform boundary**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The engine dragging the plates is…
a) the wind  b) mantle convection  c) the tides
**Answer: b) mantle convection**
