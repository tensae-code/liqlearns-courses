# Irans Climate Regions

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Lesson 17 Iran's climate regions (نواحی آب و هوایی ایران): our country Iran is a vast country — some of its places mountainous, some low and of little height, and parts of it lying beside the sea; now let us meet Iran's different climate regions — the temperate and humid Caspian region (ناحیه‌ی معتدل و مرطوب خزری) on the Caspian shore, where the rain…; and the hot and dry interior region (ناحیه‌ی گرم و خشک داخلی) with hot summers and cold winters and little rainfall, each region with its climate chart; Lesson 11 The diversity of Iran's climate (تنوع آب و هوای ایران): a habitat (زیستگاه) is a large region where a set of particular plants and animals live, and climate is among the chief makers of habitats; reading climate graphs (temperature drawn as a line chart); Iran's climate regions — among them the temperate humid Caspian region (between the Alborz and the Caspian Sea, rain in every month of the year) and the hot humid southern coasts (along the Persian Gulf and the Sea of Oman, from the Khuzestan plain to the port of Chabahar, with very hot summers) — as Iran's book teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 4 Social Studies): a primary Social Studies book of 22 lessons in 6 chapters, working outward from the neighbourhood to the whole country. The Geography parts placed below are Ch. 1 Our neighbourhood (Lessons 2 and 4, printed pp. 5–23), Ch. 2 My city, my village (Lessons 5–7, printed pp. 24–37), Ch. 3 The arising of city and village (Lessons 8–9, printed pp. 38–46) and Ch. 5 My beautiful country (Lessons 15–19, printed pp. 74–98). The other parts are named here, not placed: Ch. 1 Lesson 1 Our neighbour (printed p. 2) and Lesson 3 Buying and selling in the neighbourhood (printed p. 10, Economics); Ch. 3 Lesson 10 How historians study the past (printed p. 47, History method); Ch. 4 A trip to the ancient cities (Lessons 11–14, printed pp. 51–70, History — Hamadan, Persepolis in two lessons, Kermanshah); Ch. 6 We are Iranians (Lessons 20–22, printed pp. 99–110, Civics — our national symbols, the calendar, important days).

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 7 Social Studies): one combined Social Studies book of 24 lessons in 12 chapters. The Geography parts placed below are Ch. 5 Knowing our living environment (Lessons 9–10, printed pp. 43–56), Ch. 6 Protecting Iran's habitats (Lessons 11–12, printed pp. 57–72), Ch. 7 Population and resources (Lessons 13–14, printed pp. 73–88) and Ch. 8 Tourism in Iran (Lessons 15–16, printed pp. 89–102), plus the sectors part of Ch. 4. The other parts are named here, not placed: Civics — Ch. 1 Our rights and responsibilities (Lessons 1–2, printed pp. 1–12), Ch. 2 Law (Lessons 3–4, printed pp. 13–20), Ch. 3 Coping with incidents — empathy and insurance (Lessons 5–6, printed pp. 21–30); Economics — Ch. 4 Production, distribution, consumption (Lesson 8 Consumption, printed pp. 37–42; Lesson 7 placed above); History and Culture — Ch. 9 Our land, searching the distant past (Lessons 17–18, from printed p. 103), Ch. 10 Iran in ancient times (Lessons 19–20), Ch. 11 The coming of Islam to Iran and Islamic Iran (Lessons 21–22), and Ch. 12 Culture and art (Lessons 23–24) fill the rest of the book (printed pp. 103–178).
