# Hands-on — Irans Climate Regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 17 Iran's climate regions (نواحی آب و هوایی ایران): our country Iran is a vast country — some of its places mountainous, some low and of little height, and parts of it lying beside the sea; now let us meet Iran's different climate regions — the temperate and humid Caspian region (ناحیه‌ی معتدل و مرطوب خزری) on the Caspian shore, where the rain…; and the hot and dry interior region (ناحیه‌ی گرم و خشک داخلی) with hot summers and cold winters and little rainfall, each region with its climate chart; Lesson 11 The diversity of Iran's climate (تنوع آب و هوای ایران): a habitat (زیستگاه) is a large region where a set of particular plants and animals live, and climate is among the chief makers of habitats; reading climate graphs (temperature drawn as a line chart); Iran's climate regions — among them the temperate humid Caspian region (between the Alborz and the Caspian Sea, rain in every month of the year) and the hot humid southern coasts (along the Persian Gulf and the Sea of Oman, from the Khuzestan plain to the port of Chabahar, with very hot summers). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
