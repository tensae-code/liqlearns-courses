# Weather and Climate — The Controllers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six controllers.
**Answer:** Latitude, altitude, ocean currents, distance from the sea, wind and air pressure, and mountain barriers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does altitude relate to precipitation?
**Answer:** Moist air forced up a mountain side cools and drops its rain.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What do ocean currents carry along coasts?
**Answer:** Warmth or cold.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Why is a slanting sunbeam weaker?
**Answer:** It spreads over more ground, so each part gets less heat.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Name the three forces that deflect or slow winds.
**Answer:** Coriolis effect, centrifugal acceleration, surface friction.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Work the first kilometre of the Ras Dejen sum.
**Answer:** 30 − 6.4 = 23.6 °C after one kilometre.
