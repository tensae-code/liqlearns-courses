# Weather and Climate — The Controllers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which list names controllers, not elements?
a) temperature, precipitation, wind  b) latitude, altitude, ocean currents  c) water vapour, air pressure
**Answer: b) latitude, altitude, ocean currents**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Latitude controls temperature because —
a) the nearer the equator, the more direct the Sun  b) mountains block wind  c) the sea is near
**Answer: a) the nearer the equator, the more direct the Sun**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** As altitude rises, temperature —
a) rises  b) falls — high land is colder  c) never changes
**Answer: b) falls — high land is colder**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A mountain barrier makes —
a) both sides equally wet  b) a wet windward side and a dry sheltered side  c) no rain anywhere
**Answer: b) a wet windward side and a dry sheltered side**
