## Sub lesson 2 — Latitude and altitude at work

Latitude controls temperature: the nearer the equator, the more direct the Sun, the hotter the place. Altitude works the other way: the higher the land, the colder it is — and altitude also relates to precipitation, as moist air forced up a mountain side cools and drops its rain.

## Sub lesson 3 — Sea, currents and mountains

Distance from the sea softens or sharpens a place's temperatures; ocean currents carry warmth or cold along coasts; and mountain barriers block winds, making a wet windward side and a dry sheltered side.
