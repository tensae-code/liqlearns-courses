Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 5): Three geography units climb from the planet to the country: general geography and the American continent's location, then how climate varies across the continents — with climographs the children draw and read themselves — then the Dominican Republic's own climate, activities and population, ending with a “discovering the geography of my country” field activity.

Papua New Guinea's own way (Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG) (teacher guide — evidence): at secondary level Social Science splits into five strands — Geography, History, Political Science, Economics, Environment. Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 10 SD Geography 10): A second-edition foundations book (the preface says necessity forced this 2nd edition): physical geography first (universe to rivers), then climate and vegetation, then a long practical cartography unit, closing with GIS — geography taught as a skills subject, with an activity (نشاط) box on most spreads.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 9 G9 Geography Venezuela): a full national geography written as Geohistoria — every physical topic (position, climate, relief, hydrography) is paired with the historical stages that organised the same space (agrarian Venezuela, oil Venezuela, 21st-century Venezuela), and the Amazon rainforest closes the book as a defence-of-life argument.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 7 guide-geo-1as) (teacher guide — evidence): A competency-based guide (IPN 2025 reform) that fixes the whole Grade 7 course in three units — Earth and its main relief, Climate, Population and urbanisation — each unit ending in a class project, and every general topic anchored to Mauritania itself: the country's astronomical location, its relief map and Atlantic coast, rainfall differences between its wilayas, its age pyramid, and the growth of Nouakchott. Sample lesson plans (fiches) are given for one lesson per unit, plus model term exams with answers. That evidence is stocked here as the way Mauritania teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Mauritania is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 7 geo-1as-m): The student book of the course specified in Book 1 (the teacher guide): the same three units and 13 lessons, each lesson opening with expected outcomes and key concepts and closing with a concepts list per unit. General topics are worked with Mauritanian examples throughout, as the guide prescribes.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 11 geo-5as-lm): The senior Letters cycle re-runs physical geography at full depth in four units — Planet Earth, Climate, the Effects of Climate, the Earth (structure, erosion, coasts) — with a class project closing Units 1 and 4.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 10 Geography (Dari)): the upper-secondary pattern changes from continent tours to themes studied in depth through Afghanistan itself: a full map-skills chapter (map types, scale, colour, projection, satellite photos), Afghanistan's climate and each of its river basins one by one (Amu, Kabul, Helmand–Sistan, Harirud, the closed basins), an environment-protection chapter aimed at Kabul's air and streets, a natural-hazards chapter, then earth-and-space and atmosphere chapters.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 4 SS4): one combined Social Studies book in Urdu where the Geography chapter (Ch.5, printed pp. 61–93) works entirely through Pakistan's own map, landforms, weather and census, with BOLTS as the map-elements mnemonic and "I have learnt!" / "Work for me to do" boxes closing every topic.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6): world physical Geography taught through Pakistan's own land — its fault lines and 1935/2005 earthquakes, Khewra salt and Thar coal among the rocks, K2 and the Karakoram among the mountains, Pakistan's four climatic regions, its six forest types from mangrove to alpine, and the Indus flood as the country's defining disaster.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 10 np-g10): Grade 10 climate is taught by world climate types and the three heat zones (tropical, temperate, cold), then continent studies of North America, South America and Africa, earthquakes, map work and modern map technology (GIS); population closes the book — Nepal's size/growth, structure, distribution, family planning and the population–environment interrelation.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 1): distance-learning self-study module — every lesson follows the same rhythm (introduction, development with worked examples, exercises, answer key, lesson summary); the four Earth spheres are the organising spine of the whole module, each sphere closed by a "use, protection and conservation" lesson; local anchors throughout (Maputo temperature worked example, Zambezi delta, Moma/Namialo weather contrast).

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 10 laos-g10-geography-teacher): a second world-geography pass at a higher level — Earth and map projection first, then life in the climate zones (hot, temperate, cold, mountain/coastal), population characteristics and problems, humans and environment, and a distinctive closing block on how geographic space is organised (agricultural areas, industrial areas, tourism areas, rural and urban geography).

Laos's own way (Laos Grade 10 laos-g10-geography-student): the student twin of Book 6 — same 6 parts / 20 lessons with full illustrated text, opening with a concept map of the whole year (Earth, life zones, population and quality of life, humans and environment, organisation of geographic space, rural and urban geography).
