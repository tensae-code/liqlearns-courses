Latitude controls temperature: the nearer the equator, the more direct the Sun, the hotter the place.

Distance from the sea softens or sharpens a place's temperatures; ocean currents carry warmth or cold along coasts; and mountain barriers block winds, making a wet windward side and a dry sheltered side.
