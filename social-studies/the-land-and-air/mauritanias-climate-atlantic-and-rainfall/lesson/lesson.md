## Sub lesson 2 — Mauritania's way — the book's own teaching way

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 7 guide-geo-1as) (teacher guide — evidence): A competency-based guide (IPN 2025 reform) that fixes the whole Grade 7 course in three units — Earth and its main relief, Climate, Population and urbanisation — each unit ending in a class project, and every general topic anchored to Mauritania itself: the country's astronomical location, its relief map and Atlantic coast, rainfall differences between its wilayas, its age pyramid, and the growth of Nouakchott. Sample lesson plans (fiches) are given for one lesson per unit, plus model term exams with answers. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Mauritania is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 5 geo-5af-m): The whole book is Mauritania itself, in four "I get to know" axes: my country's location and borders, my country's climate, my society, my country's wealth. Climate is taught through the country's own three wind types and the effect of rain on where people, animals and plants live; farming includes the flood-recession farming of the Chamama plain after the Senegal River falls.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 9 geo-3as-m): A home-and-region year in three units: the Mauritanian geographic space (the country set astronomically between 15°–27° N and 5°–17° W, area about 1,030,700 km²), Mauritania's economic activities, then the two spaces Mauritania belongs to — the Maghreb and West Africa — ending on sub-regional economic integration.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 10 geo-4as-m): This book re-teaches the Grade 9 programme lesson-for-lesson (its contents list is identical to Book 8's) at greater depth and with fuller map work — its Mauritania unit works through three location maps (in the Arab world, geographic and astronomical location) where the Grade 9 book works from text. Filed as its own book because it is a distinct, longer edition (116 pp vs 104 pp), not a duplicate file.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 13 geo-7as-lm): The final-year Letters book is an exam-facing synthesis: Mauritania's natural and human features and its economic activities in full (including the structural problems of the Mauritanian economy and a lesson on how to write a geography essay), then the Arab world and Africa as economic powers (oil and minerals as the lenses), closing with the two great powers — the American and Chinese economies, their foundations, features and problems. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
