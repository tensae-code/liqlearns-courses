# Hands-on — Pakistans Forests

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Types of forests in Pakistan: coniferous and alpine forests of the north (deodar, fir, blue pine, spruce, chilghoza, juniper, chir), piedmont forests, tropical thorn forests (Quetta, Kalat, Zhob, Sibbi), riverine forests (shisham, babul), irrigated plantations (Changa Manga) and coastal mangroves; why Pakistan's forests differ by region. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
