Pakistan's book, the *Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6* (printed pp. 53–55), teaches: Types of forests in Pakistan: coniferous and alpine forests of the north (deodar, fir, blue pine, spruce, chilghoza, juniper, chir), piedmont forests, tropical thorn forests (Quetta, Kalat, Zhob, Sibbi), riverine forests (shisham, babul), irrigated plantations (Changa Manga) and coastal mangroves; why Pakistan's forests differ by region.

Pakistan's own way (Pakistan Grade 6 Geography 6): world physical Geography taught through Pakistan's own land — its fault lines and 1935/2005 earthquakes, Khewra salt and Thar coal among the rocks, K2 and the Karakoram among the mountains, Pakistan's four climatic regions, its six forest types from mangrove to alpine, and the Indus flood as the country's defining disaster.
