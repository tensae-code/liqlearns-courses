# Locating by latitude and longitude — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Parallels measure —
a) east–west from Greenwich  b) depth  c) north–south from the equator
**Answer: c) north–south from the equator**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Meridians run —
a) around the equator only  b) pole to pole  c) east–west
**Answer: b) pole to pole**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The reading order is —
a) latitude, then longitude  b) longitude, then latitude  c) either order
**Answer: a) latitude, then longitude**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Point C is fixed at —
a) 75°N, 10°W  b) 10°S, 75°E  c) 10°N, 75°W
**Answer: c) 10°N, 75°W**
