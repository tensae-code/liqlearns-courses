# Hands-on — Locating by latitude and longitude

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Grid fixing
On a graticule map, fix three marked points by the book’s method and write each in the full form — degrees plus hemisphere letters.

## Address Ethiopia
Fix Addis Ababa and one border town from an atlas graticule, and check both against the grid lesson’s box for Ethiopia (3–15°N, 33–48°E).
