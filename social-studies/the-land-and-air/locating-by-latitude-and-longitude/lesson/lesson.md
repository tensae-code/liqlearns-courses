# Locating by latitude and longitude

## Do it first

Every point on earth has one address in degrees. Read the graticule and fix a point the book’s way: latitude first, then longitude.

## Sub lesson 1 — Parallels and meridians

The geographic grid — the graticule — is woven of parallels (lines of latitude, running east–west, measuring north–south from the equator) and meridians (lines of longitude, running pole to pole, measuring east–west from Greenwich). A place sits where its parallel and meridian cross.

## Sub lesson 2 — The worked example

Read latitude first, then longitude. The book’s worked example follows point C’s parallel to 10°N and its meridian to 75°W, and fixes the point as 10°N, 75°W — always in that order, latitude before longitude, with the hemisphere letters attached.
