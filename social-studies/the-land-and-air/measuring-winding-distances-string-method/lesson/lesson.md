# Measuring winding distances — string method

## Do it first

A river will not lie straight under a ruler. But a thread will follow it anywhere. Read the string method — the book’s worked example makes 8.4 cm into 4.2 km.

## Sub lesson 1 — Trace the curve

Lay a thread or string along the curved feature — road, railway, river or coast — following every bend; or step along the curve with dividers in near-straight portions. Mark where the thread ends on the feature.

## Sub lesson 2 — Straighten and convert

Straighten the thread against a ruler and read its length; or sum the divider portions. Then convert by the scale. The book’s worked example: 8.4 cm at 1:50,000 = 4.2 km on the ground.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the string method, ending with the worked example from memory.
