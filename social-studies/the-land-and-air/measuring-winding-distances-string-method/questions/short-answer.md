# Measuring winding distances — string method — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe the tracing step.
**Answer: Lay thread along the curve following every bend (or step dividers along it), marking the end.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Recite the worked example.
**Answer: 8.4 cm on the map at 1:50,000 = 4.2 km on the ground.**
