# Measuring winding distances — string method — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Winding features are measured with —
a) a straight ruler alone  b) the compass rose  c) thread or dividers
**Answer: c) thread or dividers**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Features measured this way include —
a) only borders  b) rivers and coasts  c) only roads
**Answer: b) rivers and coasts**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After tracing, the thread is —
a) straightened against a ruler and read  b) burned  c) tied
**Answer: a) straightened against a ruler and read**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The worked example’s map length is —
a) 4.2 cm  b) 10 cm  c) 8.4 cm
**Answer: c) 8.4 cm**
