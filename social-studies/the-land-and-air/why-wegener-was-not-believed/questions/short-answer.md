# Why Wegener was not believed — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give two of the four reasons drift was rejected.
**Answer:** Any two of: Wegener was not a geologist by training; the influential geoscientists were northern while his data were southern; his late Cenozoic breakup timing was implausible; he offered no mechanism and no direct evidence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did the missing mechanism matter so much?
**Answer:** Because nothing in the theory could explain what force could move a whole continent.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is drift's legacy in one line?
**Answer:** The paradigm shift from fixism to mobilism — the idea that continents move at all.
