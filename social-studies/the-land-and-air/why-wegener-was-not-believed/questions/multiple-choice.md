# Why Wegener was not believed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One reason Wegener was doubted was that he…
a) was not a geologist by training  b) had no map  c) never published
**Answer: a) was not a geologist by training**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** His data came mainly from…
a) the northern continents  b) the southern continents  c) the oceans only
**Answer: b) the southern continents**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The influential geoscientists of his day were mostly…
a) southern  b) northern  c) Ethiopian
**Answer: b) northern**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Wegener's breakup timing was doubted because he placed it…
a) too early  b) late, in the Cenozoic  c) exactly right
**Answer: b) late, in the Cenozoic**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** What could Wegener not offer?
a) a mechanism and direct evidence  b) a book title  c) a fossil name
**Answer: a) a mechanism and direct evidence**

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The shift from fixism to mobilism means…
a) from fixed continents to moving continents  b) from maps to globes  c) from land to sea
**Answer: a) from fixed continents to moving continents**
