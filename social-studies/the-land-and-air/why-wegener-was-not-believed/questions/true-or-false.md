# Why Wegener was not believed — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Wegener was a trained geologist, which is why he was trusted.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Wegener had a clear mechanism that could move continents.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mobilism is the idea that continents move.
**Answer: True**
