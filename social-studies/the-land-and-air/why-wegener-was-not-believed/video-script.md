# Video script — Why Wegener was not believed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A true idea can still be refused — Wegener's drift was rejected for who he was, where his data came from, his timing, and his missing mechanism. First, the man and the map of science: The first reason drift was refused was about the man: Wegener was not a geologist by training, and the geologists of his day did not trust an outsider's theory about their own science. Then, the timing and the missing engine: The third reason was his timing: Wegener placed the breakup late, in the Cenozoic, and that late timing was implausible against the evidence of much older separations. Hold those two parts together and you hold the lesson.
