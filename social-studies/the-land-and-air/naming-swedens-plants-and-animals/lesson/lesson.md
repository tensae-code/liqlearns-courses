# Naming Sweden's Plants and Animals

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: naming Sweden's plants and animals and sorting them by where they live — forest, farmed land, coast and sea, rivers and lakes, and fells — from Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise D. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Forest and farmed land — naming what lives there

Exercise D of Sweden's training material names the plants and animals and sorts them by environment. Forest: squirrel, pine, blackbird, ant, moss, spruce. Farmed land: wheat, potato, onion, beetroot, carrots, cow, pig.

## Sub lesson 2 — Coast and sea, rivers and lakes, and the fells

Coast and sea: reed, perch, seaweed, gull, swan, herring. Rivers and lakes: swan, ducks. Fells: reindeer, cloudberry. The name is learned together with the place it lives — that joining is the lesson.

Sweden's own way: the textbook's Geography is fixed by doing — colouring Sweden on the globe, sorting picture cards of Swedish houses, nature types, plants and animals under the right heading, and sorting photographs under warm climate / cold climate.
