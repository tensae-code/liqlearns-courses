**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —

**Answer: a) naming Sweden's plants and animals and sorting them by where they live — forest, farmed land, coast and sea, rivers and lakes, and fells — from Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise D**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
