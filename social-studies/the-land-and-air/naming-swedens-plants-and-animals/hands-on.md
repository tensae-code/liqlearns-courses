# Hands-on — Naming Sweden's Plants and Animals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the name cards
Write the lesson's plant and animal names on cards and sort them under the headings: forest, farmed land, coast and sea, rivers and lakes, fells.

## One name, one place
Pick five names and for each say its place and one thing that ties it to that place, from the lesson alone.
