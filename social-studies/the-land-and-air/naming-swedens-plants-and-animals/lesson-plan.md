# Lesson plan — Naming Sweden's Plants and Animals

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** Naming Sweden's Plants and Animals
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Sweden, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Naming Sweden's plants and animals and sorting them by where they live — forest, farmed land, coast and sea, rivers and lakes, and fells — from Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise D.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — naming Sweden's plants and animals and sorting them by where they live — forest, farmed land, coast and sea, rivers and lakes, and fells — from Sweden's Grade 1 training material, Exercise D.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
