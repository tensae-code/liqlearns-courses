**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Vietnam, Jordan, Iraq).**

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Địa lí 10): a single Grade 10 course that runs the whole discipline — map craft, the Earth's spheres, then economic geography sector by sector — with Vietnam itself as the standing worked example (its power-plant and rice maps, the Red River's regime at Hanoi, Hanoi's climate chart, Vietnam's GDP structure, its internet and tourism figures) inside world-scale treatment.

Searchable tags: the biosphere, Vietnam, social studies, the-land-and-air

Jordan's own way (Jordan G10 Geography T1): Grade 10 Geography opens with physical geography (atmosphere, biosphere, biodiversity), then tourism and transport as Jordanian economic geography, then geographic technologies.

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 11 Physical Geography): a pure physical-geography course in six chapters — landforms, weather and climate, water, the biosphere, soil, and the environment — with Iraqi anchors kept throughout (flood visits to Iraq's rivers, the hills of northern Iraq, groundwater and the desert caravans, soil salinity in the alluvial plain).
