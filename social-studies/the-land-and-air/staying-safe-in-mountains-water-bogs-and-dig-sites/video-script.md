# Video script — Staying safe in mountains, water, bogs and dig sites

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mountains: loose rock, fast weather. Pack the safety rucksack.
Water: no dubious drinking, no bare feet, never past the knee.
Bogs: probe ahead with a pole — the green carpet can be water.
Dig sites: sand collapses. Respect every terrain’s rule.
