# Hands-on — Staying safe in mountains, water, bogs and dig sites

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pack the rucksack
Assemble (or list and draw) the safety rucksack: first-aid kit, compass, whistle, torch, survival bags, extra food. For each item, name the mountain moment it answers.

## Rule cards
Make four terrain rule cards — mountain, water, bog, dig site — each with its three sharpest rules, for the class fieldwork box.
