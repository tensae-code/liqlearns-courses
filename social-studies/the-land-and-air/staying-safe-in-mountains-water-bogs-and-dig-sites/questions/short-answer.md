# Staying safe in mountains, water, bogs and dig sites — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three mountain hazards.
**Answer: Steep/loose rock, ice/snow, fast weather change (any three).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What carpets can hide water in bogs?
**Answer: Sphagnum and peat carpets, and vegetation rafts.**
