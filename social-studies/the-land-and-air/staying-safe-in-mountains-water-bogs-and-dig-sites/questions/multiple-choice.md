# Staying safe in mountains, water, bogs and dig sites — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The safety rucksack holds —
a) only food and water  b) a map only  c) first-aid kit, compass, whistle, torch, survival bags, extra food
**Answer: c) first-aid kit, compass, whistle, torch, survival bags, extra food**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In water, never wade deeper than —
a) neck-deep  b) knee-deep  c) waist-deep
**Answer: b) knee-deep**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In a bog, you should —
a) probe ahead with a pole  b) run across quickly  c) follow the greenest carpet
**Answer: a) probe ahead with a pole**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In excavations, the less stable material is —
a) clay  b) solid rock  c) sand, far less stable than clay
**Answer: c) sand, far less stable than clay**
