# Video script — The Nile-bound rivers — Abbay, Tekeze, Baro

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Three great rivers run west.
The Abbay, from Lake Tana, through its gorge.
The Tekeze, from the northern highlands.
The Baro, from the wet south-west. All Nile-bound.
