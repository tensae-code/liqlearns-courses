# The Nile-bound rivers — Abbay, Tekeze, Baro

## Do it first

Name Ethiopia’s most famous river. Now name two more that end in the same great river. Read the Nile-bound three.

## Sub lesson 1 — Abbay and Tekeze

The Abbay — the Blue Nile — rises around Lake Tana and cuts a great gorge westward out of the highlands. North of it, the Tekeze drains the northern highlands, also westward. Together they carry a large share of the water Ethiopia sends to the Nile.

## Sub lesson 2 — Baro-Akobo

In the south-west, the Baro-Akobo system drains the wettest highlands westward across the western lowlands. The three Nile-bound systems together — Abbay, Tekeze and Baro-Akobo — carry most of Ethiopia’s runoff volume out of the country to the west.
