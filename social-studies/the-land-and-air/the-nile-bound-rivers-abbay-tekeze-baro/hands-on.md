# Hands-on — The Nile-bound rivers — Abbay, Tekeze, Baro

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Trace the three
On an outline map, draw the Abbay, Tekeze and Baro flowing west. Mark Lake Tana at the Abbay’s start.

## Gorge profile
Draw the Abbay’s path in side view: lake, plateau, deep gorge, lowland, border. Say what happens to the river at each part.
