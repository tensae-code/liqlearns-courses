# The Nile-bound rivers — Abbay, Tekeze, Baro — short answer

Answer in a short sentence.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Which way do all three Nile-bound rivers flow?
**Answer: Westward, out of the highlands toward the Nile.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three Nile-bound systems.
**Answer: Abbay, Tekeze and Baro-Akobo.**
