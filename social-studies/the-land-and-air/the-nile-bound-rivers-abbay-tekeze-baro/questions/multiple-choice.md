# The Nile-bound rivers — Abbay, Tekeze, Baro — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Abbay is also called —
a) the White Nile  b) the Great River  c) the Blue Nile
**Answer: c) the Blue Nile**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Abbay rises around —
a) Lake Turkana  b) Lake Tana  c) Lake Ziway
**Answer: b) Lake Tana**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The south-western Nile-bound system is —
a) Baro-Akobo  b) Genale-Dawa  c) Wabe Shebelle
**Answer: a) Baro-Akobo**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Together the three systems carry —
a) only a small share  b) no water in the dry season  c) most of Ethiopia’s runoff volume
**Answer: c) most of Ethiopia’s runoff volume**
