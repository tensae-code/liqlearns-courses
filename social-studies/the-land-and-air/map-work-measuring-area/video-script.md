# Video script — Map Work — Measuring Area

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Area on a map takes three steps.

Step one: turn the scale into words. 1 centimetre to 2 kilometres.

Step two: turn map lengths into ground lengths.
A rectangle, 4 centimetres by 2 centimetres,
becomes 8 kilometres by 4 kilometres.

Step three: use the formula. Length times width.
8 times 4 is 32. The ground area is 32 square kilometres.

A circle: radius 3 centimetres becomes 6 kilometres.
Pi times r squared: 3.14 times 36 is 113 square kilometres.

Measure your own house plot the same way.
