## Sub lesson 1 — Regular shapes have formulas

Squares, rectangles, circles and triangles have their own area formulas: a square is side × side (side²); a rectangle is length × width; a circle is πr² with π = 3.14; a triangle is ½ × base × height. This lesson works only with such regular shapes.

## Sub lesson 2 — The book's method, step by step

The method runs in steps: first convert the scale to a statement scale; then convert each measured map length to its ground length; then apply the formula. The order matters — never put map centimetres into a formula and call the answer square kilometres.

## Sub lesson 3 — Two worked examples — and your plot

A rectangle 4 cm × 2 cm at 1 cm to 2 km becomes 8 km × 4 km = 32 km². A circle of radius 3 cm becomes radius 6 km, and πr² = 3.14 × 36 = 113 km². Your task: measure your own house plot's sides and find its area the same way.
