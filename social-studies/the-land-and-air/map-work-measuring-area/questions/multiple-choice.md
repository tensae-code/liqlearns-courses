# Map Work — Measuring Area — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The area of a circle is —
a) length × width  b) πr², with π = 3.14  c) ½ × base × height
**Answer: b) πr², with π = 3.14**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The area of a triangle is —
a) side²  b) πr²  c) ½ × base × height
**Answer: c) ½ × base × height**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's method starts by —
a) applying the formula  b) converting the scale to a statement scale  c) measuring the plot
**Answer: b) converting the scale to a statement scale**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A 4 cm × 2 cm rectangle at 1 cm to 2 km has ground area —
a) 8 km²  b) 32 km²  c) 16 km²
**Answer: b) 32 km²**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A circle of map radius 3 cm at the same scale has area about —
a) 113 km²  b) 32 km²  c) 18 km²
**Answer: a) 113 km²**
