# Map Work — Measuring Area — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the rectangle and square formulas.
**Answer:** Rectangle: length × width. Square: side × side (side²).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the three steps in order.
**Answer:** Convert the scale to a statement scale; convert each map length to its ground length; apply the formula.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Show the circle's working.
**Answer:** Radius 3 cm → 6 km; πr² = 3.14 × 6² = 3.14 × 36 = 113 km² (rounded).
