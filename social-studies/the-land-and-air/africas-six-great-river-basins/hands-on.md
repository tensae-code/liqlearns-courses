# Hands-on — Africa's six great river basins

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Six-basin map
On a blank Africa outline, draw the six basin areas in six colours with the main rivers as lines joining to their outlets. Add a key with the six names.

## Rain's journey
Pick three points on your map — one in each of three basins. For each, write rain's journey: tributary → confluence → main river → outlet. Read one journey aloud to a partner.
