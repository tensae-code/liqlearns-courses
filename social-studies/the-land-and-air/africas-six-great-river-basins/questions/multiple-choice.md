# Africa's six great river basins — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A watershed is —
a) a river mouth  b) the raised divide between two basins  c) a lake  d) a waterfall
**Answer: b) the raised divide between two basins** — it separates one basin's water from the next.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A confluence is —
a) where streams join  b) a single spring  c) the sea  d) a watershed
**Answer: a) where streams join** — tributaries meet there.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which basin's outlet is an inland lake, not the sea?
a) Nile  b) Congo  c) Chad  d) Orange
**Answer: c) Chad** — the Chad basin gathers water into the interior.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Zambezi drains toward —
a) the Mediterranean  b) the Atlantic in the west  c) the Indian Ocean  d) Lake Chad
**Answer: c) the Indian Ocean** — it drains the south-central lands eastward.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** How many great basin systems does the lesson name?
a) Four  b) Five  c) Six  d) Nine
**Answer: c) Six** — Nile, Congo, Niger, Zambezi, Orange, Chad.
