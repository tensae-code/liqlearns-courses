# Africa's six great river basins — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Every drop in a basin converges to one outlet.
**Answer: True** — that convergence defines the basin.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A basin's border follows political boundaries.
**Answer: False** — it follows watersheds — a water line.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Congo basin drains the equatorial centre of Africa.
**Answer: True** — to the Atlantic.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Nile drains to the Indian Ocean.
**Answer: False** — the Nile drains north to the Mediterranean.
