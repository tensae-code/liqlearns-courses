# Africa's six great river basins — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the six great basins.
**Answer:** Nile, Congo, Niger, Zambezi, Orange and Chad.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What question tests whether a place belongs to a basin?
**Answer:** 'If rain falls here, which outlet does it finally reach?' — the answer decides the basin.
