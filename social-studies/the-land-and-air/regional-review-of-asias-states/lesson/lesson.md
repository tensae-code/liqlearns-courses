## Sub lesson 2 — North Macedonia's way — the book's own teaching way

North Macedonia's own way (Grade 9 mk-g9): Grade 9 finishes the regional-geography cycle with the non-European continents — Asia again in greater depth (climate factors and phenomena, monsoons and typhoons, air pollution), then Africa, the Americas, and Australia with Oceania and the polar regions — each continent in the same pattern: position and coastline, relief, climate, hydrography, natural wealth and economy, population, a regional review, one or two spotlight countries (China, India; South Africa; USA, Brazil) and one problem lesson (food and water shortage in Africa; air pollution in Asia; global warming in the polar regions). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
