# Lesson plan — The Mekong River And Its Commission

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Land and Air · **Lesson:** The Mekong River And Its Commission
**Place in the curriculum:** The Land and Air (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Topic 11.1 (part): The Mekong River basin (4,763 km long, 810,000 km², six countries; over 20,000 plant species and 1,000 kinds of freshwater fish; 65 million people in the lower basin; farming, fisheries, river transport and some 60,000 MW of hydropower — and growing water stress) and the Mekong River Commission (from the 1957 committee to the 1995 Mekong Agreement; Cambodia, Laos, Thailand and Vietnam; its five water procedures; cooperation with China, the USA, Japan, Korea and India; and Vietnam's own stake — the Mekong Delta, over 17 million people) — as Vietnam's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Topic 11.1 (part): The Mekong River basin (4,763 km long, 810,000 km², six countries; over 20,000 plant species and 1,000 kinds of freshwater fish; 65 million people in the lower basin; farming, fisheries, river transport and some 60,000 MW of hydropower — and growing water stress) and the Mekong River Commission (from the 1957 committee to the 1995 Mekong Agreement; Cambodia, Laos, Thailand and Vietnam; its five water procedures; cooperation with China, the USA, Japan, Korea and India; and Vietnam's own stake — the Mekong Delta, over 17 million people).
