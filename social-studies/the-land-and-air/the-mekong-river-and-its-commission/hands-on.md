# Hands-on — The Mekong River And Its Commission

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Topic 11.1 (part): The Mekong River basin (4,763 km long, 810,000 km², six countries; over 20,000 plant species and 1,000 kinds of freshwater fish; 65 million people in the lower basin; farming, fisheries, river transport and some 60,000 MW of hydropower — and growing water stress) and the Mekong River Commission (from the 1957 committee to the 1995 Mekong Agreement; Cambodia, Laos, Thailand and Vietnam; its five water procedures; cooperation with China, the USA, Japan, Korea and India; and Vietnam's own stake — the Mekong Delta, over 17 million people). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
