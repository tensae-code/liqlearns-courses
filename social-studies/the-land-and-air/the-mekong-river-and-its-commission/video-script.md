Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11* (printed pp. 5–15), teaches: Topic 11.1 (part): The Mekong River basin (4,763 km long, 810,000 km², six countries; over 20,000 plant species and 1,000 kinds of freshwater fish; 65 million people in the lower basin; farming, fisheries, river transport and some 60,000 MW of hydropower — and growing water stress) and the Mekong River Commission (from the 1957 committee to the 1995 Mekong Agreement; Cambodia, Laos, Thailand and Vietnam; its five water procedures; cooperation with China, the USA, Japan, Korea and India; and Vietnam's own stake — the Mekong Delta, over 17 million people).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11): the Grade 11 electives take three deep dives that all circle home — sharing the Mekong and the East Sea with neighbours, the world's tourism and the jobs it offers Vietnamese school-leavers, and the Fourth Industrial Revolution remaking work.
