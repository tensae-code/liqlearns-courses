Mauritania's book, the *Mauritania Grade 9 geo-3as-m* (printed p. 80), teaches: Unit 3 Lesson 11: natural and human features of West Africa. Mauritania's book, the *Mauritania Grade 10 geo-4as-m* (printed p. 90), teaches: Unit 3 Lesson 11: natural and human features of West Africa.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 9 geo-3as-m): A home-and-region year in three units: the Mauritanian geographic space (the country set astronomically between 15°–27° N and 5°–17° W, area about 1,030,700 km²), Mauritania's economic activities, then the two spaces Mauritania belongs to — the Maghreb and West Africa — ending on sub-regional economic integration.

Mauritania's own way (Mauritania Grade 10 geo-4as-m): This book re-teaches the Grade 9 programme lesson-for-lesson (its contents list is identical to Book 8's) at greater depth and with fuller map work — its Mauritania unit works through three location maps (in the Arab world, geographic and astronomical location) where the Grade 9 book works from text. Filed as its own book because it is a distinct, longer edition (116 pp vs 104 pp), not a duplicate file.
