# Land-Shaping Forces — The East African Rift Valley — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The East African Rift Valley is —
a) a small local crack  b) the largest rift system on Earth  c) a fold range
**Answer: b) the largest rift system on Earth**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How much of the rift lies in Ethiopia and Eritrea?
a) 560 km  b) 1,700 km  c) 5,600 km
**Answer: b) 1,700 km**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How much of it lies in Africa?
a) 1,700 km  b) 5,600 km  c) 7,200 km
**Answer: b) 5,600 km**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The rift's cause is —
a) folding  b) faulting — a block sinking between faults  c) river erosion
**Answer: b) faulting — a block sinking between faults**
