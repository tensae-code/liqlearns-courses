# Land-Shaping Forces — The East African Rift Valley — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How long is the whole system?
**Answer:** 7,200 km.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What share of the whole rift is the Ethiopia-Eritrea stretch, roughly?
**Answer:** Nearly a quarter (1,700 of 7,200 km).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain the rift's cause in one sentence.
**Answer:** Faulting: a great block of crust sank between two faults.
