## Sub lesson 1 — The largest rift system on Earth

The East African Rift Valley is the largest rift system on Earth. It runs 7,200 km in all — from Syria to Mozambique.

[Picture 11461 — the rift drawn from Syria to Mozambique with the Ethiopia-Eritrea stretch highlighted]

## Sub lesson 2 — How much of it is Africa's — and Ethiopia's

Of its 7,200 km, 5,600 km lie in Africa, and 1,700 km of it in Ethiopia and Eritrea. Nearly a quarter of the whole system crosses Ethiopian and Eritrean land.

## Sub lesson 3 — Its cause — and your research task

Its cause is faulting: a great block sinking between faults, as the last lesson showed. Your task: explain that cause in your own words, then research and report on Ethiopia's own rift — its lakes, volcanoes and the life along it.
