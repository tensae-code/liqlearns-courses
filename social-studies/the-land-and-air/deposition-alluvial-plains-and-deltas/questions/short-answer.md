# Deposition — Alluvial Plains and Deltas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or a short list.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three carriers whose loads deposition lays down.
**Answer:** Any three of: water, wind, ice, sea, gravity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is new soil built into at river mouths called?
**Answer:** Alluvial plains (and deltas where the river meets the sea).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** (Teacher guide — evidence) Name two named dropped loads.
**Answer:** Any two of: sand dune, barchan, loess, scree.
