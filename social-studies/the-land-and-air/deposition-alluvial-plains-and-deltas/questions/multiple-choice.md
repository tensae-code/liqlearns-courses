# Deposition — Alluvial Plains and Deltas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Deposition is —
a) the breaking of rock  b) carried material being laid down somewhere new  c) the rising of magma
**Answer: b) carried material being laid down somewhere new**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mass movement is —
a) gravity moving masses suddenly  b) a river's slow flow  c) ice forming in cracks
**Answer: a) gravity moving masses suddenly**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Alluvial plains build at —
a) mountain tops only  b) river mouths, sea coasts and feet of hills  c) the focus of earthquakes
**Answer: b) river mouths, sea coasts and feet of hills**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Which rivers are named as delta-builders?
a) the Nile, Mississippi and Amazon  b) the Bering and Caribbean  c) none
**Answer: a) the Nile, Mississippi and Amazon**
