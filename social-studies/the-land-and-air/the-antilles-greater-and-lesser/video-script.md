# Video script — The Antilles — Greater and Lesser

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Dominican Republic's Grade 3 student book, the *Ciencias Sociales, Tercer Grado, Primaria (Libro Abierto)* (printed p. 26), teaches: The Antilles, a beautiful place: Greater and Lesser Antilles and the islands and archipelagos of the Caribbean (Unidad 2). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 3): For third graders geography is the home island first: the planet and its components, then the Antilles and the relief of their own country, then countryside versus city, and finally where their own families came from — migration and a family census the children run themselves.
