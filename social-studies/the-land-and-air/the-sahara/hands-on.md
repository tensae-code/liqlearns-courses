# Hands-on — The Sahara

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three surfaces table
Make a three-column table — dunes / hamada / oasis — and under each write what the ground looks like, what lives there, and what a traveller needs. Use only what the lesson teaches.

## Desert in a tray
In a tray or box lid, build the Sahara: sand at one end, small stones spread flat for hamada, and a small green paper-and-thread palm cluster where a bottle-cap of water sits — the oasis.
