# The Sahara — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sahara lies on which landform region?
a) The Southern Platform  b) The African Massif  c) The Alpine System  d) The Rift System
**Answer: b) The African Massif** — the ancient northern block.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hyper-arid means —
a) rain every week  b) rain is rare and unreliable, evaporation outruns it  c) snow all year  d) rivers everywhere
**Answer: b) rain is rare and unreliable, evaporation outruns it** — drier than merely arid.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hamada is —
a) a dune shape  b) a bare stony and rocky desert surface  c) a desert town  d) a palm grove
**Answer: b) a bare stony and rocky desert surface** — stone, not sand.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** An oasis forms where —
a) rain is heaviest  b) groundwater reaches the surface  c) the escarpment drops  d) rivers flood yearly
**Answer: b) groundwater reaches the surface** — water, not land, is scarce in the Sahara.
