# The Sahara — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sahara is the world's great hot desert.
**Answer: True** — the largest hot desert on Earth.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The whole Sahara is covered in sand dunes.
**Answer: False** — wide areas are hamada and gravel plains.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Oases are the Sahara's living points of settlement.
**Answer: True** — life gathers where water reaches the surface.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sahara lies across southern Africa.
**Answer: False** — it stretches across North Africa.
