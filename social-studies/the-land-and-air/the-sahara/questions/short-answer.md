# The Sahara — short answer

Answer in one or two short sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three surfaces of the Sahara.
**Answer:** Dune fields, hamada (bare stony/rocky surface) and gravel plains (the oasis is its green living point).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is water, not land, the scarce thing in the Sahara?
**Answer:** Land is vast, but rain is rare and unreliable; life and settlement appear only where water reaches the surface, as at oases.
