# Hands-on — Kiribati Traditional Government Kaainga Mwaneaba Uea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 5 Traditional Systems of Government in Kiribati — teaches: Kiribati’s three pre-contact ruling systems — Te Kaainga (family/kin rule, southern islands), Te Mwaneaba / Te Unimwane (the meeting house and its elders, democratic in feature) and Te Uea (chiefly rule, aristocratic in feature) — and how climate and resources shaped each. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
