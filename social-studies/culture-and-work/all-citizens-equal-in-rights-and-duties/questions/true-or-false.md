# All Citizens Equal in Rights and Duties — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: All residents are equal citizens with the same rights and duties — schooling and media in their own language, health care, work, celebrating their own holidays — and in return the duty to respect others' rights, customs and holidays, to obey the state's laws and to help one another.
**Answer: True**
