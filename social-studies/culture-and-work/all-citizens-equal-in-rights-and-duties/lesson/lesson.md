# All Citizens Equal in Rights and Duties

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: all residents are equal citizens with the same rights and duties — schooling and media in their own language, health care, work, celebrating their own holidays — and in return the duty to respect others' rights, customs and holidays, to obey the state's laws and to help one another — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Становништво нашег краја (The population of our region)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed p. 29), teaches: all residents are equal citizens with the same rights and duties — schooling and media in their own language, health care, work, celebrating their own holidays — and in return the duty to respect others' rights, customs and holidays, to obey the state's laws and to help one another. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
