# Hands-on — All Citizens Equal in Rights and Duties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Становништво нашег краја (The population of our region)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: all residents are equal citizens with the same rights and duties — schooling and media in their own language, health care, work, celebrating their own holidays — and in return the duty to respect others' rights, customs and holidays, to obey the state's laws and to help one another. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
