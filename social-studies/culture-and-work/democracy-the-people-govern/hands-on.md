# Hands-on — Democracy the People Govern

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 4 "Democracia en América"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 174, teaches: democracy from the Greek demos (people) and kratos (government) — the form of government where power is given by the people through elections, all are equal and hold the same rights, and political parties propose candidates and watch over the government. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
