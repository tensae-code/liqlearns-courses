# Democracy the People Govern

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: democracy from the Greek demos (people) and kratos (government) — the form of government where power is given by the people through elections, all are equal and hold the same rights, and political parties propose candidates and watch over the government — as Guatemala's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 174, teaches: democracy from the Greek demos (people) and kratos (government) — the form of government where power is given by the people through elections, all are equal and hold the same rights, and political parties propose candidates and watch over the government. ## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it..
