# Video script — Democracy the People Govern

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 174, teaches: democracy from the Greek demos (people) and kratos (government) — the form of government where power is given by the people through elections, all are equal and hold the same rights, and political parties propose candidates and watch over the government.

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.
