# Video script — Young Patriots Serve Their Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eritrea Grade 6 Citizenship Education (printed p. 79), teaches: A child can be a patriot now — a country is the sum of its schools, hospitals and villages, so keeping the school clean, planting trees at the new clinic and running a school environmental club are serving the country.

Eritrea's own way: a standalone Citizenship Education subject built entirely from Eritrean stories, poems and dialogues — responsibility is taught through a shop-queue drama and a family Tandoor day, the state through the 1993 referendum story, and patriotism as practical service (keeping the school clean, planting trees at the new clinic) that even a child can do.
