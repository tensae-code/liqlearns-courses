# Hands-on — Young Patriots Serve Their Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a child can be a patriot now — a country is the sum of its schools, hospitals and villages, so keeping the school clean, planting trees at the new clinic and running a school environmental club are serving the country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
