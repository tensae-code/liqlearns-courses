## Sub lesson 3 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way: the primary cycle closes by widening the circle — from how my community is organised and who organises it (juntas de vecinos, authorities, institutions), through tax duties written into the Constitution and the Judicial Power, to the pupil as an "ambassador of peace" in home, school and community. the last primary volume grounds the State in its thinkers and its text — Montesquieu's separation of powers, the Constitution as the backbone (columna vertebral) of the State, the Escudo defined by Article 32, and the municipality split into its two organs, the regidores who norm and oversee and the Alcaldía that executes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
