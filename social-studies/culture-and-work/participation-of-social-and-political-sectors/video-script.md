# Video script — Participation of Social and Political Sectors

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 6° Primaria student guide (printed p. 46), teaches: The participation of social, political and civil-society sectors in the school and in the nation.

Dominican Republic — Ciencias Sociales 6° Primaria (printed p. 154), teaches: Democracy and social participation — the participation bodies exist to develop citizen conscience on common matters and defend fundamental rights.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 5): the primary cycle closes by widening the circle — from how my community is organised and who organises it (juntas de vecinos, authorities, institutions), through tax duties written into the Constitution and the Judicial Power, to the pupil as an "ambassador of peace" in home, school and community.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 10): the last primary volume grounds the State in its thinkers and its text — Montesquieu's separation of powers, the Constitution as the backbone (columna vertebral) of the State, the Escudo defined by Article 32, and the municipality split into its two organs, the regidores who norm and oversee and the Alcaldía that executes.
