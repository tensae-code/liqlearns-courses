# Hands-on — Women in Politics

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Women's empowerment changing the political structure — a woman Prime Minister, Speaker, Education Minister and deputy leader of parliament, women elected as Upazila vice-chairmen and Union Parishad members, and rising participation in city corporations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
