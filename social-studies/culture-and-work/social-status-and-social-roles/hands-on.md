# Hands-on — Social Status and Social Roles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A social status as a person's position, the whole set forming their status set — prescribed (ascribed), achieved, and mixed statuses, the main status (usually professional), personal status in the small group against group status; the role as the behaviour expected of a status, with role expectation, role performance and role conflict; and social mobility — individual and group, horizontal and vertical — with the "social lift" that moves people between strata. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
