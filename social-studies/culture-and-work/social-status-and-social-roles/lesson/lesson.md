## Sub lesson 1 — Social statuses, roles and mobility (Социальные статусы, роли и мобильность) — Grade 10

Belarus Grade 10 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 23), teaches: A social status as a person's position, the whole set forming their status set — prescribed (ascribed), achieved, and mixed statuses, the main status (usually professional), personal status in the small group against group status; the role as the behaviour expected of a status, with role expectation, role performance and role conflict; and social mobility — individual and group, horizontal and vertical — with the "social lift" that moves people between strata. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
