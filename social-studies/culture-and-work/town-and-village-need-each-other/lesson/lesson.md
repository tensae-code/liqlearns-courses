# Town and Village Need Each Other

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: town and village live by exchange — clothes, machines, medicines and books go down to the village; meat, milk, eggs and vegetables come up to the town — villagers also travel in daily for jobs, school, doctors and museums, because both places have the same needs and each needs the other — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Од града до села, од села до града (From town to village, from village to town)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 32–33), teaches: town and village live by exchange — clothes, machines, medicines and books go down to the village; meat, milk, eggs and vegetables come up to the town — villagers also travel in daily for jobs, school, doctors and museums, because both places have the same needs and each needs the other. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
