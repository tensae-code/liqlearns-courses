# Hands-on — Town and Village Need Each Other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Од града до села, од села до града (From town to village, from village to town)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: town and village live by exchange — clothes, machines, medicines and books go down to the village; meat, milk, eggs and vegetables come up to the town — villagers also travel in daily for jobs, school, doctors and museums, because both places have the same needs and each needs the other. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
