# Town and Village Need Each Other — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Town and village live by exchange — clothes, machines, medicines and books go down to the village; meat, milk, eggs and vegetables come up to the town — villagers also travel in daily for jobs, school, doctors and museums, because both places have the same needs and each needs the other.
**Answer: True**
