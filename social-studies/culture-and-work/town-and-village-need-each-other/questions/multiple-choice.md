# Town and Village Need Each Other — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called “Од града до села, од села до града (From town to village, from village to town)” is —
a) Од града до села, од села до града (From town to village, from village to town)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Од града до села, од села до града (From town to village, from village to town)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part is mainly about —
a) town and village live by exchange — clothes, machines, medicines and books go down to the village; meat, milk, eggs and vegetables come up to the town — villagers also travel in daily for jobs, school, doctors and museums, because both places have the same needs and each needs the other  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) town and village live by exchange — clothes, machines, medicines and books go down to the village; meat, milk, eggs and vegetables come up to the town — villagers also travel in daily for jobs, school, doctors and museums, because both places have the same needs and each needs the other**
