# Video script — Town and Village Need Each Other

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 32–33), teaches: Town and village live by exchange — clothes, machines, medicines and books go down to the village; meat, milk, eggs and vegetables come up to the town — villagers also travel in daily for jobs, school, doctors and museums, because both places have the same needs and each needs the other.

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
