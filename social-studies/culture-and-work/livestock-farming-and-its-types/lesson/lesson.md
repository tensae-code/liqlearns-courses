## Sub lesson 2 — Mozambique's way — the book's own teaching way

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 2): human/economic geography organised sector by sector — population first, then each economic activity taught world-then-Mozambique (agriculture, livestock, fishing, forestry, industry, commerce, tourism, transport), with Mozambique's own distribution tables, ports and exports as the local evidence.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 6): farming taught historically first — the Neolithic revolution, the Iron civilisation, and what the Industrial Revolution gave agriculture — then agrarian space and its natural/human factors, farming systems by development level, and the tropical systems (dryland, oasis, monsoon rice) before modern farming and livestock. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
