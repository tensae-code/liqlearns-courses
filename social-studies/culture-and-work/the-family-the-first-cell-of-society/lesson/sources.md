**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Syria). Cuba is named here as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Cuba's own way (teacher guide — evidence): Citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors).

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 6): civics is taught inside the Arab-world regional tour — the state lesson interrupts the geography of Africa to define the state from Syria's own case (sovereignty won 17 April 1946, land, nation, authority), and the family/institutions unit teaches society as a ladder of organised groups, from the family cell to the cultural institutions the Ministry of Culture runs.
