# Hands-on — The Family the First Cell of Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the family as society's fundamental cell; the Constitution (arts. 81, 84, 88) gives everyone the right to found a family, sets parents' duties to feed, respect and protect their children, children's duty to respect, attend and protect their parents, and the protection of older adults; the family tree; each member's responsibilities at home; equality of man and woman in housework; talking and listening with respect, and affectionate treatment of grandparents. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
