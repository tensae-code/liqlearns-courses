# The Family the First Cell of Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Family the First Cell of Society — the family as society's fundamental cell; the Constitution (arts. 81, 84, 88) gives everyone the right to found a family, sets parents' duties to feed, respect and protect their children, children's duty to respect, attend and protect their parents, and the protection of older adults; the family tree; each member's responsibilities at home; equality of man and woman in housework; talking and listening with respect, and affectionate treatment of grandparents (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors) (any close wording).**
