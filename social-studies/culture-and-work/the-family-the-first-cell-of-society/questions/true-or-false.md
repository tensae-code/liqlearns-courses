# The Family the First Cell of Society — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The family as society's fundamental cell; the Constitution (arts. 81, 84, 88) gives everyone the right to found a family, sets parents' duties to feed, respect and protect their children, children's duty to respect, attend and protect their parents, and the protection of older adults; the family tree; each member's responsibilities at home; equality of man and woman in housework; talking and listening with respect, and affectionate treatment of grandparents.
**Answer: True**

**Answer: False — it stands on Cuba's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
