# Hands-on — Ndi Umunyarwanda National Unity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## National Unity and 'Ndi Umunyarwanda'
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Ndi Umunyarwanda programme (begun 2013) — patriotism and teamwork, and its taboos: selfishness, refusing to admit a mistake, genocide ideology and hate. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Rwanda's way
Say how Rwanda's book teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
