# Hands-on — The School Coexistence Manual and School Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tema 26 La construcción de las normas (school part)
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado06, printed pp. 231–234, teaches: the Manual de Convivencia holds the principles, functions, norms, conflict-resolution behaviours and participation forms of the whole educational community (students, teachers, parents, administrators); everyone helps build and revise it yearly, and electing the student personero is democracy practised in school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: the norms unit is anchored in the Colombian school itself — the Manual de Convivencia that every school community builds together, the elected student personero, and the real Colombian rights-defence mechanisms (petición, tutela, cumplimiento, Personería). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
