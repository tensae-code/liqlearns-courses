**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Nicaragua's way — the books' own teaching way — civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book. Grade 3 civics adds Nicaragua-specific law to the values base — women's rights under Ley 779 and a Mother Earth declaration — and respect for public and private institutions as a civic duty. Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State. (any close wording).**
