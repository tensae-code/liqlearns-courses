# Video script — Child Violence and the Cultural Values That Prevent It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 29–44, teaches: how child maltreatment shows itself and what it does to a child's body, feelings and learning, and the cultural values of the Maya, Xinka and Garífuna peoples practised as the antidote — the class builds the chart of violence types against the values that prevent each.

Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.
