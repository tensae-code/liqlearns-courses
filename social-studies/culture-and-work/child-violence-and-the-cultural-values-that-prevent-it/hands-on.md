# Hands-on — Child Violence and the Cultural Values That Prevent It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 2 "Violencia infantil y valores culturales"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 29–44, teaches: how child maltreatment shows itself and what it does to a child's body, feelings and learning, and the cultural values of the Maya, Xinka and Garífuna peoples practised as the antidote — the class builds the chart of violence types against the values that prevent each. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
