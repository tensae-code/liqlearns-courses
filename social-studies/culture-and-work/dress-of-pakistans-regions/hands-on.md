# Hands-on — Dress of Pakistans Regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 2 Dress, Ch. 2 Dresses of Pakistan, Ch. 6 Salient Features: Dress and Food (dress), Ch. 6 Salient Features: Dress, Ch. 2 Dress, Ch. 2 (chapter exercise) — teaches: shalwar kameez is worn all over Pakistan, shaped by each region's weather and custom — turbans and caps, Sindhi topi and ajrak, Balochi dress, northern woollens; the national shalwar kameez takes a regional face in each province — Sindhi topi and ajrak, Punjabi dress with phulkari, Balochi embroidered dress, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's waistcoat and cap, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Kashmir dress — each pictured child by child; shalwar kameez as the national dress with regional variations in cloth, cut and ornament from province to province; dress follows province, climate and tradition — dhoti, kurta and turban giving way to shalwar; women's shalwar, kurta and dupatta; sherwani, waistcoat, coat and pant in the cities; shalwar qamees the national dress in its regional styles — dhoti kurta, lungi and turban in the villages, waist coat and sherwani, desert ladies' gharara and bangles, the black cloak and caps of Chitral valley and Hunza; Ajrak confirmed as a special part of Sindhi dress, and glass, wool and silk thread in Balochi embroidery — dress as provincial identity, tested as fact. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
