# Hands-on — Bangladeshs Parliament and National Elections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How Bangladesh's Parliament (Sangsad) is elected — citizens of 18+ vote, party and independent candidates, 300 constituencies each electing one MP, 50 reserved women's seats chosen by the MPs, who also elect the President, while the President appoints the Prime Minister. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
