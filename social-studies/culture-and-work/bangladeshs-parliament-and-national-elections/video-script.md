# Video script — Bangladeshs Parliament and National Elections

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bangladesh Grade 7 BD G7 HSS (printed pp. 108–109), teaches: How Bangladesh's Parliament (Sangsad) is elected — citizens of 18+ vote, party and independent candidates, 300 constituencies each electing one MP, 50 reserved women's seats chosen by the MPs, who also elect the President, while the President appoints the Prime Minister.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 7): Civics is learned by doing it — the class runs a real club election with an Election Commission, code of conduct and manifestos, then sits as a Shadow Parliament and passes a law, and only then is told that the national Parliament works the same way; sociology is anchored in living communities the class visits (the Bedey boat fleet) and in guests the class interviews (Sharifa, a third-gender person)..
