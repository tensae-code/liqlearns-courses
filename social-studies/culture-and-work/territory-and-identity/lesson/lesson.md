## Sub lesson 2 — El Salvador's way — the books' own teaching way

El Salvador's own way: the Tercer Ciclo book is built as five themes, and its civics is El Salvador's own — Salvadoran identity, Salvadoran families, the institutions that protect Salvadoran children, and the rights of Salvadorans under their own constitution. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
