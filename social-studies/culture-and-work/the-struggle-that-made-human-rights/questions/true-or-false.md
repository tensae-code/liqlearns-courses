# The Struggle That Made Human Rights — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the idea of human rights grew through history's struggles — Hammurabi's written law, Confucius's golden rule, and above all Magna Carta (1215), where England's King John, pressed by his barons, signed away the power to tax, imprison or punish any free man except by lawful judgement — the first written limit on a ruler; then the English Bill of Rights (1689) and the American Declaration of Independence (1776); "the history of mankind is the history of the struggle for human rights", and the documents are compared in a class timeline game.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
