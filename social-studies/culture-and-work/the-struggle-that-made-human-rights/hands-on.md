# Hands-on — The Struggle That Made Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.1 "Human rights — the history the struggle created"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The idea of human rights grew through history's struggles — Hammurabi's written law, Confucius's golden rule, and above all Magna Carta (1215), where England's King John, pressed by his barons, signed away the power to tax, imprison or punish any free man except by lawful judgement — the first written limit on a ruler; then the English Bill of Rights (1689) and the American Declaration of Independence (1776); "the history of mankind is the history of the struggle for human rights", and the documents are compared in a class timeline game. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
