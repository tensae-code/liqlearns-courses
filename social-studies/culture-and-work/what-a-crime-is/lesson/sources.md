Kuwait's own way (Grade 9 Term 1): The law strand that began in Grade 8 with what law is ends in Grade 9 with crime — the pupil learns to separate a crime from a merely wrong act, the three elements every crime must have, how crimes rank by the danger they pose to society's security, and the drug crime worked end to end, from use and possession to the harm done to person and society.
