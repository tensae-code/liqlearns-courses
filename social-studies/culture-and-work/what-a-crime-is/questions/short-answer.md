# What a Crime Is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The concept of crime and its difference from violating behaviours — what a crime is, the difference between a wrong act and a violating behaviour, and the two basic elements a crime is tied to (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The elements of crime in law — a crime's three elements — the legal element (no act is a crime unless the law defines it; its purpose is protecting people from injustice), the material element and the moral element (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way — the law strand that began in Grade 8 with what law is ends in Grade 9 with crime — the pupil learns to separate a crime from a merely wrong act, the three elements every crime must have, how crimes rank by the danger they pose to society's security, and the drug crime worked end to end, from use and possession to the harm done to person and society (any close wording).**
