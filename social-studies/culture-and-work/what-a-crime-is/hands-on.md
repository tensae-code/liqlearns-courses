# Hands-on — What a Crime Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What a crime is, the difference between a wrong act and a violating behaviour, and the two basic elements a crime is tied to. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A crime's three elements — the legal element (no act is a crime unless the law defines it; its purpose is protecting people from injustice), the material element and the moral element. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
