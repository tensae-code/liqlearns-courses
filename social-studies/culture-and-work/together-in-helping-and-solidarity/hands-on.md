# Hands-on — Together in Helping and Solidarity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Turkey's old helping institutions — the sadaka taşı, a stone at squares and fountains where givers left money and the needy took only what they needed, unseen, so nobody was shamed; the National Struggle's donation orders; and today's Türk Kızılay and AFAD, with a class running its own Kızılay donation-box and fair campaign for earthquake victims by petition Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Turkey's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
