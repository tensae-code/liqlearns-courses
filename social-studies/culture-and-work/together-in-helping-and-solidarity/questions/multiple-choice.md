# Together in Helping and Solidarity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Turkey's old helping institutions — the sadaka taşı, a stone at squares and fountains where givers left money and the needy took only what they needed, unseen, so nobody was shamed; the National Struggle's donation orders; and today's Türk Kızılay and AFAD, with a class running its own Kızılay donation-box and fair campaign for earthquake victims by petition  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
