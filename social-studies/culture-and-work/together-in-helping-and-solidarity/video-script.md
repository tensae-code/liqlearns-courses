Turkey Grade 5 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 34, teaches: Turkey's old helping institutions — the sadaka taşı, a stone at squares and fountains where givers left money and the needy took only what they needed, unseen, so nobody was shamed; the National Struggle's donation orders; and today's Türk Kızılay and AFAD, with a class running its own Kızılay donation-box and fair campaign for earthquake victims by petition

Turkey's own way: the middle-school course opens with living together, not with land or dates: a ten-year-old first maps the groups she belongs to and the rights and duties each role carries, then learns that Turkey's many cultures are an aşure — many different ingredients in one pot making one good taste — and that helping has an old Turkish technology for protecting the receiver's dignity, the sadaka taşı (charity stone).
