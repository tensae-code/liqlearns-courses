**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Turkey).**

Turkey's own way (Sosyal Bilgiler 5. Sınıf, 1. Kitap): the middle-school course opens with living together, not with land or dates: a ten-year-old first maps the groups she belongs to and the rights and duties each role carries, then learns that Turkey's many cultures are an aşure — many different ingredients in one pot making one good taste — and that helping has an old Turkish technology for protecting the receiver's dignity, the sadaka taşı (charity stone).
