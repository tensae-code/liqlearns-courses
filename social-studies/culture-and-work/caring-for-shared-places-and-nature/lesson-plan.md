# Lesson plan — Caring for Shared Places and Nature

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Caring for Shared Places and Nature
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Burkina Faso, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** caring for the places and nature everyone shares — sweeping the classroom, using and maintaining the school latrines, keeping stray animals from wandering, cleaning the schoolyard, planting trees, caring for plants, picking up plastic waste and fighting abusive wood-cutting — as Burkina Faso's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — caring for the places and nature everyone shares — sweeping the classroom, using and maintaining the school latrines, keeping stray animals from wandering, cleaning the schoolyard, planting trees, caring for plants, picking up plastic waste and fighting abusive wood-cutting.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
