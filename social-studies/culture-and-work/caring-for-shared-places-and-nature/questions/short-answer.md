**13. (Sub lesson 13)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Burkina Faso's way — the book's own teaching way — civic and moral education is a standalone primary subject from CP1, taught through short prepared lesson sheets built on a story situation, group exchange and a spoken resolution the class takes together; road safety, the flag ceremony and care of the schoolyard and latrines are taught as a young citizen's first duties, each fiche ending in a resolution the pupils speak aloud. CP2 widens CP1's duties — children now elect their class leaders, run a school cooperative, and take up the fight against excision and plastic waste as citizens. (any close wording).**
