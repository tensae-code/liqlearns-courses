Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 51–58), teaches: Bài 12: Developing Vietnam's farming — its natural and social strengths and limits, the shifting structure (crops 65.3% against livestock 30.6% of output value in 2024), crops in detail (rice first — the Mekong Delta the largest producer, the Red River Delta second; perennial industrial crops on 17.9% of the sown area, with coffee, rubber and pepper among the world's leading exports; fruit and vegetables), livestock (pigs giving 62.8% of the meat), and the directions ahead.
