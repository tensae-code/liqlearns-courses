# Hands-on — Caring for Our Environment as Citizens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Environmental Conservation
Work through this part with your hands: the community's duty to assess and overcome conservation challenges (poverty-driven destruction among them), knowing natural and planted forests, and obeying the laws that protect the environment. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cherishing Your Community
Work through this part with your hands: cherishing your community means evaluating how the environment where you live is degraded — by farming, livestock keeping, mining and industry, burning forests, polluting water sources and overstocking — and educating the community on its effects: polluted water and skin diseases, dead livestock, drought, soil erosion, food and water shortage and desertification. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
