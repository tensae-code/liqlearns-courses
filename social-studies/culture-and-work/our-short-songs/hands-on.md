# Hands-on — Our Short Songs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 16 "Mongolian short songs" (Монгол богино дуу)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Beside the long song stands the short song (богино дуу) — compact, of three to five verses, sung with or without instruments, carrying daily life, work and feeling; its kinds — lullaby (бүүвэйн дуу, the first song a person ever hears, with each region's own lullaby), praise songs, work songs, wedding and feast songs, humorous songs, love songs and historical songs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
