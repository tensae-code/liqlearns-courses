# Our Short Songs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: beside the long song stands the short song (богино дуу) — compact, of three to five verses, sung with or without instruments, carrying daily life, work and feeling; its kinds — lullaby (бүүвэйн дуу, the first song a person ever hears, with each region's own lullaby), praise songs, work songs, wedding and feast songs, humorous songs, love songs and historical songs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
