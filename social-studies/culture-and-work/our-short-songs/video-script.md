Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 46, teaches: Beside the long song stands the short song (богино дуу) — compact, of three to five verses, sung with or without instruments, carrying daily life, work and feeling; its kinds — lullaby (бүүвэйн дуу, the first song a person ever hears, with each region's own lullaby), praise songs, work songs, wedding and feast songs, humorous songs, love songs and historical songs.

Mongolia's own way (Book 4): the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other.
