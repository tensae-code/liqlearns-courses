# Video script — Peace in the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar Teacher Guide — Morality and Civic — Grade 6 (printed p. 29), teaches: Admirable behaviours that keep a family peaceful, and practising love within the family.

Myanmar's own way (Book 8): Grade 6 is where the values course becomes a civics course — rights and responsibilities are named as such, the school has a council, rules are explained up to the rules of a country, and the last unit is peace: what conflict is, the bundle-of-firewood lesson, peace symbols, and rowing in unison.
