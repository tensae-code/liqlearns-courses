# Video script — Advertising How It Shapes What We Want

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India Grade 7 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Social and Political Life-II (printed p. 75), teaches: advertisements are repeated to make people buy, and social advertising can instead carry public messages.

India's own way: Equality is taught through real lives — a domestic worker voting, a Dalit writer made to sweep his school, a Muslim couple refused a flat — and government through one running case (a diarrhoea outbreak in a fictional district) that forces every institution to act.
