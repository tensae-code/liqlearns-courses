# Hands-on — Saudi Systems and State Sovereignty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every state has systems that govern it and none may act against them — the state's sovereignty; a system as the general rules organising a defined aspect, prepared by the regulatory authority and enforced by the executive after its issuance stages; Saudi systems are of two kinds — basic systems (governance, state administration, the relations and competencies of the authorities, and individuals' main rights) and general systems (how state bodies perform their tasks and services and the rules of society's relations); their development under King Abdulaziz — the Basic Instructions of 1345H before unification (founding the Shura Council and the Council of Deputies, later the Council of Ministers), more than 250 administrative systems in his reign, all on the Islamic Shariah method — and the National Center for Archives and Records that documents and preserves them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
