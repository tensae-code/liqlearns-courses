# Hands-on — Human Development and the Hdi Index

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Bloque IV, Secuencia 5 Desarrollo humano e Índice de Desarrollo Humano
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human development is the process by which a society improves its citizens' living conditions, widening the goods that cover basic and complementary needs inside an environment where everyone's human rights are respected — it is the quantity of options a person has to be or do what they wish, and so a way of measuring quality of life and grading a country; its pillars begin with equity (equal access to opportunities) and sustainability (not compromising future generations); the UNDP's Human Development Index joins three parameters — a long and healthy life, education and a dignified standard of living — and Honduras's 2016 index, 0.625, placed it 130th, in medium human development (the sequence's remaining pages are population growth, distribution and migration — Geography, named not placed).
