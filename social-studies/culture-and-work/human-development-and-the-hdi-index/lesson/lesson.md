# Human Development and the Hdi Index

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: human development is the process by which a society improves its citizens' living conditions, widening the goods that cover basic and complementary needs inside an environment where everyone's human rights are respected — it is the quantity of options a person has to be or do what they wish, and so a way of measuring quality of life and grading a country; its pillars begin with equity (equal access to opportunities) and sustainability (not compromising future generations); the UNDP's Human Development Index joins three parameters — a long and healthy life, education and a dignified standard of living — and Honduras's 2016 index, 0.625, placed it 130th, in medium human development (the sequence's remaining pages are population growth, distribution and migration — Geography, named not placed) — as Honduras's books teach it.
