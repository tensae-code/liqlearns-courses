The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Jordan Grade 8 SS G8 T1, printed p. 118, teaches: The media's part in public policy — how media stands between the state and the public.

Jordan's own way: human rights are taught as a ladder of charters — the UN, the international bill, the Arab charter — landing at last in Jordan's own legislation; and the media unit ends on public opinion, the citizen's collective voice.
