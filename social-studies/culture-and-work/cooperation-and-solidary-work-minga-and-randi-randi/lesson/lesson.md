## Sub lesson 1 — Cooperation and Solidary Work Minga and Randi Randi

Ecuador Grade 3 Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016), printed p. 86, teaches: Cooperation and solidary work as how groups reach common goals for the common good; the minga, collective work for the community kept alive above all by Ecuador's indigenous communities and nationalities, and randi-randi — Kichwa for "giving-giving" — reciprocity practised by the Randi-Randi corporation for conservation, sustainable development and social and gender equity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador's own way: the SM edition teaches local government from the child's own ground up — parish, then canton — with Ecuador's real institutions (the elected junta parroquial, the teniente político, the concejo municipal and the GADs), and teaches values through the Andean minga and the Kichwa reciprocity idea randi-randi, under the book's recurring Cultura del Buen Vivir spreads. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
