# Video script — Cooperation and Solidary Work Minga and Randi Randi

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 3 Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016), printed p. 86, teaches: Cooperation and solidary work as how groups reach common goals for the common good; the minga, collective work for the community kept alive above all by Ecuador's indigenous communities and nationalities, and randi-randi — Kichwa for "giving-giving" — reciprocity practised by the Randi-Randi corporation for conservation, sustainable development and social and gender equity.

Ecuador's own way: the SM edition teaches local government from the child's own ground up — parish, then canton — with Ecuador's real institutions (the elected junta parroquial, the teniente político, the concejo municipal and the GADs), and teaches values through the Andean minga and the Kichwa reciprocity idea randi-randi, under the book's recurring Cultura del Buen Vivir spreads.
