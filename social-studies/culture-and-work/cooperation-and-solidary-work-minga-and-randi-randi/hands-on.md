# Hands-on — Cooperation and Solidary Work Minga and Randi Randi

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Cooperation and solidary work as how groups reach common goals for the common good; the minga, collective work for the community kept alive above all by Ecuador's indigenous communities and nationalities, and randi-randi — Kichwa for "giving-giving" — reciprocity practised by the Randi-Randi corporation for conservation, sustainable development and social and gender equity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
