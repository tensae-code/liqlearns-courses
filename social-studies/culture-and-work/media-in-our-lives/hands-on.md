# Hands-on — Media in Our Lives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: communication as a basic human need and a medium (رسانه) as anything that carries a message from sender to receiver — from language to print, radio, television and the internet; the communications age in daily life (e-mail, e-government services, distance learning, instant news), and its warnings: news must be checked against its source or public opinion is led astray, advertising pushes consumerism, and the internet holds false information and dangers of its own. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
