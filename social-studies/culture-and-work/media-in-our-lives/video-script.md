# Video script — Media in Our Lives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 8 (student book, Persian), printed p. 36, teaches: communication as a basic human need and a medium (رسانه) as anything that carries a message from sender to receiver — from language to print, radio, television and the internet; the communications age in daily life (e-mail, e-government services, distance learning, instant news), and its warnings: news must be checked against its source or public opinion is led astray, advertising pushes consumerism, and the internet holds false information and dangers of its own.

Iran's own way: the civics year is built as an institutional tour taken in the order a citizen meets the state — cooperation in home and neighbourhood, then the executive (this book's "government"), then the judiciary — and every institution is introduced through newspaper headlines and the pupil's own town.
