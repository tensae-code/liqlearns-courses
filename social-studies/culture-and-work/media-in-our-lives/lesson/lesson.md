## Sub lesson 1 — Media in Our Lives

Iran Social Studies Grade 8 (student book, Persian), printed p. 36, teaches: communication as a basic human need and a medium (رسانه) as anything that carries a message from sender to receiver — from language to print, radio, television and the internet; the communications age in daily life (e-mail, e-government services, distance learning, instant news), and its warnings: news must be checked against its source or public opinion is led astray, advertising pushes consumerism, and the internet holds false information and dangers of its own. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the civics year is built as an institutional tour taken in the order a citizen meets the state — cooperation in home and neighbourhood, then the executive (this book's "government"), then the judiciary — and every institution is introduced through newspaper headlines and the pupil's own town. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
