# The Individual and Society — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Individual and Society — the relation between society's members is the ties and bonds existing between a group of individuals who live in one society, organised by the laws and customs present in society; and man, being a social being, always needs his society and the individuals who live with him, and no society is free of social bonds and relations between individuals and groups; but these relations must be based on love and respect, to preserve society's security and stability, for society needs the individual's efforts and his role in building society and preserving it; and the individual's relation with society must rest on foundations, among them respecting society's customs, its public manners and its laws, and the individual can serve his society and contribute to its development by committing to the regulations and laws prevailing in it (any close wording).**
