# Video script — The Individual and Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies (printed p. 142), teaches: The relation between society's members is the ties and bonds existing between a group of individuals who live in one society, organised by the laws and customs present in society; and man, being a social being, always needs his society and the individuals who live with him, and no society is free of social bonds and relations between individuals and groups; but these relations must be based on love and respect, to preserve society's security and stability, for society needs the individual's efforts and his role in building society and preserving it; and the individual's relation with society must rest on foundations, among them respecting society's customs, its public manners and its laws, and the individual can serve his society and contribute to its development by committing to the regulations and laws prevailing in it.

Iraq's own way: values are defined first as a system — standards that steer behaviour and spread peace "regardless of sex, religion, nationality, language or race" — and citizenship is cashed out in named rights (education, work, candidacy, the vote, freedom of opinion and belief), while the internet chapter warns in plain terms that the open network "is governed by no restrictions or conditions".
