# Video script — Giving and Taking Advice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mauritania Grade 6 Civic Education SB (printed p. 60), teaches: Sincere advice — giving it and accepting it.

Mauritania's own way: civics is built as three competencies — the child's rights and the state's law first, then Mauritania inside its regional and world organisations, then a long values ladder (moderation, advice, volunteering, equality, heritage, honour-brotherhood-justice) taught one value per lesson with observe–learn–discuss–conclude steps
