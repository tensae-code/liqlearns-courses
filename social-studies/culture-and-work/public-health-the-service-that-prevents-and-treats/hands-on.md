# Hands-on — Public Health the Service That Prevents and Treats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Ministry of Public Health carries the country's health in two parts — prevention (vaccination against polio, smallpox, measles, tetanus and more) and treatment through state and private hospitals and clinics; keeping home, school and environment clean is every citizen's share in it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
