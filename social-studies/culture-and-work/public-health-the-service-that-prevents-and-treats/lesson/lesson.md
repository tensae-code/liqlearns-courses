## Sub lesson 1 — Public Health the Service That Prevents and Treats

Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 15, teaches: the Ministry of Public Health carries the country's health in two parts — prevention (vaccination against polio, smallpox, measles, tetanus and more) and treatment through state and private hospitals and clinics; keeping home, school and environment clean is every citizen's share in it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: society is taught as a ladder of belonging — family, school, village, district, city, country, and the wider Islamic umma — and the state appears as the servant of that society: ministries named one by one, each with its service, and peace named the first need of a country that has known decades of war. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
