# Law and Morality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Legal rules and moral rules — the comparison of legal rules (written, set by the state, governing outward behaviour, for order and stability) with moral rules (unwritten values that refine conduct), and law's character — general and equal: the speed-limit law applies to every driver and every violator is held to account without exception, with speed cameras ensuring the law is carried out justly (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way — amid a book about the Islamic world, Kuwait teaches law abstractly and concretely at once — what law is, why a society without it falls into disorder, how legal rules differ from moral rules, and the speed camera on a Kuwaiti road as the picture of a law that applies to everyone without exception (any close wording).**
