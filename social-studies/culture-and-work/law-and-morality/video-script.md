---

Kuwait Grade 8 Term 1 (printed pp. 91–96), teaches: The comparison of legal rules (written, set by the state, governing outward behaviour, for order and stability) with moral rules (unwritten values that refine conduct), and law's character — general and equal: the speed-limit law applies to every driver and every violator is held to account without exception, with speed cameras ensuring the law is carried out justly.

Kuwait's own way: amid a book about the Islamic world, Kuwait teaches law abstractly and concretely at once — what law is, why a society without it falls into disorder, how legal rules differ from moral rules, and the speed camera on a Kuwaiti road as the picture of a law that applies to everyone without exception.
