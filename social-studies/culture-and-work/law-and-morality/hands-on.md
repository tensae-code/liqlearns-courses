# Hands-on — Law and Morality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The comparison of legal rules (written, set by the state, governing outward behaviour, for order and stability) with moral rules (unwritten values that refine conduct), and law's character — general and equal: the speed-limit law applies to every driver and every violator is held to account without exception, with speed cameras ensuring the law is carried out justly. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
