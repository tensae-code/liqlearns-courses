## Sub lesson 1 — Courts as Guardians

United States Grade 13 American Government 3e (printed p. 448), teaches: The courts' unexpected centrality — an independent judiciary in a common-law system becoming the interpreter of the Constitution and of individual rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United States' way — the book's own teaching way

United States' own way (Grade 13): the United States teaches its own government as one complete system in one book — the Constitution is not a topic but the frame: every institution (Congress, presidency, courts, states, counties, cities, the civil service) is derived from it in turn, and rights are taught as the courts have interpreted them, case by case. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
