Jordan's book, the *Jordan G2 SS T2* (printed p. 50), teaches: Unit 5 Lesson 1: Production and Consumption (الإنتاج والاستهلاك). Jordan's book, the *Jordan G3 SS T2* (printed p. 29), teaches: Unit 4 Lesson 3: Production and Consumption (الإنتاج والاستهلاك). Jordan's book, the *Jordan G6 SS T2* (printed p. 75), teaches: Unit 8 Lesson 3: Production, Distribution and Consumption (الإنتاج والتوزيع والاستهلاك).

Jordan's own way (Jordan G2 SS T2): Term 2 teaches change over time (transport, housing, writing) and the child's economy (production, consumption, recycling, Jordanian-made goods), closing with the blue planet, Earth's surface shapes and a first map of Jordan.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G3 SS T2): Grade 3 Term 2 contrasts past and present (trade, production, the smart consumer), then studies Earth's natural features in Jordan, environment care, Jordan's map and its antiquities.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G6 SS T2): Term 2's geography is climate done properly — elements, effects on human activity, Jordan's own climate regions — plus economic concepts and three more governorate journeys (Mafraq, Balqa, Aqaba).
