# Hands-on — National Awards Honouring Service to Guyana

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: National Awards are special gifts from a National Award Committee, presented by the Executive President, to citizens (and others who helped Guyana) for work done for the country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The award ladder — Order of Excellence (highest), Order of Roraima, Cacique Crown of Honour, Golden Arrow of Achievement, Medal of Service — and who receives each. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The award ladder in full — the Order of Excellence (highest, for citizens of high esteem), the Order of Roraima, the Cacique Crown of Honour, the Golden Arrow of Achievement, the Medal of Service, and the Military Service Star for GDF officers — and who receives each. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
