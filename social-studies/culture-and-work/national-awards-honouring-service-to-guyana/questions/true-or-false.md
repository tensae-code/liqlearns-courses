# National Awards Honouring Service to Guyana — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: National Awards are special gifts from a National Award Committee, presented by the Executive President, to citizens (and others who helped Guyana) for work done for the country.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: The award ladder — Order of Excellence (highest), Order of Roraima, Cacique Crown of Honour, Golden Arrow of Achievement, Medal of Service — and who receives each.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: The award ladder in full — the Order of Excellence (highest, for citizens of high esteem), the Order of Roraima, the Cacique Crown of Honour, the Golden Arrow of Achievement, the Medal of Service, and the Military Service Star for GDF officers — and who receives each.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
