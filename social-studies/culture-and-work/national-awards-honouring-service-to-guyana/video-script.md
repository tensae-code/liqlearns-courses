# Video script — National Awards Honouring Service to Guyana

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 4 Social Studies For Our Children Book 4 (printed p. 46), teaches: National Awards are special gifts from a National Award Committee, presented by the Executive President, to citizens (and others who helped Guyana) for work done for the country.

Guyana Grade 4 Social Studies For Our Children Book 4 (printed p. 47 (NEW slug first proposed in the line above)), teaches: The award ladder — Order of Excellence (highest), Order of Roraima, Cacique Crown of Honour, Golden Arrow of Achievement, Medal of Service — and who receives each.

Guyana Grade 5 Social Studies For Our Children Book 5 (printed p. 77 (NEW slug first proposed in Book 4 above)), teaches: The award ladder in full — the Order of Excellence (highest, for citizens of high esteem), the Order of Roraima, the Cacique Crown of Honour, the Golden Arrow of Achievement, the Medal of Service, and the Military Service Star for GDF officers — and who receives each.

Guyana's own way: Book 4 turns from "my community" to "our homeland" — Guyana is taught as six peoples who each brought a way of life, and citizenship is taught through honour (National Awards) and shared observance (Road Safety, Fire Prevention and Youth Weeks) before it is taught through rules. Book 5 widens the child from Guyana to the Caribbean Community — the peoples are taught by what each group still does today (foods, villages, festivals), neighbours are taught as people who help each other in disasters, and leadership is taught whole: Mayor, Touchau, Regional Chairman and trade-union leader side by side, all elected for a fixed time and removable.
