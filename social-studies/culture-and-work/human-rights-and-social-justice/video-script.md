# Video script — Human Rights and Social Justice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 10 Book 1 (printed p. 23), teaches: What human rights are (fair and equal treatment, a reasonable standard of living, deciding your own future, whatever your race, religion, gender, age or abilities) and what social justice means, starting from the rights learners claim for their own classroom.

Samoa's own way: Government is taught as Samoa's own Westminster-style parliament first (matai seats, the Individual Voters Roll, its parties and its real 2001 election, problems included), then Japan's Peace-Constitution government as the comparison; rights are taught from the classroom's own rights list outward to the UN and Amnesty.
