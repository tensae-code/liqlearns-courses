# Hands-on — Human Rights and Social Justice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What human rights are (fair and equal treatment, a reasonable standard of living, deciding your own future, whatever your race, religion, gender, age or abilities) and what social justice means, starting from the rights learners claim for their own classroom. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Samoa's way
Say how Samoa's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
