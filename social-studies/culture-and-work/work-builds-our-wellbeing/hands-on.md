# Hands-on — Work Builds Our Wellbeing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: work as the source of all material and spiritual wealth; every kind of work is necessary and honoured; workers' duties and rights guaranteed by law; women's incorporation into work as social justice; self-employment as a form of work; and voluntary, socially useful work, with Che Guevara's voluntary labour as the example. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
