# Work Builds Our Wellbeing

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: work as the source of all material and spiritual wealth; every kind of work is necessary and honoured; workers' duties and rights guaranteed by law; women's incorporation into work as social justice; self-employment as a form of work; and voluntary, socially useful work, with Che Guevara's voluntary labour as the example — as Cuba's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Work Builds Our Wellbeing

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 45–49), teaches: Work as the source of all material and spiritual wealth; every kind of work is necessary and honoured; workers' duties and rights guaranteed by law; women's incorporation into work as social justice; self-employment as a form of work; and voluntary, socially useful work, with Che Guevara's voluntary labour as the example. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
