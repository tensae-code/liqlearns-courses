# Video script — Work Builds Our Wellbeing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 45–49), teaches: Work as the source of all material and spiritual wealth; every kind of work is necessary and honoured; workers' duties and rights guaranteed by law; women's incorporation into work as social justice; self-employment as a form of work; and voluntary, socially useful work, with Che Guevara's voluntary labour as the example.

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors).
