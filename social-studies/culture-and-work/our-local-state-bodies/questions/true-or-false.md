# Our Local State Bodies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: a state organisation is an organisation that works for the citizens: citizens of a democratic society elect their state bodies, which guard the citizens' common interests and answer for breaking the law before the court; local self-governance is the Citizens' Representatives' Khural (Иргэдийн Төлөөлөгчдийн Хурал) — elected by the soum's or district's citizens, it discusses local work, approves the rules and the local budget, and reports to the citizens; the Governor's office (Засаг даргын тамгын газар) carries the Khural's approved plan out, and the governor is appointed on the Khural's nomination; the court (шүүх) protects a person's rights — anyone who believes a right was violated may turn to it; and the school itself is a state organisation, from which pupils have rights to information.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
