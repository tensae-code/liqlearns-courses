# Our Local State Bodies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 23 "Local state organisations" (Орон нутгийн төрийн байгууллага) — A state organisation is an organisation that works for the citizens: citizens of a democratic society elect their state bodies, which guard the citizens' common interests and answer for breaking the law before the court; local self-governance is the Citizens' Representatives' Khural (Иргэдийн Төлөөлөгчдийн Хурал) — elected by the soum's or district's citizens, it discusses local work, approves the rules and the local budget, and reports to the citizens; the Governor's office (Засаг даргын тамгын газар) carries the Khural's approved plan out, and the governor is appointed on the Khural's nomination; the court (шүүх) protects a person's rights — anyone who believes a right was violated may turn to it; and the school itself is a state organisation, from which pupils have rights to information (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other (any close wording).**
