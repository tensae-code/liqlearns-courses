# Hands-on — Our Local State Bodies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 23 "Local state organisations" (Орон нутгийн төрийн байгууллага)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A state organisation is an organisation that works for the citizens: citizens of a democratic society elect their state bodies, which guard the citizens' common interests and answer for breaking the law before the court; local self-governance is the Citizens' Representatives' Khural (Иргэдийн Төлөөлөгчдийн Хурал) — elected by the soum's or district's citizens, it discusses local work, approves the rules and the local budget, and reports to the citizens; the Governor's office (Засаг даргын тамгын газар) carries the Khural's approved plan out, and the governor is appointed on the Khural's nomination; the court (шүүх) protects a person's rights — anyone who believes a right was violated may turn to it; and the school itself is a state organisation, from which pupils have rights to information. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
