# Our Local State Bodies

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a state organisation is an organisation that works for the citizens: citizens of a democratic society elect their state bodies, which guard the citizens' common interests and answer for breaking the law before the court; local self-governance is the Citizens' Representatives' Khural (Иргэдийн Төлөөлөгчдийн Хурал) — elected by the soum's or district's citizens, it discusses local work, approves the rules and the local budget, and reports to the citizens; the Governor's office (Засаг даргын тамгын газар) carries the Khural's approved plan out, and the governor is appointed on the Khural's nomination; the court (шүүх) protects a person's rights — anyone who believes a right was violated may turn to it; and the school itself is a state organisation, from which pupils have rights to information — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 23 "Local state organisations" (Орон нутгийн төрийн байгууллага)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 66, teaches: A state organisation is an organisation that works for the citizens: citizens of a democratic society elect their state bodies, which guard the citizens' common interests and answer for breaking the law before the court; local self-governance is the Citizens' Representatives' Khural (Иргэдийн Төлөөлөгчдийн Хурал) — elected by the soum's or district's citizens, it discusses local work, approves the rules and the local budget, and reports to the citizens; the Governor's office (Засаг даргын тамгын газар) carries the Khural's approved plan out, and the governor is appointed on the Khural's nomination; the court (шүүх) protects a person's rights — anyone who believes a right was violated may turn to it; and the school itself is a state organisation, from which pupils have rights to information. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
