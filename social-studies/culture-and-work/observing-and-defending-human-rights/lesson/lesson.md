## Sub lesson 1 — Observing Human Rights

Tanzania Standard Six tz-civic6-en (printed p. 156), teaches: Human rights are fundamental rights from birth to death regardless of race, tribe, age, position, gender or religion (Universal Declaration, UN 1948 — life, quality education, participation among them); critically reflecting on traditions and customs that violate rights, the effects of corruption on rights, and cooperating with social organisations to defend rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way

Tanzania's own way (Standard Six, Civic and Moral Education Standard Six Pupil's Book): TIE teaches civics as a good-governance wheel (the cover itself: transparency, accountability, participation, human rights, integrity, efficiency, reconciliation, democracy, equality, rule of law around “GOOD GOVERNANCE”) and builds the state from the child's own levels — street, village, ward, district, region, nation — with the Union of Tanganyika and Zanzibar as the founding fact and Kiswahili, unity and humanism (utu) as named national treasures. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
