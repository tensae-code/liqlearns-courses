# Hands-on — Observing and Defending Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Observing Human Rights
Work through this part with your hands: human rights are fundamental rights from birth to death regardless of race, tribe, age, position, gender or religion (Universal Declaration, UN 1948 — life, quality education, participation among them); critically reflecting on traditions and customs that violate rights, the effects of corruption on rights, and cooperating with social organisations to defend rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
