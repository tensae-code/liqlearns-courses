# Moral Conscience and Civic Values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 2 La noción de conciencia moral — Moral conscience makes us distinguish good actions from bad; moral and civic values regulate human conduct through our conscious acts under society's laws, norms and customs, and what opposes them is an antivalue; civic values strengthen respect for fundamental rights and guarantees and the fulfilment of duties, preparing responsible citizens of conviction; the class works through written moral dilemmas, and today's media also shape the young's models and values (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 2, teoría adicional — Moral conscience is the knowledge we have or must have of moral norms and rules — the faculty that lets us judge whether our moral conduct is valuable or not; two positions explain its nature: the innatist (conscience is born with the person) and the empirical; from belief in the supernatural it was seen as an expression of divine law — in medieval Christianity, God gave conscience so humans could recognise the natural law — while naturalist positions defend other origins (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 8 is the moral-and-legal year — moral conscience and dilemmas, the Anti-Bullying Law read article by article, the rule of law traced to 1789, and human rights taught through the Americas' own instruments (the American Declaration, the OAS, the anti-torture and forced-disappearance conventions). (any close wording).**
