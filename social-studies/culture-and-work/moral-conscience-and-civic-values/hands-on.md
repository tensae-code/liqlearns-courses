# Hands-on — Moral Conscience and Civic Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 2 La noción de conciencia moral
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Moral conscience makes us distinguish good actions from bad; moral and civic values regulate human conduct through our conscious acts under society's laws, norms and customs, and what opposes them is an antivalue; civic values strengthen respect for fundamental rights and guarantees and the fulfilment of duties, preparing responsible citizens of conviction; the class works through written moral dilemmas, and today's media also shape the young's models and values. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 2, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Moral conscience is the knowledge we have or must have of moral norms and rules — the faculty that lets us judge whether our moral conduct is valuable or not; two positions explain its nature: the innatist (conscience is born with the person) and the empirical; from belief in the supernatural it was seen as an expression of divine law — in medieval Christianity, God gave conscience so humans could recognise the natural law — while naturalist positions defend other origins. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
