# Rights And Duties Of Pedestrians And Drivers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Direitos e deveres do pedestre e do condutor — pedestrians and drivers each hold rights and duties on the road (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Deveres do motorista, do motociclista e do ciclista — the duties of the car driver, the motorcyclist and the cyclist (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Angola's way — the book's own teaching way — a moral-and-civic book built on the child's own reflection — every unit opens with the pupil's own life (my habits, my family, my school) and only then names the rule; equality is taught straight from Angola's own Constitution (art. 23: all are equal before the Constitution and the law), and the book ends not on the state but on national reconciliation — building a better Angola, then a better world. (any close wording).**
