# Hands-on — Deciding Together in a Group

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Three ways groups decide — a leader deciding on members' advice, majority voting, and consensus through discussion — applied to family, village, class, school, church and government. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Samoa's way
Say how Samoa's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
