Turkey's own way (Hayat Bilgisi 2. Sınıf, 1. Kitap): Hayat Bilgisi (Life Studies) is Turkey's integrated primary subject, rebuilt under the Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli as six learning areas; this first book runs Me-and-My-School → My Health and Safety → My Family and Society, every topic in the same rhythm (remember, explore, knowledge journey, practise, what we learned), with the year's special days carried as poems at the head of each area and the Student Oath (Andımız) printed at the front.


Samoa's own way: Social organisation is taught from the inside out — the groups a child belongs to first, then the aiga and its matai, then the village's own circle of groups (matai at the centre, aumaga as the matai's right hand), and only then a neighbour kingdom's political system as the worked example of government.

**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Samoa, Ecuador, Turkey, North Macedonia).**

North Macedonia's own way (Book 1): Society in Grade 2 is the child in three widening circles — school, home, settlement — and already runs a miniature democracy: the class community elects its president by secret ballot, sits in a pupil parliament, and learns decision-making, equality and respect as daily school practice, in a multi-ethnic classroom cast (Macedonian, Albanian and other names throughout).

North Macedonia's own way (Book 2): Grade 3 turns the Grade 2 classroom democracy into explicit civics — children's right to education, the state as education's provider, and group decision-making taught as a process with rules, ending in the difference between a majority decision and a consensus; Theme 3's love of country is carried by the country's own lakes, mountains and the Vardar, then by what a child can contribute and the languages of its classrooms.
