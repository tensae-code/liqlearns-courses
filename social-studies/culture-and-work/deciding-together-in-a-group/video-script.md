# Video script — Deciding Together in a Group

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 9 Book 1 (printed p. 61), teaches: Three ways groups decide — a leader deciding on members' advice, majority voting, and consensus through discussion — applied to family, village, class, school, church and government.

Samoa's own way: Social organisation is taught from the inside out — the groups a child belongs to first, then the aiga and its matai, then the village's own circle of groups (matai at the centre, aumaga as the matai's right hand), and only then a neighbour kingdom's political system as the worked example of government.
