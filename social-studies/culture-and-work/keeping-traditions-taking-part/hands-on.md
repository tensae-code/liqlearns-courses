# Hands-on — Keeping Traditions Taking Part

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Theme 3 Kā saglabāt tradīcijas un līdzdarboties kopienā? (How to preserve traditions and take part in the community) — teaches: celebrating festivals, the festivals of Latvia and their origins, a festival calendar, and the Latvian cultural canon. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
