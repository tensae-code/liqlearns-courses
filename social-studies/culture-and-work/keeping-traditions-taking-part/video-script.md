# Video script — Keeping Traditions Taking Part

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Latvia Grade 4 Sociālās zinības un vēsture teacher methodological book (printed p. 16), teaches: Theme 3 Kā saglabāt tradīcijas un līdzdarboties kopienā? (How to preserve traditions and take part in the community) — teaches: celebrating festivals, the festivals of Latvia and their origins, a festival calendar, and the Latvian cultural canon.

Latvia's own way: the social strand is taught through Latvia’s own festivals and cultural canon and through living Latvian figures (a national sportsman; the sitting State President Edgars Rinkēvičs, used to teach fact versus opinion).
