# All Ethnic Groups Have the Same Rights

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: pupils read Article 7 of Colombia's Political Constitution ("The State recognises and protects the ethnic and cultural diversity of the Colombian nation"), then judge whether the Constitution defends the rights of indigenous, Black/Afro-Colombian, palenquero, raizal and Rrom communities, which indigenous rights they think are violated, and whether constitutionally defined special rights are in fact respected and defended — as Colombia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.
