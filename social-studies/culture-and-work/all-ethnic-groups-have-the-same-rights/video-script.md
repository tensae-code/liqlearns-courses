# Video script — All Ethnic Groups Have the Same Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_01, printed p. 70, teaches: pupils read Article 7 of Colombia's Political Constitution ("The State recognises and protects the ethnic and cultural diversity of the Colombian nation"), then judge whether the Constitution defends the rights of indigenous, Black/Afro-Colombian, palenquero, raizal and Rrom communities, which indigenous rights they think are violated, and whether constitutionally defined special rights are in fact respected and defended.

Colombia's own way: the rights lesson starts from the constitutional text itself — Article 7 read aloud — and immediately asks whether the special rights it defines for ethnic groups are actually respected and defended in the country.
