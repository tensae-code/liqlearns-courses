# Hands-on — All Ethnic Groups Have the Same Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 6 Todos los grupos étnicos tienen los mismos derechos (opening)
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_01, printed p. 70, teaches: pupils read Article 7 of Colombia's Political Constitution ("The State recognises and protects the ethnic and cultural diversity of the Colombian nation"), then judge whether the Constitution defends the rights of indigenous, Black/Afro-Colombian, palenquero, raizal and Rrom communities, which indigenous rights they think are violated, and whether constitutionally defined special rights are in fact respected and defended. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 13) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: the rights lesson starts from the constitutional text itself — Article 7 read aloud — and immediately asks whether the special rights it defines for ethnic groups are actually respected and defended in the country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
