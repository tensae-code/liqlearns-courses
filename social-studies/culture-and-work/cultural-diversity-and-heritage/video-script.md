# Video script — Cultural Diversity and Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Albania Grade 5 Qytetaria teacher guide (printed p. 22), teaches: The community's cultural diversity and heritage, under the rule that every right carries a responsibility.

Albania's own way: Citizenship in Grade 5 is taught as a ladder — family, group, community, then the state: democracy is first practised inside the family and in class elections, local self-government is compared with the class's own elections, and a whole chapter (Kreu 3, The Road) treats the child as pedestrian, cyclist and passenger with duties on the road.
