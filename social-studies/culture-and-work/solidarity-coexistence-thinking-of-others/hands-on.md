# Hands-on — Solidarity Coexistence Thinking of Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 1 "Convivencia solidaria"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 6 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 50, teaches: a society of harmony, not inequality and exclusion, is built on solidarity coexistence — think of others and stand in their place; all persons enjoy the same dignity, respect and opportunities, and hold rights regardless of social, economic, ethnic, religious or gender condition; work for unity, tolerance and dialogue. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 8) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the sixth-grade book widens the lens — democracy is traced from Athens to Guatemala's own authoritarian past, and citizenship ends at the world: Guatemala's Belize claim put to its own citizens in the 2018 popular consultation on the International Court of Justice.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
