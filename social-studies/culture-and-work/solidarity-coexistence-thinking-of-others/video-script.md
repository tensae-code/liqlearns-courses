The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Guatemala Grade 6 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 50, teaches: a society of harmony, not inequality and exclusion, is built on solidarity coexistence — think of others and stand in their place; all persons enjoy the same dignity, respect and opportunities, and hold rights regardless of social, economic, ethnic, religious or gender condition; work for unity, tolerance and dialogue.

Guatemala's own way: the sixth-grade book widens the lens — democracy is traced from Athens to Guatemala's own authoritarian past, and citizenship ends at the world: Guatemala's Belize claim put to its own citizens in the 2018 popular consultation on the International Court of Justice.
