# The Family Budget — Planning Household Money

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the family economy — the household budget: how a family plans its money, as Mongolia's Grade 7 *Нийгэм судлал VII (Social Studies VII)* teaches it in Chapter III Individual and Economy: 3.2 The family economy — the household budget on printed p. 43. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The household budget

Mongolia's Grade 7 book teaches the family economy through the household budget. The topic shows a family counting what comes in and what must go out, and planning the two against each other — the budget is the family's plan for its money, made before the money is spent.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the book's own teaching way

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the Grade 7 year works at the scale of the child's own life — my groups, my culture, my family's economy and budget, our school — before the local-area year in Grade 8. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
