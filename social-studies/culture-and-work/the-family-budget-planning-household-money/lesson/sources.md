Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Economic & Legal Education): a full school Economics course built on Vietnamese cases and current law — the Si Ma Cai periodic market for exchange, pork-price swings for market functions, the 2025 State Budget Law's own revenue/expenditure tables, corporate income-tax tiers and the progressive personal income-tax schedule, and a student's 80/13/7 spending rule for personal finance.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Economic & Legal Education): the macro course is built on Vietnam's national accounts — the expenditure formula GDP = C + I + G + (X − M), GDP/GNI tables against China and Singapore, the 8.12% growth of 2022, sector-share charts 2018 vs 2022, and the 50/30/20-style family budget rule (50% essentials / 20% non-essentials / 30% saving, investment and reserve).

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 7 Venezuela G7 Social Sciences SB Spanish): at secondary level the household budget is taught with the central bank's own family-budget survey and the national food-basket indicator, and consumption is taught as a legal right policed against speculation and hoarding.
