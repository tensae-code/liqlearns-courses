# Hands-on — Work is a Right and a Duty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Working is a right and a duty — the Constitution holds work to be what it is: every person has the right to work and the duty to work, freely choosing their economic activity; adolescents are protected against work that harms their development and against all economic exploitation; everyone who works has the right to a salary that lets them live with dignity with their family, to strike under the law, to belong to a union, and to weekly rest and paid holidays; and by Article 111 everyone has the right to sport and recreation, with physical education obligatory at every level. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
