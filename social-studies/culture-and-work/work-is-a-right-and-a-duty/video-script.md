# Video script — Work is a Right and a Duty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente, printed p. 148, teaches: Working is a right and a duty — the Constitution holds work to be what it is: every person has the right to work and the duty to work, freely choosing their economic activity; adolescents are protected against work that harms their development and against all economic exploitation; everyone who works has the right to a salary that lets them live with dignity with their family, to strike under the law, to belong to a union, and to weekly rest and paid holidays; and by Article 111 everyone has the right to sport and recreation, with physical education obligatory at every level.

Venezuela's own way: the primary course closes with "Misión Ciudadanía" — citizenship as a mission: the constitution's liberty carries the duty to respect others' liberty, democracy is practised in communal councils where "Popular Power is all of us", work is both a right and a duty, and the book ends on peace — human rights begin in the small places near home, in Eleanor Roosevelt's words, and pupils keep a notebook of rights kept and rights violated in their own small world.
