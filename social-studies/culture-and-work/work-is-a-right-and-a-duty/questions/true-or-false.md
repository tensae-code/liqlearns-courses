# Work is a Right and a Duty — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Working is a right and a duty — the Constitution holds work to be what it is: every person has the right to work and the duty to work, freely choosing their economic activity; adolescents are protected against work that harms their development and against all economic exploitation; everyone who works has the right to a salary that lets them live with dignity with their family, to strike under the law, to belong to a union, and to weekly rest and paid holidays; and by Article 111 everyone has the right to sport and recreation, with physical education obligatory at every level.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Venezuela's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
