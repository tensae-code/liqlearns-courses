## Sub lesson 2 — Pakistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Pakistan's own way: citizenship is taught before everything else — a child first learns what a citizen, a global citizen and a digital citizen is, then Pakistan's own 1973 Constitution, its federal and provincial organs with real seat counts, and only then history, geography and economics. Class 5 deepens Class 4 — rights now change with time, the UN and the 1948 Declaration enter, freedom of speech gets its own lesson with John Stuart Mill quoted, and government is compared across federal, provincial AND local levels with a table of who does what. the older book teaches the same state from the other end — culture defined before it is described, languages given a whole chapter with Urdu named the language of national integration, and national integration itself defined, factored and argued like a civics concept, not a slogan. the same Single National Curriculum in the KPK board's English — citizenship taught identically in Peshawar and Lahore, with the global-citizen lesson anchored by Dr Ruth Pfau, the German doctor who gave 57 years to Pakistan's leprosy patients, and Malala Yousafzai, KPK's own daughter, as the worked examples in the pupil's copy. KPK's Class 10 book ends where the PCTB book's culture chapter begins — society first (its features and its named evils: feudalism, the curse of dowry, child labour), then culture defined as a people's inherited way of life, province by province, with the minorities' role and Jinnah's 11 August 1947 speech as the closing civic ground.
