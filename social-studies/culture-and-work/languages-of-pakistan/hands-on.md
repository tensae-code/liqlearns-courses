# Hands-on — Languages of Pakistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 2 Language, Ch. 2 Cultural Diversity: Languages, Ch. 7 Pakistani Languages, Ch. 2 Language, Ch. 4 Origin and Evolution of National and Regional Languages — teaches: Urdu is Pakistan's national language, spoken and understood in every region, while Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, Saraiki, Hindko and others are the mother tongues of its regions; Urdu, the national language, is every group's shared tongue, while each region speaks its own — Punjabi in Punjab, Sindhi in Sindh, Pashto, Balochi, Saraiki, Hindko, Brahui, Kashmiri, Shina and more, each with its own literature; the country's languages one by one — Urdu, the language of national integration; Punjabi; Sindhi with its 52 letters and its poets (Shah Abdul Lateef Bhitai, Sachal Sar Mast); Pushto (Khushal Khan Khatak, Rehman Baba); Balochi; Kashmiri; Urdu the national language, and the regional and local languages named one by one — Balochi, Sindhi, Punjabi, Pashto, Shina, Balti, Kashmiri, Thari, Gujarati, Brahui, Broshiski, Hindko, Saraiki; the chapter traces where Pakistan's national and regional languages came from and how they grew — set by the chapter's own outcomes as required knowledge. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
