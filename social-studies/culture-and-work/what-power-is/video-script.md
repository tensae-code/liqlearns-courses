# Video script — What Power is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Armenia Grade 8 Social Science student, printed p. 86, teaches: power as realising your will against others' will; power in the family, in the legislature and in politics, and "power belongs to the people" as the democratic case.

Armenia's own way: the democracy year opens from Armenia's own Constitution — Articles 1–2 ("the Republic of Armenia is a sovereign, democratic, social, rule-of-law state; power belongs to the people") — and every value is then tested against daily-life cases before rights are sorted by category and by holder.
