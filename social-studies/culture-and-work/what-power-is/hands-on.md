# Hands-on — What Power is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: power as realising your will against others' will; power in the family, in the legislature and in politics, and "power belongs to the people" as the democratic case. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
