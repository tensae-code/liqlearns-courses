## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the book's own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 9 Tolybekova): the whole Grade 9 year is Kazakhstan itself — Part 1 walks the country's own relief, climate, rivers, lakes, natural zones and protected areas (opening with the research of Kazakhstani geographers, Shokan Ualikhanov first), and Part 2 its population, industries, economic regions and place in the world economy, ending with the geographic position of the Republic of Kazakhstan (§64).

Kazakhstan's own way (Kazakhstan Grade 9 Sakhova NIS): the NIS trial Grade 9 course is organised by problems rather than by the standard sections — forecasting lithospheric cataclysms and safety measures (3.1.2–3.1.3), the causes of water scarcity (3.3.4), the noosphere and its development in Kazakhstan (3.4.1–3.4.2), and a research lesson on the very concept of sustainable development (5.1.4). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
