The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 4 Identity & Citizenship TG T2, printed pp. 33–38, teaches: The vote framed with the subject's own master-word — a trust (أمانة) to be guarded and used honestly; the longest lesson of the term (4 periods).

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 4, Term 2): the primary civics course ends at the ballot box — a nine-year-old in Oman learns to elect who represents her, to be an active member of her community, and that her vote is an أمانة, a trust, given four full periods; then the same word — trust — is carried to the whole planet.
