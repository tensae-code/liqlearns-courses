# Hands-on — Citizenship and Patriotism Sierra Leone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: who a citizen is, the five types of citizenship (by birth, registration/marriage, naturalisation, adoption, honorary), patriotism, and a citizen's duties — respect the constitution, protect government property, vote, pay taxes, help the police keep order. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
