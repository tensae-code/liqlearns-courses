## Sub lesson 1 — Citizenship and Patriotism Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's SL General Paper Class 6 teacher guide (printed pp. 92–93), teaches: Who a citizen is, the five types of citizenship (by birth, registration/marriage, naturalisation, adoption, honorary), patriotism, and a citizen's duties — respect the constitution, protect government property, vote, pay taxes, help the police keep order. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sierra Leone's way — the book's own teaching way

Sierra Leone's own way: primary civics is taught as Sierra Leone's own citizenship — the flag's green-white-blue meanings, the coat of arms, anthem and pledge, and the country's own provinces, districts and 190 chiefdoms. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
