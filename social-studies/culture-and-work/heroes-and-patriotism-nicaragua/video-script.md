Nicaragua Grade 2 Convivencia y Civismo G2 (printed p. 58), teaches: Nicaragua's heroes and heroines as examples of patriotism.

Nicaragua Grade 3 Convivencia y Civismo G3 (printed p. 59), teaches: Remembering Nicaragua's heroes and heroines.

Nicaragua Grade 5 Convivencia y Civismo G5 (printed p. 72), teaches: Heroes and founding figures worthy of imitation.

Nicaragua Grade 4 Convivencia y Civismo G4 (printed p. 64), teaches: The founding figures and makers of the homeland.

Nicaragua's own way: civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book. Grade 3 civics adds Nicaragua-specific law to the values base — women's rights under Ley 779 and a Mother Earth declaration — and respect for public and private institutions as a civic duty. the upper-primary civics book teaches the state itself — Constitution, Executive and Judicial powers, the DGI tax office — and sets Nicaragua's symbols beside all six Central American neighbours'. Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State.
