## Sub lesson 5 — Nicaragua's way — the books' own teaching way

Nicaragua's own way: civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book. Grade 3 civics adds Nicaragua-specific law to the values base — women's rights under Ley 779 and a Mother Earth declaration — and respect for public and private institutions as a civic duty. the upper-primary civics book teaches the state itself — Constitution, Executive and Judicial powers, the DGI tax office — and sets Nicaragua's symbols beside all six Central American neighbours'. Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
