# Hands-on — Freedom in a Democracy Political Economic and Religious

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ch. 10 Freedom
Work through this part with your hands: freedom in a democracy is real only where it stops at another's freedom, and it comes in three kinds — political freedom, economic freedom and religious freedom. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ch. 10 Economic and religious freedom
Work through this part with your hands: economic freedom (any lawful occupation, and workers' rights) and religious freedom (to follow and to propagate one's own faith) worked as the second and third of democracy's freedoms. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: systems of government are taught as a ladder the pupil climbs from Sri Lanka's own unitary state outward — democracy defined through Lincoln and the French declaration, its three freedoms separated and worked one by one, dictatorship and monarchy set against it with Mussolini, Hitler and Ashoka as the named cases, and human rights told as a single long struggle from Magna Carta to the UN Charter and the regional charters of Europe, America and Africa.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
