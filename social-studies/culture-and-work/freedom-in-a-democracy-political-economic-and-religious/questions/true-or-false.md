# Freedom in a Democracy Political Economic and Religious — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: freedom in a democracy is real only where it stops at another's freedom, and it comes in three kinds — political freedom, economic freedom and religious freedom.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: economic freedom (any lawful occupation, and workers' rights) and religious freedom (to follow and to propagate one's own faith) worked as the second and third of democracy's freedoms.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Sri Lanka's student books only, named in lesson/sources.md.**
