# Freedom in a Democracy Political Economic and Religious

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: freedom in a democracy is real only where it stops at another's freedom, and it comes in three kinds — political freedom, economic freedom and religious freedom — as Sri Lanka's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 10 Freedom

Sri Lanka Grade 10 Social Education 1998, printed p. 94, teaches: Freedom in a democracy is real only where it stops at another's freedom, and it comes in three kinds — political freedom, economic freedom and religious freedom. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 10 Economic and religious freedom

Sri Lanka Grade 10 Social Education 1998, printed p. 95, teaches: Economic freedom (any lawful occupation, and workers' rights) and religious freedom (to follow and to propagate one's own faith) worked as the second and third of democracy's freedoms. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way

Sri Lanka's own way: systems of government are taught as a ladder the pupil climbs from Sri Lanka's own unitary state outward — democracy defined through Lincoln and the French declaration, its three freedoms separated and worked one by one, dictatorship and monarchy set against it with Mussolini, Hitler and Ashoka as the named cases, and human rights told as a single long struggle from Magna Carta to the UN Charter and the regional charters of Europe, America and Africa. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
