# Uniting the Somali Nation

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: national unity as the first political duty — the one nation above clan and region — as Somalia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Mideynta Ummadda Soomaaliyeed (Uniting the Somali Nation)

Somalia Grade 6 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 8), teaches: national unity as the first political duty — the one nation above clan and region. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 6): by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
