# Uniting the Somali Nation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Mideynta Ummadda Soomaaliyeed (Uniting the Somali Nation)" is mainly about —
a) national unity as the first political duty — the one nation above clan and region  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) national unity as the first political duty — the one nation above clan and region**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The part of this lesson called "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" is mainly about —
a) by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed.  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed.**
