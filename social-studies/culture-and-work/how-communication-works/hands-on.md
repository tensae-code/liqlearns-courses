# Hands-on — How Communication Works

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.1 "How do we communicate?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Communication (харилцаа) is the ordered, continuous foundation of social life — without it no one could make themselves understood, and misunderstanding is where conflict is born; the process itself, part by part: the sender, encoding (илэрхийлэх — putting the message into words, signs and gestures), the channel (уулзах, утсаар ярих, захидал, цахим шуудан — meeting, phone, letter, e-mail), decoding, the receiver, feedback (эргэх холбоо), the environment the exchange happens in, and noise (саад) — the factors that distort a message on its way; and communication itself changes as technology and globalisation change the society around it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
