# How Communication Works

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: communication (харилцаа) is the ordered, continuous foundation of social life — without it no one could make themselves understood, and misunderstanding is where conflict is born; the process itself, part by part: the sender, encoding (илэрхийлэх — putting the message into words, signs and gestures), the channel (уулзах, утсаар ярих, захидал, цахим шуудан — meeting, phone, letter, e-mail), decoding, the receiver, feedback (эргэх холбоо), the environment the exchange happens in, and noise (саад) — the factors that distort a message on its way; and communication itself changes as technology and globalisation change the society around it — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 9 is where the spiral turns civic — communication first (how people actually reach each other), then rights as a working system of state duties and citizen duties, then the state itself: how a law is made, how a cabinet decides, how a court sits, and the seven doors through which a citizen walks into all three.
