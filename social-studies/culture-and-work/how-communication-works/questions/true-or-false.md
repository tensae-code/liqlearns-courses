# How Communication Works — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: communication (харилцаа) is the ordered, continuous foundation of social life — without it no one could make themselves understood, and misunderstanding is where conflict is born; the process itself, part by part: the sender, encoding (илэрхийлэх — putting the message into words, signs and gestures), the channel (уулзах, утсаар ярих, захидал, цахим шуудан — meeting, phone, letter, e-mail), decoding, the receiver, feedback (эргэх холбоо), the environment the exchange happens in, and noise (саад) — the factors that distort a message on its way; and communication itself changes as technology and globalisation change the society around it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
