# Video script — How Communication Works

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 9 Social Studies, printed p. 6, teaches: Communication (харилцаа) is the ordered, continuous foundation of social life — without it no one could make themselves understood, and misunderstanding is where conflict is born; the process itself, part by part: the sender, encoding (илэрхийлэх — putting the message into words, signs and gestures), the channel (уулзах, утсаар ярих, захидал, цахим шуудан — meeting, phone, letter, e-mail), decoding, the receiver, feedback (эргэх холбоо), the environment the exchange happens in, and noise (саад) — the factors that distort a message on its way; and communication itself changes as technology and globalisation change the society around it.

Mongolia's own way (Book 7): Grade 9 is where the spiral turns civic — communication first (how people actually reach each other), then rights as a working system of state duties and citizen duties, then the state itself: how a law is made, how a cabinet decides, how a court sits, and the seven doors through which a citizen walks into all three.
