# Hands-on — When a Party is Banned Freedom of Association in Costa Rica

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the 1949 Constitution's article 98 gives citizens the right to group in parties and in the same breath bans parties of communist tendency; the book weighs the two motives (1948 resentments, Cold War anticommunism), calls the restriction very dangerous because any group could be labelled "slightly communist" with no appeal, prints Manuel Mora's speech after ten years of forced silence, and closes with the 1975 repeal. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Costa Rica's way
Say how Costa Rica's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
