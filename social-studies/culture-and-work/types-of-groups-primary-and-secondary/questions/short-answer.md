# Types of Groups Primary and Secondary — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: What is a group? Primary and secondary groups — a group is two or more persons linked by a common purpose or interest, and groups give us a sense of direction, reveal our attitudes, organise us for positive development and prepare us for the world; primary groups are small and face-to-face with intimacy and permanence — the family and the class with its teacher — while secondary groups are larger and lack that intimacy, like the whole school at General Assembly (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Peer, formal, informal, interest and negative groups — the peer group of the same age and status is very influential and can help members develop loyalty, make and abide by rules and gain belonging; formal groups have rules, membership requirements and an organised structure (the school, Girl Guides, Boy Scouts, Lions Clubs); informal groups form spontaneously for the needs of the moment; interest groups gather around a shared interest (Bible Club, Debating Society, Drama Group); negative groups display attitudes society does not expect — taking drugs, breaking laws and vandalism (any close wording).**
