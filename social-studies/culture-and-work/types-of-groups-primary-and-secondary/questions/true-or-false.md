# Types of Groups Primary and Secondary — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: A group is two or more persons linked by a common purpose or interest, and groups give us a sense of direction, reveal our attitudes, organise us for positive development and prepare us for the world; primary groups are small and face-to-face with intimacy and permanence — the family and the class with its teacher — while secondary groups are larger and lack that intimacy, like the whole school at General Assembly.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: The peer group of the same age and status is very influential and can help members develop loyalty, make and abide by rules and gain belonging; formal groups have rules, membership requirements and an organised structure (the school, Girl Guides, Boy Scouts, Lions Clubs); informal groups form spontaneously for the needs of the moment; interest groups gather around a shared interest (Bible Club, Debating Society, Drama Group); negative groups display attitudes society does not expect — taking drugs, breaking laws and vandalism.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
