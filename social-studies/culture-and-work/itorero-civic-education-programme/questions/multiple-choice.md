# Itorero Civic Education Programme — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Itorero ry'Igihugu  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Itorero ry'Igihugu**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Itorero as a national civic-education programme — a home-grown solution introduced by the government to rebuild the nation  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Itorero as a national civic-education programme — a home-grown solution introduced by the government to rebuild the nation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Rwanda's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Rwanda's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches Itorero as a national civic-education programme — a home-grown solution introduced by the government to rebuild the nation.  b) Rwanda's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) This lesson teaches Itorero as a national civic-education programme — a home-grown solution introduced by the government to rebuild the nation.**
