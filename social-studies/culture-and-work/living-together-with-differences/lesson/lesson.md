## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way: the new Moral, Cívica y Ética Ciudadana guides teach citizenship as four 8-week learning projects, each run observe–think–act–talk and ending in a pupil-run values campaign for the school and community; the Dominican content is explicit — the social, democratic rule-of-law State, the Constitution, the powers of the State and the símbolos patrios. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
