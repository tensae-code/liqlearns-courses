# Video script — Diversity Many Peoples Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pakistan's Grade 4 SS4 (printed pp. 7–8); Grade 5 SS5 (printed p. 11); Grade 4 SS4-KPK (printed p. 7), teaches: Ch. 1 Diversity, Ch. 2 Diverse Cultural Groups living in Pakistan; Cultural Diversity in Pakistan, Ch. 1 Diversity and Tolerance: Diversity — teaches: people differ in race, religion, language and region (Pakistan's own mix, with Canada, Spain, Nigeria, Singapore and America as world examples) and that difference is called diversity; Hassan's evening in the park, where children of every dress and faith play together and share Diwali, Baisakhi, Christmas and Spring Festival joys; Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, Saraiki, Hindko, Kashmiri, Gilgiti and other groups each keep their own dress, food, festivals and folk songs inside one country; Islam is the faith of about 97% and every other faith lives freely beside it; people of different colour, caste, religion, language and area live together in Pakistan, leading their lives on the basis of common characteristics — diversity named and defined.

Pakistan's own way: citizenship is taught before everything else — a child first learns what a citizen, a global citizen and a digital citizen is, then Pakistan's own 1973 Constitution, its federal and provincial organs with real seat counts, and only then history, geography and economics. Class 5 deepens Class 4 — rights now change with time, the UN and the 1948 Declaration enter, freedom of speech gets its own lesson with John Stuart Mill quoted, and government is compared across federal, provincial AND local levels with a table of who does what. the same Single National Curriculum in the KPK board's English — citizenship taught identically in Peshawar and Lahore, with the global-citizen lesson anchored by Dr Ruth Pfau, the German doctor who gave 57 years to Pakistan's leprosy patients, and Malala Yousafzai, KPK's own daughter, as the worked examples in the pupil's copy.
