# Hands-on — Diversity Many Peoples Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 1 Diversity, Ch. 2 Diverse Cultural Groups living in Pakistan; Cultural Diversity in Pakistan, Ch. 1 Diversity and Tolerance: Diversity — teaches: people differ in race, religion, language and region (Pakistan's own mix, with Canada, Spain, Nigeria, Singapore and America as world examples) and that difference is called diversity; Hassan's evening in the park, where children of every dress and faith play together and share Diwali, Baisakhi, Christmas and Spring Festival joys; Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, Balochi, Saraiki, Hindko, Kashmiri, Gilgiti and other groups each keep their own dress, food, festivals and folk songs inside one country; Islam is the faith of about 97% and every other faith lives freely beside it; people of different colour, caste, religion, language and area live together in Pakistan, leading their lives on the basis of common characteristics — diversity named and defined. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
