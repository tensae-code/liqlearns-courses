## Sub lesson 1 — Women's organisations

Guyana Grade 9 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 3 (printed pp. 53–56), teaches: Individual women and women's organisations have worked for the improvement of the lives of women in Guyana since the first, the Women's Political and Economic Organisation, was formed in July 1946; by the end of 1991 there were over forty, grouped as social service, trade union, political, religious and professional — governmental bodies such as the Women's Affairs Bureau (1981), whose mission is to work towards the removal of all discrimination against women, and NGOs such as the Red Thread Women's Development Project, the Albouystown Women's Support Group and the Anglican Mothers' Union. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: the secondary book teaches the same citizenship at national scale — CARICOM as the region's own institution, the family as society's oldest institution, women's equality written into the 1980 Constitution and the Acts of the 1990s, and the state answering pollution through the Environmental Protection Agency.
