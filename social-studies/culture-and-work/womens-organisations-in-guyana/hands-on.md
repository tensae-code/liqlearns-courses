# Hands-on — Womens Organisations in Guyana

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Individual women and women's organisations have worked for the improvement of the lives of women in Guyana since the first, the Women's Political and Economic Organisation, was formed in July 1946; by the end of 1991 there were over forty, grouped as social service, trade union, political, religious and professional — governmental bodies such as the Women's Affairs Bureau (1981), whose mission is to work towards the removal of all discrimination against women, and NGOs such as the Red Thread Women's Development Project, the Albouystown Women's Support Group and the Anglican Mothers' Union.
