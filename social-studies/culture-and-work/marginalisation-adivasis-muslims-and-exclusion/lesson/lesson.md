## Sub lesson 1 — Ch 7 Understanding Marginalisation

India Grade 8 Social and Political Life-III (printed p. 80), teaches: to be marginalised is to be pushed to the fringes and denied resources and rights — how Adivasis lost forest and land, and how Muslims face exclusion. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — India's way — the book's own teaching way

India's own way: The Constitution is the spine of the whole book — every later institution (Parliament, courts, police) and every injustice (marginalisation) is measured against it, and marginalised people are shown invoking its Fundamental Rights to force change. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
