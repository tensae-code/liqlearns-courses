# Video script — Duties Toward the Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies (printed pp. 94–95), teaches: Love of the homeland and sacrifice for its sake — we all love the homeland on whose land we live and under whose sky, and we all work for protecting the homeland and raising it; commitment to the regulations and laws — respecting the regulations and laws the state sets, in order to organise society's life and protect it and achieve the principle of justice and equality among its individuals, for there is a strong relation between commitment to the regulations and laws and the peoples' progress, and everyone, in whatever position — pupil, teacher or official — must apply the order with trustworthiness and sincerity, believing in the homeland's sanctity; and preserving public property — by rationalising water consumption, preserving the parks and the electricity, and preserving public property such as schools and hospitals: I keep my school's order, I do not tamper with the public parks' facilities, I throw waste in the places designated for it, and I do not waste water, because it is the secret of life (the chapter's fourth duty, preserving the environment, printed p.

Iraq's own way: the state is defined as institutions that serve, its pillars are named homeland, people, federal authorities and sovereignty, and citizenship is taught as a two-way street — the state's services (free education first) on one side, and on the other a good citizen whose first quality is knowing his own abilities so he can fix his duties and his rights.
