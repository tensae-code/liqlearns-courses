# Hands-on — Values of the Righteous Caliphs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the values in the caliphs' lives — giving (Abu Bakr giving all his wealth, Uthman equipping the Army of Hardship with three hundred camels and a thousand dinars), justice and good dealing (Uthman returning the land price to the seller who felt cheated, and the Prophet's saying that God admits to Paradise one who is easy as buyer, seller, judge and claimant), and Umar's personal care for a blind old woman of Madinah. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
