# Video script — The Right To A Name Nationality And Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Angola Educação Moral e Cívica G5 (printed p. 98), teaches: Every child has the right to a name, a nationality and citizenship.

Angola Educação Moral e Cívica G5 (printed p. 100), teaches: Civil registration — why registering a birth matters: it proves who you are and opens your rights.

Angola's own way: a moral-and-civic book built on the child's own reflection — every unit opens with the pupil's own life (my habits, my family, my school) and only then names the rule; equality is taught straight from Angola's own Constitution (art. 23: all are equal before the Constitution and the law), and the book ends not on the state but on national reconciliation — building a better Angola, then a better world.
