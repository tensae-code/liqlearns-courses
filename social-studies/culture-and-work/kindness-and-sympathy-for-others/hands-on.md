# Hands-on — Kindness and Sympathy for Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A person with sympathy
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Feeling for others and helping them, in the chapter's picture stories. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Understand with sympathy
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Including and helping friends who cannot walk or see well — in football, on the bicycle, in the classroom. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Needs to understand
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Understanding others with sympathy — the "understandable one" Ma Sabai, who helps the needy, pities the young and respects the elder. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Loving-kindness and goodwill
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Metta in action — wishing others well and showing it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
