# Kindness and Sympathy for Others — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: feeling for others and helping them, in the chapter's picture stories.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: including and helping friends who cannot walk or see well — in football, on the bicycle, in the classroom.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: understanding others with sympathy — the "understandable one" Ma Sabai, who helps the needy, pities the young and respects the elder.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: metta in action — wishing others well and showing it.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
