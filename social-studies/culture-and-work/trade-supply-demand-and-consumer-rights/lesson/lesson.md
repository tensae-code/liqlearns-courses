## Sub lesson 1 — When supply falls short

When **demand rises and supply falls short, the economy is shaken** and producers cannot work at full capacity — in practice the imbalance bites through **fuel and foreign-exchange** supply and demand.

**A market is the institution where individuals and organisations meet to exchange goods and services** out of want and buying power. Its main actors: **producers, cooperatives, wholesalers, retailers and consumers**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Equilibrium and the perfect-competition test (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide puts price on a graph (teacher guide — evidence): **price is set by the interaction of supply and demand, shown at the market equilibrium point** — where the supply and demand curves cross.

A **competitive ("perfect competition") market** is tested by conditions (teacher guide — evidence): **the goods offered are exactly the same; buyers and sellers have knowledge of the market; and there are very many buyers and sellers, each with negligible impact on price**.

## Sub lesson 3 — Malpractice, protection and the consumer's claim

To stabilise markets in cost-of-living crises and protect consumers, **governments act** — and Ethiopia's consumer-protection legal framework, **Proclamation No. 813/2006 as printed**, gives the state room to act against unfair trade and threats to consumer safety, with the **federal Trade and Industry Minister** and the **regional trade and market-development bureaus** as the enforcing authorities.

The guide names the deliberate practices behind Ethiopia's supply problems (teacher guide — evidence): **black marketing, hoarding, profiteering, unethical advertising** — and in its review key **hoarding, profiteering, inferior quality, wrong weight and measures and adulteration**. Its keyed consumer rights are concrete (teacher guide — evidence): the right to **accurate information on the quality of goods purchased**, to **selectively buy** goods and services, and to **be received humbly and respectfully** by any business person.
