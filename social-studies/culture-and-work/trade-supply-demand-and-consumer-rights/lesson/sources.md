Jamaica's own way (Grades 7–9 Social Studies, curriculum guide — evidence): one guide carries all three secondary grades, each year mixing Civics, heritage and a strong resources-and-environment Geography spine — natural and human resources, movements of the Earth, Caribbean hazards, climate change, green technology and tourism — taught through Jamaican case material (bauxite communities, Caribbean disasters). That evidence is stocked here as the way Jamaica teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Mongolia's own way (Grade 9): Social Studies is a five-chapter spiral (Individual & Society, Individual & Culture, Individual & Economy, Human Rights & Law, Public Administration) continued from Grade 8; there is no physical-geography chapter — Mongolia's geography is a separate subject (Газарзүй) — so this book's Geography-wave content is its culture and economics teaching, grounded in Mongolian surveys, wage tables and heritage inventories. The book's Sociology parts (Chapter 1, printed pp. 6–13) and Law/Civics parts (Chapters 4–5, printed pp. 62–92) are named in the placements, not placed. That way is stocked here as the way Mongolia teaches this thing.

Pairing warning (honesty): Book 2 is an Addis Ababa bureau guide in English; Book 1 is an Amhara bureau student book in Amharic. They are NOT edition-matched companions — the guide's unit order differs, its activity numbers key to its own companion textbook, and whole student-book topics have no guide counterpart. Guide material is used only as labelled teacher evidence, never as a page-key to the student book. Pupil content in this lesson comes from the Amhara student book.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 6) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 6 shelter becomes settlement geography — development of settlements in Zimbabwe, siting of shelter in rural and urban areas, and disaster and risk management; transport adds road signs/signals/markings and Zimbabwe's transport and communication links with other countries, plus ICT development. That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 np-g7): Grade 7 Earth science is motion-first — Earth's daily and annual motion, surface form and the factors that change it, surface form's relation to human life — then Nepal's climate, the seven provinces of federal Nepal, climate change, disasters and map work; population is taught as structure, change and management.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 TG np-g7tg) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide teaches the same Grade 7 course as np-g7 with globe-and-ball demonstrations of rotation/revolution, chart work on Nepal's demographic structure (age, sex, religion, caste, language distributions), and discussion-led lessons; its geography content mirrors the student book lesson for lesson, so placements repeat with guide page references. That evidence is stocked here as the way Nepal teaches this thing; it is cited as evidence only — Nepal is counted in this lesson through its student book(s).

Jordan's own way (Jordan G3 SS T2): Grade 3 Term 2 contrasts past and present (trade, production, the smart consumer), then studies Earth's natural features in Jordan, environment care, Jordan's map and its antiquities.

Jordan's own way (Jordan G5 SS T2): Term 2 teaches the economy by sectors, then location skills (relative/absolute location, GPS) and Jordan's Arab world region by region — Nile Valley and Horn of Africa, then the Maghreb.
