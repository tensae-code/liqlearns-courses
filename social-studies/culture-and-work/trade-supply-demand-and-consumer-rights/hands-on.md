# Hands-on — Trade — supply, demand and consumer rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Walk a market chain on paper: producer, cooperative, wholesaler, retailer, consumer — write what each hands to the next for one real good.

## Activity 2
Draw two crossing lines, label them supply and demand, and ring the crossing point. That ring is the equilibrium point.

## Activity 3
Write the consumer's three rights as a small poster for a shop wall.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say what happens when demand rises and supply falls short, and through what it bites. Name the market's five actors. Read an equilibrium point and state the perfect-competition conditions (teacher guide — evidence). Name the malpractices and the consumer's three keyed rights (teacher guide — evidence), and the proclamation as printed.
