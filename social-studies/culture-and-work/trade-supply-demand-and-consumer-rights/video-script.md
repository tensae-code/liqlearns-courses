Reading an equilibrium point.

Draw two lines that cross. One line climbs: supply — the higher the price, the more sellers offer. One line falls: demand — the higher the price, the less buyers take.

Where the two lines cross, ring the point. That is the market equilibrium point.

At that point, the amount offered and the amount wanted meet. The guide's teaching is short: price is set by the interaction of supply and demand — and the crossing point is where you read it.
