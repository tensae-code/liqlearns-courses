# Trade — supply, demand and consumer rights — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When demand rises and supply falls short —
a) Prices always fall  b) The economy is shaken and producers cannot work at full capacity  c) Trade stops being needed  d) Imports end
**Answer: b) The economy is shaken and producers cannot work at full capacity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A market's main actors include —
a) Only consumers  b) Producers, cooperatives, wholesalers, retailers and consumers  c) Only government  d) Only wholesalers
**Answer: b) Producers, cooperatives, wholesalers, retailers and consumers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Price is set at (teacher guide — evidence) —
a) The tax point  b) The market equilibrium point, where supply and demand interact  c) The border  d) The factory gate only
**Answer: b) The market equilibrium point, where supply and demand interact**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A perfect-competition condition is —
a) One seller only  b) Goods offered are exactly the same, with very many buyers and sellers  c) Secret prices  d) No market knowledge
**Answer: b) Goods offered are exactly the same, with very many buyers and sellers**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Which is a named malpractice (teacher guide — evidence)?
a) Hoarding  b) Fair weighing  c) Clear labelling  d) Respectful service
**Answer: a) Hoarding**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A consumer's keyed right is —
a) To set all prices  b) Accurate information on the quality of goods purchased  c) To take goods free  d) To close shops
**Answer: b) Accurate information on the quality of goods purchased**
