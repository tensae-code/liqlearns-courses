# Trade — supply, demand and consumer rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Through what two supplies does the imbalance bite in practice?
**Answer:** Fuel and foreign exchange.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** State the three perfect-competition conditions.
**Answer:** Goods exactly the same; buyers and sellers have knowledge of the market; very many buyers and sellers, each with negligible impact on price.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the enforcing authorities under the framework as printed.
**Answer:** The federal Trade and Industry Minister and the regional trade and market-development bureaus.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the consumer's three keyed rights.
**Answer:** Accurate information on the quality of goods purchased; to selectively buy goods and services; to be received humbly and respectfully by any business person.
