# Hands-on — Unofficial Symbols of the Country Baiterek Berkut and the Golden Man

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a country's unofficial symbols beside its state symbols — for Kazakhstan the Baiterek, the Mangilik El triumphal arch, the Golden Man on the winged bars, the soaring berkut and steppe wormwood, and the folk name Uly Dala eli, the Land of the Great Steppe. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kazakhstan's way
Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
