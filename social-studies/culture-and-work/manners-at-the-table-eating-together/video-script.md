Pakistan's Grade 6 Ethics6 (printed pp. 54–56), teaches: Ch. 5 Manners of Eating (کھانا کھانے کے آداب) — teaches: eat together, wash hands, take only what you will finish, never waste food while others hunger, and remember the poor at your table — manners that turn a meal into gratitude.

Pakistan's own way: the senior primary Ethics book turns values into civic machinery — laws learned from a flock of birds, time priced like money, traffic signs taught as a citizen's duty — and opens with story after story where greed ruins kings and gratitude saves the lame.
