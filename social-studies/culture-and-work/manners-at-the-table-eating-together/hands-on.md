# Hands-on — Manners at the Table Eating Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 5 Manners of Eating (کھانا کھانے کے آداب) — teaches: eat together, wash hands, take only what you will finish, never waste food while others hunger, and remember the poor at your table — manners that turn a meal into gratitude. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
