# Hands-on — The Islands and Ports of the South

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: some of our country's people travel to southern Iran in winter or at the Nowruz holiday, for the south is not cold in winter and its mild, pleasant weather suits tourists; on the map: the provinces beside the Persian Gulf and the Sea of Oman, a great port, and the islands of Kish and Qeshm. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
