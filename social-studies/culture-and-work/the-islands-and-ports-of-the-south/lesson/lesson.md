## Sub lesson 1 — The Islands and Ports of the South

Iran Social Studies Grade 6 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 48, teaches: some of our country's people travel to southern Iran in winter or at the Nowruz holiday, for the south is not cold in winter and its mild, pleasant weather suits tourists; on the map: the provinces beside the Persian Gulf and the Sea of Oman, a great port, and the islands of Kish and Qeshm. ## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the last special-needs primary book turns outward — life skills at school and at work, then the country learned by travelling it (green Gilan and the Caspian ports, the warm southern islands), and the institutions that stand behind the citizen: the insurance booklet and the Welfare Organisation's card.
