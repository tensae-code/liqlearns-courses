# Hands-on — Government Social Programmes in the Municipality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the national government's municipal welfare programmes (Amor para los más chiquitos, Usura Cero, Hambre Cero, Casas/Calles para el Pueblo, Plan Techo, school nutrition and school packs) as rights-restoring public action. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Programas sociales del Gobierno de Reconciliación y Unidad Nacional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Government's emblematic social programmes (Hambre Cero, Usura Cero, Viviendas Dignas) and free universal health and education as restored rights under the Person, Family and Community model. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bono Productivo Alimentario — Hambre Cero
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Hambre Cero productive food bond (pregnant cattle and pigs, poultry, seeds) given to rural families with the woman as partner and owner, for food security and sovereignty. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Programa Social Usura Cero
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: usura Cero — ten years of micro-credit for women opening small businesses, with repeat credits for successful partners. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Programa Hambre Cero: un aporte al sector primario
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Hambre Cero productive bond (pregnant cow and sow, poultry, seeds, building materials) delivered to poor families with the woman as partner-owner — over 50,000 cows and 600,000 birds distributed, women organised in 1,411 nuclei and 225 cooperatives — as the government's road out of poverty. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Usura Cero
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Usura Cero micro-credit programme for women, presented beside Hambre Cero as state social policy. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Alternativas de solución ante la vulnerabilidad
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the State's alternatives against national vulnerability — the National Human Development Plan to reduce poverty, support to the disaster-prevention system (SINAPRED), social programmes to eradicate poverty, and Civil Defence organisation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
