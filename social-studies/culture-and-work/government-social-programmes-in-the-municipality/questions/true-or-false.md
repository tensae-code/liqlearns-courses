# Government Social Programmes in the Municipality — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The national government's municipal welfare programmes (Amor para los más chiquitos, Usura Cero, Hambre Cero, Casas/Calles para el Pueblo, Plan Techo, school nutrition and school packs) as rights-restoring public action.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: The Government's emblematic social programmes (Hambre Cero, Usura Cero, Viviendas Dignas) and free universal health and education as restored rights under the Person, Family and Community model.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: The Hambre Cero productive food bond (pregnant cattle and pigs, poultry, seeds) given to rural families with the woman as partner and owner, for food security and sovereignty.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part teaches: Usura Cero — ten years of micro-credit for women opening small businesses, with repeat credits for successful partners.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's part teaches: The Hambre Cero productive bond (pregnant cow and sow, poultry, seeds, building materials) delivered to poor families with the woman as partner-owner — over 50,000 cows and 600,000 birds distributed, women organised in 1,411 nuclei and 225 cooperatives — as the government's road out of poverty.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson's part teaches: The Usura Cero micro-credit programme for women, presented beside Hambre Cero as state social policy.
**Answer: True**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** This lesson's part teaches: The State's alternatives against national vulnerability — the National Human Development Plan to reduce poverty, support to the disaster-prevention system (SINAPRED), social programmes to eradicate poverty, and Civil Defence organisation.
**Answer: True**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Nicaragua's student books, named in lesson/sources.md.**
