# Government Social Programmes in the Municipality — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Programas sociales impulsados por el Gobierno en los municipios — The national government's municipal welfare programmes (Amor para los más chiquitos, Usura Cero, Hambre Cero, Casas/Calles para el Pueblo, Plan Techo, school nutrition and school packs) as rights-restoring public action (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Programas sociales del Gobierno de Reconciliación y Unidad Nacional — The Government's emblematic social programmes (Hambre Cero, Usura Cero, Viviendas Dignas) and free universal health and education as restored rights under the Person, Family and Community model (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bono Productivo Alimentario — Hambre Cero — The Hambre Cero productive food bond (pregnant cattle and pigs, poultry, seeds) given to rural families with the woman as partner and owner, for food security and sovereignty (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Programa Social Usura Cero — Usura Cero — ten years of micro-credit for women opening small businesses, with repeat credits for successful partners (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Programa Hambre Cero: un aporte al sector primario — The Hambre Cero productive bond (pregnant cow and sow, poultry, seeds, building materials) delivered to poor families with the woman as partner-owner — over 50,000 cows and 600,000 birds distributed, women organised in 1,411 nuclei and 225 cooperatives — as the government's road out of poverty (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Usura Cero — The Usura Cero micro-credit programme for women, presented beside Hambre Cero as state social policy (any close wording).**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Alternativas de solución ante la vulnerabilidad — The State's alternatives against national vulnerability — the National Human Development Plan to reduce poverty, support to the disaster-prevention system (SINAPRED), social programmes to eradicate poverty, and Civil Defence organisation (any close wording).**
