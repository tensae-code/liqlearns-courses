**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Nicaragua).**

Nicaragua's own way (Book 1 — Estudios Sociales G3 (student book, Spanish)): community is taught through Rama Cay — an ancestral Caribbean island community governed by a council of elders — and citizenship through the current government's named municipal social programmes and the Caribbean Coast autonomy system.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 10 — CCSS Geografía Módulo G10 (student book, Spanish)): even the pure-Geography distance module stops to teach the State's hand in family welfare — free health and education as restored rights, and the named productive and credit programmes, each with the woman as the programme's named partner.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 15 — Ciencias Sociales G10 full-year (student book, Spanish)): the final secondary book presents anti-poverty policy as the State's first answer to national vulnerability — the National Human Development Plan and the named programmes, with women's cooperatives as their delivery form.
