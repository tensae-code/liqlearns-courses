# Hands-on — Joining in School Activities Willingly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: cleaning the classroom, planting trees, giving to poorer classmates, celebrating the teacher — taking part because the school is yours. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
