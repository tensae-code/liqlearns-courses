# What Ideology is — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part 1 teaches: worldview and ideology defined; religious, economic and political ideology as types; ideology as a group's system of beliefs defending its interests, and ideocracy (rule by one ideology held as absolute truth).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part 2 teaches: the ideology theme is taught through one fictional Armenian community — pensioner Aram of Bardzrashen village and his granddaughter Anahit, a municipality worker in Lusaber, argue pensions and a mining plant, and a fictional "Lernastan" election case lets liberal, conservative and socialist forces present rival answers to the same village problem..
**Answer: True**
