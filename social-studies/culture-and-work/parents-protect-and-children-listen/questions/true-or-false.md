# Parents Protect and Children Listen — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: a lion cub who wanders from his parents is chased by wolves and saved by them — parents protect, children stay close and listen.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
