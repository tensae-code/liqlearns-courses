## Sub lesson 1 — Poe Poe's family

Myanmar Morality and Civics, Grade 1 (printed p. 1), teaches: A lion cub who wanders from his parents is chased by wolves and saved by them — parents protect, children stay close and listen. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way

Myanmar's own way: every value is taught as a named story or picture-sequence about one child (or one lion cub) plus a question set — never as a rule list; the patriotism chapter teaches love of country as the eight ethnic peoples in their own dress holding hands around the Independence Monument. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
