# Hands-on — Behaving Well When Travelling

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Путовање и понашање (Travelling and behaviour)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: good behaviour is a duty everywhere — on the street and in transport: judging pictured bus behaviour (giving up seats, not pushing, not hanging off the bus), and in a car never sitting beside the driver but belted in the back seat. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
