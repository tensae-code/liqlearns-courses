# Behaving Well When Travelling

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: good behaviour is a duty everywhere — on the street and in transport: judging pictured bus behaviour (giving up seats, not pushing, not hanging off the bus), and in a car never sitting beside the driver but belted in the back seat — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Путовање и понашање (Travelling and behaviour)

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed p. 32), teaches: good behaviour is a duty everywhere — on the street and in transport: judging pictured bus behaviour (giving up seats, not pushing, not hanging off the bus), and in a car never sitting beside the driver but belted in the back seat. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
