# Video script — Behaving Well When Travelling

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed p. 32), teaches: Good behaviour is a duty everywhere — on the street and in transport: judging pictured bus behaviour (giving up seats, not pushing, not hanging off the bus), and in a car never sitting beside the driver but belted in the back seat.

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays.
