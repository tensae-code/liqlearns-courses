# Behaving Well When Travelling — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Путовање и понашање (Travelling and behaviour) — good behaviour is a duty everywhere — on the street and in transport: judging pictured bus behaviour (giving up seats, not pushing, not hanging off the bus), and in a car never sitting beside the driver but belted in the back seat (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way — good behaviour is a duty everywhere — on the street and in transport: judging pictured bus behaviour (giving up seats, not pushing, not hanging off the bus), and in a car never sitting beside the driver but belted in the back seat (any close wording).**
