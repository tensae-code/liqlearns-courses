## Sub lesson 4 — Nicaragua's way — the books' own teaching way

Nicaragua's own way: the upper-primary civics book teaches the state itself — Constitution, Executive and Judicial powers, the DGI tax office — and sets Nicaragua's symbols beside all six Central American neighbours'. Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State. the first combined book builds the social world in rings — family, neighbours, school, homeland, municipality — and even its taxes lesson is told through the family's shopping receipts and the works taxes build in the child's own community. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
