**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Nicaragua).**

Nicaragua's own way (Book 4 — Convivencia y Civismo G5 (student book, Spanish)): the upper-primary civics book teaches the state itself — Constitution, Executive and Judicial powers, the DGI tax office — and sets Nicaragua's symbols beside all six Central American neighbours'.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 6 — Convivencia y Civismo G4 (student book, Spanish)): Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 7 — Conociendo mi Mundo G2 (student book, Spanish)): the first combined book builds the social world in rings — family, neighbours, school, homeland, municipality — and even its taxes lesson is told through the family's shopping receipts and the works taxes build in the child's own community.
