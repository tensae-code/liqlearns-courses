Nicaragua Grade 5 Convivencia y Civismo G5 (printed p. 41), teaches: Nicaragua's traditional typical foods as heritage.

Nicaragua Grade 4 Convivencia y Civismo G4 (printed p. 51), teaches: Our typical foods and drinks.

Nicaragua Grade 2 Conociendo mi Mundo G2 (printed p. 142), teaches: Nicaragua's typical foods, drinks and sweets.

Nicaragua's own way: the upper-primary civics book teaches the state itself — Constitution, Executive and Judicial powers, the DGI tax office — and sets Nicaragua's symbols beside all six Central American neighbours'. Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State. the first combined book builds the social world in rings — family, neighbours, school, homeland, municipality — and even its taxes lesson is told through the family's shopping receipts and the works taxes build in the child's own community.
