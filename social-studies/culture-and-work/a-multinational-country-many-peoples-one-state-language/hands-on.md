# Hands-on — A Multinational Country Many Peoples One State Language

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Kazakhstan as a multinational country — more than 130 peoples living in friendship and unity, each with its own language and culture, with Kazakh as the state language uniting all. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kazakhstan's way
Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
