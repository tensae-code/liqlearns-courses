# Hands-on — Sierra Leones Ethnic Groups and Their Ways of Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sierra Leone's ~17 ethnic groups (Mende, Temne, Limba, Loko, Sherbro, Krio…), their origins, regions, dress, music, dance, arts and crafts and societies (Poro, Ojei, Gbandbani…). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
