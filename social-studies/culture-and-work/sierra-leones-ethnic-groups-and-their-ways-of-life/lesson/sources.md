Sierra Leone's own way (teacher guide — evidence): the whole syllabus is taught through Sierra Leone's own peoples and institutions — culture is taught ethnic group by ethnic group (Mende, Temne, Limba, Krio…: dress, music, dance, craft, secret societies), and global bodies are taught through what they have done for Sierra Leone itself (UN Special Court, Sierra Leone as the UN's 100th member in 1961).

Sierra Leone's own way (SL General Paper Class 6, teacher guide — evidence): primary civics is taught as Sierra Leone's own citizenship — the flag's green-white-blue meanings, the coat of arms, anthem and pledge, and the country's own provinces, districts and 190 chiefdoms.
