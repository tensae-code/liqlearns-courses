## Sub lesson 1 — The Ethnic Groups of Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's SL SS BECE JSS3 teacher guide (printed pp. 40, 47–50), teaches: Sierra Leone's ~17 ethnic groups (Mende, Temne, Limba, Loko, Sherbro, Krio…), their origins, regions, dress, music, dance, arts and crafts and societies (Poro, Ojei, Gbandbani…). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethnic Groups in Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's SL General Paper Class 6 teacher guide (printed p. 103), teaches: What an ethnic group is, Sierra Leone's ethnic groups, and the groups Sierra Leone shares with Guinea and Liberia (primary-level companion to Book 1's Unit 11/13 treatment). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Sierra Leone's way — the book's own teaching way

Sierra Leone's own way: the whole syllabus is taught through Sierra Leone's own peoples and institutions — culture is taught ethnic group by ethnic group (Mende, Temne, Limba, Krio…: dress, music, dance, craft, secret societies), and global bodies are taught through what they have done for Sierra Leone itself (UN Special Court, Sierra Leone as the UN's 100th member in 1961). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
