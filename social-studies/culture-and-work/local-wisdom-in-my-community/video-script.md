Keberagaman yang Menyatukan — teaches: local wisdom (kearifan lokal) around me — interviewing family, friends and teachers about customs handed down, and why they are kept; the traditions and culture of the community around me, including traditional dress (ageman songkok); guidance for cultural traces full of noble values — heritage still practised locally and the meaning and values inside that heritage; guidance for the variety of local wisdom in the home region and applying its values in daily life; guidance for diversity that unites — identifying local diversity and applying local-wisdom values inside it to keep national unity; essential material and guidance for local wisdom, traditions and attitudes to cultural diversity; cultural traces laden with noble values — the filosofi (philosophy of goodness, wisdom and care) inside inherited traditions; pupils interview ten teachers/staff on why each heritage is still kept; the colours of local wisdom — the variety of kearifan lokal and how local wisdom and culture form a region's identity; diversity that unites — Bhinneka Tunggal Ika as the answer to what holds Indonesia's cultures together, worked through cases on respecting accents, customs and prohibitions.

Indonesia's own way: IPAS Grade 4 teaches community through kearifan lokal (local wisdom) — pupils interview classmates and teachers about habits handed down in their families (jamu drinking, speaking Batak at home) before any national culture is named. the guide's stated aim for Bab IV is unity — pupils dig out the noble values (nilai luhur) inside their own locality's traditions and then apply local-wisdom values to keep persatuan dan kesatuan (unity and oneness) amid diversity. the guide's Bab VI essential material states roles, rules and responsibilities as one civic package across three environments — home first, then school, then masyarakat (society). the primary IPAS course ends its social strand on the state motto itself — Bhinneka Tunggal Ika (unity in diversity) — taught through two discussion cases: a migrant child mocked for her accent, and a grandmother's maghrib prohibition weighed against a lit modern field.
