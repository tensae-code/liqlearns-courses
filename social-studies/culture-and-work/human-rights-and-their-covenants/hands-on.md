# Hands-on — Human Rights and Their Covenants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unidad 10 Ciudadanía e identidad, Los Derechos Humanos
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human rights as a topic with its international frame, including the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights, and a closing classification of human rights (food, clothing, housing, medical care, maternity and childhood protection). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unidad 11 Educación Ciudadana, Derechos humanos y gobernanza
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human rights and governance — the fundamental rights all people possess simply for being human, including freedom of expression. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unidad 12, Derechos humanos y Estado de derecho
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human rights as inherent to every person regardless of gender, religion, ideology, colour or economic condition, guaranteed by the Dominican Constitution and by treaties and conventions, with the 1948 Universal Declaration — and the duty of authorities and citizens to protect, respect and guarantee them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Dominican Republic's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
