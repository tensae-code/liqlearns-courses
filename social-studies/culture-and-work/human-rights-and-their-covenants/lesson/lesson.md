## Sub lesson 4 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way: secondary civics is taught as constitutional history — Duarte's own draft constitution, the 1963 Bosch constitution and the current 2010 text read side by side — and citizenship as practice: civic education defined as learning to contribute to public processes and debate real problems. Grade 9 civics pairs the abstract frame (human rights and governance, the rule of law) with the country's named social ill — a full unit on narcotráfico, its violence and the building of a drug-free society through education. the Grade 10 citizen-education unit is built as close reading — pupils paraphrase the texts aloud in their own words: the Constitution as Carta Magna / Ley de Leyes, sovereignty residing exclusively in the people, Locke's division of powers, and democratic coexistence resting on education and the culture of peace. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
