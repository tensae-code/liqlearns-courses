## Sub lesson 1 — Government and non-government organisations

Fiji Grade 5 Year 5 Social Studies (printed p. 9), teaches: Government organisations run parts of the state (Public Service Commission, Judicial Department, Department of Finance) and NGOs are non-profit voluntary citizens' groups that serve, advocate and bring citizen concerns to government. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Non-government organisations in Fiji

Fiji Grade 9 Social Science Year 9 (printed p. 64), teaches: Fiji's NGOs (Save the Children Fiji, Live and Learn, Red Cross Society, Fiji Women's Crisis Centre, church organisations) share one goal — assisting those who need help with food, education, sanitation, home and health. ## Sub lesson 3 — Fiji's way — the books' own teaching way

Fiji's own way (Grade 5): community is taught as the social groups a child belongs to — family first, then school and community groups — and Fiji's multicultural mix is the civics lesson itself: all born in Fiji are Fijian, each keeping their own culture. Fiji's own way (Grade 9): society is analysed through Fiji's own structures — the iTaukei hierarchy of Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau and Tauvanua with its ascribed chiefly status — and conflict is settled the iTaukei way, by Bulubulu. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
