# Hands-on — Government and Non Governmental Organisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Government and non-government organisations
Work through this part with your hands: Government organisations run parts of the state (Public Service Commission, Judicial Department, Department of Finance) and NGOs are non-profit voluntary citizens' groups that serve, advocate and bring citizen concerns to government. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Non-government organisations in Fiji
Work through this part with your hands: Fiji's NGOs (Save the Children Fiji, Live and Learn, Red Cross Society, Fiji Women's Crisis Centre, church organisations) share one goal — assisting those who need help with food, education, sanitation, home and health. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
