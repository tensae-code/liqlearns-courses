# Government and Non Governmental Organisations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Government organisations run parts of the state (Public Service Commission, Judicial Department, Department of Finance) and NGOs are non-profit voluntary citizens' groups that serve, advocate and bring citizen concerns to government.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Fiji's NGOs (Save the Children Fiji, Live and Learn, Red Cross Society, Fiji Women's Crisis Centre, church organisations) share one goal — assisting those who need help with food, education, sanitation, home and health.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Fiji's student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
