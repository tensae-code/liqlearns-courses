# The October Revolution and the New State — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Kacaanka (The Revolution)" teaches: the 21 October 1969 Revolution as a question-and-answer catechism — there was no Supreme Revolutionary Council or new government in 1965; the Revolution was born on 21 October 1969; Jaalle Siyaad is president now; since he came, work, hospitals and schools have grown, hospitals became free, and the written Somali script spread — the child learns the new state as the bringer of change and progress.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Dhalashadii Kacaanka Oktoobar (The Birth of the October Revolution)" teaches: how the Revolution was born — the corruption and division before it and the night of 21 October 1969.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part "Muxuu Qabtay Kacaanku? (What Did the Revolution Do?)" teaches: the Revolution's works as the proof of the new state — what it built and changed for the people.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in..
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
