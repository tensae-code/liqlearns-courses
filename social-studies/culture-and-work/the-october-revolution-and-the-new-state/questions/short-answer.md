# The October Revolution and the New State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Kacaanka (The Revolution) — the 21 October 1969 Revolution as a question-and-answer catechism — there was no Supreme Revolutionary Council or new government in 1965; the Revolution was born on 21 October 1969; Jaalle Siyaad is president now; since he came, work, hospitals and schools have grown, hospitals became free, and the written Somali script spread — the child learns the new state as the bringer of change and progress (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Dhalashadii Kacaanka Oktoobar (The Birth of the October Revolution) — how the Revolution was born — the corruption and division before it and the night of 21 October 1969 (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Muxuu Qabtay Kacaanku? (What Did the Revolution Do?) — the Revolution's works as the proof of the new state — what it built and changed for the people (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way — this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in. (any close wording).**
