# The October Revolution and the New State — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Kacaanka (The Revolution)" is mainly about —
a) the 21 October 1969 Revolution as a question-and-answer catechism — there was no Supreme Revolutionary Council or new government in 1965; the Revolution was born on 21 October 1969; Jaalle Siyaad is president now; since he came, work, hospitals and schools have grown, hospitals became free, and the written Somali script spread — the child learns the new state as the bringer of change and progress  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the 21 October 1969 Revolution as a question-and-answer catechism — there was no Supreme Revolutionary Council or new government in 1965; the Revolution was born on 21 October 1969; Jaalle Siyaad is president now; since he came, work, hospitals and schools have grown, hospitals became free, and the written Somali script spread — the child learns the new state as the bringer of change and progress**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The part of this lesson called "Dhalashadii Kacaanka Oktoobar (The Birth of the October Revolution)" is mainly about —
a) how the Revolution was born — the corruption and division before it and the night of 21 October 1969  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) how the Revolution was born — the corruption and division before it and the night of 21 October 1969**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The part of this lesson called "Muxuu Qabtay Kacaanku? (What Did the Revolution Do?)" is mainly about —
a) the Revolution's works as the proof of the new state — what it built and changed for the people  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the Revolution's works as the proof of the new state — what it built and changed for the people**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The part of this lesson called "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" is mainly about —
a) this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in.  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in.**
