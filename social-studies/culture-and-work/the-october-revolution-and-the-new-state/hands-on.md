# Hands-on — The October Revolution and the New State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Kacaanka (The Revolution)
Work through this part with your hands: the 21 October 1969 Revolution as a question-and-answer catechism — there was no Supreme Revolutionary Council or new government in 1965; the Revolution was born on 21 October 1969; Jaalle Siyaad is president now; since he came, work, hospitals and schools have grown, hospitals became free, and the written Somali script spread — the child learns the new state as the bringer of change and progress. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Dhalashadii Kacaanka Oktoobar (The Birth of the October Revolution)
Work through this part with your hands: how the Revolution was born — the corruption and division before it and the night of 21 October 1969. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Muxuu Qabtay Kacaanku? (What Did the Revolution Do?)
Work through this part with your hands: the Revolution's works as the proof of the new state — what it built and changed for the people. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
