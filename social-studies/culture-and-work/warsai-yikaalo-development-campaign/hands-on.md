# Hands-on — Warsai Yikaalo Development Campaign

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the state building services after independence — roads, the Asmara–Massawa railway, ports, telecom, schools fourfold, clinics, electricity, and care for orphans, the displaced, war-disabled fighters and martyrs' families — gathered from 2002 into the Warsai-Yikaalo recovery and reconstruction campaign. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
