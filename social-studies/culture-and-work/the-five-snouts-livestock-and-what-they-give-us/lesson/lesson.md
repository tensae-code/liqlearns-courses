## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 9 Таван хошуу мал (The five snouts)

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: civic education for six-year-olds is pure Mongolian custom — greeting elders the Mongolian way, the ger and the five snouts, colour symbolism, the biyelgee dance and the Tsagaan Sar greeting embrace — with traditional script verses running down every page beside the Cyrillic.
