# Hands-on — The Five Snouts Livestock And What They Give Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 9 Таван хошуу мал (The five snouts)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Mongolia's five kinds of livestock — horse, cow, sheep, goat, camel — their young, and what each gives people: milk, wool and cashmere, meat, skins and hides, worked into food, clothes, felt, boots, rope and the morin khuur. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
