# Hands-on — What is a Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Costa Rica's own constitution taught as its own way: a Constituent Assembly after the civil war writes a modern constitution on the 1871 base — autonomous institutions created, the Legislative Power strengthened over the other powers, discrimination by sex, ethnicity or religion prohibited, women given the vote, the Central Bank and the Tribunal Supremo de Elecciones created, the Social Guarantees kept, the army abolished so its money goes to education and health and a civilian culture is forged, banks nationalised, and a neutral Civil Service to end political clientelism. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Costa Rica's way
Say how Costa Rica's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
