# Cultural Richness Helps Us Live Together

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 2 — Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: the middle-school course opens with living together, not with land or dates: a ten-year-old first maps the groups she belongs to and the rights and duties each role carries, then learns that Turkey's many cultures are an aşure — many different ingredients in one pot making one good taste — and that helping has an old Turkish technology for protecting the receiver's dignity, the sadaka taşı (charity stone). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
