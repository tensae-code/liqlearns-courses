Turkey Grade 5 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 20, teaches: meeting different cultural features (the zeybek dance, foods, greetings) and respecting them builds a culture of living together — the aşure distributed in Germany's mosques, where many nations and cultures "boil in the same pot" into one good taste; Turkish students abroad and guests in Turkey won over by hospitality and respect; differences accepted, prejudices dissolved

Turkey's own way: the middle-school course opens with living together, not with land or dates: a ten-year-old first maps the groups she belongs to and the rights and duties each role carries, then learns that Turkey's many cultures are an aşure — many different ingredients in one pot making one good taste — and that helping has an old Turkish technology for protecting the receiver's dignity, the sadaka taşı (charity stone).
