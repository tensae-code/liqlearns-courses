# Hands-on — Cultural Richness Helps Us Live Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: meeting different cultural features (the zeybek dance, foods, greetings) and respecting them builds a culture of living together — the aşure distributed in Germany's mosques, where many nations and cultures "boil in the same pot" into one good taste; Turkish students abroad and guests in Turkey won over by hospitality and respect; differences accepted, prejudices dissolved Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
