# Hands-on — Pakistans Main Crops Rabi And Kharif

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Main crops of Pakistan in the Rabi and Kharif seasons: rice, wheat, maize, cotton (5% of world output), sugarcane, tobacco, fruits and vegetables, each with its growing regions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
