# Hands-on — The Founders Who Formulated Pancasila

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 2 E. Mengenal Perumus Pancasila, Bab 4 B. Teladan Perumus Pancasila, Bab 4 C. Sejarah Perumusan Pancasila, Bab 4 D. Karakter Para Perumus Pancasila, Bab 1 A. Sejarah Kelahiran Pancasila, Panduan Khusus Bab 4: Ayo Mengenal Pancasila, Panduan Khusus Bab 4: Pancasila dalam Diriku, Panduan Khusus Bab 1: Pancasila dalam Kehidupanku — teaches: meeting the formulators of Pancasila as Indonesia's own founding story for citizens; the example of Pancasila's formulators — their deliberation and selflessness as a model; the history of the formulation of Pancasila, taught as the citizen's own founding story; the character of Pancasila's formulators as a moral model; the history of the birth of Pancasila, with Sukarno pictured as first president and a pupil timeline exercise; guidance for being a Pancasila student and the formulators' example; essential material and guidance for Pancasila's meanings in society, its formulation history and the formulators' character; essential material and guidance for the birth of Pancasila and habituating its behaviour. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
