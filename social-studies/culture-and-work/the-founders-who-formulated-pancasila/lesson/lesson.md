## Sub lesson 2 — Indonesia's way — the books' own teaching way

Indonesia's own way: Grade 2 turns Pancasila from a symbol to copy into behaviour at home — each principle (sila) is matched to a picture-symbol on the Garuda shield and then to a concrete act a 7-year-old can do in the family. Grade 3 is the first book to name the state the child lives in — your own street is taught as part of the NKRI (Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia), and rights are introduced paired, always, with duties. Grade 4 introduces the child's first government — the perangkat desa/kelurahan, the village and urban-ward officials — inside a book about the child's own street, before any national institution is named. Grade 5 names the machinery of Indonesian civic life — norma (religious, decency, social and legal norms as one tree) and musyawarah, deciding by deliberation until consensus (mufakat), practised first in the family. the guide gives teachers their own reading (Bahan Bacaan Guru) per chapter, so the civics content is taught to the teacher first, then to the class. each chapter's guide opens with the essential material (Materi Esensial) stated for the teacher in adult form before the child version is taught. the guide's Materi Esensial sections state the norms/musyawarah material in full adult form, including the norm tree, before it is simplified for pupils. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
