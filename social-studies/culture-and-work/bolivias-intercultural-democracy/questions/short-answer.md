# Bolivias Intercultural Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: La Democracia Directa y Participativa — Bolivia's intercultural democracy — three complementary forms in equality: direct-participative (referendum, recall of mandate, assemblies and cabildos, prior consultation), representative (universal suffrage — equal, individual, secret, free and obligatory — electing authorities at every level), and communitarian; political rights under CPE Art. 144 and the Electoral Regime Law: citizenship from 18, the right to elect and to be elected, and the political organisations (parties, citizen groups, NPIOC organisations) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship is taught as intercultural democracy — the Western forms (direct, participative, representative) completed by the indigenous peoples' own communitarian democracy, with parity and alternation between women and men written into every candidate list, and the year's closing civics arc runs from women's rights to despatriarcalización, the dismantling of patriarchy as a task of the Plurinational State itself (any close wording).**
