# Hands-on — Bolivias Intercultural Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bolivia's intercultural democracy — three complementary forms in equality: direct-participative (referendum, recall of mandate, assemblies and cabildos, prior consultation), representative (universal suffrage — equal, individual, secret, free and obligatory — electing authorities at every level), and communitarian; political rights under CPE Art. 144 and the Electoral Regime Law: citizenship from 18, the right to elect and to be elected, and the political organisations (parties, citizen groups, NPIOC organisations). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
