Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 6): in senior primary the community strand becomes civics proper — the groups and organisations of PNG, how they relate and speak for people, and how development changes the community — while the culture strand runs from rituals to taking part in cultural events.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 7): Grade 7 civics is the nation itself — how Papua New Guinea is governed, its elections and government services, and how its courts and problem-solvers keep the peace — beside a culture strand that climbs from lifestyle and identity to national events.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 8): Grade 8 turns outward — types and structures of government, international relations and aid partners, multinational companies, and a culture strand taught entirely at world scale: globalisation, global culture and global cultural events.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science Upper Primary TG, Grades 6–8): the 2003 guide teaches the same climb as the Standards Based guides but through outcomes — local groups in Grade 6, PNG's constitutional government and elections in Grade 7, world governments and regional organisations in Grade 8 — with the wantok system, traditional law beside constitutional law, and class elections run by secret ballot with first-past-the-post and preferential counting.
