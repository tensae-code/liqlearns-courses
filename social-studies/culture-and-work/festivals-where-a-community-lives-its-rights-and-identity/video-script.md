# Video script — Festivals Where a Community Lives Its Rights and Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 186–221 (Módulo 6), teaches: Colombia is a festive country; its year-round festivals carry the traditions of its peoples and collective enthusiasm, the community takes ownership of them as part of its identity, and festive culture is a space where human rights are lived — pupils register and analyse their own community's festivals (including the fiesta del campesino).

Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims.
