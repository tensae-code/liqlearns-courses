# Festivals Where a Community Lives Its Rights and Identity

## Sub lesson 1 — Módulo 6 Guía 16 Las fiestas colombianas: un espacio para vivir los derechos humanos

Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 186–221 (Módulo 6), teaches: Colombia is a festive country; its year-round festivals carry the traditions of its peoples and collective enthusiasm, the community takes ownership of them as part of its identity, and festive culture is a space where human rights are lived — pupils register and analyse their own community's festivals (including the fiesta del campesino). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 25) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
