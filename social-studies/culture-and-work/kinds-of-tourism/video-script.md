Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11* (printed pp. 35–40), teaches: Topic 11.2 (part): Popular kinds of tourism in the world — sorted by resource (nature and eco-tourism; cultural tourism), by purpose (sightseeing, entertainment, rest and recuperation, adventure, business, religious and health tourism), by place (sea, mountain, city and rural tourism) and by territory (domestic and international), each with its Vietnamese examples.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11): the Grade 11 electives take three deep dives that all circle home — sharing the Mekong and the East Sea with neighbours, the world's tourism and the jobs it offers Vietnamese school-leavers, and the Fourth Industrial Revolution remaking work.
