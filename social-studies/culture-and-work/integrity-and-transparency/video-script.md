# Video script — Integrity and Transparency

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan Grade 7 SS G7 T2, printed p. 43, teaches: Integrity and transparency — why applying them matters and the principles that plant them.

Jordan Grade 8 SS G8 T2, printed p. 15, teaches: Integrity — holding to it against corruption, as a lasting practice.

Jordan's own way: media is taught as a citizen's defence — content, misinformation, rumours and social media in one unit — and government as Jordan's own constitutional story, ending in the rule of law and integrity. the set closes on the young citizen — living together across difference without extremism, then youth themselves: their place in society, in public life and the national economy, the challenges they face, and the behaviour expected of them.
