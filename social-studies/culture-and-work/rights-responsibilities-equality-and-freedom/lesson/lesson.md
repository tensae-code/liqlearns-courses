## Sub lesson 1 — Rights, responsibilities, equality and freedom

Fiji Grade 9 Social Science Year 9 (printed p. 41), teaches: Rights come in written laws (the Constitution), unwritten customs and human rights; children's rights from the UN Convention (name, nationality, education, protection from hurt and harmful work), and we are responsible for our own actions. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fiji's way — the books' own teaching way

Fiji's own way (Grade 9): society is analysed through Fiji's own structures — the iTaukei hierarchy of Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau and Tauvanua with its ascribed chiefly status — and conflict is settled the iTaukei way, by Bulubulu. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
