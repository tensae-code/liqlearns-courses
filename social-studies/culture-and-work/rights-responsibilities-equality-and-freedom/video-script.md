# Video script — Rights Responsibilities Equality and Freedom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 9 Social Science Year 9 (printed p. 41), teaches: Rights come in written laws (the Constitution), unwritten customs and human rights; children's rights from the UN Convention (name, nationality, education, protection from hurt and harmful work), and we are responsible for our own actions.

Fiji's own way (Grade 9): society is analysed through Fiji's own structures — the iTaukei hierarchy of Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau and Tauvanua with its ascribed chiefly status — and conflict is settled the iTaukei way, by Bulubulu.
