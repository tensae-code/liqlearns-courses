# Hands-on — Rights Responsibilities Equality and Freedom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rights, responsibilities, equality and freedom
Work through this part with your hands: Rights come in written laws (the Constitution), unwritten customs and human rights; children's rights from the UN Convention (name, nationality, education, protection from hurt and harmful work), and we are responsible for our own actions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
