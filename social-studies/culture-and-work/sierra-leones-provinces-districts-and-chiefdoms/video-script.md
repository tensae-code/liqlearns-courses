# Video script — Sierra Leones Provinces Districts and Chiefdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sierra Leone's SL General Paper Class 6 teacher guide (printed pp. 85–89), teaches: how Sierra Leone is divided and run — provinces with their headquarters, administrative regions, districts and 190 chiefdoms (the physical-environment half of the unit is Geography, named not placed).

Sierra Leone's own way: primary civics is taught as Sierra Leone's own citizenship — the flag's green-white-blue meanings, the coat of arms, anthem and pledge, and the country's own provinces, districts and 190 chiefdoms.
