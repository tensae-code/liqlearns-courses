# Hands-on — Working for My Familys Happiness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the family that holds you most dear; playing with your little brother while mother makes dinner; the warm heart when the family cares for you in a fever. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
