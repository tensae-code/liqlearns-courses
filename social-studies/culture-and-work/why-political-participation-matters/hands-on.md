# Hands-on — Why Political Participation Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Why Political Participation Matters
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Political participation is tied to political and social life and is a principle of the democratic civil society: it lets individuals share in making decisions, contribute to developing their own independent personality and to developing their society with their ideas and experience, increases their awareness of the events around them, and strengthens solidarity, belonging and respect for plurality. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
