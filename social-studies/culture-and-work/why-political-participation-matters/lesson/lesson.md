# Why Political Participation Matters

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: political participation is tied to political and social life and is a principle of the democratic civil society: it lets individuals share in making decisions, contribute to developing their own independent personality and to developing their society with their ideas and experience, increases their awareness of the events around them, and strengthens solidarity, belonging and respect for plurality — as Palestine's book teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Why Political Participation Matters

Palestine Grade 9 Social Studies Part 1 (printed p. 64), teaches: political participation is tied to political and social life and is a principle of the democratic civil society: it lets individuals share in making decisions, contribute to developing their own independent personality and to developing their society with their ideas and experience, increases their awareness of the events around them, and strengthens solidarity, belonging and respect for plurality. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way

Palestine's own way (Grade 9 Social Studies Part 1): participation is taught as existence itself — "I participate, therefore I am" — and the lesson counts women's actual seats (56 of 744 in the National Council, 17 of 132 in the Legislative Council) before teaching the quota women won to change them; elections close the course with Palestine's own candidacy ages: 40 for president, 28 for the Legislative Council, 25 for local councils. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
