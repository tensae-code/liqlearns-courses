## Sub lesson 1 — Significant persons in our communities

Guyana Grade 2 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 66–67), teaches: People whose work made their community and nation proud — rice farmer and philanthropist Kayman Sankar, who created jobs and supported cricket; educator Juliet Persico, honoured with the Medal of Service; and businessman Peter D'Aguiar, founder of Banks DIH. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: the Grade 2 book of the new series starts citizenship at the child's own doorstep — the many communities a child belongs to at once, the councils that serve them, the peoples and festivals that fill them, then decisions, conflict and rights, all before any map or market is taught. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
