# Hands-on — People Who Helped Build Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: People whose work made their community and nation proud — rice farmer and philanthropist Kayman Sankar, who created jobs and supported cricket; educator Juliet Persico, honoured with the Medal of Service; and businessman Peter D'Aguiar, founder of Banks DIH. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
