# Hands-on — What Law Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What law means, the basic rules law rests on, and what law is for — opening with imagining a classroom with no rules. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
