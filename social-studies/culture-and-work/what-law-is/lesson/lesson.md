## Sub lesson 1 — Law — definition, rules and goals

Kuwait Grade 8 Term 1 (printed pp. 85–87), teaches: What law means, the basic rules law rests on, and what law is for — opening with imagining a classroom with no rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way

Kuwait's own way: amid a book about the Islamic world, Kuwait teaches law abstractly and concretely at once — what law is, why a society without it falls into disorder, how legal rules differ from moral rules, and the speed camera on a Kuwaiti road as the picture of a law that applies to everyone without exception. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
