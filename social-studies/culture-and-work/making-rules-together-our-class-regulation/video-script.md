# Video script — Making Rules Together Our Class Regulation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Angola Educação Moral e Cívica G6 (printed p. 54), teaches: The class writes its own regulation — the school guarantees members' rights and duties, and the pupils draft the rules themselves, including a chapter of no violence (verbal, gestural or physical) in class and playground.

Angola's own way: Grade 6 makes the pupils legislators of their own classroom — the class writes its own regulation (with a no-violence chapter) as the lived rehearsal for democracy — and teaches duties concretely, down to naming the taxes (IVA, income, property) and calling the non-payer a tax evader; tolerance is defined as the price of choosing democracy.
