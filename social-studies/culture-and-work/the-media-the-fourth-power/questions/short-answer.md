# The Media the Fourth Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 5.3 "The media's influence in modern society and political life" — What mass media are — the press, radio, television, news agencies, documentary film and electronic press that deliver true information quickly and regularly; why the media are called the fourth power — because they secure the citizen's freedom to express views, an inseparable part of democratic values; their work in a democracy, in three universal functions — to inform, to help citizens form their own views and take part, and to oversee and criticise (the watchdog that exposes government error and rouses the public) — with entertainment and education beside them; the principles media must keep in return — truth for the whole public over one person's interest, equal reach regardless of age, sex, faith or education, being the people's voice, and protecting journalists' rights; Mongolia's evidence — the 2015 Democratic Governance study finding media judged dependent on politics and money (42.6% not agreeing they are independent), and the 2016 trust scores, national television at 90 and national radio at 80 while the yellow press is trusted least; and the new arrival — social media, set beside mass media in one picture (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the final year goes deep on law as the citizen's toolkit — property, harm, crime, the complaint against the state itself, and the national and UN machinery that guards rights — with sociology closing on how societies change and what moves them (any close wording).**
