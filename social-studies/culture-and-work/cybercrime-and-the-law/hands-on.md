# Hands-on — Cybercrime and the Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Technology and crime
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: electronic crimes defined (fraud, blackmail, theft and money-laundering moved online); harassment and blackmail punished under Article 16 of the UAE cybercrime law; privacy violations under Article 21 (eavesdropping, photographing others, publishing their news); the eight motives of the e-criminal; and how to protect yourself from becoming a victim Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
