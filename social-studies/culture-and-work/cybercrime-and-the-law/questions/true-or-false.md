# Cybercrime and the Law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: electronic crimes defined (fraud, blackmail, theft and money-laundering moved online); harassment and blackmail punished under Article 16 of the UAE cybercrime law; privacy violations under Article 21 (eavesdropping, photographing others, publishing their news); the eight motives of the e-criminal; and how to protect yourself from becoming a victim
