# Video script — Cybercrime and the Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education in the Digital Age, G11 Term 3 activity book, printed p. 42, teaches: electronic crimes defined (fraud, blackmail, theft and money-laundering moved online); harassment and blackmail punished under Article 16 of the UAE cybercrime law; privacy violations under Article 21 (eavesdropping, photographing others, publishing their news); the eight motives of the e-criminal; and how to protect yourself from becoming a victim

United Arab Emirates' own way: digital citizenship is taught as rights-and-duties law, not etiquette tips — every ethical rule is tied to the UAE's own cybercrime law articles and penalties, and equal access is proven from the state's own projects (Smart Dubai, Abu Dhabi's e-government) and its four-category support for People of Determination online.
