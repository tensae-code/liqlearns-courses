Indonesia's Grade 8 IPS-G8 student (printed p. 179), teaches: Bab 3 D. Proses Keberlanjutan dalam Kehidupan Masyarakat — teaches: continuity and change in community life — today's institutions as the product of interaction sustained from the past.

Indonesia's own way: social integration is taught through marakka bola — the South Sulawesi gotong royong tradition of literally lifting and carrying a neighbour's house to its new site, without regard to difference.
