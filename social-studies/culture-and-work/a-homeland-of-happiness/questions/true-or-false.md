# A Homeland of Happiness — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: society (المجتمع) defined as a group of people living in one place, and happiness as something the homeland provides — schools, shopping centres and entertainment places — with the UAE's first Minister of Happiness named as a national fact
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: at Grade 1 "order" is taught as one widening circle — home, then school, then neighbourhood — each part closing with a "Zayed taught me" saying and a named national value (respect, safety), and the country itself is introduced as a homeland of happiness and tolerance with its own Minister of Happiness and a Year of Tolerance.
**Answer: True**
