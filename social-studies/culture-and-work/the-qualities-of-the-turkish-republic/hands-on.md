# Hands-on — The Qualities of the Turkish Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the 1982 Constitution's first three articles cannot be changed or even proposed for change; Article 1 — Turkey is a Republic, the nation holding sovereignty and using it through deputies it elects for set terms; Article 2 — the state respects human rights, is bound to Atatürk nationalism (a citizenship-based nationalism without racial distinction), and is a democratic, secular, social and rule-of-law state; the book traces the roots in old Turkish states — beys chosen by election in the toy assembly, Kutadgu Bilig's command to rule justly, endowments aiding orphans Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
