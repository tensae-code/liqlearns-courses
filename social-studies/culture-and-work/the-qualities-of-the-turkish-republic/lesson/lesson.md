## Sub lesson 2 — Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: the course ends where citizenship becomes constitutional: thirteen-year-olds read Articles 1–3 of their own constitution (the ones that cannot even be proposed for change), separate the state's three powers, walk democracy's road from the Tanzimat edicts to the first multi-party election of 1946, and finish by naming the behaviours that wound democracy in daily life.
