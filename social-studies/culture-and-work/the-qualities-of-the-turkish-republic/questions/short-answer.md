# The Qualities of the Turkish Republic — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Qualities of the Republic of Turkey — the 1982 Constitution's first three articles cannot be changed or even proposed for change; Article 1 — Turkey is a Republic, the nation holding sovereignty and using it through deputies it elects for set terms; Article 2 — the state respects human rights, is bound to Atatürk nationalism (a citizenship-based nationalism without racial distinction), and is a democratic, secular, social and rule-of-law state; the book traces the roots in old Turkish states — beys chosen by election in the toy assembly, Kutadgu Bilig's command to rule justly, endowments aiding orphans (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
