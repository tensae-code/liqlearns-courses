# Hands-on — Honesty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Truthfulness in speech — Lana, who had spent the day playing with her visiting friends instead of learning her lesson, decides without hesitation to tell her teacher the truth and apologise to her, and her friends answer in one voice: well done, for salvation is in truthfulness; truthfulness is among my traits and my motto in my life, and by it I earned respect; and play is one of my rights, so organising my time between play and study is what matters. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iraq's way
Say how Iraq's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
