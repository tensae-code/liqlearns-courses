# Video script — Equal Participation of Women and People with Disabilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 5 Estudios Sociales 5to EGB, printed p. 184, teaches: Contrasting the participation of men, women and people with disabilities in society at the start of the 19th century and in the 20th, fair treatment and inclusion of people with disabilities in Ecuador (what has been achieved, what has not, the attitudes inclusion needs), and fair participation practised in the pupil's own school.

Ecuador's own way: the Grade 5 book ends its history-and-geography march with a civics section that teaches participation as Ecuador's own story — its social movements, its fight against child labour, and inclusion of women and people with disabilities — practised in miniature through a class assembly that votes on proposals.
