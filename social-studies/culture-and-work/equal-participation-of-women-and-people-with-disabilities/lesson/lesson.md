## Sub lesson 1 — Equal Participation of Women and People with Disabilities

Ecuador Grade 5 Estudios Sociales 5to EGB, printed p. 184, teaches: Contrasting the participation of men, women and people with disabilities in society at the start of the 19th century and in the 20th, fair treatment and inclusion of people with disabilities in Ecuador (what has been achieved, what has not, the attitudes inclusion needs), and fair participation practised in the pupil's own school. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador's own way: the Grade 5 book ends its history-and-geography march with a civics section that teaches participation as Ecuador's own story — its social movements, its fight against child labour, and inclusion of women and people with disabilities — practised in miniature through a class assembly that votes on proposals.
