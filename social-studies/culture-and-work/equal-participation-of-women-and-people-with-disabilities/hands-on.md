# Hands-on — Equal Participation of Women and People with Disabilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Contrasting the participation of men, women and people with disabilities in society at the start of the 19th century and in the 20th, fair treatment and inclusion of people with disabilities in Ecuador (what has been achieved, what has not, the attitudes inclusion needs), and fair participation practised in the pupil's own school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
