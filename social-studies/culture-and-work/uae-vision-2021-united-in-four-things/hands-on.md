# Hands-on — Uae Vision 2021 United in Four Things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Vision of the United Arab Emirates 2021
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the national vision as four "united" pillars — United in Responsibility (confident responsible citizens, cohesive families, strong social relations, a rich vibrant culture); United in Destiny (following the founding fathers, the homeland's safety, the Emirates' standing in the world arena); United in Knowledge (the citizen's latent powers, a sustainable diverse economy, a knowledge economy of high productivity); United in Prosperity (a healthy life, a first-class education system, an integrated lifestyle, protecting the environment) Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
