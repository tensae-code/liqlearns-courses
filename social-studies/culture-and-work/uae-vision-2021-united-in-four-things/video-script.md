# Video script — Uae Vision 2021 United in Four Things

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G9 teacher guide, Term 1 — front-matter fragment, printed p. 6, teaches: the national vision as four "united" pillars — United in Responsibility (confident responsible citizens, cohesive families, strong social relations, a rich vibrant culture); United in Destiny (following the founding fathers, the homeland's safety, the Emirates' standing in the world arena); United in Knowledge (the citizen's latent powers, a sustainable diverse economy, a knowledge economy of high productivity); United in Prosperity (a healthy life, a first-class education system, an integrated lifestyle, protecting the environment)

United Arab Emirates' own way: every teacher guide opens by teaching the flag before any lesson — its colours are glossed from the poet Safi al-Din al-Hilli's famous verse ("white are our deeds, green our meadows, black our battles, red our swords"), recast for the federation: white for good deeds and giving, green for growth and renaissance, red for the founders' and martyrs' sacrifices, black for the people's strength and the rejection of injustice and extremism.
