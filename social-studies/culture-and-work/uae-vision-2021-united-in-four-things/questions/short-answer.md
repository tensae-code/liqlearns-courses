# Uae Vision 2021 United in Four Things — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Vision of the United Arab Emirates 2021 — the national vision as four "united" pillars — United in Responsibility (confident responsible citizens, cohesive families, strong social relations, a rich vibrant culture); United in Destiny (following the founding fathers, the homeland's safety, the Emirates' standing in the world arena); United in Knowledge (the citizen's latent powers, a sustainable diverse economy, a knowledge economy of high productivity); United in Prosperity (a healthy life, a first-class education system, an integrated lifestyle, protecting the environment) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — every teacher guide opens by teaching the flag before any lesson — its colours are glossed from the poet Safi al-Din al-Hilli's famous verse ("white are our deeds, green our meadows, black our battles, red our swords"), recast for the federation: white for good deeds and giving, green for growth and renaissance, red for the founders' and martyrs' sacrifices, black for the people's strength and the rejection of injustice and extremism. (any close wording).**
