# Uae Vision 2021 United in Four Things — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the national vision as four "united" pillars — United in Responsibility (confident responsible citizens, cohesive families, strong social relations, a rich vibrant culture); United in Destiny (following the founding fathers, the homeland's safety, the Emirates' standing in the world arena); United in Knowledge (the citizen's latent powers, a sustainable diverse economy, a knowledge economy of high productivity); United in Prosperity (a healthy life, a first-class education system, an integrated lifestyle, protecting the environment)
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: every teacher guide opens by teaching the flag before any lesson — its colours are glossed from the poet Safi al-Din al-Hilli's famous verse ("white are our deeds, green our meadows, black our battles, red our swords"), recast for the federation: white for good deeds and giving, green for growth and renaissance, red for the founders' and martyrs' sacrifices, black for the people's strength and the rejection of injustice and extremism.
**Answer: True**
