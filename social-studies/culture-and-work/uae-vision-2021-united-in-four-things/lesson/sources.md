United Arab Emirates' own way (Social Studies & National Education, G9 teacher guide, Term 1 — front-matter fragment) (teacher guide — evidence): every teacher guide opens by teaching the flag before any lesson — its colours are glossed from the poet Safi al-Din al-Hilli's famous verse ("white are our deeds, green our meadows, black our battles, red our swords"), recast for the federation: white for good deeds and giving, green for growth and renaissance, red for the founders' and martyrs' sacrifices, black for the people's strength and the rejection of injustice and extremism.
