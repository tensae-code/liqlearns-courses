# Uae Vision 2021 United in Four Things

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the national vision as four "united" pillars — United in Responsibility (confident responsible citizens, cohesive families, strong social relations, a rich vibrant culture); United in Destiny (following the founding fathers, the homeland's safety, the Emirates' standing in the world arena); United in Knowledge (the citizen's latent powers, a sustainable diverse economy, a knowledge economy of high productivity); United in Prosperity (a healthy life, a first-class education system, an integrated lifestyle, protecting the environment) — as United Arab Emirates's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Vision of the United Arab Emirates 2021

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G9 teacher guide, Term 1 — front-matter fragment, printed p. 6, teaches: the national vision as four "united" pillars — United in Responsibility (confident responsible citizens, cohesive families, strong social relations, a rich vibrant culture); United in Destiny (following the founding fathers, the homeland's safety, the Emirates' standing in the world arena); United in Knowledge (the citizen's latent powers, a sustainable diverse economy, a knowledge economy of high productivity); United in Prosperity (a healthy life, a first-class education system, an integrated lifestyle, protecting the environment) This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: every teacher guide opens by teaching the flag before any lesson — its colours are glossed from the poet Safi al-Din al-Hilli's famous verse ("white are our deeds, green our meadows, black our battles, red our swords"), recast for the federation: white for good deeds and giving, green for growth and renaissance, red for the founders' and martyrs' sacrifices, black for the people's strength and the rejection of injustice and extremism. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
