The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bangladesh Grade 8 BD G8 HSS (printed p. 99), teaches: The Meena cartoon case — when a groom's family demands dowry, the village elder confirms that giving and taking dowry are both crimes, and the villagers together resolve that no girl of the village will marry a dowry-demanding boy, so the suitor leaves empty-handed.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): Sociology is taught as a toolkit for changing one's own society — socialisation, control and change are each defined, then immediately used: the class stops a dowry in a cartoon case, studies Martin Luther King as a movement model, and finishes by setting new norms for itself to practise through the civic club..
