# Hands-on — Stopping Dowry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Meena cartoon case — when a groom's family demands dowry, the village elder confirms that giving and taking dowry are both crimes, and the villagers together resolve that no girl of the village will marry a dowry-demanding boy, so the suitor leaves empty-handed. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
