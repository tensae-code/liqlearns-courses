# Video script — Diwaniya Etiquette

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 6 Term 2 (printed pp. 102–103), teaches: The manners of Kuwait's sitting-room of public life — the values drawn from a diwaniya visit: respect, listening, generosity (ihsan), sacrifice and passing experience on.

Kuwait's own way: Term 2's civics is Gulf civics — after Kuwait's own constitution (placed by the History wave), the pupil's political world widens to the Gulf Cooperation Council: what it is, how the six states cooperate in security, economy, culture and education, and where they want to go (Kuwait Vision 2035); the values unit keeps the same widening local — Ramadan's drummer, the diwaniya's manners, a helping hand, a kind word.
