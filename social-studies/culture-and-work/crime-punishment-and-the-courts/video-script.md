# Video script — Crime Punishment and the Courts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 83), teaches: A crime as the gravest kind of offence and punishment as a measure of state coercion imposed only by a court's sentence — the listed punishments from public works and fines to imprisonment, with the death penalty named as the exceptional measure for especially grave crimes; the circumstances that exclude criminality (necessary defence, detaining an offender, extreme necessity) and those that aggravate responsibility (repeat offending, a group or criminal organisation, cruelty, concealment of another crime).
Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics..
