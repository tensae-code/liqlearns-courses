# Crime Punishment and the Courts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Foundations of criminal law (Основы уголовного права) — Grade 11 — a crime as the gravest kind of offence and punishment as a measure of state coercion imposed only by a court's sentence — the listed punishments from public works and fines to imprisonment, with the death penalty named as the exceptional measure for especially grave crimes; the circumstances that exclude criminality (necessary defence, detaining an offender, extreme necessity) and those that aggravate responsibility (repeat offending, a group or criminal organisation, cruelty, concealment of another crime) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics. (any close wording).**
