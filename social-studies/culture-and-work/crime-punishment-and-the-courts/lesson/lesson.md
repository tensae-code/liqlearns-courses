## Sub lesson 1 — Foundations of criminal law (Основы уголовного права) — Grade 11

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 83), teaches: A crime as the gravest kind of offence and punishment as a measure of state coercion imposed only by a court's sentence — the listed punishments from public works and fines to imprisonment, with the death penalty named as the exceptional measure for especially grave crimes; the circumstances that exclude criminality (necessary defence, detaining an offender, extreme necessity) and those that aggravate responsibility (repeat offending, a group or criminal organisation, cruelty, concealment of another crime). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
