# Hands-on — Crime Punishment and the Courts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A crime as the gravest kind of offence and punishment as a measure of state coercion imposed only by a court's sentence — the listed punishments from public works and fines to imprisonment, with the death penalty named as the exceptional measure for especially grave crimes; the circumstances that exclude criminality (necessary defence, detaining an offender, extreme necessity) and those that aggravate responsibility (repeat offending, a group or criminal organisation, cruelty, concealment of another crime). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
