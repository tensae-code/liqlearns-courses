# Video script — Countries Where Gender Equality Works

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 86, teaches: Rwanda, Norway, Iceland, Sweden, Nicaragua and New Zealand are the worked examples; they succeeded through strong political commitment, women empowerment strategies, the constitution, committed leaders and eliminating gender-based discrimination — and South Sudan's ways are equal opportunities, promoting human rights, improving living standards and equitable education for all.

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies TG, printed p. 65, teaches: the guide adds the comparative method — learners explore laws and systems in other countries related to gender equality and use these to inform their own ideas for the promotion of equality in South Sudan (the countries lesson is filed NEW from Book 13 in this wave).

South Sudan's own way: Gender equality is argued from South Sudanese lives — an elder who educates his daughters against tradition, and four named women leaders — and peace itself is put in women's hands: the book prints the view that gender equality is a tool that can end South Sudan's civil war.
