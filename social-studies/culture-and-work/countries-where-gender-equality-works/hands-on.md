# Hands-on — Countries Where Gender Equality Works

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 3 Examples of countries where gender equality is practised
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Rwanda, Norway, Iceland, Sweden, Nicaragua and New Zealand are the worked examples; they succeeded through strong political commitment, women empowerment strategies, the constitution, committed leaders and eliminating gender-based discrimination — and South Sudan's ways are equal opportunities, promoting human rights, improving living standards and equitable education for all. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unit 4 overview: learning from other countries' systems
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the guide adds the comparative method — learners explore laws and systems in other countries related to gender equality and use these to inform their own ideas for the promotion of equality in South Sudan (the countries lesson is filed NEW from Book 13 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
