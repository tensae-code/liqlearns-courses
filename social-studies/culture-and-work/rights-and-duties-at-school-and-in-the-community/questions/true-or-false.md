# Rights and Duties at School and in the Community — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: a pupil's rights in school (education, developing one's talents, security, equality, expressing ideas, association, safeguarding one's identity), the free provision that upholds them, and the duties that go with each right — including treating a diverse school community with brotherhood.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: the two-way duties of the classroom — pupils' duties by their teachers (commitment, obedience, respect) and teachers' duties by their pupils (a good education, safety, guidance, spotting and sharpening talents), and pupils' responsibilities to their peers.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: the two sides of public service — providers must serve properly, efficiently, fairly and without bias; receivers must pay taxes correctly, protect the institutions' property and not obtain services by improper means.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Sri Lanka's student books only, named in lesson/sources.md.**
