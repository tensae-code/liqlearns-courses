Indonesia's own way: junior IPS opens sociology before any state content — a child saying goodbye to parents and greeting friends is the first example of interaksi sosial, and status is taught through Sukarno and Hatta's assigned status as Fathers of the Proclamation. the guide condenses each theme into a Materi Esensial map for the teacher before any lesson steps, so the sociology content is stated whole, in adult form, first.
