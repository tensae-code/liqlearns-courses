# Hands-on — Communities and Their Role in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Tema IV D. Peranan Komunitas dalam Kehidupan Masyarakat, Tema IV D, empowerment forms, Panduan Khusus Tema IV D. Penyajian Materi Esensial Tema IV — teaches: communities — organised social units with shared interests in one territory, from hobby and farmer communities to kakak asuh education-care communities; community empowerment in two forms — empowerment inside communities (skills training) and village empowerment (pemberdayaan masyarakat desa); essential material for Tema IV — social-cultural diversity, social-cultural problems and the role of communities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
