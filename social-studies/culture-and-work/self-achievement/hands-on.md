# Hands-on — Self Achievement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the culture of initiative as a foundation of self-achievement and a pillar of the national economy, through creative ideas for small projects that grow; self-achievement strengthens a person's confidence in himself and others' confidence in him, gives satisfaction, and is a source of pride for the person and his homeland; its helping factors — ambition, perseverance and persistence, self-confidence, bearing responsibilities, stocking up on knowledge and skills, and benefiting from mistakes so they are not repeated; its steps — setting the goals precisely, planning the works, exerting effort and hard work, and cooperating with others and using their experience; and the achiever's traits — doing what he is tasked with perfectly, seeking distinguished performance, keeping appointments and organising time, doing duties regularly, loving difficult things done with perfection, and persevering past obstacles. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
