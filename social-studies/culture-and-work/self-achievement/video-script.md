Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 250–252), teaches: The culture of initiative as a foundation of self-achievement and a pillar of the national economy, through creative ideas for small projects that grow; self-achievement strengthens a person's confidence in himself and others' confidence in him, gives satisfaction, and is a source of pride for the person and his homeland; its helping factors — ambition, perseverance and persistence, self-confidence, bearing responsibilities, stocking up on knowledge and skills, and benefiting from mistakes so they are not repeated; its steps — setting the goals precisely, planning the works, exerting effort and hard work, and cooperating with others and using their experience; and the achiever's traits — doing what he is tasked with perfectly, seeking distinguished performance, keeping appointments and organising time, doing duties regularly, loving difficult things done with perfection, and persevering past obstacles.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes.
