# Hands-on — We Are Artists Symbols and Patterns

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## We are artists
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what a symbol (رمز) is — a thing that stands for an idea — and what a pattern (نقش) is — a shape or design repeated for decoration; reading the symbols and patterns on old pieces (the country's flag, an old bracelet, a statue, an Islamic mosaic); why our ancestors decorated the things they made; and whether the people of the past were creative artists and innovators, as we are today Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
