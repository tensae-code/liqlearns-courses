# We Are Artists Symbols and Patterns

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what a symbol (رمز) is — a thing that stands for an idea — and what a pattern (نقش) is — a shape or design repeated for decoration; reading the symbols and patterns on old pieces (the country's flag, an old bracelet, a statue, an Islamic mosaic); why our ancestors decorated the things they made; and whether the people of the past were creative artists and innovators, as we are today — as United Arab Emirates's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
