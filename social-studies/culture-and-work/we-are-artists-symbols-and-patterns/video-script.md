# Video script — We Are Artists Symbols and Patterns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 21, teaches: what a symbol (رمز) is — a thing that stands for an idea — and what a pattern (نقش) is — a shape or design repeated for decoration; reading the symbols and patterns on old pieces (the country's flag, an old bracelet, a statue, an Islamic mosaic); why our ancestors decorated the things they made; and whether the people of the past were creative artists and innovators, as we are today

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
