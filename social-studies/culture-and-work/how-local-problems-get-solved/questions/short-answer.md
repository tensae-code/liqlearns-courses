# How Local Problems Get Solved — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 5.2 "How are the problems facing the locality solved?" — Local self-governance (нутгийн өөрөө удирдах ёс) — the people of a territory deciding their own life's questions together, on the basis of their own resources and the law — set against state administration in one table (source of power: the citizens' meeting and election, against appointment from above; term: four years, both); and the working method, in five steps — find the locality's pressing problem (school, youth, environment, rights, social environment), choose one by group study, gather information about it from library, media, administration and experts, build solution options and a work plan, and present the proposal to the local administration — inside the six-stage policy cycle: initiate, develop, discuss, approve, implement, evaluate (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the local year turns rights and government into neighbourhood questions — the human-rights chapter ends with the soum administration's duties and Ulaanbaatar's air, and the governance chapter is one long worked example: a playground, a Khural decision, and the children who answer it (any close wording).**
