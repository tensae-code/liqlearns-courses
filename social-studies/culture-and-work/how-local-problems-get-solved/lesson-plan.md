# Lesson plan — How Local Problems Get Solved

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** How Local Problems Get Solved
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Mongolia, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** How Local Problems Get Solved — Local self-governance (нутгийн өөрөө удирдах ёс) — the people of a territory deciding their own life's questions together, on the basis of their own resources and the law — set against state administration in one table (source of power: the citizens' meeting and election, against appointment from above; term: four years, both); and the working method, in five steps — find the locality's pressing problem (school, youth, environment, rights, social environment), choose one by group study, gather information about it from library, media, administration and experts, build solution options and a work plan, and present the proposal to the local administration — inside the six-stage policy cycle: initiate, develop, discuss, approve, implement, evaluate — as Mongolia's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
