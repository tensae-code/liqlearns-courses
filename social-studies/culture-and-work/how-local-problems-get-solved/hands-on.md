# Hands-on — How Local Problems Get Solved

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 5.2 "How are the problems facing the locality solved?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Local self-governance (нутгийн өөрөө удирдах ёс) — the people of a territory deciding their own life's questions together, on the basis of their own resources and the law — set against state administration in one table (source of power: the citizens' meeting and election, against appointment from above; term: four years, both); and the working method, in five steps — find the locality's pressing problem (school, youth, environment, rights, social environment), choose one by group study, gather information about it from library, media, administration and experts, build solution options and a work plan, and present the proposal to the local administration — inside the six-stage policy cycle: initiate, develop, discuss, approve, implement, evaluate. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
