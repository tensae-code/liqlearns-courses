## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 4 §4.2 Deslocação do ser humano

Angola Estudo do Meio G3 (printed p. 67), teaches: How people move — children walk or ride to school, workers travel to factories, offices and services, and in some seasons people travel to other provinces or countries. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Angola's way — the book's own teaching way

Angola's own way: Grade 3 is where Estudo do Meio turns outward — the child's own locality is inventoried place by place (school, hospital, market, bank, water fountain), and Angola's customs are taught region by region: dress, mother tongue, food, and festivals such as efico and circumcision celebrations. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
