# Video script — How People Move From Place To Place

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Angola Estudo do Meio G3 (printed p. 67), teaches: How people move — children walk or ride to school, workers travel to factories, offices and services, and in some seasons people travel to other provinces or countries.

Angola's own way: Grade 3 is where Estudo do Meio turns outward — the child's own locality is inventoried place by place (school, hospital, market, bank, water fountain), and Angola's customs are taught region by region: dress, mother tongue, food, and festivals such as efico and circumcision celebrations.
