# Video script — Citizen Participation Parties and Oversight

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mexico Grade 8 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 2 (secundaria) (printed pp. 331–340), teaches: Democratic government exercised by the people through elected representatives — its reaches and limits; human-rights organisations that monitor and denounce, parties that carry citizens' demands, and the autonomous bodies of 1990s Mexico, the CNDH among them, that watch the watchers.

---

Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality.
