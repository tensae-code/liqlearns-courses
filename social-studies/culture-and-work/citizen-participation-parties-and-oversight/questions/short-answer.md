**Answer: Citizen Participation Parties and Oversight — democratic government exercised by the people through elected representatives — its reaches and limits; human-rights organisations that monitor and denounce, parties that carry citizens' demands, and the autonomous bodies of 1990s Mexico, the CNDH among them, that watch the watchers (any close wording).**
