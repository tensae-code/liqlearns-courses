## Sub lesson 1 — Citizen Participation Parties and Oversight

Mexico Grade 8 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 2 (secundaria) (printed pp. 331–340), teaches: Democratic government exercised by the people through elected representatives — its reaches and limits; human-rights organisations that monitor and denounce, parties that carry citizens' demands, and the autonomous bodies of 1990s Mexico, the CNDH among them, that watch the watchers. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way

Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
