## Sub lesson 1 — My Family and Our Time Together

Mauritius Grade 1 VACE Teacher’s Handbook (printed p. 36), teaches: Helping, sharing and taking part in family life, and completing a family tree. This lesson teaches the placements’ thing in the course’s own words — the book’s text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mauritius’s way — the book’s own teaching way

Mauritius’s own way: Values taught through the child’s widening circles — self, people around me, then "I love my country" — with Mauritius’s own flag colours, anthem words, and its three islands (Mauritius, Rodrigues, Agalega) as the identity content, and unity taught through the country’s many festivals. This lesson teaches the placements’ thing in the course’s own words — the book’s text itself is never transcribed.
