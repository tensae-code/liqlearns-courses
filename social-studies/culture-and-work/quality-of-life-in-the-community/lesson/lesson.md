## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 3 Qualidade de vida

Angola Estudo do Meio G4 (printed p. 55), teaches: Quality of life — a community has it when it has education, health, housing, food, hygiene, culture and leisure; these are the conditions of personal and collective well-being. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Angola's way — the book's own teaching way

Angola's own way: Grade 4 names the idea itself — instituições públicas: buildings that belong to no private person but serve everyone — and defines quality of life as a checklist a community can judge itself by (education, health, housing, food, hygiene, culture, leisure), ending on the shared duties of sanitation and water. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
