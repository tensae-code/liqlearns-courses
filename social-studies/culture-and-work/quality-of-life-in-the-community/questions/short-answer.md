# Quality Of Life In The Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Tema 3 Qualidade de vida — quality of life — a community has it when it has education, health, housing, food, hygiene, culture and leisure; these are the conditions of personal and collective well-being (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Angola's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 4 names the idea itself — instituições públicas: buildings that belong to no private person but serve everyone — and defines quality of life as a checklist a community can judge itself by (education, health, housing, food, hygiene, culture, leisure), ending on the shared duties of sanitation and water. (any close wording).**
