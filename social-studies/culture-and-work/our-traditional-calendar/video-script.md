Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 50, teaches: Time is counted from the movements of sun, moon and stars — the lunar calendar (билгийн тоолол) against the solar (аргын тоолол); sixty years make one жаран and twelve years one cycle of animal years; the month is split into its first and last fifteen days; and the day is twelve two-hour цаг, each named for an animal and read off the sun's place — with the almanac's good and bad days for beginning great work.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
