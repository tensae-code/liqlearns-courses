# Our Traditional Calendar — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: time is counted from the movements of sun, moon and stars — the lunar calendar (билгийн тоолол) against the solar (аргын тоолол); sixty years make one жаран and twelve years one cycle of animal years; the month is split into its first and last fifteen days; and the day is twelve two-hour цаг, each named for an animal and read off the sun's place — with the almanac's good and bad days for beginning great work.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
