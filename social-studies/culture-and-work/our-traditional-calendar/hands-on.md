# Hands-on — Our Traditional Calendar

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 13 "Traditional time-reckoning" (Уламжлалт цаг тоолол)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Time is counted from the movements of sun, moon and stars — the lunar calendar (билгийн тоолол) against the solar (аргын тоолол); sixty years make one жаран and twelve years one cycle of animal years; the month is split into its first and last fifteen days; and the day is twelve two-hour цаг, each named for an animal and read off the sun's place — with the almanac's good and bad days for beginning great work. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
