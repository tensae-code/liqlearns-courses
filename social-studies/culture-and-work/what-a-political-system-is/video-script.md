The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado6, printed pp. 164–171, teaches: a political system is an organisational form of power, made of the people who exercise power, institutions and the rules/norms that normalise behaviour and consolidate social order; governments where power is divine (the Maya Halach Uinic, a divine king holding legislative power) contrasted with norms in force in Colombia today.

Colombia's own way: civics is taught through the political life of origin peoples — their assemblies, authorities and norms are treated as real political systems, and pupils are asked what indigenous participation and democracy can teach their own region's democratic exercises.
