# What a Political System is

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 22) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: civics is taught through the political life of origin peoples — their assemblies, authorities and norms are treated as real political systems, and pupils are asked what indigenous participation and democracy can teach their own region's democratic exercises.
