# Hands-on — What a Political System is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Módulo 5 Guía 14 Sistemas políticos en los pueblos originarios
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado6, printed pp. 164–171, teaches: a political system is an organisational form of power, made of the people who exercise power, institutions and the rules/norms that normalise behaviour and consolidate social order; governments where power is divine (the Maya Halach Uinic, a divine king holding legislative power) contrasted with norms in force in Colombia today. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 22) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: civics is taught through the political life of origin peoples — their assemblies, authorities and norms are treated as real political systems, and pupils are asked what indigenous participation and democracy can teach their own region's democratic exercises. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
