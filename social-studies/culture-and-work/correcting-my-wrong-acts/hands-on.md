# Hands-on — Correcting My Wrong Acts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.1, middle units
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A wrong act begins in a wrong thought, is spoken, and is then done — so it is corrected at the root: the tree of wrong acts (body: taking life, stealing; speech: lying, gossip, harsh words; mind: greed, malice) against the tree of right acts; the daily practice of examining my acts morning, noon and evening, judging what I thought, said and did, and resolving the correction — with the "Who am I?" essay, in which the writer finds a second self inside who hears criticism, fears, dreams and must be known. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
