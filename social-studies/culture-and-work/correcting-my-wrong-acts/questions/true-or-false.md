# Correcting My Wrong Acts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: a wrong act begins in a wrong thought, is spoken, and is then done — so it is corrected at the root: the tree of wrong acts (body: taking life, stealing; speech: lying, gossip, harsh words; mind: greed, malice) against the tree of right acts; the daily practice of examining my acts morning, noon and evening, judging what I thought, said and did, and resolving the correction — with the "Who am I?" essay, in which the writer finds a second self inside who hears criticism, fears, dreams and must be known.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
