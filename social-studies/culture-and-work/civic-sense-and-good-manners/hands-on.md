# Hands-on — Civic Sense and Good Manners

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 1 Common Etiquettes, Ch. 1 Diversity and Tolerance: Civic Sense and Etiquettes — teaches: greet everyone with salaam, behave well with all, respect parents, teachers and elders, love younger children, never litter shared places, thank people who help you, help others in difficulty, and keep your home, school, parks and streets clean; civic sense shown by examples (traffic rules, keeping the environment clean) and the common etiquettes — good manners, politeness, respect for elders, helping others, dignity of labour and discipline. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
