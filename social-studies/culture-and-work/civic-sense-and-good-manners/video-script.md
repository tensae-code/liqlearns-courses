# Video script — Civic Sense and Good Manners

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pakistan's Grade 5 SS5 (printed p. 7); Grade 4 SS4-KPK (printed p. 7), teaches: Ch. 1 Common Etiquettes, Ch. 1 Diversity and Tolerance: Civic Sense and Etiquettes — teaches: greet everyone with salaam, behave well with all, respect parents, teachers and elders, love younger children, never litter shared places, thank people who help you, help others in difficulty, and keep your home, school, parks and streets clean; civic sense shown by examples (traffic rules, keeping the environment clean) and the common etiquettes — good manners, politeness, respect for elders, helping others, dignity of labour and discipline.

Pakistan's own way: Class 5 deepens Class 4 — rights now change with time, the UN and the 1948 Declaration enter, freedom of speech gets its own lesson with John Stuart Mill quoted, and government is compared across federal, provincial AND local levels with a table of who does what. the same Single National Curriculum in the KPK board's English — citizenship taught identically in Peshawar and Lahore, with the global-citizen lesson anchored by Dr Ruth Pfau, the German doctor who gave 57 years to Pakistan's leprosy patients, and Malala Yousafzai, KPK's own daughter, as the worked examples in the pupil's copy.
