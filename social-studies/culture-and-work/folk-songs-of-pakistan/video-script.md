# Video script — Folk Songs of Pakistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pakistan's Grade 5 SS5 (printed p. 17); Grade 5 SS5-KPK (printed p. 26), teaches: Ch. 2 Folk Songs, Ch. 2 (chapter exercise) — teaches: folk songs carry each region's joys and sorrows in its own language and instruments — the dhol, chimta and algoza traditions of Punjab, Sindhi, Balochi and Pashto folk singing; the culture chapter's own test of itself names Mahiya as a folk song of Punjab — the provinces' songs carried as knowledge every pupil must hold.

Pakistan's own way: Class 5 deepens Class 4 — rights now change with time, the UN and the 1948 Declaration enter, freedom of speech gets its own lesson with John Stuart Mill quoted, and government is compared across federal, provincial AND local levels with a table of who does what. the KPK Class 5 book gives the citizen's charter in two plain lists — seven rights (ending in the right to vote) against nine responsibilities (ending in honest dealing) — and makes Abdul Sattar Edhi, who "served the suffering humanity", its model of what a responsible citizen is.
