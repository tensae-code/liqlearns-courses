## Sub lesson 1 — Social Movements

Bangladesh Grade 8 BD G8 HSS (printed p. 100), teaches: A social movement as people's organised collective effort to change an unfair order — Ram Mohan Roy against sati, Vidyasagar for widow remarriage and women's education, and today's movements for democracy, good governance, human rights, women's empowerment and the environment. ## Sub lesson 2 — Bangladesh's way — the book's own teaching way

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): Sociology is taught as a toolkit for changing one's own society — socialisation, control and change are each defined, then immediately used: the class stops a dowry in a cartoon case, studies Martin Luther King as a movement model, and finishes by setting new norms for itself to practise through the civic club.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
