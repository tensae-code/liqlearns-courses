# Hands-on — Social Movements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A social movement as people's organised collective effort to change an unfair order — Ram Mohan Roy against sati, Vidyasagar for widow remarriage and women's education, and today's movements for democracy, good governance, human rights, women's empowerment and the environment. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
