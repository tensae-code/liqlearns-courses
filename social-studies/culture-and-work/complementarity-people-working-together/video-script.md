# Video script — Complementarity People Working Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rwanda P2 SB Kiny, printed p. 17, teaches: residents care for the village's features in umuganda — clearing bushes, unblocking channels, building and repairing health centres and schools.

Rwanda Grade 5 P5 SB, printed p. 13, teaches: leaders, farmers, parents, children, police and soldiers each play a part in keeping peace and development, and leaders at cell/sector/district level solve conflicts and promote equality.

Rwanda Grade 5 P5 SB, printed p. 16, teaches: the signs that people are working together — helping the needy, respecting each other, sharing resources equally, reporting wrongdoers, dialogue.

Rwanda P6 SB, printed p. 13, teaches: building common projects together, umuganda community work, cleaning the environment together, children of different homes playing together, housing orphans.

Rwanda P6 SB, printed p. 16, teaches: how a community actively builds unity and shared work.

Rwanda's own way (Primary Two — Rwanda P2 SB Kiny): P2 civics starts with who you belong to (small family, wide family, the exact Kinyarwanda name for every relation), then the two smallest rungs of the state — the village and the cell, their elected committees and what umuganda owes them — and the behaviour of peace: playing without discrimination, listening without shouting, and the rights of children and of people with disabilities

Rwanda's own way (Primary Five — Rwanda Grade 5 P5 SB): the province is the child's civic world — complementarity (everyone's work fitting together) is the organising value, and national symbols, the 2003/2015 constitution and province leadership are taught as Rwanda's own frame inside it

Rwanda's own way (Primary Six — Rwanda P6 SB): P6 civics is nationhood — unity through 'Ndi Umunyarwanda', co-operation through umuganda, the world's organisations and Rwanda's own (REB, NURC), Rwandan values and Itorero, and the three organs with the citizen's duties beside the government's
