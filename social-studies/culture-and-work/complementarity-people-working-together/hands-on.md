# Hands-on — Complementarity People Working Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Caring for the Village
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Residents care for the village's features in umuganda — clearing bushes, unblocking channels, building and repairing health centres and schools. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The roles of different people in society
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Leaders, farmers, parents, children, police and soldiers each play a part in keeping peace and development, and leaders at cell/sector/district level solve conflicts and promote equality. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indicators of complementarity in our province
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The signs that people are working together — helping the needy, respecting each other, sharing resources equally, reporting wrongdoers, dialogue. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Situations That Depict Unity and Co-operation
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Building common projects together, umuganda community work, cleaning the environment together, children of different homes playing together, housing orphans. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ways of Promoting Unity and Co-operation
