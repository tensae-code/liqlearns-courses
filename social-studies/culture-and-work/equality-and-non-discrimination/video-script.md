# Video script — Equality and Non Discrimination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Libya Grade 5 National Education G5 (printed p. 26), teaches: equality between my classmate and me means we hold the same rights and the same duties — there is no difference between us; equality between women and men means equal treatment in rights and duties, and stereotypes about either are to be rejected.

Libya's own way: Grade 5 is a pure values book — eight lessons, each a single civic virtue (equality, justice, solidarity, tolerance, accepting the other, dialogue), each anchored in a real instrument a child can hold: the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, UNESCO's 1995 Declaration on Tolerance, and the Libyan Scouts' "A World of Peace for Children" charter, which the pupil signs.
