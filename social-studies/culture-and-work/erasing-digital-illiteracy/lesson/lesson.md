## Sub lesson 1 — Tools for erasing digital illiteracy and Digital technology issues

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: digital citizenship is a named school subject of its own, run with the Aqdar empowerment programme — access is a right the state measures (the DAI index, 100% internet spread in the Emirati market), the Emirati ID card's number is dissected as a civics lesson, and every abuse is answered with the federation's own law and its own reporting service (Abu Dhabi Police "Aman", 8002626). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
