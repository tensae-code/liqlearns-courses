# Hands-on — Erasing Digital Illiteracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tools for erasing digital illiteracy and Digital technology issues
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: digital illiteracy (الأمية الإلكترونية) as not knowing when or how to use technology properly, and e-culture as using communication tools appropriately; the UAE as a state that escaped technical illiteracy; and the live issues — violating printing/copyright, identity theft and forged credit cards — with the Emirati penalty (prison and a fine of 250,000–1,000,000 dirhams) Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
