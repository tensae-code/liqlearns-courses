# Erasing Digital Illiteracy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: digital illiteracy (الأمية الإلكترونية) as not knowing when or how to use technology properly, and e-culture as using communication tools appropriately; the UAE as a state that escaped technical illiteracy; and the live issues — violating printing/copyright, identity theft and forged credit cards — with the Emirati penalty (prison and a fine of 250,000–1,000,000 dirhams)
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: digital citizenship is a named school subject of its own, run with the Aqdar empowerment programme — access is a right the state measures (the DAI index, 100% internet spread in the Emirati market), the Emirati ID card's number is dissected as a civics lesson, and every abuse is answered with the federation's own law and its own reporting service (Abu Dhabi Police "Aman", 8002626).
**Answer: True**
