The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bolivia Compendio Ciencias Sociales (compendium p. 301), teaches: Nationality and citizenship in Bolivia's own constitution — nationality as the legal bond with the State, acquired by ius soli and ius sanguinis and by naturalisation (CPE Arts. 141–143, dual nationality protected), citizenship exercised from 18 (Art. 144: to elect and be eligible, and to hold public office), the difference between the two bonds, the stateless (apátrida) who is denied both, and duties as the counterpart of rights.

Bolivia's own way: the teachers' compendium carries two civics units no held student book carries — the Year 3 pair of women's-protection laws, taught from real cases (a councilwoman driven from office; the murdered concejala Juana Quispe), and the Year 5 citizenship unit, which builds Bolivian citizenship straight from CPE Articles 141–144 and ends with the stateless person who can neither study, work nor marry.
