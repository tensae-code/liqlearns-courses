# Rights and Duties of Citizens — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Nationality and citizenship in Bolivia's own constitution — nationality as the legal bond with the State, acquired by ius soli and ius sanguinis and by naturalisation (CPE Arts. 141–143, dual nationality protected), citizenship exercised from 18 (Art. 144: to elect and be eligible, and to hold public office), the difference between the two bonds, the stateless (apátrida) who is denied both, and duties as the counterpart of rights.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Bolivia's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
