# Rights and Duties of Citizens — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ciudadanía Boliviana, Derechos y Deberes — nationality and citizenship in Bolivia's own constitution — nationality as the legal bond with the State, acquired by ius soli and ius sanguinis and by naturalisation (CPE Arts. 141–143, dual nationality protected), citizenship exercised from 18 (Art. 144: to elect and be eligible, and to hold public office), the difference between the two bonds, the stateless (apátrida) who is denied both, and duties as the counterpart of rights (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way — the teachers' compendium carries two civics units no held student book carries — the Year 3 pair of women's-protection laws, taught from real cases (a councilwoman driven from office; the murdered concejala Juana Quispe), and the Year 5 citizenship unit, which builds Bolivian citizenship straight from CPE Articles 141–144 and ends with the stateless person who can neither study, work nor marry (any close wording).**
