# Hands-on — Rights and Duties of Citizens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: nationality and citizenship in Bolivia's own constitution — nationality as the legal bond with the State, acquired by ius soli and ius sanguinis and by naturalisation (CPE Arts. 141–143, dual nationality protected), citizenship exercised from 18 (Art. 144: to elect and be eligible, and to hold public office), the difference between the two bonds, the stateless (apátrida) who is denied both, and duties as the counterpart of rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
