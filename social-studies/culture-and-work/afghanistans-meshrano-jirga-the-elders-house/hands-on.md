# Hands-on — Afghanistans Meshrano Jirga the Elders House

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The elders' house — 102 members, two-thirds elected (from provincial and district councils) and one-third appointed by the president, including two members for people with disabilities and two for the Kuchis; aged 35+, five-year terms; bills pass here after the Wolesi Jirga before the president signs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
