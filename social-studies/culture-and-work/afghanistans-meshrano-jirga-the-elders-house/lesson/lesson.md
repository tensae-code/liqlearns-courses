## Sub lesson 1 — Afghanistans Meshrano Jirga the Elders House

Afghanistan Grade 6 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 13, teaches: the elders' house — 102 members, two-thirds elected (from provincial and district councils) and one-third appointed by the president, including two members for people with disabilities and two for the Kuchis; aged 35+, five-year terms; bills pass here after the Wolesi Jirga before the president signs. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: the Afghan state is taught as the child's own house — society needs a state as a family needs order; the 2004 constitution is the "mother law"; the two houses are named in Pashto (Wolesi Jirga, Meshrano Jirga) and every right (children's, women's, human dignity) is grounded first in Islam and then in the constitution's own articles. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
