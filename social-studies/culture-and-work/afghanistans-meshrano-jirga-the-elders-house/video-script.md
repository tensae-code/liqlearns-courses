# Video script — Afghanistans Meshrano Jirga the Elders House

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Afghanistan Grade 6 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 13, teaches: the elders' house — 102 members, two-thirds elected (from provincial and district councils) and one-third appointed by the president, including two members for people with disabilities and two for the Kuchis; aged 35+, five-year terms; bills pass here after the Wolesi Jirga before the president signs.

Afghanistan's own way: the Afghan state is taught as the child's own house — society needs a state as a family needs order; the 2004 constitution is the "mother law"; the two houses are named in Pashto (Wolesi Jirga, Meshrano Jirga) and every right (children's, women's, human dignity) is grounded first in Islam and then in the constitution's own articles.
