## Sub lesson 1 — Teaching Social Studies to Pupils with Intellectual Disability

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 teacher guide (special needs) (teacher guide, Persian), printed p. 11, teaches: why Social Studies is a core subject for pupils with intellectual disability — their chief limitation is weak adaptive behaviour, with behavioural and emotional difficulties beside it, and the curriculum's end is independent living: taking one's own life into one's own hands and controlling it (Goodwin, 1991); the topic areas run in a scope-and-sequence across Grades 3–6 — self-knowledge and self-direction (rights and responsibilities) among them — with skills (reading, writing, speaking, number and art skills carried inside the subject's activities) and values and attitudes named as strands of their own; the teacher's greatest care must go to what is actually learned and assessed, and in every exercise the point is generalisation — carrying the learning into the real environment of life and making it usable; the educational package (the software of strategies, models, tasks, frameworks, guidance, assignments and tests, with its hardware) completes the book. ## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the guide states the whole philosophy of the special-needs course — social studies exists to make independent living possible; what is learned must be generalised to real life and the real environment, and the family is a teaching partner, not an audience.
