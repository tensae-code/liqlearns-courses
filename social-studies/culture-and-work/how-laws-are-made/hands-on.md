# Hands-on — How Laws Are Made

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How a law is made in Samoa — a proposal becomes a bill, is read three times in parliament, then goes to the Head of State whose signature makes it law. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Samoa's way
Say how Samoa's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
