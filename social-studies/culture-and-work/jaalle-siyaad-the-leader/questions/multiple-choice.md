# Jaalle Siyaad the Leader — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Jaalle Siyaad (Comrade Siyaad)" is mainly about —
a) the president presented to children as the nation's leader and father-figure — who ruled Somalia after independence, who divided its people, and how Jaalle Siyaad ended the corrupt governments and made the 21 October Revolution  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
