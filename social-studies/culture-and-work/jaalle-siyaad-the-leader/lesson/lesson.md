## Sub lesson 1 — Jaalle Siyaad (Comrade Siyaad)

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 13), teaches: the president presented to children as the nation's leader and father-figure — who ruled Somalia after independence, who divided its people, and how Jaalle Siyaad ended the corrupt governments and made the 21 October Revolution. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
