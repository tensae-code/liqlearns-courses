**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Morocco).**

Morocco's own way (Book 3, Fadaa Ijtiamiat 1AC): collège Social Studies carries a full Citizenship Education component built on ten value lessons in two axes — five personal-social values (dignity, freedom, equality, justice, solidarity), then tolerance, democracy, peace, the role of law, and a field visit to a human-rights institution — each lesson built from constitutional and international-charter texts.

Morocco's own way (Book 5, Fadaa Ijtiamiat 2AC): the second collège year turns citizenship into the machinery of the state itself — the constitution, the King, parliament, government and courts, then the citizen's constitutional rights, parties, unions, press, local democracy and associations.

Morocco's own way (Book 6, Al-Tajdid Ijtiamiat 3AC): the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role.
