## Sub lesson 4 — Morocco's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Morocco's own way: collège Social Studies carries a full Citizenship Education component built on ten value lessons in two axes — five personal-social values (dignity, freedom, equality, justice, solidarity), then tolerance, democracy, peace, the role of law, and a field visit to a human-rights institution — each lesson built from constitutional and international-charter texts. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 5 — Morocco's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way

Morocco's own way: the second collège year turns citizenship into the machinery of the state itself — the constitution, the King, parliament, government and courts, then the citizen's constitutional rights, parties, unions, press, local democracy and associations. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 6 — Morocco's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way

Morocco's own way: the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
