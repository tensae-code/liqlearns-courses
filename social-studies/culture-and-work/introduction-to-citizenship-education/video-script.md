# Video script — Introduction to Citizenship Education

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Morocco Grade 7 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 1AC, printed p. 143, teaches: what citizenship education is, its competencies, and the ten value lessons of the year.

Morocco Grade 8 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 2AC, printed p. 143, teaches: the year's two axes, constitutional institutions and constitutional rights.

Morocco Grade 9 Tajdid Ijtiamiat 3AC, printed p. 155, teaches: the year's theme and the two practice axes, right-and-responsibility and heritage-and-coexistence.

Morocco's own way: collège Social Studies carries a full Citizenship Education component built on ten value lessons in two axes — five personal-social values (dignity, freedom, equality, justice, solidarity), then tolerance, democracy, peace, the role of law, and a field visit to a human-rights institution — each lesson built from constitutional and international-charter texts.

Morocco's own way: the second collège year turns citizenship into the machinery of the state itself — the constitution, the King, parliament, government and courts, then the citizen's constitutional rights, parties, unions, press, local democracy and associations.

Morocco's own way: the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role.
