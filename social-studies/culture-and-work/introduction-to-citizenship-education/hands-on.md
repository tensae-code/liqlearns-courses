# Hands-on — Introduction to Citizenship Education

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Citizenship Education component introduction and programme
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 7 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 1AC, printed p. 143, teaches: what citizenship education is, its competencies, and the ten value lessons of the year. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Citizenship Education component introduction and programme
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 8 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 2AC, printed p. 143, teaches: the year's two axes, constitutional institutions and constitutional rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Citizenship Education component presentation
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 9 Tajdid Ijtiamiat 3AC, printed p. 155, teaches: the year's theme and the two practice axes, right-and-responsibility and heritage-and-coexistence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: collège Social Studies carries a full Citizenship Education component built on ten value lessons in two axes — five personal-social values (dignity, freedom, equality, justice, solidarity), then tolerance, democracy, peace, the role of law, and a field visit to a human-rights institution — each lesson built from constitutional and international-charter texts. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the second collège year turns citizenship into the machinery of the state itself — the constitution, the King, parliament, government and courts, then the citizen's constitutional rights, parties, unions, press, local democracy and associations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
