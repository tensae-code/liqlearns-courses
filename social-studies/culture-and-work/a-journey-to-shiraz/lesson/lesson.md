## Sub lesson 1 — A Journey to Shiraz

Iran Social Studies Grade 5 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 86, teaches: choosing a journey by consultation (مشورت) — Saman's family will travel at Nowruz to one of Iran's sights; father says they should consult together: each member says their own view and the others listen carefully; mother suggests searching the internet for the sights — and the journey chosen is Shiraz, with its famous places and its province of Fars to find on the map. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 5 special-needs book teaches social life through the Iranian year and the Iranian map — feelings and manners at the Yalda gathering and on the phone to the taxi office, safety as a daily habit in three places, and the country learned by travelling it at Nowruz, to Tabriz and to Shiraz. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
