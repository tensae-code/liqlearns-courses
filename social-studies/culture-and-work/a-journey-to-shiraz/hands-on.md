# Hands-on — A Journey to Shiraz

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: choosing a journey by consultation (مشورت) — Saman's family will travel at Nowruz to one of Iran's sights; father says they should consult together: each member says their own view and the others listen carefully; mother suggests searching the internet for the sights — and the journey chosen is Shiraz, with its famous places and its province of Fars to find on the map. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
