# Hands-on — Citizens of Laos

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lesson 18 Citizens of Laos (ພົນລະເມືອງລາວ)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What a citizen of Laos is, how many citizens the country has, and its 49 ethnic groups living together in unity to protect and build the nation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Laos's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Laos's book teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
