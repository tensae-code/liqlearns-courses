# Video script — Citizens of Laos

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Laos Grade 4 laos-g04-world-around-us-teacher (teacher guide), printed p. 63, teaches: What a citizen of Laos is, how many citizens the country has, and its 49 ethnic groups living together in unity to protect and build the nation.

Laos's own way (Book 5): citizenship is introduced inside the science book as a fact of belonging — a Lao citizen is one of 5.6 million people of 49 ethnic groups who live together and must unite to protect and build the country — and Lao culture is taught through dress, livelihoods and festivals, with the duty to protect and promote it.
