## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 18 Citizens of Laos (ພົນລະເມືອງລາວ)

Laos Grade 4 laos-g04-world-around-us-teacher (teacher guide), printed p. 63, teaches: What a citizen of Laos is, how many citizens the country has, and its 49 ethnic groups living together in unity to protect and build the nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Laos's way — the book's own teaching way

Laos's own way: citizenship is introduced inside the science book as a fact of belonging — a Lao citizen is one of 5.6 million people of 49 ethnic groups who live together and must unite to protect and build the country — and Lao culture is taught through dress, livelihoods and festivals, with the duty to protect and promote it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
