**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Eritrea, Jordan).**

Eritrea's own way (ER G5 Social Studies): Grade 5 widens from the village to the world — Eritrea's regions (zobas) and neighbours, the continents, islands, oceans, seas, lakes and rivers of the Earth, then back home to the Red Sea, trade and transport, maps and weather.

Eritrea's own way — Economics expansion (Eritrea Grade 5 Social Studies 5 (Tigrinya)): trade is taught in the mother tongue a full three years before the English-medium Grade 8 book repeats it — the same Eritrean examples (the farmer at market, sesame and fish exports) carried from Tigrinya into English.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 3 Term 2): Trade is taught as a Jordanian family story that starts at the hundred-year-old Hejaz railway, runs from barter and camel peddlers to e-commerce, and lands on the child's own choices — what a family can and cannot do without, where income comes from, and what 2, 3, 5 or 12 dinars in hand can buy.

Jordan's own way (Jordan Grade 5 Term 2): Grade 5 gets the first formal market lesson in the whole set — supply and demand open with frost-damaged vegetables and Ramadan meat prices, trade is read off Aqaba port and Jordan's real export/import lists, and services are toured through Jordan's own schools platform, hospitals and tourist sites.
