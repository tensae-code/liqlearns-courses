# Hands-on — Justice Moral and Legal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: justice as the measure for judging acts, laws and policies; Justinian's definition (to give each their due) and a Martin Luther King Jr. text on just and unjust laws. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
