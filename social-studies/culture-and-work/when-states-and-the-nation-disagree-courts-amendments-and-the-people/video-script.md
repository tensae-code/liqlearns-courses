The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Micronesia Grade 11 Micronesian Government (printed p. 259), teaches: the fishing-licence fight — the Supreme Court ruled a licence fee is not a tax, the governors sought a constitutional amendment (75% of voters in three of four states), and the people voted no in 2001; the people always have the final say.

Micronesia's own way: present-day government is taught as “Micronesia's Two Governments” — the elected FSM state/national system and the traditional leadership system running beside it — and every structure (federal levels, checks and balances, the rotating presidency) is explained through the compromises of the first constitutional convention and through village-level stories (a Yap bus bill stopped by chiefs, a fishing-fee fight between governors and Palikir, the Amnesty Bill stopped by the people).
