# Hands-on — When States and the Nation Disagree Courts Amendments and the People

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the fishing-licence fight — the Supreme Court ruled a licence fee is not a tax, the governors sought a constitutional amendment (75% of voters in three of four states), and the people voted no in 2001; the people always have the final say. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
