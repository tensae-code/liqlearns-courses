# Hands-on — Responsibility Punctuality Determination and Duty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Responsibility practised a week at a time — punctuality (Pema, who snoozed her alarm and missed the bus; the child plans wake-up, breakfast and getting-ready times), determination (the Itsy Bitsy Spider sung and climbed again; three things I will do to reach what I want to become), and duty (Sonam's unfinished holiday homework; my responsibilities written out for home, the clean classroom and the environment). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
