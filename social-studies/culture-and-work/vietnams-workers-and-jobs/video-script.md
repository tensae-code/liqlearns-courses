The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 39–42), teaches: Bài 8: Vietnam's labour and jobs — a workforce of 52.9 million (52.2% of the population), only 28.4% of it trained (the 2010–2024 training table), how workers are spread (the Red River Delta holding 23.3%), the shift out of farming (49.5% of jobs in 2010 to 26.4% in 2024) and between ownership sectors, unemployment and underemployment, and the ways jobs are created.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
