## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnams Workers And Jobs

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 39–42), teaches: Bài 8: Vietnam's labour and jobs — a workforce of 52.9 million (52.2% of the population), only 28.4% of it trained (the 2010–2024 training table), how workers are spread (the Red River Delta holding 23.3%), the shift out of farming (49.5% of jobs in 2010 to 26.4% in 2024) and between ownership sectors, unemployment and underemployment, and the ways jobs are created. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
