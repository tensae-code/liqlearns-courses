# Hands-on — Vietnams Workers And Jobs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 8: Vietnam's labour and jobs — a workforce of 52.9 million (52.2% of the population), only 28.4% of it trained (the 2010–2024 training table), how workers are spread (the Red River Delta holding 23.3%), the shift out of farming (49.5% of jobs in 2010 to 26.4% in 2024) and between ownership sectors, unemployment and underemployment, and the ways jobs are created. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
