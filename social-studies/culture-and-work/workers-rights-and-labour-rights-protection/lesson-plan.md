# Lesson plan — Workers Rights and Labour Rights Protection

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Workers Rights and Labour Rights Protection
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Belarus, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The working person — his rights and duties (the §'s profit passage is the Economics wave's, placed there and not repeated here): the rights to work and to dispose freely of one's abilities, to unite in trade unions to defend one's interests, to strike as the extreme measure, and to take part in managing the enterprise through the labour collective's general meetings; the worker's rights to healthy and safe work conditions, respect for his personal dignity, timely payment, rest (breaks, days off, holidays) and social insurance and pension provision; his duties — to work conscientiously, keep safety rules, treat the employer's property carefully, carry out orders and keep the employer's commercial secret; discipline and the organisation of work — a worker who breaks his duties bears disciplinary, material, administrative or criminal responsibility; trade unions and the administration — professional solidarity, unions as voluntary formations defending their members' social-economic rights independently, subordinated only to the law; and the employer's duties, with the Labour Code's protection of minors (no employment contract under 16; from 14 only with a parent's written consent, Articles 173–174) — as Belarus' book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
