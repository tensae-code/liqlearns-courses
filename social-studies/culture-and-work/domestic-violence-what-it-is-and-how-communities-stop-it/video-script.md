# Video script — Domestic Violence What It is and How Communities Stop It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bhutan Class VI Social Studies VI, printed p. 31, teaches: domestic violence as a pattern of control, not only physical blows — its six types (physical, emotional, financial, sexual, spiritual, technological), its causes and effects on victims and children, and the community answers: education and awareness, prevention programmes, cutting alcohol and drug use, and working with the Royal Bhutan Police, NCWC and RENEW.

Bhutan's own way: the primary course closes by putting the whole state in front of the child — what a government is, Bhutan's own change from monarchy to Democratic Constitutional Monarchy in 2008, its three branches told through the courts' own ladder and the kabney/patang ranks the King bestows, the Article 7 fundamental rights quoted from Bhutan's Constitution, and a social-problems chapter that names Bhutan's own helping agencies (Royal Bhutan Police, RENEW, NCWC) for every problem.
