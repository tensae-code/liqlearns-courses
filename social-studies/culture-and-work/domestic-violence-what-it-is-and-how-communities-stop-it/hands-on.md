# Hands-on — Domestic Violence What It is and How Communities Stop It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Domestic violence as a pattern of control, not only physical blows — its six types (physical, emotional, financial, sexual, spiritual, technological), its causes and effects on victims and children, and the community answers: education and awareness, prevention programmes, cutting alcohol and drug use, and working with the Royal Bhutan Police, NCWC and RENEW. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
