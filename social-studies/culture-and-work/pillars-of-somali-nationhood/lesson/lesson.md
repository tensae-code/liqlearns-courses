# Pillars of Somali Nationhood

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what makes the Somalis one nation — the shared foundations of nationhood a third-grader can name — as Somalia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rukumada asaasiga ah ee ummadnimada Soomaaliyeed (The Basic Pillars of Somali Nationhood)

Somalia Grade 3 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 1), teaches: what makes the Somalis one nation — the shared foundations of nationhood a third-grader can name. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 3): Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
