# Hands-on — Features of a Successful Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 8 Key features for a successful change
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a successful change needs a clear goal (a good reason for the change), goodwill from the leaders who have their citizens at heart, adherence to the rule of law so the change does not harm others' lives, and adequate civic education so citizens are aware of the change. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unit 1 overview: tolerance, respect and equality
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the guide adds that promoting tolerance, respect and equality are key features of successfully implementing change, alongside the pupil's book's goal, goodwill, rule of law and civic education (the successful-change lesson is filed NEW from Book 11 in this wave).
