# Features of a Successful Change

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a successful change needs a clear goal (a good reason for the change), goodwill from the leaders who have their citizens at heart, adherence to the rule of law so the change does not harm others' lives, and adequate civic education so citizens are aware of the change — as South Sudan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Activity 8 Key features for a successful change

South Sudan Primary 6 P6 Social Studies SB, printed p. 13, teaches: a successful change needs a clear goal (a good reason for the change), goodwill from the leaders who have their citizens at heart, adherence to the rule of law so the change does not harm others' lives, and adequate civic education so citizens are aware of the change. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

South Sudan's own way: The country's own conflict is the textbook — secession is explained by marginalisation and exploitation of the South's resources, and the constitution enters as homework: explain why South Sudan's constitution is important and name five rights it guarantees.
