# Video script — Features of a Successful Change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 6 P6 Social Studies SB, printed p. 13, teaches: a successful change needs a clear goal (a good reason for the change), goodwill from the leaders who have their citizens at heart, adherence to the rule of law so the change does not harm others' lives, and adequate civic education so citizens are aware of the change.

South Sudan Primary 6 P6 Social Studies TG, printed p. 1, teaches: the guide adds that promoting tolerance, respect and equality are key features of successfully implementing change, alongside the pupil's book's goal, goodwill, rule of law and civic education (the successful-change lesson is filed NEW from Book 11 in this wave).

South Sudan's own way: The country's own conflict is the textbook — secession is explained by marginalisation and exploitation of the South's resources, and the constitution enters as homework: explain why South Sudan's constitution is important and name five rights it guarantees.
