## Sub lesson 1 — The ethnic groups in Myanmar

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide teaches the ethnic groups in Myanmar in its social geography strand (§2.3): the country's own ethnic groups are the subject — Myanmar is a country of many ethnic groups, and naming and studying them is part of its geography.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — its own people, in its own geography

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Myanmar the social geography strand first defines social geography and the main ethnic groups (§2.1), then teaches the arrival of the Mongoloid ethnic groups (§2.2), and then the ethnic groups in Myanmar (§2.3) — the country's own way is to teach its own peoples as geography, in that order.

Myanmar's own way: the year is split into five parallel strands revisited week by week — physical geography, social geography, environmental geography, neighbouring countries and cartography — each lesson plan built student-centred (Bloom steps, key questions, classroom assessment) around Myanmar and its five neighbours. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
