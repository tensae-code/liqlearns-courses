# Hands-on — Sights of My Homeland Places That Matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the sights of my homeland — every oblast of Kazakhstan has its natural and historical sights, from the Baiterek monument to the house in Petropavlovsk where Abylai Khan lived, gathered into the pupils' own booklet. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kazakhstan's way
Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
