# Social Responsibility in Practice — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: responsibility put to work — pupils name the issues of their own local community, draw up an action plan with steps a group can really take (such as volunteering to improve a neglected place), and rate their own responsible habits: keeping their belongings and the school's, considering themselves responsible people, helping at home, and volunteering at school and in the community
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: in the Moral Education course, nine-year-olds are trained as peer mediators with a named toolkit — settlement, concession, active listening — and the five ways of facing a dispute are taught through animals (the shark forces, the turtle withdraws, the bear yields, the fox compromises, the owl solves); responsibility is taught by a lost wallet with 800 dirhams in it.
**Answer: True**
