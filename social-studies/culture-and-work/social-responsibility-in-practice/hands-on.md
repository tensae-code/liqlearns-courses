# Hands-on — Social Responsibility in Practice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Social responsibility in practice
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: responsibility put to work — pupils name the issues of their own local community, draw up an action plan with steps a group can really take (such as volunteering to improve a neglected place), and rate their own responsible habits: keeping their belongings and the school's, considering themselves responsible people, helping at home, and volunteering at school and in the community Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
