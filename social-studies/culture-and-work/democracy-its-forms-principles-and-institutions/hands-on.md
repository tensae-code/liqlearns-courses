# Hands-on — Democracy Its Forms Principles and Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Democracy from the Greek demos; ancient direct democracy against its modern forms; the people exercising power personally (elections, referendums) or through representatives; direct democracy kept alive in the modern state beside the representative kind; and democracy's principles — majority rule with account of the minority, equality of citizens in politics — as the book's own scheme of forms and principles lays them out. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
