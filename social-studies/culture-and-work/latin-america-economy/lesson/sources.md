**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Armenia, Nicaragua).**

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 5 Estudios Sociales 5): the lens widens from Nicaragua to Central America as one economy — plantation export crops against smallholder subsistence farming, with Nicaragua's 2012–2015 real GDP growth (4.7%) compared against the Central American average (3.7%).

Nicaragua's own way (Nicaragua Grade 8 Estudios Sociales 8): the same "Space and Economy" unit as the distance-education module, but fuller — after the sectors and the continent's three regional economies, Nicaragua's own economy is toured sector by sector (agroindustry, commerce, fishing, tourism, telecoms, mining, energy, free zones, organic production, forestry, and the MIPYMEs that are over 90% of its firms), and human development is set against GDP per capita.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 8 Geography student): a pure regional-geography year — the world is divided into regions following the UN scheme, each region and chosen state is studied in the same fixed order (position, natural conditions and resources, population, economy), and Armenia's neighbours (Georgia, Azerbaijan, Turkey, Iran) get full country lessons of their own.
