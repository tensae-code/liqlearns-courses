# Hands-on — Listening to Teaching Words

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 2.1, opening units
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The teaching word (сургаал үг) is the distilled experience of the ancestors, forgotten by the young at their cost — Chinggis's Bilig teachings and Ravjaa's verse agree: a fault-correcting word, however harsh, taken humbly is medicine, and taken proudly is poison; how to receive correction — first ask what the person means by it and why they say it to me, listen to the end without interrupting, do not take offence, receive it gently, and then correct the fault; Togtokhtör Wang's household teaching and the paper-bird poem close the unit: there is no person who does not need teaching. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
