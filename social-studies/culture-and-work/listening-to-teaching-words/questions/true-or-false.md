# Listening to Teaching Words — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the teaching word (сургаал үг) is the distilled experience of the ancestors, forgotten by the young at their cost — Chinggis's Bilig teachings and Ravjaa's verse agree: a fault-correcting word, however harsh, taken humbly is medicine, and taken proudly is poison; how to receive correction — first ask what the person means by it and why they say it to me, listen to the end without interrupting, do not take offence, receive it gently, and then correct the fault; Togtokhtör Wang's household teaching and the paper-bird poem close the unit: there is no person who does not need teaching.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
