## Sub lesson 1 — How The First Villages Arose

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 4 Social Studies* (printed p. 38), teaches: Lesson 8 How did the first villages come to be? (نخستین روستاها چگونه به‌وجود آمدند؟) — early man's first tools were mostly of stone, wood and bone; the arising of the villages (پیدایش روستاها): people knew that what they had planted in the farmlands needed care, so they gradually settled beside their fields, and settled life began. ## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 4 Social Studies): a primary Social Studies book of 22 lessons in 6 chapters, working outward from the neighbourhood to the whole country. The Geography parts placed below are Ch. 1 Our neighbourhood (Lessons 2 and 4, printed pp. 5–23), Ch. 2 My city, my village (Lessons 5–7, printed pp. 24–37), Ch. 3 The arising of city and village (Lessons 8–9, printed pp. 38–46) and Ch. 5 My beautiful country (Lessons 15–19, printed pp. 74–98). The other parts are named here, not placed: Ch. 1 Lesson 1 Our neighbour (printed p. 2) and Lesson 3 Buying and selling in the neighbourhood (printed p. 10, Economics); Ch. 3 Lesson 10 How historians study the past (printed p. 47, History method); Ch. 4 A trip to the ancient cities (Lessons 11–14, printed pp. 51–70, History — Hamadan, Persepolis in two lessons, Kermanshah); Ch. 6 We are Iranians (Lessons 20–22, printed pp. 99–110, Civics — our national symbols, the calendar, important days). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
