# Friendship and Safe Relations Online

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: friendship (from Latin amicus/amore) needs a positive valuation of oneself, trust, reciprocity and certainty in good and bad moments; friendship is a relation between equals — with adults the relation must rest on respect and never join anything that harms or discomforts us; a bad friendship can drag us to vice, and the class studies the risks of using social networks — as Honduras's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.
