# Video script — Friendship and Safe Relations Online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras Grade 7 G7 Student CCSS, printed p. 26, teaches: Friendship (from Latin amicus/amore) needs a positive valuation of oneself, trust, reciprocity and certainty in good and bad moments; friendship is a relation between equals — with adults the relation must rest on respect and never join anything that harms or discomforts us; a bad friendship can drag us to vice, and the class studies the risks of using social networks.

Honduras's own way (Book 7): secondary civics starts from the family as a legal institution — the Family Code (Decree 76-84) enters in Lesson 1 — and builds to the State itself: its elements (population, territory, power), Montesquieu's separation, and the full legislative process ending in publication in La Gaceta.
