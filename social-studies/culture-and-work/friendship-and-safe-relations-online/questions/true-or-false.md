# Friendship and Safe Relations Online — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: friendship (from Latin amicus/amore) needs a positive valuation of oneself, trust, reciprocity and certainty in good and bad moments; friendship is a relation between equals — with adults the relation must rest on respect and never join anything that harms or discomforts us; a bad friendship can drag us to vice, and the class studies the risks of using social networks.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
