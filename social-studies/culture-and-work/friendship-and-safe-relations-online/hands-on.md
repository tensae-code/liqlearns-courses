# Hands-on — Friendship and Safe Relations Online

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 4 La amistad en un mundo digital
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Friendship (from Latin amicus/amore) needs a positive valuation of oneself, trust, reciprocity and certainty in good and bad moments; friendship is a relation between equals — with adults the relation must rest on respect and never join anything that harms or discomforts us; a bad friendship can drag us to vice, and the class studies the risks of using social networks. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
