# Hands-on — All People Abiding by the Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Universal observance — every member of society respecting and believing in the law, exercising rights and fulfilling duties according to law, building credit and honesty systems, so that observing the law is honoured and breaking it is shamed. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
