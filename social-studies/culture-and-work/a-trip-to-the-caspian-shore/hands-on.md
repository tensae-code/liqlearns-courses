# Hands-on — A Trip to the Caspian Shore

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ali's family leaves Rasht for Bandar Anzali, another city of Gilan province; a port (بندر) is a place beside the sea where ships arrive, leave and come and go; Anzali stands beside the sea and is a port — find it on last lesson's map. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
