# Hands-on — Uganda as an Independent Nation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: independence as freedom from another country's control; a nation has its own leader, marked boundaries, national forces, citizens and symbols, and its people elect their leaders. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Uganda's way
Say how Uganda's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
