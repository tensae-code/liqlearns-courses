# Video script — Big States Small States the Fsm Congress

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Micronesia Grade 11 Micronesian Government (printed p. 267), teaches: two kinds of congressmen balance big and small states — one four-year at-large member from each state, plus two-year members by population, recounted every ten years.

Micronesia's own way: present-day government is taught as “Micronesia's Two Governments” — the elected FSM state/national system and the traditional leadership system running beside it — and every structure (federal levels, checks and balances, the rotating presidency) is explained through the compromises of the first constitutional convention and through village-level stories (a Yap bus bill stopped by chiefs, a fishing-fee fight between governors and Palikir, the Amnesty Bill stopped by the people).
