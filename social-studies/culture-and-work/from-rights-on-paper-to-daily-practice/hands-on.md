# Hands-on — From Rights on Paper to Daily Practice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: official decisions (authorities, justice, press) judged against rights and values, and pupils engaging in an inclusive-school project and in daily respect of rights at school and in the community. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Haiti's way
Say how Haiti's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
