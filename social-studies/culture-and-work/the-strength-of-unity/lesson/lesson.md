## Sub lesson 3 — Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way

Myanmar's own way: Grade 6 is where the values course becomes a civics course — rights and responsibilities are named as such, the school has a council, rules are explained up to the rules of a country, and the last unit is peace: what conflict is, the bundle-of-firewood lesson, peace symbols, and rowing in unison. Grade 4 names children's RIGHTS as a picture inventory (food, water, a house, clothes, care, medicine, play, learning, family) and turns environmental care into two full units — preserving the earth and the natural surroundings, with drought, flood, felled forests and factory smoke as the evidence. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
