# National Antiquities and Heritage — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Antiquities as the homeland's civilisational record expressing its history and heritage, found in museums and historic sites (al-Faw, al-Rabadha, Jurash, al-Hijr); preserving them is a national duty — the Heritage Commission cares for them and the antiquities law punishes offences (unlicensed digging, seizing, damaging or forging antiquities); their kinds — buildings, water installations, pottery, glass, inscriptions, coins, weapons and jewellery — and what the homeland gains from them: national belonging, culture, tourism and documented history.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Saudi Arabia's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
