# Hands-on — National Antiquities and Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: antiquities as the homeland's civilisational record expressing its history and heritage, found in museums and historic sites (al-Faw, al-Rabadha, Jurash, al-Hijr); preserving them is a national duty — the Heritage Commission cares for them and the antiquities law punishes offences (unlicensed digging, seizing, damaging or forging antiquities); their kinds — buildings, water installations, pottery, glass, inscriptions, coins, weapons and jewellery — and what the homeland gains from them: national belonging, culture, tourism and documented history. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
