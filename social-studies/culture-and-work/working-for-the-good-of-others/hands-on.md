# Hands-on — Working for the Good of Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Contribution for bringing advantages for other people
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Work whose fruit is other people's good — cleaning the road, helping the injured, mending the bridge — done while working for one's own living. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Work together for everyone's good
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Joining hands in work whose benefit is everyone's. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
