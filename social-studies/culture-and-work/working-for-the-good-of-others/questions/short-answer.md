# Working for the Good of Others — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Contribution for bringing advantages for other people — Work whose fruit is other people's good — cleaning the road, helping the injured, mending the bridge — done while working for one's own living (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Work together for everyone's good — Joining hands in work whose benefit is everyone's (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way — the Grade 3 units keep the story-and-poem shape but argue finer points: fairness is NOT equal shares (the hungry son gets bread, the full daughter a candy; the girl with glasses sits in front), trust is keeping a friend's weakness secret, and the clean-environment unit teaches Reduce–Reuse–Recycle by name. Grade 4 names children's RIGHTS as a picture inventory (food, water, a house, clothes, care, medicine, play, learning, family) and turns environmental care into two full units — preserving the earth and the natural surroundings, with drought, flood, felled forests and factory smoke as the evidence. (any close wording).**
