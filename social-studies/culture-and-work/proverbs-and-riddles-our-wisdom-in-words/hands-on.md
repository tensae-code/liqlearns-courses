# Hands-on — Proverbs And Riddles Our Wisdom in Words

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 12 "Mongolian proverbs and riddles" (Монгол зүйр цэцэн үг, оньсого)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A proverb (зүйр цэцэн үг) packs the people's life, thought and custom into a few sharp words — its deep meaning is the lesson; riddles picture a thing without naming it and train the mind; seasonal sayings even carry the herder's weather knowledge. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
