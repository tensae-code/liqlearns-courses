# Proverbs And Riddles Our Wisdom in Words

## Do it first

Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 12 "Mongolian proverbs and riddles" (Монгол зүйр цэцэн үг, оньсого)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 42, teaches: A proverb (зүйр цэцэн үг) packs the people's life, thought and custom into a few sharp words — its deep meaning is the lesson; riddles picture a thing without naming it and train the mind; seasonal sayings even carry the herder's weather knowledge. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
