# Hands-on — Punctuality Valuing Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: why appointments matter — a factory's output falls because its workers do not keep work times; the causes of lateness (no programme, no fixed time, forgetting) and the cures, a family's weekly timetable, and the rules for organising your own time (a daily schedule, early sleep, school things ready the night before). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
