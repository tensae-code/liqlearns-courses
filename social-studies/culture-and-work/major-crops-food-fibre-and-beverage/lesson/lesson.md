## Sub lesson 1 — Major Crops — Food, Fibre and Beverage

India's Grade 8 Resources and Development book, the *India Grade 8 Resources and Development* (printed p. 42), teaches: Ch.4: major crops — rice, wheat, maize, millets; fibre crops jute and cotton; beverage crops tea and coffee, and what each needs to grow. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — India's way — the book's own teaching way

India's own way (Grade 8 Resources and Development): Grade 8 is a resources year, world-first but India-anchored throughout: resources and their conservation, then land/soil/water/vegetation, minerals and power, agriculture, industries and human resources — every world pattern is paired with its Indian distribution map and an Indian case study (Jamshedpur steel, Ahmedabad textiles, Bangalore IT, a farm in India set against a farm in the USA). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
