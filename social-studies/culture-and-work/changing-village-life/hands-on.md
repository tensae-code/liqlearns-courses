# Hands-on — Changing Village Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Investigating twenty years of change in one's own area, and Bangladesh's changing village — ploughs giving way to tillers, tractors and harvesters, shepherd boys disappearing into school, work diversifying beyond paddy — with about three million child labourers still to be brought into education. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
