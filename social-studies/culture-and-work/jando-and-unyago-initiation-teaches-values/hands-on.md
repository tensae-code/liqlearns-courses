# Hands-on — Jando and Unyago Initiation Teaches Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Jando and Unyago Initiation Teaches Values
Work through this part with your hands: Jando (boys' initiation to manhood) and Unyago (girls' initiation to womanhood), practised by societies such as the Kurya, Maasai, Gogo and Ngoni, teach youths good behaviour, ethics and the laws of their society — education and life skills, love and respect for others, self-reliance, cooperation in work, weddings, funerals and childcare, love of work, self-care and environmental cleaning, protection of society and patriotism, and caring for children, elders and the sick. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
