## Sub lesson 1 — Jando and Unyago Initiation Teaches Values

Tanzania Standard Six tz-ss6 (printed p. 54), teaches: Jando (boys' initiation to manhood) and Unyago (girls' initiation to womanhood), practised by societies such as the Kurya, Maasai, Gogo and Ngoni, teach youths good behaviour, ethics and the laws of their society — education and life skills, love and respect for others, self-reliance, cooperation in work, weddings, funerals and childcare, love of work, self-care and environmental cleaning, protection of society and patriotism, and caring for children, elders and the sick.
