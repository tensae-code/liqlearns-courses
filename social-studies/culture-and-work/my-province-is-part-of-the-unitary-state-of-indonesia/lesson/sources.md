**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Indonesia).** Indonesia's teacher-guide parts in this lesson are teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere — Indonesia counts through its student-book parts.

Indonesia's own way (Book 1): the primary Pancasila course closes by widening every earlier theme to the nation — norms become norms of berbangsa dan bernegara (national and state life), and gotong royong is finally named as bela negara, defending the country by working together.

Indonesia's own way (Book 2): the final primary guide states the subject's position (kedudukan) explicitly — Pendidikan Pancasila is the vehicle of the state ideology in school — and sequences outcomes to the NKRI chapters.
