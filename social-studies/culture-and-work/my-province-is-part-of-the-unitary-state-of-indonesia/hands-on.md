# Hands-on — My Province is Part of the Unitary State of Indonesia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 6 A. Mengenal Provinsi di Indonesia, Bab 6 B. Bangga dengan Provinsiku, Panduan Khusus Bab 6: Provinsiku Bagian dari Wilayah Negara Kesatuan Republik Indonesia — teaches: Indonesia's provinces — their territories and each province's distinctive features, mine and others'; pride in my province — my province is special, and respecting the provinces of others; guidance for provinces inside the NKRI and pride in one's own province. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indonesia's way
Say how Indonesia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
