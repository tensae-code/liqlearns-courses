# Hands-on — Elections How Leaders Are Chosen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Elections How Leaders Are Chosen
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The call to elections — general elections are called by decision of the President three months before the end of his term or the Legislative Council's, fixing the dates of candidacy and polling and published in the official gazette and the local daily newspapers, while local elections are called by a Cabinet decision; registering the voters in the voters' register, to determine who meets the conditions of the right to vote; publishing the preliminary voters' register so voters can check their names and the correctness of their data; electoral campaigning — the activities by which the lists and candidates explain their programmes in a fixed period to urge voters to vote for them, provided they do not conflict with the laws and regulations; polling — the voters go to the electoral centres on a set day, and the day of general elections is an official holiday; and counting and announcing the results inside the polling centres — the ballot boxes are opened and the papers sorted in the presence of the centre's committee, local and international observers and media representatives, and after counting the election result is announced. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
