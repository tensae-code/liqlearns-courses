# Deciding Together Collective Leadership — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 5.1 "Why are collective leadership and decisions important?" — "If we discuss together, we cannot go wrong" — collective leadership (хамтын удирдлага) means a question concerning everyone is decided by everyone it concerns, in person or through chosen representatives, as the Constitution promises a humane, civil, democratic society; the decision path — find the problem, understand it, develop options, choose, implement; the rules of discussion — be brief and to the point, argue with evidence, listen without interrupting, keep to the agreed time; and the proof at national scale: the 1992 Constitution itself was discussed by some 900,000 people in 7,000 meetings before adoption (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work (any close wording).**
