# Deciding Together Collective Leadership

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: "If we discuss together, we cannot go wrong" — collective leadership (хамтын удирдлага) means a question concerning everyone is decided by everyone it concerns, in person or through chosen representatives, as the Constitution promises a humane, civil, democratic society; the decision path — find the problem, understand it, develop options, choose, implement; the rules of discussion — be brief and to the point, argue with evidence, listen without interrupting, keep to the agreed time; and the proof at national scale: the 1992 Constitution itself was discussed by some 900,000 people in 7,000 meetings before adoption — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.
