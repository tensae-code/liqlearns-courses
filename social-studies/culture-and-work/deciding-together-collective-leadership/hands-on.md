# Hands-on — Deciding Together Collective Leadership

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 5.1 "Why are collective leadership and decisions important?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: "If we discuss together, we cannot go wrong" — collective leadership (хамтын удирдлага) means a question concerning everyone is decided by everyone it concerns, in person or through chosen representatives, as the Constitution promises a humane, civil, democratic society; the decision path — find the problem, understand it, develop options, choose, implement; the rules of discussion — be brief and to the point, argue with evidence, listen without interrupting, keep to the agreed time; and the proof at national scale: the 1992 Constitution itself was discussed by some 900,000 people in 7,000 meetings before adoption. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
