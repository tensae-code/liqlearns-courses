# Video script — Deciding Together Collective Leadership

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 7 Social Studies, printed p. 80, teaches: "If we discuss together, we cannot go wrong" — collective leadership (хамтын удирдлага) means a question concerning everyone is decided by everyone it concerns, in person or through chosen representatives, as the Constitution promises a humane, civil, democratic society; the decision path — find the problem, understand it, develop options, choose, implement; the rules of discussion — be brief and to the point, argue with evidence, listen without interrupting, keep to the agreed time; and the proof at national scale: the 1992 Constitution itself was discussed by some 900,000 people in 7,000 meetings before adoption.

Mongolia's own way (Book 5): the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work.
