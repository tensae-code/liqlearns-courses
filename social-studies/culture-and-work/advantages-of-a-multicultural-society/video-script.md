# Video script — Advantages of a Multicultural Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pakistan's Grade 5 SS5 (printed p. 17), teaches: Ch. 2 Advantages of Multicultural Society — teaches: a society of many cultures is richer, not weaker — people learn each other's languages, foods and customs, respect grows, and the country presents many faces to the world; cultural difference is defined and respected, not erased.

Pakistan's own way: Class 5 deepens Class 4 — rights now change with time, the UN and the 1948 Declaration enter, freedom of speech gets its own lesson with John Stuart Mill quoted, and government is compared across federal, provincial AND local levels with a table of who does what.
