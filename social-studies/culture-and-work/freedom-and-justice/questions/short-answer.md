# Freedom and Justice — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Freedom and Justice — freedom is the state in which a person is not subject to coercion or restriction and his choices and conduct follow his own will, provided he does not assault others' freedoms; it is a human right no one may deny or limit to another, and it drives the work for independence, national liberation and building the independent Palestinian state with Jerusalem as its capital; its importance is that the individual practises his capacities and develops his energies and skills, so the people share in shaping the laws and feel the state moves by their proposals, which deepens their sense of responsibility; justice is the belief that all people are equal with no discrimination, that each person has a set of rights to enjoy and responsibilities to keep, and applying it in society achieves equality between its members, security and safety for all without fear of injustice, and spreads love and familiarity among them (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
