## Sub lesson 1 — Ch 1 On Equality

India Grade 7 Social and Political Life-II (printed p. 4), teaches: one person one vote is real equality at the ballot box, but caste, religion and poverty make daily life unequal; dignity is what equality protects (Kanta, Omprakash Valmiki, the Ansaris). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — India's way — the book's own teaching way

India's own way: Equality is taught through real lives — a domestic worker voting, a Dalit writer made to sweep his school, a Muslim couple refused a flat — and government through one running case (a diarrhoea outbreak in a fictional district) that forces every institution to act.
