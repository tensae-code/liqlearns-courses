# Video script — Inheritance and Deceased Estates Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe Grade 7 zw-g7 PlusOne Social Science Grade 7 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 67), teaches: Inheritance is passing property/rights after death, guided by Zimbabwe's Deceased Estates Succession Act — spouse and children are first heirs, a will must be written, signed and witnessed, and the Master of the High Court confirms heirs and shares the estate, with the Legal Resources Centre and ZHRL as help institutions — role-played as testator, heirs and master of court.
Zimbabwe Grade 7 zw-ppp-g7 Best Approach to Heritage-Social Studies Teacher's Resource Book Grade 7 (printed pp. 22–32), teaches: What inheritance is and the channels, institutions and organisations which facilitate it.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 9) (teacher guide — evidence): the primary cycle closes with adult law taught straight — inheritance under the Deceased Estates Succession Act with the Master of the High Court role-played in class, and gender equity taught through Zimbabwe's own institutions (Zimbabwe Gender Commission, Msasa Project, Women's Bank, community savings groups) with Constitution excerpts in the materials list.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 11) (teacher guide — evidence): the parallel Grade 7 book adds what the PlusOne guide does not teach — elections themselves (forms of elections, the electoral process, role-played class elections for class monitor) — and teaches international organisations as a table of UN agencies with their functions and years of formation.
