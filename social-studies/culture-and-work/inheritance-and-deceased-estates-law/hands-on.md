# Hands-on — Inheritance and Deceased Estates Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: inheritance is passing property/rights after death, guided by Zimbabwe's Deceased Estates Succession Act — spouse and children are first heirs, a will must be written, signed and witnessed, and the Master of the High Court confirms heirs and shares the estate, with the Legal Resources Centre and ZHRL as help institutions — role-played as testator, heirs and master of court. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
