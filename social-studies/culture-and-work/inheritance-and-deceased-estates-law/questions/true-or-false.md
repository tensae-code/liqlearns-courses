# Inheritance and Deceased Estates Law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Inheritance is passing property/rights after death, guided by Zimbabwe's Deceased Estates Succession Act — spouse and children are first heirs, a will must be written, signed and witnessed, and the Master of the High Court confirms heirs and shares the estate, with the Legal Resources Centre and ZHRL as help institutions — role-played as testator, heirs and master of court.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Zimbabwe's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
