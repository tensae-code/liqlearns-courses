# Hands-on — Understanding and Including People with Disabilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: disability is a physical or mental condition affecting daily activities — physical (paralysis, loss of limbs), visual (blindness, low vision) and hearing impairments; people with disabilities are accommodated through inclusive infrastructure (ramps, elevators, accessible restrooms, braille signs) and inclusive education with peer support and a sense of belonging. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
