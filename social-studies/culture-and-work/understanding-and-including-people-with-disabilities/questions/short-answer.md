**Answer: Unit 32: Understanding people with disabilities — Zimbabwe Grade 4 zw-g4 PlusOne Social Science Grade 4 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 100), teaches: Disability is a physical or mental condition affecting daily activities — physical (paralysis, loss of limbs), visual (blindness, low vision) and hearing impairments; people with disabilities are accommodated through inclusive infrastructure (ramps, elevators, accessible restrooms, braille signs) and inclusive education with peer support and a sense of belonging.
