## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 32: Understanding people with disabilities

Zimbabwe, *PlusOne Social Science Grade 4 Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 100), teaches: Disability is a physical or mental condition affecting daily activities — physical (paralysis, loss of limbs), visual (blindness, low vision) and hearing impairments; people with disabilities are accommodated through inclusive infrastructure (ramps, elevators, accessible restrooms, braille signs) and inclusive education with peer support and a sense of belonging. ## Sub lesson 2 — Zimbabwe's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 6) (teacher guide — evidence): child rights are sourced, not just listed — the Constitution of Zimbabwe Section 81, the African Charter on the Rights and Welfare of the Child and the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child; cultural beliefs are tested against those rights (FGM, child marriage and corporal punishment named as violations), and local welfare includes the traditional Zunde Ramambo / Isiphala Senkosi (the chief's granary) alongside councils, BEAM and church feeding schemes.
