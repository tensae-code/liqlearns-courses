# Hands-on — Norms the Rules That Hold Life Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 2 A. Macam-Macam Norma dalam Kehidupanku, Bab 2 B. Penerapan Norma dalam Kehidupanku, Bab 2 C. Mempraktikkan Norma di Lingkunganku, Bab 3 A. Mengenal Norma, Hak, dan Kewajiban dalam Kehidupan Berbangsa dan Bernegara, Panduan Khusus Bab 2: Norma dalam Kehidupanku, Panduan Khusus Bab 3: Mengenal Norma, Hak, dan Kewajiban dalam Kehidupan Berbangsa dan Bernegara, Bab 3 B, comic Nilai-Nilai Ajaib yang Tersembunyi di Rumah Kita, Bab III: Peduli dan Berbagi, Tujuan Pembelajaran 3.5, Bab 2 C. Aktivitas Manusia Menyesuaikan dengan Nilai dan Norma Sosial, Panduan Khusus Bab 2: Ragam Aktivitas Manusia — teaches: the kinds of norms in my life — religious, decency, social and legal norms as branches of one norm tree; applying norms as the everyday form of rights and duties; practising norms in my own environment; norms, rights and obligations in the life of nation and state, including children's rights and obligations; essential material and guidance for the kinds of norms, applying them, and musyawarah; guidance for norms, rights and obligations in national and state life; norma are the good rules that make a harmonious environment, and nilai are the goodnesses inside us — tidying toys is a norm whose value is responsibility; watering plants shows the value of caring; guidance for the values contained in the family's varied activities and for applying values and norms in the home environment; people adjust their behaviour to social values and norms — hospital, school, bank, market and house-of-worship rules, with pupils tabulating local rules (RT, village, school, youth organisations) and their sanctions; guidance for human activities adjusting to social values and norms. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
