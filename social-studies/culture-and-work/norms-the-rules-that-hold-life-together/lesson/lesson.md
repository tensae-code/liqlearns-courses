## Sub lesson 2 — Indonesia's way — the books' own teaching way

Indonesia's own way: Grade 5 names the machinery of Indonesian civic life — norma (religious, decency, social and legal norms as one tree) and musyawarah, deciding by deliberation until consensus (mufakat), practised first in the family. the primary Pancasila course closes by widening every earlier theme to the nation — norms become norms of berbangsa dan bernegara (national and state life), and gotong royong is finally named as bela negara, defending the country by working together. the guide's Materi Esensial sections state the norms/musyawarah material in full adult form, including the norm tree, before it is simplified for pupils. the final primary guide states the subject's position (kedudukan) explicitly — Pendidikan Pancasila is the vehicle of the state ideology in school — and sequences outcomes to the NKRI chapters. IPAS hides its civics inside a story — a mother explains norma and nilai (norms and values) to her children over tidying toys, so abstract civic words arrive as named goodnesses a child already does. the guide states the chapter's learning outcome in civic terms — pupils must apply "nilai dan norma di lingkungan rumah" (values and norms in the home environment) while doing the family's varied activities. social integration is taught through marakka bola — the South Sulawesi gotong royong tradition of literally lifting and carrying a neighbour's house to its new site, without regard to difference. the guide builds each chapter around an Aktivitas Kolaborasi (collaboration activity), so the norms and institutions content is taught by group work, not lecture. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
