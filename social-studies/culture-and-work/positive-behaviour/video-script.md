# Video script — Positive Behaviour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan Grade 7 SS G7 T1, printed p. 124, teaches: Positive behaviour — helping the elderly and listening well to others.

Jordan Grade 8 SS G8 T2, printed p. 98, teaches: Behaving well in society — down to the rules of riding in a car and sharing public ways.

Jordan's own way: citizenship is taught in three widening rings — Jordanian citizenship, Jordanian national identity, then global citizenship — and rights are taught through Jordan's own legislation for women and children. the set closes on the young citizen — living together across difference without extremism, then youth themselves: their place in society, in public life and the national economy, the challenges they face, and the behaviour expected of them.
