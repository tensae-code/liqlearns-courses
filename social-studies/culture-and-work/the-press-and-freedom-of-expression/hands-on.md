# Hands-on — The Press and Freedom of Expression

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Press (الصحافة)
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 8 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 2AC, printed p. 188, teaches: the press, its kinds and its role, read through real newspaper pages. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the second collège year turns citizenship into the machinery of the state itself — the constitution, the King, parliament, government and courts, then the citizen's constitutional rights, parties, unions, press, local democracy and associations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
