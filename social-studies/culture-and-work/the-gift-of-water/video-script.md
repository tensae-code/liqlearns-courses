# Video script — The Gift of Water

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 6 Term 1 (printed pp. 109–110), teaches: Treasuring water and the merit of giving it freely, through Sabil bin Duaij and the Kuwaiti benefactors who spent their own money to bring fresh drinking water to a town whose wells and water ships could not meet the need.

Kuwait's own way: every term closes with a "Stations with Good Kuwait" values unit — each value taught through a named Kuwaiti story or person (a Failaka neighbourhood, the poet Mudhi al-Ubaidi, the water benefactor Sabil bin Duaij), then practice activities asking how the value changes the pupil's own daily behaviour.
