# Hands-on — The Gift of Water

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Treasuring water and the merit of giving it freely, through Sabil bin Duaij and the Kuwaiti benefactors who spent their own money to bring fresh drinking water to a town whose wells and water ships could not meet the need. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
