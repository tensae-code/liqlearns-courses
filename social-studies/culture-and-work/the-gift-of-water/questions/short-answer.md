# The Gift of Water — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The blessing and value of giving water — treasuring water and the merit of giving it freely, through Sabil bin Duaij and the Kuwaiti benefactors who spent their own money to bring fresh drinking water to a town whose wells and water ships could not meet the need (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way — every term closes with a "Stations with Good Kuwait" values unit — each value taught through a named Kuwaiti story or person (a Failaka neighbourhood, the poet Mudhi al-Ubaidi, the water benefactor Sabil bin Duaij), then practice activities asking how the value changes the pupil's own daily behaviour (any close wording).**
