## Sub lesson 1 — Social solidarity

Kuwait Grade 6 Term 1 (printed pp. 102–104), teaches: The value of social solidarity through the old Failaka neighbourhood story — when the fisherman Bu Khalid fell ill and lost his only income, neighbours visited him and quietly left a rupee or two under his pillow so he was helped without embarrassment, until he recovered and returned to the sea. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way

Kuwait's own way: every term closes with a "Stations with Good Kuwait" values unit — each value taught through a named Kuwaiti story or person (a Failaka neighbourhood, the poet Mudhi al-Ubaidi, the water benefactor Sabil bin Duaij), then practice activities asking how the value changes the pupil's own daily behaviour. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
