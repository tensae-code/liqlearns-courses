# Hands-on — Social Solidarity Helping Each Other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The value of social solidarity through the old Failaka neighbourhood story — when the fisherman Bu Khalid fell ill and lost his only income, neighbours visited him and quietly left a rupee or two under his pillow so he was helped without embarrassment, until he recovered and returned to the sea. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
