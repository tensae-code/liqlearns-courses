# Video script — Common Threads That Shape Us as a Caribbean People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Trinidad and Tobago Standard 3 Jeanville Social Studies Std 3 (printed p. 78), teaches: Shared history (slavery and indentureship, plantation agriculture, Crown Colony rule), shared sports and music, and Caribbean disaster management — neighbours helping each other after natural disasters.

Trinidad and Tobago's own way: one "My Country" theme for the whole year — the multicultural society is taught as "Different But The Same": each ancestral people's contributions first, then the FUSION of food, music and dance into forms unique to Trinidad and Tobago, then the emblems, pledge and festival calendar that unite the same peoples.
