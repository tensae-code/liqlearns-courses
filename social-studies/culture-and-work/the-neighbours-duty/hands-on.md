# Hands-on — The Neighbours Duty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The neighbour's duty in the pearl-diving season — Khalid sails with his three sons leaving his family without provision, trusting God then his neighbour Hamad, known for generosity and chivalry; Hamad notices the absence, asks after them, and provides for the family until they return. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
