# The Neighbours Duty

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the neighbour's duty in the pearl-diving season — Khalid sails with his three sons leaving his family without provision, trusting God then his neighbour Hamad, known for generosity and chivalry; Hamad notices the absence, asks after them, and provides for the family until they return — as Kuwait's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The neighbour to the neighbour

Kuwait Grade 7 Term 2 (printed pp. 94–96), teaches: The neighbour's duty in the pearl-diving season — Khalid sails with his three sons leaving his family without provision, trusting God then his neighbour Hamad, known for generosity and chivalry; Hamad notices the absence, asks after them, and provides for the family until they return. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way

Kuwait's own way: Grade 7 Term 2 teaches the pupil to read their region's troubles whole — political (occupation, separatism, nuclear risk), social (addiction, rumours, bullying) — and then to answer them with plans: a national vision with traits and shared Arab goals, Kuwait's own 2035 vision, and Arab integration through named organisations; the values unit grounds it at home — the grandmother who runs the family's learning, and the neighbour who feeds the absent diver's family. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
