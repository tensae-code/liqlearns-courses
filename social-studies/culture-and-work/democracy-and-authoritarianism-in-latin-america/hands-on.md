# Hands-on — Democracy and Authoritarianism in Latin America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 4 "La democracia en países latinoamericanos"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 6 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 174–175, teaches: Latin America was long governed by authoritarian rulers (Porfirio Díaz in Mexico, Manuel Estrada Cabrera's 22 years in Guatemala, Maximiliano Hernández Martínez in El Salvador) with only short democratic experiences; through democratic exercises and electoral processes, representative systems now aspire to participative democracy, which needs free elections, freedom of thought and a culture of peace — and in 1993 the Constitutional Court removed President Serrano Elías after he suppressed the Constitution. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 8) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the sixth-grade book widens the lens — democracy is traced from Athens to Guatemala's own authoritarian past, and citizenship ends at the world: Guatemala's Belize claim put to its own citizens in the 2018 popular consultation on the International Court of Justice.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
