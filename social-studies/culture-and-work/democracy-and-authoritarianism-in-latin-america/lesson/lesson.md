# Democracy and Authoritarianism in Latin America

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Latin America was long governed by authoritarian rulers (Porfirio Díaz in Mexico, Manuel Estrada Cabrera's 22 years in Guatemala, Maximiliano Hernández Martínez in El Salvador) with only short democratic experiences; through democratic exercises and electoral processes, representative systems now aspire to participative democracy, which needs free elections, freedom of thought and a culture of peace — and in 1993 the Constitutional Court removed President Serrano Elías after he suppressed the Constitution — as Guatemala's books teach it.
