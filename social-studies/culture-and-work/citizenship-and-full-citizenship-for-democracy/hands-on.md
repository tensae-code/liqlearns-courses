# Hands-on — Citizenship and Full Citizenship for Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 2 "Ciudadanía" and "Ciudadanía plena para la democracia"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 94–95, teaches: citizenship is the quality and right of the citizen granted at majority, bringing rights (labour, civic, quality-of-life, universal suffrage, representation, organisation) and obligations together; full citizenship means committing to country and community, respecting the rights granted and fulfilling the obligations — that is living in democracy. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
