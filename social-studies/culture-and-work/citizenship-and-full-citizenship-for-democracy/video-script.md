The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 94–95, teaches: citizenship is the quality and right of the citizen granted at majority, bringing rights (labour, civic, quality-of-life, universal suffrage, representation, organisation) and obligations together; full citizenship means committing to country and community, respecting the rights granted and fulfilling the obligations — that is living in democracy.

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.
