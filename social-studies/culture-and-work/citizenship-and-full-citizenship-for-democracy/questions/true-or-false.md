# Citizenship and Full Citizenship for Democracy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part teaches: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 94–95, teaches: citizenship is the quality and right of the citizen granted at majority, bringing rights (labour, civic, quality-of-life, universal suffrage, representation, organisation) and obligations together; full citizenship means committing to country and community, respecting the rights granted and fulfilling the obligations — that is living in democracy.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part is Guatemala's own way of teaching this thing, as named in lesson/sources.md.
**Answer: True**
