# Citizenship and Full Citizenship for Democracy

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: citizenship is the quality and right of the citizen granted at majority, bringing rights (labour, civic, quality-of-life, universal suffrage, representation, organisation) and obligations together; full citizenship means committing to country and community, respecting the rights granted and fulfilling the obligations — that is living in democracy — as Guatemala's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2 "Ciudadanía" and "Ciudadanía plena para la democracia"

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 94–95, teaches: citizenship is the quality and right of the citizen granted at majority, bringing rights (labour, civic, quality-of-life, universal suffrage, representation, organisation) and obligations together; full citizenship means committing to country and community, respecting the rights granted and fulfilling the obligations — that is living in democracy. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
