The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Fiji Grade 10 Year 10 Social Science (printed p. 11), teaches: Parliamentary procedure and its five principles (order, justice, equality, minority heard, majority rules), then Fiji's own Parliament — President as head of state, Prime Minister and cabinet holding executive power, House of Representatives and the Senate as house of review.

Fiji's own way (Grade 10): government is taught from Fiji outward — Fiji's own traditional chiefly government and western-style government first, then Fiji's Parliament as the worked example of parliamentary rule, then the United Nations.
