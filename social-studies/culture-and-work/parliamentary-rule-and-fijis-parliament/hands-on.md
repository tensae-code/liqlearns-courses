# Hands-on — Parliamentary Rule and Fijis Parliament

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Parliamentary rule and Fiji's Parliament
Work through this part with your hands: Parliamentary procedure and its five principles (order, justice, equality, minority heard, majority rules), then Fiji's own Parliament — President as head of state, Prime Minister and cabinet holding executive power, House of Representatives and the Senate as house of review. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
