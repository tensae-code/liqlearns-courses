# Culture — symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A symbol is —
a) A tool for digging  b) A known thing, sound or image that stands for something else  c) A kind of tax  d) A written law
**Answer: b) A known thing, sound or image that stands for something else**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's example, ululation signals —
a) Grief  b) Trouble  c) Joy  d) Danger
**Answer: c) Joy**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Wailing cries signal —
a) Joy  b) A market opening  c) Grief or trouble  d) Rain
**Answer: c) Grief or trouble**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Symbols change —
a) Never  b) Only in cities  c) Over time  d) Only when governments order it
**Answer: c) Over time**
