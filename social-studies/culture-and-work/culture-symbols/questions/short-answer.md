# Culture — symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two sounds the book gives as symbols, with their meanings.
**Answer:** Ululation — joy; wailing cries — grief or trouble.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three holders of symbols the book names.
**Answer:** Any three of: priests, Christianity, Islam, government, nation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things are true of symbols across cultures and time?
**Answer:** They differ from culture to culture, and they change over time.
