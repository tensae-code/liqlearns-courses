# Hands-on — Culture — symbols

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Walk your street or market and list five symbols you meet — a flag, a dress pattern, a sign, a sound. Write what each stands for.

## Activity 2
Ask an elder about one symbol that has changed in their lifetime. Write what it meant then and what it means now.

## Activity 3
Draw one symbol of your school or your nation and write its meaning underneath in one sentence.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say what a symbol is. Give the book's two sound examples with their meanings. Say how symbols relate to culture and time: they differ between cultures and change over time.
