# Hands-on — The Articles of Confederation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Articles of Confederation
Work through this part with your hands: America's first frame — a confederation whose national government could declare war, coin money and conduct foreign affairs but could not tax, regulate commerce or raise an army. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
