# Video script — The Articles of Confederation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

United States Grade 13 American Government 3e (printed p. 37), teaches: America's first frame — a confederation whose national government could declare war, coin money and conduct foreign affairs but could not tax, regulate commerce or raise an army.

---

United States' own way (Grade 13): the United States teaches its own government as one complete system in one book — the Constitution is not a topic but the frame: every institution (Congress, presidency, courts, states, counties, cities, the civil service) is derived from it in turn, and rights are taught as the courts have interpreted them, case by case.
