Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 5 Social Studies & Creative Arts G5): production is mapped before it is argued — a district table and symbol map of who produces what where, a national table sorting goods by agriculture, cottage industry and large-scale industry, and only then the industrialist's case for using national products: industries grow, people get jobs, the country's money stays in the country.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 5 Teacher's Guide G5 (2081)): the guide frames the whole unit around the child's own community first — respect the labour around you, name the economic activities of your own locality, list your local goods and the market that sells them — and only then the nation's main activities and the case for using domestic goods. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 9 Social Studies): Grade 9 economics is the production unit taught through people — an agriculture expert's address and a returned migrant's successful vegetable farm for farming, the industry law's own capital table for industry, and a mushroom farmer who borrowed from his cooperative for labour and entrepreneurship.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 6 np-g6): Grade 6 has a full "Hamro Prithvi" (Our Earth) unit — Earth's introduction, latitude/longitude, outer-layer structure, then Nepal itself: geographical description, climate, rivers/glaciers/lakes, natural vegetation, natural resources and map study, closing with disaster management; a population unit follows (demography, the census). Economic activities are organised by region and resource: agriculture and industry, services and trade, tourism, and economic resources (land and water; forest, minerals and people).

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 np-g7): Grade 7 Earth science is motion-first — Earth's daily and annual motion, surface form and the factors that change it, surface form's relation to human life — then Nepal's climate, the seven provinces of federal Nepal, climate change, disasters and map work; population is taught as structure, change and management.

Nepal's own way (Nepal Grade 7 TG np-g7tg) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide teaches the same Grade 7 course as np-g7 with globe-and-ball demonstrations of rotation/revolution, chart work on Nepal's demographic structure (age, sex, religion, caste, language distributions), and discussion-led lessons; its geography content mirrors the student book lesson for lesson, so placements repeat with guide page references. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; Nepal is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.
