# Hands-on — Rules in the Community Around Me

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the neighbourhood has rules like home and school — no ball games or loud noise while neighbours rest, radio and TV kept low, and care not to dirty the building, street and shared places. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
