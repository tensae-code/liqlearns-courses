# Hands-on — Human Dignity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every person — woman or man, old or young, poor or rich, disabled or healthy — is owed respect and may not be shamed in word or deed; the constitution itself declares liberty and human dignity inviolable and obliges the state to protect them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Afghanistan's way
Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
