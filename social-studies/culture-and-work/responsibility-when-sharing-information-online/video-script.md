# Video script — Responsibility When Sharing Information Online

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education in the Digital Age, G11 Term 3 activity book, printed p. 19, teaches: the duties that come with digital facilities (no misuse of others' content, no vandalising facilities, no hacking) and the sharer's responsibilities — never spread false or misleading news, never judge people from their posts, and treat members of other ethnic and religious groups gently, as national belonging requires

United Arab Emirates' own way: digital citizenship is taught as rights-and-duties law, not etiquette tips — every ethical rule is tied to the UAE's own cybercrime law articles and penalties, and equal access is proven from the state's own projects (Smart Dubai, Abu Dhabi's e-government) and its four-category support for People of Determination online.
