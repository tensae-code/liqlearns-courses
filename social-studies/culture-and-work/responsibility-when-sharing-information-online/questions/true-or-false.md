# Responsibility When Sharing Information Online — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the duties that come with digital facilities (no misuse of others' content, no vandalising facilities, no hacking) and the sharer's responsibilities — never spread false or misleading news, never judge people from their posts, and treat members of other ethnic and religious groups gently, as national belonging requires
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: digital citizenship is taught as rights-and-duties law, not etiquette tips — every ethical rule is tied to the UAE's own cybercrime law articles and penalties, and equal access is proven from the state's own projects (Smart Dubai, Abu Dhabi's e-government) and its four-category support for People of Determination online.
**Answer: True**
