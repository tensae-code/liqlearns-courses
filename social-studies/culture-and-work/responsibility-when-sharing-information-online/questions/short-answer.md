# Responsibility When Sharing Information Online — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The responsibilities to grasp when sharing information and knowledge — the duties that come with digital facilities (no misuse of others' content, no vandalising facilities, no hacking) and the sharer's responsibilities — never spread false or misleading news, never judge people from their posts, and treat members of other ethnic and religious groups gently, as national belonging requires (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — digital citizenship is taught as rights-and-duties law, not etiquette tips — every ethical rule is tied to the UAE's own cybercrime law articles and penalties, and equal access is proven from the state's own projects (Smart Dubai, Abu Dhabi's e-government) and its four-category support for People of Determination online. (any close wording).**
