## Sub lesson 1 — Generosity Without Waste

Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies (printed p. 108), teaches: Generosity is giving from one's own property to others without return, and the Iraqis boast of it, especially hospitality; and its benefits are increasing affection and love between individuals, teaching patience, increasing people's respect, and entrenching tranquillity; but generosity must be distinguished from waste, which squanders time, effort and money. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: the components are named one by one with where each lives and what each built, equality is stated in the concept itself — all equal in rights and duties — and the values are taught as a civilisational inheritance, from keeping covenants "for thousands of years" to altruism as the road to social justice. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
