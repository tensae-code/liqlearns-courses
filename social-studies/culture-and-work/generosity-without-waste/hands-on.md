# Hands-on — Generosity Without Waste

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Generosity is giving from one's own property to others without return, and the Iraqis boast of it, especially hospitality; and its benefits are increasing affection and love between individuals, teaching patience, increasing people's respect, and entrenching tranquillity; but generosity must be distinguished from waste, which squanders time, effort and money. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iraq's way
Say how Iraq's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
