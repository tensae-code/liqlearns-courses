Turkey Grade 5 Sosyal Bilgiler 2. Kitap, printed p. 50, teaches: which door to knock on — the municipality, the district governor's office, the muhtar, CİMER (the Presidency's communication centre, ALO 150), consumer arbitration boards, and the civil-society organisations (STKs) that work in fields from education to disaster relief, which we can join and support

Turkey's own way: democracy is taught from the classroom outwards — the class first writes and votes its own rules item by item, then meets the same machinery at state scale (elections, petitions, CİMER), and an "active citizen" is defined by what she does about a dirty canteen, a faulty vacuum cleaner and a damaged historic fountain.
