# What Authority Owes Citizens and Citizens Owe the State — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Authority organises citizens' rights and duties and owes them internal and external security, laws applied, social care (health and education) and public services (water, electricity, roads, banks) — and the true citizen performs his duties as keenly as he claims his rights; food security as a test of the state's strength.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: What the state owes the citizen in return — protection of citizens through the laws the legislative authority issues and watches applied, and the spread of educational, health, social and economic services and institutions (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 6, Book 2).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: Civics is taught inside the Arab-world regional tour — the state lesson interrupts the geography of Africa to define the state from Syria's own case (sovereignty won 17 April 1946, land, nation, authority), and the family/institutions unit teaches society as a ladder of organised groups, from the family cell to the cultural institutions the Ministry of Culture runs..
**Answer: True**
