# Hands-on — What Authority Owes Citizens and Citizens Owe the State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: authority organises citizens' rights and duties and owes them internal and external security, laws applied, social care (health and education) and public services (water, electricity, roads, banks) — and the true citizen performs his duties as keenly as he claims his rights; food security as a test of the state's strength. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
