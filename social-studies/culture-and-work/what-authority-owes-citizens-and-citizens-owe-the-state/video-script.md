# Video script — What Authority Owes Citizens and Citizens Owe the State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Syria Grade 6 Social Studies (Syria Grade 6 Social Studies G6, printed p. 32), teaches: Authority organises citizens' rights and duties and owes them internal and external security, laws applied, social care (health and education) and public services (water, electricity, roads, banks) — and the true citizen performs his duties as keenly as he claims his rights; food security as a test of the state's strength

Syria Grade 4 Social Studies (Syria Grade 4 Social Studies G4, printed p. 104), teaches: What the state owes the citizen in return — protection of citizens through the laws the legislative authority issues and watches applied, and the spread of educational, health, social and economic services and institutions (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 6, Book 2)

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 6): civics is taught inside the Arab-world regional tour — the state lesson interrupts the geography of Africa to define the state from Syria's own case (sovereignty won 17 April 1946, land, nation, authority), and the family/institutions unit teaches society as a ladder of organised groups, from the family cell to the cultural institutions the Ministry of Culture runs.

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 4): Grade 4 drops the Me-and-You ring structure for a straight civics unit that defines citizenship in one sentence — love of the land, defending it, enjoying the rights, committing to the duties, respecting the state's systems and laws — then cashes each word out: rights are what the state hands the citizen free (school, clinic, clean streets), duties are what the citizen hands back, down to crossing at the pedestrian crossing and not throwing litter; the unit closes by honouring the martyrs.
