# Starting a Small Business Project

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a small business project — planning a small business of your own in your local area, as Mongolia's Grade 8 *Нийгэм судлал VIII (Social Studies VIII)* teaches it in Chapter III Individual and Economy: 3.3 A small business project on printed p. 55. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Planning a small business project

Mongolia's Grade 8 book closes its local-economy chapter with a small business project. After studying the local economy and the local market, the learner plans a small business of their own for that market — the chapter's knowledge is turned into a project with a product, customers and costs.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the book's own teaching way

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the Grade 8 year is the LOCAL year — every chapter is re-anchored to the child's own орон нутаг (local area/homeland): its environment and problems, its heritage, its economy and market, its governance. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
