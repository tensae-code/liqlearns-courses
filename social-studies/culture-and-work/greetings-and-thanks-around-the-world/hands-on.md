# Hands-on — Greetings and Thanks Around the World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every country has its own hello and thank-you — Russia, Germany, America and more, in their own words. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
