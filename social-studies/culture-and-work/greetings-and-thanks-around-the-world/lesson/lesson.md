# Greetings and Thanks Around the World

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: every country has its own hello and thank-you — Russia, Germany, America and more, in their own words — as Japan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — せかいの「こんにちは」「ありがとう」("Hello" and "thank you" around the world)

Japan Grades 1–2 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 64), teaches: Every country has its own hello and thank-you — Russia, Germany, America and more, in their own words. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Japan's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 1): MEXT's own moral reader for the youngest pupils is built from short stories and picture tales around four value topics per unit — orderly living, doing your own work, doing willingly what is right, and honesty — each story followed by talk-it-over pages for school, home and neighbourhood. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
