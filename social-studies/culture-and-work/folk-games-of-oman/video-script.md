# Video script — Folk Games of Oman

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 2 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 76–84, teaches: The neighbourhood's shared play — the folk games handed down in Oman's streets and courtyards — belonging practised as playing together.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 2, Term 1): Grade 2 turns Grade 1's circles into verbs — accept, communicate, cooperate, consult, belong — and plants two purely Omani institutions in the child's civic world: the sablah, the village meeting hall where the community gathers, and the folk games of the neighbourhood.
