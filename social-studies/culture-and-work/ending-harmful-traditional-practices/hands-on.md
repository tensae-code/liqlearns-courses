# Hands-on — Ending Harmful Traditional Practices

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ending Harmful Traditional Practices
Work through this part with your hands: some old customs were harmful — female genital mutilation during girls' initiation damages the reproductive organs, causes heavy bleeding that can kill during delivery, can spread HIV/AIDS when the same blade is used on more than one girl, and can lead to death from bleeding. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
