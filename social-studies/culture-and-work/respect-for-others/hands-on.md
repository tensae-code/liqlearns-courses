# Hands-on — Respect for Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Respect is a trait of the polite person, and respecting others is what makes a person remain in others' memory: Nour, a former pupil, knocks on the classroom door, greets her old teacher, apologises for interrupting the lesson, and explains why she came — and years later the teacher still remembers her by name, telling the class that they all share Nour's trait, polite pupils who respect others; I respect others and deal with them politely, and I apologise if I disturb them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
