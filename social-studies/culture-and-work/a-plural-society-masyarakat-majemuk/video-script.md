Indonesia's Grade 9 IPS-G9 student (printed p. 72); Grade 9 IPS-G9 student (printed p. 76); Grade 9 IPS-G9 teacher (printed p. 46), teaches: Bab 1 C.1 Pengertian Masyarakat Majemuk, Bab 1 C.2 Ciri-ciri Masyarakat Majemuk, Panduan Khusus Bab 1: Nenek Moyang Bangsa Indonesia dan Jalur Rempah Nusantara — teaches: a plural society (masyarakat majemuk) — groups of different ethnicity, culture, religion and race living side by side, whose diversity becomes strength under Bhinneka Tunggal Ika, with Indonesia's tolerance cited as a world example; the characteristics of a plural society; guidance for the plural-society and national-integration part of the chapter (student-book section C).

Indonesia's own way: national integration is taught as a contrast — the old integrations of Sriwijaya and Majapahit were made by conquest and stayed fragile (semu), while the modern one grew from shared fate under colonisation and is held by tolerance, with Indonesia cited by its own Foreign Ministry as a world reference for unity in diversity. the guide's graduate-profile mapping names Kewargaan (citizenship) as the dimension carried by the plural-society and social-change chapters — the civics strand is labelled as such for the teacher.
