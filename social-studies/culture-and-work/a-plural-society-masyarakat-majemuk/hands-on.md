# Hands-on — A Plural Society Masyarakat Majemuk

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 1 C.1 Pengertian Masyarakat Majemuk, Bab 1 C.2 Ciri-ciri Masyarakat Majemuk, Panduan Khusus Bab 1: Nenek Moyang Bangsa Indonesia dan Jalur Rempah Nusantara — teaches: a plural society (masyarakat majemuk) — groups of different ethnicity, culture, religion and race living side by side, whose diversity becomes strength under Bhinneka Tunggal Ika, with Indonesia's tolerance cited as a world example; the characteristics of a plural society; guidance for the plural-society and national-integration part of the chapter (student-book section C). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indonesia's way
Say how Indonesia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
