# Lesson plan — Basic Needs and Public Services

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Basic Needs and Public Services
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Venezuela, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Humanity's basic needs — five fundamental needs at every moment of life (to eat, dress, be housed, be cured, be entertained) plus water as a vital need and the services that supply them; and the citizen's side of the bargain: paying taxes is the duty that gives right to public services — a tax is the money contribution the nation and municipality require, income tax direct on salary or profits, VAT indirect on everything bought, vehicle and property taxes to the municipality — funding street cleaning, hospitals and schools; Satisfying basic needs is a human right — like all Earth's inhabitants, Venezuelans have indispensable needs from birth through their whole development, and meeting them is not a favour but a human right — as Venezuela's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
