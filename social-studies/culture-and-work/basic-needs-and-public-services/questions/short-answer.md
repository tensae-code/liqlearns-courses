# Basic Needs and Public Services — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Necesidades básicas de la humanidad — Humanity's basic needs — five fundamental needs at every moment of life (to eat, dress, be housed, be cured, be entertained) plus water as a vital need and the services that supply them; and the citizen's side of the bargain: paying taxes is the duty that gives right to public services — a tax is the money contribution the nation and municipality require, income tax direct on salary or profits, VAT indirect on everything bought, vehicle and property taxes to the municipality — funding street cleaning, hospitals and schools (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Satisfacer necesidades básicas es un derecho humano — Satisfying basic needs is a human right — like all Earth's inhabitants, Venezuelans have indispensable needs from birth through their whole development, and meeting them is not a favour but a human right (any close wording).**
