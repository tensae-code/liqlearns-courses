# Basic Needs and Public Services — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Humanity's basic needs — five fundamental needs at every moment of life (to eat, dress, be housed, be cured, be entertained) plus water as a vital need and the services that supply them; and the citizen's side of the bargain: paying taxes is the duty that gives right to public services — a tax is the money contribution the nation and municipality require, income tax direct on salary or profits, VAT indirect on everything bought, vehicle and property taxes to the municipality — funding street cleaning, hospitals and schools.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Satisfying basic needs is a human right — like all Earth's inhabitants, Venezuelans have indispensable needs from birth through their whole development, and meeting them is not a favour but a human right.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Venezuela's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
