# Tolerance Acceptance and the Anti Bullying Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 6 Nuestro universo valorativo — Accept one another regardless of gender, origin, race or skin colour; in an ever more diverse society tolerance is crucial for a systematic, integrating society, and behaviour norms should be built taking human rights as reference; Honduras's Anti-Bullying Law (Ley Contra el Acoso Escolar) defines school bullying (physical, verbal and other maltreatment) article by article — and whether the aggressor succeeds depends on bystanders: laughing, applauding or keeping silent enables it (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 8 is the moral-and-legal year — moral conscience and dilemmas, the Anti-Bullying Law read article by article, the rule of law traced to 1789, and human rights taught through the Americas' own instruments (the American Declaration, the OAS, the anti-torture and forced-disappearance conventions). (any close wording).**
