# Tolerance Acceptance and the Anti Bullying Law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: accept one another regardless of gender, origin, race or skin colour; in an ever more diverse society tolerance is crucial for a systematic, integrating society, and behaviour norms should be built taking human rights as reference; Honduras's Anti-Bullying Law (Ley Contra el Acoso Escolar) defines school bullying (physical, verbal and other maltreatment) article by article — and whether the aggressor succeeds depends on bystanders: laughing, applauding or keeping silent enables it.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
