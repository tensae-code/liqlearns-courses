# Video script — Tolerance Acceptance and the Anti Bullying Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras Grade 8 G8 Student CCSS, printed p. 38, teaches: Accept one another regardless of gender, origin, race or skin colour; in an ever more diverse society tolerance is crucial for a systematic, integrating society, and behaviour norms should be built taking human rights as reference; Honduras's Anti-Bullying Law (Ley Contra el Acoso Escolar) defines school bullying (physical, verbal and other maltreatment) article by article — and whether the aggressor succeeds depends on bystanders: laughing, applauding or keeping silent enables it.

Honduras's own way (Book 8): Grade 8 is the moral-and-legal year — moral conscience and dilemmas, the Anti-Bullying Law read article by article, the rule of law traced to 1789, and human rights taught through the Americas' own instruments (the American Declaration, the OAS, the anti-torture and forced-disappearance conventions).
