# Hands-on — Tolerance Acceptance and the Anti Bullying Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 6 Nuestro universo valorativo
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Accept one another regardless of gender, origin, race or skin colour; in an ever more diverse society tolerance is crucial for a systematic, integrating society, and behaviour norms should be built taking human rights as reference; Honduras's Anti-Bullying Law (Ley Contra el Acoso Escolar) defines school bullying (physical, verbal and other maltreatment) article by article — and whether the aggressor succeeds depends on bystanders: laughing, applauding or keeping silent enables it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
