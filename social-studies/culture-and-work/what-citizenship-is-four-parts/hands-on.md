# Hands-on — What Citizenship is Four Parts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## What Citizenship is Four Parts
Work through this part with your hands: Citizenship in Oman's curriculum has four components — identity, belonging, rights and duties, and community participation — taught in widening circles from the self to the family, the neighbourhood, the school, the homeland and the world. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
