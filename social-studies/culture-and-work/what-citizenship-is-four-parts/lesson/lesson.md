## Sub lesson 1 — What Citizenship is Four Parts

## Sub lesson 2 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: citizenship is its own school subject from age six, built as widening circles — the child first names herself, her feelings and her family, and only then her neighbourhood; the state prints its map and anthem at the front of the teacher's guide, and the fourth unit-component is always participation, not obedience. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
