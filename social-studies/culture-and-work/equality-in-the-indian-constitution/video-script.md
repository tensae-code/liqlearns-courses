India Grade 6 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Social and Political Life-I (printed p. 21), teaches: the Constitution promises equality to all, Dr Ambedkar and the freedom struggle fought discrimination, and government schemes try to make equality real.

India's own way: Civics opens not with institutions but with the child's own classroom diversity, then builds government in widening rings — nation, panchayat village, district offices, city corporation — always through named people and a story (a sarpanch, a patwari, a waste-picker).
