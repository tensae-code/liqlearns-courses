# Video script — Bullying

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 7 Term 2 (printed p. 62), teaches: Bullying defined — aggressive behaviour by a person or group against another, repeated, intending to harm them psychologically, physically or socially — named among Kuwait's own social challenges alongside air pollution.

Kuwait Grade 9 Term 2 (printed pp. 77–79), teaches: Bullying named again among the future's social challenges, at the world scale — the Grade 9 return to the Grade 7 definition.

Kuwait's own way: (Grade 7 Term 2) Grade 7 Term 2 teaches the pupil to read their region's troubles whole — political (occupation, separatism, nuclear risk), social (addiction, rumours, bullying) — and then to answer them with plans: a national vision with traits and shared Arab goals, Kuwait's own 2035 vision, and Arab integration through named organisations; the values unit grounds it at home — the grandmother who runs the family's learning, and the neighbour who feeds the absent diver's family. (Grade 9 Term 2) the last Kuwaiti schoolbook widens citizenship to the planet — a Kuwaiti pupil is taught global citizenship with Kuwaiti evidence (women's rights under Kuwait's own practice, Kuwait as the model of cultural cooperation, a WHO prize named for its late Amir), then the machinery of world order — international relations, world organisations, the UN — and finally the future's challenges, where identity and bullying sit beside poverty as things a citizen must face.
