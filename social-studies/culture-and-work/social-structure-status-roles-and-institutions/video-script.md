# Video script — Social Structure Status Roles and Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bangladesh Grade 6 BD G6 HSS (printed pp. 126–130), teaches: Society and social structure — the lasting pattern of relationships between people and institutions (family, school, Union Parishad, police, government) interacting; its two element kinds are social norms and social groups/institutions.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 6): An inquiry-based combined History and Social Science course taught through a class teacher character (Khushi Apa) and group investigations; civics begins not with definitions but with a football game played with and without rules, and sociology is built from a village flood story (the Dharmagola grain bank) before any term is named..
