# Video script — Democratic Rule Why It Matters

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Uganda Excel P5 SST (Term-Three printed p. 13 (PDF p. 177)), teaches: Democracy's meaning, advantages and disadvantages, its challenges (poverty, corruption, tribalism) and the citizen's roles — respecting others' rights, voting, accepting election results, watching how leaders use power.

Uganda Grade 6 P6 SST Home-Study (printed p. 52), teaches: Why leaders chosen by the people matter, and the peaceful ways leaders are chosen.

Uganda Grade 6 P6 SST Home-Study (printed p. 53), teaches: In a democracy every citizen is equal regardless of sex, religion, colour or tribe, equally protected by and equal before the law, with a chance to take part in decisions.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Excel P5 SST): P5 civics is the independent state itself — what a nation is, its symbols and how to honour them, democracy and elections in Uganda's own terms, then the government: central and local levels, the three arms, and the constitution the people made in 1995.
Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 6 P6 SST Home-Study): democracy is taught regionally — East Africans choosing their own leaders through their own parties and electoral commissions, with equality before the law as its stated fruit.
