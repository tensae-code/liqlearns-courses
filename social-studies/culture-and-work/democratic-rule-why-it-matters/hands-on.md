# Hands-on — Democratic Rule Why It Matters

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: democracy's meaning, advantages and disadvantages, its challenges (poverty, corruption, tribalism) and the citizen's roles — respecting others' rights, voting, accepting election results, watching how leaders use power. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Uganda's way
Say how Uganda's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
