# Respecting the Opposite Sex and Gender Equality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Respecting the Opposite Sex and Gender Equality — sex is the biological difference between male and female, while gender is the roles and expectations a society creates for men and women; gender equality means equal education for girls and boys, understanding that both are equal as human beings, and no gender discrimination in work at home or school; through puberty each sex must respect the other's dignity and boundaries — never laugh at a girl for menstruation, never use offensive language or touch another's private parts (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way — Tanzania's own way (Standard Five, Civic and Moral Education Standard Five Pupil's Book): Standard Five civics teaches the state from the ward up — Ward Development Committee, district council and its heads of departments, regional commissioner — and the nation through its symbols in detail (the emblem's thirteen signs, the shilling, the Uhuru torch first lit 9 December 1961, national festivals from Zanzibar Revolution Day to Farmers' Day); democracy is taught with political parties' duties and political tolerance, and peace as peaceful coexistence that condemns segregation on any ground. (any close wording).**
