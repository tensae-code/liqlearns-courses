# Hands-on — Respecting the Opposite Sex and Gender Equality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Respecting the Opposite Sex and Gender Equality
Work through this part with your hands: sex is the biological difference between male and female, while gender is the roles and expectations a society creates for men and women; gender equality means equal education for girls and boys, understanding that both are equal as human beings, and no gender discrimination in work at home or school; through puberty each sex must respect the other's dignity and boundaries — never laugh at a girl for menstruation, never use offensive language or touch another's private parts. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
