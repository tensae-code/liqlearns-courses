# Hands-on — The Knowledge of Herding

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 7 "Herding knowledge" (Мал маллах ухаан)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Herding is learned from childhood — from four or five, children turn the lambs and kids, from five or six gather dung and help father and mother, and even Chinggis's envoys remarked on Mongol children riding at five or six; the herding customs children learn: water the animals without crowding or frightening them at the well, and refill the trough with clean water — it is a kindness to every creature that drinks; care for the young animals, for the herd's increase rests on them; gather dung and horse droppings as fuel, drying the wet; and every animal is named by its years. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
