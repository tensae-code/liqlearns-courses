# The Knowledge of Herding — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: herding is learned from childhood — from four or five, children turn the lambs and kids, from five or six gather dung and help father and mother, and even Chinggis's envoys remarked on Mongol children riding at five or six; the herding customs children learn: water the animals without crowding or frightening them at the well, and refill the trough with clean water — it is a kindness to every creature that drinks; care for the young animals, for the herd's increase rests on them; gather dung and horse droppings as fuel, drying the wet; and every animal is named by its years.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
