# The Knowledge of Herding — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 7 "Herding knowledge" (Мал маллах ухаан) — Herding is learned from childhood — from four or five, children turn the lambs and kids, from five or six gather dung and help father and mother, and even Chinggis's envoys remarked on Mongol children riding at five or six; the herding customs children learn: water the animals without crowding or frightening them at the well, and refill the trough with clean water — it is a kindness to every creature that drinks; care for the young animals, for the herd's increase rests on them; gather dung and horse droppings as fuel, drying the wet; and every animal is named by its years (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for (any close wording).**
