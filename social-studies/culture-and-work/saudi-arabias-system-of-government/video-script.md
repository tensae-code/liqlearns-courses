The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 4 Social Studies (printed pp. 20–21), teaches: Saudi Arabia as an Arab Islamic state with full sovereignty — Islam its religion, the Quran and the Prophet's Sunnah its constitution, Arabic its language, Riyadh its capital; the Basic Law of Governance as the document that sets the state's principles and the rights and duties of citizens and state; a monarchy in the line of King Abdulaziz's descendants, bound by the bay'ah pledge of allegiance.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 4 book opens with citizenship — national identity, the system of government, national symbols and the founding story of security and unity under one state — and returns in Unit 6 to responsible citizenship through family and society, rights and duties, the good citizen, teamwork, and heritage stories that teach patience and hard work.
