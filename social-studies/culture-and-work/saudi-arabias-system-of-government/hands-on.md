# Hands-on — Saudi Arabias System of Government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Saudi Arabia as an Arab Islamic state with full sovereignty — Islam its religion, the Quran and the Prophet's Sunnah its constitution, Arabic its language, Riyadh its capital; the Basic Law of Governance as the document that sets the state's principles and the rights and duties of citizens and state; a monarchy in the line of King Abdulaziz's descendants, bound by the bay'ah pledge of allegiance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
