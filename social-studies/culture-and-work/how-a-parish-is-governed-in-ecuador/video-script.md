# Video script — How a Parish is Governed in Ecuador

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 3 Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016), printed p. 62, teaches: Urban parishes answer to the alcalde and concejales, while rural parishes since 2000 elect a junta parroquial by popular vote every four years — an autonomous sectional government led by its president, which approves the parish development plan and budget, guarantees citizen participation and watches over security and production; beside it the teniente político, appointed by the Ministry of Government, represents the President and acts as parish judge and police commissioner.

Ecuador's own way: the SM edition teaches local government from the child's own ground up — parish, then canton — with Ecuador's real institutions (the elected junta parroquial, the teniente político, the concejo municipal and the GADs), and teaches values through the Andean minga and the Kichwa reciprocity idea randi-randi, under the book's recurring Cultura del Buen Vivir spreads.
