**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ecuador).**

Ecuador's own way (Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016)): the SM edition teaches local government from the child's own ground up — parish, then canton — with Ecuador's real institutions (the elected junta parroquial, the teniente político, the concejo municipal and the GADs), and teaches values through the Andean minga and the Kichwa reciprocity idea randi-randi, under the book's recurring Cultura del Buen Vivir spreads.
