# How a Parish is Governed in Ecuador

## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — How a Parish is Governed in Ecuador

Ecuador Grade 3 Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016), printed p. 62, teaches: Urban parishes answer to the alcalde and concejales, while rural parishes since 2000 elect a junta parroquial by popular vote every four years — an autonomous sectional government led by its president, which approves the parish development plan and budget, guarantees citizen participation and watches over security and production; beside it the teniente político, appointed by the Ministry of Government, represents the President and acts as parish judge and police commissioner. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador's own way: the SM edition teaches local government from the child's own ground up — parish, then canton — with Ecuador's real institutions (the elected junta parroquial, the teniente político, the concejo municipal and the GADs), and teaches values through the Andean minga and the Kichwa reciprocity idea randi-randi, under the book's recurring Cultura del Buen Vivir spreads.
