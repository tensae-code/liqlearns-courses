# Hands-on — How a Parish is Governed in Ecuador

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Urban parishes answer to the alcalde and concejales, while rural parishes since 2000 elect a junta parroquial by popular vote every four years — an autonomous sectional government led by its president, which approves the parish development plan and budget, guarantees citizen participation and watches over security and production; beside it the teniente político, appointed by the Ministry of Government, represents the President and acts as parish judge and police commissioner. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
