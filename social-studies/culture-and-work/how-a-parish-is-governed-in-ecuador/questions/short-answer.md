# How a Parish is Governed in Ecuador — short answer

**Answer: How a Parish is Governed in Ecuador — Urban parishes answer to the alcalde and concejales, while rural parishes since 2000 elect a junta parroquial by popular vote every four years — an autonomous sectional government led by its president, which approves the parish development plan and budget, guarantees citizen participation and watches over security and production; beside it the teniente político, appointed by the Ministry of Government, represents the President and acts as parish judge and police commissioner (any close wording).**
