# How a Parish is Governed in Ecuador — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Urban parishes answer to the alcalde and concejales, while rural parishes since 2000 elect a junta parroquial by popular vote every four years — an autonomous sectional government led by its president, which approves the parish development plan and budget, guarantees citizen participation and watches over security and production; beside it the teniente político, appointed by the Ministry of Government, represents the President and acts as parish judge and police commissioner.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Ecuador's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
