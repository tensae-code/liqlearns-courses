# Hands-on — My Countrys Heritage Material and Moral

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My country's heritage
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the kinds of heritage in the United Arab Emirates, material and moral (المادي والمعنوي); the heritage sports of the Emirates; Emirati folk literature (الأدب الشعبي); the old crafts and handmade industries; the traditional dress men wear; and why heritage matters to the individual and to society, and keeping it alive Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
