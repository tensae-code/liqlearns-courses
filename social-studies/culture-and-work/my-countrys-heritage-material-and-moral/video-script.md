# Video script — My Countrys Heritage Material and Moral

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G6 teacher guide, volume 2, printed p. 60, teaches: the kinds of heritage in the United Arab Emirates, material and moral (المادي والمعنوي); the heritage sports of the Emirates; Emirati folk literature (الأدب الشعبي); the old crafts and handmade industries; the traditional dress men wear; and why heritage matters to the individual and to society, and keeping it alive

United Arab Emirates' own way: the term pairs the world outside (Asia's great states) with the heritage inside — the founder's civilising work, the country's material and moral inheritance, and the Emirati school itself studied as one of the nation's own institutions.
