# Hands-on — Government Offices and Treating Everyone Equally

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: four faults of government offices in small societies — favouritism for family and chiefs, favours returned for favours, secrets that leak, and public information kept hidden — against the official rule that everyone is treated equally. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Micronesia's way
Say how Micronesia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
