# Culture — religion and freedom of belief

## Do it first

Discuss with a partner: what does religion do in holding people together and helping one another in your community? Write two things.

**Religion is a part of culture**, and the book defines it through **freedom**: a person is **free to follow and preach the faith they choose**, and **no one may be forced into a religion or worship against their will**.

The book also sets pupils discussing religion's role in **holding people together and helping one another**.

Hold the two halves together: religion belongs inside culture — and inside a culture, belief is chosen, never forced.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Say that religion is a part of culture. State the freedom plainly: follow and preach the faith you choose; no one may be forced.

## Do It Yourself

1. Discuss with a partner: what does religion do in holding people together and helping one another in your community? Write two things.
2. Write the freedom rule in your own words, as one sentence you could say to a younger learner.
