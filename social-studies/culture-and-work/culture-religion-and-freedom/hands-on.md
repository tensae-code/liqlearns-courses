# Hands-on — Culture — religion and freedom of belief

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Discuss with a partner: what does religion do in holding people together and helping one another in your community? Write two things.

## Activity 2
Write the freedom rule in your own words, as one sentence you could say to a younger learner.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say that religion is a part of culture. State the freedom plainly: follow and preach the faith you choose; no one may be forced.
