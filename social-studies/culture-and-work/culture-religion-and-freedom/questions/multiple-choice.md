# Culture — religion and freedom of belief — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Religion is named in the book as —
a) Outside culture  b) A part of culture  c) A kind of trade  d) A government office
**Answer: b) A part of culture**

**2.** A person is free to —
a) Force others to worship  b) Follow and preach the faith they choose  c) Stop all worship  d) Tax believers
**Answer: b) Follow and preach the faith they choose**

**3.** May a person be forced into a religion against their will?
a) Yes, by elders  b) Yes, by government  c) No  d) Only children
**Answer: c) No**

**4.** Religion's role discussed in the book includes —
a) Holding people together and helping one another  b) Setting taxes  c) Building maps  d) Measuring land
**Answer: a) Holding people together and helping one another**
