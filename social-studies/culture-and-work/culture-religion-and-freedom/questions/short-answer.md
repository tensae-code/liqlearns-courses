# Culture — religion and freedom of belief — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** How does the book define religion's place?
**Answer:** Religion is a part of culture, defined through freedom of belief.

**2.** State the two halves of the freedom.
**Answer:** A person is free to follow and preach the faith they choose; no one may be forced into a religion or worship against their will.

**3.** What does the book ask pupils to discuss about religion?
**Answer:** Religion's role in holding people together and helping one another.
