# Lesson plan — Culture — religion and freedom of belief

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Culture — religion and freedom of belief
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 9 — Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder: culture, economic activities, trade, and tax).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** religion as a part of culture, defined through freedom — a person is free to follow the faith they choose, and no one may be forced
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title, at Grade 8 depth in the course's level ladder (levels, not grades).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Culture — what culture is (in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say that religion is a part of culture.
2. State the freedom plainly: follow and preach the faith you choose; no one may be forced.
