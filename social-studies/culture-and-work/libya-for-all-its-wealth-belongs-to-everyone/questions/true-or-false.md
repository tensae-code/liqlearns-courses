# Libya for All Its Wealth Belongs to Everyone — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: all Libyans have an equal right to benefit from their country's wealth, from whatever region they come; belonging to different regions of one homeland strengthens, not divides.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Libya's student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
