Turkey Grade 7 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 30, teaches: equality of opportunity means the same rights whatever one's birthplace, gender, family means or features — every child at school, girls and boys in the same conditions, and for people with special needs the ramps, lifts and special-education support that let them learn and join social life fully; the Constitution's Article 10 makes everyone equal before the law without discrimination, and such equality builds a society of justice and peace

Turkey's own way: the last middle-school year opens by widening the circle one final time — from how we talk to each other in a group, to the equality a constitution owes a child in a wheelchair, to the whole society closing ranks on a national issue, and finally to the country's own seat at the world's problem-solving table.
