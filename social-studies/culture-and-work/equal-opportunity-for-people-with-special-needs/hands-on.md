# Hands-on — Equal Opportunity for People with Special Needs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: equality of opportunity means the same rights whatever one's birthplace, gender, family means or features — every child at school, girls and boys in the same conditions, and for people with special needs the ramps, lifts and special-education support that let them learn and join social life fully; the Constitution's Article 10 makes everyone equal before the law without discrimination, and such equality builds a society of justice and peace Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
