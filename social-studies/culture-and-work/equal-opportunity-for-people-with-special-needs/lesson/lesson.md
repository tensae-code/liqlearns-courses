# Equal Opportunity for People with Special Needs

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: equality of opportunity means the same rights whatever one's birthplace, gender, family means or features — every child at school, girls and boys in the same conditions, and for people with special needs the ramps, lifts and special-education support that let them learn and join social life fully; the Constitution's Article 10 makes everyone equal before the law without discrimination, and such equality builds a society of justice and peace — as Turkey's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.
