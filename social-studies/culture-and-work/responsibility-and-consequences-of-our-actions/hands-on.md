# Hands-on — Responsibility and Consequences of Our Actions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 7 Las responsabilidades
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every action has results (studying→learning, caring for the environment→good); we are responsible for our acts because they benefit or harm others; good actions need will, thought and good results. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
