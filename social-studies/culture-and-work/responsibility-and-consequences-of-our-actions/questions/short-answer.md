# Responsibility and Consequences of Our Actions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 7 Las responsabilidades — Every action has results (studying→learning, caring for the environment→good); we are responsible for our acts because they benefit or harm others; good actions need will, thought and good results (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — civics begins inside the child's own circles — feelings, family norms and tasks, classroom rules and school authorities, a rehearsal student-government election with a paper ballot, and only then the community's elected authorities (alcaldesa, regidores, patronato, junta de agua). (any close wording).**
