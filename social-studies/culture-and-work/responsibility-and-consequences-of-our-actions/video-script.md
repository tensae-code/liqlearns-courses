# Video script — Responsibility and Consequences of Our Actions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras Grade 1 G1 Student CCSS, printed p. 34, teaches: Every action has results (studying→learning, caring for the environment→good); we are responsible for our acts because they benefit or harm others; good actions need will, thought and good results.

Honduras's own way (Book 1): civics begins inside the child's own circles — feelings, family norms and tasks, classroom rules and school authorities, a rehearsal student-government election with a paper ballot, and only then the community's elected authorities (alcaldesa, regidores, patronato, junta de agua).
