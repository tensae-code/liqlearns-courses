# Hands-on — Isfahan Half The World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 11 Isfahan, half the world (اصفهان، نصف جهان): touring Isfahan — you and your friends have bought the map of the previous page: find on it and name the historical and sight-seeing places, the mosques, the bazaar; the importance of the city of Isfahan in the different periods of history; the Sheikh Lotfollah mosque, built by Shah Abbas's order; and the Ali Qapu palace, built in six floors with about fifty rooms, where Shah Abbas ran the affairs of state. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
