# Hands-on — Elections and the Electoral Process

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How Samoa's general elections work on two levels — electing MPs (matai candidates, universal suffrage since 1991, parties and independents, bloc voting, election petitions to the Supreme Court) and parliament then electing the Speaker and Prime Minister — worked through the real 2 March 2001 election results. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Samoa's way
Say how Samoa's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
