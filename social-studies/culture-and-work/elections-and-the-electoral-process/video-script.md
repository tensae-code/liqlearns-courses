# Video script — Elections and the Electoral Process

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 10 Book 1 (printed p. 10), teaches: How Samoa's general elections work on two levels — electing MPs (matai candidates, universal suffrage since 1991, parties and independents, bloc voting, election petitions to the Supreme Court) and parliament then electing the Speaker and Prime Minister — worked through the real 2 March 2001 election results.

Samoa's own way: Government is taught as Samoa's own Westminster-style parliament first (matai seats, the Individual Voters Roll, its parties and its real 2001 election, problems included), then Japan's Peace-Constitution government as the comparison; rights are taught from the classroom's own rights list outward to the UN and Amnesty.
