## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 2: Unhu/Ubuntu/Vumunhu and village and community culture

Zimbabwe, *Ventures Primary Family and Heritage Studies Learner's Book Grade 2* (printed pp. 12–15), teaches: Unhu values (respect, honesty, humility, welcoming visitors) lived in community culture — comforting at funerals, celebrating together (the Ndebele first-fruits ceremony inxwala keeps people united), and elders teaching children good manners through kuraya/ukulaya counsel and folktales (ngano/inganekwane). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Zimbabwe's way (Book 12) — the book's own teaching way

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 12): the learner's-book voice of the same syllabus — the community is "like a big family" that meets at the dare, shares the tap, sports grounds and clinics, and feeds its elderly, orphans and poor from the chief's own field (zunde ramambo/isiphal senkosi); children's roles are taught as changed by equality — in the past cooking was girls' work and herding boys', now both do the same roles. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
