# Video script — We Are Archaeologists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 27, teaches: who an archaeologist (عالم آثار) is — one who studies the bones, tools and other remains of ancient people to discover how they lived; what an archaeological site and evidence (دليل) are; the care archaeologists must take so the pieces are not harmed when they are taken out of the ground; and learning about the past from what the dig uncovers, with Yasmin the archaeologist in the story "An unexpected visitor"

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
