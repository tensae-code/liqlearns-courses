# We Are Archaeologists

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: who an archaeologist (عالم آثار) is — one who studies the bones, tools and other remains of ancient people to discover how they lived; what an archaeological site and evidence (دليل) are; the care archaeologists must take so the pieces are not harmed when they are taken out of the ground; and learning about the past from what the dig uncovers, with Yasmin the archaeologist in the story "An unexpected visitor" — as United Arab Emirates's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — We are archaeologists

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 27, teaches: who an archaeologist (عالم آثار) is — one who studies the bones, tools and other remains of ancient people to discover how they lived; what an archaeological site and evidence (دليل) are; the care archaeologists must take so the pieces are not harmed when they are taken out of the ground; and learning about the past from what the dig uncovers, with Yasmin the archaeologist in the story "An unexpected visitor" This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
