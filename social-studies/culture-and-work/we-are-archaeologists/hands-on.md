# Hands-on — We Are Archaeologists

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## We are archaeologists
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: who an archaeologist (عالم آثار) is — one who studies the bones, tools and other remains of ancient people to discover how they lived; what an archaeological site and evidence (دليل) are; the care archaeologists must take so the pieces are not harmed when they are taken out of the ground; and learning about the past from what the dig uncovers, with Yasmin the archaeologist in the story "An unexpected visitor" Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
