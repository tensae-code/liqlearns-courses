# We Are Archaeologists — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: We are archaeologists — who an archaeologist (عالم آثار) is — one who studies the bones, tools and other remains of ancient people to discover how they lived; what an archaeological site and evidence (دليل) are; the care archaeologists must take so the pieces are not harmed when they are taken out of the ground; and learning about the past from what the dig uncovers, with Yasmin the archaeologist in the story "An unexpected visitor" (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece. (any close wording).**
