# We Are Archaeologists — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: who an archaeologist (عالم آثار) is — one who studies the bones, tools and other remains of ancient people to discover how they lived; what an archaeological site and evidence (دليل) are; the care archaeologists must take so the pieces are not harmed when they are taken out of the ground; and learning about the past from what the dig uncovers, with Yasmin the archaeologist in the story "An unexpected visitor"
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
**Answer: True**
