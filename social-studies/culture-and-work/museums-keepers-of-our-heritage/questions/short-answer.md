# Museums Keepers of Our Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Museums in the United Arab Emirates — a museum (متحف) as a building that houses and displays important objects such as antiquities; archaeology (علم الآثار) as the science that studies the past, above all bones and old tools; heritage (التراث) as a community's shared history including its customs and values, and culture (الثقافة) as a people's shared values and customs — with the Louvre, the British Museum and Sharjah's Museum of Islamic Civilization (over 5,000 artefacts) as the worked examples, and how museums and art exhibitions guard Emirati culture as it changes over time (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — the federation's diversity is taught as a museum subject — the child learns the words cultural diversity and inclusion by walking the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization, and the story lesson ends on the line that Emirati society today is a mixture of people from all over the world, bound to them and respecting their cultures. (any close wording).**
