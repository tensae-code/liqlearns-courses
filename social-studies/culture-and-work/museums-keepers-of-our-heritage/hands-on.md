# Hands-on — Museums Keepers of Our Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Museums in the United Arab Emirates
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a museum (متحف) as a building that houses and displays important objects such as antiquities; archaeology (علم الآثار) as the science that studies the past, above all bones and old tools; heritage (التراث) as a community's shared history including its customs and values, and culture (الثقافة) as a people's shared values and customs — with the Louvre, the British Museum and Sharjah's Museum of Islamic Civilization (over 5,000 artefacts) as the worked examples, and how museums and art exhibitions guard Emirati culture as it changes over time Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
