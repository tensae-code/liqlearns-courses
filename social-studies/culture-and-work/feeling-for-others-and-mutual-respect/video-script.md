# Video script — Feeling for Others and Mutual Respect

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Morocco Grade 5 Dalil Moufid Ijtiamiat (Teacher Guide), printed p. 173, teaches: empathy and respect for others as the basis of living together, with the guide's scientific summary on equality between men and women as one of the school's biggest stakes — naming the stereotypes that keep equality unapplied and learning to deal with others as one would be dealt with.

Morocco's own way: Moroccan primary Social Studies is one integrated subject (History + Geography + Civic Education) built around "my homeland"; its civics third is a full civic-education programme in its own right — civic behaviour defined as the citizen's relation to society and to the state through its institutions, civic values (freedom, dignity, solidarity, equality, tolerance, rejecting discrimination) learned by practice not memorisation, and the classroom itself run as a small democracy (a negotiated class charter and an elected class cooperative with ballot box and vote count).
