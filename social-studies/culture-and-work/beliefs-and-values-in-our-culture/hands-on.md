# Hands-on — Beliefs and Values in our Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Beliefs and values in the local culture
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The beliefs of the local culture — natural signs and what they warn of, Christian beliefs and practices beside traditional beliefs, traditional medicine, and the values and virtues promoted in family, school and church. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Papua New Guinea's way
Say how Papua New Guinea's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
