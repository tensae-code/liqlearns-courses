# Video script — Beliefs and Values in our Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Papua New Guinea Grade 3 Social Science TG (printed p. 52), teaches: The beliefs of the local culture — natural signs and what they warn of, Christian beliefs and practices beside traditional beliefs, traditional medicine, and the values and virtues promoted in family, school and church.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 3): the youngest primary guide starts civics at home scale — what a community is and how it changes, how people relate in families, school and church, and rules in different places including safety rules for travel by vehicle and canoe — beside a culture strand taught by doing: singing and dancing, fishing and hunting, ceremonies for birth and death, food taboos and sacred places.
