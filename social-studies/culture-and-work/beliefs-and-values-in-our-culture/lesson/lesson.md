## Sub lesson 2 — Papua New Guinea's way — the books' own teaching way

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 3): the youngest primary guide starts civics at home scale — what a community is and how it changes, how people relate in families, school and church, and rules in different places including safety rules for travel by vehicle and canoe — beside a culture strand taught by doing: singing and dancing, fishing and hunting, ceremonies for birth and death, food taboos and sacred places. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
