## Sub lesson 2 — Indonesia's way — the books' own teaching way

Indonesia's own way: Grade 5 names the machinery of Indonesian civic life — norma (religious, decency, social and legal norms as one tree) and musyawarah, deciding by deliberation until consensus (mufakat), practised first in the family. the guide's Materi Esensial sections state the norms/musyawarah material in full adult form, including the norm tree, before it is simplified for pupils. IPAS Grade 4 teaches community through kearifan lokal (local wisdom) — pupils interview classmates and teachers about habits handed down in their families (jamu drinking, speaking Batak at home) before any national culture is named. the guide's Bab VI essential material states roles, rules and responsibilities as one civic package across three environments — home first, then school, then masyarakat (society). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
