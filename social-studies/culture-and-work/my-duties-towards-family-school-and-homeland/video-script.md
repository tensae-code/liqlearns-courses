# Video script — My Duties Towards Family School and Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education G5 Term 2, printed p. 98, teaches: duties (الواجبات) as the moral commitments a person owes their society and homeland; duties sorted by direction — towards my family (kindness to parents, help and participation), towards my school (respecting its rules and cleanliness, caring for its furniture and tools) and towards my homeland (defending it, respecting its laws, protecting its property and representing it well — Article 43: defending the Union is a sacred duty of every citizen)

United Arab Emirates' own way: civics at Grade 5 is taught straight from the country's own constitution — Article 14 for equality, Article 43 for the duty of defence — with the state's own rankings, laws and named institutions (Family Development Foundation, Ministry of Community Development, the 2018 Emirati sign-language dictionary) as the evidence.
