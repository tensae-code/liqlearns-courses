# Hands-on — My Duties Towards Family School and Homeland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Duties
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: duties (الواجبات) as the moral commitments a person owes their society and homeland; duties sorted by direction — towards my family (kindness to parents, help and participation), towards my school (respecting its rules and cleanliness, caring for its furniture and tools) and towards my homeland (defending it, respecting its laws, protecting its property and representing it well — Article 43: defending the Union is a sacred duty of every citizen) Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
