# My Duties Towards Family School and Homeland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Duties — duties (الواجبات) as the moral commitments a person owes their society and homeland; duties sorted by direction — towards my family (kindness to parents, help and participation), towards my school (respecting its rules and cleanliness, caring for its furniture and tools) and towards my homeland (defending it, respecting its laws, protecting its property and representing it well — Article 43: defending the Union is a sacred duty of every citizen) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — civics at Grade 5 is taught straight from the country's own constitution — Article 14 for equality, Article 43 for the duty of defence — with the state's own rankings, laws and named institutions (Family Development Foundation, Ministry of Community Development, the 2018 Emirati sign-language dictionary) as the evidence. (any close wording).**
