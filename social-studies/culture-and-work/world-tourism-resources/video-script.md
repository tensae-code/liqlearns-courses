Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11* (printed pp. 27–33), teaches: Topic 11.2 (part): The world's tourism resources — natural ones (landforms from mountains and karst caves to coasts and islands, climate, ecosystems, water) and cultural ones (897 UNESCO cultural heritage listings in 2020; the Great Wall, the Eiffel Tower, Angkor Wat; festivals from Brazil's Carnival to America's balloon fiestas; great modern cities).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11): the Grade 11 electives take three deep dives that all circle home — sharing the Mekong and the East Sea with neighbours, the world's tourism and the jobs it offers Vietnamese school-leavers, and the Fourth Industrial Revolution remaking work.
