# Work and Hantiwadaag Socialism

## Sub lesson 1 — Hawl iyo Hantiwadaag (Work and Socialism)

Somalia Grade 1 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (Teacher Guide) (printed p. 17), teaches: work and shared ownership as the country's chosen way — people work together and hold the means of life in common, presented to first-graders as how a fair society is built. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Hantiwadaagga cilmiga ku dhisan (Scientific Socialism)

Somalia Grade 6 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 58), teaches: socialism restated for sixth-graders as a science — the doctrine of the state set out systematically. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 1): this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Somalia's own way (Grade 6): by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
