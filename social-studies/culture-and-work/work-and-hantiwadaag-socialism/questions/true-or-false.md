# Work and Hantiwadaag Socialism — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Hawl iyo Hantiwadaag (Work and Socialism)" teaches: work and shared ownership as the country's chosen way — people work together and hold the means of life in common, presented to first-graders as how a fair society is built.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Hantiwadaagga cilmiga ku dhisan (Scientific Socialism)" teaches: socialism restated for sixth-graders as a science — the doctrine of the state set out systematically.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in..
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
