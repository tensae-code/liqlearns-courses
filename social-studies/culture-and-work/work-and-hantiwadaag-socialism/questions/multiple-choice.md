# Work and Hantiwadaag Socialism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Hawl iyo Hantiwadaag (Work and Socialism)" is mainly about —
a) work and shared ownership as the country's chosen way — people work together and hold the means of life in common, presented to first-graders as how a fair society is built  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) work and shared ownership as the country's chosen way — people work together and hold the means of life in common, presented to first-graders as how a fair society is built**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The part of this lesson called "Hantiwadaagga cilmiga ku dhisan (Scientific Socialism)" is mainly about —
a) socialism restated for sixth-graders as a science — the doctrine of the state set out systematically  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) socialism restated for sixth-graders as a science — the doctrine of the state set out systematically**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The part of this lesson called "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" is mainly about —
a) this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in.  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in.**
