# Hands-on — Work and Hantiwadaag Socialism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hawl iyo Hantiwadaag (Work and Socialism)
Work through this part with your hands: work and shared ownership as the country's chosen way — people work together and hold the means of life in common, presented to first-graders as how a fair society is built. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Hantiwadaagga cilmiga ku dhisan (Scientific Socialism)
Work through this part with your hands: socialism restated for sixth-graders as a science — the doctrine of the state set out systematically. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
