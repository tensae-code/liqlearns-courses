## Sub lesson 1 — Special Days in Guyana: Republic Day

Guyana Grade 2 Social Studies For Our Children Book 2 (printed p. 31), teaches: Republic Day on 23 February remembers Cuffy, a national hero, and its celebrations are called Mashramani. ## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: Book 2 widens Book 1's world — the same Ron and Sue now meet family types, class rules, neighbourhood shops and, for the first time, the country's leaders (President, Prime Minister, Ministers) and its national days (Republic Day, May Day, Enmore Martyrs' Day, Freedom Day).
