# Hope Courage and Working Toward a Dream

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Naoko, who cannot keep up jogging with her father and brother, sets small daily goals and climbs step by step like a long staircase (reading) — as Japan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — 読みもの「きっとできる」("I can surely do it")

Japan Grades 3–4 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 26), teaches: Naoko, who cannot keep up jogging with her father and brother, sets small daily goals and climbs step by step like a long staircase (reading). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — (2) 希望と勇気をもって、くじけずに (With hope and courage, never giving up)

Japan Grades 5–6 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 18), teaches: Holding a dream and striving toward it, with people who did (astronaut Mukai Chiaki and others). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — 読みもの「ヘレンと共に ―アニー・サリバン」("With Helen — Annie Sullivan")

Japan Grades 5–6 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 22), teaches: Perseverance and teaching through the story of Helen Keller and her teacher (reading). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — 夢に向かって確かな一歩を (A sure step toward your dream)

Japan Grades 5–6 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 26), teaches: Steady daily steps toward a goal, through baseball player Ichiro's training. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 5 — Japan's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 2): the middle pupils' reader widens in the same four steps as the other two Doutoku books — raise yourself, relate to people, feel life, relate to everyone — and plants the first civics inside moral teaching: society's rules are introduced through a banned swing the class wins back by making rules, and fairness is taught as never shutting anyone out. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 6 — Japan's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 3): the older pupils' reader moves from self-discipline to society in four widening units — polish yourself, connect with people, cherish life, connect with everyone — and teaches civics inside moral stories: rights are introduced as the other face of duties, the Diet appears as the place where rules are made, and fairness is taught through a story of a Vietnamese classmate facing discrimination. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
