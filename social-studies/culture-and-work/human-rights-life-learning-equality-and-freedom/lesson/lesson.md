## Sub lesson 1 — Human Rights Life Learning Equality and Freedom

Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 9, teaches: from the Qur'an's guidance and the Universal Declaration — the right to life, the right to education, the right to a clean and green living environment, equality (no one is better for language, religion or region) and freedom and personal safety, each bounded by respect for others' freedom. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: society is taught as a ladder of belonging — family, school, village, district, city, country, and the wider Islamic umma — and the state appears as the servant of that society: ministries named one by one, each with its service, and peace named the first need of a country that has known decades of war. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
