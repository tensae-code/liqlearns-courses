# Hands-on — Human Rights Life Learning Equality and Freedom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: From the Qur'an's guidance and the Universal Declaration — the right to life, the right to education, the right to a clean and green living environment, equality (no one is better for language, religion or region) and freedom and personal safety, each bounded by respect for others' freedom. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Afghanistan's way
Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
