# Video script — Human Rights Life Learning Equality and Freedom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 9, teaches: from the Qur'an's guidance and the Universal Declaration — the right to life, the right to education, the right to a clean and green living environment, equality (no one is better for language, religion or region) and freedom and personal safety, each bounded by respect for others' freedom.

Afghanistan's own way: society is taught as a ladder of belonging — family, school, village, district, city, country, and the wider Islamic umma — and the state appears as the servant of that society: ministries named one by one, each with its service, and peace named the first need of a country that has known decades of war.
