# Video script — Insurance — sharing risk

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam's Grade 12 book teaches insurance as a machine for sharing risk: many people pay small premiums, one fund stands ready, and whoever the risk strikes is compensated from it.

Its types run from social, health and unemployment insurance to commercial, property, personal and civil-liability cover.

And the machine works twice — economically, mobilising capital and stabilising costs and the budget; socially, reducing losses, teaching saving and creating jobs.
