# Insurance — sharing risk — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The participant pays a premium and the organisation builds a reserve fund.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Social insurance in Vietnam is governed by the 2024 Social Insurance Law.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Insurance plays no role in forming a saving habit.
**Answer: False — socially, insurance forms a saving habit in society, a flexible form of saving part of income for a safer future.**
