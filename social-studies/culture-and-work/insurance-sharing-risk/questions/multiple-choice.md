# Insurance — sharing risk — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Insurance transfers risk from the participant to —
a) the insurance organisation  b) the participant's neighbour  c) nobody
**Answer: a) the insurance organisation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is a type of insurance in the lesson?
a) social insurance  b) weather insurance only  c) school insurance
**Answer: a) social insurance**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Insurers' largely idle premium funds can be —
a) invested back into the economy  b) destroyed  c) hidden from the budget
**Answer: a) invested back into the economy**
