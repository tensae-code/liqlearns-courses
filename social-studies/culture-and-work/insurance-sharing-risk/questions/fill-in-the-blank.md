# Insurance — sharing risk — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the word or words from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Insurance is the activity of transferring _____ between participants and insurance organisations.
**Answer: risk**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The participant pays a _____ so the organisation can build a reserve fund.
**Answer: premium**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** _____ insurance covers health, body and life.
**Answer: Personal**
