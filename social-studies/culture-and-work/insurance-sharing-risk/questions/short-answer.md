# Insurance — sharing risk — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does insurance work, in one chain?
**Answer:** The participant pays a premium; the organisation builds a reserve fund; when a risk or insured event occurs, the organisation uses the fund to compensate, subsidise or pay within the insurance scope.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three types of insurance from the lesson.
**Answer:** Any three of: social, health, unemployment, commercial, property, personal, civil-liability insurance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Give one economic and one social role of insurance.
**Answer:** Economic: any of risk transfer, mobilising capital for investment, stabilising production costs and the state budget, promoting integration. Social: any of reducing losses and keeping life safe, forming a saving habit, creating jobs.
