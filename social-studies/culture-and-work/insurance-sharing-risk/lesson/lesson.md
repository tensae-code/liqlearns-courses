# Insurance — sharing risk

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold the lesson's one thing in mind and say what you already think it means: Insurance is the activity of transferring risk between insurance participants and insurance organisations: the participant pays a premium so the organisation builds a reserve fund and commits to compensate, subsidise or pay when a risk or insured event occurs — in types from social, health and unemployment insurance to commercial, property, personal and civil-liability insurance — and it works economically (mobilising capital, stabilising costs and the budget) and socially (reducing losses, forming a saving habit, creating jobs). Hold your answer — the lesson takes it in parts.

## Sub lesson 1 — Transferring risk — premium, fund, compensation

**Insurance** is the activity of **transferring risk** between insurance participants and insurance organisations: the participant **pays a premium**, the organisation **builds a reserve fund**, and commits that when a **risk or insured event** occurs it will use the fund to **compensate, subsidise or pay** within the insurance scope.

## Sub lesson 2 — The types — social, health, unemployment, commercial

The types: **social insurance** (replacing or partly compensating a worker's income lost to sickness, maternity, work accident, occupational disease, retirement or death — compulsory or voluntary under the **2024 Social Insurance Law**), **health insurance**, **unemployment insurance**, and **commercial insurance** — **property insurance**, **personal insurance** (on health, body and life) and **civil-liability insurance**.

## Sub lesson 3 — The role — capital, stability, saving and jobs

Economically, insurance **transfers risk**, is a **channel for mobilising capital** for development investment (Vietnam's insurers reinvested **468.56 trillion đồng by 2020**), **stabilises production costs and the state budget** and promotes integration. Socially, it **reduces losses and keeps life safe**, **forms a saving habit**, and **creates jobs** — 71 insurance enterprises operated in Vietnam in 2020.
