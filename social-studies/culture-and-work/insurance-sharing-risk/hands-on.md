# Hands-on — Insurance — sharing risk

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do the lesson's own activity
List the risks your household faces this year (illness, accident, a broken phone, a lost job). Beside each, write which type of insurance from this lesson answers it — or 'none' where no type fits — and what premium-for-fund exchange that insurance would be. Finish with one line: which single risk would you insure first, and why?

## Draw it and check it
Draw the lesson's main sorting or chain from memory and label its parts with the lesson's key words. Then open lesson/lesson.md, compare, and fix every label you placed wrong.

## Teach it on the spot
Close the lesson. Teach the whole of sub lesson 1 aloud, as if to a younger learner, with no notes. Where you stop or guess, that is the part to re-read.
