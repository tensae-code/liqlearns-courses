# We Are Skilled Investigators Artefacts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: what an artefact (قطعة أثرية) is — a thing made by people, usually of historical or cultural value; heritage sorted into material (مادي) and non-material (معنوي) — a statue, an old pot and a pearl necklace against Eid celebrations and a trip to the museum; describing old things closely with the senses; and, in the story of Lubna and her grandmother's coffee pot, how the things in our own homes carry memories and tell us about the past
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
**Answer: True**
