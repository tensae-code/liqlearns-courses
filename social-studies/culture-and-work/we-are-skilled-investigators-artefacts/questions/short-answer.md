# We Are Skilled Investigators Artefacts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: We are skilled investigators — what an artefact (قطعة أثرية) is — a thing made by people, usually of historical or cultural value; heritage sorted into material (مادي) and non-material (معنوي) — a statue, an old pot and a pearl necklace against Eid celebrations and a trip to the museum; describing old things closely with the senses; and, in the story of Lubna and her grandmother's coffee pot, how the things in our own homes carry memories and tell us about the past (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece. (any close wording).**
