# We Are Skilled Investigators Artefacts

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what an artefact (قطعة أثرية) is — a thing made by people, usually of historical or cultural value; heritage sorted into material (مادي) and non-material (معنوي) — a statue, an old pot and a pearl necklace against Eid celebrations and a trip to the museum; describing old things closely with the senses; and, in the story of Lubna and her grandmother's coffee pot, how the things in our own homes carry memories and tell us about the past — as United Arab Emirates's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — We are skilled investigators

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
