# Video script — We Are Skilled Investigators Artefacts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 3, teaches: what an artefact (قطعة أثرية) is — a thing made by people, usually of historical or cultural value; heritage sorted into material (مادي) and non-material (معنوي) — a statue, an old pot and a pearl necklace against Eid celebrations and a trip to the museum; describing old things closely with the senses; and, in the story of Lubna and her grandmother's coffee pot, how the things in our own homes carry memories and tell us about the past

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
