# Hands-on — We Are Skilled Investigators Artefacts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## We are skilled investigators
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what an artefact (قطعة أثرية) is — a thing made by people, usually of historical or cultural value; heritage sorted into material (مادي) and non-material (معنوي) — a statue, an old pot and a pearl necklace against Eid celebrations and a trip to the museum; describing old things closely with the senses; and, in the story of Lubna and her grandmother's coffee pot, how the things in our own homes carry memories and tell us about the past Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
