# Looking at Myself and Living My Strengths — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: 自分を見つめ、自分を生かそう (Look at myself, and live my own way) — Japan Grades 3–4 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 20), teaches: A poem of the road running on before me — straight, winding, uphill — and looking at yourself to live your own life well. (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Japan's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way — Japan's own way (Book 2): the middle pupils' reader widens in the same four steps as the other two Doutoku books — raise yourself, relate to people, feel life, relate to everyone — and plants the first civics inside moral teaching: society's rules are introduced through a banned swing the class wins back by making rules, and fairness is taught as never shutting anyone out.
