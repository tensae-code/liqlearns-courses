# Hands-on — The Principles of Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.2 "Do we know the principles of human rights?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: After the Second World War the world's peoples built the UN (1945) and adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) — Eleanor Roosevelt holding the poster; the two 1966 Covenants carry it into law, and Mongolia is party to some forty rights treaties; the eight principles — respect for human dignity; non-discrimination; inalienability (rights cannot be taken away); universality; indivisibility; interdependence; equality; and accountability — each with its emblem, and the Declaration's articles set side by side with Chapter Two of Mongolia's own Constitution. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
