# The Principles of Human Rights — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: after the Second World War the world's peoples built the UN (1945) and adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) — Eleanor Roosevelt holding the poster; the two 1966 Covenants carry it into law, and Mongolia is party to some forty rights treaties; the eight principles — respect for human dignity; non-discrimination; inalienability (rights cannot be taken away); universality; indivisibility; interdependence; equality; and accountability — each with its emblem, and the Declaration's articles set side by side with Chapter Two of Mongolia's own Constitution.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
