## Sub lesson 1 — The Overall Goal and Principles of Comprehensively Advancing Rule of Law

China Senior 思想政治必修3 (printed p. 80), teaches: The overall goal — building a socialist rule-of-law system with Chinese characteristics and a socialist rule-of-law country — and its principles: the Party's leadership, the people's principal position, equality before the law, joining rule of law with rule of virtue, and proceeding from China's own conditions, under Xi Jinping Thought on Rule of Law. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — China's way — the book's own teaching way

China's own way: the civics core of the senior Politics course, built as three blocks in one chain — who leads (the Party: chosen by history and the people, advanced, ruling through political, ideological and organisational leadership), who owns the state (the people, through the people's democratic dictatorship, the people's congress as fundamental system, and the three basic systems of multiparty cooperation, ethnic regional autonomy and grassroots self-governance), and how the state is run (comprehensively by law: a rule-of-law country, government and society built together through scientific legislation, strict enforcement, impartial justice and universal observance) — every lesson opening with 探究与分享 cases and closing in a unit 综合探究 project. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
