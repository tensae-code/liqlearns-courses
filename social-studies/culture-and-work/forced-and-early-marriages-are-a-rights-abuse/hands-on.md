# Hands-on — Forced and Early Marriages Are a Rights Abuse

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activities 5–6 Forced marriages and human rights abuse
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: early and forced marriages are widespread in South Sudan (nearly half of women aged 15–19 married, some as young as 12) and are a serious violation of human rights leading to poor maternal health, violence against women, poverty and pain — told through Nyanyot, 13, kept from school for bride price and beaten by her husband, with the class asked what can rescue her. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Sudan's way
Say how South Sudan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
