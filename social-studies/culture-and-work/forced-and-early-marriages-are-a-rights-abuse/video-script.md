# Video script — Forced and Early Marriages Are a Rights Abuse

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 6 P6 Social Studies SB, printed p. 56, teaches: early and forced marriages are widespread in South Sudan (nearly half of women aged 15–19 married, some as young as 12) and are a serious violation of human rights leading to poor maternal health, violence against women, poverty and pain — told through Nyanyot, 13, kept from school for bride price and beaten by her husband, with the class asked what can rescue her.

South Sudan's own way: The country's own conflict is the textbook — secession is explained by marginalisation and exploitation of the South's resources, and the constitution enters as homework: explain why South Sudan's constitution is important and name five rights it guarantees.
