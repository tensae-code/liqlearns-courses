Bhutan's own way: the workbook turns each value into a week of doing — a sorry-card actually drawn and given, a day planned to the minute, kind acts collected as leaves on a classroom Kindness Tree until they earn a fruit, and obedience sealed with written pledges to teacher and parents; the values are Bhutan's own (Tha-Dam-Tse Ley-Judy, Tsawa Sum) carried in stories the whole world tells (the Giving Tree, Little Red Riding Hood, the Itsy Bitsy Spider).

Bhutan's own way (teacher guide — evidence): values are not imported abstractions here — the whole course hangs on Gross National Happiness and the Buddhist principle of Tha-Dam-Tse Ley-Judy, loyalty is taught as Tha Damtshig (mutual trust) and love of Tsawa Sum (King, country and people), responsibility carries the law of cause and effect (ley judrey) inside it, and honour is anchored to a national day — Teachers' Day on 2 May, the third Druk Gyalpo's birthday.
