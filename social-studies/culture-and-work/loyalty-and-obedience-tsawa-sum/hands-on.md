# Hands-on — Loyalty and Obedience Tsawa Sum

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Obedience learned through Little Red Riding Hood, printed in the workbook as a play to perform — what her mother told her, what befell her when she stopped for flowers and talked to the wolf — and sealed with three written obedience pledges each to the child's teacher and parents. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
