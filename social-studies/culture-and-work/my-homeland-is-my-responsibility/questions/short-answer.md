# My Homeland is My Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: My homeland is my responsibility — the child's own role in group work (العمل الجماعي); the value of honesty/trustworthiness (الأمانة) in dealing with others; why truthfulness (الصدق) is a must in dealing with others; and taking an active part in class discussions and debates with classmates and peers — responsibility taught as how a small citizen behaves every day (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — for seven-year-olds the state is a person first — the whole identity unit hangs on the founding builder: who Zayed was and what he built, then what "my identity" is made of (down to the child's own identity papers), then the child's share: my homeland is my responsibility. (any close wording).**
