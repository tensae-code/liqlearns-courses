# My Homeland is My Responsibility — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the child's own role in group work (العمل الجماعي); the value of honesty/trustworthiness (الأمانة) in dealing with others; why truthfulness (الصدق) is a must in dealing with others; and taking an active part in class discussions and debates with classmates and peers — responsibility taught as how a small citizen behaves every day
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: for seven-year-olds the state is a person first — the whole identity unit hangs on the founding builder: who Zayed was and what he built, then what "my identity" is made of (down to the child's own identity papers), then the child's share: my homeland is my responsibility.
**Answer: True**
