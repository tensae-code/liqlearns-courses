# Video script — My Homeland is My Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G2 teacher guide, volume 2, printed p. 70, teaches: the child's own role in group work (العمل الجماعي); the value of honesty/trustworthiness (الأمانة) in dealing with others; why truthfulness (الصدق) is a must in dealing with others; and taking an active part in class discussions and debates with classmates and peers — responsibility taught as how a small citizen behaves every day

United Arab Emirates' own way: for seven-year-olds the state is a person first — the whole identity unit hangs on the founding builder: who Zayed was and what he built, then what "my identity" is made of (down to the child's own identity papers), then the child's share: my homeland is my responsibility.
