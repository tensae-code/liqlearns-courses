# Hands-on — My Homeland is My Responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My homeland is my responsibility
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the child's own role in group work (العمل الجماعي); the value of honesty/trustworthiness (الأمانة) in dealing with others; why truthfulness (الصدق) is a must in dealing with others; and taking an active part in class discussions and debates with classmates and peers — responsibility taught as how a small citizen behaves every day Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
