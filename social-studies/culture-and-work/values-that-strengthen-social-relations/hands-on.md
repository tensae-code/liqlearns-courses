# Hands-on — Values That Strengthen Social Relations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Values That Strengthen Social Relations
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Positive social relations are built by practising values and good conduct — honesty, truthfulness, protecting public property, defending the homeland, and taking part in charitable and cooperative works — which make society strong and cohesive and its members active citizens. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
