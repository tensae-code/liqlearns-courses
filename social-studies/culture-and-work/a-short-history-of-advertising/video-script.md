# Video script — A Short History of Advertising

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 10–15, teaches: Advertising is ancient — a 5,000-year-old Babylonian clay tablet, the Thebes papyrus, town criers who announced by shouting, Pompeii's 10,000 graffiti, and colonial Buenos Aires' pregoneros with their drums and street vendors crying their goods before shops settled into markets.

Argentina's own way: the ministry teaches media literacy as social studies — a free pupil fascicle that treats the adverts surrounding children as a social phenomenon to be read critically, from the town crier of colonial Buenos Aires to the television spot.
