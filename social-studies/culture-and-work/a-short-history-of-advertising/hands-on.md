# Hands-on — A Short History of Advertising

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Advertising is ancient — a 5,000-year-old Babylonian clay tablet, the Thebes papyrus, town criers who announced by shouting, Pompeii's 10,000 graffiti, and colonial Buenos Aires' pregoneros with their drums and street vendors crying their goods before shops settled into markets. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
