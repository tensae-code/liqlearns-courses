## Sub lesson 1 — A Short History of Advertising

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 10–15, teaches: Advertising is ancient — a 5,000-year-old Babylonian clay tablet, the Thebes papyrus, town criers who announced by shouting, Pompeii's 10,000 graffiti, and colonial Buenos Aires' pregoneros with their drums and street vendors crying their goods before shops settled into markets. ## Sub lesson 2 — Argentina's way — the book's own teaching way

Argentina's own way: the ministry teaches media literacy as social studies — a free pupil fascicle that treats the adverts surrounding children as a social phenomenon to be read critically, from the town crier of colonial Buenos Aires to the television spot.
