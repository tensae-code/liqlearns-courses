# How the President is Elected — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Iran is governed by three powers under the Leadership, and "the government" means the executive; the President is elected by the people's direct vote for four years (one consecutive re-election allowed), from candidates whose qualification the Guardian Council has confirmed, after election campaigning; the Leader then endorses (تنفیذ) the result, the President swears his oath in the Majlis, and forms a cabinet whose ministers need the Majlis's vote of confidence.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iran's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
