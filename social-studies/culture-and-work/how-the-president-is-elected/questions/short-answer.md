# How the President is Elected — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: How the President is Elected — Iran is governed by three powers under the Leadership, and "the government" means the executive; the President is elected by the people's direct vote for four years (one consecutive re-election allowed), from candidates whose qualification the Guardian Council has confirmed, after election campaigning; the Leader then endorses (تنفیذ) the result, the President swears his oath in the Majlis, and forms a cabinet whose ministers need the Majlis's vote of confidence (any close wording).**
