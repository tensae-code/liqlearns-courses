# Hands-on — How the President is Elected

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Iran is governed by three powers under the Leadership, and "the government" means the executive; the President is elected by the people's direct vote for four years (one consecutive re-election allowed), from candidates whose qualification the Guardian Council has confirmed, after election campaigning; the Leader then endorses (تنفیذ) the result, the President swears his oath in the Majlis, and forms a cabinet whose ministers need the Majlis's vote of confidence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
