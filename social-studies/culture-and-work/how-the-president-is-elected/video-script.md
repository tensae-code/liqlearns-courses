# Video script — How the President is Elected

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 8 (student book, Persian), printed p. 14, teaches: Iran is governed by three powers under the Leadership, and "the government" means the executive; the President is elected by the people's direct vote for four years (one consecutive re-election allowed), from candidates whose qualification the Guardian Council has confirmed, after election campaigning; the Leader then endorses (تنفیذ) the result, the President swears his oath in the Majlis, and forms a cabinet whose ministers need the Majlis's vote of confidence.

Iran's own way: the civics year is built as an institutional tour taken in the order a citizen meets the state — cooperation in home and neighbourhood, then the executive (this book's "government"), then the judiciary — and every institution is introduced through newspaper headlines and the pupil's own town.
