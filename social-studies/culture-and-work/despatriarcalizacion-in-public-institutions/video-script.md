The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bolivia Grade 12 BO Social Sciences Sec 6 (printed p. 452), teaches: Despatriarcalización inside the state itself — Decreto Supremo Nº 4650 (5 January 2022) declaring 2022 the Year of the Cultural Revolution for Despatriarcalización "for a life free of violence against women": what patriarchy is, gender parity in public institutions, equal pay and a life free of violence as rights, positive discrimination, and unlinking professions from gender.

Bolivia's own way: the final year turns from history to the citizen's own legal armour — identity in a plural nation, a supreme decree ordering the state itself to despatriarcalise, a Youth Law, an anti-trafficking law taught with self-protection advice for teenagers, and the labour rights of working children under Bolivia's own Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente.
