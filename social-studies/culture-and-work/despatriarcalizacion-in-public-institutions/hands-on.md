# Hands-on — Despatriarcalizacion in Public Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: despatriarcalización inside the state itself — Decreto Supremo Nº 4650 (5 January 2022) declaring 2022 the Year of the Cultural Revolution for Despatriarcalización "for a life free of violence against women": what patriarchy is, gender parity in public institutions, equal pay and a life free of violence as rights, positive discrimination, and unlinking professions from gender. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
