# Secondary activities — cottage industries

## Do it first

List your area's craft industries, craft by craft — who weaves, who makes pots, who works metal — as the book's task sets it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Craft production in the home

By development level, industries divide into **cottage and modern**. **Cottage industries make craft products with limited labour** — **blacksmithing, carpentry, silverwork, weaving, basketry and pottery** are named — and **the work is done inside the home**.

[Picture 11511 — a home workshop: a loom, a forge and a pottery wheel]

## Sub lesson 2 — Watch the shuttle

Of all the named crafts, weaving shows its motion best: the shuttle carries the thread through the warp, pass after pass, and cloth grows.

[GIF 11512 — a shuttle moving through a hand loom]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Name the six named cottage crafts. Say the two marks of a cottage industry: craft products with limited labour, work done inside the home.
