# Lesson plan — Secondary activities — cottage industries

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Secondary activities — cottage industries
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 9 — Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder: culture, economic activities, trade, and tax).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cottage industries — craft production inside the home with limited labour: blacksmithing, carpentry, silverwork, weaving, basketry and pottery
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title, at Grade 8 depth in the course's level ladder (levels, not grades).
