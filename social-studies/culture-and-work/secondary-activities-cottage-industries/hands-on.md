# Hands-on — Secondary activities — cottage industries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
List your area's craft industries, craft by craft — who weaves, who makes pots, who works metal — as the book's task sets it.

## Activity 2
Visit or picture one home workshop. Write what is made there, with how many workers, and where the work happens.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Name the six named cottage crafts. Say the two marks of a cottage industry: craft products with limited labour, work done inside the home.
