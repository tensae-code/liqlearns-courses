# Secondary activities — cottage industries — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weaving and pottery are cottage industries.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cottage industries use unlimited factory labour.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Industries divide by development level into cottage and modern.
**Answer: True**
