# Secondary activities — cottage industries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name all six named crafts.
**Answer:** Blacksmithing, carpentry, silverwork, weaving, basketry and pottery.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the two marks of a cottage industry?
**Answer:** Craft products made with limited labour; the work done inside the home.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does cloth grow on a hand loom, in the GIF's motion?
**Answer:** The shuttle carries the thread through the warp, pass after pass.
