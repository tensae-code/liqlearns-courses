# Secondary activities — cottage industries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cottage industries make —
a) Mass factory goods  b) Craft products with limited labour  c) Only food  d) Electric power
**Answer: b) Craft products with limited labour**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cottage work is done —
a) In great factories  b) Inside the home  c) On ships  d) In mines
**Answer: b) Inside the home**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is a named cottage craft?
a) Shipbuilding  b) Silverwork  c) Aircraft making  d) Railway engineering
**Answer: b) Silverwork**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The shuttle in weaving carries —
a) The warp  b) The thread through the warp  c) The finished cloth away  d) The loom frame
**Answer: b) The thread through the warp**
