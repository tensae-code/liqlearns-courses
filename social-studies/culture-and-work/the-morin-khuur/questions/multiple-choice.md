# The Morin Khuur — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 10 "The matchless wonder — the morin khuur" (Хосгүй гайхамшиг — Морин хуур)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 10 "The matchless wonder — the morin khuur" (Хосгүй гайхамшиг — Морин хуур)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) The horse-head fiddle was born from the people's love of the horse — its legend tells of a man who carved his dead horse's head and made its hair sing; its melodies picture the horse's gaits and the life of the homeland, it has its own respectful way of being held (татлага), the master player G. **Answer: a) The horse-head fiddle was born from the people's love of the horse — its legend tells of a man who carved his dead horse's head and made its hair sing; its melodies picture the horse's gaits and the life of the homeland, it has its own respectful way of being held (татлага), the master player G. **3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —

**Answer: a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
