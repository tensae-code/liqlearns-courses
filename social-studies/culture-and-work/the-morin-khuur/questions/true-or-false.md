# The Morin Khuur — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the horse-head fiddle was born from the people's love of the horse — its legend tells of a man who carved his dead horse's head and made its hair sing; its melodies picture the horse's gaits and the life of the homeland, it has its own respectful way of being held (татлага), the master player G. Jamyan began its teaching, and since 1992 the Morin Khuur Ensemble has played it to the world.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
