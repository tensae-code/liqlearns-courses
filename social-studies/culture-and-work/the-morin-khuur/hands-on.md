# Hands-on — The Morin Khuur

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 10 "The matchless wonder — the morin khuur" (Хосгүй гайхамшиг — Морин хуур)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The horse-head fiddle was born from the people's love of the horse — its legend tells of a man who carved his dead horse's head and made its hair sing; its melodies picture the horse's gaits and the life of the homeland, it has its own respectful way of being held (татлага), the master player G. Jamyan began its teaching, and since 1992 the Morin Khuur Ensemble has played it to the world. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
