# Lesson plan — Social Movements and Transformation in Latin America

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Social Movements and Transformation in Latin America
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Honduras, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Social Movements and Transformation in Latin America — latin America's inequality, from the bad distribution of goods, breeds discontent that moves societies to transform themselves; the labour movement (in Honduras, the 1954 banana strike that won benefits for the working class), cultural movements bringing art to the people, and women's new roles drive the change; a country has development when its population's needs — food, clothing, health, housing, services, work, education — can be met — as Honduras's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Social Movements and Transformation in Latin America — latin America's inequality, from the bad distribution of goods, breeds discontent that moves societies to transform themselves; the labour movement (in Honduras, the 1954 banana strike that won benefits for the working class), cultural movements bringing art to the people, and women's new roles drive the change; a country has development when its population's needs — food, clothing, health, housing, services, work, education — can be met.
