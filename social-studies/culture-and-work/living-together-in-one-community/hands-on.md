# Hands-on — Living Together in One Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: living together in one community — people of different languages, cultures and faiths (Eritrea's nine peoples named) held together by shared needs, in harmony and love; the elders pass the community's good values to the young: trustworthiness, truth, love of work, respect for elders, and helping the disabled and the weak. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
