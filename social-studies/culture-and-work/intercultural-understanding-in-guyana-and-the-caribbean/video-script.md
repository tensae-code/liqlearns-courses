Guyana Grade 6 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 53–56), teaches: Understanding across cultures in Guyana and the Caribbean is built through shared traditional music, dance and drama (Chutney, Soca, Reggae, Kathak), language exchange, sports and games, and visiting each other's festivals.

Guyana Grade 1 Guyanese in the World (printed p. 59 (NEW slug first proposed in Book 8 above)), teaches: In Guyana everyone takes part in the different religious festivals — people are invited to the different places of worship to celebrate, and share a meal together and enjoy fellowship.

Guyana's own way: the newest ministry series teaches citizenship from the child's own roles outward — family duties, community roles, youth responsibilities at home and across the Caribbean, then power, privilege and human rights in plain language, with Guyanese and Caribbean case studies (Annette Arjoon-Martins, Mia Mottley) and a value component woven into every unit. the series opens at Grade 1 inside the home — the family that cooks and gardens together, the chores shared out fairly, the groups a small child already belongs to, and rules taught as the way a home and a classroom stay in harmony.
