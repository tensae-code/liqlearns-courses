# Video script — Change and Growing Into a Better Person

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G5 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 61, teaches: change (تغيير) defined as the ability to develop and become a new person; how our skills, habits and outlook change as we grow older, the difference between a positive and a negative outlook on the same event, and choosing habits that build praiseworthy traits

United Arab Emirates' own way: the course defines its key words for the child in a back-of-book glossary — forgiveness is "the ability to get past the feeling of hurt or anger you feel toward another person", a tribe is "a group of individuals living on one patch of land, bound by shared ties" — and the righteous person is analysed as mind, heart and hand: what he thinks, what he feels, what he does.
