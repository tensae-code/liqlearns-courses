## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: the course defines its key words for the child in a back-of-book glossary — forgiveness is "the ability to get past the feeling of hurt or anger you feel toward another person", a tribe is "a group of individuals living on one patch of land, bound by shared ties" — and the righteous person is analysed as mind, heart and hand: what he thinks, what he feels, what he does. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
