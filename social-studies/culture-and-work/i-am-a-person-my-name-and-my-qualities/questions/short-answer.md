# I Am a Person My Name and My Qualities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: I Am a Human Being — who I am — my name, my family, my birth certificate, my talents and qualities; people share some traits and differ in others, and each person is distinct (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Libya's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole book is written in the first person — every lesson is "I and …" (I and my family, I and my school, I and the neighbourhood) — citizenship starts as the child's own relationships, and even the environment unit is framed as the child's own behaviour: what I throw away, what I plant, which beach I clean. (any close wording).**
