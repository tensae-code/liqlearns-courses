# Hands-on — I Am a Person My Name and My Qualities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: who I am — my name, my family, my birth certificate, my talents and qualities, and how each person is distinct. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Libya's way
Say how Libya's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
