# The Emirati School — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: what the Emirati school is — its concept and its goals — and its anthem; the stages education passed through in the Emirates before the Union (1953–1971) and the modern school system after it (1972–2016); education's developmental role in the country's society, with the citizens' own roles and participation in Emirati life; and Article 14 of the constitution, on education, drawn from the learning sources — the school studied as the nation's own house of learning
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: the term pairs the world outside (Asia's great states) with the heritage inside — the founder's civilising work, the country's material and moral inheritance, and the Emirati school itself studied as one of the nation's own institutions.
**Answer: True**
