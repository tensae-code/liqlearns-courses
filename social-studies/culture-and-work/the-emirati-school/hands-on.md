# Hands-on — The Emirati School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Emirati school
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what the Emirati school is — its concept and its goals — and its anthem; the stages education passed through in the Emirates before the Union (1953–1971) and the modern school system after it (1972–2016); education's developmental role in the country's society, with the citizens' own roles and participation in Emirati life; and Article 14 of the constitution, on education, drawn from the learning sources — the school studied as the nation's own house of learning Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
