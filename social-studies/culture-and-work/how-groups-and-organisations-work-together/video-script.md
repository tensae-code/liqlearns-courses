# Video script — How Groups and Organisations Work Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG (printed p. 64), teaches: How groups and organizations relate to and communicate with each other, including groups that speak up for others.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 6): in senior primary the community strand becomes civics proper — the groups and organisations of PNG, how they relate and speak for people, and how development changes the community — while the culture strand runs from rituals to taking part in cultural events.
