# Hands-on — The Public Force and the States Monopoly of Force

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Fuerza Pública (Armed Forces and National Police) as essential institutions of the state under the national government — police keep security and public order, the armed forces defend the state's integrity and sovereignty — and whether that monopoly of force is limited and how it guarantees citizens' rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
