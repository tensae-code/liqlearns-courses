# Video script — The Public Force and the States Monopoly of Force

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 8 Estudios Sociales 8vo EGB, printed p. 180, teaches: The Fuerza Pública (Armed Forces and National Police) as essential institutions of the state under the national government — police keep security and public order, the armed forces defend the state's integrity and sovereignty — and whether that monopoly of force is limited and how it guarantees citizens' rights.

Ecuador's own way: at Superior level the civics strand is built on Ecuador's own 2008 constitutional order — Buen Vivir (Sumak Kawsay) as the state's development idea, a plurinational and intercultural state, nature itself as a subject of rights, and children's rights taught from Ecuador's own Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia.
