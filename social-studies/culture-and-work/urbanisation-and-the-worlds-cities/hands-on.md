# Hands-on — Urbanisation And The Worlds Cities

Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 1: Towards a world becoming urban (به سوی جهانی در حال شهری شدن) — the growth of cities and urbanisation the greatest change in geographical space in the 20th and 21st centuries; the table of urbanisation levels by world region in 2023 (Africa 45%); great cities of 5–10 million and cities over 10 million; suburbs (حومه‌ها) growing around the great cities, their people commuting in for shopping, work and services; chains of mother-cities along transport routes making megalopolises; Tokyo with its metropolitan region, 38 million, the world's most populous metropolis.
