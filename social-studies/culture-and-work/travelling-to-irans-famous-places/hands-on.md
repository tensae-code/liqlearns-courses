# Hands-on — Travelling to Irans Famous Places

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: holiday journeys through our own country — Sara's family travels to Mashhad, with the shrine of Imam Reza, the tomb of Ferdowsi and saffron; Maryam's family goes by bus to Isfahan and stays in a hotel, and the two friends compare what each city is famous for. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
