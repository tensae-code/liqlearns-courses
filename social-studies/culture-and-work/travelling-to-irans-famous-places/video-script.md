# Video script — Travelling to Irans Famous Places

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 82, teaches: holiday journeys through our own country — Sara's family travels to Mashhad, with the shrine of Imam Reza, the tomb of Ferdowsi and saffron; Maryam's family goes by bus to Isfahan and stays in a hotel, and the two friends compare what each city is famous for.

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces.
