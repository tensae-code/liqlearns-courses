# Travelling to Irans Famous Places

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: holiday journeys through our own country — Sara's family travels to Mashhad, with the shrine of Imam Reza, the tomb of Ferdowsi and saffron; Maryam's family goes by bus to Isfahan and stays in a hotel, and the two friends compare what each city is famous for — as Iran's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Travelling to Irans Famous Places

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 82, teaches: holiday journeys through our own country — Sara's family travels to Mashhad, with the shrine of Imam Reza, the tomb of Ferdowsi and saffron; Maryam's family goes by bus to Isfahan and stays in a hotel, and the two friends compare what each city is famous for. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 4 special-needs book widens the circle from self to country — my identity booklet, my address, my neighbourhood and its people, city against village, and finally Iran itself as "our big home" divided into provinces. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
