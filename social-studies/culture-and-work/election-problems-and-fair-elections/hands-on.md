# Hands-on — Election Problems and Fair Elections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The recurring problems of Samoan elections — last-minute voter registration, voters transferring between constituencies, allegations of bribery and undue influence, constituency candidate selection — and the government Commission set up to study and fix them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Samoa's way
Say how Samoa's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
