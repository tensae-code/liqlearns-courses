# Choosing a Profession Abilities Education and the Labour Market — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Work as a life value — through purposeful work a person satisfies his needs and realises his abilities; the division of labour (specialisation) breeds the multitude of professions, a profession being a concrete kind of labour activity told apart by its variety, monotony, independence and complexity; choosing one is the person's own affair, decided by the book's scheme — your individual abilities, the level of your education, and the productive specialisation of your region (with the Minsk automobile plant, Fiat and Zhiguli, and northern cotton against southern citrus as its specialisation examples); and the labour market as a system of economic relations arising between the worker (the person), the employer and the state, where the supply of labour power, the demand for it and its price (the payment for labour) meet.
