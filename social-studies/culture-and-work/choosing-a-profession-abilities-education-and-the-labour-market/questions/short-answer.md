# Choosing a Profession Abilities Education and the Labour Market — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Choosing a profession (Выбар прафесіі) — Grade 9 — work as a life value — through purposeful work a person satisfies his needs and realises his abilities; the division of labour (specialisation) breeds the multitude of professions, a profession being a concrete kind of labour activity told apart by its variety, monotony, independence and complexity; choosing one is the person's own affair, decided by the book's scheme — your individual abilities, the level of your education, and the productive specialisation of your region (with the Minsk automobile plant, Fiat and Zhiguli, and northern cotton against southern citrus as its specialisation examples); and the labour market as a system of economic relations arising between the worker (the person), the employer and the state, where the supply of labour power, the demand for it and its price (the payment for labour) meet (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the civics track is the pupil's own state and law read from inside — "our state" is taught straight from the 1994 Constitution of the Republic of Belarus (unitary, democratic, social, law-governed; the people as the only source of power; the 1996 referendum; the 1999 Union treaty with Russia), rights are taught article by article from that Constitution (Arts. 1–3, 21–25, 30, 41–49, 53, 59–62) beside the Universal Declaration, and the course closes not with a test but with a discussion, "I — a citizen of the Republic of Belarus", on the citizen's own responsibility before the state. (any close wording).**
