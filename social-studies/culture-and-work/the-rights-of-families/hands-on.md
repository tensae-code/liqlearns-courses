# Hands-on — The Rights Of Families

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Os direitos das famílias: a qualidade de vida das famílias
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: families have rights — to quality of life and to the habits and conditions a family needs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## A família e as suas necessidades
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the family and its needs — what every family must have and how its members work to meet them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Angola's way
