**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Afghanistan, Honduras, Costa Rica).**

Costa Rica's own way (Estudios Sociales CONED 10): a world-history book whose economics spine runs crisis to crisis — 1929, Bretton Woods, oil shocks, the Latin American debt crisis and neoliberal adjustment — ending at home with Costa Rica's trade opening to China and its shallower, state-keeping reform path.

Afghanistan's own way (Afghanistan Grade 11 Geography (Dari)): an economic-geography year in two halves — first Afghanistan (farming and livestock, energy and mines, industries and trade), then the same themes for the world (water and farming, energy, mines and industries, trade and tourism), each Afghanistan chapter closing with a "review of the situation" lesson on the country's own state.

Honduras's own way (Honduras Grade 5 G5 Student CCSS): Grade 5 turns outward to the Americas — Honduras's trade network and treaties, its free-trade zone at Choloma, tourism, and the integration blocs (MCCA, CARICOM, CELAC) as the road to development.
