## Sub lesson 1 — Pillars of National Security

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 210–213), teaches: The eight pillars national security stands on — religion (Islam as creed and law, fixing the values that discipline behaviour), the ruler (the monarchy of the Al Saud, ruling by right and justice, not by narrow ideology, tribe or region), the institutions that achieve security, the individuals, identity, the economy, justice (independent courts applying the Shariah with all equal before the judiciary) and unity (the Kingdom's firm unity, whose keeping is a duty of state and citizen). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
