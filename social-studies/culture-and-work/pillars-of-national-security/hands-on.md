# Hands-on — Pillars of National Security

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the eight pillars national security stands on — religion (Islam as creed and law, fixing the values that discipline behaviour), the ruler (the monarchy of the Al Saud, ruling by right and justice, not by narrow ideology, tribe or region), the institutions that achieve security, the individuals, identity, the economy, justice (independent courts applying the Shariah with all equal before the judiciary) and unity (the Kingdom's firm unity, whose keeping is a duty of state and citizen). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
