The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India Grade 6 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Social and Political Life-I (printed p. 35), teaches: people participate through elections and protest, government settles conflicts peacefully, and justice and equality are its key promises (South Africa's apartheid struggle as the mirror case).

India's own way: Civics opens not with institutions but with the child's own classroom diversity, then builds government in widening rings — nation, panchayat village, district offices, city corporation — always through named people and a story (a sarpanch, a patwari, a waste-picker).
