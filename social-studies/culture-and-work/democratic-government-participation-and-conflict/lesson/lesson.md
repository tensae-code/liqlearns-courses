## Sub lesson 2 — India's way — the book's own teaching way

India's own way: Civics opens not with institutions but with the child's own classroom diversity, then builds government in widening rings — nation, panchayat village, district offices, city corporation — always through named people and a story (a sarpanch, a patwari, a waste-picker). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
