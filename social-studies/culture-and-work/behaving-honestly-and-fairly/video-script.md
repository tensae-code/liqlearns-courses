# Video script — Behaving Honestly and Fairly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

North Macedonia Grade 4 History and Society (printed p. 23), teaches: honest, fair and proper behaviour as a rule of life.

North Macedonia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 names the enemies of honest public life to nine-year-olds — nepotism, bribes and bribery — before it ever describes the state; then the state is built from the municipality up (its symbols, its mayor, its care for citizens) to the capital, the institutions and the symbols of the republic, whose defining character is taught as multicultural, multiethnic and civic.
