## Sub lesson 1 — Behaving Honestly and Fairly

North Macedonia Grade 4 History and Society (printed p. 23), teaches: honest, fair and proper behaviour as a rule of life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — North Macedonia's way — the books' own teaching way

North Macedonia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 names the enemies of honest public life to nine-year-olds — nepotism, bribes and bribery — before it ever describes the state; then the state is built from the municipality up (its symbols, its mayor, its care for citizens) to the capital, the institutions and the symbols of the republic, whose defining character is taught as multicultural, multiethnic and civic. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
