# Hands-on — Tsagaan Sar the White Month

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 13 Сайхан шинэлээрэй (Happy New Year)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Tsagaan Sar, the lunar new year — the zolgokh greeting embrace performed with elders, the festival greeting words ("Амархан сайн байна уу?"), and the shagai ankle-bone games played at the festival. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Topic 14 "From the customs of Tsagaan Sar food and offering" (Цагаан сарын идээ, шуус тавих ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The White Month, the nation's greatest festival, kept since the ancestors: the whole-sheep offering (их бүхэл) is laid out in its fixed order — head, ууц, хаа, гуя, neck and сээр — and never in an ordinary household; on the eve (битүүн) the family "closes" the old year with fullness games and full dishes; and on the first morning comes шинэлэх ёс — the золгох greeting in strict order of age, the younger supporting the elder's elbows, the хадаг offered on upturned palms, snuff exchanged, and the blessing answers wished back. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Topic 19 "Tsagaan Sar symbolism and the taboo custom" (Цагаан сарын бэлгэдэл ба цээрийн ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: White is the Mongols' most honoured colour — a white heart, a white road, white food; the festival's honoured dish is white food, and a guest first tastes the white food and tastes it again on leaving, so that no work is hindered and the white blessing travels with them; and the Tsagaan Sar taboos guard the whole festival — no lending out in debt at the greeting, no bad words on the eve, no quarrelling or fighting, nothing taken out of the ger in the first six days, no staying overnight at another's home on the first night. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
