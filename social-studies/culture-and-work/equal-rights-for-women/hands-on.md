# Hands-on — Equal Rights for Women

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Why parliament needs women members (reserved seats) — men and women hold equal rights; the long world history of the vote (in 1908 women could vote in only a small part of the world; Australia once let only white people vote by law). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
