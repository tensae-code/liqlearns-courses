# Video script — Our Traditional Foods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 17, teaches: Traditional food falls into white foods (milk), red foods (meat) and green foods (plants), more than 3,000 dishes named over the centuries; eating follows the season — light dairy in summer, meat in winter — and children and elders are served with special respect; how cheese is made differently from raw and from boiled milk, and stored for winter.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
