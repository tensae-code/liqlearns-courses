# Hands-on — Our Traditional Foods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 5 "From the customs of traditional food and drink" (Уламжлалт идээ, ундааны ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Traditional food falls into white foods (milk), red foods (meat) and green foods (plants), more than 3,000 dishes named over the centuries; eating follows the season — light dairy in summer, meat in winter — and children and elders are served with special respect; how cheese is made differently from raw and from boiled milk, and stored for winter. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
