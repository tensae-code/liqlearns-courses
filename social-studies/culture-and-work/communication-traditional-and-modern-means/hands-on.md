# Hands-on — Communication Traditional and Modern Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: traditional means (drums, fire and smoke, town crier, messengers) and modern means (telecommunications, print and electronic mass media, postal services), and what mass media are for. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
