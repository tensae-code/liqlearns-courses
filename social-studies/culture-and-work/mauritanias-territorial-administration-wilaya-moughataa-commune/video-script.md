# Video script — Mauritanias Territorial Administration Wilaya Moughataa Commune

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mauritania Grade 5 Civic Education SB (printed p. 7), teaches: How Mauritania's territory is administered, in fifteen wilayas and their divisions.

Mauritania Grade 5 Civic Education SB (printed p. 9), teaches: The wilaya (region) and its governor.

Mauritania Grade 5 Civic Education SB (printed p. 11), teaches: The moughataa (district) and its hakem.

Mauritania Grade 5 Civic Education SB (printed p. 13), teaches: The administrative centre, the smallest territorial unit.

Mauritania Grade 5 Civic Education SB (printed p. 19), teaches: The districts together making the territorial entity of the Mauritanian state.

Mauritania's own way: Year 5 is the state itself — the child climbs the whole ladder of Mauritania's administration (commune → administrative centre → moughataa → wilaya), meets its elections and its three powers, then walks through the working community (professions, crafts) and the uniformed services that protect it, ending with the environmental police
