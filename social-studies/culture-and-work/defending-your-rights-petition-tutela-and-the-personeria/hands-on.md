# Hands-on — Defending Your Rights Petition Tutela and the Personeria

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tema 26 (closing part)
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado06, printed pp. 238–242, teaches: knowing the mechanisms makes rights real — derecho de petición, acción de tutela and acción de cumplimiento; the Personería watches over human rights and advises citizens free of charge; workers and campesinos whose rights are violated can claim them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
