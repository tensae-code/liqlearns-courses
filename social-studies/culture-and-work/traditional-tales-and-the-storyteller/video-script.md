# Video script — Traditional Tales and the Storyteller

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela Grade 1 G1 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 66, teaches: A storyteller visits the school — traditional tales (the Tío Conejo cycle) told aloud to the class as living Venezuelan oral culture.

Venezuela's own way: the child's own world first — school, family, classroom and house are taught by direct observation and riddles before any map; rights are taught as the child's own rights (to a house with a flag window, to Venezuelan foods, to traditional games, to school, health and respect), each paired with the duty that goes with it.
