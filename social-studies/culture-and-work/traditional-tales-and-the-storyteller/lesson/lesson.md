## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: the child's own world first — school, family, classroom and house are taught by direct observation and riddles before any map; rights are taught as the child's own rights (to a house with a flag window, to Venezuelan foods, to traditional games, to school, health and respect), each paired with the duty that goes with it.
