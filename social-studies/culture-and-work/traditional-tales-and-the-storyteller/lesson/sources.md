**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Venezuela).**

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 1 G1 CS Viva Venezuela): the child's own world first — school, family, classroom and house are taught by direct observation and riddles before any map; rights are taught as the child's own rights (to a house with a flag window, to Venezuelan foods, to traditional games, to school, health and respect), each paired with the duty that goes with it.
