# Video script — Languages and Costumes of My Village

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal Grade 3 My Social Studies and Creative Arts (printed p. 12), teaches: The languages spoken and dress worn in my village.

Nepal Grade 4 My Social Studies and Creative Arts (printed p. 12), teaches: The languages and dress of our people.

Nepal Grade 5 My Social Studies and Creative Arts (printed p. 14), teaches: Our language and dress.

Nepal's own way: Grade 3 names caste directly — "Abolish Untouchability" is a named lesson — and local government arrives as the child's own ward and what it does. Grade 4's civic unit is the fullest primary civics in the series — rights, child participation, traffic rules and both tiers of local government (VDC and municipality) — and its social-problems unit teaches settling a quarrel as a named skill. Grade 5's civic sense reaches the district — the District Coordination Committee and its functions get two full lessons — and the state itself arrives inside the history unit: head of state against head of government, through the 2062–65 BS movement and the republic.
