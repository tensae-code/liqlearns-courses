## Sub lesson 4 — Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way

Nepal's own way: Grade 3 names caste directly — "Abolish Untouchability" is a named lesson — and local government arrives as the child's own ward and what it does. Grade 4's civic unit is the fullest primary civics in the series — rights, child participation, traffic rules and both tiers of local government (VDC and municipality) — and its social-problems unit teaches settling a quarrel as a named skill. Grade 5's civic sense reaches the district — the District Coordination Committee and its functions get two full lessons — and the state itself arrives inside the history unit: head of state against head of government, through the 2062–65 BS movement and the republic. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
