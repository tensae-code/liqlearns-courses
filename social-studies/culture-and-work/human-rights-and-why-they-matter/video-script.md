# Video script — Human Rights and Why They Matter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vanuatu Citoyenneté Sociale Guide Years 1–6 (printed p. 26), teaches: Telling wants, needs and rights apart — a right is what every human can expect as a member of the community.

Vanuatu's own way: social citizenship is taught as roles before rules — the Year 1 child maps who they are in family and village (chief, pastor, nurse, police, women's and youth group leaders), learns that roles change and are not fixed by gender, and only in Years 5–6 reaches rights, and how a community's rules are made and by whom.
