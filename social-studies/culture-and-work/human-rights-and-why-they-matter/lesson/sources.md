**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Uganda). Vanuatu is named here as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Uganda's own way (Uganda Excel P6 SST): P6 looks outward and inward at once — the East African Community's own organs and symbols beside the member states' flags, then the citizen: what citizenship is, the child's rights, what violating rights looks like (mob justice named plainly), and how elections actually run.

Vanuatu's own way (teacher guide — evidence): social citizenship is taught as roles before rules — the Year 1 child maps who they are in family and village (chief, pastor, nurse, police, women's and youth group leaders), learns that roles change and are not fixed by gender, and only in Years 5–6 reaches rights, and how a community's rules are made and by whom.
