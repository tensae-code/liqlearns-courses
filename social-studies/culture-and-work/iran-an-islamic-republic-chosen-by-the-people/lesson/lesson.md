## Sub lesson 1 — Iran an Islamic Republic Chosen by the People

Iran Social Studies Grade 5 (student book, Persian), printed p. 53, teaches: the Pahlavi monarchy, which the people had not chosen, was overthrown in 1357; in Farvardin 1358 the people went to the ballot boxes and over 98 per cent voted "yes" to the Islamic Republic, and elected worthy people to write the Constitution. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: civics is taught by analogy from the school camp trip — a group needs roles, rules and a leader, so a country needs a government — and the Islamic Republic is presented as the people's own choice, made in the Farvardin 1358 referendum and renewed in every Majlis election. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
