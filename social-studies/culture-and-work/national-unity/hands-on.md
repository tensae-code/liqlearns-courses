# Hands-on — National Unity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unity is the people's oneness in national events and in defending country and state; Islam is named its foundation in Afghanistan — one God, one Prophet, one Book, one qibla — and unity with peace brings security, schooling, work and welfare. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Afghanistan's way
Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
