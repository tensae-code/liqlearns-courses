# Digital Access and the Digital Divide — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the digital divide (الفجوة الرقمية) defined as the gap between countries — and people — in reaching information infrastructure and devices; the DAI digital-access index and its five categories (infrastructure, affordability, knowledge, quality, usage); and equal electronic opportunity for People of Determination as a state duty
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: digital citizenship is a named school subject of its own, run with the Aqdar empowerment programme — access is a right the state measures (the DAI index, 100% internet spread in the Emirati market), the Emirati ID card's number is dissected as a civics lesson, and every abuse is answered with the federation's own law and its own reporting service (Abu Dhabi Police "Aman", 8002626).
**Answer: True**
