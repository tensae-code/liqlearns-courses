# Digital Access and the Digital Divide — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Electronic access — the digital divide (الفجوة الرقمية) defined as the gap between countries — and people — in reaching information infrastructure and devices; the DAI digital-access index and its five categories (infrastructure, affordability, knowledge, quality, usage); and equal electronic opportunity for People of Determination as a state duty (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — digital citizenship is a named school subject of its own, run with the Aqdar empowerment programme — access is a right the state measures (the DAI index, 100% internet spread in the Emirati market), the Emirati ID card's number is dissected as a civics lesson, and every abuse is answered with the federation's own law and its own reporting service (Abu Dhabi Police "Aman", 8002626). (any close wording).**
