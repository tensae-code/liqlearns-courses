# Setting a Goal and Working Until I Reach It — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Reaching a goal in steps — set the goal, organise your time, practise, and try again if you fail — told through Julia learning the accordion and the bear who keeps trying for the honey despite the bee stings.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Shared goals against personal goals as the class plans its Evacuation Day celebration — agree the programme, share out the roles, and Razan's written plan for her acting role: the goal, one week to reach it, and a step for every day (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: A goal with its how — Sally's goal is protecting her teeth, so she cuts the sweets; Raed's is helping his mother, so he tidies his toys and books; Jana's is her family's happiness, so she makes them greeting cards; every one of us has a goal they strive for (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part teaches: The whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free)..
**Answer: True**
