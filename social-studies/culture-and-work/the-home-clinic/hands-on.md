# Hands-on — The Home Clinic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Kuwaiti woman of the past as her family's home clinic — nursing its members with the remedies she knew, the caring role at the centre of the old Kuwaiti home. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
