# Hands-on — What a Government is for

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what government is for — defence, peace and security, taxes, development and social services — and the separation of powers into Legislature, Judiciary and Executive so no branch becomes too strong. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
