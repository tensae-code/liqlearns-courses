# Video script — What a Government is for

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eritrea Grade 7 ER G7 Social Studies (printed p. 83), teaches: What government is for — defence, peace and security, taxes, development and social services — and the separation of powers into Legislature, Judiciary and Executive so no branch becomes too strong.

Eritrea's own way: government is taught as a growth story — from clan and tribal administration run by appointed, removable leaders in the common interest, through class society and city-states (Athens beside Eritrea's own Adulis, Coloe and Toconda), to Eritrea's present unitary state with Regional Administration.
