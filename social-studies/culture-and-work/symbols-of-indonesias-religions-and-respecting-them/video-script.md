Indonesia's Grade 2 PP-G2 student (printed p. 114); Grade 6 PP-G6 student (printed p. 96); Grade 6 PP-G6 teacher (printed p. 153), teaches: Bab 3 C. Simbol Keberagaman Agama, Bab 5 A. Menjaga Keberagaman Budaya dan Agama, Panduan Khusus Bab 5: Menghormati Perbedaan Budaya dan Agama dalam Kehidupan Sehari-hari — teaches: recognising the symbols and houses of worship of Indonesia's religions and respecting each other's faith; guarding cultural and religious diversity — the beauty of diversity and tolerance inside it; guidance for respecting cultural and religious difference and teaching tolerance.

Indonesia's own way: Grade 2 turns Pancasila from a symbol to copy into behaviour at home — each principle (sila) is matched to a picture-symbol on the Garuda shield and then to a concrete act a 7-year-old can do in the family. the primary Pancasila course closes by widening every earlier theme to the nation — norms become norms of berbangsa dan bernegara (national and state life), and gotong royong is finally named as bela negara, defending the country by working together. the final primary guide states the subject's position (kedudukan) explicitly — Pendidikan Pancasila is the vehicle of the state ideology in school — and sequences outcomes to the NKRI chapters.
