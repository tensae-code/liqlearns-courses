## Sub lesson 2 — Indonesia's way — the books' own teaching way

Indonesia's own way: Grade 2 turns Pancasila from a symbol to copy into behaviour at home — each principle (sila) is matched to a picture-symbol on the Garuda shield and then to a concrete act a 7-year-old can do in the family. the primary Pancasila course closes by widening every earlier theme to the nation — norms become norms of berbangsa dan bernegara (national and state life), and gotong royong is finally named as bela negara, defending the country by working together. the final primary guide states the subject's position (kedudukan) explicitly — Pendidikan Pancasila is the vehicle of the state ideology in school — and sequences outcomes to the NKRI chapters. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
