# Hands-on — Symbols of Indonesias Religions and Respecting Them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 3 C. Simbol Keberagaman Agama, Bab 5 A. Menjaga Keberagaman Budaya dan Agama, Panduan Khusus Bab 5: Menghormati Perbedaan Budaya dan Agama dalam Kehidupan Sehari-hari — teaches: recognising the symbols and houses of worship of Indonesia's religions and respecting each other's faith; guarding cultural and religious diversity — the beauty of diversity and tolerance inside it; guidance for respecting cultural and religious difference and teaching tolerance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indonesia's way
Say how Indonesia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
