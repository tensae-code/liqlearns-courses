# Hands-on — The Bedey River Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Bedey (river-gypsy) community's own way — 25 families living in boats under a Sarder who guards and arbitrates, women as the earners, the groom-on-a-tree wedding promise, the Thaar language, and all sharing one another's joys and dangers. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
