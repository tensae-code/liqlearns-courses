# Trust and Learning Together

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: friendship built by understanding each other and noticing friends' good points — as Japan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — (3) 友達とたがいに理解し合って (Understanding each other with friends)

Japan Grades 3–4 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 70), teaches: Friendship built by understanding each other and noticing friends' good points. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — 読みもの「同じ仲間だから」("Because We Are the Same Team")

Japan Grades 3–4 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 76), teaches: A sports-day story of friends who carry each other because they are one team (reading). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — (3) たがいに信頼し、学び合って (Trusting each other, learning together)

Japan Grades 5–6 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 72), teaches: Friendship as mutual trust and learning that lifts both. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — 読みもの「知らない間の出来事」("What Happened Without My Knowing")

Japan Grades 5–6 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 76), teaches: One event seen from two sides — a new transfer pupil and the class that received her (reading story told twice). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 5 — Japan's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 2): the middle pupils' reader widens in the same four steps as the other two Doutoku books — raise yourself, relate to people, feel life, relate to everyone — and plants the first civics inside moral teaching: society's rules are introduced through a banned swing the class wins back by making rules, and fairness is taught as never shutting anyone out. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 6 — Japan's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 3): the older pupils' reader moves from self-discipline to society in four widening units — polish yourself, connect with people, cherish life, connect with everyone — and teaches civics inside moral stories: rights are introduced as the other face of duties, the Diet appears as the place where rules are made, and fairness is taught through a story of a Vietnamese classmate facing discrimination. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
