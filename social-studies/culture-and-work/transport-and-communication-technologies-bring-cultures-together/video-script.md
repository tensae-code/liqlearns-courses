Turkey Grade 6 Sosyal Bilgiler 2. Kitap, printed p. 88, teaches: from the old vehicles and letters to today's fast transport and instant communication, each new technology has widened cultural interaction — people, goods and ideas now meet across the world in hours

Turkey's own way: the Grade 6 democracy area asks who actually moves a government — the people's needs, parties, civil society, media and the kamuoyu (public opinion) they form together — and then follows citizenship itself online, where rights are now exercised through a screen and the active citizen is the one who still writes the petition.
