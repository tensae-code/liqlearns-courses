# Video script — Parents and Children Love as the Basis of the Family

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 9 Person. Society. State, Book 2 (printed p. 86), teaches: Full family life begins with the birth of a child — children are a source of happiness and bring parents additional rights and duties, and parental love enriches the love of husband and wife; Belarus's falling birth rate and the risks of the one-child family (egoism, loneliness, conflict, and a shrinking population if all families were one-child); the kinds of love inside a family — parents' for children, children's for parents, between brothers and sisters, between spouses — and love as the family's foundation, in the book's scheme where affection of soul, mind and body gives birth to friendship, respect and desire and "the family teaches a person to live in love"; Eric Berne's three inner states — Parent (the complex of moral norms and prohibitions), Adult and Child — whose crossings and parallels explain family conflicts.
Belarus' own way: the civics track is the pupil's own state and law read from inside — "our state" is taught straight from the 1994 Constitution of the Republic of Belarus (unitary, democratic, social, law-governed; the people as the only source of power; the 1996 referendum; the 1999 Union treaty with Russia), rights are taught article by article from that Constitution (Arts. 1–3, 21–25, 30, 41–49, 53, 59–62) beside the Universal Declaration, and the course closes not with a test but with a discussion, "I — a citizen of the Republic of Belarus", on the citizen's own responsibility before the state..
