# Hands-on — Parents and Children Love as the Basis of the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Full family life begins with the birth of a child — children are a source of happiness and bring parents additional rights and duties, and parental love enriches the love of husband and wife; Belarus's falling birth rate and the risks of the one-child family (egoism, loneliness, conflict, and a shrinking population if all families were one-child); the kinds of love inside a family — parents' for children, children's for parents, between brothers and sisters, between spouses — and love as the family's foundation, in the book's scheme where affection of soul, mind and body gives birth to friendship, respect and desire and "the family teaches a person to live in love"; Eric Berne's three inner states — Parent (the complex of moral norms and prohibitions), Adult and Child — whose crossings and parallels explain family conflicts. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Belarus's way
Say how Belarus' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
