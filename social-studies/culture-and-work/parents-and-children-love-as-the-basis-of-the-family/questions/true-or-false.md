# Parents and Children Love as the Basis of the Family — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Full family life begins with the birth of a child — children are a source of happiness and bring parents additional rights and duties, and parental love enriches the love of husband and wife; Belarus's falling birth rate and the risks of the one-child family (egoism, loneliness, conflict, and a shrinking population if all families were one-child); the kinds of love inside a family — parents' for children, children's for parents, between brothers and sisters, between spouses — and love as the family's foundation, in the book's scheme where affection of soul, mind and body gives birth to friendship, respect and desire and "the family teaches a person to live in love"; Eric Berne's three inner states — Parent (the complex of moral norms and prohibitions), Adult and Child — whose crossings and parallels explain family conflicts.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Belarus' student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
