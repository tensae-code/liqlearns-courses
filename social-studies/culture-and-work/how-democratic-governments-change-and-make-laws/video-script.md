The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Papua New Guinea Grade 9 Social Science TG (printed p. 147), teaches: How democratic governments are created, maintained or changed (for example through elections); how decisions are made and laws passed in a democracy; and how citizens' demands are addressed and met through democratic government processes.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 9): Grade 9 Political Science is a full civics course built on PNG itself — democracy's principles, then democracy's PNG history from the Melanesian Way and communal land through colonial rule to the Westminster constitutional monarchy of 1975, PNG's symbols and pledge, its bilateral and multilateral relations, and citizenship that ends with the PNG Constitution as the basis of day-to-day civic life.
