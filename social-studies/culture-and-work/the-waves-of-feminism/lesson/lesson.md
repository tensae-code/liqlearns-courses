## Sub lesson 1 — Las Olas del Feminismo

Bolivia Grade 10 BO Social Sciences Sec 4 (printed p. 99), teaches: Feminism as the theory and movement claiming women's rights and equality, in its waves — the first for civil rights (work, education), the second for the vote, property and paid work, the third questioning patriarchy itself, a fourth now in the digital world — with Olympe de Gouges' 1791 Declaration of the Rights of Woman and the English suffragettes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way

Bolivia's own way: citizenship is taught as intercultural democracy — the Western forms (direct, participative, representative) completed by the indigenous peoples' own communitarian democracy, with parity and alternation between women and men written into every candidate list, and the year's closing civics arc runs from women's rights to despatriarcalización, the dismantling of patriarchy as a task of the Plurinational State itself. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
