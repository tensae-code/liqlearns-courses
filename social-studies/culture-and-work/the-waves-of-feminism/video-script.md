The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia Grade 10 BO Social Sciences Sec 4 (printed p. 99), teaches: Feminism as the theory and movement claiming women's rights and equality, in its waves — the first for civil rights (work, education), the second for the vote, property and paid work, the third questioning patriarchy itself, a fourth now in the digital world — with Olympe de Gouges' 1791 Declaration of the Rights of Woman and the English suffragettes.

Bolivia's own way: citizenship is taught as intercultural democracy — the Western forms (direct, participative, representative) completed by the indigenous peoples' own communitarian democracy, with parity and alternation between women and men written into every candidate list, and the year's closing civics arc runs from women's rights to despatriarcalización, the dismantling of patriarchy as a task of the Plurinational State itself.
