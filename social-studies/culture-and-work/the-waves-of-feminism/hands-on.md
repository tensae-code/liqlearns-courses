# Hands-on — The Waves of Feminism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: feminism as the theory and movement claiming women's rights and equality, in its waves — the first for civil rights (work, education), the second for the vote, property and paid work, the third questioning patriarchy itself, a fourth now in the digital world — with Olympe de Gouges' 1791 Declaration of the Rights of Woman and the English suffragettes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
