# Hands-on — What a Citizen is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 14 ¿Qué significa ser ciudadano?
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 110–113, teaches: being a citizen means belonging to a society with rights (to live in conditions like most Colombians, food, public services, participation) and duties; even a child is a citizen, as in the school community where pupils elect their personero. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
