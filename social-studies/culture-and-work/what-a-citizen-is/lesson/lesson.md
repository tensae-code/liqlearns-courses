# What a Citizen is

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: being a citizen means belonging to a society with rights (to live in conditions like most Colombians, food, public services, participation) and duties; even a child is a citizen, as in the school community where pupils elect their personero — as Colombia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 14 ¿Qué significa ser ciudadano?

Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 110–113, teaches: being a citizen means belonging to a society with rights (to live in conditions like most Colombians, food, public services, participation) and duties; even a child is a citizen, as in the school community where pupils elect their personero. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
