# Video script — What a Citizen is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 110–113, teaches: being a citizen means belonging to a society with rights (to live in conditions like most Colombians, food, public services, participation) and duties; even a child is a citizen, as in the school community where pupils elect their personero.

Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city.
