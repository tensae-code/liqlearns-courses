# Video script — The Regions System

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 160–161), teaches: The Regions System aims to raise administrative work and development, keep security and order and guarantee society members' rights; its history — the Emirs System of 1359H dividing the Kingdom into emirates each under an Emir representing the government, then the Interior Ministry's supervision of all the regions' emirates; the Kingdom's thirteen administrative regions, each with governorates and centres, the division regarding geography, population, environment and transport; the King appoints each region's Emir and deputy, the Emir answerable to the Interior Minister; and the Region Council — the Emir as president, his deputy, the emirate's undersecretary, the governors, the heads of government bodies in the region and a number of knowledgeable, experienced residents — which studies the region's needs and proposes them for the state's development plans.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes.
