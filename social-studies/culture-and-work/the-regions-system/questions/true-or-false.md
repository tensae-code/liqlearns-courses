# The Regions System — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The Regions System aims to raise administrative work and development, keep security and order and guarantee society members' rights; its history — the Emirs System of 1359H dividing the Kingdom into emirates each under an Emir representing the government, then the Interior Ministry's supervision of all the regions' emirates; the Kingdom's thirteen administrative regions, each with governorates and centres, the division regarding geography, population, environment and transport; the King appoints each region's Emir and deputy, the Emir answerable to the Interior Minister; and the Region Council — the Emir as president, his deputy, the emirate's undersecretary, the governors, the heads of government bodies in the region and a number of knowledgeable, experienced residents — which studies the region's needs and proposes them for the state's development plans.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Saudi Arabia's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
