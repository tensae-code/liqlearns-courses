# Hands-on — The Regions System

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Regions System aims to raise administrative work and development, keep security and order and guarantee society members' rights; its history — the Emirs System of 1359H dividing the Kingdom into emirates each under an Emir representing the government, then the Interior Ministry's supervision of all the regions' emirates; the Kingdom's thirteen administrative regions, each with governorates and centres, the division regarding geography, population, environment and transport; the King appoints each region's Emir and deputy, the Emir answerable to the Interior Minister; and the Region Council — the Emir as president, his deputy, the emirate's undersecretary, the governors, the heads of government bodies in the region and a number of knowledgeable, experienced residents — which studies the region's needs and proposes them for the state's development plans. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
