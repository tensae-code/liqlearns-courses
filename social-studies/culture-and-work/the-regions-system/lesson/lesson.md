# The Regions System

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the Regions System aims to raise administrative work and development, keep security and order and guarantee society members' rights; its history — the Emirs System of 1359H dividing the Kingdom into emirates each under an Emir representing the government, then the Interior Ministry's supervision of all the regions' emirates; the Kingdom's thirteen administrative regions, each with governorates and centres, the division regarding geography, population, environment and transport; the King appoints each region's Emir and deputy, the Emir answerable to the Interior Minister; and the Region Council — the Emir as president, his deputy, the emirate's undersecretary, the governors, the heads of government bodies in the region and a number of knowledgeable, experienced residents — which studies the region's needs and proposes them for the state's development plans — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Regions System

Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 160–161), teaches: The Regions System aims to raise administrative work and development, keep security and order and guarantee society members' rights; its history — the Emirs System of 1359H dividing the Kingdom into emirates each under an Emir representing the government, then the Interior Ministry's supervision of all the regions' emirates; the Kingdom's thirteen administrative regions, each with governorates and centres, the division regarding geography, population, environment and transport; the King appoints each region's Emir and deputy, the Emir answerable to the Interior Minister; and the Region Council — the Emir as president, his deputy, the emirate's undersecretary, the governors, the heads of government bodies in the region and a number of knowledgeable, experienced residents — which studies the region's needs and proposes them for the state's development plans. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes.
