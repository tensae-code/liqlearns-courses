# Hands-on — Sex and Gender

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sex identity (physical difference) versus gender identity (the jobs, roles and behaviour society assigns to boys and girls — toys, dress, work), and that there are no absolute rules for how boys and girls must be. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
