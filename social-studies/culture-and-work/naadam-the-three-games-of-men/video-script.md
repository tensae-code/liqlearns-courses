The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 38, teaches: The three games of men — horse racing by the horses' age classes, wrestling with its ranked titles (аварга, арслан, гарьд, заан, харцага, начин), and archery shot from 75–80 metres with its own ladder of marksman titles (мэргэн); the cries and gear of the festival — the гийнгоо, the уухай, the wrestler's outfit.

Mongolia's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact.
