# Naadam the Three Games of Men — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the three games of men — horse racing by the horses' age classes, wrestling with its ranked titles (аварга, арслан, гарьд, заан, харцага, начин), and archery shot from 75–80 metres with its own ladder of marksman titles (мэргэн); the cries and gear of the festival — the гийнгоо, the уухай, the wrestler's outfit.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
