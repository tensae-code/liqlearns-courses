## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 12 "Mongolian Naadam" (Монгол наадам)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 2, printed p. 38, teaches: The three games of men — horse racing by the horses' age classes, wrestling with its ranked titles (аварга, арслан, гарьд, заан, харцага, начин), and archery shot from 75–80 metres with its own ladder of marksman titles (мэргэн); the cries and gear of the festival — the гийнгоо, the уухай, the wrestler's outfit. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
