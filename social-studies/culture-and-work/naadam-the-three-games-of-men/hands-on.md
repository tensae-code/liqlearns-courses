# Hands-on — Naadam the Three Games of Men

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 12 "Mongolian Naadam" (Монгол наадам)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The three games of men — horse racing by the horses' age classes, wrestling with its ranked titles (аварга, арслан, гарьд, заан, харцага, начин), and archery shot from 75–80 metres with its own ladder of marksman titles (мэргэн); the cries and gear of the festival — the гийнгоо, the уухай, the wrestler's outfit. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
