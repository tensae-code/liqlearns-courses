# Hands-on — Democracy and Taking Part

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Democracy (الديمقراطية)
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 7 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 1AC, printed p. 177, teaches: what democracy means and how citizens take part in public life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: collège Social Studies carries a full Citizenship Education component built on ten value lessons in two axes — five personal-social values (dignity, freedom, equality, justice, solidarity), then tolerance, democracy, peace, the role of law, and a field visit to a human-rights institution — each lesson built from constitutional and international-charter texts. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
