# Hands-on — Why We Need Norms and Authorities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 3 opening, "¿Son necesarias las normas y las autoridades para la vida?"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 6 Ciencias Sociales Segundo ciclo TG, printed p. 60, teaches: the concepts of norm, authority and sanction, gathered from adults and built into a concept map — why a community cannot live without norms and recognised authorities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: coexistence is taught from the Maya cosmovision — its four threads are respect, gratitude, service and care for nature — and classroom rules are not imposed but built by the pupils as agreements.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
