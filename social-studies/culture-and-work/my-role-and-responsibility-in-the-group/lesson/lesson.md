# My Role and Responsibility in the Group

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: every group member's role and responsibility — no role, no group — as Japan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — (3) 自分の役割を自覚して (Knowing my own role)

Japan Grades 5–6 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 140), teaches: Every group member's role and responsibility — no role, no group. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — 読みもの「小川笙船」("Ogawa Shosen")

Japan Grades 5–6 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 146), teaches: The Edo doctor who treated the poor — a role carried for others (reading). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Japan's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 3): the older pupils' reader moves from self-discipline to society in four widening units — polish yourself, connect with people, cherish life, connect with everyone — and teaches civics inside moral stories: rights are introduced as the other face of duties, the Diet appears as the place where rules are made, and fairness is taught through a story of a Vietnamese classmate facing discrimination. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
