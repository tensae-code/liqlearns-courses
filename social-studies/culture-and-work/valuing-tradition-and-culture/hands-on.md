# Hands-on — Valuing Tradition and Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Japan's traditions handed down — kimono, Japanese food and rooms, the year's festivals (Hinamatsuri, Tanabata) — and Koizumi Yakumo (Lafcadio Hearn), who was drawn to Japanese culture. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
