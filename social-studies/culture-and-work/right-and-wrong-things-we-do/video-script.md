# Video script — Right and Wrong Things We Do

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 4 P4 Social Studies SB, printed p. 77, teaches: right things are good things and wrong things are bad things, judged in a community table — taking someone's property without permission, gossiping, not greeting the elders, making noise in class and drug abuse are wrong; helping parents at home and working hard in class are right.

South Sudan's own way: Law enters as the rules each community governs itself by, with arrest and the court as its pictures; the moral half of the conflict unit is a right/wrong table judged by community eyes — gossiping and not greeting elders are wrongs alongside stealing — and the unit closes on resisting negative peer pressure, with drug abuse named as the Health-course part (named here, not placed).
