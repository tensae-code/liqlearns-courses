# Hands-on — Right and Wrong Things We Do

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 5: right and wrong
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: right things are good things and wrong things are bad things, judged in a community table — taking someone's property without permission, gossiping, not greeting the elders, making noise in class and drug abuse are wrong; helping parents at home and working hard in class are right. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Sudan's way
Say how South Sudan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
