# Hands-on — Elections to State and Community Bodies and the Referendum in Armenia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: direct and indirect elections, elections classified by object (parliamentary, presidential, local), elections to Armenia's state and community bodies, and the referendum. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
