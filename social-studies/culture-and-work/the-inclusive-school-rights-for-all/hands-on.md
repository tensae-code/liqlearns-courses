# Hands-on — The Inclusive School Rights for All

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what an inclusive school is against the texts giving every child the fundamental rights, a critical audit of how the state, administrations and citizens actually respect those rights and duties, and the conditions needed to set inclusive schooling up. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
