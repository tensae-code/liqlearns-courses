## Sub lesson 1 — The Inclusive School Rights for All

Haiti Grade 8 EC2 8e Droits devoirs valeurs (printed p. 17), teaches: what an inclusive school is against the texts giving every child the fundamental rights, a critical audit of how the state, administrations and citizens actually respect those rights and duties, and the conditions needed to set inclusive schooling up. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way

Haiti's own way: Rights are taught from a Haitian street-level case — out-of-school street children and restavèk children — upward: case first, then the positions of officials and press, then the constitutional and international texts, then a school project that practises the right. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
