# Video script — The Inclusive School Rights for All

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 8 EC2 8e Droits devoirs valeurs (printed p. 17), teaches: what an inclusive school is against the texts giving every child the fundamental rights, a critical audit of how the state, administrations and citizens actually respect those rights and duties, and the conditions needed to set inclusive schooling up.

Haiti's own way: Rights are taught from a Haitian street-level case — out-of-school street children and restavèk children — upward: case first, then the positions of officials and press, then the constitutional and international texts, then a school project that practises the right.
