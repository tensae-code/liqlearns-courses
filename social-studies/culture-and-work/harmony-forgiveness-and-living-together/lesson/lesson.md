# Harmony Forgiveness and Living Together

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: harmony is made by people — respect what others do and think, master your feelings, reject aggression; forgive, apologise after recognising your errors, give, share, be kind, grateful, supportive and treat everyone equally; yield and dialogue when others' ideas are as valuable as yours — as Honduras's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way

Honduras's own way: Grade 2 turns civics municipal — the child learns the Corporación Municipal's full membership, the open town meeting (cabildo abierto), a citizen transparency commission, and the country's own Citizen Participation Law with its three instruments (cabildo abierto, plebiscite, citizen initiatives).
