# Harmony Forgiveness and Living Together — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: harmony is made by people — respect what others do and think, master your feelings, reject aggression; forgive, apologise after recognising your errors, give, share, be kind, grateful, supportive and treat everyone equally; yield and dialogue when others' ideas are as valuable as yours.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
