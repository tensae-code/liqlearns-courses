**Answer: Lección 3 Vivir en armonía — Harmony is made by people — respect what others do and think, master your feelings, reject aggression; forgive, apologise after recognising your errors, give, share, be kind, grateful, supportive and treat everyone equally; yield and dialogue when others' ideas are as valuable as yours (any close wording).**
