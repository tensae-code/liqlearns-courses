# Video script — Harmony Forgiveness and Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras Grade 2 G2 Student CCSS, printed p. 18, teaches: Harmony is made by people — respect what others do and think, master your feelings, reject aggression; forgive, apologise after recognising your errors, give, share, be kind, grateful, supportive and treat everyone equally; yield and dialogue when others' ideas are as valuable as yours.

Honduras's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 turns civics municipal — the child learns the Corporación Municipal's full membership, the open town meeting (cabildo abierto), a citizen transparency commission, and the country's own Citizen Participation Law with its three instruments (cabildo abierto, plebiscite, citizen initiatives).
