# Hands-on — Harmony Forgiveness and Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 3 Vivir en armonía
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Harmony is made by people — respect what others do and think, master your feelings, reject aggression; forgive, apologise after recognising your errors, give, share, be kind, grateful, supportive and treat everyone equally; yield and dialogue when others' ideas are as valuable as yours. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
