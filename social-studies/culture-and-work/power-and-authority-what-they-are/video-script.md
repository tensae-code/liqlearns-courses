# Video script — Power and Authority What They Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Canada Social Studies 5 IRSR Guide (printed p. 26), teaches: Power and authority in a child's own life — who decides at home, in class and in school, and how a person's power over their own decisions grows as they grow older.

Canada's own way: The Grade 5 guide opens with the land and the family as the first teachers, before the school system is named — reconciliation at this level begins from respect for the land and learning handed down the generations.
