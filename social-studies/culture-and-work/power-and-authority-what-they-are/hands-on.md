# Hands-on — Power and Authority What They Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Power and authority in a child's own life — who decides at home, in class and in school, and how a person's power over their own decisions grows as they grow older. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Canada's way
Say how Canada's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
