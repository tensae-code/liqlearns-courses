# Hands-on — Rights and Duties Change with Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 1 Change in Rights and Responsibilities with the Passage of Time, Ch. 1 Change in Rights and Responsibilities with Passage of Time — teaches: rights and duties are not fixed — when Pakistan was founded the needs were basic (food, water, housing); as the country grew, new rights and duties arrived with schools, technology and the digital age; lifestyles change with time, so rights and duties change too — from the refugee-settling hardships of 1947 to today's banking, roads, housing and energy; the 21st century has made the world a global village and its people digital citizens.
