# Video script — Rights and Duties Change with Time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pakistan's Grade 5 SS5 (printed p. 3); Grade 5 SS5-KPK (printed p. 3), teaches: Ch. 1 Change in Rights and Responsibilities with the Passage of Time, Ch. 1 Change in Rights and Responsibilities with Passage of Time — teaches: rights and duties are not fixed — when Pakistan was founded the needs were basic (food, water, housing); as the country grew, new rights and duties arrived with schools, technology and the digital age; lifestyles change with time, so rights and duties change too — from the refugee-settling hardships of 1947 to today's banking, roads, housing and energy; the 21st century has made the world a global village and its people digital citizens.

Pakistan's own way: Class 5 deepens Class 4 — rights now change with time, the UN and the 1948 Declaration enter, freedom of speech gets its own lesson with John Stuart Mill quoted, and government is compared across federal, provincial AND local levels with a table of who does what. the KPK Class 5 book gives the citizen's charter in two plain lists — seven rights (ending in the right to vote) against nine responsibilities (ending in honest dealing) — and makes Abdul Sattar Edhi, who "served the suffering humanity", its model of what a responsible citizen is.
