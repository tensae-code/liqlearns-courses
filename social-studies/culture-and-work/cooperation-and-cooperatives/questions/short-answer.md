# Cooperation and Cooperatives — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Iskaashiga iyo Iskaashatooyinka (Cooperation and the Cooperatives) — people join in cooperatives to pool knowledge and tools, raise their production and learn — the cooperative as the school of working together (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic. (any close wording).**
