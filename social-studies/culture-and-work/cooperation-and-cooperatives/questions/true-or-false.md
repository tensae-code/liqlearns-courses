# Cooperation and Cooperatives — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Iskaashiga iyo Iskaashatooyinka (Cooperation and the Cooperatives)" teaches: people join in cooperatives to pool knowledge and tools, raise their production and learn — the cooperative as the school of working together.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic..
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
