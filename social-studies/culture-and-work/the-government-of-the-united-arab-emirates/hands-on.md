# Hands-on — The Government of the United Arab Emirates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The government
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the tasks of the government (مهام الحكومة) and the shape of the government structure; of whom the Supreme Council of the Union (المجلس الأعلى للاتحاد) is made; who the President of the Union is and who his deputy is; the competences of the Council of Ministers (اختصاصات مجلس الوزراء); why the Federal National Council (المجلس الوطني الاتحادي) matters; how the Emirates' government works to give its people a decent life (سبل العيش الكريم); and growing loyalty and belonging through respect for the state's government Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
