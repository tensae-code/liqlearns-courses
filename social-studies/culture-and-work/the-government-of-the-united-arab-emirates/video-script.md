# Video script — The Government of the United Arab Emirates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G4 teacher guide, volume 1, printed p. 129, teaches: the tasks of the government (مهام الحكومة) and the shape of the government structure; of whom the Supreme Council of the Union (المجلس الأعلى للاتحاد) is made; who the President of the Union is and who his deputy is; the competences of the Council of Ministers (اختصاصات مجلس الوزراء); why the Federal National Council (المجلس الوطني الاتحادي) matters; how the Emirates' government works to give its people a decent life (سبل العيش الكريم); and growing loyalty and belonging through respect for the state's government

United Arab Emirates' own way: the fourth-grade guide teaches its civics as a set of named concepts first — citizenship, belonging, participation, responsibility, identity — and then walks them into the child's world in order: the family he lives in, the government that runs his state, and the martyrs the state honours.
