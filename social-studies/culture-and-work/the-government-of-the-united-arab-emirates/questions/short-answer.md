# The Government of the United Arab Emirates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The government — the tasks of the government (مهام الحكومة) and the shape of the government structure; of whom the Supreme Council of the Union (المجلس الأعلى للاتحاد) is made; who the President of the Union is and who his deputy is; the competences of the Council of Ministers (اختصاصات مجلس الوزراء); why the Federal National Council (المجلس الوطني الاتحادي) matters; how the Emirates' government works to give its people a decent life (سبل العيش الكريم); and growing loyalty and belonging through respect for the state's government (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — the fourth-grade guide teaches its civics as a set of named concepts first — citizenship, belonging, participation, responsibility, identity — and then walks them into the child's world in order: the family he lives in, the government that runs his state, and the martyrs the state honours. (any close wording).**
