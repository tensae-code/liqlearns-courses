## Sub lesson 1 — Ama Qhilla, Ama Llulla, Ama Suwa

Bolivia Grade 8 BO Social Sciences Sec 2 (printed p. 16), teaches: The ancestral Quechua laws of living together, adopted as principles of the Plurinational State (CPE Article 8) — Ama Suwa (do not steal: honesty and transparency), Ama Llulla (do not lie: truth makes us free), Ama Qhilla (do not be lazy: love of work as the key to success) — beside suma qamaña (living well), ñandereco, teko kavi, ivi maraei and qhapaj ñan. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way

Bolivia's own way: the constitution itself is taught as the home of the ancestors' moral law — the Plurinational State's founding values are the Quechua Ama Qhilla, Ama Llulla, Ama Suwa and the other peoples' suma qamaña, ñandereco, teko kavi, ivi maraei and qhapaj ñan, written into CPE Article 8. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
