# Ama Qhilla Ama Llulla Ama Suwa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ama Qhilla, Ama Llulla, Ama Suwa — the ancestral Quechua laws of living together, adopted as principles of the Plurinational State (CPE Article 8) — Ama Suwa (do not steal: honesty and transparency), Ama Llulla (do not lie: truth makes us free), Ama Qhilla (do not be lazy: love of work as the key to success) — beside suma qamaña (living well), ñandereco, teko kavi, ivi maraei and qhapaj ñan (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way — the constitution itself is taught as the home of the ancestors' moral law — the Plurinational State's founding values are the Quechua Ama Qhilla, Ama Llulla, Ama Suwa and the other peoples' suma qamaña, ñandereco, teko kavi, ivi maraei and qhapaj ñan, written into CPE Article 8 (any close wording).**
