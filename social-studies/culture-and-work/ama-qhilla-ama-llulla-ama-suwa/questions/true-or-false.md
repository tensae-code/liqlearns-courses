# Ama Qhilla Ama Llulla Ama Suwa — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The ancestral Quechua laws of living together, adopted as principles of the Plurinational State (CPE Article 8) — Ama Suwa (do not steal: honesty and transparency), Ama Llulla (do not lie: truth makes us free), Ama Qhilla (do not be lazy: love of work as the key to success) — beside suma qamaña (living well), ñandereco, teko kavi, ivi maraei and qhapaj ñan.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Bolivia's Grade 8 student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
