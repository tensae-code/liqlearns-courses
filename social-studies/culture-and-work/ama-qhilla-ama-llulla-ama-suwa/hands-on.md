# Hands-on — Ama Qhilla Ama Llulla Ama Suwa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the ancestral Quechua laws of living together, adopted as principles of the Plurinational State (CPE Article 8) — Ama Suwa (do not steal: honesty and transparency), Ama Llulla (do not lie: truth makes us free), Ama Qhilla (do not be lazy: love of work as the key to success) — beside suma qamaña (living well), ñandereco, teko kavi, ivi maraei and qhapaj ñan. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
