Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study (printed p. 29), teaches: the duties people carry at home and in the district, and being a responsible person in the community. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Uganda's way — the book's own teaching way (Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study)

Uganda's own way: the district is the child's civic unit — its leaders (LCV chairperson, CAO, RDC, DPC, DEO, DAO) are named by title and role, and children's rights are taught straight from Uganda's laws (a child is anyone below 18). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
