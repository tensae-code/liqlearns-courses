# Hands-on — Responsibilities of People in Our District

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the duties people carry at home and in the district, and being a responsible person in the community. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Uganda's way
Say how Uganda's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
