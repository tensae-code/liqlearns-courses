# Hands-on — Love of Homeland Belonging and Loyalty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What love of homeland, belonging and loyalty mean, and the good citizen's values — honesty, trustworthiness, sacrifice, sincerity, cooperation and respect for order. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahrain's way
Say how Bahrain's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
