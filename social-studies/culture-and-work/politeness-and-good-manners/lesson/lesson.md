Sri Lanka's own way: civics starts inside the child's own school — its history, its people and their duties, its rules, symbols and the pupil's own rights — then widens to the region (the Grama Niladhari ladder up to the province, the officers and councils that serve it) and closes with the good citizen's virtues: polite speech, listening, discipline, telling good from bad, using resources frugally, guarding public property and preserving the local identity with pride.
