# Politeness and Good Manners — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: 3.1 Practicing polite language — polite speech as a citizen's habit — its features (discipline in speech, clear ideas, respect, listening patiently, eye contact), fitting words to the person, place and occasion, and what polite and impolite language each earn (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way — civics starts inside the child's own school — its history, its people and their duties, its rules, symbols and the pupil's own rights — then widens to the region (the Grama Niladhari ladder up to the province, the officers and councils that serve it) and closes with the good citizen's virtues: polite speech, listening, discipline, telling good from bad, using resources frugally, guarding public property and preserving the local identity with pride. (any close wording).**
