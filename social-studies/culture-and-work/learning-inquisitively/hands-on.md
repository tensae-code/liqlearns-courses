# Hands-on — Learning Inquisitively

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Learning Inquisitively
Work through this part with your hands: learning by curiosity — using the environment to obtain information, testing concepts to prove the truth, evaluating your own learning ability and cooperating with others in learning. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
