# Hands-on — Cultural Diversity and Inclusion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Museums in the UAE and Exhibits in museums
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the unit's key words defined — cultural diversity (التعددية الثقافية), diversity (التنوع) and inclusion (الإدماج) — and practised: a boy visiting the Sharjah museum concludes that today's Emirati society is a mixture of people from all over the world, joined to them and respecting their cultures, and a class visit to another culture's exhibits teaches respect for other cultures and the shared rule of not harming others Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
