# Cultural Diversity and Inclusion — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the unit's key words defined — cultural diversity (التعددية الثقافية), diversity (التنوع) and inclusion (الإدماج) — and practised: a boy visiting the Sharjah museum concludes that today's Emirati society is a mixture of people from all over the world, joined to them and respecting their cultures, and a class visit to another culture's exhibits teaches respect for other cultures and the shared rule of not harming others
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: the federation's diversity is taught as a museum subject — the child learns the words cultural diversity and inclusion by walking the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization, and the story lesson ends on the line that Emirati society today is a mixture of people from all over the world, bound to them and respecting their cultures.
**Answer: True**
