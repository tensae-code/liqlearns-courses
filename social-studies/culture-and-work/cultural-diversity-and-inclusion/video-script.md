# Video script — Cultural Diversity and Inclusion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G6 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 37, teaches: the unit's key words defined — cultural diversity (التعددية الثقافية), diversity (التنوع) and inclusion (الإدماج) — and practised: a boy visiting the Sharjah museum concludes that today's Emirati society is a mixture of people from all over the world, joined to them and respecting their cultures, and a class visit to another culture's exhibits teaches respect for other cultures and the shared rule of not harming others

United Arab Emirates' own way: the federation's diversity is taught as a museum subject — the child learns the words cultural diversity and inclusion by walking the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization, and the story lesson ends on the line that Emirati society today is a mixture of people from all over the world, bound to them and respecting their cultures.
