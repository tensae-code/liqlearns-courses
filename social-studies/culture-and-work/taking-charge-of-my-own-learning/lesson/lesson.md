## Sub lesson 2 — Eager to learn

Vietnam Grade 3 Đạo đức 3, printed pp. 22–25, teaches: Loving inquiry — asking boldly when you do not understand, reading to widen knowledge, learning in a group, asking about everything around you — with the story of Nguyễn Hiền, the poor herd-boy who listened outside the classroom and became the youngest trạng nguyên (the slug was proposed as NEW in Book 1 of this file; counted here as an existing target). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: Đạo đức (Ethics) is Vietnam's standalone primary moral-education subject; every lesson runs the same four moves — a song to open, picture stories to explore, practice pictures to judge, a role-play to apply — and closes with a two-line rhyme that states the duty; the family is taught three-generational (ông bà – cha mẹ – anh chị em) and school rules are taught as the child's first shared rules. Grade 3 opens the Ethics course with the state itself — the country's full official name, its flag and its anthem are taught as things a child must know and honour standing still — before widening to neighbours, promises and quarrels; traffic safety returns as a full closing theme, now split into walking rules and rules for riding in vehicles. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
