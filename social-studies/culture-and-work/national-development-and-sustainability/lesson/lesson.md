# National Development and Sustainability

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: development as the continuous improvement of the homeland at the economic, social and cultural levels, the government investing resources to serve citizens through development programmes, with Vision 2030 achieving it actively and permanently; its fields — human development (social stability, care and skills), economic development (encouraging work, production and new means for the economy's sectors), health, education, social affairs, industry, agriculture and infrastructure; sustainable development as developing and investing resources while regarding future generations' needs, guarding the resources and avoiding waste; the Council of Economic and Development Affairs chaired by the Crown Prince, and the citizen's role in achieving sustainable development — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — National Development and Sustainability

Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 100–102), teaches: Development as the continuous improvement of the homeland at the economic, social and cultural levels, the government investing resources to serve citizens through development programmes, with Vision 2030 achieving it actively and permanently; its fields — human development (social stability, care and skills), economic development (encouraging work, production and new means for the economy's sectors), health, education, social affairs, industry, agriculture and infrastructure; sustainable development as developing and investing resources while regarding future generations' needs, guarding the resources and avoiding waste; the Council of Economic and Development Affairs chaired by the Crown Prince, and the citizen's role in achieving sustainable development. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
