# Hands-on — National Development and Sustainability

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: development as the continuous improvement of the homeland at the economic, social and cultural levels, the government investing resources to serve citizens through development programmes, with Vision 2030 achieving it actively and permanently; its fields — human development (social stability, care and skills), economic development (encouraging work, production and new means for the economy's sectors), health, education, social affairs, industry, agriculture and infrastructure; sustainable development as developing and investing resources while regarding future generations' needs, guarding the resources and avoiding waste; the Council of Economic and Development Affairs chaired by the Crown Prince, and the citizen's role in achieving sustainable development. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
