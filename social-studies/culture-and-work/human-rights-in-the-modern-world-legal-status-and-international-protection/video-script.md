# Video script — Human Rights in the Modern World Legal Status and International Protection

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 9 Person. Society. State, Book 2 (printed p. 172), teaches: The legal status of the citizen — the person as a subject and bearer of law and a participant in legal relations, the ideal of the free personality; the principle of the formal equality of different people; the two theories that root that status — the theory of natural law and the theory of the social contract, by which people enter a civil union to secure their safety, freedom, property and happiness; and the modern system of protection — the International Bill of Human Rights (the Universal Declaration and the two 1966 Covenants), the UN's human-rights machinery and the European system (the European Convention and the European Social Charter) — human rights as "the foundations of the whole legal order".
Belarus' own way: the civics track is the pupil's own state and law read from inside — "our state" is taught straight from the 1994 Constitution of the Republic of Belarus (unitary, democratic, social, law-governed; the people as the only source of power; the 1996 referendum; the 1999 Union treaty with Russia), rights are taught article by article from that Constitution (Arts. 1–3, 21–25, 30, 41–49, 53, 59–62) beside the Universal Declaration, and the course closes not with a test but with a discussion, "I — a citizen of the Republic of Belarus", on the citizen's own responsibility before the state..
