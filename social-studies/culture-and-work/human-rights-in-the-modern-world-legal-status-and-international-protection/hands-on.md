# Hands-on — Human Rights in the Modern World Legal Status and International Protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The legal status of the citizen — the person as a subject and bearer of law and a participant in legal relations, the ideal of the free personality; the principle of the formal equality of different people; the two theories that root that status — the theory of natural law and the theory of the social contract, by which people enter a civil union to secure their safety, freedom, property and happiness; and the modern system of protection — the International Bill of Human Rights (the Universal Declaration and the two 1966 Covenants), the UN's human-rights machinery and the European system (the European Convention and the European Social Charter) — human rights as "the foundations of the whole legal order". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
