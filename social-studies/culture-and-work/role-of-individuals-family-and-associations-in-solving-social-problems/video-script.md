# Video script — Role of Individuals Family and Associations in Solving Social Problems

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal Grade 4 Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha (trial) (printed p. 77), teaches: Local bodies as the solvers of local problems.

Nepal Grade 6 Social Studies and Human Value Education (printed p. 80), teaches: Who does the solving.

Nepal Grade 7 Social Studies and Human Value Education (printed p. 76), teaches: What organisations do about social problems.

Nepal's own way: the trial Human Value Education edition re-teaches Grade 4 social studies through values first — forgiveness is a named lesson, and civic awareness opens with our security and our rights-and-duties together. Grade 6 is where the federation becomes concrete — rural municipality against municipality, each with its services listed — and where conflict itself becomes a school subject: a lesson on disputes and a lesson on resolving them close the social-problems unit. Grade 7 completes the state — elements of the state, democratic government, our constitution, human and women's rights, the provincial government's functions and the security agencies — then turns outward: the United Nations, the proper use of social media, and the world's current events close the book.
