## Sub lesson 4 — Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way

Nepal's own way: the trial Human Value Education edition re-teaches Grade 4 social studies through values first — forgiveness is a named lesson, and civic awareness opens with our security and our rights-and-duties together. Grade 6 is where the federation becomes concrete — rural municipality against municipality, each with its services listed — and where conflict itself becomes a school subject: a lesson on disputes and a lesson on resolving them close the social-problems unit. Grade 7 completes the state — elements of the state, democratic government, our constitution, human and women's rights, the provincial government's functions and the security agencies — then turns outward: the United Nations, the proper use of social media, and the world's current events close the book. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
