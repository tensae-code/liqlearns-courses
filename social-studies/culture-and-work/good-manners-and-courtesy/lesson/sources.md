Nicaragua's own way (Book 2 — Convivencia y Civismo G2 (student book, Spanish)): civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 3 — Convivencia y Civismo G3 (student book, Spanish)): Grade 3 civics adds Nicaragua-specific law to the values base — women's rights under Ley 779 and a Mother Earth declaration — and respect for public and private institutions as a civic duty.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 6 — Convivencia y Civismo G4 (student book, Spanish)): Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State.
