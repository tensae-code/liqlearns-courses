# Hands-on — Good Manners and Courtesy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book’s topic
Work through this lesson’s topic with your hands: Greetings and polite behaviour at home, school and with relatives, practised through the "magic words" (please, sorry, thank you). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mauritius’s way
Say how Mauritius’s book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
