## Sub lesson 2 — Pakistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Pakistan's own way: at Class 10 the state tells its story whole — ideology first, then the society it made: thirteen named features of Pakistani culture, minorities' rights written into the culture chapter, and a full closing chapter on women's rights, women nation-builders and the laws that protect them. the English-medium Class 9 book states the ideology in plain definitions, quotes Jinnah's promise that minorities "are free to go to your places of worship", and closes with the women's chapter in its sharpest form — myth-versus-fact boxes that refuse to blame victims.
