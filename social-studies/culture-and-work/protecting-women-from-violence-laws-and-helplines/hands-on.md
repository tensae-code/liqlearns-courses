# Hands-on — Protecting Women from Violence Laws and Helplines

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 8 Violence Against Women: Definition, Constitutional Provisions and Government Efforts, Ch. 4 Violence Against Women: Myths and Facts, Laws and Government Efforts — teaches: what counts as violence, what the constitution promises, and the machinery built to protect — the Punjab Marriage Restraint Act 2015, the Punjab Protection of Women Against Violence Act 2016, district protection officers and committees, and the 1043 helpline; CEDAW is named; the myths are answered one by one (violence is only the offender's fault; it happens inside homes too), then the machinery — Punjab Marriage Restraint Act 2015, Protection of Women Against Violence Act 2016, district Violence Against Women Centres, DWPO rescue teams, helpline 1043 and SMS 8787 — and a "Do you know?" box on the Universal Declaration of Human Rights and CEDAW. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
