# Hands-on — National and Confessional Policy in a Multinational State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Belarus as a land of interethnic and interconfessional accord — about half the adults believers, Orthodoxy and Catholicism the largest confessions beside some 1,000 Protestant communities and Jewish and Muslim associations, with religious tolerance and no conflicts on national or religious grounds; the state's national policy — the 1990 Law on Languages, the 1995 referendum giving Russian equal status with Belarusian, ethnic tolerance (about two-thirds of Belarusians have relatives of other nationalities) — as a condition of the country's stable development. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
