# National and Confessional Policy in a Multinational State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: National and confessional policy (Национальная и конфессиональная политика) — Grade 11 — Belarus as a land of interethnic and interconfessional accord — about half the adults believers, Orthodoxy and Catholicism the largest confessions beside some 1,000 Protestant communities and Jewish and Muslim associations, with religious tolerance and no conflicts on national or religious grounds; the state's national policy — the 1990 Law on Languages, the 1995 referendum giving Russian equal status with Belarusian, ethnic tolerance (about two-thirds of Belarusians have relatives of other nationalities) — as a condition of the country's stable development (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics. (any close wording).**
