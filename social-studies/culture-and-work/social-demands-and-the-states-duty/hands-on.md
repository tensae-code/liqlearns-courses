# Hands-on — Social Demands and the States Duty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 38 Demandas sociales
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Hondurans demand of the State health, work, education, housing, security, care for children and families, roads and ports; everyone has the right to ask, request or claim (demandar) that needs be met, and the State is obliged to listen and satisfy them through its secretariats, institutions and laws; citizens also cooperate — through water boards and the patronato, demands reach the Alcaldía. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 37 Necesidades de la población centroamericana
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Central America's population needs — poverty and malnutrition rising (FAO and the regional food-security programme), corruption denying citizens opportunities and deepening inequality, youth exposed to transnational gangs; the answers taught are society organising itself, opening study and work opportunities, and access to school and university for all, with indigenous peoples' demands on the State included. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
