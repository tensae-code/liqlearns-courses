# Social Demands and the States Duty — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: hondurans demand of the State health, work, education, housing, security, care for children and families, roads and ports; everyone has the right to ask, request or claim (demandar) that needs be met, and the State is obliged to listen and satisfy them through its secretariats, institutions and laws; citizens also cooperate — through water boards and the patronato, demands reach the Alcaldía.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: central America's population needs — poverty and malnutrition rising (FAO and the regional food-security programme), corruption denying citizens opportunities and deepening inequality, youth exposed to transnational gangs; the answers taught are society organising itself, opening study and work opportunities, and access to school and university for all, with indigenous peoples' demands on the State included.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
