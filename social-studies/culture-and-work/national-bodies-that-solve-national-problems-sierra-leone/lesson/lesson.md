## Sub lesson 1 — National Bodies That Solve National Problems Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone's SL SS BECE JSS3 teacher guide (printed p. 100), teaches: The Sierra Leone Red Cross, NARECOM (National Rehabilitation Committee, 1997) and the Sierra Leone Human Rights Commission (Act of Parliament 2004 — monitors violations, promotes human rights) and what each does. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sierra Leone's way — the book's own teaching way

Sierra Leone's own way: the whole syllabus is taught through Sierra Leone's own peoples and institutions — culture is taught ethnic group by ethnic group (Mende, Temne, Limba, Krio…: dress, music, dance, craft, secret societies), and global bodies are taught through what they have done for Sierra Leone itself (UN Special Court, Sierra Leone as the UN's 100th member in 1961). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
