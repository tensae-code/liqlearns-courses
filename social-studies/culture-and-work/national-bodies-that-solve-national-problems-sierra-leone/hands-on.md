# Hands-on — National Bodies That Solve National Problems Sierra Leone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Sierra Leone Red Cross, NARECOM (National Rehabilitation Committee, 1997) and the Sierra Leone Human Rights Commission (Act of Parliament 2004 — monitors violations, promotes human rights) and what each does. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
