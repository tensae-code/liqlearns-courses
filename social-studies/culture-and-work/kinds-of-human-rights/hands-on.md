# Hands-on — Kinds of Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The three generations of human rights, in the book's scheme — the first, civil and political rights, proclaimed in the bourgeois revolutions of the 18th century; the second, social-economic rights, which arose from the workers' and socialist movement of the 19th – early 20th century; the third, social-cultural rights, forming under the influence of scientific-technical progress; and, joining them in the 20th century, the collective rights of solidarity — of peoples, communities and states (self-determination, disposal of their own natural resources, a clean environment); modern international legal standards — human rights as the subject of international law on an equal footing with the state, from the Universal Declaration to the International Bill (1948, 1966) and the 1990 Paris Charter for a New Europe. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
