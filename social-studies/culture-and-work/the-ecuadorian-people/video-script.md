# Video script — The Ecuadorian People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After the land, Grade 3 turns to its people: la gente ecuatoriana — the Ecuadorian people — who they are and where they live across the country's regions and parishes. The lesson sits in the book's journey through Ecuador (Un viaje por el Ecuador), beside the natural and cultural heritage topic, so the people are learned as part of the country itself, not as an afterthought.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the older SM edition is a classic skills-first book — a whole opening unit on orientation, cardinal points, the compass rose and plans before any content; every unit runs the same skeleton (content lessons, “Ser Estudiante” test, Cultura del Buen Vivir, scientific work, digital skills, unit evaluation), and the country is learned parish → canton → province → nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
