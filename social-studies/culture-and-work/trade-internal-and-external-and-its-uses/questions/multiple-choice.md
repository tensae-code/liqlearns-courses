# Trade — internal and external trade, and its uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Internal trade happens —
a) Between countries  b) Inside the country  c) Only at ports  d) Only by barter
**Answer: b) Inside the country**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** External trade's two faces in the student book are —
a) Tax and revenue  b) Imports and exports  c) Barter and money  d) Roads and ships
**Answer: b) Imports and exports**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The earliest form of trade (teacher guide — evidence) was —
a) Banking  b) Entrepot  c) Bartering — article for article of equal value  d) Online selling
**Answer: c) Bartering — article for article of equal value**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's third face of external trade is —
a) Entrepot trade  b) Smuggling  c) Tithe trade  d) Local trade
**Answer: a) Entrepot trade**
