# Trade — internal and external trade, and its uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four uses of trade.
**Answer:** Any four of: getting what people need through the market; carrying goods from surplus to scarcity for profit; creating jobs and division of labour; pushing production for the world market; pushing quality and competitiveness; substituting own products and cutting foreign pressure; moving knowledge, skill and technology.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name trade's four preconditions.
**Answer:** Different products in different places; over-production here and under-production there; demand for raw materials; adequate communication services.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is entrepot trade's place in the guide's account?
**Answer:** It is the third face of external trade, alongside import and export (teacher guide — evidence).
