# Trade — internal and external trade, and its uses — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Internal trade's size depends on the country's area and its transport and communication.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ethiopia's exports are keyed as dominated by agricultural products such as coffee.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade can move knowledge and technology between countries.
**Answer: True**
