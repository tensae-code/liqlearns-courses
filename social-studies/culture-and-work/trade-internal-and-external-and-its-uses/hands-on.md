# Hands-on — Trade — internal and external trade, and its uses

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Sort your town's trade: which exchanges are internal, which cross the country's border as imports or exports? Write three of each.

## Activity 2
Barter once, on paper: swap one thing your household has in surplus for one it lacks, at equal value, with no money. Write both sides.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Tell internal from external trade, and name external trade's faces — two in the student book, three in the guide (teacher guide — evidence). Name four uses of trade and its four preconditions. Say what barter is and where it stands in trade's history (teacher guide — evidence).
