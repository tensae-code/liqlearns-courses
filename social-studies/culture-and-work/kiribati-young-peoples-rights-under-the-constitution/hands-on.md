# Hands-on — Kiribati Young Peoples Rights Under the Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 3 Kiribati Youth and the Community (rights background) — teaches: the 1980 Constitution as supreme law, its Chapter II fundamental rights and freedoms applying to young people too, the responsibilities that go with them, and the vote at 18 for the Maneaba ni Maungatabu. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kiribati's way
Say how Kiribati's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
