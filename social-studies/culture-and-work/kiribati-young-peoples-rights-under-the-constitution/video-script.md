# Video script — Kiribati Young Peoples Rights Under the Constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kiribati Year 7 Social Science TG (printed p. 14), teaches: Unit 3 Kiribati Youth and the Community (rights background) — teaches: the 1980 Constitution as supreme law, its Chapter II fundamental rights and freedoms applying to young people too, the responsibilities that go with them, and the vote at 18 for the Maneaba ni Maungatabu.

Kiribati's own way: Social Science is built island-first — the maneaba (mwaneaba) meeting house is at once the culture-keeping building, the traditional parliament and the centre of village life, and the modern civics (constitution, rights, voting at 18 for the Maneaba ni Maungatabu) is taught growing out of the traditional systems pupils already live under (kaainga family rule, unimwane elders, uea chiefs).
