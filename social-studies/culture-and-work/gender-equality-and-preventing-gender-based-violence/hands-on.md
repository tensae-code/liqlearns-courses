# Hands-on — Gender Equality and Preventing Gender Based Violence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Gender
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Men and women, boys and girls must be treated equally and respect each other; gender-based violence is being hurt because of your gender, and pupils plan how to prevent gender-based violence and sexual abuse. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
