# Video script — Civil Society What It is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 6 Social Studies TG T2, printed pp. 93–102, teaches: What civil society means, the services some of its institutions provide, and their role in serving society — the unit's facts add that the Basic Statute of the State and the laws organise these institutions' work, and that they support the services and programmes that meet society's needs.

Oman's own way (Social Studies Grade 6, Term 2): by Grade 6 the citizen is an organiser — the state teaches civil society as a named system: associations (الجمعيات الأهلية), the sablah, volunteering, national occasions and even elections listed among the unit's concepts, and the people's participation in preparing Oman Vision 2040 given as the model example.
