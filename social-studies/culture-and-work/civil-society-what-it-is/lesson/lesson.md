## Sub lesson 1 — Civil Society What It is

This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: by Grade 6 the citizen is an organiser — the state teaches civil society as a named system: associations (الجمعيات الأهلية), the sablah, volunteering, national occasions and even elections listed among the unit's concepts, and the people's participation in preparing Oman Vision 2040 given as the model example. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
