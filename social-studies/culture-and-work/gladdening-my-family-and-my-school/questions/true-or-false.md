# Gladdening My Family And My School — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: ethics becomes daily practice at home and school — the morning greeting that sets the family's day (мэндчилгээ: "Did you sleep well? Did you dream sweetly?"); planning the day ahead, since a planned day is the habit of every person who lives happily; gratitude (талархал) to everyone whose work carries us, and filial duty (ачлал) — repaying parents' kindness, the first of all duties, in Gamzatov's lines; doing the housework with a glad heart rather than a forced one, for the heart in the work is what the family sees; asking the elders life's teachings (including the tea-offering customs); the evening reckoning of the day's right and wrong acts; and at school — listening to the lesson with full attention, fulfilling and thanking the teacher's teaching, and apologising and forgiving (уучлал) — saying "Уучлаарай" is not weakness but the act that keeps every relationship whole.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
