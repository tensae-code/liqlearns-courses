# Hands-on — Despatriarcalizacion Dismantling Patriarchy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: patriarchy and colonialism as twin structures to be dismantled — despatriarcalización as the subversion of the patriarchal order in family, community and state: breaking the power relations that subordinate women, eliminating discriminatory cultural patterns and stereotypes, gender as a social construction, and discrimination against women inside institutions — a goal the CPE sets the Plurinational State under the paradigm of Vivir Bien. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
