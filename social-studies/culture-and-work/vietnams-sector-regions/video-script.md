Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12* (printed pp. 31–39), teaches: Topic 12.2 (part): Vietnam's sector regions — the seven agro-ecological regions and specialised farming zones (Central Highlands coffee, Thai Nguyen tea, Mekong rice and aquaculture), high-technology farming regions, the six industrial regions (Ho Chi Minh City the largest centre) and the seven tourism regions with their signature products.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12): the final-year electives turn homeward — the disasters Vietnam actually faces and how to live with them, how Vietnam itself is divided into regions for development, and the craft villages that carry its rural economy and culture — even printing the country's new 34-province table in force from 1 July 2025.
