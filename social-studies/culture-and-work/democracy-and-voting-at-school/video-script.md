# Video script — Democracy and Voting at School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dominican Republic — Ciencias Sociales 3° Primaria (printed p. 94), teaches: The concept of democracy — freedom and respect — practised in the school's yearly consejo de curso election, where every student has the right and the duty to vote.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 8): Grade 3 ends the book with a whole annex most countries never give 8-year-olds — the Constitución Dominicana Infantil, the country's constitution rewritten for children, grouped as civil, social and cultural rights; and its democracy unit starts from the school's own consejo de curso election.
