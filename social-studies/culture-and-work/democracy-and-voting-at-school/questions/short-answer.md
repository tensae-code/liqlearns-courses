# Democracy and Voting at School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Unidad 6 Derechos y deberes para vivir en democracia — The concept of democracy — freedom and respect — practised in the school's yearly consejo de curso election, where every student has the right and the duty to vote (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 3 ends the book with a whole annex most countries never give 8-year-olds — the Constitución Dominicana Infantil, the country's constitution rewritten for children, grouped as civil, social and cultural rights; and its democracy unit starts from the school's own consejo de curso election. (any close wording).**
