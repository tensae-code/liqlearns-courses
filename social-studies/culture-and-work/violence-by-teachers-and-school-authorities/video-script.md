# Video script — Violence by Teachers and School Authorities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 9 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana III TG, printed pp. 41–44, teaches: the climate of insecurity in Guatemalan schools where pupils are attacked by the very teachers meant to protect them; the manifestations (guilt-making reprimands, moralising sermons, ridicule, contempt, threats, bribes), who is most victimised, and the risk factors.

Guatemala's own way: the oldest básico pupils study the newest faces of violence — the teacher's own authority, the phone in the pupil's pocket, and prejudice over identity — each answered from human-rights law and the peoples' own values.
