# Hands-on — Violence by Teachers and School Authorities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 2 content theme "Violencia de docentes y directivos escolares"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 9 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana III TG, printed pp. 41–44, teaches: the climate of insecurity in Guatemalan schools where pupils are attacked by the very teachers meant to protect them; the manifestations (guilt-making reprimands, moralising sermons, ridicule, contempt, threats, bribes), who is most victimised, and the risk factors. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 4) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the oldest básico pupils study the newest faces of violence — the teacher's own authority, the phone in the pupil's pocket, and prejudice over identity — each answered from human-rights law and the peoples' own values.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
