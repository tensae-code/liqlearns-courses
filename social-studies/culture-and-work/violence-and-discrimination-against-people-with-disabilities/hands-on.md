# Hands-on — Violence and Discrimination Against People with Disabilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 3 content theme "Violencias contra los discapacitados"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 8 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana II TG, printed pp. 60–64, teaches: how people with disabilities have been discriminated against across history; indifference and overprotection as maltreatment; how society violates their rights through prejudice; the typology of abuse — the aggressor's conducts and the consequences for the victim. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: each kind of violence is taught with Guatemala's own law and institutions — the Femicide Law, the Defensoría de la Mujer Indígena (DEMI) — and through the victims the country's classrooms actually contain: indigenous women, grandparents, pupils with disabilities.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
