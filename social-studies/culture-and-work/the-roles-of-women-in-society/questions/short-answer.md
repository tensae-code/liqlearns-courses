# The Roles of Women in Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The roles of women — in all societies women were long given subordinate roles and their contributions to development were not recognised; the equality movement and the UN Decade for Women (1976–85) asserted equality in dignity, rights, opportunities and responsibilities; women hold four important roles — producers, home managers, mothers and community organisers — and International Women's Day, March 8, grew out of working women's protests for better wages, conditions and the vote (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Traditional and changing roles of women — traditionally Guyanese women were seen in the domestic sphere — mothers, cooks and wives — their work a "labour of love", unrecognised as work; today women choose to work or stay home, most work longer hours than men, and they have moved into the informal sector, the professions and the military and para-military sectors (any close wording).**
