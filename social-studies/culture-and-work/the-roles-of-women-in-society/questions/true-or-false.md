# The Roles of Women in Society — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: In all societies women were long given subordinate roles and their contributions to development were not recognised; the equality movement and the UN Decade for Women (1976–85) asserted equality in dignity, rights, opportunities and responsibilities; women hold four important roles — producers, home managers, mothers and community organisers — and International Women's Day, March 8, grew out of working women's protests for better wages, conditions and the vote.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: Traditionally Guyanese women were seen in the domestic sphere — mothers, cooks and wives — their work a "labour of love", unrecognised as work; today women choose to work or stay home, most work longer hours than men, and they have moved into the informal sector, the professions and the military and para-military sectors.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
