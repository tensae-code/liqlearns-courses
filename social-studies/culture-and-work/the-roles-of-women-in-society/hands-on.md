# Hands-on — The Roles of Women in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: In all societies women were long given subordinate roles and their contributions to development were not recognised; the equality movement and the UN Decade for Women (1976–85) asserted equality in dignity, rights, opportunities and responsibilities; women hold four important roles — producers, home managers, mothers and community organisers — and International Women's Day, March 8, grew out of working women's protests for better wages, conditions and the vote. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Traditionally Guyanese women were seen in the domestic sphere — mothers, cooks and wives — their work a "labour of love", unrecognised as work; today women choose to work or stay home, most work longer hours than men, and they have moved into the informal sector, the professions and the military and para-military sectors. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
