# Hands-on — Forgiveness Letting Go of Hurt and Anger

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Forgiveness
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: forgiveness (عفو) defined as the ability to get past the feeling of hurt or anger you feel toward another person; the two sentences that work it — "I am sorry" (أنا آسف) from the one who erred and "I forgive you" (عفوتُ لك) from the one wronged — practised in acted scenes between friends where a quarrel needs pardon Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
