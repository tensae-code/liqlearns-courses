# Forgiveness Letting Go of Hurt and Anger — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Forgiveness — forgiveness (عفو) defined as the ability to get past the feeling of hurt or anger you feel toward another person; the two sentences that work it — "I am sorry" (أنا آسف) from the one who erred and "I forgive you" (عفوتُ لك) from the one wronged — practised in acted scenes between friends where a quarrel needs pardon (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — the course defines its key words for the child in a back-of-book glossary — forgiveness is "the ability to get past the feeling of hurt or anger you feel toward another person", a tribe is "a group of individuals living on one patch of land, bound by shared ties" — and the righteous person is analysed as mind, heart and hand: what he thinks, what he feels, what he does. (any close wording).**
