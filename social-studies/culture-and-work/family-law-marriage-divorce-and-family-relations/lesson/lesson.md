# Family Law Marriage Divorce and Family Relations

## Sub lesson 1 — Foundations of family law (Основы семейного права) — Grade 11

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 72), teaches: Family law under the Code on Marriage and Family — marriage as a voluntary union that counts in law only when registered at the civil registry (a religious ceremony alone has no legal force), its conditions (mutual consent, marriageable age, no existing marriage, no marriage between close relatives or adopters and adopted) and bars; the spouses' rights and duties; and dissolution — by a death certificate or a court decision, not permitted during a wife's pregnancy or while a child is under three without her written consent. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
