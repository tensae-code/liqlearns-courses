# Family Law Marriage Divorce and Family Relations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Foundations of family law (Основы семейного права) — Grade 11 — family law under the Code on Marriage and Family — marriage as a voluntary union that counts in law only when registered at the civil registry (a religious ceremony alone has no legal force), its conditions (mutual consent, marriageable age, no existing marriage, no marriage between close relatives or adopters and adopted) and bars; the spouses' rights and duties; and dissolution — by a death certificate or a court decision, not permitted during a wife's pregnancy or while a child is under three without her written consent (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics. (any close wording).**
