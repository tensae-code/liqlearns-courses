# Hands-on — Family Law Marriage Divorce and Family Relations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Family law under the Code on Marriage and Family — marriage as a voluntary union that counts in law only when registered at the civil registry (a religious ceremony alone has no legal force), its conditions (mutual consent, marriageable age, no existing marriage, no marriage between close relatives or adopters and adopted) and bars; the spouses' rights and duties; and dissolution — by a death certificate or a court decision, not permitted during a wife's pregnancy or while a child is under three without her written consent. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
