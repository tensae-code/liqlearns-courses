The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

China Senior 思想政治必修3 (printed p. 8), teaches: The Party leading the people through the new-democratic revolution and the founding of the PRC (standing up), socialist revolution and construction, and reform and opening into the new era (growing rich, becoming strong).

China Senior 思想政治必修3 (printed p. 15), teaches: The Party's nature as vanguard of the working class and of the Chinese people and nation, its purpose of serving the people wholeheartedly, and its governing stance — founded for the public, ruling for the people, holding the mass line.

China Senior 思想政治必修3 (printed p. 25), teaches: The Party as the highest political leadership force, its leadership comprehensive, systematic and whole — political, ideological and organisational leadership — exercised scientifically, democratically and according to law.

China's own way: the civics core of the senior Politics course, built as three blocks in one chain — who leads (the Party: chosen by history and the people, advanced, ruling through political, ideological and organisational leadership), who owns the state (the people, through the people's democratic dictatorship, the people's congress as fundamental system, and the three basic systems of multiparty cooperation, ethnic regional autonomy and grassroots self-governance), and how the state is run (comprehensively by law: a rule-of-law country, government and society built together through scientific legislation, strict enforcement, impartial justice and universal observance) — every lesson opening with 探究与分享 cases and closing in a unit 综合探究 project.
