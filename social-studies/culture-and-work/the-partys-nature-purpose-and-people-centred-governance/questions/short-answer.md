# The Partys Nature Purpose and People Centred Governance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Party Leads the People to Stand Up Grow Rich and Become Strong — the Party leading the people through the new-democratic revolution and the founding of the PRC (standing up), socialist revolution and construction, and reform and opening into the new era (growing rich, becoming strong) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Partys Nature Purpose and People Centred Governance — the Party's nature as vanguard of the working class and of the Chinese people and nation, its purpose of serving the people wholeheartedly, and its governing stance — founded for the public, ruling for the people, holding the mass line (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Upholding the Partys Overall Leadership — the Party as the highest political leadership force, its leadership comprehensive, systematic and whole — political, ideological and organisational leadership — exercised scientifically, democratically and according to law (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: China's way — the book's own teaching way — the civics core of the senior Politics course, built as three blocks in one chain — who leads (the Party: chosen by history and the people, advanced, ruling through political, ideological and organisational leadership), who owns the state (the people, through the people's democratic dictatorship, the people's congress as fundamental system, and the three basic systems of multiparty cooperation, ethnic regional autonomy and grassroots self-governance), and how the state is run (comprehensively by law: a rule-of-law country, government and society built together through scientific legislation, strict enforcement, impartial justice and universal observance) — every lesson opening with 探究与分享 cases and closing in a unit 综合探究 project. (any close wording).**
