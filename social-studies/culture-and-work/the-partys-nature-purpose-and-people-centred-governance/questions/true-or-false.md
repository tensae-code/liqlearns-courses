# The Partys Nature Purpose and People Centred Governance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The Party leading the people through the new-democratic revolution and the founding of the PRC (standing up), socialist revolution and construction, and reform and opening into the new era (growing rich, becoming strong).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: The Party's nature as vanguard of the working class and of the Chinese people and nation, its purpose of serving the people wholeheartedly, and its governing stance — founded for the public, ruling for the people, holding the mass line.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: The Party as the highest political leadership force, its leadership comprehensive, systematic and whole — political, ideological and organisational leadership — exercised scientifically, democratically and according to law.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on China's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
