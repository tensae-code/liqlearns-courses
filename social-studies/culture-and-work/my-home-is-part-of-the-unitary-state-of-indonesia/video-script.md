Indonesia's Grade 3 PP-G3 student (printed p. 20); Grade 3 PP-G3 teacher (printed p. 23), teaches: Bab 1 B. Tempat Tinggalku Bagian dari NKRI, Panduan Khusus Bab 1: Aku Anak Indonesia — teaches: the place where I live is part of the NKRI — home district inside province inside the one Indonesian state; guidance and teacher reading for me/family/friends and my place as part of the NKRI.

Indonesia's own way: Grade 3 is the first book to name the state the child lives in — your own street is taught as part of the NKRI (Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia), and rights are introduced paired, always, with duties. the guide gives teachers their own reading (Bahan Bacaan Guru) per chapter, so the civics content is taught to the teacher first, then to the class.
