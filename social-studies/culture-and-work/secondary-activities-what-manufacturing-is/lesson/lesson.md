# Secondary activities — what manufacturing is

## Do it first

Follow one thing in your home backwards: cloth to cotton, bread to wheat, a table to timber. Write each primary beginning and the secondary change.

## Sub lesson 1 — From raw material to useful goods

**Secondary activities take primary products as raw material and change or prepare them into useful goods** — making both goods people consume directly and the **machines that make consumer goods**. In the main, secondary activity is **manufacturing**, plus **construction and power generation**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Industry and manufacturing

**Industry is the place** where manufacturing happens; **manufacturing is the process** of changing raw material **partly or wholly** into finished goods **inside factories**. Place and process — do not swap them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Say what secondary activities do to primary products. Tell industry (the place) from manufacturing (the process). Name the two companions of manufacturing: construction and power generation.
