# Secondary activities — what manufacturing is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Secondary activities take primary products and —
a) Bury them  b) Change or prepare them into useful goods  c) Tax them  d) Export them raw
**Answer: b) Change or prepare them into useful goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Besides manufacturing, secondary activities include —
a) Construction and power generation  b) Only trade  c) Only farming  d) Only transport
**Answer: a) Construction and power generation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Industry is —
a) The process  b) The place where manufacturing happens  c) A raw material  d) A kind of tax
**Answer: b) The place where manufacturing happens**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Manufacturing changes raw material —
a) Never  b) Only in homes  c) Partly or wholly into finished goods  d) Into services
**Answer: c) Partly or wholly into finished goods**
