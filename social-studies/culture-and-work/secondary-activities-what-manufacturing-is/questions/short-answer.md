# Secondary activities — what manufacturing is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are secondary activities, in one sentence?
**Answer:** Activities that take primary products as raw material and change or prepare them into useful goods.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell industry from manufacturing.
**Answer:** Industry is the place where manufacturing happens; manufacturing is the process of changing raw material into finished goods inside factories.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one direct consumer good and say its primary beginning.
**Answer:** For example cloth from cotton, or bread from wheat — any good traced to its primary product.
