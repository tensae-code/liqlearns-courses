# Secondary activities — what manufacturing is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Secondary activities also make the machines that make consumer goods.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Industry is the process and manufacturing is the place.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Power generation is a secondary activity.
**Answer: True**
