The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Algeria Grade 1 Kitabi unified volume (printed p. 89), teaches: Algeria's flag — green and white with a red crescent and star — observed, described and memorised with pride ("I am an Algerian pupil, proud of my country's flag").

Algeria Grade 1 Kitabi unified volume (printed p. 137), teaches: The anthem "Qassaman" and its writer Moufdi Zakaria, learned by heart.

Algeria's own way: civic education is one named lesson inside every unit of the single unified Grade 1 book, always under the running header "I love my homeland" (أحب وطني) for the symbols lessons — greeting, school card, respecting elders and kindness to the young first, then flag, currency, identity documents and the anthem "Qassaman" by Moufdi Zakaria.
