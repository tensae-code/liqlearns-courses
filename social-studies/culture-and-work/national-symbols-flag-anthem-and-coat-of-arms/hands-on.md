# Hands-on — National Symbols Flag Anthem and Coat of Arms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Algeria's flag — green and white with a red crescent and star — observed, described and memorised with pride ("I am an Algerian pupil, proud of my country's flag"). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The anthem "Qassaman" and its writer Moufdi Zakaria, learned by heart. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Algeria's way
Say how Algeria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
