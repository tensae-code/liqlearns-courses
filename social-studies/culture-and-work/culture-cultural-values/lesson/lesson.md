# Culture — cultural values

## Do it first

Describe your own community's mutual help — who helps whom, and when. That practice is a cultural value at work.

## Sub lesson 1 — How a society judges

**Cultural values are the thought-and-practice foundation by which a society judges good and bad, beautiful and ugly.** They are the measures a community carries inside itself.

[Picture 11520 — two villages judging the same act differently, side by side]

## Sub lesson 2 — Every culture has its own

What one culture counts a **good** value, another may lack — or count **bad**. Every culture has its own values: sometimes shared with others, sometimes different.

And a person **inherits from their culture a way of seeing themselves and the world** — learned at **home, at school and in religious institutions**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Say what cultural values are. Give the book's warning: a value counted good in one culture may be counted bad in another. Name the three places values are learned.
