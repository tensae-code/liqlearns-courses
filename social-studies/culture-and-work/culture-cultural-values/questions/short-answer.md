# Culture — cultural values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two pairs do cultural values judge?
**Answer:** Good and bad; beautiful and ugly.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three places where a person learns their culture's way of seeing.
**Answer:** At home, at school and in religious institutions.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Can two cultures share a value? Answer from the lesson.
**Answer:** Yes — values are sometimes shared, sometimes different.
