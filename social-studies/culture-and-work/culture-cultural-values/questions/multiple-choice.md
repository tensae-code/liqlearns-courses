# Culture — cultural values — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cultural values are the foundation by which a society judges —
a) Prices  b) Good and bad, beautiful and ugly  c) Weather  d) Trade routes
**Answer: b) Good and bad, beautiful and ugly**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A value counted good in one culture may in another be —
a) Always good  b) Against the law everywhere  c) Lacking, or counted bad  d) Impossible
**Answer: c) Lacking, or counted bad**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Values are learned at home, at school and in —
a) Religious institutions  b) Markets only  c) Factories  d) Armies
**Answer: a) Religious institutions**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** A person inherits from their culture —
a) Land  b) A way of seeing themselves and the world  c) A trade  d) A language family
**Answer: b) A way of seeing themselves and the world**
