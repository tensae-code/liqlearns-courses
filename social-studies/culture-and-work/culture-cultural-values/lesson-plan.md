# Lesson plan — Culture — cultural values

**Place in the curriculum:** Title 9 — Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder: culture, economic activities, trade, and tax).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** cultural values — the thought-and-practice foundation by which a society judges good and bad, beautiful and ugly
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title, at Grade 8 depth in the course's level ladder (levels, not grades).

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what cultural values are.
2. Give the book's warning: a value counted good in one culture may be counted bad in another.
3. Name the three places values are learned.
