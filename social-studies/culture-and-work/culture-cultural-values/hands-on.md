# Hands-on — Culture — cultural values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Describe your own community's mutual help — who helps whom, and when. That practice is a cultural value at work.

## Activity 2
Pick one act — for example how guests are received. Ask how two different areas judge it. Write both judgements.

## Activity 3
List one value you learned at home, one at school and one in a religious institution.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say what cultural values are. Give the book's warning: a value counted good in one culture may be counted bad in another. Name the three places values are learned.
