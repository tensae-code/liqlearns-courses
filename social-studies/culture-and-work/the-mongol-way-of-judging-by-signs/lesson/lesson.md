## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 6 "The Mongol custom of judging" (Монгол шинжих ёс)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 19, teaches: From childhood Mongolians are taught to observe — the traditional knowledge (шинжих ёс) reads the signs of everything: people by their bearing and deeds, livestock by colour and behaviour, the weather and the seasons by the sky; much of it is "eye-learned" knowledge (нүдлүүлэх) never written down, by which a herder knows tomorrow's weather and a person's character. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
