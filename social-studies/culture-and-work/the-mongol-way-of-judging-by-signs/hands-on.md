# Hands-on — The Mongol Way of Judging by Signs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 6 "The Mongol custom of judging" (Монгол шинжих ёс)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: From childhood Mongolians are taught to observe — the traditional knowledge (шинжих ёс) reads the signs of everything: people by their bearing and deeds, livestock by colour and behaviour, the weather and the seasons by the sky; much of it is "eye-learned" knowledge (нүдлүүлэх) never written down, by which a herder knows tomorrow's weather and a person's character. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
