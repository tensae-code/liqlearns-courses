# The Mongol Way of Judging by Signs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: from childhood Mongolians are taught to observe — the traditional knowledge (шинжих ёс) reads the signs of everything: people by their bearing and deeds, livestock by colour and behaviour, the weather and the seasons by the sky; much of it is "eye-learned" knowledge (нүдлүүлэх) never written down, by which a herder knows tomorrow's weather and a person's character.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
