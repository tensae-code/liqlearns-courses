**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Indonesia).**

Indonesia's own way (Book 1): junior IPS opens sociology before any state content — a child saying goodbye to parents and greeting friends is the first example of interaksi sosial, and status is taught through Sukarno and Hatta's assigned status as Fathers of the Proclamation.

Indonesia's own way (Book 2): senior IPS defines its own subject map — sociology is entered through sosialisasi at a Yogyakarta landfill (TPA Piyungan) and a bullying-awareness video, and differentiation is counted in Indonesia's own census numbers: 1,340 ethnic groups and 747 regional languages (428 of them in Papua).
