# Video script — Social and Cultural Problems in Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Indonesia's Grade 7 IPS-G7 student (printed p. 186); Grade 10 IPS-G10 student (printed p. 222), teaches: Tema IV B.2 Permasalahan Sosial Budaya, Bab IV B. Ragam Gejala Sosial dalam Masyarakat — teaches: today's social-cultural problems, including social gap and poverty (kesenjangan sosial dan kemiskinan) as a global problem with local faces; social phenomena (gejala sosial, after Sorokin and Soekanto) and social problems as mismatches endangering group life — poverty, crime, family, population and environment problems, with stunting (21.6% in SSGI 2022) as the worked Indonesian case.

Indonesia's own way: junior IPS opens sociology before any state content — a child saying goodbye to parents and greeting friends is the first example of interaksi sosial, and status is taught through Sukarno and Hatta's assigned status as Fathers of the Proclamation. senior IPS defines its own subject map — sociology is entered through sosialisasi at a Yogyakarta landfill (TPA Piyungan) and a bullying-awareness video, and differentiation is counted in Indonesia's own census numbers: 1,340 ethnic groups and 747 regional languages (428 of them in Papua).
