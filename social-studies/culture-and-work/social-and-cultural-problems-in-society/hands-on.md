# Hands-on — Social and Cultural Problems in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Tema IV B.2 Permasalahan Sosial Budaya, Bab IV B. Ragam Gejala Sosial dalam Masyarakat — teaches: today's social-cultural problems, including social gap and poverty (kesenjangan sosial dan kemiskinan) as a global problem with local faces; social phenomena (gejala sosial, after Sorokin and Soekanto) and social problems as mismatches endangering group life — poverty, crime, family, population and environment problems, with stunting (21.6% in SSGI 2022) as the worked Indonesian case. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
