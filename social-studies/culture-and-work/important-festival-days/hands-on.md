# Hands-on — Important Festival Days

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lesson 19 Important festival days (ວັນບຸນສໍາຄັນຕ່າງໆ)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The year's great days — Lao New Year, the boat-racing and That Luang festivals, National Day (2 December), Women's Day, Children's Day — and joining in each one, with their songs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Laos's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Laos's book teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
