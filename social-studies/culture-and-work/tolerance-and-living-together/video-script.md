# Video script — Tolerance and Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Libya Grade 5 National Education G5 (printed p. 53), teaches: tolerance means respect, acceptance and appreciation of the diversity of our cultures and forms of expression; it is not only a moral duty but a political and legal requirement — UNESCO Declaration of Principles on Tolerance, Paris, 28 November 1995.

Libya's own way: Grade 5 is a pure values book — eight lessons, each a single civic virtue (equality, justice, solidarity, tolerance, accepting the other, dialogue), each anchored in a real instrument a child can hold: the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, UNESCO's 1995 Declaration on Tolerance, and the Libyan Scouts' "A World of Peace for Children" charter, which the pupil signs.
