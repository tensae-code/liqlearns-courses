# Hands-on — Rules in Public Places

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: relations of respect and courtesy with classmates, teachers, family and neighbours; obeying the school regulations; and courtesy in transport — giving your seat to the elderly, to pregnant women and to mothers with newborns, not speaking loudly, not writing on or scratching the walls. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
