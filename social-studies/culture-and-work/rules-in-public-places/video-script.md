The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba Grade 3 El mundo en que vivimos (printed pp. 236–237), teaches: Relations of respect and courtesy with classmates, teachers, family and neighbours; obeying the school regulations; and courtesy in transport — giving your seat to the elderly, to pregnant women and to mothers with newborns, not speaking loudly, not writing on or scratching the walls.

Cuba's own way: third-graders join Nature Protection Brigades — organised pioneer groups that clean and care for the classroom, school and community — and the book's closing civics chapter binds courtesy, the patriotic symbols and the traffic law into one code of “comportamiento social”.
