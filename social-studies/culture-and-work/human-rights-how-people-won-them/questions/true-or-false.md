# Human Rights How People Won Them — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: rights were won by long struggle — Magna Carta binding King John in 13th-century England, the fall of Charles I, the 1689 Bill of Rights and the Habeas Corpus Act limiting arbitrary arrest.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: the American and French revolutions and the rights declarations they issued, then the 20th-century struggles — Russia 1917, China 1911 and 1949 — and the rights struggles inside Asian countries, Sri Lanka's own among them.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Sri Lanka's student books only, named in lesson/sources.md.**
