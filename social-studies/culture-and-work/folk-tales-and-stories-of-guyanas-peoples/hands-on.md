# Hands-on — Folk Tales and Stories of Guyanas Peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The folk tales the peoples of Guyana pass down, and what the stories teach about each people's way of life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
