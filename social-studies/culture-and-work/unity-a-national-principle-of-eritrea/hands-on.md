# Hands-on — Unity a National Principle of Eritrea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: unity as a national principle — the farmer's family pulling the ox from the well only when all nine pull together, and the Peace and Unity poem from Ras Kessar to Ras Dumera. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
