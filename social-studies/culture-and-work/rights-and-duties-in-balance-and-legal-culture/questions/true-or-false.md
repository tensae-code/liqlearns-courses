# Rights and Duties in Balance and Legal Culture — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Rights and duties as two sides of one status — everyone who holds natural rights must also know and fulfil his duties before society (the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus obliges everyone to observe the Constitution and laws, respect others' dignity, rights and lawful interests, protect nature, pay taxes, care for parents, and defend the Motherland); against this stand legal nihilism and moral cynicism, which destroy law-abidingness; and legal culture — laid down in childhood as legal consciousness, knowledge of the law and lawful behaviour, on whose level the lawfulness and legal order of the whole society depend (the UN declared 1996–2005 the decade of human-rights education).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Belarus' student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
