# Hands-on — Rights and Duties in Balance and Legal Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Rights and duties as two sides of one status — everyone who holds natural rights must also know and fulfil his duties before society (the Constitution of the Republic of Belarus obliges everyone to observe the Constitution and laws, respect others' dignity, rights and lawful interests, protect nature, pay taxes, care for parents, and defend the Motherland); against this stand legal nihilism and moral cynicism, which destroy law-abidingness; and legal culture — laid down in childhood as legal consciousness, knowledge of the law and lawful behaviour, on whose level the lawfulness and legal order of the whole society depend (the UN declared 1996–2005 the decade of human-rights education). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
