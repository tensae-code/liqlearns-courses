# Hands-on — Good And Bad Deeds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 7 Сайн уу, муу юу? (Good or bad?)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Deeds are good or bad in three ways — in thought (kind vs envious), in speech (truthful and gentle vs lying and gossiping) and in body (saving a life, giving, walking rightly vs killing, stealing, walking wrongly) — and how a bad deed is put right. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Topic 4 "Good and bad deeds" (Сайн ба муу үйл)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A good deed comes from a beautiful heart — it is done sincerely, with compassion for people, animals and plants, helping without expecting anything back; good and bad deeds of thought, word and body are set side by side (kind thoughts against envy and greed, truthful speech against lies and insults, saving life against taking what was not given). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
