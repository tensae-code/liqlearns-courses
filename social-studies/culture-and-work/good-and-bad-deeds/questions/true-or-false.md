# Good And Bad Deeds — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: deeds are good or bad in three ways — in thought (kind vs envious), in speech (truthful and gentle vs lying and gossiping) and in body (saving a life, giving, walking rightly vs killing, stealing, walking wrongly) — and how a bad deed is put right.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: a good deed comes from a beautiful heart — it is done sincerely, with compassion for people, animals and plants, helping without expecting anything back; good and bad deeds of thought, word and body are set side by side (kind thoughts against envy and greed, truthful speech against lies and insults, saving life against taking what was not given).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
