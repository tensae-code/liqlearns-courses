# Hands-on — Primary activities — kinds of farming

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Survey your own family and community: which kind of farming does each household use, in each of the three sortings? Write the survey as a small table.

## Activity 2
Find one extensive and one intensive farm near you. Write the land and input evidence for each placing.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Sort farming by product, by purpose and by input. Tell extensive from intensive farming. Place a real local farm in all three sortings.
