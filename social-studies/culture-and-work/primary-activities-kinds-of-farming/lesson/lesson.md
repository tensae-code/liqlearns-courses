# Primary activities — kinds of farming

## Do it first

Survey your own family and community: which kind of farming does each household use, in each of the three sortings? Write the survey as a small table.

## Sub lesson 1 — By product, and by purpose

**By product:** crop farming, livestock farming and **mixed farming**.

**By purpose:** **market (commercial) crop farming** and **subsistence farming** — the book's words for it are "hand-to-mouth": grown to live on, not to sell.

[Picture 11507 — the three sortings of farming as three labelled charts]

## Sub lesson 2 — By input — extensive and intensive

**By capital, labour and land used:** **extensive farming** uses **much land and little input**; **intensive farming** uses **little land and much money and labour**. The same farm can be placed in all three sortings at once — product, purpose and input each answer a different question.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Sort farming by product, by purpose and by input. Tell extensive from intensive farming. Place a real local farm in all three sortings.

## Do It Yourself

1. Survey your own family and community: which kind of farming does each household use, in each of the three sortings? Write the survey as a small table.
2. Find one extensive and one intensive farm near you. Write the land and input evidence for each placing.
