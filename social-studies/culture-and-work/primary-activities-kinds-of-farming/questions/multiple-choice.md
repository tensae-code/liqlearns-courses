# Primary activities — kinds of farming — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mixed farming belongs to the sorting by —
a) Purpose  b) Product  c) Input  d) Country
**Answer: b) Product**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Subsistence farming is described as —
a) For export  b) Hand-to-mouth  c) Machine-only  d) Intensive always
**Answer: b) Hand-to-mouth**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Extensive farming uses —
a) Little land, much input  b) Much land, little input  c) No land  d) Only labour
**Answer: b) Much land, little input**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Intensive farming uses —
a) Much land, no money  b) Little land, much money and labour  c) Only animals  d) Forest land
**Answer: b) Little land, much money and labour**
