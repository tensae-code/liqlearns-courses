# Primary activities — kinds of farming — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Market crop farming is a sorting by purpose.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A farm can be placed in all three sortings at once.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Livestock farming is a sorting by input.
**Answer: False**
