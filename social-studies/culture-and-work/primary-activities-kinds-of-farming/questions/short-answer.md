# Primary activities — kinds of farming — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three sortings.
**Answer:** By product, by purpose, and by capital/labour/land used (input).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three kinds by product.
**Answer:** Crop farming, livestock farming and mixed farming.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell extensive from intensive in one line each.
**Answer:** Extensive: much land, little input. Intensive: little land, much money and labour.
