# Electronic Crimes and Their Penalties — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: e-crimes defined as crimes committed through the use of technologies; the named crimes with their Emirati penalties — fraud on banking business online, harassment, blackmail, and violating intellectual property — and the hidden causes behind e-crime (forgery, identity theft, protest, revenge, fame-seeking, bad behaviour) and its effects (lost profits, wasted time, damaged reputation, reduced productivity)
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: digital citizenship is a named school subject of its own, run with the Aqdar empowerment programme — access is a right the state measures (the DAI index, 100% internet spread in the Emirati market), the Emirati ID card's number is dissected as a civics lesson, and every abuse is answered with the federation's own law and its own reporting service (Abu Dhabi Police "Aman", 8002626).
**Answer: True**
