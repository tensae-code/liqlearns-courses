# Hands-on — Electronic Crimes and Their Penalties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rules and electronic security and Electronic crimes
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: e-crimes defined as crimes committed through the use of technologies; the named crimes with their Emirati penalties — fraud on banking business online, harassment, blackmail, and violating intellectual property — and the hidden causes behind e-crime (forgery, identity theft, protest, revenge, fame-seeking, bad behaviour) and its effects (lost profits, wasted time, damaged reputation, reduced productivity) Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
