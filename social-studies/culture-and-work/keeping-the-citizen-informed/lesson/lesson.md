# Keeping the Citizen Informed

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the citizen's right to be informed and the media's role in a democracy — as Greece's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Informing the citizen

Greece Grade 6 Koinoniki kai Politiki Agogi ST' Dimotikou (printed p. 61), teaches: The citizen's right to be informed and the media's role in a democracy. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greece's way — the book's own teaching way

Greece's own way: two Aristophanes characters (Dikaiopolis and Eirini, "Justice-City" and "Peace") guide the pupil outward from the family to the state, the EU and the world; Greece's own municipalities, constitution and EU membership are the worked examples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
