# Hands-on — Developing Peaceful Co Existence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activities 9 and 12 Peaceful co-existence and resilience
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: peaceful co-existence is living together in peace rather than constant hostility, developed through community dialogue, caring and sharing (the rich share with the poor to eradicate the poverty that brings conflict), equality and mutual benefit, respecting other people's religions and beliefs, peace education in schools, seminars and rallies — and resilience in working in harmony is built by organising civic education regularly. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
