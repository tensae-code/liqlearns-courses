# Video script — Developing Peaceful Co Existence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 8 P8 Social Studies SB, printed p. 71, teaches: peaceful co-existence is living together in peace rather than constant hostility, developed through community dialogue, caring and sharing (the rich share with the poor to eradicate the poverty that brings conflict), equality and mutual benefit, respecting other people's religions and beliefs, peace education in schools, seminars and rallies — and resilience in working in harmony is built by organising civic education regularly.

South Sudan's own way: The final primary book is a civics of unity for a country at war with itself — power sharing is taught from the 2016 Transitional Government of National Unity cabinet photograph, the constitution's Section 33 equality clause is quoted in full, and Kenya's 2007–08 crisis and Kofi Annan's mediation are the worked example of how a neighbour's unity process shapes South Sudan's own.
