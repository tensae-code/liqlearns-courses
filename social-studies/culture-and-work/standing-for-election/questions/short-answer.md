# Standing for Election — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Standing for Election — candidacy is the process by which individuals, members of a political party, or of a government or civic institution compete to take over the post of president or membership in it; its conditions in Palestine — for the presidency: Palestinian, born to two Palestinian parents, having completed forty years of age or more on polling day, permanently resident in the Palestinian territories, and registered in the final voters' register; for the Legislative Council: Palestinian, twenty-eight years or more on polling day, name entered in the final voters' register, not convicted of a felony or a misdemeanour against honour and trust, and permanently resident; for the local councils: twenty-five years or more on polling day, name in the final register of the local body, no such conviction, not an employee of the Ministry of Local Government or of any public security body unless he has resigned and attached proof of its acceptance, resident within the body for at least a year before the elections, and not a candidate for another local body or list (any close wording).**
