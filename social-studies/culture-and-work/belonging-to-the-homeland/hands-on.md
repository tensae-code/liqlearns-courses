# Hands-on — Belonging to the Homeland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Belonging is the state in which a person feels his joining of a particular group, bound to it by a relation reflected positively in his behaviour; belonging to the homeland strengthens the relation of the individuals, the society and the homeland with one another, a relation that makes the enemy fear approaching or touching this homeland's security; and among the most important means that embody belonging to the homeland: complying with the public rulings and rules, the rules of work and the rules of behaviour, and preserving public property and the cleanliness of the public facilities, places and streets on the homeland's land, through participating in the voluntary and charitable works that aim at serving society; adopting the method of conscious dialogue when disagreements and disputes occur, between individuals or different groups, and keeping away from the negative behaviours — using violence as a means of solving problems and disputes, cooperating with the enemies to harm the homeland's and society's interest, not committing to the laws, stealing public property and funds and assaulting their sanctity; and belonging increases the strength of the bond between persons through their love of their homeland, returning to the homeland success, renaissance and progress. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
