# Lesson plan — Belonging to the Homeland

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Belonging to the Homeland
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Iraq, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Belonging is the state in which a person feels his joining of a particular group, bound to it by a relation reflected positively in his behaviour; belonging to the homeland strengthens the relation of the individuals, the society and the homeland with one another, a relation that makes the enemy fear approaching or touching this homeland's security; and among the most important means that embody belonging to the homeland: complying with the public rulings and rules, the rules of work and the rules of behaviour, and preserving public property and the cleanliness of the public facilities, places and streets on the homeland's land, through participating in the voluntary and charitable works that aim at serving society; adopting the method of conscious dialogue when disagreements and disputes occur, between individuals or different groups, and keeping away from the negative behaviours — using violence as a means of solving problems and disputes, cooperating with the enemies to harm the homeland's and society's interest, not committing to the laws, stealing public property and funds and assaulting their sanctity; and belonging increases the strength of the bond between persons through their love of their homeland, returning to the homeland success, renaissance and progress — as Iraq's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
