# Watching My Body Speech And Mind — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 1.1, opening units — Everything I do is an act of body, of speech or of mind (бие, хэл, сэтгэл), and the first ethical skill is observation (ажиглалт) with awareness (сэрэмж): watch what you are about to do, what you are doing, and what you have done, and ask before each act whether it is right; the writer D. Natsagdorj is taken apart as an observation exercise — his appearance, his clothes, his character — and the tale of Baal the river-guardian teaches that the mind is a guardhouse: whatever thought you let sit in it rules the house (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — from Grade 6 the civics subject changes its name and its method — no longer "Civic Education" but "Civic Ethics" (Иргэний ёс зүйн боловсрол), a self-observation workbook in the Buddhist-inflected Mongolian moral tradition: the pupil keeps a diary of their own acts of body, speech and mind, and ethics is practised as a daily discipline before it is ever a rule about others (any close wording).**
