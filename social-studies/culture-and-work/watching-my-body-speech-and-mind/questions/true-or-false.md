# Watching My Body Speech And Mind — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: everything I do is an act of body, of speech or of mind (бие, хэл, сэтгэл), and the first ethical skill is observation (ажиглалт) with awareness (сэрэмж): watch what you are about to do, what you are doing, and what you have done, and ask before each act whether it is right; the writer D. Natsagdorj is taken apart as an observation exercise — his appearance, his clothes, his character — and the tale of Baal the river-guardian teaches that the mind is a guardhouse: whatever thought you let sit in it rules the house.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
