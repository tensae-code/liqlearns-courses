# Hands-on — Watching My Body Speech And Mind

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.1, opening units
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Everything I do is an act of body, of speech or of mind (бие, хэл, сэтгэл), and the first ethical skill is observation (ажиглалт) with awareness (сэрэмж): watch what you are about to do, what you are doing, and what you have done, and ask before each act whether it is right; the writer D. Natsagdorj is taken apart as an observation exercise — his appearance, his clothes, his character — and the tale of Baal the river-guardian teaches that the mind is a guardhouse: whatever thought you let sit in it rules the house. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
