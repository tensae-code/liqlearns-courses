# Hands-on — Religious Festivals of Trinidad and Tobago

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Divali and Ramleela, Eid-ul-Fitr, the Easter cycle and Good Friday Bobolee, Spiritual Shouter Baptist Liberation Day, Corpus Christi, Christmas and parang, Phagwa/Holi — every community's holy days, learned by all. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Trinidad and Tobago's way
Say how Trinidad and Tobago's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
