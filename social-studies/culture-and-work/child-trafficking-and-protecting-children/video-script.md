# Video script — Child Trafficking and Protecting Children

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bhutan Class VI Social Studies VI, printed p. 35, teaches: child trafficking — moving children for forced labour or sexual use — its causes (poverty, oppression, missing rights and opportunities, war), its consequences (health harm, lost schooling, disease, depression), and prevention by community groups, rules and education, with the RBP, RENEW and NCWC named as the agencies fighting it.

Bhutan's own way: the primary course closes by putting the whole state in front of the child — what a government is, Bhutan's own change from monarchy to Democratic Constitutional Monarchy in 2008, its three branches told through the courts' own ladder and the kabney/patang ranks the King bestows, the Article 7 fundamental rights quoted from Bhutan's Constitution, and a social-problems chapter that names Bhutan's own helping agencies (Royal Bhutan Police, RENEW, NCWC) for every problem.
