# Hands-on — Child Trafficking and Protecting Children

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Child trafficking — moving children for forced labour or sexual use — its causes (poverty, oppression, missing rights and opportunities, war), its consequences (health harm, lost schooling, disease, depression), and prevention by community groups, rules and education, with the RBP, RENEW and NCWC named as the agencies fighting it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
