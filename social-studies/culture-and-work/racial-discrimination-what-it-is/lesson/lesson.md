# Racial Discrimination What It is

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: racial discrimination, as it came in Article 1 of the International Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination, signed in the UN General Assembly and in force since January 1969, means any distinction, exclusion, restriction or preference based on race, colour, descent, or national or ethnic origin, which has the purpose or effect of nullifying or impairing the recognition, enjoyment or exercise, on an equal footing, of human rights and fundamental freedoms in the political, economic, social, cultural or any other field of public life; and by this definition the signatory states undertake: not to practise racial discrimination, encourage it or ban it against persons, groups or institutions, and to end it by all possible means; to amend or repeal the policies, laws and regulations that lead to racial discrimination in the state; to encourage and support the humanitarian organisations aiming to remove the differences and barriers between the races and to prevent division in society on a racial basis; and to take the political, economic, cultural and other measures and procedures to protect the racial groups in the state and guarantee their enjoyment of human rights and fundamental freedoms; and the State of Palestine joined the Convention in April 2014, with no reservations on its articles, the signature expressing respect for the Convention's principles and spirit — as Palestine's book teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.
