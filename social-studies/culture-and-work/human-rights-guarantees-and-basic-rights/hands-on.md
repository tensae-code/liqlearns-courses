# Hands-on — Human Rights Guarantees and Basic Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: I.1 인권 보장과 기본권, Ⅰ 인권과 헌법 1 인권과 기본권, Ⅰ 인권과 기본권, 인권과 기본권 1 인권과 헌법 — teaches: what human rights are and the basic rights the constitution guarantees; human rights and the basic rights — liberty, equality, political participation, petition and social rights — and how the constitution guarantees them; human rights and the basic rights, how they may be restricted only by law and never in their essential content, and relief by constitutional complaint; human rights and the constitution. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Korea's way
Say how South Korea's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
