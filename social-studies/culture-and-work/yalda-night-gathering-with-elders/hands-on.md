# Hands-on — Yalda Night Gathering with Elders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: manners learned at the Yalda night (شب چله) — the first night of Dey, the longest night of the year, which Iranians have celebrated since old times: people gather in the elders' house, eat nuts, fruit and special foods, and talk together; Saman's family spends the night at grandfather and grandmother's. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
