## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2 Lesson 1 The Administrative Division

Yemen Grade 5 G5 National Education, printed p. 24, teaches: how Yemen is divided administratively into governorates and districts so the state can serve and govern the whole country. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Yemen's way — the book's own teaching way

Yemen's own way: a standalone National Education book that teaches the state through Yemen's own map and constitution-era institutions — the country is divided into governorates and districts the child can name, the three authorities are taught one per lesson, and democracy is introduced through shura (consultation), with Yemenis presented as early practitioners of it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
