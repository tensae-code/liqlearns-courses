# The Right to Life — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the right to life is inviolable and the Constitution (Art. 67) grants all rights and guarantees to the unborn; life is physical, psychological and social at once, and a norm is not enough — quality of life (health, work, family, housing opportunities) must be assured; respecting life begins with valuing ourselves — self-valuation and self-esteem (body-hygiene detail named, not placed — Health course's).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: human beings are unique and unrepeatable and life is the first value, without price; violence and insecurity have made life look like merchandise that can be snatched, and our duty is to change that conception; the first ethical commitment toward other living beings is to respect their life.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: moral values give our life meaning and show the class of person we are; the right to life is inherent to all human beings without distinction of ethnicity, sex, creed, politics or condition, and it implies living with dignity; the lesson sets the prohibitions of torture, forced disappearance and cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment, and compares constitutions.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: valuing life is the first step to happiness — life has good and bad things and is a treasure to enjoy to the full; each person's life must be treated as the most sacred thing they have, and it is irrational that people lose their lives for skin colour, sexual orientation, political ideology or social condition; violence must stop and conflicts resolved otherwise; children's right to life has two parts: the inherent right to life and the right to survival and development.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it also stands on Honduras's teacher guide(s), named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
