# The Right to Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 3 Respeto y valoración a la vida — The right to life is inviolable and the Constitution (Art. 67) grants all rights and guarantees to the unborn; life is physical, psychological and social at once, and a norm is not enough — quality of life (health, work, family, housing opportunities) must be assured; respecting life begins with valuing ourselves — self-valuation and self-esteem (body-hygiene detail named, not placed — Health course's) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 6 Valor y respeto a la vida — Human beings are unique and unrepeatable and life is the first value, without price; violence and insecurity have made life look like merchandise that can be snatched, and our duty is to change that conception; the first ethical commitment toward other living beings is to respect their life (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 5 Valor moral de la vida humana — Moral values give our life meaning and show the class of person we are; the right to life is inherent to all human beings without distinction of ethnicity, sex, creed, politics or condition, and it implies living with dignity; the lesson sets the prohibitions of torture, forced disappearance and cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment, and compares constitutions (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the fourth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 6, teoría adicional — Valuing life is the first step to happiness — life has good and bad things and is a treasure to enjoy to the full; each person's life must be treated as the most sacred thing they have, and it is irrational that people lose their lives for skin colour, sexual orientation, political ideology or social condition; violence must stop and conflicts resolved otherwise; children's right to life has two parts: the inherent right to life and the right to survival and development (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does the fifth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 6 completes primary civics with the world system — international organisations defined as voluntary associations of sovereign states, the UN and its specialised agencies with their real founding dates, and Honduras's own memberships and poverty figures (52.6% in poverty, World Bank 2018) as the home case. secondary civics starts from the family as a legal institution — the Family Code (Decree 76-84) enters in Lesson 1 — and builds to the State itself: its elements (population, territory, power), Montesquieu's separation, and the full legislative process ending in publication in La Gaceta. Grade 8 is the moral-and-legal year — moral conscience and dilemmas, the Anti-Bullying Law read article by article, the rule of law traced to 1789, and human rights taught through the Americas' own instruments (the American Declaration, the OAS, the anti-torture and forced-disappearance conventions). (any close wording).**
