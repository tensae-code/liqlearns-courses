# Hands-on — The Right to Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 3 Respeto y valoración a la vida
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The right to life is inviolable and the Constitution (Art. 67) grants all rights and guarantees to the unborn; life is physical, psychological and social at once, and a norm is not enough — quality of life (health, work, family, housing opportunities) must be assured; respecting life begins with valuing ourselves — self-valuation and self-esteem (body-hygiene detail named, not placed — Health course's). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 6 Valor y respeto a la vida
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human beings are unique and unrepeatable and life is the first value, without price; violence and insecurity have made life look like merchandise that can be snatched, and our duty is to change that conception; the first ethical commitment toward other living beings is to respect their life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 5 Valor moral de la vida humana
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Moral values give our life meaning and show the class of person we are; the right to life is inherent to all human beings without distinction of ethnicity, sex, creed, politics or condition, and it implies living with dignity; the lesson sets the prohibitions of torture, forced disappearance and cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment, and compares constitutions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 6, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Valuing life is the first step to happiness — life has good and bad things and is a treasure to enjoy to the full; each person's life must be treated as the most sacred thing they have, and it is irrational that people lose their lives for skin colour, sexual orientation, political ideology or social condition; violence must stop and conflicts resolved otherwise; children's right to life has two parts: the inherent right to life and the right to survival and development. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
