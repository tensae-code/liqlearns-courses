## Sub lesson 5 — Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way

Honduras's own way: Grade 6 completes primary civics with the world system — international organisations defined as voluntary associations of sovereign states, the UN and its specialised agencies with their real founding dates, and Honduras's own memberships and poverty figures (52.6% in poverty, World Bank 2018) as the home case. secondary civics starts from the family as a legal institution — the Family Code (Decree 76-84) enters in Lesson 1 — and builds to the State itself: its elements (population, territory, power), Montesquieu's separation, and the full legislative process ending in publication in La Gaceta. Grade 8 is the moral-and-legal year — moral conscience and dilemmas, the Anti-Bullying Law read article by article, the rule of law traced to 1789, and human rights taught through the Americas' own instruments (the American Declaration, the OAS, the anti-torture and forced-disappearance conventions). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
