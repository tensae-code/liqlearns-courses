# Video script — Our National Games

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 44, teaches: Games are the people's wisdom at play, fitted around work and never played idly for long ("a game is good up to the top of the head" is the warning); stone games, in which children build a whole model camp — ger walls, door, herd — from chosen stones; the 80-plus shagai (anklebone) games, the four sides of the bone named horse, camel, sheep and goat; and the word games, ending in дайралцаан, the sung verbal duel accompanied by the morin khuur.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
