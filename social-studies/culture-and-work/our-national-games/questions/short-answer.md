# Our National Games — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 12 (games) "From the customs of Mongolian national games" (Монгол үндэсний тоглоом наадмын ёсноос) — Games are the people's wisdom at play, fitted around work and never played idly for long ("a game is good up to the top of the head" is the warning); stone games, in which children build a whole model camp — ger walls, door, herd — from chosen stones; the 80-plus shagai (anklebone) games, the four sides of the bone named horse, camel, sheep and goat; and the word games, ending in дайралцаан, the sung verbal duel accompanied by the morin khuur (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone (any close wording).**
