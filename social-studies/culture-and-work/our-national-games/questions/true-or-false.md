# Our National Games — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: games are the people's wisdom at play, fitted around work and never played idly for long ("a game is good up to the top of the head" is the warning); stone games, in which children build a whole model camp — ger walls, door, herd — from chosen stones; the 80-plus shagai (anklebone) games, the four sides of the bone named horse, camel, sheep and goat; and the word games, ending in дайралцаан, the sung verbal duel accompanied by the morin khuur.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
