# Hands-on — Our National Games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 12 (games) "From the customs of Mongolian national games" (Монгол үндэсний тоглоом наадмын ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Games are the people's wisdom at play, fitted around work and never played idly for long ("a game is good up to the top of the head" is the warning); stone games, in which children build a whole model camp — ger walls, door, herd — from chosen stones; the 80-plus shagai (anklebone) games, the four sides of the bone named horse, camel, sheep and goat; and the word games, ending in дайралцаан, the sung verbal duel accompanied by the morin khuur. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
