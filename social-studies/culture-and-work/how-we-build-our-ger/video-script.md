The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 24, teaches: The ger is raised on a chosen place — sheltered from wind and flood, its door facing south or south-east; the order of building: crown wheel (тооно) set first, then the wall sections (хана), door, posts (багана), roof poles (унь), ropes and covers, layer upon layer by season; when the ger stands, tea is brewed and the neighbours are invited in, and taking it down runs the order backwards; the blessing verse (мялаалга) praises the new ger piece by piece.

Mongolia's own way (Book 4): the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other.
