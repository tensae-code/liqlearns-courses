# How We Build Our Ger — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the ger is raised on a chosen place — sheltered from wind and flood, its door facing south or south-east; the order of building: crown wheel (тооно) set first, then the wall sections (хана), door, posts (багана), roof poles (унь), ropes and covers, layer upon layer by season; when the ger stands, tea is brewed and the neighbours are invited in, and taking it down runs the order backwards; the blessing verse (мялаалга) praises the new ger piece by piece.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
