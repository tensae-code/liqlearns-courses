# Hands-on — How We Build Our Ger

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 8 "From the customs of building a ger" (Гэр барих ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The ger is raised on a chosen place — sheltered from wind and flood, its door facing south or south-east; the order of building: crown wheel (тооно) set first, then the wall sections (хана), door, posts (багана), roof poles (унь), ropes and covers, layer upon layer by season; when the ger stands, tea is brewed and the neighbours are invited in, and taking it down runs the order backwards; the blessing verse (мялаалга) praises the new ger piece by piece. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
