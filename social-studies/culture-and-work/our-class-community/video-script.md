# Video script — Our Class Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed p. 10), teaches: The class as a community with a poster of shared rules — I exercise my rights, I carry out my duties, I respect others, I respect the rules of behaviour, I accept the consequences when I break a rule, I gladly cooperate — where trust, friendship and togetherness are grown and pupils agree their own activities.

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays.
