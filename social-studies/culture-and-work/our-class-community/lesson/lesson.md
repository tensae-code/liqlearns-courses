## Sub lesson 1 — Одељенска заједница (The class community)

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed p. 10), teaches: the class as a community with a poster of shared rules — I exercise my rights, I carry out my duties, I respect others, I respect the rules of behaviour, I accept the consequences when I break a rule, I gladly cooperate — where trust, friendship and togetherness are grown and pupils agree their own activities. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
