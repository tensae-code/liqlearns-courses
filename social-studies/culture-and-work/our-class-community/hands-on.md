# Hands-on — Our Class Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Одељенска заједница (The class community)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the class as a community with a poster of shared rules — I exercise my rights, I carry out my duties, I respect others, I respect the rules of behaviour, I accept the consequences when I break a rule, I gladly cooperate — where trust, friendship and togetherness are grown and pupils agree their own activities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
