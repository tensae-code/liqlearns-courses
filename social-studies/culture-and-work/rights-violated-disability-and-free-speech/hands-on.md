# Hands-on — Rights Violated Disability And Free Speech

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.5 "Human-rights violations and their consequences"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How rights fail in real life — from Mongolia's 2010 report to the UN: people with disabilities are shut out of information, culture and sport (television news without sign-language translation), out of theatres and cinemas whose buildings have no access, out of public transport with no provision — so their rights to education, work and a full social life are violated together, and many leave their home districts for the capital; and how rights hang together — freedom of expression (speech, writing and publishing, art, peaceful assembly) is bound to the rights to education, health and work, which is why regimes burn books: the Qin emperor's burning of Confucius's books in 213 BC and the Nazi book-burning in Berlin in 1933, answered today by Germany's Day of the Book; the media's four duties — informing, forming citizens' views, overseeing and criticising, and educating. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
