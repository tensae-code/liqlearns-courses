# Rights Violated Disability And Free Speech — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: how rights fail in real life — from Mongolia's 2010 report to the UN: people with disabilities are shut out of information, culture and sport (television news without sign-language translation), out of theatres and cinemas whose buildings have no access, out of public transport with no provision — so their rights to education, work and a full social life are violated together, and many leave their home districts for the capital; and how rights hang together — freedom of expression (speech, writing and publishing, art, peaceful assembly) is bound to the rights to education, health and work, which is why regimes burn books: the Qin emperor's burning of Confucius's books in 213 BC and the Nazi book-burning in Berlin in 1933, answered today by Germany's Day of the Book; the media's four duties — informing, forming citizens' views, overseeing and criticising, and educating.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
