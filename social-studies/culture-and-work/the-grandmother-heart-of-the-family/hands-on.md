# Hands-on — The Grandmother Heart of the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Kuwaiti grandmother of old — her word heard in the house, running the family and assigning each member their role, teaching daughters and daughters-in-law cooking, sewing and house management, and gathering the children for the family's culture and stories — the elder as the family's teacher. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
