# Video script — Indonesias Ethnic Groups and Their Cultures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Indonesia's Grade 3 PP-G3 student (printed p. 89); Grade 4 PP-G4 student (printed p. 65); Grade 3 PP-G3 teacher (printed p. 129), teaches: Bab 3 A. Kekayaan Suku Bangsaku, Bab 3 A. Keberagaman Sosial dan Budaya, Panduan Khusus Bab 3: Berbeda Itu Indah — teaches: the wealth of my nation's ethnic groups — traditional dress and customs across the islands as riches, not differences to fear; social and cultural diversity, opened with a Youth Pledge Day (Sumpah Pemuda, 28 October) school story about respecting diversity; guidance for ethnic and cultural wealth and the language of unity.

Indonesia's own way: Grade 3 is the first book to name the state the child lives in — your own street is taught as part of the NKRI (Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia), and rights are introduced paired, always, with duties. Grade 4 introduces the child's first government — the perangkat desa/kelurahan, the village and urban-ward officials — inside a book about the child's own street, before any national institution is named. the guide gives teachers their own reading (Bahan Bacaan Guru) per chapter, so the civics content is taught to the teacher first, then to the class.
