The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 3 A. Kekayaan Suku Bangsaku, Bab 3 A. Keberagaman Sosial dan Budaya, Panduan Khusus Bab 3: Berbeda Itu Indah — teaches: the wealth of my nation's ethnic groups — traditional dress and customs across the islands as riches, not differences to fear; social and cultural diversity, opened with a Youth Pledge Day (Sumpah Pemuda, 28 October) school story about respecting diversity; guidance for ethnic and cultural wealth and the language of unity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
