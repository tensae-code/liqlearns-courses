## Sub lesson 2 — Indonesia's way — the books' own teaching way

Indonesia's own way: Grade 3 is the first book to name the state the child lives in — your own street is taught as part of the NKRI (Unitary State of the Republic of Indonesia), and rights are introduced paired, always, with duties. Grade 4 introduces the child's first government — the perangkat desa/kelurahan, the village and urban-ward officials — inside a book about the child's own street, before any national institution is named. the guide gives teachers their own reading (Bahan Bacaan Guru) per chapter, so the civics content is taught to the teacher first, then to the class. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
