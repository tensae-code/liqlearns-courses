# Hands-on — Mutual Respect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Respect is one of the basic pillars for building sound social relations and their continuity among society's members, for it expresses appreciation and love between two or more parties; and mutual respect means people's dealing with one another in a good way, such that each individual or group accepts the others' ideas and opinions, regardless of their sex, colour or lineage, and respects difference of opinion with others, for it is the key to relations' success; and among the most important examples of mutual respect: mutual respect for religious beliefs, mutual respect for national and local occasions, and mutual respect for others' rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
