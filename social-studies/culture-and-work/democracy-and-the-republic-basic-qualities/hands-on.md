# Hands-on — Democracy and the Republic Basic Qualities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: democracy's qualities are freedom, equality, the right to vote and be elected, national sovereignty (ruling power belongs to the people), pluralism (more than one party or candidate, and respect for different views whatever the result), participation (voting, petitions, civil-society work) and the rule of law; in the republic the president and deputies come by election for fixed terms and the people can change rulers they are not pleased with; democracy also lives at home and in class-president elections Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
