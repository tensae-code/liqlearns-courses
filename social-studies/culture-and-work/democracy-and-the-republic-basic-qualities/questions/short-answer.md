# Democracy and the Republic Basic Qualities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Basic qualities of democracy and the republic — democracy's qualities are freedom, equality, the right to vote and be elected, national sovereignty (ruling power belongs to the people), pluralism (more than one party or candidate, and respect for different views whatever the result), participation (voting, petitions, civil-society work) and the rule of law; in the republic the president and deputies come by election for fixed terms and the people can change rulers they are not pleased with; democracy also lives at home and in class-president elections (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
