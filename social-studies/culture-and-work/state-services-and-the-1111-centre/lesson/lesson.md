## Sub lesson 1 — Section 5.4 "State services"

This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: the senior book teaches society as a science and the state as a machine the citizen operates — sociology's three lenses, the law encountered as real contracts, dismissals and complaints, and democracy measured against Mongolia's own survey numbers.
