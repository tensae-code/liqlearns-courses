# Hands-on — State Services And the 1111 Centre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 5.4 "State services"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What the state does for the citizen day to day — a state service is the activity by which state bodies, within the Constitution and laws, carry out their purposes for citizens, funded by the taxes citizens pay, and aimed at a decent quality of life, accessible social services and less poverty and unemployment; its new faces — the Government's Citizen and Public Relations Centre, the "1111 centre" (1,678 citizens applied in April 2017 alone; 1,010 requests resolved, 668 in process; the ministries' league table of who was asked what), the One-Stop Service, and the state-service kiosk (ТҮЦ машин), which since 2013 has issued twenty-four kinds of reference — civil registration, identity-card, residence, marriage, tax, vehicle, licence, social-insurance — near the citizen's home; the citizen's side of the bargain (services are received by those who meet their duties — taxes paid truly and on time); and the case of the woman whose health-insurance booklet number was registered wrong, and who paid out of pocket for care her contributions had already bought. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
