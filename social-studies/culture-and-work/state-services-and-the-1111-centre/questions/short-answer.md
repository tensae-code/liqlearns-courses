# State Services And the 1111 Centre — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 5.4 "State services" — What the state does for the citizen day to day — a state service is the activity by which state bodies, within the Constitution and laws, carry out their purposes for citizens, funded by the taxes citizens pay, and aimed at a decent quality of life, accessible social services and less poverty and unemployment; its new faces — the Government's Citizen and Public Relations Centre, the "1111 centre" (1,678 citizens applied in April 2017 alone; 1,010 requests resolved, 668 in process; the ministries' league table of who was asked what), the One-Stop Service, and the state-service kiosk (ТҮЦ машин), which since 2013 has issued twenty-four kinds of reference — civil registration, identity-card, residence, marriage, tax, vehicle, licence, social-insurance — near the citizen's home; the citizen's side of the bargain (services are received by those who meet their duties — taxes paid truly and on time); and the case of the woman whose health-insurance booklet number was registered wrong, and who paid out of pocket for care her contributions had already bought (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the senior book teaches society as a science and the state as a machine the citizen operates — sociology's three lenses, the law encountered as real contracts, dismissals and complaints, and democracy measured against Mongolia's own survey numbers (any close wording).**
