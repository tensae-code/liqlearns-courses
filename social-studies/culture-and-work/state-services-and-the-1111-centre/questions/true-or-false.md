# State Services And the 1111 Centre — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: what the state does for the citizen day to day — a state service is the activity by which state bodies, within the Constitution and laws, carry out their purposes for citizens, funded by the taxes citizens pay, and aimed at a decent quality of life, accessible social services and less poverty and unemployment; its new faces — the Government's Citizen and Public Relations Centre, the "1111 centre" (1,678 citizens applied in April 2017 alone; 1,010 requests resolved, 668 in process; the ministries' league table of who was asked what), the One-Stop Service, and the state-service kiosk (ТҮЦ машин), which since 2013 has issued twenty-four kinds of reference — civil registration, identity-card, residence, marriage, tax, vehicle, licence, social-insurance — near the citizen's home; the citizen's side of the bargain (services are received by those who meet their duties — taxes paid truly and on time); and the case of the woman whose health-insurance booklet number was registered wrong, and who paid out of pocket for care her contributions had already bought.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
