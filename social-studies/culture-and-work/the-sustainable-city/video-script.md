# Video script — The Sustainable City

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 28), teaches: Lesson 2: Positive steps in the world's cities — waste management, patterns of water and electricity use, sewage disposal, cutting pollution; urban furniture (پارک‌ها، نورپردازی و روشنایی معابر، تابلوها، کف‌پوش پیاده‌روها) for all residents; smart cities, whose citizens do their administrative work over the internet without visiting offices; the sustainable city (شهر پایدار) — residents' social, cultural and economic needs met fittingly and fairly — as the main goal of today's city managers and planners; planners measuring each land use's share of the city's area, and the street and transport network.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
