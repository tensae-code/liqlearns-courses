# Hands-on — The Sustainable City

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 2: Positive steps in the world's cities — waste management, patterns of water and electricity use, sewage disposal, cutting pollution; urban furniture (پارک‌ها، نورپردازی و روشنایی معابر، تابلوها، کف‌پوش پیاده‌روها) for all residents; smart cities, whose citizens do their administrative work over the internet without visiting offices; the sustainable city (شهر پایدار) — residents' social, cultural and economic needs met fittingly and fairly — as the main goal of today's city managers and planners; planners measuring each land use's share of the city's area, and the street and transport network. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
