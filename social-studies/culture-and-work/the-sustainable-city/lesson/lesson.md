## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
