# Video script — The Universal Declaration of Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 2 Estudios Sociales 2do EGB, printed p. 87 (Tema 2, printed pp. 87–113), teaches: Through a story of colours, that all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights, how rights are respected at school, and that rights come with duties.

Ecuador's own way: the transition-curricular Grade 2 book is a workbook of songs, stories and activity spreads organised in three sections that always start from the child — family first, then school and community, then Ecuador and South America — with rights taught from the family outward to universal human rights.
