## Sub lesson 1 — The Universal Declaration of Human Rights

Ecuador Grade 2 Estudios Sociales 2do EGB, printed p. 87 (Tema 2, printed pp. 87–113), teaches: Through a story of colours, that all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights, how rights are respected at school, and that rights come with duties. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador's own way: the transition-curricular Grade 2 book is a workbook of songs, stories and activity spreads organised in three sections that always start from the child — family first, then school and community, then Ecuador and South America — with rights taught from the family outward to universal human rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
