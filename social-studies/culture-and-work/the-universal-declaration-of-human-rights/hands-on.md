# Hands-on — The Universal Declaration of Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Through a story of colours, that all people are born free and equal in dignity and rights, how rights are respected at school, and that rights come with duties. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
