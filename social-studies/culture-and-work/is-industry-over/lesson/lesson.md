# Is Industry Over?

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Folha 9: A indústria já era? / Is industry over? — industrial change in Brazil, as Brazil's Grade 10 Geografia (SEED-PR) teaches it in one of its 17 Folhas, each opening with a provocative question. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Is industry over? — the opening question

Folha 9 asks A indústria já era? / Is industry over? The question is provocative on purpose: it asks the learner to test whether industry, as it was, has ended, rather than assuming it goes on unchanged.

## Sub lesson 2 — Industrial change in Brazil

The Folha teaches industrial change in Brazil: industry in Brazil has changed, and the lesson examines that change through the opening question. Whether industry is over is answered from the change itself, not from the question alone.

Brazil's book has no chapters in the old style: it is 17 "Folhas" (sheets) like this one, each opening with a provocative question, grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
