# Hands-on — Is Industry Over?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ask the question locally
Name one factory or industrial workplace you know and write whether it is the same as it was, changed, or closed, as Folha 9 asks: A indústria já era?

## Describe the change
Write a short account of one industrial change in Brazil, in your own words, as the lesson teaches industrial change in Brazil.
