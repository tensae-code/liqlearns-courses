# Video script — Is Industry Over?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Folha 9 asks A indústria já era? / Is industry over? The question is provocative on purpose: it asks the learner to test whether industry, as it was, has ended, rather than assuming it goes on unchanged.

The Folha teaches industrial change in Brazil: industry in Brazil has changed, and the lesson examines that change through the opening question. Whether industry is over is answered from the change itself, not from the question alone.
