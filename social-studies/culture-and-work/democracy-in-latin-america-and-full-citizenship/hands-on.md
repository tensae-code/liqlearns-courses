# Hands-on — Democracy in Latin America and Full Citizenship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 33 La democracia como forma de organización política
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Democracy as political organisation gives priority to the human being and builds citizenship; full (integral) citizenship joins political, civil and social citizenship; Latin America's non-democratic regimes (military dictatorships) gave way to transitions, most countries beginning democratic life in the 1980s, though the risk of authoritarian return remains; CEPAL data show poverty falling from 43.9% (2002) to 30.8% (2019).
