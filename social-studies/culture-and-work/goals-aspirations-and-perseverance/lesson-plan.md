# Lesson plan — Goals Aspirations and Perseverance

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Goals Aspirations and Perseverance
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Honduras, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Goals Aspirations and Perseverance — what I want to be when I grow up; the most important goal is becoming a better person who helps others; will, study, dedication and perseverance reach goals — as Honduras's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Goals Aspirations and Perseverance — what I want to be when I grow up; the most important goal is becoming a better person who helps others; will, study, dedication and perseverance reach goals.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
