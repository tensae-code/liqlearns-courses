# Hands-on — Goals Aspirations and Perseverance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 2 Mis aspiraciones
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What I want to be when I grow up; the most important goal is becoming a better person who helps others; will, study, dedication and perseverance reach goals. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 1 Recuerdo e imagino
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A life plan (plan de vida) — a detailed list of what I want in the future, with questions (what do I want to be, in how long, what service will I give others); a plan needs discipline, organisation, commitment, effort, responsibility and punctuality. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 2 La vida que yo quiero
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Believe "sí podemos" — set personal objectives and fight for the life you want; never stop dreaming, because dreams become goals; positive attitudes and putting learning into practice bring success. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 1 Así quiero ser
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A life project (proyecto de vida) organises the actions that reach our goals and must be realistic, accepting our potential and limits; Morazán, self-taught after only 18 months of school, is the model; parents are our first life models, and an autobiography gathers what identifies us as unique persons. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 1 Conociendo mis vocaciones
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Vocation and profession — work done with vocation is done with greater care and enjoyed; pedagogy and psychology recommend the vocation be a personal, voluntary and rational decision; choosing a career by institutional offer rather than vocation is the error the lesson warns against, and adults are the examples for the decision. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 4 Construyendo un proyecto de vida
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Life cannot be managed as a game of chance — a life project is the whole of what the person wants to be and what they will do, with the possibilities of achieving it; the lesson builds it with Robert Dilts's life-project tree and its formulating questions (what are our goals, what do we want to be after school and university, where are we going). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 1, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Vocation is not a list to look up but a passion woken by doing many activities, and a person may hold several vocations at once; personal satisfaction in the work done well is self-realisation (autorrealización) — giving everything within our reach to make dreams and life projects concrete stage by stage — and the family's counsel, weighing each profession's pros and cons from its knowledge of the national occupational field, eases the choice. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Secuencia 1 Vida y facetas
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A professional is not only a title-holder — professionalism also means commitment, ethics and excellence in the work; beneath it stands the human vocation, an interior call, unique and unrepeatable in each person, to develop one's own potentialities in commitment to one's neighbour, the world and transcendence, not in selfish enclosure; a person holds not one vocation but many, and choosing a career weighs likings against abilities, economic and social possibilities and the participation in society the occupation allows — a choice without labour outlet ends in marginalisation and frustration, while recognition gratifies more than money. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
