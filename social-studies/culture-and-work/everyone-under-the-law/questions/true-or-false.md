# Everyone Under the Law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: in a democracy the law rules, not men — no one above the law, no arrest without a charge the written law knows, no detention without the court's leave, no confession forced by the police and none taken as evidence, no second trial after acquittal, no distinction in court by language, race, sex, wealth, caste, politics or rank, and everyone innocent until proved guilty.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Sri Lanka's student books only, named in lesson/sources.md.**
