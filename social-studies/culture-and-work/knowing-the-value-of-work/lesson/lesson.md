# Knowing the Value of Work

## Sub lesson 1 — (2) はたらくことのよさをかんじて (Feel the good of working)

Japan Grades 1–2 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 130), teaches: Working people's faces shine; everyone in town, school and home works for someone; my own week on lunch duty, wiping the serving table clean and being thanked. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — 読みもの「森のゆうびんやさん」("The Forest Postman")

Japan Grades 1–2 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 134), teaches: Bear delivers letters and parcels house to house through the forest, even in the snow, and the forest animals rejoice (reading). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — (2) 働くことの大切さを知って (Know the value of working)

Japan Grades 3–4 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 130), teaches: Why people work, the jobs done for everyone at school (cleaning duty and the rest), and workers who shine at their work, including a heart surgeon. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Japan's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 1): MEXT's own moral reader for the youngest pupils is built from short stories and picture tales around four value topics per unit — orderly living, doing your own work, doing willingly what is right, and honesty — each story followed by talk-it-over pages for school, home and neighbourhood. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 5 — Japan's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 2): the middle pupils' reader widens in the same four steps as the other two Doutoku books — raise yourself, relate to people, feel life, relate to everyone — and plants the first civics inside moral teaching: society's rules are introduced through a banned swing the class wins back by making rules, and fairness is taught as never shutting anyone out. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
