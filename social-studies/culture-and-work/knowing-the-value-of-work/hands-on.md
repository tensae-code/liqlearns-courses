# Hands-on — Knowing the Value of Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: working people's faces shine; everyone in town, school and home works for someone; my own week on lunch duty, wiping the serving table clean and being thanked. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
