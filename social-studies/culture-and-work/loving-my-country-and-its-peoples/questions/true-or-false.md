# Loving My Country and Its Peoples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Myanmar is village and town together, and its eight ethnic peoples (Kachin, Kayah, Karen, Chin, Bamar, Mon, Rakhine, Shan) in their own dress, hands joined around the Independence Monument.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: Myanmar on the map with its ethnic children in their own dress under the flag, and the "blood-relations" song naming the peoples one by one.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: loving your native place and the whole union — highlanders and plains people as siblings in the "Our Union" poem, different languages, one national spirit.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: love of country through the "Forever Myanmar" song and the "Our grandparents' heritage" poem.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Myanmar's student book(s) and its Grade 6 teacher guide, named in lesson/sources.md, the guide as evidence only.**
