# Hands-on — Loving My Country and Its Peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A person who loves the country
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Myanmar is village and town together, and its eight ethnic peoples (Kachin, Kayah, Karen, Chin, Bamar, Mon, Rakhine, Shan) in their own dress, hands joined around the Independence Monument. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Our special country
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Myanmar on the map with its ethnic children in their own dress under the flag, and the "blood-relations" song naming the peoples one by one. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cherish the motherland
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Loving your native place and the whole union — highlanders and plains people as siblings in the "Our Union" poem, different languages, one national spirit. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Our country that we love
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Love of country through the "Forever Myanmar" song and the "Our grandparents' heritage" poem. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
