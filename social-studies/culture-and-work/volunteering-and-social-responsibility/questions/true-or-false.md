# Volunteering and Social Responsibility — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: volunteering (التطوع) defined as every individual or group effort aimed at a public benefit without return or wage, done willingly and never forced; the social responsibility of the private sector (a share of profits for charities, centres for orphans and the elderly, clubs and hospitals); and the National Volunteering Platform aiming to register 200,000 volunteers
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries.
**Answer: True**
