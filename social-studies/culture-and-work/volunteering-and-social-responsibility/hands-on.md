# Hands-on — Volunteering and Social Responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Volunteering
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: volunteering (التطوع) defined as every individual or group effort aimed at a public benefit without return or wage, done willingly and never forced; the social responsibility of the private sector (a share of profits for charities, centres for orphans and the elderly, clubs and hospitals); and the National Volunteering Platform aiming to register 200,000 volunteers Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
