# Video script — Volunteering and Social Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G6 Term 1, printed p. 80, teaches: volunteering (التطوع) defined as every individual or group effort aimed at a public benefit without return or wage, done willingly and never forced; the social responsibility of the private sector (a share of profits for charities, centres for orphans and the elderly, clubs and hospitals); and the National Volunteering Platform aiming to register 200,000 volunteers

United Arab Emirates' own way: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries.
