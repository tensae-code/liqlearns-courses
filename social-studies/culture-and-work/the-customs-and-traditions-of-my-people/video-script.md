# Video script — The Customs and Traditions of My People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 12–21), teaches: What makes the Cuban nation — its territory, its people and their mixture, the Spanish language, and its living traditions and customs: the danzón as national dance, carnival, traditional games and songs, learned as part of belonging to the patria.

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors).
