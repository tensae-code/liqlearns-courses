# Hands-on — The Customs and Traditions of My People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what makes the Cuban nation — its territory, its people and their mixture, the Spanish language, and its living traditions and customs: the danzón as national dance, carnival, traditional games and songs, learned as part of belonging to the patria. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
