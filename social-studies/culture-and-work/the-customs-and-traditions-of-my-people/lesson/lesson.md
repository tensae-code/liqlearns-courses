# The Customs and Traditions of My People

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what makes the Cuban nation — its territory, its people and their mixture, the Spanish language, and its living traditions and customs: the danzón as national dance, carnival, traditional games and songs, learned as part of belonging to the patria — as Cuba's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Customs and Traditions of My People

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 12–21), teaches: What makes the Cuban nation — its territory, its people and their mixture, the Spanish language, and its living traditions and customs: the danzón as national dance, carnival, traditional games and songs, learned as part of belonging to the patria. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
