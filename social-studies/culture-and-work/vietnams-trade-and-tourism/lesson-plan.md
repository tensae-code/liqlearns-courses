# Lesson plan — Vietnams Trade And Tourism

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Vietnams Trade And Tourism
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-14).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bài 22: Vietnam's trade and tourism — domestic trade (retail sales and consumer services of 6,447.4 thousand billion dong in 2024; fast-growing e-commerce), foreign trade (a 24.9 billion USD surplus in 2024; exports 405.9 billion against imports 381.0 billion, electronics and machinery leading) and tourism (33.6 million served trips in 2024, 10.7 million of them international; revenue 79.0 thousand billion dong; the national tourism areas and the 2024 tourism map; sustainable tourism) — as Vietnam's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
