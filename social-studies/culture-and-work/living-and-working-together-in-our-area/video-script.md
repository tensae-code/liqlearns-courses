# Video script — Living and Working Together in Our Area

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sri Lanka Grade 6 Civic Education (printed p. 50), teaches: Why people live and work together — co-operation at shramadana campaigns, festivals, funerals and disasters, and what it yields: goodwill, brotherhood, unity, saved time and money, success.

Sri Lanka Grade 6 Civic Education (printed p. 57), teaches: The voluntary societies that serve an area and what each provides — co-operative, farmer, fishery, rural development, funeral, youth, sports, environment and lending societies.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 6 Civic Education): civics starts inside the child's own school — its history, its people and their duties, its rules, symbols and the pupil's own rights — then widens to the region (the Grama Niladhari ladder up to the province, the officers and councils that serve it) and closes with the good citizen's virtues: polite speech, listening, discipline, telling good from bad, using resources frugally, guarding public property and preserving the local identity with pride.
