# Hands-on — Living and Working Together in Our Area

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## 2.2 Living co-operatively with the people of the area
Work through this part with your hands: why people live and work together — co-operation at shramadana campaigns, festivals, funerals and disasters, and what it yields: goodwill, brotherhood, unity, saved time and money, success. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## 2.3 Societies in the region
Work through this part with your hands: the voluntary societies that serve an area and what each provides — co-operative, farmer, fishery, rural development, funeral, youth, sports, environment and lending societies. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: civics starts inside the child's own school — its history, its people and their duties, its rules, symbols and the pupil's own rights — then widens to the region (the Grama Niladhari ladder up to the province, the officers and councils that serve it) and closes with the good citizen's virtues: polite speech, listening, discipline, telling good from bad, using resources frugally, guarding public property and preserving the local identity with pride.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
