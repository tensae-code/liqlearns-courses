# Hands-on — Who is a Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what citizenship, citizen, resident, native and foreigner mean, and who can be an Eritrean citizen — worked from the Eritrean Constitution through Robel, whose mother is Sudanese and father Eritrean. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
