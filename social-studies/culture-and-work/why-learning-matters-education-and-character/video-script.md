Pakistan's Grade 3 Ethics3 (printed pp. 25–26), teaches: Ch. 2 Education (تعلیم) — teaches: knowledge makes the human — it polishes character, teaches manners and one's rights and duties, and lifts a whole nation; seeking it is every person's duty.

Pakistan's own way: the Class 3 Ethics book grounds every value in religion itself — good manners are what every faith commands — and spends its longest stretch on the equal honour of every human life, rich or poor.
