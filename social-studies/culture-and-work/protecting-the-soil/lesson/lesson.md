# Protecting the Soil

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: soil is a habitat, a water source and food for people, and people are its greatest polluters — quarries, dumps, chemicals and floods damage it; the protective measures: less waste, recycling, planting forests, natural fertiliser, fewer chemicals, and checking soil quality — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Значај и заштита земљишта (Importance and protection of the soil)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed pp. 74–75), teaches: soil is a habitat, a water source and food for people, and people are its greatest polluters — quarries, dumps, chemicals and floods damage it; the protective measures: less waste, recycling, planting forests, natural fertiliser, fewer chemicals, and checking soil quality. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
