# Hands-on — Protecting the Soil

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Значај и заштита земљишта (Importance and protection of the soil)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: soil is a habitat, a water source and food for people, and people are its greatest polluters — quarries, dumps, chemicals and floods damage it; the protective measures: less waste, recycling, planting forests, natural fertiliser, fewer chemicals, and checking soil quality. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
