# Protecting the Soil — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Значај и заштита земљишта (Importance and protection of the soil) — soil is a habitat, a water source and food for people, and people are its greatest polluters — quarries, dumps, chemicals and floods damage it; the protective measures: less waste, recycling, planting forests, natural fertiliser, fewer chemicals, and checking soil quality (any close wording).**
