# Protecting the Soil — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Soil is a habitat, a water source and food for people, and people are its greatest polluters — quarries, dumps, chemicals and floods damage it; the protective measures: less waste, recycling, planting forests, natural fertiliser, fewer chemicals, and checking soil quality.
**Answer: True**
