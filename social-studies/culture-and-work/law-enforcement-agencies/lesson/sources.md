**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Bahamas, Belarus).**

Bahamas' own way (Grade 5): Grade 5 civics looks outward — law enforcement at home, then The Bahamas inside the Caribbean and the Commonwealth, and conflict settled by resolution.

Bahamas' own way (Grade 6): Grade 6 is the state year — government type, elections, courts, the constitution and citizenship are taught as one civics block closing primary school.

Searchable tags: law enforcement agencies, law enforcement, Bahamas, social studies, culture-and-work, Belarus

Belarus's own way (Grade 11): The whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.
