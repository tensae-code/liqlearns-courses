# Culture — what culture is — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nothing human stands outside culture.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture is found ready-made in nature.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Culture shapes behaviour as people acquire it from others.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Artefacts are one of culture's parts in the guide's list.
**Answer: True**
