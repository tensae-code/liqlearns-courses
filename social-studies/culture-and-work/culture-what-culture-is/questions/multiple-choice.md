# Culture — what culture is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture is —
a) Nature ready-made  b) A society's whole way of life  c) Only art and music  d) Only old customs
**Answer: b) A society's whole way of life**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture is made, learned and —
a) Bought  b) Inherited in the blood  c) Accumulated through history  d) Found in the forest
**Answer: c) Accumulated through history**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names culture's parts (teacher guide — evidence)?
a) Symbols, language, religion, beliefs, norms, values, artefacts  b) Rivers, mountains, rain, soil  c) Tools only  d) Laws only
**Answer: a) Symbols, language, religion, beliefs, norms, values, artefacts**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** A child copies an elder's greeting. That is culture passing by —
a) Invention  b) Imitation  c) Trade  d) Weather
**Answer: b) Imitation**
