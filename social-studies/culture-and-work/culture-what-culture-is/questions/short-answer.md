# Culture — what culture is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two material things and two spiritual values that belong to culture.
**Answer:** Material things people make — for example tools or dress; spiritual values people hold — for example beliefs or ideas of beauty.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three of culture's parts from the guide's list.
**Answer:** Any three of: symbols, language, religion, beliefs, norms, values, artefacts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Through what three routes does social transmission work?
**Answer:** Teaching, imitation and other forms of social transmission.
