# Hands-on — Culture — what culture is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
List five things from one ordinary day at home — a greeting, a food, a tool, a song, a rule — and sort each under the guide's parts list: symbol, language, religion, belief, norm, value or artefact.

## Activity 2
Watch a younger child learn one custom — a greeting, a coffee step, a game rule. Write whether they learned it by being taught, by imitating, or by the group passing it on.

## Activity 3
Ask an elder: what is one thing people do today that their grandparents did not? That is culture growing and widening.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say what culture is in one sentence, in your own words. Name culture's parts from the guide's list (teacher guide — evidence). Explain how a child acquires culture — teaching, imitation and social transmission (teacher guide — evidence).
