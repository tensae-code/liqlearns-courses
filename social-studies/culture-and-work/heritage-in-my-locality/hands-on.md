# Hands-on — Heritage in My Locality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Family and community heritage in two forms — tangible (the old Friday Mosque of Fenfushi, monuments, objects) and intangible (language, folklore, music, festivals like Eid) — and why both should be preserved. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Maldives's way
Say how Maldives's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
