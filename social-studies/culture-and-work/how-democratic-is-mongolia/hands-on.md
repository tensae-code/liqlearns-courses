# Hands-on — How Democratic Is Mongolia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 5.2 "The state of democracy in Mongolia: an assessment"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Democracy audited with data — Churchill's "worst form of government except all the others" as the epigraph; the Asian Barometer's Mongolia tables for 2006, 2010 and 2014, scoring the values one by one (do officials who break the law get punished — 62.7, then 27.6, then 42.8; is the press free to criticise the government — 78.6, 75.4, 72.7; do citizens feel they can influence decisions); Freedom House's 2016 verdict — Mongolia "free" at 1.5, up from "partly free" in 2001; corruption measured (Transparency International 2016: 38 points, 87th of 176) and named as the chief solvent of democratic values; and citizens' own mixed report — half calling democracy's development average, a fifth poor. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
