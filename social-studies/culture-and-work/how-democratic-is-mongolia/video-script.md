# Video script — How Democratic Is Mongolia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 10 Social Studies, printed p. 106, teaches: Democracy audited with data — Churchill's "worst form of government except all the others" as the epigraph; the Asian Barometer's Mongolia tables for 2006, 2010 and 2014, scoring the values one by one (do officials who break the law get punished — 62.7, then 27.6, then 42.8; is the press free to criticise the government — 78.6, 75.4, 72.7; do citizens feel they can influence decisions); Freedom House's 2016 verdict — Mongolia "free" at 1.5, up from "partly free" in 2001; corruption measured (Transparency International 2016: 38 points, 87th of 176) and named as the chief solvent of democratic values; and citizens' own mixed report — half calling democracy's development average, a fifth poor.

Mongolia's own way (Book 8): the senior book teaches society as a science and the state as a machine the citizen operates — sociology's three lenses, the law encountered as real contracts, dismissals and complaints, and democracy measured against Mongolia's own survey numbers.
