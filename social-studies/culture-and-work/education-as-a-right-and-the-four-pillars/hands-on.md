# Hands-on — Education as a Right and the Four Pillars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 5 Educación: ruta del conocimiento
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Education is the best road to success and has carried social prestige since Egyptians, Greeks, Romans, Maya and Aztecs; the Delors report "Learning: the treasure within" (UN) sets the four pillars of modern education — learning to know, to be, to do and to live together, including the intelligent, peaceful solution of conflicts; Honduras's exclusion is quantified (school coverage 25% for adolescents in its figures; illiteracy 12.8%, some 900,000 people), making defence of the right to education a duty. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 38, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The right to education is a fundamental, universal human right: it lets every person acquire knowledge and reach a full social life, and is vital for the economic, social and cultural development of all societies; it means free compulsory primary education for all, an obligation to develop secondary education accessible to every child, equitable access to higher education, and basic education for those who never completed primary school; through education, common principles and a society's values pass to new generations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 5, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The global scenario needs citizens who exercise democracy critically and build knowledge, so education must evolve from the school that served industrial society to one that prepares for the knowledge society; literacy is not mere decoding but a form of representing the world and participating in it, and the basic skill-set now runs to information handling, communication, problem-solving, critical thought, creativity, innovation, autonomy, collaboration and teamwork (21st Century Skills); UNESCO (2013) is cited for the State's commitment to guarantee the right to education so it is a reality for every human being, not only an idea. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Secuencia 3 Estudiar ¿para qué?
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Each person is unique and unrepeatable, and education — at home and at school — forms the personality inseparably from it; study is an activity of the intelligence for understanding, and it silently trains labour, order, strength, justice and responsibility, with techniques of its own (underline the essential, outline, prefer books over superficial web pages); to study well and reach school success three things are needed — poder (having the faculties), querer (the motivation, whose lack — not lack of intelligence — is why many fail) and saber estudiar. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
