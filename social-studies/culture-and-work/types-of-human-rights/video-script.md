The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine Grade 6 Social Studies Part 1, printed p. 85, teaches: rights divide into civil and political rights (freedom — of expression and opinion, of knowledge and thought — the right to life, equality before the law, dignity such as a good reputation, respect for privacy, and the right to stand and to vote in elections); economic rights (the right to work and to obtain a suitable standard of living); social rights (education, health care, suitable food and drink, security and safety); and environmental rights (living in a clean environment free of pollution, and a share in protecting it and in the work of development).

Palestine's own way: the constitution is taught from Palestine's own draft Basic Law — pupils extract legal rules from its actual articles (the people as source of the authorities, a democratic parliamentary system, equality before the law) before they are given the definition, and the three authorities are each named by the Palestinian institution that holds them.
