# Hands-on — Types of Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Types of Human Rights
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Rights divide into civil and political rights (freedom — of expression and opinion, of knowledge and thought — the right to life, equality before the law, dignity such as a good reputation, respect for privacy, and the right to stand and to vote in elections); economic rights (the right to work and to obtain a suitable standard of living); social rights (education, health care, suitable food and drink, security and safety); and environmental rights (living in a clean environment free of pollution, and a share in protecting it and in the work of development). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
