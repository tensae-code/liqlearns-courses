Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the road ran from the Tanzimat and Islahat edicts reordering rights and freedoms, through the First and Second Constitutional Monarchies that opened a parliament and let the people join government in a limited way, to the Republic, women's political rights, and the first multi-party general election of 21 July 1946, after which different political views could win representation by election Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
