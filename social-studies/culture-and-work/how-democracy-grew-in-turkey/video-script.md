Turkey Grade 7 Sosyal Bilgiler 2. Kitap, printed p. 38, teaches: the road ran from the Tanzimat and Islahat edicts reordering rights and freedoms, through the First and Second Constitutional Monarchies that opened a parliament and let the people join government in a limited way, to the Republic, women's political rights, and the first multi-party general election of 21 July 1946, after which different political views could win representation by election

Turkey's own way: the course ends where citizenship becomes constitutional: thirteen-year-olds read Articles 1–3 of their own constitution (the ones that cannot even be proposed for change), separate the state's three powers, walk democracy's road from the Tanzimat edicts to the first multi-party election of 1946, and finish by naming the behaviours that wound democracy in daily life.
