# Writing Carries Culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Writing Carries Culture — writing is among the oldest means of communication — picture writing in Pharaonic Egypt, cuneiform in Iraq, and the Canaanites' alphabetic writing of some 4,000 years ago, its letters drawn from their environment; writing matters as a means of teaching and learning, of passing knowledge and sciences between people and peoples, of communication and expression, of recording the history and culture of nations, and of preserving people's rights from loss (any close wording).**
