# Hands-on — Writing Carries Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Writing Carries Culture
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Writing is among the oldest means of communication — picture writing in Pharaonic Egypt, cuneiform in Iraq, and the Canaanites' alphabetic writing of some 4,000 years ago, its letters drawn from their environment; writing matters as a means of teaching and learning, of passing knowledge and sciences between people and peoples, of communication and expression, of recording the history and culture of nations, and of preserving people's rights from loss. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
