# Hands-on — Youth Rights in Mexico

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Youth Rights in Mexico
Work through this part with your hands: young people are holders of rights — Mexico's own General Law of the Rights of Girls, Boys and Adolescents (2014) lists them, from life, identity and living in family to association, privacy, migrant children's rights and access to information technologies. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
