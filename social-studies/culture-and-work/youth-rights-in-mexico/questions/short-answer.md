# Youth Rights in Mexico — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Youth Rights in Mexico — young people are holders of rights — Mexico's own General Law of the Rights of Girls, Boys and Adolescents (2014) lists them, from life, identity and living in family to association, privacy, migrant children's rights and access to information technologies (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way — Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution.
