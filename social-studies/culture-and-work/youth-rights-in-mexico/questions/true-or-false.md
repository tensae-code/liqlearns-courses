# Youth Rights in Mexico — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Young people are holders of rights — Mexico's own General Law of the Rights of Girls, Boys and Adolescents (2014) lists them, from life, identity and living in family to association, privacy, migrant children's rights and access to information technologies.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Mexico's student books, named in lesson/sources.md.**
