# Hands-on — Strategies for Gender Equality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activities 6–7 Minimising barriers and promoting gender equality
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: invest in education and adult literacy, improve health care, enforce laws against early marriage and gender-based violence, reduce women's workload, train female legal professionals, run gender programmes in schools and universities, sensitise the community, set policies, and teach both genders their rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
