# Video script — Strategies for Gender Equality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 94, teaches: invest in education and adult literacy, improve health care, enforce laws against early marriage and gender-based violence, reduce women's workload, train female legal professionals, run gender programmes in schools and universities, sensitise the community, set policies, and teach both genders their rights.

South Sudan's own way: Gender equality is argued from South Sudanese lives — an elder who educates his daughters against tradition, and four named women leaders — and peace itself is put in women's hands: the book prints the view that gender equality is a tool that can end South Sudan's civil war.
