# Video script — Living Together at School Respect and Shared Rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Libya Grade 6 National Education G6 (printed p. 36), teaches: being at school obliges me to respect the rules of living together — respect for teachers, no cheating, no mocking younger or disabled pupils, keeping the school clean — and pupils themselves propose how to reduce violence at school.

Libya's own way: Grade 6 defines the citizen by a chain of relations — rights paired with duties, freedom paired with responsibility, then one lesson per circle the child belongs to (family, school, neighbourhood) — and grounds the rights lesson in named world instruments: "human rights" as the UN defines them, the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child, and UNICEF.
