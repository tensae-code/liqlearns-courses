# Lesson plan — Human Rights and Equality Before the Law

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Human Rights and Equality Before the Law
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Honduras, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Human Rights and Equality Before the Law — all human beings are equal in dignity and rights and in Honduras all citizens are equal before the law; human rights are inseparable from the human person, and the UN Universal Declaration exists to guarantee liberty, peace and justice; the Constitution (Art. 60) declares all are born free and equal in rights and that Honduras has no privileged classes; habeas corpus and amparo are the remedies when rights are violated — as Honduras's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Human Rights and Equality Before the Law — all human beings are equal in dignity and rights and in Honduras all citizens are equal before the law; human rights are inseparable from the human person, and the UN Universal Declaration exists to guarantee liberty, peace and justice; the Constitution (Art. 60) declares all are born free and equal in rights and that Honduras has no privileged classes; habeas corpus and amparo are the remedies when rights are violated.
