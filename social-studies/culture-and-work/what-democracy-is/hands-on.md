# Hands-on — What Democracy Is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 1 "Historia de la democracia"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 6 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 30, teaches: the fifth-century Athenians held democracy to be the government of the people, with all citizens taking part — but citizens were free men born in Athens; slaves, women and foreigners were excluded; their participative democracy let citizens reach public office and vote on equal terms, deciding together in assembly. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 8) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the sixth-grade book widens the lens — democracy is traced from Athens to Guatemala's own authoritarian past, and citizenship ends at the world: Guatemala's Belize claim put to its own citizens in the 2018 popular consultation on the International Court of Justice.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
