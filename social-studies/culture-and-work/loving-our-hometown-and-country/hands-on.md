# Hands-on — Loving Our Hometown and Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the town you grow up in — school, park, library, hospital, shopping street, friends — introduced in a town newspaper the pupils make, with its festivals and favourite places. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
