## Sub lesson 1 — Vaccination is My Right Every Childs Right to Health Care

Syria Grade 1 Social Studies (Syria Grade 1 Social Studies G1, printed p. 33), teaches: Vaccination as a right — every child has the right to health care, and the Syrian state provides the vaccine free to all its children to protect them from dangerous diseases. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Syria's way — the book's own teaching way

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 1): the whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
