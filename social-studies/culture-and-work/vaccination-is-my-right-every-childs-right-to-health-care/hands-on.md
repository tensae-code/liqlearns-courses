# Hands-on — Vaccination is My Right Every Childs Right to Health Care

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: vaccination as a right — every child has the right to health care, and the Syrian state provides the vaccine free to all its children to protect them from dangerous diseases. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
