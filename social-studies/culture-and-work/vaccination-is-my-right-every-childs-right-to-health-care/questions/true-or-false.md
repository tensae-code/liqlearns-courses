# Vaccination is My Right Every Childs Right to Health Care — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Vaccination as a right — every child has the right to health care, and the Syrian state provides the vaccine free to all its children to protect them from dangerous diseases.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: The whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free)..
**Answer: True**
