# Video script — Service-Industry Location Factors and Their Changes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Chapter 3 §3 teaches service-industry location factors and their changes （服务业区位因素及其变化） — what decides where services sit, and how those decisions change — and carries a geographic-information-technology application: GIS in delivery services, one of this book's two GIS applications. This chapter is read and placed from the student book itself: it is one of the chapters missing from the teacher-guide file.

China learns this inside its own books' way: senior-high human geography built entirely around China's own questions — population opens the book (world distribution, push–pull migration, carrying capacity and the Hu Huanyong line as a self-study window), then villages and towns, then where each industry sits and how that changes, then transport and regional development in both directions, closing with the environment and China's own national strategies (main functional zones, the Yangtze Economic Belt, blue-economy space); every chapter ends with a problem inquiry drawn from Chinese everyday life (migrant workers, choosing where to live, physical shops, city traffic, low-carbon food), and two sections carry geographic-information-technology applications (GIS in city management inside Chapter 2 §2; GIS in delivery services inside Chapter 3 §3). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
