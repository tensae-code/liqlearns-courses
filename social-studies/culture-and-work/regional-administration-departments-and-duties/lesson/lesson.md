## Sub lesson 1 — Regional Administration Departments and Duties

Eritrea Grade 5 ER G5 Social Studies (printed p. 5), teaches: the working departments under the administration — Economic Development (agriculture: training, inputs and animal vaccination down to village level; water, land and environment development: wells, pumps, solar panels, dams; tourism and investment), Social Services, and Infrastructure — and what each actually delivers to a village. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Eritrea's way — the book's own teaching way

Eritrea's own way: even the Tigrinya primary book teaches the state concretely — a Grade 5 child learns that their region is run by an elected council (baito), an administration and independent courts, and which department brings the water pump, the seeds and the clinic. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
