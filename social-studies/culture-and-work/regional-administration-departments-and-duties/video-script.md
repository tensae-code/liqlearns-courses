# Video script — Regional Administration Departments and Duties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eritrea Grade 5 ER G5 Social Studies (printed p. 5), teaches: The working departments under the administration — Economic Development (agriculture: training, inputs and animal vaccination down to village level; water, land and environment development: wells, pumps, solar panels, dams; tourism and investment), Social Services, and Infrastructure — and what each actually delivers to a village.

Eritrea's own way: even the Tigrinya primary book teaches the state concretely — a Grade 5 child learns that their region is run by an elected council (baito), an administration and independent courts, and which department brings the water pump, the seeds and the clinic.
