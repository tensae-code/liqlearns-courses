# Hands-on — Shura Consultation in Governance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: shura (consultation) as a distinguishing principle of Islam — the gathering of the people of decision (ahl al-hall wa-l-'aqd: officials, scholars, thinkers, notables and specialists named by the ruler) to consult on a matter with no revealed ruling and reach an opinion for the ruler; its models — Abu Bakr consulting on Umar's succession, Umar consulting on finance and creating the diwans, Uthman consulted on the Quran's differing readings. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
