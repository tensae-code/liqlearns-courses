Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 176–177), teaches: Shura (consultation) as a distinguishing principle of Islam — the gathering of the people of decision (ahl al-hall wa-l-'aqd: officials, scholars, thinkers, notables and specialists named by the ruler) to consult on a matter with no revealed ruling and reach an opinion for the ruler; its models — Abu Bakr consulting on Umar's succession, Umar consulting on finance and creating the diwans, Uthman consulted on the Quran's differing readings.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue.
