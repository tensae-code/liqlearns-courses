# Components of a Strong and Influential State

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a state's standing follows its components — its resources, capacities, institutions, production, geographic area, population and military and cultural aspects; the civilisational components (an influential geographic location, a wide area, the people's number, education and culture, and historical depth), the political components (a stable political system with effective performance and good governance, and a clear legal framework such as a basic law of governance or constitution fixing responsibilities and achieving governance), the religious components (holy places bound to great numbers of people), the military components (all arms — land, sea, air and technical — manufacture, export and training), the touristic components (historic sites and natural landmarks drawing visitors) and the national components (security and strong institutions guarding stability and interests) — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Components of a Strong and Influential State

Saudi Arabia Grade 10 Social Studies (Secondary Tracks, First Common Year) (printed pp. 11–13), teaches: A state's standing follows its components — its resources, capacities, institutions, production, geographic area, population and military and cultural aspects; the civilisational components (an influential geographic location, a wide area, the people's number, education and culture, and historical depth), the political components (a stable political system with effective performance and good governance, and a clear legal framework such as a basic law of governance or constitution fixing responsibilities and achieving governance), the religious components (holy places bound to great numbers of people), the military components (all arms — land, sea, air and technical — manufacture, export and training), the touristic components (historic sites and natural landmarks drawing visitors) and the national components (security and strong institutions guarding stability and interests). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
