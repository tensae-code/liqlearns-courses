# Components of a Strong and Influential State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Components of a Strong and Influential State — a state's standing follows its components — its resources, capacities, institutions, production, geographic area, population and military and cultural aspects; the civilisational components (an influential geographic location, a wide area, the people's number, education and culture, and historical depth), the political components (a stable political system with effective performance and good governance, and a clear legal framework such as a basic law of governance or constitution fixing responsibilities and achieving governance), the religious components (holy places bound to great numbers of people), the military components (all arms — land, sea, air and technical — manufacture, export and training), the touristic components (historic sites and natural landmarks drawing visitors) and the national components (security and strong institutions guarding stability and interests) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way — Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 10 Tracks book starts from the components that make a state strong and influential and Saudi Arabia's own foundations and components, then follows the Kingdom's work for Arab, Islamic and international causes, and surveys the organisations it belongs to — the European Union as a model of integration, the League of Arab States, the Muslim World League, the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation, OPEC, the United Nations and the G20. (any close wording).**
