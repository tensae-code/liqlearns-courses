# Hands-on — Components of a Strong and Influential State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a state's standing follows its components — its resources, capacities, institutions, production, geographic area, population and military and cultural aspects; the civilisational components (an influential geographic location, a wide area, the people's number, education and culture, and historical depth), the political components (a stable political system with effective performance and good governance, and a clear legal framework such as a basic law of governance or constitution fixing responsibilities and achieving governance), the religious components (holy places bound to great numbers of people), the military components (all arms — land, sea, air and technical — manufacture, export and training), the touristic components (historic sites and natural landmarks drawing visitors) and the national components (security and strong institutions guarding stability and interests). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
