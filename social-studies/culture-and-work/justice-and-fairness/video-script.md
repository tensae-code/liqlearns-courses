# Video script — Justice and Fairness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Libya Grade 5 National Education G5 (printed p. 33), teaches: justice is a supreme value: giving every right-holder their due without regard to colour, sex or nature; justice in the family, the courts, trade and the school — including the extra exam time a pupil with Down syndrome needs, which is justice, not favour (Convention on the Rights of the Child art. 2).

Libya's own way: Grade 5 is a pure values book — eight lessons, each a single civic virtue (equality, justice, solidarity, tolerance, accepting the other, dialogue), each anchored in a real instrument a child can hold: the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, UNESCO's 1995 Declaration on Tolerance, and the Libyan Scouts' "A World of Peace for Children" charter, which the pupil signs.
