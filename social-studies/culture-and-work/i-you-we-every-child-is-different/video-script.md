# Video script — I You We Every Child Is Different

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 1 Civic Education, printed p. 34, teaches: Every child has their own traits — the fastest, strongest, bravest, most patient in the class are named in a table — yet "no two people are alike, and no saddle fits two horses: we Mongol children, descendants of Chinggis, all bear equal rights and duties".

Mongolia's own way (Book 1): civic education for six-year-olds is pure Mongolian custom — greeting elders the Mongolian way, the ger and the five snouts, colour symbolism, the biyelgee dance and the Tsagaan Sar greeting embrace — with traditional script verses running down every page beside the Cyrillic.
