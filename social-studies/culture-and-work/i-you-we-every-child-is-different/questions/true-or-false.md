# I You We Every Child Is Different — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: every child has their own traits — the fastest, strongest, bravest, most patient in the class are named in a table — yet "no two people are alike, and no saddle fits two horses: we Mongol children, descendants of Chinggis, all bear equal rights and duties".
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
