# Hands-on — I You We Every Child Is Different

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 15 Би, чи, бид (I, you, we)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every child has their own traits — the fastest, strongest, bravest, most patient in the class are named in a table — yet "no two people are alike, and no saddle fits two horses: we Mongol children, descendants of Chinggis, all bear equal rights and duties". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
