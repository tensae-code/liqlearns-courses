# Hands-on — Rights and Duties at Home and at School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 2 C. Hak dan Kewajibanku di Rumah, Bab 2 D. Hak dan Kewajibanku di Sekolah, Bab 2 C. Mendapatkan Hak dan Melakukan Kewajiban, Bab 3 B. Mengenal Kewajiban Anak, Panduan Khusus Bab 2: Aku Patuh Aturan — teaches: my rights and my duties at home, taught as a pair; my rights and my duties at school, taught as a pair; receiving rights and doing duties — the pair stated as one lesson; a child's obligations — listing your duties, home tidiness rules, and the duty to be brave and honest; guidance for school rules and agreements and rights/duties at home and school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indonesia's way
Say how Indonesia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
