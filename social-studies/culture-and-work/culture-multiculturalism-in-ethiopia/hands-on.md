# Hands-on — Culture — multiculturalism in Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
With partners, dramatise respectful, tolerant living together: one short scene in a market or school where different cultures meet well.

## Activity 2
Count the cultures present in your own school or neighbourhood by language and custom. Write the count and one thing each adds.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say what multiculturalism is and name four ways cultures in one place can differ. Give Ethiopia's number: more than 80 nations, nationalities and peoples. Name multiculturalism's great value, and say how the guide widens diversity (teacher guide — evidence).
