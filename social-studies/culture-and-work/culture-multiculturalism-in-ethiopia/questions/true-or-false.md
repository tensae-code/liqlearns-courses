# Culture — multiculturalism in Ethiopia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cultures in one place can differ in customary law and values.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A country holding many cultures holds a beauty.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Multiculturalism requires everyone to share one language.
**Answer: False**
