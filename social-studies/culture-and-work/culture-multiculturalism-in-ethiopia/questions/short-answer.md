# Culture — multiculturalism in Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four ways cultures in one place can differ.
**Answer:** Any four of: language, religion, ethnicity, customary law, custom, values.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four dimensions in the guide's widened diversity list.
**Answer:** Any four of: nationality, language, religion, race, health difference, sexual orientation, gender, age, disability, ethnicity.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How does the guide define human race as a cultural element?
**Answer:** A socially constructed category of people sharing distinctive physical characteristics such as skin colour, facial features and stature.
