# Culture — multiculturalism in Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Multiculturalism means —
a) One culture everywhere  b) Different kinds of culture present in one area  c) No customs at all  d) Cultures kept apart by law
**Answer: b) Different kinds of culture present in one area**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ethiopia holds more than —
a) 8 peoples  b) 80 nations, nationalities and peoples  c) 800 languages only  d) 18 regions
**Answer: b) 80 nations, nationalities and peoples**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The great value inside multiculturalism is —
a) Competition  b) Respect and tolerance  c) Speed  d) Uniformity
**Answer: b) Respect and tolerance**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide, race as a cultural element is —
a) A biological law  b) A socially constructed category  c) A tax class  d) A language family
**Answer: b) A socially constructed category**
