# Hands-on — Values That Guide Our Conduct

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 1 Lo que sentimos
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Values are the principles that guide our conduct and give life meaning — honesty, justice, solidarity, respect, dignity, equity, generosity, loyalty; emotions (joy, sadness, anger, fear) express feelings, positive ones (love, friendship, bravery) and negative ones (hate, resentment); attitudes are formed from what we feel, think and know. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 9 Respetamos los valores y normas
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sensitivity is perceiving and understanding others' moods and circumstances so as to act for their benefit, and it is tightly linked to solidarity — several people working jointly for shared ends; Latin America's common problems (lack of sensitivity, lack of solidarity, inequality) are answered by practising these values and permanently respecting the accepted social norms in family, community and country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 3 Cualidades personales promovidas en familia
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The home gives the first notions of appropriate conduct; honesty is taught at home and shown in every moment — no fraud, economic or academic; tolerance is fundamental for daily coexistence — understanding and accepting that not everyone is equal; values humanise us, and mutual respect is the road to a better country, society and family (Guillén Zelaya's "Lo esencial" on the dignity of work opens the lesson). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 1 La familia como formadora de valores
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A value is a quality important or desirable for a person; the family is the fundamental unit for forming them — at home values are the guidelines or virtues the family agrees in order to live together and stay faithful to them; they are learned from our first years by the example of those around us, above all the closest family. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 3 El trabajo, un valor de superación
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Values and antivalues are learned and unlearned across life in the family, the school and other social spaces; the values tied to work were driven from the Modern Age with capitalism and the Industrial Revolution; the lesson ties the value of work to fair salaries and agreeable working conditions, and notes unemployment (64% men, 36% women in its figures) pushing Hondurans to emigrate. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 3, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Family values are relations of feelings, affections and interests based on mutual respect; joy is sown first in the family and does not depend on circumstances or on having things; justice is giving each person what corresponds to them — the just person constantly respects others' rights; responsibility is assuming the consequences of one's own acts, before oneself and before others; in the family one learns that ideas and feelings deserve respect. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 3, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The human being is social by nature and work is the activity that lets them contribute their abilities to the common good, so its value is incalculable; work is at once a universal right — through it come economic, political, social and cultural development and personal autonomy — and a duty owed to family, community and society; organised movements won the improvements that dignify it (better salaries, health access, free organisation, study and technical training), and the values that must characterise the worker are named: respect, service, social justice, dignity, integrity, equality, tolerance, transparency, equity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Secuencia 2 El significado de trabajar
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Work completes the meaning of a life when it matches desires, tendencies and aptitudes, and it models the personality of whoever does it; joining the human and professional vocations brings adaptation, satisfaction, greater yield, personalisation and self-improvement; work is every activity by which the human being wins the sustenance to subsist — before capitalism it could be one's own products or barter — and its value is not measured by the type of activity but by the person who does it, the only creature who works in a programmed, rational, conscious and free way, humanising nature; the sequence adds the stages of professional experience (initial, recognition, practice) and the steps of choosing a good job well. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
