# Video script — Valuing Our National Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Nicaragua Grade 3 Convivencia y Civismo G3 (printed p. 38), teaches: Appreciating and promoting Nicaragua's national culture.

Nicaragua Grade 5 Convivencia y Civismo G5 (printed p. 31), teaches: National culture as pride and identity.

Nicaragua Grade 4 Convivencia y Civismo G4 (printed p. 36), teaches: What Nicaraguan cultural identity is made of.

Nicaragua's own way: Grade 3 civics adds Nicaragua-specific law to the values base — women's rights under Ley 779 and a Mother Earth declaration — and respect for public and private institutions as a civic duty. the upper-primary civics book teaches the state itself — Constitution, Executive and Judicial powers, the DGI tax office — and sets Nicaragua's symbols beside all six Central American neighbours'. Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State.
