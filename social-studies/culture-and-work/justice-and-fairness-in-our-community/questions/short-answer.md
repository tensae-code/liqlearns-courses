# Justice and Fairness in Our Community — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Unit 5 Activities 1–3 Meaning and promotion of justice and fairness — justice is the legal process by which fairness is administered and fairness is treating people in the right manner (opened by the story of a teacher who punished a whole class without asking); justice is promoted by equal distribution of resources, equal application of law, gender balance in job distribution and equal sharing of responsibilities — in South Sudan girls and boys both go to school, men and women get equal job opportunities, everyone including leaders faces the law equally, and government resources reach towns and remote areas alike (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Activity 7 Principles of justice and fairness in South Sudan — using courts when aggrieved to get justice; equitable sharing and distribution of national resources (the principle of equity); and ensuring human rights are respected at all levels (the justice lesson is filed NEW from this book in this wave) (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Unit 5 overview and outcomes: justice underpins the legal system — the guide adds that ideas of justice and fairness underpin the legal system, that pupils should judge the effectiveness of law and order in their community, state and country, and that law and justice in the community aim to uphold human rights (the justice lesson is filed NEW from Book 13 in this wave) (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Activity 4 Importance of equal distribution of resources — equal distribution means everyone has equal rights over the available resources; it stops resources being exploited by a few, keeps a sufficient flow to every section of society, eliminates the difference between rich and poor and supports sustainable development (the justice lesson is filed NEW from Book 13 in this wave) (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does the last part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
