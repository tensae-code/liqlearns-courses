# Hands-on — Justice and Fairness in Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 5 Activities 1–3 Meaning and promotion of justice and fairness
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: justice is the legal process by which fairness is administered and fairness is treating people in the right manner (opened by the story of a teacher who punished a whole class without asking); justice is promoted by equal distribution of resources, equal application of law, gender balance in job distribution and equal sharing of responsibilities — in South Sudan girls and boys both go to school, men and women get equal job opportunities, everyone including leaders faces the law equally, and government resources reach towns and remote areas alike. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Activity 7 Principles of justice and fairness in South Sudan
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: using courts when aggrieved to get justice; equitable sharing and distribution of national resources (the principle of equity); and ensuring human rights are respected at all levels (the justice lesson is filed NEW from this book in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unit 5 overview and outcomes: justice underpins the legal system
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the guide adds that ideas of justice and fairness underpin the legal system, that pupils should judge the effectiveness of law and order in their community, state and country, and that law and justice in the community aim to uphold human rights (the justice lesson is filed NEW from Book 13 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Activity 4 Importance of equal distribution of resources
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: equal distribution means everyone has equal rights over the available resources; it stops resources being exploited by a few, keeps a sufficient flow to every section of society, eliminates the difference between rich and poor and supports sustainable development (the justice lesson is filed NEW from Book 13 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
