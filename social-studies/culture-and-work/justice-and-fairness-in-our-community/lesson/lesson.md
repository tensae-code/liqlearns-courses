# Justice and Fairness in Our Community

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: justice is the legal process by which fairness is administered and fairness is treating people in the right manner (opened by the story of a teacher who punished a whole class without asking); justice is promoted by equal distribution of resources, equal application of law, gender balance in job distribution and equal sharing of responsibilities — in South Sudan girls and boys both go to school, men and women get equal job opportunities, everyone including leaders faces the law equally, and government resources reach towns and remote areas alike — as South Sudan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 5 Activities 1–3 Meaning and promotion of justice and fairness

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 100, teaches: justice is the legal process by which fairness is administered and fairness is treating people in the right manner (opened by the story of a teacher who punished a whole class without asking); justice is promoted by equal distribution of resources, equal application of law, gender balance in job distribution and equal sharing of responsibilities — in South Sudan girls and boys both go to school, men and women get equal job opportunities, everyone including leaders faces the law equally, and government resources reach towns and remote areas alike. ## Sub lesson 2 — Activity 7 Principles of justice and fairness in South Sudan

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 107, teaches: using courts when aggrieved to get justice; equitable sharing and distribution of national resources (the principle of equity); and ensuring human rights are respected at all levels (the justice lesson is filed NEW from this book in this wave). ## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 5 overview and outcomes: justice underpins the legal system

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies TG, printed p. 87, teaches: the guide adds that ideas of justice and fairness underpin the legal system, that pupils should judge the effectiveness of law and order in their community, state and country, and that law and justice in the community aim to uphold human rights (the justice lesson is filed NEW from Book 13 in this wave). ## Sub lesson 4 — Activity 4 Importance of equal distribution of resources

South Sudan Primary 8 P8 Social Studies SB, printed p. 62, teaches: equal distribution means everyone has equal rights over the available resources; it stops resources being exploited by a few, keeps a sufficient flow to every section of society, eliminates the difference between rich and poor and supports sustainable development (the justice lesson is filed NEW from Book 13 in this wave). ## Sub lesson 5 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way: Gender equality is argued from South Sudanese lives — an elder who educates his daughters against tradition, and four named women leaders — and peace itself is put in women's hands: the book prints the view that gender equality is a tool that can end South Sudan's civil war. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
