# Loving the Beauty of Our Surroundings

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the beauty of river, hill, flowers and wind around us, and enjoying the park without spoiling it (Nwe Nwe's story) — as Myanmar's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Our pleasant world

Myanmar Morality and Civics, Grade 2 (printed p. 61), teaches: The beauty of river, hill, flowers and wind around us, and enjoying the park without spoiling it (Nwe Nwe's story). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Appreciating the beauty of nature

Myanmar Morality and Civics Grade 3 (printed p. 61), teaches: Noticing and valuing the beauty of the nature around the native place. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way

Myanmar's own way: Grade 2 widens Grade 1's values from home to school — the same story-plus-pictures shape now carries school property, school rules, honest judgement of classroom incidents, friends with disabilities, and the whole country drawn as its ethnic children around the map. the Grade 3 units keep the story-and-poem shape but argue finer points: fairness is NOT equal shares (the hungry son gets bread, the full daughter a candy; the girl with glasses sits in front), trust is keeping a friend's weakness secret, and the clean-environment unit teaches Reduce–Reuse–Recycle by name. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
