# Loving the Beauty of Our Surroundings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Our pleasant world — The beauty of river, hill, flowers and wind around us, and enjoying the park without spoiling it (Nwe Nwe's story) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Appreciating the beauty of nature — Noticing and valuing the beauty of the nature around the native place (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 2 widens Grade 1's values from home to school — the same story-plus-pictures shape now carries school property, school rules, honest judgement of classroom incidents, friends with disabilities, and the whole country drawn as its ethnic children around the map. the Grade 3 units keep the story-and-poem shape but argue finer points: fairness is NOT equal shares (the hungry son gets bread, the full daughter a candy; the girl with glasses sits in front), trust is keeping a friend's weakness secret, and the clean-environment unit teaches Reduce–Reuse–Recycle by name.
