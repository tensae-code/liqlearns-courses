# Training Horses Camels And Oxen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 12 "The custom of training and using large livestock" (Бод малыг сургах, эдлэх ёс) — Each animal is trained at its age and in its way — the horse is broken as a two-year-old (даага) and trained to saddle and race work, the camel is nose-pegged (буйл) at three and taught to kneel (сөг) and rise (хөж) by word, the ox is trained from a шүдлэн to the cart; and the rules of kind use: water a ridden horse well, never ride a fat horse hard in summer, work the ox cart in the cool of morning and evening and rest it at midday (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other (any close wording).**
