# Training Horses Camels And Oxen — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: each animal is trained at its age and in its way — the horse is broken as a two-year-old (даага) and trained to saddle and race work, the camel is nose-pegged (буйл) at three and taught to kneel (сөг) and rise (хөж) by word, the ox is trained from a шүдлэн to the cart; and the rules of kind use: water a ridden horse well, never ride a fat horse hard in summer, work the ox cart in the cool of morning and evening and rest it at midday.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
