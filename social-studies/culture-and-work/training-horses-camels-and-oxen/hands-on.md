# Hands-on — Training Horses Camels And Oxen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 12 "The custom of training and using large livestock" (Бод малыг сургах, эдлэх ёс)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Each animal is trained at its age and in its way — the horse is broken as a two-year-old (даага) and trained to saddle and race work, the camel is nose-pegged (буйл) at three and taught to kneel (сөг) and rise (хөж) by word, the ox is trained from a шүдлэн to the cart; and the rules of kind use: water a ridden horse well, never ride a fat horse hard in summer, work the ox cart in the cool of morning and evening and rest it at midday. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
