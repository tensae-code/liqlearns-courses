Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 37, teaches: Each animal is trained at its age and in its way — the horse is broken as a two-year-old (даага) and trained to saddle and race work, the camel is nose-pegged (буйл) at three and taught to kneel (сөг) and rise (хөж) by word, the ox is trained from a шүдлэн to the cart; and the rules of kind use: water a ridden horse well, never ride a fat horse hard in summer, work the ox cart in the cool of morning and evening and rest it at midday.

Mongolia's own way (Book 4): the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other.
