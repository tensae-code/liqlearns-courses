# Hands-on — Festivals of the World's Religions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the five festivals
Draw five boxes, one for each religion in the lesson: Buddhism, Hinduism, Christianity, Islam and Judaism. In each box draw children celebrating a festival of that religion. You are matching each festival to the religion it belongs to.

## Name a festival and its religion
Name one festival from the lesson and say which religion it belongs to, and who celebrates it — the children of that religion and their families.
