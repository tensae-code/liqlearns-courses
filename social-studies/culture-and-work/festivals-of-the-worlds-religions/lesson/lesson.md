# Festivals of the World's Religions

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: In Iceland, young children meet the world's religions through five child narrators, and every religion they meet has its own festivals — special days its people celebrate. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Every religion has its festivals

Iceland's Grade 1 teacher guide for Trúarbrögðin okkar (Our Religions) teaches five world religions through five child narrators: a Buddhist, a Hindu, a Christian, a Muslim and a Jewish child. Each of these religions has festivals — special days that its people keep and celebrate, in their own way.

## Sub lesson 2 — Children's festivals — Hátíð barnanna

The guide gives the festivals their own place in the student book: Hátíð barnanna — the children's festivals — at student book p. 37, as the teacher guide presents it at printed p. 17. The festivals are met through the children themselves: what the children of each religion celebrate, each festival belonging to its own religion.
