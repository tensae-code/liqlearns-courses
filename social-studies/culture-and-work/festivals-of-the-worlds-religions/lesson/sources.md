Iceland's own way: World religions are introduced in primary school through five child narrators (a Buddhist, Hindu, Christian, Muslim and Jewish child), their festivals, food, clothing, sacred texts and symbols, including work with a world map to locate the religions. This lesson stands on that teacher guide as evidence: it teaches the festivals part in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
