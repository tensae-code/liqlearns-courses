# Festivals of the World's Religions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The five religions in Iceland's Grade 1 religions book are —
a) Buddhism, Hinduism, Christianity, Islam and Judaism  b) only Christianity  c) five Icelandic churches
**Answer: a) Buddhism, Hinduism, Christianity, Islam and Judaism**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A festival is —
a) a special day that a religion's people keep and celebrate  b) a kind of map  c) a school book
**Answer: a) a special day that a religion's people keep and celebrate**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hátíð barnanna means —
a) the children's festivals  b) the children's houses  c) the children's maps
**Answer: a) the children's festivals**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the student book, the children's festivals stand at —
a) student book p. 37  b) student book p. 1  c) the back cover
**Answer: a) student book p. 37**
