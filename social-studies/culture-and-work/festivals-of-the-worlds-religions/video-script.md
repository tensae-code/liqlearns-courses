# Video script — Festivals of the World's Religions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Iceland, Grade 1 children learn the festivals of five world religions.

Buddhism, Hinduism, Christianity, Islam and Judaism — each religion has its own festivals.

The festivals are met through the children: Hátíð barnanna, the children's festivals.

Every festival belongs to its own religion, and its own people celebrate it.
