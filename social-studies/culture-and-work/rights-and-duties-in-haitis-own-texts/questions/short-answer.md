# Rights and Duties in Haitis Own Texts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Rights and Duties in Haitis Own Texts — rights and duties read in Haiti's constitutional texts against the international declarations and conventions Haiti has ratified, a bilingual Creole/French glossary of the legal words, a comparative table of rights in national vs international texts, and what a value is and how it becomes law (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way — Rights are taught from a Haitian street-level case — out-of-school street children and restavèk children — upward: case first, then the positions of officials and press, then the constitutional and international texts, then a school project that practises the right. (any close wording).**
