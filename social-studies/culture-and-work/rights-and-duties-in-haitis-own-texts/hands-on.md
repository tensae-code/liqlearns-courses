# Hands-on — Rights and Duties in Haitis Own Texts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: rights and duties read in Haiti's constitutional texts against the international declarations and conventions Haiti has ratified, a bilingual Creole/French glossary of the legal words, a comparative table of rights in national vs international texts, and what a value is and how it becomes law. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Haiti's way
Say how Haiti's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
