## Sub lesson 1 — Rights and Duties in Haitis Own Texts

Haiti Grade 8 EC2 8e Droits devoirs valeurs (printed p. 11), teaches: rights and duties read in Haiti's constitutional texts against the international declarations and conventions Haiti has ratified, a bilingual Creole/French glossary of the legal words, a comparative table of rights in national vs international texts, and what a value is and how it becomes law. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way

Haiti's own way: Rights are taught from a Haitian street-level case — out-of-school street children and restavèk children — upward: case first, then the positions of officials and press, then the constitutional and international texts, then a school project that practises the right. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
