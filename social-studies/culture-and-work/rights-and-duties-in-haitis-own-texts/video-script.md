# Video script — Rights and Duties in Haitis Own Texts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 8 EC2 8e Droits devoirs valeurs (printed p. 11), teaches: rights and duties read in Haiti's constitutional texts against the international declarations and conventions Haiti has ratified, a bilingual Creole/French glossary of the legal words, a comparative table of rights in national vs international texts, and what a value is and how it becomes law.

Haiti's own way: Rights are taught from a Haitian street-level case — out-of-school street children and restavèk children — upward: case first, then the positions of officials and press, then the constitutional and international texts, then a school project that practises the right.
