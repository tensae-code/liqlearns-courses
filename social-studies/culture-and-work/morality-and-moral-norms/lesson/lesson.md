## Sub lesson 1 — Morality (Мораль) — Grade 10

Belarus Grade 10 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 172), teaches: Morality (Cicero's term) built around the opposition of good and evil, its structure of values, ideals and norms; ethics as the teaching about morality; нравственность as a person's individual morality against society's accepted ideas — the gap between them a source both of immoral behaviour and of morality's own development; moral regulation working both ways (my goals against others' good); and how moral norms and ideals change with a society's worldview. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
