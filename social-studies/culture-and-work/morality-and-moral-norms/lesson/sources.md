Armenia's own way (G07 Social Science student): Social Science opens with the person, not the state — nature, society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and citizenship is introduced from the child's own documents: an Armenian citizen's membership is "fixed by the birth certificate and the passport", with rights from the state and duties owed back.

Armenia's own way (G07 Social Science teacher guide a15432): the authors' own guide teaches the values chapters through Armenian everyday cases — men's shorts, Lent fasting, and the new Armenian animal-cruelty punishment — and sets Aristotle's state-first text against Article 3 of Armenia's own Constitution.
