# Hands-on — Morality and Moral Norms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Morality (Cicero's term) built around the opposition of good and evil, its structure of values, ideals and norms; ethics as the teaching about morality; нравственность as a person's individual morality against society's accepted ideas — the gap between them a source both of immoral behaviour and of morality's own development; moral regulation working both ways (my goals against others' good); and how moral norms and ideals change with a society's worldview. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
