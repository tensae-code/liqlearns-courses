# Video script — The Marimba Guatemalas National Instrument

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed p. 127, teaches: the marimba, national instrument since 1978 and national symbol since 1999 by decree of Congress; its Asian-African origin story and why Guatemalans identify with it.

Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).
