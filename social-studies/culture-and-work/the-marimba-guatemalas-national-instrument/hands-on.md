# Hands-on — The Marimba Guatemalas National Instrument

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 3 "La marimba en Guatemala"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed p. 127, teaches: the marimba, national instrument since 1978 and national symbol since 1999 by decree of Congress; its Asian-African origin story and why Guatemalans identify with it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
