# Hands-on — Economic activities — the three levels

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Ask at home: what work do the adults in your family do? Sort each job into primary, secondary or tertiary.

## Activity 2
Draw the two farm scenes from the guide's contrast for your own area: how many people farm, and how much is produced?

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Name the three levels in order. Say what economic activity is. Read a country's development from its activity mix (teacher guide — evidence).
