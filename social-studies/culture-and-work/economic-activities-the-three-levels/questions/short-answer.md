# Economic activities — the three levels — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three levels.
**Answer:** Primary, secondary and tertiary.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the guide's sharp agriculture contrast?
**Answer:** Developing countries: many people work the land but production is little. Developed countries: a surplus produced with very few people engaged, using modern methods.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Where do developed countries put a large share of their people?
**Answer:** In secondary activities — manufacturing.
