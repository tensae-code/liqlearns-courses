# Economic activities — the three levels — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic activity produces, distributes and consumes goods and services to meet —
a) Taxes  b) Human wants  c) Maps  d) Customs
**Answer: b) Human wants**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The three levels in order are —
a) Tertiary, primary, secondary  b) Primary, secondary, tertiary  c) Secondary, tertiary, primary  d) Trade, tax, farming
**Answer: b) Primary, secondary, tertiary**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Developed countries carry a large share of —
a) Subsistence farming  b) Manufacturing, with many in secondary activities  c) Hunting  d) Gathering
**Answer: b) Manufacturing, with many in secondary activities**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In developing countries, agriculture is keyed as —
a) Few people, surplus  b) Many people working the land, production little  c) No people, machines only  d) Export only
**Answer: b) Many people working the land, production little**
