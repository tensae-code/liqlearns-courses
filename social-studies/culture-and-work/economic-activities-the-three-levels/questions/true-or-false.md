# Economic activities — the three levels — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tertiary activities are support services, an aid to production.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Developing economies are described as basically industrial.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Economic activity includes distribution and consumption, not only production.
**Answer: True**
