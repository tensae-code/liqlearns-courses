# Hands-on — The Sampeah Greeting and Respect

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: good habits at home, taught through the sampeah greeting — the respectful joined-palms greeting, with a different posture for monks, king, elders, parents, teachers and friends — and the family's daily routine Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
