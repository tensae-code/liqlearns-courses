# Hands-on — The Good Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The citizen is the person who lives on the state's land and carries its nationality; the good citizen is the citizen who respects the regulations, the laws and the constitution — he is an active member in building his state, the state has rights and privileges for him that the constitution guarantees, and on him are duties, and the good citizen contributes to developing his homeland by serving it with sincerity and is keen to respect the other citizens' rights; and good citizenship requires a clear understanding of the citizen's role, known through his qualities: he understands the nature of his abilities and possibilities in society, to determine his duties and his rights; he understands the nature of his society's problems and takes part in treating them; he appreciates his homeland's standing in the world; he respects the other citizens' rights and does not transgress against them; and he is diligent and sincere in his duties toward his homeland. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
