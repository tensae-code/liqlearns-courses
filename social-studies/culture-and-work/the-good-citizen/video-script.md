# Video script — The Good Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies (printed p. 88), teaches: The citizen is the person who lives on the state's land and carries its nationality; the good citizen is the citizen who respects the regulations, the laws and the constitution — he is an active member in building his state, the state has rights and privileges for him that the constitution guarantees, and on him are duties, and the good citizen contributes to developing his homeland by serving it with sincerity and is keen to respect the other citizens' rights; and good citizenship requires a clear understanding of the citizen's role, known through his qualities: he understands the nature of his abilities and possibilities in society, to determine his duties and his rights; he understands the nature of his society's problems and takes part in treating them; he appreciates his homeland's standing in the world; he respects the other citizens' rights and does not transgress against them; and he is diligent and sincere in his duties toward his homeland.

Iraq's own way: the state is defined as institutions that serve, its pillars are named homeland, people, federal authorities and sovereignty, and citizenship is taught as a two-way street — the state's services (free education first) on one side, and on the other a good citizen whose first quality is knowing his own abilities so he can fix his duties and his rights.
