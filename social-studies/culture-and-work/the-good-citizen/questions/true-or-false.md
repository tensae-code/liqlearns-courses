# The Good Citizen — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The citizen is the person who lives on the state's land and carries its nationality; the good citizen is the citizen who respects the regulations, the laws and the constitution — he is an active member in building his state, the state has rights and privileges for him that the constitution guarantees, and on him are duties, and the good citizen contributes to developing his homeland by serving it with sincerity and is keen to respect the other citizens' rights; and good citizenship requires a clear understanding of the citizen's role, known through his qualities: he understands the nature of his abilities and possibilities in society, to determine his duties and his rights; he understands the nature of his society's problems and takes part in treating them; he appreciates his homeland's standing in the world; he respects the other citizens' rights and does not transgress against them; and he is diligent and sincere in his duties toward his homeland.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iraq's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
