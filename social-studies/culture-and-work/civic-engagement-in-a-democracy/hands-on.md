# Hands-on — Civic Engagement in a Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Civic Engagement in a Democracy
Work through this part with your hands: civic engagement as a ladder of acts — informing yourself, writing to your representative, complaining at city hall, rallying, voting, campaigning, volunteering, protesting — and who tends to climb it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United States' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how the United States' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
