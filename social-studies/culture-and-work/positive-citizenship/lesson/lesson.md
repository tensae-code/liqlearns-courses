## Sub lesson 1 — Positive citizenship and The concept of positive citizenship

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G6 Term 1, printed p. 60, teaches: citizenship (المواطنة) as a shared inheritance of principles, values and customs among the people of one state, giving equality before the law and participation in public life; its components — belonging, the ethics of citizenship, rights, duties, and community participation This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
