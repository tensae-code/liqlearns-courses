# Video script — Positive Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G6 Term 1, printed p. 60, teaches: citizenship (المواطنة) as a shared inheritance of principles, values and customs among the people of one state, giving equality before the law and participation in public life; its components — belonging, the ethics of citizenship, rights, duties, and community participation

United Arab Emirates' own way: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries.
