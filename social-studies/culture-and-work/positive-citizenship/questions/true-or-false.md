# Positive Citizenship — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: citizenship (المواطنة) as a shared inheritance of principles, values and customs among the people of one state, giving equality before the law and participation in public life; its components — belonging, the ethics of citizenship, rights, duties, and community participation
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries.
**Answer: True**
