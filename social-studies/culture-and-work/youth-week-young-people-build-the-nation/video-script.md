# Video script — Youth Week Young People Build the Nation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 4 Social Studies For Our Children Book 4 (printed p. 57), teaches: Every May, around Independence Day — young people develop themselves and their communities through debates, seminars, community cleaning, visits to shut-ins, sports and rallies.

Guyana's own way: Book 4 turns from "my community" to "our homeland" — Guyana is taught as six peoples who each brought a way of life, and citizenship is taught through honour (National Awards) and shared observance (Road Safety, Fire Prevention and Youth Weeks) before it is taught through rules.
