# Video script — Loving Work and Doing My Share

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 4 Đạo đức 4, printed pp. 19–24, teaches: Work as a joy and a duty — wiping the board for the teacher, gardening, cleaning the house and the village lane, the girl who helps because she sees the sweat on her mother's forehead; laziness is shameful and every child does the work fitted to their strength (the lesson's practice includes the class voting to elect its exemplary member).

Vietnam's own way: Grade 4 turns outward to society's work and property: gratitude is owed to every worker including the night street-sweeper of Tố Hữu's poem; "public property" (của công) is named as a legal-moral category of its own — what belongs to everyone is guarded by everyone — and the book closes with the child's place in law itself: the basic rights and duties of children, including a children's forum where pupils speak.
