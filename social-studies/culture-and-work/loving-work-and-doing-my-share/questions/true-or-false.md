# Loving Work and Doing My Share — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Work as a joy and a duty — wiping the board for the teacher, gardening, cleaning the house and the village lane, the girl who helps because she sees the sweat on her mother's forehead; laziness is shameful and every child does the work fitted to their strength (the lesson's practice includes the class voting to elect its exemplary member).

**Answer: False — it stands on Vietnam's student book(s), *Đạo đức 4*, named in lesson/sources.md.**
