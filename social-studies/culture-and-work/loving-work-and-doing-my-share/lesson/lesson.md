## Sub lesson 1 — Love work

Vietnam Grade 4 Đạo đức 4, printed pp. 19–24, teaches: Work as a joy and a duty — wiping the board for the teacher, gardening, cleaning the house and the village lane, the girl who helps because she sees the sweat on her mother's forehead; laziness is shameful and every child does the work fitted to their strength (the lesson's practice includes the class voting to elect its exemplary member). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: Grade 4 turns outward to society's work and property: gratitude is owed to every worker including the night street-sweeper of Tố Hữu's poem; "public property" (của công) is named as a legal-moral category of its own — what belongs to everyone is guarded by everyone — and the book closes with the child's place in law itself: the basic rights and duties of children, including a children's forum where pupils speak. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
