# Hands-on — Loving Work and Doing My Share

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: work as a joy and a duty — wiping the board for the teacher, gardening, cleaning the house and the village lane, the girl who helps because she sees the sweat on her mother's forehead; laziness is shameful and every child does the work fitted to their strength (the lesson's practice includes the class voting to elect its exemplary member). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
