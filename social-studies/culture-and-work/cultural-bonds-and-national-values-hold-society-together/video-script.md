Turkey Grade 6 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 24, teaches: the bonds are language (folk songs, poems, epics), religion, history and its works, art (handicrafts, architecture) and custom; the values are family integrity, sensitivity, respect and love; the state's Culture Road Festivals keep the culture alive in city after city

Turkey's own way: Grade 6 replays the Grade 5 "living together" area one level up — roles are now followed across a whole lifetime, social unity is traced to cultural bonds (language, faith, history, art, custom), and social problems are answered with the ancestors' own institution, the vakıf, before any modern agency is named.
