# Hands-on — Rights and Duties in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Matching rights (play, food, study) to responsibilities (work, help at home, give love) for each family member, then the family setting its own agreements and commitments. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
