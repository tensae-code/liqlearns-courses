Turkey Grade 6 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 38, teaches: a social problem is one that wounds justice and shared life; like the fire ants that raft together and take turns underwater, we take responsibility for solutions — as our ancestors did with thousands of selfless vakıfs (a 1321 Sivas foundation treating leprosy patients shut in at home, a foundation paying the funerals of the poor, one feeding wild animals with table leftovers, one repairing mosques and schools after earthquake and fire), and as scientists do today with an AI forest-fire warning system

Turkey's own way: Grade 6 replays the Grade 5 "living together" area one level up — roles are now followed across a whole lifetime, social unity is traced to cultural bonds (language, faith, history, art, custom), and social problems are answered with the ancestors' own institution, the vakıf, before any modern agency is named.
