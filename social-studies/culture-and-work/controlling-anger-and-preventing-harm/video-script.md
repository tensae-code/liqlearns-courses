# Video script — Controlling Anger and Preventing Harm

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 8 (student book, Persian), printed p. 24, teaches: adolescence (ages 12–18) and its changes; anger is natural but must be mastered — the three rules for anger: harm neither your own or others' property, nor other people, nor yourself — with the sayings of the Prophet and Imams on conquering anger.

Iran's own way: the civics year is built as an institutional tour taken in the order a citizen meets the state — cooperation in home and neighbourhood, then the executive (this book's "government"), then the judiciary — and every institution is introduced through newspaper headlines and the pupil's own town.
