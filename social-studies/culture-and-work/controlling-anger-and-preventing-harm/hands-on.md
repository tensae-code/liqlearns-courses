# Hands-on — Controlling Anger and Preventing Harm

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: adolescence (ages 12–18) and its changes; anger is natural but must be mastered — the three rules for anger: harm neither your own or others' property, nor other people, nor yourself — with the sayings of the Prophet and Imams on conquering anger. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
