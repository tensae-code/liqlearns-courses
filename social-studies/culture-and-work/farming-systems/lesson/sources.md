Bhutan's own way (Bhutan Grade 9 Geography): The Intermediate level (IX–X) treats each world topic at full textbook depth and keeps the Bhutan case inside it — Soils of Bhutan, agro-ecological zones and farming practices of Bhutan, and common disasters in Bhutan (including GLOF) alongside the world disaster survey.

Searchable tags: farming systems, Bhutan, social studies, culture-and-work

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB): Landforms are made, not just named — learners push a sheet of paper from both ends to make a fold mountain, and the home examples are the Ironstone Plateau and a farming landscape being changed by the farming itself.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies SB): The climate unit is two-way and practical — how climate shapes people's activities (poor climate ruins roads) and how people adapt: use scarce water efficiently, build flood defences and raise dykes, grow drought-tolerant crops, and make biogas.
