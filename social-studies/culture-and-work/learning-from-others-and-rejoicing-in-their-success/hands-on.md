# Hands-on — Learning From Others And Rejoicing in Their Success

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.2, closing units
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Learn from others' knowledge and support their right acts, never their wrong ones — of two friends, the best is the one who always sees your good side and carries you toward good; a person's quality is read in how they meet others' deeds; and rejoice in others' success without envy (атаархалгүй баярлая): "the root of my happiness lies in others' happiness" — envy is a fire that burns its owner, while a glad heart at another's good fortune doubles it; the old Indian's tale of the two dogs, one white, one black, fighting inside every person — the one that wins is the one you feed. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
