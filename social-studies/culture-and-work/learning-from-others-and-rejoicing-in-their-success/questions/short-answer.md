# Learning From Others And Rejoicing in Their Success — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 1.2, closing units — Learn from others' knowledge and support their right acts, never their wrong ones — of two friends, the best is the one who always sees your good side and carries you toward good; a person's quality is read in how they meet others' deeds; and rejoice in others' success without envy (атаархалгүй баярлая): "the root of my happiness lies in others' happiness" — envy is a fire that burns its owner, while a glad heart at another's good fortune doubles it; the old Indian's tale of the two dogs, one white, one black, fighting inside every person — the one that wins is the one you feed (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — from Grade 6 the civics subject changes its name and its method — no longer "Civic Education" but "Civic Ethics" (Иргэний ёс зүйн боловсрол), a self-observation workbook in the Buddhist-inflected Mongolian moral tradition: the pupil keeps a diary of their own acts of body, speech and mind, and ethics is practised as a daily discipline before it is ever a rule about others (any close wording).**
