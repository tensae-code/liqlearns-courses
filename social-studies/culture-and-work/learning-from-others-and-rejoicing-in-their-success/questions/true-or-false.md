# Learning From Others And Rejoicing in Their Success — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: learn from others' knowledge and support their right acts, never their wrong ones — of two friends, the best is the one who always sees your good side and carries you toward good; a person's quality is read in how they meet others' deeds; and rejoice in others' success without envy (атаархалгүй баярлая): "the root of my happiness lies in others' happiness" — envy is a fire that burns its owner, while a glad heart at another's good fortune doubles it; the old Indian's tale of the two dogs, one white, one black, fighting inside every person — the one that wins is the one you feed.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
