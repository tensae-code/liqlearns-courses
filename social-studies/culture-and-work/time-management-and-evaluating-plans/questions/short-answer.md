**Answer: Time Management and Evaluating Plans — the three methods of managing time — by clock timing (arranging the day's works precisely by hours and minutes in a weekly schedule), by periods (morning, noon and other daily periods, when works are few), and by the school term (planning the term's works week by week); and evaluating one's planning to learn from mistakes — recording unfinished works to see how time is wasted, keeping a supporting record of what was achieved against what was planned, and the signs of neglected advance planning: anxiety on some days, time pressure in revision, and works piling up as the exams approach (any close wording).**
