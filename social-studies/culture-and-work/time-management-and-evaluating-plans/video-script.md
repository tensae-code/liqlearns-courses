Saudi Arabia Grade 8 Social Studies (printed pp. 277–283), teaches: The three methods of managing time — by clock timing (arranging the day's works precisely by hours and minutes in a weekly schedule), by periods (morning, noon and other daily periods, when works are few), and by the school term (planning the term's works week by week); and evaluating one's planning to learn from mistakes — recording unfinished works to see how time is wasted, keeping a supporting record of what was achieved against what was planned, and the signs of neglected advance planning: anxiety on some days, time pressure in revision, and works piling up as the exams approach.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 8 book places Saudi Arabia inside the Gulf Cooperation Council — its organs and goals, its achievements and its challenges — then teaches systems and regulations (how a system is made, their kinds and their benefits), and ends with planning priorities, time management and evaluating plans, and hobbies including the old folk games.
