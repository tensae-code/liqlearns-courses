# Video script — Values That Hold a Family Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Libya Grade 4 National Education G4 (printed p. 45), teaches: family life rests on values — respect, affection, giving, cooperation and kindness (ihsan) between its members.

Libya's own way: the whole book is written in the first person — every lesson is "I and …" (I and my family, I and my school, I and the neighbourhood) — citizenship starts as the child's own relationships, and even the environment unit is framed as the child's own behaviour: what I throw away, what I plant, which beach I clean.
