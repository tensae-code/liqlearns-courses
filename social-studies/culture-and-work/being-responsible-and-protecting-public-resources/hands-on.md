# Hands-on — Being Responsible and Protecting Public Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Being Responsible and Protecting Public Resources
Work through this part with your hands: the community's role in national development (hard work, obeying laws, protecting public resources, national security), the educated person's role, strategies for protecting public resources and fighting their misuse. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Protecting Resources and National Interests
Work through this part with your hands: Tanzania's national resources — minerals (gold, tanzanite, diamond), land, forests, water, national parks such as Serengeti, mountains, and people themselves — serve the nation's development, and every citizen is obliged to protect them; vandalism such as tearing school books, breaking taps and windows and uprooting flowers is condemned, and misuse of public resources is reported to the responsible institutions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
