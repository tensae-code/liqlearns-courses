# Video script — Forms of Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine Grade 8 Social Studies Part 1, printed p. 78, teaches: citizenship takes several forms — rights citizenship, which aims to strengthen citizens' civil rights and entrench the rule of law without discrimination (freedom of worship and practising religious rites, freedom of opinion and expression, the right of movement); political citizenship, the citizens' right to take part in running public affairs and in political participation — Article 26 of the Palestinian Basic Law confirms Palestinians' right to participate in political life, individually and in groups: forming political parties and joining them according to law, forming unions, associations and federations, voting and standing in elections, holding public posts and jobs on the basis of equal opportunity, and holding public meetings; and responsible citizenship, the citizens' positive participation in developing their society through active participation in making decisions and influencing them, engagement with national issues, strengthening community responsibility, and the citizens' right to hold those who occupy public posts accountable.

Palestine's own way: citizenship is taught as a contract with named forms — including two the Palestinian classroom adds to the usual list, responsible citizenship (with the citizen's right to hold officials accountable) and digital citizenship — and its second lesson turns the same lens on corruption, fought in the book by Palestine's own Anti-Corruption Law and Commission and the AMAN coalition.
