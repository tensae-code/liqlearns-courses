# Forms of Citizenship — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: citizenship takes several forms — rights citizenship, which aims to strengthen citizens' civil rights and entrench the rule of law without discrimination (freedom of worship and practising religious rites, freedom of opinion and expression, the right of movement); political citizenship, the citizens' right to take part in running public affairs and in political participation — Article 26 of the Palestinian Basic Law confirms Palestinians' right to participate in political life, individually and in groups: forming political parties and joining them according to law, forming unions, associations and federations, voting and standing in elections, holding public posts and jobs on the basis of equal opportunity, and holding public meetings; and responsible citizenship, the citizens' positive participation in developing their society through active participation in making decisions and influencing them, engagement with national issues, strengthening community responsibility, and the citizens' right to hold those who occupy public posts accountable.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Palestine's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
