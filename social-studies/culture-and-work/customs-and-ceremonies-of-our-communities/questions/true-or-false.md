# Customs and Ceremonies of our Communities — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: local customs and traditions practised by taking part — traditional song and dance, traditional fishing and hunting, respecting and caring for someone who has died, practices around the birth of a child, initiation ceremonies, food taboos, people with special knowledge and sacred places.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: the customs and ceremonies of PNG communities — bride price and weddings, religious practices, customs about birth and marriage, ceremonies and initiations, rituals and taboos.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: the elements of culture and the rituals different cultures practise.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part teaches: the cultural rituals of the community — rituals for birth, marriage, death and church, clan rituals such as initiations for raising children, and patrilineal and matrilineal patterns of relationship.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Papua New Guinea's teacher guides only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
