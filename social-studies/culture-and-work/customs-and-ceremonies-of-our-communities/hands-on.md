# Hands-on — Customs and Ceremonies of our Communities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Local customs and traditions
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Local customs and traditions practised by taking part — traditional song and dance, traditional fishing and hunting, respecting and caring for someone who has died, practices around the birth of a child, initiation ceremonies, food taboos, people with special knowledge and sacred places.
