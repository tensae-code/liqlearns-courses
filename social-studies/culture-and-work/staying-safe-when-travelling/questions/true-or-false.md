# Staying Safe When Travelling — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The road-crossing drill (look right, look left, look right again; cross quickly when clear) and walking on the right-hand side of the road.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: Obeying safety rules when travelling — bicycles with bells, riding on the left-hand side of the road.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: River-travel safety — keeping away from sandbanks and rocks and reading the warnings buoys give.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: Every October the Police Traffic Department reminds drivers, cyclists and pedestrians to use the road with care; Schools' Safety Patrols, talks, rallies and leaflets.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
