# Video script — Mass Media and Politics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Greece Grade 9 Koinoniki kai Politiki Agogi (printed p. 82), teaches: The media's place in political life.

Greece's own way: the Gymnasium course is built as four widening circles — the individual in society, in the state, in the European Union, in the international community — and its sociology is full-dress: stratification, institutions, socialisation and social control each get their own sections before the state is ever mentioned.
