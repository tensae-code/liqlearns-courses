# Hands-on — Corruption What It is and Its Forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: corruption seen through social responsibility and its consequences — bribery, nepotism and favouritism with worked examples, why corruption grows, what it damages (trust, fair competition, public services), and human trafficking as exploitation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
