**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Nicaragua).**

Nicaragua's own way (Book 2 — Convivencia y Civismo G2 (student book, Spanish)): civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 4 — Convivencia y Civismo G5 (student book, Spanish)): the upper-primary civics book teaches the state itself — Constitution, Executive and Judicial powers, the DGI tax office — and sets Nicaragua's symbols beside all six Central American neighbours'.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 5 — Convivencia y Civismo G6 (student book, Spanish)): the final primary civics book widens from Nicaragua to Central America (symbols and heroes compared across the isthmus) and from values to the machinery of rights — the Children's Convention, the CNA code, consumer rights and tax education.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 6 — Convivencia y Civismo G4 (student book, Spanish)): Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State.
