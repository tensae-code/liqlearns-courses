# Video script — Values for Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Canada Grade 4 Stories of Our Origins TG (printed p. 22), teaches: The class builds its own list of respectful behaviours for the storytelling circle — respect for people, for ourselves, for the land and for animals.

Canada's own way: The unit opens not with the state's law but with the Dene's own laws, handed down in the Yamoria story — law as story, kept by Elders and by the group's shared memory.
