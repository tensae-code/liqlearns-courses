# Video script — Simulating a Communal Council Meeting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Morocco Grade 6 Al-Massar Ijtiamiat, printed p. 158, teaches: the whole class re-enacts a council meeting on a chosen local topic — agenda on the board under the flag, roles assigned (presidency, council secretary, budget rapporteur, ministry representative, local-association representatives, participants, a filming team), deliberation under democratic participation rules, a decision taken and its implementation followed; a UNICEF text explains children's and youth councils as real participation.

Morocco's own way: the Grade 6 civics course is a working apprenticeship in democracy — it opens with law, freedom and responsibility (Rousseau: no freedom without responsibility), runs the class as a republic (charter, elected cooperative), then walks out of the school gate: local democracy is learned by studying the elected communal council from the 2011 Constitution (Articles 135 and 139), visiting the commune headquarters, simulating a full council meeting with agenda, president, budget rapporteur and association representatives, and finally pleading a real solidarity proposal before that council.
