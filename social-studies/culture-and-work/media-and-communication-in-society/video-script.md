# Video script — Media and Communication in Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yemen Grade 4 G4 Social Studies, printed p. 56, teaches: media as a community service — newspapers, radio and television carry local, Arab and world news and educate the public; the government supervises the official stations and channels.

Yemen's own way: Grade 4 turns from the child's surroundings to the homeland's institutions — education, health, media and traffic police are taught one ministry at a time, each with the supervising ministry named, its real Yemeni buildings pictured (the Ministry of Education and al-Thawra Hospital in Sana'a among them), and the government's duty to keep improving the service.
