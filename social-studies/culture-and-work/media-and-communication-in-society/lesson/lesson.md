## Sub lesson 2 — Yemen's way — the book's own teaching way

Yemen's own way: Grade 4 turns from the child's surroundings to the homeland's institutions — education, health, media and traffic police are taught one ministry at a time, each with the supervising ministry named, its real Yemeni buildings pictured (the Ministry of Education and al-Thawra Hospital in Sana'a among them), and the government's duty to keep improving the service.
