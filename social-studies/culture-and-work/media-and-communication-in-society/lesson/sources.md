**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Yemen, Afghanistan).**

Yemen's own way (G4 Social Studies): Grade 4 turns from the child's surroundings to the homeland's institutions — education, health, media and traffic police are taught one ministry at a time, each with the supervising ministry named, its real Yemeni buildings pictured (the Ministry of Education and al-Thawra Hospital in Sana'a among them), and the government's duty to keep improving the service.

Afghanistan's own way (Grade 5 Social Studies): society is taught as a ladder of belonging — family, school, village, district, city, country, and the wider Islamic umma — and the state appears as the servant of that society: ministries named one by one, each with its service, and peace named the first need of a country that has known decades of war.
