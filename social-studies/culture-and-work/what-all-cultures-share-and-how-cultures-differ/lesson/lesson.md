## Sub lesson 1 — What all cultures share — and how they differ

Mongolia's Grade 10 book, having defined culture and its components, asks what is universal in it: every culture, however different its foods, clothes and customs, shares common features as a culture, and cultures can be classified — sorted into kinds — by those features. The two topics together teach the learner to compare cultures without ranking them.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the book's own teaching way

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the senior cycle drops the “Individual and…” frame for straight disciplines — Society, Culture, Law, Economics, Politics, International Relations — with Mongolia's own culture, macroeconomy and foreign relations as the worked examples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
