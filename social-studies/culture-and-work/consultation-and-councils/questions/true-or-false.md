# Consultation and Councils — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: group members consult (مشورت) and decide together — the Qur'anic principle of shura; bigger groups in school, neighbourhood, factory, city and village elect representatives who form councils (شورا), and the class elects its own representative by vote.
