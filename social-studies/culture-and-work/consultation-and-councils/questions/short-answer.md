# Consultation and Councils — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Consultation and Councils — group members consult (مشورت) and decide together — the Qur'anic principle of shura; bigger groups in school, neighbourhood, factory, city and village elect representatives who form councils (شورا), and the class elects its own representative by vote (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Iran's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
