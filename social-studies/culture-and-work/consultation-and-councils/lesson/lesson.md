## Sub lesson 1 — Consultation and Councils

Iran Social Studies Grade 5 (student book, Persian), printed p. 18, teaches: group members consult (مشورت) and decide together — the Qur'anic principle of shura; bigger groups in school, neighbourhood, factory, city and village elect representatives who form councils (شورا), and the class elects its own representative by vote. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: civics is taught by analogy from the school camp trip — a group needs roles, rules and a leader, so a country needs a government — and the Islamic Republic is presented as the people's own choice, made in the Farvardin 1358 referendum and renewed in every Majlis election. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
