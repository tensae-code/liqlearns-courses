# Hands-on — Consultation and Councils

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: group members consult (مشورت) and decide together — the Qur'anic principle of shura; bigger groups in school, neighbourhood, factory, city and village elect representatives who form councils (شورا), and the class elects its own representative by vote. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
