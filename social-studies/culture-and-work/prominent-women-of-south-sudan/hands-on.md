# Hands-on — Prominent Women of South Sudan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 4 Prominent women in South Sudan
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: four women's own careers as the evidence — Ann Itto Leonardo (agriculture minister, SPLM acting secretary-general), Jemma Nunu Kumba (first woman governor after the CPA), Awut Deng Achuil (Wunlit peace conference, minister of gender, child and social welfare) and Angelina Teny (energy minister, women-and-peace conferences). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Sudan's way
