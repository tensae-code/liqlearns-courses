# Video script — Prominent Women of South Sudan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 87, teaches: four women's own careers as the evidence — Ann Itto Leonardo (agriculture minister, SPLM acting secretary-general), Jemma Nunu Kumba (first woman governor after the CPA), Awut Deng Achuil (Wunlit peace conference, minister of gender, child and social welfare) and Angelina Teny (energy minister, women-and-peace conferences).

South Sudan's own way: Gender equality is argued from South Sudanese lives — an elder who educates his daughters against tradition, and four named women leaders — and peace itself is put in women's hands: the book prints the view that gender equality is a tool that can end South Sudan's civil war.
