# Hands-on — Getting a Job Vocational Training the Employment Contract and the Collective Agreement

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Where a profession is learned — the first labour skills at school, then vocational-technical schools (PTU), secondary special institutions and higher institutions, with the state guaranteeing free professional education and a job in one's speciality; the state employment service (republican, oblast, city and district employment centres) whose task is to help citizens find work and employers to staff their enterprises, and labour exchanges abroad; getting a job — the employment contract (працоўны дагавор) concluded between the owner-employer and the hired worker, and the collective agreement that sets the work conditions of the whole labour collective, whose highest organ is the general meeting (conference) of the workers; the forms of employment — independent (individual labour activity and entrepreneurship, under a licence or patent) or by contract; and the guarantees of the Labour Code of the Republic of Belarus (КЗаП): the order of hiring and dismissal, working time and rest time, pay, guaranteed and compensation payments, labour protection and benefits. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
