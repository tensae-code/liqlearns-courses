# Video script — Civic Sense and Common Etiquette

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pakistan's Grade 4 SS4 (printed pp. 9–10), teaches: Ch. 1 Civic Sense, Ethics of Civic Sense and Common Etiquettes — teaches: civic sense is the awareness a good citizen carries into shared places — keep home and city clean, do not waste water, care for plants; and the common etiquettes — speak gently, admit mistakes, respect everyone, keep time, work honestly, think before speaking, help others and queue in public places.

Pakistan's own way: citizenship is taught before everything else — a child first learns what a citizen, a global citizen and a digital citizen is, then Pakistan's own 1973 Constitution, its federal and provincial organs with real seat counts, and only then history, geography and economics.
