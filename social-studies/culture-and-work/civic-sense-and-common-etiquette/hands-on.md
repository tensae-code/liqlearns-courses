# Hands-on — Civic Sense and Common Etiquette

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 1 Civic Sense, Ethics of Civic Sense and Common Etiquettes — teaches: civic sense is the awareness a good citizen carries into shared places — keep home and city clean, do not waste water, care for plants; and the common etiquettes — speak gently, admit mistakes, respect everyone, keep time, work honestly, think before speaking, help others and queue in public places. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
