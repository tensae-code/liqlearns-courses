## Sub lesson 1 — The Citizen and National Security

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 229–231), teaches: The citizen as the first security official; citizenship in security begins with self-security — the steps a citizen takes to protect himself and his family (caution against extremists, promoters and criminal gangs, raising and following up children, securing home and car) — then interaction with society and support of the state; the citizen's duties: obedience to the ruler and those he appoints, avoiding promoters of extremist ideas, and guarding the homeland's information against enemies who seek it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
