# Hands-on — The Citizen and National Security

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the citizen as the first security official; citizenship in security begins with self-security — the steps a citizen takes to protect himself and his family (caution against extremists, promoters and criminal gangs, raising and following up children, securing home and car) — then interaction with society and support of the state; the citizen's duties: obedience to the ruler and those he appoints, avoiding promoters of extremist ideas, and guarding the homeland's information against enemies who seek it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
