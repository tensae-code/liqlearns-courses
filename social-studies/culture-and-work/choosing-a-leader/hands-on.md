# Hands-on — Choosing a Leader

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What leadership is; the qualities of a good leader (cooperation, sincerity, courage, bearing responsibility, honesty, sacrifice); choosing the class leader and standing for the student council; justice as the basis of rule (Constitution Art. 4); and what a leader does — keeps order, settles disagreements, protects rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahrain's way
Say how Bahrain's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
