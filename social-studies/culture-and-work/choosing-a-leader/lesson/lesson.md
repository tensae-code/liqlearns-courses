## Sub lesson 1 — Choosing a Leader

Bahrain Grade 4 BH Citizenship G4 (printed p. 37), teaches: What leadership is; the qualities of a good leader (cooperation, sincerity, courage, bearing responsibility, honesty, sacrifice); choosing the class leader and standing for the student council; justice as the basis of rule (Constitution Art. 4); and what a leader does — keeps order, settles disagreements, protects rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bahrain's way — the book's own teaching way

Bahrain's own way: citizenship is taught outwards from the child — homeland love and public property first, then the services of Bahrain's own government, the child's own rights under the UN Convention Bahrain ratified, choosing leaders from the class council up to the King, Crown Prince and Prime Minister, and finally Bahrain inside the Gulf Cooperation Council, its own regional family. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
