# Video script — Choosing a Leader

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bahrain Grade 4 BH Citizenship G4 (printed p. 37), teaches: What leadership is; the qualities of a good leader (cooperation, sincerity, courage, bearing responsibility, honesty, sacrifice); choosing the class leader and standing for the student council; justice as the basis of rule (Constitution Art. 4); and what a leader does — keeps order, settles disagreements, protects rights.

Bahrain's own way: citizenship is taught outwards from the child — homeland love and public property first, then the services of Bahrain's own government, the child's own rights under the UN Convention Bahrain ratified, choosing leaders from the class council up to the King, Crown Prince and Prime Minister, and finally Bahrain inside the Gulf Cooperation Council, its own regional family.
