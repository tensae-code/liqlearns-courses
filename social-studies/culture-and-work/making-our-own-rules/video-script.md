# Video script — Making Our Own Rules

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bangladesh Grade 6 BD G6 HSS (printed p. 28), teaches: Pupils in groups write the rules they will follow in the classroom, on the school premises, and in family and society, hang them on the wall, follow them all year, and add or remove rules by open discussion.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 6): An inquiry-based combined History and Social Science course taught through a class teacher character (Khushi Apa) and group investigations; civics begins not with definitions but with a football game played with and without rules, and sociology is built from a village flood story (the Dharmagola grain bank) before any term is named..
