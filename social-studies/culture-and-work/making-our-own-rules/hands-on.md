# Hands-on — Making Our Own Rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Pupils in groups write the rules they will follow in the classroom, on the school premises, and in family and society, hang them on the wall, follow them all year, and add or remove rules by open discussion. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
