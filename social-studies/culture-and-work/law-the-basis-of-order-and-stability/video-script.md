# Video script — Law the Basis of Order and Stability

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 8 Term 1 (printed pp. 88–90), teaches: Society's need for law to organise people's behaviour and relations, what follows when law is absent, and law's goals in society.

Kuwait's own way: amid a book about the Islamic world, Kuwait teaches law abstractly and concretely at once — what law is, why a society without it falls into disorder, how legal rules differ from moral rules, and the speed camera on a Kuwaiti road as the picture of a law that applies to everyone without exception.
