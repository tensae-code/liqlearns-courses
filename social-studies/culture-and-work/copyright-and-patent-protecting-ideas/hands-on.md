# Hands-on — Copyright and Patent Protecting Ideas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: intellectual property (fikrî mülkiyet) — a work's material and moral rights are its creator's copyright (telif hakkı); an invention is protected by a patent so others cannot copy it; the Turkish Patent and Trademark Office registers and protects both, which encourages people to create and invent Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
