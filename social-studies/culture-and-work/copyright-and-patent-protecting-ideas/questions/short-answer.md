# Copyright and Patent Protecting Ideas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The copyright and patent process — intellectual property (fikrî mülkiyet) — a work's material and moral rights are its creator's copyright (telif hakkı); an invention is protected by a patent so others cannot copy it; the Turkish Patent and Trademark Office registers and protects both, which encourages people to create and invent (any close wording).**
