# Copyright and Patent Protecting Ideas — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: intellectual property (fikrî mülkiyet) — a work's material and moral rights are its creator's copyright (telif hakkı); an invention is protected by a patent so others cannot copy it; the Turkish Patent and Trademark Office registers and protects both, which encourages people to create and invent
**Answer: True**
