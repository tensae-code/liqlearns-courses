Turkey Grade 6 Sosyal Bilgiler 2. Kitap, printed p. 100, teaches: intellectual property (fikrî mülkiyet) — a work's material and moral rights are its creator's copyright (telif hakkı); an invention is protected by a patent so others cannot copy it; the Turkish Patent and Trademark Office registers and protects both, which encourages people to create and invent

Turkey's own way: the Grade 6 democracy area asks who actually moves a government — the people's needs, parties, civil society, media and the kamuoyu (public opinion) they form together — and then follows citizenship itself online, where rights are now exercised through a screen and the active citizen is the one who still writes the petition.
