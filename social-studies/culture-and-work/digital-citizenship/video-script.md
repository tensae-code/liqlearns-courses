# Video script — Digital Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mexico Grade 8 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 2 (secundaria) (printed pp. 319–330), teaches: Digital citizenship — knowing the virtual space's resources and its role in exercising fundamental rights — and using ICT to inform, organise and act, as a strategy against school and gender violence and for strengthening democracy.

---

Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality.
