Nicaragua Grade 2 Convivencia y Civismo G2 (printed p. 4), teaches: Controlling one's own impulses and behaviour in daily situations.

Nicaragua Grade 2 Convivencia y Civismo G2 (printed p. 6), teaches: Recognising and mastering emotions and feelings instead of being carried by them.

Nicaragua Grade 3 Convivencia y Civismo G3 (printed p. 29), teaches: Controlling impulses and emotions.

Nicaragua's own way: civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book. Grade 3 civics adds Nicaragua-specific law to the values base — women's rights under Ley 779 and a Mother Earth declaration — and respect for public and private institutions as a civic duty.
