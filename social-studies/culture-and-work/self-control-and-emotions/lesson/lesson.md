## Sub lesson 4 — Nicaragua's way — the books' own teaching way

Nicaragua's own way: civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book. Grade 3 civics adds Nicaragua-specific law to the values base — women's rights under Ley 779 and a Mother Earth declaration — and respect for public and private institutions as a civic duty. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
