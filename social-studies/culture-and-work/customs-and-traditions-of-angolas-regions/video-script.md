Angola Estudo do Meio G3 (printed p. 35), teaches: The customs and traditions of Angola's people differ by region — ways of dressing, mother tongue, food customs, traditional festivals such as efico and circumcision, city festivals and religious pilgrimages.

Angola's own way: Grade 3 is where Estudo do Meio turns outward — the child's own locality is inventoried place by place (school, hospital, market, bank, water fountain), and Angola's customs are taught region by region: dress, mother tongue, food, and festivals such as efico and circumcision celebrations.
