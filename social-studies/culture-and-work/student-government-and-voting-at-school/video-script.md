Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 30, teaches: at school as in the family there are norms and rules that keep order and harmonious coexistence; besides the director's authority, pupils form a school government through an elected junta directiva (president and board) that organises activities of collective benefit.

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.
