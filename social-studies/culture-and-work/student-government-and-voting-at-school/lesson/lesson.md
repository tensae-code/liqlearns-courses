## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1 "Gobierno escolar"

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 30, teaches: at school as in the family there are norms and rules that keep order and harmonious coexistence; besides the director's authority, pupils form a school government through an elected junta directiva (president and board) that organises activities of collective benefit. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
