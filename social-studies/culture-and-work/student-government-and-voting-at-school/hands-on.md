# Hands-on — Student Government and Voting at School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 1 "Gobierno escolar"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 30, teaches: at school as in the family there are norms and rules that keep order and harmonious coexistence; besides the director's authority, pupils form a school government through an elected junta directiva (president and board) that organises activities of collective benefit. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
