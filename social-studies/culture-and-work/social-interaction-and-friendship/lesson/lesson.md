## Sub lesson 1 — Social Interaction and Friendship

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 195–197), teaches: Social interaction as the communication, understanding and mutual relationship among society's members, governed by religion, values, customs and regulations, inside family, school and society — its positive forms (filial kindness, cooperation, respecting elders, honest competition, serving the homeland) against its negative (name-calling, envy, disturbing others); friendship as a social relationship of affection and benefit under religion, and the good friend who helps toward every good; peers as those at one level in age, class or work. ## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue.
