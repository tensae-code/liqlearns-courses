# Hands-on — Social Interaction and Friendship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: social interaction as the communication, understanding and mutual relationship among society's members, governed by religion, values, customs and regulations, inside family, school and society — its positive forms (filial kindness, cooperation, respecting elders, honest competition, serving the homeland) against its negative (name-calling, envy, disturbing others); friendship as a social relationship of affection and benefit under religion, and the good friend who helps toward every good; peers as those at one level in age, class or work. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
