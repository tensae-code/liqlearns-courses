# Hands-on — Choosing Together Dialogue Persuasion and the Vote

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how a group decides — when the class cannot agree on the open-day activity, it completes the manners of dialogue (calm voice, listening, not interrupting, respecting opinions), each group proposes and persuades, then all vote into a ballot box and the winning proposal is carried out. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
