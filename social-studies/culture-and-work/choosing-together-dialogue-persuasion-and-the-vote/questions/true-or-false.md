# Choosing Together Dialogue Persuasion and the Vote — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: How a group decides — when the class cannot agree on the open-day activity, it completes the manners of dialogue (calm voice, listening, not interrupting, respecting opinions), each group proposes and persuades, then all vote into a ballot box and the winning proposal is carried out.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Grade 3 is where the series turns civic — the child meets the vote inside a classroom quarrel (proposals, persuasion, a ballot box, the winner implemented), meets rights on World Children's Rights Day (20 November) as picture pairs where every right is matched by its duty, and meets the inclusive city: the society-we-want lesson is built around services for people with special needs, down to the sign-language alphabet and Braille..
**Answer: True**
