# Hands-on — Clothes of Irans Peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: clothes mark social and work groups, occasions and climates; Iran's peoples — Fars, Turk, Lor, Gilak, Kurd, Baluch and others — each have their own local dress, kept and honoured in the anthropology museum. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
