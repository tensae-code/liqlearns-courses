# Video script — Clothes of Irans Peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 6 (student book, Persian), printed p. 95, teaches: clothes mark social and work groups, occasions and climates; Iran's peoples — Fars, Turk, Lor, Gilak, Kurd, Baluch and others — each have their own local dress, kept and honoured in the anthropology museum.

Iran's own way: the last primary year turns from places to the inner life — friendship, decisions and free time — and every moral rule is anchored in a saying of Imam Ali or a classroom dilemma (a friend who urges cheating, a found purse with no name in it).
