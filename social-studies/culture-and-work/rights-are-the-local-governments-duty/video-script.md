# Video script — Rights Are the Local Governments Duty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 8 Social Studies, printed p. 76, teaches: Citizen and state are the two ends of one relation — the person is the rights-holder (эрх эдлэгч) and the state the duty-bearer (үүрэг хүлээгч), because rights exist from birth while the state alone can be made to answer for them; the national rights system has three parts (state bodies, the national human-rights institution, civil society), and the state's four duties toward every right are to respect it, to provide/fulfil it (schools and hospitals), to protect it from others' violation, and to promote progress in it; at the local level the administration delivers those duties as services — and answers for failures, as in the case of the 14-year-old whose asthma worsened in Ulaanbaatar's smoke while the city's air stayed far above the permitted pollution level.

Mongolia's own way (Book 6): the local year turns rights and government into neighbourhood questions — the human-rights chapter ends with the soum administration's duties and Ulaanbaatar's air, and the governance chapter is one long worked example: a playground, a Khural decision, and the children who answer it.
