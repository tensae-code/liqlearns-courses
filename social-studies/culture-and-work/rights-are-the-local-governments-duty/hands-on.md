# Hands-on — Rights Are the Local Governments Duty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.5 "Providing human rights is the local administration's duty"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Citizen and state are the two ends of one relation — the person is the rights-holder (эрх эдлэгч) and the state the duty-bearer (үүрэг хүлээгч), because rights exist from birth while the state alone can be made to answer for them; the national rights system has three parts (state bodies, the national human-rights institution, civil society), and the state's four duties toward every right are to respect it, to provide/fulfil it (schools and hospitals), to protect it from others' violation, and to promote progress in it; at the local level the administration delivers those duties as services — and answers for failures, as in the case of the 14-year-old whose asthma worsened in Ulaanbaatar's smoke while the city's air stayed far above the permitted pollution level. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
