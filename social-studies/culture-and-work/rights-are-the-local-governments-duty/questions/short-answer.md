# Rights Are the Local Governments Duty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 4.5 "Providing human rights is the local administration's duty" — Citizen and state are the two ends of one relation — the person is the rights-holder (эрх эдлэгч) and the state the duty-bearer (үүрэг хүлээгч), because rights exist from birth while the state alone can be made to answer for them; the national rights system has three parts (state bodies, the national human-rights institution, civil society), and the state's four duties toward every right are to respect it, to provide/fulfil it (schools and hospitals), to protect it from others' violation, and to promote progress in it; at the local level the administration delivers those duties as services — and answers for failures, as in the case of the 14-year-old whose asthma worsened in Ulaanbaatar's smoke while the city's air stayed far above the permitted pollution level (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the local year turns rights and government into neighbourhood questions — the human-rights chapter ends with the soum administration's duties and Ulaanbaatar's air, and the governance chapter is one long worked example: a playground, a Khural decision, and the children who answer it (any close wording).**
