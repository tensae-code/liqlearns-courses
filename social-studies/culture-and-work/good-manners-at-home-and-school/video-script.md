# Video script — Good Manners at Home and School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar Morality and Civics, Grade 1 (printed p. 9), teaches: Good manners at the table, at home and with guests, and not disturbing others, as picture pairs of polite and rude behaviour.

Myanmar Teacher Guide — Morality and Civic — Grade 6 (printed p. 14), teaches: Politeness at home and in communication, down to saying thank you, taught with a poster the class makes.

Myanmar's own way (Book 2): every value is taught as a named story or picture-sequence about one child (or one lion cub) plus a question set — never as a rule list; the patriotism chapter teaches love of country as the eight ethnic peoples in their own dress holding hands around the Independence Monument.

Myanmar's own way (Book 8): Grade 6 is where the values course becomes a civics course — rights and responsibilities are named as such, the school has a council, rules are explained up to the rules of a country, and the last unit is peace: what conflict is, the bundle-of-firewood lesson, peace symbols, and rowing in unison.
