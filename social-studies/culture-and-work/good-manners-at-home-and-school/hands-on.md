# Hands-on — Good Manners at Home and School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Living politely
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Good manners at the table, at home and with guests, and not disturbing others, as picture pairs of polite and rude behaviour. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Good manners at home
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Politeness at home and in communication, down to saying thank you, taught with a poster the class makes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
