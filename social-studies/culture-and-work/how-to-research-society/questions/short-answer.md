# How to Research Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 1.4 "Social research" — How society is studied scientifically — a social research takes a question about the social world and answers it by a designed method, and the lesson walks two real Mongolian studies end to end (the national household socio-economic survey, and the Asian Development Bank's 2005 participatory poverty study of 1,516 people in Ulaanbaatar and five aimags); the two logics — deduction (from theory to cases) and induction (from cases to theory); quantitative against qualitative inquiry; the instruments — the questionnaire and its five parts, the experiment with its independent and dependent variables, content analysis, the individual and focus-group interview, observation from inside and outside, and document analysis; research ethics; and the seven-part research proposal the pupil must write: basis, aims, questions, design, analysis plan, timetable, budget (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the senior book teaches society as a science and the state as a machine the citizen operates — sociology's three lenses, the law encountered as real contracts, dismissals and complaints, and democracy measured against Mongolia's own survey numbers (any close wording).**
