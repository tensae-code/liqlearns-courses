# How to Research Society — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: how society is studied scientifically — a social research takes a question about the social world and answers it by a designed method, and the lesson walks two real Mongolian studies end to end (the national household socio-economic survey, and the Asian Development Bank's 2005 participatory poverty study of 1,516 people in Ulaanbaatar and five aimags); the two logics — deduction (from theory to cases) and induction (from cases to theory); quantitative against qualitative inquiry; the instruments — the questionnaire and its five parts, the experiment with its independent and dependent variables, content analysis, the individual and focus-group interview, observation from inside and outside, and document analysis; research ethics; and the seven-part research proposal the pupil must write: basis, aims, questions, design, analysis plan, timetable, budget.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
