# Video script — How to Research Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 10 Social Studies, printed p. 19, teaches: How society is studied scientifically — a social research takes a question about the social world and answers it by a designed method, and the lesson walks two real Mongolian studies end to end (the national household socio-economic survey, and the Asian Development Bank's 2005 participatory poverty study of 1,516 people in Ulaanbaatar and five aimags); the two logics — deduction (from theory to cases) and induction (from cases to theory); quantitative against qualitative inquiry; the instruments — the questionnaire and its five parts, the experiment with its independent and dependent variables, content analysis, the individual and focus-group interview, observation from inside and outside, and document analysis; research ethics; and the seven-part research proposal the pupil must write: basis, aims, questions, design, analysis plan, timetable, budget.

Mongolia's own way (Book 8): the senior book teaches society as a science and the state as a machine the citizen operates — sociology's three lenses, the law encountered as real contracts, dismissals and complaints, and democracy measured against Mongolia's own survey numbers.
