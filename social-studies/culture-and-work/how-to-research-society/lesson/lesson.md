# How to Research Society

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: how society is studied scientifically — a social research takes a question about the social world and answers it by a designed method, and the lesson walks two real Mongolian studies end to end (the national household socio-economic survey, and the Asian Development Bank's 2005 participatory poverty study of 1,516 people in Ulaanbaatar and five aimags); the two logics — deduction (from theory to cases) and induction (from cases to theory); quantitative against qualitative inquiry; the instruments — the questionnaire and its five parts, the experiment with its independent and dependent variables, content analysis, the individual and focus-group interview, observation from inside and outside, and document analysis; research ethics; and the seven-part research proposal the pupil must write: basis, aims, questions, design, analysis plan, timetable, budget — as Mongolia's books teach it.
