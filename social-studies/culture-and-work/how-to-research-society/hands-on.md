# Hands-on — How to Research Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.4 "Social research"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How society is studied scientifically — a social research takes a question about the social world and answers it by a designed method, and the lesson walks two real Mongolian studies end to end (the national household socio-economic survey, and the Asian Development Bank's 2005 participatory poverty study of 1,516 people in Ulaanbaatar and five aimags); the two logics — deduction (from theory to cases) and induction (from cases to theory); quantitative against qualitative inquiry; the instruments — the questionnaire and its five parts, the experiment with its independent and dependent variables, content analysis, the individual and focus-group interview, observation from inside and outside, and document analysis; research ethics; and the seven-part research proposal the pupil must write: basis, aims, questions, design, analysis plan, timetable, budget. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
