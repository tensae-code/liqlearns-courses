Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 10 Địa lí 10): a single Grade 10 course that runs the whole discipline — map craft, the Earth's spheres, then economic geography sector by sector — with Vietnam itself as the standing worked example (its power-plant and rice maps, the Red River's regime at Hanoi, Hanoi's climate chart, Vietnam's GDP structure, its internet and tourism figures) inside world-scale treatment.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 9 G9 Business and Accounting Studies 1996): Economics is introduced inside a Commerce book that starts from the pupil's own meals, clothes and schoolbooks, then reads Sri Lanka's whole economy off Central Bank 1990 tables (sector shares, import/export composition) before teaching trade channels, with People's Bank cheque and bill-of-exchange specimens as the money teaching.

Sri Lanka's own way (Grade 10 Commerce Introductory Book 1974): a full British-style commerce course rebuilt for Ceylon — every concept is landed on Ceylon data: the 1953 census of occupations, Ceylon's trade balances 1954–59, the rice subsidy accounts, mint-par exchange worked in rupees, and Gresham's law and the trade cycle taught a decade before the later Business Studies books appear.
