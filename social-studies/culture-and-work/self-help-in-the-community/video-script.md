# Video script — Self Help in the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Somalia Grade 1 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (Teacher Guide) (printed p. 38), teaches: the self-help campaign as a national habit — communities build what they need by their own voluntary labour instead of waiting.

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 21), teaches: the self-help lesson widened into a rule of life — living together, working together and loving each other as one habit.

Somalia Grade 3 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 11), teaches: self-help in Grade 3 as organised visits and homework — pupils visit a self-help workplace and do a self-help task at home.

Somalia Grade 6 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 63), teaches: self-help at the top of primary school — the nation's voluntary labour as an institution, not a slogan.

Somalia's own way (Grade 1): this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in.

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag.

Somalia's own way (Grade 3): Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic.

Somalia's own way (Grade 6): by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed.
