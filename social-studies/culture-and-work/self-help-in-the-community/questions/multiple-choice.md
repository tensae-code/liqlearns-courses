# Self Help in the Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Iskaa Wax U Qabso (Do It Yourselves — Voluntary Self-Help)" is mainly about —
a) the self-help campaign as a national habit — communities build what they need by their own voluntary labour instead of waiting  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the self-help campaign as a national habit — communities build what they need by their own voluntary labour instead of waiting**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The part of this lesson called "Iskaa wax u Qabso: isla jir, isla shaqayn, isjecel (Self-Help: Live Together, Work Together, Love Each Other)" is mainly about —
a) the self-help lesson widened into a rule of life — living together, working together and loving each other as one habit  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the self-help lesson widened into a rule of life — living together, working together and loving each other as one habit**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The part of this lesson called "Iskaa wax u Qabso (Self-Help)" is mainly about —
a) self-help in Grade 3 as organised visits and homework — pupils visit a self-help workplace and do a self-help task at home  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) self-help in Grade 3 as organised visits and homework — pupils visit a self-help workplace and do a self-help task at home**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The part of this lesson called "Iskaa wax u Qabso (Self-Help)" is mainly about —
a) self-help at the top of primary school — the nation's voluntary labour as an institution, not a slogan  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) self-help at the top of primary school — the nation's voluntary labour as an institution, not a slogan**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** The part of this lesson called "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" is mainly about —
a) this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in.  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in.**
