# Self Help in the Community — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Iskaa Wax U Qabso (Do It Yourselves — Voluntary Self-Help)" teaches: the self-help campaign as a national habit — communities build what they need by their own voluntary labour instead of waiting.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Iskaa wax u Qabso: isla jir, isla shaqayn, isjecel (Self-Help: Live Together, Work Together, Love Each Other)" teaches: the self-help lesson widened into a rule of life — living together, working together and loving each other as one habit.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part "Iskaa wax u Qabso (Self-Help)" teaches: self-help in Grade 3 as organised visits and homework — pupils visit a self-help workplace and do a self-help task at home.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's part "Iskaa wax u Qabso (Self-Help)" teaches: self-help at the top of primary school — the nation's voluntary labour as an institution, not a slogan.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in..
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
