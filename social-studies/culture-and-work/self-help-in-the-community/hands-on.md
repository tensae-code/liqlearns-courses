# Hands-on — Self Help in the Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Iskaa Wax U Qabso (Do It Yourselves — Voluntary Self-Help)
Work through this part with your hands: the self-help campaign as a national habit — communities build what they need by their own voluntary labour instead of waiting. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iskaa wax u Qabso: isla jir, isla shaqayn, isjecel (Self-Help: Live Together, Work Together, Love Each Other)
Work through this part with your hands: the self-help lesson widened into a rule of life — living together, working together and loving each other as one habit. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iskaa wax u Qabso (Self-Help)
Work through this part with your hands: self-help in Grade 3 as organised visits and homework — pupils visit a self-help workplace and do a self-help task at home. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iskaa wax u Qabso (Self-Help)
