# Video script — Trustworthiness and Public Money

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies (printed p. 106), teaches: Trustworthiness is a form of loyalty and truthfulness, and it is preserving people's property and keeping their secrets, among the best and noblest traits of Iraqi society with all its spectra; and its kinds are trustworthiness in performing work, in preserving public money, in transmitting speech, and in keeping secrets; and through trustworthiness man learns to preserve his personal property, his family's property, his school's property, people's property, and the homeland's property.

Iraq's own way: the components are named one by one with where each lives and what each built, equality is stated in the concept itself — all equal in rights and duties — and the values are taught as a civilisational inheritance, from keeping covenants "for thousands of years" to altruism as the road to social justice.
