# Hands-on — Trustworthiness and Public Money

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Trustworthiness is a form of loyalty and truthfulness, and it is preserving people's property and keeping their secrets, among the best and noblest traits of Iraqi society with all its spectra; and its kinds are trustworthiness in performing work, in preserving public money, in transmitting speech, and in keeping secrets; and through trustworthiness man learns to preserve his personal property, his family's property, his school's property, people's property, and the homeland's property. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iraq's way
Say how Iraq's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
