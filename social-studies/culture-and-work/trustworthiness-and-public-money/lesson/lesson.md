## Sub lesson 1 — Trustworthiness and Public Money

Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies (printed p. 106), teaches: Trustworthiness is a form of loyalty and truthfulness, and it is preserving people's property and keeping their secrets, among the best and noblest traits of Iraqi society with all its spectra; and its kinds are trustworthiness in performing work, in preserving public money, in transmitting speech, and in keeping secrets; and through trustworthiness man learns to preserve his personal property, his family's property, his school's property, people's property, and the homeland's property. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: the components are named one by one with where each lives and what each built, equality is stated in the concept itself — all equal in rights and duties — and the values are taught as a civilisational inheritance, from keeping covenants "for thousands of years" to altruism as the road to social justice.
