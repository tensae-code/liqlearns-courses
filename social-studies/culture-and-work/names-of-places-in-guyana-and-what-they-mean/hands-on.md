# Hands-on — Names of Places in Guyana and What They Mean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The meanings behind Guyanese place and building names — Linden named for President Linden Forbes Sampson Burnham, Bartica meaning "red earth", and Umana Yana, a Wai-Wai name meaning "meeting place of the people". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
