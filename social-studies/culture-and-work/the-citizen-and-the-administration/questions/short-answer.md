# The Citizen And the Administration — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 3.5 "Relations between citizen and administration" — The citizen facing the state, opened by the Zaisan case — two households' homes demolished in a land dispute where a district governor's 2008 order and a ministry's 2009 grant overlapped; the relation's two directions in one table — the citizen's rights (to human rights and freedoms; to petition and complain; to information) against the administration's duties (to decide lawfully; to resolve petitions and complaints; to give the information requested); the three instruments, distinguished — the complaint (гомдол, against a rights violation), the petition (өргөдөл, asking a body to start or stop an activity) and the request (хүсэлт, asking that a matter touching oneself or others be resolved); the road a complaint travels — first to the directly superior official, then to the Administrative Court — with its thirty-day windows and the three-month restoration for a deadline missed for good reason; what an administrative act is and the formal demands on it; the pre-trial resolution procedure; and administrative sanctions — the fine, detention, and deprivation of a special right (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the senior book teaches society as a science and the state as a machine the citizen operates — sociology's three lenses, the law encountered as real contracts, dismissals and complaints, and democracy measured against Mongolia's own survey numbers (any close wording).**
