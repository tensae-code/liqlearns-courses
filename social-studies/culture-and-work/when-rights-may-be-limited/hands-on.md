# Hands-on — When Rights May Be Limited

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.4 "Is it right to limit human rights?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: No right is absolute — every right carries duties, and rights may be limited only within the measure the law sets: the Universal Declaration's Article 29(2) and Mongolia's Constitution Article 19(2) say the same — limits only by law, to protect others' rights and freedoms, security, public order and morals; in a democracy limits are allowed in exactly two situations — where a person's act violates another's rights as a court decides, and in a declared emergency for national security; the worked example is quarantine: in an epidemic the movement of people in the affected place may be limited for a set time, to stop the disease spreading.
