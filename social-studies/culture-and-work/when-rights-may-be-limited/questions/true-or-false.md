# When Rights May Be Limited — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: no right is absolute — every right carries duties, and rights may be limited only within the measure the law sets: the Universal Declaration's Article 29(2) and Mongolia's Constitution Article 19(2) say the same — limits only by law, to protect others' rights and freedoms, security, public order and morals; in a democracy limits are allowed in exactly two situations — where a person's act violates another's rights as a court decides, and in a declared emergency for national security; the worked example is quarantine: in an epidemic the movement of people in the affected place may be limited for a set time, to stop the disease spreading.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
