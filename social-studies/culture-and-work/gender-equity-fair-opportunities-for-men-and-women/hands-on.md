# Hands-on — Gender Equity Fair Opportunities for Men and Women

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 1 "Equidad de género"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 45, teaches: gender equity means fair and equal opportunities for men and women; women were recognised as citizens with the vote in Guatemala only in 1945, and inequality also lives at home, where working women lack family support — with the Social Development Law's Article 4 on equality. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
