# Video script — Gender Equity Fair Opportunities for Men and Women

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 45, teaches: gender equity means fair and equal opportunities for men and women; women were recognised as citizens with the vote in Guatemala only in 1945, and inequality also lives at home, where working women lack family support — with the Social Development Law's Article 4 on equality.

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.
