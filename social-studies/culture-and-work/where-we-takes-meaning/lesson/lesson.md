## Sub lesson 1 — Where We Takes Meaning

Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente, printed p. 142, teaches: Where the word "WE" takes on meaning — Alarico Gómez's tale of the kind, hardworking little ants whose town faces the scorpion together; people with real houses must likewise face their locality's problems solidarily, as heaps of little ants, with community participation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: the primary course closes with "Misión Ciudadanía" — citizenship as a mission: the constitution's liberty carries the duty to respect others' liberty, democracy is practised in communal councils where "Popular Power is all of us", work is both a right and a duty, and the book ends on peace — human rights begin in the small places near home, in Eleanor Roosevelt's words, and pupils keep a notebook of rights kept and rights violated in their own small world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
