# Video script — Where We Takes Meaning

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente, printed p. 142, teaches: Where the word "WE" takes on meaning — Alarico Gómez's tale of the kind, hardworking little ants whose town faces the scorpion together; people with real houses must likewise face their locality's problems solidarily, as heaps of little ants, with community participation.

Venezuela's own way: the primary course closes with "Misión Ciudadanía" — citizenship as a mission: the constitution's liberty carries the duty to respect others' liberty, democracy is practised in communal councils where "Popular Power is all of us", work is both a right and a duty, and the book ends on peace — human rights begin in the small places near home, in Eleanor Roosevelt's words, and pupils keep a notebook of rights kept and rights violated in their own small world.
