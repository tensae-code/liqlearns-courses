# Hands-on — Where We Takes Meaning

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Where the word "WE" takes on meaning — Alarico Gómez's tale of the kind, hardworking little ants whose town faces the scorpion together; people with real houses must likewise face their locality's problems solidarily, as heaps of little ants, with community participation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
