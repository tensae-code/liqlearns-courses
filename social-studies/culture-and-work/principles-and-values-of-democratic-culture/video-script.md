# Video script — Principles and Values of Democratic Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mexico Grade 9 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 3 (secundaria) (printed pp. 313–320), teaches: The principles and values of democratic culture — responsible, informed action to promote and defend them, and young people's own experiences of citizen participation against democratic disenchantment.

---

Mexico's own way (Grade 9): the third year turns the course outward to action — participation spaces for children and adolescents, community projects for democratic culture, and democracy's own challenges (corruption, violence, disenchantment) named as things the young citizen is expected to work on.
