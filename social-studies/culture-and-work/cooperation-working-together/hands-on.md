# Hands-on — Cooperation Working Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book’s topic
Work through this lesson’s topic with your hands: Cooperation through the story of two cows tied together who eat only when they walk the same way and share. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mauritius’s way
Say how Mauritius’s book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
