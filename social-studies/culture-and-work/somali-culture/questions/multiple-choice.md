# Somali Culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Dhaqanka Soomaaliyeed (Somali Culture)" is mainly about —
a) the nation's own culture — its customs, poetry and ways — as the closing lesson of the grade  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the nation's own culture — its customs, poetry and ways — as the closing lesson of the grade**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The part of this lesson called "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" is mainly about —
a) Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic.  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic.**
