# The Six Families of Civic Values — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Six Families of Civic Values — civil society's values are varied and interlocking, covering all sides of human life: national values (love of homeland, sacrifice, struggle for its freedom and development, defending it); political values (freedom, democracy, the rule of law, justice, equality, accountability, respecting opinion and the other opinion); social values (respect for human dignity, tolerance, social solidarity, care for education, truthfulness, sincerity, honesty, cooperation, selflessness); economic values (justice in distributing wealth, no waste, respecting work practised according to law, respecting time, valuing manual work, forbidding cheating in commercial dealings, strengthening integrity and fighting corruption in all its forms); cultural values (interest in culture, literature and art, research and reading, adopting dialogue as a means of understanding, and using scientific research to develop the sides of life); and religious values (drawn from religion — faith in God, justice, equality, respect for human rights, tolerance, and rejecting racism and extremism) (any close wording).**
