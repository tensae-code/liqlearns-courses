Palestine's own way (Grade 7 Social Studies Part 1): civil society is taught entirely through Palestinian institutions named one by one — the Red Crescent, Al-Haq, the Independent Commission for Human Rights, the teachers' and women's unions — and its values lesson ends in a project: mining Palestine's own Declaration of Independence and Basic Law for their values and sorting them into the book's six families.
