# Hands-on — The Future is Built in the Present

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The future is built in the present — the world to build is one of social justice, equal conditions, democracy, social participation and peoples living together, with health and education guaranteed and happiness not founded on money and consumerism; building it is everyone's task and begins now, with the child's own part: plant and care for a tree, do not waste paper, avoid aerosols and throwaway things. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
