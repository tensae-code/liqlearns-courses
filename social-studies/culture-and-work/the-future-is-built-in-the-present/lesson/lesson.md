# The Future is Built in the Present

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: The future is built in the present — the world to build is one of social justice, equal conditions, democracy, social participation and peoples living together, with health and education guaranteed and happiness not founded on money and consumerism; building it is everyone's task and begins now, with the child's own part: plant and care for a tree, do not waste paper, avoid aerosols and throwaway things — as Venezuela's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Future is Built in the Present

Venezuela Grade 2 G2 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 138, teaches: The future is built in the present — the world to build is one of social justice, equal conditions, democracy, social participation and peoples living together, with health and education guaranteed and happiness not founded on money and consumerism; building it is everyone's task and begins now, with the child's own part: plant and care for a tree, do not waste paper, avoid aerosols and throwaway things. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: the family is taught through Venezuela's own Family Protection Law and through how families actually form and grow (courtship, weddings, births, learning from babyhood); equality is taught straight from the 1999 Constitution — equal rights whatever your skin, religion, sex or disability, in a mestizo population. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
