# Video script — The Future is Built in the Present

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela Grade 2 G2 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 138, teaches: The future is built in the present — the world to build is one of social justice, equal conditions, democracy, social participation and peoples living together, with health and education guaranteed and happiness not founded on money and consumerism; building it is everyone's task and begins now, with the child's own part: plant and care for a tree, do not waste paper, avoid aerosols and throwaway things.

Venezuela's own way: the family is taught through Venezuela's own Family Protection Law and through how families actually form and grow (courtship, weddings, births, learning from babyhood); equality is taught straight from the 1999 Constitution — equal rights whatever your skin, religion, sex or disability, in a mestizo population.
