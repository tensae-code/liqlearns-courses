# Gratitude to Those Who Served the Homeland — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Be grateful to those who have served the homeland — the homeland's benefactors in every field — the heroine Võ Thị Sáu, General Võ Nguyên Giáp, the composer Văn Cao, the surgeon Tôn Thất Tùng, a Heroic Vietnamese Mother, a woman professor of mathematics, a dairy company's founder — and the village that bears its founder's name; gratitude shown by caring for war-invalid and martyr families, laying incense at the memorial on 27 July, learning the homeland's history, and studying to build it (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way — the last primary Ethics book is organised as three strands by the authors themselves — moral education, life skills, economic education. Gratitude is widened from workers to everyone who has served the homeland in any field, famous or quiet, and "repaying gratitude" (đền ơn đáp nghĩa) is stated as every citizen's duty; the environment is taught as a commons — "the environment is ours; keeping and protecting it is what makes us civilised." (any close wording).**
