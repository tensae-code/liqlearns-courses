# Gratitude to Those Who Served the Homeland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The this part of this lesson is —
a) Be grateful to those who have served the homeland  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Be grateful to those who have served the homeland**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) The homeland's benefactors in every field — the heroine Võ Thị Sáu, General Võ Nguyên Giáp, the composer Văn Cao, the surgeon Tôn Thất Tùng, a Heroic Vietnamese Mother, a woman professor of mathematics, a dairy company's founder — and the village that bears its founder's name; gratitude shown by caring for war-invalid and martyr families, laying incense at the memorial on 27 July, learning the homeland's history, and studying to build it  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) The homeland's benefactors in every field — the heroine Võ Thị Sáu, General Võ Nguyên Giáp, the composer Văn Cao, the surgeon Tôn Thất Tùng, a Heroic Vietnamese Mother, a woman professor of mathematics, a dairy company's founder — and the village that bears its founder's name; gratitude shown by caring for war-invalid and martyr families, laying incense at the memorial on 27 July, learning the homeland's history, and studying to build it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The last part of this lesson is —
a) Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Vietnam's own way: the last primary Ethics book is organised as three strands by the authors themselves — moral education, life skills, economic education. Gratitude is widened from workers to everyone who has served the homeland in any field, famous or quiet, and "repaying gratitude" (đền ơn đáp nghĩa) is stated as every citizen's duty; the environment is taught as a commons — "the environment is ours; keeping and protecting it is what makes us civilised."  b) Vietnam's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) Vietnam's own way: the last primary Ethics book is organised as three strands by the authors themselves — moral education, life skills, economic education. Gratitude is widened from workers to everyone who has served the homeland in any field, famous or quiet, and "repaying gratitude" (đền ơn đáp nghĩa) is stated as every citizen's duty; the environment is taught as a commons — "the environment is ours; keeping and protecting it is what makes us civilised."**
