# Hands-on — Gratitude to Those Who Served the Homeland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the homeland's benefactors in every field — the heroine Võ Thị Sáu, General Võ Nguyên Giáp, the composer Văn Cao, the surgeon Tôn Thất Tùng, a Heroic Vietnamese Mother, a woman professor of mathematics, a dairy company's founder — and the village that bears its founder's name; gratitude shown by caring for war-invalid and martyr families, laying incense at the memorial on 27 July, learning the homeland's history, and studying to build it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
