# Video script — Gratitude to Those Who Served the Homeland

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 5 Đạo đức 5, printed pp. 5–12, teaches: The homeland's benefactors in every field — the heroine Võ Thị Sáu, General Võ Nguyên Giáp, the composer Văn Cao, the surgeon Tôn Thất Tùng, a Heroic Vietnamese Mother, a woman professor of mathematics, a dairy company's founder — and the village that bears its founder's name; gratitude shown by caring for war-invalid and martyr families, laying incense at the memorial on 27 July, learning the homeland's history, and studying to build it.

Vietnam's own way: the last primary Ethics book is organised as three strands by the authors themselves — moral education, life skills, economic education. Gratitude is widened from workers to everyone who has served the homeland in any field, famous or quiet, and "repaying gratitude" (đền ơn đáp nghĩa) is stated as every citizen's duty; the environment is taught as a commons — "the environment is ours; keeping and protecting it is what makes us civilised."
