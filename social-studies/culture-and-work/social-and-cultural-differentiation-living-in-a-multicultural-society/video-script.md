Indonesia's Grade 10 IPS-G10 student (printed p. 230), teaches: Bab IV C. Diferensiasi Sosial Budaya — teaches: social-cultural differentiation — horizontal, equal-level difference by physical, social and cultural marks (ethnicity, gender, religion, profession), the mark of a multicultural society.

Indonesia's own way: senior IPS defines its own subject map — sociology is entered through sosialisasi at a Yogyakarta landfill (TPA Piyungan) and a bullying-awareness video, and differentiation is counted in Indonesia's own census numbers: 1,340 ethnic groups and 747 regional languages (428 of them in Papua).
