# Video script — Advertising Changes from Place to Place

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 8–9, teaches: The form of advertising follows the place — in a small town one poster or the local radio reaches everyone (and word of mouth does the rest), while big cities multiply and diversify the media.

Argentina's own way: the ministry teaches media literacy as social studies — a free pupil fascicle that treats the adverts surrounding children as a social phenomenon to be read critically, from the town crier of colonial Buenos Aires to the television spot.
