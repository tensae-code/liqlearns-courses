# Hands-on — Advertising Changes from Place to Place

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The form of advertising follows the place — in a small town one poster or the local radio reaches everyone (and word of mouth does the rest), while big cities multiply and diversify the media. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
