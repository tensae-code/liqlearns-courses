# Hands-on — Responsibility the Other Side of Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 2 "Responsabilidad"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed p. 96, teaches: UNESCO's 1998 Declaration of Human Responsibilities and Duties — rights can only be enjoyed together with the duties that come with them; responsibility as answering for the consequences of our acts. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
