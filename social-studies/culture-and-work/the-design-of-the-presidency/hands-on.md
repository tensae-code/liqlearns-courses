# Hands-on — The Design of the Presidency

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Design of the Presidency
Work through this part with your hands: the presidency as designed — election, two four-year terms at most, impeachment and removal — and the office's two centuries of growth, war powers above all. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
