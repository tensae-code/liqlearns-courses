# Video script — The Arrival of Mongoloid Ethnic Groups in Myanmar

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar's Grade 6 Geography teacher guide teaches the arrival of Mongoloid ethnic groups in Myanmar in its social geography strand (§2.2): Myanmar's own people-story is geography here — the coming of the Mongoloid ethnic groups into Myanmar is studied as part of how Myanmar's population came to be.

This is a teacher guide, written for the teacher, not handed to the pupil. In Myanmar the social geography strand opens with the definition of social geography and the main ethnic groups, then turns to this arrival (§2.2) and then to the ethnic groups in Myanmar today (§2.3) — the country's own way of teaching its people is to begin with how they arrived.
