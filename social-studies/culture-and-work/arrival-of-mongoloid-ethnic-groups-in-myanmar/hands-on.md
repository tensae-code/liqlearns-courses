# Hands-on — The Arrival of Mongoloid Ethnic Groups in Myanmar

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Arrival on the map
Draw a simple map of Myanmar and mark, from the lesson, that this is where the Mongoloid ethnic groups arrived.

## Arrival, then today
Put the lesson's two people-topics in order — arrival first, the ethnic groups of today second — and say why that order makes sense.
