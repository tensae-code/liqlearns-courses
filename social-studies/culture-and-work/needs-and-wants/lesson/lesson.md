Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Однос потреба и жеља (Needs and wants)

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed pp. 16–17), teaches: telling needs from wants — water, sleep and food are basic needs no one can live without, while the trainers in the shop window and a new computer are wants, however strong — and respecting other people's needs and wants too. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
