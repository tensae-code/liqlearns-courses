# Hands-on — Needs and Wants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Однос потреба и жеља (Needs and wants)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: telling needs from wants — water, sleep and food are basic needs no one can live without, while the trainers in the shop window and a new computer are wants, however strong — and respecting other people's needs and wants too. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
