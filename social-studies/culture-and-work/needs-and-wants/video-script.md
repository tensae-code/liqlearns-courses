# Video script — Needs and Wants

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed pp. 16–17), teaches: Telling needs from wants — water, sleep and food are basic needs no one can live without, while the trainers in the shop window and a new computer are wants, however strong — and respecting other people's needs and wants too.

Serbia's own way: the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays.
