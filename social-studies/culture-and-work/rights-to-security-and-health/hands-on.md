# Hands-on — Rights to Security and Health

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: security as one of the most important human rights, stated in the Convention on the Rights of the Child and widened by the 1994 Human Development Report (food, health, environmental, economic and personal security); personal security is every human's right and attacking it is a crime the law punishes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mauritania's way
Say how Mauritania's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
