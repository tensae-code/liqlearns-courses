# Rights to Security and Health — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Security as one of the most important human rights, stated in the Convention on the Rights of the Child and widened by the 1994 Human Development Report (food, health, environmental, economic and personal security); personal security is every human's right and attacking it is a crime the law punishes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Health among the rights in the child-rights convention and the world's constitutions; the right means prevention first, then treatment in good time, with health-insurance and solidarity funds carrying the costs.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Mauritania's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
