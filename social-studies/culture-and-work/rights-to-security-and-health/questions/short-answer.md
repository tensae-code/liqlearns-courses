# Rights to Security and Health — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Right to Security — Security as one of the most important human rights, stated in the Convention on the Rights of the Child and widened by the 1994 Human Development Report (food, health, environmental, economic and personal security); personal security is every human's right and attacking it is a crime the law punishes (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Right to Health — Health among the rights in the child-rights convention and the world's constitutions; the right means prevention first, then treatment in good time, with health-insurance and solidarity funds carrying the costs (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mauritania's way — the book's own teaching way — Mauritania's own way: Year 4 is the commune — the whole first half walks the municipality's own law (Law 87/289), its mayor, councillors and council, and what it owes the school, the playground and the health centre; the second half turns to identity, belonging, two rights, and the behaviour values (any close wording).**
