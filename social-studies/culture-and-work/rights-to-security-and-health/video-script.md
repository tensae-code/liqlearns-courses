# Video script — Rights to Security and Health

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mauritania Grade 4 Civic Education SB (printed p. 27), teaches: Security as one of the most important human rights, stated in the Convention on the Rights of the Child and widened by the 1994 Human Development Report (food, health, environmental, economic and personal security); personal security is every human's right and attacking it is a crime the law punishes.

Mauritania Grade 4 Civic Education SB (printed p. 29), teaches: Health among the rights in the child-rights convention and the world's constitutions; the right means prevention first, then treatment in good time, with health-insurance and solidarity funds carrying the costs.

Mauritania's own way: Year 4 is the commune — the whole first half walks the municipality's own law (Law 87/289), its mayor, councillors and council, and what it owes the school, the playground and the health centre; the second half turns to identity, belonging, two rights, and the behaviour values
