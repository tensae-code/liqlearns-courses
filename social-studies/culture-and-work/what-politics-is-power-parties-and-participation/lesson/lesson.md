## Sub lesson 1 — Politics and its role in public life (Политика и её роль в общественной жизни) — Grade 10

Belarus Grade 10 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 53), teaches: Politics from Aristotle's "state or public affairs" to the modern science of politology; politics as activity aimed at winning state power and actually exercising it, including the struggle for power and the changing of power relations; power itself as the ability to subordinate others' behaviour to one's will — present in the family and the work collective too, with subordination as its essential sign. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system..
