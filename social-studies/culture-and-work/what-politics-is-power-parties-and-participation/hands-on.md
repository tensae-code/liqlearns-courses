# Hands-on — What Politics is Power Parties and Participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Politics from Aristotle's "state or public affairs" to the modern science of politology; politics as activity aimed at winning state power and actually exercising it, including the struggle for power and the changing of power relations; power itself as the ability to subordinate others' behaviour to one's will — present in the family and the work collective too, with subordination as its essential sign. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
