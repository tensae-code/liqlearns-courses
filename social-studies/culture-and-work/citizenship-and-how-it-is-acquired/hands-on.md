# Hands-on — Citizenship and How It is Acquired

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: becoming a Ugandan citizen by registration or by descent (parents or grandparents of the indigenous communities of 1926), and the rights and duties of citizens. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Uganda's way
Say how Uganda's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
