# Dress Customs Hat Sash And Boots — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: every part of the dress carries meaning — the hat (its crown, brim and red tassels, in Zanabazar's legend), the deel buttoned from the bottom up and never hung unfolded, the sash that must never be thrown away or left untied, and the boot whose upturned toe does not wound the earth; national dress is worn at state ceremony, from the President's dress to the honour guard's.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
