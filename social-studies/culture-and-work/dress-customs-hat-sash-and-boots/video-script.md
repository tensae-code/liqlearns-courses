Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 25, teaches: Every part of the dress carries meaning — the hat (its crown, brim and red tassels, in Zanabazar's legend), the deel buttoned from the bottom up and never hung unfolded, the sash that must never be thrown away or left untied, and the boot whose upturned toe does not wound the earth; national dress is worn at state ceremony, from the President's dress to the honour guard's.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
