# Hands-on — Dress Customs Hat Sash And Boots

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 7 "From the customs of Mongolian national dress" (Монгол үндэсний хувцасны ёсноос)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every part of the dress carries meaning — the hat (its crown, brim and red tassels, in Zanabazar's legend), the deel buttoned from the bottom up and never hung unfolded, the sash that must never be thrown away or left untied, and the boot whose upturned toe does not wound the earth; national dress is worn at state ceremony, from the President's dress to the honour guard's. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
