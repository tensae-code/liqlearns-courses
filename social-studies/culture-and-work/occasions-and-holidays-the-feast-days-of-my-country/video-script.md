# Video script — Occasions and Holidays the Feast Days of My Country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Syria Grade 3 Social Studies (Syria Grade 3 Social Studies G3, printed p. 108), teaches: The feast days Syria's children celebrate together — Teacher's Day, Evacuation Day (17 April), Army Day (1 August), Tree Day, the Hijri New Year, Eid al-Fitr and the Gregorian New Year — matching each feast to its symbol and telling the customs of the feast you love

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 3): Grade 3 is where the series turns civic — the child meets the vote inside a classroom quarrel (proposals, persuasion, a ballot box, the winner implemented), meets rights on World Children's Rights Day (20 November) as picture pairs where every right is matched by its duty, and meets the inclusive city: the society-we-want lesson is built around services for people with special needs, down to the sign-language alphabet and Braille.
