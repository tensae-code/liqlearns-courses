# Hands-on — Occasions and Holidays the Feast Days of My Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the feast days Syria's children celebrate together — Teacher's Day, Evacuation Day (17 April), Army Day (1 August), Tree Day, the Hijri New Year, Eid al-Fitr and the Gregorian New Year — matching each feast to its symbol and telling the customs of the feast you love. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
