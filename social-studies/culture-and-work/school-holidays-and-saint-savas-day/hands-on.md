# Hands-on — School Holidays and Saint Savas Day

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Школски празници (School holidays)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every school celebrates its own School Day for the place it stands in, and on 27 January every school in Serbia celebrates its school slava, Saint Sava's Day, with a performance and competitions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Школски празници (School holidays)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: each school keeps its own School Day, and on 27 January all schools in Serbia celebrate the school slava of Saint Sava, the enlightener, with performances, sports competitions, exhibitions of pupils' work and awards. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
