# Video script — School Holidays and Saint Savas Day

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 1 Свет око нас 1 (printed p. 30), teaches: Every school celebrates its own School Day for the place it stands in, and on 27 January every school in Serbia celebrates its school slava, Saint Sava's Day, with a performance and competitions.

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed p. 11), teaches: Each school keeps its own School Day, and on 27 January all schools in Serbia celebrate the school slava of Saint Sava, the enlightener, with performances, sports competitions, exhibitions of pupils' work and awards.

Serbia's own way: the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast. the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays.
