Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 34, teaches: The large livestock (бод мал — cattle, horses, camels) are pastured by season and by kind: cattle graze out early and come home at dusk, horses are driven to distant pasture and watched by turns, camels seek the cool places; the yak (сарлаг) of the high mountains and the Mongol cow of the steppe are fed and grazed differently, and each kind's fattening depends on fitting pasture and water to its nature.

Mongolia's own way (Book 4): the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other.
