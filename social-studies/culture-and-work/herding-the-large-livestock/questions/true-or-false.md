# Herding the Large Livestock — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the large livestock (бод мал — cattle, horses, camels) are pastured by season and by kind: cattle graze out early and come home at dusk, horses are driven to distant pasture and watched by turns, camels seek the cool places; the yak (сарлаг) of the high mountains and the Mongol cow of the steppe are fed and grazed differently, and each kind's fattening depends on fitting pasture and water to its nature.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
