# Hands-on — Herding the Large Livestock

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 11 "The special way of herding large livestock" (Бод мал маллах онцлог)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The large livestock (бод мал — cattle, horses, camels) are pastured by season and by kind: cattle graze out early and come home at dusk, horses are driven to distant pasture and watched by turns, camels seek the cool places; the yak (сарлаг) of the high mountains and the Mongol cow of the steppe are fed and grazed differently, and each kind's fattening depends on fitting pasture and water to its nature. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
