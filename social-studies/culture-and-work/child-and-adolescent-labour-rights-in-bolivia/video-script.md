# Video script — Child and Adolescent Labour Rights in Bolivia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia Grade 12 BO Social Sciences Sec 6 (printed p. 458), teaches: Child and adolescent labour and its protection — from Industrial-Revolution exploitation to the ILO Conventions 138 (minimum age) and 182 (worst forms), the 1924 Geneva Declaration and the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child; Bolivia's own Ley Nº 548 Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente: equal pay for adolescents, union rights, protective rules for self-employed work (Art. 133), the banned dangerous jobs list (Art. 136), and the Plurinational Integral Protection System.

Bolivia's own way: the final year turns from history to the citizen's own legal armour — identity in a plural nation, a supreme decree ordering the state itself to despatriarcalise, a Youth Law, an anti-trafficking law taught with self-protection advice for teenagers, and the labour rights of working children under Bolivia's own Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente.
