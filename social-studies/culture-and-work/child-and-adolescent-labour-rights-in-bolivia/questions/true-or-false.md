# Child and Adolescent Labour Rights in Bolivia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Child and adolescent labour and its protection — from Industrial-Revolution exploitation to the ILO Conventions 138 (minimum age) and 182 (worst forms), the 1924 Geneva Declaration and the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child; Bolivia's own Ley Nº 548 Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente: equal pay for adolescents, union rights, protective rules for self-employed work (Art. 133), the banned dangerous jobs list (Art. 136), and the Plurinational Integral Protection System.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Bolivia's Grade 12 student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
