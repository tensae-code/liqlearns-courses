# Child and Adolescent Labour Rights in Bolivia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Revisión Historiográfica del Trabajo Infantil y Adolescente — child and adolescent labour and its protection — from Industrial-Revolution exploitation to the ILO Conventions 138 (minimum age) and 182 (worst forms), the 1924 Geneva Declaration and the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child; Bolivia's own Ley Nº 548 Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente: equal pay for adolescents, union rights, protective rules for self-employed work (Art. 133), the banned dangerous jobs list (Art. 136), and the Plurinational Integral Protection System (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way — the final year turns from history to the citizen's own legal armour — identity in a plural nation, a supreme decree ordering the state itself to despatriarcalise, a Youth Law, an anti-trafficking law taught with self-protection advice for teenagers, and the labour rights of working children under Bolivia's own Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente (any close wording).**
