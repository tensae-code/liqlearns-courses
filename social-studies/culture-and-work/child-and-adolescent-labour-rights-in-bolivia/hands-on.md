# Hands-on — Child and Adolescent Labour Rights in Bolivia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: child and adolescent labour and its protection — from Industrial-Revolution exploitation to the ILO Conventions 138 (minimum age) and 182 (worst forms), the 1924 Geneva Declaration and the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child; Bolivia's own Ley Nº 548 Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente: equal pay for adolescents, union rights, protective rules for self-employed work (Art. 133), the banned dangerous jobs list (Art. 136), and the Plurinational Integral Protection System. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
