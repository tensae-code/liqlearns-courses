# Hands-on — Exclusion and Inclusion in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 15 Excluir o incluir, ¡esa es la cuestión!
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 118–121, teaches: social exclusion is separating people out for ethnic, economic, cultural, political or practical reasons, the opposite of inclusion; pupils map the "spaces of exclusion" between home and school and ask how they can keep others from being discriminated against. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
