# Exclusion and Inclusion in Society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guía 15 Excluir o incluir, ¡esa es la cuestión! — Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 118–121, teaches: social exclusion is separating people out for ethnic, economic, cultural, political or practical reasons, the opposite of inclusion; pupils map the "spaces of exclusion" between home and school and ask how they can keep others from being discriminated against. (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Colombia's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way — Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city. (any close wording).**
