## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 15 Excluir o incluir, ¡esa es la cuestión!

Colombia Grade 4 CS_Grado4_02, printed pp. 118–121, teaches: social exclusion is separating people out for ethnic, economic, cultural, political or practical reasons, the opposite of inclusion; pupils map the "spaces of exclusion" between home and school and ask how they can keep others from being discriminated against. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: citizenship is taught from the child's own standing — a child can be a citizen, participation is practised first in family and school (electing the personero), and exclusion is studied through Colombian cases: displaced children mocked at school and Arhuaco indigenous people in the city. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
