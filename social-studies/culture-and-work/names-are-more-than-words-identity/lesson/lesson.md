## Sub lesson 1 — Names are more than words

Greece Grade 5 Koinoniki kai Politiki Agogi E' Dimotikou (printed p. 12), teaches: Names carry identity, family and origin; respecting other people's names. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greece's way — the book's own teaching way

Greece's own way: citizenship is taught through two child guides (Danai and Alexandros) who walk the pupil from belonging, through rights, to the Greek Parliament and the municipality — Greece's own institutions are the examples throughout. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
