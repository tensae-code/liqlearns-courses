The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India's Grade 8 Resources and Development book, the *India Grade 8 Resources and Development* (printed p. 45), teaches: Ch.4: paired case studies comparing a small Indian farm with a large commercial USA farm.

India's own way (Grade 8 Resources and Development): Grade 8 is a resources year, world-first but India-anchored throughout: resources and their conservation, then land/soil/water/vegetation, minerals and power, agriculture, industries and human resources — every world pattern is paired with its Indian distribution map and an Indian case study (Jamshedpur steel, Ahmedabad textiles, Bangalore IT, a farm in India set against a farm in the USA).
