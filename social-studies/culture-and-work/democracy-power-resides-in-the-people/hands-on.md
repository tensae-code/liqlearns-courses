# Hands-on — Democracy Power Resides in the People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: since the 1987 Constitution Nicaragua defines itself as a democratic, participative and representative republic — power resides in the people, who delegate it through free elections. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Principios de la forma de gobierno
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the sovereign power of the people expressed through the Powers; the republican, democratic, participative and representative form of government; and the division of powers centred on justice, limit and control. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Nicaragua's way
Say how Nicaragua's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
