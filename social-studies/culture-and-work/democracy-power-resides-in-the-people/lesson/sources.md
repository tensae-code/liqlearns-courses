**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Nicaragua).**

Nicaragua's own way (Book 11 — Estudios Sociales G7 full-year (student book, Spanish)): secondary Social Studies opens with the State itself — the municipality and its mayor first, then Nicaragua's FOUR powers (the Electoral Power beside Executive, Legislative and Judicial), in a republic defined as democratic, participative and representative, where power resides in the people.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 12 — CCSS Módulo Autoformativo G7 (student book, Spanish)): the rural self-study twin of Book 11 teaches the same State in more institutional detail — the Electoral Power gets its full ladder (Supreme Electoral Council, departmental and municipal councils, Caribbean autonomous-region councils, vote-receiving boards) and participatory democracy is practised through the Family, Community and Life Cabinets.
