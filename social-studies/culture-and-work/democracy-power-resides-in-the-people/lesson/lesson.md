## Sub lesson 1 — Una república democrática, participativa y representativa

Nicaragua Grade 7 Estudios Sociales G7 full-year (printed p. 15), teaches: Since the 1987 Constitution Nicaragua defines itself as a democratic, participative and representative republic — power resides in the people, who delegate it through free elections. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Principios de la forma de gobierno

Nicaragua Grade 7 CCSS Módulo Autoformativo G7 (printed p. 20), teaches: The sovereign power of the people expressed through the Powers; the republican, democratic, participative and representative form of government; and the division of powers centred on justice, limit and control. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Nicaragua's way — the books' own teaching way

Nicaragua's own way: secondary Social Studies opens with the State itself — the municipality and its mayor first, then Nicaragua's FOUR powers (the Electoral Power beside Executive, Legislative and Judicial), in a republic defined as democratic, participative and representative, where power resides in the people. the rural self-study twin of Book 11 teaches the same State in more institutional detail — the Electoral Power gets its full ladder (Supreme Electoral Council, departmental and municipal councils, Caribbean autonomous-region councils, vote-receiving boards) and participatory democracy is practised through the Family, Community and Life Cabinets. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
