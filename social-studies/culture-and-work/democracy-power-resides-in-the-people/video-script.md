# Video script — Democracy Power Resides in the People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nicaragua Grade 7 Estudios Sociales G7 full-year (printed p. 15), teaches: Since the 1987 Constitution Nicaragua defines itself as a democratic, participative and representative republic — power resides in the people, who delegate it through free elections.

Nicaragua Grade 7 CCSS Módulo Autoformativo G7 (printed p. 20), teaches: The sovereign power of the people expressed through the Powers; the republican, democratic, participative and representative form of government; and the division of powers centred on justice, limit and control.

Nicaragua's own way: secondary Social Studies opens with the State itself — the municipality and its mayor first, then Nicaragua's FOUR powers (the Electoral Power beside Executive, Legislative and Judicial), in a republic defined as democratic, participative and representative, where power resides in the people. the rural self-study twin of Book 11 teaches the same State in more institutional detail — the Electoral Power gets its full ladder (Supreme Electoral Council, departmental and municipal councils, Caribbean autonomous-region councils, vote-receiving boards) and participatory democracy is practised through the Family, Community and Life Cabinets.
