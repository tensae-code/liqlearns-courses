# Democracy Power Resides in the People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Una república democrática, participativa y representativa — Since the 1987 Constitution Nicaragua defines itself as a democratic, participative and representative republic — power resides in the people, who delegate it through free elections (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Principios de la forma de gobierno — The sovereign power of the people expressed through the Powers; the republican, democratic, participative and representative form of government; and the division of powers centred on justice, limit and control (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Nicaragua's way — the books' own teaching way — secondary Social Studies opens with the State itself — the municipality and its mayor first, then Nicaragua's FOUR powers (the Electoral Power beside Executive, Legislative and Judicial), in a republic defined as democratic, participative and representative, where power resides in the people. the rural self-study twin of Book 11 teaches the same State in more institutional detail — the Electoral Power gets its full ladder (Supreme Electoral Council, departmental and municipal councils, Caribbean autonomous-region councils, vote-receiving boards) and participatory democracy is practised through the Family, Community and Life Cabinets. (any close wording).**
