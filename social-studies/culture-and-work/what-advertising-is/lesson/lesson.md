## Sub lesson 1 — What Advertising is

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 5–7, teaches: Advertising defined two ways — the Royal Academy's (commercial announcements to attract buyers) and UNESCO's (a text that promotes a product, an idea, or any effect the advertiser wants) — and the flood of adverts that surrounds us in television, radio, street billboards, newspapers and the internet. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Slogans and Advertising Strategies

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 20–23, teaches: The slogan — a short, memorable phrase that fixes a brand or a product's advantage in the public's memory — and the strategies behind adverts: famous faces and testimonials, scientific arguments, beautiful models, humour, and the staged family table — so readers can recognise them. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Argentina's way — the book's own teaching way

Argentina's own way: the ministry teaches media literacy as social studies — a free pupil fascicle that treats the adverts surrounding children as a social phenomenon to be read critically, from the town crier of colonial Buenos Aires to the television spot.
