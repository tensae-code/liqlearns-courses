# Video script — What Advertising is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 5–7, teaches: Advertising defined two ways — the Royal Academy's (commercial announcements to attract buyers) and UNESCO's (a text that promotes a product, an idea, or any effect the advertiser wants) — and the flood of adverts that surrounds us in television, radio, street billboards, newspapers and the internet.

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 20–23, teaches: The slogan — a short, memorable phrase that fixes a brand or a product's advantage in the public's memory — and the strategies behind adverts: famous faces and testimonials, scientific arguments, beautiful models, humour, and the staged family table — so readers can recognise them.

Argentina's own way: the ministry teaches media literacy as social studies — a free pupil fascicle that treats the adverts surrounding children as a social phenomenon to be read critically, from the town crier of colonial Buenos Aires to the television spot.
