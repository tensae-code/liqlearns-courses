# Hands-on — What Advertising is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Advertising defined two ways — the Royal Academy's (commercial announcements to attract buyers) and UNESCO's (a text that promotes a product, an idea, or any effect the advertiser wants) — and the flood of adverts that surrounds us in television, radio, street billboards, newspapers and the internet. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Slogans and Advertising Strategies
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The slogan — a short, memorable phrase that fixes a brand or a product's advantage in the public's memory — and the strategies behind adverts: famous faces and testimonials, scientific arguments, beautiful models, humour, and the staged family table — so readers can recognise them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
