# What Advertising is — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Advertising defined two ways — the Royal Academy's (commercial announcements to attract buyers) and UNESCO's (a text that promotes a product, an idea, or any effect the advertiser wants) — and the flood of adverts that surrounds us in television, radio, street billboards, newspapers and the internet.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: The slogan — a short, memorable phrase that fixes a brand or a product's advantage in the public's memory — and the strategies behind adverts: famous faces and testimonials, scientific arguments, beautiful models, humour, and the staged family table — so readers can recognise them.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Argentina's student fascicle, named in lesson/sources.md.**
