# Kinds of Laws and Regulations

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: systems are of two kinds — basic systems, issued by royal order, which concern the state's foundation, its ruling institutions and authorities and the main rights (the Basic Law of Governance, the Shura Council system, the Allegiance Commission system, the Council of Ministers system, the Regions system), and general systems, issued by royal decree, on public topics or the provisions of administrative bodies (the Child Protection system, the Anti-Cybercrime system, the Saudi Central Bank system, the Food and Drug Authority system); and regulations are of three kinds — executive, organisational and control (ضبط) — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 8 book places Saudi Arabia inside the Gulf Cooperation Council — its organs and goals, its achievements and its challenges — then teaches systems and regulations (how a system is made, their kinds and their benefits), and ends with planning priorities, time management and evaluating plans, and hobbies including the old folk games.
