# Hands-on — Kinds of Laws and Regulations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: systems are of two kinds — basic systems, issued by royal order, which concern the state's foundation, its ruling institutions and authorities and the main rights (the Basic Law of Governance, the Shura Council system, the Allegiance Commission system, the Council of Ministers system, the Regions system), and general systems, issued by royal decree, on public topics or the provisions of administrative bodies (the Child Protection system, the Anti-Cybercrime system, the Saudi Central Bank system, the Food and Drug Authority system); and regulations are of three kinds — executive, organisational and control (ضبط). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
