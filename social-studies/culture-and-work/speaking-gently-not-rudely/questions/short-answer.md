# Speaking Gently Not Rudely — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Do not speak rudely — Toe Toe speaks rudely to his mother and his teacher and learns to say the same things gently and correctly (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way — every value is taught as a named story or picture-sequence about one child (or one lion cub) plus a question set — never as a rule list; the patriotism chapter teaches love of country as the eight ethnic peoples in their own dress holding hands around the Independence Monument. (any close wording).**
