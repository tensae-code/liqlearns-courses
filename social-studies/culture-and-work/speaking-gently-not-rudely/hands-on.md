# Hands-on — Speaking Gently Not Rudely

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do not speak rudely
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Toe Toe speaks rudely to his mother and his teacher and learns to say the same things gently and correctly. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
