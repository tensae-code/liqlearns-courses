# Citizenship Values Engagement and Responsibility — short answer

**Answer: Citizenship Values Engagement and Responsibility — the quality of citizen read in the Constitution (a democratic and solidary republic; Title III ch. III on citizens' duties), citizenship as social responsibility in attitude, action and engagement, a case study of citizenship in Cité Soleil, and a class charter of civic responsibility and engagement (any close wording).**

**Answer: Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way — Citizenship is closed by the money bond: after defining the citizen and practising engagement, pupils end on contribution — the civic duty to pay taxes (Constitution article 52) and the state's duty to redistribute in national solidarity.
