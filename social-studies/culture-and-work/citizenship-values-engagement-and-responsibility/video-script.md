# Video script — Citizenship Values Engagement and Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 8 EC3 8e Citoyenneté (printed p. 11), teaches: the quality of citizen read in the Constitution (a democratic and solidary republic; Title III ch. III on citizens' duties), citizenship as social responsibility in attitude, action and engagement, a case study of citizenship in Cité Soleil, and a class charter of civic responsibility and engagement.

Haiti's own way: Citizenship is closed by the money bond: after defining the citizen and practising engagement, pupils end on contribution — the civic duty to pay taxes (Constitution article 52) and the state's duty to redistribute in national solidarity.
