# Hands-on — Principles Policies and Development Goals of Democratic Nations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Principles and practices in democratic nations; Policies, programs and activities of democratic nations; Development goals of developed and developing countries
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The social, economic, cultural and religious principles and practices of democratic nations; the policies, programs and activities of democratic governments; and comparing the development policies, plans and goals of developed and developing democratic countries. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
