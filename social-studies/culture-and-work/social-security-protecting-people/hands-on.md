# Hands-on — Social Security Protecting People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the social institutions (مؤسسات اجتماعی) and what they do for us — last year we met the police and the fire service; this year the health-insurance booklet (دفترچه بیمه درمانی), which people show at the clinic and the pharmacy and which carries each person's details — guard it like the identity booklet, do not soil or lose it; and the Welfare Organisation (سازمان بهزیستی), which serves people with special difficulties and issues them a welfare card carrying their details; at the clinic we keep our turn. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
