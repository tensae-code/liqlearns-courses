# Video script — Social Security Protecting People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 6 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 72, teaches: the social institutions (مؤسسات اجتماعی) and what they do for us — last year we met the police and the fire service; this year the health-insurance booklet (دفترچه بیمه درمانی), which people show at the clinic and the pharmacy and which carries each person's details — guard it like the identity booklet, do not soil or lose it; and the Welfare Organisation (سازمان بهزیستی), which serves people with special difficulties and issues them a welfare card carrying their details; at the clinic we keep our turn.

Iran's own way: the last special-needs primary book turns outward — life skills at school and at work, then the country learned by travelling it (green Gilan and the Caspian ports, the warm southern islands), and the institutions that stand behind the citizen: the insurance booklet and the Welfare Organisation's card.
