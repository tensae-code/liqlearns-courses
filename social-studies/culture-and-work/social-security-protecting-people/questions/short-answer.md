# Social Security Protecting People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Social Security Protecting People — the social institutions (مؤسسات اجتماعی) and what they do for us — last year we met the police and the fire service; this year the health-insurance booklet (دفترچه بیمه درمانی), which people show at the clinic and the pharmacy and which carries each person's details — guard it like the identity booklet, do not soil or lose it; and the Welfare Organisation (سازمان بهزیستی), which serves people with special difficulties and issues them a welfare card carrying their details; at the clinic we keep our turn (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Iran's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
