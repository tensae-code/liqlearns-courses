# Democracys Values And Mongolias Constitution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 5.1 "Democracy's values and Mongolia's Constitution" — Democracy from its first Mongolian morning — the Democratic Union's first rally on Human Rights Day, 10 December 1989, and the petition it carried to the party's Central Committee and the People's Great Khural (end one-party direction of the state; citizens' inviolable rights placed above the party; a permanent parliament; national questions settled by referendum); what democracy is (demos + kratos — the people's power), in its direct and representative forms; its five principles — openness, justice, the rule of law, respect for the minority (the minority is not a negligible thing: its rights are protected by law), and consensus; its values drawn as one diagram — human rights, citizen participation, equal rights, accountability and transparency, the multi-party system, oversight of power, the rule of law, education, and regular, free, fair elections; and the two constitutions side by side — 1960's articles against 1992's Article 16, so pupils can see exactly what changed (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the senior book teaches society as a science and the state as a machine the citizen operates — sociology's three lenses, the law encountered as real contracts, dismissals and complaints, and democracy measured against Mongolia's own survey numbers (any close wording).**
