# Hands-on — Identity Who Am I

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Self-identity explored through identity cards (interests and qualities nobody can guess from looks); one card matching several classmates shows people share similarities inside their differences. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
