# Video script — Tongas Womens Kautaha

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 9 Book 1 (printed p. 73), teaches: How Tongan women organise in kautaha (national, religion-based and special-interest women's organisations) to build good homes and develop their country, and women's roles and responsibilities in Tongan society.

Samoa's own way: Social organisation is taught from the inside out — the groups a child belongs to first, then the aiga and its matai, then the village's own circle of groups (matai at the centre, aumaga as the matai's right hand), and only then a neighbour kingdom's political system as the worked example of government.
