# Hands-on — Tongas Womens Kautaha

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How Tongan women organise in kautaha (national, religion-based and special-interest women's organisations) to build good homes and develop their country, and women's roles and responsibilities in Tongan society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Samoa's way
Say how Samoa's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
