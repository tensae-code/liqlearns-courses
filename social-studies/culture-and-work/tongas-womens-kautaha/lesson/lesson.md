## Sub lesson 2 — Samoa's way — the book's own teaching way

Samoa's own way: Social organisation is taught from the inside out — the groups a child belongs to first, then the aiga and its matai, then the village's own circle of groups (matai at the centre, aumaga as the matai's right hand), and only then a neighbour kingdom's political system as the worked example of government. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
