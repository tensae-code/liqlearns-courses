# Hands-on — Historically Marginalised Groups and Their Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Historically Marginalised Groups and Their Rights
Work through this part with your hands: who the historically marginalised are — indigenous peoples, Afro-descendants, women, people with disabilities, migrants and refugees, LGBTTIQ+ people — how each is excluded, and the actions that secure their equal opportunities under human rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
