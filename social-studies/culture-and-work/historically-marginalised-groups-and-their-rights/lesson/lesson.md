Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Historically Marginalised Groups and Their Rights

Mexico Grade 8 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 2 (secundaria) (printed pp. 230–236), teaches: Who the historically marginalised are — indigenous peoples, Afro-descendants, women, people with disabilities, migrants and refugees, LGBTTIQ+ people — how each is excluded, and the actions that secure their equal opportunities under human rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way

Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
