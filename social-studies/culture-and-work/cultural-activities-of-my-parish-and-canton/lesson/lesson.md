## Sub lesson 1 — Cultural Activities of My Parish and Canton

Ecuador Grade 3 Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016), printed p. 102, teaches: Telling apart and valuing the cultural activities of one's own parish and canton — festivals, customs and typical dishes (such as the Fiestas de San Pedro de Cayambe) — and why children must value and pass on their customs and traditions, which identify them as Ecuadorians. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador's own way: the SM edition teaches local government from the child's own ground up — parish, then canton — with Ecuador's real institutions (the elected junta parroquial, the teniente político, the concejo municipal and the GADs), and teaches values through the Andean minga and the Kichwa reciprocity idea randi-randi, under the book's recurring Cultura del Buen Vivir spreads. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
