# Hands-on — Cultural Activities of My Parish and Canton

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Telling apart and valuing the cultural activities of one's own parish and canton — festivals, customs and typical dishes (such as the Fiestas de San Pedro de Cayambe) — and why children must value and pass on their customs and traditions, which identify them as Ecuadorians. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
