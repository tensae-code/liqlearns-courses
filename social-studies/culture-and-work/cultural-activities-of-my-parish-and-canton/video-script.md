# Video script — Cultural Activities of My Parish and Canton

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 3 Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016), printed p. 102, teaches: Telling apart and valuing the cultural activities of one's own parish and canton — festivals, customs and typical dishes (such as the Fiestas de San Pedro de Cayambe) — and why children must value and pass on their customs and traditions, which identify them as Ecuadorians.

Ecuador's own way: the SM edition teaches local government from the child's own ground up — parish, then canton — with Ecuador's real institutions (the elected junta parroquial, the teniente político, the concejo municipal and the GADs), and teaches values through the Andean minga and the Kichwa reciprocity idea randi-randi, under the book's recurring Cultura del Buen Vivir spreads.
