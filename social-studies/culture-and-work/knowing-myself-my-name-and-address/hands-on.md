# Hands-on — Knowing Myself My Name and Address

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book’s topic
Work through this lesson’s topic with your hands: Presenting yourself (name, age, address) in a circle game and filling an "about me" flower. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mauritius’s way
Say how Mauritius’s book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
