# Hands-on — Community Policing Groups Keep Us Safe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A community policing group on patrol helps protect people and their property and makes communities safe and peaceful; our responsibility to protect other citizens, their property, schools, churches, temples and mosques. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
