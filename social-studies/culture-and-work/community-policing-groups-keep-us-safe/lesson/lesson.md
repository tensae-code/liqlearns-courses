# Community Policing Groups Keep Us Safe

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a community policing group on patrol helps protect people and their property and makes communities safe and peaceful; our responsibility to protect other citizens, their property, schools, churches, temples and mosques — as Guyana's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Protecting our community

Guyana Grade 3 Social Studies For Our Children Book 3 (printed p. 57), teaches: A community policing group on patrol helps protect people and their property and makes communities safe and peaceful; our responsibility to protect other citizens, their property, schools, churches, temples and mosques. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: the whole book is one widening idea — a community is people living and working together who share services; three invented communities (Riverview, Mountain Gap, Runsvale) carry every lesson, and the book ends not on facts but on duties: courtesy, respect for national symbols, and protecting the community. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
