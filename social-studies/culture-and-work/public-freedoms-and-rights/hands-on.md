# Hands-on — Public Freedoms and Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: freedom as doing/saying what one wants within known limits; the freedoms and rights the state guarantees all citizens (constitution Article 10) and when the state may restrict freedom. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mauritania's way
Say how Mauritania's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
