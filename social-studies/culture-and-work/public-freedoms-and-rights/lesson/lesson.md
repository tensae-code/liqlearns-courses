## Sub lesson 2 — Mauritania's way — the book's own teaching way

Mauritania's own way: civics is built as three competencies — the child's rights and the state's law first, then Mauritania inside its regional and world organisations, then a long values ladder (moderation, advice, volunteering, equality, heritage, honour-brotherhood-justice) taught one value per lesson with observe–learn–discuss–conclude steps This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
