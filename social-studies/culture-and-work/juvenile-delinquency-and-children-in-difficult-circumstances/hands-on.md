# Hands-on — Juvenile Delinquency and Children in Difficult Circumstances

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A juvenile delinquent is a child between 11 and 17 guilty of an offence, vandalism, pilferage or behaviour beyond parental control; causes include poor socialization, poor parent-child relationships, neglect, bad companions and drugs; young offenders go before a juvenile court, to a detention home or on probation — never jailed with adults — and Guyana cares for its C.E.D.C (Children in Especially Difficult Circumstances) through the Children's Service Department and the New Horizon Home at Sophia: "we must be our brother's keeper". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
