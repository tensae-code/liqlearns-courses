# Video script — Juvenile Delinquency and Children in Difficult Circumstances

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 9 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 3 (printed pp. 34–35), teaches: A juvenile delinquent is a child between 11 and 17 guilty of an offence, vandalism, pilferage or behaviour beyond parental control; causes include poor socialization, poor parent-child relationships, neglect, bad companions and drugs; young offenders go before a juvenile court, to a detention home or on probation — never jailed with adults — and Guyana cares for its C.E.D.C (Children in Especially Difficult Circumstances) through the Children's Service Department and the New Horizon Home at Sophia: "we must be our brother's keeper".

Guyana's own way: the secondary book teaches the same citizenship at national scale — CARICOM as the region's own institution, the family as society's oldest institution, women's equality written into the 1980 Constitution and the Acts of the 1990s, and the state answering pollution through the Environmental Protection Agency.
