# Juvenile Delinquency and Children in Difficult Circumstances — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Juvenile delinquency — a juvenile delinquent is a child between 11 and 17 guilty of an offence, vandalism, pilferage or behaviour beyond parental control; causes include poor socialization, poor parent-child relationships, neglect, bad companions and drugs; young offenders go before a juvenile court, to a detention home or on probation — never jailed with adults — and Guyana cares for its C.E.D.C (Children in Especially Difficult Circumstances) through the Children's Service Department and the New Horizon Home at Sophia: "we must be our brother's keeper" (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
