# Juvenile Delinquency and Children in Difficult Circumstances — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: A juvenile delinquent is a child between 11 and 17 guilty of an offence, vandalism, pilferage or behaviour beyond parental control; causes include poor socialization, poor parent-child relationships, neglect, bad companions and drugs; young offenders go before a juvenile court, to a detention home or on probation — never jailed with adults — and Guyana cares for its C.E.D.C (Children in Especially Difficult Circumstances) through the Children's Service Department and the New Horizon Home at Sophia: "we must be our brother's keeper".
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
