## Sub lesson 1 — Juvenile delinquency

Guyana Grade 9 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 3 (printed pp. 34–35), teaches: A juvenile delinquent is a child between 11 and 17 guilty of an offence, vandalism, pilferage or behaviour beyond parental control; causes include poor socialization, poor parent-child relationships, neglect, bad companions and drugs; young offenders go before a juvenile court, to a detention home or on probation — never jailed with adults — and Guyana cares for its C.E.D.C (Children in Especially Difficult Circumstances) through the Children's Service Department and the New Horizon Home at Sophia: "we must be our brother's keeper".
