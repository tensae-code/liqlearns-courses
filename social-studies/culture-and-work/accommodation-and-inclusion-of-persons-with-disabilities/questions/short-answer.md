# Accommodation and Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Accommodation and Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities — accommodation is preparing and equipping buildings — schools, hospitals, universities, workplaces, streets and public places — so persons with disabilities can use them and reach them easily, to ease their inclusion in the society they live in, together with founding special centres to train and qualify them to rely on themselves and engage in their society; inclusion means giving persons with disabilities the opportunity to take part in public life like other ordinary persons — joining the education system in schools and universities and gaining the qualifications that enable them to hold posts in state institutions, achieving justice and equality, and taking part in social and voluntary activities (any close wording).**
