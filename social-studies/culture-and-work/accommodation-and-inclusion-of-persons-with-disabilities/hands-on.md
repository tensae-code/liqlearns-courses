# Hands-on — Accommodation and Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Accommodation and Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Accommodation is preparing and equipping buildings — schools, hospitals, universities, workplaces, streets and public places — so persons with disabilities can use them and reach them easily, to ease their inclusion in the society they live in, together with founding special centres to train and qualify them to rely on themselves and engage in their society; inclusion means giving persons with disabilities the opportunity to take part in public life like other ordinary persons — joining the education system in schools and universities and gaining the qualifications that enable them to hold posts in state institutions, achieving justice and equality, and taking part in social and voluntary activities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
