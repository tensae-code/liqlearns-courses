# Video script — Accommodation and Inclusion of Persons with Disabilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine Grade 7 Social Studies Part 2, printed p. 64, teaches: accommodation is preparing and equipping buildings — schools, hospitals, universities, workplaces, streets and public places — so persons with disabilities can use them and reach them easily, to ease their inclusion in the society they live in, together with founding special centres to train and qualify them to rely on themselves and engage in their society; inclusion means giving persons with disabilities the opportunity to take part in public life like other ordinary persons — joining the education system in schools and universities and gaining the qualifications that enable them to hold posts in state institutions, achieving justice and equality, and taking part in social and voluntary activities.

Palestine's own way: human rights are taught where Palestine lives them — the Geneva Conventions are read against the occupation's prisons, with the prisoners' own day (17 April) as a national day, and disability is taught with the occupation named among its causes in Palestine, down to the 1987 bone-breaking policy.
