**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Yemen, Eritrea).**

Yemen's own way (G6 National Education): Grade 6 National Education builds citizenship in widening circles — family, then tribe, then constitution — and teaches the tribe honestly as a real social component with its own customs and a role in keeping social peace, before the constitution unit names the citizen's rights, duties and the qualities of the good citizen (love of homeland shown in deeds, guarding public property, constructive criticism).

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Citizenship Education): a standalone Citizenship Education subject built entirely from Eritrean stories, poems and dialogues — responsibility is taught through a shop-queue drama and a family Tandoor day, the state through the 1993 referendum story, and patriotism as practical service (keeping the school clean, planting trees at the new clinic) that even a child can do.
