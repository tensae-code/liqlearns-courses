# Hands-on — Socialisation Becoming a Member of Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how a child is socialised by the family, the school (rules, team spirit, cooperation) and the peer group — learning discipline, respect for elders and the culture of the society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
