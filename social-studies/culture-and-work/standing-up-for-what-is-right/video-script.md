# Video script — Standing Up for What is Right

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 5 Đạo đức 5, printed pp. 25–30, teaches: Moral courage — refuse to let a friend copy, make the little brother wear his helmet, speak for the classmate wrongly blamed, step in when a small child is bullied, back the girl mocked for collecting recycling, return the money a seller dropped — and the sentry who asked even Hồ Chí Minh for his papers, because the rule binds everyone.

Vietnam's own way: the last primary Ethics book is organised as three strands by the authors themselves — moral education, life skills, economic education. Gratitude is widened from workers to everyone who has served the homeland in any field, famous or quiet, and "repaying gratitude" (đền ơn đáp nghĩa) is stated as every citizen's duty; the environment is taught as a commons — "the environment is ours; keeping and protecting it is what makes us civilised."
