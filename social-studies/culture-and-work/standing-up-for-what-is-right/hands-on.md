# Hands-on — Standing Up for What is Right

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: moral courage — refuse to let a friend copy, make the little brother wear his helmet, speak for the classmate wrongly blamed, step in when a small child is bullied, back the girl mocked for collecting recycling, return the money a seller dropped — and the sentry who asked even Hồ Chí Minh for his papers, because the rule binds everyone. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
