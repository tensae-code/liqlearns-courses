Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12* (printed pp. 45–53), teaches: Topic 12.3 (part): Developing the craft villages — their roles (586,000 jobs in 2024 at some 4–5 million đồng a month, shifting the rural economy, keeping national culture, drawing tourists), the present state (1,975 recognised villages in 2024, counted by trade group and by region; Hanoi has 334), the directions to 2030, and their effects for good and ill on economy, society, resources and environment (pollution the hard problem).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12): the final-year electives turn homeward — the disasters Vietnam actually faces and how to live with them, how Vietnam itself is divided into regions for development, and the craft villages that carry its rural economy and culture — even printing the country's new 34-province table in force from 1 July 2025.
