# Hands-on — Developing Craft Villages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Topic 12.3 (part): Developing the craft villages — their roles (586,000 jobs in 2024 at some 4–5 million đồng a month, shifting the rural economy, keeping national culture, drawing tourists), the present state (1,975 recognised villages in 2024, counted by trade group and by region; Hanoi has 334), the directions to 2030, and their effects for good and ill on economy, society, resources and environment (pollution the hard problem). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
