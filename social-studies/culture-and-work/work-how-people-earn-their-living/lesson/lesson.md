## Sub lesson 1 — Tema 9 O Trabalho

Angola Estudo do Meio G2 (printed p. 75), teaches: Work as the activity by which men, women and young people earn their living — the fisherman sells fish to buy what his family needs; the doctor heals; the teacher teaches. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Angola's way — the book's own teaching way

Angola's own way: Grade 2 widens each Grade 1 topic by one step — the family gains kinship names and needs, the school gains its parts and the care of school materials, and the book closes with two short themes that are pure community life: work, and transport and communications. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
