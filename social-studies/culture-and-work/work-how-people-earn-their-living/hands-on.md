# Hands-on — Work How People Earn Their Living

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tema 9 O Trabalho
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: work as the activity by which men, women and young people earn their living — the fisherman sells fish to buy what his family needs; the doctor heals; the teacher teaches. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
