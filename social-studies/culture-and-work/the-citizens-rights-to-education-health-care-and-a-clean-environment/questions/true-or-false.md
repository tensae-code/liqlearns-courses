# The Citizens Rights to Education Health Care and a Clean Environment — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The citizen's three rights from the state — education (free in every stage and compulsory to the end of basic school, schools built equally in all governorates, the school book given free by the Ministry), health care (free services and government hospitals, free vaccinations for children), and living in a clean environment (parks, street cleaning, waste containers, laws against cutting trees, a ministry for the environment).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Grade 4 drops the Me-and-You ring structure for a straight civics unit that defines citizenship in one sentence — love of the land, defending it, enjoying the rights, committing to the duties, respecting the state's systems and laws — then cashes each word out: rights are what the state hands the citizen free (school, clinic, clean streets), duties are what the citizen hands back, down to crossing at the pedestrian crossing and not throwing litter; the unit closes by honouring the martyrs..
**Answer: True**
