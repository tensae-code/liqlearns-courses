# Hands-on — The Citizens Rights to Education Health Care and a Clean Environment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the citizen's three rights from the state — education (free in every stage and compulsory to the end of basic school, schools built equally in all governorates, the school book given free by the Ministry), health care (free services and government hospitals, free vaccinations for children), and living in a clean environment (parks, street cleaning, waste containers, laws against cutting trees, a ministry for the environment). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
