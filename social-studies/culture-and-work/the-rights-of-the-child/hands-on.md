# Hands-on — The Rights of the Child

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (a child is under 18; 192 states including Bahrain); the personal rights — privacy, freedom of expression by speech, writing and drawing, the right to life and to grow up in family care, and Bahrain's own institutions caring for orphans. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The rights to food and healthy housing, health care, free compulsory education; the special care of children with disabilities; protection from drugs, exploitation and child labour; the same rights for every child whatever their colour or race; the rights to information, to rest, play and culture; no conscription under 18; and the child's own duties at school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahrain's way
Say how Bahrain's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
