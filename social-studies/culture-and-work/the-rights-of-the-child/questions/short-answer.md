# The Rights of the Child — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Rights of the Child 1 — the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (a child is under 18; 192 states including Bahrain); the personal rights — privacy, freedom of expression by speech, writing and drawing, the right to life and to grow up in family care, and Bahrain's own institutions caring for orphans (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Rights of the Child 2 — the rights to food and healthy housing, health care, free compulsory education; the special care of children with disabilities; protection from drugs, exploitation and child labour; the same rights for every child whatever their colour or race; the rights to information, to rest, play and culture; no conscription under 18; and the child's own duties at school (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bahrain's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
