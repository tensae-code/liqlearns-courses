# The Rights of the Child — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (a child is under 18; 192 states including Bahrain); the personal rights — privacy, freedom of expression by speech, writing and drawing, the right to life and to grow up in family care, and Bahrain's own institutions caring for orphans.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: The rights to food and healthy housing, health care, free compulsory education; the special care of children with disabilities; protection from drugs, exploitation and child labour; the same rights for every child whatever their colour or race; the rights to information, to rest, play and culture; no conscription under 18; and the child's own duties at school.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Bahrain's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
