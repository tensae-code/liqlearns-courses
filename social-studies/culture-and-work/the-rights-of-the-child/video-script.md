# Video script — The Rights of the Child

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bahrain Grade 4 BH Citizenship G4 (printed p. 27), teaches: The UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (a child is under 18; 192 states including Bahrain); the personal rights — privacy, freedom of expression by speech, writing and drawing, the right to life and to grow up in family care, and Bahrain's own institutions caring for orphans.

Bahrain Grade 4 BH Citizenship G4 (printed p. 33), teaches: The rights to food and healthy housing, health care, free compulsory education; the special care of children with disabilities; protection from drugs, exploitation and child labour; the same rights for every child whatever their colour or race; the rights to information, to rest, play and culture; no conscription under 18; and the child's own duties at school.

Bahrain's own way: citizenship is taught outwards from the child — homeland love and public property first, then the services of Bahrain's own government, the child's own rights under the UN Convention Bahrain ratified, choosing leaders from the class council up to the King, Crown Prince and Prime Minister, and finally Bahrain inside the Gulf Cooperation Council, its own regional family.
