## Sub lesson 2 — Angola's way — the book's own teaching way

Angola's own way: Grade 6 makes the pupils legislators of their own classroom — the class writes its own regulation (with a no-violence chapter) as the lived rehearsal for democracy — and teaches duties concretely, down to naming the taxes (IVA, income, property) and calling the non-payer a tax evader; tolerance is defined as the price of choosing democracy. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
