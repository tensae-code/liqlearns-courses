# Hands-on — Electronic Accountability and Acceptable Use

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Electronic accountability
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: electronic accountability (المحاسبة الإلكترونية) defined as the ethics and governance of using digital technologies, carrying responsibility and deserving blame; its values — bearing responsibility, ethics, accountability; the acceptable-use policy (سياسة الاستخدام المقبول) as a written set of rules an owner or network manager applies, with its parts (philosophy, definitions, code of conduct, consequences of violation, approval signatures, disclaimer) — and, without it, chaos and corruption Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
