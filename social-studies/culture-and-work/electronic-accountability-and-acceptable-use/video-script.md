# Video script — Electronic Accountability and Acceptable Use

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Digital Citizenship & Electronic Communication Culture, G12 Term 2 activity book, answered edition, printed p. 87, teaches: electronic accountability (المحاسبة الإلكترونية) defined as the ethics and governance of using digital technologies, carrying responsibility and deserving blame; its values — bearing responsibility, ethics, accountability; the acceptable-use policy (سياسة الاستخدام المقبول) as a written set of rules an owner or network manager applies, with its parts (philosophy, definitions, code of conduct, consequences of violation, approval signatures, disclaimer) — and, without it, chaos and corruption

United Arab Emirates' own way: digital citizenship is a named school subject of its own, run with the Aqdar empowerment programme — access is a right the state measures (the DAI index, 100% internet spread in the Emirati market), the Emirati ID card's number is dissected as a civics lesson, and every abuse is answered with the federation's own law and its own reporting service (Abu Dhabi Police "Aman", 8002626).
