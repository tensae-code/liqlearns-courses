# Electronic Accountability and Acceptable Use — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: electronic accountability (المحاسبة الإلكترونية) defined as the ethics and governance of using digital technologies, carrying responsibility and deserving blame; its values — bearing responsibility, ethics, accountability; the acceptable-use policy (سياسة الاستخدام المقبول) as a written set of rules an owner or network manager applies, with its parts (philosophy, definitions, code of conduct, consequences of violation, approval signatures, disclaimer) — and, without it, chaos and corruption
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: digital citizenship is a named school subject of its own, run with the Aqdar empowerment programme — access is a right the state measures (the DAI index, 100% internet spread in the Emirati market), the Emirati ID card's number is dissected as a civics lesson, and every abuse is answered with the federation's own law and its own reporting service (Abu Dhabi Police "Aman", 8002626).
**Answer: True**
