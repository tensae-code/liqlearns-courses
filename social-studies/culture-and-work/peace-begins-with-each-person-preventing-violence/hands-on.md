# Hands-on — Peace Begins with Each Person Preventing Violence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 4 "Cultura de Paz" and "Prevención de la cultura de violencia"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 192–194, teaches: peace, the value peoples most aspire to, is bound to justice and human rights and begins with each person; when a conflict comes there are two options — turn it into a fight, or use the difference to dialogue constructively; and the state measures (education first) that prevent a culture of violence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
