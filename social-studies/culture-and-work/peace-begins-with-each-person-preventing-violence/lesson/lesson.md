# Peace Begins with Each Person Preventing Violence

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: peace, the value peoples most aspire to, is bound to justice and human rights and begins with each person; when a conflict comes there are two options — turn it into a fight, or use the difference to dialogue constructively; and the state measures (education first) that prevent a culture of violence — as Guatemala's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4 "Cultura de Paz" and "Prevención de la cultura de violencia"

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 192–194, teaches: peace, the value peoples most aspire to, is bound to justice and human rights and begins with each person; when a conflict comes there are two options — turn it into a fight, or use the difference to dialogue constructively; and the state measures (education first) that prevent a culture of violence. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
