# Video script — Peace Begins with Each Person Preventing Violence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed pp. 192–194, teaches: peace, the value peoples most aspire to, is bound to justice and human rights and begins with each person; when a conflict comes there are two options — turn it into a fight, or use the difference to dialogue constructively; and the state measures (education first) that prevent a culture of violence.

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.
