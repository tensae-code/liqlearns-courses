# Hands-on — Taking Part in School Councils

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Applications in the institution's councils (تطبيقات في مجالس المؤسسة)
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 9 Tajdid Ijtiamiat 3AC, printed p. 159, teaches: the school's councils, who sits on them and how a pupil takes part. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
