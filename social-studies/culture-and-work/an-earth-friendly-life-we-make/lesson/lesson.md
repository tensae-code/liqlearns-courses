# An Earth Friendly Life We Make

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the waste our life throws away, sorting and recycling, and searching for what we ourselves can do for the earth — as Japan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — わたしたちがつくる、地球にやさしい生活 (An earth-friendly life that we make)

Japan Grades 3–4 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 184), teaches: The waste our life throws away, sorting and recycling, and searching for what we ourselves can do for the earth. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Japan's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 2): the middle pupils' reader widens in the same four steps as the other two Doutoku books — raise yourself, relate to people, feel life, relate to everyone — and plants the first civics inside moral teaching: society's rules are introduced through a banned swing the class wins back by making rules, and fairness is taught as never shutting anyone out. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
