# Civic Education and Civic Values Morocco — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Civics framework: what civic education is — Morocco Grade 5 Dalil Moufid Ijtiamiat (Teacher Guide), printed p. 119, teaches: civic education as learning democratic rights and citizenship founded on communication, sharing and living together; civic behaviour as the person's relation to society and to the state through its institutions, with legal, moral, social and environmental dimensions; the civic values — self-respect, respect for others, freedom with responsibility, honesty, dignity, equality, fairness, solidarity, rejecting discrimination, tolerance and coexistence amid difference — learned by practice, and the fight against uncivic behaviour (violence, cheating, bribery, mistreatment). **2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Morocco's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way — Morocco's own way: Moroccan primary Social Studies is one integrated subject (History + Geography + Civic Education) built around "my homeland"; its civics third is a full civic-education programme in its own right — civic behaviour defined as the citizen's relation to society and to the state through its institutions, civic values (freedom, dignity, solidarity, equality, tolerance, rejecting discrimination) learned by practice not memorisation, and the classroom itself run as a small democracy (a negotiated class charter and an elected class cooperative with ballot box and vote count). (any close wording).**
