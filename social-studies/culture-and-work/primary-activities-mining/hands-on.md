# Hands-on — Primary activities — mining

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
List minerals or mineral products used in your home — salt, fuel, metal. Trace one back: where in the ground did it begin?

## Activity 2
Take an imaginary deposit near your town. Work through the factor list: which factors help it become a mine, which block it?

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say what a mineral is, with the book's named examples. Say what extraction is. Name the factors that decide whether a deposit is dug.
