# Primary activities — mining

## Do it first

List minerals or mineral products used in your home — salt, fuel, metal. Trace one back: where in the ground did it begin?

## Sub lesson 1 — What minerals are

**Minerals are naturally occurring chemical substances or compounds in or on the crust** — **solid, liquid or gas**: non-metallic and metallic ores, **petroleum, natural gas and coal** are named.

**Extraction** is the work of **separating and taking them out**; some deposits lie **deep**, some **shallow**.

## Sub lesson 2 — What decides whether they are dug

A mineral in the ground is not yet a mine. The factors that decide extraction: **the deposit's size, its depth, its grade/quality, price, the level of technology, skilled manpower, capital, whether a market exists, political conditions, climate and transport**. Miss the decisive ones — no capital, no market, no transport — and the deposit stays in the ground.

## Say it to your companion
