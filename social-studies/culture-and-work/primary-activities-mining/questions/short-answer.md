# Primary activities — mining — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three states minerals can take.
**Answer:** Solid, liquid or gas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name five factors that decide extraction.
**Answer:** Any five of: size, depth, grade/quality, price, technology level, skilled manpower, capital, market, political conditions, climate, transport.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What two depths are deposits found at?
**Answer:** Deep and shallow.
