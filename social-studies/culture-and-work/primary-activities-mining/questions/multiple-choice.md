# Primary activities — mining — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Minerals are —
a) Factory-made chemicals  b) Naturally occurring chemical substances or compounds in or on the crust  c) Only metals  d) Only solids
**Answer: b) Naturally occurring chemical substances or compounds in or on the crust**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Extraction is —
a) Selling minerals  b) Separating minerals and taking them out  c) Burning minerals  d) Grading minerals
**Answer: b) Separating minerals and taking them out**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a factor deciding extraction?
a) The deposit's grade or quality  b) The miners' songs  c) The town's festivals  d) The river's name
**Answer: a) The deposit's grade or quality**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A deposit with no market and no transport will likely —
a) Be dug at once  b) Stay in the ground  c) Become a farm  d) Raise prices
**Answer: b) Stay in the ground**
