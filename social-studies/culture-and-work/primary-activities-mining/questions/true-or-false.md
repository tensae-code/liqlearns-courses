# Primary activities — mining — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Petroleum, natural gas and coal are named as minerals.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** All mineral deposits lie deep underground.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Price is one of the factors that decide extraction.
**Answer: True**
