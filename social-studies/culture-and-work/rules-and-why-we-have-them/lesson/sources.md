**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Venezuela, Bangladesh).**

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 6): an inquiry-based combined History and Social Science course taught through a class teacher character (Khushi Apa) and group investigations; civics begins not with definitions but with a football game played with and without rules, and sociology is built from a village flood story (the Dharmagola grain bank) before any term is named.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela): coexistence is the spine of the primary book — respect, rules, family and community are each defined in plain words, then practised: the class votes in assembly, community leaders must learn to agree, and solidarity is taught from Lincoln's collection for an enslaved sailor to Caracas's free children's heart hospital serving the whole continent.
