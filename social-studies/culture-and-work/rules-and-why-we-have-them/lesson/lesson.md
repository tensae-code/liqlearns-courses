## Sub lesson 1 — Rules and Why We Have Them

Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 20, teaches: From the rules of the game to the rules of the school — every game has its rules (in football only the goalkeeper may touch the ball with his hands; the referee is the judge who can warn, sanction and send off), and school likewise has its norms or rules for living together. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: coexistence is the spine of the primary book — respect, rules, family and community are each defined in plain words, then practised: the class votes in assembly, community leaders must learn to agree, and solidarity is taught from Lincoln's collection for an enslaved sailor to Caracas's free children's heart hospital serving the whole continent.
