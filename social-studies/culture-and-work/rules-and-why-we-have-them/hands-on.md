# Hands-on — Rules and Why We Have Them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: From the rules of the game to the rules of the school — every game has its rules (in football only the goalkeeper may touch the ball with his hands; the referee is the judge who can warn, sanction and send off), and school likewise has its norms or rules for living together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
