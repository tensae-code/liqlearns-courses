# The Homeland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Jordan is my homeland  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Jordan is my homeland**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Jordan as the child's own homeland — belonging to it and loving it  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Jordan as the child's own homeland — belonging to it and loving it**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Jordan's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Jordan's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Jordan's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.  b) Jordan's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) Jordan's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.**
