## Sub lesson 1 — ¿Cómo eligen a sus representantes políticos las NPIOC?

Bolivia Grade 10 BO Social Sciences Sec 4 (printed p. 41), teaches: How the Indigenous Originary Campesino Nations and the Afro-Bolivian people elect their representatives directly, by their own norms and procedures, into departmental and regional assemblies and municipal councils in eight departments — and parity democracy: candidate lists of 50% women and 50% men, alternated one by one. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way

Bolivia's own way: citizenship is taught as intercultural democracy — the Western forms (direct, participative, representative) completed by the indigenous peoples' own communitarian democracy, with parity and alternation between women and men written into every candidate list, and the year's closing civics arc runs from women's rights to despatriarcalización, the dismantling of patriarchy as a task of the Plurinational State itself.
