# Hands-on — Npioc Representatives and Gender Parity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how the Indigenous Originary Campesino Nations and the Afro-Bolivian people elect their representatives directly, by their own norms and procedures, into departmental and regional assemblies and municipal councils in eight departments — and parity democracy: candidate lists of 50% women and 50% men, alternated one by one. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
