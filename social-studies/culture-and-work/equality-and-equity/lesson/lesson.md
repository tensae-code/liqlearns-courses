## Sub lesson 1 — Equality and Equity

Rwanda P4 SRS SB, printed p. 24, teaches: equality means we are all the same and should be treated the same way; equity means people get a fair share of what they need and are treated in a fair or just way. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rwanda's way — the book's own teaching way

Rwanda's own way (Primary Four — Rwanda P4 SRS SB): P4 states rights before duties of the state: rights belong to everybody whatever their place, age, sex or wealth; equality is then split from equity (same treatment vs a fair share of what each needs), and district government is taught as a council that collects local taxes and elects its own Mayor This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
