# Hands-on — Equality and Equity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Equality and Equity
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Equality means we are all the same and should be treated the same way; equity means people get a fair share of what they need and are treated in a fair or just way. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Rwanda's way
Say how Rwanda's book teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
