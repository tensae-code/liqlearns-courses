# Video script — Equality and Equity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rwanda P4 SRS SB, printed p. 24, teaches: equality means we are all the same and should be treated the same way; equity means people get a fair share of what they need and are treated in a fair or just way.

Rwanda's own way (Primary Four — Rwanda P4 SRS SB): P4 states rights before duties of the state: rights belong to everybody whatever their place, age, sex or wealth; equality is then split from equity (same treatment vs a fair share of what each needs), and district government is taught as a council that collects local taxes and elects its own Mayor
