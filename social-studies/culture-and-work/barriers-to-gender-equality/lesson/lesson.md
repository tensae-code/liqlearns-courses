## Sub lesson 1 — Activity 5 Barriers and hindrances to gender equality in South Sudan

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 93, teaches: the barrier map — culture and rigid attitudes, lack of government support, ignorance, poverty and lack of role models; and the hindrance list — high illiteracy among women, social perceptions that devalue girls' education, gender-based violence, no money for court claims, female genital mutilation in some communities, and early marriage. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way: Gender equality is argued from South Sudanese lives — an elder who educates his daughters against tradition, and four named women leaders — and peace itself is put in women's hands: the book prints the view that gender equality is a tool that can end South Sudan's civil war.
