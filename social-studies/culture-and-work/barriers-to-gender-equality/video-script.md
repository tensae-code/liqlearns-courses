# Video script — Barriers to Gender Equality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 93, teaches: the barrier map — culture and rigid attitudes, lack of government support, ignorance, poverty and lack of role models; and the hindrance list — high illiteracy among women, social perceptions that devalue girls' education, gender-based violence, no money for court claims, female genital mutilation in some communities, and early marriage.

South Sudan's own way: Gender equality is argued from South Sudanese lives — an elder who educates his daughters against tradition, and four named women leaders — and peace itself is put in women's hands: the book prints the view that gender equality is a tool that can end South Sudan's civil war.
