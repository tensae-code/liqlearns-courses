# Hands-on — Barriers to Gender Equality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 5 Barriers and hindrances to gender equality in South Sudan
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the barrier map — culture and rigid attitudes, lack of government support, ignorance, poverty and lack of role models; and the hindrance list — high illiteracy among women, social perceptions that devalue girls' education, gender-based violence, no money for court claims, female genital mutilation in some communities, and early marriage. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
