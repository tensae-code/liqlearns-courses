# Culture — culture and identity

## Do it first

Research the cultural practice of the traders in your local market: how do they dress, greet and arrange their goods? Write three observations.

## Sub lesson 1 — Culture tells people how to live

Culture tells people **how to live, what to wear, how to use their resources and what tools to use** — it is a **marker of identity**: it shows who a group is.

[Picture 11521 — a market where each trade group dresses and works its own way]

## Sub lesson 2 — Every group has its own

**Countries, nations, nationalities, peoples and even single areas** have their own culture and cultural system. **Every family** can have a cultural practice of its own — and so can groups like **traders and professionals**.

## Say it to your companion
