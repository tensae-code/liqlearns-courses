# Culture — culture and identity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture is a marker of —
a) Wealth  b) Identity  c) Climate  d) Age
**Answer: b) Identity**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture tells people how to live, what to wear, how to use resources and —
a) What tools to use  b) How to fly  c) How to tax  d) How to sail
**Answer: a) What tools to use**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which can have a cultural practice of its own?
a) Only countries  b) Only nations  c) Even a single family  d) Only capitals
**Answer: c) Even a single family**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Traders and professionals are named as —
a) Groups that can have their own cultural practice  b) People without culture  c) Tax collectors  d) Map makers
**Answer: a) Groups that can have their own cultural practice**
