# Culture — culture and identity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is culture called a marker of identity?
**Answer:** Because it shows who a group is — how they live, dress, use resources and tools.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four levels of group with their own culture.
**Answer:** Any four of: countries, nations, nationalities, peoples, single areas, families, traders, professionals.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What local group does the book send pupils to research?
**Answer:** The local traders and their cultural practice.
