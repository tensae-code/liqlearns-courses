# Hands-on — Culture — culture and identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Research the cultural practice of the traders in your local market: how do they dress, greet and arrange their goods? Write three observations.

## Activity 2
Write one cultural practice that belongs to your family alone — a food, a day, a way of doing something.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say four things culture tells people. Name the levels of group that each have their own culture. Give the book's example of smaller groups with their own practice: families, traders, professionals.
