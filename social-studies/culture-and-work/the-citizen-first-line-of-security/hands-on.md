# Hands-on — The Citizen First Line of Security

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the good citizen is the homeland's first line of defence and the first official of security; we keep the blessing of security by commitment (obeying the order), support (backing the security agencies and following their instructions) and responsibility (reporting criminals) — on seeing suspicious acts, call 990, help the security men and keep out of their way; spreading rumours, begging that funds terrorism and treachery harm the homeland's security. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
