# Video script — The Citizen First Line of Security

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 5 Social Studies (printed pp. 53–54), teaches: The good citizen is the homeland's first line of defence and the first official of security; we keep the blessing of security by commitment (obeying the order), support (backing the security agencies and following their instructions) and responsibility (reporting criminals) — on seeing suspicious acts, call 990, help the security men and keep out of their way; spreading rumours, begging that funds terrorism and treachery harm the homeland's security.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 5 book teaches security and state institutions as service — security is defined first, the citizen is named the first line of security, the security agencies are listed one by one with their tasks, state institutions are split into public and private, and each region's Emir receives citizens and hears their needs.
