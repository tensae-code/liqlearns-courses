# Social Change Evolution Reform Revolution and Progress — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Social processes and the change of society (Социальные процессы и изменение общества) — Grade 10 — a social process as a series of connected social changes (mass, sustained, with cause-and-effect between its stages), its external and internal conditions and its tempos — evolutionary against revolutionary; the forms of society's change — evolution, reform and revolution — and the ideas of progress and regress by which change is judged (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system. (any close wording).**
