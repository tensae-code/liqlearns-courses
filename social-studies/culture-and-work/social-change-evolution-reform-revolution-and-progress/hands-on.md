# Hands-on — Social Change Evolution Reform Revolution and Progress

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A social process as a series of connected social changes (mass, sustained, with cause-and-effect between its stages), its external and internal conditions and its tempos — evolutionary against revolutionary; the forms of society's change — evolution, reform and revolution — and the ideas of progress and regress by which change is judged. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Belarus's way
Say how Belarus' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
