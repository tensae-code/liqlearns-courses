# Hands-on — Self Discipline

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Self-Discipline
Work through this part with your hands: self-discipline as identifying and focusing on priorities, making timely decisions, supporting your stand with arguments and evidence, and developing sincerity and honesty in daily responsibilities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Achieving Your Goals
Work through this part with your hands: a goal is what you aim to achieve; set specific goals that fit your ability, make a plan with performance criteria, follow a timetable, evaluate yourself daily, weekly, monthly and yearly, and adjust; discipline — controlling and leading yourself, doing the right thing without supervision — is essential, as Neema passed by following her timetable while Athumani failed by playing instead of studying. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
