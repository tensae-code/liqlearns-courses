# Video script — Social Movements and Social Organisation in Ecuador

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 5 Estudios Sociales 5to EGB, printed p. 184, teaches: Ecuador's social movements (environmentalists, peasants, women, workers, students) on a timeline with the changes each won, and why social organisations matter for building a just and equitable society, with their contributions and what is still unachieved (criterion CE.CS.3.13).

Ecuador's own way: the Grade 5 book ends its history-and-geography march with a civics section that teaches participation as Ecuador's own story — its social movements, its fight against child labour, and inclusion of women and people with disabilities — practised in miniature through a class assembly that votes on proposals.
