# Hands-on — Social Movements and Social Organisation in Ecuador

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ecuador's social movements (environmentalists, peasants, women, workers, students) on a timeline with the changes each won, and why social organisations matter for building a just and equitable society, with their contributions and what is still unachieved (criterion CE.CS.3.13). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
