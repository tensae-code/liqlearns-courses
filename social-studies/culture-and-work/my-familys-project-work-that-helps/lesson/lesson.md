## Sub lesson 2 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: Grade 4 turns skill into service in three widening steps — what I am good at, what my family can build together (a planned family project, with its effect on society traced), and then volunteering in the neighbourhood, named in the last lesson without metaphor: "my volunteering is life".
