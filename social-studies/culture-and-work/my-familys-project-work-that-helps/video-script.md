# Video script — My Familys Project Work That Helps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 4 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 45–60, teaches: Work as family and community life — the family pools what each member does well into a planned project, shares in investing it in a piece of work, and traces the project's effect in the neighbourhood, the society and the world.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 4, Term 1): Grade 4 turns skill into service in three widening steps — what I am good at, what my family can build together (a planned family project, with its effect on society traced), and then volunteering in the neighbourhood, named in the last lesson without metaphor: "my volunteering is life".
