# Recycling and Turkeys Zero Waste Project — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) recycling turns some waste back into usable material; paper, glass, plastic, metal and battery waste are sorted into their own coloured boxes (paper into blue, glass into green) under Turkey's Zero Waste Project, so shared places and nature are not fouled  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) recycling turns some waste back into usable material; paper, glass, plastic, metal and battery waste are sorted into their own coloured boxes (paper into blue, glass into green) under Turkey's Zero Waste Project, so shared places and nature are not fouled**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
