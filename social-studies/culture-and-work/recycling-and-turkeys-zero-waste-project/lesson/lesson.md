## Sub lesson 2 — Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: the second Grade 1 book turns from school and family to the homeland: the flag and the anthem are taught as the symbols of independence a six-year-old stands for at the school ceremony, the five national days are learned as one family of celebrations, and the religious bayrams are taught through visiting elders and helping each other — the state and the faith calendar side by side in one learning area.
