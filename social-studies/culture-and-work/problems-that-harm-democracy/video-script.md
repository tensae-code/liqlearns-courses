# Video script — Problems That Harm Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 7 Sosyal Bilgiler 2. Kitap, printed p. 56, teaches: democracy is harmed by undemocratic behaviour and by rights being violated or blocked (the rights themselves rehearsed as riddles — life, health, privacy, education, travel, voting); what protects fundamental rights, what a person should do when they are blocked, and the responsibilities each of us carries so rights can be used without trouble

Turkey's own way: the course ends where citizenship becomes constitutional: thirteen-year-olds read Articles 1–3 of their own constitution (the ones that cannot even be proposed for change), separate the state's three powers, walk democracy's road from the Tanzimat edicts to the first multi-party election of 1946, and finish by naming the behaviours that wound democracy in daily life.
