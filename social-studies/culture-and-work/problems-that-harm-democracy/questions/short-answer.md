# Problems That Harm Democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Problems met in the process of applying democracy — democracy is harmed by undemocratic behaviour and by rights being violated or blocked (the rights themselves rehearsed as riddles — life, health, privacy, education, travel, voting); what protects fundamental rights, what a person should do when they are blocked, and the responsibilities each of us carries so rights can be used without trouble (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
