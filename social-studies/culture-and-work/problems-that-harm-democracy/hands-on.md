# Hands-on — Problems That Harm Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: democracy is harmed by undemocratic behaviour and by rights being violated or blocked (the rights themselves rehearsed as riddles — life, health, privacy, education, travel, voting); what protects fundamental rights, what a person should do when they are blocked, and the responsibilities each of us carries so rights can be used without trouble Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Turkey's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
