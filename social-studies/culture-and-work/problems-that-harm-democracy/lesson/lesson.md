# Problems That Harm Democracy

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: democracy is harmed by undemocratic behaviour and by rights being violated or blocked (the rights themselves rehearsed as riddles — life, health, privacy, education, travel, voting); what protects fundamental rights, what a person should do when they are blocked, and the responsibilities each of us carries so rights can be used without trouble — as Turkey's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: the course ends where citizenship becomes constitutional: thirteen-year-olds read Articles 1–3 of their own constitution (the ones that cannot even be proposed for change), separate the state's three powers, walk democracy's road from the Tanzimat edicts to the first multi-party election of 1946, and finish by naming the behaviours that wound democracy in daily life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
