# Hands-on — Power Privilege and Economic Resources

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Privilege as advantages some hold (for example in accessibility for people without disabilities), and how economic resources — wealth, access to capital and social connections — create opportunities for obtaining privileges, while economic differences between countries and people create inequality. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
