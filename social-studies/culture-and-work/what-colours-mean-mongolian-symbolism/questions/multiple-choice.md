# What Colours Mean Mongolian Symbolism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 11 Өнгөний бэлгэдэл (The symbolism of colours)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) The five basic colours and what each symbolises in Mongolian custom — blue the eternal sky and peace, white purity and good fortune, red the hearth fire's rising flame, green growth and flourishing, yellow learning and faith  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
