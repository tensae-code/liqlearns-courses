# Hands-on — What Colours Mean Mongolian Symbolism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 11 Өнгөний бэлгэдэл (The symbolism of colours)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The five basic colours and what each symbolises in Mongolian custom — blue the eternal sky and peace, white purity and good fortune, red the hearth fire's rising flame, green growth and flourishing, yellow learning and faith. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
