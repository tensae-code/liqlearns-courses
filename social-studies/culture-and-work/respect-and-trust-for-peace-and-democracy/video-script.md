# Video script — Respect and Trust for Peace and Democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 6 P6 Social Studies SB, printed p. 50, teaches: where the village elder (Wari) is fair and just in judgment he earns respect and the village lives in peaceful co-existence; respect and trust promote democracy and peace because everybody's rights are observed, there are equal opportunities for all, resources are distributed with equity, and gender equality is observed.

South Sudan's own way: The country's own conflict is the textbook — secession is explained by marginalisation and exploitation of the South's resources, and the constitution enters as homework: explain why South Sudan's constitution is important and name five rights it guarantees.
