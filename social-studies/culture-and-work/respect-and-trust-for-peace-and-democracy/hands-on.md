# Hands-on — Respect and Trust for Peace and Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 2 Respect and trust in promoting peace and democracy
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: where the village elder (Wari) is fair and just in judgment he earns respect and the village lives in peaceful co-existence; respect and trust promote democracy and peace because everybody's rights are observed, there are equal opportunities for all, resources are distributed with equity, and gender equality is observed. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
