## Sub lesson 1 — Advertising Technology and Society

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 16–19, teaches: Each technology remakes advertising and society together — the printing press spreads literacy and newspapers, cheap radios after 1930 put a set in nearly every home, television becomes the biggest advertising medium, and the internet opens a new one; children are exposed to advertising messages from very small. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Argentina's way — the book's own teaching way

Argentina's own way: the ministry teaches media literacy as social studies — a free pupil fascicle that treats the adverts surrounding children as a social phenomenon to be read critically, from the town crier of colonial Buenos Aires to the television spot.
