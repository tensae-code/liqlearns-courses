# Hands-on — Advertising Technology and Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Each technology remakes advertising and society together — the printing press spreads literacy and newspapers, cheap radios after 1930 put a set in nearly every home, television becomes the biggest advertising medium, and the internet opens a new one; children are exposed to advertising messages from very small. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
