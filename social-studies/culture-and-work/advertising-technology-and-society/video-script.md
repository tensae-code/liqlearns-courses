# Video script — Advertising Technology and Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Argentina Primary A gusto del consumidor (Piedra Libre), printed pp. 16–19, teaches: Each technology remakes advertising and society together — the printing press spreads literacy and newspapers, cheap radios after 1930 put a set in nearly every home, television becomes the biggest advertising medium, and the internet opens a new one; children are exposed to advertising messages from very small.

Argentina's own way: the ministry teaches media literacy as social studies — a free pupil fascicle that treats the adverts surrounding children as a social phenomenon to be read critically, from the town crier of colonial Buenos Aires to the television spot.
