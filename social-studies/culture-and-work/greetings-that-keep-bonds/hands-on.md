# Hands-on — Greetings That Keep Bonds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The value of social connection on religious occasions — the Eid greeting phrases Kuwaitis exchange ("Eiduk Mubarak", "may you be among those who return to it and the winners") spoken to plant affection and keep the bonds of love between neighbours. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
