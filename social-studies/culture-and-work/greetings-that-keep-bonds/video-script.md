# Video script — Greetings That Keep Bonds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 7 Term 1 (printed pp. 99–100), teaches: The value of social connection on religious occasions — the Eid greeting phrases Kuwaitis exchange ("Eiduk Mubarak", "may you be among those who return to it and the winners") spoken to plant affection and keep the bonds of love between neighbours.

Kuwait's own way: the values unit widens from Grade 6's neighbourhood to the nation's exemplars — a ruler nicknamed for the rice he spread for passers-by, a Bedouin weaver whose craft UNESCO now lists, a famous poet who bowed to his son's teacher, and the Eid greeting phrases that plant affection between neighbours.
