# The Enemy of Somalia

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the country has enemies, inside and outside, and the citizen-child learns to know and resist them — the state's own account of loyalty and vigilance — as Somalia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cadowga Soomaaliya (The Enemy of Somalia)

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 47), teaches: the country has enemies, inside and outside, and the citizen-child learns to know and resist them — the state's own account of loyalty and vigilance. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
