# Hands-on — The Enemy of Somalia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cadowga Soomaaliya (The Enemy of Somalia)
Work through this part with your hands: the country has enemies, inside and outside, and the citizen-child learns to know and resist them — the state's own account of loyalty and vigilance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
