# Hands-on — The Four Spheres of Social Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The life of society as four interconnected spheres, drawn in the book's own scheme — the productive/economic sphere (production of material goods; productive forces and production relations; property relations), the social sphere (groups by sex and age, ethnic groups — clan, tribe, nationality, nation — with national self-consciousness, and classes picked out by property, profession, place of residence and income), the political sphere (the struggle for state power and its use — the state, political parties, public organisations) and the spiritual sphere (science, art, religion, philosophy, morality); and, framing them, the idea of the socio-economic formation (Spencer's organism comparison, Marx's formation theory). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
