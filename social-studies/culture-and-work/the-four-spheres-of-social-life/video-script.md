# Video script — The Four Spheres of Social Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 9 Person. Society. State, Book 2 (printed p. 114), teaches: The life of society as four interconnected spheres, drawn in the book's own scheme — the productive/economic sphere (production of material goods; productive forces and production relations; property relations), the social sphere (groups by sex and age, ethnic groups — clan, tribe, nationality, nation — with national self-consciousness, and classes picked out by property, profession, place of residence and income), the political sphere (the struggle for state power and its use — the state, political parties, public organisations) and the spiritual sphere (science, art, religion, philosophy, morality); and, framing them, the idea of the socio-economic formation (Spencer's organism comparison, Marx's formation theory).
Belarus' own way: the civics track is the pupil's own state and law read from inside — "our state" is taught straight from the 1994 Constitution of the Republic of Belarus (unitary, democratic, social, law-governed; the people as the only source of power; the 1996 referendum; the 1999 Union treaty with Russia), rights are taught article by article from that Constitution (Arts. 1–3, 21–25, 30, 41–49, 53, 59–62) beside the Universal Declaration, and the course closes not with a test but with a discussion, "I — a citizen of the Republic of Belarus", on the citizen's own responsibility before the state..
