# Pride in My Family and Clan Traditions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Be proud of your family and clan traditions — the good traditions families and clans hand down — love of country, love of learning (the Đặng clan of Sơn La), diligent work, a family craft kept alive (đàn bầu playing, Mid-Autumn toy making), and house customs like "respect those above, yield to those below" and "when drinking water, remember the source"; pride in them gives strength, and each person keeps them alive by fitting deeds (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship is taught straight from the statute book — the Nationality Law decides who is a citizen case by case, the 2013 Constitution's Chapter II is read group by group (political, civil, economic, cultural-social rights, then duties), and children's rights arrive through the UN Convention and the 2016 Children Law in four groups: survival, protection, development, participation. (any close wording).**
