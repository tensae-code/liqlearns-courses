# Hands-on — Pride in My Family and Clan Traditions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the good traditions families and clans hand down — love of country, love of learning (the Đặng clan of Sơn La), diligent work, a family craft kept alive (đàn bầu playing, Mid-Autumn toy making), and house customs like "respect those above, yield to those below" and "when drinking water, remember the source"; pride in them gives strength, and each person keeps them alive by fitting deeds. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
