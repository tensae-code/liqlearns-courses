# Video script — Pride in My Family and Clan Traditions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 6 Giáo dục công dân 6, printed pp. 5–8, teaches: The good traditions families and clans hand down — love of country, love of learning (the Đặng clan of Sơn La), diligent work, a family craft kept alive (đàn bầu playing, Mid-Autumn toy making), and house customs like "respect those above, yield to those below" and "when drinking water, remember the source"; pride in them gives strength, and each person keeps them alive by fitting deeds.

Vietnam's own way: citizenship is taught straight from the statute book — the Nationality Law decides who is a citizen case by case, the 2013 Constitution's Chapter II is read group by group (political, civil, economic, cultural-social rights, then duties), and children's rights arrive through the UN Convention and the 2016 Children Law in four groups: survival, protection, development, participation.
