# Hands-on — Government Ethics and Public Servants

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unidad 4 Lección 3 La ética gubernamental
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ethics as the study of moral conduct in society; a public servant serves in the state, municipalities and autonomous bodies, elected or not; the principles of public service (public interest first, probity, impartiality, transparency, accountability and more); and El Salvador's 2006 Ley de Ética Gubernamental against corruption. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
