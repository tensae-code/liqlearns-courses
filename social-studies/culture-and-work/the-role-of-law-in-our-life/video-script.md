Morocco Grade 7 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 1AC, printed p. 186, teaches: why law organises daily life and what it protects.

Morocco's own way: collège Social Studies carries a full Citizenship Education component built on ten value lessons in two axes — five personal-social values (dignity, freedom, equality, justice, solidarity), then tolerance, democracy, peace, the role of law, and a field visit to a human-rights institution — each lesson built from constitutional and international-charter texts.
