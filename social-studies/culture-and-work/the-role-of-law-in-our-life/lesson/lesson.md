Morocco Grade 7 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 1AC, printed p. 186, teaches: why law organises daily life and what it protects. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Morocco's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Morocco's own way: collège Social Studies carries a full Citizenship Education component built on ten value lessons in two axes — five personal-social values (dignity, freedom, equality, justice, solidarity), then tolerance, democracy, peace, the role of law, and a field visit to a human-rights institution — each lesson built from constitutional and international-charter texts. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
