# Video script — Financial Markets — Shares and Bonds

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 10 book gives financial markets their own topic in the Economics chapter. A financial market is where savings meet needs: money is gathered from many people and directed to the enterprises and states that will use it, through instruments such as shares and bonds. The topic teaches what these markets are and what work they do in the economy.

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: the senior cycle drops the “Individual and…” frame for straight disciplines — Society, Culture, Law, Economics, Politics, International Relations — with Mongolia's own culture, macroeconomy and foreign relations as the worked examples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
