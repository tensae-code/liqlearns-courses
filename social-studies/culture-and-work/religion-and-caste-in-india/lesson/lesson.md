# Religion and Caste in India

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Most people in India follow one of two great religions, and for a long time Indian society was also divided into castes — this lesson holds both. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Hinduism and Islam in India

Chapter 2 of Iceland's India guide, Trúarbrögð og stéttaskipting — religions and the division into castes — teaches India's two largest religions with their shares of the people: Hinduism, followed by 80%, and Islam, followed by 13%. Together they are the faiths of most of India.

## Sub lesson 2 — The caste system

The same chapter teaches the caste system: the old division of society into ranked castes. That division was formally abolished in 1947 — in law, it ended. But the guide also teaches that it weakens rather than vanishes at once: it is weakening among the young and in the cities.
