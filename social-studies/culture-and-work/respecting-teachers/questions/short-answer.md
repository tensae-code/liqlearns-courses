# Respecting Teachers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Respecting the teacher — respecting the teacher as a value inherited generation after generation in Kuwaiti society — the teacher as symbol of knowledge and model of conduct, and the humility of the great before knowledge: the poet Yaqoub al-Subaie, asked by his son about the meaning of one of his own verses set as homework, and the father's answer "the teacher knows best" (المدرس أبخص) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way — the values unit widens from Grade 6's neighbourhood to the nation's exemplars — a ruler nicknamed for the rice he spread for passers-by, a Bedouin weaver whose craft UNESCO now lists, a famous poet who bowed to his son's teacher, and the Eid greeting phrases that plant affection between neighbours (any close wording).**
