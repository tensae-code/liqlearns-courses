# Video script — Respecting Teachers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 7 Term 1 (printed pp. 97–98), teaches: Respecting the teacher as a value inherited generation after generation in Kuwaiti society — the teacher as symbol of knowledge and model of conduct, and the humility of the great before knowledge: the poet Yaqoub al-Subaie, asked by his son about the meaning of one of his own verses set as homework, and the father's answer "the teacher knows best" (المدرس أبخص).

Kuwait's own way: the values unit widens from Grade 6's neighbourhood to the nation's exemplars — a ruler nicknamed for the rice he spread for passers-by, a Bedouin weaver whose craft UNESCO now lists, a famous poet who bowed to his son's teacher, and the Eid greeting phrases that plant affection between neighbours.
