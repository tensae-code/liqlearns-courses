# Hands-on — Respecting Teachers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Respecting the teacher as a value inherited generation after generation in Kuwaiti society — the teacher as symbol of knowledge and model of conduct, and the humility of the great before knowledge: the poet Yaqoub al-Subaie, asked by his son about the meaning of one of his own verses set as homework, and the father's answer "the teacher knows best" (المدرس أبخص). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
