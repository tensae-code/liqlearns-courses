# Video script — Social Stratification

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bangladesh Grade 9 BD G9-10 HSS (printed pp. 116–118), teaches: The four kinds of social stratification — slavery (people as property), estate (medieval Europe's clergy, nobility and serfs), caste (birth-fixed rank and occupation in India) and class (modern, open, moved by achievement as well as birth).

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 9–10): Civics becomes a full field manual — pupils questionnaire a real Union Parishad chairman and their own headteacher, then learn the whole local-government ladder tier by tier with membership counts, and meet the United Nations through Bangladesh's own seat in it (Bangabandhu's 1974 Bengali speech) before the Charter is summarised..
