# Hands-on — Social Stratification

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The four kinds of social stratification — slavery (people as property), estate (medieval Europe's clergy, nobility and serfs), caste (birth-fixed rank and occupation in India) and class (modern, open, moved by achievement as well as birth). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
