**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Belarus).**

Belarus's own way (Grade 11): The whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.

Belarus's own way (Grade 10): The Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system.
