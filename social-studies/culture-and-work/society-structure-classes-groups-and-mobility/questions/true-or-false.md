# Society Structure Classes Groups and Mobility — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Sociology as the science that "paints" a society's portrait; the social composition of a country — groups by place of residence (about 74% urban), ethnic groups (130 nationalities: Belarusians about 81%, Russians 11%, Poles 4%, Ukrainians 2%), 25 registered confessions, and groups by property, profession, income and education, with the structure shifting as in other developed industrial states.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: Society as self-organised groups with common interests and values; the horizontal structure (demographic, ethnic, confessional, professional and kin groups — one person pictured as woman, Belarusian, agro-town resident at once) and the vertical structure, where groups differ by property and income, power, education and prestige — classes as the large groups of the class approach (Marx), with stratification by income, power, education and prestige.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Belarus' student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
