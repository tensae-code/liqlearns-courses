# Video script — Society Structure Classes Groups and Mobility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 117), teaches: Sociology as the science that "paints" a society's portrait; the social composition of a country — groups by place of residence (about 74% urban), ethnic groups (130 nationalities: Belarusians about 81%, Russians 11%, Poles 4%, Ukrainians 2%), 25 registered confessions, and groups by property, profession, income and education, with the structure shifting as in other developed industrial states.
Belarus Grade 10 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 15), teaches: Society as self-organised groups with common interests and values; the horizontal structure (demographic, ethnic, confessional, professional and kin groups — one person pictured as woman, Belarusian, agro-town resident at once) and the vertical structure, where groups differ by property and income, power, education and prestige — classes as the large groups of the class approach (Marx), with stratification by income, power, education and prestige.
Belarus' own way: (Grade 11) the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics. (Grade 10) the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system..
