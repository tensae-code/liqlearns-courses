# Hands-on — Society Structure Classes Groups and Mobility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sociology as the science that "paints" a society's portrait; the social composition of a country — groups by place of residence (about 74% urban), ethnic groups (130 nationalities: Belarusians about 81%, Russians 11%, Poles 4%, Ukrainians 2%), 25 registered confessions, and groups by property, profession, income and education, with the structure shifting as in other developed industrial states. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Society as self-organised groups with common interests and values; the horizontal structure (demographic, ethnic, confessional, professional and kin groups — one person pictured as woman, Belarusian, agro-town resident at once) and the vertical structure, where groups differ by property and income, power, education and prestige — classes as the large groups of the class approach (Marx), with stratification by income, power, education and prestige. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
