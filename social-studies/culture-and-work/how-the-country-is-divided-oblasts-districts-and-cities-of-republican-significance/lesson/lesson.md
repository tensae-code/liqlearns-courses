## Sub lesson 1 — How the Country is Divided Oblasts Districts and Cities of Republican Significance

Kazakhstan Grade 4 Poznanie mira (110/88) (printed p. 8), teaches: Kazakhstan as an independent state in the centre of Eurasia with over 18 million people and its capital at Nur-Sultan; an administrative-territorial unit, and the country divided into 17 of them — 14 oblasts and 3 cities of republican significance (Nur-Sultan, Almaty, and Shymkent, which passed a million people in 2018, when the oblast centre moved to Turkestan and Turkestan oblast appeared) — each oblast in turn divided into smaller units, districts (районы). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the books' own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 5): Grade 4 is where the primary course turns civic in earnest — the republic's own map of 14 oblasts and three cities of republican significance, an elected school council drawn as a working model of the state, the Constitution's rights quoted article by article, and the country's face to the world built from the UN seat, the closed Semipalatinsk test site and EXPO-2017. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
