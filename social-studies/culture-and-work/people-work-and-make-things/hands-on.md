# Hands-on — People Work and Make Things

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Човек ради и ствара (People work and create)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: by work people make the goods that meet their needs, from one material or several — followed from tree to chair — and people collect waste, separate materials and remake them into new products. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
