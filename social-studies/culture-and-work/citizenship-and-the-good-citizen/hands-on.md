# Hands-on — Citizenship and the Good Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A citizen belongs to a country and holds rights, including voting when grown; the values of good citizenship — compassion, respect, honesty and fairness, courage, responsibility — and standing against unfair practices like bullying. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Maldives's way
Say how Maldives's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
