# Hands-on — Discipline and Following Rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dishibliinka iyo Usluubta (Discipline and Manners)
Work through this part with your hands: discipline (nidaam) as the order every part of life runs by — at school: keep time, arrive before the lesson, do not be late for the line, let the teacher enter first, do not talk in class, raise a finger to ask, do not break or dirty the school, guard its cleanliness, write nothing on walls or desks, take nothing of another's, insult no one, respect pupils, teachers and staff as parents and siblings; outside school: the road has its own order — do not stand where cars pass, walk on your right, look both ways before crossing, red light means the road is closed and green means open; and manners (anshax) as the person's good character. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
