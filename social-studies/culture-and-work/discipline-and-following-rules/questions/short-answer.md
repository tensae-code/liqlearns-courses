# Discipline and Following Rules — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Dishibliinka iyo Usluubta (Discipline and Manners) — discipline (nidaam) as the order every part of life runs by — at school: keep time, arrive before the lesson, do not be late for the line, let the teacher enter first, do not talk in class, raise a finger to ask, do not break or dirty the school, guard its cleanliness, write nothing on walls or desks, take nothing of another's, insult no one, respect pupils, teachers and staff as parents and siblings; outside school: the road has its own order — do not stand where cars pass, walk on your right, look both ways before crossing, red light means the road is closed and green means open; and manners (anshax) as the person's good character (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way — this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in. (any close wording).**
