Somalia's own way (teacher guide — evidence): this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in.
