# Hands-on — National Security What It is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: national security as the plans, procedures and actions the state takes to achieve political, economic and social stability for society's members, institutions and lands, and to defend its vital interests against external aggression or internal threat; security as a basic human need and one of God's greatest blessings, which the Basic Law (Article 36) obliges the state to provide to all citizens and residents; security's fruits against its loss — peace, trustworthiness and development against fear, extremism, treachery, division and crime. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
