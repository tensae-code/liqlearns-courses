## Sub lesson 1 — National Security What It is

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 204–208), teaches: National security as the plans, procedures and actions the state takes to achieve political, economic and social stability for society's members, institutions and lands, and to defend its vital interests against external aggression or internal threat; security as a basic human need and one of God's greatest blessings, which the Basic Law (Article 36) obliges the state to provide to all citizens and residents; security's fruits against its loss — peace, trustworthiness and development against fear, extremism, treachery, division and crime. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
