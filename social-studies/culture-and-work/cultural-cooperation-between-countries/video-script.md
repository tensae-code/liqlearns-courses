Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2, printed p. 67, teaches: cultural relations between civilisations and states run from the oldest ages to today — international festivals showing dress, dabka dances, folk foods and heritage crafts, annual book exhibitions, and the Olympic Games every four years, which states use to present their civilisation and culture; international organisations guard culture, among them UNESCO and the Arab ALECSO.

Palestine's own way: civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom.
