# Hands-on — Cultural Cooperation Between Countries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cultural Cooperation Between Countries
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Cultural relations between civilisations and states run from the oldest ages to today — international festivals showing dress, dabka dances, folk foods and heritage crafts, annual book exhibitions, and the Olympic Games every four years, which states use to present their civilisation and culture; international organisations guard culture, among them UNESCO and the Arab ALECSO. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
