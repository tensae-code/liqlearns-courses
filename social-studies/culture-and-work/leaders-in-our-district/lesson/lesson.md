# Leaders in Our District

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the titled leaders of a Ugandan district from village/zone up to the LCV Chairperson — as Uganda's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Titles of Leaders in Our District

Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study (printed p. 21), teaches: the titled leaders of a Ugandan district from village/zone up to the LCV Chairperson. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Roles of Leaders

Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study (printed p. 24), teaches: what each district leader does — DPC security, CAO heads civil servants, RDC represents the President, DEO education, DAO agriculture — and that leaders must serve all people equally. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Uganda's way — the book's own teaching way (Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study)

Uganda's own way: the district is the child's civic unit — its leaders (LCV chairperson, CAO, RDC, DPC, DEO, DAO) are named by title and role, and children's rights are taught straight from Uganda's laws (a child is anyone below 18). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
