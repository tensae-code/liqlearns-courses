# Leaders in Our District — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part 1 teaches: The titled leaders of a ugandan district from village/zone up to the lcv chairperson
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part 2 teaches: What each district leader does — dpc security, cao heads civil servants, rdc represents the president, deo education, dao agriculture — and that leaders must serve all people equally
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part 3 teaches: Uganda's own way: the district is the child's civic unit — its leaders (LCV chairperson, CAO, RDC, DPC, DEO, DAO) are named by title and role, and children's rights are taught straight from Uganda's laws (a child is anyone below 18)
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Uganda's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
