**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Social Values and Relations — the social values are a set of innate (acquired) principles that determine the individual's dealing with others, based on the principles of good morals, respect, humanity, tolerance, truthfulness and social solidarity, regardless of religion, sect, race, sex or colour; and social relations are one form of the social values — the social bonds existing between the individuals or the general public of society — and they divide into two kinds: formal social relations, such as the pupil's relation with the school administration or the employee's relation with the department he works in; and informal social relations, such as man's relation with the members of his family, his neighbours or his relatives; and social relations can be positive, dominated by friendliness and love, leading to solidarity and cooperation, or negative, dominated by hatred and envy, leading to quarrels and enmity between people (any close wording).**
