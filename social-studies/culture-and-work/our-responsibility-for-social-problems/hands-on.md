# Hands-on — Our Responsibility for Social Problems

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Morocco in solving social problems, and our responsibility (المغرب في حل المشاكل الاجتماعية ومسؤوليتنا نحن)
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 9 Tajdid Ijtiamiat 3AC, printed p. 167, teaches: how the state and solidarity institutions such as the Mohammed VI Foundation answer social problems, and the citizen's own share of responsibility. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
