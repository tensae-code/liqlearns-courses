United Arab Emirates' own way (Social Studies & National Education, G6 Term 1): citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries. That way is stocked here as the way United Arab Emirates teaches this thing.
