# Video script — Pillars and Values of Positive Citizenship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G6 Term 1, printed p. 64, teaches: the pillars (love of the homeland and belonging to it, loyalty to the wise leadership, belief in national unity, deepening national culture, cooperating with the state's agencies, refining behaviour and morals) and the values — equality of all citizens in rights and duties without distinction of religion, sex, colour, race or social level; bearing responsibility for the homeland's gains, security and stability; and positive participation in public life — with the state's own volunteering institutions named (the National Volunteering Platform, Takatof, Sanid, the Emirates Red Crescent, Dubai Cares)

United Arab Emirates' own way: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries.
