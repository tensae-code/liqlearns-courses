# Pillars and Values of Positive Citizenship — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Pillars and Values of positive citizenship — the pillars (love of the homeland and belonging to it, loyalty to the wise leadership, belief in national unity, deepening national culture, cooperating with the state's agencies, refining behaviour and morals) and the values — equality of all citizens in rights and duties without distinction of religion, sex, colour, race or social level; bearing responsibility for the homeland's gains, security and stability; and positive participation in public life — with the state's own volunteering institutions named (the National Volunteering Platform, Takatof, Sanid, the Emirates Red Crescent, Dubai Cares) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries. (any close wording).**
