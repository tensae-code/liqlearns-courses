# Hands-on — Pillars and Values of Positive Citizenship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pillars and Values of positive citizenship
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the pillars (love of the homeland and belonging to it, loyalty to the wise leadership, belief in national unity, deepening national culture, cooperating with the state's agencies, refining behaviour and morals) and the values — equality of all citizens in rights and duties without distinction of religion, sex, colour, race or social level; bearing responsibility for the homeland's gains, security and stability; and positive participation in public life — with the state's own volunteering institutions named (the National Volunteering Platform, Takatof, Sanid, the Emirates Red Crescent, Dubai Cares) Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
