# Hands-on — Community Leaders Who Help Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What a leader is and the leaders all around us — teachers, parents, coaches, island and city councilors, magistrates, health and social-protection officers — and what each is responsible for. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Maldives's way
Say how Maldives's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
