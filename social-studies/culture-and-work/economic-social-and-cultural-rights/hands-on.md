# Hands-on — Economic Social and Cultural Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Economic, social and cultural rights (الحقوق الاقتصادية والاجتماعية والثقافية)
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 8 Fadaa Ijtiamiat 2AC, printed p. 193, teaches: rights to work, health, education and culture in the international covenants and Moroccan law. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the second collège year turns citizenship into the machinery of the state itself — the constitution, the King, parliament, government and courts, then the citizen's constitutional rights, parties, unions, press, local democracy and associations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
