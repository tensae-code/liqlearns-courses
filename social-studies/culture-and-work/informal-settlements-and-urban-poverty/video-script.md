# Video script — Informal Settlements And Urban Poverty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 21), teaches: Lesson 2 Managing city and village (مدیریت شهر و روستا): informal or illegal settlement (اسکان غیررسمی، حاشیه‌نشینی) forming around the great cities as a result of the housing problem — activity: research an example in Iran or the world (its geographical position, the kind of migrants, their living conditions); urban security and social harms — low income, unemployment, bad housing and marginal settlement leading to urban poverty, strongly linked to crime.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
