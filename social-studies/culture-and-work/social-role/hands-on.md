# Hands-on — Social Role

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Position and role through football (goalkeeper/centre-forward) — status is acquired (judge, player) or inherited (by birth); a role is the rights, responsibilities and behaviour society expects from a position, and status is weighed by reputation, power, wealth, age and gender. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
