India Grade 9 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Democratic Politics-I (printed p. 17), teaches: why a democracy needs a constitution, how South Africa's enemies wrote one together, and how India's Constituent Assembly (1946–49) wrote the values of the Preamble into the supreme law.

India's own way: Democracy is built as an argument, not a list — a Meghalaya classroom debates the definition itself, Pakistan under Musharraf and Mexico's one-party years serve as the "pretend democracy" tests, and South Africa's constitution-making is the mirror for India's own.
