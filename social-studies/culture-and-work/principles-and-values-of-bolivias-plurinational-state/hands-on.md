# Hands-on — Principles and Values of Bolivias Plurinational State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bolivia's own state as its constitution defines it — what a State is, the CPE (Carta Magna) as its charter, Article 1's "Estado Unitario Social de Derecho Plurinacional Comunitario", the founding pillars (liberty, independence, democracy, sovereignty, interculturality, autonomy, plurality), and the State's principles and values: unity, equality, dignity, respect, complementarity, harmony, transparency, gender and social equity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
