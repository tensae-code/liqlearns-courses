# Principles and Values of Bolivias Plurinational State — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bases fundamentales del Estado — Bolivia's own state as its constitution defines it — what a State is, the CPE (Carta Magna) as its charter, Article 1's "Estado Unitario Social de Derecho Plurinacional Comunitario", the founding pillars (liberty, independence, democracy, sovereignty, interculturality, autonomy, plurality), and the State's principles and values: unity, equality, dignity, respect, complementarity, harmony, transparency, gender and social equity (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way — the constitution itself is taught as the home of the ancestors' moral law — the Plurinational State's founding values are the Quechua Ama Qhilla, Ama Llulla, Ama Suwa and the other peoples' suma qamaña, ñandereco, teko kavi, ivi maraei and qhapaj ñan, written into CPE Article 8 (any close wording).**
