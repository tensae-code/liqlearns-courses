# Hands-on — Kiribati Youth Roles and Worthwhile Citizenship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 3 Kiribati Youth and the Community — teaches: who "youth" are (community, law and statistical definitions), their roles and responsibilities to family, community and nation, and what worthwhile citizenship and the behaviour that sustains society mean, against the Kiribati Development Plan. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kiribati's way
Say how Kiribati's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
