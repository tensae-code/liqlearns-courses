# Video script — Media Ethics Telling the Truth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Afghanistan Grade 6 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 41, teaches: media (press, radio, TV, internet) carry messages and shape minds; the ethics they must keep are accuracy, balance and trustworthiness, avoiding insult, violence and content against Islamic values, the constitution and national interests, and promoting unity and good morals.

Afghanistan's own way: the Afghan state is taught as the child's own house — society needs a state as a family needs order; the 2004 constitution is the "mother law"; the two houses are named in Pashto (Wolesi Jirga, Meshrano Jirga) and every right (children's, women's, human dignity) is grounded first in Islam and then in the constitution's own articles.
