Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Media Ethics Telling the Truth

Afghanistan Grade 6 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 41, teaches: media (press, radio, TV, internet) carry messages and shape minds; the ethics they must keep are accuracy, balance and trustworthiness, avoiding insult, violence and content against Islamic values, the constitution and national interests, and promoting unity and good morals. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: the Afghan state is taught as the child's own house — society needs a state as a family needs order; the 2004 constitution is the "mother law"; the two houses are named in Pashto (Wolesi Jirga, Meshrano Jirga) and every right (children's, women's, human dignity) is grounded first in Islam and then in the constitution's own articles. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
