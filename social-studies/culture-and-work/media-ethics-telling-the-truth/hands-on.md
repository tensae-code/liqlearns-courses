# Hands-on — Media Ethics Telling the Truth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Media (press, radio, TV, internet) carry messages and shape minds; the ethics they must keep are accuracy, balance and trustworthiness, avoiding insult, violence and content against Islamic values, the constitution and national interests, and promoting unity and good morals. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
