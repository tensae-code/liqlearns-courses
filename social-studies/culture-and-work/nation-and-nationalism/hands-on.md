## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 2 Nation, Nationalism and "Proud to be a Pakistani", Ch. 2 Nationalism — "The way we live together", Ch. 1 Ideological Basis of Pakistan: Definition, Sources and Significance of Ideology, Ch. 1 Ideological Basis of Pakistan: Definition, Sources and Importance of Ideology; Meanings of Ideology of Pakistan, Ch. 1 Islam as the Ideological Basis of Pakistan: Meaning of Ideology and the Two-Nation Theory, Ch. 1 Ideology of Pakistan; Quaid-e-Azam and Ideology of Pakistan; Allama Iqbal and Ideology of Pakistan, Ch. 2 Nation and Nationality, Ch. 1 Meaning of Ideology; Sources of Ideology; Need and Significance of Ideology, Ch. 1 اسلامی جمہوریہ پاکستان کا قیام: نظریہ پاکستان (The Ideology of Pakistan) — teaches: a nation is a people joined by shared language, religion and culture in one land; nationalism is love and loyalty to that nation — Pakistan is an Islamic democratic state whose people share one national language, one faith for most, and one homeland to be proud of; a nation (from Latin "natio", birth) is a people bound by shared feeling; nationalism is standing together as one Pakistani nation above region, language and sect; what an ideology is (a set of beliefs that binds a nation and directs its life), why it matters, and what the Ideology of Pakistan means — a separate Muslim homeland built on Islamic principles, per the chapter's own definitions; ideology defined four ways, its five sources (common religion, political, educational, economic and cultural objectives), why it keeps a nation alive and united, and what Pakistan's ideology means — the soul in the body of the nation; ideology as a nation's shared set of beliefs and aims; Pakistan's ideology sourced in the Qur'an and Sunnah; the Two-Nation Theory stated plainly — two nations, two ways of life, one separate homeland; Pakistan was demanded as the practical shape of an idea — Jinnah's words are quoted ("We are only Muslims", Dhaka, 21 March 1948) and Iqbal's Allahabad address of 1930 is given as the idea's first clear statement; the chapter's outcomes close with defining the term nation and nationality and explaining the reasons for pride in being Pakistani — patriots named as the lesson's fruit; ideology as a nation's collective wisdom aimed at a predetermined objective; its sources in religion, moral values, culture, civilisation and social customs; and its seven services — uniting the nation, guiding its constitution and laws, guarding its culture and values, giving courage in difficult conditions, shaping national identity and character, motivating a purposeful life, and guiding it toward knowledge and action; what the ideology is and why it matters (نظریہ کی اہمیت), how the Two-Nation Theory formed through the Pakistan Movement's own history, and the ideology stated in the words of Iqbal and Jinnah.
