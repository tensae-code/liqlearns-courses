# Caring for Water And Nature

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: water, air, soil, plants and all living things are the vessel and foundation of our life in this world, and we must cherish them as we cherish our own bodies; water is a priceless jewel (ус — чандмань эрдэнэ) — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 10 Уул усны үр сад (Children of the mountains and waters)

Mongolia Grade 1 Civic Education, printed p. 26, teaches: Water, air, soil, plants and all living things are the vessel and foundation of our life in this world, and we must cherish them as we cherish our own bodies; water is a priceless jewel (ус — чандмань эрдэнэ). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: civic education for six-year-olds is pure Mongolian custom — greeting elders the Mongolian way, the ger and the five snouts, colour symbolism, the biyelgee dance and the Tsagaan Sar greeting embrace — with traditional script verses running down every page beside the Cyrillic. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
