# Hands-on — Caring for Water And Nature

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 10 Уул усны үр сад (Children of the mountains and waters)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Water, air, soil, plants and all living things are the vessel and foundation of our life in this world, and we must cherish them as we cherish our own bodies; water is a priceless jewel (ус — чандмань эрдэнэ). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
