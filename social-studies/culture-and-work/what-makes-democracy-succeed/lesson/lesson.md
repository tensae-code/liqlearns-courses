# What Makes Democracy Succeed

Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 4 The features of a successful democracy

Sri Lanka Grade 11 Social Education and History 2000, printed pp. 101–103, teaches: Switzerland held up as the model, and the six conditions — a politically aware people who cannot be bought or swayed, dedicated and responsible leaders, a free and unbiased media, two strong parties, a majority that does not oppress the minority, and a country lifted above poverty with its people's basic needs met. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way

Sri Lanka's own way: the combined book's second half is a full civics course in disguise — democracy first as an idea with its thinkers and its six conditions, then Sri Lanka's own 1978 constitution opened article by article: the rights chapter with its Ombudsman and Human Rights Commission, the vote's road from McCallum to Donoughmore, the preferential presidential election, Parliament's 225 seats, the cabinet that falls with its bill, provinces and pradeshiya sabhas, and a court ladder that ends in village mediation boards. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
