# What Our Clothes Mean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 9 "The meaning and symbolism of Mongolian dress" (Монгол хувцасны утга, бэлгэдэл) — Dress has carried meaning since the khans' state dress rules; the hat is honoured as one's own head — two people meeting exchange hats as equals; a man's spirit (хийморь) is held to live in his deel's collar, so the collar is gripped when the deel is taken off; the sash is strength and freedom (sworn brothers — андууд — exchanged sashes); the silver belt set (хэт хутга, flint and knife) is a man's travelling fire and tool; and the boot's ornaments are counted — mostly eight to thirty-two (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other (any close wording).**
