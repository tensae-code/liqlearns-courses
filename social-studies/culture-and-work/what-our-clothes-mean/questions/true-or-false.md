# What Our Clothes Mean — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: dress has carried meaning since the khans' state dress rules; the hat is honoured as one's own head — two people meeting exchange hats as equals; a man's spirit (хийморь) is held to live in his deel's collar, so the collar is gripped when the deel is taken off; the sash is strength and freedom (sworn brothers — андууд — exchanged sashes); the silver belt set (хэт хутга, flint and knife) is a man's travelling fire and tool; and the boot's ornaments are counted — mostly eight to thirty-two.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
