# Hands-on — What Our Clothes Mean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 9 "The meaning and symbolism of Mongolian dress" (Монгол хувцасны утга, бэлгэдэл)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Dress has carried meaning since the khans' state dress rules; the hat is honoured as one's own head — two people meeting exchange hats as equals; a man's spirit (хийморь) is held to live in his deel's collar, so the collar is gripped when the deel is taken off; the sash is strength and freedom (sworn brothers — андууд — exchanged sashes); the silver belt set (хэт хутга, flint and knife) is a man's travelling fire and tool; and the boot's ornaments are counted — mostly eight to thirty-two. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
