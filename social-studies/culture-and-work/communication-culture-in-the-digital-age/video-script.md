# Video script — Communication Culture in the Digital Age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia's Grade 9 book gives digital communication its own named topic inside the culture chapter: communicating is a culture, with manners and care of its own, and the digital environment — messages, posts and online talk — is where that culture is now practised every day. The topic teaches the learner to treat online communication as real communication with real people, not as words without consequences.

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: Social Studies is a five-chapter spiral (Individual & Society, Individual & Culture, Individual & Economy, Human Rights & Law, Public Administration) continued from Grade 8; there is no physical-geography chapter — Mongolia's geography is a separate subject (Газарзүй) — so this book's Geography-wave content is its culture and economics teaching, grounded in Mongolian surveys, wage tables and heritage inventories. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
