Saudi Arabia Grade 6 Social Studies Term 1 (printed pp. 142–144), teaches: Education as the basis for developing citizens' abilities; the programme gives citizens the values, knowledge and skills that qualify them for the labour market — preparing pupils for future jobs and creativity, building teachers' and school leaders' capacity, expanding early-childhood education and attractive learning environments, on the values of determination and perseverance.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 6 book ties citizenship to Saudi Vision 2030 — what a vision is, its realisation programmes and projects, the Quality of Life Programme and the Human Capability Development Programme — and then to citizenship itself, honesty and trustworthiness, guarding national antiquities, and taxes and VAT.
