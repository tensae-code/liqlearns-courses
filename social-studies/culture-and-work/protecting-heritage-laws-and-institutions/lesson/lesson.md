# Protecting Heritage Laws and Institutions

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: protecting cultural heritage is a human responsibility at the international and personal levels, and among the ways aimed at it: issuing the legislation and laws that guarantee its protection, such as the laws protecting antiquities, manuscripts and historical documents; committing to the international conventions; documenting heritage in the local and world registers; awareness of cultural heritage's importance and preserving it by restoring and rehabilitating the archaeological buildings, archiving them, establishing museums, and media campaigns; the institutions working in heritage protection divide into international and foreign bodies — at their head UNESCO, which sets the programmes caring for cultural heritage's preservation worldwide (its Palestinian listings are placed by the History wave) — and Arab and Islamic bodies: the Arab League Educational, Cultural and Scientific Organization (ALECSO), founded by the Arab League in 1970, which cares for spreading heritage and the culture of preserving it inside the Arab homeland and chose Jerusalem as the Arab Culture Capital in 2009; and the Islamic Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (ISESCO), whose goals include preserving Islamic culture and identity and the landmarks of Islamic civilisation, its role showing in documentary films on Jerusalem, rehabilitating the Islamic Museum project, and material support to the Palestinian Centre for Culture and Arts; and for Jerusalem's heritage, exposed to forgery and theft, the book calls for coordination between the internal and external bodies concerned with the city's heritage, specialised conferences and workshops, deep scientific studies of the violations, a specialised heritage centre for training leaders and experts, putting heritage into the Palestinian school and university curricula, a financial fund to protect the city's heritage, and raising the violations in the international forums — as Palestine's book teaches it.
