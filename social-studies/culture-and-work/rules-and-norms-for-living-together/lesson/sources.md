**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Panama, Ecuador, Colombia).**

Panama's own way (Panama Grade 1 Ciencias Sociales workbook): Grade 1 social studies is a home workbook ("Conéctate en casa") worked through with family; rights and duties are taught as "normas de convivencia" — the rules of living together — sorted by the child into family, school and community.

Ecuador's own way (Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016)): the SM edition teaches local government from the child's own ground up — parish, then canton — with Ecuador's real institutions (the elected junta parroquial, the teniente político, the concejo municipal and the GADs), and teaches values through the Andean minga and the Kichwa reciprocity idea randi-randi, under the book's recurring Cultura del Buen Vivir spreads. That evidence is stocked here as the way Ecuador teaches this thing; it is counted in the acceptance count above (student book).
