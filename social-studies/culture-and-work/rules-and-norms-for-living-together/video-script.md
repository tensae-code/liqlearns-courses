# Video script — Rules and Norms for Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Panama Grade 1 Ciencias Sociales workbook, Área 3 Contenido 1 "Normas de convivencia social en la comunidad. Deberes y derechos de niñas, niños y de cada miembro" (printed p. 26), teaches: The rules of living together, and the duties and rights of girls, boys and every member, at home, school and in the community.

Panama's own way: Grade 1 social studies is a home workbook ("Conéctate en casa") worked through with family; rights and duties are taught as "normas de convivencia" — the rules of living together — sorted by the child into family, school and community.
