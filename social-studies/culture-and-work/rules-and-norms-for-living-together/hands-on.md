# Hands-on — Rules and Norms for Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The rules of living together, and the duties and rights of girls, boys and every member, at home, school and in the community. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Panama's way
Say how Panama's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
