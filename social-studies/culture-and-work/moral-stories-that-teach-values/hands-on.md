# Hands-on — Moral Stories That Teach Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 1 Moral Stories (اخلاقی کہانیاں) and poems — teaches: values carried by story — the king ruined by endless desire, the lame man who learns gratitude, Mohan Das who gives his own food away, Joseph who lives for others, the fruit of oppression — closed by the advice and sympathy poems of Ismail Meruthi and Iqbal. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
