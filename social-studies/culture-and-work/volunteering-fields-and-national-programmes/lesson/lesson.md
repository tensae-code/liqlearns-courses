## Sub lesson 1 — Volunteering Fields and National Programmes

Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 305–308), teaches: The fields of individual and institutional volunteering — serving religion (memorisation of the Quran and teaching its principles), education, society, health (blood donation, medical and first-aid work), the environment (cleaning wild parks and public gardens, planting trees, the food-preservation programmes distributing surplus food to the needy), heritage and relief — with tree-planting initiatives such as "Plant It, Don't Cut It"; the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development organises and enables volunteering (the National Volunteer Portal and the targeted growth of volunteer numbers), and the national programmes: Kun Awnan ("Be a Helper", Ministry of Hajj and Umrah — receiving, guiding, translating and medically serving the Guests of the Merciful), Takatuf (the Saudi association organising volunteering work), Waqar (supporting the elderly and their rights) and Ita'am (the food charity collecting surplus food for the needy). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
