# Hands-on — Volunteering Fields and National Programmes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the fields of individual and institutional volunteering — serving religion (memorisation of the Quran and teaching its principles), education, society, health (blood donation, medical and first-aid work), the environment (cleaning wild parks and public gardens, planting trees, the food-preservation programmes distributing surplus food to the needy), heritage and relief — with tree-planting initiatives such as "Plant It, Don't Cut It"; the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development organises and enables volunteering (the National Volunteer Portal and the targeted growth of volunteer numbers), and the national programmes: Kun Awnan ("Be a Helper", Ministry of Hajj and Umrah — receiving, guiding, translating and medically serving the Guests of the Merciful), Takatuf (the Saudi association organising volunteering work), Waqar (supporting the elderly and their rights) and Ita'am (the food charity collecting surplus food for the needy). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
