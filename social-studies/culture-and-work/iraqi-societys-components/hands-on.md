# Hands-on — Iraqi Societys Components

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Our Iraqi society is composed of several components spread over Iraq's governorates from the far north to the far south — the Arabs, the Kurds, the Turkmen, the Assyrians, the Chaldeans, the Syriacs, the Armenians, the Sabians, the Shabak and the Yazidis — bound together on Iraq's land by rights and duties; and belonging to the homeland and pride in it is a value that must be planted in the human being from his childhood, so that he serves his country and works for its advancement. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
