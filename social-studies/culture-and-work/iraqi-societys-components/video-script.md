# Video script — Iraqi Societys Components

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies (printed pp. 82–83), teaches: Our Iraqi society is composed of several components spread over Iraq's governorates from the far north to the far south — the Arabs, the Kurds, the Turkmen, the Assyrians, the Chaldeans, the Syriacs, the Armenians, the Sabians, the Shabak and the Yazidis — bound together on Iraq's land by rights and duties; and belonging to the homeland and pride in it is a value that must be planted in the human being from his childhood, so that he serves his country and works for its advancement.

Iraq's own way: the state is defined as institutions that serve, its pillars are named homeland, people, federal authorities and sovereignty, and citizenship is taught as a two-way street — the state's services (free education first) on one side, and on the other a good citizen whose first quality is knowing his own abilities so he can fix his duties and his rights.
