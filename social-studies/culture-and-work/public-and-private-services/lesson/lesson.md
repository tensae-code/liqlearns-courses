# Public and Private Services

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: public and private services, as Ecuador's Grade 3 *Estudios Sociales 3ro EGB (SM 2016)* teaches it in Servicios públicos y privados on printed p. 126. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Services for everyone, and services that are bought

Grade 3 teaches the services a community lives by, in two kinds: servicios públicos — public services, provided for everyone — and servicios privados — private services, provided by particular people or businesses and paid for by the user. The child learns to sort the services around them (water, health, schooling, transport and more) into the two kinds and to say who provides each one.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: the older SM edition is a classic skills-first book — a whole opening unit on orientation, cardinal points, the compass rose and plans before any content; every unit runs the same skeleton (content lessons, “Ser Estudiante” test, Cultura del Buen Vivir, scientific work, digital skills, unit evaluation), and the country is learned parish → canton → province → nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
