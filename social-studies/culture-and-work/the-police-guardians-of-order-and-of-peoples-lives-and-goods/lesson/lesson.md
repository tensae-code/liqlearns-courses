## Sub lesson 1 — The Police Guardians of Order and of Peoples Lives and Goods

Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 33, teaches: the police keep order and security across the country so people live in peace; they pursue and arrest thieves, bribe-takers, embezzlers, land-grabbers and smugglers, guard people's lives and property, state offices, embassies and roads — and citizens must cooperate with them and keep their phone numbers at hand. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: society is taught as a ladder of belonging — family, school, village, district, city, country, and the wider Islamic umma — and the state appears as the servant of that society: ministries named one by one, each with its service, and peace named the first need of a country that has known decades of war. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
