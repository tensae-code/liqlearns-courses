# Hands-on — The Police Guardians of Order and of Peoples Lives and Goods

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The police keep order and security across the country so people live in peace; they pursue and arrest thieves, bribe-takers, embezzlers, land-grabbers and smugglers, guard people's lives and property, state offices, embassies and roads — and citizens must cooperate with them and keep their phone numbers at hand. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Afghanistan's way
Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
