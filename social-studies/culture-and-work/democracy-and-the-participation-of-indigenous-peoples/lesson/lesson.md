# Democracy and the Participation of Indigenous Peoples

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: democracy as a political system where decisions answer to the collective will — direct participation in assemblies or indirect participation through elections; origin peoples' own assemblies and collective decisions, their inclusion in Latin America's democracies, and "no democracy is complete if the minority is discriminated against" — as Colombia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Módulo 5 Guía 16 Democracia para los pueblos originarios

Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado6, printed pp. 180–189, teaches: democracy as a political system where decisions answer to the collective will — direct participation in assemblies or indirect participation through elections; origin peoples' own assemblies and collective decisions, their inclusion in Latin America's democracies, and "no democracy is complete if the minority is discriminated against". This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 22) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: civics is taught through the political life of origin peoples — their assemblies, authorities and norms are treated as real political systems, and pupils are asked what indigenous participation and democracy can teach their own region's democratic exercises. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
