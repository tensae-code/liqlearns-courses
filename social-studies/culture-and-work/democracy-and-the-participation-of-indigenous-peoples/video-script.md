# Video script — Democracy and the Participation of Indigenous Peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado6, printed pp. 180–189, teaches: democracy as a political system where decisions answer to the collective will — direct participation in assemblies or indirect participation through elections; origin peoples' own assemblies and collective decisions, their inclusion in Latin America's democracies, and "no democracy is complete if the minority is discriminated against".

Colombia's own way: civics is taught through the political life of origin peoples — their assemblies, authorities and norms are treated as real political systems, and pupils are asked what indigenous participation and democracy can teach their own region's democratic exercises.
