# Hands-on — Democracy and the Participation of Indigenous Peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Módulo 5 Guía 16 Democracia para los pueblos originarios
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado6, printed pp. 180–189, teaches: democracy as a political system where decisions answer to the collective will — direct participation in assemblies or indirect participation through elections; origin peoples' own assemblies and collective decisions, their inclusion in Latin America's democracies, and "no democracy is complete if the minority is discriminated against". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 22) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: civics is taught through the political life of origin peoples — their assemblies, authorities and norms are treated as real political systems, and pupils are asked what indigenous participation and democracy can teach their own region's democratic exercises. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
