# Who Makes Decisions for Us — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Who makes decisions — a decision is a choice; parents decide at home, the principal at school, religious leaders in churches, mandirs and mosques, and councils, the President and the Government for the country; community leaders vote by show of hands or secretly; in Amerindian communities the Toshao and Village Council decide, the NDC Chairman for coastal villages and the Mayor and City Council for towns (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
