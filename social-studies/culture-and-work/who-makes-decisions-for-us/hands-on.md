# Hands-on — Who Makes Decisions for Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A decision is a choice; parents decide at home, the principal at school, religious leaders in churches, mandirs and mosques, and councils, the President and the Government for the country; community leaders vote by show of hands or secretly; in Amerindian communities the Toshao and Village Council decide, the NDC Chairman for coastal villages and the Mayor and City Council for towns. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
