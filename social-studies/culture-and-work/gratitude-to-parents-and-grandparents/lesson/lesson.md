## Sub lesson 5 — Laos's way — the books' own teaching way

Laos's own way: Grade 4 turns from conduct toward character — honesty in learning itself, perseverance, speaking one's opinion while respecting others', gratitude widened to parents, grandparents and all benefactors, and unity across Laos's many ethnic groups. Grade 2 widens Grade 1's start (road, school, family) into duties — classroom rules, care of the national flag, responsibility for shared property, and keeping the community environment clean. the subject opens in the first weeks of a child's schooling with the road, the school and the flag, then moves home — thank-you and sorry, elders, neighbours — ending the year by widening respect to the whole community. the last primary moral year widens the circles to their widest — neighbours, hometown, local government, nation, peace and the United Nations — and closes with care of the country's natural resources. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
