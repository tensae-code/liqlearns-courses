# Video script — Social Deviance and Law and Order

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 9 Social Science Year 9 (printed p. 46), teaches: Deviance as behaviour that breaks society's norms, and how law and order answer it (unit's own summary pairs deviance with law and order, disadvantage and leadership).

Fiji's own way (Grade 9): society is analysed through Fiji's own structures — the iTaukei hierarchy of Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau and Tauvanua with its ascribed chiefly status — and conflict is settled the iTaukei way, by Bulubulu.
