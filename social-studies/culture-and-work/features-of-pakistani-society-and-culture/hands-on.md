# Hands-on — Features of Pakistani Society and Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 6 Salient Features of Pakistani Society and Culture, Ch. 6 Salient Features of Pakistani Culture: Mixed Culture; Religious Uniformity, Ch. 4 Basic Features of Pakistani Society and the Major Social Problems — teaches: the thirteen features the book itself lists — Islamic culture's colours, the joint family system, customs and traditions, religious harmony, festivals, dress and food, mixed culture, urs and fairs, sports, minority rights, hospitality, architecture and painting, poetry and literature; Pakistan's culture is a mixture — peoples from Iran, Arabia, Turkey, Central Asia, Greece and Europe each left traditions, food and ways of life — held together by one dominant Islamic colour and a living religious cooperation in which minorities lead safe and honorable lives; the society's features stated plainly, then its problems named without flinching — social stratification by creed and caste, feudalism, the curse of dowry, drug trafficking and smuggling, terrorism, and child labour, "not just a problem but an evil". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
