# Video script — Human Development Versus Economic Growth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 8 Estudios Sociales 8vo EGB, printed p. 137, teaches: Telling human development from economic development, testing the claim "economic growth has not led to human development in Ecuador" with education, health and employment data, and the place of recreation, leisure and sport in a developed life.

Ecuador's own way: at Superior level the civics strand is built on Ecuador's own 2008 constitutional order — Buen Vivir (Sumak Kawsay) as the state's development idea, a plurinational and intercultural state, nature itself as a subject of rights, and children's rights taught from Ecuador's own Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia.
