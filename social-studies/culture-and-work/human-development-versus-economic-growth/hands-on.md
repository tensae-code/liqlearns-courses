# Hands-on — Human Development Versus Economic Growth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Telling human development from economic development, testing the claim "economic growth has not led to human development in Ecuador" with education, health and employment data, and the place of recreation, leisure and sport in a developed life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
