# Hands-on — Patrimony What It is and Why Care for It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: heritage as a common good — local heritage sketched and classified, natural heritage through Haiti's botanical gardens, intangible heritage typed against the UNESCO inventories and article 215 of the Constitution, and the citizen's duty to preserve and promote it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Haiti's way
Say how Haiti's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
