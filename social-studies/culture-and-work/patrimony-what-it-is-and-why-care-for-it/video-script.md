# Video script — Patrimony What It is and Why Care for It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 7 EC1 7AF (printed p. 15), teaches: heritage as a common good — local heritage sketched and classified, natural heritage through Haiti's botanical gardens, intangible heritage typed against the UNESCO inventories and article 215 of the Constitution, and the citizen's duty to preserve and promote it.

Haiti's own way: Citizenship is built from Haiti's own foundations — the nation defined by shared identity, history, territory, values and institutions and the will to live together (unity, solidarity, social cohesion), read through the Haitian Constitution itself in French and Creole, and practised on the symbols and memory places of the pupils' own locality.
