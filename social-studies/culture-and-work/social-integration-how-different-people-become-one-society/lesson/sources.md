**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Indonesia).** Indonesia's teacher-guide parts in this lesson are teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere — Indonesia counts through its student-book parts.

Indonesia's own way (Book 1): social integration is taught through marakka bola — the South Sulawesi gotong royong tradition of literally lifting and carrying a neighbour's house to its new site, without regard to difference.

Indonesia's own way (Book 2): national integration is taught as a contrast — the old integrations of Sriwijaya and Majapahit were made by conquest and stayed fragile (semu), while the modern one grew from shared fate under colonisation and is held by tolerance, with Indonesia cited by its own Foreign Ministry as a world reference for unity in diversity.

Indonesia's own way (Book 3): the guide builds each chapter around an Aktivitas Kolaborasi (collaboration activity), so the norms and institutions content is taught by group work, not lecture.
