# Hands-on — Social Integration How Different People Become One Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 4 D. Integrasi Sosial, Bab 4 D.2–3 Syarat dan Faktor Integrasi Sosial, Bab 1 C.3 Terbentuknya Integrasi Nasional, Panduan Khusus Bab 4: Perdagangan Antarwilayah di Indonesia — teaches: social integration defined as different elements blending while guarding each other into peace — gotong royong, and marakka bola house-lifting in South Sulawesi, as the popular example; the conditions for integration (members filling each other's needs; shared agreement on norms and values, consistently kept) and its speed factors (group homogeneity, group size, geographic mobility, communication effectiveness); how national integration formed — Sriwijaya and Majapahit uniting by trade, religion and conquest (fragile, semu) against the modern unity born of shared colonial fate and national consciousness; guidance for the social-integration part of the trade chapter, including the marakka bola gotong royong case. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
