Pairing warning (honesty): Book 2 is an Addis Ababa bureau guide in English; Book 1 is an Amhara bureau student book in Amharic. They are NOT edition-matched companions — the guide's unit order differs, its activity numbers key to its own companion textbook, and whole student-book topics have no guide counterpart. Guide material is used only as labelled teacher evidence, never as a page-key to the student book. Pupil content in this lesson comes from the Amhara student book.

TAX-HISTORY TENSION, carried honestly and never silently resolved: the guide's keyed Activity 2.4 answer describes a practice of traditional taxation in the Aksumite kingdom, and then credits the reign of King Zera Yacob (1434–1468) with introducing "the first tax system" (guide printed pp. 28, 29–30; guide verification block, error 8). Both lines are taught as the guide gives them, and the contradiction is named in the lesson and here. Also: the guide's §2.4 competencies end "List the main benefits of trade" where tax/revenue is meant (guide verification block, error 6) — a misprint, not taught.
