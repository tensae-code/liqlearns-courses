# Tax — government revenue in federal Ethiopia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The wool tax was levied particularly on —
a) Coastal areas  b) Mountainous areas  c) Desert areas  d) Island areas
**Answer: b) Mountainous areas**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Elephant hunters paid hunting taxes in the form of —
a) Wool  b) Butter  c) Ivory taxes  d) Coffee
**Answer: c) Ivory taxes**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Menelik II's reform established —
a) No tax at all  b) A fixed tithe in place of arbitrary agricultural taxes  c) Entrepot trade  d) A lottery tax only
**Answer: b) A fixed tithe in place of arbitrary agricultural taxes**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a FEDERAL revenue source?
a) Regional employees' tax  b) Land-use fees  c) Customs on goods entering and leaving the country  d) Regional licence fees
**Answer: c) Customs on goods entering and leaving the country**

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Royalties on large mining and petroleum works are —
a) Regional only  b) Federal only  c) Joint sources  d) Not revenue
**Answer: c) Joint sources**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Tax and investment —
a) Never meet  b) Feed each other: tax builds infrastructure that draws investors, widening the tax base  c) Compete until one ends  d) Are the same thing
**Answer: b) Feed each other: tax builds infrastructure that draws investors, widening the tax base**
