# Tax — government revenue in federal Ethiopia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Whose reign is credited with the first tax system, and what tension stands beside that credit?
**Answer:** King Zera Yacob (1434–1468). The tension: the same key also places a practice of traditional taxation in the Aksumite kingdom, long before him.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two regional sources.
**Answer:** Any two of: regional employees' tax; land-use fees; tax on individual traders in the region; tax on regionally owned houses and property; fees for regional licences and services.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name four of the guide's seven benefits of revenue.
**Answer:** Any four of: removal of inequalities in income and wealth; economic stability; reduction in regional imbalances; capital accumulation; employment creation; beneficial diversion of resources; encouragement of export.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the guide define government revenue?
**Answer:** All government income from tax and non-tax sources.
