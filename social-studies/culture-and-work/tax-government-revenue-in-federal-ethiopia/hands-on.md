# Hands-on — Tax — government revenue in federal Ethiopia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
List what your community gains from tax — roads, water, power, bridges — and match each to the closing loop.

## Activity 2
Debate taxpayers' rights and duties with a partner, as the book's task sets it: two rights, two duties, each defended in one sentence.

## Activity 3
Draw the three-column chart from memory and fill each column with two sources from the lesson.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Tell tax's Ethiopian history as the guide keys it — wool, ivory, Aksum, Zera Yacob, Menelik's tithe — and name the tension in it (teacher guide — evidence). Sort revenue sources into federal, regional and joint. State the closing loop of tax and investment, and name the guide's seven benefits of revenue (teacher guide — evidence).
