# Video script — Groups and Roles Change Over Time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 6 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 12, teaches: some roles are ours from birth and some we choose; across childhood, school and working life our groups and roles change (student, class president for a week, teammate; later the occupational groups — teacher, pilot, doctor, farmer — and perhaps a manager's role), and with them our rights and responsibilities; pupils interview an elder about the groups and roles of their lifetime

Turkey's own way: Grade 6 replays the Grade 5 "living together" area one level up — roles are now followed across a whole lifetime, social unity is traced to cultural bonds (language, faith, history, art, custom), and social problems are answered with the ancestors' own institution, the vakıf, before any modern agency is named.
