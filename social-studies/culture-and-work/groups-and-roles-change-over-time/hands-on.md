# Hands-on — Groups and Roles Change Over Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: some roles are ours from birth and some we choose; across childhood, school and working life our groups and roles change (student, class president for a week, teammate; later the occupational groups — teacher, pilot, doctor, farmer — and perhaps a manager's role), and with them our rights and responsibilities; pupils interview an elder about the groups and roles of their lifetime Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Turkey's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
