# Groups and Roles Change Over Time — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Groups and roles changing over time — some roles are ours from birth and some we choose; across childhood, school and working life our groups and roles change (student, class president for a week, teammate; later the occupational groups — teacher, pilot, doctor, farmer — and perhaps a manager's role), and with them our rights and responsibilities; pupils interview an elder about the groups and roles of their lifetime (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
