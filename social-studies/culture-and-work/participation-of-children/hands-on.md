# Hands-on — Participation of Children

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 5.3 "Why is our participation in collective leadership and decisions needed?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Children's participation (хүүхдийн оролцоо) means children freely expressing their views and joining in deciding the questions that concern their own upbringing, education and development — under three principles: openness, equality, and no discrimination; Roger Hart's ladder shows how adults meet that participation — from opponents who break children's trust and blockers who undervalue it, through those who use children as decoration, to partners who initiate together with children and organisations children themselves create; and real initiatives count: pupils' own proposals, carried to the district's Education Department, changed how their schools ran. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
