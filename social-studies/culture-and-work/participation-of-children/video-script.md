# Video script — Participation of Children

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 7 Social Studies, printed p. 90, teaches: Children's participation (хүүхдийн оролцоо) means children freely expressing their views and joining in deciding the questions that concern their own upbringing, education and development — under three principles: openness, equality, and no discrimination; Roger Hart's ladder shows how adults meet that participation — from opponents who break children's trust and blockers who undervalue it, through those who use children as decoration, to partners who initiate together with children and organisations children themselves create; and real initiatives count: pupils' own proposals, carried to the district's Education Department, changed how their schools ran.

Mongolia's own way (Book 5): the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work.
