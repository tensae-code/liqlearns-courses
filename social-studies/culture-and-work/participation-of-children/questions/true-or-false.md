# Participation of Children — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: children's participation (хүүхдийн оролцоо) means children freely expressing their views and joining in deciding the questions that concern their own upbringing, education and development — under three principles: openness, equality, and no discrimination; Roger Hart's ladder shows how adults meet that participation — from opponents who break children's trust and blockers who undervalue it, through those who use children as decoration, to partners who initiate together with children and organisations children themselves create; and real initiatives count: pupils' own proposals, carried to the district's Education Department, changed how their schools ran.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
