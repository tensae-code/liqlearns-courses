# Participation of Children — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 5.3 "Why is our participation in collective leadership and decisions needed?" — Children's participation (хүүхдийн оролцоо) means children freely expressing their views and joining in deciding the questions that concern their own upbringing, education and development — under three principles: openness, equality, and no discrimination; Roger Hart's ladder shows how adults meet that participation — from opponents who break children's trust and blockers who undervalue it, through those who use children as decoration, to partners who initiate together with children and organisations children themselves create; and real initiatives count: pupils' own proposals, carried to the district's Education Department, changed how their schools ran (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work (any close wording).**
