# Participation of Children

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: children's participation (хүүхдийн оролцоо) means children freely expressing their views and joining in deciding the questions that concern their own upbringing, education and development — under three principles: openness, equality, and no discrimination; Roger Hart's ladder shows how adults meet that participation — from opponents who break children's trust and blockers who undervalue it, through those who use children as decoration, to partners who initiate together with children and organisations children themselves create; and real initiatives count: pupils' own proposals, carried to the district's Education Department, changed how their schools ran — as Mongolia's books teach it.
