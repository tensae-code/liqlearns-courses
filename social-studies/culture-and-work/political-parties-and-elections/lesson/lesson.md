## Sub lesson 1 — Political parties and public associations (Политические партии и общественные объединения) — Grade 10

Belarus Grade 10 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 90), teaches: The political party and its functions against the wider family of public associations — trade-union, youth, anti-war organisations, mass movements, associations and funds; many parties is not by itself multipartyism; associations help satisfy citizens' interests without state power; and the right to association as fundamental, quoted from Article 36 of the Constitution — everyone has the right to freedom of association. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
