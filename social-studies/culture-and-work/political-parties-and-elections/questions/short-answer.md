# Political Parties and Elections — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Political parties and public associations (Политические партии и общественные объединения) — Grade 10 — the political party and its functions against the wider family of public associations — trade-union, youth, anti-war organisations, mass movements, associations and funds; many parties is not by itself multipartyism; associations help satisfy citizens' interests without state power; and the right to association as fundamental, quoted from Article 36 of the Constitution — everyone has the right to freedom of association (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system. (any close wording).**
