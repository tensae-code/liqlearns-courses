# Hands-on — The Contributions of Our Ancestors to Trinidad and Tobago

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Each people who came (Amerindians, Europeans, Africans, East Indians, Chinese, Portuguese, Syrians/Lebanese, Venezuelan Cocoa Panyols) and what each brought — food, religion, dress, music and dance, language, place names, folklore. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Trinidad and Tobago's way
Say how Trinidad and Tobago's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
