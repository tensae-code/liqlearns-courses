# Martyrs of the Union

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: why the United Arab Emirates took part in the war in Yemen (the Arab coalition); the first martyr of the Emirates; the names of some of the states that shared in the coalition; and the efforts the state makes to keep the memory of its martyrs alive (تخليد ذكرى شهداء الإمارات) — sacrifice for the homeland taught with the state's own honouring of its fallen — as United Arab Emirates's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Martyrs of the Union

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G4 teacher guide, volume 1, printed p. 133, teaches: why the United Arab Emirates took part in the war in Yemen (the Arab coalition); the first martyr of the Emirates; the names of some of the states that shared in the coalition; and the efforts the state makes to keep the memory of its martyrs alive (تخليد ذكرى شهداء الإمارات) — sacrifice for the homeland taught with the state's own honouring of its fallen This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: the fourth-grade guide teaches its civics as a set of named concepts first — citizenship, belonging, participation, responsibility, identity — and then walks them into the child's world in order: the family he lives in, the government that runs his state, and the martyrs the state honours. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
