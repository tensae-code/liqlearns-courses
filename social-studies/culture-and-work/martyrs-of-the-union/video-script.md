# Video script — Martyrs of the Union

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G4 teacher guide, volume 1, printed p. 133, teaches: why the United Arab Emirates took part in the war in Yemen (the Arab coalition); the first martyr of the Emirates; the names of some of the states that shared in the coalition; and the efforts the state makes to keep the memory of its martyrs alive (تخليد ذكرى شهداء الإمارات) — sacrifice for the homeland taught with the state's own honouring of its fallen

United Arab Emirates' own way: the fourth-grade guide teaches its civics as a set of named concepts first — citizenship, belonging, participation, responsibility, identity — and then walks them into the child's world in order: the family he lives in, the government that runs his state, and the martyrs the state honours.
