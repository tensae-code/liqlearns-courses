# Hands-on — Labour Spirit Craftsmanship and Social Responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Values inquiry in three paths — honouring the model-worker/labour/craftsman spirits and forming a right view of work and enterprise; green production and green, moderate consumption; and rural revitalisation and common prosperity in the poverty-alleviation spirit, joining personal development to social progress. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## China's way
Say how China's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
