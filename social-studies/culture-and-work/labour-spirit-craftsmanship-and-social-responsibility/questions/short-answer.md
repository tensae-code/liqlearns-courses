# Labour Spirit Craftsmanship and Social Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Labour Spirit Craftsmanship and Social Responsibility — values inquiry in three paths — honouring the model-worker/labour/craftsman spirits and forming a right view of work and enterprise; green production and green, moderate consumption; and rural revitalisation and common prosperity in the poverty-alleviation spirit, joining personal development to social progress (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: China's way — the book's own teaching way — China's economics is taught inside the Politics course as the working of its own basic economic system — public ownership as the mainstay, the market decisive and the government better — and the course closes by turning the economics into values: the model-worker, labour and craftsman spirits, green and moderate consumption, and the poverty-alleviation spirit as duties of the citizen. (any close wording).**
