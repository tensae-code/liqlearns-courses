The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

China Senior 思想政治必修2 (printed p. 57), teaches: Values inquiry in three paths — honouring the model-worker/labour/craftsman spirits and forming a right view of work and enterprise; green production and green, moderate consumption; and rural revitalisation and common prosperity in the poverty-alleviation spirit, joining personal development to social progress.

China's own way: China's economics is taught inside the Politics course as the working of its own basic economic system — public ownership as the mainstay, the market decisive and the government better — and the course closes by turning the economics into values: the model-worker, labour and craftsman spirits, green and moderate consumption, and the poverty-alleviation spirit as duties of the citizen.
