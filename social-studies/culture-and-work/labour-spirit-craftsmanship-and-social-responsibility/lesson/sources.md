China's own way: China's economics is taught inside the Politics course as the working of its own basic economic system — public ownership as the mainstay, the market decisive and the government better — and the course closes by turning the economics into values: the model-worker, labour and craftsman spirits, green and moderate consumption, and the poverty-alleviation spirit as duties of the citizen.
