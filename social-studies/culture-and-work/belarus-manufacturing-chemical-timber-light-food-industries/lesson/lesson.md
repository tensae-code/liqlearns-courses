## Sub lesson 1 — Machine building and the chemical industry

Theme VII §§44–45 of Belarus's Grade 10 book covers machine building — the industry that makes machines — and the chemical industry.

## Sub lesson 2 — Timber, woodworking and pulp-and-paper; building materials; light industry; food industry

Theme VII §§46–49 follows with the timber, woodworking and pulp-and-paper industries, building materials, light industry and the food industry — the branches that work wood, build, clothe and feed.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
