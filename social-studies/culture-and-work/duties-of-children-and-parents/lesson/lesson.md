## Do it first

## Sub lesson 1 — Children's Duties Toward the Family

Mauritania Grade 3 Civic Education SB (printed p. 11), teaches: What children owe their family. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Family's Duties Toward the Children

Mauritania Grade 3 Civic Education SB (printed p. 13), teaches: What the family owes its children — duties run in both directions. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mauritania's way — the book's own teaching way

Mauritania's own way: Year 3 civics is the family and the school — duties run both ways (children to family, family to children) and the values are solidarity, cooperation, tolerance and volunteering practised inside those two communities This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
