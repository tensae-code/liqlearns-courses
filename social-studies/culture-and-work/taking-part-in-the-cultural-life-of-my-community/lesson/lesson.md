# Taking Part In The Cultural Life Of My Community

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: taking part in the cultural life of my community — joining in its activities as a member, not a spectator — as Angola's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Participo na vida cultural da minha comunidade

Angola Educação Moral e Cívica G5 (printed p. 97), teaches: Taking part in the cultural life of my community — joining in its activities as a member, not a spectator. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Angola's way — the book's own teaching way

Angola's own way: a moral-and-civic book built on the child's own reflection — every unit opens with the pupil's own life (my habits, my family, my school) and only then names the rule; equality is taught straight from Angola's own Constitution (art. 23: all are equal before the Constitution and the law), and the book ends not on the state but on national reconciliation — building a better Angola, then a better world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
