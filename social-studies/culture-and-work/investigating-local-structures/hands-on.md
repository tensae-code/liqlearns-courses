# Hands-on — Investigating Local Structures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Pupils investigate how local structures are run with scientific questionnaires — the school (headteacher) as a social structure and the Union Parishad / City Corporation as a political structure — then name their own position and role in each, and form a grade-9 Civic Club by democratic election. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
