# Video script — Investigating Local Structures

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bangladesh Grade 9 BD G9-10 HSS (printed pp. 50–53), teaches: Pupils investigate how local structures are run with scientific questionnaires — the school (headteacher) as a social structure and the Union Parishad / City Corporation as a political structure — then name their own position and role in each, and form a grade-9 Civic Club by democratic election.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 9–10): Civics becomes a full field manual — pupils questionnaire a real Union Parishad chairman and their own headteacher, then learn the whole local-government ladder tier by tier with membership counts, and meet the United Nations through Bangladesh's own seat in it (Bangabandhu's 1974 Bengali speech) before the Charter is summarised..
