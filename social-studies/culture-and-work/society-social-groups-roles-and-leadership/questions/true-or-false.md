# Society Social Groups Roles and Leadership — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: society is a group of people interacting in a place, sharing a culture, organised by people and institutions; social groups (family, friends, churches, firms, clubs, patronatos) have common objectives, structure, norms, values and roles; doing your entrusted role responsibly builds leadership, and a leader has six basic functions, beginning with setting the group's objectives.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: societies organise because needs point social actors to a form of organisation that defends individual and collective rights; the forms run from the first small equal groups of nomad hunter-gatherers through horde, family, tribe, clan and village as farming settles people; workers' and peasant organisations defend their members' interests, and organisation is an integrated system of valuable people solving community problems together.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: society is a grouping of people who decide to meet and live together to improve their quality of life; it is composed of all its members and needs a set of rules and norms regulating life in society; Honduras's decentralised institutions serve it (National Printing Enterprise ENAG, the Fire Corps, the Honduran Social Investment Fund FHIS) — the lesson's national-identity part was placed by the History wave.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: the "social component" is the whole structure that builds a population — density, characteristics, composition and migratory movements; social organisation is any grouping established from shared elements, common ideas and similar ways of seeing the world; Honduran society shows sharp contrasts: high densities in San Pedro Sula, Atlántida and Tegucigalpa against a dispersed countryside.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's fifth part teaches: latin America's social stratification, studied by sociology (Germani's three phases among them): at the top the oligarchy — groups that traditionally hold power: business, union, religious and military elites and civilian technocrats, with complex relations among them; business sectors tended to link themselves to the groups of greatest economic and social power.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it also stands on Honduras's teacher guide(s), named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
