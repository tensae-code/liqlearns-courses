# Hands-on — Society Social Groups Roles and Leadership

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 2 Vivo en una sociedad
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Society is a group of people interacting in a place, sharing a culture, organised by people and institutions; social groups (family, friends, churches, firms, clubs, patronatos) have common objectives, structure, norms, values and roles; doing your entrusted role responsibly builds leadership, and a leader has six basic functions, beginning with setting the group's objectives. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 30 Organización social
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Societies organise because needs point social actors to a form of organisation that defends individual and collective rights; the forms run from the first small equal groups of nomad hunter-gatherers through horde, family, tribe, clan and village as farming settles people; workers' and peasant organisations defend their members' interests, and organisation is an integrated system of valuable people solving community problems together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 30 La construcción de la sociedad
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Society is a grouping of people who decide to meet and live together to improve their quality of life; it is composed of all its members and needs a set of rules and norms regulating life in society; Honduras's decentralised institutions serve it (National Printing Enterprise ENAG, the Fire Corps, the Honduran Social Investment Fund FHIS) — the lesson's national-identity part was placed by the History wave. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 30, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The "social component" is the whole structure that builds a population — density, characteristics, composition and migratory movements; social organisation is any grouping established from shared elements, common ideas and similar ways of seeing the world; Honduran society shows sharp contrasts: high densities in San Pedro Sula, Atlántida and Tegucigalpa against a dispersed countryside. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 34, teoría adicional
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Latin America's social stratification, studied by sociology (Germani's three phases among them): at the top the oligarchy — groups that traditionally hold power: business, union, religious and military elites and civilian technocrats, with complex relations among them; business sectors tended to link themselves to the groups of greatest economic and social power. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
