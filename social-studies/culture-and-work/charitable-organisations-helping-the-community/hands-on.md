# Hands-on — Charitable Organisations Helping the Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Charitable organisations
Work through this part with your hands: Charities are set up to help people in need with money given voluntarily — Fiji Red Cross Society, Save the Children Fund and women's and youth clubs supplying food, clothes, uniforms and school needs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Fiji's way
Say how Fiji's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
