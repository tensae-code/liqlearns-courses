# Video script — Charitable Organisations Helping the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 5 Year 5 Social Studies (printed p. 6), teaches: Charities are set up to help people in need with money given voluntarily — Fiji Red Cross Society, Save the Children Fund and women's and youth clubs supplying food, clothes, uniforms and school needs.

Fiji's own way (Grade 5): community is taught as the social groups a child belongs to — family first, then school and community groups — and Fiji's multicultural mix is the civics lesson itself: all born in Fiji are Fijian, each keeping their own culture.
