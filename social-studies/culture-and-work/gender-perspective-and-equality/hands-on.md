# Hands-on — Gender Perspective and Equality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Gender Perspective (Grade 7)
Work through this part with your hands: the gender perspective — seeing how socially built prejudices make men and women unequal in exercising their rights, and using that lens in language, ideas and daily dealings to build a just, equal society free of violence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ethical Judgements on Injustice and Discrimination (Grade 8)
Work through this part with your hands: ethical judgement practised on everyday injustice — inclusion, interculturality and the gender perspective as tools, and substantive equality (same treatment and opportunities for all, in Mexico's General Law for Equality between Women and Men) against subordination, exclusion and power relations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
