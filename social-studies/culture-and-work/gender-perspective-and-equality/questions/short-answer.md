# Gender Perspective and Equality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Gender Perspective (Grade 7) — the gender perspective — seeing how socially built prejudices make men and women unequal in exercising their rights, and using that lens in language, ideas and daily dealings to build a just, equal society free of violence (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ethical Judgements on Injustice and Discrimination (Grade 8) — ethical judgement practised on everyday injustice — inclusion, interculturality and the gender perspective as tools, and substantive equality (same treatment and opportunities for all, in Mexico's General Law for Equality between Women and Men) against subordination, exclusion and power relations (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way — Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution. Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality. (any close wording).**
