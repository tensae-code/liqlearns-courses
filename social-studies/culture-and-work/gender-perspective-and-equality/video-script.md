# Video script — Gender Perspective and Equality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mexico Grade 7 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 1 (secundaria) (printed pp. 315–316), teaches: The gender perspective — seeing how socially built prejudices make men and women unequal in exercising their rights, and using that lens in language, ideas and daily dealings to build a just, equal society free of violence.

---

Mexico Grade 8 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 2 (secundaria) (printed pp. 293–300), teaches: Ethical judgement practised on everyday injustice — inclusion, interculturality and the gender perspective as tools, and substantive equality (same treatment and opportunities for all, in Mexico's General Law for Equality between Women and Men) against subordination, exclusion and power relations.

---

Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution. Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality.
