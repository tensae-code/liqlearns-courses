# Hands-on — Human Rights Problems at Home and Abroad and Solutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: IV.주제5 우리 사회의 인권 문제, IV.03 국내외 인권 문제와 해결 방안, IV.3 인권 문제의 양상과 해결 방안, IV.3 인권 문제의 양상과 해결, IV.05 우리 사회의 인권 문제에 대한 해결 방안은?, 인권 보장과 헌법 3 인권 문제의 양상과 해결 방안 — teaches: human-rights problems in Korean society itself and how they are addressed; human-rights problems at home and abroad and the ways they are solved; the faces of human-rights problems and the plans that solve them; the faces of human-rights problems and their resolution; solutions to the human-rights problems of Korean society; human-rights problems at home and in the world, and their solutions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
