# Hands-on — Social Control Formal and Informal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Social control — formal and informal
Work through this part with your hands: Communities control members' behaviour so law and order is kept — formal control (written: school rules, constitution, laws) and informal control (unwritten: customs, traditions, family rules), working through rewards and punishments. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
