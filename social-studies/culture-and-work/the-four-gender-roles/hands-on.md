# Hands-on — The Four Gender Roles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Four Gender Roles
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A role is the set of behaviours a person performs for a purpose, and gender roles are shaped by social conditions, not by biology — childrearing and housework are traditionally women's, yet men can do them; the book counts four roles: the reproductive role (women's from conception and birth, with child care and upbringing shared), the productive role (production and market work, where women's work is still undervalued despite distinguished results), the community role (for women an extension of the reproductive role around family and near community, for men an extension of productive work relations), and the political role (decision-making authority inside and outside the family, where Palestinian women leaders have emerged in many fields but not at the required level) — and the aim is sharing the roles, not exchanging them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
