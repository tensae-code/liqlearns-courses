# The Four Gender Roles — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: a role is the set of behaviours a person performs for a purpose, and gender roles are shaped by social conditions, not by biology — childrearing and housework are traditionally women's, yet men can do them; the book counts four roles: the reproductive role (women's from conception and birth, with child care and upbringing shared), the productive role (production and market work, where women's work is still undervalued despite distinguished results), the community role (for women an extension of the reproductive role around family and near community, for men an extension of productive work relations), and the political role (decision-making authority inside and outside the family, where Palestinian women leaders have emerged in many fields but not at the required level) — and the aim is sharing the roles, not exchanging them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Palestine's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
