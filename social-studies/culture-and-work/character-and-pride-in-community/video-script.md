# Video script — Character and Pride in Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Latvia Grade 4 Sociālās zinības un vēsture teacher methodological book (printed p. 20), teaches: Theme 4 Kā kļūt lepnuma vērtiem kopienā? (How to become worthy of pride in the community) — teaches: human character qualities and recognising them, a Latvian personality (Kaspars Daugaviņš), facts and opinions about State President Edgars Rinkēvičs, and people we are proud of.

Latvia's own way: the social strand is taught through Latvia’s own festivals and cultural canon and through living Latvian figures (a national sportsman; the sitting State President Edgars Rinkēvičs, used to teach fact versus opinion).
