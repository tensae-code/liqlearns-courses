# Hands-on — Character and Pride in Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Theme 4 Kā kļūt lepnuma vērtiem kopienā? (How to become worthy of pride in the community) — teaches: human character qualities and recognising them, a Latvian personality (Kaspars Daugaviņš), facts and opinions about State President Edgars Rinkēvičs, and people we are proud of. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Latvia's way
Say how Latvia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
