# Video script — Responsibility and Mutual Help

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nicaragua Grade 2 Convivencia y Civismo G2 (printed p. 25), teaches: Responsibility joined to mutual help at home and school.

Nicaragua Grade 3 Convivencia y Civismo G3 (printed p. 12), teaches: Responsibility as the way a person grows.

Nicaragua Grade 4 Convivencia y Civismo G4 (printed p. 30), teaches: Responsibility as a practised value.

Nicaragua's own way: civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book. Grade 3 civics adds Nicaragua-specific law to the values base — women's rights under Ley 779 and a Mother Earth declaration — and respect for public and private institutions as a civic duty. Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State.
