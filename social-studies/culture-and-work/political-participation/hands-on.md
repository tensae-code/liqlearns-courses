# Hands-on — Political Participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Political participation is a concept linked to political and social life, represented in giving the democratic and constitutional right to all the adult members of society to participate in political decision-making, whether directly — through candidacy for elections, joining one of the civil-society institutions, or holding any of the executive and legislative offices — or indirectly, through casting their votes for one of the candidates. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
