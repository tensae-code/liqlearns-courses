## Sub lesson 2 — Laos's way — the book's own teaching way

Laos's own way: the first secondary civics year moves from personal virtues (diligence, thrift, discipline, gratitude, manners) to named legal rights, each rights chapter anchored in a numbered article of Laos's own 2003 Constitution or a named law — the pupil meets the state first as the protector of their own life, home and schooling.
