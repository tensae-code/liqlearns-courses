# Hands-on — Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Human Rights 1
Work through this part with your hands: The child states her rights in her homeland and learns to value them — rights named as something the homeland guarantees, the first half of the term's standard. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Human Rights 2
Work through this part with your hands: The child's rights begin at home — she expresses her family rights as real claims on the people closest to her. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
