## Sub lesson 3 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: at Grade 3 the subject turns from feelings to law-shaped habits — rights first, then duties, then the homeland held as a trust (أمانة); and the same rights-and-duties pair is then taught again at world scale, where equality, safety and being heard are the child's global rights. rights and duties are taught at family scale before homeland scale — and the family unit ends with the children drafting a ميثاق, a charter, for their own family and pledging to keep it: constitution-making at the kitchen table. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
