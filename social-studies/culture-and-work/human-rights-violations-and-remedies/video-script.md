# Video script — Human Rights Violations and Remedies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 8 CS_Grado8, printed pp. 222–241, teaches: conflicts and confrontations over human rights in the Colombian context — the conditions of human rights nationally, fundamental rights as standing above any person's condition, and citizen participation as the democratic answer.

Colombia's own way: the whole rights course is anchored in Colombia's own conflict and its own answer — peace dialogues and the 1991 Constitution, which pupils read as a deliberate act of inclusion of the ethnic groups, peoples and ideologies the country had excluded.
