# Hands-on — Human Rights Violations and Remedies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Módulo 6 Guía 18 Conflictos y enfrentamientos en relación con los derechos humanos en Colombia
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 8 CS_Grado8, printed pp. 222–241, teaches: conflicts and confrontations over human rights in the Colombian context — the conditions of human rights nationally, fundamental rights as standing above any person's condition, and citizen participation as the democratic answer. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 24) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: the whole rights course is anchored in Colombia's own conflict and its own answer — peace dialogues and the 1991 Constitution, which pupils read as a deliberate act of inclusion of the ethnic groups, peoples and ideologies the country had excluded. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
