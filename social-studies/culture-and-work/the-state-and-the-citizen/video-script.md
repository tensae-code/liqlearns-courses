The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Armenia Grade 7 Social Science teacher guide a15432, printed p. 72, teaches: Aristotle's state-first view set against Article 3 of the RA Constitution — the person as the highest value, public power limited by fundamental rights and freedoms as directly applicable law.

Armenia Grade 9 Social Science, printed p. 32, teaches: how the state and the citizen belong to each other — citizenship and the citizen's place in the state.

Armenia's own way: the authors' own guide teaches the values chapters through Armenian everyday cases — men's shorts, Lent fasting, and the new Armenian animal-cruelty punishment — and sets Aristotle's state-first text against Article 3 of Armenia's own Constitution.

Armenia's own way: the state is taught through Armenia itself — its Constitution, its constitutional order and state bodies, electing its deputies and mayors, and its referendum.
