Armenia's own way (G07 Social Science teacher guide a15432): the authors' own guide teaches the values chapters through Armenian everyday cases — men's shorts, Lent fasting, and the new Armenian animal-cruelty punishment — and sets Aristotle's state-first text against Article 3 of Armenia's own Constitution.

Armenia's own way (G09 Social Science student): the state is taught through Armenia itself — its Constitution, its constitutional order and state bodies, electing its deputies and mayors, and its referendum.
