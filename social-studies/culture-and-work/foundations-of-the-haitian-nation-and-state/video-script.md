# Video script — Foundations of the Haitian Nation and State

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 8 EC1 8e Fondements (printed p. 10), teaches: the genesis, creation and evolution of the Haitian state studied through the current Constitution and the historical constitutions, and the relations between nation and state.

Haiti's own way: The Grade 7 nation/state sequence is deepened by returning to the territory itself: pupils draw Haiti from memory, then test their drawing against the Constitution's own definition of the territory (article 8) and against the island shared with a second state.
