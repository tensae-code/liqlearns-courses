## Sub lesson 1 — Foundations of the Haitian Nation and State

Haiti Grade 8 EC1 8e Fondements (printed p. 10), teaches: the genesis, creation and evolution of the Haitian state studied through the current Constitution and the historical constitutions, and the relations between nation and state. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way

Haiti's own way: The Grade 7 nation/state sequence is deepened by returning to the territory itself: pupils draw Haiti from memory, then test their drawing against the Constitution's own definition of the territory (article 8) and against the island shared with a second state. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
