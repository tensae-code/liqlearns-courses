# Hands-on — Security and Protection Services

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the government bodies that achieve security and each one's task — the Ministry of Defence (defending the homeland by land, sea and air), the National Guard, the Presidency of State Security, the Public Prosecution (justice and protecting rights), Public Security (catching criminals, traffic), the Anti-Narcotics Directorate, Civil Defence (fires and rescue), Highway Security, the Border Guard, and the National Cybersecurity Authority. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
