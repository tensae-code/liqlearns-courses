## Sub lesson 1 — Security and Protection Services

Saudi Arabia Grade 5 Social Studies (printed p. 55), teaches: The government bodies that achieve security and each one's task — the Ministry of Defence (defending the homeland by land, sea and air), the National Guard, the Presidency of State Security, the Public Prosecution (justice and protecting rights), Public Security (catching criminals, traffic), the Anti-Narcotics Directorate, Civil Defence (fires and rescue), Highway Security, the Border Guard, and the National Cybersecurity Authority. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 5 book teaches security and state institutions as service — security is defined first, the citizen is named the first line of security, the security agencies are listed one by one with their tasks, state institutions are split into public and private, and each region's Emir receives citizens and hears their needs. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
