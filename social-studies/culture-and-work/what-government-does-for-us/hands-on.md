# Hands-on — What Government Does for Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The state has built many government service institutions to provide all the necessary services to the individuals of Iraqi society: education — our state is keen to provide education services free of charge to its pupil sons and to the students of all study stages, through the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research ("it is my right to learn"); health services — the state provides them to the members of Iraqi society through the hospitals and health centres spread across Iraq's governorates; security — the Iraqi citizen has the right to be secure in his homeland, and the state provides security through the army and police forces that protect the citizen from internal and external dangers; and other services the state provides to the citizen, such as water, electricity, transport and communications services, roads, bridges, parks, gardens and libraries. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iraq's way
Say how Iraq's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
