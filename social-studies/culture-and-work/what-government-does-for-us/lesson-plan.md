# Lesson plan — What Government Does for Us

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** What Government Does for Us
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Iraq, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The state has built many government service institutions to provide all the necessary services to the individuals of Iraqi society: education — our state is keen to provide education services free of charge to its pupil sons and to the students of all study stages, through the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research ("it is my right to learn"); health services — the state provides them to the members of Iraqi society through the hospitals and health centres spread across Iraq's governorates; security — the Iraqi citizen has the right to be secure in his homeland, and the state provides security through the army and police forces that protect the citizen from internal and external dangers; and other services the state provides to the citizen, such as water, electricity, transport and communications services, roads, bridges, parks, gardens and libraries — as Iraq's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
