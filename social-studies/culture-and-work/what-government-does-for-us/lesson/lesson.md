# What Government Does for Us

## Sub lesson 1 — What Government Does for Us

Iraq Grade 5 Social Studies (printed pp. 90–93), teaches: The state has built many government service institutions to provide all the necessary services to the individuals of Iraqi society: education — our state is keen to provide education services free of charge to its pupil sons and to the students of all study stages, through the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research ("it is my right to learn"); health services — the state provides them to the members of Iraqi society through the hospitals and health centres spread across Iraq's governorates; security — the Iraqi citizen has the right to be secure in his homeland, and the state provides security through the army and police forces that protect the citizen from internal and external dangers; and other services the state provides to the citizen, such as water, electricity, transport and communications services, roads, bridges, parks, gardens and libraries. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: the state is defined as institutions that serve, its pillars are named homeland, people, federal authorities and sovereignty, and citizenship is taught as a two-way street — the state's services (free education first) on one side, and on the other a good citizen whose first quality is knowing his own abilities so he can fix his duties and his rights.
