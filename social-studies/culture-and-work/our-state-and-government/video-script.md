The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 60, teaches: In a democratic country the citizens themselves build their state and government (төр, засаг): the state protects the citizens, who elect it and support it, and obeying its laws and rules is doing something for one's own good; the citizens pay tax (татвар), the taxes form the state budget (төсөв), and the budget is spent for the people's good; against that stand the two columns — the state's duties (giving children the conditions to learn, health services, healthy food, a safe environment) and the pupil's duties beside them.

Mongolia's own way (Link book L1): Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for.
