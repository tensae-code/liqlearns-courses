# Our State And Government

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: in a democratic country the citizens themselves build their state and government (төр, засаг): the state protects the citizens, who elect it and support it, and obeying its laws and rules is doing something for one's own good; the citizens pay tax (татвар), the taxes form the state budget (төсөв), and the budget is spent for the people's good; against that stand the two columns — the state's duties (giving children the conditions to learn, health services, healthy food, a safe environment) and the pupil's duties beside them — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 17 "Our state and government" (Манай төр, засаг)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 60, teaches: In a democratic country the citizens themselves build their state and government (төр, засаг): the state protects the citizens, who elect it and support it, and obeying its laws and rules is doing something for one's own good; the citizens pay tax (татвар), the taxes form the state budget (төсөв), and the budget is spent for the people's good; against that stand the two columns — the state's duties (giving children the conditions to learn, health services, healthy food, a safe environment) and the pupil's duties beside them. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
