# Hands-on — Our State And Government

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 17 "Our state and government" (Манай төр, засаг)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: In a democratic country the citizens themselves build their state and government (төр, засаг): the state protects the citizens, who elect it and support it, and obeying its laws and rules is doing something for one's own good; the citizens pay tax (татвар), the taxes form the state budget (төсөв), and the budget is spent for the people's good; against that stand the two columns — the state's duties (giving children the conditions to learn, health services, healthy food, a safe environment) and the pupil's duties beside them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
