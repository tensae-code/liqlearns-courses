# Our State And Government — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 17 "Our state and government" (Манай төр, засаг) — In a democratic country the citizens themselves build their state and government (төр, засаг): the state protects the citizens, who elect it and support it, and obeying its laws and rules is doing something for one's own good; the citizens pay tax (татвар), the taxes form the state budget (төсөв), and the budget is spent for the people's good; against that stand the two columns — the state's duties (giving children the conditions to learn, health services, healthy food, a safe environment) and the pupil's duties beside them (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for (any close wording).**
