# Our State And Government — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: in a democratic country the citizens themselves build their state and government (төр, засаг): the state protects the citizens, who elect it and support it, and obeying its laws and rules is doing something for one's own good; the citizens pay tax (татвар), the taxes form the state budget (төсөв), and the budget is spent for the people's good; against that stand the two columns — the state's duties (giving children the conditions to learn, health services, healthy food, a safe environment) and the pupil's duties beside them.
