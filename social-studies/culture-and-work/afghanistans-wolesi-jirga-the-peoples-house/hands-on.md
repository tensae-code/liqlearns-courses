# Hands-on — Afghanistans Wolesi Jirga the Peoples House

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The people's house — 250 members elected by vote from all 34 provinces for five years, aged 25+; it approves laws and the budget, gives confidence to ministers and oversees the government, deciding by majority. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
