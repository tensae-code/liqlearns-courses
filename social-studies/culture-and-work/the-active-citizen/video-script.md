The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 5 Sosyal Bilgiler 2. Kitap, printed p. 24, teaches: a citizen is a state's member holding rights and responsibilities set by law; the active citizen notices problems and acts within the rules — reporting unhygienic canteen staff, pursuing a faulty product's consumer rights, reporting damage to the neighbourhood's historic fountain — as the careful carpenter and the helmet-wearing cyclist are "active" in their own fields

Turkey's own way: democracy is taught from the classroom outwards — the class first writes and votes its own rules item by item, then meets the same machinery at state scale (elections, petitions, CİMER), and an "active citizen" is defined by what she does about a dirty canteen, a faulty vacuum cleaner and a damaged historic fountain.
