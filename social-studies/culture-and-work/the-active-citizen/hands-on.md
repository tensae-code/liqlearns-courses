# Hands-on — The Active Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a citizen is a state's member holding rights and responsibilities set by law; the active citizen notices problems and acts within the rules — reporting unhygienic canteen staff, pursuing a faulty product's consumer rights, reporting damage to the neighbourhood's historic fountain — as the careful carpenter and the helmet-wearing cyclist are "active" in their own fields Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
