# Hands-on — Key Ideals of Democracy and the Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Key ideals of democratic governments; Constitution as the basis for citizenship
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The key ideals of democratic government — individual human dignity, liberty, justice, equality and the rule of law — at local, provincial and national level; and the role the Papua New Guinea Constitution plays in governing PNG and in citizens' day-to-day life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
