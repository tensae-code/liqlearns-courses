## Sub lesson 1 — Respecting and Cooperating with the Police

## Sub lesson 2 — Egypt's way — the book's own teaching way

Egypt's own way: the child's first government lesson is the map of their own governorate — the state is introduced as the administration that runs the child's home province, with its own emblem and feast day, before it is ever a parliament or a president.
