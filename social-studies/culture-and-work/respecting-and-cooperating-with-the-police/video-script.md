# Video script — Respecting and Cooperating with the Police

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Egypt's Grade 4 Social Studies T1, Unit 2 Lesson 3 "Service places in our city" (security services) (printed p. 55), teaches: We must respect police officers and appreciate their role in protecting us, cooperate with them, and avoid any act that would put us or others in danger, while the state keeps developing the police service.

Egypt's own way: the child's first government lesson is the map of their own governorate — the state is introduced as the administration that runs the child's home province, with its own emblem and feast day, before it is ever a parliament or a president.
