# Hands-on — Respecting and Cooperating with the Police

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: We must respect police officers and appreciate their role in protecting us, cooperate with them, and avoid any act that would put us or others in danger, while the state keeps developing the police service. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
