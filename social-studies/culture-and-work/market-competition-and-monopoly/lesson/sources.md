**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Armenia, Laos, Vietnam).**

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Economic & Legal Education): the macro topics are taught from Vietnam's own statistics and history — the CPI chart 2016–2021, the 775% inflation of 1986, COVID-era unemployment of 3.2%, the Q3 2022 labour-market bulletin — and the State's controlling role is quoted from the Law on the State Bank and the Labour Code.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 10 Social Science student): the senior Social Science course turns practical — after ideologies it teaches the market economy and then personal finance (choosing financial services, savings, credit, consumer rights, fraud) as life skills.

Laos' own way (Laos Grade 10 Business student): Economics is taught as the theory inside a "become a businessperson" course, drawn from village life — weaving, market stalls and repair shops sorted into producing, trading and services; the circular flow is drawn between households and the village business, and "produce for whom?" is answered with a Rolls-Royce.
