# Video script — Childrens Rights and Duties

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 8 Estudios Sociales 8vo EGB, printed p. 173, teaches: The rights and duties of girls, boys and adolescents in Ecuador's Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia (life, identity and citizenship, protection from exploitation, priority health, education and food, sport and recreation), how they compare with universal human rights (universal, indivisible, interdependent), and the public institutions that protect citizens (childhood courts, cantonal rights boards, labour inspectorate, police and prosecution).

Ecuador's own way: at Superior level the civics strand is built on Ecuador's own 2008 constitutional order — Buen Vivir (Sumak Kawsay) as the state's development idea, a plurinational and intercultural state, nature itself as a subject of rights, and children's rights taught from Ecuador's own Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia.
