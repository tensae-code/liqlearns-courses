# Hands-on — Childrens Rights and Duties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The rights and duties of girls, boys and adolescents in Ecuador's Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia (life, identity and citizenship, protection from exploitation, priority health, education and food, sport and recreation), how they compare with universal human rights (universal, indivisible, interdependent), and the public institutions that protect citizens (childhood courts, cantonal rights boards, labour inspectorate, police and prosecution). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
