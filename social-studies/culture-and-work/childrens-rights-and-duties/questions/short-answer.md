# Childrens Rights and Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Childrens Rights and Duties — The rights and duties of girls, boys and adolescents in Ecuador's Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia (life, identity and citizenship, protection from exploitation, priority health, education and food, sport and recreation), how they compare with universal human rights (universal, indivisible, interdependent), and the public institutions that protect citizens (childhood courts, cantonal rights boards, labour inspectorate, police and prosecution) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
