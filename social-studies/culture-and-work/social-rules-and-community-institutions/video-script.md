Nepal Grade 5 Teacher's Guide (2081) (guide p. 64), teaches: The guide's role-play lesson sorts the work of the community's own institutions (guthi, water-user groups and the like) and the social rules they keep [TEACHER].

Nepal's own way: the guide teaches community through doing — its scripts run on role-play (an uncle-and-father dialogue), song, drama of a local event, and group charts of the community's own work, with the teacher building the concept of community out of the children's answers before naming it.
