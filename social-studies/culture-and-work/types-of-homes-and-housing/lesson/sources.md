Ecuador's own way (Grade 2 book): Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. That way is stocked here as the way Ecuador teaches this thing.

Panama's own way (Panama Grade 1 Ciencias Sociales workbook): Grade 1 social studies is a home workbook ("Conéctate en casa") worked through with family; rights and duties are taught as "normas de convivencia" — the rules of living together — sorted by the child into family, school and community. That way is stocked here as the way Panama teaches this thing.

Angola's own way (Book 3): Estudo do Meio teaches the child's world through one named child at a time (Vihemba and her extended family, Mário and his school) — family includes grandparents, uncles and cousins who come to live in the house, and the home is located by bairro, município, street and house number so the child never gets lost.

Angola's own way (Book 4): Grade 2 widens each Grade 1 topic by one step — the family gains kinship names and needs, the school gains its parts and the care of school materials, and the book closes with two short themes that are pure community life: work, and transport and communications.
