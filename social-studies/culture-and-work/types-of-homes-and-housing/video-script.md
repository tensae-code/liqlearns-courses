# Video script — Types of Homes and Housing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador's Grade 2 book teaches homes as geography: children look at the viviendas around them — the kinds of houses people live in and their characteristics — and learn that a home's shape and materials answer to the place, the weather and the family's life. Naming the types of homes is the child's first read of their own settlement.

Ecuador learns this inside its own books' way: Geography is taught inside three thematic sections that always start from the child's family, school and province and widen to Ecuador and South America; heavy use of hazard maps (floods, landslides, volcanoes) and emergency plans from Grade 2, reflecting Ecuador's volcanic / El Niño risk profile. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
