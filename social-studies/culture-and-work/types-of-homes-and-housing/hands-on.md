# Hands-on — Types of Homes and Housing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Look at the homes near you
Walk a short way and note the kinds of homes you pass — what each is made of and how it is shaped, as the lesson directs.

## My home's characteristics
Describe your own home in three sentences: its kind, its materials, and one characteristic that fits the place you live in.
