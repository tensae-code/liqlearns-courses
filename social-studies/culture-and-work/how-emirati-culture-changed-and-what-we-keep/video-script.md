# Video script — How Emirati Culture Changed and What We Keep

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G6 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 43, teaches: studying the faces of change in Emirati culture over time and its causes, and the ways cultural bodies can preserve the heritage through that change — what of the past must be kept, and what may be learned from it for the future

United Arab Emirates' own way: the federation's diversity is taught as a museum subject — the child learns the words cultural diversity and inclusion by walking the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization, and the story lesson ends on the line that Emirati society today is a mixture of people from all over the world, bound to them and respecting their cultures.
