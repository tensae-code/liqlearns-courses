Nepal's own way: Grade 7 completes the state — elements of the state, democratic government, our constitution, human and women's rights, the provincial government's functions and the security agencies — then turns outward: the United Nations, the proper use of social media, and the world's current events close the book. Grade 9 gives civic awareness its fullest form yet — constitution from first introduction through the constituent assembly, fundamental rights, citizen identity and duties, good governance and democratic conduct — and ends the values unit with a culture of peace.
