# Video script — Democracy and Interculturality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 8 Estudios Sociales 8vo EGB, printed p. 162, teaches: Building the idea of democracy from freedom, government, human rights, solidarity, communication and equity; Athens 508 BC as its origin; democratic acts of the Ecuadorian state (free expression, human rights, social equity); whether democracy in Ecuador is vulnerable; media monopoly and free expression.

Ecuador's own way: at Superior level the civics strand is built on Ecuador's own 2008 constitutional order — Buen Vivir (Sumak Kawsay) as the state's development idea, a plurinational and intercultural state, nature itself as a subject of rights, and children's rights taught from Ecuador's own Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia.
