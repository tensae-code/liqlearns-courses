# Hands-on — Our Folk Tales

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 11 "Mongolian tales and legends" (Монгол үлгэр домог)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every people keeps its treasure in tales — wishes, knowledge, life's lessons and ideals carried from the ancestors; the kinds of tales (animal, magic, heroic, everyday, humorous) and the honoured storyteller (үлгэрч), listened to in silence after the evening milking; the tale of the bat, who lied to both the beasts and the birds and now hides from both. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
