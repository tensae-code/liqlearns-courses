# Our Folk Tales — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Every people keeps its treasure in tales — wishes, knowledge, life's lessons and ideals carried from the ancestors; the kinds of tales (animal, magic, heroic, everyday, humorous) and the honoured storyteller (үлгэрч), listened to in silence after the evening milking; the tale of the bat, who lied to both the beasts and the birds and now hides from both  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
