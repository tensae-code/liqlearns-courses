# Our Folk Tales — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: every people keeps its treasure in tales — wishes, knowledge, life's lessons and ideals carried from the ancestors; the kinds of tales (animal, magic, heroic, everyday, humorous) and the honoured storyteller (үлгэрч), listened to in silence after the evening milking; the tale of the bat, who lied to both the beasts and the birds and now hides from both.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
