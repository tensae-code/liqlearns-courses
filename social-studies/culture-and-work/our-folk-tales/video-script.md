# Video script — Our Folk Tales

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 39, teaches: Every people keeps its treasure in tales — wishes, knowledge, life's lessons and ideals carried from the ancestors; the kinds of tales (animal, magic, heroic, everyday, humorous) and the honoured storyteller (үлгэрч), listened to in silence after the evening milking; the tale of the bat, who lied to both the beasts and the birds and now hides from both.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
