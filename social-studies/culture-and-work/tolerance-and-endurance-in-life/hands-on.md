# Hands-on — Tolerance and Endurance in Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tolerance in Life
Work through this part with your hands: tolerance and endurance when facing life's challenges — accept the challenge, find a solution, seek advice, never harm yourself — and motivating others through your own experience, keeping hope and celebrating victory over challenges. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Being Tolerant
Work through this part with your hands: tolerance lets a person persevere through challenges; unaddressed challenges harm the body and the mind (fear, loss of confidence, despair), but challenges are part of life — do not despair, learn from elders, teachers and counsellors and from others who overcame the same challenges, and turn a challenge into an opportunity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
