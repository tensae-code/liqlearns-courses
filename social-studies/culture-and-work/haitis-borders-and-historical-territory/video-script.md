# Video script — Haitis Borders and Historical Territory

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 8 EC1 8e Fondements (printed p. 13), teaches: how Haiti's territory and its land and maritime frontiers were historically built, and the border as the line between an inside and an outside that people and goods cross.

Haiti's own way: The Grade 7 nation/state sequence is deepened by returning to the territory itself: pupils draw Haiti from memory, then test their drawing against the Constitution's own definition of the territory (article 8) and against the island shared with a second state.
