Turkey Grade 6 Sosyal Bilgiler 2. Kitap, printed p. 24, teaches: when the Ahıska Turks received citizenship they gained the whole set of rights and duties at once; and order holds because rules bind everyone alike — the driver who misused an emergency light lost the privilege and was fined, whoever he was; rights used properly and duties done keep social order standing

Turkey's own way: the Grade 6 democracy area asks who actually moves a government — the people's needs, parties, civil society, media and the kamuoyu (public opinion) they form together — and then follows citizenship itself online, where rights are now exercised through a screen and the active citizen is the one who still writes the petition.
