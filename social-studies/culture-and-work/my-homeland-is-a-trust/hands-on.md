# Hands-on — My Homeland is a Trust

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My Homeland is a Trust
Work through this part with your hands: The homeland as an أمانة — something held in trust, to be guarded and handed on unharmed; responsibility taught as safekeeping, not slogans. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Oman's way
Say how Oman's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
