Pakistan's Grade 4 SS4 (printed pp. 37–39), teaches: Ch. 3 Provincial Government and Organs of Provincial Government — teaches: the province runs the services people touch daily (schools, health, law and order) through its own three organs — the Provincial Assembly (Punjab's has 371 members), the Chief Minister and Provincial Cabinet, the Governor as the province's head, and the High Court (Lahore High Court pictured).

Pakistan's own way: citizenship is taught before everything else — a child first learns what a citizen, a global citizen and a digital citizen is, then Pakistan's own 1973 Constitution, its federal and provincial organs with real seat counts, and only then history, geography and economics.
