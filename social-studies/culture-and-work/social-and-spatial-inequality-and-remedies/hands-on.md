# Hands-on — Social and Spatial Inequality and Remedies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: VI.03 사회 및 공간 불평등 현상과 개선 방안, VI.3 사회 및 공간 불평등 현상과 정의로운 사회, 사회정의와 불평등 3 사회 및 공간 불평등 현상과 개선 방안 — teaches: social and spatial (regional) inequality — its faces and the remedies proposed for it; social and spatial inequality, and the institutions and practical plans for a just society; social and spatial inequality and the plans that improve it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
