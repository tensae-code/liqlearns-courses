# Hands-on — Peaceful Coexistence in the Maya Culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 2 framing "La convivencia pacífica en la cultura maya"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales Segundo ciclo TG, printed p. 43, teaches: coexistence as dialogue and gentle speech; principles and values of peaceful coexistence in family, society and community; equity and complementarity between men and women, boys and girls; Maya values lived in school and community spaces. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: coexistence is taught from the Maya cosmovision — its four threads are respect, gratitude, service and care for nature — and classroom rules are not imposed but built by the pupils as agreements.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
