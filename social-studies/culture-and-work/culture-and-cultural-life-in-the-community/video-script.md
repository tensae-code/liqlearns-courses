# Video script — Culture And Cultural Life In The Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Angola Estudo do Meio G4 (printed p. 56), teaches: Culture as part of a community's life and services — the cultural activities a community keeps and shares.

Angola's own way: Grade 4 names the idea itself — instituições públicas: buildings that belong to no private person but serve everyone — and defines quality of life as a checklist a community can judge itself by (education, health, housing, food, hygiene, culture, leisure), ending on the shared duties of sanitation and water.
