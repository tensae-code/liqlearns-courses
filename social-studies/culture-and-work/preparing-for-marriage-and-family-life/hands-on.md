# Hands-on — Preparing for Marriage and Family Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Marriage is a legal union and one of the accepted institutions of society, and a successful marriage must be prepared for — choose a partner with common interests, ideals and values, learn about caring for children, be considerate, honest and dependable; marital happiness is built by being determined to succeed, being realistic, adjusting to the other person, nourishing love and resolving conflicts early and wisely. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
