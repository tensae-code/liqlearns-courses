# Preparing for Marriage and Family Life

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: marriage is a legal union and one of the accepted institutions of society, and a successful marriage must be prepared for — choose a partner with common interests, ideals and values, learn about caring for children, be considerate, honest and dependable; marital happiness is built by being determined to succeed, being realistic, adjusting to the other person, nourishing love and resolving conflicts early and wisely — as Guyana's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Preparing for marriage

Guyana Grade 9 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 3 (printed pp. 23–24), teaches: Marriage is a legal union and one of the accepted institutions of society, and a successful marriage must be prepared for — choose a partner with common interests, ideals and values, learn about caring for children, be considerate, honest and dependable; marital happiness is built by being determined to succeed, being realistic, adjusting to the other person, nourishing love and resolving conflicts early and wisely. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: the secondary book teaches the same citizenship at national scale — CARICOM as the region's own institution, the family as society's oldest institution, women's equality written into the 1980 Constitution and the Acts of the 1990s, and the state answering pollution through the Environmental Protection Agency.
