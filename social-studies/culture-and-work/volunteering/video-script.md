The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies (printed p. 139), teaches: Voluntary work is that man devotes part of his own time to performing a public work, by his own desire and without return, for humanitarian or social aims, among them spreading cooperation among others and strengthening the bonds of love and familiarity among society's members; it also develops the individual's personality and increases his self-confidence, besides investing time and spending it in everything useful; and its fields include the social field (the humanitarian, charitable works), the educational field (giving lessons in literacy-eradication campaigns, or developmental and awareness lessons), and the environmental field (campaigns for cleaning the neighbourhoods, streets or public gardens, and learning the sound ways of disposing of waste).

Iraq's own way: values are defined first as a system — standards that steer behaviour and spread peace "regardless of sex, religion, nationality, language or race" — and citizenship is cashed out in named rights (education, work, candidacy, the vote, freedom of opinion and belief), while the internet chapter warns in plain terms that the open network "is governed by no restrictions or conditions".
