# Hands-on — Volunteering

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Voluntary work is that man devotes part of his own time to performing a public work, by his own desire and without return, for humanitarian or social aims, among them spreading cooperation among others and strengthening the bonds of love and familiarity among society's members; it also develops the individual's personality and increases his self-confidence, besides investing time and spending it in everything useful; and its fields include the social field (the humanitarian, charitable works), the educational field (giving lessons in literacy-eradication campaigns, or developmental and awareness lessons), and the environmental field (campaigns for cleaning the neighbourhoods, streets or public gardens, and learning the sound ways of disposing of waste). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
