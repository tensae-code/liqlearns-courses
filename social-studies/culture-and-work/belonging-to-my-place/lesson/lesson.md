## Sub lesson 6 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: citizenship is its own school subject from age six, built as widening circles — the child first names herself, her feelings and her family, and only then her neighbourhood; the state prints its map and anthem at the front of the teacher's guide, and the fourth unit-component is always participation, not obedience. term 2 completes the widening circles — after self, family and neighbourhood come the homeland (its flag raised every morning, its Sultan named with a prayer, its Renaissance told as one man's building) and then the whole world, where the Omani child is taught that difference itself is what makes a good citizen. even inside the combined Social Studies book, the identity strand keeps its own unit with its own name — belonging and loyalty are taught as concepts with definitions, facts and values (among them: respecting the regulations and laws, preserving Omani culture and civilisation, volunteering), not left as decoration on the history lessons. the world unit is taught as three verbs of the heart — communicate, interact, empathise — while the homeland unit grounds belonging in heritage, folk tales and the Omani personalities who shaped history. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
