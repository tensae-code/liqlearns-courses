# Belonging to My Place — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The unit is passed not on facts but on a feeling — the child ends it belonging to the place she lives in, the second component of citizenship practised at street level.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: Belonging, practised at the scale of the whole country — the second component of citizenship, aimed this term at the homeland and its symbols.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: The two concepts defined and argued — belonging grows from loving the homeland and prizing its history and civilisation, drives work for its sake, binds its sons together and guards its interests; the family's and the school's roles in growing it are discussed outright.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: What belonging looks like in practice — diligence in study and honesty in work as the road to the homeland's progress, respecting national occasions and taking part in them, keeping Omani dress, language, customs and traditions, and respecting the regulations and laws.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's fifth part teaches: Belonging at homeland scale for a seven-year-old — describing the homeland's features, showing its heritage, and taking pride in the Omani personalities who influenced its history.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: True — Oman's student books were not held; it stands on teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere, as named in lesson/sources.md.**
