## Sub lesson 1 — Self Development

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 190–193), teaches: Self-development as one's efforts to develop skills, abilities and talents by thinking and creativity toward a successful character; its methods — learning, positivity, ambition, responsibility (bearing the consequences of one's words and acts), planning, developing abilities, choosing friends well and using time; learning from mistakes and low marks instead of despairing, with King Abdulaziz as the model of a character that transformed Saudi society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
