# Hands-on — Self Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: self-development as one's efforts to develop skills, abilities and talents by thinking and creativity toward a successful character; its methods — learning, positivity, ambition, responsibility (bearing the consequences of one's words and acts), planning, developing abilities, choosing friends well and using time; learning from mistakes and low marks instead of despairing, with King Abdulaziz as the model of a character that transformed Saudi society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
