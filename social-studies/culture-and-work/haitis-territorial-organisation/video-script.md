# Video script — Haitis Territorial Organisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 7 EC1 7AF (printed p. 11), teaches: Haiti's territorial organisation read off the school's own postal address — département, arrondissement, commune, section communale — and mapped department by department.

Haiti's own way: Citizenship is built from Haiti's own foundations — the nation defined by shared identity, history, territory, values and institutions and the will to live together (unity, solidarity, social cohesion), read through the Haitian Constitution itself in French and Creole, and practised on the symbols and memory places of the pupils' own locality.
