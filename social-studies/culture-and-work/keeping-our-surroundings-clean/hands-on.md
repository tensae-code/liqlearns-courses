# Hands-on — Keeping Our Surroundings Clean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Keeping the classroom, school and community clean and tidy is everyone's job, not a punishment — the class takes responsibility for a place and shares the tasks. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
