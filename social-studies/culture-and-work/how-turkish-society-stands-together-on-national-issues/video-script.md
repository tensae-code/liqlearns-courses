Turkey Grade 7 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 40, teaches: a national issue — anything touching the nation's survival, identity and independence, from disasters and economic hardship to science and sport — concerns every individual, not only rulers; Turkish society's rooted habit is to lock together around a common will, as in the National Struggle's "Independence or Death"

Turkey's own way: the last middle-school year opens by widening the circle one final time — from how we talk to each other in a group, to the equality a constitution owes a child in a wheelchair, to the whole society closing ranks on a national issue, and finally to the country's own seat at the world's problem-solving table.
