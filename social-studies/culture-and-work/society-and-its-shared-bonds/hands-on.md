# Hands-on — Society and Its Shared Bonds

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Society is a group of individuals who live on one land and are bound by shared ties; the most important shared ties that gather the sons of Iraqi society are the homeland, the shared history, the shared language, the shared interests, and the shared customs and traditions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Cultural diversity — each nationality and sect has its own cultural heritage, and this diversity strengthened the brotherhood among the sons of Iraqi society, for they share the unity of origin, which is the homeland; customs and traditions — the intellectual and moral traits the sons of society inherited from their ancestors, stemming from Islam and the other heavenly religions, never a barrier in the way of Iraqi cultural unity; language and religion — the linguistic diversity (Arabic, Kurdish, Turkmen, Syriac and others), and the constitution stipulated respect for all the Iraqi religions and sects, showing the constitution's respect for the rights and freedoms of all spectra of Iraqi society; the shared history — the sons of Iraqi society stand in one rank in facing the challenges and dangers, as in all Iraq's spectra standing together in fighting terrorism; and tolerance and rejecting violence — seeking peaceful coexistence with others in dignity and peace, far from violence, rejecting disputes, adopting a culture of dialogue with others, and respecting the other's opinion. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
