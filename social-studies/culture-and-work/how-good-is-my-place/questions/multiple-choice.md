# How Good is My Place — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) How good is my place!  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) How good is my place!**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) pride in the good of one's own place  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) pride in the good of one's own place**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches Pride in the good of one's own place.  b) Nepal's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) This lesson teaches Pride in the good of one's own place.**
