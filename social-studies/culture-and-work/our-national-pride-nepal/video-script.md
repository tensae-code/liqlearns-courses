# Video script — Our National Pride Nepal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal Grade 5 My Social Studies and Creative Arts (printed p. 18), teaches: Our country as our pride.

Nepal Grade 6 Social Studies and Human Value Education (printed p. 34), teaches: What we are proud of as a nation.

Nepal Grade 10 Social Studies (printed p. 15), teaches: The things Nepal is proud of as a nation — its symbols, heroes and heritage.

Nepal's own way: Grade 5's civic sense reaches the district — the District Coordination Committee and its functions get two full lessons — and the state itself arrives inside the history unit: head of state against head of government, through the 2062–65 BS movement and the republic. Grade 6 is where the federation becomes concrete — rural municipality against municipality, each with its services listed — and where conflict itself becomes a school subject: a lesson on disputes and a lesson on resolving them close the social-problems unit. the Grade 10 book is organised as the citizen's widening world — society and identity, social values, social problems, then a full civic-awareness unit on Nepal's own federal legislature, executive and judiciary, closing with Nepal's foreign policy and the UN.
