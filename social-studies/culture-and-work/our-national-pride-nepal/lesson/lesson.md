## Sub lesson 4 — Nepal's way — the book's own teaching way

Nepal's own way: Grade 5's civic sense reaches the district — the District Coordination Committee and its functions get two full lessons — and the state itself arrives inside the history unit: head of state against head of government, through the 2062–65 BS movement and the republic. Grade 6 is where the federation becomes concrete — rural municipality against municipality, each with its services listed — and where conflict itself becomes a school subject: a lesson on disputes and a lesson on resolving them close the social-problems unit. the Grade 10 book is organised as the citizen's widening world — society and identity, social values, social problems, then a full civic-awareness unit on Nepal's own federal legislature, executive and judiciary, closing with Nepal's foreign policy and the UN. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
