# Hands-on — Planning and Its Goals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: planning as the organised preparation that sets future tasks, their times and how to accomplish them; why we plan — success and excellence, knowing what must be done and when, building the successful national character that keeps pace with Vision 2030's call to achievement and perfection, and enjoying time while keeping health through regular sleep and meals. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
