## Sub lesson 1 — Planning and Its Goals

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 78–80), teaches: Planning as the organised preparation that sets future tasks, their times and how to accomplish them; why we plan — success and excellence, knowing what must be done and when, building the successful national character that keeps pace with Vision 2030's call to achievement and perfection, and enjoying time while keeping health through regular sleep and meals. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
