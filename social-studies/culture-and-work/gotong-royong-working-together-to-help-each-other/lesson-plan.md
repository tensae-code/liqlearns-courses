# Lesson plan — Gotong Royong Working Together to Help Each Other

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Gotong Royong Working Together to Help Each Other
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Indonesia, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bab 4 B. Aku Suka Bergotong Royong, Bab 4 C. Bekerja Sama Menjaga Lingkungan, Bab 3 B. Gotong Royong, Bab 4 B. Gotong Royong di Lingkungan Sekitar, Bab 4 C. Praktik Gotong Royong di Lingkungan Sekitar, Bab 7 A.1 Gotong Royong dalam Mewujudkan Bela Negara, Bab 7 A. Pentingnya Melaksanakan Gotong Royong, Bab 4 D/F. Materi Esensial dan Panduan Pembelajaran: Aku dan Lingkunganku, Panduan Khusus Bab 3: Kerja Sama di Lingkunganku, Panduan Khusus Bab 4: Aku dan Lingkungan Sekitarku, Panduan Khusus Bab 7: Menjaga Persatuan dan Kesatuan dengan Gotong Royong, Bab 4 D, marakka bola case — teaches: gotong royong — Indonesians' mutual-cooperation way of working together and helping neighbours; working together (gotong royong) to look after the environment; gotong royong as the working-together system of the neighbourhood; gotong royong in the surrounding environment, opening with Mohammad Hatta as exemplar; gotong royong in practice — a "my city clean" campaign designed and run by pupils, and traditional games played together; gotong royong as bela negara — defending the country by working together; why carrying out gotong royong keeps the unity and oneness (persatuan dan kesatuan) of the nation; essential material and guidance for neighbourhood, gotong royong and caring for the school; essential material and guidance for diversity, gotong royong and unity; essential material and guidance for my region, its government offices and gotong royong in practice; guidance for keeping unity through gotong royong, including gotong royong as bela negara; marakka bola as gotong royong in its strongest form — voluntarily lifting a neighbour's whole house, regardless of difference — as Indonesia's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
