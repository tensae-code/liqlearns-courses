# Hands-on — Duties of a Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Duties of a Citizen 1
Work through this part with your hands: The matching half — the child applies her duties toward her homeland and appreciates why rights and duties must be honoured together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Duties of a Citizen 2
Work through this part with your hands: The matching duties inside the family, stated by the child herself. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Duties of a Citizen 3
Work through this part with your hands: Rights and duties in the neighbourhood framed as a balance — what I need from my الحي against what I commit to it; taking matched by giving. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Oman's way
