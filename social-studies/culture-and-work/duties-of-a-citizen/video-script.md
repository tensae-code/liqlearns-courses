# Video script — Duties of a Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Oman Grade 3 Identity & Citizenship TG T2, printed pp. 29–34, teaches: The matching half — the child applies her duties toward her homeland and appreciates why rights and duties must be honoured together.

Oman Grade 3 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 55–60, teaches: The matching duties inside the family, stated by the child herself.

Oman Grade 3 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 67–85, teaches: Rights and duties in the neighbourhood framed as a balance — what I need from my الحي against what I commit to it; taking matched by giving.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 3, Term 2): at Grade 3 the subject turns from feelings to law-shaped habits — rights first, then duties, then the homeland held as a trust (أمانة); and the same rights-and-duties pair is then taught again at world scale, where equality, safety and being heard are the child's global rights.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 3, Term 1): rights and duties are taught at family scale before homeland scale — and the family unit ends with the children drafting a ميثاق, a charter, for their own family and pledging to keep it: constitution-making at the kitchen table.
