## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 5 overview: gender equality

South Sudan Primary 3 P3 Social Studies TG, printed p. 43, teaches: pupils should come to understand the need for gender equality in their community, defining examples and situations where this attitude is particularly relevant (guide-only teaching; the pupil's book's Unit 5 has no gender part). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Activity 9: gender equality defined

South Sudan Primary 5 P5 Social Studies SB, printed p. 57, teaches: gender equality is where everyone is regarded as equal whether a boy or a girl and male or female, and the class surveys why the country should embrace human rights and gender equality (the gender-equality lesson is filed NEW from Book 6 in this wave). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 3 overview: what underpins peaceful coexistence

South Sudan Primary 5 P5 Social Studies TG, printed p. 34, teaches: the guide adds that respect for human rights and gender equality underpins peaceful coexistence, including challenging gender stereotyping (the gender-equality lesson is filed NEW from Book 6 in this wave). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 4 Activities 1–2 Meaning and effects of gender equality

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 83, teaches: gender is the state of being male or female and gender equality is having the same rights, status and opportunities regardless of one's gender; the story of Mr Adek, a village elder who educates all his children against his community's tradition, treats his wife as an equal partner and wills inheritance to daughters and sons alike, carries the equal division of labour in the family (the gender-equality lesson is filed NEW from Book 6 in this wave). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 5 — Activity 8 Benefits of gender equality

South Sudan Primary 7 P7 Social Studies SB, printed p. 96, teaches: equal representation with women actively involved, equal access to job opportunities and empowerment of women, improvement of democracy, and promotion of peace and reduction of violence (the gender-equality lesson is filed NEW from Book 6 in this wave). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 6 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way: The guide widens the pupil's book in two places — national symbols are defined as the items that bring unity in a country (the presidency itself counted among them), and the peace unit is extended to the risks of mines and unexploded ordnance left by the conflict, and to gender equality in the community. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
