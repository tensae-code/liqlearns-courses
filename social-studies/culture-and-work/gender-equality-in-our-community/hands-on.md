# Hands-on — Gender Equality in Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 5 overview: gender equality
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: pupils should come to understand the need for gender equality in their community, defining examples and situations where this attitude is particularly relevant (guide-only teaching; the pupil's book's Unit 5 has no gender part). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Activity 9: gender equality defined
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: gender equality is where everyone is regarded as equal whether a boy or a girl and male or female, and the class surveys why the country should embrace human rights and gender equality (the gender-equality lesson is filed NEW from Book 6 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unit 3 overview: what underpins peaceful coexistence
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the guide adds that respect for human rights and gender equality underpins peaceful coexistence, including challenging gender stereotyping (the gender-equality lesson is filed NEW from Book 6 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Unit 4 Activities 1–2 Meaning and effects of gender equality
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: gender is the state of being male or female and gender equality is having the same rights, status and opportunities regardless of one's gender; the story of Mr Adek, a village elder who educates all his children against his community's tradition, treats his wife as an equal partner and wills inheritance to daughters and sons alike, carries the equal division of labour in the family (the gender-equality lesson is filed NEW from Book 6 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Activity 8 Benefits of gender equality
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: equal representation with women actively involved, equal access to job opportunities and empowerment of women, improvement of democracy, and promotion of peace and reduction of violence (the gender-equality lesson is filed NEW from Book 6 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
