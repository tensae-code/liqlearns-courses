## Sub lesson 1 — Interior Ministry Agencies

Saudi Arabia Grade 5 Social Studies (printed p. 111), teaches: The Interior Ministry's agencies and what each does — the Police (keeping security and applying the regulations), Highway Security, the Traffic Department, the Anti-Narcotics Directorate, Civil Defence (fire procedures and rescue — call 998 for a fire), the Border Guard, Passports (issuing passports and residents' affairs), Prisons, and Civil Affairs (the national identity card and registering births). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 5 book teaches security and state institutions as service — security is defined first, the citizen is named the first line of security, the security agencies are listed one by one with their tasks, state institutions are split into public and private, and each region's Emir receives citizens and hears their needs. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
