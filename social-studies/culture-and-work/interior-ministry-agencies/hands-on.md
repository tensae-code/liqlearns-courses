# Hands-on — Interior Ministry Agencies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Interior Ministry's agencies and what each does — the Police (keeping security and applying the regulations), Highway Security, the Traffic Department, the Anti-Narcotics Directorate, Civil Defence (fire procedures and rescue — call 998 for a fire), the Border Guard, Passports (issuing passports and residents' affairs), Prisons, and Civil Affairs (the national identity card and registering births). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
