# Video script — Trade Routes as Cultural Channels

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G4 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 21, teaches: the old trade routes did not carry goods only — along them travelled art, music, languages, religions and ideas between the regions of the world, so trade itself became a channel of culture

United Arab Emirates' own way: in the Moral Education course, nine-year-olds are trained as peer mediators with a named toolkit — settlement, concession, active listening — and the five ways of facing a dispute are taught through animals (the shark forces, the turtle withdraws, the bear yields, the fox compromises, the owl solves); responsibility is taught by a lost wallet with 800 dirhams in it.
