## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: in the Moral Education course, nine-year-olds are trained as peer mediators with a named toolkit — settlement, concession, active listening — and the five ways of facing a dispute are taught through animals (the shark forces, the turtle withdraws, the bear yields, the fox compromises, the owl solves); responsibility is taught by a lost wallet with 800 dirhams in it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
