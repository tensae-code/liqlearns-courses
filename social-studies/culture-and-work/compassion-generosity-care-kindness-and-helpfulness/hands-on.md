# Hands-on — Compassion Generosity Care Kindness and Helpfulness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Compassion as a week of practice — generosity (the Giving Tree read whole, then judged in four everyday choices of one's own), care (the son who took his old father to dinner; my caring acts written down day by day), kindness (the Kindness Tree — a leaf for every kind act, a fruit when three are gathered) and helpfulness (Mother Teresa's words on helping anyone; helping at home, marked by parents). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
