## Sub lesson 2 — Morocco's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way

Morocco's own way: the second collège year turns citizenship into the machinery of the state itself — the constitution, the King, parliament, government and courts, then the citizen's constitutional rights, parties, unions, press, local democracy and associations. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
