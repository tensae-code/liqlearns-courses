Dominican Republic's own way: Grade 3 ends the book with a whole annex most countries never give 8-year-olds — the Constitución Dominicana Infantil, the country's constitution rewritten for children, grouped as civil, social and cultural rights; and its democracy unit starts from the school's own consejo de curso election. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
