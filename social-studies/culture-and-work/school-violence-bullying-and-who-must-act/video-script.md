# Video script — School Violence Bullying and Who Must Act

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 59–76, teaches: school violence and bullying studied through a real Guatemalan case (CERIGUA), the effects on victims, the duties of parents, teachers and directors in prevention, the pupil's right to participate under the school rules, and MINEDUC's National Strategy for Peaceful Coexistence and Violence Prevention.

Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.
