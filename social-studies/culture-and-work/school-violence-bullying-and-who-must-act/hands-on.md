# Hands-on — School Violence Bullying and Who Must Act

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 4 "Violencia escolar"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 59–76, teaches: school violence and bullying studied through a real Guatemalan case (CERIGUA), the effects on victims, the duties of parents, teachers and directors in prevention, the pupil's right to participate under the school rules, and MINEDUC's National Strategy for Peaceful Coexistence and Violence Prevention. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
