# Hands-on — Our Duty to the Martyrs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: our duty toward the martyrs of duty and homeland — pray for them, be proud of them, help their families and never forget their sacrifices; the state keeps dedicated administrations for martyrs' families in the defence, interior and guard ministries, giving them priority in services and help with education. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
