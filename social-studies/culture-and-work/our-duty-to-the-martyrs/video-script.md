# Video script — Our Duty to the Martyrs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 5 Social Studies (printed pp. 57–58), teaches: Our duty toward the martyrs of duty and homeland — pray for them, be proud of them, help their families and never forget their sacrifices; the state keeps dedicated administrations for martyrs' families in the defence, interior and guard ministries, giving them priority in services and help with education.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 5 book teaches security and state institutions as service — security is defined first, the citizen is named the first line of security, the security agencies are listed one by one with their tasks, state institutions are split into public and private, and each region's Emir receives citizens and hears their needs.
