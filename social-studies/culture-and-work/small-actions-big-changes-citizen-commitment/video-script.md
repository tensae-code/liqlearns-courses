# Video script — Small Actions Big Changes Citizen Commitment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 6° Primaria student guide (printed p. 64), teaches: A pupil campaign, "small actions, big changes", and my citizen commitment — putting rights and duties into concrete actions.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 5): the primary cycle closes by widening the circle — from how my community is organised and who organises it (juntas de vecinos, authorities, institutions), through tax duties written into the Constitution and the Judicial Power, to the pupil as an "ambassador of peace" in home, school and community.
