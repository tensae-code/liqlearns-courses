## Sub lesson 1 — 3.4 Let us distinguish between the good and the bad

This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way

Sri Lanka's own way: civics starts inside the child's own school — its history, its people and their duties, its rules, symbols and the pupil's own rights — then widens to the region (the Grama Niladhari ladder up to the province, the officers and councils that serve it) and closes with the good citizen's virtues: polite speech, listening, discipline, telling good from bad, using resources frugally, guarding public property and preserving the local identity with pride. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
