# Video script — National Visions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 7 Term 2 (printed pp. 72–73), teaches: What a national vision is — a long-term plan setting a country's development path for future generations — the traits of a successful vision, and the shared goals of the Arab visions.

Kuwait's own way: Grade 7 Term 2 teaches the pupil to read their region's troubles whole — political (occupation, separatism, nuclear risk), social (addiction, rumours, bullying) — and then to answer them with plans: a national vision with traits and shared Arab goals, Kuwait's own 2035 vision, and Arab integration through named organisations; the values unit grounds it at home — the grandmother who runs the family's learning, and the neighbour who feeds the absent diver's family.
