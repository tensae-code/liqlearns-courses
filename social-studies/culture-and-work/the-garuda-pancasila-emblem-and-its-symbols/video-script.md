Indonesia's Grade 1 PP-G1 student (printed p. 100); Grade 2 PP-G2 student (printed p. 40); Grade 1 PP-G1 teacher (printed p. 112), teaches: Bab 3 C. Aku Mengenal Simbol pada Lambang Garuda Pancasila, Bab 2 A. Lambang Pancasila, Bab 3 D/F. Materi Esensial dan Panduan Pembelajaran: Aku Mengenal Indonesia — teaches: the Garuda Pancasila emblem and what each symbol on it stands for; the Pancasila emblem on the Garuda shield, part by part; essential material and guidance for flag, anthem and Garuda Pancasila symbols.

Indonesia's own way: Pancasila values are taught from the child's own body, friends and family outward — five recurring child characters from Papua, Bali, Jakarta, Sulawesi and Kalimantan carry every chapter, so national unity is shown as named children from different islands. Grade 2 turns Pancasila from a symbol to copy into behaviour at home — each principle (sila) is matched to a picture-symbol on the Garuda shield and then to a concrete act a 7-year-old can do in the family. the guide frames the whole subject through the Profil Pelajar Pancasila — six learner dimensions (faith and noble character, global diversity, gotong royong, independence, critical reasoning, creativity) that every chapter must build.
