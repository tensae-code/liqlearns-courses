## Sub lesson 2 — Indonesia's way — the books' own teaching way

Indonesia's own way: Pancasila values are taught from the child's own body, friends and family outward — five recurring child characters from Papua, Bali, Jakarta, Sulawesi and Kalimantan carry every chapter, so national unity is shown as named children from different islands. Grade 2 turns Pancasila from a symbol to copy into behaviour at home — each principle (sila) is matched to a picture-symbol on the Garuda shield and then to a concrete act a 7-year-old can do in the family. the guide frames the whole subject through the Profil Pelajar Pancasila — six learner dimensions (faith and noble character, global diversity, gotong royong, independence, critical reasoning, creativity) that every chapter must build. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
