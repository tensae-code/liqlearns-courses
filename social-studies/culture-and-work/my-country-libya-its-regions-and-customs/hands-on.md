# Hands-on — My Country Libya Its Regions and Customs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Libya's regions and their dress, dishes and customs — and how that diversity is the country's wealth. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Libya's way
Say how Libya's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
