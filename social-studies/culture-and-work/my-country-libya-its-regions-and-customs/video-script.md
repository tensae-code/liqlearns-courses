# Video script — My Country Libya Its Regions and Customs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Libya Grade 5 National Education G5 (printed p. 8), teaches: Libya's regions differ in dress, dishes and customs, and that diversity is the country's wealth — each region of my homeland has customs and traditions that distinguish it.

Libya's own way: Grade 5 is a pure values book — eight lessons, each a single civic virtue (equality, justice, solidarity, tolerance, accepting the other, dialogue), each anchored in a real instrument a child can hold: the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, UNESCO's 1995 Declaration on Tolerance, and the Libyan Scouts' "A World of Peace for Children" charter, which the pupil signs.
