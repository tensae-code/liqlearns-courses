# My Country Libya Its Regions and Customs

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Libya's regions and their dress, dishes and customs — and how that diversity is the country's wealth — as Libya's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — My Country Libya

Libya Grade 5 National Education G5 (printed p. 8), teaches: Libya's regions differ in dress, dishes and customs, and that diversity is the country's wealth — each region of my homeland has customs and traditions that distinguish it. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Libya's way — the book's own teaching way

Libya's own way: Grade 5 is a pure values book — eight lessons, each a single civic virtue (equality, justice, solidarity, tolerance, accepting the other, dialogue), each anchored in a real instrument a child can hold: the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the Convention on the Rights of the Child, UNESCO's 1995 Declaration on Tolerance, and the Libyan Scouts' "A World of Peace for Children" charter, which the pupil signs. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
