# Hands-on — Being Kind to Animals and Helping Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Do not be unkind to animals, be orderly in a queue, and help the elderly and lame across the road. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
