# Video script — Being Kind to Animals and Helping Others

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 3 Social Studies For Our Children Book 3 (printed p. 55), teaches: Do not be unkind to animals, be orderly in a queue, and help the elderly and lame across the road.

Guyana's own way: the whole book is one widening idea — a community is people living and working together who share services; three invented communities (Riverview, Mountain Gap, Runsvale) carry every lesson, and the book ends not on facts but on duties: courtesy, respect for national symbols, and protecting the community.
