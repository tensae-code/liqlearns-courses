## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: do not be unkind to animals, be orderly in a queue, and help the elderly and lame across the road — as Guyana's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Responsibilities: other courtesy rules

Guyana Grade 3 Social Studies For Our Children Book 3 (printed p. 55), teaches: Do not be unkind to animals, be orderly in a queue, and help the elderly and lame across the road. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: the whole book is one widening idea — a community is people living and working together who share services; three invented communities (Riverview, Mountain Gap, Runsvale) carry every lesson, and the book ends not on facts but on duties: courtesy, respect for national symbols, and protecting the community. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
