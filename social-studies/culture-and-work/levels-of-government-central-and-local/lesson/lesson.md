## Sub lesson 1 — Mga Antas ng Pamahalaan

Philippines Grade 4 AP4 Unit III (printed p. 237), teaches: The levels of government from national down to local, and why needs are answered faster when each level does its part. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ang mga Namumuno sa Bansa

Philippines Grade 4 AP4 Unit III (printed p. 242), teaches: The country's leaders — who heads each branch and level and the direction each gives the nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Philippines's way — the book's own teaching way

Philippines's own way: the whole third unit of Grade 4 is one civics arc — the national government and its three branches first, then levels down to the local government, then what government does for citizens (health, education, peace, infrastructure, rights), closing on local government working with community servants; the Bill of Rights is taught straight from Article III of the Philippine Constitution. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
