China Senior 思想政治必修1 (printed p. 42), teaches: What the "new era" means — the changed principal contradiction of Chinese society, and China's standing in the world and in socialist history.

China's own way: the senior Politics course opens with a single arc — only socialism could save China, only Chinese socialism can develop it, only holding to it can achieve national rejuvenation — and teaches today's governing system (road, theory, system, culture; the Party's leadership; the people's congress order founded in the 1954 Constitution) as the conclusion of that history, every lesson built from 探究与分享 discussion cases, 相关链接 source boxes and 专家点评 commentary.
