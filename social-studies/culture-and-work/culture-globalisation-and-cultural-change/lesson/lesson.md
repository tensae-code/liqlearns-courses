## Sub lesson 1 — Globalisation

**Globalisation is countries linking and coordinating** in economic, political, social and cultural matters — above all **trade, money movement and spreading new technologies for shared use**. And globalisation itself **changes cultures**.

[Picture 11522 — two villages joining by road, phone and market arrows]

## Sub lesson 2 — No culture stands still

**No culture stands still.** Change is **slow or fast**, and since a culture's parts touch each other, **one part's change pulls others** with it.

The named drivers of change: **science and technology change; education, migration and contact; the many media; and cultures meeting each other**.

## Say it to your companion
