# Lesson plan — Culture — globalisation and cultural change

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Culture — globalisation and cultural change
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 9 — Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder: culture, economic activities, trade, and tax).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** globalisation — countries linking in trade, money, technology and culture — and why no culture stands still
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title, at Grade 8 depth in the course's level ladder (levels, not grades).
A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved (the gate: README, "Units, Lessons and Sub Lessons (ruled 2026-11-08)"):

1. **Globalisation** — Globalisation is countries linking and coordinating in economic, political, social and cultural matters — above all trade, money movement and spreading new tech…
2. **No culture stands still** — No culture stands still. Change is slow or fast, and since a culture's parts touch each other, one part's change pulls others with it.…

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what globalisation is, with its three named fields.
2. Name the four drivers of cultural change.
3. Explain why one part's change pulls the rest.
