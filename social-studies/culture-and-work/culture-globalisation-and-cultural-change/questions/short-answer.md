# Culture — globalisation and cultural change — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the matters countries link in, under globalisation.
**Answer:** Economic, political, social and cultural matters — above all trade, money movement and new technologies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the four drivers of cultural change.
**Answer:** Science and technology change; education, migration and contact; the many media; cultures meeting each other.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does one changed part pull others?
**Answer:** Because a culture's parts touch each other.
