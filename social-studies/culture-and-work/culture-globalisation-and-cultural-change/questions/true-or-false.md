# Culture — globalisation and cultural change — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A culture can stand completely still for centuries with no change at all.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Education, migration and contact are drivers of change.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cultural change is always fast.
**Answer: False**
