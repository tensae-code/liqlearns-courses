# Culture — globalisation and cultural change — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Globalisation is countries linking and coordinating above all in —
a) Trade, money movement and spreading technologies  b) War only  c) Farming only  d) Map making
**Answer: a) Trade, money movement and spreading technologies**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Does globalisation change cultures?
a) No  b) Yes — it itself changes cultures  c) Only languages  d) Only dress
**Answer: b) Yes — it itself changes cultures**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is a named driver of cultural change?
a) Rainfall  b) Science and technology change  c) Soil type  d) River length
**Answer: b) Science and technology change**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When one part of a culture changes —
a) Nothing else moves  b) It pulls other parts with it  c) The culture ends  d) Only elders notice
**Answer: b) It pulls other parts with it**
