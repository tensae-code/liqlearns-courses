# Hands-on — Culture — globalisation and cultural change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Find one new technology that reached your area in living memory. Write what it changed — and what that change then pulled with it.

## Activity 2
Interview an elder about one custom that has changed. Was the change slow or fast? Write which driver from the lesson best explains it.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say what globalisation is, with its three named fields. Name the four drivers of cultural change. Explain why one part's change pulls the rest.
