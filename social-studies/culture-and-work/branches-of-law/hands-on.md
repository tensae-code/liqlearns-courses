# Hands-on — Branches of Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the main branches one by one — constitutional, civil (property and personal relations, legal capacity and emancipation, the 1998 Civil Code), criminal, administrative, labour, family and others, each with its subject and method. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
