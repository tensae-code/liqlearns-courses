# Video script — Branches of Law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Armenia Grade 11 Social Science, printed p. 10, teaches: the main branches one by one — constitutional, civil (property and personal relations, legal capacity and emancipation, the 1998 Civil Code), criminal, administrative, labour, family and others, each with its subject and method.

Armenia's own way: the course teaches the state through Armenia's own institutions — its branches of law and codes, its law-enforcement bodies and Human Rights Defender, its state and community elections and referendum, its own party system, and its foreign-policy goals and principles.
