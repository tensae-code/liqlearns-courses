# Video script — Helping Others

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 5 Social Studies TG T1, printed pp. 88–103, teaches: Volunteering is listed among the unit's own key concepts — serving the homeland by unpaid work for others is presented as belonging in action.

Oman Grade 6 Social Studies TG T2, printed p. 86, teaches: Volunteering sits in the unit's own concept list, between elections and national occasions — unpaid service named as one of participation's normal forms.

Oman Grade 4 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 61–78, teaches: Volunteering taught as a complete practice — discovering what the neighbourhood's members do well, joining them in investing it in voluntary work, planning that work, and naming its worth in the community's life.

Oman's own way (Social Studies Grade 5, Term 1): even inside the combined Social Studies book, the identity strand keeps its own unit with its own name — belonging and loyalty are taught as concepts with definitions, facts and values (among them: respecting the regulations and laws, preserving Omani culture and civilisation, volunteering), not left as decoration on the history lessons.

Oman's own way (Social Studies Grade 6, Term 2): by Grade 6 the citizen is an organiser — the state teaches civil society as a named system: associations (الجمعيات الأهلية), the sablah, volunteering, national occasions and even elections listed among the unit's concepts, and the people's participation in preparing Oman Vision 2040 given as the model example.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 4, Term 1): Grade 4 turns skill into service in three widening steps — what I am good at, what my family can build together (a planned family project, with its effect on society traced), and then volunteering in the neighbourhood, named in the last lesson without metaphor: "my volunteering is life".
