# Hands-on — Helping Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Helping Others 1
Work through this part with your hands: Volunteering is listed among the unit's own key concepts — serving the homeland by unpaid work for others is presented as belonging in action. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Helping Others 2
Work through this part with your hands: Volunteering sits in the unit's own concept list, between elections and national occasions — unpaid service named as one of participation's normal forms. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Helping Others 3
Work through this part with your hands: Volunteering taught as a complete practice — discovering what the neighbourhood's members do well, joining them in investing it in voluntary work, planning that work, and naming its worth in the community's life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
