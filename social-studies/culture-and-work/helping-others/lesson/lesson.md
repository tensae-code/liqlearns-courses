## Sub lesson 4 — Oman's way — the books' own teaching way

Oman's own way: even inside the combined Social Studies book, the identity strand keeps its own unit with its own name — belonging and loyalty are taught as concepts with definitions, facts and values (among them: respecting the regulations and laws, preserving Omani culture and civilisation, volunteering), not left as decoration on the history lessons. by Grade 6 the citizen is an organiser — the state teaches civil society as a named system: associations (الجمعيات الأهلية), the sablah, volunteering, national occasions and even elections listed among the unit's concepts, and the people's participation in preparing Oman Vision 2040 given as the model example. Grade 4 turns skill into service in three widening steps — what I am good at, what my family can build together (a planned family project, with its effect on society traced), and then volunteering in the neighbourhood, named in the last lesson without metaphor: "my volunteering is life". This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
