# Hands-on — Friendship Respect and Mutual Help

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: friendship built on respect, trust, mutual understanding and unselfish mutual help — a true friend never betrays or offends, and friendship cannot be forced. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
