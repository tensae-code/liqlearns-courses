## Sub lesson 1 — Conceptos de Educación Moral, Cívica y Ética Ciudadana

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 4° Primaria teacher orientations (printed p. 9), teaches: What moral, civic and citizen-ethics education is and its purposes — building conscious, participative citizenship — as the frame for the whole subject. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Conceptos de Educación Moral, Cívica y Ética Ciudadana

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 5° Primaria teacher orientations (printed p. 9), teaches: The subject framed for Grade 5 — forming pupils who know their fundamental rights and duties, their nationality and citizenship, and value their heritage. ## Sub lesson 3 — Conceptos de Educación Moral, Cívica y Ética Ciudadana

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 6° Primaria teacher orientations (printed p. 9), teaches: The subject framed for Grade 6 — pupils understanding how their community is organised, their fundamental rights and duties, and a culture of peace based on respect and solidarity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way: MINERD pairs every pupil guide with a slim teacher-orientations volume that frames the subject itself — what moral, civic and citizen-ethics education is for, the teacher as a model of integrity, and the shared roles of school, family and community in forming citizens. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
