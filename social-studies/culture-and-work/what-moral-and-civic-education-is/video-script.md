# Video script — What Moral and Civic Education is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 4° Primaria teacher orientations (printed p. 9), teaches: What moral, civic and citizen-ethics education is and its purposes — building conscious, participative citizenship — as the frame for the whole subject.

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 5° Primaria teacher orientations (printed p. 9), teaches: The subject framed for Grade 5 — forming pupils who know their fundamental rights and duties, their nationality and citizenship, and value their heritage.

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 6° Primaria teacher orientations (printed p. 9), teaches: The subject framed for Grade 6 — pupils understanding how their community is organised, their fundamental rights and duties, and a culture of peace based on respect and solidarity.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 2): MINERD pairs every pupil guide with a slim teacher-orientations volume that frames the subject itself — what moral, civic and citizen-ethics education is for, the teacher as a model of integrity, and the shared roles of school, family and community in forming citizens.
