# What Moral and Civic Education is — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: what moral, civic and citizen-ethics education is and its purposes — building conscious, participative citizenship — as the frame for the whole subject.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: the subject framed for Grade 5 — forming pupils who know their fundamental rights and duties, their nationality and citizenship, and value their heritage.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: the subject framed for Grade 6 — pupils understanding how their community is organised, their fundamental rights and duties, and a culture of peace based on respect and solidarity.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Dominican Republic's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
