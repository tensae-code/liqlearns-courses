# What Moral and Civic Education is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Conceptos de Educación Moral, Cívica y Ética Ciudadana — What moral, civic and citizen-ethics education is and its purposes — building conscious, participative citizenship — as the frame for the whole subject (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Conceptos de Educación Moral, Cívica y Ética Ciudadana — The subject framed for Grade 5 — forming pupils who know their fundamental rights and duties, their nationality and citizenship, and value their heritage (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Conceptos de Educación Moral, Cívica y Ética Ciudadana — The subject framed for Grade 6 — pupils understanding how their community is organised, their fundamental rights and duties, and a culture of peace based on respect and solidarity (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the fourth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way — MINERD pairs every pupil guide with a slim teacher-orientations volume that frames the subject itself — what moral, civic and citizen-ethics education is for, the teacher as a model of integrity, and the shared roles of school, family and community in forming citizens. (any close wording).**
