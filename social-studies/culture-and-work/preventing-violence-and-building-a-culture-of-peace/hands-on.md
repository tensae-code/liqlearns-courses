# Hands-on — Preventing Violence and Building a Culture of Peace

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 1 closing "Tejiendo actitudes para evitar la violencia" and "Algunas ideas para la prevención de la violencia"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 23–25, 28, teaches: prevention works on risk factors — healthy civic attitudes in children, warmer family environments, community action — and each pupil weaves personal commitments to avoid violence at home and school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
