# Video script — Preventing Violence and Building a Culture of Peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 23–25, 28, teaches: prevention works on risk factors — healthy civic attitudes in children, warmer family environments, community action — and each pupil weaves personal commitments to avoid violence at home and school.

Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.
