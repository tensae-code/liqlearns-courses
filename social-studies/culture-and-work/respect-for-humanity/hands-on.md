# Hands-on — Respect for Humanity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 3 Respect for Humanity, Ch. 2 Respecting Human Life, Ch. 2 Honouring People Without Distinction, Ch. 4 Respect for Humanity (احترام آدمیت) — teaches: every human deserves honour simply as a human; the lesson works through how we treat neighbours — sharing their joys and troubles, never harming them, helping before being asked; human life is a trust from God — never waste or harm it; clean water, clean air and honest food protect it, and every faith commands its care; honour is owed to every person, poor as much as rich — the teacher's story of the poor boy mocked for his dress ends with the class learning that worth is not worn; honour is owed at home first — parents, grandparents, servants and guests — then to every human; the one who serves others and shares their pain earns the name of human. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
