**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Pakistan).**

Pakistan's own way (Book 3): Ethics is the subject Punjab gives children who do not take Islamiat — the world's religions are taught respectfully first, and the shared moral core (respect, helping, cleanliness, punctuality) is then taught as every faith's common teaching.

Pakistan's own way (Book 5): the Class 3 Ethics book grounds every value in religion itself — good manners are what every faith commands — and spends its longest stretch on the equal honour of every human life, rich or poor.

Pakistan's own way (Book 6): the senior primary Ethics book turns values into civic machinery — laws learned from a flock of birds, time priced like money, traffic signs taught as a citizen's duty — and opens with story after story where greed ruins kings and gratitude saves the lame.
