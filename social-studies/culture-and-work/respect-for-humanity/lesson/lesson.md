## Sub lesson 2 — Pakistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Pakistan's own way: Ethics is the subject Punjab gives children who do not take Islamiat — the world's religions are taught respectfully first, and the shared moral core (respect, helping, cleanliness, punctuality) is then taught as every faith's common teaching. the Class 3 Ethics book grounds every value in religion itself — good manners are what every faith commands — and spends its longest stretch on the equal honour of every human life, rich or poor. the senior primary Ethics book turns values into civic machinery — laws learned from a flock of birds, time priced like money, traffic signs taught as a citizen's duty — and opens with story after story where greed ruins kings and gratitude saves the lame. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
