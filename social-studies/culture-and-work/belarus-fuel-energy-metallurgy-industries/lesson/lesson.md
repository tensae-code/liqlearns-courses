# Belarus Fuel, Energy and Metallurgy Industries

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Belarus's fuel industry, its electric power, and its ferrous and non-ferrous metallurgy, as Belarus's Grade 10 book teaches them. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fuel industry and electric power

Theme VII of Belarus's Grade 10 book takes the economy branch by branch, starting (§§41–42) with the fuel industry and electric power — where the country's fuel and electricity come from.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ferrous and non-ferrous metallurgy

Theme VII §43 follows with metallurgy, ferrous and non-ferrous: the making of iron, steel and other metals in Belarus.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
