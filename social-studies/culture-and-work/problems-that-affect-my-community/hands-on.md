# Hands-on — Problems That Affect My Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Problems That Affect My Community
Work through this part with your hands: the social and environmental problems that strike a community — climate change, air pollution, deforestation, ecosystem damage and above all water: floods of three kinds and what they do to homes, food, health and basic services. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mexico's way
Say how Mexico's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
