# Problems That Affect My Community

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the social and environmental problems that strike a community — climate change, air pollution, deforestation, ecosystem damage and above all water: floods of three kinds and what they do to homes, food, health and basic services — as Mexico's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Problems That Affect My Community

Mexico Grade 7 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 1 (secundaria) (printed pp. 116–118), teaches: The social and environmental problems that strike a community — climate change, air pollution, deforestation, ecosystem damage and above all water: floods of three kinds and what they do to homes, food, health and basic services. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way

Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
