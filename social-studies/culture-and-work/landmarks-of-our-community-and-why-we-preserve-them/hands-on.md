# Hands-on — Landmarks of Our Community and Why We Preserve Them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A landmark is an important place, monument, building or structure that enables one to locate an area; landmarks may have historical significance (forts, monuments, trees, tombs) or religious significance (churches, mosques, temples); landmarks and boundaries help us to find places in our environment; some landmarks are under the care of the National Trust of Guyana — and we must preserve our landmarks. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
