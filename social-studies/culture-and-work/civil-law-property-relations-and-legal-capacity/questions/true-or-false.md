# Civil Law Property Relations and Legal Capacity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Civil law as the branch regulating property and personal non-property relations (buying, transport, loans, hire, gift, insurance; honour, dignity, an author's rights in a created work); its subjects — individuals, legal persons (organisations with their own property that answer for their own obligations) and the state on equal terms; the principles of the 1999 Civil Code (equality of participants, inviolability of property, freedom of contract, judicial protection); and legal capacity — правоспособность from birth, дееспособность in full at 18, with minors under 14 limited to small everyday deals and 14–18-year-olds able to dispose of their own earnings, stipends and author's rights.
