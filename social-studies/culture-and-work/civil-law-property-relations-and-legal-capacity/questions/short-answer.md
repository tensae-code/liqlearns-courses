# Civil Law Property Relations and Legal Capacity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Foundations of civil law (Основы гражданского права) — Grade 11 — civil law as the branch regulating property and personal non-property relations (buying, transport, loans, hire, gift, insurance; honour, dignity, an author's rights in a created work); its subjects — individuals, legal persons (organisations with their own property that answer for their own obligations) and the state on equal terms; the principles of the 1999 Civil Code (equality of participants, inviolability of property, freedom of contract, judicial protection); and legal capacity — правоспособность from birth, дееспособность in full at 18, with minors under 14 limited to small everyday deals and 14–18-year-olds able to dispose of their own earnings, stipends and author's rights (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics. (any close wording).**
