# Civil Law Property Relations and Legal Capacity

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: civil law as the branch regulating property and personal non-property relations (buying, transport, loans, hire, gift, insurance; honour, dignity, an author's rights in a created work); its subjects — individuals, legal persons (organisations with their own property that answer for their own obligations) and the state on equal terms; the principles of the 1999 Civil Code (equality of participants, inviolability of property, freedom of contract, judicial protection); and legal capacity — правоспособность from birth, дееспособность in full at 18, with minors under 14 limited to small everyday deals and 14–18-year-olds able to dispose of their own earnings, stipends and author's rights — as Belarus' book teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Foundations of civil law (Основы гражданского права) — Grade 11

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 58), teaches: Civil law as the branch regulating property and personal non-property relations (buying, transport, loans, hire, gift, insurance; honour, dignity, an author's rights in a created work); its subjects — individuals, legal persons (organisations with their own property that answer for their own obligations) and the state on equal terms; the principles of the 1999 Civil Code (equality of participants, inviolability of property, freedom of contract, judicial protection); and legal capacity — правоспособность from birth, дееспособность in full at 18, with minors under 14 limited to small everyday deals and 14–18-year-olds able to dispose of their own earnings, stipends and author's rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
