# Video script — Civil Law Property Relations and Legal Capacity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 58), teaches: Civil law as the branch regulating property and personal non-property relations (buying, transport, loans, hire, gift, insurance; honour, dignity, an author's rights in a created work); its subjects — individuals, legal persons (organisations with their own property that answer for their own obligations) and the state on equal terms; the principles of the 1999 Civil Code (equality of participants, inviolability of property, freedom of contract, judicial protection); and legal capacity — правоспособность from birth, дееспособность in full at 18, with minors under 14 limited to small everyday deals and 14–18-year-olds able to dispose of their own earnings, stipends and author's rights.
Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics..
