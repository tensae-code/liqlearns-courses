Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2, printed p. 49, teaches: social relations are the relations that arise between groups of individuals, built on good treatment, good morals, respect, appreciation, cooperation, equality and justice; a person cannot live alone, and the fields of our relations widen in circles — family relations, friends, neighbours, the quarter, study and work colleagues, and society, with the school itself a builder of social relations.

Palestine's own way: civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom.
