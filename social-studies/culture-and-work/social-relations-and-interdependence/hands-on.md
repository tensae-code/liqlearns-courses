# Hands-on — Social Relations and Interdependence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Social Relations and Interdependence
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Social relations are the relations that arise between groups of individuals, built on good treatment, good morals, respect, appreciation, cooperation, equality and justice; a person cannot live alone, and the fields of our relations widen in circles — family relations, friends, neighbours, the quarter, study and work colleagues, and society, with the school itself a builder of social relations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
