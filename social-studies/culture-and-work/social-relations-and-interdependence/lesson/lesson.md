## Sub lesson 2 — Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way

Palestine's own way (Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2): civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom.
