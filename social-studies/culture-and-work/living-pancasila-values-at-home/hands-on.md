# Hands-on — Living Pancasila Values at Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 2 D. Menerapkan Nilai-Nilai Pancasila di Lingkungan Rumah, Bab 4 A. Aku Pelajar Pancasila, Bab 4 B. Sikap dan Perilaku yang Mencerminkan Pengamalan Pancasila dalam Kehidupan Bermasyarakat, Bab 1 B. Meneladani Perilaku Pancasila, Bab 1 C. Membiasakan Perilaku Pancasila, Bab 1 B. Belajar Mengamalkan Pancasila, Bab 2 B. Mengajak Teman Mengamalkan Pancasila, Panduan Umum B. Dimensi Profil Pelajar Pancasila, Panduan Khusus Bab 2: Mengamalkan Pancasila untuk Kebahagiaan Bersama — teaches: applying Pancasila values in the home environment — praying, helping, sharing, deciding together; being a Pancasila student — the meaning of each sila worked out in family, school and community life; attitudes and behaviour that show Pancasila practised in community life; emulating the founders' Pancasila behaviour — and spotting behaviour that contradicts Pancasila values; making Pancasila behaviour a daily habit, tracked with Pancasila cards; learning to practise Pancasila — a personal programme, a letter, and not discriminating between friends; inviting friends to practise Pancasila — through your own works and through inviting them to care; the six dimensions of the Pancasila Student Profile as the subject's values framework for teachers; guidance for practising Pancasila as the nation's way of life and inviting friends to practise it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
