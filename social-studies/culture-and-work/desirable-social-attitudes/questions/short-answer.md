# Desirable Social Attitudes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Desirable social attitudes — the attitudes we are expected to adopt — honesty (not only returning things to the rightful owner, but being honest with ourselves, showing sympathy to friends in times of need, displaying trust and showing loyalty); punctuality ("the early bird catches the worm"); respect — show respect for elders and those in authority, obey teachers and prefects, and always be courteous, with thank you, pardon me, good day, please, excuse me and may I help as part of our everyday vocabulary, our etiquette, our life; and obeying rules — keeping classrooms and surroundings tidy, caring for public and private property, obeying the laws of our country and avoiding delinquent groups (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
