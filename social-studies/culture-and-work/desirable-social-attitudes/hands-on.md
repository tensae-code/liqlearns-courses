# Hands-on — Desirable Social Attitudes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The attitudes we are expected to adopt — honesty (not only returning things to the rightful owner, but being honest with ourselves, showing sympathy to friends in times of need, displaying trust and showing loyalty); punctuality ("the early bird catches the worm"); respect — show respect for elders and those in authority, obey teachers and prefects, and always be courteous, with thank you, pardon me, good day, please, excuse me and may I help as part of our everyday vocabulary, our etiquette, our life; and obeying rules — keeping classrooms and surroundings tidy, caring for public and private property, obeying the laws of our country and avoiding delinquent groups. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
