# Hands-on — What Society is and the Golden Rule of Politeness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: society as all the people around you, joined by common rules, customs and goals; etiquette and politeness as society's rules, crowned by the golden rule — treat others as you wish to be treated. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
