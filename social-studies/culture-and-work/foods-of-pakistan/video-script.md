# Video script — Foods of Pakistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Pakistan's Grade 4 SS4 (printed p. 16); Grade 5 SS5 (printed pp. 13–14); Grade 10 PS10 (printed p. 115); Grade 12 PS12-2014 (printed p. 88); Grade 5 SS5-KPK (printed p. 26), teaches: Ch. 2 Foods, Ch. 2 Foods, Ch. 6 Salient Features: Dress and Food (food), Ch. 6 Salient Features: Food, Ch. 2 (chapter exercise) — teaches: every region cooks its own dishes — karahi gosht, biryani, pulao, saag, chapli kebab, sajji and kheer pictured and named; regional tables — Sindhi biryani, sarson ka saag, seekh kebab, lassi, pulao and fish pictured; Balochi sajji and kaak, Kashmiri dishes and Gilgiti foods named — food as each region's identity; the shared table of Pakistan — wheat, rice and meat dishes that change by region yet mark one food culture; the provincial table — meat and fruit in NWFP and Balochistan, fish by the sea and rivers, milk and lassi in Punjab, green tea in Balochistan and NWFP, sajji and balti meat travelling from Peshawar to Karachi; Chapli Kabab, Kabli Pulao and Roghan Josh named as the famous dishes of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — the host province's own table set in the national list.

Pakistan's own way: citizenship is taught before everything else — a child first learns what a citizen, a global citizen and a digital citizen is, then Pakistan's own 1973 Constitution, its federal and provincial organs with real seat counts, and only then history, geography and economics. Class 5 deepens Class 4 — rights now change with time, the UN and the 1948 Declaration enter, freedom of speech gets its own lesson with John Stuart Mill quoted, and government is compared across federal, provincial AND local levels with a table of who does what. at Class 10 the state tells its story whole — ideology first, then the society it made: thirteen named features of Pakistani culture, minorities' rights written into the culture chapter, and a full closing chapter on women's rights, women nation-builders and the laws that protect them. the older book teaches the same state from the other end — culture defined before it is described, languages given a whole chapter with Urdu named the language of national integration, and national integration itself defined, factored and argued like a civics concept, not a slogan. the KPK Class 5 book gives the citizen's charter in two plain lists — seven rights (ending in the right to vote) against nine responsibilities (ending in honest dealing) — and makes Abdul Sattar Edhi, who "served the suffering humanity", its model of what a responsible citizen is.
