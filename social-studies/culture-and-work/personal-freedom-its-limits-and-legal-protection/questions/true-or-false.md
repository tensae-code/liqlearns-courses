# Personal Freedom Its Limits and Legal Protection — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Personal freedom as the basis of the legal status of the personality — a person's dignity is fixed in the whole integral system of his rights, political, economic and cultural, and freedom is his ability to choose and act on inner reasoning, not external compulsion (the book distinguishes political, personal, professional, moral, family and even intimate freedoms, and defines freedom as the right to do everything that does not harm others); freedom is not arbitrariness — the Constitution allows limits only on the basis of law and only for national security, public order, morality, public health and the rights of others (Article 23); legal protection — the presumption of innocence, the right to legal aid and defence (Articles 59–62), freedom of movement and choice of residence (Article 30), freedom of thought, conscience and the press (Articles 31, 33, 34), all guarded by the constitutional defence of the person and by the prokuratura's supervision.
**Answer: True**
