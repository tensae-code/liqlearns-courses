# Hands-on — Fields of National Security

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the fields of national security — intellectual security against extremism and terrorism (terrorism defined as any act of violence or threat meant to spread terror and endanger lives, security men and property), social security (sound relations in family, society and homeland, far from tribal or regional prejudice, built by family upbringing and the school), economic security (sound production, guarding national wealth and sustainable development), and cybersecurity (protecting networks, systems and data, the National Cybersecurity Authority, and the 1428H Anti-Cybercrime Law — blackmail, unlawful entry, eavesdropping and defamation). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
