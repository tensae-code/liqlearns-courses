# Video script — Fields of National Security

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 215–219), teaches: The fields of national security — intellectual security against extremism and terrorism (terrorism defined as any act of violence or threat meant to spread terror and endanger lives, security men and property), social security (sound relations in family, society and homeland, far from tribal or regional prejudice, built by family upbringing and the school), economic security (sound production, guarding national wealth and sustainable development), and cybersecurity (protecting networks, systems and data, the National Cybersecurity Authority, and the 1428H Anti-Cybercrime Law — blackmail, unlawful entry, eavesdropping and defamation).

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue.
