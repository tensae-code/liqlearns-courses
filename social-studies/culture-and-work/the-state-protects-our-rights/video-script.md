# Video script — The State Protects Our Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 9 Social Studies, printed p. 67, teaches: The Constitution (Article 19) makes the state answerable to its citizens for creating the economic, social and legal guarantees of their rights and for fighting violations; the state does this through its administration, whose powers are fixed by the Constitution and laws and which may act only inside them — an administrative body works by law, serves the public interest, and answers for decisions that touch people's rights; Mongolia's administration stands in four levels (ministries; aimag/capital; soum/district; bag/khoroo), and its face to the citizen is the One-Stop Service centre (Нэг цэгийн үйлчилгээ), where social welfare, insurance, civil registration, property registration, land, petitions and notary services are gathered in one place.

Mongolia's own way (Book 7): Grade 9 is where the spiral turns civic — communication first (how people actually reach each other), then rights as a working system of state duties and citizen duties, then the state itself: how a law is made, how a cabinet decides, how a court sits, and the seven doors through which a citizen walks into all three.
