# The State Protects Our Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 4.3 "What duties does the state bear regarding human rights?" — The Constitution (Article 19) makes the state answerable to its citizens for creating the economic, social and legal guarantees of their rights and for fighting violations; the state does this through its administration, whose powers are fixed by the Constitution and laws and which may act only inside them — an administrative body works by law, serves the public interest, and answers for decisions that touch people's rights; Mongolia's administration stands in four levels (ministries; aimag/capital; soum/district; bag/khoroo), and its face to the citizen is the One-Stop Service centre (Нэг цэгийн үйлчилгээ), where social welfare, insurance, civil registration, property registration, land, petitions and notary services are gathered in one place (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 9 is where the spiral turns civic — communication first (how people actually reach each other), then rights as a working system of state duties and citizen duties, then the state itself: how a law is made, how a cabinet decides, how a court sits, and the seven doors through which a citizen walks into all three (any close wording).**
