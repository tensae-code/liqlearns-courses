# The State Protects Our Rights — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the Constitution (Article 19) makes the state answerable to its citizens for creating the economic, social and legal guarantees of their rights and for fighting violations; the state does this through its administration, whose powers are fixed by the Constitution and laws and which may act only inside them — an administrative body works by law, serves the public interest, and answers for decisions that touch people's rights; Mongolia's administration stands in four levels (ministries; aimag/capital; soum/district; bag/khoroo), and its face to the citizen is the One-Stop Service centre (Нэг цэгийн үйлчилгээ), where social welfare, insurance, civil registration, property registration, land, petitions and notary services are gathered in one place.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
