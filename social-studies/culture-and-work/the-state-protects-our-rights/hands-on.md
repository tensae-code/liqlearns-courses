# Hands-on — The State Protects Our Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.3 "What duties does the state bear regarding human rights?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Constitution (Article 19) makes the state answerable to its citizens for creating the economic, social and legal guarantees of their rights and for fighting violations; the state does this through its administration, whose powers are fixed by the Constitution and laws and which may act only inside them — an administrative body works by law, serves the public interest, and answers for decisions that touch people's rights; Mongolia's administration stands in four levels (ministries; aimag/capital; soum/district; bag/khoroo), and its face to the citizen is the One-Stop Service centre (Нэг цэгийн үйлчилгээ), where social welfare, insurance, civil registration, property registration, land, petitions and notary services are gathered in one place. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
