# Video script — Exploring Our Cultures and Catching Prejudice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G6 student book, volume 2 — Units 3–4 portion with parents' guide, printed p. 47, teaches: the subcultures (الثقافة الفرعية) inside one society, met through the unfinished jigsaw — no one sees the whole picture alone; exploring the cultures around us and where we stand towards them; and recognising prejudgements (الأحكام المسبقة) in society and facing them, with solidarity (التضامن) as the answer

United Arab Emirates' own way: tolerance is not left as a slogan — the sixth-grade volume opens the unit under the Louvre Abu Dhabi's dome and asks outright whether culture changes or stands still, how it shapes who we are, and how a person catches his own prejudgements before they harden.
