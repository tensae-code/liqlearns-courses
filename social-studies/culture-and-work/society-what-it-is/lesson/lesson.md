## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a society is a group of people who live together a long time in one territory with common aims — a family and a school are small societies, the people of a country a large one (the nation), and Muslims across the world the wider umma — as Afghanistan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Society What It is

Afghanistan Grade 5 Social Studies (Dari), printed p. 1, teaches: a society is a group of people who live together a long time in one territory with common aims — a family and a school are small societies, the people of a country a large one (the nation), and Muslims across the world the wider umma. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Afghanistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Afghanistan's own way: society is taught as a ladder of belonging — family, school, village, district, city, country, and the wider Islamic umma — and the state appears as the servant of that society: ministries named one by one, each with its service, and peace named the first need of a country that has known decades of war. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
