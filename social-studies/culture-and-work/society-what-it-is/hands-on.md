# Hands-on — Society What It is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A society is a group of people who live together a long time in one territory with common aims — a family and a school are small societies, the people of a country a large one (the nation), and Muslims across the world the wider umma. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
