# Video script — How Different Peoples See the Land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 2 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 125–127), teaches: A worldview is the way you think and feel about the things around you; the Amerindians viewed the environment as sacred and holy and took only what they needed, while the Europeans came seeking wealth — the El Dorado stories — and used the land to make their countries richer.

Guyana's own way: the Grade 2 book of the new series starts citizenship at the child's own doorstep — the many communities a child belongs to at once, the councils that serve them, the peoples and festivals that fill them, then decisions, conflict and rights, all before any map or market is taught.
