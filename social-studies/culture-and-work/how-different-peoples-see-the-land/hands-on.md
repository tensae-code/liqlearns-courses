# Hands-on — How Different Peoples See the Land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A worldview is the way you think and feel about the things around you; the Amerindians viewed the environment as sacred and holy and took only what they needed, while the Europeans came seeking wealth — the El Dorado stories — and used the land to make their countries richer. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
