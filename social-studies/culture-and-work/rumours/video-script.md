The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Jordan Grade 7 SS G7 T2, printed p. 18, teaches: Rumours — why people spread them and the harm they do between people.

Jordan's own way: media is taught as a citizen's defence — content, misinformation, rumours and social media in one unit — and government as Jordan's own constitutional story, ending in the rule of law and integrity.
