# Hands-on — Qualities That Help Us Live Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 1 Miremos nuestro interior
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 2 CS_Grado2_01, printed pp. 15–16, teaches: qualities (honesty, joy, kindness, solidarity, respect, affection) help us live with others and reach goals, and everyone also has aspects of their way of being to improve. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: Escuela Nueva self-paced guides (parts A–D) set in rural Colombia; rights are introduced through Colombia's own documents (Political Constitution, Declaration of the Rights of the Child, Universal Declaration of Human Rights), and local government through the real Colombian ladder department → municipality → corregimiento → vereda, with resguardos inside municipalities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
