# Hands-on — Womens Changing Place in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Women's rising status — the Kalsindur village girls who won the SAFF Women's Championship 2022 against village opposition, and women leading the government (Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina; first woman Speaker Shirin Sharmin Chaudhury), against women's confined life 100 years ago. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
