# Hands-on — City And Countryside Links

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 1: City–village relations (روابط شهر و روستا) — the tourism-function village of Kandovan in Iran and a fishing village in Vietnam pictured; the chart of Iran's changing urban and rural population; the land reform of 1341 (1962) in the villages, changing land-ownership law and redistributing land to the farmers in three stages, taking it from the great landlords. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
