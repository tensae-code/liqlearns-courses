Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 18), teaches: Lesson 1: City–village relations (روابط شهر و روستا) — the tourism-function village of Kandovan in Iran and a fishing village in Vietnam pictured; the chart of Iran's changing urban and rural population; the land reform of 1341 (1962) in the villages, changing land-ownership law and redistributing land to the farmers in three stages, taking it from the great landlords.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
