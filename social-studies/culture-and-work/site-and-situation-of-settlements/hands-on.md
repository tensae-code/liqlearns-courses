# Hands-on — Site And Situation Of Settlements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 1 Cities and villages (شهرها و روستاها): Earth the settlement of over seven and a half billion people, the land only a third of its surface, most people crowded into villages and cities; the village of Hollókő in Hungary, its first nucleus a castle built in the mid-13th century against Mongol attacks; site (مقر) — the local ground a settlement stands on — and situation (موقعیت) — its wider connections — as complementary ideas; a settlement near populous villages or mineral resources drawing labour and growing.
