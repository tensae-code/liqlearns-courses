The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Iran's book, the *Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)* (printed p. 2), teaches: Lesson 1 Cities and villages (شهرها و روستاها): Earth the settlement of over seven and a half billion people, the land only a third of its surface, most people crowded into villages and cities; the village of Hollókő in Hungary, its first nucleus a castle built in the mid-13th century against Mongol attacks; site (مقر) — the local ground a settlement stands on — and situation (موقعیت) — its wider connections — as complementary ideas; a settlement near populous villages or mineral resources drawing labour and growing.

Iran's own way (Iran Grade 12 Geography (3)): 'applied' geography organised as three problems of Iranian life — settlements, transport, and natural hazards — each taken in two lessons (what it is, then how to manage it) with a geographical-skills section (GIS, time zones, remote sensing) closing each chapter.
