# Doing My Own Jobs Properly — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The this part of this lesson is —
a) 自分でやることはしっかりと (Do the things that are yours to do, properly)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) 自分でやることはしっかりと (Do the things that are yours to do, properly)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Japan Grades 1–2 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 22), teaches: Doing your own jobs thoroughly by your own effort.  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Japan Grades 1–2 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 22), teaches: Doing your own jobs thoroughly by your own effort.**
