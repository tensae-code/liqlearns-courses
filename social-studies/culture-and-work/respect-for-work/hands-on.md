# Hands-on — Respect for Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Work is the activity man performs to achieve a goal, and it is the basis of the nations' and societies' progress, and through it the human civilisations were built; it is a duty upon man, and the Iraqis of all their components respected work and performed it with trustworthiness and sincerity, a source of their strength and their dignity, and it builds the sound society and achieves security, prosperity and stability. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iraq's way
Say how Iraq's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
