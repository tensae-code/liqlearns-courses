## Sub lesson 1 — Respect for Work

Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies (printed p. 104), teaches: Work is the activity man performs to achieve a goal, and it is the basis of the nations' and societies' progress, and through it the human civilisations were built; it is a duty upon man, and the Iraqis of all their components respected work and performed it with trustworthiness and sincerity, a source of their strength and their dignity, and it builds the sound society and achieves security, prosperity and stability. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: the components are named one by one with where each lives and what each built, equality is stated in the concept itself — all equal in rights and duties — and the values are taught as a civilisational inheritance, from keeping covenants "for thousands of years" to altruism as the road to social justice. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
