# State Youth Policy and the Rights of Young Citizens — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Why a state needs a youth policy (young people's employment, values, education, leisure, health and young families' housing); Article 32 of the Constitution guaranteeing youth the right to spiritual, moral and physical development and the conditions for free participation in the country's political, social, economic and cultural development; and the Law on the Foundations of State Youth Policy defining it as a system of measures for citizens aged 14 to 31, worked out in the "Youth of Belarus" programme with youth organisations themselves taking part.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Belarus' student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md.**
