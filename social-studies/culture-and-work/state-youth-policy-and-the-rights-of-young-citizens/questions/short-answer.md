# State Youth Policy and the Rights of Young Citizens — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Youth policy (Молодежная политика) — Grade 11 — why a state needs a youth policy (young people's employment, values, education, leisure, health and young families' housing); Article 32 of the Constitution guaranteeing youth the right to spiritual, moral and physical development and the conditions for free participation in the country's political, social, economic and cultural development; and the Law on the Foundations of State Youth Policy defining it as a system of measures for citizens aged 14 to 31, worked out in the "Youth of Belarus" programme with youth organisations themselves taking part (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics. (any close wording).**
