# Hands-on — State Youth Policy and the Rights of Young Citizens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Why a state needs a youth policy (young people's employment, values, education, leisure, health and young families' housing); Article 32 of the Constitution guaranteeing youth the right to spiritual, moral and physical development and the conditions for free participation in the country's political, social, economic and cultural development; and the Law on the Foundations of State Youth Policy defining it as a system of measures for citizens aged 14 to 31, worked out in the "Youth of Belarus" programme with youth organisations themselves taking part. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Belarus's way
Say how Belarus' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
