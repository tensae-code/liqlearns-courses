# State Youth Policy and the Rights of Young Citizens

Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Youth policy (Молодежная политика) — Grade 11

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 163), teaches: Why a state needs a youth policy (young people's employment, values, education, leisure, health and young families' housing); Article 32 of the Constitution guaranteeing youth the right to spiritual, moral and physical development and the conditions for free participation in the country's political, social, economic and cultural development; and the Law on the Foundations of State Youth Policy defining it as a system of measures for citizens aged 14 to 31, worked out in the "Youth of Belarus" programme with youth organisations themselves taking part. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
