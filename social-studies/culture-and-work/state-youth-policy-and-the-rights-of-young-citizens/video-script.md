# Video script — State Youth Policy and the Rights of Young Citizens

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 163), teaches: Why a state needs a youth policy (young people's employment, values, education, leisure, health and young families' housing); Article 32 of the Constitution guaranteeing youth the right to spiritual, moral and physical development and the conditions for free participation in the country's political, social, economic and cultural development; and the Law on the Foundations of State Youth Policy defining it as a system of measures for citizens aged 14 to 31, worked out in the "Youth of Belarus" programme with youth organisations themselves taking part.
Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics..
