# Hands-on — Children Have the Right to Education

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: education is a child's right; at school children learn and grow into useful members of society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## North Macedonia's way
Say how North Macedonia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
