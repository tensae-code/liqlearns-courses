Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 154–158), teaches: Shura as a principle Islam urged and the Prophet and the Righteous Caliphs practised, present in the Saudi state since its founding (the imams of the first and second states seeking scholars' and experts' counsel) and institutionalised under King Abdulaziz; the modern Council's first term (1414–1418H) of a president and sixty members, grown to one hundred and twenty and then one hundred and fifty; its competencies — discussing the general plan for economic and social development, studying systems, regulations, treaties, international agreements and concessions, reviewing systems, and discussing the annual reports of ministries and government bodies; its General Commission (president, deputy, committee chairs) that sets the council's plan and session agendas, and its fortnightly sessions.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes.
