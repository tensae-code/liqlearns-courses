# Hands-on — The Shura Council

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: shura as a principle Islam urged and the Prophet and the Righteous Caliphs practised, present in the Saudi state since its founding (the imams of the first and second states seeking scholars' and experts' counsel) and institutionalised under King Abdulaziz; the modern Council's first term (1414–1418H) of a president and sixty members, grown to one hundred and twenty and then one hundred and fifty; its competencies — discussing the general plan for economic and social development, studying systems, regulations, treaties, international agreements and concessions, reviewing systems, and discussing the annual reports of ministries and government bodies; its General Commission (president, deputy, committee chairs) that sets the council's plan and session agendas, and its fortnightly sessions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
