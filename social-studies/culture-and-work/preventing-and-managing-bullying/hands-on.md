# Hands-on — Preventing and Managing Bullying

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: bullying is deliberate, repeated harm to someone weaker — verbal, physical, social and cyberbullying — its causes (power, low self-esteem, low empathy, anger/jealousy) and how to prevent, report and manage it at school, work, home and online. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zimbabwe's way
Say how Zimbabwe's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
