# Video script — Preventing and Managing Bullying

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe Grade 5 zw-g5 PlusOne Social Science Grade 5 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 90), teaches: Bullying is deliberate, repeated harm to someone weaker — verbal, physical, social and cyberbullying — its causes (power, low self-esteem, low empathy, anger/jealousy) and how to prevent, report and manage it at school, work, home and online.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 7) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 5 civics runs on two ladders at once — the traditional ladder (village head → headman → chief, with the councillor bridging into the state) and the citizen ladder (rights matched one-for-one with responsibilities: "every right has a matching responsibility"), with Zimbabwe-specific protectors named (Police Victim-Friendly Unit, village health workers, social workers).
