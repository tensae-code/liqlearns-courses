Pakistan's Grade 3 Ethics3 (printed pp. 20–21), teaches: Ch. 2 The Importance of Religious Ethics — teaches: every religion stands on good character — honesty, truth, promise-keeping, respect for elders, time-keeping — and religion and ethics cannot be separated; a society without them cannot hold.

Pakistan's own way: the Class 3 Ethics book grounds every value in religion itself — good manners are what every faith commands — and spends its longest stretch on the equal honour of every human life, rich or poor.
