# Families and Assessment in Special Social Studies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Families and Assessment in Special Social Studies — the book's aim — social upbringing and the skills of social life — begins at home, where the child's character is formed and most of the child's time is spent; the family is the most available support system, and the book's goals cannot be reached without the family's partnership — so the teacher must tell parents exactly and transparently what is expected of them (for example: "tell your child the safety points for using the gas stove at home, and repeat and practise them with him"), and put it in writing, which works better than scattered speech; and assessment (ارزشیابی) is the gathering of information about what pupils have learned and the judging of its extent — measurement (سنجش) the comparison of a pupil's performance against a fixed criterion, the test only a tool for gaining the information (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Iran's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
