# Families and Assessment in Special Social Studies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the book's aim — social upbringing and the skills of social life — begins at home, where the child's character is formed and most of the child's time is spent; the family is the most available support system, and the book's goals cannot be reached without the family's partnership — so the teacher must tell parents exactly and transparently what is expected of them (for example: "tell your child the safety points for using the gas stove at home, and repeat and practise them with him"), and put it in writing, which works better than scattered speech; and assessment (ارزشیابی) is the gathering of information about what pupils have learned and the judging of its extent — measurement (سنجش) the comparison of a pupil's performance against a fixed criterion, the test only a tool for gaining the information.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iran's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
