# Video script — Families and Assessment in Special Social Studies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 4 teacher guide (special needs) (teacher guide, Persian), printed p. 41, teaches: the book's aim — social upbringing and the skills of social life — begins at home, where the child's character is formed and most of the child's time is spent; the family is the most available support system, and the book's goals cannot be reached without the family's partnership — so the teacher must tell parents exactly and transparently what is expected of them (for example: "tell your child the safety points for using the gas stove at home, and repeat and practise them with him"), and put it in writing, which works better than scattered speech; and assessment (ارزشیابی) is the gathering of information about what pupils have learned and the judging of its extent — measurement (سنجش) the comparison of a pupil's performance against a fixed criterion, the test only a tool for gaining the information.

Iran's own way: the guide states the whole philosophy of the special-needs course — social studies exists to make independent living possible; what is learned must be generalised to real life and the real environment, and the family is a teaching partner, not an audience.
