The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Ethics Grade 6, printed p. 17, teaches: Greed (шунал) always wants more and is never filled, like salt water drunk against thirst; anger and pride (уур омог) are a coal in the mind that burns its holder first — the angry person suffers more than the one they rage at; and the counter-practice: each pupil writes their own "rule" (өөрийн дүрэм) — a personal law in proverbs of their own making ("right thought, right word, right deed") — and follows it from today.

Mongolia's own way (Link book L2): from Grade 6 the civics subject changes its name and its method — no longer "Civic Education" but "Civic Ethics" (Иргэний ёс зүйн боловсрол), a self-observation workbook in the Buddhist-inflected Mongolian moral tradition: the pupil keeps a diary of their own acts of body, speech and mind, and ethics is practised as a daily discipline before it is ever a rule about others.
