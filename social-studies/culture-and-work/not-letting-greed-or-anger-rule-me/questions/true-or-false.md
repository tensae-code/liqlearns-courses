# Not Letting Greed Or Anger Rule Me — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: greed (шунал) always wants more and is never filled, like salt water drunk against thirst; anger and pride (уур омог) are a coal in the mind that burns its holder first — the angry person suffers more than the one they rage at; and the counter-practice: each pupil writes their own "rule" (өөрийн дүрэм) — a personal law in proverbs of their own making ("right thought, right word, right deed") — and follows it from today.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
