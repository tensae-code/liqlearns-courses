# Hands-on — Not Letting Greed Or Anger Rule Me

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.1, greed and anger units
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Greed (шунал) always wants more and is never filled, like salt water drunk against thirst; anger and pride (уур омог) are a coal in the mind that burns its holder first — the angry person suffers more than the one they rage at; and the counter-practice: each pupil writes their own "rule" (өөрийн дүрэм) — a personal law in proverbs of their own making ("right thought, right word, right deed") — and follows it from today. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
