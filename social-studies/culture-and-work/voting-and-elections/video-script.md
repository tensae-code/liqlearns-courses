# Video script — Voting and Elections

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 20), teaches: The kinds of electoral systems (majoritarian in its three forms, proportional, mixed, minority-representation) and that Belarus uses the majoritarian system; who may vote and be elected (vote at 18; local Soviets from 18, Chamber of Representatives from 21, Council of the Republic from 30, President — a citizen by birth of 35+ resident 10 years); the eight stages of the electoral process from setting the date to publishing results, run by election commissions headed by the Central Commission; elections free, equal, direct and secret (Arts. 65–68); and the referendum as direct democracy whose decisions can be changed only by another referendum (Arts. 76–77).
Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics..
