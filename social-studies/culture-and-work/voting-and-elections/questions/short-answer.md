# Voting and Elections — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Elections and referendums (Выборы и референдумы) — Grade 11 — the kinds of electoral systems (majoritarian in its three forms, proportional, mixed, minority-representation) and that Belarus uses the majoritarian system; who may vote and be elected (vote at 18; local Soviets from 18, Chamber of Representatives from 21, Council of the Republic from 30, President — a citizen by birth of 35+ resident 10 years); the eight stages of the electoral process from setting the date to publishing results, run by election commissions headed by the Central Commission; elections free, equal, direct and secret (Arts. 65–68); and the referendum as direct democracy whose decisions can be changed only by another referendum (Arts. 76–77) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics. (any close wording).**
