# Voting and Elections — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The kinds of electoral systems (majoritarian in its three forms, proportional, mixed, minority-representation) and that Belarus uses the majoritarian system; who may vote and be elected (vote at 18; local Soviets from 18, Chamber of Representatives from 21, Council of the Republic from 30, President — a citizen by birth of 35+ resident 10 years); the eight stages of the electoral process from setting the date to publishing results, run by election commissions headed by the Central Commission; elections free, equal, direct and secret (Arts. 65–68); and the referendum as direct democracy whose decisions can be changed only by another referendum (Arts. 76–77).
