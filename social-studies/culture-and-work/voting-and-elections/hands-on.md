# Hands-on — Voting and Elections

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The kinds of electoral systems (majoritarian in its three forms, proportional, mixed, minority-representation) and that Belarus uses the majoritarian system; who may vote and be elected (vote at 18; local Soviets from 18, Chamber of Representatives from 21, Council of the Republic from 30, President — a citizen by birth of 35+ resident 10 years); the eight stages of the electoral process from setting the date to publishing results, run by election commissions headed by the Central Commission; elections free, equal, direct and secret (Arts. 65–68); and the referendum as direct democracy whose decisions can be changed only by another referendum (Arts. 76–77). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Belarus' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
