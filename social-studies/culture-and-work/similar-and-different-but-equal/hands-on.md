# Hands-on — Similar and Different But Equal

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Слични и различити (Similar and different)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: children differ by sex, looks, age, abilities and interests — a classmate in a wheelchair plays and learns alongside — yet all children are future people with the same rights, and difference is a wealth to respect, not a reason for anger. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
