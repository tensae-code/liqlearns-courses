# Video script — Similar and Different But Equal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 1 Свет око нас 1 (printed pp. 10–11), teaches: Children differ by sex, looks, age, abilities and interests — a classmate in a wheelchair plays and learns alongside — yet all children are future people with the same rights, and difference is a wealth to respect, not a reason for anger.

Serbia's own way: the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast.
