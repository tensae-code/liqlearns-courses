# Similar and Different But Equal — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Слични и различити (Similar and different) — children differ by sex, looks, age, abilities and interests — a classmate in a wheelchair plays and learns alongside — yet all children are future people with the same rights, and difference is a wealth to respect, not a reason for anger (any close wording).**
