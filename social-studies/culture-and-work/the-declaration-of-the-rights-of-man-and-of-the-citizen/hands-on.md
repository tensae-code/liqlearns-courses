# Hands-on — The Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen (1789) — equality of all before the law, the modern figure of the citizen as the basis of democratic governments, written law codes and the separation of powers as its legacy, closing with the Declaration's own full text (arts. 1–17: liberty, property, security, sovereignty in the Nation, law as the general will). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
