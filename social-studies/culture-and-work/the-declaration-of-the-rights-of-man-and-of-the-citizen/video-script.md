The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Costa Rica Grade 9 CONED Antología 9 (printed p. 127), teaches: The declaration of the rights of man and of the citizen (1789) — equality of all before the law, the modern figure of the citizen as the basis of democratic governments, written law codes and the separation of powers as its legacy, closing with the declaration's own full text (arts. 1–17: liberty, property, security, sovereignty in the nation, law as the general will).

Costa Rica's own way: the civics seed is planted inside the History line — the Enlightenment is taught through its political thinkers (Montesquieu's separation of powers, Voltaire's tolerance, Rousseau's general will), and the French Revolution unit closes by printing the full text of the 1789 Declaration and asking pupils to judge its meaning for Costa Rica today.
