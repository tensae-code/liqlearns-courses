# Hands-on — Culture — language

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Count the languages spoken in your own area — ask at home and at the market. Write the count and the names you can gather.

## Activity 2
Take one proverb or one story told at home. Write what would be lost if it were never passed on in language.

## Activity 3
Write one spoken sentence from home, then write it down. That second step is the 5,000-year-old step: writing.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say why language is called culture's carrier. Say when writing began and what it changed. Name Ethiopia's four language families (teacher guide — evidence).
