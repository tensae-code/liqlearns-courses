Guatemala's own way (teacher guide — evidence): Social Studies is taught through three long “learning experiences” rooted in the country's own peoples (Maya, Garífuna, Xinka, Ladino); Geography enters as lived territory — the Maya unity of peoples and territory, care for Mother Earth, and a linguistic map of Guatemala played as a lotería game. That evidence is stocked here as the way Guatemala teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Guatemala's own way: one combined Social Studies and Citizenship book per grade, organised in four units that alternate History of Guatemala/Central America with the physical geography of the isthmus — every map carries the note about the unresolved Belize territorial differendum, and Geography is always worked on maps of the seven Central American countries with Guatemala's own Sierra de las Minas, Motagua river and Maya Biosphere as the home examples. That way is stocked here as the way Guatemala teaches this thing.

That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 2) (teacher guide — evidence): The spiral widens from family to community — ethnic groups and languages of Zimbabwe (Kalanga, Sotho and others), production in the community, shelter types in the neighbourhood (granary, silos, community hall, clinic), and global issues introduced as floods and deforestation. That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Zimbabwe's own way (Grade 5) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 5 transport is taught as a landlocked country's system — goods by rail/road/air/sea, animal transport (riding, leading, herding), and the roadports, seaports and airports Zimbabwe depends on; shelter links heritage sites (Matobo Hills) and map-drawing of Zimbabwe to building safely in disaster-prone areas. That evidence is stocked here as the way Zimbabwe teaches this thing; it does not change the acceptance count above.

Venezuela's own way (Venezuela Grade 2 G2 CS Viva Venezuela): population education (UNESCO population-education guides reworked) woven into story form — Tío Conejo tales, grandparents' memories and the child's own school and street carry population, housing, landscape and weather-sign content.
