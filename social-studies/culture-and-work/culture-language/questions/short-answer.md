# Culture — language — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does language carry into use?
**Answer:** Thought, growth and experience.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the beginning of writing do to civilisation?
**Answer:** It raised civilisation steeply.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two of Ethiopia's language families.
**Answer:** Any two of: Semitic, Cushitic, Omotic, Nilo-Saharan.
