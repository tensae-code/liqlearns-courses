# Culture — language — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Language is called culture's carrier because it —
a) Is written in books only  b) Passes experience, knowledge and heritage between generations  c) Is the oldest tool  d) Makes trade goods
**Answer: b) Passes experience, knowledge and heritage between generations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Writing thought down began about —
a) 500 years ago  b) 5,000 years ago  c) 50 years ago  d) 50,000 years ago
**Answer: b) 5,000 years ago**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which set names Ethiopia's language families (teacher guide — evidence)?
a) Semitic, Cushitic, Omotic, Nilo-Saharan  b) Latin, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew  c) Amharic, Tigrinya, Oromo, Somali  d) Highland, lowland, river, desert
**Answer: a) Semitic, Cushitic, Omotic, Nilo-Saharan**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Languages are grouped into families by —
a) Their alphabet size  b) How close they are to each other  c) Their country's size  d) Their speakers' wealth
**Answer: b) How close they are to each other**
