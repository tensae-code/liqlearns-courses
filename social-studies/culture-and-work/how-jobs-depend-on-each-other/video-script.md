The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed p. 31), teaches: No job stands alone — before bread smells and browns on the table, the ploughman, sower, reaper, combine driver, miller, baker and seller must each do their part, and the apple's chain runs orchard–truck–factory–shop–child.

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
