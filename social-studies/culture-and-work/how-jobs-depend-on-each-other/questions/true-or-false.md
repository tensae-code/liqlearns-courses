**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: No job stands alone — before bread smells and browns on the table, the ploughman, sower, reaper, combine driver, miller, baker and seller must each do their part, and the apple's chain runs orchard–truck–factory–shop–child.
