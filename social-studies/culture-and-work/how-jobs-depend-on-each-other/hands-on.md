# Hands-on — How Jobs Depend on Each Other

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Делатности су међусобно повезане (Activities are interconnected)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: no job stands alone — before bread smells and browns on the table, the ploughman, sower, reaper, combine driver, miller, baker and seller must each do their part, and the apple's chain runs orchard–truck–factory–shop–child. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Serbia's way
Say how Serbia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
