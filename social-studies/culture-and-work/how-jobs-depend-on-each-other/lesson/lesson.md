## Sub lesson 1 — Делатности су међусобно повезане (Activities are interconnected)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed p. 31), teaches: no job stands alone — before bread smells and browns on the table, the ploughman, sower, reaper, combine driver, miller, baker and seller must each do their part, and the apple's chain runs orchard–truck–factory–shop–child. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
