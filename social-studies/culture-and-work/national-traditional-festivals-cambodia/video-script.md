# Video script — National Traditional Festivals — Cambodia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Cambodia's book, the *Cambodia Grade 4 Social Studies* (printed p. 60), teaches: Ch.3 L2 បុណ្យប្រពៃណីជាតិ (national traditional festivals). Cambodia's book, the *Cambodia Grade 5 Social Studies* (printed p. 60), teaches: Ch.3 L2 ពិធីបុណ្យប្រពៃណីជាតិ (national traditional festivals). Cambodia's book, the *Cambodia Grade 6 Social Studies* (printed p. 60), teaches: Ch.3 L2 ពិធីបុណ្យប្រពៃណីជាតិ (national traditional festivals).

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 4 Social Studies): Geography enters primary Social Studies inside a “society and culture” chapter — festivals, the value of work and regional land use taught from Cambodian village life before any map work.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 5 Social Studies): the same four-chapter frame as Grade 4, spiralling up — culture and land use return at a higher level, and the past chapter adds Khmer art motifs and traditional instruments as living heritage.

Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 6 Social Studies): the primary Social Studies spiral ends with its first real map work — maps, the globe and the equator/parallels/meridians grid taught inside the “society today” chapter, anchored on a world map with Cambodia marked.
