# Hands-on — Charities Ngos and Waqf Helping Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: help organised at society's scale — benefactors who give individually and in groups, charities and non-governmental organisations (NGOs, سمن) that must be licensed by the Interior Ministry, school-building benefactors, and waqf — the old Iranian endowment whose schools, bridges, caravanserais, water reservoirs and baths serve everyone forever, today funding over 2,500 hospitals and clinics. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
