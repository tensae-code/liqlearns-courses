## Sub lesson 1 — Charities Ngos and Waqf Helping Society

Iran Social Studies Grade 8 (student book, Persian), printed p. 7, teaches: help organised at society's scale — benefactors who give individually and in groups, charities and non-governmental organisations (NGOs, سمن) that must be licensed by the Interior Ministry, school-building benefactors, and waqf — the old Iranian endowment whose schools, bridges, caravanserais, water reservoirs and baths serve everyone forever, today funding over 2,500 hospitals and clinics. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the civics year is built as an institutional tour taken in the order a citizen meets the state — cooperation in home and neighbourhood, then the executive (this book's "government"), then the judiciary — and every institution is introduced through newspaper headlines and the pupil's own town. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
