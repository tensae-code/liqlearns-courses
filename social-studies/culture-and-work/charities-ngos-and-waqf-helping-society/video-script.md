# Video script — Charities Ngos and Waqf Helping Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 8 (student book, Persian), printed p. 7, teaches: help organised at society's scale — benefactors who give individually and in groups, charities and non-governmental organisations (NGOs, سمن) that must be licensed by the Interior Ministry, school-building benefactors, and waqf — the old Iranian endowment whose schools, bridges, caravanserais, water reservoirs and baths serve everyone forever, today funding over 2,500 hospitals and clinics.

Iran's own way: the civics year is built as an institutional tour taken in the order a citizen meets the state — cooperation in home and neighbourhood, then the executive (this book's "government"), then the judiciary — and every institution is introduced through newspaper headlines and the pupil's own town.
