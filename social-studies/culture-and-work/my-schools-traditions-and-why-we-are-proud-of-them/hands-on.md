# Hands-on — My Schools Traditions and Why We Are Proud of Them

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: finding out your own school's traditions as a research project — the year it was founded, its head teachers through the ages, its teaching and learning achievements, its emulation titles and exemplary teachers and pupils — then presenting them to the class: every school has fine traditions handed down from generation to generation, and the pupils are proud of them and strive to keep and build on them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
