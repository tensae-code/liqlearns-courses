# Video script — My Schools Traditions and Why We Are Proud of Them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 3 Tự nhiên và Xã hội 3, printed pp. 26–29, teaches: Finding out your own school's traditions as a research project — the year it was founded, its head teachers through the ages, its teaching and learning achievements, its emulation titles and exemplary teachers and pupils — then presenting them to the class: every school has fine traditions handed down from generation to generation, and the pupils are proud of them and strive to keep and build on them.

Vietnam's own way: kinship is mapped on both sides of the family with the exact word of address for every relative (and a note that regions address them differently), and the school's link to its community is made of named joint deeds — flood relief, a rubbish-for-plants fair, traffic-safety campaigning and tree planting.
