## Sub lesson 1 — The Industrial Revolution Three Phases

Mozambique's book, the *Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 2* (printed p. 88), teaches: Lição 20: A Revolução Industrial — fases (coal/steam; oil/electricity; nuclear/automation) and its social consequences. Mozambique's book, the *Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 7* (printed p. 13), teaches: Lição 2: A Revolução Industrial (steam engine and coal/hulha; mass production; electricity spreading industry). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mozambique's way — the book's own teaching way

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 7 PESD I Mod 2): human/economic geography organised sector by sector — population first, then each economic activity taught world-then-Mozambique (agriculture, livestock, fishing, forestry, industry, commerce, tourism, transport), with Mozambique's own distribution tables, ports and exports as the local evidence.

Mozambique's own way (Mozambique Grade 10 PESD II Mod 7): industry taught as a long history — from Palaeolithic tool-making through manufacture and mercantilism to the Industrial Revolution and its consequences — before location, types and industrial landscapes; commerce likewise from barter and the invention of coin to trade blocs and unequal world flows. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
