## Sub lesson 1 — Education in My Country from Red Yurtas to Bolashak and Intellectual Schools

Kazakhstan Grade 4 Poznanie mira (110/88) (printed p. 108), teaches: a century of schooling — the "red yurtas" where adults learned to read, universal compulsory primary education from 1930, the first Kazakh textbooks (Satpaev's algebra, Bukeikhanov's geography), the Abai Pedagogical Institute of 1928, eight-year then ten-year then eleven-year schooling; after 1991 gymnasiums, lyceums and colleges, boarding schools for gifted children and computers in schools; the Bolashak international scholarship founded in 1993, the Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools and world-class universities — and the shift of formula from "one education for a whole life" to "learning throughout life". This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the books' own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 5): Grade 4 is where the primary course turns civic in earnest — the republic's own map of 14 oblasts and three cities of republican significance, an elected school council drawn as a working model of the state, the Constitution's rights quoted article by article, and the country's face to the world built from the UN seat, the closed Semipalatinsk test site and EXPO-2017. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
