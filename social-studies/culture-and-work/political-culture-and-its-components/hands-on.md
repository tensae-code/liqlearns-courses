# Hands-on — Political Culture and Its Components

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what political culture is, what forms it (history and traditions, institutions, social structure, world processes) and its four components — knowledge and beliefs, values and norms, feelings, and behaviour. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
