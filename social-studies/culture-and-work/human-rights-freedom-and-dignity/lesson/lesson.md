## Sub lesson 3 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: the closing block builds citizenship in steps — family first (a community with its own norms, duties and rights), then the homeland's symbols, then humanity's rights (the 1948 UN Declaration read article by article), then the needs those rights protect and the taxes that pay for the services, and finally Venezuela's own 1999 Constitution — participatory democracy and its four referendums — as the nation's rulebook for living well. Grade 5's citizenship block is a complete primary civics course in one closing movement — citizenship, the school as the first community where citizens are formed (with student councils by law), needs as human rights, the constitution as fundamental law, the five public powers including Bolívar's own Citizen and Electoral powers, and the municipal power as the one closest to daily life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
