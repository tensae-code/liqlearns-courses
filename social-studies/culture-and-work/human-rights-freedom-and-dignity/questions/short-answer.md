**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Derechos de la humanidad — The rights of humanity — human rights are the set of rights that assure respect for people's dignity and freedom, two pillars held up by justice, honesty, solidarity, loyalty, honour and respect; dignity makes a person deserving of life and security, health and education, food and housing, work and respect, justice and peace; the lesson reads the UN's 1948 Universal Declaration itself — Articles 1, 3, 5, 7, 28 and 29, including everyone's duties to the community — through Aesop's fable of the wolf who prefers hunger with dignity to the dog's collar (any close wording).**
