# Hands-on — Human Rights Freedom and Dignity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The rights of humanity — human rights are the set of rights that assure respect for people's dignity and freedom, two pillars held up by justice, honesty, solidarity, loyalty, honour and respect; dignity makes a person deserving of life and security, health and education, food and housing, work and respect, justice and peace; the lesson reads the UN's 1948 Universal Declaration itself — Articles 1, 3, 5, 7, 28 and 29, including everyone's duties to the community — through Aesop's fable of the wolf who prefers hunger with dignity to the dog's collar. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human rights and our constitution — the universal rights carried into Venezuela's own constitution, including its respect for the country's peoples and languages. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
