# Video script — Citizen Organisation and Political Expression

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado6, printed pp. 206–225, teaches: the political expressions and citizen organisation of origin peoples and of citizens today — how communities organise, participate and make their claims visible, closing the module's chain conflict → participation → organisation → citizenship.

Colombia's own way: civics is taught through the political life of origin peoples — their assemblies, authorities and norms are treated as real political systems, and pupils are asked what indigenous participation and democracy can teach their own region's democratic exercises.
