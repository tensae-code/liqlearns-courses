# Hands-on — Citizen Organisation and Political Expression

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Módulo 6 Guía 19 Expresiones políticas y organización ciudadana
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado6, printed pp. 206–225, teaches: the political expressions and citizen organisation of origin peoples and of citizens today — how communities organise, participate and make their claims visible, closing the module's chain conflict → participation → organisation → citizenship. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 22) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: civics is taught through the political life of origin peoples — their assemblies, authorities and norms are treated as real political systems, and pupils are asked what indigenous participation and democracy can teach their own region's democratic exercises. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
