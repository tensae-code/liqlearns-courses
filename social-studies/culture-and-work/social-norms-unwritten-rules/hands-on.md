# Hands-on — Social Norms Unwritten Rules

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Social norms/customs as society's unwritten rules of behaviour (greetings, respecting elders, sharing sweets at good news), learned from family and elders, carrying no legal punishment but social dislike; their characteristics (socially constructed, prescriptive and prohibitive) and their work of keeping peace and harmony. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
