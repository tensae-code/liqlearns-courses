South Korea's Grade 7 Jihaksa Middle Social Studies ① (2022), printed p. 176, teaches: 정치과정과 시민 참여 1 선거와 정치 주체 — teaches: elections and the political actors.

South Korea's own way: the 2022 middle-school course opens its society half with the pupil's own formation — socialisation and self-identity, status and roles, and the conflict and discrimination inside our society — then builds the full civics ladder in one volume: democracy's ideals and history, elections and political actors, local self-government, law in daily life and how trials work, and finally human rights with the limits and remedies for basic rights and the relief for violated labour rights.
