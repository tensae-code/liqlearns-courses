# Video script — Generosity and Giving

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 7 Term 1 (printed pp. 93–94), teaches: The value of generosity and giving in Kuwaiti society through Sheikh Jaber bin Abdullah Al Sabah, third ruler of Kuwait (45 years), nicknamed "Jaber Al-Aish" — Jaber of the Rice — because he ordered mats spread in the markets with dishes of rice and meat and invited every passer-by to eat.

Kuwait's own way: the values unit widens from Grade 6's neighbourhood to the nation's exemplars — a ruler nicknamed for the rice he spread for passers-by, a Bedouin weaver whose craft UNESCO now lists, a famous poet who bowed to his son's teacher, and the Eid greeting phrases that plant affection between neighbours.
