# Hands-on — Generosity and Giving

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The value of generosity and giving in Kuwaiti society through Sheikh Jaber bin Abdullah Al Sabah, third ruler of Kuwait (45 years), nicknamed "Jaber Al-Aish" — Jaber of the Rice — because he ordered mats spread in the markets with dishes of rice and meat and invited every passer-by to eat. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
