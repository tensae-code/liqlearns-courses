## Sub lesson 6 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: a story-led first book built around two children, Ron and Sue — family, school, rules and nation are all met through their home, street and school day, with a draw/name/complete task on almost every page. Book 2 widens Book 1's world — the same Ron and Sue now meet family types, class rules, neighbourhood shops and, for the first time, the country's leaders (President, Prime Minister, Ministers) and its national days (Republic Day, May Day, Enmore Martyrs' Day, Freedom Day). the whole book is one widening idea — a community is people living and working together who share services; three invented communities (Riverview, Mountain Gap, Runsvale) carry every lesson, and the book ends not on facts but on duties: courtesy, respect for national symbols, and protecting the community. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
