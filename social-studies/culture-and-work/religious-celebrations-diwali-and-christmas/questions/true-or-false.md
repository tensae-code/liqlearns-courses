# Religious Celebrations Diwali and Christmas — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Diwali as a celebration Guyanese families mark.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: Christmas in the family — parents give toys and children receive gifts.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: Neighbours celebrate Diwali, a happy time; making a Diwali card and a diya.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: Youman Nabi is a Muslim festival and a national holiday.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's fifth part teaches: Phagwah, the Hindu spring festival of colours — abrack powder and abeer, the burning of Holika the night before showing good wins over evil, and peace among all Guyanese.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
