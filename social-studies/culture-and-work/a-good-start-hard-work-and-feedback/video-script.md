# Video script — A Good Start Hard Work and Feedback

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 35, teaches: why we must work hard (العمل الجاد) at any task — putting serious effort into getting a job done; giving your view (إبداء الرأي) on a classmate's work and receiving views on your own; praise (الثناء) that values the effort against negative criticism (النقد السلبي) that does not help the work improve; and, in Tariq's butterfly drawing, how honest feedback makes the next version better

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
