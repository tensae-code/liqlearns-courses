# Hands-on — A Good Start Hard Work and Feedback

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A good start
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: why we must work hard (العمل الجاد) at any task — putting serious effort into getting a job done; giving your view (إبداء الرأي) on a classmate's work and receiving views on your own; praise (الثناء) that values the effort against negative criticism (النقد السلبي) that does not help the work improve; and, in Tariq's butterfly drawing, how honest feedback makes the next version better Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
