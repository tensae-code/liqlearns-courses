# A Good Start Hard Work and Feedback — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: why we must work hard (العمل الجاد) at any task — putting serious effort into getting a job done; giving your view (إبداء الرأي) on a classmate's work and receiving views on your own; praise (الثناء) that values the effort against negative criticism (النقد السلبي) that does not help the work improve; and, in Tariq's butterfly drawing, how honest feedback makes the next version better
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
**Answer: True**
