## Sub lesson 3 — Bahamas' way — the book's own teaching way

Bahamas' own way: citizenship is taught before nation history — the child first meets the law-abiding citizen, the community and their own rights, island by island. the fused lower-primary Social Science book carries citizenship from Grade 1 — rights, leaders, my country and being a peaceful citizen sit between the Bible topics and the science topics. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
