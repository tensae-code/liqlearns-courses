# Video script — Leaders in Our Country

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bahamas Grade 3 Social Science G3 (printed pp. 25–32), teaches: Who leads The Bahamas and the community, and what leaders do.

Bahamas Grade 1 Social Science G1 (printed p. 23), teaches: Leaders at home (parents), at school (principal, teacher) and in the country (government leaders; the first Prime Minister and first Governor General pictured).

Bahamas' own way: citizenship is taught before nation history — the child first meets the law-abiding citizen, the community and their own rights, island by island. the fused lower-primary Social Science book carries citizenship from Grade 1 — rights, leaders, my country and being a peaceful citizen sit between the Bible topics and the science topics.
