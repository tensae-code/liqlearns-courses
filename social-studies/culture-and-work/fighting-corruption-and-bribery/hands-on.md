# Hands-on — Fighting Corruption and Bribery

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Moralising public life and fighting bribery (تخليق الحياة العامة ومحاربة الرشوة)
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 9 Tajdid Ijtiamiat 3AC, printed p. 178, teaches: what corruption and bribery do to public life and the bodies that fight them, such as Transparency Maroc. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
