## Sub lesson 2 — Morocco's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way

Morocco's own way: the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
