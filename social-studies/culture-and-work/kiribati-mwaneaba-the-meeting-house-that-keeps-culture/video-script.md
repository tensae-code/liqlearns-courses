# Video script — Kiribati Mwaneaba the Meeting House That Keeps Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kiribati Year 7 Social Science TG (printed p. 7), teaches: Unit 2 Kiribati Culture (cultural infrastructure) — teaches: the mwaneaba at the centre of every village as the building that displays, practises and so preserves the culture for each generation.

Kiribati's own way: Social Science is built island-first — the maneaba (mwaneaba) meeting house is at once the culture-keeping building, the traditional parliament and the centre of village life, and the modern civics (constitution, rights, voting at 18 for the Maneaba ni Maungatabu) is taught growing out of the traditional systems pupils already live under (kaainga family rule, unimwane elders, uea chiefs).
