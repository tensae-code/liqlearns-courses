The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Yemen Grade 5 G5 National Education, printed p. 41, teaches: the judicial authority — the courts judge between people by the law and deliver justice.

Yemen's own way: a standalone National Education book that teaches the state through Yemen's own map and constitution-era institutions — the country is divided into governorates and districts the child can name, the three authorities are taught one per lesson, and democracy is introduced through shura (consultation), with Yemenis presented as early practitioners of it.
