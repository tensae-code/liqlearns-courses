# Hands-on — Social and Political Interactions Between Neighbours

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 3 Social and political interactions between South Sudan and her neighbours
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: social interaction is how society's members relate and learn each other's cultural values; political interaction is the relationships between the governments or leadership of different countries; pupils discuss how South Sudan's interactions with her neighbours promote peace in the region and benefit its people. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
