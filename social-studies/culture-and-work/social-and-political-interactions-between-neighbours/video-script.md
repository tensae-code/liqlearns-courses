# Video script — Social and Political Interactions Between Neighbours

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 6 P6 Social Studies SB, printed p. 51, teaches: social interaction is how society's members relate and learn each other's cultural values; political interaction is the relationships between the governments or leadership of different countries; pupils discuss how South Sudan's interactions with her neighbours promote peace in the region and benefit its people.

South Sudan's own way: The country's own conflict is the textbook — secession is explained by marginalisation and exploitation of the South's resources, and the constitution enters as homework: explain why South Sudan's constitution is important and name five rights it guarantees.
