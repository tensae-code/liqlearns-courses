# Video script — Childrens Clubs and Scouts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Nepal Grade 6 Social Studies and Human Value Education (printed p. 43), teaches: Organised childhood — clubs and the scout movement.

Nepal Grade 7 Social Studies and Human Value Education (printed p. 43), teaches: The bal club.

Nepal's own way: Grade 6 is where the federation becomes concrete — rural municipality against municipality, each with its services listed — and where conflict itself becomes a school subject: a lesson on disputes and a lesson on resolving them close the social-problems unit. Grade 7 completes the state — elements of the state, democratic government, our constitution, human and women's rights, the provincial government's functions and the security agencies — then turns outward: the United Nations, the proper use of social media, and the world's current events close the book.
