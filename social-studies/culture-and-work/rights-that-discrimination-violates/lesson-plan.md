# Lesson plan — Rights That Discrimination Violates

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Rights That Discrimination Violates
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Palestine, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Practising racial discrimination in society strikes at human dignity, for every person must be respected because of his humanity and treated with respect — and that is the basis of human rights; practising discrimination violates the rights of women, of children, of persons with disabilities and of the marginalised areas, deprives them of the basic rights set out in the international and national legislation, and violates freedom of opinion and expression, the right to participate in public life and manage public affairs, to hold public posts, the right to work and the right to education; and racial discrimination leads to extremism (fanaticism) and hatred in society instead of harmony and tolerance, which weakens society and fragments it and spreads fear, anxiety and selfishness among its members, and brings disputes and civil wars; and the occupation's racist practices and measures violate the Palestinians' right to life, their right to ownership, work and education, and restrict their freedom of movement and travel, and the other rights affirmed by the international human-rights covenants and legislation — as Palestine's book teaches it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
