# Rights That Discrimination Violates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Rights That Discrimination Violates — practising racial discrimination in society strikes at human dignity, for every person must be respected because of his humanity and treated with respect — and that is the basis of human rights; practising discrimination violates the rights of women, of children, of persons with disabilities and of the marginalised areas, deprives them of the basic rights set out in the international and national legislation, and violates freedom of opinion and expression, the right to participate in public life and manage public affairs, to hold public posts, the right to work and the right to education; and racial discrimination leads to extremism (fanaticism) and hatred in society instead of harmony and tolerance, which weakens society and fragments it and spreads fear, anxiety and selfishness among its members, and brings disputes and civil wars; and the occupation's racist practices and measures violate the Palestinians' right to life, their right to ownership, work and education, and restrict their freedom of movement and travel, and the other rights affirmed by the international human-rights covenants and legislation (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
