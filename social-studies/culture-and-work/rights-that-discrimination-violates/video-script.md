# Video script — Rights That Discrimination Violates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Palestine Grade 9 Social Studies Part 2, printed pp. 96–97, teaches: practising racial discrimination in society strikes at human dignity, for every person must be respected because of his humanity and treated with respect — and that is the basis of human rights; practising discrimination violates the rights of women, of children, of persons with disabilities and of the marginalised areas, deprives them of the basic rights set out in the international and national legislation, and violates freedom of opinion and expression, the right to participate in public life and manage public affairs, to hold public posts, the right to work and the right to education; and racial discrimination leads to extremism (fanaticism) and hatred in society instead of harmony and tolerance, which weakens society and fragments it and spreads fear, anxiety and selfishness among its members, and brings disputes and civil wars; and the occupation's racist practices and measures violate the Palestinians' right to life, their right to ownership, work and education, and restrict their freedom of movement and travel, and the other rights affirmed by the international human-rights covenants and legislation.

Palestine's own way: the course ends at world scale and comes home — war is classified and costed, heritage is identity with a UNESCO date (full membership, 31 October 2011), and racial discrimination is defined by the very convention Palestine joined in 2014 without reservations, then answered by its own Basic Law and its protected minorities, the Samaritans of Mount Gerizim first.
