# Hands-on — Values and Democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: democracy as the people's collective decision-making over common life (in a state, a family, an institution), and values as what makes that rule democratic in practice. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
