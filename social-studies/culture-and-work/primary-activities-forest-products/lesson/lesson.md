# Primary activities — forest products and their uses

## Do it first

Report how your family uses and protects forests: what wood comes in, for what, and what is done so trees remain.

## Sub lesson 1 — Two streams from the forest

Of the timber cut each year worldwide, **about 900 million m³ goes to fuel** and **about 1,100 million m³ to industry**.

**Fuel wood mostly cooks food and heats houses. Industrial wood feeds sawmills and pulp/paper.**

[Picture 11508 — one tree trunk splitting into two arrows: a cooking fire and a paper mill]

## Sub lesson 2 — Who uses which

**Brazil, China and Russia lead fuel-wood use**, with Indonesia, the USA, France, Japan and Finland next. **Less-developed countries take the larger share of fuel use**, while **technologically advanced countries dominate industrial wood use**. The hard-trunked fuel trees grow in the **tropics**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Give the two worldwide figures: 900 million m³ fuel, 1,100 million m³ industry. Say what each stream is used for. Say which countries lead each kind of use.
