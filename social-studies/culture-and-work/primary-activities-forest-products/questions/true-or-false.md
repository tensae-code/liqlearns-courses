# Primary activities — forest products and their uses — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fuel wood mostly cooks food and heats houses.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** More timber goes to fuel than to industry, by the book's figures.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The hard-trunked fuel trees grow in the tropics.
**Answer: True**
