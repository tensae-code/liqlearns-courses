# Primary activities — forest products and their uses — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the two yearly figures.
**Answer:** About 900 million m³ to fuel; about 1,100 million m³ to industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three leaders in fuel-wood use.
**Answer:** Brazil, China and Russia.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which group takes the larger share of fuel use?
**Answer:** Less-developed countries.
