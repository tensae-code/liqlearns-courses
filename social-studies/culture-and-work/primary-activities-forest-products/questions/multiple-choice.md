# Primary activities — forest products and their uses — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Timber cut yearly for fuel is about —
a) 1,100 million m³  b) 900 million m³  c) 90 million m³  d) 9 million m³
**Answer: b) 900 million m³**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Industrial wood feeds —
a) Cooking fires  b) Sawmills and pulp/paper  c) Houses' heating only  d) Charcoal pits
**Answer: b) Sawmills and pulp/paper**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which countries lead fuel-wood use?
a) Brazil, China and Russia  b) Only Finland  c) Only the USA  d) Ethiopia, Kenya and Sudan
**Answer: a) Brazil, China and Russia**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Industrial wood use is dominated by —
a) Less-developed countries  b) Technologically advanced countries  c) Island countries  d) Desert countries
**Answer: b) Technologically advanced countries**
