The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Nicaragua Grade 2 Convivencia y Civismo G2 (printed p. 23), teaches: Words can build or wound — speaking with care and keeping one's word.

Nicaragua's own way: civics starts at age 7 with living-together values, then the child's own Constitution, Children's Code and national symbols, and closes on organising for the common good — the state and its duties are present from the first civics book.
