# Identity and Participation in Social Groups

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: identity is legal (birth certificate, identity card, passport, driving licence) and personal (family and cultural roots, values, attitudes); children have the right and duty to participate in social groups — family, school ("second home"), friend groups, school committees, neighbourhood clubs — expressing ideas, taking responsibilities and deciding together — as Honduras's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 6 Participo y transformo

## Sub lesson 2 — Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way

Honduras's own way: Grade 3 is where the state appears in full — nationality by birth and registration, the Constitution as supreme law, the three powers with their real Honduran numbers (128 deputies, 15 magistrates, elections every four years), and citizens' social demands as claims the State is obliged to hear. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
