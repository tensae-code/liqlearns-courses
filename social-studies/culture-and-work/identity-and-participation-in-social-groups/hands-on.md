# Hands-on — Identity and Participation in Social Groups

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 6 Participo y transformo
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Identity is legal (birth certificate, identity card, passport, driving licence) and personal (family and cultural roots, values, attitudes); children have the right and duty to participate in social groups — family, school ("second home"), friend groups, school committees, neighbourhood clubs — expressing ideas, taking responsibilities and deciding together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
