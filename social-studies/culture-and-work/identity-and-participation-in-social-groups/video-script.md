# Video script — Identity and Participation in Social Groups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras Grade 3 G3 Student CCSS, printed p. 30, teaches: Identity is legal (birth certificate, identity card, passport, driving licence) and personal (family and cultural roots, values, attitudes); children have the right and duty to participate in social groups — family, school ("second home"), friend groups, school committees, neighbourhood clubs — expressing ideas, taking responsibilities and deciding together.

Honduras's own way (Book 3): Grade 3 is where the state appears in full — nationality by birth and registration, the Constitution as supreme law, the three powers with their real Honduran numbers (128 deputies, 15 magistrates, elections every four years), and citizens' social demands as claims the State is obliged to hear.
