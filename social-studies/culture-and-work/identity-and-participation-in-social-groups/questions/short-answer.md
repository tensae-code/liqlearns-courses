# Identity and Participation in Social Groups — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 6 Participo y transformo — Identity is legal (birth certificate, identity card, passport, driving licence) and personal (family and cultural roots, values, attitudes); children have the right and duty to participate in social groups — family, school ("second home"), friend groups, school committees, neighbourhood clubs — expressing ideas, taking responsibilities and deciding together (any close wording).**
