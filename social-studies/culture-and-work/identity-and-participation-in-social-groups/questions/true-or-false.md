# Identity and Participation in Social Groups — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: identity is legal (birth certificate, identity card, passport, driving licence) and personal (family and cultural roots, values, attitudes); children have the right and duty to participate in social groups — family, school ("second home"), friend groups, school committees, neighbourhood clubs — expressing ideas, taking responsibilities and deciding together.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
