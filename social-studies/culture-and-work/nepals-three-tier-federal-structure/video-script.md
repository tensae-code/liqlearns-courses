# Video script — Nepals Three Tier Federal Structure

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal Grade 8 Teacher's Guide (2081) (guide p. 16), teaches: Nepal's three tiers — federal, provincial and local — state powers exercised at all three levels, a provincial government in every province, and each pupil's own province, capital, district and local headquarters tabled [TEACHER].

Nepal's own way: the guide opens Grade 8 with development itself — what development is, developed against developing countries, and Nepal's possibilities — then grounds the federation in a table every pupil fills in: their own province, its capital, their district and local headquarters.
