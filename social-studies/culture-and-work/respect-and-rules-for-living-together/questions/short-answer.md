# Respect and Rules for Living Together — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 5 Respetándonos en todo momento — Respect is the base of coexistence; every place (home, school, church, shops) has norms to follow; children's rights do not depend on culture or ethnicity, and the right to say NO to anything that harms us (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — civics begins inside the child's own circles — feelings, family norms and tasks, classroom rules and school authorities, a rehearsal student-government election with a paper ballot, and only then the community's elected authorities (alcaldesa, regidores, patronato, junta de agua). (any close wording).**
