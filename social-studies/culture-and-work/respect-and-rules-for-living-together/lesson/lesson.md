## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 5 Respetándonos en todo momento

This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way

Honduras's own way: civics begins inside the child's own circles — feelings, family norms and tasks, classroom rules and school authorities, a rehearsal student-government election with a paper ballot, and only then the community's elected authorities (alcaldesa, regidores, patronato, junta de agua). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
