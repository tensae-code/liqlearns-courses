# Video script — Respect and Rules for Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras Grade 1 G1 Student CCSS, printed p. 26, teaches: Respect is the base of coexistence; every place (home, school, church, shops) has norms to follow; children's rights do not depend on culture or ethnicity, and the right to say NO to anything that harms us.

Honduras's own way (Book 1): civics begins inside the child's own circles — feelings, family norms and tasks, classroom rules and school authorities, a rehearsal student-government election with a paper ballot, and only then the community's elected authorities (alcaldesa, regidores, patronato, junta de agua).
