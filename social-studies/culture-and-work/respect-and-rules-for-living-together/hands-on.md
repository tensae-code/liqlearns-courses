# Hands-on — Respect and Rules for Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 5 Respetándonos en todo momento
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Respect is the base of coexistence; every place (home, school, church, shops) has norms to follow; children's rights do not depend on culture or ethnicity, and the right to say NO to anything that harms us. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
