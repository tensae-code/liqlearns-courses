## Sub lesson 1 — Benefits of Laws and Regulations

Saudi Arabia Grade 8 Social Studies (printed pp. 216–219), teaches: The benefits of systems and regulations — organisational (ordering government bodies and their relations), social (protecting the individual and society and treating social problems), developmental (achieving development and sustainability) and economic (stimulating the economy through transparency and order); how systems treat society's problems and protect people's rights; and the results of committing to systems: strengthened citizenship, bearing responsibility, development, keeping the individual's rights, better services, justice and equality among all, security and stability, and protecting society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 8 book places Saudi Arabia inside the Gulf Cooperation Council — its organs and goals, its achievements and its challenges — then teaches systems and regulations (how a system is made, their kinds and their benefits), and ends with planning priorities, time management and evaluating plans, and hobbies including the old folk games. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
