# Hands-on — Benefits of Laws and Regulations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the benefits of systems and regulations — organisational (ordering government bodies and their relations), social (protecting the individual and society and treating social problems), developmental (achieving development and sustainability) and economic (stimulating the economy through transparency and order); how systems treat society's problems and protect people's rights; and the results of committing to systems: strengthened citizenship, bearing responsibility, development, keeping the individual's rights, better services, justice and equality among all, security and stability, and protecting society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
