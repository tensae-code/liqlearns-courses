# National Celebrations of Trinidad and Tobago

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Independence Day and what independence changed, Republic Day, Remembrance Day — as Trinidad and Tobago's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — National Celebrations

Trinidad and Tobago Standard 3 Jeanville Social Studies Std 3 (printed p. 141), teaches: Independence Day and what independence changed, Republic Day, Remembrance Day. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Arrival, Emancipation and Labour Days

Trinidad and Tobago Standard 3 Jeanville Social Studies Std 3 (printed p. 154), teaches: Indian Arrival Day, Emancipation Day and Labour Day (the 1937 Butler riots and workers' rights) as national days. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Trinidad and Tobago's way — the book's own teaching way

Trinidad and Tobago's own way: one "My Country" theme for the whole year — the multicultural society is taught as "Different But The Same": each ancestral people's contributions first, then the FUSION of food, music and dance into forms unique to Trinidad and Tobago, then the emblems, pledge and festival calendar that unite the same peoples. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
