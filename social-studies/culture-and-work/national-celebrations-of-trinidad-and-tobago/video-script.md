# Video script — National Celebrations of Trinidad and Tobago

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Trinidad and Tobago Standard 3 Jeanville Social Studies Std 3 (printed p. 141), teaches: Independence Day and what independence changed, Republic Day, Remembrance Day.

Trinidad and Tobago Standard 3 Jeanville Social Studies Std 3 (printed p. 154), teaches: Indian Arrival Day, Emancipation Day and Labour Day (the 1937 Butler riots and workers' rights) as national days.

Trinidad and Tobago's own way: one "My Country" theme for the whole year — the multicultural society is taught as "Different But The Same": each ancestral people's contributions first, then the FUSION of food, music and dance into forms unique to Trinidad and Tobago, then the emblems, pledge and festival calendar that unite the same peoples.
