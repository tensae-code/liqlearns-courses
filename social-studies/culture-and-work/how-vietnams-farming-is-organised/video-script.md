Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 66–68), teaches: Bài 14: How Vietnam's farming is organised on the land — farms (26,111 in 2024, livestock farms now the largest group), hi-tech agriculture zones (22 planned by 2030; Ho Chi Minh City's Cu Chi zone the first model) and specialised farming zones (lychee in Hai Phong and Bac Ninh, tea in Lam Dong, sugarcane, rice and coffee belts).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
