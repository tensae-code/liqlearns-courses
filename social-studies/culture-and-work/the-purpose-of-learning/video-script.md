The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Laos Grade 6 laos-g06-civics-teacher (teacher guide), printed p. 47, teaches: Learning has a purpose beyond oneself — to become a good citizen who builds family, society and a strong nation.

Laos's own way (Book 10): the first secondary civics year moves from personal virtues (diligence, thrift, discipline, gratitude, manners) to named legal rights, each rights chapter anchored in a numbered article of Laos's own 2003 Constitution or a named law — the pupil meets the state first as the protector of their own life, home and schooling.
