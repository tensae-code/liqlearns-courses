# Hands-on — Expressing My Opinion Respecting Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## I express my opinion and I respect the other's opinion
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 6 Al-Massar Ijtiamiat, printed p. 131, teaches: what an opinion is and what the other's opinion is; the rules of expressing and of mutual respect in discussion (state your view, listen, answer the idea not the person, accept the conclusion the discussion reaches); steps for keeping mutual respect while debating a chosen topic — applied to stadium violence, where failing to respect the other's view turns a match into a fight. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the Grade 6 civics course is a working apprenticeship in democracy — it opens with law, freedom and responsibility (Rousseau: no freedom without responsibility), runs the class as a republic (charter, elected cooperative), then walks out of the school gate: local democracy is learned by studying the elected communal council from the 2011 Constitution (Articles 135 and 139), visiting the commune headquarters, simulating a full council meeting with agenda, president, budget rapporteur and association representatives, and finally pleading a real solidarity proposal before that council. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
