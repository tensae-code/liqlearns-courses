# Hands-on — National Integration and Cohesion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 8 National Integration and Cohesion: Definition, Factors and Importance, Ch. 4 Major Features of Pakistan's Culture and Commonality in Regional Cultures, Ch. 8 (National Integration): the virtues that hold the nation together — teaches: integration defined as parts merged into a whole and rival interests reconciled; its six factors (common religion, territory, language, race, tradition, democracy) and its fruits — prosperity, peace and order, mutual cooperation, welfare, strong administration, no wastage; the provinces' cultures read one by one (Punjabi culture: Luddi dance, horse riding, devotion to Sufi saints, the folk tales of Heer Ranjha, Sohni Mahiwal and Mirza Saheban) so that their commonality can be seen leading to national integration and cohesion; رواداری (tolerance — bearing difference without breaking), قوم سے وفاداری (loyalty to the nation above province and party), and دوسروں کے حقوق کا احترام (respect for the rights of others) listed among the factors of national unity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
