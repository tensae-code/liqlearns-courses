Pakistan's Grade 12 PS12-2014 (printed pp. 101–102); Grade 10 PS10-KPK (printed pp. 101, 109); Grade 12 PS12-KPK (printed pp. 151–155), teaches: Ch. 8 National Integration and Cohesion: Definition, Factors and Importance, Ch. 4 Major Features of Pakistan's Culture and Commonality in Regional Cultures, Ch. 8 (National Integration): the virtues that hold the nation together — teaches: integration defined as parts merged into a whole and rival interests reconciled; its six factors (common religion, territory, language, race, tradition, democracy) and its fruits — prosperity, peace and order, mutual cooperation, welfare, strong administration, no wastage; the provinces' cultures read one by one (Punjabi culture: Luddi dance, horse riding, devotion to Sufi saints, the folk tales of Heer Ranjha, Sohni Mahiwal and Mirza Saheban) so that their commonality can be seen leading to national integration and cohesion; رواداری (tolerance — bearing difference without breaking), قوم سے وفاداری (loyalty to the nation above province and party), and دوسروں کے حقوق کا احترام (respect for the rights of others) listed among the factors of national unity.

Pakistan's own way: the older book teaches the same state from the other end — culture defined before it is described, languages given a whole chapter with Urdu named the language of national integration, and national integration itself defined, factored and argued like a civics concept, not a slogan. KPK's Class 10 book ends where the PCTB book's culture chapter begins — society first (its features and its named evils: feudalism, the curse of dowry, child labour), then culture defined as a people's inherited way of life, province by province, with the minorities' role and Jinnah's 11 August 1947 speech as the closing civic ground. the same state taught in the nation's own language — the ideology's four components are listed in Urdu as Islam, democracy, social justice-and-equality, and fundamental human rights, and the integration chapter's virtues are named رواداری (tolerance), قوم سے وفاداری (loyalty to the nation) and دوسروں کے حقوق کا احترام (respect for the rights of others).
