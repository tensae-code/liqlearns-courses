## Sub lesson 2 — Pakistan's way — the book's own teaching way

Pakistan's own way: the older book teaches the same state from the other end — culture defined before it is described, languages given a whole chapter with Urdu named the language of national integration, and national integration itself defined, factored and argued like a civics concept, not a slogan. KPK's Class 10 book ends where the PCTB book's culture chapter begins — society first (its features and its named evils: feudalism, the curse of dowry, child labour), then culture defined as a people's inherited way of life, province by province, with the minorities' role and Jinnah's 11 August 1947 speech as the closing civic ground. the same state taught in the nation's own language — the ideology's four components are listed in Urdu as Islam, democracy, social justice-and-equality, and fundamental human rights, and the integration chapter's virtues are named رواداری (tolerance), قوم سے وفاداری (loyalty to the nation) and دوسروں کے حقوق کا احترام (respect for the rights of others).
