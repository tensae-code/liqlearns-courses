# Video script — Law Abiding Spirit and Civil Disobedience

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Korea's Grade 10 Donga Integrated Society, printed p. 112, teaches: IV.04 준법 의식과 시민 불복종이 필요한 이유는? — teaches: why the law-abiding spirit is needed — and when civil disobedience against an unjust law is justified.

South Korea's own way: Donga writes every lesson as a question ("Why is civil disobedience ever needed?", "What does universal ethics do in understanding a culture?") and is the only one of the five publishers to teach civil disobedience (시민 불복종) beside the law-abiding spirit, and to split the conditions of happiness into two lessons — living environment/economic stability, then democracy's development and moral practice.
