## Sub lesson 1 — Law Abiding Spirit and Civil Disobedience

South Korea's Grade 10 Donga Integrated Society, printed p. 112, teaches: IV.04 준법 의식과 시민 불복종이 필요한 이유는? — teaches: why the law-abiding spirit is needed — and when civil disobedience against an unjust law is justified. ## Sub lesson 2 — South Korea's way — the books' own teaching way

South Korea's own way: Donga writes every lesson as a question ("Why is civil disobedience ever needed?", "What does universal ethics do in understanding a culture?") and is the only one of the five publishers to teach civil disobedience (시민 불복종) beside the law-abiding spirit, and to split the conditions of happiness into two lessons — living environment/economic stability, then democracy's development and moral practice. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
