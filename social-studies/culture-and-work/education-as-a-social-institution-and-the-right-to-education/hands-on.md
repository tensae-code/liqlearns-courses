# Hands-on — Education as a Social Institution and the Right to Education

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Education as a social institution and a purposeful process — the shift "from education for the whole life to education through the whole life"; the right to education as a basic right, quoted from Article 49 of the Constitution (secondary specialised and higher education accessible to all, free education in state institutions on a competitive basis); the levels of education in the Republic of Belarus; and education for sustainable development in the UN's 2030 Agenda — lifelong learning opportunities for all. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Belarus's way
Say how Belarus' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
