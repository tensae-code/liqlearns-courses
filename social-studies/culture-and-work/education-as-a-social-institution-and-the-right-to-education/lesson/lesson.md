## Sub lesson 1 — Education (Образование) — Grade 10

Belarus Grade 10 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 210), teaches: Education as a social institution and a purposeful process — the shift "from education for the whole life to education through the whole life"; the right to education as a basic right, quoted from Article 49 of the Constitution (secondary specialised and higher education accessible to all, free education in state institutions on a competitive basis); the levels of education in the Republic of Belarus; and education for sustainable development in the UN's 2030 Agenda — lifelong learning opportunities for all. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
