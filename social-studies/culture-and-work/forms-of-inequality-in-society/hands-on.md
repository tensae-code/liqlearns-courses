# Hands-on — Forms of Inequality in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: VI.주제4 다양한 불평등 현상, VI.03 사회 불평등의 양상과 그 원인은? — teaches: the many forms of inequality — income, opportunity, region, gender — and their faces in society; the faces of social inequality and their causes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Korea's way
Say how South Korea's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
