# Growing Up Boys and Girls Gender Roles — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch 4 Growing Up as Boys and Girls — society, not nature, assigns boys' and girls' roles (Samoa in the 1920s against Madhya Pradesh in the 1960s), and women's housework is real work that goes unvalued (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: India's way — the book's own teaching way — Equality is taught through real lives — a domestic worker voting, a Dalit writer made to sweep his school, a Muslim couple refused a flat — and government through one running case (a diarrhoea outbreak in a fictional district) that forces every institution to act.
