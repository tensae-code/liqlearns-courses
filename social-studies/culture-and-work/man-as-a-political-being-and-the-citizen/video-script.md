# Video script — Man as a Political Being and the Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Armenia Grade 7 Social Science student, printed p. 130, teaches: politics grows from living together beyond the family; the citizen as a member of a political community who enjoys membership rights and takes on duties; Armenia as a republic whose citizens hold state-given rights and duties, fixed by birth certificate and passport.

Armenia's own way: Social Science opens with the person, not the state — nature, society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and citizenship is introduced from the child's own documents: an Armenian citizen's membership is "fixed by the birth certificate and the passport", with rights from the state and duties owed back.
