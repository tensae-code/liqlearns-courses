# Man as a Political Being and the Citizen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: 8.1 Man as a political being — politics grows from living together beyond the family; the citizen as a member of a political community who enjoys membership rights and takes on duties; Armenia as a republic whose citizens hold state-given rights and duties, fixed by birth certificate and passport (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Armenia's way — the book's own teaching way — Social Science opens with the person, not the state — nature, society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and citizenship is introduced from the child's own documents: an Armenian citizen's membership is "fixed by the birth certificate and the passport", with rights from the state and duties owed back. (any close wording).**
