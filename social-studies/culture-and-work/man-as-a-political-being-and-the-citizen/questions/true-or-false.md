# Man as a Political Being and the Citizen — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part 1 teaches: politics grows from living together beyond the family; the citizen as a member of a political community who enjoys membership rights and takes on duties; Armenia as a republic whose citizens hold state-given rights and duties, fixed by birth certificate and passport.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part 2 teaches: Social Science opens with the person, not the state — nature, society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and citizenship is introduced from the child's own documents: an Armenian citizen's membership is "fixed by the birth certificate and the passport", with rights from the state and duties owed back..
**Answer: True**
