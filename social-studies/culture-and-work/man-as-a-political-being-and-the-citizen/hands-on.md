# Hands-on — Man as a Political Being and the Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: politics grows from living together beyond the family; the citizen as a member of a political community who enjoys membership rights and takes on duties; Armenia as a republic whose citizens hold state-given rights and duties, fixed by birth certificate and passport. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
