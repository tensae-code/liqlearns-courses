# Hands-on — Voting a Right and a Civic Duty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 4 "El voto"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed pp. 191–192, teaches: the vote as the citizen's direct participation — choosing freely who will govern; under the Electoral and Political Parties Law it is a right and a civic duty, and it is universal (all citizens over 18), secret, single, personal and non-transferable. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
