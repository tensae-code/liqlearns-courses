# Voting a Right and a Civic Duty — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part teaches: Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed pp. 191–192, teaches: the vote as the citizen's direct participation — choosing freely who will govern; under the Electoral and Political Parties Law it is a right and a civic duty, and it is universal (all citizens over 18), secret, single, personal and non-transferable.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part is Guatemala's own way of teaching this thing, as named in lesson/sources.md.
**Answer: True**
