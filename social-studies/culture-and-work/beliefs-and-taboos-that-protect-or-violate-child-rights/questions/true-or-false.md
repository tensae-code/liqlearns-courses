# Beliefs and Taboos That Protect or Violate Child Rights — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Beliefs are ideas people hold to be true and taboos are actions a cultural group forbids; beliefs and taboos that uphold child rights include respect for elders, the taboo on harming children, valuing education and the community's responsibility to protect children — while female genital mutilation, child marriage and corporal punishment violate children's rights.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Zimbabwe's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
