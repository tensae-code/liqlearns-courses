# Hands-on — Beliefs and Taboos That Protect or Violate Child Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: beliefs are ideas people hold to be true and taboos are actions a cultural group forbids; beliefs and taboos that uphold child rights include respect for elders, the taboo on harming children, valuing education and the community's responsibility to protect children — while female genital mutilation, child marriage and corporal punishment violate children's rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
