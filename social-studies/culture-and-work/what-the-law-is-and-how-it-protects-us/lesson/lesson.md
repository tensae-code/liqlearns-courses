# What the Law Is And How It Protects Us

## Sub lesson 1 — Section 4.4 "What do we need to know about the law?"

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work.
