# Hands-on — What the Law Is And How It Protects Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.4 "What do we need to know about the law?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Law arose when people agreed to be ruled not by one man's will but by a law all accept — the story of the tyrant khan whose subjects at last demanded leaders appointed with the people's consent, bound by the law they make; laws are enacted by the State Great Khural to regulate relations and protect rights, and school rules are the same kind of instrument at school scale; the law protects children by name — no heavy labour for a child, a parent or teacher present when a child gives evidence, fines for littering and for taking a child to a healer without the parents' consent; and in the case of the neighbouring family, the whole protection team — khoroo governor, social worker, police, school, the child helpline 108 — acts together for one small boy. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
