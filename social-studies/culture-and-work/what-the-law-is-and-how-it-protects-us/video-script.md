# Video script — What the Law Is And How It Protects Us

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 7 Social Studies, printed p. 72, teaches: Law arose when people agreed to be ruled not by one man's will but by a law all accept — the story of the tyrant khan whose subjects at last demanded leaders appointed with the people's consent, bound by the law they make; laws are enacted by the State Great Khural to regulate relations and protect rights, and school rules are the same kind of instrument at school scale; the law protects children by name — no heavy labour for a child, a parent or teacher present when a child gives evidence, fines for littering and for taking a child to a healer without the parents' consent; and in the case of the neighbouring family, the whole protection team — khoroo governor, social worker, police, school, the child helpline 108 — acts together for one small boy.

Mongolia's own way (Book 5): the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work.
