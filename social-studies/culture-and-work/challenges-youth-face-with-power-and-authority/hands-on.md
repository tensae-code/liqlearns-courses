# Hands-on — Challenges Youth Face with Power and Authority

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The challenges youths face with power and authority, and that addressing them needs partnership between civil society and young people, through initiatives for youth empowerment and inclusivity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
