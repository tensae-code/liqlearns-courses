The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 221–227), teaches: The institutions that achieve security — the Ministry of Defence (founded 1363H; preparing and commanding the armed forces against external aggression), the Ministry of Interior (founded 1350H; security and stability, fighting crime, border security, pilgrims' safety, citizens' rights, traffic and civil safety, civil affairs — its sectors: Public Security, Border Guard, Civil Defence, Passports, Anti-Narcotics, and the Absher e-services platform), the Ministry of National Guard (1374H; defending the land and borders, guarding the holy sanctuaries and internal security), and the Presidency of State Security (1438H; the General Investigation Directorate, the Special Emergency Forces, the Special Security Forces and Security Aviation).

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue.
