# Hands-on — National Security Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the institutions that achieve security — the Ministry of Defence (founded 1363H; preparing and commanding the armed forces against external aggression), the Ministry of Interior (founded 1350H; security and stability, fighting crime, border security, pilgrims' safety, citizens' rights, traffic and civil safety, civil affairs — its sectors: Public Security, Border Guard, Civil Defence, Passports, Anti-Narcotics, and the Absher e-services platform), the Ministry of National Guard (1374H; defending the land and borders, guarding the holy sanctuaries and internal security), and the Presidency of State Security (1438H; the General Investigation Directorate, the Special Emergency Forces, the Special Security Forces and Security Aviation). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
