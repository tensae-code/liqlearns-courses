# Lesson plan — National Security Institutions

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** National Security Institutions
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Saudi Arabia, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the institutions that achieve security — the Ministry of Defence (founded 1363H; preparing and commanding the armed forces against external aggression), the Ministry of Interior (founded 1350H; security and stability, fighting crime, border security, pilgrims' safety, citizens' rights, traffic and civil safety, civil affairs — its sectors: Public Security, Border Guard, Civil Defence, Passports, Anti-Narcotics, and the Absher e-services platform), the Ministry of National Guard (1374H; defending the land and borders, guarding the holy sanctuaries and internal security), and the Presidency of State Security (1438H; the General Investigation Directorate, the Special Emergency Forces, the Special Security Forces and Security Aviation) — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — the institutions that achieve security — the Ministry of Defence (founded 1363H; preparing and commanding the armed forces against external aggression), the Ministry of Interior (founded 1350H; security and stability, fighting crime, border security, pilgrims' safety, citizens' rights, traffic and civil safety, civil affairs — its sectors: Public Security, Border Guard, Civil Defence, Passports, Anti-Narcotics, and the Absher e-services platform), the Ministry of National Guard (1374H; defending the land and borders, guarding the holy sanctuaries and internal security), and the Presidency of State Security (1438H; the General Investigation Directorate, the Special Emergency Forces, the Special Security Forces and Security Aviation).
