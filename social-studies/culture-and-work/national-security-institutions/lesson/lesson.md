## Sub lesson 1 — National Security Institutions

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 221–227), teaches: The institutions that achieve security — the Ministry of Defence (founded 1363H; preparing and commanding the armed forces against external aggression), the Ministry of Interior (founded 1350H; security and stability, fighting crime, border security, pilgrims' safety, citizens' rights, traffic and civil safety, civil affairs — its sectors: Public Security, Border Guard, Civil Defence, Passports, Anti-Narcotics, and the Absher e-services platform), the Ministry of National Guard (1374H; defending the land and borders, guarding the holy sanctuaries and internal security), and the Presidency of State Security (1438H; the General Investigation Directorate, the Special Emergency Forces, the Special Security Forces and Security Aviation). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
