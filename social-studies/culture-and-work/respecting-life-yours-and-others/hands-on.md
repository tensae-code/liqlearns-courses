# Hands-on — Respecting Life Yours and Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a life just born is the one and only precious life; the wish inside your own name, heard from your family; when we feel "I am alive"; and the tale of the elephants' graveyard. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
