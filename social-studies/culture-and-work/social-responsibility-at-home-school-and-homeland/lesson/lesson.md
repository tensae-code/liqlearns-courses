## Sub lesson 1 — Social responsibility

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G9 teacher guide, volume 2, printed p. 143, teaches: the conditions of responsibility (شروط المسؤولية) and its qualities; its importance in the individual's life; the individual's responsibilities in his family, his school and his homeland (في أسرته ومدرسته ووطنه); and telling the kinds of responsibility apart This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: the volume closes the year with identity — a whole unit on Zayed as "an identity and witnessed stances", in which the Emirati woman's changing place in society and the citizen's social responsibility are taught as parts of that identity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
