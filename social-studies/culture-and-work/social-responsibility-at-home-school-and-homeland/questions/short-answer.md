# Social Responsibility at Home School and Homeland — short answer

**Answer: Social responsibility — the conditions of responsibility (شروط المسؤولية) and its qualities; its importance in the individual's life; the individual's responsibilities in his family, his school and his homeland (في أسرته ومدرسته ووطنه); and telling the kinds of responsibility apart (any close wording).**
