## Sub lesson 1 — Ecuador a Plurinational and Intercultural State

Ecuador Grade 8 Estudios Sociales 8vo EGB, printed p. 162, teaches: Why Ecuador is an intercultural country and not a homogeneous culture (Mama Negra, Años Viejos, the toquilla-straw hat), its fourteen recognised nationality languages (Kichwa, Shuar, Tsáfiqui, Achuar, Awapit…), and how intercultural bilingual education and ethno-education build a plurinational state. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ecuador's way — the book's own teaching way

Ecuador's own way: at Superior level the civics strand is built on Ecuador's own 2008 constitutional order — Buen Vivir (Sumak Kawsay) as the state's development idea, a plurinational and intercultural state, nature itself as a subject of rights, and children's rights taught from Ecuador's own Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
