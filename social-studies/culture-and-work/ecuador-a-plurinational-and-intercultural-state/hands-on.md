# Hands-on — Ecuador a Plurinational and Intercultural State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Why Ecuador is an intercultural country and not a homogeneous culture (Mama Negra, Años Viejos, the toquilla-straw hat), its fourteen recognised nationality languages (Kichwa, Shuar, Tsáfiqui, Achuar, Awapit…), and how intercultural bilingual education and ethno-education build a plurinational state. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ecuador's way
Say how Ecuador's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
