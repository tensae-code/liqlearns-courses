# Ecuador a Plurinational and Intercultural State — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Why Ecuador is an intercultural country and not a homogeneous culture (Mama Negra, Años Viejos, the toquilla-straw hat), its fourteen recognised nationality languages (Kichwa, Shuar, Tsáfiqui, Achuar, Awapit…), and how intercultural bilingual education and ethno-education build a plurinational state.
