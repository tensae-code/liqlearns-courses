# The Peoples of Guyana and Their Ways of Life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Guyanese People: The Amerindians — the first people's way of life in Guyana today (their customs and foods within the arrival story, which is History and named not placed) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Guyanese People: The Africans — African Guyanese ways of life carried into Guyana — foods and dance among them (the enslavement and emancipation story is History, named not placed) (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Guyanese People: The East Indians — East Indian Guyanese customs — birth customs (the baby's head-shaving), formal dress and foods (indentureship arrival story is History, named not placed) (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the fourth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Amerindians: their settlements — Amerindian reservations/settlements led by a Touchau or Captain, and how their houses changed from large shared houses of shingle and troolie palm to individual enclosed houses of boards, zinc and mud-blocks (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does the fifth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Amerindians: how they work and their culture — Amerindians still work together co-operatively, in hunting, fishing, farming, timber, mining and community services, sell craft work, and keep their culture in tales and the Mari-Mari, Baboon and Carrion Crow dances and the Mashramani merriment meeting (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the sixth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Amerindians: food and place names — cassava is the staple — cassava bread, farine, casareep and pepperpot — and Amerindians named Guyana's lakes, rivers and falls (Kaieteur after Old Kaie; Bartica, "Red Earth"; names ending in "tuk" mean falls) (any close wording).**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** What does the seventh part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Story of the Africans: the villages they bought — freed Africans pooled their savings and bought whole plantations, founding villages — Victoria the first, then Buxton (Kofi's village), Friendship, Beterverwagting and Plaisance (any close wording).**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** What does the eighth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Story of the Africans: village life and Freedom Day — the bought villages were run as co-operative farms, and Freedom Day (1 August) is still celebrated with African songs, dancing and the Masquerade, Mother Sally and Bad-cow dances (any close wording).**

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** What does the ninth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The coming of the Portuguese, East Indians and Chinese — the Portuguese left the estates to open businesses still running today, the Chinese followed them into business, and the East Indians stayed to grow rice and brought cultures still practised — Indian dishes enjoyed and festivals (Phagwah, Diwali, Eid-Ul-Azha, Eid-Ul-Fitr) taken part in by all (any close wording).**

**10. (Sub lesson 10)** What does the tenth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Amerindians: the first people — the first people of Guyana and their way of life — their crafts, music and the foods they grow and make (any close wording).**

**11. (Sub lesson 11)** What does the eleventh part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Europeans — the Europeans' way of life in Guyana — their clothing, houses and building styles that still stand (any close wording).**

**12. (Sub lesson 12)** What does the twelfth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Africans — the Africans' way of life — their folk songs, music, dances and stories (any close wording).**

**13. (Sub lesson 13)** What does the thirteenth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Portuguese — the Portuguese in Guyana and the work and businesses they became known for (any close wording).**

**14. (Sub lesson 14)** What does the fourteenth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The East Indians — the East Indians' way of life — their foods, music and dress (any close wording).**

**15. (Sub lesson 15)** What does the fifteenth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Chinese — the Chinese in Guyana and how they lived and worked after arriving as indentured immigrants (any close wording).**

**16. (Sub lesson 16)** What does the sixteenth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Amerindians of Guyana — the first people of Guyana, nine groups living across the regions, using the land for food and shelter, with their songs, instruments (banjos, flutes, maracas), dances (Mari Mari, Humming Bird), foods (cassava bread, pepperpot, farine) and nine languages (any close wording).**

**17. (Sub lesson 17)** What does the seventeenth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Europeans of Guyana — the British, Portuguese, Dutch and Spanish and what they brought — their worship, instruments (mandolin, lyre, accordion), dances (Maypole, Waltz, samba, flamenco), foods (pastries, garlic pork) and clothes (any close wording).**

**18. (Sub lesson 18)** What does the eighteenth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Africans of Guyana — the Africans, brought as slaves to the sugarcane plantations, and what they brought — folksongs, African drums and shak shak, the Cumfa dance, and foods such as conkie, cook-up rice, foo-foo and metemgee (any close wording).**

**19. (Sub lesson 19)** What does the nineteenth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The East Indians of Guyana — the East Indians, who came as indentured labourers, and what they brought — bhajans and chowtal songs, sitar, dhantal, dholak and tassa, kathak and odissi dances, and roti, curry and dhal (any close wording).**

**20. (Sub lesson 20)** What does the twentieth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Chinese of Guyana — the Chinese, who also came as indentured labourers, and what they brought — flute, violin and drum, the dragon dance, and fried rice, chow mein and wanton soup (any close wording).**

**21. (Sub lesson 21)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
