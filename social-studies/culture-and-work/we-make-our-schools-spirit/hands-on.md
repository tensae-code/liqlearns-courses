# Hands-on — We Make Our Schools Spirit

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the teachers and the lunch and office people who look after us at school, what we can do together to make our class nicer, and finding our school's treasures. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
