# Sacred Texts, Food and Clothing of Religions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A sacred text is —
a) a holy writing that a religion's people read and keep  b) a map of Iceland  c) a shopping list
**Answer: a) a holy writing that a religion's people read and keep**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A symbol of a religion is —
a) a sign that stands for that religion  b) a kind of food  c) a school rule
**Answer: a) a sign that stands for that religion**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's teaching, food and clothing —
a) can be part of how people live their belief  b) have nothing to do with religion  c) are the same for all five religions
**Answer: a) can be part of how people live their belief**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The sacred texts, food, clothing and symbols stand in the student book at —
a) pp. 38–43  b) p. 1  c) the contents page only
**Answer: a) pp. 38–43**
