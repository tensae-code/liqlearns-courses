# Sacred Texts, Food and Clothing of Religions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things, besides festivals, by which each religion is known in this lesson.
**Answer: Its sacred texts and its symbols (food and clothing also accepted) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How do food and clothing connect to religion, in the guide's own question?
**Answer: What people eat and what they wear can be part of how they live their belief (any close wording).**
