# Hands-on — Sacred Texts, Food and Clothing of Religions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the sign to the religion
Draw the five religions as five boxes. In each box draw a symbol and a sacred text for that religion, as the lesson presents them. You are matching each religion's signs to the religion they stand for.

## Food, clothing and belief
Pick one religion from the lesson. Write two lines: one food and one item of clothing that go with it, and how they connect to that religion's belief.
