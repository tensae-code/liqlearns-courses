A religion can be known by more than its festivals.

Each of the five religions has its sacred texts — its holy writings — and its symbols.

Each has its food and its clothing too: what its people eat and wear can be part of their belief.

Sacred texts, symbols, food and clothing — four ways of knowing a religion.
