# Sacred Texts, Food and Clothing of Religions

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: A religion is not only its festivals: each religion also has holy writings, special foods, clothing its people wear, and symbols that stand for it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sacred texts and symbols

The teacher guide for Trúarbrögðin okkar (Our Religions) presents the five religions — Buddhism, Hinduism, Christianity, Islam and Judaism — each with its sacred texts: the holy writings its people read and keep. Each religion also has its symbols, signs that stand for it — the guide's cover itself shows the five children holding the symbols of the five religions. The student book gathers these at pp. 38–43, as the guide presents them at printed p. 17.

## Sub lesson 2 — Food and clothing

Food and clothing belong to religion too. What people eat, and what they wear, can be part of how they live their belief — the guide's own opening questions ask how food and clothing connect to religion. So the five religions are known by their food and their clothing as well, at student book pp. 38–43: the foods and dress that go with each child's religion.
