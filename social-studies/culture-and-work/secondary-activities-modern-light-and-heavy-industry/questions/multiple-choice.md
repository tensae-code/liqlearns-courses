# Secondary activities — modern light and heavy industry — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Modern manufacturing produces —
a) By hand at home only  b) In factories with machines driven by power  c) Only services  d) Only raw materials
**Answer: b) In factories with machines driven by power**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A mark of modern manufacturing is —
a) Tiny output  b) Very large output and much capital  c) No division of labour  d) Untrained manpower only
**Answer: b) Very large output and much capital**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Textiles, food and office goods are —
a) Heavy industry  b) Light industry  c) Cottage only  d) Primary activity
**Answer: b) Light industry**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Shipbuilding and railway engineering are —
a) Light industry  b) Heavy industry  c) Tertiary activity  d) Subsistence farming
**Answer: b) Heavy industry**
