# Secondary activities — modern light and heavy industry — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three marks of modern manufacturing.
**Answer:** Any three of: very large output, much capital, powerful machines, high division of labour, much fully or partly trained manpower.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell light from heavy in one line each.
**Answer:** Light: few raw materials, consumer goods. Heavy: bulky inputs in bulk, heavy goods.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three heavy-industry examples.
**Answer:** Any three of: metal engineering, shipbuilding, railway engineering, aircraft, vehicles.
