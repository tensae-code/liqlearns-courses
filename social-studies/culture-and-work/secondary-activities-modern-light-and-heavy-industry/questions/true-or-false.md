# Secondary activities — modern light and heavy industry — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Heavy industries demand the most capital and raw material.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Light industries use bulky inputs in bulk.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A high division of labour is a mark of modern manufacturing.
**Answer: True**
