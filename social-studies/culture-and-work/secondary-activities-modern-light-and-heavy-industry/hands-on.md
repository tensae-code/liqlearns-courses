# Hands-on — Secondary activities — modern light and heavy industry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Sort ten factory-made things in your home into light-industry goods and heavy-industry goods. Write the sorting.

## Activity 2
Compare a cottage weaver and a textile mill: labour, power, output. Write three differences using the lesson's marks.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Name the five marks of modern manufacturing. Tell light from heavy industry, with the book's examples of each.
