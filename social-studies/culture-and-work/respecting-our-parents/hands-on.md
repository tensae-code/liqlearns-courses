# Hands-on — Respecting Our Parents

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: respecting parents as a quality of a good citizen — succeeding at school, thankfulness, caring for them in old age and sickness, and obeying their advice. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
