# The Right Decision Thinking Honestly Before I Choose — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Deciding honestly — the shopkeeper who used to give a small sweet with the father's permission offers the boy, alone today, an expensive piece free if he tells no one; the class weighs the request, sorts which decisions are mine, mine-with-family, or others', works decision situations, and keeps a diary of decisions taken and why.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Grade 2 is the honesty-and-safety year — the right-decision lesson turns on a shopkeeper who offers a boy an expensive sweet free if he tells no one, the careful lesson warns that things found on the road may be explosives, and rights arrive as a shipwreck game: children who trash a dream island invent rules, and discover the balance — "my right to enjoy nature, my duty to protect it"..
**Answer: True**
