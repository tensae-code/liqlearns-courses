# Hands-on — The Right Decision Thinking Honestly Before I Choose

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: deciding honestly — the shopkeeper who used to give a small sweet with the father's permission offers the boy, alone today, an expensive piece free if he tells no one; the class weighs the request, sorts which decisions are mine, mine-with-family, or others', works decision situations, and keeps a diary of decisions taken and why. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
