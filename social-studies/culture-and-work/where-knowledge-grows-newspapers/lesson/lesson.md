## Sub lesson 1 — Meelaha laga kordhisto aqoonta guud (Places Where General Knowledge Grows)

Somalia Grade 6 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 75), teaches: a citizen keeps learning — the newspaper first among the sources: it must spread good ideas at home and abroad, refuse anything that harms the country's interests, and write what benefits the public, uniting people's thought in unity, equality, mutual respect and cooperation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 6): by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
