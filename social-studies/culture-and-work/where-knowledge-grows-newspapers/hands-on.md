# Hands-on — Where Knowledge Grows Newspapers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Meelaha laga kordhisto aqoonta guud (Places Where General Knowledge Grows)
Work through this part with your hands: a citizen keeps learning — the newspaper first among the sources: it must spread good ideas at home and abroad, refuse anything that harms the country's interests, and write what benefits the public, uniting people's thought in unity, equality, mutual respect and cooperation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
