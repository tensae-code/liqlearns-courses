# Where Knowledge Grows Newspapers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Meelaha laga kordhisto aqoonta guud (Places Where General Knowledge Grows) — a citizen keeps learning — the newspaper first among the sources: it must spread good ideas at home and abroad, refuse anything that harms the country's interests, and write what benefits the public, uniting people's thought in unity, equality, mutual respect and cooperation (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way — by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed. (any close wording).**
