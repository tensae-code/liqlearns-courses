# Where Knowledge Grows Newspapers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Meelaha laga kordhisto aqoonta guud (Places Where General Knowledge Grows)" teaches: a citizen keeps learning — the newspaper first among the sources: it must spread good ideas at home and abroad, refuse anything that harms the country's interests, and write what benefits the public, uniting people's thought in unity, equality, mutual respect and cooperation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: by Grade 6 the subject is open political education — the enemies of unity are named in a row (colonialism, racism, clanism, Zionism), the world's organisation is studied, the state's doctrine is taught as science, and citizenship becomes fiscal: the book ends with taxes and with the newspapers that keep a citizen informed..
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
