# Video script — Helping Each Other Small Helps Big Happiness

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 3 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed p. 86, teaches: helping people in need makes daily life easier and grows love, respect, compassion and patience; a class whose friend cannot enter a rest stop for want of a ramp builds an awareness project for a barrier-free life, and a help project is built step by step — observe the need, form the team, set the aim, divide the jobs, evaluate and report ("Small helps, big happinesses")

Turkey's own way: Grade 3 is where Hayat Bilgisi turns explicitly civic: an eight-year-old is taught that rights and responsibilities are a pair, and then reads the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child itself — in a child-worded card set (definition of the child, no discrimination, best interests, life and development) — two years before any civics course exists.
