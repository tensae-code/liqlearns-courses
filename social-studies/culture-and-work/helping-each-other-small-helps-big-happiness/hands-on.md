# Hands-on — Helping Each Other Small Helps Big Happiness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: helping people in need makes daily life easier and grows love, respect, compassion and patience; a class whose friend cannot enter a rest stop for want of a ramp builds an awareness project for a barrier-free life, and a help project is built step by step — observe the need, form the team, set the aim, divide the jobs, evaluate and report ("Small helps, big happinesses") Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
