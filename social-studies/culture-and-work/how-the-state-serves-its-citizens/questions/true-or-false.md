# How the State Serves Its Citizens — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the government the citizens elected must serve the citizens: it pays the salaries of the people who work in schools and kindergartens, builds separate schools for children with disabilities, and runs training for those who left school — and it does all this with the budget (төсөв), money gathered as tax (татвар) from the citizens and businesses; the school's own people — director, training manager, school doctor, teachers, pupils — each hold rights and duties in that service.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
