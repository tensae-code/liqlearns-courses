# How the State Serves Its Citizens — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 15 "How does the state serve its citizens?" (Төр иргэддээ хэрхэн үйлчилдэг вэ?) — The government the citizens elected must serve the citizens: it pays the salaries of the people who work in schools and kindergartens, builds separate schools for children with disabilities, and runs training for those who left school — and it does all this with the budget (төсөв), money gathered as tax (татвар) from the citizens and businesses; the school's own people — director, training manager, school doctor, teachers, pupils — each hold rights and duties in that service (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone (any close wording).**
