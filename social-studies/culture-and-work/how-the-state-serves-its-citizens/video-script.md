The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 59, teaches: The government the citizens elected must serve the citizens: it pays the salaries of the people who work in schools and kindergartens, builds separate schools for children with disabilities, and runs training for those who left school — and it does all this with the budget (төсөв), money gathered as tax (татвар) from the citizens and businesses; the school's own people — director, training manager, school doctor, teachers, pupils — each hold rights and duties in that service.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
