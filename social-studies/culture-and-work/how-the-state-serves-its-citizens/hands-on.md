# Hands-on — How the State Serves Its Citizens

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 15 "How does the state serve its citizens?" (Төр иргэддээ хэрхэн үйлчилдэг вэ?)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The government the citizens elected must serve the citizens: it pays the salaries of the people who work in schools and kindergartens, builds separate schools for children with disabilities, and runs training for those who left school — and it does all this with the budget (төсөв), money gathered as tax (татвар) from the citizens and businesses; the school's own people — director, training manager, school doctor, teachers, pupils — each hold rights and duties in that service. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
