**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Ethiopia, Mongolia).**

Mongolia's own way (Grade 9): Social Studies is a five-chapter spiral (Individual & Society, Individual & Culture, Individual & Economy, Human Rights & Law, Public Administration) continued from Grade 8; there is no physical-geography chapter — Mongolia's geography is a separate subject (Газарзүй) — so this book's Geography-wave content is its culture and economics teaching, grounded in Mongolian surveys, wage tables and heritage inventories. The book's Sociology parts (Chapter 1, printed pp. 6–13) and Law/Civics parts (Chapters 4–5, printed pp. 62–92) are named in the placements, not placed. Mongolia's own way (Grade 7): the Grade 7 year works at the scale of the child's own life — my groups, my culture, my family's economy and budget, our school — before the local-area year in Grade 8. The book's Sociology parts (Chapter I, printed pp. 7–13) and Law/Civics parts (Chapters IV–V, printed pp. 59–93) are named in the placements, not placed. Mongolia's own way (Grade 10): the senior cycle drops the “Individual and…” frame for straight disciplines — Society, Culture, Law, Economics, Politics, International Relations — with Mongolia's own culture, macroeconomy and foreign relations as the worked examples. The book's Sociology parts (Chapter 1, printed pp. 5–15) and Law/Civics parts (Chapter 3, printed pp. 49–72; Chapter 5, printed pp. 97–117; Chapter 6 except the placed topics, printed pp. 119–135) are named in the placements, not placed. Mongolia's own way (Grade 11): the final year is the state-and-world year — cultural globalization and freedom, monetary policy, foreign trade and investment, and Mongolia's signature “third neighbour” and soft-power policies taught as named topics. The book's Sociology part (Chapter I Topic 1.2, printed p. 29) and Law/Civics parts (Chapter III, printed pp. 71–103; Chapter V, printed pp. 139–165; Chapter VI except the placed topics, printed pp. 167–176) are named in the placements, not placed. That way is stocked here as the way Mongolia teaches this thing.
