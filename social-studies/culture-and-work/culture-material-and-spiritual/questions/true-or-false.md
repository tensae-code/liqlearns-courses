# Culture — material and spiritual culture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Grief and joy are material culture.
**Answer: False**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spiritual culture is held in the mind, not the hand.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Clothing kinds belong to material culture.
**Answer: True**
