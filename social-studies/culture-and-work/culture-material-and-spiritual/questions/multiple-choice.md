# Culture — material and spiritual culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Material culture is —
a) Held in the mind  b) Tangible — seen by the eye and touched by the hand  c) Only music  d) Only beliefs
**Answer: b) Tangible — seen by the eye and touched by the hand**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is material culture?
a) Mourning  b) A farm tool  c) Religion  d) Music
**Answer: b) A farm tool**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Spiritual culture shows through —
a) People's acts and expressions  b) Factory output  c) Tax records  d) Maps
**Answer: a) People's acts and expressions**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Music, mourning and religion are —
a) Material culture  b) Spiritual culture  c) Not culture  d) Trade goods
**Answer: b) Spiritual culture**
