# Culture — material and spiritual culture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three material-culture examples from the book.
**Answer:** Any three of: farm tools, house-building goods and methods, food goods, clothing kinds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three spiritual-culture examples from the book.
**Answer:** Music, mourning and religion.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What is the test that separates the two halves?
**Answer:** Material culture can be seen and touched; spiritual culture is untouchable and shows through acts and expressions.
