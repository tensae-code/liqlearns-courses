# Hands-on — Culture — material and spiritual culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
List your community's material culture: tools, building goods, foods, clothing. Aim for ten items.

## Activity 2
With partners, stage one local ceremony as a short drama — a wedding, mourning, reconciliation or festival dress — and name its spiritual parts aloud.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Sort any cultural thing into material or spiritual, and say why. Name the book's material examples and its spiritual examples.
