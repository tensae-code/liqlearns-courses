# Trade — what trade is

## Do it first

List five things your household uses that were not made where you live. For each, write where its kind of work is done.

## Sub lesson 1 — No one finds everything where they are

People live in **different places by different work**, so **no one finds everything they need where they are**. Unlimited wants create **trade — the buying, selling and exchanging of goods**.

[Picture 11525 — two villages exchanging what each lacks]

## Sub lesson 2 — Three levels of trade

Trade is carried on at **local, national or world level**. The village market, the country's markets and the trade between countries are one activity at three reaches — each exists because somewhere, someone has what someone else lacks.
