# Trade — what trade is — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the book's two reasons trade exists?
**Answer:** People live in different places by different work, so no one finds everything where they are; and wants are unlimited.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name trade's three levels.
**Answer:** Local, national and world.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Define trade.
**Answer:** The buying, selling and exchanging of goods.
