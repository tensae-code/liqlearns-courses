# Trade — what trade is — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade exists because —
a) Governments order it  b) People live in different places by different work, and wants are unlimited  c) Money was invented  d) Roads exist
**Answer: b) People live in different places by different work, and wants are unlimited**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Trade is —
a) Only selling  b) The buying, selling and exchanging of goods  c) Only exchanging without money  d) A kind of farming
**Answer: b) The buying, selling and exchanging of goods**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade is carried on at —
a) Village level only  b) Local, national and world level  c) Regional level only  d) Household level only
**Answer: b) Local, national and world level**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** No one finds everything they need —
a) At the market  b) Where they are  c) In the capital  d) Abroad
**Answer: b) Where they are**
