# Hands-on — Trade — what trade is

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
List five things your household uses that were not made where you live. For each, write where its kind of work is done.

## Activity 2
Visit a market in your mind: write three exchanges happening there, and what each side lacked before.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Say why trade exists, in the book's two reasons. Say what trade is. Name trade's three levels.
