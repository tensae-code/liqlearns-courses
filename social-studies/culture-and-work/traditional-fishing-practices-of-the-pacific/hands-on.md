# Hands-on — Traditional Fishing Practices of the Pacific

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Traditional uses of the reef and ocean (no-fishing areas and controlled harvest; Tongan shark noosing and fish herding, Tuvalu reef gathering and bird catching, Niue fagota reef gathering and the fono ban). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
