# Video script — Cyberbullying and How to Answer It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bhutan Class VI Social Studies VI, printed p. 34, teaches: bullying over phones, computers and tablets (texts, SMS, voice messages, stickers, clips, pictures), why people do it (ego, entertainment, insult, revenge), what it does (sadness, loneliness, changed sleep and eating, school dropout, substance abuse) and how to answer it (block, log off, save the messages, tell the right people).

Bhutan's own way: the primary course closes by putting the whole state in front of the child — what a government is, Bhutan's own change from monarchy to Democratic Constitutional Monarchy in 2008, its three branches told through the courts' own ladder and the kabney/patang ranks the King bestows, the Article 7 fundamental rights quoted from Bhutan's Constitution, and a social-problems chapter that names Bhutan's own helping agencies (Royal Bhutan Police, RENEW, NCWC) for every problem.
