# Hands-on — Cyberbullying and How to Answer It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bullying over phones, computers and tablets (texts, SMS, voice messages, stickers, clips, pictures), why people do it (ego, entertainment, insult, revenge), what it does (sadness, loneliness, changed sleep and eating, school dropout, substance abuse) and how to answer it (block, log off, save the messages, tell the right people). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
