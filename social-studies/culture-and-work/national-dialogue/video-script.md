# Video script — National Dialogue

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 249–252), teaches: The state's work to spread a culture of dialogue, respect of difference and diversity, and protection of national unity and the social fabric; the kinds of dialogue — national (among society's members under an approved body), educational and spontaneous; the King Abdulaziz Centre for Civilizational Communication, founded 1424H to spread dialogue's culture and strengthen national unity and cohesion, its annual national dialogue meeting across the regions (national unity, extremism and moderation, women's rights and duties, "Us and the Other"), and its programmes (Bayadir for youth volunteering, Talahom for national unity against extremism, Jusur).

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue.
