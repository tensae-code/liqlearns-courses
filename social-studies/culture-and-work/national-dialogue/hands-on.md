# Hands-on — National Dialogue

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the state's work to spread a culture of dialogue, respect of difference and diversity, and protection of national unity and the social fabric; the kinds of dialogue — national (among society's members under an approved body), educational and spontaneous; the King Abdulaziz Centre for Civilizational Communication, founded 1424H to spread dialogue's culture and strengthen national unity and cohesion, its annual national dialogue meeting across the regions (national unity, extremism and moderation, women's rights and duties, "Us and the Other"), and its programmes (Bayadir for youth volunteering, Talahom for national unity against extremism, Jusur). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
