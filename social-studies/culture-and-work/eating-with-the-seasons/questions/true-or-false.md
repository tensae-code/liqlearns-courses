# Eating With the Seasons — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: food is fitted finely to the four seasons — in the three summer months milk and white foods are the main food, while meat rules the rest of the year; winter and spring call for warming, fatty food (the lean spring meat is called "long food"), dried meat (борц) and stored dairy carry the household through, and eating against the season is itself a cause of illness in the old medical tradition.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
