# Hands-on — Eating With the Seasons

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 7 "The custom of using food fitted to the season" (Хоол, хүнсийг улиралд тохируулан хэрэглэх ёс)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Food is fitted finely to the four seasons — in the three summer months milk and white foods are the main food, while meat rules the rest of the year; winter and spring call for warming, fatty food (the lean spring meat is called "long food"), dried meat (борц) and stored dairy carry the household through, and eating against the season is itself a cause of illness in the old medical tradition. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
