Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 22, teaches: Food is fitted finely to the four seasons — in the three summer months milk and white foods are the main food, while meat rules the rest of the year; winter and spring call for warming, fatty food (the lean spring meat is called "long food"), dried meat (борц) and stored dairy carry the household through, and eating against the season is itself a cause of illness in the old medical tradition.

Mongolia's own way (Book 4): the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other.
