## Sub lesson 2 — Papua New Guinea's way — the books' own teaching way

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 10): Grade 10 deepens Grade 9's civics into the machinery of the state — the Constitution as the legal basis of government (Section 99's three arms), separation and balance of powers, what parliamentarians actually do, PNG's three levels of government and decentralisation — and ends with a full human-rights unit: PNG's rights laws, the Constitution's rights, enforcing the rights of women, people with disability and marginalised groups, and children's rights under the Lukautim Pikinini Act. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
