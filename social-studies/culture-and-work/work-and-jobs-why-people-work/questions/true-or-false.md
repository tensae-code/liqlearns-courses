# Work and Jobs Why People Work — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The value of work in one fill-in creed — work is a right and a duty, the means of living that earns the money for the basic needs (food, housing, clothing, health care).

**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: Grade 3 is where the series turns civic — the child meets the vote inside a classroom quarrel (proposals, persuasion, a ballot box, the winner implemented), meets rights on World Children's Rights Day (20 November) as picture pairs where every right is matched by its duty, and meets the inclusive city: the society-we-want lesson is built around services for people with special needs, down to the sign-language alphabet and Braille..
