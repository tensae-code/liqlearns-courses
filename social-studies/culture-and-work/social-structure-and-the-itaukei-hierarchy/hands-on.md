## Social structure and the iTaukei hierarchy
Work through this part with your hands: Every society needs order and leadership roles; in traditional iTaukei society the chief's status is ascribed by inheritance and the hierarchy (Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau, Tauvanua) fixes each person's obligations to the vanua.
