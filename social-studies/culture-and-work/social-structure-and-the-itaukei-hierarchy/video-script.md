# Video script — Social Structure and the Itaukei Hierarchy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 9 Social Science Year 9 (printed p. 17), teaches: Every society needs order and leadership roles; in traditional iTaukei society the chief's status is ascribed by inheritance and the hierarchy (Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau, Tauvanua) fixes each person's obligations to the vanua.

Fiji's own way (Grade 9): society is analysed through Fiji's own structures — the iTaukei hierarchy of Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau and Tauvanua with its ascribed chiefly status — and conflict is settled the iTaukei way, by Bulubulu.
