Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 2, Term 1) (teacher guide — evidence): Grade 2 turns Grade 1's circles into verbs — accept, communicate, cooperate, consult, belong — and plants two purely Omani institutions in the child's civic world: the sablah, the village meeting hall where the community gathers, and the folk games of the neighbourhood.
