# Video script — Pulaka — Tuvalu's Giant Swamp Taro

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tuvalu's pulaka is a giant swamp taro grown in pits filled with compost — a farming method fitted to a low coral island with little fresh soil. The plant has many uses for the islanders who grow it. And it is threatened: drought, heavy rain and salt water reach the pits as the climate changes.

Nauru learns pulaka inside a case-study reader: a single Pacific crop is studied whole — how it is grown, what it is used for, and what threatens it — alongside taro in Tonga and Niue and the coconut, the tree of life.
