# Hands-on — Roots of the American Political Tradition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Roots of the American Political Tradition
Work through this part with your hands: limited government and natural rights (life, liberty, property) as the colonists' inherited English tradition, and the social contract breaking under taxation without consent and trials without juries. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
