# Video script — Human Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies (printed p. 138), teaches: Values are a set of moral standards, principles, teachings and controls that direct the individual's behaviour and draw for him the sound path that keeps him from falling into error; and these values achieve a set of aims, among them strengthening the social bonds, entrenching the principle of social justice, achieving stability, reinforcing equality, and spreading peace among society's sons, regardless of sex, religion, nationality, language or race; values are classified by the field they belong to — national, social, religious, political and others; and the national values are the set of principles and controls that determine the citizen's behaviour in the society he belongs to, represented in good citizenship, love of the homeland, sincerity, belonging and loyalty to it, sacrifice for its sake, and commitment to the rules and laws.

Iraq's own way: values are defined first as a system — standards that steer behaviour and spread peace "regardless of sex, religion, nationality, language or race" — and citizenship is cashed out in named rights (education, work, candidacy, the vote, freedom of opinion and belief), while the internet chapter warns in plain terms that the open network "is governed by no restrictions or conditions".
