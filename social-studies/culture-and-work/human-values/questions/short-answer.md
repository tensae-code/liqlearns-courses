**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Human Values — values are a set of moral standards, principles, teachings and controls that direct the individual's behaviour and draw for him the sound path that keeps him from falling into error; and these values achieve a set of aims, among them strengthening the social bonds, entrenching the principle of social justice, achieving stability, reinforcing equality, and spreading peace among society's sons, regardless of sex, religion, nationality, language or race; values are classified by the field they belong to — national, social, religious, political and others; and the national values are the set of principles and controls that determine the citizen's behaviour in the society he belongs to, represented in good citizenship, love of the homeland, sincerity, belonging and loyalty to it, sacrifice for its sake, and commitment to the rules and laws (any close wording).**
