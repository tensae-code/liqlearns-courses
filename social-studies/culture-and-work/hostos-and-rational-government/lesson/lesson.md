## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 12, Hostos: el imperativo de un Gobierno racional

Dominican Republic — Ciencias Sociales 6° Secundaria (printed p. 218), teaches: Hostos — neither weak nor strong executives should be asked for; judges, legislators, presidents and electors must have the attributions that correspond to them, no more and no less — the call to leave irrational systems of government for a rational one. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way: the final volume teaches democracy by close-reading the country's own canon — Article 7 and Article 2 of the 2015 Constitution, a classic text on love of country, and Hostos's call for a rational government — and names the system's diseases honestly: party clientelism and support bought with favours "neither legal nor ethical".
