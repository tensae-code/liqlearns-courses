# Video script — Ley 348 a Life Free of Violence for Women

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia Compendio Ciencias Sociales (compendium p. 268), teaches: Bolivia's 2013 law against violence against women — violence defined (Art. 6) as any act or omission causing physical, psychological, patrimonial or economic harm "por el sólo hecho de ser mujer"; the law's four fields of action (prevention, attention, protection, reparation), its purpose in Art. 2, and Vivir Bien as its sustaining principle (Art. 4), taught against CEPAL's finding that Bolivia has among South America's highest feminicide rates.

Bolivia's own way: the teachers' compendium carries two civics units no held student book carries — the Year 3 pair of women's-protection laws, taught from real cases (a councilwoman driven from office; the murdered concejala Juana Quispe), and the Year 5 citizenship unit, which builds Bolivian citizenship straight from CPE Articles 141–144 and ends with the stateless person who can neither study, work nor marry.
