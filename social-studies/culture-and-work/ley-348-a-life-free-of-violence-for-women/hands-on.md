# Hands-on — Ley 348 a Life Free of Violence for Women

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bolivia's 2013 law against violence against women — violence defined (Art. 6) as any act or omission causing physical, psychological, patrimonial or economic harm "por el sólo hecho de ser mujer"; the law's four fields of action (prevention, attention, protection, reparation), its purpose in Art. 2, and Vivir Bien as its sustaining principle (Art. 4), taught against CEPAL's finding that Bolivia has among South America's highest feminicide rates. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
