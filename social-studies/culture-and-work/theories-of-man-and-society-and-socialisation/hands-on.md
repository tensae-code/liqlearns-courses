# Hands-on — Theories of Man and Society and Socialisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Plato (the common good above the private), Aristotle (man as a political being, justice in living together), Hobbes (natural vs civil state, the social contract road to the state), and socialisation as the process of joining social life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
