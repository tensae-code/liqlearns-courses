Armenia's book, the *Armenia Grade 7 Social Science student* (printed p. 125), teaches: 7.3 Personal budget, distribution of income, the role of savings now and in the future. Armenia's book, the *Armenia Grade 10 Social Science student* (printed p. 97), teaches: 4.2 Management of savings.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 7 Social Science student): Social Science opens not with the state but with the person — man-and-nature first, then society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and every chapter is built from short source materials with three levels of tasks (tell, apply, judge).

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 10 Social Science student): the senior Social Science course turns practical — after ideologies it teaches the market economy and then personal finance (choosing financial services, savings, credit, consumer rights, fraud) as life skills.
