## Sub lesson 2 — Armenia's way — the book's own teaching way

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 7 Social Science student): Social Science opens not with the state but with the person — man-and-nature first, then society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and every chapter is built from short source materials with three levels of tasks (tell, apply, judge).

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 10 Social Science student): the senior Social Science course turns practical — after ideologies it teaches the market economy and then personal finance (choosing financial services, savings, credit, consumer rights, fraud) as life skills. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
