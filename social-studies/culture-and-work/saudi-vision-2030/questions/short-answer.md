# Saudi Vision 2030 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Saudi Vision 2030 — a vision as a future plan of ambitions and goals; Vision 2030 launched by King Salman in 1437H/2016, prepared under Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, to develop the Kingdom; its three pillars of strength — the Islamic and Arab depth (the Two Holy Mosques and the Kaaba, qibla of over a billion Muslims), the Kingdom's huge investment power, and its strategic location linking the three continents (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vision 2030 Realisation Programmes — the thirteen realisation programmes and their initiatives (Quality of Life, Housing, National Transformation, Financial Sector Development, National Industrial Development and others) and the Vision's flagship projects, above all NEOM in the north-west — "the new future" — diversifying the economy, renewable energy and attracting world talent and investment; the Vision's three themes: an ambitious nation, a thriving economy, a vibrant society (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way — Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 6 book ties citizenship to Saudi Vision 2030 — what a vision is, its realisation programmes and projects, the Quality of Life Programme and the Human Capability Development Programme — and then to citizenship itself, honesty and trustworthiness, guarding national antiquities, and taxes and VAT. (any close wording).**
