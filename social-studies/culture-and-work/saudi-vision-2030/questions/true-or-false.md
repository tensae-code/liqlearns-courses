# Saudi Vision 2030 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: A vision as a future plan of ambitions and goals; Vision 2030 launched by King Salman in 1437H/2016, prepared under Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, to develop the Kingdom; its three pillars of strength — the Islamic and Arab depth (the Two Holy Mosques and the Kaaba, qibla of over a billion Muslims), the Kingdom's huge investment power, and its strategic location linking the three continents.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: The thirteen realisation programmes and their initiatives (Quality of Life, Housing, National Transformation, Financial Sector Development, National Industrial Development and others) and the Vision's flagship projects, above all NEOM in the north-west — "the new future" — diversifying the economy, renewable energy and attracting world talent and investment; the Vision's three themes: an ambitious nation, a thriving economy, a vibrant society.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Saudi Arabia's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
