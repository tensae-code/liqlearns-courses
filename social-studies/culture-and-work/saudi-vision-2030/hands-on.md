# Hands-on — Saudi Vision 2030

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a vision as a future plan of ambitions and goals; Vision 2030 launched by King Salman in 1437H/2016, prepared under Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, to develop the Kingdom; its three pillars of strength — the Islamic and Arab depth (the Two Holy Mosques and the Kaaba, qibla of over a billion Muslims), the Kingdom's huge investment power, and its strategic location linking the three continents. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
