# Rights and Duties Go Together — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: every right carries its duty, and the duties come in two kinds — the values a citizen keeps (care of parents, the society's customs and traditions, teachers' counsel) and the legal duties the state lays down (loyalty to the state, obeying the laws, helping to uphold the law, paying taxes).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Sri Lanka's student books only, named in lesson/sources.md.**
