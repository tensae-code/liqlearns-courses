# Human Rights and Social Movements — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Human rights are universal and inseparable from the person, and were won through struggles — the 1789 Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen, and Olympe de Gouges' 1791 answer demanding the same rights for women.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: Today's social and political movements — gender equality, climate justice, migrants' rights — and the ethical stance from which adolescents can promote and defend human rights.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Mexico's student books, named in lesson/sources.md.**
