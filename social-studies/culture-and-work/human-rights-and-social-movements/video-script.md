# Video script — Human Rights and Social Movements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mexico Grade 7 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 1 (secundaria) (printed pp. 323–325), teaches: Human rights are universal and inseparable from the person, and were won through struggles — the 1789 Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen, and Olympe de Gouges' 1791 answer demanding the same rights for women.

---

Mexico Grade 9 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 3 (secundaria) (printed pp. 239–247), teaches: Today's social and political movements — gender equality, climate justice, migrants' rights — and the ethical stance from which adolescents can promote and defend human rights.

---

Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution. Mexico's own way (Grade 9): the third year turns the course outward to action — participation spaces for children and adolescents, community projects for democratic culture, and democracy's own challenges (corruption, violence, disenchantment) named as things the young citizen is expected to work on.
