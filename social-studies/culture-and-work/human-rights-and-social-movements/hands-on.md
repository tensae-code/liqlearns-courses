# Hands-on — Human Rights and Social Movements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Social Movements and Human Rights in Mexico (Grade 7)
Work through this part with your hands: human rights are universal and inseparable from the person, and were won through struggles — the 1789 Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen, and Olympe de Gouges' 1791 answer demanding the same rights for women. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## An Ethical Stance and Today's Movements (Grade 9)
Work through this part with your hands: today's social and political movements — gender equality, climate justice, migrants' rights — and the ethical stance from which adolescents can promote and defend human rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mexico's way
Say how Mexico's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
