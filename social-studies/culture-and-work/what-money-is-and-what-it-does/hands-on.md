# Hands-on — What Money Is and What It Does

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow one coin
Follow one coin or note through a day — where it comes from and where it goes — and name the job money does at each step, from the lesson.

## Money can, money cannot
Make two lists: things money can do in a family's life, and things it cannot do, and explain each list from the lesson.
