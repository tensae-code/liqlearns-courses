Honduras Grade 7 G7 Student CCSS, printed p. 32, teaches: Courtship (noviazgo) is the stage where a couple learns what they share before a life together; choosing a partner weighs physical, moral and mental health and attitude to work; in Honduras legal marriage is celebrated before a notary or a mayor with set requirements, the unión libre grows ever more common, and civil marriage ends by death, presumed death legally declared, or the listed grounds of conjugal breakdown.

Honduras's own way (Book 7): secondary civics starts from the family as a legal institution — the Family Code (Decree 76-84) enters in Lesson 1 — and builds to the State itself: its elements (population, territory, power), Montesquieu's separation, and the full legislative process ending in publication in La Gaceta.
