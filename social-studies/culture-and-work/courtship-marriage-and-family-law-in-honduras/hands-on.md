# Hands-on — Courtship Marriage and Family Law in Honduras

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 5 Noviazgo y matrimonio
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Courtship (noviazgo) is the stage where a couple learns what they share before a life together; choosing a partner weighs physical, moral and mental health and attitude to work; in Honduras legal marriage is celebrated before a notary or a mayor with set requirements, the unión libre grows ever more common, and civil marriage ends by death, presumed death legally declared, or the listed grounds of conjugal breakdown. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
