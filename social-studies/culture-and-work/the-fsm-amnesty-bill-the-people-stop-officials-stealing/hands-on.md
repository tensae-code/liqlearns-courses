# Hands-on — The Fsm Amnesty Bill the People Stop Officials Stealing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: when fourteen people were charged with stealing $1.2 million of public money, a bill to pardon officials who steal was introduced in Congress — and the people forced Congress to drop it; stealing is wrong no matter who does it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Micronesia's way
Say how Micronesia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
