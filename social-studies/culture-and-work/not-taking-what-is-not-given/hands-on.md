# Hands-on — Not Taking What is Not Given

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: ask and borrow, never just take — a friend's toys, a library book, a drink in a shop. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
