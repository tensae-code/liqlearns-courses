# Hands-on — Government Protects Citizens Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every citizen's rights as written in Article III (Bill of Rights) of the Philippine Constitution, and the government's duty and agencies that uphold them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Philippines's way
Say how Philippines's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
