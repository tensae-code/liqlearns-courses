# Video script — Government Protects Citizens Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Philippines Grade 4 AP4 Unit III (printed p. 304), teaches: Every citizen's rights as written in Article III (Bill of Rights) of the Philippine Constitution, and the government's duty and agencies that uphold them.

Philippines's own way: the whole third unit of Grade 4 is one civics arc — the national government and its three branches first, then levels down to the local government, then what government does for citizens (health, education, peace, infrastructure, rights), closing on local government working with community servants; the Bill of Rights is taught straight from Article III of the Philippine Constitution.
