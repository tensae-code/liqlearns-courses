# Government Protects Citizens Rights — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Government Protects Citizens Rights — every citizen's rights as written in Article III (Bill of Rights) of the Philippine Constitution, and the government's duty and agencies that uphold them (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Philippines's way — the book's own teaching way — Philippines's own way: the whole third unit of Grade 4 is one civics arc — the national government and its three branches first, then levels down to the local government, then what government does for citizens (health, education, peace, infrastructure, rights), closing on local government working with community servants; the Bill of Rights is taught straight from Article III of the Philippine Constitution. (any close wording).**
