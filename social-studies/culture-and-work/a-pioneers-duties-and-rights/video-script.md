# Video script — A Pioneers Duties and Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 22–32), teaches: A pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, human solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys.

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors).
