# Hands-on — A Pioneers Duties and Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, human solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
