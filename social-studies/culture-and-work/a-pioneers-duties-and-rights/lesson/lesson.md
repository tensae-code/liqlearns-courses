# A Pioneers Duties and Rights

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, human solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys — as Cuba's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — A Pioneers Duties and Rights

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 22–32), teaches: A pioneer's duties to the homeland — study as the first duty, preparing to defend the patria, respecting the patriotic symbols, caring for social and personal property, human solidarity and the defence of peace — together with the school regulations every pioneer obeys. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
