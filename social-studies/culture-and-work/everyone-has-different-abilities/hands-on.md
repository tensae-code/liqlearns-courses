# Hands-on — Everyone Has Different Abilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Abilities of many kinds
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Children (and the spider in the story) have different strengths — tug-of-war, crafts, drawing — and each ability counts. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
