# Video script — Political Ideologies Liberal Conservative Socialist

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Belarus Grade 10 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 83), teaches: The term from Destutt de Tracy; a political ideology as a set of ideas, ideals and values expressing a group's interests and its claim to realise them through state power; ideology's functions and its three levels of working — theoretical, programmatic and the level of mass consciousness, where ideas become people's convictions.
Belarus' own way: the Grade 10 course teaches society and politics in general concepts, but anchors them at home — the Statute-era separation of powers on Belarusian lands, Article 36's freedom of association and Article 49's right to education quoted from its own Constitution, and Belarusian parties and public associations as the living examples of the political system..
