The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Oman Grade 2 Identity & Citizenship TG T2, printed pp. 31–42, teaches: The homeland known through what it hands down — its heritage shown and practised, and the folk tales the community tells its children.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 2, Term 2): the world unit is taught as three verbs of the heart — communicate, interact, empathise — while the homeland unit grounds belonging in heritage, folk tales and the Omani personalities who shaped history.
