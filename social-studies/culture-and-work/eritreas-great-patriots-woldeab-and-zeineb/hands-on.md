# Hands-on — Eritreas Great Patriots Woldeab and Zeineb

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: patriotism through two Eritrean lives — Aboy Woldeab Woldemariam, who survived seven attempts on his life and put being Eritrean before region and religion, and Yima Zeineb, mother of the Revolution who fought for women's equality. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
