# Hands-on — Bu Tbeila the Ramadan Drummer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Ramadan drummer of old Kuwait, who woke people for suhoor for God's sake alone — the values his round shows: giving without return and serving the neighbourhood. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
