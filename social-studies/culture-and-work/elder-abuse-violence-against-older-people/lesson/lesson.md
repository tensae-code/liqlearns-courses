## Sub lesson 1 — Experience 2 content theme "El adulto mayor víctima de la violencia intrafamiliar"

Guatemala Grade 8 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana II TG, printed pp. 44–48, teaches: maltreatment of an older person (intimidating, isolating, controlling, neglecting) as intrafamily violence; the WHO's types and how to recognise each, including economic abuse and the "slave grandmother" syndrome. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way

Guatemala's own way: each kind of violence is taught with Guatemala's own law and institutions — the Femicide Law, the Defensoría de la Mujer Indígena (DEMI) — and through the victims the country's classrooms actually contain: indigenous women, grandparents, pupils with disabilities.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
