# Hands-on — Elder Abuse Violence Against Older People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 2 content theme "El adulto mayor víctima de la violencia intrafamiliar"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 8 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana II TG, printed pp. 44–48, teaches: maltreatment of an older person (intimidating, isolating, controlling, neglecting) as intrafamily violence; the WHO's types and how to recognise each, including economic abuse and the "slave grandmother" syndrome. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: each kind of violence is taught with Guatemala's own law and institutions — the Femicide Law, the Defensoría de la Mujer Indígena (DEMI) — and through the victims the country's classrooms actually contain: indigenous women, grandparents, pupils with disabilities.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
