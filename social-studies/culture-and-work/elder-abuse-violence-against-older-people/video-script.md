# Video script — Elder Abuse Violence Against Older People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 8 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana II TG, printed pp. 44–48, teaches: maltreatment of an older person (intimidating, isolating, controlling, neglecting) as intrafamily violence; the WHO's types and how to recognise each, including economic abuse and the "slave grandmother" syndrome.

Guatemala's own way: each kind of violence is taught with Guatemala's own law and institutions — the Femicide Law, the Defensoría de la Mujer Indígena (DEMI) — and through the victims the country's classrooms actually contain: indigenous women, grandparents, pupils with disabilities.
