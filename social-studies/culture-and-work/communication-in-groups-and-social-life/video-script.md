# Video script — Communication in Groups and Social Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Turkey Grade 7 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 12, teaches: effective communication — listening with attention, daring to ask, apologising for a disturbance, respecting tastes and views unlike ours — worked through case stories (the boy who asked his teacher, the loud video on the school bus, the mocked film suggestion) and the language of communication in traffic

Turkey's own way: the last middle-school year opens by widening the circle one final time — from how we talk to each other in a group, to the equality a constitution owes a child in a wheelchair, to the whole society closing ranks on a national issue, and finally to the country's own seat at the world's problem-solving table.
