# Hands-on — Indigenous Nations and Autonomy in Bolivia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the 36 Indigenous Originary Campesino Nations inside the Bolivian state — the 2009 Constitution's recognition of their cosmovision, customs, laws and political organisation, indigenous autonomy, and Ley Nº 031 (Marco de Autonomías, 2010) under the banner "Todos somos iguales" — justice, equality and participation without discrimination. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
