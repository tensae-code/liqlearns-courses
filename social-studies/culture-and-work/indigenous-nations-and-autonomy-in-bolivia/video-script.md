The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bolivia Grade 8 BO Social Sciences Sec 2 (printed p. 16), teaches: The 36 Indigenous Originary Campesino Nations inside the Bolivian state — the 2009 Constitution's recognition of their cosmovision, customs, laws and political organisation, indigenous autonomy, and Ley Nº 031 (Marco de Autonomías, 2010) under the banner "Todos somos iguales" — justice, equality and participation without discrimination.

Bolivia's own way: the constitution itself is taught as the home of the ancestors' moral law — the Plurinational State's founding values are the Quechua Ama Qhilla, Ama Llulla, Ama Suwa and the other peoples' suma qamaña, ñandereco, teko kavi, ivi maraei and qhapaj ñan, written into CPE Article 8.
