# Hands-on — My Duties and Responsibilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: our duties and responsibilities — keeping our room, desk, toys and clothes clean and tidy, using water and electricity sparingly (tasarruf), waiting our turn in the canteen, market and play area, and caring together with neighbours for the apartment garden's cat Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
