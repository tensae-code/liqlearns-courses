The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Turkey Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed p. 94, teaches: our duties and responsibilities — keeping our room, desk, toys and clothes clean and tidy, using water and electricity sparingly (tasarruf), waiting our turn in the canteen, market and play area, and caring together with neighbours for the apartment garden's cat

Turkey's own way: Hayat Bilgisi (Life Studies) is Turkey's integrated primary subject, rebuilt under the Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli as six learning areas; this first book runs Me-and-My-School → My Health and Safety → My Family and Society, every topic in the same rhythm (remember, explore, knowledge journey, practise, what we learned), with the year's special days carried as poems at the head of each area and the Student Oath (Andımız) printed at the front.
