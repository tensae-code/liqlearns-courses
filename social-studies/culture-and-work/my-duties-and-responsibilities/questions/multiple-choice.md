# My Duties and Responsibilities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) My duty, my responsibility  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) My duty, my responsibility**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) our duties and responsibilities — keeping our room, desk, toys and clothes clean and tidy, using water and electricity sparingly (tasarruf), waiting our turn in the canteen, market and play area, and caring together with neighbours for the apartment garden's cat  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) our duties and responsibilities — keeping our room, desk, toys and clothes clean and tidy, using water and electricity sparingly (tasarruf), waiting our turn in the canteen, market and play area, and caring together with neighbours for the apartment garden's cat**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches my Duties and Responsibilities as Turkey's books teach it, in Turkey's own way.  b) Turkey's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) This lesson teaches my Duties and Responsibilities as Turkey's books teach it, in Turkey's own way.**
