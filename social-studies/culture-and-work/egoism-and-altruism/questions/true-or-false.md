# Egoism and Altruism — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part 1 teaches: egoism (seeking one's own benefit, which can still justify cooperation) against altruism (doing good for others simply wishing them well, even at cost to yourself), and feelings — empathy, guilt, others' anger — as morality's enforcement.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part 2 teaches: Social Science opens with the person, not the state — nature, society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and citizenship is introduced from the child's own documents: an Armenian citizen's membership is "fixed by the birth certificate and the passport", with rights from the state and duties owed back..
**Answer: True**
