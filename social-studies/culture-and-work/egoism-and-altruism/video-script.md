# Video script — Egoism and Altruism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Armenia Grade 7 Social Science student, printed p. 70, teaches: egoism (seeking one's own benefit, which can still justify cooperation) against altruism (doing good for others simply wishing them well, even at cost to yourself), and feelings — empathy, guilt, others' anger — as morality's enforcement.

Armenia's own way: Social Science opens with the person, not the state — nature, society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and citizenship is introduced from the child's own documents: an Armenian citizen's membership is "fixed by the birth certificate and the passport", with rights from the state and duties owed back.
