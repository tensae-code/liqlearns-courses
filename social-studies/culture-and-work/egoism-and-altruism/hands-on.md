# Hands-on — Egoism and Altruism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: egoism (seeking one's own benefit, which can still justify cooperation) against altruism (doing good for others simply wishing them well, even at cost to yourself), and feelings — empathy, guilt, others' anger — as morality's enforcement. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
