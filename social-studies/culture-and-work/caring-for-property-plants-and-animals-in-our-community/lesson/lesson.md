## Sub lesson 1 — Responsibilities: care for our community's living things

Guyana Grade 4 Social Studies For Our Children Book 4 (printed p. 75), teaches: Take care of property, plants and animals — trees give food, shade and beauty, crops need care (with the Agricultural Field Assistant's help), and useful animals and plants must be protected. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: Book 4 turns from "my community" to "our homeland" — Guyana is taught as six peoples who each brought a way of life, and citizenship is taught through honour (National Awards) and shared observance (Road Safety, Fire Prevention and Youth Weeks) before it is taught through rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
