# Hands-on — Caring for Property Plants and Animals in Our Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Take care of property, plants and animals — trees give food, shade and beauty, crops need care (with the Agricultural Field Assistant's help), and useful animals and plants must be protected. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
