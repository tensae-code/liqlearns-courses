Finland's Mullankaivajat Saimaalla teacher guide (printed p. 9), teaches: Tehtävä 1: Luonnonvaraiset – Tutustutaan jokamiehenoikeuksiin (Getting to know everyman's rights): the Nordic countries have uniquely wide rights to move freely in nature; in Finland everyone may hike, pick wild berries, mushrooms and flowers and enjoy nature, but not everything is allowed — the rights include responsibility for nature: know protected and endangered species, do not disturb, do not litter, carry out what you carried in, leave no trace of your visit.

Finland's own way: rights teaching grows out of the forest itself — before the Stone Age trips begin, pupils learn jokamiehenoikeudet, the Nordic everyman's rights that Finland shares with its neighbours: everyone may roam, pick berries, mushrooms and flowers, and the same right carries the duty to care for nature, disturb nothing, leave no litter and no trace.
