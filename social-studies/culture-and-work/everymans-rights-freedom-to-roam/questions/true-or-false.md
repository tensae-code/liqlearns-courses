# Everymans Rights Freedom to Roam — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Tehtävä 1: Luonnonvaraiset – Tutustutaan jokamiehenoikeuksiin (Getting to know everyman's rights): the Nordic countries have uniquely wide rights to move freely in nature; in Finland everyone may hike, pick wild berries, mushrooms and flowers and enjoy nature, but not everything is allowed — the rights include responsibility for nature.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Finland's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
