## Sub lesson 1 — Everymans Rights Freedom to Roam

Finland's Mullankaivajat Saimaalla teacher guide (printed p. 9), teaches: Tehtävä 1: Luonnonvaraiset – Tutustutaan jokamiehenoikeuksiin (Getting to know everyman's rights): the Nordic countries have uniquely wide rights to move freely in nature; in Finland everyone may hike, pick wild berries, mushrooms and flowers and enjoy nature, but not everything is allowed — the rights include responsibility for nature: know protected and endangered species, do not disturb, do not litter, carry out what you carried in, leave no trace of your visit. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Finland's way — the book's own teaching way

Finland's own way: rights teaching grows out of the forest itself — before the Stone Age trips begin, pupils learn jokamiehenoikeudet, the Nordic everyman's rights that Finland shares with its neighbours: everyone may roam, pick berries, mushrooms and flowers, and the same right carries the duty to care for nature, disturb nothing, leave no litter and no trace. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
