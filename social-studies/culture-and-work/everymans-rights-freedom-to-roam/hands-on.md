# Hands-on — Everymans Rights Freedom to Roam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Tehtävä 1: Luonnonvaraiset – Tutustutaan jokamiehenoikeuksiin (Getting to know everyman's rights): the Nordic countries have uniquely wide rights to move freely in nature; in Finland everyone may hike, pick wild berries, mushrooms and flowers and enjoy nature, but not everything is allowed — the rights include responsibility for nature: know protected and endangered species, do not disturb, do not litter, carry out what you carried in, leave no trace of your visit. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Finland's way
Say how Finland's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
