# Responsible Consumers Never Consumerists

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Responsible consumers, never consumerists — consuming what we need and can acquire is one thing, consumerism another: it harms the pocket and damages the environment, because more consumption means more rubbish and more waste of natural resources; answering advertising and fashion automatically feeds the overproduction–overconsumption cycle, the main cause of overexploitation and pollution — recycling is not enough; a responsible consumer behaves sensibly, denounces irregularities to the authorities, treats shop staff cordially and demands the same, and checks that measures and weights are true — as Venezuela's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: the first secondary civics book builds belonging as a ladder — family community, school community, municipal, regional and national community, with the Communal Council standing beside them — and participation itself is the lesson: the Constitution gives everyone a broad right to participate in forming, executing and controlling public management, as both a right and a common duty.
