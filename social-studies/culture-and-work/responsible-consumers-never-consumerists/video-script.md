# Video script — Responsible Consumers Never Consumerists

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela Grade 7 G7 CS Patria y Ciudadanía, printed p. 70 (PARTIAL — the secured file ends at printed p. 92, exactly as filed), teaches: Responsible consumers, never consumerists — consuming what we need and can acquire is one thing, consumerism another: it harms the pocket and damages the environment, because more consumption means more rubbish and more waste of natural resources; answering advertising and fashion automatically feeds the overproduction–overconsumption cycle, the main cause of overexploitation and pollution — recycling is not enough; a responsible consumer behaves sensibly, denounces irregularities to the authorities, treats shop staff cordially and demands the same, and checks that measures and weights are true.

Venezuela's own way: the first secondary civics book builds belonging as a ladder — family community, school community, municipal, regional and national community, with the Communal Council standing beside them — and participation itself is the lesson: the Constitution gives everyone a broad right to participate in forming, executing and controlling public management, as both a right and a common duty.
