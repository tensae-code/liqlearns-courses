# Hands-on — Responsible Consumers Never Consumerists

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Responsible consumers, never consumerists — consuming what we need and can acquire is one thing, consumerism another: it harms the pocket and damages the environment, because more consumption means more rubbish and more waste of natural resources; answering advertising and fashion automatically feeds the overproduction–overconsumption cycle, the main cause of overexploitation and pollution — recycling is not enough; a responsible consumer behaves sensibly, denounces irregularities to the authorities, treats shop staff cordially and demands the same, and checks that measures and weights are true. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
