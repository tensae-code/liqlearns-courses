# Responsible Consumers Never Consumerists — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Responsible consumers, never consumerists — consuming what we need and can acquire is one thing, consumerism another: it harms the pocket and damages the environment, because more consumption means more rubbish and more waste of natural resources; answering advertising and fashion automatically feeds the overproduction–overconsumption cycle, the main cause of overexploitation and pollution — recycling is not enough; a responsible consumer behaves sensibly, denounces irregularities to the authorities, treats shop staff cordially and demands the same, and checks that measures and weights are true.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Venezuela's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
