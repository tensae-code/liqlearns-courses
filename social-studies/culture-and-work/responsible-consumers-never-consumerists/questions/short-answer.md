# Responsible Consumers Never Consumerists — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Responsible Consumers Never Consumerists — Responsible consumers, never consumerists — consuming what we need and can acquire is one thing, consumerism another: it harms the pocket and damages the environment, because more consumption means more rubbish and more waste of natural resources; answering advertising and fashion automatically feeds the overproduction–overconsumption cycle, the main cause of overexploitation and pollution — recycling is not enough; a responsible consumer behaves sensibly, denounces irregularities to the authorities, treats shop staff cordially and demands the same, and checks that measures and weights are true (any close wording).**
