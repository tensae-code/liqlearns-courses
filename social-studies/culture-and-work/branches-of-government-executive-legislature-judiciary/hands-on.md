# Hands-on — Branches of Government Executive Legislature Judiciary

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 3 Governance and Division of Power, §4.5.3.2 — teaches: Kiribati’s division of power — executive (Beretitenti and Cabinet), legislative (Speaker and the elected Maneaba ni Maungatabu) and judiciary (Chief Justice, High Court, magistrates) checking and balancing each other to protect civil rights, the roles of each office, and how a bill passes and receives assent. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
