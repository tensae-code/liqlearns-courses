# Video script — Human Rights Evolution and Principles

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia Compendio Ciencias Sociales (compendium p. 304), teaches: What human rights are and how they were won — the Cyrus Cylinder, Magna Carta, the 1789 Declaration, and the UN's 1948 Universal Declaration — and the principles that govern them: universal, inalienable, innate, indivisible and imprescriptible.

Bolivia's own way: the teachers' compendium carries two civics units no held student book carries — the Year 3 pair of women's-protection laws, taught from real cases (a councilwoman driven from office; the murdered concejala Juana Quispe), and the Year 5 citizenship unit, which builds Bolivian citizenship straight from CPE Articles 141–144 and ends with the stateless person who can neither study, work nor marry.
