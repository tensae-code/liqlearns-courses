# Hands-on — Human Rights Evolution and Principles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what human rights are and how they were won — the Cyrus Cylinder, Magna Carta, the 1789 Declaration, and the UN's 1948 Universal Declaration — and the principles that govern them: universal, inalienable, innate, indivisible and imprescriptible. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
