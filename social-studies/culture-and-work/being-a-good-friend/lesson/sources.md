Cambodia's own way (Cambodia Grade 9 Social Studies G9): Grade 9 civics turns outward — after respect, Khmer etiquette and relations with friends and family, the community chapter teaches solving problems together in ASEAN, understanding democracy, corruption and leadership. **Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Turkey, Cambodia, Vietnam).**

Vietnam's own way (Book 2): Grade 2 widens the circle from family to homeland and public space: love of homeland is proven in small deeds (a donation box for neighbours in difficulty, flowers planted along the village road, a class visit to a Heroic Vietnamese Mother), and the civics core is rules in public places, taught place by place — school, hospital, bus stop, library, cinema, zoo, historic site.

Vietnam's own way (Book 4): Grade 4 turns outward to society's work and property: gratitude is owed to every worker including the night street-sweeper of Tố Hữu's poem; "public property" (của công) is named as a legal-moral category of its own — what belongs to everyone is guarded by everyone — and the book closes with the child's place in law itself: the basic rights and duties of children, including a children's forum where pupils speak.
