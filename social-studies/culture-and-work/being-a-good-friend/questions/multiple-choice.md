# Being a Good Friend — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Our friendship relations  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Our friendship relations**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) in friendship both sides are affected by each other's behaviour; telling positive from negative behaviour in case situations (the forgotten pencil case, the friend who wants to play ball), growing the positive, and checking one's own friendship behaviour on a checklist  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) in friendship both sides are affected by each other's behaviour; telling positive from negative behaviour in case situations (the forgotten pencil case, the friend who wants to play ball), growing the positive, and checking one's own friendship behaviour on a checklist**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) This lesson teaches being a Good Friend as Turkey's books teach it, in Turkey's own way.  b) Turkey's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
