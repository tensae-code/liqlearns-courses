# Hands-on — Being a Good Friend

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: in friendship both sides are affected by each other's behaviour; telling positive from negative behaviour in case situations (the forgotten pencil case, the friend who wants to play ball), growing the positive, and checking one's own friendship behaviour on a checklist Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Turkey's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
