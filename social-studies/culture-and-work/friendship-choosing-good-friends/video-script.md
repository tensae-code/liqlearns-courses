# Video script — Friendship Choosing Good Friends

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bahrain Grade 4 BH Citizenship G4 (printed p. 23), teaches: Friendship as a human need; the qualities of a good friend (loyal, tolerant, generous, cooperative, a sincere adviser) and the manners of friendship; true friends who stand by you against bad companions; and making peace between two quarrelling classmates.

Bahrain's own way: citizenship is taught outwards from the child — homeland love and public property first, then the services of Bahrain's own government, the child's own rights under the UN Convention Bahrain ratified, choosing leaders from the class council up to the King, Crown Prince and Prime Minister, and finally Bahrain inside the Gulf Cooperation Council, its own regional family.
