# Hands-on — Friendship Choosing Good Friends

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Friendship as a human need; the qualities of a good friend (loyal, tolerant, generous, cooperative, a sincere adviser) and the manners of friendship; true friends who stand by you against bad companions; and making peace between two quarrelling classmates. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahrain's way
Say how Bahrain's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
