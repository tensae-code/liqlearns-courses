**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Bahrain, Eritrea).**

Bahrain's own way: citizenship is taught outwards from the child — homeland love and public property first, then the services of Bahrain's own government, the child's own rights under the UN Convention Bahrain ratified, choosing leaders from the class council up to the King, Crown Prince and Prime Minister, and finally Bahrain inside the Gulf Cooperation Council, its own regional family.

Eritrea's own way (ER G6 Citizenship Education): a standalone Citizenship Education subject built entirely from Eritrean stories, poems and dialogues — responsibility is taught through a shop-queue drama and a family Tandoor day, the state through the 1993 referendum story, and patriotism as practical service (keeping the school clean, planting trees at the new clinic) that even a child can do.
