## Sub lesson 2 — Latvia's way — the book's own teaching way

Latvia's own way: social studies is taught integrated with the Latvian language itself (CLIL) for linguistically mixed classes — every topic is framed as a question ("What does it mean to be a pupil?") and the social content is carried by the vocabulary and courtesies of school life. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
