# Hands-on — Safe Relationships and Honesty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Theme 2 Kā veidot sev un citiem drošas attiecības kopienā? (How to build safe relationships in the community) — teaches: politeness words as the basis of good relations, naming emotions, "lie or truth?" — telling truth from falsehood, actions and their consequences, and what is safe or unsafe. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Latvia's way
Say how Latvia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
