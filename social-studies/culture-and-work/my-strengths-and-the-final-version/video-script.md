# Video script — My Strengths and the Final Version

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 53, teaches: knowing your own strength (نقطة القوة) — a field or skill you enjoy and succeed in — and the points you find hard; improving (التحسن) as making positive changes to do better; Sheikh Zayed's saying that the desert taught patience until goodness grows, our ancestors' adapting to the desert's harshness standing as the model of flexibility and strength of will; and finishing the project — the effect of hard work on the piece, and the pride (الفخر) felt when the work is done

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
