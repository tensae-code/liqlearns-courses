The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Morocco Grade 9 Tajdid Ijtiamiat 3AC, printed p. 163, teaches: defining a social problem, gathering evidence about it and proposing action, worked on real cases such as violence against women.

Morocco's own way: the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role.
