# Hands-on — Studying a Social Problem Step by Step

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Treating a social phenomenon through a processing grid (معالجة ظاهرة اجتماعية من خلال شبكة معالجة)
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 9 Tajdid Ijtiamiat 3AC, printed p. 163, teaches: defining a social problem, gathering evidence about it and proposing action, worked on real cases such as violence against women. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the final collège year makes citizenship a practice — pupils sit on school councils, study a social problem with a formal grid, fight corruption, protect heritage and environment, and meet the world through Morocco's international role. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
