## Sub lesson 1 — Honesty Telling the Truth

Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies (printed p. 105), teaches: Truthfulness is a supreme value that cannot be dispensed with in establishing good human relations, and all the heavenly religions emphasised it; truthfulness is that one's speech matches reality; and its benefits are that it purifies man from harm, protects him from dangers — salvation is in truthfulness — and distances him from lying and cheating. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: the components are named one by one with where each lives and what each built, equality is stated in the concept itself — all equal in rights and duties — and the values are taught as a civilisational inheritance, from keeping covenants "for thousands of years" to altruism as the road to social justice. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
