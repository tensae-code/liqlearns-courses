# Hands-on — Honesty Telling the Truth

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Truthfulness is a supreme value that cannot be dispensed with in establishing good human relations, and all the heavenly religions emphasised it; truthfulness is that one's speech matches reality; and its benefits are that it purifies man from harm, protects him from dangers — salvation is in truthfulness — and distances him from lying and cheating. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
