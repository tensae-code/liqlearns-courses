# Hands-on — Respect Courtesy and Gratitude

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Courtesy learned as a poem and a checklist — "Good Manners" (we say thank you, we say please, we don't interrupt or tease, we listen when people talk to us) and ten courteous behaviours the child ticks as their own, sealed by an "I am Sorry!" card drawn for someone they were rude to; and respect itself — treating parents, teachers, friends, grandparents and guests the way you like to be treated. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
