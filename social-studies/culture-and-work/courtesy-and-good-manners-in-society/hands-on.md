# Hands-on — Courtesy and Good Manners in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: courtesy as treating others with respect and sensitivity and respecting their rights in social life — greeting the family in the morning, table manners, being respectful and understanding in daily dealings, checked on a self-check list Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## We are courteous and well-mannered
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: courtesy words and manners inside family life — "günaydın", "elinize sağlık", "lütfen", "teşekkür ederim", knocking before entering a family member's room, asking permission for their things, sitting down and rising from the table together, respecting elders — the home version of the courtesy lesson proposed at Book 1 Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
