The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Turkey Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed p. 88, teaches: courtesy as treating others with respect and sensitivity and respecting their rights in social life — greeting the family in the morning, table manners, being respectful and understanding in daily dealings, checked on a self-check list

Turkey Grade 1 Hayat Bilgisi 1. Kitap, printed p. 92, teaches: courtesy words and manners inside family life — "günaydın", "elinize sağlık", "lütfen", "teşekkür ederim", knocking before entering a family member's room, asking permission for their things, sitting down and rising from the table together, respecting elders — the home version of the courtesy lesson proposed at Book 1

Turkey's own way: Hayat Bilgisi (Life Studies) is Turkey's integrated primary subject, rebuilt under the Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli as six learning areas; this first book runs Me-and-My-School → My Health and Safety → My Family and Society, every topic in the same rhythm (remember, explore, knowledge journey, practise, what we learned), with the year's special days carried as poems at the head of each area and the Student Oath (Andımız) printed at the front.

Turkey's own way: the Grade 1 year opens school itself as the first society: a six-year-old first learns to introduce herself and listen, then the school's rooms and workers, then rules the class itself votes on, and only then the family as the second society — nuclear and extended family, the values that hold it together, and jobs shared like a team.
