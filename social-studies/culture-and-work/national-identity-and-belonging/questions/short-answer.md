# National Identity and Belonging — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: National Identity and Belonging — the components of national identity drawn from King Abdulaziz's words of 1352H (rule by God's Book and His Prophet's Sunnah, every citizen a soldier and policeman for the homeland, no favouritism except by what benefits the people); the threats to national identity; how belonging shows — joining festivals, keeping the systems, volunteering in good works, valuing work, respecting others, guarding the homeland's gains and defending the country and its symbols; and the benefits of belonging to the homeland — strengthening national unity and the homeland's stability, psychological stability leading to positive interaction and good citizenship, driving progress politically, economically and socially, protecting the homeland and its gains, and a society free of violence, terrorism and decay (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way — Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes. (any close wording).**
