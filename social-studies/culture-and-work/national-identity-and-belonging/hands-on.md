# Hands-on — National Identity and Belonging

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the components of national identity drawn from King Abdulaziz's words of 1352H (rule by God's Book and His Prophet's Sunnah, every citizen a soldier and policeman for the homeland, no favouritism except by what benefits the people); the threats to national identity; how belonging shows — joining festivals, keeping the systems, volunteering in good works, valuing work, respecting others, guarding the homeland's gains and defending the country and its symbols; and the benefits of belonging to the homeland — strengthening national unity and the homeland's stability, psychological stability leading to positive interaction and good citizenship, driving progress politically, economically and socially, protecting the homeland and its gains, and a society free of violence, terrorism and decay. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
