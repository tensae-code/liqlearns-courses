# The Right to a Life Without Violence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The school must be a violence-free space; school violence named in its kinds — bullying in verbal, social and physical forms, always an abuse of power — alongside gender, emotional, physical, psychological, sexual and economic violence.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: School violence violates pupils' rights and dignity (UNESCO 2020: over 30% of the world's pupils have suffered bullying); the protection measures, reporting mechanisms and the institutions that work for a life free of violence, extended to gender violence and human trafficking.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: Naming gender and sexual violence and human trafficking so they are not normalised — how they appear, and the protection and reporting mechanisms for a life free of violence, in person and online (first placed from Grade 7 in this wave).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Mexico's student books, named in lesson/sources.md.**
