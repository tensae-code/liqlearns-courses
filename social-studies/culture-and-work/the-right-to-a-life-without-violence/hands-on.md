# Hands-on — The Right to a Life Without Violence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A School Free of Violence (Grade 7)
Work through this part with your hands: the school must be a violence-free space; school violence named in its kinds — bullying in verbal, social and physical forms, always an abuse of power — alongside gender, emotional, physical, psychological, sexual and economic violence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Preventing School and Gender Violence and Trafficking (Grade 8)
Work through this part with your hands: school violence violates pupils' rights and dignity (UNESCO 2020: over 30% of the world's pupils have suffered bullying); the protection measures, reporting mechanisms and the institutions that work for a life free of violence, extended to gender violence and human trafficking. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Protection and Reporting against Gender Violence and Trafficking (Grade 9)
Work through this part with your hands: naming gender and sexual violence and human trafficking so they are not normalised — how they appear, and the protection and reporting mechanisms for a life free of violence, in person and online (first placed from Grade 7 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
