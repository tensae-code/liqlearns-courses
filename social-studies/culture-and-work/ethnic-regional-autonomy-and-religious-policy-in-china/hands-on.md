# Hands-on — Ethnic Regional Autonomy and Religious Policy in China

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: China as a unitary multi-ethnic state of 56 ethnic groups in a plural-but-integrated pattern; ethnic equality, unity and common prosperity; regional autonomy under unified state leadership in areas where minorities live in concentration; and, in the same section, the policy on religion — freedom of religious belief, lawful management of religious affairs, independence and self-governance of Chinese religions, and guiding religions to adapt to socialist society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
