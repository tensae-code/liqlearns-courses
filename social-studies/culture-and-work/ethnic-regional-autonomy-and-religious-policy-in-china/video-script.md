# Video script — Ethnic Regional Autonomy and Religious Policy in China

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

China Senior 思想政治必修3 (printed p. 59), teaches: China as a unitary multi-ethnic state of 56 ethnic groups in a plural-but-integrated pattern; ethnic equality, unity and common prosperity; regional autonomy under unified state leadership in areas where minorities live in concentration; and, in the same section, the policy on religion — freedom of religious belief, lawful management of religious affairs, independence and self-governance of Chinese religions, and guiding religions to adapt to socialist society.

China's own way: the civics core of the senior Politics course, built as three blocks in one chain — who leads (the Party: chosen by history and the people, advanced, ruling through political, ideological and organisational leadership), who owns the state (the people, through the people's democratic dictatorship, the people's congress as fundamental system, and the three basic systems of multiparty cooperation, ethnic regional autonomy and grassroots self-governance), and how the state is run (comprehensively by law: a rule-of-law country, government and society built together through scientific legislation, strict enforcement, impartial justice and universal observance) — every lesson opening with 探究与分享 cases and closing in a unit 综合探究 project.
