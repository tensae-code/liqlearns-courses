# Video script — Ethnic Groups and Living Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG (printed p. 55), teaches: A chief, my clan and my tribe, comparing clans and tribes; a study of a clan or ethnic group; ethnic groups in the community; people from other countries; and marriage across different cultures.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 5): Grade 5 turns from the community's workers to its decisions — who decides in the family, the clan, the village court and the Local Level Government — and to the many societies of PNG, matrilineal and patrilineal, highlands and coast.
