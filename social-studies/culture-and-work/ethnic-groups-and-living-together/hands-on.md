# Hands-on — Ethnic Groups and Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Societies
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A chief, my clan and my tribe, comparing clans and tribes; a study of a clan or ethnic group; ethnic groups in the community; people from other countries; and marriage across different cultures. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Papua New Guinea's way
Say how Papua New Guinea's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
