# Hands-on — Life In City And Village

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 5 Life in city and village (زندگی در شهر و روستا): our country Iran has many beautiful cities and prosperous villages — do you live in a city or a village?; the village child speaks: most villagers' work is farming and gardening, and some, beside farming, keep animals and make handicrafts such as carpets and jajim; life in the big cities, with the table of Iran's most populous cities in 1401 (2022); and the map of Iran's provinces (استان‌های ایران) — our country, our big home, divided into parts so it can be run better and more easily. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
