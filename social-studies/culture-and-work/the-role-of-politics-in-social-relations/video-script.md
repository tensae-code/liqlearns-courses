Armenia Grade 7 Social Science student, printed p. 134, teaches: what politics does inside society's relations.

Armenia's own way: Social Science opens with the person, not the state — nature, society, mind, morality, beauty, law, economy, politics — and citizenship is introduced from the child's own documents: an Armenian citizen's membership is "fixed by the birth certificate and the passport", with rights from the state and duties owed back.
