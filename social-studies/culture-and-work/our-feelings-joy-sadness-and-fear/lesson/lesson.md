## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: civics is taught by analogy from the school camp trip — a group needs roles, rules and a leader, so a country needs a government — and the Islamic Republic is presented as the people's own choice, made in the Farvardin 1358 referendum and renewed in every Majlis election.
