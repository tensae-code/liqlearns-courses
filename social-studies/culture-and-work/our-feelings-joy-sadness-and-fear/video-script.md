# Video script — Our Feelings Joy Sadness and Fear

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 5 (student book, Persian), printed p. 7, teaches: joy, sadness and fear as natural feelings everyone has; fitting fear protects us from danger, and unfitting fears are faced by naming them and consulting elders.

Iran's own way: civics is taught by analogy from the school camp trip — a group needs roles, rules and a leader, so a country needs a government — and the Islamic Republic is presented as the people's own choice, made in the Farvardin 1358 referendum and renewed in every Majlis election.
