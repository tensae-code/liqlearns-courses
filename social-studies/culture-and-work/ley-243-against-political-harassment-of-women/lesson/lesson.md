## Sub lesson 1 — Ley Nº 243 (Year 3)

Bolivia Compendio Ciencias Sociales (compendium p. 269), teaches: The 2012 law born of the murder of councilwoman Juana Quispe of Ancoraimes — mechanisms of prevention, attention and sanction against individual and collective acts of political harassment and violence against women, so elected women can exercise their political rights in full; opened by the real case of the Chuquisaca councilwoman forced to resign in 2011. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way

Bolivia's own way: the teachers' compendium carries two civics units no held student book carries — the Year 3 pair of women's-protection laws, taught from real cases (a councilwoman driven from office; the murdered concejala Juana Quispe), and the Year 5 citizenship unit, which builds Bolivian citizenship straight from CPE Articles 141–144 and ends with the stateless person who can neither study, work nor marry.
