# Hands-on — Ley 243 Against Political Harassment of Women

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the 2012 law born of the murder of councilwoman Juana Quispe of Ancoraimes — mechanisms of prevention, attention and sanction against individual and collective acts of political harassment and violence against women, so elected women can exercise their political rights in full; opened by the real case of the Chuquisaca councilwoman forced to resign in 2011. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
