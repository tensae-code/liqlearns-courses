# Hands-on — Keeping Our Country Safe Police Defence and Courts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Guyana Police Force (established 1839, HQ at Eve Leary) ensures safety and order for every citizen, under the Minister of Home Affairs, with ranks from Lance Corporal to Commissioner. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Guyana Defence Force (from 1 November 1965) — men and women soldiers in corps (infantry, medical, air, ordnance, training), led by the Chief-of-Staff under the President as Commander-in-Chief. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
