# Hands-on — Social Wellbeing Bullying Peer Pressure and Substance Abuse

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A happy society rests on respect, care and love; three social problems worked in turn — bullying (repeated hurting for power; stopped by guidance, love and care), peer pressure (choosing good friends, saying no politely) and substance abuse (alcohol, doma, tobacco — harms to health, family and safety, and habits that help avoid it). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
