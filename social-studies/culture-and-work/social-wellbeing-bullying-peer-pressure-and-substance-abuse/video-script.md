# Video script — Social Wellbeing Bullying Peer Pressure and Substance Abuse

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bhutan Class IV Social Studies IV, printed p. 41, teaches: a happy society rests on respect, care and love; three social problems worked in turn — bullying (repeated hurting for power; stopped by guidance, love and care), peer pressure (choosing good friends, saying no politely) and substance abuse (alcohol, doma, tobacco — harms to health, family and safety, and habits that help avoid it).

Bhutan's own way: local government is taught from the child's own gewog upward — the elected Gup, Mangmi and Tshogpa, their dispute-settling and development roles, and the kabney/rachu they wear — and social well-being is taught as community problems (bullying, peer pressure, substance abuse) to be solved together.
