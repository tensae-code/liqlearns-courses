# Video script — The Political Spectrum Left Right and Centre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Armenia Grade 10 Social Science student, printed p. 32, teaches: the political spectrum and the left–right distinction — left for social equality and public forms, right for hierarchy, private property and the free market, centre for balance — worked through a fictional parliamentary-election case.

Armenia's own way: the ideology theme is taught through one fictional Armenian community — pensioner Aram of Bardzrashen village and his granddaughter Anahit, a municipality worker in Lusaber, argue pensions and a mining plant, and a fictional "Lernastan" election case lets liberal, conservative and socialist forces present rival answers to the same village problem.
