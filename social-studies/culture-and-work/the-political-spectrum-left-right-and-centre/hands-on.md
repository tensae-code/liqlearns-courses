# Hands-on — The Political Spectrum Left Right and Centre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the political spectrum and the left–right distinction — left for social equality and public forms, right for hierarchy, private property and the free market, centre for balance — worked through a fictional parliamentary-election case. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Armenia's way
Say how Armenia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
