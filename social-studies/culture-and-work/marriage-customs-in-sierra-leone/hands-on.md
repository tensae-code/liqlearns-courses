# Hands-on — Marriage Customs in Sierra Leone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the three forms of marriage in Sierra Leone (religious, customary/traditional, registry/judiciary) and the causes of separation and divorce. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sierra Leone's way
Say how Sierra Leone's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
