# Tiahuanaco's Andean trade network — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** About a trade network along the Andes — the lesson teaches that —
a) Tiahuanaco held a long-distance trade network along the Andes  b) textiles and camelid products — meat and wool — were exchanged with other civilisations  c) Tiahuanaco's elite controlled and monitored trade, accumulating wealth and resources

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** About the elite controls and monitors trade — the lesson teaches that —
