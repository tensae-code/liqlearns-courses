# Tiahuanaco's Andean trade network — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tiahuanaco traded only within its own city walls.
**Answer: False** — its network ran along the Andes to other civilisations.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tiahuanaco exported only gold and silver.
**Answer: False** — the book names textiles and camelid products — meat and wool.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Trade in Tiahuanaco was unregulated and leaderless.
**Answer: False** — the elite controlled and monitored it, accumulating wealth.
