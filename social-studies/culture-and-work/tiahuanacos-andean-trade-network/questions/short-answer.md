# Tiahuanaco's Andean trade network — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, from the lesson.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the lesson teach about a trade network along the Andes?
**Answer:** Tiahuanaco held a long-distance trade network along the Andes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the lesson teach about textiles and camelid products exchanged?
**Answer:** Textiles and camelid products — meat and wool — were exchanged with other civilisations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the lesson teach about the elite controls and monitors trade?
**Answer:** Tiahuanaco's elite controlled and monitored trade, accumulating wealth and resources.
