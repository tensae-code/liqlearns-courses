# Tiahuanaco's Andean trade network

## Do it first

Before you read on, write what you already think this lesson's title means — tiahuanaco's Andean trade network — in one sentence of your own. Keep it; you will check it against the lesson at the end.

## Sub lesson 1 — A trade network along the Andes

Tiahuanaco held a trade network along the Andes: its textiles and camelid products travelled to other civilisations, and goods travelled back, in long-distance networks that tied the highlands to distant peoples.

## Sub lesson 2 — Textiles and camelid products exchanged

What moved along the network: textiles and camelid products — meat and wool from the herds of the altiplano — exchanged with other civilisations for what the highlands lacked.

## Sub lesson 3 — The elite controls and monitors trade

Trade was not left to chance: the elite controlled and monitored it, accumulating wealth and resources. Long-distance exchange, in Tiahuanaco, was an instrument of power as well as of supply.
