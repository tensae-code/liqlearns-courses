# Video script — Tiahuanaco's Andean trade network

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Tiahuanaco's Andean trade network — as Bolivia's book teaches it, says the narrator: Tiahuanaco held a long-distance trade network along the Andes; textiles and camelid products — meat and wool — were exchanged with other civilisations; Tiahuanaco's elite controlled and monitored trade, accumulating wealth and resources.
