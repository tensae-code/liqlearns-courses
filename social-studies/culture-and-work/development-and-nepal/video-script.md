# Video script — Development and Nepal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal Grade 8 Teacher's Guide (2081) (guide p. 6), teaches: The guide's intro defines development as the continuous improvement of life, sorts countries into developed, developing and underdeveloped, and sets pupils to find the possibilities of carrying Nepal into the developed ranks [TEACHER].

Nepal's own way: the guide opens Grade 8 with development itself — what development is, developed against developing countries, and Nepal's possibilities — then grounds the federation in a table every pupil fills in: their own province, its capital, their district and local headquarters.
