# Mexico a Pluricultural Nation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Mexico is a pluricultural nation by its own Constitution (Article 2): 71 indigenous peoples, Afro-Mexican communities, migrants and urban groups hold identities, languages and worldviews that must be equally respected, marked each 12 October on the Day of the Pluricultural Nation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Mexico's student books, named in lesson/sources.md.**
