# Hands-on — Mexico a Pluricultural Nation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mexico a Pluricultural Nation
Work through this part with your hands: Mexico is a pluricultural nation by its own Constitution (Article 2): 71 indigenous peoples, Afro-Mexican communities, migrants and urban groups hold identities, languages and worldviews that must be equally respected, marked each 12 October on the Day of the Pluricultural Nation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
