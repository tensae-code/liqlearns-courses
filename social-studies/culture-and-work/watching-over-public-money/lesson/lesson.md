## Sub lesson 1 — Watching Over Public Money

Mexico Grade 8 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 2 (secundaria) (printed pp. 341–345), teaches: Citizens watch the public purse — transparency, accountability, order, control and fighting corruption as the guiding principles, and participation that is organised, informed and responsible, aimed at the common good. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way

Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
