# Watching Over Public Money — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Watching Over Public Money — citizens watch the public purse — transparency, accountability, order, control and fighting corruption as the guiding principles, and participation that is organised, informed and responsible, aimed at the common good (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way — Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality. (any close wording).**
