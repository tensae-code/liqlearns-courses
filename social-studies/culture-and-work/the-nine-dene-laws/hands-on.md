# Hands-on — The Nine Dene Laws

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The laws of the Dene from the story of Yamoria the Law Giver, told by Elder George Blondin — share what you have; help each other; love each other as much as possible; be respectful of Elders and everything around you; sleep at night and work during the day; be polite and don't argue with anyone; young girls and boys should behave respectfully; pass on the teachings; be as happy as possible at all times. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Canada's way
Say how Canada's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
