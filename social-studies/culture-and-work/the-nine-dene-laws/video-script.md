# Video script — The Nine Dene Laws

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Canada Grade 4 Stories of Our Origins TG (printed pp. 18–21), teaches: The laws of the Dene from the story of Yamoria the Law Giver, told by Elder George Blondin — share what you have; help each other; love each other as much as possible; be respectful of Elders and everything around you; sleep at night and work during the day; be polite and don't argue with anyone; young girls and boys should behave respectfully; pass on the teachings; be as happy as possible at all times.

Canada's own way: The unit opens not with the state's law but with the Dene's own laws, handed down in the Yamoria story — law as story, kept by Elders and by the group's shared memory.
