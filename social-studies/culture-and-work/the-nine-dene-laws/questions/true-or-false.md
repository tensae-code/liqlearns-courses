# The Nine Dene Laws — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The laws of the Dene from the story of Yamoria the Law Giver, told by Elder George Blondin — share what you have; help each other; love each other as much as possible; be respectful of Elders and everything around you; sleep at night and work during the day; be polite and don't argue with anyone; young girls and boys should behave respectfully; pass on the teachings; be as happy as possible at all times.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Canada's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
