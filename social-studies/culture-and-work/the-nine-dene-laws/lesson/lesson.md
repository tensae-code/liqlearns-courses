# The Nine Dene Laws

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the laws of the Dene from the story of Yamoria the Law Giver, told by Elder George Blondin — share what you have; help each other; love each other as much as possible; be respectful of Elders and everything around you; sleep at night and work during the day; be polite and don't argue with anyone; young girls and boys should behave respectfully; pass on the teachings; be as happy as possible at all times — as Canada's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.
