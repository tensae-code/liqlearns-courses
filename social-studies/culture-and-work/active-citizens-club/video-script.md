# Video script — Active Citizens Club

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bangladesh Grade 6 BD G6 HSS (printed pp. 29–31), teaches: Pupils form an Active Citizens Club to do development work — activities agreed by consensus, a committee with named posts, duties, membership eligibility and members' rights and duties, the committee chosen by election and the teacher as adviser.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 6): An inquiry-based combined History and Social Science course taught through a class teacher character (Khushi Apa) and group investigations; civics begins not with definitions but with a football game played with and without rules, and sociology is built from a village flood story (the Dharmagola grain bank) before any term is named..
