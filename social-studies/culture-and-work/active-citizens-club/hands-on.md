# Hands-on — Active Citizens Club

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Pupils form an Active Citizens Club to do development work — activities agreed by consensus, a committee with named posts, duties, membership eligibility and members' rights and duties, the committee chosen by election and the teacher as adviser. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
