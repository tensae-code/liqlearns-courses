# The Somali Girl — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Gabadha Soomaaliyeed (The Somali Girl)" is mainly about —
a) the Somali girl's place in the new society — her schooling, her work and her dignity as half the nation  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the Somali girl's place in the new society — her schooling, her work and her dignity as half the nation**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The part of this lesson called "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" is mainly about —
a) Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag.  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag.**
