# The Somali Girl

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the Somali girl's place in the new society — her schooling, her work and her dignity as half the nation — as Somalia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Gabadha Soomaaliyeed (The Somali Girl)

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 56), teaches: the Somali girl's place in the new society — her schooling, her work and her dignity as half the nation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
