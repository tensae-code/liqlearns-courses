# Video script — Teenage Pregnancy and Wise Choices

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 9 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 3 (printed pp. 33–34), teaches: Young people dream of good things and achieve them by having good objectives and working positively towards them; becoming pregnant when not ready means giving up those good things — teenagers need firm values, a positive self-image and a purpose in life, valuing themselves and finishing school, and leaving dating and pregnancy for later in life.

Guyana's own way: the secondary book teaches the same citizenship at national scale — CARICOM as the region's own institution, the family as society's oldest institution, women's equality written into the 1980 Constitution and the Acts of the 1990s, and the state answering pollution through the Environmental Protection Agency.
