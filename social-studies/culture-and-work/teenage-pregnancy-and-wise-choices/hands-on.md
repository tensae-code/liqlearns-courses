# Hands-on — Teenage Pregnancy and Wise Choices

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Young people dream of good things and achieve them by having good objectives and working positively towards them; becoming pregnant when not ready means giving up those good things — teenagers need firm values, a positive self-image and a purpose in life, valuing themselves and finishing school, and leaving dating and pregnancy for later in life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
