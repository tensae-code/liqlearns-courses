## Sub lesson 1 — Issues in the family: teenage pregnancy

Guyana Grade 9 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 3 (printed pp. 33–34), teaches: Young people dream of good things and achieve them by having good objectives and working positively towards them; becoming pregnant when not ready means giving up those good things — teenagers need firm values, a positive self-image and a purpose in life, valuing themselves and finishing school, and leaving dating and pregnancy for later in life. ## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: the secondary book teaches the same citizenship at national scale — CARICOM as the region's own institution, the family as society's oldest institution, women's equality written into the 1980 Constitution and the Acts of the 1990s, and the state answering pollution through the Environmental Protection Agency.
