# Hands-on — Helping in Crises Aid and First Help

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: After a flood a village pools rice, dal, vegetables, oil and salt, cooks for all with children, pregnant women, the sick and the elderly served first, and builds a Dharmagola (grain bank / Rice Bank) run by a committee of men and women, borrowing in crisis and repaying after. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
