The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Bangladesh Grade 6 BD G6 HSS (printed p. 125), teaches: After a flood a village pools rice, dal, vegetables, oil and salt, cooks for all with children, pregnant women, the sick and the elderly served first, and builds a Dharmagola (grain bank / Rice Bank) run by a committee of men and women, borrowing in crisis and repaying after.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 6): An inquiry-based combined History and Social Science course taught through a class teacher character (Khushi Apa) and group investigations; civics begins not with definitions but with a football game played with and without rules, and sociology is built from a village flood story (the Dharmagola grain bank) before any term is named..
