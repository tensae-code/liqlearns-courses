# Video script — Traditional Storytelling Carries Our Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G1 Term 2 student book, printed p. 3, teaches: what storytelling (السرد القصصي) is and what the traditional storytelling is for; the kinds of traditional stories; how the traditional tales are carried from one generation to the next (من جيل إلى جيل); what we can learn from a grandfather's tale; and why the national identity (الهوية الوطنية) that the stories carry matters — with the tale of "Generosity and charity" (الكرم والإحسان) told in class

United Arab Emirates' own way: the youngest pupils meet their heritage as a story told by a grandmother — the date palm and its fruit become a symbol of the country's identity — and meet honesty the same way, explored through tales before it is ever stated as a rule.
