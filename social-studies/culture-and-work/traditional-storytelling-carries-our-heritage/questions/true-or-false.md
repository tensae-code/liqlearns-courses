# Traditional Storytelling Carries Our Heritage — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: what storytelling (السرد القصصي) is and what the traditional storytelling is for; the kinds of traditional stories; how the traditional tales are carried from one generation to the next (من جيل إلى جيل); what we can learn from a grandfather's tale; and why the national identity (الهوية الوطنية) that the stories carry matters — with the tale of "Generosity and charity" (الكرم والإحسان) told in class
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: the youngest pupils meet their heritage as a story told by a grandmother — the date palm and its fruit become a symbol of the country's identity — and meet honesty the same way, explored through tales before it is ever stated as a rule.
**Answer: True**
