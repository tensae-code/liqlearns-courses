# Traditional Storytelling Carries Our Heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: What do you know about storytelling? and Traditional storytelling — what storytelling (السرد القصصي) is and what the traditional storytelling is for; the kinds of traditional stories; how the traditional tales are carried from one generation to the next (من جيل إلى جيل); what we can learn from a grandfather's tale; and why the national identity (الهوية الوطنية) that the stories carry matters — with the tale of "Generosity and charity" (الكرم والإحسان) told in class (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — the youngest pupils meet their heritage as a story told by a grandmother — the date palm and its fruit become a symbol of the country's identity — and meet honesty the same way, explored through tales before it is ever stated as a rule. (any close wording).**
