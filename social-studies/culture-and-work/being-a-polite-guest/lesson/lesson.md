## Sub lesson 1 — Polite guest

Myanmar Morality and Civics Grade 3 (printed p. 6), teaches: How to behave in someone else's house — greeting, sitting, eating and speaking politely as a guest. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way

Myanmar's own way: the Grade 3 units keep the story-and-poem shape but argue finer points: fairness is NOT equal shares (the hungry son gets bread, the full daughter a candy; the girl with glasses sits in front), trust is keeping a friend's weakness secret, and the clean-environment unit teaches Reduce–Reuse–Recycle by name. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
