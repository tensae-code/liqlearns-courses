# Hands-on — Being a Polite Guest

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Polite guest
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How to behave in someone else's house — greeting, sitting, eating and speaking politely as a guest. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
