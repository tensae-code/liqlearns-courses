# Days I Love Eid and Ramadan Customs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the country's loved days as customs, not doctrine — Eid day: preparing for the Eid prayer and greeting cards for parents; Ramadan: the month of fasting, good deeds in it, harees among the most famous Emirati dishes of the month, going with father to watch the iftar cannon, and the folk games and guest-welcoming customs of the occasions
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: at six years old the course already runs on two rails — the working country (how we travel, the jobs our grandfathers did, the job I will do) and the inherited country (Zayed the father, the days we love, our heritage) — and every lesson plan is required to name the citizenship concepts it builds: belonging, citizenship, social responsibility, cooperation, national identity.
**Answer: True**
