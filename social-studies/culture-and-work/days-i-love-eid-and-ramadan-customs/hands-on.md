# Hands-on — Days I Love Eid and Ramadan Customs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Days I love
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the country's loved days as customs, not doctrine — Eid day: preparing for the Eid prayer and greeting cards for parents; Ramadan: the month of fasting, good deeds in it, harees among the most famous Emirati dishes of the month, going with father to watch the iftar cannon, and the folk games and guest-welcoming customs of the occasions Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
