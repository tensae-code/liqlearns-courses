# Labour Day Honouring Workers

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: many workers march on Labour Day; honouring the country's workers — as Guyana's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Labour Day

Guyana Grade 1 Social Studies For Our Children Book 1 (printed p. 45), teaches: Many workers march on Labour Day; honouring the country's workers. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — May Day

Guyana Grade 2 Social Studies For Our Children Book 2 (printed p. 32 (NEW slug first proposed in Book 1 above)), teaches: The May Day parade on 1 May, also called Labour Day, and the colours workers wear. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: a story-led first book built around two children, Ron and Sue — family, school, rules and nation are all met through their home, street and school day, with a draw/name/complete task on almost every page. Book 2 widens Book 1's world — the same Ron and Sue now meet family types, class rules, neighbourhood shops and, for the first time, the country's leaders (President, Prime Minister, Ministers) and its national days (Republic Day, May Day, Enmore Martyrs' Day, Freedom Day). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
