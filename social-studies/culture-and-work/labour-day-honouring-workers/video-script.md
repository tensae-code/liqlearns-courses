# Video script — Labour Day Honouring Workers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Guyana Grade 1 Social Studies For Our Children Book 1 (printed p. 45), teaches: Many workers march on Labour Day; honouring the country's workers.

Guyana Grade 2 Social Studies For Our Children Book 2 (printed p. 32 (NEW slug first proposed in Book 1 above)), teaches: The May Day parade on 1 May, also called Labour Day, and the colours workers wear.

Guyana's own way: a story-led first book built around two children, Ron and Sue — family, school, rules and nation are all met through their home, street and school day, with a draw/name/complete task on almost every page. Book 2 widens Book 1's world — the same Ron and Sue now meet family types, class rules, neighbourhood shops and, for the first time, the country's leaders (President, Prime Minister, Ministers) and its national days (Republic Day, May Day, Enmore Martyrs' Day, Freedom Day).
