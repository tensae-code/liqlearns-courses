# Human Rights and Peaceful Living — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part teaches: good citizens support human rights; deciding well promotes unity, promotes peace and supports human rights — pictured as a police officer stopping a fight and children holding a "promote peace to protect human rights" banner.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part teaches: the guide's own examples — police show support for human rights by arresting a criminal instead of leaving him to mob justice, and people support human rights by giving relief food to those hit by drought and floods; our decisions determine our relationship with other people (the human-rights lesson is filed NEW from Book 3 in this wave).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This part teaches: human rights are the rights everyone is entitled to; respecting them and gender equality promotes security, justice and peace and unity, protects the rights of people with special needs, and ensures equal distribution of resources — pictured by a suspect being rescued from mob justice (the human-rights lesson is filed NEW from Book 3 in this wave).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This part teaches: human rights are basic rules for fair treatment of all human beings, and every human being has a duty to respect them — the book lists the right to equality, a fair trial and life, and the freedoms of association, assembly, speech and religion, and sets a recited pledge to respect and stand up for others' rights (the human-rights lesson is filed NEW from Book 3 in this wave).
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This part teaches: equity is the equal and just distribution of resources, opportunities and power; human rights are categorised as social (housing, freedom from hunger, security, education), civil (fair trial, vote, expression, information, association, movement), economic (property, trade, employment) and environmental rights; justice is fair treatment without discrimination by gender, race or religion, enforced through courts (the human-rights lesson is filed NEW from Book 3 in this wave).
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on South Sudan's student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md, with teacher-guide parts as evidence only.**
