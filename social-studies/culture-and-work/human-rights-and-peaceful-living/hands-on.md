# Hands-on — Human Rights and Peaceful Living

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 5: human rights and peaceful living

## Activity 9 Importance of respect to human rights and gender equality in debates

## Activity 3 (second part): human rights and the pledge
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: human rights are basic rules for fair treatment of all human beings, and every human being has a duty to respect them — the book lists the right to equality, a fair trial and life, and the freedoms of association, assembly, speech and religion, and sets a recited pledge to respect and stand up for others' rights (the human-rights lesson is filed NEW from Book 3 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Activity 8 Developing co-existence: equity, human rights and justice
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: equity is the equal and just distribution of resources, opportunities and power; human rights are categorised as social (housing, freedom from hunger, security, education), civil (fair trial, vote, expression, information, association, movement), economic (property, trade, employment) and environmental rights; justice is fair treatment without discrimination by gender, race or religion, enforced through courts (the human-rights lesson is filed NEW from Book 3 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
