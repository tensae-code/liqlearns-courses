The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Nicaragua Grade 6 Convivencia y Civismo G6 (printed p. 2), teaches: The values that form personal identity, practised to live in peace.

Nicaragua Grade 6 Convivencia y Civismo G6 (printed p. 156), teaches: Being a vanguard in values — leading by practised values.

Nicaragua Grade 4 Convivencia y Civismo G4 (printed p. 27), teaches: Naming and ordering one's own values.

Nicaragua's own way: the final primary civics book widens from Nicaragua to Central America (symbols and heroes compared across the isthmus) and from values to the machinery of rights — the Children's Convention, the CNA code, consumer rights and tax education. Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State.
