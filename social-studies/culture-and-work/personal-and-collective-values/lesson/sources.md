Nicaragua's own way (Book 5 — Convivencia y Civismo G6 (student book, Spanish)): the final primary civics book widens from Nicaragua to Central America (symbols and heroes compared across the isthmus) and from values to the machinery of rights — the Children's Convention, the CNA code, consumer rights and tax education.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 6 — Convivencia y Civismo G4 (student book, Spanish)): Grade 4 teaches cultural identity region by region (Pacific, Caribbean Coast, north and centre each with their own traditions) before the Constitution's principles and the Powers of the State.
