## Sub lesson 1 — Dhagaxtuur waa tilmaan Geesinimo (Dhagaxtuur Is a Sign of Bravery)

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 18), teaches: the Mogadishu memorial where Somali men and women, the SYL youth among them, fought the British colonialists with stones against bullets; the Revolution of 21 October 1969 revived its memory, and the memorials — Dhagaxtuur, Xaawa Taako, Sayid Maxamed Cabdulle Xasan, Daljirka Daahsoon — stand as the nation's signs of freedom (the battle narrative itself is History, named here). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
