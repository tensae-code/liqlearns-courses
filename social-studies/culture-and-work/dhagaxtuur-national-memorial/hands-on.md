# Hands-on — Dhagaxtuur National Memorial

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dhagaxtuur waa tilmaan Geesinimo (Dhagaxtuur Is a Sign of Bravery)
Work through this part with your hands: the Mogadishu memorial where Somali men and women, the SYL youth among them, fought the British colonialists with stones against bullets; the Revolution of 21 October 1969 revived its memory, and the memorials — Dhagaxtuur, Xaawa Taako, Sayid Maxamed Cabdulle Xasan, Daljirka Daahsoon — stand as the nation's signs of freedom (the battle narrative itself is History, named here). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
