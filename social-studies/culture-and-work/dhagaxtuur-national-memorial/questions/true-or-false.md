# Dhagaxtuur National Memorial — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Dhagaxtuur waa tilmaan Geesinimo (Dhagaxtuur Is a Sign of Bravery)" teaches: the Mogadishu memorial where Somali men and women, the SYL youth among them, fought the British colonialists with stones against bullets; the Revolution of 21 October 1969 revived its memory, and the memorials — Dhagaxtuur, Xaawa Taako, Sayid Maxamed Cabdulle Xasan, Daljirka Daahsoon — stand as the nation's signs of freedom (the battle narrative itself is History, named here).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag..
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
