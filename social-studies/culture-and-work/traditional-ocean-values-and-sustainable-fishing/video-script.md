# Video script — Traditional Ocean Values and Sustainable Fishing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tonga's Years 5–6 People and the Sea (printed pp. 12–13), teaches: Traditional uses of the reef and ocean, Tongan fishing values and Uloa and Pola — teaches: values of respect and honour guided fishing; places were set aside with no fishing so nature could regenerate, and the number, size, age and season of the harvest were controlled; faka'apa'apa (respect), lototo (humility), tauhi vaa (nurturing relationships) and mamahi'i me'a (loyalty) as the values that keep the ocean thriving; herding fish with a 1–2 km line of coconut leaves took the whole community working together for the good of all.

Tonga's own way: the sea itself is the community — identity, kinship and duty are taught through the ocean, its sacred fishing rituals and its traditional values.
