# Hands-on — Traditional Ocean Values and Sustainable Fishing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Traditional uses of the reef and ocean, Tongan fishing values and Uloa and Pola — teaches: values of respect and honour guided fishing; places were set aside with no fishing so nature could regenerate, and the number, size, age and season of the harvest were controlled; faka'apa'apa (respect), lototo (humility), tauhi vaa (nurturing relationships) and mamahi'i me'a (loyalty) as the values that keep the ocean thriving; herding fish with a 1–2 km line of coconut leaves took the whole community working together for the good of all. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tonga's way
Say how Tonga's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
