# Hands-on — Politeness and Sincerity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: greetings join hearts — the greetings of every day, practised with a "greeting tree" the class grows. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
