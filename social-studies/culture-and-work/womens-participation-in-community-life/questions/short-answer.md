# Womens Participation in Community Life — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Participación de la mujer en las actividades de mi municipio — Women as active members of home, community and municipality, with the same rights and duties as men, including voting, being elected and holding municipal office (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: La participación de la mujer en la sociedad nicaragüense — Women's fundamental role in the country's socio-economic and political development, in state institutions, parties, unions, media and universities (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: La participación de la mujer en la sociedad Centroamericana — Women's participation across Central American society (any close wording).**
