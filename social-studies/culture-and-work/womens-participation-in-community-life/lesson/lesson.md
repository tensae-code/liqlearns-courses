## Sub lesson 1 — Participación de la mujer en las actividades de mi municipio

Nicaragua Grade 3 Estudios Sociales G3 (printed p. 76), teaches: Women as active members of home, community and municipality, with the same rights and duties as men, including voting, being elected and holding municipal office. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — La participación de la mujer en la sociedad Centroamericana

Nicaragua Grade 5 Estudios Sociales G5 (printed p. 137), teaches: Women's participation across Central American society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Nicaragua's way — the books' own teaching way

Nicaragua's own way: community is taught through Rama Cay — an ancestral Caribbean island community governed by a council of elders — and citizenship through the current government's named municipal social programmes and the Caribbean Coast autonomy system. inside the population-and-economy unit, the book teaches "citizen power" as it is organised today — cooperatives, business chambers and the Gabinetes de Familia, Comunidad y Vida — and states citizen participation as a constitutional right. at Grade 5 the civics remnant is regional — women's participation is taught for Central American society as a whole, inside the population unit. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
