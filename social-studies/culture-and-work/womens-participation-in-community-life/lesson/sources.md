**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Nicaragua).**

Nicaragua's own way (Book 1 — Estudios Sociales G3 (student book, Spanish)): community is taught through Rama Cay — an ancestral Caribbean island community governed by a council of elders — and citizenship through the current government's named municipal social programmes and the Caribbean Coast autonomy system.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 8 — Estudios Sociales G4 (student book, Spanish)): inside the population-and-economy unit, the book teaches "citizen power" as it is organised today — cooperatives, business chambers and the Gabinetes de Familia, Comunidad y Vida — and states citizen participation as a constitutional right.

Nicaragua's own way (Book 9 — Estudios Sociales G5 (student book, Spanish)): at Grade 5 the civics remnant is regional — women's participation is taught for Central American society as a whole, inside the population unit.
