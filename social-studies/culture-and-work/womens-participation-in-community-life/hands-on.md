# Hands-on — Womens Participation in Community Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: women as active members of home, community and municipality, with the same rights and duties as men, including voting, being elected and holding municipal office. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## La participación de la mujer en la sociedad nicaragüense
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: women's fundamental role in the country's socio-economic and political development, in state institutions, parties, unions, media and universities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## La participación de la mujer en la sociedad Centroamericana
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: women's participation across Central American society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Nicaragua's way
Say how Nicaragua's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
