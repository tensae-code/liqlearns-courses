Nicaragua Grade 3 Estudios Sociales G3 (printed p. 76), teaches: Women as active members of home, community and municipality, with the same rights and duties as men, including voting, being elected and holding municipal office.

Nicaragua Grade 4 Estudios Sociales G4 (printed p. 193), teaches: Women's fundamental role in the country's socio-economic and political development, in state institutions, parties, unions, media and universities.

Nicaragua Grade 5 Estudios Sociales G5 (printed p. 137), teaches: Women's participation across Central American society.

Nicaragua's own way: community is taught through Rama Cay — an ancestral Caribbean island community governed by a council of elders — and citizenship through the current government's named municipal social programmes and the Caribbean Coast autonomy system. inside the population-and-economy unit, the book teaches "citizen power" as it is organised today — cooperatives, business chambers and the Gabinetes de Familia, Comunidad y Vida — and states citizen participation as a constitutional right. at Grade 5 the civics remnant is regional — women's participation is taught for Central American society as a whole, inside the population unit.
