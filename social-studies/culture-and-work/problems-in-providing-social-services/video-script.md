# Video script — Problems in Providing Social Services

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study (printed p. 34), teaches: The district's problems (poverty, food shortage, irresponsibility) and suggested solutions.

Uganda Excel P5 SST (Term-Three printed p. 27 (PDF p. 191)), teaches: Shortage of funds, population growth, corruption, illiteracy, poor transport, insecurity and epidemics — and the solutions.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study): the district is the child's civic unit — its leaders (LCV chairperson, CAO, RDC, DPC, DEO, DAO) are named by title and role, and children's rights are taught straight from Uganda's laws (a child is anyone below 18).
Uganda's own way (Uganda Excel P5 SST): P5 civics is the independent state itself — what a nation is, its symbols and how to honour them, democracy and elections in Uganda's own terms, then the government: central and local levels, the three arms, and the constitution the people made in 1995.
