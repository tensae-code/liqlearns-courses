# Problems in Providing Social Services

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the district's problems (poverty, food shortage, irresponsibility) and suggested solutions — as Uganda's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Problems the District Meets When Providing Social Services and Their Solutions

Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study (printed p. 34), teaches: the district's problems (poverty, food shortage, irresponsibility) and suggested solutions. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Problems Faced by the Government When Providing Social Services

Uganda Excel P5 SST (Term-Three printed p. 27 (PDF p. 191)), teaches: shortage of funds, population growth, corruption, illiteracy, poor transport, insecurity and epidemics — and the solutions. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Uganda's way — the book's own teaching way (Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study)

Uganda's own way: the district is the child's civic unit — its leaders (LCV chairperson, CAO, RDC, DPC, DEO, DAO) are named by title and role, and children's rights are taught straight from Uganda's laws (a child is anyone below 18). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Uganda's way — the book's own teaching way (Uganda Excel P5 SST)

Uganda's own way: P5 civics is the independent state itself — what a nation is, its symbols and how to honour them, democracy and elections in Uganda's own terms, then the government: central and local levels, the three arms, and the constitution the people made in 1995. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
