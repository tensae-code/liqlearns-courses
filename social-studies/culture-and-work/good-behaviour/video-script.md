# Video script — Good Behaviour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Syria Grade 1 Social Studies (Syria Grade 1 Social Studies G1, printed p. 36), teaches: Choosing the right behaviour in class and in the yard — raise your hand instead of shouting out, keep your desk clean instead of scribbling on it, walk out in order instead of pushing on the stairs

Syria Grade 2 Social Studies (Syria Grade 2 Social Studies G2, printed p. 40), teaches: Sorting behaviour at school into correct and incorrect — pushing at the water taps and jumping on the school stairs against their correct opposites (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1)

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 1): the whole primary series opens with widening rings — Me → Me and You → My Safety → My Society → My Environment → My Homeland — taught almost entirely through picture scenes with a bear mascot, one "I can…" outcome per spread; rights arrive at age six as lived scenes (the child shut out of play has lost a right; the state gives every child the vaccine free).

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 2): Grade 2 is the honesty-and-safety year — the right-decision lesson turns on a shopkeeper who offers a boy an expensive sweet free if he tells no one, the careful lesson warns that things found on the road may be explosives, and rights arrive as a shipwreck game: children who trash a dream island invent rules, and discover the balance — "my right to enjoy nature, my duty to protect it".
