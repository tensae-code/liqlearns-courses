# Hands-on — Good Behaviour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: choosing the right behaviour in class and in the yard — raise your hand instead of shouting out, keep your desk clean instead of scribbling on it, walk out in order instead of pushing on the stairs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
