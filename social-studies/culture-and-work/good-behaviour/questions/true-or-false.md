Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Choosing the right behaviour in class and in the yard — raise your hand instead of shouting out, keep your desk clean instead of scribbling on it, walk out in order instead of pushing on the stairs.
