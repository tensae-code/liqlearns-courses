# Hands-on — Festivals We Share in Guyana and the Caribbean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Guyanese and Caribbean people visit each other's festivals (Mashramani in Guyana, Carnival in Trinidad, Diwali in both) and exchange festival foods with friends — mithai, parsad, gulgula and other sweets and savoury dishes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Amerindian Heritage Month (begun 1995, honouring Stephen Campbell, the first Amerindian Member of Parliament), Emancipation Day with its vigils and libation ceremonies, Arrival Day, and Mashramani, the republic celebration "after hard work". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Phagwah, the festival of colours; Diwali, the festival of lights, with diyas and rangoli; Eid-ul-Adha, the feast of sacrifice, whose food is shared with the poor; and Christmas, celebrated by almost everyone in Guyana. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
