# Festivals We Share in Guyana and the Caribbean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Festivals we share — Guyanese and Caribbean people visit each other's festivals (Mashramani in Guyana, Carnival in Trinidad, Diwali in both) and exchange festival foods with friends — mithai, parsad, gulgula and other sweets and savoury dishes (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Celebrations of the communities — Amerindian Heritage Month (begun 1995, honouring Stephen Campbell, the first Amerindian Member of Parliament), Emancipation Day with its vigils and libation ceremonies, Arrival Day, and Mashramani, the republic celebration "after hard work" (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Festivals of the faiths — Phagwah, the festival of colours; Diwali, the festival of lights, with diyas and rangoli; Eid-ul-Adha, the feast of sacrifice, whose food is shared with the poor; and Christmas, celebrated by almost everyone in Guyana (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the fourth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Why celebrations are important — celebrations bring communities together, help people understand one another, teach gratitude, make people proud of who they are and keep their culture alive for their children (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does the fifth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Celebrations at home — family celebrations — Mother's Day, Diwali, Eid-ul-Adha and Christmas — and that families celebrate the same day differently: Sheldon's family marks birthdays with a party, Jaya's with prayers and a priest's blessing (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** What does the sixth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Celebrations at school — school traditions — Sports Day and Culture Day, when learners display their culture in clothes, food, music and dance, and school Mashramani — celebrations are important because they bring togetherness and unity (any close wording).**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
