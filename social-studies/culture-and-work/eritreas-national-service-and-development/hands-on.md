# Hands-on — Eritreas National Service and Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the National Service Program (1994) — citizens aged 18–40 train six months and work a year in national development — and the Defence Forces' double role, defending the country and building it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
