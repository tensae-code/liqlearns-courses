# Video script — Eritreas National Service and Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eritrea Grade 8 ER G8 Social Studies (printed p. 120), teaches: The National Service Program (1994) — citizens aged 18–40 train six months and work a year in national development — and the Defence Forces' double role, defending the country and building it.

Eritrea's own way: the post-independence state is taught as a single founding package a citizen should know — the 1994 National Charter's aims, land made state property with equal rights for women and men, six regions, a constitution drafted by a commission and ratified in 1997, and national service that builds as well as defends.
