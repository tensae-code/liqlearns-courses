# Taxes and Vat

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the tax as money the state collects from persons, institutions and companies to support society and develop its services; Saudi Arabia's limited, low taxes — income tax on non-Saudis' non-salary income up to 20%, excise tax on selected goods (tobacco and energy drinks 100%), and VAT, an indirect 15% tax on goods and services applied since 1439H/2018 (5% on real-estate transactions, with exemptions such as basic financial services); the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority collects them, zakat going to social-security beneficiaries and taxes to services and development — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 6 book ties citizenship to Saudi Vision 2030 — what a vision is, its realisation programmes and projects, the Quality of Life Programme and the Human Capability Development Programme — and then to citizenship itself, honesty and trustworthiness, guarding national antiquities, and taxes and VAT.
