Saudi Arabia Grade 6 Social Studies Term 1 (printed pp. 160–164), teaches: The tax as money the state collects from persons, institutions and companies to support society and develop its services; Saudi Arabia's limited, low taxes — income tax on non-Saudis' non-salary income up to 20%, excise tax on selected goods (tobacco and energy drinks 100%), and VAT, an indirect 15% tax on goods and services applied since 1439H/2018 (5% on real-estate transactions, with exemptions such as basic financial services); the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority collects them, zakat going to social-security beneficiaries and taxes to services and development.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 6 book ties citizenship to Saudi Vision 2030 — what a vision is, its realisation programmes and projects, the Quality of Life Programme and the Human Capability Development Programme — and then to citizenship itself, honesty and trustworthiness, guarding national antiquities, and taxes and VAT.
