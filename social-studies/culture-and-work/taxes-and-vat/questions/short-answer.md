# Taxes and Vat — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Taxes and Vat — the tax as money the state collects from persons, institutions and companies to support society and develop its services; Saudi Arabia's limited, low taxes — income tax on non-Saudis' non-salary income up to 20%, excise tax on selected goods (tobacco and energy drinks 100%), and VAT, an indirect 15% tax on goods and services applied since 1439H/2018 (5% on real-estate transactions, with exemptions such as basic financial services); the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority collects them, zakat going to social-security beneficiaries and taxes to services and development (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?

(any close wording).**
