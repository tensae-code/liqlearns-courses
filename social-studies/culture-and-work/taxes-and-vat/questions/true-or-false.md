# Taxes and Vat — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: The tax as money the state collects from persons, institutions and companies to support society and develop its services; Saudi Arabia's limited, low taxes — income tax on non-Saudis' non-salary income up to 20%, excise tax on selected goods (tobacco and energy drinks 100%), and VAT, an indirect 15% tax on goods and services applied since 1439H/2018 (5% on real-estate transactions, with exemptions such as basic financial services); the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority collects them, zakat going to social-security beneficiaries and taxes to services and development.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Saudi Arabia's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
