# Hands-on — Taxes and Vat

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the tax as money the state collects from persons, institutions and companies to support society and develop its services; Saudi Arabia's limited, low taxes — income tax on non-Saudis' non-salary income up to 20%, excise tax on selected goods (tobacco and energy drinks 100%), and VAT, an indirect 15% tax on goods and services applied since 1439H/2018 (5% on real-estate transactions, with exemptions such as basic financial services); the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority collects them, zakat going to social-security beneficiaries and taxes to services and development. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
