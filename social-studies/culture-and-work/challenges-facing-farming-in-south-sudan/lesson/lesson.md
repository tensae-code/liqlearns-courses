## Sub lesson 2 — South Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies SB): Landforms are made, not just named — learners push a sheet of paper from both ends to make a fold mountain, and the home examples are the Ironstone Plateau and a farming landscape being changed by the farming itself.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide insists on both sides of every feature — positive and negative effects of physical features on human activities — and on human agency: dumping material in a valley can cause flooding, and climate dictates which farming system a region of Africa can practise.
