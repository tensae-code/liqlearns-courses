# Hands-on — Electing Our Class Leaders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the class elects a president by secret ballot, with a deputy and bodies; the president represents the class in the pupil parliament, organises problem-solving and settles disagreements peacefully. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## North Macedonia's way
Say how North Macedonia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
