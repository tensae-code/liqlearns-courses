# Hands-on — Bhutans Values Framework Gnh and the Five Core Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: TEACHER: Bhutan's values course built on Gross National Happiness and the Buddhist principle of Tha-Dam-Tse Ley-Judy — five core moral values (Respect, Responsibility, Compassion, Integrity, Loyalty) carrying twenty sub-values between them, taught through the TEAM approach (Teaching, Education, Application, Modelling) so that children internalise the values in right thought, right speech and right conduct. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
