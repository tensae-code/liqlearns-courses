Bhutan Class III Values and Citizenship Education Teacher's Handbook 2017, printed pp. iv–vi, teaches: TEACHER: Bhutan's values course built on Gross National Happiness and the Buddhist principle of Tha-Dam-Tse Ley-Judy — five core moral values (Respect, Responsibility, Compassion, Integrity, Loyalty) carrying twenty sub-values between them, taught through the TEAM approach (Teaching, Education, Application, Modelling) so that children internalise the values in right thought, right speech and right conduct.

Bhutan's own way: values are not imported abstractions here — the whole course hangs on Gross National Happiness and the Buddhist principle of Tha-Dam-Tse Ley-Judy, loyalty is taught as Tha Damtshig (mutual trust) and love of Tsawa Sum (King, country and people), responsibility carries the law of cause and effect (ley judrey) inside it, and honour is anchored to a national day — Teachers' Day on 2 May, the third Druk Gyalpo's birthday.
