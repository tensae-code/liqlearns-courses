# Hands-on — Being a Responsible Person

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A person who does his duty
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What a responsible child finishes, at home and at school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Each one his own duty
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every person has a duty — mother, father, teacher, child — and the grateful heart that helps others in return. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## One's role and responsibility
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Knowing your own duty and carrying it out — Shwe Madi as master of ceremony, and the planting team dividing its duties. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Do your duty in the place where you are
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every place lays a duty on the people in it — the water pot by the road, the bus passenger, the farmer, the restaurant staff. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## One who knows duty and takes responsibility
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Seeing the duty yourself and taking it on without being told. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
