# Being a Responsible Person — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: what a responsible child finishes, at home and at school.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: every person has a duty — mother, father, teacher, child — and the grateful heart that helps others in return.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: knowing your own duty and carrying it out — Shwe Madi as master of ceremony, and the planting team dividing its duties.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson's fourth part teaches: every place lays a duty on the people in it — the water pot by the road, the bus passenger, the farmer, the restaurant staff.
**Answer: True**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** This lesson's fifth part teaches: seeing the duty yourself and taking it on without being told.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
