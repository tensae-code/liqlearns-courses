# Hands-on — Tourism In Iran

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 16 Iran's tourism attractions (جاذبه‌های گردشگری ایران): pilgrimage tourism — the Astan Quds Razavi complex (the shrine of Imam Reza, great in architecture and history, with its libraries, medical centre, guesthouse and museums); historical tourism — mosques, caravanserais, palaces, baths, bazaars and bridges; nature tourism (طبیعت‌گردی) — travelling, resting or studying in natural places: mountains, caves, mineral-water springs and deserts (the Khazineh valley in Lorestan pictured); the work of the tour guide; and tourism and protecting the environment — crowds of travellers can damage places, and ecotourism (طبیعت‌گردی) defined as travel to natural areas without harming the environment. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
