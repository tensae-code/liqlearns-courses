# Hands-on — Scientific Legislation Making Good Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Scientific legislation — legislation that respects objective laws and democratic procedure, divides rights and obligations and powers and responsibilities rationally, and uses hearings and public consultation (the Administrative Litigation Law's making and revision as the worked case) so good law promotes development and guarantees good governance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
