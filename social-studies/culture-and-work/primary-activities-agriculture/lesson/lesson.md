# Primary activities — agriculture

## Do it first

List every primary activity you can see from your home or school gate — farming, animals, timber, gathering. Write each with what it takes from nature.

## Sub lesson 1 — Directly from nature

**Primary activities take what people need directly from nature** — wheat, iron, fish, animals, timber. They include **farming, mining, fishing, forestry, hunting and fruit-gathering**.

[Picture 11523 — a farm with crops and animals together]

## Sub lesson 2 — Agriculture — the widest field

**Agriculture — crop production and animal rearing** (in some developing countries also fishing, beekeeping and forestry) — is the **widest primary field**: **about 75% of people in developing countries work in it**, so most of the world lives by farming.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Name six primary activities. Say what agriculture holds, and its share of work in developing countries: about 75%.
