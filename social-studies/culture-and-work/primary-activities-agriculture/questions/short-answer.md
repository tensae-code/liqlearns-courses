# Primary activities — agriculture — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four primary activities.
**Answer:** Any four of: farming, mining, fishing, forestry, hunting, fruit-gathering.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In some developing countries, what else counts inside agriculture?
**Answer:** Fishing, beekeeping and forestry.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is agriculture called the widest primary field?
**Answer:** Because about 75% of people in developing countries work in it.
