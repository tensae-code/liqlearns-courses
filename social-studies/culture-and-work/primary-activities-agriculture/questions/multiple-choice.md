# Primary activities — agriculture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Primary activities take what people need —
a) From factories  b) Directly from nature  c) From imports  d) From offices
**Answer: b) Directly from nature**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which is NOT a primary activity?
a) Fishing  b) Mining  c) Banking  d) Hunting
**Answer: c) Banking**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture is —
a) Crop production and animal rearing  b) Only crop production  c) Only trade  d) Only herding
**Answer: a) Crop production and animal rearing**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** About what share of people in developing countries work in agriculture?
a) 7.5%  b) 25%  c) 75%  d) 100%
**Answer: c) 75%**
