# Primary activities — agriculture — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Forestry is a primary activity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Most of the world lives by farming, by the book's reckoning.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Iron taken from the ground is a factory product.
**Answer: False**
