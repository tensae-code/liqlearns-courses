# Identity and the New Bolivian Id Card — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: La identidad / La biometría — identity as the traits that distinguish a person — name, identity-card number, biometric records (fingerprints, face, voice) — and the new Bolivian cédula de identidad in force from 1 November 2023 (Decreto 4924), which registers more professions and occupations (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship is taught as intercultural democracy — the Western forms (direct, participative, representative) completed by the indigenous peoples' own communitarian democracy, with parity and alternation between women and men written into every candidate list, and the year's closing civics arc runs from women's rights to despatriarcalización, the dismantling of patriarchy as a task of the Plurinational State itself (any close wording).**
