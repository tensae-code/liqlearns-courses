# Hands-on — Identity and the New Bolivian Id Card

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: identity as the traits that distinguish a person — name, identity-card number, biometric records (fingerprints, face, voice) — and the new Bolivian cédula de identidad in force from 1 November 2023 (Decreto 4924), which registers more professions and occupations. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
