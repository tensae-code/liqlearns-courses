# Rights of Guatemalas Four Peoples

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Guatemala is a multicultural country of four peoples — Maya, Garífuna, Xinka and Ladino — formed historically through the conquest and colonisation, each with its own values, ways of life and thought, and its own rights as a people — as Guatemala's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4 project "Derechos de los pueblos"

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 198, teaches: Guatemala is a multicultural country of four peoples — Maya, Garífuna, Xinka and Ladino — formed historically through the conquest and colonisation, each with its own values, ways of life and thought, and its own rights as a people. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
