# Video script — Rights of Guatemalas Four Peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 198, teaches: Guatemala is a multicultural country of four peoples — Maya, Garífuna, Xinka and Ladino — formed historically through the conquest and colonisation, each with its own values, ways of life and thought, and its own rights as a people.

Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.
