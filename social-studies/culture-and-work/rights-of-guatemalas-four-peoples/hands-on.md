# Hands-on — Rights of Guatemalas Four Peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 4 project "Derechos de los pueblos"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 5 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana (2025), printed p. 198, teaches: Guatemala is a multicultural country of four peoples — Maya, Garífuna, Xinka and Ladino — formed historically through the conquest and colonisation, each with its own values, ways of life and thought, and its own rights as a people. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 7) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the fifth-grade book is the series' citizenship book proper — a pupil meets democracy four times over (definition, citizenship, duties, participation) and every right is tied to the article of Guatemala's own Constitution that grants it.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
