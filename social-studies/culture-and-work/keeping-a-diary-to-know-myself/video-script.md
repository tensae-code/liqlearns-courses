---

Mongolia Civic Ethics Grade 6, printed p. 24, teaches: The diary as the instrument of self-knowledge — Tolstoy kept a daily record for over sixty years, ruling himself by it ("control yourself, develop yourself"), and Anne Frank's diary shows a thirteen-year-old examining her own faults in hiding: whether I keep my own promises, whether I judge my acts by an outsider's eye; the pupil begins the same record — what I did, what I said, what I thought.

Mongolia's own way (Link book L2): from Grade 6 the civics subject changes its name and its method — no longer "Civic Education" but "Civic Ethics" (Иргэний ёс зүйн боловсрол), a self-observation workbook in the Buddhist-inflected Mongolian moral tradition: the pupil keeps a diary of their own acts of body, speech and mind, and ethics is practised as a daily discipline before it is ever a rule about others.
