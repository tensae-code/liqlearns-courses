# Hands-on — Keeping a Diary to Know Myself

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.2, diary unit
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The diary as the instrument of self-knowledge — Tolstoy kept a daily record for over sixty years, ruling himself by it ("control yourself, develop yourself"), and Anne Frank's diary shows a thirteen-year-old examining her own faults in hiding: whether I keep my own promises, whether I judge my acts by an outsider's eye; the pupil begins the same record — what I did, what I said, what I thought. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
