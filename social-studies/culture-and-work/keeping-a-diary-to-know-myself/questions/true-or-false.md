# Keeping a Diary to Know Myself — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the diary as the instrument of self-knowledge — Tolstoy kept a daily record for over sixty years, ruling himself by it ("control yourself, develop yourself"), and Anne Frank's diary shows a thirteen-year-old examining her own faults in hiding: whether I keep my own promises, whether I judge my acts by an outsider's eye; the pupil begins the same record — what I did, what I said, what I thought.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
