# Hands-on — Making Our Community Safer

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 5 Activity 1 Making the community safer
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: protect your things from thieves (fence the home, write your name on your books, report lost items to the teacher, report thieves to the police). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## South Sudan's way
Say how South Sudan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
