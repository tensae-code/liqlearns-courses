# Video script — Making Our Community Safer

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 1 P1 Social Studies SB, printed p. 64, teaches: protect your things from thieves (fence the home, write your name on your books, report lost items to the teacher, report thieves to the police).

South Sudan's own way: Community is learned through Okot's own family and compound first — children's duties include looking after cattle and younger siblings, safety means fencing the home and reporting thieves to the police, and peace is taught as class-made rules against fighting plus a peace song in the mother tongue.
