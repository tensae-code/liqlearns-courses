**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Ubaxa Kacaanka Oktoobar (UKO) (The Flowers of the October Revolution)" is mainly about —
a) the state pioneer corps for pupils aged 7–14 — the nation's eyes and future leaders; its members are known by clean clothes, shoes, hair and teeth, hatred of dirt, good manners, never insulting or fighting, loving people as siblings, soft ears for parents and elders, never lying, bravery, loving the flag — standing and saluting when it is raised, sworn to defend it — and a uniform of sky-blue shorts, white star cap and blue scarf worn as their own flag  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
