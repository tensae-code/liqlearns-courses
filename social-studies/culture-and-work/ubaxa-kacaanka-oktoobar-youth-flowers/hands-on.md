# Hands-on — Ubaxa Kacaanka Oktoobar Youth Flowers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ubaxa Kacaanka Oktoobar (UKO) (The Flowers of the October Revolution)
Work through this part with your hands: the state pioneer corps for pupils aged 7–14 — the nation's eyes and future leaders; its members are known by clean clothes, shoes, hair and teeth, hatred of dirt, good manners, never insulting or fighting, loving people as siblings, soft ears for parents and elders, never lying, bravery, loving the flag — standing and saluting when it is raised, sworn to defend it — and a uniform of sky-blue shorts, white star cap and blue scarf worn as their own flag. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
