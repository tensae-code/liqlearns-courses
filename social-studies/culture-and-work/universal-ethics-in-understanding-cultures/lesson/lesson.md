## Sub lesson 1 — Universal Ethics in Understanding Cultures

South Korea's Grade 10 Donga Integrated Society, printed p. 206; Grade 10 Jihaksa Integrated Society 1 (2022), printed p. 106, teaches: VII.05 문화 이해에서 보편 윤리가 가지는 기능은?, 문화와 다양성 3-2 문화 이해의 자세와 보편 윤리 — teaches: what universal ethics does in understanding cultures — the shared standard that keeps relativism from excusing harm; universal ethics in understanding other cultures. ## Sub lesson 2 — South Korea's way — the books' own teaching way

South Korea's own way: Donga writes every lesson as a question ("Why is civil disobedience ever needed?", "What does universal ethics do in understanding a culture?") and is the only one of the five publishers to teach civil disobedience (시민 불복종) beside the law-abiding spirit, and to split the conditions of happiness into two lessons — living environment/economic stability, then democracy's development and moral practice. the first 2022 volume teaches the integrated perspectives and happiness first, then culture: four culture lessons — the world's culture regions, culture change together with traditional culture, cultural relativism weighed against universal ethics, and the multicultural society that respects diversity — the fullest culture treatment in any Korean integrated volume read in this wave. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
