South Korea's own way (Book 9): the first 2022 volume teaches the integrated perspectives and happiness first, then culture: four culture lessons — the world's culture regions, culture change together with traditional culture, cultural relativism weighed against universal ethics, and the multicultural society that respects diversity — the fullest culture treatment in any Korean integrated volume read in this wave.
