# Turkeys Form of Government the Republic

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: turkey's form of government is the republic, proclaimed in the Grand National Assembly on 29 October, with Atatürk its first president; in a republic the people elect those who will govern, the people's joining the government brought women's right to vote and be elected, the system is guaranteed by the Constitution (Anayasa), and the president, the ministers appointed over fields such as health, education and security, and the deputies (milletvekilleri) who carry the people's needs and views to the Assembly each have their duties — as Turkey's books teach it. ## Sub lesson 2 — Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: the republic is taught by research the children run themselves — interviewing family and school staff and searching the net to answer "how is our country governed, who serves in it, and what do we citizens contribute?" — and national unity is taught through one girl's memory notebook of the year's bayrams.
