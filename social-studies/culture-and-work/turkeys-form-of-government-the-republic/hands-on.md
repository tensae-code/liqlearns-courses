# Hands-on — Turkeys Form of Government the Republic

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Turkey's form of government is the republic, proclaimed in the Grand National Assembly on 29 October, with Atatürk its first president; in a republic the people elect those who will govern, the people's joining the government brought women's right to vote and be elected, the system is guaranteed by the Constitution (Anayasa), and the president, the ministers appointed over fields such as health, education and security, and the deputies (milletvekilleri) who carry the people's needs and views to the Assembly each have their duties Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
