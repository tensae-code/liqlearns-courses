# Lesson plan — Turkeys Form of Government the Republic

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Turkeys Form of Government the Republic
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Turkeys Form of Government the Republic — Turkey's form of government is the republic, proclaimed in the Grand National Assembly on 29 October, with Atatürk its first president; in a republic the people elect those who will govern, the people's joining the government brought women's right to vote and be elected, the system is guaranteed by the Constitution (Anayasa), and the president, the ministers appointed over fields such as health, education and security, and the deputies (milletvekilleri) who carry the people's needs and views to the Assembly each have their duties — as Turkey's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
