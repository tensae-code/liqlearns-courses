# Hands-on — Indicators and Profiles of Democratic Countries

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Indicators of democratic countries; Profiles of developed and developing commonwealth countries
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Collecting, analysing and interpreting the social, political and economic indicators of democratic countries, and building the profile of one developed and one developing Commonwealth country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Papua New Guinea's way
Say how Papua New Guinea's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
