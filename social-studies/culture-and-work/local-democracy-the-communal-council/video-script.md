# Video script — Local Democracy the Communal Council

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Morocco Grade 6 Al-Massar Ijtiamiat, printed p. 149, teaches: local democracy and citizen participation from the constitution (local authorities are founded on citizen participation; councils are elected by direct suffrage; open councils let citizens follow and petition); the kinds of councils (village — communal council; town — municipal council; district); the elected council office (president and deputies, council secretary, budget rapporteur) and the woman president's own list of her duties; how the council office is elected; and the commune's budget — what citizens' local taxes pay for.

Morocco's own way: the Grade 6 civics course is a working apprenticeship in democracy — it opens with law, freedom and responsibility (Rousseau: no freedom without responsibility), runs the class as a republic (charter, elected cooperative), then walks out of the school gate: local democracy is learned by studying the elected communal council from the 2011 Constitution (Articles 135 and 139), visiting the commune headquarters, simulating a full council meeting with agenda, president, budget rapporteur and association representatives, and finally pleading a real solidarity proposal before that council.
