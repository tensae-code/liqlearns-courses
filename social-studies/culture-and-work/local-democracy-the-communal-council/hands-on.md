# Hands-on — Local Democracy the Communal Council

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Local democracy — the communal council (rural/urban)
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco Grade 6 Al-Massar Ijtiamiat, printed p. 149, teaches: local democracy and citizen participation from the constitution (local authorities are founded on citizen participation; councils are elected by direct suffrage; open councils let citizens follow and petition); the kinds of councils (village — communal council; town — municipal council; district); the elected council office (president and deputies, council secretary, budget rapporteur) and the woman president's own list of her duties; how the council office is elected; and the commune's budget — what citizens' local taxes pay for. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Morocco's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Morocco's own way: the Grade 6 civics course is a working apprenticeship in democracy — it opens with law, freedom and responsibility (Rousseau: no freedom without responsibility), runs the class as a republic (charter, elected cooperative), then walks out of the school gate: local democracy is learned by studying the elected communal council from the 2011 Constitution (Articles 135 and 139), visiting the commune headquarters, simulating a full council meeting with agenda, president, budget rapporteur and association representatives, and finally pleading a real solidarity proposal before that council. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
