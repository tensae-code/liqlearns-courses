# Administrative Law and Administrative Offences

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: administrative law as the branch governing the executive's management relations, and the administrative offence (проступок) as an unlawful, guilty act or omission carrying administrative responsibility — distinguished from crimes and disciplinary offences by its lesser public danger (fare-dodging on a bus is its everyday example), with guilt as a necessary sign — as Belarus' book teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Foundations of administrative law (Основы административного права) — Grade 11

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 78), teaches: Administrative law as the branch governing the executive's management relations, and the administrative offence (проступок) as an unlawful, guilty act or omission carrying administrative responsibility — distinguished from crimes and disciplinary offences by its lesser public danger (fare-dodging on a bus is its everyday example), with guilt as a necessary sign. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
