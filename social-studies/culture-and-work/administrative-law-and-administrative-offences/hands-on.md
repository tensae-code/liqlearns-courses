# Hands-on — Administrative Law and Administrative Offences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Administrative law as the branch governing the executive's management relations, and the administrative offence (проступок) as an unlawful, guilty act or omission carrying administrative responsibility — distinguished from crimes and disciplinary offences by its lesser public danger (fare-dodging on a bus is its everyday example), with guilt as a necessary sign. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
