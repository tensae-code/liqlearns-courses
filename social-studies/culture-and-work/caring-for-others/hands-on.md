# Hands-on — Caring for Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book’s topic
Work through this lesson’s topic with your hands: Care and compassion in pictures — helping an elder cross the road, visiting grandparents and a sick friend. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mauritius’s way
Say how Mauritius’s book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
