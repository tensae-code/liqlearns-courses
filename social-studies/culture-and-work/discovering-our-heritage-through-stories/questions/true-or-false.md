# Discovering Our Heritage Through Stories — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the heritage found inside the tales — in the story "My favourite fruit", the dates the grandmother serves are "part of our heritage and our culture", and the children ask whether the date palm truly stands among the symbols of the Emirates' identity and heritage, and what the palm, its fronds and its fruit are used for; who the narrator (الراوي) is who keeps the tales; and the children composing tales of their own to carry the heritage on
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: the youngest pupils meet their heritage as a story told by a grandmother — the date palm and its fruit become a symbol of the country's identity — and meet honesty the same way, explored through tales before it is ever stated as a rule.
**Answer: True**
