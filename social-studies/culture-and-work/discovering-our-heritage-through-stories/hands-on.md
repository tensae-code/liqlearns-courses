# Hands-on — Discovering Our Heritage Through Stories

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Discovering the Emirates' heritage through storytelling, The narrator and Writing our own stories
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the heritage found inside the tales — in the story "My favourite fruit", the dates the grandmother serves are "part of our heritage and our culture", and the children ask whether the date palm truly stands among the symbols of the Emirates' identity and heritage, and what the palm, its fronds and its fruit are used for; who the narrator (الراوي) is who keeps the tales; and the children composing tales of their own to carry the heritage on Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
