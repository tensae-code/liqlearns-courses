# Video script — Discovering Our Heritage Through Stories

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G1 Term 2 student book, printed p. 15, teaches: the heritage found inside the tales — in the story "My favourite fruit", the dates the grandmother serves are "part of our heritage and our culture", and the children ask whether the date palm truly stands among the symbols of the Emirates' identity and heritage, and what the palm, its fronds and its fruit are used for; who the narrator (الراوي) is who keeps the tales; and the children composing tales of their own to carry the heritage on

United Arab Emirates' own way: the youngest pupils meet their heritage as a story told by a grandmother — the date palm and its fruit become a symbol of the country's identity — and meet honesty the same way, explored through tales before it is ever stated as a rule.
