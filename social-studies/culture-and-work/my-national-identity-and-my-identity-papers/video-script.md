# Video script — My National Identity and My Identity Papers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G2 teacher guide, volume 2, printed p. 59, teaches: what the Emirati national identity is, made of the customs, traditions and folk heritage that form it — honouring the guest (إكرام الضيف) and the folk games (المريحانة، الغميضة، خوصة بوصة، الحبل، الصقلة), whose rules children must respect, keeping the heritage being a national duty; words of the home dialect; and the child's own identity papers in the story of Saif — the birth certificate, the family book (خلاصة القيد), the identity card and the passport, "my document for travel"

United Arab Emirates' own way: for seven-year-olds the state is a person first — the whole identity unit hangs on the founding builder: who Zayed was and what he built, then what "my identity" is made of (down to the child's own identity papers), then the child's share: my homeland is my responsibility.
