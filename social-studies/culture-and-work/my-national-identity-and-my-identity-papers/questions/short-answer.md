# My National Identity and My Identity Papers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: My national identity — what the Emirati national identity is, made of the customs, traditions and folk heritage that form it — honouring the guest (إكرام الضيف) and the folk games (المريحانة، الغميضة، خوصة بوصة، الحبل، الصقلة), whose rules children must respect, keeping the heritage being a national duty; words of the home dialect; and the child's own identity papers in the story of Saif — the birth certificate, the family book (خلاصة القيد), the identity card and the passport, "my document for travel" (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — for seven-year-olds the state is a person first — the whole identity unit hangs on the founding builder: who Zayed was and what he built, then what "my identity" is made of (down to the child's own identity papers), then the child's share: my homeland is my responsibility. (any close wording).**
