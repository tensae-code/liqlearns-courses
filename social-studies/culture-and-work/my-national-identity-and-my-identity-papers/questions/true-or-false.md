# My National Identity and My Identity Papers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: what the Emirati national identity is, made of the customs, traditions and folk heritage that form it — honouring the guest (إكرام الضيف) and the folk games (المريحانة، الغميضة، خوصة بوصة، الحبل، الصقلة), whose rules children must respect, keeping the heritage being a national duty; words of the home dialect; and the child's own identity papers in the story of Saif — the birth certificate, the family book (خلاصة القيد), the identity card and the passport, "my document for travel"
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: for seven-year-olds the state is a person first — the whole identity unit hangs on the founding builder: who Zayed was and what he built, then what "my identity" is made of (down to the child's own identity papers), then the child's share: my homeland is my responsibility.
**Answer: True**
