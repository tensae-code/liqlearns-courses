# Hands-on — My National Identity and My Identity Papers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My national identity
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what the Emirati national identity is, made of the customs, traditions and folk heritage that form it — honouring the guest (إكرام الضيف) and the folk games (المريحانة، الغميضة، خوصة بوصة، الحبل، الصقلة), whose rules children must respect, keeping the heritage being a national duty; words of the home dialect; and the child's own identity papers in the story of Saif — the birth certificate, the family book (خلاصة القيد), the identity card and the passport, "my document for travel" Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
