# Hands-on — The Sultan Head of State of Oman

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Sultan Head of State of Oman
Work through this part with your hands: The child meets the head of state by name and picture — the Sultan on his throne inside the map of Oman — and the unit's named values are love of homeland, pride, loyalty, belonging and cooperation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Oman's way
Say how Oman's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
