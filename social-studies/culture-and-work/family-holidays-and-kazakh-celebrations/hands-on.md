# Hands-on — Family Holidays and Kazakh Celebrations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: family holidays as special days a family prepares and celebrates together — Kazakh hospitality round the dastarkhan, the Tusau keser first-steps rite, Family Day in September, and the Nauryz festival with its national games and seven-ingredient dish. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kazakhstan's way
Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
