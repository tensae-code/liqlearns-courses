# Hands-on — Somalia Our Motherland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Soomaaliya Dalkayaga Hooyo (Somalia, Our Mother Country)
Work through this part with your hands: loving the motherland as a duty learned young — the country's rivers, seas, valleys, plains, mountains, farms, great cities, tall buildings, historic places, roads, gardens and wild animals are named so the children grow to love, unite around and work for their country and its flag. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Dalkeennu waa Soomaaliya (Our Country Is Somalia)
Work through this part with your hands: the homeland lesson retold for second-graders — Somalia as the one country all its children share. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
