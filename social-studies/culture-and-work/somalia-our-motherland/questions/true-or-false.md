# Somalia Our Motherland — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Soomaaliya Dalkayaga Hooyo (Somalia, Our Mother Country)" teaches: loving the motherland as a duty learned young — the country's rivers, seas, valleys, plains, mountains, farms, great cities, tall buildings, historic places, roads, gardens and wild animals are named so the children grow to love, unite around and work for their country and its flag.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Dalkeennu waa Soomaaliya (Our Country Is Somalia)" teaches: the homeland lesson retold for second-graders — Somalia as the one country all its children share.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: this subject was created by the 1969 Revolution to form the new Somali person — personality (qofnimo) is built, not born: family, food, dress, schooling and society shape it, and the school's job is to graft onto the child Somali-ness, straight morals, a living conscience, patriotism, responsibility and tolerance, in that order, from the flag outward to the society the pupil lives in..
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
