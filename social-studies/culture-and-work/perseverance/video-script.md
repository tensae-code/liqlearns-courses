# Video script — Perseverance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 41, teaches: perseverance (المثابرة) — doing a thing despite its difficulty in order to succeed — and "don't give up" (لا تستسلم); flexibility (المرونة) — the ability to set out again after passing through difficulties, mistakes being a natural part of it; the two virtues practised in Emirati daily life through the story of the Paralympic champion Mohammed Khamis Khalaf, the first Emirati to win Paralympic gold, whose friends and coach stood with him

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
