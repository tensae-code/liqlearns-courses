# Perseverance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: perseverance (المثابرة) — doing a thing despite its difficulty in order to succeed — and "don't give up" (لا تستسلم); flexibility (المرونة) — the ability to set out again after passing through difficulties, mistakes being a natural part of it; the two virtues practised in Emirati daily life through the story of the Paralympic champion Mohammed Khamis Khalaf, the first Emirati to win Paralympic gold, whose friends and coach stood with him
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
**Answer: True**
