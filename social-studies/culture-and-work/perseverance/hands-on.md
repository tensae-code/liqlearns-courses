# Hands-on — Perseverance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Perseverance and Flexibility
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: perseverance (المثابرة) — doing a thing despite its difficulty in order to succeed — and "don't give up" (لا تستسلم); flexibility (المرونة) — the ability to set out again after passing through difficulties, mistakes being a natural part of it; the two virtues practised in Emirati daily life through the story of the Paralympic champion Mohammed Khamis Khalaf, the first Emirati to win Paralympic gold, whose friends and coach stood with him Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
