**Answer: Public Decency — public decency as the values, controls and manners governing a person's conduct in society; its foundations — respecting the homeland's prevailing values, customs, traditions and culture, avoiding appearing in a public place in immodest dress or clothing carrying offensive pictures, forms, signs or phrases, avoiding writing or drawing on walls and transport unless licensed, and avoiding in public places any word or act that harms, frightens or endangers their visitors; and the Council of Ministers' Public Decency Regulation issued to protect Saudi society from conduct against public manners and Arab and Islamic values and morals (any close wording).**
