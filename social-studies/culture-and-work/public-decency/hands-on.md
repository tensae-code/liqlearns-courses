# Hands-on — Public Decency

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: public decency as the values, controls and manners governing a person's conduct in society; its foundations — respecting the homeland's prevailing values, customs, traditions and culture, avoiding appearing in a public place in immodest dress or clothing carrying offensive pictures, forms, signs or phrases, avoiding writing or drawing on walls and transport unless licensed, and avoiding in public places any word or act that harms, frightens or endangers their visitors; and the Council of Ministers' Public Decency Regulation issued to protect Saudi society from conduct against public manners and Arab and Islamic values and morals. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
