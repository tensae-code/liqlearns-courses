# Public Decency

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: public decency as the values, controls and manners governing a person's conduct in society; its foundations — respecting the homeland's prevailing values, customs, traditions and culture, avoiding appearing in a public place in immodest dress or clothing carrying offensive pictures, forms, signs or phrases, avoiding writing or drawing on walls and transport unless licensed, and avoiding in public places any word or act that harms, frightens or endangers their visitors; and the Council of Ministers' Public Decency Regulation issued to protect Saudi society from conduct against public manners and Arab and Islamic values and morals — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Public Decency

Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 288–290), teaches: Public decency as the values, controls and manners governing a person's conduct in society; its foundations — respecting the homeland's prevailing values, customs, traditions and culture, avoiding appearing in a public place in immodest dress or clothing carrying offensive pictures, forms, signs or phrases, avoiding writing or drawing on walls and transport unless licensed, and avoiding in public places any word or act that harms, frightens or endangers their visitors; and the Council of Ministers' Public Decency Regulation issued to protect Saudi society from conduct against public manners and Arab and Islamic values and morals. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
