# Video script — Respect for the Land in First Nations Learning

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Canada Social Studies 5 IRSR Guide (printed p. 12), teaches: Respect for the land is at the centre of First Nations culture and education; Shi-shi-etko gathers plants into a memory bag with her family in her last days at home.

Canada Social Studies 5 IRSR Guide (printed p. 16), teaches: First Nations people's connections to their lands are deeply rooted and remembered through landmarks and names; each people's culture is based on its own territory.

Canada's own way: The Grade 5 guide opens with the land and the family as the first teachers, before the school system is named — reconciliation at this level begins from respect for the land and learning handed down the generations.
