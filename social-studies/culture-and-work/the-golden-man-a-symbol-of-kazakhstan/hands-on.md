# Hands-on — The Golden Man a Symbol of Kazakhstan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Golden Man — the Saka prince's gold-covered dress from the Issyk kurgan — as a historical monument and a symbol of the Kazakh people's freedom and independence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kazakhstan's way
Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
