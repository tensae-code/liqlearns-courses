# Video script — The Golden Man a Symbol of Kazakhstan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kazakhstan Grade 1 Poznanie mira (919/929) (printed p. 74), teaches: the Golden Man — the Saka prince's gold-covered dress from the Issyk kurgan — as a historical monument and a symbol of the Kazakh people's freedom and independence.

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 1): the primary course opens inside the child's own circles — class, family, school rules — and only then widens to the state: independence is taught as a checklist of a real state (territory, people, president and government, constitution, symbols, capital, tenge), and Kazakh family culture (the Tusau keser first-steps rite, the dastarkhan, hospitality) carries the family theme.
