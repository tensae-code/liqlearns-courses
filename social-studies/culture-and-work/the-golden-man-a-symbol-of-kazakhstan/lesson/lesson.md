## Sub lesson 1 — The Golden Man a Symbol of Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan Grade 1 Poznanie mira (919/929) (printed p. 74), teaches: a historical monument as buildings, places and objects tied to past events; the Issyk kurgan Saka prince's gold-covered dress with snow leopard, argali, horse and bird images that protected the warrior, and the Golden Man as a symbol of the Kazakh people's freedom and independence. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the books' own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 1): the primary course opens inside the child's own circles — class, family, school rules — and only then widens to the state: independence is taught as a checklist of a real state (territory, people, president and government, constitution, symbols, capital, tenge), and Kazakh family culture (the Tusau keser first-steps rite, the dastarkhan, hospitality) carries the family theme. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
