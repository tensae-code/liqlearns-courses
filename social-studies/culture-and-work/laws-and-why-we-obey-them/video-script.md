# Video script — Laws and Why We Obey Them

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan Grade 6 SS G6 T1, printed p. 19, teaches: What laws are, how they organise daily life, and the importance of complying with them.

Jordan Grade 4 SS G4 T1, printed p. 53, teaches: Laws and regulations and the importance of order in our lives — keeping the system in the neighbourhood and shared places.

Jordan's own way: civics opens the year — the state, Jordan's constitution and the law come before any geography or history, and media is taught as a citizen's practice (expression, ethics, criticism), not as technology. society is met through its real groups — town, village and camp — and government through Jordan's own constitution, whose timeline the child traces.
