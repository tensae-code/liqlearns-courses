# Hands-on — Traditions Rules and Regulations in Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## 3.5 Let us respect our traditions, rules and regulations
Work through this part with your hands: what traditions, rules, regulations, customs and manners each are (rules as the government's conditions, every rule carrying a punishment and the law counting everyone equal), and the advantages of following them — peace, fewer conflicts, a righteous society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: civics starts inside the child's own school — its history, its people and their duties, its rules, symbols and the pupil's own rights — then widens to the region (the Grama Niladhari ladder up to the province, the officers and councils that serve it) and closes with the good citizen's virtues: polite speech, listening, discipline, telling good from bad, using resources frugally, guarding public property and preserving the local identity with pride.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
