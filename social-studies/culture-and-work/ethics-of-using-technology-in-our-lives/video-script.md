# Video script — Ethics of Using Technology in Our Lives

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Digital Citizenship & Electronic Communication Culture, G12 Term 2 activity book, answered edition, printed p. 64, teaches: shared resources (الموارد المشتركة) and the unethical behaviours around them — downloading pirated software, printing personal files, storing unrelated files — with their consequences (security breaches, congestion, more piracy); and a case study in electronic defamation: five pupils suspended for posting offensive statements about their teachers, and the defamation lawsuit that followed — respect is the basis of life

United Arab Emirates' own way: digital citizenship is a named school subject of its own, run with the Aqdar empowerment programme — access is a right the state measures (the DAI index, 100% internet spread in the Emirati market), the Emirati ID card's number is dissected as a civics lesson, and every abuse is answered with the federation's own law and its own reporting service (Abu Dhabi Police "Aman", 8002626).
