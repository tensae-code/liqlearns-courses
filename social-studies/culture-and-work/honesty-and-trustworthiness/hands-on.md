# Hands-on — Honesty and Trustworthiness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: trustworthiness as doing duties fully and rendering others their rights, the opposite of betrayal — shown in guarding religion, keeping secrets, respecting others' rights, perfecting work, keeping order and avoiding cheating, and bringing security to the citizen, the homeland and society; honesty as telling the truth unchanged — in speech and in work — whose fruits are people's trust, a responsible character and stronger relations, while their absence spreads corruption and loses security. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
