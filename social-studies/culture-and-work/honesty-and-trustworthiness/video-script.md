The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Saudi Arabia Grade 6 Social Studies Term 1 (printed pp. 152–154), teaches: Trustworthiness as doing duties fully and rendering others their rights, the opposite of betrayal — shown in guarding religion, keeping secrets, respecting others' rights, perfecting work, keeping order and avoiding cheating, and bringing security to the citizen, the homeland and society; honesty as telling the truth unchanged — in speech and in work — whose fruits are people's trust, a responsible character and stronger relations, while their absence spreads corruption and loses security.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 6 book ties citizenship to Saudi Vision 2030 — what a vision is, its realisation programmes and projects, the Quality of Life Programme and the Human Capability Development Programme — and then to citizenship itself, honesty and trustworthiness, guarding national antiquities, and taxes and VAT.
