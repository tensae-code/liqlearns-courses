## Sub lesson 1 — Honesty and Trustworthiness

Saudi Arabia Grade 6 Social Studies Term 1 (printed pp. 152–154), teaches: Trustworthiness as doing duties fully and rendering others their rights, the opposite of betrayal — shown in guarding religion, keeping secrets, respecting others' rights, perfecting work, keeping order and avoiding cheating, and bringing security to the citizen, the homeland and society; honesty as telling the truth unchanged — in speech and in work — whose fruits are people's trust, a responsible character and stronger relations, while their absence spreads corruption and loses security. ## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 6 book ties citizenship to Saudi Vision 2030 — what a vision is, its realisation programmes and projects, the Quality of Life Programme and the Human Capability Development Programme — and then to citizenship itself, honesty and trustworthiness, guarding national antiquities, and taxes and VAT.
