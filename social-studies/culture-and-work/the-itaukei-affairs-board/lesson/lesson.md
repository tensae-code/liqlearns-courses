## Sub lesson 1 — The iTaukei Affairs Board

## Sub lesson 2 — Fiji's way — the books' own teaching way

Fiji's own way (Grade 6): local government is taught as two systems side by side — elected municipal councils with mayors in the towns, and the village Bose Vakoro led by the Turaga ni Koro in iTaukei villages — with the iTaukei Affairs Board guarding land, custom and language. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
