# Video script — The Itaukei Affairs Board

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji Grade 6 Year 6 Social Science (printed p. 12), teaches: The board protects indigenous land rights, iTaukei customs, traditions, culture and language, advises on resolving conflicts among village members, and helps landowners develop their land.

Fiji's own way (Grade 6): local government is taught as two systems side by side — elected municipal councils with mayors in the towns, and the village Bose Vakoro led by the Turaga ni Koro in iTaukei villages — with the iTaukei Affairs Board guarding land, custom and language.
