# Hands-on — Being a Law Abiding Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What makes a law-abiding citizen — obeying laws and rules at home, school and in the community. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bahamas' way
Say how Bahamas' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
