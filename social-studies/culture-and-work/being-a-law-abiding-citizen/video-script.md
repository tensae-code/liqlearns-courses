# Video script — Being a Law Abiding Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bahamas Grade 3 Social Science G3 (printed pp. 16–22), teaches: What makes a law-abiding citizen — obeying laws and rules at home, school and in the community.

Bahamas' own way: citizenship is taught before nation history — the child first meets the law-abiding citizen, the community and their own rights, island by island.
