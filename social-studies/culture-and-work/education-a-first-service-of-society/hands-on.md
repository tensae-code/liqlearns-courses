# Hands-on — Education a First Service of Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sound education is a basic need of society, served by kindergartens, schools, universities and teacher-training colleges under the Ministries of Education and of Higher Education; education is the foundation of every country's growth and development. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
