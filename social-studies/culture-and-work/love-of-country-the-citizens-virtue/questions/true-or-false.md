# Love of Country the Citizens Virtue — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: a classic text — love of country as the soul of all the citizen's virtues, for which family and interests are sacrificed, and which can never sell or betray the homeland.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
