# Hearing Criticism Without Anger — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: criticism (шүүмжлэл) is a teaching word with a purpose — "do not praise, do not flatter" runs the old saying, for flattery hides faults while criticism, heard with patience, reveals them; the six ways of listening (to the end, without looking away, without doing something else, calmly, respectfully, attending to the meaning); the story of Galdan, whose father answered the boy's every complaint until the boy understood the teaching inside the harshness; and answering a friend's mocking, teasing words (шоглож хэлсэн үг) without anger — hearing the affection inside them and never letting them wound the friendship.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
