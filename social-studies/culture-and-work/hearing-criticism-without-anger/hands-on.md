# Hands-on — Hearing Criticism Without Anger

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 2.1, criticism units
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Criticism (шүүмжлэл) is a teaching word with a purpose — "do not praise, do not flatter" runs the old saying, for flattery hides faults while criticism, heard with patience, reveals them; the six ways of listening (to the end, without looking away, without doing something else, calmly, respectfully, attending to the meaning); the story of Galdan, whose father answered the boy's every complaint until the boy understood the teaching inside the harshness; and answering a friend's mocking, teasing words (шоглож хэлсэн үг) without anger — hearing the affection inside them and never letting them wound the friendship. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
