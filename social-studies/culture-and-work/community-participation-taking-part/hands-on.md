# Hands-on — Community Participation Taking Part

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Community Participation Taking Part 1
Work through this part with your hands: The forms participation takes, joining the activities that serve society, and grasping why participation builds the homeland — the unit's facts name the people's role in preparing Oman Vision 2040 and its development as the standing example. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Community Participation Taking Part 2
Work through this part with your hands: Membership as activity, not status — the child practises ways of participating in her homeland and values what participation does for its rise. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
