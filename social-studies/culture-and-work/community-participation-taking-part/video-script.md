# Video script — Community Participation Taking Part

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 6 Social Studies TG T2, printed pp. 85–93, teaches: The forms participation takes, joining the activities that serve society, and grasping why participation builds the homeland — the unit's facts name the people's role in preparing Oman Vision 2040 and its development as the standing example.

Oman Grade 4 Identity & Citizenship TG T2, printed pp. 30–33, teaches: Membership as activity, not status — the child practises ways of participating in her homeland and values what participation does for its rise.

Oman's own way (Social Studies Grade 6, Term 2): by Grade 6 the citizen is an organiser — the state teaches civil society as a named system: associations (الجمعيات الأهلية), the sablah, volunteering, national occasions and even elections listed among the unit's concepts, and the people's participation in preparing Oman Vision 2040 given as the model example.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 4, Term 2): the primary civics course ends at the ballot box — a nine-year-old in Oman learns to elect who represents her, to be an active member of her community, and that her vote is an أمانة, a trust, given four full periods; then the same word — trust — is carried to the whole planet.
