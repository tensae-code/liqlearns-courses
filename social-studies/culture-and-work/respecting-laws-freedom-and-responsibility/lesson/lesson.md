## Sub lesson 1 — I respect the laws — freedom and responsibility

Morocco Grade 6 Al-Massar Ijtiamiat, printed p. 118, teaches: freedom compared with right and duty through examples; Rousseau's "no freedom without responsibility"; the law as written rules everyone must respect, set out as rights, duties, violations and punishments; the school's internal law read as a real law (pupils must not damage the school, must respect mutual respect); the road-use law; and a Covid-19 poster read as a public rule in an emergency. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Morocco's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way

Morocco's own way: the Grade 6 civics course is a working apprenticeship in democracy — it opens with law, freedom and responsibility (Rousseau: no freedom without responsibility), runs the class as a republic (charter, elected cooperative), then walks out of the school gate: local democracy is learned by studying the elected communal council from the 2011 Constitution (Articles 135 and 139), visiting the commune headquarters, simulating a full council meeting with agenda, president, budget rapporteur and association representatives, and finally pleading a real solidarity proposal before that council. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
