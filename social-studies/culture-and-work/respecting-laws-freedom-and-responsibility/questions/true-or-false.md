# Respecting Laws Freedom and Responsibility — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part teaches: Morocco Grade 6 Al-Massar Ijtiamiat, printed p. 118, teaches: freedom compared with right and duty through examples; Rousseau's "no freedom without responsibility"; the law as written rules everyone must respect, set out as rights, duties, violations and punishments; the school's internal law read as a real law (pupils must not damage the school, must respect mutual respect); the road-use law; and a Covid-19 poster read as a public rule in an emergency.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part teaches: Morocco's own way: the Grade 6 civics course is a working apprenticeship in democracy — it opens with law, freedom and responsibility (Rousseau: no freedom without responsibility), runs the class as a republic (charter, elected cooperative), then walks out of the school gate: local democracy is learned by studying the elected communal council from the 2011 Constitution (Articles 135 and 139), visiting the commune headquarters, simulating a full council meeting with agenda, president, budget rapporteur and association representatives, and finally pleading a real solidarity proposal before that council.
**Answer: True**
