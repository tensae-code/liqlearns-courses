# Hands-on — Rights of the Amerindian People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The Amerindians were the first people of Guyana and the land belonged to them; today their land rights are disregarded by some miners and loggers, the government has made laws to protect their land and way of life, and Amerindian communities have the right to decide how the resources of their land are used. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
