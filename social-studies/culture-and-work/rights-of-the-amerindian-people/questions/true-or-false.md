# Rights of the Amerindian People — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The Amerindians were the first people of Guyana and the land belonged to them; today their land rights are disregarded by some miners and loggers, the government has made laws to protect their land and way of life, and Amerindian communities have the right to decide how the resources of their land are used.
