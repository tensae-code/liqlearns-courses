# Hands-on — Proverbs of the Five Snouts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 10 "Sayings and proverbs connected with the five snouts" (Таван хошуу малын холбогдолтой хэлц, зүйр цэцэн үг)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The people's wisdom about life is carried in proverbs drawn from the herding life — "a person is known by their tongue, an animal by its legs", "do not sit idle in summer, do not panic in a zud"; parents teach their children through proverbs, matching the saying to the moment, and the child learns to hear the deep meaning inside the comparison. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
