# Proverbs of the Five Snouts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the people's wisdom about life is carried in proverbs drawn from the herding life — "a person is known by their tongue, an animal by its legs", "do not sit idle in summer, do not panic in a zud"; parents teach their children through proverbs, matching the saying to the moment, and the child learns to hear the deep meaning inside the comparison.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
