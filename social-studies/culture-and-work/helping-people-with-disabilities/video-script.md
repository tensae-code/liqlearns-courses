# Video script — Helping People with Disabilities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Pakistan's Grade 5 Ethics5 (printed pp. 41–42), teaches: Unit 3 Special People — teaches: people with physical, hearing, sight or learning disabilities are "special people", not lesser people — twelve concrete ways to help (guide them across roads, give up your seat, include them in play, arrange treatment and schooling) and the duty to help the needy without shaming them.

Pakistan's own way: Ethics is the subject Punjab gives children who do not take Islamiat — the world's religions are taught respectfully first, and the shared moral core (respect, helping, cleanliness, punctuality) is then taught as every faith's common teaching.
