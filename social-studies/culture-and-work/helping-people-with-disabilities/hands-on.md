# Hands-on — Helping People with Disabilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 3 Special People — teaches: people with physical, hearing, sight or learning disabilities are "special people", not lesser people — twelve concrete ways to help (guide them across roads, give up your seat, include them in play, arrange treatment and schooling) and the duty to help the needy without shaming them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
