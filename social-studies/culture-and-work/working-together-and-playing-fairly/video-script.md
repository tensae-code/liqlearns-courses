# Video script — Working Together and Playing Fairly

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar Morality and Civics, Grade 1 (printed p. 47), teaches: Pu Pu's sand house is knocked down in play; the friends argue, then rebuild it together — play fairly and settle quarrels by rebuilding together.

Myanmar's own way (Book 2): every value is taught as a named story or picture-sequence about one child (or one lion cub) plus a question set — never as a rule list; the patriotism chapter teaches love of country as the eight ethnic peoples in their own dress holding hands around the Independence Monument.
