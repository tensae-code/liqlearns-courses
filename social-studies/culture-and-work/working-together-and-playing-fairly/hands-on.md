# Hands-on — Working Together and Playing Fairly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The fairness Pu Pu knows
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Pu Pu's sand house is knocked down in play; the friends argue, then rebuild it together — play fairly and settle quarrels by rebuilding together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
