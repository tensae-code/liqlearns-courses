# Hands-on — The Socialist Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hooyo hantiwadaag ah (A Socialist Mother)
Work through this part with your hands: the ideal mother of the new society told as a story — her work, her care and her part in building the country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Aabaha hantiwadaagga ah (The Socialist Father)
Work through this part with your hands: the ideal father told as a story — Raage the fisherman, a Guulwade who believes in socialism and works for his people and country, goes out in groups with men and women, and is decorated with medals of bravery — the family as the smallest unit of the new state. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
