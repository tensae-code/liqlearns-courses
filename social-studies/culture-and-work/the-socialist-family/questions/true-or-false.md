# The Socialist Family — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part "Hooyo hantiwadaag ah (A Socialist Mother)" teaches: the ideal mother of the new society told as a story — her work, her care and her part in building the country.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part "Aabaha hantiwadaagga ah (The Socialist Father)" teaches: the ideal father told as a story — Raage the fisherman, a Guulwade who believes in socialism and works for his people and country, goes out in groups with men and women, and is decorated with medals of bravery — the family as the smallest unit of the new state.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" teaches: Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag..
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide only, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Somalia's student book(s) named in lesson/sources.md, counted in acceptance.**
