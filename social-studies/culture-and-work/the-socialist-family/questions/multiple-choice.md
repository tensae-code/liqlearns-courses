# The Socialist Family — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Hooyo hantiwadaag ah (A Socialist Mother)" is mainly about —
a) the ideal mother of the new society told as a story — her work, her care and her part in building the country  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the ideal mother of the new society told as a story — her work, her care and her part in building the country**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The part of this lesson called "Aabaha hantiwadaagga ah (The Socialist Father)" is mainly about —
a) the ideal father told as a story — Raage the fisherman, a Guulwade who believes in socialism and works for his people and country, goes out in groups with men and women, and is decorated with medals of bravery — the family as the smallest unit of the new state  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the ideal father told as a story — Raage the fisherman, a Guulwade who believes in socialism and works for his people and country, goes out in groups with men and women, and is decorated with medals of bravery — the family as the smallest unit of the new state**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The part of this lesson called "Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way" is mainly about —
a) Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag.  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag.**
