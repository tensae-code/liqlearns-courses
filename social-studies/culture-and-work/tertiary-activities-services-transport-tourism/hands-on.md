# Hands-on — Tertiary activities — services, transport, tourism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Follow one good from producer to your home. Write every tertiary service that touched it on the way.

## Activity 2
Name tourism's value to a country's economy, and its main problems, for your own area — as the book's task sets it.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Name the five named tertiary services. Say what transport does, in its four named works. Match each transport kind to its typical load.
