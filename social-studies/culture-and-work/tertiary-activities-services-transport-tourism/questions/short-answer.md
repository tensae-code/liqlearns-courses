# Tertiary activities — services, transport, tourism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five named tertiary services.
**Answer:** Transport, communication, tourism, trade, and professional and non-professional office services.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name transport's four works.
**Answer:** Joins producer and consumer; widens regional product specialisation; controls costs; spreads innovations.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What loads does water transport carry?
**Answer:** People, heavy and costly goods and crude oil.
