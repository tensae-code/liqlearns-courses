# Tertiary activities — services, transport, tourism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tertiary activities are —
a) Raw materials  b) Services that serve the making and movement of goods  c) Heavy goods  d) Farm work
**Answer: b) Services that serve the making and movement of goods**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Office services are —
a) Secondary activity  b) Tertiary activity  c) Primary activity  d) Not economic activity
**Answer: b) Tertiary activity**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Transport is called —
a) The backbone of world trade  b) The enemy of trade  c) A cottage industry  d) A tax
**Answer: a) The backbone of world trade**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Air transport suits —
a) Crude oil in bulk  b) Light, valuable or perishable loads  c) Heavy cheap goods  d) Timber
**Answer: b) Light, valuable or perishable loads**
