**Acceptance count: taught in 4 countries (Ethiopia, Ukraine, Turkmenistan, Dominican Republic).**

Ukraine's own way: A "Geography: Regions and Countries" course — every region is done in the same order (position, nature/resources, population, primary/secondary/tertiary sectors) then typological country studies with rubrics (country visiting-card, capital, pride of the country, unique experience), ending with Ukraine's own place in the world; research tasks (Дослідження) embedded in chapters. That way is stocked here as the way Ukraine teaches this thing.

Honesty: pupil content comes from the Amhara student book. The Addis Ababa Grade 8 teacher guide is NOT edition-matched to this student book and is truncated (only printed pp. 1–47, Units 1–3, in hand); no guide coverage is claimed for this lesson beyond what its sources rows name.

Searchable tags: tertiary activities: services transport tourism, culture and work, social studies, ethiopia, Ukraine, Geografiya Gilberg

Turkmenistan's own way (Grade 10): A classic post-Soviet economic-and-social geography of the whole world in one book: Part I general world (political map, resources, population, world economy by sector), Part II the same template region by region and country by country, Part III global problems — with Turkmenistan's own memberships and ecological policy woven into the questions.

Dominican Republic's own way (Grade 5): Three geography units climb from the planet to the country: general geography and the American continent's location, then how climate varies across the continents — with climographs the children draw and read themselves — then the Dominican Republic's own climate, activities and population, ending with a “discovering the geography of my country” field activity.
