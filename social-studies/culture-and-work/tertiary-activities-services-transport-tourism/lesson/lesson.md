## Sub lesson 1 — Services, not goods

**Tertiary activities are services: they do not directly make goods**, but serve the **making, quality, distribution and consumption** of goods — **transport, communication, tourism, trade and professional and non-professional office services**.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transport — the backbone — and tourism

**Transport is called the backbone of world trade**: it **joins producer and consumer, widens regional product specialisation, controls costs and spreads innovations**. Its kinds: **land** (rail and vehicles), **water** (ships and boats carrying people, heavy and costly goods and crude oil) and **air** (fast, costly; for people and light, valuable or perishable loads like vegetables, fruit, diamonds and gold).

**Tourism** — from "tour", visiting countries, places and historical and spiritual sites — gives **pleasure and mental and physical satisfaction**.
