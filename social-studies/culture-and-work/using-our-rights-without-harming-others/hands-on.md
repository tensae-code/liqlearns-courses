# Hands-on — Using Our Rights Without Harming Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.2 "How do we exercise our rights?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Rights written in treaties and laws are not yet rights in life — their realisation depends on the country, its traditions, its economy and people's awareness; exercising my right begins with respecting others' — not disturbing a lesson, listening to the end, not peering at another's phone or letter without permission, not taking the class's attention from the teacher; and pupils audit their own school: the crowded classroom, the mocked thin child, the forgotten notebook on the bus. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
