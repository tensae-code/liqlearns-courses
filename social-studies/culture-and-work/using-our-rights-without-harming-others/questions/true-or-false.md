# Using Our Rights Without Harming Others — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: rights written in treaties and laws are not yet rights in life — their realisation depends on the country, its traditions, its economy and people's awareness; exercising my right begins with respecting others' — not disturbing a lesson, listening to the end, not peering at another's phone or letter without permission, not taking the class's attention from the teacher; and pupils audit their own school: the crowded classroom, the mocked thin child, the forgotten notebook on the bus.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
