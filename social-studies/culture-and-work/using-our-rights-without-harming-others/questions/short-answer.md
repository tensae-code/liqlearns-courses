# Using Our Rights Without Harming Others — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 4.2 "How do we exercise our rights?" — Rights written in treaties and laws are not yet rights in life — their realisation depends on the country, its traditions, its economy and people's awareness; exercising my right begins with respecting others' — not disturbing a lesson, listening to the end, not peering at another's phone or letter without permission, not taking the class's attention from the teacher; and pupils audit their own school: the crowded classroom, the mocked thin child, the forgotten notebook on the bus (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work (any close wording).**
