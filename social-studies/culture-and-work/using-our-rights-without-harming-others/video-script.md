# Video script — Using Our Rights Without Harming Others

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 7 Social Studies, printed p. 65, teaches: Rights written in treaties and laws are not yet rights in life — their realisation depends on the country, its traditions, its economy and people's awareness; exercising my right begins with respecting others' — not disturbing a lesson, listening to the end, not peering at another's phone or letter without permission, not taking the class's attention from the teacher; and pupils audit their own school: the crowded classroom, the mocked thin child, the forgotten notebook on the bus.

Mongolia's own way (Book 5): the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work.
