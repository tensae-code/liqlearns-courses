## Sub lesson 1 — Keeping Promises

Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies (printed p. 107), teaches: Keeping the covenant is being truthful in promises and covenants in word and in deed; the heavenly religions and the human civilisations emphasised it for thousands of years, and it spread among the sons of the Mesopotamian civilisation; and its benefits are increasing trust between individuals, strengthening cooperation, the individual's tranquillity and stability, and spreading truthfulness in society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: the components are named one by one with where each lives and what each built, equality is stated in the concept itself — all equal in rights and duties — and the values are taught as a civilisational inheritance, from keeping covenants "for thousands of years" to altruism as the road to social justice. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
