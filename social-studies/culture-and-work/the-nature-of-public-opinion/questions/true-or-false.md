# The Nature of Public Opinion — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Public opinion as the sum of attitudes formed from childhood by the agents of socialisation — family, school, peers, media — blending belief and feeling.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: What Americans actually think about their institutions and the great policy questions, and how events and framing move those views.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's third part teaches: Opinion's real power — candidates live and die by polls, presidents govern with approval and mandates, and institutions bend less clearly.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on the United States' student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
