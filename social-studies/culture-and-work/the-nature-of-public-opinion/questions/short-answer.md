# The Nature of Public Opinion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Nature of Public Opinion — Public opinion as the sum of attitudes formed from childhood by the agents of socialisation — family, school, peers, media — blending belief and feeling (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: What Does the Public Think? — What Americans actually think about their institutions and the great policy questions, and how events and framing move those views (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The Effects of Public Opinion — Opinion's real power — candidates live and die by polls, presidents govern with approval and mandates, and institutions bend less clearly (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the fourth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United States' way — the book's own teaching way — United States' own way (Grade 13): the United States teaches its own government as one complete system in one book — the Constitution is not a topic but the frame: every institution (Congress, presidency, courts, states, counties, cities, the civil service) is derived from it in turn, and rights are taught as the courts have interpreted them, case by case. (any close wording).**
