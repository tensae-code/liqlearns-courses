# Video script — The Nature of Public Opinion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

United States Grade 13 American Government 3e (printed p. 186), teaches: Public opinion as the sum of attitudes formed from childhood by the agents of socialisation — family, school, peers, media — blending belief and feeling.

---

United States Grade 13 American Government 3e (printed p. 202), teaches: What Americans actually think about their institutions and the great policy questions, and how events and framing move those views.

---

United States Grade 13 American Government 3e (printed p. 211), teaches: Opinion's real power — candidates live and die by polls, presidents govern with approval and mandates, and institutions bend less clearly.

---

United States' own way (Grade 13): the United States teaches its own government as one complete system in one book — the Constitution is not a topic but the frame: every institution (Congress, presidency, courts, states, counties, cities, the civil service) is derived from it in turn, and rights are taught as the courts have interpreted them, case by case.
