## Sub lesson 1 — The Nature of Public Opinion

United States Grade 13 American Government 3e (printed p. 186), teaches: Public opinion as the sum of attitudes formed from childhood by the agents of socialisation — family, school, peers, media — blending belief and feeling. ## Sub lesson 3 — The Effects of Public Opinion

United States Grade 13 American Government 3e (printed p. 211), teaches: Opinion's real power — candidates live and die by polls, presidents govern with approval and mandates, and institutions bend less clearly. ## Sub lesson 4 — United States' way — the book's own teaching way

United States' own way (Grade 13): the United States teaches its own government as one complete system in one book — the Constitution is not a topic but the frame: every institution (Congress, presidency, courts, states, counties, cities, the civil service) is derived from it in turn, and rights are taught as the courts have interpreted them, case by case. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
