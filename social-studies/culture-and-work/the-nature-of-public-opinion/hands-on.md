# Hands-on — The Nature of Public Opinion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The Nature of Public Opinion
Work through this part with your hands: public opinion as the sum of attitudes formed from childhood by the agents of socialisation — family, school, peers, media — blending belief and feeling. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## What Does the Public Think?
Work through this part with your hands: what Americans actually think about their institutions and the great policy questions, and how events and framing move those views. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The Effects of Public Opinion
Work through this part with your hands: opinion's real power — candidates live and die by polls, presidents govern with approval and mandates, and institutions bend less clearly. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how the United States' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
