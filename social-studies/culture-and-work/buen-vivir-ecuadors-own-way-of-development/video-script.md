# Video script — Buen Vivir Ecuadors Own Way of Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ecuador Grade 8 Estudios Sociales 8vo EGB, printed p. 133, teaches: Buen Vivir as Ecuador's integral answer to the problems of education, health, housing, transport, employment and recreation (criterion CE.CS.4.8), worked through the strengths and weaknesses of the rights to education and health in Ecuador and the country's social demands — equity, sustainability, productivity, empowerment.

Ecuador's own way: at Superior level the civics strand is built on Ecuador's own 2008 constitutional order — Buen Vivir (Sumak Kawsay) as the state's development idea, a plurinational and intercultural state, nature itself as a subject of rights, and children's rights taught from Ecuador's own Código de la Niñez y Adolescencia.
