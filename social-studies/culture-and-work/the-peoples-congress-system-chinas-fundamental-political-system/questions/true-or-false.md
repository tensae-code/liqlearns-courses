# The Peoples Congress System Chinas Fundamental Political System — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: The people's congresses as the organs through which the people exercise state power — the National People's Congress as the highest organ with legislative, decision, appointment-and-removal and supervision powers, its Standing Committee, and deputies' duties to the people who elect them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: The people's congress system as China's form of government (政体) under democratic centralism — all state organs created by, responsible to and supervised by the congresses — and its advantages in guaranteeing the people's mastery, safeguarding unity and concentrating effort.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on China's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
