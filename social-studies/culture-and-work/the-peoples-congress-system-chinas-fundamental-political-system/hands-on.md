# Hands-on — The Peoples Congress System Chinas Fundamental Political System

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The people's congresses as the organs through which the people exercise state power — the National People's Congress as the highest organ with legislative, decision, appointment-and-removal and supervision powers, its Standing Committee, and deputies' duties to the people who elect them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The people's congress system as China's form of government (政体) under democratic centralism — all state organs created by, responsible to and supervised by the congresses — and its advantages in guaranteeing the people's mastery, safeguarding unity and concentrating effort. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
