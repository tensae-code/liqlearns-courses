# Video script — Communication and Assertiveness Skills

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zimbabwe Grade 7 zw-g7 PlusOne Social Science Grade 7 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 62), teaches: Communication and assertive skills as social-etiquette tools for community and work life.
Zimbabwe Grade 3 zw-g3 PlusOne Social Science Grade 3 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 95), teaches: Communication is the exchange of information, verbal (spoken words) and non-verbal (body language, gestures, facial expressions); proper communication means listening actively with full attention and eye contact, not interrupting, taking turns to speak in a calm tone, and using kind words that express gratitude and never hurt feelings.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 9) (teacher guide — evidence): the primary cycle closes with adult law taught straight — inheritance under the Deceased Estates Succession Act with the Master of the High Court role-played in class, and gender equity taught through Zimbabwe's own institutions (Zimbabwe Gender Commission, Msasa Project, Women's Bank, community savings groups) with Constitution excerpts in the materials list.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 5) (teacher guide — evidence): rights teaching is concrete and local — support services are named institutions a child can find (BEAM school-fees support, community care workers, police, health workers), government services are named ministries and ZUPCO buses, and the needy are helped by named Zimbabwean homes and trusts (Harare Children's Home, Capernaum Trust, Angel of Hope Foundation).
