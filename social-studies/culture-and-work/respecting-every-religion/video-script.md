The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Pakistan's Grade 5 Ethics5 (printed pp. 38–39), teaches: Unit 3 Respect for Religions — teaches: never mock another faith or its worship, never damage its holy places, join hands across faiths on shared occasions, and let every person follow their own religion in peace.

Pakistan's own way: Ethics is the subject Punjab gives children who do not take Islamiat — the world's religions are taught respectfully first, and the shared moral core (respect, helping, cleanliness, punctuality) is then taught as every faith's common teaching.
