## Sub lesson 1 — Protecting Our Living Environment

Cuba Grade 3 El mundo en que vivimos (printed pp. 1–5), teaches: The pioneers' nature-protection brigades do socially useful work — planting, watering and caring for plants, feeding and protecting animals, cleaning the classroom, the school, the community and its surroundings, collecting recyclable material, and saving water and energy. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Rules of Order Hygiene and Care

Cuba Grade 3 El mundo en que vivimos (printed pp. 238–239), teaches: The rules of order, hygiene and care we must keep to protect the place where we live — care for flora and fauna, no writing on walls, rubbish only in bins, keep beaches, rivers and lakes unpolluted, no felling trees or lighting fires in woods. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Cuba's way — the book's own teaching way

Cuba's own way: third-graders join Nature Protection Brigades — organised pioneer groups that clean and care for the classroom, school and community — and the book's closing civics chapter binds courtesy, the patriotic symbols and the traffic law into one code of “comportamiento social”. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
