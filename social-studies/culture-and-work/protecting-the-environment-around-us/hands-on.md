# Hands-on — Protecting the Environment Around Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the pioneers' nature-protection brigades do socially useful work — planting, watering and caring for plants, feeding and protecting animals, cleaning the classroom, the school, the community and its surroundings, collecting recyclable material, and saving water and energy. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the rules of order, hygiene and care we must keep to protect the place where we live — care for flora and fauna, no writing on walls, rubbish only in bins, keep beaches, rivers and lakes unpolluted, no felling trees or lighting fires in woods. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
