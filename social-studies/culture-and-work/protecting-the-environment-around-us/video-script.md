Cuba Grade 3 El mundo en que vivimos (printed pp. 1–5), teaches: The pioneers' nature-protection brigades do socially useful work — planting, watering and caring for plants, feeding and protecting animals, cleaning the classroom, the school, the community and its surroundings, collecting recyclable material, and saving water and energy.

Cuba Grade 3 El mundo en que vivimos (printed pp. 238–239), teaches: The rules of order, hygiene and care we must keep to protect the place where we live — care for flora and fauna, no writing on walls, rubbish only in bins, keep beaches, rivers and lakes unpolluted, no felling trees or lighting fires in woods.

Cuba's own way: third-graders join Nature Protection Brigades — organised pioneer groups that clean and care for the classroom, school and community — and the book's closing civics chapter binds courtesy, the patriotic symbols and the traffic law into one code of “comportamiento social”.
