# Hands-on — Social Justice a National Principle of Eritrea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: social justice as a national principle of the State of Eritrea — the government bringing schools, water, clinics and electricity to remote villages, in villagers' own before-and-after voices. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
