# Hands-on — Community Leaders Must Agree

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Cheo and Flor must unite — two communal leaders who both want community organisation and participation but dispute the leadership, calling rival assemblies by loudspeaker until a neighbour demands they agree: community organisation fails when its leaders will not unite. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Venezuela's way
Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
