## Sub lesson 1 — Community Leaders Must Agree

Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 50, teaches: Cheo and Flor must unite — two communal leaders who both want community organisation and participation but dispute the leadership, calling rival assemblies by loudspeaker until a neighbour demands they agree: community organisation fails when its leaders will not unite. ## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: coexistence is the spine of the primary book — respect, rules, family and community are each defined in plain words, then practised: the class votes in assembly, community leaders must learn to agree, and solidarity is taught from Lincoln's collection for an enslaved sailor to Caracas's free children's heart hospital serving the whole continent.
