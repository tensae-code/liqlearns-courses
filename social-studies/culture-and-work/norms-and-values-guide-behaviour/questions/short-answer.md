**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Unit 29: Norms and values — Zimbabwe Grade 6 zw-g6 PlusOne Social Science Grade 6 Teacher's Guide (printed p. 64), teaches: Norms are unwritten rules shared by a group that guide acceptable behaviour — prescriptive (what to do) and proscriptive (what not to do); values are beliefs that help decide right from wrong, such as loyalty, honesty and equality; norms promote social order in schools, families and communities, and behaviour that aligns with them is conformity.
