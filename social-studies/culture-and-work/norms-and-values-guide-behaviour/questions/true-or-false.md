# Norms and Values Guide Behaviour — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Norms are unwritten rules shared by a group that guide acceptable behaviour — prescriptive (what to do) and proscriptive (what not to do); values are beliefs that help decide right from wrong, such as loyalty, honesty and equality; norms promote social order in schools, families and communities, and behaviour that aligns with them is conformity.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Zimbabwe's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
