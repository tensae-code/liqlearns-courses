# Hands-on — Norms and Values Guide Behaviour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: norms are unwritten rules shared by a group that guide acceptable behaviour — prescriptive (what to do) and proscriptive (what not to do); values are beliefs that help decide right from wrong, such as loyalty, honesty and equality; norms promote social order in schools, families and communities, and behaviour that aligns with them is conformity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
