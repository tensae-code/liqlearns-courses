## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 29: Norms and values

Zimbabwe, *PlusOne Social Science Grade 6 Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 64), teaches: Norms are unwritten rules shared by a group that guide acceptable behaviour — prescriptive (what to do) and proscriptive (what not to do); values are beliefs that help decide right from wrong, such as loyalty, honesty and equality; norms promote social order in schools, families and communities, and behaviour that aligns with them is conformity. ## Sub lesson 2 — Zimbabwe's way (Book 8) — the book's own teaching way

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 8) (teacher guide — evidence): the three arms of government are taught through school comparisons (executive = school head, legislature = student council, judiciary = disciplinary committee) and the full bill-to-Act path; protection law is explicit — the age of consent is 18, sex with a minor is statutory rape, and child pledging is named as a harmful past practice; community support to families still runs through nhimbe/ilima communal work parties.
