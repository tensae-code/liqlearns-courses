# Hands-on — Rwandan Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rwandan Values
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Gukunda igihugu (patriotism), kwihesha agaciro (self-reliance — valuing your own worth, working hard, not depending on others) and ubumwe (unity — one people, 'Ndi Umunyarwanda', the country's interests before personal ones). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Rwanda's way
Say how Rwanda's book teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
