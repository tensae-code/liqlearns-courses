Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Rwandan Values  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Rwandan Values**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) gukunda igihugu (patriotism), kwihesha agaciro (self-reliance — valuing your own worth, working hard, not depending on others) and ubumwe (unity — one people, 'Ndi Umunyarwanda', the country's interests before personal ones)  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) gukunda igihugu (patriotism), kwihesha agaciro (self-reliance — valuing your own worth, working hard, not depending on others) and ubumwe (unity — one people, 'Ndi Umunyarwanda', the country's interests before personal ones)**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
