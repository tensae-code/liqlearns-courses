Oman Grade 3 Identity & Citizenship TG T1, printed pp. 60–66, teaches: The class helps draft a written charter for the family — rights and duties agreed, written down, and pledged to — the constitution idea learned by making one.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 3, Term 1): rights and duties are taught at family scale before homeland scale — and the family unit ends with the children drafting a ميثاق, a charter, for their own family and pledging to keep it: constitution-making at the kitchen table.
