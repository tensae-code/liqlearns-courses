# Hands-on — Social Change

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Social change as the transformation of culture and institutions over time — its four characteristics (everywhere but uneven, planned or unplanned, often controversial, big or small) and its causes (invention, discovery, diffusion, conflict, new ideas, demographic change, social movements). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
