# Hands-on — Traditional Taboos That Protect Sea Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sea turtle taboos and the Niue fono — teaches: turtles were protected by taboo — eaten only at special ceremonies and only by chiefs and priests; the turtle symbolised prestige; and a ban (fono) on reef activities from January to March while the migrating kalooma, atule and aheu passed. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tonga's way
Say how Tonga's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
