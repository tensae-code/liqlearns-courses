Tonga's Years 5–6 People and the Sea (printed pp. 8, 15), teaches: Sea turtle taboos and the Niue fono — teaches: turtles were protected by taboo — eaten only at special ceremonies and only by chiefs and priests; the turtle symbolised prestige; and a ban (fono) on reef activities from January to March while the migrating kalooma, atule and aheu passed.

Tonga's own way: the sea itself is the community — identity, kinship and duty are taught through the ocean, its sacred fishing rituals and its traditional values.
