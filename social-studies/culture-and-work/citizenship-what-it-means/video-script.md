# Video script — Citizenship What It Means

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 8 EC3 8e Citoyenneté (printed p. 7), teaches: the citizen and citizenship as notions with a history, built collectively and tied to nationality, from the Haitian Constitution's Preamble (inalienable rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, the 1804 Act of Independence, the 1948 Universal Declaration) and Rousseau's social contract in annex.

Haiti's own way: Citizenship is closed by the money bond: after defining the citizen and practising engagement, pupils end on contribution — the civic duty to pay taxes (Constitution article 52) and the state's duty to redistribute in national solidarity.
