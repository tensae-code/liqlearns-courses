# Hands-on — Citizenship What It Means

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the citizen and citizenship as notions with a history, built collectively and tied to nationality, from the Haitian Constitution's Preamble (inalienable rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, the 1804 Act of Independence, the 1948 Universal Declaration) and Rousseau's social contract in annex. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Haiti's way
Say how Haiti's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
