## Sub lesson 1 — Citizenship What It Means

Haiti Grade 8 EC3 8e Citoyenneté (printed p. 7), teaches: the citizen and citizenship as notions with a history, built collectively and tied to nationality, from the Haitian Constitution's Preamble (inalienable rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, the 1804 Act of Independence, the 1948 Universal Declaration) and Rousseau's social contract in annex. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way

Haiti's own way: Citizenship is closed by the money bond: after defining the citizen and practising engagement, pupils end on contribution — the civic duty to pay taxes (Constitution article 52) and the state's duty to redistribute in national solidarity. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
