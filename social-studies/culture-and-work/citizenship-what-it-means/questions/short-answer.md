# Citizenship What It Means — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Citizenship What It Means — the citizen and citizenship as notions with a history, built collectively and tied to nationality, from the Haitian Constitution's Preamble (inalienable rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness, the 1804 Act of Independence, the 1948 Universal Declaration) and Rousseau's social contract in annex (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way — Citizenship is closed by the money bond: after defining the citizen and practising engagement, pupils end on contribution — the civic duty to pay taxes (Constitution article 52) and the state's duty to redistribute in national solidarity. (any close wording).**
