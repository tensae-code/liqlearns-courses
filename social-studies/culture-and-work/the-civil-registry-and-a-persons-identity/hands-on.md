# Hands-on — The Civil Registry and a Persons Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 3 "Registro de personas"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed p. 143, teaches: why a country counts births, deaths, marriages and divorces — to organise health and education — and Guatemala's Registro Nacional de las Personas (RENAP), where registering a newborn gives the child a national identity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
