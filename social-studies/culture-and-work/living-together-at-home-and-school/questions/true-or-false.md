# Living Together at Home and School — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: living in harmony at home, school, church and with neighbours; good coexistence is teamwork where each does their best; the values named are honesty, punctuality, responsibility, cooperation and respect.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: behaviour depends on the coexistence norms learned in the family — society's base and first school of values (respect, honesty, responsibility, justice) — and practised at school (companionship, cooperation); courtesy phrases (good morning, please, thank you, excuse me) build an environment of peace.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
