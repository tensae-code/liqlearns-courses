# Living Together at Home and School — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 3 Convivencia familiar y escolar — Living in harmony at home, school, church and with neighbours; good coexistence is teamwork where each does their best; the values named are honesty, punctuality, responsibility, cooperation and respect (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 3 Convivencia social — Behaviour depends on the coexistence norms learned in the family — society's base and first school of values (respect, honesty, responsibility, justice) — and practised at school (companionship, cooperation); courtesy phrases (good morning, please, thank you, excuse me) build an environment of peace (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — civics begins inside the child's own circles — feelings, family norms and tasks, classroom rules and school authorities, a rehearsal student-government election with a paper ballot, and only then the community's elected authorities (alcaldesa, regidores, patronato, junta de agua). Grade 3 is where the state appears in full — nationality by birth and registration, the Constitution as supreme law, the three powers with their real Honduran numbers (128 deputies, 15 magistrates, elections every four years), and citizens' social demands as claims the State is obliged to hear. (any close wording).**
