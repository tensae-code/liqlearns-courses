# Hands-on — Living Together at Home and School

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 3 Convivencia familiar y escolar
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Living in harmony at home, school, church and with neighbours; good coexistence is teamwork where each does their best; the values named are honesty, punctuality, responsibility, cooperation and respect. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 3 Convivencia social
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Behaviour depends on the coexistence norms learned in the family — society's base and first school of values (respect, honesty, responsibility, justice) — and practised at school (companionship, cooperation); courtesy phrases (good morning, please, thank you, excuse me) build an environment of peace. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
