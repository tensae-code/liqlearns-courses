# Video script — Living Together at Home and School

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras Grade 1 G1 Student CCSS, printed p. 18, teaches: Living in harmony at home, school, church and with neighbours; good coexistence is teamwork where each does their best; the values named are honesty, punctuality, responsibility, cooperation and respect.

Honduras Grade 3 G3 Student CCSS, printed p. 18, teaches: Behaviour depends on the coexistence norms learned in the family — society's base and first school of values (respect, honesty, responsibility, justice) — and practised at school (companionship, cooperation); courtesy phrases (good morning, please, thank you, excuse me) build an environment of peace.

Honduras's own way (Book 1): civics begins inside the child's own circles — feelings, family norms and tasks, classroom rules and school authorities, a rehearsal student-government election with a paper ballot, and only then the community's elected authorities (alcaldesa, regidores, patronato, junta de agua).

Honduras's own way (Book 3): Grade 3 is where the state appears in full — nationality by birth and registration, the Constitution as supreme law, the three powers with their real Honduran numbers (128 deputies, 15 magistrates, elections every four years), and citizens' social demands as claims the State is obliged to hear.
