# Lesson plan — Banks and What They Do for You

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Banks and What They Do for You
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Geography expansion (Mongolia, 2026-11-14).
