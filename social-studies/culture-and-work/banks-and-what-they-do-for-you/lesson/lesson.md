# Banks and What They Do for You

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: how banks help your life — what banks do for the people who use them, as Mongolia's Grade 9 *Нийгэм судлал IX (Social Studies IX)* teaches it in Chapter 3 Individual and Economy: 3.5 How do banks help your life? on printed p. 55. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — How banks help your life

Mongolia's Grade 9 book closes its economy chapter by asking how banks help your life. The topic introduces the bank as a helper in ordinary life — a safe place for money and a bridge between people who have money to spare and people who need to borrow — taught from the customer's side, as the chapter title's question promises.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the book's own teaching way

Mongolia learns this inside its own book's way: Social Studies is a five-chapter spiral (Individual & Society, Individual & Culture, Individual & Economy, Human Rights & Law, Public Administration) continued from Grade 8; there is no physical-geography chapter — Mongolia's geography is a separate subject (Газарзүй) — so this book's Geography-wave content is its culture and economics teaching, grounded in Mongolian surveys, wage tables and heritage inventories. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
