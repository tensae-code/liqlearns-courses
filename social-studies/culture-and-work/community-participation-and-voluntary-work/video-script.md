# Video script — Community Participation and Voluntary Work

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jordan Grade 4 SS G4 T2, printed p. 42, teaches: Taking part in society — the fields of community participation and the voluntary work a child can do.

Jordan Grade 5 SS G5 T1, printed p. 15, teaches: Serving one's society — why it matters for its development, and the school committees through which pupils serve.

Jordan's own way: living together is taught as skills of the self first — personality, dialogue, respect — and only then as taking part in society, where voluntary work is the child's first public act. Grade 5 civics is Jordan's own state, part by part — the King and his constitutional powers, then each of the three authorities in its own lesson — after a first unit on how a person belongs to his society: communicating, serving it, and standing with it in solidarity.
