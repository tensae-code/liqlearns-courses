# Hands-on — Doing My Own Work and Helping Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Do things ourselves too
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Doing your own small work and helping at home and at school instead of waiting to be served (May May's story and the kindness poem). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Doing things by one's own effort
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Packing your own bag, washing your own dishes, helping serve the meal — work done by your own hands. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
