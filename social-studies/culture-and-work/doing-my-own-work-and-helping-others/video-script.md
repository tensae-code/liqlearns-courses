# Video script — Doing My Own Work and Helping Others

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar Morality and Civics, Grade 2 (printed p. 5), teaches: Doing your own small work and helping at home and at school instead of waiting to be served (May May's story and the kindness poem).

Myanmar Morality and Civics, Grade 4 (printed p. 44), teaches: Packing your own bag, washing your own dishes, helping serve the meal — work done by your own hands.

Myanmar's own way (Book 5): Grade 2 widens Grade 1's values from home to school — the same story-plus-pictures shape now carries school property, school rules, honest judgement of classroom incidents, friends with disabilities, and the whole country drawn as its ethnic children around the map.

Myanmar's own way (Book 9): Grade 4 names children's RIGHTS as a picture inventory (food, water, a house, clothes, care, medicine, play, learning, family) and turns environmental care into two full units — preserving the earth and the natural surroundings, with drought, flood, felled forests and factory smoke as the evidence.
