**Answer: Tema 3 §3.5 Eu ajudo a construir a democracia no meu país — I help build democracy in my country — dialogue is its special tool: listening to the other to understand them is a virtue (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Angola's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 6 makes the pupils legislators of their own classroom — the class writes its own regulation (with a no-violence chapter) as the lived rehearsal for democracy — and teaches duties concretely, down to naming the taxes (IVA, income, property) and calling the non-payer a tax evader; tolerance is defined as the price of choosing democracy. (any close wording).**
