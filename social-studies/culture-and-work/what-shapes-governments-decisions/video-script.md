Turkey Grade 6 Sosyal Bilgiler 2. Kitap, printed p. 12, teaches: besides voting, citizens join decision-making through political parties, civil-society organisations and the media; the shared view people form together is kamuoyu (public opinion), and it weighs on the administration's decisions — worked on a real neighbourhood problem, asking which channel would solve it best

Turkey's own way: the Grade 6 democracy area asks who actually moves a government — the people's needs, parties, civil society, media and the kamuoyu (public opinion) they form together — and then follows citizenship itself online, where rights are now exercised through a screen and the active citizen is the one who still writes the petition.
