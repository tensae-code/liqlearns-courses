# Video script — Every Right Brings a Duty

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 7 EC2 7AF (printed p. 8), teaches: rights, duties and their guarantees read in the texts, every right balanced by a duty toward oneself and others, and the class drafting a charter of rights and duties for its own school.

Haiti's own way: The democratic state is taught as resting on its citizens: rights are learned by exercising them at school (debate, a school charter of rights and duties), and Haitian texts (1987 amended Constitution, the 1801/1805 constitutions, laws on paternity and on violence against women) are read beside the international declarations.
