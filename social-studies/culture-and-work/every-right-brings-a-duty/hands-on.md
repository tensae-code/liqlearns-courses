# Hands-on — Every Right Brings a Duty

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: rights, duties and their guarantees read in the texts, every right balanced by a duty toward oneself and others, and the class drafting a charter of rights and duties for its own school. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Haiti's way
Say how Haiti's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
