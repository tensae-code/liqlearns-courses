**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Serbia). Haiti is named here as teacher-guide evidence only, counted nowhere.**

Haiti's own way: The democratic state is taught as resting on its citizens: rights are learned by exercising them at school (debate, a school charter of rights and duties), and Haitian texts (1987 amended Constitution, the 1801/1805 constitutions, laws on paternity and on violence against women) are read beside the international declarations.

Serbia's own way (Grade 1 Свет око нас): the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast.
