## Sub lesson 1 — Every Right Brings a Duty

Haiti Grade 7 EC2 7AF (printed p. 8), teaches: rights, duties and their guarantees read in the texts, every right balanced by a duty toward oneself and others, and the class drafting a charter of rights and duties for its own school. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way

Haiti's own way: The democratic state is taught as resting on its citizens: rights are learned by exercising them at school (debate, a school charter of rights and duties), and Haitian texts (1987 amended Constitution, the 1801/1805 constitutions, laws on paternity and on violence against women) are read beside the international declarations. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
