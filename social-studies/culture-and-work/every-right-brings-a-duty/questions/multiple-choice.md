# Every Right Brings a Duty — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Every Right Brings a Duty  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Every Right Brings a Duty**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) rights, duties and their guarantees read in the texts, every right balanced by a duty toward oneself and others, and the class drafting a charter of rights and duties for its own school  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
