# Every Right Brings a Duty — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Every Right Brings a Duty — rights, duties and their guarantees read in the texts, every right balanced by a duty toward oneself and others, and the class drafting a charter of rights and duties for its own school (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Haiti's way — the book's own teaching way — The democratic state is taught as resting on its citizens: rights are learned by exercising them at school (debate, a school charter of rights and duties), and Haitian texts (1987 amended Constitution, the 1801/1805 constitutions, laws on paternity and on violence against women) are read beside the international declarations. (any close wording).**
