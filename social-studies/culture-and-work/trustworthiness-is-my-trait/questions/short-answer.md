# Trustworthiness is My Trait — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Trustworthiness is My Trait — trustworthiness (al-amana) is among the important traits of the human being and the trait of the believer: Muhammad finds a new reading book on his classroom windowsill, prettier than his own old book, yet he does not think of keeping it, because he is trustworthy — he asks his classmates, then searches the schoolyard through the breaks until he finds Sara crying by the next classroom's door for her lost book, and returns it to her, and she thanks him for his trustworthiness; I am trustworthy in all circumstances, I return things to their owners, and I do not take others' things without permission (any close wording).**
