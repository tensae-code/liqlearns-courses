# Hands-on — Equal Protection for Other Groups

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Equal Protection for Other Groups
Work through this part with your hands: Latinos, Asian Americans and other groups — immigration bars, then the Civil Rights Act of 1964 turned to their own cases, from Chicano youth movements to their own lawsuits. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United States' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how the United States' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
