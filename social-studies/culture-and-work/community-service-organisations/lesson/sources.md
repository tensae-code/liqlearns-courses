**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Honduras).**

Honduras's own way (Book 3): Grade 3 is where the state appears in full — nationality by birth and registration, the Constitution as supreme law, the three powers with their real Honduran numbers (128 deputies, 15 magistrates, elections every four years), and citizens' social demands as claims the State is obliged to hear.

Honduras's own way (Book 4): Grade 4 widens civics to Central America and to rights as law — human rights with the Constitution's own Article 60 and the habeas corpus and amparo remedies, sex versus gender taught for equality, and Honduras's real civic organisations (CODEH, COFADEH, the unions) named as civil society.
