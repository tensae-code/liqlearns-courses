# Hands-on — Community Service Organisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 30 Honduras y sus formas de organización
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Community service organisations — water boards, communal patronatos, cooperatives — and national ones — the Fire Corps, the Red Cross, health centres and hospitals, schools — each with its own functions (social, rescue, health, education) that together improve the community's social, economic, political and cultural situation and strengthen democracy and participation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Lección 31 Organizaciones sociales de Honduras
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The family is the first organisation we belong to — by the Universal Declaration the natural and fundamental unit of society, protected by society and the State; Honduras's social organisations defend human rights (COFADEH, CODEH, CIPRODEH), health (children's-cancer and hospital foundations), labour rights (unions such as STIBYS and STENEE) and peasant causes, doing voluntary work that links civil society with the State in solving the country's social problems. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
