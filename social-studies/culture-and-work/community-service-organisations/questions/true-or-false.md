# Community Service Organisations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: community service organisations — water boards, communal patronatos, cooperatives — and national ones — the Fire Corps, the Red Cross, health centres and hospitals, schools — each with its own functions (social, rescue, health, education) that together improve the community's social, economic, political and cultural situation and strengthen democracy and participation.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: the family is the first organisation we belong to — by the Universal Declaration the natural and fundamental unit of society, protected by society and the State; Honduras's social organisations defend human rights (COFADEH, CODEH, CIPRODEH), health (children's-cancer and hospital foundations), labour rights (unions such as STIBYS and STENEE) and peasant causes, doing voluntary work that links civil society with the State in solving the country's social problems.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
