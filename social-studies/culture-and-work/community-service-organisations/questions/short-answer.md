# Community Service Organisations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 30 Honduras y sus formas de organización — Community service organisations — water boards, communal patronatos, cooperatives — and national ones — the Fire Corps, the Red Cross, health centres and hospitals, schools — each with its own functions (social, rescue, health, education) that together improve the community's social, economic, political and cultural situation and strengthen democracy and participation (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 31 Organizaciones sociales de Honduras — The family is the first organisation we belong to — by the Universal Declaration the natural and fundamental unit of society, protected by society and the State; Honduras's social organisations defend human rights (COFADEH, CODEH, CIPRODEH), health (children's-cancer and hospital foundations), labour rights (unions such as STIBYS and STENEE) and peasant causes, doing voluntary work that links civil society with the State in solving the country's social problems (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 3 is where the state appears in full — nationality by birth and registration, the Constitution as supreme law, the three powers with their real Honduran numbers (128 deputies, 15 magistrates, elections every four years), and citizens' social demands as claims the State is obliged to hear. Grade 4 widens civics to Central America and to rights as law — human rights with the Constitution's own Article 60 and the habeas corpus and amparo remedies, sex versus gender taught for equality, and Honduras's real civic organisations (CODEH, COFADEH, the unions) named as civil society. (any close wording).**
