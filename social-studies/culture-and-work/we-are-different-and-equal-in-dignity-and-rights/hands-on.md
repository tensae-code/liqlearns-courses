# Hands-on — We Are Different And Equal In Dignity And Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## As nossas diferenças e semelhanças
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: each person is unique and different, yet all are equal in dignity and in rights, as Angola's Constitution art. 23 guarantees — no one may be harmed or privileged for ancestry, sex, race, language, religion or condition. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Angola's way
Say how Angola's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
