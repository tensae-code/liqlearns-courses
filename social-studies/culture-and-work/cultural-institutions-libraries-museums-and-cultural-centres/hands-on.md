# Hands-on — Cultural Institutions Libraries Museums and Cultural Centres

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the institutions that carry a culture — libraries, museums and cultural centres and the media, which enlighten citizens and guard their heritage; the Library of Alexandria old and new and Souk Okaz as cases, and the Ministry of Culture's work in Syria (book fairs, festivals, Damascus Arab Capital of Culture). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
