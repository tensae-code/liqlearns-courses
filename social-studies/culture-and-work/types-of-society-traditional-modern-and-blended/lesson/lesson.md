## Sub lesson 1 — Society and types of society

Fiji Grade 9 Social Science Year 9 (printed p. 7), teaches: Society is people in a community and their interactions; traditional societies (small, related, slow-changing, skills passed down), modern societies, and societies blending both. ## Sub lesson 2 — Fiji's way — the books' own teaching way

Fiji's own way (Grade 9): society is analysed through Fiji's own structures — the iTaukei hierarchy of Turaga, Sau Turaga, Matanivanua, Bete, Bati, Mataisau, Gonedau and Tauvanua with its ascribed chiefly status — and conflict is settled the iTaukei way, by Bulubulu.
