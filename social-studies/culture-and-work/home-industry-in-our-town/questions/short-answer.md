# Home Industry in Our Town — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Home industry of the town — How Nyaungdon's banana-chip industry works, from the owner's own account — collecting, peeling, scraping, frying, packing — its 35 workers, their daily wages, its difficulties and their solutions (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 3 studies one real town (Nyaungdon, on the Pan Hlaing river) and one real village (Zee Hpyu Kyun) from big picture maps, then interviews a real home-industry owner — the town's banana-chip maker, with her 35 workers, their wages and their problems-and-solutions poster. (any close wording).**
