# Hands-on — Home Industry in Our Town

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Home industry of the town
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: How Nyaungdon's banana-chip industry works, from the owner's own account — collecting, peeling, scraping, frying, packing — its 35 workers, their daily wages, its difficulties and their solutions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
