# Home Industry in Our Town

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: how Nyaungdon's banana-chip industry works, from the owner's own account — collecting, peeling, scraping, frying, packing — its 35 workers, their daily wages, its difficulties and their solutions — as Myanmar's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Home industry of the town

Myanmar Social Studies Grade 3 (printed p. 10), teaches: How Nyaungdon's banana-chip industry works, from the owner's own account — collecting, peeling, scraping, frying, packing — its 35 workers, their daily wages, its difficulties and their solutions. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way

Myanmar's own way: Grade 3 studies one real town (Nyaungdon, on the Pan Hlaing river) and one real village (Zee Hpyu Kyun) from big picture maps, then interviews a real home-industry owner — the town's banana-chip maker, with her 35 workers, their wages and their problems-and-solutions poster. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
