# Hands-on — Bolivian Identities Diversity and Plurality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bolivian identity as plurality — the CPE Preamble's "composición plural" people, the 36 recognised nations with their rights, knowledges and languages; how identity is built in city, countryside and community (belonging, recognition, and today's media and social networks); culture as the source of identity that preserves a people's memory and values; and natural versus juridical subjects of rights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
