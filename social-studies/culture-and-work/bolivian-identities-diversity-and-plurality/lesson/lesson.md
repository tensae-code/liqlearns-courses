## Sub lesson 1 — Las Identidades Bolivianas

Bolivia Grade 12 BO Social Sciences Sec 6 (printed p. 446), teaches: Bolivian identity as plurality — the CPE Preamble's "composición plural" people, the 36 recognised nations with their rights, knowledges and languages; how identity is built in city, countryside and community (belonging, recognition, and today's media and social networks); culture as the source of identity that preserves a people's memory and values; and natural versus juridical subjects of rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way

Bolivia's own way: the final year turns from history to the citizen's own legal armour — identity in a plural nation, a supreme decree ordering the state itself to despatriarcalise, a Youth Law, an anti-trafficking law taught with self-protection advice for teenagers, and the labour rights of working children under Bolivia's own Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
