# Bolivian Identities Diversity and Plurality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Las Identidades Bolivianas — Bolivian identity as plurality — the CPE Preamble's "composición plural" people, the 36 recognised nations with their rights, knowledges and languages; how identity is built in city, countryside and community (belonging, recognition, and today's media and social networks); culture as the source of identity that preserves a people's memory and values; and natural versus juridical subjects of rights (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way — the final year turns from history to the citizen's own legal armour — identity in a plural nation, a supreme decree ordering the state itself to despatriarcalise, a Youth Law, an anti-trafficking law taught with self-protection advice for teenagers, and the labour rights of working children under Bolivia's own Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente (any close wording).**
