# Bolivian Identities Diversity and Plurality — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Bolivian identity as plurality — the CPE Preamble's "composición plural" people, the 36 recognised nations with their rights, knowledges and languages; how identity is built in city, countryside and community (belonging, recognition, and today's media and social networks); culture as the source of identity that preserves a people's memory and values; and natural versus juridical subjects of rights.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Bolivia's Grade 12 student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
