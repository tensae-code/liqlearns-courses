# Hands-on — A Multicultural Society That Respects Diversity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: VII.주제5 문화의 다양성을 존중하는 다문화 사회, VII.04 다문화 사회와 문화 다양성 존중, VII.4 다문화 사회와 문화 다양성, VII.4 다문화 사회와 문화적 다양성의 존중, VII.06 행복한 다문화 사회를 만들기 위한 방안은?, 문화와 다양성 4 다문화 사회와 문화적 다양성 존중 — teaches: the multicultural society that respects cultural diversity — conflicts in a multicultural society and how they are resolved; the multicultural society and respecting cultural diversity within it; understanding the multicultural society, its conflicts, and cultural diversity; the multicultural society and respect for cultural diversity; how to build a happy multicultural society; the multicultural society and respecting cultural diversity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
