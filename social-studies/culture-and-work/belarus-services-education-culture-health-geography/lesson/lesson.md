## Sub lesson 1 — The non-productive sphere — social-everyday services

Theme VIII §53 of Belarus's Grade 10 book studies the non-productive sphere: the social-everyday services that serve people's daily life rather than making goods.

## Sub lesson 2 — Geography of education, culture and health care

Theme VIII §54 follows with the geography of education, culture and health care: where Belarus's schools, cultural institutions and health care are, and how they are spread over the country.

Belarus's own way: the whole course is one country studied whole — position and the history of its own geographical research first (including Belarusian place-names), then nature component by component, then its five natural provinces, population, the economy sector by sector, Belarus in the world, and finally each of its six regions in turn; every paragraph opens with a "recall" rubric and closes with questions and tasks.
