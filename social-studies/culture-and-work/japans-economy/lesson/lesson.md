## Sub lesson 1 — Japans Economy

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11* (printed pp. 121–128), teaches: Bài 24: Japan's economy — its stages (the 1955–72 miracle at about 10% a year, the 1991 bubble, stagnation; GDP 5,040 billion USD in 2020), intensive small-scale farming and fisheries, industry (shipbuilding, cars at 10% of world output, electronics and 45% of the world's robots; the Tokyo–Nagoya–Osaka–Fukuoka–Kitakyushu centres), services (the Shinkansen; tourism 46.1 billion USD) and the four island economic regions. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Địa lí 11): after Grade 10's world systems, Grade 11 walks the world region by region — Latin America, the EU, Southeast Asia and ASEAN, Southwest Asia, the USA, Russia, Japan, China, Australia and South Africa — always with the same lens (position, nature, people, economy) and always measuring Vietnam's own place among them (its HDI row in the opening table, EU–Vietnam trade, Vietnam inside ASEAN, Japan's FDI into Vietnam). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
