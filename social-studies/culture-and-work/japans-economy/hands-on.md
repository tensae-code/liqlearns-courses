# Hands-on — Japans Economy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 24: Japan's economy — its stages (the 1955–72 miracle at about 10% a year, the 1991 bubble, stagnation; GDP 5,040 billion USD in 2020), intensive small-scale farming and fisheries, industry (shipbuilding, cars at 10% of world output, electronics and 45% of the world's robots; the Tokyo–Nagoya–Osaka–Fukuoka–Kitakyushu centres), services (the Shinkansen; tourism 46.1 billion USD) and the four island economic regions. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
