# Hands-on — Living Together Without Discrimination

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Living Together Without Discrimination
Work through this part with your hands: how discrimination and racism damage living together — social barriers, exclusion and marginalisation, economic inequality — against the human-rights ground that every person is valuable and must be treated equally and justly. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
