# Living Together Without Discrimination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Living Together Without Discrimination — how discrimination and racism damage living together — social barriers, exclusion and marginalisation, economic inequality — against the human-rights ground that every person is valuable and must be treated equally and justly (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way — Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution. (any close wording).**
