# Hands-on — Active Methods for Teaching Social Studies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the method is chosen for the topic and the audience — role play and storytelling suit history topics and social-skills teaching; cooperative learning, in which the group works to a shared goal from which the whole group and each member benefit, in steps (the teacher sets the topic; the group studies together, or builds a project, model, map, poster or wall newspaper); storytelling made active — the teacher asks inside the tale ("if you went to Shiraz, what souvenirs would you buy, and why? what would you put in your bag? do you prefer the train or the car?") so teacher and pupil are both active; game-based teaching in four stages, the third — the play itself, with the teacher as guide — being the most practical; the scientific visit (field trip) as a method in its own right; and teaching in virtual space. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
