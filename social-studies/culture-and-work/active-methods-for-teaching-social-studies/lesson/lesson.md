# Active Methods for Teaching Social Studies

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the method is chosen for the topic and the audience — role play and storytelling suit history topics and social-skills teaching; cooperative learning, in which the group works to a shared goal from which the whole group and each member benefit, in steps (the teacher sets the topic; the group studies together, or builds a project, model, map, poster or wall newspaper); storytelling made active — the teacher asks inside the tale ("if you went to Shiraz, what souvenirs would you buy, and why? what would you put in your bag? do you prefer the train or the car?") so teacher and pupil are both active; game-based teaching in four stages, the third — the play itself, with the teacher as guide — being the most practical; the scientific visit (field trip) as a method in its own right; and teaching in virtual space — as Iran's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the guide states the whole philosophy of the special-needs course — social studies exists to make independent living possible; what is learned must be generalised to real life and the real environment, and the family is a teaching partner, not an audience.
