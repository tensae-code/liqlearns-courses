# Lesson plan — Active Methods for Teaching Social Studies

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Active Methods for Teaching Social Studies
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Iran, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the method is chosen for the topic and the audience — role play and storytelling suit history topics and social-skills teaching; cooperative learning, in which the group works to a shared goal from which the whole group and each member benefit, in steps (the teacher sets the topic; the group studies together, or builds a project, model, map, poster or wall newspaper); storytelling made active — the teacher asks inside the tale ("if you went to Shiraz, what souvenirs would you buy, and why? what would you put in your bag? do you prefer the train or the car?") so teacher and pupil are both active; game-based teaching in four stages, the third — the play itself, with the teacher as guide — being the most practical; the scientific visit (field trip) as a method in its own right; and teaching in virtual space — as Iran's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
