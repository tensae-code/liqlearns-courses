# Active Methods for Teaching Social Studies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Active Methods for Teaching Social Studies — the method is chosen for the topic and the audience — role play and storytelling suit history topics and social-skills teaching; cooperative learning, in which the group works to a shared goal from which the whole group and each member benefit, in steps (the teacher sets the topic; the group studies together, or builds a project, model, map, poster or wall newspaper); storytelling made active — the teacher asks inside the tale ("if you went to Shiraz, what souvenirs would you buy, and why? what would you put in your bag? do you prefer the train or the car?") so teacher and pupil are both active; game-based teaching in four stages, the third — the play itself, with the teacher as guide — being the most practical; the scientific visit (field trip) as a method in its own right; and teaching in virtual space (any close wording).**
