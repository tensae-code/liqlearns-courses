# Hands-on — Security and Unity Under One State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: in the old Arabia of warring tribes, thieves and plague, a shepherd, a farmer and a merchant each despair of his trade; the children plead with them and the merchant prays for a man to protect the land — Imam Muhammad bin Saud founds the state, security spreads until the Hajj roads are safe, trade flourishes, and the people gain one identity and one ruler. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
