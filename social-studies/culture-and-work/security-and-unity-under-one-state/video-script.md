Saudi Arabia Grade 4 Social Studies (printed pp. 29–33), teaches: In the old Arabia of warring tribes, thieves and plague, a shepherd, a farmer and a merchant each despair of his trade; the children plead with them and the merchant prays for a man to protect the land — Imam Muhammad bin Saud founds the state, security spreads until the Hajj roads are safe, trade flourishes, and the people gain one identity and one ruler.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 4 book opens with citizenship — national identity, the system of government, national symbols and the founding story of security and unity under one state — and returns in Unit 6 to responsible citizenship through family and society, rights and duties, the good citizen, teamwork, and heritage stories that teach patience and hard work.
