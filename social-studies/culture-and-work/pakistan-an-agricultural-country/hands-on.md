# Hands-on — Pakistan An Agricultural Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Pakistan, an agricultural country: the canal system of dams, barrages and canals (83% of cultivated area irrigated), rainfed farming districts, Karez, tube wells and springs; agriculture's weight in the economy (37.4% of labour, about 22.7% of GDP, foreign exchange). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
