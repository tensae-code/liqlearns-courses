# Secondary activities — the Awramba case study — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Awramba is in —
a) Fogera woreda, South Gondar  b) Bahir Dar city  c) Gondar city  d) Arba Minch
**Answer: a) Fogera woreda, South Gondar**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Awramba was founded by —
a) A cooperative bank  b) Ato Zumra Nuru  c) A foreign mission  d) The regional bureau
**Answer: b) Ato Zumra Nuru**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In Awramba, everyone works to their ability and —
a) Keeps all they earn  b) Shares equally  c) Pays a tithe  d) Works alone
**Answer: b) Shares equally**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The community's economic position was secured through —
a) Mining  b) Its weaving industry  c) Fishing  d) Tourism
**Answer: b) Its weaving industry**
