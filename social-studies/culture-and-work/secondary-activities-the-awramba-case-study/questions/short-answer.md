# Secondary activities — the Awramba case study — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give Awramba's size as the book states it.
**Answer:** About 104 households and 388 family members.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Through what body does the community run itself?
**Answer:** Its farmers', craft and multi-purpose cooperative.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How did weaving grow in Awramba?
**Answer:** Zumra taught his skill to his followers; training followed and the weaving industry grew.
