The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then. This lesson's placements name a full script need, so the script below is full.

Awramba. A small community with a big rule.

Fogera woreda, South Gondar. Eight kilometres past Woreta, on the Debre Tabor road. About one hundred and four households. Three hundred and eighty-eight family members. Founded in 1972 E.C. by Ato Zumra Nuru.

Gender equality — in fact, not only in words. Work is the creed. Everyone works to their ability. Everyone shares equally. The able support the weak, so change includes all.

And the craft? Weaving. Zumra taught his weaving skill to his followers. Training followed. The looms grew. And the community's economic position was secured.

One craft, taught and shared, carried a whole community.
