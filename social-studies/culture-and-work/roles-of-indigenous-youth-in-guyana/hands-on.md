# Hands-on — Roles of Indigenous Youth in Guyana

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Indigenous youths apprentice with elders (hunting, fishing, farming, weaving, pottery, traditional medicine), preserve culture, language and oral tradition, practise environmental stewardship, are groomed for community leadership, assist the community and act as cultural ambassadors to visitors. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
