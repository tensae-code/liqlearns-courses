# Video script — Roles of Indigenous Youth in Guyana

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guyana Grade 6 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 14–16), teaches: Indigenous youths apprentice with elders (hunting, fishing, farming, weaving, pottery, traditional medicine), preserve culture, language and oral tradition, practise environmental stewardship, are groomed for community leadership, assist the community and act as cultural ambassadors to visitors.

Guyana's own way: the newest ministry series teaches citizenship from the child's own roles outward — family duties, community roles, youth responsibilities at home and across the Caribbean, then power, privilege and human rights in plain language, with Guyanese and Caribbean case studies (Annette Arjoon-Martins, Mia Mottley) and a value component woven into every unit.
