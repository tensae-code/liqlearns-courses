## Sub lesson 1 — Roles and responsibilities of Indigenous youths (hinterland)

Guyana Grade 6 Guyanese in the World (printed pp. 14–16), teaches: Indigenous youths apprentice with elders (hunting, fishing, farming, weaving, pottery, traditional medicine), preserve culture, language and oral tradition, practise environmental stewardship, are groomed for community leadership, assist the community and act as cultural ambassadors to visitors. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: the newest ministry series teaches citizenship from the child's own roles outward — family duties, community roles, youth responsibilities at home and across the Caribbean, then power, privilege and human rights in plain language, with Guyanese and Caribbean case studies (Annette Arjoon-Martins, Mia Mottley) and a value component woven into every unit. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
