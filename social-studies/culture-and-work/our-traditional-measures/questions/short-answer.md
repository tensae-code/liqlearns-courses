# Our Traditional Measures — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 18 "Traditional numbers and measures" (Уламжлалт тоо, хэмжигдэхүүн) — The old number names run past a thousand — түм (10,000), бум (100,000), сая (a million), живаа (ten million) — a 67-digit reckoning; measures are taken from the body (тохой the cubit 40 cm, дэлэм 1 m, алд the arm-span 1.6 m, төө 20 cm, сөөм 18 cm) and from vessels and handfuls; weights on the balance (дэнс): цэн 3.73 g, лан 37.3 g, жин 596.8 g, пүү 16.3 kg; and distances by the pasture each animal needs and by a day's and a night's travel — the Chinggis Stone records Esüngge's shot at 335 алд (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other (any close wording).**
