# Our Traditional Measures — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the old number names run past a thousand — түм (10,000), бум (100,000), сая (a million), живаа (ten million) — a 67-digit reckoning; measures are taken from the body (тохой the cubit 40 cm, дэлэм 1 m, алд the arm-span 1.6 m, төө 20 cm, сөөм 18 cm) and from vessels and handfuls; weights on the balance (дэнс): цэн 3.73 g, лан 37.3 g, жин 596.8 g, пүү 16.3 kg; and distances by the pasture each animal needs and by a day's and a night's travel — the Chinggis Stone records Esüngge's shot at 335 алд.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
