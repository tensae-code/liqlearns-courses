# Hands-on — Our Traditional Measures

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 18 "Traditional numbers and measures" (Уламжлалт тоо, хэмжигдэхүүн)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The old number names run past a thousand — түм (10,000), бум (100,000), сая (a million), живаа (ten million) — a 67-digit reckoning; measures are taken from the body (тохой the cubit 40 cm, дэлэм 1 m, алд the arm-span 1.6 m, төө 20 cm, сөөм 18 cm) and from vessels and handfuls; weights on the balance (дэнс): цэн 3.73 g, лан 37.3 g, жин 596.8 g, пүү 16.3 kg; and distances by the pasture each animal needs and by a day's and a night's travel — the Chinggis Stone records Esüngge's shot at 335 алд. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
