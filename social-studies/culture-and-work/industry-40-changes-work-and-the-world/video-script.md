Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11* (printed pp. 48–52), teaches: Topic 11.3 (part): Industry 4.0's effects on the world economy and society — the shift to a knowledge economy, smart production, personalised products, the sharing and circular economy models, changing skills (the worker-competency table), shrinking routine jobs, widening inequality, and new jobs appearing.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 11 Chuyên đề 11): the Grade 11 electives take three deep dives that all circle home — sharing the Mekong and the East Sea with neighbours, the world's tourism and the jobs it offers Vietnamese school-leavers, and the Fourth Industrial Revolution remaking work.
