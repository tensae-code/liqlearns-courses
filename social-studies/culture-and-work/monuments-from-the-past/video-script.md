The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Greece Grade 2 Meleti Perivallontos B' Dimotikou (printed p. 124), teaches: Monuments as the community's kept memory.

Greece's own way: Grade 2 widens Grade 1's circles — the neighbourhood is now studied service by service (its church named as a landmark, its green discovered, its past compared with today), and the child's first rights appear as their own unit: the right to life and the right to peace.
