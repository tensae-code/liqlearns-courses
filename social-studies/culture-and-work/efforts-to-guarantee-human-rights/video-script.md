South Korea's Grade 10 Cheonjae Integrated Society, printed p. 116, teaches: IV.02 인권 보장을 위한 다양한 노력 — teaches: the many efforts to guarantee human rights — constitutions, courts, commissions and civic movements.

South Korea's own way: Cheonjae's Integrated Society teaches the same nine-unit national frame as MiraeN's but ends the book with the Constitution itself and a standard labour contract (표준 근로 계약서) as appendices — pupils finish the course holding the two documents.
