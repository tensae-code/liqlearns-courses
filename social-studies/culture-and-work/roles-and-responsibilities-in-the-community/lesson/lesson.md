Papua New Guinea Grade 4 Social Science TG, printed p. 29, teaches: What roles and responsibilities are, and the roles and responsibilities of parents, children, friends, leaders, churches and other community groups, NGOs and businesses. ## Sub lesson 2 — Roles and Responsibilities of Groups and Organizations; Community Projects

Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG, printed p. 60, teaches: What groups and organizations are responsible for, and how community projects are carried out. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Papua New Guinea's way — the books' own teaching way

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 4): the community strand climbs a ladder of responsibility — the work people do, then how to behave toward others, then the roles and responsibilities of everyone from parents and friends to the PNG Defence Force, the police and government departments. Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 6): in senior primary the community strand becomes civics proper — the groups and organisations of PNG, how they relate and speak for people, and how development changes the community — while the culture strand runs from rituals to taking part in cultural events. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
