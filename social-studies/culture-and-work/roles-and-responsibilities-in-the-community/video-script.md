# Video script — Roles and Responsibilities in the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Papua New Guinea Grade 4 Social Science TG (printed p. 29), teaches: What roles and responsibilities are, and the roles and responsibilities of parents, children, friends, leaders, churches and other community groups, NGOs and businesses.

Papua New Guinea Grade 6 Social Science TG (printed p. 60), teaches: What groups and organizations are responsible for, and how community projects are carried out.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 4): the community strand climbs a ladder of responsibility — the work people do, then how to behave toward others, then the roles and responsibilities of everyone from parents and friends to the PNG Defence Force, the police and government departments.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 6): in senior primary the community strand becomes civics proper — the groups and organisations of PNG, how they relate and speak for people, and how development changes the community — while the culture strand runs from rituals to taking part in cultural events.
