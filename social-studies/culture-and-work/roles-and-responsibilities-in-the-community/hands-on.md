# Hands-on — Roles and Responsibilities in the Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Roles and Responsibilities
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What roles and responsibilities are, and the roles and responsibilities of parents, children, friends, leaders, churches and other community groups, NGOs and businesses. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Roles and Responsibilities of Groups and Organizations; Community Projects
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What groups and organizations are responsible for, and how community projects are carried out. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Papua New Guinea's way
Say how Papua New Guinea's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
