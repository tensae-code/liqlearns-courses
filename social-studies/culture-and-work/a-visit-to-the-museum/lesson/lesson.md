# A Visit to the Museum

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: what a museum is — a place where important things are shown for people to see; what history and traditions mean (traditions: customs and beliefs handed from parents to children); the class's own artefacts examined together, with Dubai Museum's fort in the pictures; why museum pieces are old, fragile and precious and must be protected (يجب حمايتها); and how museums lend pieces to each other and receive pieces from digs — as United Arab Emirates's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — A visit to the museum

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 15, teaches: what a museum is — a place where important things are shown for people to see; what history and traditions mean (traditions: customs and beliefs handed from parents to children); the class's own artefacts examined together, with Dubai Museum's fort in the pictures; why museum pieces are old, fragile and precious and must be protected (يجب حمايتها); and how museums lend pieces to each other and receive pieces from digs This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
