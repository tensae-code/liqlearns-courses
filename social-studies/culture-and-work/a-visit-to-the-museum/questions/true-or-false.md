# A Visit to the Museum — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: what a museum is — a place where important things are shown for people to see; what history and traditions mean (traditions: customs and beliefs handed from parents to children); the class's own artefacts examined together, with Dubai Museum's fort in the pictures; why museum pieces are old, fragile and precious and must be protected (يجب حمايتها); and how museums lend pieces to each other and receive pieces from digs
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
**Answer: True**
