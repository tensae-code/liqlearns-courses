# A Visit to the Museum — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: A visit to the museum — what a museum is — a place where important things are shown for people to see; what history and traditions mean (traditions: customs and beliefs handed from parents to children); the class's own artefacts examined together, with Dubai Museum's fort in the pictures; why museum pieces are old, fragile and precious and must be protected (يجب حمايتها); and how museums lend pieces to each other and receive pieces from digs (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece. (any close wording).**
