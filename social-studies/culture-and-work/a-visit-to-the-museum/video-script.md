# Video script — A Visit to the Museum

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G2 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 15, teaches: what a museum is — a place where important things are shown for people to see; what history and traditions mean (traditions: customs and beliefs handed from parents to children); the class's own artefacts examined together, with Dubai Museum's fort in the pictures; why museum pieces are old, fragile and precious and must be protected (يجب حمايتها); and how museums lend pieces to each other and receive pieces from digs

United Arab Emirates' own way: in this programme a seven-year-old meets the national heritage with his hands — sorting his own family's old things into material and non-material heritage, walking a museum, digging like an archaeologist — and meets himself the same way: hard work, honest feedback, not giving up, and pride in the finished piece.
