# Hands-on — A Visit to the Museum

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A visit to the museum
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what a museum is — a place where important things are shown for people to see; what history and traditions mean (traditions: customs and beliefs handed from parents to children); the class's own artefacts examined together, with Dubai Museum's fort in the pictures; why museum pieces are old, fragile and precious and must be protected (يجب حمايتها); and how museums lend pieces to each other and receive pieces from digs Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
