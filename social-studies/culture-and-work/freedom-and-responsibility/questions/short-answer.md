# Freedom and Responsibility — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: My Freedom Is Responsible — freedom is each person's right to take the decisions that suit them by their own will, without compulsion — so long as it agrees with the law, harms neither values nor others; freedom is a responsible act, and absolute freedom without responsibility is chaos (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Libya's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 6 defines the citizen by a chain of relations — rights paired with duties, freedom paired with responsibility, then one lesson per circle the child belongs to (family, school, neighbourhood) — and grounds the rights lesson in named world instruments: "human rights" as the UN defines them, the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child, and UNICEF. (any close wording).**
