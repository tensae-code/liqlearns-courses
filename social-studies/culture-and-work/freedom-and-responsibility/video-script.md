# Video script — Freedom and Responsibility

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Libya Grade 6 National Education G6 (printed p. 18), teaches: freedom is each person's right to take the decisions that suit them by their own will, without compulsion — so long as it agrees with the law, harms neither values nor others; freedom is a responsible act, and absolute freedom without responsibility is chaos.

Libya's own way: Grade 6 defines the citizen by a chain of relations — rights paired with duties, freedom paired with responsibility, then one lesson per circle the child belongs to (family, school, neighbourhood) — and grounds the rights lesson in named world instruments: "human rights" as the UN defines them, the 1989 Convention on the Rights of the Child, and UNICEF.
