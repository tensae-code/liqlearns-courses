# Video script — Respecting Common Property

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Burkina Faso CP1 ECM Fiches (printed p. 66), teaches: Respecting common property that belongs to everyone.

Burkina Faso's own way (Éducation Civique et Morale CP1 — Fiches d'aide à la préparation): civic and moral education is a standalone primary subject from CP1, taught through short prepared lesson sheets built on a story situation, group exchange and a spoken resolution the class takes together; road safety, the flag ceremony and care of the schoolyard and latrines are taught as a young citizen's first duties, each fiche ending in a resolution the pupils speak aloud.

Burkina Faso's own way (Éducation Civique et Morale CP2 — Fiches d'aide à la préparation): CP2 widens CP1's duties — children now elect their class leaders, run a school cooperative, and take up the fight against excision and plastic waste as citizens.
