# Respecting Common Property

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: respecting common property — the things that belong to everyone — as Burkina Faso's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Le respect des biens communs

Burkina Faso CP1 ECM Fiches (printed p. 66), teaches: Respecting common property that belongs to everyone. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Burkina Faso's way — the book's own teaching way

Burkina Faso's own way: civic and moral education is a standalone primary subject from CP1, taught through short prepared lesson sheets built on a story situation, group exchange and a spoken resolution the class takes together; road safety, the flag ceremony and care of the schoolyard and latrines are taught as a young citizen's first duties, each fiche ending in a resolution the pupils speak aloud. CP2 widens CP1's duties — children now elect their class leaders, run a school cooperative, and take up the fight against excision and plastic waste as citizens. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
