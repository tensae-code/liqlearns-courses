# Hands-on — Why Rights And Equality Matter

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 4.3 "Why are rights and equality important?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: 7.2 billion people differ in colour, faith, language, culture and custom, and each country's development differs too — yet rights are equal; people differ naturally (age, sex, body — төрөлхийн ялгаа) and by acquisition (education, work, wealth — төрөлхийн бус ялгаа), and difference itself is no wrong — the wrong is treating someone worse for it: discrimination (ялгаварлан гадуурхалт) is any distinction on grounds of ethnicity, colour, age, sex, language, religion, opinion, origin or property that denies equal rights; special measures that lift a disadvantaged group to equality — like the support that carried a boy with a disability through school to success — are not discrimination. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
