# Why Rights And Equality Matter — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: 7.2 billion people differ in colour, faith, language, culture and custom, and each country's development differs too — yet rights are equal; people differ naturally (age, sex, body — төрөлхийн ялгаа) and by acquisition (education, work, wealth — төрөлхийн бус ялгаа), and difference itself is no wrong — the wrong is treating someone worse for it: discrimination (ялгаварлан гадуурхалт) is any distinction on grounds of ethnicity, colour, age, sex, language, religion, opinion, origin or property that denies equal rights; special measures that lift a disadvantaged group to equality — like the support that carried a boy with a disability through school to success — are not discrimination.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
