The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Mongolia Grade 8 Social Studies, printed p. 70, teaches: 7.2 billion people differ in colour, faith, language, culture and custom, and each country's development differs too — yet rights are equal; people differ naturally (age, sex, body — төрөлхийн ялгаа) and by acquisition (education, work, wealth — төрөлхийн бус ялгаа), and difference itself is no wrong — the wrong is treating someone worse for it: discrimination (ялгаварлан гадуурхалт) is any distinction on grounds of ethnicity, colour, age, sex, language, religion, opinion, origin or property that denies equal rights; special measures that lift a disadvantaged group to equality — like the support that carried a boy with a disability through school to success — are not discrimination.

Mongolia's own way (Book 6): the local year turns rights and government into neighbourhood questions — the human-rights chapter ends with the soum administration's duties and Ulaanbaatar's air, and the governance chapter is one long worked example: a playground, a Khural decision, and the children who answer it.
