# Hands-on — Community Leaders Village Head Headman Chief and Councillor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Zimbabwe's local leadership ladder — village head solves minor disputes, ensures welfare and supervises rituals; headman assists and reports to the chief; chief oversees the whole community, conducts rain-asking ceremonies, keeps law and order and upholds moral standards; councillor represents the community in local government and promotes development — learned by interviewing real local leaders. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
