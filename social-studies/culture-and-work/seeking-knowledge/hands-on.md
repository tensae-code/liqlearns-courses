# Hands-on — Seeking Knowledge

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Knowledge is a necessity that cannot be dispensed with in any society, and it has great importance in the different aspects of man's life, and knowledge has many benefits: through it man overcame many of the difficulties that may face his life — by knowledge man invented electricity, cars, trains, aeroplanes and the computer, and through knowledge man reached space, discovered many of nature's bounties, and eliminated many diseases; so all nations and societies sought to care for knowledge, for it is the basis of building every society and an important means of man's welfare in any society, and through knowledge the nations and peoples ascend the ladder of success and excellence; and through knowledge and learning we can know our rights and our duties, and know also others' rights and duties, and overcome most of the problems that face us in our daily life. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
