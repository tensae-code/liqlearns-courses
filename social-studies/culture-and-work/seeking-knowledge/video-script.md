# Video script — Seeking Knowledge

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies (printed p. 146), teaches: Knowledge is a necessity that cannot be dispensed with in any society, and it has great importance in the different aspects of man's life, and knowledge has many benefits: through it man overcame many of the difficulties that may face his life — by knowledge man invented electricity, cars, trains, aeroplanes and the computer, and through knowledge man reached space, discovered many of nature's bounties, and eliminated many diseases; so all nations and societies sought to care for knowledge, for it is the basis of building every society and an important means of man's welfare in any society, and through knowledge the nations and peoples ascend the ladder of success and excellence; and through knowledge and learning we can know our rights and our duties, and know also others' rights and duties, and overcome most of the problems that face us in our daily life.

Iraq's own way: values are defined first as a system — standards that steer behaviour and spread peace "regardless of sex, religion, nationality, language or race" — and citizenship is cashed out in named rights (education, work, candidacy, the vote, freedom of opinion and belief), while the internet chapter warns in plain terms that the open network "is governed by no restrictions or conditions".
