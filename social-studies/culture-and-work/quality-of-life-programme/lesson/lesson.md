# Quality of Life Programme

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the Vision 2030 programme dedicated to improving the life of the individual and the family and building a society with a balanced, high-quality lifestyle — building arts and culture centres, stadiums, museums, entertainment cities, cinemas and theatres, and improving Saudi cities so three of them join the world's best 100 cities — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Quality of Life Programme

Saudi Arabia Grade 6 Social Studies Term 1 (printed pp. 139–141), teaches: The Vision 2030 programme dedicated to improving the life of the individual and the family and building a society with a balanced, high-quality lifestyle — building arts and culture centres, stadiums, museums, entertainment cities, cinemas and theatres, and improving Saudi cities so three of them join the world's best 100 cities. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 6 book ties citizenship to Saudi Vision 2030 — what a vision is, its realisation programmes and projects, the Quality of Life Programme and the Human Capability Development Programme — and then to citizenship itself, honesty and trustworthiness, guarding national antiquities, and taxes and VAT. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
