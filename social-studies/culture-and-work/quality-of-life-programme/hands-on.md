# Hands-on — Quality of Life Programme

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Vision 2030 programme dedicated to improving the life of the individual and the family and building a society with a balanced, high-quality lifestyle — building arts and culture centres, stadiums, museums, entertainment cities, cinemas and theatres, and improving Saudi cities so three of them join the world's best 100 cities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
