# Hands-on — National Identity in a Plurinational State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: national identity in a plurinational state — one shared Bolivian identity and, inside it, the identity of each of the 36 recognised nations (Aymara, Quechua, Guaraní, Mojeño, Afro-Bolivian…); the values democracy needs — liberty, equality, participation — against electoral absenteeism; and intercultural democracy, where no culture's form of democracy is imposed on another. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
