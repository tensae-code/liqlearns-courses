# Lesson plan — Culture — the two theories of human origin

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Culture — the two theories of human origin
**Place in the curriculum:** Title 9 — Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md / TITLES.md ladder: culture, economic activities, trade, and tax).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the two accepted but unfinished theories of where humankind came from — creationist and evolutionary — held even-handedly
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title, at Grade 8 depth in the course's level ladder (levels, not grades).

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Culture — what culture is (in this title). Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell the creationist theory in its own terms — created in present form, resting on faith.
2. Tell the evolutionary theory in its own terms — gradual change, research-based.
3. Say what both theories agree on.

## What you need
Paper and a pencil. Nothing else.
