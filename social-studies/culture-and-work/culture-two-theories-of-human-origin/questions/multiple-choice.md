# Culture — the two theories of human origin — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The creationist theory rests on —
a) Testing  b) Faith — accepted by believing, not by testing  c) Fossils  d) Trade records
**Answer: b) Faith — accepted by believing, not by testing**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the creationist theory, people were created —
a) Gradually  b) From other animals  c) Already in their present form  d) Twice
**Answer: c) Already in their present form**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The evolutionary theory is —
a) Faith-based  b) Research-based  c) A tax theory  d) A map theory
**Answer: b) Research-based**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Both theories accept that humans are —
a) Solitary  b) Social beings  c) Finished puzzles  d) Identical everywhere
**Answer: b) Social beings**
