## Sub lesson 2 — Sudan's way — the book's own teaching way

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 12 SD Geography 12): The final-year book is organised as applied geography: it opens with a full revision exercise on a Port Sudan contour map (contours, scale, cross-sections, watershed lines), then remote sensing and photo interpretation, then world economic geography, and closes on human–environment global problems, repeatedly pulling Sudanese examples (Gezira scheme, gum arabic, Sudanese oil, farming systems of Sudan) into the world treatment.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 6 SD Geography 6): A whole-Sudan book in three units — its natural environments (desert to rich savanna, plus the Red Sea coast), its productive resources taught environment by environment (farming, herding with the transhumance year of the Kabbabish and Kawahla, industry, mining), then its human geography: population, public services and its important cities one by one.

Sudan's own way (Sudan Grade 7 SD Geography 7): A continents book: after a world opener (continents, oceans, relief, world environments), each continent — Africa, Asia, Europe — gets the same treatment in the same order (relief, drainage, climate, vegetation, population, economic activity, environmental problems), closing with a British Isles case study; Sudan and Africa anchor the examples throughout. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
