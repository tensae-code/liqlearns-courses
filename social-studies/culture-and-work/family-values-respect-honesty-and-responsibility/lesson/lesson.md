## Sub lesson 1 — Unit: Family values

Zimbabwe, *PlusOne Social Science Grade 3 Teacher's Guide* (printed p. 92), teaches: Family values are the principles that guide behaviour and interaction in a family — respect, open communication, honesty, showing love and concern, supporting each other in good and bad times, tolerance of differences, and responsibility for one's duties; they build good manners, respect and responsibility in children. ## Sub lesson 2 — Zimbabwe's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 5) (teacher guide — evidence): rights teaching is concrete and local — support services are named institutions a child can find (BEAM school-fees support, community care workers, police, health workers), government services are named ministries and ZUPCO buses, and the needy are helped by named Zimbabwean homes and trusts (Harare Children's Home, Capernaum Trust, Angel of Hope Foundation).
