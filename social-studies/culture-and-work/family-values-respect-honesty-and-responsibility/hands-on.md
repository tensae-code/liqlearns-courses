# Hands-on — Family Values Respect Honesty and Responsibility

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: family values are the principles that guide behaviour and interaction in a family — respect, open communication, honesty, showing love and concern, supporting each other in good and bad times, tolerance of differences, and responsibility for one's duties; they build good manners, respect and responsibility in children. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
