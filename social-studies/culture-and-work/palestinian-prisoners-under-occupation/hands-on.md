# Hands-on — Palestinian Prisoners Under Occupation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Palestinian Prisoners Under Occupation
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: From the British Mandate to today, hundreds of thousands of Palestinians have been imprisoned, under the harshest oppression, torture and humiliation; Britain built prisons such as Sarafand and Acre, and after the occupations of 1948 and 1967 the occupation built prisons from north to south — Ashkelon, Ramla, Ofer, the Negev desert prison and others — and practised physical and psychological torture on Palestinian prisoners: beating, the shabeh stress position, electric shocks, sleep deprivation, solitary confinement, banning visits, and medical neglect that killed dozens of prisoners inside the prisons, besides fines, administrative detention, and arresting women and children; in 1974 the PLO made 17 April of every year the Palestinian Prisoner's Day, a national day of loyalty to the prisoners and their sacrifices and of support for their right to freedom. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
