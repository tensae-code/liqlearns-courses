# Hands-on — Foundations of the Saudi State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Kingdom's foundations — the religious foundation (the state stands on Islam as method and application), the historical foundation (an authentic heritage rooted over 590 years since Diriyah was built as the first Saudi state's capital in 850H; the first state in 1139H, the second in 1240H, and King Abdulaziz's unification in 1351H; the monarchy of the Al Saud ruling by Islam, with national unity), and the developmental foundation (investing human and natural resources wisely, comprehensive future planning — the five-year development plans and Vision 2030 — and continuous development, seen in Riyadh growing from 1.2 km² to 3,115 km² and Jeddah from 13 km² to over 1,765 km²). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
