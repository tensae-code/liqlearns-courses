The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 174–178), teaches: The Kingdom's foundations — the religious foundation (the state stands on Islam as method and application), the historical foundation (an authentic heritage rooted over 590 years since Diriyah was built as the first Saudi state's capital in 850H; the first state in 1139H, the second in 1240H, and King Abdulaziz's unification in 1351H; the monarchy of the Al Saud ruling by Islam, with national unity), and the developmental foundation (investing human and natural resources wisely, comprehensive future planning — the five-year development plans and Vision 2030 — and continuous development, seen in Riyadh growing from 1.2 km² to 3,115 km² and Jeddah from 13 km² to over 1,765 km²).

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes.
