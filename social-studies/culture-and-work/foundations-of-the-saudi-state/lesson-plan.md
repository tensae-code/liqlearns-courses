# Lesson plan — Foundations of the Saudi State

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Foundations of the Saudi State
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Saudi Arabia, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Kingdom's foundations — the religious foundation (the state stands on Islam as method and application), the historical foundation (an authentic heritage rooted over 590 years since Diriyah was built as the first Saudi state's capital in 850H; the first state in 1139H, the second in 1240H, and King Abdulaziz's unification in 1351H; the monarchy of the Al Saud ruling by Islam, with national unity), and the developmental foundation (investing human and natural resources wisely, comprehensive future planning — the five-year development plans and Vision 2030 — and continuous development, seen in Riyadh growing from 1.2 km² to 3,115 km² and Jeddah from 13 km² to over 1,765 km²) — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — the Kingdom's foundations — the religious foundation (the state stands on Islam as method and application), the historical foundation (an authentic heritage rooted over 590 years since Diriyah was built as the first Saudi state's capital in 850H; the first state in 1139H, the second in 1240H, and King Abdulaziz's unification in 1351H; the monarchy of the Al Saud ruling by Islam, with national unity), and the developmental foundation (investing human and natural resources wisely, comprehensive future planning — the five-year development plans and Vision 2030 — and continuous development, seen in Riyadh growing from 1.2 km² to 3,115 km² and Jeddah from 13 km² to over 1,765 km²).
