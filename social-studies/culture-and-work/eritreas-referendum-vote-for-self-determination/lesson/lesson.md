## Sub lesson 1 — Eritreas Referendum Vote for Self Determination

Eritrea Grade 6 Citizenship Education (printed p. 62), teaches: voting and the referendum as the people's own decision — self-determination, the 1993 referendum observed by the UN and OAU, 99.8% voting YES, and what a sovereign state is, taught through a classroom vote and a dialogue. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Eritrea's way — the book's own teaching way

Eritrea's own way: a standalone Citizenship Education subject built entirely from Eritrean stories, poems and dialogues — responsibility is taught through a shop-queue drama and a family Tandoor day, the state through the 1993 referendum story, and patriotism as practical service (keeping the school clean, planting trees at the new clinic) that even a child can do. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
