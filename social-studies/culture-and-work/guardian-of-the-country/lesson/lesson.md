# Guardian of the Country

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: those who guard the country — the soldier and the watchman as honoured workers whose vigilance lets everyone else sleep — as Somalia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ilaaliyaha dalka (The Guardian of the Country)

Somalia Grade 2 Barbaarinta iyo Cilmiga Beesha (printed p. 62), teaches: those who guard the country — the soldier and the watchman as honoured workers whose vigilance lets everyone else sleep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way

Somalia's own way (Grade 2): Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
