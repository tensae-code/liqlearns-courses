# Hands-on — Guardian of the Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ilaaliyaha dalka (The Guardian of the Country)
Work through this part with your hands: those who guard the country — the soldier and the watchman as honoured workers whose vigilance lets everyone else sleep. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
