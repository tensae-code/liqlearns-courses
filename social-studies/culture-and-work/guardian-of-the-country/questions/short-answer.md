**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Ilaaliyaha dalka (The Guardian of the Country) — those who guard the country — the soldier and the watchman as honoured workers whose vigilance lets everyone else sleep (any close wording).**
