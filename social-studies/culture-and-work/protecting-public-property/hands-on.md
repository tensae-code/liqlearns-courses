# Hands-on — Protecting Public Property

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Land, mountains, forests, state farms, parks, roads, mines, factories, schools and antiquities belong to the whole nation; no one may seize, destroy or use them for private gain, and protecting them is every citizen's duty. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Afghanistan's way
Say how Afghanistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
