## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: disability is an injury to a person — bodily, mental or psychological — that harms his bodily or mental growth or both and limits his ability to perform his natural role in life for his age, sex and social and cultural characteristics; its types include motor, sensory and mental disability, and it requires special opportunities for growth, education and training with tools and methods that respect their particularities; its causes are many — hereditary factors in the parents, the pregnant mother's health problems, some diseases, malnutrition and smoking, and serious accidents — and in the Palestinian case the occupation is added, as children and individuals are hit by bullets and explosions during aggression or beating during arrest, the clearest example being the bone-breaking policy its soldiers followed in the 1987 Intifada — as Palestine's book teaches it.
