# Hands-on — Tourism In Iraq

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 6 applied study: Tourism and recreation in Iraq — Sulaymaniyah's resorts and the Dukan and Darbandikhan lakes, Ahmad Awa and Tawila waterfalls, Duhok's resorts, desert tourism on the western plateau (riding, mesas and dry valleys, the Ain al-Tamr oasis), and religious tourism (activity: write a report on developing it). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
