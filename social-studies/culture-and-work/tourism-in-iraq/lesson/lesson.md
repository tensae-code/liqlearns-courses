## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography): a human-geography course in six chapters — population, cities and countryside, agriculture, industry and mining, transport and trade, political geography and tourism — and every chapter closes with an applied study of Iraq itself, so the world pattern is always brought home. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
