Iraq's book, the *Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography* (printed p. 163), teaches: Ch. 6 applied study: Tourism and recreation in Iraq — Sulaymaniyah's resorts and the Dukan and Darbandikhan lakes, Ahmad Awa and Tawila waterfalls, Duhok's resorts, desert tourism on the western plateau (riding, mesas and dry valleys, the Ain al-Tamr oasis), and religious tourism (activity: write a report on developing it).

Iraq's own way (Iraq Grade 12 Human Geography): a human-geography course in six chapters — population, cities and countryside, agriculture, industry and mining, transport and trade, political geography and tourism — and every chapter closes with an applied study of Iraq itself, so the world pattern is always brought home.
