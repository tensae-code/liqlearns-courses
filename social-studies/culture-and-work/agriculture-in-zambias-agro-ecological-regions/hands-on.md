# Hands-on — Agriculture in Zambia's Agro-Ecological Regions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Match the farm to the region
Draw three boxes, one for each of Zambia's agro-ecological regions. In each box draw a farm scene and write the weather picture that goes with that region above it. You are matching agriculture to its region.

## Read a farm against its region
Name a farm you know, or one near your school. Write two lines: which region's conditions does it farm under, and how does its farming follow those conditions? Use the lesson's order — region, climate, agriculture.
