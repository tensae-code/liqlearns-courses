# Agriculture in Zambia's Agro-Ecological Regions

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Zambia's Grade 8 (Form 2) Geography module teaches the agriculture of Zambia region by region — the farming practised in each of the country's agro-ecological regions follows that region's conditions. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Farming follows the region

In Zambia, the Geography Teaching Module for Form 2 (Grade 8), Term 3, closes its agro-ecological topic with Sub-topic 2.4.3, Agriculture and Agro-Ecological Regions, at printed p. 44. Agriculture is the farming work of a place — growing crops and keeping animals — and in Zambia it is taught through the country's own regions, not as one farming picture for the whole country.

## Sub lesson 2 — Agriculture, region by region

The order of the topic is the lesson: first the regions themselves (Sub-topic 2.4.1), then their climate (Sub-topic 2.4.2), then the agriculture practised in them (Sub-topic 2.4.3). Farming follows the region — because a region's climate and land are its farming conditions, the agriculture of one region is read against that region's conditions, region by region.
