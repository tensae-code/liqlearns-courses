# Video script — Agriculture in Zambia's Agro-Ecological Regions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Agriculture in Zambia is taught region by region.

First the regions, then their climate, then the farming practised in them.

Farming follows the region — its climate and its land.

One country, three regions, and the agriculture of each read against its own conditions.
