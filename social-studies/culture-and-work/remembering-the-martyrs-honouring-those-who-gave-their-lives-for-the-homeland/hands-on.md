# Hands-on — Remembering the Martyrs Honouring Those Who Gave Their Lives for the Homeland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: honouring those who died defending the homeland — who a martyr is, the martyr's high station, taught with Qur'anic verses and a studied photograph of a martyrs' funeral procession in the Khalidiya district of Homs, asking when and where they were martyred and why they count as martyrs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
