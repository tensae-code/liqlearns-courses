# Remembering the Martyrs Honouring Those Who Gave Their Lives for the Homeland — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Honouring those who died defending the homeland — who a martyr is, the martyr's high station, taught with Qur'anic verses and a studied photograph of a martyrs' funeral procession in the Khalidiya district of Homs, asking when and where they were martyred and why they count as martyrs.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Grade 4 drops the Me-and-You ring structure for a straight civics unit that defines citizenship in one sentence — love of the land, defending it, enjoying the rights, committing to the duties, respecting the state's systems and laws — then cashes each word out: rights are what the state hands the citizen free (school, clinic, clean streets), duties are what the citizen hands back, down to crossing at the pedestrian crossing and not throwing litter; the unit closes by honouring the martyrs..
**Answer: True**
