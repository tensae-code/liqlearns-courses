# Hands-on — Social Actors and Community Participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 7 Actores sociales participativos
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Social actors — housewives, workers, neighbours, unions, water boards, patronatos, churches — organise and cooperate to solve community problems (bringing water or electricity, cleaning plazas, improving streets); citizen participation finds answers to the population's needs and builds leaders. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
