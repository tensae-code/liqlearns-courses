# Social Actors and Community Participation

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: social actors — housewives, workers, neighbours, unions, water boards, patronatos, churches — organise and cooperate to solve community problems (bringing water or electricity, cleaning plazas, improving streets); citizen participation finds answers to the population's needs and builds leaders — as Honduras's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way

Honduras's own way: Grade 2 turns civics municipal — the child learns the Corporación Municipal's full membership, the open town meeting (cabildo abierto), a citizen transparency commission, and the country's own Citizen Participation Law with its three instruments (cabildo abierto, plebiscite, citizen initiatives). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
