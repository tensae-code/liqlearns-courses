# Social Actors and Community Participation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Lección 7 Actores sociales participativos — Social actors — housewives, workers, neighbours, unions, water boards, patronatos, churches — organise and cooperate to solve community problems (bringing water or electricity, cleaning plazas, improving streets); citizen participation finds answers to the population's needs and builds leaders (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 2 turns civics municipal — the child learns the Corporación Municipal's full membership, the open town meeting (cabildo abierto), a citizen transparency commission, and the country's own Citizen Participation Law with its three instruments (cabildo abierto, plebiscite, citizen initiatives). (any close wording).**
