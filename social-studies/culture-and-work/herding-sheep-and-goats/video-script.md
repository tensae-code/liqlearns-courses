The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 4, printed p. 32, teaches: Sheep and goats are herded together but not alike — sheep come home to the pen at dusk while goats range further; each animal has its own call (тойг for the lamb, хөөс for sheep and goats, өөв for cattle, тоор for the camel); the herding words themselves — grazing, driving to distant pasture (отор), fodder, penning, shearing, earmarking — and the guard dog (хоточ нохой, the Bankhar) as the herder's true friend.

Mongolia's own way (Book 3): Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone.
