# Hands-on — Herding Sheep And Goats

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 9 "The special way of herding small livestock" (Бог мал маллах онцлог)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sheep and goats are herded together but not alike — sheep come home to the pen at dusk while goats range further; each animal has its own call (тойг for the lamb, хөөс for sheep and goats, өөв for cattle, тоор for the camel); the herding words themselves — grazing, driving to distant pasture (отор), fodder, penning, shearing, earmarking — and the guard dog (хоточ нохой, the Bankhar) as the herder's true friend. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
