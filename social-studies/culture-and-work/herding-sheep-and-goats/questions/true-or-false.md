# Herding Sheep And Goats — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: sheep and goats are herded together but not alike — sheep come home to the pen at dusk while goats range further; each animal has its own call (тойг for the lamb, хөөс for sheep and goats, өөв for cattle, тоор for the camel); the herding words themselves — grazing, driving to distant pasture (отор), fodder, penning, shearing, earmarking — and the guard dog (хоточ нохой, the Bankhar) as the herder's true friend.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
