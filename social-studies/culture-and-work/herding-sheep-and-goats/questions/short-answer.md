# Herding Sheep And Goats — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 9 "The special way of herding small livestock" (Бог мал маллах онцлог) — Sheep and goats are herded together but not alike — sheep come home to the pen at dusk while goats range further; each animal has its own call (тойг for the lamb, хөөс for sheep and goats, өөв for cattle, тоор for the camel); the herding words themselves — grazing, driving to distant pasture (отор), fodder, penning, shearing, earmarking — and the guard dog (хоточ нохой, the Bankhar) as the herder's true friend (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 4 widens "custom" (ёс) into the whole inherited order — greetings, food, dress, herding, tales, games and the calendar are all taught as rules a good person keeps — and then sets beside it the made rules of the state: taxes, the pupils' council, laws from the Great Khural, and fairness as the same rule for everyone (any close wording).**
