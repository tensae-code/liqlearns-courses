# Herding Sheep And Goats — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 9 "The special way of herding small livestock" (Бог мал маллах онцлог)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 9 "The special way of herding small livestock" (Бог мал маллах онцлог)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) Sheep and goats are herded together but not alike — sheep come home to the pen at dusk while goats range further; each animal has its own call (тойг for the lamb, хөөс for sheep and goats, өөв for cattle, тоор for the camel); the herding words themselves — grazing, driving to distant pasture (отор), fodder, penning, shearing, earmarking — and the guard dog (хоточ нохой, the Bankhar) as the herder's true friend  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Sheep and goats are herded together but not alike — sheep come home to the pen at dusk while goats range further; each animal has its own call (тойг for the lamb, хөөс for sheep and goats, өөв for cattle, тоор for the camel); the herding words themselves — grazing, driving to distant pasture (отор), fodder, penning, shearing, earmarking — and the guard dog (хоточ нохой, the Bankhar) as the herder's true friend**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Mongolia's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.  b) Mongolia's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
**Answer: a) Mongolia's books teach this thing in their own way, as this lesson's way part shows.**
