# Video script — Violence Inside the Family and How to Prevent It

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 56–58, teaches: intrafamily violence as repeated abuse of power (physical, psychological, by omission of care), the shame and the "correcting" custom that keep it hidden, and its prevention — a harm once treated as private, now a public-health and human-rights problem.

Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.
