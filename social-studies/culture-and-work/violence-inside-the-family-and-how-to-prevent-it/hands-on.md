# Hands-on — Violence Inside the Family and How to Prevent It

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 3 content theme "Violencia intrafamiliar"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 7 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana I TG, printed pp. 56–58, teaches: intrafamily violence as repeated abuse of power (physical, psychological, by omission of care), the shame and the "correcting" custom that keep it hidden, and its prevention — a harm once treated as private, now a public-health and human-rights problem. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 2) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is taught as peace-building ("Juntos construimos la paz") — each form of violence is studied through the country's own cases and answered with the four peoples' own cultural values.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
