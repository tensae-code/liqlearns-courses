# Video script — Family Celebrations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 1 Свет око нас 1 (printed p. 19), teaches: Family holidays are the days important to the family's members — birthdays, New Year and other feasts — prepared and celebrated together with guests.

Serbia Grade 2 Свет око нас 2 (printed p. 9), teaches: A holiday is a day when something is celebrated; the family agrees beforehand how to mark it and gives everyone a task — birthdays, wedding anniversaries, a child's birth, New Year, 1 May, Women's Day, and the family slava where relatives and friends feast with the celebrant.

Serbia's own way: the integrated subject opens with the child herself (me, my feelings, my family) and widens circle by circle — family, home, neighbours, school, street — with rights taught from the first year as inseparable from duties ("every right seeks a duty"), and Serbia's own school slava, Saint Sava's Day on 27 January, taught as every school's feast. the second year widens the circles again — family rules are now made by all members together, the settlement's people become "fellow citizens" with shared things (street, park, river, school, bus are all "ours"), and the state enters through its symbols: flag, coat of arms and the anthem "Боже правде", with the behaviour owed while it plays.
