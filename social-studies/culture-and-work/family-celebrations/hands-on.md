# Hands-on — Family Celebrations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Породични празници (Family holidays)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: family holidays are the days important to the family's members — birthdays, New Year and other feasts — prepared and celebrated together with guests. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Породични празници (Family holidays)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a holiday is a day when something is celebrated; the family agrees beforehand how to mark it and gives everyone a task — birthdays, wedding anniversaries, a child's birth, New Year, 1 May, Women's Day, and the family slava where relatives and friends feast with the celebrant. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
