# Shopping Malls and Consumption Space

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: Folha 10: A gente se vê no shopping? / See you at the shopping mall? — consumption and urban space, as Brazil's Grade 10 Geografia (SEED-PR) teaches it in one of its 17 Folhas, each opening with a provocative question. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — See you at the shopping mall? — a meeting place that is also a shop

Folha 10 opens with A gente se vê no shopping? / See you at the shopping mall? The shopping mall is taught as a place of consumption that is also a meeting place — people arrange to see each other where they also buy.

## Sub lesson 2 — Consumption shaping urban space

The Folha teaches consumption and urban space together: consumption shapes the city's space, and the mall is its clear example — a piece of urban space organised around buying, where social life happens inside a consumption space.

Brazil's book has no chapters in the old style: it is 17 "Folhas" (sheets) like this one, each opening with a provocative question, grouped under four dimensions of geographical space (political, cultural-demographic, economic, socio-environmental) and taught from songs, news and the student's own place.
