# Hands-on — Shopping Malls and Consumption Space

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Visit a consumption space in your mind
Name one shopping mall or shopping place you know and write one thing people do there besides buying, as Folha 10 teaches the mall as a meeting place.

## Map consumption in your town
List the places in your town that are organised around consumption, and write how they shape the town's space.
