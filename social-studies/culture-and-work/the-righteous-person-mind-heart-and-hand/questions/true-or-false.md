# The Righteous Person Mind Heart and Hand — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the righteous person (الإنسان الصالح) works with three kinds of skill — mental skills (his thoughts), sensory skills (his feelings and sympathies) and practical skills (his deeds and behaviour) — and is known by what he does for others; real exemplars are studied, among them Malala Yousafzai, shot in 2012 for defending girls' schooling and later the youngest Nobel laureate
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: the course defines its key words for the child in a back-of-book glossary — forgiveness is "the ability to get past the feeling of hurt or anger you feel toward another person", a tribe is "a group of individuals living on one patch of land, bound by shared ties" — and the righteous person is analysed as mind, heart and hand: what he thinks, what he feels, what he does.
**Answer: True**
