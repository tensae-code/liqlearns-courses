## Sub lesson 1 — What qualities make a person righteous?

United Arab Emirates Moral Education, G5 Term 2 teacher guide, printed p. 55, teaches: the righteous person (الإنسان الصالح) works with three kinds of skill — mental skills (his thoughts), sensory skills (his feelings and sympathies) and practical skills (his deeds and behaviour) — and is known by what he does for others; real exemplars are studied, among them Malala Yousafzai, shot in 2012 for defending girls' schooling and later the youngest Nobel laureate This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way

United Arab Emirates' own way: the course defines its key words for the child in a back-of-book glossary — forgiveness is "the ability to get past the feeling of hurt or anger you feel toward another person", a tribe is "a group of individuals living on one patch of land, bound by shared ties" — and the righteous person is analysed as mind, heart and hand: what he thinks, what he feels, what he does. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
