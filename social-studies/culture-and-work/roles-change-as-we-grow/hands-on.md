# Hands-on — Roles Change as We Grow

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Tagore's Ichchha Puran (father and son swap ages by wish) — when status changes the role changes with it, and breaking a role's norms angers society; growing up brings heavier responsibilities, checked by interviewing adults. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
