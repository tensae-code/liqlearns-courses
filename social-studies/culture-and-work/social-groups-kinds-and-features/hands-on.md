# Hands-on — Social Groups Kinds and Features

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Theme 2 Kā darbojas sociālās grupas dažādos laikos? (How social groups work in different times) — teaches: hobbies, interests and occupations as group life; what a social group is, what characterises it and what values it holds; children as a social group then and now. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
