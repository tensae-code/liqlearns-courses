# Video script — Social Groups Kinds and Features

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Latvia Grade 4 Sociālās zinības un vēsture teacher methodological book (printed p. 12), teaches: Theme 2 Kā darbojas sociālās grupas dažādos laikos? (How social groups work in different times) — teaches: hobbies, interests and occupations as group life; what a social group is, what characterises it and what values it holds; children as a social group then and now.

Latvia's own way: the social strand is taught through Latvia’s own festivals and cultural canon and through living Latvian figures (a national sportsman; the sitting State President Edgars Rinkēvičs, used to teach fact versus opinion).
