# Video script — Childrens Rights Convention

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study (printed p. 25), teaches: Children are born free with equal rights held in uganda's laws; examples of children's rights and how they are violated.

Uganda Excel P6 SST (Term-Three printed p. 47 (PDF p. 191)), teaches: A child is a person below 18; children's rights to medical care, a name, play, food, protection and education protect children from harm and promote their welfare.

Uganda's own way (Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study): the district is the child's civic unit — its leaders (LCV chairperson, CAO, RDC, DPC, DEO, DAO) are named by title and role, and children's rights are taught straight from Uganda's laws (a child is anyone below 18).
Uganda's own way (Uganda Excel P6 SST): P6 looks outward and inward at once — the East African Community's own organs and symbols beside the member states' flags, then the citizen: what citizenship is, the child's rights, what violating rights looks like (mob justice named plainly), and how elections actually run.
