## Sub lesson 3 — Uganda's way — the book's own teaching way (Uganda Grade 4 P4 SST Home-Study)

Uganda's own way: the district is the child's civic unit — its leaders (LCV chairperson, CAO, RDC, DPC, DEO, DAO) are named by title and role, and children's rights are taught straight from Uganda's laws (a child is anyone below 18). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Uganda's way — the book's own teaching way (Uganda Excel P6 SST)

Uganda's own way: P6 looks outward and inward at once — the East African Community's own organs and symbols beside the member states' flags, then the citizen: what citizenship is, the child's rights, what violating rights looks like (mob justice named plainly), and how elections actually run. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
