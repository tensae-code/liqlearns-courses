# Respect in All Its Forms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Unit 2 "El respeto" — Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed pp. 93–95, teaches: respect as recognising and accepting people who differ from us; its forms — respect for oneself, for authority, for the family, for differences of culture, skin, language, taste and belief — and the classroom "decálogo de la cortesía" (ask please, say thanks, greet, do not insult, wait your turn).
