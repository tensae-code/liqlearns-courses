# Respect in All Its Forms

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: respect as recognising and accepting people who differ from us; its forms — respect for oneself, for authority, for the family, for differences of culture, skin, language, taste and belief — and the classroom "decálogo de la cortesía" (ask please, say thanks, greet, do not insult, wait your turn) — as Guatemala's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Guatemala's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way

Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
