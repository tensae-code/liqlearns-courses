# Video script — Respect in All Its Forms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed pp. 93–95, teaches: respect as recognising and accepting people who differ from us; its forms — respect for oneself, for authority, for the family, for differences of culture, skin, language, taste and belief — and the classroom "decálogo de la cortesía" (ask please, say thanks, greet, do not insult, wait your turn).

Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).
