# Hands-on — Respect in All Its Forms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 2 "El respeto"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed pp. 93–95, teaches: respect as recognising and accepting people who differ from us; its forms — respect for oneself, for authority, for the family, for differences of culture, skin, language, taste and belief — and the classroom "decálogo de la cortesía" (ask please, say thanks, greet, do not insult, wait your turn). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
