The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Colombia Grade 5 CS_Grado05_01, printed pp. 65–66, teaches: national norms — laws are made by the Congress of the Republic and bind the whole country, decrees come from the executive branch under a law; laws protect people's and groups' rights and establish order, because without them the law of the strongest or cleverest rules, which is unjust.

Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado06, printed pp. 236–238, teaches: in a democracy the legislative branch makes the laws in the people's name — Colombia's Congress (Senate by national, Chamber by regional/national circumscription); the president cannot make laws except in a state of exception lasting at most ninety days, and citizens can propose laws through their representatives.

Colombia's own way: the Constitution is read directly — pupils explain Article 25 (work as a right and social duty) and the health-protection article in their own words, then audit whether the three groups of rights are actually fulfilled in their own locality.

Colombia's own way: the norms unit is anchored in the Colombian school itself — the Manual de Convivencia that every school community builds together, the elected student personero, and the real Colombian rights-defence mechanisms (petición, tutela, cumplimiento, Personería).
