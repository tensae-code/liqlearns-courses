# Laws What They Are and What They Are for

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: national norms — laws are made by the Congress of the Republic and bind the whole country, decrees come from the executive branch under a law; laws protect people's and groups' rights and establish order, because without them the law of the strongest or cleverest rules, which is unjust — as Colombia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 5 Las normas de los países

Colombia Grade 5 CS_Grado05_01, printed pp. 65–66, teaches: national norms — laws are made by the Congress of the Republic and bind the whole country, decrees come from the executive branch under a law; laws protect people's and groups' rights and establish order, because without them the law of the strongest or cleverest rules, which is unjust. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tema 26 Las leyes en Colombia

Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado06, printed pp. 236–238, teaches: in a democracy the legislative branch makes the laws in the people's name — Colombia's Congress (Senate by national, Chamber by regional/national circumscription); the president cannot make laws except in a state of exception lasting at most ninety days, and citizens can propose laws through their representatives. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Colombia's way (Book 4) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: the Constitution is read directly — pupils explain Article 25 (work as a right and social duty) and the health-protection article in their own words, then audit whether the three groups of rights are actually fulfilled in their own locality. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Colombia's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: the norms unit is anchored in the Colombian school itself — the Manual de Convivencia that every school community builds together, the elected student personero, and the real Colombian rights-defence mechanisms (petición, tutela, cumplimiento, Personería). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
