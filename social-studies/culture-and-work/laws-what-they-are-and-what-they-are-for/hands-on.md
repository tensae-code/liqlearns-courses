# Hands-on — Laws What They Are and What They Are for

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 5 Las normas de los países
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 5 CS_Grado05_01, printed pp. 65–66, teaches: national norms — laws are made by the Congress of the Republic and bind the whole country, decrees come from the executive branch under a law; laws protect people's and groups' rights and establish order, because without them the law of the strongest or cleverest rules, which is unjust. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tema 26 Las leyes en Colombia
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 6 CS_Grado06, printed pp. 236–238, teaches: in a democracy the legislative branch makes the laws in the people's name — Colombia's Congress (Senate by national, Chamber by regional/national circumscription); the president cannot make laws except in a state of exception lasting at most ninety days, and citizens can propose laws through their representatives. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 4) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: the Constitution is read directly — pupils explain Article 25 (work as a right and social duty) and the health-protection article in their own words, then audit whether the three groups of rights are actually fulfilled in their own locality. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 6) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: the norms unit is anchored in the Colombian school itself — the Manual de Convivencia that every school community builds together, the elected student personero, and the real Colombian rights-defence mechanisms (petición, tutela, cumplimiento, Personería). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
