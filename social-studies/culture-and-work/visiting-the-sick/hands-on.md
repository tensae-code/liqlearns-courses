# Hands-on — Visiting the Sick

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Visiting the sick as a Kuwaiti custom of solidarity and compassion — its merit and manners, and the phrases Kuwaitis use at a sickbed ("Salamat", "Ghada al-sharr") taught through a school dialogue about visiting a classmate. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
