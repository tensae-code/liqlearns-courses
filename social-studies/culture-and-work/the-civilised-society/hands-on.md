# Hands-on — The Civilised Society

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Civilisation as the alternative to barbarism — the periods of savagery (appropriating economy), barbarism (herding and farming) and civilisation (towns, writing, crafts, industry, science, art), and inside civilisation the agrarian-craft, industrial and post-industrial information-computer types (Toffler); civilisations as cultural-historical types with their own cultures (Danilevsky, Toynbee); and the contradictions of modern civilisation — everyday comfort bought by destroying nature, standardisation against national-cultural identity, Belarus standing at the crossroads of the cultural influences of West and East, North and South — so progress must be judged by humanistic criteria: the harmonious relation of man, society and nature. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
