# Hands-on — Family Traditions Handed Down

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A tradition is the passing down of beliefs and customs from our ancestors — visiting grandparents every Saturday, mother's Sunday market, Nanny's morning prayers, making kites at Easter — and we learn traditions from our elders and from stories of ancestors. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
