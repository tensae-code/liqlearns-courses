# Hands-on — Regulating the Media

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Regulating the Media
Work through this part with your hands: the limits on a free press — no libel, no troop movements or undercover names, the FCC's licensing and fines, and the bans on obscenity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United States' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how the United States' book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
