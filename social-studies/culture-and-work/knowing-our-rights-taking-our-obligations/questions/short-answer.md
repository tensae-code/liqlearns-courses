# Knowing Our Rights Taking Our Obligations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: We know our rights, we take on our obligations — rights and duties come as a pair; knowing both is the citizen's start (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Greece's way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship is taught through two child guides (Danai and Alexandros) who walk the pupil from belonging, through rights, to the Greek Parliament and the municipality — Greece's own institutions are the examples throughout.
