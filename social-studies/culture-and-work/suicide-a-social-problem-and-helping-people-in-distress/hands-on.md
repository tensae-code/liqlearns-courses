# Hands-on — Suicide a Social Problem and Helping People in Distress

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Suicide as a community problem — its causes (depression, drug abuse, family problems, mental disorders, conflict), the duty to talk and to help (parents, counsellors, teachers; RBP, RENEW, NCWC and the Department of Youth and Sports), worked through a real Kuensel case study of ten cases in Mongar dzongkhag and the district committee formed to prevent them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
