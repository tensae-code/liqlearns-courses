# Suicide a Social Problem and Helping People in Distress — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: Suicide as a community problem — its causes (depression, drug abuse, family problems, mental disorders, conflict), the duty to talk and to help (parents, counsellors, teachers; RBP, RENEW, NCWC and the Department of Youth and Sports), worked through a real Kuensel case study of ten cases in Mongar dzongkhag and the district committee formed to prevent them.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Bhutan's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
