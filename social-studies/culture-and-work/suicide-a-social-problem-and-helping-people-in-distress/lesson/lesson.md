## Sub lesson 1 — Suicide a Social Problem and Helping People in Distress

Bhutan Class VI Social Studies VI, printed p. 33, teaches: suicide as a community problem — its causes (depression, drug abuse, family problems, mental disorders, conflict), the duty to talk and to help (parents, counsellors, teachers; RBP, RENEW, NCWC and the Department of Youth and Sports), worked through a real Kuensel case study of ten cases in Mongar dzongkhag and the district committee formed to prevent them. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bhutan's way — the book's own teaching way

Bhutan's own way: the primary course closes by putting the whole state in front of the child — what a government is, Bhutan's own change from monarchy to Democratic Constitutional Monarchy in 2008, its three branches told through the courts' own ladder and the kabney/patang ranks the King bestows, the Article 7 fundamental rights quoted from Bhutan's Constitution, and a social-problems chapter that names Bhutan's own helping agencies (Royal Bhutan Police, RENEW, NCWC) for every problem. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
