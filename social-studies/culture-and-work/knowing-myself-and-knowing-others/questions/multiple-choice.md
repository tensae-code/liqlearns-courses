**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The last part of this lesson is —

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
a) Angola's own way: Grade 6 makes the pupils legislators of their own classroom — the class writes its own regulation (with a no-violence chapter) as the lived rehearsal for democracy — and teaches duties concretely, down to naming the taxes (IVA, income, property) and calling the non-payer a tax evader; tolerance is defined as the price of choosing democracy.  b) Angola's books never study this thing.  c) This lesson teaches nothing about its topic
