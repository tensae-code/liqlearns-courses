# Video script — Equal Digital Participation for People of Determination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Moral Education in the Digital Age, G11 Term 3 activity book, printed p. 17, teaches: the state's duty to enable أصحاب الهمم online — four groups (motor, visual, cognitive/language, hearing) each with its access solutions, from keyboard shortcuts and screen readers to visual forms of information

United Arab Emirates' own way: digital citizenship is taught as rights-and-duties law, not etiquette tips — every ethical rule is tied to the UAE's own cybercrime law articles and penalties, and equal access is proven from the state's own projects (Smart Dubai, Abu Dhabi's e-government) and its four-category support for People of Determination online.
