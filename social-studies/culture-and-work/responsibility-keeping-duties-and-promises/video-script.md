# Video script — Responsibility Keeping Duties and Promises

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kazakhstan Grade 2 Poznanie mira NIS (1031/1019) (printed p. 20), teaches: responsibility as the necessity of fulfilling your duties and promises, set out in a rights–duties–responsibility table.

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 3): the NIS telling names things the Atamura book leaves general — citizenship by birth with rights and duties quoted from the Constitution itself (including Article 34's duty to respect the state symbols), the Kazakh custom of asar (the whole village doing unpaid work too heavy for one person) set beside modern volunteering, and the school collective drawn as a full staff chart from director to janitor.
