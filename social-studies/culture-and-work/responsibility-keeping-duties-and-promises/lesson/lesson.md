## Sub lesson 1 — Responsibility Keeping Duties and Promises

Kazakhstan Grade 2 Poznanie mira NIS (1031/1019) (printed p. 20), teaches: responsibility as the necessity of fulfilling your duties and promises, set out in a rights–duties–responsibility table (education / study at school / be diligent; live in a family / care for family members / help; rest / protect nature / keep clean) and a story of a boy who forgot to buy the bread he was sent for. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the books' own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 3): the NIS telling names things the Atamura book leaves general — citizenship by birth with rights and duties quoted from the Constitution itself (including Article 34's duty to respect the state symbols), the Kazakh custom of asar (the whole village doing unpaid work too heavy for one person) set beside modern volunteering, and the school collective drawn as a full staff chart from director to janitor. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
