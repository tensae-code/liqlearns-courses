Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 83–85), teaches: Bài 18: Vietnam's industrial parks and hi-tech parks — 447 industrial parks operating by 2024 (the first, Tan Thuan in Ho Chi Minh City, dates to 1991; ecological parks at Deep C, Gian Khau, Hoa Khanh, Hiep Phuoc and Tra Noc 1) and the five hi-tech parks (Hoa Lac 1998 — 5G phones and aircraft parts; Ho Chi Minh City 2002; Da Nang 2010; Dong Nai biotechnology 2016; Hanoi biotechnology 2025).

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
