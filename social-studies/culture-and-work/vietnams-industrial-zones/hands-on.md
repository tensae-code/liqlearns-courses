# Hands-on — Vietnams Industrial Zones

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bài 18: Vietnam's industrial parks and hi-tech parks — 447 industrial parks operating by 2024 (the first, Tan Thuan in Ho Chi Minh City, dates to 1991; ecological parks at Deep C, Gian Khau, Hoa Khanh, Hiep Phuoc and Tra Noc 1) and the five hi-tech parks (Hoa Lac 1998 — 5G phones and aircraft parts; Ho Chi Minh City 2002; Da Nang 2010; Dong Nai biotechnology 2016; Hanoi biotechnology 2025). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
