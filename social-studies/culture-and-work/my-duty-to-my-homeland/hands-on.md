# Hands-on — My Duty to My Homeland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The homeland
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: My duty to my homeland — living together in love and cooperation, and the homeland's public, social and cultural facilities that all share. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Jordan's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Jordan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
