# Hands-on — Protest Nonconformity and Citizen Participation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Módulo 5 Guía 15 Inconformidad, protesta y relaciones de poder
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 7 CS_Grado7, printed pp. 186–193, teaches: power relations in the societies studied ran vertically and authoritatively, and nonconformity and protest confronted the established order; pupils read today's social-protest news against that history and propose actions for the active participation of their communities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 23) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: civics is taught historically and locally at once — protest inside past political systems is read beside today's social-protest news, and the conflict module is worked entirely on the pupil's own rural community: its causes, its responsible parties, what worsens it, and how it can be reduced. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
