# Video script — Protest Nonconformity and Citizen Participation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 7 CS_Grado7, printed pp. 186–193, teaches: power relations in the societies studied ran vertically and authoritatively, and nonconformity and protest confronted the established order; pupils read today's social-protest news against that history and propose actions for the active participation of their communities.

Colombia's own way: civics is taught historically and locally at once — protest inside past political systems is read beside today's social-protest news, and the conflict module is worked entirely on the pupil's own rural community: its causes, its responsible parties, what worsens it, and how it can be reduced.
