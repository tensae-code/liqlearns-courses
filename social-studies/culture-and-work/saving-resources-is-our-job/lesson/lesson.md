# Saving Resources is Our Job

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: everything we use leaves a "footprint in nature"; water, forests, soil, mines and energy are limited, so we use them thriftily and efficiently — turning off needless lamps, low-energy bulbs, saving paper and water, sending metal cans to recycling, walking short distances instead of driving — as Turkey's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 3 — Turkey's own way (Hayat Bilgisi 2. Sınıf, 2. Kitap) — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: local government is taught to seven-year-olds as the answer to a child's question — "who put the new play equipment in our park?" — and the class then visits a real muhtarlık, belediye or kaymakamlık and interviews the manager with a form it wrote itself. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Turkey's own way (Hayat Bilgisi 3. Sınıf, 2. Kitap) — the book's own teaching way

Turkey's own way: the republic is taught by research the children run themselves — interviewing family and school staff and searching the net to answer "how is our country governed, who serves in it, and what do we citizens contribute?" — and national unity is taught through one girl's memory notebook of the year's bayrams. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
