**Answer: Saving is our job — everything we use leaves a "footprint in nature"; water, forests, soil, mines and energy are limited, so we use them thriftily and efficiently — turning off needless lamps, low-energy bulbs, saving paper and water, sending metal cans to recycling, walking short distances instead of driving (any close wording).**
