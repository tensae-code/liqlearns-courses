# Saving Resources is Our Job — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: everything we use leaves a "footprint in nature"; water, forests, soil, mines and energy are limited, so we use them thriftily and efficiently — turning off needless lamps, low-energy bulbs, saving paper and water, sending metal cans to recycling, walking short distances instead of driving
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: when the dams fell in Kerem's city, the municipality published the water graphs, announced planned cuts and called the public to be careful; the people's saving raised the water again — using resources thriftily and protecting nature is a duty the whole city shares, with environment-friendly practices surveyed one by one; the city-scale extension of the lesson proposed at Book 5
**Answer: True**
