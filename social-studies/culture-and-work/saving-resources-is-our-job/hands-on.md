# Hands-on — Saving Resources is Our Job

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: everything we use leaves a "footprint in nature"; water, forests, soil, mines and energy are limited, so we use them thriftily and efficiently — turning off needless lamps, low-energy bulbs, saving paper and water, sending metal cans to recycling, walking short distances instead of driving Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Environmental sustainability
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: when the dams fell in Kerem's city, the municipality published the water graphs, announced planned cuts and called the public to be careful; the people's saving raised the water again — using resources thriftily and protecting nature is a duty the whole city shares, with environment-friendly practices surveyed one by one; the city-scale extension of the lesson proposed at Book 5 Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
