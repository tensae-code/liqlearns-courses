# Video script — Saving Resources is Our Job

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey Grade 2 Hayat Bilgisi 2. Kitap, printed p. 76, teaches: everything we use leaves a "footprint in nature"; water, forests, soil, mines and energy are limited, so we use them thriftily and efficiently — turning off needless lamps, low-energy bulbs, saving paper and water, sending metal cans to recycling, walking short distances instead of driving

Turkey Grade 3 Hayat Bilgisi 2. Kitap, printed p. 79, teaches: when the dams fell in Kerem's city, the municipality published the water graphs, announced planned cuts and called the public to be careful; the people's saving raised the water again — using resources thriftily and protecting nature is a duty the whole city shares, with environment-friendly practices surveyed one by one; the city-scale extension of the lesson proposed at Book 5

Turkey's own way: local government is taught to seven-year-olds as the answer to a child's question — "who put the new play equipment in our park?" — and the class then visits a real muhtarlık, belediye or kaymakamlık and interviews the manager with a form it wrote itself.

Turkey's own way: the republic is taught by research the children run themselves — interviewing family and school staff and searching the net to answer "how is our country governed, who serves in it, and what do we citizens contribute?" — and national unity is taught through one girl's memory notebook of the year's bayrams.
