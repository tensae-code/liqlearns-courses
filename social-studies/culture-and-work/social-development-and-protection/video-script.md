The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 112–115), teaches: How the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development cares for society — social protection for women and children exposed to abuse and domestic violence (17 protection committees and a centre for receiving abuse reports), care of juveniles aged 12–18 under penalties (17 social observation homes, five social guidance homes and the girls' care institution), care of persons with disabilities (social and vocational rehabilitation, comprehensive rehabilitation centres, day care), and care of orphans; and the government Social Development Bank's easy social financing for the low-income — the Ahel product for burdened families, Kanaf for widows and divorced women, and family, marriage and home-renovation financing.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes.
