# Hands-on — Social Development and Protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development cares for society — social protection for women and children exposed to abuse and domestic violence (17 protection committees and a centre for receiving abuse reports), care of juveniles aged 12–18 under penalties (17 social observation homes, five social guidance homes and the girls' care institution), care of persons with disabilities (social and vocational rehabilitation, comprehensive rehabilitation centres, day care), and care of orphans; and the government Social Development Bank's easy social financing for the low-income — the Ahel product for burdened families, Kanaf for widows and divorced women, and family, marriage and home-renovation financing. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
