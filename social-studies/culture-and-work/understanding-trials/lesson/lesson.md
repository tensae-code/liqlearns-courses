## Sub lesson 1 — Understanding Trials

## Sub lesson 2 — South Korea's way — the books' own teaching way

South Korea's own way: the 2022 middle-school course opens its society half with the pupil's own formation — socialisation and self-identity, status and roles, and the conflict and discrimination inside our society — then builds the full civics ladder in one volume: democracy's ideals and history, elections and political actors, local self-government, law in daily life and how trials work, and finally human rights with the limits and remedies for basic rights and the relief for violated labour rights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
