# Hands-on — Getting Along with Everyone Good and Bad Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: sorting what is good to do and what must never be done, and why — class-meeting votes, lunch queues, rope play — and the story of Usagi-san, left out by the others, told so pupils feel the left-out one's place. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
