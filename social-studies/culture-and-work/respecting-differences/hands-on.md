# Hands-on — Respecting Differences

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: People can be grouped many ways and a label names only one attribute; a disabled classmate is a person first — everyone is treated with dignity and without discrimination. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vanuatu's way
Say how Vanuatu's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
