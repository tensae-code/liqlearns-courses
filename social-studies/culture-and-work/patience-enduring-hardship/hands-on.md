# Hands-on — Patience Enduring Hardship

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Patience as strength, not weakness, through Kuwaiti women of the past who carried home and family for long months while men were away pearl-diving, and the folk poet Mudhi al-Ubaidi ("Khansa of Kuwait"), who bore losing her first son Abdulaziz to drowning on a dive and turned pain into poetry. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
