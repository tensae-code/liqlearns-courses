# Video script — Patience Enduring Hardship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 6 Term 1 (printed pp. 105–106), teaches: Patience as strength, not weakness, through Kuwaiti women of the past who carried home and family for long months while men were away pearl-diving, and the folk poet Mudhi al-Ubaidi ("Khansa of Kuwait"), who bore losing her first son Abdulaziz to drowning on a dive and turned pain into poetry.

Kuwait's own way: every term closes with a "Stations with Good Kuwait" values unit — each value taught through a named Kuwaiti story or person (a Failaka neighbourhood, the poet Mudhi al-Ubaidi, the water benefactor Sabil bin Duaij), then practice activities asking how the value changes the pupil's own daily behaviour.
