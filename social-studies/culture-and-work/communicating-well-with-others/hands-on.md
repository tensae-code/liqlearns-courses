# Hands-on — Communicating Well With Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.3 "How do we communicate effectively with others?"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Through three classmates — one who talks only of herself and drives friends away, one too shy to present her own good work, two whose written introduction nobody can follow — to the two channels every message travels: verbal communication (words, spoken and written) and non-verbal (body language — signs, voice tone, face, posture, movement), which carries some 90% of what passes between people and can repeat, contradict, replace, add to or strengthen what words say; Mehrabian's measure of a first impression — 55% body, 38% voice, 7% words; the part distance plays (proxemics); and the strengths and limits of speech against body language, set side by side. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
