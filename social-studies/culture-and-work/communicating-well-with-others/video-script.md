# Video script — Communicating Well With Others

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 9 Social Studies, printed p. 13, teaches: Through three classmates — one who talks only of herself and drives friends away, one too shy to present her own good work, two whose written introduction nobody can follow — to the two channels every message travels: verbal communication (words, spoken and written) and non-verbal (body language — signs, voice tone, face, posture, movement), which carries some 90% of what passes between people and can repeat, contradict, replace, add to or strengthen what words say; Mehrabian's measure of a first impression — 55% body, 38% voice, 7% words; the part distance plays (proxemics); and the strengths and limits of speech against body language, set side by side.

Mongolia's own way (Book 7): Grade 9 is where the spiral turns civic — communication first (how people actually reach each other), then rights as a working system of state duties and citizen duties, then the state itself: how a law is made, how a cabinet decides, how a court sits, and the seven doors through which a citizen walks into all three.
