# Communicating Well With Others — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 1.3 "How do we communicate effectively with others?" — Through three classmates — one who talks only of herself and drives friends away, one too shy to present her own good work, two whose written introduction nobody can follow — to the two channels every message travels: verbal communication (words, spoken and written) and non-verbal (body language — signs, voice tone, face, posture, movement), which carries some 90% of what passes between people and can repeat, contradict, replace, add to or strengthen what words say; Mehrabian's measure of a first impression — 55% body, 38% voice, 7% words; the part distance plays (proxemics); and the strengths and limits of speech against body language, set side by side (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 9 is where the spiral turns civic — communication first (how people actually reach each other), then rights as a working system of state duties and citizen duties, then the state itself: how a law is made, how a cabinet decides, how a court sits, and the seven doors through which a citizen walks into all three (any close wording).**
