# Communicating Well With Others — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: through three classmates — one who talks only of herself and drives friends away, one too shy to present her own good work, two whose written introduction nobody can follow — to the two channels every message travels: verbal communication (words, spoken and written) and non-verbal (body language — signs, voice tone, face, posture, movement), which carries some 90% of what passes between people and can repeat, contradict, replace, add to or strengthen what words say; Mehrabian's measure of a first impression — 55% body, 38% voice, 7% words; the part distance plays (proxemics); and the strengths and limits of speech against body language, set side by side.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
