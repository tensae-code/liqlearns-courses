# Communal Councils Popular Power — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Más participación todavía: Consejos Comunales — Still more participation: the Communal Councils — instances of participation and protagonism of the organised people, varying with the problems of urban, rural and indigenous areas; neighbours group into committees for a shared purpose, and by Article 6 of the Communal Councils Law the Assembly of Citizens is their sovereign body — "Popular Power is all of us" (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: La comunidad organizada y la participación — The organised community is the people's expression for exercising power, participation and protagonism; participation, from the standpoint of popular power, emphasises action and commitment — the Constitution grants everyone a broad right to participate in the formation, execution and control of public management, reclaimed as a right and a common duty; the Communal Councils Law, Article 2: the councils are instances of participation, articulation and integration among community organisations, social groups and citizens, letting the organised people directly manage public policies and projects answering the community's needs, building a society of equity and social justice (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: ¿Cómo está organizada la comunidad? La Asamblea de Ciudadanos y Ciudadanas — How the community is organised — the Citizens' Assembly is the communal organisation's highest body: expressly and publicly convened, deliberative, its decisions binding on the Communal Council, the maximum instance of election in which the people's sovereignty resides; in it are elected the spokespersons of the Executive Organ (the work committees — land, sport, social protection, culture), of the Communal Bank and of the Social Comptrollership of the Communal Council (any close wording).**
