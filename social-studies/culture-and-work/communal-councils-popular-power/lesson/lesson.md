## Sub lesson 1 — Más participación todavía: Consejos Comunales

Venezuela Grade 6 G6 CS Venezuela y Su Gente, printed p. 144, teaches: Still more participation: the Communal Councils — instances of participation and protagonism of the organised people, varying with the problems of urban, rural and indigenous areas; neighbours group into committees for a shared purpose, and by Article 6 of the Communal Councils Law the Assembly of Citizens is their sovereign body — "Popular Power is all of us". ## Sub lesson 2 — La comunidad organizada y la participación

Venezuela Grade 7 G7 CS Patria y Ciudadanía, printed p. 64 (PARTIAL — the secured file ends at printed p. 92, exactly as filed), teaches: The organised community is the people's expression for exercising power, participation and protagonism; participation, from the standpoint of popular power, emphasises action and commitment — the Constitution grants everyone a broad right to participate in the formation, execution and control of public management, reclaimed as a right and a common duty; the Communal Councils Law, Article 2: the councils are instances of participation, articulation and integration among community organisations, social groups and citizens, letting the organised people directly manage public policies and projects answering the community's needs, building a society of equity and social justice. ## Sub lesson 3 — ¿Cómo está organizada la comunidad? La Asamblea de Ciudadanos y Ciudadanas

Venezuela Grade 7 G7 CS Patria y Ciudadanía, printed p. 66 (PARTIAL — the secured file ends at printed p. 92, exactly as filed), teaches: How the community is organised — the Citizens' Assembly is the communal organisation's highest body: expressly and publicly convened, deliberative, its decisions binding on the Communal Council, the maximum instance of election in which the people's sovereignty resides; in it are elected the spokespersons of the Executive Organ (the work committees — land, sport, social protection, culture), of the Communal Bank and of the Social Comptrollership of the Communal Council. ## Sub lesson 4 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: the primary course closes with "Misión Ciudadanía" — citizenship as a mission: the constitution's liberty carries the duty to respect others' liberty, democracy is practised in communal councils where "Popular Power is all of us", work is both a right and a duty, and the book ends on peace — human rights begin in the small places near home, in Eleanor Roosevelt's words, and pupils keep a notebook of rights kept and rights violated in their own small world. the first secondary civics book builds belonging as a ladder — family community, school community, municipal, regional and national community, with the Communal Council standing beside them — and participation itself is the lesson: the Constitution gives everyone a broad right to participate in forming, executing and controlling public management, as both a right and a common duty. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
