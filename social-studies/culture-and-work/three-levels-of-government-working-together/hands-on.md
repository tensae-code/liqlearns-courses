# Hands-on — Three Levels of Government Working Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 3 Interdependence of Federal, Provincial and Local Government, Ch. 3 Functions of the Federation and the Provinces; Relations Between Them, Ch. 3 (chapter outcomes) — teaches: the three levels need each other — a printed table divides the work: the federation guards defence, currency and foreign affairs; the province runs education, health and law; the local level builds streets and drains — and each level's money and laws flow from the others; who does what — the federation's ten functions against the provinces' five (legislation, public services, law and order, finance, supervision of local government) — and the councils that bind them: the Council of Common Interests, the National Economic Council and the National Security Council; the levels compared and their interdependence explained — the functions of political parties in the democratic system and the rule of law against unjust and illegal activities complete the chapter's civic frame. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
