# Video script — Three Levels of Government Working Together

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Pakistan's Grade 5 SS5 (printed pp. 27–28); Grade 12 PS12-PECTAA (printed pp. 41–44); Grade 5 SS5-KPK (printed p. 27), teaches: Ch. 3 Interdependence of Federal, Provincial and Local Government, Ch. 3 Functions of the Federation and the Provinces; Relations Between Them, Ch. 3 (chapter outcomes) — teaches: the three levels need each other — a printed table divides the work: the federation guards defence, currency and foreign affairs; the province runs education, health and law; the local level builds streets and drains — and each level's money and laws flow from the others; who does what — the federation's ten functions against the provinces' five (legislation, public services, law and order, finance, supervision of local government) — and the councils that bind them: the Council of Common Interests, the National Economic Council and the National Security Council; the levels compared and their interdependence explained — the functions of political parties in the democratic system and the rule of law against unjust and illegal activities complete the chapter's civic frame.

Pakistan's own way: Class 5 deepens Class 4 — rights now change with time, the UN and the 1948 Declaration enter, freedom of speech gets its own lesson with John Stuart Mill quoted, and government is compared across federal, provincial AND local levels with a table of who does what. the brand-new PECTAA book opens the final school year with the constitution as a living document — its amendments listed one by one to 2017 — and sets Pakistan's own rights charter side by side with the UN's, asking students to compare them. the KPK Class 5 book gives the citizen's charter in two plain lists — seven rights (ending in the right to vote) against nine responsibilities (ending in honest dealing) — and makes Abdul Sattar Edhi, who "served the suffering humanity", its model of what a responsible citizen is.
