# Video script — How Communities Celebrate Similarities and Differences

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 2 P2 Social Studies SB, printed p. 61, teaches: ceremonies done with the same tools, at the same time, in the same costumes are similarities, and done differently are differences; festivals bring people together, they do not divide them.

South Sudan's own way: A community is defined by common interests across different traditions and languages, with peace promoted inside the definition itself; civic life appears at age 7 as a ballot box and a president's swearing-in photograph, and decision-making is taught as a citizen's skill that ends in human rights and peaceful living.
