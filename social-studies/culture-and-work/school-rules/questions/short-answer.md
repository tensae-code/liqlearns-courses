# School Rules — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Règlement intérieur — the school's internal rules as the first law a child lives under (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Le respect du règlement intérieur de l'école et de la classe — respecting the school and class rules (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Le choix des responsables de la classe — choosing (electing) the class leaders — a child's first election (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the fourth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: La coopérative scolaire et l'esprit de coopération — the school cooperative and the spirit of cooperation (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Burkina Faso's way — the book's own teaching way — civic and moral education is a standalone primary subject from CP1, taught through short prepared lesson sheets built on a story situation, group exchange and a spoken resolution the class takes together; road safety, the flag ceremony and care of the schoolyard and latrines are taught as a young citizen's first duties, each fiche ending in a resolution the pupils speak aloud. CP2 widens CP1's duties — children now elect their class leaders, run a school cooperative, and take up the fight against excision and plastic waste as citizens. (any close wording).**
