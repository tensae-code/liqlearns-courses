The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Unit 4 Lesson 5, Pearl Diving: the diving season — al-dasha opening in May, the great dive of about four months, al-quffal closing in September, preceded by the al-khanjiya scouting trip; the ship's crew and the tawwash, the pearl merchant; the craft declined with the coming of oil and Japanese cultured pearls, and Kuwait preserves it through its annual diving festival and a UNESCO heritage bid.
