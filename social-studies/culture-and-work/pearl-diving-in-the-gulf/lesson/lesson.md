## Sub lesson 1 — Pearl Diving In The Gulf

Kuwait's book, the *Kuwait Grade 6 Social Studies G6 T1* (printed pp. 93–96), teaches: Unit 4 Lesson 5, Pearl Diving: the diving season — al-dasha opening in May, the great dive of about four months, al-quffal closing in September, preceded by the al-khanjiya scouting trip; the ship's crew and the tawwash, the pearl merchant; the craft declined with the coming of oil and Japanese cultured pearls, and Kuwait preserves it through its annual diving festival and a UNESCO heritage bid. ## Sub lesson 2 — Kuwait's way — the book's own teaching way

Kuwait's own way (Kuwait Grade 6 Social Studies G6 T1): Kuwait's intermediate Social Studies is organised as four "learning units" per term, each with its own field label (Geography / History / Civics) printed in the unit's standards page, and every unit closes with a project and a full assessment. Term 1 of Grade 6 takes the whole Arab Gulf as its region — Kuwait first among equals: Unit 1 (Geography) covers the natural features of Kuwait and the Gulf States, Unit 2 (History) the civilizational features, Unit 3 (Geography/History) population origins and migrations, Unit 4 (Geography) the old economic activities; a values unit ("Stations with Good Kuwait") and a maps appendix close the book. Non-Geography parts (named, not placed): History — Unit 2 Lessons 2–5: the historical importance of the Gulf's location and the Dilmun, Tarut, Hili and Magan civilizations, printed pp. 47–50; civilizations in Kuwait (Ubaid at Al-Subiya, Hellenistic Failaka), pp. 50–52; Islamic civilization in the Gulf region, pp. 52–55; Islamic civilization in Kuwait (Kazma, Umm al-Aish, Al-Shuqaya; Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyya), pp. 55–57. History — Unit 3 Lessons 3–5: the Utub migration and the founding of Kuwait, pp. 68–70; the naming of Kuwait and the choice of Al Sabah, pp. 70–73; the founding of the other Gulf States, pp. 74–76. Values/Civics — the values unit "Stations with Good Kuwait": social solidarity (the Failaka story), pp. 101–104; patience, pp. 104–106; visiting the sick, pp. 106–108; the gift of water (Sabil bin Duaij), pp. 108–110. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
