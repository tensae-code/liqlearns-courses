## Sub lesson 1 — The Cabinet and Its Work

Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 149–152), teaches: The Council's origin in King Abdulaziz's Council of Deputies (1351H, over finance, interior and foreign affairs) and its creation as the Council of Ministers by royal order in 1373H under Crown Prince Saud; its composition — the President, his deputies, the ministers, the ministers of state appointed by royal order and the King's advisers appointed as members — for a four-year term, seated in Riyadh; its competencies as holder of the executive authority: drawing the state's internal, external, financial and economic policy and supervising its execution; the members' oath of loyalty to religion, King and country and keeping state secrets; and the ministries of the Kingdom (Defence, Interior, National Guard, Foreign Affairs, Finance, Health, Education, Justice, Energy, Commerce, Hajj and Umrah, Culture, Tourism, Sport and the rest). ## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes.
