# Hands-on — The Cabinet and Its Work

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the Council's origin in King Abdulaziz's Council of Deputies (1351H, over finance, interior and foreign affairs) and its creation as the Council of Ministers by royal order in 1373H under Crown Prince Saud; its composition — the President, his deputies, the ministers, the ministers of state appointed by royal order and the King's advisers appointed as members — for a four-year term, seated in Riyadh; its competencies as holder of the executive authority: drawing the state's internal, external, financial and economic policy and supervising its execution; the members' oath of loyalty to religion, King and country and keeping state secrets; and the ministries of the Kingdom (Defence, Interior, National Guard, Foreign Affairs, Finance, Health, Education, Justice, Energy, Commerce, Hajj and Umrah, Culture, Tourism, Sport and the rest). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
