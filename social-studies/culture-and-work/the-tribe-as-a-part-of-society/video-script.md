# Video script — The Tribe as a Part of Society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yemen Grade 6 G6 National Education, printed p. 29, teaches: the tribe as one of the components of Yemeni society — what binds it and where it sits inside the wider society.

Yemen Grade 6 G6 National Education, printed p. 32, teaches: the customs and traditions by which the Yemeni tribe lives — hospitality, protection and settled ways of dealing.

Yemen's own way: Grade 6 National Education builds citizenship in widening circles — family, then tribe, then constitution — and teaches the tribe honestly as a real social component with its own customs and a role in keeping social peace, before the constitution unit names the citizen's rights, duties and the qualities of the good citizen (love of homeland shown in deeds, guarding public property, constructive criticism).
