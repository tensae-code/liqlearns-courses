## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2 Lesson 1 The Tribe as a Social Component

Yemen Grade 6 G6 National Education, printed p. 29, teaches: the tribe as one of the components of Yemeni society — what binds it and where it sits inside the wider society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2 Lesson 2 The Tribe's Customs, Habits and Traditions

Yemen Grade 6 G6 National Education, printed p. 32, teaches: the customs and traditions by which the Yemeni tribe lives — hospitality, protection and settled ways of dealing. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Yemen's way — the book's own teaching way

Yemen's own way: Grade 6 National Education builds citizenship in widening circles — family, then tribe, then constitution — and teaches the tribe honestly as a real social component with its own customs and a role in keeping social peace, before the constitution unit names the citizen's rights, duties and the qualities of the good citizen (love of homeland shown in deeds, guarding public property, constructive criticism). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
