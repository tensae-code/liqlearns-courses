# Hands-on — Thanking and Caring for Elders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: gratitude to grandparents who care for the children and teach them; helping an old grandparent, visiting and listening to her stories, with the Qur'anic verse on kindness to aged parents as the book's warrant. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
