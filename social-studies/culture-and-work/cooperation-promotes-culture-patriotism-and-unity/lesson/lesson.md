## Sub lesson 1 — Cooperation Promotes Culture, Patriotism and Unity

Tanzania Standard Four tz-ss4 (printed p. 44), teaches: Cooperation is working and interacting with someone to achieve goals; people cooperate in happy and sad occasions (weddings, funerals) and in farming, fishing and trade, and this united action promotes our culture, patriotism and national unity in Tanzania. ## Sub lesson 2 — Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way

Tanzania's own way (Standard Four, Social Studies Standard Four Pupil's Book): culture at Standard Four is taught as cooperation itself — working together at weddings and funerals, in farming, fishing and trade is what promotes Tanzanian culture, patriotism and national unity; relationships are taught with an equality rule (never discriminate by gender, ethnic group, religion, race or social position).
