# Hands-on — Cooperation Promotes Culture Patriotism and Unity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Cooperation Promotes Culture, Patriotism and Unity
Work through this part with your hands: cooperation is working and interacting with someone to achieve goals; people cooperate in happy and sad occasions (weddings, funerals) and in farming, fishing and trade, and this united action promotes our culture, patriotism and national unity in Tanzania. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
