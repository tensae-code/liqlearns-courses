# Hands-on — Showing Responsible Behaviour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: responsible behaviour in everyday encounters, taught through the "Please Listen to Me!" shop drama — waiting your turn, listening to others, and the harm done when nobody listens. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
