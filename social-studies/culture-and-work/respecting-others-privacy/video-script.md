Jordan Grade 7 SS G7 T1, printed p. 132, teaches: Personal privacy — its kinds (including place and time) and what violating another's privacy does.

Jordan's own way: citizenship is taught in three widening rings — Jordanian citizenship, Jordanian national identity, then global citizenship — and rights are taught through Jordan's own legislation for women and children.
