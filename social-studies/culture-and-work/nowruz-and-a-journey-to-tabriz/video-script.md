# Video script — Nowruz and a Journey to Tabriz

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iran Social Studies Grade 5 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 80, teaches: at Nowruz, the New Year at spring's start, Iranians clean and tidy their houses (خانه‌تکانی), sit with the family at the Haft-Sin table at the year's turning — apple, greens, garlic, oleaster, vinegar, coin and samanu — and spend the holiday visiting one another; the journey then goes to Tabriz and its sights.

Iran's own way: the Grade 5 special-needs book teaches social life through the Iranian year and the Iranian map — feelings and manners at the Yalda gathering and on the phone to the taxi office, safety as a daily habit in three places, and the country learned by travelling it at Nowruz, to Tabriz and to Shiraz.
