# Nowruz and a Journey to Tabriz — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: at Nowruz, the New Year at spring's start, Iranians clean and tidy their houses (خانه‌تکانی), sit with the family at the Haft-Sin table at the year's turning — apple, greens, garlic, oleaster, vinegar, coin and samanu — and spend the holiday visiting one another; the journey then goes to Tabriz and its sights.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Iran's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
