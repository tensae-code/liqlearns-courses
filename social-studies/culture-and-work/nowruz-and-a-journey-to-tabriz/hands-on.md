# Hands-on — Nowruz and a Journey to Tabriz

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: at Nowruz, the New Year at spring's start, Iranians clean and tidy their houses (خانه‌تکانی), sit with the family at the Haft-Sin table at the year's turning — apple, greens, garlic, oleaster, vinegar, coin and samanu — and spend the holiday visiting one another; the journey then goes to Tabriz and its sights. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
