## Sub lesson 1 — Nowruz and a Journey to Tabriz

Iran Social Studies Grade 5 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 80, teaches: at Nowruz, the New Year at spring's start, Iranians clean and tidy their houses (خانه‌تکانی), sit with the family at the Haft-Sin table at the year's turning — apple, greens, garlic, oleaster, vinegar, coin and samanu — and spend the holiday visiting one another; the journey then goes to Tabriz and its sights. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the Grade 5 special-needs book teaches social life through the Iranian year and the Iranian map — feelings and manners at the Yalda gathering and on the phone to the taxi office, safety as a daily habit in three places, and the country learned by travelling it at Nowruz, to Tabriz and to Shiraz. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
