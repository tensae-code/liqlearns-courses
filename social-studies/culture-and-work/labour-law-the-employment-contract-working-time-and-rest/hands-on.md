# Hands-on — Labour Law the Employment Contract Working Time and Rest

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Labour law as the branch regulating relations between employers and workers and guaranteeing labour rights; hiring by an employment contract (трудовой договор); working time and rest, annual paid leave (with social leave for childcare and study); pay, with special protection for minors (no heavy lifting, night or weekend work) and the two sides' duties — discipline and material liability on the worker, safe conditions and timely wages on the employer. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
