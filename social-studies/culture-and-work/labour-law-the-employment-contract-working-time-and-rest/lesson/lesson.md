# Labour Law the Employment Contract Working Time and Rest

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: labour law as the branch regulating relations between employers and workers and guaranteeing labour rights; hiring by an employment contract (трудовой договор); working time and rest, annual paid leave (with social leave for childcare and study); pay, with special protection for minors (no heavy lifting, night or weekend work) and the two sides' duties — discipline and material liability on the worker, safe conditions and timely wages on the employer — as Belarus' book teaches it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Foundations of labour law (Основы трудового права) — Grade 11

Belarus Grade 11 Obshchestvovedenie (printed p. 64), teaches: Labour law as the branch regulating relations between employers and workers and guaranteeing labour rights; hiring by an employment contract (трудовой договор); working time and rest, annual paid leave (with social leave for childcare and study); pay, with special protection for minors (no heavy lifting, night or weekend work) and the two sides' duties — discipline and material liability on the worker, safe conditions and timely wages on the employer. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way

Belarus' own way: the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
