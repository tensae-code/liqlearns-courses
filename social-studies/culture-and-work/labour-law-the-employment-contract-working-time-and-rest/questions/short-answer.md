# Labour Law the Employment Contract Working Time and Rest — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Foundations of labour law (Основы трудового права) — Grade 11 — labour law as the branch regulating relations between employers and workers and guaranteeing labour rights; hiring by an employment contract (трудовой договор); working time and rest, annual paid leave (with social leave for childcare and study); pay, with special protection for minors (no heavy lifting, night or weekend work) and the two sides' duties — discipline and material liability on the worker, safe conditions and timely wages on the employer (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Belarus's way — the book's own teaching way — the whole Grade 11 course is the state describing itself — the 1994 Constitution and its 1996/2004 referendums, the President as guarantor of the Constitution, a two-chamber National Assembly and elected local Soviets of deputies under appointed executive committees, and two state languages — so its civics is placed as Belarus's own constitutional order, not as generic civics. (any close wording).**
