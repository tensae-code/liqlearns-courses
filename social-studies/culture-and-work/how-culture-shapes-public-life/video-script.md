# Video script — How Culture Shapes Public Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Armenia Grade 9 Social Science, printed p. 43, teaches: what culture is (knowledge, art, literature, morality, law and customs), culture and language, culture and the economy, and how culture steers public life, with Cicero's "second nature".

Armenia's own way: the state is taught through Armenia itself — its Constitution, its constitutional order and state bodies, electing its deputies and mayors, and its referendum.
