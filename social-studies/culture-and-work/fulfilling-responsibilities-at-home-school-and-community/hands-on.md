# Hands-on — Fulfilling Responsibilities at Home School and Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Fulfilling Responsibilities
Work through this part with your hands: identifying and fulfilling responsibilities at home, school, in the community and the country, leadership at family level (a leader shows the way), solving problems with others' involvement, and revealing corruption and fraud while fulfilling responsibilities. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Being Responsible
Work through this part with your hands: responsibilities are duties that must be done voluntarily, not by force — personal responsibilities (sweeping, cleaning, watering the garden, home duties) and social responsibilities given by the community (class monitor, association secretary, leader); readiness means willingly fulfilling duties to standard, accepting advice humbly, and doing even useful things outside your timetable; being responsible connects to good governance and obeying the law. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
