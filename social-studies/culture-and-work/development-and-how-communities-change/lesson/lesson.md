## Sub lesson 4 — Papua New Guinea's way — the books' own teaching way

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 6): in senior primary the community strand becomes civics proper — the groups and organisations of PNG, how they relate and speak for people, and how development changes the community — while the culture strand runs from rituals to taking part in cultural events. Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 7): Grade 7 civics is the nation itself — how Papua New Guinea is governed, its elections and government services, and how its courts and problem-solvers keep the peace — beside a culture strand that climbs from lifestyle and identity to national events. Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 8): Grade 8 turns outward — types and structures of government, international relations and aid partners, multinational companies, and a culture strand taught entirely at world scale: globalisation, global culture and global cultural events. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
