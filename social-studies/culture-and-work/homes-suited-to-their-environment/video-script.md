# Video script — Homes Suited To Their Environment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sri Lanka's book, the *Sri Lanka Grade 6 G6 Tamil 2007* (printed p. 14), teaches: Ch. 3.1 வீடு (The home): how houses are built to suit coast, hills and dry country in Sri Lanka.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 6 G6 Tamil 2007): dialogue-driven lessons (teacher–pupil conversations) that open with Sri Lanka's location and a comparison with the world's other islands, then work through the home and its surroundings, caring for that environment, and finish with map-making and scale.
