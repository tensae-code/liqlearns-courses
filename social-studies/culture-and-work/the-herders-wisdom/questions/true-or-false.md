# The Herders Wisdom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: herding is a fine knowledge handed down by work, experience and teaching: the herder reads the sky, the sun, the plants and the moon to know tomorrow's weather, works with the seasons ("in autumn race the fattening, in winter race the cold, in spring race the birthing, in summer do not race at all"), and reads the herd's behaviour — how sheep and goats graze tells their condition.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
