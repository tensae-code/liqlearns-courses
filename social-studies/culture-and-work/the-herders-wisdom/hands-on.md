# Hands-on — The Herders Wisdom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 8 "From the traditional wisdom of herding" (Мал маллах уламжлалт арга ухаанаас)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Herding is a fine knowledge handed down by work, experience and teaching: the herder reads the sky, the sun, the plants and the moon to know tomorrow's weather, works with the seasons ("in autumn race the fattening, in winter race the cold, in spring race the birthing, in summer do not race at all"), and reads the herd's behaviour — how sheep and goats graze tells their condition. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
