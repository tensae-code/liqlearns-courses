# Hands-on — Eritreas Land Reform 1995

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the 1995 land law — all land became state property, every Eritrean, women and men alike, gained the right to use land, replacing the old small-plot traditional holding that blocked development. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
