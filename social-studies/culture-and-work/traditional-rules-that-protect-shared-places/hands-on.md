# Hands-on — Traditional Rules That Protect Shared Places

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Taboos, closures and the fono ban — teaches: communities protect shared places by rules everyone obeys — sea turtles under taboo, eaten only at special ceremonies and only by chiefs and priests; fishing areas set aside and closed for a time, with the number, size, age and season of the catch controlled so species can breed again; Niue's fono ban on reef activities while the migrating fish run. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Nauru's way
Say how Nauru's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
