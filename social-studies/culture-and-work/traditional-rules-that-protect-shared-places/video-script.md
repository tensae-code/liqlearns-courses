# Video script — Traditional Rules That Protect Shared Places

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nauru's Years 5–6 ESD Reader 1 People and the Sea (printed pp. 8, 12, 15), teaches: Taboos, closures and the fono ban — teaches: communities protect shared places by rules everyone obeys — sea turtles under taboo, eaten only at special ceremonies and only by chiefs and priests; fishing areas set aside and closed for a time, with the number, size, age and season of the catch controlled so species can breed again; Niue's fono ban on reef activities while the migrating fish run.

Nauru's own way: Nauru has no book of its own in this set — the remaining strands arrive as shared Pacific teaching: the sea is held in trust from the ancestors for future generations, fishing is governed by named values and by community rules (taboo, closure, ban), and the catch is shared with the whole village.
