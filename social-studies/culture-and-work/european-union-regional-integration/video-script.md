# Video script — European Union Regional Integration

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Armenia's book, the *Armenia Grade 8 Geography student* (printed p. 6), teaches: Lesson 2 The European Union. Armenia's book, the *Armenia Grade 12 Geography student* (printed p. 47), teaches: §6 Processes of political and economic unification of Western Europe.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 8 Geography student): a pure regional-geography year — the world is divided into regions following the UN scheme, each region and chosen state is studied in the same fixed order (position, natural conditions and resources, population, economy), and Armenia's neighbours (Georgia, Azerbaijan, Turkey, Iran) get full country lessons of their own.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 12 Geography student): the final Geography year is thematic, not regional-by-continent: first "distinctive" region types (fast-growing, urbanised, mountain, arid-agrarian, integrating Western Europe), then humanity's global problems one by one, closing on sustainable development — with mountain-country development read through Armenia's own case.
