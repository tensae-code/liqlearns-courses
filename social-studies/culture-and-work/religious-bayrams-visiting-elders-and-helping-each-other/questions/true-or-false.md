# Religious Bayrams Visiting Elders and Helping Each Other — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Ramazan Bayramı and Kurban Bayramı are our religious festivals, and Kadir Gecesi, Aşure Günü and the kandil nights our religious days; before a bayram we clean the home and prepare treats, at the bayram we visit our elders, phone or send cards to those far away, and stand in solidarity and mutual help, which strengthens our bonds
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: the religious days (the kandil nights, Kadir Gecesi, Aşure Günü) and the two religious bayrams (Ramazan and Kurban) are national and spiritual values, celebrated in solidarity, mutual help, unity and togetherness — visiting elders to kiss their hands, crowded family tables — told through a Karagöz-and-Hacivat shadow-play dialogue; the home-grade version of the lesson proposed at Book 3
**Answer: True**
