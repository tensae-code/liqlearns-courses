# Hands-on — Fairness Means Each Gets What They Need

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Getting pleasure, getting fairness
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Fair is not always equal — bread for the hungry child, a candy for the full one, the front seat for the girl who wears glasses. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
