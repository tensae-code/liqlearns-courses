# Fairness Means Each Gets What They Need — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Getting pleasure, getting fairness — Fair is not always equal — bread for the hungry child, a candy for the full one, the front seat for the girl who wears glasses (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way — the Grade 3 units keep the story-and-poem shape but argue finer points: fairness is NOT equal shares (the hungry son gets bread, the full daughter a candy; the girl with glasses sits in front), trust is keeping a friend's weakness secret, and the clean-environment unit teaches Reduce–Reuse–Recycle by name. (any close wording).**
