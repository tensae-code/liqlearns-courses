# Video script — Fairness Means Each Gets What They Need

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myanmar Morality and Civics Grade 3 (printed p. 18), teaches: Fair is not always equal — bread for the hungry child, a candy for the full one, the front seat for the girl who wears glasses.

Myanmar's own way (Book 7): the Grade 3 units keep the story-and-poem shape but argue finer points: fairness is NOT equal shares (the hungry son gets bread, the full daughter a candy; the girl with glasses sits in front), trust is keeping a friend's weakness secret, and the clean-environment unit teaches Reduce–Reuse–Recycle by name.
