# Hands-on — The Rule of Law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The long history of Chinese law from the first written codes through the Chinese legal tradition, and the new-era road — the Common Programme, the 1954 Constitution, the Chinese socialist legal system with the Constitution at its core, and the turn from 法制 (legal system) to 法治 (rule of law). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Building a rule-of-law country — a complete legal system under the Constitution, administration strictly according to law, impartial justice, and power supervised, so that state power runs within the law and citizens' rights are protected. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What a rule-of-law government is — functions scientific, powers and duties fixed by law, enforcement strict, open and fair, clean and honest, trusted by the people — and how it is built through lawful decision-making, open government affairs and accountable administration. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A rule-of-law society — law recognised and generally obeyed, public life orderly, rights relieved through law, disputes settled through mediation, arbitration and the courts, with public legal services and legal aid reaching everyone. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
