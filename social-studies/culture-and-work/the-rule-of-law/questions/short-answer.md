# The Rule of Law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Chinas Rule of Law Road from Legal History to a Socialist Rule of Law Country — the long history of Chinese law from the first written codes through the Chinese legal tradition, and the new-era road — the Common Programme, the 1954 Constitution, the Chinese socialist legal system with the Constitution at its core, and the turn from 法制 (legal system) to 法治 (rule of law) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Building a Country Under the Rule of Law — building a rule-of-law country — a complete legal system under the Constitution, administration strictly according to law, impartial justice, and power supervised, so that state power runs within the law and citizens' rights are protected (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Building a Rule of Law Government — what a rule-of-law government is — functions scientific, powers and duties fixed by law, enforcement strict, open and fair, clean and honest, trusted by the people — and how it is built through lawful decision-making, open government affairs and accountable administration (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** What does the fourth part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Building a Rule of Law Society — a rule-of-law society — law recognised and generally obeyed, public life orderly, rights relieved through law, disputes settled through mediation, arbitration and the courts, with public legal services and legal aid reaching everyone (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: China's way — the book's own teaching way — the civics core of the senior Politics course, built as three blocks in one chain — who leads (the Party: chosen by history and the people, advanced, ruling through political, ideological and organisational leadership), who owns the state (the people, through the people's democratic dictatorship, the people's congress as fundamental system, and the three basic systems of multiparty cooperation, ethnic regional autonomy and grassroots self-governance), and how the state is run (comprehensively by law: a rule-of-law country, government and society built together through scientific legislation, strict enforcement, impartial justice and universal observance) — every lesson opening with 探究与分享 cases and closing in a unit 综合探究 project. (any close wording).**
