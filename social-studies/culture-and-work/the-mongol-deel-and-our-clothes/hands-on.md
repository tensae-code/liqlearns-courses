# Hands-on — The Mongol Deel And Our Clothes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 7 "Mongolian clothing" (Монгол хувцас)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The deel is shaped for a horse people's life — free to ride in, wide sash, closed chest against the wind, long sleeves, tall boots that guard against cold and snakes; the hat and sash are honoured as the state's own symbols, and the clothing taboos — never step over a hat or clothes, never fold the deel's collar inward, never enter a ger with the sash untied, tie an untied sash in a knot rather than lay it on the ground. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
