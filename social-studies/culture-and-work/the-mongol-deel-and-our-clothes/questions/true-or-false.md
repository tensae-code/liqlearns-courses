# The Mongol Deel And Our Clothes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the deel is shaped for a horse people's life — free to ride in, wide sash, closed chest against the wind, long sleeves, tall boots that guard against cold and snakes; the hat and sash are honoured as the state's own symbols, and the clothing taboos — never step over a hat or clothes, never fold the deel's collar inward, never enter a ger with the sash untied, tie an untied sash in a knot rather than lay it on the ground.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
