# The Mongol Deel And Our Clothes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 7 "Mongolian clothing" (Монгол хувцас) — The deel is shaped for a horse people's life — free to ride in, wide sash, closed chest against the wind, long sleeves, tall boots that guard against cold and snakes; the hat and sash are honoured as the state's own symbols, and the clothing taboos — never step over a hat or clothes, never fold the deel's collar inward, never enter a ger with the sash untied, tie an untied sash in a knot rather than lay it on the ground (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 2 civics is taught through custom (ёс) and story — the respect and friendship customs come as a folk tale and a taboo list, property as a quarrel over a borrowed pen — and the state first appears as the soum office and its governor, with the child's own address as the first civic fact (any close wording).**
