# Hands-on — Social Movements and Citizen Protest in Costa Rica

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Social Movements and Citizen Protest in Costa Rica
Work through this part with your hands: social movements as citizen participation since the 1980s — university students against the "Combo ICE" energy/telecom reform, campesino organisations demanding land and fair prices, and the new social movements, with the state's answers (demobilisation, negotiation, reconversión productiva). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Social Movements and Citizen Protest in Costa Rica 2
Work through this part with your hands: protest as the citizen's other vote: the 1982 medical-incentives strike (42 days), the 1994–95 Millicom fight, the 2000 "Combo ICE" general strike that stopped the energy/telecom laws, the air controllers' strike and the 2013 EBAIS health strike — unions and pressure groups defending public services and vulnerable minorities against the adjustment programmes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
