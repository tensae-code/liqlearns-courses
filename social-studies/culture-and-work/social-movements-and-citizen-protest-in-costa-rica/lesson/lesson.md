## Sub lesson 3 — Costa Rica's way — the book's own teaching way

Costa Rica's own way: Costa Rica's civics is taught as the country's own living system, not as abstract forms — the electoral code and the Tribunal Supremo de Elecciones are explained rule by rule (who votes, at what age, free and secret, parties guaranteed by the 1949 Constitution, state funding of parties), and citizenship after 1980 is taught through the protests themselves: students, campesinos and the new social movements answering the state. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

Costa Rica's own way: the commercial workbook twin of the CONED 11 anthology teaches the same civics as a fixed cycle (my previous ideas, basic concepts, widening my knowledge, I apply my knowledge) — and it is blunt where the anthology is neutral: the banned Communist Party is judged "a very dangerous restriction", abstention is tabled year by year from the TSE's own figures, and the answer offered is civic education itself — student elections run with the Tribunal Supremo de Elecciones.
