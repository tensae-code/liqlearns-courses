# Social Movements and Citizen Protest in Costa Rica — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Social Movements and Citizen Protest in Costa Rica — social movements as citizen participation since the 1980s — university students against the "Combo ICE" energy/telecom reform, campesino organisations demanding land and fair prices, and the new social movements, with the state's answers (demobilisation, negotiation, reconversión productiva) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Social Movements and Citizen Protest in Costa Rica 2 — protest as the citizen's other vote: the 1982 medical-incentives strike (42 days), the 1994–95 Millicom fight, the 2000 "Combo ICE" general strike that stopped the energy/telecom laws, the air controllers' strike and the 2013 EBAIS health strike — unions and pressure groups defending public services and vulnerable minorities against the adjustment programmes (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Costa Rica's way — the book's own teaching way — Costa Rica's civics is taught as the country's own living system, not as abstract forms — the electoral code and the Tribunal Supremo de Elecciones are explained rule by rule (who votes, at what age, free and secret, parties guaranteed by the 1949 Constitution, state funding of parties), and citizenship after 1980 is taught through the protests themselves: students, campesinos and the new social movements answering the state (any close wording).**
