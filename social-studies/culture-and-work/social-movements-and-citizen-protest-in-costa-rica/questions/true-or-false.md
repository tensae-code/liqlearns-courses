# Social Movements and Citizen Protest in Costa Rica — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: social movements as citizen participation since the 1980s — university students against the "Combo ICE" energy/telecom reform, campesino organisations demanding land and fair prices, and the new social movements, with the state's answers (demobilisation, negotiation, reconversión productiva).
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: protest as the citizen's other vote: the 1982 medical-incentives strike (42 days), the 1994–95 Millicom fight, the 2000 "Combo ICE" general strike that stopped the energy/telecom laws, the air controllers' strike and the 2013 EBAIS health strike — unions and pressure groups defending public services and vulnerable minorities against the adjustment programmes.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Costa Rica's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
