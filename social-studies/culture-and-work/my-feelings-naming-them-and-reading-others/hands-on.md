# Hands-on — My Feelings Naming Them and Reading Others

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: naming joy, sadness, anger and fear, reading feelings from other children's faces and gestures at a birthday scene, and saying what you feel and why. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
