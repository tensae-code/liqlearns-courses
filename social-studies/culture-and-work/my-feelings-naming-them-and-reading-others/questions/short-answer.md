# My Feelings Naming Them and Reading Others — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: My Feelings Naming Them and Reading Others 1 — naming joy, sadness, anger and fear, reading feelings from other children's faces and gestures at a birthday scene, and saying what you feel and why (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: My Feelings Naming Them and Reading Others 2 — reading feelings and their causes in four scenes, choosing the fitting way to express your own (express the feeling; it is no shame if you cried), and helping a friend who fears loud sounds without mocking her feelings (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1) (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: My Feelings Naming Them and Reading Others 3 — the feelings game — draw a feeling card, mime it, let the class guess — then telling of a time you felt joy, sadness, anger or fear, and reading the feeling in Nour's cup, Taym's sickbed, Samar's fright and Jaber's anger (the NEW lesson proposed at Grade 1, Book 1) (any close wording).**
