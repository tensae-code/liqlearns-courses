# Video script — Fundamental Rights in the Constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bhutan Class VI Social Studies VI, printed p. 27, teaches: the fundamental rights Bhutan's Constitution gives its citizens, quoted in the book — freedom of speech, opinion and expression; the right to vote; freedom of movement and residence in Bhutan; equal access to the public service; and equality before the law with no discrimination on grounds of race, sex, language, religion, politics or other status.

Bhutan's own way: the primary course closes by putting the whole state in front of the child — what a government is, Bhutan's own change from monarchy to Democratic Constitutional Monarchy in 2008, its three branches told through the courts' own ladder and the kabney/patang ranks the King bestows, the Article 7 fundamental rights quoted from Bhutan's Constitution, and a social-problems chapter that names Bhutan's own helping agencies (Royal Bhutan Police, RENEW, NCWC) for every problem.
