# Hands-on — Fundamental Rights in the Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The fundamental rights Bhutan's Constitution gives its citizens, quoted in the book — freedom of speech, opinion and expression; the right to vote; freedom of movement and residence in Bhutan; equal access to the public service; and equality before the law with no discrimination on grounds of race, sex, language, religion, politics or other status. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
