# Hands-on — Deciding Truly What Happened

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## To decide truly
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Judging classroom incidents honestly — a broken vase, a ball through the window, a tangled rope — saying what really happened. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Myanmar's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
