# Video script — Sacred Fishing Traditions of the Pacific

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Among the Pacific's traditional fishing practices, the reader singles out one held sacred: No'o'anga, Tonga's sacred shark-noosing ritual. Fishing here is not only a way of taking food from the sea — it is a practice carried with ceremony and respect for the animal and the ocean.

Nauru learns this from the shared Pacific reader: the fishing knowledge of Tonga, Tuvalu and Niue is taught as living tradition — closed areas, inherited methods and sacred rituals — inside a Geography of ocean stewardship, not as a list of techniques.
