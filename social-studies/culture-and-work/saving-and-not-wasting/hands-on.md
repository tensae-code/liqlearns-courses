# Hands-on — Saving and Not Wasting

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Extravagance is using things excessively and without need, and it is a bad, harmful trait: extravagance in food harms health, in water and electricity it deprives others of them, in wasting time it cannot be compensated, and in money it harms the individual, the family and society; and its aspects include food, drink, clothing, housing, speech, staying up, sleep, water, electricity, money and time. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iraq's way
Say how Iraq's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
