Laos's own way (Book 1): Moral Education is a standalone weekly subject built as one short story-and-discussion lesson per value, sequenced self → family → friends → school → nation → the world; the same value returns at the next grade widened (the guide prints a Grade 2 ↔ Grade 3 lesson comparison table for planning).

Laos's own way (Book 4): Grade 4 turns from conduct toward character — honesty in learning itself, perseverance, speaking one's opinion while respecting others', gratitude widened to parents, grandparents and all benefactors, and unity across Laos's many ethnic groups.

Laos's own way (Book 8): Grade 2 widens Grade 1's start (road, school, family) into duties — classroom rules, care of the national flag, responsibility for shared property, and keeping the community environment clean.

Laos's own way (Book 10): the first secondary civics year moves from personal virtues (diligence, thrift, discipline, gratitude, manners) to named legal rights, each rights chapter anchored in a numbered article of Laos's own 2003 Constitution or a named law — the pupil meets the state first as the protector of their own life, home and schooling.
