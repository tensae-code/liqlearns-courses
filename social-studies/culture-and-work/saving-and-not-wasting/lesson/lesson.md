Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Saving and Not Wasting

Iraq Grade 6 Social Studies (printed p. 110), teaches: Extravagance is using things excessively and without need, and it is a bad, harmful trait: extravagance in food harms health, in water and electricity it deprives others of them, in wasting time it cannot be compensated, and in money it harms the individual, the family and society; and its aspects include food, drink, clothing, housing, speech, staying up, sleep, water, electricity, money and time. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: the components are named one by one with where each lives and what each built, equality is stated in the concept itself — all equal in rights and duties — and the values are taught as a civilisational inheritance, from keeping covenants "for thousands of years" to altruism as the road to social justice. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
