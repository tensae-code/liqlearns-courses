# Good Behaviour Respect and Tolerance

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: relationships within families and between individuals and groups; building good relationships; the groups pupils belong to in the community (church, school, sports); and good behaviour in school and in church — as Papua New Guinea's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Relationships between individuals and groups

Papua New Guinea Grade 3 Social Science TG, printed p. 27, teaches: Relationships within families and between individuals and groups; building good relationships; the groups pupils belong to in the community (church, school, sports); and good behaviour in school and in church. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Behaviours and Relationships

Papua New Guinea Grade 4 Social Science TG, printed p. 26, teaches: Good behaviour in school and good manners; being responsible; respecting yourself and others; tolerance and caring for others — and the bad behaviours to refuse (taking drugs, stealing, bad manners). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Behaviour in different relationships

Papua New Guinea Grade 5 Social Science TG, printed p. 26, teaches: Good and negative behaviours in the family, in class and school, in church, among school friends and youth groups, and the effects of behaviours. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 4 — Papua New Guinea's way — the books' own teaching way

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 3): the youngest primary guide starts civics at home scale — what a community is and how it changes, how people relate in families, school and church, and rules in different places including safety rules for travel by vehicle and canoe — beside a culture strand taught by doing: singing and dancing, fishing and hunting, ceremonies for birth and death, food taboos and sacred places. Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 4): the community strand climbs a ladder of responsibility — the work people do, then how to behave toward others, then the roles and responsibilities of everyone from parents and friends to the PNG Defence Force, the police and government departments. Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 5): Grade 5 turns from the community's workers to its decisions — who decides in the family, the clan, the village court and the Local Level Government — and to the many societies of PNG, matrilineal and patrilineal, highlands and coast. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
