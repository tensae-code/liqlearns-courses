# Good Behaviour Respect and Tolerance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: relationships within families and between individuals and groups; building good relationships; the groups pupils belong to in the community (church, school, sports); and good behaviour in school and in church.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: good behaviour in school and good manners; being responsible; respecting yourself and others; tolerance and caring for others — and the bad behaviours to refuse (taking drugs, stealing, bad manners).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson's part teaches: good and negative behaviours in the family, in class and school, in church, among school friends and youth groups, and the effects of behaviours.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Papua New Guinea's teacher guides only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
