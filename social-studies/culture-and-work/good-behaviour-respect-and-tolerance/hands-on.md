# Hands-on — Good Behaviour Respect and Tolerance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Relationships between individuals and groups
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Relationships within families and between individuals and groups; building good relationships; the groups pupils belong to in the community (church, school, sports); and good behaviour in school and in church. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Behaviours and Relationships
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Good behaviour in school and good manners; being responsible; respecting yourself and others; tolerance and caring for others — and the bad behaviours to refuse (taking drugs, stealing, bad manners). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Behaviour in different relationships
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Good and negative behaviours in the family, in class and school, in church, among school friends and youth groups, and the effects of behaviours. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Papua New Guinea's way
Say how Papua New Guinea's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
