# Hands-on — Traditions and Customs Build Values and Morals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Traditions and Customs Build Values and Morals
Work through this part with your hands: pre-colonial traditions taught caring for the elderly, children, people with disabilities and the poor, accountability and hard work, respect for elders, cooperation in happiness and sadness, hospitality to guests and good relationships without quarrels; elders and knowledgeable persons educated the young in peer groups, at home and at initiation; parenting ran from conception to puberty with the whole community responsible for raising children in good moral values. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
