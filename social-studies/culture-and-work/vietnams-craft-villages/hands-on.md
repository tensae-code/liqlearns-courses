# Hands-on — Vietnams Craft Villages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Topic 12.3 (part): Vietnam's craft villages — what a craft village is and the criteria for recognising one (at least 20% of households in the craft, at least two stable years, environmental rules; a traditional village's craft is 50–100+ years old), their features (family labour fitted between farm seasons, local materials, inherited technique meeting new technology) and their long formation (from Phung Nguyen stone tools to Thang Long's 36 guild streets; craft exports of 16.2 billion USD in wood products alone in 2024). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
