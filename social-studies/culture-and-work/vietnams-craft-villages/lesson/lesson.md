## Sub lesson 1 — Vietnams Craft Villages

Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12* (printed pp. 41–46), teaches: Topic 12.3 (part): Vietnam's craft villages — what a craft village is and the criteria for recognising one (at least 20% of households in the craft, at least two stable years, environmental rules; a traditional village's craft is 50–100+ years old), their features (family labour fitted between farm seasons, local materials, inherited technique meeting new technology) and their long formation (from Phung Nguyen stone tools to Thang Long's 36 guild streets; craft exports of 16.2 billion USD in wood products alone in 2024). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Chuyên đề 12): the final-year electives turn homeward — the disasters Vietnam actually faces and how to live with them, how Vietnam itself is divided into regions for development, and the craft villages that carry its rural economy and culture — even printing the country's new 34-province table in force from 1 July 2025.
