Nepal Grade 6 Social Studies and Human Value Education (printed p. 47), teaches: The nation's notable people.

Nepal Grade 9 Social Studies (printed p. 66), teaches: Nepal's notable people.

Nepal's own way: Grade 6 is where the federation becomes concrete — rural municipality against municipality, each with its services listed — and where conflict itself becomes a school subject: a lesson on disputes and a lesson on resolving them close the social-problems unit. Grade 9 gives civic awareness its fullest form yet — constitution from first introduction through the constituent assembly, fundamental rights, citizen identity and duties, good governance and democratic conduct — and ends the values unit with a culture of peace.
