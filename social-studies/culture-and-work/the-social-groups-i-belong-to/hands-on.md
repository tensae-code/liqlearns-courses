# Hands-on — The Social Groups I Belong to

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.1 "The social groups I belong to"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A person belongs to many groups at once, and "Who am I?" is answered through them — name, family, class, interests; primary groups (анхдагч бүлэг — family and friends: few people, close feeling, unwritten rules) against secondary groups (хоёрдогч бүлэг — school, clubs, organisations: a common goal, formal written rules); in each group I hold a place (байр суурь) and a duty (үүрэг), mapped in diagrams of the pupil's own day. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
