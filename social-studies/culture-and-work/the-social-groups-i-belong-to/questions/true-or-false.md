# The Social Groups I Belong to — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: a person belongs to many groups at once, and "Who am I?" is answered through them — name, family, class, interests; primary groups (анхдагч бүлэг — family and friends: few people, close feeling, unwritten rules) against secondary groups (хоёрдогч бүлэг — school, clubs, organisations: a common goal, formal written rules); in each group I hold a place (байр суурь) and a duty (үүрэг), mapped in diagrams of the pupil's own day.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
