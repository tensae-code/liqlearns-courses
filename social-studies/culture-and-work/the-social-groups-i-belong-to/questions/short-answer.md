# The Social Groups I Belong to — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 1.1 "The social groups I belong to" — A person belongs to many groups at once, and "Who am I?" is answered through them — name, family, class, interests; primary groups (анхдагч бүлэг — family and friends: few people, close feeling, unwritten rules) against secondary groups (хоёрдогч бүлэг — school, clubs, organisations: a common goal, formal written rules); in each group I hold a place (байр суурь) and a duty (үүрэг), mapped in diagrams of the pupil's own day (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work (any close wording).**
