# Long Song And Throat Singing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the long song (уртын дуу) is the voice of the wide steppe, the seasons and the herding life — no other land sings it so, and it is inseparable from the morin khuur; the great singer J. Dorjdagva knew more than 200 long songs; khöömii — one voice singing two tones at once — was born, the legend says, from an orphan boy who sang back to the echoing cliffs until the wind carried his song to people; the master D. Sundui took Mongol khöömii to the world.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
