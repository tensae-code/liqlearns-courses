# Long Song And Throat Singing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Topic 10 "The matchless wonder — long song and khöömii" (Хосгүй гайхамшиг — уртын дуу, хөөмий) — The long song (уртын дуу) is the voice of the wide steppe, the seasons and the herding life — no other land sings it so, and it is inseparable from the morin khuur; the great singer J. Dorjdagva knew more than 200 long songs; khöömii — one voice singing two tones at once — was born, the legend says, from an orphan boy who sang back to the echoing cliffs until the wind carried his song to people; the master D. Sundui took Mongol khöömii to the world (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for (any close wording).**
