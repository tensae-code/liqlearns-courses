The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 38, teaches: The long song (уртын дуу) is the voice of the wide steppe, the seasons and the herding life — no other land sings it so, and it is inseparable from the morin khuur; the great singer J. Dorjdagva knew more than 200 long songs; khöömii — one voice singing two tones at once — was born, the legend says, from an orphan boy who sang back to the echoing cliffs until the wind carried his song to people; the master D. Sundui took Mongol khöömii to the world.

Mongolia's own way (Link book L1): Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for.
