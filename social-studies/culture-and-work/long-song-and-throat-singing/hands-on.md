# Hands-on — Long Song And Throat Singing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 10 "The matchless wonder — long song and khöömii" (Хосгүй гайхамшиг — уртын дуу, хөөмий)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The long song (уртын дуу) is the voice of the wide steppe, the seasons and the herding life — no other land sings it so, and it is inseparable from the morin khuur; the great singer J. Dorjdagva knew more than 200 long songs; khöömii — one voice singing two tones at once — was born, the legend says, from an orphan boy who sang back to the echoing cliffs until the wind carried his song to people; the master D. Sundui took Mongol khöömii to the world. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
