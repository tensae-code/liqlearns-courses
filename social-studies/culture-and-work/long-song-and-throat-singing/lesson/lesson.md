# Long Song And Throat Singing

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the long song (уртын дуу) is the voice of the wide steppe, the seasons and the herding life — no other land sings it so, and it is inseparable from the morin khuur; the great singer J. Dorjdagva knew more than 200 long songs; khöömii — one voice singing two tones at once — was born, the legend says, from an orphan boy who sang back to the echoing cliffs until the wind carried his song to people; the master D. Sundui took Mongol khöömii to the world — as Mongolia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Topic 10 "The matchless wonder — long song and khöömii" (Хосгүй гайхамшиг — уртын дуу, хөөмий)

Mongolia Civic Education Grade 3, printed p. 38, teaches: The long song (уртын дуу) is the voice of the wide steppe, the seasons and the herding life — no other land sings it so, and it is inseparable from the morin khuur; the great singer J. Dorjdagva knew more than 200 long songs; khöömii — one voice singing two tones at once — was born, the legend says, from an orphan boy who sang back to the echoing cliffs until the wind carried his song to people; the master D. Sundui took Mongol khöömii to the world. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way

Mongolia's own way: Grade 3 turns from the family to the whole moral order — deeds and their fruits, the moving camp, the spirits of land and water, the state's banner and emblem — and ends with the child's first political ideas: deciding together, equal rights, and a government the citizens elect and pay for. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
