# Hands-on — Structure of the Government of Tanzania

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Structure of the Government of the United Republic of Tanzania
Work through this part with your hands: Tanzania's own government as its own way — the Union government (President, Vice-President, President of Zanzibar, Prime Minister, Cabinet), the three pillars (Government/Executive, Parliament, Judiciary) and their responsibilities, and decision-making levels from street and village through ward, district and region to the nation. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
