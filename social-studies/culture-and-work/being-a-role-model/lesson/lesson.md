## Sub lesson 3 — Laos's way — the books' own teaching way

Laos's own way: Grade 2 widens Grade 1's start (road, school, family) into duties — classroom rules, care of the national flag, responsibility for shared property, and keeping the community environment clean. the subject opens in the first weeks of a child's schooling with the road, the school and the flag, then moves home — thank-you and sorry, elders, neighbours — ending the year by widening respect to the whole community.
