# Hands-on — Freedom of Belief in the Constitution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The constitution guarantees preserving the Islamic identity of the majority of the Iraqi people, and guarantees all the religious rights for all individuals in freedom of belief and religious practice, such as the Christians, the Yazidis and the Sabian Mandaeans. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
