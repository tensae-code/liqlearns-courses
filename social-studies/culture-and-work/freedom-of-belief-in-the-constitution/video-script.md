# Video script — Freedom of Belief in the Constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 4 Social Studies (printed p. 58), teaches: The constitution guarantees preserving the Islamic identity of the majority of the Iraqi people, and guarantees all the religious rights for all individuals in freedom of belief and religious practice, such as the Christians, the Yazidis and the Sabian Mandaeans.

Iraq's own way: civics starts at the school desk — the first community the Grade 4 child is given duties toward is the school family — and the homeland is then built piece by piece (land, people, waters, sky), with every one of Iraq's communities named in the population list, before rights are matched one-for-one with duties.
