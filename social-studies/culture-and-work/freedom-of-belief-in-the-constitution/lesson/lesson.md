# Freedom of Belief in the Constitution

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the constitution guarantees preserving the Islamic identity of the majority of the Iraqi people, and guarantees all the religious rights for all individuals in freedom of belief and religious practice, such as the Christians, the Yazidis and the Sabian Mandaeans — as Iraq's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Freedom of Belief in the Constitution

Iraq Grade 4 Social Studies (printed p. 58), teaches: The constitution guarantees preserving the Islamic identity of the majority of the Iraqi people, and guarantees all the religious rights for all individuals in freedom of belief and religious practice, such as the Christians, the Yazidis and the Sabian Mandaeans. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iraq's way — the book's own teaching way

Iraq's own way: civics starts at the school desk — the first community the Grade 4 child is given duties toward is the school family — and the homeland is then built piece by piece (land, people, waters, sky), with every one of Iraq's communities named in the population list, before rights are matched one-for-one with duties. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
