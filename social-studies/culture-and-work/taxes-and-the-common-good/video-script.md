# Video script — Taxes and the Common Good

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Haiti Grade 8 EC3 8e Citoyenneté (printed p. 14), teaches: civic duty under article 52/52-1 (every right balanced by a duty; paying one's taxes named), what tax is and its forms, graphing what tax revenue is used for, building a fiscal culture, and the state's constitutional commitments to redistribution and solidarity.

Haiti's own way: Citizenship is closed by the money bond: after defining the citizen and practising engagement, pupils end on contribution — the civic duty to pay taxes (Constitution article 52) and the state's duty to redistribute in national solidarity.
