# Hands-on — Taxes and the Common Good

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: civic duty under article 52/52-1 (every right balanced by a duty; paying one's taxes named), what tax is and its forms, graphing what tax revenue is used for, building a fiscal culture, and the state's constitutional commitments to redistribution and solidarity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Haiti's way
