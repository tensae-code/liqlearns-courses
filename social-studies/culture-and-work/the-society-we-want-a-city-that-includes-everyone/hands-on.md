# Hands-on — The Society We Want a City That Includes Everyone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the society we want makes room for people with special needs — sign language (the full Arabic finger alphabet is printed), Braille reading, prosthetic limbs, wheelchair access and adapted transport are drawn as ordinary city services. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Syria's way
Say how Syria's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
