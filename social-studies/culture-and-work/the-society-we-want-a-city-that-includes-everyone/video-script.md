# Video script — The Society We Want a City That Includes Everyone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Syria Grade 3 Social Studies (Syria Grade 3 Social Studies G3, printed p. 47), teaches: The society we want makes room for people with special needs — sign language (the full Arabic finger alphabet is printed), Braille reading, prosthetic limbs, wheelchair access and adapted transport are drawn as ordinary city services

Syria's own way (Social Studies Grade 3): Grade 3 is where the series turns civic — the child meets the vote inside a classroom quarrel (proposals, persuasion, a ballot box, the winner implemented), meets rights on World Children's Rights Day (20 November) as picture pairs where every right is matched by its duty, and meets the inclusive city: the society-we-want lesson is built around services for people with special needs, down to the sign-language alphabet and Braille.
