# Hands-on — Doing Good for Others Eritrean Tradition

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: doing good for others as an Eritrean tradition — respect for elders and people in difficulty, shown by the pupils who earn money cleaning a shop to buy their teacher the tifhi she admired. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
