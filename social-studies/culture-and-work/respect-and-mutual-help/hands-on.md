# Hands-on — Respect and Mutual Help

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: We must respect and help each other — living together is learned by living together; a community needs union, respect, mutual help, solidarity and honesty; mutual respect means courtesy, cordiality and kindness, admits no violence or mockery, rests on equality and the value of our differences, and recognises each person's autonomy in religion, tastes and opinions — respect is both a right and a duty. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Venezuela's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
