# Video script — Respect and Mutual Help

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 10, teaches: We must respect and help each other — living together is learned by living together; a community needs union, respect, mutual help, solidarity and honesty; mutual respect means courtesy, cordiality and kindness, admits no violence or mockery, rests on equality and the value of our differences, and recognises each person's autonomy in religion, tastes and opinions — respect is both a right and a duty.

Venezuela's own way: coexistence is the spine of the primary book — respect, rules, family and community are each defined in plain words, then practised: the class votes in assembly, community leaders must learn to agree, and solidarity is taught from Lincoln's collection for an enslaved sailor to Caracas's free children's heart hospital serving the whole continent.
