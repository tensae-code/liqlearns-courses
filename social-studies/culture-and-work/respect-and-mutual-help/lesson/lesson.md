## Sub lesson 1 — Respect and Mutual Help

Venezuela Grade 3 G3 CS Viva Venezuela, printed p. 10, teaches: We must respect and help each other — living together is learned by living together; a community needs union, respect, mutual help, solidarity and honesty; mutual respect means courtesy, cordiality and kindness, admits no violence or mockery, rests on equality and the value of our differences, and recognises each person's autonomy in religion, tastes and opinions — respect is both a right and a duty. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Venezuela's way — the books' own teaching way

Venezuela's own way: coexistence is the spine of the primary book — respect, rules, family and community are each defined in plain words, then practised: the class votes in assembly, community leaders must learn to agree, and solidarity is taught from Lincoln's collection for an enslaved sailor to Caracas's free children's heart hospital serving the whole continent. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
