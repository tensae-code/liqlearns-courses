# Hands-on — How Government Began Clans Tribes and City States

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how government began — primitive communal society had none; clans and tribes were led by appointed leaders who could be removed and served the common interest; government proper came with class society, city-states (Athens' direct democracy; Eritrea's Adulis, Coloe and Toconda) and then national governments. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
