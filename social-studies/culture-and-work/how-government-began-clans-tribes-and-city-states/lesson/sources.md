**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Eritrea).**

Eritrea's own way (ER G7 Social Studies): government is taught as a growth story — from clan and tribal administration run by appointed, removable leaders in the common interest, through class society and city-states (Athens beside Eritrea's own Adulis, Coloe and Toconda), to Eritrea's present unitary state with Regional Administration.
