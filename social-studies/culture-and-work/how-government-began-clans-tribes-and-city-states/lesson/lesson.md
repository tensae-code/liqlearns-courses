## Sub lesson 1 — How Government Began Clans Tribes and City States

Eritrea Grade 7 ER G7 Social Studies (printed p. 79), teaches: how government began — primitive communal society had none; clans and tribes were led by appointed leaders who could be removed and served the common interest; government proper came with class society, city-states (Athens' direct democracy; Eritrea's Adulis, Coloe and Toconda) and then national governments. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Eritrea's way — the book's own teaching way

Eritrea's own way: government is taught as a growth story — from clan and tribal administration run by appointed, removable leaders in the common interest, through class society and city-states (Athens beside Eritrea's own Adulis, Coloe and Toconda), to Eritrea's present unitary state with Regional Administration. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
