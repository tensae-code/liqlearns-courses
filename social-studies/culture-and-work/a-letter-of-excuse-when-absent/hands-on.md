# Hands-on — A Letter of Excuse When Absent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: After being away ill, a pupil brings the teacher a letter of excuse; what you must do when absent. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
