# Hands-on — The Human Being and Society No One Meets Their Needs Alone

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Society is born of people gathering and acting on each other; no one can meet their own needs alone, members help each other in hardship and need, and each person must cooperate in collective decisions to build a better society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
