# Video script — Education in Values

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dominican Republic — Ciencias Sociales 1° Secundaria (printed p. 174), teaches: Education motivated by values — moral values and ethical principles: respecting others' and one's own authority, keeping commitments, avoiding conflicts.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 11): secondary civics is taught as constitutional history — Duarte's own draft constitution, the 1963 Bosch constitution and the current 2010 text read side by side — and citizenship as practice: civic education defined as learning to contribute to public processes and debate real problems.
