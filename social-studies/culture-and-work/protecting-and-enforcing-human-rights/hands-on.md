# Hands-on — Protecting and Enforcing Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Promoting and protecting human rights using the legal system; Enforcement of rights for different groups of people
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The difference between inalienable rights and other rights and how each is promoted and protected through the legal system; and how the rights of women and girls, people with disability and other marginalised groups are enforced. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
