# Video script — Protecting and Enforcing Human Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Papua New Guinea Grade 10 Social Science TG (printed p. 189), teaches: The difference between inalienable rights and other rights and how each is promoted and protected through the legal system; and how the rights of women and girls, people with disability and other marginalised groups are enforced.

Papua New Guinea's own way (teacher guide — evidence) (Social Science TG Grade 10): Grade 10 deepens Grade 9's civics into the machinery of the state — the Constitution as the legal basis of government (Section 99's three arms), separation and balance of powers, what parliamentarians actually do, PNG's three levels of government and decentralisation — and ends with a full human-rights unit: PNG's rights laws, the Constitution's rights, enforcing the rights of women, people with disability and marginalised groups, and children's rights under the Lukautim Pikinini Act.
