# Hands-on — Gratitude Remembering Others Kindness

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the many people who care for you at home, school and in the neighbourhood, and saying thank-you to them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Japan's way
Say how Japan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
