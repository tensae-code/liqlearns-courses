## Sub lesson 5 — Laos's way — the books' own teaching way

Laos's own way: Grade 2 widens Grade 1's start (road, school, family) into duties — classroom rules, care of the national flag, responsibility for shared property, and keeping the community environment clean. the subject opens in the first weeks of a child's schooling with the road, the school and the flag, then moves home — thank-you and sorry, elders, neighbours — ending the year by widening respect to the whole community. the first secondary civics year moves from personal virtues (diligence, thrift, discipline, gratitude, manners) to named legal rights, each rights chapter anchored in a numbered article of Laos's own 2003 Constitution or a named law — the pupil meets the state first as the protector of their own life, home and schooling. the last primary moral year widens the circles to their widest — neighbours, hometown, local government, nation, peace and the United Nations — and closes with care of the country's natural resources. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
