## Sub lesson 1 — Technology and Cultural Cooperation

Palestine Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2 (printed p. 68), teaches: today's means of communication are many — print (newspapers and magazines), audio-visual satellite channels, smart devices (computer, mobile phone) and the internet with social media — which carried peoples' cultures to the world easily and quickly, opened other cultures to easy inspection, and moved conferences, cultural seminars, platforms, books and magazines online. ## Sub lesson 2 — Palestine's way — the book's own teaching way

Palestine's own way (Grade 5 Social Studies Part 2): civics is taught as relations — the child's social, cultural and political relations are four faces of one human-relations unit — and the constitution is not abstract: fifth-graders read three named articles of Palestine's own Basic Law and finish by drafting a constitution for their own classroom. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
