Yemen's own way (G5 National Education): a standalone National Education book that teaches the state through Yemen's own map and constitution-era institutions — the country is divided into governorates and districts the child can name, the three authorities are taught one per lesson, and democracy is introduced through shura (consultation), with Yemenis presented as early practitioners of it.
