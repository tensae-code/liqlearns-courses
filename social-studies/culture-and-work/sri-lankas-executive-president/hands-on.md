# Hands-on — Sri Lankas Executive President

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ch. 5 Electing the President
Work through this part with your hands: the President elected by the whole people (Article 30) for six years and at most twice; who may stand — over thirty, qualified to sit in Parliament, never twice elected before, never removed from the office; and the election itself — one nationwide electorate marking preferences 1, 2, 3, the second preferences counted on until a candidate passes half the valid votes. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ch. 5 The President's powers
Work through this part with your hands: the executive President's reach — head of the Cabinet, appointing the Prime Minister and the ministers and giving them their subjects, declaring war and peace, and the great appointments (ambassadors, the chiefs of the services and police, the Auditor General, the Commissioner of Elections, the Chief Justice and the judges), with the pardon power and the reports that must reach him before a death sentence is touched. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: the combined book's second half is a full civics course in disguise — democracy first as an idea with its thinkers and its six conditions, then Sri Lanka's own 1978 constitution opened article by article: the rights chapter with its Ombudsman and Human Rights Commission, the vote's road from McCallum to Donoughmore, the preferential presidential election, Parliament's 225 seats, the cabinet that falls with its bill, provinces and pradeshiya sabhas, and a court ladder that ends in village mediation boards.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
