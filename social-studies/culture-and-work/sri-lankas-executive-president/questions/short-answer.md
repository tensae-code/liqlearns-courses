# Sri Lankas Executive President — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch. 5 Electing the President — the President elected by the whole people (Article 30) for six years and at most twice; who may stand — over thirty, qualified to sit in Parliament, never twice elected before, never removed from the office; and the election itself — one nationwide electorate marking preferences 1, 2, 3, the second preferences counted on until a candidate passes half the valid votes (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch. 5 The President's powers — the executive President's reach — head of the Cabinet, appointing the Prime Minister and the ministers and giving them their subjects, declaring war and peace, and the great appointments (ambassadors, the chiefs of the services and police, the Auditor General, the Commissioner of Elections, the Chief Justice and the judges), with the pardon power and the reports that must reach him before a death sentence is touched (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way — the combined book's second half is a full civics course in disguise — democracy first as an idea with its thinkers and its six conditions, then Sri Lanka's own 1978 constitution opened article by article: the rights chapter with its Ombudsman and Human Rights Commission, the vote's road from McCallum to Donoughmore, the preferential presidential election, Parliament's 225 seats, the cabinet that falls with its bill, provinces and pradeshiya sabhas, and a court ladder that ends in village mediation boards. (any close wording).**
