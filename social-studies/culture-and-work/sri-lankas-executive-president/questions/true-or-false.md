# Sri Lankas Executive President — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: the President elected by the whole people (Article 30) for six years and at most twice; who may stand — over thirty, qualified to sit in Parliament, never twice elected before, never removed from the office; and the election itself — one nationwide electorate marking preferences 1, 2, 3, the second preferences counted on until a candidate passes half the valid votes.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: the executive President's reach — head of the Cabinet, appointing the Prime Minister and the ministers and giving them their subjects, declaring war and peace, and the great appointments (ambassadors, the chiefs of the services and police, the Auditor General, the Commissioner of Elections, the Chief Justice and the judges), with the pardon power and the reports that must reach him before a death sentence is touched.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Sri Lanka's student books only, named in lesson/sources.md.**
