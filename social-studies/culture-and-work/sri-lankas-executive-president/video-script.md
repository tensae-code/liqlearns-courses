# Video script — Sri Lankas Executive President

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Sri Lanka Grade 11 Social Education and History 2000 (printed pp. 117–119), teaches: The President elected by the whole people (Article 30) for six years and at most twice; who may stand — over thirty, qualified to sit in Parliament, never twice elected before, never removed from the office; and the election itself — one nationwide electorate marking preferences 1, 2, 3, the second preferences counted on until a candidate passes half the valid votes.

Sri Lanka Grade 11 Social Education and History 2000 (printed pp. 120–121), teaches: The executive President's reach — head of the Cabinet, appointing the Prime Minister and the ministers and giving them their subjects, declaring war and peace, and the great appointments (ambassadors, the chiefs of the services and police, the Auditor General, the Commissioner of Elections, the Chief Justice and the judges), with the pardon power and the reports that must reach him before a death sentence is touched.

Sri Lanka's own way (Sri Lanka Grade 11 Social Education and History 2000): the combined book's second half is a full civics course in disguise — democracy first as an idea with its thinkers and its six conditions, then Sri Lanka's own 1978 constitution opened article by article: the rights chapter with its Ombudsman and Human Rights Commission, the vote's road from McCallum to Donoughmore, the preferential presidential election, Parliament's 225 seats, the cabinet that falls with its bill, provinces and pradeshiya sabhas, and a court ladder that ends in village mediation boards.
