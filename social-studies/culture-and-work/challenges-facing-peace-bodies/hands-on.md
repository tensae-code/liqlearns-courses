# Hands-on — Challenges Facing Peace Bodies

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 5 Challenges faced by national and international bodies in promoting peace
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: corruption (giving or receiving bribes), nepotism (favouring relatives), tribalism (favouring one's ethnic group), racism, multi-partyism and large numbers of refugees and displaced persons. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
