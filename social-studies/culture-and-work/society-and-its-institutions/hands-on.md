# Hands-on — Society and Its Institutions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What a social institution is — the five main institutions (family, church/religion, school/education, economy, government) as systems with inputs, changes and outputs that serve society's needs, and how the family once carried all five jobs. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
