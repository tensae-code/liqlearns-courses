# Video script — Society and Its Institutions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 11 Book 1 (printed p. 6), teaches: What a social institution is — the five main institutions (family, church/religion, school/education, economy, government) as systems with inputs, changes and outputs that serve society's needs, and how the family once carried all five jobs.

Samoa's own way: Society is taught as a set of institutions a Samoan child already lives inside — family, church, school, economy, government — and change is taught by pairing America's civil-rights movement with Samoa's own Mau as the home example of a social movement.
