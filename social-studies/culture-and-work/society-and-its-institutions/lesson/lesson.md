## Sub lesson 2 — Samoa's way — the book's own teaching way

Samoa's own way: Society is taught as a set of institutions a Samoan child already lives inside — family, church, school, economy, government — and change is taught by pairing America's civil-rights movement with Samoa's own Mau as the home example of a social movement. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
