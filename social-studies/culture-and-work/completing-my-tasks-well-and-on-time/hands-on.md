# Hands-on — Completing My Tasks Well and on Time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: finishing the tasks you are given at home, in class and in the group — the class wall-newspaper made by the four steps (name the task, plan the steps and the time, do the work to the plan, judge the result) — volunteering, finishing on time, and doing even the tasks you do not enjoy. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
