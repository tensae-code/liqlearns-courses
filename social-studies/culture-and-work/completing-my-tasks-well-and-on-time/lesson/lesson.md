## Sub lesson 2 — Make a personal plan

Vietnam Grade 5 Đạo đức 5, printed pp. 40–46, teaches: The planning method itself — set the goal, fix the deadline, weigh your strengths and weaknesses, break the big goal into small ones, write the action plan and adjust it as you go — modelled on Hồ Chí Minh's planned working day and practised on a chess prize, a basketball place and a sister's English (the slug was proposed as NEW in Book 3 of this file; counted here as an existing target). ## Sub lesson 3 — Diligence, perseverance

Vietnam Grade 6 Giáo dục công dân 6, printed pp. 13–16, teaches: Diligence as steady, self-driven hard work and perseverance as seeing a thing through despite difficulty — Mạc Đĩnh Chi studying by firefly light to become trạng nguyên, Hoa's daily half-hour of English, never abandoning a task halfway (the slug was proposed as NEW in Book 3 of this file; counted here as an existing target). ## Sub lesson 4 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: Grade 3 opens the Ethics course with the state itself — the country's full official name, its flag and its anthem are taught as things a child must know and honour standing still — before widening to neighbours, promises and quarrels; traffic safety returns as a full closing theme, now split into walking rules and rules for riding in vehicles. the last primary Ethics book is organised as three strands by the authors themselves — moral education, life skills, economic education. Gratitude is widened from workers to everyone who has served the homeland in any field, famous or quiet, and "repaying gratitude" (đền ơn đáp nghĩa) is stated as every citizen's duty; the environment is taught as a commons — "the environment is ours; keeping and protecting it is what makes us civilised." citizenship is taught straight from the statute book — the Nationality Law decides who is a citizen case by case, the 2013 Constitution's Chapter II is read group by group (political, civil, economic, cultural-social rights, then duties), and children's rights arrive through the UN Convention and the 2016 Children Law in four groups: survival, protection, development, participation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
