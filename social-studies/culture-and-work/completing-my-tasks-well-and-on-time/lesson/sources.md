**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Vietnam).**

Vietnam's own way (Book 3): Grade 3 opens the Ethics course with the state itself — the country's full official name, its flag and its anthem are taught as things a child must know and honour standing still — before widening to neighbours, promises and quarrels; traffic safety returns as a full closing theme, now split into walking rules and rules for riding in vehicles.

Vietnam's own way (Book 5): the last primary Ethics book is organised as three strands by the authors themselves — moral education, life skills, economic education. Gratitude is widened from workers to everyone who has served the homeland in any field, famous or quiet, and "repaying gratitude" (đền ơn đáp nghĩa) is stated as every citizen's duty; the environment is taught as a commons — "the environment is ours; keeping and protecting it is what makes us civilised."

Vietnam's own way (Book 6): citizenship is taught straight from the statute book — the Nationality Law decides who is a citizen case by case, the 2013 Constitution's Chapter II is read group by group (political, civil, economic, cultural-social rights, then duties), and children's rights arrive through the UN Convention and the 2016 Children Law in four groups: survival, protection, development, participation.
