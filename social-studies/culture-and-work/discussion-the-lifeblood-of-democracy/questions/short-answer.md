# Discussion the Lifeblood of Democracy — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ch. 4 The democratic citizen — the citizen a democracy needs — one who settles problems by discussion ("discussion is the life-blood of democracy"), tolerates opposing views, respects others' opinions, accepts change reached by agreement and wants peaceful settlements — and the aims of democratic education that form such citizens (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Sri Lanka's way — the book's own teaching way — the combined book's second half is a full civics course in disguise — democracy first as an idea with its thinkers and its six conditions, then Sri Lanka's own 1978 constitution opened article by article: the rights chapter with its Ombudsman and Human Rights Commission, the vote's road from McCallum to Donoughmore, the preferential presidential election, Parliament's 225 seats, the cabinet that falls with its bill, provinces and pradeshiya sabhas, and a court ladder that ends in village mediation boards. (any close wording).**
