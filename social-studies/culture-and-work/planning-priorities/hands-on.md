# Hands-on — Planning Priorities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: priorities as arranging works and activities by their importance with regard to the time they need — beginning with the most important, then the important, then the less important; setting priorities by time (short-term works done in a day or week, long-term works taking over a week up to a term) and by importance (the important-and-urgent matrix), and the stages of planning school homework: identifying the tasks and their dates, marking the urgent ones, designing the schedules, choosing the right time, allowing rest time, doing the tasks and reviewing the schedule continuously. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
