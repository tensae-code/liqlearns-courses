## Sub lesson 1 — Planning Priorities

Saudi Arabia Grade 8 Social Studies (printed pp. 272–275), teaches: Priorities as arranging works and activities by their importance with regard to the time they need — beginning with the most important, then the important, then the less important; setting priorities by time (short-term works done in a day or week, long-term works taking over a week up to a term) and by importance (the important-and-urgent matrix), and the stages of planning school homework: identifying the tasks and their dates, marking the urgent ones, designing the schedules, choosing the right time, allowing rest time, doing the tasks and reviewing the schedule continuously. ## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 8 book places Saudi Arabia inside the Gulf Cooperation Council — its organs and goals, its achievements and its challenges — then teaches systems and regulations (how a system is made, their kinds and their benefits), and ends with planning priorities, time management and evaluating plans, and hobbies including the old folk games. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
