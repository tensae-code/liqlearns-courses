# Acting Responsibly the Lost Wallet — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: social responsibility through the story of the lost wallet — a wallet with money and cards found on the ground is handed to the security office, because keeping what is not yours is behaviour society does not accept; the finder may feel guilt and shame if he keeps it, and pride when he returns it
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: in the Moral Education course, nine-year-olds are trained as peer mediators with a named toolkit — settlement, concession, active listening — and the five ways of facing a dispute are taught through animals (the shark forces, the turtle withdraws, the bear yields, the fox compromises, the owl solves); responsibility is taught by a lost wallet with 800 dirhams in it.
**Answer: True**
