# Hands-on — Acting Responsibly the Lost Wallet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Acting responsibly
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: social responsibility through the story of the lost wallet — a wallet with money and cards found on the ground is handed to the security office, because keeping what is not yours is behaviour society does not accept; the finder may feel guilt and shame if he keeps it, and pride when he returns it Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
