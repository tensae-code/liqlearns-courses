# Hands-on — Special Days and Community Celebrations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Children's Day, Mothers' and Fathers' Days, All Saints and All Souls, and village celebrations (fishermen's blessing, Borough Day, La Divina Pastora, Santa Rosa Festival). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Trinidad and Tobago's way
Say how Trinidad and Tobago's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
