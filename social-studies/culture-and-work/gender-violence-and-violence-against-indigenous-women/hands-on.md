# Hands-on — Gender Violence and Violence Against Indigenous Women

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 1 content theme "Violencia de género"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 8 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana II TG, printed pp. 27–32, teaches: violence against women as defined in Article 32 of Guatemala's Law against Femicide (physical, sexual, economic, psychological harm), its types and manifestations, and why indigenous women suffer it most hidden — the DEMI study's determining factors and institutional violence. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 3) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: each kind of violence is taught with Guatemala's own law and institutions — the Femicide Law, the Defensoría de la Mujer Indígena (DEMI) — and through the victims the country's classrooms actually contain: indigenous women, grandparents, pupils with disabilities.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
