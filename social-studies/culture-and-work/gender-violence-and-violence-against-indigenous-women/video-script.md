# Video script — Gender Violence and Violence Against Indigenous Women

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 8 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana II TG, printed pp. 27–32, teaches: violence against women as defined in Article 32 of Guatemala's Law against Femicide (physical, sexual, economic, psychological harm), its types and manifestations, and why indigenous women suffer it most hidden — the DEMI study's determining factors and institutional violence.

Guatemala's own way: each kind of violence is taught with Guatemala's own law and institutions — the Femicide Law, the Defensoría de la Mujer Indígena (DEMI) — and through the victims the country's classrooms actually contain: indigenous women, grandparents, pupils with disabilities.
