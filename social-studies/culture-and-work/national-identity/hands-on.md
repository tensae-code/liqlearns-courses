# Hands-on — National Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Saudi national identity as the characteristics the whole society shares, showing love of the homeland, loyalty to its leadership, honouring its symbols and guarding its gains; the homeland as the land we belong to and our fathers lived on; pupils introduce their own identity (Islam, Arabic, Makkah and Madinah, heritage). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
