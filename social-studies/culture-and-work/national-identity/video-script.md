# Video script — National Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 4 Social Studies (printed pp. 16–17), teaches: Saudi national identity as the characteristics the whole society shares, showing love of the homeland, loyalty to its leadership, honouring its symbols and guarding its gains; the homeland as the land we belong to and our fathers lived on; pupils introduce their own identity (Islam, Arabic, Makkah and Madinah, heritage).

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 4 book opens with citizenship — national identity, the system of government, national symbols and the founding story of security and unity under one state — and returns in Unit 6 to responsible citizenship through family and society, rights and duties, the good citizen, teamwork, and heritage stories that teach patience and hard work.
