United States' own way (Grade 13): the United States teaches its own government as one complete system in one book — the Constitution is not a topic but the frame: every institution (Congress, presidency, courts, states, counties, cities, the civil service) is derived from it in turn, and rights are taught as the courts have interpreted them, case by case.
