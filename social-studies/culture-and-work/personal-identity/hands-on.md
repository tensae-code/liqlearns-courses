# Hands-on — Personal Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: growth as continuous change in body, thinking and abilities, whose requirements are accepting bodily change, healthy food, sport, sleep and self-confidence; respecting and accepting people's differing appearances and characters as part of identity; self-identity as the set of inherited and acquired traits, abilities, skills and motives that distinguish a person — its elements: awareness, outward form, traits, religion and beliefs, motives and desires, abilities and skills, and one's personal history. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
