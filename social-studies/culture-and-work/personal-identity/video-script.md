Saudi Arabia Grade 7 Social Studies (printed pp. 186–188), teaches: Growth as continuous change in body, thinking and abilities, whose requirements are accepting bodily change, healthy food, sport, sleep and self-confidence; respecting and accepting people's differing appearances and characters as part of identity; self-identity as the set of inherited and acquired traits, abilities, skills and motives that distinguish a person — its elements: awareness, outward form, traits, religion and beliefs, motives and desires, abilities and skills, and one's personal history.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 7 book moves from planning and organising time, through shura and the values of the Righteous Caliphs, personal identity, self-development and social interaction, to national security in its concept, pillars, fields and institutions and the citizen's part in it, and closes with dialogue — its concept, pillars and manners, and national dialogue.
