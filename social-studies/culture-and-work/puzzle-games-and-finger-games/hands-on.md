# Hands-on — Puzzle Games And Finger Games

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 17 "Games played with puzzles and fingers" (Оньсон болон хуруугаар тоглох тоглоом)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The puzzle game (оньсон тоглоом) is a national art joined to the science of interlocking — the ger itself is a puzzle building, and khans kept puzzle games at their palace gates for visitors to play; wooden puzzles are solved by a set method; and the finger games — string figures (төгцөө), "find the middle finger", and shadow animals made with the hands — train the eye and the mind. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
