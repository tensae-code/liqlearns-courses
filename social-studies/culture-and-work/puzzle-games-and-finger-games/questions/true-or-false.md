# Puzzle Games And Finger Games — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: the puzzle game (оньсон тоглоом) is a national art joined to the science of interlocking — the ger itself is a puzzle building, and khans kept puzzle games at their palace gates for visitors to play; wooden puzzles are solved by a set method; and the finger games — string figures (төгцөө), "find the middle finger", and shadow animals made with the hands — train the eye and the mind.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
