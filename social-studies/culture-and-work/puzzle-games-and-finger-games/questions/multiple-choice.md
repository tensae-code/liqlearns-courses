# Puzzle Games And Finger Games — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 17 "Games played with puzzles and fingers" (Оньсон болон хуруугаар тоглох тоглоом)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 17 "Games played with puzzles and fingers" (Оньсон болон хуруугаар тоглох тоглоом)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) The puzzle game (оньсон тоглоом) is a national art joined to the science of interlocking — the ger itself is a puzzle building, and khans kept puzzle games at their palace gates for visitors to play; wooden puzzles are solved by a set method; and the finger games — string figures (төгцөө), "find the middle finger", and shadow animals made with the hands — train the eye and the mind  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) The puzzle game (оньсон тоглоом) is a national art joined to the science of interlocking — the ger itself is a puzzle building, and khans kept puzzle games at their palace gates for visitors to play; wooden puzzles are solved by a set method; and the finger games — string figures (төгцөө), "find the middle finger", and shadow animals made with the hands — train the eye and the mind**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is true, from the lesson?
