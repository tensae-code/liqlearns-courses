Mongolia Civic Education Grade 5, printed p. 48, teaches: The puzzle game (оньсон тоглоом) is a national art joined to the science of interlocking — the ger itself is a puzzle building, and khans kept puzzle games at their palace gates for visitors to play; wooden puzzles are solved by a set method; and the finger games — string figures (төгцөө), "find the middle finger", and shadow animals made with the hands — train the eye and the mind.

Mongolia's own way (Book 4): the last primary civics book completes the customs syllabus (the guest, the genealogy book, the ger's building order, the meaning of every garment) and then turns the same lens on the modern state — the local Khural and governor's office are explained exactly as customs were, as the settled way citizens and the state behave toward each other.
