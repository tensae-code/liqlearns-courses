# Hands-on — What Civil Rights Are

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## What Civil Rights Are
Work through this part with your hands: the United States' civil-rights road as Mexico's book tells it — slavery abolished by the 13th Amendment, equal protection by the 14th, the vote by the 15th, then Jackie Robinson, Shirley Chisholm, Martin Luther King Jr. and Barack Obama against continuing discrimination. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mexico's way
Say how Mexico's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
