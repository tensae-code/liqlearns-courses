# What Civil Rights Are — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: What Civil Rights Are — the United States' civil-rights road as Mexico's book tells it — slavery abolished by the 13th Amendment, equal protection by the 14th, the vote by the 15th, then Jackie Robinson, Shirley Chisholm, Martin Luther King Jr. and Barack Obama against continuing discrimination (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way — Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution. (any close wording).**
