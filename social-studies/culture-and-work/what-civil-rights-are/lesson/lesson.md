Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — What Civil Rights Are

Mexico Grade 7 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 1 (secundaria) (printed pp. 326–328), teaches: The United States' civil-rights road as Mexico's book tells it — slavery abolished by the 13th Amendment, equal protection by the 14th, the vote by the 15th, then Jackie Robinson, Shirley Chisholm, Martin Luther King Jr. and Barack Obama against continuing discrimination. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way

Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
