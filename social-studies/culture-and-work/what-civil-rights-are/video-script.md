# Video script — What Civil Rights Are

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mexico Grade 7 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 1 (secundaria) (printed pp. 326–328), teaches: The United States' civil-rights road as Mexico's book tells it — slavery abolished by the 13th Amendment, equal protection by the 14th, the vote by the 15th, then Jackie Robinson, Shirley Chisholm, Martin Luther King Jr. and Barack Obama against continuing discrimination.

---

Mexico's own way (Grade 7): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution.
