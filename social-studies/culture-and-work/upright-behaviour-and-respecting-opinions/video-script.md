# Video script — Upright Behaviour and Respecting Opinions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mauritania Grade 4 Civic Education SB (printed p. 33), teaches: Each person is responsible for their behaviour toward family, teachers and classmates; upright behaviour builds love and shared interest between people, and builds a sound society.

Mauritania Grade 4 Civic Education SB (printed p. 35), teaches: Difference of opinion is natural and must never become hatred; differing opinions, managed with wisdom, reach the best results.

Mauritania's own way: Year 4 is the commune — the whole first half walks the municipality's own law (Law 87/289), its mayor, councillors and council, and what it owes the school, the playground and the health centre; the second half turns to identity, belonging, two rights, and the behaviour values
