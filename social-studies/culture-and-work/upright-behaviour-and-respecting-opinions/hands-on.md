# Hands-on — Upright Behaviour and Respecting Opinions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: each person is responsible for their behaviour toward family, teachers and classmates; upright behaviour builds love and shared interest between people, and builds a sound society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

Say how Mauritania's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
