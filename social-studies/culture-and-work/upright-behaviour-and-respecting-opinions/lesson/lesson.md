# Upright Behaviour and Respecting Opinions

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: each person is responsible for their behaviour toward family, teachers and classmates; upright behaviour builds love and shared interest between people, and builds a sound society — as Mauritania's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Upright Behaviour

Mauritania Grade 4 Civic Education SB (printed p. 33), teaches: Each person is responsible for their behaviour toward family, teachers and classmates; upright behaviour builds love and shared interest between people, and builds a sound society. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Respecting the Other's Opinion

Mauritania Grade 4 Civic Education SB (printed p. 35), teaches: Difference of opinion is natural and must never become hatred; differing opinions, managed with wisdom, reach the best results. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mauritania's way — the book's own teaching way

Mauritania's own way: Year 4 is the commune — the whole first half walks the municipality's own law (Law 87/289), its mayor, councillors and council, and what it owes the school, the playground and the health centre; the second half turns to identity, belonging, two rights, and the behaviour values This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
