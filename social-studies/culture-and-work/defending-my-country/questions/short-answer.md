# Defending My Country — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Dalkeyga aan daafaco (I Will Defend My Country) — defending the homeland as a child's duty too — the brave (geesi) stand between the country and its enemies (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 2 teaches the same civic world through people, not topics — a mother, a father, a fisherman-hero — and through the child's own uniformed corps, the October Revolution Flowers, whose members are known by cleanliness, honesty, bravery and love of the flag.
