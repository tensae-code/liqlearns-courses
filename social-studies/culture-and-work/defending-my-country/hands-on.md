# Hands-on — Defending My Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dalkeyga aan daafaco (I Will Defend My Country)
Work through this part with your hands: defending the homeland as a child's duty too — the brave (geesi) stand between the country and its enemies. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Somalia's way
Say how Somalia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
