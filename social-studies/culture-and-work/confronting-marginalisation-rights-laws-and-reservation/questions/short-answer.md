# Confronting Marginalisation Rights Laws and Reservation — short answer

**Answer: Ch 8 Confronting Marginalisation — marginalised groups invoke Fundamental Rights (Articles 15 and 17), win protective laws like the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989, and claim reservation in education and jobs (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: India's way — the book's own teaching way — The Constitution is the spine of the whole book — every later institution (Parliament, courts, police) and every injustice (marginalisation) is measured against it, and marginalised people are shown invoking its Fundamental Rights to force change.
