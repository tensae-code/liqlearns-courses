# Video script — Confronting Marginalisation Rights Laws and Reservation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India Grade 8 (student/teacher: STUDENT) Social and Political Life-III (printed p. 92), teaches: marginalised groups invoke Fundamental Rights (Articles 15 and 17), win protective laws like the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act 1989, and claim reservation in education and jobs.

India's own way: The Constitution is the spine of the whole book — every later institution (Parliament, courts, police) and every injustice (marginalisation) is measured against it, and marginalised people are shown invoking its Fundamental Rights to force change.
