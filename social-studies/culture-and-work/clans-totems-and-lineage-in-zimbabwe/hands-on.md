# Hands-on — Clans Totems and Lineage in Zimbabwe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a clan descends from a common ancestor and is united by its totem (an animal, bird, snake, water or ancestor name chosen for a quality like the elephant's size or the lion's bravery); clan members share one blood, may not marry within the clan in some cultures, and trace patrilineal or matrilineal lineage to the founding ancestor. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
