Zimbabwe's own way (Book 13): the College Press learner's book opens the nation with the clan — a clan is people of one common ancestor kept united by a shared totem (Mhofu the eland, Shumba the lion, Nzou the elephant, Moyo the heart), which it is taboo to eat, with patrilineal and matrilineal lineages traced across the whole country.
