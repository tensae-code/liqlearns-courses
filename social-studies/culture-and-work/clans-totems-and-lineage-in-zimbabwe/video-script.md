Zimbabwe Grade 6 zw-ventures-g6 Ventures Primary Heritage-Social Studies Learner's Book Grade 6 (printed pp. 2–5 (reader copy)), teaches: A clan descends from a common ancestor and is united by its totem (an animal, bird, snake, water or ancestor name chosen for a quality like the elephant's size or the lion's bravery); clan members share one blood, may not marry within the clan in some cultures, and trace patrilineal or matrilineal lineage to the founding ancestor.

Zimbabwe's own way (Book 13): the College Press learner's book opens the nation with the clan — a clan is people of one common ancestor kept united by a shared totem (Mhofu the eland, Shumba the lion, Nzou the elephant, Moyo the heart), which it is taboo to eat, with patrilineal and matrilineal lineages traced across the whole country.
