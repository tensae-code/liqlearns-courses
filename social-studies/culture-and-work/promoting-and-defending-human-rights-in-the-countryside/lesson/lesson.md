## Sub lesson 1 — Módulo 5 framing La promoción y defensa de los Derechos Humanos

Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 152–161, teaches: human rights are "the set of characteristics or attributes of human beings that cannot be affected or violated, such as their life and integrity"; the module studies how they are promoted and defended in the rural context, through the exercise of rights by campesino communities and the movements that carried them. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 25) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
