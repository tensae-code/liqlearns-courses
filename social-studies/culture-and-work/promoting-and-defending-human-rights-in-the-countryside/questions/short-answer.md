# Promoting and Defending Human Rights in the Countryside — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Módulo 5 framing La promoción y defensa de los Derechos Humanos — Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 152–161, teaches: human rights are "the set of characteristics or attributes of human beings that cannot be affected or violated, such as their life and integrity"; the module studies how they are promoted and defended in the rural context, through the exercise of rights by campesino communities and the movements that carried them. **2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Colombia's way (Book 25) — the book's own teaching way — Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims.
