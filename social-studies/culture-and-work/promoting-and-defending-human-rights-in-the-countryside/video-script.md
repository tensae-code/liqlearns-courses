# Video script — Promoting and Defending Human Rights in the Countryside

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 152–161, teaches: human rights are "the set of characteristics or attributes of human beings that cannot be affected or violated, such as their life and integrity"; the module studies how they are promoted and defended in the rural context, through the exercise of rights by campesino communities and the movements that carried them.

Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims.
