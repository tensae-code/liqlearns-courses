# Hands-on — Promoting and Defending Human Rights in the Countryside

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Módulo 5 framing La promoción y defensa de los Derechos Humanos
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 152–161, teaches: human rights are "the set of characteristics or attributes of human beings that cannot be affected or violated, such as their life and integrity"; the module studies how they are promoted and defended in the rural context, through the exercise of rights by campesino communities and the movements that carried them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 25) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
