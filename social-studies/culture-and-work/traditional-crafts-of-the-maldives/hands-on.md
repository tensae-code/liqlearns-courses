# Hands-on — Traditional Crafts of the Maldives

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Crafts handed down the generations — Liyelaa Jehun lacquer work of Thulhaadhoo, Thundu Kunaa mats of Gadhdoo, thatch weaving (fangi vinun), coir-rope making, boat building and kasabu embroidery — and why they must be valued and revived. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Maldives's way
Say how Maldives's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
