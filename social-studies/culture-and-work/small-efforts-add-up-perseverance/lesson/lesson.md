# Small Efforts Add Up Perseverance

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: perseverance through the story of Ninomiya Kinjiro, who studied while he worked — as Japan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — 小さな努力のつみかさね (Small efforts pile up)

Japan Grades 1–2 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 28), teaches: Perseverance through the story of Ninomiya Kinjiro, who studied while he worked. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — 読みもの「まんががすき ―まんが『サザエさん』を作った長谷川町子―」("She Loved Manga — Hasegawa Machiko, who made 'Sazae-san'")

Japan Grades 1–2 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 162), teaches: Machiko drew pictures from childhood, was told her drawing had real power, created Sazae-san from a walk by the sea, and kept drawing the manga she loved all her life (reading). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Japan's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 1): MEXT's own moral reader for the youngest pupils is built from short stories and picture tales around four value topics per unit — orderly living, doing your own work, doing willingly what is right, and honesty — each story followed by talk-it-over pages for school, home and neighbourhood. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
