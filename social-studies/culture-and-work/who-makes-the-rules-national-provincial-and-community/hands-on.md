# Hands-on — Who Makes the Rules National Provincial and Community

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A new community must agree its needs, rights and rules; national laws are made by elected national representatives, local laws by elected provincial representatives, community rules by the chief or elected community representatives — and electing representatives gives everyone a say; rules must be fair for women, the young, the old and disabled people. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vanuatu's way
Say how Vanuatu's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
