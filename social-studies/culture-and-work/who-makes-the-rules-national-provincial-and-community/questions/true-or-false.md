# Who Makes the Rules National Provincial and Community — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: A new community must agree its needs, rights and rules; national laws are made by elected national representatives, local laws by elected provincial representatives, community rules by the chief or elected community representatives — and electing representatives gives everyone a say; rules must be fair for women, the young, the old and disabled people.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Vanuatu's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
