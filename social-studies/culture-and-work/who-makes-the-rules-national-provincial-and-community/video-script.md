# Video script — Who Makes the Rules National Provincial and Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vanuatu Citoyenneté Sociale Guide Years 1–6 (printed p. 27), teaches: A new community must agree its needs, rights and rules; national laws are made by elected national representatives, local laws by elected provincial representatives, community rules by the chief or elected community representatives — and electing representatives gives everyone a say; rules must be fair for women, the young, the old and disabled people.

Vanuatu's own way: social citizenship is taught as roles before rules — the Year 1 child maps who they are in family and village (chief, pastor, nurse, police, women's and youth group leaders), learns that roles change and are not fixed by gender, and only in Years 5–6 reaches rights, and how a community's rules are made and by whom.
