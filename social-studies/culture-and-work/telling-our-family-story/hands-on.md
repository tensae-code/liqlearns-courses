# Hands-on — Telling Our Family Story

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Grandparents tell the family history at a family gathering, and the grandchildren ask to be taught the old folk dance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
