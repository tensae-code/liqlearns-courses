# Hands-on — Work That Produces and Work That Serves

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Производне и непроизводне делатности (Productive and non-productive activities)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: work divides in two — productive work makes things (agriculture, industry, mining, construction, crafts) and non-productive work serves people (education, health, culture, science, and the services: trade, transport, tourism, hospitality). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
