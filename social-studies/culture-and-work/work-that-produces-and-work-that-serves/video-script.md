# Video script — Work That Produces and Work That Serves

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed p. 30), teaches: Work divides in two — productive work makes things (agriculture, industry, mining, construction, crafts) and non-productive work serves people (education, health, culture, science, and the services: trade, transport, tourism, hospitality).

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
