# Work That Produces and Work That Serves

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: work divides in two — productive work makes things (agriculture, industry, mining, construction, crafts) and non-productive work serves people (education, health, culture, science, and the services: trade, transport, tourism, hospitality) — as Serbia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Производне и непроизводне делатности (Productive and non-productive activities)

Serbia Grade 3 Природа и друштво 3 (printed p. 30), teaches: work divides in two — productive work makes things (agriculture, industry, mining, construction, crafts) and non-productive work serves people (education, health, culture, science, and the services: trade, transport, tourism, hospitality). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Serbia's way — the books' own teaching way

Serbia's own way: the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
