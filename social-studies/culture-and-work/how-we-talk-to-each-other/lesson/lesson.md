## Sub lesson 2 — North Macedonia's way — the books' own teaching way

North Macedonia's own way (Book 1): Society in Grade 2 is the child in three widening circles — school, home, settlement — and already runs a miniature democracy: the class community elects its president by secret ballot, sits in a pupil parliament, and learns decision-making, equality and respect as daily school practice, in a multi-ethnic classroom cast (Macedonian, Albanian and other names throughout). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
