# Hands-on — Ley 263 Against Human Trafficking

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: telling trafficking in persons (a crime against people: capture, transport and exploitation by force, fraud or abuse of vulnerability — 14 offence types under Ley 263, 10–15 years) from migrant smuggling (a crime against the State), the purposes of trafficking, how victims are recruited (false job offers, social networks, informal agencies), the rules for employment agencies, and concrete self-protection advice. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
