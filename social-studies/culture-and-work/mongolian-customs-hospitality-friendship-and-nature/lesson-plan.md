# Lesson plan — Mongolian Customs Hospitality Friendship And Nature

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Mongolian Customs Hospitality Friendship And Nature
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Mongolia, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Mongolian Customs Hospitality Friendship And Nature — True friendship through Temüjin and Bo'orchu, who shared danger and refused reward, and through the tale of the dog, cat and mouse, whose friendship envy and stealing destroyed; my own friend, how we help each other, and how saying sorry keeps a friendship — as Mongolia's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — Mongolian Customs Hospitality Friendship And Nature — True friendship through Temüjin and Bo'orchu, who shared danger and refused reward, and through the tale of the dog, cat and mouse, whose friendship envy and stealing destroyed; my own friend, how we help each other, and how saying sorry keeps a friendship.
