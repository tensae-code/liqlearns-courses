# Hands-on — Children the Apple of the Familys Eye

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Ch. 4 Children — the Apple of the Family's Eye — teaches: children are the family's treasure — the long story of two brothers shows parents spending their strength, savings and prayers on their children, and children owing love and care in return. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Pakistan's way
Say how Pakistan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
