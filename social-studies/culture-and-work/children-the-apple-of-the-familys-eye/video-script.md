Pakistan's Grade 6 Ethics6 (printed pp. 34–36), teaches: Ch. 4 Children — the Apple of the Family's Eye — teaches: children are the family's treasure — the long story of two brothers shows parents spending their strength, savings and prayers on their children, and children owing love and care in return.

Pakistan's own way: the senior primary Ethics book turns values into civic machinery — laws learned from a flock of birds, time priced like money, traffic signs taught as a citizen's duty — and opens with story after story where greed ruins kings and gratitude saves the lame.
