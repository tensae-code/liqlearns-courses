# Hands-on — Social Interaction Contact and Communication

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Tema I F. Interaksi Sosial, Tema I F.2 Bentuk-Bentuk Interaksi Sosial, Panduan Khusus Tema I D. Penyajian Materi Esensial Tema I — teaches: social interaction and its two conditions — social contact (direct or through media) and communication (verbal and nonverbal); the forms of social interaction — associative (cooperation, accommodation) and dissociative (competition, conflict), with interaction in the market as picture case; essential material for Tema I, including the social-interaction content — conditions and forms of interaction. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
