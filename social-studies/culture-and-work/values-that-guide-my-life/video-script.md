# Video script — Values That Guide My Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dominican Republic — Moral Cívica 6° Primaria student guide (printed p. 76), teaches: Making a list of the values that guide my life and reflecting on how values improve my relations with myself and with others.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 5): the primary cycle closes by widening the circle — from how my community is organised and who organises it (juntas de vecinos, authorities, institutions), through tax duties written into the Constitution and the Judicial Power, to the pupil as an "ambassador of peace" in home, school and community.
