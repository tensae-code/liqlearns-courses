# Hands-on — Our Cultural Heritage

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: heritage is tangible (sites such as Troy) and intangible (traditions such as the whistled "bird language"); UNESCO's lists hold 21 tangible and 30 intangible elements from Turkey, and protecting, keeping alive and handing on the common heritage is the responsibility of all humanity, shared in Turkey by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism and local bodies Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
