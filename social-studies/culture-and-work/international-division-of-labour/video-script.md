Armenia's book, the *Armenia Grade 7 Geography student* (printed p. 138), teaches: Lesson 7.2 International geographic division of labour. Armenia's book, the *Armenia Grade 9 Geography of Armenia student* (printed p. 137), teaches: §52 The Republic of Armenia in the international division of labour. Armenia's book, the *Armenia Grade 11 Geography student* (printed p. 17), teaches: §3 Geographic division of labour: territorial structure of the world economy.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 7 Geography student): opens school geography with the geographic map itself (plan, scale, azimuth, projections) before any Earth science, and runs every theme to a summary lesson plus practical work; world economy is taught branch by branch with Armenia (ՀՀ) used as the worked example in the closing questions.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 9 Geography of Armenia student): the whole grade-9 year is the home country — first the Armenian Highlands as a physical whole, then the Republic section by section (nature, population, economy), finishing marz by marz (all 10 provinces) so every learner ends by studying their own province.

Armenia's own way (Armenia Grade 11 Geography student): the senior Geography course is two big themes only — the world economy in depth (structure, division of labour, every branch, transport systems, trade) and then the environment (ecological balance, pollution of air and water, land degradation, biodiversity, ecosystem services), i.e. economy first, its ecological bill second.
