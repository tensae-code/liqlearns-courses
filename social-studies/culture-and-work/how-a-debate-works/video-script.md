# Video script — How a Debate Works

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 5 P5 Social Studies SB, printed p. 52, teaches: a debate has a topic, proposers and opposers, time for research, set speaking order and time, and a judgment — debaters address the audience, watch their time, and there should be a fair judge who listens to both sides; the class debate topic is that a boy and a girl should be treated equally.

South Sudan's own way: Citizenship is taught as a sung list of duties — loyalty, voting, peace, defending the country, paying taxes, obeying laws, helping in disasters — and leadership is measured against citizenship itself: to be a good leader a person must first be a good citizen, and the President is named as the example of both.
