# Hands-on — How a Debate Works

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activities 6–7 Debate and essentials of an effective debate
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: a debate has a topic, proposers and opposers, time for research, set speaking order and time, and a judgment — debaters address the audience, watch their time, and there should be a fair judge who listens to both sides; the class debate topic is that a boy and a girl should be treated equally. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
