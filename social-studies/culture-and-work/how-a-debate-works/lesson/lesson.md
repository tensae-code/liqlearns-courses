South Sudan's own way: Citizenship is taught as a sung list of duties — loyalty, voting, peace, defending the country, paying taxes, obeying laws, helping in disasters — and leadership is measured against citizenship itself: to be a good leader a person must first be a good citizen, and the President is named as the example of both.
