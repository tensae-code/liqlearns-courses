# What the Flag Colours Mean — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The meanings of the colours of the flag of the United Arab Emirates — the flag's colours taken from al-Hilli's verse and glossed one by one — white: good deeds and giving, and the state's stand for security and peace in the world; green: growth, prosperity, the green environment and the cultural renaissance in the state; red: the sacrifices of the generation that founded the Union and the martyrs who defended the homeland; black: the strength of the country's sons, their dignity, and the rejection of injustice and extremism (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — every teacher guide opens by teaching the flag before any lesson — its colours are glossed from the poet Safi al-Din al-Hilli's famous verse ("white are our deeds, green our meadows, black our battles, red our swords"), recast for the federation: white for good deeds and giving, green for growth and renaissance, red for the founders' and martyrs' sacrifices, black for the people's strength and the rejection of injustice and extremism. (any close wording).**
