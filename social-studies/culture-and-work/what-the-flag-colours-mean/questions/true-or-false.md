# What the Flag Colours Mean — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches: the flag's colours taken from al-Hilli's verse and glossed one by one — white: good deeds and giving, and the state's stand for security and peace in the world; green: growth, prosperity, the green environment and the cultural renaissance in the state; red: the sacrifices of the generation that founded the Union and the martyrs who defended the homeland; black: the strength of the country's sons, their dignity, and the rejection of injustice and extremism
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches: every teacher guide opens by teaching the flag before any lesson — its colours are glossed from the poet Safi al-Din al-Hilli's famous verse ("white are our deeds, green our meadows, black our battles, red our swords"), recast for the federation: white for good deeds and giving, green for growth and renaissance, red for the founders' and martyrs' sacrifices, black for the people's strength and the rejection of injustice and extremism.
**Answer: True**
