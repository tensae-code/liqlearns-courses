# Hands-on — What the Flag Colours Mean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The meanings of the colours of the flag of the United Arab Emirates
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the flag's colours taken from al-Hilli's verse and glossed one by one — white: good deeds and giving, and the state's stand for security and peace in the world; green: growth, prosperity, the green environment and the cultural renaissance in the state; red: the sacrifices of the generation that founded the Union and the martyrs who defended the homeland; black: the strength of the country's sons, their dignity, and the rejection of injustice and extremism Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
