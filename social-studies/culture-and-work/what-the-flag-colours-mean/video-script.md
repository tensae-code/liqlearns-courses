# Video script — What the Flag Colours Mean

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G9 teacher guide, Term 1 — front-matter fragment, printed p. 5, teaches: the flag's colours taken from al-Hilli's verse and glossed one by one — white: good deeds and giving, and the state's stand for security and peace in the world; green: growth, prosperity, the green environment and the cultural renaissance in the state; red: the sacrifices of the generation that founded the Union and the martyrs who defended the homeland; black: the strength of the country's sons, their dignity, and the rejection of injustice and extremism

United Arab Emirates' own way: every teacher guide opens by teaching the flag before any lesson — its colours are glossed from the poet Safi al-Din al-Hilli's famous verse ("white are our deeds, green our meadows, black our battles, red our swords"), recast for the federation: white for good deeds and giving, green for growth and renaissance, red for the founders' and martyrs' sacrifices, black for the people's strength and the rejection of injustice and extremism.
