## Sub lesson 1 — Nationally Celebrated Weeks: Fire Prevention Week

Guyana Grade 4 Social Studies For Our Children Book 4 (printed p. 54), teaches: Every October — how fires start (carelessness, faulty wires), fire drills, checking extinguishers and escapes, and why countryside kitchens are built apart. ## Sub lesson 2 — Guyana's way — the book's own teaching way

Guyana's own way: Book 4 turns from "my community" to "our homeland" — Guyana is taught as six peoples who each brought a way of life, and citizenship is taught through honour (National Awards) and shared observance (Road Safety, Fire Prevention and Youth Weeks) before it is taught through rules. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
