# Hands-on — Fire Prevention Week and Fire Safety

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Every October — how fires start (carelessness, faulty wires), fire drills, checking extinguishers and escapes, and why countryside kitchens are built apart. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guyana's way
Say how Guyana's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
