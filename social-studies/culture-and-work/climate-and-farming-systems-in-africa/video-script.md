South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies SB* (printed p. 52), teaches: Compare farming methods in Sudan, Ethiopia, Kenya and Uganda with those of South Sudan. South Sudan's book, the *South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies TG* (printed p. 33), teaches: Guide: Unit 3 — farming systems in South Sudan and selected neighbouring countries; the system that best suits your local area depends on climatic conditions. (teacher guide — evidence)

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 5 P5 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide insists on both sides of every feature — positive and negative effects of physical features on human activities — and on human agency: dumping material in a valley can cause flooding, and climate dictates which farming system a region of Africa can practise. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies SB): The climate unit is two-way and practical — how climate shapes people's activities (poor climate ruins roads) and how people adapt: use scarce water efficiently, build flood defences and raise dykes, grow drought-tolerant crops, and make biogas.

South Sudan's own way (South Sudan Grade 8 P8 Social Studies TG) (teacher guide — evidence): The guide keeps climate personal and economic — which climate would you choose to live in, and why — and ties the farming system that suits a local area directly to its climatic conditions. That book is a teacher guide, cited as evidence only; South Sudan is counted in this lesson for its student book(s) named here, not for this guide.
