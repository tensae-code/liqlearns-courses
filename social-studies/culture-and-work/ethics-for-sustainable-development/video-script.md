# Video script — Ethics for Sustainable Development

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mexico Grade 8 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 2 (secundaria) (printed pp. 283–292), teaches: Ethics enters the decisions of obtaining, transforming, distributing and consuming goods — humans as part of nature, not its owners — and the values (respect, solidarity, responsibility) and alternatives that keep natural and social balance together.

---

Mexico Grade 9 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 3 (secundaria) (printed pp. 283–292), teaches: Ethical principles for sustainable development — present wellbeing without risking future life; collaboration, reciprocity, solidarity and equal participation that keep the natural balance (first placed from Grade 8 in this wave).

---

Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality. Mexico's own way (Grade 9): the third year turns the course outward to action — participation spaces for children and adolescents, community projects for democratic culture, and democracy's own challenges (corruption, violence, disenchantment) named as things the young citizen is expected to work on.
