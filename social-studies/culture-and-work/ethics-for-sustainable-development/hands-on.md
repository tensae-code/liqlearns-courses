# Hands-on — Ethics for Sustainable Development

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ethics for Sustainable Development (Grade 8)
Work through this part with your hands: ethics enters the decisions of obtaining, transforming, distributing and consuming goods — humans as part of nature, not its owners — and the values (respect, solidarity, responsibility) and alternatives that keep natural and social balance together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Ethical Principles for Sustainable Development (Grade 9)
Work through this part with your hands: ethical principles for sustainable development — present wellbeing without risking future life; collaboration, reciprocity, solidarity and equal participation that keep the natural balance (first placed from Grade 8 in this wave). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
