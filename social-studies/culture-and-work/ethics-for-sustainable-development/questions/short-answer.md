# Ethics for Sustainable Development — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ethics for Sustainable Development (Grade 8) — ethics enters the decisions of obtaining, transforming, distributing and consuming goods — humans as part of nature, not its owners — and the values (respect, solidarity, responsibility) and alternatives that keep natural and social balance together (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Ethical Principles for Sustainable Development (Grade 9) — ethical principles for sustainable development — present wellbeing without risking future life; collaboration, reciprocity, solidarity and equal participation that keep the natural balance (first placed from Grade 8 in this wave) (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What does the third part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way — Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality. Mexico's own way (Grade 9): the third year turns the course outward to action — participation spaces for children and adolescents, community projects for democratic culture, and democracy's own challenges (corruption, violence, disenchantment) named as things the young citizen is expected to work on.
