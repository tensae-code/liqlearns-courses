# Ethics for Sustainable Development

## Sub lesson 1 — Ethics for Sustainable Development (Grade 8)

Mexico Grade 8 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 2 (secundaria) (printed pp. 283–292), teaches: Ethics enters the decisions of obtaining, transforming, distributing and consuming goods — humans as part of nature, not its owners — and the values (respect, solidarity, responsibility) and alternatives that keep natural and social balance together. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ethical Principles for Sustainable Development (Grade 9)

Mexico Grade 9 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades 3 (secundaria) (printed pp. 283–292), teaches: Ethical principles for sustainable development — present wellbeing without risking future life; collaboration, reciprocity, solidarity and equal participation that keep the natural balance (first placed from Grade 8 in this wave). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Mexico's way — the book's own teaching way

Mexico's own way (Grade 8): the second year turns from living together to judging and acting — pupils weigh ethical judgements on real injustice, learn how laws are made and changed in Mexico's own Congress, practise mediation, and end as watchdogs: citizen participation is taught as the duty to see public money administered with honesty, transparency and legality. Mexico's own way (Grade 9): the third year turns the course outward to action — participation spaces for children and adolescents, community projects for democratic culture, and democracy's own challenges (corruption, violence, disenchantment) named as things the young citizen is expected to work on. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
