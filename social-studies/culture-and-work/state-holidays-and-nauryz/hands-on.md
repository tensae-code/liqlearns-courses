# Hands-on — State Holidays and Nauryz

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Kazakhstan's state and national holidays — Nauryz, Independence Day on 16 December, the Day of Unity of the People of Kazakhstan on 1 May — and how holidays are divided into national, state and professional. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kazakhstan's way
Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
