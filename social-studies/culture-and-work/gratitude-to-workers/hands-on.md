# Hands-on — Gratitude to Workers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what the workers around us give (the night sweeper's clean street, the farmer's rice, the driver's safe ride, the doctor's care) and how gratitude is shown — greeting the cleaner, never wasting the rice others grew, thanking the repairman, helping the school cook whose cart has tipped, and honouring manual work as much as any other. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
