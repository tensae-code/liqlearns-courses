# Gratitude to Workers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Be grateful to working people — what the workers around us give (the night sweeper's clean street, the farmer's rice, the driver's safe ride, the doctor's care) and how gratitude is shown — greeting the cleaner, never wasting the rice others grew, thanking the repairman, helping the school cook whose cart has tipped, and honouring manual work as much as any other (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way — Grade 4 turns outward to society's work and property: gratitude is owed to every worker including the night street-sweeper of Tố Hữu's poem; "public property" (của công) is named as a legal-moral category of its own — what belongs to everyone is guarded by everyone — and the book closes with the child's place in law itself: the basic rights and duties of children, including a children's forum where pupils speak. (any close wording).**
