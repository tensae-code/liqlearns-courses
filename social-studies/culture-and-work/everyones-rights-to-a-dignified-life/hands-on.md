# Hands-on — Everyones Rights to a Dignified Life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the fundamental rights for a dignified life — work, education, health and recreation, and women's rights — enjoyed without discrimination of race or sex; human rights as a lived reality in Cuba, including the Declaration of the Rights of the Child; Cuban children's lives compared with children's in other countries. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
