# Video script — Everyones Rights to a Dignified Life

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 50–58), teaches: The fundamental rights for a dignified life — work, education, health and recreation, and women's rights — enjoyed without discrimination of race or sex; human rights as a lived reality in Cuba, including the Declaration of the Rights of the Child; Cuban children's lives compared with children's in other countries.

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors).
