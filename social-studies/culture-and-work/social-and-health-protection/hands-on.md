# Hands-on — Social and Health Protection

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how the state protects society and health — the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development protects women and children from violence and abuse, runs care and observation homes for the young, receives abuse reports and fights begging; the Ministry of Health serves through primary-care centres in the neighbourhoods, hospitals and great medical cities, and the Saudi Red Crescent provides emergency ambulance care. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
