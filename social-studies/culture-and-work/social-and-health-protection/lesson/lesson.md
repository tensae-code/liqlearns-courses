# Social and Health Protection

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: how the state protects society and health — the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development protects women and children from violence and abuse, runs care and observation homes for the young, receives abuse reports and fights begging; the Ministry of Health serves through primary-care centres in the neighbourhoods, hospitals and great medical cities, and the Saudi Red Crescent provides emergency ambulance care — as Saudi Arabia's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Social and Health Protection

Saudi Arabia Grade 5 Social Studies (printed pp. 112–114), teaches: How the state protects society and health — the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development protects women and children from violence and abuse, runs care and observation homes for the young, receives abuse reports and fights begging; the Ministry of Health serves through primary-care centres in the neighbourhoods, hospitals and great medical cities, and the Saudi Red Crescent provides emergency ambulance care. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 5 book teaches security and state institutions as service — security is defined first, the citizen is named the first line of security, the security agencies are listed one by one with their tasks, state institutions are split into public and private, and each region's Emir receives citizens and hears their needs. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
