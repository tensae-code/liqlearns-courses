# Video script — The People Choose Their Leaders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cuba Grade 5 Educación Ciudadana OM (printed pp. 40–44), teaches: Sovereignty resides in the people (Constitution arts. 1, 3), who elect their rulers freely; delegates answer to their electors in accountability assemblies; the mass and student organisations of Cuban society — the pioneers' own OPJM with its motto, school structure and elected leaders, the CDR and FMC, the Communist Party as leading political organisation (art. 5) and the UJC (art. 6).

Cuba's own way: citizenship is taught through the child's own pioneer organisation and through the 2019 Constitution article by article — family duties, equality and the mass organisations are all read straight out of constitutional text, and the state is presented as the people's own power (sovereignty in the people, delegates answerable to their electors).
