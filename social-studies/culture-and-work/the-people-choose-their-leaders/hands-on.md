# Hands-on — The People Choose Their Leaders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: sovereignty resides in the people (Constitution arts. 1, 3), who elect their rulers freely; delegates answer to their electors in accountability assemblies; the mass and student organisations of Cuban society — the pioneers' own OPJM with its motto, school structure and elected leaders, the CDR and FMC, the Communist Party as leading political organisation (art. 5) and the UJC (art. 6). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Cuba's way
Say how Cuba's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
