# Laws That Govern a Community — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part teaches: laws are a system of rules used to direct someone on what to do and what not to do; each community has laws that govern them, moral laws guide us on how to behave, breaking laws leads to arrest and the court, and class and school rules matter for the same reason.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This part teaches: the guide's own law lists and answers — people are arrested for stealing, rape, corruption and murder; class rules help us concentrate, keep a clean environment, work hard and respect teachers; and the laws in the communities and the states are the same, among them taking no one's property without permission and respect for the constitution (the laws lesson is filed NEW from Book 7 in this wave).
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This part teaches: just as a school has rules, a country has laws; citizens who break laws are taken to court and given a chance to defend themselves; when people obey the laws there is order in a country, and the government employs police and other leaders, with the village chief's role examined, to keep law and order so people feel safe (the laws lesson is filed NEW from Book 7 in this wave).
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on South Sudan's student book(s), named in lesson/sources.md, with teacher-guide parts as evidence only.**
