# Hands-on — Deciding Without Bias

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Rational, impartial decisions from two case studies — a photographer who withdraws prize-winning photos rather than show her country only in poverty, and a doctor torn between his sick mother and an emergency patient — weighing a situation without bias and giving up small personal gain for the greater good. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
