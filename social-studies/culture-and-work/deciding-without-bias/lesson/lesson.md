## Sub lesson 1 — Deciding Without Bias

Bangladesh Grade 9 BD G9-10 HSS (printed pp. 115–116), teaches: Rational, impartial decisions from two case studies — a photographer who withdraws prize-winning photos rather than show her country only in poverty, and a doctor torn between his sick mother and an emergency patient — weighing a situation without bias and giving up small personal gain for the greater good. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bangladesh's way — the book's own teaching way

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 9–10): Civics becomes a full field manual — pupils questionnaire a real Union Parishad chairman and their own headteacher, then learn the whole local-government ladder tier by tier with membership counts, and meet the United Nations through Bangladesh's own seat in it (Bangabandhu's 1974 Bengali speech) before the Charter is summarised.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
