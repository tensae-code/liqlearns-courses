# Video script — Caribbean Coast Autonomy Nicaragua

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nicaragua Grade 3 Estudios Sociales G3 (printed p. 98), teaches: Nicaragua's own autonomy system — the Miskito, Sumo and Rama peoples' right to govern themselves by their own customs in the Caribbean Coast regions.

Nicaragua's own way: community is taught through Rama Cay — an ancestral Caribbean island community governed by a council of elders — and citizenship through the current government's named municipal social programmes and the Caribbean Coast autonomy system.
