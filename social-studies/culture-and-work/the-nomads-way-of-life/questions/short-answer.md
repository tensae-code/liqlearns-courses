# The Nomads Way of Life — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Reer Guuraaga (The Nomads) — the nomadic way of life — moving with the herds after water and pasture — as one of the nation's own ways of living, to be understood by townsman and farmer children (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of the lesson teach?
**Answer: Somalia's way — the books' own teaching way — Grade 3 turns from people to the working nation — its lessons are built on daily experience and field visits (to farms, cooperatives and self-help workplaces), because a pupil of this age learns society by visiting it; politics is taught as one of the three growth directions of the pupil, alongside the social and the economic.
