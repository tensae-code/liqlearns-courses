# The Nomads Way of Life — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The part of this lesson called "Reer Guuraaga (The Nomads)" is mainly about —
a) the nomadic way of life — moving with the herds after water and pasture — as one of the nation's own ways of living, to be understood by townsman and farmer children  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) the nomadic way of life — moving with the herds after water and pasture — as one of the nation's own ways of living, to be understood by townsman and farmer children**
