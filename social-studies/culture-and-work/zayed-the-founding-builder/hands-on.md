# Hands-on — Zayed the Founding Builder

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Towering glimpses of Sheikh Zayed in events the Arab homeland lived
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the values the chapter itself names — equality, cohesion and solidarity, joint Arab action, sacrifice, justice — shown in action: standing with Kuwait until its liberation and return to the GCC's march, the October 1973 war stand, and refusing to let the embargo on Iraq starve its people while rejecting its occupation of Kuwait Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zayed the father
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sheikh Zayed as the father of the nation — the founder whose portrait opens the school year, presented to first-graders as the country's father and the source of its values of giving and building Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zayed and the Emirati woman
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how the environment shaped the Emirati woman's character in the old days; the roles women played before the Union and valuing them; the main factors that changed women's social place after the Union; and the roles Emirati women play today — a society's change read through its women Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## The founding builder
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Sheikh Zayed as the ruler of Al Ain — his efforts in Al Ain and Abu Dhabi before the Union and after it; his roles and responsibilities as President of the state; the Union proclaimed in 1971 of seven emirates; the state's emblem, the falcon (الصقر); and what he built for the people — schools, hospitals and housing — the founder learned as the homeland's builder Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Zayed — a civilisation and achievements
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the founding builder's life in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi and his role in building the Union; his role in developing the state and guaranteeing the people's welfare (رفاهية الشعب); good citizenship (المواطنة الصالحة) explained as holding fast to virtuous values and principles; and the world appreciation (التقدير العالمي) the founder won in the field of the environment Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## United Arab Emirates' way — the books' own teaching way
Say how United Arab Emirates's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
