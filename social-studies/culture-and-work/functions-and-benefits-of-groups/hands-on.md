# Hands-on — Functions and Benefits of Groups

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Groups have common goals, clearly defined status, shared values and common symbols such as uniforms; their functions are interdependence for survival, security and belonging, shared experience, and social control — the individual conforms to the group's rules, bringing order in society; joining groups lets members practise justice, loyalty and cooperation and develop acceptable attitudes and values. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
