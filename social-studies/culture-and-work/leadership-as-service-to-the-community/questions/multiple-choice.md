# Leadership as Service to the Community — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Paglilingkod sa Komunidad  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
a) Epekto ng Pamumuno at Paglilingkod sa Komunidad  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson is mainly about —
