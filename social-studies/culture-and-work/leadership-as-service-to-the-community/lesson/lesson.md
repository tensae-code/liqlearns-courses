# Leadership as Service to the Community

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: leadership as service — what serving the community means and how a leader's service answers people's needs — as Philippines's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Paglilingkod sa Komunidad

Philippines Grade 2 AP2 Tagalog Unit 3 (printed p. 187), teaches: Leadership as service — what serving the community means and how a leader's service answers people's needs. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Epekto ng Pamumuno at Paglilingkod sa Komunidad

Philippines Grade 2 AP2 Tagalog Unit 3 (printed p. 196), teaches: The effects of good leadership and service on the community — unity, cooperation and development follow from a leader who serves. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 3 — Philippines's way — the book's own teaching way

Philippines's own way: Grade 2 social studies is organised by community ("Buhay Komunidad"): work, leadership and service are taught inside the child's own barangay-scale community, with leaders drawn from every part of it (barangay captain, school principal, priest/pastor/imam, doctor, parents). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
