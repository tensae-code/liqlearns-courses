# Video script — Leadership as Service to the Community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Philippines Grade 2 AP2 Tagalog Unit 3 (printed p. 187), teaches: Leadership as service — what serving the community means and how a leader's service answers people's needs.

Philippines's own way: Grade 2 social studies is organised by community ("Buhay Komunidad"): work, leadership and service are taught inside the child's own barangay-scale community, with leaders drawn from every part of it (barangay captain, school principal, priest/pastor/imam, doctor, parents).
