# Loving and Caring for Other People

## Do it first

Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Love people

Vietnam Grade 6 Giáo dục công dân 6, printed pp. 9–12, teaches: Love of people as care, help and good deeds for others, above all for those in difficulty — shown in words, deeds and attitude at home, at school and in society: the seven-year-old whose donated corneas gave sight to two strangers, the charity programmes that carry the poor and the disabled, forgiving a fault once it is mended, and giving up your own interest for another when needed. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way

Vietnam's own way: citizenship is taught straight from the statute book — the Nationality Law decides who is a citizen case by case, the 2013 Constitution's Chapter II is read group by group (political, civil, economic, cultural-social rights, then duties), and children's rights arrive through the UN Convention and the 2016 Children Law in four groups: survival, protection, development, participation. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
