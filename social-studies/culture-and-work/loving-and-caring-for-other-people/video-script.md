# Video script — Loving and Caring for Other People

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 6 Giáo dục công dân 6, printed pp. 9–12, teaches: Love of people as care, help and good deeds for others, above all for those in difficulty — shown in words, deeds and attitude at home, at school and in society: the seven-year-old whose donated corneas gave sight to two strangers, the charity programmes that carry the poor and the disabled, forgiving a fault once it is mended, and giving up your own interest for another when needed.

Vietnam's own way: citizenship is taught straight from the statute book — the Nationality Law decides who is a citizen case by case, the 2013 Constitution's Chapter II is read group by group (political, civil, economic, cultural-social rights, then duties), and children's rights arrive through the UN Convention and the 2016 Children Law in four groups: survival, protection, development, participation.
