# Hands-on — Loving and Caring for Other People

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: love of people as care, help and good deeds for others, above all for those in difficulty — shown in words, deeds and attitude at home, at school and in society: the seven-year-old whose donated corneas gave sight to two strangers, the charity programmes that carry the poor and the disabled, forgiving a fault once it is mended, and giving up your own interest for another when needed. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
