# Loving and Caring for Other People — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Love people — love of people as care, help and good deeds for others, above all for those in difficulty — shown in words, deeds and attitude at home, at school and in society: the seven-year-old whose donated corneas gave sight to two strangers, the charity programmes that carry the poor and the disabled, forgiving a fault once it is mended, and giving up your own interest for another when needed (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Vietnam's way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship is taught straight from the statute book — the Nationality Law decides who is a citizen case by case, the 2013 Constitution's Chapter II is read group by group (political, civil, economic, cultural-social rights, then duties), and children's rights arrive through the UN Convention and the 2016 Children Law in four groups: survival, protection, development, participation. (any close wording).**
