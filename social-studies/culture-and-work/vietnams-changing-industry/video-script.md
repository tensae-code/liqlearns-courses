Vietnam's book, the *Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12* (printed pp. 70–73), teaches: Bài 16: How Vietnam's industry is shifting — by branch (manufacturing up to 91.7% of industrial output value in 2024, mining down to 3.2%), by ownership (the 2010 and 2024 pies) and by territory (the Southeast at 34.9% and the Red River Delta at 36.5% of the nation's industry in 2024; fast FDI growth in Bac Ninh, Thai Nguyen and Quang Ngai) — toward green industry by 2030 and 2045.

Vietnam's own way (Vietnam Grade 12 Địa lí 12): the school course ends with a full portrait of Vietnam itself — its monsoon nature and how it varies, its people, each economic sector, each of its regions, and its seas and islands — all carried on the newest statistics (2024 yearbook figures, and the 34-province map in force from 2025).
