The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 186–221 (Módulo 6), teaches: identity is a collective construction — communities appropriate and re-signify expressions, traditions and customs across time as part of who they are, with particular elements in rural Colombian contexts; and fundamental rights stand above gender, political affiliation, religion and ethnicity.

Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims.
