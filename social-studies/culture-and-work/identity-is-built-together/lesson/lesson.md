## Sub lesson 1 — Módulo 6 Guía 18 La identidad: una construcción colectiva

Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 186–221 (Módulo 6), teaches: identity is a collective construction — communities appropriate and re-signify expressions, traditions and customs across time as part of who they are, with particular elements in rural Colombian contexts; and fundamental rights stand above gender, political affiliation, religion and ethnicity. ## Sub lesson 2 — Colombia's way (Book 25) — the book's own teaching way

Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
