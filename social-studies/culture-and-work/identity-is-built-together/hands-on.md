# Hands-on — Identity is Built Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Módulo 6 Guía 18 La identidad: una construcción colectiva
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 9 CS_Grado9, printed pp. 186–221 (Módulo 6), teaches: identity is a collective construction — communities appropriate and re-signify expressions, traditions and customs across time as part of who they are, with particular elements in rural Colombian contexts; and fundamental rights stand above gender, political affiliation, religion and ethnicity. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 25) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: human rights are taught from the countryside outward — defined as attributes no one may violate, then traced through the movements that defended them (students, unions, campesino communities), and identity is taught as something a rural community builds together in its festivals, traditions and rights claims. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
