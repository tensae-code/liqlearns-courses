# Video script — How I Relate to My Groups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 7 Social Studies, printed p. 10, teaches: Relations inside groups are formal (албан — by set rules) and informal (албан бус — by closeness), and a person must respect others, bear duty and responsibility, and reckon with the results of their acts; four group portraits are analysed — a school football team with its strict federation rules, a beloved Mongolian-language lesson that must not disturb other lessons, a youth police group (өсвөрийн цагдаа) recruited through the local police, and a family run by the parents' word as its law.

Mongolia's own way (Book 5): the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work.
