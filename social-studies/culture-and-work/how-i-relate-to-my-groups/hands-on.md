# Hands-on — How I Relate to My Groups

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.2 "Relations between the individual and social groups"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Relations inside groups are formal (албан — by set rules) and informal (албан бус — by closeness), and a person must respect others, bear duty and responsibility, and reckon with the results of their acts; four group portraits are analysed — a school football team with its strict federation rules, a beloved Mongolian-language lesson that must not disturb other lessons, a youth police group (өсвөрийн цагдаа) recruited through the local police, and a family run by the parents' word as its law. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
