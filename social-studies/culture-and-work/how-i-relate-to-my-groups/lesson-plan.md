# Lesson plan — How I Relate to My Groups

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** How I Relate to My Groups
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Mongolia, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** How I Relate to My Groups — Relations inside groups are formal (албан — by set rules) and informal (албан бус — by closeness), and a person must respect others, bear duty and responsibility, and reckon with the results of their acts; four group portraits are analysed — a school football team with its strict federation rules, a beloved Mongolian-language lesson that must not disturb other lessons, a youth police group (өсвөрийн цагдаа) recruited through the local police, and a family run by the parents' word as its law — as Mongolia's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
