# How I Relate to My Groups — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: relations inside groups are formal (албан — by set rules) and informal (албан бус — by closeness), and a person must respect others, bear duty and responsibility, and reckon with the results of their acts; four group portraits are analysed — a school football team with its strict federation rules, a beloved Mongolian-language lesson that must not disturb other lessons, a youth police group (өсвөрийн цагдаа) recruited through the local police, and a family run by the parents' word as its law.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
