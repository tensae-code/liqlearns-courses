# How I Relate to My Groups — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Section 1.2 "Relations between the individual and social groups" — Relations inside groups are formal (албан — by set rules) and informal (албан бус — by closeness), and a person must respect others, bear duty and responsibility, and reckon with the results of their acts; four group portraits are analysed — a school football team with its strict federation rules, a beloved Mongolian-language lesson that must not disturb other lessons, a youth police group (өсвөрийн цагдаа) recruited through the local police, and a family run by the parents' word as its law (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way — the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work (any close wording).**
