# Video script — Mutual Trust

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Iraq Grade 9 Social Studies (printed p. 143), teaches: Mutual trust is a mutual feeling expressing belief in others' truthfulness, trustworthiness and sincerity; and the most important foundations of mutual trust between people are respect, love of good, and keeping away from ill suspicion of others; trust leads to society's stability and to its members living in peace and security — we always trust the teacher and he trusts our abilities, we trust the doctor and he trusts the patients' cooperation with him, we trust our parents and they trust that we hear their directions; and the absence of mutual trust between people leads to the disintegration of human relations, at the level of the family and society, and then the absence of stability from society, so many human societies seek to plant mutual trust among their members, to achieve security, stability, society's progress and its members' welfare.

Iraq's own way: values are defined first as a system — standards that steer behaviour and spread peace "regardless of sex, religion, nationality, language or race" — and citizenship is cashed out in named rights (education, work, candidacy, the vote, freedom of opinion and belief), while the internet chapter warns in plain terms that the open network "is governed by no restrictions or conditions".
