# Mutual Trust — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Mutual Trust — mutual trust is a mutual feeling expressing belief in others' truthfulness, trustworthiness and sincerity; and the most important foundations of mutual trust between people are respect, love of good, and keeping away from ill suspicion of others; trust leads to society's stability and to its members living in peace and security — we always trust the teacher and he trusts our abilities, we trust the doctor and he trusts the patients' cooperation with him, we trust our parents and they trust that we hear their directions; and the absence of mutual trust between people leads to the disintegration of human relations, at the level of the family and society, and then the absence of stability from society, so many human societies seek to plant mutual trust among their members, to achieve security, stability, society's progress and its members' welfare (any close wording).**
