# Hands-on — Responsibility Its Kinds and Benefits

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: responsibility and its kinds (among them religious responsibility — obeying God's commands and avoiding His prohibitions — self-responsibility and social responsibility); its benefits — more self-confidence and others' trust in you to perform the works entrusted to you and bear their results — with the example of using the internet safely and responsibly (choosing only safe sites, avoiding unsuitable content, set times without excess) as personal-safety responsibility parents trust; and how to bear responsibility: relying on God then oneself, committing to morals and values, readiness of mind, soul and body, working hard and diligently, and accepting honest objective criticism without being broken by stumbling. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
