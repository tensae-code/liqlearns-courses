# Responsibility Its Kinds and Benefits — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Responsibility Its Kinds and Benefits — responsibility and its kinds (among them religious responsibility — obeying God's commands and avoiding His prohibitions — self-responsibility and social responsibility); its benefits — more self-confidence and others' trust in you to perform the works entrusted to you and bear their results — with the example of using the internet safely and responsibly (choosing only safe sites, avoiding unsuitable content, set times without excess) as personal-safety responsibility parents trust; and how to bear responsibility: relying on God then oneself, committing to morals and values, readiness of mind, soul and body, working hard and diligently, and accepting honest objective criticism without being broken by stumbling (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way — Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes. (any close wording).**
