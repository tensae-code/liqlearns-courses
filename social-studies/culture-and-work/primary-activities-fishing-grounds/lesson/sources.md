**Acceptance count: taught in 7 countries (Ethiopia, Nauru, Tuvalu, Niue, Angola, Bangladesh, Turkmenistan).**

Niue's own way: Niue uses the shared Pacific ESD reader suite (Niue advisor Birtha Togahai) — Geography is taught as Pacific peoples' lived relationship with the ocean: voyaging, reef knowledge, tapu/fono closed seasons and village fishing practice in Tonga, Tuvalu and Niue itself, ending in a pupil action plan against plastic. That way is stocked here as the way Niue teaches this thing.

Nauru's own way: Nauru uses the shared Pacific ESD reader suite (local advisors Fay Itaia & Sharon Kam for Nauru) — Geography is taught as ocean stewardship: the Pacific as a connected "sea of islands", its animals, and the traditional fishing knowledge of Tonga, Tuvalu and Niue, ending in a plastic-waste action plan. That way is stocked here as the way Nauru teaches this thing.

Tuvalu's own way: Tuvalu uses the shared Pacific ESD reader suite (local advisor Valisi Tovia for Tuvalu) — and Tuvalu itself is a case study inside it: Tuvalu's own traditional fishing and bird-catching (printed p. 14) is one of the three country fishing studies. That way is stocked here as the way Tuvalu teaches this thing.

Honesty: pupil content comes from the Amhara student book. The Addis Ababa Grade 8 teacher guide is NOT edition-matched to this student book and is truncated (only printed pp. 1–47, Units 1–3, in hand); no guide coverage is claimed for this lesson beyond what its sources rows name.

Angola's own way (Grade 6): a resources-and-environment year — every world topic (air, water, soils, forests) is closed with the Angolan case: Angola's fresh-water use, Angola's farm land, Angola's forest areas. That way is stocked here as the way Angola teaches this thing.

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 8): the year's Geography is one long chapter, "Protect the Bond between Nature and People": pupils model the world's biomes, compare world forest cover in 2000 and 2020, map Bangladesh's own forests and disaster-prone areas, and read a long inquiry-reader on biomes, sea resources and the Sundarbans mangrove.

Turkmenistan's own way (Grade 10): A classic post-Soviet economic-and-social geography of the whole world in one book: Part I general world (political map, resources, population, world economy by sector), Part II the same template region by region and country by country, Part III global problems — with Turkmenistan's own memberships and ecological policy woven into the questions.
