## Sub lesson 1 — The world's catch

**About 50 million metric tonnes of fish** are produced and consumed yearly worldwide: **about 25 million tonnes eaten as food, about 16 million going to poultry feed, fertiliser, bait and the like, and the rest to industry**.

The great consuming regions are the **densely peopled coastal regions of the northern hemisphere**, where **75% of the catch is produced**: **Asia takes a third** of world consumption, **Europe a fifth**, **Russia and North America about a tenth each**; Peru rises in the south.

## Sub lesson 2 — The five great grounds

Memorise the five main fishing grounds as a set: **North-West Pacific, North-East Atlantic, South-East Pacific, West-Central Pacific and North-West Atlantic**. Three are Pacific, two Atlantic — and all five sit where dense coastal peoples and rich waters meet.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Give the catch figures: 50 million tonnes, split 25 / 16 / the rest. Name the five great fishing grounds. Say where 75% of the catch is produced.
