# Primary activities — fishing grounds of the world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The world's yearly fish production is about —
a) 5 million tonnes  b) 50 million metric tonnes  c) 500 million tonnes  d) 16 million tonnes
**Answer: b) 50 million metric tonnes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** About how much of the catch is eaten as food?
a) 16 million tonnes  b) All of it  c) 25 million tonnes  d) 1 million tonnes
**Answer: c) 25 million tonnes**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which is one of the five great grounds?
a) North-West Pacific  b) Indian Ocean Coast  c) Lake Tana  d) Mediterranean
**Answer: a) North-West Pacific**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** 75% of the catch is produced in —
a) The southern oceans  b) Densely peopled coastal regions of the northern hemisphere  c) Inland lakes  d) Deserts
**Answer: b) Densely peopled coastal regions of the northern hemisphere**
