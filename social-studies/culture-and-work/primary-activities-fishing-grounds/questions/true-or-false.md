# Primary activities — fishing grounds of the world — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Three of the five great grounds are in the Pacific.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Asia takes about a third of world fish consumption.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Most of the catch is eaten directly as food.
**Answer: False**
