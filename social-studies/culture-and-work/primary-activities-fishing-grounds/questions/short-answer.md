# Primary activities — fishing grounds of the world — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Split the 50 million tonnes as the book does.
**Answer:** About 25 million tonnes eaten as food, about 16 million to poultry feed, fertiliser, bait and the like, and the rest to industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name all five great grounds.
**Answer:** North-West Pacific, North-East Atlantic, South-East Pacific, West-Central Pacific, North-West Atlantic.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What shares do Asia and Europe take?
**Answer:** Asia about a third; Europe about a fifth.
