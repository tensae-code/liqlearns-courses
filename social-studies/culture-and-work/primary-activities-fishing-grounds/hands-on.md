# Hands-on — Primary activities — fishing grounds of the world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
Name your own area's fishing places, and the services Lake Tana gives — as the book's task sets it. Write both.

## Activity 2
Draw a rough world map and mark the five grounds from memory. Check yourself against the lesson.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Give the catch figures: 50 million tonnes, split 25 / 16 / the rest. Name the five great fishing grounds. Say where 75% of the catch is produced.
