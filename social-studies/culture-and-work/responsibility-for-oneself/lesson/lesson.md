## Sub lesson 3 — Laos's way — the books' own teaching way

Laos's own way: Moral Education is a standalone weekly subject built as one short story-and-discussion lesson per value, sequenced self → family → friends → school → nation → the world; the same value returns at the next grade widened (the guide prints a Grade 2 ↔ Grade 3 lesson comparison table for planning). the last primary moral year widens the circles to their widest — neighbours, hometown, local government, nation, peace and the United Nations — and closes with care of the country's natural resources. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
