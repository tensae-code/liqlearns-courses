# Hands-on — Saudi Orders and Decorations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the orders granted, often at National Day, in loyalty and appreciation to those who achieved greatly or did exceptional work — the Order of King Saud (distinction in civil service, the judiciary, economy and finance, the free professions, and noble heroic deeds), the Order of King Faisal (service of Islam, diplomacy, and the military and security fields), the Order of King Khalid (service of the Arabic language, national heritage, environment protection and urban development), and the Order of King Fahd (sciences, arts and literature, medicine, education and scientific research, sustainable development, and initiatives serving Muslims and caring for God's Book). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
