Saudi Arabia Grade 9 Social Studies (printed pp. 201–202), teaches: The orders granted, often at National Day, in loyalty and appreciation to those who achieved greatly or did exceptional work — the Order of King Saud (distinction in civil service, the judiciary, economy and finance, the free professions, and noble heroic deeds), the Order of King Faisal (service of Islam, diplomacy, and the military and security fields), the Order of King Khalid (service of the Arabic language, national heritage, environment protection and urban development), and the Order of King Fahd (sciences, arts and literature, medicine, education and scientific research, sustainable development, and initiatives serving Muslims and caring for God's Book).

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 9 book teaches Saudi systems in detail — development and social protection, Saudi systems and sovereignty, the Basic Law of Governance, the three authorities, the Council of Ministers, the Shura Council and the Regions System — then the foundations of the Saudi state, national identity and national days, orders and decorations, self-achievement, responsibility, public decency, and volunteering in its fields and national programmes.
