# Hands-on — Cultural Awareness in the Digital Age

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The effect of cultural invasion on the Gulf person's identity, what social media are, the positives and negatives of using them, and Kuwait's electronic-crimes (cybercrime) law. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
