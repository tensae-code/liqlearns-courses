# Hands-on — Capitals Why There And Why They Move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Lesson 10: The capital (پایتخت) — usually becoming the country's biggest and most important city or metropolis; the factors in choosing a capital site; capitals moved from one city to another (Rio de Janeiro to Brasília in Brazil, Karachi to Islamabad in Pakistan); and the patterns of political management of space — in the federal pattern the centre coordinates the states, which follow it in defence, foreign policy and the national currency. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
