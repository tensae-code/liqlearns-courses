South Korea's Grade 10 MiraeN Integrated Society, printed p. 204; Grade 10 Jihaksa Integrated Society, printed p. 215; Grade 10 Bisang Integrated Society, printed p. 204; Grade 10 Donga Integrated Society, printed p. 198; Grade 10 Jihaksa Integrated Society 1 (2022), printed p. 98, teaches: VII.주제3 전통문화의 의의와 창조적 계승 방안, VII.2-02 전통문화의 의의와 계승, VII.2 문화 변동과 전통문화의 창조적 계승, VII.03 전통문화의 의의와 계승·발전 방안은?, 문화와 다양성 2-2 전통문화의 의미와 창조적 계승 — teaches: the meaning of traditional culture and how to inherit it creatively; the meaning of traditional culture and how it is handed down; cultural change and the creative succession of traditional culture; the meaning of traditional culture and how to inherit and develop it; traditional culture — its meaning and creative succession.

South Korea's own way: the high-school common subject 통합사회 (Integrated Society) is built around nine key questions asked from temporal, spatial, social and ethical perspectives at once; its civics core is Unit IV (human rights guaranteed by the constitution, law-abiding spirit and citizen participation) and Unit VI (justice and inequality), and its peace unit teaches the peaceful resolution of Korea's own division and of East Asia's history conflicts as the closing civics content. Jihaksa splits every section into two numbered lessons, so its civics teaching is the most granular of the three publishers — human rights alone gets five lessons (meaning/change, modern society, the constitution's role, law-abiding participation, problems and solutions), and its justice unit ends on the institutions and practical plans for a just society. Bisang's Integrated Society binds its justice unit shut with a practice section (불평등의 해결과 정의의 실현 — solving inequality and realising justice) and its culture unit with creative succession of traditional culture; its peace unit is framed as East Asia's conflicts and international peace rather than the two Koreas alone. Donga writes every lesson as a question ("Why is civil disobedience ever needed?", "What does universal ethics do in understanding a culture?") and is the only one of the five publishers to teach civil disobedience (시민 불복종) beside the law-abiding spirit, and to split the conditions of happiness into two lessons — living environment/economic stability, then democracy's development and moral practice. the first 2022 volume teaches the integrated perspectives and happiness first, then culture: four culture lessons — the world's culture regions, culture change together with traditional culture, cultural relativism weighed against universal ethics, and the multicultural society that respects diversity — the fullest culture treatment in any Korean integrated volume read in this wave.
