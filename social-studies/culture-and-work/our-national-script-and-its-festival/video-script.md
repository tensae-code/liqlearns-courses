Mongolia Civic Ethics Grade 6, printed p. 83, teaches: The national script (үндэсний бичиг) is the people's treasure and the guarantee of independence: the President's 2003 decree (No. 105) made its festival — the National Script Day each May — a state celebration of the 800th year of Mongol statehood's script; the script is "the written form of the Mongol mind" — history, culture, pride and mentality in one, written for a thousand years and carried through every script reform; a festival (баяр) itself is explained as a nation's memory and preparation, not mere celebration; and the etiquette of the script — written upright, begun with the crown (титим) and ended with the tail, handled with honour — closes the book.

Mongolia's own way (Link book L2): from Grade 6 the civics subject changes its name and its method — no longer "Civic Education" but "Civic Ethics" (Иргэний ёс зүйн боловсрол), a self-observation workbook in the Buddhist-inflected Mongolian moral tradition: the pupil keeps a diary of their own acts of body, speech and mind, and ethics is practised as a daily discipline before it is ever a rule about others.
