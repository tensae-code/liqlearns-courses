# Hands-on — Our National Script And Its Festival

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 3.2 "Traditional conduct connected with the Mongolian festival"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The national script (үндэсний бичиг) is the people's treasure and the guarantee of independence: the President's 2003 decree (No. 105) made its festival — the National Script Day each May — a state celebration of the 800th year of Mongol statehood's script; the script is "the written form of the Mongol mind" — history, culture, pride and mentality in one, written for a thousand years and carried through every script reform; a festival (баяр) itself is explained as a nation's memory and preparation, not mere celebration; and the etiquette of the script — written upright, begun with the crown (титим) and ended with the tail, handled with honour — closes the book. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
