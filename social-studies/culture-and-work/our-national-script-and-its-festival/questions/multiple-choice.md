# Our National Script And Its Festival — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Section 3.2 "Traditional conduct connected with the Mongolian festival"  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Section 3.2 "Traditional conduct connected with the Mongolian festival"**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) The national script (үндэсний бичиг) is the people's treasure and the guarantee of independence: the President's 2003 decree (No. 105) made its festival — the National Script Day each May — a state celebration of the 800th year of Mongol statehood's script; the script is "the written form of the Mongol mind" — history, culture, pride and mentality in one, written for a thousand years and carried through every script reform; a festival (баяр) itself is explained as a nation's memory and preparation, not mere celebration; and the etiquette of the script — written upright, begun with the crown (титим) and ended with the tail, handled with honour — closes the book  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) The national script (үндэсний бичиг) is the people's treasure and the guarantee of independence: the President's 2003 decree (No. 105) made its festival — the National Script Day each May — a state celebration of the 800th year of Mongol statehood's script; the script is "the written form of the Mongol mind" — history, culture, pride and mentality in one, written for a thousand years and carried through every script reform; a festival (баяр) itself is explained as a nation's memory and preparation, not mere celebration; and the etiquette of the script — written upright, begun with the crown (титим) and ended with the tail, handled with honour — closes the book**
