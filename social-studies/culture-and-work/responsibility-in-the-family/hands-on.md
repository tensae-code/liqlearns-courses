# Hands-on — Responsibility in the Family

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every family member's roles and tasks (the Tandoor Day story), loving and respecting parents and elders (the grandfather and the crow), and discipline and honesty (the New King and the boiled seed). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Eritrea's way
Say how Eritrea's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
