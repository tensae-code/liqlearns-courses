# Democracy and Good Governance in Tanzania — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part 1 teaches: How democracy is practised in Tanzania — freedom of expression, free and fair elections managed under law (electing the President, MPs and local leaders), multiparty politics since 1992, freedom of assembly and peaceful demonstration, participation, transparency and accountability — plus the qualities of a national leader and the principles and importance of good governance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part 2 teaches: Democracy is government that draws legitimacy from the people and works for the people, deciding by the majority (voters are citizens of 18 years and above); it guarantees human rights, truth and transparency, freedoms of expression, worship, association and movement, equality before the law, responsible leaders, and power divided among judiciary, legislature and executive; political parties educate citizens, criticise the government, contest elections and must maintain peace, and political tolerance — respecting others' opinions and accepting election results — brings peace, harmony and stability; the 1977 constitution's first chapter sets out basic rights and duties.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on Tanzania's student books, counted in the acceptance count in lesson/sources.md.
**Answer: True**
