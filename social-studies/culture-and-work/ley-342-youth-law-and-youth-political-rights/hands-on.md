# Hands-on — Ley 342 Youth Law and Youth Political Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bolivia's Youth Law (2013) — youth defined as 16–28, the Plurinational Youth Day, the law's values (plurinationality, gender equality, participation, co-responsibility) — and young people's political rights: citizenship from 18 (CPE Art. 144), the right to elect and to be elected, with the age requirements for each level of authority. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
