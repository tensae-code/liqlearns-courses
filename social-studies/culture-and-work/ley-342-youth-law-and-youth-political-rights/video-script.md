# Video script — Ley 342 Youth Law and Youth Political Rights

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia Grade 12 BO Social Sciences Sec 6 (printed p. 454), teaches: Bolivia's Youth Law (2013) — youth defined as 16–28, the Plurinational Youth Day, the law's values (plurinationality, gender equality, participation, co-responsibility) — and young people's political rights: citizenship from 18 (CPE Art. 144), the right to elect and to be elected, with the age requirements for each level of authority.

Bolivia's own way: the final year turns from history to the citizen's own legal armour — identity in a plural nation, a supreme decree ordering the state itself to despatriarcalise, a Youth Law, an anti-trafficking law taught with self-protection advice for teenagers, and the labour rights of working children under Bolivia's own Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente.
