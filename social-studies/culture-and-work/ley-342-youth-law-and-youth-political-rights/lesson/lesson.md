## Sub lesson 1 — Ley Nº 342, Ley Plurinacional de la Juventud

Bolivia Grade 12 BO Social Sciences Sec 6 (printed p. 454), teaches: Bolivia's Youth Law (2013) — youth defined as 16–28, the Plurinational Youth Day, the law's values (plurinationality, gender equality, participation, co-responsibility) — and young people's political rights: citizenship from 18 (CPE Art. 144), the right to elect and to be elected, with the age requirements for each level of authority. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bolivia's way — the book's own teaching way

Bolivia's own way: the final year turns from history to the citizen's own legal armour — identity in a plural nation, a supreme decree ordering the state itself to despatriarcalise, a Youth Law, an anti-trafficking law taught with self-protection advice for teenagers, and the labour rights of working children under Bolivia's own Código Niña, Niño y Adolescente. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
