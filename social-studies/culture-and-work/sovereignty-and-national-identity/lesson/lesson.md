## Sub lesson 2 — Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way

Dominican Republic's own way: the Grade 10 citizen-education unit is built as close reading — pupils paraphrase the texts aloud in their own words: the Constitution as Carta Magna / Ley de Leyes, sovereignty residing exclusively in the people, Locke's division of powers, and democratic coexistence resting on education and the culture of peace.
