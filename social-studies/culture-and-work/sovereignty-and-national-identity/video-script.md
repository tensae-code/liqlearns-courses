The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Dominican Republic — Ciencias Sociales 4° Secundaria (printed p. 190), teaches: National sovereignty — the State's power over its territory; by the Constitution sovereignty resides exclusively in the people, from whom all powers emanate and who exercise them through their representatives — set beside national identity as belonging to a community of language, religion and culture.

Dominican Republic's own way (Book 14): the Grade 10 citizen-education unit is built as close reading — pupils paraphrase the texts aloud in their own words: the Constitution as Carta Magna / Ley de Leyes, sovereignty residing exclusively in the people, Locke's division of powers, and democratic coexistence resting on education and the culture of peace.
