# Sovereignty and National Identity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Unidad 12, Soberanía e identidad nacional — National sovereignty — the State's power over its territory; by the Constitution sovereignty resides exclusively in the people, from whom all powers emanate and who exercise them through their representatives — set beside national identity as belonging to a community of language, religion and culture (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Dominican Republic's way — the book's own teaching way — the Grade 10 citizen-education unit is built as close reading — pupils paraphrase the texts aloud in their own words: the Constitution as Carta Magna / Ley de Leyes, sovereignty residing exclusively in the people, Locke's division of powers, and democratic coexistence resting on education and the culture of peace. (any close wording).**
