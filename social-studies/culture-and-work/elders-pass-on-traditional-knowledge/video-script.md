# Video script — Elders Pass on Traditional Knowledge

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Canada Social Studies 10 IRSR Guide (printed p. 14), teaches: In traditional First Nations societies education was woven into daily, ceremonial and spiritual life; Elders passed knowledge on through oral history and storytelling, and learning centred on respect and the relationship with ancestors and the land.

Canada Social Studies 5 IRSR Guide (printed p. 14), teaches: In traditional First Nations culture education was a family matter — children learn from elders through the seasonal round, and cultural knowledge passes from generation to generation.

Canada's own way: Reconciliation is taught as a relationship to be rebuilt — on four principles the Royal Commission named: mutual recognition, mutual respect, sharing and mutual responsibility.

Canada's own way: The Grade 5 guide opens with the land and the family as the first teachers, before the school system is named — reconciliation at this level begins from respect for the land and learning handed down the generations.
