Canada's own way (teacher guide — evidence): Reconciliation is taught as a relationship to be rebuilt — on four principles the Royal Commission named: mutual recognition, mutual respect, sharing and mutual responsibility.

Canada's own way (teacher guide — evidence): The Grade 5 guide opens with the land and the family as the first teachers, before the school system is named — reconciliation at this level begins from respect for the land and learning handed down the generations.

Tonga's own way: land is ancestral and sacred — knowledge of it is an inheritance elders hand down, and using it well is a duty owed to grandchildren.
