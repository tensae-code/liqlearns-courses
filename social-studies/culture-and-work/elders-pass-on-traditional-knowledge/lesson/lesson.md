## Sub lesson 1 — Traditional First Nations Societies and Education

Canada Social Studies 10 IRSR Guide (printed p. 14), teaches: In traditional First Nations societies education was woven into daily, ceremonial and spiritual life; Elders passed knowledge on through oral history and storytelling, and learning centred on respect and the relationship with ancestors and the land. Canada Social Studies 5 IRSR Guide (printed p. 14), teaches: In traditional First Nations culture education was a family matter — children learn from elders through the seasonal round, and cultural knowledge passes from generation to generation. ## Sub lesson 3 — Canada's way — the book's own teaching way

Canada's own way: Reconciliation is taught as a relationship to be rebuilt — on four principles the Royal Commission named: mutual recognition, mutual respect, sharing and mutual responsibility. The Grade 5 guide opens with the land and the family as the first teachers, before the school system is named — reconciliation at this level begins from respect for the land and learning handed down the generations.
