# Elders Pass on Traditional Knowledge — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: In traditional First Nations societies education was woven into daily, ceremonial and spiritual life; Elders passed knowledge on through oral history and storytelling, and learning centred on respect and the relationship with ancestors and the land.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's second part teaches: In traditional First Nations culture education was a family matter — children learn from elders through the seasonal round, and cultural knowledge passes from generation to generation.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: False — it stands on Canada's teacher guide only, named in lesson/sources.md, as teacher-guide evidence.**
