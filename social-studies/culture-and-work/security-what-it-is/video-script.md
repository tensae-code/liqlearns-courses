# Video script — Security What It is

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 5 Social Studies (printed pp. 52–53), teaches: Security as the tranquillity and stability we feel in our homeland, which provides the right environment for development and prosperity; protecting the homeland and spreading security for citizens and residents is one of the state's tasks, and the security man's work — achieving security and protection, organising society's affairs, spreading reassurance, confronting terrorism and sacrificing for the homeland.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 5 book teaches security and state institutions as service — security is defined first, the citizen is named the first line of security, the security agencies are listed one by one with their tasks, state institutions are split into public and private, and each region's Emir receives citizens and hears their needs.
