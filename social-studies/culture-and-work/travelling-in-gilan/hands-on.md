# Hands-on — Travelling in Gilan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: every year many of our country's people travel to northern Iran — a very green and beautiful region and an important tourism area; reading the map: which sea lies in Iran's north, which three provinces stand beside it, and which mountain range — the Alborz — stands south of them. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Iran's way
Say how Iran's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
