## Sub lesson 1 — Travelling in Gilan

Iran Social Studies Grade 6 (special needs) (student book, Persian), printed p. 36, teaches: every year many of our country's people travel to northern Iran — a very green and beautiful region and an important tourism area; reading the map: which sea lies in Iran's north, which three provinces stand beside it, and which mountain range — the Alborz — stands south of them. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the last special-needs primary book turns outward — life skills at school and at work, then the country learned by travelling it (green Gilan and the Caspian ports, the warm southern islands), and the institutions that stand behind the citizen: the insurance booklet and the Welfare Organisation's card. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
