# Hands-on — Musyawarah Deciding Together Until Consensus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 2 D. Arti Penting Musyawarah dalam Kehidupanku, Bab 4 A. Manfaat Musyawarah, Bab 4 B. Belajar Bermusyawarah, Panduan Khusus Bab 4: Belajar Bermusyawarah — teaches: musyawarah — reaching a fair decision by deliberation and shared agreement (mufakat), modelled in a family deliberation; the benefits of musyawarah — deliberation for consensus (mufakat) and its steps; practising musyawarah and taking decisions through it; guidance for learning musyawarah — benefits, steps and decision-taking. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
