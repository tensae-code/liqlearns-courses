## Sub lesson 3 — North Macedonia's way — the books' own teaching way

North Macedonia's own way (Book 2): Grade 3 turns the Grade 2 classroom democracy into explicit civics — children's right to education, the state as education's provider, and group decision-making taught as a process with rules, ending in the difference between a majority decision and a consensus; Theme 3's love of country is carried by the country's own lakes, mountains and the Vardar, then by what a child can contribute and the languages of its classrooms. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
