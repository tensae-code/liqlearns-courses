# Bii Biyelgee Mongolias Dance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this lesson is —
a) Topic 12 Хосгүй гайхамшиг — бий биелгээ (The peerless wonder — the biyelgee)  b) learning the alphabet  c) counting to ten
**Answer: a) Topic 12 Хосгүй гайхамшиг — бий биелгээ (The peerless wonder — the biyelgee)**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson is mainly about —
a) The biyelgee, the seated dance of western Mongolia that imitates the horseman's gait and daily work, learned from photographs and a poem — "I dance, we dance"  b) ancient kings only  c) the Moon's phases
**Answer: a) The biyelgee, the seated dance of western Mongolia that imitates the horseman's gait and daily work, learned from photographs and a poem — "I dance, we dance"**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this lesson is —
