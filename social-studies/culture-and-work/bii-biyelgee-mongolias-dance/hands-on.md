# Hands-on — Bii Biyelgee Mongolias Dance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Topic 12 Хосгүй гайхамшиг — бий биелгээ (The peerless wonder — the biyelgee)
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The biyelgee, the seated dance of western Mongolia that imitates the horseman's gait and daily work, learned from photographs and a poem — "I dance, we dance". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mongolia's way — the books' own teaching way
Say how Mongolia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
