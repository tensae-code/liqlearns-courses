# Hands-on — Celebrating National Days

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what citizens do on Founding Day, National Day and Flag Day — raise the homeland's flag and respect it, join the school's celebrations, take pride in the homeland and its government's and citizens' achievements, express national unity and social cohesion, and pledge sincere, perfected work to build the homeland. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
