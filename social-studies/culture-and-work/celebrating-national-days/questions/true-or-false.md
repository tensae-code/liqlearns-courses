# Celebrating National Days — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's part teaches: What citizens do on Founding Day, National Day and Flag Day — raise the homeland's flag and respect it, join the school's celebrations, take pride in the homeland and its government's and citizens' achievements, express national unity and social cohesion, and pledge sincere, perfected work to build the homeland.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's part teaches: Why Founding Day is celebrated — pride in the Saudi state's firm roots and the citizens' close bond with its leaders since Imam Muhammad bin Saud founded the first state in mid-1139H (1727) with Diriyah its capital and the Quran and Sunnah its constitution, the unity and security it planted after centuries of division, and its survival through the second state (1240H/1824) to unification; the Founding Day logo's five elements — the palm, the Saudi flag, the falcon, the Arabian horse and the market; why National Day is celebrated — honouring the founder's and his men's efforts, deepening belonging and loyalty, recalling the homeland's history, heritage and foundations, and showing its achievements — and the negative practices to avoid (misusing the flag, breaking traffic systems); and the flag's specifications and rules — green, rectangular, its width two-thirds of its length, the two testimonies in white Thuluth script above a drawn sword, never lowered, never touching ground or water, not raised faded, with prison or a fine for whoever drops or insults it.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
