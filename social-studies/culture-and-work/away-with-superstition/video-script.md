# Video script — Away with Superstition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal Grade 3 My Social Studies and Creative Arts (printed p. 24), teaches: Refusing superstition as a social value.

Nepal Grade 10 Social Studies (printed p. 86), teaches: Superstition as a social problem and reason as its cure.

Nepal's own way: Grade 3 names caste directly — "Abolish Untouchability" is a named lesson — and local government arrives as the child's own ward and what it does. the Grade 10 book is organised as the citizen's widening world — society and identity, social values, social problems, then a full civic-awareness unit on Nepal's own federal legislature, executive and judiciary, closing with Nepal's foreign policy and the UN.
