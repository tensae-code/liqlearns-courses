# How My Groups Shape Me — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: groups shape a person many-sidedly — the family's trades, income and interests reach straight into the child's life (the talents of Mozart, Raphael, Faraday and Hamilton all showed by age 3–13 inside a family's world); the school timetable rules the pupil's day; a group one joins takes on the group's customs and manners, and a member who cannot fit may leave the group and lose its standing; and rival groups differ in character — the Chelsea and Liverpool fans who dug up the pitch their own teams were to play on.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
