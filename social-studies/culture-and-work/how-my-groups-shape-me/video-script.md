# Video script — How My Groups Shape Me

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongolia Grade 7 Social Studies, printed p. 13, teaches: Groups shape a person many-sidedly — the family's trades, income and interests reach straight into the child's life (the talents of Mozart, Raphael, Faraday and Hamilton all showed by age 3–13 inside a family's world); the school timetable rules the pupil's day; a group one joins takes on the group's customs and manners, and a member who cannot fit may leave the group and lose its standing; and rival groups differ in character — the Chelsea and Liverpool fans who dug up the pitch their own teams were to play on.

Mongolia's own way (Book 5): the secondary spiral teaches civics as the child's widening circles of membership — my groups, my rights, our decisions — and the law chapter is built from the child's own day: the borrowed phone, the noisy neighbours, the boy taken out of school to work.
