# Hands-on — How My Groups Shape Me

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Section 1.3 "How social groups influence the individual"
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Groups shape a person many-sidedly — the family's trades, income and interests reach straight into the child's life (the talents of Mozart, Raphael, Faraday and Hamilton all showed by age 3–13 inside a family's world); the school timetable rules the pupil's day; a group one joins takes on the group's customs and manners, and a member who cannot fit may leave the group and lose its standing; and rival groups differ in character — the Chelsea and Liverpool fans who dug up the pitch their own teams were to play on. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
