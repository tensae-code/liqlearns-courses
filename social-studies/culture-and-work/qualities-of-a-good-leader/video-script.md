The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Kazakhstan Grade 4 Poznanie mira (110/88) (printed p. 30), teaches: the qualities of a leader — organisation, discipline, honesty, charisma and responsibility for the group's work — and how a leader's standing differs from group to group and can be developed.

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 5): Grade 4 is where the primary course turns civic in earnest — the republic's own map of 14 oblasts and three cities of republican significance, an elected school council drawn as a working model of the state, the Constitution's rights quoted article by article, and the country's face to the world built from the UN seat, the closed Semipalatinsk test site and EXPO-2017.
