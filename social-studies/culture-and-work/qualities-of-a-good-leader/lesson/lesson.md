# Qualities of a Good Leader

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: the qualities of a leader — organisation, discipline, honesty, charisma and responsibility for the group's work — and how a leader's standing differs from group to group and can be developed — as Kazakhstan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Qualities of a Good Leader

Kazakhstan Grade 4 Poznanie mira (110/88) (printed p. 30), teaches: in any group or council a pupil takes a certain position — some are listened to and their tasks carried out, others ignored, because groups accept only a person with definite qualities as leader; the same person behaves differently in different groups (Akmaral leads the yard games and organises plays for the little ones, yet is quiet in class), so a leader's standing is not constant and leadership qualities — organisation, discipline, honesty, charisma, responsibility for the group's work — can be developed. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kazakhstan's way — the books' own teaching way

Kazakhstan's own way (Book 5): Grade 4 is where the primary course turns civic in earnest — the republic's own map of 14 oblasts and three cities of republican significance, an elected school council drawn as a working model of the state, the Constitution's rights quoted article by article, and the country's face to the world built from the UN seat, the closed Semipalatinsk test site and EXPO-2017. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the books' text itself is never transcribed.
