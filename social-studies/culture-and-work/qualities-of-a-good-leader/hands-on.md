# Hands-on — Qualities of a Good Leader

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the qualities of a leader — organisation, discipline, honesty, charisma and responsibility for the group's work — and how a leader's standing differs from group to group and can be developed. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kazakhstan's way
Say how Kazakhstan's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
