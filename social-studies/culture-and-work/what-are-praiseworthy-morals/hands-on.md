# Hands-on — What Are Praiseworthy Morals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## What are praiseworthy morals?
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: praiseworthy traits (خصال حميدة) defined in the glossary as behaviours that show a person's high moral character — morals (أخلاق) being the principles of right and wrong that govern a person's behaviour — and how such traits are recognised in daily conduct Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
