# Video script — Obligatory and Voluntary Duties of the Citizen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G6 Term 1, printed p. 63, teaches: the citizen's duties in two kinds — obligatory duties the law accounts for (guarding the state's secrets, respecting the constitution and laws, keeping the homeland's security and defending it, protecting public facilities, performing national service) and voluntary duties done willingly without pay (taking part in society and protecting the environment, contributing to community projects, serving the community's members)

United Arab Emirates' own way: citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries.
