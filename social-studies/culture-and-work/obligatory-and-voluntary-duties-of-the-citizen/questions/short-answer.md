# Obligatory and Voluntary Duties of the Citizen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does part 1 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: The duties diagram — the citizen's duties in two kinds — obligatory duties the law accounts for (guarding the state's secrets, respecting the constitution and laws, keeping the homeland's security and defending it, protecting public facilities, performing national service) and voluntary duties done willingly without pay (taking part in society and protecting the environment, contributing to community projects, serving the community's members) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does part 2 of this lesson teach?
**Answer: United Arab Emirates' way — the book's own teaching way — citizenship at Grade 6 is a whole unit of its own between History and Geography — its duties are split into what the law compels and what the citizen volunteers, its model citizens are the mothers of martyrs, and its proof of goodness is measured in tonnes of aid flown to a hundred countries. (any close wording).**
