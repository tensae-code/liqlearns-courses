# Hands-on — Obligatory and Voluntary Duties of the Citizen

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The duties diagram
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the citizen's duties in two kinds — obligatory duties the law accounts for (guarding the state's secrets, respecting the constitution and laws, keeping the homeland's security and defending it, protecting public facilities, performing national service) and voluntary duties done willingly without pay (taking part in society and protecting the environment, contributing to community projects, serving the community's members) Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
