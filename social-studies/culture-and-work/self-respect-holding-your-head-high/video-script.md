Pakistan's Grade 3 Ethics3 (printed p. 25), teaches: Ch. 2 Self-Respect (عزت نفس) — teaches: self-respect is a person's natural wealth — guard it by refusing greed, deceit and flattery, and by eating only from your own honest invitation.

Pakistan's own way: the Class 3 Ethics book grounds every value in religion itself — good manners are what every faith commands — and spends its longest stretch on the equal honour of every human life, rich or poor.
