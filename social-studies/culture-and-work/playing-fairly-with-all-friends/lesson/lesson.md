## Sub lesson 1 — Fair-minded children

Myanmar Morality and Civics, Grade 4 (printed p. 1), teaches: Playing and learning fairly with every kind of friend. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Myanmar's way — the book's own teaching way

Myanmar's own way: Grade 4 names children's RIGHTS as a picture inventory (food, water, a house, clothes, care, medicine, play, learning, family) and turns environmental care into two full units — preserving the earth and the natural surroundings, with drought, flood, felled forests and factory smoke as the evidence. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
