# Video script — Valuing One Another

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South Sudan Primary 6 P6 Social Studies SB, printed p. 49, teaches: valuing one another is regarding each other highly; it promotes human rights, demonstrates good citizenship and patriotism, promotes peaceful co-existence and reduces conflict, and raises people's confidence and self-esteem — told through Aketch the village elder's monthly meeting and cleaning duty in Magwi.

South Sudan's own way: The country's own conflict is the textbook — secession is explained by marginalisation and exploitation of the South's resources, and the constitution enters as homework: explain why South Sudan's constitution is important and name five rights it guarantees.
