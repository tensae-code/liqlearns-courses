Palestine Grade 6 Social Studies Part 1, printed p. 84, teaches: human rights are universal — they apply to all people without discrimination by religion, colour, sex or standing; interconnected and interlocked — all of a person's rights must be respected without exception, for it is not acceptable to enjoy the right to education while being denied security, or to respect one right and violate another; and inalienable — they exist in every person and no individual or group may dispose of them except as the law provides in specific cases.

Palestine's own way: the constitution is taught from Palestine's own draft Basic Law — pupils extract legal rules from its actual articles (the people as source of the authorities, a democratic parliamentary system, equality before the law) before they are given the definition, and the three authorities are each named by the Palestinian institution that holds them.
