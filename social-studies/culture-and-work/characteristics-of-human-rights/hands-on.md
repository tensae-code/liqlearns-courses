# Hands-on — Characteristics of Human Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Characteristics of Human Rights
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Human rights are universal — they apply to all people without discrimination by religion, colour, sex or standing; interconnected and interlocked — all of a person's rights must be respected without exception, for it is not acceptable to enjoy the right to education while being denied security, or to respect one right and violate another; and inalienable — they exist in every person and no individual or group may dispose of them except as the law provides in specific cases. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
