# Hands-on — Looking After Public Property

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: what "public property" is (the school, the village cultural house, park exercise machines, classroom desks, the sports ground) and guarding it together — stopping friends drawing on desks, cleaning the village wall paintings, turning off the tap, never picking the cultural house's fruit "because it belongs to everyone". Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Vietnam's way
Say how Vietnam's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
