# Video script — Looking After Public Property

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vietnam Grade 4 Đạo đức 4, printed pp. 31–36, teaches: What "public property" is (the school, the village cultural house, park exercise machines, classroom desks, the sports ground) and guarding it together — stopping friends drawing on desks, cleaning the village wall paintings, turning off the tap, never picking the cultural house's fruit "because it belongs to everyone".

Vietnam's own way: Grade 4 turns outward to society's work and property: gratitude is owed to every worker including the night street-sweeper of Tố Hữu's poem; "public property" (của công) is named as a legal-moral category of its own — what belongs to everyone is guarded by everyone — and the book closes with the child's place in law itself: the basic rights and duties of children, including a children's forum where pupils speak.
