# Citizen Participation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Citizen Participation — voting and staying informed about what government is doing are the two most important ways citizens take part, closing with the call that the FSM's future is in the students' hands (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Micronesia's way — the book's own teaching way — present-day government is taught as “Micronesia's Two Governments” — the elected FSM state/national system and the traditional leadership system running beside it — and every structure (federal levels, checks and balances, the rotating presidency) is explained through the compromises of the first constitutional convention and through village-level stories (a Yap bus bill stopped by chiefs, a fishing-fee fight between governors and Palikir, the Amnesty Bill stopped by the people) (any close wording).**
