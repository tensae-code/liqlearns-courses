Mengamalkan Pancasila sebagai Pandangan Hidup Bangsa, Panduan Khusus Bab 2: Aku Berperilaku Pancasila, Panduan Khusus Bab 1: Belajar Pancasila dengan Menyenangkan — teaches: the five sila (principles) of Pancasila in order, in words a child can say; what each shield symbol means — star, chain, banyan, buffalo head, rice and cotton matched to the five principles; the meaning of the Pancasila principles in society, not only at home; Pancasila's values are interconnected goods — practising one sila in society practises them all; Pancasila as the nation's way of life and unifier (pemersatu bangsa), and pupils' own practice of it; guidance for the emblem, the five sila, their meanings, applying values at home and the formulators/heroes material; guidance for the interconnected values of Pancasila in daily practice.

Indonesia's own way: Grade 2 turns Pancasila from a symbol to copy into behaviour at home — each principle (sila) is matched to a picture-symbol on the Garuda shield and then to a concrete act a 7-year-old can do in the family. Grade 4 introduces the child's first government — the perangkat desa/kelurahan, the village and urban-ward officials — inside a book about the child's own street, before any national institution is named. the primary Pancasila course closes by widening every earlier theme to the nation — norms become norms of berbangsa dan bernegara (national and state life), and gotong royong is finally named as bela negara, defending the country by working together. the guide organises Grade 2 by Fase A (the Kurikulum Merdeka phase for Grades 1–2), so civics outcomes are stated as phase competences, not yearly facts. the final primary guide states the subject's position (kedudukan) explicitly — Pendidikan Pancasila is the vehicle of the state ideology in school — and sequences outcomes to the NKRI chapters.
