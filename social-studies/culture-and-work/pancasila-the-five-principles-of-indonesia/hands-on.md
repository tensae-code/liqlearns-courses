# Hands-on — Pancasila the Five Principles of Indonesia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Bab 2 B. Sila-Sila dalam Pancasila, Bab 2 C. Arti dan Makna Lambang Pancasila, Bab 4 A. Makna Sila-Sila Pancasila di Masyarakat, Bab 1 A. Hubungan Nilai-Nilai Pancasila dalam Praktik Hidup Sehari-hari, Bab 2 A. Mengamalkan Pancasila sebagai Pandangan Hidup Bangsa, Panduan Khusus Bab 2: Aku Berperilaku Pancasila, Panduan Khusus Bab 1: Belajar Pancasila dengan Menyenangkan — teaches: the five sila (principles) of Pancasila in order, in words a child can say; what each shield symbol means — star, chain, banyan, buffalo head, rice and cotton matched to the five principles; the meaning of the Pancasila principles in society, not only at home; Pancasila's values are interconnected goods — practising one sila in society practises them all; Pancasila as the nation's way of life and unifier (pemersatu bangsa), and pupils' own practice of it; guidance for the emblem, the five sila, their meanings, applying values at home and the formulators/heroes material; guidance for the interconnected values of Pancasila in daily practice. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Indonesia's way
Say how Indonesia's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
