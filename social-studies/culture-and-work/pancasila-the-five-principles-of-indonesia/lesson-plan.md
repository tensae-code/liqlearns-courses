# Lesson plan — Pancasila the Five Principles of Indonesia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Pancasila the Five Principles of Indonesia
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Indonesia, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bab 2 B. Sila-Sila dalam Pancasila, Bab 2 C. Arti dan Makna Lambang Pancasila, Bab 4 A. Makna Sila-Sila Pancasila di Masyarakat, Bab 1 A. Hubungan Nilai-Nilai Pancasila dalam Praktik Hidup Sehari-hari, Bab 2 A. Mengamalkan Pancasila sebagai Pandangan Hidup Bangsa, Panduan Khusus Bab 2: Aku Berperilaku Pancasila, Panduan Khusus Bab 1: Belajar Pancasila dengan Menyenangkan — teaches: the five sila (principles) of Pancasila in order, in words a child can say; what each shield symbol means — star, chain, banyan, buffalo head, rice and cotton matched to the five principles; the meaning of the Pancasila principles in society, not only at home; Pancasila's values are interconnected goods — practising one sila in society practises them all; Pancasila as the nation's way of life and unifier (pemersatu bangsa), and pupils' own practice of it; guidance for the emblem, the five sila, their meanings, applying values at home and the formulators/heroes material; guidance for the interconnected values of Pancasila in daily practice — as Indonesia's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the lesson itself — the five sila (principles) of Pancasila in order, in words a child can say.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of ..." in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
