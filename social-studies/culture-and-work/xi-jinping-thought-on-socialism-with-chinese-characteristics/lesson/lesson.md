## Sub lesson 4 — China's way — the book's own teaching way

China's own way: the senior Politics course opens with a single arc — only socialism could save China, only Chinese socialism can develop it, only holding to it can achieve national rejuvenation — and teaches today's governing system (road, theory, system, culture; the Party's leadership; the people's congress order founded in the 1954 Constitution) as the conclusion of that history, every lesson built from 探究与分享 discussion cases, 相关链接 source boxes and 专家点评 commentary. A standalone reader (one of a four-volume primary-to-senior set) that teaches the governing thought as a complete civics of today's China — who leads (the Party), for whom (the people), in what layout (five spheres, four strategies), guarded how (security, defence, reunification) and facing the world how (peaceful development and a shared future) — each lecture closing with 学习思考 questions for the student citizen. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
