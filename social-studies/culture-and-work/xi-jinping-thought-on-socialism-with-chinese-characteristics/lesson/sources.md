**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (China).**

China's own way (思想政治必修1 中国特色社会主义 (Ideological & Political, Compulsory 1: Socialism with Chinese Characteristics)): the senior Politics course opens with a single arc — only socialism could save China, only Chinese socialism can develop it, only holding to it can achieve national rejuvenation — and teaches today's governing system (road, theory, system, culture; the Party's leadership; the people's congress order founded in the 1954 Constitution) as the conclusion of that history, every lesson built from 探究与分享 discussion cases, 相关链接 source boxes and 专家点评 commentary.

China's own way (习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想学生读本·高中 (Student Reader on Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, senior-high volume)): a standalone reader (one of a four-volume primary-to-senior set) that teaches the governing thought as a complete civics of today's China — who leads (the Party), for whom (the people), in what layout (five spheres, four strategies), guarded how (security, defence, reunification) and facing the world how (peaceful development and a shared future) — each lecture closing with 学习思考 questions for the student citizen.
