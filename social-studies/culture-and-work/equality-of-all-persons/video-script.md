The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oman Grade 3 Identity & Citizenship TG T2, printed pp. 39–45, teaches: The global citizen's first right — all people are equal; the world-scale half of the rights standard opens with equality.

Oman's own way (Identity and Citizenship Grade 3, Term 2): at Grade 3 the subject turns from feelings to law-shaped habits — rights first, then duties, then the homeland held as a trust (أمانة); and the same rights-and-duties pair is then taught again at world scale, where equality, safety and being heard are the child's global rights.
