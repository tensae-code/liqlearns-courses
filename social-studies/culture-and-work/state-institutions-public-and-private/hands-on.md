# Hands-on — State Institutions Public and Private

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the state stands on institutions that share its tasks to serve citizens and residents, in two kinds — public institutions, the state institutions (ministries such as Interior, Education, Health, Defence, Justice; authorities; government hospitals), and private, commercial institutions (companies, banks, factories, markets); state institutions are the government bodies charged with serving the homeland and the citizen. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
