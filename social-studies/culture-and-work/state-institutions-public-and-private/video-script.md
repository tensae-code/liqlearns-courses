# Video script — State Institutions Public and Private

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Saudi Arabia Grade 5 Social Studies (printed pp. 106–107), teaches: The state stands on institutions that share its tasks to serve citizens and residents, in two kinds — public institutions, the state institutions (ministries such as Interior, Education, Health, Defence, Justice; authorities; government hospitals), and private, commercial institutions (companies, banks, factories, markets); state institutions are the government bodies charged with serving the homeland and the citizen.

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 5 book teaches security and state institutions as service — security is defined first, the citizen is named the first line of security, the security agencies are listed one by one with their tasks, state institutions are split into public and private, and each region's Emir receives citizens and hears their needs.
