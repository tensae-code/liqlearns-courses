# Hands-on — Myths Legends Customs and Traditions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Guía 7 Mitos, costumbres y tradiciones
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 19–25, teaches: myths (from Greek mythos, story) are traditional tales of astonishing events — Colombia's indigenous Madre Monte among them; legends and stories are cultural manifestations too; customs show in food, music and festivals, and traditions are the legacies/heritage our ancestors hand down. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
