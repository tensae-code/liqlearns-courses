# Myths Legends Customs and Traditions — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Guía 7 Mitos, costumbres y tradiciones — Colombia Grade 3 CS_Grado3_02, printed pp. 19–25, teaches: myths (from Greek mythos, story) are traditional tales of astonishing events — Colombia's indigenous Madre Monte among them; legends and stories are cultural manifestations too; customs show in food, music and festivals, and traditions are the legacies/heritage our ancestors hand down. **2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does this part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Colombia's way (Book 11) — the book's own teaching way — Colombia's own way: culture is taught through named Colombian expressions (Pasto's Carnaval de Negros y Blancos, Riosucio's Carnaval del Diablo, the Madre Monte myth) and through imagining life inside another Colombian culture — speaking Pamié or Tucano — while emotions and affection get a full primary unit of their own.
