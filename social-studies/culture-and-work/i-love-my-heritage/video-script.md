# Video script — I Love My Heritage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

United Arab Emirates Social Studies & National Education, G1 teacher guide, volume 2, printed p. 54, teaches: heritage (التراث) as what the country hands down — its customs, crafts and ways — and the child's own part in loving and keeping it, closing the Zayed and heritage unit

United Arab Emirates' own way: at six years old the course already runs on two rails — the working country (how we travel, the jobs our grandfathers did, the job I will do) and the inherited country (Zayed the father, the days we love, our heritage) — and every lesson plan is required to name the citizenship concepts it builds: belonging, citizenship, social responsibility, cooperation, national identity.
