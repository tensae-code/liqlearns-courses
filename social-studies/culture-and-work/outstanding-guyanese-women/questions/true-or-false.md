# Outstanding Guyanese Women — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: Guyanese women have been outstanding in every field — art, broadcasting, business, education, engineering, law, medicine, the military, mining, music, politics and trade union work; Janet Jagan became Guyana's first female Executive President in 1997; Jane Phillips-Gay was among the first three women elected to the Legislative Assembly in 1953; Viola Burnham served as Vice President and Deputy Prime Minister; and Desiree Patricia Bernard became the first female judge of the High Court, the first female Justice of Appeal and Chief Justice, and in 2001 the first woman appointed Chancellor of the Judiciary.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a teacher guide, with no student book.
**Answer: False — it stands on Guyana's student book, named in lesson/sources.md.**
