Guyana Grade 9 New Horizons in Social Studies Book 3 (printed pp. 57–65), teaches: Guyanese women have been outstanding in every field — art, broadcasting, business, education, engineering, law, medicine, the military, mining, music, politics and trade union work; Janet Jagan became Guyana's first female Executive President in 1997; Jane Phillips-Gay was among the first three women elected to the Legislative Assembly in 1953; Viola Burnham served as Vice President and Deputy Prime Minister; and Desiree Patricia Bernard became the first female judge of the High Court, the first female Justice of Appeal and Chief Justice, and in 2001 the first woman appointed Chancellor of the Judiciary.

Guyana's own way: the secondary book teaches the same citizenship at national scale — CARICOM as the region's own institution, the family as society's oldest institution, women's equality written into the 1980 Constitution and the Acts of the 1990s, and the state answering pollution through the Environmental Protection Agency.
