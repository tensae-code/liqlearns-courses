# Hands-on — Outstanding Guyanese Women

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Guyanese women have been outstanding in every field — art, broadcasting, business, education, engineering, law, medicine, the military, mining, music, politics and trade union work; Janet Jagan became Guyana's first female Executive President in 1997; Jane Phillips-Gay was among the first three women elected to the Legislative Assembly in 1953; Viola Burnham served as Vice President and Deputy Prime Minister; and Desiree Patricia Bernard became the first female judge of the High Court, the first female Justice of Appeal and Chief Justice, and in 2001 the first woman appointed Chancellor of the Judiciary. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
