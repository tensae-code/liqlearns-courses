# Hands-on — Groups and Our Roles

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: people who share aims and values form groups — family (our first group and society's foundation), relatives, school, teams and clubs; we hold several roles at once (child at home, student at school, class president, goalkeeper), and every role brings its own rights and responsibilities; society itself continues through the harmony of its groups and their support for each other Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way
Say how Turkey's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
