# Groups and Our Roles — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Groups and our roles — people who share aims and values form groups — family (our first group and society's foundation), relatives, school, teams and clubs; we hold several roles at once (child at home, student at school, class president, goalkeeper), and every role brings its own rights and responsibilities; society itself continues through the harmony of its groups and their support for each other (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the second part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Turkey's way — the book's own teaching way (any close wording).**
