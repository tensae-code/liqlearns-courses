Turkey Grade 5 Sosyal Bilgiler 1. Kitap, printed p. 12, teaches: people who share aims and values form groups — family (our first group and society's foundation), relatives, school, teams and clubs; we hold several roles at once (child at home, student at school, class president, goalkeeper), and every role brings its own rights and responsibilities; society itself continues through the harmony of its groups and their support for each other

Turkey's own way: the middle-school course opens with living together, not with land or dates: a ten-year-old first maps the groups she belongs to and the rights and duties each role carries, then learns that Turkey's many cultures are an aşure — many different ingredients in one pot making one good taste — and that helping has an old Turkish technology for protecting the receiver's dignity, the sadaka taşı (charity stone).
