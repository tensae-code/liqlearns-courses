# Hands-on — Womens Equality and Rights

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: the slow Bolivian road — Ateneo Femenino 1923, divorce law 1932, the municipal vote 1945, universal suffrage 1952, the Bartolina Sisa federation 1983 — today's gaps in education, pay and the digital treatment of women's bodies, and the emblematic women of Bolivian democracy from Juana Azurduy and Adela Zamudio to Domitila Barrios and Remedios Loza. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bolivia's way
Say how Bolivia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
