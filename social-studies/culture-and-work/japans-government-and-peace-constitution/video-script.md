# Video script — Japans Government and Peace Constitution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samoa Year 10 Book 1 (printed p. 19), teaches: Japan's democratic government under its Peace Constitution — the Emperor as titular head, the two-house Diet as sole law-making organ, Prime Minister and Cabinet as Executive, the court system, and what government ministries are responsible for.

Samoa's own way: Government is taught as Samoa's own Westminster-style parliament first (matai seats, the Individual Voters Roll, its parties and its real 2001 election, problems included), then Japan's Peace-Constitution government as the comparison; rights are taught from the classroom's own rights list outward to the UN and Amnesty.
