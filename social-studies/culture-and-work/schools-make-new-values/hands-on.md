# Hands-on — Schools Make New Values

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: The school practises society's customs and values and also forms new ones — the class chooses norms and values the society still lacks, writes a guideline for practising them in family, school and institutions, and practises them all year through the active civic club. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bangladesh's way
Say how Bangladesh's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
