**Acceptance count: taught in 2 countries (Palestine, Rwanda).**

Rwanda's own way (Primary Two — Rwanda P2 SB Kiny): P2 civics starts with who you belong to (small family, wide family, the exact Kinyarwanda name for every relation), then the two smallest rungs of the state — the village and the cell, their elected committees and what umuganda owes them — and the behaviour of peace: playing without discrimination, listening without shouting, and the rights of children and of people with disabilities

Rwanda's own way (Primary Three — Rwanda P3 SB Kiny): P3 widens duty from the household to the state: an extended-family member's responsibilities are listed as supporting government programmes (health insurance, cooperatives, sending children to school), and local democracy is taught as a procedure — who may stand, how campaigns run, how votes are cast, counted and announced, and the pledge to vote at 18
