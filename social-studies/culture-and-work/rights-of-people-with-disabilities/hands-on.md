# Hands-on — Rights of People with Disabilities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rights of People with Disabilities
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: National legislation and international conventions affirm the rights of persons with disabilities — respect for their dignity, independence and freedom to choose their decisions, full and effective participation in society, and equality in rights and duties with other, non-disabled persons — and providing the material environment that enables their participation, in buildings, roads, housing, means of transport and joining education; among these conventions is the International Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities of 2006, which the State of Palestine joined in April 2014, and Palestine has its own disability law, issued in 2009 as the book prints it. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
