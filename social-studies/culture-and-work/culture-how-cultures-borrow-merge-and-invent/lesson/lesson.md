# Culture — how cultures borrow, merge and invent

## Do it first

List inventions made in your own area. Pick one and trace its cultural effect: what changed because it exists?

## Sub lesson 1 — Diffusion and acculturation

**Diffusion:** a culture **borrows from another and changes** — religion, food crops, animals, dress and language standards all travel this way.

**Acculturation:** through **long contact** a society takes **many things, or whole culture-complexes**, from another — and lets its own go.

## Sub lesson 2 — Assimilation and invention

**Assimilation:** a society **abandons its own culture and takes another's wholesale** — typically when people leave their homeland and join another culture. It is a road by which **minority identities can disappear**.

**Invention:** making a **new thing** — a tool, an idea, a food, a dress — which through history has repeatedly driven **great cultural change**.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion: Tell diffusion from acculturation in one sentence each. Say what assimilation risks for minority identities. Say what invention is and what it has repeatedly driven.
