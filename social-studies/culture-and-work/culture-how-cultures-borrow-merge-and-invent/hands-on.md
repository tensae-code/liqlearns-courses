# Hands-on — Culture — how cultures borrow, merge and invent

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Activity 1
List inventions made in your own area. Pick one and trace its cultural effect: what changed because it exists?

## Activity 2
Debate with a partner, using the lesson's words: are Ethiopia's cultures mixing, or holding? Give one example for your side.

## With a partner
Tell your companion: Tell diffusion from acculturation in one sentence each. Say what assimilation risks for minority identities. Say what invention is and what it has repeatedly driven.
