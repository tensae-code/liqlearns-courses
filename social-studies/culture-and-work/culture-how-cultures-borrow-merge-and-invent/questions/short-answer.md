# Culture — how cultures borrow, merge and invent — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What travels by diffusion? Name three.
**Answer:** Any three of: religion, food crops, animals, dress, language standards.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** When does assimilation typically happen?
**Answer:** When people leave their homeland and join another culture.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** How is acculturation stronger than diffusion?
**Answer:** Acculturation takes many things or whole culture-complexes through long contact, and lets the society's own go.
