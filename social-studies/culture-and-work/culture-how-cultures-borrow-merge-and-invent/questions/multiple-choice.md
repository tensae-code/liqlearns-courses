# Culture — how cultures borrow, merge and invent — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A culture borrows one thing from another and changes. That is —
a) Assimilation  b) Diffusion  c) Invention  d) Taxation
**Answer: b) Diffusion**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Through long contact, a society takes whole culture-complexes and lets its own go. That is —
a) Diffusion  b) Acculturation  c) Invention  d) Barter
**Answer: b) Acculturation**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Assimilation means a society —
a) Invents a tool  b) Borrows one crop  c) Abandons its own culture and takes another's wholesale  d) Trades goods
**Answer: c) Abandons its own culture and takes another's wholesale**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Making a new tool, idea, food or dress is —
a) Acculturation  b) Assimilation  c) Diffusion  d) Invention
**Answer: d) Invention**
