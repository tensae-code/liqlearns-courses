# Hands-on — Al Fazaa Rushing to Help

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Al-faz'a — rushing to help — as an authentic value rooted in Kuwaiti society, shown when the rains of 1934 (the first Hadama, the demolition year) destroyed homes and the community rebuilt together. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
