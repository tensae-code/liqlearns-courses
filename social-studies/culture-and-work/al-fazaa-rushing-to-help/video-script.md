# Video script — Al Fazaa Rushing to Help

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 8 Term 2 (printed pp. 103–104), teaches: Al-faz'a — rushing to help — as an authentic value rooted in Kuwaiti society, shown when the rains of 1934 (the first Hadama, the demolition year) destroyed homes and the community rebuilt together.

Kuwait's own way: Grade 8 Term 2 frames the whole Islamic world as one body of challenges and answers — wars, contested borders and besieged minorities on one side; on the other, an organisation with principles (self-determination, human rights, rule of law) and Kuwait itself cast as the helper-state — its Fund, its charities, its Red Crescent — while the values unit keeps the same virtues at home scale: unity, altruism, rushing to help, the mother's home clinic.
