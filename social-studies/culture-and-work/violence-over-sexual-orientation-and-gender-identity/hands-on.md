# Hands-on — Violence over Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Experience 4 content theme "Violencia contra personas por orientaciones sexuales y de género"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 9 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana III TG, printed pp. 71–76, teaches: the Yogyakarta Principles' definitions; the Inter-American Court's ruling that orientation is bound to freedom and self-determination; the forms and contexts of violence against LGBTI people (UNICEF), including spiritual and cultural violence when an indigenous person is cut off from their community. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 4) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: the oldest básico pupils study the newest faces of violence — the teacher's own authority, the phone in the pupil's pocket, and prejudice over identity — each answered from human-rights law and the peoples' own values.. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
