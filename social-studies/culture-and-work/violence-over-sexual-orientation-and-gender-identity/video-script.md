# Video script — Violence over Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala Grade 9 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana III TG, printed pp. 71–76, teaches: the Yogyakarta Principles' definitions; the Inter-American Court's ruling that orientation is bound to freedom and self-determination; the forms and contexts of violence against LGBTI people (UNICEF), including spiritual and cultural violence when an indigenous person is cut off from their community.

Guatemala's own way: the oldest básico pupils study the newest faces of violence — the teacher's own authority, the phone in the pupil's pocket, and prejudice over identity — each answered from human-rights law and the peoples' own values.
