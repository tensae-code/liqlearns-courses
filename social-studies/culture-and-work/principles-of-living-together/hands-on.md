# Hands-on — Principles of Living Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Unit 1 "Principios de la convivencia humana"
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala Grade 4 Ciencias Sociales y Formación Ciudadana, printed p. 46, teaches: four principles for living in peace — respect others' rights, fulfil responsibilities at home, school and in society, take part in group activities and decisions, be solidary and live in harmony — each matched to everyday examples. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Guatemala's way (Book 5) — the book's own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: Guatemala's own way: citizenship is woven through every unit of the history/geography book — a fourth-grader meets the three powers, the human-rights declaration, the civil registry and the ballot box inside the same volume that tells the national story, under the unit motto "Vivamos en Armonía" (Let us live in harmony).. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
