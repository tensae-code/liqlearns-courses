**Acceptance count: taught in 3 countries (Mongolia, Mexico, Serbia).**

Mexico's own way (Grade 7 Ética, naturaleza y sociedades): one combined volume per grade under the Nueva Escuela Mexicana, built as teacher-written narrative articles grouped by discipline area; civics is taught as rights-in-action — discrimination, violence and public service are each opened from the pupil's own school and community, and Mexico's pluricultural nationhood is anchored in Article 2 of its own Constitution.

Mongolia's own way: the Grade 7 year works at the scale of the child's own life — my groups, my culture, my family's economy and budget, our school — before the local-area year in Grade 8. That way is stocked here as the way Mongolia teaches this thing. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.


Serbia's own way (Grade 3 Природа и друштво): the third-grade book widens "my region" into the whole republic — its many peoples named one by one as equal citizens, work divided into making and serving, and the environment taught as a duty: soil, water and parks protected by rules every child can keep.
