# Critical Attitude Against Injustice

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: a critical attitude analyses a situation or information before deciding; we must not be indifferent before injustice and inequality, nor stay silent — say what you think analytically and reasonably; responsibility before injustice is a quality of the good citizen, practised through solidarity, equality, respect and tolerance — as Honduras's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Honduras's way — the books' own teaching way

Honduras's own way: Grade 5 teaches democracy as a continental story — Latin America's dictatorships and transitions, integral citizenship (political, civil, social), social movements from the 1954 banana strike onward — while the pupil's own rehearsal is the student government with its annual operating plan and suggestion box.
