# Critical Attitude Against Injustice — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: a critical attitude analyses a situation or information before deciding; we must not be indifferent before injustice and inequality, nor stay silent — say what you think analytically and reasonably; responsibility before injustice is a quality of the good citizen, practised through solidarity, equality, respect and tolerance.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson stands on a student book, with no teacher guide.
**Answer: True**
