# Hands-on — Critical Attitude Against Injustice

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Lección 9 Debemos generar una actitud crítica
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: A critical attitude analyses a situation or information before deciding; we must not be indifferent before injustice and inequality, nor stay silent — say what you think analytically and reasonably; responsibility before injustice is a quality of the good citizen, practised through solidarity, equality, respect and tolerance. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
