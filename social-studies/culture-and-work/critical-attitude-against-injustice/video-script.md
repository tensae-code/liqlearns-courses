# Video script — Critical Attitude Against Injustice

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras Grade 5 G5 Student CCSS, printed p. 50, teaches: A critical attitude analyses a situation or information before deciding; we must not be indifferent before injustice and inequality, nor stay silent — say what you think analytically and reasonably; responsibility before injustice is a quality of the good citizen, practised through solidarity, equality, respect and tolerance.

Honduras's own way (Book 5): Grade 5 teaches democracy as a continental story — Latin America's dictatorships and transitions, integral citizenship (political, civil, social), social movements from the 1954 banana strike onward — while the pupil's own rehearsal is the student government with its annual operating plan and suggestion box.
