# Hands-on — About My Country Mauritius

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book’s topic
Work through this lesson’s topic with your hands: The Republic of Mauritius as three islands — Mauritius, Rodrigues and Agalega — naming your island and your country. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Mauritius’s way
Say how Mauritius’s book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
