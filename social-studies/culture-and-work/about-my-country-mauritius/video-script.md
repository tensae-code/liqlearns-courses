# Video script — About My Country Mauritius

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot’s written form until then.

---

Mauritius Grade 1 VACE Teacher’s Handbook (printed p. 70), teaches: The Republic of Mauritius as three islands — Mauritius, Rodrigues and Agalega — naming your island and your country.

Mauritius’s own way: Values taught through the child’s widening circles — self, people around me, then "I love my country" — with Mauritius’s own flag colours, anthem words, and its three islands (Mauritius, Rodrigues, Agalega) as the identity content, and unity taught through the country’s many festivals.
