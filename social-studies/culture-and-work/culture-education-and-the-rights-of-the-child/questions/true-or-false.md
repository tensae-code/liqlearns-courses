# Culture Education and the Rights of the Child — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's first part teaches: To be a cultured person in the information-computer civilisation — a person's level of culture and education now directly decides his place in production and society, so education, "the basis of a civilised society", stands at the centre of the social rights: the right to education (Article 26 of the Universal Declaration; Article 49 of the Constitution, on accessible and free general secondary and vocational education); and the rights of the child — the legislative defence of children's rights drawn as a scheme of three documents: the Declaration of the Rights of the Child (1959), the Convention on the Rights of the Child (1989) and the Law of the Republic of Belarus "On the Rights of the Child" (1993), childhood lasting to 18, with the child's own duties to society, the state and his family set beside his rights.
