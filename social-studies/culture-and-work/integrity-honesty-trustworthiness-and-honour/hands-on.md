# Hands-on — Integrity Honesty Trustworthiness and Honour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Integrity practised — honesty (the song "Always speak the truth" and a secret-code worksheet that decodes into its maxims — truth wins, honesty is the best policy, truth will set you free), trustworthiness (the big-mouth fox, whose friends forgive him only when he learns to keep his word) and honour (the person I honour, drawn and described in five sentences, and an Honouring Table of the days I celebrated). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Bhutan's way
Say how Bhutan's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
