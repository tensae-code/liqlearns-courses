## Sub lesson 1 — Integrity Honesty Trustworthiness and Honour

Bhutan Class III Values and Citizenship Education Student Workbook 2017, printed pp. 40–52, teaches: integrity practised — honesty (the song "Always speak the truth" and a secret-code worksheet that decodes into its maxims — truth wins, honesty is the best policy, truth will set you free), trustworthiness (the big-mouth fox, whose friends forgive him only when he learns to keep his word) and honour (the person I honour, drawn and described in five sentences, and an Honouring Table of the days I celebrated); and Bhutan Class III Values and Citizenship Education Teacher's Handbook 2017, printed pp. 42–54, teaches: TEACHER: integrity as mind, speech and action held in line with conscience — honesty (speaking the truth, taking nothing that is not yours, admitting your mistakes), trustworthiness (the big-mouth fox, who lost his friends' trust by telling their secret and earned it back by keeping his word) and honour (holding people in esteem for noble service — Bhutan's Teachers' Day falls on 2 May, the third Druk Gyalpo's birthday). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bhutan's way — the book's own teaching way

Bhutan's own way: the workbook turns each value into a week of doing — a sorry-card actually drawn and given, a day planned to the minute, kind acts collected as leaves on a classroom Kindness Tree until they earn a fruit, and obedience sealed with written pledges to teacher and parents; the values are Bhutan's own (Tha-Dam-Tse Ley-Judy, Tsawa Sum) carried in stories the whole world tells (the Giving Tree, Little Red Riding Hood, the Itsy Bitsy Spider). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
