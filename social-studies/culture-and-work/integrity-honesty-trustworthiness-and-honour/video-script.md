# Video script — Integrity Honesty Trustworthiness and Honour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bhutan Class III Values and Citizenship Education Student Workbook 2017, printed pp. 40–52, teaches: integrity practised — honesty (the song "Always speak the truth" and a secret-code worksheet that decodes into its maxims — truth wins, honesty is the best policy, truth will set you free), trustworthiness (the big-mouth fox, whose friends forgive him only when he learns to keep his word) and honour (the person I honour, drawn and described in five sentences, and an Honouring Table of the days I celebrated); and Bhutan Class III Values and Citizenship Education Teacher's Handbook 2017, printed pp. 42–54, teaches: TEACHER: integrity as mind, speech and action held in line with conscience — honesty (speaking the truth, taking nothing that is not yours, admitting your mistakes), trustworthiness (the big-mouth fox, who lost his friends' trust by telling their secret and earned it back by keeping his word) and honour (holding people in esteem for noble service — Bhutan's Teachers' Day falls on 2 May, the third Druk Gyalpo's birthday).

Bhutan's own way: the workbook turns each value into a week of doing — a sorry-card actually drawn and given, a day planned to the minute, kind acts collected as leaves on a classroom Kindness Tree until they earn a fruit, and obedience sealed with written pledges to teacher and parents; the values are Bhutan's own (Tha-Dam-Tse Ley-Judy, Tsawa Sum) carried in stories the whole world tells (the Giving Tree, Little Red Riding Hood, the Itsy Bitsy Spider).
