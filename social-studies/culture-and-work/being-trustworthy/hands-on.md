# Hands-on — Being Trustworthy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Being Trustworthy
Work through this part with your hands: trustworthiness as evaluating how you discharge your responsibilities, working hard for your own and the community's benefit, and avoiding behaviour that jeopardises your integrity and good relationships. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Being Moral and Ethical
Work through this part with your hands: moral means principles of right and wrong behaviour — responsibility, transparency, trustworthiness and obedience; use the Internet wisely, taking knowledge that fits Tanzanian values and avoiding immoral content; a moral person's acts cannot be questioned — secrecy, unexplained money and possessions breed doubt and mistrust, while transparency and accountability breed trust. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Tanzania's way — the books' own teaching way
Work through this part with your hands: say how Tanzania's books teach this thing, in your own words, from the lesson. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
