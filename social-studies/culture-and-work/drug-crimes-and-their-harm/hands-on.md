# Hands-on — Drug Crimes and Their Harm

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: What drug use and drug possession mean, the elements of the use-or-possession crime, and what the drug crime does to the person and to society. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Kuwait's way
Say how Kuwait's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
