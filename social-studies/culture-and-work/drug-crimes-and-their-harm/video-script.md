# Video script — Drug Crimes and Their Harm

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). This script is full: the placements name a video-script need for this lesson. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Kuwait Grade 9 Term 1 (printed pp. 99–103), teaches: What drug use and drug possession mean, the elements of the use-or-possession crime, and what the drug crime does to the person and to society.

Kuwait's own way: the law strand that began in Grade 8 with what law is ends in Grade 9 with crime — the pupil learns to separate a crime from a merely wrong act, the three elements every crime must have, how crimes rank by the danger they pose to society's security, and the drug crime worked end to end, from use and possession to the harm done to person and society.
