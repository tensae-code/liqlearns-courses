## Sub lesson 2 — Bangladesh's way — the book's own teaching way

Bangladesh's own way (Grade 6): An inquiry-based combined History and Social Science course taught through a class teacher character (Khushi Apa) and group investigations; civics begins not with definitions but with a football game played with and without rules, and sociology is built from a village flood story (the Dharmagola grain bank) before any term is named.. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
