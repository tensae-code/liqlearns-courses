# Hands-on — National Culture What Makes Us Us

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: national culture as a people's way of life and the values, ideas and customs they share; its components — religion and values, language, traditional crafts, folk arts, customs and traditions, stories, antiquities, foods and clothing — in Saudi examples (Saudi coffee, historic Jeddah, the Ardah dance, al-Masmak Palace, heritage dress). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Saudi Arabia's way
Say how Saudi Arabia's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
