## Sub lesson 1 — National Culture What Makes Us Us

Saudi Arabia Grade 4 Social Studies (printed pp. 18–19), teaches: National culture as a people's way of life and the values, ideas and customs they share; its components — religion and values, language, traditional crafts, folk arts, customs and traditions, stories, antiquities, foods and clothing — in Saudi examples (Saudi coffee, historic Jeddah, the Ardah dance, al-Masmak Palace, heritage dress). This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Saudi Arabia's way — the book's own teaching way

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 4 book opens with citizenship — national identity, the system of government, national symbols and the founding story of security and unity under one state — and returns in Unit 6 to responsible citizenship through family and society, rights and duties, the good citizen, teamwork, and heritage stories that teach patience and hard work. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
