Saudi Arabia Grade 4 Social Studies (printed pp. 18–19), teaches: National culture as a people's way of life and the values, ideas and customs they share; its components — religion and values, language, traditional crafts, folk arts, customs and traditions, stories, antiquities, foods and clothing — in Saudi examples (Saudi coffee, historic Jeddah, the Ardah dance, al-Masmak Palace, heritage dress).

Saudi Arabia's own way: the Grade 4 book opens with citizenship — national identity, the system of government, national symbols and the founding story of security and unity under one state — and returns in Unit 6 to responsible citizenship through family and society, rights and duties, the good citizen, teamwork, and heritage stories that teach patience and hard work.
