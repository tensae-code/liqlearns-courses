# Hands-on — How Leaders Are Chosen Elections and Appointment

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Government leaders are elected by the people's votes (council members, parliament members); others are appointed through hiring on qualifications (principals, doctors, teachers). Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
