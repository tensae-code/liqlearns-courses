# Hands-on — Our Rights and Duties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: Rules keep order and harmony for the common good; children's rights (to learn, to be safe, to be healthy); responsibilities at home, school and in the community — the three fit together like puzzle pieces. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.

## Maldives's way
Say how Maldives's book teaches this thing, in your own words, from the lesson.
