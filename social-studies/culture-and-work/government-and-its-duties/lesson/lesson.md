# Government and Its Duties

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: how is a country administered? — think of a great ship at sea: to reach its destination its different parts must work together, and the one who creates that coordination is the captain (ناخدا); society is the same — it is formed of different parts, each with a duty of its own toward people's needs, and a society can do its work well only when connection and coordination between its parts are established; establishing that coordination in every society is the government's charge, and it uses laws and special instructions for it; our country's government carries out the laws and commands of the religion of Islam — the Islamic Republic — and the highest office in its order is the Leader of Iran, who supervises all the country's affairs; the government's duties include law-making, executing the laws, guarding the country's borders against the enemy's attacks, and creating welfare and social justice for all the people — as Iran's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 2 — Iran's way — the book's own teaching way

Iran's own way: the special-needs secondary book is a citizen's primer in the plainest form — needs first (things, then love and company), then the circles that meet them: family, groups, institutions — and at the top, government and the Majlis, explained through a ship and its captain and a school and its rules.
