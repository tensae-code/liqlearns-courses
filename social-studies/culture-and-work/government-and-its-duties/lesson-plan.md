# Lesson plan — Government and Its Duties

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Culture and Work · **Lesson:** Government and Its Duties
**Place in the curriculum:** Culture and Work (CURRICULUM.md) — built in the Social Studies remaining-strands expansion (Iran, 2026-11-17).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** how is a country administered? — think of a great ship at sea: to reach its destination its different parts must work together, and the one who creates that coordination is the captain (ناخدا); society is the same — it is formed of different parts, each with a duty of its own toward people's needs, and a society can do its work well only when connection and coordination between its parts are established; establishing that coordination in every society is the government's charge, and it uses laws and special instructions for it; our country's government carries out the laws and commands of the religion of Islam — the Islamic Republic — and the highest office in its order is the Leader of Iran, who supervises all the country's affairs; the government's duties include law-making, executing the laws, guarding the country's borders against the enemy's attacks, and creating welfare and social justice for all the people — as Iran's books teach it (see lesson/sources.md for the book and printed page).
