# Government and Its Duties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does the first part of this lesson teach?
**Answer: Government and Its Duties — how is a country administered? — think of a great ship at sea: to reach its destination its different parts must work together, and the one who creates that coordination is the captain (ناخدا); society is the same — it is formed of different parts, each with a duty of its own toward people's needs, and a society can do its work well only when connection and coordination between its parts are established; establishing that coordination in every society is the government's charge, and it uses laws and special instructions for it; our country's government carries out the laws and commands of the religion of Islam — the Islamic Republic — and the highest office in its order is the Leader of Iran, who supervises all the country's affairs; the government's duties include law-making, executing the laws, guarding the country's borders against the enemy's attacks, and creating welfare and social justice for all the people (any close wording).**
