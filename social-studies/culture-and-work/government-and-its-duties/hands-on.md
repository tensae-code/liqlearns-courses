# Hands-on — Government and Its Duties

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The book's topic
Work through this lesson's topic with your hands: how is a country administered? — think of a great ship at sea: to reach its destination its different parts must work together, and the one who creates that coordination is the captain (ناخدا); society is the same — it is formed of different parts, each with a duty of its own toward people's needs, and a society can do its work well only when connection and coordination between its parts are established; establishing that coordination in every society is the government's charge, and it uses laws and special instructions for it; our country's government carries out the laws and commands of the religion of Islam — the Islamic Republic — and the highest office in its order is the Leader of Iran, who supervises all the country's affairs; the government's duties include law-making, executing the laws, guarding the country's borders against the enemy's attacks, and creating welfare and social justice for all the people. Say each part out loud as you do it, from the lesson.
