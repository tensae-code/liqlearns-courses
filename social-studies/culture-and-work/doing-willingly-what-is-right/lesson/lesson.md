# Doing Willingly What is Right

## Do it first

Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think this is about: stepping forward to do the good thing without being told — as Japan's books teach it. Hold your idea; the lesson will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — よいと思うことはすすんで (Willingly do what you think is right)

Japan Grades 1–2 Watashitachi no Doutoku (printed p. 32), teaches: Stepping forward to do the good thing without being told. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Japan's way (Book 1) — the book's own teaching way

Japan's own way (Book 1): MEXT's own moral reader for the youngest pupils is built from short stories and picture tales around four value topics per unit — orderly living, doing your own work, doing willingly what is right, and honesty — each story followed by talk-it-over pages for school, home and neighbourhood. This lesson teaches the placements' thing in the course's own words — the book's text itself is never transcribed.
