# Manifestations of flourishing of the ancient Arab civilisations in Asia — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Manifestations of flourishing of the ancient Arab civilisations in Asia.
**Answer: yemen's kings rule by shura with a general council and market laws inscribed on a pillar at Tamna', mint coins, work tens of mines, and write in 29-letter Musnad while their doctors embalm and operate; the Gulf kings of Dilmun and Magan grow rich on pearls and re-export; Mesopotamia gives Hammurabi's 282-article code, cuneiform, Ashurbanipal's classified library, the Gilgamesh epic and the ziggurats; and the Levant's city-kingdoms turn cuneiform into the 22-letter alphabet at Ugarit and pass it through Phoenicia to Greece, Rome and the alphabets of today (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
