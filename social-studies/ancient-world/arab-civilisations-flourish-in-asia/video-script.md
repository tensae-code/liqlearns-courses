# Video script — Manifestations of flourishing of the ancient Arab civilisations in Asia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yemen's kings rule by shura with a general council and market laws inscribed on a pillar at Tamna', mint coins, work tens of mines, and write in 29-letter Musnad while their doctors embalm and operate; the Gulf kings of Dilmun and Magan grow rich on pearls and re-export; Mesopotamia gives Hammurabi's 282-article code, cuneiform, Ashurbanipal's classified library, the Gilgamesh epic and the ziggurats; and the Levant's city-kingdoms turn cuneiform into the 22-letter alphabet at Ugarit and pass it through Phoenicia to Greece, Rome and the alphabets of today.
