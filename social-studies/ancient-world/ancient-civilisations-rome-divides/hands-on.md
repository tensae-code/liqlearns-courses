# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — Rome divides

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Split-map drawing
Draw the Mediterranean as a wavy band. Put Rome on the west, Constantinople on the east, colour the halves differently and label languages and fall dates.

## Thousand-year line
Stretch a string across a room as years 330–1453. Peg 476 near the start and 1453 at the far end — feel how long the East endured.
