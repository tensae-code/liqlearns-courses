# Ancient civilisations — Rome divides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The empire split in order to —
a) Trade more  b) Defend itself  c) Grow crops  d) Please the Senate
**Answer: b) Defend itself** — Too wide to hold as one.
**2.** The Eastern half's capital was —
a) Rome  b) Athens  c) Constantinople  d) Crete
**Answer: c) Constantinople** — Greek-speaking, founded by Constantine in 330 AD.
**3.** The West fell in 476 AD to —
a) Ottoman Turks  b) Germanic peoples — Goths and Visigoths  c) Persia  d) Macedon
**Answer: b) Germanic peoples — Goths and Visigoths** — The Ottomans end the EAST, in 1453.
**4.** The East endured until —
a) 476 AD  b) 753 AD  c) 1453 AD  d) 330 AD
**Answer: c) 1453 AD** — Another 1,000 years after the split's western fall.
