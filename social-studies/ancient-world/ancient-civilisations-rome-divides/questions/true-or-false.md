# Ancient civilisations — Rome divides — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Western empire was Latin-speaking with its capital at Rome.
**Answer: True** — The East was Greek-speaking.
**2.** Both halves of the empire fell in 476 AD.
**Answer: False** — Only the West fell in 476; the East lasted to 1453.
**3.** Constantine founded Constantinople in 330 AD.
**Answer: True** — The eastern capital carries his name.
