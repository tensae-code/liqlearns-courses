# Ancient civilisations — Rome divides — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why did the Roman empire split, and what were the halves?
**Answer:** It split to defend itself: the West (Latin, capital Rome) and the East/Byzantine empire (Greek, capital Constantinople).
**2.** How much longer did the East last than the West?
**Answer:** About 1,000 years — the West fell in 476 AD, the East in 1453 AD.
