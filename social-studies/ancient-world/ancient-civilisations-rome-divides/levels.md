# Ancient civilisations — Rome divides — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Explain the split: West and East, their languages and capitals, and why the empire divided.; Give the two end dates — West 476 AD, East 1453 AD — and who ended each.. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
