# Mahmud Tarabi Uprising in Bukhara — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 23. The uprising of Mahmud Tarabi.
**Answer: a Bukhara sieve-maker raises the poor against Mongol tax-men and local lords in 1238; the revolt is crushed but remembered as the people's own rising (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* (Uzbekistan), 23. The uprising of Mahmud Tarabi, printed p. 78.**
