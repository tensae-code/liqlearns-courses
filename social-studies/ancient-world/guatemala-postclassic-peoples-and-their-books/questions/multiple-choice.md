# Guatemala — Postclassic peoples and their books — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Q'umarkaj was the capital of the —
a) K'iche'  b) Aztecs  c) Inca
**Answer: a) K'iche'** — the highland kingdom the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Iximche' was the capital of the —
a) Kaqchikel  b) Spanish crown  c) Olmecs
**Answer: a) Kaqchikel** — the Kaqchikel capital.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Popol Wuj is —
a) one of the peoples' own books, carrying their account of creation and kings  b) a Spanish tax list  c) a colonial law code
**Answer: a) one of the peoples' own books, carrying their account of creation and kings** — the indigenous book the lesson names.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Postclassic, the Kaqchikel —
a) broke from the K'iche' confederation  b) left the Americas  c) built Tikal
**Answer: a) broke from the K'iche' confederation** — the break the book tells.
