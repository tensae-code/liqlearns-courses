# Guatemala — Postclassic peoples and their books — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Postclassic kingdoms both fought and made alliances.
**Answer: True** — fight and ally, as the book tells it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Popol Wuj was written by Spanish conquerors.
**Answer: False** — it is one of the peoples' own books.
