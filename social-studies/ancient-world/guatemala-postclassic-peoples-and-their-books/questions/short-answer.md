# Guatemala — Postclassic peoples and their books — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two Postclassic peoples besides the K'iche' and Kaqchikel.
**Answer: Any two of: Itza', Lacandón, Mam, Poqomam, Poqomchi', Q'eqchi', Tz'utujil, Lenca (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do the peoples' own books matter for history?
**Answer: They carry the peoples' own account of creation and kings, in their own voice (any close wording).**
