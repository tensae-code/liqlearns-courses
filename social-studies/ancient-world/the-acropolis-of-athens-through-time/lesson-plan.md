# Lesson plan — The Acropolis Of Athens Through Time

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Acropolis Of Athens Through Time
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** from Mycenaean citadel to Pericles's temples to church, mosque and powder magazine blown up in 1687, one rock keeps being re-read by every age
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Teaching hour 1: What the Acropolis's history reveals about the place's meaning over time** — from Mycenaean citadel to Pericles's temples to church, mosque and powder magazine blown up in 1687, one rock keeps being re-read by every age
2. **Teaching hour 2: What the art, sculpture and architecture of the Acropolis reveal about their makers** — the Parthenon frieze's procession becomes an argument — scholars dispute what it shows, and pupils build their own comic from the stones
3. **Teaching hour 3: Deepening — heritage, restoration and the marbles** — restoration scaffolds, Stuart and Revett's 1765 drawings, du Moncel's 1845 watercolour and the demand for the Parthenon marbles' return ask who the monument belongs to

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
