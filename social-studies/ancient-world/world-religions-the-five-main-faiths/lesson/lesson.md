# World religions — the five main faiths

## Do it first

Look at the sky before a storm, at a river in flood, at the moon changing shape. The first religions were born in that wonder. Now count the faiths around your own town — how many can you name? The world holds thousands; five hold most of humankind. Meet them evenly, one by one.

## Sub lesson 1 — The five main faiths

Religion's root is placed in **ancient people's wonder at natural events** — worship of **rivers, trees, mountains, the Moon**. **Over 4,300 religions** are said to exist; **five hold three-quarters of humanity**. **Christianity**: one God, the Creator's laws — no stealing, no adultery, love others as yourself — and eternal life; begun in **Bethlehem, Judaea, in the 1st century**, and spread empire-wide in the 4th when **Constantine** accepted it. **Islam**: taught openly by the **Prophet Muhammad (born 570)** against Quraysh idolatry in Arabia; unifying Arabia, then spreading after his death to **Egypt, Tunisia, Algeria, Mesopotamia, Palestine, Spain, Morocco**; its teaching — belief in Allah and the Prophet's messengership, fasting, prayer, alms for the needy, the Hajj. **Judaism**: begun by the **ancient Israelites before 1500 BC**; one God, the Creator's laws, God guiding and protecting; followers in Israel and worldwide, keeping **Moses' teaching**. **Hinduism**: begun in **India about 1500 BC** (also called Brahmanism); life and soul flow on like a river; many gods — **Vishnu the preserver, Shiva creator-destroyer**, as printed. **Buddhism**: founded by the Indian **Siddharta Gautama about 500 BC**; no creator god; the teaching of balanced self-control, neither extreme approved; kept in **Korea, Tibet, China and Japan**. ## Sub lesson 2 — Christianity spreads (teacher guide — evidence)

The teacher guide gives Christianity its own thread: it **emerged in Palestine in the 1st century AD** and **spread throughout the world to become a major universal faith**. Its keyed answers name the pivotal imperial figures: **Emperor Constantine** and **Emperor Theodosius** — the emperors under whom the faith moved from persecuted sect to the empire's own religion. The guide's review key adds **Bethlehem** as the faith's birthplace fact. Trace the spread in the picture: one small land, then the whole Roman world.

## Sub lesson 3 — Islam rises (teacher guide — evidence)

The guide teaches Islam's rise as world history, in a vocabulary set. The early Arabs were **Semitic-speaking nomads of the Arabian Peninsula, polytheists before Islam**. **Islam** is the religion **preached by the Prophet Muhammad in the 7th century AD**: the word **'Islam' means submission to the will of God**; **Allah** is the Arabic name for God; believers are called **Muslims**; the holy book is the **Quran**. Muhammad was persecuted by the **Quraysh — the powerful merchant tribe that controlled Mecca and its Kaaba** — and his flight from Mecca to Medina is the **Hejira**, the journey the picture draws.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the five faiths with one beginning and one teaching for each; then Christianity's two emperors; then Islam's six key words — Islam, Allah, Muslim, Quran, Quraysh, Hejira.
