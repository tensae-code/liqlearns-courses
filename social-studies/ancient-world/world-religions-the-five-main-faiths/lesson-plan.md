# Lesson plan — World religions — the five main faiths

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** World religions — the five main faiths
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 7 — The Ancient World (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Five faiths — **Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, Buddhism** — hold **three-quarters of humanity** among **over 4,300 religions**; this lesson meets each one evenly, then follows **Christianity's spread** and **Islam's rise** in the guide's own evidence.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the lessons before it in this title — the learner has met the ancient civilisations and medieval states named so far in this title's order. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or being made — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The five main faiths** — Religion's root in wonder at nature; 4,300 religions, five holding three-quarters of humanity; each faith's beginning and core teaching, told evenly.
2. **Christianity spreads (teacher guide — evidence)** — Christianity from Palestine in the 1st century AD to a universal faith; Constantine and Theodosius as its pivotal emperors; Bethlehem.
3. **Islam rises (teacher guide — evidence)** — The guide's vocabulary of Islam's beginnings: Semitic nomads, Muhammad, Islam as submission, Allah, Muslims, the Quran, the Quraysh, and the Hejira from Mecca to Medina.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: the earlier lessons of this title, in the title's order — most nearest, the lesson just before this one. Recommended, never forced.
- A learner may jump straight in here: the lesson stands alone, and names are explained where they first appear.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the five main faiths and give each one's beginning and core teaching as the book tells it.
2. Tell Christianity's spread with Constantine and Theodosius (teacher guide — evidence).
3. Give the guide's key words of Islam's beginning: Islam, Allah, Muslim, Quran, Quraysh, Hejira (teacher guide — evidence).
