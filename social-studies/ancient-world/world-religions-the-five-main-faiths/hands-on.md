# Hands-on — World religions — the five main faiths

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Five-panel gallery
Draw five equal panels on one big sheet — one per faith — with its building, beginning and one teaching. Equal size is the point: the gallery itself teaches even-handedness.

## Interview a faith elder
With an adult's help, interview a religious father or elder of any faith: how did religion begin, and what does your faith teach about treating others? Report back in the book's own task shape.
