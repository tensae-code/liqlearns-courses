# World religions — the five main faiths — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Where did religion's root lie, in the book's telling?
**Answer:** In ancient people's wonder at natural events — worship of rivers, trees, mountains and the Moon.
**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Give the guide's account of Islam's beginning in three facts.
**Answer:** Islam was preached by the Prophet Muhammad in the 7th century AD among the Semitic-speaking Arabs; 'Islam' means submission to the will of God; persecuted by the Quraysh of Mecca, Muhammad's flight to Medina is the Hejira (teacher guide — evidence).
