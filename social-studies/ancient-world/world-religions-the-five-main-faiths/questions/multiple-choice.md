# World religions — the five main faiths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The five main faiths together hold —
a) Half of humanity  b) Three-quarters of humanity  c) One-quarter  d) Almost no one
**Answer: b) Three-quarters of humanity** — Among over 4,300 religions said to exist.
**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Christianity began in —
a) Rome  b) Bethlehem, Judaea, in the 1st century  c) Mecca  d) India
**Answer: b) Bethlehem, Judaea, in the 1st century** — And spread empire-wide in the 4th under Constantine.
**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Buddhism was founded by —
a) Moses  b) Vishnu  c) Siddharta Gautama, about 500 BC  d) Constantine
**Answer: c) Siddharta Gautama, about 500 BC** — An Indian teacher; Buddhism has no creator god.
**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's pivotal emperors for Christianity are —
a) Osman and Orhan  b) Constantine and Theodosius  c) Justinian and Philip  d) Pericles and Cleisthenes
**Answer: b) Constantine and Theodosius** — Keyed in the guide's Activity 4.4 (teacher guide — evidence).
**5. (Sub lesson 3)** 'Islam' means —
a) Peace through war  b) Submission to the will of God  c) The merchant tribe  d) The holy book
**Answer: b) Submission to the will of God** — The guide's vocabulary set (teacher guide — evidence).
**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The Hejira is —
a) The holy book  b) Muhammad's flight from Mecca to Medina  c) A tax  d) A temple
**Answer: b) Muhammad's flight from Mecca to Medina** — Fleeing Quraysh persecution (teacher guide — evidence).
