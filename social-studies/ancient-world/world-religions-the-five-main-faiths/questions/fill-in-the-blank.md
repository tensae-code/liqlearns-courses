# World religions — the five main faiths — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Islam's teaching includes fasting, prayer, alms for the needy and the ____.
**Answer: Hajj**
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Christianity emerged in ____ in the 1st century AD.
**Answer: Palestine**
**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The ____ were the powerful merchant tribe controlling Mecca and its Kaaba.
**Answer: Quraysh**
