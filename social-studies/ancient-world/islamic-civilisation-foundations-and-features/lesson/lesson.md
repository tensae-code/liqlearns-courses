# Islamic civilisation — what it is and what marks it

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Kuwait's Grade 8 book puts Islamic civilisation first among civilisations — a civilisation of faith, knowledge and building, known by marks learners can spot in any city, book or building it left. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Islamic civilisation as the most important civilisation

Learners weigh the great civilisations against each other and argue why the islamic one, joining faith, knowledge and building, stands first for them.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: islamic civilisation as the most important civilisation; the concept of islamic civilisation and the bases it rose on; characteristics of islamic civilisation. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
