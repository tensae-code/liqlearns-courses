# Islamic civilisation — what it is and what marks it — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What three things does Islamic civilisation join?
**Answer: Faith, knowledge and building (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What do learners do in this lesson?
**Answer: Weigh the great civilisations against each other and argue the Islamic one's place.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the pieces of the definition.
**Answer: Values, ethics, inventions and works (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** What two sides of the civilisation do learners sort?
**Answer: Its material side and its spiritual side.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Why learn a civilisation's characteristics?
**Answer: So learners can spot them in any city, book or building it left behind (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the lesson present the characteristics?
**Answer: Drawn out one by one.**
