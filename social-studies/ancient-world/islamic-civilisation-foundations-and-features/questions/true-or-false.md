# Islamic civilisation — what it is and what marks it — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book treats Islamic civilisation as a minor offshoot.
**Answer: False** — it argues the Islamic civilisation stands first

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Comparing civilisations is part of how the book teaches.
**Answer: True** — learners weigh the great civilisations against each other

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Islamic civilisation is only its buildings and inventions.
**Answer: False** — it has material and spiritual sides — values and ethics too

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A civilisation's definition can be rebuilt from its pieces.
**Answer: True** — learners rebuild it from values, ethics, inventions and works

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A civilisation's marks can only be seen in its capital.
**Answer: False** — they can be spotted in any city, book or building it left

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Characteristics are a civilisation's recognisable marks.
**Answer: True** — the marks are drawn out one by one
