# Islamic civilisation — what it is and what marks it — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson asks learners to —
a) weigh the great civilisations against each other  b) memorise one date  c) copy a map
**Answer: a) weigh the great civilisations against each other** — learners weigh the great civilisations against each other

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Islamic civilisation joins —
a) faith, knowledge and building  b) armies, taxes and ports  c) pearls, ships and caravans
**Answer: a) faith, knowledge and building** — joining faith, knowledge and building

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's argument it stands —
a) first among civilisations  b) last among civilisations  c) outside civilisation
**Answer: a) first among civilisations** — it stands first for them

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The definition is rebuilt from —
a) values, ethics, inventions and works  b) dates and battles  c) kings and capitals
**Answer: a) values, ethics, inventions and works** — values, ethics, inventions and works

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Islamic civilisation has —
a) a material side and a spiritual side  b) only a material side  c) only a spiritual side
**Answer: a) a material side and a spiritual side** — learners sort its material and spiritual sides

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** To define a civilisation, learners —
a) rebuild the definition from its pieces  b) copy it from a dictionary only  c) skip the definition
**Answer: a) rebuild the definition from its pieces** — learners rebuild the definition from its pieces

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The civilisation's characteristics are —
a) marks learners can spot in its cities, books and buildings  b) secret signs  c) only in its coins
**Answer: a) marks learners can spot in its cities, books and buildings** — learners can spot them in any city, book or building

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The lesson draws the marks out —
a) one by one  b) all in one date  c) from one building only
**Answer: a) one by one** — the marks are drawn out one by one

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** A learner who knows the marks can —
a) recognise the civilisation wherever it left remains  b) name every caliph  c) read any manuscript
**Answer: a) recognise the civilisation wherever it left remains** — spot them in any city, book or building it left behind
