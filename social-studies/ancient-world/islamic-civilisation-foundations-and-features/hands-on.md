# Hands-on — Islamic civilisation — what it is and what marks it

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Make a weighing-scales drawing: Islamic civilisation on one side, another great civilisation on the other — list what each pan holds.

## Task 2
Write your own verdict in three sentences, with one reason.

## Task 3
Sort six cards — mosque, hospital, prayer, algebra book, honesty, bridge — into material and spiritual piles.

## Task 4
Write your own one-sentence definition of Islamic civilisation from its pieces.

## Task 5
Play spotter: describe a city scene and list which Islamic-civilisation marks you can spot in it.

## Task 6
Write a field guide entry: three marks to look for in an old Islamic city.
