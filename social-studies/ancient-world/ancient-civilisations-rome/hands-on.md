# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — Rome

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hill-city model
Mound soil or stack books as Rome's hill, run a blue ribbon or string as the Tiber below, and place three paper bands for the classes beside it.

## Legion march
March a measured route in step as a group — out fast, back in order. Discipline and fast movement, felt in the legs, are two of the army's marks.

## Road-builder's brief (Grade 9)
You are a Roman engineer ordered to bind a new province: plan the road, one bridge and one aqueduct on a sketch map, and write the brief in three lines — then add the legal gift Rome sends with them: citizenship for all free males, in time.
