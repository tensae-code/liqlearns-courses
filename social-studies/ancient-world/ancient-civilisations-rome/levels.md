# Ancient civilisations — Rome — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Tell Rome's founding (753 BC, Latin tribes, the Romulus and Remus legend, Etruscan rule) and name its gods.; Recite the three classes with what each held, and explain Rome's strength: the army's marks and the republic's order.. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
