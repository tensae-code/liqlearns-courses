# Ancient civilisations — Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What made Rome's expansion possible?
**Answer:** A strong army — universal service, strict discipline, fast movement and good treatment of the conquered — under a republic of elected councils, the Senate and consuls.
**2.** Who founded Rome, where and when?
**Answer:** Latin tribes founded Rome in 753 BC on a hill above the River Tiber.
