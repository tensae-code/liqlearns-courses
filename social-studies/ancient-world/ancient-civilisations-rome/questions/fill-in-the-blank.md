# Ancient civilisations — Rome — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Rome stands on a hill above the River ____.
**Answer: Tiber**
**2.** The founding legend names Romulus and ____.
**Answer: Remus**
**3.** Rome's officials in the republic were called ____.
**Answer: consuls**
**4. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'the conventional year of the Western Empire's fall' is ______.
**Answer: 476 AD**
