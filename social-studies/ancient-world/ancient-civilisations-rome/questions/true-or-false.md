# Ancient civilisations — Rome — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Etruscans held Rome for about 250 years from about 750 BC.
**Answer: True** — And they were more civilised than the early Romans.
**2.** Neptune was Rome's god of war.
**Answer: False** — Mars was war's god; Neptune ruled the sea, Venus love.
**3.** War-captive slaves worked on patrician estates.
**Answer: True** — Slavery stood under the three classes.
