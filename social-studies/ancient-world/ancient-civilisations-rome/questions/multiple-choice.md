# Ancient civilisations — Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Rome was founded in —
a) 338 BC  b) 753 BC  c) 476 AD  d) 221 BC
**Answer: b) 753 BC** — By Latin tribes, on a hill above the Tiber.
**2.** Rome's ruling class, holding power and great land, was the —
a) Plebeians  b) Equites  c) Patricians  d) Helots
**Answer: c) Patricians** — Equites held trade wealth; Plebeians worked small.
**3.** Rome's army was marked by —
a) Universal service, strict discipline, fast movement, good treatment of the conquered  b) Mercenaries only  c) Sea power only  d) Slow heavy columns
**Answer: a) Universal service, strict discipline, fast movement, good treatment of the conquered** — The book's four marks, whole.
**4.** Above Rome's elected councils stood —
a) The consuls  b) The unelected Senate  c) The assembly of slaves  d) The Etruscan king
**Answer: b) The unelected Senate** — Consuls served as officials under it.

**5.** Rome began about — on hills above the —.
a) 509 BC, Nile  b) 753 BC, Tiber  c) 312 AD, Po
**Answer: b) 753 BC, Tiber** — 753 BC, the Tiber.

**6.** The Republic dates from —
a) 509 BC  b) 753 BC  c) AD 313
**Answer: a) 509 BC** — Kingdom, then Republic (509 BC), then Empire.

**7.** Roman law is the foundation of legal systems in —
a) only Italy  b) most of Western Europe and Latin America  c) China and India
**Answer: b) most of Western Europe and Latin America** — The book's legacy claim.

**8.** Roman engineering gave —
a) roads, bridges, aqueducts  b) canals only in Egypt  c) paper mills
**Answer: a) roads, bridges, aqueducts** — The engineering triad.

**9.** Citizenship eventually came to —
a) senators only  b) all free males  c) soldiers only
**Answer: b) all free males** — The empire-wide grant.
**10. (Grade 11 depth)** What is citizenship?
a) the conventional year of the Western Empire's fall
b) Rome's spreading legal membership, binding an empire's peoples
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: b) Rome's spreading legal membership, binding an empire's peoples**
