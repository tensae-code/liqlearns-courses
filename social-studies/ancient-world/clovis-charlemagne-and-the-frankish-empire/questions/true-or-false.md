# Clovis Charlemagne And The Frankish Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clovis unites the Franks, accepts baptism at Reims and wins the Church for his dynasty; as Merovingian kings fade into "do-nothing" rulers, their mayors of the palace take power — Charles Martel stops the Arabs in 732, Pepin founds the Carolingian line and carves out the Papal State, and Charlemagne, crowned emperor in Rome in 800, builds an empire whose court school at Aachen sparks a Carolingian Renaissance — before Verdun splits it into France, Italy and Germany.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 1: the state of the Franks

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istorija 7 (History 7), Grade 7* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 1: the state of the Franks

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Clovis's baptism wins the Church for the Franks; Charles Martel halts the Arabs, Pepin takes a crown, and Charlemagne's Aachen court sparks a renaissance before Verdun splits his empire three ways — Otto I's imperial coronation in 962 refounds an "empire" that is really a loose league of princes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 2: the Franks and the Holy Roman Empire

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istorija I (History I), Grade 10* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 2: the Franks and the Holy Roman Empire
