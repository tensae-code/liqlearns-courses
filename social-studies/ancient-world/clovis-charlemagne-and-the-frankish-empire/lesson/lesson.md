# Clovis Charlemagne And The Frankish Empire

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Clovis unites the Franks, accepts baptism at Reims and wins the Church for his dynasty; as Merovingian kings fade into "do-nothing" rulers, their mayors of the palace take power As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 1: the state of the Franks

Clovis unites the Franks, accepts baptism at Reims and wins the Church for his dynasty; as Merovingian kings fade into "do-nothing" rulers, their mayors of the palace take power — Charles Martel stops the Arabs in 732, Pepin founds the Carolingian line and carves out the Papal State, and Charlemagne, crowned emperor in Rome in 800, builds an empire whose court school at Aachen sparks a Carolingian Renaissance — before Verdun splits it into France, Italy and Germany
