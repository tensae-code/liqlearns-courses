## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Theme 1: reforms and the empire's second rise** — with Avars at the walls, Heraclius rebuilds the state into soldier-farmer themes and Greek replaces Latin; three centuries later the Macedonian dynasty brings a Byzantine renaissance — Basil I, a peasant from the theme of Macedonia, seizes the throne, and Basil II extends the empire again
2. **Theme 2: Byzantium survives and renews itself** — Heraclius answers siege and collapse with soldier-farmer themes; the empire tears itself over icons, splits from Rome for good in 1054, flowers again under the Macedonian dynasty — then eats its own strength by granting pronoia lands to magnates
