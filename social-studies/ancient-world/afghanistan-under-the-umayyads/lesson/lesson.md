# Afghanistan under the Umayyads

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Umayyads, a house of Quraysh, hold the caliphate from 41 AH/661 CE for ninety years and Islam spreads widely under them, Afghanistan included As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
