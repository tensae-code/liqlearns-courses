# Lesson plan — Afghanistan under the Umayyads

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Afghanistan under the Umayyads
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Afghanistan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Umayyads, a house of Quraysh, hold the caliphate from 41 AH/661 CE for ninety years and Islam spreads widely under them, Afghanistan included
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
