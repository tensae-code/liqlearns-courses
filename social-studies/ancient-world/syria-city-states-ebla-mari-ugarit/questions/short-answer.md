# Syria's city-states — Ebla, Mari and Ugarit — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: From city to kingdom — Ebla, Mari and Ugarit.
**Answer: Syria's cities grow into city-states, then kingdoms — Ebla, Mari and Ugarit each rule their own land. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Syria's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ — الصف السابع الأساسي (History, Grade 7)* (Grade 7), Unit 3, Lesson 1: من المدينة إلى المملكة (From city to kingdom), printed p. 73.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Ebla's tablet library, Mari's palace and Ugarit's alphabet.
**Answer: Ebla (Tell Mardikh) near Aleppo kept a huge library of thousands of clay tablets, Mari on the Euphrates had its royal palace, temples and statues, and Ugarit (Ras Shamra) invented the alphabetic writing it gave to all peoples, its tablets kept in the National Museum in Damascus.
