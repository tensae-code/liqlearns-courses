# Syria's city-states — Ebla, Mari and Ugarit — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Syria's cities grow into city-states, then kingdoms — Ebla, Mari and Ugarit each rule their own land.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 3, Lesson 1: من المدينة إلى المملكة (From city to kingdom)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف السابع الأساسي (History, Grade 7)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about From city to kingdom — Ebla, Mari and Ugarit

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ebla (Tell Mardikh) near Aleppo kept a huge library of thousands of clay tablets, Mari on the Euphrates had its royal palace, temples and statues, and Ugarit (Ras Shamra) invented the alphabetic writing it gave to all peoples, its tablets kept in the National Museum in Damascus.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2, Lesson سورية مهد الحضارة (Syria, cradle of civilisation)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الرابع (Social Studies, Grade 4)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ebla's tablet library, Mari's palace and Ugarit's alphabet
