# Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia's gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** How was a cuneiform tablet made?
**Answer:** A reed stylus pressed wedge-shaped picture-signs into a wet clay tablet, which was then sun-dried hard so the record kept.
**2.** Name four gifts of Mesopotamia.
**Answer:** Any four of: bronze weapons, the plough, sailboats, astronomy, mathematics for administration, the 60-minute hour, the 360-degree circle, cuneiform writing.
