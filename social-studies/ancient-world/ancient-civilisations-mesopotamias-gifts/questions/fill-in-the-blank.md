# Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia's gifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Mesopotamia gave farming the ____.
**Answer: plough**
**2.** A circle holds ____ degrees, a Mesopotamian measure.
**Answer: 360**
**3.** Cuneiform picture-signs were pressed with a ____ stylus.
**Answer: reed**
