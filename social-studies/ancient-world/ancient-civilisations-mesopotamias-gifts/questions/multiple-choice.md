# Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia's gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Mesopotamia was first to use bronze for —
a) Coins  b) Weapons  c) Ships  d) Tablets
**Answer: b) Weapons** — Bronze weapons — and the plough — are the book's first gifts.
**2.** The hour of 60 minutes comes from —
a) Rome  b) Egypt  c) Mesopotamia  d) Greece
**Answer: c) Mesopotamia** — Their mathematics for administration gave both measures.
**3.** Cuneiform means —
a) Sun-dried  b) Wedge-shaped  c) Picture-perfect  d) River-written
**Answer: b) Wedge-shaped** — A Latin word for the wedge marks the reed left.
**4.** Cuneiform signs were pressed into —
a) Stone walls  b) Papyrus  c) Sun-dried clay tablets  d) Wooden boards
**Answer: c) Sun-dried clay tablets** — Pressed wet, then dried hard in the sun.
