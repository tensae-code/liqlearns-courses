# Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia's gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The 360-degree circle is a Mesopotamian measure.
**Answer: True** — Alongside the 60-minute hour.
**2.** Cuneiform was written with a brush and ink.
**Answer: False** — A reed stylus pressed the signs into clay.
**3.** Mesopotamia's mathematics served administration.
**Answer: True** — Counting for the state's taxing, storing and building.
