# Video script — Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia's gifts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mesopotamia gave gifts we still use.
Bronze. The plough. The sailboat.
Sixty minutes in an hour — theirs.
Three hundred and sixty degrees in a circle — theirs.
And writing: wedges pressed in clay. Cuneiform.
Press. Lift. Press. The record stays.
