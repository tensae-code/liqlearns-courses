# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia's gifts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Press your own tablet
Use soft clay, mud or dough and a reed or matchstick end. Press five wedges in a row, then a second row. Let it sun-dry — you have made a cuneiform tablet the old way.

## Count like a scribe
Count 60 matchsticks into one pile for the hour, then draw a circle and mark it 360. Say aloud whose numbers these are.
