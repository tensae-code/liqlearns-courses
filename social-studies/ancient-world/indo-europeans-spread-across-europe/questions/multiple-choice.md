# The Indo-Europeans spread across Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The settlement of the Indo-Europeans  b) The settlement of the Slavs in Europe  c) Ancient Rus — Kiev binds the East Slavic lands
**Answer: a) The settlement of the Indo-Europeans** — this part tells the settlement of the Indo-Europeans

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) steppe horsemen between the Danube and the Volga spread their languages from India to the Atlantic, becoming Balts, Slavs, Germans and others  b) in the Great Migration the Slavs pour between the Danube, Elbe and Dnieper and divide into western, southern and eastern unions  c) Kiev's grand princes bind the East Slavic lands into one state whose wars and tribute reach Constantinople
**Answer: a)** — steppe horsemen between the Danube and the Volga spread their languages from India to the Atlantic, becoming Balts, Slavs, Germans and others
