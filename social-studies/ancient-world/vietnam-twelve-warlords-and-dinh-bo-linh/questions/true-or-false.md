# Vietnam Twelve Warlords And Dinh Bo Linh — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the buffalo-herding boy of Hoa Lư who drilled children with reed flags grows into the leader who, in 966–967, by turns soft and hard, defeats or wins over all twelve warlords and founds the Đinh.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 9, the Twelve Warlords and Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's unification

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 9, the Twelve Warlords and Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's unification
