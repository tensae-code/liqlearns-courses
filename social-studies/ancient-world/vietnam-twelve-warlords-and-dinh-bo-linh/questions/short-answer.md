# Vietnam Twelve Warlords And Dinh Bo Linh — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 9, the Twelve Warlords and Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's unification.
**Answer: the buffalo-herding boy of Hoa Lư who drilled children with reed flags grows into the leader who, in 966–967, by turns soft and hard, defeats or wins over all twelve warlords and founds the Đinh (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 9, the Twelve Warlords and Đinh Bộ Lĩnh's unification, printed p. 46.**
