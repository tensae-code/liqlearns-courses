# China Northern Song Centralisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fearing another warlord age, Song Taizu invites his generals to wine and persuades them to trade their armies for estates — 'a cup of wine releases military power' — then splits command so generals never own their troops.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Northern Song politics

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Northern Song politics

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Song's founders trade military glory for civil rule — strong trunk, weak branches — gaining order and losing armies that can win.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Song politics and military

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Song politics and military
