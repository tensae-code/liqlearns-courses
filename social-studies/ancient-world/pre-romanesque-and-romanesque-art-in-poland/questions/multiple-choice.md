# Pre-Romanesque and Romanesque art in Poland — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) First stone churches and doors  b) A new Christian state builds  c) Europe's story, in Polish stone
**Answer: b) A new Christian state builds** — The new Christian Polish state begins to build in stone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) First stone churches and doors  b) A new Christian state builds  c) Europe's story, in Polish stone
**Answer: a) First stone churches and doors** — Poland's first stone churches and cathedral doors rise in the same styles as the West.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) A new Christian state builds  b) First stone churches and doors  c) Europe's story, in Polish stone
**Answer: c) Europe's story, in Polish stone** — Within a generation of its baptism, Poland joins Europe's story in stone.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The new Christian Polish state builds its first stone churches and cathedral doors in the same styles as the West — Poland joins Europe's story in stone within a generation of its baptism  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
