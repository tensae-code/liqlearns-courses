# Hands-on — Pre-Romanesque and Romanesque art in Poland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw one of Poland's first stone churches and its carved cathedral doors from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: A new Christian state builds; First stone churches and doors; Europe's story, in Polish stone. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
