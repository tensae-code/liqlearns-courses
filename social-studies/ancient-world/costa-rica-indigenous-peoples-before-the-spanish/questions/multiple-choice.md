# Costa Rica's indigenous peoples before the Spanish — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —

**Answer: a) Los pueblos originarios del territorio que hoy es Costa Rica** — Costa Rica's own first peoples are introduced in their territories before the Spanish — the national past begins with its indigenous societies, not with colonisation
