# Arab-Islamic civilisation — science, literature and art — short answer

**Answer: Islam appears in seventh-century Arabia and turns scattered tribes into one nation; the caliphate runs from the Rashidun through the Umayyads of Damascus — Arabic diwans, Islamic coinage — to the Abbasids of Baghdad and the House of Wisdom; Medina's charter stands as the first written constitution; Muslim scholars translate Greece and Persia, add discoveries of their own, and Latin translations of their books become a foundation of Europe's Renaissance.
