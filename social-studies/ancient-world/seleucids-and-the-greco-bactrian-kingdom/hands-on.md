# Hands-on — Seleucids and the Greco Bactrian Kingdom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Seleucids and the Greco Bactrian Kingdom — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, §§41–42 The Seleucid state and the Greco-Bactrian kingdom, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
