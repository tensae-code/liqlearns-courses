# Hands-on — Vietnam Le So Dai Viet

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Le So Dai Viet — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 17, the founding of the Lê sơ and its state (1428), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
