# Vietnam Le So Dai Viet — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 17, the founding of the Lê sơ and its state (1428)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Lê Lợi takes the throne, restores the name Đại Việt and the capital Thăng Long; the emperor holds all power down a chain of central organs, Đạo/Thừa tuyên, phủ, huyện and xã, and the Quốc triều hình luật (the Hồng Đức code, 722 articles) is praised by a later scholar as "truly a model for governing the country"

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 17, Lê sơ economy, territory and society
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — agriculture commissioners, a ban on leaving fields waste, and the quân điền equal-field system restore the villages ("in Thái Tổ's and Thái Tông's reigns, rice filled the fields and the buffaloes were too full to eat"); export kilns at Chu Đậu and Bát Tràng fill foreign orders — some 240,000 Chu Đậu ceramics were raised from the Cù Lao Chàm shipwreck — and in 1471 the border reaches the Cù Mông pass, while Lê Thánh Tông orders that not one inch of the ancestors' land be yielded

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 17, Lê sơ culture, education and its great figures
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Confucianism reigns alone; the Hội Tao đàn poetry society gathers 28 members around Lê Thánh Tông; Ngô Sĩ Liên (doctorate 1442) compiles the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, Lương Thế Vinh (first laureate 1463) writes his mathematics, and from 1484 the doctors' stelae rise at Văn Miếu under Thân Nhân Trung's line "talented men are the nation's vital spirit"

## Sub lesson 4 — Chuyên đề 3.II Lê Thánh Tông (1442–1497): the reforming king — administration rebuilt into thirteen circuits, the Hồng Đức law code of 722 articles, the Tao Đàn poetry society, examinations regularised, and the 1473 edict that not one foot of the Thái Tổ's mountains or rivers may be traded away
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a king who writes poetry, maps and laws also writes the border doctrine his country still quotes

## Sub lesson 5 — Bài 10 Lê Thánh Tông's reforms (1400s): after child kings and the Lệ Chi Viên injustice, the king strips the great mandarins, perfects the Six Ministries with Six Offices and Six Departments watching them, redraws five circuits into thirteen thừa tuyên (1466–1471), promulgates the 722-article Hồng Đức code (1483) protecting women, the old, the disabled and children, reorganises the army, shares land by quân điền down to orphans and widows, and holds twelve examinations passing over 500 tiến sĩ
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a king builds a machine of mutual checks so well that the chronicle says the country's posture became hard to shake
