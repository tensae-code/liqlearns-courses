# Vietnam Le So Dai Viet — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 17, the founding of the Lê sơ and its state (1428).
**Answer: Lê Lợi takes the throne, restores the name Đại Việt and the capital Thăng Long; the emperor holds all power down a chain of central organs, Đạo/Thừa tuyên, phủ, huyện and xã, and the Quốc triều hình luật (the Hồng Đức code, 722 articles) is praised by a later scholar as "truly a model for governing the country" (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 17, the founding of the Lê sơ and its state (1428), printed p. 83.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 17, Lê sơ economy, territory and society.
**Answer: agriculture commissioners, a ban on leaving fields waste, and the quân điền equal-field system restore the villages ("in Thái Tổ's and Thái Tông's reigns, rice filled the fields and the buffaloes were too full to eat"); export kilns at Chu Đậu and Bát Tràng fill foreign orders — some 240,000 Chu Đậu ceramics were raised from the Cù Lao Chàm shipwreck — and in 1471 the border reaches the Cù Mông pass, while Lê Thánh Tông orders that not one inch of the ancestors' land be yielded (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 17, Lê sơ culture, education and its great figures.
**Answer: Confucianism reigns alone; the Hội Tao đàn poetry society gathers 28 members around Lê Thánh Tông; Ngô Sĩ Liên (doctorate 1442) compiles the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư, Lương Thế Vinh (first laureate 1463) writes his mathematics, and from 1484 the doctors' stelae rise at Văn Miếu under Thân Nhân Trung's line "talented men are the nation's vital spirit" (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
