# Palaeolithic minds and cave art — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Palaeolithic people wrote books about their beliefs.
**Answer: False** — they wrote nothing; burials, images and objects are all that speak for them.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A grave with goods in it is evidence that its makers thought about death.
**Answer: True** — burials are read as the voice of people who thought deeply but wrote nothing.
