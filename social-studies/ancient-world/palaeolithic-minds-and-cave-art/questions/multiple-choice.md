# Palaeolithic minds and cave art — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Palaeolithic people left no writing, so we learn what they thought from —
a) their burials, images and objects  b) their books  c) later kings' records
**Answer: a) their burials, images and objects** — the only voice left by people who wrote nothing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A burial with objects placed beside the dead tells us Palaeolithic people —
a) thought about death and what follows it  b) had writing  c) lived in cities
**Answer: a) thought about death and what follows it** — the grave goods are the evidence of the thought.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The images Palaeolithic people made are best described as —
a) their only voice, since they wrote nothing  b) shopping lists  c) maps of villages
**Answer: a) their only voice, since they wrote nothing** — images and objects speak for minds that left no words.
