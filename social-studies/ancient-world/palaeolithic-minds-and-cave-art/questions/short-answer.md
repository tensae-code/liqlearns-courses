# Palaeolithic minds and cave art — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What are the only voices left by Palaeolithic people?
**Answer: Their burials, images and objects.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why must we be careful when we say what Palaeolithic people believed?
**Answer: They wrote nothing, so every claim rests on reading objects and images as evidence (any close wording).**
