# Hands-on — Palaeolithic minds and cave art

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw a cave wall: paint the animals your band hunts, then write one sentence on what a future historian could — and could not — prove from your picture.

## Task 2
Bury a time-capsule box (drawn or real) with three objects that show what you think; swap with a companion and read their mind from their objects.
