# Palaeolithic minds and cave art

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: palaeolithic people thought deeply but wrote nothing — their burials, images and objects are the only voice they left, and we read their minds from what they made and how they buried their dead. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.
