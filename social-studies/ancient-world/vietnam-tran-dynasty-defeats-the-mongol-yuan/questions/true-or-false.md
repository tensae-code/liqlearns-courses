# Vietnam Tran Dynasty Defeats The Mongol Yuan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** three invasions (1258, 1285, 1287–88) are met by Trần Thủ Độ's "my head has not yet fallen — do not worry", the elders of Diên Hồng shouting "Fight!", and young Trần Quốc Toản, barred from the war council, crushing an orange in his fist and raising his own flag.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 10, the resistance against the Mongol–Yuan invasions

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 10, the resistance against the Mongol–Yuan invasions

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Mongols' three envoys are seized at Thăng Long; 30,000 men invade from Yunnan; the court empties the capital ("empty gardens, empty houses"), Trần Thủ Độ tells the anxious king "my head has not yet fallen to the ground — Your Majesty need not worry", and the counterstroke at Đông Bộ Đầu frees Thăng Long within the month.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 14, the first resistance, 1258

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 14, the first resistance, 1258

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** at the Diên Hồng conference the grey-haired elders shout "Fight!" as one voice; soldiers tattoo "Sát Thát" (Kill the Tartars) on their arms; young Trần Quốc Toản, barred from the war council for his age, crushes an orange in his fist and raises a thousand men; the captured Trần Bình Trọng refuses every bribe — "I would rather be a ghost of the South than a king of the North".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 14, the second resistance, 1285

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 14, the second resistance, 1285

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Trần Khánh Dư destroys the Yuan supply fleet at Vân Đồn; asked how the year will go, Trần Quốc Tuấn answers "this year fighting the enemy will be more leisurely"; in April 1288 Ô Mã Nhi's fleet is driven onto the Bạch Đằng stake field at ebb tide, fire ships finish it, and he is taken alive.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 14, the third resistance and Bạch Đằng, 1287–1288

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 14, the third resistance and Bạch Đằng, 1287–1288

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Trần Quốc Tuấn sums it up — "king and subjects in concord, brothers in harmony, the whole country contributing its strength"; the three victories shield Japan and Southeast Asia from the Yuan and weaken the Mongol empire itself.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 14, why Đại Việt won, and what it meant

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 14, why Đại Việt won, and what it meant

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** the general who refused surrender in the throne room wins the river battle that ends an empire's ambition.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chuyên đề 3.III Trần Quốc Tuấn (?–1300): appointed Quốc công tiết chế, he answers the frightened king "if Your Majesty wants to surrender, first cut off my head", empties the country before the Mongol storm, then strikes the decisive battle on the Bạch Đằng in 1288 and writes the Binh thư yếu lược for future armies

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** This part of *Chuyên đề học tập Lịch sử 11 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
