# Vietnam Tran Dynasty Defeats The Mongol Yuan — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 10, the resistance against the Mongol–Yuan invasions
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — three invasions (1258, 1285, 1287–88) are met by Trần Thủ Độ's "my head has not yet fallen — do not worry", the elders of Diên Hồng shouting "Fight!", and young Trần Quốc Toản, barred from the war council, crushing an orange in his fist and raising his own flag

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 14, the first resistance, 1258
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Mongols' three envoys are seized at Thăng Long; 30,000 men invade from Yunnan; the court empties the capital ("empty gardens, empty houses"), Trần Thủ Độ tells the anxious king "my head has not yet fallen to the ground — Your Majesty need not worry", and the counterstroke at Đông Bộ Đầu frees Thăng Long within the month

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 14, the second resistance, 1285
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — at the Diên Hồng conference the grey-haired elders shout "Fight!" as one voice; soldiers tattoo "Sát Thát" (Kill the Tartars) on their arms; young Trần Quốc Toản, barred from the war council for his age, crushes an orange in his fist and raises a thousand men; the captured Trần Bình Trọng refuses every bribe — "I would rather be a ghost of the South than a king of the North"

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 14, the third resistance and Bạch Đằng, 1287–1288
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Trần Khánh Dư destroys the Yuan supply fleet at Vân Đồn; asked how the year will go, Trần Quốc Tuấn answers "this year fighting the enemy will be more leisurely"; in April 1288 Ô Mã Nhi's fleet is driven onto the Bạch Đằng stake field at ebb tide, fire ships finish it, and he is taken alive

## Sub lesson 5 — Bài 14, why Đại Việt won, and what it meant
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Trần Quốc Tuấn sums it up — "king and subjects in concord, brothers in harmony, the whole country contributing its strength"; the three victories shield Japan and Southeast Asia from the Yuan and weaken the Mongol empire itself

## Sub lesson 6 — Chuyên đề 3.III Trần Quốc Tuấn (?–1300): appointed Quốc công tiết chế, he answers the frightened king "if Your Majesty wants to surrender, first cut off my head", empties the country before the Mongol storm, then strikes the decisive battle on the Bạch Đằng in 1288 and writes the Binh thư yếu lược for future armies
