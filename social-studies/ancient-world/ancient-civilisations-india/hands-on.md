# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — India

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Brick-city builders
Build a tiny street of brick-shaped blocks or mud bricks: houses in a straight row, a pot beside each door. You are laying out Mohenjo-daro in miniature.

## Ruins detective
Bury three household objects in sand or soil, then dig them up carefully and write what each one proves about its owner — the archaeologist's game.
