# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — India

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Brick-city builders
Build a tiny street of brick-shaped blocks or mud bricks: houses in a straight row, a pot beside each door. You are laying out Mohenjo-daro in miniature.

## Ruins detective
Bury three household objects in sand or soil, then dig them up carefully and write what each one proves about its owner — the archaeologist's game.

## Standard weights (Grade 9)
The Indus cities measured and weighed alike. Make three 'standard' weights from stones or clay, mark them, and use them to 'trade' classroom goods with another group — then say why shared measures make long-distance trade possible.
