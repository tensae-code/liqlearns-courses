# Ancient civilisations — India — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Date India's beginning (about 2500 BC), name its two valleys and its Dravidian founders.; Say what Harappa and Mohenjo-daro's ruins prove, and how the civilisation ended about 1500 BC.; Tell the 4th-century BC turn: Alexander, Chandragupta Maurya, and India's first dynasty (321 BC–185 AD) unifying the north.. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
