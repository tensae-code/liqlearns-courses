# Ancient civilisations — India — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** India's civilisation began in the Indus and ____ valleys.
**Answer: Ganges**
**2.** The Aryan invasion drove the Dravidians ____.
**Answer: south**
**3.** Chandragupta Maurya ____ the north under his dynasty.
**Answer: unified**
