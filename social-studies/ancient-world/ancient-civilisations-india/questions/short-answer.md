# Ancient civilisations — India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What ended the Indus civilisation about 1500 BC?
**Answer:** Climate disasters struck, and Aryan tribes invaded the north, driving the Dravidians south and founding small states.
**2.** How do we know the Indus civilisation was real and organised?
**Answer:** Its ruins at Harappa and Mohenjo-daro — brick houses, clay vessels and household goods — prove an organised, city-building civilisation.
