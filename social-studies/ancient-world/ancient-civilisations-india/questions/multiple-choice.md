# Ancient civilisations — India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** India's civilisation began about —
a) 3500 BC  b) 2500 BC  c) 753 BC  d) 321 BC
**Answer: b) 2500 BC** — In the Indus and Ganges valleys.
**2.** The founders were the —
a) Aryans  b) Greeks  c) Dravidian peoples  d) Mauryas
**Answer: c) Dravidian peoples** — The Aryans came later, about 1500 BC.
**3.** Harappa and Mohenjo-daro prove the civilisation by their —
a) Gold coins  b) Brick houses, clay vessels and household goods  c) Ships  d) Walls in China
**Answer: b) Brick houses, clay vessels and household goods** — Ruins are the evidence.
**4.** India's first dynasty was founded in 321 BC by —
a) Alexander  b) Chandragupta Maurya  c) Shih Huang-ti  d) Menes
**Answer: b) Chandragupta Maurya** — After he defeated the Greeks and unified the north.

**5.** The first Indian civilisation began in — about 2500 BC.
a) the Ganges plain  b) the Indus Valley  c) the Deccan
**Answer: b) the Indus Valley** — The Indus Valley.

**6.** Indus trade reached as far as —
a) Rome  b) Mesopotamia  c) China
**Answer: b) Mesopotamia** — Westward, to Mesopotamia.

**7.** Buddhism emerged —
a) from outside India  b) from within Hinduism, as a reform movement  c) from Zoroastrianism
**Answer: b) from within Hinduism, as a reform movement** — A reform from within.

**8.** The guide names Buddhism's founder as —
a) Confucius  b) Siddhartha Gautama  c) Ashoka
**Answer: b) Siddhartha Gautama** — C.563–483 BC (teacher guide — evidence).

**9.** Buddhism spread beyond India to —
a) South-East Asia, China and Japan  b) Europe and Africa  c) the Americas
**Answer: a) South-East Asia, China and Japan** — East and south-east.
