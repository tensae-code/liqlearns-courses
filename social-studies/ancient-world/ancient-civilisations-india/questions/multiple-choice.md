# Ancient civilisations — India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** India's civilisation began about —
a) 3500 BC  b) 2500 BC  c) 753 BC  d) 321 BC
**Answer: b) 2500 BC** — In the Indus and Ganges valleys.
**2.** The founders were the —
a) Aryans  b) Greeks  c) Dravidian peoples  d) Mauryas
**Answer: c) Dravidian peoples** — The Aryans came later, about 1500 BC.
**3.** Harappa and Mohenjo-daro prove the civilisation by their —
a) Gold coins  b) Brick houses, clay vessels and household goods  c) Ships  d) Walls in China
**Answer: b) Brick houses, clay vessels and household goods** — Ruins are the evidence.
**4.** India's first dynasty was founded in 321 BC by —
a) Alexander  b) Chandragupta Maurya  c) Shih Huang-ti  d) Menes
**Answer: b) Chandragupta Maurya** — After he defeated the Greeks and unified the north.
