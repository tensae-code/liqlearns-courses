# Ancient civilisations — India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Indus civilisation ended about 1500 BC, with climate disasters and Aryan invasion.
**Answer: True** — Both causes are the book's.
**2.** Alexander founded India's first dynasty.
**Answer: False** — Alexander took the north-west; Chandragupta founded the dynasty.
**3.** The first dynasty lasted from 321 BC to 185 AD.
**Answer: True** — Over five centuries of unified northern rule.
