# Video script — Ancient civilisations — India

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India began on two rivers — the Indus and the Ganges.
About 2500 BC, Dravidian people built cities of brick.
Harappa. Mohenjo-daro. Their ruins still speak.
Invaders came. Then Chandragupta drove out the Greeks
and made the north one land.

---

Grade 9 story addition (Ethiopia Wave 4):

The map lights the Indus. Cities rise on a grid — drains, canals, standard weights.

Temples follow. Then a prince walks away from a palace to seek an end to suffering.

Buddhism crosses mountains and seas — to China, to Japan. India's religions travel the world.
