# Papua New Guinea — the first people: origins and arrival — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Origins of the First People of Papua New Guinea
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — where did the first Papua New Guineans come from? Children trace and name their origins on a map and a timeline, using basic research skills

## Sub lesson 2 — Prehistoric migration
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — where did our ancestors originate, how did they cross the sea, what did the sea throw at them, and why did they settle here — in PNG the answers decide identity and customary land rights today

## Sub lesson 3 — Papua New Guinea's Early History
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — students write the settlement story themselves — how the early ancestors reached PNG and the techniques they used to feed and clothe themselves — and build timelines from prehistoric times to 1800
