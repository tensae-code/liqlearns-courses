# Video script — Papua New Guinea — the first people: origins and arrival

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Where did the first Papua New Guineans come from? Children trace and name their origins on a map and a timeline, using basic research skills.

Where did our ancestors originate, how did they cross the sea, what did the sea throw at them, and why did they settle here — in PNG the answers decide identity and customary land rights today.

Students write the settlement story themselves — how the early ancestors reached PNG and the techniques they used to feed and clothe themselves — and build timelines from prehistoric times to 1800.
