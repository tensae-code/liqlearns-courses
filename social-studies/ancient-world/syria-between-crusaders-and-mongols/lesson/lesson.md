# Syria between Crusaders and Mongols

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A divided Arab state is invaded from west and east — Crusaders, then Mongols — and Syria's ancient civilisation is put to the sword. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Syria between Crusaders and Mongols

A divided Arab state is invaded — Europeans come as Crusaders and others from the east — and a civilisation of thousands of years is put to the sword.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
