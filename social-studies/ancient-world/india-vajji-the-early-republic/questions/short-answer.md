# India — Vajji, the early republic — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: A closer look — Vajji; Ajatasattu and the Vajjis.
**Answer: In the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas rule together by assembly, discussion and debate; when Ajatasattu sends his minister to ask the Buddha how to conquer them, the Buddha answers they will prosper while they meet fully and often, act together, honour elders and protect their women and shrines — the sanghas outlast kingdoms until the Guptas finally absorb them (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
