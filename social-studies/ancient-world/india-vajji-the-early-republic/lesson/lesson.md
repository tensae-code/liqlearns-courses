# India — Vajji, the early republic

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas ruled together by assembly, discussion and debate — and the Buddha said they would prosper while they met fully and often and acted together. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — A closer look — Vajji; Ajatasattu and the Vajjis

In the Vajji gana, thousands of rajas rule together by assembly, discussion and debate; when Ajatasattu sends his minister to ask the Buddha how to conquer them, the Buddha answers they will prosper while they meet fully and often, act together, honour elders and protect their women and shrines — the sanghas outlast kingdoms until the Guptas finally absorb them.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
