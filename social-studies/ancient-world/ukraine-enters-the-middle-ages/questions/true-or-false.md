# Ukraine enters the Middle Ages — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Greek poleis stood on the Black Sea coast of ancient Ukraine.
**Answer: True — Olbia and Chersonesus are named as Greek poleis on the coast.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Slavs came before the Scythians on Ukrainian lands.
**Answer: False — The Slavs come after the ancient peoples; the story continues from their farming communities.**
