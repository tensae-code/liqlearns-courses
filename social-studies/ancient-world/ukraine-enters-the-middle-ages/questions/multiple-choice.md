# Ukraine enters the Middle Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The steppe peoples named for ancient Ukraine are —
a) Cimmerians, Scythians and Sarmatians  b) Romans, Vikings and Mongols  c) Greeks, Romans and Egyptians
**Answer: a) Cimmerians, Scythians and Sarmatians**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Olbia and Chersonesus were —
a) Greek poleis on the Black Sea coast  b) Slavic farming villages  c) Mongol camps in the steppe
**Answer: a) Greek poleis on the Black Sea coast**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The medieval story continues from —
a) The Slavs' farming communities  b) The Greek poleis  c) The Scythian kings
**Answer: a) The Slavs' farming communities**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson opens the course by —
a) Periodising the Middle Ages and looking back at ancient Ukraine  b) Starting with the Mongol invasion  c) Starting with the baptism of Rus
**Answer: a) Periodising the Middle Ages and looking back at ancient Ukraine**
