# Ukraine enters the Middle Ages

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the course opens by periodising the Middle Ages and looking back at ancient Ukraine — Cimmerians, Scythians and Sarmatians in the steppe, Greek poleis like Olbia and Chersonesus on the coast — before the Slavs, from whose farming communities the story continues. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ancient Ukraine before the Slavs

Before the Middle Ages begin, the book looks back at ancient Ukraine. Cimmerians, Scythians and Sarmatians ride the steppe, and Greek poleis like Olbia and Chersonesus stand on the Black Sea coast.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Slavs arrive

After those ancient peoples come the Slavs. From their farming communities the medieval story of Ukraine continues — the Slavs are the people the rest of the course follows.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Ukraine enters the Middle Ages in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
