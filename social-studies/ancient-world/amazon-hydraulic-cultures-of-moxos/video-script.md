# Video script — Amazon hydraulic cultures of Moxos

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the Llanos de Moxos a hydraulic culture of raised fields and lomas settles from the 4th c. BC to the 13th c. AD — Moxos, Baures, Casarabes, Takanas, Mosetenes and Tsimanes purify water with the tapore plant.

Teacher-compendium unit on the Moxos hydraulic cultures, same arc as the student text.
