# Amazon hydraulic cultures of Moxos

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In the Llanos de Moxos a hydraulic culture of raised fields and lomas settles from the 4th c. BC to the 13th c. AD — Moxos, Baures, Casarabes, Takanas, Mosetenes and Tsimanes purify water with the tapore plant. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
