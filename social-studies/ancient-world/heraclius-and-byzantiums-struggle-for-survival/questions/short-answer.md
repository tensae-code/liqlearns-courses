**Answer: Persians take Jerusalem and the True Cross; Heraclius wins it back, then Arab armies take Syria and Egypt in his old age (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Μεσαιωνική και Νεότερη Ιστορία Β΄ Γυμνασίου — Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Medieval and Modern History, Grade 8 Gymnasium)* (Greece), Ch. 1.II.2 Heraclius and his dynasty (610–717): internal reform and struggle for survival, printed p. 19.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The reign of Heraclius (610–641): decisive struggles and reforms.
**Answer: Heraclius beats Persia in a war of survival, reorganises the army into themes — and lives to see the Arabs take Syria and Egypt (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Μεσαιωνικού και του Νεότερου Κόσμου 565–1815 (History of the Medieval and Modern World), Β' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Dimitroukas, Thoukydidis P. Ioannou & Kostas Baroutas* (Greece), The reign of Heraclius (610–641): decisive struggles and reforms, printed p. 11.**
