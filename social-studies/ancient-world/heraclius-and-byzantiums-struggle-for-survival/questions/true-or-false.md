# Heraclius And Byzantiums Struggle For Survival — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Persians take Jerusalem and the True Cross; Heraclius wins it back, then Arab armies take Syria and Egypt in his old age.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 1.II.2 Heraclius and his dynasty (610–717): internal reform and struggle for survival

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Μεσαιωνική και Νεότερη Ιστορία Β΄ Γυμνασίου — Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Medieval and Modern History, Grade 8 Gymnasium)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 1.II.2 Heraclius and his dynasty (610–717): internal reform and struggle for survival

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Heraclius beats Persia in a war of survival, reorganises the army into themes — and lives to see the Arabs take Syria and Egypt.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The reign of Heraclius (610–641): decisive struggles and reforms

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Ιστορία του Μεσαιωνικού και του Νεότερου Κόσμου 565–1815 (History of the Medieval and Modern World), Β' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Dimitroukas, Thoukydidis P. Ioannou & Kostas Baroutas* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The reign of Heraclius (610–641): decisive struggles and reforms
