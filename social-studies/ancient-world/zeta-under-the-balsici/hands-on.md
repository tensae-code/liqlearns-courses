# Hands-on — Zeta under the Balšići

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Zeta under the Balšići — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch.4.1 Zeta in the age of the Balšići, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
