# Hands-on — The Christianisation of the South Slavs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Christianisation of the South Slavs — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch.2.3 The states of the South Slavs: Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
