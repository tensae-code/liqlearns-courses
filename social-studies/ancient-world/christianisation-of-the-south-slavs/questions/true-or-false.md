# The Christianisation of the South Slavs — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Slavs get the gospel in words they understand, and letters of their own to write it in.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2.3 The states of the South Slavs: Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ИСТОРИЈА за седми разред основне школе (Istorija 7)* is about Everyday life in interwar Yugoslavia.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.2.3 The states of the South Slavs: Christianity spreads among the Slavs from Frankish, Byzantine and Roman neighbours, above all in the later 9th century, with the Thessalonican brothers (Cyril and Methodius) bringing the faith in the Slavs' own language
