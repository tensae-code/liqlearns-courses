# Video script — Medieval world — the Ottomans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A chief named Osman founded an empire.
His name became its name: Ottoman.
Orhan built the Janissaries — an army that never goes home.
Gallipoli opened Europe. Murad took the Balkans.
The medieval world had a new power.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The Ottoman Empire in depth. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 depth follows the Ottoman arc whole: a small Anatolian frontier state growing by war and organisation until, in 1453, it takes Constantinople itself — and builds an empire on three continents. The names to hold: 1453, sultan-caliph.
