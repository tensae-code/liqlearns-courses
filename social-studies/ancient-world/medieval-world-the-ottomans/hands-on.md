# Hands-on — Medieval world — the Ottomans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three-reign relay
Three runners, three stations: Osman (found), Orhan (organise and cross), Murad (expand). Pass a token along a floor map from Anatolia through Gallipoli into the Balkans, each runner naming their deed.

## Standing army drill
As a group, hold a formation for two full minutes without breaking ranks — a small taste of what 'standing' discipline means beside armies that gather and scatter.
