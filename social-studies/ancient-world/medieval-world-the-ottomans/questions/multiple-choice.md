# Medieval world — the Ottomans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Ottoman Empire was founded by —
a) Murad I  b) Osman (1300–1326)  c) Justinian  d) Constantine
**Answer: b) Osman (1300–1326)** — His name became 'Ottoman'.
**2.** The Janissary army was formed by —
a) Orhan  b) Osman  c) Murad I  d) Shih Huang-ti
**Answer: a) Orhan** — A standing army, formed in his reign.
**3.** The first European land the Ottomans took was —
a) The Balkans whole  b) Gallipoli  c) Italy  d) Spain
**Answer: b) Gallipoli** — Under Orhan — the toe-hold in Europe.
**4.** Murad I (1359–1389) carried the Ottomans into —
a) China  b) The Balkans  c) Egypt  d) India
**Answer: b) The Balkans** — From Gallipoli into south-eastern Europe.
