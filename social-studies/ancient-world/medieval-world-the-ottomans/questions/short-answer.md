# Medieval world — the Ottomans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why could the early Ottomans outgrow their neighbours?
**Answer:** They excelled in military organisation and weapons, and Orhan gave them a standing Janissary army that served permanently.
**2.** How did the Ottomans enter Europe?
**Answer:** Orhan first took European land at Gallipoli; Murad I then carried the Ottomans into the Balkans.
