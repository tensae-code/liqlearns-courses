# Medieval world — the Ottomans — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Osman led the Turks of ____.
**Answer: Anatolia**
**2.** Orhan formed the standing ____ army.
**Answer: Janissary**
**3.** Murad I reigned 1359–____.
**Answer: 1389**
**4. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'the Ottoman ruler as both emperor and head of the faith' is ______.
**Answer: sultan-caliph**
