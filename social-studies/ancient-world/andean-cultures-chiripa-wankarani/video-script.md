# Video script — Early Andean cultures — Chiripa and Wankarani

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Chiripa, on Lake Titicaca's shore, is one of the Altiplano's first civilisations, with its sunken temple and early farming villages.

The first cultures of today's Bolivian territory, Chiripa among them, open the national deep past.
