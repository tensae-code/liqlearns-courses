# Aztec Maya And Inca Compared — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Three empires are compared side by side — Tenochtitlan, the Maya city-states and Cuzco — alike in stone building and many gods, different in language, rule and writing.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Civilizaciones de América: Aztecas, Mayas e Incas

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Estudios Sociales — Primaria Regular, 6to Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Civilizaciones de América: Aztecas, Mayas e Incas

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Maya calendars and glyphs, Aztec codices and Tenochtitlan itself, Inca Machu Picchu and Quechua survive as the three civilisations' living legacy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Legado de las civilizaciones Mayas, Aztecas e Incas

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Estudios Sociales — Primaria Regular, 6to Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Legado de las civilizaciones Mayas, Aztecas e Incas

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Only three peoples reach the highest, postclassic level — Maya, Aztec and Inca — and the unit studies them in turn as America's great civilisations.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Civilizaciones indígenas de América

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Educación Secundaria, 8 Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Civilizaciones indígenas de América
