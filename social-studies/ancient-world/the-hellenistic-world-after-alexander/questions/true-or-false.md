# The Hellenistic World After Alexander — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Alexander's generals carve his empire into Seleucid, Ptolemaic and other kingdoms, and Alexandria's Museum, Library and Lighthouse make it the world's classroom.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §21 The Hellenistic world

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն 7» (World History 7) — Armen Khachikyan, Gagik Hovakimyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §21 The Hellenistic world

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's model lesson tells Alexander's ten-year conquest, then turns it home — Armenia freed from Persia, Greek language in Armenian official writing.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Sample lesson: Lesson 22, the Hellenistic world

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն VII դասարան. Մեթոդական ուղեցույց» (World History, Grade 7: Methodological Guide) — Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Sample lesson: Lesson 22, the Hellenistic world

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Alexander's conquests fuse Greek language and art with Egypt and Persia, and Alexandria becomes the ancient world's brain.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 3 §3: Hellenism as the merging of Greek and Eastern civilisations

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 10 (World History 10), by Aram Boyajyan (Themes 1, 2, 4), Aram Nazaryan (Themes 1, 5, 6), Robert Ghazaryan (Theme 3)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
