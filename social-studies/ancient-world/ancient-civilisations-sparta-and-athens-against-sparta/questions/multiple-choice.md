# Ancient civilisations — Sparta, and Athens against Sparta — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Sparta's government was —
a) Direct democracy  b) An oligarchic landowners' state  c) A republic of traders  d) Ruled by lot
**Answer: b) An oligarchic landowners' state** — With no place for democracy.
**2.** Spartan boys owed military training from age —
a) Five  b) Seven  c) Twelve  d) Eighteen
**Answer: b) Seven** — Every boy, from age seven.
**3.** At Marathon (490 BC) Athens beat —
a) Sparta  b) Macedon  c) Persia  d) Rome
**Answer: c) Persia** — Yet Athens was later ruined in the civil war.
**4.** Greece's freedom ended in 338 BC when —
a) Rome invaded  b) Philip of Macedon conquered Greece  c) Persia returned  d) Crete rebelled
**Answer: b) Philip of Macedon conquered Greece** — The last date of the Greek story.
