# Ancient civilisations — Sparta, and Athens against Sparta — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name Sparta's three classes.
**Answer:** Spartan landowners, free perioikoi, and helot farm slaves.
**2.** List four contributions of Greece.
**Answer:** Any four of: sport (Olympics and marathon), Hippocratic medicine, building, theatre, literature, mathematics, philosophy.
