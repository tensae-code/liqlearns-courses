# Ancient civilisations — Sparta, and Athens against Sparta — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Sparta won the Athens–Sparta civil war of 431–404 BC.
**Answer: True** — Sparta uppermost at the war's end.
**2.** Helots were Sparta's landowning rulers.
**Answer: False** — Helots were farm slaves; Spartan landowners ruled.
**3.** The Olympics and the marathon count among Greece's contributions.
**Answer: True** — Sport heads the book's diagram, with medicine, theatre, mathematics and philosophy.
