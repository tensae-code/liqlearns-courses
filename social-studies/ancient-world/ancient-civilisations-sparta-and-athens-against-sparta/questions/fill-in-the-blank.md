# Ancient civilisations — Sparta, and Athens against Sparta — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Sparta lies in the southern ____.
**Answer: Peloponnese**
**2.** Free non-landowners in Sparta were called ____.
**Answer: perioikoi**
**3.** Greek medicine is named for ____.
**Answer: Hippocrates**
