# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — Sparta, and Athens against Sparta

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-city debate
Half the group argues Athens' way (every citizen decides), half argues Sparta's way (everyone trained, few decide). Each side must name one strength and one cost of its own city.

## Marathon measure
The marathon remembers Marathon. Measure a running course in your yard and run a short relay — sport itself is one of Greece's gifts, still alive.
