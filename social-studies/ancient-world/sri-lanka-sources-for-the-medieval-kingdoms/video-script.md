# Video script — Sri Lanka — Sources for the Medieval kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sri Lanka's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The witnesses change with the centuries — Culavamsa continuations, the Pujavaliya, Kotte's Saddharma Ratnakaraya and Rajavaliya, and outsiders' eyes: Marco Polo, Ibn Battuta and John de Marignolli all describe the island.
