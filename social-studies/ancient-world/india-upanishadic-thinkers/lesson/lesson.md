# India — Upanishadic thinkers

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Upanishadic teachers hunted what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman — while Gargi debated in royal courts and Panini wrote Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Upanishads; the wise beggar; Panini

Upanishadic teachers hunt what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul; a hungry beggar shames two sages into sharing food ("if the universal soul fills me too, you deny it food"); women like Gargi debate in royal courts, the slave's son Satyakama Jabala becomes a famed thinker, and Panini writes Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Upanishads; the wise beggar; Panini. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
