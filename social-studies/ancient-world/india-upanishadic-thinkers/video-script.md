# Video script — India — Upanishadic thinkers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Upanishadic teachers hunt what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul; a hungry beggar shames two sages into sharing food ("if the universal soul fills me too, you deny it food"); women like Gargi debate in royal courts, the slave's son Satyakama Jabala becomes a famed thinker, and Panini writes Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae.
