# India — Upanishadic thinkers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Upanishads; the wise beggar; Panini.
**Answer: Upanishadic teachers hunt what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul; a hungry beggar shames two sages into sharing food ("if the universal soul fills me too, you deny it food"); women like Gargi debate in royal courts, the slave's son Satyakama Jabala becomes a famed thinker, and Panini writes Sanskrit grammar in 3,000 algebra-like formulae (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.6 / Upanishads; the wise beggar; Panini, printed pp. 59–61.**
