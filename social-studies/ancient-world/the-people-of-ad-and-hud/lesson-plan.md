## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The people of 'Ad** — after the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and injustice, they refuse, and after drought a barren wind blows seven nights and eight days and leaves them like hollow palm trunks while Hud and the believers are saved
2. **The civilisation of 'Ad** — 'Ad, the first strong nation after the flood, rooted in al-Ahqaf in the south of Arabia, are blessed with mighty bodies, workers, herds, farms, the city of Iram and a feared army, their own prophet Hud warns them and the chiefs mock him, drought comes first and then the barren wind for seven nights and eight days, and Hud and the believers walk away to build again
