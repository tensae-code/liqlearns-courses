# The people of 'Ad

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: after the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and injustice, they refuse. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
