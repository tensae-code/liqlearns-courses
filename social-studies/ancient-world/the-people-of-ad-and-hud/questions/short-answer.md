# The people of 'Ad — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The people of 'Ad.
**Answer: after the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and injustice, they refuse, and after drought a barren wind blows seven nights and eight days and leaves them like hollow palm trunks while Hud and the believers are saved (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* (Yemen), The people of 'Ad, printed p. 17.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The civilisation of 'Ad.
**Answer: 'Ad, the first strong nation after the flood, rooted in al-Ahqaf in the south of Arabia, are blessed with mighty bodies, workers, herds, farms, the city of Iram and a feared army, their own prophet Hud warns them and the chiefs mock him, drought comes first and then the barren wind for seven nights and eight days, and Hud and the believers walk away to build again (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), The civilisation of 'Ad, printed p. 40.**
