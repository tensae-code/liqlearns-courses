# The people of 'Ad — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and injustice, they refuse, and after drought a barren wind blows seven nights and eight days and leaves them like hollow palm trunks while Hud and the believers are saved.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The people of 'Ad

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The people of 'Ad

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Ad, the first strong nation after the flood, rooted in al-Ahqaf in the south of Arabia, are blessed with mighty bodies, workers, herds, farms, the city of Iram and a feared army, their own prophet Hud warns them and the chiefs mock him, drought comes first and then the barren wind for seven nights and eight days, and Hud and the believers walk away to build again.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The civilisation of 'Ad

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The civilisation of 'Ad
