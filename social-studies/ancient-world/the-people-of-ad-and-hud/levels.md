## Sub lesson 1 — The people of 'Ad
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — after the flood the strong-bodied people of 'Ad grow rich in cities and herds in the south of Arabia, their prophet Hud warns them against pride and injustice, they refuse, and after drought a barren wind blows seven nights and eight days and leaves them like hollow palm trunks while Hud and the believers are saved
