# The Ohrid Archbishopric Church Of A Vanished Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** conquering Samuil's empire in 1018, Basil II keeps its church alive as an autocephalous archbishopric seated at Ohrid, first under the Slav Jovan of Debar; for centuries it crowns Balkan rulers — Demetrios Chomatenos crowns an Epirote despot emperor — and guards Slavic letters while empires come and go around it.
