# Video script — The Mongols — Genghis Khan and the fall of Baghdad

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Palestine's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Genghis Khan unites the nomad tribes of the Mongolian plateau into an empire reaching the Danube, and the invasions come for expansion and plunder of the Islamic lands.

The Golden Horde is the first Mongol people to embrace Islam, others follow, and the converts become defenders and builders of Islamic civilisation, raising monuments such as the Taj Mahal in India.
