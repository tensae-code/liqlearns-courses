## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Human rights in the Arab Islamic civilisation** — rights in Islam are sacred duties tied to man's vicegerency on earth: a woman argues her case before the Prophet and a Qur'an verse answers her, a commoner tells Umar they would straighten his crookedness with their swords and Umar thanks God for him, and the lesson catalogues six families of rights — political (opinion and expression), judicial equality, economic (protected money and ownership), social (free belief, equality from one dust, movement, migration and asylum, work and social guarantee), cultural (learning, free thought) and civil (life, honour and lineage, the inviolable home)
