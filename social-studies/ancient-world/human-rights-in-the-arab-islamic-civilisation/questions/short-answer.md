# Human rights in the Arab Islamic civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Human rights in the Arab Islamic civilisation.
**Answer: rights in Islam are sacred duties tied to man's vicegerency on earth: a woman argues her case before the Prophet and a Qur'an verse answers her, a commoner tells Umar they would straighten his crookedness with their swords and Umar thanks God for him, and the lesson catalogues six families of rights — political (opinion and expression), judicial equality, economic (protected money and ownership), social (free belief, equality from one dust, movement, migration and asylum, work and social guarantee), cultural (learning, free thought) and civil (life, honour and lineage, the inviolable home) (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), Human rights in the Arab Islamic civilisation, printed p. 93.**
