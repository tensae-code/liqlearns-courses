# Video script — Historical conditions before the Birth

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

as the book tells it, the Israelites after Sulayman split into two warring kingdoms and slide into insulting God, renaming their religion, killing prophets, distorting and hiding the Book, usury and corruption, and are punished in turn — Assyrians and Babylonians destroy their state, dispersion scatters them, Rome occupies the Levant and uses them as its tool — until, in that darkness of material tyranny, Isa is born as God's word and spirit.
