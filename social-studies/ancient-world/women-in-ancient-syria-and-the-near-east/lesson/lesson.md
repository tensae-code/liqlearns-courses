# Women in ancient Syria and the Near East

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: When farming and the first crafts begin in ancient Syria, women work beside men in field, home and workshop — and settled society is built by both. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Women in ancient Syria and the Near East

When farming and the first crafts begin, women work beside men in field, home and workshop and help found settled society.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Women in ancient Syria and the Near East. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
