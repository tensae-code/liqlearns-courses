# The Geto Dacians And Sarmizegetusa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea IV, Lecția 2: Geto-dacii. Sarmizegetusa.
**Answer: the northern Thracians become the Geto-Dacians — Greeks and Romans' name for one people — with tribes from the Carpathians to the Black Sea and their holy capital in the mountains (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală. Manual pentru clasa a V-a* (Moldova), Unitatea IV, Lecția 2: Geto-dacii. Sarmizegetusa, printed p. 93.**
