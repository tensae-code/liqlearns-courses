# Video script — The Canaanites and the first alphabet

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Palestine's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

About 4,000 years ago the Canaanites of Palestine invent alphabetic writing, borrowing signs from their own world — the ox-head for the first letter — and the alphabet travels from them to the whole world.
