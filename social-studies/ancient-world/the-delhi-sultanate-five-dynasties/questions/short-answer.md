# The Delhi Sultanate — five dynasties — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Delhi Sultanate — five dynasties.
**Answer: Mahmood of Ghazni raids India seventeen times (1000–1027), but Shahab-ud-Din Ghori plants rule — Tarain 1192 — and his slave-general Qutb-ud-Din Aibak founds the Sultanate in 1206; five dynasties follow — Mamluk (Iltutmish the real organiser, Razia the queen, Balban), Khilji (Ala-ud-Din's market reforms and Mongol-proof armies), Tughlaq (Firoz Shah's canals and employment bureau), Sayyid, and Lodhi, until Babar ends it at Panipat. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
