# Video script — Cambodia — Chaktomuk and Ponhea Yat

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet.

Ponhea Yat (1434–1463) plants the capital where the four rivers meet after Angkor's fall; his successors Norayreachea, Srei Reachea and Srei Sokonthor rule a kingdom rebuilding between Siam and Vietnam.

Ponhea Yat reigns ten years at Angkor (1421–1431), flees the Siamese sack to Srei Santhor, then founds Chaktomuk in 1434 with a grand royal name; Norayreachea (1463–1469) and Srei Reachea (1469–1485) follow.
