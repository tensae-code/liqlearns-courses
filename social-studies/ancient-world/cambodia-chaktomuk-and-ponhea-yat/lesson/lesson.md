# Cambodia — Chaktomuk and Ponhea Yat

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
