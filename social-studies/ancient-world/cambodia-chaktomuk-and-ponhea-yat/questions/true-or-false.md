# Cambodia — Chaktomuk and Ponhea Yat — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Siam sacks Angkor in 1431; Ponhea Yat (1421–1463) gathers the kingdom again, rules ten years at Srei Santhor, then moves the capital to Chaktomuk where the four rivers meet.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2 Lesson 1, The Chaktomuk era (15th century)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី១១ (History, Grade 11)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2 Lesson 1, The Chaktomuk era (15th century)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ponhea Yat (1434–1463) plants the capital where the four rivers meet after Angkor's fall; his successors Norayreachea, Srei Reachea and Srei Sokonthor rule a kingdom rebuilding between Siam and Vietnam.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 4 Lesson 2, The Chaktomuk era (15th–16th centuries)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៦ (Social Studies, Grade 6)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 4 Lesson 2, The Chaktomuk era (15th–16th centuries)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Ponhea Yat reigns ten years at Angkor (1421–1431), flees the Siamese sack to Srei Santhor, then founds Chaktomuk in 1434 with a grand royal name; Norayreachea (1463–1469) and Srei Reachea (1469–1485) follow.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Chaktomuk era in the 15th century

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៨ (Social Studies, Grade 8)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Chaktomuk era in the 15th century
