# Sinja civilisation and the Khas kingdom

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: pupils watch a documentary on Sinja, Jumla, capital of the Khas kingdom founded by Nagaraj from Tibet's Ngari; kings Nagraj, Krachalla, Ashok Challa (builder of Kankre Bihar, Surkhet), Jitari and Ripu Malla extend it until it breaks apart around 1450 BS into the Baise and Chaubise states — and from its Khas speech grows the Nepali language, whose first inscription stands at Dullu As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
