# Sri Lanka — Yapahuwa, Kurunegala and Gampola kingdoms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bhuvanekabahu I builds a rock fortress at Yapahuwa and trades with Egypt, Parakramabahu IV makes Kurunegala a seat of learning, and at Gampola the Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya temples rise.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Yapahuwa, Kurunegala and Gampola (1272–1415)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History Tamil 2011* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Yapahuwa, Kurunegala and Gampola (1272–1415)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After Magha, capitals hop south-west — Dambadeniya's Parakramabahu II makes a golden age of literature, Yapahuwa guards the Tooth Relic, and Gampola's Bhuvanaikabahu IV rules a shrinking but cultured kingdom.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The south-western kingdoms and their culture

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G10 History Tamil 2013* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The south-western kingdoms and their culture
