# Video script — Sri Lanka — Yapahuwa, Kurunegala and Gampola kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sri Lanka's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bhuvanekabahu I builds a rock fortress at Yapahuwa and trades with Egypt, Parakramabahu IV makes Kurunegala a seat of learning, and at Gampola the Lankatilaka and Gadaladeniya temples rise.
