# Rus-Ukraine fragmented — Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: after Mstyslav the unity is gone for good — Kyiv is fought over and even sacked by a Rus prince (Andrei Bogoliubsky, 1169), while Chernihiv and Pereiaslav go their own ways on the steppe frontier. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kyiv fought over

After Mstyslav the unity is gone for good. Kyiv is fought over — and in 1169 even sacked by a Rus prince, Andrei Bogoliubsky: Rus princes now make war on Rus's own capital.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chernihiv and Pereiaslav go their own ways

Chernihiv and Pereiaslav go their own ways on the steppe frontier. The one state is now separate principalities, each facing the steppe alone.
