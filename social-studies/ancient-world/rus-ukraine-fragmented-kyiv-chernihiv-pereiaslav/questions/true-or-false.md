# Rus-Ukraine fragmented — Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kyiv was sacked by a foreign invader in 1169.
**Answer: False — It was sacked by a Rus prince, Andrei Bogoliubsky.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The principalities each faced the steppe on their own.
**Answer: True — They go their own ways on the steppe frontier.**
