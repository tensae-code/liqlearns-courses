# Rus-Ukraine fragmented — Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kyiv was sacked in 1169 by —
a) Andrei Bogoliubsky, a Rus prince  b) Batu the Mongol  c) A Byzantine emperor
**Answer: a) Andrei Bogoliubsky, a Rus prince**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After Mstyslav, the unity of Rus was —
a) Gone for good  b) Stronger than ever  c) Restored by the church
**Answer: a) Gone for good**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The principalities named in this lesson are —
a) Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav  b) Halych, Volyn and Lviv only  c) Kyiv and Constantinople
**Answer: a) Kyiv, Chernihiv and Pereiaslav**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Chernihiv and Pereiaslav lay —
a) On the steppe frontier  b) On the Baltic coast  c) In the Carpathians
**Answer: a) On the steppe frontier**
