# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — Magha invasion and Polonnaruwa falls

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — Magha invasion and Polonnaruwa falls — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Kalinga Magha's invasion and the fall of Polonnaruwa, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
