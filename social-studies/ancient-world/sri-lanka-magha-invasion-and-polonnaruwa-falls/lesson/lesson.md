# Sri Lanka — Magha invasion and Polonnaruwa falls

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
