# Sri Lanka — Magha invasion and Polonnaruwa falls — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Magha lands in 1215 with 24,000 men, blinds King Parakrama Pandya, burns books and tanks, and the ruined kingdom can never be rebuilt.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Kalinga Magha's invasion and the fall of Polonnaruwa

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History Tamil 2011* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Kalinga Magha's invasion and the fall of Polonnaruwa

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kalinga Magha's 1215 invasion breaks Polonnaruwa for good, and the kingdom's centre of gravity slides south-west to Dambadeniya.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Magha and the move to Dambadeniya

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G12-13 History of Sri Lanka Part II Tamil 2014* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Magha and the move to Dambadeniya
