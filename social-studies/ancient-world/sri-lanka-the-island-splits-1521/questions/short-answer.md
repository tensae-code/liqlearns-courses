# Sri Lanka — The island splits, 1521 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The splitting of Kotte.
**Answer: The king's three sons murder their father in 1521 and divide Kotte, Sitawaka and Raigama between them, while Jaffna and Kandy stand apart and the Portuguese are already on the coast (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Sri Lanka's book, where is this part taught?
