# Sri Lanka — The island splits, 1521 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The king's three sons murder their father in 1521 and divide Kotte, Sitawaka and Raigama between them, while Jaffna and Kandy stand apart and the Portuguese are already on the coast.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The splitting of Kotte

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History Tamil 2011* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The splitting of Kotte
