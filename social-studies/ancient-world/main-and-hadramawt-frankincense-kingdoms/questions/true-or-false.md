# Ma'in and Hadramawt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows rich on dams, irrigation, incense and myrrh caravans, ruled by a king (mukarrib) advised by a council.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ma'in and Hadramawt

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ma'in and Hadramawt

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ma'in at Qarnaw in al-Jawf minds trade above all and plants stations up the road to north Arabia, Qataban at Timna organises the land caravans, Hadramawt at Shabwa holds the frankincense groves themselves and reaches Socotra and Zafar in Oman, and Awsan in Wadi Markha breaks from Qataban before being folded into Himyar.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Ma'in, Qataban, Hadramawt, Awsan)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Ma'in, Qataban, Hadramawt, Awsan)
