# Ma'in and Hadramawt

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ma'in rises in al-Jawf in 1400 BCE with its capital at Qarnaw and Hadramawt in 1020 BCE with its capital at Shabwa, and the frankincense land grows rich on dams, irrigation. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
