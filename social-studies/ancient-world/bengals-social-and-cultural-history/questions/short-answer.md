# Bengal's social and cultural history — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four signs the book uses to trace Bengal's culture.
**Answer: Harvest festivals, temples, food, dress, ritual and sculpture — any four (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** How is cultural history different from a history of kings?
**Answer: It studies how ordinary people lived — celebrated, worshipped, ate, dressed and built — not only who ruled (any close wording).**
