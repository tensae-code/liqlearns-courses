# Bengal's social and cultural history — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's telling, Bengal's culture stayed exactly the same for centuries.
**Answer: False** — it diversified century by century.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A temple can be evidence for a historian of culture.
**Answer: True** — buildings are among the sources cultural history reads.
