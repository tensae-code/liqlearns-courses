# Bengal's social and cultural history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Social and cultural history in the book follows —
a) Only kings and battles  b) Festivals, faith, food, dress, ritual and building  c) Only coins
**Answer: b) Festivals, faith, food, dress, ritual and building** — the trail of ordinary life.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Harvest festivals in Bengal came with —
a) Dance and music  b) Silence  c) Tax collectors only
**Answer: a) Dance and music** — the festivals' companions.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Temples rising over villages show —
a) Faith taking built form  b) Rivers changing course  c) Armies growing
**Answer: a) Faith taking built form** — what building testifies.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Changes in food, dress, ritual and sculpture trace —
a) Nothing  b) Bengal's culture diversifying century by century  c) The weather
**Answer: b) Bengal's culture diversifying century by century** — the book's thread.
