# Hands-on — Bengal's social and cultural history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Culture trail

Make a five-box trail — festival, temple, food, dress, sculpture — and write in each box how it could change over a century.

## Your town's footprints

Write three things in your own town today — a festival, a building, a food — that a future historian could read the same way, and what each would show.
