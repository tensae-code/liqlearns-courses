# Video script — Bengal's social and cultural history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bangladesh book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Grade 8 book reads Bengal's past in festivals, not battles.

Harvest festivals with dance and music; temples rising over villages; food, dress, ritual and sculpture changing century by century.

Each change is a footprint — of new peoples, faiths and ideas entering Bengal. That trail is social and cultural history.
