# Ukrainian lands in the Lithuanian state

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: after the Romanovych line ends (1340), Lithuania gathers most Ukrainian lands — Algirdas takes Kyiv after Blue Waters (1362) — and for a century the lands keep their laws, faith and Rus language inside a state whose princes rule "without changing the old ways". As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lithuania gathers the lands

After the Romanovych line ends in 1340, Lithuania gathers most Ukrainian lands. Algirdas takes Kyiv after the battle of Blue Waters in 1362.

## Sub lesson 2 — Without changing the old ways

For a century the lands keep their laws, faith and Rus language. Lithuania's princes rule "without changing the old ways" — new rulers, old life.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Ukrainian lands in the Lithuanian state in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
