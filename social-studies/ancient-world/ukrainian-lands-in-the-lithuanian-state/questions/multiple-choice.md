# Ukrainian lands in the Lithuanian state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Algirdas took Kyiv after —
a) Blue Waters, 1362  b) The Kalka, 1223  c) His coronation in 1253
**Answer: a) Blue Waters, 1362**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Lithuania gathered most Ukrainian lands after —
a) The Romanovych line ended in 1340  b) The baptism of 988  c) The sack of 1169
**Answer: a) The Romanovych line ended in 1340**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Inside Lithuania, the lands kept —
a) Their laws, faith and Rus language  b) Only their laws  c) Nothing of their old life
**Answer: a) Their laws, faith and Rus language**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Lithuania's princes ruled, in the book's phrase —
a) "Without changing the old ways"  b) By replacing every law  c) From Kyiv as Mongols
**Answer: a) "Without changing the old ways"**
