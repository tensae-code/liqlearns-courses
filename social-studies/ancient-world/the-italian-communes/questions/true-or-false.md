# The Italian Communes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italian city-dwellers swear collective oaths and govern themselves through consuls and podestà, raising tower-houses and writing their own statutes while bishops and emperors look on.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§1–2 The medieval cities; The development of the Communes

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§1–2 The medieval cities; The development of the Communes

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the communes swear their oath at Pontida (1167) and beat Barbarossa's empire at Legnano; canon law grows in the same cities as their other constitution.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in L'Europa dei comuni; Lo scontro tra l'imperatore e i comuni italiani; Il giuramento di Pontida

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about L'Europa dei comuni; Lo scontro tra l'imperatore e i comuni italiani; Il giuramento di Pontida
