# Hands-on — Micronesia — first settlement: Austronesian voyagers

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sing the route
Star paths were sung, not written. Make up a short chant that names three things in order — for example the star you start under, the wind you expect, and the island you aim for — and teach it to a companion until they can sing it back without notes.

## Pack the canoe
Draw an outrigger canoe. Inside it, draw or list everything a family would need to start life on an empty island. Beside each item, write what it is for. Compare your list with the book's tool kit: adzes, coir rope, pigs, chickens and seed crops.
