# Micronesia — first settlement: Austronesian voyagers — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the books teach them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Centuries of migration and the two paths of settlement
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that Austronesian families left southeast China for Taiwan about 5,000 BC and reached Micronesia along two paths, while older peoples had crossed the Sunda and Sahul land bridges.

## Sub lesson 2 — Traveling beyond the horizon: the outrigger, star songs and the tool kit
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that navigators sang star paths instead of writing them, tacked against the wind in outrigger canoes, and landed with adzes, coir rope, pigs, chickens and seed crops.

## Sub lesson 3 — The settlement science in the atolls' own languages
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that the Kapingamarangi, Mwoakilloa and Nukuoro readers retell the same two-wave settlement science — land-bridge peoples first, then Austronesian Lapita voyagers — in each atoll's own language.
