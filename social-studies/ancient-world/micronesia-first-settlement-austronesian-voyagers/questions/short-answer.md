# Micronesia — first settlement: Austronesian voyagers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** How did navigators find islands without written maps, and what did they bring to live on a new island?
**Answer: They sang star paths held in memory and sailed outrigger canoes that could tack against the wind; they landed with adzes, coir rope, pigs, chickens and seed crops to build a subsistence life (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What two waves of settlement do the atoll readers describe?
**Answer: First peoples crossing the Sunda and Sahul land bridges long ago, then Austronesian (Lapita) voyagers from Taiwan spreading through the Pacific to the outlier atolls (any close wording).**
