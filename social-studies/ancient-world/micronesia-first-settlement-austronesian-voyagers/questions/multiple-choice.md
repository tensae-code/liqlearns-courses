# Micronesia — first settlement: Austronesian voyagers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Austronesian families in this story first left southeast China for —
a) Taiwan  b) Japan  c) Australia
**Answer: a) Taiwan** — the first step the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The older peoples who came before them crossed —
a) The Pacific by canoe  b) The Sunda and Sahul land bridges  c) The Bering Strait
**Answer: b) The Sunda and Sahul land bridges** — the land-bridge crossing the book describes.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Micronesian navigators held their routes in —
a) Written charts  b) Star songs, sung from memory  c) Printed maps
**Answer: b) Star songs, sung from memory** — the navigation the book teaches.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** A landing party carried —
a) Only fishing nets  b) Adzes, coir rope, pigs, chickens and seed crops  c) Nothing — they lived off each island alone
**Answer: b) Adzes, coir rope, pigs, chickens and seed crops** — the tool kit the book lists.
