# Micronesia — first settlement: Austronesian voyagers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Austronesian settlement of Micronesia followed two paths into the islands.
**Answer: True** — the book teaches two paths of settlement.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The atoll readers teach a different settlement story from the Grade 9 book.
**Answer: False** — the Kapingamarangi, Mwoakilloa and Nukuoro readers retell the same two-wave science, each in its own language.
