# Lesson plan — Cycladic Civilisation Of The Aegean Islands

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Cycladic Civilisation Of The Aegean Islands
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** five thousand years ago islanders of the Cyclades sail, fish and trade obsidian across the Aegean from small coastal settlements
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 8, ch. 1 The life of the Cycladians** — five thousand years ago islanders of the Cyclades sail, fish and trade obsidian across the Aegean from small coastal settlements
2. **Unit 8, ch. 2 The art of the Cycladians** — Cycladic craftsmen carve the famous marble figurines and paint frescoes like the Thera fishermen and boxing boys
3. **Unit 8, ch. 1 Life of the Cycladians exercises** — exercises open the Bronze Age part of the book with the islanders' sea life
4. **Unit 8, ch. 2 Art of the Cycladians exercises** — close picture-reading of Cycladic figurines and frescoes as evidence of island life
5. **Ch. B2 The Cycladic Civilisation** — Bronze Age islanders carve marble figures and sail obsidian across the Aegean centuries before the palaces

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
