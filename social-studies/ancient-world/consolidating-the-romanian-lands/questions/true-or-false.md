# Consolidating The Romanian Lands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Alexandru cel Bun and his fellow rulers strengthen Moldavia and Wallachia with laws, trade, churches and embroidery workshops at Bistrița monastery.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 11: Consolidarea țărilor române

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 11: Consolidarea țărilor române

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Alexander the Good defends Moldavia's independence, guards its trade roads, founds bishoprics and brings St John the New's relics to Suceava, while Poland and Hungary sign the 1412 Treaty of Lubla over his land.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unitatea II, Lecția 8: Viața politică a Țării Moldovei. Alexandru cel Bun
