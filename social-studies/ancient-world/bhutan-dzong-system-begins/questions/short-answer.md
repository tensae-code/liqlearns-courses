# Bhutan — the dzong system begins — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter Three, Emergence of Dzong System in Bhutan.
**Answer: the first fortified monastic dzong rises at Chelkha in Paro in 1194 under Nyo Gyalwa Lhanangpa of the Lhapa Kagyu, but the experiment fades when Phajo Drukgom Zhigpo brings the Drukpa Kagyu in 1222, and dzongs wait four centuries for revival (any close wording).**
