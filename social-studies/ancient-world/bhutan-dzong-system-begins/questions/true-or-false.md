# Bhutan — the dzong system begins — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the first fortified monastic dzong rises at Chelkha in Paro in 1194 under Nyo Gyalwa Lhanangpa of the Lhapa Kagyu, but the experiment fades when Phajo Drukgom Zhigpo brings the Drukpa Kagyu in 1222, and dzongs wait four centuries for revival
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Three, Emergence of Dzong System in Bhutan).
