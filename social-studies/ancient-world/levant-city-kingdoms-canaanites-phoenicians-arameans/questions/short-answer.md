**Answer: the Levant, bridge of three continents and caravan road between Yemen, Mesopotamia and Egypt, fills with city kingdoms built largely by migrants from south Arabia — Canaanites founding Jaffa, Gaza, Jericho and Jerusalem, Amorites, Phoenicians who master the sea trade and give the world a 22-letter alphabet from which Greek, Roman and European letters descend, and Arameans at Damascus — until great-power greed from Assyria, Babylon and Persia, and, in the book's main verdict, their turning from the prophets, bring them down (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
