## Sub lesson 2 — Part 1, Unit 1 "Which Theories Explain Sāmoa's Origins?", Theory One
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Lapita pottery, carbon-dated at Mulifanua to about 2,990 years old, traces Austronesian ancestors from Taiwan through the Philippines, Indonesia and New Guinea to the Bismarcks by 1500 BC, Tonga by 1300 BC and Sāmoa by about 1000 BC — backed by DNA, the Austronesian language family and shared practices (siapo, tatau, ava)
