**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 13, the founding of the Trần (1226) and its government.
**Answer: the fishing clan Trần gathers power behind a failing Lý throne until, in January 1226, the last Lý ruler Lý Chiêu Hoàng is made to abdicate to her husband Trần Cảnh; the dynasty rules through retired emperors (Thượng hoàng), clan endogamy, the Quốc triều hình luật, and new offices from the National History Institute to river-dyke commissioners (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: reclamation, flood dykes and tax remissions revive farming; princes build great estates (điền trang), craft villages and guilds multiply, and Vân Đồn and Hội Thống ports crowd with foreign ships, while more peasants sink into tenancy and estate slavery (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
