# Vietnam Tran Dynasty Builds The Country — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 10, the Trần dynasty builds the country
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — founded in 1226, the Trần keep retired emperors (Thượng hoàng) guiding young kings — Nhân Tông rides back to court, finds his son drunk and asleep, and teaches him the throne is not his to neglect; exams and schools produce the dynasty's talents

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 13, the founding of the Trần (1226) and its government
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the fishing clan Trần gathers power behind a failing Lý throne until, in January 1226, the last Lý ruler Lý Chiêu Hoàng is made to abdicate to her husband Trần Cảnh; the dynasty rules through retired emperors (Thượng hoàng), clan endogamy, the Quốc triều hình luật, and new offices from the National History Institute to river-dyke commissioners

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 13, Trần economy and society
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — reclamation, flood dykes and tax remissions revive farming; princes build great estates (điền trang), craft villages and guilds multiply, and Vân Đồn and Hội Thống ports crowd with foreign ships, while more peasants sink into tenancy and estate slavery

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 13, Trần culture, education and science
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — all three teachings are respected, but Confucian scholars rise and Trần Nhân Tông founds the Trúc Lâm Zen school; examinations run every seven years from 1232 with the Tam khôi titles; Lê Văn Hưu writes the first national history, Trần Quốc Tuấn his military treatises, and Tuệ Tĩnh his herbal medicine
