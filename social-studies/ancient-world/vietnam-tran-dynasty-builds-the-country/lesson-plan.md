## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 10, the Trần dynasty builds the country** — founded in 1226, the Trần keep retired emperors (Thượng hoàng) guiding young kings — Nhân Tông rides back to court, finds his son drunk and asleep, and teaches him the throne is not his to neglect; exams and schools produce the dynasty's talents
2. **Bài 13, the founding of the Trần (1226) and its government** — the fishing clan Trần gathers power behind a failing Lý throne until, in January 1226, the last Lý ruler Lý Chiêu Hoàng is made to abdicate to her husband Trần Cảnh; the dynasty rules through retired emperors (Thượng hoàng), clan endogamy, the Quốc triều hình luật, and new offices from the National History Institute to river-dyke commissioners
3. **Bài 13, Trần economy and society** — reclamation, flood dykes and tax remissions revive farming; princes build great estates (điền trang), craft villages and guilds multiply, and Vân Đồn and Hội Thống ports crowd with foreign ships, while more peasants sink into tenancy and estate slavery
4. **Bài 13, Trần culture, education and science** — all three teachings are respected, but Confucian scholars rise and Trần Nhân Tông founds the Trúc Lâm Zen school; examinations run every seven years from 1232 with the Tam khôi titles; Lê Văn Hưu writes the first national history, Trần Quốc Tuấn his military treatises, and Tuệ Tĩnh his herbal medicine
