# Hands-on — The Gulf's first civilisations — Dilmun, Magan and Failaka

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw a simple map of the Gulf and mark Dilmun, Tarut, Hili and Magan where the book places them.

## Task 2
List the four goods Dilmun shipped and write one sentence on why each was wanted.
