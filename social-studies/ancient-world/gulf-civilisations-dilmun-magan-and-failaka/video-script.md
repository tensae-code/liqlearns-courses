# Video script — The Gulf's first civilisations — Dilmun, Magan and Failaka

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Kuwait's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The gulf sits between the old world's continents, so dilmun in bahrain ships copper, wood, dates and pearls, tarut farms palms, hili buries its dead in round stone tombs and magan smelts copper into swords.
