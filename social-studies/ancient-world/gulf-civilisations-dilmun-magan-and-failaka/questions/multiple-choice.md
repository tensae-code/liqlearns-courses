# The Gulf's first civilisations — Dilmun, Magan and Failaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dilmun, in Bahrain, shipped —
a) copper, wood, dates and pearls  b) only silk and glass  c) only horses and wine
**Answer: a) copper, wood, dates and pearls** — Dilmun ships copper, wood, dates and pearls

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Hili is remembered for —
a) round stone tombs  b) glass pyramids  c) iron bridges
**Answer: a) round stone tombs** — Hili buries its dead in round stone tombs

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Magan's craft was —
a) smelting copper into swords  b) weaving silk  c) building steamships
**Answer: a) smelting copper into swords** — Magan smelts copper into swords
