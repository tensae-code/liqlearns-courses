# The Gulf's first civilisations — Dilmun, Magan and Failaka — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gulf's location helped its first civilisations grow rich on trade.
**Answer: True** — the Gulf sits between the old world's continents

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dilmun's ships carried nothing but water.
**Answer: False** — Dilmun ships copper, wood, dates and pearls
