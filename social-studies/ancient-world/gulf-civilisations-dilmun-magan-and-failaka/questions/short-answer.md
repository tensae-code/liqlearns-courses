# The Gulf's first civilisations — Dilmun, Magan and Failaka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did civilisations rise around the Gulf?
**Answer: Because the Gulf sits between the old world's continents, so trade passed through it and its peoples grew rich on copper, dates, pearls and craft (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Tarut do, and what did Magan make?
**Answer: Tarut farmed palms; Magan smelted copper into swords.**
