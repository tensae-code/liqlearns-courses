# The Gulf's first civilisations — Dilmun, Magan and Failaka

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Gulf's position between the old world's lands made it a trading crossroads where Dilmun, Tarut, Hili and Magan rose on copper, dates, pearls and craft. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dilmun, Tarut, Hili and Magan — the Gulf between worlds

The gulf sits between the old world's continents, so dilmun in bahrain ships copper, wood, dates and pearls, tarut farms palms, hili buries its dead in round stone tombs and magan smelts copper into swords.
