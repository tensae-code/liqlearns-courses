# Women in the Arab-Islamic state

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Women of the Arab-Islamic state create in the home, the sciences, letters, crafts, markets, politics and administration — partners in building life. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Women in the Arab-Islamic state

Women of the Arab state create in the home, the sciences, letters, crafts, markets, politics and administration.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Women in the Arab-Islamic state. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
