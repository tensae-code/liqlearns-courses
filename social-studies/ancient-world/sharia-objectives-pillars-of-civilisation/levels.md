## Sub lesson 1 — Pillars of civilisation in the objectives of the Islamic Sharia
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Sharia's universal objectives fix civilisation's pillars at four levels: for the individual, guarding life, mind, religion, honour and property; for the family, lawful marriage, progeny, lineage, and tranquillity, affection and mercy between spouses; for the umma, security, justice and equality, unity and the spread of knowledge; and for humanity, mutual acquaintance, cooperation, world peace and man's vicegerency on earth
