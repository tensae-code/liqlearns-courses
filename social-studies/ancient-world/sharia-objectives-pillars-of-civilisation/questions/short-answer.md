# Pillars of civilisation in the objectives of the Islamic Sharia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Pillars of civilisation in the objectives of the Islamic Sharia.
**Answer: the Sharia's universal objectives fix civilisation's pillars at four levels: for the individual, guarding life, mind, religion, honour and property; for the family, lawful marriage, progeny, lineage, and tranquillity, affection and mercy between spouses; for the umma, security, justice and equality, unity and the spread of knowledge; and for humanity, mutual acquaintance, cooperation, world peace and man's vicegerency on earth (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), Pillars of civilisation in the objectives of the Islamic Sharia, printed p. 12.**
