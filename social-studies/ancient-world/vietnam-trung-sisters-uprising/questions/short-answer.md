# Vietnam Trung Sisters Uprising — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 8, story of Trưng Vương and the Trưng sisters.
**Answer: governor Tô Định kills Thi Sách, so his widow Trưng Trắc and her sister Trưng Nhị raise the banner "to repay the country's debt and avenge the house", sweep through Mê Linh, Cổ Loa and Luy Lâu, and Trưng Trắc reigns as queen, 40–43 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 8, story of Trưng Vương and the Trưng sisters, printed p. 37.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 16, the Trưng sisters' uprising in detail.
**Answer: Trưng Trắc swears her oath at Mê Linh ("avenge the country, restore the Hùng line"); in spring AD 40 Hát Môn rises, 65 citadels join, Luy Lâu falls and Tô Định flees; she reigns as queen until Mã Viện crushes the rising in 43 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
