# First settlements and the birth of civilisation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: beside the great rivers people settle for good, raise the first towns, and divide work between farmer, herder, craftworker and ruler. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 3.1 Where and how did people first settle?

beside the great rivers people settle for good, raise the first towns, and divide work between farmer, herder, craftworker and ruler

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 3.2 How did civilisation develop?

writing, the wheel, irrigation and laws appear; a settled people's inventions become the pattern every later civilisation inherits

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Ch. 3.1 Where and how did people first settle? — beside the great rivers people settle for good, raise the first towns, and divide work between farmer, herder, craftworker and ruler; Ch. 3.2 How did civilisation develop? — writing, the wheel, irrigation and laws appear; a settled people's inventions become the pattern every later civilisation inherits. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
