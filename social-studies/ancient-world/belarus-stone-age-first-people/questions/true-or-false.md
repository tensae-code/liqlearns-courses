# Belarus — Stone Age: the first people — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The first people — Stone Age, Ice Age period

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Atlas: History of Belarus, Grade 6* is about The Bronze Age on the Belarusian lands.
**Answer: False** — it is about The first people — Stone Age, Ice Age period

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** As the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Settlement after the ice — the post-glacial period

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Atlas: History of Belarus, Grade 6* is about The Balts — the pre-Slavic population.
**Answer: False** — it is about Settlement after the ice — the post-glacial period
