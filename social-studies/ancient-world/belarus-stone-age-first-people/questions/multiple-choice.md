# Belarus — Stone Age: the first people — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The first people — Stone Age, Ice Age period  b) The Bronze Age on the Belarusian lands  c) The first Slavs on the territory of Belarus
**Answer: a) The first people — Stone Age, Ice Age period** — this part tells the first people — Stone Age, Ice Age period

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi  b) copper and tin travel from the south, bronze axes appear, and farming and herding take root first in the south, then the north  c) Slavs move up from the Pripyat in the mid-1st millennium, bringing slash-and-burn farming, wooden building and their own gods and shrines
**Answer: a)** — Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Settlement after the ice — the post-glacial period  b) The Balts — the pre-Slavic population  c) Tribal unions — Krivichi, Dregovichi and Radimichi
**Answer: a) Settlement after the ice — the post-glacial period** — this part tells settlement after the ice — the post-glacial period

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) as the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land  b) Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day  c) Krivichi, Dregovichi and Radimichi form tribal unions, elders and princes grow rich, and hillforts rise over the rivers
**Answer: a)** — as the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land
