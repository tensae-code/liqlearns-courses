# Syria — first crafts: pottery and tools

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In ancient Syria stone, bone, wood and clay are shaped into the first tools and pots — raw nature becomes craft. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Syria — first crafts: pottery and tools

Stone, bone, wood and clay are shaped into the first tools and pots, and raw nature becomes craft.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
