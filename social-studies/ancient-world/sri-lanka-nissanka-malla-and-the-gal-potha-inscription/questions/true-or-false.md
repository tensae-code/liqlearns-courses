# Sri Lanka — Nissanka Malla and the Gal Potha inscription — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A Kalinga prince claims the throne by birth, cuts taxes, tours the island, and leaves his deeds carved on the great Gal Potha stone book.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Nissanka Malla (1187–1196)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History Tamil 2011* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Nissanka Malla (1187–1196)
