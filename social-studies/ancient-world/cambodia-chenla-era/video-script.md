# Video script — Cambodia — the Chenla era

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla.

Funan's inland successor splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla; Bhavavarman I (550–600) heads the king list of a state the Chinese call Zhenla.

Land Chenla of the upper Mekong (in today's Laos) and Water Chenla facing the sea divide Funan's inheritance between them (map, p. 142).
