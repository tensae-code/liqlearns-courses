# Cambodia — the Chenla era — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the kingdom that follows Funan spreads over the middle Mekong (map, p. 108) under Bhavavarman I (550–600), Mahendravarman (600–615), Isanavarman I (615–635, builder of Sambor Prei Kuk) and Jayavarman I (655–681) — then splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 4 Lesson 4, The Chenla era

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៥ (Social Studies, Grade 5)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 4 Lesson 4, The Chenla era

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Funan's inland successor splits into Land Chenla and Water Chenla; Bhavavarman I (550–600) heads the king list of a state the Chinese call Zhenla.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Chenla kingdom (550–802 CE)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៧ (Social Studies, Grade 7)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Chenla kingdom (550–802 CE)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Land Chenla of the upper Mekong (in today's Laos) and Water Chenla facing the sea divide Funan's inheritance between them (map, p. 142).
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Chenla kingdom

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី១០ (Social Studies, Grade 10)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Chenla kingdom
