# Europes Revival After The Year 1000 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after the year 1000 a warmer climate, new ploughs and cleared land feed a growing population, and the fear that the world would end in the Mille quietly gives way to expansion.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–3 The climate improves and the population grows; The progress of agriculture; A society in movement: the countryside

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§1–3 The climate improves and the population grows; The progress of agriculture; A society in movement: the countryside
