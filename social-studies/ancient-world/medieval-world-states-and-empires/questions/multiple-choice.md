# Medieval world — states and empires — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Byzantium is —
a) Rome's empire surviving in the East  b) A steppe khanate  c) A feudal manor
**Answer: a) Rome's empire surviving in the East** — the emperor in Constantinople.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sassanids ruled —
a) Feudal Europe  b) Persia  c) The steppe
**Answer: b) Persia** — Byzantium's great rival.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A feudal lord's army is made of —
a) Paid foreign troops only  b) A whole people in the saddle  c) The men his land owes
**Answer: c) The men his land owes** — service owed for land.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The book reads medieval politics through —
a) Its soldiers — how each state raises its army  b) Its coins only  c) Its poets
**Answer: a) Its soldiers — how each state raises its army** — army-raising shows the rule.
