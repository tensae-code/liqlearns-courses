# Medieval world — states and empires — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Feudal Europe had a strong single emperor at its centre.
**Answer: False** — its kings were weak at the centre; lords ruled their own lands and men.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** A steppe khan's strength rode with him — a whole people in the saddle.
**Answer: True** — the steppe power's army as the book describes it.
