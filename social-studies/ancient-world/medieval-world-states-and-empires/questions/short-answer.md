# Medieval world — states and empires — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four medieval powers the book surveys.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does army-raising show how a state is ruled?
**Answer: Each kind of state raises its army its own way — paid troops, men owed by land, a people in the saddle — so the army mirrors the rule (any close wording).**
