# Hands-on — Medieval world — states and empires

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Four kinds of rule

Draw four small scenes: an emperor in Constantinople, a Persian shah, a feudal lord at his castle gate, a khan among horsemen. Under each, write one line: who holds power here, and over whom.

## Raise the army

For each of the three — emperor, lord, khan — write how the army is raised: paid troops, men owed by the land, a people in the saddle. Say which army could gather fastest, and why.

## A world with no centre

Sketch a rough map from Constantinople to the steppe. Mark each power's land in a different colour. Write one sentence on what a traveller would notice: the laws and rulers keep changing.
