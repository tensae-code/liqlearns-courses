# Video script — Medieval world — states and empires

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The medieval world had no single centre. Turkey's Grade 9 book surveys its rulers: Byzantium, keeping Rome's empire alive in the East; the Sassanid shahs of Persia; feudal Europe, where lords ruled their own lands under weak kings; and the steppe powers, whose khans led peoples of horsemen.

Then the book reads the age through its soldiers. The emperor pays troops; the lord summons the men his land owes; the khan rides with a whole people in the saddle.

Tell me how a state raises its army, the book says, and you have told me how it is ruled.
