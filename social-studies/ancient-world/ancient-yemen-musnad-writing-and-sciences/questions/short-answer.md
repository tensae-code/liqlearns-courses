# Intellectual and scientific manifestations of ancient Yemen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Intellectual and scientific manifestations of ancient Yemen.
**Answer: the old Yemenis write in the Musnad and Zabur scripts on stone, bronze and wood, keep a library in the Awwam temple at Marib, practise medicine from teeth and eyes to fractures and embalming, read the stars for farming seasons and sailing, count for trade and state, and refine copper and bronze into coins, jewellery, weapons and statues of kings (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Intellectual and scientific manifestations of ancient Yemen, printed p. 76.**
