# Prophet of God Yusuf

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Yusuf, eleventh of Yaqub's twelve sons in Canaan, dreams of eleven stars and the sun and moon bowing to him, his jealous brothers throw him into a well. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Prophet of God Yusuf

Yusuf, eleventh of Yaqub's twelve sons in Canaan, dreams of eleven stars and the sun and moon bowing to him, his jealous brothers throw him into a well, a trade caravan fishes him out and sells him in Egypt to the Aziz, whose wife he refuses at the price of prison — and in prison the servant of God becomes a teacher, reading the dreams of the cupbearer and the baker

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Prophet of God Yusuf — yusuf, eleventh of Yaqub's twelve sons in Canaan, dreams of eleven stars and the sun and moon bowing to him, his jealous brothers throw him into a well, a trade caravan fishes him out and sells him in Egypt to the Aziz, whose wife he refuses at the price of prison — and in prison the servant of God becomes a teacher, reading the dreams of the cupbearer and the baker. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
