# Prophet of God Yusuf — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yusuf, eleventh of Yaqub's twelve sons in Canaan, dreams of eleven stars and the sun and moon bowing to him, his jealous brothers throw him into a well, a trade caravan fishes him out and sells him in Egypt to the Aziz, whose wife he refuses at the price of prison — and in prison the servant of God becomes a teacher, reading the dreams of the cupbearer and the baker.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Prophet of God Yusuf

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Prophet of God Yusuf
