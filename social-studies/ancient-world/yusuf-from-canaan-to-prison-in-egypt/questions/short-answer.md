# Prophet of God Yusuf — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Prophet of God Yusuf.
**Answer: yusuf, eleventh of Yaqub's twelve sons in Canaan, dreams of eleven stars and the sun and moon bowing to him, his jealous brothers throw him into a well, a trade caravan fishes him out and sells him in Egypt to the Aziz, whose wife he refuses at the price of prison — and in prison the servant of God becomes a teacher, reading the dreams of the cupbearer and the baker (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Prophet of God Yusuf, printed p. 28.**
