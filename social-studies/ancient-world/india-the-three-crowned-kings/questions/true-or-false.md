# India — the three crowned kings — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — hold an inland and a coastal capital each (Puhar, Madurai), take gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and reward praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in New kingdoms along the coasts

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about samudragupta's four policies; genealogies; Chandragupta II.
**Answer: False** — it is about new kingdoms along the coasts
