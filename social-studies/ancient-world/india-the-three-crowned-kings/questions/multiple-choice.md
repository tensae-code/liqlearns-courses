# India — the three crowned kings — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) New kingdoms along the coasts  b) Samudragupta's four policies; genealogies; Chandragupta II  c) The world of books: Silappadikaram and Manimekalai
**Answer: a) New kingdoms along the coasts** — this part tells new kingdoms along the coasts

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) The muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — hold an inland and a coastal capital each (Puhar, Madurai), take gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and reward praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants  b) One conqueror, four treatments — Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours like Assam, Nepal and the gana sanghas bound by tribute and court attendance, and Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters  c) Jataka and Panchatantra tales are written down and carved on stupa railings — a monkey king of 80,000 followers makes his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escapes a human king, who, watching him die, mourns and honours him
**Answer: a)** — the muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — hold an inland and a coastal capital each (Puhar, Madurai), take gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and reward praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants
