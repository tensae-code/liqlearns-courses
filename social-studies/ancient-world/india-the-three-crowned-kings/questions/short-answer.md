# India — the three crowned kings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: New kingdoms along the coasts.
**Answer: The muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — hold an inland and a coastal capital each (Puhar, Madurai), take gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and reward praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants; 200 years later the Satavahanas rise in the west, Gautamiputra Shri Satakarni styling himself lord of the dakshinapatha and marching to three coasts (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.9 / New kingdoms along the coasts, printed pp. 92–93.**
