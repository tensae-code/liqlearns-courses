# India — the three crowned kings

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The three crowned kings — Chola, Chera and Pandya — held inland and coastal capitals and took gifts and tribute; 200 years later the Satavahanas rose in the west. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — New kingdoms along the coasts

The muvendar — Chola, Chera and Pandya chiefs — hold an inland and a coastal capital each (Puhar, Madurai), take gifts and tribute instead of regular taxes, and reward praise-poets with gold, horses and elephants; 200 years later the Satavahanas rise in the west, Gautamiputra Shri Satakarni styling himself lord of the dakshinapatha and marching to three coasts.
