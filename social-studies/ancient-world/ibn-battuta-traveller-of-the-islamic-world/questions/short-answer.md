# Ibn Battuta — traveller of the Islamic world — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name, in order, the lands the lesson names on Ibn Battuta's way to India, and the three ways he travelled.
**Answer: Algeria, Egypt and Bahrain, on to India; he travelled on camels and horses and in sailing ships (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What did Ibn Battuta write down on his journeys, and what two things in this lesson still carry his name or his words today?
**Answer: He wrote down the customs and traditions of the peoples he saw, in the book of his travels; today his old description of China as one of the safest countries to travel in is still quoted, and Dubai's great Ibn Battuta shopping centre is named after him (any close wording).**
