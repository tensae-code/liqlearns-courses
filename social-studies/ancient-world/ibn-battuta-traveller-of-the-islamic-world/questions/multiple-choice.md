# Ibn Battuta — traveller of the Islamic world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ibn Battuta was born in —
a) India  b) Morocco  c) China
**Answer: b) Morocco** — he introduces himself as a Moroccan, real name Muhammad ibn Abdullah ibn Muhammad al-Tanji.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** His journeys began by setting out for —
a) A trading fair  b) The Hajj  c) A new school
**Answer: b) The Hajj** — he left home for the pilgrimage, and did not stop travelling.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ibn Battuta travelled for —
a) Twenty-seven years  b) Two years  c) Seven months
**Answer: a) Twenty-seven years** — visiting more than forty countries.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The title the world gave Ibn Battuta was —
a) King of Morocco  b) Prince of the Muslim Travellers  c) Father of the Map
**Answer: b) Prince of the Muslim Travellers** — earned by his travels and the book of them.
