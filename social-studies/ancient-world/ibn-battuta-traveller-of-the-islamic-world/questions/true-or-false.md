# Ibn Battuta — traveller of the Islamic world — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Ibn Battuta visited more than forty countries in his twenty-seven years of travel.
**Answer: True** — the book counts more than forty countries, crossed on camels and horses and in sailing ships.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Ibn Battuta described China as one of the safest countries to travel in.
**Answer: True** — the book keeps his old description of China among his achievements.
