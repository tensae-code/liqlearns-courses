**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (United Arab Emirates).**

United Arab Emirates' own way: at Grade 1, history is national identity — a visit to the Union Museum, the map of the seven emirates, Sheikh Zayed's sayings quoted on almost every spread under the heading "Zayed taught me", and one great traveller of the shared Arab past, Ibn Battuta, as the child's first named person from history. A young Moroccan leaves home for the pilgrimage and does not stop for twenty-seven years, and his book becomes the map of a whole medieval world. The traveller's story is filed here as a plot summary in the course's own words only — never transcribed. A short First Steps telling of the same book part (first-steps/ibn-battuta-traveller-and-explorer, Geography expansion) already exists for beginning readers; this Ancient World lesson is the History telling, at the full depth of the placement (printed pp. 70–81).
