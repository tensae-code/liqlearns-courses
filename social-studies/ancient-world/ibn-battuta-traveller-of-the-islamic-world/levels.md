# Ibn Battuta — traveller of the Islamic world — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — A young man from Morocco
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say where Ibn Battuta was born, give his real name as the book gives it, and say that his journeys began by setting out for the Hajj.

## Sub lesson 2 — Twenty-seven years on the road
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say how long he travelled, how he travelled, how many countries he visited, and name lands on his way in order — Algeria, Egypt and Bahrain to India.

## Sub lesson 3 — The book and the title
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what his book holds, what title he earned, what he said of China, and what place in Dubai is named after him.
