# Hands-on — Ibn Battuta — traveller of the Islamic world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Follow the journey line
Draw a simple journey line on paper. Mark the lands in the order the lesson names them: Morocco, Algeria, Egypt, Bahrain, India. Beside the line, draw the three ways he travelled: a camel, a horse and a sailing ship.

## Twenty-seven years
Draw 27 small boxes in a row — one for each year of Ibn Battuta's travelling. Say out loud how old you would be if you travelled for that many years from today.

## A traveller's notebook
Write three short lines as if you were Ibn Battuta arriving in a new land: one thing people eat, one thing people wear, one custom you would write down. Say why a traveller's notebook becomes a map of a whole world.
