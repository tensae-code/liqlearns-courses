# Video script — Ibn Battuta — traveller of the Islamic world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the traveller tells his own story, as the Grade 1 book lets him introduce himself. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A young man stands in Morocco, his home. His name is Muhammad ibn Abdullah ibn Muhammad al-Tanji — the world will know him as Ibn Battuta.

He sets out for the Hajj, the pilgrimage. But when the pilgrimage ends, he does not turn for home. He keeps going.

Year after year, he crosses the lands — on camels, on horses, in sailing ships. Algeria. Egypt. Bahrain. On to India. More than forty countries pass under his feet, and everywhere he goes, he watches how people live, and he writes down their customs and traditions.

Twenty-seven years pass before the travelling ends. What is left is a book — the book of his travels — and a title the world gives him: Prince of the Muslim Travellers. In his book, China stands as one of the safest countries to travel in.

And the name travels still: in Dubai today, a great shopping centre carries his name — Ibn Battuta — the traveller a Grade 1 child in the Emirates meets as the first named person from history.
