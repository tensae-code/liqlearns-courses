# Syria's historic cities — Palmyra, Bosra and Apamea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Palmyra in the middle of the Badia with its temples, tombs and colonnaded street ending at the triumphal arch, Bosra's great amphitheatre in Hawran, Apamea above the Ghab plain, and Rusafa, the desert city the Umayyad caliph Hisham took as his retreat.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2, Lesson مواقع تاريخية (Historic sites)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الرابع (Social Studies, Grade 4)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Palmyra, Bosra, Apamea and Rusafa

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Damascus is the oldest inhabited capital in the world, split into an old city inside its wall — the Umayyad Mosque, the citadel and the old souks — and a new city, and Aleppo likewise joins an old part, with its famous citadel, to a new one.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 3, Lesson من المدن السورية (Among Syria's cities)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الرابع (Social Studies, Grade 4)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Damascus and Aleppo — old city and new
