# Syria's historic cities — Palmyra, Bosra and Apamea — short answer

**Answer: Palmyra in the middle of the Badia with its temples, tombs and colonnaded street ending at the triumphal arch, Bosra's great amphitheatre in Hawran, Apamea above the Ghab plain, and Rusafa, the desert city the Umayyad caliph Hisham took as his retreat. **Answer: Damascus is the oldest inhabited capital in the world, split into an old city inside its wall — the Umayyad Mosque, the citadel and the old souks — and a new city, and Aleppo likewise joins an old part, with its famous citadel, to a new one.
