# Greek Myth Jason And The Argonauts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the flying ram rescues Phrixos and Helle, Helle falls into the strait named Hellespont after her, and the ram's golden fleece ends up guarded in Colchis.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos, Helle and the golden-fleeced ram

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Ιστορία Γ΄ Δημοτικού — Από τη Μυθολογία στην Ιστορία (History, Grade 3 Primary: From Mythology to History)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos, Helle and the golden-fleeced ram
