# Lesson plan — Greek Myth Jason And The Argonauts

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Greek Myth Jason And The Argonauts
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the flying ram rescues Phrixos and Helle, Helle falls into the strait named Hellespont after her, and the ram's golden fleece ends up guarded in Colchis
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos, Helle and the golden-fleeced ram** — the flying ram rescues Phrixos and Helle, Helle falls into the strait named Hellespont after her, and the ram's golden fleece ends up guarded in Colchis
2. **Unit 4, ch. 2 Pelias and Jason** — Pelias, who stole Iolcus from Jason's father, is warned of a one-sandalled man; Jason arrives with one sandal and is sent to fetch the fleece to win back the throne
3. **Unit 4, ch. 3 The Argonaut expedition** — the Argo's crew of heroes squeezes through the clashing Symplegades rocks, Jason yokes fire-breathing bulls and wins the fleece with Medea's help
4. **Unit 4, ch. 4 Talos, the first robot?** — on the wandering voyage home the Argonauts meet Talos, the bronze giant guarding Crete, whose single vein of ichor is his undoing
5. **Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos and Helle exercises** — pupils connect the Hellespont's name to Helle's fall — myth as an explanation of the map
6. **Unit 4, ch. 2 Pelias and Jason exercises** — pupils build their own Argo in paper craft, entering the expedition through its ship
7. **Unit 4, ch. 3 Argonaut expedition exercises** — reading work follows the Argo through the clashing rocks to Colchis and back

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
