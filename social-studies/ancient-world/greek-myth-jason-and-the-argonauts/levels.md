# Greek Myth Jason And The Argonauts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4, ch. 1 Phrixos, Helle and the golden-fleeced ram
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the flying ram rescues Phrixos and Helle, Helle falls into the strait named Hellespont after her, and the ram's golden fleece ends up guarded in Colchis

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 4, ch. 2 Pelias and Jason
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Pelias, who stole Iolcus from Jason's father, is warned of a one-sandalled man; Jason arrives with one sandal and is sent to fetch the fleece to win back the throne

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 4, ch. 3 The Argonaut expedition
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Argo's crew of heroes squeezes through the clashing Symplegades rocks, Jason yokes fire-breathing bulls and wins the fleece with Medea's help

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 4, ch. 4 Talos, the first robot?
