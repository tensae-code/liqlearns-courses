# Byzantine Civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Byzantine civilisation: letters, sciences, technology, art.
**Answer: a thousand years of scholarship, law, icons and domes preserve and extend the ancient inheritance (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Μεσαιωνικού και του Νεότερου Κόσμου 565–1815 (History of the Medieval and Modern World), Β' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Dimitroukas, Thoukydidis P. Ioannou & Kostas Baroutas* (Greece), Byzantine civilisation: letters, sciences, technology, art, printed p. 91.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Chapter 7: Byzantine art.
**Answer: the dome, the icon and the gold mosaic turn the church into an image of heaven for a thousand years (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία της Τέχνης (History of Art), Β' & Γ' ΕΠΑ.Λ., Applied Arts sector, by Olga Ziro, Eleni Mertzani & Vasiliki Petridou* (Greece), Chapter 7: Byzantine art, printed p. 93.**
