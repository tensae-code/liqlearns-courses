# Video script — Bulgaria — the Second Empire: the Asenevtsi

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bulgaria's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Asen brothers raise the Second Bulgarian Empire (1185) and Kaloyan and Ivan Asen II make it great.

Two brothers raise a tax revolt at Tarnovo and resurrect the Bulgarian state.

Kaloyan bargains with the Pope and crushes the Latin knights at Adrianople.

Klokotnitsa (1230) makes Ivan Asen II master of Southeastern Europe.
