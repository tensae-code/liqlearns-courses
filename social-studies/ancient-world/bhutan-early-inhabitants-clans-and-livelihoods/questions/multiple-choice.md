# Bhutan — early inhabitants, clans and livelihoods — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter Two, Social Organisation and Economic Life  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter Two, Social Organisation and Economic Life** — cut off valley by valley, early Bhutanese live in clan settlements under chieftains, herd yaks down to winter pasture, learn farming and tseri shifting cultivation, then crafts and barter, and finally trade with Tibet and India
