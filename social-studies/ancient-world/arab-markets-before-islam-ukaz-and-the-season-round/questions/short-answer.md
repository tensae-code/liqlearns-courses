# Arab markets before Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Arab markets before Islam.
**Answer: the Arabs keep a travelling year of markets — Dumat al-Jandal in Rabi' al-Awwal, Hubasha in Rajab, Aden in Ramadan, Sanaa at the Hajj month, al-Shihr and al-Rabiya in Hadramawt — safe-conduct for every trader's life and goods, taxes taken by kings except at free 'Ukaz, the greatest of them all, where the market is also parliament and poetry festival: alliances like Quraysh's and the Fudul are sworn, disputes judged, news carried, and poets hang their reputations on the crowd's verdict — trade, the book argues, was already stitching the Arabs into one people before Islam (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم القديم — للصف الأول الثانوي (History: Civilisations of the Ancient World, for the First Secondary Grade)* (Yemen), Arab markets before Islam, printed p. 162.**
