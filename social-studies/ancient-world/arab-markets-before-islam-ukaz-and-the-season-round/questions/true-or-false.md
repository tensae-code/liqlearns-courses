# Arab markets before Islam — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Arabs keep a travelling year of markets — Dumat al-Jandal in Rabi' al-Awwal, Hubasha in Rajab, Aden in Ramadan, Sanaa at the Hajj month, al-Shihr and al-Rabiya in Hadramawt — safe-conduct for every trader's life and goods, taxes taken by kings except at free 'Ukaz, the greatest of them all, where the market is also parliament and poetry festival: alliances like Quraysh's and the Fudul are sworn, disputes judged, news carried, and poets hang their reputations on the crowd's verdict — trade, the book argues, was already stitching the Arabs into one people before Islam.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Arab markets before Islam
