# Cyprus Neolithic — Choirokoitia and the first villages

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: farming pinned people to one place, and in Cyprus the first farmers raised Choirokoitia — a village of round stone houses whose remains still argue about how they were roofed.
