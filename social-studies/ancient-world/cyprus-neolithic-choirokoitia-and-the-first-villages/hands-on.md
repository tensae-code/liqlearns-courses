# Hands-on — Cyprus Neolithic — Choirokoitia and the first villages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Build a model round house of Choirokoitia from clay or paper — then draw the two roof shapes scholars argue about.

## Task 2
On a map of Cyprus mark Choirokoitia and Sotira, and write one thing the two villages share with farming villages abroad.
