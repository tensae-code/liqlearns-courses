# Cyprus Neolithic — Choirokoitia and the first villages — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Choirokoitia's houses were round.
**Answer: True** — stone, mud and timber become round houses at Choirokoitia.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Neolithic villages existed only in Cyprus.
**Answer: False** — Cypriot settlements are compared with villages abroad; the Neolithic pattern is shared.
