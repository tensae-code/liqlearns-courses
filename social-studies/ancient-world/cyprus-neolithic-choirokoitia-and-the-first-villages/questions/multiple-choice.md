# Cyprus Neolithic — Choirokoitia and the first villages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Neolithic people moved less than Palaeolithic people because —
a) farming pinned them to one place  b) the seas froze  c) kings forbade travel
**Answer: a) farming pinned them to one place** — farming pins people to one place.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The houses of Choirokoitia were —
a) round, built of stone, mud and timber  b) square brick towers  c) tents of hide
**Answer: a) round, built of stone, mud and timber** — the round-house village of Choirokoitia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Choirokoitia and Sotira are both —
a) Neolithic settlements in Cyprus  b) Bronze Age ports  c) Roman theatres
**Answer: a) Neolithic settlements in Cyprus** — Cypriot settlements compared with villages abroad.
