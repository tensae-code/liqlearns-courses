# Hands-on — Vietnam Dai Co Viet Dinh And Tien Le

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Dai Co Viet Dinh And Tien Le — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 10, the Đinh state of Đại Cổ Việt (968), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
