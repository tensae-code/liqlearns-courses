# Vietnam Dai Co Viet Dinh And Tien Le — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Đinh Bộ Lĩnh takes the imperial title, names the country Đại Cổ Việt and rules from mountain-walled Hoa Lư with a ten-đạo army, his own coinage, stern law and envoys sent to the Song.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 10, the Đinh state of Đại Cổ Việt (968)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 10, the Đinh state of Đại Cổ Việt (968)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lê Hoàn's Tiền Lê keeps building — provinces reorganised by 1002, a law code issued, a two-part army of palace guards and local troops — in a society of rulers and ruled where Buddhism flourishes, monks are the learned class, and folk singing, wrestling and boat races live on.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 10, the Tiền Lê state and Đinh–Tiền Lê society and culture

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 10, the Tiền Lê state and Đinh–Tiền Lê society and culture

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** after a thousand years of northern rule, a warlord's son reunites a broken country and gives it a name and a coin.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chuyên đề 3.II Đinh Bộ Lĩnh (924–979): the boy from Đại Hữu who subdues the twelve warlords one by one, takes the throne in 968, names the country Đại Cồ Việt, builds Hoa Lư's mountain citadel, divides the land into ten circuits and mints the Thái Bình hưng bảo coin

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Chuyên đề học tập Lịch sử 11 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Chuyên đề 3.II Đinh Bộ Lĩnh (924–979): the boy from Đại Hữu who subdues the twelve warlords one by one, takes the throne in 968, names the country Đại Cồ Việt, builds Hoa Lư's mountain citadel, divides the land into ten circuits and mints the Thái Bình hưng bảo coin
