## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 10, the Đinh state of Đại Cổ Việt (968)** — Đinh Bộ Lĩnh takes the imperial title, names the country Đại Cổ Việt and rules from mountain-walled Hoa Lư with a ten-đạo army, his own coinage, stern law and envoys sent to the Song
2. **Bài 10, the Tiền Lê state and Đinh–Tiền Lê society and culture** — Lê Hoàn's Tiền Lê keeps building — provinces reorganised by 1002, a law code issued, a two-part army of palace guards and local troops — in a society of rulers and ruled where Buddhism flourishes, monks are the learned class, and folk singing, wrestling and boat races live on
3. **Chuyên đề 3.II Đinh Bộ Lĩnh (924–979): the boy from Đại Hữu who subdues the twelve warlords one by one, takes the throne in 968, names the country Đại Cồ Việt, builds Hoa Lư's mountain citadel, divides the land into ten circuits and mints the Thái Bình hưng bảo coin** — after a thousand years of northern rule, a warlord's son reunites a broken country and gives it a name and a coin
