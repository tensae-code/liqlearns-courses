# Medieval Nepal — the Malla kingdoms

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Nepal's history is periodised — ancient (Gopal, Mahispal, Kirat, Lichchhavi), medieval from 936 BS (Nepal Sambat) for some 900 years, modern after 1825 BS; in the medieval age the country splits into the Khas kingdom, Tirhut and Nepalmandal, the Mallas enter the valley from Kusinagar, Aridev Malla takes the "Malla" name from a wrestling bout, and after Yaksha Malla's death in 1538 BS his sons split the valley into Kantipur, Patan and Bhaktapur As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
