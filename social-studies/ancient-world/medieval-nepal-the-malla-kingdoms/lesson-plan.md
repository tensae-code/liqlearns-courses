# Lesson plan — Medieval Nepal — the Malla kingdoms

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Medieval Nepal — the Malla kingdoms
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Nepal, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Nepal's history is periodised — ancient (Gopal, Mahispal, Kirat, Lichchhavi), medieval from 936 BS (Nepal Sambat) for some 900 years, modern after 1825 BS; in the medieval age the country splits into the Khas kingdom, Tirhut and Nepalmandal, the Mallas enter the valley from Kusinagar, Aridev Malla takes the "Malla" name from a wrestling bout, and after Yaksha Malla's death in 1538 BS his sons split the valley into Kantipur, Patan and Bhaktapur
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Medieval Nepal (Grade 7)** — Nepal's history is periodised — ancient (Gopal, Mahispal, Kirat, Lichchhavi), medieval from 936 BS (Nepal Sambat) for some 900 years, modern after 1825 BS; in the medieval age the country splits into…
2. **Medieval Nepal — Political, Social and Economic Condition (Grade 7)** — rival valley kings sometimes ally and sometimes invade, helped by Sen or Gorkha armies; society runs on caste and occupation, sati, child marriage and festivals like Indra Jatra, while Malla courts…
3. **The Medieval Political Condition (Grade 12)** — after the Licchavis the Malla kings rule, Yaksha Malla's sons divide the valley into three rival kingdoms, and the lesson maps the medieval political order of Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur
4. **The Medieval Economic, Social and Cultural Condition (Grade 12)** — medieval life is reconstructed whole — land and trade, caste-ordered society, festivals, and the pagoda art and craft that made the valley famous
