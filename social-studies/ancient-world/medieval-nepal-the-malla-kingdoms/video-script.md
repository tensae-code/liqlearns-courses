# Video script — Medieval Nepal — the Malla kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Nepal's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Nepal's history is periodised — ancient (Gopal, Mahispal, Kirat, Lichchhavi), medieval from 936 BS (Nepal Sambat) for some 900 years, modern after 1825 BS; in the medieval age the country splits into the Khas kingdom, Tirhut and Nepalmandal, the Mallas enter the valley from Kusinagar, Aridev Malla takes the "Malla" name from a wrestling bout, and after Yaksha Malla's death in 1538 BS his sons split the valley into Kantipur, Patan and Bhaktapur

---

rival valley kings sometimes ally and sometimes invade, helped by Sen or Gorkha armies; society runs on caste and occupation, sati, child marriage and festivals like Indra Jatra, while Malla courts keep pandits and monastery schools, coin silver money, and grow rich trading with Tibet

---

after the Licchavis the Malla kings rule, Yaksha Malla's sons divide the valley into three rival kingdoms, and the lesson maps the medieval political order of Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur

---

medieval life is reconstructed whole — land and trade, caste-ordered society, festivals, and the pagoda art and craft that made the valley famous
