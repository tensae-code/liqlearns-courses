# Medieval Nepal — the Malla kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Medieval Nepal.
**Answer: Nepal's history is periodised — ancient (Gopal, Mahispal, Kirat, Lichchhavi), medieval from 936 BS (Nepal Sambat) for some 900 years, modern after 1825 BS; in the medieval age the country splits into the Khas kingdom, Tirhut and Nepalmandal, the Mallas enter the valley from Kusinagar, Aridev Malla takes the "Malla" name from a wrestling bout, and after Yaksha Malla's death in 1538 BS his sons split the valley into Kantipur, Patan and Bhaktapur (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 7* (Grade 7), Unit 5 Lesson 1: Medieval Nepal, printed p. 85.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Medieval Nepal — Political, Social and Economic Condition.
**Answer: rival valley kings sometimes ally and sometimes invade, helped by Sen or Gorkha armies; society runs on caste and occupation, sati, child marriage and festivals like Indra Jatra, while Malla courts keep pandits and monastery schools, coin silver money, and grow rich trading with Tibet (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 7* (Grade 7), Unit 5 Lesson 3: Medieval Nepal — Political, Social and Economic Condition, printed p. 91.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: The Medieval Political Condition.
**Answer: after the Licchavis the Malla kings rule, Yaksha Malla's sons divide the valley into three rival kingdoms, and the lesson maps the medieval political order of Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Jibanopayogi Shiksha, Class 12* (Grade 12), Unit 6 Lesson 3: The Medieval Political Condition, printed p. 158.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: The Medieval Economic, Social and Cultural Condition.
**Answer: medieval life is reconstructed whole — land and trade, caste-ordered society, festivals, and the pagoda art and craft that made the valley famous (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
