# Medieval Nepal — the Malla kingdoms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nepal's history is periodised — ancient (Gopal, Mahispal, Kirat, Lichchhavi), medieval from 936 BS (Nepal Sambat) for some 900 years, modern after 1825 BS; in the medieval age the country splits into the Khas kingdom,…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Medieval Nepal

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 7* is about dividing Nepal's history into periods.
**Answer: False** — it is about Medieval Nepal

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** rival valley kings sometimes ally and sometimes invade, helped by Sen or Gorkha armies; society runs on caste and occupation, sati, child marriage and festivals like Indra Jatra, while Malla courts keep pandits and…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Medieval Nepal — Political, Social and Economic Condition

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 7* is about The Meaning and Origin of the Word Nepal.
**Answer: False** — it is about Medieval Nepal — Political, Social and Economic Condition

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** after the Licchavis the Malla kings rule, Yaksha Malla's sons divide the valley into three rival kingdoms, and the lesson maps the medieval political order of Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Medieval Political Condition

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Jibanopayogi Shiksha, Class 12* is about My Ancestors.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Medieval Political Condition

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** medieval life is reconstructed whole — land and trade, caste-ordered society, festivals, and the pagoda art and craft that made the valley famous
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Medieval Economic, Social and Cultural Condition

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Jibanopayogi Shiksha, Class 12* is about My Friend's Birthday.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Medieval Economic, Social and Cultural Condition
