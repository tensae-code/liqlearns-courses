# Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Princess Olha avenges Ihor, then reforms the tribute itself — fixed "uroky" dues and "pohosty" collection points instead of arbitrary seizure — and is baptised in Constantinople; her son Sviatoslav the Brave smashes Khazaria and wars in the Balkans, and dies in a Pecheneg ambush at the Dnipro rapids in 972. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Olha reforms the tribute

Princess Olha avenges Ihor — then reforms the tribute itself. Fixed "uroky" dues and "pohosty" collection points replace arbitrary seizure, and Olha is baptised in Constantinople.

## Sub lesson 2 — Sviatoslav the Brave

Olha's son Sviatoslav the Brave smashes Khazaria and wars in the Balkans. In 972 he dies in a Pecheneg ambush at the Dnipro rapids.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
