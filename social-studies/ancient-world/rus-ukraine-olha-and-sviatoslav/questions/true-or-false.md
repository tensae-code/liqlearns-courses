# Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Olha's reform made tribute fixed and collected at set points.
**Answer: True — Uroky are fixed dues and pohosty are collection points, replacing arbitrary seizure.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sviatoslav died of old age in Kyiv.
**Answer: False — He died in a Pecheneg ambush at the Dnipro rapids in 972.**
