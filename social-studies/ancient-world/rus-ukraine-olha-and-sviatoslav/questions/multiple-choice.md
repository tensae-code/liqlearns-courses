# Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Olha replaced arbitrary seizure with —
a) Fixed uroky dues and pohosty collection points  b) No tribute at all  c) Tribute paid only in soldiers
**Answer: a) Fixed uroky dues and pohosty collection points**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Olha was baptised in —
a) Constantinople  b) Kyiv  c) Rome
**Answer: a) Constantinople**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sviatoslav smashed —
a) Khazaria  b) Byzantium's capital  c) The Derevlians only
**Answer: a) Khazaria**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Sviatoslav died in 972 —
a) In a Pecheneg ambush at the Dnipro rapids  b) In Constantinople  c) Fighting in Kyiv
**Answer: a) In a Pecheneg ambush at the Dnipro rapids**
