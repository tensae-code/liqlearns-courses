# Video script — Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Ukraine's *Історія України* (Grade 7) tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A widow takes power. Olha avenges her husband — then fixes the system that killed him.



Fixed dues. Set collection points. And a baptism in Constantinople.



Her son Sviatoslav breaks Khazaria — and falls at the Dnipro rapids in 972.
