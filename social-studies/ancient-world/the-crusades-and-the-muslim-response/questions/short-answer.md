# The Crusades and the Muslim response — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Egypt in the Ayyubid age: Saladin and the Crusades.
**Answer: Hattin restores Jerusalem and lifts Muslim hearts; Richard's crusade ends in the peace of 1192 and Saladin dies the next year; his brother al-Adil sees that the Crusaders now aim at Egypt herself — the Fifth Crusade takes Damietta, but al-Kamil floods the Delta plain and the invaders, cut off from their base, evacuate Egypt in 1221 for nothing; a generation later Louis IX lands for the Seventh Crusade, al-Salih Ayyub dies mid-crisis, and his widow Shajar al-Durr hides the death, holds the state together and sees Louis captured at al-Mansura. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Egypt's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* (Grade 11 (Second Secondary)), Egypt in the Ayyubid age: Saladin and the Crusades, printed p. 91.**
