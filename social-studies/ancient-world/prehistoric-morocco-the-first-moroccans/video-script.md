# Video script — Prehistoric Morocco — the first Moroccans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Morocco's story as its Grade 5 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bones and tools found in Moroccan soil show prehistoric Moroccan man — the first Moroccans — long before any state, city or writing.

From those remains the guide rebuilds his way of life: hunting, sheltering and surviving in the land that would become Morocco.
