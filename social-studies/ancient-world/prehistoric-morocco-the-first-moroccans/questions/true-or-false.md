# Prehistoric Morocco — the first Moroccans — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Prehistoric Morocco had kings and written laws.
**Answer: False — it comes long before any state; it is known from bones and tools, not writings.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide teaches prehistoric Morocco from remains in Moroccan soil.
**Answer: True — bones and tools in Moroccan soil are its evidence.**
