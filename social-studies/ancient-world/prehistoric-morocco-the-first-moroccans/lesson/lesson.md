# Prehistoric Morocco — the first Moroccans

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Morocco's long story as one line: first Moroccans, Amazigh, then dynasty after dynasty. This lesson is one chapter of that line, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Remains in Moroccan soil

Bones and tools found in Moroccan soil show prehistoric Moroccan man — the first Moroccans — long before any state, city or writing.
