# Bulgaria — Christianisation under Boris I — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Boris I baptises Bulgaria (864/865) and bargains between Rome and Constantinople for his church.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 10: The Christianisation of the Bulgarian state

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История и цивилизации за шести клас (History and Civilizations for the 6th Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 10: The Christianisation of the Bulgarian state

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Boris I accepts baptism and fights Rome and Constantinople for a church of Bulgaria's own.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 8: The Christianisation of the Bulgarians and the struggle for an independent Bulgarian church

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История и цивилизации, 10. клас (History and Civilizations, 10th Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
