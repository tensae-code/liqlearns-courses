# How slave society worked — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** People first became slaves, in this lesson, from —
a) debt and capture in war  b) old age  c) failing school
**Answer: a) debt and capture in war** — debtors and prisoners of war became slaves.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The change that made classes possible was —
a) surplus and private property  b) the invention of writing  c) sea trade
**Answer: a) surplus and private property** — surplus food and private property split society into classes.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Alongside slaves and slave-owners there were —
a) free farmers and craftsmen  b) only kings  c) no one else
**Answer: a) free farmers and craftsmen** — free farmers and craftsmen stood between.
