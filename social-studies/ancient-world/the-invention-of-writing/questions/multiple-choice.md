# The invention of writing — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first writing steps the book names are —
a) Tokens and picture-signs  b) Printed books  c) Stone alphabets
**Answer: a) Tokens and picture-signs** — memory outside the head comes first.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cuneiform was made by —
a) Carving pictures in stone  b) Pressing a reed into clay  c) Painting on papyrus
**Answer: b) Pressing a reed into clay** — the wedge marks of Mesopotamia.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Hieroglyphs belong to —
a) The Göktürks  b) Rome  c) Egypt
**Answer: c) Egypt** — Egypt's picture-sign script.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The alphabet made writing easier because —
a) Its signs stand for sounds, and there are few of them  b) It used no signs at all  c) Only kings could use it
**Answer: a) Its signs stand for sounds, and there are few of them** — the great simplification the book ends with.
