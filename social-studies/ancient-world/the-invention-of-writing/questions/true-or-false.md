# The invention of writing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book treats writing as the line where prehistory ends and history proper begins.
**Answer: True** — from writing on, the past can speak for itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cuneiform was Egypt's script.
**Answer: False** — cuneiform is Mesopotamia's; Egypt's is hieroglyphs.
