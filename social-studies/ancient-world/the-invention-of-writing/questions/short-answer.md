# The invention of writing — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the road of writing in order, as the book teaches it.
**Answer: Tokens and pictures, then cuneiform and hieroglyphs, then the alphabet (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does history proper begin with writing?
**Answer: Because written records let the past speak for itself, instead of dying with the rememberer (any close wording).**
