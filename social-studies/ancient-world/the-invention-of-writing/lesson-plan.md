# Lesson plan — The invention of writing

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The invention of writing

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell the road of writing in order: tokens and pictures, cuneiform, hieroglyphs, the alphabet.
2. Say where cuneiform was written, and with what — a reed pressed into clay in Mesopotamia.
3. Say what made the alphabet easier: a small set of signs standing for sounds.
4. Explain, in their own words, why the book says history proper begins with writing.
