# Video script — The invention of writing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before writing, the past lived only in memory — and died with the rememberer.

Turkey's Grade 9 book tells how that changed. First came tokens and picture-signs, standing for goods and things. Then, in Mesopotamia, a scribe pressed a reed into wet clay and left wedge-shaped marks: cuneiform. In Egypt, picture-signs became hieroglyphs.

Last came the alphabet — a small set of signs for sounds, which anyone could learn.

That is why the book says history proper begins the day a Sumerian presses a reed into clay: from that day, the past could speak for itself.
