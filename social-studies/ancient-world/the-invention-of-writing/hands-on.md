# Hands-on — The invention of writing

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Press your own tablet

Flatten a ball of clay, dough or thick mud into a tablet. With a stick, press wedge marks in rows — one row for each thing in your home you want to remember. Let it dry: you have made a record that can outlive your memory.

## Three scripts, one word

Write your name three ways: as a picture-sign you invent, as wedge marks, and in alphabet letters. Say which was fastest to make, and which a stranger could read.

## The first thing written

On paper, write the very first thing you would record if writing were new — a count, a debt, a king's name, a prayer. Under it, write one sentence on why you chose it.
