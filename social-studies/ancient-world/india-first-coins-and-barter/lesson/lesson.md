# India — first coins and barter

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Punch-marked coins circulated for 500 years across the subcontinent — while salt merchants still carted paddy to swap for coastal salt, barter living beside money. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Coins; other means of exchange

Punch-marked coins, stamped with symbols and uninscribed, circulate for 500 years across the subcontinent — while a Sangam poem's salt merchants still cart white paddy to swap for coastal salt, whole families travelling roads "through sands white as moonlight".

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
