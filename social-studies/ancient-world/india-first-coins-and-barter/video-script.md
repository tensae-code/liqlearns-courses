# Video script — India — first coins and barter

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Punch-marked coins, stamped with symbols and uninscribed, circulate for 500 years across the subcontinent — while a Sangam poem's salt merchants still cart white paddy to swap for coastal salt, whole families travelling roads "through sands white as moonlight".
