# Hands-on — Kievan Rus and the Slavs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Kievan Rus and the Slavs — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 18. Slavic tribes. Kievan Rus, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
