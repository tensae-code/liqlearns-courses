## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH)** — yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina; he reaches Yemen in 280 AH, settles disputes, founds a state on the Qur'an and the Sunna with its capital at Saada, unifies Yemen from Najran to Aden, and dies in 298 AH beside his mosque in Saada — and his imams, named for Zayd bin Ali, rule Yemen in stretches from then until the revolution of 1962
2. **Yemeni states that contributed to the Islamic civilisation: the Zaydi state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)** — a delegation of Yemeni tribal leaders invites Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Rassi from Medina; he takes the title al-Hadi ila al-Haqq, founds the Zaydi state at Saada (284 AH), and its schools and "hijar al-'ilm" make Saada a meeting point of scholars while al-Hadi himself writes works of tafsir, hadith and law
