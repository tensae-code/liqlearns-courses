# Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH) — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina; he reaches Yemen in 280 AH, settles disputes, founds a state on the Qur'an and the Sunna with its capital at Saada, unifies Yemen from Najran to Aden, and dies in 298 AH beside his mosque in Saada — and his imams, named for Zayd bin Ali, rule Yemen in stretches from then until the revolution of 1962.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a delegation of Yemeni tribal leaders invites Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Rassi from Medina; he takes the title al-Hadi ila al-Haqq, founds the Zaydi state at Saada (284 AH), and its schools and "hijar al-'ilm" make Saada a meeting point of scholars while al-Hadi himself writes works of tafsir, hadith and law.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Yemeni states that contributed to the Islamic civilisation: the Zaydi state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الثاني (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Yemeni states that contributed to the Islamic civilisation: the Zaydi state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)
