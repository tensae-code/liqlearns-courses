# Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH).
**Answer: yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina; he reaches Yemen in 280 AH, settles disputes, founds a state on the Qur'an and the Sunna with its capital at Saada, unifies Yemen from Najran to Aden, and dies in 298 AH beside his mosque in Saada — and his imams, named for Zayd bin Ali, rule Yemen in stretches from then until the revolution of 1962 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* (Yemen), Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH), printed p. 68.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Yemeni states that contributed to the Islamic civilisation: the Zaydi state (Unit 4 Lesson 2).
**Answer: a delegation of Yemeni tribal leaders invites Yahya ibn al-Husayn al-Rassi from Medina; he takes the title al-Hadi ila al-Haqq, founds the Zaydi state at Saada (284 AH), and its schools and "hijar al-'ilm" make Saada a meeting point of scholars while al-Hadi himself writes works of tafsir, hadith and law (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الثاني (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 2)* (Yemen), Yemeni states that contributed to the Islamic civilisation: the Zaydi state (Unit 4 Lesson 2), printed p. 19.**
