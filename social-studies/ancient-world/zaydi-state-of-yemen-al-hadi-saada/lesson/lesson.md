# Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH)

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Founding of the Islamic (Zaydi) state in Yemen (284-298 AH)

Yemenis sick of Abbasid governors' wars invite Yahya bin al-Husayn al-Rassi, al-Hadi, from al-Rass near Medina; he reaches Yemen in 280 AH, settles disputes, founds a state on the Qur'an and the Sunna with its capital at Saada, unifies Yemen from Najran to Aden, and dies in 298 AH beside his mosque in Saada — and his imams, named for Zayd bin Ali, rule Yemen in stretches from then until the revolution of 1962
