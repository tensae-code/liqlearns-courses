# Video script — Yemen in the Umayyad and Abbasid ages

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yemen gets mostly harsh governors from both dynasties and answers with revolts — the Tawwabun rising for Husayn after Karbala among them — while Yemeni scholars shine inside and outside the country, Malik bin Anas al-Asbahi, Abd al-Razzaq al-San'ani and Ma'mar bin Rashid, and al-Shafi'i himself studies in Yemen.
