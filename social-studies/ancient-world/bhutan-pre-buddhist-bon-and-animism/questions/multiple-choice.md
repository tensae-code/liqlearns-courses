# Bhutan — pre-Buddhist Bon and animism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter One, Pre-Buddhist Traditions  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter One, Pre-Buddhist Traditions** — before Buddhism, Himalayan people take refuge in mountains, forests and lakes, and animist and shamanist practice hardens into Bon — Black Bon with animal sacrifice, White Bon without — much of it surviving later as folk rite inside Buddhist Bhutan
