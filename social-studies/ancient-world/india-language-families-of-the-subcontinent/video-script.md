# Video script — India — language families of the subcontinent

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sanskrit's word matr, Hindi's ma and English's mother betray one Indo-European family, while Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam (Dravidian), the north-east's Tibeto-Burman tongues and central India's Austro-Asiatic languages map the subcontinent's other families.
