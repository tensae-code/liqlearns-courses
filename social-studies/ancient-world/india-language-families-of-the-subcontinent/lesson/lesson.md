# India — language families of the subcontinent

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The subcontinent's languages fall into families: Indo-European Sanskrit and its kin, the Dravidian south, Tibeto-Burman in the north-east and Austro-Asiatic in central India. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sanskrit and other languages

Sanskrit's word matr, Hindi's ma and English's mother betray one Indo-European family, while Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam (Dravidian), the north-east's Tibeto-Burman tongues and central India's Austro-Asiatic languages map the subcontinent's other families.
