# The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH).
**Answer: najah, an Abyssinian freedman of the Ziyadid chamberlain Marjan, outlives his master, takes Zabid with the Abbasid caliph's blessing and founds a state; his sons Sa'id al-Ahwal and Jayyash trade Zabid back and forth with the Sulayhids — Jayyash even slips away to the Hajj and returns to retake the city — until the last ruler, Fatiq, sunk in pleasure, is killed by the Zaydi Imam Ahmad bin Sulayman (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* (Yemen), The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH), printed p. 77.**
