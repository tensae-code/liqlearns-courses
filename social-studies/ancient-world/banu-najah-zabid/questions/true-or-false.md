# The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH) — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Najah, an Abyssinian freedman of the Ziyadid chamberlain Marjan, outlives his master, takes Zabid with the Abbasid caliph's blessing and founds a state; his sons Sa'id al-Ahwal and Jayyash trade Zabid back and forth with the Sulayhids — Jayyash even slips away to the Hajj and returns to retake the city — until the last ruler, Fatiq, sunk in pleasure, is killed by the Zaydi Imam Ahmad bin Sulayman.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH)
