## Sub lesson 1 — The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH)

Najah, an Abyssinian freedman of the Ziyadid chamberlain Marjan, outlives his master, takes Zabid with the Abbasid caliph's blessing and founds a state; his sons Sa'id al-Ahwal and Jayyash trade Zabid back and forth with the Sulayhids — Jayyash even slips away to the Hajj and returns to retake the city — until the last ruler, Fatiq, sunk in pleasure, is killed by the Zaydi Imam Ahmad bin Sulayman

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
