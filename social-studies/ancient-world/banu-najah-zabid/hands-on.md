# Hands-on — The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH) — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The state of Bani Najah (412-553 AH), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
