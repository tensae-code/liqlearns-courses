# Lesson plan — Byzantium At Its Height The Macedonian Dynasty

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Byzantium At Its Height The Macedonian Dynasty
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Leo VI and the Macedonian emperors reissue the laws in Greek and tighten the themes' government
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 23 Legislation and administration are modernised** — Leo VI and the Macedonian emperors reissue the laws in Greek and tighten the themes' government
2. **Unit 25 Byzantium reaches the peak of its flourishing** — Basil II grinds Bulgaria down to "the Bulgar-slayer", and the empire again stretches from Italy to the Euphrates
3. **Unit 23 Laws modernised exercises** — exercises follow the Macedonian emperors reissuing law in Greek
4. **Unit 25 Peak of Byzantium exercises** — exercises crown Basil II's long grinding of Bulgaria
5. **Ch. 3.I.3 The reign of Michael III and the dawn of the New Age** — Photius at the patriarchate, missions planned, learning reborn — the Macedonian dawn breaks under an underestimated emperor
6. **Ch. 3.I.5 The Byzantine Epic: epic struggles and expansion of the Empire** — Phokas and Tzimiskes retake Crete, Cyprus and Antioch, and Basil II finishes Bulgaria — the empire's military epic
