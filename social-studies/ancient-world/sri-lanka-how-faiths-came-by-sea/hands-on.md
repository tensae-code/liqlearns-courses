# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — How faiths came by sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — How faiths came by sea — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Religions arrive with the traders, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
