# Video script — Bengal's political history — from the Mauryas to the Palas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bangladesh book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Grade 9 book traces Bengal's political path. First, empires reach in — Maurya and Gupta emperors, with Samudra Gupta capturing Banga and Pundra.

Then Gupta power fades, and the story turns: Shashanka of Karnasubarna becomes Bengal's first great independent king.

After him, the Pala dynasty. Ruled from outside, then ruling itself — Bengal's thousand-year pattern begins here.
