Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Name the empires that reached into Bengal: Maurya and Gupta. Say what Samudra Gupta captured: Banga and Pundra. Name Bengal's first great independent king and his seat: Shashanka of Karnasubarna; and the dynasty that followed: the Palas.
