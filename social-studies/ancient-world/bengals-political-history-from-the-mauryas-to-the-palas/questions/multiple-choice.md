# Bengal's political history — from the Mauryas to the Palas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The empires that first reached into Bengal were —
a) Maurya and Gupta  b) Mughal and British  c) Pala and Sena
**Answer: a) Maurya and Gupta** — the first empires in the book's path.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Samudra Gupta captured —
a) Karnasubarna  b) Banga and Pundra  c) Tamralipti only
**Answer: b) Banga and Pundra** — the Gupta capture.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bengal's first great independent king was —
a) Shashanka of Karnasubarna  b) Samudra Gupta  c) Akbar
**Answer: a) Shashanka of Karnasubarna** — Bengal's own first king.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The dynasty that followed Shashanka in the book's path was —
a) The Sena dynasty  b) The Pala dynasty  c) The Gupta dynasty
**Answer: b) The Pala dynasty** — the Palas follow.
