# Bengal's political history — from the Mauryas to the Palas — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bengal was always ruled by its own kings from the earliest times.
**Answer: False** — Maurya and Gupta empires ruled it first; independence came with Shashanka.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Samudra Gupta was a Gupta emperor who captured Banga and Pundra.
**Answer: True** — the book names his capture.
