## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 8, story of Lý Bí and the Vạn Xuân state** — under the harsh Liang, local leader Lý Bí rises in the 6th century, the occupiers flee, and in spring 544 he takes the throne as Lý Nam Đế of a state named Vạn Xuân, "ten thousand springs"
2. **Bài 16, Lý Bí's uprising and the state of Vạn Xuân** — in 542 Lý Bí throws off the Lương; in 544 he proclaims himself Lý Nam Đế of Vạn Xuân, building the Vạn Thọ palace and Khai Quốc pagoda; Triệu Quang Phục holds out in the Dạ Trạch swamp and reigns as Triệu Việt Vương, until the Sui end Vạn Xuân in 602
