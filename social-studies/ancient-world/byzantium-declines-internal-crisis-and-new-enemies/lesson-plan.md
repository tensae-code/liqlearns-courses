## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 28 The state faces great internal problems** — after Basil II, weak emperors, court factions and a debased coinage hollow out the empire from inside
2. **Unit 29 New enemies appear and tear away imperial lands** — Seljuk Turks take Anatolia after Manzikert while Normans seize the Italian provinces — the empire shrinks on both fronts
3. **Unit 28 Internal problems exercises** — exercises list the empire's inner rot after Basil II
4. **Unit 29 New enemies exercises** — map work shows Seljuks and Normans tearing the edges at once
5. **Revision of Units 23–32** — gap-fill discussion names the reasons for Byzantium's decline from Leo III to the Palaiologoi
6. **Ch. 4.I.1 The crisis and losses of the empire in the 11th century (1025–1081)** — after Basil II the themes decay, the coinage is debased, Bari falls to Normans and Anatolia to Turks after Manzikert
