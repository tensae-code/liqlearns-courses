# The Lapita people and Pacific settlement

## Sub lesson 1 — 1.1 Lapita Pottery and the three Pacific peoples

Thousands of years ago Lapita voyagers from the Bismarck Archipelago carried a named pottery, farming and seafaring culture to New Caledonia, Fiji, Tonga and Samoa (sherds from about 1500 BC), ancestors of today's Melanesian, Micronesian and Polynesian peoples.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Chapter 2 Unit 1 History of Fiji: Pre-Contact Period / What was early Fiji like?

Lapita pottery found at Lakeba, Beqa, Natunuku and Naigani shows the first Fijians shared a culture with the western Pacific; chiefs rule villages and islands, and daily life mixes farming, fishing, ceremony and the constant threat of war.
