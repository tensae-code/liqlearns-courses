# Video script — The Lapita people and Pacific settlement

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Thousands of years ago Lapita voyagers from the Bismarck Archipelago carried a named pottery, farming and seafaring culture to New Caledonia, Fiji, Tonga and Samoa (sherds from about 1500 BC), ancestors of today's Melanesian, Micronesian and Polynesian peoples. Lapita pottery found at Lakeba, Beqa, Natunuku and Naigani shows the first Fijians shared a culture with the western Pacific; chiefs rule villages and islands, and daily life mixes farming, fishing, ceremony and the constant threat of war.
