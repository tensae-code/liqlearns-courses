# The Phoenicians — alphabet and sea trade

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Phoenicians of the Levant coast build a civilisation of ships, ports and letters rather than of one river valley. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Civilisation of Phoenicia

The sea-people of the Levant coast take their place beside river-born Egypt and Iraq — a civilisation of ships, ports and letters rather than of one river valley.
