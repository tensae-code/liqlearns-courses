# Video script — India — the Gupta empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One conqueror, four treatments — Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours like Assam, Nepal and the gana sanghas bound by tribute and court attendance, and Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters; prashasti genealogies show the family climbing from maha-raja to maharaj-adhiraja, and Chandragupta II finishes the Shakas in the west.
