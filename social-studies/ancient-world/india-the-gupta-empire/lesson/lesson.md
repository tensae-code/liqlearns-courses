# India — the Gupta empire

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Samudragupta treated the kings he met four ways — annexed, defeated and restored, bound by tribute, or received in submission — and Chandragupta II finished the Shakas in the west. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Samudragupta's four policies; genealogies; Chandragupta II

One conqueror, four treatments — Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours like Assam, Nepal and the gana sanghas bound by tribute and court attendance, and Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters; prashasti genealogies show the family climbing from maha-raja to maharaj-adhiraja, and Chandragupta II finishes the Shakas in the west.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
