# India — the Gupta empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Samudragupta's four policies; genealogies; Chandragupta II.
**Answer: One conqueror, four treatments — Aryavarta's nine kings uprooted and annexed, Dakshinapatha's twelve defeated and restored, neighbours like Assam, Nepal and the gana sanghas bound by tribute and court attendance, and Kushana, Shaka and Sri Lankan rulers submitting with gifts and daughters; prashasti genealogies show the family climbing from maha-raja to maharaj-adhiraja, and Chandragupta II finishes the Shakas in the west (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.10 / Samudragupta's four policies; genealogies; Chandragupta II, printed pp. 104–106.**
