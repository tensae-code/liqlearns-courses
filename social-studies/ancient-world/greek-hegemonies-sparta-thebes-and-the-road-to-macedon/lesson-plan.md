## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 27 The hegemony of Sparta** — victorious Sparta rules Greece harshly yet Agesilaus shows mercy to the conquered, and the cities soon combine against her
2. **Ch. 28 Thebes and the Boeotian League** — Thebes builds a Boeotian federation with its own coinage and rises to challenge Sparta's hold on Greece
3. **Ch. 29 The hegemony of Thebes** — Epaminondas's slanting phalanx and the Sacred Band smash Sparta at Leuctra and free Messenia, making Thebes briefly first in Greece
4. **Ch. 27 Hegemony of Sparta exercises** — names sorted into columns place victors and victims of Sparta's harsh decade
5. **Ch. 28 Thebes and the Boeotian League exercises** — exercises build Thebes's federation as Sparta's challenger
6. **Ch. 29 Hegemony of Thebes exercises** — marking tasks follow Epaminondas's Leuctra revolution in tactics
7. **Ch. F3 The hegemony of Sparta: a contested dominion** — Sparta's harmosts and garrisons make her empire hated within a decade of winning it
8. **Ch. F4 The dominion of Thebes in Greece** — Pelopidas and Epaminondas free Thebes, shatter Sparta at Leuctra and Mantinea, and die leaving no successor system
