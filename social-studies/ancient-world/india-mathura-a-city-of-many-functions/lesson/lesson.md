# India — Mathura, a city of many functions

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Mathura, at the crossroads of two great routes, was a walled city of superb sculpture, the Kushanas' second capital, and a home to Buddhist monasteries, Jaina shrines and Krishna worship at once. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cities with many functions: Mathura

At the crossroads of the north-west–east and north–south routes, walled Mathura feeds itself from its hinterland, carves superb sculpture, becomes the Kushanas' second capital, and hosts Buddhist monasteries, Jaina shrines and Krishna worship; its gift-inscriptions name the city's goldsmiths, weavers, basket makers and perfumers.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
