# China Qin Unification And The First Emperor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Qin builds the unified state.
**Answer: Li Si's memorial stops the expulsion of foreign advisers, Ying Zheng conquers the six states by 221 BCE, and the First Emperor standardises script, coinage, weights and axle-widths and rules through commanderies (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 3: Qin builds the unified state, printed p. 15.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Burning books, burying scholars, and Qin's fall.
**Answer: on Li Si's advice the First Emperor burns the histories of the other states and buries scholars alive; within four years of his death peasant rebels and Xiang Yu and Liu Bang tear the empire down (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
