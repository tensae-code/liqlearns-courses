# Video script — The Guaraní peoples and Ivy Marei

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Guaraní seek Ivy Marei, the Land Without Evil; their tenta communities are led by a Mburuvicha, and the Tenta Guasu by the Mburuvicha guasu, under the law of freedom (yeyora).

Teacher-compendium unit on the Guaraní, same arc as the student text.
