# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan Usun And Kangly

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan Usun And Kangly
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Usuns build a state in the Ili valley and grow rich between China and the steppe
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§36–37 History of the Usuns and their economy** — the Usuns build a state in the Ili valley and grow rich between China and the steppe
2. **§38 Material culture of the Usuns** — Usun pottery, tools and dwellings show a settled-herder civilisation
3. **§39–40 The Kangly** — the Kangly hold the Syr Darya and its cities at the steppe's southern gate
4. **4.1 Written sources about the Usuns** — Chinese envoys describe the Usun kunbi's court and herds for the Han emperor
5. **4.2 The Kangly's urban culture** — Kangly towns on the Syr Darya mix farming, crafts and steppe trade
6. **4.3 The Kangly's social structure** — Kangly chiefs, townsfolk and herders hold a state together at the steppe's gate
7. **4.4 The Usuns and the Kangly (chapter review)** — two neighbouring peoples are compared as states between China and the steppe
8. **§43 Who were the ancient Usuns?** — the Usuns appear in the Ili valley as a great herding state
9. **§44 Chinese sources about the Usuns** — Han envoys count Usun herds, towns and kings for their emperor
10. **§45 Who were the ancient Kangly?** — the Kangly hold the middle Syr Darya between steppe and cities
11. **§46 Urban culture of the ancient Kangly** — Kangly towns raise walls, crafts and markets on the river
12. **§47 Kangly social structure and political life** — a khan, tribal chiefs and townsfolk run the Kangly state
13. **§48 The accounts of Sima Qian and Zhang Qian** — China's great historian and its explorer-envoy open the steppe to written record
14. **§49 Culture of the Usuns and Kangly** — pottery, jewellery and dwellings show two settled-herder cultures
15. **§50 Archaeological monuments of the Usuns and Kangly** — excavated towns and graves anchor both peoples in Kazakhstan's soil
16. **4.1 Written sources about the Usuns (lesson plans)** — Chinese envoy reports are mined for Usun life
17. **4.2 The Kangly's urban culture develops (lesson plans)** — Kangly towns show farming and craft beside the steppe
18. **4.3 The Kangly's social structure (lesson plans)** — the Kangly state is diagrammed from khan to townsfolk
19. **4.4 Material and spiritual culture of Usuns and Kangly (lesson plans)** — objects and beliefs of two peoples are compared in one lesson arc
