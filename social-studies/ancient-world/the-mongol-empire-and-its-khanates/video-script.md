The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mongolia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Ögödei builds Karakorum's walls, extends the yam and the law, and sends the armies that take Rus' and Hungary. regencies and succession struggles end with Möngke, whose census, religious tolerance and world-embracing court make the empire a single system. Khubilai moves the capital to Khanbaliq (Beijing), takes the Chinese dynastic name Yuan in 1271 and completes the conquest of the Song. Batu's ulus on the Volga rules the Russian principalities for two centuries — the "Mongol yoke" of Russian history. Chinggis's second son's ulus holds Central Asia's oases and steppe, astride the Silk Road. Hülegü takes Baghdad in 1258 and founds the Ilkhanate; his heirs adopt Islam and Persian culture. Ögödei, Güyük, Möngke and Khubilai extend and administer the largest land empire ever — and its succession quarrels begin. one empire becomes four khanates, each going its own way — Yuan in China, the Horde on the Volga, Chagatai in Central Asia, the Ilkhans in Persia.
