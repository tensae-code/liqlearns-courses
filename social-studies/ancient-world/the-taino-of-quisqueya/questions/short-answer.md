# The Taïno of Quisqueya — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: A territory and an archipelago society.
**Answer: An island people spread across an archipelago — villages, ball courts and sea paths link quisqueya's communities long before any european sail appears (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The Taïno and the caciquat.
**Answer: Chiefs called caciques rule the villages — but pupils test whether the 'caciquat' they read about is taïno reality or a shape imposed by spanish writers (any close wording).**
