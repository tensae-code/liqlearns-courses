# The Taïno of Quisqueya — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An island people spread across an archipelago — villages, ball courts and sea paths link quisqueya's communities long before any european sail appears
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 2, Un territoire et une société « archipéliques »).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Chiefs called caciques rule the villages — but pupils test whether the 'caciquat' they read about is taïno reality or a shape imposed by spanish writers
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 3, Les Taïno et le caciquat, des constructions ethno-historiques ?).
