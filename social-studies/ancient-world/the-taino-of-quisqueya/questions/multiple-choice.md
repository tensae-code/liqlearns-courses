# The Taïno of Quisqueya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) A territory and an archipelago society  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) A territory and an archipelago society** — an island people spread across an archipelago — villages, ball courts and sea paths link Quisqueya's communities long before any European sail appears.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide this part is —
a) Séance 2, Un territoire et une société « archipéliques »  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Séance 2, Un territoire et une société « archipéliques »** — the guide's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Taïno and the caciquat  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Taïno and the caciquat** — chiefs called caciques rule the villages — but pupils test whether the 'caciquat' they read about is Taïno reality or a shape imposed by Spanish writers.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide this part is —
a) Séance 3, Les Taïno et le caciquat, des constructions ethno-historiques ?  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Séance 3, Les Taïno et le caciquat, des constructions ethno-historiques ?** — the guide's own heading for this part.
