# Romanesque art — churches of stone — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Thick walls, round arches and carved portals preach the Bible to people who cannot read.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Around the year 1000 Europe covers itself in stone churches — thick walls, round arches and carved portals preaching the Bible to people who cannot read, built by pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries from France to Poland.
