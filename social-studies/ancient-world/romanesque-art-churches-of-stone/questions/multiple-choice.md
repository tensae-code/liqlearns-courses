# Romanesque art — churches of stone — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Thick walls and carved portals  b) Europe covers itself in stone  c) Pilgrims' roads and monasteries
**Answer: b) Europe covers itself in stone** — Around the year 1000 Europe covers itself in stone churches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Thick walls and carved portals  b) Europe covers itself in stone  c) Pilgrims' roads and monasteries
**Answer: a) Thick walls and carved portals** — Thick walls, round arches and carved portals preach the Bible to people who cannot read.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Europe covers itself in stone  b) Thick walls and carved portals  c) Pilgrims' roads and monasteries
**Answer: c) Pilgrims' roads and monasteries** — The churches are built by pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries, from France to Poland.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Around the year 1000 Europe covers itself in stone churches — thick walls, round arches and carved portals preaching the Bible to people who cannot read, built by pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries from France to Poland  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
