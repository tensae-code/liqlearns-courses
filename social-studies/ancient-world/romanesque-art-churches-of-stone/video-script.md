# Video script — Romanesque art — churches of stone

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Around the year 1000 Europe covers itself in stone churches.

Thick walls, round arches and carved portals preach the Bible to people who cannot read.

The churches are built by pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries, from France to Poland.

That is the story: around the year 1000 Europe covers itself in stone churches — thick walls, round arches and carved portals preaching the Bible to people who cannot read, built by pilgrims' roads and rich monasteries from France to Poland.
