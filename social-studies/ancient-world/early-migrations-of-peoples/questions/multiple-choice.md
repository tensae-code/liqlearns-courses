# Early migrations of peoples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's three pushes behind early migration are —
a) Climate, hunger and pressure  b) Kings, coins and roads  c) Ships, sails and storms
**Answer: a) Climate, hunger and pressure** — the three pushes named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A migrating people carries with it —
a) Nothing — it starts empty  b) Tools, seeds, animals and its language  c) Only weapons
**Answer: b) Tools, seeds, animals and its language** — what travels with the people.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Migration spreads skills because —
a) Books are printed in every land  b) Travellers forget their crafts  c) Peoples bring their tools and seeds where they settle
**Answer: c) Peoples bring their tools and seeds where they settle** — the engine the book describes.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Pressure, as a push, means —
a) Stronger neighbours pressing at the border  b) Heavy carts  c) Mountain weather only
**Answer: a) Stronger neighbours pressing at the border** — one of the three pushes.
