# Early migrations of peoples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's telling, migration mixes peoples and spreads languages.
**Answer: True** — languages travel with the people who speak them.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Early peoples moved only for adventure.
**Answer: False** — the book's pushes are climate, hunger and pressure — needs, not adventures.
