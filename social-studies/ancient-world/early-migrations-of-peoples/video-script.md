# Video script — Early migrations of peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Why did the first peoples move? Turkey's Grade 9 book gives three pushes: climate turning against a homeland, hunger, and pressure from stronger neighbours.

But a migrating people never moves empty-handed. It carries its tools, its seeds, its animals and its language — and where it settles, all of them arrive.

That is why the book calls migration the engine of the ancient world: it carried skills and tongues across continents, long before any ship or road.
