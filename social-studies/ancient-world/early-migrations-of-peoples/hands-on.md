# Hands-on — Early migrations of peoples

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pack the cart

Draw a migrant family's cart and load it: tools, seed sacks, animals walking beside, and a speech bubble for the language they carry. Label each load with what it will do in the new land.

## Three pushes

Write the three pushes — climate, hunger, pressure — and beside each, one sentence imagining it happening to a real village: the rains fail, the stores empty, a stronger people arrives.

## What arrived with people

Choose one thing your own town has that came from far away — a food, a word, a craft. Write how it most likely travelled: in somebody's bundle, on somebody's cart.
