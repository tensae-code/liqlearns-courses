# Heresies Inquisition And The Mendicant Orders — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§4–5 Heresies and the Inquisition; The mendicant orders: Dominicans and Franciscans.
**Answer: Waldensians and Cathars preach poverty against a rich Church and meet the Inquisition; Francis and Dominic answer the same hunger from inside, with orders of begging friars (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§4–5 Heresies and the Inquisition; The mendicant orders: Dominicans and Franciscans, printed pp. 82, 88.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Gli ordini mendicanti (with the heresy and inquisitor dossiers).
**Answer: Francis' rule (DOC) and Dominic's preachers answer the cities' new poverty and heresy; the Church replies with both embrace and inquisition (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), Gli ordini mendicanti (with the heresy and inquisitor dossiers), printed p. 90.**
