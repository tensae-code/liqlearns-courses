# Heresies Inquisition And The Mendicant Orders — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Waldensians and Cathars preach poverty against a rich Church and meet the Inquisition; Francis and Dominic answer the same hunger from inside, with orders of begging friars.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§4–5 Heresies and the Inquisition; The mendicant orders: Dominicans and Franciscans

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 1, Cap. 3 §§4–5 Heresies and the Inquisition; The mendicant orders: Dominicans and Franciscans

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Francis' rule (DOC) and Dominic's preachers answer the cities' new poverty and heresy; the Church replies with both embrace and inquisition.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Gli ordini mendicanti (with the heresy and inquisitor dossiers)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Gli ordini mendicanti (with the heresy and inquisitor dossiers)
