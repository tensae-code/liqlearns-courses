# Rus-Ukraine — a state is born — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The chronicle dates Rus to —
a) The ninth century  b) The fifth century  c) The thirteenth century
**Answer: a) The ninth century**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before telling how the state formed, the book weighs —
a) What the word "Rus" meant  b) How Kyiv was burned  c) Who wrote the Ruska Pravda
**Answer: a) What the word "Rus" meant**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The new state was formed by uniting —
a) The East Slavic tribes under one prince  b) The Greek poleis under one king  c) The steppe peoples under one khan
**Answer: a) The East Slavic tribes under one prince**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The head of the new state was —
a) Kyiv  b) Chernihiv  c) Novgorod
**Answer: a) Kyiv**
