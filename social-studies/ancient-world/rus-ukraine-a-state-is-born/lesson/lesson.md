# Rus-Ukraine — a state is born

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the chronicle dates Rus to the ninth century; the book weighs what the word "Rus" meant, how the East Slavic tribes were united under one prince, and how a loose tribal world becomes a state with Kyiv at its head. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — What the word Rus meant

The chronicle dates Rus to the ninth century. The book weighs what the word "Rus" meant before it tells how the state formed — a name first, then a state.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tribes united under one prince

The East Slavic tribes are united under one prince. A loose tribal world becomes a state, with Kyiv at its head — Rus-Ukraine is born.
