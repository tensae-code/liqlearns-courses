# Himyar, Qataban and Awsan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Awsan in Wadi Markha and Qataban in Wadi Bayhan give way to Himyar at Zafar, which unites all Yemen under Shammar Yuhar'ish, king of Saba and Dhu Raydan and Hadramawt and Yamnat, down to Abu Karib As'ad and the last king, Sayf bin Dhi Yazan.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Himyar, Qataban and Awsan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Himyar, Qataban and Awsan

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** in the second Sabaean age, the Himyarite kings of Saba and Dhu Raydan unite Yemen from their capital Zafar — Dhamar Ali Watar annexes Raydan, Shammar Yuhar'ish adds Hadramawt and most of Yemen, Abu Karib As'ad al-Kamil stretches rule the length and breadth of Arabia — until the last king, Yusuf As'ar Yath'ar, called Dhu Nuwas, turns to Judaism, massacres the people of Najran, and brings the Abyssinians down on Yemen with Byzantine ships.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (the Himyarite age)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (the Himyarite age)
