**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (the Himyarite age).
**Answer: in the second Sabaean age, the Himyarite kings of Saba and Dhu Raydan unite Yemen from their capital Zafar — Dhamar Ali Watar annexes Raydan, Shammar Yuhar'ish adds Hadramawt and most of Yemen, Abu Karib As'ad al-Kamil stretches rule the length and breadth of Arabia — until the last king, Yusuf As'ar Yath'ar, called Dhu Nuwas, turns to Judaism, massacres the people of Najran, and brings the Abyssinians down on Yemen with Byzantine ships (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
