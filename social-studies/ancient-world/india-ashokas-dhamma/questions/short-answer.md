# India — Ashoka's dhamma — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: What was Ashoka's dhamma?; Ashoka's messages to his subjects.
**Answer: Dhamma needs no god or sacrifice — gentleness to servants, respect for elders, compassion to creatures, and respect for other religions ("praising your own while criticising another's harms your own"); dhamma mahamattas tour teaching it, messengers carry it to Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka, and the emperor plants roads, wells, rest houses and medical care for people and animals (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.7 / What was Ashoka's dhamma?; Ashoka's messages to his subjects, printed pp. 72–73.**
