# India — Ashoka's dhamma

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ashoka's dhamma needed no god or sacrifice: gentleness, respect for elders, compassion to creatures and respect for other religions, carried by his officers and messengers as far as Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — What was Ashoka's dhamma?; Ashoka's messages to his subjects

Dhamma needs no god or sacrifice — gentleness to servants, respect for elders, compassion to creatures, and respect for other religions ("praising your own while criticising another's harms your own"); dhamma mahamattas tour teaching it, messengers carry it to Syria, Egypt, Greece and Sri Lanka, and the emperor plants roads, wells, rest houses and medical care for people and animals.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
