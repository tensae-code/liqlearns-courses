# Armenia After Ani Seljuks And Byzantium — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §1.1 The region after the fall of Ani's Bagratuni kingdom.
**Answer: Byzantium annexes Armenia, disbands its armies and exiles its catholicos — and the Seljuks walk through the opened gate (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *«Հայոց պատմություն 8. Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար» (History of Armenia 8, textbook for general schools) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, Ala A. Kharatyan, Zaruhi A. Hakobyan* (Armenia), §1.1 The region after the fall of Ani's Bagratuni kingdom, printed p. 10.**
