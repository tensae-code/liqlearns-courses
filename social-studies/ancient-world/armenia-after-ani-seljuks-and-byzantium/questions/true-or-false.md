# Armenia After Ani Seljuks And Byzantium — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Byzantium annexes Armenia, disbands its armies and exiles its catholicos — and the Seljuks walk through the opened gate.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §1.1 The region after the fall of Ani's Bagratuni kingdom

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 8. Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար» (History of Armenia 8, textbook for general schools) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, Ala A. Kharatyan, Zaruhi A. Hakobyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §1.1 The region after the fall of Ani's Bagratuni kingdom
