# Sri Lanka — The Jaffna kingdom

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
