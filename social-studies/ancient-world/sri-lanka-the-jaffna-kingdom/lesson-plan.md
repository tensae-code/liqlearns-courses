## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Jaffna kingdom of the Arya Chakravartis** — a northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619
2. **The Jaffna kingdom** — the Aryachakravarti line rules the north for centuries, twelve kings keep a Tamil court with its own coinage, temples and laws while the south fragments
3. **The Jaffna regional state** — the north stands as its own Aryachakravarti state through these centuries, treated here as a regional power beside the southern kingdoms
