# Sri Lanka — The Jaffna kingdom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A northern Tamil kingdom, listed from Vijayakulanga to Sangili, taxes the south at times, grows strong on South Indian and Vijayanagara ties, and lasts until the Portuguese take it in 1619.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Jaffna kingdom of the Arya Chakravartis

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History Tamil 2011* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Jaffna kingdom of the Arya Chakravartis

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Aryachakravarti line rules the north for centuries, twelve kings keep a Tamil court with its own coinage, temples and laws while the south fragments.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Jaffna kingdom

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G10 History Tamil 2013* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Jaffna kingdom

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The north stands as its own Aryachakravarti state through these centuries, treated here as a regional power beside the southern kingdoms.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Jaffna regional state

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *G12-13 History of Sri Lanka Part II Tamil 2014* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Jaffna regional state
