## Sub lesson 1 — Maize and Mesoamerica — one food, one shared world

Guatemala's Grade 4 book teaches Mesoamerica through its food first. A tiny wild maize cob becomes a staple once lime-cooking — nixtamal — unlocks its food value. Around that food, the peoples from Mexico to Costa Rica share one culture: pyramids, the ball game, calendars, glyph writing and jade. Mesoamerica is named here not by one kingdom, but by what its peoples held in common.

In your mind's eye: a small wild maize cob beside a tall cultivated cob, a lime-cooking pot, and behind them a pyramid, a ball court and carved glyphs. Name maize and two shared things.
