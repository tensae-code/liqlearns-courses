# Mesoamerica — maize and the shared culture — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mesoamerica's peoples shared calendars and glyph writing.
**Answer: True** — part of the shared culture.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Nixtamal is a way of building pyramids.
**Answer: False** — it is lime-cooking of maize.
