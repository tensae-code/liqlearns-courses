# Mesoamerica — maize and the shared culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nixtamal is —
a) lime-cooking that unlocks maize's food value  b) a kind of pyramid  c) a Maya king
**Answer: a) lime-cooking that unlocks maize's food value** — the cooking the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mesoamerica in the book runs from —
a) Mexico to Costa Rica  b) Canada to Chile  c) Spain to Peru
**Answer: a) Mexico to Costa Rica** — the region the book defines.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these did Mesoamerican peoples share?
a) Pyramids, ball game, calendars, glyphs and jade  b) Castles, knights and cathedrals  c) Camels and desert trade
**Answer: a) Pyramids, ball game, calendars, glyphs and jade** — the shared culture list.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Maize began as —
a) a tiny wild cob  b) a tall modern cob  c) a wheat plant
**Answer: a) a tiny wild cob** — the wild ancestor the book starts from.
