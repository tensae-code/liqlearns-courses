# Video script — The Canaanites — cities and achievements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Palestine's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Arab tribes migrate from the Peninsula and settle al-Sham as Canaanites, excelling in farming, building and trade.

The Canaanites build cities from Ras Shamra (Ugarit) to Gaza and Rafah — Urusalim (Jerusalem, "god of peace"), Shechem (Nablus), Jaffa, Hebron, Megiddo — mint shekel coinage, trade with Babylon, Egypt and Yemen, and endure successive Pharaonic, Assyrian, Chaldean and Persian occupations because their land commands the routes.
