# The Canaanites — cities and achievements

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Arab tribes migrate from the Peninsula and settle al-Sham as Canaanites, excelling in farming, building and trade As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2 Lesson 2: civilisations of Bilad al-Sham — the Canaanites

Arab tribes migrate from the Peninsula and settle al-Sham as Canaanites, excelling in farming, building and trade (Unit 2 Lesson 2: civilisations of Bilad al-Sham — the Canaanites)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2 Lesson 3: Palestine (Land of Canaan)

The Canaanites build cities from Ras Shamra (Ugarit) to Gaza and Rafah — Urusalim (Jerusalem, "god of peace"), Shechem (Nablus), Jaffa, Hebron, Megiddo — mint shekel coinage, trade with Babylon, Egypt and Yemen, and endure successive Pharaonic, Assyrian, Chaldean and Persian occupations because their land commands the routes (Unit 2 Lesson 3: Palestine (Land of Canaan))

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
