# Islamic architecture in Syria — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arab builders blend simplicity and splendour, learning from every land their state embraced and then surpassing it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4, Lesson 2: مظاهر معمارية وفنية (Architectural and artistic forms)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *كتاب التاريخ — سورية الحضارة — الصف الثامن الأساسي (History Book — Syria the Civilisation, Grade 8)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Islamic architecture in Syria — simplicity and splendour

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Castles built for war in places enemies could hardly reach — Salah al-Din's castle, freed from the Franks by the hero Salah al-Din, Aleppo's citadel, the Krak and al-Marqab — the Umayyad Mosque in Damascus built by the caliph al-Walid ibn Abd al-Malik, the church of St Simeon near Aleppo, and the palaces: al-Azem in Damascus, Harun al-Rashid's in Raqqa, al-Hayr East and West near Palmyra.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2, Lesson مواقع تاريخية (Historic sites)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الرابع (Social Studies, Grade 4)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Castles, the Umayyad Mosque and the palaces
