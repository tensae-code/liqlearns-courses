**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Islamic architecture in Syria — simplicity and splendour.
**Answer: Arab builders blend simplicity and splendour, learning from every land their state embraced and then surpassing it. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Castles, the Umayyad Mosque and the palaces.

(any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
