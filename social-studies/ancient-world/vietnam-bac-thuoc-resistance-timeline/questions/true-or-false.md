# Vietnam Bac Thuoc Resistance Timeline — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after Triệu Đà takes Âu Lạc in 179 BC, rising follows rising — 40, 248, 542, 713, 766, 905 — until Ngô Quyền's Bạch Đằng victory of 938 ends domination and opens lasting independence.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 8, a thousand years of northern domination and its resistance timeline

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 8, a thousand years of northern domination and its resistance timeline

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** empire after empire must re-conquer the same small country, until the river battle of 938 closes the account.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 8 A thousand years of uprisings under northern rule: the Trưng sisters (40–43), Bà Triệu (248), Lý Bí founding Vạn Xuân (542–544) with Triệu Quang Phục's guerrilla victory, Phùng Hưng at Đường Lâm (late 700s), until Khúc Thừa Dụ and Ngô Quyền's 938 Bạch Đằng end the domination
