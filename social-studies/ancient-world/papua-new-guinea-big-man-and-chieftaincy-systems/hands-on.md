# Hands-on — Papua New Guinea — Big Men and chieftaincy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Papua New Guinea — Big Men and chieftaincy — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Culture of dominant and minority groups, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
