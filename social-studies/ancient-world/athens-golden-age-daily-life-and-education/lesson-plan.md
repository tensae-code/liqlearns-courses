## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 22 The daily life and education of the Athenians** — Athenian children learn letters, music and wrestling, women run the household, and games fill the courtyards of the golden-age city
2. **Ch. 22 Daily life and education exercises** — vase scenes are mined for how Athenian children were schooled and played
3. **Ch. E4 The composition of society — daily life** — citizens, metics, slaves and women live four different Athens inside one city
4. **Ch. E5 The process of education — the Athenian and work — Athens celebrates** — school, palaestra and festivals shape the Athenian from boyhood to the Panathenaea
