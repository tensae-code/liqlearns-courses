# Bengal — a river land and its old names — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bengal as a region — a river land with old names
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Name the natural ring around Bengal: the Himalayas, the hills and the Bay of Bengal. List Bengal's old names: Banga, Pundra, Gauda, Radha, Samatata and Harikela. Explain why state borders and the land's natural borders did not match.

## Sub lesson 2 — Landforms shape settlement — living with the rivers
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Name the four great rivers people settled beside: Ganges, Padma, Brahmaputra and Meghna. Say why the flood-fed soil drew settlers. Name the price of the rivers' gifts: flood, storm and tiger.

## Sub lesson 3 — The peoples who made Bengal's culture
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Name, in order, the language peoples who entered Bengal: Austric, Dravidian, then Arya. Explain how their mixing founded Bengali society, language and custom.
