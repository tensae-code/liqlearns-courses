# Bengal — a river land and its old names — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bengal's natural ring is made of —
a) The Himalayas, hills and the Bay of Bengal  b) Deserts and steppes  c) One great wall
**Answer: a) The Himalayas, hills and the Bay of Bengal** — the ring the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Banga, Pundra, Gauda, Radha, Samatata and Harikela are —
a) Kings of Bengal  b) Old names of the Bengal region  c) River names
**Answer: b) Old names of the Bengal region** — the land's names across time.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** People settled beside the Ganges, Padma, Brahmaputra and Meghna because —
a) The flood-fed soil grew rich crops  b) There were no animals there  c) The land was dry
**Answer: a) The flood-fed soil grew rich crops** — the rivers' gift.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The peoples who entered Bengal in turn were —
a) Arya, then Austric, then Dravidian  b) Austric, Dravidian, then Arya  c) Only the Arya
**Answer: b) Austric, Dravidian, then Arya** — the order the Grade 8 book gives.
