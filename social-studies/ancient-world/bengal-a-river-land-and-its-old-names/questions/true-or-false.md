# Bengal — a river land and its old names — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's telling, the borders of Bengal's states always matched the land's natural borders.
**Answer: False** — the shifting state borders never matched the natural ones.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bengal's first settlements were built beside its great rivers.
**Answer: True** — people settled beside the Ganges, Padma, Brahmaputra and Meghna.
