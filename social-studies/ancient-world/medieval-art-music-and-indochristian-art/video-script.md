# Video script — Medieval art, music and Indo-Christian art

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Gregorian chant, Islamic art without images, and the Indo-Christian art Indigenous artists create in the 1500s carry the medieval world's faith in sound and picture.

Gregorian chant such as Puer natus est survives as an early carol, Islamic art decorates without images, and Indigenous artists of the 1500s create what is named Indo-Christian art.
