# India — names of the land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Names of the land  b) Storing grain and "storing" animals; finding out about the first farmers  c) In search of raw materials
**Answer: a) Names of the land** — this part tells names of the land

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Iranians and Greeks meet the river Sindhu, call it Hindos/Indos, and its eastern land becomes "India"  b) Surplus grain forces invention — clay pots, woven baskets, storage pits  c) Dholavira splits into three walled parts and displays giant letters of the script in white stone
**Answer: a)** — Iranians and Greeks meet the river Sindhu, call it Hindos/Indos, and its eastern land becomes "India"
