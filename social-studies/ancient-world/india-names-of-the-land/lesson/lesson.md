# India — names of the land

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The land has two old names: India, from the river Sindhu as Iranians and Greeks heard it, and Bharat, from the Bharata people named in the Rigveda. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Names of the land

Iranians and Greeks meet the river Sindhu, call it Hindos/Indos, and its eastern land becomes "India"; the Bharata people named in the Rigveda give the land its other name, Bharat.
