# China Liao Western Xia And Song Coexistence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Khitan nomads build Liao, the Tangut Yuanhao proclaims Western Xia in 1038 with its own script, coinage and law, and three states share the map with Song.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Liao, Western Xia and Northern Song side by side

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Liao, Western Xia and Northern Song side by side

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Khitan, Tangut and Jurchen build full Chinese-style states beside Song, each with its own script and law.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (1)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (1)
