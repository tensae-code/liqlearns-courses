# China Liao Western Xia And Song Coexistence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Liao, Western Xia and Northern Song side by side.
**Answer: Khitan nomads build Liao, the Tangut Yuanhao proclaims Western Xia in 1038 with its own script, coinage and law, and three states share the map with Song (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 7: Liao, Western Xia and Northern Song side by side, printed p. 33.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (1).
**Answer: Khitan, Tangut and Jurchen build full Chinese-style states beside Song, each with its own script and law (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 10: Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (1), printed p. 58.**
