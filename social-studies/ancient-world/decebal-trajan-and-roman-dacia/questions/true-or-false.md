# Decebal Trajan And Roman Dacia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Decebal hides Dacia's treasure by diverting a river, Trajan conquers Dacia anyway, and from Romans and Dacians together the Romanian people and language are born.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 9: De la romani la români

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 9: De la romani la români

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Decebal reunifies and reforms Dacia, fights Trajan in 101–102, breaks the peace in Rome's telling, and falls in 105–106 when Rome comes for him and his capital.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unitatea VI, Lecția 2: Dacia pe timpul lui Decebal. Războaiele daco-romane

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală. Manual pentru clasa a V-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unitatea VI, Lecția 2: Dacia pe timpul lui Decebal. Războaiele daco-romane
