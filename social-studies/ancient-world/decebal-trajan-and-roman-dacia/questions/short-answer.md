# Decebal Trajan And Roman Dacia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 9: De la romani la români.
**Answer: Decebal hides Dacia's treasure by diverting a river, Trajan conquers Dacia anyway, and from Romans and Dacians together the Romanian people and language are born (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* (Moldova), Tema 9: De la romani la români, printed p. 26.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea VI, Lecția 2: Dacia pe timpul lui Decebal. Războaiele daco-romane.
**Answer: Decebal reunifies and reforms Dacia, fights Trajan in 101–102, breaks the peace in Rome's telling, and falls in 105–106 when Rome comes for him and his capital (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală. Manual pentru clasa a V-a* (Moldova), Unitatea VI, Lecția 2: Dacia pe timpul lui Decebal. Războaiele daco-romane, printed p. 139.**
