# Decebal Trajan And Roman Dacia

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Decebal hides Dacia's treasure by diverting a river, Trajan conquers Dacia anyway, and from Romans and Dacians together the Romanian people and language are born As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
