# India — the Buddha

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Siddhartha Gautama left home comfort, sought teachers, and meditated under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment made him the Buddha; he taught first at Sarnath and walked and taught until his death at Kusinara. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The story of the Buddha

Siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, leaves home comfort, debates other thinkers for years, then meditates under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment makes him the Buddha; he teaches first at Sarnath and walks and teaches until his death at Kusinara.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
