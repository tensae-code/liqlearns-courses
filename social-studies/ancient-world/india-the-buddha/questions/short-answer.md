**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The story of the Buddha.
**Answer: Siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, leaves home comfort, debates other thinkers for years, then meditates under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment makes him the Buddha; he teaches first at Sarnath and walks and teaches until his death at Kusinara (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
