# India — the Buddha — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The story of the Buddha  b) A very big kingdom = an empire  c) Coins; other means of exchange
**Answer: a) The story of the Buddha** — this part tells the story of the Buddha

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, leaves home comfort, debates other thinkers for years, then meditates under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment makes him the Buddha  b) Chandragupta Maurya founds the empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by the wise Chanakya whose ideas fill the Arthashastra  c) Potters fire mirror-black Northern Black Polished Ware, Varanasi and Madurai weave famous cloth, and craft and merchant shrenis train workers, buy raw material — and bank deposits whose interest endows monasteries
**Answer: a)** — Siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, leaves home comfort, debates other thinkers for years, then meditates under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment makes him the Buddha
