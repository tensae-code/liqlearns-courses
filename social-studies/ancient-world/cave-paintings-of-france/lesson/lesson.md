# Cave paintings of France

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In a French cave painted 20,000–10,000 years ago, four schoolchildren stumbled on horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours — perhaps painted for rituals before the hunt. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cave paintings in France

Four schoolchildren stumble on a French cave painted 20,000–10,000 years ago with horses, aurochs and bison in mineral colours — perhaps for rituals before the hunt.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Cave paintings in France. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
