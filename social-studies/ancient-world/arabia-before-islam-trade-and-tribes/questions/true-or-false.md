# Arabia before Islam — trade and tribes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Jahiliyya Arabs worship stones, trees, sun and moon beside the Kaaba's idols; the family and then the tribe rule life, women range from Queen Zenobia to daughters buried alive (wa'd), and Bedouin tent-dwellers live hard against settled townsfolk of Yemen, al-Sham, Iraq and the Hijaz.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4 Lesson 1: religious and social life of the Arabs before Islam

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف السادس الأساسي، الجزء الثاني (Social Studies, Grade 6 Basic, Part 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 4 Lesson 1: religious and social life of the Arabs before Islam

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Hereditary kingdoms (Ma'in, Saba and Himyar in Yemen, the Munadhira in Iraq, Nabataeans, Ghassanids and Palmyra in al-Sham) stand beside tribal sheikhdoms, while Persia and Rome occupy most Arab lands and the Hijaz cities — Mecca under Qusay's Quraysh, feuding Yathrib, Taif — keep their independence.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4 Lesson 2: political life before Islam — kingdoms and empires

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف السادس الأساسي، الجزء الثاني (Social Studies, Grade 6 Basic, Part 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 4 Lesson 2: political life before Islam — kingdoms and empires

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Abraha's attempt on Mecca in the Year of the Elephant fails, and around 610 CE the Arab tribes unite and beat Persia at Dhi Qar after the Persians kill al-Nu'man, king of al-Hira — the first Arab victory over an empire.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4 Lesson 2: Abraha and the Battle of Dhi Qar

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف السادس الأساسي، الجزء الثاني (Social Studies, Grade 6 Basic, Part 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 4 Lesson 2: Abraha and the Battle of Dhi Qar

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Seasonal fairs like Ukaz east of Taif mix trade with poetry contests and tribal gathering before the Hajj, so the market is Arabia's parliament and newspaper in one.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Arab markets
