# Arabia before Islam — trade and tribes — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4 Lesson 1: religious and social life of the Arabs before Islam
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — in the Jahiliyya Arabs worship stones, trees, sun and moon beside the Kaaba's idols; the family and then the tribe rule life, women range from Queen Zenobia to daughters buried alive (wa'd), and Bedouin tent-dwellers live hard against settled townsfolk of Yemen, al-Sham, Iraq and the Hijaz.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 4 Lesson 2: political life before Islam — kingdoms and empires
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — hereditary kingdoms (Ma'in, Saba and Himyar in Yemen, the Munadhira in Iraq, Nabataeans, Ghassanids and Palmyra in al-Sham) stand beside tribal sheikhdoms, while Persia and Rome occupy most Arab lands and the Hijaz cities — Mecca under Qusay's Quraysh, feuding Yathrib, Taif — keep their independence.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 4 Lesson 2: Abraha and the Battle of Dhi Qar
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Abraha's attempt on Mecca in the Year of the Elephant fails, and around 610 CE the Arab tribes unite and beat Persia at Dhi Qar after the Persians kill al-Nu'man, king of al-Hira — the first Arab victory over an empire.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Arab markets
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — seasonal fairs like Ukaz east of Taif mix trade with poetry contests and tribal gathering before the Hajj, so the market is Arabia's parliament and newspaper in one.
