# Arabia before Islam — trade and tribes

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in the Jahiliyya Arabs worship stones, trees, sun and moon beside the Kaaba's idols; the family and then the tribe rule life, women range from Queen Zenobia to daughters buried alive (wa'd), and Bedouin tent-dwellers live hard against settled townsfolk of Yemen, al-Sham, Iraq and the Hijaz As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4 Lesson 1: religious and social life of the Arabs before Islam

In the Jahiliyya Arabs worship stones, trees, sun and moon beside the Kaaba's idols; the family and then the tribe rule life, women range from Queen Zenobia to daughters buried alive (wa'd), and Bedouin tent-dwellers live hard against settled townsfolk of Yemen, al-Sham, Iraq and the Hijaz (Unit 4 Lesson 1: religious and social life of the Arabs before Islam)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 4 Lesson 2: political life before Islam — kingdoms and empires

Hereditary kingdoms (Ma'in, Saba and Himyar in Yemen, the Munadhira in Iraq, Nabataeans, Ghassanids and Palmyra in al-Sham) stand beside tribal sheikhdoms, while Persia and Rome occupy most Arab lands and the Hijaz cities — Mecca under Qusay's Quraysh, feuding Yathrib, Taif — keep their independence (Unit 4 Lesson 2: political life before Islam — kingdoms and empires)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 4 Lesson 2: Abraha and the Battle of Dhi Qar

Abraha's attempt on Mecca in the Year of the Elephant fails, and around 610 CE the Arab tribes unite and beat Persia at Dhi Qar after the Persians kill al-Nu'man, king of al-Hira — the first Arab victory over an empire (Unit 4 Lesson 2: Abraha and the Battle of Dhi Qar)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Arab markets

Seasonal fairs like Ukaz east of Taif mix trade with poetry contests and tribal gathering before the Hajj, so the market is Arabia's parliament and newspaper in one (Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Arab markets)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
