# Video script — The Maya

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — City-states in the rainforest. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
In the rainforests of Mesoamerica, the Maya built a civilisation of city-states — no single emperor, but rival cities, each with its king, its temples and its pyramids rising above the trees.

Farming in forest clearings fed them; war and trade bound them together and set them against each other. Watch the names appear one by one: Maya, city-state, pyramid. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — A calendar and a full writing. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Maya kept one of the ancient world's most exact calendars, tracking sun, moon and Venus in carved stone. Watch the names appear one by one: calendar, glyphs, monument. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — What the Maya teach the comparison. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Stand the Maya beside Egypt and Mesopotamia and the same marks appear: farming surplus, cities, kings, priests, writing, calendars. Watch the names appear one by one: surplus, civilisation, Mesoamerica. This is the part of the story to hold.
