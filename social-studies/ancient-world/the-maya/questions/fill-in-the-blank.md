# The Maya — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the stepped temple-mountain at the heart of a Maya city' is ______.
**Answer: pyramid**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'a carved stone record set up by a Maya king' is ______.
**Answer: monument**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the region of Mexico and Central America where the Maya lived' is ______.
**Answer: Mesoamerica**
