# The Maya — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Maya?
a) pyramid
b) the people who built city-state civilisation in Mesoamerica's rainforest
c) city-state
**Answer: b) the people who built city-state civilisation in Mesoamerica's rainforest**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is calendar?
a) glyphs
b) the Maya system for tracking time, among the ancient world's most exact
c) monument
**Answer: b) the Maya system for tracking time, among the ancient world's most exact**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is surplus?
a) civilisation
b) food beyond need, which frees hands for building and writing
c) Mesoamerica
**Answer: b) food beyond need, which frees hands for building and writing**
