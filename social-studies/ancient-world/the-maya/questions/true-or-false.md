# The Maya — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: A city ruling itself and its surrounding land, as the Maya cities did — this is city-state.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Maya means the stepped temple-mountain at the heart of a Maya city.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The picture-signs of Maya writing — this is glyphs.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, calendar means a carved stone record set up by a Maya king.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: A society with cities, states, writing and specialised work — this is civilisation.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, surplus means the region of Mexico and Central America where the Maya lived.
**Answer: False**
