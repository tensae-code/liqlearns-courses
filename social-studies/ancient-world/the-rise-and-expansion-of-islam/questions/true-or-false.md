# The rise and expansion of Islam — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Islamic civilisation's scholarship and trade are part of the book's teaching, not only its conquests.
**Answer: True** — mathematics, medicine, astronomy, philosophy — and trade across three continents.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The four channels are listed in the Grade 11 student book itself.
**Answer: False** — the formulation is the Grade 9 teacher guide's, stocked as labelled evidence.

**3. (Sub lesson 1) ** The Islamic world's trade networks bound three continents.
**Answer: True** — whose eastern roads reached Ethiopia's sultanates.
