# Hands-on — The rise and expansion of Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The four channels map
Draw the Islamic world c.750. Mark one conquest arrow, one trade route, one pilgrimage road to Mecca and one missionary journey, each in a different colour, with a legend of the four channels.

## Beyond the armies
List three lands Islam reached mainly without conquest (from the lesson's logic — trade coasts, pilgrimage lands). Say which channels did the work in each.

## Grade 11 depth — The rise and expansion of Islam in depth

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
