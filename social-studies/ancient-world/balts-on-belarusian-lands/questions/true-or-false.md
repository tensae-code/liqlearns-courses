# The Balts on the Belarusian lands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Balts — the pre-Slavic population

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Atlas: History of Belarus, Grade 6* is about Tribal unions — Krivichi, Dregovichi and Radimichi.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Balts — the pre-Slavic population

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Baltic peoples hold the south-eastern Baltic shore while bog iron is smelted in forest furnaces
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The ancient Balts in Europe — and how iron was obtained

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Atlas: History of Belarus, Grade 6* is about East Slavic tribal unions and their neighbours.
**Answer: False** — it is about The ancient Balts in Europe — and how iron was obtained
