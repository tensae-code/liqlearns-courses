# The Balts on the Belarusian lands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The Balts — the pre-Slavic population  b) Tribal unions — Krivichi, Dregovichi and Radimichi  c) Fragmentation on the Belarusian lands
**Answer: a) The Balts — the pre-Slavic population** — this part tells the Balts — the pre-Slavic population

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day  b) Krivichi, Dregovichi and Radimichi form tribal unions, elders and princes grow rich, and hillforts rise over the rivers  c) Polotsk and Turov splinter into appanage principalities-volosts as princes settle sons on ever smaller towns
**Answer: a)** — Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The ancient Balts in Europe — and how iron was obtained  b) East Slavic tribal unions and their neighbours  c) The fragmentation of Rus
**Answer: a) The ancient Balts in Europe — and how iron was obtained** — this part tells the ancient Balts in Europe — and how iron was obtained

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Baltic peoples hold the south-eastern Baltic shore while bog iron is smelted in forest furnaces  b) the East Slavs trade and fight with Balts, Finns, Khazars and Varangians along the amber and "from the Varangians to the Greeks" routes  c) brother-princes make war on each other, Kiev's unity dissolves, and Polovtsian and crusader pressure grows on the fragments
**Answer: a)** — Baltic peoples hold the south-eastern Baltic shore while bog iron is smelted in forest furnaces
