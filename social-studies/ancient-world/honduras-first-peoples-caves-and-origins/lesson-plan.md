# Lesson plan — Honduras First Peoples Caves And Origins

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Honduras First Peoples Caves And Origins
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Honduras, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** bone tools and stone artefacts in the Cueva del Gigante (Marcala) date Honduras's origins to millennia before Christ; Yarumela and the Talgua Caves, where ancestors buried bones at the entrance to another world, complete the evidence
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lección 37 Origen de la población** — bone tools and stone artefacts in the Cueva del Gigante (Marcala) date Honduras's origins to millennia before Christ; Yarumela and the Talgua Caves, where ancestors buried bones at the entrance to another world, complete the evidence
2. **the peopling theories debated** — three rival theories for America's first peoples — Ameghino's autochthonous origin, the Polynesian Pacific crossing (judged unlikely), and the Bering Strait route — are weighed with their evidence
