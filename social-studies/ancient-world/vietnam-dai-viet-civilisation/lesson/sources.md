through the Ngô, Đinh, Tiền Lê, Lý, Trần, Hồ and Hậu Lê dynasties the centralised monarchy is built and perfected and Đại Việt becomes a strong state with influence in East and Southeast Asia, while selectively absorbing the civilisations outside — India's, China's, the West's — in thought, politics, education and culture, law and technique, with even Chăm-pa civilisation values such as tower-building technique and rice varieties gradually becoming part of Đại Việt civilisation;
