# Hands-on — Magna Carta, 1215 — the king under law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Magna Carta, 1215 — the king under law — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Magna Carta, 1215 — the king under law, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
