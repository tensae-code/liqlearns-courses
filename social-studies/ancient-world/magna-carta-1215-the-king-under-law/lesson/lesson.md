# Magna Carta, 1215 — the king under law

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In 1215 England's barons corner King John and make him sign the Great Charter — for the first time a king's power is written down as less than the law, a seed from which parliaments grow. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Magna Carta, 1215 — the king under law
