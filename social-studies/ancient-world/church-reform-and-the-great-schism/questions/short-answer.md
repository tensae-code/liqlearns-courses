# Church Reform And The Great Schism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§3–4 The reform of the Church in the 11th century; The reform of the Papacy and the Schism of the East.
**Answer: Cluniac monks and reforming popes fight simony and clerical marriage; in 1054 Rome and Constantinople split, dividing Western and Eastern Christianity for good (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§3–4 The reform of the Church in the 11th century; The reform of the Papacy and the Schism of the East, printed pp. 47, 52.**
