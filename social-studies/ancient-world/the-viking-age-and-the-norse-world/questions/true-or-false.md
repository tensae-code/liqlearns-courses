# The Viking Age and the Norse world — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Everyone who lived in the Viking Age was a Viking.
**Answer: False** — "to go a-viking" was a job, not a people.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Norse sailors crossed the ocean steering by sun, birds and memory.
**Answer: True** — navigation without maps, as the beat tells it.

**3. (Sub lesson 6)** In the turf hall, men wove and kept the household keys while women sailed.
**Answer: False** — women ran the household, wove and kept the keys while men farmed and sailed.
