# The Viking Age and the Norse world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Norse people's ships —
a) Stayed the same for centuries  b) Grew ever better, and the ships opened the world  c) Were only for fishing
**Answer: b) Grew ever better, and the ships opened the world** — the ships open the world.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** "To go a-viking" was —
a) The name of a people  b) A job, not a people  c) A kind of ship
**Answer: b) A job, not a people** — not everyone in the age was a Viking.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Norse sailors steered without maps by —
a) Sun, birds and memory  b) Compasses and printed charts  c) Following other ships only
**Answer: a) Sun, birds and memory** — steering by sun, birds and memory.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Outlawed Erik the Red colonised —
a) Greenland  b) Italy  c) Egypt
**Answer: a) Greenland** — Erik colonises Greenland.

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** At the Althing, free farmers —
a) Were ruled by a king  b) Governed themselves  c) Never met
**Answer: b) Governed themselves** — free farmers govern themselves at the Althing.

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Inside the turf hall, the keys of the household were kept by —
a) The women who ran the household  b) Visiting sailors  c) The allsherjargoði
**Answer: a) The women who ran the household** — women run the household, weave and keep the keys.
