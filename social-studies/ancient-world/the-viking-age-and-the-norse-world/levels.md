# The Viking Age and the Norse world — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Origins and the ships
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: a seafaring people builds ever better ships, and the ships open the world — the Norse people's origins and the development of their ships (Uppruni, student book pp. 8–13)

## Sub lesson 2 — What a Viking was, 800–1050
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: not everyone in the age was a Viking — "to go a-viking" was a job, not a people (Víkingaöld, student book pp. 14–15)

## Sub lesson 3 — Raids, voyages and steering without maps
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Norse sailors raid, trade and settle from Russia to America, steering by sun, birds and memory (Víkingar herjuðu víðs vegar, student book pp. 16–27)

## Sub lesson 4 — Erik the Red and the seeress
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: outlawed Erik colonises Greenland, and a seeress is feasted and questioned about the future (Eiríks saga rauða, extracts, ch. 3–4)

## Sub lesson 5 — Free farmers and the Althing
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: free farmers govern themselves at the Althing, where the allsherjargoði opens the assembly — Norse society, government and religion, and the Althing at Þingvellir (Norræn samfélög, student book pp. 28–37)

## Sub lesson 6 — Inside the turf hall
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: inside the turf hall, women run the household, weave and keep the keys while men farm and sail — homes and daily life (Heimkynni og daglegt líf, student book pp. 38–46)
