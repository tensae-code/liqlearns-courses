# Greek Myth Theseus And The Minotaur — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus, the prince of Troezen
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — young Theseus lifts the rock, takes his father Aegeus's sword and sandals, and chooses the dangerous land road to Athens over the safe sea

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 3, ch. 2 The exploits of Theseus / Theseus kills the Minotaur
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Theseus clears the road of robbers, then sails to Crete, enters the Labyrinth with Ariadne's thread and kills the Minotaur, freeing Athens from its tribute of youths

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 3, ch. 3 Theseus returns to Athens
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the returning ship forgets to change its black sail, Aegeus throws himself into the sea that takes his name, and Theseus becomes king

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 3, reading: Daedalus and Icarus
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — imprisoned Daedalus builds wings of feathers and wax, Icarus flies too near the sun despite the warning, and falls into the sea
