# Lesson plan — Greek Myth Theseus And The Minotaur

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Greek Myth Theseus And The Minotaur
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** young Theseus lifts the rock, takes his father Aegeus's sword and sandals, and chooses the dangerous land road to Athens over the safe sea
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus, the prince of Troezen** — young Theseus lifts the rock, takes his father Aegeus's sword and sandals, and chooses the dangerous land road to Athens over the safe sea
2. **Unit 3, ch. 2 The exploits of Theseus / Theseus kills the Minotaur** — Theseus clears the road of robbers, then sails to Crete, enters the Labyrinth with Ariadne's thread and kills the Minotaur, freeing Athens from its tribute of youths
3. **Unit 3, ch. 3 Theseus returns to Athens** — the returning ship forgets to change its black sail, Aegeus throws himself into the sea that takes his name, and Theseus becomes king
4. **Unit 3, reading: Daedalus and Icarus** — imprisoned Daedalus builds wings of feathers and wax, Icarus flies too near the sun despite the warning, and falls into the sea
5. **Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus of Troezen exercises** — reading Aethra's and Theseus's thoughts in the picture at the lifting of the rock
6. **Unit 3, ch. 2 Theseus kills the Minotaur exercises** — pupils draw Theseus's route through the Labyrinth — the thread made into a mapping task
7. **Unit 3, ch. 3 Return to Athens exercises** — class discussion on human effort and forgetfulness around the black sail and Aegeus's death

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
