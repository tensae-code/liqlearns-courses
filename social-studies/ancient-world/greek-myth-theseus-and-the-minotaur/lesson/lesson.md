## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus, the prince of Troezen — young Theseus lifts the rock, takes his father Aegeus's sword and sandals, and chooses the dangerous land road to Athens over the safe sea, Unit 3, ch. 2 The exploits of Theseus / Theseus kills the Minotaur — Theseus clears the road of robbers, then sails to Crete, enters the Labyrinth with Ariadne's thread and kills the Minotaur, freeing Athens from its tribute of youths, Unit 3, ch. 3 Theseus returns to Athens — the returning ship forgets to change its black sail, Aegeus throws himself into the sea that takes his name, and Theseus becomes king, Unit 3, reading: Daedalus and Icarus — imprisoned Daedalus builds wings of feathers and wax, Icarus flies too near the sun despite the warning, and falls into the sea, Unit 3, ch. 1 Theseus of Troezen exercises — reading Aethra's and Theseus's thoughts in the picture at the lifting of the rock, Unit 3, ch. 2 Theseus kills the Minotaur exercises — pupils draw Theseus's route through the Labyrinth — the thread made into a mapping task, Unit 3, ch. 3 Return to Athens exercises — class discussion on human effort and forgetfulness around the black sail and Aegeus's death.
