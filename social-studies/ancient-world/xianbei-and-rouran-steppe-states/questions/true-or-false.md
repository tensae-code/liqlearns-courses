# Xianbei and Rouran — steppe states — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after the Xiongnu fade, the Xianbei and then the Rouran khaganate hold the steppe, keeping the nomad state tradition alive.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 5.2 The Xianbei and Rouran states

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *mn-g6h Түүх VI (History VI)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 5.2 The Xianbei and Rouran states

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Xianbei, Rouran, Turkic, Uyghur and Khitan states succeed one another on the steppe, each inheriting and remaking the nomad state tradition.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2.2 The states after the Xiongnu

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *mn-g10mh Монголын түүх X (History of Mongolia X)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2.2 The states after the Xiongnu
