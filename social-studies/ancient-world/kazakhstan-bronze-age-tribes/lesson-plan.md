# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan Bronze Age Tribes

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan Bronze Age Tribes
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** bronze replaces stone and great tribal cultures spread across the Kazakh steppe
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§14–15 The Bronze Age (2nd–1st millennia BC)** — bronze replaces stone and great tribal cultures spread across the Kazakh steppe
2. **§16–17 Economy of the early-metal-age tribes** — herders and metalworkers divide the work and the steppe economy takes shape
3. **§18–19 Religious ideas of the early-metal-age tribes** — sun, fire and ancestor cults give the bronze tribes a shared unseen world
4. **1.5 Metallurgy appears in Kazakhstan** — copper and bronze smelting arrives and metal begins to replace stone
5. **1.6 Andronovo and Begazy-Dandybai cultures** — Andronovo herder-farmers and the later Begazy-Dandybai chiefs spread across the steppe
6. **§11 Periodising the Bronze Age and its cultures** — bronze cultures are sorted into early, middle and late stages across the steppe
7. **§12 Ancient miners and production centres** — ancient miners dig copper and tin and smelt bronze at steppe production centres
8. **§13 The Andronovo culture** — Andronovo herder-farmers spread their pottery, bronze and sun cult over the steppe
9. **§14 The Begazy-Dandybai culture** — late-bronze chiefs raise great stone mausoleums in central Kazakhstan
10. **1.5 Metallurgy begins in Kazakhstan (lesson plans)** — pupils follow copper and bronze from ore to tool in the lesson sequence
11. **1.6 Andronovo and Begazy-Dandybai cultures (lesson plans)** — two bronze cultures are compared through their graves and settlements
