# India — how the new kingdoms were run

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The new kingdoms ran on hereditary offices piled up in one man and on unpaid samantas who held land and owed troops — while southern assemblies, sabha, ur and nagaram, ran villages for centuries. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — How were these kingdoms administered?; a new kind of army; assemblies in the south

Offices turn hereditary and pile up in one man (Harishena is judge, minister and war-and-peace minister at once); unpaid samantas hold land grants and owe troops, growing independent whenever the king weakens; in the south, sabha, ur and nagaram assemblies run irrigation, roads and temples for centuries.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
