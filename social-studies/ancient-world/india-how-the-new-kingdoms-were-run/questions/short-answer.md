# India — how the new kingdoms were run — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: How were these kingdoms administered?; a new kind of army; assemblies in the south.
**Answer: Offices turn hereditary and pile up in one man (Harishena is judge, minister and war-and-peace minister at once); unpaid samantas hold land grants and owe troops, growing independent whenever the king weakens; in the south, sabha, ur and nagaram assemblies run irrigation, roads and temples for centuries (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.10 / How were these kingdoms administered?; a new kind of army; assemblies in the south, printed pp. 108–109.**
