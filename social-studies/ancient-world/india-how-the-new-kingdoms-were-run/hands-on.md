# Hands-on — India — how the new kingdoms were run

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — How were these kingdoms administered?; a new kind of army; assemblies in the south — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, How were these kingdoms administered?; a new kind of army; assemblies in the south, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
