# Lesson plan — India — stupas and early temples

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** India — stupas and early temples
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (India, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A stupa grows around a relic casket behind a railed walking path, and early temples centre on the garbhagriha under a shikhara tower — built with quarried stone and paid for by kings, queens and hundreds of ordinary donors.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Buildings in brick and stone (Grade 6)** — a stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path devotees walk clockwise
2. **How were stupas and temples built? (Grade 6)** — find and quarry good stone, haul it, shape pillars and panels, set them exactly — paid for by kings and queens, and decorated by gifts from devotees: an association of ivory workers pays for a whole Sanchi gateway, and hundreds of donors survive only as names carved on railings
