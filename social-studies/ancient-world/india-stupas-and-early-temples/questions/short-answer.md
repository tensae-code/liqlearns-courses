**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: How were stupas and temples built?.
**Answer: Find and quarry good stone, haul it, shape pillars and panels, set them exactly — paid for by kings and queens, and decorated by gifts from devotees: an association of ivory workers pays for a whole Sanchi gateway, and hundreds of donors survive only as names carved on railings (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
