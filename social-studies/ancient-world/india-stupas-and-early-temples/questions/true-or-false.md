# India — stupas and early temples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A stupa grows around a relic casket — earth, brick, carved stone slabs, a railed pradakshina path devotees walk clockwise.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Buildings in brick and stone

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about the invention of paper.
**Answer: False** — it is about buildings in brick and stone

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Find and quarry good stone, haul it, shape pillars and panels, set them exactly — paid for by kings and queens, and decorated by gifts from devotees: an association of ivory workers pays for a whole Sanchi gateway, and hundreds of donors survive only as names carved on railings.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in How were stupas and temples built?

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about rasheeda's question.
**Answer: False** — it is about how were stupas and temples built?
