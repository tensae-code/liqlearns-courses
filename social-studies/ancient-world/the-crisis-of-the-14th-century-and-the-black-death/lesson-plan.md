# Lesson plan — The Crisis Of The 14th Century And The Black Death

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Crisis Of The 14th Century And The Black Death
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Italy, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** harvests fail, famine and war thin the population, then in 1347 the plague Boccaccio describes kills a third of Europe and changes the price of everything, above all labour
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–3 Economy and society: the factors of the crisis; The effects of the crisis and the spread of war; The Great Plague** — harvests fail, famine and war thin the population, then in 1347 the plague Boccaccio describes kills a third of Europe and changes the price of everything, above all labour
2. **La crisi del '300 (module: causes, plague, epidemics and social control)** — famine, plague and war strike together; cities answer with health boards and quarantines — an early machinery of social control is born of the epidemic

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
