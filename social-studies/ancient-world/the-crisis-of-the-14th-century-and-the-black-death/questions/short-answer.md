# The Crisis Of The 14th Century And The Black Death — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–3 Economy and society: the factors of the crisis; The effects of the crisis and the spread of war; The Great Plague.
**Answer: harvests fail, famine and war thin the population, then in 1347 the plague Boccaccio describes kills a third of Europe and changes the price of everything, above all labour (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–3 Economy and society: the factors of the crisis; The effects of the crisis and the spread of war; The Great Plague, printed pp. 212, 215, 219.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: La crisi del '300 (module: causes, plague, epidemics and social control).
**Answer: famine, plague and war strike together; cities answer with health boards and quarantines — an early machinery of social control is born of the epidemic (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), La crisi del '300 (module: causes, plague, epidemics and social control), printed pp. 213, 234.**
