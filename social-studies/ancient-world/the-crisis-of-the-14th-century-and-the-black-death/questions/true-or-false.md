# The Crisis Of The 14th Century And The Black Death — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** harvests fail, famine and war thin the population, then in 1347 the plague Boccaccio describes kills a third of Europe and changes the price of everything, above all labour.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–3 Economy and society: the factors of the crisis; The effects of the crisis and the spread of war; The Great Plague

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§1–3 Economy and society: the factors of the crisis; The effects of the crisis and the spread of war; The Great Plague

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** famine, plague and war strike together; cities answer with health boards and quarantines — an early machinery of social control is born of the epidemic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in La crisi del '300 (module: causes, plague, epidemics and social control)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about La crisi del '300 (module: causes, plague, epidemics and social control)
