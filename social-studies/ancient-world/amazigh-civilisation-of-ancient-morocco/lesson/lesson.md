# Amazigh civilisation of ancient Morocco

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Morocco's long story as one line: first Moroccans, Amazigh, then dynasty after dynasty. This lesson is one chapter of that line, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Amazigh system of rule

The guide reads the Amazigh system of rule from a text and a diagram: before any foreigner landed, the Amazigh governed themselves in their own order.

## Sub lesson 2 — Daily life and the Amazigh script

Daily life follows — housing, clothing and food, including Morocco's Jewish popular dishes taught as heritage — and the Amazigh script, the Amazigh's own letters.
