# Amazigh civilisation of ancient Morocco — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Amazigh civilisation began only when foreigners landed.
**Answer: False — the Amazigh governed themselves, built, dressed, ate and wrote before any foreigner landed.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Morocco's Jewish popular dishes are taught in this lesson as heritage.
**Answer: True — the guide includes them in Amazigh daily-life food as heritage.**
