# Video script — Amazigh civilisation of ancient Morocco

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Morocco's story as its Grade 5 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The guide reads the Amazigh system of rule from a text and a diagram: before any foreigner landed, the Amazigh governed themselves in their own order.

Daily life follows — housing, clothing and food, including Morocco's Jewish popular dishes taught as heritage — and the Amazigh script, the Amazigh's own letters.
