**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: first evidence and cultural areas.
**Answer: human footprints in lava deposits (~10,000 BCE), rock art at Ayasta and Oropolí, and the ancient sites of Playa de los Muertos, Los Naranjos and Yarumela show Honduras peopled long before the Maya, between Mesoamerica and the Intermediate Area (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), first evidence and cultural areas, printed p. 138.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Honduras between two areas.
**Answer: Honduras's pre-Hispanic peoples shared traits with both Mesoamerica and the Intermediate Area, living as chiefdoms and tribes on farming, hunting and fishing, each with its own language (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
