# Honduras Prehispanic Peoples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gatherers, hunters and fishers discover farming and maize; the numerous Lenca practise the Guancasco, Tolupán live in the Yoro mountains, Pech along the Aguán–Coco, Chorotega round the Gulf of Fonseca, and the Maya invent zero and watch the stars.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 21: los pueblos antes de 1502

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 21: los pueblos antes de 1502

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Human footprints in lava deposits (~10,000 BCE), rock art at Ayasta and Oropolí, and the ancient sites of Playa de los Muertos, Los Naranjos and Yarumela show Honduras peopled long before the Maya, between Mesoamerica and the Intermediate Area.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in first evidence and cultural areas

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about first evidence and cultural areas

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Honduras's pre-Hispanic peoples shared traits with both Mesoamerica and the Intermediate Area, living as chiefdoms and tribes on farming, hunting and fishing, each with its own language.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Honduras between two areas
