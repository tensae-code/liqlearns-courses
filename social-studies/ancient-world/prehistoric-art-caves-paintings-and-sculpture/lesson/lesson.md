# Prehistoric art — cave paintings and sculpture

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Painted cave walls and carved figures are the first images humans made of their world — and the first settled villagers add sculpture and megaliths, new images of the unseen world. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
