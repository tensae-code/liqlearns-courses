# Frederick Ii And The End Of Swabian Italy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 2, Cap. 6 §§2–4 The empire of Frederick II; Frederick II and the Italian Communes; The end of Swabian rule in Italy.
**Answer: the 'wonder of the world' rules a modern state from Palermo and fights popes and communes; after his death his heirs fall at Benevento and Tagliacozzo and Angevin Frenchmen take the South (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 2, Cap. 6 §§2–4 The empire of Frederick II; Frederick II and the Italian Communes; The end of Swabian rule in Italy, printed pp. 178, 183, 186.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: L'impero e il Mezzogiorno d'Italia dopo la morte di Federico II; Il Vespro siciliano (Eventi).
**Answer: Frederick's Liber Augustalis (DOC) orders the South; a generation later the Sicilian Vespers (1282) throw out the Angevins and split the island from Naples (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), L'impero e il Mezzogiorno d'Italia dopo la morte di Federico II; Il Vespro siciliano (Eventi), printed pp. 97, 98.**
