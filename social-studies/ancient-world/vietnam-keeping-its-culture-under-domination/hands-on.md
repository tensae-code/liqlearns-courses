# Hands-on — Vietnam Keeping Its Culture Under Domination

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 17, the survival of Vietnam's own culture, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
