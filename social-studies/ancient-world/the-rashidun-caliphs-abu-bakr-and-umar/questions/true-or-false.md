# The Rashidun caliphs — Abu Bakr and Umar — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Abu Bakr and Umar are studied, the lesson itself asks who the two caliphs after them were — the pair who held the community together and then stretched it across Persia and the Levant
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 7: The Rashidun caliphate — Abu Bakr and Umar

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للسنة الأولى الإعدادية (History for the First Preparatory Year)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 7: The Rashidun caliphate — Abu Bakr and Umar

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The four caliphs who ruled by consultation after the Prophet carry the young community through its first crisis
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 4: The rightly-guided caliphs

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ السنة السادسة من التعليم الابتدائي (History, Sixth Year of Primary Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 4: The rightly-guided caliphs

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The first two caliphs hold and expand the community
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 4: The Rashidun caliphate — Abu Bakr and Umar

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *كتاب التاريخ للسنة الخامسة من التعليم الثانوي (History Book for the Fifth Year of Secondary Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 4: The Rashidun caliphate — Abu Bakr and Umar
