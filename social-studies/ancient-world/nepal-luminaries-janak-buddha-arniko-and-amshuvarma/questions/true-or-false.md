# Nepal luminaries — Janak, Buddha, Arniko and Amshuvarma — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** King Janak makes Mithila flourish and his daughter Sita marries Ram; Buddha born at Lumbini preaches truth and non-violence as the Light of Asia; architect Arniko carries pagoda building to Tibet and China; Sankhadhar…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Our National Heroes — Janak, Sita, Buddha, Arniko, Sankhadhar and Amshuvarma

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *My Social Studies and Creative Arts, Grade 5* is about Social Structure and Lifestyle, Past and Present.
**Answer: False** — it is about Our National Heroes — Janak, Sita, Buddha, Arniko, Sankhadhar and Amshuvarma

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** born at Lumbini on Vaishakh Purnima in 563 BCE to Suddhodana and Mayadevi, the prince sees an old man, a sick man, a corpse and an ascetic, leaves the palace, and wins enlightenment under the Bodhi tree at Bodh Gaya;…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in National Luminary Gautam Buddha

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 7* is about Ancient Nepal — Economic and Social Condition.
**Answer: False** — it is about National Luminary Gautam Buddha

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** born in Nepal in 1245 CE, the boy sculptor Balbahu is chosen by the king for his skill; when China asks for master craftsmen, Arniko leads 80 artists, builds the great chaitya (the Miaoying Temple White Stupa) in China,…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in National Luminary Arniko

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 7* is about The Civilisation of the Kathmandu Valley.
**Answer: False** — it is about National Luminary Arniko

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** the gallery opens with King Janak of Mithila and his daughter Sita, then Gautam Buddha, Light of Asia, born at Lumbini; Amshuvarma, the Licchavi minister who balanced Tibet and India and raised the Kailashkut palace;…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Our National Luminaries (ancient and medieval figures)

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan, Class 9* is about The Contribution of the Heroes in the Nepal–British War.
**Answer: False** — it is about Our National Luminaries (ancient and medieval figures)
