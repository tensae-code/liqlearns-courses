**Answer: born at Lumbini on Vaishakh Purnima in 563 BCE to Suddhodana and Mayadevi, the prince sees an old man, a sick man, a corpse and an ascetic, leaves the palace, and wins enlightenment under the Bodhi tree at Bodh Gaya; his teaching of truth, non-violence and the middle way spreads worldwide, Lumbini becomes a World Heritage site, and UNESCO marks his birthday as International Day of Peace (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: born in Nepal in 1245 CE, the boy sculptor Balbahu is chosen by the king for his skill; when China asks for master craftsmen, Arniko leads 80 artists, builds the great chaitya (the Miaoying Temple White Stupa) in China, is made chief of the imperial craftsmen, and spreads Nepali pagoda art so widely that China calls Nepal the land of sculpture (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
