# Lesson plan — Nepal luminaries — Janak, Buddha, Arniko and Amshuvarma

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Nepal luminaries — Janak, Buddha, Arniko and Amshuvarma
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Nepal, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** King Janak makes Mithila flourish and his daughter Sita marries Ram; Buddha born at Lumbini preaches truth and non-violence as the Light of Asia; architect Arniko carries pagoda building to Tibet and China; Sankhadhar pays off the poor's debts and starts Nepal Sambat; Lichchhavi king Amshuvarma reforms society
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
