# Video script — Nepal luminaries — Janak, Buddha, Arniko and Amshuvarma

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Nepal's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

King Janak makes Mithila flourish and his daughter Sita marries Ram; Buddha born at Lumbini preaches truth and non-violence as the Light of Asia; architect Arniko carries pagoda building to Tibet and China; Sankhadhar pays off the poor's debts and starts Nepal Sambat; Lichchhavi king Amshuvarma reforms society

---

born at Lumbini on Vaishakh Purnima in 563 BCE to Suddhodana and Mayadevi, the prince sees an old man, a sick man, a corpse and an ascetic, leaves the palace, and wins enlightenment under the Bodhi tree at Bodh Gaya; his teaching of truth, non-violence and the middle way spreads worldwide, Lumbini becomes a World Heritage site, and UNESCO marks his birthday as International Day of Peace

---

born in Nepal in 1245 CE, the boy sculptor Balbahu is chosen by the king for his skill; when China asks for master craftsmen, Arniko leads 80 artists, builds the great chaitya (the Miaoying Temple White Stupa) in China, is made chief of the imperial craftsmen, and spreads Nepali pagoda art so widely that China calls Nepal the land of sculpture

---

the gallery opens with King Janak of Mithila and his daughter Sita, then Gautam Buddha, Light of Asia, born at Lumbini; Amshuvarma, the Licchavi minister who balanced Tibet and India and raised the Kailashkut palace; and Arniko of Patan, the boy-genius artist who led 80 craftsmen to Tibet and China and carried Nepali art to the world
