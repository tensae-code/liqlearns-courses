# Hands-on — Nepal luminaries — Janak, Buddha, Arniko and Amshuvarma

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Our National Heroes — Janak, Sita, Buddha, Arniko, Sankhadhar and Amshuvarma; National Luminary Gautam Buddha; National Luminary Arniko; Our National Luminaries (ancient and medieval figures) — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Our National Heroes — Janak, Sita, Buddha, Arniko, Sankhadhar and Amshuvarma, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
