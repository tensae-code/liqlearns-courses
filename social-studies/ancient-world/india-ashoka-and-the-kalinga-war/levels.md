## Sub lesson 1 — Ashoka, a unique ruler; Ashoka's war in Kalinga
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Ashoka, first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquers coastal Kalinga — then reads his own casualty figures (a lakh and a half captured, more than a lakh killed) and, horrified, renounces war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning.
