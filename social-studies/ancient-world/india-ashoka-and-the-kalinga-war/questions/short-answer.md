# India — Ashoka and the Kalinga war — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ashoka, a unique ruler; Ashoka's war in Kalinga.
**Answer: Ashoka, first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquers coastal Kalinga — then reads his own casualty figures (a lakh and a half captured, more than a lakh killed) and, horrified, renounces war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
