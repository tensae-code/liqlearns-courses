# India — Ashoka and the Kalinga war — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ashoka, first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquers coastal Kalinga — then reads his own casualty figures (a lakh and a half captured, more than a lakh killed) and, horrified, renounces war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning.
