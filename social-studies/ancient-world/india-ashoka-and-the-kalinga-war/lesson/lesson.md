# India — Ashoka and the Kalinga war

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ashoka conquered Kalinga, read his own casualty figures, and — horrified — renounced war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ashoka, a unique ruler; Ashoka's war in Kalinga

Ashoka, first king to speak to his people through inscriptions in Prakrit and Brahmi, conquers coastal Kalinga — then reads his own casualty figures (a lakh and a half captured, more than a lakh killed) and, horrified, renounces war: the only king in world history to give up conquest after winning.
