# Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abu Karib As'ad, Himyarite king of Saba, Dhu Raydan, Hadramawt and Yamnat — adding "and their Bedouin in the highlands and Tihama" to the royal title — rules about 370-440 CE from Zafar, first clothes the Kaaba in a full luxurious covering with a door and a key, unifies all Arabia under one authority, secures the trade roads by campaign, reaches across the Red Sea, and, knowing from the old books that a prophet named Muhammad is coming, believes in him before his birth — "do not curse Tubba', for he was a Muslim" — and settles the Yemeni tribes Aws and Khazraj in Yathrib so that, generations later, they will be there to receive him; he dies on campaign in 440 CE and is buried at Ghayman in Sanaa.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood
