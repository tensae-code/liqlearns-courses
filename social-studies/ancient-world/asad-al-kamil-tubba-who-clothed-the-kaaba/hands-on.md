# Hands-on — Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Al-Tubba' As'ad al-Kamil: the monotheist king and glad tidings of prophethood, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
