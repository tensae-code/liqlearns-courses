# Video script — Trade in the medieval world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The chronicles tell of wars, but Turkey's Grade 9 book tells of something quieter. Farming makes a surplus; the surplus gathers into towns; towns hold markets, and craftsmen organise into guilds that guard their skills.

Beyond the towns run the great trade routes — caravans across steppe and desert, ships between ports — carrying goods between worlds that are otherwise enemies.

Caravans and ships, the book says, stitch the medieval world together richer than its wars divide it.
