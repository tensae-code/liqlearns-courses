# Trade in the medieval world — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The medieval economy's chain begins with —
a) Guilds  b) Agriculture  c) Caravans
**Answer: b) Agriculture** — farming feeds everyone first.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A guild is —
a) A king's army  b) A tax on markets  c) Craftsmen of one trade, organised to guard skills and standards
**Answer: c) Craftsmen of one trade, organised to guard skills and standards** — the towns' craft organisations.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The great trade routes were travelled by —
a) Caravans and ships  b) Armies only  c) Pilgrims only
**Answer: a) Caravans and ships** — by land and by sea.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's beat is that trade —
a) Divided the world more than wars  b) Stitched the world together richer than its wars divided it  c) Mattered only to kings
**Answer: b) Stitched the world together richer than its wars divided it** — the reversal of the chronicles.
