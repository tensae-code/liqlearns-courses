# Trade in the medieval world — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Guilds guarded the skills and standards of their craft.
**Answer: True** — that is the guild's work as the book teaches it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Medieval trade stopped at the borders of enemy kingdoms.
**Answer: False** — caravans and ships carried goods between worlds otherwise enemies.
