# Hands-on — Trade in the medieval world

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## From field to caravan

Draw the chain as five pictures: a farm, a surplus store, a town market, a guild workshop, a departing caravan. Join them with arrows and label what moves along each arrow.

## Join the guild

Choose a craft — baker, smith, weaver. Write your guild's three rules: who may join, what standard the work must meet, and what happens to shoddy goods.

## Two worlds, one bale

Pick one good — silk, spice, wool, glass. Write its journey: where it is made, whose caravan or ship carries it, and who buys it at the end, in a land whose king may be the first land's enemy.
