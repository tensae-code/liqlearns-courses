# Lesson plan — Trade in the medieval world

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Trade in the medieval world
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 3, Topic 3.2, Economy from Agriculture to Trade: medieval farming, towns, guilds and the great trade routes.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: caravans and ships stitch the medieval world together richer than its wars divide it. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell the economy's chain in order: farming, surplus, towns and markets, guilds, long trade routes.
2. Say what a guild is: craftsmen of one trade organised to guard its skills and standards.
3. Name what travels the great routes: caravans by land and ships by sea, between worlds otherwise divided.
4. Explain the book's beat in their own words: trade stitches the world richer than wars divide it.
