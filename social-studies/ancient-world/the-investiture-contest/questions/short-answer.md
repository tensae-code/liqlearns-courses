# The Investiture Contest — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 1, Cap. 2 §5 The clash over investitures between pope and emperor.
**Answer: Gregory VII and Henry IV fight over who appoints bishops; the emperor stands barefoot in the snow at Canossa before Matilda's castle, then marches on Rome — the contest ends in compromise at Worms (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 1, Cap. 2 §5 The clash over investitures between pope and emperor, printed p. 54.**
