# The Investiture Contest — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gregory VII and Henry IV fight over who appoints bishops; the emperor stands barefoot in the snow at Canossa before Matilda's castle, then marches on Rome — the contest ends in compromise at Worms.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 1, Cap. 2 §5 The clash over investitures between pope and emperor

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 1, Cap. 2 §5 The clash over investitures between pope and emperor
