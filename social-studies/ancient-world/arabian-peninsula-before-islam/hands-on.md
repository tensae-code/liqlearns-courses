# Hands-on — The Arabian Peninsula before Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two maps of one land
Draw the peninsula twice. On the first map, draw many small tribal territories with borders everywhere and a star for no capital — because there is none. On the second, draw the caravan routes through Makkah to Syria and Yemen. Say what the two maps together teach: divided rule, joined trade.

## The winter and summer journeys
Draw two caravans: one wrapped against cold, labelled "winter journey", one under a hot sun, labelled "summer journey". Under each, write where a Makkah caravan might go and what it might carry. Say why a merchant city needs both journeys.

## Values in action
Act out, with family or classmates, a traveller arriving tired at a tent at night. One person plays the host, showing the book's three values — courage, generosity, protecting the neighbour. Afterwards, each person names which value they saw, and where.
