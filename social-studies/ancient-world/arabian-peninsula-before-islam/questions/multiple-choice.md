# The Arabian Peninsula before Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before Islam, the Arabian Peninsula was —
a) One kingdom under one king  b) A tribal land with no single state  c) Ruled from Egypt
**Answer: b) A tribal land with no single state** — the condition the book describes (Unit 7, Lesson 26).

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Tasm and Jadis were —
a) Two caravan cities  b) The lost peoples the book names  c) Two seasons
**Answer: b) The lost peoples the book names** — the vanished peoples in the lesson.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The values the book praises in the tribes are —
a) Courage, generosity and protecting the neighbour  b) Silence, saving and solitude  c) Speed, strength and secrecy
**Answer: a) Courage, generosity and protecting the neighbour** — the three values named.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Makkah grew rich as —
a) A farming valley only  b) A sea port  c) The meeting point of caravan routes
**Answer: c) The meeting point of caravan routes** — the economy the book teaches.
