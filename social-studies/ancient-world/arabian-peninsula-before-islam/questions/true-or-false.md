# The Arabian Peninsula before Islam — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Khadija bint Khuwaylid directed her caravans from Makkah to Syria and Yemen.
**Answer: True** — the merchant example the book gives.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** No goods from outside Arabia passed through the peninsula.
**Answer: False** — Silk Road goods from China passed through toward Europe.
