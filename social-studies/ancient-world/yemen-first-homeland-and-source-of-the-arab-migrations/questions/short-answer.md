**Answer: yemen is the southern gateway of the Arab civilisations, watching Bab al-Mandab and the roads to East Africa and India, and the Arabs' first homeland, where the line of Sam ibn Nuh settled; when the ice-age rains failed, dams were neglected, kingdoms quarrelled and trade slipped to the sea, Yemen's peoples poured out in waves — Akkadians to Iraq, Canaanites and Phoenicians to the Levant coast, Amorites to Babylon, Arameans to Damascus, Chaldeans back to Babylon, and later Nabataeans, Palmyrenes, Manadhira and Ghassanids — carrying Yemeni frankincense, worth then what oil is worth now, to the whole old world (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
