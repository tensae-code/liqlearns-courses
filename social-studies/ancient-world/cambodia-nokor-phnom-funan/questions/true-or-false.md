# Cambodia — Nokor Phnom (Funan), the first kingdom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Indian prince Hun Tien (Kaundinya) sails in, marries the local queen and founds a kingdom of ports and canals whose farmers and traders grow rich on gold, silver and pearls.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §2, The Nokor Phnom (Funan) era

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៤ (Social Studies, Grade 4)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 4 Lesson 2 §2, The Nokor Phnom (Funan) era

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Chinese envoys' reports (read by Paul Pelliot) describe a delta kingdom 3,000 li across (map, p. 120) whose ports grow rich on the India–China sea road.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Funan kingdom (50–550 CE)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៧ (Social Studies, Grade 7)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Funan kingdom (50–550 CE)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Chinese records measure Funan at 3,000 li (1 li = 576 m); its civilisation glitters at Oc Eo — gold jewellery, Vishnu and Buddha images (photos, p. 136).
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The ancient Funan kingdom

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី១០ (Social Studies, Grade 10)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
