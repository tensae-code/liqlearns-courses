# Video script — Cambodia — Nokor Phnom (Funan), the first kingdom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the Indian prince Hun Tien (Kaundinya) sails in, marries the local queen and founds a kingdom of ports and canals whose farmers and traders grow rich on gold, silver and pearls.

Chinese envoys' reports (read by Paul Pelliot) describe a delta kingdom 3,000 li across (map, p. 120) whose ports grow rich on the India–China sea road.

Chinese records measure Funan at 3,000 li (1 li = 576 m); its civilisation glitters at Oc Eo — gold jewellery, Vishnu and Buddha images (photos, p. 136).
