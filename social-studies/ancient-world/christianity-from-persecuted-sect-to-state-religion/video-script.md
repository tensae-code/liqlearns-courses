# Video script — Christianity — from persecuted sect to state religion

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Palestine. A small light. The teaching of Jesus, born at Bethlehem.
The light spreads — city to city. Then darkness: Nero. Persecution. The light hides, but does not go out.
312. An emperor turns. Constantine. 313 — the Edict of Milan. Toleration!
Church doors open across the empire. Under Theodosius, the faith of the persecuted becomes the religion of the state.
And from there — to Aksum, to everywhere. Watch the lights cover the map.
