# Lesson plan — Christianity — from persecuted sect to state religion

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Christianity — from persecuted sect to state religion
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 9 History, Unit 2 — Ancient World Civilizations to c. 500 AD (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked into this title in the Ethiopia Wave 4 build.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Christianity began in **1st-century Palestine**, on the teaching of **Jesus**, born at **Bethlehem in Roman Judea**.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the learner having met ancient Rome — republic to empire — in this title; the new faith begins inside that empire.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built and while the AI learns from the books; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. Images follow the picture rule (a real photograph, or real art, with its source). GIFs come first where a thing must be shown moving or popping — a GIF here is 1–2 seconds.

## If the learner is stuck
- Merges conversion and edict → conversion 312 is Constantine's own turn; the Edict of Milan, 313, is the law that followed it.
- Thinks toleration = official religion → Milan only allowed the faith; official status came later, under Theodosius.
