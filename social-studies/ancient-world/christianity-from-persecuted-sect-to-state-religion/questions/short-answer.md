# Christianity — from persecuted sect to state religion — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Rome first treat Christians as a threat?
**Answer:** Their faith refused the state's gods and emperor-cult, which Rome read as disloyalty to the empire.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the turn in order: Edict of Milan, Nero's persecution, Theodosius, Constantine's conversion.
**Answer:** Nero's persecution — Constantine's conversion (312) — Edict of Milan (313) — official religion under Theodosius.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the Edict of Milan change in practice?
**Answer:** Christians could practise openly; toleration replaced persecution and church property was restored.
