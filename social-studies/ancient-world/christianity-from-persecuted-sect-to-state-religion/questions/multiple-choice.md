# Christianity — from persecuted sect to state religion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Christianity began in —
a) Rome in AD 300  b) 1st-century Palestine  c) Axum
**Answer: b) 1st-century Palestine** — On the teaching of Jesus.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Jesus was born at —
a) Nazareth's market  b) Bethlehem in Roman Judea  c) Alexandria
**Answer: b) Bethlehem in Roman Judea** — Bethlehem, in Roman Judea.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Persecution of Christians began under —
a) Constantine  b) Theodosius  c) Nero
**Answer: c) Nero** — Nero, AD 54–68.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Constantine's conversion is dated —
a) AD 54  b) 312  c) 313 for the conversion, edict a year later
**Answer: a) AD 54** — Conversion 312; the Edict of Milan followed in 313.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Edict of Milan (313) granted —
a) toleration  b) a crusade  c) the papacy
**Answer: a) toleration** — Open practice, restored churches.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Christianity became the Empire's official religion under —
a) Nero  b) Constantine  c) Theodosius
**Answer: c) Theodosius** — Theodosius completed the arc.
