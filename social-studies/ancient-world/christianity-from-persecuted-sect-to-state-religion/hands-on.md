# Hands-on — Christianity — from persecuted sect to state religion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The arc in five cards
Make five date cards (1st century, AD 54–68, 312, 313, Theodosius). Lay them along a string in order; at each card, a learner states the change it brought, in one sentence.

## Edict reading
Draft a four-line 'Edict of Milan' in your own words — what is now allowed, what is restored. Read it aloud in a formal voice; the class checks it against the lesson.

## Grade 11 depth — Christianity's spread in depth — from Judea to state religion

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
