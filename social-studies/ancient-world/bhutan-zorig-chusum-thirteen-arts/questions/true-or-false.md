# Bhutan — Zorig Chusum, the thirteen arts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** thirteen traditional arts grow out of Buddhist and pre-Buddhist practice, carried by master craftsmen — 13th-century weaver Khandro Sonam Peldon and Thangtong Gyalpo, who forges eight iron-chain bridges from Bhutanese ore
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Three, Zorig Chusum).
