# Danylo, king of Rus

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Roman's son Danylo fights boyars, Poles, Hungarians and Mongols to rebuild his father's state, founds Kholm and Lviv, bows to Batu at Sarai to buy time — and in 1253 accepts a royal crown from the Pope's legate, becoming king of Rus. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Danylo rebuilds his father's state

Roman's son Danylo fights boyars, Poles, Hungarians and Mongols to rebuild his father's state. He founds Kholm and Lviv — new cities for a state being made again.

## Sub lesson 2 — A crown in 1253

Danylo bows to Batu at Sarai to buy time. Then, in 1253, he accepts a royal crown from the Pope's legate — Danylo becomes king of Rus.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Danylo, king of Rus in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
