# Lesson plan — Danylo, king of Rus

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Danylo, king of Rus
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Roman's son Danylo fights boyars, Poles, Hungarians and Mongols to rebuild his father's state, founds Kholm and Lviv, bows to Batu at Sarai to buy time — and in 1253 accepts a royal crown from the Pope's legate, becoming king of Rus.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Danylo rebuilds his father's state** — Roman's son Danylo fights boyars, Poles, Hungarians and Mongols to rebuild his father's state. He founds Kholm and Lviv — new cities for a state being made again.
2. **A crown in 1253** — Danylo bows to Batu at Sarai to buy time. Then, in 1253, he accepts a royal crown from the Pope's legate — Danylo becomes king of Rus.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: The Mongols invade Rus-Ukraine. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Danylo's rebuilding: fighting boyars, Poles, Hungarians and Mongols; founding Kholm and Lviv.
2. Tell the crown story: bowing to Batu at Sarai to buy time, then accepting a royal crown from the Pope's legate in 1253 as king of Rus.
