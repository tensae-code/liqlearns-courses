# Danylo, king of Rus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Danylo was the son of —
a) Roman Mstyslavych  b) Yaroslav the Wise  c) Monomakh
**Answer: a) Roman Mstyslavych**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Danylo founded —
a) Kholm and Lviv  b) Kyiv and Chernihiv  c) Olbia and Chersonesus
**Answer: a) Kholm and Lviv**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Danylo bowed to Batu at Sarai in order to —
a) Buy time  b) Become a Mongol  c) End all tribute forever
**Answer: a) Buy time**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Danylo was crowned king of Rus in —
a) 1253  b) 1199  c) 1240
**Answer: a) 1253**
