# Danylo, king of Rus — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Danylo's crown came from the Pope's legate.
**Answer: True — In 1253 he accepts a royal crown from the Pope's legate.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Danylo never dealt with the Mongols.
**Answer: False — He bows to Batu at Sarai to buy time.**
