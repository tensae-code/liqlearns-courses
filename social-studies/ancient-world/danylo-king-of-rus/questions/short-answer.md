# Danylo, king of Rus — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two peoples or powers Danylo fought to rebuild the state.
**Answer: Boyars, Poles, Hungarians and Mongols — any two (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did Danylo bow to Batu, and what happened in 1253?
**Answer: To buy time; in 1253 he accepted a royal crown and became king of Rus (any close wording).**
