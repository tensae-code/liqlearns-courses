# The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh.
**Answer: called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands — worship God alone, and let the Israelites go — Pharaoh stakes everything on a festival-day contest, Musa's staff swallows the magicians' ropes, the magicians believe on the spot and face crucifixion unmoved, plagues of drought, flood, locusts, lice, frogs and blood are promised away and the promises broken, until the night exodus ends at the sea, which splits like a great mountain for Israel and closes over Pharaoh, whose body is preserved as a sign (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh, printed p. 52.**
