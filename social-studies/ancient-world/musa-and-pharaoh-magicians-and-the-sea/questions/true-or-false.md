# The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** called at the burning bush in the sacred valley, Musa comes to Pharaoh with two demands — worship God alone, and let the Israelites go — Pharaoh stakes everything on a festival-day contest, Musa's staff swallows the magicians' ropes, the magicians believe on the spot and face crucifixion unmoved, plagues of drought, flood, locusts, lice, frogs and blood are promised away and the promises broken, until the night exodus ends at the sea, which splits like a great mountain for Israel and closes over Pharaoh, whose body is preserved as a sign.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The great confrontation between Musa and Pharaoh
