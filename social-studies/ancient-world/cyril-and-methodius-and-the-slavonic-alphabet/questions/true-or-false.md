# Cyril and Methodius and the Slavonic alphabet — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two brothers invent an alphabet for the Slavs; their disciples carry it to Bulgaria.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 22: The work of SS Cyril and Methodius and their disciples

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История и цивилизации за шести клас (History and Civilizations for the 6th Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 22: The work of SS Cyril and Methodius and their disciples

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The brothers give the Slavs letters, and Bulgaria shelters their disciples.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 9: The holy brothers Cyril and Methodius — apostles and enlighteners of the Slavic world

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История и цивилизации, 10. клас (History and Civilizations, 10th Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 9: The holy brothers Cyril and Methodius — apostles and enlighteners of the Slavic world
