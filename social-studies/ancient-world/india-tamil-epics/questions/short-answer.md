# India — the Tamil epics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The world of books: Silappadikaram and Manimekalai.
**Answer: Merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Ilango's epic, and Sattanar's sequel about Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, are lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.11 / The world of books: Silappadikaram and Manimekalai, printed pp. 119–120.**
