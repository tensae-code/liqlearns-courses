# India — the Tamil epics — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The world of books: Silappadikaram and Manimekalai

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about where did people live?.
**Answer: False** — it is about the world of books: Silappadikaram and Manimekalai
