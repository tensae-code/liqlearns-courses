# India — the Tamil epics

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Tamil epics Silappadikaram and Manimekalai tell of Kovalan, Kannagi — whose grief and rage burn Madurai down — and their daughter, and were lost for centuries before being rediscovered. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The world of books: Silappadikaram and Manimekalai

Merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Ilango's epic, and Sattanar's sequel about Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, are lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago.
