# Video script — India — the Tamil epics

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Merchant Kovalan abandons his wife Kannagi for the courtesan Madhavi, is falsely accused of theft in Madurai and executed by the Pandya king — and Kannagi's grief and rage burn the city down; Ilango's epic, and Sattanar's sequel about Kovalan and Madhavi's daughter, are lost for centuries and rediscovered only about a hundred years ago.
