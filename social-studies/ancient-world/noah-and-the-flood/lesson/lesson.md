# Prophet of God Noah

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns even his unbelieving son. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Prophet of God Noah

Noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns even his unbelieving son, and the ark settles on Mount al-Judi where a new humanity begins from Noah's offspring

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — People of the prophet Noah

generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah preaches 950 years against chiefs who lead the rejection, drought and barrenness strike first, then he builds the ark to heaven's own measurements — a leap in shipbuilding — loads pairs of every animal, watches his own son choose a mountain and drown, and lands on al-Judi to start mankind again

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Prophet of God Noah — noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns even his unbelieving son, and the ark settles on Mount al-Judi where a new humanity begins from Noah's offspring; People of the prophet Noah — generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah preaches 950 years against chiefs who lead the rejection, drought and barrenness strike first, then he builds the ark to heaven's own measurements — a leap in shipbuilding — loads pairs of every animal, watches his own son choose a mountain and drown, and lands on al-Judi to start mankind again. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
