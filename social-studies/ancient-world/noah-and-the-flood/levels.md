# Prophet of God Noah — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Prophet of God Noah
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns even his unbelieving son, and the ark settles on Mount al-Judi where a new humanity begins from Noah's offspring

## Sub lesson 2 — People of the prophet Noah
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah preaches 950 years against chiefs who lead the rejection, drought and barrenness strike first, then he builds the ark to heaven's own measurements — a leap in shipbuilding — loads pairs of every animal, watches his own son choose a mountain and drown, and lands on al-Judi to start mankind again
