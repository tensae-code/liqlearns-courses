# Prophet of God Noah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Prophet of God Noah.
**Answer: noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns even his unbelieving son, and the ark settles on Mount al-Judi where a new humanity begins from Noah's offspring (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* (Yemen), Prophet of God Noah, printed p. 12.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: People of the prophet Noah.
**Answer: generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah preaches 950 years against chiefs who lead the rejection, drought and barrenness strike first, then he builds the ark to heaven's own measurements — a leap in shipbuilding — loads pairs of every animal, watches his own son choose a mountain and drown, and lands on al-Judi to start mankind again (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), People of the prophet Noah, printed p. 30.**
