# Prophet of God Noah — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Noah preaches to his people for 950 years and only a few believe, so he builds the first ship in history to exact measures while passers-by mock him, the flood drowns even his unbelieving son, and the ark settles on Mount al-Judi where a new humanity begins from Noah's offspring.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Prophet of God Noah

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Prophet of God Noah

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** generations after Adam, and after Idris who first wrote with the pen and taught sewing, people slide back to worshipping statues and planets, Noah preaches 950 years against chiefs who lead the rejection, drought and barrenness strike first, then he builds the ark to heaven's own measurements — a leap in shipbuilding — loads pairs of every animal, watches his own son choose a mountain and drown, and lands on al-Judi to start mankind again.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in People of the prophet Noah

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about People of the prophet Noah
