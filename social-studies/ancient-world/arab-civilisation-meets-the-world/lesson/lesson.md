# Arab civilisation meets the world

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Arab-Islamic civilisation opens to Greek, Persian and Indian cultures — and its creations go out to the whole world. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Arab civilisation meets the world

Arab-Islamic civilisation opens to Greek, Persian and Indian cultures, old-rooted and newly arrived, and its creations go out to the world.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
