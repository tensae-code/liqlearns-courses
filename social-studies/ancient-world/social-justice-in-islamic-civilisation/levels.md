## Sub lesson 1 — Social justice in the Arab Islamic civilisation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — justice is equality in rights and duties regardless of colour, race, creed or lineage: Islam lifts women out of a Jahiliyya that buried daughters alive into inheritance, the bay'a and public life, protects the dhimmis' worship, courts, property and blood-money, and raises the freed mawali into administration and leadership in the Abbasid age — while Ibn Khaldun's warning hangs over the lesson: injustice is the harbinger of civilisation's ruin, killing a society's cohesion, creativity and defence
