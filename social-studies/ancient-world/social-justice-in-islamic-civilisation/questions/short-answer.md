# Social justice in the Arab Islamic civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Social justice in the Arab Islamic civilisation.
**Answer: justice is equality in rights and duties regardless of colour, race, creed or lineage: Islam lifts women out of a Jahiliyya that buried daughters alive into inheritance, the bay'a and public life, protects the dhimmis' worship, courts, property and blood-money, and raises the freed mawali into administration and leadership in the Abbasid age — while Ibn Khaldun's warning hangs over the lesson: injustice is the harbinger of civilisation's ruin, killing a society's cohesion, creativity and defence (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), Social justice in the Arab Islamic civilisation, printed p. 86.**
