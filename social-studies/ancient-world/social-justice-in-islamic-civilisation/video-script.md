# Video script — Social justice in the Arab Islamic civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

justice is equality in rights and duties regardless of colour, race, creed or lineage: Islam lifts women out of a Jahiliyya that buried daughters alive into inheritance, the bay'a and public life, protects the dhimmis' worship, courts, property and blood-money, and raises the freed mawali into administration and leadership in the Abbasid age — while Ibn Khaldun's warning hangs over the lesson: injustice is the harbinger of civilisation's ruin, killing a society's cohesion, creativity and defence.
