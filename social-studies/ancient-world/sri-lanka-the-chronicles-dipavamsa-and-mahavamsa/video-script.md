# Video script — Sri Lanka — The chronicles: Dipavamsa and Mahavamsa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sri Lanka's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Dipavamsa comes first, then the monk Mahanama shapes the Mahavamsa in the 5th–6th century — 37 chapters ending with Mahasena — and later hands continue it as the Culavamsa, while Wilhelm Geiger's edition lets the world read them critically.
