# Lesson plan — Persia — Cyrus, roads, post and Zoroaster

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Persia — Cyrus, roads, post and Zoroaster
**Place in the curriculum:** Grade 9 History, Unit 2 — Ancient World Civilizations up to c.500 AD (CURRICULUM.md) — stocked into this title in the Ethiopia Wave 4 History fix build.
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From the land of modern Iran and Afghanistan, speakers of an 'Aryan' language built the Persian Empire — founded by Cyrus the Great c.550 BC, the largest empire of the ancient world — and its gifts were government, law and religion: royal roads, the world's first postal service, and Zoroastrianism.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the Ancient World title's river-civilisation lessons — Egypt, Mesopotamia, India, China. It needs nothing else.
