# Persia — Cyrus, roads, post and Zoroaster — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Persian Empire was founded by —
a) Darius the Mede  b) Cyrus the Great, c.550 BC  c) Nebuchadnezzar  d) Alexander
**Answer: b) Cyrus the Great, c.550 BC — Cyrus founded the empire about 550 BC.**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** At its greatest extent the Persian Empire stretched —
a) From Egypt to Nubia only  b) From the Balkans to the Indus Valley and into North Africa  c) Around the Persian Gulf only  d) From Rome to India
**Answer: b) From the Balkans to the Indus Valley and into North Africa — The book gives the empire that full span.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The world's first postal service ran on —
a) Sea routes  b) Persia's new royal roads  c) The Silk Road  d) Roman roads
**Answer: b) Persia's new royal roads — Roads with way-stations carried the royal couriers.**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Persia's contributions are placed by the book in —
a) Shipbuilding and alphabets  b) Government, law and religion  c) Glass and coinage only  d) Pyramids and canals
**Answer: b) Government, law and religion — Roads and post (government), a shared royal order (law), Zoroastrianism (religion).**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Zoroastrianism was founded by —
a) Cyrus  b) The prophet Zoroaster  c) A Greek priest  d) The pharaoh
**Answer: b) The prophet Zoroaster — Zoroaster is named as the faith's founder.**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Zoroastrianism taught its followers to see the world as —
a) A wheel of dynasties  b) A contest between good and evil, light and darkness  c) A market of many gods  d) A dream
**Answer: b) A contest between good and evil, light and darkness — Each person is called to choose the good side.**
