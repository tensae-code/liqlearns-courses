# Persia — Cyrus, roads, post and Zoroaster — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The Persian Empire was the largest empire of the ancient world.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: Persia's postal service was the world's first.
**Answer: True**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Zoroastrianism was founded by Cyrus the Great.
**Answer: False — It was founded by the prophet Zoroaster.**
