# Persia — Cyrus, roads, post and Zoroaster — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Persian Empire was founded by Cyrus the Great about ____ BC.
**Answer: 550**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Royal couriers on Persia's new roads ran the world's first ____ service.
**Answer: postal**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The prophet who founded Zoroastrianism was ____.
**Answer: Zoroaster**
