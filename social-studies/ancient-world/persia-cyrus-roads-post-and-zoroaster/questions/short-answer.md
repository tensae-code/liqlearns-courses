# Persia — Cyrus, roads, post and Zoroaster — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences, in your own words.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In your own words: why was ruling Persia's empire a problem of distance, and how did the Persians answer it?
**Answer: The empire stretched from the Balkans to the Indus; the Persians answered with royal roads and the world's first postal service, so orders, news and armies moved faster.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In your own words: name Persia's three contributions as the book places them.
**Answer: Government (roads and administration), law (a shared royal order), and religion (Zoroastrianism, founded by Zoroaster).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In your own words: what choice does Zoroastrianism put before every person?
**Answer: To choose the good side — light — in the world's contest between good and evil.**
