# Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH; Umar, named by him, founds Kufa and Fustat and the register of stipends before Abu Lu'lu'a kills him in 23 AH; Uthman, chosen by a council of six, builds the first fleet and is killed in his besieged house in 35 AH; Ali fights at the Camel, Siffin and Nahrawan and falls to Ibn Muljam in Ramadan 40 AH; and Hasan makes peace in 41 AH, is poisoned, in the book's telling, at Muawiya's instigation, clearing the way for Husayn's stand against Yazid

## Sub lesson 2 — In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the four caliphs are chosen four different ways — Saqifa bay'a, designation, a council of six, emergency choice — and between 11 and 40 AH they gather the Qur'an into one mushaf, found Basra, Kufa and Fustat with Yemenis sharing in the planning, create the diwans of army, land-tax and stipends, the night police and the first fleet that beats Byzantium at Dhat al-Sawari, and dig the canal joining the Nile to the Red Sea
