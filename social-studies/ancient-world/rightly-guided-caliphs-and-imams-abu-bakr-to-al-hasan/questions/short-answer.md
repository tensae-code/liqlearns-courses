# Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams.
**Answer: abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH; Umar, named by him, founds Kufa and Fustat and the register of stipends before Abu Lu'lu'a kills him in 23 AH; Uthman, chosen by a council of six, builds the first fleet and is killed in his besieged house in 35 AH; Ali fights at the Camel, Siffin and Nahrawan and falls to Ibn Muljam in Ramadan 40 AH; and Hasan makes peace in 41 AH, is poisoned, in the book's telling, at Muawiya's instigation, clearing the way for Husayn's stand against Yazid (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* (Yemen), Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams, printed p. 33.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half).
**Answer: the four caliphs are chosen four different ways — Saqifa bay'a, designation, a council of six, emergency choice — and between 11 and 40 AH they gather the Qur'an into one mushaf, found Basra, Kufa and Fustat with Yemenis sharing in the planning, create the diwans of army, land-tax and stipends, the night police and the first fleet that beats Byzantium at Dhat al-Sawari, and dig the canal joining the Nile to the Red Sea (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half), printed p. 21.**
