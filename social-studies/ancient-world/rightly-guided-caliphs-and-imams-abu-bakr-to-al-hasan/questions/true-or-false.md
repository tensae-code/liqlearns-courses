# Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH; Umar, named by him, founds Kufa and Fustat and the register of stipends before Abu Lu'lu'a kills him in 23 AH; Uthman, chosen by a council of six, builds the first fleet and is killed in his besieged house in 35 AH; Ali fights at the Camel, Siffin and Nahrawan and falls to Ibn Muljam in Ramadan 40 AH; and Hasan makes peace in 41 AH, is poisoned, in the book's telling, at Muawiya's instigation, clearing the way for Husayn's stand against Yazid.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the four caliphs are chosen four different ways — Saqifa bay'a, designation, a council of six, emergency choice — and between 11 and 40 AH they gather the Qur'an into one mushaf, found Basra, Kufa and Fustat with Yemenis sharing in the planning, create the diwans of army, land-tax and stipends, the night police and the first fleet that beats Byzantium at Dhat al-Sawari, and dig the canal joining the Nile to the Red Sea.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half)
