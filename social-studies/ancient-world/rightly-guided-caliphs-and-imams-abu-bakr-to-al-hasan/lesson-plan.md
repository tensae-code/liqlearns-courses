# Lesson plan — Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Yemen, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams** — abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH; Umar, named by him, founds Kufa and Fustat and the register of stipends before Abu Lu'lu'a kills him in 23 AH; Uthman, chosen by a council of six, builds the first fleet and is killed in his besieged house in 35 AH; Ali fights at the Camel, Siffin and Nahrawan and falls to Ibn Muljam in Ramadan 40 AH; and Hasan makes peace in 41 AH, is poisoned, in the book's telling, at Muawiya's instigation, clearing the way for Husayn's stand against Yazid
2. **In the era of the Rashidun caliphs (Unit 2 Lesson 1, second half)** — the four caliphs are chosen four different ways — Saqifa bay'a, designation, a council of six, emergency choice — and between 11 and 40 AH they gather the Qur'an into one mushaf, found Basra, Kufa and Fustat with Yemenis sharing in the planning, create the diwans of army, land-tax and stipends, the night police and the first fleet that beats Byzantium at Dhat al-Sawari, and dig the canal joining the Nile to the Red Sea
