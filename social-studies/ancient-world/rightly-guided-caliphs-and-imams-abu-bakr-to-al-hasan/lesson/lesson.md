# Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Age of the Rightly Guided Caliphs and Imams

Abu Bakr, chosen at the Saqifa in 11 AH, crushes the apostasy wars and dies in 13 AH; Umar, named by him, founds Kufa and Fustat and the register of stipends before Abu Lu'lu'a kills him in 23 AH; Uthman, chosen by a council of six, builds the first fleet and is killed in his besieged house in 35 AH; Ali fights at the Camel, Siffin and Nahrawan and falls to Ibn Muljam in Ramadan 40 AH; and Hasan makes peace in 41 AH, is poisoned, in the book's telling, at Muawiya's instigation, clearing the way for Husayn's stand against Yazid
