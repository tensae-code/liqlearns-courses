# Video script — Tiwanaku — state and trade

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tiwanaku builds the Akapana pyramid and the Puerta del Sol and trades camelid textiles, meat and wool across long-distance networks.

The Tiwanaku civilisation is given its own unit as the Altiplano's great state.
