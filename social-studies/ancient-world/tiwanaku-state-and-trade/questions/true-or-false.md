# Tiwanaku — state and trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tiwanaku builds the Akapana pyramid and the Puerta del Sol and trades camelid textiles, meat and wool across long-distance networks.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in La cultura Tiahuanaco

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Texto de Aprendizaje — Ciencias Sociales, 1er Año de Educación Secundaria Comunitaria Productiva* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about La cultura Tiahuanaco

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Tiwanaku civilisation is given its own unit as the Altiplano's great state.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Año 1: La civilización Tiwanakota

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros — Textos de Aprendizaje 2023–2024, Secundaria Comunitaria Productiva, Área Ciencias Sociales* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Año 1: La civilización Tiwanakota
