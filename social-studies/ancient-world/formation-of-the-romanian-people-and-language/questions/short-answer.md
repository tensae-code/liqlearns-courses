**Answer: the Geto-Dacian locals absorb Rome's civilisation and popular Latin; Constantine's 313 turn spreads Christianity, and one people with one language — in dialects north and south of the Danube — emerges between Nistru, Tisa and the sea (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
