# Burebista Unifier Of The Geto Dacians — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor.
**Answer: Burebista gathers the scattered Geto-Dacian tribes into one kingdom, the first unification in the ancestors' story (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* (Moldova), Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor, printed p. 24.**

**Answer: Burebista unifies the quarrelling tribes by war and diplomacy, treats with Pompey against Caesar, and escapes punishment only by Caesar's assassination (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
