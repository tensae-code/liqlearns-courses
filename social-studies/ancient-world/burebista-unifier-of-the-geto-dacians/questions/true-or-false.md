# Burebista Unifier Of The Geto Dacians — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Burebista gathers the scattered Geto-Dacian tribes into one kingdom, the first unification in the ancestors' story.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Burebista unifies the quarrelling tribes by war and diplomacy, treats with Pompey against Caesar, and escapes punishment only by Caesar's assassination.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unitatea IV, Lecția 4: Dacia pe timpul lui Burebista

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală. Manual pentru clasa a V-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unitatea IV, Lecția 4: Dacia pe timpul lui Burebista

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Burebista's unified state shatters at his death into small kingdoms, and a century of fragmentation follows until Decebal gathers Dacia again.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unitatea VI, Lecția 1: Dacia de la Burebista la Decebal

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală. Manual pentru clasa a V-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unitatea VI, Lecția 1: Dacia de la Burebista la Decebal
