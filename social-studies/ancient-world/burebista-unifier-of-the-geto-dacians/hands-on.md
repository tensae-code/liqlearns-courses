# Hands-on — Burebista Unifier Of The Geto Dacians

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Burebista Unifier Of The Geto Dacians — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Tema 8: Burebista, regele unificator al geto-dacilor, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
