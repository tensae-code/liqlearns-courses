# The Crusades — two centuries for Jerusalem

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: For two centuries Europe marches east for Jerusalem — storming it in 1099, losing it to Salahuddin, and shaming itself by sacking Christian Constantinople in 1204 — and the wars end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Crusades — two centuries for Jerusalem

After Manzikert, Byzantium begs the West for help and Pope Urban II answers with holy war — the First Crusade (1095–1099) storms Jerusalem; Nuruddin Zengi and then Salahuddin unite the Muslims; the Third Crusade pits Salahuddin against Richard; the Fourth (1202–1204) shames itself by sacking Christian Constantinople; two centuries of war end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
