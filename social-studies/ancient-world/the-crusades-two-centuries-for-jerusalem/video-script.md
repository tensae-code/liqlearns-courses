# Video script — The Crusades — two centuries for Jerusalem

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

For two centuries Europe marches east for Jerusalem — storming it in 1099, losing it to Salahuddin, and shaming itself by sacking Christian Constantinople in 1204 — and the wars end by teaching Europe geography, trade and tolerance of a stronger neighbour.
