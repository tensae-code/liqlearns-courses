# Video script — Baghdad's great schools — the Nizamiyya and the Mustansiriyya

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iraq's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

the Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university.
