# Baghdad's great schools — the Nizamiyya and the Mustansiriyya

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Seljuk vizier Nizam al-Mulk's Nizamiyya (finished 459 AH) boards and feeds its students free; al-Mustansir's Mustansiriyya (631 AH) adds four colleges, a teaching hospital and an 80,000-book library — the first specialised Islamic university. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
