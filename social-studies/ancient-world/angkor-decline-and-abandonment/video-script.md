# Video script — Angkor — decline and abandonment

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jayavarman VIII's long reign strains the empire; Cham wars (1262) and Mongol-Yuan pressure (embassies of the 1280s) close in — the causes of decline are weighed one by one.

Jayavarman VIII (1243–1296), Indravarman III (1295–1307, Zhou Daguan's host), the last inscription kings to Jayavarman IX (1327–1336) — then the court's slow road away from Angkor.
