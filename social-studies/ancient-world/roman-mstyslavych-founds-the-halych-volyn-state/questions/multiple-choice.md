# Roman Mstyslavych founds the Halych-Volyn state — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Roman took Halych in —
a) 1199  b) 1132  c) 1240
**Answer: a) 1199**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Roman was prince of —
a) Volyn  b) Chernihiv  c) Pereiaslav
**Answer: a) Volyn**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Roman died in —
a) 1205, in Poland  b) 1199, in Halych  c) 1253, in Lviv
**Answer: a) 1205, in Poland**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After Roman's death, his sons were —
a) Infants, circled by boyars and foreign kings  b) Adult kings  c) Sent to Constantinople
**Answer: a) Infants, circled by boyars and foreign kings**
