# Roman Mstyslavych founds the Halych-Volyn state

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Roman Mstyslavych, prince of Volyn, takes Halych in 1199 and unites the two lands into the strongest state in Rus — then dies in Poland in 1205, leaving infant sons, grasping boyars and foreign kings circling his widow's court. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 1199 — Volyn and Halych united

Roman Mstyslavych, prince of Volyn, takes Halych in 1199. He unites the two lands into the strongest state in Rus — the Romanovych state.

## Sub lesson 2 — 1205 — a state left to infants

Roman dies in Poland in 1205. He leaves infant sons, grasping boyars and foreign kings circling his widow's court — the new state must fight to survive its founder.

Tell your companion the story of Roman Mstyslavych founds the Halych-Volyn state in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
