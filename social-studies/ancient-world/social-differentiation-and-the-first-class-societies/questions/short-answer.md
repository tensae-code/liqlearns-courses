# Social differentiation and the first class societies — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Social differentiation.
**Answer: surpluses split once-equal communities into classes; Egypt is used as the case where rulers and ruled first appear (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Mozambique's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *História — Módulo 1 (PESD I, Programa do Ensino Secundário à Distância – 1º Ciclo)* (Mozambique), Social differentiation, printed p. 72.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The rise of social differentiation.
**Answer: growing production, population, taxation and defence split society into layers and set them struggling — differentiation, the precondition of the state (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Mozambique's book, where is this part taught?
