# Video script — Social differentiation and the first class societies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mozambique's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Surpluses split once-equal communities into classes; Egypt is used as the case where rulers and ruled first appear.
Growing production, population, taxation and defence split society into layers and set them struggling — differentiation, the precondition of the state.
