# Ukrainian towns, crafts and Magdeburg law

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: three-field farming spreads, guild crafts grow — Lviv's armourers are famous by 1394 — trade roads link the lands to Europe and the Black Sea, and town after town wins Magdeburg self-government. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Fields, guilds and trade roads

Three-field farming spreads across the lands. Guild crafts grow — Lviv's armourers are famous by 1394 — and trade roads link the lands to Europe and the Black Sea.

## Sub lesson 2 — Magdeburg law — towns govern themselves

Town after town wins Magdeburg self-government. A town with Magdeburg law rules itself by its own charter — a new kind of freedom inside the old order.
