# Ukrainian towns, crafts and Magdeburg law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Guild crafts grew in the towns of this age.
**Answer: True — The book lists guild crafts growing, with Lviv's armourers famous by 1394.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Magdeburg law meant a prince governed the town directly.
**Answer: False — Magdeburg law is self-government for the town.**
