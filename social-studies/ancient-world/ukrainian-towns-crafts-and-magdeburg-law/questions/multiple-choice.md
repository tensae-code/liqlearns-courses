# Ukrainian towns, crafts and Magdeburg law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The farming system spreading in this age is —
a) Three-field farming  b) No-field farming  c) Irrigation farming only
**Answer: a) Three-field farming**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Famous by 1394 were —
a) Lviv's armourers  b) Kyiv's shipwrights  c) Halych's salt miners only
**Answer: a) Lviv's armourers**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Magdeburg law gave a town —
a) Self-government  b) A prince's garrison  c) Freedom from all trade
**Answer: a) Self-government**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Trade roads linked the lands to —
a) Europe and the Black Sea  b) Only Moscow  c) Only Crimea
**Answer: a) Europe and the Black Sea**
