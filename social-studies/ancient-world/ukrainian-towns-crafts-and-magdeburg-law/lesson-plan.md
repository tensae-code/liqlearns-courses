# Lesson plan — Ukrainian towns, crafts and Magdeburg law

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ukrainian towns, crafts and Magdeburg law
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three-field farming spreads, guild crafts grow — Lviv's armourers are famous by 1394 — trade roads link the lands to Europe and the Black Sea, and town after town wins Magdeburg self-government.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Fields, guilds and trade roads** — Three-field farming spreads across the lands. Guild crafts grow — Lviv's armourers are famous by 1394 — and trade roads link the lands to Europe and the Black Sea.
2. **Magdeburg law — towns govern themselves** — Town after town wins Magdeburg self-government. A town with Magdeburg law rules itself by its own charter — a new kind of freedom inside the old order.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
