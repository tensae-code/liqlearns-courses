# Lesson plan — Vandals and Byzantines in North Africa

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Vandals and Byzantines in North Africa
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Libya, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Genseric's Vandals cross from Spain in 429, take Carthage in 439 and western Libya in 455, and tear down Rome's walls at Tripoli, Leptis and Sabratha — until Libyan tribes defeat them in the battles of 527–533
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Vandals in Libya** — Genseric's Vandals cross from Spain in 429, take Carthage in 439 and western Libya in 455, and tear down Rome's walls at Tripoli, Leptis and Sabratha — until Libyan tribes defeat them in the battles of 527–533
2. **The Byzantines in Libya** — Belisarius lands in 533 with 35,000 men and 600 ships, captures King Gelimer and restores North Africa to Justinian; forts rise along the coast manned by Libyan soldiers, but corrupt clergy and quarrelling sects hollow the empire out
