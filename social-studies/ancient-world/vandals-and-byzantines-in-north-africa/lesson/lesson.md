# Vandals and Byzantines in North Africa

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Genseric's Vandals cross from Spain in 429, take Carthage in 439 and western Libya in 455, and tear down Rome's walls at Tripoli, Leptis and Sabratha — until Libyan tribes defeat them in the battles of 527–533. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Vandals in Libya
