# Vandals and Byzantines in North Africa — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Genseric's Vandals cross from Spain in 429, take Carthage in 439 and western Libya in 455, and tear down Rome's walls at Tripoli, Leptis and Sabratha — until Libyan tribes defeat them in the battles of 527–533.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Vandals in Libya

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History of Libya and the Ancient World* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Vandals in Libya

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Belisarius lands in 533 with 35,000 men and 600 ships, captures King Gelimer and restores North Africa to Justinian; forts rise along the coast manned by Libyan soldiers, but corrupt clergy and quarrelling sects hollow the empire out.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Byzantines in Libya

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G7 History of Libya and the Ancient World* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Byzantines in Libya
