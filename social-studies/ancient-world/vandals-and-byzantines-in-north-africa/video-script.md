The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Libya's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Belisarius lands in 533 with 35,000 men and 600 ships, captures King Gelimer and restores North Africa to Justinian; forts rise along the coast manned by Libyan soldiers, but corrupt clergy and quarrelling sects hollow the empire out.
