# Video script — Al-Andalus — taifa kingdoms and the fall of Granada

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Palestine's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After eight centuries, luxury and power-struggles split al-Andalus into 22 taifa statelets that fall one by one; Spain then breaks the Granada surrender treaty — expelling hundreds of thousands, closing mosques, burning Arabic books and setting up the Inquisition.
