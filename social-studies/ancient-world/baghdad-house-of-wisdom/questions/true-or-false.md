# Baghdad — the House of Wisdom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Al-Mansur's palace library grows under Harun al-Rashid and al-Ma'mun into the House of Wisdom, where Christian Yuhanna ibn Masawayh and Persian al-Fadl ibn Nawbakht lead teams translating the world's books into Arabic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bayt al-Hikma and the houses of knowledge

**Answer: False** — it is about Bayt al-Hikma and the houses of knowledge
