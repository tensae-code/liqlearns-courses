# Halych and Volyn rise

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in the west Yaroslav Osmomysl makes Halych rich on salt and the Dnister trade while Volyn grows beside it — and all the while Pechenegs, Torks and Polovtsians ride the steppe and Crimea changes hands beyond the mountains. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Halych rich on salt, Volyn beside it

In the west, Yaroslav Osmomysl makes Halych rich on salt and the Dnister trade. Volyn grows beside it — the west rises while the old centre weakens.

## Sub lesson 2 — The steppe peoples and Crimea

All the while Pechenegs, Torks and Polovtsians ride the steppe. Beyond the mountains, Crimea changes hands — the south is its own world of nomads and ports.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Halych and Volyn rise in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Say how Halych grew rich under Yaroslav Osmomysl: on salt and the Dnister trade, with Volyn growing beside it.
2. Name the nomadic peoples who rode the steppe in this age: Pechenegs, Torks and Polovtsians — and that Crimea changed hands beyond the mountains.
