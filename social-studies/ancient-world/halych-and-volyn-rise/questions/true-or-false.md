# Halych and Volyn rise — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Volyn grew beside Halych in the west.
**Answer: True — The book sets Volyn growing beside a Halych rich on salt and Dnister trade.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The steppe was quiet and empty in the 12th century.
**Answer: False — Pechenegs, Torks and Polovtsians ride the steppe all the while.**
