# Halych and Volyn rise — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Halych grew rich on —
a) Salt and the Dnister trade  b) Silk from China  c) Gold mines
**Answer: a) Salt and the Dnister trade**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Halych's prince in this lesson is —
a) Yaroslav Osmomysl  b) Andrei Bogoliubsky  c) Batu
**Answer: a) Yaroslav Osmomysl**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The steppe peoples named are —
a) Pechenegs, Torks and Polovtsians  b) Cimmerians only  c) Mongols only
**Answer: a) Pechenegs, Torks and Polovtsians**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Crimea in this age —
a) Changed hands beyond the mountains  b) Was part of Halych  c) Was empty
**Answer: a) Changed hands beyond the mountains**
