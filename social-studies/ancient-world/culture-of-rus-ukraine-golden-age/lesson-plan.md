# Lesson plan — Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the golden age

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the golden age
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** byliny epics sung about Volodymyr's heroes, the first schools and libraries, chronicle writing begins, and St Sophia's mosaics and the Desiatynna church make Kyiv a city of stone and books.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Songs, schools and chronicles** — Byliny epics are sung about Volodymyr's heroes. The first schools and libraries open, and chronicle writing begins — Rus starts writing its own story down.
2. **A city of stone and books** — St Sophia's mosaics and the Desiatynna church rise in Kyiv. Stone churches and written books together make Kyiv a city of stone and books.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Rus-Ukraine — how the land was ruled and lived. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the golden-age culture the book lists: byliny epics, the first schools and libraries, and the beginning of chronicle writing.
2. Say what made Kyiv a city of stone and books: St Sophia's mosaics and the Desiatynna church, with its schools and libraries.
