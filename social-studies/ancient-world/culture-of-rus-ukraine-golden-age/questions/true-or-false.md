# Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the golden age — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first schools and libraries appear in this golden age.
**Answer: True — The book lists the first schools and libraries among its culture.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kyiv in this age was built only of wood and had no books.
**Answer: False — St Sophia's mosaics and the Desiatynna church make it a city of stone and books.**
