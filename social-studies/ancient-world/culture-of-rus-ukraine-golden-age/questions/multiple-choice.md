# Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the golden age — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Byliny are —
a) Epics sung about Volodymyr's heroes  b) Law codes  c) Church tax lists
**Answer: a) Epics sung about Volodymyr's heroes**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In this age, chronicle writing —
a) Begins  b) Ends  c) Is forbidden
**Answer: a) Begins**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** St Sophia in Kyiv is famous here for its —
a) Mosaics  b) Wooden walls  c) Harbour
**Answer: a) Mosaics**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Desiatynna church helps make Kyiv —
a) A city of stone and books  b) A fortress camp  c) A trading fair only
**Answer: a) A city of stone and books**
