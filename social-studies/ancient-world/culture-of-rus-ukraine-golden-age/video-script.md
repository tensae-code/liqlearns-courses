# Video script — Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the golden age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Ukraine's *Історія України* (Grade 7) tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.
