# Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the golden age

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: byliny epics sung about Volodymyr's heroes, the first schools and libraries, chronicle writing begins, and St Sophia's mosaics and the Desiatynna church make Kyiv a city of stone and books. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Songs, schools and chronicles

Byliny epics are sung about Volodymyr's heroes. The first schools and libraries open, and chronicle writing begins — Rus starts writing its own story down.

## Sub lesson 2 — A city of stone and books

St Sophia's mosaics and the Desiatynna church rise in Kyiv. Stone churches and written books together make Kyiv a city of stone and books.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the golden age in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
