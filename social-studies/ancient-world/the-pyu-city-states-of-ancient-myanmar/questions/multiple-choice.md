# The Pyu city-states of ancient Myanmar — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Myanmar's first cities were created by the growth of — —
a) Knowledge, capability and technology  b) Sea trade only  c) Foreign conquest only
**Answer: a) Knowledge, capability and technology** — Knowledge, capability and technology — farming skill and craft knowledge gather people behind walls.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** People gathered behind walls as — —
a) Farming and craft skills grew  b) Rivers dried up  c) Empires ordered them to
**Answer: a) Farming and craft skills grew** — Farming and craft skills grew.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which list names Pyu city-states? —
a) Beikthano, Hanlin and Sri Ksetra  b) Bagan, Rome and Athens  c) Only Maingmaw alone
**Answer: a) Beikthano, Hanlin and Sri Ksetra** — Beikthano, Hanlin and Sri Ksetra — with Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi and Waitharli.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Pyu cities are described as — —
a) Walled, irrigated, Buddhist-and-Hindu trading cities  b) Unwalled fishing camps only  c) A single empire's capital
**Answer: a) Walled, irrigated, Buddhist-and-Hindu trading cities** — Walled, irrigated, Buddhist-and-Hindu trading cities.
