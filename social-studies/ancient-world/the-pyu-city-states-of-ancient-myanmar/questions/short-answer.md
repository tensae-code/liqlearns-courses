# The Pyu city-states of ancient Myanmar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How does the guide explain the emergence of cities in ancient Myanmar?
**Answer: Knowledge, capability and technology developed — farming skill and craft knowledge gathered people behind walls into the first city-states (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three Pyu city-states and say what kind of cities they were.
**Answer: Any three of Beikthano, Hanlin, Sri Ksetra, Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi, Waitharli; they were walled, irrigated, Buddhist-and-Hindu trading cities (any close wording).**
