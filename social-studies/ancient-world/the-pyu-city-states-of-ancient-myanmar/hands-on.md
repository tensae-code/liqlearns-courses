# Hands-on — The Pyu city-states of ancient Myanmar

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Map the chain of cities
Write the six Pyu city names — Beikthano, Hanlin, Sri Ksetra, Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi and Waitharli — as a chain on paper, one link per city. Under each link write one word for what held the chain together (walls, irrigation, trade, faith).
