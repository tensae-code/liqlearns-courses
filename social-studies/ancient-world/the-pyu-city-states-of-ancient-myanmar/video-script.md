# Video script — The Pyu city-states of ancient Myanmar

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Myanmar's story as its Grade 6 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Farming skill and craft knowledge gather people behind walls: the development of knowledge, capability and technology creates Myanmar's first cities — the ancient city-states.

The Pyu city-states one by one — Beikthano (the Vishnu city), Hanlin, Sri Ksetra, Maingmaw, Thuwunnabumi and Waitharli — a chain of walled, irrigated, Buddhist-and-Hindu trading cities flourishing for centuries before any Myanmar empire exists.
