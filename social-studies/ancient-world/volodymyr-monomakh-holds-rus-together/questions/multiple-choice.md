# Volodymyr Monomakh holds Rus together — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Liubech congress met in —
a) 1097  b) 988  c) 1240
**Answer: a) 1097**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The congress failed to stop —
a) The princes' feuds  b) The Polovtsian language  c) Church building
**Answer: a) The princes' feuds**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Monomakh wrote —
a) The Instruction to his children  b) The Ruska Pravda  c) The Tale of Ihor's Campaign
**Answer: a) The Instruction to his children**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Mstyslav held Rus together until —
a) 1132  b) 1097  c) 1253
**Answer: a) 1132**
