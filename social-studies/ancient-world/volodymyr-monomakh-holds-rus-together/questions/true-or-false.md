# Volodymyr Monomakh holds Rus together — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Monomakh campaigned against the Polovtsians.
**Answer: True — The book names him a campaigner against the Polovtsians.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After Mstyslav, the old unity of Rus lasted for centuries more.
**Answer: False — Mstyslav holds it together until 1132 — after him the unity is gone for good.**
