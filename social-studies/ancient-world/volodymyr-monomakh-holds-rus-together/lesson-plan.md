# Lesson plan — Volodymyr Monomakh holds Rus together

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Volodymyr Monomakh holds Rus together
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Liubech congress of princes (1097) fails to stop the feuds, but Monomakh — campaigner against the Polovtsians, author of the famous Instruction to his children — reunites most of Rus under Kyiv once more, and his son Mstyslav holds it together until 1132.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Liubech fails, Monomakh rises** — The Liubech congress of princes in 1097 fails to stop the feuds. But Volodymyr Monomakh — campaigner against the Polovtsians — reunites most of Rus under Kyiv once more.
2. **The Instruction and Mstyslav** — Monomakh is also the author of the famous Instruction to his children. His son Mstyslav the Great holds Rus together until 1132 — the last holding of the old unity.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Rus-Ukraine breaks apart — the Yaroslavychi. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the Liubech congress of 1097 failed to do, and what Monomakh did instead: reunited most of Rus under Kyiv.
2. Say what Monomakh wrote and how long the unity lasted: the Instruction to his children; his son Mstyslav held Rus together until 1132.
