# Cambodia — the Longvek era — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Longvek era (16th century)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី១១ (History, Grade 11)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Longvek era (16th century)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ang Chan walls a new capital at Longvek (its moats and ramparts still traceable, photos p. 110); Siam presses from the west, and after Longvek's fall the court wanders again.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 4 Lesson 3, The Longvek era (16th–17th centuries)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៦ (Social Studies, Grade 6)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 4 Lesson 3, The Longvek era (16th–17th centuries)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Longvek is a fortress-city of moats, five gates and a royal palace with pagodas, open to Portuguese and Japanese traders — built strong after Ang Chan's death (1526) against the Siamese threat.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Longvek era in the 16th century

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៨ (Social Studies, Grade 8)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about History Ch. 2 Lesson 3, The Longvek era in the 16th century
