# Video script — Cambodia — the Longvek era

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ang Chan I (1516–1566) founds a new walled capital at Longvek and holds a still-large Cambodia (map, p. 132) between rising Siam and Vietnam.

Ang Chan walls a new capital at Longvek (its moats and ramparts still traceable, photos p. 110); Siam presses from the west, and after Longvek's fall the court wanders again.

Longvek is a fortress-city of moats, five gates and a royal palace with pagodas, open to Portuguese and Japanese traders — built strong after Ang Chan's death (1526) against the Siamese threat.
