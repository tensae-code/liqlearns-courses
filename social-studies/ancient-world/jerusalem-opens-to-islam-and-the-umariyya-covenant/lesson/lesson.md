# Jerusalem opens to Islam and the Umariyya Covenant

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: at Ajnadayn (13 AH / 634 CE) near Ajjur — a village the occupation would destroy in 1948 — Amr ibn al-As opens Palestine; Jerusalem surrenders to Caliph Umar himself, whose Umariyya Covenant, read in full, guarantees its people safety for lives, property, churches and crosses in return for jizya As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
