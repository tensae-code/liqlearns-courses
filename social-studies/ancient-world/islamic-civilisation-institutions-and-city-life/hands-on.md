# Hands-on — Islamic civilisation — its state, markets and cities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw the state as a chart: caliph at top, then ministry, diwans, provinces, police — one duty under each.

## Task 2
Write a police chief's evening report in four sentences.

## Task 3
Draw a purse with four strings — markets, crafts, farming, trade — and what each string buys.

## Task 4
Write a market day scene naming one craft good, one farm good and one traded good.

## Task 5
Sketch a planned Islamic city quarter: street, mosque, houses — label each.

## Task 6
Write why planning matters: two sentences from a city-dweller's view.
