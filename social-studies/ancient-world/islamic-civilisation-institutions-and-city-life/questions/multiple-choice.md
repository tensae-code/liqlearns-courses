# Islamic civilisation — its state, markets and cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The sahib al-shurta was —
a) the police chief  b) the chief judge  c) the market inspector
**Answer: a) the police chief** — Ali ibn Abi Talib's police chief, the sahib al-shurta

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The state's offices included —
a) caliphate, ministry, diwans and provinces  b) only the army  c) only the treasury
**Answer: a) caliphate, ministry, diwans and provinces** — caliphate, ministry, diwans, provinces and police

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The diwans were —
a) the state's administrative bureaus  b) city markets  c) police stations
**Answer: a) the state's administrative bureaus** — diwans take shape as part of the working state

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Islamic world's purse was filled by —
a) markets, crafts, farming and trade  b) plunder only  c) pearl diving only
**Answer: a) markets, crafts, farming and trade** — markets, crafts, farming and trade fill its purse

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** That wealth paid for —
a) its cities and schools  b) only its palaces  c) only its armies
**Answer: a) its cities and schools** — the wealth that pays for its cities and schools

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson treats the economy as —
a) a manifestation of the civilisation  b) separate from civilisation  c) a modern invention
**Answer: a) a manifestation of the civilisation** — economic manifestations of Islamic civilisation

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** Islamic cities rose —
a) in planned stone  b) by accident  c) of tents only
**Answer: a) in planned stone** — Islamic cities rise in planned stone

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The urban face of the civilisation shows in —
a) streets, mosques and houses  b) only in fortresses  c) only in ports
**Answer: a) streets, mosques and houses** — streets, mosques and houses

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson runs the economy story on into —
a) the cities it built  b) foreign lands  c) the desert
**Answer: a) the cities it built** — the money story runs on into streets, mosques and houses
