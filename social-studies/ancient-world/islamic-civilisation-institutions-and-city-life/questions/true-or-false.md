# Islamic civilisation — its state, markets and cities — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Islamic state had no administration — only an army.
**Answer: False** — caliphate, ministry, diwans, provinces and police form a working state

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Policing is part of the civilisation's political life.
**Answer: True** — the sahib al-shurta keeps the streets

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Islamic civilisation's wealth came from one source only.
**Answer: False** — markets, crafts, farming and trade all fill its purse

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Schools are paid for by a working economy.
**Answer: True** — the wealth pays for its cities and schools

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Islamic cities were unplanned camps.
**Answer: False** — they rise in planned stone

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** A civilisation's economy and its cities are one story.
**Answer: True** — the money story runs on into streets, mosques and houses
