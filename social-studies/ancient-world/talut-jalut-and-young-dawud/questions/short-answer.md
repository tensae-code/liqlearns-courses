# Talut and Jalut — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Talut and Jalut.
**Answer: humbled in the Levant and robbed of the Ark, the Israelites beg their prophet for a king, then recoil when fighting is actually decreed and again when the chosen king, Talut, is a nobody without wealth; his sign is the Ark returning on angels' wings, his test is the river — drink more than a handful and fall away — and the little band that is left faces Jalut's giant army, prays, and watches the youth Dawud kill Jalut and turn the day (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Talut and Jalut, printed p. 68.**
