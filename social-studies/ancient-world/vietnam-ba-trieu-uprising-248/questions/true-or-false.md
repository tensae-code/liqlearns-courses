# Vietnam Ba Trieu Uprising 248 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Triệu Thị Trinh, 23, leads Thanh Hoá from her Núi Nưa base, riding an elephant into battle, vowing to ride the strong winds and cut down the whales of the Eastern Sea rather than bow as anyone's concubine; Ngô's 8,000 troops end her stand at Núi Tùng.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 16, the uprising of Bà Triệu (248)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 16, the uprising of Bà Triệu (248)
