# The Cisterns of Aden — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aden, the port bridging east-west trade, solves its thirst with giant cisterns cut into Wadi al-Tawila on the slopes of Jabal Shamsan under the Himyarite kings — a cascading chain of tanks some 229 metres long that cleans the water as it falls, holding some 14 million litres and shielding the crater city from the floods; of 55 cisterns, 18 still stand in good repair after the British occupation destroyed many.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Cisterns of Aden

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Cisterns of Aden
