# Hands-on — Yemen in the Prophetic age

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Yemen in the Prophetic age — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Yemen in the Prophetic age, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
