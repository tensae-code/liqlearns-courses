# Greek Myth Heracles And His Labours

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: baby Heracles, son of Alcmene, strangles the two snakes Hera sends to his cradle, and the frightened household sees the hero he will become As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
