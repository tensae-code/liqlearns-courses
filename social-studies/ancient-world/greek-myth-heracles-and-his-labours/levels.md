# Greek Myth Heracles And His Labours — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 2, ch. 1 The birth of Heracles
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — baby Heracles, son of Alcmene, strangles the two snakes Hera sends to his cradle, and the frightened household sees the hero he will become

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2, ch. 2 The Nemean lion
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — sent to serve Eurystheus, Heracles wrestles the unpierceable lion bare-handed and comes back wearing its skin, terrifying the king into a jar

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 2, ch. 3 The Lernaean Hydra and the Erymanthian boar
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — every Hydra head cut grows back two until Iolaus burns the necks, and Heracles then traps the giant boar in deep snow and carries it alive

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 2, ch. 4 The golden-horned deer, the Stymphalian birds, the Augean stables
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a year chasing Artemis's deer, bronze-beaked birds scattered by noise, and a whole filthy stable cleaned in one day by diverting two rivers

## Sub lesson 5 — Unit 2, ch. 5 More labours
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the fire-breathing mares of Diomedes, Hippolyta's belt among the Amazons, and the cattle of three-bodied Geryon at the edge of the world

## Sub lesson 6 — Unit 2, ch. 6 Cerberus of Hades and the golden apples of the Hesperides
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Heracles wrestles the three-headed dog of the dead bare-handed and tricks Atlas into fetching the golden apples, then tricks him back under the sky

## Sub lesson 7 — Unit 2, ch. 7 The end of Heracles
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the poisoned shirt of Nessus burns Heracles, he dies on a pyre on Oeta, and Zeus raises him to Olympus as a god
