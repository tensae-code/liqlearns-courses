# The Abbasid Caliphate — Baghdad's golden age

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Abbasids inherit the caliphate and its frontiers — caliphs patronise the sciences, cities rise in an urban renaissance, and the empire's own weaknesses finally bring it down. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

The Abbasid age carries the same story forward — caliphs patronising the sciences, cities rising in an urban renaissance, and an empire whose own weaknesses finally bring it down.
