# Vietnam Ly Thuong Kiet Defeats The Song — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** refusing to "sit idle and wait", he strikes Song bases first, holds the Như Nguyệt river line in 1076, counterattacks in 1077 as the poem Nam quốc sơn hà is read out to the troops, then offers peace and the Song withdraw.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 9, story of Lý Thường Kiệt and the war against the Song

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 9, story of Lý Thường Kiệt and the war against the Song

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lý Thường Kiệt (1019–1105, born Ngô Tuấn) judges that "sitting and waiting for the enemy is not as good as striking first to blunt their strength"; in October 1075 he leads over 100,000 men by water and land into Song territory, and in 42 days takes Yongzhou, burns its stores and withdraws.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 12, the pre-emptive strike of 1075
