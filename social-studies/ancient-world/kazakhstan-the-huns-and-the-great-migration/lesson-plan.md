# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan The Huns And The Great Migration

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan The Huns And The Great Migration
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chinese chronicles record the Hunnu empire pressing on China's wall
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§45–46 The Hunnu in written sources** — Chinese chronicles record the Hunnu empire pressing on China's wall
2. **§47 Hunnu economy, social and state order** — the shanyu rules a confederation of herder-warriors from a mobile court
3. **§48–49 The Great Migration of peoples** — Hun horsemen roll west and push whole peoples across Eurasia, redrawing its map
4. **5.1 The unification of the Hun tribes** — Mode Shanyu welds the Hun tribes into one empire north of China
5. **5.2 The Huns and neighbouring states** — the Hun empire treats, trades and wars with China and its steppe neighbours
6. **5.3 The Huns' migration to the West** — Hun bands roll west across Kazakhstan toward Europe, moving peoples before them
7. **5.4 Attila and his conquests** — Attila's horde terrifies two Roman empires from his wooden capital
8. **§51 Who were the Huns?** — the Huns ride out of the eastern steppe into world history
9. **§52 Mode Tengrikut's state policy** — Mode builds the Hun empire with decimal army order and hard diplomacy
10. **§53 The Huns and the Great Wall of China** — China walls itself against the Hun horsemen it cannot defeat
11. **§54 The Hun state's structure, economy and culture** — a shanyu, tribal wings and rich graves define the Hun state
12. **§55 The Huns and the 'Great Migration of Peoples'** — Hun pressure sets Europe's peoples moving like dominoes
13. **§56 Attila (Edil) and his campaigns to the West** — Attila extorts two empires and shakes Gaul and Italy
14. **5.1 The unification of the Hun tribes (lesson plans)** — Mode Shanyu's unification is staged as state-building
15. **5.2 Huns' relations with neighbouring states (lesson plans)** — treaties, marriages and wars with China structure the lessons
16. **5.3 The Huns' resettlement to the West (lesson plans)** — the westward migration is traced as the Great Migration's trigger
17. **5.4 Attila and his conquests (lesson plans)** — Attila's campaigns close the Hun chapter at Europe's gates
