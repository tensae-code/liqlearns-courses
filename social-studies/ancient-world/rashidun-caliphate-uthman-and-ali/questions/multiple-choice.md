# Uthman and Ali — the Rashidun age ends — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The fourth caliph was —
a) Ali ibn Abi Talib  b) Uthman ibn Affan  c) Abu Bakr
**Answer: a) Ali ibn Abi Talib** — Ali, the fourth caliph

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Under Uthman, expansion —
a) continued  b) stopped  c) reversed
**Answer: a) continued** — expansion continues under Uthman

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** With Ali, the book closes —
a) the Rashidun age  b) the Prophet's life  c) the Ridda
**Answer: a) the Rashidun age** — the term closes with Ali as the Rashidun age ends
