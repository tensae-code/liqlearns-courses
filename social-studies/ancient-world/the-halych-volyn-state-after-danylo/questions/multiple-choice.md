# The Halych-Volyn state after Danylo — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lev moved the capital to —
a) Lviv  b) Kyiv  c) Kholm
**Answer: a) Lviv**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Yurii I was Danylo's —
a) Grandson  b) Father  c) Brother
**Answer: a) Grandson**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Romanovych line died out by —
a) 1340  b) 1253  c) 1362
**Answer: a) 1340**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The inheritance was divided by —
a) Poland and Lithuania  b) Mongols and Byzantines  c) Hungary and Moldova only
**Answer: a) Poland and Lithuania**
