# The Halych-Volyn state after Danylo

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Danylo's son Lev moves the capital to Lviv and holds Galicia against all comers; his grandson Yurii I takes the royal title again — but by 1340 the Romanovych line dies out and Poland and Lithuania divide the inheritance. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lev and Yurii I hold the state

Danylo's son Lev moves the capital to Lviv and holds Galicia against all comers. His grandson Yurii I takes the royal title again — the Romanovych state endures.

## Sub lesson 2 — 1340 — the line dies out

By 1340 the Romanovych line dies out. Poland and Lithuania divide the inheritance — the Halych-Volyn state ends, and new rulers take its lands.
