# Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Oleh seized Kyiv in —
a) 882  b) 988  c) 945
**Answer: a) 882**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Oleh sailed on Constantinople until Byzantium —
a) Signed trade treaties  b) Destroyed Kyiv  c) Baptised Rus
**Answer: a) Signed trade treaties**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ihor was killed by —
a) The Derevlians  b) The Pechenegs  c) His own druzhyna
**Answer: a) The Derevlians**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Ihor died in —
a) 945  b) 882  c) 972
**Answer: a) 945**
