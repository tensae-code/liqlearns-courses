# Lesson plan — Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Oleh seizes Kyiv in 882, kills Askold and Dir, subdues the neighbouring tribes and sails on Constantinople until Byzantium signs trade treaties; his successor Ihor keeps the tribute rounds going — until the Derevlians, squeezed once too often, kill him in 945.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Oleh takes Kyiv** — In 882 Oleh seizes Kyiv and kills Askold and Dir. He subdues the neighbouring tribes and sails on Constantinople, until Byzantium signs trade treaties with Rus.
2. **Ihor and the tribute rounds** — Oleh's successor Ihor keeps the tribute rounds going. But the Derevlians, squeezed once too often, kill him in 945 — tribute taken without limit ends in revolt.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Oleh's story in order: Kyiv seized in 882, Askold and Dir killed, tribes subdued, Constantinople sailed on, trade treaties signed.
2. Tell how Ihor died: killed in 945 by the Derevlians, squeezed once too often on the tribute rounds.
