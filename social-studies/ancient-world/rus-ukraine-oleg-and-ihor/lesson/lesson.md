# Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Oleh seizes Kyiv in 882, kills Askold and Dir, subdues the neighbouring tribes and sails on Constantinople until Byzantium signs trade treaties; his successor Ihor keeps the tribute rounds going — until the Derevlians, squeezed once too often, kill him in 945. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Oleh takes Kyiv

In 882 Oleh seizes Kyiv and kills Askold and Dir. He subdues the neighbouring tribes and sails on Constantinople, until Byzantium signs trade treaties with Rus.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ihor and the tribute rounds

Oleh's successor Ihor keeps the tribute rounds going. But the Derevlians, squeezed once too often, kill him in 945 — tribute taken without limit ends in revolt.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Rus-Ukraine under Oleh and Ihor in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Tell Oleh's story in order: Kyiv seized in 882, Askold and Dir killed, tribes subdued, Constantinople sailed on, trade treaties signed.
2. Tell how Ihor died: killed in 945 by the Derevlians, squeezed once too often on the tribute rounds.
