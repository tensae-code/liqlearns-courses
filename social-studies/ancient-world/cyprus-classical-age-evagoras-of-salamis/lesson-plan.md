# Lesson plan — Cyprus Classical Age — Evagoras of Salamis

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Cyprus Classical Age — Evagoras of Salamis
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Cyprus, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Evagoras of Salamis rebuilt his city, courted Athens and fought the Persian Great King for a decade — and sea power, the trireme above all, decided Cyprus's fate.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **'And a dead man wins' — the trireme, a powerful warship (475–325 BC)** — sea power decides Cyprus's fate, and the trireme, rowed by free crews, becomes the weapon every king must have ('And a dead man wins' — the trireme, a powerful warship (475–325 BC))
2. **Evagoras and his plans for Cyprus; Evagoras's war against the Persians** — Evagoras of Salamis rebuilds his city, courts Athens, then fights the Great King for a decade before a negotiated peace leaves him king (Evagoras and his plans for Cyprus; Evagoras's war against the Persians)
3. **Masters of the seas** — Cypriot ships and sailors serve, and sometimes defy, the great powers contesting the eastern Mediterranean (Masters of the seas)
4. **Daily life — how the ancients dressed; symposia, the diet of the ancients** — clothes, banquets and everyday food show a Greek-style city life lived Cypriot-fashion (Daily life — how the ancients dressed; symposia, the diet of the ancients)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
