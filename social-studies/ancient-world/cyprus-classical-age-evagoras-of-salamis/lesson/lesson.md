# Cyprus Classical Age — Evagoras of Salamis

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: evagoras of Salamis rebuilt his city, courted Athens and fought the Persian Great King for a decade — and sea power, the trireme above all, decided Cyprus's fate. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — 'And a dead man wins' — the trireme, a powerful warship (475–325 BC)

Sea power decides Cyprus's fate, and the trireme, rowed by free crews, becomes the weapon every king must have.

## Sub lesson 2 — Evagoras and his plans for Cyprus; Evagoras's war against the Persians

Evagoras of Salamis rebuilds his city, courts Athens, then fights the Great King for a decade before a negotiated peace leaves him king.

## Sub lesson 3 — Masters of the seas

Cypriot ships and sailors serve, and sometimes defy, the great powers contesting the eastern Mediterranean.

## Sub lesson 4 — Daily life — how the ancients dressed; symposia, the diet of the ancients

Clothes, banquets and everyday food show a Greek-style city life lived Cypriot-fashion.
