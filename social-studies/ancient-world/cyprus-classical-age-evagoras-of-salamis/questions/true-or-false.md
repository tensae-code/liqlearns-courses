# Cyprus Classical Age — Evagoras of Salamis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Evagoras courted Athens as an ally against Persia.
**Answer: True** — he rebuilds his city and courts Athens.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Cypriot ships and sailors never served foreign powers.
**Answer: False** — they serve, and sometimes defy, the great powers.
