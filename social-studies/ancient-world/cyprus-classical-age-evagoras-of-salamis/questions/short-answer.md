# Cyprus Classical Age — Evagoras of Salamis — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did every king need triremes?
**Answer: Sea power decided Cyprus's fate, and the trireme, rowed by free crews, was the weapon that won at sea (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Describe Evagoras's plan for Cyprus.
**Answer: He rebuilt Salamis, courted Athens as an ally, then fought Persia for a decade before a negotiated peace left him king (any close wording).**
