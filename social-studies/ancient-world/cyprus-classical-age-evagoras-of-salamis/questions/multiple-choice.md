# Cyprus Classical Age — Evagoras of Salamis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The powerful warship of the age was the —
a) trireme  b) longship  c) steamboat
**Answer: a) trireme** — the trireme, rowed by free crews.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Evagoras fought a decade-long war against —
a) the Persian Great King  b) Rome  c) the Phoenicians
**Answer: a) the Persian Great King** — he fights the Great King for a decade.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Evagoras's war ended with —
a) a negotiated peace that left him king  b) his exile  c) the fall of Salamis
**Answer: a) a negotiated peace that left him king** — a negotiated peace leaves him king.
