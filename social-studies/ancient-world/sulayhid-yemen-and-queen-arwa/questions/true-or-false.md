# The Sulayhid state (439-532 AH) — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ali bin Muhammad al-Sulayhi, seasoned guarding the Hajj road and preaching the Fatimid call with Cairo's leave, declares himself from Haraz, defeats the Zaydis of Saada, Al Hatim of Sanaa, Al Zari' of Aden and Bani Najah of Zabid and unites Yemen to Mecca's doorstep; his killer Sa'id al-Ahwal captures his widow Asma, his son al-Mukarram storms Sanaa and frees her, moves the capital to Jibla on his wife Sayyida Arwa's advice, and when illness takes him Arwa rules Yemen herself — the first woman to rule Yemen after Islam — until her death ends the state.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Sulayhid state (439-532 AH)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Sulayhid state (439-532 AH)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** among the models of women's rising roles — Aisha the scholar and teacher, women soldiers in the armies, political guides stronger in counsel than the caliph himself — the book names Yemen's own Sayyida bint Ahmad al-Sulayhi, Queen Arwa, who ruled the Sulayhid state and stands among the most famous kings of Yemen in Islamic history, beside Egypt's Shajar al-Durr.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The development of women's roles: the ruling queen (Unit 3 Lesson 3, fifth part)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The development of women's roles: the ruling queen (Unit 3 Lesson 3, fifth part)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the Sulayhids unite Yemen under one leadership for nearly a century, and Queen Arwa rules almost fifty years with justice, building the Jibla mosque and some twenty-two mosques, endowing scholars' and students' salaries, while Sulayhid roads are cut through mountains with pillars, arches and bridges that still stand.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Yemeni states that contributed: the Sulayhid state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الثاني (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Yemeni states that contributed: the Sulayhid state (Unit 4 Lesson 2)
