# Christianity comes to the Belarusian lands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Christianity on the territory of Belarus  b) The glacier in Europe  c) The settlement of the Indo-Europeans
**Answer: a) Christianity on the territory of Belarus** — this part tells christianity on the territory of Belarus

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) bishoprics at Polotsk and Turov, St Sophia's cathedral and Euphrosyne of Polotsk's cross carry the new faith and book culture into the towns  b) an ice sheet kilometres thick buries northern Europe, and life crowds into the ice-free south until the warming begins  c) steppe horsemen between the Danube and the Volga spread their languages from India to the Atlantic, becoming Balts, Slavs, Germans and others
**Answer: a)** — bishoprics at Polotsk and Turov, St Sophia's cathedral and Euphrosyne of Polotsk's cross carry the new faith and book culture into the towns

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Christianity in Eastern Europe  b) Settlement after the ice — the post-glacial period  c) The Balts — the pre-Slavic population
**Answer: a) Christianity in Eastern Europe** — this part tells christianity in Eastern Europe

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) the faith spreads slowly against pagan resistance and splits in 1054 into Orthodox East and Catholic West, with Belarus on the seam  b) as the ice retreats ~10,000 years ago, hunters and fishers follow the rivers north and settle the whole land  c) Baltic tribes work iron, farm the clearings and fill the land so completely that its river names stay Baltic to this day
**Answer: a)** — the faith spreads slowly against pagan resistance and splits in 1054 into Orthodox East and Catholic West, with Belarus on the seam
