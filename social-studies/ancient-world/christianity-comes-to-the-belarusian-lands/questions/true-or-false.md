# Christianity comes to the Belarusian lands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bishoprics at Polotsk and Turov, St Sophia's cathedral and Euphrosyne of Polotsk's cross carry the new faith and book culture into the towns
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Christianity on the territory of Belarus

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Atlas: History of Belarus, Grade 6* is about The glacier in Europe.
**Answer: False** — it is about Christianity on the territory of Belarus

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The faith spreads slowly against pagan resistance and splits in 1054 into Orthodox East and Catholic West, with Belarus on the seam
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Christianity in Eastern Europe

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Atlas: History of Belarus, Grade 6* is about Settlement after the ice — the post-glacial period.
**Answer: False** — it is about Christianity in Eastern Europe
