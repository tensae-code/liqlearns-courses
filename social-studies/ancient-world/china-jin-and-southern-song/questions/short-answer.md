# China Jin And Southern Song — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Jin confronts Southern Song.
**Answer: Aguda's Jurchen destroy Liao in 1125, then turn on their Song allies; two Song emperors are carried off, Kaifeng falls in 1127, and a Jin general marvels that a few thousand men on the Yellow River bank could have stopped him (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 8: Jin confronts Southern Song, printed p. 37.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (2).
**Answer: Jin destroys Liao and Northern Song, then the Mongols destroy Jin, Xia and Southern Song in turn (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 10: Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (2), printed p. 58.**
