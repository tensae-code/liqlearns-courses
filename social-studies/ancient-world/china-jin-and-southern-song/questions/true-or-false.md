# China Jin And Southern Song — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aguda's Jurchen destroy Liao in 1125, then turn on their Song allies; two Song emperors are carried off, Kaifeng falls in 1127, and a Jin general marvels that a few thousand men on the Yellow River bank could have stopped him.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Jin confronts Southern Song

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Jin confronts Southern Song

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Jin destroys Liao and Northern Song, then the Mongols destroy Jin, Xia and Southern Song in turn.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (2)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Liao, Xia, Jin and Yuan rule (2)
