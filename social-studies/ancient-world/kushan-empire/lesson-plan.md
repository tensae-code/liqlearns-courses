# Lesson plan — The Kushan Empire

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Kushan Empire
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Afghanistan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Rabatak inscription recites the Kushan royal line — Kujula Kadphises, Vima Takto, Vima Kadphises, Kanishka — and names the empire's furthest cities
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 13: Do you know the Kushan dynasty?** — the Rabatak inscription recites the Kushan royal line — Kujula Kadphises, Vima Takto, Vima Kadphises, Kanishka — and names the empire's furthest cities
2. **Lesson 14: Begram, political centre of the Kushan empire** — the Kushans rule their vast empire from two political centres, Peshawar and Begram, and Begram's hoard shows the trade of three worlds meeting
3. **Lesson 15: Hadda, cultural centre of the Kushan empire** — Hadda, ten kilometres south-west of Jalalabad, is remembered by travellers and pilgrims as one of ancient Afghanistan's great cultural centres, rich in temples and sculpture
4. **Lesson 16: Bamiyan, religious centre of the Kushan empire** — Bamiyan, registered as world heritage in 2003, roots its painted caves and colossal Buddhas in the Kushan age, when it was the empire's house of faith
5. **Lesson 14: The Kushan empire** — the Kushans raise the land's greatest ancient empire, its line recited in the Rabatak inscription
6. **Lesson 15: Begram, capital of the Kushan empire** — Begram shares imperial rule with Peshawar and hoards the trade goods of three worlds
7. **Lesson 16: Hadda, cultural centre of the Kushan empire** — Hadda by Jalalabad flourishes as the empire's cultural heart
8. **Lesson 17: Bamiyan, religious centre of the Kushans** — Bamiyan's painted caves and colossal Buddhas make it the empire's house of faith
