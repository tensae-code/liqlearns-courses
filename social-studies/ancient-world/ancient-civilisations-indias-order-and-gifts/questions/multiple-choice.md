# Ancient civilisations — India's order and gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The FIRST rank in India's order was the —
a) Sudra  b) Vaishya  c) Brahmin  d) Kshatriya
**Answer: c) Brahmin** — Priests and nobles stood first.
**2.** The taxpayers of the order were the —
a) Vaishya — farmers and craftsmen  b) Brahmin  c) Slaves  d) Kshatriya
**Answer: a) Vaishya — farmers and craftsmen** — Third rank, carrying the tax burden.
**3.** India's mathematical gifts include —
a) The compass  b) Zero, algebra and the decimal use  c) Gunpowder  d) The 60-minute hour
**Answer: b) Zero, algebra and the decimal use** — The Mesopotamian hour and China's compass belong to other lessons.
**4.** Beneath the four ranks stood —
a) Foreign merchants  b) Slaves and servants, without rights  c) Priests  d) Soldiers
**Answer: b) Slaves and servants, without rights** — The diagram's base, outside the ranks.
