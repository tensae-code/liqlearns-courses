# Ancient civilisations — India's order and gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Kshatriya were the soldier rank, second in the order.
**Answer: True** — Soldiers stood second, below the Brahmin.
**2.** A person's rank in this order was decided by descent.
**Answer: True** — By descent and colour, fixed from birth.
**3.** Soap and glass making are listed among India's gifts.
**Answer: True** — Alongside dyeing, leather work, iron smelting and more.
