# Ancient civilisations — India's order and gifts — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** The lowest citizens, fourth in rank, were the ____.
**Answer: Sudra**
**2.** India's writings in the old learned language are called ____ writings.
**Answer: Sanskrit**
**3.** In mathematics India gave the world ____, algebra and the decimal use.
**Answer: zero**
