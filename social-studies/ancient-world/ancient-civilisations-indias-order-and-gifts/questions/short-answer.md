# Ancient civilisations — India's order and gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the four ranks in order.
**Answer:** Brahmin (priests and nobles), Kshatriya (soldiers), Vaishya (farmers and craftsmen, taxpayers), Sudra (the lowest citizens) — with slaves and servants beneath, without rights.
**2.** Name five gifts of ancient India.
**Answer:** Any five of: cloth-dyeing, leather work, soap, glass making, iron smelting, pottery, architecture, sculpture, Sanskrit writings, plant medicines, zero, algebra, the decimal use.
