# Ancient civilisations — India's order and gifts

## Do it first

Imagine a society where your grandfather's work decides your work, and your place is fixed before you are born. Write one thing that would be good about such an order, and one thing that would be unjust. India lived inside that order — here it is, exactly as its own book draws it.

## The four ranks

India's society was divided **by descent and colour into four ranks**, as the diagram prints them. **First, the Brahmin** — priests and nobles. **Second, the Kshatriya** — soldiers. **Third, the Vaishya** — farmers and craftsmen, the taxpayers. **Fourth, the Sudra** — the lowest citizens. And **beneath them all stood slaves and servants, without rights**. This is a historical diagram of how that society was ordered — learn it as it stands, rank by rank.

## India's gifts

Against that fixed order, set what India made and gave: **cloth-dyeing, leather work, soap and glass making, iron smelting, pottery, architecture and sculpture, Sanskrit writings, and plant medicines**. And in mathematics, three gifts the whole world still uses every day: **zero, algebra, and the decimal use**. The numbers you write — 0, 1, 2, 3 in tens — travel from India.
