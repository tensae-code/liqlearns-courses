# Video script — Ancient civilisations — India's order and gifts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

India stood in four ranks.
Brahmin prayed. Kshatriya fought.
Vaishya farmed and paid. Sudra served.
Below them, slaves with no rights.
But India's gifts were for everyone:
soap, glass, iron — and zero. Zero itself.
