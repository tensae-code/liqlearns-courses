# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — India's order and gifts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Build the ladder
Lay four strips of paper as steps, widest at the bottom. Write one rank on each with its people, and a fifth strip underneath for those without rights.

## Zero hunt
Find zero at work: a price tag, a phone number, a temperature. Write down three places zero serves you today.
