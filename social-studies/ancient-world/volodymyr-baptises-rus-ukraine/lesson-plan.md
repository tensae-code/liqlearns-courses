# Lesson plan — Volodymyr baptises Rus-Ukraine

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Volodymyr baptises Rus-Ukraine
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Volodymyr wins the throne in a brothers' war, rings the steppe frontier with fortress towns, and in 988 baptises Rus in the Dnipro — churches, schools and a tithe follow, and the young state enters Christian Europe.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Volodymyr wins the throne** — Volodymyr wins the throne in a brothers' war. He rings the steppe frontier with fortress towns, guarding Rus against the steppe.
2. **988 — the baptism of Rus** — In 988 Volodymyr baptises Rus in the Dnipro. Churches, schools and a tithe follow — and the young state enters Christian Europe.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Rus-Ukraine under Olha and Sviatoslav. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell how Volodymyr won the throne and guarded the frontier: a brothers' war won, fortress towns ringing the steppe frontier.
2. Tell what happened in 988 and what followed: Rus baptised in the Dnipro; churches, schools and a tithe; Rus enters Christian Europe.
