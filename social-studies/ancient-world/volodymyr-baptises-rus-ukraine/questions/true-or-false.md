# Volodymyr baptises Rus-Ukraine — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Volodymyr baptised Rus in the Dnipro in 988.
**Answer: True — The baptism of 988 is the centre of this lesson.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The baptism cut Rus off from Christian Europe.
**Answer: False — It brought the young state into Christian Europe.**
