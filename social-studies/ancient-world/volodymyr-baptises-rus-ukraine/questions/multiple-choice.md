# Volodymyr baptises Rus-Ukraine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Volodymyr won the throne in —
a) A brothers' war  b) An election by the viche  c) A gift from Byzantium
**Answer: a) A brothers' war**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Against the steppe, Volodymyr built —
a) A ring of fortress towns  b) A fleet of ships  c) A wall of wood only around Kyiv
**Answer: a) A ring of fortress towns**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rus was baptised in —
a) 988  b) 882  c) 1240
**Answer: a) 988**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After the baptism came —
a) Churches, schools and a tithe  b) The Ruska Pravda at once  c) The Mongol invasion
**Answer: a) Churches, schools and a tithe**
