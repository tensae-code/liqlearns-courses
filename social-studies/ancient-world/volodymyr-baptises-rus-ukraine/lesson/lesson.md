# Volodymyr baptises Rus-Ukraine

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Volodymyr wins the throne in a brothers' war, rings the steppe frontier with fortress towns, and in 988 baptises Rus in the Dnipro — churches, schools and a tithe follow, and the young state enters Christian Europe. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Volodymyr wins the throne

Volodymyr wins the throne in a brothers' war. He rings the steppe frontier with fortress towns, guarding Rus against the steppe.

## Sub lesson 2 — 988 — the baptism of Rus

In 988 Volodymyr baptises Rus in the Dnipro. Churches, schools and a tithe follow — and the young state enters Christian Europe.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Volodymyr baptises Rus-Ukraine in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.

## Do It Yourself

1. Tell how Volodymyr won the throne and guarded the frontier: a brothers' war won, fortress towns ringing the steppe frontier.
2. Tell what happened in 988 and what followed: Rus baptised in the Dnipro; churches, schools and a tithe; Rus enters Christian Europe.
