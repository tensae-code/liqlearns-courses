# Mithila civilisation of the Terai — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Mithila Civilisation.
**Answer: in the Terai, King Nimi's son Mithi gives Mithila its name and Janakpur its capital; over some 5,000 years (c. 12th century BCE to 8th century CE) the Maithili language, folk art painted with local materials, and festivals like Chhath and Jitiya grow up around the Ramayana memory of Janak and Sita (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 6* (Grade 6), Unit 5 Lesson 7: The Mithila Civilisation, printed p. 109.**
