# Mithila civilisation of the Terai — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in the Terai, King Nimi's son Mithi gives Mithila its name and Janakpur its capital; over some 5,000 years (c. 12th century BCE to 8th century CE) the Maithili language, folk art painted with local materials, and…

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 6* is about Our Sinja, Our Civilisation.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Mithila Civilisation
