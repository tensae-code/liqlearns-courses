## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 3: the twilight and fall of the Byzantine Empire
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Michael VIII Palaiologos wins Constantinople back in 1261, but the restored empire never recovers its trade or strength; civil wars invite the Ottomans in, and by the 1400s Byzantium is a city with an empire's memories — in 1453 Mehmed II's great army breaks the Theodosian walls and the last emperor, Constantine XI Dragash, dies in the defence; its scholars flee west, Moscow claims its mantle, and its fall pushes Europe to seek new sea roads

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2: the fall of Byzantium and its legacy
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Manzikert opens Anatolia to the Seljuks, the Fourth Crusade sacks Constantinople itself in 1204, and though Nicaea restores the empire in 1261 it shrinks to a city — Mehmed II takes it in 1453 and the last emperor dies on the walls; what survives is law, letters and the mission of Cyril and Methodius, while Moscow dreams of being the Third Rome
