**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 3: the twilight and fall of the Byzantine Empire.
**Answer: Michael VIII Palaiologos wins Constantinople back in 1261, but the restored empire never recovers its trade or strength; civil wars invite the Ottomans in, and by the 1400s Byzantium is a city with an empire's memories — in 1453 Mehmed II's great army breaks the Theodosian walls and the last emperor, Constantine XI Dragash, dies in the defence; its scholars flee west, Moscow claims its mantle, and its fall pushes Europe to seek new sea roads (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 2: the fall of Byzantium and its legacy.
**Answer: Manzikert opens Anatolia to the Seljuks, the Fourth Crusade sacks Constantinople itself in 1204, and though Nicaea restores the empire in 1261 it shrinks to a city — Mehmed II takes it in 1453 and the last emperor dies on the walls; what survives is law, letters and the mission of Cyril and Methodius, while Moscow dreams of being the Third Rome (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
