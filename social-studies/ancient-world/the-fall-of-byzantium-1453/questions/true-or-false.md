# The Fall Of Byzantium 1453 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Michael VIII Palaiologos wins Constantinople back in 1261, but the restored empire never recovers its trade or strength; civil wars invite the Ottomans in, and by the 1400s Byzantium is a city with an empire's memories — in 1453 Mehmed II's great army breaks the Theodosian walls and the last emperor, Constantine XI Dragash, dies in the defence; its scholars flee west, Moscow claims its mantle, and its fall pushes Europe to seek new sea roads.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 3: the twilight and fall of the Byzantine Empire

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istorija 7 (History 7), Grade 7* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 3: the twilight and fall of the Byzantine Empire

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Manzikert opens Anatolia to the Seljuks, the Fourth Crusade sacks Constantinople itself in 1204, and though Nicaea restores the empire in 1261 it shrinks to a city — Mehmed II takes it in 1453 and the last emperor dies on the walls; what survives is law, letters and the mission of Cyril and Methodius, while Moscow dreams of being the Third Rome.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 2: the fall of Byzantium and its legacy
