## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 3: the twilight and fall of the Byzantine Empire

Michael VIII Palaiologos wins Constantinople back in 1261, but the restored empire never recovers its trade or strength; civil wars invite the Ottomans in, and by the 1400s Byzantium is a city with an empire's memories — in 1453 Mehmed II's great army breaks the Theodosian walls and the last emperor, Constantine XI Dragash, dies in the defence; its scholars flee west, Moscow claims its mantle, and its fall pushes Europe to seek new sea roads
