**Answer: Otto I, crowned emperor in 962, binds the Church to his crown; two centuries of empire-versus-papacy explode when Gregory VII and Henry IV fight over who appoints bishops, and the emperor stands barefoot in the snow at Canossa begging pardon — yet by the Golden Bull of 1356 the empire is a league of near-independent princes (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
