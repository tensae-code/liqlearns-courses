# Bhutan — the many names of the land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter 5.2 Different names given to our country  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter 5.2 Different names given to our country** — a land in darkness before Buddhism is Lhomon, then the four-gates land, the land of medicinal herbs and cypresses, until Druk Yul the Thunder Dragon land and Bhutan the high land give it its lasting names

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Chapter 5.2 Different names given to our country  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Chapter 5.2 Different names given to our country** — the book's own heading for this part.
