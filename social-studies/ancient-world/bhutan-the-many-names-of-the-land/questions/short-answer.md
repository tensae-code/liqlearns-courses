# Bhutan — the many names of the land — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter 5.2 Different names given to our country.
**Answer: a land in darkness before Buddhism is Lhomon, then the four-gates land, the land of medicinal herbs and cypresses, until Druk Yul the Thunder Dragon land and Bhutan the high land give it its lasting names (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter Two, Ancient Names.
**Answer: toponymy unpacks Mon/Mon Yul, Ri Drag Gi Yul, Lho Mon Kha Zhi of the four gateways, Menjong of the medicinal herbs and Tsenden Köpaijong, then shows outsiders fumbling Bottan/Boutan until George Bogle fixes 'Bhutan' apart from Tibet in the late 1700s (any close wording).**
