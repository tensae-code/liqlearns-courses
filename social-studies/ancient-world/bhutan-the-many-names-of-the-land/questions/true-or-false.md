# Bhutan — the many names of the land — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a land in darkness before Buddhism is Lhomon, then the four-gates land, the land of medicinal herbs and cypresses, until Druk Yul the Thunder Dragon land and Bhutan the high land give it its lasting names
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter 5.2 Different names given to our country).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** toponymy unpacks Mon/Mon Yul, Ri Drag Gi Yul, Lho Mon Kha Zhi of the four gateways, Menjong of the medicinal herbs and Tsenden Köpaijong, then shows outsiders fumbling Bottan/Boutan until George Bogle fixes 'Bhutan' apart from Tibet in the late 1700s
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Two, Ancient Names).
