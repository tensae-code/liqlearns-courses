# Video script — Bhutan — the many names of the land

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bhutan book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

a land in darkness before Buddhism is Lhomon, then the four-gates land, the land of medicinal herbs and cypresses, until Druk Yul the Thunder Dragon land and Bhutan the high land give it its lasting names; toponymy unpacks Mon/Mon Yul, Ri Drag Gi Yul, Lho Mon Kha Zhi of the four gateways, Menjong of the medicinal herbs and Tsenden Köpaijong, then shows outsiders fumbling Bottan/Boutan until George Bogle fixes 'Bhutan' apart from Tibet in the late 1700s
