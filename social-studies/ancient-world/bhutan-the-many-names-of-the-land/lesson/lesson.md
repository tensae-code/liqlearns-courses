# Bhutan — the many names of the land

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: a land in darkness before Buddhism is Lhomon, then the four-gates land, the land of medicinal herbs and cypresses, until Druk Yul the Thunder Dragon land and Bhutan the high land give it its lasting names; toponymy unpacks Mon/Mon Yul, Ri Drag Gi Yul, Lho Mon Kha Zhi of the four gateways, Menjong of the medicinal herbs and Tsenden Köpaijong, then shows outsiders fumbling Bottan/Boutan until George Bogle fixes 'Bhutan' apart from Tibet in the late 1700s. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
