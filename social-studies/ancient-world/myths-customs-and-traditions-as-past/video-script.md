# Video script — Myths, customs and traditions as the past

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Myths are traditional tales of gods and heroes, customs bind a community, and traditions are the past handed down from hand to hand.

Myth comes from Greek mythos, a tale of gods and extraordinary beings; customs bind a community and traditions are handed down, and children album their community's traditions and research others across Colombia.
