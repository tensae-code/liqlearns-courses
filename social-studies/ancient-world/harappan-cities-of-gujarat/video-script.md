# Video script — Harappan cities of Gujarat — Dholavira and Lothal

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dholavira splits into three walled parts and displays giant letters of the script in white stone; Lothal's great tank may be a dockyard, its warehouse full of seals and clay sealings that proved a goods-bag had arrived untampered.
