# Harappan cities of Gujarat — Dholavira and Lothal

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Dholavira and Lothal show the Harappan world in Gujarat: a city in three walled parts with giant letters in white stone, and a port whose warehouse seals proved goods arrived untampered. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — A closer look — Dholavira and Lothal; seals and sealings

Dholavira splits into three walled parts and displays giant letters of the script in white stone; Lothal's great tank may be a dockyard, its warehouse full of seals and clay sealings that proved a goods-bag had arrived untampered.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: A closer look — Dholavira and Lothal; seals and sealings. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
