# Ecuador's first peoples and chiefdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Raíces de la sociedad  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Raíces de la sociedad** — hunter-gatherers settle, farm and raise chiefdoms (señoríos) across coast, sierra and Amazonia, trading and allying long before any empire arrives

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Sección 1 Tema 1: Raíces de la sociedad  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Sección 1 Tema 1: Raíces de la sociedad** — the book's own heading for this part.
