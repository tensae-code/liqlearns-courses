# Bhutan — Guru Rinpoche brings Buddhism — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the lotus-born Padmasambhava enters 8th-century Bhutan, heals the sick king of Bumthang by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo, leaves his body-print at Kurjey and hides treasures for later discoverers, and Buddhism flourishes across the country
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter 6.3-6.4 Guru Rinpoche).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** invited c.750 by the ailing King Sindha of Bumthang, Guru Rinpoche travels via Nabji, subdues the local spirits, plants tantric Buddhism, and leaves Bhutan itself a beyul — a hidden sanctuary land seeded with his treasures and ringed by his sacred ney from Taktshang to Singye Dzong
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter One, Guru Rinpoche, Idea of Beyul and Terma, Significance of Sacred Sites).
