# Bhutan — Guru Rinpoche brings Buddhism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter 6.3-6.4 Guru Rinpoche.
**Answer: the lotus-born Padmasambhava enters 8th-century Bhutan, heals the sick king of Bumthang by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo, leaves his body-print at Kurjey and hides treasures for later discoverers, and Buddhism flourishes across the country (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter One, Guru Rinpoche, Idea of Beyul and Terma, Significance of Sacred Sites.
**Answer: invited c.750 by the ailing King Sindha of Bumthang, Guru Rinpoche travels via Nabji, subdues the local spirits, plants tantric Buddhism, and leaves Bhutan itself a beyul — a hidden sanctuary land seeded with his treasures and ringed by his sacred ney from Taktshang to Singye Dzong (any close wording).**
