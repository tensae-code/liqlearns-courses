# Video script — Bhutan — Guru Rinpoche brings Buddhism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bhutan book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

the lotus-born Padmasambhava enters 8th-century Bhutan, heals the sick king of Bumthang by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo, leaves his body-print at Kurjey and hides treasures for later discoverers, and Buddhism flourishes across the country; invited c.750 by the ailing King Sindha of Bumthang, Guru Rinpoche travels via Nabji, subdues the local spirits, plants tantric Buddhism, and leaves Bhutan itself a beyul — a hidden sanctuary land seeded with his treasures and ringed by his sacred ney from Taktshang to Singye Dzong
