# Lesson plan — Bhutan — Guru Rinpoche brings Buddhism

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Bhutan — Guru Rinpoche brings Buddhism
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Bhutan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the lotus-born Padmasambhava enters 8th-century Bhutan, heals the sick king of Bumthang by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo, leaves his body-print at Kurjey and hides treasures for later discoverers, and Buddhism flourishes across the country; invited c.750 by the ailing King Sindha of Bumthang, Guru Rinpoche travels via Nabji, subdues the local spirits, plants tantric Buddhism, and leaves Bhutan itself a beyul — a hidden sanctuary land seeded with his treasures and ringed by his sacred ney from Taktshang to Singye Dzong
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
