# Bhutan — Guru Rinpoche brings Buddhism

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the lotus-born Padmasambhava enters 8th-century Bhutan, heals the sick king of Bumthang by subduing the local deity Shelging Karpo, leaves his body-print at Kurjey and hides treasures for later discoverers, and Buddhism flourishes across the country; invited c.750 by the ailing King Sindha of Bumthang, Guru Rinpoche travels via Nabji, subdues the local spirits, plants tantric Buddhism, and leaves Bhutan itself a beyul — a hidden sanctuary land seeded with his treasures and ringed by his sacred ney from Taktshang to Singye Dzong. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
