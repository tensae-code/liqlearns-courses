# The history of maps — Egypt and the Arab scholars

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Maps begin with the ancient Egyptians, are refined by Arab scholars and their instruments, and end as the satellite maps in a traveller's hand. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 1 Lesson 3: the development of the map

Ancient Egyptians draw some of the first maps, Arab scholars refine map-making, Maryam al-Asturlabi's astrolabes guide travellers, and hand-drawn maps give way to GPS and satellite maps.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Unit 1 Lesson 3: the development of the map. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
