# Founding Of Moldavia Bogdan I — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Dragoș's first voivodate, Bogdan I crosses from Maramureș in 1359 and founds an independent Moldavia, with Baia as its first capital.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 10: Întemeierea Țării Moldovei. Bogdan I

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 10: Întemeierea Țării Moldovei. Bogdan I

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bogdan rebels against Maramureș's lost autonomy, crosses the Carpathians in 1359, defeats Balc and becomes voivode of an independent Moldavia.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Moldovei

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor şi universală: Manual pentru clasa a VI-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Moldovei
