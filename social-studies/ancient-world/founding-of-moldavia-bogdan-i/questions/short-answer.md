# Founding Of Moldavia Bogdan I — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 10: Întemeierea Țării Moldovei. Bogdan I.
**Answer: after Dragoș's first voivodate, Bogdan I crosses from Maramureș in 1359 and founds an independent Moldavia, with Baia as its first capital (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* (Moldova), Tema 10: Întemeierea Țării Moldovei. Bogdan I, printed p. 28.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Moldovei.
**Answer: Bogdan rebels against Maramureș's lost autonomy, crosses the Carpathians in 1359, defeats Balc and becomes voivode of an independent Moldavia (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor şi universală: Manual pentru clasa a VI-a* (Moldova), Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Moldovei, printed p. 62.**
