Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea VII, Lecția 1: Criza Imperiului Roman.
**Answer: 235–284, soldiers proclaim their own emperors and the empire nearly dies; Constantine ends the persecutions with the Edict of Milan in 313 and Christianity becomes official (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
