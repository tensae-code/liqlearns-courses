# The Greek Language Three Thousand Years

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Greek has been spoken and written continuously for over three and a half thousand years, from Linear B to the child's own schoolbook As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 41b The language I speak

Greek has been spoken and written continuously for over three and a half thousand years, from Linear B to the child's own schoolbook

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Ch. 41b The language I speak — Greek has been spoken and written continuously for over three and a half thousand years, from Linear B to the child's own schoolbook. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
