# Video script — Ancient civilisations — Athens and democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Athens did something new under the sun.
The citizens themselves made the laws.
Not a king. Not a few rich men. The citizens.
Five hundred sat on the council — chosen by lot.
Under Pericles, democracy reached its peak.

---

Grade 9 story addition (Ethiopia Wave 4):

The map shatters into city-states — a hundred poleis, one language, one set of gods.

Athens raises the Parthenon. A theatre fills; the first drama plays.

Herodotus writes the wars; Thucydides weighs them. Greece invents how the past is told.
