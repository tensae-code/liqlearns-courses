# Ancient civilisations — Athens and democracy — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Cleisthenes legislated free speech and equality before the law.
**Answer: True** — Two of his great laws.
**2.** After 750 BC most Athenians crafted, fished and traded.
**Answer: True** — Workshop, boat and market people.
**3.** In Athens a small council made all laws without citizens.
**Answer: False** — The citizens themselves made law and policy.
