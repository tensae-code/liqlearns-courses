# Ancient civilisations — Athens and democracy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What did Cleisthenes give Athens?
**Answer:** He legislated free speech and equality before the law, and set up a council of 500 chosen yearly by lot.
**2.** How did Athenians mostly live after 750 BC?
**Answer:** They mostly crafted, fished and traded — a city of workshop, boat and market people.
