# Ancient civilisations — Athens and democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Athens stands in —
a) The Peloponnese  b) Attica, central-east Greece  c) Crete  d) Anatolia
**Answer: b) Attica, central-east Greece** — Sparta holds the southern Peloponnese.
**2.** Direct democracy means —
a) A king consults citizens  b) Citizens elect rulers to decide  c) Citizens themselves make law and policy  d) Soldiers decide
**Answer: c) Citizens themselves make law and policy** — Athens was the first in practice.
**3.** The council of 500 was chosen —
a) By the king  b) Yearly, by lot  c) By wealth  d) For life
**Answer: b) Yearly, by lot** — Cleisthenes' design, so no family could own a seat.
**4.** Athenian democracy peaked under —
a) Cleisthenes  b) Philip  c) Pericles (461–429 BC)  d) Minos
**Answer: c) Pericles (461–429 BC)** — Participation widened under him.

**5.** The Hellenes were united — but never —.
a) culturally, politically  b) by language, by trade  c) in religion, in sport
**Answer: a) culturally, politically** — One culture, many poleis.

**6.** A polis is —
a) an empire  b) a city-state: a town plus its villages and farmland  c) a temple
**Answer: b) a city-state: a town plus its villages and farmland** — The Greek city-state.

**7.** The best-known poleis are —
a) Rome and Carthage  b) Athens and Sparta  c) Memphis and Thebes
**Answer: b) Athens and Sparta** — Athens and Sparta.

**8.** The Parthenon is the book's example of Greek —
a) medicine  b) architecture  c) drama
**Answer: b) architecture** — Architecture's showpiece.

**9.** Thucydides wrote an unbiased account of —
a) the Persian Wars  b) the Peloponnesian War  c) the Trojan War
**Answer: b) the Peloponnesian War** — Herodotus took the Persian Wars; Thucydides the Peloponnesian.
**10. (Grade 11 depth)** What is Hellenistic?
a) Greece's questioning of justice and truth themselves
b) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
c) the Greek-spread world after Alexander, from Egypt to India
**Answer: c) the Greek-spread world after Alexander, from Egypt to India**
