# Ancient civilisations — Athens and democracy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Athens stands in —
a) The Peloponnese  b) Attica, central-east Greece  c) Crete  d) Anatolia
**Answer: b) Attica, central-east Greece** — Sparta holds the southern Peloponnese.
**2.** Direct democracy means —
a) A king consults citizens  b) Citizens elect rulers to decide  c) Citizens themselves make law and policy  d) Soldiers decide
**Answer: c) Citizens themselves make law and policy** — Athens was the first in practice.
**3.** The council of 500 was chosen —
a) By the king  b) Yearly, by lot  c) By wealth  d) For life
**Answer: b) Yearly, by lot** — Cleisthenes' design, so no family could own a seat.
**4.** Athenian democracy peaked under —
a) Cleisthenes  b) Philip  c) Pericles (461–429 BC)  d) Minos
**Answer: c) Pericles (461–429 BC)** — Participation widened under him.
