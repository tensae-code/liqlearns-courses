# Ancient civilisations — Athens and democracy — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Athens built history's first ____ democracy.
**Answer: direct**
**2.** The council of Athens held ____ members.
**Answer: 500**
**3.** ____ legislated free speech and equality before the law.
**Answer: Cleisthenes**
**4. (Grade 11 depth)** The term for 'Greece's questioning of justice and truth themselves' is ______.
**Answer: philosophy**
