# Ancient civilisations — Athens and democracy — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Say where Athens is (Attica, central-east Greece) and how its people lived after 750 BC.; Explain direct democracy, Cleisthenes' laws (free speech, equality before the law, council of 500 by lot), and democracy's peak under Pericles (461–429 BC).. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
