# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — Athens and democracy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Assembly in the yard
Gather a group outdoors as an 'assembly'. Put one real small question (which game, which song) to a direct vote — everyone present votes, majority decides. That is Athens' method, alive.

## The lot draw
Write each group member's name on slips, fold them, and draw five for a 'council'. Talk about what the lot does that an election cannot: no buying, no family grip.
