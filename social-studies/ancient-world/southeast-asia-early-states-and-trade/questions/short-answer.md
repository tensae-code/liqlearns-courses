**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 8.1, the development journey of ancient-to-medieval Southeast Asian civilisation.
**Answer: the lesson opens with Borobudur in Indonesia — in 1814 a group of Europeans discovers the temple complex buried under earth and vegetation, and everyone is astonished at its grandeur; over many decades UNESCO and scientists worldwide join its restoration, completed only in early 1983; Southeast Asia, a region of peninsulas and archipelagos stretching from the Pacific to the Indian Ocean, sits on the sea route joining the two oceans — a "crossroads", a bridge between the world's great civilisations — and its civilisation develops in three periods: from the centuries before the Common Era to the 7th century, the first states appear — Văn Lang–Âu Lạc, Phù Nam, the states of the lower Chao Phraya — and on the indigenous foundation the influence of Indian and Chinese civilisation shows clearly; from the 7th century to the late 15th, the feudal states form and flourish economically and politically and the region's culture develops brilliantly, selectively absorbing the essences of Indian and Chinese civilisation; from the 16th to the 19th century, Western capitalism invades and the feudal dynasties weaken, and under Western influence the civilisation undergoes important transformations, still achieving much in politics, culture, science and technique (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
