# Hands-on — Ancient Bengal — Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mound detective

List three things found in or on a settlement mound — pottery, coins, an inscription — and write what each one can prove about the people who left it.

## Village to city

Draw the same place twice: first a village of huts, then the walled city of Mahasthangarh. Label what changed: wall, streets, writing.

## Three witnesses

Make a chart with three rows — granary, inscription, copper plate — and write what each one testifies about Bengal's farming.

## A grant of land

Write your own copper-plate style grant: name a piece of land, its produce from the lesson's list, and who receives it.
