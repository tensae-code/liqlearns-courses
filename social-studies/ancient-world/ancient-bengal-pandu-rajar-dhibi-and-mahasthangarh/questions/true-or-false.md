# Ancient Bengal — Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh show villages growing into Bengal's first city.
**Answer: True** — the two sites together show that growth.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Ancient Bengal's farming is known only from stories, not from evidence in the ground.
**Answer: False** — granary rice, an inscription and copper plates all testify.
