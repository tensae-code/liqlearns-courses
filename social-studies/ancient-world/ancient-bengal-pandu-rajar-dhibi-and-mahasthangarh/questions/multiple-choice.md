# Ancient Bengal — Pandu Rajar Dhibi and Mahasthangarh — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mahasthangarh was —
a) A sea port  b) Bengal's first walled city, Pundranagar on the Karatoya  c) A Mughal fort
**Answer: b) Bengal's first walled city, Pundranagar on the Karatoya** — the walled city the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bengal's oldest Brahmi stone inscription (c. 300 BCE) is at —
a) Mahasthangarh  b) Pandu Rajar Dhibi  c) Dhaka
**Answer: a) Mahasthangarh** — the inscription's home.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The rice found near Pandu Rajar Dhibi was about —
a) 300 years old  b) 3,500 years old  c) 100 years old
**Answer: b) 3,500 years old** — the granary rice's age.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Copper-plate land grants name —
a) Ships and sails  b) Mango, betel, coconut and cotton  c) Coins only
**Answer: b) Mango, betel, coconut and cotton** — the produce the grants list.
