# Lesson plan — The Mongol World Empire

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Mongol World Empire
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chinggis's heirs rule the largest land empire the world has seen
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§12 The Mongols' world empire** — Chinggis's heirs rule the largest land empire the world has seen
2. **§13 Eurasia's political map after the Mongol conquests** — four khanates divide Eurasia and a new order settles over the steppe
3. **§12 The Mongol Empire forms** — Chinggis unites the steppe and his sons conquer half the world
4. **§13 The Mongol invasion's effect on Eurasia's political map** — khanates replace old states from China to Rus'
5. **5.1 Why did the Mongols win quick victories over their enemies?** — discipline, horses and siege engineers outmatch settled armies
6. **5.2 How much did the Mongols and conquered peoples change each other?** — conquerors adopt religions and scripts while subjects adopt Mongol order
7. **§12 Chinggis Khan; the Mongol Empire is created** — Temujin's kurultai of 1206 launches the largest land empire
8. **§13 The Mongols' military art** — decimal ranks, composite bows and feigned flight win battles before they start
9. **§14–15 Mongol campaigns' influence on Eurasia's ethnopolitical map** — peoples are moved, merged and renamed across the conquered steppe
10. **§12 Chinggis Khan** — Temujin's rise from orphan to world conqueror opens the Mongol age
11. **§13 The Mongol Empire's structure** — uluses, the yam post and the Yasa organise the giant empire
12. **§14 The Mongols' military art** — tumen discipline and siege trains defeat larger settled armies
13. **§15 The Mongol conquests' effect on Eurasia's political map** — new khanates and peoples settle over the conquered continent

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
