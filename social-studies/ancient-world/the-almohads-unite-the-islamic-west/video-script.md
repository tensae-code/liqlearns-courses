# Video script — The Almohads unite the Islamic West

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Morocco's story as its Grade 5 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The guide teaches the Almohad state's founding and stages: a mountain preacher's movement swallows the Almoravids' empire and forms an empire across the Maghreb, ruling from Tripoli to Seville under one creed.

The empire is known also by what it built: the lesson teaches the characteristics of Almohad architecture.
