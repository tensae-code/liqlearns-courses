# The Almohads unite the Islamic West — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Almohads ruled from Tripoli to Seville under one creed.
**Answer: True — that span is the story beat of the lesson.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Almohads came before the Almoravids.
**Answer: False — they swallowed the Almoravids' empire, so they came after.**
