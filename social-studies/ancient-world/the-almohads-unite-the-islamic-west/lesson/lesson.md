# The Almohads unite the Islamic West

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Morocco's long story as one line: first Moroccans, Amazigh, then dynasty after dynasty. This lesson is one chapter of that line, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Founding and stages — an empire across the Maghreb

The guide teaches the Almohad state's founding and stages: a mountain preacher's movement swallows the Almoravids' empire and forms an empire across the Maghreb, ruling from Tripoli to Seville under one creed.
