# Rus-Ukraine — how the land was ruled and lived

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: prince, boyars, druzhyna and viche at the top; free smerds, dependent zakupy and kholopy below; towns of craftsmen and merchants, villages of farmers — how the golden-age state actually worked day to day. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Who ruled

At the top stand the prince, the boyars, the druzhyna and the viche. This is how the golden-age state was ruled — power shared between a prince, his men, his nobles and the town assembly.

## Sub lesson 2 — Who worked

Below stand free smerds, dependent zakupy and kholopy. Towns hold craftsmen and merchants; villages hold farmers — the everyday life the state rested on.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Rus-Ukraine — how the land was ruled and lived in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
