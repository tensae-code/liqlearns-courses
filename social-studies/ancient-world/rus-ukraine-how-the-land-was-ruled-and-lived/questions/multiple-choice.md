# Rus-Ukraine — how the land was ruled and lived — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At the top of Rus-Ukraine stood —
a) Prince, boyars, druzhyna and viche  b) Kholopy and zakupy  c) Only the church
**Answer: a) Prince, boyars, druzhyna and viche**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The viche is —
a) The town assembly  b) The prince's war band  c) A kind of tax
**Answer: a) The town assembly**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Free farming people were called —
a) Smerds  b) Boyars  c) Kholopy
**Answer: a) Smerds**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Towns were filled with —
a) Craftsmen and merchants  b) Only soldiers  c) Only farmers
**Answer: a) Craftsmen and merchants**
