# Rus-Ukraine — how the land was ruled and lived — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zakupy were fully free with no dependence.
**Answer: False — Zakupy are dependent, between free smerds and kholopy.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Villages were where the farmers lived and worked.
**Answer: True — The book sets villages of farmers below the towns of craftsmen and merchants.**
