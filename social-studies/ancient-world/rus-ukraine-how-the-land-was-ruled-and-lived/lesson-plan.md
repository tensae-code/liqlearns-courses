# Lesson plan — Rus-Ukraine — how the land was ruled and lived

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Rus-Ukraine — how the land was ruled and lived
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** prince, boyars, druzhyna and viche at the top; free smerds, dependent zakupy and kholopy below; towns of craftsmen and merchants, villages of farmers — how the golden-age state actually worked day to day.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Who ruled** — At the top stand the prince, the boyars, the druzhyna and the viche. This is how the golden-age state was ruled — power shared between a prince, his men, his nobles and the town assembly.
2. **Who worked** — Below stand free smerds, dependent zakupy and kholopy. Towns hold craftsmen and merchants; villages hold farmers — the everyday life the state rested on.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name who stood at the top of Rus-Ukraine: prince, boyars, druzhyna and viche.
2. Name the layers below — free smerds, dependent zakupy and kholopy — and say who filled the towns and the villages.
