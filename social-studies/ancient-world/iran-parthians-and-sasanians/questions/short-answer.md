# Iran — Parthians and Sasanians — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Parthians and Sasanians.
**Answer: Surena's horse-archers destroy Crassus at Carrhae; Ardashir overthrows the Parthians in 224 and founds the Sasanians; Shapur captures the Roman emperor Valerian — then, after Khosrow Parviz's fall and court chaos, the empire dies at Nahavand and Yazdgerd III is killed in 651.
