# Video script — Iran — Parthians and Sasanians

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iran's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Surena's horse-archers destroy Crassus at Carrhae; Ardashir overthrows the Parthians in 224 and founds the Sasanians; Shapur captures the Roman emperor Valerian — then, after Khosrow Parviz's fall and court chaos, the empire dies at Nahavand and Yazdgerd III is killed in 651.
