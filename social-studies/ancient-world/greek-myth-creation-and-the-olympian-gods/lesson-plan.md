# Lesson plan — Greek Myth Creation And The Olympian Gods

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Greek Myth Creation And The Olympian Gods
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Chaos gives birth to Earth and Sky, the Titans rule, and Zeus leads the younger gods to overthrow them
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 1, ch. 1 The Titanomachy** — Chaos gives birth to Earth and Sky, the Titans rule, and Zeus leads the younger gods to overthrow them
2. **Unit 1, ch. 2 The creation of the world and the gods / The gods of Olympus** — the twelve immortal gods settle on Olympus drinking nectar, each with a realm and symbols children learn to read in pictures
3. **Unit 1, ch. 3 The goddesses of Olympus** — Hera, Athena, Aphrodite and the other goddesses take their places and powers beside the gods, and Athena wins Athens from Poseidon with the gift of the olive tree
4. **Unit 1, ch. 4 Prometheus, Pandora, Deucalion and Pyrrha** — Prometheus steals fire for humans and is chained, Pandora opens the jar of evils, and Deucalion and Pyrrha survive the flood to remake humankind from stones
5. **Unit 1, ch. 1 Titanomachy exercises** — children trace everyday words back to Titan and god names, re-entering the war of the gods through language
6. **Unit 1, ch. 2 Gods of Olympus exercises** — pupils reconstruct a day of feasting and quarrelling on Olympus from the chapter's readings
7. **Unit 1, ch. 3 Goddesses of Olympus exercises** — matching each god to symbols and realms drills how Greeks pictured divine power
8. **Unit 1, ch. 4 Prometheus/Pandora exercises** — reading Prometheus's defiance of Zeus, pupils judge whether stealing fire for humans was worth his punishment
9. **Unit 1 revision lesson** — matching and gap-fill revision binds the creation stories into one family tree of gods and humans

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
