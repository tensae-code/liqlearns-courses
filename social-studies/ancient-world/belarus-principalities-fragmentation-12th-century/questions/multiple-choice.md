# Belarus — the principalities fragment, 12th century — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Fragmentation on the Belarusian lands  b) Belarus on the map of Europe  c) The beginning of farming and herding in Europe
**Answer: a) Fragmentation on the Belarusian lands** — this part tells fragmentation on the Belarusian lands

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Polotsk and Turov splinter into appanage principalities-volosts as princes settle sons on ever smaller towns  b) pupils first find their own country outlined in white between the Baltic and the Black Sea before any past is opened  c) farming creeps north-west across Europe over millennia until grain and tame animals reach the forest zone
**Answer: a)** — Polotsk and Turov splinter into appanage principalities-volosts as princes settle sons on ever smaller towns

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The fragmentation of Rus  b) The first people — Stone Age, Ice Age period  c) The Bronze Age on the Belarusian lands
**Answer: a) The fragmentation of Rus** — this part tells the fragmentation of Rus

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) brother-princes make war on each other, Kiev's unity dissolves, and Polovtsian and crusader pressure grows on the fragments  b) Neanderthals and then Cro-Magnons hunt reindeer and mammoth at the glacier's edge, leaving flint scrapers at Berdyzh and Yurovichi  c) copper and tin travel from the south, bronze axes appear, and farming and herding take root first in the south, then the north
**Answer: a)** — brother-princes make war on each other, Kiev's unity dissolves, and Polovtsian and crusader pressure grows on the fragments
