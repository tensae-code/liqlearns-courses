# Hands-on — The kingdoms of northern Arabia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The kingdoms of northern Arabia — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Kingdoms of the North, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
