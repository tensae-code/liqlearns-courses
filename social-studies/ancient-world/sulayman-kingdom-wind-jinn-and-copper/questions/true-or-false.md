# Prophet of God Sulayman — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have — the wind runs at his command like a transport fleet, a spring of molten copper feeds his industries, jinn dive for pearls and raise buildings and cauldrons too heavy to move, and he understands the speech of birds and ants; he dies leaning on his staff and the jinn labour on, not knowing, until a termite eats the staff through — and after him his kingdom splits into Judah and Israel.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Prophet of God Sulayman

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Prophet of God Sulayman
