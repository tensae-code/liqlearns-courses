# Prophet of God Sulayman — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Prophet of God Sulayman.
**Answer: sulayman inherits Dawud's prophethood and kingdom and asks for a kingdom no one after him will have — the wind runs at his command like a transport fleet, a spring of molten copper feeds his industries, jinn dive for pearls and raise buildings and cauldrons too heavy to move, and he understands the speech of birds and ants; he dies leaning on his staff and the jinn labour on, not knowing, until a termite eats the staff through — and after him his kingdom splits into Judah and Israel (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Prophet of God Sulayman, printed p. 80.**
