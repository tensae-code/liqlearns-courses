# Herodotus Polycrates Ring And The Tyrants — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Amasis warns the too-lucky tyrant of Samos; Polycrates throws his ring into the sea, a fish returns it, and his fortune turns to a Persian cross.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Book III, Unit 7 The ring of Polycrates

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Αρχαία Ελληνικά Κείμενα — Ηροδότου Ιστορίες, Α΄ Γυμνασίου, Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Ancient Greek Texts: Herodotus's Histories, Grade 7 Gymnasium)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Book III, Unit 7 The ring of Polycrates
