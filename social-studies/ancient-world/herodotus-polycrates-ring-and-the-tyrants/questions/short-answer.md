# Herodotus Polycrates Ring And The Tyrants — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Book III, Unit 7 The ring of Polycrates.
**Answer: Amasis warns the too-lucky tyrant of Samos; Polycrates throws his ring into the sea, a fish returns it, and his fortune turns to a Persian cross (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Αρχαία Ελληνικά Κείμενα — Ηροδότου Ιστορίες, Α΄ Γυμνασίου, Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Ancient Greek Texts: Herodotus's Histories, Grade 7 Gymnasium)* (Greece), Book III, Unit 7 The ring of Polycrates, printed p. 42.**
