## Sub lesson 1 — Harshavardhana and the Harshacharita

Not the eldest son, Harsha inherits Thanesar on two deaths, avenges his murdered brother-in-law by taking Kanauj and conquering east to Bengal — but at the Narmada the Chalukya Pulakeshin II stops him cold; his court poet Banabhatta's biography and Xuan Zang's stay at his court give us the reign twice over.
