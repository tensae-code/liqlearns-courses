## Sub lesson 4 — Lesson 3: the Nabataean Kingdom
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — an Arab tribe settles Edom's land in the 6th century BCE and carves Petra, the rose-red city — the 1,200-metre Siq, the Khazneh tomb, the theatre, Qasr al-Bint (built, not carved, its walls laced with wood against earthquakes) and the Deir; they write Aramaic in 22 letters, speak Greek to foreign merchants, sell Dead Sea salt and bitumen to Egypt's embalmers, and grow rich protecting caravans with towers, stations and taxes — until weak kings and Rome end them; Burckhardt reveals Petra to the West in 1812, UNESCO lists it in 1985, and a Petra Region Authority (2009) guards it now
