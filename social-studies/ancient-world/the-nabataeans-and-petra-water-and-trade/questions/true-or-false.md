# The Nabataeans and Petra — water and trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Arab Nabataeans adapt to the desert, harvesting rainwater with dams, channels and rock-cut cisterns, protecting Petra from floods and trading widely.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2 Lesson 1: the Nabataean kingdom

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الرابع، الفصل الدراسي الأول (Social Studies, Grade 4, Term 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 2 Lesson 1: the Nabataean kingdom

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Samir and Salma enter the rose-red city through the Siq rock corridor and see the Treasury, the Court and the Monastery the Nabataeans carved.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 3 Lesson 2: Petra

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الخامس، الفصل الدراسي الأول (Social Studies, Grade 5, Term 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 3 Lesson 2: Petra

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Arab Nabataeans unite Jordan's kingdoms, carve Petra in rose-red rock, channel water along the Siq — and their queens share rule and appear on the coins.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Nabataean kingdom

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف السابع، الفصل الدراسي الأول (Social Studies, Grade 7, Term 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 4 Lesson 3: the Nabataean kingdom

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** an Arab tribe settles Edom's land in the 6th century BCE and carves Petra, the rose-red city — the 1,200-metre Siq, the Khazneh tomb, the theatre, Qasr al-Bint (built, not carved, its walls laced with wood against earthquakes) and the Deir; they write Aramaic in 22 letters, speak Greek to foreign merchants, sell Dead Sea salt and bitumen to Egypt's embalmers, and grow rich protecting caravans with towers, stations and taxes — until weak kings and Rome end them; Burckhardt reveals Petra to the West in 1812, UNESCO lists it in 1985, and a Petra Region Authority (2009) guards it now.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 3: the Nabataean Kingdom

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *تاريخ الأردن — الصف الحادي عشر، الفرع المهني (History of Jordan, Grade 11, Vocational Track), Term 1 — Unit 1: Jordan in the Ancient Ages* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 3: the Nabataean Kingdom

**9. **Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 3: the Nabataean Kingdom
