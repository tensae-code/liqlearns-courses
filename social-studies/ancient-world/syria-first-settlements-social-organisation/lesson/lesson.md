# Syria — first settlements and social organisation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: On Syria's land people leave the caves of Yabroud for the first villages like Abu Hureyra, and learn to organise work, houses and kin — the first settled society. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Syria — first settlements and social organisation

Humans in Syria move from the Yabroud caves to the first villages like Abu Hureyra and organise work, houses and kin.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Syria — first settlements and social organisation. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
