# China An Lushan Rebellion And Tang Decline — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Eight years of rebellion leave the north 'empty for a thousand li' in Du Fu's poem; warlords carve the empire into fanzhen, Huang Chao's rebels take Chang'an, and Zhu Wen ends Tang in 907.
