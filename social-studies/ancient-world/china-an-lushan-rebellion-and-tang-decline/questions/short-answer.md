# China An Lushan Rebellion And Tang Decline — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: An Lushan rebellion and Tang's fall.
**Answer: eight years of rebellion leave the north 'empty for a thousand li' in Du Fu's poem; warlords carve the empire into fanzhen, Huang Chao's rebels take Chang'an, and Zhu Wen ends Tang in 907 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Tang falls apart.
**Answer: after 755 the court's writ shrinks to the capital's shadow while warlords keep the taxes and the armies (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 6: Tang falls apart, printed p. 34.**
