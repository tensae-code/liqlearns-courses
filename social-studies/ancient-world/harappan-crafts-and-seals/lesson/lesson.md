# Harappan crafts and seals

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Harappan specialists cut carnelian beads, shaped precise stone weights and blades, carved animal seals, and glazed sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — New crafts in the city; faience

Specialists cut, polish and bore carnelian beads, shape precise stone weights and blades, carve animal seals, and glaze sand into blue-green faience — a material invented, not found.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: New crafts in the city; faience. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
