# The Bagan Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bagan was Myanmar's first great empire.
**Answer: True — it gathered the Irrawaddy plain from the Pyu world.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide asks learners to judge Bagan's economy, society, literature and architecture critically.
**Answer: True — those four are studied and judged critically.**
