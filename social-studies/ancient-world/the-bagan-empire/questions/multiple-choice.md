# The Bagan Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bagan gathered which plain into Myanmar's first great empire? —
a) The Irrawaddy plain  b) The Nile plain  c) The Yellow River plain
**Answer: a) The Irrawaddy plain** — The Irrawaddy plain — from the Pyu world a new power gathers it.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bagan emerged from the world of — —
a) The Pyu  b) Rome  c) Greece
**Answer: a) The Pyu** — The Pyu — Bagan follows the Pyu city-states.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bagan's taxes were paid in — —
a) Rice  b) Silver coins only  c) Salt only
**Answer: a) Rice** — Rice — taxes in rice helped make Bagan rich.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Thousands of pagodas and a sangha of monks made Bagan — —
a) Rich, literate and stone-built  b) A sea empire only  c) A city with no writing
**Answer: a) Rich, literate and stone-built** — Rich, literate and stone-built.
