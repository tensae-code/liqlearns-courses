# Video script — The Bagan Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Myanmar's story as its Grade 6 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From the Pyu world a new power gathers the Irrawaddy plain into Myanmar's first great empire: how the Bagan Empire emerged and became powerful.

Taxes in rice, a sangha of monks and thousands of pagodas make Bagan rich, literate and stone-built — its economy, society, literature and architecture, judged critically.
