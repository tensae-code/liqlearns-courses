# Hands-on — The Bagan Empire

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Rice, monks and pagodas
Draw three boxes labelled Rice, Sangha and Pagodas. In each box write what it gave Bagan (wealth, learning, stone building). Underneath, write one critical question of your own about Bagan — the guide judges Bagan critically, so practise doing the same.
