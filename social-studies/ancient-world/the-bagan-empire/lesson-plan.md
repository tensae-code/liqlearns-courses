# Lesson plan — The Bagan Empire

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Bagan Empire
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Myanmar, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ch.3 Emergence of ancient Myanmar: how the Bagan Empire emerged, became powerful, and built its economy, society, literature and architecture.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Myanmar's Grade 6 national story, as the Myanmar History teacher guide teaches it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bagan emerges and becomes powerful** — From the Pyu world a new power gathers the Irrawaddy plain into Myanmar's first great empire: how the Bagan Empire emerged and became powerful.
2. **Bagan's economy, society, literature and architecture** — Taxes in rice, a sangha of monks and thousands of pagodas make Bagan rich, literate and stone-built — its economy, society, literature and architecture, judged critically.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
