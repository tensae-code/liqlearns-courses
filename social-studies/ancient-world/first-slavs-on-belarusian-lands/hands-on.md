# Hands-on — The first Slavs on the Belarusian lands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The first Slavs on the territory of Belarus — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The first Slavs on the territory of Belarus, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
