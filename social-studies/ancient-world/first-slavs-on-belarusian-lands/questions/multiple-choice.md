# The first Slavs on the Belarusian lands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The first Slavs on the territory of Belarus  b) Polotsk and Turov — the first principalities  c) Christianity on the territory of Belarus
**Answer: a) The first Slavs on the territory of Belarus** — this part tells the first Slavs on the territory of Belarus

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Slavs move up from the Pripyat in the mid-1st millennium, bringing slash-and-burn farming, wooden building and their own gods and shrines  b) Polotsk under its own princes and Turov under Kiev's hand become the first states on Belarusian soil, and Minsk, Vitebsk, Brest and Grodno are founded  c) bishoprics at Polotsk and Turov, St Sophia's cathedral and Euphrosyne of Polotsk's cross carry the new faith and book culture into the towns
**Answer: a)** — Slavs move up from the Pripyat in the mid-1st millennium, bringing slash-and-burn farming, wooden building and their own gods and shrines
