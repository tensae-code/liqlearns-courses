# Salahuddin and the Ayyubids — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Salahuddin ibn Ayyub, a Kurdish soldier from Tikrit, unites Egypt and Syria, retakes Jerusalem in 1187, and faces Richard the Lionheart in the Third Crusade — the book's sultans list runs his house to 1260.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 3: the Ayyubid Dynasty (1171–1260)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History 7 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Salahuddin and the Ayyubids
