# Belarus — the Bronze Age: metal arrives — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The Bronze Age on the Belarusian lands  b) The first Slavs on the territory of Belarus  c) Polotsk and Turov — the first principalities
**Answer: a) The Bronze Age on the Belarusian lands** — this part tells the Bronze Age on the Belarusian lands

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) copper and tin travel from the south, bronze axes appear, and farming and herding take root first in the south, then the north  b) Slavs move up from the Pripyat in the mid-1st millennium, bringing slash-and-burn farming, wooden building and their own gods and shrines  c) Polotsk under its own princes and Turov under Kiev's hand become the first states on Belarusian soil, and Minsk, Vitebsk, Brest and Grodno are founded
**Answer: a)** — copper and tin travel from the south, bronze axes appear, and farming and herding take root first in the south, then the north
