# Lesson plan — The Crusades Causes And Course

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Crusades Causes And Course
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** pope, knights and pilgrims converge on a holy war for Jerusalem
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§10 Causes of the Crusades** — pope, knights and pilgrims converge on a holy war for Jerusalem
2. **§11 Europe and the Muslim world after the Crusades** — two centuries of war end in trade, translation and wary coexistence
3. **§10 The Crusades: causes, course, consequences** — two centuries of holy war for Jerusalem change both worlds
4. **§11 Christian Europe and the Muslim world after the Crusades** — enemies become trading partners and translators of each other's books
5. **4.1 Why did Catholic Christians organise Crusades against Islamic lands?** — pope, knights and faith combine into the crusading call
6. **4.2 How did the Crusades change Christian Europe and the Muslim world?** — trade, taxes and tolerance all shift after two centuries of war
7. **§10 The Crusades: causes, course, consequences** — crusader states rise and fall on the Levant coast
8. **§11 Christian Europe and the Muslim world after the Crusades** — war gives way to trade, translation and borrowed technologies
9. **§10 The Crusades: causes, course and consequences** — crusader kingdoms rise in the Levant and fall two centuries later
10. **§11 Christian Europe and the Muslim world after the Crusades** — former enemies exchange goods, words and technologies
