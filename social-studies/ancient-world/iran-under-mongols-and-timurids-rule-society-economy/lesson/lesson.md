# Iran under the Mongols and Timurids — rule, society and economy

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: conquest empties cities and qanats, then Ilkhanid and Timurid rulers rebuild an administration and economy on the ruins As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
