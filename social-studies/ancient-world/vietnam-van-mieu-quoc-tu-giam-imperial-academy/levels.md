## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 10.3d, Đại Việt's education and examinations
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — education and examinations begin under the Lý — in 1070 King Lý Thánh Tông builds the Văn Miếu (Temple of Literature) to worship Confucius, and in 1076 King Lý Nhân Tông founds the Quốc Tử Giám (Imperial Academy) at the capital, the court's highest Confucian school; under the Trần the examinations are organised more regularly, under the early Lê Confucian examinations flourish, and under the Mạc, the Lê restoration and the Nguyễn the state keeps valuing them — the early Lê holds 26 examinations graduating nearly 1,000 tiến sĩ (doctors) and 20 trạng nguyên (first-rank laureates), and the Mạc at Thăng Long (1527–1592) holds an examination every three years, graduating 484 tiến sĩ; the dynasties encourage learning with honours — from 1442 the early Lê holds the ceremony of proclaiming the graduates' names and worshipping the ancestors at the Văn Miếu, and with the motto "Virtuous talent is the nation's vital energy" (hiền tài là nguyên khí quốc gia), in 1484 King Lê Thánh Tông begins erecting stone stelae at the Văn Miếu engraving the tiến sĩ's names (a stele erected in 1484 is pictured), while the Nguyễn sets up a Đốc học office in every province to take charge of education and examinations; and the graduates become the nation's great cultural figures — Lê Văn Hưu, Mạc Đĩnh Chi, Chu Văn An, Nguyễn Trãi, Lê Quý Đôn
