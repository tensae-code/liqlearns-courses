# The Ghassanids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Ghassanids.
**Answer: Azd tribes flee Yemen after the Marib dam breaks and, as Byzantium's Arab allies against Persia and the desert raiders, build the White Palace, al-Mushatta and al-Qastal (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Iraq's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *الاجتماعيات للصف الأول المتوسط (Social Studies for the First Intermediate Grade)* (Iraq), The Ghassanids, printed p. 80.**
