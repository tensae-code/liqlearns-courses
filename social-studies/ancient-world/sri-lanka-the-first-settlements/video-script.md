# Video script — Sri Lanka — The first settlements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sri Lanka's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Vijaya legend is retold and then weighed — a prince exiled with 700 followers, a yakkha princess Kuveni who helps and is betrayed — against the archaeology of real first villages along the rivers.
