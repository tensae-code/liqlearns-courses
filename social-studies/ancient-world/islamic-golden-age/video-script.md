# Video script — The Islamic Golden Age — a civilisation of science

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey's Grade 9 book calls it Bilim Medeniyeti — the Civilisation of Science. From the 8th to the 12th century, the Islamic world leads the world's learning.

It begins with translation: the books of older civilisations gathered into Arabic. Then the work goes beyond them — algebra takes shape, medicine is written into great textbooks, observatories measure the heavens.

Baghdad and Cordoba become the world's classrooms. A civilisation measured not by its armies, but by its questions.
