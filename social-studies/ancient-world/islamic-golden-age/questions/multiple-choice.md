# The Islamic Golden Age — a civilisation of science — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Civilisation of Science flowered in —
a) The 8th–12th centuries  b) The 1st century only  c) The 19th century
**Answer: a) The 8th–12th centuries** — the book's span.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The age's work began with —
a) War  b) Translation of older civilisations' books  c) Printing
**Answer: b) Translation of older civilisations' books** — gathered into Arabic first.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The two classroom cities named are —
a) Damascus and Cairo  b) Mecca and Medina  c) Baghdad and Cordoba
**Answer: c) Baghdad and Cordoba** — east and west.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Observatories served —
a) Medicine  b) Algebra  c) Measuring the heavens
**Answer: c) Measuring the heavens** — the stars tracked and timed.
