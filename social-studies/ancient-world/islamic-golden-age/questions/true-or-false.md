# The Islamic Golden Age — a civilisation of science — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Algebra took shape as a science of its own in this age.
**Answer: True** — one of the four works named.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book measures this civilisation chiefly by its armies.
**Answer: False** — it measures it by its questions — translation, algebra, medicine, observatories.
