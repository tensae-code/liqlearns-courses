# Hands-on — The Islamic Golden Age — a civilisation of science

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The translator's desk

Choose one thing your language has no old word for. Write how a Baghdad translator would handle it: find the foreign book, render it, then improve it. Your three steps are the age's method.

## Four works, four tools

Draw the four works with their tools: translation (two books), algebra (a balance of unknowns), medicine (a physician's casebook), observatories (an astrolabe). Label what each tool lets people do.

## A classroom city

Design a poster inviting a young scholar of the year 1000 to study in Baghdad or Cordoba. It must name what can be learned there that can be learned nowhere better.
