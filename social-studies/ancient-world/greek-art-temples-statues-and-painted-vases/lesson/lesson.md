# Greek art — temples, statues and painted vases

## Do it first

Before you read on, picture the scene this lesson is about, from its title alone: Greek art — temples, statues and painted vases. What do you expect to happen, and what do you expect to change? Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The hunt for perfect proportion

Greek aesthetics is a hunt for perfect proportion in everything made.

This is a real turn in the story, not a detail at its edge: hold it by its place in the order — what came before it, and what it makes possible next.
