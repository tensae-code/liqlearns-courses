# Hands-on — Greek art — temples, statues and painted vases

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw a Greek temple, a statue in living balance and a black-and-red painted vase from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: The hunt for perfect proportion; Temples and statues come alive; Vases carry the story home. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
