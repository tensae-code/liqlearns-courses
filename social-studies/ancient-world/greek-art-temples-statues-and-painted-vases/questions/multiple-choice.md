# Greek art — temples, statues and painted vases — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Temples and statues come alive  b) The hunt for perfect proportion  c) Vases carry the story home
**Answer: b) The hunt for perfect proportion** — Greek aesthetics is a hunt for perfect proportion in everything made.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Temples and statues come alive  b) The hunt for perfect proportion  c) Vases carry the story home
**Answer: a) Temples and statues come alive** — Temples are tuned like music, and statues step out of stiffness into living balance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) The hunt for perfect proportion  b) Temples and statues come alive  c) Vases carry the story home
**Answer: c) Vases carry the story home** — Black-and-red vases carry the myths and the games into every Greek home.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The Greeks hunt for perfect proportion — temples tuned like music, statues that step out of stiffness into living balance, and black-and-red vases that carry the myths and the games into every Greek home  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
