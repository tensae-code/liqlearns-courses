# The Aztecs — Tenochtitlan — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record of the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: the learner proves each sub lesson before the next opens, and practice deepens inside each part (more items, harder corners) without new rungs.

## Sub lesson 1 — A city on a lake

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **Tenochtitlan** (the Aztec capital, built on an island in a lake).

## Sub lesson 2 — An empire of tribute

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **tribute** (payments in goods exacted from conquered peoples).

## Sub lesson 3 — Religion at the centre

Pass mark: the learner can retell this part in order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key term is **sacrifice** (the offering at the centre of Aztec religion, made to keep the world alive).
