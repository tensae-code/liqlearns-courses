# Video script — The Aztecs — Tenochtitlan

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — A city on a lake. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Aztecs built their capital, Tenochtitlan, on an island in a lake — a city of canals, causeways and temples, joined to the shore by raised roads and fed by gardens floating on the water.

It grew to be one of the largest cities on earth in its day, raised by a people who had arrived in the valley as newcomers and made themselves masters of it.. Watch the names appear one by one: Tenochtitlan, causeway, Aztecs. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — An empire of tribute. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Aztec state ruled an empire not by governing every town directly but by tribute: conquered peoples paid — food, cloth, feathers, warriors' gear — and rebellion was answered with war.

Tribute empires are rich and resented in equal measure. Watch the names appear one by one: tribute, empire, rebellion. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Religion at the centre. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Aztec religion centred on sacrifice, offered to keep the sun moving and the world alive — the great temple at the city's heart was its stage.

However strange it looks from outside, the teaching point is the same as at Aksum or Thebes: in the ancient world, religion was not beside the state; it was inside its machinery.. Watch the names appear one by one: sacrifice, great temple, state religion. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Grade 9 depth: The Inca — Cuzco, quipu and the imperial roads — added scene. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Tell this part as story, in the lesson's own order, in the super-simple voice: who acted, what they wanted, and what changed because of it.
