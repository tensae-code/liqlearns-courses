# The Aztecs — Tenochtitlan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: A raised road joining the island city to the shore — this is causeway.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Tenochtitlan means the people who built Tenochtitlan and its empire.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: A state ruling many peoples, here by tribute and war — this is empire.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, tribute means the answer tribute empires repeatedly provoked.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The temple at Tenochtitlan's heart, the stage of its religion — this is great temple.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, sacrifice means a faith woven into the machinery of rule, as in Aztec, Aksumite and Egyptian states.
**Answer: False**
