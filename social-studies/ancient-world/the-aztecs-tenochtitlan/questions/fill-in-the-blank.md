# The Aztecs — Tenochtitlan — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the people who built Tenochtitlan and its empire' is ______.
**Answer: Aztecs**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the answer tribute empires repeatedly provoked' is ______.
**Answer: rebellion**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'a faith woven into the machinery of rule, as in Aztec, Aksumite and Egyptian states' is ______.
**Answer: state religion**
