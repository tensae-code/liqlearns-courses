# The Aztecs — Tenochtitlan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Tenochtitlan?
a) causeway
b) the Aztec capital, built on an island in a lake
c) Aztecs
**Answer: b) the Aztec capital, built on an island in a lake**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is tribute?
a) payments in goods exacted from conquered peoples
b) rebellion
c) empire
**Answer: a) payments in goods exacted from conquered peoples**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is sacrifice?
a) the offering at the centre of Aztec religion, made to keep the world alive
b) state religion
c) great temple
**Answer: a) the offering at the centre of Aztec religion, made to keep the world alive**
