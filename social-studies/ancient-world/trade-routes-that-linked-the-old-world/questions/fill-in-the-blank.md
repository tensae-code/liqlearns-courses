# Trade routes that linked the old world — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the desert and highland convoys at the system's heart' is ______.
**Answer: caravan**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the brokers between producers and distant buyers' is ______.
**Answer: middlemen**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'to choke a state by closing its road, a power the routes gave' is ______.
**Answer: strangle**
