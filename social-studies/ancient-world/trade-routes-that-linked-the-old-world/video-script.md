# Video script — Trade routes that linked the old world

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — The engine under the story. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Read the Grade 11 book again watching only for trade, and a second book appears: Red Sea, trans-Saharan, Indian Ocean, caravan and Atlantic routes linking nearly every unit. Watch the names appear one by one: connecting engine, Red Sea trade, caravan. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Five route-worlds. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Draw them together: ships between Adulis, Arabia and India; camels between the Maghreb and the goldfields; monsoon dhows down the Swahili coast; highland caravans to Zeila and Massawa; and finally the Atlantic triangle that joined three continents in goods and grief.

Each route-world had its goods, its seasons, its middlemen — and its own lesson in this course. Watch the names appear one by one: route-world, monsoon, middlemen. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Mapping as mastery. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Trade-route mapping is the book's most repeated skill because it is the historian's X-ray: draw the routes and the period's skeleton shows — who needed whom, who taxed whom, who could strangle whom by closing a road.

Master the routes of a period and its politics become legible. Watch the names appear one by one: X-ray, legible, strangle. This is the part of the story to hold.
