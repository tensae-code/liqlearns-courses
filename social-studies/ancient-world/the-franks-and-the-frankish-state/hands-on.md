# Hands-on — The Franks And The Frankish State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Franks And The Frankish State — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 4.2–4.3 The Franks, their expansion; economic, social and political traits of the Frankish state, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
