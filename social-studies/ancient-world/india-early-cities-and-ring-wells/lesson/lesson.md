# India — early cities and ring wells

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In India's early cities, rows of ceramic ring wells served as toilets, drains and rubbish pits, and a Greek sailor's port-guide lists what Barygaza imported and exported. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ring wells; the story of Barygaza

Rows of ceramic rings sunk in house plots served as toilets, drains and rubbish pits; an unnamed Greek sailor's port-guide lists Barygaza's imports (wine, copper, coral, gold and silver coins) and exports (ivory, agate, cotton, silk, perfumes) and the singing boys and fine cloth merchants gifted to its king.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Ring wells; the story of Barygaza. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
