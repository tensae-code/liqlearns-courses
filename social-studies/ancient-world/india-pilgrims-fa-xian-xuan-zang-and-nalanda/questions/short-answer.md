# India — pilgrims: Fa Xian, Xuan Zang and Nalanda — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The quest of the pilgrims.
**Answer: Fa Xian, caught in a 13-day storm on the voyage home, throws his belongings overboard but clings to his books and Buddha statues, taking 90 days to Java; Xuan Zang loses 50 manuscripts when his Indus boat capsizes yet hauls 600 texts home on 20 horses and spends his life translating them; both study at Nalanda, where the gatekeeper's hard questions turn away seven or eight of every ten hopefuls (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.9 / The quest of the pilgrims, printed pp. 97–98.**
