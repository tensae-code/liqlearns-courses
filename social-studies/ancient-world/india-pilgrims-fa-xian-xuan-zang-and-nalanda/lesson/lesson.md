# India — pilgrims: Fa Xian, Xuan Zang and Nalanda

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Fa Xian and Xuan Zang crossed storms and rivers to study in India, studied at Nalanda, and hauled hundreds of texts home to China to translate for the rest of their lives. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The quest of the pilgrims

Fa Xian, caught in a 13-day storm on the voyage home, throws his belongings overboard but clings to his books and Buddha statues, taking 90 days to Java; Xuan Zang loses 50 manuscripts when his Indus boat capsizes yet hauls 600 texts home on 20 horses and spends his life translating them; both study at Nalanda, where the gatekeeper's hard questions turn away seven or eight of every ten hopefuls.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: The quest of the pilgrims. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
