# Hands-on — Seven Wonders of the Ancient World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Seven Wonders of the Ancient World — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 11. The seven wonders of the ancient world, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
