# Lesson plan — Crimea and the northern Black Sea in the late Middle Ages

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Crimea and the northern Black Sea in the late Middle Ages
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Genoese trading cities on the coast, the Greek principality of Theodoro in the mountains, and from the 1440s the Crimean Khanate of the Girays — a new steppe power that will face Ukraine for three centuries.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Genoese cities and Theodoro** — Genoese trading cities stand on the Crimean coast. In the mountains sits the Greek principality of Theodoro — traders and a small Greek state share the peninsula.
2. **The Crimean Khanate of the Girays** — From the 1440s rises the Crimean Khanate of the Girays. It is a new steppe power — one that will face Ukraine for three centuries.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ukrainian lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two powers on Crimea before the Khanate: Genoese trading cities on the coast and the Greek principality of Theodoro in the mountains.
2. Say what arose from the 1440s: the Crimean Khanate of the Girays, a steppe power that would face Ukraine for three centuries.
