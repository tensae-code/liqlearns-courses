# Crimea and the northern Black Sea in the late Middle Ages

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Genoese trading cities on the coast, the Greek principality of Theodoro in the mountains, and from the 1440s the Crimean Khanate of the Girays — a new steppe power that will face Ukraine for three centuries. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Genoese cities and Theodoro

Genoese trading cities stand on the Crimean coast. In the mountains sits the Greek principality of Theodoro — traders and a small Greek state share the peninsula.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Crimean Khanate of the Girays

From the 1440s rises the Crimean Khanate of the Girays. It is a new steppe power — one that will face Ukraine for three centuries.
