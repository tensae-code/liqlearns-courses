# Crimea and the northern Black Sea in the late Middle Ages — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Crimean Khanate would face Ukraine for three centuries.
**Answer: True — The book presents the Girays' Khanate as a power facing Ukraine for three centuries.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Crimea in the late Middle Ages had only one ruler and one people.
**Answer: False — Genoese cities, Theodoro and then the Khanate share its story.**
