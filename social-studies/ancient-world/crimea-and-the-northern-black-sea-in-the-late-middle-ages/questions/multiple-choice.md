# Crimea and the northern Black Sea in the late Middle Ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The trading cities on the coast were —
a) Genoese  b) Lithuanian  c) Halych's
**Answer: a) Genoese**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Theodoro was —
a) A Greek principality in the mountains  b) A Mongol camp  c) A Genoese warehouse
**Answer: a) A Greek principality in the mountains**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Crimean Khanate arose in —
a) The 1440s  b) 988  c) 1223
**Answer: a) The 1440s**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Khanate's ruling house was —
a) The Girays  b) The Romanovychi  c) The Rurikids of Kyiv
**Answer: a) The Girays**
