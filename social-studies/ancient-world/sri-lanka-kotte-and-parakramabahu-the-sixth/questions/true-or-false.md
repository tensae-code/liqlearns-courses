# Sri Lanka — Kotte and Parakramabahu the Sixth — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A fortress rises in the marshes, Parakramabahu VI takes Jaffna in 1450 through his adopted son Sapumal Kumaraya, and rules a united island for the last time.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Kotte (1415–1521) and Parakramabahu VI

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History Tamil 2011* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Kotte (1415–1521) and Parakramabahu VI

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Yapahuwa's rock fortress, Kurunegala, Gampola, and at last Kotte, where Parakramabahu VI rules a reunified island and receives Zheng He's challenge.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Political history 1271–1509: the later kingdoms

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G12-13 History of Sri Lanka Part II Tamil 2014* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Political history 1271–1509: the later kingdoms
