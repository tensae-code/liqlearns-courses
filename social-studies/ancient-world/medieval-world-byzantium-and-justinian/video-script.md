# Video script — Medieval world — Byzantium and Justinian

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The East did not fall.
Its walls were strong. Its gold was deep. Its army stood.
Justinian took back the west, wrote the laws into one Code,
raised Hagia Sophia, and stole silk from China.
Byzantium kept the old world alive.

---

Grade 9 story addition (Ethiopia Wave 4):

Rome falls in the west — the map darkens. But in the east, one city still burns bright: Constantinople.

A thousand years it stands — Orthodox, imperial, Roman to the end.

1453. The Ottoman banners rise over the walls. The long empire closes its book.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Two Romes in depth — the fallen West and the thousand-year East. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 book opens the medieval world with the double story: the West falling to Germanic kingdoms while the Eastern Roman — Byzantine — Empire survives a thousand years from Constantinople. The names to hold: Byzantine Empire, Justinian.
