# Medieval world — Byzantium and Justinian — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Byzantium is named from the Greek city ____.
**Answer: Byzantium**
**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Justinian built ____ in Constantinople.
**Answer: Hagia Sophia**
**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The empire finally ended in ____ at Ottoman hands.
**Answer: 1453**
**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The term for 'the Byzantine emperor of reconquest and codified law' is ______.
**Answer: Justinian**
