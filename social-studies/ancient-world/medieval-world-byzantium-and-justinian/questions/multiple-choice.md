# Medieval world — Byzantium and Justinian — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The East survived because of —
a) Luck only  b) Position, wealth and a strong army  c) Having no enemies  d) Roman legions from the West
**Answer: b) Position, wealth and a strong army** — The book's three reasons.
**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Justinian ruled —
a) 330–360  b) 527–565  c) 461–429 BC  d) 1300–1326
**Answer: b) 527–565** — The empire's great medieval emperor.
**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Justinian Code was —
a) A silk pattern  b) Collected and revised laws, later used across Europe  c) A war plan  d) A tax list
**Answer: b) Collected and revised laws, later used across Europe** — His gift to every later law-maker.
**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide frames Byzantium as —
a) A trading port only  b) The centre that kept Greco-Roman and Christian civilisation alive while the West declined  c) The founder of feudalism  d) A western kingdom
**Answer: b) The centre that kept Greco-Roman and Christian civilisation alive while the West declined** — Guide evidence, §4.3 (teacher guide — evidence).
