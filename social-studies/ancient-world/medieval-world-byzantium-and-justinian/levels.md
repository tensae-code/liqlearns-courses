# Medieval world — Byzantium and Justinian — sub lessons and depth

This is the depth record for the lesson, re-expressed as sub lessons: each sub lesson below is one part of the lesson, proved in order, and practice inside it deepens from its first items to its hardest corners.

## Sub lesson 1 — Byzantium endures — and Justinian
Pass mark: the learner can do this part from memory — Why the East lasted: position, wealth, strong army. Justinian's reign: western expansion, the Justinian Code, Hagia Sophia, silk smuggled from China; then shrinkage until 1453. Practice deepens with more items, then exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them.

## Sub lesson 2 — The keeper of Greco-Roman civilisation (teacher guide — evidence)
Pass mark: the learner can do this part from memory — The guide's frame: Byzantium as the centre that kept Greco-Roman and Christian civilisation alive in Europe while the West declined, and the Crusades as Western Europe's military response in the Holy Land. Practice deepens with more items, then exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them.

Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files (the Grade 9 items). Pass mark: retell the Grade 9 part's story and answer its questions without the book open — its names, dates and places all correct.

## Sub lesson 4 — Grade 11 depth: Two Romes in depth — the fallen West and the thousand-year East

Pass mark: the learner can retell the Grade 11 depth part in the story's order and answer its gate-pool questions — the key terms are **Byzantine Empire** (the Eastern Roman Empire surviving a thousand years at Constantinople) and **Justinian** (the Byzantine emperor of reconquest and codified law).
