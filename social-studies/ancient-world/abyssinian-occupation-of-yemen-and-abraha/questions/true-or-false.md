# The Abyssinian occupation of Yemen — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after the Himyarite king Dhu Nuwas burns the believers of Najran in the trenches, Abyssinian armies cross in 525 CE and take Zafar, their ruler Abraha builds a great church to turn pilgrims from Mecca and marches with elephants to demolish the Kaaba, and flocks of birds destroy his army in the Year of the Elephant, the year Muhammad is born.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Abyssinian occupation of Yemen

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Abyssinian occupation of Yemen

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Rome and Persia compete for Yemen's trade-road position and play its Christian-Jewish conflict until Dhu Nuwas burns Najran's Christians in the trenches and Byzantium's ally Abyssinia invades in 523 CE under Aryat; Abraha rules after him, tries to demolish the Kaaba and is destroyed, and Abyssinian rule lasts some fifty years — but the Yemenis never submit, and Sayf bin Dhi Yazan wins Persia's help through al-Hira's mediation, lands with Wahriz and some 800 men in 575 CE, and the Yemenis rise with him to kill Masruq and free their country.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Yemen's condition before Islam: the Abyssinian invasion and the Yemeni resistance
