# India — the Pallavas and Chalukyas

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raided each other's capitals for generations — and Pulakeshin II's praise-poem boasts he checked Harsha himself. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Pallavas, Chalukyas and Pulakeshin's prashasti

Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raid each other's rich capitals for generations; Ravikirti's praise-poem boasts Pulakeshin II checked Harsha ("after this defeat, Harsha was no longer Harsha — happiness") and drove the Pallava king behind his walls — yet both dynasties soon give way to Rashtrakutas and Cholas.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: The Pallavas, Chalukyas and Pulakeshin's prashasti. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
