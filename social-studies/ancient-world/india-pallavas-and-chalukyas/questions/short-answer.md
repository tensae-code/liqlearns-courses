**Answer: Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raid each other's rich capitals for generations; Ravikirti's praise-poem boasts Pulakeshin II checked Harsha ("after this defeat, Harsha was no longer Harsha — happiness") and drove the Pallava king behind his walls — yet both dynasties soon give way to Rashtrakutas and Cholas (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
