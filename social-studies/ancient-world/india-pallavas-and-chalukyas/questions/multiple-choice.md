# India — the Pallavas and Chalukyas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The Pallavas, Chalukyas and Pulakeshin's prashasti  b) Painting  c) Finding out about the past
**Answer: a) The Pallavas, Chalukyas and Pulakeshin's prashasti** — this part tells the Pallavas, Chalukyas and Pulakeshin's prashasti

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raid each other's rich capitals for generations  b) In Ajanta's dark rock-cut monasteries, unknown artists paint vivid scenes by torchlight in colours ground from plants and minerals that still glow after 1500 years  c) People who eat up one place's plants, follow migrating herds, chase seasonal fruit and dry-season water can never settle
**Answer: a)** — Kanchipuram's Pallavas and Aihole's Chalukyas raid each other's rich capitals for generations
