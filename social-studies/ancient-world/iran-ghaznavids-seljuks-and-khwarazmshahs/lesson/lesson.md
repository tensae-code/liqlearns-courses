# Iran — Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Turkish soldier-dynasties take the plateau in turn — Mahmud's raids, Tughril's Seljuk sultanate shielding the caliph, and the Khwarazmshahs who rule on the eve of the Mongol storm As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
