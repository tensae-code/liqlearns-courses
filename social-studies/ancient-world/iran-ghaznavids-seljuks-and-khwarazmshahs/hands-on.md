# Hands-on — Iran — Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iran — Ghaznavids, Seljuks and Khwarazmshahs — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Iran in the Ghaznavid, Seljuk and Khwarazmshah era, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
