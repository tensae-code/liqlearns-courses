# Hands-on — Baghdad falls to the Mongols, 1258

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Baghdad falls to the Mongols, 1258 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Weakness and Fall of the Abbasid State, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
