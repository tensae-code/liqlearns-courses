# Video script — Economic and social conditions of the Arabs before Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Arabs live by herding, farming in Yemen, Taif and Yamama, and the winter journey to Yemen and summer journey to Syria with markets like Ukaz, in a tribal society the book faults for wine, usury and burying daughters yet praises for generosity and protecting the neighbour.
