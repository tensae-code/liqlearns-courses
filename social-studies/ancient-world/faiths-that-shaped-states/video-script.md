# Video script — Faiths that shaped states

Story-style script in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, routes light up. Super-simple voice; made later, when videos are made.

---

[Scene 1 — Religion inside the machinery. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Across this course, three faiths — Christianity, Islam and Judaism — arrive in unit after unit not as private beliefs but as state-shaping forces: crowning kings, founding cities, binding unions, justifying wars.

This lesson gathers the thread the book weaves through Units 2 to 8, so it can be seen whole.. Watch the names appear one by one: state-shaping, thread, machinery. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Three faiths, many states. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Christianity made Aksum a Christian kingdom, Gondar a confessional court, and Susenyos's imposition a civil war. Watch the names appear one by one: confessional court, sultanates, identity. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — How to study faith historically. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The historian's questions are not 'which faith is true?' but 'what did this faith do here — to law, land, trade, war and kingship, and what did states do to it in return?'

Asked that way, religion becomes one of history's clearest engines — and its students need believe nothing to see it work.. Watch the names appear one by one: engine, in return, historically. This is the part of the story to hold.
