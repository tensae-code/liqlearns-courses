# Faiths that shaped states — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'the working system of a state, inside which faith sat' is ______.
**Answer: machinery**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'what Judaism preserved for the Bete-Israel under pressure' is ______.
**Answer: identity**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'studying what a faith did, not which faith is true' is ______.
**Answer: historically**
