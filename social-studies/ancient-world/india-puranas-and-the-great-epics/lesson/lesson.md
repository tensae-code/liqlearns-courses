# India — the Puranas and the great epics

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Puranas and the great epics — the Mahabharata with the Gita inside it, and the Ramayana — retold gods, creation and kings in verse meant to be heard by everyone. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Recording and preserving old stories

The Puranas retell gods, creation and kings in simple Sanskrit verse meant to be heard by everyone — women and shudras included, though barred from the Vedas; the Mahabharata's cousin-war for Hastinapura (with the Gita inside it) and the Ramayana's exiled Rama, abducted Sita and victorious return are written down in the forms we know about 1500 years ago.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Recording and preserving old stories. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
