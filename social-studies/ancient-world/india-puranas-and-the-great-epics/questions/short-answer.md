# India — the Puranas and the great epics — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Recording and preserving old stories.
**Answer: The Puranas retell gods, creation and kings in simple Sanskrit verse meant to be heard by everyone — women and shudras included, though barred from the Vedas; the Mahabharata's cousin-war for Hastinapura (with the Gita inside it) and the Ramayana's exiled Rama, abducted Sita and victorious return are written down in the forms we know about 1500 years ago (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.11 / Recording and preserving old stories, printed pp. 120–121.**
