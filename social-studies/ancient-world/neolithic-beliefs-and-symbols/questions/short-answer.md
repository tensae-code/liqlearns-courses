# Neolithic beliefs and symbols — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two kinds of objects that hint at Neolithic beliefs.
**Answer: Figurines and decorated pots (or burials under house floors).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why do we call Neolithic beliefs hints and not facts?
**Answer: The makers never explained them; we read the objects as evidence (any close wording).**
