# Neolithic beliefs and symbols — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Neolithic people buried some of their dead under house floors.
**Answer: True** — the burials are among the hints at their beliefs.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** We know exactly what each Neolithic figurine meant.
**Answer: False** — the makers never explained them.
