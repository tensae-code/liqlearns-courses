# Neolithic beliefs and symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** We guess at Neolithic beliefs mainly from —
a) figurines, burials and decorated pots  b) their alphabet  c) travellers' tales
**Answer: a) figurines, burials and decorated pots** — the hints the makers left, unexplained.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Some Neolithic dead were buried —
a) under the floors of houses  b) in pyramids  c) at sea
**Answer: a) under the floors of houses** — burials under house floors hint at their beliefs.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The makers of the figurines explained what they meant —
a) never, in anything we can read  b) in long inscriptions  c) to Greek visitors
**Answer: a) never, in anything we can read** — beliefs the makers never explained.
