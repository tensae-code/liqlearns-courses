# Neolithic beliefs and symbols

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this question in your mind: neolithic people left no words for what they believed — their figurines, burials under house floors and decorated pots are the hints we read their beliefs from. As you read, test every part of the story against that one thing — who came, what they did, and what changed because of it. Hold your ideas; the story will test them.
