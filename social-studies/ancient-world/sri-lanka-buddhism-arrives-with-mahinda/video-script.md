# Video script — Sri Lanka — Buddhism arrives with Mahinda

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sri Lanka's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ashoka sends his own son Mahinda, who meets King Devanampiyatissa hunting on Mihintale, preaches, and stays — and the king who hosted Asoka's envoys for five months at Pataliputra gives the new faith a kingdom.
