# What is a civilisation?

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A civilisation rises where rivers force cooperation, roads meet, minerals wait in the hills and people struggle with their environment — and Egypt is the oldest model of them all. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Factors for the rise of civilisations

Rivers that force cooperation, meeting-places of continents, minerals in the hills, safety from invasion, and man's struggle with his environment — with Egypt, owner of the oldest of the ancient world's civilisations, taken as the model, and Jamal Hamdan's "genius of place" as its motto.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
