# What is a civilisation? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Factors for the rise of civilisations.
**Answer: Rivers that force cooperation, meeting-places of continents, minerals in the hills, safety from invasion, and man's struggle with his environment — with Egypt, owner of the oldest of the ancient world's civilisations, taken as the model, and Jamal Hamdan's "genius of place" as its motto. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
