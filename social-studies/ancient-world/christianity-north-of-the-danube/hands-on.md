# Hands-on — Christianity North Of The Danube

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Christianity North Of The Danube — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unitatea VI, Lecția 7: Creștinismul în spațiul nord-dunărean, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
