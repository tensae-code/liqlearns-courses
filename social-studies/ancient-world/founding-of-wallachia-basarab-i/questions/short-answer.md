# Founding Of Wallachia Basarab I — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Românească.
**Answer: the voivodeships of Glad, Gelu and Menumorut give way to Basarab I, who unifies the local autonomies and founds Wallachia, named as its ruler in a 1324 document (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor şi universală: Manual pentru clasa a VI-a* (Moldova), Unitatea II, Lecția 4: Formarea statelor medievale româneşti — Țara Românească, printed p. 62.**
