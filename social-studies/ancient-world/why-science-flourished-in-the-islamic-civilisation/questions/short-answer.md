# Factors in the rise of the scientific renaissance (Unit 6 Lesson 1) — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Factors in the rise of the scientific renaissance (Unit 6 Lesson 1).
**Answer: six factors compound: a religion whose first revealed word commands reading and makes scholars heirs of the prophets; the Arabic language, spread by Uthman's gathering of the Qur'an and Abd al-Malik's Arabising of the diwans; stable rule and competing caliphs; a vast, varied geography whose culture centres — Mecca, Alexandria, Baghdad, Sanaa, Cordoba, Jundishapur — fall inside one abode; merchant wealth and the waqf endowing schools; and inherited civilisations, Greek translation, paper and observatories (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الثاني (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 2)* (Yemen), Factors in the rise of the scientific renaissance (Unit 6 Lesson 1), printed p. 75.**
