# Lesson plan — Bengal's textiles, boats and ports

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Bengal's textiles, boats and ports
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Bangladesh, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bengal clothed and carried a trading world: fine textiles praised by the 9th-century Arab merchant Sulaiman and coveted into the 1600s, a river-forged boatbuilding industry, its own coins, and the ports of Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar trading 2,000 years ago with the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Industry — fine textiles and river-forged boats** — Bengal's famed fine textiles, praised by the 9th-century Arab merchant Sulaiman and coveted by Europeans into the 1600s, and its river-forged boatbuilding industry clothe and carry a trading people
2. **Trade, commerce and money — coins and ports** — from barter to punch-marked and Gupta, Gauda and Harikela coins, and through the ports of Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar, Bengal traded with the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia 2,000 years ago

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Bengal's two great industries in the book: fine textiles and boatbuilding.
2. Say who praised Bengal's textiles in the 9th century: the Arab merchant Sulaiman.
3. Say how long Europeans coveted Bengal's cloth: into the 1600s.
4. Tell the money story: from barter to punch-marked and Gupta, Gauda and Harikela coins.
5. Name Bengal's ports: Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar.
6. Name the regions Bengal traded with 2,000 years ago: the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia.
