# Hands-on — Bengal's textiles, boats and ports

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Loom and boat

Draw Bengal's two industries side by side — a loom with fine cloth, a boat on a river — and write under each what it did for trade.

## A traveller's praise

Write two sentences as Sulaiman might have: what Bengal's cloth was like, and why buyers far away wanted it.

## Money grows

Draw the money ladder: barter, punch-marked coin, named kingdom coins — and write one problem each step solved.

## Port map

Mark Bengal's three ports on your river-land map and draw trade arrows to the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia.
