# Bengal's textiles, boats and ports — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Industry — fine textiles and river-forged boats
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Name Bengal's two great industries in the book: fine textiles and boatbuilding. Say who praised Bengal's textiles in the 9th century: the Arab merchant Sulaiman. Say how long Europeans coveted Bengal's cloth: into the 1600s.

## Sub lesson 2 — Trade, commerce and money — coins and ports
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tell the money story: from barter to punch-marked and Gupta, Gauda and Harikela coins. Name Bengal's ports: Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar. Name the regions Bengal traded with 2,000 years ago: the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia.
