# Bengal's textiles, boats and ports — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bengal's famed industry praised by Sulaiman was —
a) Fine textiles  b) Iron swords  c) Glass
**Answer: a) Fine textiles** — the cloth Sulaiman praised.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Bengal's second great industry, forged by its rivers, was —
a) Paper making  b) Boatbuilding  c) Mining
**Answer: b) Boatbuilding** — the river-forged industry.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tamralipti, Chandraketugarh and Samandar were —
a) Coins  b) Ports of Bengal  c) Kings
**Answer: b) Ports of Bengal** — Bengal's doors to the sea.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 2,000 years ago Bengal traded with —
a) Only its villages  b) The Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia  c) The Americas
**Answer: b) The Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia** — the reach of Bengal's trade.
