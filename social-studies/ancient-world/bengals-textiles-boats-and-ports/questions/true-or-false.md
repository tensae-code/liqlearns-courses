# Bengal's textiles, boats and ports — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bengal's fine textiles were known and praised far beyond Bengal.
**Answer: True** — Sulaiman praised them and Europeans coveted them into the 1600s.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bengal's trade 2,000 years ago reached only the next village.
**Answer: False** — it reached the Mediterranean, Arabia, Africa and Southeast Asia.
