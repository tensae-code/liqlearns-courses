# Bengal's textiles, boats and ports — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who praised Bengal's textiles in the 9th century, and until when did Europeans covet them?
**Answer: The Arab merchant Sulaiman praised them; Europeans coveted them into the 1600s (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did Bengal's money change over time?
**Answer: From barter to punch-marked coins and the coins of Gupta, Gauda and Harikela times (any close wording).**
