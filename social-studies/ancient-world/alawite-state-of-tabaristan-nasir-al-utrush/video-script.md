# Video script — The Islamic state in the age of the Alawites in Tabaristan (Imam al-Nasir al-Utrush, 230-304 AH)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

al-Hasan bin Ali al-Utrush, born in Medina in 230 AH and schooled there and in Iraq, carries the call to Tabaristan south of the Caspian, takes it in 301 AH and enters Amul, its capital, at the head of his army, and rules Tabaristan, Gilan and Daylam on the same principles as the Zaydi states of Yemen.
