# Hands-on — The Islamic state in the age of the Alawites in Tabaristan (Imam al-Nasir al-Utrush, 230-304 AH)

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Islamic state in the age of the Alawites in Tabaristan (Imam al-Nasir al-Utrush, 230-304 AH) — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Islamic state in the age of the Alawites in Tabaristan (Imam al-Nasir al-Utrush, 230-304 AH), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
