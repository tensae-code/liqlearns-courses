# The Islamic state in the age of the Alawites in Tabaristan (Imam al-Nasir al-Utrush, 230-304 AH) — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** al-Hasan bin Ali al-Utrush, born in Medina in 230 AH and schooled there and in Iraq, carries the call to Tabaristan south of the Caspian, takes it in 301 AH and enters Amul, its capital, at the head of his army, and rules Tabaristan, Gilan and Daylam on the same principles as the Zaydi states of Yemen.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Islamic state in the age of the Alawites in Tabaristan (Imam al-Nasir al-Utrush, 230-304 AH)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Islamic state in the age of the Alawites in Tabaristan (Imam al-Nasir al-Utrush, 230-304 AH)
