# Story of Adam's two sons — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Story of Adam's two sons.
**Answer: in the first family on earth the two brothers offer sacrifices, Habil the shepherd his best animal and Qabil the farmer his crops, fire takes Habil's offering and not Qabil's — not for the gift but for the giver's lack of God-consciousness — envy turns to a threat, Habil refuses to raise his hand, Qabil kills his brother in his sleep, and a crow scratching the earth teaches the first murderer how to bury a body (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Story of Adam's two sons, printed p. 22.**
