# Islam comes to Afghanistan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 1 (Afghanistan and Islam), Lesson 1: The entry of Islam into Afghanistan

**Answer: False** — it is about Chapter 1 (Afghanistan and Islam), Lesson 1: The entry of Islam into Afghanistan

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 20: The state of Afghanistan at the advent of Islam

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History, Grade 10* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
