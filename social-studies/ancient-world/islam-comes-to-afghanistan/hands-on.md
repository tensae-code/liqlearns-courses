# Hands-on — Islam comes to Afghanistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Islam comes to Afghanistan — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Chapter 1 (Afghanistan and Islam), Lesson 1: The entry of Islam into Afghanistan, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
