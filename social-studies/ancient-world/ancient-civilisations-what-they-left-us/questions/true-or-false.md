# Ancient Civilisations What They Left Us — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Writing, law, calendars and building pass from the first civilisations into today's world, and pupils trace what still survives.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 1 §3: the influence of the ancient civilisations on the modern world

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 10 (World History 10), by Aram Boyajyan (Themes 1, 2, 4), Aram Nazaryan (Themes 1, 5, 6), Robert Ghazaryan (Theme 3)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 1 §3: the influence of the ancient civilisations on the modern world

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The year's opening theme makes pupils place themselves inside history and trace what the first civilisations still give the modern world.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Thematic plan, Theme 1: History and I

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 10 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց (World History 10, Methodical Guide), by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Thematic plan, Theme 1: History and I
