# Role of Yemenis in Islamic civilisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yemen becomes a scholarly centre of the Islamic world — Ahmad bin Hanbal, al-Shafi'i, Yahya bin Ma'in and Ishaq bin Rahwayh all study there, its schools shine at Zabid, Sanaa, al-Janad, Aden, Tarim, Kawkaban and Dhamar, and its scholars write the books others read, Wahb bin Munabih and al-Hasan al-Hamdani on history and the geography of Arabia, Abd al-Razzaq al-San'ani's Musannaf, Nashwan al-Himyari's Shams al-Ulum.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Role of Yemenis in Islamic civilisation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Role of Yemenis in Islamic civilisation

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sanaa's early stars — Tawus ibn Kaysan, Wahb ibn Munabbih, Ma'mar ibn Rashid, Abd al-Razzaq with his Musannaf — give way to Zabid's age of al-Umrani's al-Bayan and the Sufi Ibn Alwan, while al-Hamdani maps the Peninsula in Sifat Jazirat al-Arab and fills ten volumes of al-Iklil with Yemen's kings, and Nashwan al-Himyari gathers the language in Shams al-'Ulum.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Yemeni figures in the Arab Islamic sciences (Unit 4 Lesson 4)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الثاني (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Yemeni figures in the Arab Islamic sciences (Unit 4 Lesson 4)
