# Rus-Ukraine breaks apart — the Yaroslavychi — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yaroslav's testament kept Rus under one single ruler.
**Answer: False — It divides Rus among his sons.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Fragmentation left the land weaker against Polovtsian raids.
**Answer: True — The Polovtsians raid a land whose princes no longer stand together.**
