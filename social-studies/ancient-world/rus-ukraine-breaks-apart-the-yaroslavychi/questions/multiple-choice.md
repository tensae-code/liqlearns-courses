# Rus-Ukraine breaks apart — the Yaroslavychi — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** By Yaroslav's testament, Kyiv went to —
a) Iziaslav  b) Sviatoslav  c) Vsevolod
**Answer: a) Iziaslav**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Chernihiv went to —
a) Sviatoslav  b) Iziaslav  c) Vsevolod
**Answer: a) Sviatoslav**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Within years, the brothers were —
a) At war with each other  b) United against the steppe  c) All monks
**Answer: a) At war with each other**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** While the princes fought, the raiders were —
a) The Polovtsians  b) The Pechenegs of 972  c) The Mongols
**Answer: a) The Polovtsians**
