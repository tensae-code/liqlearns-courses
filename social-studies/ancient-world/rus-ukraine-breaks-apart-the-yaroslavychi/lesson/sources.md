**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Ukraine).**

Ukraine's own way: one national story told reign by reign from the book's own chronicle sources — every paragraph opens with a question, works a primary source (the Povist / chronicle excerpts, Ruska Pravda, Monomakh's Instruction), and closes with "Знаю минуле — осмислюю сьогодення — прогнозую майбутнє" (I know the past, I make sense of the present, I forecast the future) tasks, so the medieval Rus state is taught as Ukraine's own first state, "Rus-Ukraine", not as a neighbour's prehistory.

History in this lesson is summary in the course's own words — the book's stories and readings are filed as plot summaries only, never transcribed.
