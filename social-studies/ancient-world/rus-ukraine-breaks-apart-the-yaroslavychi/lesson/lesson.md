# Rus-Ukraine breaks apart — the Yaroslavychi

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Yaroslav's testament divides Rus among his sons — Iziaslav gets Kyiv, Sviatoslav Chernihiv, Vsevolod Pereiaslav — and within years the brothers are at war with each other while the Polovtsians raid a land whose princes no longer stand together. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Yaroslav's testament divides Rus

Yaroslav's testament divides Rus among his sons: Iziaslav gets Kyiv, Sviatoslav Chernihiv, Vsevolod Pereiaslav. One state becomes three shares — the Yaroslavychi.

## Sub lesson 2 — Brothers at war, Polovtsians raiding

Within years the brothers are at war with each other. Meanwhile the Polovtsians raid a land whose princes no longer stand together — fragmentation has a price, paid by ordinary people.
