# Kuwait's first civilisations — Ubaid Subiya and Hellenistic Failaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ubaid settlers at Subiya were —
a) potters and grain-grinders  b) iron smelters and sailors  c) silk weavers
**Answer: a) potters and grain-grinders** — potters and grain-grinders settle at Subiya

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Greeks called Failaka —
a) Ikaros  b) Dilmun  c) Magan
**Answer: a) Ikaros** — Greeks call Failaka Ikaros

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Greeks left behind on Failaka —
a) coins and Dilmun seals  b) steam engines  c) printed books
**Answer: a) coins and Dilmun seals** — they leave coins and Dilmun seals behind

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kuwait's Grade 10 book opens the national story with —
a) the civilisations that stood on Kuwait's coast and islands before any state  b) the oil age  c) the 1962 constitution
**Answer: a) the civilisations that stood on Kuwait's coast and islands before any state** — before any state, Kuwait's land gathers civilisations on its coast and islands

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The modern country claims these early civilisations as —
a) its deep roots  b) a foreign story  c) a legend to forget
**Answer: a) its deep roots** — the deep roots the modern country claims as its own

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The roots stand on —
a) Kuwait's coast and islands  b) distant continents only  c) the desert alone
**Answer: a) Kuwait's coast and islands** — civilisations gather on its coast and islands
