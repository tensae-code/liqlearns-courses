# Kuwait's first civilisations — Ubaid Subiya and Hellenistic Failaka — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** People lived in Kuwait's land thousands of years before the modern state.
**Answer: True** — Ubaid settlers stood at Subiya in 5500–4500 BCE

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Failaka's Greek name was Magan.
**Answer: False** — its Greek name was Ikaros

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kuwait's history begins only with the Al Sabah.
**Answer: False** — civilisations stood on its land long before any state

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Grade 10 book treats the early civilisations as Kuwait's roots.
**Answer: True** — they are the deep roots the modern country claims
