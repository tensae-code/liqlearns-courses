# Hands-on — Kuwait's first civilisations — Ubaid Subiya and Hellenistic Failaka

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Make a timeline strip: Ubaid Subiya, then Greek Failaka — mark the long gap between them.

## Task 2
Write what a coin and a seal each prove about the people who left them.

## Task 3
Draw Kuwait's coast and islands and mark where the first civilisations gathered.

## Task 4
Write two sentences: why does a modern country claim ancient roots?
