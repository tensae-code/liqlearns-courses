# Kuwait's first civilisations — Ubaid Subiya and Hellenistic Failaka

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Kuwait's land has drawn settlers for seven thousand years — Ubaid potters at Subiya, then Greeks on Failaka who called it Ikaros. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ubaid at Subiya and Hellenistic Failaka

Potters and grain-grinders settle at subiya in ubaid times (5500–4500 bce), then greeks settle well-watered failaka, call it ikaros, and leave coins and dilmun seals behind.
