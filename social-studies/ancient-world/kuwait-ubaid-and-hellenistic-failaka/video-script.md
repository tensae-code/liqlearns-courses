# Video script — Kuwait's first civilisations — Ubaid Subiya and Hellenistic Failaka

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Kuwait's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Potters and grain-grinders settle at subiya in ubaid times (5500–4500 bce), then greeks settle well-watered failaka, call it ikaros, and leave coins and dilmun seals behind.

Before any state, kuwait's land gathers civilisations on its coast and islands, the deep roots the modern country claims as its own.
