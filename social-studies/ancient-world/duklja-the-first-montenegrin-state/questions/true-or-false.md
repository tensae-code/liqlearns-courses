# Duklja — the first Montenegrin state — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On Rome's old frontier the Slavs of Duklja build the first state Montenegrins claim as their own.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2.1 Duklja (Zeta)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ИСТОРИЈА за седми разред основне школе (Istorija 7)* is about The Kingdom of Yugoslavia and the royal dictatorship.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.2.1 Duklja (Zeta)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A small coastal kingdom plays two empires and two churches against each other to stay a kingdom.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2.2 Zeta in the age of the Vojislavljevići: Stefan Vojislav's line holds Zeta; Prince Mihailo (1046–1081) inherits, and the 1054 division of the Christian Church into Orthodox and Catholic shapes his politics between Byzantium and Rome

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *ИСТОРИЈА за седми разред основне школе (Istorija 7)* is about Montenegro in the Yugoslav kingdom.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.2.2 Zeta in the age of the Vojislavljevići: Stefan Vojislav's line holds Zeta; Prince Mihailo (1046–1081) inherits, and the 1054 division of the Christian Church into Orthodox and Catholic shapes his politics between Byzantium and Rome
