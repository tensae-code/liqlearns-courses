## Sub lesson 1 — The Marib Dam

Saba throws a dam across Wadi Adhana between the two Balaq mountains in the 7th or 8th century BCE, kings restore it for centuries, and it gathers the rains, waters the two gardens and makes the kingdom feed itself — until Saba turns from God's path and the Flood of al-Arim bursts the dam, the gardens die, and the tribes scatter north in the famous scattering of Saba
