# The Marib Dam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Marib Dam.
**Answer: saba throws a dam across Wadi Adhana between the two Balaq mountains in the 7th or 8th century BCE, kings restore it for centuries, and it gathers the rains, waters the two gardens and makes the kingdom feed itself — until Saba turns from God's path and the Flood of al-Arim bursts the dam, the gardens die, and the tribes scatter north in the famous scattering of Saba (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), The Marib Dam, printed p. 83.**
