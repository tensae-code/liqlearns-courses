# The Marib Dam — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saba throws a dam across Wadi Adhana between the two Balaq mountains in the 7th or 8th century BCE, kings restore it for centuries, and it gathers the rains, waters the two gardens and makes the kingdom feed itself — until Saba turns from God's path and the Flood of al-Arim bursts the dam, the gardens die, and the tribes scatter north in the famous scattering of Saba.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Marib Dam

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Marib Dam
