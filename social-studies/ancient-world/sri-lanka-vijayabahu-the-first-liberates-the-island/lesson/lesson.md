# Sri Lanka — Vijayabahu the First liberates the island

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Vijayabahu I's youth and war of liberation

A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Political history 1017–1270: liberation

From a Chola-ruled island, Vijayabahu I fights his way back and is crowned at Anuradhapura in 1070, then rules from Polonnaruwa

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
