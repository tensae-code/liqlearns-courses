# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — Vijayabahu the First liberates the island

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — Vijayabahu the First liberates the island — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Vijayabahu I's youth and war of liberation, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
