# Sri Lanka — Vijayabahu the First liberates the island — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A prince raised in hiding gathers Ruhuna, survives defeat in 1065, and in 1070 is crowned king of the whole island at Anuradhapura.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Vijayabahu I's youth and war of liberation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History Tamil 2011* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Vijayabahu I's youth and war of liberation

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From a Chola-ruled island, Vijayabahu I fights his way back and is crowned at Anuradhapura in 1070, then rules from Polonnaruwa.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Political history 1017–1270: liberation
