# Hands-on — The Hebrews — one God and a covenant

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Hebrews — one God and a covenant — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, History Ch. 1 Lesson 4, The early Israelites, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
