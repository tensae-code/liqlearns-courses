# The Hittites Empire Of Anatolia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Hittites.
**Answer: an Anatolian empire masters iron and chariots, fights Egypt to a standstill and signs the first known peace treaty (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Αρχαίου Κόσμου — Από τους προϊστορικούς πολιτισμούς της Ανατολής έως την εποχή του Ιουστινιανού (History of the Ancient World, A' General Lyceum)* (Greece), The Hittites, printed p. 42.**
