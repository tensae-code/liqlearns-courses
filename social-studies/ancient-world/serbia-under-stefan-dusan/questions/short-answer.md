# Serbia Under Stefan Dusan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The rise of Serbian power under Stefan Dušan (1331–1355).
**Answer: Dušan crowns himself emperor of Serbs and Romans and nearly replaces Byzantium in the Balkans (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Μεσαιωνικού και του Νεότερου Κόσμου 565–1815 (History of the Medieval and Modern World), Β' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Dimitroukas, Thoukydidis P. Ioannou & Kostas Baroutas* (Greece), The rise of Serbian power under Stefan Dušan (1331–1355), printed p. 81.**
