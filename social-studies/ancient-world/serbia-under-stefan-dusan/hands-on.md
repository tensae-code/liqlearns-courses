# Hands-on — Serbia Under Stefan Dusan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Serbia Under Stefan Dusan — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The rise of Serbian power under Stefan Dušan (1331–1355), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
