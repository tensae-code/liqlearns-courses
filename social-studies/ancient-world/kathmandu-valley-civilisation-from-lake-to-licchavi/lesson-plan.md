# Lesson plan — Kathmandu Valley civilisation — from lake to Licchavi

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Kathmandu Valley civilisation — from lake to Licchavi
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Nepal, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the valley begins as the Nagdaha lake; the sage plants the lotus, Manjushree cuts the Chobhar gorge and drains it, Gopal, Mahispal and Kirat dynasties follow, and pupils chart the valley's ancient social condition against its condition now
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
