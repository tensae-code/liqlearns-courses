# Lesson plan — Chinggis Khan and the founding of the Mongol state

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Chinggis Khan and the founding of the Mongol state
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Mongolia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the orphaned Temüjin of the Secret History — his family captured, his wife abducted and rescued — becomes the man who will unite the steppe
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 4.1.1 The Chinggis Khan we know** — the orphaned Temüjin of the Secret History — his family captured, his wife abducted and rescued — becomes the man who will unite the steppe
2. **Ch. 4.1.2 Chinggis Khan founds the Great Mongol State** — in 1206 the Onon kurultai proclaims Chinggis Khan over all the felt-walled tribes; the Great Mongol State (Yeke Mongγol Ulus) is born
3. **Ch. 4.1.3 Chinggis Khan consolidates his state** — the Yassa law code, the decimal army, the Uyghur-derived script and the yam relay bind the new state together before the conquests begin
4. **Ch. 4.1.5 Chinggis Khan, founder of the Great Mongol nation** — the book weighs his legacy — one people, one script, one law and a state tradition every later Mongol age claims descent from
5. **Ch. 3.2 The Great Mongol State** — 1206's kurultai, the conquests of Jin, Khwarezm and Rus', the Yassa, the script and the yam — the state Chinggis built, studied through its sources
6. **Ch. 3.3 Chinggis Khan's role in history** — conqueror or state-founder? The chapter weighs his destruction against the Pax Mongolica, the relay roads and the single Eurasian world he opened

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
