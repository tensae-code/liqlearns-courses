# India — rivers and early peoples

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: People lived along the Narmada for hundreds of thousands of years; farming began in the Sulaiman–Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago, and cities rose on the Indus and then the Ganga. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Where did people live?

Gatherers live along the Narmada for hundreds of thousands of years; wheat and barley are first grown in the Sulaiman–Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago; cities rise on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago, where Magadha's rulers grow powerful.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
