# Video script — India — rivers and early peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Gatherers live along the Narmada for hundreds of thousands of years; wheat and barley are first grown in the Sulaiman–Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago; cities rise on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago, where Magadha's rulers grow powerful.
