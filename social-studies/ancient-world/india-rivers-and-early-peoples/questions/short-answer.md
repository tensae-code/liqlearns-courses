# India — rivers and early peoples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Where did people live?.
**Answer: Gatherers live along the Narmada for hundreds of thousands of years; wheat and barley are first grown in the Sulaiman–Kirthar hills about 8000 years ago; cities rise on the Indus about 4700 years ago and on the Ganga about 2500 years ago, where Magadha's rulers grow powerful (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.1 / Where did people live?, printed pp. 1–3.**
