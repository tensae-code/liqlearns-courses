# Video script — Muhammad bin Qasim and the coming of Islam to Sindh

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In 712 the young Umayyad general Muhammad bin Qasim lands in Sindh, defeats Raja Dahir, and opens the door through which Islam enters South Asia — where Pakistan's own story begins.
