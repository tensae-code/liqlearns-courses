# Hands-on — Muhammad bin Qasim and the coming of Islam to Sindh

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Muhammad bin Qasim and the coming of Islam to Sindh — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Muhammad bin Qasim and the coming of Islam to Sindh, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
