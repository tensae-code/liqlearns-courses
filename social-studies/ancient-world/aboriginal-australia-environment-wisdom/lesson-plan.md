**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

1. **Ancient civilisations in Australia** — australia's first peoples, arrived by sea perhaps from South-East Asia by 1600 BCE (as the book dates it), master rock engraving and painting thousands of years before Europe finds them, and hold a knowledge so deep in nature's seasons and secrets that historians name it "environment wisdom"; one old language splinters into more than five hundred tribal tongues yet the people still understand one another at gatherings, their songs and dances carry the ancestors' stories down the generations, and their philosophies — a pure spiritual force in nature, a force preserving life's continuation, and the sages' teaching to love and defend the earth — keep man and nature in balance
