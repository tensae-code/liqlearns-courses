# Video script — Ancient civilisations in Australia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Australia's first peoples, arrived by sea perhaps from South-East Asia by 1600 BCE (as the book dates it), master rock engraving and painting thousands of years before Europe finds them, and hold a knowledge so deep in nature's seasons and secrets that historians name it "environment wisdom"; one old language splinters into more than five hundred tribal tongues yet the people still understand one another at gatherings, their songs and dances carry the ancestors' stories down the generations, and their philosophies — a pure spiritual force in nature, a force preserving life's continuation, and the sages' teaching to love and defend the earth — keep man and nature in balance.
