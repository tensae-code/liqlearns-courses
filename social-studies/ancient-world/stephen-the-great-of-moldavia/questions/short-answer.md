# Stephen The Great Of Moldavia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare.
**Answer: Stephen the Great rules 47 years, wins battle after battle against the Ottomans and raises a church after each victory (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* (Moldova), Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare, printed p. 32.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare.
**Answer: caught between Hungary, Poland and the Ottomans, Stephen wins at Vaslui in 1475, calls Moldavia "the gate of Christendom" and begs Europe's monarchs for help that barely comes (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor şi universală: Manual pentru clasa a VI-a* (Moldova), Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare, printed p. 88.**
