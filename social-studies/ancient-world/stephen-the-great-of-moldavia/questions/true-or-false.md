# Stephen The Great Of Moldavia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stephen the Great rules 47 years, wins battle after battle against the Ottomans and raises a church after each victory.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 12: Domnia lui Ștefan cel Mare

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Caught between Hungary, Poland and the Ottomans, Stephen wins at Vaslui in 1475, calls Moldavia "the gate of Christendom" and begs Europe's monarchs for help that barely comes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor şi universală: Manual pentru clasa a VI-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unitatea II, Lecția 10: Țara Moldovei în timpul domniei lui Ştefan cel Mare
