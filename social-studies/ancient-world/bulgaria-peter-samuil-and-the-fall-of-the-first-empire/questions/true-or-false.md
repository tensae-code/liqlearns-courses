# Bulgaria — Peter, Samuil and the fall of the First Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Peter's long peace, then Samuil's desperate wars end with Basil II and the fall of the First Empire (1018).
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 12: Tsar Peter and his heirs; the wars of Samuil

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История и цивилизации за шести клас (History and Civilizations for the 6th Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 12: Tsar Peter and his heirs; the wars of Samuil

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Samuil's last stand ends at Kleidion; the First Empire falls to Byzantium.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 12: The sunset of the First Bulgarian Kingdom (976–1018)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История и цивилизации, 10. клас (History and Civilizations, 10th Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 12: The sunset of the First Bulgarian Kingdom (976–1018)
