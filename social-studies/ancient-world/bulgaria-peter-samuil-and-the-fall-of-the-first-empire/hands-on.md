# Hands-on — Bulgaria — Peter, Samuil and the fall of the First Empire

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bulgaria — Peter, Samuil and the fall of the First Empire — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Lesson 12: Tsar Peter and his heirs; the wars of Samuil, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
