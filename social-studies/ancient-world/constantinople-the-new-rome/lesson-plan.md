# Lesson plan — Constantinople The New Rome

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Constantinople The New Rome
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Constantine founds a New Rome on the Bosporus, where Europe meets Asia and the sea lanes cross
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 6 A new capital, Constantinople** — Constantine founds a New Rome on the Bosporus, where Europe meets Asia and the sea lanes cross
2. **Unit 7 Constantinople is fortified and adorned with works of art** — Theodosian walls ring the city while forums, columns and churches dress the new capital as an empress
3. **Unit 6 New capital exercises** — pupils match other refounded capitals to Constantinople's story
4. **Unit 7 Fortified and adorned Constantinople exercises** — picture work reads the walls and monuments as the city's armour and crown
5. **Ch. J5 Constantine: the great turn** — Constantine wins under the cross, tolerates and favours Christianity, and founds Constantinople — antiquity turns toward Byzantium
6. **Ch. 1.I.1 From Rome to the New Rome** — Constantine moves the empire's heart east and the Roman state begins its thousand-year Greek transformation

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
