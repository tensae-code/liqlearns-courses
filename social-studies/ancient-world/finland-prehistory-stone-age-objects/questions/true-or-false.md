# Finland prehistory — Stone Age objects — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stone Age hands shape a small clay elk whose meaning is still unknown, a figure from a world that left no words.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Esine 1: Savi-idoli-hirvi (clay elk idol, Neolithic)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Esihistorian mysteeriesineet (student, FI/SV)* is about Esine 6: Lukkoja ja avaimia (Iron Age / Viking Age locks and a padlock from Hämeenlinna, c. 800–1050).
**Answer: False** — it is about Esine 1: Savi-idoli-hirvi (clay elk idol, Neolithic)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A chewed lump of birch pitch, chewed to clean teeth, still carries the tooth marks of a Stone Age chewer.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Esine 7: Purupihka (birch-pitch chewing gum, Stone Age)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Esihistorian mysteeriesineet (student, FI/SV)* is about Esine 5: Korvalusikka (ear spoon, Viking Age c. 800–1050).
**Answer: False** — it is about Esine 7: Purupihka (birch-pitch chewing gum, Stone Age)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A near half-metre chisel of stone carried hundreds of kilometres, never used, is sunk in rapids — apparently an offering.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Esine 8: Korutaltta (giant green-schist gouge, Neolithic, found at Kiuruvesi)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Esihistorian mysteeriesineet (student, FI/SV)* is about Taustatietoa Carta Marinasta (background: Finland enters Europe's maps).
**Answer: False** — it is about Esine 8: Korutaltta (giant green-schist gouge, Neolithic, found at Kiuruvesi)
