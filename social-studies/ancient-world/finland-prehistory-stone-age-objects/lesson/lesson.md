# Finland prehistory — Stone Age objects

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Stone Age hands shape a small clay elk whose meaning is still unknown, a figure from a world that left no words As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Esine 1: Savi-idoli-hirvi (clay elk idol, Neolithic)

Stone Age hands shape a small clay elk whose meaning is still unknown, a figure from a world that left no words.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Esine 7: Purupihka (birch-pitch chewing gum, Stone Age)

A chewed lump of birch pitch, chewed to clean teeth, still carries the tooth marks of a Stone Age chewer.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Esine 8: Korutaltta (giant green-schist gouge, Neolithic, found at Kiuruvesi)

A near half-metre chisel of stone carried hundreds of kilometres, never used, is sunk in rapids — apparently an offering.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
