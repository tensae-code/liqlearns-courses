# Video script — Iran — ancient art and architecture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iran's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Persepolis's columns and Sasanian arches are read as arguments in stone — what a civilisation builds is what it believes about itself.

the book closes its ancient arc with what the mind made — knowledge and art as the civilisation's surviving voice.

two whole lessons inside one ruin — columns, staircases and tribute processions teach what the Achaemenid Empire was by what it built.
