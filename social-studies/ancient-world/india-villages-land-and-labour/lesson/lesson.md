# India — villages, land and labour

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In India's villages, irrigation followed the tax demand for surer crops; Tamil vellalar, uzhavar and landless kadaisiyar and adimai, and the north's grama bhojaka, stack the village order. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Irrigation and who lived in the villages

Kings tax farmers, farmers demand surer crops, irrigation follows — a chain the chapter makes pupils complete; in Tamil villages vellalar landowners, uzhavar ploughmen and landless kadaisiyar and adimai labourers stack the social order, while in the north the hereditary grama bhojaka is landlord, tax-collector, judge and policeman in one.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
