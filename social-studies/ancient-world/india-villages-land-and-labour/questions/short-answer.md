# India — villages, land and labour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Irrigation and who lived in the villages.
**Answer: Kings tax farmers, farmers demand surer crops, irrigation follows — a chain the chapter makes pupils complete; in Tamil villages vellalar landowners, uzhavar ploughmen and landless kadaisiyar and adimai labourers stack the social order, while in the north the hereditary grama bhojaka is landlord, tax-collector, judge and policeman in one (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.8 Vital Villages, Thriving Towns / irrigation and who lived in the villages, printed pp. 79–81.**
