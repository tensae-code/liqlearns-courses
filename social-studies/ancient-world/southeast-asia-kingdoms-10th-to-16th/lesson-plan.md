# Lesson plan — Southeast Asia Kingdoms 10th To 16th

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Southeast Asia Kingdoms 10th To 16th
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pagan unifies Myanmar, the Khmer empire flourishes, Srivijaya commands Sumatra; the Mongol invasions of the 13th century push the Thai to unite Sukhothai and Ayutthaya, the Lao to found Lan Xang, and the islands to gather under Majapahit, while Malacca is founded

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 6, the kingdoms consolidate, late 10th–early 16th centuries** — Pagan unifies Myanmar, the Khmer empire flourishes, Srivijaya commands Sumatra; the Mongol invasions of the 13th century push the Thai to unite Sukhothai and Ayutthaya, the Lao to found Lan Xang, and the islands to gather under Majapahit, while Malacca is founded
2. **Bài 6, Malacca, the world's market, and stronger states** — a quoted account calls 15th-century Malacca perhaps the one place on Earth where Chinese porcelain and silk, Javanese rice, West Asian woollens and Southeast Asian timber, incense and pepper all change hands; meanwhile law codes (Indonesia's Sivasasana, Đại Việt's Hình thư) tighten royal power
3. **Bài 6, the culture of the kingdoms** — Theravada Buddhism spreads from the 13th century and Islam comes with Arab and Indian traders; Thai script forms about 1200, Lao in the 14th century, the Viet create chữ Nôm from Han characters; the Nagarakretagama and the Malay Annals are written, and Angkor Wat and the Shwedagon rise
