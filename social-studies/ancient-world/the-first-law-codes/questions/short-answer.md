# The first law codes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the two lawgivers the book names, in order.
**Answer: Ur-Namma first, then Hammurabi (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book call the published stone the birth of laws?
**Answer: Because law written and set where everyone can read it binds judge and judged alike, instead of depending on a ruler's mood (any close wording).**
