# Hands-on — The first law codes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your stone of laws

Write three laws for your classroom or home on a big sheet — your stone. Post it where everyone can see it. Under each law, write what happens if it is broken, so the consequence is known before, not decided in anger.

## Before the stone

Act out a quarrel judged twice: first by a 'chief' deciding from his mood, then by the same chief reading a written law. Afterwards, say which judgement felt fairer, and why.

## Read the stele

Draw Hammurabi's tall stone with rows of carved signs. Above it, draw the king receiving the laws. Write one sentence on why the stone stood in a public place, not inside the palace.
