# Video script — The first law codes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before written law, justice was the strong man's mood. Turkey's Grade 9 book tells how laws were born.

The earliest code it names is Ur-Namma's, set down in Sumer. The most famous is Hammurabi's of Babylon, carved on a tall stone in public.

The stone is the point. Written and published, law binds the judge as well as the judged — a poor man can point at it. Justice stops being a mood and becomes a stone everyone can read.
