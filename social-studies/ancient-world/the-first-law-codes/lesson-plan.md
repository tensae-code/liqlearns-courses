# Lesson plan — The first law codes

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The first law codes
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 2, Topic 2.6, Laws Are Born: the first written law codes, from Ur-Namma to Hammurabi.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: justice stops being the strong man's mood and becomes a stone everyone can read. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the earliest law code the book names: Ur-Namma's, in Sumer.
2. Name the famous Babylonian code: Hammurabi's, carved on stone for all to see.
3. Say what changes when law is written and published: it binds judge and judged alike.
4. Explain the book's beat in their own words: justice becomes a stone everyone can read.
