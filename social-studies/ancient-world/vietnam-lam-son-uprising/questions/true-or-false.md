# Vietnam Lam Son Uprising — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** at the 1416 Lũng Nhai oath Lê Lợi and 18 heroes swear together; in 1418 he raises the banner as Bình Định Vương and, with Nguyễn Trãi's strategy of winning hearts, fights the Ming for ten years to complete victory.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 12, the Lam Sơn uprising, 1418–1427

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 12, the Lam Sơn uprising, 1418–1427

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** besieged on Chí Linh mountain in 1418, Lê Lai puts on Lê Lợi's robe, charges out and is killed in his place; the saying runs "the 21st for Lê Lai, the 22nd for Lê Lợi".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 12, story of Lê Lai giving his life for his lord

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 12, story of Lê Lai giving his life for his lord

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the Ming relief army is drawn into the Chi Lăng pass; cannon fire signals the ambush from both slopes and commander Liễu Thăng falls to a javelin at Mã Yên slope, breaking the relief of Đông Quan.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 12, the Chi Lăng victory

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 12, the Chi Lăng victory

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** at the Lũng Nhai oath of 1416 Lê Lợi and eighteen heroes swear to expel the Ming; Nguyễn Trãi joins him; three times the army falls back on the Chí Linh mountains, once to barely a hundred men — Lê Lai disguises himself as Lê Lợi to draw off the pursuit and dies, and in the famine of 1421 Lê Lợi kills his own elephants and horses to feed the troops.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 16, the raising of Lam Sơn and the hard years, 1418–1423

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 16, the raising of Lam Sơn and the hard years, 1418–1423

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Nguyễn Chích's plan — "Nghệ An is strategic ground, wide land, many people" — breaks the encirclement; by late 1424 Nghệ An is free and the liberation runs from Thanh Hoá to the Hải Vân pass; in 1426 the army marches north, the people rise everywhere, and the Ming shut themselves in Đông Quan.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 16, the turn: Nghệ An and the march north, 1424–1426

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 16, the turn: Nghệ An and the march north, 1424–1426

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** Vương Thông's reinforcements are ambushed at Tốt Động – Chúc Động (November 1426); in October 1427 Liễu Thăng is killed in the Chi Lăng pass ambush and Lê Lợi sends his seal and the news to the second column, which flees; on 10 December 1427 Lê Lợi and Vương Thông swear the Đông Quan oath on the Hồng river bank, Nguyễn Trãi proclaims the Bình Ngô đại cáo, and in January 1428 the Ming withdraw.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 16, total victory: Tốt Động – Chúc Động, Chi Lăng – Xương Giang and the Đông Quan oath, 1426–1427

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.

**13. (Sub lesson 7)** a pen besieges Đông Quan more cheaply than an army, then writes the independence proclamation in verse.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chuyên đề 3.IV Nguyễn Trãi (1380–1442): Lê Lợi's strategist of "tâm công" — attacking the enemy's hearts; his letters, said to be worth 100,000 troops, win cities without slaughter; after victory he drafts the Bình Ngô đại cáo and founds chữ Nôm literature with the Quốc âm thi tập; UNESCO honours him in 1980

**15. (Sub lesson 8)** ten years from hunted band to liberation army, closed not with a massacre but a sworn peace.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 8 The Lam Sơn uprising (1418–1427): after the Hồ fall, the Ming rename the country Giao Chỉ and burn its books; Lê Lợi swears the Lũng Nhai oath (1416), survives three retreats to Chí Linh, turns south on Nguyễn Chích's plan, wins Tốt Động–Chúc Động (1426) and Chi Lăng–Xương Giang (1427), then ends the war with the Đông Quan oath and the Bình Ngô đại cáo
