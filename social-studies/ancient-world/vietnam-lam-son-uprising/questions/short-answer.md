**Answer: at the 1416 Lũng Nhai oath Lê Lợi and 18 heroes swear together; in 1418 he raises the banner as Bình Định Vương and, with Nguyễn Trãi's strategy of winning hearts, fights the Ming for ten years to complete victory (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 16, total victory: Tốt Động – Chúc Động, Chi Lăng – Xương Giang and the Đông Quan oath, 1426–1427.
**Answer: Vương Thông's reinforcements are ambushed at Tốt Động – Chúc Động (November 1426); in October 1427 Liễu Thăng is killed in the Chi Lăng pass ambush and Lê Lợi sends his seal and the news to the second column, which flees; on 10 December 1427 Lê Lợi and Vương Thông swear the Đông Quan oath on the Hồng river bank, Nguyễn Trãi proclaims the Bình Ngô đại cáo, and in January 1428 the Ming withdraw (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
