# Abu Bakr and the Ridda — the first caliphate holds Arabia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abu Bakr was caliph before the Prophet's death.
**Answer: False** — he is pledged at the Saqifa after the Prophet dies

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ridda wars reunited Arabia.
**Answer: True** — they pull Arabia back into one state
