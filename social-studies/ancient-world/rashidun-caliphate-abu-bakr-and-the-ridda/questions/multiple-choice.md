# Abu Bakr and the Ridda — the first caliphate holds Arabia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At the Saqifa the community —
a) nearly split, then pledged Abu Bakr  b) chose no leader  c) left Madinah
**Answer: a) nearly split, then pledged Abu Bakr** — it nearly splits before pledging Abu Bakr

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ridda was —
a) tribes apostatising and withholding zakat  b) a tax reform  c) a building project
**Answer: a) tribes apostatising and withholding zakat** — tribes apostatise and withhold zakat in the Ridda

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Abu Bakr's wars —
a) pulled Arabia back into one state  b) divided Arabia  c) stayed inside Madinah
**Answer: a) pulled Arabia back into one state** — his wars pull Arabia back into one state
