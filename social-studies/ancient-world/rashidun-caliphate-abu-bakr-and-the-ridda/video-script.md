# Video script — Abu Bakr and the Ridda — the first caliphate holds Arabia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Kuwait's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The prophet dies and the community nearly splits at the saqifa before pledging abu bakr; tribes then apostatise and withhold zakat in the ridda, and abu bakr's wars pull arabia back into one state that begins to expand.
