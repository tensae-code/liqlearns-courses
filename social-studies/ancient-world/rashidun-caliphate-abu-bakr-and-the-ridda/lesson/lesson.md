# Abu Bakr and the Ridda — the first caliphate holds Arabia

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: When the Prophet died, the community nearly split at the Saqifa before pledging Abu Bakr; when tribes apostatised in the Ridda, his wars pulled Arabia back into one state. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Abu Bakr — the Saqifa and the Ridda

The prophet dies and the community nearly splits at the saqifa before pledging abu bakr; tribes then apostatise and withhold zakat in the ridda, and abu bakr's wars pull arabia back into one state that begins to expand.
