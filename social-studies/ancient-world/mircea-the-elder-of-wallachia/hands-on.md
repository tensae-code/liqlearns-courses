# Hands-on — Mircea The Elder Of Wallachia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Mircea The Elder Of Wallachia — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unitatea II, Lecția 7: Țara Românească în timpul domniei lui Mircea cel Bătrân (1386–1418), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
