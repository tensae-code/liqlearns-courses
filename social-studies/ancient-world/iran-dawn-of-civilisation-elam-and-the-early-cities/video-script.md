# Video script — Iran — dawn of civilisation: Elam and the early cities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iran's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

long before Persia, Elam and the plateau's early cities farm, build and write — Iranian civilisation has a dawn far older than its empires.

the first Iranian farmers settle, build and bury their dead in the plateau's oldest sites, thousands of years before any king.

villages grow walls, markets and rulers, and the city appears on the Iranian plateau.
