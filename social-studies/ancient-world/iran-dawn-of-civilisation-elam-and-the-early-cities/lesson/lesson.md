# Iran — dawn of civilisation: Elam and the early cities

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: long before Persia, Elam and the plateau's early cities farm, build and write — Iranian civilisation has a dawn far older than its empires As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
