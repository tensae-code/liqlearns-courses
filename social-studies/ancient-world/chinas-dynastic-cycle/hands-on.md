# Hands-on — China's dynastic cycle

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Turn the wheel
Draw a big wheel on card, divided into rise / good rule / ageing / fall. A narrator reads a dynasty's story aloud; the class turns the wheel to the right station at each event and defends the turn.

## The Mandate court
Stage a court scene: officials report a flood and a tax revolt. The 'emperor' must answer in Mandate terms — what will he change to show heaven he still deserves to rule?
