# Video script — China's dynastic cycle

The story-style script for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list), in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Super-simple voice. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on China. A wheel appears above the palace. It begins to turn.
A founder rises. Order returns. The Mandate of Heaven shines on his house.
Generations pass. The wheel turns. Taxes bite, rivers flood, officials grow fat.
Heaven withdraws its Mandate. The dynasty falls — and a new founder steps onto the wheel.
Two thousand years, the wheel turned. Rise. Decay. Replacement. That is the dynastic cycle.

[Grade 11 depth scene — China's dynastic cycle in depth — Tang, Song and Ming. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The cycle turns at Grade 11 depth: each dynasty rises in founding vigour, flowers in prosperity, decays in corruption and over-taxation, and falls to rebellion — replaced by the next. The names to hold: dynastic cycle, bureaucracy.
