# China's dynastic cycle — short answer

Answer in one or two sentences.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Explain the Mandate of Heaven in two sentences.
**Answer:** Heaven grants the right to rule; the dynasty keeps that right only while it governs well, and disasters and rebellion signal the Mandate is being withdrawn.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Draw the dynastic cycle and label its three stages.
**Answer:** Rise — decay — replacement (then the new dynasty rises).

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Give one argument that the Mandate idea encouraged rebellion, and one that it restrained rulers.
**Answer:** Encouraged: a rebel could claim a failing dynasty had lost the Mandate. Restrained: an emperor who feared losing it had reason to govern well.
