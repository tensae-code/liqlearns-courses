# China's dynastic cycle — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Imperial China was administered day to day by —
a) hereditary warlords  b) highly educated Confucian scholar-officials  c) elected assemblies
**Answer: b) highly educated Confucian scholar-officials** — Scholar-officials ran the autocracy.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mandate of Heaven says rule lasts —
a) forever, once seized  b) while heaven's mandate is kept  c) for exactly 300 years
**Answer: b) while heaven's mandate is kept** — Good rule keeps the Mandate.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The dynastic cycle runs —
a) rise, decay, replacement  b) fall, rise, fall only  c) one dynasty, eternally
**Answer: a) rise, decay, replacement** — The wheel's three beats.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Signs the Mandate was slipping included —
a) good harvests  b) floods, rebellions and decay  c) new classics written
**Answer: b) floods, rebellions and decay** — Disaster read as heaven's warning.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide's evaluation asks whether the cycle and Mandate —
a) encouraged rebellion  b) were invented by Europeans  c) ended Confucianism
**Answer: a) encouraged rebellion** — A failing dynasty had 'lost' the Mandate — so what had a rebel done wrong?

**6. (Sub lesson 1)** An autocracy is rule by —
a) an assembly  b) one ruler  c) age grades
**Answer: b) one ruler** — One emperor, served by officials.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What is dynastic cycle?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) China's pattern of founding, prosperity, decay, rebellion, replacement
c) rule by examined officials, a Chinese achievement of the cycle years
**Answer: b) China's pattern of founding, prosperity, decay, rebellion, replacement**
