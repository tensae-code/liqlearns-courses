# China's dynastic cycle — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The emperor needed no justification beyond his own power, in this system.
**Answer: False** — Heaven's mandate stood above him.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Every dynasty in the cycle was expected eventually to decay and be replaced.
**Answer: True** — That is the cycle's teaching.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The scholar-officials were Confucian — educated in the classics.
**Answer: True** — Highly educated Confucian scholar-officials.
