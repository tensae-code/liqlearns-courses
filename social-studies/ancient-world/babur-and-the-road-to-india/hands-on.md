# Hands-on — Babur and the Road to India

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Babur and the Road to India — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 30. India and Babur, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
