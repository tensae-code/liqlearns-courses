# Babur and the Road to India

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Temur's heir Babur loses Samarkand three times, takes Kabul, and at Panipat (1526) founds the Mughal empire in India, writing his Baburnama. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
