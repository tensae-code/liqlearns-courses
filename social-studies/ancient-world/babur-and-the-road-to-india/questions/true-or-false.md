# Babur and the Road to India — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Temur's heir Babur loses Samarkand three times, takes Kabul, and at Panipat (1526) founds the Mughal empire in India, writing his Baburnama on the way.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 30. India and Babur

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 30. India and Babur

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Fergana prince who lost Samarkand to Shaybani three times becomes the book's model ruler-writer; his road leads through Kabul to India and the Baburnama.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. I §4 Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur — a great statesman and thinker

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Узбекистана 8 (History of Uzbekistan 8 — 16th to mid-19th century)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. I §4 Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur — a great statesman and thinker
