# Babur and the Road to India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 30. India and Babur.
**Answer: Temur's heir Babur loses Samarkand three times, takes Kabul, and at Panipat (1526) founds the Mughal empire in India, writing his Baburnama on the way (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* (Uzbekistan), 30. India and Babur, printed p. 96.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. I §4 Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur — a great statesman and thinker.
**Answer: the Fergana prince who lost Samarkand to Shaybani three times becomes the book's model ruler-writer; his road leads through Kabul to India and the Baburnama (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Узбекистана 8 (History of Uzbekistan 8 — 16th to mid-19th century)* (Uzbekistan), Ch. I §4 Zahiriddin Muhammad Babur — a great statesman and thinker, printed p. 14.**
