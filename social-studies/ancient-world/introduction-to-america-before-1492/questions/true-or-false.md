# Introduction To America Before 1492 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Americas hold everything from bands to empires at once; pupils must explain why development was so uneven.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 5.4–5.5 Introduction to America; unequal development of American aboriginal communities

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia del Mundo Antiguo al Medieval* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 5.4–5.5 Introduction to America; unequal development of American aboriginal communities

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** at 1492 the Americas hold bands, chiefdoms and empires side by side; the book asks why the ladder's rungs are so uneven.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1.2 Unequal development of American aboriginal communities

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de América — Décimo grado (record title: Temas de Historia de América)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1.2 Unequal development of American aboriginal communities
