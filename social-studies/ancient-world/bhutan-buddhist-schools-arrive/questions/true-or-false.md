# Bhutan — Buddhist schools arrive — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** from the 11th to the 17th century, with no central authority to stop them, Tibetan masters of Nyingma, Kagyu, Sakya and Geluk offshoots settle valley by valley, until Nyingma and Drukpa Kagyu absorb or outlast the rest and Drukpa Kagyu becomes the state religion
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter One, Arrival of Buddhist Traditions in Bhutan).
