# Prehistoric art on Polish lands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The caves, amber-working and early settlements of the Polish lands carry the early story.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — The world story of prehistoric art comes home — the caves, amber-working and early settlements of the Polish lands show the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns happening in the learners' own ground.
