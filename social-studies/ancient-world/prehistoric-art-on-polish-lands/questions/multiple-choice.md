# Prehistoric art on Polish lands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Caves and amber  b) The story comes home  c) The same turns, in our own ground
**Answer: b) The story comes home** — After the world's caves and first farms, the book turns to the Polish lands themselves.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Caves and amber  b) The story comes home  c) The same turns, in our own ground
**Answer: a) Caves and amber** — The caves, amber-working and early settlements of the Polish lands carry the early story.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) The story comes home  b) Caves and amber  c) The same turns, in our own ground
**Answer: c) The same turns, in our own ground** — The Polish lands show the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns — hunting art first, then settled farming life — happening in the learners' own ground.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The world story of prehistoric art comes home — the caves, amber-working and early settlements of the Polish lands show the same Palaeolithic and Neolithic turns happening in the learners' own ground  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
