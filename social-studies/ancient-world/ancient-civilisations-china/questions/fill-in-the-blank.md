# Ancient civilisations — China — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** China was famed for silk and ____.
**Answer: porcelain**
**2.** The ____ dynasty centred on the Huang He valley came first.
**Answer: Shang**
**3.** Shih Huang-ti ruled the ____ dynasty.
**Answer: Qin**
