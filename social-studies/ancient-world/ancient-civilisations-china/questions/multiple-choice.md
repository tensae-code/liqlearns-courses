# Ancient civilisations — China — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** China's civilisation grew on the Yellow and the —
a) Indus  b) Yangtze  c) Tigris  d) Nile
**Answer: b) Yangtze** — The Huang He (Yellow) and the Yangtze.
**2.** China was unified under Shih Huang-ti by the —
a) Shang  b) Zhou  c) Qin  d) Han
**Answer: c) Qin** — Qin, 221–206 BC — short but unifying.
**3.** Under the Han, Confucian philosophy —
a) Was banned  b) Spread, as arts and science flourished  c) Vanished  d) Reached Rome only
**Answer: b) Spread, as arts and science flourished** — Han, 206 BC–220 AD.
**4.** Logographic writing means —
a) Writing with wedges  b) Words built from pictures  c) Writing on clay  d) Alphabet letters
**Answer: b) Words built from pictures** — China's picture-built words, written on paper.

**5.** Chinese civilisation began in the — valley.
a) Yangtze  b) Yellow River (Hwang Ho)  c) Indus
**Answer: b) Yellow River (Hwang Ho)** — Third millennium BC.

**6.** The Shang dynasty produced —
a) paper and printing  b) bronze vessels, war chariots and a writing system  c) gunpowder
**Answer: b) bronze vessels, war chariots and a writing system** — The Shang triad.

**7.** Confucius lived —
a) 551–479 BC  b) 1809–1882 AD  c) 1312–1337 AD
**Answer: a) 551–479 BC** — The Zhou-age teacher.

**8.** Confucianism teaches —
a) a classless society  b) a well-ordered society of ranked duties  c) rule by age grades
**Answer: b) a well-ordered society of ranked duties** — Ranked duties, each in place.

**9.** The ancient Chinese also built —
a) the pyramids  b) the Great Wall  c) the Colosseum
**Answer: b) the Great Wall** — Across the northern hills.
