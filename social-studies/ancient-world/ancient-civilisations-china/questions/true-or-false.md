# Ancient civilisations — China — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Warring States period came between Zhou and Qin.
**Answer: True** — 476–221 BC, when no single house ruled.
**2.** China traded with Aksum.
**Answer: True** — With Constantinople, India and Aksum.
**3.** The Shang are remembered for coin money and iron tools.
**Answer: False** — Those are Zhou marks; Shang cast bronze vessels and used horse chariots and writing.
