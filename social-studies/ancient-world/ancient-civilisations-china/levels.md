# Ancient civilisations — China — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Recite the dynasty sequence — Shang, Zhou, the Warring States, Qin, Han — with each one's dates and marks.; Say what China traded, what it was famed for, and how its logographic writing works.. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
