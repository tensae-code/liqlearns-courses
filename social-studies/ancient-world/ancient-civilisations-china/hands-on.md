# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — China

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Dynasty line
Make five cards — Shang, Zhou, Warring States, Qin, Han. Shuffle them, then race a companion to lay them in order with dates.

## Picture-writing
Write a short message using only drawn pictures, no letters. Trade messages with a companion and read each other's — logographic writing, first-hand.

## Ranked duties chart (Grade 9)
Confucius taught a well-ordered society of ranked duties. Draw the chart his teaching implies — ruler, official, parent, child — with one duty written on each link, then one duty each owes upward. Compare with the Gadaa offices in the Ethiopia title.

## Grade 11 depth — China in depth — Mandate of Heaven and the written character

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
