# Video script — Ancient civilisations — China

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

China rose on two rivers.
Shang cast bronze. Zhou coined money.
States warred. Qin united the land.
Han made arts and science flourish.
Silk and porcelain travelled the world —
and China wrote in pictures.

---

Grade 9 story addition (Ethiopia Wave 4):

The Yellow River winds across the map. Bronze vessels gleam — Shang.

Scrolls unroll: Zhou classics. A teacher walks among students — Confucius, order through duty.

And in the north, a wall stitches the hills together, stone on stone.

[Grade 11 depth scene — China in depth — Mandate of Heaven and the written character. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Shang and Zhou China receive the Grade 11 depth: a kingship held by the Mandate of Heaven — rule kept while heaven approves, forfeited when it withdraws — and a writing born on oracle bones, questions to ancestors cracked in heat, growing into the characters China still writes. The names to hold: Mandate of Heaven, oracle bones.
