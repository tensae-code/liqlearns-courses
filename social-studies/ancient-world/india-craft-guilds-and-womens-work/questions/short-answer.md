# India — craft guilds and women's work — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Crafts persons; shrenis; rules for spinning and weaving.
**Answer: Potters fire mirror-black Northern Black Polished Ware, Varanasi and Madurai weave famous cloth, and craft and merchant shrenis train workers, buy raw material — and bank deposits whose interest endows monasteries; the Arthashastra's workshop rules show widows and retired temple women spinning for wages under a superintendent forbidden even to talk to them of anything but work (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
