# India — craft guilds and women's work

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Craft and merchant guilds — shrenis — trained workers, bought raw material and even banked deposits, while the Arthashastra's rules show women, widows among them, spinning for wages. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Crafts persons; shrenis; rules for spinning and weaving

Potters fire mirror-black Northern Black Polished Ware, Varanasi and Madurai weave famous cloth, and craft and merchant shrenis train workers, buy raw material — and bank deposits whose interest endows monasteries; the Arthashastra's workshop rules show widows and retired temple women spinning for wages under a superintendent forbidden even to talk to them of anything but work.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Crafts persons; shrenis; rules for spinning and weaving. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
