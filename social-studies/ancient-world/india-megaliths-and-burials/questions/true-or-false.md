# India — megaliths and burials — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From about 3000 years ago, big-stone burials spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir, the dead laid with Black and Red Ware pots, iron tools and horse gear — Brahmagiri's one grave with 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots betrays rich and poor, chiefs and followers.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Silent sentinels — the story of the megaliths

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about a closer look — Magadha.
**Answer: False** — it is about silent sentinels — the story of the megaliths

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A man is buried cross-legged in a huge four-legged clay jar in the courtyard of the settlement's largest, five-roomed house with its own granary — likely a chief.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in A special burial at Inamgaon; occupations at Inamgaon

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about a closer look — Vajji; Ajatasattu and the Vajjis.
**Answer: False** — it is about a special burial at Inamgaon; occupations at Inamgaon
