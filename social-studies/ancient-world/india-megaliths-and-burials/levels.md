This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Silent sentinels — the story of the megaliths
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — from about 3000 years ago, big-stone burials spread across the Deccan, the south, the north-east and Kashmir, the dead laid with Black and Red Ware pots, iron tools and horse gear — Brahmagiri's one grave with 33 gold beads beside others with a few pots betrays rich and poor, chiefs and followers.

## Sub lesson 2 — A special burial at Inamgaon; occupations at Inamgaon
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a man is buried cross-legged in a huge four-legged clay jar in the courtyard of the settlement's largest, five-roomed house with its own granary — likely a chief.
