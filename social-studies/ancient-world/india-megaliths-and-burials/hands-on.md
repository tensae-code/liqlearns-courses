# Hands-on — India — megaliths and burials

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Silent sentinels — the story of the megaliths, A special burial at Inamgaon; occupations at Inamgaon — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Silent sentinels — the story of the megaliths, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
