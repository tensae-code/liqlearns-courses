# Video script — Abbasid decline and the breakaway states

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Palestine's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

An empire stretched from India's edge to the Atlantic outgrows its caliphs' grip; palace luxury, army interference, sectarian revolts and separatist governors hollow it out from the second Abbasid era, provinces slip into nominal loyalty, and al-Andalus is the first region to break away.
