# China Mongol Rise And Yuan Founding — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Mongols rise and Yuan is founded.
**Answer: Temujin unites the steppe in 1206 as Genghis Khan, dies at Liupan Mountain leaving orders to borrow Song's road against Jin; Mongke falls at Hezhou, yet in 1234 Jin falls and a 40-year war with Southern Song begins (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 10: The Mongols rise and Yuan is founded, printed p. 47.**
