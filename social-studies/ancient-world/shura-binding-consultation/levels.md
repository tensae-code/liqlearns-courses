## Sub lesson 1 — Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — shura is set against mere consultation: advice a ruler may ignore is istishara, but shura binds; it stands only on its conditions — freedom, bindingness, solidarity, morality, consensus, persuasion, correctness and strength — and is staffed by qualified ahl al-hall wa-l-'aqd representing all society; the book argues binding shura simply is democracy, older than the name by fourteen centuries, and asks pupils to name the councils that embody shura and democracy in the Yemeni Republic and how Yemen can develop its experience of both
