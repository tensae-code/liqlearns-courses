# Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** shura is set against mere consultation: advice a ruler may ignore is istishara, but shura binds; it stands only on its conditions — freedom, bindingness, solidarity, morality, consensus, persuasion, correctness and strength — and is staffed by qualified ahl al-hall wa-l-'aqd representing all society; the book argues binding shura simply is democracy, older than the name by fourteen centuries, and asks pupils to name the councils that embody shura and democracy in the Yemeni Republic and how Yemen can develop its experience of both.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation
