# Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation.
**Answer: shura is set against mere consultation: advice a ruler may ignore is istishara, but shura binds; it stands only on its conditions — freedom, bindingness, solidarity, morality, consensus, persuasion, correctness and strength — and is staffed by qualified ahl al-hall wa-l-'aqd representing all society; the book argues binding shura simply is democracy, older than the name by fourteen centuries, and asks pupils to name the councils that embody shura and democracy in the Yemeni Republic and how Yemen can develop its experience of both (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation, printed p. 81.**
