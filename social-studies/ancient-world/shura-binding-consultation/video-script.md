# Video script — Shura in the Arab Islamic civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

shura is set against mere consultation: advice a ruler may ignore is istishara, but shura binds; it stands only on its conditions — freedom, bindingness, solidarity, morality, consensus, persuasion, correctness and strength — and is staffed by qualified ahl al-hall wa-l-'aqd representing all society; the book argues binding shura simply is democracy, older than the name by fourteen centuries, and asks pupils to name the councils that embody shura and democracy in the Yemeni Republic and how Yemen can develop its experience of both.
