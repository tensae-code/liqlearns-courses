# Iraqi scholars of the Islamic age — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Iraqi scholars: grammar, chemistry, history and light.
**Answer: Abu al-Aswad invents Arabic grammar in Basra; Jabir ibn Hayyan founds chemistry on experiment; the Mandaean al-Sabi writes on the astrolabe; al-Masudi travels and writes Meadows of Gold; Basra-born Ibn al-Haytham explains the eye and the rainbow; Shuhda of Baghdad teaches hadith to men and women (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Iraq's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *الاجتماعيات للصف الخامس الابتدائي (Social Studies for the Fifth Primary Grade)* (Iraq), Iraqi scholars: grammar, chemistry, history and light, printed p. 71.**
