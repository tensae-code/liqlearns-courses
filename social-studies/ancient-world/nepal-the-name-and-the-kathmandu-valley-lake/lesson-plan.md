# Lesson plan — Nepal — the name and the Kathmandu Valley lake

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Nepal — the name and the Kathmandu Valley lake
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Nepal, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** rival stories compete — Krishna or the sage Manjushree drains the valley lake, herdsman Nippa or sage Ne gives the name, Newars, Kirant "Nepar", Newari "middle country" and Tibetan "house of wool" — until unification in 1825 BS makes "Nepal" the whole country
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

4. **The Ancient History of Nepal (Grade 12)** — history is divided into ancient, medieval and modern; the land itself is explained first — thirteen crore years ago a sea (Tethys) covered it, the Himalaya rose, and the Kathmandu valley lay under a…
