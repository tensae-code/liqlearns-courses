# Video script — Nepal — the name and the Kathmandu Valley lake

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Nepal's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

rival stories compete — Krishna or the sage Manjushree drains the valley lake, herdsman Nippa or sage Ne gives the name, Newars, Kirant "Nepar", Newari "middle country" and Tibetan "house of wool" — until unification in 1825 BS makes "Nepal" the whole country

---

the guide scripts the lake story — in ancient times the Kathmandu Valley was a great lake, the monk Manjushree from China cut the gorge at Chobhar and drained it, settlement followed — then sets pupils to weigh the rival religious, historical and linguistic bases for the name "Nepal"

---

the guide sets pupils to weigh every basis for the name — the sage "Ne", Kiranti language, Tibetan, Newari and Lepcha languages, Sanskrit literature — and to hunt the naming basis of their own settlement

---

history is divided into ancient, medieval and modern; the land itself is explained first — thirteen crore years ago a sea (Tethys) covered it, the Himalaya rose, and the Kathmandu valley lay under a great lake whose waters drained away; then the name's rival proofs are weighed (geographical, mythological, historical — Nepal first written about 500–600 CE, the Tistung inscription of 569 BS the first use of the word) and the Gopal age begins
