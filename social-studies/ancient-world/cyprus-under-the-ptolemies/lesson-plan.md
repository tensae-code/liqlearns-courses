# Lesson plan — Cyprus under the Ptolemies

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Cyprus under the Ptolemies
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Cyprus, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Under the Ptolemies Cyprus lost its kings and became one province of an Egyptian empire — its copper, timber, wine and harbours feeding Ptolemaic power for nearly three centuries.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The end of the Cypriot kingdoms; administration — the koina (325–30 BC)** — Alexander's successors abolish Cyprus's kings, and Ptolemaic governors rule the island as one province of an Egyptian empire (The end of the Cypriot kingdoms; administration — the koina (325–30 BC))
2. **The importance of Cyprus for the Ptolemaic kingdom — the Kyrenia ship** — Cypriot copper, timber, wine and harbours feed Ptolemaic power, and a merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the trade story (The importance of Cyprus for the Ptolemaic kingdom — the Kyrenia ship)
3. **Daily life; art — theatre of the Hellenistic age** — Hellenistic Cypriots live in Greek-style cities, watch plays in stone theatres and fill houses with new fashions (Daily life; art — theatre of the Hellenistic age)
4. **The religion of the Cypriots in the Hellenistic age** — old Cypriot cults blend with Greek gods and ruler worship under Ptolemaic rule (The religion of the Cypriots in the Hellenistic age)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
