# Cyprus under the Ptolemies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Ptolemies, Cyprus was ruled as —
a) one province of an Egyptian empire  b) ten free kingdoms  c) a Roman colony
**Answer: a) one province of an Egyptian empire** — governors rule the island as one province of an Egyptian empire.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The merchant ship sunk off Kyrenia still tells the story of —
a) Cyprus's trade  b) a sea battle  c) Alexander's march
**Answer: a) Cyprus's trade** — the Kyrenia ship still tells the trade story.

**3. (Sub lesson 4)** Hellenistic religion in Cyprus blended —
a) old Cypriot cults with Greek gods and ruler worship  b) only Roman gods  c) no gods at all
**Answer: a) old Cypriot cults with Greek gods and ruler worship** — old Cypriot cults blend with Greek gods and ruler worship.
