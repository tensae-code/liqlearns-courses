# Cyprus under the Ptolemies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Ptolemies kept Cyprus's old kings on their thrones.
**Answer: False** — the kings were abolished and governors ruled.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Hellenistic Cypriots watched plays in stone theatres.
**Answer: True** — they watch plays in stone theatres.
