# Hands-on — Cucuteni Trypillia Civilisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Cucuteni Trypillia Civilisation — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unitatea I, Lecția 5: Urme ale omului preistoric în spațiul românesc. Civilizația Cucuteni-Tripolie, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
