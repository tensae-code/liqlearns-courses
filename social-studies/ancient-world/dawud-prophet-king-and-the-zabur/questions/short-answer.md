# Civilisation of the prophet Dawud — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Civilisation of the prophet Dawud.
**Answer: dawud unites the tribes under one rule, founds the kingdom and takes Jerusalem as its capital, receives the Zabur, and rules as prophet, king and judge — startled by two litigants over the wall (angels, with their parable of the ninety-nine ewes and the one), he judges too fast, knows it was a test and repents, while in the case of the sheep in the crop his young son Sulayman's ruling improves on his own; mountains and birds join his praise, and iron softens in his hands into coats of mail (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Civilisation of the prophet Dawud, printed p. 75.**
