# Civilisation of the prophet Dawud — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dawud unites the tribes under one rule, founds the kingdom and takes Jerusalem as its capital, receives the Zabur, and rules as prophet, king and judge — startled by two litigants over the wall (angels, with their parable of the ninety-nine ewes and the one), he judges too fast, knows it was a test and repents, while in the case of the sheep in the crop his young son Sulayman's ruling improves on his own; mountains and birds join his praise, and iron softens in his hands into coats of mail.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Civilisation of the prophet Dawud

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Civilisation of the prophet Dawud
