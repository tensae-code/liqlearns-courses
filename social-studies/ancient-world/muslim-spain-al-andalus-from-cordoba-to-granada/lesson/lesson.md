# Muslim Spain — al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A refugee Umayyad prince refounds his dynasty at Cordoba, the caliphate shatters into taifa kingdoms, and Granada survives alone as the last Muslim state in Spain. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
