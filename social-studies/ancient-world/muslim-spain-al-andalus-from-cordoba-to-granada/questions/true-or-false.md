# Muslim Spain — al-Andalus from Cordoba to Granada — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A refugee Umayyad prince refounds his dynasty at Cordoba, the caliphate shatters into taifa kingdoms, and Granada survives alone as the last Muslim state in Spain.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 5: al-Andalus

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G11 Medieval Arab World* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Chapter 5: al-Andalus

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In 1492 Granada surrenders, eight centuries of Muslim Spain end, and its people are expelled across the sea to North Africa.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 5: the fall of Granada and the expulsion of the Muslims from al-Andalus

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G11 Medieval Arab World* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
