# Video script — The Umayyad Caliphate — from Damascus to Cordoba

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

After Ali's martyrdom the Umayyads rule for about ninety-one years — conquests widen to the state's greatest reach, government and building flourish — until the causes of their fall gather.
