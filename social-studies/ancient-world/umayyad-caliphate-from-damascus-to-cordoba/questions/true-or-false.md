# The Umayyad Caliphate — from Damascus to Cordoba — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Ali's martyrdom a new age opens — the Umayyad state rules for about ninety-one years, the conquests widen, government and administration develop, and building and learning flourish — until the causes of its fall gather and the caliphate passes on.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Islamic State: the Umayyads

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Prim6 T1 "The Arab World — a Homeland that Gathers Us"* is about The Islamic conquests in the Umayyad age.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Islamic State: the Umayyads

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the Umayyads the paused movement of conquest rolls again to the state's widest reach.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Islamic conquests in the Umayyad age

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* is about The Islamic State: the Umayyads.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Islamic conquests in the Umayyad age
