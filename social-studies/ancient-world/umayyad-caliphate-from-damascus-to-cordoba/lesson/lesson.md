# The Umayyad Caliphate — from Damascus to Cordoba

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: After Ali's martyrdom the Umayyads rule for about ninety-one years — conquests widen to the state's greatest reach, government and building flourish — until the causes of their fall gather. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Islamic State: the Umayyads

After Ali's martyrdom a new age opens — the Umayyad state rules for about ninety-one years, the conquests widen, government and administration develop, and building and learning flourish — until the causes of its fall gather and the caliphate passes on.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Islamic conquests in the Umayyad age

Under the Umayyads the paused movement of conquest rolls again to the state's widest reach.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: The Islamic State: the Umayyads; The Islamic conquests in the Umayyad age. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
