# Hands-on — The lost Arabs — Ad, Thamud and Madain Salih

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The lost Arabs — Ad, Thamud and Madain Salih — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The perished Arabs: Ad, Thamud, Tasm and Jadis, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
