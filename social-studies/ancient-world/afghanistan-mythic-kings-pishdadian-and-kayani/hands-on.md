# Hands-on — Afghanistan's mythic kings — Pishdadians, Kayanis and Aspa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Afghanistan's mythic kings — Pishdadians, Kayanis and Aspa — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Lesson 9: The mythical history of Afghanistan (Pishdadians, Kayanis and Aspa), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
