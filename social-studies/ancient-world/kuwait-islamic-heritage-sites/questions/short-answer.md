# Islamic Kuwait — its sites and the guardians of its heritage — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What does Kuwait do about its Islamic-era sites?
**Answer: It founded the Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah to collect and guard Islamic heritage (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why guard old sites at all?
**Answer: Because they are the country's surviving Islamic heritage — its past in stone and objects.**
