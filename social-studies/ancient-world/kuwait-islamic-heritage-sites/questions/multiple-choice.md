# Islamic Kuwait — its sites and the guardians of its heritage — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah was founded to —
a) collect and guard Islamic heritage  b) build oil refineries  c) train pearl divers
**Answer: a) collect and guard Islamic heritage** — the state founds it to collect and guard Islamic heritage

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Islamic-era remains inside Kuwait are —
a) sites that survive to this day  b) all destroyed  c) only stories
**Answer: a) sites that survive to this day** — Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Caring for old remains is the work of —
a) the whole state, through its heritage house  b) no one  c) foreign museums only
**Answer: a) the whole state, through its heritage house** — the state answers by founding the Dar al-Athar
