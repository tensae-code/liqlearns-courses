# Islamic Kuwait — its sites and the guardians of its heritage — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait has no Islamic-era sites of its own.
**Answer: False** — Islamic-era sites survive inside Kuwait

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Dar al-Athar al-Islamiyyah guards Islamic heritage.
**Answer: True** — it was founded to collect and guard it
