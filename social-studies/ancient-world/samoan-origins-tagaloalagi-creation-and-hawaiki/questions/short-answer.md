# Sāmoan origins — Tagaloalagi, creation and Hawaiki — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Part 1, Unit 1, Theory Three.
**Answer: Sāmoan oral tradition answers the scientists from inside — the god Tagaloalagi created the Sāmoan people in Sāmoa itself and organised them into districts and villages, with genealogies placing him about 2600 BC; other Polynesian traditions name a homeland, Hawaiki, or descent from the gods (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *History, Year 12* (Samoa), Part 1, Unit 1, Theory Three, printed pp. 11–12.**
