# Amir Temur Builds His State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 26. 'We will revive Samarkand' and 27. Amir Temur — founder of the centralised state.
**Answer: Temur rebuilds ruined Samarkand into a world capital of domes and craftsmen and binds his conquests into one centralised state with laws and roads (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* (Uzbekistan), 26. 'We will revive Samarkand' and 27. Amir Temur — founder of the centralised state, printed pp. 84, 86.**
