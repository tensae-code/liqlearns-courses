# Amir Temur Builds His State — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Temur rebuilds ruined Samarkand into a world capital of domes and craftsmen and binds his conquests into one centralised state with laws and roads.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 26. 'We will revive Samarkand' and 27. Amir Temur — founder of the centralised state

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 26. 'We will revive Samarkand' and 27. Amir Temur — founder of the centralised state
