## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **2.1 The formation of Yervanduni Armenia and Achaemenid Persia** — after Van falls, Yervanduni kings rule a satrapy of Persia, Armenia joins the great revolt of 522–521 BCE recorded at Behistun, and Tigran Yervanduni hosts Xenophon's Greeks
2. **2.2 Yervanduni Armenia, Alexander's empire and the Seleucids** — Alexander's conquest reshuffles the East, and Yervanduni kings keep Armenia together between Macedonian successors
3. **2.3 The socio-economic and cultural life of Yervanduni Armenia** — trade routes cross the Highland, cities grow, and Greek and Iranian cultures meet in Armenian temples and coinage
