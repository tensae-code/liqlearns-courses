# Empire And Kingdoms In 11th Century Europe — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Ottonian emperors rebuild imperial power in Germany while the weak Capetians hold a small French royal domain and Norman adventurers carve kingdoms from England to southern Italy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ottonian emperors invest bishop-counts to hold Germany and Italy (the body text follows Otto I to Otto III's Roman dream), weak kings share Europe with new regna, and Normans take the South.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia
