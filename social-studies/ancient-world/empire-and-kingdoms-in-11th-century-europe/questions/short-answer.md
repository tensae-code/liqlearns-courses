# Empire And Kingdoms In 11th Century Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms.
**Answer: the Ottonian emperors rebuild imperial power in Germany while the weak Capetians hold a small French royal domain and Norman adventurers carve kingdoms from England to southern Italy (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 1, Cap. 2 §§1–2 The rebirth of the Empire in Germany; Capetian France and the Norman kingdoms, printed pp. 40, 44.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia.
**Answer: Ottonian emperors invest bishop-counts to hold Germany and Italy (the body text follows Otto I to Otto III's Roman dream), weak kings share Europe with new regna, and Normans take the South (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), Il quadro europeo e l'universalismo cattolico; L'Impero; I regni; I normanni in Italia, printed pp. 7, 13, 18, 21.**
