# Foundations of Islamic civilisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a civilisation is born at Medina on three foundations — the creed of Qur'an and Sunna, one Islamic order covering politics, economy and society, and the Arabic language, which carries the Qur'an to the world and absorbs Greek and Indian learning — built by a drive for knowledge, schools and mosques, translation and public libraries.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Foundations of Islamic civilisation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Foundations of Islamic civilisation

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing the door of ijtihad; the Arab umma, honoured to bear the message, whose Prophet is an Arab from the land God chose as Islam's cradle; and the Arabic language, perfected among the old Arab civilisations, renewed and unified by the Qur'an's descent and carried by Islam into every land it reached.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation
