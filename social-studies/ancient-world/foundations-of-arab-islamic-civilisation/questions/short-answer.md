# Foundations of Islamic civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Foundations of Islamic civilisation.
**Answer: a civilisation is born at Medina on three foundations — the creed of Qur'an and Sunna, one Islamic order covering politics, economy and society, and the Arabic language, which carries the Qur'an to the world and absorbs Greek and Indian learning — built by a drive for knowledge, schools and mosques, translation and public libraries (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الإسلامي للصف السادس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History of the Islamic Arabs for Grade 6, Basic Education)* (Yemen), Foundations of Islamic civilisation, printed p. 83.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation.
**Answer: three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing the door of ijtihad; the Arab umma, honoured to bear the message, whose Prophet is an Arab from the land God chose as Islam's cradle; and the Arabic language, perfected among the old Arab civilisations, renewed and unified by the Qur'an's descent and carried by Islam into every land it reached (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation, printed p. 8.**
