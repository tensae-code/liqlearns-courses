## Sub lesson 1 — Foundations of Islamic civilisation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. ## Sub lesson 2 — Foundations of the rise of the Arab Islamic civilisation
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing the door of ijtihad; the Arab umma, honoured to bear the message, whose Prophet is an Arab from the land God chose as Islam's cradle; and the Arabic language, perfected among the old Arab civilisations, renewed and unified by the Qur'an's descent and carried by Islam into every land it reached
