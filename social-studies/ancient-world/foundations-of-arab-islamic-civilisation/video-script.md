# Video script — Foundations of Islamic civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

a civilisation is born at Medina on three foundations — the creed of Qur'an and Sunna, one Islamic order covering politics, economy and society, and the Arabic language, which carries the Qur'an to the world and absorbs Greek and Indian learning — built by a drive for knowledge, schools and mosques, translation and public libraries.
three foundations carry the whole civilisation: Islam, the final heavenly religion whose Qur'an and Sunna organise every side of life without closing the door of ijtihad; the Arab umma, honoured to bear the message, whose Prophet is an Arab from the land God chose as Islam's cradle; and the Arabic language, perfected among the old Arab civilisations, renewed and unified by the Qur'an's descent and carried by Islam into every land it reached.
