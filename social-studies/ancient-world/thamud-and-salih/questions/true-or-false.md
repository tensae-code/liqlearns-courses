# The civilisation of Thamud — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Thamud in the Hijaz carve palaces and houses into the mountains and prosper, their prophet Salih brings a she-camel out of the rock as a sign, the leaders hamstring her anyway, and an earthquake and a mighty shout destroy the people while Salih and the believers survive.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The civilisation of Thamud

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The civilisation of Thamud

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Thamud rise in the Hijaz after 'Ad, carving palaces in the plains and safe houses inside the mountains at al-Hijr, whose ruins still stand, Salih — known among them for wisdom — is accused of sorcery and challenged to bring a sign, a she-camel comes out of the rock and shares their water day about, the leaders plot against Salih and hamstring her under Qudar bin Salif, and three days later earthquake and shout end Thamud while Salih is saved.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The civilisation of Thamud

**Answer: False** — it is about The civilisation of Thamud
