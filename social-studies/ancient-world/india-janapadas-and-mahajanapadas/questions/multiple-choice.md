# India — janapadas and mahajanapadas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Janapadas and mahajanapadas; Painted Grey Ware  b) Upanishads; the wise beggar; Panini  c) China's Great Wall
**Answer: a) Janapadas and mahajanapadas; Painted Grey Ware** — this part tells janapadas and mahajanapadas; Painted Grey Ware

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Rajas of peoples become rajas of lands where "the jana set its foot"  b) Upanishadic teachers hunt what survives death — atman, the soul, one with brahman, the universal soul  c) Poems composed around 2300 years ago are gathered in assemblies — sangams — of poets at Madurai, preserving the Tamil words for the village's people and becoming a historian's source in verse
**Answer: a)** — rajas of peoples become rajas of lands where "the jana set its foot"

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Armies, taxes and changes in agriculture  b) Jainism  c) Irrigation and who lived in the villages
**Answer: a) Armies, taxes and changes in agriculture** — this part tells armies, taxes and changes in agriculture

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Kings who build forts and keep salaried, year-round armies must tax — bhaga, a sixth of the crop, labour from craftsmen, animals from herders  b) Vardhamana Mahavira, 24th tirthankara, teaches ahimsa so strict that followers must not kill even insects — traders embrace it, farmers who must kill crop pests struggle  c) A Jataka tale follows a penniless young man who turns a dead rat into a coin, storm-fallen garden wood into fuel money, and 500 grass-cutters' bundles into a thousand coins from a horse dealer — fiction used carefully, with sculpture and travellers' tales, as evidence for city life
**Answer: a)** — kings who build forts and keep salaried, year-round armies must tax — bhaga, a sixth of the crop, labour from craftsmen, animals from herders
