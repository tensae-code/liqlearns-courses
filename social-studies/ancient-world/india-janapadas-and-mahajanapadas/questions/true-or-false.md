# India — janapadas and mahajanapadas — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rajas of peoples become rajas of lands where "the jana set its foot".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Janapadas and mahajanapadas; Painted Grey Ware

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about upanishads; the wise beggar; Panini.
**Answer: False** — it is about janapadas and mahajanapadas; Painted Grey Ware

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kings who build forts and keep salaried, year-round armies must tax — bhaga, a sixth of the crop, labour from craftsmen, animals from herders.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Armies, taxes and changes in agriculture

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about jainism.
**Answer: False** — it is about armies, taxes and changes in agriculture
