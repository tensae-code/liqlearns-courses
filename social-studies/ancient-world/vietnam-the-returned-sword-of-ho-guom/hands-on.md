# Hands-on — Vietnam The Returned Sword Of Ho Guom

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam The Returned Sword Of Ho Guom — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 12, the legend of Hồ Gươm (the Lake of the Returned Sword), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
