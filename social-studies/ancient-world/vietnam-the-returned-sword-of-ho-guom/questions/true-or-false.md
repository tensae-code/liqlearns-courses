# Vietnam The Returned Sword Of Ho Guom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after defeating the Ming, King Lê Thái Tổ is boating on the lake when the Golden Tortoise rises and asks for the magic sword back for the Dragon King.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 12, the legend of Hồ Gươm (the Lake of the Returned Sword)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 4 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 12, the legend of Hồ Gươm (the Lake of the Returned Sword)
