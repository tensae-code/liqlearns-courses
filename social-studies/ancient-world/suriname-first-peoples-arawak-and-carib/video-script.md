# Video script — Suriname — first peoples: Arawak and Carib

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here — Arawak and Carib tribes in river villages, in huts, fishing, hunting, roasting on barbecues, each with its own territory and language.

The first Indigenous people reach Suriname 7,000–10,000 years ago via the Sipaliwini savanna; Arawak ancestors come from the Amazon/Orinoco 3,000+ years ago, and around 1100 the Caribs reach the Guiana coast by sea.
