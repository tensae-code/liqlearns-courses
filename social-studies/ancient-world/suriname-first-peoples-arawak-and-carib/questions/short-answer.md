# Suriname — first peoples: Arawak and Carib — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: De eerste bewoners van ons land.
**Answer: 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here — Arawak and Carib tribes in river villages, in huts, fishing, hunting, roasting on barbecues, each with its own territory and language. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 6 Geschiedenis*, Hoofdstuk 1: De eerste bewoners van ons land, printed p. 7.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Eerste Inheemsen in ons land.
**Answer: The first Indigenous people reach Suriname 7,000–10,000 years ago via the Sipaliwini savanna; Arawak ancestors come from the Amazon/Orinoco 3,000+ years ago, and around 1100 the Caribs reach the Guiana coast by sea. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden*, Thema 1 Les 3: Eerste Inheemsen in ons land, printed p. 19.**
