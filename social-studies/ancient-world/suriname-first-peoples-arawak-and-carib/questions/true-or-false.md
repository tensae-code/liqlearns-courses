# Suriname — first peoples: Arawak and Carib — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here — Arawak and Carib tribes in river villages, in huts, fishing, hunting, roasting on barbecues, each with its own territory and language.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De eerste bewoners van ons land

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 6 Geschiedenis* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about De eerste bewoners van ons land

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The first Indigenous people reach Suriname 7,000–10,000 years ago via the Sipaliwini savanna; Arawak ancestors come from the Amazon/Orinoco 3,000+ years ago, and around 1100 the Caribs reach the Guiana coast by sea.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Eerste Inheemsen in ons land

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Eerste Inheemsen in ons land
