# Hands-on — Suriname — first peoples: Arawak and Carib

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Suriname — first peoples: Arawak and Carib — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, De eerste bewoners van ons land, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
