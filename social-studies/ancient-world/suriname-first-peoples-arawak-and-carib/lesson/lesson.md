# Suriname — first peoples: Arawak and Carib

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here — Arawak and Carib tribes in river villages, in huts, fishing, hunting, roasting on barbecues, each with its own territory and language As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — De eerste bewoners van ons land

500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here — Arawak and Carib tribes in river villages, in huts, fishing, hunting, roasting on barbecues, each with its own territory and language (De eerste bewoners van ons land)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Eerste Inheemsen in ons land

The first Indigenous people reach Suriname 7,000–10,000 years ago via the Sipaliwini savanna; Arawak ancestors come from the Amazon/Orinoco 3,000+ years ago, and around 1100 the Caribs reach the Guiana coast by sea (Eerste Inheemsen in ons land)

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: De eerste bewoners van ons land — 500 years ago only Indigenous peoples lived here — Arawak and Carib tribes in river villages, in huts, fishing, hunting, roasting on barbecues, each with its own territory and language; Eerste Inheemsen in ons land — The first Indigenous people reach Suriname 7,000–10,000 years ago via the Sipaliwini savanna; Arawak ancestors come from the Amazon/Orinoco 3,000+ years ago, and around 1100 the Caribs reach the Guiana coast by sea. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
