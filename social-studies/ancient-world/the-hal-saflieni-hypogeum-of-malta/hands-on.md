# Hands-on — The Hal Saflieni Hypogeum Of Malta

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Hal Saflieni Hypogeum Of Malta — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Chapter 5.7: The rescue of the underground city of Saflieni (Ħal Saflieni), Malta, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
