# Avignon The Great Schism And The Crisis Of Universal Powers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1–3 The crisis of the Empire; The Papacy of Avignon and the Great Schism; New spiritual stirrings and conciliarism.
