# Avignon The Great Schism And The Crisis Of Universal Powers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Boniface VIII is seized at Anagni, the popes move to Avignon for seventy years, then two and three rival popes split Christendom until councils claim authority over popes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1–3 The crisis of the Empire; The Papacy of Avignon and the Great Schism; New spiritual stirrings and conciliarism

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1–3 The crisis of the Empire; The Papacy of Avignon and the Great Schism; New spiritual stirrings and conciliarism
