# The Nabataean and Palmyrene civilisations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** two Arab caravan kingdoms grow rich as middlemen of the desert — the Nabataeans carve Petra out of living rock, run their monarchy with the people's shura, mint coins and work copper and iron until Rome diverts the trade to the Red Sea and seizes them in 106 CE; then Palmyra, neutral between Persia and Rome, takes Petra's place, its merchants speaking Aramaic and Greek and trading to Gaul and the China Sea, until Queen Zenobia's ambition to build an Arab empire brings Aurelian down on the city — she is captured and Palmyra falls in 274 CE.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Nabataean and Palmyrene civilisations

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم القديم — للصف الأول الثانوي (History: Civilisations of the Ancient World, for the First Secondary Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Nabataean and Palmyrene civilisations
