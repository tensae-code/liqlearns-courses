## Sub lesson 1 — The Nabataean and Palmyrene civilisations
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — two Arab caravan kingdoms grow rich as middlemen of the desert — the Nabataeans carve Petra out of living rock, run their monarchy with the people's shura, mint coins and work copper and iron until Rome diverts the trade to the Red Sea and seizes them in 106 CE; then Palmyra, neutral between Persia and Rome, takes Petra's place, its merchants speaking Aramaic and Greek and trading to Gaul and the China Sea, until Queen Zenobia's ambition to build an Arab empire brings Aurelian down on the city — she is captured and Palmyra falls in 274 CE
