# Myanmar prehistory — rock, bronze and metal ages

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Myanmar's long story as the guide tells it in two arcs: Myanmar's own past first, then the world. This lesson is one chapter of that story, as Myanmar's own Grade 6 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stone first — the Rock Age in Myanmar

Myanmar's first people knap stone: they chip rock into tools, and stone is their power over nature — cutting, scraping and hunting with what the land itself gives them.

In your mind's eye: the key scene of this lesson as the book teaches it. Name the people and the place in the picture and say what happened there.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bronze, then iron — the Metal Ages

Then bronze is cast, then iron is worked: each new metal buys people new power over nature, while the guide stresses how they lived in harmony with their environment as their technology gradually improved.
