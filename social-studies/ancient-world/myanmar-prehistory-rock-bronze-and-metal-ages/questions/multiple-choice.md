# Myanmar prehistory — rock, bronze and metal ages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Myanmar's prehistory begins with people working — —
a) Stone  b) Bronze only  c) Iron only
**Answer: a) Stone** — Stone — they knap stone into tools in the Rock Age.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Rock Age, stone tools gave people — —
a) Power over nature  b) Cities and walls  c) Writing and laws
**Answer: a) Power over nature** — Power over nature — cutting, scraping and hunting with stone.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After the Rock Age came — —
a) The Bronze Age, then the Metal (iron) Age  b) The age of empires first  c) The age of ships first
**Answer: a) The Bronze Age, then the Metal (iron) Age** — The Bronze Age, then the Metal (iron) Age — technology gradually improves.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** As Myanmar's tools improved, the guide says people lived — —
a) In harmony with their environment  b) Against nature in every way  c) Only inside cities
**Answer: a) In harmony with their environment** — In harmony with their environment.
