# Myanmar prehistory — rock, bronze and metal ages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Myanmar's first people do with stone, and what did it give them?
**Answer: They knapped (chipped) stone into tools, giving them new power over nature — cutting, scraping and hunting (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Put the three ages in order and say what changed at each step.
**Answer: Rock Age (stone tools), then Bronze Age (cast bronze), then Metal Age (worked iron); each step gradually improved technology and people's power over nature (any close wording).**
