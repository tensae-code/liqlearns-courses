# Myanmar prehistory — rock, bronze and metal ages — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Myanmar's first people began with iron tools.
**Answer: False — they began by knapping stone in the Rock Age; bronze and iron came later.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Each new metal in Myanmar's prehistory bought people new power over nature.
**Answer: True — bronze, then iron, gradually improved their technology.**
