## Sub lesson 1 — Stone first — the Rock Age in Myanmar
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Myanmar's first people knap stone: they chip rock into tools, and stone is their power over nature — cutting, scraping and hunting with what the land itself gives them.
