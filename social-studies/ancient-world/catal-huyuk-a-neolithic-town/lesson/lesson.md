# Çatal Höyük — a Neolithic town

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Çatal Höyük was a Neolithic farming town in Turkey already plugged into long-distance exchange — flint from Syria, cowries from the Red Sea and shells from the Mediterranean arrived on pack animals' backs. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
