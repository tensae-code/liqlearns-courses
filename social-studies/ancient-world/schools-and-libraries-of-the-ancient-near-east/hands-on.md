# Hands-on — Schools and libraries of the ancient Near East

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Schools and libraries of the ancient Near East — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Schools and libraries of the ancient Near East, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
