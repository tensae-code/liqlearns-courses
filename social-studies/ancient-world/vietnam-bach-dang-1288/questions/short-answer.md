**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 10, story of the Bạch Đằng river battle, 1288.
**Answer: Trần Quốc Tuấn hides a stake field at the river mouth, feigns retreat at high tide, and the Yuan fleet impales itself as the tide falls; commander Ô Mã Nhi is captured alive (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
