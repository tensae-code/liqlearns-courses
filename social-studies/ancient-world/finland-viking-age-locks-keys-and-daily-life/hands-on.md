# Hands-on — Finland — Viking Age locks, keys and daily life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Finland — Viking Age locks, keys and daily life — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Esine 4: Rauta-avain (iron key, Viking Age c. 800–1050), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
