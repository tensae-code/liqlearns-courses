## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Esine 4: Rauta-avain (iron key, Viking Age c. 800–1050)** — for the first time valuables are kept behind locks, and an iron key is needed to open them
2. **Esine 6: Lukkoja ja avaimia (Iron Age / Viking Age locks and a padlock from Hämeenlinna, c. 800–1050)** — padlocks with loops for chains guard Viking Age stores, security becoming a thing you carry
3. **Esine 5: Korvalusikka (ear spoon, Viking Age c. 800–1050)** — a 59 mm, 7-gram spoon for cleaning ears shows Iron Age daily hygiene, its wax later prized in folk medicine
