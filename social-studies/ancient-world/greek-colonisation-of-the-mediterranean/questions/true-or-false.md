# Greek Colonisation Of The Mediterranean — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** weeping settlers take the sacred fire from home and found the rich cities of Magna Graecia, which soon mint their own silver coins.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 5 New colonies of the Greeks
