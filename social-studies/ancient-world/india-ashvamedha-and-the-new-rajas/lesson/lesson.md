# India — the ashvamedha and the new rajas

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A raja became a great king through the ashvamedha: his sacrificial horse wandered a year under guard, and every king who let it pass admitted his power. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — How some men became rulers

A raja lets a sacrificial horse wander a year under guard; kings who stop it must fight, kings who let it pass admit his power and come bearing gifts to the ashvamedha, where he sits enthroned while his charioteer sings his glory.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
