# Hands-on — India — the ashvamedha and the new rajas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

List the parts of this story in order — How some men became rulers — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, How some men became rulers, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
