# Video script — India — the ashvamedha and the new rajas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A raja lets a sacrificial horse wander a year under guard; kings who stop it must fight, kings who let it pass admit his power and come bearing gifts to the ashvamedha, where he sits enthroned while his charioteer sings his glory.
