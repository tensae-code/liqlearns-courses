# India — the ashvamedha and the new rajas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) How some men became rulers  b) The story of the Buddha  c) Ashoka, a unique ruler; Ashoka's war in Kalinga

a) A raja lets a sacrificial horse wander a year under guard  b) Siddhartha Gautama, a kshatriya of the small Sakya gana, leaves home comfort, debates other thinkers for years, then meditates under a peepal tree at Bodh Gaya until enlightenment makes him the Buddha  c) Somewhat before Ashoka, China's emperors raise a 6,400 km stone-and-brick wall against northern pastoral peoples and keep adding to it for 2,000 years — a wall against neighbours, set against Ashoka's messengers to them
