# India — the ashvamedha and the new rajas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: How some men became rulers.
**Answer: A raja lets a sacrificial horse wander a year under guard; kings who stop it must fight, kings who let it pass admit his power and come bearing gifts to the ashvamedha, where he sits enthroned while his charioteer sings his glory (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
