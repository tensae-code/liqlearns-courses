# The Children of Israel in the Sinai Peninsula — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** saved through the sea, Israel at once asks Musa for a visible god, demands to see God and is struck down and revived, worships al-Samiri's lowing calf while Musa is on the mountain, argues over the cow they are ordered to slaughter until the murdered man, struck with a piece of it, sits up and names his killer — then refuses to enter the holy land, "go, you and your Lord, and fight", and is sentenced to forty years of wandering in Sinai, fed on manna and quails and water from the rock, until Harun and Musa themselves die and a new generation under Yusha bin Nun goes in.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Children of Israel in the Sinai Peninsula

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Children of Israel in the Sinai Peninsula
