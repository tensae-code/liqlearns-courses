# Vietnam Later Le And Le Thanh Tong — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lê Lợi founds the dynasty in 1428 at Đông Kinh; under Lê Thánh Tông (1460–1497) come reforms, the Hồng Đức map, the national criminal code, regular examinations and stelae at the Văn Miếu — and a warning that not one inch of the founder's land may be given away.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 12, the Later Lê dynasty and King Lê Thánh Tông

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 12, the Later Lê dynasty and King Lê Thánh Tông

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the maths prodigy weighs a Ming envoy's elephant by marking a boat's waterline and swapping the elephant for stones, then writes the Đại thành toán pháp, the age's great maths book.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 12, story of Trạng Lường Lương Thế Vinh
