**Answer: as empires falter, strongmen seize rock fortresses — Dobromir Hrs at Prosek above the Vardar, who ambushes Emperor Alexios III's army by night and marries his way to peace; Strez, who holds Prosek after him and is murdered in his tent in 1214 (folk said Saint Sava's prayers killed him); and Alexiy Slav at Melnik, who survives twenty years by marrying into the Latin emperor's family, then switching to Epirus (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
