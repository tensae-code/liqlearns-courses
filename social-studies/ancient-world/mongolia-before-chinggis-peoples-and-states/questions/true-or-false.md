# Mongolia before Chinggis — peoples and states — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mongol-speaking tribes drift west and settle the Onon, Kherlen and Tuul river valleys, the homeland from which a nation will be made.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2.1 The Mongols' migration and settlement
