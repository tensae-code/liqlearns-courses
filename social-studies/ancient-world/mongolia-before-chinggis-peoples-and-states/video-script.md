Mongol-speaking tribes drift west and settle the Onon, Kherlen and Tuul river valleys, the homeland from which a nation will be made. the Khamag Mongol confederation and its khans hold the eastern steppe, fighting and trading with the Khitan Liao and Jin empires. Chinggis's own Borjigin line descends through the Darligin and Nirugun clans; their feuds and marriages set the board his unification will overturn. Khamag Mongol khans, Tatar, Kereit, Naiman and Merkit rivals — a crowded steppe of feuding aimags awaits a unifier.
