# Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Yaroslav beats his brothers and the Pechenegs, raises St Sophia in Kyiv, marries his children into Europe's royal houses — and gives Rus its first written law code, the Ruska Pravda, replacing blood vengeance with fines. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Yaroslav secures Rus

Yaroslav beats his brothers and the Pechenegs. He raises St Sophia in Kyiv and marries his children into Europe's royal houses — Kyiv stands at Europe's top table.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Ruska Pravda

Yaroslav gives Rus its first written law code, the Ruska Pravda. It replaces blood vengeance with fines — a killing is answered with money, not with another killing.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
