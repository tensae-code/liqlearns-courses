# Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yaroslav raised in Kyiv —
a) St Sophia  b) The Desiatynna church only  c) A fortress on the rapids
**Answer: a) St Sophia**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Yaroslav married his children into —
a) Europe's royal houses  b) The Pecheneg camps  c) The Byzantine army only
**Answer: a) Europe's royal houses**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Ruska Pravda is —
a) Rus's first written law code  b) A chronicle of Yaroslav's wars  c) A church service book
**Answer: a) Rus's first written law code**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Ruska Pravda replaced blood vengeance with —
a) Fines  b) Exile  c) Duels
**Answer: a) Fines**
