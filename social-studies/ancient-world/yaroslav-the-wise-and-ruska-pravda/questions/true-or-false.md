# Yaroslav the Wise and the Ruska Pravda — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Ruska Pravda, a killing was answered with another killing.
**Answer: False — The code replaces blood vengeance with fines.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Yaroslav defeated the Pechenegs.
**Answer: True — The book tells that Yaroslav beats his brothers and the Pechenegs.**
