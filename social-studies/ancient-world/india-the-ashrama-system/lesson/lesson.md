## Sub lesson 1 — The system of ashramas

Brahmins answer the new religions with a life-map in four stages — student, householder, forest meditator, renouncer — open in full only to men of the three upper varnas, while women follow their husbands' stages.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
