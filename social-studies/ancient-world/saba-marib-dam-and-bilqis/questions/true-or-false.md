# The state of Saba — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Saba, first at Sirwah then at Marib, unifies Yemen under Karib'il Watar, builds the Marib Dam and trades between north and south Arabia, and its queen Bilqis meets Solomon and, in the book's telling, brings the Yemenis into his faith.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The state of Saba

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The state of Saba

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Saba, the longest and oldest of Yemen's kingdoms, rules first from Sirwah then Marib, reaches the al-Ghala oasis in the north of the Hijaz, and its most famous hour is Queen Bilqis's dealings with Solomon, which the Qur'an itself records.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Saba)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Saba)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Bilqis, a king's daughter ruling Saba at its height — great army, rich farms, the incense trade — receives Sulayman's letter, consults her chiefs who boast of strength yet leave the decision to her, tests him with a treasure-gift which he firmly refuses, then travels herself; her throne is brought to Jerusalem faster than an eye blinks and slightly altered to test her, the glass floor of the palace she mistakes for water, and she declares her Islam with Sulayman and goes home to rule Yemen by it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Queen Bilqis: a civilisation and faith

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Queen Bilqis: a civilisation and faith
