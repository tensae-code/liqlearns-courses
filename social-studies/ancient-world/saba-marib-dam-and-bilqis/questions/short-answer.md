# The state of Saba — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The state of Saba.
**Answer: saba, first at Sirwah then at Marib, unifies Yemen under Karib'il Watar, builds the Marib Dam and trades between north and south Arabia, and its queen Bilqis meets Solomon and, in the book's telling, brings the Yemenis into his faith (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* (Yemen), The state of Saba, printed p. 33.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Saba).
**Answer: saba, the longest and oldest of Yemen's kingdoms, rules first from Sirwah then Marib, reaches the al-Ghala oasis in the north of the Hijaz, and its most famous hour is Queen Bilqis's dealings with Solomon, which the Qur'an itself records (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Saba), printed p. 63.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Queen Bilqis: a civilisation and faith.
**Answer: bilqis, a king's daughter ruling Saba at its height — great army, rich farms, the incense trade — receives Sulayman's letter, consults her chiefs who boast of strength yet leave the decision to her, tests him with a treasure-gift which he firmly refuses, then travels herself; her throne is brought to Jerusalem faster than an eye blinks and slightly altered to test her, the glass floor of the palace she mistakes for water, and she declares her Islam with Sulayman and goes home to rule Yemen by it (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Queen Bilqis: a civilisation and faith, printed p. 85.**
