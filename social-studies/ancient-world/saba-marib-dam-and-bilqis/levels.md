# The state of Saba — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The state of Saba
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — saba, first at Sirwah then at Marib, unifies Yemen under Karib'il Watar, builds the Marib Dam and trades between north and south Arabia, and its queen Bilqis meets Solomon and, in the book's telling, brings the Yemenis into his faith

## Sub lesson 2 — States of the ancient Yemeni civilisations (Saba)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — saba, the longest and oldest of Yemen's kingdoms, rules first from Sirwah then Marib, reaches the al-Ghala oasis in the north of the Hijaz, and its most famous hour is Queen Bilqis's dealings with Solomon, which the Qur'an itself records

## Sub lesson 3 — Queen Bilqis: a civilisation and faith
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — bilqis, a king's daughter ruling Saba at its height — great army, rich farms, the incense trade — receives Sulayman's letter, consults her chiefs who boast of strength yet leave the decision to her, tests him with a treasure-gift which he firmly refuses, then travels herself; her throne is brought to Jerusalem faster than an eye blinks and slightly altered to test her, the glass floor of the palace she mistakes for water, and she declares her Islam with Sulayman and goes home to rule Yemen by it
