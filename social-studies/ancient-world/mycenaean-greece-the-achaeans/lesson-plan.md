# Lesson plan — Mycenaean Greece The Achaeans

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Mycenaean Greece The Achaeans
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Achaeans settle Greece and build warrior kingdoms; their king at Mycenae leads the very expedition to Troy the myths described
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 10, ch. 1 Achaeans, the first Greeks** — the Achaeans settle Greece and build warrior kingdoms; their king at Mycenae leads the very expedition to Troy the myths described
2. **Unit 10, ch. 2–3 Trade with Cyprus and the acropolis of Mycenae** — Mycenaean merchants sail to Cyprus for its copper, while at home the citadel of Mycenae crowns its hill with palace and massive walls
3. **Unit 10, ch. 4 The daily life of the Mycenaeans** — Mycenaean ships trade across the Mediterranean while warriors, farmers and craftsmen live under the palace's scribes
4. **Unit 10, ch. 5 The religion and writing of the Mycenaeans** — Mycenaeans worship the Great Goddess and the later Olympians, write Greek in Linear B, and bury kings in tholos tombs with gold masks like "Agamemnon's"
5. **Unit 10, ch. 6 The art of the Mycenaeans** — taught by Minoan art, Mycenaean craftsmen work gold, crystal and ivory into vases and weapons for their warrior kings
6. **Unit 10, ch. 1 Achaeans exercises** — exercises open the Mycenaean unit with the arrival of the first Greeks
7. **Unit 10, ch. 3 Acropolis of Mycenae exercises** — pupils read the citadel's walls and gate as a fortress built for war
8. **Unit 10, ch. 4 Daily life exercises** — a trade table of what Mycenaeans bought and sold maps their sea business
9. **Unit 10, ch. 5 Religion and writing exercises** — pupils imagine a Mycenaean explaining Linear B — writing as the palace's memory
10. **Unit 10, ch. 6 Art of the Mycenaeans exercises** — listing objects by material shows the range of Mycenaean craft in gold, bronze and crystal
11. **Ch. B5 The Mycenaean world** — warrior kings in citadels from Mycenae to Pylos trade and raid across the Mediterranean and keep palace accounts in Linear B
12. **Ch. B6 Mycenaean religion and art** — gold masks, tholos tombs and frescoed palaces bury and celebrate the Mycenaean kings
13. **The Mycenaean civilisation** — warrior kings in citadels at Mycenae and Pylos keep palace records in Linear B, then the palaces burn and writing is lost
