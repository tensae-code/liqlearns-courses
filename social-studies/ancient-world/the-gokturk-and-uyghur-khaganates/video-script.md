# Video script — The Göktürk and Uyghur khaganates

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The steppe clans became states: first the Göktürk khaganate, then the Uyghur khaganate. Turkey's Grade 9 book puts one idea at their centre — kut, power granted by Gök Tanrı, Heaven itself.

You can hear it in Mete Han's letter to the Chinese emperor, speaking as a ruler Heaven raised. You can read it on the Orhun stones, where Bilge Kağan warns his people in the Turks' own alphabet.

An empire that writes its own story in stone: that is the Göktürk gift to history.
