# The Göktürk and Uyghur khaganates — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Orhun inscriptions are written in the Turks' own alphabet.
**Answer: True** — their own story in their own letters.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kut means power seized by the strongest clan, with no part for Heaven.
**Answer: False** — kut is power granted by Gök Tanrı — and can be withdrawn.
