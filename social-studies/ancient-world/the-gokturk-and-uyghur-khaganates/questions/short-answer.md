# The Göktürk and Uyghur khaganates — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is kut, and who grants it?
**Answer: The ruler's sacred power or fortune, granted by Gök Tanrı (Heaven) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the book's two proofs of the khaganates' idea of rule.
**Answer: Mete Han's letter to the Chinese emperor, and Bilge Kağan's words on the Orhun stones (any close wording).**
