# The Göktürk and Uyghur khaganates — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two khaganates, in order, are —
a) Uyghur, then Göktürk  b) Göktürk, then Uyghur  c) Hun, then Roman
**Answer: b) Göktürk, then Uyghur** — from clans to state, in the book's order.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kut is —
a) A tax on herds  b) A kind of felt tent  c) The ruler's power granted by Gök Tanrı (Heaven)
**Answer: c) The ruler's power granted by Gök Tanrı (Heaven)** — the sacred fortune of rule.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Bilge Kağan's words survive on —
a) The Orhun stones  b) Papyrus scrolls  c) Clay tablets
**Answer: a) The Orhun stones** — carved in the Turks' own alphabet.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Mete Han's letter was written to —
a) A feudal lord  b) The Chinese emperor  c) The caliph of Baghdad
**Answer: b) The Chinese emperor** — the ruler-to-ruler proof the book uses.
