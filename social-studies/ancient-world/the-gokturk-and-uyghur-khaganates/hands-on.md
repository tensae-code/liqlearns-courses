# Hands-on — The Göktürk and Uyghur khaganates

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Your standing stone

Draw a tall stone and carve (in letters you can read) the three lines you would want your people to remember in a thousand years. Under it, write why stone outlasts a speech.

## Kut, in a diagram

Draw Heaven (Gök Tanrı) above, the khagan below, and the people below him, with arrows for kut flowing down and loyalty flowing up. Label what happens when kut is withdrawn.

## A letter between rulers

Write Mete Han's letter in your own words — five lines, proud and polite — answering a Chinese emperor who treats him as a small chief. Then write the emperor's one-line reply.
