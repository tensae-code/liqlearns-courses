# The Etruscans — Rome's teachers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The Etruscans bury their dead in painted tomb-houses full of feasting and dancing.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Before Rome rules Italy, the Etruscans do — a people who bury their dead in painted tomb-houses full of feasting and dancing, and teach the young Rome its arches, its gods' temples and its portrait faces.
