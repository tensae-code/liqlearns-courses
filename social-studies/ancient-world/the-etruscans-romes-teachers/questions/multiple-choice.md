# The Etruscans — Rome's teachers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Painted tomb-houses  b) Before Rome, the Etruscans  c) Rome's teachers
**Answer: b) Before Rome, the Etruscans** — Before Rome rules Italy, the Etruscans do.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Painted tomb-houses  b) Before Rome, the Etruscans  c) Rome's teachers
**Answer: a) Painted tomb-houses** — The Etruscans bury their dead in painted tomb-houses full of feasting and dancing.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Before Rome, the Etruscans  b) Painted tomb-houses  c) Rome's teachers
**Answer: c) Rome's teachers** — The Etruscans teach the young Rome its arches, its gods' temples and its portrait faces.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Before Rome rules Italy, the Etruscans do — a people who bury their dead in painted tomb-houses full of feasting and dancing, and teach the young Rome its arches, its gods' temples and its portrait faces  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
