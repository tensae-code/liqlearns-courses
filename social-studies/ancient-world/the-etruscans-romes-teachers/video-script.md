# Video script — The Etruscans — Rome's teachers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Before Rome rules Italy, the Etruscans do.

The Etruscans bury their dead in painted tomb-houses full of feasting and dancing.

The Etruscans teach the young Rome its arches, its gods' temples and its portrait faces.

That is the story: before Rome rules Italy, the Etruscans do — a people who bury their dead in painted tomb-houses full of feasting and dancing, and teach the young Rome its arches, its gods' temples and its portrait faces.
