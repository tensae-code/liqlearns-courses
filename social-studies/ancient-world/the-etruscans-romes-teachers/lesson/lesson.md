# The Etruscans — Rome's teachers

## Do it first

Before you read on, picture the scene this lesson is about, from its title alone: The Etruscans — Rome's teachers. What do you expect to happen, and what do you expect to change? Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Before Rome, the Etruscans

Before Rome rules Italy, the Etruscans do.

This is a real turn in the story, not a detail at its edge: hold it by its place in the order — what came before it, and what it makes possible next.

## Sub lesson 2 — Painted tomb-houses

The Etruscans bury their dead in painted tomb-houses full of feasting and dancing.

This is a real turn in the story, not a detail at its edge: hold it by its place in the order — what came before it, and what it makes possible next.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rome's teachers

The Etruscans teach the young Rome its arches, its gods' temples and its portrait faces.

This is a real turn in the story, not a detail at its edge: hold it by its place in the order — what came before it, and what it makes possible next.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as three beats: Before Rome rules Italy, the Etruscans do. Then: The Etruscans bury their dead in painted tomb-houses full of feasting and dancing. And finally: The Etruscans teach the young Rome its arches, its gods' temples and its portrait faces. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
