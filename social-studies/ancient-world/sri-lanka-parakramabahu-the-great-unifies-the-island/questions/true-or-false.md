# Sri Lanka — Parakramabahu the Great unifies the island — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A prince fostered at his uncle's court takes Dakkinadesa, beats Gajabahu II, survives his mother Sugala's revolt, and in 1153 rules one island.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Parakramabahu I's rise and unification

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G7 History Tamil 2011* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Parakramabahu I's rise and unification

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The great unifier rebuilds a war-torn kingdom, Nissanka Malla boasts in stone, and the line weakens toward invasion.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Parakramabahu I and his successors

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G12-13 History of Sri Lanka Part II Tamil 2014* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Parakramabahu I and his successors
