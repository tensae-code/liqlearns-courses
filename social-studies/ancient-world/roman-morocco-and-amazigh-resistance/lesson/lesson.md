# Roman Morocco and Amazigh resistance

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Morocco's long story as one line: first Moroccans, Amazigh, then dynasty after dynasty. This lesson is one chapter of that line, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Roman occupation — the map and the exploitation

The guide maps the Roman occupation of Morocco and shows how Rome exploited the land — taking the coast and the grain.

In your mind's eye: the key scene of this lesson as the book teaches it. Name the people and the place in the picture and say what happened there.

## Sub lesson 2 — Aedemon's revolt — the Amazigh answer

The Amazigh answer is taught through a model case: Aedemon, an Amazigh freedman, raises the mountains against the empire in revolt.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of this lesson in your own words, keeping its story beat: Rome takes the coast and the grain, and an Amazigh freedman named Aedemon raises the mountains against the empire. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
