# Video script — Roman Morocco and Amazigh resistance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Morocco's story as its Grade 5 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The guide maps the Roman occupation of Morocco and shows how Rome exploited the land — taking the coast and the grain.

The Amazigh answer is taught through a model case: Aedemon, an Amazigh freedman, raises the mountains against the empire in revolt.
