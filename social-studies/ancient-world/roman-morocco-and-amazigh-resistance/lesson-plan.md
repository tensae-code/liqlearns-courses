# Lesson plan — Roman Morocco and Amazigh resistance

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Roman Morocco and Amazigh resistance
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Morocco, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** History Lesson 6: Ancient Morocco — the Roman occupation and Amazigh resistance — Rome takes the coast and the grain, and an Amazigh freedman named Aedemon raises the mountains against the empire.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Morocco's Grade 5 national story, as the Al-Moufid teacher guide teaches it. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Roman occupation — the map and the exploitation** — The guide maps the Roman occupation of Morocco and shows how Rome exploited the land — taking the coast and the grain.
2. **Aedemon's revolt — the Amazigh answer** — The Amazigh answer is taught through a model case: Aedemon, an Amazigh freedman, raises the mountains against the empire in revolt.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
