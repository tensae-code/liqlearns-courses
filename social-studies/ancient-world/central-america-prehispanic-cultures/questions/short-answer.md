**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 22: los pueblos y la leyenda de Sac-Nicté.
**Answer: from the 5th century BC maize-farming peoples fill the isthmus — Quiché, Cakchiquel, Pipil, Chorotega, Matagalpa, Ulwa and Macro-Chibcha groups sharing cacao money and communal land; the Sac-Nicté legend tells how Canek of Chichén Itzá steals the promised princess and his Itzaes abandon their city to the pursuers (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
