# Vardan Mamikonian And The Battle Of Avarayr — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 5.1 The struggle for the homeland's independence.
**Answer: Persia's king Hazkert II tries to force Zoroastrianism on Christian Armenia, and Vardan Mamikonian's army falls at Avarayr on 26 May 451 defending the faith — told through Ghazar Parpetsi (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* (Armenia), 5.1 The struggle for the homeland's independence, printed p. 129.**
