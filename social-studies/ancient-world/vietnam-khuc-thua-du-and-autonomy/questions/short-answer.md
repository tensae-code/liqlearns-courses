# Vietnam Khuc Thua Du And Autonomy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 18, the Khúc family builds autonomy and Dương Đình Nghệ defends it.
**Answer: as the Tang fade, Khúc Thừa Dụ takes Đại La in 905; his son Khúc Hạo reforms taxes, registers and corvée under the motto of a lenient, simple government; in 931 Dương Đình Nghệ drives out the Southern Han and keeps the autonomy (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 18, the Khúc family builds autonomy and Dương Đình Nghệ defends it, printed p. 81.**
