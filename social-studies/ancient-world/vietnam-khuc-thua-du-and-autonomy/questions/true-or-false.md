# Vietnam Khuc Thua Du And Autonomy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** as the Tang fade, Khúc Thừa Dụ takes Đại La in 905; his son Khúc Hạo reforms taxes, registers and corvée under the motto of a lenient, simple government; in 931 Dương Đình Nghệ drives out the Southern Han and keeps the autonomy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 18, the Khúc family builds autonomy and Dương Đình Nghệ defends it

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 18, the Khúc family builds autonomy and Dương Đình Nghệ defends it
