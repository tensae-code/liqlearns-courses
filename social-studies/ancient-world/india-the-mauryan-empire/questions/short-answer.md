# India — the Mauryan empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: A very big kingdom = an empire.
**Answer: Chandragupta Maurya founds the empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by the wise Chanakya whose ideas fill the Arthashastra; the Maurya dynasty runs Chandragupta, Bindusara, Ashoka — and an empire, unlike a kingdom, needs bigger armies and more tax-collecting officials (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.7 Ashoka, the Emperor Who Gave Up War / a very big kingdom = an empire, printed pp. 67–69.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Ruling the empire.
**Answer: Around Pataliputra the emperor rules directly through salaried officials, messengers and spies; provinces answer from Taxila and Ujjain under royal princes; between the centres, roads and rivers are gripped for tax and tribute — blankets from the north-west, gold from the south — while forest peoples stay near-independent, sending elephants, timber, honey and wax (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.7 / Ruling the empire, printed pp. 69–70.**
