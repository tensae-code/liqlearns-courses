# India — the Mauryan empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) A very big kingdom = an empire  b) The earliest Tamil compositions  c) The spread of Buddhism
**Answer: a) A very big kingdom = an empire** — this part tells a very big kingdom = an empire

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Chandragupta Maurya founds the empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by the wise Chanakya whose ideas fill the Arthashastra  b) Poems composed around 2300 years ago are gathered in assemblies — sangams — of poets at Madurai, preserving the Tamil words for the village's people and becoming a historian's source in verse  c) Devotion to a chosen deity — Shiva, Vishnu, Durga — opens worship to rich and poor, high caste and low, men and women alike, without elaborate sacrifice
**Answer: a)** — Chandragupta Maurya founds the empire more than 2300 years ago, advised by the wise Chanakya whose ideas fill the Arthashastra

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Ruling the empire  b) Finding out about cities: stories, travellers, sculpture and archaeology; the clever poor man  c) The quest of the pilgrims
**Answer: a) Ruling the empire** — this part tells ruling the empire

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) Around Pataliputra the emperor rules directly through salaried officials, messengers and spies  b) A Jataka tale follows a penniless young man who turns a dead rat into a coin, storm-fallen garden wood into fuel money, and 500 grass-cutters' bundles into a thousand coins from a horse dealer — fiction used carefully, with sculpture and travellers' tales, as evidence for city life  c) About 2000 years ago in West Asia the teaching of Jesus begins the faith that will travel the same trade routes the chapter has been tracing
**Answer: a)** — around Pataliputra the emperor rules directly through salaried officials, messengers and spies
