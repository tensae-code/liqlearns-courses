# Causes of the collapse of the ancient Arab civilisations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Causes of the collapse of the ancient Arab civilisations.
**Answer: one by one the sisters fall the same way — Yemen's farmers turn Bedouin, five kingdoms fight at once, the Ptolemies steal the monsoon secrets and break the incense monopoly, Aelius Gallus is beaten at Marib's walls in 24 BCE but Abyssinia finishes the work in 525 CE after Dhu Nuwas burns Najran's Christians; Babylon falls to Cyrus in 539 BCE, the divided Levant passes from Assyria to Babylon to Persia to Alexander, Egypt is passed hand to hand until Alexander takes it in 332 BCE, and Rome, sworn that Carthage must be destroyed, starves it out in 146 BCE (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم القديم — للصف الأول الثانوي (History: Civilisations of the Ancient World, for the First Secondary Grade)* (Yemen), Causes of the collapse of the ancient Arab civilisations, printed p. 63.**
