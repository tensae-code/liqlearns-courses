# Pacific voyaging and island exchange — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — One large exchange community
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: name the islands the reader names as once part of one large exchange community — Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea.

## Sub lesson 2 — Voyaging between islands
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that island peoples regularly voyaged between islands, and that the sea joined their homes instead of dividing them.

## Sub lesson 3 — What travelled
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say what circulated on the voyages — wealth, skills and goods — and explain "The Ocean in our blood" in your own words.
