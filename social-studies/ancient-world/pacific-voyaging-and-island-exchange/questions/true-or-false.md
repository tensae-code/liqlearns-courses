# Pacific voyaging and island exchange — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The islands in the exchange community were joined by regular voyaging, not cut off from each other by the sea.
**Answer: True** — island peoples regularly voyaged between islands, and the sea joined their homes.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Only goods travelled between the islands; skills stayed at home.
**Answer: False** — wealth, skills and goods all circulated between the islands.
