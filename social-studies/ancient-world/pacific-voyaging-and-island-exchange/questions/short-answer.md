# Pacific voyaging and island exchange — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name four of the islands the reader names as once part of one large exchange community.
**Answer: Any four of Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Explain in your own words why the reader calls this story "The Ocean in our blood".
**Answer: Because the sea joined the islands into one community — peoples regularly voyaged between them circulating wealth, skills and goods, so the ocean was part of who they were, one sea joining many homes (any close wording).**
