# Pacific voyaging and island exchange — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea were once part of —
a) One large exchange community  b) One large city  c) One large desert kingdom
**Answer: a) One large exchange community** — the reader's opening story of the Pacific past.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Island peoples joined their islands by —
a) Building bridges  b) Regularly voyaging between islands  c) Waiting for visitors from far away
**Answer: b) Regularly voyaging between islands** — voyaging was the ordinary way the community stayed one community.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What circulated on the voyages was —
a) Only fish  b) Only stories  c) Wealth, skills and goods
**Answer: c) Wealth, skills and goods** — each island shared in what the others had and knew.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The reader's heading for this story is —
a) "The Ocean in our blood"  b) "The Mountain in our way"  c) "The River in our fields"
**Answer: a) "The Ocean in our blood"** — one sea joining many homes.
