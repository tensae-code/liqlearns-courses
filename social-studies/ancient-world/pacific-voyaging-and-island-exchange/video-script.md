# Video script — Pacific voyaging and island exchange

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the sea itself tells how it joined the islands. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Look at a map of the Pacific. The islands look far apart, each one alone in a wide blue sea.

But the reader tells a different story. Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea were once part of one large exchange community.

The peoples of these islands did not stay home. They voyaged — regularly, again and again — from island to island across the water.

And the boats did not travel empty. Wealth travelled. Skills travelled. Goods travelled, circulating from home to home.

So the sea was not a wall between the islands. It was the road that joined them — one sea, many homes. That is why the reader calls it: the ocean in our blood.
