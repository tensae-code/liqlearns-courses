# Hands-on — Pacific voyaging and island exchange

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Join the islands
Draw nine dots on paper for the nine islands the reader names: Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, Niue, Rotuma, Tokelau, Tuvalu, Futuna and Uvea. Draw voyaging lines joining them into one community, the way the lesson's map slot shows.

## What travels in your boat?
Choose one island as your home. Write three loads your boat would carry to a neighbour island — one thing of wealth, one skill you could teach, and one good you could trade. Say what your neighbour's boat might bring back.

## One sea, many homes
In two sentences, explain to a partner why the sea joined these islands instead of dividing them. Use the words wealth, skills and goods at least once.
