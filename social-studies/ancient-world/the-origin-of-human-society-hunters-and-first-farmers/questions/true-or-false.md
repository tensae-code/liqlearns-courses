# The origin of human society — hunters and first farmers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mastering fire was one of the first technologies of the human story.
**Answer: True — hunters learn to shape stone and tame fire.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Farming began, in this lesson, in Greece and Rome.
**Answer: False — the guide places agriculture's rise in Mesopotamia and Egypt.**
