# The origin of human society — hunters and first farmers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rock Age hunters developed — —
a) Weapons and tools, and mastered fire  b) Writing and cities first  c) Ships and sails first
**Answer: a) Weapons and tools, and mastered fire** — Weapons and tools, and mastered fire.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The hunters in this lesson belong to — —
a) The early, middle and late Rock Age  b) The Bronze Age only  c) The age of empires
**Answer: a) The early, middle and late Rock Age** — The early, middle and late Rock Age.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Agriculture first becomes part of civilisation in the guide's story in — —
a) Mesopotamia and Egypt  b) Greece and Rome  c) China and India
**Answer: a) Mesopotamia and Egypt** — Mesopotamia and Egypt.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** When farming arrives, villages replace — —
a) Camps  b) Cities  c) Pagodas
**Answer: a) Camps** — Camps — planting replaces chasing, villages replace camps.
