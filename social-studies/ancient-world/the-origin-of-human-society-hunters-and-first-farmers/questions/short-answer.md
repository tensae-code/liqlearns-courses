# The origin of human society — hunters and first farmers — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What two first technologies do the hunters gain in this lesson?
**Answer: They develop stone weapons and tools, and they master fire (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does farming remake how humans live together?
**Answer: Planting replaces chasing and villages replace camps, as agriculture gradually becomes part of human civilisation in Mesopotamia and Egypt (any close wording).**
