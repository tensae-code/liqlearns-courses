# Video script — The origin of human society — hunters and first farmers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Myanmar's story as its Grade 6 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Early, middle and late Rock Age hunters develop weapons and tools and master fire: bands of hunters learn to shape stone and tame fire, the first technologies of the human story.

Agriculture gradually becomes part of human civilisation, in the Mesopotamia region and Egypt: planting replaces chasing, villages replace camps, and farming remakes how humans live together.
