# Tomaris and Shirak — legends of Central Asia — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 13. The feat of Tomaris and Shirak.
**Answer: Queen Tomaris of the Massagetae defeats Cyrus the Great and dips his head in blood; the shepherd Shirak maims himself to lead Darius's army into the desert — two homeland legends of sacrifice against Persia (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
