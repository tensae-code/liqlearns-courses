# Video script — Tomaris and Shirak — legends of Central Asia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uzbekistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Queen Tomaris of the Massagetae defeats Cyrus the Great and dips his head in blood; the shepherd Shirak maims himself to lead Darius's army into the desert — two homeland legends of sacrifice against Persia.
