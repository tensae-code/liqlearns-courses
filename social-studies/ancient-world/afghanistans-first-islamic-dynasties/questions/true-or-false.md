# Afghanistan's first Islamic dynasties — Tahirids, Saffarids and Samanids — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 2 (Independent Islamic Governments of Afghanistan), Lesson 5: Tahirids, Saffarids and Samanids

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History, Grade 8* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Chapter 2 (Independent Islamic Governments of Afghanistan), Lesson 5: Tahirids, Saffarids and Samanids

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tahir of Fushanj makes the first post-Islam bid for the country's independence with his semi-independent Tahirid state, and the Saffarids carry the work against the Abbasid caliphate further.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 7: Tahirids and Saffarids

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History, Grade 11* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 7: Tahirids and Saffarids
