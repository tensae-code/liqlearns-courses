# Iran — Islam arrives — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arab armies end Sasanian rule, but Iran converts gradually and on its own terms, preparing a civilisation that will be both Islamic and Iranian.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Islam in Iran; the grounds for the rise of Iranian-Islamic civilisation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 11 History 2* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Islam in Iran; the grounds for the rise of Iranian-Islamic civilisation

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sasanian Iran, exhausted and divided, falls to Arab armies battle by battle, and a new era opens.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The entry of Islam into Iran

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 8 Social Studies* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The entry of Islam into Iran
