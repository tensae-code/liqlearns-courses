# Iran — Islam arrives

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Arab armies end Sasanian rule, but Iran converts gradually and on its own terms, preparing a civilisation that will be both Islamic and Iranian As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
