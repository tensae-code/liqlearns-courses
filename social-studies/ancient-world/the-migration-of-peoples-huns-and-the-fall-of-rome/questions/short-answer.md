# The Migration of Peoples — the Huns and the fall of Rome — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the migration's chain in order.
**Answer: The Huns push west; the Germanic peoples, pressed in their turn, move and cross Rome's frontiers; the western Roman world gives way (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why could Rome not simply hold its borders?
**Answer: Because whole peoples, not armies alone, were on the move, and the strained empire could not hold frontiers against them (any close wording).**
