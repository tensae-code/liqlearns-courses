# The Migration of Peoples — the Huns and the fall of Rome — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kavimler Göçü means —
a) The Building of Roads  b) The Migration of Peoples  c) The War of Kings
**Answer: b) The Migration of Peoples** — the event's Turkish name.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The push begins with —
a) The Huns moving westward  b) Rome marching east  c) The Germanic peoples sailing south
**Answer: a) The Huns moving westward** — the first stone falls on the steppe.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Germanic peoples moved because —
a) They were invited to rule Rome  b) The Huns' push pressed them in their turn  c) Their lands flooded
**Answer: b) The Huns' push pressed them in their turn** — people against people.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The chain ends with —
a) The founding of Constantinople  b) The western Roman world giving way  c) The Huns ruling Egypt
**Answer: b) The western Roman world giving way** — the ancient world closes in Europe.
