# The Migration of Peoples — the Huns and the fall of Rome — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Migration of Peoples helped end Rome in the West.
**Answer: True** — the book's chain ends at Rome's frontiers giving way.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Huns conquered and ruled the whole Roman Empire.
**Answer: False** — their push set others moving; the book does not make them Rome's rulers.
