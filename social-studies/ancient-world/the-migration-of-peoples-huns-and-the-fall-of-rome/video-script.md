# Video script — The Migration of Peoples — the Huns and the fall of Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Migration of Peoples — Kavimler Göçü — begins on the steppe. The Huns push westward out of Asia, and their push travels: people pressed against people, the Germanic peoples move in their turn and cross Rome's frontiers.

An empire already strained cannot hold borders that whole peoples are crossing. The western Roman world gives way.

A steppe people's migration crosses two continents — and closes the ancient world in Europe.
