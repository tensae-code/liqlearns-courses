# Lesson plan — The Migration of Peoples — the Huns and the fall of Rome

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Migration of Peoples — the Huns and the fall of Rome
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 4, Topic 4.4, The Migration of Peoples (Kavimler Göçü): the Huns' westward push sets the Germanic peoples moving and helps end Rome.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: a steppe people's migration crosses two continents and closes the ancient world in Europe. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the event in Turkish and English: Kavimler Göçü, the Migration of Peoples.
2. Tell the chain in order: the Huns push west; the Germanic peoples move; Rome's frontiers give way.
3. Say where the push begins: on the steppe, with the Huns moving westward out of Asia.
4. Explain, in their own words, how a migration can close an age in a land it never ruled.
