# Hands-on — The Migration of Peoples — the Huns and the fall of Rome

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The falling-stones line

Draw a line of peoples as standing stones from the steppe to Rome. Tip the first — the Huns — and draw arrows showing each people pushing the next. Label the last stone: Rome's frontier.

## Two continents, one push

On a rough map of Asia and Europe, draw the Huns' westward arrow, then the Germanic peoples' arrows toward Rome. Write one sentence under the map: how far a push can travel.

## A frontier diary

Write four diary lines as a Roman frontier guard across four years: first rumours, then refugees, then whole peoples at the river, then orders that no longer make sense. Say what changed.
