# China Warring States Reforms And Hundred Schools — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shang Yang leaves Wei for Qin in 356 BCE preaching 'Tang and Wu did not follow the ancients and became kings'; his reforms reward farming and war and make Qin rich and feared — and Qin executes him anyway, but keeps his laws.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Feudal lords contend, reform movements rise

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Feudal lords contend, reform movements rise

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Amid the chaos Confucius, Laozi, Mozi, Han Fei and the rest argue rival answers to one question — how shall the world be ordered again?.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Hundred Schools

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Hundred Schools

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Shang Yang's Qin reforms open a two-thousand-year procession — every strong dynasty reforms or declines.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Reforms through Chinese history (1)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 选择性必修1 国家制度与社会治理 (Senior High History, Selective Compulsory 1: State Systems and Social Governance)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Reforms through Chinese history (1)
