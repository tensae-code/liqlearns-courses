**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Feudal lords contend, reform movements rise.
**Answer: Shang Yang leaves Wei for Qin in 356 BCE preaching 'Tang and Wu did not follow the ancients and became kings'; his reforms reward farming and war and make Qin rich and feared — and Qin executes him anyway, but keeps his laws (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
