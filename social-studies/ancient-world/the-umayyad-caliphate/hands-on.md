# Hands-on — The Umayyad Caliphate

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chosen or born?

Draw two ladders to one throne: on the first, leading men raise a chosen caliph; on the second, a son climbs by birth. Label each ladder, and write one strength and one danger of each way.

## Damascus, the capital

On a rough map, mark Damascus and shade the wide empire it governed. Write one sentence on why a capital's place matters to an empire.

## The question that stayed

Write the question the Umayyads leave behind — is the caliph chosen, or born? — and under it, one argument for each side, as two courtiers might give them.
