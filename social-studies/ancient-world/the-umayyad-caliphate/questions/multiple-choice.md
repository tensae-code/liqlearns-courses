# The Umayyad Caliphate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Umayyad capital was —
a) Baghdad  b) Damascus  c) Medina
**Answer: b) Damascus** — the dynasty's seat.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Umayyads, the caliphate became —
a) A dynasty, passing by inheritance  b) A republic  c) A tribal council
**Answer: a) A dynasty, passing by inheritance** — rule by birth.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The change the book stresses is —
a) A new alphabet  b) Rule by election giving way to rule by inheritance  c) A move to Egypt
**Answer: b) Rule by election giving way to rule by inheritance** — the beat of the topic.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The first caliphs had been raised —
a) By birth in one family  b) By choice among the community's leaders  c) By lot
**Answer: b) By choice among the community's leaders** — the earlier way.
