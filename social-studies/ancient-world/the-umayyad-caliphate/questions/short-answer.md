# The Umayyad Caliphate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What changed in the caliphate under the Umayyads?
**Answer: It became a dynasty at Damascus — rule by inheritance replaced rule by election (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What question does the Umayyad change leave to later Islamic states?
**Answer: Whether the caliph should be chosen or born to the office (any close wording).**
