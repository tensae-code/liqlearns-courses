# The Umayyad Caliphate — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Umayyads ruled from Damascus.
**Answer: True** — the dynasty's capital.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Umayyads, each caliph was newly elected by the whole community.
**Answer: False** — succession ran by inheritance within the family.
