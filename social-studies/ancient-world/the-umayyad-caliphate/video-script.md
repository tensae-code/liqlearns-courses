# Video script — The Umayyad Caliphate

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The first caliphs were chosen. Then, Turkey's Grade 9 book tells, the caliphate became a dynasty: the Umayyads, ruling from Damascus, passed power by birth within one family.

The empire was vast, and Damascus governed it as a hereditary monarchy in all but name.

Rule by election gives way to rule by inheritance — and the question, chosen or born, outlives the dynasty.
