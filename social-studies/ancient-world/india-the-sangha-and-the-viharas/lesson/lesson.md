## Sub lesson 1 — The sangha; monasteries

Buddha and Mahavira hold that true knowledge needs leaving home, so monks and nuns live simply under the Vinaya Pitaka's rules, begging at fixed hours; rainy-season shelters harden into permanent viharas — wood, brick, then rock-cut caves — built on land gifted by kings and merchants.
