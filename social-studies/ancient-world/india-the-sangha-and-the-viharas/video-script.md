# Video script — India — the sangha and the viharas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Buddha and Mahavira hold that true knowledge needs leaving home, so monks and nuns live simply under the Vinaya Pitaka's rules, begging at fixed hours; rainy-season shelters harden into permanent viharas — wood, brick, then rock-cut caves — built on land gifted by kings and merchants.
