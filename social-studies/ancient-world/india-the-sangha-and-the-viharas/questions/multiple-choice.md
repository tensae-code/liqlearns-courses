**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The sangha; monasteries  b) What was Ashoka's dhamma?; Ashoka's messages to his subjects  c) A poem about trade; sea routes and monsoon winds

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
