# Video script — Gothic art — cathedrals and towns

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cathedrals race each other into the sky on pointed arches and coloured glass.

Towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches.

Giotto starts painting people with weight and grief again, the Netherlandish masters fill panels with the whole visible world, and Bosch fills his Garden with warning — the Middle Ages handing over to something new.

That is the story: the cathedrals race each other into the sky on pointed arches and coloured glass; towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches; and at the period's end Giotto, the Netherlandish masters and Bosch hand the Middle Ages over to something new.
