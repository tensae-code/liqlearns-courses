# Gothic art — cathedrals and towns — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Towns build beside bishops  b) Cathedrals race into the sky  c) The Middle Ages hand over
**Answer: b) Cathedrals race into the sky** — Cathedrals race each other into the sky on pointed arches and coloured glass.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Towns build beside bishops  b) Cathedrals race into the sky  c) The Middle Ages hand over
**Answer: a) Towns build beside bishops** — Towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Cathedrals race into the sky  b) Towns build beside bishops  c) The Middle Ages hand over
**Answer: c) The Middle Ages hand over** — Giotto starts painting people with weight and grief again, the Netherlandish masters fill panels with the whole visible world, and Bosch fills his Garden with warning — the Middle Ages handing over to something new.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The cathedrals race each other into the sky on pointed arches and coloured glass; towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches; and at the period's end Giotto, the Netherlandish masters and Bosch hand the Middle Ages over to something new  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
