# Gothic art — cathedrals and towns — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — The cathedrals race each other into the sky on pointed arches and coloured glass; towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches; and at the period's end Giotto, the Netherlandish masters and Bosch hand the Middle Ages over to something new.
