# Lesson plan — Gothic art — cathedrals and towns

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Gothic art — cathedrals and towns
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The cathedrals race each other into the sky on pointed arches and coloured glass; towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches; and at the period's end Giotto, the Netherlandish masters and Bosch hand the Middle Ages over to something new.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Cathedrals race into the sky** — Cathedrals race each other into the sky on pointed arches and coloured glass.
2. **Towns build beside bishops** — Towns grow rich and build town halls beside the bishops' churches.
3. **The Middle Ages hand over** — Giotto starts painting people with weight and grief again, the Netherlandish masters fill panels with the whole visible world, and Bosch fills his Garden with warning — the Middle Ages handing over to something new.
