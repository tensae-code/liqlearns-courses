# China Sui Unification Grand Canal And Keju — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Sui unification and fall.
**Answer: Yang Jian reunifies China in 589 after centuries of division; the Grand Canal links five river systems over 2,700 km and the keju examinations open office to talent, but Yangdi's palaces, dragon boats and three Goguryeo campaigns drive people to cut off their own hands to escape service, and he is killed in 618 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 1: Sui unification and fall, printed p. 1.**
