# China Sui Unification Grand Canal And Keju — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yang Jian reunifies China in 589 after centuries of division; the Grand Canal links five river systems over 2,700 km and the keju examinations open office to talent, but Yangdi's palaces, dragon boats and three Goguryeo campaigns drive people to cut off their own hands to escape service, and he is killed in 618.
