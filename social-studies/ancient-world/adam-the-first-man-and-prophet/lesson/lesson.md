# Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: God creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all peoples spread across the earth. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
