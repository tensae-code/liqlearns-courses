# Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** God creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all peoples spread across the earth.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** God announces a deputy on earth, teaches Adam the names of all things while the angels confess they know only what they were taught, Iblis alone refuses to bow and is expelled, Adam and Eve live a while in a temporary garden, eat of the one forbidden tree at Satan's false oath, repent and are forgiven, and come down to earth with Satan as man's permanent enemy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Abu al-Bashar Adam

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Abu al-Bashar Adam
