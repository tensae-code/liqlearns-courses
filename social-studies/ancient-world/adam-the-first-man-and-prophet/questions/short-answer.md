# Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God.
**Answer: god creates Adam from dust and makes him His deputy on earth, the angels bow to him while Iblis refuses out of pride, and from Adam and Eve all peoples spread across the earth (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* (Yemen), Adam, father of mankind, prophet of God, printed p. 10.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Abu al-Bashar Adam.
**Answer: god announces a deputy on earth, teaches Adam the names of all things while the angels confess they know only what they were taught, Iblis alone refuses to bow and is expelled, Adam and Eve live a while in a temporary garden, eat of the one forbidden tree at Satan's false oath, repent and are forgiven, and come down to earth with Satan as man's permanent enemy (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Abu al-Bashar Adam, printed p. 15.**
