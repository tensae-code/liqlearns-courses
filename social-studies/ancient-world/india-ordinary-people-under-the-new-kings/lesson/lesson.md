# India — ordinary people under the new kings

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Under the new kings, ordinary people met power at the gate: a poor fisherman accused over the king's ring, untouchables striking wood to warn others away, and armies trampling villagers' huts on the march. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ordinary people in the kingdoms

In Kalidasa's play a poor fisherman who returns the king's ring found in a fish is accused of theft by the gatekeepers, then rewarded — and the officials demand a cut; Fa Xian records untouchables striking wood to warn others away in markets; Banabhatta's royal army on the march swallows the world in dust, trampling villagers' huts as it passes.
