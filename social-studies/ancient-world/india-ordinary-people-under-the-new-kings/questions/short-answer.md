# India — ordinary people under the new kings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ordinary people in the kingdoms.
**Answer: In Kalidasa's play a poor fisherman who returns the king's ring found in a fish is accused of theft by the gatekeepers, then rewarded — and the officials demand a cut; Fa Xian records untouchables striking wood to warn others away in markets; Banabhatta's royal army on the march swallows the world in dust, trampling villagers' huts as it passes (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.10 / Ordinary people in the kingdoms, printed pp. 110–111.**
