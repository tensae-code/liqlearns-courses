# India — ordinary people under the new kings — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Kalidasa's play a poor fisherman who returns the king's ring found in a fish is accused of theft by the gatekeepers, then rewarded — and the officials demand a cut.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ordinary people in the kingdoms

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about recording and preserving old stories.
**Answer: False** — it is about ordinary people in the kingdoms
