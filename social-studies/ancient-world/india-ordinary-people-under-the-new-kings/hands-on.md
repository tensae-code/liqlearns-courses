# Hands-on — India — ordinary people under the new kings

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Ordinary people in the kingdoms — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ordinary people in the kingdoms, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
