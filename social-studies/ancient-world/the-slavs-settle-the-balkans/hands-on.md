# Hands-on — The Slavs settle the Balkans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Slavs settle the Balkans — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch.2 The arrival of the ancestors of the Montenegrins and the South Slavs in the Balkans: South Slavs settle around the middle and lower Danube; Avars arrive in Pannonia in the later 6th century and Slavs and Avars together raid Byzantine provinces to Thessalonica, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
