# The Slavs settle the Balkans — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The child's ancestors walk into the Balkans behind the Avar wars and stay.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2 The arrival of the ancestors of the Montenegrins and the South Slavs in the Balkans: South Slavs settle around the middle and lower Danube; Avars arrive in Pannonia in the later 6th century and Slavs and Avars together raid Byzantine provinces to Thessalonica

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ИСТОРИЈА за седми разред основне школе (Istorija 7)* is about The Kingdom of Serbs, Croats and Slovenes.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.2 The arrival of the ancestors of the Montenegrins and the South Slavs in the Balkans: South Slavs settle around the middle and lower Danube; Avars arrive in Pannonia in the later 6th century and Slavs and Avars together raid Byzantine provinces to Thessalonica
