# Phoenicians in Cyprus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Phoenicians came to Cyprus mainly as —
a) traders  b) desert farmers  c) Roman soldiers
**Answer: a) traders** — Phoenician traders settle on the island.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Cyprus the Phoenicians built —
a) colonies and temples  b) pyramids  c) aqueducts
**Answer: a) colonies and temples** — they build colonies and temples.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the Phoenicians carry east from Cyprus?
a) copper and goods  b) ice  c) horses only
**Answer: a) copper and goods** — taking copper and goods east.
