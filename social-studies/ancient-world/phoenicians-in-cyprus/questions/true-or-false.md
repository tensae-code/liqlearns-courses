# Phoenicians in Cyprus — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Phoenician temples stood in Cyprus.
**Answer: True** — the Phoenicians build colonies and temples on the island.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Phoenicians never settled in Cyprus; they only sailed past.
**Answer: False** — they settle on the island and change Cypriot life.
