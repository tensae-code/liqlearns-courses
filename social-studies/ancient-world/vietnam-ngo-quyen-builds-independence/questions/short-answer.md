# Vietnam Ngo Quyen Builds Independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 9, Ngô Quyền builds the independent state (939).
**Answer: after Bạch Đằng he drops the Tiết độ sứ title, proclaims himself King and rules from Cổ Loa with civil and military officials and generals holding the provinces; the Trần-era historian Lê Văn Hưu judges that with him "the orthodox line of our country's kingship was restored" (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 9, Ngô Quyền builds the independent state (939), printed p. 45.**
