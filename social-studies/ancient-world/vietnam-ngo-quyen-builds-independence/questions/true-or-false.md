# Vietnam Ngo Quyen Builds Independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after Bạch Đằng he drops the Tiết độ sứ title, proclaims himself King and rules from Cổ Loa with civil and military officials and generals holding the provinces; the Trần-era historian Lê Văn Hưu judges that with him "the orthodox line of our country's kingship was restored".

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 7 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
