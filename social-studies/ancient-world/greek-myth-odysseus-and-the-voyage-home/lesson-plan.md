# Lesson plan — Greek Myth Odysseus And The Voyage Home

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Greek Myth Odysseus And The Voyage Home
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes** — Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep
2. **Unit 6, ch. 2 With Aeolus, the Laestrygonians and on Circe's island** — Aeolus's bag of winds is opened within sight of home, giants destroy the fleet, and Circe turns the scouts into swine until Odysseus resists her
3. **Unit 6, ch. 3 In Hades, at the Sirens, at Scylla and Charybdis** — Odysseus questions the dead in Hades, hears the Sirens bound to the mast, and threads the strait losing six men to Scylla
4. **Unit 6, ch. 4 The Sun's island, Calypso's island and the Phaeacians** — the crew slaughter the Sun's cattle and drown for it; Odysseus alone survives, spends years with Calypso, and the Phaeacians carry him home asleep
5. **Unit 6, ch. 5 Odysseus in Ithaca** — disguised as a beggar, Odysseus is sheltered by the loyal swineherd Eumaeus and tests who in his house stayed faithful
6. **Unit 6, ch. 6 Odysseus kills the suitors** — Penelope's weaving trick revealed, Odysseus alone strings the great bow in the contest and turns it on the suitors who consumed his house
7. **Unit 6, ch. 1 Cicones, Lotus-eaters, Cyclopes exercises** — exercises follow Odysseus's first three landfalls and the blinding of Polyphemus
8. **Unit 6, ch. 2 Aeolus, Laestrygonians, Circe exercises** — pupils work out why the pictured ship is named as it is while retelling the bag of winds and Circe's swine
9. **Unit 6, ch. 3 Hades, Sirens, Scylla and Charybdis exercises** — exercises thread Odysseus between the monsters and through the land of the dead
10. **Unit 6, ch. 4 Sun's island, Calypso, Phaeacians exercises** — pupils judge the crew's fatal meal of the Sun's cattle and Odysseus's lone survival
11. **Unit 6, ch. 5 Odysseus in Ithaca exercises** — exercises find signs of the disguised king in today's lesson pictures — recognition as detective work
12. **Unit 6, ch. 6 Killing of the suitors exercises** — pupils judge Odysseus's revenge on the suitors — the voyage judged as a moral question

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
