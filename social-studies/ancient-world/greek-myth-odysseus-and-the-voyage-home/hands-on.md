# Hands-on — Greek Myth Odysseus And The Voyage Home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Greek Myth Odysseus And The Voyage Home — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
