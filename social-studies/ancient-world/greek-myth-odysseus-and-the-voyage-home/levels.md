# Greek Myth Odysseus And The Voyage Home — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 6, ch. 1 With the Cicones, the Lotus-eaters and the Cyclopes
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Odysseus raids the Cicones, loses men to the forgetful lotus, and escapes Polyphemus's cave by blinding him and hiding under the sheep
