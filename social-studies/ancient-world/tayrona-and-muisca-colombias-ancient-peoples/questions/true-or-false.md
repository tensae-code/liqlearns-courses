# Tayrona and Muisca — Colombia's ancient peoples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tayrona and Muisca built great villages and complex cultures; Muisca descendants still live on the Cundiboyacense high plain and Tayrona descendants in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Empresa Conquistadora, Colombia panel

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Empresa Conquistadora (board-game insert for Ciencias Sociales, Grado 4, Cartilla 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Empresa Conquistadora, Colombia panel

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** America's ancient peoples work metals into sacred art, thanking Mother Earth for the materials, a craft Colombia's own ancient peoples carried to mastery
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Módulo 2 / Guía 6: Goldwork of the millenary peoples

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Grado 6, Postprimaria Rural (Sociales)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Módulo 2 / Guía 6: Goldwork of the millenary peoples

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Tairona culture of the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta and the Caribbean plain farms its steep land with its own techniques
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 16: The Taironas and their agricultural techniques

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Grado 6, Secundaria Activa (student module)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 16: The Taironas and their agricultural techniques

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In Kogi mythology the elder brothers hold the centre of humanity, the Tairona build artificial terraces to farm, and the Wayúu hold La Guajira at South America's northern tip
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Guía 8: How other cultures related to the environment
