# China Song Yuan Cities And Culture — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kaifeng's night markets never close, Daxiangguo Temple opens five times a month as a fair for birds, books and antiques, and Lin'an's wazi theatres fill with acrobats, storytellers and zaju plays.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Song-Yuan cities and culture

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Song-Yuan cities and culture

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Su Shi's ci, Sima Guang's history mirror, Song landscape painting and Yuan stage drama carry Chinese culture to a new peak.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Liao-Song-Xia-Jin-Yuan culture

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Liao-Song-Xia-Jin-Yuan culture
