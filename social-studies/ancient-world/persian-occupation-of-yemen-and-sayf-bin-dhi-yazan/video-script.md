# Video script — The Persian occupation of Yemen

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sayf bin Dhi Yazan begs Khosrow of Persia for an army, Wahrez leads it and drives the Abyssinians out, but Sayf rules only by paying tribute and is assassinated, Wahrez takes Yemen for Persia, and Persian rule lasts until the governor Badhan's time, when Yemenis answer Muhammad's call and Yemen joins the Islamic state.
Sayf's victory turns to tutelage — Abyssinian servants assassinate him in his palace, Khosrow sends Wahriz back in vengeance, and Persian rule begins, shrinking under Yemeni revolts until it holds only Sanaa, Aden and their surroundings under the governor Badhan; then Islam arrives, Badhan's Persians enter it, and Yemen passes freely into the new Arab-Islamic state, Islam uniting the Yemenis under one banner and carrying their civilisation to every corner of the world.
