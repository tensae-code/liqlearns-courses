# Ghaznavids and Seljuks — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Alptigin, a Turkish commander of the Samanids, plants the Ghaznavid seed and his son-in-law Sebuktigin makes it a kingdom at Ghazni; the Seljuks follow as the second great Afghan-based power.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 6: Ghaznavids and Seljuks

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History, Grade 8* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 6: Ghaznavids and Seljuks

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ghazni's Turkish sultans and then the Seljuks hold the land in two successive great powers.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 9: Ghaznavids and Seljuks

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History, Grade 11* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 9: Ghaznavids and Seljuks
