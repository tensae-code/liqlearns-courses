# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — the Great Wall and China's gifts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Wall of books
Build the longest wall you can from books or stones across a room or yard. Measure it, then compare: how many of your walls make one kilometre? How many make the Great Wall?

## Compass from a needle
Float a magnetised needle on a leaf in a bowl of water and watch it settle north–south — the compass principle China gave the world (an adult helps with the needle).
