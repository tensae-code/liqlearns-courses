# Ancient civilisations — the Great Wall and China's gifts

## Do it first

The longest building human beings ever made is a wall. Before you read: why would a rich, strong empire spend generations piling stone across mountains? Write your guess — then meet the Wall.

## A wall across the mountains

The **Great Wall — 21,196 km long, as the book prints it** — was **begun under the Qin** dynasty **to keep out invaders from Asia**. Stand back and take its measure from the picture: no road, no palace, no tomb on Earth runs so far. In the same Qin age, **law was centralised across China** — one wall on the border, one law inside it.

[Picture 11355 — the Great Wall crossing mountains]

## China's gifts

China's contribution list runs: **paper-making, the compass, gunpowder, an earthquake-measuring instrument, the calendar, fertiliser-making, art and sculpture**. Paper to write on, the compass to travel by, an instrument that feels the earth shake before people do — gifts that left China and changed every land they reached.
