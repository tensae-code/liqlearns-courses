# Ancient civilisations — the Great Wall and China's gifts — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Say the Great Wall's printed length, under whom it was begun, and why.; Name China's contribution list, and say what the same Qin age did for law across China.. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
