# Video script — Ancient civilisations — the Great Wall and China's gifts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A wall longer than any road on Earth.
Twenty-one thousand kilometres, over the mountains.
Qin began it, to keep invaders out.
And China gave paper, the compass, gunpowder —
gifts that travelled farther than the Wall.
