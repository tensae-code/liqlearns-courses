# Ancient civilisations — the Great Wall and China's gifts — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** The Qin age also centralised law across China.
**Answer: True** — One wall outside, one law inside.
**2.** China made an instrument for measuring earthquakes.
**Answer: True** — It is on the contribution list.
**3.** The Great Wall was built as a trade road for silk.
**Answer: False** — It was begun to keep out invaders.
