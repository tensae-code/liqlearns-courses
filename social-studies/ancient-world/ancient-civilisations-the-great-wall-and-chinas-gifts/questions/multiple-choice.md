# Ancient civilisations — the Great Wall and China's gifts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Great Wall was begun under —
a) Han  b) Qin  c) Shang  d) Zhou
**Answer: b) Qin** — The unifying dynasty built the shield too.
**2.** The Wall was built to —
a) Carry silk  b) Mark a race course  c) Keep out invaders from Asia  d) Store grain
**Answer: c) Keep out invaders from Asia** — A shield across the north.
**3.** China's gifts include —
a) Paper-making, the compass and gunpowder  b) Cuneiform  c) The 60-minute hour  d) Hieroglyphics
**Answer: a) Paper-making, the compass and gunpowder** — Plus the earthquake instrument, calendar, fertiliser, art and sculpture.
**4.** The Wall's length, as printed, is —
a) 2,196 km  b) 21,196 km  c) 1,196 km  d) 211,960 km
**Answer: b) 21,196 km** — Taught exactly as the book prints it.
