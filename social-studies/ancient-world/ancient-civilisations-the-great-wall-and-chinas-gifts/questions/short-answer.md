# Ancient civilisations — the Great Wall and China's gifts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why was the Great Wall built?
**Answer:** It was begun under Qin to keep out invaders from Asia — a shield wall across China's northern mountains, 21,196 km as printed.
**2.** Name five of China's contributions.
**Answer:** Any five of: paper-making, the compass, gunpowder, an earthquake-measuring instrument, the calendar, fertiliser-making, art and sculpture.
