# Bronze Age — myth and history — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Myths are useless to historians.
**Answer: False** — they carry a people's memory and imagination, tested against the finds.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A story being old and loved proves every event in it happened.
**Answer: False** — it must be tested against what the finds actually show.
