# Bronze Age — myth and history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A myth is best read as —
a) a people's memory and imagination in story form  b) a newspaper report  c) a list of kings
**Answer: a) a people's memory and imagination in story form** — myths are read as a people's memory and imagination.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The historian tests a myth against —
a) what the finds actually show  b) what feels nicest  c) the longest version
**Answer: a) what the finds actually show** — tested against what the finds actually show.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** If the finds disagree with a myth, the historian —
a) keeps both in view and trusts the evidence more  b) burns the finds  c) stops asking
**Answer: a) keeps both in view and trusts the evidence more** — the myth is read, but the finds decide.
