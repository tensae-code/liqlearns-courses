# The Fourth Crusade And 1204 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Fourth Crusade, diverted by Venice and debt, storms the Christian capital in 1204 and parcels the empire among Latins.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 30 The capture of Constantinople by the Franks

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Ιστορία Ε΄ Δημοτικού — Στα Βυζαντινά Χρόνια (History, Grade 5 Primary: In Byzantine Times)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 30 The capture of Constantinople by the Franks

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** ordering the Crusade's diversion ends at the storming of a Christian capital.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 30 Fourth Crusade and the Frankish capture exercises

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Ιστορία Ε΄ Δημοτικού — Στα Βυζαντινά Χρόνια, Τετράδιο Εργασιών (History Grade 5 workbook)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 30 Fourth Crusade and the Frankish capture exercises
