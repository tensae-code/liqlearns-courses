# Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Onesilos died fighting the Persians.
**Answer: True** — he dies in battle against the Persians.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After Onesilos, Cyprus stayed free of Persia for good.
**Answer: False** — the island falls back under Persian rule.
