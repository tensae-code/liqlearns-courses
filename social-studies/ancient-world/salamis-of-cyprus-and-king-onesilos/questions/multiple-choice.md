# Salamis of Cyprus and King Onesilos — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Salamis showed its wealth and power by —
a) striking its own coins  b) building a pyramid  c) closing its harbour
**Answer: a) striking its own coins** — Salamis strikes its own coins.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Salamis buried its kings with —
a) horses and chariots in royal tombs  b) ships at sea  c) nothing at all
**Answer: a) horses and chariots in royal tombs** — kings buried with horses and chariots in royal tombs.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Onesilos led Cyprus in revolt against —
a) the Persians  b) the Phoenicians  c) Rome
**Answer: a) the Persians** — Onesilos leads Cyprus into the revolt against Persia.
