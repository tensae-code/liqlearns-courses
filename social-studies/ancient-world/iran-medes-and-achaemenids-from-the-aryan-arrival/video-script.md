# Video script — Iran — Medes and Achaemenids, from the Aryan arrival

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iran's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Aryan tribes enter the plateau, the Medes unite them and help destroy Assyria at Nineveh, and Darius then rebuilds the Achaemenid Empire into provinces, roads and law after civil war.

Aryan tribes arrive, Medes and Persians build the first powerful Iranian states, and empire becomes possible.

the class visits Ecbatana, the Medes' golden capital, and walks where the first Iranian kingdom ruled.

at Bisotun, Darius's carved cliff inscription tells how he seized and held the empire — the king's own words in stone.
