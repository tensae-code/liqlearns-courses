## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: — Medes to Darius — Aryan tribes enter the plateau, the Medes unite them and help destroy Assyria at Nineveh, and Darius then rebuilds the Achaemenid Empire into provinces, roads and law after civil war; The Aryans and the formation of powerful governments in Iran — Aryan tribes arrive, Medes and Persians build the first powerful Iranian states, and empire becomes possible; A trip to the ancient city of Hamadan — the class visits Ecbatana, the Medes' golden capital, and walks where the first Iranian kingdom ruled; A trip to the ancient city of Kermanshah — at Bisotun, Darius's carved cliff inscription tells how he seized and held the empire — the king's own words in stone. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
