**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The earliest Tamil compositions.
**Answer: Poems composed around 2300 years ago are gathered in assemblies — sangams — of poets at Madurai, preserving the Tamil words for the village's people and becoming a historian's source in verse (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
