# India — Sangam literature

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Sangam literature — poems composed around 2300 years ago and gathered in poets' assemblies at Madurai — preserves the earliest Tamil voices and the words for a village's people. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The earliest Tamil compositions

Poems composed around 2300 years ago are gathered in assemblies — sangams — of poets at Madurai, preserving the Tamil words for the village's people and becoming a historian's source in verse.
