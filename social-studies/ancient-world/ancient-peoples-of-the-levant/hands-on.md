# Hands-on — The ancient peoples of the Levant

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The ancient peoples of the Levant — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The eras of ancient civilisation in the Levant, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
