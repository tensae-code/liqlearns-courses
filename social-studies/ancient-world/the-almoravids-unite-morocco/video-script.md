# Video script — The Almoravids unite Morocco

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Morocco's story as its Grade 5 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Veiled Saharan reformers ride north, take Morocco and unify the country; the guide follows the extension of their state — founding Marrakech and pushing into Spain.

The dynasty is studied through one standing monument: the Almoravid Dome (Qubbat al-Barudiyyin) in Marrakech.
