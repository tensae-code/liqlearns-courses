# The Almoravids unite Morocco

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Morocco's long story as one line: first Moroccans, Amazigh, then dynasty after dynasty. This lesson is one chapter of that line, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unifying the country and the extension of the state

Veiled Saharan reformers ride north, take Morocco and unify the country; the guide follows the extension of their state — founding Marrakech and pushing into Spain.
