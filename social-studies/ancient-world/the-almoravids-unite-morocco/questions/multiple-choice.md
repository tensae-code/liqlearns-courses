# The Almoravids unite Morocco — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Almoravids came to Morocco as — —
a) Veiled Saharan reformers  b) Roman soldiers  c) Sea traders from Tyre
**Answer: a) Veiled Saharan reformers** — Veiled Saharan reformers — who rode north.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The city the Almoravids founded is — —
a) Marrakech  b) Fez  c) Lixus
**Answer: a) Marrakech** — Marrakech — founded as their capital.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Their state extended as far as — —
a) Spain  b) Egypt  c) Persia
**Answer: a) Spain** — Spain — the extension the guide follows.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Their monument studied here is — —
a) The Almoravid Dome in Marrakech  b) El Badi Palace  c) A Marinid madrasa
**Answer: a) The Almoravid Dome in Marrakech** — The Almoravid Dome (Qubbat al-Barudiyyin) in Marrakech.
