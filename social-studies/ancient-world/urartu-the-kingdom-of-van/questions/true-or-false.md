# Urartu The Kingdom Of Van — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sarduri I raises Tushpa-Van, Ishpuini and Menua organise the kingdom, and Argishti I plants Erebuni in 782 BCE.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1.4 The founding of the Van (Urartu) kingdom and its flourishing

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1.4 The founding of the Van (Urartu) kingdom and its flourishing

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Sarduri II clashes with Tiglath-Pileser III, Rusa I fights Assyria and Cimmerians, and by the early 6th century BCE the kingdom is gone — but its lands pass to Armenian rulers.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1.5 The fall of the Van kingdom and independence

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1.5 The fall of the Van kingdom and independence

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Canals still water fields today, bronze and iron workshops arm the kingdom, and cuneiform inscriptions — read as a class source — speak for its kings.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1.6 The socio-economic and cultural life of the Van kingdom

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1.6 The socio-economic and cultural life of the Van kingdom
