# Hands-on — Urartu The Kingdom Of Van

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Urartu The Kingdom Of Van — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 1.4 The founding of the Van (Urartu) kingdom and its flourishing, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
