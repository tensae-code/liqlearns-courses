## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1.4 The founding of the Van (Urartu) kingdom and its flourishing** — Sarduri I raises Tushpa-Van, Ishpuini and Menua organise the kingdom, and Argishti I plants Erebuni in 782 BCE
2. **1.5 The fall of the Van kingdom and independence** — Sarduri II clashes with Tiglath-Pileser III, Rusa I fights Assyria and Cimmerians, and by the early 6th century BCE the kingdom is gone — but its lands pass to Armenian rulers
3. **1.6 The socio-economic and cultural life of the Van kingdom** — canals still water fields today, bronze and iron workshops arm the kingdom, and cuneiform inscriptions — read as a class source — speak for its kings
4. **Teaching guidance for Theme 1, the Highland's first civilisations and Urartu** — lesson designs turn inscriptions, the Lchashen wagon and satellite maps into evidence pupils argue from

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
