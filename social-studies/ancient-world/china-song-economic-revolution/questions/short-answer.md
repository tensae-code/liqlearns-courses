# China Song Economic Revolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Song economic development.
**Answer: early rice and the yangma transplanting horse push rice to first place — 'when Su and Hu ripen, the world is fed' — cotton spreads north, and southern silk, porcelain and shipbuilding outstrip the north (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 9: Song economic development, printed p. 41.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Liao-Song-Xia-Jin-Yuan economy and society.
**Answer: paper money, sea-going junks, coal and iron at medieval heights, and cities whose night markets outshine Europe's (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 11: Liao-Song-Xia-Jin-Yuan economy and society, printed p. 64.**
