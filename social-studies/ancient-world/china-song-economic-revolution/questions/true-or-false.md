# China Song Economic Revolution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Early rice and the yangma transplanting horse push rice to first place — 'when Su and Hu ripen, the world is fed' — cotton spreads north, and southern silk, porcelain and shipbuilding outstrip the north.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Song economic development

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Song economic development

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Paper money, sea-going junks, coal and iron at medieval heights, and cities whose night markets outshine Europe's.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Liao-Song-Xia-Jin-Yuan economy and society

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Liao-Song-Xia-Jin-Yuan economy and society
