# China Traditional Culture Essence And Character — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The essence and character of fine traditional Chinese culture.
**Answer: every spring the Western Zhou king ploughs and prays at the Altar of Land and Grain — sheji — until the altar's name becomes the word for the state itself; agriculture-first, family and harmony are read out of such rites (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 选择性必修3 文化交流与传播 (Senior High History, Selective Compulsory 3: Cultural Exchange and Dissemination)* (China), Lesson 1: The essence and character of fine traditional Chinese culture, printed p. 2.**
