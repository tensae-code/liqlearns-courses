# Vietnam Mai Thuc Loan Uprising — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** from Hoan Châu the revolt draws tens of thousands, wins Chăm-pa and Chenla support, takes Tống Bình and frees the country; Mai Thúc Loan proclaims himself emperor at Vạn An citadel before the Tang crush the rising in 722.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 16, the uprising of Mai Thúc Loan (713–722)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 16, the uprising of Mai Thúc Loan (713–722)
