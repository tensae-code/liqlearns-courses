# Vietnam Mai Thuc Loan Uprising — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 16, the uprising of Mai Thúc Loan (713–722).
**Answer: from Hoan Châu the revolt draws tens of thousands, wins Chăm-pa and Chenla support, takes Tống Bình and frees the country; Mai Thúc Loan proclaims himself emperor at Vạn An citadel before the Tang crush the rising in 722 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 16, the uprising of Mai Thúc Loan (713–722), printed p. 75.**
