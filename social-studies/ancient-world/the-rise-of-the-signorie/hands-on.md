# Hands-on — The Rise Of The Signorie

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Rise Of The Signorie — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unità 3, Cap. 9 §1 The birth of the city Signorie, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
