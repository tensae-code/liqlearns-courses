# Cyprus Archaic Age — kingdoms and conquerors — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did ordinary people live in an Archaic Cypriot kingdom?
**Answer: As farmers and craftspeople in and around a walled city ruled by a king, under foreign shadows (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three empires that conquered Cyprus in this age, in order.
**Answer: Assyrians, then Egyptians, then Persians.**
