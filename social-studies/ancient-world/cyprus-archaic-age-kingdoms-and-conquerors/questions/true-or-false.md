# Cyprus Archaic Age — kingdoms and conquerors — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Cypriot kings paid tribute to foreign conquerors.
**Answer: True** — they pay tribute to survive each new master.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cyprus had no kings of its own in the Archaic Age.
**Answer: False** — each kingdom had its own king; they paid tribute to survive.
