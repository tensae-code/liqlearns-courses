# Cyprus Archaic Age — kingdoms and conquerors — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Archaic Age Cyprus was —
a) split into kingdoms, each with its walled city and king  b) one united republic  c) empty of cities
**Answer: a) split into kingdoms, each with its walled city and king** — Cyprus is split into kingdoms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The conquerors who took Cyprus in turn were —
a) Assyrians, Egyptians and Persians  b) Romans, Greeks and Phoenicians  c) Macedonians and Ptolemies
**Answer: a) Assyrians, Egyptians and Persians** — Assyrians, Egyptians and Persians take the island in turn.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cypriot kings survived each new master by —
a) paying tribute  b) hiding in caves  c) sinking their cities
**Answer: a) paying tribute** — Cypriot kings pay tribute to survive each new master.
