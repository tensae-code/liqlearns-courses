# Tigran The Great And Armenias Sea To Sea Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tigran (95–55 BCE) builds an empire from sea to sea, founds Tigranakert, and makes Armenia Rome's great eastern rival.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 3.2 The Armenian world power: Tigran II the Great

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 3.2 The Armenian world power: Tigran II the Great

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lucullus and Pompey invade, Tigran keeps his crown but loses the empire, and Armenia becomes the prize Rome and Parthia fight over.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 3.3 The Armenian–Roman wars and the decline of Tigran's empire

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 3.3 The Armenian–Roman wars and the decline of Tigran's empire

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Teachers stage the debate over Tigran's sea-to-sea empire — how it rose, and what its fall cost.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Teaching guidance for Themes 2–3, Yervanduni and Artashesian Armenia

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն. Մեթոդական ուղեցույց. 7-րդ դասարան» (History of Armenia: Methodological Guide for the Textbook, Grade 7) — Smbat Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam M. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Teaching guidance for Themes 2–3, Yervanduni and Artashesian Armenia
