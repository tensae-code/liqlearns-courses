**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ancient Chinese political systems form and develop.
**Answer: at a birthday banquet in 213 BCE a guest praises the First Emperor for replacing lords with commanderies; another begs him to restore the old enfeoffments — Li Si wins the argument, and centralised government is fixed for two thousand years (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
