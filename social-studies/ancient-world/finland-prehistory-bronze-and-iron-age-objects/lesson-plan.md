## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Esine 9: Pronssiset silmälasisoljet (bronze spectacle brooches, c. 1800–500 BCE)** — cloak fasteners shaped like spectacles are cast in the age's new luxury metal, bronze, and can span 20 cm
2. **Esine 2: Soikea tuluskivi (oval strike-a-light, older Iron Age, found at Lapua and Kangasala)** — an oval stone struck for fire-making sparks, believed to guard harvest and fertility in ritual hands
3. **Esine 3: Pronssikattila (bronze cauldron from the Levänluhta water burial, made in the Roman world, Merovingian c. 550–800)** — a precious Roman-made cauldron ends up in an Ostrobothnian pond used as a burial place, one of only three known in the area
