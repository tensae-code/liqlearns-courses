# Finland prehistory — Bronze and Iron Age objects — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cloak fasteners shaped like spectacles are cast in the age's new luxury metal, bronze, and can span 20 cm.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Esine 9: Pronssiset silmälasisoljet (bronze spectacle brooches, c. 1800–500 BCE)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Esihistorian mysteeriesineet (student, FI/SV)* is about History lesson: reading the 1572 Carta Marina together.
**Answer: False** — it is about Esine 9: Pronssiset silmälasisoljet (bronze spectacle brooches, c. 1800–500 BCE)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** An oval stone struck for fire-making sparks, believed to guard harvest and fertility in ritual hands.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Esine 2: Soikea tuluskivi (oval strike-a-light, older Iron Age, found at Lapua and Kangasala)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Esihistorian mysteeriesineet (student, FI/SV)* is about Why the map is full of pictures: Olaus Magnus and the lost Catholic North.
**Answer: False** — it is about Esine 2: Soikea tuluskivi (oval strike-a-light, older Iron Age, found at Lapua and Kangasala)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A precious Roman-made cauldron ends up in an Ostrobothnian pond used as a burial place, one of only three known in the area.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Esine 3: Pronssikattila (bronze cauldron from the Levänluhta water burial, made in the Roman world, Merovingian c. 550–800)
