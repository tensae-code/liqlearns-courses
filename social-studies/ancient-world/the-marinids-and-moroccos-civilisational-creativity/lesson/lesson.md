# The Marinids and Morocco's civilisational creativity

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Morocco's long story as one line: first Moroccans, Amazigh, then dynasty after dynasty. This lesson is one chapter of that line, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

The guide teaches the Marinid state's founding and stages: Zenata horsemen become sultans of Morocco.

## Sub lesson 2 — Madrasas and the Mawlid — creativity still alive

Marinid creativity is studied through madrasa architecture — colleges in Fez so beautiful the dynasty is remembered in tile and cedar — and through a Marinid tradition still alive: the celebration of the Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid).
