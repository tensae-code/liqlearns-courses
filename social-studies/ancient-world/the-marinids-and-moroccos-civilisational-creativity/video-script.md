# Video script — The Marinids and Morocco's civilisational creativity

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Morocco's story as its Grade 5 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The guide teaches the Marinid state's founding and stages: Zenata horsemen become sultans of Morocco.

Marinid creativity is studied through madrasa architecture — colleges in Fez so beautiful the dynasty is remembered in tile and cedar — and through a Marinid tradition still alive: the celebration of the Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid).
