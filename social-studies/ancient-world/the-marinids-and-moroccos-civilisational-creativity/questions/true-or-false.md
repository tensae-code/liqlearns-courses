# The Marinids and Morocco's civilisational creativity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Marinids filled Fez with colleges.
**Answer: True — their madrasas fill Fez, in the guide's telling.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nothing of the Marinids survives today.
**Answer: False — the Mawlid celebration is a Marinid tradition still alive.**
