# The Marinids and Morocco's civilisational creativity — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Marinids began as — —
a) Zenata horsemen  b) Saharan reformers  c) Mountain preachers
**Answer: a) Zenata horsemen** — Zenata horsemen — who became sultans.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Marinid creativity is studied through — —
a) Madrasa architecture  b) Sugar works  c) Kasbahs
**Answer: a) Madrasa architecture** — Madrasa architecture — the colleges of Fez.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Marinid tradition still alive is — —
a) The celebration of the Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid)  b) The Almoravid Dome  c) Aedemon's revolt
**Answer: a) The celebration of the Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid)** — The celebration of the Prophet's Birthday (Mawlid).

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Marinids are remembered in — —
a) Tile and cedar  b) Sugar and gold  c) Coins only
**Answer: a) Tile and cedar** — Tile and cedar — the beauty of their madrasas.
