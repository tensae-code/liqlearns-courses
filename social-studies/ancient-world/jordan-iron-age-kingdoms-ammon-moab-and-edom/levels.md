## Sub lesson 4 — Lesson 2: Jordan in the Iron Age (1200–332 BCE)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Arab tribes out of the peninsula found three kingdoms — Edom in the red-sandstone south, whose people channel rain into dams and wells and guide caravans to the Gulf (the Nabataeans inherit their building craft); Moab, whose King Mesha defeats the Hebrews, paves roads, builds Madaba, and leaves his victories on the black basalt Mesha Stele, oldest of its kind, to his war-god Chemosh; and Ammon, ruling from Rabbath Ammon — today's Amman — under kings like Nahash I, its farmers guarding their vines from towers like Rujm al-Malfouf
