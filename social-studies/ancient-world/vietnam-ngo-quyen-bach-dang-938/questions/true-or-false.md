# Vietnam Ngo Quyen Bach Dang 938 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in 938 Ngô Quyền plants iron-tipped stakes under the Bạch Đằng river, lures the Southern Han fleet in at high tide and traps it on the falling tide; Prince Hoằng Tháo is killed and independence is secured.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 8, story of Ngô Quyền's great victory over the Southern Han

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 8, story of Ngô Quyền's great victory over the Southern Han

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the chronicle preserves his order — great iron-tipped stakes under the tide — and the battle itself: Hoằng Tháo's fleet rides the flood in, the ebb strands it on the stakes, and half the invaders drown; the victory ends the Bắc thuộc era for good.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 18, Ngô Quyền and the Bạch Đằng victory of 938

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
