# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan Ancient Nomads Economy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan Ancient Nomads Economy
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** steppe tribes commit fully to nomadic herding, the economy that defines them
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§31 The formation of cattle-breeding in Kazakhstan** — steppe tribes commit fully to nomadic herding, the economy that defines them
2. **§32 Economy of the steppe tribes** — herds on seasonal routes feed, clothe and carry whole peoples
3. **§33 Crafts and trade of cattle-breeding tribes** — smiths and traders link the nomads to farming cities in exchange
4. **§34–35 Social order and religious ideas of steppe tribes** — chiefs, clans and sky-god beliefs order nomad society
5. **2.2 The formation of nomadic cattle-breeding** — herders take to seasonal migration and the nomad way of life is born
6. **2.3 Material culture of the ancient nomads** — felt tents, horse gear and animal-style gold show a civilisation built to move
7. **2.4 The ancient nomads' worldview** — sky, sun and ancestor spirits order the nomads' unseen world
8. **§21 Formation of nomadic herding and its way of life** — seasonal migration becomes a complete civilisation on horseback
9. **§22 The nomads' contribution to world civilisation** — horsemanship, trousers and the composite bow — the nomads' gifts to the world
10. **§24 The nomads' dwelling — the felt house** — the portable felt yurt makes the whole steppe a home
11. **§25 Early nomads' beliefs and customs** — sky worship and ancestor rites bind the nomad clans
12. **§26 Early nomads' ideas about the world** — the nomads picture a three-layer world of sky, earth and underworld
13. **2.2 The rise of nomadic cattle-breeding (lesson plans)** — pupils model the seasonal migration that made nomadism work
14. **2.3 Material culture of the ancient nomads (lesson plans)** — felt, horse gear and gold objects carry the material-culture lessons
15. **2.4 The ancient nomads' worldview (lesson plans)** — sky and ancestor beliefs are explored through myth and rite
