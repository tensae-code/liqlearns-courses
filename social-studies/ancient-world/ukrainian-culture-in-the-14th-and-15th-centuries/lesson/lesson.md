# Ukrainian culture in the 14th and 15th centuries

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: no state of their own, yet the culture holds — church and monastery schools, students like Yurii Drohobych reaching Bologna and Kraków (he becomes rector at Bologna), fortress and church building from Lutsk to Kamianets. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Schools and scholars abroad

No state of their own, yet the culture holds. Church and monastery schools teach at home, and students like Yurii Drohobych reach Bologna and Kraków — Drohobych becomes rector at Bologna.

## Sub lesson 2 — Building from Lutsk to Kamianets

Fortress and church building rises from Lutsk to Kamianets. A stateless culture still builds in stone — learning and building carry the people's identity.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Ukrainian culture in the 14th and 15th centuries in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
