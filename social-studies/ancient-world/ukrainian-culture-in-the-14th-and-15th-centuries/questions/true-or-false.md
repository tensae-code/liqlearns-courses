# Ukrainian culture in the 14th and 15th centuries — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Drohobych studied and taught in Europe's universities.
**Answer: True — He reaches Bologna and Kraków and becomes rector at Bologna.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Without a state, Ukrainian culture stopped completely.
**Answer: False — The culture holds — schools, scholars and building continue.**
