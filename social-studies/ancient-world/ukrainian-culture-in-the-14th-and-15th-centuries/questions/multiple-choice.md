# Ukrainian culture in the 14th and 15th centuries — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Schools at home were run by —
a) Churches and monasteries  b) The Khanate  c) Town guilds only
**Answer: a) Churches and monasteries**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Yurii Drohobych became rector at —
a) Bologna  b) Kyiv  c) Vilnius
**Answer: a) Bologna**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Fortress and church building is named from —
a) Lutsk to Kamianets  b) Lviv to Kyiv only  c) Olbia to Theodoro
**Answer: a) Lutsk to Kamianets**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In this age, Ukrainians had —
a) No state of their own, yet their culture held  b) Their own king in Kyiv  c) A Magdeburg empire
**Answer: a) No state of their own, yet their culture held**
