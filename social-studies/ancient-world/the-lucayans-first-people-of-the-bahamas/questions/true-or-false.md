# The Lucayans — first people of The Bahamas — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a peaceful Arawak-speaking people sail from South America, settle by the sea, fish and farm cassava, sweet potato and corn, live in branch-and-grass huts and sleep in hammocks
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bahamian History: the Lucayans, first people of the islands).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** the peaceful Lucayans, skilled sailors and fishermen who live in huts, sleep in hammocks and live off the land, leave a legacy the whole world still uses — the hammock
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Lucayans and Eleutheran Adventurers: the Lucayans).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** the first arrivals are peaceful sailors and fishermen who live in huts, sleep in hammocks and live off the land in harmony with nature
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Early Settlers: the Lucayans, first people to come to the islands).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** after Columbus, group after group finds refuge on the islands — Eleutheran Adventurers (1684 in this telling), pirates, Loyalists, slaves — each changing the islands for better or worse; with Woodes Rogers an organised society begins that leads, idea by idea, to independence
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bahamian History Prior to Independence 1973 Part 1: the timeline of peoples).
