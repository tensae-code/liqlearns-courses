# The Lucayans — first people of The Bahamas — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Bahamian History: the Lucayans, first people of the islands  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Bahamian History: the Lucayans, first people of the islands** — a peaceful Arawak-speaking people sail from South America, settle by the sea, fish and farm cassava, sweet potato and corn, live in branch-and-grass huts and sleep in hammocks
