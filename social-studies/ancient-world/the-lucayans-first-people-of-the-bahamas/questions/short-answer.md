# The Lucayans — first people of The Bahamas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Bahamian History: the Lucayans, first people of the islands.
**Answer: a peaceful Arawak-speaking people sail from South America, settle by the sea, fish and farm cassava, sweet potato and corn, live in branch-and-grass huts and sleep in hammocks (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Lucayans and Eleutheran Adventurers: the Lucayans.
**Answer: the peaceful Lucayans, skilled sailors and fishermen who live in huts, sleep in hammocks and live off the land, leave a legacy the whole world still uses — the hammock (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Early Settlers: the Lucayans, first people to come to the islands.
**Answer: the first arrivals are peaceful sailors and fishermen who live in huts, sleep in hammocks and live off the land in harmony with nature (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Bahamian History Prior to Independence 1973 Part 1: the timeline of peoples.
**Answer: after Columbus, group after group finds refuge on the islands — Eleutheran Adventurers (1684 in this telling), pirates, Loyalists, slaves — each changing the islands for better or worse; with Woodes Rogers an organised society begins that leads, idea by idea, to independence (any close wording).**
