# The Lucayans — first people of The Bahamas

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Lucayans, a peaceful Arawak-speaking people who sailed from South America, settle the Bahama islands by the sea — fishing, farming cassava, sweet potato and corn, living in branch-and-grass huts and sleeping in hammocks — and leave the hammock as their gift to the world. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
