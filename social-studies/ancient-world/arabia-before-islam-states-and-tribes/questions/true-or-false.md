# Political conditions of the Arabs before Islam — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arabia before Islam is a patchwork — Kinda in Najd and the Hijaz, Quraysh in Mecca and the Yemeni tribes Aws and Khazraj in Yathrib, the Lakhmids, Nabataeans and Ghassanids in the north — squeezed between two great neighbours, Persia at al-Mada'in and Byzantium at Constantinople.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Political conditions of the Arabs before Islam

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Political conditions of the Arabs before Islam

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** while Rome and Persia fight their long war over trade and resources with the Levant and Iraq as the arena, Arab buffer states line the frontier — the Nabataeans at rock-carved Petra, Yemeni migrants who even fight the Jews settled among them; Palmyra, whose Queen Zenobia seizes Rome's weakness, declares independence and overruns the Levant, Egypt and part of Turkey; Yemeni Kinda at al-Faw guarding central Arabia until internal quarrels fell it; the Ghassanids, Rome's Christian Arab tool in the south of the Levant; and the Lakhmids at al-Hira, Persia's Arab tool on the Euphrates.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in States of the north of the Peninsula between the Birth and the Mission

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about States of the north of the Peninsula between the Birth and the Mission
