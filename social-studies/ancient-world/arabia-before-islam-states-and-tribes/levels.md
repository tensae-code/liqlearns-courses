## Sub lesson 2 — States of the north of the Peninsula between the Birth and the Mission
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — while Rome and Persia fight their long war over trade and resources with the Levant and Iraq as the arena, Arab buffer states line the frontier — the Nabataeans at rock-carved Petra, Yemeni migrants who even fight the Jews settled among them; Palmyra, whose Queen Zenobia seizes Rome's weakness, declares independence and overruns the Levant, Egypt and part of Turkey; Yemeni Kinda at al-Faw guarding central Arabia until internal quarrels fell it; the Ghassanids, Rome's Christian Arab tool in the south of the Levant; and the Lakhmids at al-Hira, Persia's Arab tool on the Euphrates
