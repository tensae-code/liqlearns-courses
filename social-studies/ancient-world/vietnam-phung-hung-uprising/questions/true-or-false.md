# Vietnam Phung Hung Uprising — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Đường Lâm hào trưởng, a strongman who could wrestle buffalo and tiger, masters his region, besieges and takes Tống Bình and rules nine years; the people later honour him as Bố Cái Đại Vương.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 16, the uprising of Phùng Hưng

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 16, the uprising of Phùng Hưng
