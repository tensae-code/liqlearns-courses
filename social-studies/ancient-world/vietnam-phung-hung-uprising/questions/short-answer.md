# Vietnam Phung Hung Uprising — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 16, the uprising of Phùng Hưng.
**Answer: the Đường Lâm hào trưởng, a strongman who could wrestle buffalo and tiger, masters his region, besieges and takes Tống Bình and rules nine years; the people later honour him as Bố Cái Đại Vương (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 16, the uprising of Phùng Hưng, printed p. 77.**
