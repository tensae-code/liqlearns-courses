# Hands-on — Vietnam Phung Hung Uprising

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Phung Hung Uprising — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 16, the uprising of Phùng Hưng, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
