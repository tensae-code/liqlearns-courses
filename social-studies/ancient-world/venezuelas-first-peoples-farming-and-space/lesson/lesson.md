# Venezuelas First Peoples Farming And Space

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Indigenous work creates Venezuela's first lived spaces; the Timotocuica people's farming is the most advanced, and their stone-and-bahareque building orders the land before any Spaniard As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
