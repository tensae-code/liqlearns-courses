# Lesson plan — Vietnam Under Northern Domination

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Under Northern Domination
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Triệu Đà annexes the country in 179 BC; châu, quận and huyện replace its order, Han officials rule down to district level after AD 43, land is seized into estates, taxes and tribute (pearls, ivory, spices) flow north, and iron and salt are monopolised
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 15, how the northern dynasties ruled Âu Lạc** — Triệu Đà annexes the country in 179 BC; châu, quận and huyện replace its order, Han officials rule down to district level after AD 43, land is seized into estates, taxes and tribute (pearls, ivory, spices) flow north, and iron and salt are monopolised
2. **Bài 15, how Âu Lạc changed under a thousand years of rule** — dykes and great rice regions spread, crafts grow (paper-making learned from China, glass, fine pottery at Luy Lâu), some Han officials are Vietnamised, local hào trưởng become an aristocracy — and the contradiction between people and regime sharpens
