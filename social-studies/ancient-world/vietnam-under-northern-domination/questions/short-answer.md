# Vietnam Under Northern Domination — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 15, how the northern dynasties ruled Âu Lạc.
**Answer: Triệu Đà annexes the country in 179 BC; châu, quận and huyện replace its order, Han officials rule down to district level after AD 43, land is seized into estates, taxes and tribute (pearls, ivory, spices) flow north, and iron and salt are monopolised (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 6 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 15, how the northern dynasties ruled Âu Lạc, printed p. 65.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 15, how Âu Lạc changed under a thousand years of rule.
**Answer: dykes and great rice regions spread, crafts grow (paper-making learned from China, glass, fine pottery at Luy Lâu), some Han officials are Vietnamised, local hào trưởng become an aristocracy — and the contradiction between people and regime sharpens (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
