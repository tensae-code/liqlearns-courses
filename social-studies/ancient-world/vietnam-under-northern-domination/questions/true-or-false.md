# Vietnam Under Northern Domination — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Triệu Đà annexes the country in 179 BC; châu, quận and huyện replace its order, Han officials rule down to district level after AD 43, land is seized into estates, taxes and tribute (pearls, ivory, spices) flow north, and iron and salt are monopolised.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 15, how the northern dynasties ruled Âu Lạc

**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 15, how the northern dynasties ruled Âu Lạc
