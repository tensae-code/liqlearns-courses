## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 15, how the northern dynasties ruled Âu Lạc
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Triệu Đà annexes the country in 179 BC; châu, quận and huyện replace its order, Han officials rule down to district level after AD 43, land is seized into estates, taxes and tribute (pearls, ivory, spices) flow north, and iron and salt are monopolised

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 15, how Âu Lạc changed under a thousand years of rule
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files.
