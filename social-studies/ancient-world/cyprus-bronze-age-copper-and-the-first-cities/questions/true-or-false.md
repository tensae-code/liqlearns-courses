# Cyprus Bronze Age — copper and the first cities — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bronze is made with copper, and Cyprus had copper of its own.
**Answer: True** — copper and bronze reach Cyprus, and its copper drew outsiders.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Bronze Age Cyprus traded only with its own villages.
**Answer: False** — ships and trade routes tie Cyprus to Egypt and the Levant.
