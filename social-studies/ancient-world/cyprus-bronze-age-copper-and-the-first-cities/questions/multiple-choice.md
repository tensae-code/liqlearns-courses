# Cyprus Bronze Age — copper and the first cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The treasure outsiders sailed to Cyprus for was —
a) copper  b) silk  c) horses
**Answer: a) copper** — the island's copper becomes the treasure outsiders sail for.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Cyprus's Bronze Age cities rose —
a) on the coast, where the trade ships landed  b) high in the mountains  c) only far inland
**Answer: a) on the coast, where the trade ships landed** — coastal cities rise where the boats land.

**3. (Sub lesson 5)** Cyprus's kingdoms dealt with the great powers abroad as —
a) partners and prizes  b) complete strangers  c) one single empire
**Answer: a) partners and prizes** — partners and prizes, says the guide's proposal.
