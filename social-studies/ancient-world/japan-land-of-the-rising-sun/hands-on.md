# Hands-on — Japan Land Of The Rising Sun

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Japan Land Of The Rising Sun — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Japan, land of the rising sun, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
