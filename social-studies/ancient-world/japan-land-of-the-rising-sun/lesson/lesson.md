# Japan Land Of The Rising Sun

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: An island civilisation takes Chinese writing and Buddhism and makes them its own As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Japan, land of the rising sun

An island civilisation takes Chinese writing and Buddhism and makes them its own

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Japan, land of the rising sun — an island civilisation takes Chinese writing and Buddhism and makes them its own. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
