# Video script — King Marko And The Last Macedonian Kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as North Macedonia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dušan's death frees the brothers Volkašin, crowned king at Prilep in 1365, and Ugleša, despot at Serres; they defeat the tsar's army at Kosovo Field yet fall together at the Maritsa in 1371; Volkašin's son Marko keeps only Pelagonia as the sultan's vassal — and dies fighting for the Ottomans at Rovine in 1395, beside Konstantin Dejanović of Velbužd, even as epic song makes him the greatest hero of the Balkans.
