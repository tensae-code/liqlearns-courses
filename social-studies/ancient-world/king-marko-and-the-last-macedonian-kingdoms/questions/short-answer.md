# King Marko And The Last Macedonian Kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 4: feudal rulers in Macedonia in the 14th century.
**Answer: Dušan's death frees the brothers Volkašin, crowned king at Prilep in 1365, and Ugleša, despot at Serres; they defeat the tsar's army at Kosovo Field yet fall together at the Maritsa in 1371; Volkašin's son Marko keeps only Pelagonia as the sultan's vassal — and dies fighting for the Ottomans at Rovine in 1395, beside Konstantin Dejanović of Velbužd, even as epic song makes him the greatest hero of the Balkans (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
