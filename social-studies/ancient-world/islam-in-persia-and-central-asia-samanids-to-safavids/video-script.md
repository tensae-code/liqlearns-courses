# Video script — Islamic civilisation in Persia and Central Asia (Unit 5 Lesson 1)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Khosrow tears the Prophet's letter and the Prophet foretells his kingdom torn; Sa'd enters al-Mada'in, Yazdegerd III is killed in 31 AH and the Sassanian state ends, then Tahirids, Saffarids, Samanids and Seljuks raise Bukhara and Samarkand into centres where Persian is written in Arabic letters and Nizam al-Mulk founds the Nizamiyya schools.
