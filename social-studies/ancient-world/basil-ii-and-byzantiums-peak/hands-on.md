# Hands-on — Basil Ii And Byzantiums Peak

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Basil Ii And Byzantiums Peak — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The international radiance of Byzantium, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
