# Umar and the great conquests — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under Umar, the state —
a) burst outward in great conquests  b) shrank to Madinah  c) stopped expanding
**Answer: a) burst outward in great conquests** — under Umar the Islamic state bursts outward

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The eastern conquests rolled over —
a) Persia's world  b) India  c) China
**Answer: a) Persia's world** — the eastern conquests roll over Persia's world

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Syria was taken from —
a) Byzantium  b) Persia  c) Egypt
**Answer: a) Byzantium** — Syria is taken from Byzantium
