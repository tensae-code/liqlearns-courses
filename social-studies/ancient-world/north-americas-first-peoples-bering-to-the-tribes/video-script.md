# Video script — Ancient civilisations in North America

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mongoloid peoples cross the Bering Strait in prehistory and become the continent's first nations — the name "Red Indians" sticking only because Columbus in 1492 thought San Salvador was India; Vikings reach Greenland in 900 CE and the Anasazi raise Pueblo Bonito by 1000 CE, but the peoples themselves live by tribal law — each tribe under a shaykh with no absolute power, its men's council choosing his successor — hunting buffalo on the plains, fishing from bark canoes, carving defensive caves in Arizona, painting their battles on leather, and worshipping the Great Spirit through sun, moon and the forces of nature.
