**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ancient civilisations in North America.
**Answer: mongoloid peoples cross the Bering Strait in prehistory and become the continent's first nations — the name "Red Indians" sticking only because Columbus in 1492 thought San Salvador was India; Vikings reach Greenland in 900 CE and the Anasazi raise Pueblo Bonito by 1000 CE, but the peoples themselves live by tribal law — each tribe under a shaykh with no absolute power, its men's council choosing his successor — hunting buffalo on the plains, fishing from bark canoes, carving defensive caves in Arizona, painting their battles on leather, and worshipping the Great Spirit through sun, moon and the forces of nature (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
