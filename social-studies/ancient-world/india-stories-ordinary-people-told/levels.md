## Sub lesson 1 — Stories told by ordinary people; the monkey king
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Jataka and Panchatantra tales are written down and carved on stupa railings — a monkey king of 80,000 followers makes his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escapes a human king, who, watching him die, mourns and honours him.
