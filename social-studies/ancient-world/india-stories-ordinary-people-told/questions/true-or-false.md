# India — stories ordinary people told — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jataka and Panchatantra tales are written down and carved on stupa railings — a monkey king of 80,000 followers makes his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escapes a human king, who, watching him die, mourns and honours him.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Stories told by ordinary people; the monkey king

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Our Pasts-I* is about finding out about the past.
**Answer: False** — it is about stories told by ordinary people; the monkey king
