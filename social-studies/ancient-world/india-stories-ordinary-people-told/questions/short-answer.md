# India — stories ordinary people told — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Stories told by ordinary people; the monkey king.
**Answer: Jataka and Panchatantra tales are written down and carved on stupa railings — a monkey king of 80,000 followers makes his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escapes a human king, who, watching him die, mourns and honours him (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.11 / Stories told by ordinary people; the monkey king, printed pp. 121–122.**
