# India — stories ordinary people told

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Jataka and Panchatantra tales ordinary people told were written down and carved on stupa railings — like the monkey king who made his own body a bridge so his troop could escape. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Stories told by ordinary people; the monkey king

Jataka and Panchatantra tales are written down and carved on stupa railings — a monkey king of 80,000 followers makes his own body the last span of a branch-bridge over the Ganga so his troop escapes a human king, who, watching him die, mourns and honours him.
