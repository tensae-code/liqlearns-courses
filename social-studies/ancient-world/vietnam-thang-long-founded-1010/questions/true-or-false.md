# Vietnam Thang Long Founded 1010 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in 1010 King Lý Thái Tổ sails from Hoa Lư, sees a golden dragon rise at Đại La, and moves his capital there as Thăng Long, "rising dragon", justified in his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 12, the founding and names of Thăng Long

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 4 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 12, the founding and names of Thăng Long

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** officials raise Lý Công Uẩn to the throne in 1009; in 1010 he moves the capital from Hoa Lư to Đại La, and children read his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital itself, quoted from the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 9, the Lý dynasty sets its capital at Thăng Long

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 9, the Lý dynasty sets its capital at Thăng Long
