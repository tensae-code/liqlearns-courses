# Lesson plan — Vietnam Thang Long Founded 1010

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Thang Long Founded 1010
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in 1010 King Lý Thái Tổ sails from Hoa Lư, sees a golden dragon rise at Đại La, and moves his capital there as Thăng Long, "rising dragon", justified in his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 12, the founding and names of Thăng Long** — in 1010 King Lý Thái Tổ sails from Hoa Lư, sees a golden dragon rise at Đại La, and moves his capital there as Thăng Long, "rising dragon", justified in his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital
2. **Bài 9, the Lý dynasty sets its capital at Thăng Long** — officials raise Lý Công Uẩn to the throne in 1009; in 1010 he moves the capital from Hoa Lư to Đại La, and children read his Edict on the Transfer of the Capital itself, quoted from the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư
