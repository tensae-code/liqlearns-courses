# Cyprus Geometric Age — the city-kingdoms are founded — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cyprus's city-kingdoms were founded in the years —
a) 1050–750 BC  b) 475–325 BC  c) 58–30 BC
**Answer: a) 1050–750 BC** — the founding of new kingdoms in Cyprus, 1050–750 BC.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The pot patterns that mark the Geometric Age are —
a) geometric patterns  b) photographs  c) alphabet letters only
**Answer: a) geometric patterns** — geometric-patterned pots mark the age.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The sea people dominating Mediterranean trade drew Cyprus into —
a) a wide web of exchange  b) total isolation  c) a land empire
**Answer: a) a wide web of exchange** — little Cyprus is drawn into a wide web of exchange.
