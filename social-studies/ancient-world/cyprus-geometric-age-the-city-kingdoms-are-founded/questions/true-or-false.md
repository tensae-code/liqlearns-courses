# Cyprus Geometric Age — the city-kingdoms are founded — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Iron arrives in Cyprus in the Geometric Age.
**Answer: True** — iron arrives, and geometric-patterned pots mark the age.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Each Cypriot kingdom's founding is explained by history alone, with no myth.
**Answer: False** — myth and history together explain where each kingdom came from.
