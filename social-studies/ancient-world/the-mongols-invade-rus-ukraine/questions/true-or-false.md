# The Mongols invade Rus-Ukraine — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kyiv fell in the first Mongol attack in 1223.
**Answer: False — The Kalka battle was 1223; Kyiv was stormed and burned in December 1240.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Golden Horde is the Ulus of Jochi.
**Answer: True — The book names the Ulus of Jochi — the Golden Horde.**
