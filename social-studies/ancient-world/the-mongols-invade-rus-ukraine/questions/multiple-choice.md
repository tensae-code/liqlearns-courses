# The Mongols invade Rus-Ukraine — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At the Kalka in 1223, Rus princes fell alongside —
a) The Polovtsians  b) The Byzantines  c) The Lithuanians
**Answer: a) The Polovtsians**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mongol leader of the return invasion was —
a) Batu  b) Roman  c) Monomakh
**Answer: a) Batu**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kyiv was stormed and burned in —
a) December 1240  b) 1223  c) 1253
**Answer: a) December 1240**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** After the conquest, the Ukrainian lands entered —
a) The Golden Horde's tribute system  b) The Lithuanian state at once  c) The Byzantine Empire
**Answer: a) The Golden Horde's tribute system**
