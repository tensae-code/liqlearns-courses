# The Mongols invade Rus-Ukraine

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Rus princes and Polovtsians fall together at the Kalka river in 1223; Batu returns in 1237–1241, Kyiv is stormed and burned in December 1240, and the Ukrainian lands are folded into the Golden Horde's tribute system. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 1223 — the Kalka

The Rus princes and the Polovtsians fall together at the Kalka river in 1223. Old enemies die side by side — and the warning is not heeded.

## Sub lesson 2 — 1240 — Kyiv stormed and burned

Batu returns in 1237–1241. Kyiv is stormed and burned in December 1240, and the Ukrainian lands are folded into the Golden Horde's tribute system — the Ulus of Jochi.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of The Mongols invade Rus-Ukraine in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
