# Macedon Conquers Greece — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §30 The conquest of Greece by Macedon.
**Answer: the quarrelling poleis exhaust each other until Philip II's phalanx wins at Chaeronea (338 BC) and Greece passes to Macedon — and to Alexander (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История. Древний мир (Istoriya. Drevny mir — History: The Ancient World), Grade 6* (Uzbekistan), §30 The conquest of Greece by Macedon, printed p. 106.**
