# Medieval Cities Trade And The Maritime Republics — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** towns wake up, merchants and artisans become new powers, trade routes reopen across medieval Europe and Venice, Genoa, Pisa and Amalfi take to the sea.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§4–6 The cities revive with new protagonists; The expansion of trade; The maritime republics

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 1, Cap. 1 §§4–6 The cities revive with new protagonists; The expansion of trade; The maritime republics

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** mills, roads and ships remake the landscape; merchants invent a new culture of credit and companies while Venice and Genoa rule the Mediterranean routes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Uomini e spazi; Innovazioni tecniche; Le grandi macchine; La viabilità; Splendori e miserie delle città; Il nuovo mondo dei mercanti; Le città marinare italiane

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
