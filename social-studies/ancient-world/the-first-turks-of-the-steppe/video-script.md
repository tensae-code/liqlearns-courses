# Video script — The first Turks of the steppe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey's Grade 9 book gives the Turks a unit of their own, and it begins with the land: the steppe, the endless grassland across Eurasia, where the first Turkish traces — the Huns among them — appear.

The life fits the land. The horse gives speed, milk and war-power. The felt tent is a house that packs up and moves with the herds. The herds are wealth that walks.

Horse, felt tent and herds: the geography of the steppe writes the Turks' whole way of life.
