# The first Turks of the steppe — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book, the Turks' way of life is read out of the geography of their land.
**Answer: True** — the steppe writes the way of life.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Steppe wealth was stored mainly in stone storehouses.
**Answer: False** — wealth walked — the herds themselves.
