# The first Turks of the steppe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first Turkish traces in the book belong to —
a) The Nile valley  b) The steppe of Eurasia  c) The islands of the sea
**Answer: b) The steppe of Eurasia** — the grassland belt.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Among the first Turkish traces the book names —
a) The Huns  b) The Romans  c) The Egyptians
**Answer: a) The Huns** — the steppe people named.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The felt tent suits the steppe because —
a) It is made of stone  b) It never moves  c) It packs up and moves with the herds
**Answer: c) It packs up and moves with the herds** — a house for a moving life.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The steppe's three answers are —
a) Horse, felt tent and herds  b) Ship, plough and mill  c) Castle, coin and code
**Answer: a) Horse, felt tent and herds** — as the book names them.
