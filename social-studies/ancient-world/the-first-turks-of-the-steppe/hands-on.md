# Hands-on — The first Turks of the steppe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Answers to the grassland

Draw the steppe: grass to the horizon, a felt tent, horses, a herd. Draw lines from each thing to the need it answers — movement, food, shelter, speed — and label each line.

## A house that walks

Design a home for a family that must move four times a year: it must pack onto animals, stand up in an hour, and keep out a steppe winter. Draw it and list its parts.

## Wealth on four legs

Write two sentences as a steppe herder explaining why his wealth walks, and two as a farmer explaining why his wealth stays in the ground. Say which wealth a drought threatens more.
