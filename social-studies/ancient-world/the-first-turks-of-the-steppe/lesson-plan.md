# Lesson plan — The first Turks of the steppe

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The first Turks of the steppe
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 4, Topics 4.1–4.2, The First Turkish Traces in Eurasia, and A Way of Life Shaped by Geography: the Huns and the steppe world; horse, felt tent and herds as answers to the grassland.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: the geography of the steppe writes the Turks' whole way of life. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the steppe as the Turks' first homeland in the book, and the Huns among their first traces in Eurasia.
2. Name the three answers to the grassland: horse, felt tent and herds.
3. Say why a felt tent suits the steppe: it packs up and moves with the herds.
4. Explain the book's beat in their own words: geography writes the way of life.
