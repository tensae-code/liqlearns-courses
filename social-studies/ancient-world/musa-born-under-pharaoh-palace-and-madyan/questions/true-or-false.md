# Prophet Musa in the heart of Egyptian civilisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Israelites Yusuf brought to Egypt sink over the centuries into a crushed labouring class under a Pharaoh who calls himself the highest lord and slaughters their sons; the doomed baby Musa is floated down the Nile in a chest, picked up at the palace itself, loved by Pharaoh's wife Asiya, and returned by God's design to his own mother's breast — raised in the palace in horsemanship and rule, he kills an Egyptian overseer by accident while defending an Israelite, flees to Madyan, waters the flocks of two shepherdesses, and marries into the house of Shu'ayb for eight to ten years of herding.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Prophet Musa in the heart of Egyptian civilisation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Prophet Musa in the heart of Egyptian civilisation
