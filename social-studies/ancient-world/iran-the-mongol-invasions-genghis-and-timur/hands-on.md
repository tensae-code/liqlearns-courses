# Hands-on — Iran — the Mongol invasions: Genghis and Timur

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iran — the Mongol invasions: Genghis and Timur — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The attack of Genghis and Timur on Iran, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
