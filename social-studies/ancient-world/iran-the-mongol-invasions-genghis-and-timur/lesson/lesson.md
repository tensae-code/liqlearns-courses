# Iran — the Mongol invasions: Genghis and Timur

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: twice in two centuries Iran is devastated by steppe conquerors — Genghis Khan's campaign annihilates the Khwarazmshah state, Timur repeats the terror As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
