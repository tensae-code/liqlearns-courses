# Jalaluddin Manguberdi Resistance to the Mongols — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Khorezmshah's son fights the Mongols for eleven years, wins at Parwan and leaps the Indus on horseback rather than surrender — the homeland's emblem of resistance.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 22. Jalaluddin Manguberdi

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 22. Jalaluddin Manguberdi
