# Gandhara — where Greece met the Buddha — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Pakistan's own north-west, around Peshawar, Swat and Taxila, a kingdom stands where empires collide — Persian satraps, Alexander's march of 327–326 BCE, Ashoka turning it Buddhist and raising stupas, Kanishka spreading the faith, and Greek sculptors carving the Buddha's face — until the White Huns burn it down around 500 CE.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 4: the Gandhara civilisation (600 BCE–5th century CE)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Studies, Class 5 (PCTB, SNC 2020)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
