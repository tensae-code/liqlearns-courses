# Video script — Gandhara — where Greece met the Buddha

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Pakistan's own north-west, Gandhara stands where empires collide — Persians, Alexander, Ashoka and Kanishka pass through, Greek sculptors carve the Buddha's face, and the White Huns burn it down.
