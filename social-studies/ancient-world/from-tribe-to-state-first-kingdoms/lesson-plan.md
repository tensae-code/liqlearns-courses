# Lesson plan — From tribe to state — the first kingdoms

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** From tribe to state — the first kingdoms
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 2, Topic 2.5, From Tribe to State: how kin groups become the first states.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: a chief's word becomes a king's law, and the state is born. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how a tribe is held together: kinship, custom and the chief's word.
2. Name what the state adds: a king, written law, officials, taxes and soldiers.
3. Say why settled towns of strangers need more than custom: rule must bind people who share no blood.
4. Explain the book's beat in their own words: a chief's word becomes a king's law.
