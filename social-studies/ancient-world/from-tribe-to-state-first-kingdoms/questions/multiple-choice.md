# From tribe to state — the first kingdoms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A tribe is held together chiefly by —
a) Written law and taxes  b) Kinship, custom and the chief's word  c) Coins and markets
**Answer: b) Kinship, custom and the chief's word** — the tribe's bonds as the book teaches them.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The state adds, above the tribe —
a) A king, written law, officials, taxes and soldiers  b) Only a bigger tribe  c) Nothing new
**Answer: a) A king, written law, officials, taxes and soldiers** — what makes a state a state.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Towns of strangers need written law because —
a) Strangers share no kinship or custom to bind them  b) Paper is cheap  c) Chiefs dislike speaking
**Answer: a) Strangers share no kinship or custom to bind them** — why custom is not enough.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's beat for the change is —
a) The king's law becomes the chief's word  b) A chief's word becomes a king's law  c) The tribe swallows the state
**Answer: b) A chief's word becomes a king's law** — the state is born.
