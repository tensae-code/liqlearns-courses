# From tribe to state — the first kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three things the state has that a tribe does not.
**Answer: Any three of: a king above the chiefs, written law, officials, taxes, soldiers (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can custom not rule a town of strangers?
**Answer: Because strangers share no kinship or common custom, so rule must be written law backed by office (any close wording).**
