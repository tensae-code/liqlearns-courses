# From tribe to state — the first kingdoms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In a tribe, the chief's word settles disputes because he is kin and his word is known.
**Answer: True** — custom and kinship carry the chief's authority.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** A state rules only people who share the same blood.
**Answer: False** — the state's law binds strangers who share no blood at all.
