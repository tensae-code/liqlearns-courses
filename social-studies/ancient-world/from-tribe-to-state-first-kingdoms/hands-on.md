# Hands-on — From tribe to state — the first kingdoms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tribe and state, two columns

Draw two columns. In the first, draw a chief among his kin; in the second, a king with officials, a law stone and soldiers. Label three things the second column has that the first does not.

## The first tax

Imagine the first storehouse tax: every farming family brings one basket in ten. Write two sentences — one from a farmer who resents it, one from the official who explains what it pays for.

## A law for strangers

Write one short law that would work for strangers in a new town — about wells, markets or boundaries — and say why a tribe would not need it written down.
