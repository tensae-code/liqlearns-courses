# Video script — From tribe to state — the first kingdoms

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How was the state born? Turkey's Grade 9 book starts with the tribe: kin held together by blood and custom, where the chief's word settles every quarrel.

Then farming, surplus and towns fill the land with strangers — and custom cannot rule strangers. Authority hardens into office: a king above the chiefs, written law above the spoken word, officials, taxes and soldiers.

A chief's word becomes a king's law — and the state is born.
