# Romanisation Of The Geto Dacians — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea VI, Lecția 4: Romanizarea geto-dacilor.
**Answer: colonists, towns of villa and vicus, and above all the legions and their settled veterans carry Latin into Dacia until the Dacians speak and live Roman (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală. Manual pentru clasa a V-a* (Moldova), Unitatea VI, Lecția 4: Romanizarea geto-dacilor, printed p. 147.**
