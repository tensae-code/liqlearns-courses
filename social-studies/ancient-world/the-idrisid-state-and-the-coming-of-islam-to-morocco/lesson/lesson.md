# The Idrisid state and the coming of Islam to Morocco

## Do it first

Before you read on, think of Morocco's long story as one line: first Moroccans, Amazigh, then dynasty after dynasty. This lesson is one chapter of that line, as Morocco's own Grade 5 guide teaches it. Say in your mind what you already know of it; the story will test it.

## Sub lesson 1 — The founding of the Idrisid state and Fez

The guide teaches the Idrisid state's founding and extent, and the spread of Islam in Morocco — and the city of Fez in Idrisid times, founded by the refugee descendant of the Prophet who founded the state.

In your mind's eye: the key scene of this lesson as the book teaches it. Name the people and the place in the picture and say what happened there.

## Sub lesson 2 — Monument study — the Qarawiyyin Mosque-University

The dynasty is studied through one standing monument: the Qarawiyyin Mosque-University in Fez, the world's enduring university-mosque.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of this lesson in your own words, keeping its story beat: a refugee descendant of the Prophet founds a state, and his successors' city builds the world's enduring university-mosque. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
