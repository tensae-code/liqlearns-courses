# Vietnam Ly Dynasty Builds The Country — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** over 200 years the Lý complete the state and encourage farming; a girl glimpsed leaning on an orchid bush at Dâu pagoda becomes Consort Ỷ Lan, regent who helps plan the resistance of 1076.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 9, the Lý dynasty builds and defends the country, with the story of Nguyên phi Ỷ Lan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 11, the founding of the Lý and the move to Thăng Long (1010)
