**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 11, the founding of the Lý and the move to Thăng Long (1010).
**Answer: the orphan Lý Công Uẩn, raised by the monk Vạn Hạnh, is enthroned in 1009; his Edict on Moving the Capital praises Đại La's dragon-coiling, tiger-crouching site, legend sees a golden dragon rise where his boat lands, and the new capital is named Thăng Long, the Rising Dragon (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 11, Lý government, law, army and economy.
**Answer: 24 provinces, the Hình thư code of 1042 (the country's first written law), an army of palace guards and farmer-soldiers ("soldiers lodged among the farmers"); the king himself ploughs the ritual field in 1038 and burns the Song brocade in store so his officials must wear Vietnamese weave (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: Buddhism is honoured above a rising Confucianism; King Lý Thái Tông dreams of the Buddha Quan Âm rising on a lotus from the earth and builds the One Pillar Pagoda (1049); chèo theatre, water puppetry and the poems Chiếu dời đô and Nam quốc sơn hà mark the age (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
