## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 9, the Lý dynasty builds and defends the country, with the story of Nguyên phi Ỷ Lan** — over 200 years the Lý complete the state and encourage farming; a girl glimpsed leaning on an orchid bush at Dâu pagoda becomes Consort Ỷ Lan, regent who helps plan the resistance of 1076
2. **Bài 11, the founding of the Lý and the move to Thăng Long (1010)** — the orphan Lý Công Uẩn, raised by the monk Vạn Hạnh, is enthroned in 1009; his Edict on Moving the Capital praises Đại La's dragon-coiling, tiger-crouching site, legend sees a golden dragon rise where his boat lands, and the new capital is named Thăng Long, the Rising Dragon
3. **Bài 11, Lý government, law, army and economy** — 24 provinces, the Hình thư code of 1042 (the country's first written law), an army of palace guards and farmer-soldiers ("soldiers lodged among the farmers"); the king himself ploughs the ritual field in 1038 and burns the Song brocade in store so his officials must wear Vietnamese weave
4. **Bài 11, Lý culture and religion** — Buddhism is honoured above a rising Confucianism; King Lý Thái Tông dreams of the Buddha Quan Âm rising on a lotus from the earth and builds the One Pillar Pagoda (1049); chèo theatre, water puppetry and the poems Chiếu dời đô and Nam quốc sơn hà mark the age
