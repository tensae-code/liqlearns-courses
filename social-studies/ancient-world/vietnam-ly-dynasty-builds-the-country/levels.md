# Vietnam Ly Dynasty Builds The Country — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 9, the Lý dynasty builds and defends the country, with the story of Nguyên phi Ỷ Lan
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — over 200 years the Lý complete the state and encourage farming; a girl glimpsed leaning on an orchid bush at Dâu pagoda becomes Consort Ỷ Lan, regent who helps plan the resistance of 1076

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 11, the founding of the Lý and the move to Thăng Long (1010)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the orphan Lý Công Uẩn, raised by the monk Vạn Hạnh, is enthroned in 1009; his Edict on Moving the Capital praises Đại La's dragon-coiling, tiger-crouching site, legend sees a golden dragon rise where his boat lands, and the new capital is named Thăng Long, the Rising Dragon

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 11, Lý government, law, army and economy
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — 24 provinces, the Hình thư code of 1042 (the country's first written law), an army of palace guards and farmer-soldiers ("soldiers lodged among the farmers"); the king himself ploughs the ritual field in 1038 and burns the Song brocade in store so his officials must wear Vietnamese weave

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 11, Lý culture and religion
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Buddhism is honoured above a rising Confucianism; King Lý Thái Tông dreams of the Buddha Quan Âm rising on a lotus from the earth and builds the One Pillar Pagoda (1049); chèo theatre, water puppetry and the poems Chiếu dời đô and Nam quốc sơn hà mark the age
