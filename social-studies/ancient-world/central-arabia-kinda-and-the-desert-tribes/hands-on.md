# Hands-on — Central Arabia — Kinda and the desert tribes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Central Arabia — Kinda and the desert tribes — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Civilisation in the centre, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
