# Medieval world — the Dark Age and feudalism — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson, with no sub lessons. Pass mark: the learner can do the lesson's outcomes — Describe the Dark Age: what fell, why (internal division, misuse of power, invasion of 476), and its bright spots.; Explain feudalism step by step: land bought and sold, gathering into few hands, the weak giving land to lords and paying tribute for protection — great estates, few owners, landless tenants.. Practice deepens inside the whole lesson: more items per question set, then the harder corners — exact dates, exact names, and the cause-and-effect links between them — until the retelling in 'Say it to your companion' runs clean from memory.
