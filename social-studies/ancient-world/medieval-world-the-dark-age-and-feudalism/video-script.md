# Video script — Medieval world — the Dark Age and feudalism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rome fell, and Europe went dark.
Cities shrank. Roads emptied.
But monks kept copying the old books.
And the weak gave their land to lords,
and paid for protection. That bargain is feudalism.
