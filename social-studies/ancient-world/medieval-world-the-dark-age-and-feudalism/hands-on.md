# Hands-on — Medieval world — the Dark Age and feudalism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The bargain acted
In pairs: one plays a lord with 'protection' (a stick as a spear), one a tenant with a paper deed. Act the exchange — deed and tribute for protection — then swap and discuss how each side feels.

## Monk's copy desk
Copy one paragraph of any book by hand, slowly and exactly, as the monks did. Time yourself: this is why copied knowledge was precious.

## Manor in a box (Grade 9)
Model a manor with paper and counters: lord's hall, church, mill, and peasants' strips in the fields. Label who controls land, who works it, who fights — then state the book's power equation in one line.
