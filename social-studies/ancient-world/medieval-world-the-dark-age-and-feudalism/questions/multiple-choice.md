# Medieval world — the Dark Age and feudalism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Dark Age followed —
a) The founding of Rome  b) The fall of Greek and Roman civilisation  c) The Qin unification  d) Minoan Crete
**Answer: b) The fall of Greek and Roman civilisation** — Cities decayed and population shrunk.
**2.** A bright spot of the age was —
a) Steam engines  b) Popes and monks copying ancient writings  c) Ocean trade empires  d) Printing presses
**Answer: b) Popes and monks copying ancient writings** — Their copies saved Greece and Rome for later ages.
**3.** Feudalism arose when —
a) Cities grew rich  b) The weak gave land to lords and paid tribute for protection  c) Kings abolished land ownership  d) Farmers bought estates
**Answer: b) The weak gave land to lords and paid tribute for protection** — Land had first gathered into few hands.
**4.** Under feudalism the land pattern was —
a) Equal small farms  b) Great estates, few landowners, landless tenants  c) State farms  d) No estates
**Answer: b) Great estates, few landowners, landless tenants** — The feudal shape, in one line.

**5.** In feudal society, power followed control of —
a) ships  b) land and people  c) coinage
**Answer: b) land and people** — Church and lords held land — and therefore power.

**6.** The feudal economy was —
a) industrial  b) agricultural, low but rising in productivity  c) based on banking
**Answer: b) agricultural, low but rising in productivity** — Farming, slowly improving.

**7.** Society was ordered in —
a) age grades  b) estates  c) clans
**Answer: b) estates** — The estates of the realm.

**8.** Knights were feudal society's —
a) judges  b) mounted soldiers  c) tax farmers
**Answer: b) mounted soldiers** — Soldiers on horseback.
**9. (Grade 11 depth)** What is feudalism?
a) the self-working estate at feudalism's base
b) the order of lords, vassals and land held for service
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: b) the order of lords, vassals and land held for service**
