# Medieval world — the Dark Age and feudalism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Name the causes of the Dark Age's fall.
**Answer:** Internal division and misuse of power, and external invasion (476) — Greek and Roman civilisation fell, cities decayed and population shrunk.
**2.** Explain feudalism in two sentences.
**Answer:** Once land could be bought and sold it gathered into few hands. People who could not defend themselves gave their land to lords and paid tribute for protection, leaving great estates, few landowners and landless tenants.
