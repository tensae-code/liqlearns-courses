# Medieval world — the Dark Age and feudalism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Germanic rulers normalised buying and selling land.
**Answer: True** — So land gathered into few hands.
**2.** Nothing at all was built or preserved in the Dark Age.
**Answer: False** — Churches and palaces were built, writings copied, knights kept order.
**3.** Tenants under feudalism owned the land they worked.
**Answer: False** — They were landless, paying tribute to the lord who held it.

**4.** The manor village is the unit's working picture of feudal life.
**Answer: True** — Hall, church and strips.

**5.** Feudal productivity was high from the start.
**Answer: False** — Low but rising.
