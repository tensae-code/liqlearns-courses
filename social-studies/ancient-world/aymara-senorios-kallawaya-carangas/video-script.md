# Video script — The Aymara señoríos — Kallawaya and Carangas

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After Tiwanaku the Aymara lordships rule the Altiplano; the Kallawaya healers keep their Machaj Juyay language, and Carangas declines only with the Spanish arrival in the 16th century.

The Aymara people and their lordships carry the story from Tiwanaku toward the Inca.
