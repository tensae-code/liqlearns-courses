# The Aymara señoríos — Kallawaya and Carangas — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Tiwanaku the Aymara lordships rule the Altiplano; the Kallawaya healers keep their Machaj Juyay language, and Carangas declines only with the Spanish arrival in the 16th century.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Los señoríos aymaras, Kallawaya y Carangas

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Texto de Aprendizaje — Ciencias Sociales, 1er Año de Educación Secundaria Comunitaria Productiva* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Los señoríos aymaras, Kallawaya y Carangas

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Aymara people and their lordships carry the story from Tiwanaku toward the Inca.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Año 1: Los Aymaras

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Compendio para Maestras y Maestros — Textos de Aprendizaje 2023–2024, Secundaria Comunitaria Productiva, Área Ciencias Sociales* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Año 1: Los Aymaras
