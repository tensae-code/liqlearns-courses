# India — the Harappan cities end

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Around 3900 years ago people walked away from the Harappan cities; writing, seals and weights fell out of use — and nobody yet knows why. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The mystery of the end

Around 3900 years ago people walk away from the cities; writing, seals and weights fall out of use, long-distance raw materials stop coming — and nobody yet knows why.
