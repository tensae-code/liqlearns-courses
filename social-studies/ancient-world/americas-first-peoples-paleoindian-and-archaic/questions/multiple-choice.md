# Americas' first peoples — Paleoindian and Archaic — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first hunters crossed into the Americas by —
a) the ice-covered Bering Strait  b) a bridge of boats  c) a warm desert road
**Answer: a) the ice-covered Bering Strait** — the crossing the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Paleoindian hunters chased —
a) mammoth and mastodon  b) horses and cattle  c) whales and seals
**Answer: a) mammoth and mastodon** — the great animals of the ice age.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Archaic period people began to —
a) plant maize, beans and chile  b) build stone castles  c) sail to Europe
**Answer: a) plant maize, beans and chile** — the settled farming that follows hunting.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Archaic people built villages and began to —
a) trade  b) write printed books  c) mine gold for Spain
**Answer: a) trade** — village life and trade.
