# Americas' first peoples — Paleoindian and Archaic — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first peoples crossed the Bering Strait when it was covered in ice.
**Answer: True** — the ice-covered crossing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Archaic people lived only by hunting and never farmed.
**Answer: False** — they settled and planted maize, beans and chile.
