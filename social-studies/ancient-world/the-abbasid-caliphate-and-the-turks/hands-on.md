# Hands-on — The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The guard at the gate

Draw Baghdad's palace gate with its Turkish guard. Write two speech bubbles: the caliph, saying why he trusts steppe soldiers; the guard captain, saying what service in Baghdad is teaching him.

## A payroll, not a battle

Write three lines showing how an empire and a people can meet without war: a hiring, a promotion, a marriage or a school. Say which line the Abbasid story follows first.

## Rising inside

Draw a staircase of five steps from 'new recruit from the steppe' to 'commander in Baghdad'. Label what each step needs: skill, trust, language, faith, favour.
