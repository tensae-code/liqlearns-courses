# The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do the Turks enter the Abbasid state?
**Answer: As soldiers — guards and commanders hired from the steppe into the caliph's service (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book call the hiring the start of a thousand-year meeting?
**Answer: Because service inside the empire brought steppe and Islam together before conversion — the empire hired the Turks first (any close wording).**
