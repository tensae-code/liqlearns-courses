# The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Baghdad was the centre of the Abbasid state.
**Answer: True** — the capital the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Turks entered the Abbasid story only as its enemies.
**Answer: False** — they entered as its soldiers, and rose inside it.
