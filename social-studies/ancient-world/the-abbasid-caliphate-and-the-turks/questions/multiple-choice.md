# The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Abbasid capital was —
a) Damascus  b) Baghdad  c) Cordoba
**Answer: b) Baghdad** — the Abbasid seat.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Turks first rose in the Abbasid state as —
a) Tax farmers only  b) Soldiers and commanders in the caliph's service  c) Sea merchants
**Answer: b) Soldiers and commanders in the caliph's service** — hired swords of the steppe.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Turkish soldiers' skills were learned —
a) On the steppe  b) In Baghdad's schools  c) At sea
**Answer: a) On the steppe** — the grassland's teaching.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's beat is that the empire —
a) Fought the Turks first  b) Ignored the Turks  c) Hired the Turks before it converted them
**Answer: c) Hired the Turks before it converted them** — a payroll before a conversion.
