# Lesson plan — The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 5, Topic 5.4, The Abbasid State and the Turks: Baghdad's caliphate leans on Turkish soldiers, and Turks rise inside it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: the empire that will convert the Turks first hires them. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the Abbasid capital: Baghdad.
2. Say how Turks first rise in the Abbasid state: as soldiers and commanders in the caliph's service.
3. Say where the Turkish soldiers' skills were learned: on the steppe.
4. Explain the book's beat in their own words: the empire that will convert the Turks first hires them.
