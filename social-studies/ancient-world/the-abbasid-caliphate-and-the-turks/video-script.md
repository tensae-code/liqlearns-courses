# Video script — The Abbasid Caliphate and the Turks

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Abbasids rule from Baghdad, the centre of a great civilisation. Turkey's Grade 9 book watches one group rise inside their state: the Turks.

The caliphs hire Turkish soldiers from the steppe — guards and commanders valued for the skills the grassland taught. Step by step, Turks rise inside the empire.

The empire that will convert the Turks first hires them: the meeting of steppe and Islam begins with a payroll, not a battle.
