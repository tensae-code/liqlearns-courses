# Video script — The Second Bulgarian Empire And Nemanjic Serbia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as North Macedonia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the brothers Petar and Asen raise Bulgaria in 1185 and Byzantium concedes the Second Empire at Tarnovo in 1187; Kaloyan takes a crown from the pope's legate and captures the Latin emperor himself; Ivan Asen II crushes Epirus at Klokotnitsa in 1230 — meanwhile Stefan Nemanja founds Serbia, his son is crowned king in 1217, Saint Sava wins an autocephalous church, Milutin takes Skopje, and Dušan — crowned tsar in Skopje in 1346 — gives his empire a law code before it shatters under Uroš V.
