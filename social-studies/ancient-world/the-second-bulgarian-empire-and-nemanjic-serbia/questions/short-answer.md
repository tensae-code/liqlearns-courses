# The Second Bulgarian Empire And Nemanjic Serbia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 4: the rise of Bulgaria and Serbia.
**Answer: the brothers Petar and Asen raise Bulgaria in 1185 and Byzantium concedes the Second Empire at Tarnovo in 1187; Kaloyan takes a crown from the pope's legate and captures the Latin emperor himself; Ivan Asen II crushes Epirus at Klokotnitsa in 1230 — meanwhile Stefan Nemanja founds Serbia, his son is crowned king in 1217, Saint Sava wins an autocephalous church, Milutin takes Skopje, and Dušan — crowned tsar in Skopje in 1346 — gives his empire a law code before it shatters under Uroš V (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
