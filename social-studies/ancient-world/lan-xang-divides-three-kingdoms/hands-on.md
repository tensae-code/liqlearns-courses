# Hands-on — Lan Xang Divides Three Kingdoms

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Lan Xang Divides Three Kingdoms — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Part II, Lesson 10: Lan Xang breaks apart and is invaded by Siamese feudalism, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
