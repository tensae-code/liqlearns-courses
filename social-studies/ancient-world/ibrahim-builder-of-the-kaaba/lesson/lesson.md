# Our master Ibrahim

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ibrahim, born in Iraq in the Babylonian age, smashes his people's idols and faces the tyrant Nimrod, is thrown into the fire and saved. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
