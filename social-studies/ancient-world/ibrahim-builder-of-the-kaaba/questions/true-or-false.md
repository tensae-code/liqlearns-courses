# Our master Ibrahim — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ibrahim, born in Iraq in the Babylonian age, smashes his people's idols and faces the tyrant Nimrod, is thrown into the fire and saved, then raises the Kaaba in Mecca with his son Ismail and founds the Hajj rites people still perform.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Our master Ibrahim

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الخامس من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (History for Grade 5, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Our master Ibrahim

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ibrahim is born in southern Iraq under the first Babylonian state, where a tyrant authority claims divinity, bans the mention of God on pain of burning and makes idolatry the state religion, and the boy — son of Azar the idol-maker — grows up untouched by it, reasons his way past star, moon and sun that set, and begins his call with his own father before facing Nimrod and the whole society.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ibrahim in Mesopotamia

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ibrahim in Mesopotamia

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** arguments exhausted, Ibrahim waits for the festival day when the temple stands empty, smashes every idol but the biggest and hangs the axe on it, answers the public trial by pointing at the chief idol until the crowd's own conscience stirs — then hardens — they build a vast pyre and hurl him in by catapult, the fire turns cool and safe, and in open debate he silences Nimrod: God brings the sun from the east, bring it from the west.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Smashing the idols: demolishing misguidance in souls

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Smashing the idols: demolishing misguidance in souls

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Ibrahim migrates with Sarah and his nephew Lot to the blessed land, settles in Palestine where al-Khalil still bears his name, sends Lot to Sodom, and is given Ismail by Hajar and then Ishaq by Sarah — two lines of prophets, Ismail's running to Muhammad; he carries Hajar and the infant Ismail to Mecca to rebuild the Kaaba on Adam's foundations, sees in a dream that he must sacrifice his son, and at the very moment of obedience God ransoms the boy with a great sacrifice, the root of Eid al-Adha's takbirs.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ibrahim in the Levant and Mecca

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *التاريخ للصف السابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 7, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ibrahim in the Levant and Mecca
