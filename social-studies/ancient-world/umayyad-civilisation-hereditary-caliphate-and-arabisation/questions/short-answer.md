# In the Umayyad state era — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: In the Umayyad state era.
**Answer: mu'awiya turns shura-rule into hereditary kingship and moves the capital to Damascus; in 41–132 AH the state Arabises its coinage and its diwans, builds the Dome of the Rock, al-Aqsa and the Umayyad Mosque, raises the first Damascus bimaristan with wards, pharmacy and music for the mentally ill, conscripts a five-part army and a great Levantine fleet, and opens chemistry with Khalid ibn Yazid and history-writing with Yemen's Ubayd ibn Sharya and Wahb ibn Munabbih (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), In the Umayyad state era, printed p. 34.**
