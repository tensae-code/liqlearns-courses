# The south Arabian kingdoms — Ma'in, Saba and Himyar — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ma'in trades from Qarnaw in the fertile Jawf; Saba builds the great dam of Marib and Queen Bilqis visits Solomon, then fades as the dam breaks and trade takes to the sea; Himyar unites Yemen from Zafar until Abyssinia and Persia invade; Yemen writes in the twenty-nine letters of the Musnad, terraces its mountains and grows rich on incense — its myrrh reaches even Egypt's temples and embalmers.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Models of the civilisations of ancient Yemen

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Prep8 T1 "Egypt and its Relations with Asia and Europe"* is about Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the southern kingdoms.
**Answer: False** — it is about Models of the civilisations of ancient Yemen

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, Saba of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states grown rich on the incense roads.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the southern kingdoms

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* is about Models of the civilisations of ancient Yemen.
**Answer: False** — it is about Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the southern kingdoms
