# The south Arabian kingdoms — Ma'in, Saba and Himyar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Models of the civilisations of ancient Yemen.
**Answer: Ma'in trades from Qarnaw in the fertile Jawf; Saba builds the great dam of Marib and Queen Bilqis visits Solomon, then fades as the dam breaks and trade takes to the sea; Himyar unites Yemen from Zafar until Abyssinia and Persia invade; Yemen writes in the twenty-nine letters of the Musnad, terraces its mountains and grows rich on incense — its myrrh reaches even Egypt's temples and embalmers. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Egypt's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Prep8 T1 "Egypt and its Relations with Asia and Europe"* (Grade 8 (Second Preparatory), Term 1), Models of the civilisations of ancient Yemen, printed p. 36.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the southern kingdoms.
**Answer: In the irrigated south the kingdoms rise in turn — Ma'in, Saba of the Ma'rib dam, and Himyar — farming states grown rich on the incense roads. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Egypt's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* (Grade 11 (Second Secondary)), Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the southern kingdoms, printed p. 9.**
