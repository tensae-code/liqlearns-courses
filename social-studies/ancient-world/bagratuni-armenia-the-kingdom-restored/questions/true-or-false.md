# Bagratuni Armenia The Kingdom Restored — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ashot Bagratuni wins recognition as king in 885, and a crowned Armenian kingdom returns after four and a half centuries.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 7.1 The establishment of the Bagratunis in Armenia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 7.1 The establishment of the Bagratunis in Armenia
