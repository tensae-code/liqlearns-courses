# Hands-on — India — hunter-gatherers on the move

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Why hunter-gatherers moved, and their stone tools, Choosing a place to live in; rock paintings; finding out about fire — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Why hunter-gatherers moved, and their stone tools, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
