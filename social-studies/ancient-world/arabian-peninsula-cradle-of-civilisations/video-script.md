# Video script — The Arabian Peninsula — cradle of civilisations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Saudi Arabia Grade 4 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Long before Islam, the Arabian Peninsula was already a cradle of civilisations. That is what Saudi Arabia's Grade 4 book teaches its children first about their land.

The peninsula is the Arabs' homeland since antiquity. It stands as a bridge between Egypt on one side and Iraq on the other. And it is the birthplace of the Arabic language.

Its early kingdoms have famous names: 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans and Kinda. They rose and vanished in the deserts — but they did not vanish without a trace.

Their ancestors wrote their history in rock inscriptions. The carved rocks are their books — and they can still be read today.
