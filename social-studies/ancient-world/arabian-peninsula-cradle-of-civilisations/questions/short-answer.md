# The Arabian Peninsula — cradle of civilisations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three early peoples or kingdoms of the peninsula.
**Answer: Any three of: 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans, Kinda (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the peninsula's early history survive, and why does that matter?
**Answer: In rock inscriptions carved by its ancestors; the carved rocks are the surviving books of peoples who left no libraries (any close wording).**
