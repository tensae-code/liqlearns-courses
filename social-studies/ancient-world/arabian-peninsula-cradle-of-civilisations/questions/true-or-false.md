# The Arabian Peninsula — cradle of civilisations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Arabic language was born in the Arabian Peninsula, as the book teaches it.
**Answer: True** — the birthplace the book claims.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The peninsula had no peoples or kingdoms before Islam.
**Answer: False** — the book names 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans and Kinda — all before Islam.
