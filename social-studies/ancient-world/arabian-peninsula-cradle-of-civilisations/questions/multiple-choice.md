# The Arabian Peninsula — cradle of civilisations — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 4 book calls the Arabian Peninsula —
a) An empty desert with no early history  b) The Arabs' homeland since antiquity and a cradle of civilisations  c) A land civilisation reached only recently
**Answer: b) The Arabs' homeland since antiquity and a cradle of civilisations** — the book's opening claim (Unit 7, Lesson 25).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of the peninsula's early kingdoms named in the book?
a) Rome  b) The Nabataeans  c) China
**Answer: b) The Nabataeans** — one of the seven names the book lists.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The peninsula stands as a bridge between —
a) Egypt and Iraq  b) India and China  c) Rome and Greece
**Answer: a) Egypt and Iraq** — the position the book gives it.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The peninsula's ancestors wrote their history in —
a) Paper libraries  b) Clay tablets only  c) Rock inscriptions
**Answer: c) Rock inscriptions** — the survival the book names.
