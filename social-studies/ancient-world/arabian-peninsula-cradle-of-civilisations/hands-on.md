# Hands-on — The Arabian Peninsula — cradle of civilisations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Read a rock
Find a smooth stone. With a pencil's side or a crayon, "carve" your name and one thing that happened to you this year onto a paper rubbing of it. Exchange stones with a partner: read their rock, and say what a stranger in a thousand years could learn from it — and what they could never know.

## Bridge between two rivers
Draw a map with Egypt on the left, Iraq on the right, and the peninsula between them. Draw a caravan crossing the bridge. Write under the map why standing between two great lands made the peninsula important — and what goods, words and ideas might cross with the caravans.

## Name the kingdoms
Write the seven names — 'Ad, Thamud, Madyan, al-Maqar, Dadan, the Nabataeans, Kinda — on seven cards. Shuffle, lay them out, and say the list from memory. Then pick one card and say one sentence about where its people lived: in this peninsula, long before Islam.
