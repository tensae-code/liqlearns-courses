# Video script — Why civilisations rise and fall

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as South Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Civilisations fall from outside (war, natural disaster) and inside (drought, disease, economic disruption, political struggle, overpopulation), and pupils test Greece, Rome and Egypt against the list.
