# Vietnam Thanh Hoang Village Tutelary Cult — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a village chooses its own god, and the choice is its history book.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chuyên đề 1 The village tutelary god (Thành hoàng): born under northern domination, the cult makes every village's guardian a mirror of its memory — Lý–Trần kings enfeoff the capital's guardian as a great king, the Lê sơ court builds state altars beside the folk gods of wind, cloud, rain and thunder, and villages enthrone national heroes (the Trưng sisters, Bà Triệu), craft founders (Bát Tràng's potters' ancestor) and land-reclaimers (Thoại Ngọc Hầu in An Giang)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Chuyên đề học tập Lịch sử 12 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Chuyên đề 1 The village tutelary god (Thành hoàng): born under northern domination, the cult makes every village's guardian a mirror of its memory — Lý–Trần kings enfeoff the capital's guardian as a great king, the Lê sơ court builds state altars beside the folk gods of wind, cloud, rain and thunder, and villages enthrone national heroes (the Trưng sisters, Bà Triệu), craft founders (Bát Tràng's potters' ancestor) and land-reclaimers (Thoại Ngọc Hầu in An Giang)
