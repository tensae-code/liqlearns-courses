# Hands-on — Vietnam Thanh Hoang Village Tutelary Cult

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Thanh Hoang Village Tutelary Cult — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Chuyên đề 1 The village tutelary god (Thành hoàng): born under northern domination, the cult makes every village's guardian a mirror of its memory — Lý–Trần kings enfeoff the capital's guardian as a great king, the Lê sơ court builds state altars beside the folk gods of wind, cloud, rain and thunder, and villages enthrone national heroes (the Trưng sisters, Bà Triệu), craft founders (Bát Tràng's potters' ancestor) and land-reclaimers (Thoại Ngọc Hầu in An Giang), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
