# Alexander the Great and Cyprus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cyprus's kings brought Alexander their —
a) fleets  b) tribute in gold only  c) temples
**Answer: a) fleets** — Cypriot kings bring their fleets to Alexander at Tyre.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cypriot kings met Alexander at —
a) Tyre  b) Athens  c) Rome
**Answer: a) Tyre** — their fleets come to him at Tyre.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** By joining Alexander, the Cypriot kings traded Persian masters for —
a) a Macedonian conqueror  b) independence  c) Phoenician rule
**Answer: a) a Macedonian conqueror** — trading Persian masters for a Macedonian conqueror.
