# Alexander the Great and Cyprus — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cypriot fleets joined Alexander at Tyre.
**Answer: True** — the kings bring their fleets to Alexander at Tyre.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Alexander freed Cyprus into independence.
**Answer: False** — Persian masters were traded for a Macedonian conqueror.
