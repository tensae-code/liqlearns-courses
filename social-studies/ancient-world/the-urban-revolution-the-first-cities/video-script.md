# Video script — The Urban Revolution — the first cities

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bangladesh book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Gordon Childe named two revolutions. The Grade 6 book teaches the second: the Urban Revolution.

Farming's surplus raised cities — and cities brought writing, science and art.

The chapter tours the worlds rivers made possible: the Indus, Egypt, Mesopotamia, China and Greece. No river, no surplus; no surplus, no city.
