# Hands-on — The Urban Revolution — the first cities

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Village or city?

Draw two columns — village and city — and sort these into them: writing, science, art, one family's field, walls, temples, a market of strangers.

## River tour

On a blank map sketch, mark the five city worlds — Indus, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Chinese, Greek — each beside its river or sea.
