# Lesson plan — The Urban Revolution — the first cities

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Urban Revolution — the first cities
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Bangladesh, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Gordon Childe's second revolution: after farming came the city — writing, science and art — and rivers and seas raised the Indus, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Chinese and Greek city worlds.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name Childe's second revolution: the Urban Revolution.
2. Name what cities brought: writing, science and art.
3. List the river-valley city worlds the chapter tours: Indus, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Chinese and Greek.
