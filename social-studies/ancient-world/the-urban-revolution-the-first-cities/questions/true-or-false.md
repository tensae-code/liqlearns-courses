# The Urban Revolution — the first cities — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In Childe's scheme, the Urban Revolution came before the farming revolution.
**Answer: False** — farming (the Neolithic Revolution) came first; the city was second.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Rivers and seas made the first city worlds possible.
**Answer: True** — the chapter tours the worlds rivers and seas made possible.
