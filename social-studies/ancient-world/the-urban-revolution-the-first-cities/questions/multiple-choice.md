# The Urban Revolution — the first cities — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Childe's 'Urban Revolution' is —
a) The first farming  b) The rise of cities with writing, science and art  c) The invention of the wheel
**Answer: b) The rise of cities with writing, science and art** — the second revolution.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Urban Revolution was Childe's —
a) First revolution  b) Second revolution, after farming  c) Only revolution
**Answer: b) Second revolution, after farming** — it follows the Neolithic Revolution.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these is a river-valley city world in the chapter's tour?
a) The Indus world  b) The Aztec world  c) The Viking world
**Answer: a) The Indus world** — one of the five toured.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Cities became possible because —
a) Kings ordered them  b) Farming's surplus fed people who did other work  c) Rivers dried up
**Answer: b) Farming's surplus fed people who did other work** — surplus first, city second.
