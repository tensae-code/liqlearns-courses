# Video script — The Great Seljuk Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Seljuks, a clan of Oghuz Turks, defeat the Ghaznavids at Dandanakan in 1040. Within a generation their empire runs from Central Asia to the Mediterranean, and Baghdad's caliph stands under Seljuk protection.

Then comes the machine: the sultan, his vizier Nizam al-Mulk, iqta land grants to support soldiers and officials — and the Nizamiyya madrasas, schools across the empire.

Nizam al-Mulk builds the machine, and the schools, of a Turkish-Islamic empire. That is what a victory needs to become an age.
