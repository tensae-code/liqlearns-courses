# Hands-on — The Great Seljuk Empire

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## From clan to empire

Draw a rough map from Central Asia to the Mediterranean and shade the Seljuk span. Mark Dandanakan and Baghdad, and write one line at each: what was won there.

## The machine, drawn

Draw the machine as boxes and arrows: sultan, vizier, iqta-holder, madrasa student. Label what each gives the one above and receives from the one below.

## Build the school

Design a Nizamiyya madrasa for your town: what is taught, who may enter, who pays. Write its three rules — then say what an empire gains by founding schools instead of only barracks.
