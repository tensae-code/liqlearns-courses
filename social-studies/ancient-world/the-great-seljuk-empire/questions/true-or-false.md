# The Great Seljuk Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the Seljuks, Baghdad's caliph stood under the sultan's protection.
**Answer: True** — the clan inherits the caliphate's protection.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book credits the empire's organisation to the sultans alone, with no vizier named.
**Answer: False** — it names Nizam al-Mulk, the vizier who builds the machine.
