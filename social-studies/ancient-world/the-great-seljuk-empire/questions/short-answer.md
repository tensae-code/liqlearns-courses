# The Great Seljuk Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell the Seljuk rise in three beats.
**Answer: An Oghuz clan; victory over the Ghaznavids at Dandanakan in 1040; an empire from Central Asia to the Mediterranean with the caliph under its protection (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the machine's parts and its schools.
**Answer: Sultan, vizier and iqta grants; the schools are the Nizamiyya madrasas (any close wording).**
