# The Great Seljuk Empire — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Seljuks were —
a) A clan of Oghuz Turks  b) A Persian dynasty  c) An Arab tribe
**Answer: a) A clan of Oghuz Turks** — the book's naming.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dandanakan (1040) was —
a) The Seljuks' defeat  b) Their founding victory over the Ghaznavids  c) A peace treaty with Rome
**Answer: b) Their founding victory over the Ghaznavids** — the empire's opening.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Iqta was —
a) A kind of madrasa  b) A land grant supporting soldiers and officials  c) A tax on Baghdad
**Answer: b) A land grant supporting soldiers and officials** — part of the machine.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Nizamiyya madrasas were —
a) Army barracks  b) Markets  c) Schools founded across the empire, tied to Nizam al-Mulk's organising work
**Answer: c) Schools founded across the empire, tied to Nizam al-Mulk's organising work** — the machine's schools.
