# Lesson plan — The Great Seljuk Empire

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Great Seljuk Empire
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a clan of Oghuz Turks defeats the Ghaznavids and inherits the caliphate's protection — and Nizam al-Mulk builds the machine, and the schools, of a Turkish-Islamic empire.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: a clan of Oghuz Turks defeats the Ghaznavids and inherits the caliphate's protection — and Nizam al-Mulk builds the machine, and the schools, of a Turkish-Islamic empire. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the Seljuks as a clan of Oghuz Turks, and Dandanakan (1040) as their founding victory over the Ghaznavids.
2. Say the empire's span: from Central Asia to the Mediterranean, with Baghdad's caliph under Seljuk protection.
3. Name the government machine's parts: sultan, vizier and iqta land grants.
4. Say what the Nizamiyya madrasas were, and whose organising work the book credits: Nizam al-Mulk's.
