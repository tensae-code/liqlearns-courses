# Lesson plan — Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the age of fragmentation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the age of fragmentation
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** even as the state splinters, its culture flowers — Nestor's chronicle, the Tale of Ihor's Campaign, graffiti scratched by ordinary Kyivans on St Sophia's walls, schools, medicine and white-stone churches in every principality.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Writing flowers in a splintering land** — Even as the state splinters, its culture flowers. Nestor's chronicle and the Tale of Ihor's Campaign are written — Rus tells its own story even while its princes fight.
2. **Ordinary people's culture** — Graffiti scratched by ordinary Kyivans covers St Sophia's walls. Schools, medicine and white-stone churches spread in every principality — culture belongs to more than princes.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two great writings of this age: Nestor's chronicle and the Tale of Ihor's Campaign.
2. Say what shows ordinary people's part in this culture: graffiti on St Sophia's walls, with schools, medicine and white-stone churches in every principality.
