# Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the age of fragmentation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: even as the state splinters, its culture flowers — Nestor's chronicle, the Tale of Ihor's Campaign, graffiti scratched by ordinary Kyivans on St Sophia's walls, schools, medicine and white-stone churches in every principality. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Writing flowers in a splintering land

Even as the state splinters, its culture flowers. Nestor's chronicle and the Tale of Ihor's Campaign are written — Rus tells its own story even while its princes fight.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ordinary people's culture

Graffiti scratched by ordinary Kyivans covers St Sophia's walls. Schools, medicine and white-stone churches spread in every principality — culture belongs to more than princes.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the age of fragmentation in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
