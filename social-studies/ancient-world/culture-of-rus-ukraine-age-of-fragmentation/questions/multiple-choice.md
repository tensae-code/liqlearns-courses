# Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the age of fragmentation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nestor is remembered for —
a) His chronicle  b) Founding Kyiv  c) The Ruska Pravda
**Answer: a) His chronicle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tale of Ihor's Campaign is —
a) A great written work of this age  b) A Mongol law  c) A Byzantine treaty
**Answer: a) A great written work of this age**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Graffiti by ordinary Kyivans is found —
a) On St Sophia's walls  b) On the Dnipro rapids  c) In Crimea only
**Answer: a) On St Sophia's walls**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** White-stone churches rose —
a) In every principality  b) Only in Kyiv  c) Nowhere in this age
**Answer: a) In every principality**
