# Culture of Rus-Ukraine — the age of fragmentation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Culture died out when the state fragmented.
**Answer: False — Even as the state splinters, its culture flowers.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Schools and medicine spread in this age.
**Answer: True — The book lists schools and medicine among this age's culture.**
