# Cambodia — the rise of Angkor — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 4 Lesson 5, The Angkor era
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Jayavarman II (802–850), back from captivity in Java, is proclaimed universal monarch on Phnom Kulen in 802 and unites the two Chenlas; Yasovarman I founds the city of Angkor in the 9th century, and temple-building kings raise Angkor Wat over a land watered by great reservoirs.

## Sub lesson 2 — History Ch. 2 Lesson 1, The Angkor Empire
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the empire runs 802–1432 through six centuries of temple kings: Jayavarman II (802–850), Indravarman I (877–889; Bakong and a 3,000 × 8,000 m reservoir), Yasovarman I (889–900; Bakheng and the great eastern baray).

## Sub lesson 3 — History Ch. 2 Lesson 4, The kings of Angkor
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the reign-by-reign account runs to Indravarman II (944–968), Jayavarman V (968–1001), then Suryavarman I (1010–1050) and Udayadityavarman II (1050–1066), builder of the Baphuon and the 8 × 2.2 km western reservoir.
