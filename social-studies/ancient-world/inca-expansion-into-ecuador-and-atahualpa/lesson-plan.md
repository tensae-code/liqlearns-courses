**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Incas push north into Ecuador, Atahualpa wins the civil war against his brother Huáscar, and the Tahuantinsuyo he inherits is already split when the Spaniards land
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.
