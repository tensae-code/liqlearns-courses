# India — ancient science and medicine

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ancient India gave the world Aryabhata's astronomy, the symbol for zero, and Ayurveda — Charaka's medicine and Sushruta's surgery. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Writing books on science; zero; Ayurveda

Aryabhata's Aryabhatiyam explains day and night by the earth's own rotation and gives eclipses a scientific cause and a near-modern value for a circle's circumference; Indian mathematicians invent the symbol for zero, which travels via the Arabs to Europe and the world; Charaka and Sushruta found Ayurveda's medicine and surgery in their Samhitas.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Writing books on science; zero; Ayurveda. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
