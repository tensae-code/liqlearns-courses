# India — ancient science and medicine — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Writing books on science; zero; Ayurveda.
**Answer: Aryabhata's Aryabhatiyam explains day and night by the earth's own rotation and gives eclipses a scientific cause and a near-modern value for a circle's circumference; Indian mathematicians invent the symbol for zero, which travels via the Arabs to Europe and the world; Charaka and Sushruta found Ayurveda's medicine and surgery in their Samhitas (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In India's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Our Pasts-I* (Grade 6), Ch.11 / Writing books on science; zero; Ayurveda, printed pp. 122–123.**
