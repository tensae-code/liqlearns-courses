# Video script — India — ancient science and medicine

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as India's own book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Aryabhata's Aryabhatiyam explains day and night by the earth's own rotation and gives eclipses a scientific cause and a near-modern value for a circle's circumference; Indian mathematicians invent the symbol for zero, which travels via the Arabs to Europe and the world; Charaka and Sushruta found Ayurveda's medicine and surgery in their Samhitas.
