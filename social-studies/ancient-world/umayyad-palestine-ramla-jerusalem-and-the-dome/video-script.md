# Video script — Umayyad Palestine — Ramla, Jerusalem and the Dome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Palestine's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Muawiya restores Jerusalem's walls, the Umayyads rebuild Ascalon, expand Caesarea and Acre, and raise the Dome of the Rock; Sulayman ibn Abd al-Malik founds Ramla, the only city Muslims built from nothing in Palestine, and Jerusalem's wall gets its last rebuilding under the Ottoman sultan.
