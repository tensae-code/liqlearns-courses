# Papua New Guinea — early settlers of the local community — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who lived in this place first, how they lived, and how the community came to be — the class collects the settlement story from past records and stories.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Early settlers

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Science Teacher Guide — Primary Grade 4, Standards Based* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Early settlers

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Houses, dress, transport, work and leisure of PNG's early settlers are rebuilt from photographs and books, then explained — why clothes, shelters and work took the shape they did.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lifestyle of the First People / Food / Shelter / Clothing
