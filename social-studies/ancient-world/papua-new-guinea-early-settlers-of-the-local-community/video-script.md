# Video script — Papua New Guinea — early settlers of the local community

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Who lived in this place first, how they lived, and how the community came to be — the class collects the settlement story from past records and stories.

Houses, dress, transport, work and leisure of PNG's early settlers are rebuilt from photographs and books, then explained — why clothes, shelters and work took the shape they did.

The first people survive by what they grow, build and wear, each fitted to its environment — houses against sickness, enemies and wild animals; children draw the foods, shelters and clothes and compare them with today's.
