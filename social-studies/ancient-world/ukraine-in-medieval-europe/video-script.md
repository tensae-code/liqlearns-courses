# Video script — Ukraine in medieval Europe

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Ukraine's *Історія України* (Grade 7) tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A year of history ends with one question: is Ukraine the gates of Europe?



Test the image against everything — Kyiv, the baptism, the crowns, the Khanate.



Then travel it again, stamp by stamp, to the end of the 1400s.
