# Caribbean first peoples — fishers and farmers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Antillean world of fisher-gatherers and farmer-herders  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Antillean world of fisher-gatherers and farmer-herders** — in the Antilles, sea-going fisher-gatherers and pottery-making farming villagers meet — their ceramics carry the evidence of two worlds touching.

**Answer: a) Séance 6, Le monde antillais des pêcheurs-cueilleurs et des agriculteurs-éleveurs** — the guide's own heading for this part.
