# Lesson plan — Caribbean first peoples — fishers and farmers

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** Caribbean first peoples — fishers and farmers
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Haiti, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In the Antilles, sea-going fisher-gatherers and pottery-making farming villagers meet — their ceramics carry the evidence of two worlds touching.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
