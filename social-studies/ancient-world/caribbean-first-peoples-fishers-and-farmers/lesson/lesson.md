# Caribbean first peoples — fishers and farmers

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In the Antilles, sea-going fisher-gatherers and pottery-making farming villagers meet — their ceramics carry the evidence of two worlds touching. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Antillean world of fisher-gatherers and farmer-herders

In the antilles, sea-going fisher-gatherers and pottery-making farming villagers meet — their ceramics carry the evidence of two worlds touching (Séance 6, Le monde antillais des pêcheurs-cueilleurs et des agriculteurs-éleveurs)

In your mind's eye: an Antillean fisher-gatherer camp beside a farming village. Say what evidence shows the two worlds met.
