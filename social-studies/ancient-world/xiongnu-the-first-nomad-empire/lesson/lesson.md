# The Xiongnu — the first nomad empire

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in 209 BC Modu unites the steppe tribes into the Xiongnu empire, the first great nomad state, which treats and wars with Han China as an equal. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 5.1 The Xiongnu empire, the nomads' first state

in 209 BC Modu unites the steppe tribes into the Xiongnu empire, the first great nomad state, which treats and wars with Han China as an equal

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 2.1 The Xiongnu empire

Modu's Xiongnu (209 BC) organises the steppe into a decimal military state, builds towns and crafts, and faces Han China as an equal empire

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
