# The Crusades and the Latin East

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Bernard of Clairvaux preaches a crusade, and knights sail east to found fragile Latin states in the East. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bernard preaches a crusade

Bernard preaches a crusade and knights sail east to found fragile Latin states — France's chapter names Bernard de Clairvaux as the voice that sends them.

[Picture slot — a picture of Bernard preaching to kneeling knights, with ships waiting to sail east — the student says who preached, who sailed and what they founded]

## Sub lesson 2 — Fragile Latin states in the East

The knights who sail east found Latin states — real, but fragile: new states planted far from home in the East they had come to claim.
