# The Crusades and the Latin East — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who preaches the crusade in France's chapter?
a) Bernard de Clairvaux  b) Augustus  c) Colbert
**Answer: a) Bernard de Clairvaux** — Bernard de Clairvaux.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After the preaching, the knights —
a) Stay home  b) Sail east  c) Sail west to America
**Answer: b) Sail east** — knights sail east.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the East the knights founded —
a) Latin states  b) A new Roman empire in Britain  c) No states at all
**Answer: a) Latin states** — they found Latin states.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Those Latin states are described as —
a) Unbreakable  b) Fragile  c) Imaginary
**Answer: b) Fragile** — fragile Latin states, in the beat's word.
