# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk the recipe
On paper, draw a river. Add fields, then houses, then a town wall, then a tablet. Label each step of the recipe as you draw it — you have just rebuilt the birth of civilisation.

## Two rivers from memory
Without looking, draw two roughly parallel wavy lines and place Sumer, Akkad and Assyria. Check against the lesson picture. Repeat until the order runs clean.
