# Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the five common elements of the first civilisations.
**Answer:** A well-organised state, religious belief, towns, government and a writing system (teacher guide — evidence).
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List Mesopotamia's society, from the lesson.
**Answer:** Farmers, priests, soldiers, craftsmen, merchants, employees and slaves.
