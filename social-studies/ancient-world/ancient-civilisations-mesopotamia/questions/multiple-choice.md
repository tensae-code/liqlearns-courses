# Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Civilisation began when farming and domestication produced —
a) Wandering  b) Permanent settlement  c) Sea trade  d) War
**Answer: b) Permanent settlement** — Settled farming is the recipe's first turn (teacher guide — evidence).
**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first civilisations formed on river banks because rivers gave —
a) Gold only  b) Steady water, fertile land and transport  c) Stone walls  d) Horses
**Answer: b) Steady water, fertile land and transport** — The three river gifts (teacher guide — evidence).
**3. (Sub lesson 1)** A lugal was —
a) A Greek city-state  b) A Roman official  c) The absolute ruler of a Sumerian city-state  d) A common citizen
**Answer: c) The absolute ruler of a Sumerian city-state** — From the guide's working glossary (teacher guide — evidence).
**4. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Mesopotamia' means —
a) Land of kings  b) The land between the rivers  c) The fertile desert  d) City of writing
**Answer: b) The land between the rivers** — A Greek name for the Euphrates–Tigris land.
**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Civilisation began in Mesopotamia about —
a) 500 BC  b) 1500 BC  c) 3500 BC  d) 753 BC
**Answer: c) 3500 BC** — The book's date for the beginning.
**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The Assyrians lived in Mesopotamia's —
a) South-east  b) Centre  c) North  d) Islands
**Answer: c) North** — Sumer south-east, Akkad centre/north-west, Assyria north.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** What is cuneiform?
a) justice published on stone for all to read
b) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
c) Mesopotamia's wedge writing, from accounts to law
**Answer: c) Mesopotamia's wedge writing, from accounts to law**
