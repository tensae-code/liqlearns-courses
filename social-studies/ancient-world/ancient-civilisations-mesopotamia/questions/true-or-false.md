# Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Writing is one of the common elements of the first civilisations.
**Answer: True** — State, religion, towns, government and writing (teacher guide — evidence).
**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mesopotamia was always one single united state.
**Answer: False** — Four civilisations rose there in turn; it was never one single state.
**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mesopotamia's government was royal, religious and absolute.
**Answer: True** — The king ruled, backed by religion, with absolute power.
