# Video script — Ancient civilisations — Mesopotamia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Between two rivers, civilisation began.
The Euphrates. The Tigris.
First the Sumerians. Then Akkad. Then Babylon. Then Assyria.
Never one state — four in turn, on the same rich ground.
Kings ruled. Priests prayed. Farmers fed them all.
It started about 3500 BC. Remember the rivers.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Mesopotamia in depth — cities, cuneiform and the law code. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Between Tigris and Euphrates the Grade 11 book slows down: Sumerian cities inventing the urban template; cuneiform turning accounts into literature and law; Hammurabi's code publishing justice on stone for all to read; and then the empires in succession — Babylonian and Assyrian — inheriting the valley's cities, scripts and ambitions. The names to hold: cuneiform, Hammurabi's law code.
