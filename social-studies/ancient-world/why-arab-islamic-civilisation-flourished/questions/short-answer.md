# Causes of the flourishing of the Arab Islamic civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Causes of the flourishing of the Arab Islamic civilisation.
**Answer: the civilisation flowers because it refuses to close in on itself: it takes trade, dams, irrigation and poetry from the old Arab civilisations, administration and architecture from Persia, mathematics and medicine from India, philosophy from Greece, law and road-building from Rome, absorbs the conquered lands' sciences, criticises them and adds its own creations — and lets Muslim, Christian and Jew live and create side by side in tolerance and coexistence (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), Causes of the flourishing of the Arab Islamic civilisation, printed p. 15.**
