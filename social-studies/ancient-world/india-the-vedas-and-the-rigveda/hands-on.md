# Hands-on — India — the Vedas and the Rigveda

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — One of the oldest books in the world — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, One of the oldest books in the world, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
