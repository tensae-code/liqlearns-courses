# India — the Vedas and the Rigveda

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns were composed about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were written down. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — One of the oldest books in the world

The Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma are composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were ever written or printed.
