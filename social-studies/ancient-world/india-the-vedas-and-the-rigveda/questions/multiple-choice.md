Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) One of the oldest books in the world  b) Writing in China  c) Upanishads; the wise beggar; Panini

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) The Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma are composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were ever written or printed  b) Around 3500 years ago the Chinese write on animal bones — among the first writing anywhere, a parallel beginning to the subcontinent's book story  c) Buddha and Mahavira hold that true knowledge needs leaving home, so monks and nuns live simply under the Vinaya Pitaka's rules, begging at fixed hours
**Answer: a)** — the Rigveda's thousand-plus hymns to Agni, Indra and Soma are composed by sages about 3500 years ago and memorised syllable by syllable for centuries — heard and recited long before they were ever written or printed
