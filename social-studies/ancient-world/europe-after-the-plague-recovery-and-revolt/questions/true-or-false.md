# Europe After The Plague Recovery And Revolt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** survivors inherit land and demand wages; Florentine wool-workers rise in the Ciompi revolt (1378), banks crash, and a new image of death haunts art and faith.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§4–6 The economic transformations; New social tensions in countryside and cities; Cultural life during the crisis

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 3, Cap. 7 §§4–6 The economic transformations; New social tensions in countryside and cities; Cultural life during the crisis

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Florence's wool-workers seize the city in 1378; across Europe the poor, taxed for wars, turn revolt into the crisis century's second language.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Le rivolte (with Eventi: La rivolta dei Ciompi)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Le rivolte (with Eventi: La rivolta dei Ciompi)
