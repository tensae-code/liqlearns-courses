# Video script — Egypt — Alexander, the Ptolemies and Rome

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Alexander is welcomed as a liberator and founds Alexandria; his general Ptolemy's Greek dynasty rules Egypt for three centuries, until Rome takes her for her wheat and taxes her into collapse.
