# Lesson plan — India — first farmers and herders

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** India — first farmers and herders
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (India, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** India's first farmers stored grain in pots, baskets and pits, kept animals as a living store of milk and meat, dug pit-houses at Burzahom, and buried their dead at Mehrgarh with goats for the next world.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Storing grain and "storing" animals; finding out about the first farmers (Grade 6)** — surplus grain forces invention — clay pots, woven baskets, storage pits
2. **Towards a settled life (Grade 6)** — at Burzahom in Kashmir people dig pit-houses with steps and cook indoors or out by the weather
3. **A closer look — Living and dying in Mehrgarh (Grade 6)** — in one of the earliest villages, by the Bolan Pass route to Iran, square houses store the barley and wheat people first grew here
