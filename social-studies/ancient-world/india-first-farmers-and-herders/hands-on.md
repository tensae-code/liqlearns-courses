# Hands-on — India — first farmers and herders

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Storing grain and "storing" animals; finding out about the first farmers, Towards a settled life, A closer look — Living and dying in Mehrgarh — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Storing grain and "storing" animals; finding out about the first farmers, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
