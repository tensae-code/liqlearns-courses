# Zeta under the Crnojevići and the Ottoman advance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Crnojevići rule a shrinking mountain state while Venice eats its coast and the Ottomans close in.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.4.2 Zeta (Montenegro) in the age of the Crnojevići: Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ИСТОРИЈА за седми разред основне школе (Istorija 7)* is about The Greco-Persian Wars.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.4.2 Zeta (Montenegro) in the age of the Crnojevići: Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast
