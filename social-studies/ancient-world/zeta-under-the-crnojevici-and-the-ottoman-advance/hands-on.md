# Hands-on — Zeta under the Crnojevići and the Ottoman advance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Zeta under the Crnojevići and the Ottoman advance — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch.4.2 Zeta (Montenegro) in the age of the Crnojevići: Venice seizes Ulcinj, Budva, Bar and Drivast and masters the whole Zeta coast; the Serbian despot Stefan Lazarević pushes the Venetians back from Bar and Drivast, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
