# Islam — from the Prophet's death to Karbala — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** succession is disputed from the first day; within fifty years Husayn is killed at Karbala, and the martyrdom becomes the axis of Shi'a memory.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in From the Prophet's death to the uprising of Karbala (Nineveh)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 8 Social Studies* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about From the Prophet's death to the uprising of Karbala (Nineveh)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a pilgrimage trip frames the Karbala story itself — Husayn's stand and martyrdom retold for ten-year-olds at the shrine's door.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Journey to Karbala (1) and (2)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 5 Social Studies* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Journey to Karbala (1) and (2)
