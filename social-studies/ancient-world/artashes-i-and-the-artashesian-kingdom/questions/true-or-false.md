# Artashes I And The Artashesian Kingdom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Artashes (189–160 BCE) reunites Armenian lands, founds Artashat on Hannibal's advice in the story Plutarch tells, and reforms land and army.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 3.1 The formation of Artashesian Armenia: Artashes I

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 3.1 The formation of Artashesian Armenia: Artashes I

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Artavasdes II plays Rome against Parthia, is seized by Mark Antony, and the dynasty fades as foreign kings are imposed.
