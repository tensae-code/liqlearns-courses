# Video script — Marathon and the Olympic Games

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A runner's road from Marathon in 490 BCE gives every later marathon its name and distance, and the games of Olympia in 776 BCE sleep for fifteen centuries and wake again in 1896 as the modern Olympics.
