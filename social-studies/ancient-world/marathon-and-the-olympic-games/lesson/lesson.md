# Marathon and the Olympic Games

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A runner's road from Marathon in 490 BCE gives every later marathon its name and distance, and the games of Olympia in 776 BCE sleep for fifteen centuries and wake again in 1896 as the modern Olympics. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Marathon and the Olympic Games

When Persia lands at Marathon in 490 BCE, Pheidippides runs the road to Athens with the news and gives every later marathon its name and distance; and at Olympia the Greeks' games of 776 BCE sleep for fifteen centuries and wake again in 1896 as the modern Olympics.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
