# Medieval Nepal — Baise, Chaubise and Sen kingdoms

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: pupil groups research and present: the Baise states west of the Kali Gandaki and the Chaubise in the Gandak region, born as the Sinja and valley kingdoms weakened and local chiefs declared themselves kings (Kaski's Sinja chiefs ruling some 300 years); and the Sen kingdom, founded when the Muslim-invasion refugee Jog Sen's line under Rudra Sen made Palpa a state in 1550 BS, split by Mukunda Sen among his sons and later broken into Makwanpur, Chaudandi and Vijaypur As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
