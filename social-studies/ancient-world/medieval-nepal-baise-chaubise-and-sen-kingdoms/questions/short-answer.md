# Medieval Nepal — Baise, Chaubise and Sen kingdoms — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Medieval Nepal — Baise, Chaubise and Sen Kingdoms.
**Answer: pupil groups research and present: the Baise states west of the Kali Gandaki and the Chaubise in the Gandak region, born as the Sinja and valley kingdoms weakened and local chiefs declared themselves kings (Kaski's Sinja chiefs ruling some 300 years); and the Sen kingdom, founded when the Muslim-invasion refugee Jog Sen's line under Rudra Sen made Palpa a state in 1550 BS, split by Mukunda Sen among his sons and later broken into Makwanpur, Chaudandi and Vijaypur (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 7* (Grade 7), Unit 5 Lesson 2: Medieval Nepal — Baise, Chaubise and Sen Kingdoms, printed p. 88.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The Rise and Fall of the Small Kingdoms in the Medieval Period.
**Answer: beyond the valley, the Baise and Chaubise principalities and the Sen kingdoms of the hills and Terai rise, compete and weaken — the divided map that unification would later gather into one Nepal (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Nepal's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Jibanopayogi Shiksha, Class 12* (Grade 12), Unit 6 Lesson 5: The Rise and Fall of the Small Kingdoms in the Medieval Period, printed p. 169.**
