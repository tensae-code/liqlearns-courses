# India — Vedic battles, cattle and rajas

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Vedic prayers beg for cattle, sons and horses; battles are fought for herds, pasture, water and captives, and assemblies of men choose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Cattle, horses and chariots

Prayers beg for cattle, sons and horses; battles are fought for herds, pasture, water and captives; the spoils feed great fire-sacrifices, and assemblies of men choose brave warriors as rajas — chiefs with no capitals, palaces, armies or taxes.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
