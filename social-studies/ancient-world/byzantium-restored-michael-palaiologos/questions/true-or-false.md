# Byzantium Restored Michael Palaiologos — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** from the Nicaean exile state, Michael's general slips into Constantinople by night in 1261 and the empire is restored — a shadow of itself.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 31 The recovery of the City by Michael VIII Palaiologos

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Ιστορία Ε΄ Δημοτικού — Στα Βυζαντινά Χρόνια (History, Grade 5 Primary: In Byzantine Times)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 31 The recovery of the City by Michael VIII Palaiologos

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** exercises retell the night entry of 1261 that restored the empire.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 31 Recovery of the City exercises

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Ιστορία Ε΄ Δημοτικού — Στα Βυζαντινά Χρόνια, Τετράδιο Εργασιών (History Grade 5 workbook)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 31 Recovery of the City exercises

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** a restored but shrunken empire survives by diplomacy while its fleet and coin decay.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Michael VIII and Andronikos II Palaiologos

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Ιστορία του Μεσαιωνικού και του Νεότερου Κόσμου 565–1815 (History of the Medieval and Modern World), Β' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Dimitroukas, Thoukydidis P. Ioannou & Kostas Baroutas* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Michael VIII and Andronikos II Palaiologos
