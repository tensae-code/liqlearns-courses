# Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text — the Prophet approving it in his dialogue with Mu'adh, whom he was sending to Yemen — powers five centuries of scientific renaissance and the experimental method Europe later borrows; the striving mujtahid earns two rewards if right and one if wrong, but when ijtihad weakens and its gate closes in the late Mamluk and Ottoman ages, superstitious thought spreads, Arab Muslim society closes in on itself, and the civilisation's torch dies.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation
