# Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation.
**Answer: ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text — the Prophet approving it in his dialogue with Mu'adh, whom he was sending to Yemen — powers five centuries of scientific renaissance and the experimental method Europe later borrows; the striving mujtahid earns two rewards if right and one if wrong, but when ijtihad weakens and its gate closes in the late Mamluk and Ottoman ages, superstitious thought spreads, Arab Muslim society closes in on itself, and the civilisation's torch dies (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ — حضارات العالم العربي الإسلامي — للصف الثاني الثانوي — الجزء الأول (History: Civilisations of the Arab Islamic World, for the Second Secondary Grade, Part 1)* (Yemen), Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation, printed p. 103.**
