# Video script — Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text — the Prophet approving it in his dialogue with Mu'adh, whom he was sending to Yemen — powers five centuries of scientific renaissance and the experimental method Europe later borrows; the striving mujtahid earns two rewards if right and one if wrong, but when ijtihad weakens and its gate closes in the late Mamluk and Ottoman ages, superstitious thought spreads, Arab Muslim society closes in on itself, and the civilisation's torch dies.
