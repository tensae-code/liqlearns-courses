## Sub lesson 1 — Ijtihad in the Arab Islamic civilisation
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — ijtihad, exerting the mind to derive rulings where Qur'an and Sunna give no text — the Prophet approving it in his dialogue with Mu'adh, whom he was sending to Yemen — powers five centuries of scientific renaissance and the experimental method Europe later borrows; the striving mujtahid earns two rewards if right and one if wrong, but when ijtihad weakens and its gate closes in the late Mamluk and Ottoman ages, superstitious thought spreads, Arab Muslim society closes in on itself, and the civilisation's torch dies
