# Lesson plan — The rise of Islam — the Prophet Muhammad and Medina

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The rise of Islam — the Prophet Muhammad and Medina
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Egypt, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Prophet's call begins in Mecca, survives persecution by the Hijra, and builds the first Islamic state at Medina on the Medina Charter.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Arabian Peninsula after the appearance of Islam** — The Prophet's upbringing and mission in Mecca, and the hardships he and his followers face as Quraysh turns on the new call — the ground the next lesson's escape is built on
2. **The Hijra and the universality of the call** — Persecution drives the Muslims to leave their wealth and homes behind; Quraysh's leaders plot in Dar al-Nadwa to kill the Prophet, but the escape is planned like a campaign — Abu Bakr readies two camels, the two men hide three days in the cave of Thawr and take a desert guide down a road nobody uses; Yathrib receives them with joy, and there the first Islamic state is built on the Medina Charter before the call is carried to the whole world
3. **The appearance of Islam** — The call begins in Mecca and a new community forms around the Prophet — the second unit's story opens here
4. **Pillars of building the Islamic state, and the dangers it faced in Medina** — In Medina the state is built on firm pillars — and at once beset by dangers from inside and outside, which the young state survives

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
