# Hands-on — The rise of Islam — the Prophet Muhammad and Medina

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The rise of Islam — the Prophet Muhammad and Medina — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Arabian Peninsula after the appearance of Islam, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
