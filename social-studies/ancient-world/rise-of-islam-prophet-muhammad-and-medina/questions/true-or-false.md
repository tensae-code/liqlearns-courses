# The rise of Islam — the Prophet Muhammad and Medina — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Prophet's upbringing and mission in Mecca, and the hardships he and his followers face as Quraysh turns on the new call — the ground the next lesson's escape is built on.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Arabian Peninsula after the appearance of Islam

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Prim6 T1 "The Arab World — a Homeland that Gathers Us"* is about The Hijra and the universality of the call.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Arabian Peninsula after the appearance of Islam

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Persecution drives the Muslims to leave their wealth and homes behind; Quraysh's leaders plot in Dar al-Nadwa to kill the Prophet, but the escape is planned like a campaign — Abu Bakr readies two camels, the two men hide three days in the cave of Thawr and take a desert guide down a road nobody uses; Yathrib receives them with joy, and there the first Islamic state is built on the Medina Charter before the call is carried to the whole world.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Hijra and the universality of the call

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Prim6 T1 "The Arab World — a Homeland that Gathers Us"* is about The appearance of Islam.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Hijra and the universality of the call

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The call begins in Mecca and a new community forms around the Prophet — the second unit's story opens here.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The appearance of Islam

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* is about Pillars of building the Islamic state, and the dangers it faced in Medina.
**Answer: False** — it is about The appearance of Islam

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In Medina the state is built on firm pillars — and at once beset by dangers from inside and outside, which the young state survives.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Pillars of building the Islamic state, and the dangers it faced in Medina

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* is about The Arabian Peninsula after the appearance of Islam.
**Answer: False** — it is about Pillars of building the Islamic state, and the dangers it faced in Medina
