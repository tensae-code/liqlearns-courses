# Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran.
**Answer: in a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl, Maryam, who is raised in seclusion in the sanctuary under her uncle Zakariya's care, fed with out-of-season fruit; seeing her devotion, the aged Zakariya prays in that very place for a child and is given Yahya, who takes the Book firmly from childhood — the first reformer of his age (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran, printed p. 98.**
