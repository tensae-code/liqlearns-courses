# Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl, Maryam, who is raised in seclusion in the sanctuary under her uncle Zakariya's care, fed with out-of-season fruit; seeing her devotion, the aged Zakariya prays in that very place for a child and is given Yahya, who takes the Book firmly from childhood — the first reformer of his age.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ للصف الثامن من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (History for Grade 8, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran
