# Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl, Maryam. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran

in a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl, Maryam, who is raised in seclusion in the sanctuary under her uncle Zakariya's care, fed with out-of-season fruit; seeing her devotion, the aged Zakariya prays in that very place for a child and is given Yahya, who takes the Book firmly from childhood — the first reformer of his age

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran — in a Levant ground between Roman occupation and a distorted religion, Hannah vows her unborn child to the service of al-Aqsa and bears a girl, Maryam, who is raised in seclusion in the sanctuary under her uncle Zakariya's care, fed with out-of-season fruit; seeing her devotion, the aged Zakariya prays in that very place for a child and is given Yahya, who takes the Book firmly from childhood — the first reformer of his age. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
