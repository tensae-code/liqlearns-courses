# Hands-on — Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Zakariya, Yahya and Maryam bint Imran, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
