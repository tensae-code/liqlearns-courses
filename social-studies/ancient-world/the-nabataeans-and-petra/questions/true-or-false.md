# The Nabataeans and Petra — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arab Nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 BC) at Petra where the Iraq–Yemen–Syria–Egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as Rhodes, Delos and Rome, and carve theatre, monastery and the Daughter's Palace.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.4 Arabia's centres: the Nabataeans

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الاجتماعيات للصف الأول المتوسط (Social Studies for the First Intermediate Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.4 Arabia's centres: the Nabataeans
