# Video script — The Nabataeans and Petra

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iraq's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Arab Nabataeans build a kingdom (from c.587 BC) at Petra where the Iraq–Yemen–Syria–Egypt caravan roads meet, trading as far as Rhodes, Delos and Rome, and carve theatre, monastery and the Daughter's Palace.
