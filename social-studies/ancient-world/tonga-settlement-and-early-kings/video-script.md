# Video script — Tonga — settlement and the early kings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

ancestors sail from South East Asia across the Pacific and settle Tonga about three thousand years ago; a monarchy more than a thousand years old grows from those settlements, and Europeans such as Captain Cook later visit and name it the Friendly Islands.
