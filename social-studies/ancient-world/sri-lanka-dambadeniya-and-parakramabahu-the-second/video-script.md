# Video script — Sri Lanka — Dambadeniya and Parakramabahu the Second

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sri Lanka's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Vijayabahu III founds a safer capital, hides the Tooth Relic on Kotmale hill, and his son wins it back, beats Kalinga Magha in 1255 with Pandya help, and drives off Chandrabhanu of Java.
