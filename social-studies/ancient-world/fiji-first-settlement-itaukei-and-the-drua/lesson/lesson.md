# Fiji's first settlement — the iTaukei and the drua

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: more than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes; chief Lutunasobasoba landed at Vuda in the Kaunitoni, Degei's party settled by Nakauvadra, and people lived communally in bures planting food and making stone and wood tools As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 5.2.1 The Early Fijians — iTaukei
