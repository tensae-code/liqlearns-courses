# Video script — Fiji's first settlement — the iTaukei and the drua

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

More than 3,000 years ago the first iTaukei island-hopped in drua canoes; chief Lutunasobasoba landed at Vuda in the Kaunitoni, Degei's party settled by Nakauvadra, and people lived communally in bures planting food and making stone and wood tools.
