## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **4.1 The establishment of the Arshakunis in Armenia** — Rome and Parthia settle Armenia's crown on Tiridates I in the treaty of Rhandeia (62–63), founding a dynasty that lasts four centuries
2. **4.2 The Arshakuni kingdom at its height (same chapter, political part)** — strong Arshakuni kings hold the buffer kingdom together while Rome and Persia watch each other across it
3. **4.3 The division and fall of Arshakuni Armenia** — Rome and Persia partition Armenia in 387, the last Arshakuni king is deposed in 428, and the kingdom ends — but not the nation
4. **4.4 The socio-economic and cultural life of Arshakuni Armenia** — nakharar houses, cities and church schools structure a feudal Christian kingdom
