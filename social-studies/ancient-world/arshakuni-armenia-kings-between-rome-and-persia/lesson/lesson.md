## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Rome and Parthia settle Armenia's crown on Tiridates I in the treaty of Rhandeia (62–63), founding a dynasty that lasts four centuries As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
