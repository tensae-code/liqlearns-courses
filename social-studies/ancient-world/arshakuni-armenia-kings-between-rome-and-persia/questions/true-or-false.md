# Arshakuni Armenia Kings Between Rome And Persia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Rome and Parthia settle Armenia's crown on Tiridates I in the treaty of Rhandeia (62–63), founding a dynasty that lasts four centuries.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 4.1 The establishment of the Arshakunis in Armenia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 7» (History of Armenia 7) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, ed. Mariam A. Karapetyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 4.1 The establishment of the Arshakunis in Armenia

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Strong Arshakuni kings hold the buffer kingdom together while Rome and Persia watch each other across it.
