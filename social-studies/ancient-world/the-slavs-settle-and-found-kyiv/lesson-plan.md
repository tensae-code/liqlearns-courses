# Lesson plan — The Slavs settle and found Kyiv

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Slavs settle and found Kyiv
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Slavic tribes spread across Eastern Europe, farm, trade and organise in tribal unions — and on the Dnipro hills Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid found Kyiv, the city everything after this grows from.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Great Slavic settlement** — Slavic tribes spread across Eastern Europe. They farm, trade and organise themselves in tribal unions — the Great Slavic settlement fills the land the course has just surveyed.
2. **Kyi founds Kyiv** — On the Dnipro hills, the book tells, Kyi, Shchek and Khoryv and their sister Lybid found Kyiv. This is the city everything after this grows from.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Ukraine enters the Middle Ages. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell how the Slavs spread across Eastern Europe and organised in tribal unions, farming and trading.
2. Tell the founding story of Kyiv: Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid, on the Dnipro hills.
