# The Slavs settle and found Kyiv — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Slavs organised themselves in —
a) Tribal unions  b) Greek poleis  c) A single empire from the start
**Answer: a) Tribal unions**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Great Slavic settlement, the Slavs spread across —
a) Eastern Europe  b) Western Europe only  c) The Black Sea coast only
**Answer: a) Eastern Europe**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kyiv was founded, in the book's story, by —
a) Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid  b) Oleh alone  c) The Greek colonists of Olbia
**Answer: a) Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kyiv stands on —
a) The Dnipro hills  b) The Carpathian mountains  c) The Crimean coast
**Answer: a) The Dnipro hills**
