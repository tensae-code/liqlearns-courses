# The Slavs settle and found Kyiv — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Slavs only hunted and never farmed.
**Answer: False — They farm, trade and organise in tribal unions.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Kyiv is the city everything after this in the course grows from.
**Answer: True — The book presents Kyiv's founding as the seed of all that follows.**
