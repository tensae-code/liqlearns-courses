# The Slavs settle and found Kyiv — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did the Slavs organise themselves as they settled?
**Answer: In tribal unions, farming and trading (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who founded Kyiv, and where?
**Answer: Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid, on the Dnipro hills (any close wording).**
