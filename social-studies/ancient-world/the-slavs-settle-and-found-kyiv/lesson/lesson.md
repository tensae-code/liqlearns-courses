# The Slavs settle and found Kyiv

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Slavic tribes spread across Eastern Europe, farm, trade and organise in tribal unions — and on the Dnipro hills Kyi, Shchek, Khoryv and their sister Lybid found Kyiv, the city everything after this grows from. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Great Slavic settlement

Slavic tribes spread across Eastern Europe. They farm, trade and organise themselves in tribal unions — the Great Slavic settlement fills the land the course has just surveyed.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kyi founds Kyiv

On the Dnipro hills, the book tells, Kyi, Shchek and Khoryv and their sister Lybid found Kyiv. This is the city everything after this grows from.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of The Slavs settle and found Kyiv in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
