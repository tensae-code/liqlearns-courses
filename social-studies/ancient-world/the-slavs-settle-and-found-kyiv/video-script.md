# Video script — The Slavs settle and found Kyiv

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Ukraine's *Історія України* (Grade 7) tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Slavs spread over Eastern Europe — farming, trading, joining in tribal unions.



On the hills above the Dnipro, Kyi and his brothers and sister found a city.



Kyiv. Everything after this grows from here.
