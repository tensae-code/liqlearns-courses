# The Arab states of the north before Islam — Ghassanids and Lakhmids — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the desert's edge two Arab kingdoms face each other as the shields of two empires — the Ghassanids for Rome, whose last king Jabala ibn al-Ayham meets the Muslims at Yarmouk in 634, and the Lakhmids of al-Hira for Persia, remembered in al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the northern kingdoms

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the northern kingdoms
