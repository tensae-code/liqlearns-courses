# The Arab states of the north before Islam — Ghassanids and Lakhmids — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the northern kingdoms.
**Answer: On the desert's edge two Arab kingdoms face each other as the shields of two empires — the Ghassanids for Rome, whose last king Jabala ibn al-Ayham meets the Muslims at Yarmouk in 634, and the Lakhmids of al-Hira for Persia, remembered in al-Nu'man ibn al-Mundhir. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Sec11 "Egypt and Islamic Civilisation"* (Grade 11 (Second Secondary)), Civilisations of the Arabian Peninsula before Islam: the northern kingdoms, printed p. 9.**
