# Cuba First Inhabitants Gatherers And Farmers — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** canoe migrants from South America cross the Antilles; gatherer-hunter-fisher groups live in caves while farmer-potter groups plant conucos, make casabe from yuca, and are led by a cacique and a behíque.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1.1 First settlers, occupations and ways of life

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1.1 First settlers, occupations and ways of life

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** two ways of aboriginal life are set side by side in a table; the pupil then hunts for the aborigines who once lived in their own town.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.1 activities: comparing gatherer and farmer-potter groups, casabe-making, investigating aborigines in your own locality

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Cuaderno de actividades — Historia de Cuba, Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.1 activities: comparing gatherer and farmer-potter groups, casabe-making, investigating aborigines in your own locality

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** teachers are walked through presenting Cuba's oldest inhabitants with descriptions, anecdotes and stories suited to 10-year-olds.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit guidance, Part I Aboriginal community society (Intro unit + Unit 1)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Orientaciones Metodológicas — Historia de Cuba, quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit guidance, Part I Aboriginal community society (Intro unit + Unit 1)

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** the island's different aboriginal communities are surveyed again, now with the Amazon-to-Caribbean canoe expedition as evidence.
