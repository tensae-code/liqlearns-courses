# Video script — Cuba First Inhabitants Gatherers And Farmers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cuba's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Canoe migrants from South America cross the Antilles; gatherer-hunter-fisher groups live in caves while farmer-potter groups plant conucos, make casabe from yuca, and are led by a cacique and a behíque.
