# Cuba First Inhabitants Gatherers And Farmers — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — 1.1 First settlers, occupations and ways of life
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — canoe migrants from South America cross the Antilles; gatherer-hunter-fisher groups live in caves while farmer-potter groups plant conucos, make casabe from yuca, and are led by a cacique and a behíque
## Sub lesson 2 — Ch.1 activities: comparing gatherer and farmer-potter groups, casabe-making, investigating aborigines in your own locality
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — two ways of aboriginal life are set side by side in a table; the pupil then hunts for the aborigines who once lived in their own town
## Sub lesson 3 — Unit guidance, Part I Aboriginal community society (Intro unit + Unit 1)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — teachers are walked through presenting Cuba's oldest inhabitants with descriptions, anecdotes and stories suited to 10-year-olds
## Sub lesson 4 — Intro 1.1 (second part) Aboriginal communities in Cuba at the end of the 15th c.
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the island's different aboriginal communities are surveyed again, now with the Amazon-to-Caribbean canoe expedition as evidence
