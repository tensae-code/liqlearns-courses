# Lesson plan — Ukrainian lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Ukrainian lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Ukraine, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the borderlands go other ways — Transcarpathia under Hungary since the Magyar crossing of the Carpathians and Prince Laborets' doomed defence of Ungvar, Northern Bukovina inside Moldavia, and the north-eastern borderlands drawn toward Moscow.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Transcarpathia and Bukovina go other ways** — Transcarpathia lies under Hungary since the Magyar crossing of the Carpathians and Prince Laborets' doomed defence of Ungvar. Northern Bukovina sits inside Moldavia — the borderlands go other ways than the Lithuanian centre.
2. **The north-east turns toward Moscow** — The north-eastern borderlands are drawn toward Moscow. One former land now faces three different futures: Hungary and Moldavia in the south-west, Moscow in the north-east.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

2. Say where the north-eastern borderlands were drawn: toward Moscow.
