# Ukrainian lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** All Ukrainian lands went into the Lithuanian state.
**Answer: False — The borderlands go other ways — Hungary, Moldavia and Muscovy.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Magyars crossed the Carpathians into Transcarpathia.
**Answer: True — Transcarpathia is under Hungary since the Magyar crossing of the Carpathians.**
