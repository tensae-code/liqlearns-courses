# Ukrainian lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Transcarpathia lay under —
a) Hungary  b) Lithuania  c) The Golden Horde
**Answer: a) Hungary**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Prince Laborets is remembered for —
a) His doomed defence of Ungvar  b) Founding Lviv  c) The battle of Blue Waters
**Answer: a) His doomed defence of Ungvar**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Northern Bukovina sat inside —
a) Moldavia (Russo-Wallachia)  b) Hungary  c) Muscovy
**Answer: a) Moldavia (Russo-Wallachia)**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The north-eastern borderlands were drawn toward —
a) Moscow  b) Vilnius  c) Constantinople
**Answer: a) Moscow**
