# Ukrainian lands under Hungary, Moldova and Muscovy

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the borderlands go other ways — Transcarpathia under Hungary since the Magyar crossing of the Carpathians and Prince Laborets' doomed defence of Ungvar, Northern Bukovina inside Moldavia, and the north-eastern borderlands drawn toward Moscow. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Transcarpathia and Bukovina go other ways

Transcarpathia lies under Hungary since the Magyar crossing of the Carpathians and Prince Laborets' doomed defence of Ungvar. Northern Bukovina sits inside Moldavia — the borderlands go other ways than the Lithuanian centre.

## Sub lesson 2 — The north-east turns toward Moscow

The north-eastern borderlands are drawn toward Moscow. One former land now faces three different futures: Hungary and Moldavia in the south-west, Moscow in the north-east.
