# China Early Humans Peking Man And Upper Cave Man

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: at Zhoukoudian, Peking Man chips stone tools, hunts and gathers in groups and masters fire in a cave of ash and burnt bone, while Upper Cave Man, 30,000 years later, learns to make fire himself As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

at Zhoukoudian, Peking Man chips stone tools, hunts and gathers in groups and masters fire in a cave of ash and burnt bone, while Upper Cave Man, 30,000 years later, learns to make fire himself
