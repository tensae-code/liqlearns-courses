# China Early Humans Peking Man And Upper Cave Man — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At Zhoukoudian, Peking Man chips stone tools, hunts and gathers in groups and masters fire in a cave of ash and burnt bone, while Upper Cave Man, 30,000 years later, learns to make fire himself.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The homeland of our primitive ancestors

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 七年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The homeland of our primitive ancestors

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In May 1965 geologists dig two shovel-shaped incisor fossils from a Yunnan hillside — Yuanmou Man, 1.7 million years old — opening a map of hundreds of sites from Peking Man to Upper Cave Man.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Origins of Chinese civilisation

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Origins of Chinese civilisation
