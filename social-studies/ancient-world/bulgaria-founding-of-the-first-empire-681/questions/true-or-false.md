# Bulgaria — founding of the First Empire, 681 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 6: The creation and strengthening of the Bulgarian state on the Balkan Peninsula

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kubrat's son Asparuh founds Danubian Bulgaria south of the river (681).
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 6: The birth of Danubian Bulgaria
