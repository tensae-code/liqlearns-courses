## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **From Zhenguan to Kaiyuan** — Taizong learns from Sui's fall, keeps Wei Zheng as his 'mirror' through 200 remonstrances; Wu Zetian deposes her own sons to become China's only woman emperor and still grows the realm; Xuanzong's Kaiyuan reign makes Tang the strongest state on earth
2. **Flourishing Tang** — curved ploughs and tube wheels, Shu silk, Tang sancai pottery and Chang'an in flower season — a century of farm, craft and city prosperity and warm ethnic relations
3. **From Sui-Tang prosperity to the Five Dynasties** — reunification in 589, the Zhenguan and Kaiyuan heights, then An Lushan's wreckage and a half-century of ten squabbling kingdoms
