# China Tang Zhenguan Kaiyuan And Wu Zetian — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Taizong learns from Sui's fall, keeps Wei Zheng as his 'mirror' through 200 remonstrances; Wu Zetian deposes her own sons to become China's only woman emperor and still grows the realm; Xuanzong's Kaiyuan reign makes Tang the strongest state on earth.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in From Zhenguan to Kaiyuan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about From Zhenguan to Kaiyuan

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Curved ploughs and tube wheels, Shu silk, Tang sancai pottery and Chang'an in flower season — a century of farm, craft and city prosperity and warm ethnic relations.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Flourishing Tang

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Flourishing Tang

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Reunification in 589, the Zhenguan and Kaiyuan heights, then An Lushan's wreckage and a half-century of ten squabbling kingdoms.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in From Sui-Tang prosperity to the Five Dynasties

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about From Sui-Tang prosperity to the Five Dynasties
