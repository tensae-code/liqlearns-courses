# China Tang Zhenguan Kaiyuan And Wu Zetian — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: From Zhenguan to Kaiyuan.
**Answer: Taizong learns from Sui's fall, keeps Wei Zheng as his 'mirror' through 200 remonstrances; Wu Zetian deposes her own sons to become China's only woman emperor and still grows the realm; Xuanzong's Kaiyuan reign makes Tang the strongest state on earth (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 2: From Zhenguan to Kaiyuan, printed p. 6.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Flourishing Tang.
**Answer: curved ploughs and tube wheels, Shu silk, Tang sancai pottery and Chang'an in flower season — a century of farm, craft and city prosperity and warm ethnic relations (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 3: Flourishing Tang, printed p. 11.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: From Sui-Tang prosperity to the Five Dynasties.
**Answer: reunification in 589, the Zhenguan and Kaiyuan heights, then An Lushan's wreckage and a half-century of ten squabbling kingdoms (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 6: From Sui-Tang prosperity to the Five Dynasties, printed p. 34.**
