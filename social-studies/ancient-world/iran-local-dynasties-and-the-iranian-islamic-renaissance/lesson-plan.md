# Lesson plan — Iran — local dynasties and the Iranian-Islamic renaissance

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** Iran — local dynasties and the Iranian-Islamic renaissance
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Iran, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Tahirids, Saffarids, Samanids, Buyids and the Alavids of Tabaristan rule Iran in the caliph's name or against him, and Persian language and learning flower again under their courts
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The rise and spread of Iranian-Islamic civilisation** — Tahirids, Saffarids, Samanids, Buyids and the Alavids of Tabaristan rule Iran in the caliph's name or against him, and Persian language and learning flower again under their courts.
2. **The golden age of Iranian-Islamic culture and civilisation** — conquered Iran conquers back with culture — Persian administrators, scientists and poets make the Islamic golden age substantially Iranian.
3. **Muslim Iranians form governments** — within two centuries of the conquest, Iranians are ruling Iran again under Islam — Tahirid, Saffarid and Samanid governments rise.
