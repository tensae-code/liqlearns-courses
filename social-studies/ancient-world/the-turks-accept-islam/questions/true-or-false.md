# The Turks accept Islam — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Turks accepted Islam all in a single day after Talas.
**Answer: False** — the book teaches a meeting over generations, people by people.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches the conversion as change and continuity together.
**Answer: True** — titles, law and city change; the steppe's customs remain.
