# The Turks accept Islam — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happened at Talas, and in what year?
**Answer: The encounter of 751 that brought Turks and the Islamic world into lasting contact — the start of their thousand-year meeting (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name three changes the faith brought, and one thing it did not erase.
**Answer: Changes: the khagan's titles, the law, the city. Kept: the steppe's customs (any close wording).**
