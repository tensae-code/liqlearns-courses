# The Turks accept Islam — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Talas encounter happened in —
a) 751  b) 624  c) 1071
**Answer: a) 751** — the riverside meeting.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first Turkish Muslim state was —
a) The Umayyads  b) The Karakhanids  c) The Göktürks
**Answer: b) The Karakhanids** — the first of its kind.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Islam changed, in the Turkish state —
a) Only the calendar  b) Nothing at all  c) The khagan's titles, the law and the city
**Answer: c) The khagan's titles, the law and the city** — the three changes named.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Through the change, the steppe kept —
a) Its customs  b) Nothing  c) Only its tents
**Answer: a) Its customs** — continuity beside change.
