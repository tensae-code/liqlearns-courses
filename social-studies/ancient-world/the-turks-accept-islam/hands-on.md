# Hands-on — The Turks accept Islam

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The door at Talas

Draw the Talas river with two banks: steppe tents on one side, a Muslim city on the other, and a bridge between. Label what crosses the bridge in each direction over the generations.

## Changed and kept

Make two lists side by side: 'Changed by the faith' (titles, law, the city) and 'Kept from the steppe' (customs, horsemanship, assemblies). Draw one item from each list and say why both lists are true at once.

## A thousand-year meeting

Draw a timeline from 751 forward with three marks: Talas, the Karakhanids, and today. Write one sentence at each mark on what the meeting looks like by then.
