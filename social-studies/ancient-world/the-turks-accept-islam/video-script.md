# Video script — The Turks accept Islam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 9 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In 751, beside the Talas river, Turks and the Islamic world meet in earnest. Turkey's Grade 9 book calls it the start of a thousand-year meeting of steppe and Islam.

Over generations the Turks accept the faith, until the Karakhanids stand as the first Turkish Muslim state. The faith changes the khagan's titles, the law, and the city that grows beside the camp.

But the steppe keeps its customs too. Change and continuity, weighed together — that is how the book teaches a people's conversion.
