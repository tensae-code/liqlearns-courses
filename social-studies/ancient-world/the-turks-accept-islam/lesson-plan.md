# Lesson plan — The Turks accept Islam

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Turks accept Islam
**Place in the curriculum:** Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a battle beside a river in 751 starts a thousand-year meeting of steppe and Islam — and the faith changes the khagan's titles, the law and the city, but the steppe keeps its customs too.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: a battle beside a river in 751 starts a thousand-year meeting of steppe and Islam — and the faith changes the khagan's titles, the law and the city, but the steppe keeps its customs too. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the Talas encounter and its year: 751.
2. Name the first Turkish Muslim state: the Karakhanids.
3. Name three things Islam changes in Turkish state and society: the khagan's titles, the law, and the city.
4. Say what the steppe keeps through the change: its customs — and why the book teaches change and continuity together.
