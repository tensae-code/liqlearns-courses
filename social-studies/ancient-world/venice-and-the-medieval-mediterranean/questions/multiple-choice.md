# Venice and the medieval Mediterranean — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Venice was built —
a) On water  b) On a high mountain  c) In a desert
**Answer: a) On water** — Venice is a city built on water.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Venice grew —
a) Poor, cut off from the sea  b) Rich  c) Only as a farming village
**Answer: b) Rich** — the manual's beat: it grows rich.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Venice commanded —
a) Only its own lagoon  b) The sea's trade routes  c) No trade at all
**Answer: b) The sea's trade routes** — it grows rich commanding the sea's trade routes.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Venice's story belongs to —
a) The medieval Mediterranean  b) The Atlantic in 1800  c) Ancient Egypt alone
**Answer: a) The medieval Mediterranean** — Thème 1, La Méditerranée médiévale.
