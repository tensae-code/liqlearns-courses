# Venice and the medieval Mediterranean

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Venice, a city built on water, grows rich commanding the medieval Mediterranean's trade routes. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — A city built on water

A city built on water grows rich commanding the sea's trade routes — that is Venice in France's medieval Mediterranean chapter.

[Picture slot — a picture of Venice rising from the water, with trading ships loading goods at its quays — the student says how a city on water could grow rich]

## Sub lesson 2 — Rich on the sea's trade

Venice's wealth comes not from farming a hinterland but from commanding the sea's trade routes, so the city on water becomes a great power of the medieval Mediterranean.
