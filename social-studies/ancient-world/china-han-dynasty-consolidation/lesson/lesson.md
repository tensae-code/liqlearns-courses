## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Han Wudi centralises, strikes the Xiongnu and opens the west, and the restored Eastern Han carries the unified state to its first great height — and its slow decay As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
