# Lesson plan — The Peloponnesian War

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Ancient World · **Lesson:** The Peloponnesian War
**Place in the curriculum:** The Ancient World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sparta's alliance votes for war against the Athenian empire, and Greek begins killing Greek in a war Thucydides calls the greatest ever
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 25 Causes and occasions of the war** — Sparta's alliance votes for war against the Athenian empire, and Greek begins killing Greek in a war Thucydides calls the greatest ever
2. **Ch. 26 The main phases of the war** — plague, civil strife and the Sicilian disaster bleed Athens until Lysander's fleet starves her into surrender on harsh Spartan terms
3. **Ch. 25 Causes of the war exercises** — pupils separate the deep causes from the sparks of the Peloponnesian War
4. **Ch. 26 Phases of the war exercises** — ordering sentences, then retelling aloud, walks the war from plague to surrender
5. **Ch. F1 Causes and occasions of the Peloponnesian War — the Archidamian War (431–421)** — fear of Athens's growth drives Sparta to invade Attica yearly while plague, not battle, breaks Athens's strength
6. **Ch. F2 The Sicilian expedition (415–413) — the Decelean War (413–404)** — Athens throws an armada at Sicily and loses it whole; Sparta, funded by Persia, starves Athens into surrender

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
