# Hands-on — The Peloponnesian War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Peloponnesian War — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch. 25 Causes and occasions of the war, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
