# Bhutan — first Buddhist temples

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo (c.605-650) builds Jampa Lhakhang in Bumthang and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, and that temple-building is taken as the start of Bhutanese recorded history, the Early Diffusion; Songtsen Gampo's 7th-century temple network pins down a demoness across the Himalayas, with Jampa Lhakhang on her left knee and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, while later kings add the thadul-yangdul temples around them. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
