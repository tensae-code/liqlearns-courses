# Bhutan — first Buddhist temples — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo (c.605-650) builds Jampa Lhakhang in Bumthang and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, and that temple-building is taken as the start of Bhutanese recorded history, the Early Diffusion
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter One, Introduction).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Songtsen Gampo's 7th-century temple network pins down a demoness across the Himalayas, with Jampa Lhakhang on her left knee and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, while later kings add the thadul-yangdul temples around them
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter One, The Arrival of Buddhism).
