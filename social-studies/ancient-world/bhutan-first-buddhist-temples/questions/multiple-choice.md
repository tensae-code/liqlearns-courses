# Bhutan — first Buddhist temples — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter One, Introduction  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter One, Introduction** — Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo (c.605-650) builds Jampa Lhakhang in Bumthang and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, and that temple-building is taken as the start of Bhutanese recorded history, the Early Diffusion

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Chapter One, Introduction  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Chapter One, Introduction** — the book's own heading for this part.

**Answer: a) Chapter One, The Arrival of Buddhism** — Songtsen Gampo's 7th-century temple network pins down a demoness across the Himalayas, with Jampa Lhakhang on her left knee and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, while later kings add the thadul-yangdul temples around them
