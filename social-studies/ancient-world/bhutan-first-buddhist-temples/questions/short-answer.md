# Bhutan — first Buddhist temples — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter One, Introduction.
**Answer: Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo (c.605-650) builds Jampa Lhakhang in Bumthang and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, and that temple-building is taken as the start of Bhutanese recorded history, the Early Diffusion (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter One, The Arrival of Buddhism.
**Answer: Songtsen Gampo's 7th-century temple network pins down a demoness across the Himalayas, with Jampa Lhakhang on her left knee and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, while later kings add the thadul-yangdul temples around them (any close wording).**
