# Video script — Bhutan — first Buddhist temples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bhutan book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo (c.605-650) builds Jampa Lhakhang in Bumthang and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, and that temple-building is taken as the start of Bhutanese recorded history, the Early Diffusion; Songtsen Gampo's 7th-century temple network pins down a demoness across the Himalayas, with Jampa Lhakhang on her left knee and Kyichu Lhakhang in Paro, while later kings add the thadul-yangdul temples around them
