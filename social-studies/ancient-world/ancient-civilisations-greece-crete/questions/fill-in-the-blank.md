# Ancient civilisations — Greece: Crete — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1.** Crete's civilisation is called ____, after King Minos.
**Answer: Minoan**
**2.** Crete spread its civilisation to the ____ islands.
**Answer: Aegean**
**3.** Good ____ turned Crete's people to trade.
**Answer: harbours**
