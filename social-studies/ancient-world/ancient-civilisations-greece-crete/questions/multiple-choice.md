# Ancient civilisations — Greece: Crete — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The Minoan civilisation flourished on —
a) The Greek mainland  b) Crete  c) Sicily  d) The Tiber
**Answer: b) Crete** — An island in the eastern Mediterranean.
**2.** Its dates are —
a) 753–476 BC  b) 1600–1050 BC  c) 3000–1400 BC  d) 221–206 BC
**Answer: c) 3000–1400 BC** — Sixteen centuries of civilisation.
**3.** 'Minoan' comes from —
a) A river  b) The legendary King Minos  c) A harbour town  d) A Greek god
**Answer: b) The legendary King Minos** — The name remembers the king.
**4.** Crete's people turned to trade because they had —
a) Great farmland  b) Little farmland but good harbours  c) Gold mines  d) No ships
**Answer: b) Little farmland but good harbours** — The harbour beat the field.
