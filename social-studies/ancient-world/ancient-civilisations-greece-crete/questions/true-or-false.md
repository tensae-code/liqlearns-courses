# Ancient civilisations — Greece: Crete — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1.** Minoan civilisation spread to the Aegean islands.
**Answer: True** — From Crete outward across the sea.
**2.** Crete grew rich mainly by farming its wide fields.
**Answer: False** — Its farmland was little; trade from good harbours carried it.
**3.** Crete lies in the eastern Mediterranean.
**Answer: True** — The map's first fact.
