# Ancient civilisations — Greece: Crete — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why did Crete's people become traders?
**Answer:** Crete had little farmland but good harbours, so its people turned to the sea and trade carried the civilisation.
**2.** Where and when was the Minoan civilisation?
**Answer:** On Crete in the eastern Mediterranean, from 3000 to 1400 BC, spreading to the Aegean islands.
