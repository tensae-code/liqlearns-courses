# Ancient civilisations — Greece: Crete

## Do it first

An island with poor, thin farmland — by farming logic, its people should be poor. Instead they grew rich and built a high civilisation for sixteen centuries. What else does an island have? Answer that, and you have Crete.

## The island that traded

From **3000 to 1400 BC** a high civilisation flourished on **Crete**, in the **eastern Mediterranean** — the **Minoan** civilisation, named from the legendary **King Minos**. It **spread to the Aegean islands** around it. Look at the picture: one great island, small islands scattered like stepping stones, and sea routes between them all.

## Harbour beats field

Crete had **little farmland — but good harbours**. So its people did the island thing: they **turned to trade**. Ships carried their goods across the Aegean and the Mediterranean, and trade, not farming, carried the civilisation. Land is not the only wealth a place can have; a harbour facing many shores is wealth too.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion where Crete is, how long its civilisation lasted, whose name it carries, and why its people traded instead of farming.
