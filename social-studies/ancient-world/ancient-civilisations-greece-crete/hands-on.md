# Hands-on — Ancient civilisations — Greece: Crete

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Harbour map
Draw your own island on paper: mark one small field and one good harbour, then draw three ship routes to neighbouring 'islands' (chairs or mats) and list what each route could carry.

## Stepping-stone sail
Lay paper 'islands' across the floor and 'sail' goods (small objects) from Crete to each island in turn, naming the Aegean spread.
