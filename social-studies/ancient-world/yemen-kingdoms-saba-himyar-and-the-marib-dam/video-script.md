# Video script — Yemen's kingdoms — Saba, Himyar and the Marib Dam

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Palestine's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ma'in (1200–650 BCE, capital Qarnaw) and Saba (950–115 BCE) rise in Yemen on rain, the Zamzam-like wells and incense trade; Saba's farmers dam the floods at Marib, terrace the mountains and leave Bilqis's throne as their emblem.
