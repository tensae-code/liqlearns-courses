# Hands-on — John Hunyadi And Vlad The Impaler Against The Ottomans

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — John Hunyadi And Vlad The Impaler Against The Ottomans — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unitatea II, Lecția 9: Țările Române în lupta antiotomană. Iancu de Hunedoara și Vlad Țepeş, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
