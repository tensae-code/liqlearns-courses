# Culture of the Halych-Volyn land

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: under Mongol shadow the west keeps the Rus inheritance alive — the Halych-Volyn Chronicle, church schools and writing, stone fortresses and painted icons in the land that preserved a Ukrainian state tradition. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Halych-Volyn Chronicle

Under Mongol shadow the west keeps the Rus inheritance alive. The Halych-Volyn Chronicle carries the story on, with church schools and writing keeping letters alive.

## Sub lesson 2 — Fortresses and icons

Stone fortresses and painted icons fill the land. This is the land that preserved a Ukrainian state tradition — culture holding what armies could not.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story of Culture of the Halych-Volyn land in your own words, part by part, using the sub lesson headings as your beats. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
