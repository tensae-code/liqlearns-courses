# Culture of the Halych-Volyn land — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The great written work of this land is —
a) The Halych-Volyn Chronicle  b) The Ruska Pravda  c) Monomakh's Instruction
**Answer: a) The Halych-Volyn Chronicle**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Writing was kept alive by —
a) Church schools  b) Mongol offices  c) Greek merchants only
**Answer: a) Church schools**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The land's buildings named here are —
a) Stone fortresses  b) Wooden palaces only  c) Roman baths
**Answer: a) Stone fortresses**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Painted religious images of this culture are —
a) Icons  b) Mosaics of St Sophia only  c) Graffiti
**Answer: a) Icons**
