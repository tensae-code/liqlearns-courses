# Culture of the Halych-Volyn land — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This culture flowered with no shadow over it.
**Answer: False — It kept the Rus inheritance alive under Mongol shadow.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Halych-Volyn land preserved a Ukrainian state tradition.
**Answer: True — The book presents it as the land that preserved that tradition.**
