# Herodotus Croesus Solon And The Fall Of Lydia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Solon warns the richest king to call no man happy before his end; Croesus, deaf to it, loses his son Atys to Adrastus's spear.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Book I, Unit 2 Croesus and Solon; the novella of Adrastus

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Αρχαία Ελληνικά Κείμενα — Ηροδότου Ιστορίες, Α΄ Γυμνασίου, Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Ancient Greek Texts: Herodotus's Histories, Grade 7 Gymnasium)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Book I, Unit 2 Croesus and Solon; the novella of Adrastus
