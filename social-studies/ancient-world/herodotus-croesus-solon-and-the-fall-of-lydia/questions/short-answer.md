# Herodotus Croesus Solon And The Fall Of Lydia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Book I, Unit 2 Croesus and Solon; the novella of Adrastus.
**Answer: Solon warns the richest king to call no man happy before his end; Croesus, deaf to it, loses his son Atys to Adrastus's spear (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Αρχαία Ελληνικά Κείμενα — Ηροδότου Ιστορίες, Α΄ Γυμνασίου, Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Ancient Greek Texts: Herodotus's Histories, Grade 7 Gymnasium)* (Greece), Book I, Unit 2 Croesus and Solon; the novella of Adrastus, printed p. 18.**
