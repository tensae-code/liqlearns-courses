# Hands-on — From barter to banknotes — the story of money

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — From barter to banknotes — the story of money — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The evolution of money, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
