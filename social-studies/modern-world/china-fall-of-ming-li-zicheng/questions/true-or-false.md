# China Fall Of Ming Li Zicheng — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Shepherd boy turned soldier Li Zicheng promises 'equal land, no grain tax', keeps strict discipline, feeds the poor, and marches a million men into Xi'an to found Dashun in 1644 as drought and the three surtaxes empty the villages.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The fall of Ming

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The fall of Ming

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Eunuchs and land-grabbing lords hollow out late Ming until drought-starved Shaanxi rises; the folk song runs 'kill the cattle and sheep, open the gates, welcome King Chuang — when King Chuang comes, no grain tax'.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ming falls

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ming falls

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Chongzhen emperor hangs himself on Coal Hill as rebels and Qing armies divide his empire between them.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1644

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1644
