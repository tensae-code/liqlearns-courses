# China Fall Of Ming Li Zicheng — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The fall of Ming.
**Answer: shepherd boy turned soldier Li Zicheng promises 'equal land, no grain tax', keeps strict discipline, feeds the poor, and marches a million men into Xi'an to found Dashun in 1644 as drought and the three surtaxes empty the villages (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 17: The fall of Ming, printed p. 84.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Ming falls.
**Answer: eunuchs and land-grabbing lords hollow out late Ming until drought-starved Shaanxi rises; the folk song runs 'kill the cattle and sheep, open the gates, welcome King Chuang — when King Chuang comes, no grain tax' (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Unit 5 Lesson 1: Ming falls, printed p. 3.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: 1644.
**Answer: the Chongzhen emperor hangs himself on Coal Hill as rebels and Qing armies divide his empire between them (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 13: 1644, printed p. 77.**
