**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The fall of Ming** — shepherd boy turned soldier Li Zicheng promises 'equal land, no grain tax', keeps strict discipline, feeds the poor, and marches a million men into Xi'an to found Dashun in 1644 as drought and the three surtaxes empty the villages
2. **Ming falls** — eunuchs and land-grabbing lords hollow out late Ming until drought-starved Shaanxi rises; the folk song runs 'kill the cattle and sheep, open the gates, welcome King Chuang — when King Chuang comes, no grain tax'
3. **1644** — the Chongzhen emperor hangs himself on Coal Hill as rebels and Qing armies divide his empire between them
