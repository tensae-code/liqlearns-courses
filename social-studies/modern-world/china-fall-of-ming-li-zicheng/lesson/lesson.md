## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: The fall of Ming — shepherd boy turned soldier Li Zicheng promises 'equal land, no grain tax', keeps strict discipline, feeds the poor, and marches a million men into Xi'an to found Dashun in 1644 as drought and the three surtaxes empty the villages. Ming falls — eunuchs and land-grabbing lords hollow out late Ming until drought-starved Shaanxi rises; the folk song runs 'kill the cattle and sheep, open the gates, welcome King Chuang — when King Chuang comes, no grain tax'. 1644 — the Chongzhen emperor hangs himself on Coal Hill as rebels and Qing armies divide his empire between them. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
