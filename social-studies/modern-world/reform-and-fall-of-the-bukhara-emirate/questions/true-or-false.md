# Reform and Fall of the Bukhara Emirate — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Young Bukharan reformers beg schools and a constitution; the Emir answers with pogroms, and in 1920 the Red Army ends the emirate altogether.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 5 §26 The attempt to carry out reforms and the fall of the Bukhara Emirate

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Узбекистана (History of Uzbekistan — second half of the 19th to beginning of the 20th century), Grade 9* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 5 §26 The attempt to carry out reforms and the fall of the Bukhara Emirate
