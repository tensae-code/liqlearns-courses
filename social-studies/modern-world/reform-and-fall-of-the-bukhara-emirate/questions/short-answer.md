# Reform and Fall of the Bukhara Emirate — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. 5 §26 The attempt to carry out reforms and the fall of the Bukhara Emirate.
**Answer: Young Bukharan reformers beg schools and a constitution; the Emir answers with pogroms, and in 1920 the Red Army ends the emirate altogether (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Узбекистана (History of Uzbekistan — second half of the 19th to beginning of the 20th century), Grade 9* (Uzbekistan), Ch. 5 §26 The attempt to carry out reforms and the fall of the Bukhara Emirate, printed p. 95.**
