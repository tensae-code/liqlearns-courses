# The partitions of the Commonwealth — Belarus to Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The partitions — Belarus passes to Russia.
**Answer: Poniatowski's reforms and the Constitution of 3 May 1791 come too late; Russia, Prussia and Austria partition the state in 1772, 1793 and 1795 and Belarus passes to Russia (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Belarus's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Atlas: History of Belarus, Grade 8* (Grade 8), The partitions of the Commonwealth and the inclusion of the Belarusian lands in the Russian Empire, printed p. 22.**
