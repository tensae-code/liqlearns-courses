# Nineteen Sixty Eight — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** students and workers shake Paris, Berlin, Rome and Berkeley in one season; Friedan's American housewife (Document) and Robert Kennedy's critique of GDP give the revolt its domestic and economic manifestos.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 16 §4 From the United States to Western Europe: the Sixty-Eight

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 16 §4 From the United States to Western Europe: the Sixty-Eight

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kurlansky's "1968: the epicentre of a turning" and the occupied faculties' own document (Fare storia) — the affluent society's children indict it in its own universities.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 11 §7 Critica del consumismo e contestazione giovanile

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 11 §7 Critica del consumismo e contestazione giovanile
