# Cuba Guaimaro Assembly 1869 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** quarrelling regions meet at Guáimaro and agree one Republic in Arms, a constitution and a single flag — unity is proclaimed even as rivalries remain.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 5.4 Assembly of Guáimaro, 10 April 1869

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 5.4 Assembly of Guáimaro, 10 April 1869

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** delegates argue civil vs military power and leave with a constitution and a republic that exists first on paper.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 3.4 The Assembly of Guáimaro

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Noveno grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 3.4 The Assembly of Guáimaro
