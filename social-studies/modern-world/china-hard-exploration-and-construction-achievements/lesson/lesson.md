Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Hard exploration and construction achievements — the young republic stumbles through the Great Leap and commune years yet still builds Daqing oilfield, its first atomic and hydrogen bombs and a satellite, carried by model workers and scientists. Socialist construction twists and turns — the Eighth Congress's good start, then the Great Leap, communes and the Cultural Revolution's decade — against Daqing, the bombs and the satellite.
