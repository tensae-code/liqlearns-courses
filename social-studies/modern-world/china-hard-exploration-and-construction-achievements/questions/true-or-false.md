# China Hard Exploration And Construction Achievements — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The young republic stumbles through the Great Leap and commune years yet still builds Daqing oilfield, its first atomic and hydrogen bombs and a satellite, carried by model workers and scientists.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Hard exploration and construction achievements

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
