# Hands-on — Bismarck's Germany and the young Wilhelm II

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bismarck's Germany and the young Wilhelm II — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch. II §§9–10 The political and economic position of the Empire, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
