# Bismarck's Germany and the young Wilhelm II

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Bismarck's anti-socialist laws sit beside Europe's first social insurance, but Wilhelm II will share power with no one — the old chancellor. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
