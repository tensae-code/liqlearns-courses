# Hands-on — United Arab Emirates — the union and Sheikh Zayed

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Seven emirates, one country
Draw a simple map with seven shapes side by side, as the book's map of the seven emirates shows them together. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what it means that the seven together make one country.

## Old and new landmarks
Draw two boxes. In one, draw an old landmark from the lesson — Al Maqta Fort or Ajman Museum. In the other, draw a new one — Burj Khalifa or Louvre Abu Dhabi. Say out loud what it shows that a young country keeps both kinds of place.

## The schooling line
Draw a line with three stops. Mark them in order: the Mutawa'a, Al Qasimiyah School in Sharjah, and 1953 — modern formal education begins. Under each stop, write in your own words what changed at that stop.
