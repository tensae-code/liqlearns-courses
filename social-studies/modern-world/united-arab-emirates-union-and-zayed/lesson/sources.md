**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (United Arab Emirates).**

United Arab Emirates' own way: at Grade 1, history is national identity — a visit to the Union Museum, the map of the seven emirates, Sheikh Zayed's sayings quoted on almost every spread under the heading "Zayed taught me", and one great traveller of the shared Arab past, Ibn Battuta, as the child's first named person from history. A state younger than the children's grandparents is introduced through the museum that keeps its birth certificate and the words of the man who united it. Sheikh Zayed's sayings are filed here as the book's teaching device and paraphrased in summary only — never transcribed.
