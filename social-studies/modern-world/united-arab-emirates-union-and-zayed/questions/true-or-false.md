# United Arab Emirates — the union and Sheikh Zayed — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The United Arab Emirates is a young country — younger than the grandparents of the children who read this book.
**Answer: True** — the book introduces it as a state younger than the children's grandparents, through the museum that keeps its union story.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Before there were regular schools in the Emirates, the Mutawa'a taught children Qur'an, reading, writing and arithmetic.
**Answer: True** — in the beginning, teaching rested on the Mutawa'a.
