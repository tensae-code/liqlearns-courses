# United Arab Emirates — the union and Sheikh Zayed — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Etihad Museum in Dubai is, in the book —
a) A fort  b) The house of the union  c) A school
**Answer: b) The house of the union** — the museum that keeps the story of how the country came together.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The United Arab Emirates is made of —
a) Seven emirates  b) Two emirates  c) Forty countries
**Answer: a) Seven emirates** — shown together on the book's map.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is an OLD landmark in this lesson?
a) Burj Khalifa  b) Louvre Abu Dhabi  c) Al Maqta Fort
**Answer: c) Al Maqta Fort** — in Umm Al Quwain, standing beside the new landmarks.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The first regular school in the country was —
a) Al Qasimiyah School in Sharjah  b) The Etihad Museum  c) Ajman Museum
**Answer: a) Al Qasimiyah School in Sharjah** — after the time of the Mutawa'a.
