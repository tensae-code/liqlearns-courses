# United Arab Emirates — the union and Sheikh Zayed — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Who was Sheikh Zayed bin Sultan Al Nahyan, and what is "Zayed taught me" in the book?
**Answer: Sheikh Zayed was the founding president, the man who united the emirates; "Zayed taught me" is the heading under which his sayings appear on almost every spread, teaching the child through the book (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell how schooling began in the Emirates, in order, ending with the year modern formal education began.
**Answer: Teaching first rested on the Mutawa'a, who taught children Qur'an, reading, writing and arithmetic; then Al Qasimiyah School in Sharjah became the first regular school; modern formal education began in 1953 (any close wording).**
