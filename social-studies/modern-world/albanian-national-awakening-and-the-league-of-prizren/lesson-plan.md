# Lesson plan — Albanian National Awakening And The League Of Prizren

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Albanian National Awakening And The League Of Prizren
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (North Macedonia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** before Albanians have a state, teachers give them an alphabet and books of their own
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Theme 2: Albania from the early 19th century to the Great Eastern Crisis, and the Albanian enlightenment — primers and translations teach children to read Albanian as the empire crumbles** — before Albanians have a state, teachers give them an alphabet and books of their own
2. **Theme 2: the League of Prizren (1878) and the rise of the Albanian liberation movement — Abdyl Frashëri and the League demand one united Albanian vilayet with wide autonomy and plead the Albanian case in Europe's capitals** — faced with partition among neighbours, Albanian beys and teachers form a league to keep Albanian lands together
3. **Theme 4: Albania on the road to independence — no single alphabet, contacts with the Young Turks, and Ismail Qemali proclaiming independence at Vlora (1912) before Wilhelm of Wied arrives as prince** — thirty-four years after Prizren, the declaration is finally raised over Vlora
4. **Theme 4: the League of Prizren** — when Berlin starts handing Albanian-inhabited lands to the neighbours, Albanian leaders gather at Prizren in June 1878 to stop the partition — at first backed by the sultan, dissolved by his soldiers in 1881 when it dares demand autonomy; the road from Prizren leads to the Albanian state of 1912
