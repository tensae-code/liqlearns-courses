# Hands-on — Vietnam Saigon To Ho Chi Minh City

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Saigon To Ho Chi Minh City — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 25, the city's names across 300 years (Gia Định, Sài Gòn, Sài Gòn – Chợ Lớn, renamed Hồ Chí Minh City in 1976), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
