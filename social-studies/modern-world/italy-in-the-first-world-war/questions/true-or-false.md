# Italy In The First World War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Benedict XV begs the powers to stop the "horrendous carnage" (Document) while Italy bleeds on the Isonzo and the Carso; Gilbert's Storiografia follows Germany's unrestricted submarine war widening the conflict.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§4–5 Italy enters the war (1915); A bloody two years of stalemate (1915–1916)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 2, Cap. 4 §§4–5 Italy enters the war (1915); A bloody two years of stalemate (1915–1916)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a neutral country argues itself into war — interventionists against Giolitti's "quite a lot", the secret London Pact (in the Fare storia documents) buys Italy with other peoples' lands.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §4 L'Italia dalla neutralità all'intervento

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §4 L'Italia dalla neutralità all'intervento
