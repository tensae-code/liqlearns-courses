# Hands-on — The great indigenous rebellions, 1780–1781

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The great indigenous rebellions, 1780–1781 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Las grandes rebeliones indígenas de 1780–1781, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
