# Hands-on — Cold War crises — oil, 1973, and Afghanistan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Crisis del petróleo y Afganistán, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
