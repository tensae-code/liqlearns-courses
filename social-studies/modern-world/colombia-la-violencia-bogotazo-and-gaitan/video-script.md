# Video script — Colombia — La Violencia, the Bogotazo and Gaitán

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jorge Eliécer Gaitán's assassination on 9 April 1948 fires the Bogotazo and La Violencia covers the country, emptying the countryside in a massive exodus.

Violence first erupts as conservative Mariano Ospina Pérez takes power; Jorge Eliécer Gaitán's many faces and the Bogotazo of 9 April, during the IX Pan-American Conference, are read through industrialists' and conservatives' rival versions.

Confrontation reaches the cities as the army represses harder, Gaitán's killers are demanded, by 1948 violence covers the country, and a massive exodus empties the countryside — learners interview their own elders about it.
