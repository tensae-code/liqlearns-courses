# Colombia — La Violencia, the Bogotazo and Gaitán

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Jorge Eliécer Gaitán's assassination on 9 April 1948 fires the Bogotazo and La Violencia covers the country, emptying the countryside in a massive exodus. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
