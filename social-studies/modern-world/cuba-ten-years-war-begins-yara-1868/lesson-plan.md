# Lesson plan — Cuba Ten Years War Begins Yara 1868

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Cuba Ten Years War Begins Yara 1868
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Cuba, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Carlos Manuel de Céspedes frees his slaves at La Demajagua on 10 Oct 1868 and calls them to fight for a free Cuba; the Grito de Yara opens the independence war
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **5.1 Start of the Ten Years' War** — Carlos Manuel de Céspedes frees his slaves at La Demajagua on 10 Oct 1868 and calls them to fight for a free Cuba; the Grito de Yara opens the independence war
2. **Ch.5 activities: great men and facts of the Ten Years' War, with document fragments** — pupils read a hero's own words, then answer what Céspedes, Bayamo and Guáimaro meant
3. **Unit guidance, Part II Colonial exploitation (Units 2–7, conquest to the War of 1895)** — unit-by-unit guidance follows the textbook's arc from Velázquez to Weyler, stressing personalities and notable facts as the teaching vehicle
4. **3.1–3.2 Situation of Cuba in 1868; start of the national liberation movement** — taxes, crisis and humiliation converge in 1868; Céspedes's Demajagua rising turns grievance into war
5. **2.1 The Revolution of 1868** — the Ten Years' War treated whole — Yara, Guáimaro, the invasion attempts, Zanjón and Baraguá in one analytical chapter
6. **Unit 2 suggestions: independence struggles against Spanish colonial rule 1868–1898, incl. the Revolution of 1895** — two wars are taught as one independence process with a pause, not two separate wars

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
