# Hands-on — Cuba Ten Years War Begins Yara 1868

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 5.1 Start of the Ten Years' War, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
