# The founding of the other Gulf states

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The same Arab migrations that founded Kuwait settled the whole Gulf coast, and founding leaders in each land raised the states learners know today. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Birth of the other Arab Gulf states

Arab migrations settle the whole coast, and founding leaders in each land raise the political bodies that become today's gulf states.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
