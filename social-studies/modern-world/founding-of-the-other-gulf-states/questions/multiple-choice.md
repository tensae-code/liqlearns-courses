# The founding of the other Gulf states — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Today's Gulf states grew from —
a) political bodies raised by founding leaders as Arab migrations settled the coast  b) a single empire's decree  c) European map-drawing alone
**Answer: a) political bodies raised by founding leaders as Arab migrations settled the coast** — founding leaders raise the political bodies that become today's Gulf states

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The migrations settled —
a) the whole Gulf coast  b) only Kuwait  c) only the islands
**Answer: a) the whole Gulf coast** — Arab migrations settle the whole coast

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Each Gulf land's state was raised by —
a) its own founding leaders  b) the same single ruler  c) pearl merchants
**Answer: a) its own founding leaders** — founding leaders in each land
