## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§33. The Kazakh SSR's Declaration of state sovereignty** — October 1990 — the republic claims its laws outrank the Union's
2. **§34. Socio-political movements in Kazakhstan; closing the Semipalatinsk nuclear test site** — the Nevada–Semipalatinsk movement forces the polygon's closure in 1991
3. **§35. The crisis of the Soviet political system; the "August coup" and the USSR's collapse** — the 1991 putsch fails; the Union dissolves beneath the republics
4. **§36. The formation of the statehood of independent Kazakhstan** — 16 December 1991 — the last republic to leave proclaims independence
5. **§16 The proclamation of the independence of the Republic of Kazakhstan** — 16 December 1991 — independence is proclaimed
