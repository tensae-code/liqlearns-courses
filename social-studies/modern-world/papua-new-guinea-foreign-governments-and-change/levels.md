# Papua New Guinea — foreign governments and change — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Governments and change in PNG / Impact of foreign government actions in PNG / Governments' reactions and contributions to change and sustainability
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — colonial rulers set out to transform peoples they called backward — western schooling erodes traditional knowledge even as laws like corpse-burial protect health; English becomes the language of government, and today's governments still react to change with laws such as the 2016 Cybercrime Code
