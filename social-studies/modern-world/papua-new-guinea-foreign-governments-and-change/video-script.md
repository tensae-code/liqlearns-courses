# Video script — Papua New Guinea — foreign governments and change

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colonial rulers set out to transform peoples they called backward — western schooling erodes traditional knowledge even as laws like corpse-burial protect health; English becomes the language of government, and today's governments still react to change with laws such as the 2016 Cybercrime Code.

Intervention never ended — it changed costume: Australian police partnerships, the Manus asylum programme, WHO immunisation and aid from Australia, New Zealand, the EU and UN agencies, judged by historical example.

Foreign governments, interest groups, multinationals and powerful individuals create, maintain and change Pacific power systems — the class investigates who holds the region's strings.

Foreign money and muscle keep steering the region — UN, EU, IMF and World Bank support, and when Solomon Islands collapses into ethnic conflict, Australia and New Zealand step in to restore peace.

"development" itself has a history — the field built to lift previously colonised countries out of poverty becomes a world industry of classifications, policies and agendas that Asia and the Pacific must answer to.

PNG looks outward — the "Look North" policy, NGOs, multinationals and aid donors in the country, and two rival verdicts on aid itself: modernisation against neo-colonial dependency.
