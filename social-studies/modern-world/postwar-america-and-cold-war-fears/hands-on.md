# Hands-on — Postwar America and Cold War fears, 1945–1960

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The story on a line
Draw a line. Mark this lesson's parts on it in order: Veterans come home to the American Dream; The Cold War freezes policy; McCarthyism hunts at home; Underneath, the civil rights struggle gathers force. Under each mark, write in your own words what happened and what changed.

## Tell it walking
Walk the line slowly. At each mark, stop and say that part of the story out loud, as if you were telling it to a younger learner who has never heard it.

## One hard question
Write the hardest question you can ask about this lesson. Swap with a companion: answer theirs, and let them answer yours — from the lesson.
