# Lesson plan — Postwar America and Cold War fears, 1945–1960

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Postwar America and Cold War fears, 1945–1960
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (United States, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Veterans came home to suburbs and televisions — the American Dream at full shine. Underneath: a frozen Cold War abroad, a hunt for enemies at home, and a freedom struggle gathering force.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Veterans come home to the American Dream** — Veterans came home to the American Dream of suburbs and televisions — houses, cars, appliances and babies, prosperity as a national promise.
2. **The Cold War freezes policy; McCarthyism hunts at home** — The Cold War froze foreign policy into a permanent standoff with the Soviet Union.
3. **Underneath, the civil rights struggle gathers force** — Underneath the prosperity the African American struggle for civil rights gathered force (§28.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
