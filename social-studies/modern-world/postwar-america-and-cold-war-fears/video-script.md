# Video script — Postwar America and Cold War fears, 1945–1960

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Veterans came home to suburbs and televisions — the American Dream at full shine. Underneath: a frozen Cold War abroad, a hunt for enemies at home, and a freedom struggle gathering force.

Veterans came home to the American Dream of suburbs and televisions — houses, cars, appliances and babies, prosperity as a national promise. For those let inside it, the postwar years glittered.

The Cold War froze foreign policy into a permanent standoff with the Soviet Union. At home the fear turned inward: McCarthyism hunted supposed enemies inside America's own institutions, and accusation itself became a weapon.

Underneath the prosperity the African American struggle for civil rights gathered force (§28.5). The Dream's exclusions were being named — the movement that would break into the open in the next decade was already marching.
