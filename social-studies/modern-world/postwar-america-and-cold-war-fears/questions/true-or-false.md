# Postwar America and Cold War fears, 1945–1960 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Postwar prosperity reached every American equally.
**Answer: False** — Underneath it, the struggle for civil rights gathered against the Dream's exclusions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cold War fear shaped both foreign policy and life at home.
**Answer: True** — It froze foreign policy and fed McCarthyism at home.
