# Postwar America and Cold War fears, 1945–1960 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Postwar prosperity is pictured as —
a) Breadlines  b) Suburbs and televisions  c) Dust storms
**Answer: b) Suburbs and televisions** — the American Dream of suburbs and televisions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Cold War was a standoff mainly with —
a) Britain  b) The Soviet Union  c) Mexico
**Answer: b) The Soviet Union** — the Cold War freezes foreign policy.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** McCarthyism was —
a) A jobs programme  b) A hunt for enemies at home  c) A suburb
**Answer: b) A hunt for enemies at home** — McCarthyism hunts enemies at home.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Gathering force underneath the prosperity was —
a) The civil rights struggle  b) The Populists  c) Prohibition
**Answer: a) The civil rights struggle** — the African American struggle for civil rights gathers force.
