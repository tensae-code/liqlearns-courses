# The Progressive Movement, 1890–1920 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Progressivism began only in the White House and never in the streets or press.
**Answer: False** — Muckrakers and grassroots reformers came first; it reached the White House later.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Trust-busting and regulation marked the Progressive presidencies.
**Answer: True** — Trust-busting, regulation and reform from the presidency itself.
