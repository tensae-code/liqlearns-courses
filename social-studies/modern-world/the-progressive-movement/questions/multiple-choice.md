# The Progressive Movement, 1890–1920 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Muckrakers were —
a) Farmers  b) Journalists exposing corruption and squalor  c) Railroad owners
**Answer: b) Journalists exposing corruption and squalor** — muckrakers attack corruption, monopoly and city squalor.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Progressivism attacked all of these EXCEPT —
a) Corruption  b) Monopoly  c) City squalor were left alone
**Answer: c) City squalor were left alone** — city squalor was attacked, not left alone.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** New voices rose in this era for —
a) Women and African Americans  b) Only bankers  c) European kings
**Answer: a) Women and African Americans** — new voices rise for women and African Americans.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Progressivism reached the White House with —
a) Jackson and Lincoln  b) Roosevelt and Wilson  c) Hoover and Coolidge
**Answer: b) Roosevelt and Wilson** — Theodore Roosevelt and Woodrow Wilson.
