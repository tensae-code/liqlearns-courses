# Video script — The Progressive Movement, 1890–1920

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Muckrakers dug up the filth, grassroots reformers organised, new voices rose for women and African Americans — and Progressivism walked into the White House.

Muckrakers — journalists who raked up the muck — attacked corruption, monopoly and city squalor in print. Grassroots reformers organised beneath them: Progressivism began as citizens refusing the Gilded Age as normal.

New voices rose for women and African Americans — demanding the vote, an end to lynching and exclusion, and a place in the reforming nation. Progressivism's promise and its limits both show here: it widened who could speak, unevenly.

Progressivism reached the White House with Theodore Roosevelt and Woodrow Wilson — trust-busting, regulation and reform from the presidency itself. Reform had travelled from magazine exposé to federal power.
