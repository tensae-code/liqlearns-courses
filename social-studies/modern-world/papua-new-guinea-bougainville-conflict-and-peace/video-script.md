# Video script — Papua New Guinea — the Bougainville conflict and peace

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Oceania's biggest conflict — ten years of civil war between the PNG Government and the Bougainville Revolutionary Army, an estimated 15,000–20,000 dead by 1999, and a slow reconciliation still being woven.

1988–1998, government against the BRA; 15,000–20,000 Bougainvillean lives; the 1998 Peace Agreement trades the guns for an Autonomous Bougainville Government — students analyse causes, effects and the conflict's mark on community and nation.

A report on the Crisis's significant events and what they did to every stakeholder and to the national economy — the nation's deepest wound studied as current history.
