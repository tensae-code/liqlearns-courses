# Hands-on — Vietnam Tay Son Uprising

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Tay Son Uprising — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 8, the raising of Tây Sơn, 1771, and the fall of the Nguyễn lords, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
