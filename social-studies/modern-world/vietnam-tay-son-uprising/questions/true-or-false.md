# Vietnam Tay Son Uprising — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in Đàng Trong the boy-lord Nguyễn Phúc Thuần "loves only play and singing" while the regent Trương Phúc Loan, in the quoted history's words, "sells offices and buys verdicts" for over thirty years; in 1771 the three brothers Nguyễn Nhạc, Nguyễn Huệ and Nguyễn Lữ — Nguyễn Huệ with eyes like lightning and a voice like a bell — raise the banner at Tây Sơn under the slogan "take from the rich, give to the poor"; squeezed between the Trịnh in the north and the Nguyễn in the south, Nguyễn Nhạc makes a temporary peace with the Trịnh, strikes Gia Định four times, and in 1777 captures and kills the Nguyễn lord — only Nguyễn Ánh escapes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 8, the raising of Tây Sơn, 1771, and the fall of the Nguyễn lords

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 8, the raising of Tây Sơn, 1771, and the fall of the Nguyễn lords

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a highland peasant rising unifies a divided country and beats two empires in four years.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 8 The Tây Sơn movement: against the corrupt Trương Phúc Loan regency, three brothers raise the flag in spring 1771; the table runs Quy Nhơn 1773, the Nguyễn lords fallen 1777, 50,000 Siamese smashed at Rạch Gầm–Xoài Mút 1785, Phú Xuân and the Trịnh 1786, and Quang Trung's Tết victory over 290,000 Qing troops in 1789
