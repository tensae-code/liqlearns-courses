# Video script — Vietnam Tay Son Uprising

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Vietnam's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

in Đàng Trong the boy-lord Nguyễn Phúc Thuần "loves only play and singing" while the regent Trương Phúc Loan, in the quoted history's words, "sells offices and buys verdicts" for over thirty years; in 1771 the three brothers Nguyễn Nhạc, Nguyễn Huệ and Nguyễn Lữ — Nguyễn Huệ with eyes like lightning and a voice like a bell — raise the banner at Tây Sơn under the slogan "take from the rich, give to the poor"; squeezed between the Trịnh in the north and the Nguyễn in the south, Nguyễn Nhạc makes a temporary peace with the Trịnh, strikes Gia Định four times, and in 1777 captures and kills the Nguyễn lord — only Nguyễn Ánh escapes
