# Lesson plan — Colombia's internal conflict — guerrillas, paramilitaries and peace talks

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Colombia's internal conflict — guerrillas, paramilitaries and peace talks
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Colombia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** from Marquetalia and the FARC's expansion to the paramilitaries and forced displacement, Colombia's internal conflict is also the story of peace talks — the M-19's negotiated demobilisation is the model case
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Módulo 5 / Guías 13–14: The internal conflict and the peace dialogues** — the army attacks Marquetalia, the "independent republic"; the FARC expand on a war economy, paramilitary units spread in the late 1980s and grow from guerrilla ally-enemy into a power of their own, and the M-19's successful negotiation — demobilisation, reduced sentences, a political party — is the model peace case, with the AUC talks and their law beside it
2. **Guía 6: Why is there violence, unemployment and poverty in a resource-rich Colombia?** — forced displacement is named as the conflict's rural face, and learners must propose definitions and solutions of their own
3. **Capítulo 9 / Temas 28–29: Colombia from the nineties** — the violence wave does not stop; violence-generating agents are catalogued as the government opens peace offensives and the economy transforms

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
