# Colombia's internal conflict — guerrillas, paramilitaries and peace talks

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: from Marquetalia and the FARC's expansion to the paramilitaries and forced displacement, Colombia's internal conflict is also the story of peace talks — the M-19's negotiated demobilisation is the model case. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Módulo 5 / Guías 13–14: The internal conflict and the peace dialogues

The army attacks Marquetalia, the "independent republic"; the FARC expand on a war economy, paramilitary units spread in the late 1980s and grow from guerrilla ally-enemy into a power of their own, and the M-19's successful negotiation — demobilisation, reduced sentences, a political party — is the model peace case, with the AUC talks and their law beside it
