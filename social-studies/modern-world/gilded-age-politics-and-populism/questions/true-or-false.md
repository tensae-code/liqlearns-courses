# Gilded Age politics and populism, 1870–1900 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Populists organised against railroads, banks and the gold squeeze.
**Answer: True** — Farmers organised against railroads, banks and the gold standard.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Gilded Age politics was free of corruption.
**Answer: False** — Corruption and patronage dominate postbellum politics.
