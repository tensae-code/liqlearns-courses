# Gilded Age politics and populism, 1870–1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Patronage means —
a) Handing jobs and contracts to supporters  b) Free elections only  c) A gold coin
**Answer: a) Handing jobs and contracts to supporters** — patronage dominates postbellum politics.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The two questions dividing the parties were —
a) Tariff and gold  b) Railroads and canals  c) North and South
**Answer: a) Tariff and gold** — the tariff and gold questions divide the parties.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Populist movement was a revolt of —
a) Bankers  b) Indebted farmers  c) Planters
**Answer: b) Indebted farmers** — indebted farmers revolt in the Populist movement.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1890s closed in —
a) Peace and plenty for all  b) Strikes and social unrest  c) A return to monarchy
**Answer: b) Strikes and social unrest** — the 1890s close in strikes and social unrest.
