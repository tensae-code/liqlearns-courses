# Gilded Age politics and populism, 1870–1900 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is the age called 'gilded' rather than 'golden'?
**Answer: Gilding is a thin gold surface over cheaper metal — the age glittered while corruption and patronage ran underneath (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who were the Populists, and what squeezed them?
**Answer: Indebted farmers, squeezed by debt, railroads, banks and the gold standard (any close wording).**
