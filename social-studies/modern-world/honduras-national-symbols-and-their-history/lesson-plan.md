## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lección 9 Símbolos patrios de Honduras** — flag, shield, anthem, pine, orchid, macaw and deer are introduced one by one, each standing for a piece of the country, and the class asks what is celebrated on 15 September
2. **Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia** — the symbols themselves tell the story — the National Anthem narrates what happened from the arrival of the Spanish to independence, and the class is sent to find where the anthem speaks of Lempira
3. **Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres: los símbolos** — the flag's five stars are the five Central American countries, the anthem (words by Augusto C. Coello, music by Carlos Hartling) narrates historical events, and the shield has changed several times through time
4. **Lección 9 Identidad Nacional: los símbolos** — greater symbols (flag, shield, anthem) and lesser symbols (orchid, scarlet macaw, white-tailed deer, pine, official map) are classified, each representing the country's history, culture and natural wealth
5. **Lección 30 El patriotismo de América Central** — the seven countries' shields, flags and flowers are set side by side, and 15 September is kept by five of them as the shared independence feast
