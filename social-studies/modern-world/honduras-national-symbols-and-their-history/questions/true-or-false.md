# Honduras National Symbols And Their History — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Flag, shield, anthem, pine, orchid, macaw and deer are introduced one by one, each standing for a piece of the country, and the class asks what is celebrated on 15 September.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 9 Símbolos patrios de Honduras

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Primer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 9 Símbolos patrios de Honduras

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The symbols themselves tell the story — the National Anthem narrates what happened from the arrival of the Spanish to independence, and the class is sent to find where the anthem speaks of Lempira.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Primer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The flag's five stars are the five Central American countries, the anthem (words by Augusto C. Coello, music by Carlos Hartling) narrates historical events, and the shield has changed several times through time.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres: los símbolos

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres: los símbolos

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Greater symbols (flag, shield, anthem) and lesser symbols (orchid, scarlet macaw, white-tailed deer, pine, official map) are classified, each representing the country's history, culture and natural wealth.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 9 Identidad Nacional: los símbolos

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 9 Identidad Nacional: los símbolos

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The seven countries' shields, flags and flowers are set side by side, and 15 September is kept by five of them as the shared independence feast.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 30 El patriotismo de América Central

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 30 El patriotismo de América Central
