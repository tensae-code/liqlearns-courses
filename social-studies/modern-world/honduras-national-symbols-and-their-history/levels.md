# Honduras National Symbols And Their History — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 9 Símbolos patrios de Honduras
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — flag, shield, anthem, pine, orchid, macaw and deer are introduced one by one, each standing for a piece of the country, and the class asks what is celebrated on 15 September

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 28 Nos identificamos y así es nuestra historia
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the symbols themselves tell the story — the National Anthem narrates what happened from the arrival of the Spanish to independence, and the class is sent to find where the anthem speaks of Lempira

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 9 Símbolos patrios y hombres ilustres: los símbolos
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the flag's five stars are the five Central American countries, the anthem (words by Augusto C. Coello, music by Carlos Hartling) narrates historical events, and the shield has changed several times through time

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 9 Identidad Nacional: los símbolos
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — greater symbols (flag, shield, anthem) and lesser symbols (orchid, scarlet macaw, white-tailed deer, pine, official map) are classified, each representing the country's history, culture and natural wealth

## Sub lesson 5 — Lección 30 El patriotismo de América Central
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the seven countries' shields, flags and flowers are set side by side, and 15 September is kept by five of them as the shared independence feast
