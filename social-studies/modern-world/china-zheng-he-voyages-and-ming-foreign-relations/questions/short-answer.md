# China Zheng He Voyages And Ming Foreign Relations — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ming foreign relations.
**Answer: from 1405 to 1433 Zheng He's fleets — 200 ships, 27,000 men, treasure ships carrying a thousand — visit 30 countries to East Africa and the Red Sea, signalling by flags by day and lanterns by night, trading silk and porcelain and bringing 16 nations' envoys home (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 15: Ming foreign relations, printed p. 70.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Zheng He and Columbus compared.
**Answer: pupils set Zheng He's great treasure fleets of 1405 beside Columbus's three small ships of 1492 and ask why the two voyages led to such different worlds (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Unit 6 Inquiry: Zheng He and Columbus compared, printed p. 52.**
