# China Zheng He Voyages And Ming Foreign Relations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From 1405 to 1433 Zheng He's fleets — 200 ships, 27,000 men, treasure ships carrying a thousand — visit 30 countries to East Africa and the Red Sea, signalling by flags by day and lanterns by night, trading silk and porcelain and bringing 16 nations' envoys home.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ming foreign relations

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ming foreign relations

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils set Zheng He's great treasure fleets of 1405 beside Columbus's three small ships of 1492 and ask why the two voyages led to such different worlds.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Zheng He and Columbus compared

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Zheng He and Columbus compared
