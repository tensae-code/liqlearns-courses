# Postwar Greece Reconstruction Junta And 1974 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Marshall aid, emigration and growth rebuild Greece until the colonels' junta of 1967–74 ends in the Cyprus tragedy and democracy's return.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit E, ch. 10 The postwar reconstruction of Greece (1950–1974)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Ιστορία ΣΤ΄ Δημοτικού — Ιστορία του Νεότερου και Σύγχρονου Κόσμου (History, Grade 6 Primary: History of the Modern and Contemporary World)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit E, ch. 10 The postwar reconstruction of Greece (1950–1974)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lambrakis's murder, the apostasia, the colonels' tanks, the Polytechnic uprising and the Cyprus collapse of the junta.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §55 Sharpening of the political crisis and the dictatorship of 21 April 1967 (1963–1974)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία Γ΄ Γυμνασίου — Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Modern and Contemporary History, Grade 9 Gymnasium)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §55 Sharpening of the political crisis and the dictatorship of 21 April 1967 (1963–1974)
