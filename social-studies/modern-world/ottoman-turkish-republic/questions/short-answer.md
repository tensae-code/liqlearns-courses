# The Turkish Republic is recognised — Mudanya and Lausanne — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give both signatures, their towns and dates.
**Answer: Mudanya, 11 October 1922 (armistice); Lausanne, 24 July 1923 (peace treaty) (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What does 'what the sword won, the pen keeps' mean?
**Answer: That the victories of the war (Sakarya and the offensives) were secured permanently by diplomacy at Lausanne (any close wording).**
