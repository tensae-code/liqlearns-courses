# The Turkish Republic is recognised — Mudanya and Lausanne — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lausanne won the new state the world's recognition as an equal.
**Answer: True** — the treaty takes its seat among nations.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mudanya was the final peace treaty.
**Answer: False** — Mudanya is the armistice; Lausanne is the peace.
