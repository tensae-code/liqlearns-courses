# The Turkish Republic is recognised — Mudanya and Lausanne — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Mudanya Armistice (11 October 1922) —
a) Silenced the guns  b) Founded the republic  c) Partitioned Anatolia
**Answer: a) Silenced the guns** — the fighting ends first.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Treaty of Lausanne was signed on —
a) 11 October 1922  b) 24 July 1923  c) 30 August 1922
**Answer: b) 24 July 1923** — the peace and recognition.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Lausanne buried —
a) The Sèvres partition  b) The Grand National Assembly  c) The Mudanya Armistice
**Answer: a) The Sèvres partition** — the death warrant undone.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The book's beat pairs —
a) The pen and the printing press  b) The sword's winnings and the pen's keeping of them  c) Two armistices
**Answer: b) The sword's winnings and the pen's keeping of them** — Sakarya and Lausanne.
