# Video script — The Turkish Republic is recognised — Mudanya and Lausanne

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Wars end twice. At Mudanya, on 11 October 1922, the armistice silences the guns of Turkey's National Struggle.

Then the pen must win what the sword won. At Lausanne, the new state faces the powers that had signed Sèvres — and on 24 July 1923 the Treaty of Lausanne wins the peace and the world's recognition. The partition is buried.

What the sword won at Sakarya, the pen keeps at Lausanne.
