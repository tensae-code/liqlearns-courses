# Hands-on — The Turkish Republic is recognised — Mudanya and Lausanne

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Armistice and treaty

Draw two documents: an armistice (guns crossed out) and a treaty (a map made whole). Label each with its town and date — Mudanya 1922, Lausanne 1923 — and one line on what each one does that the other cannot.

## Sèvres against Lausanne

Draw the homeland's map twice: cut into pieces (Sèvres) and whole (Lausanne). Write one sentence a delegate might say at Lausanne while pointing at the whole map.

## The pen keeps it

Write a four-line speech for the Turkish delegate on 24 July 1923: what the sword won, what the pen now keeps, and what the signatures mean for a child born that day.
