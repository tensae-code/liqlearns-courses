# Lesson plan — The Turkish Republic is recognised — Mudanya and Lausanne

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Turkish Republic is recognised — Mudanya and Lausanne
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 2, Section E, The Mudanya Armistice and the Lausanne Peace Treaty: Mudanya (11 October 1922) silences the guns; Lausanne (24 July 1923) wins the peace and the world's recognition.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: what the sword won at Sakarya, the pen keeps at Lausanne. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the Mudanya Armistice and its date: 11 October 1922 — it silences the guns.
2. Name the Treaty of Lausanne and its date: 24 July 1923 — the peace and the world's recognition.
3. Say what Lausanne buries: the Sèvres partition of the homeland.
4. Explain the book's beat in their own words: what the sword won, the pen keeps.
