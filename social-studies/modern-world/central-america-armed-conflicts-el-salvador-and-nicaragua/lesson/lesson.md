## Sub lesson 1 — Two neighbours' wars — Chapultepec and the ballot box

Guatemala's Grade 4 book sets its own war beside its neighbours'. El Salvador's FMLN war ends at Chapultepec in 1992. Nicaragua's Sandinistas topple the Somoza dynasty in 1979, fight the Contras through the 1980s, and lose the 1990 election to Violeta Chamorro — a war ended not by victory, but by a vote. Central America's conflicts, the book shows, ended in different ways.
