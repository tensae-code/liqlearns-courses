# Central America — armed conflicts: El Salvador and Nicaragua — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** El Salvador's war ended at Chapultepec in —
a) 1992  b) 1979  c) 1996
**Answer: a) 1992** — the peace year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Sandinistas toppled —
a) the Somoza dynasty  b) the Spanish crown  c) United Fruit
**Answer: a) the Somoza dynasty** — the dynasty.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Contras fought against —
a) the Sandinistas  b) the FMLN  c) Carrera
**Answer: a) the Sandinistas** — their opponents.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Nicaragua's war path ended in 1990 with —
a) an election won by Violeta Chamorro  b) a scorched-earth offensive  c) annexation to Mexico
**Answer: a) an election won by Violeta Chamorro** — the ballot-box ending.
