# Central America — armed conflicts: El Salvador and Nicaragua — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did El Salvador's and Nicaragua's wars end differently?
**Answer: El Salvador's ended in a peace accord at Chapultepec (1992); Nicaragua's Sandinistas lost the 1990 election to Chamorro (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the armed groups in the two neighbours' wars.
**Answer: FMLN in El Salvador; Sandinistas and Contras in Nicaragua (any close wording).**
