# From Maastricht To The European Union — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Maastricht turns the Community into a Union with a single currency project (the chapter's maps of members and candidates); Tulli's Storiografia (unit) weighs the Euro-Atlantic relations that frame it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 6, Cap. 21 §4 Europe after the Cold War: still more integration

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 6, Cap. 21 §4 Europe after the Cold War: still more integration

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Maastricht builds the Union and enlargement carries it east; European citizenship (the module's Civica box) is the promise the difficult road must keep.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 14 §§8–9 Dalla Cee all'Unione europea; Il difficile cammino dell'Unione allargata

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
