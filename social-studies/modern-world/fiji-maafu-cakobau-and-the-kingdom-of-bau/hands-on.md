# Hands-on — Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
