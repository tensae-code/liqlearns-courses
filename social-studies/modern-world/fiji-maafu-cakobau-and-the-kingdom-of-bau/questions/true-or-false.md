# Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tongan prince Ma'afu arrives in 1847 and builds a power base in Lau; Cakobau rises at Bau, planters Wilkinson and Thurston push a joint Fijian-European government, rival annexation schemes are weighed and rejected, and the 1871 Kingdom of Bau constitution makes Cakobau king of Fiji outside the northern and eastern confederacy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau
