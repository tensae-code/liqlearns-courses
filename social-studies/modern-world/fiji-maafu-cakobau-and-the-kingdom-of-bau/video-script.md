# Video script — Ma'afu, Cakobau and the Kingdom of Bau

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tongan prince Ma'afu arrives in 1847 and builds a power base in Lau; Cakobau rises at Bau, planters Wilkinson and Thurston push a joint Fijian-European government, rival annexation schemes are weighed and rejected, and the 1871 Kingdom of Bau constitution makes Cakobau king of Fiji outside the northern and eastern confederacy. Early Fijian society governs itself through chiefdoms; Ma'afu extends his influence even to Beqa in opposition to Cakobau, yet the Bauan kingdom leads Fiji only because of Tongan intervention, and Cakobau's Tui Viti claim outruns his real power.
