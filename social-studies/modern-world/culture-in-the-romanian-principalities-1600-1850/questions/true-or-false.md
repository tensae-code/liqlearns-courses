# Culture In The Romanian Principalities 1600 1850 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ureche, Costin, Cantemir and Cantacuzino found Romanian historiography; primers are printed, and in 1835 Iași inaugurates its Academy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lecția 27: Cultura în Principatele Române

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a VII-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lecția 27: Cultura în Principatele Române

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Over 300 printed titles appear in 18th-century Moldavia and Wallachia; boyars hire foreign tutors for Greek, French and German, and Cantemir's learning wins European renown.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §50: Evoluţia culturii Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor şi universală: Epoca modernă. Manual pentru clasa a XI-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §50: Evoluţia culturii Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII
