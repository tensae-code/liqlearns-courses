# China Ethnic Unity And Regional Autonomy

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: 56 peoples under regional autonomy — Tibet's serfs emancipated, border regions schooled and doctored — presented as one family As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Great ethnic unity

56 peoples under regional autonomy — Tibet's serfs emancipated, border regions schooled and doctored — presented as one family

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Today's Chinese ethnic policy

56 recognised peoples, five autonomous regions and equality-and-unity written into the constitution's first articles

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
