# China Ethnic Unity And Regional Autonomy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Great ethnic unity.
**Answer: 56 peoples under regional autonomy — Tibet's serfs emancipated, border regions schooled and doctored — presented as one family (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Lesson 12: Great ethnic unity, printed p. 60.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Today's Chinese ethnic policy.
**Answer: 56 recognised peoples, five autonomous regions and equality-and-unity written into the constitution's first articles (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 选择性必修1 国家制度与社会治理 (Senior High History, Selective Compulsory 1: State Systems and Social Governance)* (China), Lesson 13: Today's Chinese ethnic policy, printed p. 75.**
