# China Ethnic Unity And Regional Autonomy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** 56 peoples under regional autonomy — Tibet's serfs emancipated, border regions schooled and doctored — presented as one family.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Great ethnic unity

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Great ethnic unity

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 56 recognised peoples, five autonomous regions and equality-and-unity written into the constitution's first articles.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Today's Chinese ethnic policy

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 选择性必修1 国家制度与社会治理 (Senior High History, Selective Compulsory 1: State Systems and Social Governance)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Today's Chinese ethnic policy
