# Baroque Spain — art of a golden-age court — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The court fills its palaces and monasteries with grave, intense painting.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Spain's Habsburg court, rich on American silver and strict in faith, fills its palaces and monasteries with grave, intense painting — an empire's pride and melancholy in one frame.
