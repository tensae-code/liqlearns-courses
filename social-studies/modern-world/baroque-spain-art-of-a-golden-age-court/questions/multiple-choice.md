# Baroque Spain — art of a golden-age court — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Palaces and monasteries fill  b) Rich on silver, strict in faith  c) Pride and melancholy
**Answer: b) Rich on silver, strict in faith** — Spain's Habsburg court is rich on American silver and strict in faith.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Palaces and monasteries fill  b) Rich on silver, strict in faith  c) Pride and melancholy
**Answer: a) Palaces and monasteries fill** — The court fills its palaces and monasteries with grave, intense painting.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Rich on silver, strict in faith  b) Palaces and monasteries fill  c) Pride and melancholy
**Answer: c) Pride and melancholy** — The painting holds an empire's pride and melancholy in one frame.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Spain's Habsburg court, rich on American silver and strict in faith, fills its palaces and monasteries with grave, intense painting — an empire's pride and melancholy in one frame  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
