# Baroque Spain — art of a golden-age court

## Do it first

Before you read on, picture the scene this lesson is about, from its title alone: Baroque Spain — art of a golden-age court. What do you expect to happen, and what do you expect to change? Hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Rich on silver, strict in faith

Spain's Habsburg court is rich on American silver and strict in faith.

This is a real turn in the story, not a detail at its edge: hold it by its place in the order — what came before it, and what it makes possible next.

And finally: The painting holds an empire's pride and melancholy in one frame. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
