# Asean Founded And Vietnam Joins — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 21, the founding of ASEAN
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — on 8 August 1967 in Bangkok, Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines and Singapore sign ASEAN into being to keep the region peaceful and develop together

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 21, Vietnam in ASEAN
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — on 28 July 1995 Vietnam becomes ASEAN's seventh member, opening markets, investment and cultural exchange, and later chairs the association in 2010 and 2020

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 12, Southeast Asia wins independence and founds ASEAN
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Indonesia and Vietnam seize the moment of Japan's surrender in August 1945, Laos that October; the Philippines follows (July 1946), Burma (January 1948), Indonesia's recognition (1949), Cambodia (1953), Malaya (August 1957), Singapore's self-rule (1959) and Brunei (1984); the book's building table contrasts inward industrialisation in the 1950s–60s with the outward, export-led turn of the 1970s–80s that makes Singapore an "Asian dragon"; on 8 August 1967 five countries sign the Bangkok Declaration founding ASEAN — its aims quoted here — and the 1976 Bali Declaration of Concord sets the principles of sovereignty, non-interference, no force and peaceful settlement

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 19, ASEAN from six to ten and the Community
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — with the Cold War over and the Cambodia question settled, ASEAN expands: Vietnam becomes the seventh member in July 1995, Laos and Myanmar join in July 1997, and Cambodia's admission in April 1999 — its ceremony photographed in Hanoi — makes all Southeast Asia one organisation for the first time; the 1992 framework and AFTA agreements lay the basis of an economic community, the 2007 ASEAN Charter gives legal personality, the Community is officially founded in 2015 on three pillars (Political-Security, Economic, Socio-Cultural), and RCEP, signed in 2020 and in force from January 2022, creates the world's largest free-trade area

## Sub lesson 5 — Bài 4 ASEAN founded: after two short-lived forerunners (ASA 1961, MAPHILINDO 1963) die of bilateral quarrels, the foreign ministers of Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand sign the Bangkok Declaration on 8-8-1967, pledging growth, social progress and regional peace under the UN Charter
