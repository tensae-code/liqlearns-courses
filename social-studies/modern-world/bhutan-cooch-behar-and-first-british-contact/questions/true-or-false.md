# Bhutan — Cooch Behar and first British contact — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bhutan's capture of Cooch Behar's raja in 1772 brings Captain John Jones's British troops south, Bhutan loses the foothill lands, exiled Desi Zhidar flees to Tibet, and the Ten Points Treaty of 25 April 1774 turns enemies into trading friends
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Six, Battle of Cooch Behar and the Anglo-Bhutanese Treaty 1774).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** told again at Class X depth, the Cooch Behar intervention and first clash with the East India Company end in the 1774 friendship treaty that abolishes duties on Bhutanese traders in Rangpur and opens exclusive trade
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Two, Bhutan and British India (opening sections)).
