# Political storms, 1968–1980 — Vietnam and Watergate — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The old coalitions fractured along —
a) Weather lines  b) Lines of race, region, gender and generation  c) Railroad lines
**Answer: b) Lines of race, region, gender and generation** — identity politics fractures the old coalitions.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Vietnam's effect at home was to —
a) Unite everyone  b) Divide the nation  c) Be ignored
**Answer: b) Divide the nation** — Vietnam spirals down and divides the nation.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Watergate ended with —
a) A president's resignation  b) A war victory  c) A new constitution
**Answer: a) A president's resignation** — Watergate brings down a president.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** By 1980, trust in government was —
a) At its highest ever  b) In ruins  c) Unchanged
**Answer: b) In ruins** — the seventies end with trust in government in ruins.
