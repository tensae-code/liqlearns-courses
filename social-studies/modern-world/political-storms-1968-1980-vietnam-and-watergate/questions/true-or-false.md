# Political storms, 1968–1980 — Vietnam and Watergate — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Watergate was only about a war overseas.
**Answer: False** — Watergate was a break-in and cover-up that brought down a president.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Vietnam divided Americans at home as well as being fought abroad.
**Answer: True** — Vietnam spirals down and divides the nation.
