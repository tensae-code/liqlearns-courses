# Mongolia 1921 — the People's Revolution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sükhbaatar and Choibalsan's partisans, helped by the Red Army, free Urga in July 1921; the Bogd is kept as a limited monarch over a people's government.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2.8 The Mongolian revolution of 1921

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *mn-g9h Түүх IX (History IX)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2.8 The Mongolian revolution of 1921

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** serfdom and noble privilege are abolished, a constitution is drafted, and the new government begins remaking state and society.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2.9 The first measures of the people's government

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the MPP's founding, Kyakhta's fall, Ungern's capture of Urga, and the July 1921 liberation with Red Army help — independence regained, at a price.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. II.4 The victory and significance of the 1921 revolution
