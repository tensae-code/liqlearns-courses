## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch. 2.8 The Mongolian revolution of 1921** — Sükhbaatar and Choibalsan's partisans, helped by the Red Army, free Urga in July 1921; the Bogd is kept as a limited monarch over a people's government
2. **Ch. 2.9 The first measures of the people's government** — serfdom and noble privilege are abolished, a constitution is drafted, and the new government begins remaking state and society
3. **Ch. II.4 The victory and significance of the 1921 revolution** — the MPP's founding, Kyakhta's fall, Ungern's capture of Urga, and the July 1921 liberation with Red Army help — independence regained, at a price
4. **Ch. II.5 Mongolia, a limited constitutional monarchy, 1921–1924** — the Tangaragiin treaty bounds the Bogd's power; serfdom is abolished (1922), local rule democratised, and the first constitution drafted — a monarchy dissolving into a republic
