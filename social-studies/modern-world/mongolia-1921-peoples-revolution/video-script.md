Sükhbaatar and Choibalsan's partisans, helped by the Red Army, free Urga in July 1921; the Bogd is kept as a limited monarch over a people's government. serfdom and noble privilege are abolished, a constitution is drafted, and the new government begins remaking state and society. the MPP's founding, Kyakhta's fall, Ungern's capture of Urga, and the July 1921 liberation with Red Army help — independence regained, at a price. the Tangaragiin treaty bounds the Bogd's power; serfdom is abolished (1922), local rule democratised, and the first constitution drafted — a monarchy dissolving into a republic.
