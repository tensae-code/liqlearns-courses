# Video script — Fiji — missionaries, chiefs and conversion

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cross and Cargill are received at Lakeba in 1835, Hunt and Lythe follow, four Wesleyan stations stand by 1840, and conversion works through chiefs; explorer Wilkes meanwhile wins Tanoa of Bau's promise to protect foreign ships and crews. After the Battle of Kaba in 1854 Cakobau's conversion secures Methodism's advance, cession follows in 1874, Catholic missionaries arrive, and Hinduism and Islam come with the girmitiya — Hindus and Muslims cooperating at festivals despite prejudice.
