# Fiji — missionaries, chiefs and conversion — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cross and Cargill are received at Lakeba in 1835, Hunt and Lythe follow, four Wesleyan stations stand by 1840, and conversion works through chiefs; explorer Wilkes meanwhile wins Tanoa of Bau's promise to protect foreign ships and crews.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Missionaries and their influence

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Science Year 9 — Rediscovering Society* is about Fiji — education: mission schools to integration.
**Answer: False** — it is about Missionaries and their influence

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After the Battle of Kaba in 1854 Cakobau's conversion secures Methodism's advance, cession follows in 1874, Catholic missionaries arrive, and Hinduism and Islam come with the girmitiya — Hindus and Muslims cooperating at festivals despite prejudice.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 4.3 Emergence of religious groups in Fiji

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History Year 11 — Cultural Interaction, Unit 4: Religious Groups in Fiji* is about Fiji — education: mission schools to integration.
**Answer: False** — it is about 4.3 Emergence of religious groups in Fiji
