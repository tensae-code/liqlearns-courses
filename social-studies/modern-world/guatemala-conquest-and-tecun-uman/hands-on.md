# Hands-on — Guatemala — conquest and Tecún Umán

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Part 1 — First stage — Alvarado, smallpox and El Pinar

Draw or map this part from memory, then write its story beat in your own words: warned by Moctezuma's envoys, the highland lords still fail to unite; Pedro de Alvarado's army and smallpox advance together; Tecún Umán falls at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burns, and Santiago is founded at Iximche'.

## Part 2 — Second stage — the Kaqchikel rise and the Lienzo

Draw or map this part from memory, then write its story beat in your own words: the Kaqchikel, betrayed as allies, rise; Jorge de Alvarado retraces his brother's route with Quauhquechollan allies, whose weavers paint the campaign on cloth.
