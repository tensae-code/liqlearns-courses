# Lesson plan — Guatemala — conquest and Tecún Umán

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala — conquest and Tecún Umán
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Guatemala, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Pedro de Alvarado's army and smallpox advance together against highland kingdoms that fail to unite; Tecún Umán falls at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burns, Santiago is founded at Iximche', and the betrayed Kaqchikel rise in the second stage, painted by their Quauhquechollan allies on the Lienzo.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **First stage — Alvarado, smallpox and El Pinar** — warned by Moctezuma's envoys, the highland lords still fail to unite; Pedro de Alvarado's army and smallpox advance together; Tecún Umán falls at El Pinar, Q'umarkaj burns, and Santiago is founded at Iximche'
2. **Second stage — the Kaqchikel rise and the Lienzo** — the Kaqchikel, betrayed as allies, rise; Jorge de Alvarado retraces his brother's route with Quauhquechollan allies, whose weavers paint the campaign on cloth

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the Spanish commander: Pedro de Alvarado.
2. Name the disease that advanced with his army: smallpox.
3. Say where Tecún Umán fell: at El Pinar.
4. Say who rose in the second stage: the Kaqchikel, betrayed as allies.
5. Name the painted source: the Lienzo de Quauhquechollan.
6. Name the second Alvarado: Jorge de Alvarado.
