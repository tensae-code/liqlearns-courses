# Guatemala — conquest and Tecún Umán — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tecún Umán fell at —
a) El Pinar  b) Iximche' market  c) Lake Atitlán
**Answer: a) El Pinar** — the place the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Alongside Alvarado's army advanced —
a) smallpox  b) the plague of locusts  c) a flood
**Answer: a) smallpox** — the disease in the story.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Lienzo de Quauhquechollan is —
a) a campaign painted on cloth by Quauhquechollan weavers  b) a Spanish printed book  c) a stone pyramid
**Answer: a) a campaign painted on cloth by Quauhquechollan weavers** — the painted source.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the second stage, the Kaqchikel —
a) rose after being betrayed as allies  b) left for Mexico  c) became Spanish governors
**Answer: a) rose after being betrayed as allies** — their rising.
