# Guatemala — conquest and Tecún Umán — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Santiago was founded at Iximche' after Q'umarkaj burned.
**Answer: True** — the first-stage ending the book gives.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Kaqchikel stayed loyal allies throughout the conquest.
**Answer: False** — betrayed as allies, they rose.
