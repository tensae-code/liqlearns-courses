# Lesson plan — Timor-Leste — the Santa Cruz massacre, 1991

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Timor-Leste — the Santa Cruz massacre, 1991
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Timor-Leste, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Santa Cruz massacre of 12 November 1991 and its impact on the world, with the Kraras massacre recalled: young mourners march to a cemetery, soldiers open fire, and foreign journalists film the shooting — one cameraman's tape turns the world's eyes onto Timor, and the occupation can no longer be hidden.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Timor-Leste's Grade 6 national story, taught as lived memory — songs, drama, photographs, poems and interviews — as the Period 2 teacher guide teaches 1975–2002. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **12 November 1991 — the cemetery shooting** — Young mourners march to Santa Cruz cemetery on 12 November 1991 and soldiers open fire on them.
2. **The tape that turned the world's eyes** — Foreign journalists film the cemetery shooting; its impact reaches the whole world, and the earlier Kraras massacre is recalled alongside it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
