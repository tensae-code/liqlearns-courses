# Hands-on — Timor-Leste — the Santa Cruz massacre, 1991

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Being seen
Write two short lines: 'Kraras — not filmed' and 'Santa Cruz 1991 — filmed'. Under them, write one sentence on what difference being seen made.

## A date to keep
Make a small memorial card for 12 November 1991: the place, who marched, and what happened. Keep the words plain and true, as a memorial should be.
