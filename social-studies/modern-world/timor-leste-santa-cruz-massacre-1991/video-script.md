# Video script — Timor-Leste — the Santa Cruz massacre, 1991

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Timor-Leste's story as its Grade 6 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

On 12 November 1991, young mourners march to the Santa Cruz cemetery. Soldiers open fire on them there, among the graves. The book names this for what it was: a massacre — the Santa Cruz massacre — and teaches it as a turning point children must know by its date.

What makes Santa Cruz different from earlier killings is a camera. Foreign journalists film the shooting, and the tape travels: the occupation can no longer be hidden, and the massacre's impact reaches the whole world. The book also recalls the Kraras massacre alongside it — an earlier massacre the world did not see — so children learn both what happened, and what difference being seen made.
