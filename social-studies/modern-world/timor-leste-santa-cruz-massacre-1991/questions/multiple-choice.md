# Timor-Leste — the Santa Cruz massacre, 1991 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Santa Cruz massacre happened on —
a) 12 November 1991  b) 28 November 1975  c) 30 August 1999
**Answer: a) 12 November 1991** — 12 November 1991 — at Santa Cruz cemetery.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The people soldiers fired on at Santa Cruz were —
a) Young mourners marching to a cemetery  b) An invading army  c) Foreign journalists only
**Answer: a) Young mourners marching to a cemetery** — Young mourners — marching to the cemetery.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Why could the occupation no longer be hidden after Santa Cruz?
a) Foreign journalists filmed the shooting  b) The soldiers confessed  c) No one filmed anything
**Answer: a) Foreign journalists filmed the shooting** — Foreign journalists filmed it — and the tape turned the world's eyes onto Timor.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The earlier massacre the book recalls alongside Santa Cruz is —
a) The Kraras massacre  b) The Tasi-Tolu mass  c) The Soibada assembly
**Answer: a) The Kraras massacre** — The Kraras massacre — recalled in the same lesson.
