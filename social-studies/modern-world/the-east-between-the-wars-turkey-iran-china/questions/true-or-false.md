# The East Between The Wars Turkey Iran China — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Atatürk remakes Turkey, Reza Shah drives Iran to modernise, and China tears itself between nationalists and communists.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §9 The East's traditional societies in the 1920s–30s

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §9 The East's traditional societies in the 1920s–30s

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From Turkey to Japan and China, eastern societies wrestle with whether to copy the West or defend their own ways as empires press in.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 2, Lessons 27–30: the traditional societies of the East in the 1920s–1930s

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 2, Lessons 27–30: the traditional societies of the East in the 1920s–1930s
