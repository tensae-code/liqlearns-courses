# Guatemala — dictatorships: Estrada Cabrera and Ubico — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Estrada Cabrera called himself protector of —
a) studious youth  b) the banana companies  c) the four peoples
**Answer: a) studious youth** — his self-styled title.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Estrada Cabrera's rule ended when —
a) the Assembly declared him insane  b) he won an election  c) he died at Chalchuapa
**Answer: a) the Assembly declared him insane** — his fall.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Ubico built palaces and roads using —
a) forced labour  b) volunteers only  c) banana profits only
**Answer: a) forced labour** — the labour behind his building.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Ubico was brought down in June 1944 by —
a) street protests  b) an earthquake  c) a foreign army
**Answer: a) street protests** — the protests.
