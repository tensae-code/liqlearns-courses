# Video script — Dutch Golden Age — art of a republic of traders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One Low Countries split in two — Catholic Flanders and the Protestant Dutch Republic.

Flanders paints big and sensuous for church and court with Rubens, while the Dutch Republic, rich on trade, buys small pictures of its own homes, ships, landscapes and faces.

Rembrandt turns light inward — onto faces and feelings rather than display.

That is the story: one Low Countries split in two — Catholic Flanders paints big and sensuous for church and court with Rubens, while the Protestant Dutch Republic, rich on trade, buys small pictures of its own homes, ships, landscapes and faces, and Rembrandt turns light inward.
