# Dutch Golden Age — art of a republic of traders — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Flanders paints big and sensuous for church and court with Rubens, while the Dutch Republic, rich on trade, buys small pictures of its own homes, ships, landscapes and faces.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — One Low Countries split in two — Catholic Flanders paints big and sensuous for church and court with Rubens, while the Protestant Dutch Republic, rich on trade, buys small pictures of its own homes, ships, landscapes and faces, and Rembrandt turns light inward.
