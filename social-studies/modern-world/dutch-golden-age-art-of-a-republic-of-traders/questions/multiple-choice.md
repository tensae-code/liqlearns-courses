# Dutch Golden Age — art of a republic of traders — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Flanders paints big, Holland paints its own life  b) One land splits in two  c) Rembrandt turns light inward
**Answer: b) One land splits in two** — One Low Countries split in two — Catholic Flanders and the Protestant Dutch Republic.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Flanders paints big, Holland paints its own life  b) One land splits in two  c) Rembrandt turns light inward
**Answer: a) Flanders paints big, Holland paints its own life** — Flanders paints big and sensuous for church and court with Rubens, while the Dutch Republic, rich on trade, buys small pictures of its own homes, ships, landscapes and faces.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) One land splits in two  b) Flanders paints big, Holland paints its own life  c) Rembrandt turns light inward
**Answer: c) Rembrandt turns light inward** — Rembrandt turns light inward — onto faces and feelings rather than display.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) One Low Countries split in two — Catholic Flanders paints big and sensuous for church and court with Rubens, while the Protestant Dutch Republic, rich on trade, buys small pictures of its own homes, ships, landscapes and faces, and Rembrandt turns light inward  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
