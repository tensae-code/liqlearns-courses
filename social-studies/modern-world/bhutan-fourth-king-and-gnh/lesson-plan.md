# Lesson plan — Bhutan — the Fourth King and Gross National Happiness

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bhutan — the Fourth King and Gross National Happiness
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Bhutan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** crowned in 1974 before the world's dignitaries, the fourth King declares Gross National Happiness more important than Gross National Product (1979), builds it on four pillars, writes it into Article 9 of the Constitution, and takes it to the United Nations
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.
