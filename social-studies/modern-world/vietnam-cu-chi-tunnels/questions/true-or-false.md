# Vietnam Cu Chi Tunnels — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** some 250 km of tunnels on three levels, 3–12 m deep, hide meeting rooms, stores, wells and the smokeless Hoàng Cầm kitchen invented in the earlier war against France.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 26, the Củ Chi tunnels as a built underground world

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 4 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 26, the Củ Chi tunnels as a built underground world

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** teams dig toward the tapping sounds of the team opposite, hide the soil in the river, disguise entrances as termite mounds, and hold out against US sweeps, bombs and poison — "land of steel, wall of bronze".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 26, digging the tunnels and the war underground
