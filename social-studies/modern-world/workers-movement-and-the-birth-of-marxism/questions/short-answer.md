# Workers Movement And The Birth Of Marxism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Workers' movements and Marxism is born.
**Answer: factory workers organise and strike for their rights, and Marx and Engels give their struggle a theory and a programme (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Unit 7 Lesson 2: Workers' movements and Marxism is born, printed p. 62.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Marxism is born and spreads.
**Answer: Marx and Engels publish the Communist Manifesto in 1848, the First International and the Paris Commune carry the workers' cause forward (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（下） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, lower volume — world history)* (China), Lesson 11: Marxism is born and spreads, printed p. 62.**
