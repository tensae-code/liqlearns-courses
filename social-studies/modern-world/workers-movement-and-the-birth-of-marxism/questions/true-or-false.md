# Workers Movement And The Birth Of Marxism — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Factory workers organise and strike for their rights, and Marx and Engels give their struggle a theory and a programme.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Workers' movements and Marxism is born

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Workers' movements and Marxism is born

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Marx and Engels publish the Communist Manifesto in 1848, the First International and the Paris Commune carry the workers' cause forward.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Marxism is born and spreads

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（下） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, lower volume — world history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Marxism is born and spreads
