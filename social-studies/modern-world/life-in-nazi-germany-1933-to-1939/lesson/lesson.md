# Life in Nazi Germany, 1933 to 1939

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Life in Nazi Germany reshapes women, the young, work and living standards to fit the regime. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

Kinder, Küche, Kirche — the regime's ideal woman marries early, bears many 'Aryan' children and leaves work and politics to men.
