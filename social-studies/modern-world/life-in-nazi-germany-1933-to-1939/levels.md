# Life in Nazi Germany, 1933 to 1939 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — 10.1 The Nazi view of the role of women
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Kinder, Küche, Kirche — the regime's ideal woman marries early, bears many 'Aryan' children and leaves work and politics to men

## Sub lesson 2 — 10.2 The changing role of women
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Marriage loans and the Mother's Cross push the birth rate up, yet by the late 1930s labour shortage quietly draws women back into work the ideology said was not theirs

## Sub lesson 3 — 11.1 Control of the young through education
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Rust's ministry Nazifies schools — race science and maths problems about 'aliens', Napola and Adolf Hitler Schools for the elite, teachers sworn to the regime

## Sub lesson 4 — 11.2 The Hitler Youth
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — All rival youth groups are banned and membership becomes compulsory; seven million boys drill for soldiering and girls of the League of German Maidens train for motherhood — though many quietly stop turning up

## Sub lesson 5 — 12.1 Policies to reduce unemployment
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The Reich Labour Service, autobahn building and rearmament erase the six million unemployed — with Jews and women struck off the count and conscription absorbing the young

## Sub lesson 6 — 12.2 Changes in the standard of living
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Strength through Joy holidays and the promised Volkswagen sweeten workers' lives while wages are frozen, unions gone and hours lengthen — better off is judged both ways
