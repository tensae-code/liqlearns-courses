# Life in Nazi Germany, 1933 to 1939 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kinder, Küche, Kirche — the regime's ideal woman marries early, bears many 'Aryan' children and leaves work and politics to men.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 10.1 The Nazi view of the role of women

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *GCSE History for Edexcel: Weimar and Nazi Germany, 1918–39* is about 10.2 The changing role of women.
**Answer: False** — it is about 10.1 The Nazi view of the role of women
