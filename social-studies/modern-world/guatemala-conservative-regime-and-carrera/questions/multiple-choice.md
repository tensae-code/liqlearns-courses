# Guatemala — the conservative regime and Carrera — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Carrera founded the Republic of Guatemala in —
a) 1847  b) 1821  c) 1871
**Answer: a) 1847** — the founding year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The separatists Carrera crushed were in —
a) Los Altos  b) Belize  c) El Salvador
**Answer: a) Los Altos** — the separatist region.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** William Walker was —
a) a filibuster expelled from Nicaragua  b) a Maya king  c) a Spanish viceroy
**Answer: a) a filibuster expelled from Nicaragua** — the adventurer in the story.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Carrera's regime is called the conservative regime of —
a) the thirty years  b) the ten years  c) the spring
**Answer: a) the thirty years** — its name in the book.
