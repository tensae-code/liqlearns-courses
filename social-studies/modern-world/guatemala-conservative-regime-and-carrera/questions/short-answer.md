# Guatemala — the conservative regime and Carrera — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who was Rafael Carrera, in the book's words?
**Answer: The mountain caudillo who founded the Republic of Guatemala in 1847 and led its conservative regime (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two things Carrera's forces did beyond founding the Republic.
**Answer: Crushed the Los Altos separatists; sent troops to expel William Walker from Nicaragua (any close wording).**
