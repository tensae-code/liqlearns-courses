# The Arab Springs And Their Aftermath — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** squares fill with demands for democracy (the chapter's videomap) and meet repression, civil war and Daesh's territories (the maps of 2015–18) — hope and catastrophe in one decade.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 6, Cap. 22 §3 Hopes and dramas of the Arab world

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 6, Cap. 22 §3 Hopes and dramas of the Arab world

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tahrir Square (Eventi) raises democracy's banner; the springs divide into transitions, restorations and civil wars, and Islam's internal divisions (Kepel's Fare storia piece on the war for Islam's control) decide their fates.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 19 §§5–6 Le "primavere arabe"; Le divisioni del mondo islamico

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
