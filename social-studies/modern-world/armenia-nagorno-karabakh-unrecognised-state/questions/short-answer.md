# Armenia Nagorno Karabakh Unrecognised State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 5.2 The NKR as an unrecognised state.
**Answer: Karabakh builds a state — elections, an army, a constitution — that no country, not even Armenia, formally recognises (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* (Armenia), 5.2 The NKR as an unrecognised state, printed p. 132.**
