# Venezuela From Castro To Gomez Centralisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gómez turns Castro's restoration into a long centralised rule, unifying the National Treasury from 1918 and remaking the army and the state.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1900–1936: from Castro to Gómez, milestones of centralisation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1900–1936: from Castro to Gómez, milestones of centralisation

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gómez rules 1908–1935, centralises the state, pays the foreign debt and hands out the oil concessions that change the country.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Gomecismo

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Nuestra Historia Republicana — Ciencias Sociales, 2do año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Gomecismo
