# Hands-on — Venezuela From Castro To Gomez Centralisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela From Castro To Gomez Centralisation — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 1900–1936: from Castro to Gómez, milestones of centralisation, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
