# Video script — Famous lives that shaped the world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Children pick one life — Lincoln, Gandhi, Mandela, Lenin, Churchill, Columbus, da Vinci, Plato, plus Fiji's own Mara, Sukuna, Bavadra, Chaudhry and Luveni — and explain why that person is famous and what their life changed.
