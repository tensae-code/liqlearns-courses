## Sub lesson 1 — Algeria's war of independence, 1954–1962
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The Constantine massacre of 8 May 1945, in which 45,000 Algerians were martyred, taught the national movement that only arms would free the country; the Great Algerian Revolution opened on 1 November 1954 from the Aurès, Jamila Bouhired among its fighters, a provisional government formed in Cairo in 1958, De Gaulle failed to crush it, and the Evian accords brought independence on 5 July 1962 after a struggle that cost over a million martyrs.
