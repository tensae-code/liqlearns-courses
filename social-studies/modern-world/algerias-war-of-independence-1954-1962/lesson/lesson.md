# Algeria's war of independence, 1954–1962

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The massacre of 8 May 1945 teaches Algeria that only arms will free it — the revolution of 1 November 1954 wins independence on 5 July 1962, at a cost of over a million martyrs. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Algeria's war of independence, 1954–1962

The Constantine massacre of 8 May 1945, in which 45,000 Algerians were martyred, taught the national movement that only arms would free the country; the Great Algerian Revolution opened on 1 November 1954 from the Aurès, Jamila Bouhired among its fighters, a provisional government formed in Cairo in 1958, De Gaulle failed to crush it, and the Evian accords brought independence on 5 July 1962 after a struggle that cost over a million martyrs.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
