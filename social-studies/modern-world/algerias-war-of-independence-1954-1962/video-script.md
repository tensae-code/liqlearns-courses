# Video script — Algeria's war of independence, 1954–1962

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Syria's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

The massacre of 8 May 1945 teaches Algeria that only arms will free it — the revolution of 1 November 1954 wins independence on 5 July 1962, at a cost of over a million martyrs.
