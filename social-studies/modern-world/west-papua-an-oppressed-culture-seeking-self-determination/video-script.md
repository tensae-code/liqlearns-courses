# Video script — West Papua — an oppressed culture seeking self-determination

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One island, two fates — West Papuans, Melanesians with the same ancestors and cultures as PNG, have been part of Indonesia since Dutch colonisation and still struggle for the independence their eastern half won.
