# Greece First Assemblies And Constitutions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The revolutionaries' first attempts to build a state.
**Answer: while fighting, the Greeks hold national assemblies and vote constitutions among the most liberal in Europe (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία (Modern and Contemporary History), Γ' Γυμνασίου, by Evangelia Louvi & Dimitrios Chr. Xifaras* (Greece), The revolutionaries' first attempts to build a state, printed p. 33.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Rivalries over the exercise of power.
**Answer: captains, islanders and politicians fall into civil wars even while the enemy is at the gates (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Νεότερου και Σύγχρονου Κόσμου από το 1453 μ.Χ. έως σήμερα (History of the Modern and Contemporary World from 1453 to today), Α' ΕΠΑ.Λ. (vocational lyceum), General Education* (Greece), Rivalries over the exercise of power, printed p. 43.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: The revolutionaries build a state; Kapodistrias and the settlement.
**Answer: assemblies vote constitutions mid-war, civil conflict follows, and Kapodistrias governs a recognised but tiny kingdom-in-waiting (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του νεότερου και του σύγχρονου κόσμου (από το 1815 έως σήμερα) (History of the Modern and Contemporary World, from 1815 to today), Γ' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Koliopoulos, Konstantinos Svolopoulos, Evanthis Hatzivassiliou, Theodoros Nimas & Haris Scholinaki-Chelioti* (Greece), The revolutionaries build a state; Kapodistrias and the settlement, printed p. 31.**
