# Video script — Women's rights — from Olympe de Gouges to Bolivia

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From the Declaration of the Rights of Woman and the Female Citizen against patriarchal colonialism, through important women in politics, education, literature and economics, to women's participation in Bolivian democracy.
