# Hands-on — Women's rights — from Olympe de Gouges to Bolivia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Women's rights — from Olympe de Gouges to Bolivia — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Las mujeres en la historia, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
