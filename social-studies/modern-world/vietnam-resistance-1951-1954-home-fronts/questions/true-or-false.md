# Vietnam Resistance 1951 1954 Home Fronts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Party's Second Congress meets at Vĩnh Quang, Tuyên Quang in February 1951 — the "Resistance Congress" — and the Liên Việt Front is founded that March; the 1952 Congress of Emulation Soldiers honours heroes Cù Chính Lan, La Văn Cầu, Nguyễn Thị Chiên and Trần Đại Nghĩa; production emulation and thrift feed the front, rent reduction and land reform begin in early 1953, the defence industry supplies thousands of tons of weapons and equipment, aid arrives from China and the USSR, and education is reformed under the motto "serve the resistance, serve the people, serve production".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 15, the home fronts, 1951–1954

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 9 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 15, the home fronts, 1951–1954

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** by 1953 the resistance is no longer surviving — it is administering, taxing, reforming land and choosing where to attack.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 7 The resistance matures, 1951–1953: the Second Party Congress (Feb 1951) brings the Party into the open as the Vietnam Workers' Party and restates the revolution's twin tasks; the Liên Việt Front unites the home front (3-3-1951); production-and-thrift campaigns, the first national emulation congress (1952) and the December 1953 Land Reform Law feed and legitimise the war, while the Trần Hưng Đạo, Hoàng Hoa Thám, Quang Trung, Hòa Bình, Tây Bắc and Thượng Lào campaigns keep the battlefield initiative
