# Vietnam Resistance 1951 1954 Home Fronts

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Party's Second Congress meets at Vĩnh Quang, Tuyên Quang in February 1951 — the "Resistance Congress" — and the Liên Việt Front is founded that March; the 1952 Congress of Emulation Soldiers honours heroes Cù Chính Lan, La Văn Cầu, Nguyễn Thị Chiên and Trần Đại Nghĩa; production emulation and thrift feed the front, rent reduction and land reform begin in early 1953, the defence industry supplies thousands of tons of weapons and equipment, aid arrives from China and the USSR, and education is reformed under the motto "serve the resistance, serve the people, serve production" As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
