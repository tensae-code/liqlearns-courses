# Video script — Bolivia — dictatorships and the Todos Santos massacre

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Bolivia's dictatorships end in spasms — Natusch Busch's coup and the All Saints' Day (Todos Santos) massacre, then Lidia Gueiler's transitional government.

The military-dictatorship cycle (1964–1982) is taught whole.
