# Weimar Germany — origins and early challenges — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — 1.1 The legacy of the First World War
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The Kaiser abdicates, revolution spreads from the Kiel mutiny and Ebert's new government signs the Armistice; the republic is born from defeat and inherits the blame for it

## Sub lesson 2 — 1.2 The setting up of the Weimar Republic
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Elections in January 1919 and a constitution finalised at Weimar give women the vote at 20 and the world's most democratic frame — yet proportional representation and the president's Article 48 emergency powers plant its instability

## Sub lesson 3 — 2.2 Challenges to the Republic from the left and right
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Spartacists under Luxemburg and Liebknecht rise in Berlin and are crushed by the Freikorps Ebert calls in; the Kapp Putsch then marches from the right and is stopped only by a general strike

## Sub lesson 4 — 2.3 The challenges of 1923
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — France occupies the Ruhr over unpaid reparations, passive resistance shuts the mines, and hyperinflation wipes out savings until Stresemann calls off resistance and issues the Rentenmark
