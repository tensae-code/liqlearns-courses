# Lesson plan — Weimar Germany — origins and early challenges

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Weimar Germany — origins and early challenges
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (United Kingdom, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Weimar Republic is born from defeat in 1918 and fights challenges from left and right through the crisis of 1923.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1.1 The legacy of the First World War** — The Kaiser abdicates, revolution spreads from the Kiel mutiny and Ebert's new government signs the Armistice; the republic is born from defeat and inherits the blame for it
2. **1.2 The setting up of the Weimar Republic** — Elections in January 1919 and a constitution finalised at Weimar give women the vote at 20 and the world's most democratic frame — yet proportional representation and the president's Article 48 emergency powers plant its instability
3. **2.2 Challenges to the Republic from the left and right** — Spartacists under Luxemburg and Liebknecht rise in Berlin and are crushed by the Freikorps Ebert calls in; the Kapp Putsch then marches from the right and is stopped only by a general strike
4. **2.3 The challenges of 1923** — France occupies the Ruhr over unpaid reparations, passive resistance shuts the mines, and hyperinflation wipes out savings until Stresemann calls off resistance and issues the Rentenmark
