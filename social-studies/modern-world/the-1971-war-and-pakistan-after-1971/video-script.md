# Video script — The 1971 war and Pakistan after 1971

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

In 1971 India intervenes in East Pakistan's crisis and the eastern wing becomes Bangladesh; a generation later the Afghan war spills over and Pakistan joins the war on terrorism, its army fighting militant groups.
