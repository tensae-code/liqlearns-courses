# Hands-on — Iran — the 15 Khordad uprising, 1963

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iran — the 15 Khordad uprising, 1963 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The uprising of 15 Khordad (June 1963), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
