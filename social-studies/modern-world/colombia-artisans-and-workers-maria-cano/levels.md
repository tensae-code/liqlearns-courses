## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 4: Artisans and the labour movement in Colombia
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — late-1800s artisans form Democratic Societies against free trade; river, tramway, oil and banana workers follow, their early strikes are violently repressed, and María Cano of Medellín — a woman and no worker herself — commits her reading-formed ideas to the workers' struggle

## Sub lesson 2 — Guía 14: Workers unite to defend their rights
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — after a disaster no union groupings arise in Colombia until after the First World War, and politicians' incursion into the unions then blocks their free development

## Sub lesson 3 — Tema 16: Popular movements and social reform in Colombia
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files.
