# Bahamas — Loyalists and the plantation islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Loyalists: the king's men who remade the islands  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Loyalists: the king's men who remade the islands** — when America wins independence in the 1780s, Loyalists who stayed loyal to the English king flee to still-British Bahamas, plant cotton with enslaved labour, rake salt in the off-season, and build Christ Church Cathedral and St. Matthew's, start schools, and John Wells starts a newspaper

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Loyalists: the king's men who remade the islands  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Loyalists: the king's men who remade the islands** — the book's own heading for this part.
