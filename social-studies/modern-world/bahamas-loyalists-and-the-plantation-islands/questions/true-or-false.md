# Bahamas — Loyalists and the plantation islands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** when America wins independence in the 1780s, Loyalists who stayed loyal to the English king flee to still-British Bahamas, plant cotton with enslaved labour, rake salt in the off-season, and build Christ Church Cathedral and St. Matthew's, start schools, and John Wells starts a newspaper
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Loyalists: the king's men who remade the islands).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Loyalists from the United States build Christ Church Cathedral, establish the first newspaper, the Gazette, and organise law and order in the community
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Early Connections: the Loyalists build the colony's institutions).
