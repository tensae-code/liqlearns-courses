# Bahamas — Loyalists and the plantation islands

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: When America wins independence in the 1780s, Loyalists who stayed loyal to the English king flee to still-British Bahamas, plant cotton with enslaved labour, rake salt in the off-season, and build the colony's churches, schools, newspaper and law and order. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
