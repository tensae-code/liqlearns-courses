# The Malayan Union, 1946, and the Federation of Malaya, 1948 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Malayan Union fight and the cessions  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Malayan Union fight and the cessions** — after the British Military Administration, Sir Harold MacMichael is sent to extract the sultans' signatures for the Malayan Union (H.C. Willan scouting ahead); Malays rise in protest — 10,000 rally at Kota Bharu on 15 December 1945 as he arrives, thousands more in Kedah on 19 January 1946; the same years, Sarawak and Sabah are ceded to the British Crown, and Sarawak's anti-cession youths found the secret Rukun 13 at Sibu (20 August 1948) under Awang Rambli, Morshidi Sidek and Bujang Suntong

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 4: the Malayan Union fight and the cessions  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 4: the Malayan Union fight and the cessions** — the book's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Federation of Malaya 1948  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Federation of Malaya 1948** — the rejected Malayan Union of 1946 is replaced by the Federation of Malaya, drafted by a Working Committee into the Federation Agreement — while another camp proposes its own alternative, the Perlembagaan Rakyat (People's Constitution)
