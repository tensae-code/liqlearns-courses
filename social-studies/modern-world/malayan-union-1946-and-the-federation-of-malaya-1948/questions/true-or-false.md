# The Malayan Union, 1946, and the Federation of Malaya, 1948 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the British Military Administration, Sir Harold MacMichael is sent to extract the sultans' signatures for the Malayan Union (H.C. Willan scouting ahead); Malays rise in protest — 10,000 rally at Kota Bharu on 15 December 1945 as he arrives, thousands more in Kedah on 19 January 1946; the same years, Sarawak and Sabah are ceded to the British Crown, and Sarawak's anti-cession youths found the secret Rukun 13 at Sibu (20 August 1948) under Awang Rambli, Morshidi Sidek and Bujang Suntong
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 4: the Malayan Union fight and the cessions).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The rejected Malayan Union of 1946 is replaced by the Federation of Malaya, drafted by a Working Committee into the Federation Agreement — while another camp proposes its own alternative, the Perlembagaan Rakyat (People's Constitution)
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 5: the Federation of Malaya 1948).
