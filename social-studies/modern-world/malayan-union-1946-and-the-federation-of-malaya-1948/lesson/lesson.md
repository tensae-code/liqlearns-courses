# The Malayan Union, 1946, and the Federation of Malaya, 1948

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The British Malayan Union of 1946 is rejected by Malay protest and replaced by the Federation of Malaya in 1948, while Sarawak and Sabah are ceded to the British Crown. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Malayan Union fight and the cessions

After the British Military Administration, Sir Harold MacMichael is sent to extract the sultans' signatures for the Malayan Union (H.C. Willan scouting ahead); Malays rise in protest — 10,000 rally at Kota Bharu on 15 December 1945 as he arrives, thousands more in Kedah on 19 January 1946; the same years, Sarawak and Sabah are ceded to the British Crown, and Sarawak's anti-cession youths found the secret Rukun 13 at Sibu (20 August 1948) under Awang Rambli, Morshidi Sidek and Bujang Suntong (Bab 4: the Malayan Union fight and the cessions)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this lesson's story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Federation of Malaya 1948

The rejected Malayan Union of 1946 is replaced by the Federation of Malaya, drafted by a Working Committee into the Federation Agreement — while another camp proposes its own alternative, the Perlembagaan Rakyat (People's Constitution) (Bab 5: the Federation of Malaya 1948)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this lesson's story. Name two things that changed in the story.
