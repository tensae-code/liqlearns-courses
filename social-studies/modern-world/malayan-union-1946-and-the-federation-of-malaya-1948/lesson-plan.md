# Lesson plan — The Malayan Union, 1946, and the Federation of Malaya, 1948

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Malayan Union, 1946, and the Federation of Malaya, 1948

**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The British Malayan Union of 1946 is rejected by Malay protest and replaced by the Federation of Malaya in 1948, while Sarawak and Sabah are ceded to the British Crown
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Malayan Union fight and the cessions** — after the British Military Administration, Sir Harold MacMichael is sent to extract the sultans' signatures for the Malayan Union (H.C. Willan scouting ahead); Malays rise in protest — 10,000 rally at Kota Bharu on 15 December 1945 as he arrives, thousands more in Kedah on 19 January 1946; the same years, Sarawak and Sabah are ceded to the British Crown, and Sarawak's anti-cession youths found the secret Rukun 13 at Sibu (20 August 1948) under Awang Rambli, Morshidi Sidek and Bujang Suntong (Bab 4: the Malayan Union fight and the cessions)
2. **The Federation of Malaya 1948** — the rejected Malayan Union of 1946 is replaced by the Federation of Malaya, drafted by a Working Committee into the Federation Agreement — while another camp proposes its own alternative, the Perlembagaan Rakyat (People's Constitution) (Bab 5: the Federation of Malaya 1948)
