Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Borrowed styles everywhere  b) An age that can build anything  c) The iron skeleton underneath
**Answer: b) An age that can build anything** — An age that can build anything argues about what anything should look like.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Borrowed styles everywhere  b) An age that can build anything  c) The iron skeleton underneath
**Answer: a) Borrowed styles everywhere** — Historicism spreads across Britain, France, the German lands and the Polish lands — neo-Gothic parliaments and neo-Renaissance opera houses.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) An age that can build anything  b) Borrowed styles everywhere  c) The iron skeleton underneath
**Answer: c) The iron skeleton underneath** — Underneath the costume, the engineering current raises the iron skeleton of the modern city being born.
