# Suriname — resistance: Maroons and uprisings

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Indigenous fighters (Kaikoesie), enslaved rebels and Maroons resist in turn; the Cottica Maroons under Baron, Boni and Joli Coeur hold swamp Fort Buku until betrayal reveals its underwater path, and Boni fights on until 1792; later contract workers rise at Zoelen, Alliance and Mariënburg under leaders like Ramdjanee As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
