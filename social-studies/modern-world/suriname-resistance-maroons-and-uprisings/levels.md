# Suriname — resistance: Maroons and uprisings — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Onderdrukking en verzet
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Indigenous fighters (Kaikoesie), enslaved rebels and Maroons resist in turn; the Cottica Maroons under Baron, Boni and Joli Coeur hold swamp Fort Buku until betrayal reveals its underwater path, and Boni fights on until 1792; later contract workers rise at Zoelen, Alliance and Mariënburg under leaders like Ramdjanee.

## Sub lesson 2 — Het verzet van slaafgemaakten
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Resistance runs from stories and songs through sabotage of work to open joint revolt on the plantation and flight to camps in the forest.

## Sub lesson 3 — Strijden voor de vrijheid
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Maroon villages deep inland organise well-planned attacks on plantations to free people and seize supplies, while forest patrols hunt the escapees — the Tempati uprising is studied for its consequences.

## Sub lesson 4 — De strijd gaat door
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Peace with the Ndyuka (10 October 1760) and two more Maroon groups follows, but the Boni wars go on around Fort Boekoe and the Cordonpad under Governor Nepveu's counter-measures.

## Sub lesson 5 — Het verzet van de contractarbeiders
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Cheated of the free housing and fair treatment their contracts promised, contract workers resist; in 1902 the great Mariënburg uprising sees a plantation director killed.
