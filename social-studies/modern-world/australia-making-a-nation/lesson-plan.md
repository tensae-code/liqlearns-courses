# Lesson plan — Australia — making a nation

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Australia — making a nation
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Australia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Settlement expands, living and working conditions are fought over, self-government and democracy are won, and social legislation builds the new Australian nation; Indigenous contact experiences and non-European migrants' experiences stand inside nation-making, closing with Indigenous and women's rights
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
