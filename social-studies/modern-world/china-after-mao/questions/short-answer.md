Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Modulo 12 §§7–8 I conflitti nell'Asia comunista; La Cina post-maoista.
**Answer: the communist world goes to war with itself (Vietnam, Cambodia, China's border war) while Deng's China quietly changes system — socialism's Asian centre of gravity shifts (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
