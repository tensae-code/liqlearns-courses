# Gagauzia Autonomy Within Moldova — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 51: Unitatea Teritorială Autonomă cu statut special Găgăuzia (Gagauz-Yeri).
**Answer: the law makes Gagauzia "a form of self-determination of the Gagauz, part of the Republic of Moldova", with its own authorities and the Valkaneș free economic zone (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a XII-a* (Moldova), Tema 51: Unitatea Teritorială Autonomă cu statut special Găgăuzia (Gagauz-Yeri), printed p. 252.**
