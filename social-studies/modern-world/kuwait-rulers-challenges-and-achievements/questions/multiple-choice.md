# Kuwait's rulers — challenges and achievements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The rulers' march runs down to —
a) Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler  b) Sabah bin Jaber, the first  c) Abdullah Al Salem
**Answer: a) Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler** — down to Ahmad Al Jaber, the tenth ruler

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Against pressure, the rulers held —
a) Kuwait's independence  b) the pearl monopoly  c) the Ottoman alliance
**Answer: a) Kuwait's independence** — they hold the country's independence against pressure

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The building of the modern state was opened by —
a) Mubarak Al Sabah  b) Muhammad bin Sabah  c) Abdullah II
**Answer: a) Mubarak Al Sabah** — Mubarak Al Sabah opens the building of the modern state
