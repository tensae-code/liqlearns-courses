# Bengal resists the Company and the Raj — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two fighting resistances and one thinking resistance from the lesson.
**Answer: Any two of Majnu Shah's bands, indigo ryots, Titumir 1831, sepoys 1857; and any one of Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar, Derozio (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book put fighters and thinkers on one road?
**Answer: Both resisted British rule in their own way, and together their road ends with the British forced to leave in 1947 (any close wording).**
