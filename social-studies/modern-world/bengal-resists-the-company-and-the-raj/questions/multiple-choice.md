# Bengal resists the Company and the Raj — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Titumir's rising was in —
a) 1831  b) 1857  c) 1947
**Answer: a) 1831** — Titumir's year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The indigo ryots rose against —
a) Forced indigo planting  b) River floods  c) The Sultanate
**Answer: a) Forced indigo planting** — the ryots' grievance.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio fought —
a) With armies  b) With ideas  c) With boats
**Answer: b) With ideas** — the thinkers' weapon.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The British were forced to leave in —
a) 1857  b) 1905  c) 1947
**Answer: c) 1947** — the road's end.
