# Bengal resists the Company and the Raj

## Do it first

Some fought the British with weapons. Others fought them with schools, newspapers and new ideas. Before you read on, decide which fight you think lasted longer. Hold your answer; Majnu Shah and Rammohan Roy are both coming.

## Sub lesson 1 — Resistance to British rule — fighters and thinkers

The Grade 9 book closes Bengal's political path with resistance in two kinds. The fighters: Fakir Majnu Shah's bands roaming against the Company, the indigo ryots rising against forced planting, Titumir's rising of 1831, and the sepoys of 1857. The thinkers: Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio, who fight the same rule with ideas — education, reform and reason. The book runs both fights on one road, and the road ends the same way: the British are forced to leave in 1947.
