# Video script — Bengal resists the Company and the Raj

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bangladesh book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 9 closes with resistance in two kinds. The fighters: Fakir Majnu Shah's bands, the indigo ryots, Titumir's rising of 1831, the sepoys of 1857.

The thinkers: Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio, fighting with ideas.

Both fights run on one road — and it ends in 1947, when the British are forced to leave.
