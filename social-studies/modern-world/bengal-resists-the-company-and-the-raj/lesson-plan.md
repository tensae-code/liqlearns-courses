# Lesson plan — Bengal resists the Company and the Raj

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bengal resists the Company and the Raj
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Bangladesh, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** resistance in two kinds: fighters — Fakir Majnu Shah's bands, the indigo ryots, Titumir's 1831 rising and the sepoys of 1857 — and thinkers — Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio fighting with ideas — until the British are forced to leave in 1947.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

2. Name the thinkers: Rammohan Roy, Vidyasagar and Derozio.
3. Say how the road ends: the British are forced to leave in 1947.
