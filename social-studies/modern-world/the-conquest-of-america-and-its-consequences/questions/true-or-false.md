# The Conquest Of America And Its Consequences — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** disease, encomienda and the mines empty the Indies; Las Casas' Brevissima (in the Documents) accuses Spain before Europe, and the colonial system takes shape.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 12 §5 The consequences of the conquest in America

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 12 §5 The consequences of the conquest in America

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cortés meets Montezuma and an empire falls to steel, horses and smallpox; Spain and Portugal build colonial empires and argue over the souls and rights of the conquered.
