# The Conquest Of America And Its Consequences — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 4, Cap. 12 §5 The consequences of the conquest in America.
**Answer: disease, encomienda and the mines empty the Indies; Las Casas' Brevissima (in the Documents) accuses Spain before Europe, and the colonial system takes shape (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 4, Cap. 12 §5 The consequences of the conquest in America, printed p. 380.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: La conquista del Nuovo Mondo; Personaggi: Montezuma; L'impero coloniale spagnolo; L'impero coloniale portoghese; La politica delle conversioni.
**Answer: Cortés meets Montezuma and an empire falls to steel, horses and smallpox; Spain and Portugal build colonial empires and argue over the souls and rights of the conquered (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), La conquista del Nuovo Mondo; Personaggi: Montezuma; L'impero coloniale spagnolo; L'impero coloniale portoghese; La politica delle conversioni, printed pp. 416, 418, 420, 424, 426.**
