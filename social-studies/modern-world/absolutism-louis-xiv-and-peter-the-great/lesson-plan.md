4. **§20–21 Stages of the feudal state's development; absolutism's features in the West (France, England, Russia)** — monarchy climbs from feudal to absolute across three kingdoms
5. **§21 Features of absolutism in England, France and Russia** — three crowns show absolutism's strengths and varieties
