# The Giolittian Age — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Giolitti (Personaggi) opens to unions and suffrage (1912), industry booms in the northern triangle, emigration drains the South (Fare storia: Volpe), and the Libyan war (1911) arms the nationalism that will end his system.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 6, Cap. 21 §§8–10 Economic development and the problems of the South; The Giolittian age; Nationalism, the Libyan war and the end of Giolittism

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 2 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XVII al XIX secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 6, Cap. 21 §§8–10 Economic development and the problems of the South; The Giolittian age; Nationalism, the Libyan war and the end of Giolittism

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 1, Cap. 3 §3 Giolitti's domestic policy
