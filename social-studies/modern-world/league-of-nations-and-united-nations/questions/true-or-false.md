# The League of Nations and the United Nations — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the First World War the world builds the League of Nations to keep peace, and pupils judge its record
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The League of Nations

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Secondary History 4 Student's Book* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The League of Nations

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After the Second World War the League's successor, the United Nations, takes up the promotion of peace
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The United Nations Organisation (UNO)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Secondary History 4 Student's Book* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The United Nations Organisation (UNO)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Learners compare the League and the UN in aims and structure, then case-study UN peacekeeping in the Congo crisis, the Korean War and the Gulf War, and the UN's role in South Sudan or Africa
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in UN peacekeeping case studies
