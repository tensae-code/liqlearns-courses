# Video script — The League of Nations and the United Nations

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as South Sudan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

After the Second World War the League's successor, the United Nations, takes up the promotion of peace.

Learners compare the League and the UN in aims and structure, then case-study UN peacekeeping in the Congo crisis, the Korean War and the Gulf War, and the UN's role in South Sudan or Africa.
