# Hands-on — The League of Nations and the United Nations

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The League of Nations and the United Nations — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The League of Nations, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
