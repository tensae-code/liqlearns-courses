Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the word "dân tộc" carries two senses — the nation, the whole people of a country (the Vietnamese nation), and the ethnic group, a community sharing a language, a culture and an ethnic self-consciousness (the Kinh, Mường and Thái peoples); by population the country's groups divide into the majority group and the minority groups, the 2011 government decree quoted defining a minority ethnic group as one whose population is smaller than the majority's on the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam, the majority being the group over 50% of the national population in the census; the 2019 census table (General Statistics Office) then names all 54 groups with their numbers — the Kinh 82,085,826 people, 85.32% of the country, then the Tày (1,845,492), Thái (1,820,950), Mường (1,452,095), Mông (1,393,547), Khơ-me (1,319,652), Nùng, Dao, Hoa, Gia Rai, Ê Đê, Ba Na, Xơ Đăng, Sán Chay, Cơ Ho, Sán Dìu, Chăm (178,948), Hrê and on down through the Raglai, Mnông, Xtiêng, Bru–Vân Kiều, Thổ, Khơ Mú, Cơ Tu, Giáy, Gié–Triêng, Tà Ôi, Mạ, Co, Chơ Ro, Xinh Mun, Hà Nhì, Chu Ru, Lào and Kháng to the smallest communities — the La Chí, Phù Lá, La Hủ, La Ha, Pà Thẻn, Chứt, Lự, Lô Lô, Mảng, Cờ Lao, Bố Y, Cống, Ngái (1,649), Si La (909), Pu Péo (903), Rơ Măm (639), Brâu (525) and Ơ Đu (428);
