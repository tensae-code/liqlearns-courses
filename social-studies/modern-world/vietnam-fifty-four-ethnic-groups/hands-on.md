# Hands-on — Vietnam Fifty Four Ethnic Groups

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Fifty Four Ethnic Groups — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 11, the ethnic groups on Vietnam's territory, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
