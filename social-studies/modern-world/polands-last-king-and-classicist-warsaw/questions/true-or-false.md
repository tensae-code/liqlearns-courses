# Poland's last king and classicist Warsaw — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The capital fills with theatres, palaces and Łazienki park.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Stanisław August, Poland's last king, makes Warsaw a classicist capital of theatres, palaces and Łazienki park even as the partitions close in — a court culture flowering in the shadow of the state's destruction.
