# Poland's last king and classicist Warsaw — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Theatres, palaces and Łazienki  b) The last king builds  c) Flowering in the shadow
**Answer: b) The last king builds** — Stanisław August, Poland's last king, makes Warsaw a classicist capital.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Theatres, palaces and Łazienki  b) The last king builds  c) Flowering in the shadow
**Answer: a) Theatres, palaces and Łazienki** — The capital fills with theatres, palaces and Łazienki park.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) The last king builds  b) Theatres, palaces and Łazienki  c) Flowering in the shadow
**Answer: c) Flowering in the shadow** — The court culture flowers even as the partitions close in — in the shadow of the state's destruction.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Stanisław August, Poland's last king, makes Warsaw a classicist capital of theatres, palaces and Łazienki park even as the partitions close in — a court culture flowering in the shadow of the state's destruction  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
