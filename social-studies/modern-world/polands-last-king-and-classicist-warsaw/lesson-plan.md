# Lesson plan — Poland's last king and classicist Warsaw

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Poland's last king and classicist Warsaw
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Stanisław August, Poland's last king, makes Warsaw a classicist capital of theatres, palaces and Łazienki park even as the partitions close in — a court culture flowering in the shadow of the state's destruction.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

3. **Flowering in the shadow** — The court culture flowers even as the partitions close in — in the shadow of the state's destruction.
