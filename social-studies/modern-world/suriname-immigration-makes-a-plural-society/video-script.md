# Video script — Suriname — immigration makes a plural society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From a plantation colony in 1651 the population is remade group by group — including Portuguese Jews arriving to empty promises of houses, land and tools — until Suriname holds many peoples.

Taking its title from the anthem, the lesson recounts how each population group came to Suriname, building one multicultural society.

Suriname as melting pot: cultural exchange, respect and national unity across the groups, voiced by poet Robin Ravales (Dobru).
