# Suriname — immigration makes a plural society — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From a plantation colony in 1651 the population is remade group by group — including Portuguese Jews arriving to empty promises of houses, land and tools — until Suriname holds many peoples.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Onze samenleving verandert

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Onze samenleving verandert

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Taking its title from the anthem, the lesson recounts how each population group came to Suriname, building one multicultural society.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Hoe wij hier ook samenkwamen

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Hoe wij hier ook samenkwamen

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Suriname as melting pot: cultural exchange, respect and national unity across the groups, voiced by poet Robin Ravales (Dobru).
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ons land, een smeltkroes van culturen

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ons land, een smeltkroes van culturen
