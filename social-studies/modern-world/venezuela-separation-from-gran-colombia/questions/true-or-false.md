# Venezuela Separation From Gran Colombia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The great union breaks and Venezuela walks out to be its own republic in 1830 — the founding separation of the modern country.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Venezuela separates from Gran Colombia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 5to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
