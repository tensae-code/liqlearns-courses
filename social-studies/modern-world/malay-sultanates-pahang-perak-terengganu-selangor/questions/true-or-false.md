# The Malay sultanates — Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Four more sultanates carry Melaka's inheritance — its law above all: Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99; Perak's 15th sultan, Iskandar Dzulkarnain (1752–1765), tightens the state's administration, and Selangor's Sultan Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah (1898–1938) builds roads and railways across Kuala Lumpur and Klang and is honoured with a Golden Jubilee at Klang
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 7: Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor).
