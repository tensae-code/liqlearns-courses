# The Malay sultanates — Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Pahang, Perak, Terengganu and Selangor** — four more sultanates carry Melaka's inheritance — its law above all: Hukum Kanun Melaka fathers Hukum Kanun Pahang and Perak's Undang-Undang 99; Perak's 15th sultan, Iskandar Dzulkarnain (1752–1765), tightens the state's administration, and Selangor's Sultan Ala'iddin Suleiman Shah (1898–1938) builds roads and railways across Kuala Lumpur and Klang and is honoured with a Golden Jubilee at Klang
