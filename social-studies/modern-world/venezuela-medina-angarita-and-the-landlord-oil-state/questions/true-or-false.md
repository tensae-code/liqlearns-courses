# Venezuela Medina Angarita And The Landlord Oil State — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Medina's government is judged through Picón Salas's and Uslar Pietri's eyes as oil income grows and the state settles into renting its subsoil to the companies.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Medina Angarita and the landlord oil state

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Medina Angarita and the landlord oil state

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Medina legalises the parties, passes the 1943 Hydrocarbons Law and is overthrown in 1945 just as democracy widens.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Government of Medina Angarita

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Nuestra Historia Republicana — Ciencias Sociales, 2do año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
