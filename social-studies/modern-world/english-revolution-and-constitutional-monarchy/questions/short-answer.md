# English Revolution And Constitutional Monarchy — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bourgeois revolution — England.
**Answer: Charles I storms Parliament to arrest its leaders and starts a civil war; Cromwell wins it, Charles is executed in 1649, and after the bloodless Glorious Revolution of 1688 the Bill of Rights makes Parliament supreme — the world's first constitutional monarchy (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Unit 6 Lesson 3: Bourgeois revolution — England, printed p. 44.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bourgeois revolutions — England.
**Answer: Parliament executes a king, survives Cromwell and a restoration, and in 1688–89 settles power in Parliament for good (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（下） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, lower volume — world history)* (China), Lesson 9: Bourgeois revolutions — England, printed p. 50.**
