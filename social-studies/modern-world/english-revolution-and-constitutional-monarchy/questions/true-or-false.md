# English Revolution And Constitutional Monarchy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Charles I storms Parliament to arrest its leaders and starts a civil war; Cromwell wins it, Charles is executed in 1649, and after the bloodless Glorious Revolution of 1688 the Bill of Rights makes Parliament supreme — the world's first constitutional monarchy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bourgeois revolution — England

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bourgeois revolution — England

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bourgeois revolutions — England
