## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 4: cultural life since 1921
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the founder-king is a poet who fills his court with writers; Jordan's first newspaper, al-Haqq Ya'lu, is printed at Ma'an in 1920–21 carrying the revolt's principles, the press reaches Amman in 1923 and the official al-Sharq al-Arabi follows (renamed 1926); a Culture and Arts Department (1966) becomes a Ministry of Culture and Youth (1977); Jordan Television opens in black and white at Umm al-Hiran in 1968; Prince Hassan founds the Arab Thought Forum in 1981; Amman is Arab Capital of Culture in 2002; and the Jerash, Azraq and Fuheis festivals and the Reading-for-All Family Library carry culture to every town
