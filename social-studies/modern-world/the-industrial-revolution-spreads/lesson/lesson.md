# The Industrial Revolution spreads

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The new industry crosses to France, Germany and Belgium, but France — loving its handmade goods, short of coal, banning machinery exports' rivals — industrialises a generation late. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The revolution spreads and France lags

The new industry crosses to France, Germany and Belgium, but France — loving its handmade goods, short of coal, banning machinery exports' rivals — industrialises a generation late

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
