# Belize and Guatemala's territorial claim — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first English settlers in Belize cut —
a) logwood  b) coffee  c) indigo
**Answer: a) logwood** — their trade.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Belize became independent from Britain in —
a) 1981  b) 1821  c) 1996
**Answer: a) 1981** — its independence year.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Guatemala's position on Belize is —
a) it claims the territory  b) it never claimed it  c) it sold it in 2008
**Answer: a) it claims the territory** — the claim.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** In 2008 Guatemala and Belize accepted —
a) taking the dispute to a court  b) going to war  c) splitting Belize in half
**Answer: a) taking the dispute to a court** — the court agreement.
