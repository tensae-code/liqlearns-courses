# Suriname — independence, 1975 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Onafhankelijkheid.
**Answer: After the war Surinamers demand to be "boss in their own house"; on 25 November 1975 Suriname becomes independent with its own flag and anthem and Johan Ferrier as first president. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 6 Geschiedenis*, Hoofdstuk 7: Onafhankelijkheid, printed p. 85.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: De onafhankelijkheid van ons land.
**Answer: After long talks with the Netherlands, on 25 November 1975 the Dutch flag comes down, the Surinamese flag and anthem (with its Sranan verse) rise, and Suriname becomes a republic. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden*, Thema 7 Les 1: De onafhankelijkheid van ons land, printed p. 93.**
