# Suriname — independence, 1975 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the war Surinamers demand to be "boss in their own house"; on 25 November 1975 Suriname becomes independent with its own flag and anthem and Johan Ferrier as first president.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Onafhankelijkheid

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 6 Geschiedenis* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Onafhankelijkheid

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After long talks with the Netherlands, on 25 November 1975 the Dutch flag comes down, the Surinamese flag and anthem (with its Sranan verse) rise, and Suriname becomes a republic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De onafhankelijkheid van ons land

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about De onafhankelijkheid van ons land
