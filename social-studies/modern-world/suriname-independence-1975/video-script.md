# Video script — Suriname — independence, 1975

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After the war Surinamers demand to be "boss in their own house"; on 25 November 1975 Suriname becomes independent with its own flag and anthem and Johan Ferrier as first president.

After long talks with the Netherlands, on 25 November 1975 the Dutch flag comes down, the Surinamese flag and anthem (with its Sranan verse) rise, and Suriname becomes a republic.
