# Suriname — independence, 1975

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: After the war Surinamers demand to be "boss in their own house"; on 25 November 1975 Suriname becomes independent with its own flag and anthem and Johan Ferrier as first president As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Onafhankelijkheid

After the war Surinamers demand to be "boss in their own house"; on 25 November 1975 Suriname becomes independent with its own flag and anthem and Johan Ferrier as first president (Onafhankelijkheid)

After long talks with the Netherlands, on 25 November 1975 the Dutch flag comes down, the Surinamese flag and anthem (with its Sranan verse) rise, and Suriname becomes a republic (De onafhankelijkheid van ons land)
