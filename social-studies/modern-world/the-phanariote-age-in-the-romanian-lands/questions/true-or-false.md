# The Phanariote Age In The Romanian Lands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Porte appoints Greek Phanariote princes for three-year terms, confirmed yearly and deposed at whim — a political fix that becomes an age of burden.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lecția 14: Epoca fanariotă în Țările Române

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a VII-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lecția 14: Epoca fanariotă în Țările Române

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Mavrocordats, a Greek family from Chios, supply princes; the case study follows the grand vizier's messenger the moment a Moldavian prince dies and a Phanariote is named.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §15: Organizarea politică a Principatelor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII
