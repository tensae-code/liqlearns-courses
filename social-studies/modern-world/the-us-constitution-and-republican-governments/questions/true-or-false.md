# The US Constitution and republican governments, 1776–1790 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The states argued over how much change the Revolution really made.
**Answer: True** — The states experiment and argue over how much change the Revolution really made.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Constitution kept the Articles exactly as they were.
**Answer: False** — The Convention built a federal republic to replace the failing Articles.
