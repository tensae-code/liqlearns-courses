# The US Constitution and republican governments, 1776–1790 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Common Sense was written by —
a) Washington  b) Paine  c) King George III
**Answer: b) Paine** — Paine's Common Sense.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Common Sense attacked —
a) Taxes only  b) The very idea of kingship  c) The French
**Answer: b) The very idea of kingship** — it kills the king in print — kingship itself.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The first union frame that began to fail was —
a) The Constitution  b) The Articles  c) The Stamp Act
**Answer: b) The Articles** — the failing Articles.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Convention of 1787 built —
a) A monarchy  b) A federal republic  c) Thirteen separate kingdoms
**Answer: b) A federal republic** — a federal republic strong enough to replace the Articles.
