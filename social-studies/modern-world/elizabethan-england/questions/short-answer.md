# Elizabethan England — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 5, Cap. 17 §2 The England of Elizabeth.
**Answer: Elizabeth holds a divided kingdom together for 45 years, defeats the Armada (1588) and presides over Shakespeare's stage and the buccaneer sea-dogs (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 5, Cap. 17 §2 The England of Elizabeth, printed p. 512.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Elisabetta I regina d'Inghilterra; La sconfitta dell'Invincibile Armata.
**Answer: Elizabeth (Personaggi) holds England Protestant and beats the Armada; female monarchy itself is put on trial by the age (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), Elisabetta I regina d'Inghilterra; La sconfitta dell'Invincibile Armata, printed p. 547.**
