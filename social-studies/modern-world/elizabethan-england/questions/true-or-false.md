# Elizabethan England — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Elizabeth holds a divided kingdom together for 45 years, defeats the Armada (1588) and presides over Shakespeare's stage and the buccaneer sea-dogs.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 5, Cap. 17 §2 The England of Elizabeth

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 5, Cap. 17 §2 The England of Elizabeth

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Elizabeth (Personaggi) holds England Protestant and beats the Armada; female monarchy itself is put on trial by the age.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Elisabetta I regina d'Inghilterra; La sconfitta dell'Invincibile Armata

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Elisabetta I regina d'Inghilterra; La sconfitta dell'Invincibile Armata
