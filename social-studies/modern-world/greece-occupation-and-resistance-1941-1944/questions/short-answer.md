# Greece Occupation And Resistance 1941 1944 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Occupation, Resistance and Liberation.
**Answer: famine winters, a mass resistance movement in mountains and cities, and liberation in 1944 shadowed by coming civil conflict (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Occupation and National Resistance.
**Answer: famine, collaboration, and a resistance that liberates much of the countryside before Athens (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Νεότερου και Σύγχρονου Κόσμου από το 1453 μ.Χ. έως σήμερα (History of the Modern and Contemporary World from 1453 to today), Α' ΕΠΑ.Λ. (vocational lyceum), General Education* (Greece), Occupation and National Resistance, printed p. 126.**
