# Lesson plan — Colombia — the peasant movement and coffee

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Colombia — the peasant movement and coffee
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Colombia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** peasant families work the coffee harvests, organise leagues, invade land and are driven off it — and coffee, organised by the Federación de Cafeteros, remakes Colombia's economy
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guía 3: The Colombian peasant movement** — peasant families work the coffee harvests together, organise, and are driven off the land; rural statistics and the question of whether their organisations still exist close the guide
2. **Módulo 4 / Guía 10: Migrations and conflicts of our land** — colonos travel to baldío lands, peasants answer the agrarian conflict with land invasions and the first peasant leagues, and López Pumarejo's thought is set against Laureano Gómez's discourse
3. **Módulo 4 / Guías 10–12: Migrations and displacements in our territory / Peasant communities in defence of their territory** — by the late 1800s and early 1900s western Colombia becomes the economic centre as colonists suffer the "illness of the cold lands" and tertian fevers; today peasant women and leaders demand territoriality and an end to forced displacement
4. **Tema 15: Sharpening of the coffee crisis in Colombia and the Second World War** — coffee production jumps 62% by 1933, the Federación de Cafeteros organises a fund and becomes decisive for industrial development itself
