# Colombia — the peasant movement and coffee

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: peasant families work the coffee harvests, organise leagues, invade land and are driven off it — and coffee, organised by the Federación de Cafeteros, remakes Colombia's economy. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
