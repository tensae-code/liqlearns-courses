# Colombia — the peasant movement and coffee — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 3: The Colombian peasant movement
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — peasant families work the coffee harvests together, organise, and are driven off the land; rural statistics and the question of whether their organisations still exist close the guide

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — colonos travel to baldío lands, peasants answer the agrarian conflict with land invasions and the first peasant leagues, and López Pumarejo's thought is set against Laureano Gómez's discourse

## Sub lesson 3 — Módulo 4 / Guías 10–12: Migrations and displacements in our territory / Peasant communities in defence of their territory
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — by the late 1800s and early 1900s western Colombia becomes the economic centre as colonists suffer the "illness of the cold lands" and tertian fevers; today peasant women and leaders demand territoriality and an end to forced displacement
