# Colombia — the peasant movement and coffee — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Peasant families work the coffee harvests together, organise, and are driven off the land; rural statistics and the question of whether their organisations still exist close the guide
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Guía 3: The Colombian peasant movement

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Primera Cartilla, Grado Quinto, Escuela Nueva* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Guía 3: The Colombian peasant movement

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Colonos travel to baldío lands, peasants answer the agrarian conflict with land invasions and the first peasant leagues, and López Pumarejo's thought is set against Laureano Gómez's discourse
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Módulo 4 / Guía 10: Migrations and conflicts of our land

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Grado 8, Postprimaria Rural (Sociales)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Módulo 4 / Guía 10: Migrations and conflicts of our land

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** By the late 1800s and early 1900s western Colombia becomes the economic centre as colonists suffer the "illness of the cold lands" and tertian fevers; today peasant women and leaders demand territoriality and an end to forced displacement
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Módulo 4 / Guías 10–12: Migrations and displacements in our territory / Peasant communities in defence of their territory

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Grado 9, Postprimaria Rural (Sociales)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Módulo 4 / Guías 10–12: Migrations and displacements in our territory / Peasant communities in defence of their territory

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Coffee production jumps 62% by 1933, the Federación de Cafeteros organises a fund and becomes decisive for industrial development itself
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 15: Sharpening of the coffee crisis in Colombia and the Second World War
