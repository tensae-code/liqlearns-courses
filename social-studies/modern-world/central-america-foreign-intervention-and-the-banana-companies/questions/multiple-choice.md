# Central America — foreign intervention and the banana companies — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Soto-Keith contract paid a railway builder in —
a) land  b) gold coins  c) coffee only
**Answer: a) land** — the payment the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The United Fruit Company ended up holding —
a) Izabal and Tiquisate  b) Tikal and Q'umarkaj  c) Belize City
**Answer: a) Izabal and Tiquisate** — its Guatemalan holdings.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The railway builder came from —
a) the United States  b) Spain  c) Britain
**Answer: a) the United States** — his country.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign intervention in this lesson arrives as —
a) railway contracts and banana plantations  b) a declared war only  c) missionaries only
**Answer: a) railway contracts and banana plantations** — its form here.
