# Central America — foreign intervention and the banana companies — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** United Fruit held land in Guatemala.
**Answer: True** — Izabal and Tiquisate.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Soto-Keith contract was a peace treaty.
**Answer: False** — it was a railway-for-land contract, the model deal.
