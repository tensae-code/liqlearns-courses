# Vietnam August Revolution 1945

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Japan surrenders; on 19 August Hanoi's crowds take the Residency, on 23 August Huế rises, on 25 August Saigon rises, and by month's end the general uprising has won across the country As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 14, the August Revolution in Hanoi, Huế and Saigon

Japan surrenders; on 19 August Hanoi's crowds take the Residency, on 23 August Huế rises, on 25 August Saigon rises, and by month's end the general uprising has won across the country

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 8, the road to August: famine, Việt Minh and the Japanese coup

France falls in June 1940 and Japan enters at Lạng Sơn that September, imposing a dual rule whose grain seizures bring the famine of late 1944–early 1945; the first risings flare at Bắc Sơn (1940), Nam Kì — where the red flag with gold star first appears (November 1940) — and Đô Lương (1941); at Pác Bó in May 1941 the Eighth Plenum under Nguyễn Ái Quốc declares "only one urgent problem: national liberation", founds the Việt Minh (19 May 1941) and, in December 1944, the Propaganda Liberation Army Team that wins its first fights at Phay Khắt and Nà Ngần; when Japan coups the French on 9 March 1945, the Party's directive of 12 March — "Japan and France shoot each other" — unleashes the anti-Japanese high tide

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 8, the August days

Japan surrenders on 15 August 1945 — the "once-in-a-thousand-years" chance; the Uprising Committee issues Military Order No. 1 (13 August), the Tân Trào congresses elect a National Liberation Committee under Hồ Chí Minh, and the country rises: Thái Nguyên on 16 August, Hanoi on 19 August, Huế on 23 August, Saigon on 24–25 August, the last provinces on 28 August

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 8, the Democratic Republic is proclaimed

Hồ Chí Minh reaches Hanoi on 25 August and on 2 September 1945, at Ba Đình Square, reads the Declaration of Independence — the book quotes its claim that Vietnam has the right to freedom and independence and has in fact become free and independent; the victory smashes the French yoke and Japanese rule together, ends the monarchy, and heartens Cambodia and Laos

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 5 — Bài 6 The August Revolution: Potsdam assigns Indochina's Japanese disarmament to Chinese troops north and British south of the 16th parallel; the atomic bombs and the Soviet entry break Japan, which surrenders on 15-8-1945; the Party founds the National Uprising Committee and issues Military Order No. 1 (13-8), Tân Trào's conference and National Congress (14–17-8) adopt the uprising and Việt Minh's ten policies; Võ Nguyên Giáp's unit marches from Tân Trào, Hanoi rises on 19-8, Huế on 23-8, Saigon on 25-8, the last provinces by 28-8; Bảo Đại abdicates on 30-8 and Hồ Chí Minh reads the Declaration of Independence at Ba Đình on 2-9-1945

a fifteen-year-old party seizes a two-week window between Japan's surrender and the Allies' arrival and takes a whole country

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 6 — Bài 6 Why August 1945 won, and what it means: the subjective causes (a creative Party line, a patriotic united people, fifteen years of preparing forces and bases, political, armed and diplomatic struggle combined) and the objective one (the Allied and Red Army defeat of fascism); the meaning — over 80 years of French rule and nearly five of Japanese ended, a thousand years of monarchy closed, slaves become masters — and the lessons: Party leadership, the great-unity bloc, seizing the moment

Hồ Chí Minh's own verdict is the lesson's headline — the first colonial revolution led to power by a party only fifteen years old

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 7 — Bài 16 Preparing and leading August 1945: back on 28-1-1941, Nguyễn Ái Quốc chairs the 8th Central Conference at Pác Bó (10–19-5-1941), which makes national liberation the task, founds Việt Minh and orders uprising preparation as the centre; the forces are built piece by piece — Việt Minh's programme of unity without distinction of rich or poor, creed or party, the first guerrilla team (11-1941), the Propaganda Liberation Army directive (22-12-1944), the Việt Bắc Liberation Zone (6-1945), the 1942 China mission under his new name; in August 1945, ill and exhausted, he drives the two Tân Trào meetings through — "even if we must burn the whole Trường Sơn range, we must resolutely win independence" — and drafts and reads the Declaration

the revolution's fortnight is run by a sick man in a mountain hut who refuses to let the moment pass

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
