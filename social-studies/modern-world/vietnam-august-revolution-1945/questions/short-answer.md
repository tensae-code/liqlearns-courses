# Vietnam August Revolution 1945 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 14, the August Revolution in Hanoi, Huế and Saigon.
**Answer: Japan surrenders; on 19 August Hanoi's crowds take the Residency, on 23 August Huế rises, on 25 August Saigon rises, and by month's end the general uprising has won across the country (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 14, the August Revolution in Hanoi, Huế and Saigon, printed p. 61.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 8, the road to August: famine, Việt Minh and the Japanese coup.
**Answer: France falls in June 1940 and Japan enters at Lạng Sơn that September, imposing a dual rule whose grain seizures bring the famine of late 1944–early 1945; the first risings flare at Bắc Sơn (1940), Nam Kì — where the red flag with gold star first appears (November 1940) — and Đô Lương (1941); at Pác Bó in May 1941 the Eighth Plenum under Nguyễn Ái Quốc declares "only one urgent problem: national liberation", founds the Việt Minh (19 May 1941) and, in December 1944, the Propaganda Liberation Army Team that wins its first fights at Phay Khắt and Nà Ngần; when Japan coups the French on 9 March 1945, the Party's directive of 12 March — "Japan and France shoot each other" — unleashes the anti-Japanese high tide (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 8, the August days.
**Answer: Japan surrenders on 15 August 1945 — the "once-in-a-thousand-years" chance; the Uprising Committee issues Military Order No. 1 (13 August), the Tân Trào congresses elect a National Liberation Committee under Hồ Chí Minh, and the country rises: Thái Nguyên on 16 August, Hanoi on 19 August, Huế on 23 August, Saigon on 24–25 August, the last provinces on 28 August (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 8, the Democratic Republic is proclaimed.
**Answer: Hồ Chí Minh reaches Hanoi on 25 August and on 2 September 1945, at Ba Đình Square, reads the Declaration of Independence — the book quotes its claim that Vietnam has the right to freedom and independence and has in fact become free and independent; the victory smashes the French yoke and Japanese rule together, ends the monarchy, and heartens Cambodia and Laos (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

(Sub lesson 5)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 6 The August Revolution: Potsdam assigns Indochina's Japanese disarmament to Chinese troops north and British south of the 16th parallel; the atomic bombs and the Soviet entry break Japan, which surrenders on 15-8-1945; the Party founds the National Uprising Committee and issues Military Order No. 1 (13-8), Tân Trào's conference and National Congress (14–17-8) adopt the uprising and Việt Minh's ten policies; Võ Nguyên Giáp's unit marches from Tân Trào, Hanoi rises on 19-8, Huế on 23-8, Saigon on 25-8, the last provinces by 28-8; Bảo Đại abdicates on 30-8 and Hồ Chí Minh reads the Declaration of Independence at Ba Đình on 2-9-1945.
