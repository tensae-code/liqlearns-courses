# Vietnam August Revolution 1945 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 14, the August Revolution in Hanoi, Huế and Saigon
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Japan surrenders; on 19 August Hanoi's crowds take the Residency, on 23 August Huế rises, on 25 August Saigon rises, and by month's end the general uprising has won across the country

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 8, the road to August: famine, Việt Minh and the Japanese coup
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — France falls in June 1940 and Japan enters at Lạng Sơn that September, imposing a dual rule whose grain seizures bring the famine of late 1944–early 1945; the first risings flare at Bắc Sơn (1940), Nam Kì — where the red flag with gold star first appears (November 1940) — and Đô Lương (1941); at Pác Bó in May 1941 the Eighth Plenum under Nguyễn Ái Quốc declares "only one urgent problem: national liberation", founds the Việt Minh (19 May 1941) and, in December 1944, the Propaganda Liberation Army Team that wins its first fights at Phay Khắt and Nà Ngần; when Japan coups the French on 9 March 1945, the Party's directive of 12 March — "Japan and France shoot each other" — unleashes the anti-Japanese high tide

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 8, the August days
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Japan surrenders on 15 August 1945 — the "once-in-a-thousand-years" chance; the Uprising Committee issues Military Order No. 1 (13 August), the Tân Trào congresses elect a National Liberation Committee under Hồ Chí Minh, and the country rises: Thái Nguyên on 16 August, Hanoi on 19 August, Huế on 23 August, Saigon on 24–25 August, the last provinces on 28 August

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 8, the Democratic Republic is proclaimed
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Hồ Chí Minh reaches Hanoi on 25 August and on 2 September 1945, at Ba Đình Square, reads the Declaration of Independence — the book quotes its claim that Vietnam has the right to freedom and independence and has in fact become free and independent; the victory smashes the French yoke and Japanese rule together, ends the monarchy, and heartens Cambodia and Laos

## Sub lesson 5 — Bài 6 The August Revolution: Potsdam assigns Indochina's Japanese disarmament to Chinese troops north and British south of the 16th parallel; the atomic bombs and the Soviet entry break Japan, which surrenders on 15-8-1945; the Party founds the National Uprising Committee and issues Military Order No. 1 (13-8), Tân Trào's conference and National Congress (14–17-8) adopt the uprising and Việt Minh's ten policies; Võ Nguyên Giáp's unit marches from Tân Trào, Hanoi rises on 19-8, Huế on 23-8, Saigon on 25-8, the last provinces by 28-8; Bảo Đại abdicates on 30-8 and Hồ Chí Minh reads the Declaration of Independence at Ba Đình on 2-9-1945
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a fifteen-year-old party seizes a two-week window between Japan's surrender and the Allies' arrival and takes a whole country

## Sub lesson 6 — Bài 6 Why August 1945 won, and what it means: the subjective causes (a creative Party line, a patriotic united people, fifteen years of preparing forces and bases, political, armed and diplomatic struggle combined) and the objective one (the Allied and Red Army defeat of fascism); the meaning — over 80 years of French rule and nearly five of Japanese ended, a thousand years of monarchy closed, slaves become masters — and the lessons: Party leadership, the great-unity bloc, seizing the moment
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Hồ Chí Minh's own verdict is the lesson's headline — the first colonial revolution led to power by a party only fifteen years old

## Sub lesson 7 — Bài 16 Preparing and leading August 1945: back on 28-1-1941, Nguyễn Ái Quốc chairs the 8th Central Conference at Pác Bó (10–19-5-1941), which makes national liberation the task, founds Việt Minh and orders uprising preparation as the centre; the forces are built piece by piece — Việt Minh's programme of unity without distinction of rich or poor, creed or party, the first guerrilla team (11-1941), the Propaganda Liberation Army directive (22-12-1944), the Việt Bắc Liberation Zone (6-1945), the 1942 China mission under his new name; in August 1945, ill and exhausted, he drives the two Tân Trào meetings through — "even if we must burn the whole Trường Sơn range, we must resolutely win independence" — and drafts and reads the Declaration
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the revolution's fortnight is run by a sick man in a mountain hut who refuses to let the moment pass
