# Hands-on — Vietnam Quang Trung Defeats The Qing 1789

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Quang Trung Defeats The Qing 1789 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 8, the marches north: the Trịnh fall and the Lê collapses, 1786–1788, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
