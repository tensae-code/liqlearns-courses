# Kazakhstan Ibrai Altynsarin — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Altynsarin opens schools for Kazakh children, writes their first textbooks and trains their first teachers.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§49–50 Ibrai Altynsarin — outstanding enlightener and innovator-teacher

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§49–50 Ibrai Altynsarin — outstanding enlightener and innovator-teacher

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Altynsarin builds schools, textbooks and a Kazakh alphabet for print.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §57 The innovation of Ibrai Altynsarin

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §57 The innovation of Ibrai Altynsarin

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** schools for girls, a Kazakh reader, teacher training — the innovations are listed and judged.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §6.5 Where does Y. Altynsarin's innovation in the enlightenment field show?

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы. 8-сынып [History of Kazakhstan, Grade 8]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §6.5 Where does Y. Altynsarin's innovation in the enlightenment field show?
