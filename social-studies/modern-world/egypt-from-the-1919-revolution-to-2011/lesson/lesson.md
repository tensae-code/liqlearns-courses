# Egypt — from the 1919 revolution to 2011

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: From the protectorate of 1914 and the revolution of 1919 through Nasser, Sadat and Camp David, Egypt's story in Syria's book ends with the popular revolution of 2011. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt — from the 1919 revolution to 2011

The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fired the revolution of 1919; Britain's declaration of 28 February 1922 ended the protectorate with reservations, and the 1936 treaty followed; the coup of 23 July 1952 made Egypt a republic under Naguib then Nasser — evacuation, nationalisations, the High Dam, the Tripartite Aggression of 1956, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ended Mubarak's rule in 2011.
