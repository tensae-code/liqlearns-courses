## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt — from the 1919 revolution to 2011
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fired the revolution of 1919; Britain's declaration of 28 February 1922 ended the protectorate with reservations, and the 1936 treaty followed; the coup of 23 July 1952 made Egypt a republic under Naguib then Nasser — evacuation, nationalisations, the High Dam, the Tripartite Aggression of 1956, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ended Mubarak's rule in 2011.
