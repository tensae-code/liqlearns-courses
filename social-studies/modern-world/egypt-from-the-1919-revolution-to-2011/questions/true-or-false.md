# Egypt — from the 1919 revolution to 2011 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The protectorate of 1914 and Saad Zaghloul's exile fired the revolution of 1919; Britain's declaration of 28 February 1922 ended the protectorate with reservations, and the 1936 treaty followed; the coup of 23 July 1952 made Egypt a republic under Naguib then Nasser — evacuation, nationalisations, the High Dam, the Tripartite Aggression of 1956, union with Syria 1958–1961, the June 1967 loss of Sinai, Sadat and the October 1973 war and Camp David 1979 — until the popular revolution ended Mubarak's rule in 2011.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4, Lesson مصر منذ الحماية البريطانية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Egypt from the British protectorate to the present)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف التاسع (History, Grade 9)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Egypt — from the 1919 revolution to 2011
