# Hands-on — Egypt — from the 1919 revolution to 2011

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Egypt — from the 1919 revolution to 2011 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Egypt — from the 1919 revolution to 2011, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
