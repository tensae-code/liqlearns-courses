# Video script — Muslim reform movements after 1857

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After the catastrophe of 1857, Muslim India's leaders answer two ways — Sir Syed's Aligarh turns to modern education and the Two-Nation Theory, while Deoband and Nadva guard the faith — and together they shape the road to Pakistan.
