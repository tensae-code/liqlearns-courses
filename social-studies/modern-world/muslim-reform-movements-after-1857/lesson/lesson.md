# Muslim reform movements after 1857

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: After the catastrophe of 1857, Muslim India's leaders answer two ways — Sir Syed's Aligarh turns to modern education and the Two-Nation Theory, while Deoband and Nadva guard the faith — and together they shape the road to Pakistan. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Sir Syed Ahmad Khan

After the catastrophe of 1857, Sir Syed argues the Muslims of India must learn the new knowledge or be left behind — he opens schools at Muradabad and Ghazipur, founds the Scientific Society in 1863, raises the college at Aligarh that becomes a university, and plants the two-nation idea from which Pakistan grows.
