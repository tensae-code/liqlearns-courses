# Muslim reform movements after 1857 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the catastrophe of 1857, Sir Syed argues the Muslims of India must learn the new knowledge or be left behind — he opens schools at Muradabad and Ghazipur, founds the Scientific Society in 1863, raises the college at Aligarh that becomes a university, and plants the two-nation idea from which Pakistan grows.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 4: Historical Personalities — Sir Syed Ahmad Khan (1817–1898)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Studies, Class 5 (PCTB, SNC 2020)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Sir Syed Ahmad Khan

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A defeated community is punished and shut out, and its leaders must decide whether to fight the British or learn from them.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2 Reform Movements: after 1857 Muslims are blamed as rebels, lose jobs and land, fall behind in education as Persian gives way to English

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about After 1857 — a community punished

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Sir Syed tells Muslims to stop mourning the Mughals, take English education, and recognise Hindus and Muslims as two nations.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2: Sir Syed Ahmed Khan argues Muslims were not alone in 1857, urges reconciliation with the British and modern education, and presents the Two-Nation Theory

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Sir Syed and the Two-Nation Theory

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Reform becomes bricks and classrooms, as community-funded colleges carry a generation of Muslims into modern professions.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2: the Aligarh movement builds schools — Muhammadan Educational Conference 1886, Anjuman-i-Himayat-i-Islam and Islamia College Lahore, Sindh Madressa-tul-Islam Karachi, Islamia College Peshawar

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Aligarh builds schools and colleges

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** While Aligarh looks West, Deoband guards the faith, and the two answers to 1857 shape Muslim India together.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.2: Darul-Uloom Deoband (1866) and Nadva-tul-Ulema, Lucknow (1894) answer the same crisis from the religious side, training scholars and teaching trades

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Deoband and Nadva answer from the faith
