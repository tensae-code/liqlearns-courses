# Comintern And The 1918 1923 Revolutionary Wave — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 2, the revolutionary wave and the Communist International.
**Answer: on 9 November 1918 a Berlin general strike becomes an armed rising and the German monarchy falls; England counts 6.5 million strikers (1919–21), France a general strike of over a million (1 May 1920), Italy 1,600 strikes in 1919 and Red Guards in 1920; communist parties spring up (Hungary 1918, France and England 1920, Italy 1921) and in March 1919 the Comintern is founded in Moscow — its 1920 Theses on the national and colonial questions are the very text in which Nguyễn Ái Quốc finds his road; the Comintern is dissolved in 1943 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 9 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 2, the revolutionary wave and the Communist International, printed p. 10.**
