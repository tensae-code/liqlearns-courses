# Video script — Vietnam Founding Of The Communist Party 1930

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Vietnam's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

in 1929 three rival communist organisations appear — the Indochinese Communist Party (Hanoi, June), the Annam Communist Party (Saigon, August) and the Indochinese Communist League (September); Nguyễn Ái Quốc, as the Comintern's envoy, chairs the unification conference at Hương Cảng from 6 January 1930, which adopts the Brief Programme, Strategy and Charter, and the League joins on 24 February; the founding, the Institute of History is quoted as saying, ends the crisis over the road of national salvation
