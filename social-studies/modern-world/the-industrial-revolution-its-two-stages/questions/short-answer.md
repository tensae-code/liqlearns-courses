# The Industrial Revolution — its two stages — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give three marks of the first stage.
**Answer:** The first complex machines spread; early factories were built; steam powered production and transport; coal, iron and steel were produced in bulk; new transport kinds multiplied (any three).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give three marks of the second stage.
**Answer:** Electricity, petroleum and atomic energy as power; science put to building industry; synthetics in bulk; aeroplanes, radio, television and electronic messages; satellites and planetary craft (any three).
