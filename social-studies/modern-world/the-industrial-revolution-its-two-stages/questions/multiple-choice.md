# The Industrial Revolution — its two stages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first Industrial Revolution ran —
a) 1450–1600  b) 1750–1870  c) 1870–2000  d) 1900–1950
**Answer: b) 1750–1870** — 1750–1870: steam, coal, iron and the early factories.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The first stage's power source for production and transport was —
a) Electricity  b) Petroleum  c) Steam  d) Atomic energy
**Answer: c) Steam** — Steam ruled the first stage.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the first stage, which were produced in bulk?
a) Plastic and nylon  b) Coal, iron and steel  c) Radios  d) Satellites
**Answer: b) Coal, iron and steel** — Coal, iron and steel in bulk are first-stage marks.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The second stage's new power sources included —
a) Wind and water only  b) Electricity, petroleum and atomic energy  c) Animal muscle  d) Wood only
**Answer: b) Electricity, petroleum and atomic energy** — After 1870 the power list changed completely.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's verdict on the Industrial Revolution is —
a) Its harms dominated  b) It changed nothing  c) Despite negative results, its benefits dominated  d) It ended in 1870
**Answer: c) Despite negative results, its benefits dominated** — Changed the face of the world; benefits dominated despite negatives.
