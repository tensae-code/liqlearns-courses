# The Industrial Revolution — its two stages — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aeroplanes and television belong to the first stage.
**Answer: False** — They are second-stage inventions, after 1870.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Synthetics like plastic and nylon were produced in bulk in the second stage.
**Answer: True** — Synthetics are a second-stage mark.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The second stage runs to satellites and craft sent to other planets.
**Answer: True** — The book carries the age to satellites and other planets.
