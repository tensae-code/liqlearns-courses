# Hands-on — The Industrial Revolution — its two stages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Sort the inventions
Write these on slips: steam engine, railway, electricity, plastic, radio, satellite, Spinning Jenny, aeroplane, nylon. Sort them into two piles — first stage, second stage. Check against the lesson.

## Verdict debate
The book says benefits dominated despite negatives. With a partner, argue one side each for two minutes, using only goods and harms from this title's lessons.
