# Iceland's late industrialisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iceland industrialised at the same time and in the same way as England.
**Answer: False** — the revolution arrives late, through fish.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Steam trawlers were part of Iceland's late industrialisation.
**Answer: True** — steam trawlers and new towns.
