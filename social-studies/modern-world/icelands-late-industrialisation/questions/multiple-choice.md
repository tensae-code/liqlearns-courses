# Iceland's late industrialisation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iceland's industrial revolution arrived —
a) Before England's  b) Late  c) Never
**Answer: b) Late** — it arrives late to Iceland.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It arrived through —
a) Fish  b) Cotton mills  c) Gold mines
**Answer: a) Fish** — through fish.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The new ships of Iceland's change were —
a) Steam trawlers  b) Viking longships  c) Sailing clippers only
**Answer: a) Steam trawlers** — steam trawlers.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** With the trawlers grew —
a) New towns  b) Castles  c) Deserts
**Answer: a) New towns** — new towns.
