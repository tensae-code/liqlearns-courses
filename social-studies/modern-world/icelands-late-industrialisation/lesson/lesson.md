# Iceland's late industrialisation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The industrial revolution arrives late to Iceland — not through cotton mills, but through fish, steam trawlers and new towns. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The revolution arrives late, through fish

the industrial revolution arrives late to Iceland, through fish (Síðbúin atvinnubylting á Íslandi, student book pp. 54–63)
