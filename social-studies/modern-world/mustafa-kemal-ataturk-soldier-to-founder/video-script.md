# Video script — Mustafa Kemal Atatürk — soldier to founder

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Turkey's Grade 12 book opens the republic's story with a schoolboy. Mustafa Kemal climbs a ladder of classrooms: the Şemsi Efendi school, the military schools of Salonica and Monastir, the War College and the Academy.

Then the postings season him — from Damascus to Sofia, an empire's frontiers under one officer's eyes.

The founder is introduced the Turkish way: formed by teachers before he is a general. The classrooms come first.
