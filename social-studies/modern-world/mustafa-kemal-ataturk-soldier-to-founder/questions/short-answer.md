# Mustafa Kemal Atatürk — soldier to founder — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell his education ladder in order.
**Answer: Şemsi Efendi school; Salonica and Monastir military schools; the War College and Academy (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book begin with the founder's schooling?
**Answer: To show the founder was made — formed by teachers — before he was a general (any close wording).**
