# Mustafa Kemal Atatürk — soldier to founder — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mustafa Kemal's first school named in the book is —
a) The War College  b) The Şemsi Efendi school  c) The Academy
**Answer: b) The Şemsi Efendi school** — the ladder's first rung.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** His military schools were in —
a) Salonica and Monastir  b) Damascus and Sofia  c) Ankara and İzmir
**Answer: a) Salonica and Monastir** — before the War College and Academy.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** His early postings ran —
a) Only in Istanbul  b) From Damascus to Sofia  c) Only at sea
**Answer: b) From Damascus to Sofia** — an empire's worth of frontiers.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The book introduces the founder as —
a) A born ruler  b) A wealthy merchant  c) A schoolboy formed by teachers before he is a general
**Answer: c) A schoolboy formed by teachers before he is a general** — the Turkish way of opening the story.
