# Hands-on — Mustafa Kemal Atatürk — soldier to founder

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The ladder of schools

Draw Mustafa Kemal's education as a ladder of four rungs — Şemsi Efendi, Salonica, Monastir, War College and Academy. Beside your own name, draw your ladder so far, and one rung you hope to climb.

## Postings on a map

On a rough map of the old empire, mark Damascus and Sofia and join them with the road of one officer's postings. Write what an officer learns by serving at an empire's far ends.

## Formed by teachers

Write three lines: one teacher who formed the schoolboy in the book's telling, one teacher who has formed you, and what each gave that a battlefield could not.
