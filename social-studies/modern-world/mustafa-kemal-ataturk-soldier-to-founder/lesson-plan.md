# Lesson plan — Mustafa Kemal Atatürk — soldier to founder

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Mustafa Kemal Atatürk — soldier to founder
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 1, Section A, Mustafa Kemal's Education and Military Life: Şemsi Efendi school, Salonica and Monastir military schools, the War College and Academy; postings from Damascus to Sofia.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: the republic's founder is introduced the Turkish way, as a schoolboy formed by teachers before he is a general. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell Mustafa Kemal's education ladder in order: Şemsi Efendi school; Salonica and Monastir military schools; the War College and Academy.
2. Name the span of his early postings: from Damascus to Sofia.
3. Say how the book introduces him: as a schoolboy formed by teachers before he is a general.
4. Explain, in their own words, why a national history might open with one man's schooling.
