# Video script — Finland — old occupations in photographs

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Finland's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Lift girls at Stockmann in 1930, a gas-lamp cleaner up his ladder in 1912, a shoeblack boy at the station in Olympic 1952 and log-floaters on the Vantaa river show trades that have vanished or changed beyond recognition.
