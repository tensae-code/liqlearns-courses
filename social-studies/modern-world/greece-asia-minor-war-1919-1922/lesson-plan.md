## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Entente and Greek claims on the Ottoman Empire** — the victors carve up the Ottoman lands; Greece lands at Smyrna in 1919 with Allied blessing
2. **The Greeks of western Asia Minor and Pontus** — ancient Greek communities of the coast and Pontus, prosperous under late Ottoman rule, are caught between two nationalisms
3. **The Turkish national movement** — Mustafa Kemal rejects the Sèvres treaty, builds an army in Anatolia and fights Greeks, French and Armenians
4. **The Asia Minor war (1919–1922)** — Greece advances to the Sakarya, stalls, and in August 1922 its front collapses; Smyrna burns and a million refugees cross the Aegean
5. **Greece and Turkey after the Asia Minor war** — the Lausanne treaty and the compulsory exchange of populations close the story; a republic is proclaimed in Greece after the king falls
6. **The Asia Minor war (1918–1923) and the Treaty of Lausanne** — from the Smyrna landing and diplomatic triumph to the 1922 collapse and the population exchange
7. **The Asia Minor War (1919–1922)** — the Greek advance to Ankara breaks at the Sakarya; the 1922 catastrophe, the burning of Smyrna and the Lausanne exchange end the Great Idea
8. **Theme 3 B: The Asia Minor Catastrophe — the exodus** — the front collapses in August 1922 and hundreds of thousands flee burning Smyrna and the interior by any boat that floats
