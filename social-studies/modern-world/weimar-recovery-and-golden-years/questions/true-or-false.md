# Weimar recovery and golden years — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stresemann's Rentenmark, the Dawes Plan's American loans and restarted reparations steady the economy from 1924 — a recovery built on borrowed money.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 3.1 Reasons for economic recovery

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *GCSE History for Edexcel: Weimar and Nazi Germany, 1918–39* is about 3.2 Stresemann's policies abroad.
**Answer: False** — it is about 3.1 Reasons for economic recovery

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Stresemann wins Locarno, League of Nations entry (1926) and the Young Plan, restoring Germany's standing abroad; his death in October 1929 removes the republic's most trusted leader weeks before the Crash.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 3.2 Stresemann's policies abroad

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *GCSE History for Edexcel: Weimar and Nazi Germany, 1918–39* is about 3.1 Reasons for economic recovery.
**Answer: False** — it is about 3.2 Stresemann's policies abroad

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Wages and welfare rise in the 'golden years', yet farmers, the Mittelstand and those ruined in 1923 never share the recovery and feel ignored by the republic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 4.1 The standard of living

**Answer: False** — it is about 4.1 The standard of living
