# Lesson plan — Cuba War Of 1895 Begins

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Cuba War Of 1895 Begins
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Cuba, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** on 24 Feb 1895 risings across Cuba restart the war Martí organised; he lands to fight and falls at Dos Ríos on 19 May
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **7.1 The war Martí called necessary** — on 24 Feb 1895 risings across Cuba restart the war Martí organised; he lands to fight and falls at Dos Ríos on 19 May
2. **Ch.7 activities + closing list of outstanding dates: War of 95, incl. Weyler's reconcentration order and the Manifiesto de Montecristi fragment** — pupils read the enemy's order and Martí–Gómez's manifesto side by side and pick out each side's purpose
3. **5.1–5.2 Start of the War of 1895; course of the war to October 1895** — simultaneous risings, Martí's death at Dos Ríos, Gómez and Maceo reunited — grief and organisation in the same months
4. **2.3 The Revolution of 1895** — the 1895 war whole: invasion, reconcentration, US intervention and the frustrated republic — pre-university depth on the primary book's story

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
