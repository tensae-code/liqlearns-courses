# Vatican Ii And The Critique Of Consumer Society — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Vatican II opens the Church's windows while youth counterculture (Storia culturale), Angela Davis' autobiography (book study) and the Black Panthers' programme (Document) contest the affluent society from inside.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The demand for renewal in the Church; The critique of consumer society: the contestation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The demand for renewal in the Church; The critique of consumer society: the contestation

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Vatican II and secularisation (Menozzi), Don Milani's duty not to obey, Populorum Progressio and Boff's liberation theology (Fare storia pieces) — the Church argues with modernity from inside it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 11 §9 La Chiesa e il Concilio

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 11 §9 La Chiesa e il Concilio
