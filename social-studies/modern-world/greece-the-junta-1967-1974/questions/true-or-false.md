# Greece The Junta 1967 1974 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Deepening political crisis and the dictatorship of 21 April 1967 (1963–1974)

**Answer: False** — it is about Deepening political crisis and the dictatorship of 21 April 1967 (1963–1974)
