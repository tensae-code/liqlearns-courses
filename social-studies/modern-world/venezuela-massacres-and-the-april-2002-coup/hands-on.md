# Hands-on — Venezuela Massacres And The April 2002 Coup

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela Massacres And The April 2002 Coup — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Cantaura, Yumare, El Amparo, the Caracazo dead and 11 April 2002, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
