## Sub lesson 1 — Yemeni resistance to Ottoman rule
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — yemen answers occupation with its mountains — fortresses like Thula, Kawkaban and Shahara, cave-and-pass warfare, and Imams who will side with a just Imam against a tyrant wali or the reverse, caring only for justice; Zabid and Aden resist from the first day, the southern plains tribes raid, Taiz and Ibb bleed the columns in Wadi Dhubab, Imam Sharaf al-Din's sons maul the army marching on Sanaa, and al-Qasim's revolt rolls from Aden to Saada until al-Mu'ayyad and Muhammad bin al-Qasim drive the Ottomans out entirely in 1635; in the second rule the pattern repeats — Sanaa besieged in 1892, Shahara's slaughter earning Yemen the Ottoman name "graveyard of Anatolia", uprisings everywhere forcing the moderate Husayn Hilmi Pasha in 1908, revolt and siege again in 1910, the Da'an peace of 1911, and Imam Yahya entering Sanaa in 1918
