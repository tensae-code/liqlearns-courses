Yemen's own way (Book 7 — G9 History Part 1 (student book, Arabic)): an older-style textbook — dense text, maps and timelines — that teaches modern Arab history as one long struggle of the umma against foreign domination, with Yemen's own Ottoman and British centuries given a full unit of their own and the preface itself telling the story through 1962, the siege of Sanaa and the Seventy Days.
