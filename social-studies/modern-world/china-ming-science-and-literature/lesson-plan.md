**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Li Shizhen's Compendium of Materia Medica records 1,800 drugs and 11,000 prescriptions and travels the world in translation, while Song Yingxing's Tiangong Kaiwu pictures 18 trades at work
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ming science, building and literature** — Li Shizhen's Compendium of Materia Medica records 1,800 drugs and 11,000 prescriptions and travels the world in translation, while Song Yingxing's Tiangong Kaiwu pictures 18 trades at work
2. **Ming-Qing culture develops** — the Ming-Qing cultural flowering — science, novels and art of a society rich in goods but ruled ever more tightly
3. **Ming to mid-Qing economy and culture** — sprouts of capitalism in Jiangnan workshops, the great novels, Xu Guangqi's science with Ricci, and evidential scholarship under the inquisition's shadow
