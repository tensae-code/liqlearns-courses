# China Ming Science And Literature — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Li Shizhen's Compendium of Materia Medica records 1,800 drugs and 11,000 prescriptions and travels the world in translation, while Song Yingxing's Tiangong Kaiwu pictures 18 trades at work.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ming science, building and literature

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ming science, building and literature

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Ming-Qing cultural flowering — science, novels and art of a society rich in goods but ruled ever more tightly.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ming-Qing culture develops

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ming-Qing culture develops

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Sprouts of capitalism in Jiangnan workshops, the great novels, Xu Guangqi's science with Ricci, and evidential scholarship under the inquisition's shadow.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ming to mid-Qing economy and culture
