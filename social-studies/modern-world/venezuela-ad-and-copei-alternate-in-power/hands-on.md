# Hands-on — Venezuela Ad And Copei Alternate In Power

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela Ad And Copei Alternate In Power — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, AD and COPEI take turns in the Presidency, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
