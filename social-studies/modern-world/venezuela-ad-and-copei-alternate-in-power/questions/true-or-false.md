# Venezuela Ad And Copei Alternate In Power — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two parties alternate in power for decades while oil income rises and falls and the pact system slowly wears out.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in AD and COPEI take turns in the Presidency

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 6to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about AD and COPEI take turns in the Presidency

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Caldera pacifies the guerrillas, Pérez nationalises oil and iron amid the boom — the two-party alternation at its height.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in First governments of Pérez and Caldera (1969–1979)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Nuestra Historia Republicana — Ciencias Sociales, 2do año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about First governments of Pérez and Caldera (1969–1979)
