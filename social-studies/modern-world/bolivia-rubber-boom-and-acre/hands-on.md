# Hands-on — Bolivia — the rubber boom and Acre

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bolivia — the rubber boom and Acre — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, El auge de la goma y el Acre, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
