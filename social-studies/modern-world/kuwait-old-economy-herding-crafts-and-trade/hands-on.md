# Hands-on — Old Kuwait's economy — herding, crafts and the sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw the samil and label how churning works, in your own words.

## Task 2
List everything a palm tree gives a settlement.

## Task 3
Design a household goods list for an old Kuwait home and mark which craft made each item.

## Task 4
Write three sentences on what is gained and lost when crafts give way to factories.

## Task 5
Sketch a Gulf sailing ship and label the builder's jobs: timber, planking, sails, launching.

## Task 6
Write a fisherman's dawn: four sentences on the oldest grounds.
