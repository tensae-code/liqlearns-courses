# Old Kuwait's economy — herding, crafts and the sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Describe a herding day in old Kuwait.
**Answer: Herders water the flocks while women milk them and churn the milk in the leather samil (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What two kinds of farming fed the settlements?
**Answer: Palm farming and field farming.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** What materials did the old Gulf crafts use?
**Answer: Local wool, clay and fibre (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did every household need the crafts?
**Answer: Because the crafts made the goods every household needs from local materials.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What are the twin crafts of this lesson?
**Answer: Shipbuilding and fishing (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the twin crafts do for the coast?
**Answer: They fed it (fishing) and carried it (ships).**
