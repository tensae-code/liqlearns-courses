# Old Kuwait's economy — herding, crafts and the sea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Old Kuwait's food came mostly from shops and imports.
**Answer: False** — herding and palm and field farming fed the settlements

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The samil is a leather vessel for churning milk.
**Answer: True** — milk is churned in the leather samil

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Old Gulf households bought all their goods ready-made.
**Answer: False** — the crafts made the goods every household needs

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Craft work was social — women gathered to work wool together.
**Answer: True** — women gather to work it

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Gulf ships were all bought from India.
**Answer: False** — builders raise the Gulf's named sailing ships

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Fishing in the Gulf is an ancient livelihood.
**Answer: True** — its grounds are some of the oldest in the region
