# Old Kuwait's economy — herding, crafts and the sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Milk was churned in —
a) the leather samil  b) a clay oven  c) a wooden barrel
**Answer: a) the leather samil** — women churn the milk in the leather samil

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The settlements were fed by —
a) palm and field farming  b) rice imports  c) factory farms
**Answer: a) palm and field farming** — palm and field farming feed the settlements

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** At the flocks, women —
a) milked them and churned the milk  b) built the ships  c) dived for pearls
**Answer: a) milked them and churned the milk** — women milk them and churn the milk

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The old crafts turned local wool, clay and fibre into —
a) the goods every household needs  b) export luxuries only  c) ship sails only
**Answer: a) the goods every household needs** — the crafts turn them into the goods every household needs

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Wool work began with —
a) clipping the wool  b) buying yarn  c) burning the fleece
**Answer: a) clipping the wool** — wool is clipped first

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Women's part in the crafts was —
a) gathering to work the wool  b) none  c) only selling
**Answer: a) gathering to work the wool** — women gather to work it

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The Gulf's sailing ships were —
a) built by local builders, each kind with its name  b) imported ready-made  c) made of iron
**Answer: a) built by local builders, each kind with its name** — builders raise the Gulf's named sailing ships

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** The fishing grounds of the Gulf are —
a) some of the oldest in the region  b) newly discovered  c) barren
**Answer: a) some of the oldest in the region** — fishermen work some of the oldest grounds in the region

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** Shipbuilding and fishing together —
a) fed and carried the coast  b) fed the coast only  c) carried goods only
**Answer: a) fed and carried the coast** — the twin crafts that fed and carried the coast
