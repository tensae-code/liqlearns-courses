# Hands-on — The Fall of Kokand and the Creation of Russian Turkestan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Fall of Kokand and the Creation of Russian Turkestan — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch. 2 §§12–13 The conquest of the Kokand Khanate and its inclusion in the Turkestan Governor-Generalship, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
