# Transylvania Under The Habsburgs — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lecția 13: Transilvania sub dominația habsburgică.
**Answer: Vienna makes Transylvania its south-eastern military bastion, annexes Oltenia toward the Lower Danube, and reforms every corner of life — Bishop Inochentie Micu pleads the Romanians' cause (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a VII-a* (Moldova), Lecția 13: Transilvania sub dominația habsburgică, printed p. 56.**
