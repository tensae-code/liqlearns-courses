# Video script — The East African Community — rise, collapse and revival

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uganda's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The High Commission of 1946 becomes EACSO, then the Community of Amin, Kenyatta and Nyerere's portraits collapses — trade declines, movement is restricted, enmity grows — and is revived for common taxes, peace and unity.
