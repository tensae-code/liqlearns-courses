# The Truman Doctrine and the Marshall Plan

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Yalta and Potsdam paper over a split; the atomic race and Soviet spies deepen it; Stalin builds his 1945–48 buffer of communist governments, and Truman answers not with armies but money — the Doctrine of 12 March 1947 ("misery and want" are communism's roots) and the Marshall Plan's US$13 billion to sixteen countries (1948–52), which Moscow forbids its satellites to touch. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
