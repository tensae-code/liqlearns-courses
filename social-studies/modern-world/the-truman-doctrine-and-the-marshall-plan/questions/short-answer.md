# The Truman Doctrine and the Marshall Plan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Part 5, Unit 2.
**Answer: Yalta and Potsdam paper over a split; the atomic race and Soviet spies deepen it; Stalin builds his 1945–48 buffer of communist governments, and Truman answers not with armies but money — the Doctrine of 12 March 1947 ("misery and want" are communism's roots) and the Marshall Plan's US$13 billion to sixteen countries (1948–52), which Moscow forbids its satellites to touch (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *History, Year 13* (Samoa), Part 5, Unit 2, printed pp. 110–115.**
