# Sri Lanka — The Portuguese arrive, 1505

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A storm blows Lourenço de Almeida into Galle in 1505, the strangers in strange dress are led to Kotte by a road made long on purpose — "like going to the Portuguese fort" becomes a proverb. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
