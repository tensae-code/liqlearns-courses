# The Romanian Principalities Economy And Society 1650 1800 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under Ottoman suzerainty the Principalities keep medieval relations while market seeds sprout; boyar tables take oriental spices and olives while peasants rarely taste meat.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lecția 10: Principatele Române: economie și societate

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a VII-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lecția 10: Principatele Române: economie și societate

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After long war and calamity, harvests grow and towns fill with locksmiths, armourers and masons, but the Porte's excessive demands brake the Principalities' integration into the market.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §4: Dezvoltarea economică a Ţărilor Române în secolele XVII–XVIII
