# Kazakhstan The Completion Of Russian Annexation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Russian columns take Turkestan, Shymkent and Tashkent's approaches, joining the Orenburg and Siberian lines and ending the annexation.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§29–30 The completion of Kazakhstan's joining to the Russian Empire

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§29–30 The completion of Kazakhstan's joining to the Russian Empire

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cherniaev's columns take Shymkent and Aulie-Ata and the south is annexed.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§31–32 The joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§31–32 The joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the south is taken by storm from Kokand, not by oath — a different annexation.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §4.7 What are the features of the joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire?

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы. 8-сынып [History of Kazakhstan, Grade 8]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §4.7 What are the features of the joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire?
