# Kazakhstan The Completion Of Russian Annexation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §§29–30 The completion of Kazakhstan's joining to the Russian Empire.
**Answer: Russian columns take Turkestan, Shymkent and Tashkent's approaches, joining the Orenburg and Siberian lines and ending the annexation (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Kazakhstan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* (Kazakhstan), §§29–30 The completion of Kazakhstan's joining to the Russian Empire, printed p. 104.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: §§31–32 The joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire.
**Answer: Cherniaev's columns take Shymkent and Aulie-Ata and the south is annexed (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Kazakhstan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* (Kazakhstan), §§31–32 The joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire, printed p. 92.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: §4.7 What are the features of the joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire?.
**Answer: the south is taken by storm from Kokand, not by oath — a different annexation (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Kazakhstan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Қазақстан тарихы. 8-сынып [History of Kazakhstan, Grade 8]* (Kazakhstan), §4.7 What are the features of the joining of Kazakhstan's southern regions to the Russian Empire?, printed p. 97.**
