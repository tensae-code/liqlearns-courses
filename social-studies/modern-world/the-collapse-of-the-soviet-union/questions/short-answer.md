# The Collapse Of The Soviet Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 4, Cap. 18 §7 The implosion of the USSR.
**Answer: Gorbachev's "common European home" (Document) meets republics walking out; Darnton's Document stands at the fallen Berlin Wall — the USSR dissolves without the war everyone feared (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 4, Cap. 18 §7 The implosion of the USSR, printed p. 632.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Modulo 14 §§1–4 Un impero in crisi; Il crollo del Muro di Berlino e la riunificazione tedesca; La fine delle "democrazie popolari"; La dissoluzione dell'Urss.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
