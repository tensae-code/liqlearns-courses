# The Collapse Of The Soviet Union — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gorbachev's "common European home" (Document) meets republics walking out; Darnton's Document stands at the fallen Berlin Wall — the USSR dissolves without the war everyone feared.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 18 §7 The implosion of the USSR

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 18 §7 The implosion of the USSR

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 9 November 1989 (Eventi) opens the Wall; Bongiovanni, Darnton at the Wall and Maier on reunification (Fare storia pieces) argue the causes — an empire ends by implosion, not invasion.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 14 §§1–4 Un impero in crisi; Il crollo del Muro di Berlino e la riunificazione tedesca; La fine delle "democrazie popolari"; La dissoluzione dell'Urss

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
