# Video script — Vietnam Resistance To France 1858 1862

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Vietnam's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

on 1 September 1858 the Franco-Spanish fleet attacks Đà Nẵng meaning to win quickly and strike at Huế; Nguyễn Tri Phương's soldiers and the Đà Nẵng people fight so fiercely that the "quick attack, quick victory" plan collapses; the French swing south, take Gia Định (February 1859) and build their bridgehead while the court wavers and the people rise on their own; Nguyễn Tri Phương builds the great Chí Hoà entrenched camp, which falls on 24 February 1861, and the three eastern provinces of Nam Kì follow — yet Nguyễn Trung Trực's men still burn the French warship Espérance on the Nhật Tảo river that December; in 1862 the court signs the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất, recognising French rule over the three provinces and Côn Lôn island and an indemnity of some 20 million quan
