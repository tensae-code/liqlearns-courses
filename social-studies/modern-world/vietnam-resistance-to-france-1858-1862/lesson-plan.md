# Lesson plan — Vietnam Resistance To France 1858 1862

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Resistance To France 1858 1862
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** on 1 September 1858 the Franco-Spanish fleet attacks Đà Nẵng meaning to win quickly and strike at Huế; Nguyễn Tri Phương's soldiers and the Đà Nẵng people fight so fiercely that the "quick attack, quick victory" plan collapses; the French swing south, take Gia Định (February 1859) and build their bridgehead while the court wavers and the people rise on their own; Nguyễn Tri Phương builds the great Chí Hoà entrenched camp, which falls on 24 February 1861, and the three eastern provinces of Nam Kì follow — yet Nguyễn Trung Trực's men still burn the French warship Espérance on the Nhật Tảo river that December; in 1862 the court signs the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất, recognising French rule over the three provinces and Côn Lôn island and an indemnity of some 20 million quan
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
