# Hands-on — Vietnam Resistance To France 1858 1862

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Resistance To France 1858 1862 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 17, Đà Nẵng, Chí Hoà and the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
