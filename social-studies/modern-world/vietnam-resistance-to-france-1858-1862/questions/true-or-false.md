# Vietnam Resistance To France 1858 1862 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** on 1 September 1858 the Franco-Spanish fleet attacks Đà Nẵng meaning to win quickly and strike at Huế; Nguyễn Tri Phương's soldiers and the Đà Nẵng people fight so fiercely that the "quick attack, quick victory" plan collapses; the French swing south, take Gia Định (February 1859) and build their bridgehead while the court wavers and the people rise on their own; Nguyễn Tri Phương builds the great Chí Hoà entrenched camp, which falls on 24 February 1861, and the three eastern provinces of Nam Kì follow — yet Nguyễn Trung Trực's men still burn the French warship Espérance on the Nhật Tảo river that December; in 1862 the court signs the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất, recognising French rule over the three provinces and Côn Lôn island and an indemnity of some 20 million quan.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 17, Đà Nẵng, Chí Hoà and the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 17, Đà Nẵng, Chí Hoà and the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất
