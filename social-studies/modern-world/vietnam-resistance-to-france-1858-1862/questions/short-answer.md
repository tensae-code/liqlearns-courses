# Vietnam Resistance To France 1858 1862 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 17, Đà Nẵng, Chí Hoà and the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất.
**Answer: on 1 September 1858 the Franco-Spanish fleet attacks Đà Nẵng meaning to win quickly and strike at Huế; Nguyễn Tri Phương's soldiers and the Đà Nẵng people fight so fiercely that the "quick attack, quick victory" plan collapses; the French swing south, take Gia Định (February 1859) and build their bridgehead while the court wavers and the people rise on their own; Nguyễn Tri Phương builds the great Chí Hoà entrenched camp, which falls on 24 February 1861, and the three eastern provinces of Nam Kì follow — yet Nguyễn Trung Trực's men still burn the French warship Espérance on the Nhật Tảo river that December; in 1862 the court signs the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất, recognising French rule over the three provinces and Côn Lôn island and an indemnity of some 20 million quan (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 17, Đà Nẵng, Chí Hoà and the Treaty of Nhâm Tuất, printed p. 77.**
