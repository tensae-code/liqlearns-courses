# Big business and industrial America, 1870–1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Edison is this lesson's emblem of —
a) The lone farmer  b) The inventor lighting the age  c) The union leader
**Answer: b) The inventor lighting the age** — inventors like Edison light the age.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inventions became —
a) Illegal  b) Industries and corporations  c) Smaller workshops only
**Answer: b) Industries and corporations** — inventions become industries and corporations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Big business was built on —
a) The backs of labour  b) No workers at all  c) Imports only
**Answer: a) The backs of labour** — built on the backs of labour.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Labour answered back with —
a) Strikes and unions  b) Silence  c) Emigration only
**Answer: a) Strikes and unions** — whose strikes and unions answer back.
