# Big business and industrial America, 1870–1900 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The corporation became industrial America's ruling shape.
**Answer: True** — Industries became corporations big enough to dominate whole markets.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Workers welcomed every condition without protest.
**Answer: False** — Labour answered back with strikes and unions.
