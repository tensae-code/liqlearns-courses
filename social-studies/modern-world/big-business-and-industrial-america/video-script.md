# Video script — Big business and industrial America, 1870–1900

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Edison lit the age. Inventions became corporations, corporations rode on labour's backs — and labour, in strikes and unions, answered back.

Inventors like Edison lit the age — literally, with electric light — and invention itself became a business, laboratories turning ideas into products on an industrial rhythm.

Inventions became industries, and industries became corporations — organisations big enough to dominate whole markets. The corporation, not the lone owner, became industrial America's ruling shape.

The corporations were built on the backs of labour: long hours, dangerous work, low pay. Labour answered back with strikes and unions — the age of big business is also the age organised labour was born in. A new consumer culture sold the products to everyone, workers included.
