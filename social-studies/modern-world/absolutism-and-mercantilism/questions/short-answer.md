# Absolutism And Mercantilism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Absolutismo y Mercantilismo.
**Answer: Felipe II consolidates absolutism on American gold and silver, and Louis XIV makes divine right its maximum expression, while mercantilist protection guards the colonial empires' wealth (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
