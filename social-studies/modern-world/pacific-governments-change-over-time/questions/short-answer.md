# Pacific governments — change over time — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Change of governments over time in the Pacific Region / Election processes in the Pacific Region.
**Answer: Governments mutate — traditional systems, colonial rule, then reorganised participation: Samoa's Legislative Council majority in 1947, French territories' autonomy in 1956, Western Samoa first to independence in 1962; elections every five years now make and unmake Pacific governments, with coups in Fiji and crises in Vanuatu and Solomon Islands as the exceptions that test the rule (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
