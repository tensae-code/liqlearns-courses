# Southeast Asia Colonised Islands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the spice islands are the doorway, and four empires fight over the key for four centuries.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 5 The islands fall first: Portugal's fleet takes Malacca in 1511 (the Flor de Mar); Spain rules the Philippines from the mid-1500s until America takes them in 1898 — Lapu-Lapu's 1521 victory remembered as the first resistance; the Dutch complete Indonesia only in the mid-1800s; Britain takes Malaya, Singapore and Brunei

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử 11 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 5 The islands fall first: Portugal's fleet takes Malacca in 1511 (the Flor de Mar); Spain rules the Philippines from the mid-1500s until America takes them in 1898 — Lapu-Lapu's 1521 victory remembered as the first resistance; the Dutch complete Indonesia only in the mid-1800s; Britain takes Malaya, Singapore and Brunei
