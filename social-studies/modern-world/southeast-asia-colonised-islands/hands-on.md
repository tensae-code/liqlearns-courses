# Hands-on — Southeast Asia Colonised Islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Southeast Asia Colonised Islands — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 5 The islands fall first: Portugal's fleet takes Malacca in 1511 (the Flor de Mar); Spain rules the Philippines from the mid-1500s until America takes them in 1898 — Lapu-Lapu's 1521 victory remembered as the first resistance; the Dutch complete Indonesia only in the mid-1800s; Britain takes Malaya, Singapore and Brunei, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
