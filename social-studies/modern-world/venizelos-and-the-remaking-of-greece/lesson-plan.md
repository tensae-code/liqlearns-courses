# Lesson plan — Venizelos And The Remaking Of Greece

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Venizelos And The Remaking Of Greece
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** officers revolt at Goudi in 1909 and call the Cretan Venizelos, who rewrites the constitution and rebuilds army and state
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit E, ch. 2 The Goudi movement and the Venizelos government** — officers revolt at Goudi in 1909 and call the Cretan Venizelos, who rewrites the constitution and rebuilds army and state
2. **Unit E, ch. 4 Greece in the First World War** — Venizelos and King Constantine split the country in the National Schism — two governments, Thessaloniki against Athens — before Greece joins the Allies
3. **Unit E exercise dossier, portrait task: Eleftherios Venizelos** — a source portrait of Venizelos anchors the 20th-century unit — the statesman as the thread from Goudi to Sèvres
4. **§27 The Goudi movement (1909)** — the Military League's pronunciamento forces reform and summons Venizelos from Crete
5. **§28 Eleftherios Venizelos as prime minister: Venizelist policy 1910–1912** — a revised constitution, a rebuilt army and navy, and alliances prepared in two feverish years
6. **§32 Greece in the First World War — the National Schism** — king and prime minister split Greece into two states, Thessaloniki against Athens, until exile settles it

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
