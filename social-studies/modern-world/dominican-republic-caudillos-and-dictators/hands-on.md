# Hands-on — Dominican Republic — caudillos and dictators

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Dominican Republic — caudillos and dictators — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unidad 7 Impacto de las dictaduras y el caudillismo: gobiernos rojos y azules y Gobierno de los Seis Años, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
