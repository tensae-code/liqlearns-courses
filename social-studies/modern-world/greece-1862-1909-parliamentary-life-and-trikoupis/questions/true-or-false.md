# Greece 1862 1909 Parliamentary Life And Trikoupis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a new Danish king, Trikoupis modernises and bankrupts the state, the 1897 war is lost, and the century ends in frustration.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in From Otto's expulsion (1862) to the Goudi movement (1909)

**Answer: False** — it is about From Otto's expulsion (1862) to the Goudi movement (1909)
