# Poland's painters under partition — Matejko and history painting — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Matejko's enormous canvases of Grunwald and the old Commonwealth's triumphs and sins hang in Kraków.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — A partitioned nation paints its memory to survive — Matejko's enormous canvases of Grunwald and the old Commonwealth's triumphs and sins hang in Kraków as a substitute parliament of the imagination, teaching Poles their history when schools could not.
