# Video script — Poland's painters under partition — Matejko and history painting

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: people, places, what happened, what changed. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A partitioned nation paints its memory to survive.

Matejko's enormous canvases of Grunwald and the old Commonwealth's triumphs and sins hang in Kraków.

The canvases work as a substitute parliament of the imagination, teaching Poles their history when schools could not.

That is the story: a partitioned nation paints its memory to survive — Matejko's enormous canvases of Grunwald and the old Commonwealth's triumphs and sins hang in Kraków as a substitute parliament of the imagination, teaching Poles their history when schools could not.
