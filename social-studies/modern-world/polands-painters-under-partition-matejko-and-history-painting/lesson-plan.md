# Lesson plan — Poland's painters under partition — Matejko and history painting

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Poland's painters under partition — Matejko and history painting
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A partitioned nation paints its memory to survive — Matejko's enormous canvases of Grunwald and the old Commonwealth's triumphs and sins hang in Kraków as a substitute parliament of the imagination, teaching Poles their history when schools could not.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.
