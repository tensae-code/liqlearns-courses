# Belarus — culture, education and science after the war — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Schools, universities and the Academy of Sciences are rebuilt from ruins and a new educated generation rises
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Revival of education and science in the postwar BSSR

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History of Belarus, Grade 11 (Famin)* is about Social and political life in the Thaw.
**Answer: False** — it is about Revival of education and science in the postwar BSSR

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A writers' union shrunk to 47 members by war regrows around Melezh, Shamyakin and Bryl, but a 1947 party decree orders literature back into line
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Literature and art

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History of Belarus, Grade 11 (Famin)* is about Social and economic development in the Thaw.
**Answer: False** — it is about Literature and art
