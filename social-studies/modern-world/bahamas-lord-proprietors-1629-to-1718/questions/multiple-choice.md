# Bahamas — Lord Proprietors, 1629 to 1718 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Lord Proprietors: the kings give the islands away  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Lord Proprietors: the kings give the islands away** — in 1629 Charles I grants The Bahamas and the Carolinas to Sir Robert Heath; the Adventurers even donate money to help build Harvard College; in 1670 Charles II hands the islands to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina, who want profit but show little interest and are refused their rents — their rule ends in 1718 with Woodes Rogers

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Lord Proprietors: the kings give the islands away  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Lord Proprietors: the kings give the islands away** — the book's own heading for this part.
