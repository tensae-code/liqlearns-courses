# Bahamas — Lord Proprietors, 1629 to 1718 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Lord Proprietors: the kings give the islands away.
**Answer: in 1629 Charles I grants The Bahamas and the Carolinas to Sir Robert Heath; the Adventurers even donate money to help build Harvard College; in 1670 Charles II hands the islands to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina, who want profit but show little interest and are refused their rents — their rule ends in 1718 with Woodes Rogers (any close wording).**
