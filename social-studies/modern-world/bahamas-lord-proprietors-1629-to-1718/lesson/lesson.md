# Bahamas — Lord Proprietors, 1629 to 1718

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In 1629 Charles I grants The Bahamas away, and in 1670 Charles II hands the islands to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina, who want profit but show little interest and are refused their rents — their rule ends in 1718 with Woodes Rogers. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lord Proprietors: the kings give the islands away

in 1629 Charles I grants The Bahamas and the Carolinas to Sir Robert Heath; the Adventurers even donate money to help build Harvard College; in 1670 Charles II hands the islands to the Lords Proprietors of Carolina, who want profit but show little interest and are refused their rents — their rule ends in 1718 with Woodes Rogers (Lord Proprietors: the kings give the islands away)
