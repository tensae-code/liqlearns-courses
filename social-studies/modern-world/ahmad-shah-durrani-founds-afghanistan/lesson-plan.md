**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Ahmad Shah gathers the divided country into one state again, and pupils debate the role of Sabir Shah Kabuli beside the founder's own
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chapter 2 (Historical Events of Afghanistan, 1747–1879), Lesson 4: How did Ahmad Shah Baba revive Afghanistan anew?** — Ahmad Shah gathers the divided country into one state again, and pupils debate the role of Sabir Shah Kabuli beside the founder's own
2. **Lesson 5: What were Ahmad Shah Baba's administrative reforms and military activities?** — the founder orders the new state's administration and leads its armies across the region
3. **Lesson 6: What do you know of Ahmad Shah Baba's qualities?** — the founder's character is summed up as the model of the national hero
4. **Lesson 2: Ahmad Shah Baba and the consolidation of the central state** — the founder gathers the tribes into one central state in 1747
5. **Lesson 3: Ahmad Shah Baba's military campaigns** — the founder's armies carry the new state to its greatest extent, India included
