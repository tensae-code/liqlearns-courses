# Video script — United Nations agencies and their founders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Polish bacteriologist Ludwik Rajchman founds UNICEF and chairs it 1946-1950 until it becomes a permanent UN body in 1953, alongside the founding stories of WHO, UNESCO and FAO. The UN is built after WWII to keep peace, with veto-bearing permanent members; UNICEF is founded in 1946 for children left homeless by the Second World War.
