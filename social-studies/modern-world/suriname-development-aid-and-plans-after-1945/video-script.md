# Video script — Suriname — development aid and plans after 1945

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

After 1945 development aid arrives — Marshall-era thinking, the Welvaartsfonds and its projects, and a Planbureau to plan them.

The plans also target living and working conditions — development planning is explained step by step, with Plan Wageningen's mechanised agriculture among the projects.
