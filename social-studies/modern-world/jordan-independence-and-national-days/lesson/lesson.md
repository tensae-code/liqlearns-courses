# Jordan — independence and national days

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan became independent on 25 May 1946, and pupils celebrate Independence Day, the King's Accession Day (9 June). As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
