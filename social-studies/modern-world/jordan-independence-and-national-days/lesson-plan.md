## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit 3 Lesson 3: our national occasions** — the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan became independent on 25 May 1946, and pupils celebrate Independence Day, the King's Accession Day (9 June) and Flag Day
2. **Unit 3 Lesson 1: King Abdullah II ibn al-Hussein** — Abdullah, eldest son of King Hussein, is born in Amman in 1962, marries Rania in 1993, and takes his constitutional powers as King on 7 February 1999
3. **Unit 4 Lesson 1: development in my homeland** — the same place photographed in past and present shows development as change for the better over time — housing, transport and education change, and Jordan Valley (Ghor) farming is renewed with modern irrigation
4. **Unit 2 Lesson 2: kings of Jordan** — Emir Abdullah founds the state, independence is proclaimed as the Hashemite Kingdom, the two banks unite in 1950, Abdullah I is martyred at al-Aqsa in 1951, and under Hussein the army command is Arabised in 1956
5. **Unit 5 Lesson 2: the Jordanian state** — from the founding of the Emirate of East Jordan through popular marches demanding full independence, the 1952 constitution, King Abdullah I martyred at al-Aqsa's door on 20 July 1951, the unity of the two banks, and Jordan helping found the Arab League in 1945
6. **Unit 5 Lesson 3: the state's civilisational achievements** — al-Salt Secondary School, the Emirate's oldest school; the first health department in 1921; the University of Jordan in 1962; King Hussein Medical City — a young state builds schools, hospitals and universities
