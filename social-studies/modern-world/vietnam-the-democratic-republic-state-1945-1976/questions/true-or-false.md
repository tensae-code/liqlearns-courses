# Vietnam The Democratic Republic State 1945 1976 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan surrenders unconditionally on 15 August 1945; in Vietnam the Japanese army is demoralised, its puppet government collapses, and the Indochinese Communist Party leads the August Revolution to found the Democratic Republic of Vietnam — ending the monarchy and opening the republican era, "one of the most important achievements of the August Revolution"; the state is a democratic republic in which the National Assembly, elected by the whole people, decides the country's most important questions — the first general election of 6 January 1946 lets Vietnamese directly exercise their mastery for the first time — and its nature is a new-type democratic state, elected by the people and acting for the people: Article 1 is quoted ("Vietnam is a Democratic Republic. All power in the country belongs to the whole Vietnamese people, without distinction of race, sex, wealth, class or religion") beside Hồ Chí Minh's rule for government ("Whatever benefits the people, do it; whatever harms the people, avoid it"); the role table follows the state through flexible diplomacy and emergency relief in 1945–46 (land shared out to peasants, illiteracy and the empty treasury attacked), the victorious resistance against France in 1946–54 (war economy, defence industry, land reform — porters carry rice to Điện Biên Phủ in the photograph), and 1954–76 (the anti-American resistance to unification; in the North, post-war recovery, agricultural cooperatives, state industry and trade, education and health for all).
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chuyên đề 3, II.1, the Democratic Republic of Vietnam as a state

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Chuyên đề học tập Lịch sử 10 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Chuyên đề 3, II.1, the Democratic Republic of Vietnam as a state
