# Video script — Imperialism and its crisis

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Brazil's Grade 10 História SEED-PR tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Capitalism did not stay at home.



The capitalist states reached outward and carved up the world into empires — colonies for markets, materials and land.



But rivals make a fragile order. Each empire guarded its share and envied the rest.



The rivalry brought the imperial order to crisis — and the crisis broke open as world war.
