# Hands-on — Imperialism and its crisis

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Carve and clash
Draw a simple world shape and divide it between three rival empires in three colours. Where two colours meet, draw a star for rivalry. Underneath, write the chain from this lesson in five steps.

## Not an accident
Write one sentence beginning: 'The book teaches the world war was not an accident because…' Finish it from the lesson.
