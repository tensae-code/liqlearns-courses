# Imperialism and its crisis — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's teaching, imperialism is —
a) Capitalist states carving up the world into empires  b) States giving up all colonies  c) A system with no colonies
**Answer: a) Capitalist states carving up the world into empires**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Inside the imperial order, the empires were —
a) Rivals, each guarding its share and envying the others'  b) Close friends with no disputes  c) Ruled by one single emperor
**Answer: a) Rivals, each guarding its share and envying the others'**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The rivalry between the empires brought the imperial order to —
a) Crisis, breaking open as world war  b) A hundred years of peace  c) An end to all states
**Answer: a) Crisis, breaking open as world war**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The capitalist states reached outward hungry for —
a) Markets, materials and land  b) Nothing at all  c) Stories and songs only
**Answer: a) Markets, materials and land**
