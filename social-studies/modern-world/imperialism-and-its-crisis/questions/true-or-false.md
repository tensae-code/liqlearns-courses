# Imperialism and its crisis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The imperial order was stable and free of rivalry.
**Answer: False — it was built on rivalry between the empires.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The book teaches world war as the imperial system's crisis breaking open.
**Answer: True — the crisis of the imperial order broke open as world war.**
