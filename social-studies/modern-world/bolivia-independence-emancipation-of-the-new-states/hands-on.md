# Hands-on — Bolivia's independence — emancipation of the new states

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bolivia's independence — emancipation of the new states — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Año 3: Tercera etapa — la guerra por la emancipación de los nuevos estados, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
