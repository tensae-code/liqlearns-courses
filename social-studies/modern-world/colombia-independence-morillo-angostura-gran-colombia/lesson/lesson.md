# Colombia's independence — Morillo, Angostura and Gran Colombia

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: New Granada wins independence through Morillo's reconquest and siege of Cartagena to the Congress of Angostura's Republic of Colombia in 1819 — and Bolívar's union dream then fails as Gran Colombia splits. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
