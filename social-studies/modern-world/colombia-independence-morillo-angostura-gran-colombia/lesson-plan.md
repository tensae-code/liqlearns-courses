# Lesson plan — Colombia's independence — Morillo, Angostura and Gran Colombia

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Colombia's independence — Morillo, Angostura and Gran Colombia
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Colombia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** New Granada wins independence through Morillo's reconquest and siege of Cartagena to the Congress of Angostura's Republic of Colombia in 1819 — and Bolívar's union dream then fails as Gran Colombia splits
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Guía 2: The birth of a nation** — the early acts still name Fernando VII while demanding criollo power; Pablo Morillo besieges Cartagena from 18 August 1815 in the Spanish reconquest; on 17 December 1819 the Congress of Angostura proclaims the Republic of Colombia, with Santander vice-president of Cundinamarca, Bolívar's union dream fails at the Congress of Panama, and bolivarianos and santanderistas split
2. **Módulo 2 / Guía 4: Why did we become independent?** — the causes of New Granada's independence are built from sources — Captain Antonio Baraya's company guards the council houses as the movement opens, and a merchant lover of liberty asks whether Europe will let an old serpent devour the fairest part of the globe
3. **Unidad 4 / Tema 19: Formation of the Colombian State, 1819–1849** — Nariño's authority is disowned and war declared on Santafé; Bolívar's arrival forces Viceroy Sámano to flee; Gran Colombia forms, Quito separates in May 1830, haciendas harden social relations, and the Guerra de los Supremos follows

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
