# Babar and Panipat — the founding of the Mughal Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zahir-ud-Din Babar (1483–1530), heir of Timur and Genghis Khan, loses Samarkand and Ferghana and turns to India; at Panipat in 1526 his small organised army, cannon and tulughma tactics destroy Ibrahim Lodhi's host; Khanwa (1527) breaks the Rajputs; his Babarnama remains the empire's founding diary.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 4: Babar, the first Mughal ruler

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History 7 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Babar and Panipat — the founding of the Mughal Empire
