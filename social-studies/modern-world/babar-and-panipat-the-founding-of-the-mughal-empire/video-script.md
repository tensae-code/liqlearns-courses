# Video script — Babar and Panipat — the founding of the Mughal Empire

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zahir-ud-Din Babar, heir of Timur and Genghis Khan, loses Samarkand and Ferghana, turns to India, and at Panipat in 1526 his small organised army, cannon and tulughma tactics found the Mughal Empire.
