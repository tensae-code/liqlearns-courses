# Babar and Panipat — the founding of the Mughal Empire

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Zahir-ud-Din Babar, heir of Timur and Genghis Khan, loses Samarkand and Ferghana, turns to India, and at Panipat in 1526 his small organised army, cannon and tulughma tactics found the Mughal Empire. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Babar and Panipat — the founding of the Mughal Empire

Zahir-ud-Din Babar (1483–1530), heir of Timur and Genghis Khan, loses Samarkand and Ferghana and turns to India; at Panipat in 1526 his small organised army, cannon and tulughma tactics destroy Ibrahim Lodhi's host; Khanwa (1527) breaks the Rajputs; his Babarnama remains the empire's founding diary.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
