# The Moldavian Assr 1924 1940 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 14: RASSM (1924–1940) și politica expansionistă a URSS.
**Answer: Moscow builds a Moldavian ASSR on the Nistru's left bank as a claim on Bessarabia; collectivisation reaches 96.6% by 1937, the "kulaks" are destroyed, and the Latin script is banned as a Romanianising danger (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a IX-a* (Moldova), Tema 14: RASSM (1924–1940) și politica expansionistă a URSS, printed p. 69.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 18: Constituirea RASSM: politici și impact.
**Answer: the memorandum of 4 February 1924 sets up the Moldavian ASSR; agriculture expands, then the liquidation of the "chiaburi" catalogues over 3,200 peasant households for destruction (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
