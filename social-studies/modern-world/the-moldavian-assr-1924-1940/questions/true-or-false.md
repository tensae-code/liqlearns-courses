# The Moldavian Assr 1924 1940 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Moscow builds a Moldavian ASSR on the Nistru's left bank as a claim on Bessarabia; collectivisation reaches 96.6% by 1937, the "kulaks" are destroyed, and the Latin script is banned as a Romanianising danger.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 14: RASSM (1924–1940) și politica expansionistă a URSS

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a IX-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 14: RASSM (1924–1940) și politica expansionistă a URSS

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The memorandum of 4 February 1924 sets up the Moldavian ASSR; agriculture expands, then the liquidation of the "chiaburi" catalogues over 3,200 peasant households for destruction.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 18: Constituirea RASSM: politici și impact
