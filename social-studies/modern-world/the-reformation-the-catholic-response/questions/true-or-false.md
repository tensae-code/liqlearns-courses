# The Reformation — the Catholic Counter-Reformation — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The money collected as indulgence was stopped after the Council of Trent.
**Answer: True** — Trent's reforms stopped it — the very abuse Luther protested.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Jesuits were a Protestant society.
**Answer: False** — They were founded to spread the Catholic faith.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book asks pupils to discuss faith tolerance's role in peace.
**Answer: True** — That discussion closes the section, and this lesson.
