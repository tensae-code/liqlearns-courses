# The Reformation — the Catholic Counter-Reformation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Catholic Church's first reform step was —
a) Building more churches  b) Appointing strong, capable leadership  c) Banning all councils  d) Closing the schools
**Answer: b) Appointing strong, capable leadership** — Capable, determined people replaced the corrupt in leading posts.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Council of Trent is dated in the book —
a) 1517  b) 1545–1563  c) 1642–1727  d) 1497–1498
**Answer: b) 1545–1563** — The council years are 1545–1563.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After Trent, which of these was stopped?
a) Preaching  b) Appointing people by kinship connection  c) Singing in church  d) Reading scripture
**Answer: b) Appointing people by kinship connection** — Kinship appointments — along with worldly inclination and indulgence money — were stopped.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Jesuit society was established by —
a) Martin Luther  b) John Calvin  c) Ignatius of Loyola  d) Henry the Navigator
**Answer: c) Ignatius of Loyola** — Loyola founded the Jesuits, dated 1543 as printed.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Jesuits' main aim was to —
a) Sell indulgences  b) Spread the Catholic faith by preaching across the world  c) Build railways  d) Map the oceans
**Answer: b) Spread the Catholic faith by preaching across the world** — Preaching, openly and in secret, across the parts of the world.
