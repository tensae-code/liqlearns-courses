# The Reformation — the Catholic Counter-Reformation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three abuses stopped after Trent.
**Answer:** Worldly inclination, the money collected as indulgence, and appointing people by kinship connection.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How does tolerance serve peace between faiths?
**Answer:** It lets neighbours of different faiths live side by side without the rivalry turning to war — the guard the book asks pupils to discuss.
