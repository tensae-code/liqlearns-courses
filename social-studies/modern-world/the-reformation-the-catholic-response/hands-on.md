# Hands-on — The Reformation — the Catholic Counter-Reformation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Decree drafting
Sit as a council. Write three short decrees stopping the three abuses: worldly inclination, indulgence money, kinship appointments. Read them aloud formally — councils rule by written decrees.

## Tolerance talk
With a partner of a different faith or view, practise the book's discussion: each states one way tolerance protects peace in your own street or school. Listen fully before answering.
