# American independence — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The colonists objected to being taxed by a Parliament they did not elect.
**Answer: True** — no taxation without representation.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** The Declaration of Independence was written after the war had already been won.
**Answer: False** — the Declaration came in 1776, during the struggle.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** France fought on Britain's side in the American war.
**Answer: False** — France joined the colonists against Britain.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** The thirteen colonies had their own assemblies for much of their daily government.
**Answer: True** — the grievance was Parliament's taxes, not local self-rule.
