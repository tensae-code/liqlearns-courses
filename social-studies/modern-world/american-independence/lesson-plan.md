# Lesson plan — American independence

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** American independence
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — the age of revolutions unit; the Grade 11 book's American lesson (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain's thirteen American colonies, taxed without a voice in the taxing parliament, declared themselves independent in 1776 and, with French help, won the war that made the United States a republic.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on nothing but the learner's own sense of fairness: a tax you had no say in. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Taxed without a voice** — the colonies' grievance — Britain taxing America while America sent no members to Parliament.
2. **1776 — a new country declares itself** — the Declaration of Independence, drafted by Jefferson, and the war won with French help.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Nationalism — the feeling that made nation states. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the colonies objected to — taxation without representation.
2. Tell the Declaration of 1776: who wrote it, what it claimed.
3. Explain how the colonists won — and whose help mattered.
4. Say what was new about the republic they founded.

## What you need
