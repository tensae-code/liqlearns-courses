# Video script — American independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on the Atlantic. Thirteen colonies glow along the coast; across the water, London.
A tax stamp lands on tea, on paper, on newspapers. In Boston the harbour darkens with tea. The colonies' assemblies light up, one after another, and join.
A document unrolls — 1776. Then the map turns to war: red lines from Britain, blue lines holding on, and at last French ships crossing the ocean to stand beside the colonies.
The red lines withdraw. A new name is written across the thirteen: the United States.
