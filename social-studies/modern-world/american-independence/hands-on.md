# Hands-on — American independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Tea in the harbour
Retell the Boston protest as a news report of five sentences: what was taxed, who acted, what they did, how Britain answered, what the colonies did next.

## Consent of the governed
In a group, draft a one-sentence rule for a fair tax in your own school or town, using the Declaration's idea: who must agree before money is taken?
