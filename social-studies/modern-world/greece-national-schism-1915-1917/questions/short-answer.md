# Greece National Schism 1915 1917 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Greece in the First World War — the National Schism.
**Answer: king and prime minister split the country in two — two governments, two armies — until the Allies force unity on Allied terms (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία (Modern and Contemporary History), Γ' Γυμνασίου, by Evangelia Louvi & Dimitrios Chr. Xifaras* (Greece), Greece in the First World War — the National Schism, printed p. 92.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The National Schism in Greece.
**Answer: Venizelos and King Constantine pull Greece apart over joining the war (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Νεότερου και Σύγχρονου Κόσμου από το 1453 μ.Χ. έως σήμερα (History of the Modern and Contemporary World from 1453 to today), Α' ΕΠΑ.Λ. (vocational lyceum), General Education* (Greece), The National Schism in Greece, printed p. 94.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Greece in the First World War.
**Answer: the National Schism splits Greece into two states; the Thessaloniki government joins the Allies and the Macedonian front ends the war in the Balkans (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του νεότερου και του σύγχρονου κόσμου (από το 1815 έως σήμερα) (History of the Modern and Contemporary World, from 1815 to today), Γ' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Koliopoulos, Konstantinos Svolopoulos, Evanthis Hatzivassiliou, Theodoros Nimas & Haris Scholinaki-Chelioti* (Greece), Greece in the First World War, printed p. 81.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 2 D: The National Schism (1915–1922).
**Answer: the Venizelos–Constantine clash splits parties, state and army into two Greeces whose feud outlives the war (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Θέματα Νεοελληνικής Ιστορίας (Themes of Modern Greek History), Γ' Γενικού Λυκείου, Humanities orientation group; one author per theme: Georgios Margaritis (economy), Agathoklis Azelis (political parties), Nikolaos Andriotis (the refugee question), Theocharis Detorakis (the Cretan question), Konstantinos Fotiadis (Black Sea Hellenism)* (Greece), Theme 2 D: The National Schism (1915–1922), printed p. 93.**
