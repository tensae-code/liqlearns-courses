# China Korean War Resist America Aid Korea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mao argues 'if we let the enemy press to the Yalu, not fighting harms us more'; on the night of 19 October 1950 the Volunteers cross secretly, win their first battle on 25 October, and in five campaigns drive MacArthur — who promised his men home by Christmas — back to the 38th parallel.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Resist America, aid Korea

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Resist America, aid Korea
