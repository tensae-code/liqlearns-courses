# China Korean War Resist America Aid Korea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Resist America, aid Korea.
**Answer: Mao argues 'if we let the enemy press to the Yalu, not fighting harms us more'; on the night of 19 October 1950 the Volunteers cross secretly, win their first battle on 25 October, and in five campaigns drive MacArthur — who promised his men home by Christmas — back to the 38th parallel (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Lesson 2: Resist America, aid Korea, printed p. 8.**
