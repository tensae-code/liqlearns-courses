# Hands-on — China Korean War Resist America Aid Korea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — China Korean War Resist America Aid Korea — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Resist America, aid Korea, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
