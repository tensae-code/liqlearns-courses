# Fascisms Racial Laws 1938 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** on 18 September 1938 Mussolini announces the racial laws at Trieste (the unit's video); Sarfatti's and De Felice's Storiografie argue over Italian racial policy's roots — persecution written into law by the state itself.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 3, Cap. 8 §5 The racial laws

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 3, Cap. 8 §5 The racial laws

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the racial laws (Fare storia Document) turn persecution into statute; against the squeeze stand the fuorusciti — Garosci's and Rosselli's Fare storia pieces, "liberty as means and end" — and Togliatti's rival reading of fascism as class dictatorship.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 6 §§6–7 La stretta totalitaria e le leggi razziali; L'opposizione al fascismo

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
