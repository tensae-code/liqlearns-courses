# The Bolshevik Regime And The Civil War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§3–4 The new Bolshevik regime; The civil war and the centrifugal pushes in the Soviet state.
**Answer: the first revolutionary decrees and the literacy posters ("knowledge makes you free") build a new order while civil war consumes the old empire — Ekaterinburg, where the tsar's family is shot, is its last frontier (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 2, Cap. 5 §§3–4 The new Bolshevik regime; The civil war and the centrifugal pushes in the Soviet state, printed pp. 177, 183.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Modulo 1, Cap. 1 §10 Guerra civile e dittatura.
**Answer: the Anteprima's essay follows the experiment's turn — a movement announcing liberty and equality breaks with civil society over the collectivist project and hardens into a pitilessly authoritarian regime that still fascinates revolutionaries worldwide (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
