## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: §§35–36 The deportation of the peoples of the USSR to Kazakhstan — Koreans, Germans, Chechens, Ingush and others are dumped from cattle cars onto the steppe, and Kazakh families share their bread, §60 Kazakhstan — a land of the deported peoples of the USSR — whole peoples arrive in freight cars and are taken in by Kazakh auls, §§35–36 The deportation of the USSR's peoples to Kazakhstan — whole peoples are dumped on the steppe and Kazakh families share their bread, §6.8 How were the deported peoples received in Kazakhstan? — whole peoples arrive in cattle cars and Kazakh families share their bread. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
