# Kazakhstan Deportations To Kazakhstan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Koreans, Germans, Chechens, Ingush and others are dumped from cattle cars onto the steppe, and Kazakh families share their bread.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§35–36 The deportation of the peoples of the USSR to Kazakhstan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана [History of Kazakhstan]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§35–36 The deportation of the peoples of the USSR to Kazakhstan

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §60 Kazakhstan — a land of the deported peoples of the USSR
