# Sri Lanka — Dutch administration and law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Land Raad gives villagers cheap justice, and clerks write every family, field and tax into thombo books — one man's 195 coconut palms owe a single rix-dollar.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Dutch government, courts and the thombo registers

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G9 History Tamil 2019* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Dutch government, courts and the thombo registers

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The class collects what the Dutch left behind — words, clothes, foods, furniture, new port towns — and exhibits them as a wall paper.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Dutch administration, economy and culture
