# Greece Building The Kingdom 1830 1881 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Greek state and its evolution (1830–1881).
**Answer: Otto's Bavarian monarchy, the 1843 constitution, George I, the Great Idea and Thessaly's annexation in 1881 close the kingdom's first half-century (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του νεότερου και του σύγχρονου κόσμου (από το 1815 έως σήμερα) (History of the Modern and Contemporary World, from 1815 to today), Γ' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Koliopoulos, Konstantinos Svolopoulos, Evanthis Hatzivassiliou, Theodoros Nimas & Haris Scholinaki-Chelioti* (Greece), The Greek state and its evolution (1830–1881), printed p. 34.**
