# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia — unification under Abdulaziz: the brave courier

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The watched road
Draw a map of two towns joined by one road. Mark where enemies might watch. Draw the courier's journey as a dashed line and write, at three points along it, what the courier must do or avoid. Say why each choice keeps the letter secret.

## Seal the letter
Fold a paper letter and seal it with a sticker or a wax-like blob of clay. Carry it across the room — or across the yard — without letting a chosen "watcher" see it pass. Afterwards, say what made the carrying hard, and what the watcher could have learned if they had seen it.

## One person, one kingdom
Stand in a line with classmates or family, passing a sealed letter hand to hand in silence, each person moving it one step closer to its destination. When it arrives, say together what the story teaches: a whole kingdom can depend on one brave, trusted person.
