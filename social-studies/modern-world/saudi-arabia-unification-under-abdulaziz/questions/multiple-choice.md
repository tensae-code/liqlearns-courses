# Saudi Arabia — unification under Abdulaziz: the brave courier — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before the story begins, King Abdulaziz had —
a) Lost the country  b) Secured the roads and unified the country  c) Never left his palace
**Answer: b) Secured the roads and unified the country** — the story's starting point, as the book tells it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The king needed to send —
a) A gift of gold  b) An army  c) A secret letter to his men in another city
**Answer: c) A secret letter to his men in another city** — the errand at the story's heart.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The road between the cities was dangerous because —
a) Enemies watched it  b) It crossed a river  c) It had no water
**Answer: a) Enemies watched it** — the danger the book names.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The story "The Brave Man" belongs to the series called —
a) Stories I Belong To  b) Tales of the Sea  c) Kings of the Desert
**Answer: a) Stories I Belong To** — the series named in the book (قصص أنتمي إليها).
