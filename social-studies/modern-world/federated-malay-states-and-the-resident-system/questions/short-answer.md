# The Federated Malay States and the Resident system — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The Federated Malay States.
**Answer: tin makes the west-coast states rich — Larut's mines from the 1840s and Chinese miners raise Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan into world tin leaders; Britain binds the rulers by treaty, beginning with the Pangkor Treaty of 1874 in Perak, installs Residents whose "advice" must be followed, and federates Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang (any close wording).**
