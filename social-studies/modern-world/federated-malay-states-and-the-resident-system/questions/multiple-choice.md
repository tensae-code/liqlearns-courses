# The Federated Malay States and the Resident system — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Federated Malay States  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Federated Malay States** — tin makes the west-coast states rich — Larut's mines from the 1840s and Chinese miners raise Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan into world tin leaders; Britain binds the rulers by treaty, beginning with the Pangkor Treaty of 1874 in Perak, installs Residents whose "advice" must be followed, and federates Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang
