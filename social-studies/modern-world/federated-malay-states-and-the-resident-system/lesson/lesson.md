# The Federated Malay States and the Resident system

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Tin makes the west-coast states rich; Britain binds their rulers by treaty from Pangkor in 1874, installs Residents whose advice must be followed, and federates Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Federated Malay States

Tin makes the west-coast states rich — Larut's mines from the 1840s and Chinese miners raise Perak, Selangor and Negeri Sembilan into world tin leaders; Britain binds the rulers by treaty, beginning with the Pangkor Treaty of 1874 in Perak, installs Residents whose "advice" must be followed, and federates Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang (Bab 3: the Federated Malay States)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this lesson's story. Name two things that changed in the story.
