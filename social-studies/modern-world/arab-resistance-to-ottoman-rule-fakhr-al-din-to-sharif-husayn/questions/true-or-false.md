# Arab resistance to Ottoman rule — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** four centuries of Ottoman government — walis rotated yearly and milking their provinces, craftsmen deported to Istanbul, taxes doubled, thought frozen — breed a chain of Arab risings the book tabulates: Fakhr al-Din al-Ma'ni in Lebanon, executed at Istanbul in 1635; Zahir al-Umar in Palestine, destroyed at Acre in 1775; Ali Bey al-Kabir declaring Egypt independent in 1769 and undone by his own son-in-law's betrayal; the Saudi-Wahhabi movement in Najd from 1744, crushed at Diriyah after seventy-four years by Muhammad Ali's armies; and the rising national feeling, sharpened by Turkification, that ends in Sharif Husayn's Arab Revolt of 1916.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Arab resistance to Ottoman rule

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الحديث والمعاصر للصف التاسع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (Modern and Contemporary History of the Arabs for Grade 9, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Arab resistance to Ottoman rule
