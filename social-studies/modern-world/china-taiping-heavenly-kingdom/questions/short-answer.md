# China Taiping Heavenly Kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Seeking a way out (1) — Taiping.
**Answer: failed examination candidate Hong Xiuquan preaches a heavenly kingdom, takes Nanjing and holds half of China for 14 years before the gentry armies crush him (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 17: Seeking a way out (1) — Taiping, printed p. 102.**
