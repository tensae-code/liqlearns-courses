# Video script — The Red Cross, the Red Crescent and international humanitarian law

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Jordan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the International Committee founded in 1863 protects war's victims and visits prisoners; after the First World War the federation of societies follows, the Arab Red Crescent organisation settles in Riyadh in 1975, and Jordan's own Red Crescent volunteers serve their country; behind them stands humanitarian law — the rules that shield everyone not fighting, or no longer fighting, in a war.
