# Armenia Fall Of The First Republic 1920 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Squeezed between Kemalist Turkey's invasion and the Red Army, the government hands power to the Bolsheviks on 2 December 1920 and the first republic ends.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 2.10 Armenia in the sights of Soviet Russia and Mustafa Kemal's governments; the fall of the Republic

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 2.10 Armenia in the sights of Soviet Russia and Mustafa Kemal's governments; the fall of the Republic
