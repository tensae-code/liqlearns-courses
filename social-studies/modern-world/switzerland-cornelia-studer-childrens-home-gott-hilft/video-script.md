# Video script — Switzerland — Cornelia Studer and the Gott hilft children's home

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Switzerland's *Sorge oder Zwang?* set tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

She was seven. The home promised rest and order.



Inside: work before dawn, beatings, hunger as punishment — for eight years.



The inspectors came, praised the home, and left.



Fifty years later, Cornelia finally wrote it down.
