# Hands-on — Switzerland — Cornelia Studer and the Gott hilft children's home

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Facade and behind it
Draw a house front with a smiling sign. Behind the front, list what Cornelia's account says was inside. Write one sentence on who was supposed to look behind the facade, and what they did instead.

## The inspector's second visit
You are the inspector returning in 1955 with Cornelia's account in hand. Write three questions you would now ask, and say whose answers you would check them against.
