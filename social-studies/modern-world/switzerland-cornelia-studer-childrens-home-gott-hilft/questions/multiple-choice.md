# Switzerland — Cornelia Studer and the Gott hilft children's home — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cornelia and her brother were sent to the home for —
a) "Rest and order"  b) Farm training they chose  c) A school holiday
**Answer: a) "Rest and order"**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Cornelia wrote her account down —
a) Fifty years later  b) The week she left  c) Before she arrived
**Answer: a) Fifty years later**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Gott hilft network, founded in 1916, paid for itself through —
a) Children's farm labour and unpaid carers  b) State salaries for all staff  c) Church taxes
**Answer: a) Children's farm labour and unpaid carers**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The full 1955 inspection of the home —
a) Praised its people and passed over its punishments  b) Closed it at once  c) Was never carried out
**Answer: a) Praised its people and passed over its punishments**
