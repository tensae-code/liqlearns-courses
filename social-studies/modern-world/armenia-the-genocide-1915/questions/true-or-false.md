# Armenia The Genocide 1915 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under cover of war the Young Turk government arrests Constantinople's Armenian leaders on 24 April 1915, then deports and kills the Ottoman Armenians, while Van and Musa Dagh resist.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1.3 The First World War and the Armenian Genocide

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1.3 The First World War and the Armenian Genocide

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide walks teachers through the Hamidian massacres, the wartime genocide and its condemnation as the course's opening national catastrophe.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Appendix theme guide 1: the Armenian people at the start of the 20th century; the Armenian Genocide (lessons 1–18 of the thematic plan, keyed to student chapters 1.1–1.4)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց (History of Armenia 9, Methodical Guide)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Appendix theme guide 1: the Armenian people at the start of the 20th century; the Armenian Genocide (lessons 1–18 of the thematic plan, keyed to student chapters 1.1–1.4)
