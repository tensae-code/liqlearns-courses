# Armenia The Genocide 1915 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 1.3 The First World War and the Armenian Genocide.
**Answer: under cover of war the Young Turk government arrests Constantinople's Armenian leaders on 24 April 1915, then deports and kills the Ottoman Armenians, while Van and Musa Dagh resist (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* (Armenia), 1.3 The First World War and the Armenian Genocide, printed p. 24.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Appendix theme guide 1: the Armenian people at the start of the 20th century; the Armenian Genocide (lessons 1–18 of the thematic plan, keyed to student chapters 1.1–1.4).
**Answer: the guide walks teachers through the Hamidian massacres, the wartime genocide and its condemnation as the course's opening national catastrophe (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց (History of Armenia 9, Methodical Guide)* (Armenia), Appendix theme guide 1: the Armenian people at the start of the 20th century; the Armenian Genocide (lessons 1–18 of the thematic plan, keyed to student chapters 1.1–1.4), printed p. 116.**
