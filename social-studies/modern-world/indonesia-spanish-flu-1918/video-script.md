# Video script — Indonesia — the Spanish flu of 1918 in the Indies

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The 1918 Spanish flu sweeps the Indies along the shipping routes, and the colonial state answers with pamphlets, newspaper campaigns against pandemic hoaxes, and mass cholera vaccination.
