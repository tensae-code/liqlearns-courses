# Lesson plan — The Bolsheviks in power, 1917 to 1924

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Bolsheviks in power, 1917 to 1924
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (United Kingdom, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From 1917 to 1924, the Bolsheviks seize power, win a civil war and build a one-party state.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **2.1 Early consolidation of power, 1917–18** — Trotsky signs away land at Brest-Litovsk to keep the promise of peace, the Constituent Assembly is dispersed and the Romanov family is murdered
2. **2.2 The Civil War, 1918–21** — Reds against Whites, Greens and foreign intervention armies; war communism, requisitioning and famine follow, and the Reds' unity and control of the railways win through
3. **2.3 Moves towards totalitarianism** — Within weeks the new rulers ban rival newspapers and create the Cheka; show trials and terror turn a revolutionary government into a one-party dictatorship
4. **2.4 Economic and social change, 1918–24** — War communism collapses into the NEP retreat, while society is remade — women declared equal (1917), free compulsory education, legal abortion (1920) and Church land nationalised
