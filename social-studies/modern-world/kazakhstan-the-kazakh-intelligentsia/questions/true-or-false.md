# Kazakhstan The Kazakh Intelligentsia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a small generation of teachers, poets and lawyers invents modern Kazakh public life.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§8–9 Outstanding representatives of the Kazakh intelligentsia of the early 20th century

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана [History of Kazakhstan]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§8–9 Outstanding representatives of the Kazakh intelligentsia of the early 20th century

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a thin layer of graduates becomes the nation's voice.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§5–6 The Kazakh national intelligentsia and its influence on socio-political processes

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (1900–1945 гг.) [History of Kazakhstan (1900–1945)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§5–6 The Kazakh national intelligentsia and its influence on socio-political processes

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** six portraits — the statesman, the teacher, the poet, and Shokai who carries the cause into exile.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§13–14 Political leaders of the Kazakh people (A. Bukeikhanov, A. Baitursynov, M. Dulatov, B. Karaev, M. Tynyshpaev, M. Shokai)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *История Казахстана (1900–1945 гг.) [History of Kazakhstan (1900–1945)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§13–14 Political leaders of the Kazakh people (A. Bukeikhanov, A. Baitursynov, M. Dulatov, B. Karaev, M. Tynyshpaev, M. Shokai)

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** a small generation invents modern Kazakh public life.
