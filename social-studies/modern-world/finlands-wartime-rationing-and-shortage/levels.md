# Finland's wartime rationing and shortage — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — introduction: the Ministry of Public Provision and the ration-card system
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — foreign trade stops, men and fields are lost to the war, so a new ministry rations sugar and coffee first, then nearly all food and clothes by work-heavy card classes

## Sub lesson 2 — Task 1, Rantasalmi letter 1942
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a pregnant evacuee mother of three, husband at the front, begs bigger B/G2/M2 cards and is told where to appeal

## Sub lesson 3 — Task 2, Ivalo letter 1942
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a Lapland writer exposes under-the-counter marmalade sales for favoured customers and asks if Lapland lives under a different law

## Sub lesson 4 — Task 3, Tohmajärvi letter 1943
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a poor potato year breaks the delivery quota, and eight people share one pair of new shoes all war — the family asks how to get footwear permits

## Sub lesson 5 — Task 4, Pietarsaari letter 1943
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — workers miss the egg distribution twice because the eggs run out before their shift ends; equality of coupons becomes the argument

## Sub lesson 6 — Task 5, Helsinki letter 1942
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a self-styled 'home and finance minister' mother of five jokes through vinegar-and-saccharin dinners and black-market tobacco prices — humour as survival

## Sub lesson 7 — Task 6, Kauhava letter 1942
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the state requisitions 320 kg of hay at 1.3 mk/kg while free hay costs 3.5; a smallholder wife faces destroying her herd
