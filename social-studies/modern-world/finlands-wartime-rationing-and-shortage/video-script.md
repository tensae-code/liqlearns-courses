# Video script — Finland's wartime rationing and shortage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Finland's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Foreign trade stops, men and fields are lost to the war, so a new ministry rations sugar and coffee first, then nearly all food and clothes by work-heavy card classes.
