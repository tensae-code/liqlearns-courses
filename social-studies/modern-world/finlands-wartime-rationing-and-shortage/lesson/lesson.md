# Finland's wartime rationing and shortage

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Foreign trade stops, men and fields are lost to the war, so a new ministry rations sugar and coffee first, then nearly all food and clothes by work-heavy card classes As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — introduction: the Ministry of Public Provision and the ration-card system

Foreign trade stops, men and fields are lost to the war, so a new ministry rations sugar and coffee first, then nearly all food and clothes by work-heavy card classes.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — Task 3, Tohmajärvi letter 1943

A poor potato year breaks the delivery quota, and eight people share one pair of new shoes all war — the family asks how to get footwear permits.

## Sub lesson 5 — Task 4, Pietarsaari letter 1943

Workers miss the egg distribution twice because the eggs run out before their shift ends; equality of coupons becomes the argument.

[Picture 63277 — the eggs ran out]
