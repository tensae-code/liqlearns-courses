# Finland's wartime rationing and shortage — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Foreign trade stops, men and fields are lost to the war, so a new ministry rations sugar and coffee first, then nearly all food and clothes by work-heavy card classes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in introduction: the Ministry of Public Provision and the ration-card system

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Jahvetti teema-yksi* is about Task 6 source-criticism question.
**Answer: False** — it is about introduction: the Ministry of Public Provision and the ration-card system

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A pregnant evacuee mother of three, husband at the front, begs bigger B/G2/M2 cards and is told where to appeal.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Task 1, Rantasalmi letter 1942

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Jahvetti teema-yksi* is about introduction: cession and resettlement.
**Answer: False** — it is about Task 1, Rantasalmi letter 1942

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A Lapland writer exposes under-the-counter marmalade sales for favoured customers and asks if Lapland lives under a different law.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Task 2, Ivalo letter 1942

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Jahvetti teema-yksi* is about Task 1, Säkylä letter 1941.
**Answer: False** — it is about Task 2, Ivalo letter 1942

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** A poor potato year breaks the delivery quota, and eight people share one pair of new shoes all war — the family asks how to get footwear permits.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Task 3, Tohmajärvi letter 1943

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Jahvetti teema-yksi* is about Task 2, Rovaniemi letter 1941.
**Answer: False** — it is about Task 3, Tohmajärvi letter 1943

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Workers miss the egg distribution twice because the eggs run out before their shift ends; equality of coupons becomes the argument.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Task 4, Pietarsaari letter 1943

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Jahvetti teema-yksi* is about Task 3, Suonenjoki letter 1941.
**Answer: False** — it is about Task 4, Pietarsaari letter 1943

**11. (Sub lesson 6)** A self-styled 'home and finance minister' mother of five jokes through vinegar-and-saccharin dinners and black-market tobacco prices — humour as survival.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Task 5, Helsinki letter 1942

**12. (Sub lesson 6)** This part of *Jahvetti teema-yksi* is about Task 4, Oulu letter 1941.
**Answer: False** — it is about Task 5, Helsinki letter 1942

**13. (Sub lesson 7)** The state requisitions 320 kg of hay at 1.3 mk/kg while free hay costs 3.5; a smallholder wife faces destroying her herd.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Task 6, Kauhava letter 1942

**14. (Sub lesson 7)** This part of *Jahvetti teema-yksi* is about Task 5, Säkylä repeated and Reposaari 1944 letters.
**Answer: False** — it is about Task 6, Kauhava letter 1942
