## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 9, commerce and the great trading towns
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — market networks spread across deltas and coasts; in the North, Thăng Long — Kẻ Chợ with its 36 guild streets — and Phố Hiến, with its twenty wards and its many resident foreigners, become great centres; in the South, Thanh Hà, Gia Định and above all Hội An, "the largest port town of Đàng Trong", where Japanese, Chinese, Portuguese, Dutch, English and French merchants sell weapons, fine crafts, zinc and silver and buy silk, forest treasures, bird's nests and farm produce past the Japanese-built Covered Bridge — until, in the later 18th century, both governments restrict foreign trade and the towns fade
