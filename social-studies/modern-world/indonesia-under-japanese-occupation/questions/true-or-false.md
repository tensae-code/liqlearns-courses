# Indonesia — under Japanese occupation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Occupation means propaganda posters and Asia Raya newspapers, internment camps where Dutch women bow to the Emperor, occupation money, Fujinkai women's labour, the Selokan Mataram works and romusa forced labour.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab III.B-C Transformasi Pemerintahan / Dampak Penjajahan Jepang

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — the Proclamation, 17 August 1945.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab III.B-C Transformasi Pemerintahan / Dampak Penjajahan Jepang
