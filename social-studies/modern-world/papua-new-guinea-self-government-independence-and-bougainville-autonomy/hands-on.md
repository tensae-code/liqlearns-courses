# Hands-on — Papua New Guinea — self-government, Independence and Bougainville autonomy

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Papua New Guinea — self-government, Independence and Bougainville autonomy — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Self-Government in Papua New Guinea / Independence in Papua New Guinea / Birth of Autonomous Government in Bougainville, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
