# Guatemala — the peace process and accords — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Firm and Lasting Peace was signed on —
a) 29 December 1996  b) 20 October 1944  c) 16 January 1992
**Answer: a) 29 December 1996** — the peace date.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Serrano's autogolpe in 1993 —
a) collapsed under civil pressure  b) started the war  c) signed the peace
**Answer: a) collapsed under civil pressure** — its fate.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** De León Carpio signed accords on —
a) human rights, resettlement and indigenous rights  b) banana contracts  c) annexation
**Answer: a) human rights, resettlement and indigenous rights** — the accord topics.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The peace process opened by Cerezo is called —
a) Esquipulas  b) Chapultepec  c) Decree 900
**Answer: a) Esquipulas** — its name.
