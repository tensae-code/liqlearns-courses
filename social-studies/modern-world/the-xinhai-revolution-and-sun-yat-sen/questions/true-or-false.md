# The Xinhai Revolution and Sun Yat Sen — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sun Yat-sen's Tongmenghui (1905) programmes the republic — down with the Qing, equal land rights, a people's livelihood; in December 1911 he returns, Nanjing proclaims the Republic with him as provisional president, but the revolution stalls into dual power with Yuan Shikai's Beiyang north.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. V §29 The socio-economic and political position of China

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Всемирная история 9 (Vsemirnaya istoriya — World History), Grade 9* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. V §29 The socio-economic and political position of China
