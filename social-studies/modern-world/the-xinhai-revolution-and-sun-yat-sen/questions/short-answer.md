# The Xinhai Revolution and Sun Yat Sen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. V §29 The socio-economic and political position of China.
**Answer: Sun Yat-sen's Tongmenghui (1905) programmes the republic — down with the Qing, equal land rights, a people's livelihood; in December 1911 he returns, Nanjing proclaims the Republic with him as provisional president, but the revolution stalls into dual power with Yuan Shikai's Beiyang north (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Uzbekistan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Всемирная история 9 (Vsemirnaya istoriya — World History), Grade 9* (Uzbekistan), Ch. V §29 The socio-economic and political position of China, printed p. 95.**
