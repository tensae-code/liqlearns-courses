# The Xinhai Revolution and Sun Yat Sen

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Sun Yat-sen's Tongmenghui (1905) programmes the republic — down with the Qing, equal land rights, a people's livelihood; in December 1911. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. V §29 The socio-economic and political position of China

Sun Yat-sen's Tongmenghui (1905) programmes the republic — down with the Qing, equal land rights, a people's livelihood; in December 1911 he returns, Nanjing proclaims the Republic with him as provisional president, but the revolution stalls into dual power with Yuan Shikai's Beiyang north

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
