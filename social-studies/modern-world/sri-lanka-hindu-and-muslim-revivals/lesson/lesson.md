# Sri Lanka — Hindu and Muslim revivals

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Arumuga Navalar answers the missionaries with Tamil prose and Hindu schools in Jaffna, while M. C. Siddhi Lebbe, aided by the exiled Arabi Pasha, builds Muslim education. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
