# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — Hindu and Muslim revivals

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — Hindu and Muslim revivals — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Hindu and Islamic revivals, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
