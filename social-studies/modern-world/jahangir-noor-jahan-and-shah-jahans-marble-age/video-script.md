# Video script — Jahangir, Noor Jahan and Shah Jahan's marble age

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jahangir rules through art and justice while his queen Noor Jahan — poet, patron and power behind the throne — shapes the court, and his son Shah Jahan turns the empire's wealth into marble: the Taj Mahal, Delhi's Jama Masjid, Lahore Fort and the Shalimar Gardens.
