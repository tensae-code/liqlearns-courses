# Jahangir, Noor Jahan and Shah Jahan's marble age

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Jahangir rules through art and justice while his queen Noor Jahan — poet, patron and power behind the throne — shapes the court, and his son Shah Jahan turns the empire's wealth into marble: the Taj Mahal, Delhi's Jama Masjid, Lahore Fort and the Shalimar Gardens. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Jahangir, Noor Jahan and Shah Jahan's marble age

Jahangir (1605–1627) rules through art and justice while his queen Noor Jahan (1577–1645) — poet, patron and power behind the throne — shapes the court; his son Shah Jahan turns the empire's wealth into marble: the Taj Mahal, Delhi's Jama Masjid, Lahore Fort and the Shalimar Gardens.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
