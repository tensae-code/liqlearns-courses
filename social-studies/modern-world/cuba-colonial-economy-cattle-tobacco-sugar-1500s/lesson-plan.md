**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Cuba Colonial Economy Cattle Tobacco Sugar 1500s

**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **3.1 Progress of some economic activities** — gold runs out and Spaniards leave for Mexico and Peru; those who stay turn to cattle hatos and corrales, tobacco and early sugar, worked by Indians, slaves and free men
2. **Ch.3–4 activities: colonial exploitation 16th–18th c. and Cuba to 1867, incl. a document fragment on US designs on Cuba** — exercises move from hatos and tobacco monopoly to steam, railways and the question of who else wanted the island
3. **2.3 Evolution of the colony during the 17th c.** — with gold gone, cattle, contraband and corsairs define a poor but stubborn colony
4. **1.1 Colonial society 16th–18th c.; formation of the Cuban people** — three centuries of colony forge a people out of Spaniards, Africans and surviving Indians — nationality as a slow product, not a starting point
5. **Unit 1 suggestions: the road toward Cuban nationality and nationhood (colonial centuries)** — guidance shows how to teach creole identity emerging inside the slave colony — the nation before the nation-state

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
