# Hands-on — Venezuela From Coup To Coup 1945 1958

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela From Coup To Coup 1945 1958 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, From one coup to another, 1945–1958, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
