# Venezuela From Coup To Coup 1945 1958 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1945 coup opens a democratic triennium, a 1948 coup ends it, and juntas carry the country toward dictatorship.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in From one coup to another, 1945–1958

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 6to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about From one coup to another, 1945–1958

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Civic-military Junta and government of Gallegos
