# Greece Interwar And The Metaxas Dictatorship — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Interwar Greece; the dictatorship of 4 August 1936.
**Answer: republic, coups and restoration follow each other until Metaxas, with the king's consent, imposes dictatorship (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία (Modern and Contemporary History), Γ' Γυμνασίου, by Evangelia Louvi & Dimitrios Chr. Xifaras* (Greece), Interwar Greece; the dictatorship of 4 August 1936, printed p. 118.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Greece in the interwar period: the political situation.
**Answer: republic and monarchy alternate amid coups until the 1936 dictatorship (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του Νεότερου και Σύγχρονου Κόσμου από το 1453 μ.Χ. έως σήμερα (History of the Modern and Contemporary World from 1453 to today), Α' ΕΠΑ.Λ. (vocational lyceum), General Education* (Greece), Greece in the interwar period: the political situation, printed p. 113.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Greece in the critical decade 1930–1940.
**Answer: default, the failed 1935 Venizelist coup, the restored king and Metaxas's 4th of August dictatorship (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του νεότερου και του σύγχρονου κόσμου (από το 1815 έως σήμερα) (History of the Modern and Contemporary World, from 1815 to today), Γ' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Koliopoulos, Konstantinos Svolopoulos, Evanthis Hatzivassiliou, Theodoros Nimas & Haris Scholinaki-Chelioti* (Greece), Greece in the critical decade 1930–1940, printed p. 106.**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Θέματα Νεοελληνικής Ιστορίας (Themes of Modern Greek History), Γ' Γενικού Λυκείου, Humanities orientation group; one author per theme: Georgios Margaritis (economy), Agathoklis Azelis (political parties), Nikolaos Andriotis (the refugee question), Theocharis Detorakis (the Cretan question), Konstantinos Fotiadis (Black Sea Hellenism)* (Greece), Theme 2 E: Modernisation and interventions (1923–1936), printed p. 99.**
