# European rivalry in the Gulf — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The European powers raced for —
a) the Gulf's sea lanes and ports  b) the Gulf's deserts  c) pearl beds only
**Answer: a) the Gulf's sea lanes and ports** — they race for the Gulf's sea lanes and ports

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The power whose grip finally closed over the coast was —
a) Britain  b) Portugal  c) France
**Answer: a) Britain** — Britain's grip closes over the coast

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** For the Gulf peoples, the coast was —
a) home  b) a foreign shore  c) empty land
**Answer: a) home** — the coast the Gulf peoples call home
