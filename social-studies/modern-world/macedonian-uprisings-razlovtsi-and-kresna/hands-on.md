# Hands-on — Macedonian Uprisings Razlovtsi And Kresna

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Macedonian Uprisings Razlovtsi And Kresna — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Theme 2: the Razlovtsi Uprising (1876) — Dimitar Berovski returns to Maleševo with a small četa, Razlovtsi is twice burned and looted in reprisal, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
