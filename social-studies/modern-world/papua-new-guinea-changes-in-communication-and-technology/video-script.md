# Video script — Papua New Guinea — changes in communication and technology

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Messages once travelled by garamut slit-gong, conch shell and kundu drum, and work by dugout canoe, salt-making and stone tools; modern communication and technology arrive and the class weighs what was gained and what was lost.
