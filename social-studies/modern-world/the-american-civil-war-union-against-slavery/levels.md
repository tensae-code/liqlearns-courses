# The American Civil War — union against slavery's states — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number, as before. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view.

## Sub lesson 1 — Eleven states walk out
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Explain that eleven southern states seceded in 1860–61 after Lincoln's election, forming the Confederacy, over the long-standing disagreement about slavery.

## Sub lesson 2 — War, and slavery ends
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: State that Lincoln and the North opposed secession, the war ran 1861–65, and it ended with the Confederacy's defeat and the abolition of slavery in the USA.
