# The American Civil War — union against slavery's states — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The seceding states formed the ____ States of America.
**Answer: Confederate**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The president who held the Union was Abraham ____.
**Answer: Lincoln**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The war ended with the abolition of ____ in the USA.
**Answer: slavery**
