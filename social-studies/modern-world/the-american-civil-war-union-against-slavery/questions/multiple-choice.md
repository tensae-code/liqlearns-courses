# The American Civil War — union against slavery's states — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Confederate States were formed by —
a) Eleven southern states that seceded  b) Northern factory states  c) British colonies  d) Mexican provinces
**Answer: a) Eleven southern states that seceded** — Eleven southern states seceded in 1860–61.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The secession followed the election of —
a) Washington  b) Lincoln  c) Jefferson  d) Grant
**Answer: b) Lincoln** — The states seceded after Lincoln's election.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The long-standing disagreement behind the war was about —
a) Tariffs on tea  b) Slavery  c) The capital's site  d) Religion
**Answer: b) Slavery** — The book names slavery as the disagreement.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Civil War ran from —
a) 1776 to 1783  b) 1861 to 1865  c) 1815 to 1848  d) 1870 to 1871
**Answer: b) 1861 to 1865** — 1861–1865.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Lincoln and the North —
a) Accepted secession  b) Opposed secession  c) Joined the Confederacy  d) Stayed neutral
**Answer: b) Opposed secession** — Lincoln and the North opposed secession.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** The war ended with —
a) Two American nations  b) The Confederacy's defeat and the abolition of slavery  c) Slavery extended north  d) British mediation
**Answer: b) The Confederacy's defeat and the abolition of slavery** — The Confederacy was defeated and slavery abolished in the USA.
