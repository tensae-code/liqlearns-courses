# The American Civil War — union against slavery's states — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The southern states seceded before Lincoln's election.
**Answer: False** — They seceded in 1860–61, after his election.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Civil War lasted four years.
**Answer: True** — 1861 to 1865.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Slavery survived the Civil War in the USA.
**Answer: False** — The war ended with slavery abolished in the USA.
