# The American Civil War — union against slavery's states — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did the eleven states leave the Union when they did?
**Answer:** They seceded in 1860–61 after Lincoln's election, over the long-standing disagreement about slavery, which the southern states defended.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the war's two great results.
**Answer:** The Confederacy was defeated and the Union preserved; and slavery was abolished in the USA.
