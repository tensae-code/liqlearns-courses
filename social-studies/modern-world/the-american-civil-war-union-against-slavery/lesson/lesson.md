# The American Civil War — union against slavery's states

## Do it first

Look at the title and the dates. This is a story before it is a list: people act, other people answer, and the map changes. As you read, keep asking — who acted, what did they want, and what changed because of it?

## Sub lesson 1 — Eleven states walk out

Across the Atlantic, the same century asked America the nation question — in its hardest form. The United States was one nation with an open wound: the long-standing disagreement about **slavery**. When **Lincoln** was elected president, the southern states that defended slavery answered by leaving: **eleven southern states seceded in 1860–61** and formed their own state, the **Confederate States of America**. One flag had become two.

## Sub lesson 2 — War, and slavery ends

**Lincoln and the North opposed secession** — the Union, they held, could not be walked out of. So from **1861 to 1865** the two Americas fought the **American Civil War**, the bloodiest test nationalism faced in this unit. It ended with the **Confederacy's defeat** — and with the cause of the quarrel destroyed: **slavery was abolished in the USA**. The nation that emerged was one again, and free of the institution that had split it.
