# Video script — The American Civil War — union against slavery's states


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on America — one flag over a wide land.

But the land argues with itself about one thing: slavery.

An election. Lincoln wins. And in the south, state after state walks out — eleven of them.

A second flag rises: the Confederate States of America.

Lincoln shakes his head: the Union stays. War — 1861, 1862, 1863, 1864, 1865.

The southern flag comes down. The Union holds.

And the chains at the story's root are broken: slavery is abolished.
