# Hands-on — The American Civil War — union against slavery's states

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One flag, two flags
Draw the US flag; then draw the split of 1861 as a torn map with eleven southern states shaded. Write under it the single word the book gives as the disagreement's root: slavery.

## Lincoln's sentence
Write Lincoln's position in one sentence of your own words: the Union cannot be left. Then write what the war's end added: slavery abolished.
