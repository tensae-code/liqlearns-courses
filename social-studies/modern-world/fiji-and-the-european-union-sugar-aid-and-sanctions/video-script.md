# Video script — Fiji and the European Union — sugar, aid and sanctions

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The EU, formed by treaty on the Rewa Bridge's plaque as in Suva, becomes Fiji's sugar patron — then, after the December 2006 military coup, the European Commission axes $43 million of sugar support and opens Article 96 consultations.
