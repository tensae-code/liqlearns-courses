The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mongolia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

after the empire breaks, khans rise and fall in quick succession and the aimags pull apart while Ming China presses from the south. the seven-year-old Dayan Khan is raised on Chinggis's throne before the Eight White Gers and reunites the Mongols; a century later Altan Khan founds Khökh Khot and in 1577 receives the title Dalai Lama from Sonam Gyatso. a divided Mongolia is squeezed from three sides — Ming China, the rising Manchu, and Russia pushing across Siberia to Lake Baikal. after Yuan falls in 1368 the khans return to the steppe; the Northern Yuan fights Ming and itself in turn. Dayan Khan's reunification and his sons' appanages — Khalkha, Chakhar, Ordos, Tümed — set the map the Manchu will conquer piece by piece.
