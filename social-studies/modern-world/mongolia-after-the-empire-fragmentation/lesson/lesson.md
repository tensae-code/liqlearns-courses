# Mongolia after the Empire — fragmentation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: after the empire breaks, khans rise and fall in quick succession and the aimags pull apart while Ming China presses from the south. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

after the empire breaks, khans rise and fall in quick succession and the aimags pull apart while Ming China presses from the south
