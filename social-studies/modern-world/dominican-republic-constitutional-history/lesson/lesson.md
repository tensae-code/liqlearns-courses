# Dominican Republic — constitutional history

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The republic is born writing constitutions, starting with November 1844. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unidad 7 Evolución constitucional: antecedentes y la Constitución de noviembre de 1844

The republic is born writing constitutions, starting with November 1844.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unidad 7 Desarrollos ulteriores: autocracia "democrática" bajo Trujillo y la Constitución de 2015

Constitutions are rewritten by dictators and democrats alike, down to 2015.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
