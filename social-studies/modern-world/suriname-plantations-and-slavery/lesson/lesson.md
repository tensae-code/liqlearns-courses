# Suriname — plantations and slavery

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Plantations are laid out along the rivers; Indigenous people are first forced to work, then enslaved Africans are shipped in, sold in Paramaribo and driven by overseers while planters grow rich As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Plantages en slaven

Plantations are laid out along the rivers; Indigenous people are first forced to work, then enslaved Africans are shipped in, sold in Paramaribo and driven by overseers while planters grow rich (Plantages en slaven)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — De aanleg van plantages

Plantations are drained and laid out along the lower rivers and creeks with sluices and polders; today's place names (Beekhuizen, Berlijn, Totness) still remember them (De aanleg van plantages)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Plantage Meerzorg

One plantation studied whole: Meerzorg in Commewijne; by 1712 there are some 200 plantations, Cassard's French attack exposes weak defences, and Fort Nieuw Amsterdam is built in answer (Plantage Meerzorg)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?

A plantation is a whole organisation under its owner and director; enslaved field gangs do the planting, tending and harvesting under the basya's watch while others look on at punishments (Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 5 — Slaafgemaakten in Paramaribo

Life after the auction depends on the work assigned; the story of Afi Jaba, born in Africa and enslaved in 18th-century Suriname, stands beside the free Black people who also lived in the colony (Slaafgemaakten in Paramaribo)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
