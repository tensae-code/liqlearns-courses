# Suriname — plantations and slavery — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Plantations are laid out along the rivers; Indigenous people are first forced to work, then enslaved Africans are shipped in, sold in Paramaribo and driven by overseers while planters grow rich.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Plantages en slaven

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 6 Geschiedenis* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Plantages en slaven

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Plantations are drained and laid out along the lower rivers and creeks with sluices and polders; today's place names (Beekhuizen, Berlijn, Totness) still remember them.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De aanleg van plantages

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about De aanleg van plantages

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** One plantation studied whole: Meerzorg in Commewijne; by 1712 there are some 200 plantations, Cassard's French attack exposes weak defences, and Fort Nieuw Amsterdam is built in answer.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Plantage Meerzorg

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Plantage Meerzorg

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** A plantation is a whole organisation under its owner and director; enslaved field gangs do the planting, tending and harvesting under the basya's watch while others look on at punishments.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Life after the auction depends on the work assigned; the story of Afi Jaba, born in Africa and enslaved in 18th-century Suriname, stands beside the free Black people who also lived in the colony.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Slaafgemaakten in Paramaribo

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Slaafgemaakten in Paramaribo
