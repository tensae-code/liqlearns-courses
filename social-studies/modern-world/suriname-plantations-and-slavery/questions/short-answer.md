# Suriname — plantations and slavery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Plantages en slaven.
**Answer: Plantations are laid out along the rivers; Indigenous people are first forced to work, then enslaved Africans are shipped in, sold in Paramaribo and driven by overseers while planters grow rich. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 6 Geschiedenis*, Hoofdstuk 3: Plantages en slaven, printed p. 30.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: De aanleg van plantages.
**Answer: Plantations are drained and laid out along the lower rivers and creeks with sluices and polders; today's place names (Beekhuizen, Berlijn, Totness) still remember them. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden*, Thema 4 Les 1: De aanleg van plantages, printed p. 59.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Plantage Meerzorg.
**Answer: One plantation studied whole: Meerzorg in Commewijne; by 1712 there are some 200 plantations, Cassard's French attack exposes weak defences, and Fort Nieuw Amsterdam is built in answer. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden*, Thema 4 Les 2: Plantage Meerzorg, printed p. 63.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?.
**Answer: A plantation is a whole organisation under its owner and director; enslaved field gangs do the planting, tending and harvesting under the basya's watch while others look on at punishments. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden*, Thema 4 Les 3: Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?, printed p. 67.**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Retell this part in your own words: Slaafgemaakten in Paramaribo.
**Answer: Life after the auction depends on the work assigned; the story of Afi Jaba, born in Africa and enslaved in 18th-century Suriname, stands beside the free Black people who also lived in the colony. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** In Suriname's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden*, Thema 5 Les 3: Slaafgemaakten in Paramaribo, printed p. 83.**
