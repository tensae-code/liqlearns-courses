## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Plantages en slaven** — Plantations are laid out along the rivers; Indigenous people are first forced to work, then enslaved Africans are shipped in, sold in Paramaribo and driven by overseers while planters grow rich.
2. **De aanleg van plantages** — Plantations are drained and laid out along the lower rivers and creeks with sluices and polders; today's place names (Beekhuizen, Berlijn, Totness) still remember them.
3. **Plantage Meerzorg** — One plantation studied whole: Meerzorg in Commewijne; by 1712 there are some 200 plantations, Cassard's French attack exposes weak defences, and Fort Nieuw Amsterdam is built in answer.
4. **Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?** — A plantation is a whole organisation under its owner and director; enslaved field gangs do the planting, tending and harvesting under the basya's watch while others look on at punishments.
5. **Slaafgemaakten in Paramaribo** — Life after the auction depends on the work assigned; the story of Afi Jaba, born in Africa and enslaved in 18th-century Suriname, stands beside the free Black people who also lived in the colony.
