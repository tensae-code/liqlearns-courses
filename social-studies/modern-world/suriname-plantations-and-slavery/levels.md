# Suriname — plantations and slavery — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Plantages en slaven
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Plantations are laid out along the rivers; Indigenous people are first forced to work, then enslaved Africans are shipped in, sold in Paramaribo and driven by overseers while planters grow rich.

## Sub lesson 2 — De aanleg van plantages
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Plantations are drained and laid out along the lower rivers and creeks with sluices and polders; today's place names (Beekhuizen, Berlijn, Totness) still remember them.

## Sub lesson 3 — Plantage Meerzorg
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — One plantation studied whole: Meerzorg in Commewijne; by 1712 there are some 200 plantations, Cassard's French attack exposes weak defences, and Fort Nieuw Amsterdam is built in answer.

## Sub lesson 4 — Hoe was het werk verdeeld op een plantage?
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — A plantation is a whole organisation under its owner and director; enslaved field gangs do the planting, tending and harvesting under the basya's watch while others look on at punishments.

## Sub lesson 5 — Slaafgemaakten in Paramaribo
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Life after the auction depends on the work assigned; the story of Afi Jaba, born in Africa and enslaved in 18th-century Suriname, stands beside the free Black people who also lived in the colony.
