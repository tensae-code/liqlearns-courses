# Hands-on — Venezuela 23 January 1958

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela 23 January 1958 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, A good day: 23 January 1958; the provisional Junta, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
