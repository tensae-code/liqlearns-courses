# Lesson plan — The Marianas and Carolines — first Catholic missions

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Marianas and Carolines — first Catholic missions
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Fr. Sanvitores planted the first Catholic mission on Guam, the Ladrones were renamed the Marianas, Spanish soldiers turned welcome into conflict, and Fr. Cantova later carried word of the faith into the Carolines — the first missions joined the islands to Spain's empire and its church at the same time.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Fr. Sanvitores plants the first mission on Guam** — say that Fr. Sanvitores planted the first mission on Guam.
2. **The Ladrones renamed the Marianas — welcome turns to conflict** — say that the Ladrones were renamed the Marianas, and that Spanish soldiers turned welcome into conflict.
3. **Fr. Cantova carries the faith into the Carolines** — say that Fr. Cantova later carried word of the faith into the Carolines.
