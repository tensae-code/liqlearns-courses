# The Marianas and Carolines — first Catholic missions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first Catholic mission on Guam was planted by —
a) Fr. Cantova  b) Fr. Sanvitores  c) Dioga da Rocha
**Answer: b) Fr. Sanvitores** — the priest the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Ladrones were renamed —
a) The Carolines  b) The Marianas  c) The Marshalls
**Answer: b) The Marianas** — the new name the book gives.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On Guam, welcome turned into conflict through —
a) Spanish soldiers  b) A storm  c) A trade quarrel
**Answer: a) Spanish soldiers** — the cause the book names.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Fr. Cantova carried word of the faith into —
a) The Marshalls  b) The Carolines  c) Kosrae
**Answer: b) The Carolines** — the island group the book names.
