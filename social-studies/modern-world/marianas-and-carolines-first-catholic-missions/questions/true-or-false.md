# The Marianas and Carolines — first Catholic missions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first Catholic mission in the islands was on Guam.
**Answer: True** — the book plants Sanvitores' first mission on Guam.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The Carolines first heard of the Catholic faith from Protestant missionaries.
**Answer: False** — Fr. Cantova, a Catholic priest, carried word of the faith into the Carolines.
