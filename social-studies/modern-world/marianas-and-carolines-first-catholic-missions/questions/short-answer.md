# The Marianas and Carolines — first Catholic missions — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What two things arrived on Guam with the first mission?
**Answer: A permanent church presence under Fr. Sanvitores — and Spanish power: the islands renamed the Marianas, with soldiers whose presence turned welcome into conflict (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Who carried the Catholic faith into the Carolines, and from where did the mission story spread?
**Answer: Fr. Cantova carried it into the Carolines, spreading south from Sanvitores' mission on Guam in the Marianas (any close wording).**
