# Hands-on — The Marianas and Carolines — first Catholic missions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## One island, two arrivals
Draw Guam's shore twice: first the mission as the priest hoped it would be, then the mission with soldiers behind it. Under each drawing, write one sentence about what changed for the islanders.

## Follow the journey
On a blank Pacific map (or a drawn one), mark Guam in the Marianas and the Carolines to its south. Draw Cantova's journey as an arrow, and say aloud why a mission journey is also a map of an empire growing.
