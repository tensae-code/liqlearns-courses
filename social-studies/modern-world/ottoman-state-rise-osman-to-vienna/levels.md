## Sub lesson 1 — Rise of the Ottoman state
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a Turkish tribe fleeing the Mongols becomes, under Osman, a frontier emirate propped up by the Seljuks in 1299, grows on Byzantine weakness and its own fierce soldiering, takes Constantinople in 1453 where the early Arab attempts had stopped, renames it Istanbul, carries Islam into Hungary, the Balkans, Serbia, Bosnia and Albania, halts the Russian danger on the Black Sea, and is only turned back at the walls of Vienna in 1529 — then, with the Arab world exhausted by Mongols, Crusaders and Portuguese sea-power, turns south to inherit the Mamluks' place
