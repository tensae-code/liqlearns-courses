# Rise of the Ottoman state — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Rise of the Ottoman state.
**Answer: a Turkish tribe fleeing the Mongols becomes, under Osman, a frontier emirate propped up by the Seljuks in 1299, grows on Byzantine weakness and its own fierce soldiering, takes Constantinople in 1453 where the early Arab attempts had stopped, renames it Istanbul, carries Islam into Hungary, the Balkans, Serbia, Bosnia and Albania, halts the Russian danger on the Black Sea, and is only turned back at the walls of Vienna in 1529 — then, with the Arab world exhausted by Mongols, Crusaders and Portuguese sea-power, turns south to inherit the Mamluks' place (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الحديث والمعاصر للصف التاسع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (Modern and Contemporary History of the Arabs for Grade 9, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), Rise of the Ottoman state, printed p. 34.**
