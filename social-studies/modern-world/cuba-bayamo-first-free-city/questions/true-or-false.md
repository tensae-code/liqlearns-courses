# Cuba Bayamo First Free City — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 5.2 Bayamo, first free city of Cuba

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 5.2 Bayamo, first free city of Cuba

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bayamo taken and burned, the hymn composed, the war's first martyrs — the rising becomes a nation in arms.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 3.3 Main events at the war's start

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Noveno grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
