# Cuba Literacy Campaign 1961

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in 1961 an army of young literacy teachers goes to the countryside and Cuba declares itself free of illiteracy in a year As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 6.4 How the Revolution fought the country's cultural backwardness

in 1961 an army of young literacy teachers goes to the countryside and Cuba declares itself free of illiteracy in a year

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

literacy campaign, universities and science built by a poor country are presented as the Revolution's cultural proof

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: 6.4 How the Revolution fought the country's cultural backwardness — in 1961 an army of young literacy teachers goes to the countryside and Cuba declares itself free of illiteracy in a year; 6.5 Panorama of culture, science and education in Cuba from 1959 — literacy campaign, universities and science built by a poor country are presented as the Revolution's cultural proof. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
