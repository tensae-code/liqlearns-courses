# China May Fourth To Resistance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 3, China from May Fourth to the resistance war.
**Answer: on 4 May 1919 three thousand Beijing students march and a movement is born; the Chinese Communist Party is founded in July 1921; from 1927 to 1937 it fights a revolutionary war against the Kuomintang, then in 1937 proposes the united front against Japan (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
