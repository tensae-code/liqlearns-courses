# Hands-on — China May Fourth To Resistance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — China May Fourth To Resistance — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 3, China from May Fourth to the resistance war, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
