# Suriname — the Brokopondo dam and its people — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the 1955 Ten-Year Plan the government and Alcoa agree in 1958 to dam the Suriname river at Brokopondo for hydroelectricity, flooding the homes of the people and animals of the area.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De stuwdam in de Surinamerivier

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about De stuwdam in de Surinamerivier
