# Video script — Suriname — the Brokopondo dam and its people

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Under the 1955 Ten-Year Plan the government and Alcoa agree in 1958 to dam the Suriname river at Brokopondo for hydroelectricity, flooding the homes of the people and animals of the area.
