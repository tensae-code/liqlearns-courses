# The Young Turks and the Ottoman Constitution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. V §34 Turkey at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century.
**Answer: the Young Ottomans win a constitution (1876), Abdulhamid II dissolves the parliament and rules by terror for thirty years; the officers' Union and Progress society — the Young Turks — rises in the army, and in July 1908 the Monastir garrison's revolt forces the constitution back to life (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Всемирная история 9 (Vsemirnaya istoriya — World History), Grade 9* (Uzbekistan), Ch. V §34 Turkey at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century, printed p. 111.**
