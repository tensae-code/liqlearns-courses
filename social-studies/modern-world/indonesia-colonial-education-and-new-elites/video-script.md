# Video script — Indonesia — colonial schools and the new educated elite

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colonial schools — STOVIA's medical students, the Kartini schools, Padang Panjang's Madrasah Diniyah Putri — are built to serve the colony, and instead produce the educated generation that will end it.
