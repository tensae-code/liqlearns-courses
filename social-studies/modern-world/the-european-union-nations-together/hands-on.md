# Hands-on — The European Union Nations Together

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Tema 17: Europa, uniune a națiunilor libere, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
