# Lesson plan — The European Union Nations Together

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The European Union Nations Together
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Moldova, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** free European nations pool their future in the EU — parliament at Strasbourg, motto "Unity in diversity" proposed by Europe's young people in 2002
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tema 17: Europa, uniune a națiunilor libere** — free European nations pool their future in the EU — parliament at Strasbourg, motto "Unity in diversity" proposed by Europe's young people in 2002
2. **Tema 40: Integrarea Europeană. Uniunea Europeană la începutul mileniului III** — integration begins in 1951 as the answer to war's crisis; Nice (2001) brings majority voting, and on 1 June 2023 Europe's leaders summit at Bulboaca in Moldova itself
3. **Tema 40: Integrarea economică europeană. Crearea și evoluția Uniunii Europene** — Schuman's 9 May 1950 declaration makes war between France and Germany "materially impossible" through shared coal and steel; the 1985 Single European Act promises a space without internal frontiers
