# Video script — America's age of empire, 1890–1914

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The frontier closed, a sea-power prophet preached fleets — and in 1898 the United States won an overseas empire in a single short war.

Turner's frontier thesis argued the frontier had made America — and was now closing. Mahan's sea-power writings argued nations rose on navies and trade. Together they argued America outward: the nation's future lay beyond its shores.

The Spanish-American War of 1898 won the United States an overseas empire — Spain's former colonies passed under American power. In one war, the republic became an empire; the book lets the tension in that sentence stand.

The Open Door reached into China — demanding American trade access without colonies there. Roosevelt's 'Big Stick' diplomacy made the Caribbean an American lake, guarded by the fleet. Empire had a doctrine on each ocean.
