# America's age of empire, 1890–1914 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Turner's frontier thesis argued the frontier —
a) Was just opening  b) Had made America and was closing  c) Never mattered
**Answer: b) Had made America and was closing** — Turner's frontier thesis argues America outward.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mahan preached the power of —
a) Cavalry  b) Sea power — navies and trade  c) Isolation
**Answer: b) Sea power — navies and trade** — Mahan's sea power.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Spanish-American War was fought in —
a) 1861  b) 1898  c) 1917
**Answer: b) 1898** — the Spanish-American War (1898).

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The 'Big Stick' made which sea an American lake?
a) The Mediterranean  b) The Caribbean  c) The Baltic
**Answer: b) The Caribbean** — Roosevelt's Big Stick makes the Caribbean an American lake.
