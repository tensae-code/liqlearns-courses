# America's age of empire, 1890–1914 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Open Door policy concerned trade access in China.
**Answer: True** — The Open Door reaches into China.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The United States took no overseas empire in 1898.
**Answer: False** — The Spanish-American War wins an overseas empire.
