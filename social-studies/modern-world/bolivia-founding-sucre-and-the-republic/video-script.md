# Video script — Bolivia's founding — Sucre and the republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sucre arrives crowned by Ayacucho; Peru and Argentina press colonial-era claims on Alto Perú, and at Chuquisaca's Casa de la Libertad the new republic is decided.

Teacher-compendium unit on the creation of Bolivia, same arc as the Secundaria 4 student text.
