# Lesson plan — Bolivia's founding — Sucre and the republic

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Bolivia's founding — Sucre and the republic
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Bolivia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sucre arrives crowned by Ayacucho; Peru and Argentina press colonial-era claims on Alto Perú, and at Chuquisaca's Casa de la Libertad the new republic is decided.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
