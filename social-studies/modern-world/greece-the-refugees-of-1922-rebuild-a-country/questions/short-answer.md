# Greece The Refugees Of 1922 Rebuild A Country — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §44 The refugee question in interwar Greece.
**Answer: a quarter of the population arrives with nothing; refugee quarters from Kokkinia to Nea Ionia remake Greek cities, music and politics (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
