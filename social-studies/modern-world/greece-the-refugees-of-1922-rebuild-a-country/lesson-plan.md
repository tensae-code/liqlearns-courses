## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§44 The refugee question in interwar Greece** — a quarter of the population arrives with nothing; refugee quarters from Kokkinia to Nea Ionia remake Greek cities, music and politics
2. **The refugee question in interwar Greece** — one and a half million refugees are housed, fed and settled — and remake Greece's cities, farming and politics
3. **Economy and society; the refugee problem** — a state of five million absorbs over a million refugees and is transformed by them
4. **Internal developments in Greece (1923–1930)** — a republic is proclaimed, a million and a half refugees are settled in Macedonia and the cities, and Venizelos's 1928 return brings a last reforming burst
5. **Theme 3 B: The Lausanne Convention and the exchange of populations** — the 1923 convention makes the flight compulsory and mutual — about 1.2 million Orthodox to Greece, some 400,000 Muslims to Turkey
6. **Theme 3 C: The rehabilitation of the refugees** — the League of Nations' Refugee Settlement Commission houses the newcomers in Macedonian villages and new urban quarters
7. **Theme 3 E: The integration of the refugees into Greece** — within a generation the refugees transform Greece's farming, industry, cities and culture — and are transformed by it
8. **Theme 5 E: The presence of Black Sea Hellenism today — the refugee problem from 1922** — the survivors reach Greece as refugees and rebuild Pontic villages in Macedonia, carrying the loss with them
