# Lesson plan — Militarism in Japan — the army above the state

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Militarism in Japan — the army above the state
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — the interwar unit; the Grade 10 book's short Japan lesson completing the Axis pattern (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in Japan, militarism — begun in the late Meiji period and peaking in 1931–1945 — rested on the belief that the military should dominate the political and social life of the whole society: the third leg of the Axis.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Fascism and Nazism — two dictatorships compared; Japan is the pattern's third case. It needs nothing else.
