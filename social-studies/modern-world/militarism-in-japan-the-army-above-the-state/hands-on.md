# Hands-on — Militarism in Japan — the army above the state

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Above or below?
Draw two diagrams: a state where the government commands the army, and a militarist state where the army dominates the government. Label who decides war, budget and schools in each.

## Three legs, one pattern
Make a three-column chart — Italy, Germany, Japan — with one row: how did civilian rule give way? Fill it from the three lessons.
