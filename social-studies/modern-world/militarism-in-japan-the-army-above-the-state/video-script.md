# Video script — Militarism in Japan — the army above the state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Japan. Soldiers drill; behind them, the ministries shrink in the frame.
The years run: Meiji modernisation, an army built with the empire — then 1931. The generals' shadows lengthen over the whole map of Asia.
Three flags rise on the world map — Rome, Berlin, Tokyo. The camera pulls back to 1939, and the war the three patterns made.
