# Militarism in Japan — the army above the state — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. ** Militarism means simply having a strong army.
**Answer: False** — it means the military dominating political and social life — standing above the state.

**2. ** Militarist Japan fought the Second World War on the Axis side.
**Answer: True** — with Fascist Italy and Nazi Germany.

**3. ** Japan's militarism carried it into expansion in Asia in its peak years.
**Answer: True** — 1931–1945, into the Second World War on the Axis side.

**4. ** In a militarist state, civilian governments reliably command the army.
**Answer: False** — the lesson's point is the reverse — the army dominates the government.
