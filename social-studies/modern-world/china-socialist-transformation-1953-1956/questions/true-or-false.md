# China Socialist Transformation 1953 1956 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Farming, handicrafts and capitalist industry and commerce are guided step by step into public and collective ownership, completing the socialist transformation by 1956.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The three great transformations

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The three great transformations

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The general line, the First Five-Year Plan's 156 projects and the three transformations carry China into a socialist system by 1956.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Transition to socialism

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Transition to socialism
