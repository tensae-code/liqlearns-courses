# China Socialist Transformation 1953 1956

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: farming, handicrafts and capitalist industry and commerce are guided step by step into public and collective ownership, completing the socialist transformation by 1956 As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The three great transformations

farming, handicrafts and capitalist industry and commerce are guided step by step into public and collective ownership, completing the socialist transformation by 1956

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Transition to socialism

the general line, the First Five-Year Plan's 156 projects and the three transformations carry China into a socialist system by 1956

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: The three great transformations — farming, handicrafts and capitalist industry and commerce are guided step by step into public and collective ownership, completing the socialist transformation by 1956. Transition to socialism — the general line, the First Five-Year Plan's 156 projects and the three transformations carry China into a socialist system by 1956. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
