# Nicaragua Sandino And Sovereignty — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Efemérides: anniversary of General Augusto C. Sandino's passing
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sandino is presented as a duty to remember, a Nicaraguan who defended the homeland, his story to be known by every generation

## Sub lesson 2 — Efemérides: birth of Augusto Nicolás Calderón Sandino
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sandino is born 18 May; the book pairs him with Darío as Nicaragua's two greatest expressions and links him forward to Carlos Fonseca

## Sub lesson 3 — Héroes de la Patria: Augusto Nicolás Calderón Sandino
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sandino, born 18 May in Niquinohomo, becomes "General of Free Men and Women"; the book says you cannot speak of Nicaragua or homeland without him

## Sub lesson 4 — Héroes de la Patria: Blanca Arauz
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Blanca Arauz stands beside Sandino in the defence of national sovereignty, the telegraphist wife who carried the general's messages in wartime

## Sub lesson 5 — Personajes históricos: Augusto Nicolás Calderón Sandino
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sandino, General of Free Men and Women, is retold as the patriot who would not accept foreign occupation

## Sub lesson 6 — La Gesta de Benjamín Zeledón
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — when Mena is captured Zeledón takes command of the 1912 resistance, holds out at La Barranca and El Coyotepe in Masaya with only courage and love of country, and is captured and killed after an unequal fight

## Sub lesson 7 — Segunda intervención norteamericana y la gesta de Sandino
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sandino, working in Mexico, hears of the invasion, returns, and on 26 Oct 1926 rises at the San Albino mine rather than lay down arms

## Sub lesson 8 — Fundación del Ejército Defensor de la Soberanía Nacional; ventajas guerrilleras; asesinato de Sandino
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sandino's small army beats the marines by refusing long battles, vanishing on paths only locals know; after the marines leave, Sandino is murdered in 1934

## Sub lesson 9 — Lucha del General Augusto C. Sandino
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the boy who saw Zeledón's corpse carted past in 1912 refuses the 1927 pact, marries Blanca Aráuz and raises the Army in Defence of National Sovereignty from 29 men and 40 rifles at El Chipote

## Sub lesson 10 — Asesinato de Sandino
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the marines, unable to beat the EDSN, build a native Guardia; after peace talks with Sacasa, Sandino is seized and murdered on 21 February 1934, and over 300 Sandinista peasants are massacred at Wiwilí

## Sub lesson 11 — Lucha de Sandino
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sandino refuses the Espino Negro betrayal and withdraws to the Segovias mountains to restore the violated constitution and expel the intervention forces; solidarity committees rise from New York to Buenos Aires as marine columns are wiped out
