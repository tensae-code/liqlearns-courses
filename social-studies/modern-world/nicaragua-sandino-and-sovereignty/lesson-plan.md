## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Efemérides: anniversary of General Augusto C. Sandino's passing** — Sandino is presented as a duty to remember, a Nicaraguan who defended the homeland, his story to be known by every generation
2. **Efemérides: birth of Augusto Nicolás Calderón Sandino** — Sandino is born 18 May; the book pairs him with Darío as Nicaragua's two greatest expressions and links him forward to Carlos Fonseca
3. **Héroes de la Patria: Augusto Nicolás Calderón Sandino** — Sandino, born 18 May in Niquinohomo, becomes "General of Free Men and Women"; the book says you cannot speak of Nicaragua or homeland without him
4. **Héroes de la Patria: Blanca Arauz** — Blanca Arauz stands beside Sandino in the defence of national sovereignty, the telegraphist wife who carried the general's messages in wartime
5. **Personajes históricos: Augusto Nicolás Calderón Sandino** — Sandino, General of Free Men and Women, is retold as the patriot who would not accept foreign occupation
6. **La Gesta de Benjamín Zeledón** — when Mena is captured Zeledón takes command of the 1912 resistance, holds out at La Barranca and El Coyotepe in Masaya with only courage and love of country, and is captured and killed after an unequal fight
7. **Segunda intervención norteamericana y la gesta de Sandino** — Sandino, working in Mexico, hears of the invasion, returns, and on 26 Oct 1926 rises at the San Albino mine rather than lay down arms
8. **Fundación del Ejército Defensor de la Soberanía Nacional; ventajas guerrilleras; asesinato de Sandino** — Sandino's small army beats the marines by refusing long battles, vanishing on paths only locals know; after the marines leave, Sandino is murdered in 1934
9. **Lucha del General Augusto C. Sandino** — the boy who saw Zeledón's corpse carted past in 1912 refuses the 1927 pact, marries Blanca Aráuz and raises the Army in Defence of National Sovereignty from 29 men and 40 rifles at El Chipote
10. **Asesinato de Sandino** — the marines, unable to beat the EDSN, build a native Guardia; after peace talks with Sacasa, Sandino is seized and murdered on 21 February 1934, and over 300 Sandinista peasants are massacred at Wiwilí
11. **Lucha de Sandino** — Sandino refuses the Espino Negro betrayal and withdraws to the Segovias mountains to restore the violated constitution and expel the intervention forces; solidarity committees rise from New York to Buenos Aires as marine columns are wiped out
