# The Unfederated Malay States and Johor — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Perlis, Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu live first under Siam's shadow before British influence reaches them, and Johor follows its own path; kept outside the federation, these five sultanates retain more of their rulers' hand in administration
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 4: the Unfederated Malay States).
