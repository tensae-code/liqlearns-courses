# The Unfederated Malay States and Johor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Unfederated Malay States  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Unfederated Malay States** — Perlis, Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu live first under Siam's shadow before British influence reaches them, and Johor follows its own path; kept outside the federation, these five sultanates retain more of their rulers' hand in administration

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 4: the Unfederated Malay States  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 4: the Unfederated Malay States** — the book's own heading for this part.
