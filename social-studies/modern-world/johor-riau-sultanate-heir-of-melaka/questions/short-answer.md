# The Johor Riau Sultanate — heir of Melaka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The Johor Riau Sultanate.
**Answer: Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories; it survives the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle (Tun Habib Abdul Majid against Tun Abdul Jamil, who withdraws to Terengganu) and Raja Kechil's threat — moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch — and flowers again as a trade centre reviving commerce with Arabia, India and China, a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut (any close wording).**
