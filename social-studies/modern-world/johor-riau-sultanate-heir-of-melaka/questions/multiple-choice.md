# The Johor Riau Sultanate — heir of Melaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Johor Riau Sultanate  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Johor Riau Sultanate** — Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories; it survives the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle (Tun Habib Abdul Majid against Tun Abdul Jamil, who withdraws to Terengganu) and Raja Kechil's threat — moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch — and flowers again as a trade centre reviving commerce with Arabia, India and China, a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut

a) Bab 6: the Johor Riau Sultanate  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 6: the Johor Riau Sultanate** — the book's own heading for this part.
