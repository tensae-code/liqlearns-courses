# The Johor Riau Sultanate — heir of Melaka — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories; it survives the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle (Tun Habib Abdul Majid against Tun Abdul Jamil, who withdraws to Terengganu) and Raja Kechil's threat — moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch — and flowers again as a trade centre reviving commerce with Arabia, India and China, a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 6: the Johor Riau Sultanate).
