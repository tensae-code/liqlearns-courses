# Lesson plan — The Johor Riau Sultanate — heir of Melaka

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Johor Riau Sultanate — heir of Melaka
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Malaysia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau, survive rivalry and war, and flower again as a trade centre and a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Johor Riau Sultanate** — Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories; it survives the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle (Tun Habib Abdul Majid against Tun Abdul Jamil, who withdraws to Terengganu) and Raja Kechil's threat — moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch — and flowers again as a trade centre reviving commerce with Arabia, India and China, a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut (Bab 6: the Johor Riau Sultanate)
