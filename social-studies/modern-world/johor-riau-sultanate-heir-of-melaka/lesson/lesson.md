# The Johor Riau Sultanate — heir of Melaka

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau, survive rivalry and war, and flower again as a trade centre and a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Johor Riau Sultanate

Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories; it survives the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle (Tun Habib Abdul Majid against Tun Abdul Jamil, who withdraws to Terengganu) and Raja Kechil's threat — moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch — and flowers again as a trade centre reviving commerce with Arabia, India and China, a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut (Bab 6: the Johor Riau Sultanate)
