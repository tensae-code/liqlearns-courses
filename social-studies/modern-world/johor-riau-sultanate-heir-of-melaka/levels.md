## Sub lesson 1 — The Johor Riau Sultanate
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Melaka's heirs refound the sultanate at Johor Riau with Melaka's government and territories; it survives the Johor–Acheh–Portuguese rivalry, the Johor–Jambi war, the Bendahara–Laksamana struggle (Tun Habib Abdul Majid against Tun Abdul Jamil, who withdraws to Terengganu) and Raja Kechil's threat — moving its capital, building forts and allying with the Dutch — and flowers again as a trade centre reviving commerce with Arabia, India and China, a centre of Malay letters, guarded at sea by the Orang Laut
