# Sri Lanka — The Dutch take the coast — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Dutch come to Asia and Kandy seeks their help.
**Answer: The VOC is founded in 1602, Spilbergen lands at Batticaloa, Sebald de Weert is killed after insulting Vimaladharmasuriya I, yet Rajasinghe II signs the 1638 treaty and the Dutch take the Portuguese forts but refuse to hand them back (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Sri Lanka's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *G9 History Tamil 2019* (Sri Lanka), The Dutch come to Asia and Kandy seeks their help, printed p. 1.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Establishment of Dutch power and Kandyan relations (competency level 1.1, 10 periods).
**Answer: Teachers stage the story of a king who invites the Dutch in to expel one enemy and gains another, from the VOC's charter to the broken promise over the forts (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Sri Lanka's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *G9 History Teacher Guide NIE 2018* (Sri Lanka), Establishment of Dutch power and Kandyan relations (competency level 1.1, 10 periods), printed p. 1.**
