# Sri Lanka — The Dutch take the coast — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The VOC is founded in 1602, Spilbergen lands at Batticaloa, Sebald de Weert is killed after insulting Vimaladharmasuriya I, yet Rajasinghe II signs the 1638 treaty and the Dutch take the Portuguese forts but refuse to hand them back.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Dutch come to Asia and Kandy seeks their help

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G9 History Tamil 2019* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Dutch come to Asia and Kandy seeks their help

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Teachers stage the story of a king who invites the Dutch in to expel one enemy and gains another, from the VOC's charter to the broken promise over the forts.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Establishment of Dutch power and Kandyan relations (competency level 1.1, 10 periods)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G9 History Teacher Guide NIE 2018* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Establishment of Dutch power and Kandyan relations (competency level 1.1, 10 periods)
