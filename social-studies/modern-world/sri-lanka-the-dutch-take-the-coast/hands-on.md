# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — The Dutch take the coast

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — The Dutch take the coast — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Dutch come to Asia and Kandy seeks their help, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
