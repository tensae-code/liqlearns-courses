# Cuba Moncada 26 July 1953 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** young attackers assault the Moncada and Bayamo barracks at carnival dawn; the attack fails militarily, prisoners are murdered, but a programme and a movement are born.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 4.3 The actions of 26 July 1953

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Sexto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 4.3 The actions of 26 July 1953

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Ortodoxo youth become a vanguard, attack at dawn, and turn the courtroom into a tribune.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 8.1–8.3 Preparation of the political vanguard; assault on the Moncada and Carlos Manuel de Céspedes barracks; trial of the 26 July survivors

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Noveno grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 8.1–8.3 Preparation of the political vanguard; assault on the Moncada and Carlos Manuel de Céspedes barracks; trial of the 26 July survivors

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the Moncada generation becomes a movement: 26th of July, civic resistance and the Frank País network organise a people for war.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 5.1–5.2 The new revolutionary vanguard; organisation of revolutionary forces and popular mobilisation
