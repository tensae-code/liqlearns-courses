## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **4.3 The actions of 26 July 1953** — young attackers assault the Moncada and Bayamo barracks at carnival dawn; the attack fails militarily, prisoners are murdered, but a programme and a movement are born
2. **8.1–8.3 Preparation of the political vanguard; assault on the Moncada and Carlos Manuel de Céspedes barracks; trial of the 26 July survivors** — the Ortodoxo youth become a vanguard, attack at dawn, and turn the courtroom into a tribune
3. **5.1–5.2 The new revolutionary vanguard; organisation of revolutionary forces and popular mobilisation** — the Moncada generation becomes a movement: 26th of July, civic resistance and the Frank País network organise a people for war
