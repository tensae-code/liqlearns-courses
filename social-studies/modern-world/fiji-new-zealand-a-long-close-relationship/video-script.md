# Video script — Fiji and New Zealand — a long, close relationship

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Bank of New Zealand opens in Levuka in 1877; in WWI Fijians travel to join the Māori Battalion and Sukuna's schooling links the two peoples; the RNZAF builds at Laucala Bay, and in November 1941 the US agrees to finance two 7,000-foot runways at Nadi, built by a New Zealand firm — the origin of Nadi Airport. A Bauan learns rugby in New Zealand and returns to teach it; a Fiji Times report has the Naililili side singing "We licked them on their own ground", and Robert Dewey's research is set against the myth of Fiji rugby's origins.
