# Fiji and New Zealand — a long, close relationship

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Bank of New Zealand opens in Levuka in 1877; in WWI Fijians travel to join the Māori Battalion and Sukuna's schooling links the two peoples; the RNZAF builds at Laucala Bay, and in November 1941 the US agrees to finance two 7,000-foot runways at Nadi, built by a New Zealand firm — the origin of Nadi Airport As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

A Bauan learns rugby in New Zealand and returns to teach it; a Fiji Times report has the Naililili side singing "We licked them on their own ground", and Robert Dewey's research is set against the myth of Fiji rugby's origins.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
