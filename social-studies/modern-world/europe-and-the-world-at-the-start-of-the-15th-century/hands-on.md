# Hands-on — Europe and the world at the start of the 15th century

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Europe and the world at the start of the 15th century — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Europe and the world at the start of the 15th century, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
