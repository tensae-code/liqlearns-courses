# Kazakhstan Steppe Fairs And Trade — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** great fairs like Koyandy and Karkaraly link nomad producers to Russian and Central Asian merchants.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§39–40 The development of fair trade

**Answer: False** — it is about §§39–40 The development of fair trade
