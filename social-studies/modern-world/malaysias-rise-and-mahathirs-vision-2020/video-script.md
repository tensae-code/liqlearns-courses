# Video script — Malaysia's rise and Mahathir's Vision 2020

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Jordan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

a trading crossroads that welcomed Arab and Indian merchants for centuries outgrows colonial rule, Singapore's separation and the 1997 Asian crisis by diversifying — cars, electronics, steel, textiles; Mahathir Mohamad, doctor and prime minister, sets a thirty-year plan (1990–2020) to stand level with the West on world-class education and social justice, planned by a committee of national and foreign experts; Jordan and Malaysia exchange embassies from 1965, Malaysia's first in Amman in 1994.
