# Venezuela Days To Remember — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The school year is pinned to shared dates, among them 12 October, Day of Indigenous Resistance, when the country remembers first peoples instead of celebrating conquest.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Days to remember
