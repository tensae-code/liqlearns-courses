# China Xinhai Revolution 1911 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Xinhai Revolution.
**Answer: the Qing sells the railways, Sichuan rises to protect them, an accidental bomb in Wuchang starts the revolution on 10 October 1911, 14 provinces secede in two months, and Sun Yat-sen takes office on New Year's Day 1912 — but the fruits fall to Yuan Shikai (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 19: The Xinhai Revolution, printed p. 116.**
