# Video script — China Xinhai Revolution 1911

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as China's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Qing sells the railways, Sichuan rises to protect them, an accidental bomb in Wuchang starts the revolution on 10 October 1911, 14 provinces secede in two months, and Sun Yat-sen takes office on New Year's Day 1912 — but the fruits fall to Yuan Shikai
