# Guatemala's four peoples — Maya, Garífuna, Xinka, Ladino — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Guatemala's four peoples are —
a) Maya, Garífuna, Xinka and Ladino  b) Maya, Aztec, Inca and Spanish  c) Only Maya and Ladino
**Answer: a) Maya, Garífuna, Xinka and Ladino** — the four the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Garífuna descend from —
a) Africans brought as slaves  b) the first Maya kings  c) Spanish settlers only
**Answer: a) Africans brought as slaves** — their origin as the book gives it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mitijul is the Maya value of —
a) work, with gratitude  b) trading gold  c) building pyramids
**Answer: a) work, with gratitude** — the value the guide names.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The language lottery uses word cards in —
a) Cho'rti', Mopan, Tz'utujil and other Mayan languages  b) only Spanish  c) only English
**Answer: a) Cho'rti', Mopan, Tz'utujil and other Mayan languages** — the languages in the activity.
