# Guatemala's four peoples — Maya, Garífuna, Xinka, Ladino — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Peace Accords recognise all four peoples.
**Answer: True** — as the Grade 4 book tells it.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** One Guatemalan classroom can hold many peoples and more than twenty languages.
**Answer: True** — the lottery and family-tree activities show it.
