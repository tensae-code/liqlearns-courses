# Cambodia — the Sangkum, 1953–1970 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 2, the Sangkum Reastr Niyum regime (1953–1970)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា សំណួរ និងចម្លើយ ឆ្នាំ២០១៤ ដល់ ២០២២ — ត្រៀមប្រឡងសញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាទុតិយភូមិ (History Questions and Answers 2014–2022, for the upper-secondary diploma exam)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 2, the Sangkum Reastr Niyum regime (1953–1970)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Geneva (1954) confirms independence; Sihanouk abdicates on 2 March 1955, founds the Sangkum on 22 March, and sweeps the September 1955 elections — the prince rules as head of a national movement.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum regime (1955–1970)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី១២ (History, Grade 12)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum regime (1955–1970)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Sihanouk (born 31 October 1922; photo, p. 112) wins independence on 9 November 1953, abdicates in March 1955, founds the Sangkum, and takes 99.80% in the September 1955 elections.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum era (1955–1970)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៩ (Social Studies, Grade 9)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
