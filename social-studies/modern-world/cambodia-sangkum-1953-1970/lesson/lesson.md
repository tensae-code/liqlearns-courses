# Cambodia — the Sangkum, 1953–1970

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 2, the Sangkum Reastr Niyum regime (1953–1970)

Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case (Lesson 2, the Sangkum Reastr Niyum regime (1953–1970))

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum regime (1955–1970)

Geneva (1954) confirms independence; Sihanouk abdicates on 2 March 1955, founds the Sangkum on 22 March, and sweeps the September 1955 elections — the prince rules as head of a national movement (Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum regime (1955–1970))

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum era (1955–1970)

Sihanouk (born 31 October 1922; photo, p. 112) wins independence on 9 November 1953, abdicates in March 1955, founds the Sangkum, and takes 99.80% in the September 1955 elections (History Ch. 2 Lesson 2, The Sangkum era (1955–1970))
