# Video script — Cambodia — the Sangkum, 1953–1970

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sihanouk founds the Sangkum, builds schools, roads and ports, joins the UN in 1955, stands neutral at Bandung — and wins the Preah Vihear case.

Geneva (1954) confirms independence; Sihanouk abdicates on 2 March 1955, founds the Sangkum on 22 March, and sweeps the September 1955 elections — the prince rules as head of a national movement.

Sihanouk (born 31 October 1922; photo, p. 112) wins independence on 9 November 1953, abdicates in March 1955, founds the Sangkum, and takes 99.80% in the September 1955 elections.
