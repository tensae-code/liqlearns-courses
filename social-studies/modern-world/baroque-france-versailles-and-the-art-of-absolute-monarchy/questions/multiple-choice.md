# Baroque France — Versailles and the art of absolute monarchy — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Versailles, a machine for power  b) Art becomes government  c) England answers
**Answer: b) Art becomes government** — Louis XIV turns art into government.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Versailles, a machine for power  b) Art becomes government  c) England answers
**Answer: a) Versailles, a machine for power** — Versailles rises as a machine for displaying absolute power, and French classicising Baroque sets the taste of every court in Europe.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Art becomes government  b) Versailles, a machine for power  c) England answers
**Answer: c) England answers** — England answers with its own restrained grandeur.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Louis XIV turns art into government — Versailles rises as a machine for displaying absolute power, French classicising Baroque sets the taste of every court in Europe, and England answers with its own restrained grandeur  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
