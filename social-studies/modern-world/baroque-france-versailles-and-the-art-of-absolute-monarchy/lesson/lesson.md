# Baroque France — Versailles and the art of absolute monarchy

## Do it first

Before you read on, picture the scene this lesson is about, from its title alone: Baroque France — Versailles and the art of absolute monarchy. What do you expect to happen, and what do you expect to change? Hold your ideas; the story will test them.
