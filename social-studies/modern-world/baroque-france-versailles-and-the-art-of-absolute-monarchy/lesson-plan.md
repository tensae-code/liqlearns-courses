# Lesson plan — Baroque France — Versailles and the art of absolute monarchy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Baroque France — Versailles and the art of absolute monarchy
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Louis XIV turns art into government — Versailles rises as a machine for displaying absolute power, French classicising Baroque sets the taste of every court in Europe, and England answers with its own restrained grandeur.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
