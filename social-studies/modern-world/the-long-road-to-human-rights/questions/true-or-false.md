# The Long Road To Human Rights — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A timeline runs from Cyrus's clay cylinder (539 BC) through Magna Carta (1215) to America's 1776 and France's 1789 declarations and the UN's 1948 charter — with Armenia's own Shahamir Shahamirian printing his «Vorogayt Parats» constitution draft in Madras in 1773.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §6.3 The history of human rights and freedoms

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Հասարակագիտություն 7. Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար» (Social Science 7, textbook for general schools) — Hrach Bayadyan, Davit Stepanyan, Vahram Soghomonyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §6.3 The history of human rights and freedoms

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Teachers are advised to present rights as a long struggle won step by step, not a gift handed down.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson plan for §6.3, the history of human rights

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *«"Հասարակագիտություն" առարկայի 7-րդ դասարանի դասագրքի օգտագործման մեթոդական ուղեցույց» (Methodological Guide for Using the Grade 7 Social Science Textbook) — H. Bayadyan, D. Stepanyan, V. Soghomonyan (the student book's own authors)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson plan for §6.3, the history of human rights

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Rights arrive in generations — civil and political rights won in the age of revolutions, social rights in the industrial age, collective rights after 1945.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §2.1 The components and generations of human rights

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *«Հասարակագիտություն 8» (Social Science 8) — Davit Stepanyan, Davit Tumasyan, Hrach Bayadyan, Vahram Soghomonyan, Siranush Tonoyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
