# Karposhs Uprising And The Great Turkish War — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 5: the Karposh uprising.
**Answer: the Great Turkish War (1683–1699) breaks Ottoman power at Vienna; Austria's Piccolomini storms down to Skopje in 1689, finds it plague-ridden and burns it for two days; in the vacuum the hajduk leader Karposh — once the Ottomans' own hajduk-hunter — raises north-eastern Macedonia, holds a free territory around Kriva Palanka, Kumanovo and Skopje for six weeks as a buffer both empires court (Leopold I writes to the "Macedonian people"), then falls at Kumanovo; the Ottomans kill him at Skopje's Stone Bridge, and thousands of Christians emigrate north with the retreating Austrians (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
