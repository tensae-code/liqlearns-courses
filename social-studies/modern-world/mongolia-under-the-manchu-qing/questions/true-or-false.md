# Mongolia under the Manchu Qing — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Khalkha splits into Tüsheet, Sechen and Jasagtu khanates, the Oirats rise in the west under Galdan, and Mongol-on-Mongol war opens the door to the Manchu.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. I.3: Mongolia (mid-17th–18th c.)
