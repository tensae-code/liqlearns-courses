# Video script — Mongolia under the Manchu Qing

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mongolia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Khalkha splits into Tüsheet, Sechen and Jasagtu khanates, the Oirats rise in the west under Galdan, and Mongol-on-Mongol war opens the door to the Manchu. Ligdan, the last great khan, is defeated by the Manchu and dies in flight in 1634; Inner Mongolia submits in 1636, and in 1691 the Khalkha nobles at Dolon Nor accept Qing rule. under Qing rule the resistance flares in Chingünjav's 1756 uprising in Khotgoid and Amarsanaa's Jungar war — both crushed, both remembered. the Qing bind Mongolia with banners and hereditary jasag princes, forbid Mongol-Chinese mixing and movement, and rule through the Lifan Yuan. the late Qing "new policy" opens Mongolia to Chinese farming colonists and firms like the Mongolor gold company (1900) — and the pressure pushes the nobles toward the 1911 break. divided Mongols fall in sequence — Inner Mongolia 1636, Khalkha at Dolon Nuur 1691, the Khoshud in Tibet, and finally the Jungar state destroyed 1755-58. the book weighs internal division, the khans' rivalries and Ligdan's failed unification against Ming decline, Russian advance and Manchu statecraft.
