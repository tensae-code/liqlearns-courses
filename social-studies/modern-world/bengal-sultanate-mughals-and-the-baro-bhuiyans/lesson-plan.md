# Lesson plan — Bengal — Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bengal — Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Bangladesh, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Bengal independent, then Bengal resisting: Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds an independent Bengal Sultanate — 'Shah-i Bangalah'; centuries later Akbar's armies kill Daud Karrani in 1576, yet the Baro Bhuiyan zamindars under Isa Khan keep resisting the Mughal subadars river by river.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the founder of the independent Bengal Sultanate and his title: Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah, 'Shah-i Bangalah'.
2. Say what happened in 1576: Akbar's armies killed Daud Karrani.
3. Name the resisters and their leader: the Baro Bhuiyans under Isa Khan, fighting the Mughal subadars river by river.
