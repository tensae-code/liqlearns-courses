# Bengal — Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The independent Bengal Sultanate was founded by —
a) Isa Khan  b) Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah  c) Akbar
**Answer: b) Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah** — the Sultanate's founder.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Shah-i Bangalah' means —
a) King of Bengal  b) Lord of the Rivers  c) Friend of Bengal
**Answer: a) King of Bengal** — Ilyas Shah's title.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Daud Karrani was killed by Akbar's armies in —
a) 1576  b) 1757  c) 1971
**Answer: a) 1576** — the year of the fall.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Baro Bhuiyans under Isa Khan resisted —
a) The British  b) The Mughal subadars  c) The Palas
**Answer: b) The Mughal subadars** — who the Bhuiyans fought.
