# Bengal — Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bengal's resistance to the Mughals ended the day Daud Karrani fell.
**Answer: False** — the Baro Bhuiyans kept resisting river by river.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Ilyas Shah's Sultanate was an independent Bengal state.
**Answer: True** — he founded an independent Sultanate as Shah-i Bangalah.
