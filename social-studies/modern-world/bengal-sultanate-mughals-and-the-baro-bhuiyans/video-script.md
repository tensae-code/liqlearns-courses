# Video script — Bengal — Sultanate, Mughals and the Baro Bhuiyans

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bangladesh book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 9 gives Bengal two fortunes. First, independence: Shamsuddin Ilyas Shah founds the Bengal Sultanate — Shah-i Bangalah, King of Bengal.

Centuries later, conquest: Akbar's armies kill Daud Karrani in 1576.

But the Baro Bhuiyans under Isa Khan keep resisting the Mughal subadars, river by river. The story does not end with the battle.
