# Video script — Papua New Guinea — colonial governance to self-government

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

How the colonial government actually ran PNG, who led inside it, what it did to the country — and how the long transition from colonial rule toward self-government began.

Billy Hughes claims New Guinea at Paris in 1919 as Australia's fortress wall; the mandate years bring the 1929 Rabaul strike, Edie Creek and Bulolo gold, the Leahy patrols into the Highlands, then war, the 1949 union of the two territories, self-government in 1972 and Independence on 16 September 1975.

How and why New Guinea was handed to Australia, the League of Nations looking over its shoulder, and German rule compared with Australian rule from the sources.
