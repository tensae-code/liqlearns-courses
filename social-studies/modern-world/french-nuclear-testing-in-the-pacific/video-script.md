# Video script — French nuclear testing in the Pacific

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

2 July 1966, Mururoa atoll — France, having lost Algeria, moves its bomb tests to its remote Pacific territory; the lagoon boils, coconut palms bend, and a radioactive cloud rises over Fangataufa and Mururoa.
