# Nicaragua National Symbols Emblems And Traditions — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Vivencias y nociones de mi Patria Nicaragua
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the homeland is introduced as a mother; land of lakes Cocibolca and Xolotlán and volcanoes, whose people are solidary, hardworking and peace-loving

## Sub lesson 2 — Símbolos Patrios y su significado (flag, shield, anthem)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — blue-white-blue flag and triangle shield are decoded piece by piece — rainbow is peace, Phrygian cap is liberty, five volcanoes are the five Central American countries united

## Sub lesson 3 — Emblemas Nacionales (sacuanjoche flower, madroño tree, guardabarranco bird)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — three living emblems are chosen from Nicaragua's own nature and each gets its story and use, the madroño flowering in December for La Purísima altars
