# Nicaragua National Symbols Emblems And Traditions — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The homeland is introduced as a mother; land of lakes Cocibolca and Xolotlán and volcanoes, whose people are solidary, hardworking and peace-loving.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Vivencias y nociones de mi Patria Nicaragua

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Conociendo mi Mundo — 2do Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Vivencias y nociones de mi Patria Nicaragua

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Blue-white-blue flag and triangle shield are decoded piece by piece — rainbow is peace, Phrygian cap is liberty, five volcanoes are the five Central American countries united.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Símbolos Patrios y su significado (flag, shield, anthem)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Conociendo mi Mundo — 2do Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Símbolos Patrios y su significado (flag, shield, anthem)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Three living emblems are chosen from Nicaragua's own nature and each gets its story and use, the madroño flowering in December for La Purísima altars.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Emblemas Nacionales (sacuanjoche flower, madroño tree, guardabarranco bird)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Conociendo mi Mundo — 2do Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Emblemas Nacionales (sacuanjoche flower, madroño tree, guardabarranco bird)
