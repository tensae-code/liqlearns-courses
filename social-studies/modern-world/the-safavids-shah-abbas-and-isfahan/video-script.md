# Video script — The Safavids — Shah Abbas and Isfahan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Shah Ismail founds a Shi'a empire in Persia in 1501, and under Shah Abbas Isfahan is rebuilt into one of the world's most beautiful capitals — "Isfahan is half the world" — before Afghan revolt ends the dynasty.
