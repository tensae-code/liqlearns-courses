# Palestine's villages destroyed in 1948 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The village grows from stone mountain houses to cement streets, then in 1948 the occupation destroys more than 500 villages, commits massacres such as Deir Yassin, and expels their people into exile as refugees.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4 Lesson 4: the Palestinian village and its destruction in 1948

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الخامس الأساسي، الجزء الثاني (Social Studies, Grade 5 Basic, Part 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 4 Lesson 4: the Palestinian village and its destruction in 1948

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** After defining migration, the lesson turns to the Nakba: the 1948 war drives about a million Palestinians from their homes into camps in Jordan, Syria and Lebanon, and pupils are asked for a just solution to the refugee question.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2 Lesson 1: migration and the displacement of the Palestinians

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف السادس الأساسي، الجزء الأول (Social Studies, Grade 6 Basic, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 2 Lesson 1: migration and the displacement of the Palestinians
