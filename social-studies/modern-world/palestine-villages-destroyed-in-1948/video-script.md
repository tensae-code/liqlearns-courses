# Video script — Palestine's villages destroyed in 1948

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Palestine's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The village grows from stone mountain houses to cement streets, then in 1948 the occupation destroys more than 500 villages, commits massacres such as Deir Yassin, and expels their people into exile as refugees.

After defining migration, the lesson turns to the Nakba: the 1948 war drives about a million Palestinians from their homes into camps in Jordan, Syria and Lebanon, and pupils are asked for a just solution to the refugee question.
