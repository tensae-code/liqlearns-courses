# Palestine's villages destroyed in 1948

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the village grows from stone mountain houses to cement streets, then in 1948 the occupation destroys more than 500 villages, commits massacres such as Deir Yassin, and expels their people into exile as refugees As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4 Lesson 4: the Palestinian village and its destruction in 1948

The village grows from stone mountain houses to cement streets, then in 1948 the occupation destroys more than 500 villages, commits massacres such as Deir Yassin, and expels their people into exile as refugees (Unit 4 Lesson 4: the Palestinian village and its destruction in 1948)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
