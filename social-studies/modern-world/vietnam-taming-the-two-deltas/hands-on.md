# Hands-on — Vietnam Taming The Two Deltas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Taming The Two Deltas — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Shared Theme 1 (in the Geography part), how people reclaimed and mastered the two deltas, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
