# Armenia First Karabakh War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** From local clashes the conflict becomes full war; Shushi and Lachin are taken in 1992, and the 1994 Bishkek ceasefire freezes an Armenian victory.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 5.1 The war in Nagorno-Karabakh

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 5.1 The war in Nagorno-Karabakh

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Teachers handle the 1992–94 war, the unrecognised state and the return to war in 2020 as living history pupils' families remember.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Appendix theme guide 5: the Nagorno-Karabakh Republic between war and status quo

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց (History of Armenia 9, Methodical Guide)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Appendix theme guide 5: the Nagorno-Karabakh Republic between war and status quo
