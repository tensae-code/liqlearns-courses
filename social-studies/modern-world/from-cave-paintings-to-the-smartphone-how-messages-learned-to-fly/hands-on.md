# Hands-on — From cave paintings to the smartphone — how messages learned to fly

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — From cave paintings to the smartphone — how messages learned to fly — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Cave walls to the computer, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
