# Lesson plan — Venezuela Founding Of Cities

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Venezuela Founding Of Cities
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Venezuela, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **With the founding of cities a different country is born** — Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world
2. **Cities are born, grow and can also die** — founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die
3. **Coro, a curious founding; El Tocuyo, mother city** — Coro is founded in a curious bargain with a cacique, and El Tocuyo becomes the mother city from which other towns are seeded
