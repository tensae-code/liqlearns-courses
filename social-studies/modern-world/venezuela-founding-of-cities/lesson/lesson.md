# Venezuela Founding Of Cities

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — With the founding of cities a different country is born

Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cities are born, grow and can also die

Founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Coro, a curious founding; El Tocuyo, mother city

Coro is founded in a curious bargain with a cacique, and El Tocuyo becomes the mother city from which other towns are seeded

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: With the founding of cities a different country is born — Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world. Cities are born, grow and can also die — founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die. Coro, a curious founding; El Tocuyo, mother city — Coro is founded in a curious bargain with a cacique, and El Tocuyo becomes the mother city from which other towns are seeded. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
