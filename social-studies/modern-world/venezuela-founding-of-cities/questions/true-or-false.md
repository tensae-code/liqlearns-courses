# Venezuela Founding Of Cities — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spanish-founded cities, Caracas and the capitals among them, plant a new kind of country over the indigenous world.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in With the founding of cities a different country is born

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 2do grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about With the founding of cities a different country is born

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Founded on a grid in the sixteenth century, cities grow with their people's needs — and a city, like a living thing, can also die.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Cities are born, grow and can also die

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 4to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Cities are born, grow and can also die

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Coro is founded in a curious bargain with a cacique, and El Tocuyo becomes the mother city from which other towns are seeded.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Coro, a curious founding; El Tocuyo, mother city

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela y de Nuestra América — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Coro, a curious founding; El Tocuyo, mother city
