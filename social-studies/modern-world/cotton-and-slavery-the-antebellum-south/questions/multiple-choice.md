# Cotton and slavery — the antebellum South, 1800–1860 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cotton gin made slavery —
a) Unprofitable  b) More profitable than ever  c) Illegal
**Answer: b) More profitable than ever** — the cotton gin makes slavery more profitable than ever.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** 'Cotton is King' points to —
a) A real king  b) Cotton's economic rule over the South  c) A British monarch
**Answer: b) Cotton's economic rule over the South** — the economics of cotton ruled the antebellum South.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Free African Americans in the South lived —
a) With full equality  b) Free in law but fenced by racism  c) As planters only
**Answer: b) Free in law but fenced by racism** — free in law but fenced by racism in a slave society.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Filibusters were hunting —
a) Gold in California  b) New slave states in the Caribbean  c) Whales in the Pacific
**Answer: b) New slave states in the Caribbean** — the filibusters hunting new slave states in the Caribbean.
