# Cotton and slavery — the antebellum South, 1800–1860 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cotton gin reduced the demand for enslaved labour.
**Answer: False** — It made slavery more profitable than ever, growing demand.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Planter culture defended slavery as a way of life.
**Answer: True** — Cotton built a planter culture that defended its system.
