# Lesson plan — Guatemala — annexation, Federation and Morazán

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Guatemala — annexation, Federation and Morazán
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Guatemala, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** annexed to Iturbide's Mexico in 1822, Central America declares absolute independence when he falls, abolishes slavery in 1824, and builds a Federation whose liberal reforms under Morazán and Gálvez break against cholera and Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion in 1838.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **From annexation to Federation — and its breaking** — annexed to Iturbide's Mexico in 1822, Central America declares absolute independence when he falls, loses Chiapas and Soconusco, abolishes slavery in 1824; Morazán and Gálvez push liberal reforms until cholera and Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion break the Federation in 1838

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the empire Central America was annexed to in 1822: Iturbide's Mexico.
2. Name the year slavery was abolished: 1824.
3. Name the rebellion that broke the Federation: Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion, 1838.
