# Guatemala — annexation, Federation and Morazán — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Central America was annexed in 1822 to —
a) Iturbide's Mexico  b) Spain again  c) the United States
**Answer: a) Iturbide's Mexico** — the annexation the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Slavery was abolished in the Federation in —
a) 1824  b) 1773  c) 1944
**Answer: a) 1824** — the abolition year.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Federation's liberal leaders named are —
a) Morazán and Gálvez  b) Barrios and Ubico  c) Arévalo and Arbenz
**Answer: a) Morazán and Gálvez** — its reformers.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Federation broke in 1838 amid cholera and —
a) Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion  b) a volcanic eruption  c) a British invasion
**Answer: a) Rafael Carrera's Montaña rebellion** — the breaking the book tells.
