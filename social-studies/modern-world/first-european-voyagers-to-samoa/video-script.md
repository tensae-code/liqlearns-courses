# Video script — The first European voyagers to Sāmoa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Roggeveen sights the Manu'a islands in 1722, Bougainville follows, La Pérouse's men are attacked at Asu, Tutuila, with twelve killed, and in 1791 the Pandora's crew answer rocks and spears with Sāmoa's first taste of gunfire.
