# Finland's soldiers — leave, loss and home-front worry — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — reading the letters through women's experience
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — most letter-writers are wives and mothers; their pens carry the home front's war

## Sub lesson 2 — introduction: who served, where, and the field post
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — hundreds of thousands serve but only a third fight at the front; age and trade decide a man's post, letters are the only link home, and ~95,000 Finns die

## Sub lesson 3 — Task 1, Oulu letter 1944
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a mother who has buried two sons begs that the third be let home for his brother's hero's funeral; leave is refused

## Sub lesson 4 — Task 2, Hämeenlinna letter Dec 1944
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — parents protest that the 1925-born still fight in the north after the armistice while their letters and parcels vanish — the Lapland War turn

## Sub lesson 5 — Task 3, Kotala letter 1942
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a wounded B2-class farmer idles in a labour company while his wife and three small children run the farm alone — release him where he is useful

## Sub lesson 6 — Task 4, Perniö letter Oct 1944
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a wife charges that shirkers and artillerymen were demobilised and rested first while front infantry, her husband among them, got nothing

## Sub lesson 7 — Task 5, Inkeroinen letter 1944 with Jahvetti's reply printed
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a soldier's granted funeral leave is cancelled; the office answers that only his commander can judge a total leave ban

## Sub lesson 8 — Task 6, Lappeenranta letter c.1941
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a 1918 veteran protests 6–7-month prison sentences for soldiers who return late from leave over broken transport
