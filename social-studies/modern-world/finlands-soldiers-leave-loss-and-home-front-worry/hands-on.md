# Hands-on — Finland's soldiers — leave, loss and home-front worry

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Finland's soldiers — leave, loss and home-front worry — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, reading the letters through women's experience, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
