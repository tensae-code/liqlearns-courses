## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **reading the letters through women's experience** — most letter-writers are wives and mothers; their pens carry the home front's war
2. **introduction: who served, where, and the field post** — hundreds of thousands serve but only a third fight at the front; age and trade decide a man's post, letters are the only link home, and ~95,000 Finns die
3. **Task 1, Oulu letter 1944** — a mother who has buried two sons begs that the third be let home for his brother's hero's funeral; leave is refused
4. **Task 2, Hämeenlinna letter Dec 1944** — parents protest that the 1925-born still fight in the north after the armistice while their letters and parcels vanish — the Lapland War turn
5. **Task 3, Kotala letter 1942** — a wounded B2-class farmer idles in a labour company while his wife and three small children run the farm alone — release him where he is useful
6. **Task 4, Perniö letter Oct 1944** — a wife charges that shirkers and artillerymen were demobilised and rested first while front infantry, her husband among them, got nothing
7. **Task 5, Inkeroinen letter 1944 with Jahvetti's reply printed** — a soldier's granted funeral leave is cancelled; the office answers that only his commander can judge a total leave ban
8. **Task 6, Lappeenranta letter c.1941** — a 1918 veteran protests 6–7-month prison sentences for soldiers who return late from leave over broken transport
