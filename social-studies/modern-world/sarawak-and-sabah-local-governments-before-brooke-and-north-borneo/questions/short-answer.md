# Sarawak and Sabah — local governments before Brooke and North Borneo — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Sarawak and Sabah.
**Answer: Brunei's sultanate rises and draws Sarawak's trade to itself while the Sulu Sultanate (15th century) lifts north Sabah's trade in sea and forest produce — bird's nests for China; government runs through the wakil raja system inherited from Majapahit and Sambas and used by Brunei, and in Sabah through free chiefs (ketua bebas) like Syarif Osman at Marudu and Datu Kurunding at Tungku; the bumiputera peoples — Orang Ulu (Kayan, Kenyah, Kelabit, Penan, Punan), Bidayuh, Melanau, Kadazandusun, Bajau, Suluk and the Murut, "hill people" in Brunei's naming — order their lives by the rivers (any close wording).**
