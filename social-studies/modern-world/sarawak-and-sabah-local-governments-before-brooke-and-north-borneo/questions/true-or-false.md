# Sarawak and Sabah — local governments before Brooke and North Borneo — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Brunei's sultanate rises and draws Sarawak's trade to itself while the Sulu Sultanate (15th century) lifts north Sabah's trade in sea and forest produce — bird's nests for China; government runs through the wakil raja system inherited from Majapahit and Sambas and used by Brunei, and in Sabah through free chiefs (ketua bebas) like Syarif Osman at Marudu and Datu Kurunding at Tungku; the bumiputera peoples — Orang Ulu (Kayan, Kenyah, Kelabit, Penan, Punan), Bidayuh, Melanau, Kadazandusun, Bajau, Suluk and the Murut, "hill people" in Brunei's naming — order their lives by the rivers
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 10: Sarawak and Sabah).
