# The League Of Nations The First World Parliament — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first attempt at a world parliament keeps small peace but cannot stop Japan, Italy or Germany.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §6 The League of Nations

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §6 The League of Nations

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The world's first league for peace meets in Geneva without the United States in it, settles small disputes and fails before the big aggressors.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 2, Lessons 19–20: the League of Nations

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 2, Lessons 19–20: the League of Nations
