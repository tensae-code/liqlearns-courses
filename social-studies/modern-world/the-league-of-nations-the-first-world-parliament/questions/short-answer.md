# The League Of Nations The First World Parliament — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §6 The League of Nations.
**Answer: the first attempt at a world parliament keeps small peace but cannot stop Japan, Italy or Germany (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), §6 The League of Nations, printed p. 45.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 2, Lessons 19–20: the League of Nations.
**Answer: the world's first league for peace meets in Geneva without the United States in it, settles small disputes and fails before the big aggressors (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), Theme 2, Lessons 19–20: the League of Nations, printed p. 94.**
