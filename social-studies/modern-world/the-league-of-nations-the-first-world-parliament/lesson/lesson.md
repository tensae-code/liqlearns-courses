# The League Of Nations The First World Parliament

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the first attempt at a world parliament keeps small peace but cannot stop Japan, Italy or Germany As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — §6 The League of Nations

the first attempt at a world parliament keeps small peace but cannot stop Japan, Italy or Germany

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Theme 2, Lessons 19–20: the League of Nations

the world's first league for peace meets in Geneva without the United States in it, settles small disputes and fails before the big aggressors

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
