## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The constitution bends with the federation** — the Federal Constitution, grown from the Malay states' own laws, is amended in 1963 to admit Singapore, Sabah and Sarawak — and amended again in 1965 when Singapore leaves (Bab 2.4: the constitution bends with the federation)
2. **Forming Malaysia** — Tunku Abdul Rahman announces the Malaysia idea on 27 May 1961; Singapore votes to join in its referendum of early September 1962, the Inter-Governmental Committee (August 1962) writes the safeguards for Sarawak and Sabah into the constitutional frame, Indonesia protests at the United Nations and U Thant's UN Malaysia Mission under Laurence Michelmore (12 August 1963) verifies the Borneo peoples' wishes; the July 1963 Agreement is signed and Malaysia is proclaimed — then Indonesia's Konfrontasi is fought off diplomatically, the counter-campaign run by the Foreign Ministry under Muhammad Ghazali Shafie (Bab 5: forming Malaysia)
