# The formation of Malaysia, 1963 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Federal Constitution, grown from the Malay states' own laws, is amended in 1963 to admit Singapore, Sabah and Sarawak — and amended again in 1965 when Singapore leaves
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 2.4: the constitution bends with the federation).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tunku Abdul Rahman announces the Malaysia idea on 27 May 1961; Singapore votes to join in its referendum of early September 1962, the Inter-Governmental Committee (August 1962) writes the safeguards for Sarawak and Sabah into the constitutional frame, Indonesia protests at the United Nations and U Thant's UN Malaysia Mission under Laurence Michelmore (12 August 1963) verifies the Borneo peoples' wishes; the July 1963 Agreement is signed and Malaysia is proclaimed — then Indonesia's Konfrontasi is fought off diplomatically, the counter-campaign run by the Foreign Ministry under Muhammad Ghazali Shafie
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 5: forming Malaysia).
