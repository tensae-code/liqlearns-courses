# The formation of Malaysia, 1963 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The constitution bends with the federation  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The constitution bends with the federation** — the Federal Constitution, grown from the Malay states' own laws, is amended in 1963 to admit Singapore, Sabah and Sarawak — and amended again in 1965 when Singapore leaves

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 2.4: the constitution bends with the federation  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 2.4: the constitution bends with the federation** — the book's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Forming Malaysia  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Forming Malaysia** — Tunku Abdul Rahman announces the Malaysia idea on 27 May 1961; Singapore votes to join in its referendum of early September 1962, the Inter-Governmental Committee (August 1962) writes the safeguards for Sarawak and Sabah into the constitutional frame, Indonesia protests at the United Nations and U Thant's UN Malaysia Mission under Laurence Michelmore (12 August 1963) verifies the Borneo peoples' wishes; the July 1963 Agreement is signed and Malaysia is proclaimed — then Indonesia's Konfrontasi is fought off diplomatically, the counter-campaign run by the Foreign Ministry under Muhammad Ghazali Shafie

**Answer: a) Bab 5: forming Malaysia** — the book's own heading for this part.
