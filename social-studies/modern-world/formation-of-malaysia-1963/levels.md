## Sub lesson 2 — Forming Malaysia
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tunku Abdul Rahman announces the Malaysia idea on 27 May 1961; Singapore votes to join in its referendum of early September 1962, the Inter-Governmental Committee (August 1962) writes the safeguards for Sarawak and Sabah into the constitutional frame, Indonesia protests at the United Nations and U Thant's UN Malaysia Mission under Laurence Michelmore (12 August 1963) verifies the Borneo peoples' wishes; the July 1963 Agreement is signed and Malaysia is proclaimed — then Indonesia's Konfrontasi is fought off diplomatically, the counter-campaign run by the Foreign Ministry under Muhammad Ghazali Shafie
