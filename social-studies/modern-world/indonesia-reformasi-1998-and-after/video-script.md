# Video script — Indonesia — Reformasi, 1998 and after

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Reformasi opens politics and expression, reforms the bureaucracy and state enterprises, widens education and builds infrastructure — an era pupils study partly through oral-history interviews with their own parents.
