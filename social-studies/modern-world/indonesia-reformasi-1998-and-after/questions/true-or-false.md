# Indonesia — Reformasi, 1998 and after — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reformasi opens politics and expression, reforms the bureaucracy and state enterprises, widens education and builds infrastructure, and is studied partly through pupils' own oral-history interviews with parents who lived it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 4, Indonesia pada Masa Reformasi (Gambaran Umum)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Buku Panduan Guru Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII* is about Indonesia — the Battle of Surabaya, 1945.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 4, Indonesia pada Masa Reformasi (Gambaran Umum)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book first asks what separates revolution from reform, then replays the seconds before B.J. Habibie is sworn in as president and the open democratic era begins.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 4.A Reformasi: Awal Demokrasi Politik yang Terbuka

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII (Kurikulum Merdeka)* is about Indonesia — the Battle of Surabaya, 1945.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 4.A Reformasi: Awal Demokrasi Politik yang Terbuka
