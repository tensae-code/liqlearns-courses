# Hands-on — Montenegro, 1851–1878 — principality and independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Montenegro, 1851–1878 — principality and independence — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch.4.1 Montenegro 1851–1876: Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
