# Montenegro, 1851–1878 — principality and independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A secular prince replaces the prince-bishops, and a battlefield victory makes the powers draw Montenegro's borders.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.4.1 Montenegro 1851–1876: Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ИСТОРИЈА за осми разред основне школе (Istorija 8)* is about Montenegro becomes a constitutional monarchy and kingdom.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.4.1 Montenegro 1851–1876: Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A murder in a border town lights the war that finally makes Montenegro an internationally recognised state.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.4.2 Montenegro 1876–1905
