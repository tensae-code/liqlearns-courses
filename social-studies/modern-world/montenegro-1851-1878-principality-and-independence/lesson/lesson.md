# Montenegro, 1851–1878 — principality and independence

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A secular prince replaces the prince-bishops, and a battlefield victory makes the powers draw Montenegro's borders As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch.4.1 Montenegro 1851–1876: Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire

A secular prince replaces the prince-bishops, and a battlefield victory makes the powers draw Montenegro's borders.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch.4.2 Montenegro 1876–1905

A murder in a border town lights the war that finally makes Montenegro an internationally recognised state.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
