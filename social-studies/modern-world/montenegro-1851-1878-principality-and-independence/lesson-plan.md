**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Ch.4.1 Montenegro 1851–1876: Danilo, named heir by Petar II, takes power, proclaims Montenegro a principality, builds state unity, reforms administration and army, issues a law code, and wins at Grahovac, gaining territory and an international delimitation with the Ottoman Empire** — a secular prince replaces the prince-bishops, and a battlefield victory makes the powers draw Montenegro's borders
2. **Ch.4.2 Montenegro 1876–1905** — a murder in a border town lights the war that finally makes Montenegro an internationally recognised state
