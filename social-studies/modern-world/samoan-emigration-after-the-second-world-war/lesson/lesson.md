# Sāmoan emigration after the Second World War

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Pacific war brings thousands of American troops and a cash boom to Sāmoa; when the military leaves, the taste for wages stays — in 1952 almost a thousand American Sāmoans apply for navy transport to Hawaii, over 6,000 reach the USA in the 1950s, and New Zealand's post-war labour hunger, its 1955 permit system and independence in 1962 open the second great flow. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
