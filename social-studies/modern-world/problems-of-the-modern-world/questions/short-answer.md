# Problems Of The Modern World — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §20 The problems of the modern world.
**Answer: climate, poverty, terror and pandemics are set out as problems no state can solve alone (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), §20 The problems of the modern world, printed p. 154.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 4, Lessons 60–61: the problems of the modern world.
**Answer: terrorism, trafficking, cybercrime and ecological danger cross every border, and no state can answer them alone (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), Theme 4, Lessons 60–61: the problems of the modern world, printed p. 96.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 4 §18: environmental, war and other problems.
**Answer: the greenhouse effect, the Green movement, the Balkan wars, Iraq, the Arab-Israeli conflict and a demographic map being redrawn — the problems theme closes in the pupils' own present (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 12 (World History 12), by Vahram Petrosyan (Themes 1–3), Aram Nazaryan (Introduction, Themes 4, 6, Theme 5 §§19–20), Aram Kosyan (Theme 5 §§21–24); methodical part by Armine Sargsyan* (Armenia), Theme 4 §18: environmental, war and other problems, printed p. 93.**
