# Video script — Reconstruction, 1865–1877

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The war destroyed slavery. Reconstruction tried to build freedom's meaning — amendments, Black officeholders, a remade South — and then, in 1877, the trying stopped.

Lincoln's lenient restoration would have brought the South back quickly and softly. Congress answered with its own remaking of the South — Radical Reconstruction, imposed on the defeated states with federal power behind it.

Reconstruction wrote freedom into the Constitution: its amendments abolished slavery, made citizens, and promised the vote. Black officeholders sat in legislatures and in Congress — a revolution in who could rule, built in a decade.

Violence against the freedpeople, and Northern exhaustion with the struggle, wore Reconstruction down. The compromise of 1877 collapsed it: federal protection withdrew, and the freedpeople were left to Jim Crow — the system of segregation and exclusion that followed.
