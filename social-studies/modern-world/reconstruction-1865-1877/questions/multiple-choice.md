# Reconstruction, 1865–1877 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Lincoln's plan for restoration was —
a) Harsh punishment  b) Lenient  c) To keep slavery
**Answer: b) Lenient** — Lincoln's lenient restoration.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Radical Reconstruction was driven by —
a) Congress  b) The planters  c) Britain
**Answer: a) Congress** — Congress's remaking of the South.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Reconstruction's amendments —
a) Restored slavery  b) Abolished slavery and promised citizenship and the vote  c) Banned voting
**Answer: b) Abolished slavery and promised citizenship and the vote** — Radical Reconstruction's amendments.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Reconstruction collapsed with the compromise of —
a) 1850  b) 1877  c) 1820
**Answer: b) 1877** — the compromise of 1877 collapses it.
