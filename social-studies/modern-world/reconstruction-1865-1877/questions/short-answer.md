# Reconstruction, 1865–1877 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Reconstruction build, and what replaced it?
**Answer: It built constitutional freedom — amendments and Black officeholding; after its collapse in 1877, Jim Crow followed (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two forces that wore Reconstruction down.
**Answer: Violence against the freedpeople and Northern exhaustion (any close wording).**
