# Reconstruction, 1865–1877 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Black officeholders held office during Reconstruction.
**Answer: True** — Amendments and Black officeholders mark the era.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After 1877 the freedpeople were fully protected by federal power.
**Answer: False** — Protection withdrew and the freedpeople were left to Jim Crow.
