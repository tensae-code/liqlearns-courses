# The Paris Commune

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: defeated Paris refuses Thiers' Versailles government, elects the Commune Council (26 March 1871) and runs a workers' city-state — free. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. II §4 The state coup in France and its consequences (the Paris Commune)

defeated Paris refuses Thiers' Versailles government, elects the Commune Council (26 March 1871) and runs a workers' city-state — free schools, abandoned factories handed to their workers — before Versailles' army retakes the city street by street

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
