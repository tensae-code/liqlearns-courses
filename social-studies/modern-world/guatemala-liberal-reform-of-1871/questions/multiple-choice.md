# Guatemala — the Liberal Reform of 1871 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Liberal Reform won on —
a) 30 June 1871  b) 15 September 1821  c) 20 October 1944
**Answer: a) 30 June 1871** — the reform date.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Barrios ordered schooling that was —
a) free and secular  b) church-only and paid  c) for boys only
**Answer: a) free and secular** — his school reform.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Barrios died at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to —
a) reunite Central America by force  b) sign the peace accords  c) build the Panama Canal
**Answer: a) reunite Central America by force** — his last campaign.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** With García Granados, Barrios invaded from —
a) Mexico  b) Belize  c) Spain
**Answer: a) Mexico** — the invasion route.
