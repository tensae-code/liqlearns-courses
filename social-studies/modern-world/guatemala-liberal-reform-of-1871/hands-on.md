# Hands-on — Guatemala — the Liberal Reform of 1871

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Part 1 — 30 June 1871 — Barrios remakes Guatemala

Draw or map this part from memory, then write its story beat in your own words: García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invade from Mexico and win on 30 June 1871; Barrios strips the Church, orders free secular schooling, brings railway, telegraph and coffee — and dies at Chalchuapa in 1885 trying to reunite Central America by force.
