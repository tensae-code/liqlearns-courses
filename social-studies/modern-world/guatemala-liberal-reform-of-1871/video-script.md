# Video script — Guatemala — the Liberal Reform of 1871

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Guatemala book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Guatemala's Grade 4 book tells the Liberal Reform as one man's remake. García Granados and Justo Rufino Barrios invade from Mexico and win on 30 June 1871. Barrios strips the Church, orders free secular schooling, and brings railway, telegraph and coffee — a new economy and a new state. He dies at Chalchuapa in 1885, still trying to reunite Central America by force: reformer and would-be unifier in one.
