# Sri Lanka — Independence governments, 1948 to 1956

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: D. S. Senanayake leads the UNP into independence, Dudley follows, the 1953 hartal topples him, and in 1956 Bandaranaike's MEP, carried by the five great forces, wins and makes Sinhala the official language. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
