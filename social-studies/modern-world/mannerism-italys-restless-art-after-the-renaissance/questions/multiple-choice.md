# Mannerism — Italy's restless art after the Renaissance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Elegant unease over balance  b) After the giants  c) An invaded Italy
**Answer: b) After the giants** — The generation after the giants feels there is nothing left to perfect.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Elegant unease over balance  b) After the giants  c) An invaded Italy
**Answer: a) Elegant unease over balance** — Artists stretch bodies, twist poses and choose elegant unease over balance.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) After the giants  b) Elegant unease over balance  c) An invaded Italy
**Answer: c) An invaded Italy** — Italy itself is invaded and Rome is sacked — the unease in the art matches the age.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The generation after the Renaissance giants feels there is nothing left to perfect — so it stretches bodies, twists poses and chooses elegant unease over balance, while Italy itself is invaded and Rome is sacked  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
