# Papua New Guinea — nation builders: the independence leaders — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Grand Chief Michael Somare, Sir Maori Kiki, Vincent Eri and Sir John Guise — what each man did in the events of self-government and Independence, and what it built for the nation

## Sub lesson 2 — Sir Tei Abal
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sir Michael Somare, Dame Josephine Abaijah, and Sir Tei Abal — the Enga medical assistant with no formal schooling who reached Parliament in 1964, led the Opposition at Independence in 1975, and said moka talks were "much the same as meetings of Parliament" — each leader's contribution to independence is profiled and their home province mapped
