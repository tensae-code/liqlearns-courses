## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Important persons or people – nation** — Grand Chief Michael Somare, Sir Maori Kiki, Vincent Eri and Sir John Guise — what each man did in the events of self-government and Independence, and what it built for the nation
2. **Sir Tei Abal** — Sir Michael Somare, Dame Josephine Abaijah, and Sir Tei Abal — the Enga medical assistant with no formal schooling who reached Parliament in 1964, led the Opposition at Independence in 1975, and said moka talks were "much the same as meetings of Parliament" — each leader's contribution to independence is profiled and their home province mapped
3. **Agents of change in PNG** — the named changemakers — Somare, Julius Chan, Maori Kiki, Tei Abal, John Momis, WWII hero Yauwiga of Sepik, Kondom Agaundo, the Bully Beef Club and the Pangu Party — many already half-forgotten, their legacies still shaping the country
4. **Prominent Papua New Guineans** — a 15-year-old Gulf Province schoolgirl, Susan Karike, beats the Australian government's proposed flag in the 1971 national competition — her bird-of-paradise flag rises over Independence Hill on 16 September 1975 beside Somare and first Governor-General Sir John Guise
