## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Lesson 29: The Bonn Conference — after 2001 the Afghan factions meet at Bonn and agree a road back to an elected government under international protection; Lesson 30: The Interim Administration — Karzai's interim administration reopens schools to girls and boys and restores women's right to work and study; Lesson 31: The Emergency Loya Jirga — the grand assembly gathers, hands power to a transitional Islamic state and confirms Karzai at its head; Lesson 32: The Islamic Transitional Government of Afghanistan — the transitional government is charged with a constitution, free elections and new currency; Lesson 33: The beginning of the country's reconstruction — with the world beside it, Afghanistan rebuilds and paves thousands of kilometres of roads and highways; Lesson 34: The presidential elections — Afghan men and women vote in calm and freedom at ballot boxes to choose their president. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
