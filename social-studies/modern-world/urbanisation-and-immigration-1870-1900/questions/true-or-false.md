# Urbanisation and immigration, 1870–1900 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Urban growth brought no problems worth reforming.
**Answer: False** — Housing, water, disease and corruption outran government; reformers fought the chaos.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Writers and thinkers reflected the changed, urban America.
**Answer: True** — A new generation of writers and thinkers reflects a changed America.
