# Urbanisation and immigration, 1870–1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The cities' core problem was that they —
a) Shrank too fast  b) Swelled faster than they could govern themselves  c) Had no people
**Answer: b) Swelled faster than they could govern themselves** — cities swell faster than they can govern themselves.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Great Migration in this lesson is —
a) Birds flying south  b) African Americans moving out of the South  c) A cattle drive
**Answer: b) African Americans moving out of the South** — the African American Great Migration remakes the cities.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The second great movement remaking the cities was —
a) New European immigration  b) Gold rushes  c) Canal building
**Answer: a) New European immigration** — new European immigration.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Reformers responded to the chaos by —
a) Leaving the cities  b) Fighting it — cleaning, housing, governing  c) Banning cities
**Answer: b) Fighting it — cleaning, housing, governing** — reformers fight the chaos.
