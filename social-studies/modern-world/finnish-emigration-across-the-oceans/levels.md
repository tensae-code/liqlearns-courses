# Finnish emigration across the oceans — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — introduction: Finns chase the American dream
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — fast-industrialising America pulls migrants in crowds around 1900; Finns cross the Atlantic after the same dream, later to the whole world

## Sub lesson 2 — Task 1, Mimmi Törnroos's studio portrait
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a young woman sits for a formal studio photograph before departure; pupils read her hopes out of the pose

## Sub lesson 3 — Task 2, emigrant workers in photographs
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — photo sets show the jobs Finns actually landed in, and pupils ask why those jobs, and whether being a man or a woman decided them

## Sub lesson 4 — Task 3, Finnish miners at Red Lodge
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a crew poses underground in work clothes; pupils judge the clothes against mine work and ask why the photograph was taken at all

## Sub lesson 5 — introduction and task choice
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the same Atlantic crossing is offered twice, as a solo diary/letter/story or as a pair dialogue — the pupil must live the departure, not summarise it

## Sub lesson 6 — individual task: write from one photograph
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a ship lands Finnish Americans, the Valjakka family poses before leaving, a house rises in Sointula, emigrants crowd the Astrea, settlers gather in Argentina's Colonia Finlandesa — one pictured life becomes the pupil's own entry

## Sub lesson 7 — pair/group task: a dialogue performed to the class
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — two pupils voice the people inside one photograph, arguing out the leaving and the hopes before an audience
