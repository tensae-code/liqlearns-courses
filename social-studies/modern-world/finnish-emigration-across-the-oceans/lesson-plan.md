# Lesson plan — Finnish emigration across the oceans

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Finnish emigration across the oceans
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Finland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Fast-industrialising America pulls migrants in crowds around 1900; Finns cross the Atlantic after the same dream, later to the whole world
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **introduction: Finns chase the American dream** — fast-industrialising America pulls migrants in crowds around 1900; Finns cross the Atlantic after the same dream, later to the whole world
2. **Task 1, Mimmi Törnroos's studio portrait** — a young woman sits for a formal studio photograph before departure; pupils read her hopes out of the pose
3. **Task 2, emigrant workers in photographs** — photo sets show the jobs Finns actually landed in, and pupils ask why those jobs, and whether being a man or a woman decided them
4. **Task 3, Finnish miners at Red Lodge** — a crew poses underground in work clothes; pupils judge the clothes against mine work and ask why the photograph was taken at all
5. **introduction and task choice** — the same Atlantic crossing is offered twice, as a solo diary/letter/story or as a pair dialogue — the pupil must live the departure, not summarise it
6. **individual task: write from one photograph** — a ship lands Finnish Americans, the Valjakka family poses before leaving, a house rises in Sointula, emigrants crowd the Astrea, settlers gather in Argentina's Colonia Finlandesa — one pictured life becomes the pupil's own entry
7. **pair/group task: a dialogue performed to the class** — two pupils voice the people inside one photograph, arguing out the leaving and the hopes before an audience

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
