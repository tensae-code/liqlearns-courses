# Finnish emigration across the oceans

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Fast-industrialising America pulls migrants in crowds around 1900; Finns cross the Atlantic after the same dream, later to the whole world As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — introduction: Finns chase the American dream

Fast-industrialising America pulls migrants in crowds around 1900; Finns cross the Atlantic after the same dream, later to the whole world.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Task 2, emigrant workers in photographs

Photo sets show the jobs Finns actually landed in, and pupils ask why those jobs, and whether being a man or a woman decided them.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

[Picture 63282 — the jobs they landed]

## Sub lesson 4 — Task 3, Finnish miners at Red Lodge

A crew poses underground in work clothes; pupils judge the clothes against mine work and ask why the photograph was taken at all.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

[Picture 63283 — miners at red lodge]

## Sub lesson 5 — introduction and task choice

The same Atlantic crossing is offered twice, as a solo diary/letter/story or as a pair dialogue — the pupil must live the departure, not summarise it.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

[Picture 63284 — live the departure]

## Sub lesson 6 — individual task: write from one photograph

A ship lands Finnish Americans, the Valjakka family poses before leaving, a house rises in Sointula, emigrants crowd the Astrea, settlers gather in Argentina's Colonia Finlandesa — one pictured life becomes the pupil's own entry.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

[Picture 63285 — one pictured life]

## Sub lesson 7 — pair/group task: a dialogue performed to the class

Two pupils voice the people inside one photograph, arguing out the leaving and the hopes before an audience.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

[Picture 63286 — voicing the photograph]

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: introduction: Finns chase the American dream; Task 1, Mimmi Törnroos's studio portrait; Task 2, emigrant workers in photographs; Task 3, Finnish miners at Red Lodge; introduction and task choice; individual task: write from one photograph; pair/group task: a dialogue performed to the class. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
