# Video script — Mongolian life and culture, 15th–18th centuries

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mongolia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

herding life goes on under the khans; Erdene Zuu rises on Karakorum's ruins, Zanabazar casts his Buddhas, and in 1662 Sagan Setsen writes the Erdeniin Tovch chronicle. taxes, corvée and debt to Chinese firms grind the herders while the banner princes and monasteries hold the land and herds. chronicles, medicine, printing and temple art keep a national culture alive under foreign rule. Buddhism's second coming, Altan Khan and the Dalai Lama title, Erdene Zuu's founding — faith remakes Mongol culture before the Qing remakes its politics. khoshuu banners freeze herders to their lords' land; alba taxes, Chinese merchant debt and great monasteries reshape life, while learning, law codes and chronicles quietly flourish.
