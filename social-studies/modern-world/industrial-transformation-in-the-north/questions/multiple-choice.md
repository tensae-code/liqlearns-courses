# Industrial transformation in the North, 1800–1850 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The mills first rose in —
a) The South  b) The Northeast  c) The far West
**Answer: b) The Northeast** — mills rise in the Northeast.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The economy that followed the mills is described as —
a) A barter economy  b) A capitalist republic of banks and markets  c) A royal monopoly
**Answer: b) A capitalist republic of banks and markets** — a vibrant capitalist republic of banks and markets follows.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Canals and railroads brought a —
a) Transportation revolution  b) Return to walking  c) End of trade
**Answer: a) Transportation revolution** — a transportation revolution stitching it together.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Wage labour sorted the North into —
a) One equal class  b) New classes  c) No classes at all
**Answer: b) New classes** — wage labour sorts the North into new classes.
