# Industrial transformation in the North, 1800–1850 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Factory work left the way people worked completely unchanged.
**Answer: False** — Wage labour and the mills changed work and sorted society into new classes.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Canals and railroads were part of a transportation revolution.
**Answer: True** — Canals and railroads stitch it together in a transportation revolution.
