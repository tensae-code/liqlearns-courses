# Industrial transformation in the North, 1800–1850 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name three changes that made up the North's industrial transformation.
**Answer: Mills in the Northeast; a capitalist republic of banks and markets; canals and railroads — with wage labour sorting society into new classes (any three, close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is wage labour, and why did it matter?
**Answer: Working for wages — selling one's time to an employer; it sorted the North into new classes (any close wording).**
