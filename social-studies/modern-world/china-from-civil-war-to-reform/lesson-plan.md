# Lesson plan — China From Civil War To Reform

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** China From Civil War To Reform
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Kuomintang loses the civil war (1946–49), flees to Taiwan, and Mao proclaims the People's Republic on 1 October 1949; land reform and recovery (1950–52) precede the first Five-Year Plan (1953–57) with Soviet help — the book pictures the 1956 "Liberation" truck — then the "Three Red Banners" of 1958, the Great Leap's steel campaign and People's Communes bring decline and famine, and the Cultural Revolution convulses 1966–76; border clashes with India (1962) and the USSR (1969) give way to détente with America, and the reform line of December 1978 sets the economy at the centre — GDP then grows over 9.1% a year (1980–91), the world's highest, with special economic zones at Shenzhen and Zhuhai
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 12, China: civil war, campaigns and reform** — the Kuomintang loses the civil war (1946–49), flees to Taiwan, and Mao proclaims the People's Republic on 1 October 1949; land reform and recovery (1950–52) precede the first Five-Year Plan (1953–57) with Soviet help — the book pictures the 1956 "Liberation" truck — then the "Three Red Banners" of 1958, the Great Leap's steel campaign and People's Communes bring decline and famine, and the Cultural Revolution convulses 1966–76; border clashes with India (1962) and the USSR (1969) give way to détente with America, and the reform line of December 1978 sets the economy at the centre — GDP then grows over 9.1% a year (1980–91), the world's highest, with special economic zones at Shenzhen and Zhuhai
2. **Bài 4 China takes the socialist road: after the national democratic revolution, the People's Republic is proclaimed in 1949, with North Vietnam following in 1954 and Laos in December 1975** — Mao's proclamation in 1949 moves a quarter of humanity into the socialist camp
3. **Bài 4 China's reform and opening: from December 1978 the socialist market economy lifts GDP from 367.9 billion yuan to over 114 trillion by 2021 — about 9.5% a year — turns a Guangdong fishing village into Shenzhen, and launches Shenzhou craft and the BeiDou satellites** — in forty years a fishing village becomes a top Asian tech city, and the pupil is asked what the miracle proves
