# The Mau and Black Saturday

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Talune brings Spanish influenza on 7 November 1918 and a fifth to a quarter of Western Samoa dies; grievances over interference with the fa'a Samoa, taxes and exclusion from government form the Mau in 1926; on Black Saturday, 28 December 1929, police fire on an unarmed Apia march, killing eleven including Tupua Tamasese, whose dying words plead for peace; Samoan delegations then press the UN until independence. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
