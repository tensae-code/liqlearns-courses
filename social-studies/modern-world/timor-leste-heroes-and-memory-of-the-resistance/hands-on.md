# Hands-on — Timor-Leste — heroes and memory of the resistance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A name card
Choose one of the four names from this lesson and make a name card: the name large, and one sentence on who they were in the resistance story. Keep it as summary, in your own words.

## Two kinds of memory
Draw two boxes: 'heroes' and 'truth'. In the first, write two heroes' names; in the second, write 'CAVR — CHEGA!'. Say out loud why a country needs both kinds of memory.
