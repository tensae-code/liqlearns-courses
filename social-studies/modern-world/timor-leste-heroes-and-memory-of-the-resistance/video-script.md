# Video script — Timor-Leste — heroes and memory of the resistance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Timor-Leste's story as its Grade 6 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The annex gives children names to inherit. Its biographies tell of Rosa 'Muki' Bonaparte and Maria Goretti Joaquim — women of the resistance's first generation — and of Taur Matan Ruak and Rui Maria de Araújo, who carried the struggle into the restored republic's leadership. A young republic writes down who its heroes were, so the next generation inherits names, not silence. These lives are filed here as summaries only, never transcribed.

Memory is also organised, not only honoured. The annex records the Soibada plenary assembly of 1976 and the prolonged people's war, with chronologies for 1976–78 and 1983. It records the world's recognition: the 1996 Nobel Peace Prize to Bishop Belo and José Ramos-Horta. And it records the reckoning: the CAVR, the truth and reconciliation commission whose report is known as CHEGA! — the nation writing down what was done to it, as evidence for its children.
