# Lesson plan — Timor-Leste — heroes and memory of the resistance

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Timor-Leste — heroes and memory of the resistance
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Timor-Leste, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The annex reference texts from the Grade 6 Social Science student book, as filed in the teacher guide: biographies of Rosa 'Muki' Bonaparte, Maria Goretti Joaquim, Taur Matan Ruak and Rui Maria de Araújo; the Soibada plenary assembly of 1976 and the prolonged people's war; chronologies 1976–78 and 1983; the 1996 Nobel Peace Prize to Bishop Belo and José Ramos-Horta; and the CAVR truth and reconciliation commission (CHEGA!) — a young republic writes down who its heroes were and what was done to it, so the next generation inherits names, not silence.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on Timor-Leste's Grade 6 national story, taught as lived memory — songs, drama, photographs, poems and interviews — as the Period 2 teacher guide teaches 1975–2002. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Names, not silence — the heroes' biographies** — Biographies of Rosa 'Muki' Bonaparte, Maria Goretti Joaquim, Taur Matan Ruak and Rui Maria de Araújo, from the student book's annex texts.
2. **Writing down what was done — Soibada, the Nobel Prize and CHEGA!** — The Soibada plenary assembly of 1976 and the prolonged people's war; chronologies 1976–78 and 1983; the 1996 Nobel Peace Prize to Bishop Belo and José Ramos-Horta; and the CAVR truth and reconciliation commission (CHEGA!).

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
