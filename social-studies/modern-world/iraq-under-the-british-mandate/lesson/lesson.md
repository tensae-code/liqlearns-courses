# Iraq under the British mandate

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Britain occupies Iraq for its wealth and oil, the Revolution of 1920 answers, and the 1930 treaty recognises independence while keeping two air bases. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Iraq under the British mandate

Britain, eyeing Iraq's wealth and oil, occupied it from al-Faw in 1914 and took the mandate at San Remo; its plunder and forced labour raised the Revolution of 1920, so Britain set Faisal on the throne in 1921, kept Mosul in Iraq by a League decision tied to a 25-year mandate, and by the 1930 treaty recognised independence while keeping two air bases for 25 years.
