## Sub lesson 1 — Iraq under the British mandate
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Britain, eyeing Iraq's wealth and oil, occupied it from al-Faw in 1914 and took the mandate at San Remo; its plunder and forced labour raised the Revolution of 1920, so Britain set Faisal on the throne in 1921, kept Mosul in Iraq by a League decision tied to a 25-year mandate, and by the 1930 treaty recognised independence while keeping two air bases for 25 years.
