# Lesson plan — Iraq under the British mandate

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Iraq under the British mandate
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Syria, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain occupies Iraq for its wealth and oil, the Revolution of 1920 answers, and the 1930 treaty recognises independence while keeping two air bases.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Iraq under the British mandate** — Britain, eyeing Iraq's wealth and oil, occupied it from al-Faw in 1914 and took the mandate at San Remo; its plunder and forced labour raised the Revolution of 1920, so Britain set Faisal on the throne in 1921, kept Mosul in Iraq by a League decision tied to a 25-year mandate, and by the 1930 treaty recognised independence while keeping two air bases for 25 years.
