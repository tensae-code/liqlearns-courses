# Video script — Papua New Guinea — colonisation: powers and impacts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Outside powers take the island and draw governing boundaries between them; children name and map the colonial powers, plot colonisation's events on a timeline, and judge what colonisation did to PNG and its people.

Portuguese de Menezes names Papua ("frizzy hair") in 1526 and de Retes names New Guinea in 1545, but the real taking comes in 1884 — Germany seizes the north-east as Kaiser-Wilhelmsland through the German New Guinea Company while Britain proclaims its protectorate over the south, MacGregor governing "by patrol".

Gold-rush wealth in Australia seeks outlets and the colonists become imperialists themselves — economic, political and strategic motives, in PNG as in Asia and Africa, with little regard for the colonised.
