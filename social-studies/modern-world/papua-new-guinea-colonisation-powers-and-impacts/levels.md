## Sub lesson 2 — German and British colonisation of Papua and New Guinea
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Portuguese de Menezes names Papua ("frizzy hair") in 1526 and de Retes names New Guinea in 1545, but the real taking comes in 1884 — Germany seizes the north-east as Kaiser-Wilhelmsland through the German New Guinea Company while Britain proclaims its protectorate over the south, MacGregor governing "by patrol"

## Sub lesson 3 — Motives for colonisation of PNG and the Pacific
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — gold-rush wealth in Australia seeks outlets and the colonists become imperialists themselves — economic, political and strategic motives, in PNG as in Asia and Africa, with little regard for the colonised
