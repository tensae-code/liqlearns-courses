# Hands-on — Spanish American Independence Second Stage Ayacucho

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Spanish American Independence Second Stage Ayacucho — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 3.5 Second stage: Mexico's independence; Bolívar the Liberator's battles; San Martín and the southern campaign; Guayaquil meeting; Junín and Ayacucho, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
