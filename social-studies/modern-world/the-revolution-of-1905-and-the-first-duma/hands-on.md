# Hands-on — The Revolution of 1905 and the First Duma

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Revolution of 1905 and the First Duma — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Ch. IV §21 The formation of the State Duma in Russia, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
