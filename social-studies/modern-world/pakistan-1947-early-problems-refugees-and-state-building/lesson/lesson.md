# Pakistan 1947 — early problems, refugees and state-building

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A state born in partition must house, feed and govern millions before it has offices, money or staff — and it takes three constitutions and twenty-six years to settle the rules its founders argued over, on the founding frame of the 1949 Objectives Resolution. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
