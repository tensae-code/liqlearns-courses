# Pakistan 1947 — early problems, refugees and state-building — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A state born in partition must house, feed and govern millions before it has offices, money or staff.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.5 Pakistan a New Country: unfair boundary awards (Ferozepur), millions of refugees arrive, canal water is cut and administration starts from almost nothing in Karachi

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about A state born in partition

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before any constitution, the founding assembly agrees sovereignty belongs to Allah and power is a trust from the people.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.5: the Objectives Resolution (12 March 1949), moved by Liaquat Ali Khan, fixes the Islamic-democratic frame every later constitution follows

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Objectives Resolution, 1949

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** It takes three constitutions and twenty-six years for Pakistan to settle the rules its founders argued over.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.5: the constitutions of 1956, 1962 and 1973 compared — parliamentary, presidential, then federal-parliamentary with Islamic provisions

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Three constitutions — 1956, 1962 and 1973
