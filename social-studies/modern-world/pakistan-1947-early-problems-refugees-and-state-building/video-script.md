# Video script — Pakistan 1947 — early problems, refugees and state-building

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

A state born in partition must house, feed and govern millions before it has offices, money or staff — and it takes three constitutions and twenty-six years to settle the rules its founders argued over, on the founding frame of the 1949 Objectives Resolution.
