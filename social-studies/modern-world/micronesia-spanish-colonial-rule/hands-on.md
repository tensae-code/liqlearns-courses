# Hands-on — Micronesia — Spanish colonial rule

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Paper rule, real rule
Draw two columns: 'claimed on paper' and 'ruled in person'. Place these under the right column: an old map claim, a governor arriving, a tax, a treaty, an attack three months in. Say what moved Spain from one column to the other.

## 1899: sold
Write the Kapingamarangi announcement of 1899 as the atoll's record-keeper: one paragraph saying who sold the islands, who bought them, and what the atoll had — and had not — been asked.
