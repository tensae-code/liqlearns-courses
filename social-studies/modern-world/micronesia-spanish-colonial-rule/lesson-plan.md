# Lesson plan — Micronesia — Spanish colonial rule

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — Spanish colonial rule
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Spain, the first explorer of the Carolines, became their first coloniser, with administrations on Yap and Pohnpei, fighting Pohnpeians who attacked only three months in, and quarrelling with Protestant missionaries such as Reverend Doane — while far-off Kapingamarangi lived its Spanish era at the edge of the empire until Spain sold the islands in 1899.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Spain becomes first coloniser: Yap and Pohnpei** — say that Spain, first explorer of the Carolines, became first coloniser with administrations on Yap and Pohnpei, and fought Pohnpeians who attacked only three months in.
2. **Quarrels with the missionaries: Reverend Doane** — say that Spain quarrelled with Protestant missionaries such as Reverend Doane.
3. **Kapingamarangi's Spanish era, to 1899** — say that Kapingamarangi lived its Spanish era at the edge of an empire that ruled the Carolines from far away, until Spain sold the islands in 1899.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
