# Micronesia — Spanish colonial rule — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spain set up its Carolines administrations on —
a) Yap and Pohnpei  b) Kosrae and Chuuk  c) Guam and Saipan only
**Answer: a) Yap and Pohnpei** — the islands the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Pohnpeians first attacked the new Spanish rule after about —
a) Three months  b) Three years  c) Thirty years
**Answer: a) Three months** — the span the book gives.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Reverend Doane was —
a) A Spanish governor  b) A Protestant missionary Spain quarrelled with  c) A German trader
**Answer: b) A Protestant missionary Spain quarrelled with** — the man the book names.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Spain's rule over the Carolines ended in 1899 when Spain —
a) Lost a war on Pohnpei  b) Sold the islands  c) Gave them to the chiefs
**Answer: b) Sold the islands** — the ending both books give.
