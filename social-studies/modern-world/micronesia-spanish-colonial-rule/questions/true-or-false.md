# Micronesia — Spanish colonial rule — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spain was the first European power to colonise the Carolines.
**Answer: True** — the book teaches Spain, first explorer, became first coloniser.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Kapingamarangi was the centre of Spain's administration in the Carolines.
**Answer: False** — the reader teaches the atoll lived at the edge of an empire ruling from far away; administrations sat on Yap and Pohnpei.
