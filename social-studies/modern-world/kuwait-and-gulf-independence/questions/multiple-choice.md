# Kuwait and the Gulf win independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait's independence day was —
a) 19 June 1961  b) 19 June 1899  c) 2 December 1971
**Answer: a) 19 June 1961** — Kuwait wins independence on 19 June 1961

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Father of Independence is —
a) Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem  b) Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah  c) Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber
**Answer: a) Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem** — Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem, Father of Independence

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** As Britain withdrew, Gulf peoples —
a) pressed for freedom  b) asked Britain to stay  c) left the coast
**Answer: a) pressed for freedom** — Gulf peoples press for freedom as Britain withdraws
