# Kuwait and the Gulf win independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait's independence came in 1899.
**Answer: False** — independence came on 19 June 1961; 1899 was the protection agreement

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Abdullah Al Salem is honoured as Father of Independence.
**Answer: True** — he leads Kuwait to independence in 1961
