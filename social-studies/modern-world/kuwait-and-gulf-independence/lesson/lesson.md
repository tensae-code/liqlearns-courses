# Kuwait and the Gulf win independence

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: As Britain withdrew, Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem — Father of Independence — led Kuwait to independence on 19 June 1961, and the other Gulf states followed. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The independence phase of Kuwait and the Gulf states

Gulf peoples press for freedom as britain withdraws, and sheikh abdullah al salem — father of independence — leads kuwait to independence on 19 june 1961, with the other gulf states following.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
