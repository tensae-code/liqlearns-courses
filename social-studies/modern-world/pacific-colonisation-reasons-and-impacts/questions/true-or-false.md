# Pacific colonisation — reasons and impacts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain settles Australia in 1788 and the invasion spreads — whalers, then competition for spice, sandalwood, pearl shell and bêche-de-mer; students analyse the social, economic, political and cultural impacts and visit Queen Emma's plantation at Rabaul.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Colonisation in the Pacific

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Science Junior High Grade 10 Teacher Guide, Standards-Based* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Colonisation in the Pacific

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Colonisers bring a whole way of life — administration and mission stations, schools, new clothes for "naked" natives, ancestral worship branded pagan, and a new language, Tok Pisin, born from the contact itself.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Colonizers' cultures in the Pacific

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Social Science Junior High Grade 10 Teacher Guide, Standards-Based* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Colonizers' cultures in the Pacific

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The coloniser's shopping list — raw materials, gold, silver, land, spices, captive markets and cheap labour — and the bill: a widening economic gap between coloniser and colony that outlives the flags.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Economic factors and colonization
