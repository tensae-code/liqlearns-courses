# Pacific colonisation — reasons and impacts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Colonisation in the Pacific
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Britain settles Australia in 1788 and the invasion spreads — whalers, then competition for spice, sandalwood, pearl shell and bêche-de-mer; students analyse the social, economic, political and cultural impacts and visit Queen Emma's plantation at Rabaul

## Sub lesson 2 — Colonizers' cultures in the Pacific
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — colonisers bring a whole way of life — administration and mission stations, schools, new clothes for "naked" natives, ancestral worship branded pagan, and a new language, Tok Pisin, born from the contact itself

## Sub lesson 3 — Economic factors and colonization
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the coloniser's shopping list — raw materials, gold, silver, land, spices, captive markets and cheap labour — and the bill: a widening economic gap between coloniser and colony that outlives the flags
