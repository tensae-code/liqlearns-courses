# Hands-on — The English and French voyages of discovery

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw the race as lanes: Portugal, Spain, then England and France — mark who opened each sea.

## Task 2
Write a latecomer's complaint and a latecomer's hope, two lines each.

## Task 3
Shade a world map's last blanks: mark the poles and write why they were last.

## Task 4
Write an explorer's polar diary entry in four sentences.
