# The English and French voyages of discovery

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: England and France followed late into the discovery race, planting flags across North America — until the race for new lands ended at the earth's frozen poles. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
