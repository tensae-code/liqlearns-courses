# The English and French voyages of discovery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** England and France entered the discovery race —
a) late, after Portugal and Spain  b) first  c) never
**Answer: a) late, after Portugal and Spain** — they follow late into the race

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Their flags and claims spread across —
a) North America  b) Australia only  c) Japan
**Answer: a) North America** — planting flags and claims across North America

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** They sailed seas opened by —
a) Portugal and Spain  b) the Gulf states  c) China
**Answer: a) Portugal and Spain** — the seas Portugal and Spain opened

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The discovery race ended at —
a) the earth's frozen ends, the poles  b) the equator  c) the Gulf
**Answer: a) the earth's frozen ends, the poles** — it ends at the earth's frozen ends

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The poles were —
a) the last blank spaces on the map  b) the first lands found  c) never reached
**Answer: a) the last blank spaces on the map** — the last blank spaces on the map

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Polar voyages belong to the age of —
a) discovery voyages  b) railways  c) steam factories
**Answer: a) discovery voyages** — discovery voyages to the North and South Poles
