# The English and French voyages of discovery — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** England led the discovery race from the start.
**Answer: False** — England and France follow late

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Latecomers could still claim whole coasts.
**Answer: True** — they plant flags and claims across North America

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The poles were discovered before the Americas.
**Answer: False** — they are the last blank spaces, reached at the race's end

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Discovery ends when the map has no blanks left.
**Answer: True** — the poles are the last blank spaces on the map
