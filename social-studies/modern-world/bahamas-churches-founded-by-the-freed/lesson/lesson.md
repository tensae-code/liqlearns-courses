# Bahamas — churches founded by the freed

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Prince William, a freed slave, founds the Baptist church in The Bahamas, and freed Black people break ground for Bethel Baptist on 1 August 1790 — the old churches of Nassau rise from the freed community itself. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
