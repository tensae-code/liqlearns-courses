# Nicaragua First Peoples And Indigenous Roots — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 16 — Desarrollo y legado cultural de los grupos aborígenes
Sub lesson 16 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — migrant peoples from Mexico build temple-plaza towns with stepped sacrifice mounds, and their legacy still shapes Nicaraguan identity

## Sub lesson 17 — Pobladores del Centro y Caribe: origen Macro-Chibcha y afrodescendientes
Sub lesson 17 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Matagalpa, Mayangna, Miskitu and Rama peoples of Chibcha origin share the centre and Caribbean with Afro-descendant Zambos, Creoles and Garífuna; war chiefs are elected for skill and dismissed for misconduct

## Sub lesson 18 — Primeros pobladores de origen mesoamericano
Sub lesson 18 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — war in central Mexico in the 7th-8th centuries pushes Chorotegas, Maribios and Nicaraos south; by about 800 CE they hold the Pacific strip from León to Rivas

## Sub lesson 19 — Persistencia de los grupos aborígenes en el territorio actual
Sub lesson 19 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — today's towns are mapped to their ancestry — Chorotega Jinotega, Mozonte and Totogalpa, Nahoa Ostional and Salinas — the first peoples never vanished

## Sub lesson 20 — El medio geográfico en Nicaragua
Sub lesson 20 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Nicaragua's lakes, plains and coasts decide where the first peoples can settle and how their cultures take shape

## Sub lesson 21 — Sociedades precoloniales del Pacífico: la migración mesoamericana
Sub lesson 21 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the fall of Teotihuacán and Tula pushes Chorotegas, Maribios and Nicaraos south from Mexico between the 8th and 15th centuries; the Chorotegas reach Nicaragua about 800 CE and displace earlier peoples toward the centre

## Sub lesson 22 — Organización política y social indígena
Sub lesson 22 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Monéxico council of elders is elected by vote and in turn elects — and can remove — each town's cacique, so Pacific power is shared, not absolute

## Sub lesson 23 — Organización económica: tierra, agricultura y subsistencia
Sub lesson 23 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — land is held by the community, never individually; farming feeds the village and cacao beans serve as money

## Sub lesson 24 — La artesanía indígena
Sub lesson 24 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — deer-bone combs and hand-worked objects from farm, plant and hunt remains show a craft economy inside every village

Sub lesson 25 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — women traders fill the plaza markets where goods move by cacao money and barter, and only same-language traders are admitted

Sub lesson 26 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — religion binds the whole culture together; offerings, including human sacrifice, are made at sacred places such as the Masaya volcano, the indigenous Popocatepetl
