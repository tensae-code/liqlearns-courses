# Lesson plan — Sher Ali Khan and the Second Anglo-Afghan War

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Sher Ali Khan and the Second Anglo-Afghan War
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Afghanistan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Dost Mohammad's son succeeds amid his brothers' claims and begins a reforming reign
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 16: Do you know Amir Sher Ali Khan's first reign?** — Dost Mohammad's son succeeds amid his brothers' claims and begins a reforming reign
2. **Lesson 17: Do you know the person of Amir Muhammad Afzal Khan?** — Afzal Khan, the elder brother, holds Kabul briefly in the succession struggle
3. **Lesson 18: Do you know the person of Amir Muhammad Azam Khan?** — Azam Khan follows his brother Afzal on Kabul's throne as the civil war of the princes runs on
4. **Lesson 19: Do you know Amir Sher Ali Khan's second reign?** — Sher Ali returns and resumes reform until British demands drive the country toward a second invasion
5. **Lesson 20: Do you know the second British invasion of Afghanistan?** — the Treaty of Gandamak is signed amid anarchy by an Amir unrecognised in his own capital; it cuts Khyber, Kurram, Pishin and Sibi away to British India and brings a British envoy, Sir Louis Cavagnari, to Kabul
6. **Lessons 10 (unnumbered reign sections): The first reign of Amir Sher Ali Khan (1863–1866), the reign of Amir Muhammad Afzal Khan (1866–1867) and the reign of Amir Muhammad Azam Khan (1867–1868)** — three brothers hold Kabul in turn through the succession war
7. **Lesson 11: Reforms and reforming (1868–1879)** — Sher Ali's restored reign reforms army, administration and schools until the British ultimatum
8. **Lesson 12: The reign of Amir Muhammad Yaqub Khan (March–October 1879)** — Yaqub signs Gandamak, loses the border districts and his throne within a single year
