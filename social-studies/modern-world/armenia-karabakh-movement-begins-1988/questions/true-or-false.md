# Armenia Karabakh Movement Begins 1988 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On 20 February 1988 Karabakh's soviet votes to join Armenia, Yerevan fills with a million marchers, and the Sumgait pogrom a week later turns a petition into a national movement.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 4.1 The beginning of the Karabakh movement; Nagorno-Karabakh within Azerbaijan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 4.1 The beginning of the Karabakh movement; Nagorno-Karabakh within Azerbaijan

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The movement of 1988, the earthquake and the 1991 referendum are taught as the hinge between Soviet Armenia and the third republic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Appendix theme guide 4: the Karabakh movement; winning independence

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց (History of Armenia 9, Methodical Guide)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Appendix theme guide 4: the Karabakh movement; winning independence
