# Women win the vote — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The debate asks whether women may —
a) Vote and hold office  b) Sail on trawlers only  c) Attend the Althing as guests only
**Answer: a) Vote and hold office** — whether women may vote and hold office.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The worksheet is a —
a) Role-play debate  b) Map exercise  c) Census
**Answer: a) Role-play debate** — a role-play.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils argue the question in —
a) Period voices  b) Modern slang only  c) Silence
**Answer: a) Period voices** — in period voices.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Icelandic question asks whether women should be allowed to —
a) Hold office (gegna embættum)  b) Keep house keys  c) Weave cloth
**Answer: a) Hold office (gegna embættum)** — Eiga konur að fá að gegna embættum?
