# Women win the vote

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The class debates, in period voices, whether women may vote and hold office — Iceland's road to women's suffrage, argued as it was argued then. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — May women vote and hold office?

the class debates whether women may vote and hold office (Verkefnablað 30: Eiga konur að fá að gegna embættum? — role-play)

In your mind's eye: a period debate, women and men arguing whether women may vote. Say what question is being debated.

## Sub lesson 2 — Argued in period voices

pupils argue the question in period voices — as it was argued at the time (Verkefnablað 30 — role-play)
