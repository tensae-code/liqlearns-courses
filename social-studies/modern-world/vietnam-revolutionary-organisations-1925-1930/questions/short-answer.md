# Vietnam Revolutionary Organisations 1925 1930 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 5, three organisations, three roads.
**Answer: in June 1925 Nguyễn Ái Quốc founds the Revolutionary Youth League in Canton and publishes Đường Kách mệnh (1927); its "proletarianisation" drive roots bases in all three kì by May 1929; the Tân Việt party turns toward the proletarian road, while the VNQDĐ of Nguyễn Thái Học (December 1927) answers the Bazin assassination (February 1929) with the Yên Bái uprising of 9–10 February 1930 — crushed at once, proving the bourgeois road impotent (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 9 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 5, three organisations, three roads, printed p. 25.**
