# Hands-on — Assessment — early-modern and modern world history

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Teach-back round
Take three matching pairs from this pool and teach them to a partner as mini-lessons — person, deed, and one sentence of why. Teaching is the hardest prove-it.

## Two-wars table
Rule a page into two columns — First World War, Second World War — and fill results for each from memory. Then check against the two results lessons and correct in another colour.
