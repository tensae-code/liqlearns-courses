## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Match the works and deeds to their people, with the book's distractors rejected.
2. List both world wars' results and industry's political, economic and social results from memory.
3. Argue the challenging questions in writing: Germany and both wars, the two wars compared, and what Africans gained from Second World War service.
