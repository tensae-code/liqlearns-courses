**1.** Challenging question (chapter review): why did Germany start both world wars? Argue in a short paragraph from this title's causes lessons.
**Answer:** Direction: for 1914 — Germany leading an armed bloc amid imperialist rivalry, nationalism and the arms race, declaring war first on Russia and France; for 1939 — a fascist dictatorship, racist nationalism, heavy rearmament and the Axis, invading Poland while appeasement failed.

**2.** Challenging question (chapter review): compare the two world wars — causes, course and results — in a short essay.
**Answer:** Direction: both grew from rivalry, nationalism and arms; the first from colonial blocs and a spark at Sarajevo, the second from dictatorships, the Axis and appeasement's failure; both ended with a world body founded (League, then United Nations) and a new power order (America rising; then America and Russia).

**3.** Challenging question (chapter review): what did Africans gain from serving under their former colonisers in the Second World War? Argue with evidence from this title.
**Answer:** Direction: service widened experience of the wider world and of the colonisers' own weakness; the book's results list gives the frame — the anti-colonial struggle quickened after the war, and borders and power changed.
