# Assessment — early-modern and modern world history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The first circumnavigation of the world was completed by the expedition of —
a) Columbus  b) Magellan  c) Da Gama  d) Copernicus
**Answer: b) Magellan** — Magellan's expedition — the matching stock of the chapter review.

**2.** The Nazi leader of Germany was —
a) De Gaulle  b) Hitler  c) Luther  d) Loyola
**Answer: b) Hitler** — Hitler — named in the chapter's matching stock.

**3.** The scholar of astronomy and physics who improved the telescope's use was —
a) Galileo  b) Hargreaves  c) Newton  d) Shakespeare
**Answer: a) Galileo** — Galileo — astronomy and physics, the telescope.

**4.** The law of gravity belongs to —
a) Galileo  b) Copernicus  c) Newton  d) Magellan
**Answer: c) Newton** — Newton — calculus and the gravity law.

**5.** The weaving machine in the matching stock — the Spinning Jenny — was made by —
a) Kay  b) Hargreaves  c) Stephenson  d) Savery
**Answer: b) Hargreaves** — Hargreaves made the Spinning Jenny; Kay made the flying shuttle (the distractor's trap).
