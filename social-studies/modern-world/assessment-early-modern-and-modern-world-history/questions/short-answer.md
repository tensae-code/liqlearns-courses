# Assessment — early-modern and modern world history — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** What was the Industrial Revolution's effect on Africa? (Chapter review question — answer from this title's lessons.)
**Answer:** Industry fed Europe's rivalry and scramble for Africa and Asia, supplied the arms and transport of colonisation, and — through the world wars industry armed — drew Africa into Europe's conflicts; the assessment answer is argued from the exploration, industry and war lessons of this title.

**2.** Why did Europe's states organise into military blocs before the First World War?
**Answer:** Imperialist rivalry over colonies, nationalism, and the arms race bred distrust and fear; suspicion drove states into the Triple Alliance and Triple Entente camps, each racing to out-arm the other.

**3.** List the results of BOTH world wars, side by side, briefly.
**Answer:** First: 10 million dead, 20 million wounded, Europe's destruction and crisis, America rising, new states, kingly governments abolished, League of Nations. Second: heavy loss of life and property, communism spread, America and Russia as great powers, Cold War set off, borders changed, anti-colonial struggle quickened, United Nations founded.
