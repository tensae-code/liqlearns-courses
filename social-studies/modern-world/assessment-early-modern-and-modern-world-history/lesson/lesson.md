## Prove it — works, people, results

The review stock asks three kinds of proof. First, **matching**: works and deeds to people — the first circumnavigation, Nazi Germany's leader, the astronomy-and-physics scholar, the gravity law, the weaving machine's maker, with Copernicus and de Gaulle set in the list as distractors to reject. Second, **lists**: both world wars' results, industry's results, and why Europe's states organised into military blocs. Third, **argument**: the challenging questions, answered in writing in questions/writing.md. Do them in that order — match, list, argue.
