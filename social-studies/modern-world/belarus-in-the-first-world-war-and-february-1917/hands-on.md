# Hands-on — Belarus in the First World War and February 1917

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The First World War and February 1917 in Belarus — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The First World War and February 1917 in Belarus, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
