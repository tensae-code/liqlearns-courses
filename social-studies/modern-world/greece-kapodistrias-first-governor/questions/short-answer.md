**Answer: one man builds a state from ruins — schools, army, coinage — until an assassin's bullets in Nafplio end the experiment (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Kapodistrias as governor of Greece (1828–1831); the revolution completed (1829).
**Answer: a Corfiot diplomat returns to govern a ruined, tiny state, builds schools and an army — and is assassinated (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
