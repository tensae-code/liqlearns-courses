# Greece Kapodistrias First Governor — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** one man builds a state from ruins — schools, army, coinage — until an assassin's bullets in Nafplio end the experiment.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §17 Kapodistrias as Governor of Greece (1828–1831); the completion of the Greek Revolution (1829)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία Γ΄ Γυμνασίου — Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Modern and Contemporary History, Grade 9 Gymnasium)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §17 Kapodistrias as Governor of Greece (1828–1831); the completion of the Greek Revolution (1829)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** a Corfiot diplomat returns to govern a ruined, tiny state, builds schools and an army — and is assassinated.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Kapodistrias as governor of Greece (1828–1831); the revolution completed (1829)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία (Modern and Contemporary History), Γ' Γυμνασίου, by Evangelia Louvi & Dimitrios Chr. Xifaras* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Kapodistrias as governor of Greece (1828–1831); the revolution completed (1829)
