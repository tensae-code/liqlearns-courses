# Video script — Iran — the Constitutional Revolution

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Iran's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

despotism at home, colonial hands abroad, awakening intellectuals and clergy — the ingredients of constitutionalism assemble one by one.

the people move — protests and sanctuary-taking force Muzaffar al-Din Shah to grant a constitution and a national assembly in 1906.

the whole arc in one lesson — grievance, sanctuary, constitution, civil war — Iran's first attempt to bind its kings by law.

the survey version — a nation demands law over kingship and wins a parliament.
