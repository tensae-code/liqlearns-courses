# Armenia Declares Independence 1991 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 4.3 The proclamation of Armenia's independence.
**Answer: the 1990 Declaration and the referendum of 21 September 1991 restore independence; Levon Ter-Petrosyan becomes first president of the third republic (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* (Armenia), 4.3 The proclamation of Armenia's independence, printed p. 120.**
