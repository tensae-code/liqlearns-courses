**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the zar-zaman poets lament the passing old world
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§44–45 Kazakh literature and public thought in the first half of the 19th century** — the zar-zaman poets lament the passing old world
2. **§46 Features of spiritual culture and family traditions** — weddings, funerals and hospitality keep their ancient forms
3. **§47 National games** — baiga races and kokpar train riders from childhood
4. **§48 Oral folk creativity in the 19th century** — epics and aitys improvisation flourish at every gathering
5. **§49 Outstanding representatives of oral folk literature, 19th–early 20th centuries** — the great zhyraus and akyns are named and remembered
6. **§50 Kazakhstan's musical art in the 19th century** — kui compositions turn history into instrumental story
7. **§51 Kazakhstan's song art in the 19th century** — singers carry news, praise and satire across the steppe
8. **§52 The Kazakh people's national musical instruments** — dombra and kobyz anchor the steppe's sound world
9. **§§53–54 Enlightenment and education develop in Kazakhstan in the 19th century** — Russian-Kazakh schools open a new road to learning
10. **§55 Research on Kazakhstan in the 19th century** — expeditions and scholars map and describe the steppe
11. **§56 Chokan Valikhanov — the great Kazakh scholar** — the officer-scholar's journeys open Central Asia to science
12. **§57 Ybyrai Altynsarin's innovations** — Altynsarin builds Kazakh schools, textbooks and teacher training
13. **§§58–59 The greatness of Abai Kunanbaev** — Abai's poems and Words of Edification remake Kazakh thought
14. **§60 The historical value of the works of Shakarim Kudaiberdiev, Mashkhur Zhusup Kopeev and Kurbangali Khalidi** — the last chroniclers write the steppe's memory into history
15. **§§43–44 Culture develops in the 19th century** — oral tradition and new literacy run side by side
16. **§§45–46 The role of schooling and enlightenment grows** — mektebs, madrasas and Russian-Kazakh schools spread
17. **§§47–48 Shokan Ualikhanov — the Kazakh people's great scholar and traveller** — Valikhanov's Kashgar journey makes him Eurasia's famed explorer
18. **§§49–50 Ybyrai Altynsarin — educator, innovative pedagogue, public figure** — Altynsarin opens schools and writes the first Kazakh textbooks
19. **§§51–52 Abai Kunanbaev — great scholar and thinker** — Abai calls his people to learning and honest labour
20. **§§53–54 The Kazakhs' oral historical tradition (late 19th–early 20th centuries)** — shezhire genealogies and historical songs carry the national memory
