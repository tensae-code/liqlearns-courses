# China Hong Kong Macau Return — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Hong Kong and Macau return.
**Answer: Deng's 'one country, two systems' opens the road; at 23:59 on 30 June 1997 the British flag lowers in silence, and at 0:00 the five-star flag and bauhinia flag rise to the anthem as cameras catch the tears — Macau follows in 1999 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Lesson 13: Hong Kong and Macau return, printed p. 65.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: One country, two systems in practice.
**Answer: the flags change in 1997 and 1999 and the two SARs prosper inside one China (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 29: One country, two systems in practice, printed p. 188.**
