# China Hong Kong Macau Return — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Deng's 'one country, two systems' opens the road; at 23:59 on 30 June 1997 the British flag lowers in silence, and at 0:00 the five-star flag and bauhinia flag rise to the anthem as cameras catch the tears — Macau follows in 1999.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Hong Kong and Macau return

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Hong Kong and Macau return

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The flags change in 1997 and 1999 and the two SARs prosper inside one China.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in One country, two systems in practice

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about One country, two systems in practice
