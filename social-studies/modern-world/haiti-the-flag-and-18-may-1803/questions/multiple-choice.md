# Haiti — the flag and 18 May 1803 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The date 18 May  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The date 18 May** — at Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tear the white from the French flag and sew a new one; the date becomes Flag Day, retold every year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide this part is —
a) Study object: the date 18 May  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Study object: the date 18 May** — the guide's own heading for this part.
