# Lesson plan — Haiti — the flag and 18 May 1803

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Haiti — the flag and 18 May 1803
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Haiti, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** At Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tear the white from the French flag and sew a new one; the date, 18 May, becomes Flag Day, retold every year.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide.
