# Haiti — the flag and 18 May 1803

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: At Arcahaie in May 1803 the independence leaders tear the white from the French flag and sew a new one; the date, 18 May, becomes Flag Day, retold every year. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
