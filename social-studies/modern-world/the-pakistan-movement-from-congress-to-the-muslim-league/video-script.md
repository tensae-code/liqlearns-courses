# Video script — The Pakistan Movement — from Congress to the Muslim League

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From Congress in 1885 to the midnight of 14 August 1947, the Muslims of India organise, bargain and finally demand a country of their own — province by province, founder by founder — until Pakistan is born.
