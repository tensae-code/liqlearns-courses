# The Pakistan Movement — from Congress to the Muslim League — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The provinces create Pakistan.
**Answer: After the failed war of 1857 the Muslims of India organise; the Lahore Resolution of 23 March 1940 demands a separate homeland; and each province plays its part — Punjab passes the resolution's spirit at the Minar-e-Pakistan's ground, Sindh, the land where Islam first entered with Muhammad bin Qasim in 712 and which the book calls Bab-ul-Islam, becomes the first province to vote for Pakistan in 1943, Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa join by their own decisions and referendum, until 14 August 1947. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Social Studies, Class 4 (PCTB, SNC 2020)* (Grade 4 / Class 4), Chapter 4: the contribution of the provinces and regions in the creation of Pakistan, printed p. 52.**

**Answer: The new country is not built by Muslims alone — minority citizens, from the Christians, Hindus, Sikhs and Parsis who chose Pakistan to servants of the sick like Dr Ruth Pfau, share in its making, and the chapter closes with the heroes decorated with the Nishan-e-Haider. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

(any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
