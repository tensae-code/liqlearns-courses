# Indonesia — the first election, 1955, and Nasakom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The women's movement organises, ballot boxes are counted into the night in Kemayoran on 29 September 1955 in Indonesia's first election, and Sukarno answers the fragmentation with Nasakom — nationalism, religion and communism in one formula.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 2.B Ketersebaran Kekuatan dan Identitas Nasional Baru

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII (Kurikulum Merdeka)* is about Indonesia — the 30 September Movement and the fall of Sukarno.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 2.B Ketersebaran Kekuatan dan Identitas Nasional Baru
