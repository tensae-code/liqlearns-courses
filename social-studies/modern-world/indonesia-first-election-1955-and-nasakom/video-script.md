# Video script — Indonesia — the first election, 1955, and Nasakom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Ballot boxes are counted into the night in Kemayoran on 29 September 1955 in Indonesia's first election, and Sukarno answers the fragmentation with Nasakom — nationalism, religion and communism in one formula.
