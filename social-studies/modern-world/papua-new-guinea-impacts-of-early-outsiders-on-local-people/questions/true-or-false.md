# Papua New Guinea — impacts of early outsiders on local people — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The arrivals put PNG on the world record but changed local lives for good and ill — the class appraises what explorers, missionaries, miners and traders each did to the people already there.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Impacts of Missionaries on Local People / Impacts of Miners and Traders

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Science Teacher Guide — Primary Grade 7, Standards Based* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Impacts of Missionaries on Local People / Impacts of Miners and Traders

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cultures change by contact and diffusion; western schooling, phones and Christianity spread through PNG until people rarely call on ancestors for help — old skills are lost as new ones are learnt, faster in some places than others.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Cultural influence

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Social Science Junior High Grade 9 Teacher Guide, Standards-Based* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Cultural influence

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** First contact is role-played, then judged — how colonial powers imposed and held control, what imperialism did to indigenous societies, and how reactions differed from Africa to the Asia-Pacific.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 11.2 responses to imperialism
