# Papua New Guinea — impacts of early outsiders on local people — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Impacts of Missionaries on Local People / Impacts of Miners and Traders
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the arrivals put PNG on the world record but changed local lives for good and ill — the class appraises what explorers, missionaries, miners and traders each did to the people already there

## Sub lesson 2 — Cultural influence
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — cultures change by contact and diffusion; western schooling, phones and Christianity spread through PNG until people rarely call on ancestors for help — old skills are lost as new ones are learnt, faster in some places than others

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 11.2 responses to imperialism
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — first contact is role-played, then judged — how colonial powers imposed and held control, what imperialism did to indigenous societies, and how reactions differed from Africa to the Asia-Pacific
