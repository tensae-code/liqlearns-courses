# Nicaragua Sandinista Revolution And Its Heroes — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Efemérides: anniversary of the Revolución Popular Sandinista (19 July)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — pupils explain the Revolution's importance and name its heroes and martyrs

## Sub lesson 2 — Efemérides: birth of Comandante Carlos Fonseca Amador
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Fonseca, a teacher born 23 June, becomes founder and commander-in-chief of the Sandinista Revolution

## Sub lesson 3 — Héroes de la Patria: Carlos Fonseca Amador
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Fonseca founds the Frente Sandinista and is named Father of the Sandinista Popular Revolution, a life shown as proof ordinary people can change the country

## Sub lesson 4 — Héroes de la Patria: Julio Buitrago Urroz
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Buitrago, a hardworking boy who loved work from childhood, becomes Father of the Urban Resistance, with a house-museum keeping his story

## Sub lesson 5 — Héroes de la Patria: Arlen Siu
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — four years before the Revolution triumphs, young Arlen Siu gives her life; a song, "El cenzontle pregunta por Arlen Siu", asks after the girl who never came back

## Sub lesson 6 — Héroes de la Patria: Bertha Calderón
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Calderón, a reader inspired by Fidel's books, is killed a month before the Revolution's triumph and is remembered as a heroine of that final push

## Sub lesson 7 — Efemérides: anniversary of Comandante Carlos Fonseca Amador's passing
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the year closes by returning to Fonseca's mausoleum and death anniversary, the founder remembered at the moment he fell

## Sub lesson 8 — Aniversario de la Revolución Popular Sandinista
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Revolution's anniversary is celebrated as the department's living historical feast, linking 1856's defence of sovereignty to 1979

## Sub lesson 9 — La acción heroica de Rigoberto López Pérez
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — poet Rigoberto López Pérez decides to end the Somoza dictatorship at its root and shoots the dictator, an act the book calls heroic sacrifice

## Sub lesson 10 — Legado histórico de Carlos Fonseca Amador
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Fonseca's legacy is founding the FSLN and giving the later Revolution its ideas and organisation

## Sub lesson 11 — Personajes históricos: Arlen Siu
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Arlen Siu, "la chinita de Jinotepe", joins the guerrilla and dies young; her story closes the department's hero gallery

## Sub lesson 12 — Surgimiento y desarrollo del FSLN; participación de las mujeres
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Fonseca and others found the FSLN; its combats grow over two decades and women fight throughout, not at the margins

## Sub lesson 13 — Insurrección popular de 1978-1979 y ofensiva final
Sub lesson 13 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — whole towns rise in 1978-79; the people's participation in the final offensive brings the dynasty down on 19 July 1979

## Sub lesson 14 — La Revolución Popular Sandinista: gobierno, logros y dificultades; la mujer en la Revolución
Sub lesson 14 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Junta and Council of State replace Somoza's institutions, the Guardia becomes the Ejército Popular Sandinista, gains come in health, education and land — alongside real difficulties and women's central role

## Sub lesson 15 — Triunfo de la Revolución Popular Sandinista
Sub lesson 15 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — on 19 July 1979 the Revolution overthrows the Somoza dictatorship after an insurrection that costs some 50,000 lives, most of them young

## Sub lesson 16 — El gobierno sandinista: características
Sub lesson 16 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a mixed economy, single health system, free compulsory education and labour protections define the revolutionary government's programme

## Sub lesson 17 — Logros de la Revolución: reforma agraria y proyectos
Sub lesson 17 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — agrarian reform delivers 4 million manzanas to 120,000 families and organises 3,151 cooperatives, while African palm, textile and cardboard projects rebuild production

## Sub lesson 18 — La acción de Rigoberto López Pérez, 1956
Sub lesson 18 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — López Pérez shoots Somoza García at León's Casa del Obrero on 21 September 1956, proving the dictatorship is not invincible; guerrilla movements, including old Sandinista Ramón Raudales, rise after him

## Sub lesson 19 — Fundación del Frente Sandinista de Liberación Nacional
Sub lesson 19 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the FSLN is built to give the popular struggle the political-military organisation it lacked; early guerrillas fall at Raití and Bocay in 1963, the 22 January 1967 massacre breaks conservative hegemony, and Pancasán relaunches the armed struggle

## Sub lesson 20 — La ofensiva sandinista de 1974-1978
Sub lesson 20 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — after the 1972 Managua earthquake exposes the regime's theft of aid, the Juan José Quezada commando seizes the Chema Castillo house on 27 December 1974 and the dictatorship answers with savage rural repression

## Sub lesson 21 — Carlos Fonseca, Padre de la Revolución Popular Sandinista
Sub lesson 21 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Fonseca's life is told whole — Moscow and Kiev congresses, arrests and deportations, founding the Movimiento Nueva Nicaragua in 1961 and publishing Sandino's Ideario — the founder as the Revolution's father

## Sub lesson 22 — Derrocamiento del régimen somocista: la insurrección de 1978
Sub lesson 22 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Monimbó and Subtiava rise, and on 22 August 1978 a Sandinista commando seizes the National Palace with the Congress hostage, forcing Somoza to free political prisoners on live television

## Sub lesson 23 — Triunfo de la Revolución Popular Sandinista
Sub lesson 23 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the tactical retreat to Masaya, the Junta's first proclamation on 18 June and the final siege close in; Somoza Debayle flees on 17 July 1979 and revolutionary forces enter Managua

## Sub lesson 24 — Nicaragua durante la Revolución: forma de gobierno
Sub lesson 24 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the five-member Junta de Gobierno de Reconstrucción Nacional replaces the Somocista state and proclaims a Fundamental Statute of rights on the road to a new constitution

## Sub lesson 25 — Transformaciones socio-económicas de la Revolución
Sub lesson 25 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Somoza-family property is expropriated, banking and foreign trade nationalised, and an agrarian reform creates People's Property Areas and cooperatives inside a mixed economy

## Sub lesson 26 — La lucha en Nicaragua: del somocismo al triunfo de 1979
Sub lesson 26 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the FSLN learns from its Bocay-Raití defeat, builds among peasants and city masses, unites its three tendencies and launches the final offensive; Washington itself asks Somoza to resign, he flees on 17 July and the FSLN enters Managua on 19 July 1979
