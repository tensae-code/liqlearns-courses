# Hands-on — The Great Powers, the Middle East and Arab nationalism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Great Powers, the Middle East and Arab nationalism — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Great Powers and the Middle East after World War 1, and in World War 2, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
