# Armenia First Republic State And Symbols — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On 28 May 1918 the Armenian National Council proclaims the republic; Aram Manukyan's government builds a parliament, ministries and the tricolour from nothing.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 2.3 The state system and symbols of the Republic of Armenia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 2.3 The state system and symbols of the Republic of Armenia

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Teachers present 1917's collapse, Sardarabad and the 1918 republic's state-building as the nation's recovered statehood.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Appendix theme guide 2: Armenia on the road to restored independence; the First Republic

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց (History of Armenia 9, Methodical Guide)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Appendix theme guide 2: Armenia on the road to restored independence; the First Republic
