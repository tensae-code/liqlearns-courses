# Suriname — crisis: Doedel and Anton de Kom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the 1929 crisis the unemployed organise in the SAWO; the governor declares their leader Louis Doedel insane and locks him in an asylum, and Anton de Kom, returned in 1933 running a complaints office for workers, is arrested and deported after a fatal protest march.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Crisis en oorlog (crisis part)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 6 Geschiedenis* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Crisis en oorlog (crisis part)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Doedel organises the unemployed and De Kom writes a programme against the abuses he finds; the government strikes hard at both, and a Comité van Actie presses the workers' demands.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Louis Doedel en Anton de Kom

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 8 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Louis Doedel en Anton de Kom
