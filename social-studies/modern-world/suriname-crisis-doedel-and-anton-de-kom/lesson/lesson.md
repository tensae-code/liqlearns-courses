# Suriname — crisis: Doedel and Anton de Kom

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In the 1929 crisis the unemployed organise in the SAWO; the governor declares their leader Louis Doedel insane and locks him in an asylum, and Anton de Kom, returned in 1933 running a complaints office for workers, is arrested and deported after a fatal protest march As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Crisis en oorlog (crisis part)

In the 1929 crisis the unemployed organise in the SAWO; the governor declares their leader Louis Doedel insane and locks him in an asylum, and Anton de Kom, returned in 1933 running a complaints office for workers, is arrested and deported after a fatal protest march (Crisis en oorlog (crisis part))

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Louis Doedel en Anton de Kom

Doedel organises the unemployed and De Kom writes a programme against the abuses he finds; the government strikes hard at both, and a Comité van Actie presses the workers' demands (Louis Doedel en Anton de Kom)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
