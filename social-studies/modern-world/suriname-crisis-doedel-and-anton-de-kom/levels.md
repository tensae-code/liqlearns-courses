## Sub lesson 1 — Crisis en oorlog (crisis part)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — In the 1929 crisis the unemployed organise in the SAWO; the governor declares their leader Louis Doedel insane and locks him in an asylum, and Anton de Kom, returned in 1933 running a complaints office for workers, is arrested and deported after a fatal protest march.

Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Doedel organises the unemployed and De Kom writes a programme against the abuses he finds; the government strikes hard at both, and a Comité van Actie presses the workers' demands.
