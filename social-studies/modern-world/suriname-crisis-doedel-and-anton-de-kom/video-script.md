# Video script — Suriname — crisis: Doedel and Anton de Kom

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In the 1929 crisis the unemployed organise in the SAWO; the governor declares their leader Louis Doedel insane and locks him in an asylum, and Anton de Kom, returned in 1933 running a complaints office for workers, is arrested and deported after a fatal protest march.

Doedel organises the unemployed and De Kom writes a programme against the abuses he finds; the government strikes hard at both, and a Comité van Actie presses the workers' demands.
