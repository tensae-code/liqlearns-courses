# Lesson plan — Art Nouveau — Secession, a total style for 1900

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Art Nouveau — Secession, a total style for 1900
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Around 1900 the young secede from the academies — whiplash lines and plant forms swallow architecture, furniture, posters and jewellery whole from Barcelona to Vienna, and Klimt covers Vienna's new art in gold — a last attempt at one total style before modernism breaks apart.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.
