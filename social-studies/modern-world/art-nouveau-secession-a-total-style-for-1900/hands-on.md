# Hands-on — Art Nouveau — Secession, a total style for 1900

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw an Art Nouveau building, poster and piece of jewellery sharing the same whiplash line from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: The young secede; One style swallows everything; Klimt's gold, a last total style. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
