# Art Nouveau — Secession, a total style for 1900 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Whiplash lines and plant forms swallow architecture, furniture, posters and jewellery whole, from Barcelona to Vienna.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Around 1900 the young secede from the academies — whiplash lines and plant forms swallow architecture, furniture, posters and jewellery whole from Barcelona to Vienna, and Klimt covers Vienna's new art in gold — a last attempt at one total style before modernism breaks apart.
