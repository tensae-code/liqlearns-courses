# Art Nouveau — Secession, a total style for 1900 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) One style swallows everything  b) The young secede  c) Klimt's gold, a last total style
**Answer: b) The young secede** — Around 1900 the young secede from the academies.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) One style swallows everything  b) The young secede  c) Klimt's gold, a last total style
**Answer: a) One style swallows everything** — Whiplash lines and plant forms swallow architecture, furniture, posters and jewellery whole, from Barcelona to Vienna.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) The young secede  b) One style swallows everything  c) Klimt's gold, a last total style
**Answer: c) Klimt's gold, a last total style** — Klimt covers Vienna's new art in gold — a last attempt at one total style before modernism breaks apart.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Around 1900 the young secede from the academies — whiplash lines and plant forms swallow architecture, furniture, posters and jewellery whole from Barcelona to Vienna, and Klimt covers Vienna's new art in gold — a last attempt at one total style before modernism breaks apart  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
