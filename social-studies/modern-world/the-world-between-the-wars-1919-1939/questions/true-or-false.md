# The world between the wars, 1919–1939 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The interwar peace was solid and safe.
**Answer: False** — it was a shaky peace that led back to war

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Dictators rose between the wars.
**Answer: True** — rising dictators fill the twenty years
