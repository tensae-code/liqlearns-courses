# The world between the wars, 1919–1939 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The years 1919–1939 held —
a) a shaky peace, new states and rising dictators  b) a lasting peace  c) no new states
**Answer: a) a shaky peace, new states and rising dictators** — a shaky peace, new states and rising dictators

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The twenty years led —
a) straight back to war  b) to world government  c) to disarmament
**Answer: a) straight back to war** — they lead straight back to war

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The period sits between —
a) the two world wars  b) the discovery voyages  c) the cold war decades
**Answer: a) the two world wars** — developments between the two world wars
