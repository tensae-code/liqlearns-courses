# Video script — The Pacific — dominant and minority groups

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Australia, Guam, Hawaii, New Caledonia and New Zealand the indigenous islanders are minorities in their own homelands; US bases in the Marshalls, Guam and Hawaii stall Palau's decolonisation for nearly twenty years — while most Pacific islands remain indigenous-majority.
