# The Pacific — dominant and minority groups

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In Australia, Guam, Hawaii, New Caledonia and New Zealand the indigenous islanders are minorities in their own homelands; US bases in the Marshalls, Guam and Hawaii stall Palau's decolonisation for nearly twenty years — while most Pacific islands remain indigenous-majority. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
