# Hands-on — Greece From The Eastern Crisis To The Balkan Wars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Greece From The Eastern Crisis To The Balkan Wars — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Theme 4: Greece from the end of the Great Eastern Crisis to the Balkan Wars — emigration, public anger and army officers demanding the parties' grip be loosened, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
