# Venezuela The 1902 Blockade And Drago Doctrine — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** European fleets blockade the ports to collect debts by force; the Drago Doctrine answers that public debt can never justify armed intervention, settled at Washington in 1903.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The blockade of Venezuelan ports, 1902–1903

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The blockade of Venezuelan ports, 1902–1903

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In December 1902 foreign warships blockade Venezuela violently over its debts, and a child of the time hears the guns.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in A December of cannon fire: 1902

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 3er grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about A December of cannon fire: 1902

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** British, German and Italian warships blockade the coasts to collect debts by force, the crisis that produces the Drago answer.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The blockade of Venezuelan coasts, 1902

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Nuestra Historia Republicana — Ciencias Sociales, 2do año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The blockade of Venezuelan coasts, 1902
