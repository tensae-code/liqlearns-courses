# Whaling today — protecting the whales — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons. Pass mark: say that whales are now protected and counted, and that Inuit whaling continues today as a small, community-run hunt under shared rules. Practice deepens by more items first (every name and distinction in the lesson), then harder corners: retelling the story in order without the lesson open. The questions live in the type files.
