# Video script — Whaling today — protecting the whales

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the ending of the Nunavut reader's history of whaling — the whales protected, the hunt carried on. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The story of the whale does not end with the big ships. It ends — for now — with protection.

Today, the whales are protected. People watch them and count them, so everyone knows how many there are and how they are doing.

And the Inuit hunt has not disappeared. It continues today — small, run by the community itself, under shared rules. The Elders' knowledge and the crew's teamwork still carry it, as they did long ago.

The old hunt, the hard years of too many ships — and now: whales counted and protected, and a community hunting its own way, inside rules that keep the whales safe for the children who will read this story next.
