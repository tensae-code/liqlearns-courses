# Hands-on — Whaling today — protecting the whales

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Count the whales
Draw a stretch of Arctic sea with whales in it. Count them out loud and write the number down, the way whale-watchers count today. Say why counting matters: how else would anyone know the whales are safe?

## Rules we share
With others, agree three shared rules for a small community hunt — rules that let the hunt continue and keep the whales safe. Write each rule down and say, for each one, what it protects.
