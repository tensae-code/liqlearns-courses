# Lesson plan — Whaling today — protecting the whales

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Whaling today — protecting the whales
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Canada, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Whales are now protected and counted, and Inuit whaling continues today as a small, community-run hunt under shared rules — the old hunt carried on, inside limits that keep the whales safe.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the two lessons before it in this story — the traditional hunt, and the foreign ships that depleted the whales — but it also stands alone: today the whales are protected and counted, and the community hunt goes on under shared rules. It needs nothing else.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how whales are treated today: protected and counted.
2. Say how Inuit whaling continues today: a small, community-run hunt under shared rules.
3. Say why the shared rules matter — they let the hunt continue while keeping the whales safe.
