# Whaling today — protecting the whales — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1.** Inuit whaling has completely disappeared today.
**Answer: False** — it continues today as a small, community-run hunt under shared rules.

**2.** Whales are counted today as part of protecting them.
**Answer: True** — counting shows how many there are, so their safety can be guarded.
