# Whaling today — protecting the whales — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** Today, whales are —
a) Hunted in large numbers for trade  b) Protected and counted  c) Forgotten
**Answer: b) Protected and counted** — people watch over them and count their numbers.

**2.** Inuit whaling today is —
a) Stopped completely  b) Done by foreign ships  c) A small, community-run hunt under shared rules
**Answer: c) A small, community-run hunt under shared rules.**

**3.** The whales are counted so that —
a) Everyone knows how many there are and how they are doing  b) Hunters can find them faster  c) The ships can return
**Answer: a) Everyone knows how many there are and how they are doing** — counting guards their safety.

**4.** The shared rules for today's hunt are there to —
a) Make the hunt bigger  b) Let the hunt continue while keeping the whales safe  c) Stop the community hunting at all
**Answer: b) Let the hunt continue while keeping the whales safe.**
