# Hands-on — Guyana: our nation — independence and symbols

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the flag
Draw Guyana's flag from the picture slot in the lesson, as carefully as you can. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what a flag stands for.

## Two dates on a line
Draw a line. Mark 26 May 1966 at one point and 23 February 1970 further along it. Under each date, write what happened on that day, in your own words.

## A poppy to remember
Draw a red poppy. Say out loud who the poppies honour on Remembrance Day, and why a country chooses a flower to remember with.
