# Guyana: our nation — independence and symbols — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The national anthem and the national pledge are symbols of Guyana that live in people's voices — sung and said together.
**Answer: True** — the anthem is the nation's song and the pledge its promise words.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The red poppies of Remembrance Day honour those who went to war.
**Answer: True** — the country remembers them and says their going is not forgotten.
