# Guyana: our nation — independence and symbols — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Guyana became independent on —
a) 23 February 1970  b) 26 May 1966  c) 1 January 2004
**Answer: b) 26 May 1966** — the date Ron and Sue learn in the Our Nation chapter.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The independent nation of Guyana is led by —
a) A President  b) A king from another country  c) Ron and Sue's teacher
**Answer: a) A President** — the head of the country, as the book teaches it.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these is one of Guyana's national symbols in this lesson?
a) The school bell  b) The flag  c) The weather chart
**Answer: b) The flag** — with the coat of arms and the national bird.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Mashramani celebrates —
a) The start of the school year  b) Guyana becoming a Co-operative Republic on 23 February 1970  c) Remembrance Day
**Answer: b) Guyana becoming a Co-operative Republic on 23 February 1970** — the republic day festival.
