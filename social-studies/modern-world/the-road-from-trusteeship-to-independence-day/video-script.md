# Video script — The road from trusteeship to Independence Day

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the Sāmoa Act 1921 confirms the League mandate; Peter Fraser builds trusteeship after 1945; a Sāmoan parliament and Guy Powles's timetable follow in 1947; in the UN plebiscite of 1961, 79% vote for independence, and on 1 January 1962 the Sāmoan flag rises alone — then the Treaty of Friendship with New Zealand, UN membership in 1976, and the name "Sāmoa" confirmed in 1997.
