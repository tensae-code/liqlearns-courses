# America in the 1960s — civil rights and the Great Society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Great Society was whose programme?
a) Kennedy's  b) Johnson's  c) Nixon's
**Answer: b) Johnson's** — Johnson's Great Society.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Sit-ins and freedom rides belong to —
a) The labour movement of 1900  b) The civil rights movement  c) The Populists
**Answer: b) The civil rights movement** — the civil rights movement marching on — sit-ins, freedom rides.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The March on Washington is listed among —
a) Election campaigns  b) Civil rights movement landmarks  c) War battles
**Answer: b) Civil rights movement landmarks** — Birmingham, the March on Washington.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The decade's wider challenge was —
a) Only about taxes  b) A generation challenging the status quo on every front  c) A return to isolation
**Answer: b) A generation challenging the status quo on every front** — a wider generation challenges the status quo on every front.
