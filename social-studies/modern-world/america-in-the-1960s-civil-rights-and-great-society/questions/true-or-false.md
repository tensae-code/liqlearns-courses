# America in the 1960s — civil rights and the Great Society — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The civil rights movement used marches, sit-ins and freedom rides.
**Answer: True** — Sit-ins, freedom rides, Birmingham, the March on Washington.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1960s were a quiet decade of national agreement.
**Answer: False** — The book calls them Contesting Futures — America argued over its future on every front.
