# Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 — in our locality — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How many local people were killed at the village killing field?
**Answer: 46 local men and women (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book teach the war through one village first?
**Answer: So the war is real and near — a field pupils can walk to and a martyr whose mother they can picture — before it becomes national history (any close wording).**
