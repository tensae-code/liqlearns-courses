# Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 — in our locality — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Azad's mother stopped waiting for him after the war ended.
**Answer: False** — she never stopped waiting.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The killing field the pupils find is in their own village.
**Answer: True** — the war is met in our locality.
