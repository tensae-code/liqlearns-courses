# Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 — in our locality — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The village killing field is where —
a) A battle between two armies was fought  b) 46 local men and women were killed in 1971  c) A market stood
**Answer: b) 46 local men and women were killed in 1971** — the field's story.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Azad was —
a) A general  b) Captured by the Pakistani forces; his mother never stopped waiting  c) A journalist
**Answer: b) Captured by the Pakistani forces; his mother never stopped waiting** — Azad's story as the book tells it.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book, Azad stands for —
a) Only himself  b) Thousands of unknown martyrs  c) The enemy
**Answer: b) Thousands of unknown martyrs** — one martyr for thousands unknown.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Grade 6 book first meets the Liberation War —
a) In the capital's parade ground  b) In the children's own village  c) In a foreign country
**Answer: b) In the children's own village** — the war in our locality.
