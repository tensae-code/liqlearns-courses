# Hands-on — Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 — in our locality

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A place that remembers

Write about a place near your home that carries a story of the past. Say what happened there and how people keep the story alive.

## One for thousands

Write three sentences about Azad as the book presents him, ending with why one person's story can stand for thousands.
