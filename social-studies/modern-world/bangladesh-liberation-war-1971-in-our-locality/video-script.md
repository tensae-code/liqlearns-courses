# Video script — Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 — in our locality

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bangladesh book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Grade 6 book does not start the Liberation War with dates. It starts with a field — the village killing field, where 46 local men and women were killed in 1971.

And with Azad, captured by the Pakistani forces, whose mother never stopped waiting for him.

One martyr standing for thousands unknown: that is how the war becomes real.
