# Lesson plan — Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 — in our locality

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Bangladesh Liberation War 1971 — in our locality
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Bangladesh, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Liberation War first met where it happened: a village killing field where 46 local men and women were killed, and Azad — captured by the Pakistani forces, his mother waiting forever — one martyr standing for thousands unknown.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told plainly, part by part; it needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the village killing field is: where 46 local men and women were killed in 1971.
2. Tell Azad's story in two sentences: captured by the Pakistani forces; his mother never stopped waiting.
3. Explain why the book teaches the war through one village and one martyr.
