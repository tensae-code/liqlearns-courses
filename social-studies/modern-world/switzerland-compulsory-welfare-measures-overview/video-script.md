# Video script — Switzerland — Compulsory welfare measures: care or coercion?

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Switzerland's *Sorge oder Zwang?* set tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Switzerland stopped starving. But it stayed bitterly poor.



So officials sorted the poor: worthy — and unworthy.



For the unworthy there were measures, not help: children taken, adults locked away, families dissolved.



Only wealth, insurance and human rights ended the era. Care — or coercion? You must judge.
