# Switzerland — Compulsory welfare measures: care or coercion? — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What split did officials make among the poor, and what followed for each side?
**Answer: They split the poor into "worthy" and "unworthy": the worthy received help, while the unworthy faced compulsory measures — children removed, detention, families dissolved (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Name two things that ended the era of compulsory welfare measures.
**Answer: Any two of: the post-war boom, social insurance (AHV 1948 and its growth), women's suffrage, the 1968 movement, the 1974 human-rights convention (any close wording).**
