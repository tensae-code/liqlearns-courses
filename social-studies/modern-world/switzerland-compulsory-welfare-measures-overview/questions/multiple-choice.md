# Switzerland — Compulsory welfare measures: care or coercion? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the reader's opening, Switzerland had stopped —
a) Starving, though it stayed bitterly poor  b) Taxing the poor  c) Building workhouses in every village
**Answer: a) Starving, though it stayed bitterly poor**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Officials split the poor into —
a) Worthy and unworthy  b) Town and country  c) Young and old
**Answer: a) Worthy and unworthy**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1912 Civil Code gave guardianship offices —
a) Wide preventive powers  b) No powers over families  c) Power only over money
**Answer: a) Wide preventive powers**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** The system was finally broken by, among other things —
a) The 1974 human-rights convention  b) A new poor ordinance  c) Closing the welfare offices in one year
**Answer: a) The 1974 human-rights convention**
