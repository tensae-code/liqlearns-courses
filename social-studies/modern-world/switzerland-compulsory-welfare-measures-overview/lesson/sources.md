**Acceptance count: taught in 1 country (Switzerland).**

Switzerland's own way (whole set): History is taught as five true life-stories from one canton (Graubünden/Grischun/Grigioni). A slim pupil reader tells each fate as a story with facsimile original sources, and a teacher companion at each level (Sekundarstufe I ≈ Grade 7, Sekundarstufe II ≈ Grade 10) supplies the background, the worksheets and — at Sek II — extra documents and a deepening question per case. The question over the whole set is in its title: care, or coercion?

Switzerland's own way (the reader): Five representative fates stand for over 100,000 people hit by compulsory welfare measures; each story is told first, then continued by the pupil from photographed original files, and each ends on an open judgement question, not a verdict.

Switzerland's own way (Sek I companion): The companion turns each fate into a jigsaw-class (Gruppenpuzzle): groups master one case from background briefings plus worksheets and teach it to the class, judged against Lehrplan 21 competences (Swiss history of the 20th century, children's and human rights).

History in this lesson is summary in the course's own words — the books' stories and readings are filed as plot summaries only, never transcribed.
