# Hands-on — Switzerland — Compulsory welfare measures: care or coercion?

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Worthy or unworthy?
Draw the officials' sorting table: two columns, Worthy and Unworthy. Under each, write what that person could expect from the welfare office. Then write one sentence on what was wrong with the sorting itself.

## The era on a line
Draw a timeline with four marks: 1857 (poor ordinance), 1912 (Civil Code), 1948 (AHV), 1974 (human-rights convention). Under each, write in your own words what changed.
