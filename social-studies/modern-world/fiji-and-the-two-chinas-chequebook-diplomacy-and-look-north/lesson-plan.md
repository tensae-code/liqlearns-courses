# Lesson plan — Fiji and the two Chinas — chequebook diplomacy and Look North

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Fiji and the two Chinas — chequebook diplomacy and Look North
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Fiji, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Fiji must choose between the PRC (founded as the communist state) and the ROC; the first Chinese arrivals are recalled, trade and aid grow, the two Chinas compete for recognition with "cheque book diplomacy", and after 2006 Fiji adopts the Look North policy, sealed by the Prime Minister's 2013 visit to China
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.
