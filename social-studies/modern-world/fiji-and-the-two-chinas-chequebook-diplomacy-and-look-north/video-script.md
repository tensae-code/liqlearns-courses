# Video script — Fiji and the two Chinas — chequebook diplomacy and Look North

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fiji must choose between the PRC (founded as the communist state) and the ROC; the first Chinese arrivals are recalled, trade and aid grow, the two Chinas compete for recognition with "cheque book diplomacy", and after 2006 Fiji adopts the Look North policy, sealed by the Prime Minister's 2013 visit to China.
