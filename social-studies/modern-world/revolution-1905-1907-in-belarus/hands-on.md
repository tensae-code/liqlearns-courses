# Hands-on — The revolution of 1905–1907 in Belarus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The revolution of 1905–1907 in Belarus — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The revolution of 1905–1907 in Belarus, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
