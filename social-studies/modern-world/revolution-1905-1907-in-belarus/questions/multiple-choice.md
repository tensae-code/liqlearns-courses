# The revolution of 1905–1907 in Belarus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) The revolution of 1905–1907 in Belarus  b) The five gubernias — Belarus divided after the partitions  c) Economic development of Belarus, first half of the 19th century
**Answer: a) The revolution of 1905–1907 in Belarus** — this part tells the revolution of 1905–1907 in Belarus

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) pupils flag the October and December all-Russian political strikes, the towns where demonstrators were shot, the illegal teachers' congress and the towns hit by pogroms  b) pupils shade what each of the three partitions took and name the five gubernias Russia made of the Belarusian lands  c) pupils mark the first factories and sugar works, the great fairs, and Kiselyov's reform map of state-peasant allotments
**Answer: a)** — pupils flag the October and December all-Russian political strikes, the towns where demonstrators were shot, the illegal teachers' congress and the towns hit by pogroms
