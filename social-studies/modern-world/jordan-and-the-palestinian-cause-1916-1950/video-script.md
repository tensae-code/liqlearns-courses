# Video script — Jordan and the Palestinian cause, 1916–1950

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Jordan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Zionism organises at Basel in 1897; Britain plants settlements and arms the newcomers, and Palestine answers with the revolts of 1920, al-Buraq 1929 and the Great Revolt of 1936–39; Sharif Hussein refuses to sign Palestine away ("I do not accept…"); Amir Abdullah protests Balfour to Churchill in Jerusalem in 1921 and demands Palestine's and Transjordan's unity and Britain's wartime promises kept; the Peel map of 1937 proposes partition; 1948 brings war, the armistices of 1949, the West Bank under Jordan and Gaza under Egypt.
