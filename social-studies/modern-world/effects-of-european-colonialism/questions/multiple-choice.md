# The effects of European colonialism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The colonised lands paid in —
a) wealth, borders and lives  b) nothing  c) praise
**Answer: a) wealth, borders and lives** — they pay in wealth, borders and lives

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** While they paid, the colonisers —
a) grew rich  b) grew poor  c) left
**Answer: a) grew rich** — the colonisers grow rich

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The chapter lays the effects out —
a) item by item, as a bill  b) in one slogan  c) not at all
**Answer: a) item by item, as a bill** — the bill the chapter lays out item by item
