# Vietnam Dong Khoi And Special War — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 16, breaking the "Special War"
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Washington's Special War strategy fields Saigon troops under US command — "use Vietnamese to fight Vietnamese" — behind strategic hamlets, helicopters and armoured vehicles, promising pacification in eighteen months; it breaks step by step: the 1962 sweeps are defeated and the hamlets smashed, at Ấp Bắc on 2 January 1963 over 2,000 Saigon troops under US advisers are beaten (and "Emulate Ấp Bắc" sweeps the South), and An Lão, Ba Gia and Đồng Xoài (1964–65) end the strategy

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 8 A divided country, 1954–1960: the US replaces France behind Ngô Đình Diệm, aiming to partition Vietnam permanently; the North completes land reform and heals its wounds while the South demands the Geneva elections, preserves its forces, and — after the 15th Central Conference (1959) rules there is no road but revolutionary violence — rises in the Đồng khởi movement from Vĩnh Thạnh and Trà Bồng to Bến Tre (Jan 1960), whose women form the famous "long-haired army"; victory shifts the South from conserving forces to the offensive and births the National Liberation Front (20-12-1960)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — village uprisings with coconut-thorn spears crack the client state open and a front is born

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 8 "Special War" defeated, 1961–1965: Washington's Staley–Taylor plan fights Vietnamese with Vietnamese — advisers, aid, "strategic hamlets", helicopter and armoured assault tactics; the Liberation Army is unified (15-2-1961), Buddhist protest shakes the cities, the hamlets are smashed, and Ấp Bắc (Jan 1963) proves the new tactics can be beaten; Bình Giã, An Lão and Đồng Xoài (1964–65) bankrupt the strategy outright
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a war designed to be fought by proxies collapses when the proxies' hamlets become the revolution's recruiting grounds
