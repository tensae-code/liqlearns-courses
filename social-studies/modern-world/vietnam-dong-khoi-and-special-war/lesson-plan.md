# Lesson plan — Vietnam Dong Khoi And Special War

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Dong Khoi And Special War
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Washington's Special War strategy fields Saigon troops under US command — "use Vietnamese to fight Vietnamese" — behind strategic hamlets, helicopters and armoured vehicles, promising pacification in eighteen months; it breaks step by step: the 1962 sweeps are defeated and the hamlets smashed, at Ấp Bắc on 2 January 1963 over 2,000 Saigon troops under US advisers are beaten (and "Emulate Ấp Bắc" sweeps the South), and An Lão, Ba Gia and Đồng Xoài (1964–65) end the strategy

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 16, breaking the "Special War"** — Washington's Special War strategy fields Saigon troops under US command — "use Vietnamese to fight Vietnamese" — behind strategic hamlets, helicopters and armoured vehicles, promising pacification in eighteen months; it breaks step by step: the 1962 sweeps are defeated and the hamlets smashed, at Ấp Bắc on 2 January 1963 over 2,000 Saigon troops under US advisers are beaten (and "Emulate Ấp Bắc" sweeps the South), and An Lão, Ba Gia and Đồng Xoài (1964–65) end the strategy
2. **Bài 8 A divided country, 1954–1960: the US replaces France behind Ngô Đình Diệm, aiming to partition Vietnam permanently; the North completes land reform and heals its wounds while the South demands the Geneva elections, preserves its forces, and — after the 15th Central Conference (1959) rules there is no road but revolutionary violence — rises in the Đồng khởi movement from Vĩnh Thạnh and Trà Bồng to Bến Tre (Jan 1960), whose women form the famous "long-haired army"; victory shifts the South from conserving forces to the offensive and births the National Liberation Front (20-12-1960)** — village uprisings with coconut-thorn spears crack the client state open and a front is born
3. **Bài 8 "Special War" defeated, 1961–1965: Washington's Staley–Taylor plan fights Vietnamese with Vietnamese — advisers, aid, "strategic hamlets", helicopter and armoured assault tactics; the Liberation Army is unified (15-2-1961), Buddhist protest shakes the cities, the hamlets are smashed, and Ấp Bắc (Jan 1963) proves the new tactics can be beaten; Bình Giã, An Lão and Đồng Xoài (1964–65) bankrupt the strategy outright** — a war designed to be fought by proxies collapses when the proxies' hamlets become the revolution's recruiting grounds
