# Video script — Fiji's legal system — from chiefs to courts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The earliest iTaukei rely on their chiefs' decision-making; once the British colony begins, courts replace the chief's word, and the unit traces that handover as the root of Fiji's modern legal order.
