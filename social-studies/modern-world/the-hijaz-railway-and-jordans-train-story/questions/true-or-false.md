# The Hijaz Railway and Jordan's train story — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers and now runs tourist trips through Jordan's stations.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 4 Lesson 2: development of trade in my homeland, opening story

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الثالث، الفصل الدراسي الثاني (Social Studies, Grade 3, Term 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 4 Lesson 2: development of trade in my homeland, opening story
