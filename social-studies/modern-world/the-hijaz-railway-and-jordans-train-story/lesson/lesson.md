# The Hijaz Railway and Jordan's train story

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Unit 4 Lesson 2: development of trade in my homeland, opening story

Faisal visits Amman's Hijaz railway station with his family and his grandfather tells how the old train once carried goods and travellers and now runs tourist trips through Jordan's stations

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Unit 2 Lesson 5: Jordan under Ottoman rule

East Jordan stays in Damascus's orbit from first to last; Abdülhamid II drives the Hijaz Railway from Damascus toward Madinah across Jordanian stations, and the Shami Hajj road makes the land an economic lifeline

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — Unit 3 Lesson 2, the railway part

the class traces the Hijaz Railway on the map through Jordan's stations — Damascus, Daraa, Mafraq, Zarqa, Amman, Qatrana, Ma'an, Batn al-Ghul, Aqaba's branch, al-Mudawara, Tabuk, Mada'in Salih, al-Ula, Medina — the iron road that replaced the forty-day Hajj caravan

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — Unit 3 Lesson 2, the railway part

the Hijaz Railway map is traced through Jordan as in the vocational book — Qatrana, Ma'an, Batn al-Ghul and the Aqaba branch among its stations — the line along which the Circassian and Chechen villages of the previous lesson were planted

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
