# Video script — Exploration — Portugal and the sea routes to India

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Portugal sails first.
King Henry pays for the voyages.
He opens a school for sailors and map-makers.
Azores. Madeira. Cape Verde.
In 1498 da Gama reaches India by sea.
In 1500 Cabral lands in Brazil.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Voyages in depth — joining the world, at a cost. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 telling sails the whole circuit: Dias rounding Africa, da Gama reaching India, Columbus crossing to the Americas, Magellan's ships circling the earth. The names to hold: Columbian exchange, da Gama.
