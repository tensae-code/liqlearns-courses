# Exploration — Portugal and the sea routes to India — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Middle Ages, European geographic knowledge was confined to Europe, North Africa and West Asia.
**Answer: True** — That small known world is where the story starts.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Da Gama reached India by crossing the Atlantic.
**Answer: False** — He sailed around Africa to the Indian Ocean.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cabral returned to Portugal in 1501.
**Answer: True** — Brazil in 1500, Calicut, home in 1501.
