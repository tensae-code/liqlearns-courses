# Exploration — Portugal and the sea routes to India — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The country that pioneered the great exploration of the 15th and 16th centuries was —
a) Spain  b) Portugal  c) England  d) France
**Answer: b) Portugal** — Portugal, in south-west Europe, led the way.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Henry the Navigator funded voyages toward —
a) The Atlantic and Indian oceans and Africa's coasts  b) Only the Mediterranean  c) Only northern Europe  d) Inland rivers only
**Answer: a) The Atlantic and Indian oceans and Africa's coasts** — His money sent ships toward both oceans and down Africa's coasts.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Henry's school raised the skills of —
a) Farmers  b) Explorers, sailors, map-makers and astronomers  c) Soldiers only  d) Priests only
**Answer: b) Explorers, sailors, map-makers and astronomers** — All four crafts of exploration learned together there.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Vasco da Gama reached Calicut in —
a) 1492  b) 1497  c) 1498  d) 1501
**Answer: c) 1498** — He set out in 1497 and reached Calicut in 1498.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Pedro Cabral, landing in Brazil in 1500, sailed with —
a) Four ships  b) Five ships  c) Thirteen ships  d) One ship
**Answer: c) Thirteen ships** — Cabral's fleet was thirteen ships; da Gama's was four.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Columbian exchange?
a) the movement of plants, animals, peoples and diseases between hemispheres
b) the Portuguese captain who reached India by sea
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: a) the movement of plants, animals, peoples and diseases between hemispheres**
