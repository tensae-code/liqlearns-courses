# Exploration — Portugal and the sea routes to India — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Henry the Navigator's school do?
**Answer:** It was a place where explorers, sailors, map-makers and astronomers raised their skills, so voyages built on each other's knowledge.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does Cabral's voyage matter twice over?
**Answer:** He laid the foundation of Brazil as a Portuguese overseas possession, and he also rounded Africa and reached Calicut in India.
