# Hands-on — Exploration — Portugal and the sea routes to India

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Stepping-stone map
Draw a rough Atlantic and Africa. Mark the Azores, Madeira and Cape Verde as stepping stones, then draw da Gama's line around Africa to Calicut.

## Four ships, thirteen ships
Cut four small paper ships and thirteen small paper ships. Sail the four to 'India' on your map, the thirteen to 'Brazil' first. The numbers themselves tell you which fleet is which.


## Grade 11 depth — Voyages in depth — joining the world, at a cost

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
