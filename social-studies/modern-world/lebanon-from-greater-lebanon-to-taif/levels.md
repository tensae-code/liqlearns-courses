## Sub lesson 1 — Lebanon — from Greater Lebanon to Taif
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — France cut Lebanon from Syria and Gouraud proclaimed Greater Lebanon in 1920; after the 1926 constitution and the national government of Bishara al-Khoury and Riad al-Solh in 1943, France arrested the president and ministers, the people rose, and Lebanon won independence, completed on 31 December 1946; the lesson follows the civil wars of 1958 and 1975 — the Syrian army's entry, the Israeli invasions and the Sabra and Shatila massacre — to the Taif agreement of 1989.
