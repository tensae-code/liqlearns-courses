# Lebanon — from Greater Lebanon to Taif

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Cut from Syria in 1920, Lebanon wins independence by 1946, then survives civil wars and invasions to the Taif agreement of 1989. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lebanon — from Greater Lebanon to Taif

France cut Lebanon from Syria and Gouraud proclaimed Greater Lebanon in 1920; after the 1926 constitution and the national government of Bishara al-Khoury and Riad al-Solh in 1943, France arrested the president and ministers, the people rose, and Lebanon won independence, completed on 31 December 1946; the lesson follows the civil wars of 1958 and 1975 — the Syrian army's entry, the Israeli invasions and the Sabra and Shatila massacre — to the Taif agreement of 1989.
