# Video script — Switzerland — Uschi Waser and the Yenish Children of the Road

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Switzerland's *Sorge oder Zwang?* set tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

She was seven months old when the police took her.



The scheme was called Children of the Road. Its aim: end the travelling life by taking its children.



Twenty-two moves in thirteen years. In court, her own files spoke against her.



As an adult, she finally read them herself.
