# Switzerland — Uschi Waser and the Yenish Children of the Road — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ursula Waser was taken by police at —
a) Seven months old  b) Seven years old  c) Thirteen years old
**Answer: a) Seven months old**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In 13 years, Ursula was moved —
a) 22 times  b) Twice  c) Never
**Answer: a) 22 times**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Children of the Road scheme took, in all —
a) 586 Yenish children  b) 58 children  c) Only Graubünden's children
**Answer: a) 586 Yenish children**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Siegfried's office recorded Ursula's childhood on —
a) Green index cards  b) A school report  c) Police photographs only
**Answer: a) Green index cards**
