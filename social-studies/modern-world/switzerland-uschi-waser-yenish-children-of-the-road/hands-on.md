# Hands-on — Switzerland — Uschi Waser and the Yenish Children of the Road

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## 22 moves
Draw 22 small boxes in a row — one for each of Ursula's moves in 13 years. Under the row, write what a child needs that 22 moves make impossible.

## Apology audit
The federal president and Pro Juventute apologised in 1986/87. Write what a real apology for the scheme would have to name: who acted, who was harmed, and what was taken.
