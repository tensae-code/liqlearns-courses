## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 11, the Paris Commune: the first workers' state
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — after France's collapse in the Franco-Prussian war, the bourgeois government signs a humiliating peace while Paris's people resolve to defend the fatherland; on 18 March 1871 National Guard battalions and the people seize the capital — the first proletarian revolution in the world; the elected Commune dissolves the old army and police, separates church from state and bans religious teaching in schools, hands the factories of owners who fled Paris to their workers, and forbids wage fines — it lives only 72 days, to 28 May 1871, but stands as the image of a new kind of state
