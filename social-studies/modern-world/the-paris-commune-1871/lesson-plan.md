# Lesson plan — The Paris Commune 1871

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Paris Commune 1871
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** after France's collapse in the Franco-Prussian war, the bourgeois government signs a humiliating peace while Paris's people resolve to defend the fatherland; on 18 March 1871 National Guard battalions and the people seize the capital — the first proletarian revolution in the world; the elected Commune dissolves the old army and police, separates church from state and bans religious teaching in schools, hands the factories of owners who fled Paris to their workers, and forbids wage fines — it lives only 72 days, to 28 May 1871, but stands as the image of a new kind of state
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
