# Hands-on — Bolivia — the Gas War of 2003 and nationalisation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bolivia — the Gas War of 2003 and nationalisation — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, La Guerra del Gas, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
