# England's constitutional revolutions — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** England bound its crown with —
a) Habeas corpus and a bill of rights  b) The Edict of Nantes  c) The Ordinance of Villers-Cotterêts
**Answer: a) Habeas corpus and a bill of rights** — Habeas Corpus et Bill of Rights.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before binding it, England —
a) Tried its king  b) Crowned two kings at once  c) Abolished law
**Answer: a) Tried its king** — England tries its king.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Voltaire praised English liberty to —
a) Shame France  b) Praise Versailles  c) End all debate
**Answer: a) Shame France** — he praises it to shame France.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The manual then asks —
a) How free England really was  b) Nothing further  c) Only about trade
**Answer: a) How free England really was** — nuancer le modèle anglais — how free was it really?
