# Colonised Peoples Resist Latin America And India — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** As the powers carve up the world, colonised peoples rise — Latin America wins independence and Asia and Africa resist.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Colonial expansion and national liberation

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Colonial expansion and national liberation

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Orphaned in childhood, Bolívar swears his hands will not rest until Spain's chains break; in 1819 he crosses the snow-covered Andes, frees Colombia, Venezuela and Ecuador, and with San Martín, liberator of Argentina, Chile and Peru, ends Spanish America.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Colonial peoples resist — Latin America

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *世界历史 九年级 下册 (World History, Grade 9, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Colonial peoples resist — Latin America

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** India's sepoys and the warrior queen of Jhansi rise against the British East India Company and are crushed, but the rising opens India's long road to freedom.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Colonial peoples resist — India

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *世界历史 九年级 下册 (World History, Grade 9, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Colonial peoples resist — India

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** From Haiti and Bolívar to India's early congresses, the colonised world answers the colonial system with independence movements on three continents.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Asian, African and Latin American independence movements

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（下） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, lower volume — world history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Asian, African and Latin American independence movements
