## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Colonial expansion and national liberation — as the powers carve up the world, colonised peoples rise — Latin America wins independence and Asia and Africa resist. Colonial peoples resist — Latin America — orphaned in childhood, Bolívar swears his hands will not rest until Spain's chains break; in 1819 he crosses the snow-covered Andes, frees Colombia, Venezuela and Ecuador, and with San Martín, liberator of Argentina, Chile and Peru, ends Spanish America. Colonial peoples resist — India — India's sepoys and the warrior queen of Jhansi rise against the British East India Company and are crushed, but the rising opens India's long road to freedom. Asian, African and Latin American independence movements — from Haiti and Bolívar to India's early congresses, the colonised world answers the colonial system with independence movements on three continents.
