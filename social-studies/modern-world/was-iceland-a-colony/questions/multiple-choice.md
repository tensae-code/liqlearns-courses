# Was Iceland a colony? — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The worksheet asks pupils about —
a) Their own country, Iceland  b) Africa only  c) Italy
**Answer: a) Their own country, Iceland** — pupils turn the question on their own country.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Iceland was ruled from —
a) Denmark  b) Britain  c) Norway in this period
**Answer: a) Denmark** — their own country under Denmark.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Before asking the question, pupils had studied —
a) Empire (imperialism)  b) The Viking Age  c) Farming
**Answer: a) Empire (imperialism)** — after studying empire.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The question they must weigh is whether Iceland was a —
a) Colony  b) Republic in 1703  c) Sea empire
**Answer: a) Colony** — was Iceland a colony?
