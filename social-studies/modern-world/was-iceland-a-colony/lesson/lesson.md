# Was Iceland a colony?

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: After studying empire, pupils turn the definition of "colony" on their own country: was Iceland, under Denmark, a colony? As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Turning the definition on home

after studying empire, pupils turn the definition of "colony" on their own country under Denmark (Verkefnablað 24: Var Ísland nýlenda?)

In your mind's eye: a classroom debate, with a map of Iceland and Denmark on the wall. Say what question the class is weighing.

## Sub lesson 2 — Weighing Iceland under Denmark

the class weighs Iceland's life under Denmark against what a colony is (Verkefnablað 24)
