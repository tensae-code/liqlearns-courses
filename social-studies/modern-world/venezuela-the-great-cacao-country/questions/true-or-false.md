# Venezuela The Great Cacao Country — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cacao plantation owners are rich in land and power, and their crop makes colonial Venezuela's fortune and its ruling class.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in When Venezuela was a country of great cacaos

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 2do grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about When Venezuela was a country of great cacaos

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Cacao, and the colony's other products, organise work, trade and wealth for Spain's benefit.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The colonial economy

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela y de Nuestra América — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The colonial economy
