# Yemen — divided by empire, united 1990

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Split by Britain's Aden line in 1904, Yemen's north wins a republic in 1962 and its south independence in 1967 — and the two unite in 1990. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Yemen — divided by empire, united 1990

Britain seized Aden in 1839 as a station on the India route and a 1904 border line split Yemen in two; the Mutawakkilite kingdom in the north fell to the revolution of 1962 and a republic scarred by civil war, while the south won independence in 1967 after the revolt of 1963; the two Yemens united in 1990 under Ali Abdullah Saleh, who stepped down after the popular revolution of 2011.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Yemen — divided by empire, united 1990. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
