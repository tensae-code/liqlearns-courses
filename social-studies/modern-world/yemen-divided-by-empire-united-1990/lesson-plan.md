# Lesson plan — Yemen — divided by empire, united 1990

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Yemen — divided by empire, united 1990
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Syria, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Split by Britain's Aden line in 1904, Yemen's north wins a republic in 1962 and its south independence in 1967 — and the two unite in 1990.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Yemen — divided by empire, united 1990** — Britain seized Aden in 1839 as a station on the India route and a 1904 border line split Yemen in two; the Mutawakkilite kingdom in the north fell to the revolution of 1962 and a republic scarred by civil war, while the south won independence in 1967 after the revolt of 1963; the two Yemens united in 1990 under Ali Abdullah Saleh, who stepped down after the popular revolution of 2011.
