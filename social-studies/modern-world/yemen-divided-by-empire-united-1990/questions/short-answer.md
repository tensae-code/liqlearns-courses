# Yemen — divided by empire, united 1990 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Yemen — divided by empire, united 1990.
**Answer: Britain seized Aden in 1839 as a station on the India route and a 1904 border line split Yemen in two; the Mutawakkilite kingdom in the north fell to the revolution of 1962 and a republic scarred by civil war, while the south won independence in 1967 after the revolt of 1963; the two Yemens united in 1990 under Ali Abdullah Saleh, who stepped down after the popular revolution of 2011.
