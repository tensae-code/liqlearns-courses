# Hands-on — Mirwais Hotak and the Kandahar uprising

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Mirwais Hotak and the Kandahar uprising — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
