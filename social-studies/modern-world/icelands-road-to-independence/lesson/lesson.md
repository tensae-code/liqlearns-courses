# Iceland's road to independence

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Jón Sigurðsson leads Iceland's peaceful independence struggle; home rule and sovereignty in 1918 follow — and at last, in 1944, the republic is proclaimed at Þingvellir. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Jón Sigurðsson and the Fjallkona

Jón Sigurðsson leads Iceland's peaceful independence struggle, and the Fjallkona becomes the nation's face (Verkefnablöð 19–20: Var Jón Sigurðsson frelsishetja?; Fjallkonan as national symbol)

In your mind's eye: the republic proclaimed at Þingvellir in 1944, crowds on the old assembly field. Say what was proclaimed and where.
