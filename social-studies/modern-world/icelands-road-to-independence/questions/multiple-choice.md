# Iceland's road to independence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Iceland's independence struggle was led by —
a) Jón Sigurðsson  b) Jørgen Jørgensen  c) Bismarck
**Answer: a) Jón Sigurðsson** — Jón Sigurðsson leads the struggle.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Fjallkona became —
a) The nation's face, a national symbol  b) A queen  c) A ship's name only
**Answer: a) The nation's face, a national symbol** — the Fjallkona becomes the nation's face.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Iceland won sovereignty in —
a) 1918  b) 1809  c) 1703
**Answer: a) 1918** — sovereignty in 1918.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The republic was proclaimed in 1944 at —
a) Þingvellir  b) Reykjavík harbour  c) Skagafjörður
**Answer: a) Þingvellir** — the republic proclaimed at Þingvellir in 1944.
