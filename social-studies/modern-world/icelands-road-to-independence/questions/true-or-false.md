# Iceland's road to independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jón Sigurðsson led Iceland's independence struggle by war.
**Answer: False** — he led a peaceful struggle.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The Icelandic republic was proclaimed at Þingvellir in 1944.
**Answer: True** — the republic proclaimed at Þingvellir in 1944.
