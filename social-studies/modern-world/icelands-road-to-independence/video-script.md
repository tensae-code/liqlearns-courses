# Video script — Iceland's road to independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Iceland book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Jón Sigurðsson leads Iceland's peaceful independence struggle, and the Fjallkona becomes the nation's face (Verkefnablöð 19–20: Var Jón Sigurðsson frelsishetja?; Fjallkonan as national symbol)

home rule, then sovereignty in 1918, mark the road's middle steps (Þjóðfrelsi og lýðræði, student book pp. 132–151)

at last the republic is proclaimed at Þingvellir in 1944 (Leiðin til lýðveldis, student book pp. 146–151)

Hold that story: Jón Sigurðsson leads Iceland's peaceful independence struggle; home rule and sovereignty in 1918 follow — and at last, in 1944, the republic is proclaimed at Þingvellir.
