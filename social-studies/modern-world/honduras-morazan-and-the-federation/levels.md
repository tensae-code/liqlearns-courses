This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 26 Nuestro país después de la independencia: Morazán
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Morazán, born in Tegucigalpa in 1792, wins the five-hour Battle of La Trinidad (11 November 1827) against Justo Milla, retakes the country, presides over the Federation, abolishes the tithe and sets civil marriage — then Rafael Carrera brings him down and he is shot in San José on 15 September 1842

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 26: anexión, Federación y Estado
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — fearing invasion, the provinces annex to Iturbide's Mexico; on 1 July 1823 they declare absolute independence and form the Central American Federation under Arce, liberals against conservatives; in 1838 the Federation dissolves and Honduras becomes a State with its first constitution

## Sub lesson 3 — the Federal Republic 1825–1839
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the five provinces united with free press and curbed church power, but fiscal deficit, regionalism, church boycott and Guatemala's rivalry killed it; Morazán, proclaimed liberator of Costa Rica, was shot in San José on 15 September 1842, and thirty years of conservative reaction and caudillismo followed
