**Answer: Fearing France, Britain takes the Dutch coast in 1796 on the strength of a prince's letter the islanders nickname "pittu", and by 1815 rules the whole island for 133 years (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: Governor North's harsh taxes provoke revolt, Thomas Maitland (1805–1812) tours every district, calms the coast, restores the cinnamon monopoly North abolished, and fences British power in law (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
