# Sri Lanka — The British take the maritime provinces — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The British take the coast, 1796–1802

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G9 History Tamil 2019* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The British take the coast, 1796–1802

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide frames 1796, the cession of Kandy in 1815 and the struggles of 1818 and 1848 as one story of a sovereignty lost in stages.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in How British authority was established (competency level 2.1)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G9 History Teacher Guide NIE 2018* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about How British authority was established (competency level 2.1)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Fearing France, Britain takes the Dutch coast in 1796 on the strength of a prince's letter the islanders nickname "pittu", and by 1815 rules the whole island for 133 years.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in How the British came

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *G10 Social Education and History Tamil 1999* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about How the British came

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Governor North's harsh taxes provoke revolt, Thomas Maitland (1805–1812) tours every district, calms the coast, restores the cinnamon monopoly North abolished, and fences British power in law.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in British rule established: North and Maitland

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *G11 History Tamil 1999* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about British rule established: North and Maitland
