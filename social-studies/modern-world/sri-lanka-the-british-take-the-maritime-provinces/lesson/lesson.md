# Sri Lanka — The British take the maritime provinces

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The British take the coast, 1796–1802

Britain seizes the Dutch ports in 1796, the failed double government of Company and Crown gives way in 1802 to a Crown Colony under Governor North

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — How British authority was established (competency level 2.1)

The guide frames 1796, the cession of Kandy in 1815 and the struggles of 1818 and 1848 as one story of a sovereignty lost in stages

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — How the British came

Fearing France, Britain takes the Dutch coast in 1796 on the strength of a prince's letter the islanders nickname "pittu", and by 1815 rules the whole island for 133 years

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — British rule established: North and Maitland

Governor North's harsh taxes provoke revolt, Thomas Maitland (1805–1812) tours every district, calms the coast, restores the cinnamon monopoly North abolished, and fences British power in law

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
