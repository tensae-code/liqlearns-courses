## Sub lesson 3 — How the British came
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — fearing France, Britain takes the Dutch coast in 1796 on the strength of a prince's letter the islanders nickname "pittu", and by 1815 rules the whole island for 133 years

## Sub lesson 4 — British rule established: North and Maitland
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Governor North's harsh taxes provoke revolt, Thomas Maitland (1805–1812) tours every district, calms the coast, restores the cinnamon monopoly North abolished, and fences British power in law
