# Hands-on — Sri Lanka — The British take the maritime provinces

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sri Lanka — The British take the maritime provinces — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The British take the coast, 1796–1802, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
