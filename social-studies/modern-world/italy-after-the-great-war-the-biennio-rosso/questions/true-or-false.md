# Italy After The Great War The Biennio Rosso — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** inflation, unemployment and land hunger (Cordova's Storiografia on Calabria's occupations) push workers and peasants to strikes and factory occupations — the liberal state watches its authority drain away.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§1–2 The postwar crisis; The "red two years"

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§1–2 The postwar crisis; The "red two years"

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
