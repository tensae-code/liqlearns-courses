## Sub lesson 1 — Akbar — the greatest Mughal emperor

Crowned at fourteen after the Second Battle of Panipat (1556), Akbar welds northern India into one empire, abolishes the jizya, marries Rajput princesses, debates all faiths in the Ibadat Khana and proclaims Din-e-Ilahi in 1582 — the book names him the greatest Mughal emperor.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
