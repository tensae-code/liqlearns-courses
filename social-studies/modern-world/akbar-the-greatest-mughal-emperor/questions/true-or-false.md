# Akbar — the greatest Mughal emperor — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Crowned at fourteen after the Second Battle of Panipat (1556), Akbar welds northern India into one empire, abolishes the jizya, marries Rajput princesses, debates all faiths in the Ibadat Khana and proclaims Din-e-Ilahi in 1582 — the book names him the greatest Mughal emperor.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 4: Akbar (1556–1605)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History 7 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Akbar — the greatest Mughal emperor
