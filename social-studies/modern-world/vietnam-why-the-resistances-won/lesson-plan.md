# Lesson plan — Vietnam Why The Resistances Won

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Why The Resistances Won
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the same country wins when its people are one bloc and loses when its court fights alone
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 7 Why some resistances won and others failed: victories rest on a just cause, all-people unity (Bình Than conference 1282; the Diên Hồng elders shouting "Fight!" in 1285), striking first (Lý Thường Kiệt 1075) or fast (Quang Trung 1789), and gifted commanders; failures — An Dương Vương against Triệu Đà in 179 BCE, the Hồ against the Ming 1406–1407, the Nguyễn court signing away 1862–1884 — come from divided leadership and people's hearts not following, as Hồ Nguyên Trừng warned** — the same country wins when its people are one bloc and loses when its court fights alone
2. **Bài 8 The lessons the uprisings leave: build forces (Lũng Nhai's gathering; Quang Trung's Nghệ An review and Thanh Hóa levy), build the great unity bloc ("use great righteousness to defeat cruelty" — a staff of landlord, scholar, noble, poor farmer and minority chief), and master a creative military art** — the textbook turns its own heroes into three transferable lessons for defending the country today
3. **Bài 7 Why the anti-French resistance won, and what it meant: subjective causes (the Party's correct line, the whole people's patriotism and creativity, the ever-widening rear) and objective ones (the Việt–Miên–Lào alliance, Chinese and Soviet help, French and world peace opinion); the meaning — French rule on Vietnamese soil ended completely, the North freed to build socialism, a heavy blow to post-war colonialism that heartened liberation movements across Asia, Africa and Latin America** — Hồ Chí Minh's line frames it — the first time a small weak colony beat a strong colonial power
4. **Bài 8 Why the anti-American resistance won, and what it meant: the Party's independent, creative line of two simultaneous revolutions, people's-war art joining military, political and diplomatic struggle, a patriotic people, an ever-growing northern rear, Indochinese solidarity and world support — including some 2.1 billion roubles of Soviet aid (1955–1975) and the American anti-war movement itself; the meaning — 21 years against the US and 30 of revolutionary war closed, the national democratic revolution completed countrywide, neo-colonialism dealt a collapse felt worldwide** — the textbook's verdict enters the Party's own documents — among the brightest pages of national history and a great feat of the twentieth century
