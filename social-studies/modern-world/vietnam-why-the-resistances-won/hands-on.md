# Hands-on — Vietnam Why The Resistances Won

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Why The Resistances Won — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 7 Why some resistances won and others failed: victories rest on a just cause, all-people unity (Bình Than conference 1282; the Diên Hồng elders shouting "Fight!" in 1285), striking first (Lý Thường Kiệt 1075) or fast (Quang Trung 1789), and gifted commanders; failures — An Dương Vương against Triệu Đà in 179 BCE, the Hồ against the Ming 1406–1407, the Nguyễn court signing away 1862–1884 — come from divided leadership and people's hearts not following, as Hồ Nguyên Trừng warned, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
