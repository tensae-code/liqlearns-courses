# Palestine under Ottoman rule — Zahir al-Umar and al-Jazzar — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Palestine is governed as sanjaks (Jerusalem's first among them), but local power keeps breaking away — Fakhr al-Din al-Ma'ni ruling Lebanon and northern Palestine from 1603, Zahir al-Umar al-Zaydani (1689–1775) building his Galilee state, and Ahmad Pasha al-Jazzar installed at Acre after crushing Zahir in 1775.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2 Lesson 4: Palestine in the Ottoman era — its rulers

**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 2 Lesson 4: Palestine in the Ottoman era — its rulers
