# The Reformation — Luther and the spread of Protestantism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What drove the Reformation?
**Answer:** The popes' and clergy's unlimited influence over political, economic and religious matters — and the selling of pardon as indulgence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the three lands the new faith spread to in this lesson and what formed in each.
**Answer:** France — Calvinist Protestantism (the Huguenots); England — the Anglican Church; Switzerland — the Swiss Reformed Church.
