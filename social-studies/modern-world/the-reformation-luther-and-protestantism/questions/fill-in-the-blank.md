# The Reformation — Luther and the spread of Protestantism — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Martin Luther was a Catholic monk and professor of theology at ____ University.
**Answer: Wittenberg**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In England the ____ Church formed; in Switzerland, the Swiss Reformed Church.
**Answer: Anglican**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'the Catholic Church's reforming answer to Protestantism' is ______.
**Answer: Counter-Reformation**
