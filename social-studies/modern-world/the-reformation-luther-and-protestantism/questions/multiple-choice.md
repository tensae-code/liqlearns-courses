# The Reformation — Luther and the spread of Protestantism — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Reformation was the movement to —
a) Join the Roman Catholic Church  b) Separate from the Roman Catholic Church  c) Abolish all religion  d) Move the Pope to Germany
**Answer: b) Separate from the Roman Catholic Church** — Separation from Rome is its definition in the book.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** 'Indulgence' was —
a) A tax on land  b) Money collected for the pardon of sins  c) A kind of prayer book  d) A church building
**Answer: b) Money collected for the pardon of sins** — Payment for pardon is what Luther denounced.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Luther preached that people are freed from sin by —
a) Giving money  b) Good deeds, not by giving money  c) Silence  d) Travel
**Answer: b) Good deeds, not by giving money** — Good deeds, not money — his answer to indulgence.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Luther posted his 95 theses on —
a) January 1, 1500  b) October 31, 1517  c) May 25, 1963  d) August 1519
**Answer: b) October 31, 1517** — October 31, 1517, on the Wittenberg church door.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Calvin's followers in France are called —
a) Huguenots  b) Jesuits  c) Anglicans  d) Navigator's men
**Answer: a) Huguenots** — The Huguenots are Calvin's French followers.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** What is indulgences?
a) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
b) the pardons whose sale provoked Luther's 1517 protest
c) the Catholic Church's reforming answer to Protestantism
**Answer: b) the pardons whose sale provoked Luther's 1517 protest**
