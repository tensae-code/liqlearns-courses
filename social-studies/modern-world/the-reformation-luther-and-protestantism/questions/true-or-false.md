# The Reformation — Luther and the spread of Protestantism — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Protest against the church had stirred earlier, in 14th-century England and Bohemia.
**Answer: True** — Luther's 1517 act formally founded the movement; stirrings came before.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Efforts to silence Luther succeeded and the movement ended.
**Answer: False** — They failed; he won many followers and the faith spread.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In many European countries the Reformation brought religious war.
**Answer: True** — The book names that cost plainly.
