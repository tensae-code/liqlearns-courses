# Video script — The Reformation — Luther and the spread of Protestantism

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

One church holds power over everything.
Pardon is sold for money — indulgence.
Luther protests: freed by good deeds, not money.
October 31, 1517: 95 theses on the door.
The faith spreads — and religious war follows.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The Reformation in depth — protest, split and wars of religion. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Luther's 1517 protest over indulgences opens the Grade 11 depth: a monk's objection becomes, through print and princely protection, a split of Western Christianity — Protestant churches rising, the Catholic Church answering with its own Counter-Reformation, and Europe fighting wars of religion over the division. The names to hold: indulgences, Counter-Reformation.
