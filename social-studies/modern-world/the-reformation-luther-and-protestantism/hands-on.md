# Hands-on — The Reformation — Luther and the spread of Protestantism

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Pin your theses
Write three short protest lines of your own about something unfair in daily life — a price, a rule — each one sentence, numbered like theses. Pin them to a board. Luther's power was in short, public, numbered claims.

## Map the spread
Draw Europe roughly. Mark Germany (Luther), France (Calvin, Huguenots), England (Anglican), Switzerland (Swiss Reformed). Four pins, four labels.
