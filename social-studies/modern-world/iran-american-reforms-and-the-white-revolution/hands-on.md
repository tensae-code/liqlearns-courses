# Hands-on — Iran — American reforms and the White Revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iran — American reforms and the White Revolution — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The grounds and aims of the American reforms in Iran, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
