# Nicaragua Us Interventions And Zeledon — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Intervenciones norteamericanas: primera intervención y Pactos Dawson
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — from 1909 the US intervenes directly, imposing governments; the Dawson Pacts hand Adolfo Díaz's Nicaragua's finances to US control

## Sub lesson 2 — La gesta de Benjamín Zeledón
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Zeledón refuses to surrender to the marines in 1912 and dies fighting; his body is paraded to frighten others — and instead inspires Sandino

## Sub lesson 3 — Situación antes de 1924 y el Lomazo de Emiliano Chamorro
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Chamorro's 1925 Lomazo coup seizes the Loma fortress and restarts civil conflict

## Sub lesson 4 — La Guerra Constitucionalista de 1926
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sacasa's constitutionalists rise against the coup regime, and their war brings the marines back in force

## Sub lesson 5 — Intervenciones norteamericanas 1909-1933
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Taft's Big Stick continues the Monroe Doctrine; US control is tabulated sector by sector — coffee, energy, railways and banking at 100 percent

## Sub lesson 6 — Los Pactos Dawson
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — US recognition is bought with pacts that script Nicaragua's politics — a constituent assembly, Estrada president, Díaz vice-president, Zelayistas excluded

## Sub lesson 7 — Tratado Bryan-Chamorro (1914)
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Nicaragua grants the US perpetual exclusive rights to an interoceanic canal by the San Juan river route, signed in Washington on 5 August 1914

## Sub lesson 8 — Pacto del Espino Negro (1927)
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — under a black-thorn tree at Tipitapa on 4 May 1927, Stimson makes Moncada lay down his nearly victorious constitutionalist army — one general refuses, and his refusal becomes Sandino's war

## Sub lesson 9 — La gesta heroica de Benjamín Zeledón
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — ordered to surrender in 1912, Zeledón answers that he wants no cowardly peace for his children and dies defending Nicaragua's honour — the example that guides Sandino

## Sub lesson 10 — La Guerra Constitucionalista (1926)
Sub lesson 10 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a violated constitution drives liberals to war in 1926; US intervention decides the outcome and prepares the Espino Negro settlement

## Sub lesson 11 — Las intervenciones norteamericanas 1909-1933
Sub lesson 11 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — marines land in 1912 to guard the imposed government of Díaz and Chamorro, occupy Corinto and Bluefields and stay, in changing forms, until 1933

## Sub lesson 12 — La gesta de Benjamín Zeledón, 1912
Sub lesson 12 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Zeledón's 800 artisans, professionals and peasants, badly armed and some barefoot, refuse the marines' offers of gold and honours; he signs his own death sentence rather than surrender at Coyotepe

## Sub lesson 13 — Los intereses norteamericanos en Nicaragua
Sub lesson 13 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — with Panama's canal built, Washington still holds Nicaragua militarily so no rival canal can compete; the 1917 Financial Plan puts every national revenue in a foreign-owned bank

## Sub lesson 14 — El Lomazo y la Guerra Constitucionalista
Sub lesson 14 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Chamorro's 1925 Lomazo coup makes President Solórzano his puppet; the constitutionalist war that answers it brings workers and peasants led by Sandino into national politics for the first time

## Sub lesson 15 — El Pacto del Espino Negro, 1927
Sub lesson 15 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — under a black-thorn tree at Tipitapa on 4 May 1927, Stimson makes Moncada's nearly victorious liberals surrender unconditionally to the US-imposed order
