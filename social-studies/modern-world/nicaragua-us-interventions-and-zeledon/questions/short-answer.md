# Nicaragua Us Interventions And Zeledon — short answer

**Answer: from 1909 the US intervenes directly, imposing governments; the Dawson Pacts hand Adolfo Díaz's Nicaragua's finances to US control (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
