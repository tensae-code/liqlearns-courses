# Lesson plan — Japan Bubble Lost Decades And Abenomics

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Japan Bubble Lost Decades And Abenomics
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the miracle economy spends thirty years learning that the fall can be as instructive as the rise
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Chuyên đề 2 Shock, bubble and stagnation (1973–today): the 1973 oil crisis cuts GDP by 1.23% in 1974 and forces oil dependence down from 99.7% to 34.2% by 1984; per-capita GDP passes America's in 1987, the bubble of 1986–1991 bursts into lost decades of closures and 5% unemployment, the 2008 crisis strikes the export economy, Abenomics (from 2012) restarts growth, and China takes the world's second economy place in 2010** — the miracle economy spends thirty years learning that the fall can be as instructive as the rise
