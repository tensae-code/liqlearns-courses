# Video script — Capitalism — private ownership, factories, new classes


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Western Europe, about the year 1800.

Smoke rises over one English town. Then another. Then another.

Look close. Before: a weaver at home, at his own loom.

Now: a great building. Rows of machines. Steam drives them all.

The looms at home go dark, one by one. The factory rows grow longer.

Two new crowds walk through the factory gate.

The owners — few, rich, holding the keys.

The workers — many, owning nothing but their hands, selling their work for wages.

That is capitalism remade by steam: private ownership, factories, new classes.

[Grade 11 depth scene — Early capitalism in depth — towns, banks and the price revolution. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
The Grade 11 book roots capitalism earlier than the factory: from about 1500, revived towns fill with merchants; merchant capital organises trade and production beyond the guilds; banking learns to move money across Europe; and American silver drives a price revolution through every market. The names to hold: merchant capital, price revolution.
