# Hands-on — Capitalism — private ownership, factories, new classes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two workshops
Draw the same product — a shirt — being made twice: once at a home loom, once on a factory line. Label who owns the tools in each drawing, and who is paid, and how.

## Owners and workers
With a partner, list five things in your town. For each, say: who owns the means of making or selling it, and who works for wages? That sorting is this lesson's definition at work.
