# Capitalism — private ownership, factories, new classes — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Capitalism became dominant in the West after the collapse of ____.
**Answer: feudalism**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The ____ system of home workshops was replaced by the factory system.
**Answer: domestic**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the guide's terms, the owners of the factors of production are the ____.
**Answer: bourgeoisie**
