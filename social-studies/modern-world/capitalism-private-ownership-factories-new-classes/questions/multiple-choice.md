# Capitalism — private ownership, factories, new classes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Capitalism rests above all on —
a) State ownership of all land  b) Private ownership of most means of production  c) Equal shares for every worker  d) Rule by the church
**Answer: b) Private ownership of most means of production** — Private ownership of the means of production is the system's foundation.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Capitalism became dominant in the West as —
a) Feudalism collapsed  b) The Roman Empire fell  c) Steam was invented in Asia  d) Kings banned trade
**Answer: a) Feudalism collapsed** — The book ties capitalism's dominance to feudalism's collapse.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Industrial Revolution began in —
a) France  b) Belgium  c) 18th-century England  d) The USA
**Answer: c) 18th-century England** — It began in England, then spread to Belgium, France and the German states.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the factory age, coal and steam replaced —
a) Horses and carts  b) Wind and water  c) Wood and charcoal only  d) Human hands entirely
**Answer: b) Wind and water** — Coal and steam replaced wind and water power.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The factory system replaced —
a) The guild system of Rome  b) Farming  c) The domestic system of home workshops  d) All trade
**Answer: c) The domestic system of home workshops** — Factories replaced home workshops — the domestic system.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In the teacher guide's definition, the proletariat —
a) Own the factories  b) Sell their labour for wages  c) Rule the government  d) Own the land
**Answer: b) Sell their labour for wages** — The proletariat sell their labour for wages; the bourgeoisie own the factors of production.

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** What is merchant capital?
a) the inflation driven by American silver after 1500
b) wealth organised for trade and production beyond the guilds
c) a detail belonging to a different lesson in this course
**Answer: b) wealth organised for trade and production beyond the guilds**
**8. (Sub lesson 5)** In Marxian theory, the owner's profit comes from —
a) Rent on land  b) The surplus value created by the worker's labour beyond the wage  c) Taxes  d) Foreign trade only
**Answer: b) The surplus value created by the worker's labour beyond the wage — The difference between the value labour creates and the wage is kept by the owner.**

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** The Grade 12 book places Marxian economics beside capitalism's features as —
a) A footnote  b) Its great critique  c) A rival religion  d) A colonial policy
**Answer: b) Its great critique — The placement's own words: the system's great critique.**
**10. (Unit 1 review — Grade 12)** Unit 1 review: Which pair of forces does the unit set against each other inside capitalist society?
a) King and church  b) The bourgeoisie and the proletariat  c) Farmers and herders  d) North and South
**Answer: b) The bourgeoisie and the proletariat — The unit's theory pair: owners and wage-workers.**

**11. (Unit 1 review — Grade 12)** Unit 1 review: Italy and Germany both unified in —
a) 1815  b) 1848  c) 1871  d) 1914
**Answer: c) 1871 — Both unifications complete in 1871 — Rome taken 1870, the German Empire proclaimed 1871.**

**12. (Unit 1 review — Grade 12)** Unit 1 review: The Eastern Question concerned —
a) China's coast  b) The Ottoman Empire's decline and the Balkans  c) Canada's borders  d) Japan's opening
**Answer: b) The Ottoman Empire's decline and the Balkans — Great-power rivalry over the Ottoman remains.**
