# Capitalism — private ownership, factories, new classes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under capitalism, most means of production are owned by private people.
**Answer: True** — That is the system's foundation stone.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Industrial Revolution left society's classes unchanged.
**Answer: False** — It created new classes — above all industrial capitalists and factory workers.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The teacher guide says government should direct all production under capitalism.
**Answer: False** — The guide says the opposite: no restriction or interference by government.
