# Capitalism — private ownership, factories, new classes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two new socio-economic classes the Industrial Revolution brought forward.
**Answer:** Industrial capitalists, who owned the factories, and factory workers, who worked in them for wages.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** State the teacher guide's definition of capitalism in one sentence.
**Answer:** A socio-economic system in which a few rich private people own the resources and the factors of production, production is for profit, the proletariat sell their labour for wages, and trade runs free with no government interference.

**4. (Sub lesson 5)** In your own words: why does the Grade 12 book teach the Marxian foundations first, before the colonial and Cold War units?
**Answer: So that colonisation, the Russian Revolution and the Cold War can be analysed with named concepts, not just narrated — theory before the history it claims to explain.**
**5. (Unit 1 review — Grade 12)** Unit 1 review: State, in two sentences, the Marxian critique of capitalism as the unit presents it.
**Answer: Profit is surplus value taken from labour; the two antagonistic classes' struggle drives history toward socialism.**

**6. (Unit 1 review — Grade 12)** Unit 1 review: Why is 1912–13 called the fuse toward 1914?
**Answer: The Balkan Wars destroyed Ottoman power in Europe, emboldened Balkan nationalism and set Austria-Hungary and Russia against each other over Serbia.**
