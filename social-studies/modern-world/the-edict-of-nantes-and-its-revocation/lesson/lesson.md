# The Edict of Nantes and its revocation

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Henri IV grants France's Protestants peace in the Edict of Nantes in 1598 — and Louis XIV tears it up in 1685. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 1598 — Henri IV grants peace

Henri IV grants Protestants peace in 1598 — the Edict of Nantes ends the wars by giving the minority a protected place.

[Picture slot — a picture of Henri IV signing the Edict of Nantes in 1598 on one side and Louis XIV revoking it in 1685 on the other — the student says what was granted and what was taken back]

## Sub lesson 2 — 1685 — Louis XIV tears it up

Louis XIV tears it up in 1685 — the revocation takes back in one reign what 1598 had granted.
