# Video script — The Edict of Nantes and its revocation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the France book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Henri IV grants Protestants peace in 1598 — the Edict of Nantes ends the wars by giving the minority a protected place.

Louis XIV tears it up in 1685 — the revocation takes back in one reign what 1598 had granted.

Hold that story: Henri IV grants France's Protestants peace in the Edict of Nantes in 1598 — and Louis XIV tears it up in 1685.
