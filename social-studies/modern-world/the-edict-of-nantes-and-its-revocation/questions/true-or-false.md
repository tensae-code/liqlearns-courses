# The Edict of Nantes and its revocation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Edict of Nantes dates to 1598.
**Answer: True** — the beat's date.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Henri IV himself revoked the Edict of Nantes in 1685.
**Answer: False** — Louis XIV revoked it in 1685.
