# The Edict of Nantes and its revocation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Edict of Nantes was granted in —
a) 1598  b) 1685  c) 1550
**Answer: a) 1598** — Henri IV grants it in 1598.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** It granted peace to —
a) France's Protestants  b) Only foreign merchants  c) The Venetians
**Answer: a) France's Protestants** — it grants Protestants peace.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Who revoked the Edict?
a) Louis XIV  b) Henri IV  c) Colbert
**Answer: a) Louis XIV** — Louis XIV tears it up.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The revocation was in —
a) 1685  b) 1598  c) 1789
**Answer: a) 1685** — in 1685, in the beat.
