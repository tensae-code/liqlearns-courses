# The Edict of Nantes and its revocation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who granted the Edict of Nantes, when, and to whom?
**Answer: Henri IV, in 1598, granting Protestants peace (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Who revoked it, and when?
**Answer: Louis XIV, in 1685 (any close wording).**
