# Lesson plan — Costa Rica — 1948: social reform and civil war

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Costa Rica — 1948: social reform and civil war
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Costa Rica, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The 1940s social reforms (social guarantees, labour code) end in the 1948 civil war, the founding rupture of modern Costa Rica A second Costa Rican book tells the same story its own way, and both tellings stand here as the lesson's two parts.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Reforma social y guerra civil de 1948 (CONED Grade 11 anthology)** — The 1940s social reforms (social guarantees, labour code) end in the 1948 civil war, the founding rupture of modern Costa Rica (Unidad II, Tema 1-C: Reforma social y guerra civil de 1948)
2. **Costa Rica en la década de 1940 y la Guerra Civil de 1948 (Porras y Gamboa)** — Calderón Guardia's reforms — the University of Costa Rica (1940) and the Caja Costarricense de Seguro Social (1941) — the Bloque de la Victoria, Picado's government, the 1948 elections and civil war, and the 1949 Constitution (Unidad 2, C: Costa Rica en la década de 1940 y la Guerra Civil de 1948)
