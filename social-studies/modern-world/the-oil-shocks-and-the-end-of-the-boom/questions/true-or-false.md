# The Oil Shocks And The End Of The Boom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the 1973 oil crisis (Petrini's Storiografia on its causes) ends the golden age; Toyotism (Storia culturale) and Deng's opening (Vogel's Storiografia) announce the productive world that replaces Fordism.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 17 §3 The economic and technological turn

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 17 §3 The economic and technological turn

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the 1973 oil shock ends the golden age as Chernobyl (Eventi) and the Limits to Growth (Fare storia Document) announce the ecological bill — ecology is the module's keyword.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 11 §§10–11 La fine dell'«età dell'oro»: la crisi petrolifera; L'emergere del problema ambientale

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
