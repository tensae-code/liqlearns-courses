# The Italian Wars And Charles V — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 5, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The first phase of the Italian Wars; Charles V and the ideal of a universal Empire; An Empire to defend.
**Answer: Charles VIII's descent (1494) turns Italy into Europe's battlefield; Charles V inherits half the world, fights France, the Ottomans and the Lutherans, sacks Rome (1527) and leaves Italy to Spain at Cateau-Cambrésis (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* (Italy), Unità 5, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The first phase of the Italian Wars; Charles V and the ideal of a universal Empire; An Empire to defend, printed pp. 434, 440, 443.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: La guerra in Italia; Il Sacco di Roma (Eventi); La rinuncia di Carlo V.
**Answer: Italy pays for Charles V's empire with the Sack of Rome (1527); in 1555–56 the exhausted emperor divides his world and retires (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Italy's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* (Italy), La guerra in Italia; Il Sacco di Roma (Eventi); La rinuncia di Carlo V, printed pp. 524, 526, 532.**
