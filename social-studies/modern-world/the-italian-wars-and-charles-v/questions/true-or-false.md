# The Italian Wars And Charles V — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Charles VIII's descent (1494) turns Italy into Europe's battlefield; Charles V inherits half the world, fights France, the Ottomans and the Lutherans, sacks Rome (1527) and leaves Italy to Spain at Cateau-Cambrésis.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 5, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The first phase of the Italian Wars; Charles V and the ideal of a universal Empire; An Empire to defend

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 5, Cap. 14 §§1–3 The first phase of the Italian Wars; Charles V and the ideal of a universal Empire; An Empire to defend

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Italy pays for Charles V's empire with the Sack of Rome (1527); in 1555–56 the exhausted emperor divides his world and retires.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in La guerra in Italia; Il Sacco di Roma (Eventi); La rinuncia di Carlo V

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about La guerra in Italia; Il Sacco di Roma (Eventi); La rinuncia di Carlo V
