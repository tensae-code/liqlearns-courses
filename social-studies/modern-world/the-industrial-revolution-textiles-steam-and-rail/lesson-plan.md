# Lesson plan — The Industrial Revolution — textiles, steam and rail

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Industrial Revolution — textiles, steam and rail
**Place in the curriculum:** Unit 13 — The Modern World (CURRICULUM.md path; TITLES.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Cotton cloth led the way — Kay's flying shuttle and Hargreaves's Spinning Jenny — then steam took to rails: Stockton to Darlington in 1825, Liverpool to Manchester in 1830, and iron in bulk.

## Sub lessons

2. **Steam on rails — and iron in bulk** — The Stephensons put steam on rails; coal-fired smelting pours out cheap iron.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Explain the textile chain: Indian cotton's popularity, Kay's flying shuttle outpacing weavers' yarn supply, Hargreaves's Spinning Jenny (1764) spinning many threads at once.
2. Name the Stephensons and the first two railways with years: Stockton–Darlington 1825, Liverpool–Manchester 1830.
3. Say how coal-fired smelting changed iron: produced in bulk and cheaply.
