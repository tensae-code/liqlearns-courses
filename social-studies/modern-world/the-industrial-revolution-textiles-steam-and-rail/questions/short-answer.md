# The Industrial Revolution — textiles, steam and rail — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How do the shuttle and the Jenny answer each other?
**Answer:** Kay's flying shuttle made weaving faster than spinners could supply yarn; Hargreaves's Spinning Jenny then spun many threads at once, feeding the faster looms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why does cheap bulk iron feed the revolution itself?
**Answer:** Rails, engines, bridges and the machines themselves are made of iron — bulk cheap iron lets the revolution build its own tools.
