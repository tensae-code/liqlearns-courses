# The Industrial Revolution — textiles, steam and rail — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indian cotton cloth had been popular in England since —
a) The 1660s  b) 1750  c) 1825  d) The 1400s
**Answer: a) The 1660s** — Since the 1660s — that popularity started the home industry.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** John Kay's flying shuttle —
a) Spun many threads  b) Let weaving outpace the yarn supply  c) Powered the railways  d) Smelted iron
**Answer: b) Let weaving outpace the yarn supply** — Weaving sped ahead of spinning — the gap the Jenny filled.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Spinning Jenny (1764) was made by —
a) John Kay  b) George Stephenson  c) James Hargreaves  d) Savery
**Answer: c) James Hargreaves** — Hargreaves's Jenny spun many threads into yarn at once.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The first railway, in 1825, ran —
a) Liverpool to Manchester  b) Stockton to Darlington  c) London to Paris  d) Calicut to Lisbon
**Answer: b) Stockton to Darlington** — Stockton to Darlington, 1825; Liverpool to Manchester followed in 1830.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Coal-fired smelting machines let iron be produced —
a) By hand only  b) In bulk and cheaply  c) Only for coins  d) More slowly
**Answer: b) In bulk and cheaply** — Bulk, cheap iron fed rails, engines and machines.
