# The Industrial Revolution — textiles, steam and rail — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The flying shuttle was invented to spin yarn.
**Answer: False** — The shuttle is for weaving; the Jenny answers by spinning many threads.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** George Stephenson worked on rail technology with his son Robert.
**Answer: True** — Father and son drove the railway technology together.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Liverpool–Manchester railway opened before Stockton–Darlington.
**Answer: False** — Stockton–Darlington 1825 came first; Liverpool–Manchester 1830.
