# Hands-on — The Industrial Revolution — textiles, steam and rail

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Thread count
Twist fibres or wool into one thread by hand and time it. The Jenny spun many threads at once — write how a room of Jennies changes a village's cloth supply.

## Rail line on paper
Draw two towns. Lay a 'rail' line between them labelled 1825 (Stockton–Darlington), then a second labelled 1830 (Liverpool–Manchester). Add what travels: coal, cloth, people.
