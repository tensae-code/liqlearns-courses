# Honduras National Identity And Morazan

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: a child learns that being born in Honduras makes them Honduran, that heroes and próceres did great deeds for everyone, and meets Francisco Morazán, named the country's greatest prócer As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
