## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lección 8 Identidad nacional** — a child learns that being born in Honduras makes them Honduran, that heroes and próceres did great deeds for everyone, and meets Francisco Morazán, named the country's greatest prócer
2. **Lección 9: los próceres y el héroe** — Morazán, Valle, Herrera, Cabañas and Reyes are named founders of the Patria, men of flesh and blood with virtues and defects, beside national hero Cacique Lempira and a painting of the Battle of La Trinidad
3. **Lección 9: el legado de los próceres** — Valle drafted the Act of Independence, Herrera decreed the first constitution in 1825 as first Head of State, Morazán presided over the Federation and left the unionist ideal, and Lempira gave his life defending the land
4. **identity as a historical construction** — Honduran identity is the product of a long history — flag, shield, national flower and tree, archaeological parks and letters — built day by day on family and personal identity, in a pluricultural, multilingual society
