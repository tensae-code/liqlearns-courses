Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a child learns that being born in Honduras makes them Honduran, that heroes and próceres did great deeds for everyone, and meets Francisco Morazán, named the country's greatest prócer

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 9: los próceres y el héroe
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Morazán, Valle, Herrera, Cabañas and Reyes are named founders of the Patria, men of flesh and blood with virtues and defects, beside national hero Cacique Lempira and a painting of the Battle of La Trinidad

## Sub lesson 4 — identity as a historical construction
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Honduran identity is the product of a long history — flag, shield, national flower and tree, archaeological parks and letters — built day by day on family and personal identity, in a pluricultural, multilingual society
