# Honduras National Identity And Morazan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A child learns that being born in Honduras makes them Honduran, that heroes and próceres did great deeds for everyone, and meets Francisco Morazán, named the country's greatest prócer.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 8 Identidad nacional

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Primer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 8 Identidad nacional

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Morazán, Valle, Herrera, Cabañas and Reyes are named founders of the Patria, men of flesh and blood with virtues and defects, beside national hero Cacique Lempira and a painting of the Battle of La Trinidad.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 9: los próceres y el héroe

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 9: los próceres y el héroe

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Valle drafted the Act of Independence, Herrera decreed the first constitution in 1825 as first Head of State, Morazán presided over the Federation and left the unionist ideal, and Lempira gave his life defending the land.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 9: el legado de los próceres

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 9: el legado de los próceres

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Honduran identity is the product of a long history — flag, shield, national flower and tree, archaeological parks and letters — built day by day on family and personal identity, in a pluricultural, multilingual society.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in identity as a historical construction

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about identity as a historical construction
