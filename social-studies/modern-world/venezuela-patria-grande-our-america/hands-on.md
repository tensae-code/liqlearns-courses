# Hands-on — Venezuela Patria Grande Our America

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela Patria Grande Our America — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Great Homeland is Our America, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
