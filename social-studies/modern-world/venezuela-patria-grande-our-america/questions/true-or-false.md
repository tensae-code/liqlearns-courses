# Venezuela Patria Grande Our America — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The small homeland opens into Bolívar's Patria Grande, with Neruda's Chilean voice singing the continent as one home.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Great Homeland is Our America

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 4to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Great Homeland is Our America

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bolívar's own sentence opens the republic unit — the homeland to be built is a continent, not a province.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in "For us the Homeland is America"

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 5to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about "For us the Homeland is America"

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Bolívar summons America's new republics to Panama in 1826 to unite — the Patria Grande attempted in diplomacy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Amphictyonic Congress of Panama

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela y de Nuestra América — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Amphictyonic Congress of Panama
