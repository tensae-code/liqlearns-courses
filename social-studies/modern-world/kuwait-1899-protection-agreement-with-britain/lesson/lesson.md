# Kuwait's 1899 protection agreement with Britain

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Standing uneasily beside the Ottoman state, Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signed the 1899 agreement so Britain would shield Kuwait from outside claims. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The 1899 protection agreement between Kuwait and Britain

Kuwait stands uneasily beside the ottoman state, and sheikh mubarak al sabah signs the 1899 agreement so britain shields kuwait from outside claims.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
