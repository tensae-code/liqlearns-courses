# Kuwait's 1899 protection agreement with Britain — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1899 agreement was signed for Kuwait by —
a) Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah  b) Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber  c) Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem
**Answer: a) Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah** — Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signs the 1899 agreement

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Kuwait stood uneasily beside —
a) the Ottoman state  b) Persia  c) Egypt
**Answer: a) the Ottoman state** — Kuwait stands uneasily beside the Ottoman state

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the agreement, Britain would —
a) shield Kuwait from outside claims  b) rule Kuwait's markets  c) take Kuwait's pearls
**Answer: a) shield Kuwait from outside claims** — Britain shields Kuwait from outside claims
