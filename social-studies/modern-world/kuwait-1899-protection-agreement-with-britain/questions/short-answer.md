# Kuwait's 1899 protection agreement with Britain — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did Kuwait sign the 1899 agreement?
**Answer: So Britain would shield Kuwait from outside claims while it stood uneasily beside the Ottoman state (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who signed for Kuwait, and in what year?
**Answer: Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah, in 1899.**
