# Kuwait's 1899 protection agreement with Britain — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1899 agreement made Kuwait a British colony ruled from London.
**Answer: False** — it was a protection agreement — Britain shields Kuwait from outside claims

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Mubarak Al Sabah signed the 1899 agreement.
**Answer: True** — Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah signs it
