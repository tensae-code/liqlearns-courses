# Lesson plan — Micronesia — the copra trade and the rise of foreign law

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — the copra trade and the rise of foreign law
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Whale oil and beche-de-mer faded, the coconut took over through the copra trade, big trading companies and resident traders opened stores, and foreign warships and the Pohnpei treaty of 1870 brought foreign law in behind the trade.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The coconut takes over** — say that whale oil and beche-de-mer faded and the coconut — copra — took over as the islands' great trade good.
2. **Companies, resident traders and stores** — say that big trading companies and resident traders opened stores across the islands.
3. **Foreign law follows the trade: warships and the 1870 treaty** — say that foreign warships and the Pohnpei treaty of 1870 brought foreign law in behind the trade.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
