# Hands-on — Micronesia — the copra trade and the rise of foreign law

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## From coconut to copra
If you can, split a coconut and scrape the flesh as copra-makers do; if not, draw the steps: husk, split, dry, sack, ship. Beside the last step, write who set the price — and where they lived.

## Trade or rule?
Hold a class vote at three moments of the story — first store, first warship, 1870 treaty: 'still trade' or 'now rule'? Each voter must defend their moment in one sentence.
