# Micronesia — the copra trade and the rise of foreign law — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copra replaced whale oil and beche-de-mer as the islands' leading trade.
**Answer: True** — the book teaches the coconut took over as the older trades faded.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Foreign law only reached Micronesia after a colony was formally declared.
**Answer: False** — warships and the 1870 Pohnpei treaty brought foreign law in behind the trade, before colonial rule.
