# Micronesia — the copra trade and the rise of foreign law — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Copra is —
a) Dried coconut flesh  b) A kind of shell money  c) Sea cucumber
**Answer: a) Dried coconut flesh** — the trade good the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The copra trade was run through the islands by —
a) Mission schools  b) Big trading companies and resident traders  c) The Spanish navy
**Answer: b) Big trading companies and resident traders** — the traders the book describes.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Pohnpei treaty that brought foreign law in was signed in —
a) 1870  b) 1899  c) 1914
**Answer: a) 1870** — the treaty year the book gives.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Foreign law arrived backed by —
a) Island councils  b) Foreign warships  c) Mission courts
**Answer: b) Foreign warships** — the backing the book names.
