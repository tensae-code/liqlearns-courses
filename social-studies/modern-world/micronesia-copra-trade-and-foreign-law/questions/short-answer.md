# Micronesia — the copra trade and the rise of foreign law — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is a resident trader's store more than a shop?
**Answer: It is a permanent foreign foothold: a trader living on the island all year, with credit, goods and prices set by a big company far away (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** In what order did trade and law arrive, according to this chapter?
**Answer: Trade first — copra, companies and stores — then foreign law behind it, through warships and the Pohnpei treaty of 1870 (any close wording).**
