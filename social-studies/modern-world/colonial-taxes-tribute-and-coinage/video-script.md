# Video script — Colonial taxes — tribute and coinage

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Bolivia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The indigenous tribute is framed as a right of conquest, the repartimiento of merchandise breaks captive community economies, the alcabala taxes trade, and Potosí's macuquina coins carry the story into Bolivian numismatics.

Tribute, reducciones, obrajes and the commercial monopoly complete the colony's economic machinery.
