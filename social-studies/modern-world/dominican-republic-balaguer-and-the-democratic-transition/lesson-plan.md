# Lesson plan — Dominican Republic — Balaguer and the democratic transition

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Dominican Republic — Balaguer and the democratic transition
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Dominican Republic, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Balaguer rules twelve hard years between authoritarianism and development.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unidad 8 El período de Joaquín Balaguer: los doce años (1966–1978)** — Balaguer rules twelve hard years between authoritarianism and development
2. **Unidad 8 Alternativas democráticas 1978–1996** — elections finally change governments, and democracy survives its tests
3. **Unidad 8 Gobierno de Leonel Fernández (1996–2000) y transformaciones económicas, educativas y tecnológicas** — a new president promises modernisation as the century closes
4. **Unidad 4 Fase neo-autoritaria (1966–1978): el proceso político y el auge económico** — Balaguer's twelve years mix growth, repression and controlled politics
5. **Unidad 4 Contradicciones estructurales y políticas** — the model's own contradictions — inequality, dependence, unrest — pile up beneath the order
6. **Unidad 5 Transición al actual orden democrático (1978–1996): el cambio y los Diez Años** — in 1978 voters choose change; a decade of alternation tests the new democracy
7. **Unidad 5 Transición a la economía de servicios y balance estructural de fin de siglo** — tourism and services replace sugar as the century — and the old order — ends
