# Suriname — emancipation and contract labour — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Emancipatie, Staatstoezicht en contractarbeid.
**Answer: After protests and the 1804 English ban era, slavery ends 1 July 1863 but the freed face ten years of state supervision; planters import contract labourers instead — Chinese in 1853, British Indians from 1873 (Lalla Rookh), Javanese from 1890 — whose descendants together form the Surinamese people.
