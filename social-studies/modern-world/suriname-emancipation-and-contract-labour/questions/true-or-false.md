# Suriname — emancipation and contract labour — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After protests and the 1804 English ban era, slavery ends 1 July 1863 but the freed face ten years of state supervision; planters import contract labourers instead — Chinese in 1853, British Indians from 1873 (Lalla Rookh), Javanese from 1890 — whose descendants together form the Surinamese people.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Emancipatie, Staatstoezicht en contractarbeid

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 6 Geschiedenis* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Emancipatie, Staatstoezicht en contractarbeid

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ketikoti, 1 July 1863: the book traces abolition of the trans-Atlantic trade, the effect of "Uncle Tom's Cabin" (1852) and the slave registers, to emancipation day itself.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Afschaffing van de slavernij in ons land

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Afschaffing van de slavernij in ons land

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Owners and traders resist abolition; the freed live under state supervision (staatstoezicht) with newspaper notices of release, and the Maroons' freedom is recognised too.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Na de afschaffing van de slavernij

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Na de afschaffing van de slavernij

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Following British Guiana's example, the Dutch open a Calcutta emigration office and recruit Indian contract workers, then Javanese — remembered by the Mariënburg monument to the first Javanese immigrants.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in De komst van Hindostaanse en Javaanse contractarbeiders

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
