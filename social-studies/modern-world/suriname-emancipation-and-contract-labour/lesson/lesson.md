# Suriname — emancipation and contract labour

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: After protests and the 1804 English ban era, slavery ends 1 July 1863 but the freed face ten years of state supervision; planters import contract labourers instead — Chinese in 1853, British Indians from 1873 (Lalla Rookh), Javanese from 1890 — whose descendants together form the Surinamese people As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Emancipatie, Staatstoezicht en contractarbeid

After protests and the 1804 English ban era, slavery ends 1 July 1863 but the freed face ten years of state supervision; planters import contract labourers instead — Chinese in 1853, British Indians from 1873 (Lalla Rookh), Javanese from 1890 — whose descendants together form the Surinamese people (Emancipatie, Staatstoezicht en contractarbeid)
