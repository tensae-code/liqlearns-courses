# Lesson plan — The Asia Minor Campaign And Catastrophe 1922

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Asia Minor Campaign And Catastrophe 1922
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Greek army lands at Smyrna in 1919, drives deep into Anatolia, and is destroyed in 1922; Smyrna burns and a million and a half refugees cross to Greece
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit E, ch. 5 The Asia Minor Campaign and the Catastrophe** — the Greek army lands at Smyrna in 1919, drives deep into Anatolia, and is destroyed in 1922; Smyrna burns and a million and a half refugees cross to Greece
2. **Unit E exercise dossier (Greece in the 20th century)** — maps marked with the Treaty of Sèvres gains and the Smyrna zone, timelines of 1940–49, the army's advance in Northern Epirus mapped, and EOKA research links for Cyprus
3. **§35 Entente and Greek claims on the Ottoman Empire** — Sèvres promises Smyrna and Thrace; the Great Idea reaches its furthest map
4. **§38 The Asia Minor War (1919–1922)** — advance to the Sangarios, stalemate, the August 1922 collapse, Smyrna's fire and the refugee exodus
5. **§39 Developments in Greece and Turkey after the Asia Minor War** — Lausanne, the population exchange, the Trial of the Six in Athens and Ataturk's reforms in Ankara

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
