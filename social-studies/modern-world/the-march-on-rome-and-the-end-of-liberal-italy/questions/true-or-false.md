# The March On Rome And The End Of Liberal Italy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the March on Rome (image study: Mussolini and the quadrumvirs) is a bluff the king ratifies; Matteotti's Document denounces the electoral fraud, Gramsci's the socialists' paralysis — and Mussolini's own speech claims responsibility for it all.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§6–7 Fascism in power; From the liberal State to the fascist State

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 2, Cap. 7 §§6–7 Fascism in power; From the liberal State to the fascist State

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the "leggi fascistissime" (Fare storia Document) dismantle the liberal state from inside its own laws, and the authoritarian contagion spreads across Europe — the Anteprima insists it was no fatal outcome: opponents' errors made it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 3 §§5–7 Verso il regime; La dittatura a viso aperto; Il contagio autoritario

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
