# Video script — Cambodia under the French Protectorate, 1863–1953

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

pressed by Siam and Vietnam, Cambodia accepts French protection in 1863; France shields the territory and rebuilds the state, yet rules it — until Sihanouk's crusade wins full independence in 1953.

King Ang Duong (1841–1860), squeezed between Siam and Vietnam, looks for a protector; his successors sign with France in 1863, and ninety years of protection end with Sihanouk's independence crusade.

Ang Duong (1841–1860), pressed by Siam and Vietnam, writes to Napoleon III (1854); he dies on 19 October 1860, and Norodom (portrait, r. 1861–1904) signs the protectorate on 11 August 1863.
