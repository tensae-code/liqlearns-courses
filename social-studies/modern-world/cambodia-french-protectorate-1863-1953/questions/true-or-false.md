# Cambodia under the French Protectorate, 1863–1953 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** pressed by Siam and Vietnam, Cambodia accepts French protection in 1863; France shields the territory and rebuilds the state, yet rules it — until Sihanouk's crusade wins full independence in 1953.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 1, Cambodia under the French Protectorate (1863–1953)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា សំណួរ និងចម្លើយ ឆ្នាំ២០១៤ ដល់ ២០២២ — ត្រៀមប្រឡងសញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាទុតិយភូមិ (History Questions and Answers 2014–2022, for the upper-secondary diploma exam)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 1, Cambodia under the French Protectorate (1863–1953)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** King Ang Duong (1841–1860), squeezed between Siam and Vietnam, looks for a protector; his successors sign with France in 1863, and ninety years of protection end with Sihanouk's independence crusade.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2 Lesson 1, Cambodia under the French protectorate (1863–1953)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី១២ (History, Grade 12)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2 Lesson 1, Cambodia under the French protectorate (1863–1953)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Ang Duong (1841–1860), pressed by Siam and Vietnam, writes to Napoleon III (1854); he dies on 19 October 1860, and Norodom (portrait, r. 1861–1904) signs the protectorate on 11 August 1863.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 1, French colonialism (1863–1953)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៩ (Social Studies, Grade 9)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about History Ch. 2 Lesson 1, French colonialism (1863–1953)
