# Cambodia under the French Protectorate, 1863–1953

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: pressed by Siam and Vietnam, Cambodia accepts French protection in 1863; France shields the territory and rebuilds the state, yet rules it — until Sihanouk's crusade wins full independence in 1953 As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
