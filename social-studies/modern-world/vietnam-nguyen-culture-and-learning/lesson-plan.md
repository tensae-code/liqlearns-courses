# Lesson plan — Vietnam Nguyen Culture And Learning

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Nguyen Culture And Learning
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the age's finest writing is in Nôm — Nguyễn Du's Tale of Kiều, the poetry of Hồ Xuân Hương, Bà Huyện Thanh Quan and Nguyễn Công Trứ — reflecting working lives and mocking feudal society; Huế court music (Nhã nhạc), formed under the Lý–Trần and perfected under the Nguyễn, is proclaimed by UNESCO in 2003 a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity; folk painting runs from Đông Hồ to Hàng Trống and Làng Sình; and the dynasty's scholars compile the Đại Nam thực lục, the Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục and Phan Huy Chú's Lịch triều hiến chương loại chí, with geographies to match — the Nine Dynastic Urns in the Imperial City stand as the lesson's emblem
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
