# Anti Colonial Resistance in Russian Turkestan — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** from the 1892 cholera riot to the 1916 uprising against the labour draft, Turkestan answers colonial rule with revolts the empire drowns in blood.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 39. The national-liberation movement against colonialism

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Рассказы по истории (Rasskazy po istorii — Stories from History), Grade 5* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 39. The national-liberation movement against colonialism

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the 1892 Tashkent 'cholera riot' and Dukchi Ishan's Andijan rising (1898) — desperate, local, and answered with gallows.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 3 §§20–21 The national-liberation movement in the Turkestan krai at the end of the 19th century
