# Sri Lanka — Plantation economy: coffee, tea, rubber

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.
