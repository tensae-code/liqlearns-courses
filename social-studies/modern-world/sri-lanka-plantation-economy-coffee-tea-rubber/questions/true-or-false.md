# Sri Lanka — Plantation economy: coffee, tea, rubber — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Coffee booms then dies of leaf fungus after 1869, James Taylor plants tea at Loolecondera in 1873 and it covers 220,000 acres by 1890, rubber follows in 1877, and coconut becomes the third pillar.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The plantation economy

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G9 History Tamil 2019* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The plantation economy

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils fill a two-column chart — plantation agriculture introduced, indigenous agriculture declined — and argue the gains and losses of the estate economy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Economic development under the British (competency level 2.2)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G9 History Teacher Guide NIE 2018* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Economic development under the British (competency level 2.2)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The old self-sufficient village economy is dismantled and a plantation export economy built in its place.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Changes in the economic system

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *G11 History Tamil 1999* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Changes in the economic system
