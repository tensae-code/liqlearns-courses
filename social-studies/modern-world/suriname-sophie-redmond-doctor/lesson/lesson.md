# Suriname — Sophie Redmond, doctor

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to home visits until her death in 1955 As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Een mevrouw met een gouden hart

Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to home visits until her death in 1955 (Een mevrouw met een gouden hart)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Een mevrouw met een gouden hart — Sophie Redmond defies convention, chooses violin over piano and medicine as a career, qualifies in 1935 and treats poor patients free, cycling to home visits until her death in 1955. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
