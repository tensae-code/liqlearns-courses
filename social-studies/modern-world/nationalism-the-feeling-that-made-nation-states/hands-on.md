# Hands-on — Nationalism — the feeling that made nation states

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## My people's bonds
Write the four bonds — language, culture, history, religion — and beside each, one example from a people you know well. Which bond feels strongest? Say why.

## Two maps
Sketch Europe twice: once as state borders, once as language peoples. Where the two sketches disagree is exactly where nationalism pressed hardest.
