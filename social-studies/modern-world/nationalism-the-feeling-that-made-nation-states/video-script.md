# Video script — Nationalism — the feeling that made nation states


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Europe — a patchwork of empires and small states.

A book is printed in a people's own language. The readers look up: we are one people.

A church door splits — one faith becomes many, and no pope rules over all.

Ships return heavy with wealth, and kings grow strong.

Then, 1815: four great powers at Vienna draw lines to undo it all.

But under their lines, the colours of the peoples keep glowing.

Language. Culture. History. Religion. The feeling will make nations.
