# Nationalism — the feeling that made nation states — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Renaissance prepared nationalism by —
a) Building railways  b) Raising national languages and literatures  c) Abolishing kings  d) Uniting all churches
**Answer: b) Raising national languages and literatures** — National languages and literatures bred national self-consciousness.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Protestant Reformation prepared nationalism by —
a) Breaking Europe's religious unity and papal supremacy  b) Inventing printing for Asia  c) Ending all wars  d) Founding the Vienna settlement
**Answer: a) Breaking Europe's religious unity and papal supremacy** — It broke religious unity and the pope's supremacy.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Expanding commerce and colonies prepared nationalism because —
a) Wealth built strong states  b) Ships carried newspapers  c) Colonies voted in Europe  d) Trade banned local languages
**Answer: a) Wealth built strong states** — Wealth from commerce and colonies built strong states.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Vienna settlement of 1815 was drawn by —
a) Italy, Spain, France and Holland  b) Britain, Russia, Prussia and Austria  c) The USA and Britain only  d) The Ottoman Empire and Persia
**Answer: b) Britain, Russia, Prussia and Austria** — Britain, Russia, Prussia and Austria made the settlement.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The Vienna settlement aimed to —
a) Spread nationalism  b) Reverse the Napoleonic changes  c) Free all colonies  d) Unite Italy
**Answer: b) Reverse the Napoleonic changes** — It was 'reaction' — meant to reverse what Napoleon had changed.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Nationalism pressed peoples sharing language, culture, history and religion to demand —
a) Colonies overseas  b) Nation states of their own  c) One world church  d) Free trade only
**Answer: b) Nation states of their own** — The feeling pressed for nation states.
