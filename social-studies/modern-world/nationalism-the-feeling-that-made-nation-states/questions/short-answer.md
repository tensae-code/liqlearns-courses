# Nationalism — the feeling that made nation states — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the three movements that prepared nationalism, with one contribution each.
**Answer:** The Renaissance (national languages and literatures, breeding self-consciousness); the Protestant Reformation (breaking religious unity and papal supremacy); the expansion of commerce and colonies (wealth building strong states).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did the Vienna settlement fail to stop nation-making?
**Answer:** Because nationalism — peoples bound by shared language, culture, history and religion — kept pressing for nation states of their own, against the reaction the settlement imposed.
