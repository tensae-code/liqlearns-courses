# Nationalism — the feeling that made nation states — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Reformation strengthened the pope's supremacy over Europe.
**Answer: False** — It broke Europe's religious unity and papal supremacy.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The powers at Vienna in 1815 welcomed nationalism.
**Answer: False** — Their settlement was reaction — meant to reverse the changes nationalism fed on.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Shared language is one of nationalism's bonds in the book.
**Answer: True** — Language, culture, history and religion are the four bonds named.
