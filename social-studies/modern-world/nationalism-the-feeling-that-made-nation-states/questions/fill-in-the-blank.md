# Nationalism — the feeling that made nation states — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Renaissance bred national ____ through national languages and literatures.
**Answer: self-consciousness**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The settlement drawn in 1815 was made at ____.
**Answer: Vienna**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A state demanded by one people for itself is a ____ state.
**Answer: nation**
