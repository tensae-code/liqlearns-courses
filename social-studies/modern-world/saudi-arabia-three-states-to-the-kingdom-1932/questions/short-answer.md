**Answer: The first Saudi state rose at Diriyah (1745–1818) and fell to Ibrahim Pasha's campaign; the second state under Turki ibn Abdullah fell to the Al Rashid in 1891; Abd al-Aziz ibn Saud retook Riyadh in 1902, then al-Ahsa, Hail and Mecca, and proclaimed the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1932; oil became its chief resource, and King Faisal cut oil to America and Europe in the October 1973 war.
