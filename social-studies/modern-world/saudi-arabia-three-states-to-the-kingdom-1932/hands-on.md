# Hands-on — Saudi Arabia — three states to the Kingdom, 1932

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Saudi Arabia — three states to the Kingdom, 1932 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Saudi Arabia — three states to the Kingdom, 1932, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
