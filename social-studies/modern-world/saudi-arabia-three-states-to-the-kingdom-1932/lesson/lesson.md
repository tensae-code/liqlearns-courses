# Saudi Arabia — three states to the Kingdom, 1932

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Three Saudi states rise and fall across two centuries before Abd al-Aziz proclaims the Kingdom in 1932 — and oil makes it a power, cut off by King Faisal in 1973. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Saudi Arabia — three states to the Kingdom, 1932

The first Saudi state rose at Diriyah (1745–1818) and fell to Ibrahim Pasha's campaign; the second state under Turki ibn Abdullah fell to the Al Rashid in 1891; Abd al-Aziz ibn Saud retook Riyadh in 1902, then al-Ahsa, Hail and Mecca, and proclaimed the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in 1932; oil became its chief resource, and King Faisal cut oil to America and Europe in the October 1973 war.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
