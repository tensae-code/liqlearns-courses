# Sher Shah Suri — the king of five years who built an empire's system — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Sher Shah Suri — the king of five years who built an empire's system.
**Answer: Sher Shah drives Humayun into exile and in five years builds the machine the Mughals will inherit — the Grand Trunk Road from Bengal to Peshawar, the silver rupiya, the dak postal system and a measured land revenue — before dying at Kalinjar in 1545. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *History 7 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* (Grade 7 / Class 7), Chapter 4: Sher Shah Suri and the Suri interregnum (1540–1555), printed p. 64.**
