# Sher Shah Suri — the king of five years who built an empire's system — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sher Shah drives Humayun into exile and in five years builds the machine the Mughals will inherit — the Grand Trunk Road from Bengal to Peshawar, the silver rupiya, the dak postal system and a measured land revenue — before dying at Kalinjar in 1545.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 4: Sher Shah Suri and the Suri interregnum (1540–1555)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History 7 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Sher Shah Suri — the king of five years who built an empire's system
