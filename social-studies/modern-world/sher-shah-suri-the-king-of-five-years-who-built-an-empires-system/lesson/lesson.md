## Sub lesson 1 — Sher Shah Suri — the king of five years who built an empire's system

Sher Shah drives Humayun into exile and in five years builds the machine the Mughals will inherit — the Grand Trunk Road from Bengal to Peshawar, the silver rupiya, the dak postal system and a measured land revenue — before dying at Kalinjar in 1545.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
