# Video script — Sher Shah Suri — the king of five years who built an empire's system

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sher Shah drives Humayun into exile and in five years builds the machine the Mughals will inherit — the Grand Trunk Road from Bengal to Peshawar, the silver rupiya, the dak postal system and a measured land revenue.
