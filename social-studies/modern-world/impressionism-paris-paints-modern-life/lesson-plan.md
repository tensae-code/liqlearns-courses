# Lesson plan — Impressionism — Paris paints modern life

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Impressionism — Paris paints modern life
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Manet scandalises Paris, then Monet and his friends take painting outdoors to catch light itself — railways, boulevards, regattas and haystacks at every hour; the academy's rejects build their own exhibitions and invent modern art's marketplace.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.
