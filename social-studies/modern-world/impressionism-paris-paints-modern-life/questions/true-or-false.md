# Impressionism — Paris paints modern life — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Monet and his friends take painting outdoors to catch light itself — railways, boulevards, regattas and haystacks at every hour.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Manet scandalises Paris, then Monet and his friends take painting outdoors to catch light itself — railways, boulevards, regattas and haystacks at every hour; the academy's rejects build their own exhibitions and invent modern art's marketplace.
