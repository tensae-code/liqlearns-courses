# Hands-on — Impressionism — Paris paints modern life

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw an Impressionist scene outdoors from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: Manet scandalises Paris; Painting outdoors, catching light; The rejects build their own market. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
