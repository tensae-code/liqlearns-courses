# The European geographical discoveries — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The European geographical discoveries.
**Answer: armed with Arab navigation science, the compass, the astrolabe and Islamic maps, and hungry to reach India and China without Muslim middlemen and their prices, Portugal and Spain sail out — Henry the Navigator feeling down Africa's coast, Dias rounding the Cape of Storms, da Gama reaching Calicut, Columbus crossing to a world he dies believing is India, Vespucci's name given to the Americas, Magellan's men completing the circle of the earth the Muslims had already proved on paper — followed by Cabot, Cook and the French and Dutch, until discovery hardens into colonisation, the slave trade, and the wiping out of whole peoples (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الحديث والمعاصر للصف التاسع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (Modern and Contemporary History of the Arabs for Grade 9, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), The European geographical discoveries, printed p. 20.**
