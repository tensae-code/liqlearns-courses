## Sub lesson 1 — The European geographical discoveries
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — armed with Arab navigation science, the compass, the astrolabe and Islamic maps, and hungry to reach India and China without Muslim middlemen and their prices, Portugal and Spain sail out — Henry the Navigator feeling down Africa's coast, Dias rounding the Cape of Storms, da Gama reaching Calicut, Columbus crossing to a world he dies believing is India, Vespucci's name given to the Americas, Magellan's men completing the circle of the earth the Muslims had already proved on paper — followed by Cabot, Cook and the French and Dutch, until discovery hardens into colonisation, the slave trade, and the wiping out of whole peoples
