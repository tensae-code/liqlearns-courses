# Honduras Cultural Heritage And World Heritage Sites — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: protecting the national patrimony.
**Answer: the 1984 Heritage Law (Decree 81-84) protects colonial monuments and movable goods, and three Honduran sites hold UNESCO World Heritage status: Copán Ruins (1980), the Río Plátano Biosphere (1982) and San Marcos de Colón (2017) (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
