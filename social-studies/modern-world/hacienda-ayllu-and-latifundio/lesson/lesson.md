# Hacienda, ayllu and latifundio

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The hacienda grows beside the surviving ayllu and marka; yanaconas stay on usurped ayllu land, and the latifundio expands at the community's expense. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — La hacienda y el ayllu

The hacienda grows beside the surviving ayllu and marka; yanaconas stay on usurped ayllu land, and the latifundio expands at the community's expense (La hacienda y el ayllu)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
