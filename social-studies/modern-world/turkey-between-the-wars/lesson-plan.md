# Lesson plan — Turkey between the wars

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey between the wars
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 4, Turkey and the World Between the Two Wars (Turkey part): the early parties and the failed multi-party tries, the 1926 assassination attempt; abroad — Greece, France, Britain and Mosul, the Soviets, the League of Nations (1932), the Balkan Entente (1934), Montreux (1936), the Sadabad Pact (1937), Hatay joins the homeland, and Atatürk's death (10 November 1938).
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: 'peace at home, peace in the world' is practised border by border while the founder's chair empties. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the interwar milestones in order: League of Nations 1932; Balkan Entente 1934; Montreux 1936; Sadabad Pact 1937; Hatay joins the homeland.
2. Say what Montreux (1936) concerned: the Straits.
3. Say the home track's story: early parties, failed multi-party tries, and the 1926 assassination attempt.
4. Give Atatürk's death date: 10 November 1938 — and his motto: peace at home, peace in the world.
