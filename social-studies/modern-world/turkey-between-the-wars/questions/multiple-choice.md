# Turkey between the wars — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Turkey entered the League of Nations in —
a) 1932  b) 1923  c) 1936
**Answer: a) 1932** — the first dated milestone.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Montreux (1936) concerned —
a) Hatay  b) The Straits  c) Mosul's oil
**Answer: b) The Straits** — restoring Turkey's hand on them.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Balkan Entente was signed in —
a) 1937  b) 1934  c) 1932
**Answer: b) 1934** — between the League entry and Montreux.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Atatürk died on —
a) 10 November 1938  b) 29 October 1938  c) 23 April 1938
**Answer: a) 10 November 1938** — the founding age closes.
