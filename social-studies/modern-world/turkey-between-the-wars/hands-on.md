# Hands-on — Turkey between the wars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Border by border

On a rough map, mark Turkey's neighbours and write beside each border one interwar question or settlement: Mosul, the Straits, Hatay, the Balkan Entente. Colour settled borders green.

## The dated line

Draw a timeline 1923–1938 and place: 1926 attempt, 1932 League, 1934 Balkan Entente, 1936 Montreux, 1937 Sadabad, 1938 the founder's death and Hatay. Read it aloud as one sentence per mark.

## Motto into practice

Write the motto — peace at home, peace in the world — at the top of a page. Under it, two columns: one act of peace abroad from this lesson, one difficulty of peace at home. Say which column was harder to fill.
