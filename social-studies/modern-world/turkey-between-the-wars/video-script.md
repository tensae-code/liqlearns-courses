# Video script — Turkey between the wars

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

'Peace at home, peace in the world.' Between the wars, Turkey practises Atatürk's motto border by border.

At home, the young republic tries twice to allow an opposition party, and twice draws back; in 1926 an assassination attempt fails. Abroad, diplomacy settles the empire's inheritance: the League of Nations in 1932, the Balkan Entente in 1934, Montreux restoring the Straits in 1936, the Sadabad Pact in 1937, Hatay joining the homeland.

On 10 November 1938 the founder dies — and the founding age closes.
