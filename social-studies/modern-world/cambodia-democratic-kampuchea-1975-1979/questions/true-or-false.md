# Cambodia — Democratic Kampuchea, 1975–1979 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Khmer Rouge take Phnom Penh, empty the cities and impose a regime whose genocide devastates Cambodia before it falls in January 1979.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson 4, Democratic Kampuchea (1975–1979)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា សំណួរ និងចម្លើយ ឆ្នាំ២០១៤ ដល់ ២០២២ — ត្រៀមប្រឡងសញ្ញាបត្រមធ្យមសិក្សាទុតិយភូមិ (History Questions and Answers 2014–2022, for the upper-secondary diploma exam)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson 4, Democratic Kampuchea (1975–1979)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** on 17 April 1975 the Khmer Rouge enter Phnom Penh and empty it within days; their programme — evacuate the cities, abolish money, markets and religion — turns the country into a prison camp.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2 Lesson 4, Democratic Kampuchea (1975–1979)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *ប្រវត្តិវិទ្យា ថ្នាក់ទី១២ (History, Grade 12)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2 Lesson 4, Democratic Kampuchea (1975–1979)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** on 17 April 1975 the Khmer Rouge empty Phnom Penh in three days; Angkar's programme — evacuate the cities, abolish money and markets, defrock the monks, execute the old leaders — is listed point by point.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History Ch. 2 Lesson 4, The Democratic Kampuchea regime (1975–1979)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *សិក្សាសង្គម ថ្នាក់ទី៩ (Social Studies, Grade 9)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about History Ch. 2 Lesson 4, The Democratic Kampuchea regime (1975–1979)
