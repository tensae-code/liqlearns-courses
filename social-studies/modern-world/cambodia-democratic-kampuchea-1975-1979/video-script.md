# Video script — Cambodia — Democratic Kampuchea, 1975–1979

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the Khmer Rouge take Phnom Penh, empty the cities and impose a regime whose genocide devastates Cambodia before it falls in January 1979.

on 17 April 1975 the Khmer Rouge enter Phnom Penh and empty it within days; their programme — evacuate the cities, abolish money, markets and religion — turns the country into a prison camp.

on 17 April 1975 the Khmer Rouge empty Phnom Penh in three days; Angkar's programme — evacuate the cities, abolish money and markets, defrock the monks, execute the old leaders — is listed point by point.
