## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: war ruins the North's farming — communal fields pass into private hands, peasants become tenants owing rent, tax and service, and disasters drive them from their villages — while in the South reclamation builds a rich agriculture, above all in the Mekong delta; in both regions state workshops cast weapons and coin, and the folk crafts flourish in famous villages: Thổ Hà and Bát Tràng pottery, La Khê silk, the iron villages of Nho Lâm, Hiền Lương and Phú Bài, Châu Khê paper — the folk verse quoted at the lesson's head dreams of Bát Tràng bricks to build with As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
