# Vietnam Economy And Towns 16th To 18th — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 9, agriculture and crafts in the two regions.
**Answer: war ruins the North's farming — communal fields pass into private hands, peasants become tenants owing rent, tax and service, and disasters drive them from their villages — while in the South reclamation builds a rich agriculture, above all in the Mekong delta; in both regions state workshops cast weapons and coin, and the folk crafts flourish in famous villages: Thổ Hà and Bát Tràng pottery, La Khê silk, the iron villages of Nho Lâm, Hiền Lương and Phú Bài, Châu Khê paper — the folk verse quoted at the lesson's head dreams of Bát Tràng bricks to build with (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 9, agriculture and crafts in the two regions, printed p. 42.**
