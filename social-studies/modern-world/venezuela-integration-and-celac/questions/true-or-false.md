# Venezuela Integration And Celac — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Against the interference story the book sets Bolívar's old call to union, carried forward into CELAC as its modern form.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The union of the peoples of Our America and CELAC

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The union of the peoples of Our America and CELAC

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Against the US-proposed ALCA, Chávez proposes ALBA in 2001 and regional blocs UNASUR and CELAC follow.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Latin America and globalisation: ALBA, UNASUR and CELAC against ALCA

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 6to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Latin America and globalisation: ALBA, UNASUR and CELAC against ALCA
