# The Holocaust and other genocides

## Do it first

Rwanda's P6 pupils compare genocides side by side: Rwanda's 100 days, stopped by Rwandans themselves; Hitler's killing of over 6 million Jews; Pol Pot's Cambodia; and Bosnia. Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think happened — hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Holocaust — over 6 million Jews killed

Hitler's killing of over 6 million Jews is the comparison's first case. Rwanda's book sets its own genocide beside the Holocaust so pupils learn that this crime has a wider, terrible history.

In your mind's eye: the world of this lesson as the book shows it. Say what changed in this part of the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Cambodia and Bosnia

Pol Pot's Cambodia and Bosnia are the other named cases. Each is a different country and a different moment, and the lesson asks what makes them the same kind of crime.

## Sub lesson 3 — Rwanda compared — 100 days, stopped by Rwandans

Rwanda's 100 days and machetes are compared with the others, with one difference the book underlines: the genocide in Rwanda was stopped by Rwandans themselves. Comparison here is a way of understanding, not of ranking suffering.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats: The Holocaust — over 6 million Jews killed; Cambodia and Bosnia; Rwanda compared — 100 days, stopped by Rwandans. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
