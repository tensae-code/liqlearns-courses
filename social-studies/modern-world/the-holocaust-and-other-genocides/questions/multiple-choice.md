# The Holocaust and other genocides — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Holocaust was Hitler's killing of —
a) Over 6 million Jews  b) The Tutsi  c) The Cambodians
**Answer: a) Over 6 million Jews** — Over 6 million Jews.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Pol Pot's genocide was in —
a) Bosnia  b) Cambodia  c) Germany
**Answer: b) Cambodia** — Pol Pot's Cambodia.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Rwanda's genocide lasted, in this comparison, —
a) 100 days  b) Ten years  c) One day
**Answer: a) 100 days** — Rwanda's 100 days.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Rwanda's genocide was stopped by —
a) The United Nations alone  b) Rwandans themselves  c) Germany
**Answer: b) Rwandans themselves** — Stopped by Rwandans themselves, in the book's comparison.
