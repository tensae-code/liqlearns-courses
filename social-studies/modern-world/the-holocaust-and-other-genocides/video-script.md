# Video script — The Holocaust and other genocides

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Rwanda's P6 pupils compare genocides side by side: Rwanda's 100 days, stopped by Rwandans themselves; Hitler's killing of over 6 million Jews; Pol Pot's Cambodia; and Bosnia.

Hitler's killing of over 6 million Jews is the comparison's first case. Rwanda's book sets its own genocide beside the Holocaust so pupils learn that this crime has a wider, terrible history.

Pol Pot's Cambodia and Bosnia are the other named cases. Each is a different country and a different moment, and the lesson asks what makes them the same kind of crime.

Rwanda's 100 days and machetes are compared with the others, with one difference the book underlines: the genocide in Rwanda was stopped by Rwandans themselves. Comparison here is a way of understanding, not of ranking suffering.
