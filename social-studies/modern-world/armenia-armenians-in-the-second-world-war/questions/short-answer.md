# Armenia Armenians In The Second World War — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 3.7 Armenians in the years of the Second World War.
**Answer: over 300,000 Armenians from the republic and more from the diaspora fight in Soviet ranks; divisions from Armenia reach Berlin, and the home front feeds and arms them (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* (Armenia), 3.7 Armenians in the years of the Second World War, printed p. 96.**
