# Indonesia — defending independence, 1945–1950 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The guide frames 1945-1950 as state-forming under fire — a government built in weeks, early-revolution upheavals, diplomacy and guerrilla war side by side (Bandung Lautan Api, the Linggarjati talks foreign journalists cover in November 1946), and the road from RIS back to the unitary NKRI.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 1, Perjuangan Mempertahankan Kemerdekaan (Gambaran Umum)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Buku Panduan Guru Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII* is about Indonesia — Reformasi, 1998 and after.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 1, Perjuangan Mempertahankan Kemerdekaan (Gambaran Umum)
