# Sāmoa's civil wars — the struggle to be tafa'ifā

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: a tafa'ifā holds all four pāpā titles and is made by the districts, not inherited — so when foreigners push a centralised government and a "king" in 1854, Malietoa Laupepa, Malietoa Talavou, Mataafa and Tupua Tamasese all have genealogical claims, and Britain, America and Germany arm their favourites. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
