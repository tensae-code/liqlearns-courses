# Hands-on — Siam Reforms And Survival

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Siam Reforms And Survival — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 5 Siam reforms and survives: Rama IV opens trade from 1851; Rama V from 1868 reforms economy, administration (1892), education (first programme 1898 after studying Europe), cuts farm taxes in 1874, tours Europe in 1897 — and buys independence by ceding claims in Laos and Cambodia to France (1907) and in Malaya to Britain (1909), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
