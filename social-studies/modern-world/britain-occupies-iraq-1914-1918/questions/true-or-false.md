# Britain occupies Iraq, 1914–1918 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indian forces land below Abadan in 1914, are besieged at Kut, then take Baghdad and finally Mosul as the Mudros armistice is announced on 31 October 1918.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The British occupation in the First World War

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الاجتماعيات للصف الثالث المتوسط (Social Studies for the Third Intermediate Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The British occupation in the First World War

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** British interests, invasion, occupation and the mandate settlement are retold at full length.
