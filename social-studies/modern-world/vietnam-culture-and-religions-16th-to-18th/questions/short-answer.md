# Vietnam Culture And Religions 16th To 18th — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 9, culture, religions and the birth of chữ Quốc ngữ.
**Answer: Confucianism still rules study and office, Buddhism and Daoism revive, and from 1533 Catholicism spreads until by the 18th century it reaches the whole country — bringing with it the Latin-based chữ Quốc ngữ, devised by missionaries learning Vietnamese to preach and later prized for printing and science; Han literature still leads, but Nôm literature flowers — the verse chronicle Thiên Nam ngữ lục, Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm (the folk's Trạng Trình), the Trạng Quỳnh tales — beside communal-house carving of daily life, the thousand-armed Quan Âm of Bút Tháp pagoda, chèo and tuồng (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 9, culture, religions and the birth of chữ Quốc ngữ, printed p. 44.**
