## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Churchill's 1946 call for a United States of Europe becomes the Coal and Steel Community of 1951 and the Rome Treaty of 1957. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The European Union (Unit 3 Lesson 3)

Churchill's 1946 call for a United States of Europe becomes the Coal and Steel Community of 1951 and the Rome Treaty of 1957; enlargement wave follows wave — Britain in 1973, Greece, Spain and Portugal, a reunified Germany, ten states in 2004, Bulgaria and Romania in 2007 — until the EU stands as the world's biggest economic force of 27 members

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
