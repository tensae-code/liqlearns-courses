# Hands-on — Shaybanids Take Mawarannahr

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Shaybanids Take Mawarannahr — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Introduction; Ch. I §§1–3 The political situation in Turkestan and Dasht-i Kipchak; Muhammad Shaybani Khan; The establishment of Shaybanid rule in Mawarannahr, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
