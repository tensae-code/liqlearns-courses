## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: as the Timurids fade, Shaybani's nomadic Uzbeks ride out of the Kipchak steppe, take Bukhara and Samarkand (1500–01) and found a new order. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
