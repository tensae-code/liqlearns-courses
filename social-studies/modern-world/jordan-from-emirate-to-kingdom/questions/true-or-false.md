# Jordan — from emirate to kingdom — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abdullah ibn Hussein reached Ma'an in 1920 and agreed with Churchill in 1921 on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision; the national movement rejected the 1928 treaty and Balfour's promise; the 1946 treaty ended the mandate and Abdullah became king; Jordan fought in 1948 and joined the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom, lost them in June 1967, and signed the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2, Lesson الأردن منذ تأسيس الإمارة حتى الوقت الحاضر (Jordan from the emirate's founding to the present)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف التاسع (History, Grade 9)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Jordan — from emirate to kingdom
