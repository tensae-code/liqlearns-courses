# Jordan — from emirate to kingdom — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Jordan — from emirate to kingdom.
**Answer: Abdullah ibn Hussein reached Ma'an in 1920 and agreed with Churchill in 1921 on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision; the national movement rejected the 1928 treaty and Balfour's promise; the 1946 treaty ended the mandate and Abdullah became king; Jordan fought in 1948 and joined the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom, lost them in June 1967, and signed the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
