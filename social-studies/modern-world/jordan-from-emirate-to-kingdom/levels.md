## Sub lesson 1 — Jordan — from emirate to kingdom
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Abdullah ibn Hussein reached Ma'an in 1920 and agreed with Churchill in 1921 on an emirate east of the Jordan under British supervision; the national movement rejected the 1928 treaty and Balfour's promise; the 1946 treaty ended the mandate and Abdullah became king; Jordan fought in 1948 and joined the West Bank and Jerusalem to the kingdom, lost them in June 1967, and signed the Wadi Araba treaty in 1994.
