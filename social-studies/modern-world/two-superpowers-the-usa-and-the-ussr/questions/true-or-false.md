# Two Superpowers The Usa And The Ussr — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two victors of 1945 are compared as systems — markets and parties against plan and single party — each claiming the future.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §13 The bipolar world: the USA and the USSR

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §13 The bipolar world: the USA and the USSR

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 3, Lessons 40–41: the bipolar world — the USA and the USSR
