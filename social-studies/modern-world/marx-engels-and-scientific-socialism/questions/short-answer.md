# Marx Engels And Scientific Socialism — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 11, Marx, Engels and the birth of scientific socialism.
**Answer: the industrial revolution creates the working class — landless peasants crowding into factories and mines, demonstrating with the English Chartists by 1848; Karl Marx (1818–1883), a Trier doctor of philosophy at 23, is expelled from Germany for his revolutionary views and continues in Paris; Friedrich Engels (1820–1895), a Barmen factory-owner's son, sees his own class's greed at first hand and writes The Condition of the Working Class in England; the two meet in 1844, found the League of Communists — the international proletariat's first independent party — and in early 1848 publish the Communist Manifesto, whose appearance the book takes as the birth of scientific socialism (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
