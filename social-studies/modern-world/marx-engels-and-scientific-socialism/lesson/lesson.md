# Marx Engels And Scientific Socialism

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the industrial revolution creates the working class — landless peasants crowding into factories and mines, demonstrating with the English Chartists by 1848; Karl Marx (1818–1883), a Trier doctor of philosophy at 23, is expelled from Germany for his revolutionary views and continues in Paris; Friedrich Engels (1820–1895), a Barmen factory-owner's son, sees his own class's greed at first hand and writes The Condition of the Working Class in England; the two meet in 1844, found the League of Communists — the international proletariat's first independent party — and in early 1848 publish the Communist Manifesto, whose appearance the book takes as the birth of scientific socialism As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
