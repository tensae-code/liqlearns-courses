## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 11, Marx, Engels and the birth of scientific socialism
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the industrial revolution creates the working class — landless peasants crowding into factories and mines, demonstrating with the English Chartists by 1848; Karl Marx (1818–1883), a Trier doctor of philosophy at 23, is expelled from Germany for his revolutionary views and continues in Paris; Friedrich Engels (1820–1895), a Barmen factory-owner's son, sees his own class's greed at first hand and writes The Condition of the Working Class in England; the two meet in 1844, found the League of Communists — the international proletariat's first independent party — and in early 1848 publish the Communist Manifesto, whose appearance the book takes as the birth of scientific socialism
