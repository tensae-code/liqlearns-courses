# Video script — The East India Company — Plassey and Buxar

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A trading company turns political — Clive's bargain with Mir Jafar at Plassey and the victory at Buxar make it master of northern India, Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse swallows state after state, and after the failed rising of 1857 the Crown rules directly.
