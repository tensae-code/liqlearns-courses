# Hands-on — The East India Company — Plassey and Buxar

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The East India Company — Plassey and Buxar — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Crown takes over after 1857, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
