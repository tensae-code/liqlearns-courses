# Lesson plan — The East India Company — Plassey and Buxar

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The East India Company — Plassey and Buxar
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Pakistan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** A trading company turns political — Clive's bargain with Mir Jafar at Plassey and the victory at Buxar make it master of northern India, Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse swallows state after state, and after the failed rising of 1857 the Crown rules directly.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Crown takes over after 1857** — After the failed rising of 1857 the East India Company's rule passes to the Crown; the book closes the chapter with the challenges the Muslims of the Subcontinent then faced — political eclipse, economic ruin and an education gap

2. **Plassey and Buxar — a company becomes a ruler** — A trading company turns political, Clive's bargain with Mir Jafar hands it Bengal, and Buxar makes it master of northern India

3. **The Doctrine of Lapse** — A legal rule becomes a land-grab, Indian rulers learn adoption no longer protects their thrones, and resentment builds toward 1857

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
