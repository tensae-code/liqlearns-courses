# The East India Company — Plassey and Buxar — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the failed rising of 1857 the East India Company's rule passes to the Crown; the book closes the chapter with the challenges the Muslims of the Subcontinent then faced — political eclipse, economic ruin and an education gap.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapter 4: the British took over the Subcontinent

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History 7 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Crown takes over after 1857

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A trading company turns political, Clive's bargain with Mir Jafar hands it Bengal, and Buxar makes it master of northern India.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.1: the East India Company gets a Mughal farman, beats the Portuguese, then wins Bengal at Plassey (1757) through Mir Jafar's betrayal and Buxar (1764)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Plassey and Buxar — a company becomes a ruler

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A legal rule becomes a land-grab, Indian rulers learn adoption no longer protects their thrones, and resentment builds toward 1857.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.1: the Doctrine of Lapse under Dalhousie annexes state after state whose ruler dies without a natural heir

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Doctrine of Lapse
