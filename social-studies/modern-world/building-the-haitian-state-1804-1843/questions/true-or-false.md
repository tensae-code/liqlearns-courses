# Building the Haitian state, 1804–1843 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** On the ruins of saint-domingue, black and mixed-race leaders proclaim a free state no one in 1804 thought possible — and argue over what it should be
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 1, L'État, la société et la déclaration d'indépendance (1er janvier 1804)).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dessalines crowns himself emperor and rules by the sword; his assassination in 1806 leaves the young state without an agreed direction
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 2, Un État autoritaire, l'empire dessalinien et la crise d'orientation).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The 1806 constitution fight breaks the founding unity — two rival models of organising the state and the community face off
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 3, Rupture dans la stratégie unitaire de construction de l'État).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In the north henri christophe builds a kingdom — palace, code and court — betting that discipline and grandeur will earn respect
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 4, Un territoire et deux États (1): l'État d'Haïti et le royaume de Christophe).

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** In the west and south pétion's republic takes the opposite bet — land to soldiers, a senate, and help for bolívar's independence fight
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 5, Un territoire et deux États (2): l'État de l'ouest et du sud, la République d'Haïti sous Pétion).

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** After christophe's fall in 1820, boyer reunites north and republic — one haiti again, soon stretched over the whole island (1822)
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 6, La réunification de la République d'Haïti).

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Boyer's rural code tries to chain peasants back to plantation work — the state wins on paper, the countryside resists in fact
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 8, Le code rural de 1826 et la consolidation de l'État autoritaire).
