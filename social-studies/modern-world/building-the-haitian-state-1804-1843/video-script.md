# Video script — Building the Haitian state, 1804–1843

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Haiti guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

On the ruins of saint-domingue, black and mixed-race leaders proclaim a free state no one in 1804 thought possible — and argue over what it should be

Dessalines crowns himself emperor and rules by the sword; his assassination in 1806 leaves the young state without an agreed direction

The 1806 constitution fight breaks the founding unity — two rival models of organising the state and the community face off

In the north henri christophe builds a kingdom — palace, code and court — betting that discipline and grandeur will earn respect

In the west and south pétion's republic takes the opposite bet — land to soldiers, a senate, and help for bolívar's independence fight

After christophe's fall in 1820, boyer reunites north and republic — one haiti again, soon stretched over the whole island (1822)

Boyer's rural code tries to chain peasants back to plantation work — the state wins on paper, the countryside resists in fact

Hold that story: On the ruins of Saint-Domingue a free state is proclaimed in 1804 — then rival blueprints collide: Dessalines' empire, Christophe's kingdom, Pétion's republic, reunification under Boyer, and the Rural Code of 1826.
