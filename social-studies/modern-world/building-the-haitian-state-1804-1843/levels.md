# Building the Haitian state, 1804–1843 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The state, society and the Declaration of Independence
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: On the ruins of saint-domingue, black and mixed-race leaders proclaim a free state no one in 1804 thought possible — and argue over what it should be (Séance 1, L'État, la société et la déclaration d'indépendance (1er janvier 1804))

## Sub lesson 2 — An authoritarian state — the Dessalinian empire
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Dessalines crowns himself emperor and rules by the sword; his assassination in 1806 leaves the young state without an agreed direction (Séance 2, Un État autoritaire, l'empire dessalinien et la crise d'orientation)

## Sub lesson 3 — Rupture in the unitary strategy
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: The 1806 constitution fight breaks the founding unity — two rival models of organising the state and the community face off (Séance 3, Rupture dans la stratégie unitaire de construction de l'État)

## Sub lesson 4 — One territory, two states (1) — Christophe's kingdom
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In the north henri christophe builds a kingdom — palace, code and court — betting that discipline and grandeur will earn respect (Séance 4, Un territoire et deux États (1): l'État d'Haïti et le royaume de Christophe)

## Sub lesson 5 — One territory, two states (2) — Pétion's republic
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In the west and south pétion's republic takes the opposite bet — land to soldiers, a senate, and help for bolívar's independence fight (Séance 5, Un territoire et deux États (2): l'État de l'ouest et du sud, la République d'Haïti sous Pétion)

## Sub lesson 6 — The reunification of the Republic of Haiti
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: After christophe's fall in 1820, boyer reunites north and republic — one haiti again, soon stretched over the whole island (1822) (Séance 6, La réunification de la République d'Haïti)

## Sub lesson 7 — The Rural Code of 1826 and the authoritarian state
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Boyer's rural code tries to chain peasants back to plantation work — the state wins on paper, the countryside resists in fact (Séance 8, Le code rural de 1826 et la consolidation de l'État autoritaire)
