# Building the Haitian state, 1804–1843

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: On the ruins of Saint-Domingue a free state is proclaimed in 1804 — then rival blueprints collide: Dessalines' empire, Christophe's kingdom, Pétion's republic, reunification under Boyer, and the Rural Code of 1826. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The state, society and the Declaration of Independence

On the ruins of saint-domingue, black and mixed-race leaders proclaim a free state no one in 1804 thought possible — and argue over what it should be (Séance 1, L'État, la société et la déclaration d'indépendance (1er janvier 1804))

In your mind's eye: henri Christophe's kingdom in the north beside Pétion's republic in the west and south. Say how the two states bet differently.

## Sub lesson 2 — An authoritarian state — the Dessalinian empire

Dessalines crowns himself emperor and rules by the sword; his assassination in 1806 leaves the young state without an agreed direction (Séance 2, Un État autoritaire, l'empire dessalinien et la crise d'orientation)

## Sub lesson 3 — Rupture in the unitary strategy

The 1806 constitution fight breaks the founding unity — two rival models of organising the state and the community face off (Séance 3, Rupture dans la stratégie unitaire de construction de l'État)

## Sub lesson 4 — One territory, two states (1) — Christophe's kingdom

In the north henri christophe builds a kingdom — palace, code and court — betting that discipline and grandeur will earn respect (Séance 4, Un territoire et deux États (1): l'État d'Haïti et le royaume de Christophe)

## Sub lesson 5 — One territory, two states (2) — Pétion's republic

In the west and south pétion's republic takes the opposite bet — land to soldiers, a senate, and help for bolívar's independence fight (Séance 5, Un territoire et deux États (2): l'État de l'ouest et du sud, la République d'Haïti sous Pétion)

## Sub lesson 6 — The reunification of the Republic of Haiti

After christophe's fall in 1820, boyer reunites north and republic — one haiti again, soon stretched over the whole island (1822) (Séance 6, La réunification de la République d'Haïti)

## Sub lesson 7 — The Rural Code of 1826 and the authoritarian state

Boyer's rural code tries to chain peasants back to plantation work — the state wins on paper, the countryside resists in fact (Séance 8, Le code rural de 1826 et la consolidation de l'État autoritaire)
