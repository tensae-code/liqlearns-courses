# Video script — The Renaissance — what it was

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Europe sleeps through a Dark Age.
Then Greece and Rome wake again.
Italy first — Rome's ruins stir its scholars.
The study turns to people and the visible world.
Everyday languages become book languages.

[Grade 11 depth scene — The Renaissance in depth — humanism, art and the press. The animated map carries the story as the voice speaks.]
Italy's rebirth receives the Grade 11 depth: humanism returning to classical learning and putting human capability at the centre; art reborn in Leonardo and Michelangelo; and Gutenberg's printing press multiplying every book — and every idea — a hundredfold. The names to hold: humanism, printing press.
