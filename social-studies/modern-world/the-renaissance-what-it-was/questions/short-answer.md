# The Renaissance — what it was — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is the Renaissance, in one sentence?
**Answer:** The revival, from the early 14th century, of ancient Greece and Rome's civilisations with new values added — bringing great change across literature, art, politics, science and thought.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did scholars' field of study change?
**Answer:** Medieval scholars tied thinking to religion and divine things; Renaissance scholars studied human nature and the created, visible world.
