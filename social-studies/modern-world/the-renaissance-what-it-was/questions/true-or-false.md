# The Renaissance — what it was — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Renaissance changed only art.
**Answer: False** — It changed literature, art, politics, science, and social and economic thought.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Italy's ancient remains stirred emulation — the wish to match Rome.
**Answer: True** — That is the book's reason Italy came first.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Renaissance research turned toward people's daily challenges.
**Answer: True** — Rather than religious and divine ideas alone.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Gutenberg's invention, multiplying books and ideas — this is printing press.
**Answer: True**
