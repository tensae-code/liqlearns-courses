# The Renaissance — what it was — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book dates Europe's Dark Age from —
a) The 1st to the 5th century  b) The 5th to the 14th century  c) The 14th to the 17th century  d) 1750 onwards
**Answer: b) The 5th to the 14th century** — Ancient civilisation declined in Europe from the 5th to the 14th century.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Renaissance revived the civilisations of —
a) Egypt and Aksum  b) Ancient Greece and Rome  c) China and India  d) Spain and Portugal
**Answer: b) Ancient Greece and Rome** — Greece and Rome, with new values added.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Renaissance scholars made their field of study —
a) Religion only  b) Human nature and the created, visible world  c) Dreams and omens  d) War only
**Answer: b) Human nature and the created, visible world** — The centre moved from divine things to people and the visible world.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Renaissance appeared first in —
a) England  b) France  c) Italy  d) Holland
**Answer: c) Italy** — Italy — centre of ancient Roman civilisation, close to Greece.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** One fruit of the Renaissance was that vernacular languages —
a) Died out  b) Grew into literary languages  c) Were banned  d) Were replaced by Latin
**Answer: b) Grew into literary languages** — Everyday languages became fit for literature.
