# Hands-on — The Renaissance — what it was

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Ruins in your town
Think of the oldest building or ruin near your home. Write what a young scholar standing before it might want to rebuild or re-learn. That stirring is how Italy started.

## Latin or our language?
Take one sentence you say every day. The Renaissance raised everyday languages into literary languages — write your sentence as the first line of a 'book' in your own language, proudly.


## Grade 11 depth — The Renaissance in depth — humanism, art and the press

Re-draw this lesson's map or table with the Grade 11 part added in a second colour, then tell the new part's story aloud from the drawing alone. Where the drawing cannot carry the story, the story is not yet learned — re-read that part.
