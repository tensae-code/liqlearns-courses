# Mongolia in the Second World War

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Mongolia gives horses, meat, wool and a tank column to the Soviet war effort, then in August 1945 declares war on Japan and marches into Inner Mongolia. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. 3.12 The MPR's participation in the Second World War

Mongolia gives horses, meat, wool and a tank column to the Soviet war effort, then in August 1945 declares war on Japan and marches into Inner Mongolia

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Ch. III.6 The MPR in the Second World War; the consolidation of independence

Khalkhin Gol 1939, the total war economy, the "Revolutionary Mongolia" tank column, and the August 1945 campaign against Japan

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
