# Effects of industrialisation — cities, classes, empires — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Industrial cities grew because workers needed to live within reach of the factory gate.
**Answer: True** — the factory fixed where families could live.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Trade unions were organisations of factory owners.
**Answer: False** — unions were the workers' own organisations.

**3. (Sub lesson 3) ** A factory that never stops needs more raw material and more buyers than one country alone can give.
**Answer: True** — that hunger pushed states outward to empire.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Population fell during industrialisation.
**Answer: False** — population grew.
