# Hands-on — Effects of industrialisation — cities, classes, empires

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The two sides of the gate
On a big sheet, draw a factory gate down the middle. Fill the left side with everything the owning side has and does; the right side with the working side. Say each item aloud as you place it.

## A city's growth in beans
Take 20 beans for a village. Move beans, five at a time, to a drawn mill as you tell each reason people moved — wages, work, family following. Count what is left in the village at the end.
