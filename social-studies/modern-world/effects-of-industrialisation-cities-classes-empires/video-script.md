# Video script — Effects of industrialisation — cities, classes, empires

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on farmland. Small figures walk the roads, all one way — toward the smoke.
The mill town grows as we watch. Streets fill. Houses crowd. The town has no clean water yet, and still they come.
Now stand at the factory gate. On one side, the owners, counting money. On the other side, the workers, side by side — and side by side, they begin to speak with one voice.
The camera pulls back, out to sea. Ships carry raw cotton in and finished cloth out. The arrows reach for Africa, for Asia. The factory's hunger is redrawing the map of the world.
