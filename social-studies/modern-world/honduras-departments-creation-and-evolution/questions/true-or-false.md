# Honduras Departments Creation And Evolution — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Departments are born after independence from Spain (1821): Herrera creates the first seven, the Bay Islands belong to England until 1860, and Gracias a Dios, the last, is founded in 1957 to make 18.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 24 Mi departamento: su origen y evolución

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 24 Mi departamento: su origen y evolución

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ocotepeque is destroyed by the Marchala flood in 1934 and refounded as Nueva Ocotepeque in 1935; Amapala is named capital in 1876 for Soto's provisional government; Santa Bárbara (1825) is divided to create Cortés in 1893.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 26: hechos históricos de los departamentos

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The first departmental division is set on 28 June 1825 with seven departments; Congress creates the rest one by one until there are 18 departments, 298 municipalities and thousands of villages.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 33 Creación de mi departamento
