# Honduras Departments Creation And Evolution — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 24 Mi departamento: su origen y evolución.
**Answer: departments are born after independence from Spain (1821): Herrera creates the first seven, the Bay Islands belong to England until 1860, and Gracias a Dios, the last, is founded in 1957 to make 18 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* (Honduras), Lección 24 Mi departamento: su origen y evolución, printed p. 110.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 26: hechos históricos de los departamentos.
**Answer: Ocotepeque is destroyed by the Marchala flood in 1934 and refounded as Nueva Ocotepeque in 1935; Amapala is named capital in 1876 for Soto's provisional government; Santa Bárbara (1825) is divided to create Cortés in 1893 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* (Honduras), Lección 26: hechos históricos de los departamentos, printed p. 119.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 33 Creación de mi departamento.
**Answer: the first departmental division is set on 28 June 1825 with seven departments; Congress creates the rest one by one until there are 18 departments, 298 municipalities and thousands of villages (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* (Honduras), Lección 33 Creación de mi departamento, printed p. 150.**
