## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lección 24 Mi departamento: su origen y evolución** — departments are born after independence from Spain (1821): Herrera creates the first seven, the Bay Islands belong to England until 1860, and Gracias a Dios, the last, is founded in 1957 to make 18
2. **Lección 26: hechos históricos de los departamentos** — Ocotepeque is destroyed by the Marchala flood in 1934 and refounded as Nueva Ocotepeque in 1935; Amapala is named capital in 1876 for Soto's provisional government; Santa Bárbara (1825) is divided to create Cortés in 1893
3. **Lección 33 Creación de mi departamento** — the first departmental division is set on 28 June 1825 with seven departments; Congress creates the rest one by one until there are 18 departments, 298 municipalities and thousands of villages
