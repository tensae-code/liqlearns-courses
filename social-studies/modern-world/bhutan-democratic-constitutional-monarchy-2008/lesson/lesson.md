# Bhutan — democratic constitutional monarchy, 2008

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: a kingdom ruled as a monarchy until 2006 votes itself a new order, and from 2008 Bhutan is a Democratic Constitutional Monarchy with parties, an elected Assembly and the King as head of state; on 6-7 November 2008 Bhutan formally crowns Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck, the People's King, completing the transition the Class VI book names: a Democratic Constitutional Monarchy with the King as head of state. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
