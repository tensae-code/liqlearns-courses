# Bhutan — democratic constitutional monarchy, 2008 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a kingdom ruled as a monarchy until 2006 votes itself a new order, and from 2008 Bhutan is a Democratic Constitutional Monarchy with parties, an elected Assembly and the King as head of state
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter 5.1 The Government).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** on 6-7 November 2008 Bhutan formally crowns Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck, the People's King, completing the transition the Class VI book names: a Democratic Constitutional Monarchy with the King as head of state
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Four, Coronation of the Fifth Druk Gyalpo).
