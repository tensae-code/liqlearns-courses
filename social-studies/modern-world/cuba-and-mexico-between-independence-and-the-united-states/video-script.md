# Video script — Cuba and Mexico between Independence and the United States

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uzbekistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

José Martí's Cuba rises (1895), the USA crushes Spain in 1898 and takes Cuba as a protectorate in all but name; in Mexico Porfirio Díaz's dictatorship sells the country to foreign monopolies until Zapata's "Land and Freedom" and Madero's call for real elections open the revolution.
