# Hands-on — Greece Civil War 1946 1949

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The Civil War and the main problems of post-civil-war Greece (1944–1963), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
