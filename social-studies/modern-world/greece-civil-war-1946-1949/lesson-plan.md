**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Civil War and the main problems of post-civil-war Greece (1944–1963)** — resistance comrades become enemies; three years of civil war end in 1949 and leave a divided, policed democracy
2. **The Civil War (1946–1949)** — the wartime split hardens into three years of war in the mountains
3. **The Cold War's effects on Greece and the Civil War** — Greece becomes the Cold War's first battlefield; government and communist armies fight to 1949 and the country enters the Western camp scarred
