**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Libya — Omar al-Mukhtar and the Italian occupation.
**Answer: Italy invaded in 1911 and the Ottoman state ceded Libya at Ouchy in 1912; the Libyans formed the Tripoli government under Sulayman al-Baruni, Ahmad al-Sharif al-Senussi beat the Italians at Derna in 1913, and Italy recognised the Tripolitanian Republic in 1918; from 1921 Omar al-Mukhtar led the guerrilla war from the Jabal al-Akhdar until Italy walled the Egyptian border, captured him and executed him in 1931; the United Nations granted independence in 1949, Idris became king in 1951, Gaddafi's coup of 1969 brought a tyranny of prisons and the Abu Salim massacre, and the popular revolution of February 2011 ended his rule and his life.
