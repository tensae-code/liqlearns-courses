# Italys Long Transition The Second Republic — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** after Tangentopoli Italy rewrites its party system without settling it; the chapter's document is an augury for the new millennium's Italy — a transition that becomes a condition.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 6, Cap. 21 §8 The long Italian transition

**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 6, Cap. 21 §8 The long Italian transition
