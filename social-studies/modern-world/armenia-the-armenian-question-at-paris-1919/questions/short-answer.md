# Armenia The Armenian Question At Paris 1919 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 2.9 The Armenian Question at the Paris Peace Conference.
**Answer: two Armenian delegations plead in Paris, Wilson draws an arbitral boundary, and the Sèvres treaty promises a large Armenia that no army will enforce (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
