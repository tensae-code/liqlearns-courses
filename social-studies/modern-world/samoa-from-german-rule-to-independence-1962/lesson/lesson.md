# Sāmoa — from German rule to independence, 1962

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Britain asks New Zealand to seize German Samoa at the start of the First World War; troops land unresisted at Apia in August 1914; Samoa passes through League of Nations mandate (1919) and UN trusteeship (1945), with New Zealand charged to prepare it for self-government, until in 1962 Samoa becomes the first Pacific island country to win independence. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
