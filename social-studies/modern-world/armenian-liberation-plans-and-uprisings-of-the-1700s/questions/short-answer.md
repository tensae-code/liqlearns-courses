# Armenian Liberation Plans And Uprisings Of The 1700s — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §5.2 The Armenian liberation struggle in the later 1700s.
**Answer: Israel Ori begs Europe's courts for an army, Davit Bek fights in Syunik, and in Madras Shahamirian prints a constitution for a free Armenia (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *«Հայոց պատմություն 8. Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար» (History of Armenia 8, textbook for general schools) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, Ala A. Kharatyan, Zaruhi A. Hakobyan* (Armenia), §5.2 The Armenian liberation struggle in the later 1700s, printed p. 75.**
