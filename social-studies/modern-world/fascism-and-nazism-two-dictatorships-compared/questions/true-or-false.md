# Fascism and Nazism — two dictatorships compared — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** Italy was on the losing side of the First World War.
**Answer: False** — Italy won — and still felt cheated by the peace.

**2. (Sub lesson 1) ** A Fascist state stood in Italy from 1922.
**Answer: True** — a decade before Hitler's Germany.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Resentment of the Versailles settlement helped Hitler rise.
**Answer: True** — a dictated peace, nationalists called it.

**4. (Sub lesson 1) ** Fascism promised order and national greatness at the price of democracy itself.
**Answer: True** — one party, one leader, opponents silenced.
