# Hands-on — Fascism and Nazism — two dictatorships compared

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The comparison table
Draw a table: rows Soil / Leader / Year in power / What democracy lost; columns Italy / Germany. Fill it from the lesson, then read each row across as a comparison sentence.

## Promises audit
List the promises each movement made to its people. Beside each promise, write what it cost. Say which promise you think drew the most followers, and why.
