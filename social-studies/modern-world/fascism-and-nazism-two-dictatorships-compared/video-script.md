# Video script — Fascism and Nazism — two dictatorships compared

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens on Europe between the wars. In Italy, soldiers come home to empty fields and closed gates.
1919: a party is founded. 1922: black shirts march; the state changes hands. One flag, one leader.
The map shifts north. A treaty document stamped with blame; a breadline; a voice promising rebirth. 1933: the second dictatorship stands.
The two coloured blocs lean toward each other across the map — and toward a third, in the East. The camera pulls back: the road to 1939 is already drawn.
