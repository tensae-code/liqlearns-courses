# Video script — Cambodia — the Oudong era, 1620–1863

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Srei Sauriyopear moves the court to Oudong (1604); Chey Chettha II marries a Vietnamese princess and opens Prey Nokor to Vietnamese settlement (1620) — two centuries of shrinking land and civil war end at the French protectorate.

Oudong, 45 km from today's capital, is built as the royal city in 1625 (photo: Phnom Oudong); kings divided by rival queens' factions invite Siam and Vietnam in, and the kingdom shrinks for two centuries.

the usurper Sdach Korn is overthrown with Siamese help (1613); Chey Chettha II (1618–1628) moves the capital to Oudong in 1620 — and the long era of rival kings and foreign patrons begins.
