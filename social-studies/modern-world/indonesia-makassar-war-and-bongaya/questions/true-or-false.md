# Indonesia — the Makassar War and the Treaty of Bongaya — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sultan Hasanuddin's Makassar defends Fort Samba Opu against the VOC until 1669, and the imposed Treaty of Bongaya opens South Sulawesi to company rule.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.B

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — the Cultivation System (Tanam Paksa).
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.B

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gowa's Makassarese sailors and sultans fight the company for the eastern trade until defeat is sealed at Bongaya.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 2.A.5 Perlawanan Goa

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — the Cultivation System (Tanam Paksa).
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 2.A.5 Perlawanan Goa
