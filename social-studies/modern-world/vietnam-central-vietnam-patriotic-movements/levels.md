## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 19, Phan Châu Trinh and the Duy Tân movement in the Centre
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Phan Châu Trinh (1872–1926), a Phó bảng who resigns his court post to save the country, chooses the opposite road to Phan Bội Châu — rely on France to reform, under the slogan "open the people's intellect, raise the people's spirit, enrich the people's livelihood"; from 1906 his Duy Tân movement spreads new-style schools, lectures, industry and commerce, and mockery of corrupt officials, and calls on people to cut their hair short and wear short clothes against backward custom; it sparks the Central Vietnam anti-tax movement of 1908, which France drowns in arrests — Phan Châu Trinh among the arrested
