**Answer: from the Hương Khê rising and the Duy Tân and anti-tax movements to the Nghệ–Tĩnh Soviets of 1930, the centre keeps rising against French rule (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 19, Phan Châu Trinh and the Duy Tân movement in the Centre.
**Answer: Phan Châu Trinh (1872–1926), a Phó bảng who resigns his court post to save the country, chooses the opposite road to Phan Bội Châu — rely on France to reform, under the slogan "open the people's intellect, raise the people's spirit, enrich the people's livelihood"; from 1906 his Duy Tân movement spreads new-style schools, lectures, industry and commerce, and mockery of corrupt officials, and calls on people to cut their hair short and wear short clothes against backward custom; it sparks the Central Vietnam anti-tax movement of 1908, which France drowns in arrests — Phan Châu Trinh among the arrested (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 19, Phan Châu Trinh and the Duy Tân movement in the Centre, printed p. 91.**
