# Vietnam Central Vietnam Patriotic Movements — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** from the Hương Khê rising and the Duy Tân and anti-tax movements to the Nghệ–Tĩnh Soviets of 1930, the centre keeps rising against French rule.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 18, Central Vietnam's patriotic and revolutionary tradition

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 4 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 18, Central Vietnam's patriotic and revolutionary tradition

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Phan Châu Trinh (1872–1926), a Phó bảng who resigns his court post to save the country, chooses the opposite road to Phan Bội Châu — rely on France to reform, under the slogan "open the people's intellect, raise the people's spirit, enrich the people's livelihood"; from 1906 his Duy Tân movement spreads new-style schools, lectures, industry and commerce, and mockery of corrupt officials, and calls on people to cut their hair short and wear short clothes against backward custom; it sparks the Central Vietnam anti-tax movement of 1908, which France drowns in arrests — Phan Châu Trinh among the arrested.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 19, Phan Châu Trinh and the Duy Tân movement in the Centre

**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 19, Phan Châu Trinh and the Duy Tân movement in the Centre
