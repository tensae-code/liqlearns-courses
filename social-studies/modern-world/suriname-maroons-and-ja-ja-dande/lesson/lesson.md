# Suriname — Maroons and Ja-Ja Dandé

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ja-Ja Dandé, priestess daughter of Maroon leader Ayoko, warns her forest people of soldiers, heals with herbs and persuades the Maroons to make peace, even letting the whites hold church in Santea As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ja-Ja Dandé, een moedige vrouw

Ja-Ja Dandé, priestess daughter of Maroon leader Ayoko, warns her forest people of soldiers, heals with herbs and persuades the Maroons to make peace, even letting the whites hold church in Santea (Ja-Ja Dandé, een moedige vrouw)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Ja-Ja Dandé, een moedige vrouw — Ja-Ja Dandé, priestess daughter of Maroon leader Ayoko, warns her forest people of soldiers, heals with herbs and persuades the Maroons to make peace, even letting the whites hold church in Santea. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
