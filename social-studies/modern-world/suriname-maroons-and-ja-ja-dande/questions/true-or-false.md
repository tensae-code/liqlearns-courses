# Suriname — Maroons and Ja-Ja Dandé — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ja-Ja Dandé, priestess daughter of Maroon leader Ayoko, warns her forest people of soldiers, heals with herbs and persuades the Maroons to make peace, even letting the whites hold church in Santea.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ja-Ja Dandé, een moedige vrouw

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 5 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ja-Ja Dandé, een moedige vrouw
