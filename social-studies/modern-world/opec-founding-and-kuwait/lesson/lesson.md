# OPEC is founded — Kuwait among the oil states

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Because oil runs modern transport and industry, the exporting states founded OPEC to set production quotas and guard the balance of producers and consumers — Kuwait among them, and in 2016 the pact widened into OPEC+. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — OPEC as a model of an organisation independent of the UN

Oil runs modern transport and industry, so the exporting states found opec to set production quotas and guard producers' and consumers' balance, kuwait among its members, and in 2016 widen the pact into opec+.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
