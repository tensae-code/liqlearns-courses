# OPEC is founded — Kuwait among the oil states — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** OPEC was founded by —
a) the oil-exporting states  b) the oil-importing states  c) the United Nations
**Answer: a) the oil-exporting states** — the exporting states found OPEC

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** OPEC sets —
a) production quotas  b) world oil prices alone  c) shipping lanes
**Answer: a) production quotas** — OPEC sets production quotas

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In 2016 the pact widened into —
a) OPEC+  b) the GCC  c) the Arab League
**Answer: a) OPEC+** — in 2016 the pact widens into OPEC+
