# Hitler's rise to power, 1919 to 1933 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — 5.1 Hitler's early career
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — A failed Austrian art student is radicalised in Vienna and Munich, finds his voice as an army informant after the war and is sent to spy on a tiny workers' party he ends up leading

## Sub lesson 2 — 5.2 The early growth of the Nazi Party
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Drexler's DAP becomes the NSDAP with a 25-point programme; Hitler's speeches, the SA and the party newspaper turn a Munich fringe group into a movement

## Sub lesson 3 — 5.3 The Munich Putsch
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — In November 1923 Hitler bursts into a beer hall to seize Bavaria and march on Berlin; police fire stops it, but the trial gives him a national platform and prison time to write Mein Kampf

## Sub lesson 4 — 5.4 The lean years, 1924–28
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Banned then refounded, the party reorganises into a national machine with Goebbels' propaganda, yet recovery-era voters ignore it — 12 seats in 1928

## Sub lesson 5 — 6.2 Growth in support for the Nazis
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Fear of communism, Goebbels' posters and rallies, SA spectacle and Hitler's flights across Germany turn the Nazis into the party of the desperate and the frightened alike

## Sub lesson 6 — 6.3 Political developments in 1932
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Brüning falls, von Papen and Schleicher intrigue, the Nazis become the largest party in July 1932, and Hindenburg — who detests Hitler — is talked into appointing him Chancellor on 30 January 1933
