# Lesson plan — Palestine under the British Mandate and the Zionist project

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Palestine under the British Mandate and the Zionist project
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Egypt, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Britain's Balfour promise of 1917 is carried out through the San Remo mandate of 1920, and the Arab states learn to act together over Palestine — from Bludan in 1937 to the wars of 1948 and 1967.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Egypt and the Palestine question (Term 2)** — Britain's Balfour promise of 1917 — "a promise from one who does not own, to one who does not deserve" — is carried out through the San Remo mandate of 1920, and the lesson follows the question through the 1948 war and the June 1967 defeat
2. **Chapter 7: Egypt and the issues of the contemporary Arab world — the Palestine conferences** — The Arab states learn to act together first over Palestine — the Bludan conference of 1937 gathers delegates from every Arab land against the Zionist project, and London's Round Table of 1939 seats the independent Arab states as parties to the question

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
