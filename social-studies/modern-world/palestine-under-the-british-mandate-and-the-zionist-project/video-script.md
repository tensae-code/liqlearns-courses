# Video script — Palestine under the British Mandate and the Zionist project

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Britain's Balfour promise of 1917 is carried out through the San Remo mandate of 1920, and the Arab states learn to act together over Palestine — from Bludan in 1937 to the wars of 1948 and 1967.
