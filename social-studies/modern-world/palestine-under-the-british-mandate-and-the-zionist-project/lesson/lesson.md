# Palestine under the British Mandate and the Zionist project

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Britain's Balfour promise of 1917 is carried out through the San Remo mandate of 1920, and the Arab states learn to act together over Palestine — from Bludan in 1937 to the wars of 1948 and 1967. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Egypt and the Palestine question (Term 2)

Britain's Balfour promise of 1917 — "a promise from one who does not own, to one who does not deserve" — is carried out through the San Remo mandate of 1920, and the lesson follows the question through the 1948 war and the June 1967 defeat.
