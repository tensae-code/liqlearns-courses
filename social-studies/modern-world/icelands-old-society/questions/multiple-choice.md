# Iceland's old society — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In old Iceland, most people —
a) Owned their own farms  b) Owned nothing  c) Lived in big towns
**Answer: b) Owned nothing** — most people own nothing.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The vistarband tied —
a) Servants to farms  b) Ships to harbours  c) Farmers to towns
**Answer: a) Servants to farms** — the vistarband ties servants to farms.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The census that counts old Iceland's people was taken in —
a) 1703  b) 1809  c) 1944
**Answer: a) 1703** — the 1703 census.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The census counted —
a) Only landowners  b) Tenant farmers and servants as well as owners  c) Only children
**Answer: b) Tenant farmers and servants as well as owners** — the census counts them all.
