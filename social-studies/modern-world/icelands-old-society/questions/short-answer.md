# Iceland's old society — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Who worked the land in old Iceland, and what tied servants to farms?
**Answer: Tenant farmers worked other men's land, and the vistarband tied servants to farms (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the 1703 census show about old Iceland?
**Answer: That most people owned nothing — the census counts tenant farmers and servants farm by farm (any close wording).**
