# Video script — Iceland's old society

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Iceland book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

in old Iceland most people own nothing; the vistarband ties servants to farms (Gamla samfélagið, student book pp. 4–9)

the census counts the tenant farmers and servants of old Iceland — a whole people listed farm by farm (Gamla samfélagið, student book pp. 4–9)

Hold that story: In old Iceland most people own nothing: tenant farmers work other men's land, servants are tied to farms by the vistarband, and in 1703 a census counts them all.
