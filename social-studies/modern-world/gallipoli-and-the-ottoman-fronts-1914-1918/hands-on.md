# Hands-on — Gallipoli and the Ottoman fronts, 1914–1918

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three kinds of front

Draw a table of three columns — offensive, defensive, aid — and place each front the book names in its column. Under the table, write what makes a front 'aid': fighting beside allies far from home.

## The straits on a map

Sketch the straits at Gallipoli: sea, peninsula, ridges. Mark where the ships were held and where the trenches stood, and write one sentence on what falls if the straits fall.

## The colonel's name

Write two short notices: one from 1915, a newspaper naming the colonel of Gallipoli; one from years later, naming the same man as the republic's founder. Say what joins them.
