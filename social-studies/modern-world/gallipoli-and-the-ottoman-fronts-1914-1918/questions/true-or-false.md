# Gallipoli and the Ottoman fronts, 1914–1918 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The empire fought on more fronts than any of its allies.
**Answer: True** — the book's measure of its war.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Iraq and Syria-Palestine were offensive fronts in the book's sorting.
**Answer: False** — they are defensive fronts; the offensive pair is the Caucasus and the Canal.
