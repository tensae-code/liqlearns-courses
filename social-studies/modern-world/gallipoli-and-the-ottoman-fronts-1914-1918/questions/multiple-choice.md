# Gallipoli and the Ottoman fronts, 1914–1918 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The offensive fronts named are —
a) The Caucasus and the Canal  b) Galicia and Romania  c) Iraq and Gallipoli
**Answer: a) The Caucasus and the Canal** — reaching outward.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Gallipoli (Çanakkale) was a —
a) Aid front  b) Defensive front  c) Offensive front
**Answer: b) Defensive front** — guarding the straits.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The aid fronts were —
a) Galicia, Romania and Macedonia  b) The Caucasus and the Canal  c) Hijaz and Yemen
**Answer: a) Galicia, Romania and Macedonia** — fighting beside allies far from home.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Gallipoli's victory is tied in the book to —
a) The caliph  b) A German admiral  c) The colonel who will found the republic, Mustafa Kemal
**Answer: c) The colonel who will found the republic, Mustafa Kemal** — the map joined to the man.
