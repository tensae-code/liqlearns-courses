# Video script — Gallipoli and the Ottoman fronts, 1914–1918

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Ottoman Empire fights the First World War on more fronts than any ally. Turkey's Grade 12 book sorts them three ways.

Offensive fronts: the Caucasus and the Canal. Defensive fronts: Gallipoli, Iraq, Syria-Palestine, Hijaz-Yemen. Aid fronts, beside allies far from home: Galicia, Romania, Macedonia.

Above them all, Gallipoli — the empire's one immortal victory, won by a colonel named Mustafa Kemal, who will found the republic.
