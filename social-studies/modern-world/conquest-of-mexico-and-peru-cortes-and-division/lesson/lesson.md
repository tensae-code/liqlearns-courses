# The conquest of Mexico and Peru — Cortés and division

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the Requerimiento is read in Spanish far from the town, Cortés exploits Totonac discontent for alliances, and the Inca, divided, fall — bravery and Indigenous division decide the conquests. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
