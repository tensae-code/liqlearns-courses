# Hands-on — Papua New Guinea — changes in basic needs over time

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Papua New Guinea — changes in basic needs over time — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Changes in Food / Changes in Shelter / Changes in Clothing, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
