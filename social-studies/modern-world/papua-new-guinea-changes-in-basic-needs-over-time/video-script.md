# Video script — Papua New Guinea — changes in basic needs over time

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Explorers bring canned beef and rice; native sugarcane, banana and pandanus give ground — for taste, for saved garden time, for prestige, and because town life leaves no land to grow — and shelter and clothing (the meri blouse beside bark cloth) change the same way.
