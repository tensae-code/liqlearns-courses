[Scene 1 — A new picture of nature. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Between Copernicus and Newton, Europeans rebuilt their picture of nature. Watch the names appear one by one: Copernicus, Newton, experiment. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 2 — Reason turns to government. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
The Enlightenment took that method and aimed it at society. Watch the names appear one by one: Locke, Montesquieu, Rousseau. This is the part of the story to hold.

[Scene 3 — Why this lesson is a hinge. The map and the scene build as the voice speaks.]
Stand back and see the hinge: a new science of nature, then a new science of society, then — within a generation — revolutions in America and France built from these very arguments.
