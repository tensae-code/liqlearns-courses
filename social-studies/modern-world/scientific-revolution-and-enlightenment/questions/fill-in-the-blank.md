# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the lesson's own term.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The term for 'testing nature by doing and measuring, the Scientific Revolution's method' is ______.
**Answer: experiment**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The term for 'the thinker of the social contract, remade by the people' is ______.
**Answer: Rousseau**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The term for 'this lesson's place — ideas turning into the revolutions that follow' is ______.
**Answer: hinge**
