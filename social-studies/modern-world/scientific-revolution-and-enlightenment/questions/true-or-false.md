# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: The thinker whose laws of motion and gravity bound the new picture together — this is Newton.
**Answer: True**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** True or false: in this lesson, Copernicus means testing nature by doing and measuring, the Scientific Revolution's method.
**Answer: False**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: The thinker who argued power must be divided — this is Montesquieu.
**Answer: True**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** True or false: in this lesson, Locke means the thinker of the social contract, remade by the people.
**Answer: False**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: Locke's ground of legitimate government — this is consent of the governed.
**Answer: True**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: in this lesson, Enlightenment means this lesson's place — ideas turning into the revolutions that follow.
**Answer: False**
