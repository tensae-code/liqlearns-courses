# Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What is Copernicus?
a) the thinker who set the Sun at the centre of the system
b) experiment
c) Newton
**Answer: a) the thinker who set the Sun at the centre of the system**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What is Locke?
a) Rousseau
b) the Enlightenment thinker who grounded government in consent
c) Montesquieu
**Answer: b) the Enlightenment thinker who grounded government in consent**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** What is Enlightenment?
a) hinge
b) the movement applying reason to government and society
c) consent of the governed
**Answer: b) the movement applying reason to government and society**
