# Symbolism and Munch — art of dreams and anxiety — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Rodin remakes sculpture  b) The century ends dreaming  c) Munch's Scream
**Answer: b) The century ends dreaming** — Pre-Raphaelites and symbolists paint myths, dreams and death rather than the visible world.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Rodin remakes sculpture  b) The century ends dreaming  c) Munch's Scream
**Answer: a) Rodin remakes sculpture** — Rodin remakes sculpture from the inside.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) The century ends dreaming  b) Rodin remakes sculpture  c) Munch's Scream
**Answer: c) Munch's Scream** — Munch's Scream turns modern anxiety into an icon — before expressionism has a name.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The century ends dreaming — Pre-Raphaelites and symbolists paint myths, dreams and death rather than the visible world, Rodin remakes sculpture from the inside, and Munch's Scream turns modern anxiety into an icon before expressionism has a name  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
