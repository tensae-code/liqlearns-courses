# Lesson plan — Symbolism and Munch — art of dreams and anxiety

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Symbolism and Munch — art of dreams and anxiety
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The century ends dreaming — Pre-Raphaelites and symbolists paint myths, dreams and death rather than the visible world, Rodin remakes sculpture from the inside, and Munch's Scream turns modern anxiety into an icon before expressionism has a name.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.
