# Lesson plan — Vietnam North Builds Socialism 1954 1965

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam North Builds Socialism 1954 1965
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** five waves of land reform (late 1953–1956) give over 810,000 hectares to some 2.2 million households — 72.8% of northern rural households — making "the tiller has land" real, though the book records errors against some meritorious families, acknowledged and corrected; recovery (1955–57) passes pre-war farm output and opens factories like Cơ khí Hà Nội and Thuốc lá Thăng Long, trading with 27 countries; transformation (1958–60) gathers 40,000 agricultural cooperatives and wipes out illiteracy in the delta for under-50s; from 1959 the Trường Sơn supply route forms — Đoàn 559's traditional day is 19 May 1959 — and the first Five-Year Plan (1961–65) moves great volumes of food, weapons and medicine south, some 40,000 cadres and soldiers in 1961–63 alone, while the "Ba sẵn sàng" youth movement readies the North; Hồ Chí Minh's 1964 verdict is quoted: in ten years the North "has taken long steps never seen in the nation's history"
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 16, the North: land reform, recovery, first plan** — five waves of land reform (late 1953–1956) give over 810,000 hectares to some 2.2 million households — 72.8% of northern rural households — making "the tiller has land" real, though the book records errors against some meritorious families, acknowledged and corrected; recovery (1955–57) passes pre-war farm output and opens factories like Cơ khí Hà Nội and Thuốc lá Thăng Long, trading with 27 countries; transformation (1958–60) gathers 40,000 agricultural cooperatives and wipes out illiteracy in the delta for under-50s; from 1959 the Trường Sơn supply route forms — Đoàn 559's traditional day is 19 May 1959 — and the first Five-Year Plan (1961–65) moves great volumes of food, weapons and medicine south, some 40,000 cadres and soldiers in 1961–63 alone, while the "Ba sẵn sàng" youth movement readies the North; Hồ Chí Minh's 1964 verdict is quoted: in ten years the North "has taken long steps never seen in the nation's history"
2. **Bài 16, the South rises: Đồng Khởi** — America installs the Ngô Đình Diệm government, refuses the 1956 general election and unleashes "denounce and exterminate communists" campaigns under Law 10/59's mobile guillotine; the Party's 15th Central Committee Conference (early 1959) authorises revolutionary violence with political struggle primary; scattered risings at Vĩnh Thạnh, Bác Ái and Trà Bồng (1959) become the Đồng Khởi movement at Bến Tre on 17 January 1960 — three Mỏ Cày communes rise, Bến Tre's "long-hair army" fights under Nguyễn Thị Định, landlords' land goes to poor peasants — and on 20 December 1960 the National Liberation Front is founded
