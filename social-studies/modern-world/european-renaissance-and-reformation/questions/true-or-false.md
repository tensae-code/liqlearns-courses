# The modern European Renaissance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy, living next door to the flourishing Arab civilisation of Spain, Sicily and the Levant, wakes first: Constantinople's scholars flee west in 1453 with their manuscripts, rich trading cities pay for art and translation from Arabic, paper and printing spread the new learning, and over three centuries Europe climbs out of its middle ages — Luther attacks the sale of indulgences and the clergy's monopoly on the Gospel, kings gather power into national monarchies, and the humanities, Greek science and the Muslim method of doubt, observation and experiment remake the mind of a continent.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The modern European Renaissance

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الحديث والمعاصر للصف التاسع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (Modern and Contemporary History of the Arabs for Grade 9, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The modern European Renaissance

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Europe climbs out of its medieval darkness over three centuries, drawing on the Arab Islamic civilisation through al-Andalus, Sicily and Iraq; printing breaks the clergy's grip on knowledge, feudalism dissolves as cities and banks rise, national kingdoms form, Luther splits the Church, and gunpowder armies replace the feudal levy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Manifestations of the modern European renaissance (Unit 1 Lesson 1)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ العالم الحديث والمعاصر — تاريخ للصف الثالث الثانوي (History: Modern and Contemporary History of the World, for the Third Secondary Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Manifestations of the modern European renaissance (Unit 1 Lesson 1)
