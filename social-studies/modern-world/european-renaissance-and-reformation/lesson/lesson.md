As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The modern European Renaissance

Italy, living next door to the flourishing Arab civilisation of Spain, Sicily and the Levant, wakes first: Constantinople's scholars flee west in 1453 with their manuscripts, rich trading cities pay for art and translation from Arabic, paper and printing spread the new learning, and over three centuries Europe climbs out of its middle ages — Luther attacks the sale of indulgences and the clergy's monopoly on the Gospel, kings gather power into national monarchies, and the humanities, Greek science and the Muslim method of doubt, observation and experiment remake the mind of a continent
