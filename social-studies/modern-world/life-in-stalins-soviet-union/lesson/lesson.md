# Life in Stalin's Soviet Union

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In Stalin's Soviet Union of the 1930s, new schooling, work and healthcare sit beside queues, communal flats and fear. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 4.3 Life in the Soviet Union in the 1930s

Behind the posters lie queues, communal flats, Stakhanovite heroes, expanded schooling and healthcare — and the everyday fear that a knock at night brings arrest.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — 4.4 The position of women and ethnic minorities

Industrialisation draws women into work in huge numbers but leaves the domestic burden on them too, while the republics' national cultures are first fostered, then subordinated to Moscow.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: 4.3 Life in the Soviet Union in the 1930s; 4.4 The position of women and ethnic minorities. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
