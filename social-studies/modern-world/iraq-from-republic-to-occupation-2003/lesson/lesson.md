# Iraq — from republic to occupation, 2003

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: From the second British occupation of 1941 through the republic of 1958, Saddam, war with Iran and Kuwait, to the American occupation of 2003 and the withdrawal of 2011. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Iraq — from republic to occupation, 2003

Rashid Ali al-Gaylani's 1941 government was crushed by a second British occupation; the Baghdad Pact of 1955 and the Hashemite Union followed, then the revolution of 14 July 1958 made Iraq a republic; after al-Bakr and Saddam Hussein came the war with Iran (1980–1988), the invasion of Kuwait in 1990, the siege, and the American occupation of 2003 on a pretext the lesson says proved false, ending with the withdrawal of late 2011 — the same lesson tells of Arab Ahwaz (Arabistan), annexed by Reza Shah in 1925 and Persianised.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Iraq — from republic to occupation, 2003. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
