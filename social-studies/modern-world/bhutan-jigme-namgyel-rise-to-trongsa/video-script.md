# Video script — Bhutan — Jigme Namgyel's rise to Trongsa

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bhutan book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

a Pema Lingpa descendant born in 1825 walks to Trongsa at fifteen, rises from garpa retainer to zimpon by saving his Ponlop from assassins at Punakha, crushes the eastern revolt from Mongar, and in 1853 is made Trongsa Ponlop himself; the chamberlain who twice shields Trongsa Ponlop from assassins at Punakha, wears Lama Jangchub Tsondru's raven-headed helmet — prototype of the royal crown — and in 1850 marches east to reunify the rebellious Sharchog Khorlo Tsibgye districts under Trongsa for the last time
