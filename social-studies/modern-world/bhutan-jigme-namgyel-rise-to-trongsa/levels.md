Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: a Pema Lingpa descendant born in 1825 walks to Trongsa at fifteen, rises from garpa retainer to zimpon by saving his Ponlop from assassins at Punakha, crushes the eastern revolt from Mongar, and in 1853 is made Trongsa Ponlop himself
