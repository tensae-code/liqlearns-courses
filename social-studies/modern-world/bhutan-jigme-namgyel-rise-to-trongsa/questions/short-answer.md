# Bhutan — Jigme Namgyel's rise to Trongsa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter Five, Jigme Namgyel: early career.
**Answer: a Pema Lingpa descendant born in 1825 walks to Trongsa at fifteen, rises from garpa retainer to zimpon by saving his Ponlop from assassins at Punakha, crushes the eastern revolt from Mongar, and in 1853 is made Trongsa Ponlop himself (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Chapter Four, Jigme Namgyel (first half).
**Answer: the chamberlain who twice shields Trongsa Ponlop from assassins at Punakha, wears Lama Jangchub Tsondru's raven-headed helmet — prototype of the royal crown — and in 1850 marches east to reunify the rebellious Sharchog Khorlo Tsibgye districts under Trongsa for the last time (any close wording).**
