# Bhutan — Jigme Namgyel's rise to Trongsa — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter Five, Jigme Namgyel: early career  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter Five, Jigme Namgyel: early career** — a Pema Lingpa descendant born in 1825 walks to Trongsa at fifteen, rises from garpa retainer to zimpon by saving his Ponlop from assassins at Punakha, crushes the eastern revolt from Mongar, and in 1853 is made Trongsa Ponlop himself

**Answer: a) Chapter Five, Jigme Namgyel: early career** — the book's own heading for this part.
