# Hands-on — Timor-Leste — the referendum of 1999 and the restoration of 2002

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The class timeline
Draw the book's timeline in your own way: mark 1984, 1991, 1998, 30 August 1999 and 20 May 2002. Write one word under each date for what happened.

## Landslide vote
Draw a ballot box with two tallies, one very long and one very short, for the 1999 choice. Write one sentence on what 'by a landslide' means for how sure the people's answer was.
