# Timor-Leste — the referendum of 1999 and the restoration of 2002 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What opened the way to the 1999 referendum, and what did the people decide?
**Answer: Political change in Indonesia in 1998 and the founding of the CNRT opened the way; on 30 August 1999 the people chose independence by a landslide (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What happened between the vote and the restoration, and when was independence restored?
**Answer: Indonesia left amid scorched-earth destruction; independence was restored on 20 May 2002 (any close wording).**
