# Timor-Leste — the referendum of 1999 and the restoration of 2002 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The people voted against independence in 1999.
**Answer: False — they chose independence by a landslide on 30 August 1999.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The restoration of 2002 brought back the independence first proclaimed in 1975.
**Answer: True — the book teaches 20 May 2002 as the Restoration of Independence.**
