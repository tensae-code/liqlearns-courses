# Timor-Leste — the referendum of 1999 and the restoration of 2002 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Political change in Indonesia that opened the way to the referendum came in —
a) 1998  b) 1975  c) 1991
**Answer: a) 1998** — 1998 — with the CNRT founded as the resistance united.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The referendum, in which the people chose independence by a landslide, was on —
a) 30 August 1999  b) 28 November 1975  c) 20 May 2002
**Answer: a) 30 August 1999** — 30 August 1999 — after 24 years, the people finally voted.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** As Indonesia left after the vote, it carried out —
a) Scorched-earth destruction  b) A rebuilding programme  c) A second referendum
**Answer: a) Scorched-earth destruction** — Scorched-earth destruction — the country burned for its choice.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Independence was restored on —
a) 20 May 2002  b) 12 November 1991  c) 1 January 2000
**Answer: a) 20 May 2002** — 20 May 2002 — the flag raised again.
