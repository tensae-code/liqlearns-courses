# Video script — Timor-Leste — the referendum of 1999 and the restoration of 2002

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Timor-Leste's story as its Grade 6 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In 1998, political change in Indonesia opens a door that 24 years of resistance had pushed against. Timor-Leste's resistance unites in the CNRT, and on 30 August 1999 the people finally vote. They choose independence by a landslide. The book works this through a class timeline, 1984–1999, so children place the vote at the end of the long years they have studied.

The answer to the vote is fire: as Indonesia leaves, scorched-earth destruction burns the country the people have just chosen. But the story the book tells does not end in ashes. On 20 May 2002, independence is restored — the republic proclaimed for nine days in 1975 is raised again, this time to stay.
