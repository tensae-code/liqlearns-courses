# The Hungarian uprising, 1956

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Hungarians demand free elections and the right to strike, tear Stalin's star from their flag and burn his portraits; the Soviets withdraw, think again, and on November 1956 send 2,500 tanks into Budapest — the West admires and does nothing, fearing a third world war, while 200,000 Hungarians flee to Austria and at least 2,000 are executed; freedom waits until 1990. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
