# The Hungarian uprising, 1956 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Part 2, Unit 2.
**Answer: Hungarians demand free elections and the right to strike, tear Stalin's star from their flag and burn his portraits; the Soviets withdraw, think again, and on November 1956 send 2,500 tanks into Budapest — the West admires and does nothing, fearing a third world war, while 200,000 Hungarians flee to Austria and at least 2,000 are executed; freedom waits until 1990 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
