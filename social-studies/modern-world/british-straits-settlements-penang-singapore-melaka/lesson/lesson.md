# The British Straits Settlements — Penang, Singapore, Melaka

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore in 1819, the London Treaty of 1824 splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres, and Penang, Singapore and Melaka are governed together as the Straits Settlements. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Straits Settlements

Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore in January 1819 and sign a preliminary treaty with Temenggung Abdul Rahman, who insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification; rivalry is settled by the London Treaty of 1824, which splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres, and Penang, Singapore and Melaka are governed together as the Straits Settlements (Bab 2: the Straits Settlements)
