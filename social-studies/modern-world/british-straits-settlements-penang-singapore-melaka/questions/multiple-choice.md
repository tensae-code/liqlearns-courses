# The British Straits Settlements — Penang, Singapore, Melaka — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Straits Settlements  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Straits Settlements** — Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore in January 1819 and sign a preliminary treaty with Temenggung Abdul Rahman, who insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification; rivalry is settled by the London Treaty of 1824, which splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres, and Penang, Singapore and Melaka are governed together as the Straits Settlements

a) Bab 2: the Straits Settlements  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 2: the Straits Settlements** — the book's own heading for this part.
