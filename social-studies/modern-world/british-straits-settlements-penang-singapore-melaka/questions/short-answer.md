# The British Straits Settlements — Penang, Singapore, Melaka — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The Straits Settlements.
**Answer: Raffles and Farquhar land in Singapore in January 1819 and sign a preliminary treaty with Temenggung Abdul Rahman, who insists it needs Sultan Abdul Rahman's ratification; rivalry is settled by the London Treaty of 1824, which splits the Malay world into British and Dutch spheres, and Penang, Singapore and Melaka are governed together as the Straits Settlements (any close wording).**
