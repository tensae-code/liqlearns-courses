# The Arab Army — Latrun, Bab al-Wad and Karameh — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** on a school trip to the Ghor the class sees the Unknown Soldier memorial; Jordan's army fought at Latrun and Bab al-Wad for Palestine and won at Karameh, and 15 February honours the veterans.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2 Lesson 3: heroisms of the Arab Army

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف الرابع، الفصل الدراسي الأول (Social Studies, Grade 4, Term 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 2 Lesson 3: heroisms of the Arab Army

**3.
