# Video script — History of education in Afghanistan

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Afghanistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

children once learned only in mosques and homes; Amir Sher Ali Khan founds the first formal schools (one military, one civil), Habibullah opens Habibiya and Harbiya in Kabul, Amanullah adds modern schools and the Masturat girls' school, Kalakani closes them and Nadir Khan reopens them.
