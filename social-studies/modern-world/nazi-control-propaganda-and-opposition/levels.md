# Nazi control, propaganda and opposition — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — 8.1 The development of the Nazi police state
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — SS, Gestapo and SD watch the whole population and concentration camps hold the regime's enemies; fear, not consent alone, keeps the dictatorship safe

## Sub lesson 2 — 8.2 The Nazi control of the legal system
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Judges join the Nazi lawyers' league, the People's Court tries treason without appeal, and law becomes whatever serves the regime

## Sub lesson 3 — 8.3 Nazi policies towards the Church
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — A Concordat with the Vatican (1933) buys quiet while the regime builds its own Reich Church; pastors who resist, like Niemöller, end in camps

## Sub lesson 4 — 8.4 Goebbels and the Ministry of Propaganda
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Goebbels' ministry and Reich Chamber of Culture licence every journalist, actor and musician; cheap radios, rallies and film carry one message into every home

## Sub lesson 5 — 8.5 The Nazi control of the Arts
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Jazz and modern art are banned as degenerate while folk music, classical giants and monumental building glorify the regime — culture itself becomes propaganda

## Sub lesson 6 — 8.6 The Nazi control of sport
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Sport is Nazified through clubs and the 1936 Berlin Olympics are staged as a showcase of Aryan Germany for the world's cameras

## Sub lesson 7 — 9.1 The extent of support for the Nazi regime
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Many Germans conform for jobs, order and pride restored; the book weighs real popularity against fear to judge how far support was freely given

## Sub lesson 8 — 9.2 Opposition from young people
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Edelweiss Pirates and swing gangs grow their hair, play banned music and beat up Hitler Youth patrols — small refusals that the regime answers with prison and, later, the gallows

## Sub lesson 9 — 9.3 Opposition from the Churches
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Niemöller's Confessional Church and Bishop Galen's sermons defend faith against Nazi doctrine; Niemöller spends 1937–45 in prison and camps for preaching obedience to God over man
