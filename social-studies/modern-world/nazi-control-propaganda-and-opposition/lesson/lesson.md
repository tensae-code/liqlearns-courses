# Nazi control, propaganda and opposition

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Nazi regime controls Germany through a police state, propaganda and the arts — and some Germans resist. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 8.1 The development of the Nazi police state

SS, Gestapo and SD watch the whole population and concentration camps hold the regime's enemies; fear, not consent alone, keeps the dictatorship safe.
