# Hands-on — Peace Movements Between The Wars

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Peace Movements Between The Wars — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Chuyên đề 2.I.3 The struggle for peace between the wars: Lenin's Decree on Peace of 26-10-1917, the first anti-war programme in history; the League of Nations founded 10-1-1920; Locarno 1925; the 1928 Paris treaty renouncing war signed by 15 nations, later 57, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
