# Hands-on — Yugoslavia, 1945–1980 — a socialist federation

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Yugoslavia, 1945–1980 — a socialist federation — and say what changed in each part.
