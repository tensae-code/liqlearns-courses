# Yugoslavia, 1945–1980 — a socialist federation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Partisans' wartime parliament becomes a socialist federation of six republics.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.5.1 Yugoslavia 1945–1961: AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *ИСТОРИЈА — уџбеник за девети разред основне школе (Istorija 9)* is about Duklja — the first Montenegrin state.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.5.1 Yugoslavia 1945–1961: AVNOJ proclaims itself a Provisional People's Assembly in August 1945 to prepare elections for a Constituent Assembly; the Federal People's Republic and the Constitution of 1946 follow

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A socialist state borrows from the West to build a consumer society of its own.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.5.2 Yugoslavia 1961–1980

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *ИСТОРИЈА — уџбеник за девети разред основне школе (Istorija 9)* is about The Christianisation of the South Slavs.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch.5.2 Yugoslavia 1961–1980
