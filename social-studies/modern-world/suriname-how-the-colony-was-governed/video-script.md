# Video script — Suriname — how the colony was governed

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Europeans replace Indigenous self-rule with foreign governors; in 1683 the Chartered Society of Suriname takes the colony's government, and governor and Political Council often clash.

After 1863 and state supervision the country is divided into districts under district commissioners, and the long road to voting rights and representation begins.
