# Video script — Brazil — contemporary social movements

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Brazil's Grade 10 História SEED-PR tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

1968. A slogan on the streets: is it forbidden to forbid?



A generation questioned every authority that told them what they could not do.



Brazil's book brings that questioning home — to students, workers and women in the streets, to Black movements and landless movements, all contesting power in the pupils' own lifetime.



The argument over power is not finished. It is theirs now.
