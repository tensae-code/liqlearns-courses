# Hands-on — Brazil — contemporary social movements

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The slogan
Write the 1968 slogan — 'is it forbidden to forbid?' — large on a page, as a placard. Under it, write in one sentence what the students who carried it were questioning.

## Five movements, five claims
List the five movements from the lesson — students, workers, women, Black movements, landless movements. Beside each, write in a few words what claim that movement presses on the country's future.
