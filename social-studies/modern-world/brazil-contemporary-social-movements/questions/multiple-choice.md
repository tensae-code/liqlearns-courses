# Brazil — contemporary social movements — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The chapter opens with the 1968 slogan —
a) 'Is it forbidden to forbid?'  b) 'Obey everything'  c) 'Nothing can change'
**Answer: a) 'Is it forbidden to forbid?'**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The movements the book follows in Brazil include —
a) Students, workers, women, and Black and landless movements  b) Only the army  c) Only foreign governments
**Answer: a) Students, workers, women, and Black and landless movements**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** In the book's teaching, social movements —
a) Contest power from below, in the pupils' own lifetime  b) Ended long before the pupils were born  c) Never challenge who decides
**Answer: a) Contest power from below, in the pupils' own lifetime**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1968 students questioned —
a) Every authority that told them what they could not do, say or be  b) Only the weather  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Every authority that told them what they could not do, say or be**
