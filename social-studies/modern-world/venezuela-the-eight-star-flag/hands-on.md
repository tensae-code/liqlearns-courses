# Hands-on — Venezuela The Eight Star Flag

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela The Eight Star Flag — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The flag of the eight stars, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
