# Video script — Fiji — coups and constitutions, 1987 to 2014

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Mara's Alliance wins in 1972, Bavadra's coalition wins in 1987 and Rabuka's two coups make Fiji a republic, Chaudhry becomes first Indo-Fijian PM in 1999 and is toppled by Speight in 2000, Bainimarama coups in 2006, the constitution is suspended in 2009 and rewritten in 2013 before the 2014 election. Chaudhry's government, barely a year old, is seized with most MPs in the 2000 coup; the story is carried forward to the closely fought Fiji election of 2006. After the 1987 turn a new category of leadership emerges — interim ministers such as Senilagakali and Nailatikau serve governments born of coups, while non-political leaders champion integration from outside politics. Readings carry the story from the disputed aftermath of the 1977 election to Qarase's interim civilian government of 2000, formed after a coup. One month after the 1987 election, on 14 May, Major Sitiveni Rabuka leads a coup; his second coup destroys the chances of a negotiated settlement between the communities. The interim government's 1989 committee produces the 1990 constitution amid brain-drain; a three-person review commission follows, and its work issues in the 1997 Constitution Amendment Act.
