# Mass Society And Its New Politics — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Le Bon's crowd psychology and Mosse's new political style (Fare storia) describe a politics of masses that parties, unions and states all learn to organise.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 5, Cap. 16 §§4–8 Hygiene, medicine and demography; Mass society and the new middle classes; The nationalisation of the masses: school, army and universal suffrage; Parties and unions; Reformists and revolutionaries: the Second International

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 2 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XVII al XIX secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 5, Cap. 16 §§4–8 Hygiene, medicine and demography; Mass society and the new middle classes; The nationalisation of the masses: school, army and universal suffrage; Parties and unions; Reformists and revolutionaries: the Second International
