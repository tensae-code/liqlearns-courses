# Video script — Papua New Guinea — problem-solving in the past: Moka and compensation

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In Mt Hagen, wrongs and status were settled by Moka — reciprocal gifts of pigs, always bigger than what was received, building a Big Man's prestige; children compare that system with other traditional compensation across PNG and judge what it did well and where it fell short.
