# Video script — The Second World War — causes

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Dictators take Italy and Germany. Soldiers rule Japan.
Racist nationalism spreads in all three.
Arms pile up. Berlin, Rome, Tokyo join as the Axis.
Appeasement gives in. The League stands weak.
The road to a second world war lies open.

Two decades of grievance ignite at once — and when the fire dies, two superpowers stand in the ashes of Europe's primacy.
