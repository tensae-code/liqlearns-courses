# Hands-on — The Second World War — causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Axis triangle
Draw a world strip. Mark Berlin, Rome, Tokyo and join them into a triangle. Under it write what the three did together: armed heavily, prepared minds for war.

## Appeasement role-play
In pairs: one demands something unreasonable step by step; the other gives in each time 'for peace'. Stop and discuss: when does giving in feed the next demand? That is appeasement's failure.
