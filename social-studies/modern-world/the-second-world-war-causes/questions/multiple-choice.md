# The Second World War — causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Fascist-natured dictatorial governments took power in —
a) Italy and Germany  b) Britain and France  c) America and Russia  d) Spain and Portugal
**Answer: a) Italy and Germany** — Italy and Germany — with a military government in Japan.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan's government kind in the book's causes is —
a) A fascist dictatorship  b) A military government  c) A kingly government  d) A democracy
**Answer: b) A military government** — Japan: military government; Italy and Germany: fascist dictatorships.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The nationalism spreading in the three states was —
a) Gentle patriotism  b) Absolute racist nationalism  c) Cultural pride only  d) Anti-war feeling
**Answer: b) Absolute racist nationalism** — Absolute racist nationalism is the book's phrase.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Axis joined which three capitals?
a) Paris, London, Moscow  b) Berlin, Rome, Tokyo  c) Vienna, Madrid, Lisbon  d) Warsaw, Prague, Budapest
**Answer: b) Berlin, Rome, Tokyo** — The Berlin–Rome–Tokyo Axis.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Appeasement was the policy of —
a) Britain and France, and it failed  b) Germany and Italy, and it worked  c) The League, enforced strongly  d) America, by embargo
**Answer: a) Britain and France, and it failed** — Britain's and France's appeasement failed — a named cause of the war.
