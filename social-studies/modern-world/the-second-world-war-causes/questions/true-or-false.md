# The Second World War — causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The three Axis states spent heavily on arms and prepared their peoples' minds for war.
**Answer: True** — Arms, forces and minds — all three preparations are named.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The League of Nations was strong and stopped the dictators early.
**Answer: False** — The League's weakness is itself a cause in the book.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Racist nationalism spread in Italy, Germany and Japan.
**Answer: True** — In all three states, as printed.
