# The Second World War — causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the book's four basic causes of the Second World War.
**Answer:** Fascist dictatorships in Italy and Germany and a military government in Japan; absolute racist nationalism in the three states; heavy arms spending, war forces and minds prepared, joined in the Axis; the failure of appeasement and the League's weakness.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the Axis frighten other countries?
**Answer:** Three armed, war-minded states acting together made every other country's security feel threatened.
