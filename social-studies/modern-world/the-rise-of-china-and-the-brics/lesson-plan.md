**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unità 6, Cap. 22 §2 A new multipolar set-up: China, the BRICS and the Gulf countries** — the New Silk Road (the chapter's maps) draws China's reach; growth shifts the world's centre while the West debates whether its liberal order is ending
2. **Modulo 16 §§1–2 Le nuove gerarchie della ricchezza; Il boom della Cina** — wealth's map is redrawn — Stiglitz on the Washington Consensus in East Asia and Rampini's "Chinese century" (Fare storia pieces) argue how China rose inside globalisation's rules
3. **Modulo 19 §3 Russia e Cina** — an authoritarian Russia and a state-capitalist China offer rival models to the liberal order — the present's scenarios are drawn as a contest of systems
