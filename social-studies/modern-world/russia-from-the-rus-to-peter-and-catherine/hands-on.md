# Hands-on — Russia — from the Rus to Peter and Catherine

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Russia — from the Rus to Peter and Catherine — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Lesson 16: Russia in the medieval and modern ages, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
