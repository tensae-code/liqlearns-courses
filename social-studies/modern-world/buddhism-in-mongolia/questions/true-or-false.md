# Buddhism in Mongolia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** monasteries multiply across Khalkha, the Bogd Jebtsundamba's Ikh Khüree becomes a city of temples, and a large share of men live as lamas.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. I.3: Buddhism spreads widely in Mongolia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *mn-g8h Түүх VIII (History VIII)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. I.3: Buddhism spreads widely in Mongolia

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the socialist state first taxes and restricts the monasteries, then moves to destroy them; a living church of hundreds of temples is reduced to almost none.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 3.8 Buddhism and socialism

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *mn-g9h Түүх IX (History IX)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 3.8 Buddhism and socialism
