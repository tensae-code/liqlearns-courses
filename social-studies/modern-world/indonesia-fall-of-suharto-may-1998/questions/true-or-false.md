# Indonesia — the fall of Suharto, May 1998 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Asian monetary crisis empties the shops, Trisakti students are shot, Jakarta burns, and at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998 in the Istana Negara Suharto reads his resignation.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 3.D Masa Akhir Pemerintahan Orde Baru

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII (Kurikulum Merdeka)* is about Indonesia — the Krakatau eruption of 1883.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 3.D Masa Akhir Pemerintahan Orde Baru
