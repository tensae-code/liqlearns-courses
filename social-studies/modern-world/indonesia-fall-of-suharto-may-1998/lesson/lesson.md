# Indonesia — the fall of Suharto, May 1998

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The Asian monetary crisis empties the shops, Trisakti students are shot, Jakarta burns, and at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998 Suharto reads his resignation at the Istana Negara As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab 3.D Masa Akhir Pemerintahan Orde Baru

The Asian monetary crisis empties the shops, Trisakti students are shot, Jakarta burns, and at 09.00 on Thursday 21 May 1998 in the Istana Negara Suharto reads his resignation.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Bab 3.D Masa Akhir Pemerintahan Orde Baru. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
