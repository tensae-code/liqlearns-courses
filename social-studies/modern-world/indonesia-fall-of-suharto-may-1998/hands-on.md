# Hands-on — Indonesia — the fall of Suharto, May 1998

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Indonesia — the fall of Suharto, May 1998 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bab 3.D Masa Akhir Pemerintahan Orde Baru, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
