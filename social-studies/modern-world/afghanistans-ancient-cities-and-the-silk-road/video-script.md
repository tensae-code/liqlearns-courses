# Video script — Afghanistan's ancient cities and the Silk Road

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Afghanistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Balkh, "Mother of Cities", Herat, Bamiyan, Kabul, Kapisa, Bust, Ghor and Ghazni are named as the homeland's ancient cities, and the Silk Road through them once tied China and India to Europe.
