# Hands-on — Southeast Asia Colonised Mainland

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Southeast Asia Colonised Mainland — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 5 The mainland falls later: Britain needs three wars across sixty years (1824–1885) to take Burma; France needs nearly half a century (1858–1893) to take the three Indochina countries; Siam alone keeps formal independence as a buffer between the two empires, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
