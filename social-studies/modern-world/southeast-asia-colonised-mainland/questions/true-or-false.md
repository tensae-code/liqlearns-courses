# Southeast Asia Colonised Mainland — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the mainland kingdoms make the empires pay in decades, and one kingdom survives by standing between them.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 5 The mainland falls later: Britain needs three wars across sixty years (1824–1885) to take Burma; France needs nearly half a century (1858–1893) to take the three Indochina countries; Siam alone keeps formal independence as a buffer between the two empires

**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 5 The mainland falls later: Britain needs three wars across sixty years (1824–1885) to take Burma; France needs nearly half a century (1858–1893) to take the three Indochina countries; Siam alone keeps formal independence as a buffer between the two empires
