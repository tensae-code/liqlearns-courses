## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 7, the Nghệ-Tĩnh soviets
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Depression bites the colony and strikes flare at Phú Riềng, Nam Định and Bến Thuỷ; on 1 May 1930 workers demonstrate for the first time and a red flag flies on Bài Thơ mountain; in September–October 1930 the peasants of Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh raise soviets that divide the public land, abolish taxes and run literacy classes — until French aircraft bomb an 8,000-strong peasant demonstration at Hưng Nguyên (12 September 1930) and the movement is crushed by early 1931, leaving the worker–peasant alliance as its legacy
