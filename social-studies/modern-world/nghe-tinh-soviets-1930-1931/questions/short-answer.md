# Nghe Tinh Soviets 1930 1931 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 7, the Nghệ-Tĩnh soviets.
**Answer: the Depression bites the colony and strikes flare at Phú Riềng, Nam Định and Bến Thuỷ; on 1 May 1930 workers demonstrate for the first time and a red flag flies on Bài Thơ mountain; in September–October 1930 the peasants of Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh raise soviets that divide the public land, abolish taxes and run literacy classes — until French aircraft bomb an 8,000-strong peasant demonstration at Hưng Nguyên (12 September 1930) and the movement is crushed by early 1931, leaving the worker–peasant alliance as its legacy (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 9 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 7, the Nghệ-Tĩnh soviets, printed p. 30.**
