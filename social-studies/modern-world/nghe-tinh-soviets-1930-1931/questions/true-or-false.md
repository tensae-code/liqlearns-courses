# Nghe Tinh Soviets 1930 1931 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Depression bites the colony and strikes flare at Phú Riềng, Nam Định and Bến Thuỷ; on 1 May 1930 workers demonstrate for the first time and a red flag flies on Bài Thơ mountain; in September–October 1930 the peasants of Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh raise soviets that divide the public land, abolish taxes and run literacy classes — until French aircraft bomb an 8,000-strong peasant demonstration at Hưng Nguyên (12 September 1930) and the movement is crushed by early 1931, leaving the worker–peasant alliance as its legacy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 7, the Nghệ-Tĩnh soviets

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 9 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 7, the Nghệ-Tĩnh soviets
