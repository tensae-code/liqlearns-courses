# Hands-on — Brazil — from slavery to free labour

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two endings, side by side
Draw two columns. Head one Brazil and one United States. In each, write how slavery ended there. Under both columns, write the one thing that was the same: what the freed were left to do.

## Empty-handed freedom
Draw a freed person in 1888 Brazil with empty hands. Around the drawing, write the two things the book says the freed did not receive — land and rights — and one sentence on why that mattered.
