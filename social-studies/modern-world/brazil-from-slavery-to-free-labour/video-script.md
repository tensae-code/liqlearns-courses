# Video script — Brazil — from slavery to free labour

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Brazil's Grade 10 História SEED-PR tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Two countries. Two slave societies. And two very different endings to slavery.



In Brazil, slavery ended slowly, law by law, until abolition in 1888. But the freed were given no land and no rights — freedom, empty-handed.



In the United States, slavery ended through civil war.



Different roads — the same hard landing. In both countries, the freed were left to compete as wage workers, free to sell their labour, with nothing else to live on.
