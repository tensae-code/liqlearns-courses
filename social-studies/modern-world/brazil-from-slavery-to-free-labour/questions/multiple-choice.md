# Brazil — from slavery to free labour — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Brazil ended slavery —
a) By gradual laws, reaching abolition in 1888  b) In a single day, with no laws before it  c) It never ended
**Answer: a) By gradual laws, reaching abolition in 1888**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The freed people in Brazil received —
a) No land and no rights to start with  b) Large farms for every family  c) Seats in government at once
**Answer: a) No land and no rights to start with**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The United States ended slavery —
a) Through civil war  b) Through gradual laws only  c) By buying every enslaved person
**Answer: a) Through civil war**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** In both countries, after slavery ended, the freed were left to —
a) Compete as wage workers  b) Own the plantations  c) Leave work behind forever
**Answer: a) Compete as wage workers**
