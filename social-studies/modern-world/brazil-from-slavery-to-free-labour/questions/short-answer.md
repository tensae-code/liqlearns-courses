# Brazil — from slavery to free labour — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did Brazil's ending of slavery differ from the United States' ending?
**Answer: Brazil ended slavery by gradual laws to abolition in 1888; the United States ended it through civil war (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What did the freed in Brazil not receive at abolition, and what were the freed in both countries left to do?
**Answer: They received no land and no rights; in both countries the freed were left to compete as wage workers (any close wording).**
