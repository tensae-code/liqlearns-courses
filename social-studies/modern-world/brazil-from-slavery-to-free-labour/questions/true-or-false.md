# Brazil — from slavery to free labour — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abolition in Brazil came in 1888.
**Answer: True — after gradual laws, abolition came in 1888.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches that the freed in Brazil and the United States landed in the same place — competing as wage workers.
**Answer: True — different endings, the same hard landing as wage workers.**
