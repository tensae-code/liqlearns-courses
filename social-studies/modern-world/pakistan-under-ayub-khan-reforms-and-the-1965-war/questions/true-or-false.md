# Pakistan under Ayub Khan — reforms and the 1965 war — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.5: Ayub Khan's regime — presidential system, monetary policy, five-year plans, industrialisation, administrative reforms and a new capital at Islamabad; the Indus Basin Treaty (1960) divides the rivers with India

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ayub Khan — development instead of politics

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kashmir sparks a seventeen-day war that ends where it began but lives on as a national epic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.5: the Indo-Pak War of 1965 — causes, the fighting, national unity and the ceasefire
