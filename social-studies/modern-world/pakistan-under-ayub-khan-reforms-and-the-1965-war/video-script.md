# Video script — Pakistan under Ayub Khan — reforms and the 1965 war

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A general promises development instead of politics — five-year plans, industrialisation, a new capital at Islamabad and the 1960 Indus Waters Treaty — then Kashmir sparks the seventeen-day war of 1965, which ends where it began.
