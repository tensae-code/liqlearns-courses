# Lesson plan — War Crimes And The Holocaust

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** War Crimes And The Holocaust
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Moldova, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** after Kristallnacht the persecution spreads; some 135,000 Romanian Jews perish in northern Transylvania, Roma nomads are deported in summer 1942, and Polish officers are shot in the back of the head and buried in common graves
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. 2. **Tema 28: Crime împotriva umanităţii: Gulag, Holocaust, Katyn** — the Gulag deports whole peoples — 1,209,000 Soviet Germans, 479,000 Chechens and Ingush, 191,014 Crimean Tatars; the Holocaust's victim table is estimated, and Tokyo's tribunal judges Japan's war criminals from May 1946
