# Video script — Sri Lanka — The republic constitutions, 1972 and 1978

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Sri Lanka's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The 1970 coalition wins two-thirds and in 1972 Ceylon becomes the Republic of Sri Lanka under President Gopallawa, then J. R. Jayewardene's 1978 constitution installs an executive presidency and proportional representation.
