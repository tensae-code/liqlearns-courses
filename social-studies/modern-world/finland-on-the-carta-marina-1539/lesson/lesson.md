# Finland on the Carta Marina, 1539

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Finland, then part of Sweden, appears as Finlandia on late-medieval maps; exiled churchman Olaus Magnus spends 12 years drawing the North and finishes Carta Marina in 1539, the first northern map rich in place names and pictures, used for a hundred years until Anders Bure's better map replaces it As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Taustatietoa Carta Marinasta (background: Finland enters Europe's maps)

Finland, then part of Sweden, appears as Finlandia on late-medieval maps; exiled churchman Olaus Magnus spends 12 years drawing the North and finishes Carta Marina in 1539, the first northern map rich in place names and pictures, used for a hundred years until Anders Bure's better map replaces it.
