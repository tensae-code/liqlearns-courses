# Finland on the Carta Marina, 1539 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Taustatietoa Carta Marinasta (background: Finland enters Europe's maps)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Finland, then part of Sweden, appears as Finlandia on late-medieval maps; exiled churchman Olaus Magnus spends 12 years drawing the North and finishes Carta Marina in 1539, the first northern map rich in place names and pictures, used for a hundred years until Anders Bure's better map replaces it
