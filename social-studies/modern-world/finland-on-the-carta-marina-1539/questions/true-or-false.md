# Finland on the Carta Marina, 1539 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Finland, then part of Sweden, appears as Finlandia on late-medieval maps; exiled churchman Olaus Magnus spends 12 years drawing the North and finishes Carta Marina in 1539, the first northern map rich in place names and pictures, used for a hundred years until Anders Bure's better map replaces it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Taustatietoa Carta Marinasta (background: Finland enters Europe's maps)

**Answer: False** — it is about Taustatietoa Carta Marinasta (background: Finland enters Europe's maps)
