# Armenia In The 1400s Between Timur And The Turkmen — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §4.1 Armenia in the 1400s.
**Answer: Timur's invasions and the Qara Qoyunlu and Aq Qoyunlu turkmen confederations rule a land whose church becomes its only national institution (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *«Հայոց պատմություն 8. Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար» (History of Armenia 8, textbook for general schools) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, Ala A. Kharatyan, Zaruhi A. Hakobyan* (Armenia), §4.1 Armenia in the 1400s, printed p. 60.**
