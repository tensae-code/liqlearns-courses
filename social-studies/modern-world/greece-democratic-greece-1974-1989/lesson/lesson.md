# Greece Democratic Greece 1974 1989

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Karamanlis restores democracy, a referendum ends the monarchy, and Greece joins the European Community As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Greece from the Metapolitefsi to the early 1980s (1974–1981)

Karamanlis restores democracy, a referendum ends the monarchy, and Greece joins the European Community

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Greece in the 1980s (1981–1989)

PASOK's 'Change' brings social reform, European money and new scandals in equal measure

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Metapolitefsi (1974–1989)

democracy returns, the monarchy is voted away, Greece enters the European Community

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 4 — Greece of the Metapolitefsi and entry into united Europe

democracy restored, the monarchy abolished by referendum, EC membership in 1981 and the PASOK change of the 1980s

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Greece from the Metapolitefsi to the early 1980s (1974–1981) — Karamanlis restores democracy, a referendum ends the monarchy, and Greece joins the European Community, Greece in the 1980s (1981–1989) — PASOK's 'Change' brings social reform, European money and new scandals in equal measure, The Metapolitefsi (1974–1989) — democracy returns, the monarchy is voted away, Greece enters the European Community, Greece of the Metapolitefsi and entry into united Europe — democracy restored, the monarchy abolished by referendum, EC membership in 1981 and the PASOK change of the 1980s. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
