# Lesson plan — The Middle East wars, 1967–1988

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** The Middle East wars, 1967–1988
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 7, The World and Turkey in the Age of Social Revolution (world part): the Arab-Israeli wars and the Iran-Iraq War.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: Turkey's southern neighbours fight three generations of wars while oil redraws their weight in the world. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the two war stories the book teaches: the Arab-Israeli wars and the Iran-Iraq War.
2. Say how long the Iran-Iraq War ground on: eight years.
3. Say what redrew the region's weight in the world through these wars: oil.
4. Explain, in their own words, the neighbour's view the book teaches — Turkey watching wars on two of its borders.
