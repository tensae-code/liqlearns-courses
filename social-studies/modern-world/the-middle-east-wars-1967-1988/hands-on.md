# Hands-on — The Middle East wars, 1967–1988

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The neighbourhood map

Draw the region with Turkey at the top. Mark the Arab-Israeli front and the Iraq-Iran front in two colours, and write beside each one line: who fights, and since when.

## Oil under the wars

Draw a derrick beneath a battlefield map. Write two sentences: how oil makes the world's powers care about the region's wars, and how that caring can lengthen them.

## The watchful neighbour

Write a short diary of a Turkish border-town family across these decades: three entries — a war to the south, a war to the east, and a year of quiet — each in two lines.
