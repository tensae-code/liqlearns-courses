# The Middle East wars, 1967–1988 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The two war stories named are —
a) The Arab-Israeli wars and the Iran-Iraq War  b) The Korean and Vietnam wars  c) The Balkan wars only
**Answer: a) The Arab-Israeli wars and the Iran-Iraq War** — the unit's world part.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Iran-Iraq War lasted —
a) Six weeks  b) Eight years  c) One year
**Answer: b) Eight years** — the long attrition.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** What redrew the region's weight in the world?
a) Its rivers  b) Its oil  c) Its deserts
**Answer: b) Its oil** — wealth under the ground.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Turkey's place in this story is —
a) A belligerent in every war  b) The watchful neighbour on two borders  c) Far from the region
