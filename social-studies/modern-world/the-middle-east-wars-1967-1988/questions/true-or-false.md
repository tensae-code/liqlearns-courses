# The Middle East wars, 1967–1988 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Arab-Israeli wars came in repeated rounds across the decades.
**Answer: True** — three generations of wars.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Oil made the region matter less to the world's powers during these wars.
**Answer: False** — oil redrew — raised — the region's weight in the world.
