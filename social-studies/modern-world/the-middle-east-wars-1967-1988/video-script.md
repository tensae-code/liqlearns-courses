# Video script — The Middle East wars, 1967–1988

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

South of Turkey, the late twentieth century beats with wars. The Arab-Israeli wars come round after round; then Iraq and Iran grind through eight years of war on Turkey's eastern doorstep.

Under all of it, oil: the region's underground wealth redraws its weight in the world, and pulls the great powers into every quarrel.

Turkey's book teaches the neighbour's view: three generations of wars next door — and a country that must watch two of its borders at once.
