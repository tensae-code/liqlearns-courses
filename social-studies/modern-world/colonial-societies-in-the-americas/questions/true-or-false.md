# Colonial societies in the Americas, 1500–1700 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** English settler colonies transformed everyone they touched.
**Answer: True** — They transformed settlers, Native peoples and the land itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Spain, the Dutch, the French and England all built exactly the same kind of colony.
**Answer: False** — Each built a different kind — ranked, trading, and settler colonies.
