# Colonial societies in the Americas, 1500–1700 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spain's colonial society in America was —
a) Rank-free and equal  b) A ranked social order  c) Run by Native councils
**Answer: b) A ranked social order** — Spain builds a ranked colonial society.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Dutch and French colonies leaned mainly on —
a) Tobacco plantations  b) Furs and trade  c) Gold mines only
**Answer: b) Furs and trade** — the Dutch and French chase furs and empire.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Jamestown is linked in this lesson with —
a) Puritans  b) Tobacco planters  c) Fur traders only
**Answer: b) Tobacco planters** — Jamestown's tobacco planters.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Massachusetts is linked with —
a) The Puritans  b) The conquistadors  c) The Dutch
**Answer: a) The Puritans** — Massachusetts' Puritans.
