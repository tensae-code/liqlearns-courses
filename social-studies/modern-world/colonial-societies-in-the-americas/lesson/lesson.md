# Colonial societies in the Americas, 1500–1700

## Do it first

Three different empires built three different Americas. This lesson watches Spain, the Dutch and French, and England each remake Native lands — and be changed themselves. Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think happened — hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Spain builds a ranked colonial society

Spain built a ranked colonial society in America — an ordered ladder of peoples, with Spanish rulers at the top and Native peoples and enslaved Africans pressed below. The colony was a new social order, deliberately built.

[Picture slot — a picture of the world of this lesson as the book shows it: point to the picture and say what changed in this part of the story.]

## Sub lesson 2 — The Dutch and French chase furs and empire

The Dutch and French came chasing furs and empire. Their colonies leaned on trade and alliances with Native peoples — a thinner, trading kind of colony beside Spain's settled, ranked one.

## Sub lesson 3 — England's settlers remake Native lands

England's settlers remade Native lands into colonies: Jamestown's tobacco planters in the south, Massachusetts' Puritans in the north. Settler colonies transformed everyone they touched — settlers, Native peoples and the land itself.
