# Hands-on — Timor-Leste — the Indonesian invasion and the war, 1975–1979

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Mountain retreat
Draw a simple scene: a town below and mountains behind. Draw the people's army's path retreating to the mountains, and write one sentence on why the mountains mattered.

## A poem title as evidence
Write the poem's title in both languages from this lesson. Under it, write what its title alone — Torn Hopes — tells a historian about how the war was lived.
