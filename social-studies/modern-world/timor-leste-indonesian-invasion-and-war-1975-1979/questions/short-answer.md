# Timor-Leste — the Indonesian invasion and the war, 1975–1979 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happened in December 1975, and how did Fretilin first fight?
**Answer: Indonesia invaded the newly proclaimed republic, and Fretilin organised the war first as positional war (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the encirclement-and-annihilation campaigns do, and through what two things does the book teach them?
**Answer: They devastated the population; the book teaches them through photographs and Xanana Gusmão's poem Esperanças Rasgadas, Torn Hopes (any close wording).**
