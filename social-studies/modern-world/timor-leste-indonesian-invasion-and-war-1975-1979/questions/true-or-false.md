# Timor-Leste — the Indonesian invasion and the war, 1975–1979 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The invasion came years after the proclamation, when the republic was well established.
**Answer: False — Indonesia invaded in December 1975, days after the 28 November proclamation.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book teaches the devastation through photographs and a poem, not only through maps.
**Answer: True — photographs and Xanana Gusmão's Esperanças Rasgadas carry this part of the lesson.**
