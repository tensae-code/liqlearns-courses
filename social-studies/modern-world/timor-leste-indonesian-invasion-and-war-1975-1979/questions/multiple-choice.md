# Timor-Leste — the Indonesian invasion and the war, 1975–1979 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indonesia invaded Timor-Leste in —
a) December 1975  b) November 1991  c) August 1999
**Answer: a) December 1975** — December 1975 — days after the proclamation.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Fretilin first organised the war as —
a) Positional war  b) A naval war  c) No war at all
**Answer: a) Positional war** — Positional war — holding ground as a regular army.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Indonesia's campaigns against the population are named in the book as —
a) Encirclement-and-annihilation campaigns  b) Trade campaigns  c) Building campaigns
**Answer: a) Encirclement-and-annihilation campaigns** — Encirclement-and-annihilation campaigns — which devastated the population.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Esperanças Rasgadas (Torn Hopes) is a poem by —
a) Xanana Gusmão  b) Zeca Afonso  c) Francisco Xavier do Amaral
**Answer: a) Xanana Gusmão** — Xanana Gusmão — read in the book alongside photographs of the war.
