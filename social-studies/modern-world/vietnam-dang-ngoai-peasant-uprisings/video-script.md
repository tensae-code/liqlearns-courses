# Video script — Vietnam Dang Ngoai Peasant Uprisings

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Vietnam's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

the chronicles quoted here do the accusing — in 1730 tens of thousands of Hải Dương people are conscripted to dig rivers and haul bricks for Lord Trịnh Giang's pagodas; the lord's palace keeps four or five hundred arrogant eunuchs "hated and feared by the whole country"; a tax is laid on every trade, so that for the silk tax people cut down their mulberry trees and for the fish tax hide their nets; famine follows, and "a hundred đồng cannot buy one full meal"
