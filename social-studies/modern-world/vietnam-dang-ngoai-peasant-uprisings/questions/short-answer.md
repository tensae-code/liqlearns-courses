# Vietnam Dang Ngoai Peasant Uprisings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 7, why Đàng Ngoài exploded: the crisis of the Lê–Trịnh regime.
**Answer: the chronicles quoted here do the accusing — in 1730 tens of thousands of Hải Dương people are conscripted to dig rivers and haul bricks for Lord Trịnh Giang's pagodas; the lord's palace keeps four or five hundred arrogant eunuchs "hated and feared by the whole country"; a tax is laid on every trade, so that for the silk tax people cut down their mulberry trees and for the fish tax hide their nets; famine follows, and "a hundred đồng cannot buy one full meal" (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 7, why Đàng Ngoài exploded: the crisis of the Lê–Trịnh regime, printed p. 31.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 7, the great risings of the 18th-century North.
**Answer: Hoàng Công Chất (1739–1769) gathers the poor, builds his base at Mường Thanh in the far north-west, protects the border and settles people's lives so the Tây Bắc peoples support him to the end, and his son fights on after him; Nguyễn Danh Phương (1740–1751) holds the Tam Đảo mountains until he is captured; Nguyễn Hữu Cầu (1741–1751), the "Quận He" who swims like the great he fish that never harms a soul at sea, strikes from Đồ Sơn, threatens Thăng Long itself and ranges into Thanh Hoá and Nghệ An — all fail, but they shake the Lê–Trịnh regime to its foundations and clear the ground for Tây Sơn (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 7, the great risings of the 18th-century North, printed p. 33.**
