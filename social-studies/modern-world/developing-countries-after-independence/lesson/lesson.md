# Developing Countries After Independence

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: newly independent nations build industries and regional blocs and demand a fairer world economic order As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — New development of Asian, African and Latin American countries

newly independent nations build industries and regional blocs and demand a fairer world economic order

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — The colonial system collapses, emerging countries develop

1960, the 'Year of Africa', breaks the colonial map; new states build the Non-Aligned Movement and demand a new economic order

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: New development of Asian, African and Latin American countries — newly independent nations build industries and regional blocs and demand a fairer world economic order. The colonial system collapses, emerging countries develop — 1960, the 'Year of Africa', breaks the colonial map; new states build the Non-Aligned Movement and demand a new economic order. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
