# Developing Countries After Independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Newly independent nations build industries and regional blocs and demand a fairer world economic order.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in New development of Asian, African and Latin American countries

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *世界历史 九年级 下册 (World History, Grade 9, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about New development of Asian, African and Latin American countries

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1960, the 'Year of Africa', breaks the colonial map; new states build the Non-Aligned Movement and demand a new economic order.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The colonial system collapses, emerging countries develop

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（下） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, lower volume — world history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
