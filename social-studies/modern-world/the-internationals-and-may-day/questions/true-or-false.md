# The Internationals And May Day — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the First International (1864–1876), founded by Marx and Engels, carries scientific socialism into the movement and wins resolutions for strikes, unions and the eight-hour day; on 1 May 1886 some 400,000 Chicago workers strike for that eight-hour day and, though repressed, over 50,000 win it — from 1889 the day is International Labour Day; workers' parties follow (German Social Democrats 1875, French Workers' Party 1879, Russia's Emancipation of Labour group 1883), the Second International is founded in Paris on 14 July 1889, and after Engels' death, as revisionism creeps in, Lenin takes up Marx and Engels' cause.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 11, the Internationals and the workers' parties

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 11, the Internationals and the workers' parties
