# Soviet Socialist Construction — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The USSR industrialises at breakneck speed under five-year plans, building Magnitogorsk out of the steppe.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Soviet socialist construction

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *世界历史 九年级 下册 (World History, Grade 9, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Soviet socialist construction
