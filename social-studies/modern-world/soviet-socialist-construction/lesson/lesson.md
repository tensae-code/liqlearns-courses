# Soviet Socialist Construction

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the USSR industrialises at breakneck speed under five-year plans, building Magnitogorsk out of the steppe As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Soviet socialist construction

the USSR industrialises at breakneck speed under five-year plans, building Magnitogorsk out of the steppe

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Soviet socialist practice

war communism, the NEP retreat, then Stalin's five-year plans and collectivisation industrialise a peasant empire at terrible cost

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Soviet socialist construction — the USSR industrialises at breakneck speed under five-year plans, building Magnitogorsk out of the steppe. Soviet socialist practice — war communism, the NEP retreat, then Stalin's five-year plans and collectivisation industrialise a peasant empire at terrible cost. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
