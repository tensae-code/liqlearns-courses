# China Qing Consolidation Of A Multi Ethnic State — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Consolidating the unified multi-ethnic state.
**Answer: Zheng Chenggong besieges the Dutch for eight months and in 1662 recovers Taiwan after 38 years, then settles soldiers to farm it and teaches highland peoples new farming — Qing binds Taiwan, Tibet and the northwest into one state (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* (China), Lesson 18: Consolidating the unified multi-ethnic state, printed p. 88.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Early-mid Qing — height and crisis.
**Answer: Kangxi, Yongzheng and Qianlong fix China's modern map from Taiwan to Xinjiang and Tibet at the dynasty's dazzling height (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 14: Early-mid Qing — height and crisis, printed p. 82.**
