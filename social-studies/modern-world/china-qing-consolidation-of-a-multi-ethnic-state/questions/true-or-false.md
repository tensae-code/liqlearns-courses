# China Qing Consolidation Of A Multi Ethnic State — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zheng Chenggong besieges the Dutch for eight months and in 1662 recovers Taiwan after 38 years, then settles soldiers to farm it and teaches highland peoples new farming — Qing binds Taiwan, Tibet and the northwest into one state.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Consolidating the unified multi-ethnic state

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *中国历史 七年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 7, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Consolidating the unified multi-ethnic state

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kangxi, Yongzheng and Qianlong fix China's modern map from Taiwan to Xinjiang and Tibet at the dynasty's dazzling height.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Early-mid Qing — height and crisis

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Early-mid Qing — height and crisis
