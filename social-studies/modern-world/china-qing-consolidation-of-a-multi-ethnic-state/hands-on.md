# Hands-on — China Qing Consolidation Of A Multi Ethnic State

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Consolidating the unified multi-ethnic state, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
