# Lesson plan — Vietnam Viet Bac And Border Campaigns

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Viet Bac And Border Campaigns
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** north of the 16th parallel the cities fight for three months in 1946–47 — Hanoi at the Bắc Bộ Phủ, the Bờ Hồ Post Office, Hàng Cỏ Station and Khâm Thiên street, a suicide soldier hurling a three-pronged bomb at a tank — before the main forces withdraw intact; in autumn–winter 1947 some 12,000 French troops with most of Indochina's aircraft attack Việt Bắc to destroy the resistance headquarters and seal the China border, and are ambushed on Road No. 4 and the Lô river until they flee — "quick attack, quick victory" fails completely; in September–October 1950 the Border Campaign answers the US-aided Revers Plan: Đông Khê falls first, isolating Cao Bằng, the French relief and withdrawal columns are destroyed piecemeal on Road No. 4, and France abandons Cao Bằng, Thất Khê, Na Sầm and Lạng Sơn — the army's first great offensive wins the initiative and opens the international supply route
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 14, Việt Bắc 1947 and the Border Campaign 1950** — north of the 16th parallel the cities fight for three months in 1946–47 — Hanoi at the Bắc Bộ Phủ, the Bờ Hồ Post Office, Hàng Cỏ Station and Khâm Thiên street, a suicide soldier hurling a three-pronged bomb at a tank — before the main forces withdraw intact; in autumn–winter 1947 some 12,000 French troops with most of Indochina's aircraft attack Việt Bắc to destroy the resistance headquarters and seal the China border, and are ambushed on Road No. 4 and the Lô river until they flee — "quick attack, quick victory" fails completely; in September–October 1950 the Border Campaign answers the US-aided Revers Plan: Đông Khê falls first, isolating Cao Bằng, the French relief and withdrawal columns are destroyed piecemeal on Road No. 4, and France abandons Cao Bằng, Thất Khê, Na Sầm and Lạng Sơn — the army's first great offensive wins the initiative and opens the international supply route
2. **Bài 7 The resistance's first campaigns, 1946–1950: sixty days of street fighting pin the French in Hanoi and the northern cities while the government withdraws intact (Dec 1946–Feb 1947); the Việt Bắc counter-offensive (autumn–winter 1947) throws France's drive on the resistance capital back and kills the "quick attack, quick victory" strategy; the Border campaign (autumn–winter 1950) takes Đông Khê, ambushes Route 4, opens the road to China and hands the initiative on the main battlefield to Vietnam** — each French plan dies in turn — first the blitz, then the decapitation strike, then the sealed border
