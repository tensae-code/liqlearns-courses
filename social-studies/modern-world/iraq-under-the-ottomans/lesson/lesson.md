# Iraq under the Ottomans

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Suleiman the Magnificent takes Baghdad in 1534 and four centuries of Ottoman rule follow — stagnation stirred late by Tanzimat reforms, steam navigation and the telegraph, and by Baghdad's own repeated risings against its governors. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
