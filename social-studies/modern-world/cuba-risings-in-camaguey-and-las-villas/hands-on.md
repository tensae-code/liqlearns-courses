# Hands-on — Cuba Risings In Camaguey And Las Villas

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 5.3 Risings in Camagüey and Las Villas, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
