# Honduras Independence And The Civic Calendar — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The civic calendar gathers the births of Morazán and Valle, Independence Day and Labour Day, and a picture of the signing of the Act of Independence asks what event it shows.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 26 Nos identificamos con todos: fechas cívicas

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Primer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 26 Nos identificamos con todos: fechas cívicas

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1 September Flag Day, 15 September independence of Central America, 28 September arrival of the Pliegos de la Independencia, 20 July Lempira Day and 12 October arrival of the Spanish are celebrated as feasts that changed laws and authorities.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 27 Nos identificamos y así celebramos

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Primer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 27 Nos identificamos y así celebramos

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A dated civic year runs Honduran Women's Day (25 January), Suyapa (3 February), Afro-Honduran Day (12 April), Day of the Americas (14 April), Spanish Language Day (23 April) and the September independence feasts.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 30 Elementos de identidad nacional: fechas cívicas

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Sexto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 30 Elementos de identidad nacional: fechas cívicas
