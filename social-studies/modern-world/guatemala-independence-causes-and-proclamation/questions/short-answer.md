# Guatemala — independence: causes and proclamation — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give one external and one internal cause of independence.
**Answer: External: US independence / French Revolution / Napoleon seizes Spain; internal: criollo exclusion / trade monopoly / war taxes (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two revolts on the road to 1821.
**Answer: Any two of: Delgado in San Salvador 1811, Belén conspiracy 1813, Atanasio Tzul 1820 (any close wording).**
