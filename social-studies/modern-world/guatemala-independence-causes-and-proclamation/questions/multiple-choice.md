# Guatemala — independence: causes and proclamation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** An internal cause of independence was —
a) criollo exclusion, trade monopoly and war taxes  b) the building of Tikal  c) the banana trade
**Answer: a) criollo exclusion, trade monopoly and war taxes** — the inside causes.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Napoleon's seizure of which country shook the empire?
a) Spain  b) Britain  c) Mexico
**Answer: a) Spain** — the outside shock.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Central America's independence Act was signed on —
a) 15 September 1821  b) 30 June 1871  c) 20 October 1944
**Answer: a) 15 September 1821** — the proclamation date.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Act was drafted by —
a) José Cecilio del Valle  b) Pedro de Alvarado  c) Justo Rufino Barrios
**Answer: a) José Cecilio del Valle** — its drafter.
