# Hands-on — Suriname — Nieuw-Nickerie and the sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Suriname — Nieuw-Nickerie and the sea — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
