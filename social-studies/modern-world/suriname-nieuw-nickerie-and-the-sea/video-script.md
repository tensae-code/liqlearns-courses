# Video script — Suriname — Nieuw-Nickerie and the sea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river in 1879 to found Nieuw-Nickerie behind a sea dike.
