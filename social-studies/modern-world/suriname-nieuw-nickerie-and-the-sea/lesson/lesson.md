# Suriname — Nieuw-Nickerie and the sea

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river in 1879 to found Nieuw-Nickerie behind a sea dike As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad

The sea eats the town of De Punt bite by bite; its people refound Nieuw-Rotterdam, move again to De Nieuwe Wijk, and finally cross the Nickerie river in 1879 to found Nieuw-Nickerie behind a sea dike (Een verdronken stad, een nieuwe stad)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
