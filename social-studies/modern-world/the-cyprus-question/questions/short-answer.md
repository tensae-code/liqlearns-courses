# The Cyprus Question — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unit E, ch. 11 The Cyprus question.
**Answer: Cyprus's Greeks struggle for enosis through EOKA's campaign, win a fragile independence in 1960, and face invasion and division in 1974 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία ΣΤ΄ Δημοτικού — Ιστορία του Νεότερου και Σύγχρονου Κόσμου (History, Grade 6 Primary: History of the Modern and Contemporary World)* (Greece), Unit E, ch. 11 The Cyprus question, printed p. 224.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: §54b The Cyprus struggle (1955–1960).
**Answer: EOKA's campaign against Britain ends in Zurich-London independence — a republic neither enosis nor partition intended (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία Γ΄ Γυμνασίου — Βιβλίο Μαθητή (Modern and Contemporary History, Grade 9 Gymnasium)* (Greece), §54b The Cyprus struggle (1955–1960), printed p. 150.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: The Cypriot struggle (1955–1960).
**Answer: Greek Cypriots fight British rule for enosis and settle for an independence nobody designed to last (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Νεότερη και Σύγχρονη Ιστορία (Modern and Contemporary History), Γ' Γυμνασίου, by Evangelia Louvi & Dimitrios Chr. Xifaras* (Greece), The Cypriot struggle (1955–1960), printed p. 150.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: The Cyprus Problem.
**Answer: the EOKA struggle, the 1960 republic, intercommunal collapse, and the 1974 coup and Turkish invasion that divide the island to this day (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Greece's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Ιστορία του νεότερου και του σύγχρονου κόσμου (από το 1815 έως σήμερα) (History of the Modern and Contemporary World, from 1815 to today), Γ' Γενικού Λυκείου, by Ioannis Koliopoulos, Konstantinos Svolopoulos, Evanthis Hatzivassiliou, Theodoros Nimas & Haris Scholinaki-Chelioti* (Greece), The Cyprus Problem, printed p. 163.**
