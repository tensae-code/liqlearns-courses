# Babur and the Mughals — from Kabul

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Zahiruddin Muhammad Babur, of Timur's line, builds from Kabul the dynasty that will rule India and leave the Taj Mahal As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lesson 10: The Baburids of India

Zahiruddin Muhammad Babur, of Timur's line, builds from Kabul the dynasty that will rule India and leave the Taj Mahal

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
## Sub lesson 2 — Lesson 15: The Baburids and Afghanistan

Babur's line, planted from Kabul, faces Afghanistan as India's ruling house

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Lesson 10: The Baburids of India — Zahiruddin Muhammad Babur, of Timur's line, builds from Kabul the dynasty that will rule India and leave the Taj Mahal; Lesson 15: The Baburids and Afghanistan — Babur's line, planted from Kabul, faces Afghanistan as India's ruling house. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
