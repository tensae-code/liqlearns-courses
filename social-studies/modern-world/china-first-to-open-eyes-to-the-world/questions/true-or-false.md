# China First To Open Eyes To The World — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils meet Lin Zexu and Wei Yuan, who first translated the world's geography and urged learning the foreigners' strengths to master the foreigners.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The first modern Chinese to 'open their eyes to the world'

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *历史与社会 八年级 下册 (History and Society, Grade 8, second volume)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The first modern Chinese to 'open their eyes to the world'
