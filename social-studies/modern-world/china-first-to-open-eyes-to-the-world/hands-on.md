# Hands-on — China First To Open Eyes To The World

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — China First To Open Eyes To The World — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The first modern Chinese to 'open their eyes to the world', from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
