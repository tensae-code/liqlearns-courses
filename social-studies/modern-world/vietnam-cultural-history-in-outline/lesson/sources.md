sculpture reaches a high level in architectural carving and statues; folk painting has two main kinds, worship paintings and Tết paintings, mainly woodblock-printed on dó paper and finished by hand, the Lê restoration era giving the famous lines Đông Hồ (Bắc Ninh), Hàng Trống (Hanoi) and Làng Sình (Huế); performing arts divide into court and folk — in 1437 King Lê Thái Tông charges Nguyễn Trãi and Lương Đăng with the court music, and among the people tuồng, chèo and water puppetry (a performance form from the Lý era, pictured) develop widely, singing guilds are founded, ca trù appears around the 15th century at court and spreads into folk life, and hát văn (chầu văn) accompanies the Mother Goddess worship rites; science and technique close the survey: every dynasty values the recording of history and founds specialised agencies — the Lý has its Sử ký under Đỗ Thiện, the Trần founds the Quốc sử viện (National History Institute) and produces the famous Đại Việt sử ký of Lê Văn Hưu, the early Lê court's historians include Phan Phu Tiên, Ngô Sĩ Liên and Vũ Quỳnh and its typical national history is the Đại Việt sử ký toàn thư of Ngô Sĩ Liên, and the Nguyễn founds the Quốc sử quán and compiles the Đại Nam thực lục and the Khâm định Việt sử thông giám cương mục; geography produces the địa chí works — Nguyễn Trãi's Dư địa chí, Bùi Dương Lịch's Nghệ An ký, Lê Quang Định's Hoàng Việt nhất thống dư địa chí, Trịnh Hoài Đức's Gia Định thành thông chí and the Nguyễn court's Đại Nam nhất thống chí — and maps fixing the territory and sea borders, the Hồng Đức bản đồ of the early Lê and the Đại Nam nhất thống toàn đồ of the Nguyễn; mathematics gives Lương Thế Vinh's Đại thành toán pháp and Vũ Hữu's Lập thành toán pháp; military science gives Trần Quốc Tuấn's Binh thư yếu lược and Vạn Kiếp tông bí truyền thư and Đào Duy Từ's Hổ trướng khu cơ, and from the late 14th century the Vietnamese invent large divine cannons and in the 16th–17th centuries cast many kinds of cannon and build cannon-armed warships, absorbing Western technique;
