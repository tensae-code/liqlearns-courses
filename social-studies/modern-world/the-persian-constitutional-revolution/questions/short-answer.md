**Answer: Persians take bast in mosques and cemeteries where no punishment can touch them, demanding a "House of Justice"; the shah concedes a constitution and the first Majlis (1906) takes the budget and foreign loans into its own hands — then a new shah fights the revolution his father signed (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
