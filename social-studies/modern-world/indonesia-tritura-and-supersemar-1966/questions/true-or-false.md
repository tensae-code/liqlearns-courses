# Indonesia — Tritura and the Supersemar, 1966 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Students raise Tritura on 10 January 1966 (dissolve the PKI, purge the cabinet, lower prices), the 11 March 1966 Supersemar order — whose original text remains controversial — empowers Suharto, and a dualism of two presidents follows.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 3.A Masa Transisi Menuju Orde Baru

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII (Kurikulum Merdeka)* is about Indonesia — Reformasi opens politics.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 3.A Masa Transisi Menuju Orde Baru
