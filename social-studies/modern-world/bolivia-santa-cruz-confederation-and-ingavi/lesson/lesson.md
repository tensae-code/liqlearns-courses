# Bolivia — Santa Cruz, the Confederation and Ingavi

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Santa Cruz lays the legal bases of the state and builds the Peru-Bolivian Confederation; Chile attacks it, and after its fall the Battle of Ingavi (1841) secures Bolivia's independence. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
