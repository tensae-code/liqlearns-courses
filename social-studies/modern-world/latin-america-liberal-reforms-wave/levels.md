## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 28 Reforma Liberal en América
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — one continental wave — Morazán's reforms (1830–1839) named the first great Liberal Reform, Juárez in Mexico (1854–1857), López and Mosquera in Colombia, Venezuela's Federal War, Barrios in Guatemala (1871), Zelaya in Nicaragua (1893), Alfaro in Ecuador — against church property and caudillos, for land markets and world trade

## Sub lesson 2 — the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Mexico's reform built a federal, secular republic; Central America's liberal period ran 1870–1950, but in Honduras the reform stayed unfinished — no consolidated class to drive it, excessive foreign capital, a weak export economy — until Gálvez (1949–1954) completed pieces of it: roads, the Central Bank, the development bank
