# Latin America Liberal Reforms Wave — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One continental wave — Morazán's reforms (1830–1839) named the first great Liberal Reform, Juárez in Mexico (1854–1857), López and Mosquera in Colombia, Venezuela's Federal War, Barrios in Guatemala (1871), Zelaya in Nicaragua (1893), Alfaro in Ecuador — against church property and caudillos, for land markets and world trade.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 28 Reforma Liberal en América

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 28 Reforma Liberal en América

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mexico's reform built a federal, secular republic; Central America's liberal period ran 1870–1950, but in Honduras the reform stayed unfinished — no consolidated class to drive it, excessive foreign capital, a weak export economy — until Gálvez (1949–1954) completed pieces of it: roads, the Central Bank, the development bank.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall
