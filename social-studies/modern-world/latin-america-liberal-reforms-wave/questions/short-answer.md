# Latin America Liberal Reforms Wave — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 28 Reforma Liberal en América.
**Answer: one continental wave — Morazán's reforms (1830–1839) named the first great Liberal Reform, Juárez in Mexico (1854–1857), López and Mosquera in Colombia, Venezuela's Federal War, Barrios in Guatemala (1871), Zelaya in Nicaragua (1893), Alfaro in Ecuador — against church property and caudillos, for land markets and world trade (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Quinto grado* (Honduras), Lección 28 Reforma Liberal en América, printed p. 152.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall.
**Answer: Mexico's reform built a federal, secular republic; Central America's liberal period ran 1870–1950, but in Honduras the reform stayed unfinished — no consolidated class to drive it, excessive foreign capital, a weak export economy — until Gálvez (1949–1954) completed pieces of it: roads, the Central Bank, the development bank (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Octavo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), the reform across the continent, and Honduras's stall, printed p. 162.**
