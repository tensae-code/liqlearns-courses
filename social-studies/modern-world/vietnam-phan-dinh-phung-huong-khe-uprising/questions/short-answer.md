# Vietnam Phan Dinh Phung Huong Khe Uprising — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 13, story of Phan Đình Phùng and the Hương Khê uprising.
**Answer: the Hà Tĩnh scholar-official builds his base in the Hương Khê forests, draws heroes from four provinces, and holds out for over ten years until he falls in battle at the end of 1895 — the Cần Vương movement's finest rising (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 13, story of Phan Đình Phùng and the Hương Khê uprising, printed p. 60.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 18, the great Cần Vương risings: Hương Khê, Bãi Sậy, Ba Đình.
**Answer: at Hương Khê in the Hà Tĩnh mountains Phan Đình Phùng (1847–1895) and his general Cao Thắng build the movement's model army — organised in ranks, and casting rifles copied from the French pattern — fighting in two phases (building to 1888, then unified fierce resistance) until the French mass to encircle Ngàn Trươi and Phùng dies in December 1895; at Bãi Sậy Nguyễn Thiện Thuật turns Hưng Yên's reed swamps into a guerrilla fortress (1883–1892); at Ba Đình Phạm Bành and Đinh Công Tráng raise Kinh, Mường and Thái together behind village ramparts until the great French assault of January 1887 forces a bloody breakout (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 18, the great Cần Vương risings: Hương Khê, Bãi Sậy, Ba Đình, printed p. 85.**
