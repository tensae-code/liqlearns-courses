# Hands-on — Vietnam Phan Dinh Phung Huong Khe Uprising

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Phan Dinh Phung Huong Khe Uprising — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
