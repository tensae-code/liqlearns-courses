# Hands-on — Venezuela History On Horseback

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela History On Horseback — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, When history rode on horseback sowing republics, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
