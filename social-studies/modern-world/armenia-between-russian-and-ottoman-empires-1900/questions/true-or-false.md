# Armenia Between Russian And Ottoman Empires 1900 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** One people lives split between two empires, taxed, conscripted and schooled differently on each side of the Araxes, while national parties organise on both.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1.2 Armenia under Russian and Ottoman rule at the start of the 20th century

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1.2 Armenia under Russian and Ottoman rule at the start of the 20th century
