# Indonesia — the Pattimura revolt of 1817 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Moluccas, Pattimura leads the 1817 rising against the returning Dutch and is executed at Fort Victoria in Ambon.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.B

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — colonial schools and the new educated elite.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.B

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The ex-sergeant Pattimura takes Fort Duurstede on Saparua and leads Maluku's 1817 rising.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 2.B.2 Pattimura Angkat Senjata

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — colonial schools and the new educated elite.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 2.B.2 Pattimura Angkat Senjata
