# Indonesia — the Pattimura revolt of 1817

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In the Moluccas in 1817, the ex-sergeant Pattimura takes Fort Duurstede on Saparua and leads Maluku's rising against the returning Dutch — and is executed at Fort Victoria in Ambon As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
