# Hands-on — Colonisation in the Pacific — New Zealand, Australia and the islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Colonisation in the Pacific — New Zealand, Australia and the islands — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Colonisation in the Pacific, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
