# Colonisation in the Pacific — New Zealand, Australia and the islands

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Māori and British sign Waitangi in 1840 making New Zealand a colony; Aboriginal Australia is taken with Cook's 1770 landing and the 1788 convict fleet to Botany Bay; Fiji and Kiribati are ruled by Britain, Samoa by Britain and New Zealand — children weigh colonisation's positive and negative effects country by country As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
