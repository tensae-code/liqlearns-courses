# Honduras Peoples Indigenous Afro And Mestizo — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 4 Una misma nación
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — ten peoples and eight mother tongues make one nation — Indigenous, Afro-Honduran and mestizo — and difference itself becomes the lesson in respect

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 25: los pueblos de hoy
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Maya's living heirs, the Chortí, share today's Honduras with Lenca, Tolupán, Pech, Tawahka, Miskito, Nahua, Garífuna (arrived from St Vincent) and English-speaking Black communities, each keeping its own way of life

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 36 Diversidad cultural
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — each ethnic people's food, dance, music, language, sayings, legends and games enrich the national patrimony, and Lenca women of Marcala who pushed coffee exports to Europe show peoples still making history

## Sub lesson 4 — Lección 33 Un país multicultural
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — mestizos speak Spanish while Miskito, Garífuna, Tol, Chortí and Tawahka tongues survive; punta, tzolkin ceremonies and chilateadas, coconut foods against maize foods — on 3 August 1994 President Reina formally recognises Honduras as multicultural and multiethnic

## Sub lesson 5 — Lección 36 Honduras multiétnica
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — nine named groups — Maya Chortí, Miskito, Tawahka, Garífuna, Pech, English-speaking Black, Lenca, mestizo, Tolupán — spread over fifteen departments, each group's history, customs and beliefs a recognition of the country's past and present
