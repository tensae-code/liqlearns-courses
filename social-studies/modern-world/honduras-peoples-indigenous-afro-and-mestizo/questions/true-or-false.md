# Honduras Peoples Indigenous Afro And Mestizo — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ten peoples and eight mother tongues make one nation — Indigenous, Afro-Honduran and mestizo — and difference itself becomes the lesson in respect.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 4 Una misma nación

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 4 Una misma nación

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Maya's living heirs, the Chortí, share today's Honduras with Lenca, Tolupán, Pech, Tawahka, Miskito, Nahua, Garífuna (arrived from St Vincent) and English-speaking Black communities, each keeping its own way of life.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 25: los pueblos de hoy

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 25: los pueblos de hoy

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Each ethnic people's food, dance, music, language, sayings, legends and games enrich the national patrimony, and Lenca women of Marcala who pushed coffee exports to Europe show peoples still making history.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 36 Diversidad cultural

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Segundo grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 36 Diversidad cultural

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Mestizos speak Spanish while Miskito, Garífuna, Tol, Chortí and Tawahka tongues survive; punta, tzolkin ceremonies and chilateadas, coconut foods against maize foods — on 3 August 1994 President Reina formally recognises Honduras as multicultural and multiethnic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 33 Un país multicultural

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 33 Un país multicultural

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Nine named groups — Maya Chortí, Miskito, Tawahka, Garífuna, Pech, English-speaking Black, Lenca, mestizo, Tolupán — spread over fifteen departments, each group's history, customs and beliefs a recognition of the country's past and present.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 36 Honduras multiétnica

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 36 Honduras multiétnica
