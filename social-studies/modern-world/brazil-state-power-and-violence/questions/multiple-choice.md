# State power and violence — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The state claims a monopoly of —
a) Force  b) Farming  c) Storytelling
**Answer: a) Force**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The book's central question is —
a) When state force is law and when it is violence against the state's own people  b) How a state prints money  c) Which uniform looks best
**Answer: a) When state force is law and when it is violence against the state's own people**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Force that is law, in the book's teaching, —
a) Protects the state's people  b) Makes the state's own people its enemy  c) Is never questioned
**Answer: a) Protects the state's people**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, only the state may —
a) Arrest, punish and compel  b) Grow crops  c) Teach lessons
**Answer: a) Arrest, punish and compel**
