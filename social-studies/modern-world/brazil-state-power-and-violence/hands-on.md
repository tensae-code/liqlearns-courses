# Hands-on — State power and violence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Law or violence?
Write two headings: Law and Violence. Under each, write two signs that would tell you which one you are looking at when state force is used. Read your signs aloud and defend one of them.

## The claim in one picture
Draw a shield on one side of a page and a raised baton on the other. Under the shield write what force-as-law protects; under the baton write what force-as-violence threatens. The state holds both pictures — say which one a good state shows its people.
