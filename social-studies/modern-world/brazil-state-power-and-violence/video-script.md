# Video script — State power and violence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Brazil's Grade 10 História SEED-PR tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Only the state may use force. That is its claim — a monopoly of force, exercised as law.



But Brazil's book asks its pupils a harder question.



When is that force law — and when is it violence against the state's own people?



The uniform looks the same. The question teaches you to tell the difference.
