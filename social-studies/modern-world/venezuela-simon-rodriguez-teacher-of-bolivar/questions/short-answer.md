# Venezuela Simon Rodriguez Teacher Of Bolivar — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Simón Rodríguez knew how to educate for freedom.
**Answer: Bolívar's teacher forms his heart "for liberty, for justice, for what is great and beautiful", and the pupil's letter thanks the master who made him (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Venezuela's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 3er grado* (Venezuela), Simón Rodríguez knew how to educate for freedom, printed p. 18.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Builders of the dream of the Simons.
**Answer: Simón Rodríguez, who taught Bolívar to learn, and Bolívar himself set the dream of an educated, free homeland that students are called to finish building (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Venezuela's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Patria y Ciudadanía — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* (Venezuela), Builders of the dream of the Simons, printed pp. 8–14.**
