# Venezuela Simon Rodriguez Teacher Of Bolivar — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bolívar's teacher forms his heart "for liberty, for justice, for what is great and beautiful", and the pupil's letter thanks the master who made him.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Simón Rodríguez knew how to educate for freedom

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 3er grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Simón Rodríguez knew how to educate for freedom

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Simón Rodríguez, who taught Bolívar to learn, and Bolívar himself set the dream of an educated, free homeland that students are called to finish building.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Builders of the dream of the Simons

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Patria y Ciudadanía — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Builders of the dream of the Simons
