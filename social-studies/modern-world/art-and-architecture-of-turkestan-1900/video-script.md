# Video script — Art and Architecture of Turkestan 1900

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Uzbekistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Samarkand, Kokand and Bukhara miniaturists paint steamships and railways into old walls; maqom and shashmaqom music, dastan epics and the first printing houses (sampled in the chapter's pages) carry the culture through dependence.
