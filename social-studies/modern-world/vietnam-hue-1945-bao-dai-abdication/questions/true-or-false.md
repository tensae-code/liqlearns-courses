# Vietnam Hue 1945 Bao Dai Abdication — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** on 30 August 1945, before the Ngọ Môn gate, Emperor Bảo Đại reads his abdication edict and hands the royal seal and sword to the revolutionary government.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 19, the August Revolution in Huế, 1945

**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 19, the August Revolution in Huế, 1945
