# Hands-on — Vietnam Hue 1945 Bao Dai Abdication

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Hue 1945 Bao Dai Abdication — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 19, the August Revolution in Huế, 1945, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
