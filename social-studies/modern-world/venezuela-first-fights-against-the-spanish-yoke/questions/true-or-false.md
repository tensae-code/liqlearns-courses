# Venezuela First Fights Against The Spanish Yoke — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Zambo rebel José Leonardo Chirino rises at Coro and the rebels take the city; the Gual and España conspiracy follows, and both are crushed.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in First fights against the Spanish yoke: Chirino and Gual-España

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 2do grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about First fights against the Spanish yoke: Chirino and Gual-España

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gual and España conspire in La Guaira for a republic of equals and are betrayed and executed.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Conspiracy of Gual and España (1797)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela y de Nuestra América — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Conspiracy of Gual and España (1797)
