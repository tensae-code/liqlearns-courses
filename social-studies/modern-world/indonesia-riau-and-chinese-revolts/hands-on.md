# Hands-on — Indonesia — Riau rises and the Chinese revolt of 1740

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Indonesia — Riau rises and the Chinese revolt of 1740 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bab 2.A.6-7 Rakyat Riau Angkat Senjata / Orang-orang Cina Berontak, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
