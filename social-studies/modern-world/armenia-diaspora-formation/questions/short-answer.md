**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 7.1 The formation of the Armenian diaspora.
**Answer: from medieval merchant colonies to the genocide survivors' settlements in Lebanon, France and America, dispersion becomes a second Armenian homeland (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: the guide treats the diaspora as half the nation — formed by trade and genocide, bound to the homeland after 1991 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
