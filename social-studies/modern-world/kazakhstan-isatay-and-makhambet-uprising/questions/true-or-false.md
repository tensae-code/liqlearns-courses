# Kazakhstan Isatay And Makhambet Uprising — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Isatay Taimanov and the poet Makhambet Utemisov rise against Khan Zhangir's land seizures and are crushed at Akbulak.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§23–24 The Kazakh uprising of 1836–1838 in the Bukey Horde

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§23–24 The Kazakh uprising of 1836–1838 in the Bukey Horde

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Isatay and Makhambet rise against Zhangir Khan's land grants to his favourites.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§25–26 The uprising of the Kazakhs of the Bukey Horde in 1836–1838
