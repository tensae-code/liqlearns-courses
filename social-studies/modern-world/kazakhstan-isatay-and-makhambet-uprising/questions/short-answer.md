# Kazakhstan Isatay And Makhambet Uprising — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §§23–24 The Kazakh uprising of 1836–1838 in the Bukey Horde.
**Answer: Isatay Taimanov and the poet Makhambet Utemisov rise against Khan Zhangir's land seizures and are crushed at Akbulak (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Kazakhstan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* (Kazakhstan), §§23–24 The Kazakh uprising of 1836–1838 in the Bukey Horde, printed p. 81.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: §§25–26 The uprising of the Kazakhs of the Bukey Horde in 1836–1838.
**Answer: Isatay and Makhambet rise against Zhangir Khan's land grants to his favourites (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Kazakhstan's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* (Kazakhstan), §§25–26 The uprising of the Kazakhs of the Bukey Horde in 1836–1838, printed p. 72.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: §4.4 What common idea united Isatay Taimanov and Makhambet Utemisuly?.
**Answer: the batyr and the poet — sword and word in one uprising (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Қазақстан тарихы. 8-сынып [History of Kazakhstan, Grade 8]* (Kazakhstan), §4.4 What common idea united Isatay Taimanov and Makhambet Utemisuly?, printed p. 83.**
