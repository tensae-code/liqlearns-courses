# The Cold War — what it was and what it did to Africa — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cold War was led on one side by America and on the other by Russia, the ____ Union.
**Answer: Soviet**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In Africa, her tie to the ____ powers continued through the Cold War.
**Answer: colonial**
