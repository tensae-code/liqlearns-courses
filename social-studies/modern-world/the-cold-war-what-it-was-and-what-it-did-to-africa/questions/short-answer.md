# The Cold War — what it was and what it did to Africa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why is it called a 'cold' war?
**Answer:** The two leaders, America and the Soviet Union, did not fight each other directly; they fought an ideological war of words, propaganda, allies, aid and arms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Give the book's verdict on the Cold War and Africa.
**Answer:** The Cold War and colonialism together kept Africa from fast development, and both spread neo-colonialism — the indirect-hand colonial rule — in Africa.
