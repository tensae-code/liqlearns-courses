# The Cold War — what it was and what it did to Africa — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The glossary calls the Cold War a war of words and propaganda dividing the world into two ideologies.
**Answer: True** — The chapter glossary's own definition.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Africa drew close political ties with America, Russia, Britain, France, China and Cuba.
**Answer: True** — All six powers are named.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The book says the Cold War helped Africa develop fast.
**Answer: False** — The Cold War and colonialism together kept Africa from fast development.
