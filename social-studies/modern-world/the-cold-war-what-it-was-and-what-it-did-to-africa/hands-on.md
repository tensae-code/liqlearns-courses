# Hands-on — The Cold War — what it was and what it did to Africa

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two-flag map
Draw Africa. Plant a flag for America on one side and the Soviet Union on the other. Draw arrows of aid and arms crossing the continent, and write under the map the six powers Africa drew close ties with.

## Indirect hand
Move a puppet or a paper figure with a string from above/behind. That hidden hand is the picture of neo-colonialism — rule without a governor in the room. Write the definition under your drawing.
