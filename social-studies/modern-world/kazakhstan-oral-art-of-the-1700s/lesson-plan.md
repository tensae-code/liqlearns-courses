**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§13–14 Kazakh oral folk art in the 18th century** — zhyrau poets like Bukhar and Umbetai sing counsel to khans and keep the nation's memory in verse
2. **§15 Historical events reflected in the works of the akyn-zhyraus of the 18th century** — the zhyrau's verses to the khans are read as chronicles of the Dzhungar wars
3. **§3.1 How are historical events reflected in the works of the akyn-zhyraus of the 18th century?** — poems are mined as chronicles of khans and battles
4. **Lesson plans §§15–18: historical events in the akyn-zhyraus' works; spiritual culture; applied art; national dress** — poems, ornaments and dress are taught as evidence for the 18th century
