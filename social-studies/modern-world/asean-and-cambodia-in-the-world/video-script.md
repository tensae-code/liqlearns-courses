# Video script — ASEAN and Cambodia in the world

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Cambodia sits inside ASEAN's ten nations and a globalising world of food insecurity, migration and terrorism; the EU is held up as the model of regional union.

five nations found ASEAN at Bangkok on 8 August 1967; Brunei (1984), Vietnam (1995), Laos and Myanmar (1997) follow, and Cambodia completes the ten on 30 April 1999.
