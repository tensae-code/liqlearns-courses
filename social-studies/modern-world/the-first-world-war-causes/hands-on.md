# Hands-on — The First World War — causes

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two camps on the floor
Write country names on cards — Germany, Austria-Hungary, Turkey, Italy, Bulgaria, Britain, France, Russia. Sort them into two camp piles as at the war's start, then move Italy out and Bulgaria in, saying '1915' aloud.

## Budget debate
Act a parliament debating a military budget: one side argues fear of the other camp, the other argues cost. Feel how suspicion itself votes the money.
