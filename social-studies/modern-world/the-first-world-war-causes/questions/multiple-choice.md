# The First World War — causes — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book calls the First World War in character —
a) A religious war  b) An imperialists' war  c) A civil war  d) A trade dispute
**Answer: b) An imperialists' war** — An imperialists' war, from rivalry organised into two camps.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Imperialism bred conflict through rivalry to scramble for —
a) Africa and Asia  b) The oceans only  c) The Americas only  d) Antarctica
**Answer: a) Africa and Asia** — The scramble for Africa and Asia bred conflict, distrust and fear.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Nationalism, as the book defines it, is —
a) Hatred of all countries  b) Patriotic feeling, love and loyalty to country  c) A kind of tax  d) A military bloc
**Answer: b) Patriotic feeling, love and loyalty to country** — Love and loyalty to country — a main source of 19th-century conflict.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Triple Entente of 1907 joined —
a) Germany, Austria-Hungary, Turkey  b) Britain, France and Russia  c) Italy, Bulgaria, Spain  d) America, Japan, China
**Answer: b) Britain, France and Russia** — Britain, France and Russia, 1907.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** In 1915, Italy ____ the Alliance and Bulgaria ____ the Central Powers.
a) Joined; left  b) Left; joined  c) Founded; dissolved  d) Ignored; ignored
**Answer: b) Left; joined** — Italy left in 1915; Bulgaria joined in 1915.
