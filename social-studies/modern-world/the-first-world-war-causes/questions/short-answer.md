# The First World War — causes — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the book's three basic causes of the war.
**Answer:** Imperialism and colonial clashes; nationalism; bloc-building and the arms race.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** List the Central Powers as the book gives them, with the 1915 changes.
**Answer:** Under Germany: Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Turkey, Italy (left in 1915) and Bulgaria (joined in 1915).
