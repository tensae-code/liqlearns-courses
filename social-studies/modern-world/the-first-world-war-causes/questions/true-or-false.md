# The First World War — causes — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The colonial scramble bred distrust and fear among Europe's great powers.
**Answer: True** — That distrust is imperialism's path to war.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Central Powers were led under Germany.
**Answer: True** — The Triple Alliance, later Central Powers, under Germany.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The arms race calmed Europe, because everyone felt safe.
**Answer: False** — Each state racing to out-arm the other deepened suspicion and hardened the blocs.
