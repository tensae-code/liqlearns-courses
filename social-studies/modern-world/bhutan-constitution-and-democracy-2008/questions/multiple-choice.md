# Bhutan — constitution and democracy, 2008 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter Three, Drafting of the Constitution / Chapter Four, elections and signing  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter Three, Drafting of the Constitution / Chapter Four, elections and signing** — a royal Kasho of 4 September 2001 orders a written constitution, the drafting committee delivers its first draft on 9 December 2002, the King tours the country discussing it, abdicates in 2006, National Council elections follow in December 2007, and the Constitution is signed on 18 July 2008
