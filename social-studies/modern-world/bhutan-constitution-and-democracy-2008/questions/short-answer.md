**Answer: a royal Kasho of 4 September 2001 orders a written constitution, the drafting committee delivers its first draft on 9 December 2002, the King tours the country discussing it, abdicates in 2006, National Council elections follow in December 2007, and the Constitution is signed on 18 July 2008 (any close wording).**
