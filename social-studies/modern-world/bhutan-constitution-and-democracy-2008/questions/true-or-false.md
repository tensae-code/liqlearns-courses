# Bhutan — constitution and democracy, 2008 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a royal Kasho of 4 September 2001 orders a written constitution, the drafting committee delivers its first draft on 9 December 2002, the King tours the country discussing it, abdicates in 2006, National Council elections follow in December 2007, and the Constitution is signed on 18 July 2008
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Three, Drafting of the Constitution / Chapter Four, elections and signing).
