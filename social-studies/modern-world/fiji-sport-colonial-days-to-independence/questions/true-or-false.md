# Fiji — sport from colonial days to independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules; the sail-only Royal Suva Yacht Club holds its first race on 22 October 1932 for a two-shilling entry.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Sports in Fiji: colonial days — history of athletics, rugby and cricket; Royal Suva Yacht Club

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History Year 11 — Cultural Interaction, Unit 7: Sports and Recreation: Agents for Integration* is about Fiji and New Zealand — a long, close relationship.
**Answer: False** — it is about Sports in Fiji: colonial days — history of athletics, rugby and cricket; Royal Suva Yacht Club

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** From 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities — Fiji stages world amateur golf in 1979, and Waisale Serevi dedicates the 2005 Rugby World Cup Sevens win to a nation cheering as one.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Sport and recreation from independence: unifying a nation
