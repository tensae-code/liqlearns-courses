# Video script — Fiji — sport from colonial days to independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Athletics, cricket (teams photographed in 1895 and 1948) and rugby (the 1952 Fiji team to Australia) grow under colonial rules; the sail-only Royal Suva Yacht Club holds its first race on 22 October 1932 for a two-shilling entry. From 1970 sport takes up the role of unifying players and spectators across communities — Fiji stages world amateur golf in 1979, and Waisale Serevi dedicates the 2005 Rugby World Cup Sevens win to a nation cheering as one.
