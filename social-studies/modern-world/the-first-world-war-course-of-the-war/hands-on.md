# Hands-on — The First World War — course of the war

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Domino declarations
Stand five cards in a row: Austria–Serbia, Russia, Germany–Russia, Germany–France, Britain. Tip the first and watch the row fall inside a week of August — that speed is why the blocs mattered.

## Three-front map
Draw a rough Europe. Draw three bands — West, East, South (Balkan) — and write in each who fought there and one fact from the lesson.
