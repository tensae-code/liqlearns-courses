# Video script — The First World War — course of the war

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Sarajevo, June 28, 1914: the heir is shot.
July 28: Austria-Hungary declares war on Serbia.
August 2, 3, 4: Germany and Britain enter.
Three fronts. New weapons. Russia leaves in 1917.
1918: the Central Powers fall — Germany gives in.
