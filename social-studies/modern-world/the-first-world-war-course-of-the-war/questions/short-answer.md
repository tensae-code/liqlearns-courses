# The First World War — course of the war — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Give the four declaration dates in order.
**Answer:** Austria-Hungary on Serbia, July 28; Germany on Russia, August 2; Germany on France, August 3; Britain on Germany, August 4 — all 1914.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** How did the war end in 1918?
**Answer:** The Allies' attack freed part of the Balkans; Austria-Hungary left defeated; Turkey and Bulgaria had already left; Germany accepted defeat and agreed to stop firing.
