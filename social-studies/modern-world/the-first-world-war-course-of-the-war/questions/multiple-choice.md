# The First World War — course of the war — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Franz Ferdinand and his wife Sophia were assassinated at —
a) Vienna  b) Sarajevo, capital of Bosnia  c) Berlin  d) Paris
**Answer: b) Sarajevo, capital of Bosnia** — Sarajevo, on June 28, 1914.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Austria-Hungary declared war on Serbia on —
a) June 28, 1914  b) July 28, 1914  c) August 4, 1914  d) November 1918
**Answer: b) July 28, 1914** — One month after the assassination: July 28.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Germany reached France by using which neutral country as its bridge?
a) Switzerland  b) Holland  c) Belgium  d) Spain
**Answer: c) Belgium** — Neutral Belgium was Germany's bridge into France.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** On the western front, which were first used in battle?
a) Aircraft, tanks, artillery and poison gas  b) Atomic bombs  c) Submarines only  d) Catapults
**Answer: a) Aircraft, tanks, artillery and poison gas** — The west saw aircraft, tanks, artillery and poison gas first used.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Russia left the war after —
a) Losing one battle  b) The 1917 socialist revolution  c) Signing at Versailles  d) Britain's request
**Answer: b) The 1917 socialist revolution** — Heavily defeated, Russia left after its 1917 socialist revolution.
