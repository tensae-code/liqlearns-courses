# Asian leaders who changed their nations

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Aung San Suu Kyi holds her convictions through years of house arrest; Gandhi, the "political giant" who was really a social activist, breaks an empire with nonviolent disobedience — beside Mao Zedong, Hirohito, Chiang Kai-shek and Indira Gandhi. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Influences of individuals and groups on governance in Asia and the Commonwealth

Aung San Suu Kyi holds her convictions through years of house arrest; Gandhi, the "political giant" who was really a social activist, breaks an empire with nonviolent disobedience — beside Mao Zedong, Hirohito, Chiang Kai-shek and Indira Gandhi

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

The long line of Asia's changemakers — Genghis Khan and Kublai Khan, Emperor Meiji, the Tokugawa Shogunate, the Qin, the Kuomintang, and the Chinese Communist Party ruling since Mao and remaking China into the world's second economy

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
