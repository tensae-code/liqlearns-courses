# Vietnam Quang Trung Builds The Country — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 8, Quang Trung the builder, and the meaning of Tây Sơn.
**Answer: from Phú Xuân he issues the Edict Encouraging Agriculture to bring abandoned fields and wandering people back to production, cuts taxes, opens the passes and markets ("mở cửa ải, thông chợ búa"), proclaims the Edict Establishing Schools and makes chữ Nôm the state's official script, builds a strong army and conducts a diplomacy "both soft and resolute" — before his sudden death in 1792, mourned in Princess Ngọc Hân's verse; the book's verdict on the movement: it overthrew the Nguyễn and Trịnh regimes, ended two centuries of partition, and smashed the Siamese and Qing invasions, firmly defending the nation's independence (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 8, Quang Trung the builder, and the meaning of Tây Sơn, printed p. 40.**
