# Hands-on — Vietnam Quang Trung Builds The Country

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Quang Trung Builds The Country — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 8, Quang Trung the builder, and the meaning of Tây Sơn, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
