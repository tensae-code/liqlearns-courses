# Lesson plan — Poland's Renaissance — Kraków and the Jagiellonian court

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Poland's Renaissance — Kraków and the Jagiellonian court
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Jagiellonian court makes Kraków a northern Florence — Italian architects rebuild Wawel with an arcaded courtyard, the Sigismund Chapel rises as the period's jewel, and Polish nobles' manors and town halls take up the new style — Poland's own Renaissance golden age.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
