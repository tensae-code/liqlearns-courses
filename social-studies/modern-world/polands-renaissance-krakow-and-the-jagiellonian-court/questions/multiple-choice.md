# Poland's Renaissance — Kraków and the Jagiellonian court — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —

**Answer: b) Kraków, a northern Florence** — The Jagiellonian court makes Kraków a northern Florence.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Wawel and the Sigismund Chapel  b) Kraków, a northern Florence  c) The style spreads through Poland
**Answer: a) Wawel and the Sigismund Chapel** — Italian architects rebuild Wawel with an arcaded courtyard, and the Sigismund Chapel rises as the period's jewel.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Kraków, a northern Florence  b) Wawel and the Sigismund Chapel  c) The style spreads through Poland
**Answer: c) The style spreads through Poland** — Polish nobles' manors and town halls take up the new style — Poland's own Renaissance golden age.
