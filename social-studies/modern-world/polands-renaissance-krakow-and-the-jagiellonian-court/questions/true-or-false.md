# Poland's Renaissance — Kraków and the Jagiellonian court — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Italian architects rebuild Wawel with an arcaded courtyard, and the Sigismund Chapel rises as the period's jewel.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — The Jagiellonian court makes Kraków a northern Florence — Italian architects rebuild Wawel with an arcaded courtyard, the Sigismund Chapel rises as the period's jewel, and Polish nobles' manors and town halls take up the new style — Poland's own Renaissance golden age.
