## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 15, the course of the Điện Biên Phủ campaign** — from 13 March to 7 May 1954, in three waves, the army takes the strongpoint groups one by one — after hauling its artillery in by hand, and then, on new orders, hauling it all out and in again by another road
2. **Bài 15, story of capturing General De Castries alive** — on the afternoon of 7 May 1954 Tạ Quốc Luật's five-man assault team storms the bunker shouting "Hands up!"; 23 French officers file out, De Castries surrenders, and the "Determined to fight, determined to win" flag flies at 17:30
3. **Bài 15, Điện Biên Phủ and Geneva** — after the US–France joint defence agreement of December 1950, victories at Hoà Bình (1951–52), Tây Bắc (1952) and Thượng Lào (1953) answer the Navarre Plan; the Winter–Spring 1953–54 offensive disperses the French into five concentrations, and at Điện Biên Phủ — over 16,200 troops in 49 strongpoints — Võ Nguyên Giáp switches the plan from "quick attack, quick victory" to "steady attack, steady advance" (a French officer admits their aircraft "lose to the Việt Minh's porter bicycles"); three waves from 13 March end with De Castries surrendering at 17:30 on 7 May 1954; the Geneva Conference (8 May–21 July 1954) sets the 17th parallel as a temporary line and general elections for July 1956 — ending nearly a century of French colonial rule and liberating the North
4. **Chuyên đề 3.III Võ Nguyên Giáp (1911–2013): the schoolteacher who founds the first propaganda liberation platoon in December 1944, becomes the army's first general at 37, switches Điện Biên Phủ from "fight fast, win fast" to "fight surely, advance surely", and commands the lightning Spring offensive of 1975** — the hardest decision of his life is to stop his own attack and change the plan — and it wins the war
5. **Bài 1 A painting as a historical source: Nguyễn Sáng's lacquer work "Party admission at Điện Biên Phủ" (1963), declared a national treasure in 2013, is used by historians, cultural researchers and art scholars to study the campaign's values — its historical worth lying in the truthful record of a Party admission under fire** — a lacquer panel painted nine years after the battle becomes an archive the state locks away as treasure
6. **Bài 7 Điện Biên Phủ: against the American-backed Navarre Plan (July 1953), the Winter–Spring offensive breaks its first step and the Politburo chooses Điện Biên Phủ; in 56 days and nights (13-3 to 7-5-1954) three waves take Him Lam and Độc Lập, strangle Mường Thanh airfield, and close on the centre until de Castries and his whole staff surrender on the afternoon of 7-5 — porters on pack bicycles supply the guns** — the valley France chose as a trap for the Việt Minh becomes the trap, sprung in three deliberate waves
