# Brazil — Paraná urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What does the Port of Paranaguá do, and where does what it ships go?
**Answer: It ships the state's produce out into the capitalist world market (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the book call the port part of the expansion of capitalism?
**Answer: Because world demand reaches Paraná's farms and towns through the port — the global market and the home state are joined through the harbour (any close wording).**
