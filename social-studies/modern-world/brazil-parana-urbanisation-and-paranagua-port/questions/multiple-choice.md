# Brazil — Paraná urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the world and Brazil, the book lands the urbanisation story in —
a) Paraná, the pupils' own state  b) A country across the ocean  c) Ancient Rome
**Answer: a) Paraná, the pupils' own state**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Port of Paranaguá ships out —
a) The state's produce, into the capitalist world market  b) Only passengers, never goods  c) Nothing — the port is closed
**Answer: a) The state's produce, into the capitalist world market**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book teaches the port as part of —
a) The expansion of capitalism  b) The ancient world  c) A story with no connection to trade
**Answer: a) The expansion of capitalism**

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Paraná's towns, in the book's story —
a) Industrialised as part of the same world process  b) Never changed at all  c) Vanished in the 20th century
**Answer: a) Industrialised as part of the same world process**
