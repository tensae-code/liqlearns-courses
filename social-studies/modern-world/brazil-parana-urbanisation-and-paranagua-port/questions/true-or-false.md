# Brazil — Paraná urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The book lands the world urbanisation story in the pupils' own state, Paraná.
**Answer: True — Paraná's towns industrialise in the same story.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Port of Paranaguá has no connection to the world market.
**Answer: False — it ships the state's produce into the capitalist world market.**
