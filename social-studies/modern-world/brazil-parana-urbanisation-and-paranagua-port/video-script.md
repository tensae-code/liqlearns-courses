# Video script — Brazil — Paraná urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Brazil's Grade 10 História SEED-PR tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The biggest stories in history also happen at home.



Brazil's book teaches urbanisation in the world, then in Brazil — then it lands in Paraná, the pupils' own state, where the towns industrialised too.



And Paraná has a door to the world: the Port of Paranaguá. Through it, the state's produce is shipped into the capitalist world market.



A harbour, a harvest, and the whole world buying — one story.
