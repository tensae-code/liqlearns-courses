# Brazil — Paraná urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The world story of urbanisation and capitalism lands at home: Paraná's towns industrialise, and its port of Paranaguá ships the state's produce into the capitalist world market. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The world story lands at home

After teaching urbanisation and industrialisation in the world and in Brazil, the book does something its pupils can walk through: it lands the story in Paraná, the pupils' own state. Paraná's towns industrialised too — the world process had a local address.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Port of Paranaguá

Paraná's door to the world is its port of Paranaguá. Through the port, the state's produce is shipped out into the capitalist world market. The port is where a farming state's work meets the world's buyers.

## Sub lesson 3 — A port inside the expansion of capitalism

The book teaches the port as part of the expansion of capitalism itself: world demand reaches inland farms and towns through the harbour, and the harbour town grows because the world wants what Paraná produces. The global story and the home story are one story.
