# Hands-on — Brazil — Paraná urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the door
Draw Paraná as a simple shape, its towns inside it, and the Port of Paranaguá on its edge. Draw arrows from the towns to the port and from the port across the sea, and label what travels along them.

## Home in the world story
Write three sentences: one about a world change (urbanisation and industrialisation), one about that change in Brazil, and one about that change in Paraná. Read them aloud — the same story at three sizes.
