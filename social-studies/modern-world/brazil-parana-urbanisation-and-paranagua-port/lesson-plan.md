# Lesson plan — Brazil — Paraná urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Brazil — Paraná urbanisation and the Port of Paranaguá
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Brazil, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The world story of urbanisation and capitalism lands at home: Paraná's towns industrialise, and its port of Paranaguá ships the state's produce into the capitalist world market.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The world story lands at home** — After teaching urbanisation and industrialisation in the world and in Brazil, the book does something its pupils can walk through: it lands the story in Paraná, the pupils' own state. Paraná's towns industrialised too — the world process had a local address.
2. **The Port of Paranaguá** — Paraná's door to the world is its port of Paranaguá. Through the port, the state's produce is shipped out into the capitalist world market. The port is where a farming state's work meets the world's buyers.
3. **A port inside the expansion of capitalism** — The book teaches the port as part of the expansion of capitalism itself: world demand reaches inland farms and towns through the harbour, and the harbour town grows because the world wants what Paraná produces. The global story and the home story are one story.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Brazil — urbanisation and industrialisation; Effects of industrialisation — cities, classes, empires. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say how the book lands the world story of urbanisation in Paraná.
2. Say what the Port of Paranaguá does — what moves through it, and to where.
3. Say why the book teaches the port as part of the expansion of capitalism.
