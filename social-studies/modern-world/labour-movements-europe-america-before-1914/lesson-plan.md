**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Laos, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** unions and socialist parties grow across Europe and America as industry swallows the old world
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**.
