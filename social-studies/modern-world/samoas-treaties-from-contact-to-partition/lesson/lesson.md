# Sāmoa's treaties — from contact to partition

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: from Bethune's 1838 harbour-dues treaty and the first consuls (Pritchard 1847, Weber 1861) through Steinberger's 1873 peace that makes Laupepa king under a European-model constitution, the Apia Municipality of 1879 and the Berlin Treaty of 1889, to the 1899 settlement that annuls it and partitions Sāmoa between Germany (west) and America (east). As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
