# Sāmoa's treaties — from contact to partition — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Part 4, Unit 1.
**Answer: from Bethune's 1838 harbour-dues treaty and the first consuls (Pritchard 1847, Weber 1861) through Steinberger's 1873 peace that makes Laupepa king under a European-model constitution, the Apia Municipality of 1879 and the Berlin Treaty of 1889, to the 1899 settlement that annuls it and partitions Sāmoa between Germany (west) and America (east) (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Samoa's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *History, Year 12* (Samoa), Part 4, Unit 1, printed pp. 103–106.**
