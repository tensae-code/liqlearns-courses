# Central America Indigenous Resistance Leaders — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tecún Umán in Guatemala, Atlacatl in El Salvador, Lempira, Copán Galel, Entepica, Toreba and Cicumba in Honduras, Nicarao in Nicaragua, Garabito in Costa Rica and Urracá in Panama resist to the death; the Crown answers with the Audiencia de los Confines (1542), seated for a time at Gracias.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 24: los líderes de la resistencia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 24: los líderes de la resistencia

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lempira stands out with Copán Galel and Cicumba among the leaders who organised resistance; Honduras's peoples, hardened by their own wars, made the conquest long and cruel.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lempira and the Honduran resistance

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lempira and the Honduran resistance
