**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 24: los líderes de la resistencia.
**Answer: Tecún Umán in Guatemala, Atlacatl in El Salvador, Lempira, Copán Galel, Entepica, Toreba and Cicumba in Honduras, Nicarao in Nicaragua, Garabito in Costa Rica and Urracá in Panama resist to the death; the Crown answers with the Audiencia de los Confines (1542), seated for a time at Gracias (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* (Honduras), Lección 24: los líderes de la resistencia, printed p. 136.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Lempira and the Honduran resistance.
**Answer: Lempira stands out with Copán Galel and Cicumba among the leaders who organised resistance; Honduras's peoples, hardened by their own wars, made the conquest long and cruel (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
