# Video script — Central America Indigenous Resistance Leaders

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Honduras's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Tecún Umán in Guatemala, Atlacatl in El Salvador, Lempira, Copán Galel, Entepica, Toreba and Cicumba in Honduras, Nicarao in Nicaragua, Garabito in Costa Rica and Urracá in Panama resist to the death; the Crown answers with the Audiencia de los Confines (1542), seated for a time at Gracias
