# Video script — Shah Waliullah and the Faraizi Movement — revival before 1857

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Pakistan's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

As Mughal power fades, renewal comes from scholars — Shah Waliullah translates the Quran into Persian and Urdu and writes fifty-one books to revive Muslim society, and Haji Shariatullah's Faraizi Movement calls Bengal's peasants back to the faraiz, the core duties of the faith.
