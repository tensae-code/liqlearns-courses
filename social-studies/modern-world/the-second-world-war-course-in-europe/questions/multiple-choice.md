# The Second World War — course in Europe — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Second World War opened with Germany's invasion of —
a) France  b) Poland  c) Russia  d) Egypt
**Answer: b) Poland** — Poland — the invasion that opened the war.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The French who escaped and fought on were led by —
a) General de Gaulle, as Free France  b) The Vichy government  c) General Princip  d) Woodrow Wilson
**Answer: a) General de Gaulle, as Free France** — De Gaulle's Free France; Vichy in the south was Germany's own set-up government.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan bombed Pearl Harbor on —
a) June 6, 1944  b) December 7, 1941  c) June 22, 1941  d) April 25, 1945
**Answer: b) December 7, 1941** — December 7, 1941 — and America entered the war.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Germany invaded Russia on —
a) June 22, 1941  b) December 7, 1941  c) August 6, 1945  d) June 28, 1914
**Answer: a) June 22, 1941** — June 22, 1941, on three prongs.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The battle where the Red Army first defeated the Germans, in 1943, was —
a) El Alamein  b) Normandy  c) Stalingrad  d) Versailles
**Answer: c) Stalingrad** — Stalingrad 1943 — from it, the German retreat began.

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The combined force landed in Normandy on —
a) June 6, 1944  b) April 25, 1945  c) December 7, 1941  d) June 22, 1941
**Answer: a) June 6, 1944** — June 6, 1944, to free France.
**7. (Unit 4 review — Grade 12)** Unit 4 review: The settlement after WWI was made at —
a) Vienna  b) Versailles  c) Berlin  d) Geneva
**Answer: b) Versailles — The Versailles settlement and the League.**

**8. (Unit 4 review — Grade 12)** Unit 4 review: October 1917 brought to power in Russia —
a) The Tsar  b) The Bolsheviks under Lenin  c) The Mensheviks  d) The army
**Answer: b) The Bolsheviks under Lenin — The first socialist state begins.**

**9. (Unit 4 review — Grade 12)** Unit 4 review: Ethiopia's liberation from Italian occupation came in —
a) 1936  b) 1941  c) 1945  d) 1951
**Answer: b) 1941 — Inside WWII's course, as the unit teaches it.**
