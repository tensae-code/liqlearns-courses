# The Second World War — course in Europe — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** How did America enter the war?
**Answer:** America had opposed Japan's expansion with a trade embargo; Japan bombed Pearl Harbor on December 7, 1941; Germany and Italy declared war on America, and America entered the war.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is Stalingrad called the hinge of the war in Europe?
**Answer:** Before it Germany advanced into Russia; from the Red Army's 1943 victory there, Germany retreated and the Red Army advanced, clearing country after country toward Germany.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Name the two pincers that closed the war in Europe.
**Answer:** From the west, the combined American-British force through Normandy and France; from the east, the Red Army through Poland — meeting at the Elbe on April 25, 1945.
