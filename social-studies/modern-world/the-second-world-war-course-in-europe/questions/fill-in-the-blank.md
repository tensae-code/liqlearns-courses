# The Second World War — course in Europe — fill in the blank

Fill each blank with the right word or number.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A government of Germany's own was set up at ____ in southern France.
**Answer: Vichy**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At Stalingrad in ____ the Red Army first defeated the Germans.
**Answer: 1943**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** On April 25, 1945 the western force met the Red Army at the ____ river.
**Answer: Elbe**
