# The Second World War — course in Europe — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** America opposed Japan's expansion with a trade embargo before Pearl Harbor.
**Answer: True** — The embargo came first; the bombing answered it.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** After Stalingrad, the German advance into Russia continued.
**Answer: False** — From Stalingrad the German retreat and the Red Army's advance began.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Italy accepted defeat unconditionally after the invasion from North Africa.
**Answer: True** — El Alamein, then the invasion of Italy, which accepted defeat unconditionally.
