# Hands-on — The Second World War — course in Europe

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Hinge timeline
Make a strip timeline with only the five hinge dates from the stuck-note. Hang it up: open, America in, turn, landing, meeting. Five dates carry the whole course of the war.

## Pincer hands
Hold your two hands as the two armies — left hand from the west (Normandy), right hand from the east (Stalingrad). Close them slowly onto a paper 'Germany' until fingers meet: the Elbe.
