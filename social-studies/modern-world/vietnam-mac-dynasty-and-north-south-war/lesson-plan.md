# Lesson plan — Vietnam Mac Dynasty And North South War

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Mac Dynasty And North South War
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** as the Lê sơ rots in factional strife and peasant risings, the strongman Mạc Đăng Dung — a seventh-generation descendant of the laureate Mạc Đĩnh Chi, risen through the palace guard — destroys his rivals one by one; the chronicles quoted here concede that "Đăng Dung's prestige grows day by day, the court all submits" and "the people's hearts all turn to him"; in 1527 he forces the Lê king to abdicate and founds the Mạc

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 5, the founding of the Mạc dynasty (1527)** — as the Lê sơ rots in factional strife and peasant risings, the strongman Mạc Đăng Dung — a seventh-generation descendant of the laureate Mạc Đĩnh Chi, risen through the palace guard — destroys his rivals one by one; the chronicles quoted here concede that "Đăng Dung's prestige grows day by day, the court all submits" and "the people's hearts all turn to him"; in 1527 he forces the Lê king to abdicate and founds the Mạc
2. **Bài 5, the North–South dynasties war, 1533–1592** — the Lê loyalist Nguyễn Kim sets up a rival "Southern dynasty" in Thanh Hoá in 1533 under the banner "support the Lê, destroy the Mạc"; for decades Thanh–Nghệ and the northern delta are the battlefield, until the Southern army takes Thăng Long in the early 1590s and the Mạc flee to Cao Bằng; the country is left divided, its villages ruined and its families scattered — Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm's poem Thương loạn, quoted here, grieves over fields where nothing is planted and war without end
