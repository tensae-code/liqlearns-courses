## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 5, the founding of the Mạc dynasty (1527)
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — as the Lê sơ rots in factional strife and peasant risings, the strongman Mạc Đăng Dung — a seventh-generation descendant of the laureate Mạc Đĩnh Chi, risen through the palace guard — destroys his rivals one by one; the chronicles quoted here concede that "Đăng Dung's prestige grows day by day, the court all submits" and "the people's hearts all turn to him"; in 1527 he forces the Lê king to abdicate and founds the Mạc

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 5, the North–South dynasties war, 1533–1592
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Lê loyalist Nguyễn Kim sets up a rival "Southern dynasty" in Thanh Hoá in 1533 under the banner "support the Lê, destroy the Mạc"; for decades Thanh–Nghệ and the northern delta are the battlefield, until the Southern army takes Thăng Long in the early 1590s and the Mạc flee to Cao Bằng; the country is left divided, its villages ruined and its families scattered — Nguyễn Bỉnh Khiêm's poem Thương loạn, quoted here, grieves over fields where nothing is planted and war without end
