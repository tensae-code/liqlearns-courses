# Indonesia — the Women's Congress and the nationalist press

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The First Indonesian Women's Congress meets in Yogyakarta on 22–25 December 1928, while women editors and the native press carry the national idea into Malay-reading homes As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bab II.B

The First Indonesian Women's Congress meets in Yogyakarta, 22-25 December 1928, while Ruhana Kuddus edits Soenting Melayoe, Tirto Adhi Soerjo invents the native press, Rasuna Said rises toward parliament, and Max Havelaar still indicts the system.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
