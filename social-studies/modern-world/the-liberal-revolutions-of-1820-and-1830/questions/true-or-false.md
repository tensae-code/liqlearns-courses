# The Liberal Revolutions Of 1820 And 1830 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 3, Cap. 8 §§1, 3 The liberal oppositions and the revolutionary movements of the 1820s; The European revolutions of 1830–1831
