# Bhutan — joins the United Nations, 1971 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Chapter Three, International Relations  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Chapter Three, International Relations** — after joining the Universal Postal Union in 1969, Bhutan, sponsored by India, takes its seat at the United Nations on 21 September 1971, and exchanges resident representatives with India the same year
