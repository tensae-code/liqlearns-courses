# Bhutan — joins the United Nations, 1971

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: after joining the Universal Postal Union in 1969, Bhutan, sponsored by India, takes its seat at the United Nations on 21 September 1971, and exchanges resident representatives with India the same year. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
