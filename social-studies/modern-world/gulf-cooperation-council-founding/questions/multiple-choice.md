# The Gulf Cooperation Council is founded — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Gulf Cooperation Council joins —
a) six Gulf states  b) all Arab states  c) the OIC's members
**Answer: a) six Gulf states** — the six Gulf states join in one council

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The council was founded so the six could —
a) act together  b) dive for pearls together  c) share one ruler
**Answer: a) act together** — to act together

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** In the Grade 6 book, the GCC lesson opens a unit on —
a) cooperation between the Gulf states  b) ancient civilisations  c) the pearl season
**Answer: a) cooperation between the Gulf states** — the founding act the cooperation lessons then unpack
