# Honduras History In Three Periods

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Honduras's past is divided into pre-Hispanic, colonial (1502–1821) and independent (from 15 September 1821); Comayagua, founded by Alonso de Cáceres in 1537 as Santa María de la Nueva Valladolid, is capital from 1540 until the capital moves to Tegucigalpa in 1880 As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
