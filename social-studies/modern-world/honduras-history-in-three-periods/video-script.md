# Video script — Honduras History In Three Periods

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Honduras's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Honduras's past is divided into pre-Hispanic, colonial (1502–1821) and independent (from 15 September 1821); Comayagua, founded by Alonso de Cáceres in 1537 as Santa María de la Nueva Valladolid, is capital from 1540 until the capital moves to Tegucigalpa in 1880
