# Indonesia — the New Order (Orde Baru), 1966–1998 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1966-67 transition hands power from Sukarno to Suharto; the guide demands a non-black-and-white reading — state strengthening and development against weak policies, human-rights abuses, resistance, and the regime's end in crisis.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 3, Indonesia Masa Orde Baru (Gambaran Umum)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Buku Panduan Guru Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII* is about Indonesia — revolution battles across the archipelago.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 3, Indonesia Masa Orde Baru (Gambaran Umum)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The New Order fuses parties into a three-colour politics, makes the economy the commander of development through the Pelita plans, and enforces hegemony through social and cultural life — stability with a price the chapter itemises.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 3.B Penguatan Negara dan Kelemahan Kebijakan Orde Baru

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII (Kurikulum Merdeka)* is about Indonesia — revolution battles across the archipelago.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 3.B Penguatan Negara dan Kelemahan Kebijakan Orde Baru
