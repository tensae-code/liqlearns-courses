# Lesson plan — Indonesia — the New Order (Orde Baru), 1966–1998

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Indonesia — the New Order (Orde Baru), 1966–1998
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Indonesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the New Order of Suharto (1966–1998) fuses parties, makes the economy the commander of development through the Pelita plans, and enforces hegemony — stability with a price the books itemise, to be read not black-and-white but from multiple perspectives
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bab 3, Indonesia Masa Orde Baru (Gambaran Umum)** — the 1966-67 transition hands power from Sukarno to Suharto; the guide demands a non-black-and-white reading — state strengthening and development against weak policies, human-rights abuses, resistance, and the regime's end in crisis
2. **Bab 3.B Penguatan Negara dan Kelemahan Kebijakan Orde Baru** — the New Order fuses parties into a three-colour politics, makes the economy the commander of development through the Pelita plans, and enforces hegemony through social and cultural life — stability with a price the chapter itemises

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
