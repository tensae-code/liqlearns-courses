# Video script — Indonesia — the New Order (Orde Baru), 1966–1998

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The New Order of Suharto (1966–1998) fuses parties, makes the economy the commander of development through the Pelita plans, and enforces hegemony — stability with a price the books itemise, to be read not black-and-white but from multiple perspectives.
