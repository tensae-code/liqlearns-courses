# Lesson plan — Jordan since 1999 — Abdullah II and the reform papers

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Jordan since 1999 — Abdullah II and the reform papers
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Jordan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Abdullah II takes the oath on 7 February 1999; the 14th parliament (2003–2007) seats the first women's quota of six; the National Centre for Human Rights opens in 2002 and the National Agenda launches in 2005; the king's Discussion Papers invite the nation to argue its democratic road; after the royal committee of 26 April 2011 the constitution gains a Constitutional Court and an Independent Election Commission, and the 2015 Decentralisation Law elects governorate councils
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.
