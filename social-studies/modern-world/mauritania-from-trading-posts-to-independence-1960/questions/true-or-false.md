# Mauritania — from trading posts to independence, 1960 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The gum-arabic trade drew the Portuguese in the fifteenth century, then Dutch and French trading posts; France moved in stages — exploration 1858–1901, the protectorate of 1905, direct control 1909–1920 — renamed the land, split its people and fought the Arabic language; Sheikh Ma al-Aynayn led the resistance from the Adrar plateau and his son al-Hiba continued it; the parties united in 1958 under Mokhtar Ould Daddah, and the United Nations referendum of 1960 brought full independence with Ould Daddah as first president.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 5, Lesson موريتانيا منذ فرض الحماية حتى الوقت الحاضر (Mauritania from the imposition of the protectorate to the present)
