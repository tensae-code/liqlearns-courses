## Sub lesson 1 — Mauritania — from trading posts to independence, 1960
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The gum-arabic trade drew the Portuguese in the fifteenth century, then Dutch and French trading posts; France moved in stages — exploration 1858–1901, the protectorate of 1905, direct control 1909–1920 — renamed the land, split its people and fought the Arabic language; Sheikh Ma al-Aynayn led the resistance from the Adrar plateau and his son al-Hiba continued it; the parties united in 1958 under Mokhtar Ould Daddah, and the United Nations referendum of 1960 brought full independence with Ould Daddah as first president.
