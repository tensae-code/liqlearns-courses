# Finland's Continuation War, 1941–44 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — the SA-kuva archive and the TK companies
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — in summer 1941 Finland builds information companies on the German propaganda-company model; their front photographers shoot ~160,000 pictures in 1939–45 to shape mood at home and at the front

## Sub lesson 2 — Task 1, analysing one war-front photograph
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Finns carry a wounded enemy across a forest stream (June 1944), anti-tank guns fire on gunboats in Vyborg Bay (1941), Lottas give the wounded drink — pupils interrogate purpose, framing and cropping

## Sub lesson 3 — Task 1, Hitler's visit to Mannerheim's 75th birthday
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Hitler walks in with Mannerheim and President Ryti and sits through the Marshal's speech — pupils read the mood and Finland's closeness to Germany out of the courtesies

## Sub lesson 4 — Task 1, the front-life photo set
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Kino Jörö cinema opens at Lempaala, a choir sings the 72nd soldiers' evening, men carve birch-bark butter boxes and study by post — pupils ask why the Defence Forces wanted this cheerful front shown

## Sub lesson 5 — Task 2, the Lotta canteen visit
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — a Lotta canteen entertains front-line boys in August 1944; pupils write two diary entries, the soldier's and the Lotta's, across the war's gender divide

## Sub lesson 6 — Task 1, bomb damage in Kallio, Helsinki, 9 July 1941
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Soviet bombs wreck a working-class district in the war's first summer; pupils reconstruct the soundscape of the moment

## Sub lesson 7 — Task 2, overview of the Helsinki bombing, 9 July 1941
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the hundred-mark villas of Porvoonkatu lie ruined under Fleminginkatu's stone blocks; pupils compare the street then and now and ask who lived in each
