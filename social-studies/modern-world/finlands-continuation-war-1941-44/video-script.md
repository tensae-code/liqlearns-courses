# Video script — Finland's Continuation War, 1941–44

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Finland's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In summer 1941 Finland builds information companies on the German propaganda-company model; their front photographers shoot ~160,000 pictures in 1939–45 to shape mood at home and at the front.
