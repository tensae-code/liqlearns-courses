## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **the SA-kuva archive and the TK companies** — in summer 1941 Finland builds information companies on the German propaganda-company model; their front photographers shoot ~160,000 pictures in 1939–45 to shape mood at home and at the front
2. **Task 1, analysing one war-front photograph** — Finns carry a wounded enemy across a forest stream (June 1944), anti-tank guns fire on gunboats in Vyborg Bay (1941), Lottas give the wounded drink — pupils interrogate purpose, framing and cropping
3. **Task 1, Hitler's visit to Mannerheim's 75th birthday** — Hitler walks in with Mannerheim and President Ryti and sits through the Marshal's speech — pupils read the mood and Finland's closeness to Germany out of the courtesies
4. **Task 1, the front-life photo set** — Kino Jörö cinema opens at Lempaala, a choir sings the 72nd soldiers' evening, men carve birch-bark butter boxes and study by post — pupils ask why the Defence Forces wanted this cheerful front shown
5. **Task 2, the Lotta canteen visit** — a Lotta canteen entertains front-line boys in August 1944; pupils write two diary entries, the soldier's and the Lotta's, across the war's gender divide
6. **Task 1, bomb damage in Kallio, Helsinki, 9 July 1941** — Soviet bombs wreck a working-class district in the war's first summer; pupils reconstruct the soundscape of the moment
7. **Task 2, overview of the Helsinki bombing, 9 July 1941** — the hundred-mark villas of Porvoonkatu lie ruined under Fleminginkatu's stone blocks; pupils compare the street then and now and ask who lived in each
