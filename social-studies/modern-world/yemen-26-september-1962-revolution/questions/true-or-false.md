# Revolution of 26 September — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes the Yemen Arab Republic with goals of justice, a national army and a democratic cooperative society.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Revolution of 26 September

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التربية الاجتماعية للصف الرابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (Social Education for Grade 4, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Revolution of 26 September

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961; on 26 September 1962 the free officers, backed by the people's masses, strike the Imamate down forever and raise the republic on its six goals.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ العالم الحديث والمعاصر — تاريخ للصف الثالث الثانوي (History: Modern and Contemporary History of the World, for the Third Secondary Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4)
