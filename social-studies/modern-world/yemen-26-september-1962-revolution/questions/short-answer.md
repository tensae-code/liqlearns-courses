# Revolution of 26 September — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Revolution of 26 September.
**Answer: yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes the Yemen Arab Republic with goals of justice, a national army and a democratic cooperative society (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التربية الاجتماعية للصف الرابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (Social Education for Grade 4, Basic Education)* (Yemen), Revolution of 26 September, printed p. 65.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4).
**Answer: the Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961; on 26 September 1962 the free officers, backed by the people's masses, strike the Imamate down forever and raise the republic on its six goals (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العالم الحديث والمعاصر — تاريخ للصف الثالث الثانوي (History: Modern and Contemporary History of the World, for the Third Secondary Grade)* (Yemen), The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4), printed p. 53.**
