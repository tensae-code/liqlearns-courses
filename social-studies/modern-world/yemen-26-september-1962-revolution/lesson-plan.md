# Lesson plan — Revolution of 26 September

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Revolution of 26 September
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Yemen, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Revolution of 26 September** — yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes the Yemen Arab Republic with goals of justice, a national army and a democratic cooperative society
2. **The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4)** — the Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961; on 26 September 1962 the free officers, backed by the people's masses, strike the Imamate down forever and raise the republic on its six goals
