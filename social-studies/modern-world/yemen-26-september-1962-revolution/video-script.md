# Video script — Revolution of 26 September

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes the Yemen Arab Republic with goals of justice, a national army and a democratic cooperative society.
the Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961; on 26 September 1962 the free officers, backed by the people's masses, strike the Imamate down forever and raise the republic on its six goals.
