# Revolution of 26 September

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Revolution of 26 September

Yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes the Yemen Arab Republic with goals of justice, a national army and a democratic cooperative society

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Revolution of 26 September — yemenis rise against the hereditary monarchical kingdom that ruled Yemen, carry arms for long years, and on 26 September 1962 the revolution wins and the kingdom becomes the Yemen Arab Republic with goals of justice, a national army and a democratic cooperative society; The Yemeni Revolution: the North (Unit 2 Lesson 4) — the Imamate keeps Yemen backward by the sword, the hostage system and tribe set against tribe, and executes the free men of 1948, 1955 and 1961; on 26 September 1962 the free officers, backed by the people's masses, strike the Imamate down forever and raise the republic on its six goals. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
