# Venezuela Collective Lands Enslavement And Pirates — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Communal indigenous lands gain foreign owners, enslaved people rebel, colonists bring cattle, Spain imposes mercantilism and pirates raid the coasts.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Foreign owners appeared for collective lands

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 4to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Foreign owners appeared for collective lands

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Communal land becomes private property and the encomienda turns indigenous labour into a grant — a new way to exploit the worker.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in When private property appeared in Our America; the encomienda

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela y de Nuestra América — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
