# Vietnam Nguyen Dynasty Builds And Reforms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nguyễn Ánh takes the throne as Gia Long in 1802 at Phú Xuân (Huế), issues a law code, and Minh Mạng divides the country into 30 provinces in 1831–32; when a ministry spares a thieving official for his merits, Minh Mạng orders the law applied equally to all.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 13, the Nguyễn dynasty builds the country

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 13, the Nguyễn dynasty builds the country

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Quang Trung's death in 1792 removes the Tây Sơn's pillar; in 1802 Nguyễn Ánh, backed by the Gia Định landlords, defeats them and founds the Nguyễn dynasty as Emperor Gia Long, with his capital at Phú Xuân (Huế) — its Imperial City is the lesson's opening photograph, a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1993; Gia Long gathers every power into his hands, issues the Hoàng Việt luật lệ code protecting the king's absolute authority, and Minh Mạng's reforms of 1831–32 perfect the machine down to 30 provinces and one prefecture — while abroad the court keeps soft diplomacy with the Qing, refuses trade with the West and, from Minh Mạng, persecutes Catholicism.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 16, the founding of the Nguyễn and the building of the centralised state

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 16, the founding of the Nguyễn and the building of the centralised state

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** one decisive emperor erases the north–south viceroys and draws the provincial map in two years.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 11 Minh Mạng's reforms: to end the Bắc Thành / Gia Định Thành fragmentation, the emperor creates the Nội các (1829) and Cơ mật viện (1834), perfects the Six Ministries under cross-inspection, and in 1831–1832 abolishes the two viceroyalties and divides the whole country into thirty provinces — the province map Vietnam still inherits

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Lịch sử 11 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
