# The Postwar Boom The Golden Age — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** thirty years of growth, full employment and mass consumption — Packard's Document unmasks the consumer's hypnotists while Bouissou's explains Japan's social conditions for growth — the boom and its discontents in one chapter.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 4, Cap. 16 §1 The "golden age" of the world economy

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 4, Cap. 16 §1 The "golden age" of the world economy

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** baby boom, washing machines and the welfare state — Van der Wee on the demand imperative, Packard's hidden persuaders and McLuhan's medium (Fare storia pieces) describe affluence and its salesmen in one dossier.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 11 §§1–3 La crescita demografica; Il boom economico; Consumi e Welfare

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 11 §§1–3 La crescita demografica; Il boom economico; Consumi e Welfare
