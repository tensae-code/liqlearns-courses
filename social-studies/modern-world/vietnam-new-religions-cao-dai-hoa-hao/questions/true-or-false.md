# Vietnam New Religions Cao Dai Hoa Hao — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in one generation the colonised South answers imported religions by manufacturing its own.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chuyên đề 1 Faiths Vietnam invented: Cao Đài, founded in Tây Ninh in 1926 by officials, landlords and intellectuals fusing Buddhism, Confucianism, Daoism and southern folk belief under one Holy See — its followers count some 4,000 martyrs in the anti-American war — and Hòa Hảo Buddhism, founded in 1939 by Huỳnh Phú Sổ on lay practice and the Four Debts of Gratitude, with about 1.3 million followers by 2022
