# Nicaragua Literacy Crusade 1980 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1980 literacy crusade "Heroes and Martyrs for the Liberation of Nicaragua" teaches the country to read, closing officially 23 August.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Efemérides: anniversary of the Gran Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Conociendo mi Mundo — 2do Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Efemérides: anniversary of the Gran Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Adult education reaches 140,000 newly literate people a year, Río San Juan is declared free of illiteracy in 1987 winning the Krupskaya medal, and the school milk programme feeds 200,000 children.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Logros sociales: alfabetización y salud

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Módulo Autoformativo de Ciencias Sociales — 7° Grado ("Orgullo de mi País"), Educación Secundaria a Distancia en el Campo* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Logros sociales: alfabetización y salud

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Over 100,000 mostly young literacy volunteers cut illiteracy from 50.3 percent to 12.9 percent in five months of 1980, teaching also in the Caribbean coast's own languages.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Logros de la Revolución: la Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Educación Secundaria, 7 Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Logros de la Revolución: la Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización
