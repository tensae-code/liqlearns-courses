## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Efemérides: anniversary of the Gran Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización** — the 1980 literacy crusade "Heroes and Martyrs for the Liberation of Nicaragua" teaches the country to read, closing officially 23 August
2. **Logros sociales: alfabetización y salud** — adult education reaches 140,000 newly literate people a year, Río San Juan is declared free of illiteracy in 1987 winning the Krupskaya medal, and the school milk programme feeds 200,000 children
3. **Logros de la Revolución: la Cruzada Nacional de Alfabetización** — over 100,000 mostly young literacy volunteers cut illiteracy from 50.3 percent to 12.9 percent in five months of 1980, teaching also in the Caribbean coast's own languages
