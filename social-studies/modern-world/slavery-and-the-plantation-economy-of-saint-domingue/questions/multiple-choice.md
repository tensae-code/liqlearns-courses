# Slavery and the plantation economy of Saint-Domingue — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) From conquest to the plantation economy  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) From conquest to the plantation economy** — Europeans seize the island, clear it and rebuild it as sugar and coffee plantations worked by Africans — a chronology told through local remains.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the guide this part is —
a) Séance 1, De la conquête et de la colonisation européennes à l'économie de plantation  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Séance 1, De la conquête et de la colonisation européennes à l'économie de plantation** — the guide's own heading for this part.
