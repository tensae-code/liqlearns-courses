# Venezuela Zamora And The Federal War

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Ezequiel Zamora's federal struggle fills the countryside with war, and peace, when it comes, must be argued for at a neighbour's table As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
