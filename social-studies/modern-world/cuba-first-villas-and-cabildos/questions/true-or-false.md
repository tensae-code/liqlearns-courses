# Cuba First Villas And Cabildos — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** seven villas rise 1511–1515 (Baracoa to Santiago); church, plaza and cabildo at the centre, the cabildo setting prices, judging neighbours and handing out land and Indians.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 2.3 First villas founded by the conquerors

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 2.3 First villas founded by the conquerors

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** villas, cabildos, encomiendas and the Church lock the island into an empire's administration.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 2.2 Establishment of Spanish colonial rule in Cuba

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Noveno grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
