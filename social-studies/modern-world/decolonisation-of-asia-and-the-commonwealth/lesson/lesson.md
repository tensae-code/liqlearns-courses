# Decolonisation of Asia and the Commonwealth

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: 1945–1960 the empires let go, each colony its own way — peaceful and orderly in some, revolution in Indonesia and India, long civil war in French Indo-China — while the new UN and the Cold War pull the newborn states in opposite directions. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
