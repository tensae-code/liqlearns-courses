# Kazakhstan The Reforms Of 1822 1824 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Speransky's and the Orenburg statutes abolish khan rule in the Middle and Junior zhuzes and replace it with Russian districts and taxes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§21–22 The tsarist reforms of 1822–1824

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§21–22 The tsarist reforms of 1822–1824

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the statutes abolish the khanates and Kazakh society is re-sorted into Russian districts.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §23 The reforms of 1822–1824 and Kazakh society

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §23 The reforms of 1822–1824 and Kazakh society

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the 1822–1824 statutes are explained from the empire's side and judged from the steppe's.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §4.3 Why were administrative reforms carried out in Kazakhstan in the 1820s?
