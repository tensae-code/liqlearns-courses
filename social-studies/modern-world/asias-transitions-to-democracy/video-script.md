# Video script — Asia's transitions to democracy

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The closing decades of the 20th century flip Asia's regimes — crowds topple Marcos in 1986, Korea and Taiwan negotiate out of military rule in 1987, the UN intervenes in Cambodia in 1993, Suharto falls in 1998, East Timor is rehabilitated in 2001.
