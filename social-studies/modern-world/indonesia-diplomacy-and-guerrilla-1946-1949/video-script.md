# Video script — Indonesia — diplomacy and guerrilla war, 1946–1949

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Indonesia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

Linggarjati (1946) and Renville (1948) shrink the Republic at the table and Dutch aggressions break them; the answer is Sudirman's guerrilla war, Roem–Royen and the Round Table Conference, which transfers sovereignty.
