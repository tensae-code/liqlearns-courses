# Venezuela Ciudad Bolivar And Guayana Mining — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ciudad Bolívar fights its last battle as river port while gold and iron make Guayana a mining pole the oil state absorbs.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ciudad Bolívar, its Apure-Barinas hinterland and Guayana mining

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ciudad Bolívar, its Apure-Barinas hinterland and Guayana mining

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Ciudad Bolívar, founded in the colonial period on the Orinoco's bank, anchors a Guayana sold to children as riches and a refuge of freedom.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Guayana: wealth, beauty, refuge of liberty

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 3er grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
