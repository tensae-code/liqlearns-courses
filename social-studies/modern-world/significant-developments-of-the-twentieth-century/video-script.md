# Video script — Significant developments of the twentieth century

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

WWII, the Cold War, Sputnik and the internet named as the century's hinges; students investigate a global event properly — questions and hypotheses, sources located and interrogated, even visiting the ground where it happened.
