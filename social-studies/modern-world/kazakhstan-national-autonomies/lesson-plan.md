## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§12 National autonomies in Kazakhstan** — Alash Orda and the Turkestan (Kokand) autonomy try two different Kazakh statehoods and both are extinguished by the Bolsheviks
2. **§19 The activity of the national autonomies in Kazakhstan** — Alash Orda governs from Semey while Soviet power grows around it
3. **§20 Relations between the organs of Soviet power and the government of Alash Orda** — negotiation, ultimatum and amnesty end the Kazakh government without a last battle
4. **§12 National autonomies in Kazakhstan** — Alash Orda and the Kokand autonomy try two statehoods; both are extinguished
5. **§§2.3–2.4 The Alash Orda government and the Turkestan (Kokand) autonomy** — two Kazakh statehoods are proclaimed and extinguished
