# Video script — Kennedy and the Peace Corps

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

campaigning at the University of Michigan at 2 a.m. on 14 October 1960, John F. Kennedy challenges 10,000 students to serve in the developing world; the Peace Corps is set up on 1 March 1961, reaches Samoa in 1967, and sends more than 1,700 volunteers there.
