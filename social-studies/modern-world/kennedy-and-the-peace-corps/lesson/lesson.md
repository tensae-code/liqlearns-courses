# Kennedy and the Peace Corps

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: campaigning at the University of Michigan at 2 a.m. on 14 October 1960, John F. Kennedy challenges 10,000 students to serve in the developing world; the Peace Corps is set up on 1 March 1961, reaches Samoa in 1967, and sends more than 1,700 volunteers there. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
