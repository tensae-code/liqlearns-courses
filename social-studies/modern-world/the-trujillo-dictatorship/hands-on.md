# Hands-on — The Trujillo dictatorship, 1930–1961

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Trujillo dictatorship, 1930–1961 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unidad 7 Dictadura de Rafael Leónidas Trujillo: vulneración de derechos humanos (1930–1961), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
