# Lesson plan — The Trujillo dictatorship, 1930–1961

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Trujillo dictatorship, 1930–1961
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Dominican Republic, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** For thirty-one years one man owns the country and crushes every right.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unidad 7 Dictadura de Rafael Leónidas Trujillo: vulneración de derechos humanos (1930–1961)** — for thirty-one years one man owns the country and crushes every right
2. **Unidad 2 La dictadura de Trujillo: surgimiento y consolidación** — the occupation's constabulary chief seizes the state in 1930 and builds a personal regime
3. **Unidad 2 Modernización contradictoria y oposición al régimen** — roads and monuments rise while opponents are jailed, exiled or killed
4. **Unidad 2 Crisis y muerte de Trujillo** — isolated and sanctioned, the regime ends with Trujillo's assassination in 1961
