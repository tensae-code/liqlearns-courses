# Video script — Fiji — indentured Indians and blackbirding

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Governor Arthur Gordon, needing plantation labour iTaukei would not supply, has Solomon Islanders blackbirded and, from the Leonidas in 1879 to 1916, Indian girmit labourers brought from Calcutta — most stay after indenture ends and become farmers and businessmen. Girmit life is marked by a broken 40-women-per-100-men quota, jealousy and fighting, and no schooling for children, until C.F. Andrews and W.W. Pearson investigate in 1915 and their report brings the system down. In 1879 Fiji changes again as Indians arrive, most enticed by honeyed promises and deceptions to sign an indenture contract — the girmitiya, not slaves, work the plantations under a pass system, and many make Fiji home.
