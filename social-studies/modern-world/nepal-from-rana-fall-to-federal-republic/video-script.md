# Video script — Nepal — from Rana fall to federal republic

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Nepal's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

a dated timeline runs from the fall of the Ranas and democracy's birth on 7 Falgun 2007 BS, through Mahendra's 2017 BS coup and the one-party Panchayat, the 2046 BS movement, the Maoist insurgency from 2052 BS, the Constituent Assembly elections, the republic declared 15 Jestha 2065 BS and Dr Ram Baran Yadav as first President, to the constitution of 2072 BS and the local elections of 2074 BS

---

the same timeline is re-read as gains — Nepal Rastra Bank and planned development from 2013 BS, schools and Tribhuvan University, roads, radio, telephone, television and internet, and after 2046 BS private banks and industry — showing how political change changed daily life

---

pupils chart what the democratic republic (Loktantrik Ganatantra) brought and hunt its economic and social reform achievements, weighing monarchy against republic in a comparison table

---

after 104 years of Rana rule, the Nepali Congress launches an armed revolution in 2006 BS; the four martyrs of 1997 BS are remembered, King Tribhuvan shelters in the Indian embassy and flies to Delhi, and the Delhi Compromise of Falgun 2007 BS ends Rana rule and promises a constituent assembly

---

the decade after the revolution brings parties, the first elections of 2015 BS and B. P. Koirala's elected government — and ends when King Mahendra dismisses it, so the lesson weighs what democracy achieved and why it fell
