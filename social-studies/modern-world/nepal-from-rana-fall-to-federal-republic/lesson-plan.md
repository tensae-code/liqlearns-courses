# Lesson plan — Nepal — from Rana fall to federal republic

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Nepal — from Rana fall to federal republic
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Nepal, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** a dated timeline runs from the fall of the Ranas and democracy's birth on 7 Falgun 2007 BS, through Mahendra's 2017 BS coup and the one-party Panchayat, the 2046 BS movement, the Maoist insurgency from 2052 BS, the Constituent Assembly elections, the republic declared 15 Jestha 2065 BS and Dr Ram Baran Yadav as first President, to the constitution of 2072 BS and the local elections of 2074 BS
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Political Events from 2007 BS to the Present (Grade 6)** — a dated timeline runs from the fall of the Ranas and democracy's birth on 7 Falgun 2007 BS, through Mahendra's 2017 BS coup and the one-party Panchayat, the 2046 BS movement, the Maoist insurgency…
2. **Social and Economic Achievements, 2007 BS to the Present (Grade 6)** — the same timeline is re-read as gains — Nepal Rastra Bank and planned development from 2013 BS, schools and Tribhuvan University, roads, radio, telephone, television and internet, and after 2046 BS…
3. **The Democratic Republic and Its Achievements (Grade 8 (teacher guide))** — pupils chart what the democratic republic (Loktantrik Ganatantra) brought and hunt its economic and social reform achievements, weighing monarchy against republic in a comparison table
4. **The Revolution of 2007 BS and the Delhi Compromise (Grade 10)** — after 104 years of Rana rule, the Nepali Congress launches an armed revolution in 2006 BS; the four martyrs of 1997 BS are remembered, King Tribhuvan shelters in the Indian embassy and flies to…
5. **A Decade of the Democratic System (2007–2017 BS) (Grade 10)** — the decade after the revolution brings parties, the first elections of 2015 BS and B. P. Koirala's elected government — and ends when King Mahendra dismisses it, so the lesson weighs what democracy…

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
