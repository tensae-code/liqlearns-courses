# Nepal — from Rana fall to federal republic — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a dated timeline runs from the fall of the Ranas and democracy's birth on 7 Falgun 2007 BS, through Mahendra's 2017 BS coup and the one-party Panchayat, the 2046 BS movement, the Maoist insurgency from 2052 BS, the…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Political Events from 2007 BS to the Present

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 6* is about National Luminary Arniko.
**Answer: False** — it is about Political Events from 2007 BS to the Present

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the same timeline is re-read as gains — Nepal Rastra Bank and planned development from 2013 BS, schools and Tribhuvan University, roads, radio, telephone, television and internet, and after 2046 BS private banks and…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Social and Economic Achievements, 2007 BS to the Present

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan tatha Manav Mulya Shiksha, Kaksha 6* is about National Luminary Prithvi Narayan Shah.
**Answer: False** — it is about Social and Economic Achievements, 2007 BS to the Present

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** pupils chart what the democratic republic (Loktantrik Ganatantra) brought and hunt its economic and social reform achievements, weighing monarchy against republic in a comparison table
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Democratic Republic and Its Achievements

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Shikshak Nirdeshika, Class 8, Social Studies and Human Value Education 2081 (teacher's guide)* is about Our National Luminaries (later figures).
**Answer: False** — it is about The Democratic Republic and Its Achievements

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** after 104 years of Rana rule, the Nepali Congress launches an armed revolution in 2006 BS; the four martyrs of 1997 BS are remembered, King Tribhuvan shelters in the Indian embassy and flies to Delhi, and the Delhi…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Revolution of 2007 BS and the Delhi Compromise

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan, Class 10* is about Identifying and Preserving the Historical Sites of the Local Area.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Revolution of 2007 BS and the Delhi Compromise

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** the decade after the revolution brings parties, the first elections of 2015 BS and B. P. Koirala's elected government — and ends when King Mahendra dismisses it, so the lesson weighs what democracy achieved and why it…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in A Decade of the Democratic System (2007–2017 BS)

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Samajik Adhyayan, Class 10* is about The Ancient History of Nepal.
**Answer: False** — it is about A Decade of the Democratic System (2007–2017 BS)
