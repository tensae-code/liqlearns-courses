# India — Gandhi and the road to 1947

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Gandhi returns in 1915, answers the Rowlatt Act and repression with satyagraha, launches Quit India in 1942, and on 15 August 1947 India is free — but cut in two, as Jinnah's Pakistan is born beside it. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
