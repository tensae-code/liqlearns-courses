# Hands-on — India — Gandhi and the road to 1947

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — India — Gandhi and the road to 1947 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Gandhi, Quit India and independence, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
