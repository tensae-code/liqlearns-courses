# Hands-on — America at war — Vietnam, the Gulf and Iraq

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — America at war — Vietnam, the Gulf and Iraq — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Rise of USA as super power and its wars, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
