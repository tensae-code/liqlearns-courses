# Indonesia — the British interregnum, 1811–1816 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain takes Java in 1811 and Raffles's five-year interregnum interrupts Dutch rule before the colony is handed back in 1816.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.C.2 Perkembangan Kolonialisme Inggris di Indonesia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — Mangkubumi and Mas Said resist the VOC.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.C.2 Perkembangan Kolonialisme Inggris di Indonesia
