# Switzerland — Florian Branger and administrative detention — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The authority answered Branger's small convictions with —
a) Ever longer administrative stays  b) Longer trials  c) A pension and a house
**Answer: a) Ever longer administrative stays**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Among the source documents is —
a) Branger's own handwritten life story  b) A newspaper interview with his captain  c) His school reports
**Answer: a) Branger's own handwritten life story**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** People were sent to Realta —
a) By office decision, without a court  b) Only after a jury trial  c) Only at their own request
**Answer: a) By office decision, without a court**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** In 2016, archaeologists read the skeletons of —
a) 103 inmates from the institution cemetery  b) Sailors from Branger's ship  c) The institution's founders
**Answer: a) 103 inmates from the institution cemetery**
