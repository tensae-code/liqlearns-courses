# Switzerland — Florian Branger and administrative detention — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Branger's world grew larger with each administrative stay.
**Answer: False — his world shrank to walls.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Realta defended chaining inmates who ran away.
**Answer: True — its defence of chaining runaways stands in the source pages against the doctor's plea for Branger.**
