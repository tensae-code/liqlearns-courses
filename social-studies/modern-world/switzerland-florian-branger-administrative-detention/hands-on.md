# Hands-on — Switzerland — Florian Branger and administrative detention

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walls closing in
Draw four frames, one for each stage of Branger's shrinking world: the sea, Chur, Realta, the clinic. Under each, write who decided he would be there.

## Office or court?
Write two columns: What a court must do before it punishes, and What the office had to do before it detained. Circle every protection that exists in the first column and is missing in the second.
