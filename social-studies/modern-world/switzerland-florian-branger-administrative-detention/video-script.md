# Video script — Switzerland — Florian Branger and administrative detention

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Switzerland's *Sorge oder Zwang?* set tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

He sailed the whole world. Then he came home to Chur.



Small convictions — and the office answered without trials: Realta, prison, clinic.



Ten years of his life were detention, on top of his sentences.



In 1938 a former inmate named it plainly. In 2016, the cemetery told the rest.
