# Exploration — Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The New World means North and South America.
**Answer: True** — That is the book's own term for the lands Columbus reached.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Magellan himself completed the whole voyage around the world.
**Answer: False** — Magellan died in the Philippines; one surviving ship completed the loop.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Four of Magellan's five ships were lost.
**Answer: True** — Only one ship returned to Spain.
