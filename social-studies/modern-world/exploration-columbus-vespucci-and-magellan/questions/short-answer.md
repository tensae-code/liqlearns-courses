# Exploration — Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Vespucci prove?
**Answer:** He proved the lands Columbus reached were not the Indies but a different land — the Americas.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why is the first circumnavigation such strong proof?
**Answer:** One ship sailed west from Spain and arrived back in Spain from the other side — the oceans form one ring around a round Earth.
