# Exploration — Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Columbus was funded by —
a) Henry of Portugal  b) Ferdinand and Isabella of Spain  c) Charles V  d) The Pope
**Answer: b) Ferdinand and Isabella of Spain** — Spain's rulers made exploration their national agenda and funded him.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Columbus announced the lands he reached as the —
a) Americas  b) West Indies  c) Philippines  d) Cape Verde
**Answer: b) West Indies** — He believed he had reached the Indies.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The name 'America' comes from —
a) Columbus  b) Magellan  c) Amerigo Vespucci  d) Da Gama
**Answer: c) Amerigo Vespucci** — Vespucci proved the lands were not the Indies; they carry his name.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Magellan set out in August 1519 with —
a) Four ships  b) Thirteen ships  c) Five small ships  d) One ship
**Answer: c) Five small ships** — Five small ships, backed by Charles V of Spain.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** Magellan was the first to sail —
a) The Atlantic  b) The Pacific  c) The Indian Ocean  d) The Red Sea
**Answer: b) The Pacific** — He found and sailed the Pacific in October 1520.
