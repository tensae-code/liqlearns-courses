# Hands-on — Exploration — Columbus, Vespucci and Magellan

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## String around a ball
Take a ball or an orange as the Earth. Run a string from a 'Spain' mark westward, around the whole ball, and back to Spain. That closing loop is what Magellan's surviving ship did.

## Wrong name, right land
Columbus called the islands the 'West Indies' because he thought he was near the Indies. On paper, write the name he used and the name we use now — the Americas — and one sentence on how a wrong guess can still change history.
