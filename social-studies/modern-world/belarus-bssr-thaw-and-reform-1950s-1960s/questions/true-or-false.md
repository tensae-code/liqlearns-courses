# Belarus — the BSSR in the Thaw and reform, 1950s–1960s — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Stalin's death the "Thaw" opens under Mazurov; MAZ and BelAZ trucks, MTZ tractors, Soligorsk potash and Minsk electronics turn the republic industrial
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Belarus under reform

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History of Belarus, Grade 11 (Famin)* is about The republic's social and economic situation.
**Answer: False** — it is about Belarus under reform

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Khrushchev's Thaw loosens fear and denounces the cult of personality, but the party's monopoly survives untouched
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Social and political life in the Thaw

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History of Belarus, Grade 11 (Famin)* is about Attempts to accelerate the BSSR's development.
**Answer: False** — it is about Social and political life in the Thaw

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The scientific-technical revolution reaches Belarus as sovnarkhoz managers and new chemical, radio and machine plants reshape work
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Social and economic development in the Thaw

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *History of Belarus, Grade 11 (Famin)* is about Perestroika in the BSSR and its results.
**Answer: False** — it is about Social and economic development in the Thaw
