## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **El periodo de los 30 años de gobierno conservador (1857-1893)** — after the war, Tomás Martínez's pact starts three stable conservative decades that build the republic's administration and first departments
2. **Economía, industria y sociedad en la segunda mitad del siglo XIX** — cattle, commerce and new roads grow, and coffee begins remaking land and class in Nicaragua
3. **Gobierno de José Santos Zelaya y la Revolución Liberal (1893-1909)** — the coffee bourgeoisie backs Zelaya, who defeats the conservatives on 11 July 1893 and pushes a liberal constitution and modernising reforms
4. **Zelaya deja de ser presidente; aspectos culturales del siglo XIX e inicios del XX** — US pressure forces Zelaya out in 1909, closing the liberal era; the chapter closes with the culture of the century that produced Darío
5. **Los Treinta Años conservadores** — after the National War an oligarchic peace holds to 1893 — property qualifications for voting and office, income from customs and monopolies, no direct taxes
6. **Cambios económicos: el café y la modernización** — from 1858 recovery makes Nicaragua Central America's most stable economy; coffee ties it to the world market, expropriation creates an agricultural proletariat, and the first bank opens in 1888
7. **Causas de la caída de Zelaya** — internal revolts and war costs meet external US hostility; the execution of US mercenaries Cannon and Groce on 16 November 1909 gives Washington its pretext, and Zelaya resigns on 16 December
8. **Cambios en la sociedad nicaragüense 1857-1893: el gobierno conservador** — a small circle of intermarried families rules oligarchically for thirty years on customs and monopoly income, with no direct taxes
9. **El café y la modernización conservadora** — coffee spreads from the Sierras de Managua to Matagalpa and Jinotega and becomes the country's main source of foreign earnings; telegraph lines from 1876 and lake steamers stitch the republic together
10. **Características del gobierno de Zelaya** — Zelaya's liberals expropriate church and communal land for coffee, build the first professional army, supreme court and police — and Zelaya, re-elected by a loyal assembly, never calls elections in sixteen years
11. **Causas de la caída de Zelaya** — Zelaya cancels US concessions, borrows in London instead of New York and seeks canal partners in Germany and Japan; when US mercenaries Cannon and Groce are executed for mining the San Juan, the Knox Note demands his fall and he resigns on 16 December 1909
