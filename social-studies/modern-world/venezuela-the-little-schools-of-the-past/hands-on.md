# Hands-on — Venezuela The Little Schools Of The Past

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela The Little Schools Of The Past — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Those times of the little schools, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
