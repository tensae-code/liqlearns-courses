# The Atlantic economy — silver and slavery

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: American silver floods Europe and an Atlantic economy-world is built, while enslaved Africans are shipped to mine and plant — and French ports such as Nantes and Bordeaux grow rich on that trade. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Silver from the Americas

American silver floods Europe while enslaved Africans are shipped to mine and plant — the gold and silver of the Americas build a developing economy-world across the Atlantic.

In your mind's eye: a ship unloading silver in Europe on one side and enslaved Africans being shipped to the Americas on the other, with the French ports of Nantes and Bordeaux marked. Say what flowed each way and who paid the human cost.

## Sub lesson 2 — The French ports grow rich

Nantes and Bordeaux grow rich on Atlantic trade built on enslaved labour — France's own ports stand inside the same Atlantic economy.
