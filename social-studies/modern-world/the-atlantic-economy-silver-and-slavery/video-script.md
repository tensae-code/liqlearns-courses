# Video script — The Atlantic economy — silver and slavery

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the France book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

American silver floods Europe while enslaved Africans are shipped to mine and plant — the gold and silver of the Americas build a developing economy-world across the Atlantic.

Nantes and Bordeaux grow rich on Atlantic trade built on enslaved labour — France's own ports stand inside the same Atlantic economy.

Hold that story: American silver floods Europe and an Atlantic economy-world is built, while enslaved Africans are shipped to mine and plant — and French ports such as Nantes and Bordeaux grow rich on that trade.
