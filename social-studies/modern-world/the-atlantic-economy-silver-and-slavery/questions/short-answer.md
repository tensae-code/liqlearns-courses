# The Atlantic economy — silver and slavery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the Americas send to Europe, and who was shipped the other way to mine and plant?
**Answer: American silver (gold and silver) flooded Europe; enslaved Africans were shipped to mine and plant (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the French ports the manual names, and say what their wealth rested on.
**Answer: Nantes and Bordeaux; Atlantic trade built on enslaved labour (any close wording).**
