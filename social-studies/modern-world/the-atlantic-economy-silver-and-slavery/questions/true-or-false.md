# The Atlantic economy — silver and slavery — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** American silver flooded Europe in the Atlantic opening.
**Answer: True** — Thème 2's beat.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Nantes and Bordeaux grew rich without any link to enslaved labour.
**Answer: False** — their Atlantic trade was built on enslaved labour.
