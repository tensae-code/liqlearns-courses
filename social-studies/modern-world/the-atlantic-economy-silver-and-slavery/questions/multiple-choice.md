# The Atlantic economy — silver and slavery — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What flooded Europe from the Americas?
a) American silver  b) Only printed books  c) Steam engines
**Answer: a) American silver** — American silver floods Europe.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who were shipped to mine and plant?
a) Enslaved Africans  b) European kings  c) Venetian merchants only
**Answer: a) Enslaved Africans** — enslaved Africans are shipped to mine and plant.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Which French ports grow rich on Atlantic trade?
a) Nantes and Bordeaux  b) Paris and Lyon only  c) No French ports
**Answer: a) Nantes and Bordeaux** — Nantes and Bordeaux, in Thème 4.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** That Atlantic trade was built on —
a) Enslaved labour  b) Free wage labour only  c) No labour at all
**Answer: a) Enslaved labour** — Atlantic trade built on enslaved labour.
