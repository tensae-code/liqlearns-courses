# Venezuela Black Friday 1983 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The bolívar's 1983 collapse ends the rich-Venezuela story and Lusinchi inherits a debt he refinances on the creditors' terms.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Black Friday 1983 and the inherited debt

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Black Friday 1983 and the inherited debt

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Spending, debt and Black Friday 1983 break the rich-Venezuela story inside the democratic period itself.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Governments of Herrera Campíns and Lusinchi (1979–1989)
