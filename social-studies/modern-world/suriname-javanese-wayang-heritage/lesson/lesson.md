## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pak Saleh laat de poppen bewegen

Pak Saleh carves twenty leather wayang puppets and performs wayang kulit behind a screen, singing a story that teaches the audience to plant trees (Pak Saleh laat de poppen bewegen)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
