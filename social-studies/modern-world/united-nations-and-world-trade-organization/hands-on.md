# Hands-on — United Nations And World Trade Organization

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — United Nations And World Trade Organization — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The UN and the WTO, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
