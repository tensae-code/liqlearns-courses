# Lesson plan — Jordan — founding the Emirate and the road to independence

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Jordan — founding the Emirate and the road to independence
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Jordan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** France seizes Damascus and Syria's leaders beg Sharif Hussein to send a son; Abdullah reaches Amman and on 11 April 1921 forms the Council of Advisors under Rashid Tali', staffed by Syrian Istiqlal men; the 1928 Jerusalem agreement promises a Basic Law and hands legislation and executive power to the Amir, yet keeps British troops and an annual grant; the People's Party and the national conferences answer with a charter — reject Balfour, refuse to pay for Britain's Frontier Force, demand real elections and a government answerable to parliament; the 1935 Defence Law muzzles the opposition, until the March 1946 treaty ends the mandate; the state emblem, designed in 1934 at the founder's wish, is set over Raghadan Palace
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
