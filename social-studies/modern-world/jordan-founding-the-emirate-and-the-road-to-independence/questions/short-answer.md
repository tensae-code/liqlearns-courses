# Jordan — founding the Emirate and the road to independence — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lesson 1: the founding of the Jordanian Emirate.
**Answer: France seizes Damascus and Syria's leaders beg Sharif Hussein to send a son; Abdullah reaches Amman and on 11 April 1921 forms the Council of Advisors under Rashid Tali', staffed by Syrian Istiqlal men; the 1928 Jerusalem agreement promises a Basic Law and hands legislation and executive power to the Amir, yet keeps British troops and an annual grant; the People's Party and the national conferences answer with a charter — reject Balfour, refuse to pay for Britain's Frontier Force, demand real elections and a government answerable to parliament; the 1935 Defence Law muzzles the opposition, until the March 1946 treaty ends the mandate; the state emblem, designed in 1934 at the founder's wish, is set over Raghadan Palace (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *تاريخ الأردن — الصف الحادي عشر، الفرع الأكاديمي (History of Jordan, Grade 11, Academic Track), Term 2 — Unit 4: Political Life in Jordan; Unit 5: Economic Life in Jordan; Unit 6: Social Life in Jordan; Unit 7: Education and Culture in Jordan; Unit 8: Jordan and the Palestinian Cause* (Jordan), Lesson 1: the founding of the Jordanian Emirate, printed p. 8.**
