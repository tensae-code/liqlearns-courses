# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan Abylai Khan And Russian Subjecthood

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan Abylai Khan And Russian Subjecthood
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** embassies and oaths draw the khanate toward the empire
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§7–8 Kazakh-Russian relations in the 18th century** — embassies and oaths draw the khanate toward the empire
2. **§9 Causes and consequences of Abulkhair's acceptance of Russian subjecthood** — the Junior Zhuz khan's 1730 oath opens the door to colonisation
3. **§10 The Russian Empire's policy in Kazakhstan in the 1730s–50s** — fortress lines and trade posts tighten the empire's grip
4. **§11 The internal political situation worsens in the 1740s** — succession quarrels divide the khanate as pressure grows
5. **§12 Abylai Khan emerges as a strong political figure** — the young sultan's diplomacy and warcraft raise him above rivals
6. **§13 Abylai Khan and the strengthening of the united Kazakh Khanate** — Abylai gathers all three zhuzes under one authority
7. **§14 Abylai Khan's internal and foreign policy** — Abylai balances Russia, China and the steppe to keep independence
8. **§§7–8 Kazakh-Russian relations at the start of the 18th century** — first embassies test the empire's intentions
9. **§§9–10 The Kazakh Khanate's joining of Russia begins** — Abulkhair's oath and the fortress lines follow
10. **§§11–12 Abylai Khan's internal and foreign policy** — Abylai holds the zhuzes together between two empires
