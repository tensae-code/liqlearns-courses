# Kazakhstan The Dzhungar Invasion Begins — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the Dzhungar state arms for conquest while a divided Kazakh Khanate faces invasion from the east and Russian forts from the north.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§1–2 The start of the patriotic war against Dzhungar aggression

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§1–2 The start of the patriotic war against Dzhungar aggression

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Tauke's unified khanate weakens after his death just as Dzhungaria arms and Russia builds forts.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §1 The domestic and foreign political position of the Kazakh Khanate at the start of the 18th century

**Answer: False** — it is about §1 The domestic and foreign political position of the Kazakh Khanate at the start of the 18th century
