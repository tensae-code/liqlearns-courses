# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan The Dzhungar Invasion Begins

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan The Dzhungar Invasion Begins
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Dzhungar state arms for conquest while a divided Kazakh Khanate faces invasion from the east and Russian forts from the north
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§1–2 The start of the patriotic war against Dzhungar aggression** — the Dzhungar state arms for conquest while a divided Kazakh Khanate faces invasion from the east and Russian forts from the north
2. **§1 The domestic and foreign political position of the Kazakh Khanate at the start of the 18th century** — Tauke's unified khanate weakens after his death just as Dzhungaria arms and Russia builds forts
3. **§2 The organisation of nationwide resistance to the Dzhungar invasion** — khans, sultans and batyrs pull a fragmented people into one defensive war
4. **Lesson plan §1: the Kazakh Khanate's internal and external political situation in the first quarter of the 18th century (with the "Zheti Zhargy" law and the Kultobe gatherings)** — the teacher builds the khanate under Tauke — law code, councils, growing danger — before the invasion falls
