# China Founding Of The Prc 1949 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The PRC is founded.
**Answer: in September 1949 the CPPCC elects Mao Zedong chairman, renames Beiping Beijing, adopts the March of the Volunteers as anthem and the five-star flag, and on 1 October the new state is proclaimed at Tiananmen (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *中国历史 八年级 下册 (Chinese History, Grade 8, second volume)* (China), Lesson 1: The PRC is founded, printed p. 2.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The PRC founded.
**Answer: the CPPCC, the Common Programme and 1 October 1949 open the new state, and land reform and the Korean War secure it (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In China's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *普通高中教科书 历史 必修 中外历史纲要（上） (Senior High History, Compulsory: Outline of Chinese and World History, upper volume — Chinese history)* (China), Lesson 26: The PRC founded, printed p. 165.**
