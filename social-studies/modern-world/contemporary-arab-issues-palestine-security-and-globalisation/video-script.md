# Video script — Contemporary decisive Arab issues (Unit 6 Lesson 4)

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Yemen's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

three open files decide the Arab future — Palestine, harmed by the Cold War's end, America's vetoes and Arab division; Arab national security, defined as one nation's shared shield against dangers from inside and out, with charts of its internal and external threats; and globalisation, weighed type by type — financial, technological, economic, cultural, political — for what it gives the Arab homeland and what it takes.
