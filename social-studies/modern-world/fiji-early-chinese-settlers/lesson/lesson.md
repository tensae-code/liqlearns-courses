# Fiji's early Chinese settlers

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Moy Ba Ling, also called Houng Lee, sails from Australia in the 1850s, settles at Levuka, fetches relatives, and Chinese merchants open Fiji's first rural shops chasing gold, sandalwood and beche-de-mer As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Early Chinese to Fiji

Moy Ba Ling, also called Houng Lee, sails from Australia in the 1850s, settles at Levuka, fetches relatives, and Chinese merchants open Fiji's first rural shops chasing gold, sandalwood and beche-de-mer.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
