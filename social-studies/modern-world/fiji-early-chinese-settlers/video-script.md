# Video script — Fiji's early Chinese settlers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Moy Ba Ling, also called Houng Lee, sails from Australia in the 1850s, settles at Levuka, fetches relatives, and Chinese merchants open Fiji's first rural shops chasing gold, sandalwood and beche-de-mer.
