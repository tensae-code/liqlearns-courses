# Lesson plan — Micronesia — the Second World War on the islands

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — the Second World War on the islands
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** On the islands themselves, the Second World War survives in elders' stories, rusted cannons and mountain bunkers — and Micronesia's Grade 9 book teaches the war's island end by sending learners out to research how their own island remembers it.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Elders' stories: the war as told at home** — say that elders' stories keep the war on the islands alive in community memory.
2. **Rusted cannons and mountain bunkers** — say that rusted cannons and mountain bunkers remain on the islands as the war's physical evidence.
3. **Research your own island's war** — say that learners research how their own island remembers the war's end.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
