# Hands-on — Micronesia — the Second World War on the islands

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Walk to the evidence
Find the oldest human-made thing near your home that carries a story — a ruin, a memorial, an old tree or building. Sketch it, then write the story an elder tells about it. Label which parts are story-evidence and which are thing-evidence.

## Interview for the island record
Ask an older person what they were told about the war years by their own elders. Write down three sentences exactly as their memory, and one sentence of your own saying what the story proves and what it cannot prove alone.
