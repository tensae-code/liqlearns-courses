# Micronesia — the Second World War on the islands — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Nothing of the war remains on the islands today that a learner could visit.
**Answer: False** — rusted cannons and mountain bunkers remain as evidence learners can walk to.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** The book treats island memory as real historical evidence to be researched.
**Answer: True** — learners research their own island's memory of the war's end.
