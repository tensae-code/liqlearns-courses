# Micronesia — the Second World War on the islands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name two kinds of evidence that keep the war alive on the islands.
**Answer: Elders' stories told in the community, and physical remains — rusted cannons and mountain bunkers (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** How does the book turn learners into historians of the war?
**Answer: It sends them to research how their own island remembers the war's end — from elders' accounts and the remains — and write it themselves (any close wording).**
