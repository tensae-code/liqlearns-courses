# Micronesia — the Second World War on the islands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Island children first meet the war mainly through —
a) Elders' stories told at home  b) Foreign films  c) Newspapers from 1945
**Answer: a) Elders' stories told at home** — the memory the book starts from.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The war's physical evidence still standing on the islands includes —
a) Rusted cannons and mountain bunkers  b) Sunken treasure  c) Spanish churches
**Answer: a) Rusted cannons and mountain bunkers** — the remains the book names.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The book asks learners to research —
a) How their own island remembers the war's end  b) The generals' battle plans  c) Ship movements in Europe
**Answer: a) How their own island remembers the war's end** — the research task as filed.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** This lesson's war is the same war taught in the Pacific-war lesson, seen from —
a) The admirals' side  b) The islands' own ground and memory  c) Japan's home islands
**Answer: b) The islands' own ground and memory** — the island view this lesson teaches.
