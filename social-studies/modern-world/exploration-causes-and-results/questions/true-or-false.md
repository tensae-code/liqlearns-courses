# Exploration — causes and results — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** When Constantinople fell, the European land route to the Far East stopped.
**Answer: True** — That block is the first driver of the sea voyages.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Latin America's native societies were untouched by the voyages.
**Answer: False** — Their languages, histories and identity values were destroyed and overlaid by European culture.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The voyages stirred other European countries to explore.
**Answer: True** — Portugal and Spain's starts pulled the rest in.
