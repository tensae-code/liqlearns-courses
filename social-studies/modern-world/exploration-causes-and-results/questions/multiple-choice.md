# Exploration — causes and results — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Constantinople fell to the Ottoman Turks in —
a) 1453  b) 1498  c) 1517  d) 1522
**Answer: a) 1453** — 1453 — and the land route and trade stopped.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Europe most demanded which Far Eastern goods?
a) Spices  b) Wool  c) Timber  d) Iron
**Answer: a) Spices** — Spices were so demanded that a sea route had to be found.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these was one of the new sailing tools?
a) The compass and magnetic needle  b) The steam engine  c) The aeroplane  d) The railway
**Answer: a) The compass and magnetic needle** — Improved maps, globes, compass and needle cut the danger.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** One result of the voyages was that Europeans —
a) Stopped trading  b) Fought each other over overseas possessions  c) Gave up their colonies  d) Closed their ports
**Answer: b) Fought each other over overseas possessions** — Competition for overseas possessions bred conflict among Europeans themselves.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The age of exploration expanded —
a) Farming in Europe  b) The slave trade in Africa  c) Shipbuilding only in Portugal  d) Trade inside Spain only
**Answer: b) The slave trade in Africa** — The book names the expanded slave trade among the age's results.
