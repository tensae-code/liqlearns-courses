# Exploration — causes and results — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did a blocked land road push sailors to sea?
**Answer:** Europe still demanded Far Eastern goods, above all spices; with the land route stopped, an alternative sea route had to be found.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two harms the book lays at the voyages' door.
**Answer:** The slave trade in Africa expanded, and Latin America's native societies were endangered — their languages, histories and identity values destroyed and overlaid by European culture.
