# Hands-on — Exploration — causes and results

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Block the road
Draw the land road from Europe to the Far East and block it with a wall labelled 'Constantinople, 1453'. Now draw the two sea ways around — da Gama's and Columbus's. The block explains both.

## Two columns, honestly
Make two columns: 'What the world gained' and 'Who paid'. Fill each from the lesson. Read both aloud — history keeps both columns.
