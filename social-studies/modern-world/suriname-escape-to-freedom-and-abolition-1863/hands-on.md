# Hands-on — Suriname — escape to freedom and abolition, 1863

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Suriname — escape to freedom and abolition, 1863 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Varen naar de vrijheid, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
