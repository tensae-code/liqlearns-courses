# Video script — Suriname — escape to freedom and abolition, 1863

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Suriname's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana; Suriname's own abolition comes only thirty years later, 1 July 1863.
