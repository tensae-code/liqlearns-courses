# Suriname — escape to freedom and abolition, 1863

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana; Suriname's own abolition comes only thirty years later, 1 July 1863 As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Varen naar de vrijheid

In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana; Suriname's own abolition comes only thirty years later, 1 July 1863 (Varen naar de vrijheid)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Varen naar de vrijheid — In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana; Suriname's own abolition comes only thirty years later, 1 July 1863. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
