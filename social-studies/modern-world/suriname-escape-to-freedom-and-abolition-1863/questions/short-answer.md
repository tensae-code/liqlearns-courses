# Suriname — escape to freedom and abolition, 1863 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Varen naar de vrijheid.
**Answer: In 1833 the enslaved Philip and his Coronie helpers seize the sailing ship Clyde, bind Captain Sander and sail seven days to free Berbice in Guyana; Suriname's own abolition comes only thirty years later, 1 July 1863. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
