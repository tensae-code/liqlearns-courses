# Lesson plan — Micronesia — German rule and the Sokehs Rebellion

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — German rule and the Sokehs Rebellion
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Germany bought the Carolines from Spain in 1899 for just over four million dollars and pushed roads, cash crops and land and labour reforms from district offices on Saipan, Yap and Pohnpei — until in 1910 the people of Sokehs rebelled against the strict reforms and killed the German district officer, a rising German soldiers crushed in 1911, while typhoons drove outer islanders onto the high islands.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Germany buys the Carolines and pushes development** — say that Germany bought the Carolines from Spain in 1899 for just over four million dollars and pushed roads, cash crops and land and labour reforms from district offices on Saipan, Yap and Pohnpei.
2. **The Sokehs Rebellion and stormy weather** — say that in 1910 the people of Sokehs rebelled against the strict new reforms and killed the German district officer, while typhoons drove outer islanders onto the high islands and the Catholic Church expanded into Chuuk.
3. **Kapingamarangi's German time, 1899–1914** — say that German administration followed the purchase of 1899 on Kapingamarangi, bringing new officials, rules and trade until the First World War ended it.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
