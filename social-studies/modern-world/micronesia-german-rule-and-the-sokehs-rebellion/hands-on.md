# Hands-on — Micronesia — German rule and the Sokehs Rebellion

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Development, two verdicts
Write the German district officer's report on the new road in three sentences. Then write the Sokehs elder's answer in three sentences. Read both aloud — the same road, two histories.

## An era in dates
Kapingamarangi's reader divides time by rulers. Make your own dated era-line for your home: name three eras (for example 'the time of the old school'), give each its years, and say what ended each one.
