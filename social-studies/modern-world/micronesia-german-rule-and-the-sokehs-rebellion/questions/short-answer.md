# Micronesia — German rule and the Sokehs Rebellion — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did Germany's 'development' mean in practice for islanders?
**Answer: Roads, cash crops, and land and labour reforms run from district offices — reaching into how islanders held land and owed work (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did the people of Sokehs rebel, and what did they do?
**Answer: They rebelled against the strict new reforms in 1910 and killed the German district officer; German soldiers were sent against them in 1911 (any close wording).**
