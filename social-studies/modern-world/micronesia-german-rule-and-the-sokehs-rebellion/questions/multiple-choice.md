# Micronesia — German rule and the Sokehs Rebellion — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Germany bought the Carolines from Spain in —
a) 1870  b) 1899  c) 1914
**Answer: b) 1899** — the purchase year both books give.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The price Germany paid was —
a) Just over four million dollars  b) Four million marks in gold ships  c) Nothing — Spain gave them away
**Answer: a) Just over four million dollars** — the price the Grade 9 book gives.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sokehs Rebellion broke out in —
a) 1899  b) 1910  c) 1914
**Answer: b) 1910** — the rebellion year the book gives.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The rebels killed —
a) A missionary  b) The German district officer  c) A Spanish governor
**Answer: b) The German district officer** — the killing the book records.
