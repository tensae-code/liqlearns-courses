# Micronesia — German rule and the Sokehs Rebellion — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** German soldiers were sent against the Sokehs rebels in 1911.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 11 book dates the soldiers' answer to 1911.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Kapingamarangi's German time ended because the atoll expelled the Germans itself.
**Answer: False** — the reader dates the German time 1899–1914 — it ended with the First World War, from outside.
