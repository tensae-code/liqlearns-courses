# Micronesia — German rule and the Sokehs Rebellion — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the books teach them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Germany buys the Carolines and pushes development
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that Germany bought the Carolines from Spain in 1899 for just over four million dollars and pushed roads, cash crops and land and labour reforms from district offices on Saipan, Yap and Pohnpei.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Sokehs Rebellion and stormy weather
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that in 1910 the people of Sokehs rebelled against the strict new reforms and killed the German district officer, while typhoons drove outer islanders onto the high islands and the Catholic Church expanded into Chuuk.

## Sub lesson 3 — Kapingamarangi's German time, 1899–1914
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that German administration followed the purchase of 1899 on Kapingamarangi, bringing new officials, rules and trade until the First World War ended it.
