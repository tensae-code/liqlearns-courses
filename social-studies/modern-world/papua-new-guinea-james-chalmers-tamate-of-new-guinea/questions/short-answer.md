# James Chalmers — Tamate of New Guinea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Case study – James Chalmers.
**Answer: A Scottish stonemason's son, converted in a revival, sails to Rarotonga then New Guinea as "Tamate", builds the southern-coast mission chain, interprets at the 1884 Protectorate ceremony, and is killed with Oliver Tompkins on Goaribari Island in 1901 — the last punitive expedition and a judge's suicide follow (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Papua New Guinea's book, where is this part taught?
