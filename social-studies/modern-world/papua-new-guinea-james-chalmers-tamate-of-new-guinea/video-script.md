# Video script — James Chalmers — Tamate of New Guinea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

A Scottish stonemason's son, converted in a revival, sails to Rarotonga then New Guinea as "Tamate", builds the southern-coast mission chain, interprets at the 1884 Protectorate ceremony, and is killed with Oliver Tompkins on Goaribari Island in 1901 — the last punitive expedition and a judge's suicide follow.
