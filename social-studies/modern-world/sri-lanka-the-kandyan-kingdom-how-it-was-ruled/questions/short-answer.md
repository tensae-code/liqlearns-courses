# Sri Lanka — The Kandyan kingdom how it was ruled — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Government of the Kandyan kingdom.
**Answer: The king consults his great council before deciding, the land is run in rata divisions under disavas, villages govern themselves, and temple lands answer to their own officers (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *G10 Social Education and History Tamil 1999* (Sri Lanka), Government of the Kandyan kingdom, printed p. 49.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The Kandyan kingdom 1789–1815.
**Answer: After Rajadhi Rajasinghe dies in 1798 the young Sri Vikrama takes a throne ringed by mighty chiefs, the first Adigar Pilima Talauwa pulls the strings, and a kingdom run by hereditary officials waits for its crisis (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Sri Lanka's book, where is this part taught?
