# Armenia Building The Socialist Order — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 3.5 Soviet Armenia on the road to establishing the "socialist order".
**Answer: NEP gives way to collectivisation and crash industrialisation; peasants lose their land as factories and canals rise across the small republic (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար (History of Armenia 9, textbook for general-education schools), by Gevorg Kesoyan, Vahagn Hakobyan, Nzhdeh Hovsepyan, Hrachin Vardanyan, Ala Kharatyan, Tatevik Hambardzumyan, Aghasi Tadevosyan* (Armenia), 3.5 Soviet Armenia on the road to establishing the "socialist order", printed p. 90.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Appendix theme guide 3: Soviet Armenia 1920–1991.
**Answer: the guide frames seventy Soviet years as both modernisation and subordination — federation, borders fixed against Armenia, repression, war service and everyday life (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Հայաստանի պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց (History of Armenia 9, Methodical Guide)* (Armenia), Appendix theme guide 3: Soviet Armenia 1920–1991, printed p. 126.**
