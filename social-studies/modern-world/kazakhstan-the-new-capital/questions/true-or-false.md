# Kazakhstan The New Capital — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the capital moves to Akmola/Astana in 1997 — a city rises on the steppe.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §54. Our country's capital — a symbol of new Kazakhstan's prosperity

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Қозоғистон тарихи (1945–2019 йй.), 2-қисм [History of Kazakhstan (1945–2019), Part 2] — for grades 8–9* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §54. Our country's capital — a symbol of new Kazakhstan's prosperity

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the capital moves north in 1997 and a new city rises.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §26 Astana (Nur-Sultan) — the capital of independent Kazakhstan

**Answer: False** — it is about §26 Astana (Nur-Sultan) — the capital of independent Kazakhstan
