# Hands-on — Suriname — Jan Ernst Matzeliger, inventor

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Suriname — Jan Ernst Matzeliger, inventor — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Een gewone jongen wordt een uitvinder, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
