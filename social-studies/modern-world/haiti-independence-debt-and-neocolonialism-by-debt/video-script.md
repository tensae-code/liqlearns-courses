# Video script — Haiti — independence debt and neocolonialism by debt

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Haiti guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

France offers recognition at a price — the 1825 ordinance turns independence itself into a bill haiti must pay

In 2003 president aristide demands france give the money back — pupils open the file and ask what a 19th-century debt did to haiti's development

French warships anchor off port-au-prince in 1825; the ordinance offers recognition in exchange for a huge indemnity to former slave-owners, softened only by the 1838 treaties

To pay the first instalments haiti must borrow from french banks — now it owes the indemnity and the loans taken to pay it

Decades of payments and fresh loans drain the treasury — pupils follow the money and judge the book's charge: neocolonialism by debt

Hold that story: France offers recognition at a price in 1825 — an indemnity to former slave-owners that becomes a double debt, draining Haiti's treasury for decades; in 2003 President Aristide demands the money back.
