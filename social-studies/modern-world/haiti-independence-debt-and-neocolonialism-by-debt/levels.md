# Haiti — independence debt and neocolonialism by debt — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — International recognition and debt
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: France offers recognition at a price — the 1825 ordinance turns independence itself into a bill haiti must pay (Séance 7, Reconnaissance internationale et dette (developed in the companion debt sequence))

## Sub lesson 2 — The debt and Haiti's development — a contemporary debate
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: In 2003 president aristide demands france give the money back — pupils open the file and ask what a 19th-century debt did to haiti's development (Séance 1, La dette et le développement d'Haïti, un débat contemporain ?)

## Sub lesson 3 — The independence debt — negotiation and imposition
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: French warships anchor off port-au-prince in 1825; the ordinance offers recognition in exchange for a huge indemnity to former slave-owners, softened only by the 1838 treaties (Séance 2, La dette de l'indépendance, négociation et imposition)

## Sub lesson 4 — From the debt to the double debt (1)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: To pay the first instalments haiti must borrow from french banks — now it owes the indemnity and the loans taken to pay it (Séance 3, De la dette à « la double dette » (1))

## Sub lesson 5 — From the debt to the double debt (2)
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Decades of payments and fresh loans drain the treasury — pupils follow the money and judge the book's charge: neocolonialism by debt (Séance 4, De la dette à « la double dette » (2))
