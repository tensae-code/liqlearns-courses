# Haiti — independence debt and neocolonialism by debt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** France offers recognition at a price — the 1825 ordinance turns independence itself into a bill haiti must pay
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 7, Reconnaissance internationale et dette (developed in the companion debt sequence)).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In 2003 president aristide demands france give the money back — pupils open the file and ask what a 19th-century debt did to haiti's development
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 1, La dette et le développement d'Haïti, un débat contemporain ?).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** French warships anchor off port-au-prince in 1825; the ordinance offers recognition in exchange for a huge indemnity to former slave-owners, softened only by the 1838 treaties
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 2, La dette de l'indépendance, négociation et imposition).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** To pay the first instalments haiti must borrow from french banks — now it owes the indemnity and the loans taken to pay it
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 3, De la dette à « la double dette » (1)).

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Decades of payments and fresh loans drain the treasury — pupils follow the money and judge the book's charge: neocolonialism by debt
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 4, De la dette à « la double dette » (2)).
