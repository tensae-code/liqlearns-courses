## Sub lesson 1 — The rulers' wisdom
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the Bangkok Treaty of 1909 splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain; Terengganu's sultan, ulama and chiefs reject it outright, and Kelantan's Sultan Muhammad IV hears the news from the British officer J.S. Mason; Johor answers differently — Sultan Abu Bakar, with a capable and loyal Majlis Jemaah Menteri, continues Temenggong Daeng Ibrahim's modernisation of administration, finance and law precisely to leave Britain no opening to intervene
