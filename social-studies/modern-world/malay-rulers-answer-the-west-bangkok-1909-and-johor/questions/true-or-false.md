# Malay rulers answer the West — Bangkok 1909 and Johor — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Bangkok Treaty of 1909 splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain; Terengganu's sultan, ulama and chiefs reject it outright, and Kelantan's Sultan Muhammad IV hears the news from the British officer J.S. Mason; Johor answers differently — Sultan Abu Bakar, with a capable and loyal Majlis Jemaah Menteri, continues Temenggong Daeng Ibrahim's modernisation of administration, finance and law precisely to leave Britain no opening to intervene
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 8: the rulers' wisdom).
