# Malay rulers answer the West — Bangkok 1909 and Johor — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The rulers' wisdom.
**Answer: the Bangkok Treaty of 1909 splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain; Terengganu's sultan, ulama and chiefs reject it outright, and Kelantan's Sultan Muhammad IV hears the news from the British officer J.S. Mason; Johor answers differently — Sultan Abu Bakar, with a capable and loyal Majlis Jemaah Menteri, continues Temenggong Daeng Ibrahim's modernisation of administration, finance and law precisely to leave Britain no opening to intervene (any close wording).**
