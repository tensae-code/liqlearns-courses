# Malay rulers answer the West — Bangkok 1909 and Johor — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The rulers' wisdom  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The rulers' wisdom** — the Bangkok Treaty of 1909 splits the northern Malay states between Siam and Britain; Terengganu's sultan, ulama and chiefs reject it outright, and Kelantan's Sultan Muhammad IV hears the news from the British officer J.S. Mason; Johor answers differently — Sultan Abu Bakar, with a capable and loyal Majlis Jemaah Menteri, continues Temenggong Daeng Ibrahim's modernisation of administration, finance and law precisely to leave Britain no opening to intervene

**Answer: a) Bab 8: the rulers' wisdom** — the book's own heading for this part.
