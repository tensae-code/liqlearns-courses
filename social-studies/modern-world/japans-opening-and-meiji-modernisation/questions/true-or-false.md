# Japans Opening And Meiji Modernisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** American black ships force Japan open, and the Meiji leaders answer by modernising at speed.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §26 The development of Japan; the Far Eastern policy of the European countries and the "opening" of Japan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Всемирная история (1640–1900) [World History (1640–1900)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §26 The development of Japan; the Far Eastern policy of the European countries and the "opening" of Japan

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Perry's black ships anchor off Japan to force trade, and a closed country is opened at cannon-mouth.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §10.3 Why did the USA send warships to Japan?

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Всемирная история. 8 класс [World History, Grade 8]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §10.3 Why did the USA send warships to Japan?
