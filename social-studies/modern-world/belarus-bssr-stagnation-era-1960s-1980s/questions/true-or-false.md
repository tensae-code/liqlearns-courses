# Belarus — the BSSR in the stagnation era, 1960s–1980s — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Khrushchev is removed in 1964, reform cools into stability, and Masherov's long rule keeps Belarus orderly while freedoms stay frozen
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The social and political situation of the stagnation era

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History of Belarus, Grade 11 (Famin)* is about Education and science, late 1950s — 1980s.
**Answer: False** — it is about The social and political situation of the stagnation era

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1965 economic reform fades by the early 1970s, extensive growth slows and the first signs of a braking mechanism and stagnation appear
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The republic's social and economic situation

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *History of Belarus, Grade 11 (Famin)* is about Belarusian literature, late 1950s — 1980s.
**Answer: False** — it is about The republic's social and economic situation

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The audit closes — a peasant republic became an industrial one, at the price of a system that could no longer reform itself
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Results of the BSSR's development as an industrial society

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *History of Belarus, Grade 11 (Famin)* is about Main directions of social and economic development.
**Answer: False** — it is about Results of the BSSR's development as an industrial society
