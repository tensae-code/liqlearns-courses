Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 3.1 Armenia in the first half-year of Bolshevik rule.
**Answer: the Revolutionary Committee takes Yerevan, requisitioning and arrests follow, and the February 1921 uprising briefly drives the Bolsheviks out before their return (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
