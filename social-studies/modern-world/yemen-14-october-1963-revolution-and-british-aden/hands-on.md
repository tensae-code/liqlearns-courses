# Hands-on — Revolution of 14 October

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Revolution of 14 October — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Revolution of 14 October, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
