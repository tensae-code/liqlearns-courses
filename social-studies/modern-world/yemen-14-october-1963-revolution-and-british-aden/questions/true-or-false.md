# Revolution of 14 October — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Britain seizes Aden on 19 January 1839 and splits the south into sultanates and protectorates to rule it, until the first spark flies from the Radfan mountains on 14 October 1963 under Sheikh Rajeh bin Ghaleb Labouza, martyred that first day, and the struggle drives the British out on 30 November 1967.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Revolution of 14 October

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التربية الاجتماعية للصف الرابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (Social Education for Grade 4, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Revolution of 14 October

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Britain, holding India — the jewel of its crown, taken from the French after the Seven Years War — needs the road to it guarded, and Yemen commands that road: Bab al-Mandab, revived by the Suez Canal in 1869, the watchtower islands of Perim, Kamaran and Socotra, and Aden, the finest servicing port on the way east, where the first British ship called in 1609; fearing Napoleon's drive from Egypt toward India, Britain seizes Perim in 1799, signs with the Sultan of Lahj in 1802, and in 1839, on the pretext of a looted Indian ship, the Daria Dawlat, occupies Aden itself and spreads over the southern protectorates.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in British ambitions in Yemen

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ العرب الحديث والمعاصر للصف التاسع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (Modern and Contemporary History of the Arabs for Grade 9, Basic Education — Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about British ambitions in Yemen

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** against Britain's divide-and-rule in Aden, the first spark of armed revolution rises from the Radfan mountains on 14 October 1963, joining armed struggle, workers' and students' marches and diplomacy until the evacuation of 30 November 1967 raises the republic over more than 23 sultanates and sheikhdoms Britain had kept divided.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Yemeni Revolution: the South (Unit 2 Lesson 4)

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *تاريخ العالم الحديث والمعاصر — تاريخ للصف الثالث الثانوي (History: Modern and Contemporary History of the World, for the Third Secondary Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Yemeni Revolution: the South (Unit 2 Lesson 4)
