# Revolution of 14 October — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Revolution of 14 October.
**Answer: britain seizes Aden on 19 January 1839 and splits the south into sultanates and protectorates to rule it, until the first spark flies from the Radfan mountains on 14 October 1963 under Sheikh Rajeh bin Ghaleb Labouza, martyred that first day, and the struggle drives the British out on 30 November 1967 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التربية الاجتماعية للصف الرابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (Social Education for Grade 4, Basic Education)* (Yemen), Revolution of 14 October, printed p. 68.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: British ambitions in Yemen.
**Answer: britain, holding India — the jewel of its crown, taken from the French after the Seven Years War — needs the road to it guarded, and Yemen commands that road: Bab al-Mandab, revived by the Suez Canal in 1869, the watchtower islands of Perim, Kamaran and Socotra, and Aden, the finest servicing port on the way east, where the first British ship called in 1609; fearing Napoleon's drive from Egypt toward India, Britain seizes Perim in 1799, signs with the Sultan of Lahj in 1802, and in 1839, on the pretext of a looted Indian ship, the Daria Dawlat, occupies Aden itself and spreads over the southern protectorates (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العرب الحديث والمعاصر للصف التاسع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي — الجزء الأول (Modern and Contemporary History of the Arabs for Grade 9, Basic Education — Part 1)* (Yemen), British ambitions in Yemen, printed p. 90.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: The Yemeni Revolution: the South (Unit 2 Lesson 4).
**Answer: against Britain's divide-and-rule in Aden, the first spark of armed revolution rises from the Radfan mountains on 14 October 1963, joining armed struggle, workers' and students' marches and diplomacy until the evacuation of 30 November 1967 raises the republic over more than 23 sultanates and sheikhdoms Britain had kept divided (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العالم الحديث والمعاصر — تاريخ للصف الثالث الثانوي (History: Modern and Contemporary History of the World, for the Third Secondary Grade)* (Yemen), The Yemeni Revolution: the South (Unit 2 Lesson 4), printed p. 57.**
