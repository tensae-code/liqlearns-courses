# Video script — Colombia's ethnic diversity — Raizal and Indigenous peoples

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Colombia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Colombia's diversity is the result of its whole history — the Raizal people of the Caribbean islands and the Indigenous peoples learners research, each with the same rights.

Colombia's diversity is the result of the whole preceding history; the Raizal people of the Caribbean islands descend from Indigenous, Spanish, French, English, Dutch and African mixing, and learners research one Indigenous people's history and culture.
