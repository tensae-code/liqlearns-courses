# Colombia's ethnic diversity — Raizal and Indigenous peoples

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Colombia's diversity is the result of its whole history — the Raizal people of the Caribbean islands and the Indigenous peoples learners research, each with the same rights. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Guía 6: All ethnic groups have the same rights

Colombia's diversity is the result of the whole preceding history; the Raizal people of the Caribbean islands descend from Indigenous, Spanish, French, English, Dutch and African mixing, and learners research one Indigenous people's history and culture

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
