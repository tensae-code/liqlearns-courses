# Indonesia — Mangkubumi and Mas Said resist the VOC — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Insulted by VOC interference at the Surakarta court, Prince Mangkubumi and his son-in-law Mas Said raise 13,000 men from Sukowati and fight the company and the Sunan together.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 2.A.8 Perlawanan Pangeran Mangkubumi dan Mas Said

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — the Banjar War.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 2.A.8 Perlawanan Pangeran Mangkubumi dan Mas Said
