# From Cold War to culture wars, 1980–2000

## Do it first

Reagan cut taxes and confronted the Soviet Union — and won the Cold War's end. The new world order was proclaimed. Then the fighting moved home: the culture wars. Before you read on, say in your mind what you already think happened — hold your ideas; the story will test them.

## Sub lesson 1 — Reagan's revolution

Reagan's revolution cut taxes, shrank the government's domestic ambitions and confronted the Soviet Union head-on. Conservatism, insurgent a decade before, now governed.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Cold War ends; a new world order is proclaimed

The Cold War ended — the Soviet standoff that had organised two generations of American life simply stopped. A new world order was proclaimed: one superpower, and a peace whose shape nobody yet knew.

## Sub lesson 3 — Clinton's new economy — and the culture wars at home

Clinton's new economy presided over a booming, computerising nation — now fighting its culture wars at home: arguments over values, identity and the nation's story replacing the Cold War's single great argument.
