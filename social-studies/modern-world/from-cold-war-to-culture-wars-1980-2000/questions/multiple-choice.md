# From Cold War to culture wars, 1980–2000 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Reagan's revolution —
a) Raised taxes and expanded government  b) Cut taxes and confronted the Soviet Union  c) Ended the presidency
**Answer: b) Cut taxes and confronted the Soviet Union** — Reagan's revolution cuts taxes and confronts the Soviet Union.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Cold War ended during the years —
a) 1945–1950  b) 1980–2000, closing the Soviet standoff  c) 1914–1919
**Answer: b) 1980–2000, closing the Soviet standoff** — the Cold War ends in this period.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** After the Cold War, leaders proclaimed —
a) A return to monarchy  b) A new world order  c) Permanent war
**Answer: b) A new world order** — a new world order is proclaimed.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Under Clinton, the nation's home-front fighting is named —
a) The bank war  b) The culture wars  c) The tariff war
**Answer: b) The culture wars** — a nation now fighting its culture wars at home.
