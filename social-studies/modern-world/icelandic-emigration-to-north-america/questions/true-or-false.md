# Icelandic emigration to North America — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sigurður's family owned a large farm in Skagafjörður.
**Answer: False** — he was born to poor tenant parents.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Many Icelanders faced Sigurður's choice — stay, or emigrate to North America.
**Answer: True** — emigration to Canada is the choice the worksheet weighs.
