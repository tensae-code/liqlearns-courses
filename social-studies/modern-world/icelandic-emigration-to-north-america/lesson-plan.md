# Lesson plan — Icelandic emigration to North America

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Icelandic emigration to North America
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Iceland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Sigurður, born 1853 to poor tenant parents in Skagafjörður, must weigh a hard choice: stay in Iceland, or leave for Canada forever.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
