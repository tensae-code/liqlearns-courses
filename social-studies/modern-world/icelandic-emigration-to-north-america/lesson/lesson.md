# Icelandic emigration to North America

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Sigurður, born 1853 to poor tenant parents in Skagafjörður, must weigh a hard choice: stay in Iceland, or leave for Canada forever. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
