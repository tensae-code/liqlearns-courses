# The Bauhaus School — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Gropius founds the Bauhaus to reunite art and industry; Dessau, Meyer and Mies carry it until the Nazis close it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chapters 18–19: Schools of decorative and applied arts — the Bauhaus

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Ιστορία Διακοσμητικών Τεχνών (History of Decorative Arts), Β' ΕΠΑ.Λ., Applied Arts sector, by Evangelos Georgousopoulos, Eleftherios Dafnos & Ilias Konstantopoulos* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Chapters 18–19: Schools of decorative and applied arts — the Bauhaus

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Bauhaus typography throws away ornament and capitals alike and designs the modern page.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Units 17–18: The Bauhaus school; Bauhaus, graphic design and industrial design

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Ιστορία των Γραφικών Τεχνών (History of the Graphic Arts), Β' ΕΠΑ.Λ., Applied Arts sector, by Manolis Vithynos* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Units 17–18: The Bauhaus school; Bauhaus, graphic design and industrial design
