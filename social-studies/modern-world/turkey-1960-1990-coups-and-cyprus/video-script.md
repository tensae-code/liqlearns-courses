# Video script — Turkey 1960–1990 — coups and Cyprus

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

From the 1960s, Turkey's foreign policy is a story of standing questions: Greece and the Aegean, the Armenian question — and above all Cyprus, whose crisis ends in the intervention of 1974.

At home, soldiers step into politics three times in thirty years. Yet under the crises, the deeper story runs on: villages empty into cities, markets open, a country remade by migration and markets.

Crises above, remaking below — that is Turkey, 1960 to 1990.
