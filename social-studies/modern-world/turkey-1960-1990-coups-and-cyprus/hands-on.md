# Hands-on — Turkey 1960–1990 — coups and Cyprus

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The island on the map

Draw Turkey's southern coast and Cyprus below it. Write one line from Ankara's view and one from Athens' view of the island — then the year, 1974, where the crisis ends.

## Three interruptions

Draw the thirty years as a road of civilian politics, crossed three times by a soldier's boot-print marked 'intervention'. Under the road, draw the other journey: a family's road from village to city, unbroken.

## Remade from below

List three things migration and markets change in a town — its streets, its shops, its songs — and write which of the three you would notice first as a child growing up there.
