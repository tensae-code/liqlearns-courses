# Turkey 1960–1990 — coups and Cyprus — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first standing foreign question named is —
a) Cyprus  b) Korea  c) The Straits
**Answer: a) Cyprus** — the island problem.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cyprus crisis ends in intervention in —
a) 1960  b) 1974  c) 1990
**Answer: b) 1974** — the age's defining year abroad.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Soldiers step into Turkey's politics in these decades —
a) Once  b) Twice  c) Three times
**Answer: c) Three times** — three military interventions.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the crises, the country is remade by —
a) Migration and markets  b) Earthquakes  c) Foreign occupation
**Answer: a) Migration and markets** — the quiet remaking.
