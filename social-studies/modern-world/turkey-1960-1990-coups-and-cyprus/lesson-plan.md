# Lesson plan — Turkey 1960–1990 — coups and Cyprus

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey 1960–1990 — coups and Cyprus
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** three military interventions, one island crisis that ends in 1974, and a country remade by migration and markets.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: three military interventions, one island crisis that ends in 1974, and a country remade by migration and markets. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the standing foreign questions from the 1960s: Cyprus, relations with Greece, the Armenian question.
2. Give the year the Cyprus crisis ends in intervention: 1974.
3. Say how many times soldiers step into politics in these decades: three military interventions.
4. Name the quiet remaking under the crises: migration from villages to cities, and opening markets.
