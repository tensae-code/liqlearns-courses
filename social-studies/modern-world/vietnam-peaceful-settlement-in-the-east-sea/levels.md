# Vietnam Peaceful Settlement In The East Sea — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 13 Vietnam's peaceful-settlement stance: the 1977 declaration on sea zones, white papers of 1979, 1981 and 1988, joining UNCLOS 1982 (a million km² of sea, three times the land), the 2012 Sea Law, and full implementation of the 2002 ASEAN–China Declaration on Conduct signed at Phnom Penh — disputes by peaceful means, without threat or use of force

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 9 Defending sovereignty in the East Sea: the reunified state administers Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa; the 12-5-1977 declaration defines Vietnam's seas, Vietnam ratifies UNCLOS as its 63rd state (1994), island districts are founded (1982) and a Trường Sa town (2007), and sovereignty is exercised through exhibitions, surveys, markers, lighthouses and island communities; when Chinese forces attack Gạc Ma, Cô Lin and Len Đao (March 1988) and dozens of naval soldiers fall, Vietnam protests to the UN and presses negotiation; the 2012 Sea Law gives the defence its legal base, with the DOC pushed forward
