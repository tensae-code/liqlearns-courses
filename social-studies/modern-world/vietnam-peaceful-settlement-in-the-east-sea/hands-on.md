# Hands-on — Vietnam Peaceful Settlement In The East Sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Peaceful Settlement In The East Sea — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 13 Vietnam's peaceful-settlement stance: the 1977 declaration on sea zones, white papers of 1979, 1981 and 1988, joining UNCLOS 1982 (a million km² of sea, three times the land), the 2012 Sea Law, and full implementation of the 2002 ASEAN–China Declaration on Conduct signed at Phnom Penh — disputes by peaceful means, without threat or use of force, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
