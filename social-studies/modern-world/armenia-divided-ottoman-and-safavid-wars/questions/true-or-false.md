# Armenia Divided Ottoman And Safavid Wars — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Two empires fight over Armenia for a century; Shah Abbas deports Julfa's Armenians to Persia, and the land is split east–west.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §4.2 Armenia and the Ottoman–Safavid wars, 1500s–early 1600s
