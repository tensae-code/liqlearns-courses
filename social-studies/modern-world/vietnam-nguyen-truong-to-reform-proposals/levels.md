## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 17, the reform memorials of the later 19th century
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — with the feudal order in crisis and the French at the gates, literati who have seen the West dare to propose remaking the country — Nguyễn Trường Tộ, the Catholic scholar who studied Rome and Paris in 1860, sends Tự Đức fourteen memorials between 1863 and 1871, the Eight Urgent Matters among them, covering the bureaucracy, industry and commerce, finance, the army, diplomacy and education; Trần Đình Túc's group proposes opening the Trà Lý seaport in 1868, the Thương bạc office three ports in 1872, Nguyễn Lộ Trạch his "Matters of the Time" in 1877 and 1882 — almost none of it is enacted, blocked by a missing economic base and the court's conservatism, but the book credits the reform current with preparing the ground for the Duy Tân movement of the next century
