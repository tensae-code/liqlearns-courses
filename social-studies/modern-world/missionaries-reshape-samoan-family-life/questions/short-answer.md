# Missionaries reshape Sāmoan family life — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Part 1, Unit 4 and Part 2, Unit 3, social and cultural effects.
**Answer: missionaries ban polygamy and the polula night dances, push the nuclear family against the aiga, take the priestly role from the matai (the pastor inherits the chiefly address "susuga"), and bring both new diseases and the first modern health care — until the independent nation's motto reads "Sāmoa is founded on God" (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
