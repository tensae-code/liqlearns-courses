# Missionaries reshape Sāmoan family life

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: missionaries ban polygamy and the polula night dances, push the nuclear family against the aiga, take the priestly role from the matai (the pastor inherits the chiefly address "susuga"), and bring both new diseases and the first modern health care — until the independent nation's motto reads "Sāmoa is founded on God". As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
