# Kazakhstan Shakarim And The Historian Poets — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** three scholar-poets write the Kazakh past down — genealogy, chronicle and history — before it can be forgotten.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §60 The historical value of the works of Shakarim Kudaiberdiev, Mashkhur Zhusip Kopeev and Kurbangali Khalidi

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §60 The historical value of the works of Shakarim Kudaiberdiev, Mashkhur Zhusip Kopeev and Kurbangali Khalidi

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** three shezhire-writers are assessed as historians of their own people.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §6.7 What is the historical value of the works of Sh. Kudaiberduly, M. Zh. Kopeiuly and K. Khalid?

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы. 8-сынып [History of Kazakhstan, Grade 8]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §6.7 What is the historical value of the works of Sh. Kudaiberduly, M. Zh. Kopeiuly and K. Khalid?

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** the guide closes by grading the three shezhire-writers as sources.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson plan §60: the historical value of the works of Sh. Kudaiberduly, M. Zh. Kopeiuly and K. Khalid

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы (XVIII–XIX ғғ.): Әдістемелік нұсқау [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th cc.): Methodical Guide for teachers of grades 8/7]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lesson plan §60: the historical value of the works of Sh. Kudaiberduly, M. Zh. Kopeiuly and K. Khalid
