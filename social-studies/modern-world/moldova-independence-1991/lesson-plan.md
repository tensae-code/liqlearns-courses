## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tema 16: Republica Moldova, stat independent și democratic** — 750,000 people at the 1989 Great National Assembly demand Romanian as state language in Latin script, and in 1991 the Republic of Moldova declares independence
2. **Tema 36: RSSM între 1985 și 1991. Proclamarea independenței Republicii Moldova** — Gorbachev's perestroika frees speech; a Transnistrian republic is proclaimed against Chișinău, the Communist Party is suspended, and on 27 August 1991 over 600,000 people at the Great National Assembly demand independence
3. **Tema 46: Proclamarea suveranității și independenței Republicii Moldova. Simbolurile de stat** — in April 1988 the journal Nistru first demands the Latin script back; the Gagauz proclaim their own ASSR in November 1989; the tricolour is adopted on 27 April 1990, the coat of arms on 3 November 1990, and independence follows on 27 August 1991
