# Sāmoa under German and New Zealand rule — and the Mau — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Germany rules Samoa from 1900; New Zealand seizes it in 1914 as Germany loses the First World War; resentment at New Zealand rule forms the Mau in 1926 under Tupua Tamasese at Vaimoso, resisting peacefully by refusing taxes, registrations and government schools, on the road to independence in 1962.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 1, "Office of the Mau, Vaimoso"

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Studies, Year 9 Book Two* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 1, "Office of the Mau, Vaimoso"
