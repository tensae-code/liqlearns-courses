**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unit 1, "Office of the Mau, Vaimoso".
**Answer: Germany rules Samoa from 1900; New Zealand seizes it in 1914 as Germany loses the First World War; resentment at New Zealand rule forms the Mau in 1926 under Tupua Tamasese at Vaimoso, resisting peacefully by refusing taxes, registrations and government schools, on the road to independence in 1962 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
