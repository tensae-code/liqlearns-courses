# Sāmoa under German and New Zealand rule — and the Mau

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Germany rules Samoa from 1900; New Zealand seizes it in 1914 as Germany loses the First World War; resentment at New Zealand rule forms the Mau in 1926 under Tupua Tamasese at Vaimoso, resisting peacefully by refusing taxes, registrations and government schools, on the road to independence in 1962. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
