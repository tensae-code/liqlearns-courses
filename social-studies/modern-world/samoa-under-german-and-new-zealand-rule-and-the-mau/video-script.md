# Video script — Sāmoa under German and New Zealand rule — and the Mau

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Germany rules Samoa from 1900; New Zealand seizes it in 1914 as Germany loses the First World War; resentment at New Zealand rule forms the Mau in 1926 under Tupua Tamasese at Vaimoso, resisting peacefully by refusing taxes, registrations and government schools, on the road to independence in 1962.
