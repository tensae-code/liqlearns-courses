# Hands-on — Sāmoa under German and New Zealand rule — and the Mau

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Sāmoa under German and New Zealand rule — and the Mau — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unit 1, "Office of the Mau, Vaimoso", from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
