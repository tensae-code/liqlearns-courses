# Enlightened Absolutism And The Partitions Of Poland — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Five powers now rule Europe's balance; Joseph II's tolerance and serfdom patents are rolled back by Leopold II, Peter I and Catherine II build Russia's might, and Poland-Lithuania is carved up in three partitions.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lecția 12: Absolutismul luminat. Imperiul Rus. Imperiul Habsburgic

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a VII-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lecția 12: Absolutismul luminat. Imperiul Rus. Imperiul Habsburgic

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Joseph II issues the Patent abolishing serfdom, Catherine II preaches citizens' equality under the same laws, and Peter I's reforms are the case study of modernisation from the throne.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §14: Absolutismul luminat în Europa în secolul al XVIII-lea
