# Britain Loses its Industrial Lead

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Disraeli's reforms (the 54-hour week, no factory children under ten) cannot hide the turn — the "workshop of the world" falls to third in. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Ch. II §11 Britain at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century

Disraeli's reforms (the 54-hour week, no factory children under ten) cannot hide the turn — the "workshop of the world" falls to third in industrial output as its capital flows to the colonies and German and American goods chase British ones out of world markets, London keeping only the money crown
