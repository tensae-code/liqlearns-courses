# Britain Loses its Industrial Lead — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. II §11 Britain at the end of the 19th – beginning of the 20th century.
**Answer: Disraeli's reforms (the 54-hour week, no factory children under ten) cannot hide the turn — the "workshop of the world" falls to third in industrial output as its capital flows to the colonies and German and American goods chase British ones out of world markets, London keeping only the money crown (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
