# Southeast Asia After Independence Asean — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the granary that starved learns to export factories, and eleven former colonies build one common home.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 6 After independence: the colonial legacy (dependent economies, divide-and-rule ethnic wounds, Lee Kuan Yew's "we cannot live on charity") and the climb out — import substitution, then export-led industrialisation, Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia turning to markets in the late 1980s; ASEAN, founded at Bangkok on 8-8-1967, grows to eleven members and the world's fifth economy

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 4 From ASEAN 5 to ASEAN 10: Brunei joins in 1984, Vietnam in 1995, Laos and Myanmar in 1997, Cambodia in 1999; the stages run from trust-building (ZOPFAN 1971), through the 1976 Bali Concord and Treaty of Amity and the 1992 free-trade agreement and 1994 Regional Forum, to organisational completion with the 2007 Charter
