# Hands-on — Southeast Asia After Independence Asean

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Southeast Asia After Independence Asean — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 6 After independence: the colonial legacy (dependent economies, divide-and-rule ethnic wounds, Lee Kuan Yew's "we cannot live on charity") and the climb out — import substitution, then export-led industrialisation, Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia turning to markets in the late 1980s; ASEAN, founded at Bangkok on 8-8-1967, grows to eleven members and the world's fifth economy, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
