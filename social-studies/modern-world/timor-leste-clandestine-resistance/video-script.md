# Video script — Timor-Leste — the clandestine resistance

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Timor-Leste's story as its Grade 6 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The resistance does not live only in the mountains. Inside the towns, secret — clandestine — networks carry letters to Falintil in the bush, and carry food and medicine too. The couriers are often schoolchildren and students: young people moving messages under the occupier's nose, at real risk, as organisers of a hidden war.

The clandestine front reaches inside Indonesia itself. Student networks such as Renetil organise Timorese students studying there, and — the book insists children notice this — Indonesians themselves organise in solidarity groups such as Solidamor, supporting Timor-Leste's cause from inside the occupying country. The enemy, the lesson shows, was a government and an army, never a whole people.
