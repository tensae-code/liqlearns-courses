# Hands-on — Timor-Leste — the clandestine resistance

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## A courier's bag
Draw a school bag. Inside it, draw or list what a clandestine courier might carry for Falintil: a letter, food, medicine. Write one sentence on the risk in carrying it.

## Inside the other country
Draw two joined boxes: 'Renetil — students' and 'Solidamor — Indonesian solidarity'. Write one sentence on what it shows that support existed inside Indonesia itself.
