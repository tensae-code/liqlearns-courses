# Timor-Leste — the clandestine resistance — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Clandestine networks in the towns carried what to Falintil in the bush?
a) Letters, food and medicine  b) Tanks  c) Nothing
**Answer: a) Letters, food and medicine** — Letters, food and medicine — carried secretly under the occupier's nose.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Many of the couriers and organisers were —
a) Schoolchildren and students  b) Foreign soldiers  c) Ships' crews
**Answer: a) Schoolchildren and students** — Schoolchildren and students — young couriers at real risk.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Renetil was —
a) A student network  b) A Portuguese song  c) A cemetery
**Answer: a) A student network** — A student network — organising Timorese students.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Solidamor was —
a) An Indonesian solidarity group supporting Timor-Leste  b) An Indonesian army unit  c) A Timorese village
**Answer: a) An Indonesian solidarity group supporting Timor-Leste** — An Indonesian solidarity group — support from inside Indonesia itself.
