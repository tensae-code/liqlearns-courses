# Timor-Leste — the clandestine resistance — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the clandestine networks do, and who did much of that work?
**Answer: They carried letters, food and medicine to Falintil in the bush and organised resistance in the towns; schoolchildren and students did much of it as couriers and organisers (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two organisations in this lesson and say what each was.
**Answer: Renetil was a student network organising Timorese students; Solidamor was an Indonesian solidarity group supporting Timor-Leste inside Indonesia (any close wording).**
