# Hands-on — Imperial reforms and colonial protests, 1763–1774

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The story on a line
Draw a line. Mark this lesson's parts on it in order: War debt brings the Stamp Act; Boycotts, the Townshend Acts and the Boston Tea Party; The Coercive Acts and the First Continental Congress. Under each mark, write in your own words what happened and what changed.

## Tell it walking
Walk the line slowly. At each mark, stop and say that part of the story out loud, as if you were telling it to a younger learner who has never heard it.

## One hard question
Write the hardest question you can ask about this lesson. Swap with a companion: answer theirs, and let them answer yours — from the lesson.
