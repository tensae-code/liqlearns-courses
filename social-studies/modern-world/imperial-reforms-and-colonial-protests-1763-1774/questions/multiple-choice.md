# Imperial reforms and colonial protests, 1763–1774 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Stamp Act followed from —
a) A colonial request  b) Britain's war debt  c) A French invasion
**Answer: b) Britain's war debt** — Britain's war debt brings the Stamp Act.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Sons and Daughters of Liberty answered taxes with —
a) Boycotts  b) Silence  c) Moving to Britain
**Answer: a) Boycotts** — they answer with boycotts.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Boston Tea Party was part of the protest against —
a) The Townshend-era taxes and tea policy  b) A ban on farming  c) The French
**Answer: a) The Townshend-era taxes and tea policy** — the Townshend Acts and the Boston Tea Party follow in the protest chain.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Coercive Acts were Britain's punishment of —
a) Massachusetts  b) Canada  c) Spain
**Answer: a) Massachusetts** — the Coercive Acts punish Massachusetts.
