# Imperial reforms and colonial protests, 1763–1774 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Put the chain in order: war debt, Stamp Act, boycotts, Tea Party, Coercive Acts, Congress.
**Answer: Britain's war debt → Stamp Act → boycotts by the Sons and Daughters of Liberty → Townshend Acts and Boston Tea Party → Coercive Acts punishing Massachusetts → First Continental Congress (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What did the First Continental Congress discover?
**Answer: An American identity — the colonies acting as one people (any close wording).**
