# Imperial reforms and colonial protests, 1763–1774 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The First Continental Congress helped colonists discover an American identity.
**Answer: True** — In meeting together, the Congress discovers an American identity.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The colonies each protested entirely alone and never acted together.
**Answer: False** — They answered together in the First Continental Congress.
