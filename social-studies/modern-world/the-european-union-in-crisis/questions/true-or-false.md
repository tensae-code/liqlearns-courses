# The European Union In Crisis — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** debt crisis, migration and Brexit (the volume's CLIL closing) test the Union built in the nineties; the chapter asks whether integration can survive its own success.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 6, Cap. 22 §4 The difficult road of the European Union

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 6, Cap. 22 §4 The difficult road of the European Union

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the debt crisis pits North against South inside the euro; the Union built to end crises becomes one.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 19 §4 L'Europa e la crisi del debito

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 19 §4 L'Europa e la crisi del debito
