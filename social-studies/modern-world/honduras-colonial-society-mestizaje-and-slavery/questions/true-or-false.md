# Honduras Colonial Society Mestizaje And Slavery — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Three centuries of colony sort people by skin — peninsulares on top, criollos without political power, Indigenous as the labour base, mestizos born of the mixing, Black Africans brought for the heaviest work — a new world built from the three peoples.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 24 Vida social y cultural en la colonia: las clases

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 24 Vida social y cultural en la colonia: las clases

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Church teaches reading, Spanish and submission; Indigenous and African women are subjected and the mestizo people is born; epidemics the natives cannot resist (smallpox, measles, plague) shrink the population, so from 1545 Black slaves are imported for the mines.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 24: mujeres, Iglesia y enfermedad

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 24: mujeres, Iglesia y enfermedad

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Conquest and colonisation cost some 90% of the Indigenous population (smallpox, measles, flu, tuberculosis, harsh treatment, slave traffic, mestizaje); the 1778 census counts 811,675 in the Kingdom of Guatemala, and lost labour feeds the African slave traffic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lección 25: el impacto demográfico

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lección 25: el impacto demográfico

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The Audiencia de los Confines (1542, first seated at Gracias) governed Honduras, moving to Guatemala in 1566; the Alcaldía Mayor de Tegucigalpa (1578) was created to control mining, and from 1609 Honduras joined the Captaincy General of Guatemala.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in colonial government of Honduras

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
