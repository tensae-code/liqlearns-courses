# Honduras Colonial Society Mestizaje And Slavery — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 24 Vida social y cultural en la colonia: las clases.
**Answer: three centuries of colony sort people by skin — peninsulares on top, criollos without political power, Indigenous as the labour base, mestizos born of the mixing, Black Africans brought for the heaviest work — a new world built from the three peoples (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* (Honduras), Lección 24 Vida social y cultural en la colonia: las clases, printed p. 110.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 24: mujeres, Iglesia y enfermedad.
**Answer: the Church teaches reading, Spanish and submission; Indigenous and African women are subjected and the mestizo people is born; epidemics the natives cannot resist (smallpox, measles, plague) shrink the population, so from 1545 Black slaves are imported for the mines (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Tercer grado* (Honduras), Lección 24: mujeres, Iglesia y enfermedad, printed p. 112.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: Lección 25: el impacto demográfico.
**Answer: conquest and colonisation cost some 90% of the Indigenous population (smallpox, measles, flu, tuberculosis, harsh treatment, slave traffic, mestizaje); the 1778 census counts 811,675 in the Kingdom of Guatemala, and lost labour feeds the African slave traffic (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales, Cuarto grado* (Honduras), Lección 25: el impacto demográfico, printed p. 140.**

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Retell this part in your own words: colonial government of Honduras.
**Answer: the Audiencia de los Confines (1542, first seated at Gracias) governed Honduras, moving to Guatemala in 1566; the Alcaldía Mayor de Tegucigalpa (1578) was created to control mining, and from 1609 Honduras joined the Captaincy General of Guatemala (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Séptimo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), colonial government of Honduras, printed p. 156.**
