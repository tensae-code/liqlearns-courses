# Honduras Colonial Society Mestizaje And Slavery — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Lección 24 Vida social y cultural en la colonia: las clases
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — three centuries of colony sort people by skin — peninsulares on top, criollos without political power, Indigenous as the labour base, mestizos born of the mixing, Black Africans brought for the heaviest work — a new world built from the three peoples

## Sub lesson 2 — Lección 24: mujeres, Iglesia y enfermedad
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Church teaches reading, Spanish and submission; Indigenous and African women are subjected and the mestizo people is born; epidemics the natives cannot resist (smallpox, measles, plague) shrink the population, so from 1545 Black slaves are imported for the mines

## Sub lesson 3 — Lección 25: el impacto demográfico
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — conquest and colonisation cost some 90% of the Indigenous population (smallpox, measles, flu, tuberculosis, harsh treatment, slave traffic, mestizaje); the 1778 census counts 811,675 in the Kingdom of Guatemala, and lost labour feeds the African slave traffic

## Sub lesson 4 — colonial government of Honduras
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Audiencia de los Confines (1542, first seated at Gracias) governed Honduras, moving to Guatemala in 1566; the Alcaldía Mayor de Tegucigalpa (1578) was created to control mining, and from 1609 Honduras joined the Captaincy General of Guatemala
