# Video script — Migration Diasporas And The Making Of A Global Culture

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as North Macedonia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

famine, poverty and persecution push millions to America, Canada, Argentina and Australia; a Danish emigrant's recorded voice remembers that the hardest thing was not understanding a single word; empires meanwhile ship Indians to Caribbean plantations and Chinese labour across Asia — and the mixing brews new cultures: jazz, Haitian Creole, Rio's carnival, the Day of the Dead.
