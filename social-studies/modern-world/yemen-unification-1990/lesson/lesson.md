# Yemeni unity

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: a people the book calls one land and one nation, repeatedly targeted by colonisers trying to split north from south, finally proclaim the Republic of Yemen together on Thursday 22 May 1990. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
