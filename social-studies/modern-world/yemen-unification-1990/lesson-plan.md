**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Yemeni unity** — a people the book calls one land and one nation, repeatedly targeted by colonisers trying to split north from south, finally proclaim the Republic of Yemen together on Thursday 22 May 1990, the national day Yemenis celebrate each year
2. **Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5)** — one land and one people, divided only by Britain's occupation of the south, walk back together summit by summit — Cairo 1972, Tripoli 1972, Kuwait 1979, Aden 1980 and the crowning Aden agreement of 30 November 1989 — until the Republic of Yemen is proclaimed on 22 May 1990; when secession is declared in 1994 the people themselves defend the unity and the war stops on 7 July 1994
