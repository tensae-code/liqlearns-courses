## Sub lesson 2 — Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5)
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — one land and one people, divided only by Britain's occupation of the south, walk back together summit by summit — Cairo 1972, Tripoli 1972, Kuwait 1979, Aden 1980 and the crowning Aden agreement of 30 November 1989 — until the Republic of Yemen is proclaimed on 22 May 1990; when secession is declared in 1994 the people themselves defend the unity and the war stops on 7 July 1994
