# Yemeni unity — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Yemeni unity.
**Answer: a people the book calls one land and one nation, repeatedly targeted by colonisers trying to split north from south, finally proclaim the Republic of Yemen together on Thursday 22 May 1990, the national day Yemenis celebrate each year (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التربية الاجتماعية للصف الرابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (Social Education for Grade 4, Basic Education)* (Yemen), Yemeni unity, printed p. 71.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5).
**Answer: one land and one people, divided only by Britain's occupation of the south, walk back together summit by summit — Cairo 1972, Tripoli 1972, Kuwait 1979, Aden 1980 and the crowning Aden agreement of 30 November 1989 — until the Republic of Yemen is proclaimed on 22 May 1990; when secession is declared in 1994 the people themselves defend the unity and the war stops on 7 July 1994 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Yemen's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *تاريخ العالم الحديث والمعاصر — تاريخ للصف الثالث الثانوي (History: Modern and Contemporary History of the World, for the Third Secondary Grade)* (Yemen), Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5), printed p. 89.**
