# Yemeni unity — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a people the book calls one land and one nation, repeatedly targeted by colonisers trying to split north from south, finally proclaim the Republic of Yemen together on Thursday 22 May 1990, the national day Yemenis celebrate each year.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Yemeni unity

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التربية الاجتماعية للصف الرابع من مرحلة التعليم الأساسي (Social Education for Grade 4, Basic Education)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Yemeni unity

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** one land and one people, divided only by Britain's occupation of the south, walk back together summit by summit — Cairo 1972, Tripoli 1972, Kuwait 1979, Aden 1980 and the crowning Aden agreement of 30 November 1989 — until the Republic of Yemen is proclaimed on 22 May 1990; when secession is declared in 1994 the people themselves defend the unity and the war stops on 7 July 1994.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *تاريخ العالم الحديث والمعاصر — تاريخ للصف الثالث الثانوي (History: Modern and Contemporary History of the World, for the Third Secondary Grade)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Yemeni unity (Unit 3 Lesson 5)
