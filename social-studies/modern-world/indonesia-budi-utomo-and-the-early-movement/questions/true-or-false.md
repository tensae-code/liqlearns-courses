# Indonesia — Budi Utomo and the early national movement — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** STOVIA students found Budi Utomo, Sarekat Islam masses gather at Kaliwungu, and Tjipto, Douwes Dekker and Suwardi launch the Indische Partij — organisation replaces rebellion as the weapon.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab II.B Kemunculan Embrio Kebangsaan dan Nasionalisme Indonesia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — the Spanish flu of 1918 in the Indies.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab II.B Kemunculan Embrio Kebangsaan dan Nasionalisme Indonesia

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The movement organises in every key — early associations, religious organisations, youth and women's organisations, labour unions, the PKI, and Taman Siswa's schools.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 3.B Perjuangan Organisasi Pergerakan Kebangsaan

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — the Spanish flu of 1918 in the Indies.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 3.B Perjuangan Organisasi Pergerakan Kebangsaan
