# Maysalun and the Great Syrian Revolt — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Maysalun — Yusuf al-Azma and the fall of the Arab government.
**Answer: The Syrian Congress rejected the Faisal–Clemenceau agreement and San Remo put Syria under French mandate; General Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demanded the army's dissolution, and though Faisal accepted, France marched; War Minister Yusuf al-Azma led the volunteers to Maysalun and was martyred there on 24 July 1920, and the French entered Damascus and ended the Arab government. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Syria's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ — الصف التاسع (History, Grade 9)* (Grade 9), Unit 1, Lesson فرنسا تحتل سورية وتنهي الحكم العربي فيها (France occupies Syria and ends Arab rule), printed p. 20.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: The Great Syrian Revolt, 1925–1927.
**Answer: France split Syria into small statelets and looted it, and the revolts answered — Saleh al-Ali on the coast, Ibrahim Hananu in the north, Hawran in 1920, the Golan ambush of Gouraud's convoy in 1921 — until the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925–1927 under Sultan al-Atrash: al-Kafr, the siege of Suwayda, al-Mazraa where the French lost thousands, then the Ghouta and Damascus itself, which France bombarded and burned (the al-Hariqa quarter). (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Syria's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *التاريخ — الصف التاسع (History, Grade 9)* (Grade 9), Unit 1, Lesson الشعب السوري يواجه الاحتلال الفرنسي بالثورات (The Syrian people confront the French occupation with revolts), printed pp. 22–24.**
