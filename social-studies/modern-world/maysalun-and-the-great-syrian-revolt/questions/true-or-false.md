# Maysalun and the Great Syrian Revolt — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Syrian Congress rejected the Faisal–Clemenceau agreement and San Remo put Syria under French mandate; General Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demanded the army's dissolution, and though Faisal accepted, France marched; War Minister Yusuf al-Azma led the volunteers to Maysalun and was martyred there on 24 July 1920, and the French entered Damascus and ended the Arab government.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 1, Lesson فرنسا تحتل سورية وتنهي الحكم العربي فيها (France occupies Syria and ends Arab rule)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف التاسع (History, Grade 9)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Maysalun — Yusuf al-Azma and the fall of the Arab government

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** France split Syria into small statelets and looted it, and the revolts answered — Saleh al-Ali on the coast, Ibrahim Hananu in the north, Hawran in 1920, the Golan ambush of Gouraud's convoy in 1921 — until the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925–1927 under Sultan al-Atrash: al-Kafr, the siege of Suwayda, al-Mazraa where the French lost thousands, then the Ghouta and Damascus itself, which France bombarded and burned (the al-Hariqa quarter).
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 1, Lesson الشعب السوري يواجه الاحتلال الفرنسي بالثورات (The Syrian people confront the French occupation with revolts)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف التاسع (History, Grade 9)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Great Syrian Revolt, 1925–1927
