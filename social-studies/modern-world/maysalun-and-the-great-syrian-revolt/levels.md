# Maysalun and the Great Syrian Revolt — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Maysalun — Yusuf al-Azma and the fall of the Arab government
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The Syrian Congress rejected the Faisal–Clemenceau agreement and San Remo put Syria under French mandate; General Gouraud's ultimatum of 14 July 1920 demanded the army's dissolution, and though Faisal accepted, France marched; War Minister Yusuf al-Azma led the volunteers to Maysalun and was martyred there on 24 July 1920, and the French entered Damascus and ended the Arab government.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Great Syrian Revolt, 1925–1927
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — France split Syria into small statelets and looted it, and the revolts answered — Saleh al-Ali on the coast, Ibrahim Hananu in the north, Hawran in 1920, the Golan ambush of Gouraud's convoy in 1921 — until the Great Syrian Revolt of 1925–1927 under Sultan al-Atrash: al-Kafr, the siege of Suwayda, al-Mazraa where the French lost thousands, then the Ghouta and Damascus itself, which France bombarded and burned (the al-Hariqa quarter).
