# Nazi persecution of minorities — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — 13.1 Nazi racial beliefs
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — A ladder of races with 'Aryans' on top and Jews at the bottom turns prejudice into state doctrine and marks whole peoples for exclusion

## Sub lesson 2 — 13.2 The persecution of the Jews
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Boycott (1933), the Nuremberg Laws stripping citizenship (1935) and Kristallnacht's smashed shops and synagogues (1938) drive Jews from German life step by legal step

## Sub lesson 3 — 13.3 The treatment of minorities
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Roma, gay men, disabled people and the 'asocial' are registered, sterilised, camped or killed as 'burdens on the community' unfit for the Volksgemeinschaft
