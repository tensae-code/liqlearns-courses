# Lesson plan — Nazi persecution of minorities

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Nazi persecution of minorities
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (United Kingdom, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Nazi racial beliefs turn into the persecution of Jews and other minorities, step by legal step.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **13.1 Nazi racial beliefs** — A ladder of races with 'Aryans' on top and Jews at the bottom turns prejudice into state doctrine and marks whole peoples for exclusion
2. **13.2 The persecution of the Jews** — Boycott (1933), the Nuremberg Laws stripping citizenship (1935) and Kristallnacht's smashed shops and synagogues (1938) drive Jews from German life step by legal step
3. **13.3 The treatment of minorities** — Roma, gay men, disabled people and the 'asocial' are registered, sterilised, camped or killed as 'burdens on the community' unfit for the Volksgemeinschaft
