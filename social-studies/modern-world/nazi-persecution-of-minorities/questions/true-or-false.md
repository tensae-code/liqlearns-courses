# Nazi persecution of minorities — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A ladder of races with 'Aryans' on top and Jews at the bottom turns prejudice into state doctrine and marks whole peoples for exclusion.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 13.1 Nazi racial beliefs

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *GCSE History for Edexcel: Weimar and Nazi Germany, 1918–39* is about 13.2 The persecution of the Jews.
**Answer: False** — it is about 13.1 Nazi racial beliefs

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Boycott (1933), the Nuremberg Laws stripping citizenship (1935) and Kristallnacht's smashed shops and synagogues (1938) drive Jews from German life step by legal step.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 13.2 The persecution of the Jews

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *GCSE History for Edexcel: Weimar and Nazi Germany, 1918–39* is about 13.1 Nazi racial beliefs.
**Answer: False** — it is about 13.2 The persecution of the Jews

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Roma, gay men, disabled people and the 'asocial' are registered, sterilised, camped or killed as 'burdens on the community' unfit for the Volksgemeinschaft.
