# Nazi persecution of minorities — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 13.1 Nazi racial beliefs.
**Answer: A ladder of races with 'Aryans' on top and Jews at the bottom turns prejudice into state doctrine and marks whole peoples for exclusion. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the United Kingdom's book, where is this part taught?

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: 13.2 The persecution of the Jews.
**Answer: Boycott (1933), the Nuremberg Laws stripping citizenship (1935) and Kristallnacht's smashed shops and synagogues (1938) drive Jews from German life step by legal step. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the United Kingdom's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *GCSE History for Edexcel: Weimar and Nazi Germany, 1918–39* (Grade 10), 13.2 The persecution of the Jews, printed p. 234.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Retell this part in your own words: 13.3 The treatment of minorities.
**Answer: Roma, gay men, disabled people and the 'asocial' are registered, sterilised, camped or killed as 'burdens on the community' unfit for the Volksgemeinschaft. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
