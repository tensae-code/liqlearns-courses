# Nazi persecution of minorities

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Nazi racial beliefs turn into the persecution of Jews and other minorities, step by legal step. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 13.1 Nazi racial beliefs

A ladder of races with 'Aryans' on top and Jews at the bottom turns prejudice into state doctrine and marks whole peoples for exclusion.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — 13.2 The persecution of the Jews

Boycott (1933), the Nuremberg Laws stripping citizenship (1935) and Kristallnacht's smashed shops and synagogues (1938) drive Jews from German life step by legal step.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

[Picture 41077 — lawbylawout]

## Sub lesson 3 — 13.3 The treatment of minorities

Roma, gay men, disabled people and the 'asocial' are registered, sterilised, camped or killed as 'burdens on the community' unfit for the Volksgemeinschaft.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: 13.1 Nazi racial beliefs; 13.2 The persecution of the Jews; 13.3 The treatment of minorities. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
