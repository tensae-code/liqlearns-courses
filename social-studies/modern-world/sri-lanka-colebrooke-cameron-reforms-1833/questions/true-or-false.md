# Sri Lanka — The Colebrooke–Cameron reforms, 1833 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A costly colony is investigated in 1833, the coast and Kandy are united under one administration, rajakariya service is abolished, and Legislative and Executive Councils open the road to self-rule.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Colebrooke–Cameron reforms

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G9 History Tamil 2019* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Colebrooke–Cameron reforms

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Groups complete one great table — year, reform, legislature, members, changes — marching from Colebrooke 1833 through Donoughmore to Soulbury, so parliament is seen as a gradual evolution.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Constitutional reforms and the independence movement (competency level 5.1)
