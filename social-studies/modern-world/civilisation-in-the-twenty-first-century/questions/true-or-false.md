# Civilisation In The Twenty First Century — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The course ends by looking forward — what the pupils' own century may become is left as their question to answer.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §22 Prospects for civilisation's development in the 2000s

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. **Answer: False** — it is about §22 Prospects for civilisation's development in the 2000s

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
