# Hands-on — Vietnam Hoang Sa Flotilla And The Khao Le Rite

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Hoang Sa Flotilla And The Khao Le Rite — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 17, the Khao lề thế lính Hoàng Sa rite of Lý Sơn island, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
