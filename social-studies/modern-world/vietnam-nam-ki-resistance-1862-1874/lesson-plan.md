# Lesson plan — Vietnam Nam Ki Resistance 1862 1874

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Nam Ki Resistance 1862 1874
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** while Huế suppresses revolts elsewhere, France seizes the three western provinces in 1867 and the southern resistance only grows; Trương Định (1820–1864), acclaimed by the people as Bình Tây Đại Nguyên soái — Grand Marshal of the Western Pacification — fights from Gò Công and Tân Phước until, wounded in a surprise attack sprung by a traitor, he draws his sword and kills himself rather than fall into enemy hands, and his son Trương Quyền fights on from Tây Ninh; Nguyễn Trung Trực (1838–1868) raids the Kiên Giang fort from across the sea and, led to execution, declares: "Until the Westerners have pulled up all the grass of the South, there will still be Southerners to fight the Westerners"; the blind poet Nguyễn Đình Chiểu and Phan Văn Trị scourge the invaders and their collaborators in verse, and Nguyễn Hữu Huân, captured twice, composes defiant poetry on his way to the scaffold
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
