# Guatemala — living traditions: cofradías, dances and festivals — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cofradía is —
a) a colonial brotherhood keeping saints' feasts alive  b) a kind of tax  c) a Spanish fort
**Answer: a) a colonial brotherhood keeping saints' feasts alive** — the brotherhood the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The corrido is —
a) a sung ballad carrying news and memory  b) a bullfight only  c) a printed newspaper
**Answer: a) a sung ballad carrying news and memory** — the song form.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The mengalas are linked in the book to —
a) Amatitlán  b) Tikal  c) Belize
**Answer: a) Amatitlán** — their town.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** In this lesson, each people turns its history into —
a) dance, costume and song  b) printed textbooks only  c) stone pyramids
**Answer: a) dance, costume and song** — living tradition.
