## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: On 22 February 1958 Syria unites with Egypt as the United Arab Republic — until the secession of 28 September 1961 ends the Arab world's boldest unity experiment. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Pan-Arab unity, 1958 — the United Arab Republic

After the coups of 1949 and the Cold War pull of the two camps, Syria united with Egypt: on 22 February 1958 President Quwatli stepped aside for Jamal Abd al-Nasser and the United Arab Republic was born, until the secession of 28 September 1961.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
