# Lesson plan — Tunisia — from protectorate to independence, 1956

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Tunisia — from protectorate to independence, 1956
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Syria, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From Khayr al-Din's reforms through the protectorate of 1881 and the party struggle to independence in 1956 — and on to the revolution of 2011.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Tunisia — from protectorate to independence, 1956** — Khayr al-Din's reforms and the constitution of 1861, Ali ibn Ghadhahum's revolt of 1864 and the European debt commission of 1869 prepared the way; France invaded in 1881 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Bardo, and the Kairouan revolt answered; parties carried the struggle until the armed rising of 1954, fired by Algeria's revolution, won independence in 1956 and the evacuation of the Bizerte base; Bourguiba's republic of 1957, Ben Ali, and the 2011 revolution close the lesson.
