## Sub lesson 1 — Tunisia — from protectorate to independence, 1956
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Khayr al-Din's reforms and the constitution of 1861, Ali ibn Ghadhahum's revolt of 1864 and the European debt commission of 1869 prepared the way; France invaded in 1881 and imposed the protectorate by the Treaty of Bardo, and the Kairouan revolt answered; parties carried the struggle until the armed rising of 1954, fired by Algeria's revolution, won independence in 1956 and the evacuation of the Bizerte base; Bourguiba's republic of 1957, Ben Ali, and the 2011 revolution close the lesson.
