## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lección 27 La Reforma Liberal y la inversión extranjera** — Marco Aurelio Soto and Ramón Rosa (1876–1883) set out to modernise the colonial inheritance — secular free compulsory education, church separated from State, land and tax measures for farming, foreign capital invited in
2. **Lección 27: logros y enclaves** — the Rosario Mining Company opens San Juancito (the mining enclave) and banana enclaves follow; new codes, the southern road, telegraph, National Post, National Library and Archive, La Gaceta and the repaired National Railway mark the reform's reach
3. **Lección 28: logros en Honduras** — Honduras's reform yields harmonised codes, secular education, trained teachers, schools and libraries — the National Library Juan Ramón Molina founded 27 August 1880 under Soto
4. **Soto and Rosa build the modern state** — from Isla del Tigre in 1876, Marco Aurelio Soto and Ramón Rosa decreed free, secular, compulsory primary education, separated church and state and founded the national archive — but concessions of free land, timber and tax exemptions handed the economy to foreign capital and the enclave
