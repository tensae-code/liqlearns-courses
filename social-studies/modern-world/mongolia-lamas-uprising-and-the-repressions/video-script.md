in 1932 monks and herders rise against collectivisation and monastery closures across the western aimags; the rising is crushed with Soviet help. the 1937–39 terror executes tens of thousands — lamas, nobles, Buryats, intellectuals — a wound the book names plainly as the black page of the national story. Khövsgöl's Rashaant monastery rises; the revolt spreads across the west, tens of thousands flee toward Xinjiang, and Soviet armour helps crush it — civil war in all but name. the 1937-39 terror — over 20,000 executed, some 17,000 lamas killed, 700+ monasteries destroyed, and the nation's leaders (Amar, Genden, Dogsom, Losol) shot after Soviet-run trials.
