# Women through history — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Evolución femenina a través de las eras  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Evolución femenina a través de las eras** — women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña, and Rigoberta Menchú fight across eras for education, land and equality

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Sección 1 Tema 4: Evolución femenina a través de las eras  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Sección 1 Tema 4: Evolución femenina a través de las eras** — the book's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Tránsito Amaguaña  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Tránsito Amaguaña** — born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school as an Indigenous child, Tránsito Amaguaña rebels, founds Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Dolores Cacuango, and fights for agrarian reform and communal lands into old age

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book this part is —
a) Unidad 2, Cultura del Buen Vivir: Tránsito Amaguaña  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Unidad 2, Cultura del Buen Vivir: Tránsito Amaguaña** — the book's own heading for this part.
