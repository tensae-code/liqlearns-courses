# Lesson plan — Women through history

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Women through history
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Ecuador, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Women fight across the eras for education, land and equality — Greek and Roman women, Ecuador's Indigenous leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña and Rigoberta Menchú in the Grade 11 book, and Tránsito Amaguaña's own life, from huasipungo labour in Pesillo to founding Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools, in the Grade 3 book
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Evolución femenina a través de las eras** — women of Greece and Rome, Indigenous Ecuadorian leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña, and Rigoberta Menchú fight across eras for education, land and equality (Sección 1 Tema 4: Evolución femenina a través de las eras)
2. **Tránsito Amaguaña** — born to huasipungo labourers in Pesillo, humiliated out of school as an Indigenous child, Tránsito Amaguaña rebels, founds Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools with Dolores Cacuango, and fights for agrarian reform and communal lands into old age (Unidad 2, Cultura del Buen Vivir: Tránsito Amaguaña)

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
