# Video script — Women through history

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Ecuador book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Women fight across the eras for education, land and equality — Greek and Roman women, Ecuador's Indigenous leaders Dolores Cacuango and Tránsito Amaguaña and Rigoberta Menchú in the Grade 11 book, and Tránsito Amaguaña's own life, from huasipungo labour in Pesillo to founding Ecuador's first Indigenous organisations and bilingual schools, in the Grade 3 book
