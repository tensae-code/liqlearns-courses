# Hands-on — Bourgeois Revolutions Premises And Tasks

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bourgeois Revolutions Premises And Tasks — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 1 What a bourgeois revolution needs and does: capitalism growing inside feudalism (England's coal at four-fifths of Europe's output before 1640; America's cotton and tobacco chained to England — Hồ Chí Minh's "why did America make revolution?"), absolutist kings, a rich bourgeoisie without power, and Enlightenment ideas from Montesquieu, Voltaire and Rousseau; the twin tasks are national unity and democracy, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
