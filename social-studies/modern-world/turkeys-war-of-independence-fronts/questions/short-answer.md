# Turkey's War of Independence — the fronts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three fronts and one mark of each.
**Answer: East — Kars; South — the city defences (Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana); West — İnönü, Sakarya and the Great Offensive (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What were the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies?
**Answer: National obligations: levies that took a recorded share of every home's goods — food, cloth, animals, carts — for the army (any close wording).**
