# Turkey's War of Independence — the fronts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Eastern Front closed with —
a) Kars secured  b) Antep defended  c) Sakarya held
**Answer: a) Kars secured** — the east settled.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Southern Front is famous for —
a) Sea battles  b) Cities defending themselves — Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana  c) A single great field battle
**Answer: b) Cities defending themselves — Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana** — towns fighting as towns.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Tekâlif-i Milliye were —
a) Foreign loans  b) Peace terms  c) Levies taking a share of every home's goods for the army
**Answer: c) Levies taking a share of every home's goods for the army** — the nation feeding its army.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The Commander-in-Chief's battle fell on —
a) 30 August 1922  b) 29 October 1923  c) 23 April 1920
**Answer: a) 30 August 1922** — the Great Offensive's decisive day.
