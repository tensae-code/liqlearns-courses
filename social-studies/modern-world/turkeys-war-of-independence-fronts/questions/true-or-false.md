# Turkey's War of Independence — the fronts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Sakarya was the Western Front's turning stand.
**Answer: True** — the stand on the river before the offensive.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The regular army was complete and well supplied from the war's first day.
**Answer: False** — the book's beat is one regular army built from nothing, fed by levies.
