# Hands-on — Turkey's War of Independence — the fronts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Three fronts, one map

On a rough map of Anatolia, mark the three fronts in three colours: Kars in the east, the four southern cities, and the western line from İnönü to Sakarya. Arrows show which way each front moves.

## The levy list

Write a Tekâlif-i Milliye levy notice for one village: what share of cloth, food, animals and carts the army asks, and the promise that it is recorded. Then write one line from a grandmother who gives her cart.

## A fortnight that ends a war

Draw a 14-day strip for the Great Offensive: day 1 the guns open, 30 August the decisive battle, the last day the advance halts at the sea. Under it, write why speed mattered.
