# Video script — Turkey's War of Independence — the fronts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The War of Independence is fought on three fronts. In the east, Kars is secured. In the south, the cities defend themselves — Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana, fighting as towns.

The decisive war is in the west: İnönü stops the advance, Sakarya is the turning stand, and the nation feeds its army through the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies — a share of every home's goods.

Then the Great Offensive: 30 August 1922, the Commander-in-Chief's battle — and a hundred-mile offensive ends the war in a fortnight.
