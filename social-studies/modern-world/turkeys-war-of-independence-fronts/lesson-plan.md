# Lesson plan — Turkey's War of Independence — the fronts

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey's War of Independence — the fronts
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 2 (fronts part): the Eastern Front and Kars; the Southern Front's city defences (Maraş, Urfa, Antep, Adana); the Western Front — the İnönü battles, Kütahya-Eskişehir, Sakarya, the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies, the Great Offensive and the Commander-in-Chief's battle of 30 August 1922.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: three fronts, one regular army built from nothing, and a hundred-mile offensive that ends the war in a fortnight. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the three fronts: Eastern, Southern and Western.
2. Name the Southern Front's defending cities: Maraş, Urfa, Antep and Adana.
3. Tell the Western Front's order: İnönü; Kütahya-Eskişehir; Sakarya; the Great Offensive and 30 August 1922.
4. Say what the Tekâlif-i Milliye levies were: national obligations — a share of every home's goods taken for the army.
