# Poland's Baroque — churches and Sarmatian culture — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Sarmatian culture  b) Churches as fortresses of faith  c) A face found nowhere else
**Answer: b) Churches as fortresses of faith** — The Commonwealth covers itself in Baroque churches built as fortresses of faith through wars with Sweden and the Ottomans.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Sarmatian culture  b) Churches as fortresses of faith  c) A face found nowhere else
**Answer: a) Sarmatian culture** — The szlachta's Sarmatian culture shows in coffin portraits, oriental dress and country manors.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) Churches as fortresses of faith  b) Sarmatian culture  c) A face found nowhere else
**Answer: c) A face found nowhere else** — Together, church and Sarmatian culture give Polish Baroque a face found nowhere else.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) The Commonwealth covers itself in Baroque churches built as fortresses of faith through wars with Sweden and the Ottomans, while the szlachta's Sarmatian culture — coffin portraits, oriental dress, country manors — gives Polish Baroque a face found nowhere else  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
