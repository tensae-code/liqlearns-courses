# Poland's Baroque — churches and Sarmatian culture — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The szlachta's Sarmatian culture shows in coffin portraits, oriental dress and country manors.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — The Commonwealth covers itself in Baroque churches built as fortresses of faith through wars with Sweden and the Ottomans, while the szlachta's Sarmatian culture — coffin portraits, oriental dress, country manors — gives Polish Baroque a face found nowhere else.
