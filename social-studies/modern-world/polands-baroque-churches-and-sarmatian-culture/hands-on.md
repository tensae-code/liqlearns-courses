# Hands-on — Poland's Baroque — churches and Sarmatian culture

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Draw the story
Draw a Polish Baroque church and a szlachta coffin portrait in oriental dress from the picture slot in the lesson. Under your drawing, write in one sentence what changed in this story.

## Three beats on a line
Draw a line. Mark the three parts of the story in order: Churches as fortresses of faith; Sarmatian culture; A face found nowhere else. Under each, write what happened, in your own words.

## Say it standing up
Stand up and tell the story to someone in the room in under a minute — beginning, middle, end — without looking at the lesson.
