# Lesson plan — Poland's Baroque — churches and Sarmatian culture

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Poland's Baroque — churches and Sarmatian culture
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Poland, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The Commonwealth covers itself in Baroque churches built as fortresses of faith through wars with Sweden and the Ottomans, while the szlachta's Sarmatian culture — coffin portraits, oriental dress, country manors — gives Polish Baroque a face found nowhere else.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself — people, places, what happened, what changed. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio.
