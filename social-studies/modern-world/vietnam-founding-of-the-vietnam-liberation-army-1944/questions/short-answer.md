# Vietnam Founding Of The Vietnam Liberation Army 1944 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 14, story of the founding of the Vietnam Propaganda Liberation Army.
**Answer: on 22 December 1944, in a Cao Bằng forest, Võ Nguyên Giáp proclaims a unit of 34 soldiers under the rule "politics is weightier than military matters" — the seed of the People's Army (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 5 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 14, story of the founding of the Vietnam Propaganda Liberation Army, printed p. 62.**
