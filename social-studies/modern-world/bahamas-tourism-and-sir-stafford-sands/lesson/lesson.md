# Bahamas — tourism and Sir Stafford Sands

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Sir Stafford Sands, the 'Father of Tourism', turns The Bahamas' winter-season visiting into a year-round industry in the 1950s — Freeport, air conditioning, advertising, tax incentives, better air travel and legalised casinos — until tourism is the islands' number one industry. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tourism: Sir Stafford Sands builds a year-round industry

rich winter residents once came only for the season; in the 1950s Sir Stafford Sands, "Father of Tourism", heads the Development Board, develops Freeport with Wallace Groves, pushes air conditioning, advertising, tax incentives, better air travel and legalised casinos, and by 1969–1979 research and training make tourism the number one industry (Tourism: Sir Stafford Sands builds a year-round industry)

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this lesson's story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Tourism: from invalids to a year-round industry

the first tourists are invalids seeking health; the Royal Victoria Hotel (1861) and Colonial Hotel (1900) draw rich winter visitors; bootlegging customs money dredges the harbour and brings water and electricity — for tourists, not ordinary Bahamians; in the 1950s Sir Stafford Sands makes tourism a year-round business (Tourism: from invalids to a year-round industry)
