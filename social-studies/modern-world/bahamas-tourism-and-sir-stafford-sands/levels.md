# Bahamas — tourism and Sir Stafford Sands — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Tourism: Sir Stafford Sands builds a year-round industry
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: rich winter residents once came only for the season; in the 1950s Sir Stafford Sands, "Father of Tourism", heads the Development Board, develops Freeport with Wallace Groves, pushes air conditioning, advertising, tax incentives, better air travel and legalised casinos, and by 1969–1979 research and training make tourism the number one industry

## Sub lesson 2 — Tourism: from invalids to a year-round industry
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the first tourists are invalids seeking health; the Royal Victoria Hotel (1861) and Colonial Hotel (1900) draw rich winter visitors; bootlegging customs money dredges the harbour and brings water and electricity — for tourists, not ordinary Bahamians; in the 1950s Sir Stafford Sands makes tourism a year-round business
