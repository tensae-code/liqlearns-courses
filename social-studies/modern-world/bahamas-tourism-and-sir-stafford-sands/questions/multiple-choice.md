# Bahamas — tourism and Sir Stafford Sands — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Tourism: Sir Stafford Sands builds a year-round industry  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Tourism: Sir Stafford Sands builds a year-round industry** — rich winter residents once came only for the season; in the 1950s Sir Stafford Sands, "Father of Tourism", heads the Development Board, develops Freeport with Wallace Groves, pushes air conditioning, advertising, tax incentives, better air travel and legalised casinos, and by 1969–1979 research and training make tourism the number one industry

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Tourism: Sir Stafford Sands builds a year-round industry  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Tourism: Sir Stafford Sands builds a year-round industry** — the book's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Tourism: from invalids to a year-round industry  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Tourism: from invalids to a year-round industry** — the first tourists are invalids seeking health; the Royal Victoria Hotel (1861) and Colonial Hotel (1900) draw rich winter visitors; bootlegging customs money dredges the harbour and brings water and electricity — for tourists, not ordinary Bahamians; in the 1950s Sir Stafford Sands makes tourism a year-round business
