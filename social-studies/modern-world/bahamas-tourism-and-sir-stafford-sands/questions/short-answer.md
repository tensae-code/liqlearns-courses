# Bahamas — tourism and Sir Stafford Sands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Tourism: Sir Stafford Sands builds a year-round industry.
**Answer: rich winter residents once came only for the season; in the 1950s Sir Stafford Sands, "Father of Tourism", heads the Development Board, develops Freeport with Wallace Groves, pushes air conditioning, advertising, tax incentives, better air travel and legalised casinos, and by 1969–1979 research and training make tourism the number one industry (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Tourism: from invalids to a year-round industry.
**Answer: the first tourists are invalids seeking health; the Royal Victoria Hotel (1861) and Colonial Hotel (1900) draw rich winter visitors; bootlegging customs money dredges the harbour and brings water and electricity — for tourists, not ordinary Bahamians; in the 1950s Sir Stafford Sands makes tourism a year-round business (any close wording).**
