# European Colonial Domination In The Americas

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Spain and Portugal divide the new continent first, Britain and France follow; each empire rules differently — viceroyalties, sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans, family farms in the Thirteen Colonies As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dominación colonial española, portuguesa, inglesa y francesa en América

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
