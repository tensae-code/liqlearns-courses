## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Dominación colonial española, portuguesa, inglesa y francesa en América** — Spain and Portugal divide the new continent first, Britain and France follow; each empire rules differently — viceroyalties, sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans, family farms in the Thirteen Colonies
2. **Consecuencias de la dominación colonial europea** — the bill is counted in four columns — economic dependence, lost sovereignty, an indigenous population crashed by epidemics, and mestizaje under an imposed culture and religion
3. **Conquista de América y la Resistencia Indígena: las Antillas y México** — conquest is defined by massacre, cruelty and greed; after the Antilles are exhausted, Cortés sails from Cuba in 1519 with 700 men after Aztec gold
4. **Conquista española en América del Sur** — Pizarro exploits the Inca civil war between Huáscar and Atahualpa; German bankers' agents conquer Venezuela brutally on Spain's debts, and Tordesillas hands Brazil to Portugal
5. **La Dominación Colonial Europea: colonización española** — three centuries of rule through viceroys, encomiendas and tribute; the Church evangelises by liquidating indigenous belief while some friars defend the indigenous against the encomenderos' abuses
6. **Colonización portuguesa en América** — Brazil-wood dye and then sugar pull Portugal into Brazil; Souza founds San Vicente in 1532 and fights off French corsairs for the coast
7. **Colonización inglesa: causas y características** — England's thirteen colonies grow by private initiative and Crown together, a heterogeneous European population under royal governors and elected assemblies
8. **El Colonialismo y los Nuevos Estados Europeos** — Spain and Portugal absorb indigenous populations into mixed settlements while Britain and France displace them; Britain's strategic colonies from Gibraltar to Hong Kong make it mistress of the sea routes, with India as the prize
