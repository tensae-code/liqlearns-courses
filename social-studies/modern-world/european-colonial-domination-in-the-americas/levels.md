# European Colonial Domination In The Americas — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Dominación colonial española, portuguesa, inglesa y francesa en América
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Spain and Portugal divide the new continent first, Britain and France follow; each empire rules differently — viceroyalties, sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans, family farms in the Thirteen Colonies

## Sub lesson 2 — Consecuencias de la dominación colonial europea
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the bill is counted in four columns — economic dependence, lost sovereignty, an indigenous population crashed by epidemics, and mestizaje under an imposed culture and religion

## Sub lesson 3 — Conquista de América y la Resistencia Indígena: las Antillas y México
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — conquest is defined by massacre, cruelty and greed; after the Antilles are exhausted, Cortés sails from Cuba in 1519 with 700 men after Aztec gold

## Sub lesson 4 — Conquista española en América del Sur
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Pizarro exploits the Inca civil war between Huáscar and Atahualpa; German bankers' agents conquer Venezuela brutally on Spain's debts, and Tordesillas hands Brazil to Portugal

## Sub lesson 5 — La Dominación Colonial Europea: colonización española
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — three centuries of rule through viceroys, encomiendas and tribute; the Church evangelises by liquidating indigenous belief while some friars defend the indigenous against the encomenderos' abuses

## Sub lesson 6 — Colonización portuguesa en América
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Brazil-wood dye and then sugar pull Portugal into Brazil; Souza founds San Vicente in 1532 and fights off French corsairs for the coast

## Sub lesson 7 — Colonización inglesa: causas y características
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — England's thirteen colonies grow by private initiative and Crown together, a heterogeneous European population under royal governors and elected assemblies

## Sub lesson 8 — El Colonialismo y los Nuevos Estados Europeos
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Spain and Portugal absorb indigenous populations into mixed settlements while Britain and France displace them; Britain's strategic colonies from Gibraltar to Hong Kong make it mistress of the sea routes, with India as the prize
