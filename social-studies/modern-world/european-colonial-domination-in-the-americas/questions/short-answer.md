# European Colonial Domination In The Americas — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Dominación colonial española, portuguesa, inglesa y francesa en América.
**Answer: Spain and Portugal divide the new continent first, Britain and France follow; each empire rules differently — viceroyalties, sugar plantations worked by enslaved Africans, family farms in the Thirteen Colonies (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Nicaragua's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Estudios Sociales — Primaria Regular, 6to Grado* (Nicaragua), Dominación colonial española, portuguesa, inglesa y francesa en América, printed p. 148.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: Consecuencias de la dominación colonial europea.
**Answer: the bill is counted in four columns — economic dependence, lost sovereignty, an indigenous population crashed by epidemics, and mestizaje under an imposed culture and religion (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: conquest is defined by massacre, cruelty and greed; after the Antilles are exhausted, Cortés sails from Cuba in 1519 with 700 men after Aztec gold (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: Pizarro exploits the Inca civil war between Huáscar and Atahualpa; German bankers' agents conquer Venezuela brutally on Spain's debts, and Tordesillas hands Brazil to Portugal (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: three centuries of rule through viceroys, encomiendas and tribute; the Church evangelises by liquidating indigenous belief while some friars defend the indigenous against the encomenderos' abuses (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
