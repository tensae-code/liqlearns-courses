# Nazi dictatorship, 1933 to 1934 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — 7.1 The importance of the Reichstag fire
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The Reichstag burns on 27 February 1933; the Nazis blame communist van der Lubbe, win an emergency decree suspending civil rights and arrest thousands before the March election

## Sub lesson 2 — 7.2 The importance of the Enabling Act
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — With communist deputies arrested and the SA lining the chamber, the Reichstag votes itself out of power in March 1933; Hitler can now legislate without parliament or president

## Sub lesson 3 — 7.3 The removal of opposition
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Gleichschaltung brings states, unions and parties into line through 1933 — unions smashed on 2 May, parties banned by July — until the Nazi Party is the only organisation left standing

## Sub lesson 4 — 7.4 The importance of the Night of the Long Knives
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — On 30 June 1934 Hitler purges Röhm and the SA leadership who wanted a 'second revolution', murdering old comrades to buy the army's loyalty

## Sub lesson 5 — 7.5 The support of the army
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — When Hindenburg dies in August 1934 the army swears its oath to Hitler personally, and he merges Chancellor and President as Führer — the dictatorship is complete
