# Nazi dictatorship, 1933 to 1934 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Reichstag burns on 27 February 1933; the Nazis blame communist van der Lubbe, win an emergency decree suspending civil rights and arrest thousands before the March election.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 7.1 The importance of the Reichstag fire

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *GCSE History for Edexcel: Weimar and Nazi Germany, 1918–39* is about 7.2 The importance of the Enabling Act.
**Answer: False** — it is about 7.1 The importance of the Reichstag fire

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** With communist deputies arrested and the SA lining the chamber, the Reichstag votes itself out of power in March 1933; Hitler can now legislate without parliament or president.
