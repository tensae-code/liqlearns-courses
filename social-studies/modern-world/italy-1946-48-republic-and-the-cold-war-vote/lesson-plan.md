## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unità 5, Cap. 19 §§1–2 A difficult postwar; The turning point of 1948 and the years of centrism** — divided Gorizia (Storie di frontiera) mirrors a country choosing Republic and Constituent Assembly amid hunger; De Gasperi ratifies the Atlantic Pact (Document), Togliatti appeals for concord, Spinelli dreams Europe from Ventotene — Italy takes the West's side
2. **Modulo 13 §§1–3 L'Italia nel 1945; La Repubblica e la Costituente; La Costituzione e il trattato di pace** — the Republic's Constitution is itself a Fare storia Document; Colarizi on the mass parties and Zamagni on the economic choices frame a country founded by referendum, assembly and compromise — the foibe and the Cassa per il Mezzogiorno are the module's Focus shadows
3. **Modulo 13 §§4–5 Il tempo delle scelte; De Gasperi e il centrismo** — 18 April 1948 (Eventi) is the founding election — Galli and Sartori (Fare storia) debate "imperfect bipartism or polarised pluralism"; De Gasperi (Personaggi) governs the choice for a decade
