# Indonesia — the Cultivation System (Tanam Paksa) — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cultuurstelsel forces Javanese villages to grow sugar, coffee and tobacco for export, and the book weighs its exploitative weight against the plantation towns, like Deli Serdang and Malang, that grow up around it.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.C Dampak Penjajahan di Negara Koloni

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — Budi Utomo and the early national movement.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.C Dampak Penjajahan di Negara Koloni

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Restored Dutch dominance rests on the Tanam Paksa cultivation system, whose burdens on Javanese villages the chapter documents as the colonial state's engine.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.C.3 Dominasi Pemerintahan Kolonial Belanda

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — Budi Utomo and the early national movement.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.C.3 Dominasi Pemerintahan Kolonial Belanda
