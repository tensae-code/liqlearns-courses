This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 19, Phan Bội Châu and the road abroad
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Phan Bội Châu (1867–1940) of Nghệ An, a Hương examination laureate, concludes after Cần Vương's defeat that Vietnam must seek foreign help — "by whatever means restore the country"; in 1904 he founds the Hội Duy tân, in 1905 goes to Japan for arms and money, and launches the Đông du movement sending patriotic youths to study there; expelled in 1909, he founds the Việt Nam Quang phục hội in Guangdong in 1912 to "drive out the French, restore Vietnam, found the Democratic Republic of Vietnam", sends assassins home in 1913, and is arrested and jailed in Guangdong — the end of his most active phase

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 12 Phan Bội Châu and Phan Châu Trinh go abroad: Phan Bội Châu works Japan and China — 200 youths sent to study under Đông Du (1905), the Điền–Quế–Việt and East Asia alliances (1908), then the Vietnam Restoration Society at Guangzhou (early 1912) and feelers to German and Russian representatives; Phan Châu Trinh works France from 1911 — petitions to the government, exposure of colonial rule to French opinion, and a hand in founding patriotic Vietnamese organisations there
