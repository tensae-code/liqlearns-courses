# Hands-on — Vietnam Phan Boi Chau And Dong Du

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Phan Boi Chau And Dong Du — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 19, Phan Bội Châu and the road abroad, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
