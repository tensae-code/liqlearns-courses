# Hands-on — Micronesia — the China trade and the scientific voyages

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Chart your own island
Draw a chart of your street, village or school grounds as a visiting scientist would: name every feature, including names of your own invention. Then swap charts with a companion — can they find their way by your names alone?

## Crossroads game
Mark a sea road across the floor from 'home port' to 'China'. Place island cards along it. Walk the road as a trader: at each island, say one thing your ship needs and one thing you could trade for it.
