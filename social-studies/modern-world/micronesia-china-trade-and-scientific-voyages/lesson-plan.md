# Lesson plan — Micronesia — the China trade and the scientific voyages

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — the China trade and the scientific voyages
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After 150 quiet years, the China trade made Micronesia a crossroads again, and Russian and European scientific voyages such as Lütke's made first contacts in Kosrae and the Mortlocks, naming and charting the islands for the outside world.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The return of Europeans: the China trade crossroads** — say that after 150 quiet years the China trade made Micronesia a crossroads again.
2. **Scientific voyages: Lütke in Kosrae and the Mortlocks** — say that Russian and European scientific voyages such as Lütke's made first contacts in Kosrae and the Mortlocks.
3. **Naming and charting the islands** — say that the voyages named and charted the islands, fixing them on the world's maps.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
