# Micronesia — the China trade and the scientific voyages — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The ships returned to Micronesia because of trade with —
a) China  b) India  c) America
**Answer: a) China** — the trade the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Before the return, the islands had about how many quiet years?
a) 50 years  b) 150 years  c) 300 years
**Answer: b) 150 years** — the quiet span the book gives.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Lütke's voyage was a —
a) Whaling voyage  b) Scientific voyage  c) Mission voyage
**Answer: b) Scientific voyage** — the kind of voyage the book describes.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The scientific voyages made first contacts in —
a) Kosrae and the Mortlocks  b) Yap and Palau  c) Guam and Saipan
**Answer: a) Kosrae and the Mortlocks** — the places the book names.
