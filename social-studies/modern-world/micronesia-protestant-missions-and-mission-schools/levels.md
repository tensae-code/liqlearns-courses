# Micronesia — Protestant missions and mission schools — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the books teach them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — American missionaries land in Kosrae and Pohnpei
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that American Protestant missionaries landed in Kosrae and Pohnpei in the 1850s, and that chiefs such as the Nahnken of Kitti weighed the new faith.

## Sub lesson 2 — Mission schools and the language of school
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that mission schools trained islander teachers and pastors, English church-and-school words entered the island languages, and literacy strengthened native Christians.

## Sub lesson 3 — Kosrae: a church rebuilding a people
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that missionaries reached a Kosrae whose population had fallen ninety percent in fifty years, and the church rebuilt Kosraean leadership and faith together.
