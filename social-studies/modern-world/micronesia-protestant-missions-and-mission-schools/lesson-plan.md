# Lesson plan — Micronesia — Protestant missions and mission schools

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — Protestant missions and mission schools
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** American Protestant missionaries landed in Kosrae and Pohnpei in the 1850s, chiefs such as the Nahnken of Kitti weighed the new faith, islander missionaries carried it on to the Marshalls, the Mortlocks and Chuuk, and mission schools trained islander teachers and pastors — while on Kosrae, where population had fallen ninety percent, the church rebuilt leadership and faith together.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **American missionaries land in Kosrae and Pohnpei** — say that American Protestant missionaries landed in Kosrae and Pohnpei in the 1850s, and that chiefs such as the Nahnken of Kitti weighed the new faith.
2. **Mission schools and the language of school** — say that mission schools trained islander teachers and pastors, English church-and-school words entered the island languages, and literacy strengthened native Christians.
3. **Kosrae: a church rebuilding a people** — say that missionaries reached a Kosrae whose population had fallen ninety percent in fifty years, and the church rebuilt Kosraean leadership and faith together.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
