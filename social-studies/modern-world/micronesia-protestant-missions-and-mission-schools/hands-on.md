# Hands-on — Micronesia — Protestant missions and mission schools

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Words that stayed
List five English words used in your own language for church or school things. Beside each, write the older word your language used before, if you know it. You have just mapped what the mission schools did to Micronesia's languages.

## The chief weighs it
In pairs, one plays the Nahnken of Kitti and one a missionary asking leave to teach. The chief must ask three hard questions before deciding. Swap roles and compare the questions.
