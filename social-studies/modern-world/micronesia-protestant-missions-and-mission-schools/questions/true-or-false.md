# Micronesia — Protestant missions and mission schools — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Islander missionaries carried the faith on to the Marshalls, the Mortlocks and Chuuk.
**Answer: True** — the book says islander missionaries carried it on.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mission schools kept literacy in foreign hands only.
**Answer: False** — the schools trained islander teachers and pastors, and literacy strengthened native Christians.
