# Micronesia — Protestant missions and mission schools — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The American Protestant missionaries landed in the —
a) 1750s  b) 1850s  c) 1950s
**Answer: b) 1850s** — the decade the book gives.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The chief who weighed the new faith on Pohnpei was —
a) The Nahnken of Kitti  b) The Nahnmwarki of Madolenihmw  c) Henry Nanpei
**Answer: a) The Nahnken of Kitti** — the chief the book names.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mission schools trained islanders as —
a) Sailors and traders  b) Teachers and pastors  c) Soldiers
**Answer: b) Teachers and pastors** — the training the book describes.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Kosrae's population had fallen by about —
a) Ten percent  b) Half  c) Ninety percent in fifty years
**Answer: c) Ninety percent in fifty years** — the fall the government book gives.
