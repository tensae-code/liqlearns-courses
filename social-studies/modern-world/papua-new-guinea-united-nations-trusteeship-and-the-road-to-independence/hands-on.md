# Hands-on — Papua New Guinea — United Nations trusteeship and the road to Independence

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Papua New Guinea — United Nations trusteeship and the road to Independence — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, United Nations role in Papua and New Guinea, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
