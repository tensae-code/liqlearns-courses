# The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The communist threat and the Emergency  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The communist threat and the Emergency** — the MCP's delegates attend the Calcutta conferences of February–March 1948 and the party turns to armed struggle; the state answers with Emergency laws, national registration, the Briggs Plan's resettlement, amnesty offers, curfews, psychological warfare and the Baling Talks, and raises the Senoi Praaq (1956) — Orang Asli Senoi, Semai and Temuan trackers, the "Silent Killers" — to hunt in the deep jungle

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 6: the communist threat and the Emergency  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 6: the communist threat and the Emergency** — the book's own heading for this part.
