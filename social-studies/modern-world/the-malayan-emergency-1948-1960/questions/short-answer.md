# The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The communist threat and the Emergency.
**Answer: the MCP's delegates attend the Calcutta conferences of February–March 1948 and the party turns to armed struggle; the state answers with Emergency laws, national registration, the Briggs Plan's resettlement, amnesty offers, curfews, psychological warfare and the Baling Talks, and raises the Senoi Praaq (1956) — Orang Asli Senoi, Semai and Temuan trackers, the "Silent Killers" — to hunt in the deep jungle (any close wording).**
