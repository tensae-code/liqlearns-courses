# The Malayan Emergency, 1948–1960

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: From 1948 the MCP's armed struggle brings the Emergency — Emergency laws, the Briggs Plan's resettlement, amnesty offers, the Baling Talks, and the Senoi Praaq trackers hunting in the deep jungle. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The communist threat and the Emergency

The MCP's delegates attend the Calcutta conferences of February–March 1948 and the party turns to armed struggle; the state answers with Emergency laws, national registration, the Briggs Plan's resettlement, amnesty offers, curfews, psychological warfare and the Baling Talks, and raises the Senoi Praaq (1956) — Orang Asli Senoi, Semai and Temuan trackers, the "Silent Killers" — to hunt in the deep jungle (Bab 6: the communist threat and the Emergency)
