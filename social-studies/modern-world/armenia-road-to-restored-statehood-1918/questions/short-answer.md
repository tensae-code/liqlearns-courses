# Armenia Road To Restored Statehood 1918 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: 2.2 Military-political processes around Russia and the maturing preconditions for restored statehood (late 1917 – mid-May 1918).
**Answer: Ottoman armies advance into the vacuum, and at Sardarabad, Bash-Aparan and Karakilisa in May 1918 Armenian forces stop them days before independence is declared (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
