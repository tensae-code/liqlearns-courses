**9. (Sub lesson 5)** Retell this part in your own words: Lesson 4: Jordan under the Syrian Arab Kingdom and the local governments.
**Answer: Faysal enters Damascus in 1918 and builds the kingdom later called the Syrian Arab Kingdom; the King-Crane Commission tours Jordan in June–July 1919 asking the people their wishes; the Syrian General Congress (2 July 1919) speaks for all Bilad al-Sham — with Jordanian members from Karak, Ajloun and Ramtha — but San Remo (25 April 1920) imposes the mandates, splits Syria into Palestine, Lebanon and the rest, and binds Transjordan to the Balfour promise; Jordanian volunteers fight the French at Maysalun and in the south of Lebanon; then, in the vacuum, East Jordan governs itself (August 1920–April 1921): the Ajloun government at Irbid under Ali Khulqi al-Sharayri, the Salt government under Mazhar Arslan between Wadi al-Mujib and the Zarqa, and the Moab government at Karak — sealed by the Umm Qais conference of September 1920 (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
