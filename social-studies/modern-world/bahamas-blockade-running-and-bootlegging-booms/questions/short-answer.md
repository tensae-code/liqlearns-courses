**Answer: Nassau becomes the Confederacy's back door to Europe; businesses flourish and infrastructure improves on blockade money — and crime grows with the population (any close wording).**

**Answer: America's 1919 liquor ban makes Bahamian bootleggers rich from 1920 to 1933 smuggling drink into the United States; the money improves rich homes and tourist infrastructure, but the Over-the-Hill communities benefit far less (any close wording).**
