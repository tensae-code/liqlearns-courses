# Bahamas — blockade-running and bootlegging booms — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nassau becomes the Confederacy's back door to Europe; businesses flourish and infrastructure improves on blockade money — and crime grows with the population
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Blockade 1861–1865: The Bahamas runs the blockade).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** America's 1919 liquor ban makes Bahamian bootleggers rich from 1920 to 1933 smuggling drink into the United States; the money improves rich homes and tourist infrastructure, but the Over-the-Hill communities benefit far less
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bootlegging 1920: rum-running through Prohibition).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** when American prohibition laws choke the liquor flow, rum-running becomes an economic benefit to the growing colony
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Early Connections: bootlegging benefits the colony).
