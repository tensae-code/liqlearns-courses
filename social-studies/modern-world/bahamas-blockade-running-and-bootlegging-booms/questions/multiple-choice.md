# Bahamas — blockade-running and bootlegging booms — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Blockade 1861–1865: The Bahamas runs the blockade  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Blockade 1861–1865: The Bahamas runs the blockade** — Nassau becomes the Confederacy's back door to Europe; businesses flourish and infrastructure improves on blockade money — and crime grows with the population
