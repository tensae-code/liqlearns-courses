# Indonesia — from RIS to the unitary Republic, 1950 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The federal Republic of the United States of Indonesia, imposed at the Round Table, dissolves itself within months as its states merge back into the unitary Republic in 1950.
