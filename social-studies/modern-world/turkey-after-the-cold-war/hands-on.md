# Hands-on — Turkey after the Cold War

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The map, redrawn

Draw Turkey's map twice: 1989, with two blocs coloured against each other; and 1995, the blocs gone, fires marked in the Balkans and the Gulf. Write what a foreign minister's job becomes when the first map turns into the second.

## Between two pulls

Draw Turkey as a bridge with Europe on one bank and its region on the other. Label three loads the bridge must carry after 1990 — trade, security, identity — and say which load feels heaviest, and why.

## The last question

The book ends on a threshold. Write the three questions you would ask a Turkish leader of 1995 about the next twenty-five years — then one line each on what you would listen for in the answers.
