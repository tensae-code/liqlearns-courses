# Video script — Turkey after the Cold War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Grade 12 book ends on a threshold. The wall falls; the Cold War order — the blocs, the alliances, the map Turkey stood inside — dissolves.

Around Turkey, the neighbourhood catches fire: the Balkans and the Gulf burn through the 1990s.

So the republic renegotiates its place between Europe and its region — no longer a flank of someone else's alliance, but a country deciding for itself. The story stops at the threshold, because the next chapter is the reader's own time.
