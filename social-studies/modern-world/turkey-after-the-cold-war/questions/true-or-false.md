# Turkey after the Cold War — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Cold War's end removed the alliance map Turkey had stood inside.
**Answer: True** — the blocs are gone; the place must be renegotiated.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The book teaches that Turkey's region grew calm and settled after 1990.
**Answer: False** — the neighbourhood catches fire — the Balkans and the Gulf.
