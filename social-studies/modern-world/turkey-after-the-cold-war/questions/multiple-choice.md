# Turkey after the Cold War — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The threshold begins with —
a) The wall falling and the Cold War order dissolving  b) NATO's founding  c) The republic's proclamation
**Answer: a) The wall falling and the Cold War order dissolving** — the order ends.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The neighbourhood fires of the 1990s named are —
a) Korea and Vietnam  b) The Balkans and the Gulf  c) Spain and Portugal
**Answer: b) The Balkans and the Gulf** — wars on the region's edges.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** After 1990 Turkey renegotiates its place —
a) Between Europe and its region  b) Inside the Soviet bloc  c) Outside all alliances forever
**Answer: a) Between Europe and its region** — the closing beat.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The book leaves Turkey —
a) Settled and finished  b) On the threshold of the 21st century, deciding for itself  c) Back inside the empire
**Answer: b) On the threshold of the 21st century, deciding for itself** — an open ending for the reader's own time.
