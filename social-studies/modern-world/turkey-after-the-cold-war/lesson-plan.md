# Lesson plan — Turkey after the Cold War

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Turkey after the Cold War
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Unit 8, Turkey and the World on the Threshold of the 21st Century: Turkey after 1990 and the post-Cold War world.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a single contrast a child can hold: the wall falls, the neighbourhood catches fire, and the republic renegotiates its place between Europe and its region. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what ends at the threshold: the Cold War order — the blocs and the map Turkey had stood inside.
2. Name the neighbourhood fires of the 1990s the book points to: the Balkans and the Gulf.
3. Say what Turkey must renegotiate after 1990: its place between Europe and its region.
4. Explain, in their own words, why a book would end 'on the threshold' rather than with a settled ending.
