# American Peoples And First Colonial Empires — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Aztec and Inca worlds meet conquistadors and colonial empire begins.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §24 American peoples in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Всемирная история (Средние века) / Дүниежүзі тарихы (World History: The Middle Ages)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §24 American peoples in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Aztec and Inca civilisations fall to small bands of conquistadors.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §26 Peoples of America in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Всемирная история / Дүниежүзі тарихы (World History), for Grade 7(6)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §26 Peoples of America in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Maya, Aztec and Inca worlds are overrun and colonies planted.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §26 American peoples in the 15th c.; the first colonial empires (Portugal, Spain)
