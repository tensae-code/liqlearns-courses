# Vietnam Trinh Nguyen Partition

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: when Nguyễn Kim dies in 1545 his son-in-law Trịnh Kiểm seizes the army; Nguyễn Kim's son Nguyễn Hoàng asks to govern far-off Thuận Hoá and there builds a separate base, and his son Nguyễn Phúc Nguyên cuts loose from the Trịnh altogether; a quoted modern history puts the mechanism plainly — both houses borrow the Lê king's name to overshadow and destroy each other, the Trịnh demanding taxes and obedience from a court whose real power they hold, the Nguyễn proclaiming defiance; in 1627 the Trịnh attack Thuận Hoá on the pretext of unpaid taxes As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
