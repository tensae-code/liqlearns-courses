# Lesson plan — Vietnam Trinh Nguyen Partition

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Trinh Nguyen Partition
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** when Nguyễn Kim dies in 1545 his son-in-law Trịnh Kiểm seizes the army; Nguyễn Kim's son Nguyễn Hoàng asks to govern far-off Thuận Hoá and there builds a separate base, and his son Nguyễn Phúc Nguyên cuts loose from the Trịnh altogether; a quoted modern history puts the mechanism plainly — both houses borrow the Lê king's name to overshadow and destroy each other, the Trịnh demanding taxes and obedience from a court whose real power they hold, the Nguyễn proclaiming defiance; in 1627 the Trịnh attack Thuận Hoá on the pretext of unpaid taxes

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 5, how the Trịnh–Nguyễn split began** — when Nguyễn Kim dies in 1545 his son-in-law Trịnh Kiểm seizes the army; Nguyễn Kim's son Nguyễn Hoàng asks to govern far-off Thuận Hoá and there builds a separate base, and his son Nguyễn Phúc Nguyên cuts loose from the Trịnh altogether; a quoted modern history puts the mechanism plainly — both houses borrow the Lê king's name to overshadow and destroy each other, the Trịnh demanding taxes and obedience from a court whose real power they hold, the Nguyễn proclaiming defiance; in 1627 the Trịnh attack Thuận Hoá on the pretext of unpaid taxes
2. **Bài 5, fifty years of Trịnh–Nguyễn war and the divided country** — between 1627 and 1672 the two sides clash seven times; the stronger Trịnh attack six times across the Gianh river but cannot break the Lũy Thầy ramparts designed by the engineer Đào Duy Từ, guarded at the Nhật Lệ river mouth by an iron chain whose stele still records that "here fierce battles between the Trịnh and Nguyễn feudal powers took place"; in the end the Gianh river becomes the border — Đàng Ngoài (the Outer Region) under "King Lê – Lord Trịnh" in the north, Đàng Trong (the Inner Region) under the Nguyễn lords in the south — and a folk verse warns that not even wings can carry you over Lũy Thầy
