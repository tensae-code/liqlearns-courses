# Video script — Vietnam Trinh Nguyen Partition

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Vietnam's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

when Nguyễn Kim dies in 1545 his son-in-law Trịnh Kiểm seizes the army; Nguyễn Kim's son Nguyễn Hoàng asks to govern far-off Thuận Hoá and there builds a separate base, and his son Nguyễn Phúc Nguyên cuts loose from the Trịnh altogether; a quoted modern history puts the mechanism plainly — both houses borrow the Lê king's name to overshadow and destroy each other, the Trịnh demanding taxes and obedience from a court whose real power they hold, the Nguyễn proclaiming defiance; in 1627 the Trịnh attack Thuận Hoá on the pretext of unpaid taxes
