# Vietnam Trinh Nguyen Partition — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** when Nguyễn Kim dies in 1545 his son-in-law Trịnh Kiểm seizes the army; Nguyễn Kim's son Nguyễn Hoàng asks to govern far-off Thuận Hoá and there builds a separate base, and his son Nguyễn Phúc Nguyên cuts loose from the Trịnh altogether; a quoted modern history puts the mechanism plainly — both houses borrow the Lê king's name to overshadow and destroy each other, the Trịnh demanding taxes and obedience from a court whose real power they hold, the Nguyễn proclaiming defiance; in 1627 the Trịnh attack Thuận Hoá on the pretext of unpaid taxes.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 5, how the Trịnh–Nguyễn split began

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 5, how the Trịnh–Nguyễn split began

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** between 1627 and 1672 the two sides clash seven times; the stronger Trịnh attack six times across the Gianh river but cannot break the Lũy Thầy ramparts designed by the engineer Đào Duy Từ, guarded at the Nhật Lệ river mouth by an iron chain whose stele still records that "here fierce battles between the Trịnh and Nguyễn feudal powers took place"; in the end the Gianh river becomes the border — Đàng Ngoài (the Outer Region) under "King Lê – Lord Trịnh" in the north, Đàng Trong (the Inner Region) under the Nguyễn lords in the south — and a folk verse warns that not even wings can carry you over Lũy Thầy.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 5, fifty years of Trịnh–Nguyễn war and the divided country
