# Lesson plan — Kazakhstan Aktaban Shubyryndy

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Kazakhstan Aktaban Shubyryndy
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Kazakhstan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** in 1723 the Dzhungar onslaught drives whole peoples fleeing barefoot across the steppe — the Aktaban Shubyryndy, remembered in song as the nation's great calamity
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **§§3–4 Organising a united people's militia; the "Years of Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy"** — in 1723 the Dzhungar onslaught drives whole peoples fleeing barefoot across the steppe — the Aktaban Shubyryndy, remembered in song as the nation's great calamity
2. **§3 The years of "Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy"** — the 1723 invasion drives the Kazakh clans fleeing west in hunger and disorder
3. **§4 The organisation of a united people's militia** — at Ordabasy the three zhuzes unite under one banner and one command
4. **§1.1 Why did the song "Elim-ai" become a song of the whole people?** — one lament for the Dzhungar invasion years is sung by every aul until it becomes the nation's memory of the disaster
5. **Lesson plan §2: the age of "Aktaban Shubyryndy, Alkakol Sulama"** — the 1723 catastrophe is taught through Kozhabergen zhyrau's song "Elim-ai" as a historical source
