# Kazakhstan Aktaban Shubyryndy — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in 1723 the Dzhungar onslaught drives whole peoples fleeing barefoot across the steppe — the Aktaban Shubyryndy, remembered in song as the nation's great calamity.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§3–4 Organising a united people's militia; the "Years of Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy"

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§3–4 Organising a united people's militia; the "Years of Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy"

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the 1723 invasion drives the Kazakh clans fleeing west in hunger and disorder.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §3 The years of "Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy"

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §3 The years of "Great Disaster" — "Aktaban Shubyryndy"

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** at Ordabasy the three zhuzes unite under one banner and one command.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §4 The organisation of a united people's militia

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §4 The organisation of a united people's militia

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** one lament for the Dzhungar invasion years is sung by every aul until it becomes the nation's memory of the disaster.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §1.1 Why did the song "Elim-ai" become a song of the whole people?

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы. 8-сынып [History of Kazakhstan, Grade 8]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §1.1 Why did the song "Elim-ai" become a song of the whole people?

**9. (Sub lesson 5)** the 1723 catastrophe is taught through Kozhabergen zhyrau's song "Elim-ai" as a historical source.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lesson plan §2: the age of "Aktaban Shubyryndy, Alkakol Sulama"

**10. (Sub lesson 5)** This part of *Қазақстан тарихы (XVIII–XIX ғғ.): Әдістемелік нұсқау [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th cc.): Methodical Guide for teachers of grades 8/7]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
