# The War of Independence, 1857

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Sepoys refuse greased cartridges and rise in 1857; the war fails, the East India Company is abolished, and Queen Victoria's Proclamation of 1858 puts India under the Crown. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The War of Independence, 1857

A cartridge rumour ignites a subcontinent-wide revolt, it is crushed, and direct British rule replaces company rule.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
