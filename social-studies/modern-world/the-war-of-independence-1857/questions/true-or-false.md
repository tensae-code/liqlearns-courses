# The War of Independence, 1857 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** A cartridge rumour ignites a subcontinent-wide revolt, it is crushed, and direct British rule replaces company rule.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch.1: sepoys refuse greased cartridges and rise in 1857; the war fails, the Company is abolished and Queen Victoria's Proclamation of 1858 puts India under the Crown

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The War of Independence, 1857
