# The War of Independence, 1857 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The War of Independence, 1857.
**Answer: A cartridge rumour ignites a subcontinent-wide revolt, it is crushed, and direct British rule replaces company rule. (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: In *History 8 (PCTB, SNC 2022)* (Grade 8 / Class 8), Ch.1: sepoys refuse greased cartridges and rise in 1857; the war fails, the Company is abolished and Queen Victoria's Proclamation of 1858 puts India under the Crown, printed p. 13.**
