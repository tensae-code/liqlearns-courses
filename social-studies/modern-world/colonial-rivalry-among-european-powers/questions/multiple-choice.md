# Colonial rivalry among the European powers — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The empires measured their shares of —
a) Africa and Asia  b) the Americas only  c) the poles
**Answer: a) Africa and Asia** — their shares of Africa and Asia

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The rivalry itself became —
a) a road to war  b) a peace treaty  c) a trade fair
**Answer: a) a road to war** — the rivalry becomes a road to war

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Each empire compared its share against —
a) the other empires  b) its own colonies only  c) no one
**Answer: a) the other empires** — they measure their shares against each other
