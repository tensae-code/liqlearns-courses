# Micronesia — first European contacts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the books teach them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Portuguese in Ulithi, Spaniards in Yap, musket fire in the Marshalls
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that storm-blown Portuguese under Dioga da Rocha met islanders face to face, Spanish visits puzzled both sides, and welcomes turned hostile in Chuuk Lagoon and Pulap as ships demanded provisions.

## Sub lesson 2 — Kapingamarangi: the first outsiders, one by one
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that Kapingamarangi remembers its first outsiders one by one — Tongohiti, the Wareai traders of the 1800s, castaways from Kiribati, and the Moduroo people in the 1870s.

## Sub lesson 3 — Nukuoro: an atoll keeping its own contact register
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that Nukuoro logged arrivals one by one — canoes and money from Fiji, Kiribati, the Marshalls in 1870, Onotoa, Yap, Sonsorol and Namoluk, and European sightings in 1806 and 1840.
