# Hands-on — Micronesia — first European contacts

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Keep the register
Like Nukuoro, keep a contact register for one week: every new person who comes to your home, school or street, written down with the day and where they came from. At the week's end, read it back — you have written the kind of record the atolls kept.

## Two sides of one meeting
With a companion, act the first meeting at Ulithi twice: once as the storm-blown sailors, once as the islanders watching the ship come in. Each side says one sentence about what it hopes for and one about what it fears.
