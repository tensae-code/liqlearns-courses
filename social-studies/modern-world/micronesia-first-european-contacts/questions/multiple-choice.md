# Micronesia — first European contacts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The storm-blown Portuguese who met islanders in Ulithi were led by —
a) Dioga da Rocha  b) Magellan  c) Lütke
**Answer: a) Dioga da Rocha** — the captain the book names.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Chuuk Lagoon and Pulap, welcomes turned hostile as ships —
a) Offered gifts  b) Demanded provisions  c) Asked for directions
**Answer: b) Demanded provisions** — the cause the book gives.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Among Kapingamarangi's first remembered outsiders were —
a) The Moduroo people in the 1870s  b) German officials  c) Japanese teachers
**Answer: a) The Moduroo people in the 1870s** — one of the arrivals the reader lists.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Nukuoro's reader records European sightings in —
a) 1565 and 1600  b) 1806 and 1840  c) 1899 and 1914
**Answer: b) 1806 and 1840** — the dates in the atoll's register.
