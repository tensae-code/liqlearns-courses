# Micronesia — first European contacts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** First European contact with Micronesia happened in a single landing that covered all the islands at once.
**Answer: False** — the books teach many separate meetings, island by island — Ulithi, Yap, Chuuk Lagoon, Pulap and the atolls.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** Nukuoro's arrivals were recorded only by European ships' logs, never by the island itself.
**Answer: False** — the Nukuoro reader is the atoll's own register — arrivals logged one by one, dated and named.
