# Micronesia — first European contacts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did some first meetings turn hostile?
**Answer: Ships demanded provisions, and welcomes in places such as Chuuk Lagoon and Pulap turned hostile, with musket fire (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two of the first outsiders Kapingamarangi remembers, from its own record.
**Answer: Any two of: the man Tongohiti; the Wareai traders of the 1800s; castaways from Kiribati; the Moduroo people in the 1870s (any close wording).**
