## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A state bears a great man's name, so two children reconstruct Antonio José de Sucre's life to learn why As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: Two children think about Sucre — a state bears a great man's name, so two children reconstruct Antonio José de Sucre's life to learn why. Sucre, a state with a hero's name; the valour and goodness of Sucre — Cumaná's state is named for Antonio José de Sucre, whose childhood listening and refusal to shoot prisoners show goodness matching his valour. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
