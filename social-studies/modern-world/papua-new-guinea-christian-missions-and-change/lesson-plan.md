# Lesson plan — Papua New Guinea — Christian missions and change

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Papua New Guinea — Christian missions and change
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Papua New Guinea, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Missions arriving from the mid-1800s settle region by region until whole provinces belong to the first church that came; commentators say the missions changed marriage, dress, schooling and belief more deeply than the government ever did
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Religions and change in PNG** — missions arriving from the mid-1800s settle region by region until whole provinces belong to the first church that came; commentators say the missions changed marriage, dress, schooling and belief more deeply than the government ever did
2. **Religion and change in the Pacific** — region-wide, the missions' handwriting is everywhere — a church in every settlement, church schools and health services, and Christian practice woven into marriage, childrearing, dress, art, government and farming
