# The tumultuous 1850s — the road to civil war — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the four steps on this lesson's road to war, in order.
**Answer: Compromise of 1850 pauses the quarrel; Kansas-Nebraska Act bleeds Kansas and births the Republicans; Dred Scott and John Brown's raid; Lincoln's election splits the Union (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Why did Dred Scott inflame rather than settle the quarrel?
**Answer: It denied Black Americans any rights a court must respect, convincing many Northerners the slave power controlled the government (any close wording).**
