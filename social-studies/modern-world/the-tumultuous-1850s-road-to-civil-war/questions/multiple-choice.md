# The tumultuous 1850s — the road to civil war — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Compromise of 1850 —
a) Settled the slavery question forever  b) Only paused the quarrel  c) Abolished slavery
**Answer: b) Only paused the quarrel** — the Compromise of 1850 only pauses the quarrel.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Bleeding Kansas followed the —
a) Kansas-Nebraska Act  b) Stamp Act  c) Cotton gin
**Answer: a) Kansas-Nebraska Act** — the Kansas-Nebraska Act bleeds Kansas.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Kansas fighting helped birth which party?
a) The Democratic Party  b) The Republican Party  c) The Federalist Party
**Answer: b) The Republican Party** — it births the Republican Party.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Dred Scott told Black Americans they —
a) Had full citizenship  b) Had no rights a court must respect  c) Must be freed at once
**Answer: b) Had no rights a court must respect** — Dred Scott tells Black Americans they have no rights a court must respect.
