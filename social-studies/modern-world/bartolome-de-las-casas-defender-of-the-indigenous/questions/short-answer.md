# Bartolome De Las Casas Defender Of The Indigenous — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: the Dominican who accused the conquest.
**Answer: Fray Bartolomé de las Casas, who saw the encomienda as slavery, told the Council of the Indies in 1562–63 of "two species of tyranny" — the conquest itself and the repartimientos/encomiendas that stripped America's natural lords of liberty and life (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Honduras's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Libro para Estudiantes, Ciencias Sociales — Octavo Grado de Educación Básica* (Honduras), the Dominican who accused the conquest, printed p. 148.**
