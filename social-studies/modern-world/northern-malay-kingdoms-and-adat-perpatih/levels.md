## Sub lesson 1 — Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: the northern kingdoms deal with their Malay neighbours and leave a living heritage — Kedah's war poem Syair Sultan Maulana from the reign of Sultan Ahmad Tajuddin Halim Shah II (1804–1845), the Hikayat Seri Kelantan (1914), Kelantan's wau kites and batik, and wayang kulit raised to national heritage

## Sub lesson 2 — Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Minangkabau migrants settle Rembau, Sungai Ujong, Johol and Naning bringing Adat Perpatih, the matrilineal custom: descent and inheritance run through the mother, a husband joins his wife's suku as orang semenda, and the people — Perut, Suku and Luak — appoint their adat chiefs (Ibu Soko, Buapak, Lembaga), whose four Undang elect the Yamtuan Besar
