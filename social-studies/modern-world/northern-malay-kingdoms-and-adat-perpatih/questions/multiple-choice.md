# The northern Malay kingdoms and Adat Perpatih — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage** — the northern kingdoms deal with their Malay neighbours and leave a living heritage — Kedah's war poem Syair Sultan Maulana from the reign of Sultan Ahmad Tajuddin Halim Shah II (1804–1845), the Hikayat Seri Kelantan (1914), Kelantan's wau kites and batik, and wayang kulit raised to national heritage

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 8–9.1: Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 8–9.1: Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage** — the book's own heading for this part.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih** — Minangkabau migrants settle Rembau, Sungai Ujong, Johol and Naning bringing Adat Perpatih, the matrilineal custom: descent and inheritance run through the mother, a husband joins his wife's suku as orang semenda, and the people — Perut, Suku and Luak — appoint their adat chiefs (Ibu Soko, Buapak, Lembaga), whose four Undang elect the Yamtuan Besar
