# The northern Malay kingdoms and Adat Perpatih — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage.
**Answer: the northern kingdoms deal with their Malay neighbours and leave a living heritage — Kedah's war poem Syair Sultan Maulana from the reign of Sultan Ahmad Tajuddin Halim Shah II (1804–1845), the Hikayat Seri Kelantan (1914), Kelantan's wau kites and batik, and wayang kulit raised to national heritage (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih.
**Answer: Minangkabau migrants settle Rembau, Sungai Ujong, Johol and Naning bringing Adat Perpatih, the matrilineal custom: descent and inheritance run through the mother, a husband joins his wife's suku as orang semenda, and the people — Perut, Suku and Luak — appoint their adat chiefs (Ibu Soko, Buapak, Lembaga), whose four Undang elect the Yamtuan Besar (any close wording).**
