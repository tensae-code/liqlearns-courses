# The northern Malay kingdoms and Adat Perpatih — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The northern kingdoms deal with their Malay neighbours and leave a living heritage — Kedah's war poem Syair Sultan Maulana from the reign of Sultan Ahmad Tajuddin Halim Shah II (1804–1845), the Hikayat Seri Kelantan (1914), Kelantan's wau kites and batik, and wayang kulit raised to national heritage
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 8–9.1: Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Minangkabau migrants settle Rembau, Sungai Ujong, Johol and Naning bringing Adat Perpatih, the matrilineal custom: descent and inheritance run through the mother, a husband joins his wife's suku as orang semenda, and the people — Perut, Suku and Luak — appoint their adat chiefs (Ibu Soko, Buapak, Lembaga), whose four Undang elect the Yamtuan Besar
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 9.2: Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih).
