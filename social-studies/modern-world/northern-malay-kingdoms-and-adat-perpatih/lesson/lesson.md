# The northern Malay kingdoms and Adat Perpatih

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: The northern kingdoms — Kedah, Kelantan, Perlis — keep a living heritage of poems, kites, batik and wayang kulit, while Negeri Sembilan orders itself by Adat Perpatih, the matrilineal custom where descent and inheritance run through the mother. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage

The northern kingdoms deal with their Malay neighbours and leave a living heritage — Kedah's war poem Syair Sultan Maulana from the reign of Sultan Ahmad Tajuddin Halim Shah II (1804–1845), the Hikayat Seri Kelantan (1914), Kelantan's wau kites and batik, and wayang kulit raised to national heritage (Bab 8–9.1: Kedah, Kelantan and Perlis and their heritage)

## Sub lesson 2 — Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih

Minangkabau migrants settle Rembau, Sungai Ujong, Johol and Naning bringing Adat Perpatih, the matrilineal custom: descent and inheritance run through the mother, a husband joins his wife's suku as orang semenda, and the people — Perut, Suku and Luak — appoint their adat chiefs (Ibu Soko, Buapak, Lembaga), whose four Undang elect the Yamtuan Besar (Bab 9.2: Negeri Sembilan's Adat Perpatih)
