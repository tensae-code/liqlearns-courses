# Video script — The Haitian Revolution, 1791–1804

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Haiti guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Free people of colour demand their rights and the enslaved begin to rise — the colony cracks before the great august night

At bois caïman a vodou ceremony seals an oath — days later the northern plain burns; pupils separate the documented rising from the founding legend

Insurgent bands under leaders like jean-françois, biassou and the young toussaint hold the hills while empires — french, spanish, british — bid for them

War spreads across the colony; allegiances flip as spain arms the insurgents and france's commissioners promise freedom to those who fight for the republic

Sonthonax abolishes slavery in saint-domingue in 1793 and paris extends it in 1794 — the formerly enslaved become citizens and soldiers of the republic

Toussaint louverture out-generals the british and his rivals, takes the spanish side of the island, and rules saint-domingue in france's name but by his own hand

Louverture's 1801 constitution makes saint-domingue an autonomous state with himself governor for life — napoleon reads it as rebellion

Leclerc's expedition seizes louverture, but the indigenous army under dessalines wins at vertières; on 1 january 1804 haiti declares independence, and dessalines is assassinated in 1806

Pupils build illustrated biographies and mine the founding texts for who counted as a citizen in the new black state

Hold that story: From the first insurrections and Bois Caïman, through republican emancipation and Louverture's power, to Vertières and the independence of 1 January 1804 — the enslaved of Saint-Domingue free themselves and found Haiti.
