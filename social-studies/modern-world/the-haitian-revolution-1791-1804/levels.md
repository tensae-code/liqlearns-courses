# The Haitian Revolution, 1791–1804 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The first insurrections
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Free people of colour demand their rights and the enslaved begin to rise — the colony cracks before the great august night (Séance 2, Les premières insurrections)

## Sub lesson 2 — The insurrection of summer 1791 — Bois Caïman
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: At bois caïman a vodou ceremony seals an oath — days later the northern plain burns; pupils separate the documented rising from the founding legend (Séance 3, L'insurrection de l'été 1791: la cérémonie du Bois Caïman, entre l'histoire et le mythe)

## Sub lesson 3 — The insurrections 1791–1793 (1)
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Insurgent bands under leaders like jean-françois, biassou and the young toussaint hold the hills while empires — french, spanish, british — bid for them (Séance 4, Les insurrections 1791-1793 (1))

## Sub lesson 4 — The insurrections 1791–1793 (2)
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: War spreads across the colony; allegiances flip as spain arms the insurgents and france's commissioners promise freedom to those who fight for the republic (Séance 5, Les insurrections 1791-1793 (2))

## Sub lesson 5 — Republican emancipation, 1793–1798
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Sonthonax abolishes slavery in saint-domingue in 1793 and paris extends it in 1794 — the formerly enslaved become citizens and soldiers of the republic (Séance 6, L'émancipation républicaine (1793-1798))

## Sub lesson 6 — Louverturian power (1)
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Toussaint louverture out-generals the british and his rivals, takes the spanish side of the island, and rules saint-domingue in france's name but by his own hand (Séance 7, Le pouvoir louverturien (1))

## Sub lesson 7 — Louverturian power (2) — the Constitution of 1801
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Louverture's 1801 constitution makes saint-domingue an autonomous state with himself governor for life — napoleon reads it as rebellion (Séance 8, Le pouvoir louverturien (2): la Constitution de 1801)

## Sub lesson 8 — The struggles for independence
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Leclerc's expedition seizes louverture, but the indigenous army under dessalines wins at vertières; on 1 january 1804 haiti declares independence, and dessalines is assassinated in 1806 (Séance 9, Les luttes pour l'indépendance)

## Sub lesson 9 — Founding lives and founding texts
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Pupils build illustrated biographies and mine the founding texts for who counted as a citizen in the new black state (Annex document work: biographies of Louverture and Dessalines; the 1804 Declaration of Independence and the 1805 Constitution)
