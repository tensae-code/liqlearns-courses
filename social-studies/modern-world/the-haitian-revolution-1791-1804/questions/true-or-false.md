# The Haitian Revolution, 1791–1804 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Free people of colour demand their rights and the enslaved begin to rise — the colony cracks before the great august night
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 2, Les premières insurrections).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** At bois caïman a vodou ceremony seals an oath — days later the northern plain burns; pupils separate the documented rising from the founding legend
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 3, L'insurrection de l'été 1791: la cérémonie du Bois Caïman, entre l'histoire et le mythe).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Insurgent bands under leaders like jean-françois, biassou and the young toussaint hold the hills while empires — french, spanish, british — bid for them
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 4, Les insurrections 1791-1793 (1)).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** War spreads across the colony; allegiances flip as spain arms the insurgents and france's commissioners promise freedom to those who fight for the republic
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 5, Les insurrections 1791-1793 (2)).

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Sonthonax abolishes slavery in saint-domingue in 1793 and paris extends it in 1794 — the formerly enslaved become citizens and soldiers of the republic
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 6, L'émancipation républicaine (1793-1798)).

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Toussaint louverture out-generals the british and his rivals, takes the spanish side of the island, and rules saint-domingue in france's name but by his own hand
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 7, Le pouvoir louverturien (1)).

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Louverture's 1801 constitution makes saint-domingue an autonomous state with himself governor for life — napoleon reads it as rebellion
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 8, Le pouvoir louverturien (2): la Constitution de 1801).

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Leclerc's expedition seizes louverture, but the indigenous army under dessalines wins at vertières; on 1 january 1804 haiti declares independence, and dessalines is assassinated in 1806
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Séance 9, Les luttes pour l'indépendance).

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Pupils build illustrated biographies and mine the founding texts for who counted as a citizen in the new black state
**Answer: True** — as the guide teaches it (Annex document work: biographies of Louverture and Dessalines; the 1804 Declaration of Independence and the 1805 Constitution).
