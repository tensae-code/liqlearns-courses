# The Haitian Revolution, 1791–1804 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The first insurrections.
**Answer: Free people of colour demand their rights and the enslaved begin to rise — the colony cracks before the great august night (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The insurrection of summer 1791 — Bois Caïman.
**Answer: At bois caïman a vodou ceremony seals an oath — days later the northern plain burns; pupils separate the documented rising from the founding legend (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The insurrections 1791–1793 (1).
**Answer: Insurgent bands under leaders like jean-françois, biassou and the young toussaint hold the hills while empires — french, spanish, british — bid for them (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The insurrections 1791–1793 (2).
**Answer: War spreads across the colony; allegiances flip as spain arms the insurgents and france's commissioners promise freedom to those who fight for the republic (any close wording).**

**5. (Sub lesson 5)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Republican emancipation, 1793–1798.
**Answer: Sonthonax abolishes slavery in saint-domingue in 1793 and paris extends it in 1794 — the formerly enslaved become citizens and soldiers of the republic (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 6)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Louverturian power (1).
**Answer: Toussaint louverture out-generals the british and his rivals, takes the spanish side of the island, and rules saint-domingue in france's name but by his own hand (any close wording).**

**7. (Sub lesson 7)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Louverturian power (2) — the Constitution of 1801.
**Answer: Louverture's 1801 constitution makes saint-domingue an autonomous state with himself governor for life — napoleon reads it as rebellion (any close wording).**

**8. (Sub lesson 8)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The struggles for independence.
**Answer: Leclerc's expedition seizes louverture, but the indigenous army under dessalines wins at vertières; on 1 january 1804 haiti declares independence, and dessalines is assassinated in 1806 (any close wording).**

**9. (Sub lesson 9)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Founding lives and founding texts.
**Answer: Pupils build illustrated biographies and mine the founding texts for who counted as a citizen in the new black state (any close wording).**
