# Bahamas — English settlers and enslaved Africans

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: English settlers reach Eleuthera in 1648 and New Providence after, and enslaved Africans are brought to work the islands — arriving direct from Africa, with the Loyalists, by freedom and by shipwreck — carrying Junkanoo, asue, cooking ways and the Anansi and Brer tales that become Bahamian culture. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bahamian History: Eleuthera settlers, enslaved Africans and the freed Africans who chose The Bahamas

English settlers reach Eleuthera in 1648 and bring enslaved Africans to work plantations; much later Africans freed on the way to Cuba choose to settle in The Bahamas instead, carrying Junkanoo, asue, cooking ways and the Anansi and Brer Bookie and Rabbie stories with them (Bahamian History: Eleuthera settlers, enslaved Africans and the freed Africans who chose The Bahamas)
