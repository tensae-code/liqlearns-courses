# Bahamas — English settlers and enslaved Africans — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Bahamian History: Eleuthera settlers, enslaved Africans and the freed Africans who chose The Bahamas  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Bahamian History: Eleuthera settlers, enslaved Africans and the freed Africans who chose The Bahamas** — English settlers reach Eleuthera in 1648 and bring enslaved Africans to work plantations; much later Africans freed on the way to Cuba choose to settle in The Bahamas instead, carrying Junkanoo, asue, cooking ways and the Anansi and Brer Bookie and Rabbie stories with them
