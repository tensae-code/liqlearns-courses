# Bahamas — English settlers and enslaved Africans — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Bahamian History: Eleuthera settlers, enslaved Africans and the freed Africans who chose The Bahamas.
**Answer: English settlers reach Eleuthera in 1648 and bring enslaved Africans to work plantations; much later Africans freed on the way to Cuba choose to settle in The Bahamas instead, carrying Junkanoo, asue, cooking ways and the Anansi and Brer Bookie and Rabbie stories with them (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Lucayans and Eleutheran Adventurers: the Adventurers settle Eleuthera and New Providence.
**Answer: the Eleutheran Adventurers name Eleuthera, struggle to farm, trade wood and salvage wrecks; Captain Sayle moves on to the island that becomes New Providence, and settlements grow on both islands (any close wording).**

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Slavery: how enslaved and free Africans reached The Bahamas.
**Answer: Bahamian slaves arrive by different routes — direct from Africa, with the Loyalists from America, freed in America and settling, or freed by shipwreck — and bring new peoples and traditions with them (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Early Settlers: Adventurers, Loyalists and the enslaved.
**Answer: the Eleutheran Adventurers settle Eleuthera and Captain Sayle moves to New Providence; 1700s Loyalists plant cotton on the Family Islands with enslaved Africans, who win freedom in 1834 — emancipation (any close wording).**
