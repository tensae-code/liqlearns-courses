# Bahamas — English settlers and enslaved Africans — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** English settlers reach Eleuthera in 1648 and bring enslaved Africans to work plantations; much later Africans freed on the way to Cuba choose to settle in The Bahamas instead, carrying Junkanoo, asue, cooking ways and the Anansi and Brer Bookie and Rabbie stories with them
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bahamian History: Eleuthera settlers, enslaved Africans and the freed Africans who chose The Bahamas).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** the Eleutheran Adventurers name Eleuthera, struggle to farm, trade wood and salvage wrecks; Captain Sayle moves on to the island that becomes New Providence, and settlements grow on both islands
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Lucayans and Eleutheran Adventurers: the Adventurers settle Eleuthera and New Providence).

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Bahamian slaves arrive by different routes — direct from Africa, with the Loyalists from America, freed in America and settling, or freed by shipwreck — and bring new peoples and traditions with them
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Slavery: how enslaved and free Africans reached The Bahamas).

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** the Eleutheran Adventurers settle Eleuthera and Captain Sayle moves to New Providence; 1700s Loyalists plant cotton on the Family Islands with enslaved Africans, who win freedom in 1834 — emancipation
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Early Settlers: Adventurers, Loyalists and the enslaved).
