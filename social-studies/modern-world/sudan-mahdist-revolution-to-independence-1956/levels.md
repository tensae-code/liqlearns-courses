## Sub lesson 1 — Sudan — Mahdist revolution to independence, 1956
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Muhammad Ali's campaigns made the Sudan an Egyptian province, and its misrule raised the Mahdist revolution of 1881 under Muhammad Ahmad al-Mahdi; Britain returned with Kitchener's campaign and the Condominium agreement of 1899 made Britain the real ruler, sealing the south off by the closed-districts law; resistance ran from 1903 to the Military College students of 1924 and the parties, until the 1953 self-government agreement and independence in 1956 — and the lesson closes with the coups, the civil wars and the South's secession in 2011.
