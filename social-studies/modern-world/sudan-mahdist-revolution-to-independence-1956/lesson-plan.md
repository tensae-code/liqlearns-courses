# Lesson plan — Sudan — Mahdist revolution to independence, 1956

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Sudan — Mahdist revolution to independence, 1956
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Syria, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** From Muhammad Ali's province through the Mahdist revolution and the Condominium to independence in 1956 — and the long civil wars that split Sudan in 2011.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Sudan — Mahdist revolution to independence, 1956** — Muhammad Ali's campaigns made the Sudan an Egyptian province, and its misrule raised the Mahdist revolution of 1881 under Muhammad Ahmad al-Mahdi; Britain returned with Kitchener's campaign and the Condominium agreement of 1899 made Britain the real ruler, sealing the south off by the closed-districts law; resistance ran from 1903 to the Military College students of 1924 and the parties, until the 1953 self-government agreement and independence in 1956 — and the lesson closes with the coups, the civil wars and the South's secession in 2011.
