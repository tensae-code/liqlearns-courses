# Hands-on — Italy unifies — Cavour and the patriots

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Jigsaw Italy
Draw Italy's outline and cut it into five rough state pieces on paper. Join them one by one as you retell the unification, leaving the Rome piece for last.

## Architect's plan
Cavour was called an architect. Write the three 'building steps' of unification as an architect's plan: foundation (one people), walls (the states joining), roof (the kingdom proclaimed, 1861).
