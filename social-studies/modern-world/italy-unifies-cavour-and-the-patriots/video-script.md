# Video script — Italy unifies — Cavour and the patriots


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Italy — a boot cut into pieces.

Many rulers. Many little states. But one language sings in all of them.

Watch the pieces. The north joins first, guided by a planner — Cavour, the architect.

Piece by piece the boot turns one colour.

1861! A kingdom is proclaimed: the Kingdom of Italy.

One piece still waits at the centre — Rome, the pope's city. It joins last.

A people who shared everything now share a state.
