# Italy unifies — Cavour and the patriots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Before unification, Italy was —
a) One republic  b) Broken into many smaller states  c) A French province  d) Ruled from Vienna as one state
**Answer: b) Broken into many smaller states** — Italy was broken into many smaller states.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Italians of the separate states shared —
a) Only a currency  b) Language, culture, history and religion  c) One king  d) Nothing at all
**Answer: b) Language, culture, history and religion** — They shared the four bonds of one people.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The architect of the united Italy is named as —
a) Bismarck  b) Garibaldi alone  c) Cavour  d) Napoleon
**Answer: c) Cavour** — Cavour is named the architect for his central involvement.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The Kingdom of Italy was proclaimed in —
a) 1848  b) 1861  c) 1871  d) 1889
**Answer: b) 1861** — The kingdom was proclaimed in 1861.

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The new kingdom's north–south problem was —
a) An agrarian north and industrial south  b) Tension between the industrialising north and the agrarian south  c) No problem at all  d) A language split
**Answer: b) Tension between the industrialising north and the agrarian south** — Industrialising north against agrarian south — the exercise corrects the reversed claim.

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** Unification came about through —
a) One battle only  b) Different patriotic approaches working together  c) A foreign gift  d) A church decree
**Answer: b) Different patriotic approaches working together** — Different approaches worked together.
**7. (Sub lesson 3)** In the Grade 12 guide's frame, unification 'from above' means —
a) Orders from the Pope  b) Government measures by Piedmont-Sardinia  c) Foreign invasion  d) Mountain warfare
**Answer: b) Government measures by Piedmont-Sardinia — Cavour's statecraft is the from-above force.**

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Unification 'from below' means —
a) Popular revolt and volunteers  b) Secret treaties  c) Railway building  d) Colonial wars
**Answer: a) Popular revolt and volunteers — Garibaldi's Thousand is the from-below force.**
