# Italy unifies — Cavour and the patriots — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Italy's states each had a different language and religion.
**Answer: False** — They shared language, culture, history and religion.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Cavour's role in unification was central enough to earn him the name 'architect'.
**Answer: True** — The book names him the architect of the united Italy.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The papacy grew stronger inside the new Italian kingdom.
**Answer: False** — The new government inherited a weakened papacy.
**4. (Sub lesson 3)** True or false: The guide instructs that German unification be taught by comparison with Italy.
**Answer: True**
