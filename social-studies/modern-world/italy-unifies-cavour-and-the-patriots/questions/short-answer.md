# Italy unifies — Cavour and the patriots — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why does the book treat Italy as one people before it was one state?
**Answer:** Because the people of its many smaller states shared language, culture, history and religion — nationalism's four bonds.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two problems the new Italian government inherited.
**Answer:** Tension between the industrialising north and the agrarian south, and a weakened papacy.
