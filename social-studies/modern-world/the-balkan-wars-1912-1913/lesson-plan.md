# Lesson plan — The Balkan Wars 1912 1913

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The Balkan Wars 1912 1913
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Greece, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Greece, Serbia, Bulgaria and Montenegro strip Turkey of Europe; the Greek army and navy take Thessaloniki and the Aegean islands, then beat Bulgaria — the kingdom doubles
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Unit E, ch. 3 The Balkan Wars** — Greece, Serbia, Bulgaria and Montenegro strip Turkey of Europe; the Greek army and navy take Thessaloniki and the Aegean islands, then beat Bulgaria — the kingdom doubles
2. **§29 The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)** — Elassona, Giannitsa, Thessaloniki taken a day before the Bulgarians, Bizani, the Aegean fleet — then a second war against Bulgaria
3. **§30 Greece and the Balkans immediately after the Balkan Wars** — the treaties of London and Bucharest double Greece and sow the next war's grievances
4. **The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)** — the Balkan allies crush the Ottomans, then fight each other over Macedonia; Greece nearly doubles its territory
5. **Greece and the Balkans right after the Balkan Wars** — new borders, refugees already moving, and every capital arming for the next round
6. **Balkan relations at the start of the 20th century and the Balkan Wars** — allies against the Ottomans in 1912 become enemies in 1913; the map is redrawn in blood
7. **The Balkan Wars (1912–1913)** — the Balkan League frees Macedonia and Thrace, then Bulgaria attacks its allies and loses; Greece takes Thessaloniki, Ioannina and the islands
