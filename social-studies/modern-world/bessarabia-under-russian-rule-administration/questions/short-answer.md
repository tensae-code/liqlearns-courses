**Answer: tsarism rushes to integrate the annexed province for its Balkan strategy; Bessarabia is folded into Novorossiya and in 1873 made a plain gubernia with no self-rule (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**Answer: Governor Vorontsov presses integration from 1823; colonists found 24 new localities by the early 1840s, Capodistria and Scarlat Sturdza frame the province's first order, and the native peasants' lot is the hardest (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
