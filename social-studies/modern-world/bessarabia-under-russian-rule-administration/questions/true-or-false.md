# Bessarabia Under Russian Rule Administration — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tsarism rushes to integrate the annexed province for its Balkan strategy; Bessarabia is folded into Novorossiya and in 1873 made a plain gubernia with no self-rule.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Lecția 21: Administrarea Basarabiei și a teritoriilor din stânga Nistrului

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a VII-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Lecția 21: Administrarea Basarabiei și a teritoriilor din stânga Nistrului

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Governor Vorontsov presses integration from 1823; colonists found 24 new localities by the early 1840s, Capodistria and Scarlat Sturdza frame the province's first order, and the native peasants' lot is the hardest.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §25: Lichidarea autonomiei Basarabiei

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor şi universală: Epoca modernă. Manual pentru clasa a XI-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §25: Lichidarea autonomiei Basarabiei
