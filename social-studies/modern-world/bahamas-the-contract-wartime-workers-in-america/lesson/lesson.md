# Bahamas — the Contract: wartime workers in America

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In the Second World War, under 'The Contract', Bahamian men and women leave home to work America's emptied farms and factories and send their earnings back to their families. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Contract: Bahamians go to work in wartime America

in the Second World War (1939–1945) American farms and factories empty as men leave for the troops; under "The Contract" Bahamian men and women leave home to work those farms and factories and send their earnings back to their families (The Contract: Bahamians go to work in wartime America)
