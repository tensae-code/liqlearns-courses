# Bahamas — the Contract: wartime workers in America — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The Contract: Bahamians go to work in wartime America  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The Contract: Bahamians go to work in wartime America** — in the Second World War (1939–1945) American farms and factories empty as men leave for the troops; under "The Contract" Bahamian men and women leave home to work those farms and factories and send their earnings back to their families

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) The Contract: Bahamians go to work in wartime America  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) The Contract: Bahamians go to work in wartime America** — the book's own heading for this part.
