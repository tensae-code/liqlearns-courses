# Bahamas — the Contract: wartime workers in America — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in the Second World War (1939–1945) American farms and factories empty as men leave for the troops; under "The Contract" Bahamian men and women leave home to work those farms and factories and send their earnings back to their families
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (The Contract: Bahamians go to work in wartime America).
