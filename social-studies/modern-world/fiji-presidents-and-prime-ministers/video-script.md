# Video script — Fiji's presidents and prime ministers

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Children research Fiji's Presidents (Ganilau, Mara, Iloilo, Nailatikau) and Prime Ministers (Mara, Bavadra, Rabuka, Chaudhry, Qarase) — where each came from, his traditional title, and his years in office. A dated table of Fiji's leaders runs through Qarase (2001-2006), Senilagakali and Bainimarama, teaching leadership change as continuity-and-change.
