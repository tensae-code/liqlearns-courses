# The East After The Cold War Chinas Rise — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** China's reforms make it the world's workshop while Japan stagnates and the Asian tigers roar and stumble.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §18 Eastern countries in the post-Cold-War period

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Համաշխարհային պատմություն. 9-րդ դասարան. Հանրակրթական հիմնական դպրոցի դասագիրք» (World History, Grade 9: textbook for general basic schools) — Aram Nazaryan, Aram Kosyan, Vahram Petrosyan; methodical apparatus by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §18 Eastern countries in the post-Cold-War period

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** China's rise, Japan's stagnation and the Asian tigers redraw the world's economic map eastward.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Theme 4, Lessons 56–57: the countries of the East in the post-Cold War years

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Համաշխարհային պատմություն 9 — Մեթոդական ուղեցույց / Ուսուցչի ձեռնարկ (World History 9, Methodical Guide / Teacher's Manual), by Armine Sargsyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Theme 4, Lessons 56–57: the countries of the East in the post-Cold War years
