# Hands-on — Alba And Its Social Missions

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Alba And Its Social Missions — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Alianza Bolivariana para las Américas (ALBA), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
