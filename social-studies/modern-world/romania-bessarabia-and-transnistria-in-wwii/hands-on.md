# Hands-on — Romania Bessarabia And Transnistria In Wwii

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Romania Bessarabia And Transnistria In Wwii — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Tema 23: România, Basarabia și Transnistria în anii celui de-al Doilea Război Mondial, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
