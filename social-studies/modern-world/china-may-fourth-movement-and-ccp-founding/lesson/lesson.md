# China May Fourth Movement And Ccp Founding

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Versailles gives Shandong to Japan; on 4 May 1919 students march, workers strike, and within two years a dozen communists found a party in Shanghai and on a boat at South Lake As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — May Fourth and the birth of the CCP

Versailles gives Shandong to Japan; on 4 May 1919 students march, workers strike, and within two years a dozen communists found a party in Shanghai and on a boat at South Lake

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
