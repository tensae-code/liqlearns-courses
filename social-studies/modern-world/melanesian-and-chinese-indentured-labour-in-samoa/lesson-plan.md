# Lesson plan — Melanesian and Chinese indentured labour in Sāmoa

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Melanesian and Chinese indentured labour in Sāmoa
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Samoa, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** German plantation companies, finding Sāmoans unwilling, ship in Melanesians as young as fourteen from Bougainville, Malaita and the Bismarcks on three-year contracts, and from March 1903 the DHPG lands 289 Chinese — 2,184 by 1914; New Zealand repatriates 1,254 in 1915–19, restarts the system, then forces married men home away from their Sāmoan families, yet almost 200 settle for good
