# Melanesian and Chinese indentured labour in Sāmoa

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: German plantation companies, finding Sāmoans unwilling, ship in Melanesians as young as fourteen from Bougainville, Malaita and the Bismarcks on three-year contracts, and from March 1903 the DHPG lands 289 Chinese — 2,184 by 1914; New Zealand repatriates 1,254 in 1915–19, restarts the system, then forces married men home away from their Sāmoan families, yet almost 200 settle for good. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
