# Melanesian and Chinese indentured labour in Sāmoa — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Part 1, Unit 3, non-European migrants.
**Answer: German plantation companies, finding Sāmoans unwilling, ship in Melanesians as young as fourteen from Bougainville, Malaita and the Bismarcks on three-year contracts, and from March 1903 the DHPG lands 289 Chinese — 2,184 by 1914; New Zealand repatriates 1,254 in 1915–19, restarts the system, then forces married men home away from their Sāmoan families, yet almost 200 settle for good (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Samoa's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *History, Year 12* (Samoa), Part 1, Unit 3, non-European migrants, printed pp. 28–31.**
