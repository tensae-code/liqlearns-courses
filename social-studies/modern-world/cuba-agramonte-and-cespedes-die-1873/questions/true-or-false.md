# Cuba Agramonte And Cespedes Die 1873 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ignacio Agramonte falls at Jimaguayú in 1873 and Céspedes, deposed by the Chamber, dies alone at San Lorenzo in 1874; the war loses its two great chiefs.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Cuba — Quinto grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 5.8 The war from 1873; deaths of Agramonte and Céspedes
