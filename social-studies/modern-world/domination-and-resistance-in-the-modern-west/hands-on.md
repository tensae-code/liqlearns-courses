# Hands-on — Domination and resistance in the modern West

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Above and underneath
Draw a line across a page. Above it, write the modern West's domination and its three forms from the lesson. Below it, write the three resisting groups and what each did.

## From underneath
Choose one group from the lesson — colonised peoples, enslaved Africans, or the new working classes — and write the chapter's story in three sentences as that group would tell it.
