# Domination and resistance in the modern West — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book's story, domination was always answered by resistance.
**Answer: True — colonised peoples, enslaved Africans and the working classes all pushed back.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The chapter is told from the side of the colonisers only.
**Answer: False — it is told from underneath, from the side of the dominated.**
