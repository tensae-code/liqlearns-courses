# Domination and resistance in the modern West — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The modern West's domination fell on —
a) Colonised peoples, enslaved Africans and the new working classes  b) No one at all  c) Only kings and emperors
**Answer: a) Colonised peoples, enslaved Africans and the new working classes**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Enslaved Africans, in the book's story —
a) Resisted slavery  b) Never resisted  c) Ruled the colonies
**Answer: a) Resisted slavery**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The new working classes answered factory domination by —
a) Organising against it  b) Asking for longer hours  c) Leaving the cities forever
**Answer: a) Organising against it**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** 'Told from underneath' means the story is told —
a) From the side of the colonised, the enslaved and the workers  b) From the palace balcony  c) Only in numbers and tables
**Answer: a) From the side of the colonised, the enslaved and the workers**
