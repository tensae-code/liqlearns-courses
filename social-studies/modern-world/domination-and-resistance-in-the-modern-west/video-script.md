# Video script — Domination and resistance in the modern West

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Brazil's Grade 10 História SEED-PR tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The modern West rose by dominating — colonised lands, enslaved Africans, a new working class under factory discipline.



But domination was never the whole story.



Colonised peoples fought back. Enslaved Africans resisted. Workers organised.



Brazil's book tells this chapter from underneath — from the side of the people who pushed back.
