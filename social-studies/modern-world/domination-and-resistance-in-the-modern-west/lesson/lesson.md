# Domination and resistance in the modern West

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Colonised peoples, enslaved Africans and the new working classes resist the modern West's domination — the story of domination told from underneath. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Domination — the modern West rises

The modern West rose by dominating: lands colonised, peoples ruled, Africans enslaved and carried across the ocean, and at home a new working class held under factory discipline. Domination is the first half of the book's story.

## Sub lesson 2 — Resistance — the answer from underneath

The second half is resistance. Colonised peoples fought their colonisers. Enslaved Africans resisted slavery. The new working classes organised against the domination of the factory and the wage. Wherever domination stood, the book finds people pushing back.

## Sub lesson 3 — The story told from underneath

The book's way is the lesson's point: this chapter tells the earlier chapters' story from underneath — from the side of the colonised, the enslaved and the workers, for whom domination was never the whole story because resistance was always in it.
