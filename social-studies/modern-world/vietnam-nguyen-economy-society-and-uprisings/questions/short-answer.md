# Vietnam Nguyen Economy Society And Uprisings — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Bài 16, the Nguyễn economy and a society in revolt.
**Answer: the court organises reclamation — Nguyễn Công Trứ founds the new districts of Tiền Hải and Kim Sơn in the lower Red River delta — and mining and trade grow, but landlords and strongmen seize the fields, northern floods and droughts recur, and the closed-door policy starves famous towns like Thăng Long, Phố Hiến and Hội An; the people's answer is counted by the quoted history of Phan Huy Lê: from 1802 to 1862 some 405 popular uprisings against the court — Phan Bá Vành's (1821–1827), Lê Duy Lương's (1833–1838), Nông Văn Vân's (1833–1835), Cao Bá Quát's (1854–1856) among them (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Vietnam's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* (Vietnam), Bài 16, the Nguyễn economy and a society in revolt, printed p. 73.**
