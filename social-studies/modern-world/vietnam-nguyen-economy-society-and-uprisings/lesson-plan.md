# Lesson plan — Vietnam Nguyen Economy Society And Uprisings

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Nguyen Economy Society And Uprisings
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the court organises reclamation — Nguyễn Công Trứ founds the new districts of Tiền Hải and Kim Sơn in the lower Red River delta — and mining and trade grow, but landlords and strongmen seize the fields, northern floods and droughts recur, and the closed-door policy starves famous towns like Thăng Long, Phố Hiến and Hội An; the people's answer is counted by the quoted history of Phan Huy Lê: from 1802 to 1862 some 405 popular uprisings against the court — Phan Bá Vành's (1821–1827), Lê Duy Lương's (1833–1838), Nông Văn Vân's (1833–1835), Cao Bá Quát's (1854–1856) among them
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.
