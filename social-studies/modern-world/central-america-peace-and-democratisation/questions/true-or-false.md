# Central America Peace And Democratisation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The presidents' summits and the Esquipulas II accords end the isthmus's wars by negotiation and open democratisation.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Pacificación y democratización en Centroamérica: cumbres presidenciales y Esquipulas II

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Estudios Sociales — Primaria Regular, 5to Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Pacificación y democratización en Centroamérica: cumbres presidenciales y Esquipulas II

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** At Esquipulas in 1987 the five presidents adopt Óscar Arias's plan for firm and lasting peace; Guatemala and El Salvador then negotiate their own wars to an end by 1992.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Esquipulas II y los acuerdos de paz centroamericanos

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Estudios Sociales — Primaria Regular, 6to Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Esquipulas II y los acuerdos de paz centroamericanos

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Contadora group mediates from 1983 without result; Óscar Arias's 1987 presidential conference opens the talks that end the Contra war.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Contadora y Esquipulas: el camino a la paz
