# Women, work and the Second World War

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in the USA, Britain, New Zealand and Australia married women rarely worked for money until the Second World War emptied men's jobs; women fill them, refuse to hand them all back, and by the 1980s are training as accountants, doctors, engineers, lawyers, mechanics, police and pilots. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
