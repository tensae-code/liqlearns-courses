# Video script — Women, work and the Second World War

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Samoa's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

in the USA, Britain, New Zealand and Australia married women rarely worked for money until the Second World War emptied men's jobs; women fill them, refuse to hand them all back, and by the 1980s are training as accountants, doctors, engineers, lawyers, mechanics, police and pilots.
