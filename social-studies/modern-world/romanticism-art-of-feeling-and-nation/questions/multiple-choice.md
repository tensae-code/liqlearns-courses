# Romanticism — art of feeling and nation — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The first part of this story is —
a) Goya and the French romantics  b) A revolt of feeling  c) The voice of nations
**Answer: b) A revolt of feeling** — Reason's age provokes a revolt of feeling.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The second part of this story is —
a) Goya and the French romantics  b) A revolt of feeling  c) The voice of nations
**Answer: a) Goya and the French romantics** — Goya paints war's madness and monsters of sleep, and French romantics fill canvases with shipwrecks, revolutions and oriental dreams.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The third part of this story is —
a) A revolt of feeling  b) Goya and the French romantics  c) The voice of nations
**Answer: c) The voice of nations** — Everywhere art becomes the voice of nations longing to be free.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The one thing this lesson teaches is that —
a) Nothing changed in this story  b) Reason's age provokes a revolt of feeling — Goya paints war's madness and monsters of sleep, French romantics fill canvases with shipwrecks, revolutions and oriental dreams, and everywhere art becomes the voice of nations longing to be free  c) Art and history have nothing to do with each other
**Answer: b)** — the lesson's one thing, as the book teaches it.
