# Romanticism — art of feeling and nation — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Goya paints war's madness and monsters of sleep, and French romantics fill canvases with shipwrecks, revolutions and oriental dreams.
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it in this lesson.

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** This lesson teaches that the story it tells changed nothing at all.
**Answer: False** — Reason's age provokes a revolt of feeling — Goya paints war's madness and monsters of sleep, French romantics fill canvases with shipwrecks, revolutions and oriental dreams, and everywhere art becomes the voice of nations longing to be free.
