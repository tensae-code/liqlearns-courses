# Hands-on — Turkey joins the West — Cold War and NATO

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two faces, one turn

Draw a coin: on one face a ballot box (1950), on the other a NATO star and a ship for Korea. Write around the rim the five years that join them, 1945–1950 and just after.

## The brigade's road

On a rough world map, draw the Turkish brigade's road from home to Korea. Write one sentence on what a country gains, and what it risks, by fighting under an alliance's flag far away.

## Voting the founders out

Write two newspaper front pages for the morning after the 1950 election: one from the winning party's paper, one from the founders' party's. Each must concede what the result proves about the republic.
