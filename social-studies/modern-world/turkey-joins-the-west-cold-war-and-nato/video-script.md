# Video script — Turkey joins the West — Cold War and NATO

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Between 1945 and 1950, Turkey turns twice at once. At home, multi-party life arrives — and in 1950 the voters do the unthinkable peacefully: they vote the founders' own party out, and the Democrat Party wins.

Abroad, the same years anchor Turkey to the West: the Council of Europe in 1949, a Turkish brigade fighting in Korea, and NATO membership.

One turn, two faces — democracy at home, alliance abroad — and the Turkey of the 1950s begins.
