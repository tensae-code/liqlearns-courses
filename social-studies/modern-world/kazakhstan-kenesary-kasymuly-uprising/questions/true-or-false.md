# Kazakhstan Kenesary Kasymuly Uprising — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Kenesary, elected khan, fights a ten-year all-nation war to restore the khanate before falling in the Kyrgyz mountains.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§25–26 The national-liberation movement of 1837–1847 led by Kenesary Kasymuly

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§25–26 The national-liberation movement of 1837–1847 led by Kenesary Kasymuly

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kenesary restores the khan's court and taxes, and fights Russia for a decade.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §§27–28 The national-liberation movement led by Kenesary Kasymov

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *История Казахстана (XVIII–XIX вв.) [History of Kazakhstan (18th–19th centuries)]* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §§27–28 The national-liberation movement led by Kenesary Kasymov

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** all three zhuzes answer Kenesary's call — the last khan's war becomes everyone's war.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §4.5 Why did the national-liberation struggle led by Kenesary Khan take an all-national character?
