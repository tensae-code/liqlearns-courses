# Antebellum reform and abolition, 1820–1860 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The reform generation was fired by —
a) The cotton gin  b) The Second Great Awakening  c) The Mexican-American War
**Answer: b) The Second Great Awakening** — the Second Great Awakening fires a generation of reformers.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Utopian communes were —
a) Army camps  b) Attempts to live a perfect society now  c) Prisons
**Answer: b) Attempts to live a perfect society now** — utopian communes tried to live perfection now.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** Temperance campaigned against —
a) Drink  b) Farming  c) Voting
**Answer: a) Drink** — temperance — the campaign against drink.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Seneca Falls (1848) made the first organised demand for —
a) Lower taxes  b) Women's rights  c) A national bank
**Answer: b) Women's rights** — the first organised demand for women's rights.
