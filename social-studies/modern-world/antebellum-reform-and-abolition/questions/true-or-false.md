# Antebellum reform and abolition, 1820–1860 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Abolition was one of the antebellum reform movements.
**Answer: True** — The abolitionist assault on slavery stands among the reforms.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Antebellum reformers all agreed to leave society exactly as it was.
**Answer: False** — Each movement refused to accept the world as it was.
