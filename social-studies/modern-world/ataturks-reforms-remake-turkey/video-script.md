# Video script — Atatürk's reforms remake Turkey

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Turkey Grade 12 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The Turkish Revolution explains itself in six words: republicanism, nationalism, populism, statism, secularism, revolutionism. Then it rebuilds the state to match — the sultanate abolished in 1922, the republic proclaimed, the caliphate abolished.

The second wave reaches daily life: education unified, a new alphabet, calendar, clock and measures changed, surnames for every citizen in 1934 — and rights for women, up to the ballot.

And the third wave is economic: the İzmir Congress of 1923 warns that political independence must be paid for with economic independence.
