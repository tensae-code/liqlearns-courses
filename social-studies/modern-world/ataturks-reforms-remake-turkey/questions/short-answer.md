# Atatürk's reforms remake Turkey — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the six principles.
**Answer: Republicanism, nationalism, populism, statism, secularism and revolutionism (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name four social reforms of the revolution.
**Answer: Any four of: unification of education; alphabet reform; language reform; calendar, clock and measures changed; the Surname Law; rights given to women (any close wording).**
