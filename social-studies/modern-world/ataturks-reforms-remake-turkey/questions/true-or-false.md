# Atatürk's reforms remake Turkey — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** The reforms changed the calendar, clock and measures.
**Answer: True** — daily life remade to the common reckoning.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The caliphate was abolished before the sultanate.
**Answer: False** — the sultanate goes first (1 November 1922); the caliphate follows the republic's proclamation.
