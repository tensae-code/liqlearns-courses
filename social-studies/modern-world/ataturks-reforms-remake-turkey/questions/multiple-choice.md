# Atatürk's reforms remake Turkey — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The six principles number —
a) Four  b) Six  c) Eight
**Answer: b) Six** — the six arrows of Atatürkism.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The sultanate was abolished on —
a) 1 November 1922  b) 24 July 1923  c) 21 June 1934
**Answer: a) 1 November 1922** — before the republic's proclamation.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The Surname Law is dated —
a) 1923  b) 21 June 1934  c) 1938
**Answer: b) 21 June 1934** — the law that named Atatürk.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The İzmir Economic Congress taught that —
a) Trade should stop  b) Political independence must be paid for with economic independence  c) Industry is unnecessary
**Answer: b) Political independence must be paid for with economic independence** — the third wave's beat.
