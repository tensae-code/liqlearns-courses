# Hands-on — Atatürk's reforms remake Turkey

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Six arrows

Draw the six principles as six arrows on one bow, each labelled. Under each, write one reform from this lesson that the arrow could have aimed.

## One room, remade

Draw an ordinary room twice — 1922 and 1935: the clock, the calendar, the letters on a book's spine, the name on the door. Label every change the reforms made in that one room.

## The congress speaks

Write the İzmir Congress's message as a five-line speech: what was won in war, what must now be built, and the warning about a flag on loan.
