**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Atatürk's reforms remake Turkey
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Turkey, 2026-11-15).

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The six principles, and the state rebuilt** — Unit 3, Atatürkism and the Turkish Revolution (principles and political part): the six principles (republicanism, nationalism, populism, statism, secularism, revolutionism); the sultanate abolished (1 November 1922), the republic proclaimed, the caliphate abolished.
2. **Education, letters, clocks and names — the social revolution** — Unit 3 (education, culture and social part): the unification of education, the alphabet and language reforms, the calendar, clock and measures changed, the Surname Law (21 June 1934) and the rights given to women.
3. **Economic independence — the İzmir Congress and the work of building** — Unit 3 (economy and health part): the İzmir Economic Congress (1923) and the work in agriculture, trade, industry, maritime affairs and health.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Name the six principles: republicanism, nationalism, populism, statism, secularism, revolutionism.
2. Tell the political steps in order: sultanate abolished (1 November 1922); republic proclaimed; caliphate abolished.
3. Name the social reforms: unified education, alphabet and language reforms, calendar/clock/measures, the Surname Law (21 June 1934), rights for women.
4. Say what the İzmir Economic Congress (1923) declared: political independence must be paid for with economic independence.
