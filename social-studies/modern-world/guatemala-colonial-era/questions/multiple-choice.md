# Guatemala — the colonial era — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Santiago was first founded at —
a) Iximche' in 1524  b) Panchoy in 1773  c) Antigua in 1960
**Answer: a) Iximche' in 1524** — its first home.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Santa Marta earthquakes struck in —
a) 1773  b) 1541  c) 1524
**Answer: a) 1773** — the year of the last move.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The repartimiento was —
a) a weekly labour draft  b) a kind of coin  c) a university
**Answer: a) a weekly labour draft** — the forced-labour system.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Until the 1733 mint, coins in the colony could be —
a) cacao beans  b) paper notes  c) jade beads only
**Answer: a) cacao beans** — the money the book names.
