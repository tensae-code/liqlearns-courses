# Karl Marx and the idea of communism

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Marx, watching industrial Britain's rich-poor gap from London exile, casts history as class warfare — capitalists against proletariat — ending in revolution, a transitional socialism, and a classless communism where the state withers away; Lenin, whose brother was executed for plotting against the Tsar, turns the theory into a disciplined Bolshevik party. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
