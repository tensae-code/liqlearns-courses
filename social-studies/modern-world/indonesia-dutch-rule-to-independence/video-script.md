# Video script — Indonesia — Dutch rule to independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

three centuries from VOC traders to the Dutch East Indies (1799) and the Cultivation System; the 1908 awakening, the 1926–27 revolts and the 1928 Youth Pledge forge a nation before Sukarno proclaims it.

17,508 islands and 300 peoples (185 million in 1990) were ruled as one colony from the VOC's 17th-century factories to the Dutch East Indies — the largest country of the region made by empire.
