# Nepal — unification and the Anglo-Nepalese War heroes — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Our Heroes and Heroines

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *My Social Studies and Creative Arts, Grade 3* is about The Past of My Family and Neighbours.
**Answer: False** — it is about Our Heroes and Heroines
