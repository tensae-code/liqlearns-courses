# Melaka falls to the Portuguese, 1511 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) The end of Melaka-centred rule  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) The end of Melaka-centred rule** — the Portuguese take Melaka in 1511; Sultan Mahmud Shah retreats through his dependencies to Bentan, attacks three times between 1515 and 1519 to win Melaka back, loses Bentan to Portuguese destruction in 1526 and withdraws to Kampar — the sultanate ends in Melaka but not in exile

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In the book this part is —
a) Bab 5.3: the end of Melaka-centred rule  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 5.3: the end of Melaka-centred rule** — the book's own heading for this part.
