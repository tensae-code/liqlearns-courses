# Melaka falls to the Portuguese, 1511 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: The end of Melaka-centred rule.
**Answer: the Portuguese take Melaka in 1511; Sultan Mahmud Shah retreats through his dependencies to Bentan, attacks three times between 1515 and 1519 to win Melaka back, loses Bentan to Portuguese destruction in 1526 and withdraws to Kampar — the sultanate ends in Melaka but not in exile (any close wording).**
