# Melaka falls to the Portuguese, 1511 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Portuguese take Melaka in 1511; Sultan Mahmud Shah retreats through his dependencies to Bentan, attacks three times between 1515 and 1519 to win Melaka back, loses Bentan to Portuguese destruction in 1526 and withdraws to Kampar — the sultanate ends in Melaka but not in exile
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 5.3: the end of Melaka-centred rule).
