# Lesson plan — Melaka falls to the Portuguese, 1511

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Melaka falls to the Portuguese, 1511
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Malaysia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** In 1511 the Portuguese take Melaka; Sultan Mahmud Shah retreats, attacks three times to win it back, and loses Bentan in 1526 — the sultanate ends in Melaka but not in exile
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. ## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:
