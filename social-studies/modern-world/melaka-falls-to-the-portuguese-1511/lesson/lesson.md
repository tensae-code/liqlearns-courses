# Melaka falls to the Portuguese, 1511

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: In 1511 the Portuguese take Melaka; Sultan Mahmud Shah retreats, attacks three times to win it back, and loses Bentan in 1526 — the sultanate ends in Melaka but not in exile. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The end of Melaka-centred rule

The Portuguese take Melaka in 1511; Sultan Mahmud Shah retreats through his dependencies to Bentan, attacks three times between 1515 and 1519 to win Melaka back, loses Bentan to Portuguese destruction in 1526 and withdraws to Kampar — the sultanate ends in Melaka but not in exile (Bab 5.3: the end of Melaka-centred rule)
