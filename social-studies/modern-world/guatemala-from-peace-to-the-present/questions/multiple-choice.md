# Guatemala — from peace to the present — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Arzú signed the peace and —
a) privatised state firms  b) banned unions  c) annexed Belize
**Answer: a) privatised state firms** — his other policy.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The hurricanes named are —
a) Mitch, Stan and Agatha  b) Katrina and Sandy  c) only Mitch
**Answer: a) Mitch, Stan and Agatha** — the storms.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** Successors after Arzú include —
a) Portillo, Berger, Colom and Pérez Molina  b) Carrera and Barrios  c) Arévalo and Arbenz
**Answer: a) Portillo, Berger, Colom and Pérez Molina** — the later presidents.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Later governments are measured against —
a) the still-unfinished promises of 1996  b) the Soto-Keith contract  c) the 1821 Act only
**Answer: a) the still-unfinished promises of 1996** — the peace standard.
