# Colombia's constitutions — Núñez, the Regeneración and 1991

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Colombia rebuilds its state on paper again and again — Rionegro 1863, Núñez and 1886, the 1936 reform, and the 1991 charter born of a student-led Constituent Assembly. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
