# 1918 The Great Union And Bessarabia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** In 1918 the Sfatul Țării under Ion Inculeț votes Bessarabia's union with Romania, completing the Great Union of all Romanians.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 15: Anul 1918 în istorie

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 15: Anul 1918 în istorie

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** 1918, Romania's astral hour — Bessarabia, Bukovina under Iancu Flondor's Cernăuți assembly, and Transylvania with its national councils and guards unite with the Old Kingdom as two empires vanish.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 4: Formarea Statului Național Unitar Român. Recunoașterea Marii Uniri
