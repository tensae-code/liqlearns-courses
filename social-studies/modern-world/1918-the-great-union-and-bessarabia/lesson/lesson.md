# 1918 The Great Union And Bessarabia

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: in 1918 the Sfatul Țării under Ion Inculeț votes Bessarabia's union with Romania, completing the Great Union of all Romanians As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
