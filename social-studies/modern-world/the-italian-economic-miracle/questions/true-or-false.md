# The Italian Economic Miracle — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** fridges (Paris' Storiografia makes one the boom's symbol), televisions (Alfieri) and internal migrations from South to North remake Italy in a decade; Barbagallo's Cassa per il Mezzogiorno (unit Storiografia) measures the South's half-share.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 5, Cap. 19 §3 The economic miracle

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 5, Cap. 19 §3 The economic miracle

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** motorways, mass media and the robotic factory (Focus boxes) remake Italy; the miracle's geography — North industrialising, South emigrating — is its permanent question.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 13 §6 Il «miracolo economico»

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 3 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dal XX al XXI secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Modulo 13 §6 Il «miracolo economico»
