# Jesuits Inquisition And The Witch Hunts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Ignatius' Jesuits carry schools and missions from Europe to South America while the Roman Inquisition, the Index and the witch-hunts police belief at home.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The Catholic strategy for the reconquest of souls; Counter-Reformation and the repression of heresies

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 1: Dall'anno Mille al Seicento (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The Catholic strategy for the reconquest of souls; Counter-Reformation and the repression of heresies

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Roman Inquisition, the Index of forbidden books and censorship police the printed word the press had set free.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Inquisizione e censura

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lo spazio del tempo 1 — Storia Documenti Storiografia dall'XI al XVII secolo (Andrea Giardina, Giovanni Sabbatucci, Vittorio Vidotto)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Inquisizione e censura
