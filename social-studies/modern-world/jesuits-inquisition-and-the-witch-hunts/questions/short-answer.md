Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unità 5, Cap. 16 §§2–3 The Catholic strategy for the reconquest of souls; Counter-Reformation and the repression of heresies.
**Answer: Ignatius' Jesuits carry schools and missions from Europe to South America while the Roman Inquisition, the Index and the witch-hunts police belief at home (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
