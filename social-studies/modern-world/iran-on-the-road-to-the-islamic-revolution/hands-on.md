# Hands-on — Iran — on the road to the Islamic Revolution

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Iran — on the road to the Islamic Revolution — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Iran on the path of the Islamic Revolution, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
