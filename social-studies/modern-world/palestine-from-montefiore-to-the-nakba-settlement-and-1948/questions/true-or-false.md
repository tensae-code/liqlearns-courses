# Palestine from Montefiore to the Nakba — settlement and 1948 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Zionist movement plants its first settlement, Montefiore, south-west of Jerusalem's wall in 1856, and the settlements double as training camps and arms stores; after the 1948 Nakba the occupation holds over 78% of Palestine.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 1 Lesson 1: partition inside Palestine

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف التاسع الأساسي، الجزء الأول (Social Studies, Grade 9 Basic, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 1 Lesson 1: partition inside Palestine

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The lesson names 1948 the great racist catastrophe: armed Zionist gangs occupy 77% of Palestine, commit massacres, destroy 531 Palestinian villages and drive about a million Palestinians into refuge in what remains of Palestine and in Jordan, Syria, Lebanon and the wider diaspora; the rest of Palestine is occupied in June 1967.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 6 Lesson 2: racial discrimination — the 1948 catastrophe

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *الدراسات الاجتماعية — الصف التاسع الأساسي، الجزء الثاني (Social Studies, Grade 9 Basic, Part 2)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 6 Lesson 2: racial discrimination — the 1948 catastrophe

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** The Zionist idea crystallises in Europe (Shaftesbury's "a land without a people" slogan dissected and refuted in class), the colonial powers back it for their own ends, Herzl organises the movement and the Basel Congress of 1897 resolves on a Jewish national home in Palestine — while Sultan Abdul Hamid II refuses to sell "even one foot" of an empire bought with his people's blood.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2 Lesson 3: colonialism and Zionism

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *جغرافية فلسطين وتاريخها الحديث والمعاصر — الصف العاشر الأساسي، الجزء الأول (Geography of Palestine and its Modern and Contemporary History, Grade 10 Basic, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 2 Lesson 3: colonialism and Zionism

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** The machinery is built — Jewish Colonial Trust (1899), Histadrut, the kibbutz — and the first settlements rise: a Jewish quarter outside Jerusalem's walls (1857), Petah Tikva on the lands of Mlabbis (1882), Zikhron Ya'akov on Zamarin; land is got by purchase and forged deeds through corrupt officials and consulates, the empire answers with the 1883 waqf law and the 1887 "red passport" three-month rule, and Palestinians answer in print — Ruhi al-Khalidi's History of Zionism and the papers al-Karmil and al-Manar.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2 Lesson 4: Zionist immigration and settlement colonisation in the late Ottoman era

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *جغرافية فلسطين وتاريخها الحديث والمعاصر — الصف العاشر الأساسي، الجزء الأول (Geography of Palestine and its Modern and Contemporary History, Grade 10 Basic, Part 1)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 2 Lesson 4: Zionist immigration and settlement colonisation in the late Ottoman era
