# Colombia's peoples — Indigenous, Afro-Colombian, Rrom and mestizo

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Colombia is Indigenous, Afro-Colombian, Raizal, Palenquero and Rrom at once — a mestizo people born of their meeting, recognised in the 1991 Constitution. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
