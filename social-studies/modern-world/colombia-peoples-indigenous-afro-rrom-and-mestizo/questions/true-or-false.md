# Colombia's peoples — Indigenous, Afro-Colombian, Rrom and mestizo — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Indigenous peoples, Black/Afro-Colombian, Raizal and Palenquero communities, and the Rrom (Gitano) people are presented one by one, and Colombia is then named a mestizo people born of their meeting
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Guía 11: Colombia's ethnic and cultural wealth

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Ciencias Sociales — Segunda Cartilla, Grado Segundo, Escuela Nueva* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Guía 11: Colombia's ethnic and cultural wealth
