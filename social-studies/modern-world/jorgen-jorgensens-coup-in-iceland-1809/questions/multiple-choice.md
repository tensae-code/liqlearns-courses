# Jørgen Jørgensen's coup in Iceland, 1809 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jørgen Jørgensen seized Iceland in —
a) 1809  b) 1703  c) 1918
**Answer: a) 1809** — the 1809 coup.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** On seizing Iceland, Jørgensen proclaimed it —
a) Independent  b) Part of Britain  c) A republic of farmers
**Answer: a) Independent** — he proclaims it independent.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Jørgensen's rule lasted —
a) Fifty years  b) Only months — he was removed  c) Until 1944
**Answer: b) Only months — he was removed** — removed within months.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Jørgensen is described in the book as —
a) A king  b) An adventurer  c) A farmer
**Answer: b) An adventurer** — an adventurer seizes Iceland.
