# Jørgen Jørgensen's coup in Iceland, 1809 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Jørgen Jørgensen was Iceland's elected leader in 1809.
**Answer: False** — he was an adventurer who seized the country.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The independence Jørgensen proclaimed lasted for generations.
**Answer: False** — he was removed within months.
