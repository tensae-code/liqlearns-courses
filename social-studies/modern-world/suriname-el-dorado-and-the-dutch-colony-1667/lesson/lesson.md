# Suriname — El Dorado and the Dutch colony, 1667

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Spaniards chase the golden king El Dorado by Lake Parima and leave the "Wild Coast" disappointed; around 1650 Willoughby's English plant tobacco and sugar and build Fort Willoughby, until Abraham Crijnssen's Zeeland fleet takes Suriname in 1667 and the fort becomes Fort Zeelandia As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — El Dorado, Willoughby and Crijnssen

Spaniards chase the golden king El Dorado by Lake Parima and leave the "Wild Coast" disappointed; around 1650 Willoughby's English plant tobacco and sugar and build Fort Willoughby, until Abraham Crijnssen's Zeeland fleet takes Suriname in 1667 and the fort becomes Fort Zeelandia (El Dorado, Willoughby and Crijnssen)
