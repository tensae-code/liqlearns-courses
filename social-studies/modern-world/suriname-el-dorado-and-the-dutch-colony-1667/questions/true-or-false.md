# Suriname — El Dorado and the Dutch colony, 1667 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spaniards chase the golden king El Dorado by Lake Parima and leave the "Wild Coast" disappointed; around 1650 Willoughby's English plant tobacco and sugar and build Fort Willoughby, until Abraham Crijnssen's Zeeland fleet takes Suriname in 1667 and the fort becomes Fort Zeelandia.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in El Dorado, Willoughby and Crijnssen

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Grade 6 Geschiedenis* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about El Dorado, Willoughby and Crijnssen

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Columbus coasts Venezuela on his third voyage but never lands in Suriname; later Spaniards pushing up the Amazon are shot at with poisoned arrows and chase golden legends along the Wild Coast.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Europeanen in de Guyana's

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Europeanen in de Guyana's

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** In the first half of the 1600s English settlers lay out some 60 plantations shipping tobacco and sugar to England, making Suriname an English plantation colony.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Engelsen koloniseren ons land

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Engelsen koloniseren ons land

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** In the Second Anglo-Dutch War Crijnssen takes Suriname (1667); the West India Company rules it, and in 1683 Cornelis van Aerssen van Sommelsdijck becomes governor.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ons land wordt een Nederlandse kolonie

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Grade 7 Wij en ons verleden* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ons land wordt een Nederlandse kolonie
