## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **El Dorado, Willoughby and Crijnssen** — Spaniards chase the golden king El Dorado by Lake Parima and leave the "Wild Coast" disappointed; around 1650 Willoughby's English plant tobacco and sugar and build Fort Willoughby, until Abraham Crijnssen's Zeeland fleet takes Suriname in 1667 and the fort becomes Fort Zeelandia.
2. **Europeanen in de Guyana's** — Columbus coasts Venezuela on his third voyage but never lands in Suriname; later Spaniards pushing up the Amazon are shot at with poisoned arrows and chase golden legends along the Wild Coast.
3. **Engelsen koloniseren ons land** — In the first half of the 1600s English settlers lay out some 60 plantations shipping tobacco and sugar to England, making Suriname an English plantation colony.
4. **Ons land wordt een Nederlandse kolonie** — In the Second Anglo-Dutch War Crijnssen takes Suriname (1667); the West India Company rules it, and in 1683 Cornelis van Aerssen van Sommelsdijck becomes governor.
