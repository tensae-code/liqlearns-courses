# Suriname — El Dorado and the Dutch colony, 1667 — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — El Dorado, Willoughby and Crijnssen
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Spaniards chase the golden king El Dorado by Lake Parima and leave the "Wild Coast" disappointed; around 1650 Willoughby's English plant tobacco and sugar and build Fort Willoughby, until Abraham Crijnssen's Zeeland fleet takes Suriname in 1667 and the fort becomes Fort Zeelandia.

## Sub lesson 2 — Europeanen in de Guyana's
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Columbus coasts Venezuela on his third voyage but never lands in Suriname; later Spaniards pushing up the Amazon are shot at with poisoned arrows and chase golden legends along the Wild Coast.

## Sub lesson 3 — Engelsen koloniseren ons land
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — In the first half of the 1600s English settlers lay out some 60 plantations shipping tobacco and sugar to England, making Suriname an English plantation colony.

## Sub lesson 4 — Ons land wordt een Nederlandse kolonie
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — In the Second Anglo-Dutch War Crijnssen takes Suriname (1667); the West India Company rules it, and in 1683 Cornelis van Aerssen van Sommelsdijck becomes governor.
