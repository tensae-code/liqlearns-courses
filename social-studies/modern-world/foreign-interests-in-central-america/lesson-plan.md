## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Intereses ingleses y norteamericanos en Centroamérica** — Britain and the United States circle the isthmus for trade routes and a canal, treating its republics as a corridor
2. **Intervenciones militares de Estados Unidos en América Latina, siglos XIX y XX** — US military interventions repeat across Latin America for two centuries, a pattern each republic meets in turn
3. **Tratado Clayton-Bulwer** — in 1850 the US and Britain sign over Central America's head, agreeing neither will exclusively control an isthmian canal
4. **Pretensiones extranjeras en Nicaragua** — coveted since colonial times for its lake-and-river canal route, Nicaragua becomes urgent after California gold in 1848, with Tehuantepec, Nicaragua and Panama competing as transit routes
5. **Pretensiones extranjeras en Nicaragua** — California gold makes a canal urgent; the Monroe Doctrine warns Europe off while the US and Britain sign the Clayton-Bulwer Treaty of 1850 over Nicaragua's head, ignoring its sovereignty
