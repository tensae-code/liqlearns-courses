# Foreign Interests In Central America

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Britain and the United States circle the isthmus for trade routes and a canal, treating its republics as a corridor As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
