# Hands-on — Micronesia — the Trust Territory's early years

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two masters, one council
Role-play a district congress meeting that must pass one rule — say, a school attendance rule — in a way both a chief and an American administrator would accept. Afterwards, list what each side conceded.

## Trustee or ruler?
Make two columns from this lesson: 'signs of trust' (rebuilding, councils, congresses) and 'signs of rule' (foreign flag, thirty years, outside demands). Place each fact, then give your verdict in one sentence.
