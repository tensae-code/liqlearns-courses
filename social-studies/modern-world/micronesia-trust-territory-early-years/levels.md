# Micronesia — the Trust Territory's early years — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the books teach them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The US Navy rebuilds — and the Trust Territory begins, 1947
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that the US Navy rebuilt the war-torn islands, repatriation reshaped villages, and in 1947 the UN Trust Territory began with municipalities, magistrates and district congresses.

## Sub lesson 2 — From municipalities to the Congress of Micronesia
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that in the 1940s–60s islanders built municipalities and district congresses that had to respect traditional leadership and satisfy American demands for democracy at the same time.

## Sub lesson 3 — Kapingamarangi: the beginning of the American time
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that after Japan's defeat the Americans took charge of Kapingamarangi, and the atoll entered the Trust Territory world of municipalities and elected councils.
