# Micronesia — the Trust Territory's early years — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The UN Trust Territory began in —
a) 1914  b) 1947  c) 1979
**Answer: b) 1947** — the year the book gives.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Who rebuilt the war-torn islands first?
a) The US Navy  b) The Japanese Mandate  c) The Congress of Micronesia
**Answer: a) The US Navy** — the rebuilders the book names.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The new municipalities and district congresses had to respect traditional leadership and —
a) Satisfy American demands for democracy  b) Restore Japanese rule  c) Abolish the chiefs
**Answer: a) Satisfy American demands for democracy** — the double demand the government book teaches.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** On Kapingamarangi the Americans are called —
a) Baalangi  b) Wareai  c) Ariki
**Answer: a) Baalangi** — the name in the atoll's reader, as filed.
