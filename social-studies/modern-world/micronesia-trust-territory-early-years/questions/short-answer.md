# Micronesia — the Trust Territory's early years — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** What double demand did the new island governments have to meet?
**Answer: They had to respect traditional leadership and satisfy American demands for democracy at the same time (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What changed in 1947, and what did not change?
**Answer: The islands became a UN Trust Territory with municipalities, magistrates and district congresses — but foreign rule itself continued for thirty more years (any close wording).**
