# Micronesia — the Trust Territory's early years — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Trust Territory ended foreign rule in Micronesia in 1947.
**Answer: False** — 1947 began thirty more years of foreign rule — now as a UN trust, with local municipalities and congresses.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** The Congress of Micronesia grew out of the district congresses.
**Answer: True** — the government book traces the line from municipalities and district congresses to the Congress of Micronesia.
