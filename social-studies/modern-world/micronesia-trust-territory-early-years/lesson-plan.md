# Lesson plan — Micronesia — the Trust Territory's early years

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Micronesia — the Trust Territory's early years
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Micronesia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** After the war the US Navy rebuilt the war-torn islands, repatriation reshaped villages, and in 1947 the UN Trust Territory began thirty more years of foreign rule — now with municipalities, magistrates and district congresses through which islanders built municipalities and a Congress of Micronesia that had to respect traditional leadership and satisfy American demands for democracy at the same time.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on the story itself, told in order, part by part, as Micronesia's books tell it. It needs nothing else first.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The US Navy rebuilds — and the Trust Territory begins, 1947** — say that the US Navy rebuilt the war-torn islands, repatriation reshaped villages, and in 1947 the UN Trust Territory began with municipalities, magistrates and district congresses.
2. **From municipalities to the Congress of Micronesia** — say that in the 1940s–60s islanders built municipalities and district congresses that had to respect traditional leadership and satisfy American demands for democracy at the same time.
3. **Kapingamarangi: the beginning of the American time** — say that after Japan's defeat the Americans took charge of Kapingamarangi, and the atoll entered the Trust Territory world of municipalities and elected councils.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
