# Video script — The Atlantic world — rival empires and faiths

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told straight, in the order it happened. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Spain claimed the new Atlantic world alone. Then Europe's churches split — and the split sailed across the ocean with rival flags behind it.

The Reformation split Europe's churches into rival camps. Religion was no longer one shared faith but a contest — and contests travel. What divided Europe at home would soon divide its colonies abroad.

Dutch, French and English challengers carried the contest across the Atlantic. They raided Spanish treasure, traded where Spain forbade trade, and planted colonies in the world Spain claimed alone.

Spain's claim to rule the Atlantic world by itself could not hold. Rival empires and rival faiths made the Atlantic a crowded, contested space — the world the later English colonies would be born into.
