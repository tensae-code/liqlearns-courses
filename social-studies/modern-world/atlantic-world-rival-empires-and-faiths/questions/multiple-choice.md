# The Atlantic world — rival empires and faiths — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Reformation did what to Europe's churches?
a) United them under one pope  b) Split them into rival camps  c) Ended religion in Europe
**Answer: b) Split them into rival camps** — the Reformation splits Europe's churches.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which peoples challenged Spain in the Atlantic?
a) Only the Portuguese  b) Dutch, French and English challengers  c) No one — Spain was unchallenged
**Answer: b) Dutch, French and English challengers** — Dutch, French and English challengers raided, traded and planted colonies.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The challengers did all of these EXCEPT —
a) Raid Spanish shipping  b) Trade and plant colonies  c) Accept Spain's sole claim forever
**Answer: c) Accept Spain's sole claim forever** — they challenged Spain's supremacy, not accepted it.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Spain had claimed the Atlantic world —
a) To share equally  b) Alone, for itself  c) Only for one year
**Answer: b) Alone, for itself** — the world Spain claimed alone.
