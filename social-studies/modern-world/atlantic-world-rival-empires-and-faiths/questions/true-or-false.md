# The Atlantic world — rival empires and faiths — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The religious split in Europe stayed in Europe and never crossed the Atlantic.
**Answer: False** — The split crosses the ocean with the rival empires.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Dutch, French and English challengers planted colonies in lands Spain claimed.
**Answer: True** — They raided, traded and planted colonies in the world Spain claimed alone.
