# Hands-on — The Bourbon reforms — restructuring the colony

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Bourbon reforms — restructuring the colony — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Reformas borbónicas, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
