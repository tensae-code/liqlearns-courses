# Hands-on — Bangabandhu and the road to 1971

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Milestone road

Draw Mujib's road with four milestones — 1966, 1969, 1970, 7 March — and one line under each saying what happened.

## By water

Write two sentences on why a leader of a river land travels by water to reach its people.

## Early road

Add three earlier stops to your milestone road: 1920 Tungipara, 1948 the Urdu-only fight, February 1952 the hunger strike.

## Why language first?

Write two sentences: why would a fight over which language the state speaks come before a fight for a state itself?

## Ideals chart

Write Mujib's two ideals from the lesson and beside each, one everyday example a pupil could act on.

## A case study

Write a short case study of your own — a person you know who put others or their community before themselves — in the Mela's spirit.

## Road's opening

Mark December 1947 as the first stop on your milestone road, before 1948 — and write why the book puts a language protest first.

## Dutta's defiance

Write the two-sentence speech you imagine Dutta giving: what he demands, and for whom.
