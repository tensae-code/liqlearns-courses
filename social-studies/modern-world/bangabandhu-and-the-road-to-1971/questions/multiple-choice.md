# Bangabandhu and the road to 1971 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 6-Point movement was in —
a) 1952  b) 1966  c) 1971
**Answer: b) 1966** — the 6 Points' year.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Mujib was born in —
a) Dhaka, 1920  b) Tungipara, 1920  c) Tungipara, 1947
**Answer: b) Tungipara, 1920** — birthplace and year.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In February 1952, in prison, Mujib —
a) Wrote a book  b) Hunger-struck as the Language Movement ignited  c) Left the country
**Answer: b) Hunger-struck as the Language Movement ignited** — the hunger strike.

**4. (Sub lesson 4)** Dhaka University students began protesting the Urdu-only policy in —
a) December 1947  b) March 1971  c) January 1972
**Answer: a) December 1947** — the protests' start.
