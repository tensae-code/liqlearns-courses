# Bangabandhu and the road to 1971 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 9 book roots Mujib's ideals in Bengal's own thousand-year humanitarian tradition.
**Answer: True** — the book traces them to Bengal itself.

**2. (Sub lesson 4)** Dhirendranath Dutta supported imposing Urdu as the only state language.
**Answer: False** — he defied the Constituent Assembly against it.
