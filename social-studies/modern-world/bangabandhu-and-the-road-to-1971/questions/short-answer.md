# Bangabandhu and the road to 1971 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** List the milestones of Mujib's road from 1966 to 7 March.
**Answer: The 6-Point movement of 1966, the mass uprising of 1969, the 1970 election victory, and the 7 March declaration of the struggle for emancipation (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What two ideals does the Grade 9 book give Mujib?
**Answer: Love for all people regardless of language, religion or caste, and nation before self (any close wording).**
