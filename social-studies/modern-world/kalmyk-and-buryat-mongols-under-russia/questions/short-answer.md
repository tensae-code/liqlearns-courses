# Kalmyk and Buryat Mongols under Russia — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Ch. I.2 Russia dominates Kalmyk and Buryat Mongolia.
**Answer: Torghuts who migrated to the Volga become Russia's Kalmyks (and in 1771 half perish returning); Buryats are cut off by the 1689 and 1727 treaties — Mongols under a second empire (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Mongolia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *mn-g11mh Монголын түүх XI (History of Mongolia XI)* (Mongolia), Ch. I.2 Russia dominates Kalmyk and Buryat Mongolia, printed p. 10.**
