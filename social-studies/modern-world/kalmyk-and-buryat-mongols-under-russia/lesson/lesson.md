# Kalmyk and Buryat Mongols under Russia

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Torghuts who migrated to the Volga become Russia's Kalmyks (and in 1771 half perish returning); Buryats are cut off by the 1689 and 1727 treaties — Mongols under a second empire. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
