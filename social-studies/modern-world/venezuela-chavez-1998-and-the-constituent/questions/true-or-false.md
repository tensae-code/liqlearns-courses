# Venezuela Chavez 1998 And The Constituent — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Amid economic collapse Hugo Chávez wins in 1998, takes office in February 1999 and a Constituent Assembly refounds the republic in the headlines of that year.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in History of the Bolivarian Republic: Chávez's victory and the 1999 Constituent

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about History of the Bolivarian Republic: Chávez's victory and the 1999 Constituent

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Chávez wins in 1998 promising refoundation, and a constituent process opens a participatory model.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Chávez: a new model of government

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 6to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Chávez: a new model of government

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** A consultative referendum, election of the Constituent Assembly and a final referendum approve the 1999 Constitution — the people as constitution-maker.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The 1999 constituent process: meeting of dreams and knowledge

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Patria y Ciudadanía — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The 1999 constituent process: meeting of dreams and knowledge

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** Chávez refounds the republic in 1999, survives coup and oil strike, and governs through missions and a new oil policy to 2012.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Government of Hugo Rafael Chávez (1999–2012)

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Nuestra Historia Republicana — Ciencias Sociales, 2do año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
