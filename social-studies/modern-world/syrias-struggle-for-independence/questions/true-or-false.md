# Syria's struggle for independence — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Faisal's Arab government in Damascus (1918–1920) spread schools, founded the Arab Academy and made Arabic the language of administration; Faisal demanded Arab rights at Paris in 1919; the American King–Crane commission toured Bilad al-Sham, the Syrian General Congress declared Syria's complete independence including Palestine and Lebanon, and the commission recommended independence for Syria and Iraq and no mandate — and its report was shelved until 1922.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 1, Lesson التآمر الأجنبي على الحكم العربي في بلاد الشام (The foreign conspiracy against Arab rule in Bilad al-Sham)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *التاريخ — الصف التاسع (History, Grade 9)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Faisal's Arab government in Damascus, 1918–1920

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The 1928 constitution and the 1932 parliament elected Muhammad Ali al-Abid first president; the sixty-day strike forced the 1936 treaty project; in the Second World War the nationalists won the 1943 elections under Shukri al-Quwatli; France bombarded the parliament on 29 May 1945 and Arab and world opinion turned, and the French forces evacuated on 17 April 1946.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 1, Lesson الكفاح السياسي في سورية حتى الاستقلال (The political struggle in Syria until independence)
