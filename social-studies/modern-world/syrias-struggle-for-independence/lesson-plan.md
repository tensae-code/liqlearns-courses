## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Faisal's Arab government in Damascus, 1918–1920** — Faisal's Arab government in Damascus (1918–1920) spread schools, founded the Arab Academy and made Arabic the language of administration; Faisal demanded Arab rights at Paris in 1919; the American King–Crane commission toured Bilad al-Sham, the Syrian General Congress declared Syria's complete independence including Palestine and Lebanon, and the commission recommended independence for Syria and Iraq and no mandate — and its report was shelved until 1922.

2. **The political struggle to independence, 1928–1946** — The 1928 constitution and the 1932 parliament elected Muhammad Ali al-Abid first president; the sixty-day strike forced the 1936 treaty project; in the Second World War the nationalists won the 1943 elections under Shukri al-Quwatli; France bombarded the parliament on 29 May 1945 and Arab and world opinion turned, and the French forces evacuated on 17 April 1946.
