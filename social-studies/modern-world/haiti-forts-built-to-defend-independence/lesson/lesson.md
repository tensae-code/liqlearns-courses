# Haiti — forts built to defend independence

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A free Black state, fearing the slave powers would return, rings itself with forts — Fort Jacques, the Citadelle, Fort Décidé — whose faces still stare out from Haiti's banknotes. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
