## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 18, the Yên Thế peasant uprising, 1884–1913
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — in the hills of Yên Thế the peasants rise under Đề Nắm and then Đề Thám (Hoàng Hoa Thám, 1858–1913) not for a king but, in the book's words, to "keep the land, keep the village, protect a free life"; they beat off French sweeps at Cao Thượng (November 1890) and three times at Hố Chuối (December 1890) and rule the whole region; twice France buys truces (1894–95 and 1897–1908), then from 1909 besieges the base in earnest until, in February 1913, Đề Thám is murdered by French agents — the largest peasant uprising against invasion in Vietnam's modern history
