# Vietnam Yen The Uprising — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** in the hills of Yên Thế the peasants rise under Đề Nắm and then Đề Thám (Hoàng Hoa Thám, 1858–1913) not for a king but, in the book's words, to "keep the land, keep the village, protect a free life"; they beat off French sweeps at Cao Thượng (November 1890) and three times at Hố Chuối (December 1890) and rule the whole region; twice France buys truces (1894–95 and 1897–1908), then from 1909 besieges the base in earnest until, in February 1913, Đề Thám is murdered by French agents — the largest peasant uprising against invasion in Vietnam's modern history.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 18, the Yên Thế peasant uprising, 1884–1913

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 18, the Yên Thế peasant uprising, 1884–1913
