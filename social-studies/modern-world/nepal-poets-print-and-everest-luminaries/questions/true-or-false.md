# Nepal — poets, print and Everest luminaries — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Bhanubhakta translates the Ramayana into easy Nepali and becomes the pioneer poet, Motiram Bhatta publishes him and brings the printing press, Falgunanda ends animal sacrifice among the Kirat, and Pasang Lhamu Sherpa…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Our National Heroes (Bhanubhakta, Motiram, Falgunanda, Pasang Lhamu)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *My Social Studies and Creative Arts, Grade 5* is about The Nepal–British War.
**Answer: False** — it is about Our National Heroes (Bhanubhakta, Motiram, Falgunanda, Pasang Lhamu)
