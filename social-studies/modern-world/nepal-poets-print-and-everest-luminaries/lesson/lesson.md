# Nepal — poets, print and Everest luminaries

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Bhanubhakta translates the Ramayana into easy Nepali and becomes the pioneer poet, Motiram Bhatta publishes him and brings the printing press, Falgunanda ends animal sacrifice among the Kirat, and Pasang Lhamu Sherpa becomes the first Nepali woman on Everest in 2050 BS and dies on the mountain As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
