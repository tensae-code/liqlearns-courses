# Sri Lanka — The Buddhist revival — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Buddhist revival

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *G9 History Tamil 2019* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Buddhist revival

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils draw lots bearing the names Olcott, Dharmapala, Sumangala, Gunananda, Navalar and Siddhi Lebbe and must speak for three minutes as that revival leader in a musical-hat game.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Religious and cultural revival (competency level 3.1, 8 periods)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *G9 History Teacher Guide NIE 2018* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Religious and cultural revival (competency level 3.1, 8 periods)
