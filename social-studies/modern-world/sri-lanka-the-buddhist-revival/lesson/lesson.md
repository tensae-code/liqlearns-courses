# Sri Lanka — The Buddhist revival

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Buddhist revival

Buddhists, who had no press of their own, raise funds for one in 1862, and Migettuwatte Gunananda Thero defeats the missionaries in the Five Great Debates, above all at Panadura in 1873
