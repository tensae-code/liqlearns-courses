# Hands-on — Vietnam Rach Gam Xoai Mut 1785

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Rach Gam Xoai Mut 1785 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 8, the destruction of the Siamese invasion fleet at Rạch Gầm – Xoài Mút, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
