# Vietnam Rach Gam Xoai Mut 1785

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: the fugitive Nguyễn Ánh begs Siam for help, and some 50,000 Siamese troops pour into western Gia Định in 1784; Nguyễn Huệ, headquartered at Mỹ Tho, chooses the six-kilometre stretch of the Tiền river between Rạch Gầm and Xoài Mút — an islet midstream, dense banks, hidden creeks — and on 19 January 1785 feigns retreat, lures the fleet into the ambush and burns it with fire rafts; the Siamese are annihilated in a single day, and the Nguyễn court's own chronicle admits that after that defeat "though their mouths boast, in their hearts they fear Tây Sơn like a tiger" As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
