# Vietnam Rach Gam Xoai Mut 1785 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the fugitive Nguyễn Ánh begs Siam for help, and some 50,000 Siamese troops pour into western Gia Định in 1784; Nguyễn Huệ, headquartered at Mỹ Tho, chooses the six-kilometre stretch of the Tiền river between Rạch Gầm and Xoài Mút — an islet midstream, dense banks, hidden creeks — and on 19 January 1785 feigns retreat, lures the fleet into the ambush and burns it with fire rafts; the Siamese are annihilated in a single day, and the Nguyễn court's own chronicle admits that after that defeat "though their mouths boast, in their hearts they fear Tây Sơn like a tiger".
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 8, the destruction of the Siamese invasion fleet at Rạch Gầm – Xoài Mút

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 8, the destruction of the Siamese invasion fleet at Rạch Gầm – Xoài Mút
