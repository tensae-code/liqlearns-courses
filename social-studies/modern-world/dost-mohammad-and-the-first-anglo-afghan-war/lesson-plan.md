# Lesson plan — Dost Mohammad and the First Anglo-Afghan War

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Dost Mohammad and the First Anglo-Afghan War
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Afghanistan, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the Barakzai chief Dost Mohammad founds a new emirate at Kabul as the Durrani empire dissolves
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 12: Do you know Dost Mohammad Khan's first emirate?** — the Barakzai chief Dost Mohammad founds a new emirate at Kabul as the Durrani empire dissolves
2. **Lesson 13: Do you know the first British invasion of Afghanistan?** — in February 1839 the British under Sir John Keane, with Macnaghten and Shah Shuja, march through Balochistan and the Bolan Pass on Kandahar; the Kandahar brothers, refused by Russia, flee to Iran and Shuja enters the city
3. **Lesson 14: Do you know Shah Shuja's second reign?** — Shuja rules again as the British army's client king in occupied Kabul
4. **Lesson 15: Do you know Amir Dost Mohammad Khan's second reign?** — restored after the British catastrophe, Dost Mohammad reunites Kandahar, Herat and Kabul under one emirate
5. **Lesson 8: Shah Shuja and the national uprising** — the British-installed Shuja faces the national uprising of 1841 that destroys the occupation
6. **Lesson 9: The second kingship of Amir Dost Mohammad Khan** — Dost Mohammad returns and reunifies the emirate
