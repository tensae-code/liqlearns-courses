# Hands-on — The Union of Lublin — the Commonwealth is born

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Formation of the Rzeczpospolita (Commonwealth) — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Formation of the Rzeczpospolita (Commonwealth), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
