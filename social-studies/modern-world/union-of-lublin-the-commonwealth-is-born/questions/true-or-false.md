# The Union of Lublin — the Commonwealth is born — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pressed by the Livonian War, Sigismund II Augustus signs the Union of Lublin on 1 July 1569 and Poland and the Grand Duchy become one state, with southern GDL lands cut away to…
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Formation of the Rzeczpospolita (Commonwealth)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Atlas: History of Belarus, Grade 8* is about The Cossack-Peasant War 1648–1651.
**Answer: False** — it is about Formation of the Rzeczpospolita (Commonwealth)
