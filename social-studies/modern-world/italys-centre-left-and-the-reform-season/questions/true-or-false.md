# Italys Centre Left And The Reform Season — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Socialists enter government, electricity is nationalised and the middle school unified; Merlin's inquiry on the Vajont (unit Document) shows the miracle's other face — progress with a body count.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unità 5, Cap. 19 §4 The centre-left governments and the reforms

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *La storia. Progettare il futuro — Vol. 3: Il Novecento e l'età attuale (Alessandro Barbero, Chiara Frugoni, Carla Sclarandis)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unità 5, Cap. 19 §4 The centre-left governments and the reforms

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Socialists enter government and the reform season opens — divorce and the Vajont (Focus) mark its social advances and its disasters.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Modulo 13 §7 Il centro-sinistra
