# Video script — Australia's colonisation of Papua

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Papua New Guinea's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Fearing other powers and wanting gold and sugar labour, Australia takes Papua under the Papuan Act; Hubert Murray rules three decades — 99-year land leases, the 1907 labour indenture system, hospitals for TB and leprosy.
