# The Ohrid Archbishopric Under The Sultans 1767 — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Theme 5: the position of the Ohrid Archbishopric in the Ottoman state.
**Answer: the sultans keep the Archbishopric as a loyal, privileged church — its flock grows to Bulgaria, Wallachia, Moldavia and even Orthodox communities in Italy — but favour shifts to Constantinople's patriarchate; St Sophia and St Pantelejmon become mosques and the seat moves to Perivlepta; Archbishop Athanasius I tours Europe begging for an uprising that never comes; squeezed by debt and Phanariot intrigue, the last archbishop, Arsenij, is forced to resign on 16 January 1767, and the sultan abolishes the Archbishopric — the story Grigor Prlićev later sings in "1762 leto" (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**
