## Sub lesson 1 — Theme 5: the position of the Ohrid Archbishopric in the Ottoman state
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the sultans keep the Archbishopric as a loyal, privileged church — its flock grows to Bulgaria, Wallachia, Moldavia and even Orthodox communities in Italy — but favour shifts to Constantinople's patriarchate; St Sophia and St Pantelejmon become mosques and the seat moves to Perivlepta; Archbishop Athanasius I tours Europe begging for an uprising that never comes; squeezed by debt and Phanariot intrigue, the last archbishop, Arsenij, is forced to resign on 16 January 1767, and the sultan abolishes the Archbishopric — the story Grigor Prlićev later sings in "1762 leto"
