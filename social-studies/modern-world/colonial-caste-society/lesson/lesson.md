# Colonial caste society

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: A caste pyramid disgregates society — peninsulares, criollos, then the indigenous strata of curacas, comunarios, agregados and yanaconas, with African communities as labour force. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
