# Cuza And The 1859 Union — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Tema 14: Alexandru Ioan Cuza, domnul Unirii.
**Answer: Cuza is elected in both Moldavia and Wallachia in 1859 and unites them into one state — but Bessarabia, taken by Russia in 1812, is left outside (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* (Moldova), Tema 14: Alexandru Ioan Cuza, domnul Unirii, printed p. 36.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Retell this part in your own words: §32: Unirea Principatelor Române.
**Answer: the ad-hoc assemblies show Europe the Romanians' will to unite; the sultan dissolves them by firman, the double election defeats the firman, and even Bessarabia's nobles draft an address on the union (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor şi universală: Epoca modernă. Manual pentru clasa a XI-a* (Moldova), §32: Unirea Principatelor Române, printed p. 136.**
