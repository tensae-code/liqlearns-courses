# Cuza And The 1859 Union — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Cuza is elected in both Moldavia and Wallachia in 1859 and unites them into one state — but Bessarabia, taken by Russia in 1812, is left outside.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Tema 14: Alexandru Ioan Cuza, domnul Unirii

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Istoria românilor și universală: Manual pentru clasa a 4-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Tema 14: Alexandru Ioan Cuza, domnul Unirii

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The ad-hoc assemblies show Europe the Romanians' will to unite; the sultan dissolves them by firman, the double election defeats the firman, and even Bessarabia's nobles draft an address on the union.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §32: Unirea Principatelor Române

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Istoria românilor şi universală: Epoca modernă. Manual pentru clasa a XI-a* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §32: Unirea Principatelor Române
