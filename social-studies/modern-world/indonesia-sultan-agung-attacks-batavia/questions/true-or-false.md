# Indonesia — Sultan Agung attacks Batavia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Mataram's Sultan Agung twice besieges VOC Batavia against Jan Pieterszoon Coen, a war Sudjojono later reconstructs on canvas from the archives.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab I.B

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — the Pattimura revolt of 1817.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab I.B

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Mataram's king measures himself directly against the VOC's founder-governor in the sieges of Batavia.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 2.A.3 Sultan Agung Versus J.P. Coen

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — the Pattimura revolt of 1817.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 2.A.3 Sultan Agung Versus J.P. Coen
