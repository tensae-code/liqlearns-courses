## Sub lesson 1 — The Arabian Gulf — from Portuguese to British control
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — The Portuguese came round the Cape and seized Muscat and Bahrain, and the Arabs of Oman freed Muscat in 1650 and drove them out; Britain then bound the Gulf rulers by treaties (1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899), split its emirates, and after oil was found faced American rivalry; Kuwait won independence in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council was founded in 1981.
