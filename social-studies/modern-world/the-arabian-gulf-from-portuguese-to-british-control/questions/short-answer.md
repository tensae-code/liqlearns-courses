# The Arabian Gulf — from Portuguese to British control — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: The Arabian Gulf — from Portuguese to British control.
**Answer: The Portuguese came round the Cape and seized Muscat and Bahrain, and the Arabs of Oman freed Muscat in 1650 and drove them out; Britain then bound the Gulf rulers by treaties (1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899), split its emirates, and after oil was found faced American rivalry; Kuwait won independence in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council was founded in 1981.
