**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) The Portuguese came round the Cape and seized Muscat and Bahrain, and the Arabs of Oman freed Muscat in 1650 and drove them out; Britain then bound the Gulf rulers by treaties (1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899), split its emirates, and after oil was found faced American rivalry; Kuwait won independence in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council was founded in 1981.  b) a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion
