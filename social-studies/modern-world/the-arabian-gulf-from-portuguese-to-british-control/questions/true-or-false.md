# The Arabian Gulf — from Portuguese to British control — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Portuguese came round the Cape and seized Muscat and Bahrain, and the Arabs of Oman freed Muscat in 1650 and drove them out; Britain then bound the Gulf rulers by treaties (1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899), split its emirates, and after oil was found faced American rivalry; Kuwait won independence in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council was founded in 1981.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 3, Lesson الخليج العربي محور لتنافس القوى الدولية (The Arab Gulf, an axis of international rivalry)

**Answer: False** — it is about The Arabian Gulf — from Portuguese to British control
