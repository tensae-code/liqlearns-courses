# The Arabian Gulf — from Portuguese to British control

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Portuguese, then British, then American power circles the Arab Gulf — until Kuwait, the Emirates and Oman stand independent and the Gulf Cooperation Council is founded in 1981. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Arabian Gulf — from Portuguese to British control

The Portuguese came round the Cape and seized Muscat and Bahrain, and the Arabs of Oman freed Muscat in 1650 and drove them out; Britain then bound the Gulf rulers by treaties (1800, the 1820 treaty after the Qawasim resistance, Kuwait 1899), split its emirates, and after oil was found faced American rivalry; Kuwait won independence in 1961, the Trucial states united as the Emirates in 1971, Oman united in 1970, and the Gulf Cooperation Council was founded in 1981.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
