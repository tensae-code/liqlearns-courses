# Hands-on — The Chinese Revolution, 1949

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The Chinese Revolution, 1949 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Unidad 11 La Revolución China (1949), from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
