# Asia's economic rise — and its conflicts

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Since the 1960s Asia grows richer faster than any region — East Asian extreme poverty falls from 56% to 12% — yet subnational conflicts multiply against the theory that prosperity brings peace; China's turn from export industrialisation remakes its economy and its environment, and millions migrate for work. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
