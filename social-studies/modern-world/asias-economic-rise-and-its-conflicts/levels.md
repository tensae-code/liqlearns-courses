# Asia's economic rise — and its conflicts — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Economic systems in Asia / Impact of economic decisions on the people and environment / Economic influence on movement of people / Economic influence on conflicts in Asia
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — since the 1960s Asia grows richer faster than any region — East Asian extreme poverty falls from 56% to 12% — yet subnational conflicts multiply against the theory that prosperity brings peace; China's turn from export industrialisation remakes its economy and its environment, and millions migrate for work

## Sub lesson 2 — Asia's development: past and present / Regional and global development influences
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the four Asian Tigers industrialise between the 1960s and 1990s; China and India's rise shifts world power from West to East and raises the fear of a clash with US dominance

## Sub lesson 3 — Governments and change in Asia and the world / Impact of different governments' actions / Government reactions and contributions to change / Sustainability or change policies and plans of Asian governments (incl. investigating China's Belt and Road)
