# Asia's economic rise — and its conflicts — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. **Answer: True** — the book tells this in Economic systems in Asia / Impact of economic decisions on the people and environment / Economic influence on movement of people / Economic influence on conflicts in Asia
