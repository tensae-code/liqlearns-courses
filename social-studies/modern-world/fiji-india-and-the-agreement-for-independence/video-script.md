# Video script — Fiji and India — and the agreement for independence

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Fiji's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Independent India (1947) at first does not press Britain over Fiji; Ratu Mara woos India, his private talks with A.D. Patel open confidential Suva talks, and the agreement for an independence constitution follows; Indira Gandhi visits in 1981 and the Girmit Centre rises at Lautoka.
