# Vietnam Border Wars And Gac Ma — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 20, defending the borders and the sea, 1975–1989
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — on the southwestern border the Pol Pot government occupies Phú Quốc and Thổ Chu islands and massacres civilians in An Giang and Tây Ninh; Vietnam protests and offers peaceful negotiation, Pol Pot refuses and cuts relations, and in December 1978 sends 19 infantry divisions with artillery and tanks against Tây Ninh — from 23 December 1978 Vietnam's general counteroffensive defeats the invasion and opens the way for the Cambodian revolution; on the northern border, after China ends its aid in 1978, some 600,000 Chinese troops attack along the whole frontier on 17 February 1979 — Lạng Sơn, Cao Bằng and Lào Cai fight fiercely, China declares withdrawal on 5 March, but tension lasts to the late 1980s, above all at Vị Xuyên, where the soldiers' oath "live clinging to the rock, die turned to stone" is carved at Hill 468; at sea Vietnam administers Bạch Long Vĩ, Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa districts and protests at the United Nations, and in March 1988, at Gạc Ma in the Trường Sa archipelago, 64 Vietnamese naval soldiers die defending the reefs against a Chinese attack

## Sub lesson 3 — Bài 9 The Southwest border war: Pol Pot's forces raid from May 1975 — Phú Quốc and Thổ Chu seized, An Giang attacked (30-4-1977), over 3,000 civilians massacred at Ba Chúc in twelve days of April 1978 — and Vietnam answers in two stages, diplomacy alongside defence, then a general counter-offensive; at the Cambodian National United Front's request, Vietnamese volunteers join Cambodia's army and people to topple Pol Pot, and Phnom Penh is liberated on 7-1-1979
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the neighbour Vietnam helped liberate turns its guns on Vietnamese villages, and the war ends in the neighbour's own capital

## Sub lesson 4 — Bài 9 The Northern border war: relations sour from mid-1975 as China cuts aid and provokes along the frontier; on the morning of 17-2-1979 hundreds of thousands of Chinese troops attack along more than 1,000 km from Móng Cái to Phong Thổ, and six border provinces' army and people defeat the "quick attack, quick victory" aim at Lạng Sơn, Cao Bằng and Lào Cai; China declares withdrawal from 5-3-1979, yet the frontier stays tense to late 1989 — at Vị Xuyên alone some 5,000 soldiers fall in 1984–1989
