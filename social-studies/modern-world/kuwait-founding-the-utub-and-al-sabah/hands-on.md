# Hands-on — The founding of Kuwait — the Utub and Al Sabah

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
Draw the Utub route: al-Hadar → Zubara → al-Qurain, with the reason for the move written under it.

## Task 2
Write the drought's-eye view: three sentences as a Utub elder deciding to move.

## Task 3
Make a two-date timeline: 1716 the alliance, 1718 the pledge — write what happened at each.

## Task 4
Write the pledge scene: what do the people promise, and what do they ask of their ruler?

## Task 5
Write a founding charter in three lines: how a ruler is chosen, how counsel is shared, what the ruler owes.

## Task 6
Compare: chosen leadership versus seized leadership — list one strength of each.
