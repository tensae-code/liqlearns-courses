# The founding of Kuwait — the Utub and Al Sabah — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — The Utub migration and the birth of Kuwait
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — mid-1600s drought in Najd drives the Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma families — the Utub — from al-Hadar to Zubara, then on to al-Qurain in search of a lasting home.

## Sub lesson 2 — Naming Kuwait and choosing Al Sabah to rule
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — al-Qurain, named for its hill seen from the sea, becomes Kuwait, the little fort; in 1716 the three families ally, split rule, trade and sea work, and in 1718 the people pledge Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber as first ruler.

## Sub lesson 3 — The Utub migration and the birth of Kuwait's system of rule
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the Utub arrive and a way of ruling is born with them — chosen leadership and shared counsel — the seed of Kuwait's whole political order.
