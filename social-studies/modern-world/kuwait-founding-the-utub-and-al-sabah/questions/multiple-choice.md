# The founding of Kuwait — the Utub and Al Sabah — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Utub were —
a) the Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma families  b) three merchant ships  c) a single tribe
**Answer: a) the Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma families** — the three families are the Utub

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Drought drove the Utub from —
a) Najd  b) Yemen  c) Oman
**Answer: a) Najd** — mid-1600s drought in Najd drives them

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The Utub travelled by way of —
a) Zubara, then al-Qurain  b) Bahrain, then Basra  c) Failaka, then Zubara
**Answer: a) Zubara, then al-Qurain** — from al-Hadar to Zubara, then on to al-Qurain

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Kuwait's name means —
a) the little fort  b) the high hill  c) the deep harbour
**Answer: a) the little fort** — Kuwait means the little fort

**5. (Sub lesson 2)** The people pledged as first ruler, in 1718 —
a) Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber  b) Sheikh Mubarak Al Sabah  c) Sheikh Abdullah Al Salem
**Answer: a) Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber** — in 1718 the people pledge Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber

**6. (Sub lesson 2)** In 1716 the three families —
a) allied and split rule, trade and sea work  b) went to war  c) left al-Qurain
**Answer: a) allied and split rule, trade and sea work** — in 1716 the three families ally and split the work

**7. (Sub lesson 3)** The Grade 10 book calls the Utub settlement the birth of —
a) Kuwait's system of rule  b) the oil state  c) the pearl trade
**Answer: a) Kuwait's system of rule** — a way of ruling is born with them

**8. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's way of ruling rests on —
a) chosen leadership and shared counsel  b) hereditary conquest  c) foreign appointment
**Answer: a) chosen leadership and shared counsel** — chosen leadership and shared counsel

**9. (Sub lesson 3)** This founding way of ruling is the seed of —
a) Kuwait's whole political order  b) the Gulf Cooperation Council  c) the 1899 agreement
**Answer: a) Kuwait's whole political order** — the seed of Kuwait's whole political order
