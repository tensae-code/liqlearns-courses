# The founding of Kuwait — the Utub and Al Sabah — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Utub came to Kuwait as conquerors with an army.
**Answer: False** — they came as migrating families seeking a lasting home

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Al-Qurain was the Utub's lasting home.
**Answer: True** — they settle at al-Qurain

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Kuwait's first ruler seized power by force.
**Answer: False** — the people pledged Sabah bin Jaber in 1718

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Al Sabah rule in Kuwait begins in 1718.
**Answer: True** — Sabah bin Jaber is pledged as first ruler in 1718

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Kuwait's rulers were always imposed from outside.
**Answer: False** — its leadership was chosen, with shared counsel

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** The Utub founding shapes Kuwait's politics to this day.
**Answer: True** — it is the seed of Kuwait's whole political order
