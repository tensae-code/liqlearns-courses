# The founding of Kuwait — the Utub and Al Sabah — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did the Utub leave Najd?
**Answer: Drought in the mid-1600s drove them in search of a lasting home (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Name the three Utub families.
**Answer: Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma.**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** How did al-Qurain get its name, and what did it become?
**Answer: It was named for its hill seen from the sea, and it became Kuwait, the little fort (any close wording).**

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** How was Kuwait's first ruler chosen?
**Answer: The people pledged him — Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber was chosen in 1718 after the families' 1716 alliance.**

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** What way of ruling was born with the Utub?
**Answer: Chosen leadership and shared counsel (any close wording).**

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** Why does the Grade 10 book return to the Utub migration?
**Answer: Because Kuwait's whole political order grows from that founding seed.**
