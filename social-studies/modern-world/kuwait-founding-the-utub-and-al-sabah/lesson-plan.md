# Lesson plan — The founding of Kuwait — the Utub and Al Sabah

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** The founding of Kuwait — the Utub and Al Sabah
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Kuwait, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Drought drove the Utub families from Najd to al-Qurain; in 1716 they allied, and in 1718 the people pledged Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber as Kuwait's first ruler.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The Utub migration and the birth of Kuwait** — mid-1600s drought in Najd drives the Al Sabah, Al Khalifa and Al Jalahma families — the Utub — from al-Hadar to Zubara, then on to al-Qurain in search of a lasting home (Unit 3 Lesson 3: The Utub migration and the birth of Kuwait)
2. **Naming Kuwait and choosing Al Sabah to rule** — al-Qurain, named for its hill seen from the sea, becomes Kuwait, the little fort; in 1716 the three families ally, split rule, trade and sea work, and in 1718 the people pledge Sheikh Sabah bin Jaber as first ruler (Unit 3 Lesson 4: Naming Kuwait and choosing Al Sabah to rule)
3. **The Utub migration and the birth of Kuwait's system of rule** — the Utub arrive and a way of ruling is born with them — chosen leadership and shared counsel — the seed of Kuwait's whole political order (Unit 2 Lesson 2: The Utub migration and the birth of Kuwait's system of rule)
