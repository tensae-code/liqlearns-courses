# Timor-Leste — the Church and the diplomatic front — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The mass at Tasi-Tolu mattered only as a religious event, with no public meaning.
**Answer: False — the book teaches it as a demonstration as well: the people made visible to the world.**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Diplomats kept Timor-Leste's claim alive abroad during the occupation.
**Answer: True — the diplomatic service carried the cause to the UN and the world.**
