# Timor-Leste — the Church and the diplomatic front — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under occupation, the Catholic Church in Timor-Leste —
a) Sheltered the people  b) Led the invasion  c) Closed its doors
**Answer: a) Sheltered the people** — Sheltered the people — a place of protection.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** The Pope's mass at Tasi-Tolu became —
a) A demonstration as well as a mass  b) A market  c) A battle
**Answer: a) A demonstration as well as a mass** — A demonstration — a whole people visible to the world.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Timor-Leste's diplomats carried its cause to —
a) The UN and the world  b) Only one village  c) No one
**Answer: a) The UN and the world** — The UN and the world — keeping the claim alive abroad.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This lesson's two fronts are —
a) The Church and diplomacy  b) Two armies  c) Two navies
**Answer: a) The Church and diplomacy** — The Church and the diplomatic service — the fronts that work when guns cannot win.
