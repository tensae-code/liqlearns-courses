# Hands-on — Timor-Leste — the Church and the diplomatic front

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two fronts, no guns
Draw two boxes: 'the Church' and 'the diplomats'. In each, draw or write one thing that front did. Say out loud how each one fought without a gun.

## Tasi-Tolu scene
Draw a crowd at an open-air mass. Write one sentence under it explaining how the same gathering could be both a mass and a demonstration.
