# Video script — Timor-Leste — the Church and the diplomatic front

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Timor-Leste's story as its Grade 6 book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Under occupation, the Catholic Church becomes a shelter for the people — a place of protection when little else can protect them. Its power shows in one great scene the book teaches: the Pope's mass at Tasi-Tolu, where a religious gathering becomes, in the same moment, a demonstration — a whole people visible to the world.

The second weapon in this lesson carries no gun at all: diplomacy. A handful of diplomats carry the cause of a small occupied country to the United Nations and to the world, keeping its claim alive abroad through all the years when it cannot be won at home.
