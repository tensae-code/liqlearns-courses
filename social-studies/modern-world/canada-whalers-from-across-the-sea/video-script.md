# Video script — Whalers from across the sea

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the ships arrive in the Arctic story the Nunavut reader tells. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

For a long time, the only hunters in these northern waters were Inuit crews in open boats, taking a whale now and then for the whole camp.

Then sails appeared on the horizon. Big ships had come from across the sea — foreign whalers, hunting not for one camp but for trade. They took whales in large numbers, for the oil and for the baleen, to sell far away.

The ships brought trade, and Inuit life changed around it. But so many whales were taken that the whales grew few — and a community that lived from the whale felt that loss in everything.

The sea that had always provided now had strangers hunting on it — and not enough whales left.
