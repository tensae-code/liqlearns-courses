# Whalers from across the sea — depth

This lesson is one whole lesson — it has no sub lessons. Pass mark: say who the foreign whalers were, what they hunted for — oil and baleen — and how Inuit life was changed by the trade and the depletion. Practice deepens by more items first (every name and distinction in the lesson), then harder corners: retelling the story in order without the lesson open. The questions live in the type files.
