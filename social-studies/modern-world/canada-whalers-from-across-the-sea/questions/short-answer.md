# Whalers from across the sea — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1.** Why did foreign whaling ships come to Arctic waters?
**Answer: To hunt whales in large numbers for oil and baleen, as goods for trade (any close wording).**

**2.** Name two ways Inuit life was changed by the foreign whalers.
**Answer: The trade they brought changed Inuit life, and their large-scale hunt depleted the whales, so the whales grew few (any close wording).**
