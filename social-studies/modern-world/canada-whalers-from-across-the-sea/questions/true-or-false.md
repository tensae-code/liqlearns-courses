# Whalers from across the sea — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1.** Inuit life was changed by the trade the foreign whaling ships brought.
**Answer: True** — the ships brought new trade and a new presence in Inuit waters and life.

**2.** The foreign whalers hunted only one whale at a time, for a single camp, like the old Inuit hunt.
**Answer: False** — they hunted whales in large numbers, for oil and baleen to sell far away.
