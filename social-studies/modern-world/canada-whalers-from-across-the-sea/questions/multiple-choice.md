# Whalers from across the sea — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1.** The whalers from across the sea were —
a) Inuit crews from another camp  b) Foreign commercial whaling ships  c) Traders travelling by dogsled
**Answer: b) Foreign commercial whaling ships** — they arrived in Arctic waters from far away.

**2.** The foreign whalers hunted whales in large numbers for —
a) Food for one camp  b) Oil and baleen  c) Bones for sleds
**Answer: b) Oil and baleen** — goods for trade in their home countries.

**3.** The ships' hunt differed from the old Inuit hunt because —
a) It took whales in large numbers for trade  b) It used hand harpoons in open boats  c) The whole camp shared each catch
**Answer: a) It took whales in large numbers for trade.**

**4.** Depletion means —
a) More whales than ever  b) The whales grew few because so many were taken  c) The ships went home
**Answer: b) The whales grew few because so many were taken.**
