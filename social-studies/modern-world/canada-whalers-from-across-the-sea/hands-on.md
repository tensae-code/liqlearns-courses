# Hands-on — Whalers from across the sea

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two hunts, side by side
Draw two pictures side by side: an Inuit crew in an open boat, and a big foreign whaling ship. Under each, write who the hunt was for, and what the whales were taken for.

## Count the difference
Tell the story of numbers out loud: the old hunt took a whale for a camp; the ships took whales in large numbers. Act out a shore with many whales, then a shore with few — and say what changes for the camp when the whales grow few.
