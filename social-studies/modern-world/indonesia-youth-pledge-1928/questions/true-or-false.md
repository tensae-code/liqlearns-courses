# Indonesia — the Youth Pledge of 1928 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** At the Second Youth Congress in Jakarta in 1928, young delegates from rival regional associations pledge one motherland, one nation, one language — Indonesia.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab II.B

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA/SMK/MAK Kelas XI, Edisi Revisi* is about Indonesia — under Japanese occupation.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab II.B

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The road runs to the 1928 Youth Pledge, a modern nationalism that fights on two fronts at once — mass organisation outside and speeches inside the colonial Volksraad — until the colonial government's final years.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 3.C.1-3 Menuju Sumpah Pemuda / Bangkitnya Nasionalisme Modern / Perjuangan di Volksraad

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Sejarah Indonesia Kelas XI Semester 1 (Kurikulum 2013)* is about Indonesia — under Japanese occupation.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 3.C.1-3 Menuju Sumpah Pemuda / Bangkitnya Nasionalisme Modern / Perjuangan di Volksraad
