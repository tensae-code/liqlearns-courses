# Hands-on — Vietnam Hoang Sa Truong Sa 1884 To 1975

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Hoang Sa Truong Sa 1884 To 1975 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 13 Sovereignty under protectorate and republic: France, speaking for the Nguyễn court, protests China's 1909 surveys, answers a British insurer that the Qing cannot pay for the wrecked Bellona because the islands are not China's, mounts the first scientific expeditions (1925, 1927), builds the 1937 lighthouse and 1938 sovereignty stele; at San Francisco in 1951 no state objects to Vietnam's claim; after Geneva 1954 the southern governments garrison and administer both archipelagos until the Liberation Army takes over Trường Sa in April 1975, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
