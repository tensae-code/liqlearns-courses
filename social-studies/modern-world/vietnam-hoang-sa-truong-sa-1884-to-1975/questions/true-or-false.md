# Vietnam Hoang Sa Truong Sa 1884 To 1975 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** a protectorate's lawyers and lighthouse-keepers keep a claim alive that armies later inherit.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 13 Sovereignty under protectorate and republic: France, speaking for the Nguyễn court, protests China's 1909 surveys, answers a British insurer that the Qing cannot pay for the wrecked Bellona because the islands are not China's, mounts the first scientific expeditions (1925, 1927), builds the 1937 lighthouse and 1938 sovereignty stele; at San Francisco in 1951 no state objects to Vietnam's claim; after Geneva 1954 the southern governments garrison and administer both archipelagos until the Liberation Army takes over Trường Sa in April 1975

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử 11 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 13 Sovereignty under protectorate and republic: France, speaking for the Nguyễn court, protests China's 1909 surveys, answers a British insurer that the Qing cannot pay for the wrecked Bellona because the islands are not China's, mounts the first scientific expeditions (1925, 1927), builds the 1937 lighthouse and 1938 sovereignty stele; at San Francisco in 1951 no state objects to Vietnam's claim; after Geneva 1954 the southern governments garrison and administer both archipelagos until the Liberation Army takes over Trường Sa in April 1975
