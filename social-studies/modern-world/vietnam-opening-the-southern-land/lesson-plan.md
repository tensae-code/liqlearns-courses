# Lesson plan — Vietnam Opening The Southern Land

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Vietnam Opening The Southern Land
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Vietnam, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** Nguyễn Hoàng takes up the government of Thuận Hoá in 1558 — the Nguyễn chronicle praises a lord "lenient with his soldiers, gathering heroes, loved and obeyed by the people", from whom "the imperial enterprise was built"; by the late 16th century Thuận–Quảng counts some 1,226 villages, and the milestones run on: Phú Yên prefecture in 1611, Thái Khang in 1653, Thuận Thành in 1693, Gia Định prefecture in 1698 — when Nguyễn Hữu Cảnh formally organises the far south — and by 1757 the Nguyễn administration covers a Nam Bộ roughly as it is today
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Bài 6, the opening of the South, 1558–1757** — Nguyễn Hoàng takes up the government of Thuận Hoá in 1558 — the Nguyễn chronicle praises a lord "lenient with his soldiers, gathering heroes, loved and obeyed by the people", from whom "the imperial enterprise was built"; by the late 16th century Thuận–Quảng counts some 1,226 villages, and the milestones run on: Phú Yên prefecture in 1611, Thái Khang in 1653, Thuận Thành in 1693, Gia Định prefecture in 1698 — when Nguyễn Hữu Cảnh formally organises the far south — and by 1757 the Nguyễn administration covers a Nam Bộ roughly as it is today
2. **Bài 6, what the southern opening achieved** — by the late 18th century the Nguyễn lords hold everything from south of the Hoành Sơn range to Cape Cà Mau, islands included; the book's meaning is twofold — a wasteland becomes an inseparable part of Đại Việt and a new centre of production and trade where Viet settlers and the peoples already there live together — and, in the quoted judgement of Phan Huy Lê, a land "awakened and revived after centuries of near-forgetting" within little more than a hundred years
