# Hands-on — Vietnam Opening The Southern Land

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Vietnam Opening The Southern Land — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 6, the opening of the South, 1558–1757, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
