# Vietnam Opening The Southern Land — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Bài 6, the opening of the South, 1558–1757
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Nguyễn Hoàng takes up the government of Thuận Hoá in 1558 — the Nguyễn chronicle praises a lord "lenient with his soldiers, gathering heroes, loved and obeyed by the people", from whom "the imperial enterprise was built"; by the late 16th century Thuận–Quảng counts some 1,226 villages, and the milestones run on: Phú Yên prefecture in 1611, Thái Khang in 1653, Thuận Thành in 1693, Gia Định prefecture in 1698 — when Nguyễn Hữu Cảnh formally organises the far south — and by 1757 the Nguyễn administration covers a Nam Bộ roughly as it is today

## Sub lesson 2 — Bài 6, what the southern opening achieved
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — by the late 18th century the Nguyễn lords hold everything from south of the Hoành Sơn range to Cape Cà Mau, islands included; the book's meaning is twofold — a wasteland becomes an inseparable part of Đại Việt and a new centre of production and trade where Viet settlers and the peoples already there live together — and, in the quoted judgement of Phan Huy Lê, a land "awakened and revived after centuries of near-forgetting" within little more than a hundred years
