# Vietnam Opening The Southern Land — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Nguyễn Hoàng takes up the government of Thuận Hoá in 1558 — the Nguyễn chronicle praises a lord "lenient with his soldiers, gathering heroes, loved and obeyed by the people", from whom "the imperial enterprise was built"; by the late 16th century Thuận–Quảng counts some 1,226 villages, and the milestones run on: Phú Yên prefecture in 1611, Thái Khang in 1653, Thuận Thành in 1693, Gia Định prefecture in 1698 — when Nguyễn Hữu Cảnh formally organises the far south — and by 1757 the Nguyễn administration covers a Nam Bộ roughly as it is today.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 6, the opening of the South, 1558–1757

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bài 6, the opening of the South, 1558–1757

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** by the late 18th century the Nguyễn lords hold everything from south of the Hoành Sơn range to Cape Cà Mau, islands included; the book's meaning is twofold — a wasteland becomes an inseparable part of Đại Việt and a new centre of production and trade where Viet settlers and the peoples already there live together — and, in the quoted judgement of Phan Huy Lê, a land "awakened and revived after centuries of near-forgetting" within little more than a hundred years.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 6, what the southern opening achieved

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Lịch sử và Địa lí 8 (Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
