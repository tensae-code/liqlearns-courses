# Venezuela 19 April 1810 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Caracas's city council day becomes a classroom play children write, direct and stage themselves — the first step from colony to republic.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 19 April 1810

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 2do grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 19 April 1810

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** On 19 April 1810 Caracas's crowd rejects the captain general — the people, not only the council, open emancipation.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in When the people said: we do not want him!

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela y de Nuestra América — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about When the people said: we do not want him!
