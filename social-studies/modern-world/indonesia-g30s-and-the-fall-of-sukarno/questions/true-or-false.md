# Indonesia — the 30 September Movement and the fall of Sukarno — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 30 September 1965 Movement murders six generals, the book lays out the competing accounts of the 'Percobaan Kup G30S/PKI', and the dominoes fall toward a transfer of power.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bab 2.E Kemelut Pergantian Kekuasaan

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Sejarah untuk SMA/MA Kelas XII (Kurikulum Merdeka)* is about Indonesia — the fall of Suharto, May 1998.
**Answer: False** — it is about Bab 2.E Kemelut Pergantian Kekuasaan
