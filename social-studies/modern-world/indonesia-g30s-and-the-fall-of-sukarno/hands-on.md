# Hands-on — Indonesia — the 30 September Movement and the fall of Sukarno

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Indonesia — the 30 September Movement and the fall of Sukarno — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bab 2.E Kemelut Pergantian Kekuasaan, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
