# Hands-on — Venezuela Merchant Houses Boulton And Zingg

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela Merchant Houses Boulton And Zingg — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Houses, surnames and the dynamics of capital: Boulton and Zingg, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
