# New Zealand's 2002 apology to Sāmoa — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** at Samoa's 40th independence celebrations, Prime Minister Helen Clark formally apologises for New Zealand's inept administration of 1914–1962 — the 1918 influenza, the 1929 shooting of unarmed Mau protesters, the banishing of matai — presenting a waka huia; at Mangere, 78-year-old Seiuli Otto Hansell, born just after the epidemic, weeps and bows to her courage, and Tupua Tamasese Tupuola Efi, whose great-uncle was shot, calls the apology deeply moving.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Unit 2, Topic 2, Helen Clark's speech and NZ Herald articles (5 June 2002)

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Social Studies, Year 11 Book One* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Unit 2, Topic 2, Helen Clark's speech and NZ Herald articles (5 June 2002)
