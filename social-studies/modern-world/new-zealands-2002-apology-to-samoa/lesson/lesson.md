# New Zealand's 2002 apology to Sāmoa

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: at Samoa's 40th independence celebrations, Prime Minister Helen Clark formally apologises for New Zealand's inept administration of 1914–1962 — the 1918 influenza, the 1929 shooting of unarmed Mau protesters, the banishing of matai — presenting a waka huia; at Mangere, 78-year-old Seiuli Otto Hansell, born just after the epidemic, weeps and bows to her courage, and Tupua Tamasese Tupuola Efi, whose great-uncle was shot, calls the apology deeply moving. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
