# Ottoman Suzerainty Over The Romanian Lands — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Unitatea III, Lecția 2: Suzeranitatea otomană în Țările Române.
**Answer: after 1538 the Porte rules indirectly — tribute (haraciu) and gifts rise year by year, the prince must be "friend of the friends and enemy of the enemies" of the empire, and peasant households are ruined (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Moldova's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *Istoria românilor şi universală: Manual pentru clasa a VI-a* (Moldova), Unitatea III, Lecția 2: Suzeranitatea otomană în Țările Române, printed p. 103.**
