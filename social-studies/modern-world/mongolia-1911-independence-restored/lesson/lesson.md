# Mongolia 1911 — independence restored

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: as Qing collapses, Khalkha nobles and lamas send a secret delegation to Russia, then on 29 December 1911 proclaim independence and enthrone the Eighth Bogd as khan of Mongolia. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
