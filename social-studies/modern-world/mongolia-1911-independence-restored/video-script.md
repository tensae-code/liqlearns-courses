# Video script — Mongolia 1911 — independence restored

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Mongolia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

as Qing collapses, Khalkha nobles and lamas send a secret delegation to Russia, then on 29 December 1911 proclaim independence and enthrone the Eighth Bogd as khan of Mongolia. the Bogd Khanate builds ministries, an army and a parliament of two chambers, and wins the 1915 Kyakhta treaty — autonomy under Chinese suzerainty, less than the full independence proclaimed. on 29 December 1911 the Bogd is enthroned and independence declared; the book weighs 1911 as the national revolution that restarted Mongolian statehood. a two-chamber khural, an army, schools, a bank and 370 enterprises — the Bogd Khanate builds a modern state inside the limits Kyakhta allowed.
