# Mongolia 1911 — independence restored — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** as Qing collapses, Khalkha nobles and lamas send a secret delegation to Russia, then on 29 December 1911 proclaim independence and enthrone the Eighth Bogd as khan of Mongolia.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2.1 The Mongolian national revolution of 1911

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *mn-g9h Түүх IX (History IX)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2.1 The Mongolian national revolution of 1911

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** the Bogd Khanate builds ministries, an army and a parliament of two chambers, and wins the 1915 Kyakhta treaty — autonomy under Chinese suzerainty, less than the full independence proclaimed.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. 2.2 Independent Mongolia

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *mn-g9h Түүх IX (History IX)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. 2.2 Independent Mongolia

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** on 29 December 1911 the Bogd is enthroned and independence declared; the book weighs 1911 as the national revolution that restarted Mongolian statehood.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. II.1 The victory and significance of the 1911 revolution

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *mn-g11mh Монголын түүх XI (History of Mongolia XI)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. II.1 The victory and significance of the 1911 revolution

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** a two-chamber khural, an army, schools, a bank and 370 enterprises — the Bogd Khanate builds a modern state inside the limits Kyakhta allowed.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Ch. II.2 The beginning of Mongolia's restored rise, 1911–1920

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *mn-g11mh Монголын түүх XI (History of Mongolia XI)* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Ch. II.2 The beginning of Mongolia's restored rise, 1911–1920
