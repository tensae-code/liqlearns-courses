**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: Palestine — the 1936 revolt and the road to partition.
**Answer: Revolt followed revolt — Jaffa 1921, al-Buraq 1929 — until the Great Palestinian Revolt of 1936: Izz al-Din al-Qassam fell near Jenin, the strike and the fighting spread with Arab volunteers including Syrians, Britain answered with the Peel partition plan of 1937, which the Palestinians rejected, and the White Paper of 1939, which both sides rejected.
