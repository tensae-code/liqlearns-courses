# Palestine — the 1936 revolt and the road to partition

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Revolt follows revolt in mandate Palestine — Jaffa 1921, al-Buraq 1929, the Great Revolt of 1936 — until Britain's Peel plan and White Paper satisfy no one. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — Palestine — the 1936 revolt and the road to partition

Revolt followed revolt — Jaffa 1921, al-Buraq 1929 — until the Great Palestinian Revolt of 1936: Izz al-Din al-Qassam fell near Jenin, the strike and the fighting spread with Arab volunteers including Syrians, Britain answered with the Peel partition plan of 1937, which the Palestinians rejected, and the White Paper of 1939, which both sides rejected.

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.
