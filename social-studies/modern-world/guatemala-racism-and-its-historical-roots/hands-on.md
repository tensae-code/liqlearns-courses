# Hands-on — Guatemala — racism and its historical roots

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Part 1 — 2002 — the restaurant door (teacher guide — evidence)

Draw or map this part from memory, then write its story beat in your own words: a Maya K'iche' anthropologist in gala traditional dress is turned away from a Guatemala City restaurant while ladina companions are let in; her case becomes a landmark in the national fight against racism.

## Part 2 — El racismo escolar — racism defined (teacher guide — evidence)

Draw or map this part from memory, then write its story beat in your own words: racism is defined as a relation of domination built on telling peoples apart to strip their goods, its prejudices formed across history; in Guatemala the Maya peoples meet it daily — in schools, hospitals, buses and government — in everyday phrases the guide lists and names as racist.
