# Guatemala — racism and its historical roots — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Dr Velásquez Nimatuj was turned away from a restaurant in —
a) 2002  b) 1944  c) 1996
**Answer: a) 2002** — the case year.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** She is a Maya K'iche' —
a) anthropologist  b) general  c) banana executive
**Answer: a) anthropologist** — her profession.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** In the guide's definition, racism is a relation of —
a) domination  b) friendship  c) trade
**Answer: a) domination** — its definition.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The guide says Maya peoples meet racism daily in —
a) schools, hospitals, buses and government  b) only in books  c) nowhere now
**Answer: a) schools, hospitals, buses and government** — the places listed.
