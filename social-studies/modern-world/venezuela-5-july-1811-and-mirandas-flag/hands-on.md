# Hands-on — Venezuela 5 July 1811 And Mirandas Flag

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela 5 July 1811 And Mirandas Flag — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 5 July 1811: the Republic is born, and the flag Miranda brought, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
