# Venezuela 5 July 1811 And Mirandas Flag

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Congress declares Independence and the national flag is introduced as the daughter of the one Miranda brought As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.

## Sub lesson 1 — 5 July 1811: the Republic is born, and the flag Miranda brought

Congress declares Independence and the national flag is introduced as the daughter of the one Miranda brought

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Sub lesson 2 — The Congress of 1811; a short-lived First Republic

Congress declares independence in 1811, and the First Republic falls within a year to war and earthquake

In your mind's eye: the place and people of this part of the story. Name two things that changed in the story.

## Say it to your companion

Tell your companion the story as its beats, in order: 5 July 1811: the Republic is born, and the flag Miranda brought — Congress declares Independence and the national flag is introduced as the daughter of the one Miranda brought. The Congress of 1811; a short-lived First Republic — Congress declares independence in 1811, and the First Republic falls within a year to war and earthquake. Then let your companion ask you one hard question about it — answer from the lesson.
