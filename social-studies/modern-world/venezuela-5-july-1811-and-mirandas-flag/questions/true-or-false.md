# Venezuela 5 July 1811 And Mirandas Flag — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Congress declares Independence and the national flag is introduced as the daughter of the one Miranda brought.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 5 July 1811: the Republic is born, and the flag Miranda brought

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 2do grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 5 July 1811: the Republic is born, and the flag Miranda brought

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Congress declares independence in 1811, and the First Republic falls within a year to war and earthquake.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in The Congress of 1811; a short-lived First Republic

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela y de Nuestra América — Ciencias Sociales, 1er año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about The Congress of 1811; a short-lived First Republic
