# Video script — Wartime radio, propaganda and Jahvetti

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Finland's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Through the war years some 100,000 Finns write their troubles to a radio letterbox, and over 70,000 of the letters survive on 18 shelf-metres in the National Archives, anonymised for pupils to read, hear and question.
