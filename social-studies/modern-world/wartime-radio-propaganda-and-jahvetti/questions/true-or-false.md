# Wartime radio, propaganda and Jahvetti — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Through the war years some 100,000 Finns write their troubles to a radio letterbox, and over 70,000 of the letters survive on 18 shelf-metres in the National Archives, anonymised for pupils to read, hear and question.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Johdanto: the Jahvetin kirjelaatikko programme and its archive

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Jahvetin johdanto* is about Tehtävä 4 (lukio): letter from Oulu, 21 Apr 1941.
**Answer: False** — it is about Johdanto: the Jahvetin kirjelaatikko programme and its archive

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Twice a week the beloved voice "Jahvetti" — secretly teacher and writer Yrjö Kilpeläinen — answers citizens on air while his office, part of the State Information Office, sounds the nation's mood and fights rumours and Soviet propaganda, a one-man front in Finland's spiritual national defence.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Jahvetin kirjelaatikko (the programme, 1940–1945)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Jahvetin johdanto* is about Tehtävä 5 (lukio): letters from Säkylä (1941) and Reposaari, 17 Oct 1944, with the Laukko crofter eviction of 1906–07 as comparison.
