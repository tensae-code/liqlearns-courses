# Hands-on — Japan Lessons Of Success

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Japan Lessons Of Success — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Chuyên đề 2 Japan's success lessons, as Vietnam's textbook draws them: invest in education on Meiji foundations until illiteracy is among the world's lowest; a state that coordinates, invests and protects key industries rather than only refereeing; factory management that perfects people before products; buying the world's patents instead of reinventing them; and a national resilience forged by earthquakes and tsunamis, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
