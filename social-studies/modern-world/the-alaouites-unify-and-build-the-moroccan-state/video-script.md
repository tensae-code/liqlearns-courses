# Video script — The Alaouites unify and build the Moroccan state

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: Morocco's story as its Grade 5 guide tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The guide follows Moulay Rashid's efforts to unite the country on a map: another sharifian family from Tafilalt stitches a broken Morocco back together.

The building of the state is studied through military architecture under Moulay Ismail, with the kasbahs as the model — fortress-towns walling the reunited country.
