# Micronesia — the Japanese mandate years — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 2)** Name two ways the mandate tied the islands into the Japanese Empire.
**Answer: Any two of: Okinawan and Japanese immigration; kogakko schools training island children for civil-service and trade jobs; exports such as katsuobushi and phosphate shipped to Japan (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** What does 'yes-yes council' tell you about chiefs under Japanese rule?
**Answer: Chiefs were given seats but no real power — councils that only agreed with decisions made above them, so traditional leaders had no real place in the system (any close wording).**
