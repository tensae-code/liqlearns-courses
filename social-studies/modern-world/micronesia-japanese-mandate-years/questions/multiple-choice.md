# Micronesia — the Japanese mandate years — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan seized Germany's islands in —
a) 1899  b) 1914  c) 1945
**Answer: b) 1914** — the seizure year the books give.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Japan ruled the islands as —
a) A League of Nations Mandate  b) An American territory  c) A Spanish colony
**Answer: a) A League of Nations Mandate** — the mandate the book names.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Island children attended schools called —
a) Mission schools  b) Kogakko schools  c) District schools
**Answer: b) Kogakko schools** — the schools the book names.

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** The 'yes-yes councils' were bodies where chiefs —
a) Made the real decisions  b) Only agreed with decisions made above them  c) Led the schools
**Answer: b) Only agreed with decisions made above them** — the councils as the government book describes them.
