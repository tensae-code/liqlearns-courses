# Micronesia — the Japanese mandate years — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Under the mandate, islanders became a minority in their own land.
**Answer: True** — the Grade 9 book teaches immigration made islanders a minority at home.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Katsuobushi and phosphate were imports the islands bought from Japan.
**Answer: False** — they were exports — the islands' products shipped out, tying the islands to the Japanese Empire.
