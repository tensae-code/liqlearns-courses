# Hands-on — Micronesia — the Japanese mandate years

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Minority at home
Draw your classroom twice: once with your class as the whole school, once with three new classes added around it. Under the second drawing, write how the first class's voice changes — then say what 'a minority in their own land' felt like in the mandate years.

## A council that only agrees
Act a yes-yes council: one person reads decisions, everyone else must only say yes. Then replay it as a real council where chiefs can change one decision. Say which council you would trust with your island, and why.
