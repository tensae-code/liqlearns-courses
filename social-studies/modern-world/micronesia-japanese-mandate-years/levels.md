# Micronesia — the Japanese mandate years — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the books teach them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Japan seizes the islands and builds a mandate
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that Japan seized Germany's islands in 1914, ruled them as a League of Nations Mandate, and built towns, industries and a cash economy that made islanders a minority in their own land.

## Sub lesson 2 — Schools, immigrants and island work
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that Okinawan and Japanese immigrants poured in, island children passed through kogakko schools toward civil-service and trade jobs, and exports such as katsuobushi and phosphate tied the islands to the Japanese Empire.

## Sub lesson 3 — Chiefs with no place: the yes-yes councils — and Kapingamarangi's Japanese time
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: say that under Japanese rule chiefs found no real place in the system and sat in 'yes-yes councils' that only agreed, and that Kapingamarangi's Japanese time ran 1914–1945.
