# Switzerland — The Albin family dissolved — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Why did the authorities say the Albin children had to go?
**Answer: They declared Josef and Sophia Albin incapable of raising their eight children in their extreme poverty (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** What help did other poor families get that the Albins did not?
**Answer: Beds, furniture and a "family rescue" — support that kept the family together — instead of dissolution (any close wording).**
