# Switzerland — The Albin family dissolved — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Albin family was reunited after the children grew up.
**Answer: False — both parents died without the family ever reuniting.**

**2. (Sub lesson 3)** At Sek II, pupils must deliver their own verdict on the Albin case from the two files.
**Answer: True — they reconstruct 1947–1953 from Canova's appeal and the government's decision and judge it themselves (teacher guide — evidence).**
