# Switzerland — The Albin family dissolved — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The authorities took the Albin children away —
a) In stages, 1949–1953  b) All on one day in 1940  c) Only after both parents died
**Answer: a) In stages, 1949–1953**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** After the children were taken, contact between parents and children was —
a) Blocked  b) Weekly  c) Encouraged by the office
**Answer: a) Blocked**

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Under the 1912 Civil Code, the head of the family was —
a) The husband  b) The eldest child  c) The welfare office
**Answer: a) The husband**

**4. (Sub lesson 3)** Lawyer Gaudenz Canova's appeal was —
a) Rejected by the Grisons government  b) Accepted in full  c) Never answered
**Answer: a) Rejected by the Grisons government**
