# Switzerland — The Albin family dissolved — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the books teach them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — Eight children and no family any more
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Tell the Albin story in order: eight children, declared incapable, taken in stages 1949–1953, contact blocked, never reunited.

## Sub lesson 2 — Measured against the model family
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Say what model the Albins were measured against, and what other poor families received instead of dissolution.

## Sub lesson 3 — The appeal that failed
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: Say who appealed for the Albins, how the government answered, and what pupils must do with the two files.
