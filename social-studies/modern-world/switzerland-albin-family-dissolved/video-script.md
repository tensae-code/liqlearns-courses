# Video script — Switzerland — The Albin family dissolved

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, as Switzerland's *Sorge oder Zwang?* set tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Eight children in twelve years — and almost nothing to live on.



The office declared the parents incapable. Between 1949 and 1953, the children were taken, in stages. Contact: blocked.



A lawyer appealed. The government answered, point by point — and said no.



The parents died. The family never came together again.
