# Hands-on — Switzerland — The Albin family dissolved

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two files, one verdict
Write two short columns: Canova's strongest point for the family, and the government's strongest point against it. Underneath, write your own verdict in two sentences and be ready to defend it.

## What rescue could have looked like
List what a "family rescue" for the Albins could have contained — beds, furniture, money, work, help in the home. Cross out anything the authorities actually gave them, and say what is left.
