# The English empire in America, 1660–1763 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The Glorious Revolution had no effect in America.
**Answer: False** — The Glorious Revolution reaches America.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** By 1763 the colonists were beginning to think of themselves as one people.
**Answer: True** — Awakening, Enlightenment and the wars for empire taught them to.
