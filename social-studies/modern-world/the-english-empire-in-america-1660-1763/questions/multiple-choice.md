# The English empire in America, 1660–1763 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After the Restoration, the English colonies —
a) Shrank to one  b) Multiplied  c) All won independence
**Answer: b) Multiplied** — the Restoration colonies multiply.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Which two bindings tied the colonies tighter to Britain?
a) Slavery and consumer goods  b) Snow and distance  c) French alliances
**Answer: a) Slavery and consumer goods** — slavery and consumer goods bind the colonies ever tighter to Britain.

**3. (Sub lesson 3)** The Great Awakening was —
a) A war with France  b) A wave of revival preaching  c) A new tax
**Answer: b) A wave of revival preaching** — the Great Awakening — revival preaching.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** The wars for empire and the new ideas taught colonists to —
a) Forget each other  b) Think of themselves as one people  c) Return to Europe
**Answer: b) Think of themselves as one people** — they teach colonists to think of themselves as one people.
