# Hands-on — Colonial Rule In Southeast Asia

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Colonial Rule In Southeast Asia — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Bài 5 How the colonies were ruled: local feudal elites kept as tools, all real power in colonial hands, divide-and-rule, native troops, taxation and plantations draining rubber, coffee, tea and rice — and deliberate ignorance: 8.5% literacy in Malaya in 1931, over 90% illiterate in Vietnam, opium and alcohol as policy; Nguyễn Ái Quốc's Colonial Regime on Trial quoted against France, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
