# Colonial Rule In Southeast Asia — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** the empire's cheapest weapon is an unschooled, divided, drugged colony.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Bài 5 How the colonies were ruled: local feudal elites kept as tools, all real power in colonial hands, divide-and-rule, native troops, taxation and plantations draining rubber, coffee, tea and rice — and deliberate ignorance: 8.5% literacy in Malaya in 1931, over 90% illiterate in Vietnam, opium and alcohol as policy; Nguyễn Ái Quốc's Colonial Regime on Trial quoted against France
