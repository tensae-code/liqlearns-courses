# Nicaragua Conquest And Indigenous Resistance — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Spain claims the "discovered" lands and sends conquest; Gil González Dávila is welcomed by Cacique Nicaragua in 1523 and baptisms follow, but further north Diriangén asks for time to think — and prepares resistance instead.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 12 de Octubre: Día de la resistencia indígena

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Estudios Sociales 3* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 12 de Octubre: Día de la resistencia indígena

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Gil González Dávila enters from Rivas in 1523, is received by Cacique Nicaragua, and conquest follows the welcome.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Gil González y la conquista de Nicaragua

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Estudios Sociales — Libro de Texto, Cuarto Grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Gil González y la conquista de Nicaragua

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** Indigenous peoples do not submit quietly; Diriangén's answer to the Spanish becomes the emblem of resistance.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Resistencia indígena
