# Nicaragua Conquest And Indigenous Resistance — sub lessons and depth

This file is the lesson's depth record, re-expressed as its sub lessons. Practice is tied to the learner's skill number. The questions live in the type files; this file is the by-sub-lesson view. Parts are ordered as the book teaches them, as stocked.

## Sub lesson 1 — 12 de Octubre: Día de la resistencia indígena
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Spain claims the "discovered" lands and sends conquest; Gil González Dávila is welcomed by Cacique Nicaragua in 1523 and baptisms follow, but further north Diriangén asks for time to think — and prepares resistance instead

## Sub lesson 2 — Gil González y la conquista de Nicaragua
Sub lesson 2 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Gil González Dávila enters from Rivas in 1523, is received by Cacique Nicaragua, and conquest follows the welcome

## Sub lesson 3 — Resistencia indígena
Sub lesson 3 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — indigenous peoples do not submit quietly; Diriangén's answer to the Spanish becomes the emblem of resistance

## Sub lesson 4 — Consecuencias de la conquista española en Nicaragua
Sub lesson 4 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the conquest's bill is counted — population loss, exploitation and a changed land — with Diriangén's defiance recalled as the counter-story

## Sub lesson 5 — Proceso de conquista de Nicaragua
Sub lesson 5 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — conquest comes from the Pacific, not the Caribbean — Gil González reaches the Mar Dulce, then Pedrarias's captain Hernández de Córdoba founds Granada and León in 1524 and the two conquerors fight each other for the land

## Sub lesson 6 — Resistencia indígena como consecuencia de la conquista
Sub lesson 6 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — colonisation and resistance begin simultaneously in 1523; Wheelock's "indigenous roots of the anti-colonial struggle" frames resistance as a tradition that never ends

## Sub lesson 7 — Proceso de conquista en Nicaragua y resistencia indígena
Sub lesson 7 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Gil González reaches Cacique Nicarao in 1523 and their famous dialogue astonishes the Spaniards; days later, on 17 April, Diriangén's men attack — the first armed defence of the land — but Spanish arms and indigenous division decide the outcome

## Sub lesson 8 — Consecuencias de la Conquista
Sub lesson 8 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — the indigenous population collapses catastrophically; Las Casas preaches against the abuses in León and Granada and Bishop Valdivieso is murdered by the Contreras brothers for defending the indigenous, while the New Laws of 1542 arrive too late

## Sub lesson 9 — Resistencia indígena contra el dominio español en Nicaragua
Sub lesson 9 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — Diriangén's and Nicaragua's attacks are the first organised combats against a foreign invader in Nicaragua's history; in twenty-seven years of exceptional cruelty under Pedrarias and Contreras, a population estimated at a million falls to 10,000
