# Lesson plan — Argentina — the conquest of indigenous territories, and who progress left out

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Argentina — the conquest of indigenous territories, and who progress left out
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — built in the History expansion (Argentina, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** The same Argentine state that welcomed immigrants sent its army against the indigenous peoples of the south and the Chaco and took their hunting and fishing lands — and in the cities, immigrant families crowded into conventillo tenements — so Argentina's Grade 6 teacher guide asks, in the same breath as the achievements, who progress left out.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on a question, not a celebration: when a country calls something progress, who pays for it? It needs nothing else. It stands on teacher-guide evidence only (see lesson/sources.md).

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The conquest of indigenous territories** — the state sends its army against the indigenous peoples of the south and the Chaco, taking their hunting and fishing lands.
2. **"Progress" does not reach everyone** — in the cities, immigrant families crowd into conventillo tenements, and the book asks who progress left out.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: Argentina — building the state, 1860–1916; Argentina — the great immigration. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Say what the Argentine state did to the indigenous peoples of the south and the Chaco, and what it took from them.
2. Say what a conventillo was, and whose crowded homes it names.
3. Say the question the book asks about progress, and name the two groups this lesson gives as its answer.
