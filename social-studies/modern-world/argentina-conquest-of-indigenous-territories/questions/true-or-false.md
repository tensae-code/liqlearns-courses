# Argentina — the conquest of indigenous territories, and who progress left out — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The state that welcomed immigrants was a different state from the one that conquered indigenous territories.
**Answer: False** — it was the same state; the book teaches the welcoming and the conquest in the same breath.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** In the book's teaching, progress reached everyone in Argentina equally.
**Answer: False** — its heading states the opposite: "Progress" does not reach everyone — not the conquered indigenous peoples, and not the immigrant families crowded into conventillos.
