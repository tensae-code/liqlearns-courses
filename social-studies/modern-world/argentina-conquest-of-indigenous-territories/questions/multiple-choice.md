# Argentina — the conquest of indigenous territories, and who progress left out — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Against whom did the Argentine state send its army in the south and the Chaco?
a) The indigenous peoples who lived there  b) Foreign shipping companies  c) Its own railway builders
**Answer: a) The indigenous peoples who lived there** — peoples who had hunted and fished on those lands long before the state existed.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** What did the conquest take from those peoples?
a) Their ships  b) Their hunting and fishing lands  c) Their city tenements
**Answer: b) Their hunting and fishing lands** — the ground their whole way of life stood on.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** A conventillo was —
a) A government office in Europe  b) A crowded tenement where many immigrant families lived in small rooms  c) A kind of railway station
**Answer: b) A crowded tenement where many immigrant families lived in small rooms** — in the cities of the booming country.

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** The question the book asks about this period's progress is —
a) Who progress left out  b) How fast the trains ran  c) How many ports were built
**Answer: a) Who progress left out** — taught in the same breath as the achievements.
