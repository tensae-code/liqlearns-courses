# Argentina — the conquest of indigenous territories, and who progress left out — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** What happened to the indigenous peoples of the south and the Chaco as the Argentine state was built?
**Answer: The state sent its army against them and took their hunting and fishing lands, the ground their way of life stood on (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Name the two groups this lesson gives in answer to the book's question — who did progress leave out?
**Answer: The indigenous peoples whose lands were conquered, and the immigrant families crowded into conventillo tenements in the cities (any close wording).**
