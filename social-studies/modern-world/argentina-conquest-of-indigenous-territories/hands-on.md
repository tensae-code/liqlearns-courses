# Hands-on — Argentina — the conquest of indigenous territories, and who progress left out

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Two faces of progress
Fold a page in half. On one side, draw or write the progress the earlier Argentina lessons celebrated — railways, ports, new towns, a populated country. On the other side, write the two costs this lesson names. Say out loud why the book teaches both sides in the same breath.

## Whose land?
Draw the south and the Chaco as the indigenous peoples used them — hunting and fishing grounds — and write underneath what the state's army took, and what that taking meant for the people who lived from those lands.

## A conventillo courtyard
Draw a courtyard with many small rooms around it, one family in each room. Under your drawing, write one sentence on why families the state had invited across an ocean ended up living crowded like this.
