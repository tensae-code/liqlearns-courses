# Lesson plan — Cambodia — rebuilding and peace, 1979–1993

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Cambodia — rebuilding and peace, 1979–1993
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Cambodia, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** survivors rebuild from zero; the Paris Agreements of 23 October 1991 and UNTAC bring elections, and Angkor joins the World Heritage List in 1992
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **Lesson 5, People's Republic of Kampuchea and State of Cambodia (1979–1993)** — survivors rebuild from zero; the Paris Agreements of 23 October 1991 and UNTAC bring elections, and Angkor joins the World Heritage List in 1992.
2. **Ch. 2 Lesson 5, The People's Republic of Kampuchea and the State of Cambodia (1979–1993)** — cadres who fled the purges (Heng Samrin, photo) found a salvation front on 2 December 1978; victory on 7 January 1979 starts the rebuilding that ends with the Paris Agreements and UNTAC.
3. **History Ch. 2 Lesson 5, The People's Republic of Kampuchea and the State of Cambodia (1979–1991) and the Kingdom of Cambodia 1993** — survivors of the purges (Heng Samrin, photo) build the resistance that wins on 7 January 1979; the lesson runs through war and embargo to the Paris Agreements and the restored kingdom.
