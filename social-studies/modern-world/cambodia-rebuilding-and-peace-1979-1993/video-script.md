# Video script — Cambodia — rebuilding and peace, 1979–1993

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Cambodia's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

survivors rebuild from zero; the Paris Agreements of 23 October 1991 and UNTAC bring elections, and Angkor joins the World Heritage List in 1992.

survivors of the purges (Heng Samrin, photo) build the resistance that wins on 7 January 1979; the lesson runs through war and embargo to the Paris Agreements and the restored kingdom.
