# Hands-on — Republic and Reign of Terror

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## The fear chain
Write five cards — War goes badly; Revolt inside; Fear grows; Emergency powers; Terror. Shuffle them, then lay them back in the order that explains the next, saying each link aloud.

## Two voices of 1793
Write two short speeches of three lines each: a member of the Committee defending the Terror as saving the republic, and a market woman answering him. Read both aloud.
