# Video script — Republic and Reign of Terror

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

The map opens: armies press on every border of France. Inside, provinces flare red with revolt.
A crown falls. A republic's flag rises. In Paris a blade rises and falls, rises and falls — the squares empty of everything but fear.
The camera moves along a line of prisoners. Some wear silk. Some wear revolution's own cockade. The machine does not distinguish any more.
One name is called in the Convention — Robespierre. The blade falls a last time. The squares fill again, slowly, with ordinary life.
