# Republic and Reign of Terror — true or false

Say whether each sentence is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1) ** The executed king's trial showed Europe the revolution would show no mercy to crowns.
**Answer: True** — the execution was a declaration to every throne.

**2. (Sub lesson 2) ** Only nobles and priests were executed in the Terror.
**Answer: False** — ordinary people and revolutionaries themselves were also devoured.

**3. (Sub lesson 2) ** Robespierre died by the guillotine at Thermidor.
**Answer: True** — the machine he served took its master.

**4. (Sub lesson 2) ** After Thermidor, France swung away from the revolution's most radical phase.
**Answer: True** — reaction followed Robespierre's fall.
