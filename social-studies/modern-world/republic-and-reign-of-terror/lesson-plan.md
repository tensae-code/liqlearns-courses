# Lesson plan — Republic and Reign of Terror

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** Modern World · **Lesson:** Republic and Reign of Terror
**Place in the curriculum:** Modern World — the age of revolutions unit; the Grade 11 book's second French Revolution lesson (CURRICULUM.md).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** the revolution turns on itself: the monarchy falls, a republic fights all Europe, the Committee of Public Safety rules through the Reign of Terror of 1793–94 — and reaction follows at Thermidor.
**This lesson's own level:** it stands directly on 1789 — the French Revolution breaks out. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **The monarchy falls; the republic fights for its life** — war with Europe, the king's fall and execution, and revolt in the provinces squeeze the young republic.
2. **The Terror, 1793–94 — and Thermidor** — the Committee of Public Safety, Robespierre and the guillotine; the reaction that ended the Terror.

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.

## Before this lesson
- Recommended first: 1789 — the French Revolution breaks out. Recommended, never forced.

## What the learner can do when it ends
1. Tell how the monarchy fell and the republic began.
2. Explain why the republic felt besieged — war outside, revolt inside.
3. Describe the Terror: who ran it, what it did, when.
4. Say how the Terror ended at Thermidor.

## If the learner is stuck
The chain is: war and revolt frighten the republic; fear hands power to the Committee of Public Safety; the Committee's weapon is the Terror; the Terror eats so many that the Assembly finally turns on its makers — Thermidor.
