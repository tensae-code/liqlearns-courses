# Video script — British rule in Bengal — two verdicts

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story as the Bangladesh book tells it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Grade 8 teaches British rule with two verdicts face to face.

The first is a boast: a 1911 British weekly praises the peace and security given to India.

The second is a count: Angus Maddison reckons India's share of world wealth collapsed to about 3 percent by the time the British left. Boast against count — the learner judges.
