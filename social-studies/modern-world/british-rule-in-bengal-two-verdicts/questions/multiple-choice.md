# British rule in Bengal — two verdicts — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** The 1911 British weekly's verdict was —
a) India grew poorer  b) British rule gave peace and security  c) India was already independent
**Answer: b) British rule gave peace and security** — the boast.

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Angus Maddison counted India's share of world wealth at the British departure as about —
a) 30 percent  b) 3 percent  c) 50 percent
**Answer: b) 3 percent** — Maddison's count.

**3. (Sub lesson 1)** The lesson sets the two verdicts —
a) In different books  b) Face to face, for the learner to judge  c) With the count hidden
**Answer: b) Face to face, for the learner to judge** — the book's method.

**4. (Sub lesson 1)** Maddison was —
a) A British weekly editor  b) An economist  c) A viceroy
**Answer: b) An economist** — who made the count.
