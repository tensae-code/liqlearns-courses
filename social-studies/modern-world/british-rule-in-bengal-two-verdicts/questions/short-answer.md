# British rule in Bengal — two verdicts — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** State both verdicts in one sentence each.
**Answer: A 1911 British weekly boasted that British rule gave India peace and security; Angus Maddison counted India's share of world wealth collapsing to about 3 percent by the time the British left (any close wording).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Why can the two verdicts not both be the whole truth?
**Answer: A land kept in peace and security would not be expected to lose almost its whole share of world wealth; the boast and the count point opposite ways and must be weighed (any close wording).**
