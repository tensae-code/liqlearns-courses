# Bhutan — the Treaty of Sinchula, 1865

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: on 11 November 1865 Bhutan permanently cedes all the Duars — a fifth of its territory — in exchange for peace, free trade and an annual British subsidy rising to Rs 50,000, opening the modern chapter of its foreign relations; the Sinchula settlement is re-read through the man who fought the war and then rebuilt the relationship, reopening Bhutan's southern trade after 1865. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
