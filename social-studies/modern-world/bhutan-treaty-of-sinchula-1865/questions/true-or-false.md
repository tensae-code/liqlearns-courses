# Bhutan — the Treaty of Sinchula, 1865 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** on 11 November 1865 Bhutan permanently cedes all the Duars — a fifth of its territory — in exchange for peace, free trade and an annual British subsidy rising to Rs 50,000, opening the modern chapter of its foreign relations
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Six, The Treaty of Sinchula).

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** the Sinchula settlement is re-read through the man who fought the war and then rebuilt the relationship, reopening Bhutan's southern trade after 1865
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Chapter Two, Desi Jigme Namgyel: Restoring Stability in Relations with British India).
