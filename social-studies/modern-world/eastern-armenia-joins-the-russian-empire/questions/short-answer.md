# Eastern Armenia Joins The Russian Empire — short answer

Answer in a sentence or two.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Retell this part in your own words: §6.1 Eastern Armenia and the Russo-Persian wars.
**Answer: Russia's armies take Yerevan in 1827; the Turkmenchay treaty moves Eastern Armenia from Persian to Russian rule (any close wording — summary only, never the book's words).**

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** In Armenia's book, where is this part taught?
**Answer: In *«Հայոց պատմություն 8. Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար» (History of Armenia 8, textbook for general schools) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, Ala A. Kharatyan, Zaruhi A. Hakobyan* (Armenia), §6.1 Eastern Armenia and the Russo-Persian wars, printed p. 82.**
