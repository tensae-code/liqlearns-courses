# Eastern Armenia Joins The Russian Empire — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Russia's armies take Yerevan in 1827; the Turkmenchay treaty moves Eastern Armenia from Persian to Russian rule.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in §6.1 Eastern Armenia and the Russo-Persian wars

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *«Հայոց պատմություն 8. Դասագիրք հանրակրթական դպրոցի համար» (History of Armenia 8, textbook for general schools) — Smbat Kh. Hovhannisyan, Ala A. Kharatyan, Zaruhi A. Hakobyan* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about §6.1 Eastern Armenia and the Russo-Persian wars
