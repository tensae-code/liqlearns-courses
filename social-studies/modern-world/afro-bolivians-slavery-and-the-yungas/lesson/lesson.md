# Afro-Bolivians — slavery and the Yungas

## Do it first

Before you read on, hold this lesson's one thing in mind: Africans are brought in chains — how many died will never be known; escapees flee east from bandeirantes, and communities settle in Nor and Sud Yungas, Inquisivi, Caranavi, Murillo and Larecaja, keeping a syncretic culture. As you read, keep asking where each part of that sentence is proved.
