# Belarus — industry and the workers' movement, 1861–1914 — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the story is mainly about —
a) Industry and the workers' movement, 1861–1900  b) The First World War and February 1917 in Belarus  c) Events of the war of 1812 on the Belarusian lands
**Answer: a) Industry and the workers' movement, 1861–1900** — this part tells industry and the workers' movement, 1861–1900

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
a) pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes  b) pupils draw the front line of late 1915 and its 1916 stabilisation, the Sventsyany breakthrough, the Naroch and Baranovichi operations, and the towns where the first Soviets of…  c) pupils mark the GDL Provisional Government Commission in Vilnius, the French control zone and the districts where GDL regiments formed
**Answer: a)** — pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of the story is mainly about —

**Answer: a) Industry and towns at the beginning of the 20th century** — this part tells industry and towns at the beginning of the 20th century

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** Which of these belongs in this part of the story?
