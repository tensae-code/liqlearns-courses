# Belarus — industry and the workers' movement, 1861–1914 — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Pupils sign the craft and manufactory towns, the tobacco, match, shipbuilding and paper works, and the towns of the 1876–1900 strikes
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Industry and the workers' movement, 1861–1900

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Contour Maps: History of Belarus, Grade 9* is about The First World War and February 1917 in Belarus.
**Answer: False** — it is about Industry and the workers' movement, 1861–1900

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** Pupils date the railway lines decade by decade and circle the towns over 100,000 people and the great paper factories
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Industry and towns at the beginning of the 20th century

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Contour Maps: History of Belarus, Grade 9* is about Belarus during the war of 1812 — invasion and expulsion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Industry and towns at the beginning of the 20th century
