# Malayan resistance to the British — short answer

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Tell this part of the story in your own words: Local resistance.
**Answer: stripped of governing, legal and financial power, local leaders rise state by state — Dato' Maharaja Lela in Perak, Yamtuan Antah in Negeri Sembilan, Dol Said in Naning, Dato' Bahaman in Pahang, Tok Janggut in Kelantan, Haji Abdul Rahman Limbong in Terengganu, Rentap and Sharif Masahor in Sarawak, Mat Salleh and Mat Sator in Sabah; the risings win early successes before Britain crushes them one by one (any close wording).**
