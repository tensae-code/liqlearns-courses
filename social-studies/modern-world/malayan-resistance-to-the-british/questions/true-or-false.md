# Malayan resistance to the British — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Stripped of governing, legal and financial power, local leaders rise state by state — Dato' Maharaja Lela in Perak, Yamtuan Antah in Negeri Sembilan, Dol Said in Naning, Dato' Bahaman in Pahang, Tok Janggut in Kelantan, Haji Abdul Rahman Limbong in Terengganu, Rentap and Sharif Masahor in Sarawak, Mat Salleh and Mat Sator in Sabah; the risings win early successes before Britain crushes them one by one
**Answer: True** — as the book teaches it (Bab 7: local resistance).
