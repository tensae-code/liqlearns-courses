# Malayan resistance to the British — multiple choice

Pick the one right answer.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of the lesson teaches —
a) Local resistance  b) Something unrelated  c) Nothing at all
**Answer: a) Local resistance** — stripped of governing, legal and financial power, local leaders rise state by state — Dato' Maharaja Lela in Perak, Yamtuan Antah in Negeri Sembilan, Dol Said in Naning, Dato' Bahaman in Pahang, Tok Janggut in Kelantan, Haji Abdul Rahman Limbong in Terengganu, Rentap and Sharif Masahor in Sarawak, Mat Salleh and Mat Sator in Sabah; the risings win early successes before Britain crushes them one by one

a) Bab 7: local resistance  b) A geography map exercise only  c) A maths drill
**Answer: a) Bab 7: local resistance** — the book's own heading for this part.
