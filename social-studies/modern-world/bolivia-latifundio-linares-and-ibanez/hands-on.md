# Hands-on — Bolivia — latifundio, Linares and Ibáñez

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Bolivia — latifundio, Linares and Ibáñez — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, Hacienda, dictadura civil e igualitarismo, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
