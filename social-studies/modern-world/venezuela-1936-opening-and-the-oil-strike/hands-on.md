# Hands-on — Venezuela 1936 Opening And The Oil Strike

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — Venezuela 1936 Opening And The Oil Strike — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, 1936–1941: "calm and cordure", sowing the oil and the 1936 oil strike, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
