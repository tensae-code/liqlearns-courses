# Venezuela 1936 Opening And The Oil Strike — true or false

Say True or False, and why.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** After Gómez dies, López Contreras promises calm, Alberto Adriani urges "sembrar el petróleo", the Ley Lara represses, and oil workers strike in 1936 before the 1938 Triennial Plan.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in 1936–1941: "calm and cordure", sowing the oil and the 1936 oil strike

**2. (Sub lesson 1)** This part of *Historia de Venezuela Contemporánea — Ciencias Sociales, 4to año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about 1936–1941: "calm and cordure", sowing the oil and the 1936 oil strike

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The oil strike is retold through the children who lived it, waiting on fathers who had downed tools.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Children of the strike (complementary reading)

**4. (Sub lesson 2)** This part of *Viva Venezuela — Ciencias Sociales, 3er grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Children of the strike (complementary reading)

**5. (Sub lesson 3)** After Gómez dies, López Contreras acts as a bridge, parties and unions appear, and democracy advances shyly to 1945.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Between 1936 and 1945 democracy peeks out timidly

**6. (Sub lesson 3)** This part of *Venezuela y Su Gente — Ciencias Sociales, 6to grado* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Between 1936 and 1945 democracy peeks out timidly

**7. (Sub lesson 4)** After Gómez, López Contreras opens a cautious transition — February Programme, parties tolerated, oil strike faced.
**Answer: True** — the book tells this in Government of López Contreras

**8. (Sub lesson 4)** This part of *Nuestra Historia Republicana — Ciencias Sociales, 2do año* is about a different part of this story, told in another lesson of this expansion.
**Answer: False** — it is about Government of López Contreras
