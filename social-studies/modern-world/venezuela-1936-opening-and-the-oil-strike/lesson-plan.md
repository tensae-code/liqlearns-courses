# Lesson plan — Venezuela 1936 Opening And The Oil Strike

**Course:** Social Studies · **Title:** The Modern World · **Lesson:** Venezuela 1936 Opening And The Oil Strike
**Place in the curriculum:** The Modern World — built in the History expansion (Venezuela, 2026-11-15).
**The one thing this lesson teaches:** after Gómez dies, López Contreras promises calm, Alberto Adriani urges "sembrar el petróleo", the Ley Lara represses, and oil workers strike in 1936 before the 1938 Triennial Plan
**This lesson's own level:** it stands on its own story, told in full inside the lesson. It needs nothing else.

A lesson plan also says **what goes on top of the lesson**: at the exact places marked below, the lesson page carries an add-on — an image, a GIF, a video, an audio. Every slot is written as **"a picture of …" in words, plus one little line saying what the student does with it**. The words carry the picture while the course is built; on the website the real add-on is dropped into that slot and the little explanation stays as the student's guide. History is taught as story: people, places, what happened, what changed.

## Sub lessons
The parts of this lesson, in order — the list the learner sees from the start, locked ahead, each opening when the one before it is proved:

1. **1936–1941: "calm and cordure", sowing the oil and the 1936 oil strike** — after Gómez dies, López Contreras promises calm, Alberto Adriani urges "sembrar el petróleo", the Ley Lara represses, and oil workers strike in 1936 before the 1938 Triennial Plan
2. **Children of the strike (complementary reading)** — the oil strike is retold through the children who lived it, waiting on fathers who had downed tools
3. **Between 1936 and 1945 democracy peeks out timidly** — after Gómez dies, López Contreras acts as a bridge, parties and unions appear, and democracy advances shyly to 1945
4. **Government of López Contreras** — after Gómez, López Contreras opens a cautious transition — February Programme, parties tolerated, oil strike faced

Gate pool: all questions of the sub lesson — no hardness ranking is written in questions/, so the sub lesson's whole question set is the gate pool.
