# Video script — Egypt and the road to the Arab League

The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style: the story told as Egypt's own books tell it. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.

---

Shared language, history and a common struggle pull the Arabs together despite colonialism's wedges — Nahhas Pasha's call of 1943 opens the Cairo consultations, the Alexandria Protocol of 1944 fixes the rules, and the Arab League is born.
