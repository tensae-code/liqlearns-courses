## Sub lesson 1 — Chapter 7: Egypt and the issues of the contemporary Arab world — the road to the Arab League
Sub lesson 1 questions from the type files. Pass mark: retell this part in your own words — colonialism drives its wedges — Zaydi against Sunni, Maronite against Muslim, republic against throne, French and Italian forced on Arab schoolchildren — yet language, history and a shared war pull the Arabs together: Taif in 1934, the Iraq–Saudi treaty of 1936, Egypt's own treaty with Saudi Arabia the same year; Nahhas Pasha's call of March 1943 opens the Cairo consultations, the Alexandria Protocol of 1944 fixes the League's rules — equality of members, no force between them, respect for existing borders — and the Arab League is born
