in December 1989 the Mongolian Democratic Union's first rally fills a square; hunger strikes in Sükhbaatar Square force the Politburo's resignation in March 1990. parties multiply, the first free elections are held in July 1990, and a grand coalition begins the transition. in January 1992 Mongolia adopts a democratic constitution — human rights, multiparty rule and private property replace the people's republic. the Democratic Union's first rally (10 Dec 1989), the March 1990 hunger strike, the Politburo's resignation, and the first free elections that July — a revolution without a shot. multiparty registration, the Little Khural, and the 1992 Constitution — parliament, president and constitutional court replace the people's republic.
