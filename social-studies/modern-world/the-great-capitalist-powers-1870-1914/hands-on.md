# Hands-on — The great capitalist powers, 1870–1914

The lesson with your hands and your feet, not only your eyes.

## Task 1
List the parts of this story in order — The great capitalist powers, 1870–1914 — and say what changed in each part.

## Task 2
Draw or write the key scene of the first part, The great capitalist powers, from the story as the book tells it, and label the people and the place in it.
