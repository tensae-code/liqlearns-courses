# Video script — Cottage industries — skilled hands, low status


The super-simple voice, for the video that rides on top of the lesson (lesson-plan.md, add-on list). Story-style, in the animated-map spirit: the map opens, peoples and armies move, capitals rise, routes light up. Made later, when videos are made; the script is the slot's written form until then.


---


The map opens on Ethiopia — no factories, no chimneys. Everything is made by hand.

A smith lifts his hammer. A weaver throws her shuttle. A potter turns the clay.

Ploughshares. Cloth. Pots. Baskets. Jewellery. A whole country, clothed and fed by cottages.

But watch how the crowd passes the workshops — eyes down. The makers are pushed to the edge.

And so the tools stay as they always were. Skill without honour does not grow.

Remember these hands. The factory story means something different where the makers are despised.
