# Cottage industries — skilled hands, low status — true or false

Say whether each statement is true or false.

**1. (Sub lesson 1)** Weaving and basket-making were among Ethiopia's cottage industries.
**Answer: True** — Both are in the book's list.

**2. (Sub lesson 2)** Artisans held high social status in 19th-century Ethiopia.
**Answer: False** — They were disdained and marginalised by the general public.

**3. (Sub lesson 2)** The low status of artisans helped their crafts improve quickly.
**Answer: False** — It did the opposite: implements and furniture showed little improvement.
